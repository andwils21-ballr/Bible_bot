# Jude 1

**1** Jude, a servant of Jesus Christ and brother of James, to those who are called, loved in God the Father and kept for Jesus Christ:

**2** may mercy, peace and love be multiplied to you.

## Contend for the Faith

**3** Beloved, while I was making every effort to write to you about the salvation we share, I found it necessary to write to you urging you to contend for the faith that was handed down once for all to the saints.

**4** For certain men have slipped in among you, who long ago were written down for this judgment: ungodly men, who turn the grace of our God into indecency and deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.

## Judgment Kept

**5** Now I want to remind you, though you once knew all this, that Jesus, after He saved a people out of the land of Egypt, afterward destroyed those who did not believe.

**6** The angels who did not keep their own rank, but abandoned their proper dwelling, He has kept in eternal bonds under darkness for the judgment of the great day;

**7** just as Sodom and Gomorrah and the cities around them, which in the same way as these gave themselves to sexual immorality and went after other flesh, lie before us as an example, undergoing the penalty of eternal fire.

**8** Yet in the same way these men too, by their dreaming, defile the flesh, reject authority, and blaspheme the glorious ones.

**9** When Michael the archangel contended with the devil and argued over the body of Moses, he did not dare bring a blasphemous judgment against him, but said, "The Lord rebuke you."

**10** These men blaspheme whatever they do not understand, and by what they know by instinct, like the unreasoning animals, by these things they are destroyed.

**11** Woe to them! For they have gone the way of Cain, and for gain they have rushed headlong into the error of Balaam, and perished in the rebellion of Korah.

**12** These are hidden reefs at your love feasts, feasting with you without fear, shepherding themselves; clouds without water, carried along by winds; autumn trees without fruit, twice dead, uprooted;

**13** wild waves of the sea, foaming up their own shame; wandering stars, for whom the gloom of darkness has been kept forever.

**14** It was about these also that Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied, saying, "Here — the Lord came with ten thousands of His holy ones,

**15** to carry out judgment on all, and to convict all the ungodly of all their ungodly deeds that they have done in ungodliness, and of all the harsh words that ungodly sinners have spoken against Him."

**16** These are grumblers, complaining of their lot, walking according to their own desires; their mouths speak arrogant words, and they flatter people to gain advantage.

## Keep Yourselves in the Love of God

**17** But you, beloved, remember the words spoken beforehand by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ,

**18** for they told you, "In the last time there will be scoffers, walking according to their own ungodly desires."

**19** These are the ones who cause divisions, worldly people, who do not have the Spirit.

**20** But you, beloved, building yourselves up on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Spirit,

**21** keep yourselves in the love of God, waiting for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ that leads to eternal life.

**22** Have mercy on some who are at odds;

**23** save others, snatching them out of the fire; and on others have mercy with fear, hating even the tunic stained by the flesh.

**24** Now to Him who is able to keep you from stumbling, and to set you blameless before His glory with great joy,

**25** to the only God our Savior, through Jesus Christ our Lord, be glory, majesty, dominion and authority, before all time, and now, and forever. Amen.

## Notes

- **v5 "Jesus, after He saved a people out of the land of Egypt"** — so this Greek edition reads, *Iēsous*, and it marks the name as a place where manuscripts differ. As it reads, the one who brought Israel out of Egypt and afterward destroyed those who did not believe (Numbers 14:29) is named as Jesus, as Paul says of the wilderness, *the rock was Christ* (1 Corinthians 10:4).
- **vv1, 6, 13, 21 "kept"** — *tēreō*, the same verb each time: the called are *kept for Jesus Christ* (v1); the angels *did not keep* their rank, so God *has kept* them in bonds (v6); darkness *has been kept* for the wandering stars (v13); and the readers are told, *keep yourselves in the love of God* (v21). In v6 the word for *rank* is *archē*, which also means a beginning: the place first given them.
- **v6 "the angels who did not keep their own rank, but abandoned their proper dwelling"** — the story 1 Enoch tells of the Watchers, *who have left the high heaven and the eternal holy sanctuary* (1 Enoch 12:4), whom Michael was told to *bind… under the hills of the earth, until the day of their judgment* (1 Enoch 10:11–12).
- **v9 "The Lord rebuke you"** — the words of the LORD's messenger to Satan, *The LORD rebuke you, Satan!* (Zechariah 3:2). The dispute over Moses' body is not told in the Hebrew Bible, which says only that the LORD buried him and *no one knows his burial place to this day* (Deuteronomy 34:6).
- **v12 "hidden reefs"** — *spilades*, rocks under the surface, where a ship is wrecked before anyone sees the danger; it begins a run of pictures from the natural world (clouds, trees, waves, stars). The word sounds like *spiloi*, *blots*, which 2 Peter uses in the same place (2 Peter 2:13), and *espilōmenon*, *stained*, in v23.
- **vv14–15 "Here — the Lord came with ten thousands of His holy ones"** — the words of 1 Enoch 1:9, *And here — He came with ten thousands of holy ones, to carry out judgment*. Enoch is *the seventh from Adam* by the line of Genesis 5: Adam, Seth, Enosh, Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared, Enoch.
- **v22 "some who are at odds"** — *diakrinomenous*, the verb used of Michael in v9, *contended*. Of a person divided within, it means to waver or doubt; of people divided from one another, to dispute. The Greek leaves both open, and so does *at odds*.
- **v23 "snatching them out of the fire… the tunic stained by the flesh"** — the same scene in Zechariah 3: *Is not this man a brand snatched from the fire?* and *Take the filthy garments off him* (Zechariah 3:2, 4).

# Gitzew 1

In the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, one God. This is the Synod of the Christian Church, which the Apostles gave through the hand of Clement, whom they had sent out early.

## How Many Bishops Ordain a Bishop

**1** A bishop shall be ordained by two or by three bishops; but an elder and a deacon and the rest who are ordained shall be ordained by one bishop alone.

## Notes

- **The whole book** — worked from the English of George H. Schodde, "The Apostolic Canons, Translated from the Ethiopic" (1885), made from Winand Fell's edition of the Ethiopic (1871). No Ge'ez or other source language was used; Fell's printed Ethiopic, read by machine, was used only to check that the canons line up. The chapters are the canons; each verse is one of Schodde's paragraphs. The opening before canon 1 is set above verse 1, and each canon's own title stands as its heading.
- **The opening, "through the hand of Clement"** — the canons are presented as the apostles' own, delivered by Clement of Rome. *Whom they had sent out early* is as close as Schodde's English allows (*whom they early had sent*); it does not say where Clement was sent.
- **v1 one canon, two rules** — the Ethiopic joins in one canon what the Coptic collection counts as two: *A Bishop shall be ordained by two or three Bishops* (Abtilis 1) and *A Presbyter, or Deacon, and the rest of the Clergy, shall be ordained by one Bishop* (Abtilis 2). The two orders differ in kind: a bishop is made by the bishops together, every other order by one bishop.
- **v1 "an elder"** — the office the Greek calls *presbyteros*, "elder", from which English *priest* comes. Schodde keeps *elder* throughout, and so does this rendering.
- **v1 "ordained"** — the laying on of hands, as when the seven were set before the apostles and *they prayed and laid their hands on them* (Acts 6:6). Paul's warning to Timothy, *lay hands on no one hastily* (1 Timothy 5:22), is the same act.
- **Source text** — George H. Schodde, "The Apostolic Canons, Translated from the Ethiopic", *Journal of the Society of Biblical Literature and Exegesis* 5 (1885), public domain. English translation by this project.

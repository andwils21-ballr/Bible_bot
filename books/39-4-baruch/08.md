# 4 Baruch 8

**1** The day came when the Lord brought the people out of Babylon.

**2** The Lord said to Jeremiah, "Get up, you and the people, and come to the Jordan, and say to the people: Whoever wants the Lord, let him leave behind the works of Babylon.

**3** As for the men who have taken wives from among them, and the women who have taken husbands from among them: let those who listen to you cross over, and take them up to Jerusalem; but those who do not listen to you, do not bring them there."

**4** Jeremiah spoke these words to the people, and they got up and came to the Jordan to cross.

**5** As he told them the words the Lord had spoken to him, half of those who had married among them would not listen to Jeremiah, but said to him, "We will never leave our wives; we will bring them back with us to our city."

**6** So they crossed the Jordan and came to Jerusalem.

**7** Jeremiah, Baruch and Abimelech stood and said, "No one who has joined with the Babylonians shall enter this city."

**8** They said to one another, "Let us get up and go back to Babylon, to our own place." So they went.

**9** When they arrived in Babylon, the Babylonians came out to meet them, saying, "You shall not enter our city, because you hated us and left us in secret; so you will not come in among us.

**10** For we have bound one another by an oath in the name of our god to take back neither you nor your children, since you left us in secret."

**11** When they learned this, they turned back and came to a deserted place far from Jerusalem, and built themselves a city, and called its name Samaria.

**12** Jeremiah sent to them, saying, "Repent, for the angel of righteousness is coming, and he will bring you into your high place."

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "leave behind the works of Babylon… those who listen to you cross over"** — the test the letter announced, *You will test them by the water of the Jordan; whoever does not listen will be made known* (6:25). The return is told as a second crossing of the Jordan.
- **vv3–5 "the men who have taken wives from among them… We will never leave our wives"** — the question of Ezra's return: *We have broken faith with our God and have married foreign women from the peoples of the land* (Ezra 10:2). In Ezra the men agree to send the wives away (10:3); here half of them refuse.
- **vv6–8 "So they crossed the Jordan and came to Jerusalem… No one who has joined with the Babylonians shall enter this city"** — those who would not leave their wives cross with them and come as far as the city, and are stopped at it by Jeremiah, Baruch and Abimelech.
- **vv9–10 "You shall not enter our city… to take back neither you nor your children"** — the word of the letter kept: *whoever does not listen will become a stranger to Jerusalem and to Babylon* (6:24), and *I will rebuke them, so that the Babylonians will not take them back again* (6:17).
- **v11 "built themselves a city, and called its name Samaria"** — the Ge'ez has the name too. The book gives its own account of where the Samaritans came from. Kings gives another: the king of Assyria *settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the sons of Israel* (2 Kings 17:24), people brought from other lands.
- **v12 "the angel of righteousness is coming, and he will bring you into your high place"** — the angel Baruch named in his prayer (6:9). *Your high place*, *ton topon hymōn ton hypsēlon*, is not named: *hypsēlos* is used of a raised place on earth and of the height of heaven, and the sentence does not say which.
- **Source text** — Greek: Online Critical Pseudepigrapha, CC BY 4.0. Ge'ez: A. Dillmann, *Chrestomathia Aethiopica* (1866), public domain. English translation by this project.

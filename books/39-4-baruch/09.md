# 4 Baruch 9

**1** Those with Jeremiah stayed on, rejoicing and offering sacrifices for the people, for nine days.

**2** On the tenth, Jeremiah alone offered a sacrifice.

**3** He prayed, saying, "Holy, holy, holy, incense of the living trees, true light that gives me light until I am taken up to You:

**4** for Your mercy I plead; for the sweet voice of the two seraphim I plead; for another fragrance of incense.

**5** My meditation is Michael, the archangel of righteousness, who opens the gates to the righteous until he brings the righteous in.

**6** I plead with You, Lord Almighty of all creation, unbegotten and beyond understanding, in whom every judgment was hidden before these things came to be."

**7** As Jeremiah was saying this, standing at the altar with Baruch and Abimelech, he became like one who gives up his soul.

**8** Baruch and Abimelech stayed there weeping and crying out with a loud voice, "Woe to us, for our father Jeremiah, the priest of God, has left us and gone away!"

**9** All the people heard their weeping and ran to them, and they saw Jeremiah lying on the ground like a dead man.

**10** They tore their clothes, put dust on their heads and wept bitterly.

**11** After this they got ready to bury him.

**12** A voice came, saying, "Do not bury one who is still alive, for his soul is coming back into his body."

**13** When they heard the voice, they did not bury him, but stayed around his tent for three days, saying, "At what hour will he rise?"

**14** After three days his soul came back into his body, and he lifted up his voice among them all and said, "Glorify God with one voice! All of you, glorify God, and the Son of God who wakes us, Jesus the Messiah, the light of all the ages, the lamp that is never put out, the life of faith.

**15** After these times there will be another four hundred and seventy-seven years, and He comes to the earth.

**16** The tree of life that is planted in the middle of paradise will make all the fruitless trees bear fruit, and they will grow and put out shoots.

**17** The trees that have sprouted and boast, saying, 'We have lifted our tops into the air,' the firmly planted tree will make wither, with all the height of their branches, and will bring them to judgment.

**18** What is scarlet will become white as wool; the snow will turn black; the sweet waters will become salt, and the salt sweet, in the great light of the joy of God.

**19** He will bless the islands so that they bear fruit, by the word of the mouth of His Messiah.

**20** For He will come, and go out, and choose for Himself twelve apostles to preach the good news among the nations. I have seen Him adorned by His Father and coming into the world on the Mount of Olives; and He will fill the hungry souls."

**21** While Jeremiah was saying these things about the Son of God, that He is coming into the world, the people were angry and said, "These are the words spoken by Isaiah son of Amoz all over again, who said, 'I saw God and the Son of God.'

**22** Come, then: let us not kill him by that man's death, but stone him."

**23** Baruch and Abimelech were deeply grieved, because they wanted to hear in full the mysteries he had seen.

**24** Jeremiah said to them, "Be quiet and do not weep, for they will not kill me until I have told you everything I have seen."

**25** He said to them, "Bring me a stone here."

**26** He set it up and said, "Light of the ages, make this stone take on my likeness until I have told Baruch and Abimelech everything I have seen."

**27** Then by God's command the stone took on the likeness of Jeremiah.

**28** They stoned the stone, thinking it was Jeremiah.

**29** Jeremiah handed on all the mysteries he had seen to Baruch and Abimelech, and then he stood among the people, wanting to complete what was appointed for him.

**30** Then the stone cried out, "Foolish sons of Israel, why do you stone me, thinking I am Jeremiah? Jeremiah is standing among you."

**31** When they saw him, they ran at him at once with many stones, and what was appointed for him was completed.

**32** Baruch and Abimelech came and buried him, and they took the stone and set it on his tomb, writing on it: *This is the stone that was Jeremiah's helper.*

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "for nine days. On the tenth"** — the Ge'ez has the feast last seven days, and puts Jeremiah's sacrifice, as the Greek does, on the tenth day.
- **vv3–4 "Holy, holy, holy… the two seraphim"** — the song of the seraphim in Isaiah's vision: *Seraphim stood above Him… One called to another and said: "Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of hosts"* (Isaiah 6:2–3). Isaiah does not give their number.
- **vv7–14 "lying on the ground like a dead man… for three days… his soul came back into his body"** — Jeremiah lies as dead for three days, and the people wait to see *at what hour he will rise*. What he says when he rises is the book's last turn.
- **v14 "the Son of God who wakes us, Jesus the Messiah"** — the Greek names *Iēsoun Christon*, and the Ge'ez also names Jesus, as the Messiah and the Son of God. From here to v20 the book speaks of His coming, His twelve apostles and the Mount of Olives.
- **v15 "another four hundred and seventy-seven years"** — the Ge'ez gives another span, three hundred and three weeks of days.
- **v18 "What is scarlet will become white as wool"** — Isaiah's promise, *though they are red like crimson, they shall become like wool* (Isaiah 1:18), set among other things turned to their opposite.
- **v20 "He will fill the hungry souls"** — the psalm: *He… fills the hungry soul with good things* (Psalm 107:9), where the Greek has the same words, *psychēn peinōsan eneplēsen*.
- **vv21–22 "I saw God and the Son of God… let us not kill him by that man's death"** — Isaiah wrote, *I saw the Lord sitting on a throne* (Isaiah 6:1). The people hear Jeremiah saying what they blame Isaiah for saying, and they mean to kill him differently from the way Isaiah was killed, a death the book does not describe.
- **v32 "This is the stone that was Jeremiah's helper"** — *ho lithos ho boēthos*, the name the Greek of Samuel gives Ebenezer: *Lithos tou boēthou*, "Stone of the Helper," where Samuel says, *Till now the LORD has helped us* (1 Samuel 7:12). Jeremiah's stone helped him finish what he had to tell.
- **Source text** — Greek: Online Critical Pseudepigrapha, CC BY 4.0. Ge'ez: A. Dillmann, *Chrestomathia Aethiopica* (1866), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 4 Baruch 3

**1** When the hour of the night came, as the Lord had told Jeremiah, Jeremiah and Baruch went together to the walls of the city.

**2** There was a sound of trumpets, and angels came out of heaven holding torches in their hands, and they stood on the walls of the city.

**3** When Jeremiah and Baruch saw them, they wept, saying, "Now we know that the word is true."

**4** Jeremiah pleaded with the angels, saying, "I beg you, do not destroy the city yet, until I speak a word to the Lord."

**5** The Lord spoke to the angels, saying, "Do not destroy the city until I speak to My chosen one Jeremiah."

**6** Then Jeremiah spoke, saying, "I beg You, Lord, command me to speak before You."

**7** The Lord said, "Speak, My chosen one Jeremiah."

**8** Jeremiah said, "Now, Lord, we know that You are handing the city over into the hands of its enemies, and they will carry the people away to Babylon.

**9** What do You want me to do with the holy vessels of the service?"

**10** The Lord said to him, "Take them and give them over to the earth, saying, 'Hear, earth, the voice of the One who created you, who formed you in the abundance of the waters, who sealed you with seven seals for seven times; after this you will receive your beauty.

**11** Keep the vessels of the service until the gathering of the beloved.'"

**12** Jeremiah spoke, saying, "I beg You, Lord, show me what I should do for Abimelech the Ethiopian, for he has done many kindnesses to Your servant Jeremiah.

**13** He pulled me up out of the pit of mud. I do not want him to see the destruction of this city and its desolation; rather, have mercy on him, so that he may not grieve."

**14** The Lord said to Jeremiah, "Send him to the vineyard of Agrippa, and in the shadow of the mountain I will shelter him until I bring the people back to the city.

**15** But you, Jeremiah, go with your people to Babylon, and stay with them, bringing them good news until I bring them back to the city.

**16** But leave Baruch here until I speak with him."

**17** When the Lord had said this, He went up from Jeremiah into heaven.

**18** Jeremiah and Baruch went into the sanctuary, took up the vessels of the service and gave them over to the earth, as the Lord had told them.

**19** At once the earth swallowed them.

**20** The two of them sat down and wept.

**21** When morning came, Jeremiah sent Abimelech off, saying, "Take the basket and go to the estate of Agrippa by the mountain road, and bring back a few figs and give them to the sick among the people, for the joy of the Lord is on you, and glory is on your head."

**22** When Jeremiah had said this, he sent him away, and Abimelech went as he had told him.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "the walls of the city… angels came out of heaven holding torches"** — the hour named in 1:11. The fire that takes Jerusalem is brought by angels, and they stand on the walls before any Chaldean does.
- **vv9–11, 18–19 "the holy vessels of the service… the earth swallowed them"** — in the book of Jeremiah the vessels went to Babylon: *They will be carried to Babylon, and there they will stay until the day I attend to them* (Jeremiah 27:22), and the Chaldeans took *what was of gold, in gold, and what was of silver, in silver* (52:19). Here the holy vessels never leave the city. 2 Maccabees, which is not in this canon, tells a like story: Jeremiah hides the tent, the ark and the altar of incense in a cave, and the place is to stay unknown until God gathers His people together (2 Maccabees 2:4–8).
- **v11 "until the gathering of the beloved"** — *syneleusis*, a coming together, a gathering. *The beloved* has no noun with it. In 4:7 the same word stands with one, *the beloved people*, and the gathering is then Israel brought back to the city, as vv14–15 promise twice. Read alone, *the beloved* can also be a person, and *syneleusis* his coming.
- **vv12–13 "Abimelech the Ethiopian… He pulled me up out of the pit of mud"** — the man who drew Jeremiah out of the cistern, where *there was no water in the cistern, only mud, and Jeremiah sank in the mud* (Jeremiah 38:6). Jeremiah names him *Ebed-melech the Cushite* (38:7), and the Greek of Jeremiah *Abdemelech the Ethiopian*. This book calls him *Abimelech*, and the Ge'ez has the same name. God promised him, *I will deliver you on that day* (39:17); this chapter begins to keep it.
- **v14 "the vineyard of Agrippa"** — a name of a later age: Agrippa is the name of the king in Acts (Acts 25:13), six centuries after Jeremiah. In v21 the place is *the estate of Agrippa*, reached by the mountain road.
- **v15 "bringing them good news"** — *euangelizomenos*, the word of Isaiah's messenger, *How beautiful on the mountains are the feet of him who brings good news* (Isaiah 52:7). Jeremiah, sent so often with bad news, is sent to the exiles with good.
- **Source text** — Greek: Online Critical Pseudepigrapha, CC BY 4.0. Ge'ez: A. Dillmann, *Chrestomathia Aethiopica* (1866), public domain. English translation by this project.

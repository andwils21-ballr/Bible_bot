# Lamentations 3

**1** I am the man who has seen affliction  
by the rod of His wrath.

**2** It is I He has led and made to walk  
in darkness and not light.

**3** Against me alone He turns His hand  
again and again, all the day.

**4** He has worn out my flesh and my skin;  
He has broken my bones.

**5** He has built up against me and surrounded me  
with poison and hardship.

**6** He has made me dwell in darkness  
like those long dead.

**7** He has walled me in so that I cannot go out;  
He has made my chains heavy.

**8** Though I cry out and call for help,  
He shuts out my prayer.

**9** He has walled up my ways with hewn stone;  
He has made my paths crooked.

**10** He is to me a bear lying in wait,  
a lion in hiding;

**11** He turned aside my ways and tore me to pieces;  
He has made me desolate.

**12** He bent His bow  
and set me up as a target for the arrow.

**13** He drove into my kidneys  
the arrows of His quiver.

**14** I have become the laughingstock of all my people,  
their mocking song all the day.

**15** He has filled me with bitter herbs;  
He has made me drink my fill of wormwood.

**16** He has broken my teeth on gravel;  
He has made me cower in ashes.

**17** My soul is cast off from peace;  
I have forgotten what good is.

**18** So I said, "My strength has perished,  
and my hope from the LORD."

**19** Remember my affliction and my wandering,  
the wormwood and the poison!

**20** My soul surely remembers  
and is bowed down within me.

**21** This I call to mind;  
therefore I have hope:

**22** It is the LORD's loyal love that we are not finished,  
for His compassions do not fail;

**23** they are new every morning;  
great is Your faithfulness.

**24** "The LORD is my portion," says my soul;  
"therefore I will hope in Him."

**25** The LORD is good to those who wait for Him,  
to the soul who seeks Him.

**26** It is good to hope and wait in silence  
for the salvation of the LORD.

**27** It is good for a man  
to bear the yoke in his youth.

**28** Let him sit alone and be silent,  
when He has laid it on him;

**29** let him put his mouth in the dust —  
perhaps there may be hope;

**30** let him give his cheek to the one who strikes him,  
and be filled with reproach.

**31** For the Lord  
will not cast off forever.

**32** Though He causes grief, He will have compassion  
according to the abundance of His loyal love;

**33** for He does not afflict from His heart  
or grieve the children of men.

**34** To crush underfoot  
all the prisoners of the earth,

**35** to turn aside a man's right  
in the presence of the Most High,

**36** to wrong a man in his cause —  
the Lord does not approve.

**37** Who has spoken and it came to be,  
unless the Lord has commanded it?

**38** Is it not from the mouth of the Most High  
that evil and good go forth?

**39** Why should a living man complain,  
a man, about his sins?

**40** Let us search out and examine our ways,  
and return to the LORD.

**41** Let us lift up our hearts with our hands  
to God in heaven:

**42** "We have transgressed and rebelled,  
and You have not forgiven.

**43** You have wrapped Yourself in anger and pursued us;  
You have killed without pity.

**44** You have wrapped Yourself in a cloud,  
so that no prayer can pass through.

**45** You have made us scum and refuse  
among the peoples.

**46** All our enemies  
have opened their mouths against us;

**47** terror and the pit have come upon us,  
devastation and ruin."

**48** My eye runs down with streams of water  
because of the ruin of the daughter of my people.

**49** My eye flows and does not cease,  
without respite,

**50** until the LORD looks down  
and sees from heaven;

**51** my eye brings grief to my soul  
because of all the daughters of my city.

**52** Those who are my enemies without cause  
have hunted me down like a bird;

**53** they have ended my life in the pit  
and thrown stones on me;

**54** water flowed over my head;  
I said, "I am cut off."

**55** I called on Your name, LORD,  
from the lowest pit;

**56** You heard my voice: "Do not close Your ear  
to my sighing, to my cry for help!"

**57** You came near on the day I called to You;  
You said, "Do not fear!"

**58** You have pleaded my cause, Lord;  
You have redeemed my life.

**59** You have seen, LORD, the wrong done to me;  
judge my case.

**60** You have seen all their vengeance,  
all their plans against me.

**61** You have heard their reproach, LORD,  
all their plans against me,

**62** the lips of my assailants and their muttering  
against me all the day.

**63** Look at their sitting and their rising;  
I am their mocking song.

**64** You will repay them, LORD,  
according to the work of their hands.

**65** You will give them a veiled heart;  
Your curse be on them!

**66** You will pursue them in anger and destroy them  
from under the heavens of the LORD.

## Notes

- **vv1–66 "I am the man who has seen affliction"** — the alphabet again, but each letter now begins three verses in a row: three *aleph*, three *bet*, and so on, sixty-six verses. The speaker is one man, *ha-gever*, "the man," and the chapter moves from his own suffering (vv1–20) to hope (vv21–39), to the people's prayer (vv40–47) and back to him (vv48–66).
- **vv7–15 "He has walled me in… He has walled up my ways… He set me up as a target… wormwood"** — the words of Job's complaints, *He has walled up my way, so that I cannot pass* (Job 19:8) and *He set me up as His target* (Job 16:12), and of Jeremiah's sentence on the people, *I am about to feed this people with wormwood* (Jeremiah 9:15). *He has made me dwell in darkness like those long dead* (v6) is word for word in Psalm 143:3.
- **vv19–24 "Remember my affliction and my wandering… This I call to mind; therefore I have hope… The LORD is my portion"** — *affliction and wandering* are the words of Jerusalem's memory in 1:7; here, remembering them, the man turns. *The LORD is my portion* is the psalm's word (Psalm 119:57) and the Levites' inheritance, *I am your share and your inheritance* (Numbers 18:20). The Greek has nothing of vv22–24.
- **v22 "It is the LORD's loyal love that we are not finished"** — *ki lo tamnu*, "that we are not finished," as the Hebrew is pointed; the same letters read *tammu* would make it *The LORD's loyal love does not cease*, matching the next line, *His compassions do not fail*.
- **v30 "let him give his cheek to the one who strikes him"** — the servant of Isaiah, *I gave… my cheeks to those who pull out the beard* (Isaiah 50:6), and Jesus' word, *If anyone strikes you on the right cheek, turn to him the other also* (Matthew 5:39).
- **v36 "to wrong a man in his cause — the Lord does not approve"** — *Adonai lo ra'ah*, "the Lord does not see," which can be a statement, *does not approve* (he does not look on it with favor), or a question, *Does the Lord not see?*
- **v38 "Is it not from the mouth of the Most High that evil and good go forth?"** — Job's answer to his wife, *Shall we receive good from God, and not receive evil?* (Job 2:10), and the LORD's word in Isaiah, *I make peace and create disaster* (Isaiah 45:7).
- **vv43–44, 8 "You have wrapped Yourself in a cloud, so that no prayer can pass through"** — the cloud of 2:1, now a wall against prayer, as at v8, *He shuts out my prayer*.
- **vv52–58 "they have ended my life in the pit and thrown stones on me… I called on Your name, LORD, from the lowest pit… You said, 'Do not fear!'"** — *bor*, the word of Jeremiah's cistern (Jeremiah 38:6), though there *There was no water in the cistern, only mud*. *The lowest pit* is the psalm's, *You have put me in the lowest pit* (Psalm 88:6), and *Do not fear* is what Jeremiah heard at his call, *Do not be afraid of them* (Jeremiah 1:8). *You have redeemed my life*: *ga'al*, the redeemer's verb (Jeremiah 50:34).

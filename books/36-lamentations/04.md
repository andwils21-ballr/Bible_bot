# Lamentations 4

**1** How the gold has grown dim,  
the fine gold changed!  
The holy stones lie scattered  
at the head of every street.

**2** The precious sons of Zion,  
worth their weight in fine gold,  
how they are counted as clay jars,  
the work of a potter's hands!

**3** Even jackals offer the breast  
and nurse their young,  
but the daughter of my people has become cruel,  
like the ostriches in the wilderness.

**4** The tongue of the nursing infant  
sticks to the roof of its mouth for thirst;  
the children beg for bread,  
but no one breaks it for them.

**5** Those who ate delicacies  
lie desolate in the streets;  
those brought up in scarlet  
cling to heaps of refuse.

**6** The iniquity of the daughter of my people  
is greater than the sin of Sodom,  
which was overthrown in a moment,  
and no hands were laid on her.

**7** Her consecrated ones were purer than snow,  
whiter than milk;  
their bodies were more ruddy than coral,  
their form like sapphire.

**8** Now their faces are blacker than soot;  
they are not recognized in the streets;  
their skin has shriveled on their bones;  
it has become dry as wood.

**9** Those slain by the sword were better off  
than those slain by hunger,  
who waste away, pierced through  
for lack of the fruits of the field.

**10** The hands of compassionate women  
have boiled their own children;  
they became their food  
in the ruin of the daughter of my people.

**11** The LORD has spent His wrath;  
He has poured out His fierce anger;  
He has kindled a fire in Zion  
that has devoured its foundations.

**12** The kings of the earth did not believe,  
nor any of the inhabitants of the world,  
that foe or enemy could enter  
the gates of Jerusalem.

**13** It was for the sins of her prophets  
and the iniquities of her priests,  
who shed in her midst  
the blood of the righteous.

**14** They wandered blind through the streets,  
so defiled with blood  
that no one was able  
to touch their garments.

**15** "Away! Unclean!" people cried at them.  
"Away! Away! Do not touch!"  
So they fled and wandered;  
it was said among the nations,  
"They will stay here as guests no longer."

**16** The face of the LORD has scattered them;  
He will look on them no more;  
they showed no regard for the priests,  
no favor to the elders.

**17** Our eyes still fail,  
looking in vain for help;  
from our watchtower we watched  
for a nation that could not save.

**18** They hunted our steps,  
so that we could not walk in our streets;  
our end drew near; our days were fulfilled,  
for our end had come.

**19** Our pursuers were swifter  
than the eagles of the heavens;  
they chased us on the mountains;  
they lay in wait for us in the wilderness.

**20** The breath of our nostrils, the LORD's anointed,  
was caught in their pits,  
he of whom we said, "Under his shadow we will live among the nations."

**21** Rejoice and be glad, daughter of Edom,  
you who live in the land of Uz!  
To you also the cup will pass;  
you will be drunk and strip yourself bare.

**22** Your iniquity is ended, daughter of Zion;  
He will not carry you into exile again;  
He will punish your iniquity, daughter of Edom;  
He will uncover your sins.

## Notes

- **v7 "Her consecrated ones were purer than snow"** — *nezireha*, "her ones set apart": the word of the Nazirite's vow, and of Joseph, *the one set apart from his brothers* (Genesis 49:26). It can mean her princes or her Nazirites; the Greek has *her Nazirites*.
- **v20 "The breath of our nostrils, the LORD's anointed, was caught in their pits"** — Zedekiah, caught in the plains of Jericho as he fled (Jeremiah 39:5; 52:8). *Under his shadow we will live among the nations* was the hope that went with him.

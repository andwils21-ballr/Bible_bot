# Lamentations 1

**1** How lonely sits the city  
that was full of people!  
She has become like a widow,  
she who was great among the nations;  
the princess among the provinces  
has been put to forced labor.

**2** She weeps bitterly in the night,  
her tears on her cheeks;  
among all her lovers  
she has no one to comfort her;  
all her friends have betrayed her;  
they have become her enemies.

**3** Judah has gone into exile  
after affliction and hard servitude;  
she lives among the nations;  
she finds no resting place;  
all her pursuers have overtaken her  
in the narrow places.

**4** The roads to Zion mourn,  
for no one comes to the appointed feasts;  
all her gates are desolate;  
her priests groan;  
her virgins are grieving,  
and she herself is bitter.

**5** Her foes have become the head;  
her enemies are at ease,  
for the LORD has afflicted her  
for the multitude of her transgressions;  
her little children have gone into captivity  
before the foe.

**6** From the daughter of Zion  
all her splendor has departed.  
Her princes have become like stags  
that find no pasture;  
they have gone on without strength  
before the pursuer.

**7** Jerusalem remembers,  
in the days of her affliction and her wandering,  
all the precious things  
that were hers from days of old.  
When her people fell into the hand of the foe,  
and there was no one to help her,  
the foes saw her  
and laughed at her downfall.

**8** Jerusalem has sinned greatly;  
therefore she has become a thing unclean;  
all who honored her despise her,  
for they have seen her nakedness;  
she herself groans  
and turns away.

**9** Her uncleanness was in her skirts;  
she did not remember her end;  
she has come down astonishingly,  
with no one to comfort her.  
"See, LORD, my affliction,  
for the enemy has made himself great!"

**10** The foe has spread out his hand  
over all her precious things;  
for she has seen nations  
enter her sanctuary,  
those of whom You commanded  
that they should not enter Your assembly.

**11** All her people groan  
as they search for bread;  
they give their precious things for food  
to bring back their life.  
"See, LORD, and look,  
for I have become worthless."

**12** Is it nothing to you, all you who pass by?  
Look and see  
if there is any pain like my pain,  
which was dealt to me,  
which the LORD inflicted  
on the day of His fierce anger.

**13** From on high He sent fire  
into my bones, and it overcame them;  
He spread a net for my feet;  
He turned me back;  
He has made me desolate,  
faint all the day long.

**14** The yoke of my transgressions is bound fast;  
by His hand they are knotted together;  
they have come up on my neck;  
He has made my strength fail;  
the Lord has given me into the hands  
of those I cannot stand against.

**15** The Lord has thrown aside  
all my mighty men in my midst;  
He has called an appointed time against me  
to crush my young men;  
the Lord has trodden as in a winepress  
the virgin daughter of Judah.

**16** For these things I weep;  
my eye, my eye runs down with water;  
for a comforter is far from me,  
one to bring back my life;  
my children are desolate,  
for the enemy has prevailed.

**17** Zion spreads out her hands,  
but there is no one to comfort her;  
the LORD has commanded against Jacob  
that his neighbors should be his foes;  
Jerusalem has become  
an unclean thing among them.

**18** The LORD is righteous,  
for I have rebelled against His command.  
Hear now, all you peoples,  
and see my pain;  
my virgins and my young men  
have gone into captivity.

**19** I called to my lovers,  
but they deceived me;  
my priests and my elders  
perished in the city,  
while they searched for food  
to bring back their life.

**20** See, LORD, how distressed I am;  
my insides churn;  
my heart is turned over within me,  
for I have been very rebellious.  
Outside the sword bereaves;  
in the house it is like death.

**21** They have heard how I groan,  
with no one to comfort me;  
all my enemies have heard of my trouble;  
they rejoice that You have done it.  
Bring the day You have announced,  
and let them be like me.

**22** Let all their evil come before You;  
deal with them  
as You have dealt with me  
for all my transgressions;  
for my groans are many,  
and my heart is faint.

## Notes

- **vv1–22 "How lonely sits the city"** — the twenty-two verses begin with the twenty-two letters of the Hebrew alphabet in order, *aleph* to *tav*, a pattern English cannot keep. *Eikhah*, "How," the first word, is the book's Hebrew name. The Greek opens with a line the Hebrew does not have: *And it came to pass, after Israel was taken captive and Jerusalem laid waste, that Jeremiah sat weeping, and lamented this lament over Jerusalem and said*, and it heads each verse with the name of its letter.
- **v1 "She has become like a widow"** — Jeremiah's last oracle against Babylon promised *Israel and Judah have not been widowed by their God* (Jeremiah 51:5); here the city sits *like* a widow, not one.
- **vv2, 9, 16, 17, 21 "no one to comfort her"** — *ein menachem*, five times in the chapter: Zion's cry goes unanswered. Isaiah's answer begins with the same word, *Comfort, comfort My people* (Isaiah 40:1).
- **vv3, 5 "she finds no resting place… Her foes have become the head"** — the curses of the law come true: *Among those nations you will find no ease, and there will be no resting place for the sole of your foot* (Deuteronomy 28:65), and *He will be the head, and you will be the tail* (28:44). *No resting place* was the dove's before the waters went down (Genesis 8:9).
- **vv4, 15 "no one comes to the appointed feasts… He has called an appointed time against me"** — *mo'ed*, the appointed feast. The feasts of Zion are empty, and the LORD sets His own appointed time: the day He crushes her young men.
- **v8 "she has become a thing unclean"** — *nidah*, spelled with a *yod*, is found only here. In v17 Jerusalem becomes *niddah*, the word for a woman's impurity, and the Greek has that there; here the Greek has *a thing tossed about*, from *nud*, "shake, wander," the verb of the head shaken in scorn (Jeremiah 18:16).
- **v10 "nations enter her sanctuary… that they should not enter Your assembly"** — the law, *No Ammonite or Moabite shall enter the assembly of the LORD* (Deuteronomy 23:3): those not allowed into the assembly have walked into the holy place.
- **v12 "Is it nothing to you, all you who pass by?"** — *lo aleikhem*, "not to you," two words that open the verse without a verb: *Is it nothing to you?* or *May it not come to you*. The Greek has *All you who pass by the way, turn and see*.
- **v18 "The LORD is righteous, for I have rebelled"** — *tsaddiq*, the word of Jeremiah's own complaint, *You are righteous, LORD* (Jeremiah 12:1). The city does not argue: the fault is hers.

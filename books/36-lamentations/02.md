# Lamentations 2

**1** How the Lord in His anger  
has covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud!  
He has thrown down from heaven to earth  
the splendor of Israel;  
He has not remembered His footstool  
in the day of His anger.

**2** The Lord has swallowed up without pity  
all the dwellings of Jacob;  
in His wrath He has torn down  
the strongholds of the daughter of Judah;  
He has brought them down to the ground;  
He has profaned the kingdom and its princes.

**3** In fierce anger He has cut off  
every horn of Israel;  
He has drawn back His right hand  
from before the enemy;  
He has burned in Jacob like a flaming fire  
that devours all around.

**4** He has bent His bow like an enemy,  
with His right hand set like a foe;  
He has killed all who were pleasing to the eye;  
in the tent of the daughter of Zion  
He has poured out His wrath like fire.

**5** The Lord has become like an enemy;  
He has swallowed up Israel;  
He has swallowed up all its palaces,  
destroyed its strongholds,  
and multiplied in the daughter of Judah  
mourning and moaning.

**6** He has stripped His booth like a garden;  
He has destroyed His meeting place;  
the LORD has made Zion forget  
appointed feast and sabbath,  
and in His fierce indignation has spurned  
king and priest.

**7** The Lord has rejected His altar,  
disowned His sanctuary;  
He has given into the hand of the enemy  
the walls of her palaces;  
they raised a shout in the house of the LORD  
as on the day of an appointed feast.

**8** The LORD determined to destroy  
the wall of the daughter of Zion;  
He stretched out the measuring line;  
He did not hold back His hand from swallowing;  
He made rampart and wall lament;  
they languish together.

**9** Her gates have sunk into the ground;  
He has ruined and broken her bars;  
her king and her princes are among the nations;  
the law is no more,  
and her prophets too find no vision  
from the LORD.

**10** The elders of the daughter of Zion  
sit on the ground in silence;  
they have thrown dust on their heads  
and put on sackcloth;  
the virgins of Jerusalem  
have bowed their heads to the ground.

**11** My eyes are spent with tears;  
my insides churn;  
my liver is poured out on the ground  
because of the ruin of the daughter of my people,  
because children and infants faint  
in the streets of the city.

**12** They say to their mothers,  
"Where is the grain and the wine?"  
as they faint like the wounded  
in the streets of the city,  
as their life is poured out  
on their mothers' bosom.

**13** What can I say for you?  
To what can I compare you,  
daughter of Jerusalem?  
What can I liken to you, that I may comfort you,  
virgin daughter of Zion?  
For your ruin is vast as the sea;  
who can heal you?

**14** Your prophets have seen for you  
false and foolish visions;  
they have not exposed your iniquity  
to restore your fortunes,  
but have seen for you burdens  
false and misleading.

**15** All who pass along the way  
clap their hands at you;  
they hiss and shake their heads  
at the daughter of Jerusalem:  
"Is this the city that was called  
the perfection of beauty,  
the joy of all the earth?"

**16** All your enemies  
open their mouths against you;  
they hiss and gnash their teeth;  
they say, "We have swallowed her!  
This is the day we waited for;  
we have found it; we have seen it!"

**17** The LORD has done what He purposed;  
He has carried out His word,  
which He commanded long ago;  
He has torn down without pity;  
He has made the enemy rejoice over you;  
He has raised the horn of your foes.

**18** Their heart cried out to the Lord.  
Wall of the daughter of Zion,  
let tears run down like a torrent  
day and night!  
Give yourself no rest;  
let your eyes not be still!

**19** Arise, cry out in the night,  
at the beginning of the watches!  
Pour out your heart like water  
in the presence of the Lord!  
Lift up your hands to Him  
for the life of your little children,  
who faint from hunger  
at the head of every street.

**20** See, LORD, and look:  
to whom have You done this?  
Should women eat their own fruit,  
the children they have cherished?  
Should priest and prophet be killed  
in the sanctuary of the Lord?

**21** Young and old lie on the ground  
in the streets;  
my virgins and my young men  
have fallen by the sword;  
You have killed them in the day of Your anger;  
You have slaughtered without pity.

**22** You summoned, as for the day of an appointed feast,  
my terrors on every side;  
and on the day of the LORD's anger  
no one escaped or survived;  
those I cherished and reared  
my enemy has destroyed.

## Notes

- **vv1–22** — another alphabet in order, but here *pe* comes before *ayin* (vv16–17), the reverse of chapter 1; chapters 3 and 4 have the same order as this one.
- **v1 "He has covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud… He has not remembered His footstool"** — *ya'iv*, "cloud over," is found only here; the Greek has *darkened*. *His footstool* is the ark, David's *house of rest for the ark of the covenant of the LORD and for the footstool of our God* (1 Chronicles 28:2), and the temple where Israel would *worship at His footstool* (Psalm 132:7).
- **vv2, 5, 8, 16 "The Lord has swallowed up… He has swallowed up Israel… from swallowing… We have swallowed her!"** — *bala*, four times. Three times the Lord swallows; the fourth time the enemies boast of it as their own work.
- **vv6–7, 22 "His meeting place… appointed feast and sabbath… as on the day of an appointed feast… as for the day of an appointed feast"** — *mo'ed*, the word of the tent of meeting and of the feasts, as in 1:4, 15. The shouting in the temple now is the enemy's, and the feast the LORD calls is a day of terror.
- **v8 "He stretched out the measuring line"** — the builder's line used to pull down, as the LORD said through the prophets under Manasseh, *I will stretch over Jerusalem the measuring line of Samaria* (2 Kings 21:13).
- **v11 "my liver is poured out on the ground"** — *kaved*, "liver," an inward part like *me'ai*, "my insides," in the same verse: the grief is in the body. The Greek reads *kavod*, "my glory," from the same letters.
- **v14 "burdens false and misleading"** — *masa'ot*, the prophets' word *burden* for an oracle, which Jeremiah turned against them: *You are the burden* (Jeremiah 23:33).
- **v15 "the perfection of beauty, the joy of all the earth"** — the psalms' names for Zion, *the joy of all the earth* (Psalm 48:2) and *the perfection of beauty* (Psalm 50:2), now in the mouths of those who mock her.
- **v20 "Should women eat their own fruit?"** — the last curse of the siege in the law, *You will eat the fruit of your womb* (Deuteronomy 28:53).
- **v22 "my terrors on every side"** — *megurai missaviv*, Jeremiah's cry *terror on every side* (Jeremiah 6:25; 20:3), now in Zion's own mouth.

# Zechariah 8

## I Have Returned to Zion

**1** The word of the LORD of hosts came, saying,

**2** "This is what the LORD of hosts says: I am exceedingly jealous for Zion, and I am jealous for her with great wrath.

**3** This is what the LORD says: I have returned to Zion and will dwell in the middle of Jerusalem. Jerusalem will be called the City of Truth, and the mountain of the LORD of hosts the Holy Mountain.

**4** This is what the LORD of hosts says: Old men and old women will again sit in the streets of Jerusalem, each with his staff in his hand because of his great age.

**5** The streets of the city will be full of boys and girls playing in its streets.

**6** This is what the LORD of hosts says: If it is too wondrous in the eyes of the remnant of this people in those days, should it also be too wondrous in My eyes? declares the LORD of hosts.

**7** This is what the LORD of hosts says: I am about to save My people from the land of the east and from the land of the setting sun.

**8** I will bring them, and they will dwell in the middle of Jerusalem; they will be My people, and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness.

## Let Your Hands Be Strong

**9** This is what the LORD of hosts says: Let your hands be strong, you who in these days hear these words from the mouth of the prophets who spoke on the day the foundation of the house of the LORD of hosts was laid, that the temple might be built.

**10** For before those days there was no wage for man and no wage for beast, and no peace from the enemy for anyone going out or coming in; I set every man against his neighbor.

**11** But now I will not be to the remnant of this people as in the former days, declares the LORD of hosts.

**12** For there will be a sowing of peace: the vine will give its fruit, the land will give its produce, and the heavens will give their dew; and I will give the remnant of this people all these things as their inheritance.

**13** And it shall come to pass that, as you were a curse among the nations, house of Judah and house of Israel, so I will save you, and you will be a blessing. Do not be afraid; let your hands be strong.

**14** For this is what the LORD of hosts says: As I purposed to do you harm when your fathers provoked Me to wrath, says the LORD of hosts, and I did not relent,

**15** so again in these days I have purposed to do good to Jerusalem and to the house of Judah. Do not be afraid.

**16** These are the things you shall do: Speak the truth each to his neighbor; in your gates judge with truth and with justice that makes peace.

**17** Let none of you plan evil in your heart against his neighbor, and do not love a false oath; for all these are things that I hate, declares the LORD."

## Fasts Will Become Feasts

**18** The word of the LORD of hosts came to me, saying,

**19** "This is what the LORD of hosts says: The fast of the fourth month, the fast of the fifth, the fast of the seventh and the fast of the tenth will become joy and gladness for the house of Judah, and cheerful feasts. So love truth and peace.

**20** This is what the LORD of hosts says: Peoples will yet come, and those who live in many cities.

**21** Those who live in one city will go to another, saying, 'Let us go at once to plead with the LORD and to seek the LORD of hosts. I am going too.'

**22** Many peoples and strong nations will come to seek the LORD of hosts in Jerusalem and to plead with the LORD.

**23** This is what the LORD of hosts says: In those days ten men from all the languages of the nations will take hold, they will take hold of the edge of the garment of a Jewish man, saying, 'Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you.'"

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "I am exceedingly jealous for Zion… I have returned to Zion… the City of Truth"** — the words of 1:14 and 1:16, *I am exceedingly jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion… I have returned to Jerusalem with compassion*, now with *chemah gedolah*, **great wrath**: the heat of His jealousy is for her. *Ir ha-emet*, **the City of Truth**; *emet* is also **faithfulness**, and Isaiah promised Jerusalem would be called *the Faithful City* (Isaiah 1:26). *Emet* comes back in vv8, 16 and 19.
- **vv4–6 "Old men and old women… boys and girls playing… should it also be too wondrous in My eyes?"** — the two ends of life together in the streets, where the city once had neither. *Yippale*, **too wondrous**, is the word the LORD spoke to Abraham, *Is any word too wondrous for the LORD?* (Genesis 18:14), and to Jeremiah, *Is anything too wondrous for Me?* (Jeremiah 32:27).
- **vv7–8 "from the land of the east and from the land of the setting sun… they will be My people, and I will be their God"** — from sunrise to sunset, the whole earth. The covenant words, as in the new covenant of Jeremiah, *I will be their God, and they will be My people* (Jeremiah 31:33), here *be-emet u-vi-tsedaqah*, **in truth and in righteousness**.
- **vv9–12 "Let your hands be strong… the prophets who spoke on the day the foundation… was laid… the heavens will give their dew"** — the prophets of that day are Haggai and Zechariah (Ezra 5:1), and *be strong* is Haggai's word (Haggai 2:4). *Zera ha-shalom*, **a sowing of peace**, word for word **the seed of peace**, is obscure; the Greek has *I will show peace*. The promise undoes the curse in Haggai, *the heavens have held back the dew, and the earth has held back its produce* (Haggai 1:10), in the words of the law's blessing, *the land will give its produce, and the trees of the field will give their fruit* (Leviticus 26:4).
- **v13 "as you were a curse among the nations… so I will save you, and you will be a blessing"** — the word to Abraham, *Be a blessing* (Genesis 12:2), given back after exile. Judah and Israel are named together.
- **vv14–15 "As I purposed to do you harm… and I did not relent, so again… I have purposed to do good"** — *zamam*, **purposed**, as in 1:6, *As the LORD of hosts purposed to do to us*. *Nicham*, **relent**, as when *God relented of the evil He had said He would do* (Jonah 3:10); here He did not relent then, and now He has *again* purposed, *shavti*, word for word **I have turned**, the verb of 1:3, *Return to Me… and I will return to you*.
- **vv16–17 "Speak the truth each to his neighbor… do not love a false oath"** — the curse of the flying scroll was on the one who *swears falsely by My name* (5:4). *Plan evil in your heart*, the words of 7:10.
- **v19 "The fast of the fourth month, the fast of the fifth, the fast of the seventh and the fast of the tenth"** — the answer to Bethel's question (7:3). The four fasts mourn the fall of Jerusalem: in the tenth month the siege began (2 Kings 25:1), in the fourth the city was breached (Jeremiah 39:2), in the fifth the temple burned (2 Kings 25:8), and in the seventh Gedaliah was killed (2 Kings 25:25).
- **vv20–23 "Let us go at once to plead with the LORD… ten men… will take hold of the edge of the garment of a Jewish man"** — *nelkhah halokh*, word for word **let us go, going**: at once. The pleading of Bethel (7:2) is now the nations'. *Many peoples and strong nations*, as in Micah's *strong nations far away* (Micah 4:3). *Kenaf*, **the edge**, is the corner of a garment, where the law put the tassels, *make tassels on the corners of their garments* (Numbers 15:38). *Ish yehudi*, **a Jewish man**, as Mordecai is first named (Esther 2:5). *Elohim immakhem*, **God is with you**, the promise of Haggai 1:13, *I am with you*.

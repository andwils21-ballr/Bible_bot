# Zechariah 1

## Return to Me

**1** In the eighth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, the prophet, saying,

**2** "The LORD was very angry with your fathers.

**3** So say to them: This is what the LORD of hosts says: Return to Me, declares the LORD of hosts, and I will return to you, says the LORD of hosts.

**4** Do not be like your fathers, to whom the former prophets called out, saying, 'This is what the LORD of hosts says: Turn, please, from your evil ways and your evil deeds'; but they did not listen or pay attention to Me, declares the LORD.

**5** Your fathers — where are they? The prophets — do they live forever?

**6** But My words and My statutes, which I commanded My servants the prophets, did they not overtake your fathers? Then they turned and said, 'As the LORD of hosts purposed to do to us, according to our ways and our deeds, so He has done with us.'"

## The Man Among the Myrtles

**7** On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, which is the month of Shebat, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, the prophet, saying:

**8** I saw in the night a man riding on a red horse, and he was standing among the myrtle trees in the hollow; and behind him were red, sorrel and white horses.

**9** I said, "What are these, my lord?" The messenger who talked with me said to me, "I will show you what these are."

**10** The man standing among the myrtle trees answered and said, "These are the ones the LORD has sent to walk about over the earth."

**11** They answered the messenger of the LORD who was standing among the myrtle trees and said, "We have walked about over the earth, and the whole earth sits still and at rest."

**12** Then the messenger of the LORD answered and said, "LORD of hosts, how long will You have no compassion on Jerusalem and on the cities of Judah, with which You have been indignant these seventy years?"

**13** The LORD answered the messenger who talked with me with good words, comforting words.

**14** The messenger who talked with me said to me, "Call out: This is what the LORD of hosts says: I am exceedingly jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion,

**15** and I am very angry with the nations that are at ease; for I was angry only a little, and they helped the evil along.

**16** Therefore this is what the LORD says: I have returned to Jerusalem with compassion; My house will be built in it, declares the LORD of hosts, and a measuring line will be stretched out over Jerusalem.

**17** Call out again: This is what the LORD of hosts says: My cities will again overflow with good; the LORD will again comfort Zion and will again choose Jerusalem."

## Four Horns and Four Craftsmen

**18** I lifted up my eyes and saw four horns.

**19** I said to the messenger who talked with me, "What are these?" He said to me, "These are the horns that scattered Judah, Israel and Jerusalem."

**20** Then the LORD showed me four craftsmen.

**21** I said, "What are these coming to do?" He said, "These are the horns that scattered Judah, so that no one could lift up his head; and these have come to terrify them, to throw down the horns of the nations who lifted up a horn against the land of Judah to scatter it."

## Notes

- **v1 "In the eighth month, in the second year of Darius… Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo"** — two months after Haggai's first word (Haggai 1:1). Ezra names the two prophets together, *Haggai the prophet and Zechariah son of Iddo* (Ezra 5:1), and a *Zechariah* heads the house of Iddo among the priests (Nehemiah 12:16). Jesus names *Zechariah son of Berechiah, whom you murdered between the sanctuary and the altar* (Matthew 23:35); the Zechariah killed *in the court of the house of the LORD* in Chronicles is *son of Jehoiada* (2 Chronicles 24:20–21). *Zekharyah*, **Zechariah**, has the letters of *zakhar*, **remember**, with the LORD's name.
- **vv2–3 "The LORD was very angry with your fathers… Return to Me… and I will return to you"** — *qatsaf… qetsef*, word for word **was angry with anger**. *Shuvu elai… ve-ashuv aleikhem*, **return to Me… and I will return to you**, one verb both ways: the people's turning and the LORD's. The book's call is answered in v16, *I have returned to Jerusalem*. *Declares* and *says the LORD of hosts* three times in one verse.
- **vv4–6 "the former prophets… Turn, please, from your evil ways… did they not overtake your fathers?"** — *the former prophets* called in these words, *Turn now, each of you, from his evil way and from the evil of your deeds* (Jeremiah 25:5). The prophets died and the fathers died, but the words did not: *hissigu*, **overtake**, is the verb of the law's curses, *all these curses will come on you and overtake you* (Deuteronomy 28:15). *Zamam*, **purposed**, is the verb of *The LORD has done what He purposed* (Lamentations 2:17).
- **vv7–8 "the month of Shebat… a man riding on a red horse… among the myrtle trees in the hollow"** — *Shebat* is named only here. *Hadassim*, **myrtle trees**, as in *instead of the brier the myrtle will come up* (Isaiah 55:13); the Greek has *the shaded mountains*, *harim*, a word of similar sound. *Ba-metsulah*, **in the hollow**, is the word of the *depths* of the sea (Jonah 2:3; Micah 7:19). *Seruqqim*, **sorrel**, a reddish brown; the Greek adds *dappled*.
- **vv9–11 "my lord… The messenger who talked with me… We have walked about over the earth"** — *adoni*, **my lord**, is the address to a superior, here the messenger. *Ha-mal'akh ha-dover bi*, **the messenger who talked with me**, guides the visions that follow. The riders patrol as Satan did, *From roaming over the earth and from walking about on it* (Job 1:7). *Yoshevet ve-shoqatet*, **sits still and at rest**, as in *The whole earth is at rest and quiet* (Isaiah 14:7): the nations are at peace.
- **vv12–13 "how long will You have no compassion… these seventy years?… good words, comforting words"** — *ad matai*, **how long**, Habakkuk's cry (Habakkuk 2:6), now from the messenger of the LORD himself, who pleads with the LORD of hosts. *Seventy years* is Jeremiah's number, *When seventy years are completed for Babylon, I will attend to you* (Jeremiah 29:10). *Devarim nichumim*, **comforting words**, from the verb of *Comfort, comfort My people* (Isaiah 40:1).
- **vv14–16 "I am exceedingly jealous for Jerusalem… the nations that are at ease… a measuring line will be stretched out"** — *qinneti… qin'ah gedolah*, word for word **I am jealous with a great jealousy**, as in *the LORD was jealous for His land* (Joel 2:18). *Ha-sha'ananim*, **at ease**, is Amos's word, *Woe to those at ease in Zion* (Amos 6:1), now said of the nations. *Azru le-ra'ah*, **helped the evil along**: the LORD was angry *a little*, and the nations went further. The measuring line was once stretched out to destroy, *He stretched out the measuring line* (Lamentations 2:8); now it is stretched out to build, as in *The measuring line will go out farther* (Jeremiah 31:39).
- **vv18–21 "four horns… four craftsmen… to throw down the horns of the nations"** — a horn is strength, as in *All the horns of the wicked I will cut off* (Psalm 75:10), and *He has raised the horn of your foes* (Lamentations 2:17); what the four stand for, the vision does not say. *Charashim*, **craftsmen**, are smiths and workers in wood and stone, as in *I have created the smith who blows the fire of coals* (Isaiah 54:16). *Le-hacharid*, **to terrify**, is the verb of *no one shall make them afraid* (Micah 4:4). In the Hebrew text vv18–21 are 2:1–4; the English numbering is followed, and the Greek follows it too.

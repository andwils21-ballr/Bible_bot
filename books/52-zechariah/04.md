# Zechariah 4

## The Lampstand and the Two Olive Trees

**1** The messenger who talked with me came back and roused me, like a man who is roused from his sleep.

**2** He said to me, "What do you see?" I said, "I see a lampstand all of gold, with a bowl on top of it and its seven lamps on it, and seven and seven pipes for the lamps that are on top of it;

**3** and there are two olive trees by it, one on the right of the bowl and one on its left."

**4** I answered and said to the messenger who talked with me, "What are these, my lord?"

**5** The messenger who talked with me answered and said to me, "Do you not know what these are?" I said, "No, my lord."

## Not by Might

**6** Then he answered and said to me, "This is the word of the LORD to Zerubbabel: Not by might, nor by power, but by My Spirit, says the LORD of hosts.

**7** What are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel you will become a plain. He will bring out the top stone with shouts of 'Grace, grace to it!'"

**8** Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**9** "The hands of Zerubbabel have laid the foundation of this house, and his hands will finish it. Then you will know that the LORD of hosts has sent me to you.

**10** For who has despised the day of small things? They will rejoice and see the tin stone in the hand of Zerubbabel. These seven are the eyes of the LORD, which run to and fro through the whole earth."

## The Two Sons of Oil

**11** I answered and said to him, "What are these two olive trees on the right of the lampstand and on its left?"

**12** A second time I answered and said to him, "What are these two branches of the olive trees, beside the two golden pipes that pour out the golden oil from them?"

**13** He said to me, "Do you not know what these are?" I said, "No, my lord."

**14** He said, "These are the two sons of oil who stand by the Lord of all the earth."

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "roused me, like a man who is roused from his sleep… a lampstand all of gold… two olive trees"** — *va-ye'ireni*, **roused me**, the verb of 2:13, *He has roused Himself*. The lampstand is the tabernacle's, *You shall make a lampstand of pure gold… its seven lamps* (Exodus 25:31, 37), here with a *gullah*, **bowl**, as in *the golden bowl* (Ecclesiastes 12:6), on top. *Shiv'ah ve-shiv'ah mutsaqot*, **seven and seven pipes**, is uncertain: seven pipes for each lamp, or seven in all on each side. Written *va-yomer*, **he said**; read *va-omar*, **I said**, as the verse continues *I see*.
- **vv4–5 "What are these, my lord?… Do you not know what these are?"** — the question and answer come twice (vv4–5, 12–13), and the answer to the trees waits until v14.
- **v6 "Not by might, nor by power, but by My Spirit"** — *chayil*, **might**, is the word for an army (Joel 2:11, *His army*) and for wealth, and *koach*, **power**, for strength of body. *Be-ruchi*, **by My Spirit**, the Spirit of the LORD, as in *My Spirit stands among you* (Haggai 2:5). The word comes into the vision of the lampstand without a break, and the vision resumes at v11.
- **vv7–9 "What are you, great mountain?… a plain… the top stone… The hands of Zerubbabel have laid the foundation"** — *le-mishor*, **a plain**, as in Isaiah's road home, *the rough places a plain* (Isaiah 40:4). *Ha-even ha-roshah*, **the top stone**, the last or first stone of the building; the Greek has *the stone of inheritance*. *Teshu'ot chen chen lah*, **shouts of "Grace, grace to it!"**, as the people *shouted with a great shout* when the foundation was laid (Ezra 3:11). The promise was kept: *This house was finished on the third day of the month of Adar, in the sixth year of the reign of Darius the king* (Ezra 6:15).
- **v10 "who has despised the day of small things?… the tin stone… the eyes of the LORD, which run to and fro through the whole earth"** — the small day is the one the old men wept over (Haggai 2:3). *Ha-even ha-bedil*, **the tin stone**: *bedil* is **tin**, as in *the tin and the lead* (Numbers 31:22), perhaps a weight on a builder's line; the Greek has *the tin stone*. *Shiv'ah elleh einei YHWH*, **these seven are the eyes of the LORD**, explains the seven eyes on the stone (3:9), and *meshotetim be-khol ha-arets*, **run to and fro through the whole earth**, are the seer's words to Asa, *the eyes of the LORD run to and fro through the whole earth* (2 Chronicles 16:9).
- **vv11–12 "these two branches of the olive trees… the two golden pipes that pour out the golden oil"** — *shibbalei*, **branches**, is the word of an ear of grain, *Shibboleth* (Judges 12:6), here the clusters or spikes of the olive. *Tsanterot*, **pipes**, is found only here. *Ha-zahav*, word for word **the gold**: the oil is called gold for its color.
- **v14 "the two sons of oil who stand by the Lord of all the earth"** — *benei ha-yitshar*, word for word **sons of the fresh oil**, the *yitshar* of *the grain, the new wine and the oil* (Haggai 1:11). *Son of* names what a thing is full of: the two are those set apart with oil, and the chapter has just named the governor (vv6–10) and the high priest (3:1–10). *Adon kol ha-arets*, **the Lord of all the earth**, as in *the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth* (Joshua 3:11) and Micah 4:13.

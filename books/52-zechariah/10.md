# Zechariah 10

## The Shepherds and the Flock

**1** Ask the LORD for rain in the season of the late rain. The LORD makes the storm clouds, and He will give them showers of rain, to each one grass in the field.

**2** For the household gods speak emptiness, and the diviners see lies; they tell false dreams and give empty comfort. So the people wander like sheep; they are afflicted, for there is no shepherd.

**3** My anger burns against the shepherds, and I will punish the he-goats; for the LORD of hosts has cared for His flock, the house of Judah, and will make them like His majestic horse in battle.

**4** From him will come the cornerstone, from him the tent peg, from him the battle bow, from him every ruler, all together.

**5** They will be like warriors in battle, trampling the enemy in the mud of the streets; they will fight, for the LORD is with them, and the riders on horses will be put to shame.

## I Will Bring Them Back

**6** I will strengthen the house of Judah and save the house of Joseph. I will bring them back, for I have compassion on them, and they will be as though I had not rejected them; for I am the LORD their God, and I will answer them.

**7** Ephraim will be like a warrior, and their hearts will be glad as with wine; their children will see it and be glad, and their hearts will rejoice in the LORD.

**8** I will whistle for them and gather them, for I have redeemed them; they will be as many as they were before.

**9** Though I sow them among the peoples, in far countries they will remember Me; they will live, with their children, and return.

**10** I will bring them back from the land of Egypt and gather them from Assyria; I will bring them to the land of Gilead and to Lebanon, until there is no room for them.

**11** He will pass through the sea of distress and strike the waves in the sea, and all the depths of the Nile will dry up. The pride of Assyria will be brought down, and the scepter of Egypt will depart.

**12** I will strengthen them in the LORD, and they will walk in His name, declares the LORD.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "rain in the season of the late rain… the household gods speak emptiness… for there is no shepherd"** — *malqosh*, **the late rain**, of the spring, as in *the early rain and the late rain* (Deuteronomy 11:14). Jeremiah asks the same, *Are there any among the emptinesses of the nations who bring rain?* (Jeremiah 14:22). *Ha-terafim*, **the household gods**, as Rachel *stole the household gods* (Genesis 31:19). *Ki ein ro'eh*, **for there is no shepherd**, as Micaiah saw Israel *like sheep that have no shepherd* (1 Kings 22:17).
- **v3 "I will punish the he-goats; for the LORD of hosts has cared for His flock"** — *efqod… paqad*, **punish… cared for**, one verb both ways: the LORD visits the leaders to punish and the flock to care for it. *Ha-attudim*, **the he-goats**, the leaders who go *in front of the flock* (Jeremiah 50:8). The sheep are turned into *His majestic horse in battle*.
- **v4 "From him will come the cornerstone, from him the tent peg, from him the battle bow, from him every ruler"** — *pinnah*, **cornerstone**, as in *a precious cornerstone* (Isaiah 28:16); *yated*, **tent peg**, the word of *I will drive him like a peg into a firm place* (Isaiah 22:23). *Noges*, **ruler**, is the *oppressor* of 9:8: the same word, now from Judah's side. *From him*: from Judah, or from the LORD.
- **v6 "I will bring them back, for I have compassion on them… I will answer them"** — *ve-hoshevotim* has the letters of both *I will bring them back* and *I will settle them*; the Greek has *I will settle them*. *The house of Joseph* is the north, beside Judah, as in Ezekiel's two sticks joined into one, *the stick of Joseph, which is in the hand of Ephraim* (Ezekiel 37:19).
- **vv8–9 "I will whistle for them… Though I sow them among the peoples"** — *eshreqah*, **I will whistle**, as the LORD would *whistle* for the nations to come against Israel (Isaiah 5:26), now for His own people to come home. *Ve-ezra'em*, **I will sow them**: scattering as sowing, from which a harvest comes, as in *I will sow her for Myself in the land* (Hosea 2:23).
- **vv10–11 "from the land of Egypt… from Assyria… He will pass through the sea of distress"** — the first exodus and the second together. *He* is the LORD, passing through the sea as when He *made the sea into dry ground* (Exodus 14:21). *Tsarah*, **distress**; the Greek reads *a narrow sea*. *Metsulot*, **the depths**, is the word of the *hollow* in 1:8. *Ye'or*, **the Nile**.

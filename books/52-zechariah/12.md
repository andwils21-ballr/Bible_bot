# Zechariah 12

## Jerusalem a Heavy Stone

**1** The oracle. The word of the LORD concerning Israel. Declares the LORD, who stretches out the heavens, founds the earth, and forms the spirit of man within him:

**2** "I am about to make Jerusalem a cup of reeling for all the peoples around; and it will be against Judah also, in the siege against Jerusalem.

**3** And it shall come to pass on that day that I will make Jerusalem a heavy stone for all the peoples; all who lift it will be badly gashed, and all the nations of the earth will gather against her.

**4** On that day, declares the LORD, I will strike every horse with panic and its rider with madness. Over the house of Judah I will open My eyes, but every horse of the peoples I will strike with blindness.

**5** Then the clan chiefs of Judah will say in their hearts, 'The inhabitants of Jerusalem are my strength, in the LORD of hosts, their God.'

**6** On that day I will make the clan chiefs of Judah like a pot of fire among wood, like a torch of fire among sheaves; they will devour all the peoples around, on the right and on the left, and Jerusalem will again be inhabited in her own place, in Jerusalem.

**7** The LORD will save the tents of Judah first, so that the glory of the house of David and the glory of the inhabitants of Jerusalem will not be greater than Judah's.

**8** On that day the LORD will shield the inhabitants of Jerusalem; the one who stumbles among them on that day will be like David, and the house of David will be like God, like the messenger of the LORD before them.

## The Mourning for the Pierced One

**9** And it shall come to pass on that day that I will seek to utterly destroy all the nations that come against Jerusalem.

**10** I will pour out on the house of David and on the inhabitants of Jerusalem a spirit of grace and of pleading; they will look to Me, whom they have pierced, and they will mourn for him, as one mourns for an only child, and weep bitterly over him, as one weeps over a firstborn.

**11** On that day the mourning in Jerusalem will be as great as the mourning for Hadad-rimmon in the plain of Megiddo.

**12** The land will mourn, family by family, each by itself: the family of the house of David by itself, and their wives by themselves; the family of the house of Nathan by itself, and their wives by themselves;

**13** the family of the house of Levi by itself, and their wives by themselves; the family of the Shimeites by itself, and their wives by themselves;

**14** all the families that are left, family by family, each by itself, and their wives by themselves.

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "a cup of reeling… a heavy stone for all the peoples… badly gashed"** — *saf ra'al*, **a cup of reeling**, as Jerusalem once drank *the cup of reeling* (Isaiah 51:17); now the nations drink. *Saf* is also a threshold, and the Greek has *shaking doorposts*. *And it will be against Judah also* is obscure: Judah is caught in the siege, or joins it. *Even ma'amasah*, **a heavy stone**, a stone for lifting; *sarot yissaretu*, doubled, **will be badly gashed**. 1 Enoch takes up the picture (1 Enoch 56:7).
- **v10 "a spirit of grace and of pleading; they will look to Me, whom they have pierced, and they will mourn for him"** — *ruach chen*, **a spirit of grace**: *chen* is the *grace* of 4:7. *Ve-hibbitu elai et asher daqaru*, **they will look to Me, whom they have pierced**: *Me* and then *him*, in one verse. *Et asher* may also be **the one whom**, so: **they will look to Me concerning the one whom they have pierced**. The Greek has *because they danced in mockery*. Matthew joins the verse to Daniel 7:13 (Matthew 24:30).

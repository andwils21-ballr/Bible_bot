# Zechariah 14

## The Day of the LORD

**1** A day is coming for the LORD, when the plunder taken from you will be divided among you.

**2** For I will gather all the nations against Jerusalem to battle; the city will be taken, the houses plundered and the women raped. Half the city will go into exile, but the rest of the people will not be cut off from the city.

**3** Then the LORD will go out and fight against those nations, as He fights on a day of battle.

**4** On that day His feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, which lies before Jerusalem on the east; and the Mount of Olives will be split in two from east to west by a very great valley, so that half of the mountain will move toward the north and half toward the south.

**5** You will flee by the valley of My mountains, for the valley of the mountains will reach to Azal; you will flee as you fled from the earthquake in the days of Uzziah king of Judah. Then the LORD my God will come, and all the holy ones with You.

**6** And it shall come to pass on that day that there will be no light, no splendor and no frost.

**7** It will be one day, known to the LORD, neither day nor night; at evening time there will be light.

**8** And it shall come to pass on that day that living water will flow out from Jerusalem, half of it to the eastern sea and half of it to the western sea; it will be so in summer and in winter.

**9** The LORD will be king over all the earth. On that day the LORD will be one, and His name one.

**10** The whole land will be turned into a plain like the Arabah, from Geba to Rimmon south of Jerusalem; but Jerusalem will stand high and remain in her place, from the Gate of Benjamin to the place of the First Gate, to the Corner Gate, and from the Tower of Hananel to the king's winepresses.

**11** People will live in her, and she will never again be doomed to destruction; Jerusalem will live in security.

## The Plague on the Nations

**12** This will be the plague with which the LORD will strike all the peoples who have waged war against Jerusalem: their flesh will rot while they stand on their feet, their eyes will rot in their sockets, and their tongues will rot in their mouths.

**13** And it shall come to pass on that day that a great panic from the LORD will fall among them; each will seize the hand of his neighbor, and his hand will rise against the hand of his neighbor.

**14** Judah too will fight at Jerusalem; and the wealth of all the nations around will be gathered, gold, silver and garments in great abundance.

**15** A plague like this plague will strike the horse, the mule, the camel, the donkey and all the animals that are in those camps.

## Holy to the LORD

**16** And it shall come to pass that everyone who is left of all the nations that came against Jerusalem will go up year after year to worship the King, the LORD of hosts, and to keep the Festival of Booths.

**17** And it shall come to pass that if any of the families of the earth do not go up to Jerusalem to worship the King, the LORD of hosts, there will be no rain on them.

**18** If the family of Egypt does not go up and come, then not on them; there will be the plague with which the LORD strikes the nations that do not go up to keep the Festival of Booths.

**19** This will be the punishment of Egypt, and the punishment of all the nations that do not go up to keep the Festival of Booths.

**20** On that day there will be on the bells of the horses, "Holy to the LORD"; and the pots in the house of the LORD will be like the basins before the altar.

**21** Every pot in Jerusalem and in Judah will be holy to the LORD of hosts; all who sacrifice will come and take of them and cook in them. On that day there will no longer be a Canaanite in the house of the LORD of hosts.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "A day is coming for the LORD… the women raped… Half the city will go into exile"** — *yom ba la-YHWH*, **a day is coming for the LORD**, the day of the LORD of Joel and Zephaniah. Written *tishgalnah*, read *tishshakhavnah*, a softer word for the same act.
- **vv3–5 "His feet will stand on the Mount of Olives… the earthquake in the days of Uzziah… all the holy ones with You"** — *Har ha-zeitim*, **the Mount of Olives**, is named so only here; the LORD stands on it and it splits to make a way of escape. *Gei harai*, **the valley of My mountains**; the Greek reads *ve-nistam*, **will be stopped up**, for *ve-nastem*, **you will flee**: *the valley of My mountains will be stopped up*. *Azal* is unknown; the Greek has *Iasod*. The earthquake is the one Amos dates his words by, *two years before the earthquake* (Amos 1:1). *Kol qedoshim immakh*, **all the holy ones with You**: the prophet turns to speak to the LORD; the Greek has *with him*.
- **vv6–7 "no light, no splendor and no frost… neither day nor night; at evening time there will be light"** — *lo yihyeh or yeqarot ve-qippa'on* is obscure. *Yeqarot*, **splendor**, as the *moon moving in splendor* (Job 31:26), is the light of the heavens. Written *yiqpa'un*, **will congeal**; read *ve-qippa'on*, **and frost**, followed here; the Greek has *cold and frost*. *Yom echad*, **one day**, the words of creation's first day, *day one* (Genesis 1:5), known only to the LORD.
- **vv8–9 "living water will flow out from Jerusalem… the LORD will be one, and His name one"** — *mayim chayyim*, **living water**, running water. Ezekiel saw *water was flowing out from under the threshold of the house toward the east* (Ezekiel 47:1), and Joel *a spring will come out from the house of the LORD* (Joel 3:18). The eastern sea is the Dead Sea, the western the Great Sea. *The LORD will be one* is the creed of Deuteronomy 6:4, *the LORD our God, the LORD is one*, now on the lips of all the earth.
- **vv10–11 "a plain like the Arabah… the Tower of Hananel… she will never again be doomed to destruction"** — the hills around sink and Jerusalem is lifted, as *the mountain of the LORD's house shall be established as the highest of the mountains* (Isaiah 2:2). The rebuilt city runs *from the Tower of Hananel to the Corner Gate* (Jeremiah 31:38). *Cherem*, **doomed to destruction**, the word of Jericho's doom, *devoted to the LORD* (Joshua 6:17), will never fall on Jerusalem again.
- **vv12–15 "their flesh will rot while they stand on their feet… each will seize the hand of his neighbor"** — *hamek*, **rot**, three times, of flesh, eyes and tongue. *Mehumat YHWH*, **a panic from the LORD**, the confusion sent on Israel's enemies, *threw the camp of Egypt into panic* (Exodus 14:24). 1 Enoch takes up the picture of v13 (1 Enoch 56:7). *Bi-Yerushalayim*, **at Jerusalem**, may also be *against Jerusalem*.
- **vv16–19 "to worship the King… to keep the Festival of Booths… there will be no rain on them… then not on them"** — the autumn festival, *the Festival of Booths, seven days to the LORD* (Leviticus 23:34), the time of the first rains. *Ve-lo aleihem*, **then not on them**, is obscure: Egypt waters its land from the Nile, not from rain, so the rain is not the threat there, and the plague is; the Greek has *on them will be the plague*. *Chatta't*, **punishment**, is the word for sin, and for what sin brings.
- **vv20–21 "on the bells of the horses, 'Holy to the LORD'… no longer be a Canaanite"** — *Qodesh la-YHWH*, **Holy to the LORD**, the words on the high priest's gold plate, *engrave on it, with the engravings of a seal: Holy to the LORD* (Exodus 28:36), now on harness bells and kitchen pots. *Kena'ani*, **Canaanite**, is also a trader (as in 11:7); either the unholy or the merchant is gone from the LORD's house.

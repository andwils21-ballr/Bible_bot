# Zechariah 13

## A Fountain Opened

**1** On that day a fountain will be opened for the house of David and for the inhabitants of Jerusalem, for sin and for impurity.

**2** And it shall come to pass on that day, declares the LORD of hosts, that I will cut off the names of the idols from the land, and they will be remembered no more; and I will also remove the prophets and the spirit of uncleanness from the land.

**3** If anyone prophesies again, his father and mother who bore him will say to him, 'You shall not live, for you have spoken lies in the name of the LORD'; and his father and mother who bore him will pierce him through when he prophesies.

**4** And it shall come to pass on that day that the prophets will each be ashamed of his vision when he prophesies; they will not put on a hairy cloak in order to deceive.

**5** He will say, 'I am no prophet; I am a man who works the ground, for a man has owned me since my youth.'

**6** If someone asks him, 'What are these wounds between your hands?' he will say, 'The ones I was struck with in the house of those who love me.'

## Strike the Shepherd

**7** Sword, awake against My shepherd, against the man who is My companion, declares the LORD of hosts. Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered; I will turn My hand against the little ones.

**8** In all the land, declares the LORD, two parts in it will be cut off and die, and the third will be left in it.

**9** I will bring the third part through the fire, refine them as silver is refined, and test them as gold is tested. They will call on My name, and I will answer them; I will say, 'They are My people,' and they will say, 'The LORD is my God.'

## Notes

- **vv4–6 "a hairy cloak… I am no prophet… a man has owned me since my youth… wounds between your hands"** — *adderet se'ar*, **a hairy cloak**, the prophet's garment, as Elijah's *cloak* (2 Kings 2:8). *Lo navi anokhi*, **I am no prophet**, as Amos said, *I was no prophet* (Amos 7:14), but here to hide what he is. *Adam hiqnani*, **a man has owned me**: he says he has been a hired hand or a slave on the land. *Bein yadekha*, **between your hands**, is on the body, the chest or back. The prophets of Baal *gashed themselves… with swords and spears* (1 Kings 18:28). *Me'ahavai*, **those who love me**, is also Hosea's word for the *lovers* Israel went after (Hosea 2:5); the Greek has *my beloved house*.
- **v7 "Sword, awake against My shepherd… Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered"** — *gever amiti*, **the man who is My companion**; *amit* is the word for a fellow or neighbor. Jesus quotes the verse the night He is arrested, *I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered* (Matthew 26:31). *Ha-tso'arim*, **the little ones**, the lambs of the flock. The Greek has *Strike the shepherds, and draw out the sheep*.

# Zechariah 2

## The Man with a Measuring Line

**1** I lifted up my eyes and saw a man with a measuring line in his hand.

**2** I said, "Where are you going?" He said to me, "To measure Jerusalem, to see how great its width is and how great its length."

**3** Then the messenger who talked with me went out, and another messenger went out to meet him,

**4** and said to him, "Run, speak to that young man: Jerusalem will be lived in as open villages, because of the great number of people and animals inside it.

**5** I Myself will be to her, declares the LORD, a wall of fire all around, and I will be the glory inside her."

## Flee from the Land of the North

**6** Up! Up! Flee from the land of the north, declares the LORD; for I have spread you abroad like the four winds of the heavens, declares the LORD.

**7** Up! Escape to Zion, you who live with the daughter of Babylon!

**8** For this is what the LORD of hosts says: After glory He has sent me to the nations that plundered you; for whoever touches you touches the pupil of His eye.

**9** For I am about to shake My hand over them, and they will become plunder for those who served them. Then you will know that the LORD of hosts has sent me.

**10** Sing aloud and rejoice, daughter of Zion, for I am about to come, and I will dwell among you, declares the LORD.

**11** Many nations will join themselves to the LORD on that day and will be My people, and I will dwell among you; and you will know that the LORD of hosts has sent me to you.

**12** The LORD will take Judah as His portion on the holy ground, and will again choose Jerusalem.

**13** Silence, all flesh, before the LORD! For He has roused Himself from His holy dwelling.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "a man with a measuring line in his hand… To measure Jerusalem"** — in the Hebrew text vv1–13 are 2:5–17; the English numbering is followed, and the Greek follows it too. The third vision, after the horsemen (1:8) and the horns (1:18). The measuring line promised in 1:16 is now in a man's hand, as Ezekiel saw a man *with a linen cord and a measuring reed in his hand* (Ezekiel 40:3).
- **vv8–9 "After glory He has sent me… whoever touches you touches the pupil of His eye… I am about to shake My hand over them"** — *achar kavod shelachani*, **after glory He has sent me**, is obscure: after the glory of v5, or for the sake of glory; who *me* is, the prophet or the messenger, the verse does not make plain. *Bavat eino*, **the pupil of His eye**, a word found only here, as the law says of Jacob, *He guarded him as the pupil of His eye* (Deuteronomy 32:10). *Menif*, **shake**, as in *the hand that the LORD of hosts shakes over it* (Isaiah 19:16). The plunderers become plunder for *those who served them*.

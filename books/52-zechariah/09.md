# Zechariah 9

## Against the Neighboring Nations

**1** The oracle. The word of the LORD is against the land of Hadrach, and Damascus is where it comes to rest; for the eye of mankind, and of all the tribes of Israel, is on the LORD;

**2** and on Hamath also, which borders it, and on Tyre and Sidon, though they are very wise.

**3** Tyre has built herself a stronghold, and heaped up silver like dust and gold like the mud of the streets.

**4** The Lord will take possession of her and strike her power into the sea, and she will be consumed by fire.

**5** Ashkelon will see it and be afraid; Gaza too, and will writhe in great pain; Ekron also, for what she looked to is put to shame. The king will perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon will not be inhabited.

**6** A people of mixed birth will live in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines.

**7** I will take away his blood from his mouth, and his loathsome things from between his teeth. He too will be a remnant for our God; he will be like a clan chief in Judah, and Ekron like the Jebusite.

**8** I will encamp at My house as a guard, against those who pass through and return; no oppressor will pass over them again, for now I have seen with My eyes.

## Your King Comes to You

**9** Rejoice greatly, daughter of Zion! Shout, daughter of Jerusalem! Your king comes to you; righteous and saved is he, humble and riding on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of she-asses.

**10** I will cut off the chariot from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem, and the battle bow will be cut off. He will speak peace to the nations; his rule will be from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth.

**11** As for you, because of the blood of your covenant, I have set your prisoners free from the pit that has no water in it.

**12** Return to the stronghold, you prisoners of hope; today also I declare that I will repay you double.

**13** For I have bent Judah as My bow; I have filled it with Ephraim. I will rouse your sons, Zion, against your sons, Greece, and make you like a warrior's sword.

**14** The LORD will appear over them, and His arrow will go out like lightning. The Lord GOD will blow the ram's horn and march in the storms of the south.

**15** The LORD of hosts will shield them; they will devour and trample the sling stones. They will drink and roar as with wine, and be filled like a basin, like the corners of the altar.

**16** On that day the LORD their God will save them, as the flock of His people; for they are the stones of a crown, sparkling over His land.

**17** How great is His goodness, and how great His beauty! Grain will make the young men flourish, and new wine the young women.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "The oracle… for the eye of mankind, and of all the tribes of Israel, is on the LORD"** — *massa*, **oracle**, as in *The oracle concerning Nineveh* (Nahum 1:1); it opens this part of the book and again at 12:1. *Ki la-YHWH ein adam*, word for word **for to the LORD is the eye of man**: either all eyes turn to the LORD, as the text has it, or the LORD has His eye on mankind, as the Greek has it, *the Lord looks upon men*. *Hadrach* is named only here; the Greek has *Sedrach*.
- **vv3–4 "Tyre has built herself a stronghold… silver like dust and gold like the mud of the streets"** — *va-tiven Tsor matsor*, **Tyre has built a stronghold**: *Tsor* and *matsor* sound alike. Her wealth is like Solomon's, who *made silver as common in Jerusalem as stones* (1 Kings 10:27).
- **vv5–7 "Ashkelon… Gaza… Ekron… A people of mixed birth will live in Ashdod… his loathsome things from between his teeth"** — the Philistine cities of Amos 1:6–8 and Zephaniah 2:4. *Mamzer*, **of mixed birth**, is found only here and in Deuteronomy 23:2; the Greek has *foreigners*. *His blood… his loathsome things* are food the law forbids, as it forbids blood, *who eats any blood — I will set My face against the person* (Leviticus 17:10); once they are taken from his mouth, the Philistine becomes *a remnant for our God*. *Alluf*, **clan chief**, the title of Edom's *chiefs* (Genesis 36:15). *Like the Jebusite*: Jerusalem's old people, who *live with the sons of Judah in Jerusalem to this day* (Joshua 15:63).
- **vv9–10 "Your king comes to you; righteous and saved is he, humble and riding on a donkey… from sea to sea"** — Matthew quotes it of Jesus entering Jerusalem, *Your king is coming to you, gentle, and mounted on a donkey* (Matthew 21:5). *Nosha*, **saved**, is passive; the Greek has *saving*. *Ani*, **humble**, is also **poor, afflicted**; the Greek has *gentle*, *praus*. *Ayir ben atonot*, **a colt, the foal of she-asses**, is Judah's blessing, *to the choice vine his donkey's colt* (Genesis 49:11). Chariot and horse are cut off from the king's own cities, as Isaiah warned against those *who rely on horses* (Isaiah 31:1). *From sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth* is the line of Psalm 72:8.
- **vv11–12 "because of the blood of your covenant… the pit that has no water in it… prisoners of hope… double"** — *dam beritekh*, **the blood of your covenant**, as Moses said, *Here is the blood of the covenant* (Exodus 24:8). The waterless pit is Joseph's, *The pit was empty; there was no water in it* (Genesis 37:24). *Asirei ha-tiqvah*, **prisoners of hope**: the Greek has *prisoners of the gathering*. *Mishneh*, **double**, as Jerusalem *has received from the LORD's hand double for all her sins* (Isaiah 40:2).
- **vv13–15 "your sons, Greece… the ram's horn… the storms of the south… trample the sling stones"** — *Yavan*, **Greece**, as in *the king of Greece* (Daniel 8:21). *Teman*, **the south**, also a place, as in *God came from Teman* (Habakkuk 3:3). *Ve-khavshu avnei qela*, **trample the sling stones**, is obscure: they tread down the enemy's stones, or the sling stones are the enemy; the Greek has *they will bury them with sling stones*. *Mizraq*, **a basin**, is one of the altar's bowls.
- **vv16–17 "the stones of a crown, sparkling over His land… How great is His goodness"** — *nezer*, **crown**, the word of the high priest's *holy crown* (Exodus 29:6); *mitnosesot*, **sparkling**, is from *nes*, a banner raised on high. *Tuvo… yofyo*, **His goodness… His beauty**: the Hebrew *his* may be the LORD's or His people's.

# Zechariah 6

## Four Chariots

**1** Again I lifted up my eyes and looked, and saw four chariots coming out from between the two mountains; the mountains were mountains of bronze.

**2** With the first chariot were red horses, with the second chariot black horses,

**3** with the third chariot white horses, and with the fourth chariot dappled horses, strong ones.

**4** I answered and said to the messenger who talked with me, "What are these, my lord?"

**5** The messenger answered and said to me, "These are the four winds of the heavens, going out after presenting themselves before the Lord of all the earth.

**6** The one with the black horses is going out to the land of the north; the white ones went out after them, and the dappled ones went out to the land of the south.

**7** The strong ones went out and sought to go and walk about over the earth." He said, "Go, walk about over the earth." So they walked about over the earth.

**8** Then he called out to me and spoke to me, saying, "See, those that go out to the land of the north have set My Spirit at rest in the land of the north."

## A Crown for Joshua

**9** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**10** "Take from the exiles, from Heldai, from Tobijah and from Jedaiah, who have come from Babylon, and go that same day to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah.

**11** Take silver and gold, make crowns, and set them on the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.

**12** Say to him: This is what the LORD of hosts says: Here is a man whose name is the Branch; he will branch out from his place, and he will build the temple of the LORD.

**13** It is he who will build the temple of the LORD; he will bear majesty, and will sit and rule on his throne. There will be a priest on his throne, and a counsel of peace will be between the two of them.

**14** The crowns will be for Helem, Tobijah and Jedaiah, and for Hen son of Zephaniah, as a memorial in the temple of the LORD.

**15** Those who are far away will come and build in the temple of the LORD; and you will know that the LORD of hosts has sent me to you. This will come to pass if you listen to the voice of the LORD your God."

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "four chariots… mountains of bronze… red… black… white… dappled horses, strong ones"** — the last vision answers the first: horses of colors again (1:8), now with chariots. *Berudim*, **dappled**, is the word of Jacob's flocks, *striped, speckled, and dappled* (Genesis 31:10). *Amutsim*, **strong**, may be a further color; in v7 it stands alone, *the strong ones*. The Greek has *dappled grey* for it.
- **vv4–5 "These are the four winds of the heavens… the Lord of all the earth"** — *ruchot*, **winds**, as in 2:6, *like the four winds of the heavens*, and in Daniel's vision, *the four winds of heaven were stirring up the great sea* (Daniel 7:2); the same word means **spirits**. *Me-hityatsev al*, **after presenting themselves before**, is the verb of the heavenly court in Job, *the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD* (Job 1:6). *The Lord of all the earth*, as in 4:14.
- **vv6–7 "to the land of the north… after them… to the land of the south… walk about over the earth"** — the red horses of v2 are not sent anywhere. *El achareihem*, **after them**, follows the Hebrew; it might also be read **to the west**, behind the one who faces east, but the text does not say so. *Le-hithallekh ba-arets*, **walk about over the earth**, the patrol of 1:10–11.
- **v8 "have set My Spirit at rest in the land of the north"** — *heniḥu et ruchi*, **set My Spirit at rest**. *Ruach* is also temper, as in *their anger against him subsided* (Judges 8:3), and *heniach* is the verb Ezekiel uses with wrath, *I will satisfy My wrath on you* (Ezekiel 16:42). The Greek has *have given rest to My anger*. The land of the north is Babylon (2:6–7).
- **vv10–11 "Heldai… Tobijah… Jedaiah… make crowns, and set them on the head of Joshua"** — *Yeda'yah*, **Jedaiah**, is also the name of a priestly house among the returned exiles, *the sons of Jedaiah, of the house of Jeshua* (Ezra 2:36). *Atarot*, **crowns**, is plural; in v14 the same word takes a singular verb, and Job uses the plural for one crown, *I would bind it on me as a crown* (Job 31:36). The Greek has *crowns* here and *the crown* in v14.
- **vv12–13 "a man whose name is the Branch; he will branch out from his place… There will be a priest on his throne"** — the Branch promised in 3:8, *tsemach* with its verb, *yitsmach*, **he will branch out**. *Hod*, **majesty**, is the *authority* Moses put on Joshua son of Nun (Numbers 27:20). The verse is crowning Joshua the priest, but the Branch builds the temple, as Zerubbabel does (4:9). *Ve-hayah kohen al kis'o* may be **he will be a priest on his throne**, one man, or **there will be a priest on his throne**, a second, as *the two of them* suggests; the Greek has *the priest will be at his right hand*.
- **vv14–15 "Helem… Hen son of Zephaniah, as a memorial… if you listen to the voice of the LORD your God"** — *Helem* is Heldai of v10, and *Hen* is Josiah son of Zephaniah; *chen* means **favor**, and the Greek reads *for the favor of the son of Zephaniah*. *Ve-hayah im shamoa tishme'un be-qol YHWH eloheikhem* are the words of Deuteronomy 28:1, *if you listen, listen to the voice of the LORD your God*, where the blessings follow; here the sentence stops after the *if*.

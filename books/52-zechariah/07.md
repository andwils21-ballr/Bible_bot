# Zechariah 7

## A Question About Fasting

**1** And it came to pass in the fourth year of King Darius that the word of the LORD came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, Kislev.

**2** Bethel had sent Sharezer and Regem-melech and his men to plead with the LORD,

**3** saying to the priests of the house of the LORD of hosts and to the prophets, "Should I weep in the fifth month and keep myself apart, as I have done these many years?"

**4** Then the word of the LORD of hosts came to me, saying,

**5** "Say to all the people of the land and to the priests: When you fasted and mourned in the fifth month and in the seventh these seventy years, was it for Me that you fasted?

**6** When you eat and when you drink, is it not you who eat and you who drink?

**7** Are not these the words that the LORD called out through the former prophets, when Jerusalem was inhabited and at ease, with its cities around it, and the Negev and the lowland were inhabited?"

## Judge with True Justice

**8** The word of the LORD came to Zechariah, saying,

**9** "This is what the LORD of hosts says: Judge with true justice, and show loyal love and compassion each to his brother.

**10** Do not oppress the widow or the fatherless, the guest or the poor; and let none of you plan evil in your heart against his brother."

**11** But they refused to pay attention; they turned a stubborn shoulder and made their ears heavy so as not to hear.

**12** They made their hearts diamond, so as not to hear the instruction and the words that the LORD of hosts had sent by His Spirit through the former prophets. So great wrath came from the LORD of hosts.

**13** "As He called and they would not listen, so they will call and I will not listen," says the LORD of hosts,

**14** "and I scattered them with a whirlwind among all the nations they had not known. The land was left desolate behind them, with no one coming or going; they made the pleasant land a desolation."

## Notes

- **v1 "in the fourth year of King Darius… the ninth month, Kislev"** — two years after the visions (1:7). *Kislev* is named also in Nehemiah 1:1.
- **vv2–3 "Bethel had sent Sharezer and Regem-melech… to plead with the LORD… Should I weep in the fifth month"** — *Beit El Sar-etser ve-Regem melekh* may be read as here, the town of Bethel sending two men, or as one name, **Bethel-sharezer**, who sent Regem-melech; the Greek has *Sarasar and Arbeseer the king*, sent *to Bethel*. *Le-challot et penei YHWH*, **to plead with the LORD**, as *Moses pleaded with the LORD his God* (Exodus 32:11). The fifth month is when the temple was burned (2 Kings 25:8). *Hinnazer*, **keep myself apart**, is the verb of the Nazirite, who vows *to keep himself apart for the LORD* (Numbers 6:2). The town speaks as one person: *I*.
- **vv5–6 "in the fifth month and in the seventh… was it for Me that you fasted?"** — the seventh month is when Gedaliah was killed at Mizpah (2 Kings 25:25). *Ha-tsom tsamtuni ani*, word for word **did you fast Me, Me?**: the LORD's *I* is stressed. Isaiah asks the same, *Is such the fast that I choose* (Isaiah 58:5). The question about fasting is answered in 8:19.
- **vv9–10 "Judge with true justice… loyal love and compassion… the widow or the fatherless, the guest or the poor"** — *chesed*, **loyal love**, with *rachamim*, **compassion**, as in Micah 6:8, *to love loyal love*. The list of the helpless is Jeremiah's, *if you do not oppress the guest, the fatherless and the widow* (Jeremiah 7:6), with *the poor* added.
- **vv11–12 "they turned a stubborn shoulder… made their hearts diamond… by His Spirit through the former prophets"** — *va-yittenu khatef soraret*, **they turned a stubborn shoulder**, as Nehemiah's prayer says, *They turned a stubborn shoulder* (Nehemiah 9:29), the ox that will not take the yoke. *Shamir*, **diamond**, as in *with a point of diamond it is engraved on the tablet of their heart* (Jeremiah 17:1). *Be-rucho*, **by His Spirit**: the Spirit spoke through the prophets. *Ha-torah*, **the instruction**.
- **vv13–14 "As He called and they would not listen, so they will call and I will not listen… I scattered them with a whirlwind"** — the LORD speaks of Himself as *He* and then as *I* in one sentence; the Hebrew has both. Wisdom says the same, *Then they will call on me, but I will not answer* (Proverbs 1:28). *Ve-esa'arem*, **I scattered them with a whirlwind**, from *sa'ar*, a storm. *Erets chemdah*, **the pleasant land**, as in *they despised the pleasant land* (Psalm 106:24).

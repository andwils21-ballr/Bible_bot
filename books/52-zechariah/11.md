# Zechariah 11

## Wail, Cypress

**1** Open your doors, Lebanon, so that fire may devour your cedars!

**2** Wail, cypress, for the cedar has fallen, for the majestic ones are ruined! Wail, oaks of Bashan, for the dense forest has come down!

**3** The sound of the shepherds' wailing, for their glory is ruined! The sound of the young lions' roaring, for the thickets of the Jordan are ruined!

## The Two Staffs

**4** This is what the LORD my God says: Shepherd the flock doomed to slaughter.

**5** Their buyers slaughter them and are not held guilty, and those who sell them say, 'Blessed be the LORD, for I have become rich!' Their own shepherds have no pity on them.

**6** For I will no longer have pity on the inhabitants of the land, declares the LORD. I am about to hand everyone over to his neighbor and to his king; they will crush the land, and I will not deliver them from their hand.

**7** So I shepherded the flock doomed to slaughter, truly the afflicted of the flock. I took two staffs: one I called Favor, and the other I called Union; and I shepherded the flock.

**8** In one month I destroyed the three shepherds. I lost patience with them, and they also detested me.

**9** Then I said, "I will not shepherd you. What is dying, let it die; what is being destroyed, let it be destroyed; and let those that are left devour each other's flesh."

**10** I took my staff Favor and broke it in pieces, to annul my covenant that I had cut with all the peoples.

**11** It was annulled on that day, and so the afflicted of the flock who were watching me knew that it was the word of the LORD.

**12** I said to them, "If it seems good to you, give me my wages; and if not, keep them." So they weighed out my wages, thirty pieces of silver.

**13** The LORD said to me, "Throw it to the potter — the lordly price at which I was priced by them." So I took the thirty pieces of silver and threw it into the house of the LORD, to the potter.

**14** Then I broke my second staff, Union, in pieces, to annul the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.

## The Worthless Shepherd

**15** The LORD said to me, "Take for yourself once more the equipment of a foolish shepherd.

**16** For I am about to raise up a shepherd in the land who will not care for those being destroyed, or seek the young, or heal the broken, or feed the one that stands firm; but he will eat the flesh of the fat ones and tear off their hooves.

**17** Woe to My worthless shepherd, who abandons the flock! A sword on his arm and on his right eye! Let his arm wither, and his right eye go blind."

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "Wail, cypress… the majestic ones are ruined… oaks of Bashan… the thickets of the Jordan"** — the great trees fall, as Isaiah set the LORD's day *against all the cedars of Lebanon… and against all the oaks of Bashan* (Isaiah 2:13), and the shepherds and lions howl as their land is stripped. *Addirim*, **the majestic ones**, and *adderet*, **glory** (v3), are one root; *adderet* is also a prophet's cloak (13:4). *Ge'on ha-Yarden*, **the thickets of the Jordan**, as in Jeremiah 12:5.
- **vv4–6 "the flock doomed to slaughter… 'Blessed be the LORD, for I have become rich!'"** — *tson ha-haregah*, word for word **the flock of the slaughter**. The sellers bless the LORD for the profit. Here the prophet acts out the part of the shepherd, as Ezekiel was told to *take a stick* (Ezekiel 37:16), and what he acts out is the LORD's word against the shepherds who feed themselves (Ezekiel 34:2).
- **vv7–8 "truly the afflicted of the flock… Favor… Union… the three shepherds"** — *lakhen aniyyei ha-tson*, **truly the afflicted of the flock**; the same letters read as one word, *li-khna'aniyyei ha-tson*, are **the sheep merchants**, and the Greek has *in Canaan* here and *the Canaanites* in v11. *No'am*, **Favor**, is pleasantness or kindness; *chovelim*, **Union**, is from *chevel*, a cord, things bound together. Who the *three shepherds* are, the text does not say. *Va-tiqtsar nafshi*, word for word **my soul was short**: **I lost patience**.
- **vv10–11 "broke it in pieces, to annul my covenant that I had cut with all the peoples"** — the prophet speaks as the LORD's shepherd, and the covenant is the LORD's, held back from the nations. *Lehafer*, **to annul**, is the word for breaking a covenant (Genesis 17:14).
- **vv12–13 "they weighed out my wages, thirty pieces of silver… Throw it to the potter"** — thirty shekels was the price for a slave gored by an ox, *thirty shekels of silver to their master* (Exodus 21:32). *Eder ha-yeqar*, **the lordly price**, is said in scorn. *El ha-yotser*, **to the potter**, in *the house of the LORD*; the Greek has *Put them into the smelting furnace*. Matthew reads the thirty pieces of silver paid for Jesus by this verse, under Jeremiah's name (Matthew 26:15; 27:9–10).
- **v14 "to annul the brotherhood between Judah and Israel"** — *achavah*, **brotherhood**, found only here: the bond the north and south were promised in Ezekiel 37:19 is broken.
- **vv15–17 "the equipment of a foolish shepherd… He will eat the flesh of the fat ones… Woe to My worthless shepherd"** — the bad shepherd does the opposite of what Ezekiel's good shepherd does: *You have not strengthened the weak, healed the sick, bound up the injured* (Ezekiel 34:4). *Ro'i ha-elil*, **My worthless shepherd**: *elil* is the word for an idol, a thing of nothing. *Yavosh tivash… kaho tikhheh*, doubled verbs, **wither… go blind**.

# Zechariah 3

## Joshua the High Priest

**1** Then He showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the messenger of the LORD, and Satan standing at his right hand to accuse him.

**2** The LORD said to Satan, "The LORD rebuke you, Satan! The LORD who has chosen Jerusalem rebuke you! Is not this man a brand snatched from the fire?"

**3** Joshua was clothed in filthy garments, standing before the messenger.

**4** The messenger answered and said to those standing before him, "Take the filthy garments off him." To Joshua he said, "See, I have put away your iniquity from you, and I will clothe you in festal robes."

**5** Then I said, "Let them put a clean turban on his head." They put the clean turban on his head and clothed him in garments, while the messenger of the LORD stood by.

**6** The messenger of the LORD solemnly charged Joshua, saying,

**7** "This is what the LORD of hosts says: If you walk in My ways and keep My watch, then you will govern My house and guard My courts, and I will give you a place to walk among these who stand here.

## My Servant the Branch

**8** Hear, please, Joshua the high priest, you and your companions who sit before you, for they are men who are a portent: I am about to bring My servant the Branch.

**9** Here is the stone that I have set before Joshua: on one stone are seven eyes. I am about to engrave its inscription, declares the LORD of hosts, and I will remove the iniquity of that land in one day.

**10** On that day, declares the LORD of hosts, each of you will invite his neighbor under his vine and under his fig tree."

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "Then He showed me… Satan standing at his right hand to accuse him… The LORD rebuke you, Satan!"** — *va-yar'eni*, **He showed me**, with no name; in 1:20 it is *the LORD showed me*, and the Greek has *the Lord showed me* here. *Ha-satan*, **Satan**, with the article, as in Job (Job 1:6), is word for word **the accuser**, and *le-sitno*, **to accuse him**, is the same root: the accuser stands to accuse. The word is a plain noun in *the messenger of the LORD took his stand in the road as an adversary against him* (Numbers 22:22), and the right hand is the accuser's place in *let an accuser stand at his right hand* (Psalm 109:6). *Ud mutsal me-esh*, **a brand snatched from the fire**, are Amos's words, *you were like a brand snatched from the burning* (Amos 4:11): Joshua and his people are what is left after the fire of exile.
- **vv3–5 "filthy garments… I have put away your iniquity… a clean turban"** — *tso'im*, **filthy**, is the word for excrement, as in *the filth of the daughters of Zion* (Isaiah 4:4). *He'evarti me-alekha avonekha*, **I have put away your iniquity from you**, as Nathan said to David, *The LORD also has put away your sin* (2 Samuel 12:13). *Machalatsot*, **festal robes**, as in Isaiah 3:22. *Tsanif*, **turban**, as in *my justice was like a robe and a turban* (Job 29:14); *tahor*, **clean**, is the *pure* gold of the high priest's plate (Exodus 28:36). *Then I said*: the prophet speaks into his own vision; the Greek has no *I said*, and makes it part of the command.
- **vv6–7 "solemnly charged… keep My watch… a place to walk among these who stand here"** — *va-ya'ad*, **solemnly charged**, as in *The man solemnly warned us* (Genesis 43:3). *Mishmarti tishmor*, **keep My watch**, the priests' duty, *keep the LORD's watch, so that you do not die* (Leviticus 8:35). *Mahlekhim*, **a place to walk**, is found only here: a right of way among *these who stand here*, the attendants of the heavenly court (v4).
- **v8 "men who are a portent: I am about to bring My servant the Branch"** — *anshei mofet*, **men who are a portent**, as Isaiah said of himself and his children, *signs and portents in Israel* (Isaiah 8:18): the priests are themselves a sign of what is coming. *Tsemach*, **Branch**, is the name of the promised king of David's line in Jeremiah, *I will raise up for David a righteous Branch* (Jeremiah 23:5; 33:15). *Avdi*, **My servant**, as Zerubbabel is called in Haggai 2:23. The Greek has *Anatolē*, **Dawn** or **Rising**, for *Branch*: the Greek word means both a sprouting and the sun's rising.
- **v9 "on one stone are seven eyes… I am about to engrave its inscription… in one day"** — *einayim*, **eyes**, may also be facets or springs; 4:10 explains *these seven are the eyes of the LORD*. *Mefatteach pittuchah*, word for word **engraving its engraving**, the verb of the high priest's plate, *engrave on it, with the engravings of a seal: Holy to the LORD* (Exodus 28:36). *U-mashti*, **I will remove**, from *mush*, **depart**. The Greek has *I am digging a pit*.
- **v10 "each of you will invite his neighbor under his vine and under his fig tree"** — the words of Solomon's peace, *each man under his vine and under his fig tree* (1 Kings 4:25), and of Micah's promise (Micah 4:4).

# Zechariah 5

## The Flying Scroll

**1** Again I lifted up my eyes and looked, and saw a flying scroll.

**2** He said to me, "What do you see?" I said, "I see a flying scroll; its length is twenty cubits and its width ten cubits."

**3** He said to me, "This is the curse that goes out over the face of the whole land; for according to it every thief will be purged from here, and according to it everyone who swears will be purged from here.

**4** I have sent it out, declares the LORD of hosts, and it will enter the house of the thief and the house of the one who swears falsely by My name. It will lodge inside his house and consume it, its timbers and its stones."

## The Woman in the Ephah

**5** Then the messenger who talked with me went out and said to me, "Lift up your eyes, please, and see what this is that is going out."

**6** I said, "What is it?" He said, "This is the ephah that is going out." He also said, "This is their appearance throughout the land."

**7** A round cover of lead was lifted, and there was a woman sitting inside the ephah.

**8** He said, "This is Wickedness." He threw her down inside the ephah and threw the lead weight on its mouth.

**9** Then I lifted up my eyes and looked, and saw two women coming out, with the wind in their wings. They had wings like the wings of a stork, and they lifted up the ephah between the earth and the heavens.

**10** I said to the messenger who talked with me, "Where are they taking the ephah?"

**11** He said to me, "To build a house for her in the land of Shinar. When it is ready, she will be set down there on her stand."

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "a flying scroll; its length is twenty cubits and its width ten cubits"** — *megillah*, **scroll**, open in the air, since its size can be seen. Twenty cubits by ten are the measurements of the porch before Solomon's temple, *twenty cubits long, the width of the house, and ten cubits deep* (1 Kings 6:3).
- **vv3–4 "This is the curse… every thief… everyone who swears… swears falsely by My name"** — *ha-alah*, **the curse**, is the word for an oath and the curse that goes with it. The two sins are two of the commandments, *You shall not steal* (Exodus 20:15) and *You shall not swear falsely by My name* (Leviticus 19:12). *Mi-zeh*, **from here**, twice, may also be **on one side… on the other**, as the tablets were *written on both their sides, on one side and on the other* (Exodus 32:15): the scroll with one sin on each side. *Niqqah*, **will be purged**, is the verb that also means **go unpunished**. The curse *will consume* the house *its timbers and its stones*, as the law tears down a house with blight, *its stones and its timbers* (Leviticus 14:45).
- **vv5–6 "This is the ephah… This is their appearance throughout the land"** — the *ephah* is a measure of grain, and here a basket of that size; the dishonest *ephah* is what the merchants made *small* (Amos 8:5). *Einam*, **their appearance**, word for word **their eye**, as the manna's *appearance* is its *eye* (Numbers 11:7). The words answer *the eyes of the LORD, which run to and fro through the whole earth* (4:10): what the LORD's eyes see in the land is this. The Greek has *This is their iniquity*, *avonam*, a single letter different.
- **vv7–8 "A round cover of lead… This is Wickedness… threw the lead weight on its mouth"** — *kikkar*, **a round cover**, is also the word for a talent, a weight; the Greek has *a talent of lead*. *Ha-rish'ah*, **Wickedness**, is a woman, since the word is feminine; the Greek has *lawlessness*. *Even ha-oferet*, word for word **the stone of lead**, the weight that seals her in.
- **vv9–11 "wings like the wings of a stork… a house for her in the land of Shinar… on her stand"** — *ruach*, **the wind**, in their wings, lowercase. The *chasidah*, **stork**, is listed among the unclean birds (Leviticus 11:19). *Shinar* is the land of Babel's tower, where the builders *found a valley in the land of Shinar* (Genesis 11:2). *Bayit*, **house**, is also a temple, and *mekhunatah*, **her stand**, is close to the word for the bronze *stands* of Solomon's temple (1 Kings 7:27): Wickedness is carried out of the land to be housed and set up in Babylon.

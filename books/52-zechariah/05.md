# Zechariah 5

## The Flying Scroll

**1** Again I lifted up my eyes and looked, and saw a flying scroll.

**2** He said to me, "What do you see?" I said, "I see a flying scroll; its length is twenty cubits and its width ten cubits."

**3** He said to me, "This is the curse that goes out over the face of the whole land; for according to it every thief will be purged from here, and according to it everyone who swears will be purged from here.

**4** I have sent it out, declares the LORD of hosts, and it will enter the house of the thief and the house of the one who swears falsely by My name. It will lodge inside his house and consume it, its timbers and its stones."

## The Woman in the Ephah

**5** Then the messenger who talked with me went out and said to me, "Lift up your eyes, please, and see what this is that is going out."

**6** I said, "What is it?" He said, "This is the ephah that is going out." He also said, "This is their appearance throughout the land."

**7** A round cover of lead was lifted, and there was a woman sitting inside the ephah.

**8** He said, "This is Wickedness." He threw her down inside the ephah and threw the lead weight on its mouth.

**9** Then I lifted up my eyes and looked, and saw two women coming out, with the wind in their wings. They had wings like the wings of a stork, and they lifted up the ephah between the earth and the heavens.

**10** I said to the messenger who talked with me, "Where are they taking the ephah?"

**11** He said to me, "To build a house for her in the land of Shinar. When it is ready, she will be set down there on her stand."

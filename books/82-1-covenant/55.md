# 1st Book of the Covenant 55

**1** "Then let them be together in the church, and let the bishop lay a hand on them after baptism, saying and calling down over them:

**2** 'O Lord God, who through Your beloved Son Jesus Christ filled Your holy apostles with the Holy Spirit, and by the Spirit let Your blessed prophets speak; who counted these servants of Yours worthy of forgiveness of sins in Your Christ through the washing of rebirth, and cleansed them of all the mist of error and the darkness of unbelief: make them worthy to be filled with Your Holy Spirit, by Your love for mankind, giving them Your grace, so that they may serve You according to Your will, truly, O God, do Your commandments in holiness, and, always tending what is of Your will, enter into Your eternal dwellings, through You and through Your beloved Son Jesus Christ, through whom be praise and might to You with the Holy Spirit forever and ever.'

**3** Likewise, pouring the oil and placing a hand on his head, let him say, 'I anoint you in God Almighty, and in Jesus Christ, and in the Holy Spirit, so that you may be His soldier, with a perfect faith, a vessel pleasing to Him.'

**4** Sealing him on his forehead, let him give him the Peace and say, 'The Lord God of the meek be with you.' Let the one who has been sealed answer, 'And with your spirit.' So for each one in turn.

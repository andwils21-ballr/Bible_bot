# 1st Book of the Covenant 1

It came to pass, after our Lord rose from the dead and appeared to us, and was touched by Thomas and Matthew and John, and we were convinced that our Master had truly risen from the dead, that we fell on our faces and blessed the Father of the new world, God, who has saved us through Jesus Christ our Lord. Held in very great fear, we waited face down, like infants who cannot speak. But Jesus our Lord put His hand on each one of us in turn and lifted us up, saying, "Why has your heart fallen like this? Why are you struck with such astonishment? Do you not know that He who sent Me can do glorious things for the salvation of those who have believed in Him from the heart? Do not stand there astonished and staring, and do not be slack. As children of light, ask My Father who is in heaven for the Spirit of counsel and might, and He will fill you with the Holy Spirit and grant you to be with Me forever."

## The Spirit Given

**1** We answered Him, saying, "Lord, what is the Holy Spirit, and what is His power, that You told us to ask for?" Our Lord said to us, "Truly I tell you, you will not be children of the light except by the Holy Spirit." We answered Him and said, "Our Lord, give us this Spirit." At once Jesus breathed on us. After we had received the Holy Spirit, He said to us, "Truly I tell you: you who have been made disciples for the kingdom of heaven, who have believed in Me with an undoubting heart and have held fast to Me, will be with Me. All who through you know and do the will of My Father, who keep My words and know My sufferings, will be made holy. They will live in the dwellings of My Father and will be delivered from the evil days that are about to come. I will be with them, showing them My ways, in which they will live."

## Notes

- **The whole book** — worked from the English of J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord* (1902), which is translated from the **Syriac**. No Ge'ez or other source language was used. The Syriac is a sister version of this book: the Ethiopic first Book of the Covenant descends from the same text but differs from it in wording and in places in order. The chapters are Cooper and Maclean's, with their second book numbered on from the first; each verse is one of their paragraphs. The opening narrative, which stands before their chapter 1, is set above verse 1.
- **The opening, "was touched by Thomas and Matthew and John"** — the risen Lord is proved by touch, as the first letter of John begins: *what we have looked at and our hands have handled, concerning the word of life* (1 John 1:1, *hai cheires hēmōn epsēlaphēsan*). The book speaks in the apostles' own voice, *we*, from its first line.
- **The opening, "the Spirit of counsel and might"** — the words of Isaiah 11:2, *ruach etsah u-gevurah*, among the spirits that rest on the shoot from the stump of Jesse: *the Spirit of wisdom and understanding, the Spirit of counsel and might*.
- **The opening and v1, "children of light"** — *Believe in the light, so that you may become sons of light* (John 12:36, *huioi phōtos*); *walk as children of light* (Ephesians 5:8, *tekna phōtos*). In v1 the Lord adds the condition: no one becomes one *except by the Holy Spirit*.
- **v1 "At once Jesus breathed on us"** — John 20:22: *He breathed on them and said to them, "Receive the Holy Spirit"* (*enephysēsen*). In John the breath comes unasked; here it answers the disciples' prayer, *give us this Spirit*, which they were told to make in the opening.
- **v1 "the dwellings of My Father"** — the promise of John 14:2, *In My Father's house are many dwelling places* (*monai*).
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

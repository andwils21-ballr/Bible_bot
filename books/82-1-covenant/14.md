# 1st Book of the Covenant 14

**1** "I have told you these things, then, so that wherever you go you may test the souls that are holy, and tell them what is fitting and right, and what is about to be, and all that I gave you as commandments before I was glorified, so that believing them they may truly live. From now on will be the beginning of birth pains, and the mystery of destruction. Turn, then, to the Church: set it right, put it in due order and arrange it, doing all things in uprightness and holiness. Speak to each one in the way that helps him, so that your Father who is in heaven may be glorified. Be wise, so that you may persuade those held captive by error and those sunk in ignorance, so that, coming to the knowledge of God and living devoutly and purely, they may praise My Father and your God."

---

## Notes

- **v1 "the beginning of birth pains… My Father and your God"** — the Lord's words on the signs of the end, *All these are the beginning of birth pains* (Matthew 24:8), and the risen Lord's words to Mary, *I am going up to My Father and your Father, to My God and your God* (John 20:17). Here the signs end and the order of the Church begins.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 44

**1** "Let a subdeacon who is chaste be appointed in the same way, the bishop praying over him. On the first day of the week, in the hearing of all the people, let the bishop say over him:

**2** 'You, N., serve and hear the Gospel in the fear of God. Tend the knowledge of your soul in holiness; keep purity; discipline yourself; watch and obey and listen meekly; do not neglect prayer and fasting, so that the Lord may give you rest and make you worthy of a higher rank.'

**3** Let all the priests say, 'So be it, so be it, so be it.'

# 1st Book of the Covenant 56

**1** "From then on let them pray together with all the people. Let the offering be brought by the deacon, and then let the shepherd give thanks. The bread is offered as a type of My body. Let the cup be mixed with wine, wine and water, for it is a sign of blood and of the washing, so that the inner man too, what is of the soul, may be counted worthy of what is like them, that is, of the things of the body as well. Let all the people, as was said before, receive with the Amen of the Eucharist that is offered. Let the deacons watch over them, as was said before.

**2** Let the one who gives it say, 'The body of Jesus Christ, the Holy Spirit, for the healing of soul and body.' Let the one who receives say, 'Amen.'

**3** Whoever spills from the cup gathers judgment to himself; so too whoever sees it and is silent and does not rebuke him, whoever he may be. Let those who receive the Offering be urged by the priests to take care to do good works, to love strangers, to labor in fasting, and to give themselves to service in every good work. Let them also be taught about the resurrection of the body. Before anyone receives baptism, let no one know the word about the —

## Notes

- **v3 "the word about the —"** — the text held here breaks off, and the next chapter turns to gifts for widows. The break is kept.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 6

**1** Then there will be signs in heaven. A bow will be seen, and a horn, and lights; and noises out of season, sounds and ragings of the sea, and a roaring of the earth.

**2** On the earth there will be signs: dragons born of human beings, and wild beasts likewise; young brides will give birth to babies who speak perfectly and announce the last times, and ask to be put to death. They will look like men far advanced in years; they will be gray-haired when they are born. Women will also bear babies with four feet; some will bear spirits only, and some will bear their offspring with unclean spirits. There will be others who practice divination in the womb and speak with familiar spirits; and there will be many other horrible signs.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 35

## The Deacon's Call

**1** "Let him be in everything like the eye of the Church, admonishing with fear, so that he may be an example of devotion to the people. Let him admonish like this:

**2** 'Let everyone know his place.

**3** Let the catechumens depart.

**4** See that no one unclean, no one slothful, remains. Lift up the eyes of your hearts.

**5** The angels are watching.

**6** See that whoever does not trust withdraws.

**7** Let us pray in harmony.

**8** Let no fornicator, no angry man remain; if anyone who serves evil is here, let him withdraw.

**9** See: as children of light, let us ask and pray our Lord and our God and our Savior Jesus Christ.'

## The Deacon's Prayers

**10** When the presbyter or bishop begins the prayer, let the people pray and kneel.

**11** Then let the deacon say this:

**12** 'For the peace that is from heaven let us pray, that the Lord in His mercy may give us peace.

**13** For our faith let us pray, that the Lord may grant us to keep truly to the end the faith that is in Him.

**14** For harmony and concord let us pray, that the Lord may keep us together in the concord of the Spirit.

**15** For patience let us pray, that the Lord may give us patience to the end in all afflictions.

**16** For the apostles let us pray, that the Lord may grant us to please Him as they also pleased Him, and make us worthy of their inheritance.

**17** For the holy prophets let us pray, that the Lord may number us with them.

**18** For the holy confessors let us pray, that the Lord God may grant us to finish our course with the same mind as they.

**19** For the bishop let us pray, that our Lord may grant him to us for length of days in faith, rightly handling the word of truth, and standing at the head of the Church purely and without blame.

**20** For the presbyterate let us pray, that the Lord may not take the spirit of the presbyterate away from them, but give them earnestness and devotion to the end.

**21** For the deacons let us pray, that the Lord may grant them to run a perfect course and to perfect holiness, and that He may remember their work and their love.

**22** For the presbyteresses let us pray, that the Lord may hear their prayers, keep their hearts perfectly in the grace of the Spirit, and help their work.

**23** For the subdeacons, readers and deaconesses let us pray, that the Lord may grant them to receive a reward in patience.

**24** For the faithful laymen let us pray, that the Lord may grant them to keep the faith perfectly.

**25** For the catechumens let us pray, that the Lord may count them worthy of the washing of forgiveness, and sanctify them with the seal of holiness.

**26** For the kingdom let us pray, that the Lord may give it peace.

**27** For the exalted powers let us pray, that the Lord may give them prudence and the fear of Him.

**28** For all the world let us pray, that the Lord may provide for each one what is fitting.

**29** For those who travel by sea and those who go on journeys let us pray, that the Lord may guide them with the right hand of mercy.

**30** For those who are persecuted let us pray, that the Lord may give them patience and knowledge, and bring their labor to completion.

**31** For those of the Church who have fallen asleep let us pray, that the Lord may give them a place of rest.

**32** For those who have fallen let us pray, that the Lord may not hold their follies against them, but soften His threats toward them.

**33** Let all of us who need prayer pray also, that the Lord may protect and keep us with the peaceful Spirit.

**34** Let us entreat and pray the Lord, that He may receive our prayers.'

**35** When the deacon has made the remembrance, let the bishop make a sign with his hand.

**36** Let the deacon say, 'Let us rise in the Holy Spirit, so that, made wise, we may grow in His grace, boasting in His name. Built on the foundation of the apostles, let us ask and pray the Lord that He may be moved to receive our prayers.'

**37** Then let the bishop complete the prayer, and let the people say, 'Amen.'

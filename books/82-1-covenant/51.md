# 1st Book of the Covenant 51

**1** "After the catechumens pray, let the bishop or presbyter lay a hand on them and say the prayer of the laying on of the hand of the catechumens:

**2** 'O God, who send thunders and prepare lightnings, who founded the heaven and established the earth, who give light to the faithful and turn back those who go astray; who gave life to those who were dead and hope to those who were without hope, and freed the universe from error by the coming down of Your Only-begotten Son Jesus Christ: hear us, O Lord, and give these souls understanding, completeness, undoubting faith and knowledge of the truth, so that they may come to a rank higher than this, through the holy name of You and of Your beloved Son Jesus, our Lord, through whom be praise and might to You with the Holy Spirit, now and always and forever and ever. Amen.'

**3** After this let them be dismissed. If anyone who is a catechumen is arrested for My name and tried with tortures, and hurries and presses on to receive the washing, let the shepherd not hesitate, but give it to him. If he suffers violence and is killed without having received the washing, let him not be anxious; for he has been baptized in his own blood, and is justified.

---

## Notes

- **v3 "baptized in his own blood"** — the catechumen killed for the Name before he reaches the water lacks nothing: his death is his baptism. The Lord spoke of His own death in the same word: *I have a baptism to be baptized with* (Luke 12:50).
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

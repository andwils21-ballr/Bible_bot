# 1st Book of the Covenant 20

**1** "When the house has been built as is fitting and right, let the bishop be appointed, chosen by all the people according to the will of the Holy Spirit. Let him be without fault, chaste, quiet, gentle, free of anxiety, watchful, not a lover of money, blameless, not quarrelsome, ready to forgive, a teacher, not a man of many words, a lover of what is good, a lover of labor, a lover of widows, a lover of orphans, a lover of the poor, experienced in the mysteries, not slack and distracted by the company of this world, peaceful, and complete in every good thing, as one to whom the order and the place of God are entrusted. It is good that he be without a wife, but at least that he has been the husband of one wife only, so that he may sympathize with the weakness of widows. Let him be appointed when he is of middle age, not a youth.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "not a lover of money… the husband of one wife only"** — Paul's list for the overseer: *the husband of one wife… not quarrelsome, not a lover of money* (1 Timothy 3:2–3). Here the rule has a reason Paul does not give: *so that he may sympathize with the weakness of widows*.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

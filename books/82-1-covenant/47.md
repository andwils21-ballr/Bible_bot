# 1st Book of the Covenant 47

**1** "Let those who come for the first time to hear the Word first go to teachers at home, before they come in among all the people, and let the whole reason for their coming be examined with all care, so that their teachers may know why they have come and with what will. If they have come with good will and love, let them be taught diligently. Let those who bring them be well on in years, faithful people known to the Church, and let them bear witness about them, whether they are able to hear the word. Let their life and conduct also be asked about: whether they are not quarrelsome; whether they are quiet and meek, not speakers of empty things or scorners or foul-mouthed, or buffoons or misleaders or mockers. Let it be asked too whether any of them has a wife or not. If, of his own free will, he has no wife, let him be instructed carefully and diligently and persuaded with all kindness to amend his failings. Let the bishop provide for him in the Lord with the instructions of the prophets that lead him to purity; and if he makes progress, with the teachings of the apostles, then of the Gospels, and with the perfect word of teaching; and if he is worthy, let him be baptized. If he is then worthy of the hidden things, let him hear them by himself, and make progress also in what is hidden.

**2** Let there be no obstacle at all to the one who wishes to marry, so that he is not caught by the Evil One through fornication. But let him marry a Christian, a faithful woman of the Christian people, who is able to keep her husband in the faith, at the bishop's bidding, as he provides for him.

**3** Let the one who comes also be asked whether he is a slave or free. If he is the slave of one who is faithful, and his master permits him, let him hear. If his master is not faithful and does not permit him, let him be persuaded to permit him. If his master says truly of him that he wishes to become a Christian because he hates his masters, let him be cast out. But if no reason of hatred of slavery is shown, and he truly wishes to be a Christian, let him hear. If his master is faithful and does not bear witness to him, let him be cast out. Likewise, if a woman is a man's wife, let her be taught to please her husband in the fear of God. If both of them wish to serve purity in devotion, they have a reward.

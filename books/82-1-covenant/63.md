# 1st Book of the Covenant 63

**1** "Let them give thanks in turn, and not eat so as to cause offense or stumbling. Let no one taste what is strangled or sacrificed to idols.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "what is strangled or sacrificed to idols"** — the letter of the apostles from Jerusalem: *that you keep away from what has been sacrificed to idols, from blood, from what has been strangled* (Acts 15:29).
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

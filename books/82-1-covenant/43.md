# 1st Book of the Covenant 43

**1** "Let her hymns of praise be said quietly, like this:

**2** 'Holy, holy, without spot, who have Your dwelling in the light, God of Abraham and of Isaac and of Jacob, God of Enoch and David, of Elijah, of Elisha, of Moses, of Joshua, and of the prophets and the others who preached Your name in truth, God of the apostles, the God who have guided all things by Your reason and blessed those who trust in You with love: my soul praises You with the power of the spirit of my strength; my heart praises You, O Lord, and Your might, always. Let all my strength praise You, O Lord, for if You will, I am Yours, O God, the God of the poor; for You are the Helper of those in need, the One who looks on the meek, and the Supporter of the weak. Help me, O Lord, for by Your grace You were pleased in me that I should be Your handmaid, for You have given me a great name, that I should be called a Christian. You freed me from slavery so that I may serve as a slave to God, the Mighty One who is forever, who sees all, so that I may praise You without condemnation. Yes, O Lord God, strengthen my heart in You until it is made complete in the Holy Spirit. Make us young again for the building up of Your holy Church, Son and Word and Thought of the Father, the Christ who came for the salvation of the human race, who suffered and was buried and rose, and was glorified by Him who sent You. Turn, help, O Lord, set our thoughts upright by the strong faith of the Spirit. Glorify Your name in us.'

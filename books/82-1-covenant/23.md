# 1st Book of the Covenant 23

## The Loaves and the Order

**1** "Let him offer three loaves on the Sabbath, as a complete symbol of the Trinity, but on the first day of the week four loaves, as a complete symbol of the Gospel.

**2** Because the people of old went astray, when he offers let the veil in front of the door be closed, and within it let him offer with the presbyters and deacons and the enrolled widows, the subdeacons and deaconesses and readers, and those who have gifts. Let the bishop stand first, in the middle, and the presbyters right behind him on either side; the widows right behind the presbyters on the left side, and the deacons behind the presbyters on the right side; the readers behind them, the subdeacons behind the readers, and the deaconesses behind the subdeacons.

**3** Then let the bishop place his hand on the loaves that have been set on the altar, and let the presbyters place their hands with his, and let the rest only stand.

**4** Let the loaf of a catechumen not be received, not even if he has a believing son or wife and wishes to offer on their behalf; let it not be offered unless he is baptized.

**5** Before the bishop or presbyter offers, let the people give the Peace to one another.

**6** Then, when a great silence has fallen, let the deacon say this:

## The Deacon's Admonition

**7** *The Deacon's Admonition at the Eucharist.* 'Lift up your hearts to heaven.

**8** If anyone is angry with his companion, let him be reconciled.

**9** If anyone has a conscience without faith, let him confess it.

**10** If anyone has a thought foreign to the commandments, let him depart.

**11** If anyone has fallen into sin, let him not hide: he cannot hide.

**12** If anyone's reason is disordered, let him not draw near.

**13** If anyone is defiled, if anyone is not firm, let him give place.

**14** If anyone is a stranger to the commandments of Jesus, let him depart.

**15** If anyone despises the prophets, let him separate himself; let him deliver himself from the wrath of the Only-begotten.

**16** Let us not despise the cross.

**17** Let us flee from the threat.

**18** We have our Lord watching us: the Father of lights, with the Son, and the angels who visit us.

**19** See to yourselves, that you are not angry with your neighbors.

**20** See that no one is in anger: God sees. Lift up your hearts to offer for the salvation of life and of holiness.

**21** In the wisdom of God let us receive the grace that has been given us.'

**22** Then let the bishop, giving thanks, say with an awed voice, 'Our Lord be with you.' Let the people say, 'And with your spirit.'

**23** Let the bishop say, 'Lift up your hearts.' Let the people say, 'They are lifted up to the Lord.' Let the bishop say, 'Let us give thanks to the Lord.'

**24** Let all the people say, 'It is fitting and right.'

**25** Let the bishop cry out, 'Holy things in the holy.'

**26** Let the people call out, 'In heaven and on earth without ceasing.'

## The Thanksgiving

**27** *The Eucharist, or Thanksgiving, over the Offering.*

**28** Let the bishop at once say: 'We give You thanks, O God, the Holy One, Strengthener of our souls and Giver of our life, the Treasure of incorruption, and Father of the Only-begotten, our Savior, whom in the last times You sent to us as Savior and Herald of Your purpose. For it is Your purpose that we should be saved in You. Our heart gives You thanks, O Lord, our mind, our soul, with all its thinking, that Your grace may come upon us, O Lord, so that we may always praise You, and Your Only-begotten Son, and Your Holy Spirit, now and always, and forever and ever. Amen.

**29** O Power of the Father, Grace of the nations, Knowledge, true Wisdom, Exaltation of the meek, Medicine of souls, Confidence of us who believe — for You are the Strength of the righteous, the Hope of the persecuted, the Haven of those tossed by the storm, the Light of the perfect, the Son of the living God — make rise on us, out of Your gift that cannot be searched out, courage, might, trust, wisdom, strength, unfailing faith, unshaken hope, the knowledge of Your Spirit, meekness and uprightness, so that always, O Lord, we Your servants and all the people may praise You purely, bless You, give thanks to You, Lord, at all times, and pray to You.'

**30** Let the bishop also say:

**31** 'You, Lord, Founder of the heights and King of the treasuries of light, Visitor of the heavenly Zion, King of the orders of archangels, of Dominions, Praises, Thrones, Raiments, Lights, Joys, Delights; Father of kings, who hold all in Your hand and provide for all by Your reason, through Your Only-begotten Son, who was crucified for our sins: You, Lord, sent Your Word, who is of Your counsel and covenants, through whom You made all things, being well pleased with Him, into a virgin womb. When He was conceived and made flesh, He was shown to be Your Son, born of the Holy Spirit and the Virgin. Fulfilling Your will and preparing a holy people, He stretched out His hands to suffering, to set free from sufferings and corruption and death those who have hoped in You. When He was betrayed to the suffering He chose, that He might raise up those who had slipped, find those who were lost, give life to the dead, loose the pains of death, tear apart the bonds of the Devil, fulfill the counsel of the Father, tread down Sheol, open the way of life, guide the righteous to light, fix the boundary, light up the darkness, nurture the infants and reveal the resurrection, He took bread and gave it to His disciples, saying, "Take, eat, this is My body, which is broken for you for the forgiveness of sins. When you do this, you make My resurrection." The cup of wine that He mixed He also gave, as a type of the blood that He shed for us.

**32** Remembering, then, Your death and resurrection, we offer You bread and the cup, giving thanks to You, who alone are God forever, and our Savior, since You have promised us that we may stand before You and serve You as priests. So we give You thanks, we Your servants, O Lord.'

**33** Let the people say the same.

**34** Let the bishop also say: 'We offer You this thanksgiving, Eternal Trinity, O

**35** Lord Jesus Christ, O Lord the Father, before whom all creation and every nature trembles, fleeing into itself, O Lord the Holy Spirit. We have brought You this drink and this food of Your holiness. Make it be for us not for condemnation, not for reproach, not for destruction, but for the healing and support of our spirit. Yes, O God, grant that by Your name every thought of what displeases You may flee away. Grant, O God, that every proud thought may be driven from us by Your name, which is written within the veils of Your sanctuaries on high: the name at which Sheol is amazed when it hears it, the deep is torn open, the spirits are driven away, the dragon is crushed, unbelief is cast out, disobedience is subdued, anger is calmed, envy does not work, pride is rebuked, greed is rooted out, boasting is taken away, arrogance is humbled, and every root of bitterness is destroyed. Grant, then, O Lord, to our innermost eyes to see You, praising You and glorifying You, remembering You and serving You, having a portion in You alone, O Son and Word of God, who subdue all things. Sustain to the end those who have gifts of revelation. Strengthen those who have a gift of healing. Make those courageous who have the power of tongues. Keep upright those who have the word of teaching. Care always for those who do Your will. Visit the widows. Help the orphans. Remember those who have fallen asleep in the faith. Grant us an inheritance with

**36** Your saints, and give us the power to please You as they too pleased You. Feed the people in uprightness. Sanctify us all, O God, and grant that all who partake and receive of Your holy things may be made one with You, so that they may be filled with the Holy Spirit for the strengthening of the faith in truth, and may always lift up praise to You and to Your beloved Son Jesus Christ, through whom be praise and might to You, with Your Holy Spirit, forever and ever.'

**37** The deacon: 'Let us earnestly pray our Lord and our God to give us harmony of spirit.'

**38** The bishop: 'Give us harmony in the Holy Spirit, and heal our souls by this offering, so that we may live in You through all the ages of ages.'

**39** Let the people also pray in the same words.

**40** After these things, the seal of the thanksgiving, like this: 'Blessed be the name of the Lord forever.'

**41** The priest: 'Blessed is He who has come in the name of the Lord. Blessed is the name of His praise.'

**42** Let all the people say, 'So be it, so be it.'

**43** Let the bishop say, 'Send the grace of the Spirit upon us.'

## Receiving

**44** If the bishop is unclean, let him not offer, but let a presbyter offer. Let him not receive of the mystery either, not as though he were defiled, but for the honor of the altar. After he has fasted and bathed in pure water, let him come near and serve. A presbyter likewise. If a widow is in her monthly period, let her not come near. Likewise, if a woman or a layman or any of the clergy is unclean, let them not come near, for the honor of the altar, except after fasting and bathing.

**45** Let the priests receive first, in this order: the bishops, presbyters, deacons, widows, readers, subdeacons; after these, those who have gifts, the newly baptized, the infants. The people in this order: old men, virgins, and the rest. The women: deaconesses, and after them the rest.

**46** When each one receives the thanksgiving, let him say 'Amen' before he partakes. After that let him pray like this; after he receives the Eucharist, let him say, 'Holy, Holy, Holy, Trinity beyond words, grant me to receive this body for life and not for condemnation. Grant me to bear the fruits that please You, so that when I am shown to be pleasing to You I may live in You, doing Your commandments, and may call You Father with boldness when I call for Your kingdom and Your will to come to me. May Your name be hallowed in me, O Lord, for You are mighty and worthy of praise, and to You be praise forever and ever. Amen.'

**47** After the prayer let him receive.

**48** When he takes of the cup, let him say 'Amen' twice, as a complete symbol of the body and the blood.

**49** When all have received, let them pray, giving thanks for what they have received, the deacon saying:

**50** 'Let us give thanks to the Lord, as we receive His holy things, so that receiving them may be for the life and salvation of our souls. Let us ask and plead for His grace, lifting up praise to the Lord our God.'

**51** After that let the bishop say:

**52** 'O Lord, Giver of eternal light, Helmsman of souls, Guide of the saints, give us eyes of understanding that always look to You, and ears that hear You only, so that our soul may be filled with grace. Create in us a clean heart, O God, so that we may always grasp Your greatness. O God, Wonderful, who love mankind, make our souls better, and by this Eucharist that we Your servants, who fall short in much, have received, shape our thoughts so that they do not swerve. For Your kingdom is blessed, O Lord God, glorified and praised in Father and in Son and in Holy Spirit, before the ages, and now, and always, and for the ages and forever and ever without end.'

## Notes

- **v31 "When you do this, you make My resurrection"** — so the Syriac as Cooper and Maclean give it. In Paul the words over the bread end *Do this in remembrance of Me* (1 Corinthians 11:24); here what the Church does is tied to the resurrection, as in the prayer that follows, *Remembering, then, Your death and resurrection* (v32).
- **v46 "call You Father… Your kingdom and Your will… May Your name be hallowed in me"** — the Lord's Prayer, *hallowed be Your name. Your kingdom come, Your will be done* (Matthew 6:9–10), prayed by each one who receives, with *in me* and *to me*.
- **vv34–35, 35–36** — two sentences that run across the edition's paragraphs, kept as it divides them.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 2

**1** Peter and John answered Him and said, "Tell us, our Lord, the signs of the end, and all that will then be done by those who live in this world, so that we too may make them known to those who believe in

**2** Your name in all the nations, and those generations may watch for them and live." Jesus answered and said, "Did I not tell you some things about the end before I suffered for those who live on the earth?" We answered and said, "Yes, our Lord; but now we want to know the deeds that are the signs of the end of this world, if our Lord has judged it fitting for us to know them, for us and for those who will hear."

---

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "Tell us… the signs of the end"** — the question the disciples asked on the Mount of Olives, *Tell us, when will these things be, and what will be the sign of Your coming and of the end of the age?* (Matthew 24:3), asked again after the resurrection.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

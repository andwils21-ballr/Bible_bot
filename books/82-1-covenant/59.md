# 1st Book of the Covenant 59

**1** "At the supper or the feast, let those who have come together receive a portion from the shepherd in this way, as a blessing. But let no catechumen receive.

**2** If anyone is of the household of a teacher of paganism, or related to him, let him not join with him and give praise with him, and let him not eat with him for kinship's sake or for harmony; otherwise he may hand over things beyond words to a wolf, and receive judgment.

**3** Let those who are invited with the bishop to the house of one of the faithful eat seriously and with knowledge, not in drunkenness or to debauchery, and not so that whoever is present may laugh, or so as to trouble the household of him —

## Notes

- **vv3–4** — v3 breaks off in the text held here, and the paragraph numbered 4 is the edition's footnotes to 57:3 and 58:1–2, not part of the text, so no verse stands there.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 41

**1** "Let the appointment be like this. As she prays at the entrance of the altar, looking down, let the bishop say quietly, so that the priests may hear:

**2** *The Prayer of the Appointment of Widows Who Sit in Front*

**3** 'O God, the Holy One, the Most High, who see what is lowly, who have chosen the weak and the mighty; the Honored One, who created also what is despised: give, O Lord, the spirit of power to this handmaid of Yours, and strengthen her with Your truth, so that, doing Your commandment and serving in the house of Your sanctuary, she may be an honored vessel for You, and may glorify You on the day when You glorify Your poor, O Lord. Grant her power to carry out gladly Your teachings, which You have set as a rule for Your handmaid. Grant her, O Lord, the spirit of meekness and of power and of patience and of kindness, so that, bearing Your burden with joy beyond words, she may endure labors. Yes, O Lord God, who know our weakness, perfect Your handmaid for the praise of Your house; strengthen her for building up and for a good example; sanctify her, make her wise, comfort her, O God. For blessed and glorious is Your kingdom, O God the Father, and to You be praise, and to Your Only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, and to the Holy Spirit, good and worthy of worship, Maker of life, of one being with You, now and before all the ages and for the ages and forever and ever.' The people: 'Amen.'

---

## Notes

- **v3 "who have chosen the weak… who created also what is despised"** — Paul's words: *God chose what is weak in the world to shame the strong; God chose what is low and despised in the world* (1 Corinthians 1:27–28).
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

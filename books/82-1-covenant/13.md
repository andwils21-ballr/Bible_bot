# 1st Book of the Covenant 13

**1** Then those who fear My words and do them in truth and with a faithful mind will watch and pray without ceasing, counting constant prayer as their work, wandering after nothing and running after nothing in this world, anxious about nothing, but with a disciplined soul and a mind that does not doubt, taking up the cross every day, to do the will of My

**2** Father who is in heaven with a meek heart. For the one who cares for those who trust in the truth, and looks after them, is the Lord; and He sends them what is right and fitting, what He knows, and by the hands of those He knows.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 32

**1** "Let them say the daily hymn of praise in the Church, each of them at his own time, like this:

**2** 'The grace of our Lord be with you all.'

**3** The people say, 'And with your spirit.'

**4** The priest: 'Praise the Lord.'

**5** The people: 'It is fitting and right.'

**6** The priest: 'You, O Father of incorruption, Deliverer of our souls, Strengthener of our thoughts and Guardian of our hearts, who have lit up our hearts and ended the darkness of our understanding by the knowledge that is in You; who by the cross of Your Only-begotten have brought back again to incorruption the old man who was given over to corruption; who have put an end to error, and by Your commandments have brought man over to immortality; who sought what was lost: You we Your servants, and Your people too, praise.'

**7** The people: 'We praise You,' and the rest.

**8** The priest: 'We praise You, O Lord, whom the unceasing songs of glory of the archangels, the hymns of praise of the Glories and the chants of the Dominions praise without ceasing. We praise You, O Lord, who sent Your Thought, Your Word, Your Wisdom, Your Power, Him who is of old and was with You before the ages, the uncreated Word of the Uncreated, who appeared in the flesh at the end of times for the salvation of created man: Your beloved Son Jesus Christ, who made us free from the yoke of slavery. So we also, as we are accustomed, Your servants, O Lord, and Your people too, praise You.'

**9** The people: 'We praise You,' and the rest.

**10** The priest: 'We sing to You a threefold hymn of praise from our hearts, O Lord who give life: to You who visit the souls of the poor and do not neglect the spirits of the afflicted, Supporter of the persecuted, Helper of those tossed on the sea, Deliverer of those battered by the storm, Provider for the hungry, who avenge those who are wronged, Lover of the faithful, Companion of the saints, Home of the pure, Dwelling place of those who call on You in truth, Protector of widows, Liberator of orphans, who give Your Church right government, and have founded in it love feasts, ministries, the receiving of the faithful, the sharing of the Spirit, gifts of grace and powers. We praise You; we never cease to picture in our hearts the image of Your kingdom in ourselves, for Your sake and for the sake of Your beloved Son Jesus Christ, through whom be praise and might to You with the Holy Spirit, forever and ever. Amen.'

**11** Let the people say, 'Amen.'

**12** If anyone also speaks words of prophecy, let him speak them; he has a reward.

**13** At midnight let the sons of the priestly service, and those of the people who are more perfect, give praise by themselves; for at that hour our Lord, rising, praised His Father.

**14** See, children of light: whoever believes the words of the Lord walks as He walked in this world, so that where He is, he may be also.

## Notes

- **v14 "walks as He walked… where He is, he may be also"** — John's letter, *whoever says he remains in Him ought himself to walk just as He walked* (1 John 2:6), and the Lord's promise, *so that where I am, you may be also* (John 14:3).
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

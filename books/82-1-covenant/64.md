# 1st Book of the Covenant 64

**1** "In the days of Pascha, above all in the last days, on Friday and on the Sabbath, by night and by day, let the prayers be according to the number of the hymns of praise. Let the word be explained at length, and the readings be many and continuous. Let the vigils and the night watches be kept in good order.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 24

**1** "If the priest consecrates oil for the healing of those who suffer, let him say this, quietly placing the vessel before the altar:

**2** 'O Lord God, who have given us the Spirit, the Advocate, the Lord, the saving and unshaken Name, hidden from the foolish but revealed to the wise; O Christ, who sanctified us, and by Your mercies make the servants You choose wise with the wisdom that is Yours; who sent the knowledge of Your Spirit to us sinners by the holiness that is Yours, giving us the power of the Spirit; who are the Healer of every sickness and every suffering; who gave the gift of healing to those You counted worthy of it: send on this oil, the type of Your richness, the delivering power of Your good compassion, so that it may deliver those who labor, heal those who are sick, and sanctify those who return, when they come to Your faith; for You are mighty and worthy of praise forever and ever.'

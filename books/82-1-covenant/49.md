# 1st Book of the Covenant 49

**1** "Let the one who is being instructed with all care and hears the fullness of the Gospel be instructed for no less than three years; and if, out of love, he strives to be baptized, let him then be baptized.

**2** But if he is quiet and meek and earnest, persevering and staying with the one who teaches him, with labor, with watching, with confession, with submission and with prayer, and he wishes to be baptized sooner, let him be baptized. For it is not the time that is weighed, but the will of faith.

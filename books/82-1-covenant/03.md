# 1st Book of the Covenant 3

**1** Jesus answered and said, "While I was in the world, before I was glorified, I spoke to you of the signs that the end is near: that there will be famines and pestilences on the earth, tumults and commotions, nations rising against nations, and the other things I have told you of. But I commanded you to watch and pray. Now listen, children of the light. My Father, who sent Me to His inheritance, has determined beforehand in His foreknowledge that in the last days, out of the last generation, there will be vessels of grace, holy and honored and chosen. That is why I make known to you exactly what is about to be, and when he will arise, that son of destruction, the Enemy, the Adversary, and what he is like.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "that son of destruction, the Enemy, the Adversary"** — Paul's name for him: *the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of destruction* (2 Thessalonians 2:3). Cooper and Maclean have *Son of Perdition*.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 70

**1** "Let the people always take care about the early dawn: rising and washing their hands, let them pray at once, and then let each one go to the work he wishes.

**2** Let all take care to pray at the third hour, with mourning and labor, in the church, or in the house if they cannot go to the church. For this is the hour when the Only-begotten was fixed to the cross.

**3** At the sixth hour likewise let there be prayer with sorrow, for then the daylight was cut off by the darkness. Let there be at that hour a voice like the prophets' and like creation mourning.

**4** At the ninth hour also let prayer be drawn out, with a hymn of praise like the souls of those who praise God who does not lie, as One who has remembered His saints and sent His Word and Wisdom to give them light. For at that hour life was opened to the faithful, and blood and water were shed from the side of our Lord.

**5** At evening, when another day begins, showing an image of the resurrection, He has had us give praise.

**6** At midnight let them rise, praising and extolling, because of the resurrection.

**7** At dawn let them rise, praising with psalms, because after He rose He glorified the Father while they were singing psalms. But if anyone has a consort or wife who is not faithful —

## Notes

- **vv2–4 "the third hour… the sixth hour… the ninth hour"** — each hour of prayer is an hour of the cross as the Gospels tell it: *It was the third hour when they crucified Him* (Mark 15:25); *From the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land until the ninth hour* (Matthew 27:45); and *one of the soldiers pierced His side with a spear, and at once blood and water came out* (John 19:34).
- **v7 "a consort or wife who is not faithful —"** — the text held here breaks off, and the next chapter is the Lord's closing words. The break is kept.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 58

**1** "Let the end of Pascha come after the Sabbath, at midnight. At Pentecost let no one fast or kneel, for these are days of rest and joy.

**2** Let those who carry the burdens of labor rest a little in the days of Pentecost, and on every first day of the week.

**3** Before the bishop offers the Offering, let him say what is fitting for the Offering, while those who are dressed in white receive from one another and say to one another, *Hallelujah*.

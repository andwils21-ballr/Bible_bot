# 1st Book of the Covenant 37

**1** "If any woman suffers violence from a man, let the deacon find out carefully whether she is one of the faithful and has truly suffered violence, and whether the man who did it was not her lover. If it is truly so, and she mourns over the violence done to her, let him bring it to the bishop's hearing, so that she may be shown to be in communion with the Church in all things. If the man who did her violence is one of the faithful, let the deacon not bring him into the church to partake, even if he repents. But if he is a catechumen and repents, let him be baptized and partake. Let the deacon instruct those who repent and bring them to the presbyters or to the bishop, so that they may be instructed and taught knowledge. But if his power suffices to carry out the office of the diaconate perfectly, let him stay only in prayer. Let him count as his work prayer and meditation, love, the way, mourning, and keeping fear before his eyes; and he will be called a son of light.

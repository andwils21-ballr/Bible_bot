# 1st Book of the Covenant 11

**1** These are his marks: his head is like a fiery flame; his right eye is shot with blood, his left eye blue-black, and he has two pupils. His eyelashes are white, and his lower lip is large; but his right thigh is thin, his feet broad, and his great toe crushed and flat. This is the sickle of —

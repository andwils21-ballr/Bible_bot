# 1st Book of the Covenant 21

**1** "When he is such a man, let him be ordained on the first day of the week, with all agreeing to his appointment and bearing witness to him, together with all the neighboring presbyters and bishops. Let those bishops lay hands on him, having first washed their hands, while the presbyters stand beside them without speaking, in fear, lifting up their hearts in silence.

**2** After that, let the bishops lay hands on him, saying:

**3** 'We lay hands on the servant of God, who has been chosen in the Spirit, for the true and devout ordering of the Church of the invisible and living God, which alone has the rule and is not dissolved; and for the giving of true judgment and of divine and holy revelations, of divine gifts and of faithful teachings of the Trinity, by the cross, by the resurrection, by the incorruption, in the holy Church of God.'

**4** After this, let one bishop, at the bidding of the other bishops, lay hands on him, saying the words of his appointment, like this:

**5** *The Prayer of Ordination of a Bishop*

**6** 'O God, who made all things in power and established them, and founded the inhabited world in reason, and adorned the crown of all that You made; who gave them to keep Your commandments in fear; who gave us the understanding of the truth, and made known to us that good Spirit of Yours; who sent Your beloved Son, the only Savior, without spot, for our salvation: O God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, Father of compassion and God of all comfort, who live forever in the pure heights; who are high and worthy of worship, fearful and great; who see all things and know all things before they are, with whom all things were before they were made; who gave light to the Church by the grace of Your Only-begotten Son, having foreordained from the beginning those who delight in what is just and do what is holy, to live in Your dwellings; who chose Abraham, who pleased You by his faith, and took holy Enoch up to the treasure house of life; who have set princes and priests in order in Your sanctuary above: O Lord, who called them to praise and glorify Your name and the name of Your Only-begotten in the place of Your glory; O Lord God, who before the foundation of the world did not leave Your sanctuary above without a ministry, and since the foundation of the world have adorned and glorified Your sanctuaries on earth with faithful princes and priests, after the pattern of Your own heaven: You, Lord, are well pleased even now to be praised, and have granted that there be princes for Your people. Make shine and pour out understanding, and the grace that comes from Your princely Spirit, which You gave to Your beloved Son Jesus Christ. Give wisdom, O God, give reasoning, strength, power, unity of spirit, to do all things with Your help. Give the Spirit that is Yours, O holy God; send to Your holy and pure Church, and to every place that sings "Holy" to You, Him who was given to Your Holy One. Grant, O Lord, that this servant of Yours may please You in giving You glory and praise without ceasing, O God, in fitting hymns of praise and at suitable times, in acceptable prayers, in faithful asking, with an upright mind and a meek heart, in the working of life and of meekness and of truth, in the knowledge of uprightness. O Father, who know the hearts, grant to this servant of Yours, whom You have chosen to be bishop, to feed Your holy flock, and to stand at the head of the priesthood without fault, serving You day and night. Grant that Your face may be seen by him. Grant, O Lord, that he may offer You the offerings of Your holy Church carefully and with all fear. Give him to have Your powerful Spirit, to loose every bond, as You gave Him to Your apostles, so that he may please You in meekness. Fill him with love, knowledge, understanding, discipline, completeness, strength and a pure heart, when he prays for the people, and when he mourns for those who act foolishly and draws them to receive help, and when he offers You praises and thanksgivings and prayers as a soothing aroma, through Your beloved Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom praise and honor and might are given to You, with the Holy Spirit, before the ages, and now, and at all times, and forever and ever without end. Amen.'

**7** Let the people say, 'Amen,' and then let them cry out, 'He is worthy, he is worthy, he is worthy.'

**8** After he is ordained, let the people keep the feast three days, for the mystery that on the third day our

**9** Lord rose from the dead. Let everyone give him the Peace.

## Notes

- **v6 "Father of compassion and God of all comfort… took holy Enoch up"** — Paul's blessing, *the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of compassion and God of all comfort* (2 Corinthians 1:3); and Enoch, who *was taken up so that he did not see death* (Hebrews 11:5).
- **vv8–9 "three days… our / Lord rose"** — one sentence across two of the edition's paragraphs, kept as it divides them.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

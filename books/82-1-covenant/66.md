# 1st Book of the Covenant 66

**1** "Let the bishop command that it be proclaimed that no one is to taste anything until the Offering is completed. The whole body of the Church will receive a new food. Then in the evening let those who are to be baptized be baptized, after one reading.

**2** If anyone eats something else before he comes near and receives of the Eucharist, he sins, and his fast is not counted to him.

**3** When the catechumens are dismissed, let a hand be laid on them.

**4** If one of the faithful stays away because of sickness, let the deacon take the Offering to him.

**5** If it is a presbyter who cannot come, let a presbyter take it to him.

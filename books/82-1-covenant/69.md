# 1st Book of the Covenant 69

**1** "If a poor man dies, let those who provide for each one provide his clothing. If a stranger dies and has no place to be buried, let those who have a place give it; and if the Church has a place, let it give it. If he has no covering, let the Church give that too. If he has no grave clothes, let him be wrapped in a shroud.

**2** If a man is found to have possessions and does not leave them to the Church, let them be kept for a time; and after a year let the Church not take them for itself, but let them be given to the poor for his soul.

**3** If he wishes to be embalmed, let the deacons see to it, with a presbyter standing by.

**4** If the Church has a burial ground, and someone lives there and keeps it, let the bishop provide for him from the Church, so that he is no burden to those who come there.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 17

**1** Jesus also said to us, "Since you too have asked Me about the rule of the Church, I hand on and make known to you how you ought to order and instruct the one who stands at the head of the Church, and to keep the perfect, just and most excellent rule, in which My Father who sent Me is well pleased. Truly I tell you, whoever knows the power of this commandment and of this testament, and does what is written in it, will be made like the angels who praise My Father, and will be holy to God.

**2** My Father and all His host are mediators: if anyone's sins are like the sand of the seashore, which cannot be counted, and he understands these words and does them, those sins will be forgiven him, and he will live in Me.

---

## Notes

- **v2 "My Father and all His host are mediators"** — so the Syriac as Cooper and Maclean give it. Elsewhere the one mediator is the Son: *there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus* (1 Timothy 2:5).
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

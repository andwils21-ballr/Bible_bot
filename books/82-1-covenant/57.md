# 1st Book of the Covenant 57

**1** "If anyone receives a gift to take to a widow or a poor woman, or to anyone constantly busy in the work of the Church, let him give it the same day; and if not, then the next day, let him add something of his own to it and so give it. For the bread of the poor has been kept back in his hands.

**2** In the last week of Pascha, on the fifth day of the week, let the bread and the cup be offered. The one who suffered for what he has offered, he is the one who draws near.

**3** Let the lamp be brought into the temple by the deacon, saying, 'The grace of our Lord be with you all.' Let all the people say, 'And with your spirit.' Let the little boys say spiritual psalms and hymns of praise by the light of the lamp. Let all the people answer *Hallelujah* to the psalm and to the chant sung together, with one accord, with voices in harmony; and let no one kneel until the one speaking stops. So also when a reading is read or the word of teaching is spoken. When the name of the Lord is spoken, and the rest, as has been made clear enough, let no one bow who has come creeping in.

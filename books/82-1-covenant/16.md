# 1st Book of the Covenant 16

**1** Then Martha and Mary and Salome, who were with us, answered, "Yes, our Lord, teach us to know what we ought to do, so that our souls may live to You." Jesus answered them, "I want you to persevere in prayer and always serve My Gospel, and to be examples of holiness for the salvation of those who trust in Me patiently; and in all things to be figures of the kingdom of heaven."

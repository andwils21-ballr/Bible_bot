# 1st Book of the Covenant 8

**1** In the assemblies and the nations and the churches there will be many tumults, for evil shepherds will arise: unjust, lazy, greedy, lovers of pleasure, lovers of gain, lovers of money, talkative, boastful, arrogant, gluttonous, perverse, rash, given to indulgence, vain, opposing the ways of the Gospel and fleeing from the narrow gate, putting every humiliation far from themselves and not grieving for My humiliation, rejecting all the words of truth, despising all the ways of godliness, and not mourning for their sins. So unbelief will spread among the nations, and hatred of the brothers, wickedness, bitterness, laziness, envy, hatred, strife, theft, oppression, drunkenness, debauchery, lewdness, licentiousness, sexual immorality, and all such works as are contrary to the commandments of life. From many, mourning and gentleness will flee away, and peace and meekness, and poverty and godliness, and tears, because the shepherds heard these things and did not do them, and did not even show My commandments, since they themselves are examples of wickedness among the people. The time will come when some of them will deny Me, stir up confusion on the earth, and put their trust in a corruptible king. But those who endure in My name to the end will be saved. Then they will lay down commandments for men, commandments unlike the book of commandments in which the Father is well pleased; and My chosen ones and My holy ones will be rejected by them, and counted among them as the defiled. Yet these are the upright: pure, sorrowful, merciful, quiet, kind, knowing at all times Him who is among them; and they will be called mad for My sake, who have saved them. In those days My Father will gather out of that generation the pure, the pure and faithful souls, to whom I will appear and with whom I will make My dwelling. I will send them the understanding of knowledge and of truth, and the understanding of holiness, and they will not cease to praise and give thanks to their God, My Father who sent Me. They will speak the truth at all times, and they will teach others whose spirit they have tested and found upright and worthy, as for the kingdom, and will instruct them in knowledge and strength and prudence. Those who suffer persecution because they live in godliness will receive the reward of their praise. In those times all the kingdoms will be shaken, and all the world will see affliction and want; this whole world will be counted as nothing, and all its possessions will be destroyed by many destroyers. There will be a great scarcity of crops, and the winter will be very severe. The princes who rule over gold and silver and are rich in all that is in this world will be few and small; and the children of this world will hold the storerooms and barns, and will rule over the markets of buying and selling. Many will be afflicted, and so will call on their God to be delivered. Blessed are those who are not alive at that time; and blessed also are those who are alive and endure. For when these things come to pass, she who is in labor is near to giving birth, for the time is fulfilled.

## Notes

- **v1 "fleeing from the narrow gate… those who endure in My name to the end will be saved"** — the Lord's words in the Gospel: *How narrow is the gate and hard the road that leads to life* (Matthew 7:14), and *the one who endures to the end will be saved* (Matthew 24:13).
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

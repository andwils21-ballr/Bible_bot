# 1st Book of the Covenant 34

**1** "Let him carry out in the Church what is right. Let his ministry be like this. First, let him do only what the bishop commands, as for a proclamation. Let him be the counselor of the whole clergy and the mystery of the Church: one who serves the sick, serves strangers, helps the widows, is the father of the orphans, and goes around all the houses of those in need, so that no one is in affliction or sickness or misery. Let him go around the houses of the catechumens, to strengthen those who doubt and teach those who are untaught.

**2** Let him clothe the men who have died, preparing them; let him bury strangers, and guide those who leave their homes or go into captivity. For the help of those in need let him inform the Church. Let him not trouble the bishop, but only on the first day of the week report everything, so that he may know.

**3** Let him be watchful at the hour of the assembly, going around the church, and see that no one there is proud, or a buffoon, or a spy, or one who speaks idle words. Let him rebuke such a one in the sight and hearing of all, and put out the one he has judged deserving of punishment, so that the others also may fear. If the offender persuades him to let him partake, let him comfort him. But if the man persists in his wrongdoing or disorder, let him report him to the bishop, and let him be kept apart seven days and then called, so that he is not taken captive. If, when he comes, he still goes on and persists in his folly, let him be cut off until, truly repenting, he comes to himself and asks to be received back.

**4** If he is in a city by the sea, let him go quickly around the places on the shore, in case anyone has died in the sea; let him clothe him and bury him. Let him also search the guest house in the same way, in case anyone staying there is sick or in need or dead, and let him make it known to the Church, so that it may provide what is right for each one. Let him have the paralyzed and the infirm bathed as is right, so that they may have relief from their pains. Let him give each one what is right through the Church.

**5** In the Church let twelve presbyters, seven deacons, fourteen subdeacons and thirteen widows who sit in front be recognized.

**6** Of the deacons, let the one who is thought most earnest among them, and best at governing, be chosen to receive strangers. Let him always be at the guest house that is in the church, dressed in white, with a stole only on his shoulder.

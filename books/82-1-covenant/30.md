# 1st Book of the Covenant 30

**1** "Then let the appointment of the presbyter be like this. With all the priestly company leading him, the bishop laying his hand on his head and the presbyters touching him and holding him, let the bishop begin and say this:

**2** *The Prayer of Ordination of a Presbyter*

**3** 'O God, Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the One beyond words, the Light, who have neither beginning nor end, the Lord, who ordered all things and set their bounds, and by reason defined the order of all that You created: hear us, and look on this servant of Yours, and make him share in, and give him, the Spirit of grace and of reason and of strength, the Spirit of the presbyterate, who does not grow old and cannot be dissolved, of one kind, loving the faithful, rebuking, so that he may help and govern Your people with labor, with fear, with a pure heart, with holiness, with excellence, with wisdom, and by the working of the Holy Spirit, through Your care, O Lord. As, when You looked on Your chosen people, You commanded Moses to ask for the elders, and filling them with Your Spirit You gave Him to Your minister, so now, O Lord, give this man in abundance Your Spirit, whom You gave to those You Yourself made disciples, and to all who through them truly believed in You. Make him worthy, filled with Your wisdom and Your hidden mysteries, to feed Your people in holiness of heart, pure and true; praising, blessing, extolling, giving thanks, always lifting up glory day and night to Your holy and glorious name; laboring with cheerfulness and patience to be a vessel of Your Holy Spirit; always having and carrying the cross of Your Only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom be praise and might to You with the Holy Spirit forever and ever.'

**4** Let both the priests and the people give him the Peace, with a holy kiss.

---

## Notes

- **v3 "You commanded Moses to ask for the elders, and filling them with Your Spirit"** — the seventy elders in the wilderness: *Gather for Me seventy men of the elders of Israel… I will take some of the Spirit that is on you and put it on them* (Numbers 11:16–17). The presbyter, the Church's *elder*, is ordained on that pattern.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

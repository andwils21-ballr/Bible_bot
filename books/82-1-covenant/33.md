# 1st Book of the Covenant 33

**1** "The deacon is appointed, chosen as was said before: if he is of good conduct, if he is pure, if he has been chosen for purity and for freedom from distractions; if not, then if he is the husband of one wife, with all the faithful bearing witness to him, not entangled in the business of the world, not knowing a trade, without riches, without children. If he is married or has children, let his children be taught to practice devotion and to be pure, so that they may be approved by the Church according to the rule of the ministry. Let the Church take care of them, so that they may persevere in the law and in the work of the ministry.

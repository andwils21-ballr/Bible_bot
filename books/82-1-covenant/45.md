# 1st Book of the Covenant 45

**1** "A reader is appointed who is pure, quiet, meek, wise, of much experience, learned and well read, with a good memory, watchful, so that he may deserve a higher rank. First let the book be given him in the sight of the people, on the first day of the week. A hand is not laid on him, but he hears this from the bishop:

**2** 'You, N., whom Christ has called to be a minister of His words, take care, and strive to be found approved both in this rule and in a higher rank, by our Lord Jesus Christ Himself, so that He may pay you a good reward for these things in His everlasting dwellings.'

**3** Let the priests say, 'So be it, so be it, so be it.'

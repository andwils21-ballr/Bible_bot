# 1st Book of the Covenant 27

**1** "After this let the prayer be completed, and then let the reader read the Prophets and the rest. Let the presbyter or deacon read the Gospel, and then let the bishop or presbyter teach what is fitting and profitable. After that let there be a prayer, and let the catechumens receive a laying on of the hand.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 36

**1** "Let the deacon be such a man that he appears with fear, modesty and reverence. In fervor of spirit let him have a complete way of life. Let him watch and look at those who enter the house of the sanctuary, and find out who they are, so that he may know whether they are lambs or wolves. When he asks, let him bring in the one who is worthy; otherwise, if a spy enters, the freedom of the Church will be searched out, and his sin will be on his head.

**2** If anyone comes late to the praise, either when the praise of the dawn is being said or when the Offering is being offered, whoever he is, let him stay outside, and let the deacon not bring him in. It is a type of the day of judgment to come, and the noise of his entering would distract those who are praying. When he comes and finds the door shut, let him not knock, because of what has been said.

**3** After the hymn of praise that comes first is finished, let the faithful man or faithful woman enter. Let the deacon say, either over the Offering or for the hymn of praise, 'Let us pray that the Lord may write our prayer in the book of life, and that God, who is forever, may remember us in His holy dwellings of light. For this brother who is late, let us pray that the Lord may give him earnestness and labor, turn away from him every bond of this world, and give him the will of affection and love and hope.'

**4** Likewise for a sister or a deaconess, for those who are late or stay outside, let him urge all the people to pray for them. For when a deacon names them and urges this, earnestness is strengthened, the bond of love is fulfilled, and the scorner and the slothful are disciplined.

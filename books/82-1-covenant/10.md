# 1st Book of the Covenant 10

**1** Syria will be plundered and will weep for her sons. Cilicia will lift up her neck until He who judges her appears. The daughter of Babylon will rise from the throne of her glory, to drink the wine that is mixed for her. Cappadocia, Lycia and Lycaonia will bow their backs, for many multitudes will be corrupted by the decay of their wickedness. Then the camps of the barbarians will be opened, and many chariots will go out, so as to cover the face of the earth. In all Armenia, and in Pontus and in Bithynia, the young men will fall by the sword, and the sons and the daughters will be taken captive. The sons and daughters of Lycaonia will be mingled in their blood. Pisidia, which boasts and trusts in her riches, will be thrown down to the ground. The sword will pass through Phoenicia, because her people are children of corruption. Judea will clothe herself in mourning, and will be made ready for the day of destruction, because of her uncleanness. Then she will gather together the abomination of desolation. The East will be opened by him, and the roads will be opened by him. Sword and flame are in his hands; he burns with anger and fiery rage. This is the armor of the judgment of the corruption of those born on the earth: the destruction of the faithful, the way of bloodshed. For his way is in error, his power is to blaspheme, his hand is for deceit, his right hand in misery, and his left hand in darkness.

## Notes

- **v1 "the daughter of Babylon… to drink the wine that is mixed for her"** — Revelation's word on Babylon, *in the cup she mixed, mix her a double portion* (Revelation 18:6).
- **v1 "the abomination of desolation"** — the words of the Gospel, *when you see the abomination of desolation, spoken of through Daniel the prophet, standing in the holy place* (Matthew 24:15).
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 39

**1** "If anyone is attested and confesses that he was in chains and in prison and in afflictions for the name of God, a hand is not laid on him for the diaconate on that account, nor for the presbyterate. He has the honor of the clergy, having been protected by the hand of God through his confession. But if he is appointed bishop, he is also counted worthy of the laying on of the hand. Even if he is a confessor who has not been judged before the authorities and has not been beaten in chains, but has only confessed, he is counted worthy of the laying on of the hand, for he receives the prayer of the clergy. But let the bishop not pray over him repeating all these words; when the shepherd goes forward, he will receive the effect.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 9

**1** Then the son of destruction will come, the Adversary, who boasts and exalts himself, working many signs and wonders, so that he may deceive the whole earth and overcome the innocent, My holy ones. Blessed are those who endure in those days. But woe to those who are deceived.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 18

## My Mysteries for My Own

**1** "But because among the gathered people there are more and more fleshly desires, and the workers are weak and few, only My perfect workers will know My words in their fullness, all that I spoke to you at times in private before I suffered, which you know: you have them and understand them. For My mysteries are given to those who are Mine, with whom I will rejoice and be glad with My Father. When they are set free from this life, they will come to Me.

**2** But speak the rest of these words in the churches, setting them out and appointing them.

**3** From the day My faithful ones also desire to know whatever is in this testament of Mine, so that they may do the things of My Father, I will be with them and be praised among them, and I will make My dwelling with them, teaching them by power the will of My Father.

**4** See that you do not give My holy things to the dogs, or throw pearls before pigs, as I have often commanded you.

**5** Do not give My holy things to defiled and wicked people who do not carry My cross and are not subject to Me; My commandments would be mocked among them. For the one who is embittered, who does not do them but gives My words away to no profit, it will be the destruction of their souls.

**6** But it will be spoken and given to those who are firm and settled and do not fall away, who keep My commandments and this tradition, so that, keeping them, they may remain holy and upright and strong in Me, fleeing from the downfall of wickedness and the death of sin. The Holy Spirit also gives them His grace, so that they may believe rightly, may know spiritually, in the Spirit, the things of the Spirit, may endure labor in hope, serve My Gospel in joy, and bear the reproach of My cross, not doubting but rather glorying. For truly I tell you, such men and such women as these will live, after death, in the third order of My Father who sent Me.

## The Church Building

**7** I tell you, then, how the sanctuary ought to be; then I will make known the holy rule of the priests of the Church.

**8** Let the church, then, be like this: let it have three entrances, as a type of the Trinity.

**9** Let the deacons' room be to the right of the right-hand entrance, so that the eucharists, the offerings that are brought, may be seen. Let there be a forecourt, with a portico running around it, up to the deacons' room.

**11** Then inside the forecourt let there be a place for a baptistery, twenty-one cubits long, as a type of all the prophets, and twelve cubits wide, as a type of those appointed to preach the Gospel, with one entrance and three exits.

**12** Let the church have a house for the catechumens, which will also be the house of the exorcists. Let it not stand apart from the church, but be placed so that those who enter it and are in it may hear the readings, the spiritual hymns of praise and the psalms.

**13** Let there be a throne by the altar, with the places of the presbyters on the right and on the left: on the right let those sit who are more exalted and honored, and those who labor in the word, and on the left those of middle age. Let the place where the throne is be raised three steps, for the altar ought to be there.

**14** Let that house have two porches, on the right and on the left, for men and for women.

**15** Let all the places be lit, both as a type and for reading.

**16** Let the altar have a veil of pure linen, for it is without spot.

**17** Let the baptistery likewise be under a veil.

**18** Let a place be built for remembrance, so that the priest and the chief deacon, sitting with the readers, may write the names of those who bring the offerings, or of those for whom they have brought them, so that when the holy things are offered by the bishop, the reader or the chief deacon may name them in remembrance, which the priests and the people offer for them with prayer. For this type is in heaven too.

**19** Let the place of the presbyters be inside the veil, beside the place of remembrance.

**20** Let the house of the offering and the treasury be right beside the deacons' room.

**21** But let the place of reading be a little outside the altar.

**22** Let the bishop's house be beside the place called the forecourt,

**23** and that of the widows who are called 'those who sit in front.'

**24** Let that of the presbyters and deacons be behind the baptistery.

**25** Let the deaconesses stay beside the door of the Lord's house.

**26** Let the church have a guest house nearby, where the chief deacon will receive strangers.

## Notes

- **v6 "know spiritually, in the Spirit, the things of the Spirit"** — Paul's words: *The natural person does not accept the things of the Spirit of God… because they are judged spiritually* (1 Corinthians 2:14).
- **v7** — the heading of Cooper and Maclean's chapter 19 cannot be read in the scan held here, so its text stands with chapter 18. Where chapter 19 begins is not certain; the turn from the testament's words (vv1–6) to the building of the church (v7) is the likeliest place.
- **v10** — the paragraph numbered 10 in the text held here is the edition's footnotes to vv5–6, not part of the text, so no verse stands there.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

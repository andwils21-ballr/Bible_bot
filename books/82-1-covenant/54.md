# 1st Book of the Covenant 54

**1** "In the forty days of Pascha, let the people stay in the —

**2** When he takes hold of each one, let him ask, the one being baptized turning to the west, and let him say, 'Say: I renounce you, Satan, and all your service, your shows, your pleasures and all your works.' When he has said these things and confessed, let him be anointed with the oil that was exorcised, the one who anoints him saying, 'I anoint you with this oil of exorcism, for deliverance from every evil and unclean spirit and from every evil.' Then, turning him to the east, let him say, 'Say: I submit to You, Father and Son and Holy Spirit, before whom all nature trembles and is moved. Grant me to do all Your will without blame.'

**3** After these things let him hand him over to the presbyter who baptizes, and let them stand in the water naked, and let the deacon go down with him likewise. When the one being baptized goes down into the water, let the one who baptizes him put his hand on him and say, 'Do you believe in God the Father Almighty?' Let the one being baptized say, 'I believe.' Let him at once baptize him once. Let the priest also say, 'Do you believe also in Christ Jesus the Son of God, who came from the Father, who is of old with the Father, who was born of Mary the Virgin by the Holy Spirit, who was crucified in the days of Pontius Pilate, and died, and rose the third day, came to life from the dead, ascended into heaven, sat down at the right hand of the Father, and is coming to judge the living and the dead?' When he says, 'I believe,' let him baptize him the second time. Let him also say, 'Do you believe also in the Holy Spirit, in the holy Church?' Let the one being baptized say, 'I believe,' and then let him baptize him the third time.

**4** Then, when he comes up, let him be anointed by the presbyter with oil over which the thanksgiving has been said —

## Notes

- **vv1, 4** — both break off in the text held here: v1 at *in the*, after which the baptism itself begins (v2), and v4 at the anointing, after which the next chapter has the bishop's laying on of hands. The breaks are kept.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

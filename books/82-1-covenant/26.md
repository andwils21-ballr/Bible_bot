# 1st Book of the Covenant 26

## The First Hymn of the Dawn

**1** "At early dawn let the bishop gather the people, so that the service may be finished before the sun rises.

**2** When he says the First Hymn of Praise, of the Dawn, with the presbyters and deacons and the rest, and the faithful too, standing close by, let him say this:

**3** Let the people say, 'It is fitting and right.'

**4** The bishop: 'It is fitting and right that we should praise and extol and give thanks to You, who made all things, God beyond words. Stretching our souls upward, we raise to You, O Lord, a hymn of praise for the morning: to You who are all-wise, powerful, great in mercies, O God, Strengthener and Raiser-up of our souls. We praise You, the Word, begotten of the Father before the ages, who rest alone with Your saints, who are praised with the hymns of the archangels; You the Maker, who were not made with hands, who make known holy things that are invisible, pure and spotless; You who have made known to us the hidden mysteries of wisdom, and promised us immortal light. We lift up praise to You in pure holiness, we Your servants, O Lord.'

**5** Let the people say, 'We praise You, we bless You, we give thanks to You, O Lord; and we pray to You, O our God.'

**6** The bishop also: 'O God, Begetter of light, Source of life, Giver of knowledge, Gift of grace, Maker of souls, who make what is beautiful, Giver of the Holy Spirit, Treasure of wisdom and Maker of good things, the Lord, Teacher of holiness, who rule the ages by Your will, Receiver of pure prayers: we praise You, the Only-begotten Son, the Firstborn and Word of the Father, who gave all Your grace to us who call on You, the Helper, and on the Father who begot You; whose being cannot be harmed, where neither moth nor worm destroys; who give to all who trust in You with all their heart the things the angels have longed to look into; Guardian of eternal light and of treasures that do not decay; who by the will of Your Father have shed light on the darkness in us; who raised us from the depth to light; who gave us life out of death, and freedom out of slavery; who by the cross made us members of Your Father's household, by Your gospel guided us to the heights of heaven, and comforted us by Your prophets; who in Your own person made us members of the household of God the Father of lights: grant us, O Lord, to praise You, our God, so that we may always speak Your praise with unceasing thanksgiving, we Your servants, O Lord.'

**7** The people: 'We praise You, we bless You, we give thanks to You, we pray to You, O our God.'

**8** The bishop also: 'We sing to You with our mouths this threefold hymn of praise, as a figure of Your kingdom, O Son of God, who are from eternity; who are above all, with the Father; whom all creation praises, trembling with fear of Your Spirit; before whom all nature trembles in fear, and whom every soul of the righteous blesses; in whom all of us have taken refuge; who have made confusion, storms and wind cease from us; who have been to us a haven of rest and a refuge from corruption; in whom we hope for eternal salvation; who give calm weather to those tossed on the seas and in the storms; who are called upon in sickness and heal without price; who are with those shut up in prison; who loosed us from the bonds of death; Comforter of the poor, of those who mourn, and of those who have labored and worn themselves out with the cross; who turn away every threat from us; who have rebuked the cunning of Satan for us; who drive away his threats and give us courage; who thrust all error away from those who trust in You; whom the prophets and apostles praised in secret: we praise You, O Lord, we lift up glory to You, so that, having known You, we may rest in the dwellings of life, always doing Your will. Grant us, O Lord, to walk by Your commandments, and in mercy visit us all, small and great, the prince and his people, the shepherd and his flock; for You, O Lord, are our God, and blessed and praised is Your kingdom, the kingdom of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, before the ages, and now, and always, and for the ages, and forever and ever without end.'

**9** Let the people say, 'Amen.'

**10** Let them sing psalms and four hymns of praise: one by Moses, and of Solomon, and of the other prophets; like this: little singing boys; two virgins; three deacons; three presbyters. Let the hymn of praise then be said by the bishop or by one of the presbyters.

## The Hymn of the Seal

**11** Let it be said like this: 'The grace of our Lord be with you all.' Let the people say, 'And with your spirit.'

**12** Let the priest say, 'Let us also praise our Lord.'

**13** Let the people say, 'It is fitting and right.' Let the priest say, 'Let your hearts be steadfast.' Let the people say, 'We hold them steadfast with the Lord.'

**14** *The Hymn of Praise of the Seal.*

**15** 'O Lord, the Father, Giver of light, Author of all power and of all spirits, Sealer of eternal light and Guide of life, Maker of happiness and immortality, who brought us through the darkness of matter and gave us light that is not of matter; who loosed the bonds of disobedience and crowned us with the faith that is Yours; who do not keep far from Your servants, but are in them always; who do not neglect the souls that pray to You with labor and in Your fear; who know all things before they are thought, search out all things before they are considered, and give what You will give before we ask You; who are well pleased to hear those who serve You with an undoubting heart, O King of the highest lights and of the army of heaven, who hear the archangels when they praise You, and are pleased with them: answer us, O Lord, we pray You. Grant us with boldness, with unceasing voice, to praise You, to extol You, to lift up glory to You, so that, guarded by You and guided in light, we Your servants, O Lord, may praise You always.'

**16** The people: 'We praise You, we bless You, we give thanks to You, O Lord; and we pray to You, O our God.'

**17** The priest: 'O Lord Jesus, hear us, O Holy One, who were the Voice of the mute and of those without reason, the Strength of the paralyzed, Giver of light to the blind, Guide of the lame, Cleanser of the lepers, Healer of bodily flows, Healer of the deaf and mute, Rebuker of death, Tormentor of darkness, the Ray of light, the Lamp that is not put out, the Sun that is not darkened and does not rest, but always gives light to Your saints; who have fitted all things together in a fair likeness of beauty; who are the well-tempered Reason; who have given light openly to all; Savior of the sons of men and Turner of souls; who provide all things as is right, Maker of the angels, who adorn all; the Thought of the Father; who founded the ages in prudence and wisdom and fitted them together, and were sent to us from Your eternal Father; the Understanding of the Spirit, who cannot be grasped or understood, the Revealer of things invisible: You are glorious, and Your name is Wonderful. So we also, Your servants, O Lord, give You praise.'

**18** The people: 'We praise You, we bless You, we give thanks to You, O Lord; we pray to You, O our Lord.'

**19** The priest: 'We sing, O holy Lord, this threefold hymn of praise to You, who gave us a faith in You that cannot be loosed, by which You made us conquer the bonds of death; who created upright minds in those who trust in You, so that they might be gods; who by the Spirit gave us to tread underfoot all the power of the enemy, so that we may not profane what may not be profaned; who by Your mediation have made friendship for us with Your Father. Answer us, Your servants, O Lord, whom we entreat without ceasing, who at our prayer give us power against the adversary, whom we ask always for the overthrow of the Evil One. Hear us, O King Eternal: comfort the widows, help the orphans, have pity on and cleanse those who are possessed by unclean spirits, give wisdom to the unwise, turn back those who go astray, deliver those who are in prison, guard us all; for You, O Lord, are our God; blessed and glorious is Your kingdom.'

## Notes

- **v19 "so that they might be gods"** — the psalm Jesus quotes, *Is it not written in your Law, "I said, you are gods"?* (John 10:34; Psalm 82:6). Here it is what the Son does in *those who trust in You*, and in the same breath the prayer says He *made friendship for us with Your Father*.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

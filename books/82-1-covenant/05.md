# 1st Book of the Covenant 5

**1** A king of foreign race will also arise in the West, a prince of great cunning, godless, a murderer, a deceiver, a lover of gold, great at schemes, a hater of the faithful, a persecutor; he will also rule over barbarous nations and will shed much blood. At that time silver will be despised and gold honored; and in every city and every country there will be plunder and robbery and the shedding of blood.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 53

**1** "On the Sabbath let the bishop gather those who are to receive the washing, and have them kneel while the deacon makes the proclamation. When there is silence, let him exorcise them, laying a hand on them and saying:

**2** 'O God of heaven, God of the lights, God of the archangels who are under Your power, God of the angels who are under Your might, King of glories and of dominions, God of the saints, Father of our Lord Jesus Christ; who loosed the souls bound by death; who gave light to the one bound in darkness and made him firm through the firmness of the suffering of Your Only-begotten; who loosed our cords and lifted every weight from us; who drove back from us every attack of the Evil One; O Son and Word of God, who made us immortal by Your death, who glorified us with Your glory, who loosed all the bonds of our sins by Your passion, who bore the curse of our sins by Your cross, and by Your resurrection taught mankind to pass from being sons of men to becoming gods; who took our humiliation on Yourself, who trod the way to heaven for us, who changed us from corruption to incorruption: hear me, O Lord, who cry to You in pain and fear, O Lord God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, before Him before whom stand the holy hosts of archangels and of cherubim and armies without number, of princes and of seraphim; whose veil is the light, and before whose face is fire; the throne of whose glory is beyond words; the dwellings of whose delights, which You have prepared for Your saints, are beyond words, whose robes and treasures are seen by You alone and by Your holy angels; before whom all things tremble, giving praise; whose glance measures the mountains, and whose name, when it is spoken, splits the depths; whom the heavens, shut up by Your hand, hide from view; before whom the earth and the depths together tremble; before whom the sea and the dragons in it quake; before whom the wild beasts stand trembling in awe; through whom the mountains and the foundations of the earth melt with fear; at whose power the winter storm quakes and trembles, and the raging whirlwind keeps its limits; because of whom the fire of vengeance does not pass the bound set for it, but stays when rebuked by Your commandment; because of whom the whole creation is in labor, groaning, bidden to wait until its time; from whom all nature and creation that sets itself against You flees; because of whom the whole army of the adversary is subdued, the Devil fallen, the serpent trodden down and the dragon killed; because of whom the nations that have confessed You are given light and strengthened in You, O Lord; because of whom life is revealed, hope made firm, faith strengthened and the Gospel preached; because of whom corruption is brought to nothing and incorruption grows strong; through whom man was formed from the earth, but, having believed in You, is no longer earth: O Lord God Almighty, I exorcise these in the name of You and of Your beloved Son Jesus Christ.

**3** Drive away from the souls of these servants of Yours every disease and illness, every stumbling block and all unbelief, all doubt and all contempt, every unclean spirit that is at work: that is a witch, that kills, that is under the earth, that is fiery, dark, foul-smelling, given to witchcraft, lustful, gold-loving, proud, money-loving, wrathful.

**4** Yes, O Lord God, overthrow from these servants of Yours, who have been named in You, the weapons of the Devil: all magic, witchcraft, fear of idols, divination, astrology, necromancy, watching the stars, stargazing, pleasure in the passions, love of what is shameful, sadness, love of money, drunkenness, fornication, adultery, lust, defiance, quarreling, wrath, confusion, wickedness, evil suspicion. Yes, O Lord God, hear me, and breathe on these servants of Yours the spirit of calm, so that, guarded by You, they may bear in You the fruits of faith, of virtue, of wisdom, of purity, of self-discipline, of patience, of hope, of concord, of modesty, of praise. For by You they have been called as servants, in the name of Jesus Christ, being baptized in the Trinity, in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, with the angels, glories, dominions and all the army of heaven as witnesses. O Lord, the true being of our life and theirs, guard their hearts, O God, for You are mighty and glorious forever and ever.'

**5** Let all the people, and the priests too, say, 'Amen. So be it, so be it, so be it.'

**6** If anyone who is enduring anything rises suddenly while the bishop is saying these words, and weeps or cries out, or foams at the mouth or grinds his teeth, or stares, or is greatly lifted up, or runs away altogether, quickly carried off, let him be put aside by the deacons, so that there is no disturbance while the bishop is speaking. Let him be exorcised by the priests until he is cleansed, and then let him be baptized.

**7** After the priest has exorcised those who have drawn near, or the one found unclean, let him breathe on them and seal them between the eyes, on the nose, on the heart and on the ears; and then let him raise them up.

## Notes

- **v2 "the whole creation is in labor, groaning, bidden to wait until its time"** — Paul's words: *the whole creation has been groaning together in the pains of childbirth until now* (Romans 8:22). In the same prayer the Son teaches mankind *to pass from being sons of men to becoming gods*, as the morning praise says of those who trust in Him (26:19).
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

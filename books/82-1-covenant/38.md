# 1st Book of the Covenant 38

**1** "Let the appointment of a deacon be like this. Let the bishop alone lay a hand on him, because he is appointed not to the priesthood but to the service of attending on the bishop and the Church. Over the deacon, then, let the bishop say this:

**2** *The Prayer of Ordination of a Deacon*

**3** 'O God, who created all things and adorned them by the Word; who rest in the pure ages; who served us eternal life through Your prophets; who lit us with the light of knowledge; O God, who do great things, Maker of all glory, Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, whom You sent to serve Your will, so that the whole human race might be saved; who made known and revealed to us Your Thought, Your Wisdom, Your Power, Your beloved Son Jesus Christ, the Lord of light, the Prince of princes and God of gods: give the spirit of grace and earnestness to this servant of Yours, so that he may be given earnestness, quiet, strength and power to please You. Give him, O Lord, to be a worker in the law without shame, kind, a lover of orphans, a lover of the devout, a lover of widows, fervent in spirit, a lover of good things; and —

---

## Notes

- **v3 "a worker in the law without shame"** — Paul to Timothy, *a worker who has no need to be ashamed, rightly handling the word of truth* (2 Timothy 2:15), the same words the deacon prays for the bishop (35:19). The prayer breaks off at *and* in the text held here, and the next chapter turns to the confessors; the break is kept.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

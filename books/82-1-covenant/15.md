# 1st Book of the Covenant 15

**1** After Jesus had spoken these words, Peter and John and Thomas and Matthew and Andrew and Matthias and the rest said, "Our Lord, truly You have spoken words of warning and of truth to us now as well. Though we are not worthy, You have given us many things, and You have granted to those of future generations who are worthy to know Your words and to flee from the snares of the Evil One. But, our Lord, we ask You: make Your perfect light shine on us, and on those who are foreordained and set apart to be Yours. Because we have asked You many times, we pray You, teach us what kind of man the one who stands at the head of the Church ought to be, and by what rule he should raise up and order the Church. For it is urgent, when we are sent to the nations to preach the salvation that comes from You, that we do not miss how the mysteries of the Church ought to be arranged. So we want to learn from Your mouth, our Savior and Perfecter, leaving nothing out, how the chief of the holy things ought to please You, and likewise all who minister in Your Church."

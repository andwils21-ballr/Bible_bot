# 1st Book of the Covenant 48

**1** "If a prostitute, or a brothel keeper, or a drunkard, or a maker of idols, or a painter, or one who works in the shows, or a charioteer, or a wrestler, or one who goes to the contests, or a fighter in the games, or one who teaches wrestling, or a public huntsman, or a priest of idols or a keeper of them is among those who come, let him not be received.

**2** If any such person wishes to become faithful, let him give these things up; and once he is faithful in deed and baptized, let him be received and let him partake. If he does not give them up, let him be rejected.

**3** If anyone is a teacher of boys in worldly wisdom, it is good if he gives it up. But if he has no other trade to live by, let him be excused.

**4** If anyone is a soldier or in authority, let him be taught not to oppress or to kill or to rob, or to be angry or to rage at anyone and afflict him, but to be content with the rations given him. But if they wish to be baptized in the Lord, let them give up military service or the post of authority; if not, let them not be received.

**5** If a catechumen or a believer of the people wishes to be a soldier, let him either give up his intention or be rejected. For he has despised God in his thought; leaving the things of the Spirit, he has made himself complete in the flesh and treated the faith with contempt.

**6** If a prostitute or a dissolute man or a drunkard stops doing these things and, believing, wishes to become a catechumen, they may be admitted. If they make progress, let them be baptized; if not, let them be rejected.

**7** If a man's concubine is a slave and wishes to be faithful, if she brings up the children born to her and separates from her master, or is joined to him alone in marriage, let her hear; and once baptized let her share in the Offering. If not, let her be rejected.

**8** One who does things that may not be spoken of, or a diviner, or a magician, or a necromancer: these are defiled and do not come to judgment. Let a charmer, or an astrologer, or an interpreter of dreams, or a sorcerer, or one who gathers the people together, or a stargazer, or a diviner by idols either stop, and when he stops let him be exorcised and baptized; or if not, let him be rejected.

**9** If a man has a concubine, let him put her away, marry according to the law, and hear the word of instruction.

---

## Notes

- **v4 "content with the rations given him"** — John the Baptist to the soldiers: *Do not extort money from anyone by force or by false accusation, and be content with your wages* (Luke 3:14). Here John's rule is the rule for a soldier who is learning; one who wishes to be baptized must leave the army.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

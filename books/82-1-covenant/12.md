# 1st Book of the Covenant 12

**1** That is why I tell you, children of the light, that the time is at hand, and the harvest is ripe for sinners to be harvested in judgment. To many the Judge will rise as one who is kind, and He will count their own works to them. But when He is at hand, a sign will be given to the chosen, who keep the law of My Father.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "the harvest is ripe"** — the angel's cry in Revelation, *the hour to reap has come, because the harvest of the earth is ripe* (Revelation 14:15).
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

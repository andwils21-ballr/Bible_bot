# 1st Book of the Covenant 4

**1** There will, then, be signs such as these when the kingdom is approaching. After the famines and pestilences and tumults among the nations, princes will rule and rise to power who love money, who hate the truth, who kill their brothers: liars, haters of the faithful, proud, lovers of gold, joined by kinship but not joined in counsel, for each wants to destroy the life of his fellow. In their armies there will be great affliction, and flight, and bloodshed.

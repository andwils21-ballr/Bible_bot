# 1st Book of the Covenant 61

**1** "If anyone departs from the world, a faithful man or a faithful woman, who has children, let them give their possessions to the Church, so that the Church may provide for their children, and from what they have the poor may be given rest, so that God may give mercy to their children and rest to those who have left them behind. If a man has no children, let him not have many possessions, but give much of what he has to the poor and the prisoners, and keep only what is right and enough for himself. If a man has children and wishes to discipline himself in virginity, let him give all his possessions to the poor, discipline himself, and stay in the church, constant in prayer and thanksgiving.

# 1st Book of the Covenant 29

**1** "Let a presbyter be ordained with all the people bearing witness to him, as was said before: skilled in reading, meek, poor, not a lover of money, one who has labored much in serving the weak, proved to be pure, without blame; if he has been as a father to the orphans, if he has served the poor, if he has not grown cold in his love for the Church, if he is devout and quiet in all things, so that, being such a man, he may in every way be worthy to have what is fitting and suitable revealed to him by God, and may also be counted worthy of the gift of healing.

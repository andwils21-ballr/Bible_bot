# 1st Book of the Covenant 71

**1** "When you, the faithful, carry out these things, teach and instruct one another, helping the catechumens make progress, as those who love all people. You do not perish, but will be in Me, and I will be among you.

**2** Let the faithful always take care to partake of the Eucharist before he eats, so that he cannot be harmed.

**3** When you teach these things and keep them, you will be saved, and evil heresy will not prevail against you.

**4** So I have now taught you all that you desire; and what I have spoken of with you from the beginning, and taught and commanded you before I suffered, you know.

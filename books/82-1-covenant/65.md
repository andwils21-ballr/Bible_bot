# 1st Book of the Covenant 65

**1** *Of the Deacons Who Go and Pass Among the Women, So That There Are No Disorderly Young Children*

**2** "Let the readers help them, and the subdeacons likewise. Let them keep them from sleeping; for that night is a figure of the kingdom, above all the night of the Sabbath.

**3** Let those who labor and work keep at their work until midnight.

**4** Let the catechumens be dismissed first, having received blessings from the bread that is broken.

**5** When the faithful are dismissed, let them go to their houses in order and with knowledge. At their feasts let them not forget the prayers.

**6** Let the priests not cut short their ministries.

**7** Let the women go, each one keeping close to her husband.

**8** Let the widows stay in the temple until dawn, having food there.

**9** Let the virgins stay together in the temple, and let the bishop help and provide for them, and the deacons serve them.

**10** Let the presbyteresses stay with the bishop until dawn, praying and resting.

**11** So also those who were lately baptized.

**12** Let the virgins who are ready for marriage go, keeping close to their mothers. This is fitting.

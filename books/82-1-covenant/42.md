# 1st Book of the Covenant 42

**1** "After she is appointed like this, let her not be anxious about anything, but live alone and be free for devout prayer. For the foundation of holiness and life for such a widow is solitude, for she has loved no one but the God of gods, the Father who is in heaven. At the fixed times let her give praise by herself, at night and at dawn. If she is in her monthly period, let her stay in the temple and not come near the altar, not as though she were unclean, but so that the altar may have honor. Afterward, when she has fasted and bathed, let her be constant at the altar. In the days of Pentecost let her not fast. At the feast of Pascha let her give what she has to the poor, bathe, and then pray. When she gives thanks or praise, if she has friends, virgins of like mind, it is good that they pray with her for the sake of the Amen; if not, let her pray alone, in the church and in the house, above all at midnight. The times when she should give praise are the Sabbath, the first day of the week, Pascha, Epiphany and Pentecost. At other times let her give thanks meekly with psalms, hymns of praise and meditations, and labor like this. For the Most High will sanctify them and forgive all their sins, those that were written against them before, and their error. My Father, the Heavenly One, will strengthen them and make their faces shine like the faces of

**2** My sanctuaries; they will shine in My glory on the day of reward.

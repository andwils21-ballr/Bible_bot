# 1st Book of the Covenant 73

**1** Falling down, we worshiped Him, saying, "Glory to You, O Jesus, Name of light, who gave us the teaching of Your commandments, so that we may be like You, we and all who hear You." When He had spoken to us, and taught and commanded us, and shown many deliverances and miracles, He was taken up from us, giving us calm.

**2** John and Peter and Matthew wrote this Testament and sent it in copies from Jerusalem by Dositheus and Silas and Magnus and Aquila, whom they chose to send it to all the dioceses. Amen.

**3** The Second Book of Clement is ended, translated from Greek into Syriac by James the poor, in the year 998 of the Greeks.

---

## Notes

- **v3 "in the year 998 of the Greeks"** — the Syriac translator's closing line. *The years of the Greeks* count from 312 BC, so the year is AD 686–687. The line belongs to the Syriac, the sister version this book is worked from.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

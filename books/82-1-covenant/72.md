# 1st Book of the Covenant 72

**1** "You especially, John, and Andrew and Peter: even now you know all that I have spoken to you while I am with you, and what is in this Testament, so that when you hand it on to the nations the will of My Father may always be done, standing firm in care, so that there may be good fruit in those who hear.

**2** You know that I have told you a good tree cannot bear bad fruit. So do all that I have commanded you, openly and in secret. The God of calm be with you."

---

## Notes

- **v2 "a good tree cannot bear bad fruit"** — the Lord's words in the Sermon on the Mount, *A good tree cannot bear bad fruit, and a rotten tree cannot bear good fruit* (Matthew 7:18), which He here reminds them He has spoken.
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

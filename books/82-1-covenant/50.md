# 1st Book of the Covenant 50

**1** "When the teacher stops, let those who are being instructed pray apart from the faithful and go out, so that the faithful may learn when the presbyter or deacon reads the New Testament or the Gospels.

**2** Let the faithful women stand in the church by themselves, and the women catechumens by themselves, apart from the faithful women; but all the women apart from the men, and the girls apart too, each in her order.

**3** The men on the right and the women on the left; the faithful virgins first, and behind them the women who are being instructed for virginity.

**4** After the prayer let the women catechumens give the Peace to one another, men to men and women to women.

**5** Let every woman cover her head, her hair too. Let the women show their modesty becomingly and seemly in their adornment, and let them not be adorned with braided hair or precious stones, or the young men who are in the church will be caught, but with modesty and knowledge. If not, let them be instructed by the widows who sit in front; and if they rebel and resist, let the bishop rebuke them.

---

## Notes

- **v5 "not… with braided hair or precious stones"** — Paul's rule for the women: *with modesty and self-control, not with braided hair and gold or pearls or costly clothes* (1 Timothy 2:9).
- **Source text** — J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, *The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac* (1902), public domain. English translation by this project.

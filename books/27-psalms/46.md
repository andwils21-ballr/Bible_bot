# Psalms 46

*For the director. Of the sons of Korah. On Alamoth. A song.*

**1** God is our refuge and strength,  
a help in trouble, found very near.

**2** Therefore we will not fear though the earth gives way,  
though the mountains slip into the heart of the seas,

**3** though its waters roar and foam,  
though the mountains tremble at its swelling. *Selah*

**4** There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God,  
the holy dwelling place of the Most High.

**5** God is within her; she will not be moved;  
God will help her at the break of morning.

**6** Nations rage; kingdoms totter;  
He lifts His voice; the earth melts.

**7** The LORD of hosts is with us;  
the God of Jacob is our fortress. *Selah*

**8** Come, see the works of the LORD,  
who has brought desolations on the earth.

**9** He makes wars cease to the end of the earth;  
He breaks the bow and snaps the spear;  
He burns the chariots with fire.

**10** "Be still, and know that I am God.  
I will be exalted among the nations;  
I will be exalted in the earth."

**11** The LORD of hosts is with us;  
the God of Jacob is our fortress. *Selah*

## Notes

- **The title "On Alamoth"** — *al alamot*, **for young women**, perhaps for high voices (1 Chronicles 15:20).
- **vv2–3** — the world returning to chaos, the mountains sinking back into the waters (Genesis 1:2, 9); even then God's people need not fear.
- **v4 "There is a river"** — Jerusalem had no river, only the spring of Gihon; the river is the river of God's presence, *the river of Your delights* (36:8; Ezekiel 47:1–12; Revelation 22:1–2).
- **v5 "at the break of morning"** — *lifnot boqer*, the hour of rescue, as when the Assyrian army lay dead at dawn (Isaiah 37:36).
- **vv7, 11 "The LORD of hosts is with us"** — *YHWH tseva'ot immanu*: *immanu*, **with us**, the word of *Immanuel*, **God with us** (Isaiah 7:14; 8:8–10).
- **v9 "He burns the chariots with fire"** — *agalot*, **wagons**; the Greek reads **shields**. The end of war is the end of the weapons of war (Isaiah 2:4).
- **v10 "Be still, and know that I am God"** — *harpu*, **let go**, **stop**: God speaks to the raging nations (v6), and to His people.
- **v1 and the hymn** — the psalm is the source of Luther's *A Mighty Fortress Is Our God*.

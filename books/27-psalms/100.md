# Psalms 100

*A psalm of thanksgiving.*

**1** Shout to the LORD, all the earth.

**2** Serve the LORD with gladness;  
come before Him with joyful singing.

**3** Know that the LORD, He is God.  
It is He who made us, and we are His;  
we are His people and the sheep of His pasture.

**4** Enter His gates with thanksgiving,  
His courts with praise;  
give thanks to Him, bless His name.

**5** For the LORD is good;  
His loyal love endures forever,  
and His faithfulness to all generations.

---

## Notes

- **The title, "A psalm of thanksgiving"** — *letodah*, "for thanksgiving" or "for the thank offering" (Leviticus 7:12); the only psalm with this title.
- **v1 "Shout to the LORD, all the earth"** — the same words as 98:4a.
- **v3 "and we are His"** — the read form, *lo* with *vav*; the written form, *lo* with *aleph*, gives "and not we ourselves," which the Greek follows.
- **v3 "His people and the sheep of His pasture"** — as in 95:7 and 79:13.
- **v5 "His loyal love endures forever"** — the refrain of 106, 107, 118 and 136, and of the song in 1 Chronicles 16:34.

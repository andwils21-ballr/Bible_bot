# Psalms 96

**1** Sing to the LORD a new song;  
sing to the LORD, all the earth.

**2** Sing to the LORD, bless His name;  
proclaim His salvation from day to day.

**3** Declare His glory among the nations,  
His wonders among all the peoples.

**4** For great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised;  
He is to be feared above all gods.

**5** For all the gods of the peoples are idols,  
but the LORD made the heavens.

**6** Splendor and majesty are before Him;  
strength and beauty are in His sanctuary.

**7** Ascribe to the LORD, families of the peoples,  
ascribe to the LORD glory and strength.

**8** Ascribe to the LORD the glory of His name;  
bring an offering and come into His courts.

**9** Worship the LORD in the splendor of holiness;  
tremble before Him, all the earth.

**10** Say among the nations, "The LORD reigns!"  
The world is established; it shall not be moved;  
He will judge the peoples with equity.

**11** Let the heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice;  
let the sea roar, and all that fills it;

**12** let the field exult, and all that is in it.  
Then all the trees of the forest will sing for joy

**13** before the LORD, for He comes,  
for He comes to judge the earth.  
He will judge the world in righteousness,  
and the peoples in His faithfulness.

## Notes

- **The whole psalm** — nearly the same as the song David appointed when the ark came to Jerusalem (1 Chronicles 16:23–33), and the English there is followed. Where the Hebrew differs, so does the English: v1 adds "a new song" and v2 "bless His name"; v6 has "beauty… in His sanctuary" for "joy… in His place"; v8 has "into His courts" for "before Him"; v10 adds "He will judge the peoples with equity"; v13 adds the last two lines. "O" is dropped from v7, as everywhere in this rendering of the Psalms.
- **v1 "a new song"** — a song for a new act of God (33:3; 40:3; Isaiah 42:10; Revelation 5:9).
- **v5 "idols"** — *elilim*, "nothings," a play on *elohim*, "gods."
- **v9 "in the splendor of holiness"** — as in 29:2; or "in holy attire."
- **v10 "The LORD reigns!"** — *YHWH malakh*, the cry of 93:1, now told to the nations.
- **vv11–13** — all creation rejoices because the Judge is coming; 98:7–9 repeats the picture.

# Psalms 39

*For the director: for Jeduthun. A psalm of David.*

**1** I said, "I will guard my ways,  
so that I do not sin with my tongue;  
I will keep a muzzle on my mouth  
while the wicked are in front of me."

**2** I was mute and silent;  
I held my peace, to no good,  
and my pain was stirred up.

**3** My heart grew hot within me;  
as I mused, the fire burned;  
then I spoke with my tongue:

**4** "LORD, make me know my end,  
and what is the measure of my days;  
let me know how fleeting I am.

**5** You have made my days a few handbreadths,  
and my lifetime is as nothing before You.  
Surely every man, even standing firm, is only a breath. *Selah*

**6** Surely a man walks about as a shadow;  
surely for a breath they are in turmoil;  
he heaps up, and does not know who will gather.

**7** Now, Lord, what do I wait for?  
My hope is in You.

**8** Rescue me from all my transgressions;  
do not make me the scorn of the fool.

**9** I am mute; I do not open my mouth,  
for You are the one who has done it.

**10** Take away Your stroke from me;  
I am worn out by the blows of Your hand.

**11** With rebukes for iniquity You discipline a man;  
You consume like a moth what he holds dear;  
surely every man is a breath. *Selah*

**12** Hear my prayer, LORD,  
and listen to my cry;  
do not be silent at my tears.  
For I am a guest with You,  
a resident, like all my fathers.

**13** Look away from me, that I may smile again,  
before I go and am no more."

## Notes

- **v12 "a guest with You, a resident"** — *ger* and *toshav*: *the land is Mine; for you are guests and residents with Me* (Leviticus 25:23; 1 Chronicles 29:15; Hebrews 11:13).

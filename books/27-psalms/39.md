# Psalms 39

*For the director: for Jeduthun. A psalm of David.*

**1** I said, "I will guard my ways,  
so that I do not sin with my tongue;  
I will keep a muzzle on my mouth  
while the wicked are in front of me."

**2** I was mute and silent;  
I held my peace, to no good,  
and my pain was stirred up.

**3** My heart grew hot within me;  
as I mused, the fire burned;  
then I spoke with my tongue:

**4** "LORD, make me know my end,  
and what is the measure of my days;  
let me know how fleeting I am.

**5** You have made my days a few handbreadths,  
and my lifetime is as nothing before You.  
Surely every man, even standing firm, is only a breath. *Selah*

**6** Surely a man walks about as a shadow;  
surely for a breath they are in turmoil;  
he heaps up, and does not know who will gather.

**7** Now, Lord, what do I wait for?  
My hope is in You.

**8** Rescue me from all my transgressions;  
do not make me the scorn of the fool.

**9** I am mute; I do not open my mouth,  
for You are the one who has done it.

**10** Take away Your stroke from me;  
I am worn out by the blows of Your hand.

**11** With rebukes for iniquity You discipline a man;  
You consume like a moth what he holds dear;  
surely every man is a breath. *Selah*

**12** Hear my prayer, LORD,  
and listen to my cry;  
do not be silent at my tears.  
For I am a guest with You,  
a resident, like all my fathers.

**13** Look away from me, that I may smile again,  
before I go and am no more."

## Notes

- **The title "for Jeduthun"** — one of David's chief musicians, with Asaph and Heman (1 Chronicles 16:41–42; 25:1); also over Psalms 62 and 77.
- **vv1–3** — the psalmist resolves to keep silent before the wicked, as in 38:13, but the fire in his heart breaks out in prayer, not complaint (Jeremiah 20:9).
- **v4 "make me know my end"** — *Teach us to number our days* (90:12).
- **v5 "a few handbreadths"** — *tefachot*, the width of a hand, the smallest measure. **A breath** is *hevel*, three times in the psalm (vv5, 6, 11), the word of Ecclesiastes.
- **v6 "a man walks about as a shadow"** — *be-tselem*, **as an image**, a phantom (73:20; Job 8:9).
- **v6 "he heaps up, and does not know who will gather"** — *Fool! This night your soul is required of you, and the things you have prepared, whose will they be?* (Luke 12:20).
- **v9 "for You are the one who has done it"** — the psalmist accepts his trouble from God's hand, as Job did (Job 2:10).
- **v11 "You consume like a moth what he holds dear"** — the moth that eats a garment slowly (Job 13:28; Isaiah 50:9).
- **v12 "a guest with You, a resident"** — *ger* and *toshav*, as the word table has them: *the land is Mine; for you are guests and residents with Me* (Leviticus 25:23; 1 Chronicles 29:15; Hebrews 11:13).
- **v13 "Look away from me"** — *hasha*, **gaze away**: the plea of Job 7:19 and 10:20, *Leave me alone, that I may have a little cheer*.

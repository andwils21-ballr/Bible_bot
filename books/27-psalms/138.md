# Psalms 138

*Of David.*

**1** I will give thanks to You with all my heart;  
before the gods I will sing Your praise.

**2** I will bow down toward Your holy temple  
and give thanks to Your name  
for Your loyal love and Your truth,  
for You have exalted Your promise above all Your name.

**3** On the day I called, You answered me;  
You made me bold with strength in my soul.

**4** All the kings of the earth will give thanks to You, LORD,  
for they have heard the words of Your mouth.

**5** They will sing of the ways of the LORD,  
for great is the glory of the LORD.

**6** For though the LORD is high, He regards the lowly,  
but the proud He knows from afar.

**7** Though I walk in the middle of trouble,  
You preserve my life;  
You stretch out Your hand against the wrath of my enemies,  
and Your right hand saves me.

**8** The LORD will fulfill His purpose for me;  
Your loyal love, LORD, endures forever.  
Do not forsake the works of Your hands.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "before the gods"** — in defiance of the gods of the nations (96:4–5), or before the heavenly court (82:1); the Greek reads "before angels."
- **v2 "You have exalted Your promise above all Your name"** — hard to render; God's word, now kept, shows Him greater than all that was known of Him. *Imrah* is "promise," as in Psalm 119.
- **v6 "He regards the lowly"** — as in 113:5–7 and Isaiah 57:15.
- **v8 "Do not forsake the works of Your hands"** — the psalmist himself, whom God made and is still making (139:13–16; Philippians 1:6).

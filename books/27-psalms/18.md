# Psalms 18

*For the director. Of the servant of the LORD, of David, who spoke to the LORD the words of this song on the day the LORD delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul. He said:*

## The LORD My Rock

**1** I love You, LORD, my strength.

**2** The LORD is my rock, my fortress and my deliverer,  
my God, my rock, in whom I take refuge,  
my shield and the horn of my salvation, my stronghold.

**3** I call on the LORD, who is worthy to be praised,  
and I am saved from my enemies.

## Out of Many Waters

**4** The cords of death surrounded me;  
the torrents of worthlessness terrified me.

**5** The cords of Sheol were around me;  
the snares of death confronted me.

**6** In my distress I called on the LORD;  
to my God I cried for help.  
From His temple He heard my voice,  
and my cry before Him came into His ears.

**7** Then the earth shook and quaked;  
the foundations of the mountains trembled  
and shook, because He was angry.

**8** Smoke went up from His nostrils,  
and devouring fire from His mouth;  
coals blazed out from Him.

**9** He bent the heavens and came down,  
with thick darkness under His feet.

**10** He rode on a cherub and flew;  
He swooped on the wings of the wind.

**11** He made darkness His covering,  
His shelter around Him,  
darkness of waters, thick clouds of the skies.

**12** From the brightness before Him His thick clouds passed,  
hail and coals of fire.

**13** The LORD thundered in the heavens,  
and the Most High uttered His voice,  
hail and coals of fire.

**14** He sent out His arrows and scattered them,  
lightnings in abundance, and routed them.

**15** The channels of water were seen,  
and the foundations of the world were laid bare  
at Your rebuke, LORD,  
at the blast of the breath of Your nostrils.

**16** He reached from on high and took me;  
He drew me out of many waters.

**17** He delivered me from my strong enemy,  
from those who hated me,  
for they were too mighty for me.

**18** They confronted me on the day of my calamity,  
but the LORD was my support.

**19** He brought me out into a broad place;  
He rescued me, because He delighted in me.

## "With the Loyal You Show Yourself Loyal"

**20** The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness;  
according to the cleanness of my hands He repaid me.

**21** For I have kept the ways of the LORD,  
and have not wickedly departed from my God.

**22** For all His judgments were before me,  
and His statutes I did not put away from me.

**23** I was blameless before Him,  
and I kept myself from my guilt.

**24** So the LORD repaid me according to my righteousness,  
according to the cleanness of my hands in His sight.

**25** With the loyal You show Yourself loyal;  
with the blameless man You show Yourself blameless.

**26** With the pure You show Yourself pure,  
and with the crooked You show Yourself twisted.

**27** For You save a humble people,  
but haughty eyes You bring down.

**28** For You light my lamp;  
the LORD my God lights up my darkness.

**29** For by You I can run against a troop;  
by my God I can leap over a wall.

**30** This God — His way is blameless;  
the word of the LORD is refined.  
He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.

**31** For who is God but the LORD?  
Who is a rock except our God?

## The God Who Trains My Hands

**32** This God girds me with strength  
and has made my way blameless.

**33** He makes my feet like the feet of a deer  
and sets me on my high places.

**34** He trains my hands for war,  
so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze.

**35** You have given me the shield of Your salvation;  
Your right hand upholds me,  
and Your humility has made me great.

**36** You made room for my steps under me,  
and my ankles did not give way.

**37** I pursued my enemies and overtook them;  
I did not turn back until they were consumed.

**38** I struck them through, so that they could not rise;  
they fell under my feet.

**39** You girded me with strength for battle;  
You made those who rose against me bow under me.

**40** You made my enemies turn their backs to me;  
those who hated me I destroyed.

**41** They cried for help, but there was no one to save them;  
to the LORD, but He did not answer them.

**42** I beat them fine as dust before the wind;  
I poured them out like the mud of the streets.

**43** You delivered me from the strife of the people;  
You made me the head of nations.  
A people I did not know serve me.

**44** As soon as they hear, they obey me;  
foreigners come cringing to me.

**45** Foreigners lose heart  
and come trembling out of their strongholds.

## "The LORD Lives"

**46** The LORD lives! Blessed be my rock,  
and exalted be the God of my salvation,

**47** the God who gives me vengeance  
and subdues peoples under me,

**48** who delivers me from my enemies.  
You exalt me above those who rise against me;  
You deliver me from the man of violence.

**49** Therefore I will give thanks to You, LORD, among the nations,  
and sing praises to Your name.

**50** He makes great the salvation of His king,  
and shows loyal love to His anointed,  
to David and to his seed forever.

## Notes

- **The title and 2 Samuel 22** — the song stands also in 2 Samuel 22, with the same occasion; the two copies differ in many small places. Where the Hebrew words are the same, the English here is the same as there; the notes give the differences that change the sense. The Hebrew counts the title as verse 1.
- **v1 "I love You, LORD, my strength"** — *erchamkha*, from *racham*, the tender love of a mother for her child, used of man loving God only here. The line is not in 2 Samuel 22.
- **v4 "The cords of death"** — *chevlei mavet*, matching **the cords of Sheol** in v5; 2 Samuel 22:5 has *the breakers of death*.
- **v10 "He swooped on the wings of the wind"** — *va-yede*, as an eagle swoops (Deuteronomy 28:49); 2 Samuel 22:11 has *va-yera*, **He was seen**, one letter different.
- **vv12–13 "hail and coals of fire"** — twice in the Psalm, not in 2 Samuel; the Greek lacks the second.
- **v28 "For You light my lamp"** — 2 Samuel 22:29 has *For You are my lamp*.
- **v32 "This God girds me with strength"** — *ha-me'azzereni chayil*; 2 Samuel 22:33 has *my strong fortress*.
- **v35 "Your humility has made me great"** — *anvatkha*, God's stooping down to the lowly; 2 Samuel 22:36 has *Your answering*. **Your right hand upholds me** is not in Samuel.
- **v45 "come trembling out of their strongholds"** — *va-yachregu*; 2 Samuel 22:46 has *va-yachgeru*, **gird themselves**, with two letters in the other order.
- **v50 "He makes great the salvation of His king"** — the written text has *migdol*, **a tower**, the read form *magdil*, **He makes great**, followed here; 2 Samuel 22:51 is the reverse. Paul quotes v49 of the Gentiles praising God (Romans 15:9).

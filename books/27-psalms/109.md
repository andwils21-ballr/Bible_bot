# Psalms 109

*For the director. Of David. A psalm.*

**1** God of my praise,  
do not keep silent,

**2** for a wicked mouth and a deceitful mouth  
have opened against me;  
they have spoken against me with a lying tongue.

**3** They surround me with words of hatred  
and fight against me without cause.

**4** In return for my love they accuse me,  
but I am prayer.

**5** They repay me evil for good,  
and hatred for my love.

**6** Appoint a wicked man over him;  
let an accuser stand at his right hand.

**7** When he is judged, let him come out guilty,  
and let his prayer become sin.

**8** Let his days be few;  
let another take his office.

**9** Let his children be fatherless  
and his wife a widow.

**10** Let his children wander about and beg,  
seeking food far from their ruined homes.

**11** Let the creditor seize all that he has,  
and let strangers plunder the fruit of his labor.

**12** Let there be no one to extend loyal love to him,  
nor anyone to pity his fatherless children.

**13** Let his descendants be cut off;  
in the next generation let their name be blotted out.

**14** Let the iniquity of his fathers be remembered before the LORD,  
and let not the sin of his mother be blotted out.

**15** Let them be before the LORD continually,  
and may He cut off their memory from the earth,

**16** because he did not remember to show loyal love,  
but pursued the poor and needy man  
and the brokenhearted, to put them to death.

**17** He loved cursing — let it come on him;  
he did not delight in blessing — let it be far from him.

**18** He clothed himself with cursing as with his garment;  
let it soak into his body like water,  
like oil into his bones.

**19** Let it be to him like a garment he wraps around himself,  
like a belt he always wears.

**20** This is the reward of my accusers from the LORD,  
of those who speak evil against my life.

**21** But You, GOD the Lord,  
deal with me for Your name's sake;  
because Your loyal love is good, deliver me.

**22** For I am poor and needy,  
and my heart is wounded within me.

**23** I am gone like a shadow at evening;  
I am shaken off like a locust.

**24** My knees are weak through fasting;  
my flesh has grown lean, with no fat.

**25** I have become a reproach to them;  
when they see me, they shake their heads.

**26** Help me, LORD my God;  
save me according to Your loyal love,

**27** so that they may know that this is Your hand;  
You, LORD, have done it.

**28** Let them curse, but You will bless;  
when they rise up, let them be put to shame,  
but let Your servant rejoice.

**29** Let my accusers be clothed with disgrace;  
let them wrap themselves in their shame as in a cloak.

**30** With my mouth I will give great thanks to the LORD;  
I will praise Him among the many.

**31** For He stands at the right hand of the needy,  
to save him from those who judge his soul.

## Notes

- **vv6–19** — the harshest curses in the Psalms. Many read them as the psalmist's prayer against one false accuser (note the change from "they" in vv2–5 to "he"); others read them as the enemies' words against the psalmist, quoted, with v20 meaning "this is what my accusers say." The Hebrew has no quotation marks, and the English adds none.
- **vv4, 6, 20, 29 "accuse… an accuser… my accusers"** — all the same root, *satan*. In v6 the word stands without the article, a prosecutor at the right hand in court (Zechariah 3:1); the Greek has *diabolos*.
- **v4 "but I am prayer"** — the Hebrew is two words, "and I — prayer": while they accuse, he prays.
- **v8 "let another take his office"** — Peter applies this to Judas (Acts 1:20), with 69:25.
- **v21 "GOD the Lord"** — *YHWH Adonai*, the order of 68:20.
- **v25 "they shake their heads"** — the scorn of 22:7, and of the passers-by at the cross (Matthew 27:39).
- **v31 "He stands at the right hand of the needy"** — where the accuser stood in v6, God Himself now stands to defend.

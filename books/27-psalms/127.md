# Psalms 127

*A song of ascents. Of Solomon.*

**1** Unless the LORD builds the house,  
its builders labor over it in vain.  
Unless the LORD guards the city,  
the watchman stays awake in vain.

**2** It is in vain for you to rise up early  
and stay up late,  
eating the bread of hard toil;  
for He gives to His beloved sleep.

**3** Children are a heritage from the LORD,  
the fruit of the womb a reward.

**4** Like arrows in the hand of a warrior  
are the children of one's youth.

**5** Blessed is the man  
who has filled his quiver with them!  
They will not be put to shame  
when they speak with enemies in the gate.

---

## Notes

- **v3 "Children are a heritage from the LORD"** — *banim*, "sons"; a play on *boneh*, "builds," in v1: the LORD builds the house by giving children.

# Psalms 91

**1** The one who dwells in the shelter of the Most High  
will rest in the shadow of the Almighty.

**2** I will say to the LORD, "My refuge and my fortress,  
my God, in whom I trust."

**3** For He will deliver you from the snare of the fowler  
and from the deadly plague.

**4** He will cover you with His feathers,  
and under His wings you will find refuge;  
His faithfulness is a shield and a wall.

**5** You will not fear the terror of the night,  
or the arrow that flies by day,

**6** or the plague that stalks in darkness,  
or the destruction that lays waste at noon.

**7** A thousand may fall at your side,  
ten thousand at your right hand,  
but it will not come near you.

**8** You will only look with your eyes  
and see the recompense of the wicked.

**9** For You, LORD, are my refuge!  
You have made the Most High your dwelling place;

**10** no evil will befall you,  
no plague come near your tent.

**11** For He will command His messengers concerning you  
to guard you in all your ways.

**12** On their hands they will bear you up,  
so that you do not strike your foot against a stone.

**13** You will tread on the lion and the cobra;  
you will trample the young lion and the serpent.

**14** "Because he holds fast to Me in love, I will deliver him;  
I will set him on high, because he knows My name.

**15** He will call on Me, and I will answer him;  
I will be with him in trouble;  
I will rescue him and honor him.

**16** With long life I will satisfy him  
and show him My salvation."

## Notes

- **v1 "the Most High… the Almighty"** — with "the LORD" and "my God" in v2, four names of God in two verses: *Elyon*, *Shaddai*, *YHWH*, *Elohai*.
- **v3 "from the deadly plague"** — *mi-dever havvot*; the Greek read *davar*, "from a troubling word," for *dever*, "plague."
- **v4 "a shield and a wall"** — *sochera* occurs only here; "buckler" or "rampart" are other guesses.
- **v9 "For You, LORD, are my refuge! You have made the Most High your dwelling place"** — the voices shift as in v2: the believer speaks to God, then the psalmist speaks to the believer again. Many translations smooth this to "Because you have made the LORD your refuge"; the Hebrew is followed.
- **vv11–12 "He will command His messengers concerning you"** — *mal'akhim*, messengers, as everywhere in this rendering; in the temptation the devil quotes these lines to Jesus, and the Gospel has "angels" (Matthew 4:6; Luke 4:10–11). Jesus answers from Deuteronomy 6:16.
- **v13 "the lion and the cobra… the serpent"** — compare Luke 10:19, *I have given you authority to tread on serpents and scorpions.*
- **vv14–16** — God Himself answers, promising the one who trusts Him.
- **v14 "holds fast to Me in love"** — *chashaq*, to set one's love on, as God set His love on Israel (Deuteronomy 7:7).

# Psalms 94

**1** God of vengeance, LORD,  
God of vengeance, shine forth!

**2** Rise up, Judge of the earth;  
repay the proud what they deserve.

**3** How long shall the wicked, LORD,  
how long shall the wicked exult?

**4** They pour out arrogant words;  
all evildoers boast.

**5** They crush Your people, LORD,  
and afflict Your inheritance.

**6** They kill the widow and the guest  
and murder the fatherless.

**7** They say, "Yah does not see;  
the God of Jacob does not notice."

**8** Understand, you senseless among the people!  
Fools, when will you be wise?

**9** He who planted the ear, does He not hear?  
He who formed the eye, does He not see?

**10** He who disciplines the nations, does He not rebuke —  
He who teaches mankind knowledge?

**11** The LORD knows the thoughts of man,  
that they are a breath.

**12** Blessed is the man whom You discipline, Yah,  
and whom You teach from Your law,

**13** to give him rest from days of trouble,  
until a pit is dug for the wicked.

**14** For the LORD will not forsake His people;  
He will not abandon His inheritance.

**15** For justice will return to righteousness,  
and all the upright in heart will follow it.

**16** Who will rise up for me against the evildoers?  
Who will stand up for me against those who do wrong?

**17** If the LORD had not been my help,  
my soul would soon have dwelt in silence.

**18** When I said, "My foot slips,"  
Your loyal love, LORD, held me up.

**19** When my anxious thoughts are many within me,  
Your comforts delight my soul.

**20** Can a throne of destruction be allied with You,  
one that frames trouble by statute?

**21** They band together against the life of the righteous  
and condemn innocent blood.

**22** But the LORD has become my stronghold,  
and my God the rock of my refuge.

**23** He will bring back on them their own iniquity  
and destroy them for their evil;  
the LORD our God will destroy them.

## Notes

- **v1 "God of vengeance"** — *El neqamot*; vengeance here is God's just repayment, which belongs to Him alone (Deuteronomy 32:35; Romans 12:19).
- **v6 "the widow and the guest… the fatherless"** — the three the Law puts under God's special care (Exodus 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 10:18).
- **vv9–10** — the Maker of ear and eye cannot be deaf and blind; the argument from creation to judgment.
- **v11 "that they are a breath"** — *hevel*, as in Ecclesiastes; Paul quotes the verse as *The Lord knows the thoughts of the wise, that they are futile* (1 Corinthians 3:20).
- **v12 "Blessed is the man whom You discipline"** — discipline as a mark of God's care (Proverbs 3:11–12; Hebrews 12:5–11).
- **v17 "dwelt in silence"** — *dumah*, silence, the grave (115:17).
- **v20 "one that frames trouble by statute"** — rulers who make injustice law (Isaiah 10:1).

# Psalms 75

*For the director: "Do Not Destroy." A psalm of Asaph. A song.*

**1** We give thanks to You, God;  
we give thanks, for Your name is near;  
they tell of Your wonders.

**2** "When I choose the appointed time,  
I will judge with equity.

**3** When the earth and all its inhabitants melt,  
it is I who keep its pillars steady. *Selah*

**4** I said to the boastful, 'Do not boast,'  
and to the wicked, 'Do not lift up your horn;

**5** do not lift up your horn on high,  
or speak with an arrogant neck.'"

**6** For not from the east or from the west,  
and not from the wilderness, comes lifting up.

**7** But it is God who judges,  
putting down one and lifting up another.

**8** For in the hand of the LORD there is a cup  
with foaming wine, well mixed;  
He pours from it,  
and all the wicked of the earth  
will drain it down to the dregs.

**9** But I will declare it forever;  
I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.

**10** "All the horns of the wicked I will cut off,  
but the horns of the righteous will be lifted up."

## Notes

- **vv2–5, 10** — God speaks; the quotation marks show where.
- **v2 "When I choose the appointed time"** — *mo'ed*, the set time for judgment; God, not people, decides when (Habakkuk 2:3).
- **v4 "Do not lift up your horn"** — the horn is strength and pride, like the horn of a wild ox (92:10; 1 Samuel 2:1).
- **v6 "not from the wilderness, comes lifting up"** — *harim* can also be read "mountains," "not from the wilderness of the mountains"; the sentence then breaks off and v7 completes it.
- **v8 "a cup"** — the cup of God's wrath (Isaiah 51:17; Jeremiah 25:15; Revelation 14:10).

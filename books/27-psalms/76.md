# Psalms 76

*For the director: with stringed instruments. A psalm of Asaph. A song.*

**1** God is known in Judah;  
His name is great in Israel.

**2** His tent is in Salem,  
His dwelling place in Zion.

**3** There He broke the flaming arrows of the bow,  
the shield, the sword, and the weapons of war. *Selah*

**4** You are radiant,  
more majestic than the mountains of prey.

**5** The stouthearted have been stripped;  
they have sunk into their sleep;  
none of the men of war could lift their hands.

**6** At Your rebuke, God of Jacob,  
both chariot and horse lay stunned.

**7** You — You are to be feared;  
who can stand before You once Your anger is roused?

**8** From heaven You made Your judgment heard;  
the earth feared and was still

**9** when God rose up to judge,  
to save all the humble of the earth. *Selah*

**10** Surely human wrath will praise You;  
the remnant of wrath You will put on like a belt.

**11** Make vows to the LORD your God and keep them;  
let all around Him bring gifts to the One who is to be feared.

**12** He cuts off the spirit of princes;  
He is feared by the kings of the earth.

## Notes

- **The setting** — an army broken outside Jerusalem without a battle (vv3–6) fits the night the LORD struck down Sennacherib's camp (2 Kings 19:35); the Greek title adds "concerning the Assyrian."
- **v2 "Salem"** — Jerusalem, the city of Melchizedek (Genesis 14:18; Hebrews 7:1–2). "His tent" (*sukko*) is also a lion's lair (Jeremiah 25:38), fitting "the mountains of prey" in v4.
- **v4 "more majestic than the mountains of prey"** — the Hebrew is followed; the Greek reads "the everlasting mountains."
- **v5 "they have sunk into their sleep"** — the sleep of death, as in v6, "lay stunned" (*nirdam*, a deep sleep).
- **v10 "Surely human wrath will praise You"** — even rage against God ends up showing His glory; "the remnant of wrath You will put on like a belt" is obscure, perhaps God binding on what is left of His enemies' fury, or His own.
- **v11 "the One who is to be feared"** — *mora*, "the Fear," as Jacob called God "the Fear of Isaac" (Genesis 31:42, a different word).

# Psalms 56

*For the director: on "The Silent Dove of Far Places." Of David. A Miktam, when the Philistines seized him in Gath.*

**1** Be gracious to me, God, for man tramples on me;  
all day long the fighter presses me.

**2** My enemies trample on me all day long,  
for many fight against me proudly.

**3** On the day I am afraid,  
I trust in You.

**4** In God, whose word I praise,  
in God I trust; I will not be afraid.  
What can flesh do to me?

**5** All day long they twist my words;  
all their thoughts are against me for evil.

**6** They stir up strife; they lurk;  
they watch my heels,  
as they have waited for my life.

**7** For their iniquity, will they escape?  
In anger bring down the peoples, God.

**8** You have counted my wanderings;  
put my tears in Your flask.  
Are they not in Your book?

**9** Then my enemies will turn back  
on the day I call.  
This I know, that God is for me.

**10** In God, whose word I praise,  
in the LORD, whose word I praise,

**11** in God I trust; I will not be afraid.  
What can man do to me?

**12** Your vows are on me, God;  
I will pay thank offerings to You.

**13** For You have delivered my soul from death,  
and my feet from stumbling,  
so that I may walk before God  
in the light of the living.

## Notes

- **The title "The Silent Dove of Far Places"** — *yonat elem rechoqim*, the name of a tune. **When the Philistines seized him in Gath**: David's flight to Achish (1 Samuel 21:10–15), the occasion of Psalm 34 also.
- **v2 "proudly"** — *marom*, **on high**; or a name of God, **O Most High**.
- **vv4, 10–11** — a refrain, *In God I trust; I will not be afraid*; v4 ends *What can flesh do to me?*, v11 *What can man do to me?* (118:6; Hebrews 13:6).
- **v6 "They stir up strife"** — *yaguru*, from a second *gur*, **to stir up**, **attack**, not the *gur* of the guest (59:3).
- **v7 "will they escape?"** — the Hebrew is short and hard, *al aven pallet lamo*, **for iniquity, escape for them?**
- **v8 "my wanderings… Your flask"** — *nodi* and *nodekha*, a play on words: God counts every step of the fugitive (*nod*), and keeps every tear in His leather bottle (*nod*). **Your book** is God's record (139:16; Malachi 3:16).
- **v9 "God is for me"** — *Elohim li*; *If God is for us, who can be against us?* (Romans 8:31).
- **v13 "in the light of the living"** — the light of life, as against the darkness of Sheol (Job 33:30).

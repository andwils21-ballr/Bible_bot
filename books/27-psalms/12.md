# Psalms 12

*For the director: on the eighth. A psalm of David.*

**1** Save, LORD, for the faithful one is gone;  
the trustworthy have vanished from among the children of man.

**2** Everyone speaks emptiness to his neighbor;  
with smooth lips and a double heart they speak.

**3** May the LORD cut off all smooth lips,  
the tongue that speaks great things,

**4** those who say, "With our tongue we will prevail;  
our lips are our own; who is master over us?"

**5** "Because the poor are plundered, because the needy groan,  
now I will arise," says the LORD;  
"I will set him in the safety he longs for."

**6** The words of the LORD are pure words,  
silver refined in a furnace in the earth,  
purified seven times.

**7** You, LORD, will keep them;  
You will guard him from this generation forever.

**8** On every side the wicked prowl,  
when vileness is exalted among the children of man.

---

## Notes

- **v6 "silver refined… purified seven times"** — God's words against the empty words of v2: *every word of God proves true* (Proverbs 30:5; 18:30).

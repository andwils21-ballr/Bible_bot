# Psalms 25

*Of David.*

**1** To You, LORD, I lift up my soul.

**2** My God, in You I trust;  
let me not be put to shame;  
let not my enemies exult over me.

**3** Indeed, none who wait for You will be put to shame;  
those who are treacherous without cause will be put to shame.

**4** Make Your ways known to me, LORD;  
teach me Your paths.

**5** Lead me in Your truth and teach me,  
for You are the God of my salvation;  
for You I wait all the day.

**6** Remember Your compassion, LORD, and Your loyal love,  
for they have been from of old.

**7** Do not remember the sins of my youth or my transgressions;  
according to Your loyal love remember me,  
for the sake of Your goodness, LORD.

**8** Good and upright is the LORD;  
therefore He instructs sinners in the way.

**9** He leads the humble in what is right  
and teaches the humble His way.

**10** All the paths of the LORD are loyal love and faithfulness  
for those who keep His covenant and His testimonies.

**11** For Your name's sake, LORD,  
pardon my iniquity, for it is great.

**12** Who is the man who fears the LORD?  
He will instruct him in the way he should choose.

**13** His soul will lodge in good things,  
and his offspring will inherit the land.

**14** The friendship of the LORD is for those who fear Him,  
and He makes His covenant known to them.

**15** My eyes are always toward the LORD,  
for He will bring my feet out of the net.

**16** Turn to me and be gracious to me,  
for I am alone and afflicted.

**17** The troubles of my heart have grown wide;  
bring me out of my distress.

**18** See my affliction and my trouble,  
and forgive all my sins.

**19** See how many are my enemies,  
and with what violent hatred they hate me.

**20** Guard my soul and rescue me;  
let me not be put to shame, for I take refuge in You.

**21** Let integrity and uprightness keep me,  
for I wait for You.

**22** Redeem Israel, God,  
out of all his troubles.

## Notes

- **The psalm** — an acrostic: each verse begins with the next letter of the Hebrew alphabet, with a few letters missing or doubled. The last verse stands outside the alphabet, a prayer for all Israel; Psalm 34 ends the same way.
- **v1 "To You, LORD, I lift up my soul"** — *nafshi essa*, to long for, to set one's desire on (86:4; 143:8).
- **v3 "those who wait for You"** — *qoveikha*, from *qavah*, **wait**, **hope**, the root of *tiqvah*, **hope** (Job 7:6); the word returns in vv5 and 21.
- **v7 "the sins of my youth"** — sins done without understanding (Job 13:26). **Remember me** according to *chesed*, not according to sin.
- **v8 "He instructs sinners in the way"** — *yoreh*, the root of *torah*: God teaches even sinners, because He is good.
- **v13 "his offspring will inherit the land"** — *the meek shall inherit the land* (37:11; Matthew 5:5).
- **v14 "The friendship of the LORD"** — *sod YHWH*, God's close counsel, as Job had it once (Job 29:4).
- **v17 "The troubles of my heart have grown wide"** — or, with the words divided differently, **relieve the troubles of my heart**, the reverse of distress (4:1).
- **v21 "integrity and uprightness"** — *tom va-yosher*, the words used of Job (Job 1:1), here as guards set to keep the psalmist.

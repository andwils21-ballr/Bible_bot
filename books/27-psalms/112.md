# Psalms 112

**1** Praise Yah!  
Blessed is the man who fears the LORD,  
who greatly delights in His commandments.

**2** His offspring will be mighty in the land;  
the generation of the upright will be blessed.

**3** Wealth and riches are in his house,  
and his righteousness endures forever.

**4** Light dawns in the darkness for the upright;  
he is gracious, merciful and righteous.

**5** It goes well with the man who is gracious and lends,  
who conducts his affairs with justice.

**6** For he will never be moved;  
the righteous will be remembered forever.

**7** He is not afraid of bad news;  
his heart is steadfast, trusting in the LORD.

**8** His heart is firm; he will not be afraid,  
until he looks in triumph on his foes.

**9** He has scattered abroad; he has given to the poor;  
his righteousness endures forever;  
his horn will be lifted high in honor.

**10** The wicked man sees it and is angry;  
he gnashes his teeth and melts away;  
the desire of the wicked will perish.

---

## Notes

- **The form** — an acrostic, the twin of Psalm 111: what 111 says of God, 112 says of the one who fears Him. "His righteousness endures forever" (111:3; 112:3, 9) and "gracious and merciful" (111:4; 112:4) are used of both.
- **v4 "he is gracious, merciful and righteous"** — the Hebrew does not say who; it may be the upright man, as the rest of the psalm, or the LORD who gives the light.
- **v9 "He has scattered abroad; he has given to the poor"** — Paul quotes the verse of generous giving (2 Corinthians 9:9).
- **v10 "the desire of the wicked will perish"** — the last line answers the first: the righteous man's delight (v1) endures, the wicked man's desire does not (1:6).

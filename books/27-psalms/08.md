# Psalms 8

*For the director: on the Gittith. A psalm of David.*

**1** LORD, our Lord,  
how majestic is Your name in all the earth!  
You have set Your glory above the heavens.

**2** Out of the mouth of children and nursing infants  
You have founded strength because of Your foes,  
to silence the enemy and the avenger.

**3** When I look at Your heavens, the work of Your fingers,  
the moon and the stars, which You have set in place,

**4** what is man, that You remember him,  
and the son of man, that You visit him?

**5** Yet You have made him little less than God  
and crown him with glory and honor.

**6** You make him ruler over the works of Your hands;  
You have put all things under his feet,

**7** all sheep and oxen,  
and also the beasts of the field,

**8** the birds of the heavens and the fish of the sea,  
whatever passes along the paths of the seas.

**9** LORD, our Lord,  
how majestic is Your name in all the earth!

## Notes

- **The title "on the Gittith"** — *al ha-gittit*, perhaps an instrument or a tune from Gath (also Psalms 81 and 84).
- **v1 "LORD, our Lord"** — *YHWH adoneinu*; the same words close the psalm (v9), framing all of it in praise of God's name.
- **v2 "Out of the mouth of children and nursing infants"** — the Greek has *You have prepared praise*, which Jesus quotes when the children cry out in the temple (Matthew 21:16).
- **v3 "the work of Your fingers"** — the heavens as God's handiwork, done as finely as with the fingers (Exodus 31:18; Isaiah 40:12).
- **v4 "what is man, that You remember him?"** — *enosh*, man in his frailty; **visit** is *paqad*, to come to with care (Genesis 21:1). Job turns the words into a complaint (Job 7:17–18).
- **v5 "little less than God"** — *me-Elohim*, which the Greek renders **than the angels**; Hebrews 2:6–9 quotes the Greek and applies the verses to Jesus.
- **vv6–8 "You make him ruler over the works of Your hands"** — the charge of Genesis 1:26–28, *let them have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens*. *He has put all things under His feet* (1 Corinthians 15:27; Ephesians 1:22).
- **vv4–6 and the pronouns** — the psalm is rendered as the Old Testament has it, *man* and *the son of man* in lowercase; the New Testament quotations will capitalize where they apply the words to Jesus.

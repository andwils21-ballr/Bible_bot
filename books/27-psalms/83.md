# Psalms 83

*A song. A psalm of Asaph.*

**1** God, do not keep silent;  
do not hold Your peace or be still, God.

**2** For listen: Your enemies are in an uproar;  
those who hate You have raised their heads.

**3** They lay crafty plans against Your people;  
they consult together against Your treasured ones.

**4** They say, "Come, let us wipe them out as a nation,  
so that the name of Israel is remembered no more."

**5** For they plot together with one heart;  
against You they make a covenant —

**6** the tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites,  
Moab and the Hagrites,

**7** Gebal and Ammon and Amalek,  
Philistia with the inhabitants of Tyre;

**8** Assyria also has joined them;  
they have become the strong arm of the children of Lot. *Selah*

**9** Do to them as You did to Midian,  
as to Sisera and Jabin at the brook Kishon,

**10** who were destroyed at En-dor,  
who became dung for the ground.

**11** Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb,  
all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna,

**12** who said, "Let us take for ourselves  
the pastures of God."

**13** My God, make them like whirling dust,  
like chaff before the wind.

**14** As fire burns a forest,  
as a flame sets the mountains ablaze,

**15** so pursue them with Your tempest  
and terrify them with Your storm.

**16** Fill their faces with shame,  
so that they may seek Your name, LORD.

**17** Let them be put to shame and terrified forever;  
let them be disgraced and perish,

**18** so that they may know that You alone,  
whose name is the LORD,  
are the Most High over all the earth.

## Notes

- **vv6–8** — ten peoples ringing Israel on every side; no single war is known in which all of them joined. Gebal is Byblos on the coast north of Tyre, or a region south of the Dead Sea; the Hagrites lived east of the Jordan (1 Chronicles 5:10).
- **v8 "the children of Lot"** — Moab and Ammon (Genesis 19:36–38), here backed by Assyria.
- **vv9–12** — the victories of Deborah and Barak over Sisera and Jabin (Judges 4–5) and of Gideon over Midian, with its princes Oreb and Zeeb and kings Zebah and Zalmunna (Judges 7:25; 8:21).
- **v10 "at En-dor"** — near the Kishon and Mount Tabor; Judges does not name it, but it lies on the battlefield of Judges 4–5.
- **v13 "like whirling dust"** — *galgal*, something that rolls, like the tumbleweed or thistle heads that blow across the plain (Isaiah 17:13).
- **vv16, 18 "so that they may seek Your name… so that they may know"** — the prayer for shame ends in the hope that the nations will come to know the LORD.

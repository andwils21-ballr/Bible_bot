# Psalms 22

*For the director: on "The Doe of the Dawn." A psalm of David.*

## Why Have You Forsaken Me?

**1** My God, my God, why have You forsaken me?  
Far from my salvation are the words of my groaning.

**2** My God, I cry by day, and You do not answer,  
and by night, and there is no rest for me.

**3** Yet You are holy,  
enthroned on the praises of Israel.

**4** In You our fathers trusted;  
they trusted, and You rescued them.

**5** To You they cried out and were delivered;  
in You they trusted and were not put to shame.

**6** But I am a worm and not a man,  
scorned by men and despised by the people.

**7** All who see me mock me;  
they make mouths at me; they shake their heads:

**8** "He committed himself to the LORD; let Him rescue him;  
let Him deliver him, for He delights in him!"

**9** Yet You are the one who drew me from the womb;  
You made me trust at my mother's breasts.

**10** On You I was cast from birth;  
from my mother's womb You have been my God.

**11** Do not be far from me,  
for trouble is near,  
and there is no one to help.

## Like Water Poured Out

**12** Many bulls surround me;  
strong bulls of Bashan encircle me;

**13** they open their mouths wide at me,  
like a lion tearing and roaring.

**14** I am poured out like water,  
and all my bones are out of joint;  
my heart has become like wax;  
it is melted within me.

**15** My strength is dried up like a piece of pottery,  
and my tongue clings to my jaws;  
You lay me in the dust of death.

**16** For dogs surround me;  
a company of evildoers encircles me;  
they have pierced my hands and my feet.

**17** I can count all my bones;  
they look and stare at me;

**18** they divide my garments among them,  
and for my clothing they cast lots.

**19** But You, LORD, do not be far away;  
my strength, hurry to help me.

**20** Rescue my soul from the sword,  
my only life from the power of the dog.

**21** Save me from the mouth of the lion;  
from the horns of the wild oxen You have answered me.

## In the Great Congregation

**22** I will tell of Your name to my brothers;  
among the congregation I will praise You.

**23** You who fear the LORD, praise Him;  
all you descendants of Jacob, glorify Him;  
stand in awe of Him, all you descendants of Israel.

**24** For He has not despised or loathed the affliction of the afflicted,  
nor hidden His face from him,  
but heard when he cried to Him.

**25** From You comes my praise in the great congregation;  
I will pay my vows before those who fear Him.

**26** The humble will eat and be satisfied;  
those who seek Him will praise the LORD.  
May your hearts live forever!

**27** All the ends of the earth will remember and turn to the LORD,  
and all the families of the nations will bow down before You.

**28** For the kingdom belongs to the LORD,  
and He rules over the nations.

**29** All the prosperous of the earth will eat and bow down;  
before Him will kneel all who go down to the dust,  
and the one who cannot keep his soul alive.

**30** Offspring will serve Him;  
it will be told of the Lord to the generation to come.

**31** They will come and declare His righteousness  
to a people yet to be born, that He has done it.

## Notes

- **The title "on 'The Doe of the Dawn'"** — *al ayyelet ha-shachar*, the name of a tune.
- **v1 "My God, my God, why have You forsaken me?"** — *Eli Eli lamah azavtani*. Jesus cried these words from the cross in Aramaic, *Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani* (Mark 15:34; Matthew 27:46). The psalm moves from abandonment (vv1–21) to praise (vv22–31).
- **v1 "the words of my groaning"** — *sha'agati*, **my roaring**, the cry of a lion or of a man in agony (32:3; Job 3:24).
- **v8 "He committed himself to the LORD; let Him rescue him"** — the words the mockers spoke at the cross (Matthew 27:43). **Committed** is *gol*, **roll** (your way) **onto** the LORD (37:5).
- **v12 "strong bulls of Bashan"** — the rich cattle land east of the Jordan (Amos 4:1; Deuteronomy 32:14).
- **v16 "they have pierced my hands and my feet"** — the Leningrad text has *ka'ari*, **like a lion**, *like a lion my hands and my feet*, a line with no verb. The Greek, the Syriac and the Latin read **they have pierced** (or **dug**), and one of the oldest Hebrew copies, found near the Dead Sea, has *ka'aru*, a verb; the reading of the witnesses is followed here (witness rule 2).
- **v18 "they divide my garments among them"** — fulfilled at the cross, as John says in quoting the line (John 19:23–24; Mark 15:24).
- **v22 "I will tell of Your name to my brothers"** — quoted in Hebrews 2:12 as the words of Jesus. The turn from lament to praise is sudden: **You have answered me** (v21).
- **v24 "despised or loathed"** — *shiqqets*, **loathe**, as the word table has it.
- **vv27–31** — the praise widens to all the ends of the earth, to the dead (v29) and to those not yet born (v31). **He has done it** is the psalm's last word.

# Psalms 150

**1** Praise Yah!  
Praise God in His sanctuary;  
praise Him in the expanse of His might!

**2** Praise Him for His mighty deeds;  
praise Him for His abundant greatness!

**3** Praise Him with the blast of the ram's horn;  
praise Him with harp and lyre!

**4** Praise Him with tambourine and dancing;  
praise Him with strings and pipe!

**5** Praise Him with sounding cymbals;  
praise Him with clanging cymbals!

**6** Let everything that has breath praise Yah!  
Praise Yah!

---

## Notes

- **The whole psalm** — the close of the fifth book and of the Hebrew Psalter: where the other books end with a blessing (41:13; 72:18–19; 89:52; 106:48), this one is all praise; "praise" is said thirteen times.
- **v6 "everything that has breath"** — *neshamah*, the breath God blew into the first human (Genesis 2:7).

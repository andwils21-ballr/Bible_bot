# Psalms 1

**1** Blessed is the man  
who does not walk in the counsel of the wicked,  
nor stand in the way of sinners,  
nor sit in the seat of scoffers;

**2** but his delight is in the law of the LORD,  
and on His law he meditates day and night.

**3** He will be like a tree  
planted by streams of water,  
that yields its fruit in its season,  
and its leaf does not wither;  
in all that he does, he prospers.

**4** The wicked are not so,  
but are like chaff that the wind drives away.

**5** Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment,  
nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous;

**6** for the LORD knows the way of the righteous,  
but the way of the wicked will perish.

## Notes

- **The text of the Psalms** — rendered from the Hebrew. The Ethiopian Psalter, like the Greek, has a hundred and fifty-one psalms; Psalm 151 is rendered from the Greek. Verse numbers follow the English: where the Hebrew counts a psalm's title as verse 1, the title stands above the psalm without a number, and the English verse 1 is the Hebrew verse 2.
- **v1 "Blessed is the man"** — *ashrei ha-ish*, **happy**, **how fortunate**, the first word of the Psalter. Walking, standing and sitting: each line goes one step further into the company of the wicked.
- **v1 "scoffers"** — *letsim*, those who mock what is good, the fools of Proverbs (Proverbs 1:22; 9:7–8).
- **v2 "the law of the LORD"** — *torat YHWH*, **the instruction of the LORD**, the teaching given to Israel. **Meditates** is *yehgeh*, a low murmuring, reading half aloud (Joshua 1:8).
- **v3 "like a tree planted by streams of water"** — *Blessed is the man who trusts in the LORD… He is like a tree planted by water* (Jeremiah 17:7–8). **Planted** is *shatul*, transplanted, set in place on purpose.
- **v4 "like chaff that the wind drives away"** — the husks blown off the threshing floor (Job 21:18; Hosea 13:3).
- **v6 "the LORD knows the way of the righteous"** — *yodea*, knows with care and favor (Nahum 1:7). The psalm opens with **blessed** and ends with **perish**: the two ways of Deuteronomy 30:15–20.

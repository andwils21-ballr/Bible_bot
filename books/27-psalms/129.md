# Psalms 129

*A song of ascents.*

**1** Many a time they have afflicted me from my youth —  
let Israel now say —

**2** many a time they have afflicted me from my youth,  
yet they have not prevailed against me.

**3** The plowers plowed on my back;  
they made their furrows long.

**4** The LORD is righteous;  
He has cut the cords of the wicked.

**5** Let all who hate Zion  
be put to shame and turned back.

**6** Let them be like grass on the housetops,  
which withers before it is pulled up,

**7** with which the reaper does not fill his hand,  
nor the binder of sheaves his arms,

**8** nor do those who pass by say,  
"The blessing of the LORD be on you!  
We bless you in the name of the LORD!"

---

## Notes

- **v1 "from my youth"** — from Israel's beginning in Egypt (Hosea 11:1; Jeremiah 2:2).
- **v3 "The plowers plowed on my back"** — the lash on a captive's back, cutting long furrows; "their furrows," the read form *ma'anitam*, is a spelling variant of the written form.
- **v4 "He has cut the cords of the wicked"** — the cords that bound the captive, or the ropes of the plow.
- **v6 "like grass on the housetops"** — grass that sprouts in the thin soil of a flat roof and dies quickly (Isaiah 37:27).
- **v8** — the greeting harvesters gave each other (Ruth 2:4); no one will say it over the enemies' empty fields.

# Psalms 14

*For the director. Of David.*

**1** The fool says in his heart, "There is no God."  
They are corrupt; they do detestable deeds;  
there is no one who does good.

**2** The LORD looks down from heaven on the children of man,  
to see if there is anyone who understands,  
who seeks God.

**3** They have all turned aside; together they have become corrupt;  
there is no one who does good,  
not even one.

**4** Do they have no knowledge, all who do iniquity,  
who eat up my people as they eat bread,  
and do not call on the LORD?

**5** There they are in great terror,  
for God is with the generation of the righteous.

**6** You would put to shame the counsel of the poor,  
but the LORD is his refuge.

**7** Oh that the salvation of Israel would come out of Zion!  
When the LORD restores the fortunes of His people,  
let Jacob rejoice, let Israel be glad.

---

## Notes

- **The psalm** — repeated almost word for word as Psalm 53, which uses *God* (*Elohim*) where this psalm has *the LORD*, and differs in vv5–6.
- **vv1–3 "there is no one who does good, not even one"** — Paul gathers these lines with others into one charge, *None is righteous, no, not one* (Romans 3:10–12). The Greek of the psalm adds after v3 the lines Paul goes on to quote in Romans 3:13–18; they come from Psalms 5, 140, 10 and 36 and Isaiah 59, and are not in the Hebrew here.

# Psalms 149

**1** Praise Yah!  
Sing to the LORD a new song,  
His praise in the assembly of the faithful.

**2** Let Israel rejoice in its Maker;  
let the children of Zion be glad in their King.

**3** Let them praise His name with dancing;  
let them sing praises to Him with tambourine and lyre.

**4** For the LORD takes pleasure in His people;  
He adorns the humble with salvation.

**5** Let the faithful exult in glory;  
let them sing for joy on their beds.

**6** Let the high praises of God be in their throats  
and a two-edged sword in their hands,

**7** to execute vengeance on the nations  
and punishment on the peoples,

**8** to bind their kings with chains  
and their honored men with fetters of iron,

**9** to carry out on them the judgment written.  
This is honor for all His faithful ones.  
Praise Yah!

---

## Notes

- **v1 "a new song"** — as in 33:3, 96:1 and 98:1.
- **v2 "their King"** — the LORD as Israel's King (5:2; 145:1).
- **v5 "on their beds"** — where they once lay awake in fear or grief (6:6), they now sing.
- **vv6–9** — the praise of God and the sword together, carrying out "the judgment written," the judgment already decreed against the nations that oppressed Israel (Deuteronomy 32:41–43; Isaiah 41:15–16). The New Testament gives God's people the sword of the Spirit, the word of God (Ephesians 6:17; Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 19:15).

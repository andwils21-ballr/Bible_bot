# Psalms 27

*Of David.*

**1** The LORD is my light and my salvation;  
whom shall I fear?  
The LORD is the stronghold of my life;  
of whom shall I be afraid?

**2** When evildoers came near me  
to eat up my flesh,  
my adversaries and my enemies,  
they stumbled and fell.

**3** Though an army camp against me,  
my heart will not fear;  
though war rise against me,  
even then I will be confident.

**4** One thing I have asked of the LORD;  
that I will seek:  
to dwell in the house of the LORD  
all the days of my life,  
to gaze on the beauty of the LORD  
and to inquire in His temple.

**5** For He will hide me in His shelter  
in the day of trouble;  
He will conceal me in the hiding place of His tent;  
He will lift me high on a rock.

**6** Now my head will be lifted up  
above my enemies around me,  
and I will offer in His tent  
sacrifices with shouts of joy;  
I will sing and make music to the LORD.

**7** Hear, LORD, my voice when I call;  
be gracious to me and answer me.

**8** My heart says of You, "Seek My face."  
Your face, LORD, I will seek.

**9** Do not hide Your face from me;  
do not turn Your servant away in anger;  
You have been my help.  
Do not cast me off or forsake me,  
God of my salvation.

**10** For my father and my mother have forsaken me,  
but the LORD will take me in.

**11** Teach me Your way, LORD,  
and lead me on a level path  
because of those who watch me.

**12** Do not give me up to the will of my adversaries,  
for false witnesses have risen against me,  
and they breathe out violence.

**13** If I had not believed that I would see the goodness of the LORD  
in the land of the living —

**14** Wait for the LORD;  
be strong, and let your heart take courage;  
wait for the LORD!

## Notes

- **v1 "The LORD is my light"** — *ori*; *when I sit in darkness, the LORD will be a light to me* (Micah 7:8); *the LORD will be your everlasting light* (Isaiah 60:19–20; John 8:12).
- **v2 "to eat up my flesh"** — enemies as wild beasts (14:4; Daniel 7:5).
- **v4 "One thing I have asked of the LORD"** — the center of the psalm: not deliverance only, but to live in God's presence (23:6). **To inquire**, *le-vaqqer*, is to seek guidance, or to look with delight.
- **v5 "He will hide me in His shelter"** — *sukko*, His booth, His tent: the hospitality of the host who must protect his guest (15:1; 61:4).
- **v8 "My heart says of You, 'Seek My face'"** — the Hebrew is compressed, *To You my heart has said, Seek My face*: the heart repeats God's own invitation and answers it.
- **v10 "For my father and my mother have forsaken me"** — or **even if** they forsake me; **take me in**, *ya'asfeni*, as a stray is gathered into a household.
- **v13 "If I had not believed"** — *lule*, marked in the Hebrew text with special dots above and below, the scribes' sign of a doubtful word; the Greek lacks it, and reads *I believe that I will see*. With the word, the sentence breaks off: *If I had not believed… (I would have perished)*.
- **v13 "the land of the living"** — this life, as against Sheol (116:9; Isaiah 38:11).
- **v14 "Wait for the LORD"** — *qavveh*, the hope of 25:3; the psalm ends speaking to the soul, or to every hearer.

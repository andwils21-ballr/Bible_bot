# Psalms 61

*For the director: on a stringed instrument. Of David.*

**1** Hear my cry, God;  
listen to my prayer.

**2** From the end of the earth I call to You  
when my heart grows faint;  
lead me to the rock that is higher than I,

**3** for You have been a refuge for me,  
a strong tower against the enemy.

**4** Let me live as a guest in Your tent forever;  
let me take refuge in the shelter of Your wings. *Selah*

**5** For You, God, have heard my vows;  
You have given me the inheritance of those who fear Your name.

**6** Add days to the days of the king;  
may his years be as generation after generation.

**7** May he sit enthroned forever before God;  
appoint loyal love and truth to keep him.

**8** So I will sing praise to Your name forever,  
as I pay my vows day after day.

---

## Notes

- **v2 "From the end of the earth"** — far from Jerusalem, whether in exile or in flight; or at the edge of Sheol (Jonah 2:2–4).
- **v2 "the rock that is higher than I"** — a refuge the psalmist cannot climb by himself; God must lead him up to it.
- **v4 "Let me live as a guest in Your tent forever"** — *agurah*, from *gur*, as the word table has it (15:1; 27:4–5).
- **v4 "the shelter of Your wings"** — as in 17:8 and 57:1.
- **vv6–7 "Add days to the days of the king"** — a prayer for the king in the middle of the psalm (21:4); if David is the speaker, he prays for himself in the third person.
- **v7 "appoint loyal love and truth to keep him"** — *chesed* and *emet*, God's guards for the king (40:11; 57:3).

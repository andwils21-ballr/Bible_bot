# Psalms 6

*For the director: with stringed instruments, on the eighth. A psalm of David.*

**1** LORD, do not rebuke me in Your anger,  
nor discipline me in Your wrath.

**2** Be gracious to me, LORD, for I am fading away;  
heal me, LORD, for my bones are shaken.

**3** My soul also is greatly shaken.  
But You, LORD, how long?

**4** Turn, LORD, rescue my soul;  
save me for the sake of Your loyal love.

**5** For in death there is no remembrance of You;  
in Sheol who will praise You?

**6** I am weary with my sighing;  
every night I make my bed swim;  
I drench my couch with my tears.

**7** My eye wastes away with grief;  
it grows old because of all my foes.

**8** Depart from me, all who do iniquity,  
for the LORD has heard the sound of my weeping.

**9** The LORD has heard my plea;  
the LORD accepts my prayer.

**10** All my enemies will be put to shame and greatly shaken;  
they will turn back and be put to shame in a moment.

---

## Notes

- **The psalm** — the first of the seven psalms the Church has prayed as the **penitential psalms** (6, 32, 38, 51, 102, 130, 143).
- **v8 "Depart from me, all who do iniquity"** — the words Jesus will say on the last day (Matthew 7:23; Luke 13:27).

# Psalms 136

**1** Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**2** Give thanks to the God of gods,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**3** Give thanks to the Lord of lords,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**4** to Him who alone does great wonders,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**5** to Him who by understanding made the heavens,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**6** to Him who spread out the earth above the waters,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**7** to Him who made the great lights,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**8** the sun to rule by day,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**9** the moon and stars to rule by night,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**10** to Him who struck down Egypt in their firstborn,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**11** and brought Israel out from among them,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**12** with a strong hand and an outstretched arm,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**13** to Him who divided the Sea of Reeds in two,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**14** and made Israel pass through the middle of it,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**15** but shook off Pharaoh and his army into the Sea of Reeds,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**16** to Him who led His people through the wilderness,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**17** to Him who struck down great kings,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**18** and killed mighty kings,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**19** Sihon king of the Amorites,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**20** and Og king of Bashan,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**21** and gave their land as an inheritance,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**22** an inheritance to Israel His servant,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**23** who remembered us in our low estate,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**24** and rescued us from our foes,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**25** who gives food to all flesh,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**26** Give thanks to the God of heaven,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

---

## Notes

- **The refrain** — *ki le'olam chasdo*, after every line, twenty-six times; one part may have been sung by a leader and the other by the people (2 Chronicles 7:3, 6; Ezra 3:11). The opening line is the same as 106:1, 107:1 and 118:1.

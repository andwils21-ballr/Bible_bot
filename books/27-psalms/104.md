# Psalms 104

**1** Bless the LORD, my soul.  
LORD my God, You are very great;  
You are clothed with splendor and majesty,

**2** wrapping Yourself in light as in a garment,  
stretching out the heavens like a tent curtain.

**3** He lays the beams of His upper rooms on the waters;  
He makes the clouds His chariot;  
He walks on the wings of the wind.

**4** He makes the winds His messengers,  
flames of fire His servants.

**5** He set the earth on its foundations,  
so that it will never be moved.

**6** You covered it with the deep as with a garment;  
the waters stood above the mountains.

**7** At Your rebuke they fled;  
at the sound of Your thunder they hurried away.

**8** The mountains rose, the valleys sank down  
to the place You appointed for them.

**9** You set a boundary they may not pass,  
so that they will not again cover the earth.

**10** He sends springs into the valleys;  
they flow between the hills.

**11** They give drink to every beast of the field;  
the wild donkeys quench their thirst.

**12** Beside them the birds of the heavens dwell;  
they sing among the branches.

**13** He waters the mountains from His upper rooms;  
the earth is satisfied with the fruit of Your works.

**14** He makes grass grow for the cattle  
and plants for people to cultivate,  
to bring forth food from the earth,

**15** wine that gladdens the human heart,  
oil to make the face shine,  
and bread that strengthens the human heart.

**16** The trees of the LORD are well watered,  
the cedars of Lebanon that He planted,

**17** where the birds make their nests;  
the stork has its home in the fir trees.

**18** The high mountains are for the wild goats;  
the rocks are a refuge for the rock badgers.

**19** He made the moon to mark the seasons;  
the sun knows its time for setting.

**20** You make darkness, and it is night,  
when all the beasts of the forest creep about.

**21** The young lions roar for their prey,  
seeking their food from God.

**22** When the sun rises, they withdraw  
and lie down in their dens.

**23** People go out to their work  
and to their labor until evening.

**24** How many are Your works, LORD!  
In wisdom You have made them all;  
the earth is full of Your creatures.

**25** There is the sea, great and wide,  
teeming with creatures beyond number,  
living things both small and great.

**26** There the ships go,  
and Leviathan, which You formed to play in it.

**27** All of them look to You  
to give them their food in due season.

**28** When You give it to them, they gather it up;  
when You open Your hand, they are satisfied with good things.

**29** When You hide Your face, they are dismayed;  
when You take away their breath, they die  
and return to their dust.

**30** When You send out Your Spirit, they are created,  
and You renew the face of the ground.

**31** May the glory of the LORD endure forever;  
may the LORD rejoice in His works,

**32** who looks on the earth, and it trembles,  
who touches the mountains, and they smoke.

**33** I will sing to the LORD as long as I live;  
I will sing praises to my God while I have being.

**34** May my meditation be pleasing to Him;  
I will rejoice in the LORD.

**35** Let sinners be consumed from the earth,  
and let the wicked be no more.  
Bless the LORD, my soul.  
Praise Yah!

## Notes

- **The whole psalm** — creation sung in roughly the order of Genesis 1: light (v2), the heavens (v2), the waters separated and the land (vv5–9), plants (v14), sun and moon (v19), sea creatures (v25), animals and people (vv21–23), and God's breath giving life (vv29–30).
- **v4 "He makes the winds His messengers"** — *ruchot* can be "winds" or "spirits," *mal'akhav* "His messengers"; the Greek reads "His angels spirits," and Hebrews 1:7 quotes it so.
- **vv6–9 "the waters stood above the mountains… You set a boundary"** — the waters of Genesis 1:2, 9, and the promise after the flood (Genesis 9:11; Job 38:8–11).
- **v15 "wine… oil… bread"** — the three staples of the land (Deuteronomy 7:13).
- **v18 "the rock badgers"** — *shephanim*, the hyrax, a small animal of the rocks (Proverbs 30:26).
- **v26 "Leviathan, which You formed to play in it"** — in 74:14 the monster God crushed; here a creature at play in the sea God made. The line can also mean "to play with it," as in Job 41:5.
- **vv29–30 "their breath… Your Spirit"** — the same word, *ruach*: God takes away their breath and they die; He sends His Spirit and they are created (Genesis 2:7; Job 34:14–15).
- **v35 "Praise Yah!"** — *hallelu Yah*, "hallelujah," its first appearance in the Psalms.

# Psalms 24

*Of David. A psalm.*

**1** The earth is the LORD's, and all that fills it,  
the world and those who live in it,

**2** for He has founded it on the seas  
and established it on the rivers.

**3** Who shall go up the mountain of the LORD?  
Who shall stand in His holy place?

**4** The one who has clean hands and a pure heart,  
who has not lifted up his soul to what is false  
and has not sworn deceitfully.

**5** He will receive a blessing from the LORD  
and righteousness from the God of his salvation.

**6** Such is the generation of those who seek Him,  
who seek Your face, God of Jacob. *Selah*

**7** Lift up your heads, you gates,  
and be lifted up, you everlasting doors,  
that the King of glory may come in.

**8** Who is this King of glory?  
The LORD, strong and mighty,  
the LORD, mighty in battle.

**9** Lift up your heads, you gates,  
and lift them up, you everlasting doors,  
that the King of glory may come in.

**10** Who is He, this King of glory?  
The LORD of hosts,  
He is the King of glory. *Selah*

## Notes

- **v1 "The earth is the LORD's, and all that fills it"** — quoted by Paul of food offered to idols: everything is the Lord's (1 Corinthians 10:26; Exodus 19:5).
- **v2 "founded it on the seas"** — the earth set firm above the waters of the deep (136:6; Genesis 1:9–10).
- **vv3–6** — a gate liturgy like Psalm 15: the question at the entrance of the temple and the answer.
- **v4 "who has not lifted up his soul to what is false"** — the written text has *nafsho*, **his soul**, followed here; the read form has *nafshi*, **My soul**, God's own life, as in *you shall not take up the name of the LORD your God in vain* (Exodus 20:7).
- **v6 "God of Jacob"** — the Hebrew has only *Jacob*: *who seek Your face, Jacob*; the Greek and Syriac read **the face of the God of Jacob**, followed here, since Jacob is not the one being sought.
- **vv7–10 "Lift up your heads, you gates"** — the ark of the LORD coming up into Jerusalem (2 Samuel 6:12–15), the gates told to grow tall for the King who enters. The Church has sung the verses of Christ's ascension.
- **v8 "mighty in battle"** — *The LORD is a man of war* (Exodus 15:3). The ark went before Israel in battle (Numbers 10:35).
- **v10 "The LORD of hosts"** — *YHWH tseva'ot*, the LORD of the armies of heaven and of Israel (1 Samuel 17:45), the answer to the question asked twice.

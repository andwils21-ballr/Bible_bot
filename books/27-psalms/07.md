# Psalms 7

*A Shiggaion of David, which he sang to the LORD concerning Cush, a Benjaminite.*

**1** LORD my God, in You I take refuge;  
save me from all who pursue me, and rescue me,

**2** or like a lion they will tear my soul,  
dragging it away, with no one to rescue.

**3** LORD my God, if I have done this,  
if there is injustice in my hands,

**4** if I have repaid with evil the one at peace with me,  
or plundered my enemy without cause,

**5** let the enemy pursue my soul and overtake it,  
and trample my life to the ground,  
and lay my honor in the dust. *Selah*

**6** Arise, LORD, in Your anger;  
lift Yourself up against the fury of my foes;  
awake for me; You have commanded judgment.

**7** Let the assembly of the peoples gather around You,  
and over it return on high.

**8** The LORD judges the peoples;  
judge me, LORD, according to my righteousness  
and according to the integrity that is in me.

**9** Let the evil of the wicked come to an end,  
and establish the righteous,  
You who test hearts and minds, righteous God.

**10** My shield is with God,  
who saves the upright in heart.

**11** God is a righteous judge,  
and a God who is angry every day.

**12** If a man does not turn back, He will sharpen His sword;  
He has bent His bow and made it ready.

**13** He has prepared for him the weapons of death;  
He makes His arrows into fiery shafts.

**14** The wicked man is in labor with iniquity;  
he has conceived trouble and given birth to falsehood.

**15** He dug a pit and hollowed it out,  
and fell into the hole he was making.

**16** His trouble returns on his own head,  
and his violence comes down on his own skull.

**17** I will give thanks to the LORD for His righteousness,  
and sing praise to the name of the LORD Most High.

---

## Notes

- **v4 "or plundered my enemy without cause"** — *va-achalletsah*, which can also mean **rescued**: *I who rescued my enemy without cause*, as David spared Saul (1 Samuel 24:10–11).

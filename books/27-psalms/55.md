# Psalms 55

*For the director: with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David.*

**1** Give ear to my prayer, God,  
and do not hide Yourself from my plea.

**2** Attend to me and answer me;  
I am restless in my complaint, and I moan,

**3** because of the voice of the enemy,  
because of the pressure of the wicked;  
for they bring down trouble on me,  
and in anger they bear a grudge against me.

**4** My heart writhes within me;  
the terrors of death have fallen on me.

**5** Fear and trembling come on me,  
and horror covers me.

**6** I said, "Oh that I had wings like a dove!  
I would fly away and be at rest;

**7** I would wander far away;  
I would lodge in the wilderness; *Selah*

**8** I would hurry to my place of refuge  
from the raging wind and the storm."

**9** Swallow them up, Lord; divide their tongues,  
for I see violence and strife in the city.

**10** Day and night they go around it on its walls,  
and iniquity and trouble are within it;

**11** ruin is within it;  
oppression and deceit do not depart from its square.

**12** For it is not an enemy who taunts me,  
or I could bear it;  
it is not one who hates me who exalts himself against me,  
or I could hide from him.

**13** But it is you, a man of my own rank,  
my companion, my familiar friend.

**14** We used to share sweet counsel together;  
we walked in the house of God with the throng.

**15** Let death come on them by surprise;  
let them go down alive to Sheol,  
for evil is in their lodging, within them.

**16** As for me, I call to God,  
and the LORD will save me.

**17** Evening and morning and at noon  
I complain and moan,  
and He hears my voice.

**18** He redeems my soul in peace  
from the battle against me,  
for many are with me.

**19** God will hear and humble them,  
He who sits enthroned from of old, *Selah*  
those who have no changes  
and do not fear God.

**20** He stretched out his hand against those at peace with him;  
he broke his covenant.

**21** His speech was smoother than butter,  
but war was in his heart;  
his words were softer than oil,  
but they were drawn swords.

**22** Cast your burden on the LORD,  
and He will sustain you;  
He will never let the righteous be moved.

**23** But You, God, will bring them down  
to the pit of destruction;  
men of bloodshed and deceit  
will not live out half their days.  
But I will trust in You.

## Notes

- **vv6–8 "Oh that I had wings like a dove!"** — the longing to escape, as Jeremiah wished for a lodging in the desert (Jeremiah 9:2).
- **v9 "Swallow them up, Lord; divide their tongues"** — *balla… pallag*, as at Babel, when God confused the speech of the builders (Genesis 11:7–9).
- **vv12–14 "it is not an enemy who taunts me… but it is you"** — the betrayal by a close friend, as Ahithophel betrayed David (2 Samuel 15:12, 31); compare 41:9.
- **v14 "We used to share sweet counsel together"** — *namtiq sod*, **made counsel sweet**, close friendship (Job 29:4).
- **v15 "Let death come on them by surprise"** — the read form, *yashi mavet*; the written form joins the words, *yeshimot*, **desolations**. **Go down alive to Sheol** as Korah's company did (Numbers 16:30–33). **Their lodging** is *megurim*, from *gur*, the place where one lives as a guest.
- **v17 "Evening and morning and at noon"** — the three times of prayer (Daniel 6:10).
- **v18 "for many are with me"** — or **against me**; the Hebrew *be-rabbim hayu immadi* can mean either.
- **v19 "those who have no changes"** — *chalifot*, no change of heart, or no reversals of fortune, and so no fear of God.
- **v22 "Cast your burden on the LORD"** — *yehavkha*, **what He has given you**, your lot; *casting all your anxieties on Him, because He cares for you* (1 Peter 5:7).

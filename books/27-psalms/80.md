# Psalms 80

*For the director: on "Lilies of Testimony." Of Asaph. A psalm.*

**1** Shepherd of Israel, give ear,  
You who lead Joseph like a flock;  
You who are enthroned on the cherubim, shine forth.

**2** Before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh,  
stir up Your might  
and come to save us.

**3** God, restore us;  
make Your face shine, so that we may be saved.

**4** LORD God of hosts,  
how long will Your anger smoke against the prayers of Your people?

**5** You have fed them with the bread of tears  
and given them tears to drink by the measure.

**6** You make us a cause of strife to our neighbors,  
and our enemies laugh among themselves.

**7** God of hosts, restore us;  
make Your face shine, so that we may be saved.

**8** You brought a vine out of Egypt;  
You drove out the nations and planted it.

**9** You cleared the ground for it;  
it took deep root and filled the land.

**10** The mountains were covered with its shade,  
the mighty cedars with its branches.

**11** It sent out its branches to the sea  
and its shoots to the River.

**12** Why then have You broken down its walls,  
so that all who pass along the road pluck its fruit?

**13** The boar from the forest ravages it,  
and whatever moves in the field feeds on it.

**14** God of hosts, turn again;  
look down from heaven and see;  
take care of this vine,

**15** the stock that Your right hand planted,  
and the son You made strong for Yourself.

**16** It is burned with fire; it is cut down;  
they perish at the rebuke of Your face.

**17** Let Your hand be on the man of Your right hand,  
on the son of man You made strong for Yourself.

**18** Then we will not turn back from You;  
give us life, and we will call on Your name.

**19** LORD God of hosts, restore us;  
make Your face shine, so that we may be saved.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "Joseph… Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh"** — the tribes that marched behind the ark in the wilderness (Numbers 2:18–24); the psalm prays for the northern kingdom, perhaps at its fall to Assyria (2 Kings 17).
- **v1 "enthroned on the cherubim"** — as in 1 Samuel 4:4; the cherubim are those over the ark (Exodus 25:22).
- **vv3, 7, 19** — the refrain, the same each time except that the name of God grows: "God," then "God of hosts," then "LORD God of hosts."
- **v3 "make Your face shine"** — the priestly blessing (Numbers 6:25), as in 67:1.
- **v5 "by the measure"** — *shalish*, a third, probably a measure of a third of some larger unit (Isaiah 40:12).
- **vv8–16 "a vine out of Egypt"** — Israel as God's vine, as in Isaiah 5:1–7 and Jeremiah 2:21; Jesus takes up the picture: *I am the true vine* (John 15:1).
- **v10 "the mighty cedars"** — literally "the cedars of God"; "of God" as a way of saying very great, as in 36:6.
- **v13 "the boar from the forest"** — the *ayin* of *mi-ya'ar*, "from the forest," is written raised above the line in Hebrew manuscripts; the Talmud (Kiddushin 30a) counts it as the middle letter of the Psalms.
- **vv15, 17 "the son… the man of Your right hand… the son of man"** — the king, or Israel itself (Exodus 4:22), whom God raised up; "man of Your right hand" may play on Benjamin, "son of the right hand." These stay lowercase.

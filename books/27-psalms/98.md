# Psalms 98

*A psalm.*

**1** Sing to the LORD a new song,  
for He has done wonders;  
His right hand and His holy arm  
have won Him the victory.

**2** The LORD has made known His salvation;  
He has revealed His righteousness in the sight of the nations.

**3** He has remembered His loyal love and His faithfulness  
to the house of Israel;  
all the ends of the earth have seen  
the salvation of our God.

**4** Shout to the LORD, all the earth;  
break out in joyful song and sing praises.

**5** Sing praises to the LORD with the lyre,  
with the lyre and the sound of song.

**6** With trumpets and the sound of the ram's horn  
shout before the King, the LORD.

**7** Let the sea roar, and all that fills it,  
the world and those who live in it.

**8** Let the rivers clap their hands;  
let the mountains sing for joy together

**9** before the LORD, for He comes to judge the earth.  
He will judge the world in righteousness,  
and the peoples with equity.

---

## Notes

- **v3 "He has remembered His loyal love and His faithfulness to the house of Israel"** — Mary's song takes this up: *He has helped His servant Israel, in remembrance of His mercy* (Luke 1:54).

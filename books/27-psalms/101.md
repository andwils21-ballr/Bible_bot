# Psalms 101

*Of David. A psalm.*

**1** I will sing of loyal love and justice;  
to You, LORD, I will sing praises.

**2** I will give heed to the blameless way —  
when will You come to me?  
I will walk with a blameless heart  
within my house.

**3** I will not set before my eyes  
anything worthless.  
I hate the work of those who fall away;  
it will not cling to me.

**4** A perverse heart will be far from me;  
I will have nothing to do with evil.

**5** Whoever slanders his neighbor in secret  
I will destroy;  
whoever has haughty eyes and a proud heart  
I will not endure.

**6** My eyes will be on the faithful of the land,  
so that they may live with me;  
whoever walks in the blameless way  
will serve me.

**7** No one who practices deceit  
will live within my house;  
no one who speaks lies  
will stand before my eyes.

**8** Morning by morning I will destroy  
all the wicked of the land,  
cutting off all evildoers  
from the city of the LORD.

---

## Notes

- **The whole psalm** — a king's vow, for his own life (vv2–4) and for his court and city (vv5–8). The pronouns for the king stay lowercase.
- **v2 "when will You come to me?"** — the king longs for God's presence as he keeps the blameless way (Exodus 20:24, *I will come to you and bless you*).
- **v3 "anything worthless"** — *devar beliya'al*, a thing of worthlessness or wickedness; "those who fall away" (*setim*) are those who swerve from God's way.
- **v4 "I will have nothing to do with evil"** — literally "I will not know evil."
- **v8 "Morning by morning"** — the time when the king sat to judge cases (2 Samuel 15:2; Jeremiah 21:12).

# Psalms 32

*Of David. A Maskil.*

**1** Blessed is the one whose transgression is forgiven,  
whose sin is covered.

**2** Blessed is the man to whom the LORD counts no iniquity,  
and in whose spirit there is no deceit.

**3** When I kept silent, my bones wasted away  
through my groaning all day long.

**4** For day and night Your hand was heavy on me;  
my strength was dried up as by the heat of summer. *Selah*

**5** I made my sin known to You,  
and I did not cover my iniquity;  
I said, "I will confess my transgressions to the LORD,"  
and You forgave the iniquity of my sin. *Selah*

**6** Therefore let everyone who is faithful pray to You  
at a time when You may be found;  
surely in the rush of great waters  
they will not reach him.

**7** You are a hiding place for me;  
You keep me from trouble;  
You surround me with shouts of deliverance. *Selah*

**8** I will instruct you and teach you in the way you should go;  
I will counsel you with My eye on you.

**9** Do not be like a horse or a mule, without understanding,  
whose trappings must be bit and bridle to hold it in,  
or it will not come near you.

**10** Many are the sorrows of the wicked,  
but loyal love surrounds the one who trusts in the LORD.

**11** Be glad in the LORD and rejoice, you righteous;  
shout for joy, all you upright in heart!

## Notes

- **The title "A Maskil"** — *maskil*, from *haskil*, **to give insight**, the heading of thirteen psalms; perhaps a teaching song, or a skilled one. The word returns in v8, *askilkha*, **I will instruct you**.
- **The psalm** — the second of the seven penitential psalms (see 6).
- **vv1–2 "Blessed is the one whose transgression is forgiven"** — three words for sin (*pesha*, **transgression**; *chata'ah*, **sin**; *avon*, **iniquity**) and three for forgiveness (**forgiven**, *nesui*, **lifted away**; **covered**; **not counted**). Paul quotes the verses of righteousness counted apart from works (Romans 4:6–8).
- **v3 "When I kept silent"** — silence about his sin, not silence before men; the groaning was the body's protest.
- **v4 "my strength was dried up"** — *leshaddi*, a word found only here and in Numbers 11:8, perhaps **my vitality**, **my sap**.
- **v5 "I will confess my transgressions to the LORD"** — *If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us* (1 John 1:9; Proverbs 28:13).
- **v6 "at a time when You may be found"** — *Seek the LORD while He may be found* (Isaiah 55:6). **The rush of great waters** is the flood of judgment.
- **v8 "I will instruct you… with My eye on you"** — God now speaks; His pronouns are capitalized. Some take the speaker to be the psalmist teaching others, as in 51:13.
- **v9 "like a horse or a mule"** — animals that must be led by force; the forgiven are led by God's eye.

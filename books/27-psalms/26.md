# Psalms 26

*Of David.*

**1** Vindicate me, LORD,  
for I have walked in my integrity,  
and I have trusted in the LORD without wavering.

**2** Test me, LORD, and try me;  
refine my mind and my heart.

**3** For Your loyal love is before my eyes,  
and I walk in Your faithfulness.

**4** I do not sit with men of falsehood,  
nor do I go in with those who hide what they are.

**5** I hate the assembly of evildoers,  
and I will not sit with the wicked.

**6** I wash my hands in innocence  
and go around Your altar, LORD,

**7** to make the sound of thanksgiving heard  
and tell of all Your wonders.

**8** LORD, I love the dwelling of Your house  
and the place where Your glory lives.

**9** Do not gather my soul away with sinners,  
nor my life with men of bloodshed,

**10** in whose hands are evil schemes,  
and whose right hand is full of bribes.

**11** But as for me, I walk in my integrity;  
redeem me, and be gracious to me.

**12** My foot stands on level ground;  
in the assemblies I will bless the LORD.

## Notes

- **v1 "Vindicate me, LORD"** — *shofteni*, **judge me**: the plea of an innocent man who asks for a verdict, as in 7:8 and 17:1–3.
- **v1 "my integrity"** — *tummi*, the word of Job 2:3 and 27:5; it frames the psalm (v11).
- **v2 "my mind and my heart"** — *kilyotai ve-libbi*, **my kidneys and my heart** (7:9; 16:7). **Refine** is the read form *tsorfah*; the written form has the same letters in another order.
- **v4 "those who hide what they are"** — *na'alamim*, the concealed, hypocrites.
- **v6 "I wash my hands in innocence"** — a sign of innocence before God (73:13; Deuteronomy 21:6–7), before going to the altar. **Go around Your altar** is the procession of worshipers (118:27).
- **v8 "the place where Your glory lives"** — *mishkan kevodekha*, the dwelling of the glory that filled the tabernacle (Exodus 40:34–35).
- **v9 "Do not gather my soul away with sinners"** — *al te'esof*, as death gathers a man to his people: let me not die the death of the wicked.
- **v12 "level ground"** — *mishor*, a place where the foot cannot slip (27:11); **the assemblies** are the gatherings of worshipers.

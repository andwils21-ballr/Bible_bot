# Psalms 114

**1** When Israel went out from Egypt,  
the house of Jacob from a people of strange speech,

**2** Judah became His sanctuary,  
Israel His dominion.

**3** The sea saw and fled;  
the Jordan turned back.

**4** The mountains skipped like rams,  
the hills like lambs.

**5** What ails you, sea, that you flee?  
Jordan, that you turn back?

**6** Mountains, that you skip like rams?  
Hills, like lambs?

**7** Tremble, earth, before the Lord,  
before the God of Jacob,

**8** who turns the rock into a pool of water,  
the flint into a spring of water.

## Notes

- **v1 and the numbering** — the Greek Psalter joins 114 and 115 as one psalm and splits 116 into two; the Hebrew numbering is followed.

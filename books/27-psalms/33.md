# Psalms 33

**1** Shout for joy in the LORD, you righteous;  
praise is fitting for the upright.

**2** Give thanks to the LORD with the lyre;  
make music to Him with the ten-stringed harp.

**3** Sing to Him a new song;  
play skillfully, with shouts of joy.

**4** For the word of the LORD is upright,  
and all His work is done in faithfulness.

**5** He loves righteousness and justice;  
the earth is full of the loyal love of the LORD.

**6** By the word of the LORD the heavens were made,  
and by the breath of His mouth all their host.

**7** He gathers the waters of the sea as a heap;  
He puts the deeps in storehouses.

**8** Let all the earth fear the LORD;  
let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of Him.

**9** For He spoke, and it came to be;  
He commanded, and it stood firm.

**10** The LORD brings the counsel of the nations to nothing;  
He frustrates the plans of the peoples.

**11** The counsel of the LORD stands forever,  
the plans of His heart to all generations.

**12** Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD,  
the people He has chosen as His inheritance.

**13** The LORD looks down from heaven;  
He sees all the children of man;

**14** from the place where He sits enthroned He looks out  
on all the inhabitants of the earth,

**15** He who forms the hearts of them all  
and understands all their deeds.

**16** The king is not saved by his great army;  
a warrior is not rescued by his great strength.

**17** The horse is a false hope for victory,  
and by its great might it cannot rescue.

**18** The eye of the LORD is on those who fear Him,  
on those who hope in His loyal love,

**19** to deliver their soul from death  
and keep them alive in famine.

**20** Our soul waits for the LORD;  
He is our help and our shield.

**21** For our heart is glad in Him,  
because we trust in His holy name.

**22** Let Your loyal love, LORD, be upon us,  
even as we hope in You.

---

## Notes

- **v6 "By the word of the LORD the heavens were made"** — *And God said… and it was so* (Genesis 1:6–9). **The breath of His mouth** is *ruach piv*: the Church Fathers read the verse of the Word and the Spirit together at creation (Genesis 1:2; John 1:1–3).

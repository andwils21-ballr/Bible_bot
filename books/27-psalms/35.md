# Psalms 35

*Of David.*

**1** Contend, LORD, with those who contend with me;  
fight against those who fight against me.

**2** Take hold of shield and buckler  
and rise up to help me.

**3** Draw out the spear and close the way against those who pursue me;  
say to my soul, "I am your salvation."

**4** Let them be put to shame and disgraced  
who seek my life;  
let them be turned back and confounded  
who devise evil against me.

**5** Let them be like chaff before the wind,  
with the messenger of the LORD driving them on.

**6** Let their way be dark and slippery,  
with the messenger of the LORD pursuing them.

**7** For without cause they hid their net for me;  
without cause they dug a pit for my soul.

**8** Let ruin come on him when he does not know it,  
and let the net he hid catch him;  
let him fall into it, to his ruin.

**9** My soul will rejoice in the LORD,  
exulting in His salvation.

**10** All my bones will say,  
"LORD, who is like You,  
who rescues the poor from one too strong for him,  
the poor and needy from the one who robs him?"

**11** Violent witnesses rise up;  
they ask me of things I do not know.

**12** They repay me evil for good;  
my soul is bereaved.

**13** But I, when they were sick, was clothed in sackcloth;  
I afflicted my soul with fasting,  
and my prayer returned to my own bosom.

**14** I went about as for my friend or my brother;  
as one who mourns for his mother, I bowed down in mourning.

**15** But at my stumbling they rejoiced and gathered;  
they gathered against me, the lame I did not know;  
they tore at me and did not stop.

**16** Like godless mockers at a feast,  
they gnash their teeth at me.

**17** Lord, how long will You look on?  
Bring back my soul from their ruin,  
my only life from the young lions.

**18** I will give You thanks in the great congregation;  
among a mighty people I will praise You.

**19** Let not those who are my enemies without reason rejoice over me,  
nor those who hate me without cause wink the eye.

**20** For they do not speak peace,  
but against the quiet ones of the land  
they devise words of deceit.

**21** They open their mouths wide against me;  
they say, "Aha, aha! Our eyes have seen it!"

**22** You have seen, LORD; do not keep silent!  
Lord, do not be far from me!

**23** Wake up and rouse Yourself for my right,  
my God and my Lord, for my cause!

**24** Vindicate me, LORD my God, according to Your righteousness,  
and let them not rejoice over me.

**25** Let them not say in their hearts, "Aha, our desire!"  
Let them not say, "We have swallowed him up."

**26** Let them be put to shame and confounded together  
who rejoice at my trouble;  
let them be clothed with shame and disgrace  
who make themselves great against me.

**27** Let those who delight in my righteousness shout for joy and be glad,  
and say always, "Great is the LORD,  
who delights in the peace of His servant!"

**28** Then my tongue will tell of Your righteousness,  
of Your praise all the day long.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "Contend… fight… Take hold of shield and buckler"** — God called on as advocate in court (**contend**, *riv*) and as warrior in battle. **Close the way** is *segor*, which some take as **battle-axe**.
- **vv5–6 "the messenger of the LORD"** — *mal'akh YHWH*, as in 34:7, now driving the enemy before him.
- **v7 "without cause"** — *chinnam*, twice, the word of Job 1:9 and 2:3; again in v19.
- **v13 "my prayer returned to my own bosom"** — either bowed down in prayer, head to the chest (1 Kings 18:42), or the prayer for them came back unanswered on the one who prayed it (Matthew 10:13).
- **v15 "the lame I did not know"** — *nekhim*, **smitten ones**, perhaps **cripples** (2 Samuel 4:4); the word is uncertain, and some read *nokhrim*, **strangers**.
- **v16 "Like godless mockers at a feast"** — *lo'agei ma'og*, **mockers for a cake**, men who mock for a meal; the line is hard.
- **v19 "those who hate me without cause"** — *sone'ai chinnam*, quoted by Jesus of Himself: *They hated Me without a cause* (John 15:25; 69:4).
- **v20 "the quiet ones of the land"** — *rig'ei erets*, the peaceful, who make no trouble.
- **v23 "my God and my Lord"** — *Elohai va-Adonai*; Thomas's confession of the risen Jesus, *My Lord and my God* (John 20:28).
- **v27 "who delights in the peace of His servant"** — *shelom avdo*, the servant's well-being, his *shalom*.

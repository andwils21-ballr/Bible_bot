# Psalms 59

*For the director: "Do Not Destroy." Of David. A Miktam, when Saul sent men, and they watched the house to kill him.*

**1** Deliver me from my enemies, my God;  
set me on high, away from those who rise against me.

**2** Deliver me from those who do iniquity,  
and save me from men of bloodshed.

**3** For they lie in wait for my life;  
fierce men stir up strife against me,  
not for my transgression or my sin, LORD.

**4** For no guilt of mine they run and make ready;  
wake up to meet me, and see.

**5** You, LORD God of hosts, the God of Israel,  
rouse Yourself to punish all the nations;  
show no grace to any of the treacherous who do iniquity. *Selah*

**6** They return at evening;  
they howl like dogs  
and go around the city.

**7** They pour out words with their mouths;  
swords are in their lips,  
"for who hears?"

**8** But You, LORD, laugh at them;  
You mock all the nations.

**9** My strength, I will watch for You,  
for God is my stronghold.

**10** The God of my loyal love will come to meet me;  
God will let me look on those who watch me.

**11** Do not kill them, or my people will forget;  
make them wander by Your power and bring them down,  
Lord, our shield.

**12** For the sin of their mouth, the word of their lips,  
let them be caught in their pride,  
for the cursing and the lies they tell.

**13** Consume them in wrath;  
consume them till they are no more,  
so that they may know that God rules in Jacob  
to the ends of the earth. *Selah*

**14** They return at evening;  
they howl like dogs  
and go around the city.

**15** They wander about for food,  
and if they are not satisfied, they stay all night.

**16** But I will sing of Your strength;  
I will shout for joy at Your loyal love in the morning,  
for You have been a stronghold for me  
and a refuge in the day of my distress.

**17** My strength, I will sing praises to You,  
for God is my stronghold,  
the God of my loyal love.

## Notes

- **The title** — Michal let David down through a window when Saul's men watched his house by night (1 Samuel 19:11–17).
- **v3 "fierce men stir up strife"** — *yaguru*, the second *gur*, **to attack**, as in 56:6.
- **v5 "LORD God of hosts, the God of Israel"** — *YHWH Elohim tseva'ot*, a rare gathering of names; the enemies are called **the nations**, perhaps because the psalm was later used against foreign foes.
- **vv6, 14** — the refrain: the enemies as the half-wild dogs that roamed city streets at night, scavenging (1 Kings 14:11).
- **v9 "My strength"** — the Hebrew has *uzzo*, **his strength**; the Greek and some Hebrew manuscripts read *uzzi*, **my strength**, as in v17, followed here.
- **v10 "The God of my loyal love"** — the read form, *Elohei chasdi*, as in v17, followed here; the written form has *chasdo*, **the God of His loyal love**.
- **v11 "Do not kill them, or my people will forget"** — let the enemies live, scattered, as a lasting lesson of God's judgment.
- **vv16–17** — the morning song answers the evening howling (vv6, 14): night belongs to the dogs, the morning to God's loyal love (30:5).

# Psalms 132

*A song of ascents.*

**1** Remember, LORD, for David's sake  
all his hardships,

**2** how he swore to the LORD  
and vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob:

**3** "I will not enter my house  
or get into my bed;

**4** I will not give sleep to my eyes  
or slumber to my eyelids,

**5** until I find a place for the LORD,  
a dwelling place for the Mighty One of Jacob."

**6** We heard of it in Ephrathah;  
we found it in the fields of Jaar.

**7** "Let us go to His dwelling place;  
let us worship at His footstool."

**8** Arise, LORD, to Your resting place,  
You and the ark of Your strength.

**9** Let Your priests be clothed with righteousness,  
and let Your faithful ones sing for joy.

**10** For the sake of David Your servant,  
do not turn away the face of Your anointed.

**11** The LORD swore to David a sure oath  
from which He will not turn back:  
"One of the fruit of your body  
I will set on your throne.

**12** If your sons keep My covenant  
and My testimonies that I will teach them,  
their sons also will sit on your throne forever."

**13** For the LORD has chosen Zion;  
He has desired it for His dwelling:

**14** "This is My resting place forever;  
here I will dwell, for I have desired it.

**15** I will abundantly bless her provisions;  
I will satisfy her poor with bread.

**16** Her priests I will clothe with salvation,  
and her faithful ones will shout aloud for joy.

**17** There I will make a horn sprout for David;  
I have prepared a lamp for My anointed.

**18** His enemies I will clothe with shame,  
but on him his crown will shine."

## Notes

- **vv8–10** — nearly the words of Solomon's prayer at the temple's dedication (2 Chronicles 6:41–42), and the English there is followed where the Hebrew is the same: "Arise… to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength" and "do not turn away the face of Your anointed." Here the priests are clothed with "righteousness" (there "salvation"), and *chasidekha* keeps "Your faithful ones," as elsewhere in the Psalms (Chronicles has "loyal ones").
- **vv11–12** — the promise of 2 Samuel 7:12–16, with its condition (1 Kings 2:4); Peter cites v11 at Pentecost (Acts 2:30). The king's pronouns stay lowercase.

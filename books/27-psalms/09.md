# Psalms 9

*For the director: on Muth-labben. A psalm of David.*

**1** I will give thanks to the LORD with my whole heart;  
I will tell of all Your wonders.

**2** I will be glad and exult in You;  
I will sing praise to Your name, Most High.

**3** When my enemies turn back,  
they stumble and perish before You.

**4** For You have upheld my right and my cause;  
You sat on the throne, judging in righteousness.

**5** You have rebuked the nations; You have destroyed the wicked;  
You have blotted out their name forever and ever.

**6** The enemy has come to an end in ruins forever;  
You have uprooted their cities;  
the very memory of them has perished.

**7** But the LORD sits enthroned forever;  
He has set up His throne for judgment,

**8** and He will judge the world in righteousness;  
He will judge the peoples with uprightness.

**9** The LORD will be a stronghold for the oppressed,  
a stronghold in times of trouble.

**10** Those who know Your name put their trust in You,  
for You, LORD, have not forsaken those who seek You.

**11** Sing praises to the LORD, who dwells in Zion!  
Tell among the peoples His deeds!

**12** For He who avenges blood remembers them;  
He does not forget the cry of the afflicted.

**13** Be gracious to me, LORD!  
See my affliction from those who hate me,  
You who lift me up from the gates of death,

**14** so that I may tell of all Your praises  
in the gates of the daughter of Zion  
and rejoice in Your salvation.

**15** The nations have sunk in the pit they made;  
in the net they hid, their own foot has been caught.

**16** The LORD has made Himself known; He has carried out judgment;  
the wicked is snared in the work of his own hands. *Higgaion. Selah*

**17** The wicked will return to Sheol,  
all the nations that forget God.

**18** For the needy will not always be forgotten,  
nor the hope of the poor perish forever.

**19** Arise, LORD! Let not man prevail;  
let the nations be judged before You!

**20** Put them in fear, LORD!  
Let the nations know that they are only men! *Selah*

## Notes

- **v1 and Psalm 10** — Psalms 9 and 10 are one poem in the Hebrew, an alphabetic acrostic whose lines begin with the letters of the alphabet in turn, broken in places; Psalm 10 has no title of its own. The Greek, and the Ethiopian Psalter with it, count the two as one psalm, so from Psalm 10 to Psalm 147 their numbers run one behind the Hebrew and English. The English numbering is followed here.
- **The title "on Muth-labben"** — *al mut labben*, **on the death of the son**; perhaps the name of a tune.
- **v4 "my right and my cause"** — *mishpati ve-dini*, the words of a law court. God sits as judge for the oppressed.
- **v12 "He who avenges blood"** — *doresh damim*, **He who requires blood**, as God required Abel's blood (Genesis 4:10; 9:5).
- **vv12, 18 "the afflicted… the poor"** — the written text has *aniyyim*, **the afflicted**, where the read form has *anavim*, **the humble**, and the reverse in v18; the two words are near in meaning, and the read form is followed.
- **v15 "the pit they made… their own foot has been caught"** — the wicked caught in their own trap, as in 7:15–16.
- **v16 "Higgaion. Selah"** — *higgayon*, **murmuring**, **meditation**, perhaps a musical direction (92:3), with *Selah* (3:2).
- **v17 "The wicked will return to Sheol"** — **return**, as man returns to the dust (Genesis 3:19).
- **v20 "only men"** — *enosh*, man in his frailty, as in 8:4.

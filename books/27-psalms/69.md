# Psalms 69

*For the director: on "Lilies." Of David.*

**1** Save me, God,  
for the waters have come up to my neck.

**2** I sink in deep mire  
where there is no foothold;  
I have come into deep waters,  
and the flood sweeps over me.

**3** I am weary with my crying;  
my throat is parched;  
my eyes fail  
while I wait for my God.

**4** More than the hairs of my head  
are those who hate me without cause;  
mighty are those who would destroy me,  
my enemies for no reason.  
What I did not steal —  
must I now give it back?

**5** God, You know my folly,  
and my guilt is not hidden from You.

**6** Let those who hope in You not be put to shame because of me,  
Lord GOD of hosts;  
let those who seek You not be disgraced because of me,  
God of Israel.

**7** For it is for Your sake that I have borne reproach,  
that disgrace has covered my face.

**8** I have become a stranger to my brothers,  
a foreigner to my mother's sons.

**9** For zeal for Your house has consumed me,  
and the reproaches of those who reproach You have fallen on me.

**10** When I wept and humbled my soul with fasting,  
it became a reproach to me.

**11** When I made sackcloth my clothing,  
I became a byword to them.

**12** Those who sit in the gate talk about me,  
and the drinkers of strong drink make songs about me.

**13** But as for me, my prayer is to You, LORD,  
at a time of favor;  
God, in the abundance of Your loyal love,  
answer me with Your faithful salvation.

**14** Rescue me from the mire,  
and do not let me sink;  
let me be rescued from those who hate me  
and from the deep waters.

**15** Do not let the flood of waters sweep over me,  
or the deep swallow me up,  
or the pit close its mouth over me.

**16** Answer me, LORD, for Your loyal love is good;  
in Your great compassion turn to me.

**17** Do not hide Your face from Your servant,  
for I am in distress;  
answer me quickly.

**18** Draw near to my soul and redeem it;  
ransom me because of my enemies.

**19** You know my reproach,  
my shame and my disgrace;  
all my foes are before You.

**20** Reproach has broken my heart,  
and I am sick.  
I looked for sympathy, but there was none,  
and for comforters, but I found none.

**21** They put poison in my food,  
and for my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink.

**22** Let their table before them become a snare,  
and a trap for those at ease.

**23** Let their eyes be darkened so that they cannot see,  
and make their loins shake continually.

**24** Pour out Your indignation on them,  
and let Your burning anger overtake them.

**25** Let their camp be desolate;  
let no one live in their tents.

**26** For they persecute the one You have struck,  
and they tell of the pain of those You have wounded.

**27** Add guilt to their guilt;  
let them not come into Your righteousness.

**28** Let them be blotted out of the book of the living  
and not be written down with the righteous.

**29** But I am afflicted and in pain;  
let Your salvation, God, set me on high.

**30** I will praise the name of God with a song  
and magnify Him with thanksgiving.

**31** This will please the LORD more than an ox,  
a bull with horns and hoofs.

**32** The humble will see it and be glad;  
you who seek God, let your heart live.

**33** For the LORD listens to the needy  
and does not despise His own who are prisoners.

**34** Let heaven and earth praise Him,  
the seas and everything that moves in them.

**35** For God will save Zion  
and build the cities of Judah;  
they will live there and possess it.

**36** The offspring of His servants will inherit it,  
and those who love His name will dwell in it.

## Notes

- **v1 "to my neck"** — *ad nafesh*, **up to the throat**; *nefesh* is the throat as well as the life (Jonah 2:5).
- **v4 "those who hate me without cause"** — Jesus applies this to Himself: *They hated Me without a cause* (John 15:25; also Psalm 35:19). The speaker stays lowercase here, as in the other psalms the New Testament reads of Christ.
- **v9 "zeal for Your house has consumed me"** — the disciples remembered this when Jesus cleared the temple (John 2:17); the second line is quoted in Romans 15:3.
- **v10 "humbled my soul with fasting"** — the Hebrew is terse, "I wept in the fasting of my soul"; the Greek reads "I bowed my soul down with fasting."
- **v21 "poison… vinegar"** — *rosh* is a bitter, poisonous plant (Deuteronomy 29:18). At the cross Jesus was offered wine mixed with gall and then sour wine (Matthew 27:34, 48; John 19:28–30).
- **v22 "a trap for those at ease"** — *lishlomim* can also be read "for their peace offerings," or with the Greek "for a recompense." Romans 11:9–10 quotes vv22–23 from the Greek.
- **v25 "Let their camp be desolate"** — Peter applies this to Judas (Acts 1:20), with "his" for "their."
- **v28 "the book of the living"** — the register of those God keeps alive (Exodus 32:32–33); later the book of life (Revelation 3:5).
- **v31 "This will please the LORD more than an ox"** — praise above sacrifice, as in 40:6 and 50:13–14.

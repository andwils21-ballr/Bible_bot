# Psalms 31

*For the director. A psalm of David.*

**1** In You, LORD, I take refuge;  
let me never be put to shame;  
in Your righteousness rescue me.

**2** Incline Your ear to me;  
deliver me quickly;  
be a rock of refuge for me,  
a strong fortress to save me.

**3** For You are my rock and my fortress;  
for Your name's sake You lead me and guide me.

**4** You bring me out of the net they have hidden for me,  
for You are my refuge.

**5** Into Your hand I commit my spirit;  
You have redeemed me, LORD, God of truth.

**6** I hate those who hold to empty idols,  
but I trust in the LORD.

**7** I will rejoice and be glad in Your loyal love,  
because You have seen my affliction;  
You have known the distress of my soul,

**8** and You have not handed me over to the enemy;  
You have set my feet in a broad place.

**9** Be gracious to me, LORD, for I am in distress;  
my eye wastes away with grief,  
my soul and my body also.

**10** For my life is spent with sorrow,  
and my years with sighing;  
my strength stumbles because of my iniquity,  
and my bones waste away.

**11** Because of all my adversaries I have become a reproach,  
above all to my neighbors,  
and a dread to those who know me;  
those who see me in the street flee from me.

**12** I am forgotten like the dead, out of mind;  
I have become like a broken vessel.

**13** For I hear the whispering of many,  
terror on every side,  
as they plot together against me,  
as they scheme to take my life.

**14** But I trust in You, LORD;  
I say, "You are my God."

**15** My times are in Your hand;  
rescue me from the hand of my enemies and from those who pursue me.

**16** Make Your face shine on Your servant;  
save me in Your loyal love.

**17** LORD, let me not be put to shame, for I call on You;  
let the wicked be put to shame;  
let them be silent in Sheol.

**18** Let the lying lips be mute,  
which speak arrogantly against the righteous  
in pride and contempt.

**19** How great is Your goodness,  
which You have stored up for those who fear You  
and worked for those who take refuge in You  
in the sight of the children of man!

**20** In the shelter of Your presence You hide them  
from the plots of men;  
You keep them safe in Your shelter  
from the strife of tongues.

**21** Blessed be the LORD,  
for He has shown me the wonder of His loyal love  
in a city under siege.

**22** As for me, I said in my alarm,  
"I am cut off from Your sight."  
But You heard the sound of my pleas for mercy  
when I cried to You for help.

**23** Love the LORD, all you His faithful ones!  
The LORD keeps the faithful  
and repays in full the one who acts in pride.

**24** Be strong, and let your heart take courage,  
all you who wait for the LORD!

---

## Notes

- **v5 "Into Your hand I commit my spirit"** — *ruchi*, the psalmist's own spirit, his life, placed in God's keeping. Jesus prayed these words on the cross, adding *Father* (Luke 23:46); Stephen prayed them to Jesus (Acts 7:59).
- **v22 "I am cut off from Your sight"** — *nigrazti*, as Jonah said, *I am driven away from Your sight* (Jonah 2:4).

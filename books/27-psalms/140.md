# Psalms 140

*For the director. A psalm of David.*

**1** Rescue me, LORD, from evil people;  
preserve me from violent men,

**2** who devise evil things in their heart  
and stir up wars every day.

**3** They sharpen their tongue like a serpent's;  
the venom of vipers is under their lips. *Selah*

**4** Guard me, LORD, from the hands of the wicked;  
preserve me from violent men,  
who have planned to trip up my feet.

**5** The proud have hidden a trap for me,  
and with cords they have spread a net beside the path;  
they have set snares for me. *Selah*

**6** I say to the LORD, "You are my God;  
give ear, LORD, to the voice of my pleas.

**7** GOD the Lord, the strength of my salvation,  
You have covered my head on the day of battle.

**8** Do not grant, LORD, the desires of the wicked;  
do not further their plot, or they will be exalted." *Selah*

**9** As for the head of those who surround me,  
let the trouble of their lips cover them.

**10** Let burning coals fall on them;  
let them be cast into the fire,  
into deep pits, never to rise.

**11** Let not a slanderer be established in the land;  
let evil hunt down the violent man with blow upon blow.

**12** I know that the LORD will maintain the cause of the afflicted  
and justice for the needy.

**13** Surely the righteous will give thanks to Your name;  
the upright will dwell in Your presence.

---

## Notes

- **v3 "the venom of vipers is under their lips"** — Paul quotes it in his chain of verses on human sin (Romans 3:13).
- **v7 "GOD the Lord"** — *YHWH Adonai*, the order of 68:20 and 109:21.
- **v10 "Let burning coals fall on them"** — the read form; the written form reads "let them bring down coals on them."
- **v12 "I know"** — the read form; the written form has "You know."
- **v13 "the upright will dwell in Your presence"** — the hope of 11:7 and 16:11.

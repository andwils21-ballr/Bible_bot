# Psalms 134

*A song of ascents.*

**1** Come, bless the LORD,  
all you servants of the LORD,  
who stand by night in the house of the LORD.

**2** Lift up your hands to the holy place  
and bless the LORD.

**3** May the LORD bless you from Zion,  
the Maker of heaven and earth.

---

## Notes

- **vv1–2 and 3** — the people bless the LORD, and the LORD blesses them in return; the last of the Songs of Ascents ends with a blessing, as the pilgrims leave.

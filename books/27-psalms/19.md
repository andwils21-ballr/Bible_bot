# Psalms 19

*For the director. A psalm of David.*

## The Heavens

**1** The heavens declare the glory of God,  
and the sky proclaims the work of His hands.

**2** Day to day pours out speech,  
and night to night makes knowledge known.

**3** There is no speech, and there are no words;  
their voice is not heard.

**4** Yet their line has gone out through all the earth,  
and their words to the end of the world.  
In them He has set a tent for the sun,

**5** which comes out like a bridegroom from his chamber,  
and rejoices like a strong man to run his course.

**6** Its going out is from the end of the heavens,  
and its circuit to their other end,  
and nothing is hidden from its heat.

## The Law of the LORD

**7** The law of the LORD is perfect,  
bringing back the soul;  
the testimony of the LORD is sure,  
making wise the simple;

**8** the precepts of the LORD are right,  
making the heart glad;  
the commandment of the LORD is pure,  
giving light to the eyes;

**9** the fear of the LORD is clean,  
standing forever;  
the judgments of the LORD are true,  
and righteous altogether.

**10** They are more to be desired than gold,  
than much fine gold,  
sweeter also than honey  
and the drippings of the honeycomb.

**11** By them also Your servant is warned;  
in keeping them there is great reward.

**12** Who can discern his errors?  
Clear me of hidden faults.

**13** Keep back Your servant also from willful sins;  
let them not rule over me.  
Then I will be blameless  
and innocent of great transgression.

**14** Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart  
be acceptable before You,  
LORD, my rock and my redeemer.

---

## Notes

- **The psalm** — two halves: the voice of creation (vv1–6), where God is *El*, and the voice of the law (vv7–14), where He is *YHWH*, named seven times. The sun, which rules the sky, gives way to the law, which rules the soul.
- **v1 "the sky"** — *raqia*, the **expanse** of Genesis 1:6–8, the dome of the heavens.

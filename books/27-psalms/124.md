# Psalms 124

*A song of ascents. Of David.*

**1** If it had not been the LORD who was on our side —  
let Israel now say —

**2** if it had not been the LORD who was on our side  
when people rose up against us,

**3** then they would have swallowed us alive,  
when their anger blazed against us;

**4** then the waters would have swept us away;  
the torrent would have gone over our soul;

**5** then the raging waters  
would have gone over our soul.

**6** Blessed be the LORD,  
who has not given us as prey to their teeth.

**7** Our soul has escaped like a bird  
from the snare of the fowlers;  
the snare is broken,  
and we have escaped.

**8** Our help is in the name of the LORD,  
the Maker of heaven and earth.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "let Israel now say"** — a leader's call and the people's answer, as in 118:2 and 129:1.
- **v3 "they would have swallowed us alive"** — like Sheol swallowing Korah's company (Numbers 16:30–33; Proverbs 1:12).
- **vv4–5 "the waters… the torrent… the raging waters"** — floods as the picture of overwhelming enemies (18:16; 69:1–2).
- **v7 "like a bird from the snare of the fowlers"** — the snare does not open; it breaks.
- **v8 "the Maker of heaven and earth"** — as in 121:2 and 134:3.

# Psalms 118

**1** Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**2** Let Israel say,  
"His loyal love endures forever."

**3** Let the house of Aaron say,  
"His loyal love endures forever."

**4** Let those who fear the LORD say,  
"His loyal love endures forever."

**5** Out of my distress I called on Yah;  
Yah answered me and set me in a broad place.

**6** The LORD is on my side; I will not fear.  
What can man do to me?

**7** The LORD is on my side as my helper;  
I will look in triumph on those who hate me.

**8** It is better to take refuge in the LORD  
than to trust in man.

**9** It is better to take refuge in the LORD  
than to trust in nobles.

**10** All nations surrounded me;  
in the name of the LORD I cut them off.

**11** They surrounded me, yes, they surrounded me;  
in the name of the LORD I cut them off.

**12** They surrounded me like bees;  
they were put out like a fire of thorns;  
in the name of the LORD I cut them off.

**13** You pushed me hard so that I was falling,  
but the LORD helped me.

**14** My strength and my song is Yah,  
and He has become my deliverance.

**15** The sound of joyful shouting and deliverance  
is in the tents of the righteous:  
"The right hand of the LORD does valiantly!

**16** The right hand of the LORD is exalted;  
the right hand of the LORD does valiantly!"

**17** I will not die, but live,  
and tell of the works of Yah.

**18** Yah has disciplined me severely,  
but He has not given me over to death.

**19** Open to me the gates of righteousness;  
I will enter through them and give thanks to Yah.

**20** This is the gate of the LORD;  
the righteous will enter through it.

**21** I will give thanks to You, for You have answered me  
and have become my deliverance.

**22** The stone the builders rejected  
has become the cornerstone.

**23** This is the LORD's doing;  
it is marvelous in our eyes.

**24** This is the day the LORD has made;  
let us rejoice and be glad in it.

**25** Please, LORD, save us!  
Please, LORD, give us success!

**26** Blessed is he who comes in the name of the LORD!  
We bless you from the house of the LORD.

**27** The LORD is God, and He has given us light.  
Bind the festal sacrifice with cords,  
up to the horns of the altar.

**28** You are my God, and I will give thanks to You;  
my God, I will exalt You.

**29** Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

---

## Notes

- **v22 "The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone"** — Jesus applies it to Himself (Matthew 21:42), and so do Peter (Acts 4:11; 1 Peter 2:7) and Paul (Ephesians 2:20).
- **v25 "Please, LORD, save us!"** — *hoshi'ah na*, "hosanna," the cry of the crowds on Palm Sunday (Matthew 21:9).

# Psalms 93

**1** The LORD reigns; He is clothed with majesty;  
the LORD is clothed; He has put on strength like a belt.  
The world is firmly established; it cannot be moved.

**2** Your throne is established from of old;  
You are from everlasting.

**3** The floods have lifted up, LORD,  
the floods have lifted up their voice;  
the floods lift up their roaring.

**4** Mightier than the sounds of many waters,  
mightier than the breakers of the sea,  
the LORD on high is mighty.

**5** Your testimonies are very sure;  
holiness befits Your house, LORD,  
for length of days.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "The LORD reigns"** — *YHWH malakh*, the cry that opens the psalms of the LORD's kingship (93; 96–99). The Greek adds a title, "For the day before the Sabbath, when the earth was inhabited."
- **v1 "He has put on strength like a belt"** — *hitazzar*, to gird oneself, as a king or warrior does.
- **vv3–4 "The floods have lifted up"** — the sea and rivers as the forces of chaos, rising line by line; the LORD on high is greater still (29:3; 89:9).
- **v5 "Your testimonies are very sure"** — the God who rules the waters has also given His word, and it stands as firm as His throne.

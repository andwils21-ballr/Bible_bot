# Psalms 50

*A psalm of Asaph.*

**1** The Mighty One, God, the LORD, has spoken  
and summoned the earth  
from the rising of the sun to its setting.

**2** Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty,  
God shines out.

**3** Our God comes and does not keep silent;  
before Him is a devouring fire,  
and around Him a mighty storm.

**4** He calls to the heavens above  
and to the earth, to judge His people:

**5** "Gather to Me My faithful ones,  
who made a covenant with Me by sacrifice."

**6** The heavens declare His righteousness,  
for God Himself is judge. *Selah*

**7** "Hear, My people, and I will speak;  
Israel, I will testify against you.  
I am God, your God.

**8** Not for your sacrifices do I rebuke you;  
your burnt offerings are always before Me.

**9** I will not take a bull from your house  
or goats from your folds.

**10** For every beast of the forest is Mine,  
the cattle on a thousand hills.

**11** I know every bird of the mountains,  
and all that moves in the field is Mine.

**12** If I were hungry, I would not tell you,  
for the world and all that fills it are Mine.

**13** Do I eat the flesh of bulls  
or drink the blood of goats?

**14** Offer to God a sacrifice of thanksgiving,  
and pay your vows to the Most High,

**15** and call on Me in the day of trouble;  
I will rescue you, and you will glorify Me."

**16** But to the wicked God says:  
"What right have you to recite My statutes  
or take My covenant on your lips?

**17** For you hate discipline  
and throw My words behind you.

**18** When you see a thief, you are pleased with him,  
and your portion is with adulterers.

**19** You let your mouth loose for evil,  
and your tongue weaves deceit.

**20** You sit and speak against your brother;  
you slander your own mother's son.

**21** These things you have done, and I kept silent;  
you thought I was altogether like you.  
I will rebuke you and set it out before your eyes.

**22** Understand this, you who forget God,  
or I will tear you apart, and there will be no one to rescue.

**23** The one who offers thanksgiving as his sacrifice glorifies Me;  
to the one who sets his way aright  
I will show the salvation of God."

## Notes

- **The title "A psalm of Asaph"** — Asaph, David's chief musician (1 Chronicles 16:5, 7), named over Psalms 50 and 73–83.
- **v1 "The Mighty One, God, the LORD"** — *El Elohim YHWH*, three names together, as in Joshua 22:22: the full title of the Judge who summons His court.
- **v3 "before Him is a devouring fire"** — God coming as at Sinai, in fire and storm (Exodus 19:16–18; Deuteronomy 4:24).
- **v5 "who made a covenant with Me by sacrifice"** — the covenant sealed with blood at Sinai (Exodus 24:5–8).
- **vv8–13** — God does not need sacrifices as food; *the earth is the LORD's, and all that fills it* (24:1). The fault is not the sacrifices but the hearts of those who offer them (51:16–17; 1 Samuel 15:22; Hosea 6:6).
- **v14 "a sacrifice of thanksgiving"** — *todah*, the thank offering (Leviticus 7:12); *the sacrifice of praise, the fruit of lips* (Hebrews 13:15).
- **vv16–21** — the second charge, against those who recite the law and break it: theft, adultery, slander (Romans 2:21–23).
- **v21 "you thought I was altogether like you"** — *heyot ehyeh khamokha*, with *ehyeh*, the verb of God's name, *I AM WHO I AM* (Exodus 3:14): because God was silent, they thought Him no better than themselves.
- **v22 "or I will tear you apart"** — *pen etrof*, God as a lion (Hosea 5:14).

# Psalms 148

**1** Praise Yah!  
Praise the LORD from the heavens;  
praise Him in the heights!

**2** Praise Him, all His messengers;  
praise Him, all His hosts!

**3** Praise Him, sun and moon;  
praise Him, all you shining stars!

**4** Praise Him, highest heavens,  
and you waters above the heavens!

**5** Let them praise the name of the LORD,  
for He commanded, and they were created.

**6** He established them forever and ever;  
He gave a decree that will not pass away.

**7** Praise the LORD from the earth,  
you sea monsters and all deeps,

**8** fire and hail, snow and mist,  
stormy wind fulfilling His word,

**9** mountains and all hills,  
fruit trees and all cedars,

**10** beasts and all cattle,  
creeping things and winged birds,

**11** kings of the earth and all peoples,  
princes and all judges of the earth,

**12** young men and young women,  
old men and children together.

**13** Let them praise the name of the LORD,  
for His name alone is exalted;  
His splendor is above earth and heaven.

**14** He has raised up a horn for His people,  
praise for all His faithful ones,  
for the children of Israel, the people near to Him.  
Praise Yah!

## Notes

- **vv1–6 and 7–12** — the heavens first, then the earth, in the order of Genesis 1: the waters above (Genesis 1:7), sun, moon and stars (1:16), sea monsters (1:21), beasts, cattle and creeping things (1:24), and people.
- **v2 "all His messengers"** — *mal'akhav*, God's messengers in heaven, as in 103:20.
- **v4 "you waters above the heavens"** — the waters above the expanse (Genesis 1:7).
- **v6 "a decree that will not pass away"** — or "a decree they will not transgress"; the order of creation holds (Jeremiah 33:25).
- **v14 "He has raised up a horn for His people"** — strength and victory (89:17; 132:17); "the people near to Him," Israel, brought near (Deuteronomy 4:7).

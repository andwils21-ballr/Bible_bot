# Psalms 116

**1** I love the LORD,  
because He hears my voice and my pleas.

**2** Because He has inclined His ear to me,  
I will call on Him as long as I live.

**3** The cords of death surrounded me;  
the distresses of Sheol found me;  
I found trouble and sorrow.

**4** Then I called on the name of the LORD:  
"Please, LORD, deliver my soul!"

**5** Gracious is the LORD, and righteous;  
our God is merciful.

**6** The LORD guards the simple;  
I was brought low, and He saved me.

**7** Return to your rest, my soul,  
for the LORD has dealt bountifully with you.

**8** For You have delivered my soul from death,  
my eyes from tears,  
my feet from stumbling.

**9** I will walk before the LORD  
in the land of the living.

**10** I believed, even when I said,  
"I am greatly afflicted";

**11** I said in my alarm,  
"All mankind are liars."

**12** What shall I return to the LORD  
for all His benefits to me?

**13** I will lift up the cup of salvation  
and call on the name of the LORD.

**14** I will pay my vows to the LORD  
in the presence of all His people.

**15** Precious in the sight of the LORD  
is the death of His faithful ones.

**16** Please, LORD, truly I am Your servant;  
I am Your servant, the son of Your maidservant;  
You have loosed my bonds.

**17** I will offer You a sacrifice of thanksgiving  
and call on the name of the LORD.

**18** I will pay my vows to the LORD  
in the presence of all His people,

**19** in the courts of the house of the LORD,  
within you, Jerusalem.  
Praise Yah!

## Notes

- **v3 "The cords of death surrounded me"** — the same words as 18:4, and the English there is followed.
- **v9 "in the land of the living"** — the Hebrew has the plural, "the lands of the living."
- **v10 "I believed, even when I said"** — the Hebrew is terse, "I believed, for I speak"; the Greek reads "I believed, therefore I spoke," and Paul quotes it so (2 Corinthians 4:13).
- **v13 "the cup of salvation"** — a drink offering poured out in thanks (Numbers 28:7), or the cup at a thanksgiving meal; at the Passover the third cup was called the cup of blessing (1 Corinthians 10:16).
- **v15 "Precious in the sight of the LORD is the death of His faithful ones"** — precious, that is, costly: God does not let it happen lightly (72:14).
- **v16 "the son of Your maidservant"** — as in 86:16, one born into God's household.
- **vv14, 18** — the vow repeated, as the Hebrew does.

# Psalms 63

*A psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of Judah.*

**1** God, You are my God; I seek You early;  
my soul thirsts for You;  
my flesh longs for You,  
in a dry and weary land without water.

**2** So I have looked on You in the sanctuary,  
seeing Your power and Your glory.

**3** Because Your loyal love is better than life,  
my lips will praise You.

**4** So I will bless You as long as I live;  
in Your name I will lift up my hands.

**5** My soul will be satisfied as with fat and rich food,  
and my mouth will praise You with joyful lips,

**6** when I remember You on my bed  
and meditate on You in the watches of the night;

**7** for You have been my help,  
and in the shadow of Your wings I sing for joy.

**8** My soul clings to You;  
Your right hand holds me up.

**9** But those who seek my life to destroy it  
will go down into the depths of the earth;

**10** they will be handed over to the power of the sword;  
they will be a portion for jackals.

**11** But the king will rejoice in God;  
all who swear by Him will boast,  
for the mouths of those who speak lies will be stopped.

## Notes

- **The title "in the wilderness of Judah"** — David fleeing from Saul (1 Samuel 23:14) or from Absalom (2 Samuel 15:23–28).
- **v1 "I seek You early"** — *ashachareka*, from *shachar*, **dawn**: to seek at daybreak, and so earnestly. The Church has long sung the psalm at the morning prayer.
- **v1 "a dry and weary land without water"** — the desert outside, and the thirst of the soul within (42:1–2; 143:6).
- **v2 "So I have looked on You in the sanctuary"** — the memory of worship in the house of God sustains him in the desert.
- **v3 "Your loyal love is better than life"** — *chesed* above life itself.
- **v6 "the watches of the night"** — the night was divided into three watches (Judges 7:19; Lamentations 2:19).
- **v7 "the shadow of Your wings"** — as in 17:8, 36:7, 57:1 and 61:4.
- **v8 "My soul clings to You"** — *daveqah*, the word for a man holding fast to his wife (Genesis 2:24) and Israel to God (Deuteronomy 10:20).
- **v10 "a portion for jackals"** — *shu'alim*, jackals or foxes, the scavengers of the battlefield.
- **v11 "all who swear by Him"** — by God, or by the king's life (1 Samuel 17:55); the psalm ends with the king, as Psalm 61 does.

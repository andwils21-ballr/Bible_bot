# Psalms 146

**1** Praise Yah!  
Praise the LORD, my soul!

**2** I will praise the LORD as long as I live;  
I will sing praises to my God while I have being.

**3** Do not trust in nobles,  
in a son of man, in whom there is no salvation.

**4** His breath departs; he returns to his ground;  
on that very day his plans perish.

**5** Blessed is the one whose help is the God of Jacob,  
whose hope is in the LORD his God,

**6** the Maker of heaven and earth,  
the sea and everything in them,  
who keeps truth forever;

**7** who executes justice for the oppressed,  
who gives food to the hungry.  
The LORD sets the prisoners free;

**8** the LORD opens the eyes of the blind;  
the LORD raises up those who are bowed down;  
the LORD loves the righteous;

**9** the LORD guards the guests;  
He upholds the fatherless and the widow,  
but the way of the wicked He turns upside down.

**10** The LORD will reign forever,  
your God, Zion, for all generations.  
Praise Yah!

---

## Notes

- **vv1–2** — the first of the five "hallelujah" psalms that close the book (146–150); v2b is the same as 104:33b, and the English there is followed.
- **v4 "he returns to his ground"** — *adam* to his *adamah*, the man to the ground he was taken from (Genesis 3:19).

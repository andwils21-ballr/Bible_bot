# Psalms 60

*For the director: on "The Lily of Testimony." A Miktam of David, to teach; when he fought with Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah, and Joab returned and struck down twelve thousand of Edom in the Valley of Salt.*

**1** God, You have rejected us; You have broken us;  
You have been angry; restore us.

**2** You have made the land quake; You have split it open;  
heal its breaches, for it totters.

**3** You have made Your people see hard things;  
You have given us wine to drink that makes us reel.

**4** You have given a banner to those who fear You,  
to rally to it because of the bow. *Selah*

**5** So that Your beloved ones may be rescued,  
save with Your right hand, and answer me.

**6** God has spoken in His holiness:  
"I will exult; I will divide Shechem  
and measure out the Valley of Succoth.

**7** Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine;  
Ephraim is the helmet of My head;  
Judah is My scepter.

**8** Moab is My washbasin;  
on Edom I throw My sandal;  
Philistia, shout in triumph over Me!"

**9** Who will bring me to the fortified city?  
Who will lead me to Edom?

**10** Have You not rejected us, God?  
You do not go out, God, with our armies.

**11** Give us help against the foe,  
for the salvation of man is worthless.

**12** With God we will do valiantly,  
and He will trample our foes.

## Notes

- **The title** — David's wars against the Arameans and Edom (2 Samuel 8:3–14; 1 Chronicles 18:3–13). The Hebrew title takes two verses. **To teach** may mean it was to be learned by heart (Deuteronomy 31:19; 2 Samuel 1:18).
- **vv1–3** — a lament after a defeat, perhaps an Edomite raid while the army was away in the north.
- **v3 "wine… that makes us reel"** — *yayin tar'elah*, the cup of God's wrath (75:8; Isaiah 51:17).
- **v4 "because of the bow"** — *mipnei qoshet*, which may mean **to flee from the bow**, or, from another root, **for the sake of truth**; the Greek has *from before the bow*.
- **v5 "answer me"** — the read form; the written form has **answer us**.
- **vv6–8** — God's oracle, dividing the land as its owner: **Shechem** west of the Jordan and **Succoth** east of it; **Gilead** and **Manasseh**; **Ephraim** and **Judah** as His helmet and scepter. **Moab** is a mere washbasin, **Edom** the slave to whom He throws His sandal (Ruth 4:7).
- **v8 "Philistia, shout in triumph over Me!"** — a challenge; Psalm 108:9 has *over Philistia I shout in triumph*.
- **vv5–12** — repeated as the second half of Psalm 108 (108:6–13).
- **v11 "the salvation of man is worthless"** — *shav teshu'at adam* (146:3).

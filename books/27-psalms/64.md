# Psalms 64

*For the director. A psalm of David.*

**1** Hear my voice, God, in my complaint;  
keep my life from the dread of the enemy.

**2** Hide me from the secret plotting of evildoers,  
from the throng of those who do iniquity,

**3** who sharpen their tongue like a sword  
and aim bitter words like arrows,

**4** to shoot in hiding at the blameless,  
shooting at him suddenly, without fear.

**5** They hold fast to their evil plan;  
they talk of hiding snares;  
they say, "Who will see them?"

**6** They search out injustices:  
"We have finished a well-searched plan!"  
For the inner man and the heart are deep.

**7** But God shoots His arrow at them;  
suddenly they are wounded.

**8** They are made to stumble; their own tongue is against them;  
all who see them shake their heads.

**9** Then all mankind fears;  
they tell what God has done  
and ponder His work.

**10** Let the righteous rejoice in the LORD  
and take refuge in Him;  
let all the upright in heart give praise.

---

## Notes

- **vv3–4 "who sharpen their tongue like a sword… arrows"** — slander as a weapon shot from ambush (57:4; Jeremiah 9:8).
- **v5 "Who will see them?"** — the plotters think no one, not even God, sees (10:11; 59:7).
- **v6 "the inner man and the heart are deep"** — the plotters' minds are too deep for men to know; but not for God (Jeremiah 17:9–10).
- **vv7–8** — the reversal: God shoots His arrow at those who shot their arrows (v3), and their tongues, their weapons, bring them down (7:15–16).
- **v9 "all mankind fears"** — when God's judgment is seen, all people learn the lesson (40:3; 52:6).

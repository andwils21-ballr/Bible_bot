# Psalms 53

*For the director: on Mahalath. A Maskil of David.*

**1** The fool says in his heart, "There is no God."  
They are corrupt; they do detestable injustice;  
there is no one who does good.

**2** God looks down from heaven on the children of man,  
to see if there is anyone who understands,  
who seeks God.

**3** They have all fallen away; together they have become corrupt;  
there is no one who does good,  
not even one.

**4** Do they have no knowledge, those who do iniquity,  
who eat up my people as they eat bread,  
and do not call on God?

**5** There they are in great terror,  
where there was no terror,  
for God has scattered the bones of the one who camps against you;  
you have put them to shame, for God has rejected them.

**6** Oh that the salvation of Israel would come out of Zion!  
When God restores the fortunes of His people,  
let Jacob rejoice, let Israel be glad.

## Notes

- **The psalm** — Psalm 14 again, as the second book of the Psalms has it: *God* (*Elohim*) for *the LORD* (*YHWH*), and other changes. The English is that of Psalm 14 where the Hebrew is the same, and differs where it differs.

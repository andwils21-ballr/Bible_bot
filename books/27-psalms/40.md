# Psalms 40

*For the director. Of David. A psalm.*

**1** I waited patiently for the LORD;  
He inclined to me and heard my cry.

**2** He drew me up from the pit of ruin,  
out of the miry clay,  
and set my feet on a rock,  
making my steps secure.

**3** He put a new song in my mouth,  
a song of praise to our God.  
Many will see and fear,  
and will trust in the LORD.

**4** Blessed is the man who makes the LORD his trust,  
who does not turn to the proud,  
to those who go astray after falsehood.

**5** You have done many things, LORD my God,  
Your wonders and Your thoughts toward us;  
none can compare with You.  
If I would declare and speak of them,  
they are more than can be counted.

**6** Sacrifice and offering You did not desire;  
ears You have dug for me;  
burnt offering and sin offering You did not ask for.

**7** Then I said, "Here I have come;  
in the scroll of the book it is written of me.

**8** I delight to do Your will, my God;  
Your law is within my inmost being."

**9** I have told the good news of righteousness in the great congregation;  
I have not held back my lips,  
as You know, LORD.

**10** I have not hidden Your righteousness within my heart;  
I have spoken of Your faithfulness and Your salvation;  
I have not concealed Your loyal love and Your truth  
from the great congregation.

**11** As for You, LORD, do not hold back Your compassion from me;  
let Your loyal love and Your truth always keep me.

**12** For evils without number have surrounded me;  
my iniquities have overtaken me, and I cannot see;  
they are more than the hairs of my head,  
and my heart has failed me.

**13** Be pleased, LORD, to rescue me;  
LORD, hurry to help me.

**14** Let them be put to shame and confounded together  
who seek my life to sweep it away;  
let them be turned back and disgraced  
who delight in my harm.

**15** Let them be appalled because of their shame  
who say to me, "Aha, aha!"

**16** But let all who seek You  
rejoice and be glad in You;  
let those who love Your salvation  
say always, "Great is the LORD!"

**17** As for me, I am poor and needy,  
but the Lord thinks of me.  
You are my help and my deliverer;  
my God, do not delay.

## Notes

- **v2 "the pit of ruin… the miry clay"** — *bor sha'on*, **the pit of roaring**, of tumult, and *tit ha-yaven*, the mud of a cistern, like the one Jeremiah sank into (Jeremiah 38:6).
- **v3 "a new song"** — as in 33:3.
- **v6 "ears You have dug for me"** — *oznayim karita li*: God has opened the psalmist's ears to hear and obey, which He wants more than sacrifice (1 Samuel 15:22; Isaiah 50:5). The Greek reads *a body You have prepared for me*, and Hebrews 10:5–7 quotes the Greek of vv6–8 as the words of Christ coming into the world.
- **v7 "Here I have come; in the scroll of the book it is written of me"** — the obedient servant presents himself; the **scroll** may be the law that the king was to copy and keep (Deuteronomy 17:18–19).
- **v8 "Your law is within my inmost being"** — *be-tokh me'ai*, **in my inward parts**: the law written on the heart (37:31; Jeremiah 31:33).
- **v9 "I have told the good news"** — *bissarti*, the verb of *good tidings* (Isaiah 40:9; 52:7), the root of the Greek *gospel* in later use.
- **v12 "evils without number"** — *ra'ot*, troubles, and his own iniquities with them: the thanksgiving of vv1–10 turns to a new plea.
- **vv13–17** — repeated, with small differences, as Psalm 70.
- **v17 "the Lord thinks of me"** — *Adonai yachashov li*; Psalm 70:5 has *God, hurry to me*.

# Psalms 106

**1** Praise Yah!  
Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**2** Who can speak of the mighty deeds of the LORD,  
or make all His praise heard?

**3** Blessed are those who keep justice,  
who do righteousness at all times.

**4** Remember me, LORD, when You show favor to Your people;  
visit me with Your salvation,

**5** so that I may see the prosperity of Your chosen ones,  
rejoice in the gladness of Your nation,  
and glory with Your inheritance.

**6** We have sinned, like our fathers;  
we have done wrong; we have acted wickedly.

**7** Our fathers in Egypt did not consider Your wonders;  
they did not remember the abundance of Your loyal love,  
but rebelled by the sea, at the Sea of Reeds.

**8** Yet He saved them for His name's sake,  
to make His might known.

**9** He rebuked the Sea of Reeds, and it dried up;  
He led them through the depths as through a wilderness.

**10** He saved them from the hand of the one who hated them  
and redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.

**11** The waters covered their foes;  
not one of them was left.

**12** Then they believed His words;  
they sang His praise.

**13** But they soon forgot His works;  
they did not wait for His counsel.

**14** They gave in to craving in the wilderness  
and tested God in the desert.

**15** He gave them what they asked  
but sent leanness into their souls.

**16** They envied Moses in the camp,  
and Aaron, the holy one of the LORD.

**17** The earth opened and swallowed Dathan  
and covered the company of Abiram.

**18** Fire blazed among their company;  
flame burned up the wicked.

**19** They made a calf at Horeb  
and bowed down to a cast image.

**20** They exchanged their Glory  
for the image of an ox that eats grass.

**21** They forgot God their Savior,  
who had done great things in Egypt,

**22** wonders in the land of Ham,  
awesome deeds by the Sea of Reeds.

**23** So He said He would destroy them —  
had not Moses His chosen one  
stood in the breach before Him  
to turn away His wrath from destroying them.

**24** Then they despised the pleasant land;  
they did not believe His word.

**25** They grumbled in their tents  
and did not listen to the voice of the LORD.

**26** So He raised His hand against them  
to make them fall in the wilderness,

**27** to make their offspring fall among the nations  
and to scatter them in the lands.

**28** They joined themselves to the Baal of Peor  
and ate sacrifices offered to the dead.

**29** They provoked Him with their deeds,  
and a plague broke out among them.

**30** Then Phinehas stood up and intervened,  
and the plague was stopped.

**31** It was counted to him as righteousness  
from generation to generation forever.

**32** They angered Him at the waters of Meribah,  
and it went badly with Moses because of them,

**33** for they rebelled against His Spirit,  
and he spoke rashly with his lips.

**34** They did not destroy the peoples,  
as the LORD had commanded them,

**35** but mingled with the nations  
and learned their ways.

**36** They served their idols,  
which became a snare to them.

**37** They sacrificed their sons  
and their daughters to demons.

**38** They poured out innocent blood,  
the blood of their sons and daughters,  
whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan,  
and the land was defiled with blood.

**39** They became unclean by their deeds  
and were unfaithful in their practices.

**40** So the anger of the LORD burned against His people,  
and He abhorred His inheritance.

**41** He gave them into the hand of the nations,  
and those who hated them ruled over them.

**42** Their enemies oppressed them,  
and they were brought low under their power.

**43** Many times He delivered them,  
but they rebelled in their schemes  
and sank low in their iniquity.

**44** Yet He looked on their distress  
when He heard their cry.

**45** He remembered His covenant for their sake  
and relented according to the abundance of His loyal love.

**46** He caused them to be pitied  
by all who held them captive.

**47** Save us, LORD our God,  
and gather us from the nations,  
to give thanks to Your holy name  
and to glory in Your praise.

**48** Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel,  
from everlasting to everlasting!  
Let all the people say, "Amen."  
Praise Yah!

## Notes

- **vv1, 47–48** — the close of the song David appointed for the ark (1 Chronicles 16:34–36), and the English there is followed. Where the Hebrew differs, so does the English: v47 "LORD our God" for "God of our salvation," without "and deliver us"; v48 "Let all the people say" for "Then all the people said."
- **v6 "We have sinned, like our fathers"** — the confession of the exiles (1 Kings 8:47; Daniel 9:5); Psalm 105 told God's faithfulness, and this psalm tells Israel's unfaithfulness.
- **v15 "but sent leanness into their souls"** — a wasting disease; at Kibroth-hattaavah a plague struck them while the meat was in their mouths (Numbers 11:33–34). The Greek has "fullness."
- **vv16–18** — the rebellion of Korah, Dathan and Abiram (Numbers 16).
- **v20 "They exchanged their Glory for the image of an ox"** — their Glory is God Himself (Jeremiah 2:11); Paul echoes the line in Romans 1:23.
- **v23 "stood in the breach"** — like a soldier in a broken wall, Moses stood between the people and God's wrath (Exodus 32:10–14; Ezekiel 22:30).
- **vv28–31** — Israel at Baal of Peor and Phinehas's zeal (Numbers 25); "counted to him as righteousness" is the phrase of Genesis 15:6.
- **v33 "they rebelled against His Spirit, and he spoke rashly"** — the Hebrew says only "his spirit"; most take the spirit to be God's (Isaiah 63:10). The one who spoke rashly is Moses (Numbers 20:10–12). The Greek reads "they embittered his spirit," of Moses.
- **v37 "to demons"** — *shedim*, as in Deuteronomy 32:17.
- **v48** — the blessing that closes the fourth book of the Psalms.

# Psalms 45

*For the director: on "Lilies." Of the sons of Korah. A Maskil. A love song.*

**1** My heart overflows with a good word;  
I speak my verses to the king;  
my tongue is the pen of a skillful scribe.

**2** You are the most handsome of the sons of men;  
grace is poured on your lips;  
therefore God has blessed you forever.

**3** Gird your sword on your thigh, mighty one,  
your splendor and your majesty.

**4** In your majesty ride on victoriously  
for the cause of truth and humility and righteousness;  
let your right hand teach you fearsome deeds.

**5** Your arrows are sharp;  
peoples fall under you;  
they fall in the heart of the king's enemies.

**6** Your throne, O God, is forever and ever;  
the scepter of your kingdom is a scepter of uprightness.

**7** You have loved righteousness and hated wickedness;  
therefore God, your God, has anointed you  
with the oil of gladness above your companions.

**8** All your robes are myrrh and aloes and cassia;  
from palaces of ivory, stringed instruments make you glad.

**9** Daughters of kings are among your honored women;  
at your right hand stands the queen in gold of Ophir.

**10** Listen, daughter, and see, and incline your ear:  
forget your people and your father's house,

**11** and the king will desire your beauty.  
Since he is your lord, bow down to him.

**12** The daughter of Tyre will come with a gift;  
the richest of the people will seek your favor.

**13** All glorious is the king's daughter within;  
her clothing is woven with gold.

**14** In embroidered robes she is led to the king,  
with the young women, her companions, following her;  
they are brought to you.

**15** With joy and gladness they are led along;  
they enter the palace of the king.

**16** In place of your fathers will be your sons;  
you will make them princes in all the earth.

**17** I will make your name remembered in all generations;  
therefore peoples will praise you forever and ever.

## Notes

- **The title "on 'Lilies'… A love song"** — *al shoshannim*, the name of a tune (69, 80); *shir yedidot*, **a song of loves**, for a royal wedding.
- **v1 "my tongue is the pen of a skillful scribe"** — *et sofer mahir*, as Ezra was *a skilled scribe* (Ezra 7:6).
- **v2 "grace is poured on your lips"** — the king's gracious speech (Proverbs 22:11; Luke 4:22).
- **v6 "Your throne, O God, is forever and ever"** — the king addressed as *Elohim*, **God**, as rulers are called *gods* in 82:6; the line may also be read *Your throne is God's, forever and ever*, or *your divine throne*. Hebrews 1:8–9 quotes vv6–7 of the Son: *Your throne, O God, is forever and ever*. The psalm is rendered as the Old Testament has it, with the king in lowercase.
- **v7 "God, your God, has anointed you"** — the king anointed with the oil of gladness, above the other kings; *Messiah* means **Anointed** (2:2).
- **v8 "myrrh and aloes and cassia"** — the spices of the wedding garments (Song of Songs 4:14; John 19:39). **Palaces of ivory**: rooms paneled with ivory, as Ahab's house (1 Kings 22:39; Amos 3:15).
- **v9 "the queen in gold of Ophir"** — the bride, *shegal*, the queen consort (Nehemiah 2:6).
- **vv10–11 "forget your people and your father's house"** — the foreign bride leaves her own people, as Ruth did (Ruth 1:16; Genesis 2:24).
- **v13 "All glorious is the king's daughter within"** — *penimah*, inside the palace, in her chamber before the procession.
- **v17 "therefore peoples will praise you forever"** — the Church has sung the psalm of Christ and His bride, the Church; the Ethiopian Church also sings it of the Virgin Mary, the queen at the King's right hand.

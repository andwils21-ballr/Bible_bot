# Psalms 11

*For the director. Of David.*

**1** In the LORD I take refuge;  
how can you say to my soul,  
"Flee like a bird to your mountain,

**2** for the wicked bend the bow;  
they have set their arrow on the string  
to shoot in the dark at the upright in heart;

**3** if the foundations are torn down,  
what can the righteous do?"

**4** The LORD is in His holy temple;  
the LORD, His throne is in heaven;  
His eyes see, His eyelids test the children of man.

**5** The LORD tests the righteous,  
but His soul hates the wicked and the one who loves violence.

**6** On the wicked He will rain coals,  
fire and sulfur and a scorching wind  
will be the portion of their cup.

**7** For the LORD is righteous; He loves righteous deeds;  
the upright will see His face.

---

## Notes

- **v1 and the title** — here, as in Psalms 14 and 15, the short title shares the first verse with the opening words in the Hebrew, so the verse numbers of Hebrew and English agree.
- **v6 "He will rain coals, fire and sulfur"** — the Hebrew has *pachim*, **snares**; read with one vowel changed, *pachamei*, **coals of**, as the Greek has it. The fire and sulfur of Sodom (Genesis 19:24).

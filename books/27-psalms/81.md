# Psalms 81

*For the director: on the Gittith. Of Asaph.*

**1** Sing for joy to God our strength;  
shout aloud to the God of Jacob.

**2** Raise a song and sound the tambourine,  
the sweet lyre with the harp.

**3** Blow the ram's horn at the new moon,  
at the full moon, on our feast day.

**4** For it is a statute for Israel,  
a rule of the God of Jacob.

**5** He made it a testimony in Joseph  
when He went out over the land of Egypt.  
I heard a language I did not know:

**6** "I removed the burden from his shoulder;  
his hands were freed from the basket.

**7** In distress you called, and I rescued you;  
I answered you in the hiding place of thunder;  
I tested you at the waters of Meribah. *Selah*

**8** Hear, My people, and I will warn you —  
Israel, if you would only listen to Me!

**9** There shall be no strange god among you;  
you shall not bow down to a foreign god.

**10** I am the LORD your God,  
who brought you up out of the land of Egypt.  
Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it.

**11** But My people did not listen to My voice;  
Israel would have none of Me.

**12** So I gave them over to the stubbornness of their heart,  
to follow their own counsels.

**13** If only My people would listen to Me,  
if Israel would walk in My ways!

**14** I would soon subdue their enemies  
and turn My hand against their foes.

**15** Those who hate the LORD would cringe before Him,  
and their time of doom would last forever.

**16** He would feed them with the finest of the wheat,  
and with honey from the rock I would satisfy you."

## Notes

- **v3 "at the new moon, at the full moon, on our feast day"** — likely the seventh month: the ram's horn at its new moon (Leviticus 23:24) and the Feast of Booths at its full moon (Leviticus 23:34).
- **v5 "when He went out over the land of Egypt"** — God going out against Egypt (Exodus 11:4); the Greek reads "when he went out of the land of Egypt." Joseph is spelled here *Yehosef*, the only time in the Bible.
- **v5 "I heard a language I did not know"** — the psalmist hears an unfamiliar voice, God's, and what follows (vv6–16) is that word; others take it as Israel in Egypt hearing a foreign tongue (114:1).
- **v6 "the basket"** — the baskets for carrying clay and bricks in Egypt (Exodus 1:14).
- **v7 "the waters of Meribah"** — Exodus 17:1–7; Numbers 20:13; also 95:8.
- **vv9–10** — the opening of the Ten Commandments (Exodus 20:2–3), with "brought you up" (*hamma'alkha*) for "brought you out."
- **v15 "their time of doom would last forever"** — the Hebrew says only "their time"; it is the haters' time of punishment, or, some read, Israel's time of blessing.
- **v16 "honey from the rock"** — Deuteronomy 32:13; the address switches from "them" to "you," as the Hebrew does.

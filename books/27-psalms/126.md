# Psalms 126

*A song of ascents.*

**1** When the LORD restored the fortunes of Zion,  
we were like those who dream.

**2** Then our mouth was filled with laughter,  
and our tongue with shouts of joy.  
Then they said among the nations,  
"The LORD has done great things for them."

**3** The LORD has done great things for us;  
we were glad.

**4** Restore our fortunes, LORD,  
like streams in the Negev.

**5** Those who sow in tears  
will reap with shouts of joy.

**6** The one who goes out weeping,  
carrying the bag of seed,  
will surely come home with shouts of joy,  
carrying his sheaves.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "When the LORD restored the fortunes of Zion"** — *shivat Tsiyyon*, perhaps "brought back the returning ones of Zion," the return from exile (Ezra 1); "restored the fortunes" as in 14:7 and 85:1.

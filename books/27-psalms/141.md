# Psalms 141

*A psalm of David.*

**1** LORD, I call to You; hurry to me!  
Give ear to my voice when I call to You.

**2** Let my prayer be set before You as incense,  
the lifting up of my hands as the evening offering.

**3** Set a guard, LORD, over my mouth;  
keep watch over the door of my lips.

**4** Do not incline my heart to any evil thing,  
to busy myself with deeds of wickedness  
with men who do iniquity,  
and let me not eat of their delicacies.

**5** Let a righteous man strike me — it is loyal love;  
let him rebuke me — it is oil on my head;  
let my head not refuse it.  
For my prayer is still against their evil deeds.

**6** When their judges are thrown down by the sides of the rock,  
they will hear my words, for they are pleasant.

**7** As when one plows and breaks up the earth,  
our bones are scattered at the mouth of Sheol.

**8** But my eyes are toward You, GOD the Lord;  
in You I take refuge; do not leave my soul defenseless.

**9** Keep me from the hands of the snare they have laid for me,  
and from the traps of evildoers.

**10** Let the wicked fall into their own nets,  
while I pass by in safety.

## Notes

- **v2 "as incense… as the evening offering"** — the incense burned morning and evening (Exodus 30:7–8) and the evening offering (Exodus 29:41); prayer offered where the sacrifice could not be. Compare Revelation 5:8; 8:3–4.
- **v4 "let me not eat of their delicacies"** — as Daniel would not eat the king's food (Daniel 1:8).
- **v5 "Let a righteous man strike me — it is loyal love"** — correction from the righteous is kindness (Proverbs 27:6).
- **vv5–7** — among the hardest verses in the Psalms; the setting is unknown, and every line has other possible readings. The Hebrew is followed word by word.
- **v8 "GOD the Lord"** — *YHWH Adonai*, as in 140:7.
- **v10 "Let the wicked fall into their own nets"** — the trap catches its maker (7:15; 35:8; 57:6).

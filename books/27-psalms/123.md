# Psalms 123

*A song of ascents.*

**1** To You I lift up my eyes,  
You who are enthroned in the heavens.

**2** As the eyes of servants look to the hand of their master,  
as the eyes of a maid to the hand of her mistress,  
so our eyes look to the LORD our God  
until He is gracious to us.

**3** Be gracious to us, LORD, be gracious to us,  
for we have had more than enough of contempt.

**4** Our soul has had more than enough  
of the scorn of those at ease,  
of the contempt of proud oppressors.

---

## Notes

- **v4 "of proud oppressors"** — the read form splits the word in two, *ligei yonim*, "proud ones of the oppressors"; the written form is one word, "the proud." The meaning is close.

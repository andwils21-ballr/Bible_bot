# Psalms 117

**1** Praise the LORD, all nations!  
Extol Him, all peoples!

**2** For great is His loyal love toward us,  
and the truth of the LORD endures forever.  
Praise Yah!

---

## Notes

- **The whole psalm** — the shortest in the Psalms, and the shortest chapter in the Bible.
- **v1 "Praise the LORD, all nations"** — Paul quotes it to show that the nations were always meant to praise Israel's God (Romans 15:11).
- **v2 "loyal love… truth"** — *chesed* and *emet*, the pair of Exodus 34:6.
- **v2 "great is His loyal love toward us"** — *gavar*, "has prevailed over us," the word used of the flood waters rising over the earth (Genesis 7:18–24).

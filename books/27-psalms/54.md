# Psalms 54

*For the director: with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David, when the Ziphites came and said to Saul, "Is not David hiding among us?"*

**1** God, save me by Your name,  
and vindicate me by Your might.

**2** God, hear my prayer;  
listen to the words of my mouth.

**3** For strangers have risen against me,  
and ruthless men seek my life;  
they have not set God before them. *Selah*

**4** God is my helper;  
the Lord is among those who uphold my life.

**5** He will repay the evil to my enemies;  
in Your faithfulness put an end to them.

**6** With a freewill offering I will sacrifice to You;  
I will give thanks to Your name, LORD, for it is good.

**7** For He has delivered me from every trouble,  
and my eye has looked on my enemies.

---

## Notes

- **The title** — the men of Ziph, in the wilderness of Judah, twice betrayed David's hiding place to Saul (1 Samuel 23:19; 26:1). The Hebrew title takes two verses.
- **v1 "save me by Your name"** — God's name as His presence and power (20:1); **vindicate me**, *tedineni*, **judge my case**.
- **v3 "strangers have risen against me"** — *zarim*; Psalm 86:14, which repeats the line, has *zedim*, **the proud**, one letter different. The Ziphites were of David's own tribe, yet acted as strangers.
- **v4 "the Lord is among those who uphold my life"** — God stands with the few who are loyal to David (1 Samuel 23:16–18).
- **v5 "He will repay the evil"** — the read form, *yashiv*; the written form *yashuv*, **the evil will return**.
- **v6 "a freewill offering"** — *nedavah*, a sacrifice given not by vow or duty but gladly (Leviticus 7:16). **LORD**, *YHWH*, the one use of the name in this psalm.
- **v7 "my eye has looked on my enemies"** — has seen their defeat (59:10; 92:11).

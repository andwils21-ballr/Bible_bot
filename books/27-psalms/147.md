# Psalms 147

**1** Praise Yah!  
For it is good to sing praises to our God;  
for it is pleasant, and praise is fitting.

**2** The LORD builds up Jerusalem;  
He gathers the outcasts of Israel.

**3** He heals the brokenhearted  
and binds up their wounds.

**4** He counts the number of the stars;  
He calls them all by name.

**5** Great is our Lord and abundant in power;  
His understanding is beyond measure.

**6** The LORD lifts up the humble;  
He casts the wicked down to the ground.

**7** Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving;  
make music to our God on the lyre,

**8** who covers the heavens with clouds,  
who prepares rain for the earth,  
who makes grass grow on the mountains,

**9** who gives the beasts their food,  
and the young ravens when they cry.

**10** He does not delight in the strength of the horse,  
nor take pleasure in the legs of a man;

**11** the LORD takes pleasure in those who fear Him,  
in those who hope in His loyal love.

**12** Praise the LORD, Jerusalem!  
Praise your God, Zion!

**13** For He has strengthened the bars of your gates;  
He has blessed your children within you.

**14** He makes peace in your borders;  
He satisfies you with the finest of the wheat.

**15** He sends out His command to the earth;  
His word runs swiftly.

**16** He gives snow like wool;  
He scatters frost like ashes.

**17** He hurls down His hail like crumbs;  
who can stand before His cold?

**18** He sends out His word and melts them;  
He makes His wind blow, and the waters flow.

**19** He declares His words to Jacob,  
His statutes and His judgments to Israel.

**20** He has not dealt so with any other nation;  
as for His judgments, they do not know them.  
Praise Yah!

## Notes

- **v2 "He gathers the outcasts of Israel"** — the return from exile (Isaiah 56:8; Nehemiah 12:27–43, when the walls were dedicated).
- **v4 "He counts the number of the stars; He calls them all by name"** — as in Isaiah 40:26; the God who numbers the stars heals the brokenhearted (v3).
- **v9 "the young ravens when they cry"** — as in Job 38:41; compare Luke 12:24, *Consider the ravens… God feeds them*.
- **v10 "the legs of a man"** — the strength of a runner or a foot soldier; God is not impressed by armies (33:16–17).
- **v14 "the finest of the wheat"** — as in 81:16, and the English there is followed.
- **v15 "His command"** — *imrah*, kept as "promise" in Psalm 119; here what goes out to the earth is His order to snow and frost, so "command."
- **v18 "He makes His wind blow"** — *rucho*, wind, not Spirit: the warm wind that thaws the frozen water.
- **v19 "His words"** — the read form; the written form has "His word."
- **vv19–20** — the God who commands the weather has given His law to one people; as in Deuteronomy 4:7–8.

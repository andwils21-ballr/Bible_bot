# Psalms 145

*A praise of David.*

**1** I will exalt You, my God the King,  
and bless Your name forever and ever.

**2** Every day I will bless You  
and praise Your name forever and ever.

**3** Great is the LORD and greatly to be praised,  
and His greatness is unsearchable.

**4** One generation will commend Your works to another  
and declare Your mighty acts.

**5** On the glorious splendor of Your majesty  
and on Your wondrous works I will meditate.

**6** They will speak of the might of Your awesome deeds,  
and I will tell of Your greatness.

**7** They will pour out the memory of Your abundant goodness  
and sing joyfully of Your righteousness.

**8** The LORD is gracious and merciful,  
slow to anger and great in loyal love.

**9** The LORD is good to all,  
and His compassion is over all that He has made.

**10** All Your works will give thanks to You, LORD,  
and Your faithful ones will bless You.

**11** They will speak of the glory of Your kingdom  
and talk of Your might,

**12** to make known to the children of man His mighty acts  
and the glorious splendor of His kingdom.

**13** Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom,  
and Your dominion endures through all generations.

**14** The LORD upholds all who fall  
and raises up all who are bowed down.

**15** The eyes of all look to You,  
and You give them their food in due season.

**16** You open Your hand  
and satisfy the desire of every living thing.

**17** The LORD is righteous in all His ways  
and loyal in all His works.

**18** The LORD is near to all who call on Him,  
to all who call on Him in truth.

**19** He fulfills the desire of those who fear Him;  
He hears their cry and saves them.

**20** The LORD guards all who love Him,  
but all the wicked He will destroy.

**21** My mouth will speak the praise of the LORD,  
and let all flesh bless His holy name  
forever and ever.

## Notes

- **The form** — an acrostic, one verse for each letter of the Hebrew alphabet, except *nun*, which is missing after v13. The Greek has a *nun* line there, "The Lord is faithful in His words and holy in all His works," and a Dead Sea Psalms scroll has nearly the same. The Hebrew is followed, and the line is not added.
- **The title, "A praise of David"** — *tehillah*, the only psalm with this word in its title, though *Tehillim*, "praises," is the Hebrew name of the whole book.
- **v3 "Great is the LORD and greatly to be praised"** — as in 48:1 and 96:4.
- **v8 "gracious and merciful, slow to anger and great in loyal love"** — the words of Exodus 34:6, in the order of 111:4, with "great" (*gedol*) for "abundant."
- **v13 "Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom"** — echoed in Daniel 4:3, 34.
- **vv15–16 "The eyes of all look to You… You open Your hand"** — as in 104:27–28; the lines became a common grace before meals.
- **v17 "loyal in all His works"** — *chasid*, used here of God Himself: loyal to what He has made.
- **v18 "The LORD is near to all who call on Him… in truth"** — not in pretense (Isaiah 29:13); compare Deuteronomy 4:7.

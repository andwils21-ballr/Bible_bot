# Psalms 107

**1** Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**2** Let the redeemed of the LORD say so,  
those He redeemed from the hand of the foe

**3** and gathered from the lands,  
from the east and from the west,  
from the north and from the sea.

**4** Some wandered in the wilderness, on a desert track;  
they found no city to live in.

**5** Hungry and thirsty,  
their soul fainted within them.

**6** Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble,  
and He delivered them from their distress.

**7** He led them by a straight way  
to reach a city to live in.

**8** Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loyal love  
and His wonders for the children of man.

**9** For He satisfies the thirsty soul  
and fills the hungry soul with good things.

**10** Some sat in darkness and the shadow of death,  
prisoners in affliction and iron,

**11** because they had rebelled against the words of God  
and despised the counsel of the Most High.

**12** So He humbled their hearts with hard labor;  
they stumbled, and there was no one to help.

**13** Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble,  
and He saved them from their distress.

**14** He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death  
and broke their chains apart.

**15** Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loyal love  
and His wonders for the children of man.

**16** For He shatters doors of bronze  
and cuts through bars of iron.

**17** Some were fools through their rebellious ways  
and were afflicted because of their iniquities.

**18** Their soul abhorred all food,  
and they drew near to the gates of death.

**19** Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble,  
and He saved them from their distress.

**20** He sent His word and healed them  
and delivered them from their destruction.

**21** Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loyal love  
and His wonders for the children of man.

**22** Let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving  
and tell of His works with songs of joy.

**23** Some went down to the sea in ships,  
doing business on the great waters.

**24** They saw the works of the LORD,  
His wonders in the deep.

**25** He spoke and raised a stormy wind,  
which lifted up its waves.

**26** They rose up to the heavens; they went down to the depths;  
their soul melted away in their trouble.

**27** They reeled and staggered like a drunken man,  
and all their skill was swallowed up.

**28** Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble,  
and He brought them out of their distress.

**29** He made the storm a whisper,  
and their waves were hushed.

**30** They were glad because they grew quiet,  
and He guided them to their desired haven.

**31** Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loyal love  
and His wonders for the children of man.

**32** Let them exalt Him in the assembly of the people  
and praise Him in the council of the elders.

**33** He turns rivers into a desert,  
springs of water into thirsty ground,

**34** a fruitful land into a salt waste,  
because of the wickedness of those who live there.

**35** He turns a desert into pools of water,  
a dry land into springs of water.

**36** There He settles the hungry,  
and they establish a city to live in.

**37** They sow fields and plant vineyards  
and gather a fruitful harvest.

**38** He blesses them, and they multiply greatly,  
and He does not let their cattle decrease.

**39** When they are diminished and brought low  
through oppression, trouble and sorrow,

**40** He pours contempt on nobles  
and makes them wander in a trackless waste.

**41** But He lifts the needy out of affliction  
and makes their families like flocks.

**42** The upright see it and are glad,  
and injustice shuts its mouth.

**43** Whoever is wise, let him heed these things  
and consider the loyal love of the LORD.

## Notes

- **v1** — the fifth book of the Psalms opens with the same words as 106:1, answering the prayer of 106:47, *gather us from the nations*: here the redeemed have been gathered (v3).
- **v3 "from the north and from the sea"** — "the sea" usually means the west; set beside east and west here, it likely stands for the south.
- **vv4–32** — four pictures of rescue: wanderers in the desert, prisoners, the sick, sailors in a storm. Each has the same cry (vv6, 13, 19, 28) and the same refrain (vv8, 15, 21, 31).
- **v10 "the shadow of death"** — *tsalmavet*, deep darkness, as in 23:4.
- **v18 "Their soul abhorred all food"** — *ti'ev*, the verb of what is abhorrent; the sick can eat nothing.
- **v20 "He sent His word and healed them"** — compare the centurion: *only say the word, and my servant will be healed* (Matthew 8:8).
- **vv25–30** — the storm and the calm; the Gospels tell of Jesus rebuking the wind and the waves, *and there was a great calm* (Mark 4:39).
- **v40 "He pours contempt on nobles and makes them wander in a trackless waste"** — from Job 12:21a and 24b, and the English there is followed.
- **v42 "and injustice shuts its mouth"** — as in Job 5:16.

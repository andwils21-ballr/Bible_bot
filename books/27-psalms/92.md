# Psalms 92

*A psalm. A song for the Sabbath day.*

**1** It is good to give thanks to the LORD,  
to sing praises to Your name, Most High,

**2** to declare Your loyal love in the morning  
and Your faithfulness by night,

**3** on the ten-stringed instrument and the harp,  
with the music of the lyre.

**4** For You have made me glad, LORD, by what You have done;  
I sing for joy at the works of Your hands.

**5** How great are Your works, LORD!  
Your thoughts are very deep.

**6** The senseless man does not know;  
the fool does not understand this:

**7** that though the wicked sprout like grass  
and all evildoers flourish,  
they are doomed to destruction forever.

**8** But You, LORD, are on high forever.

**9** For indeed Your enemies, LORD,  
for indeed Your enemies will perish;  
all evildoers will be scattered.

**10** But You have lifted up my horn like that of the wild ox;  
I am anointed with fresh oil.

**11** My eyes have looked on those who lie in wait for me;  
my ears have heard of the evildoers who rise against me.

**12** The righteous will flourish like the palm tree  
and grow like a cedar in Lebanon.

**13** Planted in the house of the LORD,  
they will flourish in the courts of our God.

**14** They will still bear fruit in old age;  
they will be full of sap and green,

**15** to declare that the LORD is upright;  
He is my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in Him.

## Notes

- **The title, "A song for the Sabbath day"** — the only psalm the Hebrew titles assign to a day; it was sung in the temple on the Sabbath (Mishnah Tamid 7:4).
- **v8 "But You, LORD, are on high forever"** — the shortest verse of the psalm stands at its center, between the praise and the fate of the wicked and the righteous.
- **v9** — the repetition is in the Hebrew, like the rising lines of 93:3.
- **v10 "I am anointed with fresh oil"** — *balloti*, a rare form; the Greek reads "my old age with rich oil."
- **v12 "like the palm tree… like a cedar"** — the tall, long-lived trees, set against the grass of v7 (1:3; 37:2; 52:8).
- **v15 "there is no unrighteousness in Him"** — the words of Deuteronomy 32:4, *a God of faithfulness and without iniquity.*

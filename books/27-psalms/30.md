# Psalms 30

*A psalm. A song at the dedication of the house. Of David.*

**1** I will exalt You, LORD, for You have drawn me up  
and have not let my enemies rejoice over me.

**2** LORD my God, I cried to You for help,  
and You healed me.

**3** LORD, You brought up my soul from Sheol;  
You kept me alive, so that I did not go down to the pit.

**4** Sing praise to the LORD, you His faithful ones,  
and give thanks to the remembrance of His holiness.

**5** For His anger is for a moment,  
His favor for a lifetime.  
Weeping may lodge for the evening,  
but at morning there is a shout of joy.

**6** As for me, I said in my ease,  
"I will never be moved."

**7** LORD, by Your favor  
You had set my mountain firm;  
You hid Your face; I was dismayed.

**8** To You, LORD, I called,  
and to the Lord I pleaded for mercy:

**9** "What profit is there in my blood,  
if I go down to the pit?  
Will the dust praise You?  
Will it tell of Your faithfulness?

**10** Hear, LORD, and be gracious to me;  
LORD, be my helper."

**11** You have turned my mourning into dancing for me;  
You have loosed my sackcloth  
and clothed me with gladness,

**12** so that my glory may sing praise to You and not be silent.  
LORD my God, I will give thanks to You forever.

---

## Notes

- **The title "A song at the dedication of the house"** — *chanukkat ha-bayit*: perhaps David's own house (2 Samuel 5:11), or the site of the temple (1 Chronicles 22:1); later sung at the rededication of the temple, the feast of Hanukkah.
- **v11 "You have turned my mourning into dancing"** — *you will be sorrowful, but your sorrow will turn into joy* (John 16:20; Jeremiah 31:13).

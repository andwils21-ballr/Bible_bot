# Psalms 21

*For the director. A psalm of David.*

**1** LORD, in Your strength the king rejoices,  
and in Your salvation how greatly he exults!

**2** You have given him his heart's desire  
and have not withheld the request of his lips. *Selah*

**3** For You meet him with blessings of good;  
You set a crown of fine gold on his head.

**4** He asked You for life; You gave it to him,  
length of days forever and ever.

**5** His glory is great through Your salvation;  
You lay splendor and majesty on him.

**6** For You make him a blessing forever;  
You make him glad with the joy of Your presence.

**7** For the king trusts in the LORD,  
and through the loyal love of the Most High he will not be moved.

**8** Your hand will find all your enemies;  
your right hand will find those who hate you.

**9** You will make them like a blazing oven  
at the time of your appearing.  
The LORD will swallow them up in His anger,  
and fire will consume them.

**10** You will destroy their fruit from the earth,  
and their offspring from among the children of man.

**11** Though they intended evil against you  
and devised a scheme, they will not succeed.

**12** For you will make them turn their backs;  
you will aim your bowstrings at their faces.

**13** Be exalted, LORD, in Your strength!  
We will sing and praise Your might.

## Notes

- **The psalm** — the thanksgiving after victory that answers Psalm 20: there the people asked *May He give you your heart's desire* (20:4); here *You have given him his heart's desire* (v2).
- **v3 "a crown of fine gold"** — *ateret paz*, as David took the crown of the king of the Ammonites (2 Samuel 12:30).
- **v4 "length of days forever and ever"** — the language of the court, *May the king live forever* (1 Kings 1:31), and of God's promise to David's house (2 Samuel 7:16).
- **v6 "You make him a blessing forever"** — *teshitehu verakhot*, as Abraham was made a blessing (Genesis 12:2).
- **v7 "the loyal love of the Most High"** — *chesed Elyon*; the king's trust is the center of the psalm.
- **vv8–12 "your hand… your enemies"** — rendered as spoken to the king, since v9 speaks of the LORD in the third person; the verses can also be read as spoken to God, and then the pronouns would be capitalized.
- **v9 "like a blazing oven"** — *the day is coming, burning like an oven* (Malachi 4:1).
- **v12 "you will aim your bowstrings at their faces"** — *meitarekha*, **your cords**, the strings of the bow (11:2).
- **v13 "Be exalted, LORD, in Your strength!"** — the psalm ends as it began, with *Your strength* (v1).

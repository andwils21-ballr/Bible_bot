# Psalms 52

*For the director. A Maskil of David, when Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul, "David has come to the house of Ahimelech."*

**1** Why do you boast of evil, mighty man?  
The loyal love of God endures all day long.

**2** Your tongue plots ruin,  
like a sharpened razor, you worker of deceit.

**3** You love evil more than good,  
lying more than speaking what is right. *Selah*

**4** You love all words that devour,  
you deceitful tongue.

**5** God will also break you down forever;  
He will snatch you and tear you away from your tent;  
He will uproot you from the land of the living. *Selah*

**6** The righteous will see and fear,  
and will laugh at him:

**7** "Here is the man who would not make God his stronghold,  
but trusted in the abundance of his riches  
and grew strong in his ruin."

**8** But I am like a green olive tree in the house of God;  
I trust in the loyal love of God forever and ever.

**9** I will give thanks to You forever, because You have done it;  
I will wait for Your name, for it is good, in the presence of Your faithful ones.

---

## Notes

- **The title** — Doeg the Edomite, Saul's chief herdsman, saw David at Nob and told Saul, and then killed the priests of Nob at Saul's command (1 Samuel 21:7; 22:9–19). The Hebrew title takes two verses.

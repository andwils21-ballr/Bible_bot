# Psalms 121

*A song for the ascents.*

**1** I lift up my eyes to the mountains —  
where does my help come from?

**2** My help comes from the LORD,  
the Maker of heaven and earth.

**3** He will not let your foot slip;  
He who keeps you will not slumber.

**4** Indeed, He who keeps Israel  
will neither slumber nor sleep.

**5** The LORD is your keeper;  
the LORD is your shade at your right hand.

**6** The sun will not strike you by day,  
nor the moon by night.

**7** The LORD will keep you from all evil;  
He will keep your life.

**8** The LORD will keep your going out and your coming in  
from this time forth and forever.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "I lift up my eyes to the mountains"** — the hills around Jerusalem, where the pilgrim is going, or the hills of the road, with their dangers and their shrines to other gods; either way, help comes from their Maker, not from them.
- **vv3–8 "keeps… keeper… keep"** — *shamar* six times; the English keeps the one word.
- **v4 "He who keeps Israel will neither slumber nor sleep"** — unlike Baal, of whom Elijah said *perhaps he is asleep* (1 Kings 18:27).
- **v6 "nor the moon by night"** — the moon was thought to bring harm at night, as the sun did by day (compare "moonstruck").
- **v8 "your going out and your coming in"** — the whole of life, from leaving home to coming back (Deuteronomy 28:6).

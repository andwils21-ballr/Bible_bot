# Psalms 17

*A prayer of David.*

**1** Hear a just cause, LORD; listen to my cry;  
give ear to my prayer from lips without deceit.

**2** Let my vindication come from Your presence;  
let Your eyes see what is right.

**3** You have tested my heart; You have visited me by night;  
You have refined me, and You find nothing;  
I have resolved that my mouth will not transgress.

**4** As for the deeds of men, by the word of Your lips  
I have kept myself from the paths of the violent.

**5** My steps have held fast to Your tracks;  
my feet have not slipped.

**6** I call on You, for You will answer me, God;  
incline Your ear to me; hear my words.

**7** Show the wonders of Your loyal love,  
You who save by Your right hand  
those who take refuge from those who rise against them.

**8** Keep me as the apple of Your eye;  
hide me in the shadow of Your wings,

**9** from the wicked who plunder me,  
my deadly enemies who surround me.

**10** They have closed up their fat hearts;  
with their mouths they speak proudly.

**11** They have now surrounded our steps;  
they set their eyes to bring us down to the ground.

**12** He is like a lion eager to tear,  
like a young lion lurking in hiding places.

**13** Arise, LORD! Confront him; make him bow down;  
rescue my soul from the wicked by Your sword,

**14** from men by Your hand, LORD,  
from men of the world, whose portion is in this life.  
You fill their belly with what You have stored up;  
their sons are satisfied,  
and they leave what remains to their little ones.

**15** As for me, I will see Your face in righteousness;  
when I awake, I will be satisfied with Your likeness.

## Notes

- **v1 "Hear a just cause"** — *tsedeq*, **righteousness**, a cause that is right; the psalm is the plea of an innocent man before the judge (7:8; 26:1).
- **v3 "You have tested my heart; You have visited me by night"** — the night as the time when God searches the heart (16:7; 139:1–12). **Refined** is the word for silver tried in the fire (12:6).
- **v8 "the apple of Your eye"** — *ishon bat ayin*, **the little man, the daughter of the eye**, the pupil, in which one sees a small image of oneself; *He kept him as the apple of His eye* (Deuteronomy 32:10; Zechariah 2:8).
- **v8 "the shadow of Your wings"** — the wings of a bird over its young (Deuteronomy 32:11; Matthew 23:37), or the wings of the cherubim over the ark (36:7; 63:7).
- **v10 "They have closed up their fat hearts"** — *chelbamo sageru*, **their fat they have closed**: hearts grown fat and unfeeling (73:7; 119:70).
- **v11 "our steps"** — the read form, *sevavunu*, **they have surrounded us**; the written form has **me**.
- **v12 "He is like a lion"** — the enemy as one beast, as in 7:2 and 10:9.
- **v14** — among the hardest verses in the Psalms. It may mean that God fills the wicked with what He has stored up for them in punishment, or with good things in this life only; *Your stored up* (*tsefunkha*, the read form) can also be **Your treasured ones**, the righteous.
- **v15 "when I awake, I will be satisfied with Your likeness"** — *temunah*, the form of the LORD that Moses saw (Numbers 12:8). The awakening may be from the night of v3, or, as the Church has read it, from death (Daniel 12:2).

# Psalms 36

*For the director. Of the servant of the LORD, of David.*

**1** Transgression speaks to the wicked  
deep within my heart;  
there is no fear of God  
before his eyes.

**2** For he flatters himself in his own eyes  
that his iniquity will not be found out and hated.

**3** The words of his mouth are trouble and deceit;  
he has stopped acting wisely and doing good.

**4** He plots trouble on his bed;  
he sets himself on a way that is not good;  
he does not reject evil.

**5** LORD, Your loyal love is in the heavens,  
Your faithfulness reaches to the clouds.

**6** Your righteousness is like the mountains of God;  
Your judgments are a great deep;  
man and beast You save, LORD.

**7** How precious is Your loyal love, God!  
The children of man take refuge in the shadow of Your wings.

**8** They are filled with the abundance of Your house,  
and You give them drink from the river of Your delights.

**9** For with You is the fountain of life;  
in Your light we see light.

**10** Continue Your loyal love to those who know You,  
and Your righteousness to the upright in heart.

**11** Let not the foot of pride come against me,  
nor the hand of the wicked drive me away.

**12** There the doers of iniquity have fallen;  
they are thrust down and cannot rise.

## Notes

- **The title "Of the servant of the LORD"** — as in Psalm 18; David as the servant of God (2 Samuel 7:5).
- **v1 "Transgression speaks to the wicked deep within my heart"** — *ne'um pesha*, **an oracle of transgression**: sin speaks to the wicked man as God speaks to a prophet. The Hebrew has **my heart**, as though the psalmist hears it; the Greek, Syriac and some Hebrew manuscripts read **his heart**.
- **v1 "there is no fear of God before his eyes"** — the last line of Paul's charge against all mankind (Romans 3:18).
- **v4 "He plots trouble on his bed"** — the opposite of the righteous man who meditates at night (4:4; 63:6; Micah 2:1).
- **vv5–6** — God's *chesed*, faithfulness, righteousness and judgments, as high as the heavens, as great as the mountains and the deep: the whole world measured against the wicked man's small plotting.
- **v6 "man and beast You save"** — God's care reaches to the animals (104:14–15; Jonah 4:11).
- **v7 "the shadow of Your wings"** — as in 17:8.
- **v8 "the river of Your delights"** — *adanekha*, **Your Edens**, the word of the garden of Eden, which was watered by a river (Genesis 2:10; Ezekiel 47:1–12).
- **v9 "in Your light we see light"** — the Church has read it of the Son, *light from light*, *the true light that gives light to everyone* (John 1:4–9).
- **v12 "There the doers of iniquity have fallen"** — **there**, at the place and time of God's judgment, seen by the psalmist as already done.

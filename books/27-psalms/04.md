# Psalms 4

*For the director: with stringed instruments. A psalm of David.*

**1** Answer me when I call, God of my righteousness!  
You gave me room when I was in distress;  
be gracious to me and hear my prayer.

**2** Sons of men, how long will my honor be turned to shame?  
How long will you love emptiness and seek after lies? *Selah*

**3** Know that the LORD has set apart the faithful for Himself;  
the LORD hears when I call to Him.

**4** Tremble, and do not sin;  
speak in your hearts on your beds, and be silent. *Selah*

**5** Offer sacrifices of righteousness,  
and trust in the LORD.

**6** Many are saying, "Who will show us good?"  
Lift up the light of Your face on us, LORD.

**7** You have put joy in my heart,  
more than when their grain and new wine abound.

**8** In peace I will both lie down and sleep,  
for You alone, LORD, make me dwell in safety.

---

## Notes

- **v4 "Tremble, and do not sin"** — *rigzu*, **tremble** with anger or with fear. The Greek has *Be angry, and do not sin*, which Paul quotes (Ephesians 4:26).

# Psalms 4

*For the director: with stringed instruments. A psalm of David.*

**1** Answer me when I call, God of my righteousness!  
You gave me room when I was in distress;  
be gracious to me and hear my prayer.

**2** Sons of men, how long will my honor be turned to shame?  
How long will you love emptiness and seek after lies? *Selah*

**3** Know that the LORD has set apart the faithful for Himself;  
the LORD hears when I call to Him.

**4** Tremble, and do not sin;  
speak in your hearts on your beds, and be silent. *Selah*

**5** Offer sacrifices of righteousness,  
and trust in the LORD.

**6** Many are saying, "Who will show us good?"  
Lift up the light of Your face on us, LORD.

**7** You have put joy in my heart,  
more than when their grain and new wine abound.

**8** In peace I will both lie down and sleep,  
for You alone, LORD, make me dwell in safety.

## Notes

- **The title "For the director"** — *la-menatse'ach*, found in fifty-five psalm titles and in Habakkuk 3:19: for the leader of the temple music. **With stringed instruments** is *bi-neginot*.
- **v1 "You gave me room when I was in distress"** — *ba-tsar hirchavta li*: distress is a narrow place, and God sets the sufferer in a broad one (18:19; 118:5).
- **v2 "Sons of men"** — *benei ish*, men of rank, perhaps those who slander the king; **emptiness** and **lies** may be false gods (40:4).
- **v3 "the faithful"** — *chasid*, one who keeps *chesed*, **loyal love**, toward God.
- **v4 "Tremble, and do not sin"** — *rigzu*, **tremble** with anger or with fear. The Greek has *Be angry, and do not sin*, which Paul quotes (Ephesians 4:26).
- **v6 "Lift up the light of Your face on us"** — the priestly blessing, *The LORD make His face shine on you* (Numbers 6:25–26).
- **v7 "their grain and new wine"** — the joy of harvest; God's gift of joy is greater than the harvest of those who ask *Who will show us good?*
- **v8 "In peace I will both lie down and sleep"** — an evening psalm, as Psalm 3 is a morning psalm (3:5). **Alone** may go with **You** or with **me**: *You make me dwell alone in safety* (Deuteronomy 33:28).

# Psalms 67

*For the director: with stringed instruments. A psalm. A song.*

**1** May God be gracious to us and bless us  
and make His face shine on us, *Selah*

**2** so that Your way may be known on the earth,  
Your salvation among all nations.

**3** Let the peoples praise You, God;  
let all the peoples praise You.

**4** Let the nations be glad and sing for joy,  
for You judge the peoples with equity  
and guide the nations on the earth. *Selah*

**5** Let the peoples praise You, God;  
let all the peoples praise You.

**6** The earth has given its produce;  
God, our God, blesses us.

**7** God blesses us;  
let all the ends of the earth fear Him.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "May God be gracious to us and bless us and make His face shine on us"** — the priestly blessing of Numbers 6:24–26, *The LORD bless you… make His face shine on you and be gracious to you*, turned into the people's prayer.
- **v2 "so that Your way may be known on the earth"** — Israel is blessed so that the nations may know God: the promise to Abraham, *in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed* (Genesis 12:2–3).
- **vv3, 5** — the refrain, the same both times.
- **v6 "The earth has given its produce"** — the harvest as a sign of God's blessing (65:9–13; Leviticus 26:4), perhaps sung at the Feast of Weeks or of Booths.

# Psalms 135

**1** Praise Yah!  
Praise the name of the LORD;  
praise Him, servants of the LORD,

**2** you who stand in the house of the LORD,  
in the courts of the house of our God.

**3** Praise Yah, for the LORD is good;  
sing praises to His name, for it is pleasant.

**4** For Yah has chosen Jacob for Himself,  
Israel as His own treasure.

**5** For I know that the LORD is great,  
and our Lord is above all gods.

**6** Whatever the LORD pleases He does,  
in heaven and on earth,  
in the seas and all the deeps.

**7** He makes the clouds rise from the ends of the earth;  
He makes lightning for the rain  
and brings out the wind from His storehouses.

**8** He struck down the firstborn of Egypt,  
both of man and of beast.

**9** He sent signs and wonders among you, Egypt,  
against Pharaoh and all his servants.

**10** He struck down many nations  
and killed mighty kings —

**11** Sihon king of the Amorites,  
Og king of Bashan,  
and all the kingdoms of Canaan —

**12** giving their land as an inheritance,  
an inheritance to Israel His people.

**13** Your name, LORD, endures forever,  
Your remembrance, LORD, for all generations.

**14** For the LORD will judge His people  
and have compassion on His servants.

**15** The idols of the nations are silver and gold,  
the work of human hands.

**16** They have mouths but do not speak;  
eyes, but do not see;

**17** they have ears but do not give ear,  
nor is there any breath in their mouths.

**18** Those who make them will become like them,  
and so will all who trust in them.

**19** House of Israel, bless the LORD!  
House of Aaron, bless the LORD!

**20** House of Levi, bless the LORD!  
You who fear the LORD, bless the LORD!

**21** Blessed be the LORD from Zion,  
He who dwells in Jerusalem.  
Praise Yah!

## Notes

- **v4 "Israel as His own treasure"** — *segullah*, as in Exodus 19:5.
- **v7** — the same words as Jeremiah 10:13 and 51:16.
- **v13 "Your name… Your remembrance… for all generations"** — the words God spoke at the burning bush (Exodus 3:15), turned into praise; the English there is followed.
- **v14** — the same words as Deuteronomy 32:36a, and the English there is followed.
- **vv15–18** — nearly the same as 115:4–8, and the English there is followed; here "the idols of the nations," "do not give ear" (*ya'azinu*) for "do not hear," and the last line "nor is there any breath in their mouths" for the senses of 115:6b–7.
- **vv19–20** — as in 115:9–11 and 118:2–4, with the house of Levi added.
- **v21 "Blessed be the LORD from Zion"** — the answer to 134:3: the LORD blesses from Zion, and from Zion He is blessed.

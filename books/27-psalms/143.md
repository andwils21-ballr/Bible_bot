# Psalms 143

*A psalm of David.*

**1** LORD, hear my prayer;  
give ear to my pleas;  
in Your faithfulness answer me,  
in Your righteousness.

**2** Do not enter into judgment with Your servant,  
for no one living is righteous before You.

**3** For the enemy has pursued my soul;  
he has crushed my life to the ground;  
he has made me dwell in darkness  
like those long dead.

**4** So my spirit grows faint within me;  
my heart within me is appalled.

**5** I remember the days of old;  
I meditate on all Your works;  
I ponder the work of Your hands.

**6** I spread out my hands to You;  
my soul thirsts for You like a weary land. *Selah*

**7** Answer me quickly, LORD!  
My spirit fails.  
Do not hide Your face from me,  
or I will be like those who go down to the pit.

**8** Let me hear of Your loyal love in the morning,  
for in You I trust.  
Make me know the way I should go,  
for to You I lift up my soul.

**9** Deliver me from my enemies, LORD;  
I take shelter in You.

**10** Teach me to do Your will,  
for You are my God.  
Let Your good Spirit lead me  
on level ground.

**11** For Your name's sake, LORD, give me life;  
in Your righteousness bring my soul out of trouble.

**12** In Your loyal love cut off my enemies,  
and destroy all who afflict my soul,  
for I am Your servant.

## Notes

- **v2 "for no one living is righteous before You"** — Paul builds on this line: *by the works of the law no flesh will be justified in His sight* (Romans 3:20; Galatians 2:16). The last of the seven penitential psalms.
- **v3 "like those long dead"** — the same words as Lamentations 3:6.
- **v4 "So my spirit grows faint within me"** — as in 142:3.
- **v6 "my soul thirsts for You like a weary land"** — as in 63:1, *in a dry and weary land*.
- **v8 "to You I lift up my soul"** — as in 25:1 and 86:4.
- **v9 "I take shelter in You"** — *elekha khissiti*, "to You I have covered myself"; the form is unusual, and the Greek reads "to You I have fled."
- **v10 "Let Your good Spirit lead me"** — *ruach* of God, capitalized; the same phrase as Nehemiah 9:20, *Your good Spirit*.

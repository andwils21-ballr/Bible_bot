# Psalms 88

*A song. A psalm of the sons of Korah. For the director: on Mahalath Leannoth. A Maskil of Heman the Ezrahite.*

**1** LORD, God of my salvation,  
by day I cry out, and by night, before You.

**2** Let my prayer come before You;  
incline Your ear to my cry.

**3** For my soul is full of troubles,  
and my life draws near to Sheol.

**4** I am counted among those who go down to the pit;  
I have become like a man without strength,

**5** set loose among the dead,  
like the slain who lie in the grave,  
whom You remember no more,  
for they are cut off from Your hand.

**6** You have put me in the lowest pit,  
in dark places, in the depths.

**7** Your wrath lies heavy on me,  
and You have afflicted me with all Your waves. *Selah*

**8** You have put my companions far from me;  
You have made me detestable to them;  
I am shut in and cannot get out.

**9** My eye grows dim through affliction;  
I call on You, LORD, every day;  
I spread out my hands to You.

**10** Do You work wonders for the dead?  
Do the shades rise up to praise You? *Selah*

**11** Is Your loyal love told in the grave,  
Your faithfulness in Abaddon?

**12** Are Your wonders known in the darkness,  
or Your righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?

**13** But I cry to You, LORD, for help;  
in the morning my prayer comes before You.

**14** LORD, why do You reject my soul?  
Why do You hide Your face from me?

**15** I have been afflicted and close to death from my youth;  
I bear Your terrors; I am in despair.

**16** Your burning anger has swept over me;  
Your terrors have destroyed me.

**17** They surround me like water all day long;  
they close in on me together.

**18** You have put loved one and friend far from me;  
my companions are darkness.

## Notes

- **The title** — the psalm is credited both to the sons of Korah and to Heman the Ezrahite, one of the wise men surpassed by Solomon (1 Kings 4:31) and a singer under David (1 Chronicles 15:19). *Mahalath Leannoth* may mean "sickness, to afflict," or name a tune.
- **The whole psalm** — the only lament in the Psalms that ends with no turn to hope; its last word is "darkness." Yet it is prayer from first to last, addressed to the "God of my salvation" (v1).
- **v5 "set loose among the dead"** — *chofshi*, free, as a slave is freed (Exodus 21:2); here freed from life, with no place among the living.
- **v8 "You have made me detestable to them"** — *to'evot*, the word for what is abhorrent; his friends shun him like an unclean thing, as in Job 19:13–19.
- **v10 "the shades"** — *refa'im*, the weak spirits of the dead in Sheol (Isaiah 14:9; 26:14).
- **vv10–12** — the dead cannot praise God, so the psalmist pleads to be kept alive to praise (6:5; 30:9; Isaiah 38:18).
- **v11 "Abaddon"** — "destruction," a name for the place of the dead (Job 26:6; 28:22; Proverbs 15:11).
- **v15 "I am in despair"** — *afunah* occurs only here; the meaning is uncertain.

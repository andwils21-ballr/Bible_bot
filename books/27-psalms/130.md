# Psalms 130

*A song of ascents.*

**1** Out of the depths I call to You, LORD.

**2** Lord, hear my voice!  
Let Your ears be attentive  
to the voice of my pleas.

**3** If You, Yah, kept a record of iniquities,  
Lord, who could stand?

**4** But with You there is forgiveness,  
so that You may be feared.

**5** I wait for the LORD; my soul waits,  
and in His word I hope.

**6** My soul waits for the Lord  
more than watchmen for the morning,  
more than watchmen for the morning.

**7** Israel, hope in the LORD!  
For with the LORD there is loyal love,  
and with Him is abundant redemption.

**8** He will redeem Israel  
from all its iniquities.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "Out of the depths"** — the deep waters, a picture of distress and guilt (69:2, 14); one of the seven penitential psalms, known by its Latin opening, *De profundis*.
- **vv2–3, 6 "Lord… Yah… Lord"** — *Adonai* three times and *Yah*, beside *YHWH* in vv1, 5 and 7.
- **v4 "so that You may be feared"** — forgiveness does not make God less to be feared but more: the one forgiven reveres the One who forgave.
- **v6 "more than watchmen for the morning"** — the line repeated, as the Hebrew does: the long wait of the night watch for dawn.
- **v8 "He will redeem Israel from all its iniquities"** — from the guilt itself, not only its results; compare Matthew 1:21, *He will save His people from their sins*.

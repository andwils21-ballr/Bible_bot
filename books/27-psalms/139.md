# Psalms 139

*For the director. Of David. A psalm.*

**1** LORD, You have searched me and known me.

**2** You know when I sit down and when I rise up;  
You understand my thoughts from afar.

**3** You search out my path and my lying down  
and are acquainted with all my ways.

**4** For there is not a word on my tongue  
but You, LORD, know it altogether.

**5** You hem me in, behind and before,  
and lay Your hand on me.

**6** Such knowledge is too wonderful for me;  
it is high; I cannot reach it.

**7** Where can I go from Your Spirit?  
Where can I flee from Your presence?

**8** If I go up to the heavens, You are there;  
if I make my bed in Sheol, You are there.

**9** If I take the wings of the dawn  
and dwell at the farthest part of the sea,

**10** even there Your hand will lead me,  
and Your right hand will hold me.

**11** If I say, "Surely the darkness will cover me,  
and the light around me become night,"

**12** even the darkness is not dark to You;  
the night shines like the day,  
for darkness is as light to You.

**13** For You formed my inmost parts;  
You knit me together in my mother's womb.

**14** I give thanks to You,  
for I am fearfully and wonderfully made;  
wonderful are Your works,  
and my soul knows it very well.

**15** My frame was not hidden from You  
when I was made in secret,  
intricately woven in the depths of the earth.

**16** Your eyes saw my unformed body;  
in Your book they were all written,  
the days that were formed,  
and one of them was set for it.

**17** How precious to me are Your thoughts, God!  
How vast is the sum of them!

**18** If I counted them, they would outnumber the sand.  
I awake, and I am still with You.

**19** If only You would slay the wicked, God!  
Men of blood, depart from me —

**20** those who speak of You with evil intent;  
Your enemies take Your name in vain.

**21** Do I not hate those who hate You, LORD,  
and feel disgust at those who rise up against You?

**22** I hate them with complete hatred;  
they have become my enemies.

**23** Search me, God, and know my heart;  
test me and know my anxious thoughts.

**24** See if there is any grievous way in me,  
and lead me in the way everlasting.

## Notes

- **v7 "Where can I go from Your Spirit?"** — *ruach* of God, capitalized; "Your Spirit" and "Your presence" are said in parallel.
- **v8 "if I make my bed in Sheol"** — even the place of the dead is not outside God's reach (Amos 9:2; Job 26:6).
- **v13 "my inmost parts"** — literally "my kidneys," the seat of the deepest self (73:21).
- **v15 "intricately woven in the depths of the earth"** — *ruqqamti*, embroidered like many-colored cloth; "the depths of the earth" is a picture for the hidden womb (compare Job 1:21).
- **v16 "and one of them was set for it"** — the read form, *velo echad bahem*: each of the days was appointed for the unformed body. The written form has *velo*, "and not," giving the reading most English versions follow: "when as yet there was not one of them."
- **v20 "Your enemies take Your name in vain"** — *aryekha* is an Aramaic-like form for "Your enemies"; the line echoes the third commandment (Exodus 20:7).
- **v21 "feel disgust at"** — *etqotat*, as in 119:158; "loathe" is kept in this rendering for *sheqets*.
- **vv23–24 "Search me, God, and know my heart"** — the psalm ends where it began (v1), now asking for the searching it first described.

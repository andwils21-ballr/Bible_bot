# Psalms 34

*Of David, when he changed his behavior before Abimelech, who drove him out, and he went away.*

**1** I will bless the LORD at all times;  
His praise will always be in my mouth.

**2** My soul makes its boast in the LORD;  
let the humble hear and be glad.

**3** Magnify the LORD with me,  
and let us exalt His name together.

**4** I sought the LORD, and He answered me  
and delivered me from all my fears.

**5** Those who look to Him are radiant,  
and their faces will never be ashamed.

**6** This poor man cried, and the LORD heard  
and saved him out of all his troubles.

**7** The messenger of the LORD camps  
around those who fear Him, and rescues them.

**8** Taste and see that the LORD is good;  
blessed is the man who takes refuge in Him.

**9** Fear the LORD, you His holy ones,  
for those who fear Him lack nothing.

**10** The young lions suffer want and hunger,  
but those who seek the LORD lack no good thing.

**11** Come, children, listen to me;  
I will teach you the fear of the LORD.

**12** Who is the man who desires life  
and loves many days, to see good?

**13** Keep your tongue from evil  
and your lips from speaking deceit.

**14** Turn away from evil and do good;  
seek peace and pursue it.

**15** The eyes of the LORD are toward the righteous,  
and His ears toward their cry.

**16** The face of the LORD is against those who do evil,  
to cut off the memory of them from the earth.

**17** They cry, and the LORD hears  
and delivers them out of all their troubles.

**18** The LORD is near to the brokenhearted  
and saves the crushed in spirit.

**19** Many are the troubles of the righteous,  
but the LORD delivers him out of them all.

**20** He keeps all his bones;  
not one of them is broken.

**21** Evil will kill the wicked,  
and those who hate the righteous will be held guilty.

**22** The LORD redeems the soul of His servants;  
none who take refuge in Him will be held guilty.

## Notes

- **The title "when he changed his behavior before Abimelech"** — David's feigned madness before the king of Gath, called Achish in 1 Samuel 21:10–15. *Abimelech*, **my father is king**, may have been a royal title of the Philistine kings (Genesis 20:2; 26:1).
- **The psalm** — an acrostic, each verse beginning with the next letter of the alphabet (one letter is missing); the last verse stands outside the alphabet, as in Psalm 25.
- **v5 "Those who look to Him are radiant"** — *naharu*, **beam with light**, as Moses' face shone (Exodus 34:29; Isaiah 60:5).
- **v6 "This poor man"** — David speaking of himself.
- **v7 "The messenger of the LORD"** — *mal'akh YHWH*, **messenger**, as the word table has it; the one who camped around Jacob at Mahanaim, **the camp of God** (Genesis 32:1–2; 2 Kings 6:17).
- **v8 "Taste and see that the LORD is good"** — *ta'amu*, the word used in the title for David's **behavior** (*ta'mo*, **his sense**): he feigned a lack of sense, and here invites others to taste. *If indeed you have tasted that the Lord is good* (1 Peter 2:3).
- **vv12–16** — quoted in full by Peter (1 Peter 3:10–12).
- **v18 "the brokenhearted… the crushed in spirit"** — *A broken and contrite heart, O God, You will not despise* (51:17; Isaiah 57:15).
- **v20 "not one of them is broken"** — quoted by John of Jesus on the cross, whose legs were not broken (John 19:36), with the law of the Passover lamb (Exodus 12:46).

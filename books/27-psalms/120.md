# Psalms 120

*A song of ascents.*

**1** In my distress I called to the LORD,  
and He answered me.

**2** LORD, deliver my soul from lying lips,  
from a deceitful tongue.

**3** What will He give you,  
and what more will He do to you,  
you deceitful tongue?

**4** A warrior's sharp arrows,  
with burning coals of the broom tree!

**5** Woe is me, that I live as a guest in Meshech,  
that I dwell among the tents of Kedar!

**6** Too long has my soul dwelt  
with those who hate peace.

**7** I am for peace,  
but when I speak, they are for war.

---

## Notes

- **The title, "A song of ascents"** — the first of fifteen (120–134), probably sung by pilgrims going up to Jerusalem for the feasts, or on the steps of the temple.

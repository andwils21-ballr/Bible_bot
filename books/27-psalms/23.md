# Psalms 23

*A psalm of David.*

**1** The LORD is my shepherd; I lack nothing.

**2** He makes me lie down in green pastures;  
He leads me beside still waters.

**3** He brings back my soul;  
He guides me in paths of righteousness  
for His name's sake.

**4** Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death,  
I fear no evil, for You are with me;  
Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me.

**5** You prepare a table before me  
in the presence of my enemies;  
You anoint my head with oil;  
my cup overflows.

**6** Surely goodness and loyal love will pursue me  
all the days of my life,  
and I will dwell in the house of the LORD  
for length of days.

## Notes

- **v1 "The LORD is my shepherd"** — *YHWH ro'i*: the king as shepherd of his people (2 Samuel 5:2), and God as Israel's shepherd (80:1; Genesis 48:15; Ezekiel 34:11–16). David was a shepherd before he was a king (1 Samuel 16:11). *I am the good shepherd* (John 10:11).
- **v2 "still waters"** — *mei menuchot*, **waters of rest**, the quiet water a flock can drink from safely.
- **v3 "He brings back my soul"** — *yeshovev*, restores life, as in 19:7; or brings back the strayed sheep. **Paths of righteousness** are the right tracks, and also the ways of right living.
- **v4 "the valley of the shadow of death"** — *gei tsalmavet*, a valley of deep darkness (Job 10:21–22; Jeremiah 2:6). **Rod and staff**: the club against beasts and the crook to guide.
- **v4 "for You are with me"** — at the center of the psalm the speech turns from *He* to *You*.
- **v5 "You prepare a table before me"** — the shepherd becomes a host; a host who anoints his guest's head with oil (Luke 7:46) and keeps his cup full, while the enemies look on and cannot touch him.
- **v6 "goodness and loyal love will pursue me"** — *yirdefuni*, the word for enemies in pursuit (7:1): instead of foes, God's goodness and *chesed* are at his heels.
- **v6 "I will dwell in the house of the LORD"** — the Hebrew has *ve-shavti*, **and I will return**; the Greek and most translations read *ve-yashavti*, **and I will dwell**, as in 27:4, followed here. **For length of days**, *le-orekh yamim*, is for all one's life, and beyond.

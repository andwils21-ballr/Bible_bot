# Psalms 99

**1** The LORD reigns; let the peoples tremble.  
He is enthroned on the cherubim; let the earth quake.

**2** The LORD is great in Zion;  
He is exalted over all the peoples.

**3** Let them praise Your name, great and awesome.  
Holy is He!

**4** The strength of the King loves justice;  
You have established equity;  
You have done justice and righteousness in Jacob.

**5** Exalt the LORD our God,  
and worship at His footstool.  
Holy is He!

**6** Moses and Aaron were among His priests,  
Samuel among those who called on His name;  
they called to the LORD, and He answered them.

**7** He spoke to them in the pillar of cloud;  
they kept His testimonies  
and the statute He gave them.

**8** LORD our God, You answered them;  
You were a forgiving God to them,  
yet an avenger of their misdeeds.

**9** Exalt the LORD our God,  
and worship at His holy mountain,  
for the LORD our God is holy.

## Notes

- **vv3, 5, 9 "Holy is He"** — three times, ending each part of the psalm, like the threefold "Holy" of the seraphim (Isaiah 6:3).
- **v1 "enthroned on the cherubim"** — as in 80:1 and 1 Samuel 4:4.
- **v4 "The strength of the King loves justice"** — the King is the LORD; the line is hard, and some join it to v3, "and the strength of the King who loves justice."
- **v5 "His footstool"** — the ark (1 Chronicles 28:2), or Zion itself (132:7; Lamentations 2:1).
- **v6 "Moses and Aaron… Samuel"** — the great intercessors (Exodus 32:11–14; 1 Samuel 7:9; 12:23; Jeremiah 15:1).
- **v8 "a forgiving God… yet an avenger of their misdeeds"** — God forgave, yet Moses and Aaron did not enter the land (Numbers 20:12); compare Exodus 34:7.

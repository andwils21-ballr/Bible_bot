# Psalms 43

**1** Vindicate me, God, and plead my cause  
against an unfaithful nation;  
from the man of deceit and injustice rescue me.

**2** For You are the God of my stronghold;  
why have You rejected me?  
Why do I go about mourning  
because of the oppression of the enemy?

**3** Send out Your light and Your truth; let them lead me;  
let them bring me to Your holy mountain  
and to Your dwellings.

**4** Then I will come to the altar of God,  
to God, my exceeding joy,  
and I will praise You with the lyre,  
God, my God.

**5** Why are you bowed down, my soul,  
and why are you in turmoil within me?  
Hope in God, for I will yet praise Him,  
the salvation of my face and my God.

---

## Notes

- **The psalm** — the third part of the poem begun in Psalm 42 (see the note there); it has no title of its own.
- **v1 "an unfaithful nation"** — *goy lo chasid*, a people without *chesed*, without loyal love.
- **v2 "Why do I go about mourning"** — the words of 42:9, with **rejected** for **forgotten**.
- **v3 "Send out Your light and Your truth; let them lead me"** — light and truth as God's messengers, guides on the road back to the temple (27:1; 57:3).
- **v4 "God, my exceeding joy"** — *El simchat gili*, **God, the joy of my rejoicing**.
- **v5** — the refrain, as in 42:11, now ending the whole poem in hope.

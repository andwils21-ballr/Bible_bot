# Psalms 62

*For the director: for Jeduthun. A psalm of David.*

**1** For God alone my soul waits in silence;  
from Him comes my salvation.

**2** He alone is my rock and my salvation,  
my stronghold; I will not be greatly moved.

**3** How long will you rush at a man,  
all of you, to batter him down,  
like a leaning wall, a tottering fence?

**4** They only plan to push him down from his high place;  
they take pleasure in lies.  
They bless with their mouth,  
but inwardly they curse. *Selah*

**5** For God alone, my soul, wait in silence,  
for my hope is from Him.

**6** He alone is my rock and my salvation,  
my stronghold; I will not be moved.

**7** On God rests my salvation and my glory;  
my rock of strength, my refuge is in God.

**8** Trust in Him at all times, you people;  
pour out your heart before Him;  
God is a refuge for us. *Selah*

**9** The sons of men are only a breath;  
the sons of men of rank are a lie;  
in the scales they go up;  
together they are lighter than a breath.

**10** Do not trust in oppression,  
and put no empty hope in robbery;  
if wealth increases, do not set your heart on it.

**11** God has spoken once;  
twice I have heard this:  
that power belongs to God,

**12** and loyal love belongs to You, Lord;  
for You repay a man according to his work.

## Notes

- **The psalm** — *akh*, **only**, **alone**, **surely**, begins six of its lines (vv1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 9): God alone is to be trusted.
- **vv1, 5 "For God alone my soul waits in silence"** — the refrain, slightly varied: v1 *dumiyyah*, **silence**, a statement; v5 *dommi*, **be silent**, the soul told to wait.
- **vv2, 6** — the second refrain: **I will not be greatly moved** (v2) becomes **I will not be moved** (v6).
- **v3 "like a leaning wall, a tottering fence"** — the enemies attack one already near falling.
- **v8 "pour out your heart before Him"** — *shifkhu*, as water poured out (1 Samuel 1:15; Lamentations 2:19).
- **v9 "The sons of men… the sons of men of rank"** — *benei adam* and *benei ish*, the common people and the great: put on God's scales, both together weigh less than **a breath**, *hevel* (39:5).
- **v10 "if wealth increases, do not set your heart on it"** — as in 49:16–17 and 1 Timothy 6:17.
- **vv11–12 "God has spoken once; twice I have heard this"** — the counting saying of wisdom (Job 33:14; Proverbs 6:16): power and loyal love both belong to God. **You repay a man according to his work** is quoted in Romans 2:6.

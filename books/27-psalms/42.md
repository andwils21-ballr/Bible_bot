# Psalms 42

*For the director. A Maskil of the sons of Korah.*

**1** As a deer longs for streams of water,  
so my soul longs for You, God.

**2** My soul thirsts for God, for the living God.  
When shall I come and appear before God?

**3** My tears have been my bread day and night,  
while they say to me all day long,  
"Where is your God?"

**4** These things I remember as I pour out my soul within me:  
how I would pass along with the throng  
and lead them in procession to the house of God,  
with the sound of shouts of joy and thanksgiving,  
a multitude keeping the feast.

**5** Why are you bowed down, my soul,  
and why are you in turmoil within me?  
Hope in God, for I will yet praise Him  
for the salvation of His presence.

**6** My God, my soul is bowed down within me;  
therefore I remember You  
from the land of the Jordan and the Hermons,  
from Mount Mizar.

**7** Deep calls to deep  
at the roar of Your waterfalls;  
all Your breakers and Your waves  
have gone over me.

**8** By day the LORD commands His loyal love,  
and at night His song is with me,  
a prayer to the God of my life.

**9** I will say to God my rock,  
"Why have You forgotten me?  
Why do I go about mourning  
because of the oppression of the enemy?"

**10** As with a shattering in my bones, my adversaries taunt me,  
while they say to me all day long,  
"Where is your God?"

**11** Why are you bowed down, my soul,  
and why are you in turmoil within me?  
Hope in God, for I will yet praise Him,  
the salvation of my face and my God.

## Notes

- **v1 and the second book of the Psalms (42–72)** — in these psalms God is mostly called *Elohim*, **God**, where the first book has *YHWH*, **the LORD**.
- **The title "of the sons of Korah"** — the Levite singers descended from Korah (Numbers 26:11; 1 Chronicles 6:31–37), named over Psalms 42–49, 84–85 and 87–88.
- **v5 and Psalm 43** — Psalms 42 and 43 are one poem in three parts, with the same refrain (42:5, 11; 43:5); Psalm 43 has no title, and many Hebrew manuscripts join the two.
- **v2 "When shall I come and appear before God?"** — *ve-era'eh*, **be seen**, the word for appearing at the temple at the feasts (Exodus 23:17); with other vowels, *ve-ereh*, **and see the face of God**.
- **v4 "how I would pass along with the throng"** — the pilgrim procession to Jerusalem at the feasts (122:1–4; Isaiah 30:29). The psalmist is now far away.
- **v5 "for the salvation of His presence"** — *yeshu'ot panav*, **the salvations of His face**; the refrain in v11 and 43:5 has *panai ve-Elohai*, **of my face, and my God**. Many Hebrew manuscripts and the Greek read v5 as v11.
- **v6 "the land of the Jordan and the Hermons, from Mount Mizar"** — the far north, where the Jordan rises below Mount Hermon; **Mizar**, **little**, is not otherwise known.
- **v7 "Deep calls to deep"** — the torrents of the Jordan's sources roaring down from Hermon become the waves of trouble passing over him, as over Jonah (Jonah 2:3).
- **v8 "By day the LORD commands His loyal love"** — the only use of *YHWH* in Psalms 42–43.
- **vv3, 10 "Where is your God?"** — the taunt of those around him, as at the cross, *He trusts in God; let God deliver Him now* (Matthew 27:43; Joel 2:17).

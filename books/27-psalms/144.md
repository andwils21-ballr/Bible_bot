# Psalms 144

*Of David.*

**1** Blessed be the LORD, my rock,  
who trains my hands for battle  
and my fingers for war;

**2** my loyal love and my fortress,  
my stronghold and my deliverer,  
my shield, in whom I take refuge,  
who subdues my people under me.

**3** LORD, what is man, that You know him,  
or the son of man, that You think of him?

**4** Man is like a breath;  
his days are like a passing shadow.

**5** LORD, bend Your heavens and come down;  
touch the mountains, so that they smoke.

**6** Flash forth lightning and scatter them;  
send out Your arrows and rout them.

**7** Stretch out Your hands from on high;  
rescue me and deliver me from many waters,  
from the hand of foreigners,

**8** whose mouths speak falsehood,  
and whose right hand is a right hand of lies.

**9** God, I will sing a new song to You;  
on a ten-stringed harp I will sing praises to You,

**10** who gives victory to kings,  
who rescues David His servant from the deadly sword.

**11** Rescue me and deliver me  
from the hand of foreigners,  
whose mouths speak falsehood,  
and whose right hand is a right hand of lies.

**12** May our sons be like plants  
grown tall in their youth,  
our daughters like corner pillars  
carved for a palace.

**13** May our barns be full,  
supplying every kind of produce;  
may our flocks increase by thousands,  
by tens of thousands in our fields;

**14** may our cattle be heavy with young;  
may there be no breach in the walls, no going into exile,  
and no cry of distress in our streets.

**15** Blessed are the people for whom it is so!  
Blessed are the people whose God is the LORD!

## Notes

- **vv1–11** — much of it drawn from Psalm 18, and the English there is followed where the Hebrew is the same: "trains my hands for war" (18:34), "my fortress… my deliverer… my shield, in whom I take refuge" (18:2), "subdues… under me" (18:47), "bend… the heavens and come down" (18:9), "send out… arrows… rout them" (18:14), "from on high… from many waters" (18:16). Here they are prayers, where Psalm 18 told them as things done.
- **v2 "my loyal love"** — *chasdi*, God as the source of His loyal love to the king.
- **v2 "who subdues my people under me"** — some Hebrew manuscripts and the parallel 18:47 read "peoples."
- **v3 "what is man, that You know him"** — a variant of 8:4, with other verbs.
- **v4 "Man is like a breath"** — *hevel*, as in 39:5 and 62:9.
- **vv12–15** — the blessings of peace the king prays for his people: children, harvest, flocks, and safe walls.
- **v14 "may our cattle be heavy with young"** — or "may our chiefs be well established"; *alluphenu* can mean either.

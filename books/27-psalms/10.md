# Psalms 10

**1** Why, LORD, do You stand far away?  
Why do You hide Yourself in times of trouble?

**2** In arrogance the wicked hotly pursue the poor;  
let them be caught in the schemes they have devised.

**3** For the wicked boasts of the desires of his soul,  
and the greedy man curses and despises the LORD.

**4** The wicked, in the pride of his face, does not seek Him;  
all his thoughts are, "There is no God."

**5** His ways prosper at all times;  
Your judgments are on high, out of his sight;  
as for all his foes, he snorts at them.

**6** He says in his heart, "I will not be moved;  
through all generations I will not meet trouble."

**7** His mouth is full of cursing, deceit and oppression;  
under his tongue are trouble and iniquity.

**8** He sits in ambush in the villages;  
in hiding places he murders the innocent;  
his eyes watch in secret for the helpless.

**9** He lies in wait in secret like a lion in his thicket;  
he lies in wait to seize the poor;  
he seizes the poor when he draws him into his net.

**10** The helpless is crushed, he sinks down,  
and falls by his might.

**11** He says in his heart, "God has forgotten;  
He has hidden His face; He will never see it."

**12** Arise, LORD; God, lift up Your hand;  
do not forget the afflicted.

**13** Why does the wicked despise God  
and say in his heart, "You will not require it"?

**14** But You do see, for You look on trouble and grief,  
to take it into Your hand;  
the helpless commits himself to You;  
You have been the helper of the fatherless.

**15** Break the arm of the wicked and the evildoer;  
seek out his wickedness until You find none.

**16** The LORD is King forever and ever;  
the nations perish from His land.

**17** LORD, You hear the desire of the humble;  
You strengthen their heart; You incline Your ear

**18** to do justice to the fatherless and the oppressed,  
so that man, who is of the earth, may terrify no more.

## Notes

- **The psalm** — the second half of the acrostic begun in Psalm 9 (see the note there); it has no title.
- **v1 "Why, LORD, do You stand far away?"** — the same words as 9:9, *in times of trouble*, now turned to complaint: the stronghold seems absent.
- **v3 "the greedy man curses and despises the LORD"** — *botsea berekh*, **blesses**, the scribes' substitute for **curses** (as in Job 1:5). It can also be read *the greedy man blesses himself*.
- **v4 "There is no God"** — the practical atheist, who lives as if God did not see (14:1; 53:1).
- **v7 "His mouth is full of cursing"** — quoted by Paul in Romans 3:14.
- **vv8–10** — the wicked as a hunter and a lion lying in wait (17:12). **The helpless**, *chel'kah*, is a word found only in this psalm (vv8, 10, 14). In v10 the written text joins two words, *chel ka'im*, read as one, **the helpless**.
- **v13 "You will not require it"** — *lo tidrosh*, God will not call him to account; the answer comes in v15, *seek out*, the same verb.
- **v14 "the helper of the fatherless"** — *a father of the fatherless… is God in His holy habitation* (68:5; Deuteronomy 10:18).
- **v16 "The LORD is King forever and ever"** — *The LORD will reign forever and ever* (Exodus 15:18).
- **v18 "man, who is of the earth"** — *enosh min ha-arets*, frail man, made of earth, against God the King.

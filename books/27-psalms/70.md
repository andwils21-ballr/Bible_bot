# Psalms 70

*For the director. Of David, to bring to remembrance.*

**1** God, rescue me;  
LORD, hurry to help me.

**2** Let them be put to shame and confounded  
who seek my life;  
let them be turned back and disgraced  
who delight in my harm.

**3** Let them turn back because of their shame  
who say, "Aha, aha!"

**4** But let all who seek You  
rejoice and be glad in You;  
let those who love Your salvation  
say always, "Great is God!"

**5** As for me, I am poor and needy;  
God, hurry to me.  
You are my help and my deliverer;  
LORD, do not delay.

---

## Notes

- **The whole psalm** — nearly the same as 40:13–17, and the English there is followed. Where the Hebrew differs, so does the English: v1 has "God" and no "Be pleased"; v2 drops "together" and "to sweep it away"; v3 has "turn back" for "be appalled" and no "to me"; v4 has "God" for "the LORD"; v5 has "God, hurry to me" for "the Lord thinks of me," and "LORD" for "my God."
- **v3 "Aha, aha!"** — the gloating cry of enemies over someone's fall (35:21, 25; Ezekiel 25:3).
- **The title, "to bring to remembrance"** — as in Psalm 38; perhaps sung with the memorial offering (Leviticus 2:2).

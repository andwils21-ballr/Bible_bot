# Psalms 86

*A prayer of David.*

**1** Incline Your ear, LORD, and answer me,  
for I am poor and needy.

**2** Guard my life, for I am faithful;  
save Your servant who trusts in You —  
You are my God.

**3** Be gracious to me, Lord,  
for I call to You all day long.

**4** Gladden the soul of Your servant,  
for to You, Lord, I lift up my soul.

**5** For You, Lord, are good and forgiving,  
abounding in loyal love to all who call on You.

**6** Give ear, LORD, to my prayer;  
listen to my plea for grace.

**7** In the day of my trouble I call on You,  
for You answer me.

**8** There is none like You among the gods, Lord,  
and there are no works like Yours.

**9** All the nations You have made  
will come and bow down before You, Lord,  
and glorify Your name.

**10** For You are great and do wonders;  
You alone are God.

**11** Teach me Your way, LORD,  
so that I may walk in Your truth;  
unite my heart to fear Your name.

**12** I will give thanks to You, Lord my God, with all my heart,  
and I will glorify Your name forever.

**13** For great is Your loyal love toward me;  
You have delivered my soul from the depths of Sheol.

**14** God, the arrogant have risen against me,  
and a band of ruthless men seeks my life;  
they have not set You before them.

**15** But You, Lord, are God merciful and gracious,  
slow to anger, and abundant in loyal love and truth.

**16** Turn to me and be gracious to me;  
give Your strength to Your servant,  
and save the son of Your maidservant.

**17** Show me a sign of Your goodness,  
so that those who hate me may see it and be put to shame,  
because You, LORD, have helped me and comforted me.

---

## Notes

- **v9 "All the nations You have made will come and bow down before You"** — quoted in the song of the Lamb (Revelation 15:4).
- **v15 "God merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abundant in loyal love and truth"** — the LORD's words about Himself at Sinai (Exodus 34:6), and the English there is followed.

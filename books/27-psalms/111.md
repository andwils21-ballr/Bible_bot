# Psalms 111

**1** Praise Yah!  
I will give thanks to the LORD with all my heart,  
in the council of the upright and in the congregation.

**2** Great are the works of the LORD,  
studied by all who delight in them.

**3** Splendor and majesty are His work,  
and His righteousness endures forever.

**4** He has made His wonders to be remembered;  
the LORD is gracious and merciful.

**5** He gives food to those who fear Him;  
He remembers His covenant forever.

**6** He has shown His people the power of His works,  
giving them the inheritance of the nations.

**7** The works of His hands are truth and justice;  
all His precepts are trustworthy.

**8** They stand firm forever and ever,  
done in truth and uprightness.

**9** He sent redemption to His people;  
He has commanded His covenant forever.  
Holy and awesome is His name.

**10** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom;  
all who practice it have good understanding.  
His praise endures forever.

---

## Notes

- **The form** — an acrostic: after "Praise Yah!", each of the twenty-two half-lines begins with the next letter of the Hebrew alphabet. Psalm 112 has the same form and is its twin.
- **v4 "He has made His wonders to be remembered"** — through the feasts, above all the Passover (Exodus 12:14; 13:9).
- **v5 "He gives food"** — *teref*, literally "prey," food torn off; here the manna and quail of the wilderness.
- **v10 "The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom"** — as in Proverbs 9:10 and Job 28:28; Proverbs 1:7 has "knowledge."

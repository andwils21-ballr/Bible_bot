# Psalms 71

**1** In You, LORD, I take refuge;  
let me never be put to shame.

**2** In Your righteousness rescue me and deliver me;  
incline Your ear to me and save me.

**3** Be a rock of refuge for me, where I can always come;  
You have given the command to save me,  
for You are my rock and my fortress.

**4** My God, deliver me from the hand of the wicked,  
from the grasp of the unjust and cruel.

**5** For You are my hope, Lord GOD,  
my trust from my youth.

**6** I have leaned on You from birth;  
You are the one who took me from my mother's womb.  
My praise is always of You.

**7** I have been a portent to many,  
but You are my strong refuge.

**8** My mouth is filled with Your praise,  
with Your glory all day long.

**9** Do not cast me off in the time of old age;  
when my strength fails, do not forsake me.

**10** For my enemies speak against me,  
and those who watch for my life consult together,

**11** saying, "God has forsaken him;  
pursue and seize him,  
for there is no one to rescue him."

**12** God, do not be far from me;  
my God, hurry to help me.

**13** Let my accusers be put to shame and consumed;  
let those who seek my harm  
be covered with reproach and disgrace.

**14** But I will hope continually  
and will praise You yet more and more.

**15** My mouth will tell of Your righteousness,  
of Your salvation all day long,  
though I do not know their number.

**16** I will come with the mighty deeds of the Lord GOD;  
I will make mention of Your righteousness, Yours alone.

**17** God, You have taught me from my youth,  
and to this day I declare Your wonders.

**18** So even to old age and gray hair,  
God, do not forsake me,  
until I declare Your arm to this generation,  
Your might to all who are to come.

**19** Your righteousness, God, reaches the heights,  
You who have done great things.  
God, who is like You?

**20** You who have shown me many troubles and distresses  
will revive me again;  
from the depths of the earth  
You will bring me up again.

**21** You will increase my greatness  
and turn and comfort me.

**22** I will also praise You with the harp  
for Your faithfulness, my God;  
I will sing praises to You with the lyre,  
Holy One of Israel.

**23** My lips will shout for joy when I sing praises to You,  
and my soul, which You have redeemed.

**24** My tongue also will talk of Your righteousness all day long,  
for they have been put to shame, for they have been disgraced,  
who seek my harm.

## Notes

- **vv1–3** — the opening of Psalm 31, and the English there is followed. Where the Hebrew differs, so does the English: here "in Your righteousness" opens v2, and v3 has *ma'on*, "a dwelling," with "where I can always come," for 31:2's "a strong fortress."
- **v3 "You have given the command to save me"** — the Hebrew is followed; 31:2 has "a strong fortress to save me" at this point.
- **v6 "the one who took me from my mother's womb"** — *gozi* occurs only here; "my support" is another reading. Compare 22:9–10.
- **v7 "a portent to many"** — *mofet*, a sign; his long suffering is a warning or a wonder to those who watch.
- **v12 "hurry to help me"** — the line echoes 22:11, 19 and 70:1.
- **v20 "You who have shown me… will revive me… bring me up"** — the written forms say "us" twice ("shown us," "revive us"); the read forms, "me," are followed.
- **v22 "Holy One of Israel"** — a title of God found three times in the Psalms (here, 78:41, 89:18) and throughout Isaiah.
- **v24 "they have been put to shame"** — the prayer of v13 is answered; the verbs are past.

# Psalms 3

*A psalm of David, when he fled from Absalom his son.*

**1** LORD, how many are my foes!  
Many are rising against me;

**2** many are saying of my soul,  
"There is no salvation for him in God." *Selah*

**3** But You, LORD, are a shield around me,  
my glory, and the one who lifts up my head.

**4** I cried aloud to the LORD,  
and He answered me from His holy mountain. *Selah*

**5** I lay down and slept;  
I woke again, for the LORD sustains me.

**6** I will not be afraid of the tens of thousands of people  
who have set themselves against me all around.

**7** Arise, LORD!  
Save me, my God!  
For You strike all my enemies on the cheek;  
You break the teeth of the wicked.

**8** Salvation belongs to the LORD;  
Your blessing be on Your people! *Selah*

---

## Notes

- **The title and the numbering** — the Hebrew counts the title as verse 1, so the Hebrew numbers are one ahead of the English through the psalm.
- **The title "when he fled from Absalom his son"** — the flight of David over the Mount of Olives (2 Samuel 15:13–30). It is the first psalm with a title, and the first linked to a moment in David's life.
- **v2 "Selah"** — a word of uncertain meaning, found seventy-one times in the Psalms and three times in Habakkuk 3; perhaps a direction to the singers or players, a pause or a lifting up. It is kept as it stands, in italics, where the Hebrew puts it.

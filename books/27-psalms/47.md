# Psalms 47

*For the director. Of the sons of Korah. A psalm.*

**1** Clap your hands, all you peoples;  
shout to God with the voice of joy.

**2** For the LORD, the Most High, is to be feared,  
a great King over all the earth.

**3** He subdued peoples under us,  
and nations under our feet.

**4** He chose our inheritance for us,  
the pride of Jacob, whom He loves. *Selah*

**5** God has gone up with a shout,  
the LORD with the sound of the horn.

**6** Sing praises to God, sing praises;  
sing praises to our King, sing praises.

**7** For God is the King of all the earth;  
sing praises with a Maskil.

**8** God reigns over the nations;  
God sits on His holy throne.

**9** The nobles of the peoples have gathered  
as the people of the God of Abraham.  
For the shields of the earth belong to God;  
He is highly exalted.

## Notes

- **The psalm** — a hymn to God as King of all the earth, perhaps sung as the ark was carried up to the temple (24:7–10; 2 Samuel 6:15).
- **v1 "Clap your hands, all you peoples"** — the clapping and shouting of a coronation (2 Kings 11:12).
- **v4 "the pride of Jacob"** — the land of promise, Israel's glory (Amos 6:8; Nahum 2:2).
- **v5 "God has gone up with a shout"** — *alah*, **gone up**, to His throne; **the horn**, *shofar*, sounded at the crowning of a king (1 Kings 1:39). The Church sings the psalm at the feast of the Ascension.
- **vv6–7 "Sing praises"** — *zammeru*, five times in two verses.
- **v7 "sing praises with a Maskil"** — *maskil*, the word of the psalm titles (32), here perhaps **a skillful song**.
- **v9 "as the people of the God of Abraham"** — the nations gathered as God's people, the promise to Abraham fulfilled: *in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed* (Genesis 12:3). **The shields of the earth** are its rulers (89:18).

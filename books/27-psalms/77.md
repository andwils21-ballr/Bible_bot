# Psalms 77

*For the director: for Jeduthun. Of Asaph. A psalm.*

**1** My voice goes up to God, and I cry out;  
my voice goes up to God, and He will hear me.

**2** In the day of my trouble I sought the Lord;  
in the night my hand was stretched out without tiring;  
my soul refused to be comforted.

**3** I remember God, and I moan;  
I meditate, and my spirit grows faint. *Selah*

**4** You hold my eyelids open;  
I am so troubled that I cannot speak.

**5** I consider the days of old,  
the years long past.

**6** I remember my song in the night;  
I meditate in my heart,  
and my spirit searches:

**7** "Will the Lord reject forever  
and never again show favor?

**8** Has His loyal love come to an end forever?  
Is His promise at an end for all generations?

**9** Has God forgotten to be gracious?  
Has He in anger shut up His compassion?" *Selah*

**10** Then I said, "This is my grief:  
that the right hand of the Most High has changed."

**11** I will remember the deeds of Yah;  
yes, I will remember Your wonders of old.

**12** I will ponder all Your work  
and meditate on Your mighty deeds.

**13** God, Your way is holy.  
What god is great like God?

**14** You are the God who does wonders;  
You have made known Your strength among the peoples.

**15** With Your arm You redeemed Your people,  
the children of Jacob and Joseph. *Selah*

**16** The waters saw You, God;  
the waters saw You and writhed;  
the deeps also trembled.

**17** The clouds poured out water;  
the skies gave out a voice;  
Your arrows flashed back and forth.

**18** The sound of Your thunder was in the whirlwind;  
lightning lit up the world;  
the earth trembled and shook.

**19** Your way was through the sea,  
Your path through the great waters,  
yet Your footprints were not seen.

**20** You led Your people like a flock  
by the hand of Moses and Aaron.

## Notes

- **v2 "my hand was stretched out without tiring"** — *niggerah*, poured out or stretched out, the hand raised in prayer all night; *velo tafug*, it did not grow numb.
- **v10 "that the right hand of the Most High has changed"** — *shenot* can be "the change of" or "the years of"; the other reading is "I will remember the years of the right hand of the Most High." Either way v10 is the turning point: he stops looking at his grief and looks at God's deeds.
- **v11 "I will remember"** — the read form; the written form has "I will make mention of."
- **v15 "the children of Jacob and Joseph"** — Joseph stands for the northern tribes (Ephraim and Manasseh), whom the Asaph psalms often name (78:67; 80:1–2).
- **vv16–19** — the crossing of the Red Sea (Exodus 14–15) told as a storm theophany, with thunder and lightning as at Sinai (Exodus 19:16).
- **v19 "yet Your footprints were not seen"** — God led them through, but the water closed behind Him; His presence is sure even where His steps cannot be traced.
- **v20 "by the hand of Moses and Aaron"** — the psalm ends abruptly on God's shepherding, the answer to the questions of vv7–9; Psalm 78 takes up the same story.

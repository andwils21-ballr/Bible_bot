# Psalms 68

*For the director. Of David. A psalm. A song.*

**1** Let God arise, let His enemies be scattered,  
and let those who hate Him flee before Him.

**2** As smoke is driven away, drive them away;  
as wax melts before fire,  
let the wicked perish before God.

**3** But let the righteous be glad;  
let them exult before God  
and rejoice with gladness.

**4** Sing to God, sing praises to His name;  
build up a highway for Him who rides through the deserts.  
Yah is His name; exult before Him.

**5** A father of the fatherless and a defender of widows  
is God in His holy dwelling.

**6** God gives the lonely a home to live in;  
He leads prisoners out into prosperity,  
but the rebellious live in a parched land.

**7** God, when You went out before Your people,  
when You marched through the wasteland, *Selah*

**8** the earth shook, the heavens poured  
before God, the One of Sinai,  
before God, the God of Israel.

**9** You sent down a generous rain, God;  
when Your inheritance was weary, You restored it.

**10** Your flock settled in it;  
in Your goodness You provided for the poor, God.

**11** The Lord gives the word;  
the women who bring the good news are a great army:

**12** "Kings of armies flee, they flee!"  
while she who stays at home divides the spoil.

**13** Though you lie down among the sheepfolds —  
the wings of a dove covered with silver,  
its feathers with shimmering gold.

**14** When the Almighty scattered kings there,  
it snowed on Zalmon.

**15** A mountain of God is Mount Bashan;  
a mountain of many peaks is Mount Bashan.

**16** Why do you look with envy, mountains of many peaks,  
at the mountain God has desired for His dwelling?  
The LORD will dwell there forever.

**17** The chariots of God are twice ten thousand,  
thousands upon thousands;  
the Lord is among them, Sinai in the holy place.

**18** You went up on high;  
You led captives away;  
You received gifts among people,  
even among the rebellious,  
so that the LORD God might dwell there.

**19** Blessed be the Lord;  
day after day He bears our load,  
God our salvation. *Selah*

**20** Our God is a God who saves,  
and to GOD the Lord belong the ways out of death.

**21** Surely God will crush the head of His enemies,  
the hairy crown of the one who goes on in his guilt.

**22** The Lord said, "I will bring them back from Bashan;  
I will bring them back from the depths of the sea,

**23** so that you may plunge your foot in blood,  
and the tongues of your dogs have their share of the enemies."

**24** Your processions are seen, God,  
the processions of my God, my King, into the holy place.

**25** The singers go in front, the musicians last,  
between them young women playing tambourines.

**26** Bless God in the great congregation;  
bless the LORD, you who come from the fountain of Israel.

**27** There is Benjamin, the youngest, leading them,  
the princes of Judah in their throng,  
the princes of Zebulun, the princes of Naphtali.

**28** Your God has commanded your strength;  
show Your strength, God, You who have acted for us.

**29** Because of Your temple at Jerusalem  
kings will bring You gifts.

**30** Rebuke the beast among the reeds,  
the herd of bulls with the calves of the peoples,  
trampling on pieces of silver;  
He has scattered the peoples who delight in war.

**31** Envoys will come from Egypt;  
Cush will run to stretch out its hands to God.

**32** Kingdoms of the earth, sing to God;  
sing praises to the Lord, *Selah*

**33** to Him who rides in the heavens, the ancient heavens;  
listen: He sends out His voice, a mighty voice.

**34** Ascribe strength to God,  
whose majesty is over Israel  
and whose strength is in the skies.

**35** God is awesome from Your sanctuaries;  
the God of Israel gives strength and power to the people.  
Blessed be God.

## Notes

- **v1 "Let God arise, let His enemies be scattered"** — the words Moses spoke whenever the ark set out (Numbers 10:35). The psalm follows God's march from Sinai through the wilderness to His dwelling in Zion.
- **v4 "build up a highway for Him who rides through the deserts"** — the verb (*sollu*) is used of casting up a road (Isaiah 57:14; 62:10), and the Greek reads "make a way." Some read "lift up a song." *Ba'aravot* is "the deserts" (as in Isaiah 40:3); a related phrase in older Canaanite poetry, "rider of the clouds," lies behind v33 and Deuteronomy 33:26.
- **v6 "into prosperity"** — the word (*bakosharot*) occurs only here; "with singing" is another reading.
- **vv7–8 "when You went out… the earth shook, the heavens poured… the One of Sinai"** — taken from the Song of Deborah (Judges 5:4–5), and the English there is followed.
- **vv11–13** — some of the hardest lines in the Psalms. The women who announce the victory (v11) are like Miriam (Exodus 15:20–21); v13 may be a rebuke to tribes who stayed among the sheepfolds (Judges 5:16) or a picture of the spoil. The Hebrew is followed line by line.
- **v15 "A mountain of God"** — Hebrew can use "of God" to mean "mighty," and "a mighty mountain" is a fair reading. Bashan's high peaks envy little Zion, the mountain God chose (v16).
- **v17 "Sinai in the holy place"** — the Hebrew is followed. Many read *ba mi-Sinai*, "the Lord came from Sinai into the holy place," by dividing the letters differently (compare Deuteronomy 33:2).
- **v18 "You went up on high… You received gifts among people"** — Ephesians 4:8 quotes this of Christ as "He gave gifts to people." The Aramaic Targum also reads "gave," and Paul follows that reading.
- **v30 "trampling on pieces of silver"** — the line is obscure; the Hebrew is followed. "The beast among the reeds" is likely Egypt (v31), named after the Nile's reeds.
- **v35 "from Your sanctuaries"** — the address turns to God mid-line, as the Hebrew does.

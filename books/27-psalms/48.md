# Psalms 48

*A song. A psalm of the sons of Korah.*

**1** Great is the LORD and greatly to be praised  
in the city of our God, His holy mountain.

**2** Beautiful in height, the joy of all the earth,  
is Mount Zion, the heights of the north,  
the city of the great King.

**3** In her citadels God  
has made Himself known as a stronghold.

**4** For the kings assembled;  
they came on together.

**5** They saw it, and they were astounded;  
they were terrified; they fled in haste.

**6** Trembling took hold of them there,  
anguish as of a woman in labor,

**7** as when the east wind  
shatters the ships of Tarshish.

**8** As we have heard, so we have seen  
in the city of the LORD of hosts, in the city of our God;  
God will establish her forever. *Selah*

**9** We have pondered Your loyal love, God,  
within Your temple.

**10** As Your name, God,  
so Your praise reaches to the ends of the earth;  
Your right hand is full of righteousness.

**11** Let Mount Zion be glad;  
let the daughters of Judah rejoice  
because of Your judgments.

**12** Walk around Zion, go all around her;  
count her towers;

**13** set your heart on her ramparts;  
go through her citadels,  
so that you may tell the generation to come

**14** that this is God,  
our God forever and ever;  
He will guide us beyond death.

---

## Notes

- **v2 "the heights of the north"** — *yarketei tsafon*, the far north, where the mountain of the gods was said to stand in the old Canaanite poems (Isaiah 14:13); here Zion is that mountain. **The city of the great King** is quoted by Jesus (Matthew 5:35).
- **v14 "He will guide us beyond death"** — *al mut*, **over death**, or **to the end**; some read it as a musical direction belonging to the next psalm, *al mut*, as in the title of Psalm 9. The Greek has *forever*.

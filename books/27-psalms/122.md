# Psalms 122

*A song of ascents. Of David.*

**1** I was glad when they said to me,  
"Let us go to the house of the LORD."

**2** Our feet are standing  
within your gates, Jerusalem.

**3** Jerusalem, built  
as a city bound firmly together,

**4** where the tribes go up, the tribes of Yah —  
as a testimony for Israel —  
to give thanks to the name of the LORD.

**5** For there thrones for judgment were set,  
the thrones of the house of David.

**6** Pray for the peace of Jerusalem:  
"May those who love you be secure.

**7** May there be peace within your walls,  
security within your towers."

**8** For the sake of my brothers and friends  
I will say, "Peace be within you."

**9** For the sake of the house of the LORD our God  
I will seek your good.

---

## Notes

- **vv6–8 "peace… secure… security"** — a play on the city's name: *sha'alu shelom Yerushalayim yishlayu… shalom… shalvah*. "Jerusalem" sounds like "city of peace" (Hebrews 7:2).

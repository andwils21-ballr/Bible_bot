# Psalms 66

*For the director. A song. A psalm.*

**1** Shout for joy to God, all the earth;

**2** sing the glory of His name;  
make His praise glorious.

**3** Say to God, "How fearsome are Your deeds!  
Because of the greatness of Your power, Your enemies come cringing to You.

**4** All the earth bows down to You  
and sings praises to You;  
they sing praises to Your name." *Selah*

**5** Come and see the works of God,  
fearsome in His deeds toward the children of man.

**6** He turned the sea into dry land;  
they passed through the river on foot;  
there we rejoiced in Him,

**7** who rules by His might forever,  
whose eyes keep watch over the nations;  
let not the rebellious exalt themselves. *Selah*

**8** Bless our God, you peoples,  
and let the sound of His praise be heard,

**9** who has set our soul among the living  
and has not let our feet slip.

**10** For You, God, have tested us;  
You have refined us as silver is refined.

**11** You brought us into the net;  
You laid a heavy burden on our loins;

**12** You let men ride over our heads;  
we went through fire and through water,  
but You brought us out to abundance.

**13** I will come into Your house with burnt offerings;  
I will pay You my vows,

**14** which my lips uttered  
and my mouth spoke when I was in trouble.

**15** I will offer You burnt offerings of fat animals,  
with the smoke of rams;  
I will offer bulls with goats. *Selah*

**16** Come and hear, all you who fear God,  
and I will tell what He has done for my soul.

**17** I cried to Him with my mouth,  
and high praise was under my tongue.

**18** If I had regarded iniquity in my heart,  
the Lord would not have heard.

**19** But truly God has heard;  
He has attended to the voice of my prayer.

**20** Blessed be God,  
who has not turned away my prayer  
or His loyal love from me.

## Notes

- **The title** — the short title shares verse 1 with the opening words, so the numbers of Hebrew and English agree.
- **v1 "all the earth"** — the call goes out to all nations (vv1–12), then narrows to the one worshiper (vv13–20).
- **v6 "He turned the sea into dry land; they passed through the river on foot"** — the crossing of the Red Sea and of the Jordan, the two great waters of the Exodus (Exodus 14:21–22; Joshua 3:14–17; 114:3).
- **v10 "You have refined us as silver is refined"** — testing as refining (12:6; Isaiah 48:10; 1 Peter 1:7).
- **v12 "through fire and through water"** — *When you pass through the waters, I will be with you… when you walk through fire you shall not be burned* (Isaiah 43:2). **Abundance** is *revayah*, a full cup (23:5).
- **vv13–15** — the vows made in trouble paid in costly sacrifices (Leviticus 22:18–21; Jonah 2:9).
- **v18 "If I had regarded iniquity in my heart, the Lord would not have heard"** — cherished sin blocks prayer (Isaiah 59:2; John 9:31).

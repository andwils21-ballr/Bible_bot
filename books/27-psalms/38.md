# Psalms 38

*A psalm of David, to bring to remembrance.*

**1** LORD, do not rebuke me in Your wrath,  
nor discipline me in Your fury.

**2** For Your arrows have sunk into me,  
and Your hand has come down on me.

**3** There is no soundness in my flesh because of Your indignation;  
there is no peace in my bones because of my sin.

**4** For my iniquities have gone over my head;  
like a heavy burden, they are too heavy for me.

**5** My wounds stink and fester  
because of my folly.

**6** I am bent and bowed down greatly;  
all the day I go about mourning.

**7** For my loins are filled with burning,  
and there is no soundness in my flesh.

**8** I am numb and greatly crushed;  
I groan because of the turmoil of my heart.

**9** Lord, all my longing is before You;  
my sighing is not hidden from You.

**10** My heart throbs; my strength has left me,  
and the light of my eyes, even that is gone from me.

**11** My friends and companions stand aloof from my affliction,  
and my neighbors stand far off.

**12** Those who seek my life lay snares;  
those who seek my harm speak of ruin  
and plot deceit all day long.

**13** But I am like a deaf man; I do not hear,  
like a mute who does not open his mouth.

**14** I have become like a man who does not hear,  
in whose mouth are no rebukes.

**15** For in You, LORD, I hope;  
You will answer, Lord my God.

**16** For I said, "Let them not rejoice over me,  
who make themselves great against me when my foot slips."

**17** For I am ready to fall,  
and my pain is always before me.

**18** I confess my iniquity;  
I am troubled because of my sin.

**19** My enemies are alive and strong,  
and many are those who hate me without reason.

**20** Those who repay evil for good  
accuse me because I follow what is good.

**21** Do not forsake me, LORD;  
my God, do not be far from me.

**22** Hurry to help me,  
Lord, my salvation.

---

## Notes

- **The psalm** — the third of the penitential psalms (see 6). It opens with the words of 6:1.

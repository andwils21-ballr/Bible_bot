# Psalms 82

*A psalm of Asaph.*

**1** God stands in the divine council;  
among the gods He passes judgment:

**2** "How long will you judge unjustly  
and show favor to the wicked? *Selah*

**3** Give justice to the weak and the fatherless;  
uphold the right of the afflicted and the destitute.

**4** Rescue the weak and the needy;  
deliver them from the hand of the wicked."

**5** They do not know, they do not understand;  
they walk about in darkness;  
all the foundations of the earth are shaken.

**6** I said, "You are gods,  
sons of the Most High, all of you;

**7** nevertheless, you will die like mortals  
and fall like any of the princes."

**8** Arise, God, judge the earth,  
for You will inherit all the nations.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "in the divine council"** — *ba'adat El*, "in the assembly of God." **Who the "gods" are** has been read two ways: heavenly powers set over the nations (Deuteronomy 32:8; Daniel 10:13, 20), or human judges who rule in God's name (Exodus 22:8–9, where *elohim* may mean judges). The psalm itself holds them to account for injustice on earth.
- **v6 "You are gods"** — Jesus quotes this in John 10:34–35: *If He called them gods to whom the word of God came… do you say of Him whom the Father sanctified, 'You are blaspheming'?*

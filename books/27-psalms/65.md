# Psalms 65

*For the director. A psalm of David. A song.*

**1** To You, God, silence is praise in Zion,  
and to You vows will be paid.

**2** You who hear prayer,  
to You all flesh will come.

**3** Iniquities prevail against me;  
our transgressions, You atone for them.

**4** Blessed is the one You choose and bring near  
to dwell in Your courts.  
We will be satisfied with the goodness of Your house,  
the holiness of Your temple.

**5** By fearsome deeds You answer us in righteousness,  
God of our salvation,  
the trust of all the ends of the earth  
and of the farthest seas,

**6** who by His strength set the mountains firm,  
being girded with might,

**7** who stills the roaring of the seas,  
the roaring of their waves,  
and the tumult of the peoples,

**8** so that those who live at the ends of the earth are in awe of Your signs;  
You make the gates of the morning and the evening shout for joy.

**9** You visit the earth and water it;  
You greatly enrich it;  
the river of God is full of water;  
You provide their grain,  
for so You have prepared it.

**10** You water its furrows abundantly,  
settling its ridges;  
You soften it with showers  
and bless its growth.

**11** You crown the year with Your goodness,  
and Your tracks drip with plenty.

**12** The pastures of the wilderness drip,  
and the hills are girded with joy;

**13** the meadows are clothed with flocks,  
and the valleys are covered with grain;  
they shout for joy; they also sing.

## Notes

- **v1 "To You, God, silence is praise"** — *lekha dumiyyah tehillah*: the silent awe of the worshiper is itself praise (62:1). The Greek reads *dommiyah*, **praise is fitting for You**.
- **v3 "You atone for them"** — *tekhapperem*, the word of the Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16:30): God Himself covers the sins.
- **v4 "Blessed is the one You choose and bring near"** — as the priests were chosen and brought near to serve (Numbers 16:5).
- **vv5–8** — God the Creator, who set the mountains and stilled the seas, the powers of chaos (89:9; Mark 4:39).
- **v8 "the gates of the morning and the evening"** — *motsa'ei boqer va-erev*, **the goings out** of morning and evening, the east and the west, where the sun rises and sets.
- **v9 "the river of God is full of water"** — the rain stored in heaven, God's channel that waters the earth (Job 38:25–27).
- **v11 "Your tracks drip with plenty"** — the paths of God's chariot through the fields as He passes, dripping with rain and fatness.
- **vv12–13** — the whole land at harvest, the hills, meadows and valleys singing together (96:12; Isaiah 55:12).

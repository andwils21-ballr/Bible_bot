# Psalms 108

*A song. A psalm of David.*

**1** My heart is steadfast, God;  
I will sing and make music, with all my glory.

**2** Wake up, harp and lyre!  
I will wake the dawn.

**3** I will give thanks to You, LORD, among the peoples;  
I will sing praises to You among the nations.

**4** For Your loyal love is great, above the heavens,  
and Your truth reaches to the clouds.

**5** Be exalted above the heavens, God;  
let Your glory be over all the earth.

**6** So that Your beloved ones may be rescued,  
save with Your right hand, and answer me.

**7** God has spoken in His holiness:  
"I will exult; I will divide Shechem  
and measure out the Valley of Succoth.

**8** Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine;  
Ephraim is the helmet of My head;  
Judah is My scepter.

**9** Moab is My washbasin;  
on Edom I throw My sandal;  
over Philistia I shout in triumph."

**10** Who will bring me to the fortified city?  
Who will lead me to Edom?

**11** Have You not rejected us, God?  
You do not go out, God, with our armies.

**12** Give us help against the foe,  
for the salvation of man is worthless.

**13** With God we will do valiantly,  
and He will trample our foes.

## Notes

- **The whole psalm** — joined from two earlier psalms: vv1–5 are 57:7–11 and vv6–13 are 60:5–12, and the English there is followed. Where the Hebrew differs, so does the English: v1 has "My heart is steadfast" once and adds "with all my glory"; v2 drops "Wake up, my glory!"; v3 has "LORD" for "Lord"; v4 has "above the heavens" for "reaching to the heavens"; v9 has "over Philistia I shout in triumph" for "Philistia, shout in triumph over Me!"
- **v1 "with all my glory"** — *af kevodi*, "even my glory," the self or soul (16:9; 30:12); 57:8 has it as "Wake up, my glory!"
- **v9 "over Philistia I shout in triumph"** — here God's victory cry over Philistia; in 60:8 Philistia is told to shout.
- **The pairing** — 57 began in fear and 60 in defeat; joined here, the praise comes first and the plea after.

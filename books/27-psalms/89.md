# Psalms 89

*A Maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite.*

**1** I will sing of the loyal love of the LORD forever;  
with my mouth I will make known Your faithfulness to all generations.

**2** For I said, "Loyal love will be built up forever;  
in the heavens You will establish Your faithfulness."

**3** "I have made a covenant with My chosen one;  
I have sworn to David My servant:

**4** 'I will establish your offspring forever  
and build up your throne for all generations.'" *Selah*

**5** Let the heavens praise Your wonders, LORD,  
Your faithfulness also in the assembly of the holy ones.

**6** For who in the skies can compare with the LORD?  
Who among the sons of God is like the LORD,

**7** a God greatly feared in the council of the holy ones,  
and awesome above all who are around Him?

**8** LORD God of hosts,  
who is mighty like You, Yah?  
Your faithfulness surrounds You.

**9** You rule the raging of the sea;  
when its waves rise, You still them.

**10** You crushed Rahab like one who is slain;  
You scattered Your enemies with Your mighty arm.

**11** The heavens are Yours; the earth also is Yours;  
the world and all that fills it — You founded them.

**12** The north and the south — You created them;  
Tabor and Hermon sing for joy at Your name.

**13** You have a mighty arm;  
strong is Your hand, high is Your right hand.

**14** Righteousness and justice are the foundation of Your throne;  
loyal love and truth go before You.

**15** Blessed are the people who know the festal shout,  
who walk, LORD, in the light of Your face.

**16** They rejoice in Your name all day long,  
and in Your righteousness they are lifted up.

**17** For You are the glory of their strength,  
and by Your favor our horn is lifted high.

**18** For our shield belongs to the LORD,  
our king to the Holy One of Israel.

**19** Once You spoke in a vision to Your faithful ones and said:  
"I have given help to a mighty one;  
I have raised up one chosen from the people.

**20** I have found David My servant;  
with My holy oil I have anointed him,

**21** so that My hand will stay with him;  
My arm also will strengthen him.

**22** The enemy will not get the better of him;  
the wicked will not afflict him.

**23** I will crush his foes before him  
and strike down those who hate him.

**24** My faithfulness and My loyal love will be with him,  
and in My name his horn will be lifted high.

**25** I will set his hand on the sea  
and his right hand on the rivers.

**26** He will call to Me, 'You are my Father,  
my God, and the rock of my salvation.'

**27** I will also make him the firstborn,  
the highest of the kings of the earth.

**28** I will keep My loyal love for him forever,  
and My covenant will stand firm for him.

**29** I will establish his offspring forever  
and his throne as the days of heaven.

**30** If his sons forsake My law  
and do not walk in My judgments,

**31** if they profane My statutes  
and do not keep My commandments,

**32** then I will punish their transgression with the rod  
and their guilt with blows,

**33** but I will not take My loyal love from him  
or be false to My faithfulness.

**34** I will not profane My covenant  
or change what has gone out from My lips.

**35** Once for all I have sworn by My holiness;  
I will not lie to David.

**36** His offspring will endure forever,  
and his throne like the sun before Me.

**37** Like the moon it will be established forever,  
a faithful witness in the skies." *Selah*

**38** But You have rejected and spurned;  
You are furious with Your anointed.

**39** You have renounced the covenant with Your servant;  
You have profaned his crown in the dust.

**40** You have broken down all his walls;  
You have laid his strongholds in ruins.

**41** All who pass along the road plunder him;  
he has become a reproach to his neighbors.

**42** You have raised the right hand of his foes;  
You have made all his enemies rejoice.

**43** You have turned back the edge of his sword  
and have not made him stand in battle.

**44** You have ended his splendor  
and hurled his throne to the ground.

**45** You have cut short the days of his youth;  
You have covered him with shame. *Selah*

**46** How long, LORD? Will You hide Yourself forever?  
Will Your wrath burn like fire?

**47** Remember how short my life is!  
For what futility You have created all the children of man!

**48** What man can live and not see death?  
Who can deliver his soul from the hand of Sheol? *Selah*

**49** Lord, where are Your loyal loves of old,  
which You swore to David in Your faithfulness?

**50** Remember, Lord, the reproach of Your servants,  
how I carry in my bosom the insults of all the many peoples,

**51** with which Your enemies have reproached, LORD,  
with which they have reproached the footsteps of Your anointed.

**52** Blessed be the LORD forever.  
Amen and Amen.

## Notes

- **The title, "Ethan the Ezrahite"** — with Heman (88), one of the wise men of 1 Kings 4:31.
- **vv3–4, 19–37** — God's covenant with David from 2 Samuel 7:8–16, retold in poetry; v4 and v29 echo *I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever* (2 Samuel 7:13). The king's pronouns stay lowercase; the New Testament sees the covenant kept in Christ, the Son of David (Luke 1:32–33; Acts 13:22–23).
- **vv6–7 "the sons of God… the council of the holy ones"** — the heavenly beings around God's throne (82:1; Job 1:6).
- **v10 "You crushed Rahab"** — the sea monster (87:4; Job 26:12), God's victory over chaos at creation and over Egypt at the Exodus.
- **v14 "Righteousness and justice are the foundation of Your throne"** — repeated at 97:2.
- **v19 "I have given help to a mighty one"** — some read *nezer*, "a crown," for *ezer*, "help," a difference of one letter; the Hebrew is followed.
- **v27 "the firstborn, the highest of the kings of the earth"** — the king given the place of the firstborn son (Exodus 4:22); Revelation 1:5 calls Jesus *the firstborn of the dead and the ruler of the kings of the earth*.
- **vv30–33** — the promise of 2 Samuel 7:14–15: discipline for the sons, but no end to God's loyal love.
- **vv38–51** — the lament: the line of David seems cast off, perhaps at the fall of Jerusalem (2 Kings 25:6–7). The covenant promise and the present ruin stand side by side, and the psalm prays from inside that tension.
- **v52** — the blessing that closes the third book of the Psalms, as 41:13 and 72:18–19 closed the first two.

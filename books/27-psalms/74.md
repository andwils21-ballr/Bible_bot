# Psalms 74

*A Maskil of Asaph.*

**1** Why, God, have You rejected us forever?  
Why does Your anger smoke against the sheep of Your pasture?

**2** Remember Your congregation, which You acquired long ago,  
the tribe of Your inheritance, which You redeemed —  
Mount Zion, where You have dwelt.

**3** Lift up Your steps to the everlasting ruins;  
the enemy has destroyed everything in the sanctuary.

**4** Your foes have roared inside Your meeting place;  
they set up their own signs for signs.

**5** They were like those who swing axes up high  
in a thicket of trees.

**6** Now all its carved work  
they smash with hatchet and hammers.

**7** They set Your sanctuary on fire;  
they defiled the dwelling place of Your name, bringing it to the ground.

**8** They said in their hearts, "We will crush them altogether";  
they burned all the meeting places of God in the land.

**9** We do not see our signs;  
there is no longer any prophet,  
and none among us knows how long.

**10** How long, God, will the foe scoff?  
Will the enemy revile Your name forever?

**11** Why do You hold back Your hand, Your right hand?  
Take it from Your bosom and destroy them.

**12** Yet God is my King from of old,  
working salvation on the earth.

**13** You divided the sea by Your strength;  
You broke the heads of the sea monsters on the waters.

**14** You crushed the heads of Leviathan;  
You gave him as food to the creatures of the desert.

**15** You split open spring and torrent;  
You dried up ever-flowing rivers.

**16** Yours is the day, Yours also the night;  
You established the light and the sun.

**17** You have fixed all the boundaries of the earth;  
summer and winter — You made them.

**18** Remember this: the enemy scoffs, LORD,  
and a foolish people reviles Your name.

**19** Do not hand over the life of Your dove to the wild beast;  
do not forget the life of Your poor forever.

**20** Look to the covenant,  
for the dark places of the land are full of the haunts of violence.

**21** Do not let the oppressed turn back in shame;  
let the poor and needy praise Your name.

**22** Arise, God, plead Your own cause;  
remember how the fool scoffs at You all day long.

**23** Do not forget the clamor of Your foes,  
the uproar of those who rise against You, which goes up continually.

## Notes

- **The setting** — the temple burned and its carving smashed (vv3–8) fits the Babylonian destruction of 587 BC (2 Kings 25:9); the psalm is a lament over it.
- **v4 "they set up their own signs for signs"** — military standards or pagan emblems where God's signs had been; compare v9, "We do not see our signs."
- **vv5–6** — the Hebrew is hard; the picture is of woodcutters hacking at a forest, turned on the temple's carved paneling (1 Kings 6:29). "Now" follows the read form; the written form has "and the time."
- **v8 "all the meeting places of God"** — *mo'adei El*; which places are meant is not known — perhaps local places of prayer and teaching.
- **v11 "from Your bosom"** — the read form *cheqekha*; the written form has *chuqqekha*, "Your statute."
- **vv13–14 "the sea monsters… Leviathan"** — God's victory over the sea at creation and at the Exodus, told in the pictures of the old sea dragon (Job 26:12–13; Isaiah 27:1; 51:9–10).
- **v14 "to the creatures of the desert"** — literally "to a people, to desert dwellers"; the creatures may be animals (Isaiah 13:21) or desert tribes.
- **v19 "the life of Your dove"** — Israel, defenseless; the Greek reads "a soul that confesses You," from a different division of the letters.

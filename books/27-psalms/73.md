# Psalms 73

*A psalm of Asaph.*

**1** Surely God is good to Israel,  
to those who are pure in heart.

**2** But as for me, my feet had almost stumbled;  
my steps had nearly slipped.

**3** For I envied the boastful  
when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.

**4** For they have no pangs until their death;  
their body is fat and sleek.

**5** They are not in trouble as others are;  
they are not struck like other people.

**6** So pride is their necklace;  
violence covers them like a garment.

**7** Their eyes bulge out from fatness;  
the imaginations of their heart overflow.

**8** They mock and speak with malice;  
from on high they threaten oppression.

**9** They set their mouth against the heavens,  
and their tongue struts through the earth.

**10** So his people turn back to them,  
and they drink up waters in abundance.

**11** They say, "How does God know?  
Is there knowledge in the Most High?"

**12** Such are the wicked:  
always at ease, they increase in wealth.

**13** Surely in vain I have kept my heart clean  
and washed my hands in innocence.

**14** For all day long I have been stricken,  
and rebuked every morning.

**15** If I had said, "I will speak like that,"  
I would have betrayed the generation of Your children.

**16** But when I thought how to understand this,  
it seemed a wearisome task to me,

**17** until I went into the sanctuary of God;  
then I understood their end.

**18** Surely You set them in slippery places;  
You make them fall to ruin.

**19** How they are made a desolation in a moment,  
swept away, utterly consumed by terrors!

**20** Like a dream when one wakes, Lord,  
when You rouse Yourself, You will despise their image.

**21** When my heart was embittered  
and I was pierced within,

**22** I was brutish and did not understand;  
I was like a beast before You.

**23** Yet I am always with You;  
You hold my right hand.

**24** You guide me with Your counsel,  
and afterward You will take me to glory.

**25** Whom have I in heaven but You?  
Besides You I desire nothing on earth.

**26** My flesh and my heart may fail,  
but God is the rock of my heart and my portion forever.

**27** For those who are far from You will perish;  
You destroy all who are unfaithful to You.

**28** But for me it is good to be near God;  
I have made the Lord GOD my refuge,  
to tell of all Your works.

## Notes

- **The title, "A psalm of Asaph"** — the third book of the Psalms begins here and opens with eleven psalms of Asaph (73–83); Psalm 50 is the only other. Asaph led David's singers (1 Chronicles 16:5).
- **v1 "to Israel, to those who are pure in heart"** — the Hebrew is followed. Some divide the letters as *layashar El*, "God is good to the upright."
- **v4 "they have no pangs until their death"** — or, dividing the word differently, "they have no pangs; their body is sound and fat."
- **v10 "So his people turn back to them"** — the read form; the written form has "he brings his people back." The verse is obscure, perhaps of people flocking to the wicked and drinking in their words.
- **v17 "until I went into the sanctuary of God"** — the turning point; in God's presence he sees the end of the wicked (v18–20), not only their present ease.
- **v20 "when You rouse Yourself"** — *ba'ir*, as from sleep; it could also mean "in the city."
- **v21 "I was pierced within"** — literally **in my kidneys**, the seat of the deepest feelings.
- **v24 "afterward You will take me to glory"** — *laqach*, the verb used of Enoch (Genesis 5:24) and Elijah (2 Kings 2:3) and in 49:15; "receive me with honor" is another reading.
- **v26 "the rock of my heart and my portion"** — the portion of the Levites, who had no land because the LORD was their inheritance (Numbers 18:20).

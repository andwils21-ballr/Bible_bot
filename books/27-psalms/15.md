# Psalms 15

*A psalm of David.*

**1** LORD, who shall live as a guest in Your tent?  
Who shall dwell on Your holy mountain?

**2** The one who walks blamelessly and does what is right  
and speaks truth in his heart;

**3** who does not slander with his tongue,  
does no evil to his neighbor,  
nor takes up a reproach against his friend;

**4** in whose eyes a vile person is despised,  
but who honors those who fear the LORD;  
who swears to his own hurt and does not change;

**5** who does not lend his money at interest,  
nor take a bribe against the innocent.  
The one who does these things will never be moved.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "who shall live as a guest in Your tent?"** — *yagur*, from *gur*, as the word table has it: even the righteous are guests in God's house, received by His welcome (5:4; 39:12). The question was asked at the gate of the temple, and the answer is given in ten marks of the righteous (compare 24:3–6; Isaiah 33:14–16).
- **v2 "walks blamelessly"** — *tamim*, whole, of one piece, the word for Noah and Job (Genesis 6:9; Job 1:1).
- **v2 "speaks truth in his heart"** — his inner words and his outer words agree, the opposite of the *double heart* of 12:2.
- **v4 "who swears to his own hurt and does not change"** — he keeps his oath even when it costs him (Leviticus 5:4; Judges 11:35).
- **v5 "who does not lend his money at interest"** — *neshekh*, **bite**, interest charged to a fellow Israelite in need, forbidden by the law (Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 25:36–37; Deuteronomy 23:19).
- **v5 "nor take a bribe against the innocent"** — *You shall not take a bribe* (Exodus 23:8; Deuteronomy 16:19).
- **v5 "will never be moved"** — against the wicked man who boasts *I will not be moved* (10:6): only the righteous truly stands.

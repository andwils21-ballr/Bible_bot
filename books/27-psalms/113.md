# Psalms 113

**1** Praise Yah!  
Praise, servants of the LORD;  
praise the name of the LORD.

**2** Blessed be the name of the LORD  
from this time forth and forever.

**3** From the rising of the sun to its setting,  
the name of the LORD is to be praised.

**4** The LORD is high above all nations,  
His glory above the heavens.

**5** Who is like the LORD our God,  
who is enthroned on high,

**6** who stoops down to look  
on the heavens and the earth?

**7** He raises the poor from the dust;  
He lifts the needy from the ash heap,

**8** to seat them with nobles,  
with the nobles of His people.

**9** He gives the barren woman a home,  
a joyful mother of children.  
Praise Yah!

## Notes

- **v1 and Psalms 113–118** — the Hallel, "Praise," sung at the great feasts and at the Passover meal; the "hymn" Jesus and the disciples sang before going out to the Mount of Olives was likely its second part (Matthew 26:30).
- **v6 "who stoops down to look"** — so high that He must stoop even to see the heavens, yet He looks on the lowly.
- **vv7–8** — the words of Hannah's song (1 Samuel 2:8), and the English there is followed, with "with the nobles of His people" for "and make them inherit a seat of honor."
- **v9 "He gives the barren woman a home"** — Sarah, Rachel and Hannah (Genesis 21:1–7; 1 Samuel 2:5); Mary's song picks up the same theme (Luke 1:52–53).

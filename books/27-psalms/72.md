# Psalms 72

*Of Solomon.*

**1** God, give the king Your judgments,  
and Your righteousness to the king's son.

**2** May he judge Your people with righteousness,  
and Your poor with justice.

**3** May the mountains bring peace to the people,  
and the hills, in righteousness.

**4** May he defend the poor of the people,  
save the children of the needy,  
and crush the oppressor.

**5** May they fear You while the sun endures,  
and as long as the moon, through all generations.

**6** May he come down like rain on the mown grass,  
like showers that water the earth.

**7** In his days may the righteous flourish,  
and peace abound till the moon is no more.

**8** May he rule from sea to sea,  
and from the River to the ends of the earth.

**9** May the desert tribes bow down before him,  
and his enemies lick the dust.

**10** May the kings of Tarshish and of the coastlands bring tribute;  
may the kings of Sheba and Seba offer gifts.

**11** May all kings bow down to him,  
all nations serve him.

**12** For he rescues the needy who cry for help,  
the poor and the one who has no helper.

**13** He has pity on the weak and the needy  
and saves the lives of the needy.

**14** From oppression and violence he redeems their life,  
and their blood is precious in his sight.

**15** Long may he live;  
may gold of Sheba be given to him;  
may prayer be made for him continually,  
and blessings called down on him all day long.

**16** May there be abundance of grain in the land,  
on the tops of the mountains;  
may its fruit rustle like Lebanon,  
and may people flourish in the cities  
like the grass of the earth.

**17** May his name endure forever,  
his name continue as long as the sun;  
may people be blessed in him;  
may all nations call him blessed.

**18** Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel,  
who alone does wonders.

**19** Blessed be His glorious name forever;  
may the whole earth be filled with His glory.  
Amen and Amen.

**20** The prayers of David son of Jesse are ended.

## Notes

- **The title, "Of Solomon"** — for or by Solomon; the psalm prays for the king and his son, and Solomon's reign of peace, wisdom and wealth from Sheba (1 Kings 3–4; 10) colors it.
- **v1 "the king… the king's son"** — the pronouns for the king stay lowercase. Jewish and Christian readers alike took the psalm of the Messiah, whose rule has no end (Isaiah 11:1–5).
- **v5 "May they fear You"** — the Hebrew is followed; the Greek reads "May he endure," with a change of one letter.
- **v8 "from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth"** — the River is the Euphrates, the edge of the promised land (Exodus 23:31); Zechariah 9:10 takes up the line for the coming king.
- **v10 "Tarshish… Sheba and Seba"** — the far west and the far south; Isaiah 60:6 and Matthew 2:11 echo the gifts of the nations.
- **v17 "may people be blessed in him"** — the promise to Abraham (Genesis 12:3; 22:18) fulfilled in the king.
- **vv18–19** — the blessing that closes the second book of the Psalms, as 41:13 closed the first.
- **v20 "The prayers of David son of Jesse are ended"** — the close of an earlier collection; some psalms of David still follow (86, 101, 103 and others).

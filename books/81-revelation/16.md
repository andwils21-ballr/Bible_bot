# Revelation 16

## The First Five Bowls

**1** Then I heard a loud voice from the temple saying to the seven angels, "Go and pour out the seven bowls of the wrath of God on the earth."

**2** The first went and poured out his bowl on the earth, and foul and painful sores broke out on the people who had the mark of the beast and worshiped its image.

**3** The second poured out his bowl into the sea, and it became blood like that of a dead man, and every living thing in the sea died.

**4** The third poured out his bowl into the rivers and the springs of water, and they became blood.

**5** I heard the angel of the waters say,

"You are just, You who are and who were, the Holy One,  
because You have judged these things;

**6** for they poured out the blood of saints and prophets,  
and You have given them blood to drink.  
They deserve it."

**7** I heard the altar say,

"Yes, Lord God Almighty,  
true and just are Your judgments."

**8** The fourth poured out his bowl on the sun, and it was allowed to scorch people with fire.

**9** People were scorched by the great heat, and they blasphemed the name of God, who has authority over these plagues; and they did not repent and give Him glory.

**10** The fifth poured out his bowl on the throne of the beast, and its kingdom was plunged into darkness. People gnawed their tongues in anguish,

**11** and they blasphemed the God of heaven for their pains and their sores, and they did not repent of their works.

## Armageddon

**12** The sixth poured out his bowl on the great river Euphrates, and its water was dried up, to prepare the way for the kings from the rising of the sun.

**13** Then I saw, coming out of the mouth of the dragon and out of the mouth of the beast and out of the mouth of the false prophet, three unclean spirits like frogs.

**14** They are spirits of demons, performing signs, who go out to the kings of the whole world, to gather them for the battle of the great day of God the Almighty.

**15** "I am coming like a thief. Blessed is the one who stays awake and keeps his garments, so that he will not walk about naked and be seen in his shame."

**16** They gathered them to the place that in Hebrew is called Armageddon.

## The Seventh Bowl

**17** The seventh poured out his bowl into the air, and a loud voice came out of the temple, from the throne, saying, "It is done!"

**18** There were lightnings and voices and thunders, and a great earthquake, such as there had never been since people have been on the earth, so great was that earthquake.

**19** The great city was split into three parts, and the cities of the nations fell. God remembered Babylon the great, to give her the cup of the wine of the fury of His wrath.

**20** Every island fled away, and the mountains were not found.

**21** Great hailstones, each as heavy as a talent, fell from heaven on the people, and the people blasphemed God for the plague of the hail, because that plague was exceedingly great.

## Notes

- **vv2–21 sores, blood, darkness, frogs, hail** — the plagues of Egypt a last time: *boils, blisters breaking out on man and on beast* (Exodus 9:10), the Nile *turned to blood* (Exodus 7:20), *thick darkness* (Exodus 10:22), *the frog came up and covered the land of Egypt* (Exodus 8:6), and the hail (Exodus 9:24). As with Pharaoh, those struck do not repent (vv9, 11).
- **v6 "They deserve it"** — *axioi eisin*, *they are worthy*, the word sung of the One on the throne and of the Lamb, *You are worthy* (4:11; 5:9).
- **v15 "I am coming like a thief"** — Jesus speaks into the middle of the vision, in the words of the letter to Sardis (3:3), with its white garments (3:4–5, 18).
- **v16 "Armageddon"** — read as Hebrew, *Har Megiddo*, the mountain of Megiddo. At Megiddo the kings of Canaan fought (Judges 5:19), King Josiah was killed (2 Kings 23:29), and Zechariah saw a great mourning, *as great as the mourning for Hadad-rimmon in the plain of Megiddo* (Zechariah 12:11), in the chapter of *whom they have pierced* (Zechariah 12:10; Revelation 1:7).

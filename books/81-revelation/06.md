# Revelation 6

## The Four Horses

**1** I watched when the Lamb opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures say with a voice like thunder, "Come!"

**2** I looked, and there was a white horse, and its rider had a bow; a crown was given to him, and he went out overcoming, and to overcome.

**3** When He opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature say, "Come!"

**4** Another horse came out, fiery red. Its rider was allowed to take peace from the earth, so that people would slaughter one another, and he was given a great sword.

**5** When He opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature say, "Come!" I looked, and there was a black horse, and its rider had a pair of scales in his hand.

**6** I heard what seemed to be a voice among the four living creatures, saying, "A quart of wheat for a denarius, and three quarts of barley for a denarius; but do not harm the oil and the wine."

**7** When He opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature say, "Come!"

**8** I looked, and there was a pale horse, and its rider's name was Death, and Hades followed with him. They were given authority over a fourth of the earth, to kill with sword and with famine and with pestilence and by the wild beasts of the earth.

## The Souls under the Altar

**9** When He opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those who had been slain for the word of God and for the testimony they held.

**10** They cried out with a loud voice, "How long, holy and true Master, before You judge and avenge our blood on those who live on the earth?"

**11** Each of them was given a white robe, and they were told to rest a little longer, until the number was complete of their fellow servants and their brothers who were to be killed as they had been.

## The Great Day of Wrath

**12** I watched when He opened the sixth seal, and there was a great earthquake; the sun became black as sackcloth made of hair, and the whole moon became like blood,

**13** and the stars of the sky fell to the earth, as a fig tree drops its unripe figs when shaken by a great wind.

**14** The sky split apart like a scroll being rolled up, and every mountain and island was moved from its place.

**15** The kings of the earth and the great ones and the commanders, the rich and the strong, and everyone, slave and free, hid themselves in the caves and among the rocks of the mountains,

**16** and they say to the mountains and to the rocks, "Fall on us, and hide us from the face of the One seated on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb;

**17** for the great day of their wrath has come, and who can stand?"

## Notes

- **v2 "he went out overcoming, and to overcome"** — *nikōn kai hina nikēsē*, the verb used of the Lamb, who *has overcome* (5:5), and of *the one who overcomes* in every letter. The rider on the white horse is not named. He has been read as Christ, who rides a white horse in 19:11, and as a conqueror, the first of four riders who bring war, famine and death. The text does not say which.
- **vv2–8 the horses, white, red, black and pale** — Zechariah's chariots: *red horses… black horses… white horses… dappled horses* (Zechariah 6:2–3).
- **v6 "A quart of wheat for a denarius"** — a *choinix*, about a quart, a day's bread for one person; a denarius was a day's pay (*a denarius for the day*, Matthew 20:2). A day's work buys a day's food, and no more.
- **v8 "its rider's name was Death… to kill with sword and with famine and with pestilence"** — *thanatos*, death, is the rider's name and also the word for *pestilence*, as in the Greek of Ezekiel's *four terrible judgments… sword and famine and wild beasts and pestilence* (Ezekiel 14:21).
- **v9 "the souls of those who had been slain"** — *esphagmenōn*, the word used of the Lamb, *as though slain* (5:6).
- **vv12–14 "the moon became like blood… a fig tree drops its unripe figs… like a scroll being rolled up"** — *the moon to blood, before the great and fearful day of the LORD* (Joel 2:31); *the skies roll up like a scroll… like a withered fig from the fig tree* (Isaiah 34:4).
- **v16 "Fall on us"** — the words of Hosea, *They will say to the mountains, "Cover us," and to the hills, "Fall on us"* (Hosea 10:8).

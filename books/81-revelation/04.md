# Revelation 4

## The One Seated on the Throne

**1** After this I looked, and a door stood open in heaven, and the first voice I had heard, speaking with me like a trumpet, said, "Come up here, and I will show you what must take place after this."

**2** At once I was in the Spirit, and a throne stood in heaven, and One was seated on the throne.

**3** The One seated there looked like jasper and carnelian, and around the throne was a rainbow that looked like emerald.

**4** Around the throne were twenty-four thrones, and on the thrones sat twenty-four elders, clothed in white garments, with golden crowns on their heads.

**5** From the throne came lightnings and voices and thunders, and before the throne seven torches of fire were burning, which are the seven spirits of God.

**6** Before the throne was something like a sea of glass, like crystal. In the middle of the throne and around the throne were four living creatures, full of eyes in front and behind.

**7** The first living creature was like a lion, the second living creature like an ox, the third living creature had a face like a man's, and the fourth living creature was like a flying eagle.

**8** The four living creatures, each of them with six wings, are full of eyes all around and within. Day and night they never rest, saying,

"Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty,  
who was and who is and who is to come."

**9** Whenever the living creatures give glory and honor and thanks to the One seated on the throne, who lives forever and ever,

**10** the twenty-four elders fall down before the One seated on the throne and worship Him who lives forever and ever, and they cast their crowns before the throne, saying,

**11** "You are worthy, our Lord and God,  
to receive the glory and the honor and the power,  
for You created all things,  
and by Your will they existed and were created."

## Notes

- **vv6–8 "four living creatures… like a lion… an ox… a man… a flying eagle… six wings… Holy, holy, holy"** — two visions of the throne in one. Ezekiel saw four living creatures with *the face of a human… a lion… an ox… an eagle*, and rims *full of eyes all around* (Ezekiel 1:10, 18); Isaiah saw seraphim, *each had six wings*, calling *Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of hosts* (Isaiah 6:2–3). Where Isaiah has *the LORD of hosts*, the creatures here say *the Lord God Almighty*, *Kyrios ho theos ho pantokratōr*, the Greek Old Testament's words for *the LORD, the God of hosts* (Amos 4:13).
- **v5 "lightnings and voices and thunders"** — the signs of Sinai, *thunders and lightnings, and a heavy cloud on the mountain* (Exodus 19:16). *Phōnai* is both *voices* and *sounds*; the book uses it of the trumpet in v1.
- **v8 "who was and who is and who is to come"** — the name of 1:4 and 1:8, but with *who was* first, now that John stands before the throne.

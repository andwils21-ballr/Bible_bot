# Revelation 17

## The Woman on the Scarlet Beast

**1** One of the seven angels who had the seven bowls came and spoke with me, saying, "Come, I will show you the judgment of the great prostitute who is seated on many waters,

**2** with whom the kings of the earth committed sexual immorality, and with the wine of whose immorality those who live on the earth have become drunk."

**3** He carried me away in the Spirit into a wilderness, and I saw a woman seated on a scarlet beast that was full of blasphemous names, with seven heads and ten horns.

**4** The woman was clothed in purple and scarlet, adorned with gold and precious stones and pearls, and she held in her hand a golden cup full of detestable things and the impurities of her immorality.

**5** On her forehead was written a name, a mystery: "Babylon the great, the mother of prostitutes and of the detestable things of the earth."

**6** I saw the woman drunk with the blood of the saints and with the blood of the witnesses to Jesus. When I saw her, I wondered greatly.

## The Mystery of the Woman and the Beast

**7** The angel said to me, "Why do you wonder? I will tell you the mystery of the woman, and of the beast that carries her, with the seven heads and the ten horns.

**8** The beast you saw was, and is not, and is about to come up out of the abyss and go to destruction. Those who live on the earth, whose names have not been written in the book of life from the foundation of the world, will be amazed when they see the beast, because it was, and is not, and will come.

**9** This calls for a mind with wisdom. The seven heads are seven mountains on which the woman is seated; they are also seven kings.

**10** Five have fallen, one is, and the other has not yet come; and when he comes, he must remain only a little while.

**11** The beast that was and is not is itself an eighth, and it belongs to the seven, and it goes to destruction.

**12** The ten horns you saw are ten kings who have not yet received a kingdom, but they receive authority as kings for one hour, together with the beast.

**13** These are of one mind, and they give their power and authority to the beast.

**14** They will make war on the Lamb, and the Lamb will overcome them, for He is Lord of lords and King of kings, and those with Him are called and chosen and faithful."

**15** He said to me, "The waters you saw, where the prostitute is seated, are peoples and multitudes and nations and languages.

**16** The ten horns you saw, and the beast, will hate the prostitute; they will make her desolate and naked, and they will eat her flesh and burn her up with fire.

**17** For God has put it into their hearts to carry out His purpose, by being of one mind and giving their kingdom to the beast, until the words of God are fulfilled.

**18** The woman you saw is the great city that rules over the kings of the earth."

## Notes

- **vv1–2, 4 "seated on many waters… a golden cup… drunk"** — Jeremiah's words against Babylon: *You who live by many waters* (Jeremiah 51:13), and *Babylon was a golden cup in the LORD's hand, making all the earth drunk; the nations drank of her wine* (Jeremiah 51:7). In v15 the angel says what the waters are.
- **v8 "was, and is not, and will come"** — the beast's name is made on the pattern of God's, *who was and who is and who is to come* (4:8), with *is not* in the middle. And it *goes to destruction*, *apōleia*, the root of *Apollyon* (9:11).
- **v14 "Lord of lords and King of kings"** — the title of God in Moses' words, *God of gods and Lord of lords, the great God* (Deuteronomy 10:17), given to the Lamb.

# Revelation 18

## The Fall of Babylon

**1** After this I saw another angel coming down from heaven, with great authority, and the earth was lit up by his glory.

**2** He cried out with a mighty voice,

"Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great!  
She has become a dwelling place of demons,  
a haunt of every unclean spirit,  
a haunt of every unclean bird,  
a haunt of every unclean and hated beast.

**3** For all the nations have fallen by the wine of the passion of her immorality,  
and the kings of the earth committed sexual immorality with her,  
and the merchants of the earth grew rich from the power of her luxury."

**4** Then I heard another voice from heaven saying,

"Come out of her, My people,  
so that you do not share in her sins,  
and do not receive her plagues;

**5** for her sins are heaped up to heaven,  
and God has remembered her crimes.

**6** Pay her back as she herself has paid,  
and give her back double for her deeds;  
in the cup she mixed, mix her a double portion.

**7** As much as she glorified herself and lived in luxury,  
so much torment and mourning give her.  
For in her heart she says,  
'I sit as a queen; I am no widow,  
and I will never see mourning.'

**8** That is why her plagues will come in a single day,  
death and mourning and famine,  
and she will be burned up with fire;  
for mighty is the Lord God who has judged her."

## The Laments

**9** The kings of the earth who committed sexual immorality with her and lived in luxury with her will weep and mourn over her when they see the smoke of her burning.

**10** They will stand far off, for fear of her torment, and say,

"Woe, woe, great city,  
Babylon, the mighty city!  
For in one hour your judgment has come."

**11** The merchants of the earth weep and mourn over her, because no one buys their cargo anymore:

**12** cargo of gold and silver, precious stones and pearls, fine linen and purple, silk and scarlet; all kinds of scented wood, all kinds of articles of ivory, all kinds of articles of costly wood and bronze and iron and marble;

**13** cinnamon and spice, incense and myrrh and frankincense, wine and oil, fine flour and wheat, cattle and sheep, horses and carriages, and bodies, and human souls.

**14** "The fruit your soul longed for has gone from you, and all your splendor and your glitter are lost to you, never to be found again."

**15** The merchants of these things, who grew rich from her, will stand far off, for fear of her torment, weeping and mourning,

**16** saying,

"Woe, woe, great city,  
clothed in fine linen and purple and scarlet,  
adorned with gold and precious stones and pearls!

**17** For in one hour such great wealth has been laid waste!" Every ship's captain, every passenger, and sailors, and all who work the sea, stood far off

**18** and cried out as they saw the smoke of her burning, "What city was like the great city?"

**19** They threw dust on their heads and cried out, weeping and mourning,

"Woe, woe, great city,  
where all who had ships at sea grew rich from her wealth!  
For in one hour she has been laid waste."

**20** Rejoice over her, heaven,  
and you saints and apostles and prophets,  
for God has given judgment for you against her.

## The Millstone

**21** Then a mighty angel picked up a stone like a great millstone and threw it into the sea, saying,

"So with violence will Babylon the great city be thrown down,  
and will never be found again.

**22** The sound of harpists and musicians, of flute players and trumpeters,  
will never be heard in you again;  
no craftsman of any craft will ever be found in you again;  
the sound of the millstone will never be heard in you again;

**23** the light of a lamp will never shine in you again;  
the voice of the bridegroom and the bride will never be heard in you again.  
For your merchants were the great ones of the earth,  
and all the nations were deceived by your sorcery.

**24** In her was found the blood of prophets and of saints,  
and of all who have been slain on the earth."

## Notes

- **v4 "Come out of her, My people"** — the word to the exiles in Babylon: *Go out from the middle of her, My people! Let each of you save his life from the fierce anger of the LORD!* (Jeremiah 51:45).
- **v7 "I sit as a queen; I am no widow, and I will never see mourning"** — Babylon's boast in Isaiah: *who says in her heart, "I am, and there is none besides me; I will not sit as a widow or know the loss of children"* (Isaiah 47:8).
- **vv9–19 the kings, the merchants and the sailors** — Ezekiel's lament over Tyre, the city of the sea trade, whose sailors *will throw dust on their heads* and say, *Who is like Tyre?* (Ezekiel 27:30, 32).
- **v13 "and bodies, and human souls"** — *sōmatōn, kai psychas anthrōpōn*. *Bodies* was the market's word for slaves, and the list of cargo ends with them and with *human souls*. Ezekiel's list of Tyre's trade has *human beings and vessels of bronze* (Ezekiel 27:13).
- **v21 "a stone like a great millstone… thrown into the sea"** — the end of Jeremiah's book against Babylon: *tie a stone to it and throw it into the middle of the Euphrates, and say, 'So shall Babylon sink and not rise'* (Jeremiah 51:63–64).
- **vv22–23 "the sound of the millstone… the light of a lamp… the voice of the bridegroom and the bride"** — Jeremiah's sentence on Judah, turned on Babylon: *the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the sound of the millstones and the light of the lamp* (Jeremiah 25:10).

# Revelation 12

## The Woman Clothed with the Sun

**1** A great sign appeared in heaven: a woman clothed with the sun, with the moon under her feet, and on her head a crown of twelve stars.

**2** She was pregnant, and she cries out in labor, in the pain of giving birth.

**3** Another sign appeared in heaven: a great red dragon, with seven heads and ten horns, and on his heads seven diadems.

**4** His tail sweeps away a third of the stars of heaven, and he threw them to the earth. The dragon stood before the woman who was about to give birth, so that when she gave birth he might devour her child.

**5** She gave birth to a son, a male child, who is to shepherd all the nations with a rod of iron; and her child was caught up to God and to His throne.

**6** The woman fled into the wilderness, where she has a place prepared by God, so that there she may be fed for twelve hundred and sixty days.

## War in Heaven

**7** Then war broke out in heaven: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon. The dragon and his angels fought back,

**8** but he was not strong enough, and there was no longer any place for them in heaven.

**9** The great dragon was thrown down, the ancient serpent, who is called the devil and Satan, the deceiver of the whole world; he was thrown down to the earth, and his angels were thrown down with him.

**10** Then I heard a loud voice in heaven, saying,

"Now have come the salvation and the power and the kingdom of our God, and the authority of His Christ,  
for the accuser of our brothers has been thrown down,  
who accuses them before our God day and night.

**11** They have overcome him by the blood of the Lamb  
and by the word of their testimony,  
for they did not love their lives even to death.

**12** Therefore rejoice, you heavens,  
and you who pitch your tents in them!  
Woe to the earth and the sea,  
for the devil has come down to you in great fury,  
because he knows that his time is short."

## The Dragon Pursues the Woman

**13** When the dragon saw that he had been thrown down to the earth, he pursued the woman who had given birth to the male child.

**14** The woman was given the two wings of the great eagle, so that she might fly into the wilderness, to her place, where she is fed for a time, and times, and half a time, away from the face of the serpent.

**15** The serpent poured water like a river out of his mouth after the woman, to sweep her away with the flood.

**16** But the earth helped the woman: the earth opened its mouth and swallowed the river that the dragon had poured from his mouth.

**17** The dragon was enraged with the woman, and went off to make war on the rest of her seed, those who keep the commandments of God and hold the testimony of Jesus.

## Notes

- **v1 "a woman clothed with the sun, with the moon under her feet, and… twelve stars"** — Joseph's dream: *the sun and the moon and eleven stars were bowing down to me* (Genesis 37:9), which his father read as *I and your mother and your brothers* (Genesis 37:10). The woman is not named.
- **v5 "a male child, who is to shepherd all the nations with a rod of iron"** — *Before her pain came upon her, she delivered a male child* (Isaiah 66:7), and the king of Psalm 2:9 as the Greek reads it, as at 2:27.
- **vv9, 17 "the ancient serpent… the rest of her seed"** — the serpent of the garden, and the word spoken to it: *I will put hostility between you and the woman, and between your seed and her seed* (Genesis 3:15).
- **v10 "the accuser of our brothers"** — *kategōr*, the one who brings a charge in court. In Hebrew *satan* means an accuser, the one who stands *at his right hand to accuse him* (Zechariah 3:1).
- **v14 "the two wings of the great eagle… a time, and times, and half a time"** — what God told Israel at Sinai, *I carried you on eagles' wings and brought you to Myself* (Exodus 19:4); and Daniel's span (Daniel 7:25), the twelve hundred and sixty days of v6 counted another way.
- **v16 "the earth opened its mouth and swallowed the river"** — as at Korah's rebellion: *The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them* (Numbers 16:32).

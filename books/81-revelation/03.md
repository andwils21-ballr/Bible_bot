# Revelation 3

## To Sardis

**1** "To the angel of the church in Sardis write: This is what He says, the One who has the seven spirits of God and the seven stars: I know your works. You have a name for being alive, but you are dead.

**2** Wake up, and strengthen what remains, which was about to die; for I have not found your works complete before My God.

**3** Remember, then, what you received and heard; keep it, and repent. If you do not wake up, I will come like a thief, and you will not know at what hour I will come upon you.

**4** Yet you have a few names in Sardis who have not soiled their garments, and they will walk with Me in white, for they are worthy.

**5** The one who overcomes will be clothed like them in white garments, and I will never blot his name out of the book of life; I will confess his name before My Father and before His angels.

**6** Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

## To Philadelphia

**7** To the angel of the church in Philadelphia write: This is what He says, the Holy One, the True One, who has the key of David, who opens and no one will shut, who shuts and no one opens:

**8** I know your works. I have set before you an open door, which no one is able to shut. You have little power, and yet you have kept My word and have not denied My name.

**9** I will make those of the synagogue of Satan, who say they are Jews and are not, but lie, I will make them come and bow down before your feet, and they will know that I have loved you.

**10** Because you have kept My word about endurance, I also will keep you from the hour of trial that is about to come on the whole world, to try those who live on the earth.

**11** I am coming quickly. Hold fast what you have, so that no one takes your crown.

**12** The one who overcomes, I will make him a pillar in the temple of My God, and he will never go out of it again. I will write on him the name of My God, and the name of the city of My God, the new Jerusalem, which comes down out of heaven from My God, and My own new name.

**13** Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

## To Laodicea

**14** To the angel of the church in Laodicea write: This is what He says, the Amen, the faithful and true witness, the beginning of God's creation:

**15** I know your works: you are neither cold nor hot. I wish you were cold or hot.

**16** So, because you are lukewarm, and neither hot nor cold, I am about to vomit you out of My mouth.

**17** For you say, 'I am rich; I have prospered, and I need nothing,' and you do not know that you are wretched and pitiable and poor and blind and naked.

**18** I counsel you to buy from Me gold refined by fire, so that you may be rich, and white garments to clothe yourself, so that the shame of your nakedness may not be seen, and salve to anoint your eyes, so that you may see.

**19** Those I love, I reprove and discipline. So be zealous, and repent.

**20** I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in to him and eat with him, and he with Me.

**21** The one who overcomes, I will give him a place to sit with Me on My throne, as I also overcame and sat down with My Father on His throne.

**22** Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches."

## Notes

- **v5 "I will never blot his name out of the book of life"** — Moses' plea at Sinai, *blot me out, please, from Your book that You have written*, and the LORD's answer, *Whoever has sinned against Me, him I will blot out of My book* (Exodus 32:32–33).
- **v7 "the key of David, who opens and no one will shut"** — the word of Isaiah about Eliakim, steward of the king's house: *I will set the key of the house of David on his shoulder; he will open, and no one will shut; he will shut, and no one will open* (Isaiah 22:22).
- **v9 "come and bow down before your feet"** — Isaiah's promise to Zion: *all who despised you will bow down at the soles of your feet* (Isaiah 60:14). Here it is turned to a church, and those who bow are *those who say they are Jews and are not*.
- **v10 "you have kept My word… I also will keep you"** — *tēreō* twice, the one keeping answered by the other.
- **v14 "the Amen, the faithful and true witness"** — Isaiah's *the God of truth* is in Hebrew *Elohei amen*, *the God of Amen* (Isaiah 65:16). Jesus takes the word as His name.
- **v14 "the beginning of God's creation"** — *hē archē tēs ktiseōs*. *Archē* is the beginning of a thing, its first part or its source, and also a ruler; Paul calls Christ *the beginning, the firstborn from the dead* (Colossians 1:18), and Wisdom says, *The LORD got me at the beginning of His way* (Proverbs 8:22). *Beginning* keeps the first two readings open.
- **v19 "Those I love, I reprove and discipline"** — Proverbs as the Greek Old Testament has it, *whom the Lord loves He reproves* (Proverbs 3:12), with the same verb, *elenchei*.

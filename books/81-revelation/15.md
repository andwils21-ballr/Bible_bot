# Revelation 15

## The Song by the Sea of Glass

**1** Then I saw another sign in heaven, great and wonderful: seven angels with seven plagues, the last, for with them the wrath of God is finished.

**2** I saw what looked like a sea of glass mixed with fire, and those who had overcome the beast and its image and the number of its name, standing beside the sea of glass, holding harps of God.

**3** They sing the song of Moses the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb, saying,

"Great and wonderful are Your works,  
Lord God Almighty;  
just and true are Your ways,  
King of the ages.

**4** Who will not fear, Lord,  
and glorify Your name?  
For You alone are holy.  
All the nations will come  
and worship before You,  
for Your righteous acts have been revealed."

## The Seven Bowls

**5** After this I looked, and the temple of the tent of the Testimony in heaven was opened,

**6** and out of the temple came the seven angels with the seven plagues, clothed in pure, bright linen, with golden sashes around their chests.

**7** One of the four living creatures gave the seven angels seven golden bowls full of the wrath of God, who lives forever and ever.

**8** The temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God and from His power, and no one could enter the temple until the seven plagues of the seven angels were finished.

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "standing beside the sea… the song of Moses the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb"** — Israel's song beside the sea after the crossing (Exodus 15), sung now by those who have passed through the beast. The words come from Moses' last song and the psalms: *His work is whole, for all His ways are justice* (Deuteronomy 32:4); *All the nations You have made will come and bow down before You, Lord, and glorify Your name* (Psalm 86:9).
- **vv3–4 "King of the ages. Who will not fear, Lord"** — Jeremiah's *Who would not fear You, King of the nations?* (Jeremiah 10:7). The edition reads *of the ages* and marks the word as a place where manuscripts differ.
- **vv5, 8 "the tent of the Testimony… no one could enter the temple"** — the name of the tent in the wilderness, *the dwelling, the tent of the Testimony* (Numbers 9:15), and its dedication: *Moses could not enter the tent of meeting, because the cloud had settled on it and the glory of the LORD filled the dwelling* (Exodus 40:35).

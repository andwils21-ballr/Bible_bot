# Revelation 8

## The Seventh Seal

**1** When He opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven for about half an hour.

**2** I saw the seven angels who stand before God, and seven trumpets were given to them.

**3** Another angel came and stood at the altar with a golden censer, and he was given much incense to offer with the prayers of all the saints on the golden altar before the throne.

**4** The smoke of the incense rose with the prayers of the saints from the angel's hand before God.

**5** Then the angel took the censer, filled it with fire from the altar, and threw it onto the earth; and there were thunders and voices and lightnings and an earthquake.

## The First Four Trumpets

**6** The seven angels who had the seven trumpets made ready to blow them.

**7** The first blew his trumpet, and there came hail and fire mixed with blood, and they were thrown onto the earth. A third of the earth was burned up, a third of the trees were burned up, and all the green grass was burned up.

**8** The second angel blew his trumpet, and something like a great mountain burning with fire was thrown into the sea. A third of the sea became blood,

**9** a third of the living creatures in the sea died, and a third of the ships were destroyed.

**10** The third angel blew his trumpet, and a great star fell from heaven, burning like a torch, and it fell on a third of the rivers and on the springs of water.

**11** The name of the star is Wormwood. A third of the waters became wormwood, and many people died from the waters, because they had been made bitter.

**12** The fourth angel blew his trumpet, and a third of the sun was struck, and a third of the moon, and a third of the stars, so that a third of them were darkened; a third of the day had no light, and so with the night.

**13** Then I looked, and I heard an eagle flying in midheaven, saying with a loud voice, "Woe, woe, woe to those who live on the earth, at the blasts of the other trumpets that the three angels are about to blow!"

## Notes

- **vv3–5 "the prayers of all the saints… filled it with fire from the altar, and threw it onto the earth"** — the prayers of the souls under the altar, *How long… before You judge* (6:10), go up with the incense, and the answer comes down as fire. Ezekiel saw the man in linen told to *fill your hands with coals of fire from between the cherubim, and scatter them over the city* (Ezekiel 10:2).
- **vv7–12 hail and fire, water to blood, darkness** — the plagues of Egypt again: *fire flashing in the middle of the hail* (Exodus 9:24), *all the water in the Nile turned to blood* (Exodus 7:20), *thick darkness in all the land of Egypt* (Exodus 10:22). Here each strikes a third.
- **v11 "Wormwood… many people died from the waters, because they had been made bitter"** — *apsinthos*, a bitter plant. Jeremiah's word against a people who had left the law: *I am about to feed this people with wormwood and give them poisoned water to drink* (Jeremiah 9:15).

# Revelation 1

## The Revelation of Jesus Christ

**1** The revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave Him to show His servants what must take place quickly. He made it known by sending it through His angel to His servant John,

**2** who testified to the word of God and to the testimony of Jesus Christ, all that he saw.

**3** Blessed is the one who reads aloud, and blessed are those who hear the words of this prophecy and keep what is written in it, for the time is near.

## To the Seven Churches

**4** John, to the seven churches in Asia: grace to you and peace from Him who is and who was and who is to come, and from the seven spirits who are before His throne,

**5** and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead and the ruler of the kings of the earth. To Him who loves us and has freed us from our sins by His blood,

**6** and has made us a kingdom, priests to His God and Father, to Him be the glory and the dominion forever and ever. Amen.

**7** He is coming with the clouds, and every eye will see Him, even those who pierced Him; and all the tribes of the earth will mourn over Him. Yes. Amen.

**8** "I am the Alpha and the Omega," says the Lord God, "who is and who was and who is to come, the Almighty."

## The Vision on Patmos

**9** I, John, your brother and partner in the affliction and the kingdom and the endurance that are in Jesus, was on the island called Patmos because of the word of God and the testimony of Jesus.

**10** I was in the Spirit on the Lord's day, and I heard behind me a loud voice like a trumpet,

**11** saying, "Write what you see in a book, and send it to the seven churches: to Ephesus, to Smyrna, to Pergamum, to Thyatira, to Sardis, to Philadelphia and to Laodicea."

**12** I turned to see the voice that was speaking with me, and when I turned I saw seven golden lampstands,

**13** and among the lampstands one like a son of man, clothed in a robe that reached to His feet, with a golden sash around His chest.

**14** His head and His hair were white like white wool, like snow, and His eyes were like a flame of fire.

**15** His feet were like fine bronze glowing as in a furnace, and His voice was like the sound of many waters.

**16** In His right hand He held seven stars, from His mouth came a sharp two-edged sword, and His face was like the sun shining in its strength.

**17** When I saw Him, I fell at His feet as though dead. He laid His right hand on me and said, "Do not be afraid. I am the First and the Last,

**18** and the Living One. I was dead, and I am alive forever and ever, and I hold the keys of death and of Hades.

**19** So write what you have seen, what is, and what is to take place after this.

**20** As for the mystery of the seven stars you saw in My right hand, and the seven golden lampstands: the seven stars are the angels of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are the seven churches."

## Notes

- **v1 "what must take place quickly"** — *en tachei*, which can mean *soon* or *swiftly*, once it begins; *quickly* carries both. The book's last chapter repeats the words (22:6). In v3 the time is *near*.
- **vv4, 8 "Him who is and who was and who is to come"** — after *apo*, *from*, Greek grammar calls for a different case, but John leaves the title as it stands, *ho ōn*, *the One who is*, as if God's name could not be bent to fit the sentence. *Ho ōn* is the name God gives at the bush in the Greek Old Testament: *Egō eimi ho ōn*, *I am the One who is* (Exodus 3:14).
- **v6 "a kingdom, priests to His God"** — what God told Israel at Sinai: *You will be for Me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation* (Exodus 19:6).
- **v7 "He is coming with the clouds… those who pierced Him… will mourn"** — two prophets in one line: *with the clouds of heaven there came one like a son of man* (Daniel 7:13), and *they will look to Me, whom they have pierced, and they will mourn for him* (Zechariah 12:10).
- **v10 "in the Spirit"** — *en pneumati*, with no article: God's Spirit taking hold of John, or John *in spirit*, carried out of his ordinary senses. English has to choose with the capital letter; the book uses the same words when the vision moves on (4:2; 17:3; 21:10).
- **vv13–14 "one like a son of man… His head and His hair were white like white wool, like snow"** — Daniel's words for the one who comes with the clouds (Daniel 7:13), and for the Ancient of Days on the throne: *His clothing was white as snow, and the hair of His head like pure wool* (Daniel 7:9). The one like a son of man has the features of the Ancient of Days.
- **v15 "fine bronze… the sound of many waters"** — *chalkolibanon*, a word found in the Greek Bible only in this book (here and 2:18); what metal it names is uncertain. The voice is the voice of God's glory in Ezekiel: *His voice was like the sound of many waters* (Ezekiel 43:2).
- **v17 "I am the First and the Last"** — what the LORD says of Himself in Isaiah: *I am the first and I am the last; besides Me there is no god* (Isaiah 44:6; 48:12).
- **v20 "the angels of the seven churches"** — *angeloi*, the word for God's heavenly messengers that also means a messenger sent by people. Each of the next seven letters is addressed *to the angel of the church*; whether these are heavenly beings or the churches' human messengers, the Greek does not say.

# Revelation 13

## The Beast from the Sea

**1** The dragon stood on the sand of the sea. Then I saw a beast coming up out of the sea, with ten horns and seven heads, and on its horns ten diadems, and on its heads blasphemous names.

**2** The beast I saw was like a leopard; its feet were like a bear's, and its mouth like a lion's mouth. The dragon gave it his power and his throne and great authority.

**3** One of its heads seemed to have been slain, to death, but its deadly wound was healed. The whole earth was amazed and followed the beast,

**4** and they worshiped the dragon, because he had given his authority to the beast, and they worshiped the beast, saying, "Who is like the beast, and who can make war against it?"

**5** The beast was given a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies, and it was given authority to act for forty-two months.

**6** It opened its mouth in blasphemies against God, to blaspheme His name and His tent, those who pitch their tents in heaven.

**7** It was allowed to make war on the saints and to overcome them, and it was given authority over every tribe and people and language and nation.

**8** All who live on the earth will worship it, everyone whose name has not been written in the book of life of the Lamb who was slain from the foundation of the world.

**9** If anyone has an ear, let him hear.

**10** If anyone is for captivity, into captivity he goes; if anyone is to be killed with the sword, with the sword he must be killed. Here is the endurance and the faith of the saints.

## The Beast from the Earth

**11** Then I saw another beast coming up out of the earth. It had two horns like a lamb, and it spoke like a dragon.

**12** It exercises all the authority of the first beast in its presence, and it makes the earth and those who live on it worship the first beast, whose deadly wound was healed.

**13** It performs great signs, even making fire come down from heaven to the earth in front of people.

**14** By the signs it was allowed to perform in the presence of the beast, it deceives those who live on the earth, telling them to make an image for the beast that had the wound of the sword and yet lived.

**15** It was allowed to give breath to the image of the beast, so that the image of the beast would even speak, and would have those who would not worship the image of the beast killed.

**16** It makes everyone, small and great, rich and poor, free and slave, to be given a mark on their right hand or on their forehead,

**17** so that no one can buy or sell unless he has the mark, the name of the beast or the number of its name.

**18** Here is wisdom: let the one who has understanding calculate the number of the beast, for it is the number of a man, and its number is six hundred and sixty-six.

## Notes

- **v1 "The dragon stood on the sand of the sea"** — the Greek editions number this sentence 12:18; the King James Version, whose numbering this follows, begins chapter 13 with it, and reads *I stood*. This Greek edition reads *he stood*, the dragon, and marks the word as a place where manuscripts differ.
- **vv1–2, 5 "ten horns… like a leopard… a bear's… a lion's mouth… a mouth speaking great things"** — Daniel's four beasts from the sea, *like a lion… like a bear… like a leopard*, and the fourth with *ten horns*, and the little horn with *a mouth speaking great things* (Daniel 7:4–8). Here the four are one beast.
- **v3 "seemed to have been slain, to death, but its deadly wound was healed"** — *hōs esphagmenēn*, the words used of the Lamb, *as though slain* (5:6). The beast has its own death and its own return to life.
- **v8 "the book of life of the Lamb who was slain from the foundation of the world"** — the Greek puts *from the foundation of the world* after *slain*: the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world, or names not written from the foundation of the world. In 17:8 the same phrase stands with the names alone, and the Lamb is not in the sentence. The English keeps the Greek order, and both readings.
- **v10 "If anyone is for captivity, into captivity he goes"** — Jeremiah's word to the people: *those who are for the sword, to the sword… and those who are for captivity, to captivity* (Jeremiah 15:2).
- **v18 "it is the number of a man… six hundred and sixty-six"** — *arithmos anthrōpou*: the number of one man, or a human number, a number counted the human way; the same construction in 21:17, *metron anthrōpou*, is a measure counted the human way. *Calculate*, *psēphisatō*, is to count with pebbles. The edition marks the number as a place where manuscripts differ.

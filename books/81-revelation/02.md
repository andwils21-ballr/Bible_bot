# Revelation 2

## To Ephesus

**1** "To the angel of the church in Ephesus write: This is what He says, the One who holds the seven stars in His right hand, who walks among the seven golden lampstands:

**2** I know your works, your toil and your endurance, and that you cannot bear evil people. You have tested those who call themselves apostles and are not, and found them false.

**3** You have endurance, and you have borne up for My name's sake, and have not grown weary.

**4** But I have this against you: you have left your first love.

**5** Remember, then, from where you have fallen; repent, and do the first works. If not, I will come to you and remove your lampstand from its place, unless you repent.

**6** Yet you have this: you hate the works of the Nicolaitans, which I also hate.

**7** Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who overcomes I will give to eat from the tree of life, which is in the paradise of God.

## To Smyrna

**8** To the angel of the church in Smyrna write: This is what He says, the First and the Last, who was dead and came to life:

**9** I know your affliction and your poverty (but you are rich), and the slander of those who say they are Jews and are not, but are a synagogue of Satan.

**10** Do not fear what you are about to suffer. The devil is about to throw some of you into prison, so that you may be tested, and you will have affliction for ten days. Be faithful to death, and I will give you the crown of life.

**11** Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches. The one who overcomes will not be harmed by the second death.

## To Pergamum

**12** To the angel of the church in Pergamum write: This is what He says, the One who has the sharp two-edged sword:

**13** I know where you live, where the throne of Satan is. You hold fast to My name, and you did not deny My faith, even in the days of Antipas, My witness, My faithful one, who was killed among you, where Satan lives.

**14** But I have a few things against you: you have there people who hold to the teaching of Balaam, who taught Balak to put a stumbling block before the sons of Israel, to eat food sacrificed to idols and to commit sexual immorality.

**15** So you too have some who hold to the teaching of the Nicolaitans in the same way.

**16** Repent, then. If not, I will come to you quickly and make war against them with the sword of My mouth.

**17** Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who overcomes I will give some of the hidden manna, and I will give him a white stone, and on the stone a new name written, which no one knows except the one who receives it.

## To Thyatira

**18** To the angel of the church in Thyatira write: This is what He says, the Son of God, who has eyes like a flame of fire, and whose feet are like fine bronze:

**19** I know your works, your love and faith and service and endurance, and that your last works are more than the first.

**20** But I have this against you: you tolerate the woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess, and she teaches and misleads My servants to commit sexual immorality and to eat food sacrificed to idols.

**21** I gave her time to repent, and she does not want to repent of her immorality.

**22** I am about to throw her onto a bed, and those who commit adultery with her into great affliction, unless they repent of her works.

**23** I will strike her children dead, and all the churches will know that I am He who searches minds and hearts, and I will give to each of you according to your works.

**24** But to the rest of you in Thyatira, all who do not hold this teaching and have not known what they call the deep things of Satan, I say: I lay on you no other burden;

**25** only hold fast what you have until I come.

**26** The one who overcomes and keeps My works until the end, I will give him authority over the nations,

**27** and he will shepherd them with a rod of iron, as clay pots are broken in pieces,

**28** as I also have received from My Father; and I will give him the morning star.

**29** Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

## Notes

- **vv1, 8, 12, 18 "This is what He says"** — *Tade legei*, the words the Greek Old Testament uses for *This is what the LORD says* (Exodus 4:22; Amos 1:6). Each letter opens with them, and each time the speaker is named by something seen in the vision of chapter 1: the stars and the lampstands (1:13, 16), *the First and the Last* (1:17), the sword (1:16), the eyes and the feet (1:14–15).
- **vv4–5 "your first love… the first works"** — *first* can mean first in time, the love they had at the beginning, or first in place, the love that comes before all else. The Greek allows both, and the English keeps both.
- **v7 "the tree of life, which is in the paradise of God"** — the tree in the middle of the garden (Genesis 2:9), whose way the cherubim were set to keep when the human was driven out (Genesis 3:24). *Paradise* is the Greek Old Testament's word for the garden of Eden.
- **vv7, 11, 17, 26 "the one who overcomes"** — *ho nikōn*, the word of John's first letter: *everyone who has been born of God overcomes the world* (1 John 5:4). Every letter ends with a promise to the one who overcomes.
- **v13 "Antipas, My witness, My faithful one"** — the title Jesus has in 1:5, *the faithful witness*, given to a man who died for it. *Martys*, witness, is the word that came to mean a martyr.
- **v14 "the teaching of Balaam, who taught Balak"** — Moses says the women of Midian, *on Balaam's word, led the sons of Israel into unfaithfulness to the LORD in the matter of Peor* (Numbers 31:16), when Israel ate the sacrifices of the gods of Moab (Numbers 25:1–2).
- **v23 "I am He who searches minds and hearts"** — the LORD's words in Jeremiah: *I the LORD search the heart and test the mind, to give to each man according to his ways* (Jeremiah 17:10). The Son of God says them of Himself. The Greek has *kidneys and hearts*, the inmost self.
- **v27 "he will shepherd them with a rod of iron"** — Psalm 2:9 as the Greek Old Testament reads it, *poimaneis*, *you will shepherd*, where the Hebrew reads *You will break them with a rod of iron*.

# 1 Chronicles 17

## Nathan's Word

**1** And it came to pass, when David lived in his house, that David said to Nathan the prophet, "See now, I live in a house of cedar, but the ark of the covenant of the LORD stays under curtains."

**2** Nathan said to David, "Do all that is in your heart, for God is with you."

**3** And it came to pass that same night that the word of God came to Nathan, saying,

**4** "Go and tell My servant David, 'Thus says the LORD: You shall not build Me the house to dwell in.

**5** For I have not lived in a house from the day I brought up Israel to this day, but I have gone from tent to tent and from dwelling to dwelling.

**6** Wherever I have moved about among all Israel, did I ever speak a word to any of the judges of Israel whom I commanded to shepherd My people, saying, "Why have you not built Me a house of cedar?"'

**7** Now then, say this to My servant David: 'Thus says the LORD of hosts: I took you from the pasture, from following the sheep, to be leader over My people Israel.

**8** I have been with you wherever you went and have cut off all your enemies before you. I will make you a name like the name of the great ones on the earth.

**9** I will appoint a place for My people Israel and will plant them, so that they may dwell in their own place and be disturbed no more. The wicked shall wear them down no more as at first,

**10** from the days I commanded judges over My people Israel. I will subdue all your enemies. I declare to you that the LORD will build you a house.

**11** And it shall come to pass, when your days are fulfilled to go to be with your fathers, that I will raise up your seed after you, one of your own sons, and I will establish his kingdom.

**12** He shall build Me a house, and I will establish his throne forever.

**13** I will be his father, and he shall be My son. I will not take My loyal love away from him, as I took it from him who was before you.

**14** I will set him over My house and over My kingdom forever, and his throne shall be established forever.'"

**15** In accordance with all these words and all this vision, so Nathan spoke to David.

## David's Prayer

**16** Then King David went in and sat before the LORD and said, "Who am I, O LORD God, and what is my house, that You have brought me this far?

**17** Yet this was too small in Your eyes, O God; You have spoken of Your servant's house for a long time to come, and You have regarded me as a man of high rank, O LORD God.

**18** What more can David say to You for the honor shown to Your servant? For You know Your servant.

**19** O LORD, for Your servant's sake and according to Your own heart, You have done all this greatness, to make known all these great things.

**20** O LORD, there is none like You, and there is no God besides You, according to all that we have heard with our ears.

**21** Who is like Your people Israel, the one nation on earth whom God went to redeem as a people for Himself, to make for Yourself a name by great and awesome things, by driving out nations before Your people whom You redeemed from Egypt?

**22** You made Your people Israel Your own people forever, and You, O LORD, became their God.

**23** So now, O LORD, let the word You have spoken concerning Your servant and concerning his house be made sure forever, and do as You have spoken.

**24** Let it be made sure, and let Your name be great forever, saying, 'The LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, is Israel's God,' and let the house of David Your servant be established before You.

**25** For You, my God, have revealed to Your servant that You will build him a house. That is why Your servant has found courage to pray before You.

**26** So now, O LORD, You are God, and You have promised this good thing to Your servant.

**27** So now, You have been pleased to bless the house of Your servant, that it may continue forever before You. For You, O LORD, have blessed it, and it is blessed forever."

## Notes

- **vv1–27 and 2 Samuel 7** — the same chapter, with changes small and large. Chronicles leaves out *the LORD had given him rest from all his enemies* (2 Samuel 7:1).
- **v4 "You shall not build Me the house"** — a statement; Samuel has a question, *Will you build Me a house to dwell in?* (2 Samuel 7:5). The Greek of Samuel agrees with Chronicles.
- **v5 "from tent to tent and from dwelling to dwelling"** — the Hebrew breaks off, *from tent to tent and from dwelling*; *to dwelling* is supplied from the sense. Samuel has *moving about in a tent for My dwelling* (2 Samuel 7:6).
- **v6 "any of the judges of Israel"** — *shoftei*, **judges**; Samuel has *shivtei*, **tribes** (2 Samuel 7:7). The two words differ in one letter, and *judges* is what v10 also has.
- **v13 "I will be his father, and he shall be My son"** — Samuel goes on, *When he does wrong, I will discipline him with the rod of men* (2 Samuel 7:14); Chronicles has no such words. Saul is not named either: *him who was before you*. Hebrews applies the promise to Jesus: *I will be a Father to Him, and He shall be a Son to Me* (Hebrews 1:5).
- **v14 "I will set him over My house and over My kingdom forever"** — Samuel has *your house and your kingdom* (2 Samuel 7:16). In Chronicles the house and the kingdom are the LORD's, and David's son is set over them.
- **vv16–17 "O LORD God… O God… O LORD God"** — *YHWH Elohim* and *Elohim*, where Samuel's prayer has *Adonai YHWH*, **Lord GOD**, throughout.
- **v17 "You have regarded me as a man of high rank"** — *u-re'itani ke-tor ha-adam ha-ma'alah*, **You have seen me according to the manner of a man of ascent**; the sense is uncertain. Samuel has *this is the instruction for mankind* (2 Samuel 7:19). The text keeps the Hebrew of each.
- **v25 "You, my God, have revealed to Your servant"** — *galita et ozen avdekha*, **You have uncovered the ear of Your servant**, the same words as *the LORD had revealed to Samuel* the day before Saul came (1 Samuel 9:15).
- **v27 "For You, O LORD, have blessed it, and it is blessed forever"** — Samuel ends with a request, *with Your blessing let the house of Your servant be blessed forever*; Chronicles ends with the blessing already given.

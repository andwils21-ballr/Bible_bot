# 1 Chronicles 25

## The Families of the Singers

**1** David and the commanders of the army set apart for the service some of the sons of Asaph, Heman and Jeduthun, who prophesied with lyres, harps and cymbals. The number of the men who did the work of their service was:

**2** Of the sons of Asaph: Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah and Asharelah, the sons of Asaph, under the direction of Asaph, who prophesied under the direction of the king.

**3** Of Jeduthun, the sons of Jeduthun: Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Hashabiah and Mattithiah, six, under the direction of their father Jeduthun, who prophesied with the lyre in giving thanks and praise to the LORD.

**4** Of Heman, the sons of Heman: Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir and Mahazioth.

**5** All these were sons of Heman, the king's seer in the words of God, to lift up his horn. God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.

**6** All these were under the direction of their father for the song in the house of the LORD, with cymbals, harps and lyres, for the service of the house of God, under the direction of the king: Asaph, Jeduthun and Heman.

**7** Their number, with their kinsmen who were trained in song to the LORD, all of them skilled, was two hundred and eighty-eight.

**8** They cast lots for their duties, small and great alike, teacher and student.

## Notes

- **v1 "the commanders of the army"** — *sarei ha-tsava*, the title of Shophach, Hadadezer's commander (19:18), and of Joab, *the commander of the king's army* (27:34); the Greek has *the rulers of the force*. *Tsava* is also the word for the Levites' term of duty, *all who enter the service* (Numbers 4:3), so the phrase can be read **the commanders of the service**, the heads of the Levites' work. The army reading is the word's usual sense here; the singers are set apart by the king with the leaders of the realm, as the ark was brought up by *David and the elders of Israel and the commanders of thousands* (15:25).
- **vv1–3 "who prophesied with lyres, harps and cymbals"** — *ha-nibbe'im*, **who prophesied**; the written form is *ha-nevi'im*, **the prophets**. The song of the house is called prophecy. Saul met *a band of prophets* coming down *with harp, tambourine, flute and lyre* (1 Samuel 10:5), and Elisha called for a musician: *When the musician played, the hand of the LORD came on him* (2 Kings 3:15).
- **v4 "Giddalti, Romamti-ezer… Mallothi, Hothir and Mahazioth"** — the last names of Heman's sons can be read as Hebrew words: *giddalti*, **I have made great**; *romamti ezer*, **I have raised up help**; *mallothi*, **I have spoken**; *hothir*, **he has given in plenty**; *machazi'oth*, **visions**. *Giddalti ve-romamti* is the pair in *I have reared and brought up sons* (Isaiah 1:2). Whether the names were given this way or the opening words of a hymn were read as names, the text does not say.
- **v5 "to lift up his horn"** — *le-harim qeren*, the horn as strength, as Hannah sings, *my horn is lifted high in the LORD* (1 Samuel 2:1). The fourteen sons and three daughters are how the promise was kept.
- **v8 "teacher and student"** — *mevin im talmid*. *Talmid*, **student**, is found only here in the Hebrew Bible. The Greek has *teleiōn kai manthanontōn*, **the accomplished and those learning**.
- **vv9–31 the twenty-four lots** — twenty-four courses of twelve, the two hundred and eighty-eight of v7, matching the twenty-four courses of priests (24:7–18). The first line gives no count for Joseph. Some names differ in form from vv2–4: *Izri* for *Zeri*, *Jesharelah* for *Asharelah*, *Azarel* for *Uzziel*, *Shubael* for *Shebuel*, *Jeremoth* for *Jerimoth*.

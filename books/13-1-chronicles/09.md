# 1 Chronicles 9

## The Returned Exiles

**1** So all Israel was enrolled by genealogy, and they are written in the book of the kings of Israel. Judah was carried into exile to Babylon because of their unfaithfulness.

**2** The first to live again in their holdings in their towns were Israel, the priests, the Levites and the temple servants.

**3** In Jerusalem lived some of the sons of Judah, of the sons of Benjamin, and of the sons of Ephraim and Manasseh:

**4** Uthai son of Ammihud, son of Omri, son of Imri, son of Bani, of the sons of Perez son of Judah.

**5** Of the Shilonites: Asaiah the firstborn and his sons.

**6** Of the sons of Zerah: Jeuel and their kinsmen, six hundred and ninety.

**7** Of the sons of Benjamin: Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Hodaviah, son of Hassenuah,

**8** Ibneiah son of Jeroham, Elah son of Uzzi, son of Michri, and Meshullam son of Shephatiah, son of Reuel, son of Ibnijah,

**9** and their kinsmen by their generations, nine hundred and fifty-six. All these men were heads of fathers' houses by their fathers' houses.

## The Priests

**10** Of the priests: Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, Jachin,

**11** and Azariah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, the leader of the house of God;

**12** and Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah, and Maasai son of Adiel, son of Jahzerah, son of Meshullam, son of Meshillemith, son of Immer,

**13** and their kinsmen, heads of their fathers' houses, a thousand seven hundred and sixty, men of worth for the work of the service of the house of God.

## The Levites

**14** Of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, of the sons of Merari;

**15** Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah son of Mica, son of Zichri, son of Asaph;

**16** Obadiah son of Shemaiah, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun; and Berechiah son of Asa, son of Elkanah, who lived in the villages of the Netophathites.

## The Gatekeepers

**17** The gatekeepers: Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman and their kinsmen; Shallum was the chief,

**18** stationed until now at the king's gate on the east. These were the gatekeepers of the camps of the sons of Levi.

**19** Shallum son of Kore, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah, and his kinsmen of his father's house, the Korahites, were over the work of the service, keepers of the thresholds of the tent, as their fathers had been over the camp of the LORD, keepers of the entrance.

**20** Phinehas son of Eleazar was the leader over them in time past; the LORD was with him.

**21** Zechariah son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**22** All these, chosen to be gatekeepers at the thresholds, were two hundred and twelve. They were enrolled by genealogy in their villages. David and Samuel the seer appointed them in their trust.

**23** So they and their sons were in charge of the gates of the house of the LORD, the house of the tent, as guards.

**24** The gatekeepers were on the four sides, east, west, north and south.

**25** Their kinsmen in their villages were to come in every seven days, from time to time, to be with them,

**26** for the four chief gatekeepers, who were Levites, were in an office of trust, and they were over the chambers and the treasuries of the house of God.

**27** They spent the night around the house of God, for the watch was theirs, and they were in charge of opening it morning by morning.

**28** Some of them were in charge of the vessels of the service, for they brought them in by count and took them out by count.

**29** Some of them were appointed over the furniture and over all the holy vessels, and over the fine flour, the wine, the oil, the frankincense and the spices.

**30** Some of the sons of the priests mixed the ointment of the spices.

**31** Mattithiah, one of the Levites, the firstborn of Shallum the Korahite, was in an office of trust over the making of the flat cakes.

**32** Some of their kinsmen of the sons of the Kohathites were over the rows of bread, to prepare them every Sabbath.

**33** These were the singers, heads of fathers' houses of the Levites, in the chambers, free from other duty, for they were on duty day and night.

**34** These were heads of fathers' houses of the Levites by their generations, chiefs. These lived in Jerusalem.

## The House of Saul Again

**35** In Gibeon lived the father of Gibeon, Jeiel, and the name of his wife was Maacah.

**36** His firstborn son was Abdon, then Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, Nadab,

**37** Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah and Mikloth.

**38** Mikloth fathered Shimeam. They also lived opposite their kinsmen in Jerusalem, with their kinsmen.

**39** Ner fathered Kish, Kish fathered Saul, and Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchi-shua, Abinadab and Eshbaal.

**40** The son of Jonathan was Merib-baal, and Meri-baal fathered Micah.

**41** The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech and Tahrea.

**42** Ahaz fathered Jarah, and Jarah fathered Alemeth, Azmaveth and Zimri. Zimri fathered Moza.

**43** Moza fathered Binea; Rephaiah was his son, Eleasah his son, Azel his son.

**44** Azel had six sons, and these are their names: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah and Hanan. These were the sons of Azel.

## Notes

- **v1 "they are written in the book of the kings of Israel. Judah was carried into exile"** — the Hebrew can also be divided *the book of the kings of Israel and Judah. They were carried into exile*; the Greek reads it so. The text follows the accents of the Hebrew. *Be-ma'alam*, **in their unfaithfulness**, the word of Achar, *who broke faith* (2:7).
- **vv2–34 and Nehemiah 11** — the same list, with many differences, stands in Nehemiah 11:3–19, as the people who settled in Jerusalem after the exile. There the chief priest is *Seraiah son of Hilkiah* (Nehemiah 11:11) where this list has *Azariah son of Hilkiah* (v11).
- **v2 "the temple servants"** — *ha-netinim*, **the given ones**. The Levites themselves were *wholly given* to Aaron (Numbers 3:9), and Joshua *gave* the Gibeonites to be woodcutters and water carriers *for the altar of the LORD* (Joshua 9:27).
- **v4 "son of Bani"** — written *Binyamin*, **Benjamin**, read *Bani*. The text follows the read form.
- **v20 "Phinehas son of Eleazar… the LORD was with him"** — the priest who was given *a covenant of lasting priesthood, because he was jealous for his God* (Numbers 25:13).
- **v22 "David and Samuel the seer appointed them"** — *ha-ro'eh*, **the seer**, the old title: *the one who is now called a prophet was formerly called a seer* (1 Samuel 9:9).
- **v32 "the rows of bread"** — *lechem ha-ma'arakhet*, **the bread of the row**, set *in two rows, six to a row, on the pure table before the LORD* and renewed *every sabbath day* (Leviticus 24:6–8).
- **v33 "free from other duty"** — written *petirim*, read *peturim*, the same word.
- **vv35–44 "In Gibeon lived the father of Gibeon, Jeiel"** — the list of 8:29–38 again, here as the bridge to Saul's death in chapter 10. This copy has *Jeiel* and *Ner* where 8:29–30 lacks them, and lacks *Ahaz* among Micah's sons (v41) where 8:35 has him. Written *Je'uel*, read *Je'iel* (v35).
- **v40 "Merib-baal, and Meri-baal"** — two spellings of the one name in one verse.

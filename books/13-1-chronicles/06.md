# 1 Chronicles 6

## The High Priests

**1** The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath and Merari.

**2** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**3** The children of Amram: Aaron, Moses and Miriam. The sons of Aaron: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**4** Eleazar fathered Phinehas, Phinehas fathered Abishua,

**5** Abishua fathered Bukki, Bukki fathered Uzzi,

**6** Uzzi fathered Zerahiah, Zerahiah fathered Meraioth,

**7** Meraioth fathered Amariah, Amariah fathered Ahitub,

**8** Ahitub fathered Zadok, Zadok fathered Ahimaaz,

**9** Ahimaaz fathered Azariah, Azariah fathered Johanan,

**10** and Johanan fathered Azariah — he is the one who served as priest in the house that Solomon built in Jerusalem.

**11** Azariah fathered Amariah, Amariah fathered Ahitub,

**12** Ahitub fathered Zadok, Zadok fathered Shallum,

**13** Shallum fathered Hilkiah, Hilkiah fathered Azariah,

**14** Azariah fathered Seraiah, and Seraiah fathered Jehozadak;

**15** and Jehozadak went into exile when the LORD carried Judah and Jerusalem into exile by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar.

## The Clans of Levi

**16** The sons of Levi: Gershom, Kohath and Merari.

**17** These are the names of the sons of Gershom: Libni and Shimei.

**18** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**19** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of the Levites by their fathers.

**20** Of Gershom: Libni his son, Jahath his son, Zimmah his son,

**21** Joah his son, Iddo his son, Zerah his son, Jeatherai his son.

**22** The sons of Kohath: Amminadab his son, Korah his son, Assir his son,

**23** Elkanah his son, Ebiasaph his son, Assir his son,

**24** Tahath his son, Uriel his son, Uzziah his son and Shaul his son.

**25** The sons of Elkanah: Amasai and Ahimoth,

**26** Elkanah his son; the sons of Elkanah: Zophai his son, Nahath his son,

**27** Eliab his son, Jeroham his son, Elkanah his son.

**28** The sons of Samuel: Joel the firstborn, and the second, Abijah.

**29** The sons of Merari: Mahli, Libni his son, Shimei his son, Uzzah his son,

**30** Shimea his son, Haggiah his son, Asaiah his son.

## The Singers

**31** These are the men whom David put in charge of the song in the house of the LORD, after the ark came to rest.

**32** They ministered with song before the dwelling of the tent of meeting until Solomon built the house of the LORD in Jerusalem, and they served in their office according to their rule.

**33** These are the men who served, with their sons. Of the sons of the Kohathites: Heman the singer, son of Joel, son of Samuel,

**34** son of Elkanah, son of Jeroham, son of Eliel, son of Toah,

**35** son of Zuph, son of Elkanah, son of Mahath, son of Amasai,

**36** son of Elkanah, son of Joel, son of Azariah, son of Zephaniah,

**37** son of Tahath, son of Assir, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah,

**38** son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, son of Israel.

**39** His kinsman Asaph stood at his right hand: Asaph son of Berechiah, son of Shimea,

**40** son of Michael, son of Baaseiah, son of Malchijah,

**41** son of Ethni, son of Zerah, son of Adaiah,

**42** son of Ethan, son of Zimmah, son of Shimei,

**43** son of Jahath, son of Gershom, son of Levi.

**44** On the left hand were their kinsmen the sons of Merari: Ethan son of Kishi, son of Abdi, son of Malluch,

**45** son of Hashabiah, son of Amaziah, son of Hilkiah,

**46** son of Amzi, son of Bani, son of Shemer,

**47** son of Mahli, son of Mushi, son of Merari, son of Levi.

**48** Their kinsmen the Levites were appointed for all the service of the dwelling of the house of God.

## Aaron and His Sons

**49** Aaron and his sons made offerings on the altar of burnt offering and on the altar of incense, for all the work of the most holy place, and to make atonement for Israel, according to all that Moses the servant of God had commanded.

**50** These are the sons of Aaron: Eleazar his son, Phinehas his son, Abishua his son,

**51** Bukki his son, Uzzi his son, Zerahiah his son,

**52** Meraioth his son, Amariah his son, Ahitub his son,

**53** Zadok his son, Ahimaaz his son.

## The Towns of the Levites

**54** These are their dwelling places by their encampments within their borders. To the sons of Aaron, of the clan of the Kohathites, for the lot fell to them,

**55** they gave Hebron in the land of Judah with its pasture lands around it.

**56** But the fields of the town and its villages they gave to Caleb son of Jephunneh.

**57** To the sons of Aaron they gave the towns of refuge: Hebron, Libnah with its pasture lands, Jattir, Eshtemoa with its pasture lands,

**58** Hilen with its pasture lands, Debir with its pasture lands,

**59** Ashan with its pasture lands and Beth-shemesh with its pasture lands;

**60** and from the tribe of Benjamin, Geba with its pasture lands, Alemeth with its pasture lands and Anathoth with its pasture lands. All their towns throughout their clans were thirteen towns.

**61** To the rest of the sons of Kohath were given by lot ten towns from the clan of the tribe, from the half-tribe, the half of Manasseh.

**62** To the sons of Gershom, by their clans, were given thirteen towns from the tribe of Issachar, the tribe of Asher, the tribe of Naphtali and the tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.

**63** To the sons of Merari, by their clans, were given by lot twelve towns from the tribe of Reuben, the tribe of Gad and the tribe of Zebulun.

**64** So the sons of Israel gave the Levites the towns with their pasture lands.

**65** They gave by lot, from the tribe of the sons of Judah, the tribe of the sons of Simeon and the tribe of the sons of Benjamin, these towns that are mentioned by name.

**66** Some of the clans of the sons of Kohath had towns of their territory from the tribe of Ephraim.

**67** They gave them the towns of refuge: Shechem with its pasture lands in the hill country of Ephraim, Gezer with its pasture lands,

**68** Jokmeam with its pasture lands, Beth-horon with its pasture lands,

**69** Aijalon with its pasture lands and Gath-rimmon with its pasture lands;

**70** and from the half-tribe of Manasseh, Aner with its pasture lands and Bileam with its pasture lands, for the clan of the rest of the sons of Kohath.

**71** To the sons of Gershom were given, from the clan of the half-tribe of Manasseh, Golan in Bashan with its pasture lands and Ashtaroth with its pasture lands;

**72** from the tribe of Issachar, Kedesh with its pasture lands, Daberath with its pasture lands,

**73** Ramoth with its pasture lands and Anem with its pasture lands;

**74** from the tribe of Asher, Mashal with its pasture lands, Abdon with its pasture lands,

**75** Hukok with its pasture lands and Rehob with its pasture lands;

**76** and from the tribe of Naphtali, Kedesh in Galilee with its pasture lands, Hammon with its pasture lands and Kiriathaim with its pasture lands.

**77** To the rest of the sons of Merari were given, from the tribe of Zebulun, Rimmono with its pasture lands and Tabor with its pasture lands;

**78** and beyond the Jordan at Jericho, on the east side of the Jordan, from the tribe of Reuben, Bezer in the wilderness with its pasture lands, Jahzah with its pasture lands,

**79** Kedemoth with its pasture lands and Mephaath with its pasture lands;

**80** and from the tribe of Gad, Ramoth in Gilead with its pasture lands, Mahanaim with its pasture lands,

**81** Heshbon with its pasture lands and Jazer with its pasture lands.

## Notes

- **vv1–81 in the Hebrew** — the Hebrew numbers vv1–15 as 5:27–41 and vv16–81 as 6:1–66.
- **vv1–15 "Eleazar fathered Phinehas… Jehozadak went into exile"** — the line of high priests from Aaron to the exile. Ezra traces himself through the same names, *Ezra son of Seraiah, son of Azariah, son of Hilkiah* (Ezra 7:1). In v10 the note *he is the one who served as priest in the house that Solomon built* stands against the second Azariah; Kings names *Azariah son of Zadok* as Solomon's priest (1 Kings 4:2).
- **vv1, 16 "Gershon… Gershom"** — the Hebrew spells Levi's son *Gershon* in v1 and *Gershom*, the name of Moses' son, from v16 on.
- **v28 "Joel the firstborn, and the second, Abijah"** — the Hebrew reads *ha-bekhor ve-sheni va-Aviyyah*, **the firstborn and the second and Abijah**, with no name for the firstborn; the scribes' vowels and the Greek read *ve-sheni* as a name, *Vashni*. This chapter itself names Samuel's son *Joel* (v33), and Samuel's book says *the name of his firstborn son was Joel, and the name of his second, Abijah* (1 Samuel 8:2). The text follows them.
- **vv22–28, 33–38 "Elkanah… Samuel"** — Samuel counted among the Levites, of Korah's line. Samuel's own book calls his father Elkanah *an Ephrathite* from the hill country of Ephraim (1 Samuel 1:1); Chronicles lists the family among the Levites.
- **vv31–32 "David put in charge of the song… after the ark came to rest"** — the singers' service begins when the ark comes to Jerusalem (chapters 15–16). *Heman*, *Asaph* and *Ethan* (vv33, 39, 44) are names from the headings of the Psalms: *Asaph* (Psalms 50, 73–83), *Heman the Ezrahite* (Psalm 88), *Ethan the Ezrahite* (Psalm 89).
- **v49 "to make atonement for Israel… Moses the servant of God"** — *le-khapper*, the priests' work set apart from the Levites' service in v48.
- **vv54–81 "the towns of the Levites"** — the list of Joshua 21, with many differences in names and some towns missing. Joshua names thirteen towns for Aaron's sons (Joshua 21:13–19); Chronicles counts *thirteen* (v60) but names eleven, without *Juttah* and *Gibeon*, and has *Ashan* for *Ain* and *Alemeth* for *Almon*.
- **v57 "the towns of refuge: Hebron, Libnah…"** — the Hebrew has the plural, *towns of refuge*, before a list in which only Hebron was one; Joshua has *Hebron, the town of refuge for the one who killed* (Joshua 21:13). The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **v61 "from the clan of the tribe, from the half-tribe, the half of Manasseh"** — Joshua says *from the clans of the tribe of Ephraim, from the tribe of Dan and from the half-tribe of Manasseh* (Joshua 21:5). The Hebrew of Chronicles lacks Ephraim and Dan, and the phrase breaks off; the text keeps it as it stands.

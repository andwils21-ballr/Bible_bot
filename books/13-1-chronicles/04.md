# 1 Chronicles 4

## Judah

**1** The sons of Judah: Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur and Shobal.

**2** Reaiah son of Shobal fathered Jahath, and Jahath fathered Ahumai and Lahad. These were the clans of the Zorathites.

**3** These were the sons of Etam: Jezreel, Ishma and Idbash, and the name of their sister was Hazzelelponi;

**4** and Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Ezer the father of Hushah. These were the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah, the father of Bethlehem.

**5** Ashhur the father of Tekoa had two wives, Helah and Naarah.

**6** Naarah bore him Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni and Haahashtari. These were the sons of Naarah.

**7** The sons of Helah: Zereth, Zohar and Ethnan.

**8** Koz fathered Anub and Zobebah, and the clans of Aharhel son of Harum.

## Jabez

**9** Jabez was more honored than his brothers. His mother called his name Jabez, saying, "Because I bore him in pain."

**10** Jabez called on the God of Israel, saying, "If You will indeed bless me and enlarge my border, and Your hand will be with me, and You will keep me from evil so that it does not pain me!" God granted what he asked.

## More Clans of Judah

**11** Chelub the brother of Shuhah fathered Mehir, who was the father of Eshton.

**12** Eshton fathered Beth-rapha, Paseah and Tehinnah the father of Ir-nahash. These were the men of Recah.

**13** The sons of Kenaz: Othniel and Seraiah. The son of Othniel: Hathath.

**14** Meonothai fathered Ophrah, and Seraiah fathered Joab the father of Ge-harashim, for they were craftsmen.

**15** The sons of Caleb son of Jephunneh: Iru, Elah and Naam. The son of Elah: Kenaz.

**16** The sons of Jehallelel: Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria and Asarel.

**17** The sons of Ezrah: Jether, Mered, Epher and Jalon. She conceived and bore Miriam, Shammai and Ishbah the father of Eshtemoa.

**18** His Judean wife bore Jered the father of Gedor, Heber the father of Soco, and Jekuthiel the father of Zanoah. These were the sons of Bithiah the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Mered married.

**19** The sons of the wife of Hodiah, the sister of Naham, were the fathers of Keilah the Garmite and Eshtemoa the Maacathite.

**20** The sons of Shimon: Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan and Tilon. The sons of Ishi: Zoheth and Ben-zoheth.

**21** The sons of Shelah son of Judah: Er the father of Lecah, Laadah the father of Mareshah, and the clans of the house of linen workers at Beth-ashbea;

**22** and Jokim, the men of Cozeba, Joash and Saraph, who ruled in Moab, and Jashubi-lehem. The records are ancient.

**23** These were the potters who lived at Netaim and Gederah; they lived there with the king in his work.

## Simeon

**24** The sons of Simeon: Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah and Shaul;

**25** Shallum his son, Mibsam his son, Mishma his son.

**26** The sons of Mishma: Hammuel his son, Zaccur his son, Shimei his son.

**27** Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters, but his brothers did not have many sons, and all their clan did not multiply like the sons of Judah.

**28** They lived in Beersheba, Moladah and Hazar-shual,

**29** in Bilhah, Ezem and Tolad,

**30** in Bethuel, Hormah and Ziklag,

**31** in Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri and Shaaraim. These were their cities until David reigned.

**32** Their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen and Ashan, five cities,

**33** with all their villages that were around these cities as far as Baal. These were their settlements, and they kept their own genealogy.

**34** Meshobab, Jamlech and Joshah son of Amaziah,

**35** Joel, and Jehu son of Joshibiah, son of Seraiah, son of Asiel,

**36** Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel and Benaiah,

**37** Ziza son of Shiphi, son of Allon, son of Jedaiah, son of Shimri, son of Shemaiah:

**38** these, mentioned by name, were leaders in their clans, and their fathers' houses increased greatly.

**39** They went to the entrance of Gerar, to the east side of the valley, to seek pasture for their flocks.

**40** There they found rich, good pasture, and the land was spacious, quiet and at peace, for those who had lived there before were of Ham.

**41** These, written down by name, came in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah, and struck down their tents and the Meunites who were found there, and devoted them to destruction to this day, and lived in their place, because there was pasture there for their flocks.

**42** Some of them, of the sons of Simeon, five hundred men, went to Mount Seir, with Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi, at their head.

**43** They struck down the remnant of the Amalekites who had escaped, and they have lived there to this day.

## Notes

- **v1 "The sons of Judah: Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur and Shobal"** — not five brothers but descendants: Hezron is Perez's son (2:5), Hur is Hezron's grandson through Caleb (2:18–19), and Shobal is Hur's son (2:50). *Sons* is used of descendants.
- **v3 "These were the sons of Etam"** — the Hebrew reads *ve-elleh avi Eitam*, **and these the father of Etam**, a plural with a singular that cannot both stand. The Greek has *these were the sons of Etam*. The text follows the Greek.
- **vv9–10 "Because I bore him in pain… so that it does not pain me"** — *Ya'bets* and *be-otsev*, **in pain**, the same letters in another order; the word of Eve's sentence, *in pain you shall bear children* (Genesis 3:16). Jabez asks that the pain in his name not come on him, *le-vilti otsbi*. The Greek spells the name *Igabes* and has *I bore him as a gabes*, and for the end of the prayer, *so as not to humble me*.
- **v10 "If You will indeed bless me"** — *im barekh tevarakheni*, an oath form with no second half: the prayer states its conditions and never says what Jabez would do. *God granted what he asked*.
- **v17 "She conceived and bore Miriam"** — the Hebrew names no mother here; the next verse names *Bithiah the daughter of Pharaoh*, whom Mered married, and the order of the two verses is uncertain. *Miriam* here is a man of Judah, not Moses' sister.
- **v18 "Bithiah the daughter of Pharaoh"** — *Bityah*, **daughter of the LORD**, the name of an Egyptian princess married into Judah. Nothing else is told of her.
- **v22 "who ruled in Moab… The records are ancient"** — *asher ba'alu le-Mo'av*, **who were masters for Moab**, or **who married into Moab**; *ba'al* is used for both. The Greek has *who lived in Moab*. *Ve-ha-devarim attiqim*, an old note on old sources.
- **vv24, 28–33 "The sons of Simeon… These were their cities until David reigned"** — the list of Joshua 19:2–8, where Simeon's inheritance lies *within the inheritance of the sons of Judah* (Joshua 19:1, 9). Genesis gives Simeon's sons as *Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar and Shaul* (Genesis 46:10); Numbers has *Nemuel* (Numbers 26:12).
- **v39 "the entrance of Gerar"** — the Hebrew has *Gedor*, a town of Judah (v4, v18); the Greek has *Gerar*. *Gedor* and *Gerar* differ in one letter, *d* for *r*, and v40 says the people living there were *of Ham*, as the border of the Canaanites ran *toward Gerar* (Genesis 10:19). The text follows the Greek.
- **vv41–43 "the Meunites… the remnant of the Amalekites who had escaped"** — written *ha-me'inim*, read *ha-me'unim*, **the Meunites**. The LORD had said, *I will utterly blot out the memory of Amalek from under the heavens* (Exodus 17:14); Saul and David fought them, and in Hezekiah's days the last of them are struck down.

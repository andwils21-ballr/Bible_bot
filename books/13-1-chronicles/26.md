# 1 Chronicles 26

## The Gatekeepers

**1** For the divisions of the gatekeepers: of the Korahites, Meshelemiah son of Kore, of the sons of Asaph.

**2** Meshelemiah had sons: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,

**3** Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, Eliehoenai the seventh.

**4** Obed-edom had sons: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sachar the fourth, Nethanel the fifth,

**5** Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, Peullethai the eighth; for God had blessed him.

**6** To Shemaiah his son also were sons born, who ruled over their father's house, for they were men of worth.

**7** The sons of Shemaiah: Othni, Rephael, Obed and Elzabad, whose brothers were able men, Elihu and Semachiah.

**8** All these were of the sons of Obed-edom, they and their sons and their brothers, able men with strength for the service: sixty-two of Obed-edom.

**9** Meshelemiah had sons and brothers, able men, eighteen.

**10** Hosah, of the sons of Merari, had sons: Shimri the chief, for though he was not the firstborn, his father made him chief;

**11** Hilkiah the second, Tebaliah the third, Zechariah the fourth. All the sons and brothers of Hosah were thirteen.

**12** These divisions of the gatekeepers, by their chief men, had duties alongside their kinsmen, to serve in the house of the LORD.

## The Lots for the Gates

**13** They cast lots, small and great alike, by their fathers' houses, for each gate.

**14** The lot for the east fell to Shelemiah. They cast lots also for Zechariah his son, a counselor with insight, and his lot came out for the north.

**15** For Obed-edom it was the south, and for his sons the storehouse.

**16** For Shuppim and Hosah it was the west, by the Shallecheth gate on the road that goes up, guard alongside guard.

**17** On the east were six Levites, on the north four a day, on the south four a day, and for the storehouse two and two.

**18** For the precinct on the west there were four at the road and two at the precinct.

**19** These were the divisions of the gatekeepers of the sons of the Korahites and of the sons of Merari.

## The Treasurers

**20** Their kinsmen the Levites were over the treasuries of the house of God and the treasuries of the dedicated gifts.

**21** The sons of Ladan, the sons of the Gershonites belonging to Ladan, the heads of the fathers' houses belonging to Ladan the Gershonite: Jehieli.

**22** The sons of Jehieli, Zetham and Joel his brother, were over the treasuries of the house of the LORD.

**23** Of the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites and the Uzzielites:

**24** Shebuel son of Gershom, son of Moses, was chief officer over the treasuries.

**25** His kinsmen through Eliezer: Rehabiah his son, Jeshaiah his son, Joram his son, Zichri his son and Shelomoth his son.

**26** This Shelomoth and his brothers were over all the treasuries of the dedicated gifts that David the king had dedicated, with the heads of the fathers' houses, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the commanders of the army.

**27** From the wars and from the spoil they dedicated gifts to strengthen the house of the LORD.

**28** All that Samuel the seer had dedicated, and Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner and Joab son of Zeruiah, everything dedicated, was in the care of Shelomoth and his brothers.

## Officials Outside the House

**29** Of the Izharites, Chenaniah and his sons were appointed to the outside work over Israel, as officers and judges.

**30** Of the Hebronites, Hashabiah and his brothers, able men, one thousand seven hundred, had oversight of Israel west of the Jordan, for all the work of the LORD and for the service of the king.

**31** Of the Hebronites, Jerijah was chief, of the Hebronites by their generations and fathers' houses. In the fortieth year of David's reign they were searched out, and men of worth were found among them at Jazer in Gilead.

**32** His brothers, able men, two thousand seven hundred heads of fathers' houses: David the king appointed them over the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of the Manassites, for every matter of God and every matter of the king.

## Notes

- **v1 "Meshelemiah son of Kore, of the sons of Asaph"** — not Asaph the singer, who was a Gershonite (6:39–43), but the Korahite line of *Shallum son of Kore, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah* (9:19). *Ebiasaph* is a son of Korah in Exodus 6:24; the Greek here has *Abia*. Meshelemiah is *Shelemiah* in v14.
- **vv4–5 "Obed-edom had sons… for God had blessed him"** — the man who kept the ark in his house for three months, when *the LORD blessed the household of Obed-edom and all that he had* (13:14). The eight sons are the blessing, counted out.
- **v10 "though he was not the firstborn, his father made him chief"** — as Jacob set Ephraim before Manasseh (Genesis 48:19), and as the LORD chose Solomon *from all my sons, for the LORD has given me many sons* (28:5).
- **v16 "For Shuppim and Hosah"** — *le-Shuppim*. The name comes right after *ha-asuppim*, **the storehouse**, at the end of v15, and the Greek has only *Hosah*. The word may have been copied twice; the text keeps the Hebrew.
- **v18 "the precinct"** — *parbar*, a word of uncertain meaning, found only here, and as *parvarim* in 2 Kings 23:11, *the chamber of Nathan-melech the official, which was in the precincts*. The Greek leaves it untranslated.
- **v20 "Their kinsmen the Levites"** — the Hebrew reads *ve-ha-Leviyyim Achiyyah*, **and the Levites, Ahijah**, a name found nowhere else in these lists, standing where no name is expected. The Greek has *hoi Leuitai adelphoi autōn*, **the Levites their kinsmen**, which in Hebrew is *acheihem*, one letter more than *Achiyyah*. The text follows the Greek.
- **v28 "Samuel the seer… Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner and Joab son of Zeruiah"** — the dedicated gifts reach back before David: the treasury of the house held what Saul and his commander had taken in war, beside David's own.
- **v31 "In the fortieth year of David's reign"** — the last year of his reign: he *reigned over Israel forty years* (29:27). The muster that set men over the land was among his last acts. *Jazer in Gilead* is the land that the sons of Reuben and Gad saw was *a place for livestock* (Numbers 32:1).

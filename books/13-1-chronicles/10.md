# 1 Chronicles 10

## Mount Gilboa

**1** The Philistines fought against Israel, and the men of Israel fled before the Philistines and fell slain on Mount Gilboa.

**2** The Philistines closed in on Saul and his sons, and the Philistines struck down Jonathan, Abinadab and Malchi-shua, the sons of Saul.

**3** The battle pressed hard against Saul. The archers found him, and he was wounded by the archers.

**4** Saul said to his armor-bearer, "Draw your sword and run me through with it, or these uncircumcised will come and abuse me." But his armor-bearer would not, for he was greatly afraid. So Saul took the sword and fell on it.

**5** When his armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he also fell on the sword and died.

**6** So Saul died, and his three sons, and all his house died together.

**7** When all the men of Israel who were in the valley saw that the army had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned their towns and fled, and the Philistines came and lived in them.

**8** And it came to pass the next day, when the Philistines came to strip the slain, that they found Saul and his sons fallen on Mount Gilboa.

**9** They stripped him and took his head and his armor, and sent them throughout the land of the Philistines to carry the news to their idols and to the people.

**10** They put his armor in the house of their gods and fastened his skull in the house of Dagon.

**11** When all Jabesh-gilead heard all that the Philistines had done to Saul,

**12** all the men of valor arose and took the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons and brought them to Jabesh. They buried their bones under the oak in Jabesh, and they fasted seven days.

## Why Saul Died

**13** So Saul died for his unfaithfulness with which he broke faith with the LORD, because of the word of the LORD that he did not keep, and also because he asked a ghost, to inquire of it,

**14** and did not inquire of the LORD. So He put him to death and turned the kingdom over to David son of Jesse.

## Notes

- **vv1–12 and 1 Samuel 31** — Chronicles begins its story with Saul's last battle, told almost word for word as in 1 Samuel 31. Chronicles tells no other story of Saul's reign.
- **v3 "he was wounded by the archers"** — *va-yachel*, **he was wounded**, or **he writhed**; Samuel has *va-yachel me'od*, **he was in great anguish** (1 Samuel 31:3).
- **v6 "all his house died together"** — Samuel has *his armor-bearer and all his men* (1 Samuel 31:6). Saul's son Ish-bosheth reigned after him for two years (2 Samuel 2:10); Chronicles does not mention him.
- **v10 "fastened his skull in the house of Dagon"** — Samuel has the armor in *the house of the Ashtaroth* and the body *on the wall of Beth-shan* (1 Samuel 31:10). Dagon is the god whose image fell before the ark (1 Samuel 5:3–4).
- **v12 "the bodies… under the oak"** — *gufat*, **body**, found only here; Samuel has *geviyyat* and *the tamarisk tree* (1 Samuel 31:12–13). Chronicles also leaves out the burning of the bodies.
- **vv13–14 "his unfaithfulness… the word of the LORD that he did not keep"** — *be-ma'alo asher ma'al*, the word of Achar's sin (2:7) and of Judah's exile (9:1). Samuel told Saul, *You have not kept the command of the LORD your God* (1 Samuel 13:13).
- **vv13–14 "he asked a ghost… and did not inquire of the LORD"** — *sho'el ba-ov*, the medium of En-dor (1 Samuel 28:7); *ov*, fixed as **ghost**. Samuel says that *Saul inquired of the LORD, but the LORD did not answer him* (1 Samuel 28:6). *Sho'el*, **asking**, is the verb of Saul's own name, *Sha'ul*, **asked**.
- **v14 "So He put him to death"** — Saul fell on his own sword (v4), and the same death is here the LORD's act.

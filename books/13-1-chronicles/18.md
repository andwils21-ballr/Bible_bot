# 1 Chronicles 18

## David's Victories

**1** And it came to pass after this that David struck the Philistines and subdued them, and he took Gath and its villages out of the hand of the Philistines.

**2** He struck Moab, and the Moabites became David's servants and brought tribute.

**3** David also struck Hadadezer king of Zobah, toward Hamath, as he went to set up his monument at the river Euphrates.

**4** David captured from him a thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen and twenty thousand foot soldiers. David hamstrung all the chariot horses, but left enough of them for a hundred chariots.

**5** When the Arameans of Damascus came to help Hadadezer king of Zobah, David struck twenty-two thousand men of the Arameans.

**6** Then David put garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became David's servants and brought tribute. The LORD gave David victory wherever he went.

**7** David took the shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadadezer and brought them to Jerusalem.

**8** From Tibhath and from Cun, towns of Hadadezer, David took a great quantity of bronze. With it Solomon made the bronze Sea, the pillars and the vessels of bronze.

**9** When Tou king of Hamath heard that David had struck the whole army of Hadadezer king of Zobah,

**10** he sent Hadoram his son to King David to greet him and to bless him, because he had fought against Hadadezer and struck him, for Hadadezer had been at war with Tou. He brought all kinds of articles of gold, of silver and of bronze.

**11** These also King David dedicated to the LORD, together with the silver and the gold that he had carried off from all the nations: from Edom, from Moab, from the sons of Ammon, from the Philistines and from Amalek.

**12** Abishai son of Zeruiah struck down eighteen thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt.

**13** He put garrisons in Edom, and all the Edomites became David's servants. The LORD gave David victory wherever he went.

## David's Officials

**14** So David reigned over all Israel, and he did justice and righteousness for all his people.

**15** Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder;

**16** Zadok son of Ahitub and Abimelech son of Abiathar were priests; Shavsha was secretary;

**17** Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites; and David's sons were the chief officers at the king's side.

## Notes

- **vv1–17 and 2 Samuel 8** — the same chapter. Chronicles leaves out the measuring of the Moabites with a line (2 Samuel 8:2).
- **v1 "he took Gath and its villages"** — Samuel has *Metheg-ammah* (2 Samuel 8:1), a phrase whose meaning is uncertain; Chronicles names the Philistine city.
- **v3 "toward Hamath, as he went to set up his monument at the river Euphrates"** — *le-hatsiv yado*, **to set up his hand**, the phrase used of Saul's monument at Carmel, *set up a monument for himself* (1 Samuel 15:12). Samuel has *to restore his power*, *le-hashiv yado* (2 Samuel 8:3).
- **v4 "a thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen"** — the reading the text of Samuel follows from the Greek (2 Samuel 8:4); here it is in the Hebrew.
- **v8 "With it Solomon made the bronze Sea, the pillars and the vessels of bronze"** — not in the Hebrew of Samuel, though the Greek of Samuel has it. Samuel names the towns *Betah* and *Berothai* (2 Samuel 8:8).
- **v12 "Abishai son of Zeruiah struck down eighteen thousand Edomites"** — Samuel gives the deed to David, *when he returned from striking down eighteen thousand* (2 Samuel 8:13), and the heading of Psalm 60 gives it to Joab, *twelve thousand of Edom in the Valley of Salt*.
- **v14 "he did justice and righteousness"** — *mishpat u-tsedaqah*, as 2 Samuel 8:15.
- **vv9–10, 16 "Tou… Hadoram… Abimelech son of Abiathar… Shavsha"** — Samuel has *Toi*, *Joram*, *Ahimelech son of Abiathar* and *Seraiah* (2 Samuel 8:9–10, 17).
- **v17 "David's sons were the chief officers at the king's side"** — *ha-rishonim le-yad ha-melekh*, **the first at the king's hand**. Samuel has *David's sons were priests*, *kohanim* (2 Samuel 8:18). The Greek here has *the first successors of the king*.

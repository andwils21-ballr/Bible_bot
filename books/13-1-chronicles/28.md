# 1 Chronicles 28

## David Speaks to the Assembly

**1** David assembled at Jerusalem all the leaders of Israel: the leaders of the tribes, the commanders of the divisions that served the king, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, the stewards of all the property and livestock of the king and his sons, with the officials, the mighty men and every man of worth.

**2** Then King David rose to his feet and said, "Hear me, my brothers and my people. It was in my heart to build a house of rest for the ark of the covenant of the LORD and for the footstool of our God, and I made preparations to build.

**3** But God said to me, 'You shall not build a house for My name, for you are a man of wars and have shed blood.'

**4** Yet the LORD, the God of Israel, chose me from all my father's house to be king over Israel forever. For He chose Judah as leader, and in the house of Judah my father's house, and among my father's sons He was pleased with me, to make me king over all Israel.

**5** Of all my sons, for the LORD has given me many sons, He has chosen Solomon my son to sit on the throne of the kingdom of the LORD over Israel.

**6** He said to me, 'Solomon your son is the one who shall build My house and My courts, for I have chosen him to be My son, and I will be his father.

**7** I will establish his kingdom forever if he holds firm to doing My commandments and My rules, as he does today.'

**8** And now, in the sight of all Israel, the assembly of the LORD, and in the hearing of our God: keep and seek all the commandments of the LORD your God, so that you may possess this good land and leave it as an inheritance to your sons after you forever.

**9** As for you, Solomon my son, know the God of your father and serve Him with a whole heart and a willing soul, for the LORD searches all hearts and understands every shaping of the thoughts. If you seek Him, He will be found by you; but if you forsake Him, He will reject you forever.

**10** See now: the LORD has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary. Be strong and do it."

## The Pattern

**11** David gave Solomon his son the pattern of the porch and of its houses, its treasuries, its upper rooms, its inner chambers and the room of the cover;

**12** and the pattern of all that he had in his spirit for the courts of the house of the LORD, for all the chambers around it, for the treasuries of the house of God and the treasuries of the dedicated gifts;

**13** for the divisions of the priests and the Levites, for all the work of the service of the house of the LORD, and for all the vessels for the service of the house of the LORD;

**14** the weight of gold for all the gold vessels for each service, and of silver for all the silver vessels for each service;

**15** the weight of the gold lampstands and their lamps, by the weight of each lampstand and its lamps, and of the silver lampstands, by the weight of each lampstand and its lamps, according to the use of each lampstand;

**16** the weight of gold for the tables of the rows of bread, for each table, and silver for the silver tables;

**17** the forks, the basins and the pitchers of pure gold; the gold bowls, by the weight of each bowl, and the silver bowls, by the weight of each bowl;

**18** for the altar of incense, refined gold by weight; and the gold for the pattern of the chariot, the cherubim that spread their wings and shelter the ark of the covenant of the LORD.

**19** "All this is in writing from the hand of the LORD upon me. He made me understand all the work of the pattern."

**20** David said to Solomon his son, "Be strong and courageous, and do it. Do not be afraid and do not lose heart, for the LORD God, my God, is with you. He will not let you go or forsake you until all the work for the service of the house of the LORD is finished.

**21** The divisions of the priests and the Levites are ready for all the service of the house of God. With you in all the work will be every willing man with skill for any kind of service, and the officials and all the people will do all you say."

## Notes

- **v2 "a house of rest for the ark… the footstool of our God"** — *beit menuchah*, and *hadom raglei*. A psalm puts the two together: *Let us bow down at His footstool. Arise, O LORD, to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength* (Psalm 132:7–8).
- **v3 "you are a man of wars and have shed blood"** — what David told Solomon in private (22:8), now said before the whole assembly.
- **vv4–5 "He chose Judah… my father's house… me… Solomon"** — the choosing narrows four times: a tribe, a house, a son of Jesse, a son of David. *The throne of the kingdom of the LORD*: the kingdom is the LORD's, as in the promise, *I will set him over My house and over My kingdom* (17:14).
- **v9 "every shaping of the thoughts"** — *kol yetser machashavot*, the words of the flood: *every shaping of the thoughts of his heart was only evil all the day* (Genesis 6:5). The LORD who saw it then searches it still.
- **vv11–12, 19 "the pattern… in writing from the hand of the LORD upon me"** — *tavnit*, the word of Exodus 25:9 and 40, where Moses is shown *the pattern of the dwelling* on the mountain. Moses was shown the dwelling; David gives Solomon the house in writing. *That he had in his spirit*, *ba-ruach immo*, **in the spirit with him**: the Greek has *en pneumati autou*, **in his spirit**. Whether the spirit is David's own or the LORD's, v19 says where the pattern came from.
- **v11 "the room of the cover"** — *beit ha-kapporet*, the inner room where the ark stood under *the cover*, the *kapporet* of Exodus 25:17, from the root of atonement.
- **v18 "the pattern of the chariot, the cherubim"** — *ha-merkavah ha-keruvim*: the cherubim over the ark are called a chariot, as the LORD *rode on a cherub and flew* (Psalm 18:10).
- **v20 "He will not let you go or forsake you"** — *lo yarpekha ve-lo ya'azvekha*, the words of Moses to Joshua (Deuteronomy 31:6, 8) and of the LORD to Joshua (Joshua 1:5), here passed from David to Solomon with *Be strong and courageous*.

# 1 Chronicles 22

## The Site and the Materials

**1** David said, "This is the house of the LORD God, and this is the altar of burnt offering for Israel."

**2** David gave orders to gather the guests who were in the land of Israel, and he appointed stonecutters to cut dressed stones for building the house of God.

**3** David prepared iron in abundance for the nails for the doors of the gates and for the clamps, and bronze in abundance beyond weighing,

**4** and cedar logs without number, for the Sidonians and the Tyrians brought cedar logs in abundance to David.

**5** David said, "Solomon my son is young and tender, and the house to be built for the LORD must be exceedingly great, for a name and for glory throughout all lands. I will prepare for it." So David prepared in abundance before his death.

## David's Charge to Solomon

**6** Then he called for Solomon his son and charged him to build a house for the LORD, the God of Israel.

**7** David said to Solomon, "My son, it was in my heart to build a house for the name of the LORD my God.

**8** But the word of the LORD came to me, saying, 'You have shed much blood and have waged great wars. You shall not build a house for My name, because you have shed much blood on the earth before Me.

**9** A son will be born to you. He will be a man of rest, and I will give him rest from all his enemies around him. Solomon shall be his name, and I will give peace and quiet to Israel in his days.

**10** He shall build a house for My name. He shall be My son, and I will be his father, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom over Israel forever.'

**11** Now, my son, may the LORD be with you, so that you succeed and build the house of the LORD your God, as He has spoken concerning you.

**12** Only may the LORD give you insight and understanding, and give you charge over Israel, so that you keep the instruction of the LORD your God.

**13** Then you will succeed, if you take care to do the statutes and the rules that the LORD commanded Moses for Israel. Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid and do not lose heart.

**14** In my affliction I have prepared for the house of the LORD a hundred thousand talents of gold and a million talents of silver, and bronze and iron beyond weighing, for there is so much of it. I have prepared timber and stone too, and you will add to them.

**15** You have workmen in abundance: stonecutters, workers in stone and wood, and every man skilled in every kind of work,

**16** in gold, silver, bronze and iron, without number. Get up and do it, and may the LORD be with you."

## David's Charge to the Leaders

**17** David commanded all the leaders of Israel to help Solomon his son:

**18** "Is not the LORD your God with you? Has He not given you rest on every side? For He has given the inhabitants of the land into my hand, and the land is subdued before the LORD and before His people.

**19** Now set your heart and your soul to seek the LORD your God. Get up and build the sanctuary of the LORD God, so that you may bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD and the holy vessels of God into the house built for the name of the LORD."

## Notes

- **v1 "This is the house of the LORD God"** — said on the threshing floor of Ornan, where the fire fell (21:26–28). Chronicles names the place again when the building begins: *on Mount Moriah, where the LORD appeared to David his father… on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite* (2 Chronicles 3:1). *Moriah* is the name of the land where Abraham was sent to offer Isaac (Genesis 22:2).
- **v8 "You have shed much blood"** — *dam la-rov shafakhta*. In Kings, Solomon gives the reason as the wars: David *could not build a house for the name of the LORD his God because of the wars that surrounded him* (1 Kings 5:3). Chronicles gives the word of the LORD, and it names the blood. David repeats it in 28:3: *you are a man of wars and have shed blood*.
- **v9 "a man of rest… Solomon shall be his name… peace"** — *ish menuchah*, and *va-hanichoti*, **I will give him rest**, from the same root. *Shelomoh*, **Solomon**, is built on *shalom*, **peace**, the word that follows: *shalom va-sheqet*, **peace and quiet**. The name is explained by the promise.

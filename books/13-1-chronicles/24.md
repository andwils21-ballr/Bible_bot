# 1 Chronicles 24

## The Sons of Aaron

**1** The divisions of the sons of Aaron: the sons of Aaron were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**2** Nadab and Abihu died before their father and had no sons, so Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests.

**3** With Zadok of the sons of Eleazar and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, David divided them by their appointed duties in their service.

**4** More chief men were found among the sons of Eleazar than among the sons of Ithamar, so they divided them: sixteen heads of fathers' houses for the sons of Eleazar, and eight for the sons of Ithamar, by their fathers' houses.

**5** They divided them by lot, the one group with the other, for there were officers of the sanctuary and officers of God among the sons of Eleazar and among the sons of Ithamar.

**6** Shemaiah son of Nethanel the scribe, of the Levites, recorded them before the king and the officers, Zadok the priest, Ahimelech son of Abiathar, and the heads of the fathers' houses of the priests and of the Levites: one father's house taken for Eleazar, and one taken for Ithamar.

## The Lots

**7** The first lot fell to Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah,

**8** the third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim,

**9** the fifth to Malchijah, the sixth to Mijamin,

**10** the seventh to Hakkoz, the eighth to Abijah,

**11** the ninth to Jeshua, the tenth to Shecaniah,

**12** the eleventh to Eliashib, the twelfth to Jakim,

**13** the thirteenth to Huppah, the fourteenth to Jeshebeab,

**14** the fifteenth to Bilgah, the sixteenth to Immer,

**15** the seventeenth to Hezir, the eighteenth to Happizzez,

**16** the nineteenth to Pethahiah, the twentieth to Jehezkel,

**17** the twenty-first to Jachin, the twenty-second to Gamul,

**18** the twenty-third to Delaiah, the twenty-fourth to Maaziah.

**19** These were their appointed duties in their service, to come into the house of the LORD according to the rule given them through Aaron their father, as the LORD, the God of Israel, had commanded him.

## The Rest of the Levites

**20** Of the rest of the sons of Levi: of the sons of Amram, Shubael; of the sons of Shubael, Jehdeiah.

**21** Of Rehabiah: of the sons of Rehabiah, Isshiah the chief.

**22** Of the Izharites, Shelomoth; of the sons of Shelomoth, Jahath.

**23** The sons of Hebron: Jeriah, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, Jekameam the fourth.

**24** The sons of Uzziel, Micah; of the sons of Micah, Shamir.

**25** The brother of Micah, Isshiah; of the sons of Isshiah, Zechariah.

**26** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Jaaziah his son:

**27** the sons of Merari by Jaaziah his son: Shoham, Zaccur and Ibri.

**28** Of Mahli: Eleazar, who had no sons.

**29** Of Kish: the sons of Kish, Jerahmeel.

**30** The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder and Jerimoth. These were the sons of the Levites by their fathers' houses.

**31** These also cast lots, as their kinsmen the sons of Aaron did, before David the king, Zadok, Ahimelech, and the heads of the fathers' houses of the priests and of the Levites, the head of a father's house alongside his youngest brother.

## Notes

- **v2 "Nadab and Abihu died before their father and had no sons"** — Chronicles gives no cause. The law tells it: they *offered before the LORD strange fire*, and *fire came out from before the LORD and consumed them* (Leviticus 10:1–2); *they had no sons* (Numbers 3:4).
- **v5 "They divided them by lot"** — *be-goralot*. Both houses had *officers of the sanctuary and officers of God*, so neither line could claim the first places, and the lot decided the order. The Levites cast lots the same way in v31, and the singers and gatekeepers in the chapters that follow (25:8; 26:13).
- **v6 "one father's house taken for Eleazar, and one taken for Ithamar"** — *achuz le-Elazar ve-achuz achuz le-Itamar*, **taken for Eleazar, and taken, taken for Ithamar**: the Hebrew doubles the word for Ithamar only. The Greek doubles it for both, *one by one for Eleazar and one by one for Ithamar*. Either way the lots were drawn from the two houses in turn; how sixteen and eight were drawn in turn is not explained.
- **vv7–18 the twenty-four lots** — the order of the priestly courses, each serving in the house in its turn (v19). The eighth lot, *Abijah*, is the course of Zechariah, the father of John: *a priest named Zechariah, of the division of Abijah* (Luke 1:5). When his course was on duty, *by the custom of the priesthood, it fell to him by lot to burn incense* (Luke 1:9), the duty given to the sons of Aaron in 23:13.
- **vv20–31** — the Levite families of 23:12–23, carried one generation further. Some names differ in form from the earlier list: *Shubael* for *Shebuel* (23:16), *Shelomoth* for *Shelomith* (23:18).
- **vv26–27 "The sons of Jaaziah his son"** — *benei Ya'aziyahu beno*: Jaaziah, a third son of Merari not named in 23:21 or 6:19. Some English versions take *beno*, **his son**, as a name, *Beno*; the Greek reads it as *his sons*.
- **v31 "the head of a father's house alongside his youngest brother"** — *avot ha-rosh le-ummat achiv ha-qatan*. The lot made no difference between the senior house and the junior: each drew its place the same way.

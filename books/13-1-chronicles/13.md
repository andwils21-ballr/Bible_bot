# 1 Chronicles 13

## David Consults the Assembly

**1** David consulted with the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, with every leader.

**2** David said to all the assembly of Israel, "If it seems good to you, and if it is from the LORD our God, let us send word far and wide to our kinsmen who remain in all the lands of Israel, and with them to the priests and Levites in their towns that have pasture lands, that they may gather to us.

**3** Then let us bring back the ark of our God to us, for we did not seek it in the days of Saul."

**4** All the assembly agreed to do so, for the thing was right in the eyes of all the people.

## The New Cart

**5** So David gathered all Israel, from the Shihor of Egypt to Lebo-hamath, to bring the ark of God from Kiriath-jearim.

**6** David and all Israel went up to Baalah, that is, Kiriath-jearim, which belongs to Judah, to bring up from there the ark of God, the LORD, who is enthroned on the cherubim, which is called by the name.

**7** They carried the ark of God on a new cart from the house of Abinadab, and Uzza and Ahio were driving the cart.

**8** David and all Israel were celebrating before God with all their might, with songs, lyres, harps, tambourines, cymbals and trumpets.

**9** When they came to the threshing floor of Chidon, Uzza put out his hand to take hold of the ark, for the oxen stumbled.

**10** The anger of the LORD burned against Uzza, and He struck him down because he put out his hand to the ark, and he died there before God.

**11** David was angry because the LORD had broken out against Uzza, and that place is called Perez-uzza to this day.

**12** David was afraid of God that day and said, "How can I bring the ark of God home to me?"

**13** So David did not take the ark home to himself into the City of David, but turned it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.

**14** The ark of God remained with the household of Obed-edom in his house three months, and the LORD blessed the household of Obed-edom and all that he had.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "If it seems good to you, and if it is from the LORD our God"** — in Samuel David simply gathers *the chosen men of Israel* and goes (2 Samuel 6:1–2); in Chronicles the whole assembly agrees first.
- **vv2, 11 "let us send word far and wide… the LORD had broken out against Uzza"** — *nifretsah*, **let us break out**, spread out, and *parats YHWH perets*, **the LORD broke out a breach**: the same root, *parats*, for David's plan and for the LORD's anger. The place is named *Perez-uzza*, **the breach of Uzza**.
- **v3 "we did not seek it in the days of Saul"** — *lo derashnuhu*, the verb of 10:14, where Saul *did not inquire of the LORD*.
- **v5 "from the Shihor of Egypt to Lebo-hamath"** — the full length of the land Joshua was told remained to be taken, *from the Shihor, which is east of Egypt* (Joshua 13:3, 5).
- **v6 "Baalah, that is, Kiriath-jearim"** — the ark had stayed there since the Philistines sent it back, *in the house of Abinadab on the hill* (1 Samuel 7:1). Samuel calls the place *Baale-judah* (2 Samuel 6:2).
- **v7 "on a new cart"** — the way the Philistines sent the ark home (1 Samuel 6:7). The law gave the sons of Kohath no carts, *because theirs was the service of the holy things, which they carried on their shoulders* (Numbers 7:9), and David's second attempt follows it (15:2, 15).
- **v9 "the threshing floor of Chidon"** — Samuel has *Nacon* (2 Samuel 6:6).
- **v10 "because he put out his hand to the ark"** — Samuel's *for his error*, *al ha-shal*, is a word found only there (2 Samuel 6:7); Chronicles names the act.
- **v14 "the LORD blessed the household of Obed-edom"** — Obed-edom is *the Gittite*, a man of Gath, and his house is blessed while the ark is in it. In 15:18 and 16:5 an Obed-edom is among the Levite gatekeepers and musicians.

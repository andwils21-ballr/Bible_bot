# 1 Chronicles 27

## The Twelve Monthly Divisions

**1** This is the list of the sons of Israel by their number, the heads of fathers' houses, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and their officers, who served the king in every matter of the divisions that came in and went out month by month, through all the months of the year. Each division was twenty-four thousand.

**2** Over the first division, for the first month, was Jashobeam son of Zabdiel; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**3** He was of the sons of Perez, chief of all the commanders of the armies for the first month.

**4** Over the division of the second month was Dodai the Ahohite, with his division, and Mikloth the leader; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**5** The third commander of the army, for the third month, was Benaiah son of Jehoiada the priest, as chief; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**6** This is the Benaiah who was a mighty man of the thirty and over the thirty; and in his division was Ammizabad his son.

**7** The fourth, for the fourth month, was Asahel the brother of Joab, and Zebadiah his son after him; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**8** The fifth, for the fifth month, was the commander Shamhuth the Izrahite; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**9** The sixth, for the sixth month, was Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**10** The seventh, for the seventh month, was Helez the Pelonite, of the sons of Ephraim; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**11** The eighth, for the eighth month, was Sibbecai the Hushathite, of the Zerahites; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**12** The ninth, for the ninth month, was Abiezer the Anathothite, of the Benjaminites; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**13** The tenth, for the tenth month, was Maharai the Netophathite, of the Zerahites; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**14** The eleventh, for the eleventh month, was Benaiah the Pirathonite, of the sons of Ephraim; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**15** The twelfth, for the twelfth month, was Heldai the Netophathite, of Othniel; in his division were twenty-four thousand.

## The Leaders of the Tribes

**16** Over the tribes of Israel: for the Reubenites, Eliezer son of Zichri was leader; for the Simeonites, Shephatiah son of Maacah;

**17** for Levi, Hashabiah son of Kemuel; for Aaron, Zadok;

**18** for Judah, Elihu, one of David's brothers; for Issachar, Omri son of Michael;

**19** for Zebulun, Ishmaiah son of Obadiah; for Naphtali, Jerimoth son of Azriel;

**20** for the sons of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Azaziah; for the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joel son of Pedaiah;

**21** for the half of Manasseh in Gilead, Iddo son of Zechariah; for Benjamin, Jaasiel son of Abner;

**22** for Dan, Azarel son of Jeroham. These were the commanders of the tribes of Israel.

**23** David did not take the number of those twenty years old and under, for the LORD had said He would make Israel as many as the stars of heaven.

**24** Joab son of Zeruiah began to count them but did not finish. Wrath came on Israel for this, and the number was not entered in the account of the chronicles of King David.

## The King's Stewards

**25** Over the king's treasuries was Azmaveth son of Adiel. Over the treasuries in the country, in the cities, in the villages and in the towers, was Jonathan son of Uzziah.

**26** Over those who did the work of the field, tilling the ground, was Ezri son of Chelub.

**27** Over the vineyards was Shimei the Ramathite, and over the produce of the vineyards for the wine cellars was Zabdi the Shiphmite.

**28** Over the olive trees and the sycamore figs in the lowland was Baal-hanan the Gederite, and over the stores of oil was Joash.

**29** Over the herds that grazed in Sharon was Shitrai the Sharonite, and over the herds in the valleys was Shaphat son of Adlai.

**30** Over the camels was Obil the Ishmaelite, and over the donkeys was Jehdeiah the Meronothite.

**31** Over the flocks was Jaziz the Hagrite. All these were the officials over King David's property.

## The King's Counselors

**32** Jonathan, David's uncle, was a counselor, a man of understanding and a scribe; he and Jehiel son of Hachmoni were with the king's sons.

**33** Ahithophel was the king's counselor, and Hushai the Archite was the king's friend.

**34** After Ahithophel came Jehoiada son of Benaiah, and Abiathar. Joab was commander of the king's army.

## Notes

- **vv2–15 the twelve commanders** — every one of them is among David's mighty men in 11:10–47: Jashobeam (11:11), Benaiah son of Jehoiada (11:22), Asahel (11:26), Ira, Helez, Abiezer, Sibbecai, Maharai, Benaiah the Pirathonite and Heled (11:27–31). *Dodai the Ahohite* (v4) is the father's name of *Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite* (11:12).
- **v4 "and Mikloth the leader"** — *u-Miqlot ha-nagid*, a clause with no place in the sentence; the Greek does not have it. *Mikloth* is a Benjamite of the house of Gibeon in 8:29–32. The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **v7 "Asahel the brother of Joab, and Zebadiah his son after him"** — Abner killed Asahel in the war with Saul's house (2 Samuel 2:23). His division keeps his name, and his son commands it *after him*.
- **vv16–22 the tribes** — Gad and Asher are not named; Levi and Aaron are counted apart, and Manasseh twice, so the list still comes to twelve. *Jaasiel son of Abner* (v21) leads Benjamin, the tribe of Saul's commander.
- **v18 "Elihu, one of David's brothers"** — the Greek has *Eliab*, the eldest son of Jesse, the one Samuel first took for the LORD's anointed (1 Samuel 16:6). No *Elihu* is among Jesse's sons in 2:13–15. The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **vv23–24 "as many as the stars of heaven… not entered in the account of the chronicles of King David"** — the promise to Abraham, *count the stars* (Genesis 15:5). Joab's count is the census of chapter 21. The phrase *divrei ha-yamim*, **the chronicles**, literally **the words of the days**, is the Hebrew name of this book.
- **vv30–31 "Obil the Ishmaelite… Jaziz the Hagrite"** — the camels are in the charge of an Ishmaelite, the people Joseph's brothers saw coming from Gilead *with their camels* (Genesis 37:25); the flocks in the charge of a Hagrite, a people Reuben fought east of Gilead (5:10). David's herds are kept by men from the peoples of the grazing lands.
- **vv33–34 "Ahithophel… Hushai the Archite was the king's friend"** — the two men who stood on opposite sides in Absalom's revolt (2 Samuel 15:12, 32–37). Chronicles says only *after Ahithophel*; Samuel tells how his counsel was overturned and how he died (2 Samuel 17:14, 23).
- **v34 "Jehoiada son of Benaiah"** — elsewhere it is *Benaiah son of Jehoiada* (v5); the Greek has the same order as the Hebrew here, *Iōdae ho tou Banaiou*. The verse names a son of Benaiah called after his grandfather, or reverses the two names; the text keeps the Hebrew.

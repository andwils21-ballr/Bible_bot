# 1 Chronicles 23

## Solomon King, the Levites Counted

**1** When David was old and full of days, he made Solomon his son king over Israel.

**2** He gathered all the leaders of Israel, and the priests and the Levites.

**3** The Levites were counted from thirty years old and upward, and their number, head by head, was thirty-eight thousand men.

**4** "Of these, twenty-four thousand are to oversee the work of the house of the LORD, six thousand are to be officers and judges,

**5** four thousand gatekeepers, and four thousand to praise the LORD with the instruments I made for praise."

**6** David divided them into divisions by the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath and Merari.

## Gershon

**7** Of the Gershonites: Ladan and Shimei.

**8** The sons of Ladan: Jehiel the chief, Zetham and Joel, three.

**9** The sons of Shimei: Shelomith, Haziel and Haran, three. These were the heads of the fathers' houses of Ladan.

**10** The sons of Shimei: Jahath, Zina, Jeush and Beriah. These were the sons of Shimei, four.

**11** Jahath was the chief, and Zizah the second; but Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, so they became one father's house, counted as one.

## Kohath

**12** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel, four.

**13** The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. Aaron was set apart to consecrate him as most holy, he and his sons forever, to burn incense before the LORD, to serve Him and to bless in His name forever.

**14** As for Moses the man of God, his sons were named among the tribe of Levi.

**15** The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.

**16** The sons of Gershom: Shebuel the chief.

**17** The sons of Eliezer: Rehabiah the chief. Eliezer had no other sons, but the sons of Rehabiah were very many.

**18** The sons of Izhar: Shelomith the chief.

**19** The sons of Hebron: Jeriah the chief, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third and Jekameam the fourth.

**20** The sons of Uzziel: Micah the chief and Isshiah the second.

## Merari

**21** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.

**22** Eleazar died and had no sons, only daughters; and their kinsmen, the sons of Kish, married them.

**23** The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder and Jeremoth, three.

## The Work of the Levites

**24** These were the sons of Levi by their fathers' houses, the heads of fathers' houses as they were enrolled, counted by name head by head, who did the work for the service of the house of the LORD, from twenty years old and upward.

**25** For David said, "The LORD, the God of Israel, has given rest to His people, and He dwells in Jerusalem forever.

**26** So the Levites no longer need to carry the dwelling or any of its vessels for its service."

**27** For by the last words of David the sons of Levi were counted from twenty years old and upward.

**28** Their place was at the side of the sons of Aaron for the service of the house of the LORD: over the courts and the chambers, the cleansing of every holy thing, and the work of the service of the house of God;

**29** the bread set in rows, the fine flour for the grain offering, the unleavened wafers, what is baked on the griddle and what is soaked, and every measure of quantity and size;

**30** to stand every morning to give thanks and praise to the LORD, and likewise in the evening;

**31** and at every offering of burnt offerings to the LORD on the sabbaths, the new moons and the appointed feasts, by number as the rule for them required, continually before the LORD.

**32** They kept the charge of the tent of meeting and the charge of the holy place, and the charge of the sons of Aaron their kinsmen, for the service of the house of the LORD.

## Notes

- **v1 "old and full of days"** — *zaqen u-seva yamim*, the words said of Isaac at his death (Genesis 35:29) and of Job (Job 42:17); Abraham dies *old and satisfied*, *zaqen ve-savea*, without *days* (Genesis 25:8). Chronicles says it of David again at his death (29:28).
- **vv3, 24, 27 "from thirty years old… from twenty years old"** — the count in v3 starts at thirty, the age of the Levites who carried the tent in the wilderness, *from thirty years old and upward to fifty* (Numbers 4:3). Numbers 8:24 has *twenty-five*. Vv24 and 27 have *twenty*, and v27 gives the reason: *by the last words of David*. The chapter keeps both counts.
- **v4 "to oversee the work"** — *le-natseach*, the verb behind *la-menatseach*, **for the director**, the heading of many psalms. The same verb is used of the overseers of the temple repairs under Josiah (2 Chronicles 34:12).
- **v5 "the instruments I made for praise"** — *asiti*, **I made**: the list breaks into David's own voice, so vv4–5 are his words. The Greek has *which he made*. The temple singers later play *the instruments of the LORD's song that David the king had made* (2 Chronicles 7:6).
- **vv10–11 "Zina… Zizah"** — the same son, spelled two ways in two verses; the Greek has *Ziza* in both.
- **v13 "Aaron was set apart to consecrate him as most holy"** — *le-hakdisho qodesh qodashim*. It can mean that Aaron himself is consecrated as **most holy**, or that he is set apart **to consecrate the most holy things**, as the priests alone handle *the most holy things* in the law (Numbers 4:4, 19). The Greek reads the first way: *to be sanctified, holy of holies*.
- **v14 "As for Moses the man of God, his sons were named among the tribe of Levi"** — Aaron's sons are priests; the sons of Moses are counted with the Levites, and no more is said of them than of any other house.
- **v22 "their kinsmen, the sons of Kish, married them"** — as the daughters of Zelophehad, who had no brothers, *became wives to the sons of their father's brothers* (Numbers 36:11), and the inheritance stayed in the family.
- **vv25–26 "He dwells in Jerusalem forever… the Levites no longer need to carry the dwelling"** — *va-yishkon*, **He dwells**, and *ha-mishkan*, **the dwelling**, the same root. The work given to the Levites in Numbers 4 was carrying; now that the LORD dwells in one place, it becomes the service of the house.

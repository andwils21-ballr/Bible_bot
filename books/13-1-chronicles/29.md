# 1 Chronicles 29

## The Freewill Gifts

**1** King David said to all the assembly, "Solomon my son, the one God has chosen, is young and tender, and the work is great, for the palace is not for a man but for the LORD God.

**2** With all my strength I have prepared for the house of my God: the gold for things of gold, the silver for things of silver, the bronze for things of bronze, the iron for things of iron and the wood for things of wood; onyx stones and stones for setting, stones of antimony and of many colors, every kind of precious stone, and marble in abundance.

**3** Moreover, because I delight in the house of my God, I have a treasure of my own, of gold and silver, and I give it to the house of my God, over and above all that I have prepared for the holy house:

**4** three thousand talents of gold, of the gold of Ophir, and seven thousand talents of refined silver, to overlay the walls of the houses;

**5** gold for things of gold and silver for things of silver, and for all the work done by the hands of craftsmen. Who then will offer willingly, to fill his hand today for the LORD?"

**6** Then the leaders of the fathers' houses, the leaders of the tribes of Israel, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the officers over the king's work offered willingly.

**7** They gave for the service of the house of God five thousand talents and ten thousand darics of gold, ten thousand talents of silver, eighteen thousand talents of bronze and a hundred thousand talents of iron.

**8** Whoever had precious stones gave them to the treasury of the house of the LORD, in the care of Jehiel the Gershonite.

**9** The people rejoiced because they had offered willingly, for with a whole heart they offered willingly to the LORD; and David the king also rejoiced with great joy.

## David's Prayer

**10** David blessed the LORD before all the assembly. David said, "Blessed are You, O LORD, the God of Israel our father, forever and ever.

**11** Yours, O LORD, are the greatness and the power, the glory, the victory and the majesty, for everything in heaven and on earth is Yours. Yours, O LORD, is the kingdom, and You are exalted as head over all.

**12** Riches and honor come from You, and You rule over all. In Your hand are strength and power, and in Your hand it is to make great and to give strength to all.

**13** So now, our God, we give thanks to You and praise Your glorious name.

**14** But who am I, and who are my people, that we should be able to offer so willingly as this? For all things come from You, and from Your own hand we have given to You.

**15** For we are guests before You, and residents, as all our fathers were. Our days on the earth are like a shadow, and there is no hope.

**16** O LORD our God, all this abundance that we have prepared to build You a house for Your holy name comes from Your hand, and all of it is Yours.

**17** I know, my God, that You test the heart and take pleasure in uprightness. In the uprightness of my heart I have offered all these things willingly, and now I have seen with joy Your people who are present here offering willingly to You.

**18** O LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac and Israel, our fathers, keep this forever in the shaping of the thoughts of the heart of Your people, and direct their hearts toward You.

**19** Give Solomon my son a whole heart to keep Your commandments, Your testimonies and Your statutes, to do them all, and to build the palace for which I have made provision."

**20** Then David said to all the assembly, "Bless the LORD your God." All the assembly blessed the LORD, the God of their fathers, and bowed their heads and bowed down to the LORD and to the king.

## Solomon King

**21** They offered sacrifices to the LORD, and on the next day they offered burnt offerings to the LORD: a thousand bulls, a thousand rams and a thousand lambs, with their drink offerings, and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel.

**22** They ate and drank before the LORD on that day with great joy. They made Solomon son of David king a second time, and they anointed him for the LORD as leader, and Zadok as priest.

**23** Solomon sat on the throne of the LORD as king in place of David his father. He prospered, and all Israel obeyed him.

**24** All the officials and the mighty men, and all the sons of King David too, pledged themselves to Solomon the king.

**25** The LORD made Solomon exceedingly great in the sight of all Israel, and gave him royal majesty such as no king over Israel before him had had.

## The Death of David

**26** David son of Jesse reigned over all Israel.

**27** The time that he reigned over Israel was forty years: he reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.

**28** He died at a good gray age, full of days, riches and honor, and Solomon his son reigned in his place.

**29** The acts of King David, from first to last, are written in the words of Samuel the seer, in the words of Nathan the prophet and in the words of Gad the visionary,

**30** with all his reign and his might, and the times that passed over him and over Israel and over all the kingdoms of the lands.

## Notes

- **vv1, 19 "the palace is not for a man but for the LORD God"** — *ha-birah*, **the citadel**, a word found elsewhere in the books of the return: *Shushan the citadel* (Nehemiah 1:1; Esther 1:2). Here it is the house of God.
- **v3 "a treasure of my own"** — *segullah*, the word of the covenant at Sinai: *you will be My own treasure out of all the peoples* (Exodus 19:5). The king gives his own treasure to the God whose treasure Israel is.
- **v5 "to fill his hand today for the LORD"** — *le-mallot yado*, the words for ordaining a priest, and the words of Moses to the Levites after the golden calf: *Fill your hands today for the LORD* (Exodus 32:29). Here every leader may fill his own hand, with a gift.
- **v7 "ten thousand darics"** — *adarkonim*, a gold coin named again only in Ezra 8:27, where *twenty bowls of gold worth a thousand darics* are given for the house of God in the days of the return.
- **v11 "Yours, O LORD, are the greatness and the power, the glory…"** — five nouns, each with the article: *ha-gedullah ve-ha-gevurah ve-ha-tif'eret ve-ha-netsach ve-ha-hod*. *Netsach*, here **victory**, is also **lastingness**, as in *forever*, *la-netsach*.
- **v15 "we are guests before You, and residents"** — *gerim… ve-toshavim*, Abraham's words to the Hittites, *A guest and a resident I am among you* (Genesis 23:4), and the law's word to all Israel, *you are guests and residents with Me* (Leviticus 25:23). The king who gives the gold calls himself a guest.
- **v18 "the shaping of the thoughts of the heart"** — *yetser machshevot levav*, the words of Genesis 6:5, which David used of Solomon in 28:9. Here he prays that the willing heart of this day be kept in it.
- **vv22–24 "king a second time… all the sons of King David too, pledged themselves"** — the first time is 23:1. Kings tells of a struggle: Adonijah made himself king, and Solomon was anointed at Gihon (1 Kings 1). Chronicles tells of a single assembly, and says that *all the sons of King David* gave their hand to Solomon. *Natnu yad*, **gave a hand**, is a pledge. *The throne of the LORD* (v23) is the throne Kings calls *the throne of David his father* (1 Kings 2:12).
- **v28 "at a good gray age, full of days"** — *be-seivah tovah*, said of Abraham (Genesis 15:15; 25:8), and *seva yamim*, said of David in 23:1.
- **v29 "Samuel the seer… Nathan the prophet… Gad the visionary"** — three words: *ro'eh*, *navi*, *chozeh*. *The one who is now called a prophet was formerly called a seer* (1 Samuel 9:9).

# 1 Chronicles 12

## At Ziklag

**1** These are the men who came to David at Ziklag while he was still kept away from Saul son of Kish. They were among the mighty men who helped him in war.

**2** They were armed with bows and could use both the right hand and the left in slinging stones and shooting arrows with the bow. They were of Saul's kinsmen, from Benjamin:

**3** the chief Ahiezer, then Joash, the sons of Shemaah the Gibeathite; Jeziel and Pelet the sons of Azmaveth; Beracah, Jehu the Anathothite,

**4** Ishmaiah the Gibeonite, a mighty man among the Thirty and over the Thirty; Jeremiah, Jahaziel, Johanan, Jozabad the Gederathite,

**5** Eluzai, Jerimoth, Bealiah, Shemariah, Shephatiah the Haruphite,

**6** Elkanah, Isshiah, Azarel, Joezer and Jashobeam, the Korahites,

**7** and Joelah and Zebadiah the sons of Jeroham of Gedor.

## From Gad

**8** From the Gadites there went over to David at the stronghold in the wilderness men of valor, men trained for war, who could handle shield and spear, whose faces were like the faces of lions, and who were as swift as gazelles on the mountains:

**9** Ezer the chief, Obadiah the second, Eliab the third,

**10** Mishmannah the fourth, Jeremiah the fifth,

**11** Attai the sixth, Eliel the seventh,

**12** Johanan the eighth, Elzabad the ninth,

**13** Jeremiah the tenth, Machbannai the eleventh.

**14** These of the sons of Gad were heads of the army; the least was a match for a hundred, and the greatest for a thousand.

**15** These are the men who crossed the Jordan in the first month, when it was overflowing all its banks, and put to flight all those in the valleys, to the east and to the west.

## From Benjamin and Judah

**16** Some of the sons of Benjamin and Judah came to the stronghold to David.

**17** David went out to meet them and said to them, "If you have come to me in peace to help me, my heart will be joined to you; but if you have come to betray me to my enemies, though there is no violence in my hands, may the God of our fathers see and rebuke you."

**18** Then the spirit clothed Amasai, the chief of the officers:  
"We are yours, O David,  
and with you, O son of Jesse!  
Peace, peace to you,  
and peace to your helpers,  
for your God helps you."  
Then David received them and made them officers of the troop.

## From Manasseh

**19** Some from Manasseh deserted to David when he came with the Philistines to battle against Saul. But he did not help them, for the lords of the Philistines took counsel and sent him away, saying, "At the cost of our heads he will desert to his master Saul."

**20** As he went to Ziklag, these deserted to him from Manasseh: Adnah, Jozabad, Jediael, Michael, Jozabad, Elihu and Zillethai, chiefs of thousands of Manasseh.

**21** They helped David against the band of raiders, for they were all men of worth, and they became commanders in the army.

**22** Day by day men came to David to help him, until there was a great camp, like the camp of God.

## The Army at Hebron

**23** These are the numbers of the chiefs of those armed for war who came to David at Hebron to turn the kingdom of Saul over to him, according to the word of the LORD.

**24** The sons of Judah who carried shield and spear, six thousand eight hundred, armed for war.

**25** Of the sons of Simeon, men of worth for war, seven thousand one hundred.

**26** Of the sons of Levi, four thousand six hundred.

**27** Jehoiada, the leader of the house of Aaron, and with him three thousand seven hundred,

**28** and Zadok, a young man of worth, and his father's house, twenty-two commanders.

**29** Of the sons of Benjamin, Saul's kinsmen, three thousand, for until then most of them had kept their allegiance to the house of Saul.

**30** Of the sons of Ephraim, twenty thousand eight hundred, men of worth, famous men in their fathers' houses.

**31** Of the half-tribe of Manasseh, eighteen thousand, who were designated by name to come and make David king.

**32** Of the sons of Issachar, men who understood the times, to know what Israel ought to do, two hundred chiefs, and all their kinsmen under their command.

**33** Of Zebulun, fifty thousand ready for battle, armed for war with all the weapons of war, to help with an undivided heart.

**34** Of Naphtali, a thousand commanders, and with them thirty-seven thousand with shield and spear.

**35** Of the Danites, twenty-eight thousand six hundred armed for war.

**36** Of Asher, forty thousand ready for battle, armed for war.

**37** From beyond the Jordan, of the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh, a hundred and twenty thousand with all the weapons of war.

**38** All these men of war, ready in battle order, came to Hebron with a whole heart to make David king over all Israel, and all the rest of Israel too were of one heart to make David king.

**39** They were there with David three days, eating and drinking, for their kinsmen had provided for them.

**40** Those who were near them also, as far as Issachar, Zebulun and Naphtali, brought food on donkeys, camels, mules and oxen: provisions of flour, cakes of figs, clusters of raisins, wine, oil, cattle and sheep in abundance, for there was joy in Israel.

## Notes

- **vv1–40 in the Hebrew** — the Hebrew divides v4 in two (4–5) and numbers vv5–40 as 6–41.
- **v1 "while he was still kept away from Saul"** — *atsur*, **shut in, held back**: David's time among the Philistines, when Achish gave him Ziklag (1 Samuel 27:6).
- **v2 "both the right hand and the left… from Benjamin"** — Benjamin, *son of the right hand*, sends men who use either hand; Judges counts seven hundred left-handed Benjaminites who could sling a stone at a hair (Judges 20:16).
- **v8 "faces like the faces of lions… as swift as gazelles on the mountains"** — Asahel *was as swift of foot as a gazelle in the field* (2 Samuel 2:18).
- **v15 "crossed the Jordan in the first month, when it was overflowing all its banks"** — the flood season of Joshua's crossing: *the Jordan overflows all its banks all the days of harvest* (Joshua 3:15). Written *gidyotav*, read *gedotav*, **its banks**.
- **v18 "Then the spirit clothed Amasai"** — *ve-ruach lavshah et Amasai*, the verb of *The Spirit of the LORD clothed Gideon* (Judges 6:34). Here the Hebrew says only *ruach*, **spirit**, without *of God* or *of the LORD*, and it stays lowercase. Written *ha-sheloshim*, **the thirty**, read *ha-shalishim*, **the officers**.
- **v19 "At the cost of our heads he will desert to his master Saul"** — the Philistine commanders in Samuel: *how could this man win back his lord's favor? Would it not be with the heads of these men?* (1 Samuel 29:4).
- **v22 "a great camp, like the camp of God"** — *ke-machaneh Elohim*, Jacob's words when the messengers of God met him: *This is God's camp* (Genesis 32:2).
- **v32 "men who understood the times, to know what Israel ought to do"** — *yode'ei vinah la-ittim*. Issachar alone is counted by the understanding of its chiefs, with no number of fighting men.
- **vv33, 38 "an undivided heart… a whole heart… one heart"** — *be-lo lev va-lev*, **without a heart and a heart**; then *be-levav shalem* and *lev echad*. The chapter ends in a single heart and *joy in Israel* (v40).

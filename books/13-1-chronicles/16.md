# 1 Chronicles 16

## The Ark Set in the Tent

**1** They brought in the ark of God and set it inside the tent that David had pitched for it, and they offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before God.

**2** When David had finished offering the burnt offerings and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD.

**3** He distributed to all Israel, both men and women, to each a loaf of bread, a portion of meat and a cake of raisins.

**4** He appointed some of the Levites as ministers before the ark of the LORD, to bring to remembrance, to thank and to praise the LORD, the God of Israel:

**5** Asaph the chief, and second to him Zechariah, then Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom and Jeiel, with harps and lyres; Asaph was to sound the cymbals,

**6** and Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests were to blow trumpets continually before the ark of the covenant of God.

## The Song of Thanks

**7** On that day David first appointed that thanks be given to the LORD by Asaph and his kinsmen:

**8** Give thanks to the LORD, call on His name;  
make known His deeds among the peoples.

**9** Sing to Him, sing praises to Him;  
tell of all His wonders.

**10** Glory in His holy name;  
let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice.

**11** Seek the LORD and His strength;  
seek His face continually.

**12** Remember the wonders He has done,  
His signs and the judgments of His mouth,

**13** O seed of Israel His servant,  
sons of Jacob, His chosen ones!

**14** He is the LORD our God;  
His judgments are in all the earth.

**15** Remember His covenant forever,  
the word He commanded for a thousand generations,

**16** the covenant He made with Abraham,  
His oath to Isaac,

**17** which He confirmed to Jacob as a statute,  
to Israel as an everlasting covenant,

**18** saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan  
as the portion of your inheritance,"

**19** when you were few in number,  
very few, and guests in it,

**20** and they wandered from nation to nation,  
from one kingdom to another people.

**21** He allowed no one to oppress them;  
He rebuked kings on their account:

**22** "Do not touch My anointed ones,  
and do My prophets no harm."

**23** Sing to the LORD, all the earth;  
proclaim His salvation from day to day.

**24** Declare His glory among the nations,  
His wonders among all the peoples.

**25** For great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised;  
He is to be feared above all gods.

**26** For all the gods of the peoples are idols,  
but the LORD made the heavens.

**27** Splendor and majesty are before Him;  
strength and joy are in His place.

**28** Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the peoples,  
ascribe to the LORD glory and strength.

**29** Ascribe to the LORD the glory of His name;  
bring an offering and come before Him.  
Worship the LORD in the splendor of holiness;

**30** tremble before Him, all the earth.  
The world is established; it shall not be moved.

**31** Let the heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice,  
and let them say among the nations, "The LORD reigns!"

**32** Let the sea roar, and all that fills it;  
let the field exult, and all that is in it.

**33** Then the trees of the forest will sing for joy before the LORD,  
for He comes to judge the earth.

**34** Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good,  
for His loyal love endures forever.

**35** Say also:  
"Save us, O God of our salvation,  
gather us and deliver us from the nations,  
to give thanks to Your holy name  
and to glory in Your praise.

**36** Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel,  
from everlasting to everlasting!"  
Then all the people said "Amen" and praised the LORD.

## The Service Arranged

**37** So David left Asaph and his kinsmen there before the ark of the covenant of the LORD to minister before the ark continually, as each day required,

**38** and Obed-edom and their kinsmen, sixty-eight; Obed-edom son of Jeduthun and Hosah were to be gatekeepers.

**39** He left Zadok the priest and his kinsmen the priests before the dwelling of the LORD at the high place that was at Gibeon,

**40** to offer burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of burnt offering continually, morning and evening, according to all that is written in the instruction of the LORD that He commanded Israel.

**41** With them were Heman and Jeduthun and the rest of those chosen and designated by name to give thanks to the LORD, for His loyal love endures forever.

**42** Heman and Jeduthun had with them trumpets and cymbals for those who were to sound them, and the instruments for the songs of God. The sons of Jeduthun were to be at the gate.

**43** Then all the people went away, each to his house, and David returned to bless his household.

## Notes

- **vv8–36 and the Psalms** — David's song is made of three psalms: vv8–22 are Psalm 105:1–15, vv23–33 most of Psalm 96, and vv34–36 the opening and closing lines of Psalm 106 (106:1, 47–48).
- **v4 "to bring to remembrance, to thank and to praise"** — *le-hazkir*, the word in the headings of Psalms 38 and 70, *to bring to remembrance*.
- **v13 "O seed of Israel His servant"** — Psalm 105:6 has *O seed of Abraham His servant*.
- **v15 "Remember His covenant forever"** — Psalm 105:8 has *He has remembered His covenant forever*, of God; here the people are told to remember it. In v19 Chronicles has *when you were few in number* where the psalm has *when they were few* (Psalm 105:12).
- **v19 "very few, and guests in it"** — *gerim*, **guests**: the patriarchs living in Canaan without owning it.
- **v22 "Do not touch My anointed ones, and do My prophets no harm"** — *meshichai*, **My anointed ones**, said of the patriarchs; Abraham is called *a prophet* (Genesis 20:7).
- **v27 "strength and joy are in His place"** — Psalm 96:6 has *strength and beauty are in His sanctuary*. In David's day there is no sanctuary yet; the ark stands in a tent.
- **v35 "gather us and deliver us from the nations"** — the last lines of Psalm 106 (106:47), a prayer of exiles, in David's song at the ark's coming.
- **vv34, 41 "for His loyal love endures forever"** — *ki le-olam chasdo*, from *chesed*, **loyal love**: the refrain of Psalms 106 and 136 and the words the singers are appointed to say (v41).
- **vv37–42 "before the ark… before the dwelling of the LORD at the high place that was at Gibeon"** — two places of worship: the ark in its tent in Jerusalem with Asaph's singers, and the dwelling of Moses with the altar of burnt offering at Gibeon with Zadok. Chronicles names Gibeon again where Solomon goes to sacrifice, *for the tent of meeting of God was there* (2 Chronicles 1:3).

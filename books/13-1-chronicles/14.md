# 1 Chronicles 14

## David's House

**1** Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David, with cedar wood, stonemasons and carpenters, to build him a house.

**2** David knew that the LORD had established him as king over Israel, for his kingdom was highly exalted for the sake of His people Israel.

**3** David took more wives in Jerusalem, and David fathered more sons and daughters.

**4** These are the names of the children whom he had in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan and Solomon,

**5** Ibhar, Elishua, Elpelet,

**6** Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,

**7** Elishama, Beeliada and Eliphelet.

## Victory over the Philistines

**8** When the Philistines heard that David had been anointed king over all Israel, all the Philistines went up to seek David. David heard of it and went out against them.

**9** The Philistines had come and made a raid in the Valley of Rephaim.

**10** David inquired of God, "Shall I go up against the Philistines? Will You give them into my hand?" The LORD said to him, "Go up, and I will give them into your hand."

**11** So they went up to Baal-perazim, and David struck them there. David said, "God has broken through my enemies by my hand like a breakthrough of waters." So they called the name of that place Baal-perazim.

**12** They left their gods there, and David gave the command, and they were burned with fire.

**13** The Philistines raided the valley yet again.

**14** David inquired again of God, and God said to him, "You shall not go up after them. Go around and away from them, and come against them opposite the balsam trees.

**15** And it shall come to pass, when you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, that you shall go out to battle, for God has gone out before you to strike the army of the Philistines."

**16** David did as God commanded him, and they struck down the army of the Philistines from Gibeon to Gezer.

**17** David's fame went out into all the lands, and the LORD put the fear of him on all the nations.

## Notes

- **vv1–17 and 2 Samuel 5:11–25** — Samuel tells these events before the ark is moved at all; Chronicles tells them between the ark's stay with Obed-edom (13:14) and its coming to Jerusalem (chapter 15).
- **v1 "Hiram king of Tyre"** — written *Chiram*, read *Churam*. The text keeps **Hiram**, the name as written here and as Kings has it (1 Kings 5:1); Chronicles spells it *Huram* elsewhere (2 Chronicles 2:3).
- **v3 "David took more wives"** — Samuel has *more concubines and wives* (2 Samuel 5:13); Chronicles names only wives.
- **v7 "Beeliada"** — *Be'elyada*, **Baal knows**; the list in 3:8 and Samuel's (2 Samuel 5:16) have *Eliada*, **God knows**. Here the form with *Baal*, **lord**, stands.
- **v11 "God has broken through my enemies… like a breakthrough of waters… Baal-perazim"** — *parats*, the verb of 13:11, where *the LORD had broken out against Uzza*. The same God who broke out against David's men now breaks out against his enemies; *Baal-perazim*, **lord of breakthroughs**.
- **v12 "David gave the command, and they were burned with fire"** — Samuel says *David and his men carried them away* (2 Samuel 5:21). Chronicles has David do what the law commands: *The carved images of their gods you shall burn with fire* (Deuteronomy 7:25).
- **vv10, 14 "David inquired of God"** — *va-yish'al be-Elohim*, the verb Chronicles used of Saul, who *asked a ghost*, *sho'el ba-ov* (10:13). David asks God, twice in one chapter.
- **vv14–15 "the balsam trees… the sound of marching"** — *ha-bekha'im*, a tree named only here and in Samuel's account; *the sound of marching*, *qol ha-tse'adah*, is the sign that *God has gone out before you*.
- **v16 "from Gibeon to Gezer"** — Samuel has *from Geba* (2 Samuel 5:25).
- **v17 "the LORD put the fear of him on all the nations"** — the promise to Israel in the wilderness: *This day I will begin to put the dread and fear of you on the peoples under the whole heaven* (Deuteronomy 2:25).

# 1 Chronicles 5

## Reuben

**1** The sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel — he was the firstborn, but because he defiled his father's bed, his birthright was given to the sons of Joseph son of Israel, so that he is not enrolled as firstborn.

**2** Though Judah grew strong among his brothers and a leader came from him, the birthright belonged to Joseph —

**3** the sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron and Carmi.

**4** The sons of Joel: Shemaiah his son, Gog his son, Shimei his son,

**5** Micah his son, Reaiah his son, Baal his son,

**6** Beerah his son, whom Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria carried into exile; he was a leader of the Reubenites.

**7** His kinsmen by their clans, when the genealogy of their generations was recorded: the chief, Jeiel, and Zechariah,

**8** and Bela son of Azaz, son of Shema, son of Joel, who lived in Aroer and as far as Nebo and Baal-meon.

**9** To the east he lived as far as the entrance of the wilderness this side of the river Euphrates, because their livestock had multiplied in the land of Gilead.

**10** In the days of Saul they made war on the Hagrites, who fell into their hand, and they lived in their tents throughout all the region east of Gilead.

## Gad

**11** The sons of Gad lived over against them in the land of Bashan as far as Salecah:

**12** Joel the chief, Shapham the second, Janai and Shaphat in Bashan.

**13** Their kinsmen by their fathers' houses: Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jacan, Zia and Eber, seven.

**14** These were the sons of Abihail son of Huri, son of Jaroah, son of Gilead, son of Michael, son of Jeshishai, son of Jahdo, son of Buz;

**15** Ahi son of Abdiel, son of Guni, was chief of their fathers' house.

**16** They lived in Gilead, in Bashan and its villages, and in all the pasturelands of Sharon to their limits.

**17** All of them were enrolled by genealogy in the days of Jotham king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam king of Israel.

## The War with the Hagrites

**18** The sons of Reuben, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh had valiant men who carried shield and sword, drew the bow and were trained for war, forty-four thousand seven hundred and sixty, ready for battle.

**19** They made war on the Hagrites, Jetur, Naphish and Nodab.

**20** They were helped against them, and the Hagrites and all who were with them were given into their hand, for they cried out to God in the battle, and He let Himself be pleaded with by them, because they trusted in Him.

**21** They took away their livestock: fifty thousand of their camels, two hundred and fifty thousand sheep, two thousand donkeys and a hundred thousand people.

**22** Many fell slain, because the war was of God. They lived in their place until the exile.

## The Half-Tribe of Manasseh

**23** The sons of the half-tribe of Manasseh lived in the land; they were numerous from Bashan to Baal-hermon, Senir and Mount Hermon.

**24** These were the heads of their fathers' houses: Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah and Jahdiel, men of worth, famous men, heads of their fathers' houses.

**25** But they broke faith with the God of their fathers and gave themselves to the gods of the peoples of the land, whom God had destroyed before them.

**26** So the God of Israel stirred up the spirit of Pul king of Assyria, the spirit of Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria, and he carried them into exile, the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh, and brought them to Halah, Habor, Hara and the river Gozan, to this day.

## Notes

- **v26 and the Hebrew numbering** — the Hebrew continues this chapter with fifteen more verses (5:27–41), which the English numbers 6:1–15.
- **vv1–2 "because he defiled his father's bed, his birthright was given to the sons of Joseph"** — Reuben *lay with Bilhah his father's secondary wife* (Genesis 35:22), and Jacob's blessing took away his first place: *you went up to your father's bed* (Genesis 49:4). Jacob had claimed Joseph's two sons as his own, *as Reuben and Simeon* (Genesis 48:5). The birthright goes to Joseph, the leader to Judah: *nagid*, the word of David's kingship.
- **v6 "Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria"** — Chronicles' spelling of *Tiglath-pileser* (2 Kings 15:29).
- **vv10, 19–20 "the Hagrites, Jetur, Naphish and Nodab"** — *Jetur* and *Naphish* are named among the sons of Ishmael (1:31); *Hagrites* recalls Hagar, Ishmael's mother.
- **v20 "He let Himself be pleaded with by them"** — *ve-ne'tor lahem*, the rare verb of Isaac's prayer for Rebekah: *The LORD let Himself be pleaded with* (Genesis 25:21). Here it is answered *because they trusted in Him*.
- **v24 "men of worth"** — *gibborei chayil*, as the clan heads of Issachar, Benjamin and Asher are called (7:2, 7, 40).
- **v25 "gave themselves to the gods of the peoples"** — *va-yiznu acharei*, **whored after**, the word for prostitution, and the words of the warning: *they will give themselves to their gods* (Exodus 34:15).
- **v26 "stirred up the spirit of Pul… the spirit of Tilgath-pilneser"** — *ruach*, lowercase: a king's own spirit, stirred by God. Whether the two names are one king or two, the verse does not say; it repeats *the spirit of* for each. Kings names *Pul* (2 Kings 15:19) and then *Tiglath-pileser*, who took the land of Naphtali (2 Kings 15:29).

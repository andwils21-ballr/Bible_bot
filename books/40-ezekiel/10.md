# Ezekiel 10

**1** I looked: on the expanse that was over the heads of the cherubim, something like sapphire stone, like the appearance of the likeness of a throne, appeared above them.

**2** He spoke to the man clothed in linen and said, "Go in between the whirling wheels, underneath the cherub, fill your hands with coals of fire from between the cherubim, and scatter them over the city." He went in as I watched.

**3** The cherubim were standing on the south side of the house when the man went in, and the cloud filled the inner court.

**4** The glory of the LORD rose from the cherub to the threshold of the house; the house was filled with the cloud, and the court was full of the brightness of the glory of the LORD.

**5** The sound of the cherubim's wings was heard as far as the outer court, like the voice of God Almighty when He speaks.

**6** When He commanded the man clothed in linen, "Take fire from between the whirling wheels, from between the cherubim," he went in and stood beside a wheel.

**7** A cherub stretched out his hand from between the cherubim to the fire that was between the cherubim, took some and put it into the hands of the man clothed in linen, who took it and went out.

**8** The cherubim were seen to have the form of a human hand under their wings.

**9** I looked: there were four wheels beside the cherubim, one wheel beside each cherub, and the appearance of the wheels was like the gleam of beryl stone.

**10** As for their appearance, all four had one likeness, as if a wheel were inside a wheel.

**11** When they went, they went in any of their four directions without turning as they went; to the place the head faced, they followed it, without turning as they went.

**12** Their whole body, their backs, their hands and their wings, and the wheels, were full of eyes all around, the wheels that the four of them had.

**13** As for the wheels, they were called in my hearing "the whirling wheels."

**14** Each had four faces: the first was the face of the cherub, the second the face of a human, the third the face of a lion, and the fourth the face of an eagle.

**15** The cherubim rose up. It was the living creature I had seen by the river Chebar.

**16** When the cherubim went, the wheels went beside them; and when the cherubim lifted their wings to rise from the ground, the wheels did not turn from beside them.

**17** When they stood still, these stood still, and when they rose, these rose with them, for the spirit of the living creature was in them.

**18** Then the glory of the LORD went out from the threshold of the house and stood over the cherubim.

**19** The cherubim lifted their wings and rose from the ground as I watched; as they went out, the wheels went with them. They stopped at the entrance of the east gate of the house of the LORD, and the glory of the God of Israel was over them above.

**20** It was the living creature I had seen beneath the God of Israel by the river Chebar, and I knew that they were cherubim.

**21** Each had four faces and each had four wings, and the likeness of human hands under their wings.

**22** As for the likeness of their faces, they were the faces I had seen by the river Chebar, their appearance and themselves. Each went straight ahead.

## Notes

- **vv1, 15, 20 "the cherubim… It was the living creature I had seen by the river Chebar… and I knew that they were cherubim"** — the creatures of chapter 1 are named at last. The *keruvim* were the figures over the ark (Exodus 25:18–22) and in the temple; the vision of the river Chebar was of the same throne-bearers, now seen leaving their house.
- **vv2, 6, 13 "the whirling wheels"** — *galgal*, a second word for the wheels beside *ofan*, from *galal*, "roll." In v13 the wheels are given this name in Ezekiel's hearing; the Greek simply writes it, *Gelgel*.
- **v2 "fill your hands with coals of fire… and scatter them over the city"** — the man with the writing case, who marked the faithful, now carries the fire. The coals come from between the cherubim, from the throne itself.
- **v5 "like the voice of God Almighty when He speaks"** — *El Shaddai*. At the river Chebar the sound was *like the voice of the Almighty* (1:24); here it is heard in the outer court of the temple.
- **v14 "the first was the face of the cherub"** — where 1:10 had *the face of an ox*. The Greek has no v14.
- **vv18–19 "the glory of the LORD went out from the threshold… They stopped at the entrance of the east gate"** — the glory moves from the cherub to the threshold (9:3; 10:4), and now out to the gate; the next step is the mountain east of the city (11:23).

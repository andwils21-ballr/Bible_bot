# Ezekiel 4

**1** "As for you, son of man, take a brick and set it before you, and engrave on it a city, Jerusalem.

**2** Lay siege against it: build a siege wall against it, heap up a ramp against it, set camps against it, and place battering rams against it all around.

**3** As for you, take an iron griddle and set it up as an iron wall between you and the city. Set your face toward it, and it will be under siege, and besiege it. This is a sign for the house of Israel.

**4** As for you, lie on your left side and lay the guilt of the house of Israel on it. For the number of days that you lie on it, you will carry their guilt.

**5** I have assigned you the years of their guilt as a number of days, three hundred and ninety days; so you will carry the guilt of the house of Israel.

**6** When you have completed these, lie a second time, on your right side, and carry the guilt of the house of Judah forty days, a day for each year, a day for each year; I have assigned it to you.

**7** Set your face toward the siege of Jerusalem with your arm bared, and prophesy against it.

**8** I will put cords on you, so that you cannot turn from one side to the other until you have completed the days of your siege.

**9** As for you, take wheat and barley, beans and lentils, millet and spelt, put them in one vessel, and make bread of them for yourself. For the number of days that you lie on your side, three hundred and ninety days, eat it.

**10** The food you eat is to be by weight, twenty shekels a day; eat it at set times.

**11** Drink water by measure, a sixth of a hin; drink it at set times.

**12** Eat it as a barley cake, baking it on human dung in their sight."

**13** The LORD said, "This is how the sons of Israel will eat their bread unclean among the nations where I will drive them."

**14** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! I have never been made unclean. From my youth until now I have never eaten an animal that died on its own or was torn by beasts, and no foul meat has ever entered my mouth."

**15** He said to me, "See, I give you cow dung instead of human dung, and you may prepare your bread on it."

**16** He said to me, "Son of man, I am about to break the staff of bread in Jerusalem. They will eat bread by weight and in anxiety, and drink water by measure and in horror,

**17** so that they will lack bread and water, and look at one another in horror, and rot away in their guilt."

## Notes

- **vv4–6 "three hundred and ninety days… forty days, a day for each year"** — the sentence in the wilderness counted the other way: *By the number of the days you scouted out the land, forty days — a year for each day — you shall carry your guilt forty years* (Numbers 14:34). Here the years of guilt are lived out as days, and *carry the guilt* is the same phrase. The Greek has *one hundred and ninety days* in vv5 and 9.
- **v4 "lay the guilt of the house of Israel on it… you will carry their guilt"** — *nasa avon*, the law's phrase for bearing the guilt of a wrong (Leviticus 7:18; 17:16). The prophet carries the people's guilt in his own body, lying on one side for more than a year.

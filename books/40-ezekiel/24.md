# Ezekiel 24

**1** In the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, write down the name of the day, this very day. The king of Babylon has laid siege to Jerusalem this very day.

**3** Speak a parable to the rebellious house, and say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says:  
Set on the pot, set it on,  
and pour water into it too;

**4** gather into it its pieces,  
every good piece, thigh and shoulder;  
fill it with choice bones.

**5** Take the choicest of the flock,  
and pile the wood under it;  
boil it well,  
and let its bones cook in it.

**6** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says:  
Woe to the city of bloodshed,  
the pot whose rust is in it,  
whose rust has not gone out of it!  
Take it out piece by piece;  
no lot has fallen on it.

**7** For her blood is in her midst;  
she put it on the bare rock;  
she did not pour it on the ground  
to cover it with dust.

**8** To stir up wrath, to take vengeance,  
I have put her blood on the bare rock,  
so that it is not covered.

**9** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says:  
Woe to the city of bloodshed!  
I in turn will make the pile great.

**10** Heap on the wood, kindle the fire,  
cook the meat through, mix in the spices,  
and let the bones be charred.

**11** Then set it empty on its coals,  
so that it grows hot and its copper glows,  
and its uncleanness melts in it,  
and its rust is consumed.

**12** It has worn itself out with toil;  
its thick rust does not go out of it.  
Into the fire with its rust!

**13** In your uncleanness is lewdness. Because I cleansed you and you were not clean from your uncleanness, you will not be clean again until I have satisfied My wrath on you.

**14** I, the LORD, have spoken; it is coming, and I will do it. I will not hold back, I will not spare, and I will not relent. According to your ways and your deeds they will judge you, declares the Lord GOD."

## The Delight of Your Eyes

**15** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**16** "Son of man, I am about to take from you the delight of your eyes with a stroke; yet you shall not mourn or weep, nor shall your tears come.

**17** Groan in silence; make no mourning for the dead. Bind on your turban and put your sandals on your feet; do not cover your upper lip or eat the bread of mourners."

**18** So I spoke to the people in the morning, and in the evening my wife died. In the morning I did as I was commanded.

**19** The people said to me, "Will you not tell us what these things mean for us, that you are acting this way?"

**20** I said to them, "The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**21** 'Say to the house of Israel: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to profane My sanctuary, the pride of your strength, the delight of your eyes and the longing of your soul; and your sons and your daughters whom you left behind will fall by the sword.

**22** You will do as I have done: you will not cover your upper lip or eat the bread of mourners.

**23** Your turbans will be on your heads and your sandals on your feet; you will not mourn or weep, but you will rot away in your iniquities and groan to one another.

**24** So Ezekiel will be a sign to you; you will do all that he has done. When it comes, you will know that I am the Lord GOD.'

**25** As for you, son of man, on the day I take from them their stronghold, their joy and glory, the delight of their eyes and the longing of their soul, their sons and their daughters,

**26** on that day a fugitive will come to you to make it heard.

**27** On that day your mouth will be opened to the fugitive, and you will speak and no longer be mute. So you will be a sign to them, and they will know that I am the LORD."

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "In the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth day… The king of Babylon has laid siege to Jerusalem this very day"** — the date Kings gives for the beginning of the siege: *in the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month… Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came… against Jerusalem* (2 Kings 25:1). Ezekiel, in Babylon, is told to write the day down.
- **vv3–6 "Set on the pot… every good piece, thigh and shoulder"** — the princes' saying of 11:3, *This city is the pot, and we are the meat*, turned on them: the choice pieces will be boiled and taken out one by one, and *no lot has fallen on it*: none is spared by lot.
- **vv7–8 "she did not pour it on the ground to cover it with dust… I have put her blood on the bare rock, so that it is not covered"** — the law said the blood of a hunted animal must be poured out and covered with earth (Leviticus 17:13). Jerusalem left the blood she shed in the open, and God leaves it there as evidence.
- **vv11–12 "set it empty on its coals… its thick rust does not go out of it"** — the empty pot is heated to burn off its rust, and even that fails: what is ingrained in the city cannot be scoured out.
- **vv16–18 "I am about to take from you the delight of your eyes… and in the evening my wife died"** — the only time the book speaks of Ezekiel's wife, and then only in her death. He is forbidden to mourn, as Jeremiah was told, *Do not enter the house of mourning* (Jeremiah 16:5).
- **vv17, 22 "do not cover your upper lip or eat the bread of mourners"** — covering the lip was a sign of mourning and shame, as for the one with the blight, who *shall cover his upper lip and call out, "Unclean!"* (Leviticus 13:45). *The bread of mourners* is *lechem anashim*, "the bread of men," taken from the customs around it as the meal others bring to a house of mourning.
- **vv21, 25 "the delight of your eyes"** — the phrase used of his wife is used of the temple. As he did not mourn her, they will not mourn it; they will *rot away in their iniquities and groan to one another*.
- **vv26–27 "a fugitive will come to you… your mouth will be opened"** — the silence laid on him at 3:26 ends when the news of the city's fall arrives, which the book tells at 33:21–22.

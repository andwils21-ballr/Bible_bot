# Ezekiel 30

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, prophesy and say: This is what the Lord GOD says: Wail, 'Alas for the day!'

**3** For the day is near, the day of the LORD is near; a day of cloud, it will be the time of the nations.

**4** A sword will come against Egypt, and anguish will be in Cush, when the slain fall in Egypt, and her multitude is taken away and her foundations are torn down.

**5** Cush and Put and Lud, all the mixed peoples and Cub, and the people of the land of the covenant will fall with them by the sword.

**6** This is what the LORD says: Those who support Egypt will fall, and the pride of her strength will come down. From Migdol to Syene they will fall in her by the sword, declares the Lord GOD.

**7** They will be desolate among desolate lands, and her cities will lie among ruined cities.

**8** They will know that I am the LORD, when I set fire to Egypt and all her helpers are broken.

**9** On that day messengers will go out from Me in ships to terrify Cush in her security, and anguish will come on them on the day of Egypt; for it is coming.

**10** This is what the Lord GOD says: I will put an end to the multitude of Egypt by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.

**11** He and his people with him, the most ruthless of nations, will be brought in to destroy the land; they will draw their swords against Egypt and fill the land with the slain.

**12** I will make the streams dry ground and sell the land into the hand of evil men; I will make the land and all that fills it desolate by the hand of strangers. I, the LORD, have spoken.

**13** This is what the Lord GOD says: I will destroy the idols and put an end to the worthless gods in Memphis. There will no longer be a prince from the land of Egypt, and I will put fear in the land of Egypt.

**14** I will make Pathros desolate, set fire to Zoan, and carry out judgments on No.

**15** I will pour out My wrath on Sin, the stronghold of Egypt, and cut off the multitude of No.

**16** I will set fire to Egypt; Sin will writhe in anguish, No will be broken open, and Memphis will face foes by day.

**17** The young men of Aven and of Pi-beseth will fall by the sword, and the cities themselves will go into captivity.

**18** At Tahpanhes the day will grow dark, when I break there the yoke bars of Egypt and the pride of her strength comes to an end in her. A cloud will cover her, and her daughters will go into captivity.

**19** So I will carry out judgments on Egypt, and they will know that I am the LORD."

## The Broken Arm

**20** And it came to pass in the eleventh year, in the first month, on the seventh of the month, that the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**21** "Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and it has not been bound up to heal it, nor a bandage put on to bind it and make it strong enough to hold a sword.

**22** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt. I will break his arms, both the strong one and the broken one, and make the sword fall from his hand.

**23** I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them through the countries.

**24** I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon and put My sword in his hand, but I will break the arms of Pharaoh, and he will groan before him like a man mortally wounded.

**25** I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, but the arms of Pharaoh will fall; and they will know that I am the LORD, when I put My sword into the hand of the king of Babylon and he stretches it out against the land of Egypt.

**26** I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them through the countries; and they will know that I am the LORD."

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "Wail, 'Alas for the day!' For the day is near, the day of the LORD is near"** — the cry Isaiah raised over Babylon, *Wail, for the day of the LORD is near* (Isaiah 13:6), and that Ezekiel raised over Israel's land, *the day is near* (7:7). The day that came on Jerusalem now comes on Egypt.
- **v5 "Cush and Put and Lud, all the mixed peoples and Cub"** — Egypt's allies and soldiers; Put and Lud served in Tyre's army too (27:10). *Kuv* is found only here, and no one knows who they were. The Greek list differs: *Persians and Cretans and Lydians and Libyans*.
- **v5 "the people of the land of the covenant"** — *benei erets ha-berit*. It can mean the people of Egypt's allied lands, *the land of the treaty*, or the people of the covenant itself: Judeans who had gone down to Egypt (Jeremiah 43:7). The Greek has *the sons of My covenant*.
- **v9 "messengers will go out from Me in ships to terrify Cush"** — Isaiah pictured Cush as the land *which sends envoys by the sea, in vessels of papyrus on the waters* (Isaiah 18:2). Here the messengers go the other way, from the LORD to Cush, to terrify it with the day of Egypt.
- **v13 "the idols… the worthless gods"** — *gillulim*, Ezekiel's word for idols throughout the book, and *elilim*, **nothings**, the word Isaiah used of Egypt's gods, *the idols of Egypt tremble before Him* (Isaiah 19:1). The Greek has *nobles* and *rulers* instead of idols and gods.
- **vv13–18 the cities of Egypt** — the Greek gives them the names its own readers knew: Memphis (*Noph*), Tanis (*Zoan*), Diospolis (*No*, called *Amon of No* in Jeremiah 46:25), Sais (*Sin*), Heliopolis (*Aven*), Bubastis (*Pi-beseth*) and Taphnai (*Tahpanhes*, spelled *Tehaphnehes* in this verse).
- **v17 "Aven"** — the city called *On* in Joseph's story (Genesis 41:45), with the same letters pointed *aven*, **trouble** or **wickedness**.
- **v18 "the day will grow dark"** — the Leningrad Codex points the verb *chasakh*, **held back**: *the day will be held back*. With the other dot on the same letter it is *chashakh*, **grow dark**, which the Greek has and the cloud of the same verse supports.
- **v18 "when I break there the yoke bars of Egypt"** — the words of the Exodus: *I broke the bars of your yoke and made you walk upright* (Leviticus 26:13). Now the LORD breaks Egypt's own yoke bars, in Egypt. The Greek reads *the scepters of Egypt*.
- **vv21–25 "the arm of Pharaoh… the arms of the king of Babylon"** — the LORD brought Israel out of Egypt *with an outstretched arm* (Exodus 6:6). Now He breaks Pharaoh's arm and strengthens Babylon's, and puts His own sword in Babylon's hand.

# Ezekiel 14

**1** Some of the elders of Israel came to me and sat before me.

**2** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**3** "Son of man, these men have taken their idols into their hearts and set the stumbling block of their iniquity before their faces. Should I let Myself be consulted by them?

**4** Therefore speak to them and say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: Any man of the house of Israel who takes his idols into his heart and sets the stumbling block of his iniquity before his face, and then comes to the prophet — I the LORD will answer him Myself, for the multitude of his idols,

**5** so that I may lay hold of the house of Israel by their own hearts, for they have all turned away from Me through their idols.

**6** Therefore say to the house of Israel: This is what the Lord GOD says: Turn back, and turn away from your idols; turn your faces away from all your detestable things.

**7** For any man of the house of Israel, or of the guests who live as guests in Israel, who separates himself from Me, takes his idols into his heart and sets the stumbling block of his iniquity before his face, and then comes to the prophet to inquire of Me for himself — I the LORD will answer him Myself.

**8** I will set My face against that man and make him a sign and a byword, and I will cut him off from among My people; and you will know that I am the LORD.

**9** If the prophet is enticed and speaks a word, it is I, the LORD, who have enticed that prophet, and I will stretch out My hand against him and destroy him from among My people Israel.

**10** They will carry their guilt: the guilt of the one who inquires will be like the guilt of the prophet,

**11** so that the house of Israel will no longer stray from Me or make themselves unclean with all their transgressions, but they will be My people, and I will be their God, declares the Lord GOD."

**12** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**13** "Son of man, when a land sins against Me by breaking faith, and I stretch out My hand against it, break its staff of bread, send famine on it, and cut off from it human and beast,

**14** even if these three men were in it, Noah, Daniel and Job, they would save only their own lives by their righteousness, declares the Lord GOD.

**15** If I make wild beasts pass through the land and they rob it of its children, and it becomes a desolation with no one passing through because of the beasts,

**16** even if these three men were in it, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, they would not save sons or daughters; they alone would be saved, and the land would be a desolation.

**17** Or if I bring a sword on that land and say, 'Let a sword pass through the land,' and I cut off from it human and beast,

**18** even if these three men were in it, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, they would not save sons or daughters; they alone would be saved.

**19** Or if I send pestilence into that land and pour out My wrath on it in blood, to cut off from it human and beast,

**20** even if Noah, Daniel and Job were in it, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, they would save neither son nor daughter; they would save only their own lives by their righteousness.

**21** For this is what the Lord GOD says: How much more when I send My four terrible judgments against Jerusalem, sword and famine and wild beasts and pestilence, to cut off from it human and beast!

**22** Yet there will be survivors left in it, sons and daughters who will be brought out. They will come out to you, and when you see their ways and their deeds, you will be comforted for the disaster I have brought on Jerusalem, for all that I have brought on it.

**23** They will comfort you when you see their ways and their deeds, and you will know that it was not for nothing that I did all that I did in it, declares the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "Some of the elders of Israel came to me and sat before me… Should I let Myself be consulted by them?"** — *ha-iddaresh iddaresh*, the verb doubled: the plain question is whether God will answer them at all. They come to inquire with their idols already in their hearts.
- **vv4, 7 "I the LORD will answer him Myself"** — *na'aneti lo bi*, "I will be made to answer him, by Myself." The answer the idolater gets is not an oracle through the prophet but God's own act against him (v8).
- **v9 "If the prophet is enticed… it is I, the LORD, who have enticed that prophet"** — *pittah*, the verb of Micaiah's vision, where the LORD asks, *Who will entice Ahab, so that he may go up and fall at Ramoth-gilead?* (1 Kings 22:20). The prophet who answers an idolater is caught in the same judgment as the one who asked (v10).
- **vv14, 20 "Noah, Daniel and Job"** — three men whose righteousness is named in Scripture: *Noah was a righteous man, whole in his generations* (Genesis 6:9); Job *was blameless and upright* (Job 1:1). The name of the second is written *Dan'el* and read *Daniel*. Even these, God says, could save no one but themselves.
- **vv13–21 "famine… wild beasts… a sword… pestilence… My four terrible judgments"** — the four of the covenant curses (Leviticus 26:22–26), taken one at a time in vv13–20 and then all four together against Jerusalem.
- **vv22–23 "you will be comforted… you will know that it was not for nothing"** — when the survivors reach Babylon, the exiles will see *their ways and their deeds*, and will understand why the city fell; that is the comfort the verse promises.

# Ezekiel 21

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, set your face toward Jerusalem, preach against the sanctuaries, and prophesy against the land of Israel.

**3** Say to the land of Israel: This is what the LORD says: I am against you. I will draw My sword out of its sheath and cut off from you the righteous and the wicked.

**4** Because I will cut off from you the righteous and the wicked, My sword will go out of its sheath against all flesh from the south to the north.

**5** All flesh will know that I, the LORD, have drawn My sword out of its sheath; it will not go back again.

**6** As for you, son of man, groan! Groan before their eyes, with your strength breaking and with bitterness.

**7** And it shall come to pass, when they say to you, 'Why are you groaning?' that you shall say, 'Because of the news that is coming. Every heart will melt, every hand hang limp, every spirit grow faint, and every knee turn to water. It is coming, and it will happen, declares the Lord GOD.'"

## The Song of the Sword

**8** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**9** "Son of man, prophesy and say: This is what the Lord says. Say:  
A sword, a sword is sharpened,  
and polished too,

**10** sharpened for slaughter,  
polished to flash like lightning!  
Or shall we rejoice?  
The staff of my son despises every tree.

**11** He gave it to be polished,  
to be grasped in the hand;  
it is sharpened, the sword, and it is polished,  
to be put into the hand of the killer.

**12** Cry out and wail, son of man,  
for it is against My people;  
it is against all the princes of Israel.  
They are thrown to the sword with My people;  
therefore strike your thigh.

**13** For it is a testing; and what if the staff that despises is no more?  
declares the Lord GOD.

**14** As for you, son of man, prophesy,  
and strike hand against hand.  
Let the sword come down twice, three times,  
the sword for the slain;  
it is the sword for the great slaughter  
that closes in on them,

**15** so that hearts may melt  
and many stumble.  
At all their gates I have set the point of the sword.  
Ah! It is made to flash like lightning;  
it is drawn for slaughter.

**16** Cut to the right;  
set yourself to the left,  
wherever your edge is turned.

**17** I in turn will strike My hand against My hand,  
and I will satisfy My wrath.  
I, the LORD, have spoken."

## The King at the Fork in the Road

**18** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**19** "As for you, son of man, mark out two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to come by; both of them shall come out of one land. Make a signpost; make it at the head of the road to a city.

**20** Mark out a road for the sword to come to Rabbah of the Ammonites, and to Judah, to Jerusalem the fortified.

**21** For the king of Babylon stands at the parting of the way, at the head of the two roads, to practice divination. He shakes the arrows, he consults the teraphim, he looks at the liver.

**22** In his right hand comes the lot for Jerusalem: to set battering rams, to open the mouth for slaughter, to raise the voice with a battle cry, to set battering rams against the gates, to heap up a ramp, to build a siege wall.

**23** To them it will seem a false divination; they have sworn solemn oaths to them. But he brings their iniquity to remembrance, so that they will be taken.

**24** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have made your iniquity remembered, in that your transgressions are uncovered and your sins appear in all your deeds — because you have come to remembrance, you will be taken in hand.

**25** As for you, profane and wicked prince of Israel, whose day has come, the time of the final punishment,

**26** this is what the Lord GOD says: Remove the turban, and take off the crown! Things will not stay as they are: lift up the low, and bring down the high.

**27** A ruin, a ruin, a ruin I will make it! This too shall not be, until he comes to whom the judgment belongs, and I will give it to him.

## Against the Ammonites

**28** As for you, son of man, prophesy and say: This is what the Lord GOD says concerning the Ammonites and their taunts. Say:  
A sword, a sword is drawn,  
polished for slaughter,  
to consume, to flash like lightning,

**29** while they see empty visions for you  
and divine lies for you,  
to lay you on the necks of the wicked slain,  
whose day has come,  
the time of the final punishment.

**30** Put it back in its sheath!  
In the place where you were created,  
in the land of your origin, I will judge you.

**31** I will pour out My indignation on you;  
I will blow on you with the fire of My wrath,  
and give you into the hands of brutish men,  
craftsmen of destruction.

**32** You will be fuel for the fire;  
your blood will be in the middle of the land;  
you will be remembered no more,  
for I, the LORD, have spoken."

## Notes

- **vv1–32 the verse numbers** — the Hebrew begins chapter 21 with the five verses that English Bibles print as 20:45–49, so its 21:6–37 are the English 21:1–32 here, as in Swete's Greek.
- **vv3–4 "cut off from you the righteous and the wicked"** — the sword does not choose; unlike the mark of 9:4, nothing here sets the righteous apart. The Greek softens it to *the unjust and the lawless*.
- **v7 "Every heart will melt, every hand hang limp… every knee turn to water"** — the words of 7:17, *Every hand will hang limp, and every knee will turn to water*, when the news comes.
- **vv9–17 "A sword, a sword is sharpened, and polished too"** — a song of the sword, in short broken lines. Several of them are hard to read in the Hebrew (vv10, 13, 15), and the English keeps them as obscure as they stand. *The staff of my son* (vv10, 13) uses the words of Jacob's blessing of Judah, *my son* and *the staff* (Genesis 49:9–10).
- **v21 "He shakes the arrows, he consults the teraphim, he looks at the liver"** — three ways of asking the gods: drawing marked arrows, consulting household images, and reading the liver of a sacrificed animal. The lot falls on Jerusalem, and God uses the pagan king's omens for His own judgment.
- **vv25–26 "profane and wicked prince of Israel… Remove the turban, and take off the crown"** — Zedekiah, unnamed. *Mitsnefet*, *turban*, is the word for the high priest's turban among *the holy garments* (Exodus 28:4); with the crown, both signs of rule come off.
- **v27 "until he comes to whom the judgment belongs, and I will give it to him"** — *ad bo asher lo ha-mishpat*. The words echo Jacob's blessing, *The staff shall not turn aside from Judah… until Shiloh comes* (Genesis 49:10): the same verb *come*, and *asher lo*, "whose it is." The kingship is overturned until its rightful owner comes.
- **vv28–32 "concerning the Ammonites… Put it back in its sheath!… in the land of your origin, I will judge you"** — Ammon, passed over at the fork in the road (vv19–22), is not forgotten. Whom *Put it back in its sheath* addresses is not said; the next words are spoken to Ammon, *in the land of your origin, I will judge you*.

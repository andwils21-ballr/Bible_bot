# Ezekiel 11

**1** The spirit lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the house of the LORD, which faces east. At the entrance of the gate were twenty-five men, and among them I saw Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, princes of the people.

**2** He said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who devise iniquity and give evil counsel in this city,

**3** who say, 'The time is not near to build houses. This city is the pot, and we are the meat.'

**4** Therefore prophesy against them; prophesy, son of man."

**5** The Spirit of the LORD fell on me, and He said to me, "Say: This is what the LORD says: So you have said, house of Israel; I know what comes up in your minds.

**6** You have multiplied your slain in this city and filled its streets with the slain.

**7** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Your slain whom you have laid in the middle of it, they are the meat, and this city is the pot; but you will be brought out of it.

**8** You have feared the sword, and the sword I will bring on you, declares the Lord GOD.

**9** I will bring you out of it and give you into the hands of strangers, and I will carry out judgments on you.

**10** You will fall by the sword; at the border of Israel I will judge you, and you will know that I am the LORD.

**11** This city will not be your pot, and you will not be the meat in it; at the border of Israel I will judge you.

**12** You will know that I am the LORD, for you have not walked in My statutes or kept My judgments, but have acted by the judgments of the nations around you."

**13** As I was prophesying, Pelatiah son of Benaiah died. Then I fell on my face and cried out with a loud voice, "Ah, Lord GOD! Will You make a complete end of the remnant of Israel?"

**14** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**15** "Son of man, your brothers, your own brothers, your kinsmen who have the right to redeem, and the whole house of Israel, all of them, are those of whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, 'Keep far from the LORD; this land has been given to us as a possession.'

**16** Therefore say: This is what the Lord GOD says: Though I have removed them far off among the nations, and though I have scattered them among the countries, yet I have been to them a small sanctuary in the countries where they have gone.

**17** Therefore say: This is what the Lord GOD says: I will gather you from the peoples and assemble you out of the countries where you have been scattered, and I will give you the land of Israel.

**18** They will go there and remove from it all its loathsome things and all its detestable things.

**19** I will give them one heart, and I will put a new spirit within you; I will remove the heart of stone from their flesh and give them a heart of flesh,

**20** so that they will walk in My statutes and keep My judgments and do them. They will be My people, and I will be their God.

**21** But as for those whose heart goes after the heart of their loathsome things and their detestable things, I will bring their way on their own heads, declares the Lord GOD."

**22** Then the cherubim lifted their wings, with the wheels beside them, and the glory of the God of Israel was over them above.

**23** The glory of the LORD went up from the middle of the city and stood on the mountain that is east of the city.

**24** The spirit lifted me up and brought me to Chaldea, to the exiles, in the vision, by the Spirit of God; and the vision I had seen went up from me.

**25** I told the exiles all the things the LORD had shown me.

## Notes

- **v3 "The time is not near to build houses. This city is the pot, and we are the meat"** — the Hebrew has no question mark: as it stands, it says this is no time for building houses. Jeremiah had told the exiles, *Build houses and live in them* (Jeremiah 29:5). The pot was Jeremiah's picture too, *I see a boiling pot, tilting from the north* (Jeremiah 1:13); the princes take it for themselves: the city is the pot, and they are the meat inside its walls. The Greek reads the first line as a question, *Have not the houses been newly built?*
- **vv7–11 "they are the meat, and this city is the pot; but you will be brought out of it"** — God takes up their proverb: the only meat in the pot is the people they have killed. They will not be kept safe in it, but judged *at the border of Israel*, outside it.
- **v13 "Pelatiah son of Benaiah died… Will You make a complete end of the remnant of Israel?"** — *Pelatiah* means "the LORD rescues." The one named for rescue falls dead while the prophet is speaking, and Ezekiel's second plea follows (9:8).
- **v15 "your kinsmen who have the right to redeem"** — *anshei ge'ullatekha*, the men of your *ge'ullah*, the right of the next of kin to buy back a family's land: *his redeemer who is closest to him shall come and redeem what his brother has sold* (Leviticus 25:25). The Jerusalemites say the exiles are far from the LORD and the land is theirs; God names the exiles as the land's rightful kin. The Greek reads *the men of your captivity* (*golatekha*, one letter different).
- **v16 "yet I have been to them a small sanctuary"** — *le-miqdash me'at*. *Me'at* is "little," and can be "small" or "for a little while." The Greek has *a small sanctuary*. The ones the Jerusalemites call far from the LORD have Him with them in exile, while the temple is being emptied of His glory.
- **vv19–20 "one heart… a new spirit… the heart of stone… a heart of flesh… They will be My people, and I will be their God"** — Jeremiah's promise, *I will give them one heart and one way* (Jeremiah 32:39), and *they will be My people, and I will be their God* (24:7). The Greek has *another heart*, reading *acher* for *echad*, a difference of one letter.
- **vv22–23 "The glory of the LORD went up from the middle of the city and stood on the mountain that is east of the city"** — the last step of its leaving, from cherub to threshold (9:3), to the east gate (10:19), to the mountain east of the city.

# Ezekiel 18

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "What do you mean by using this proverb about the land of Israel, saying,  
'The fathers eat sour grapes,  
and the children's teeth are set on edge'?

**3** As I live, declares the Lord GOD, you shall no longer use this proverb in Israel.

**4** All souls are Mine: the soul of the father as well as the soul of the son is Mine. The soul who sins shall die.

**5** If a man is righteous and does what is just and right,

**6** if he does not eat on the mountains or lift his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, does not defile his neighbor's wife or approach a woman in her impurity,

**7** does not oppress anyone, but gives back the debtor his pledge, commits no robbery, gives his bread to the hungry and covers the naked with clothing,

**8** does not lend at interest or take any increase, keeps his hand from wrong, does true justice between man and man,

**9** walks in My statutes and keeps My judgments by acting faithfully — he is righteous; he shall surely live, declares the Lord GOD.

**10** But if he fathers a son who is violent, who sheds blood and does any of these things

**11** (though the father did none of them), who eats on the mountains and defiles his neighbor's wife,

**12** oppresses the poor and needy, commits robbery, does not give back the pledge, lifts his eyes to the idols, does what is detestable,

**13** lends at interest and takes increase, shall he live? He shall not live. He has done all these detestable things; he shall surely be put to death. His blood will be on his own head.

**14** But if this man fathers a son who sees all the sins his father has done, and sees, and does not do the like,

**15** who does not eat on the mountains or lift his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, does not defile his neighbor's wife,

**16** does not oppress anyone, does not keep a pledge, commits no robbery, gives his bread to the hungry and covers the naked with clothing,

**17** keeps his hand from the poor, takes no interest or increase, does My judgments and walks in My statutes, he shall not die for his father's iniquity; he shall surely live.

**18** As for his father, because he practiced extortion, robbed his brother, and did what is not good among his people, he has died for his iniquity.

**19** Yet you say, 'Why should the son not carry the guilt of the father?' When the son has done what is just and right, and has kept all My statutes and done them, he shall surely live.

**20** The soul who sins shall die. The son shall not carry the guilt of the father, nor the father carry the guilt of the son. The righteousness of the righteous will be on him, and the wickedness of the wicked will be on him.

**21** But if the wicked man turns from all the sins he has committed and keeps all My statutes and does what is just and right, he shall surely live; he shall not die.

**22** None of the transgressions he has committed will be remembered against him; for the righteousness he has done, he shall live.

**23** Do I take any pleasure in the death of the wicked, declares the Lord GOD, and not rather that he should turn from his ways and live?

**24** But when a righteous man turns from his righteousness and does wrong, doing all the detestable things the wicked man does, shall he live? None of the righteous deeds he has done will be remembered; for the treachery he has committed and the sin he has sinned, for them he shall die.

**25** Yet you say, 'The way of the Lord is not fair.' Hear now, house of Israel: Is My way not fair? Is it not your ways that are not fair?

**26** When a righteous man turns from his righteousness and does wrong, and dies for it, he dies for the wrong he has done.

**27** When a wicked man turns from the wickedness he has done and does what is just and right, he will save his life.

**28** Because he considered and turned from all the transgressions he had committed, he shall surely live; he shall not die.

**29** Yet the house of Israel says, 'The way of the Lord is not fair.' Are My ways not fair, house of Israel? Is it not your ways that are not fair?

**30** Therefore I will judge you, house of Israel, each according to his ways, declares the Lord GOD. Turn, and turn away from all your transgressions, so that iniquity will not be your stumbling block.

**31** Throw away from you all the transgressions you have committed, and make yourselves a new heart and a new spirit. Why will you die, house of Israel?

**32** For I take no pleasure in the death of anyone who dies, declares the Lord GOD. So turn, and live."

## Notes

- **vv2–4 "The fathers eat sour grapes, and the children's teeth are set on edge"** — the proverb Jeremiah too said would end: *In those days they will no longer say, "The fathers have eaten sour grapes, and the children's teeth are set on edge"* (Jeremiah 31:29). Jeremiah put its end in the days to come; Ezekiel forbids it now.
- **vv23, 32 "Do I take any pleasure in the death of the wicked… So turn, and live"** — the chapter turns from law to plea. The three generations show that no one is bound by his father's guilt, and the last verses say why God makes the case at all.
- **v31 "make yourselves a new heart and a new spirit"** — in 11:19 God says *I will give them one heart, and I will put a new spirit within you*; here the people are told to make one. The book holds both.

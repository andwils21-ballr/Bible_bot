# Ezekiel 41

## The Nave and the Most Holy Place

**1** Then he brought me to the nave and measured the jambs: six cubits wide on one side and six cubits wide on the other, the width of the tent.

**2** The width of the entrance was ten cubits, and the sides of the entrance were five cubits on one side and five cubits on the other. He measured its length: forty cubits; and its width: twenty cubits.

**3** Then he went inside and measured the jambs of the entrance: two cubits; the entrance: six cubits; and the width of the entrance: seven cubits.

**4** He measured its length: twenty cubits; and its width: twenty cubits, across the end of the nave. He said to me, "This is the Most Holy Place."

## The Side Chambers

**5** He measured the wall of the house: six cubits thick; and the width of the side chambers: four cubits, all around the house.

**6** The side chambers were one above another, thirty-three in all. There were ledges in the wall of the house all around for the side chambers, so that they would be supported, and not be supported by the wall of the house.

**7** The side chambers grew wider as they went up, story by story, for the structure around the house went up story by story all around the house; so the house was wider at the top, and one went up from the lowest story through the middle one to the top.

**8** I saw that the house had a raised platform all around; the foundations of the side chambers were a full reed, six cubits, to the joint.

**9** The thickness of the outer wall of the side chambers was five cubits. The open space between the side chambers of the house

**10** and the chambers was twenty cubits wide, all around the house on every side.

**11** The doors of the side chambers opened onto the open space, one door toward the north and one door toward the south; and the width of the open space was five cubits all around.

## The Building on the West

**12** The building that faced the separate yard on the west side was seventy cubits wide; the wall of the building was five cubits thick all around, and its length ninety cubits.

**13** He measured the house: a hundred cubits long; and the separate yard and the building with its walls: a hundred cubits long.

**14** The width of the front of the house and of the separate yard on the east was a hundred cubits.

**15** He measured the length of the building facing the separate yard at its rear, with its galleries on either side: a hundred cubits. The inner nave and the porches of the court,

**16** the thresholds, the recessed windows and the galleries all around the three of them, facing the threshold, were paneled with wood all around, from the floor up to the windows — and the windows were covered —

**17** above the entrance, to the inner house and outside, and on all the wall all around, inside and outside, by measure.

## Cherubim and Palm Trees

**18** It was carved with cherubim and palm trees, a palm tree between cherub and cherub. Each cherub had two faces:

**19** a human face toward the palm tree on one side, and a young lion's face toward the palm tree on the other. They were carved on the whole house all around.

**20** From the floor to above the entrance, cherubim and palm trees were carved, and on the wall of the nave.

**21** The doorposts of the nave were square. As for the front of the holy place, its appearance was like this:

**22** an altar of wood, three cubits high and two cubits long; its corners, its length and its sides were of wood. He said to me, "This is the table that is before the LORD."

**23** The nave had a double door, and so did the holy place.

**24** Each door had two leaves, two turning leaves: two for one door and two leaves for the other.

**25** On the doors of the nave were carved cherubim and palm trees, like those carved on the walls; and there was a wooden canopy on the front of the porch outside.

**26** There were recessed windows and palm trees on either side, on the sides of the porch; so also the side chambers of the house and the canopies.

## Notes

- **v1 "the width of the tent"** — *rochav ha-ohel*, the word for the tent of the wilderness. The Greek does not have the phrase. It stands as the Hebrew has it; what *the tent* measures here is not said.
- **vv3–4 "Then he went inside… This is the Most Holy Place"** — only the man goes in (*he went inside*), and Ezekiel, a priest (1:3), is told what the room is; the law let only Aaron enter *inside the veil* (Leviticus 16:2). The room is twenty cubits by twenty, the size of Solomon's inner sanctuary, *the Most Holy Place* (1 Kings 6:16, 20).
- **vv6–11 the side chambers** — *tsela'ot*, the word of Solomon's *side chambers* (1 Kings 6:5). The number in v6, *shalosh u-sheloshim pe'amim*, **three and thirty times**, and the joints and ledges of vv6–9 are obscure in the Hebrew, and the rendering keeps close to it. The Greek reads *thirty-three, twice*.
- **v12 "the separate yard"** — *ha-gizrah*, **the cut-off place**, a space set apart behind the house, with a large building on it.
- **vv13–15 "a hundred cubits"** — the house with its yard is a hundred cubits each way, as the inner court is (40:47). The plan is built of squares.
- **vv18–19 "Each cherub had two faces"** — the living creatures of the first vision had four, *the face of a human… the face of a lion… the face of an ox… the face of an eagle* (1:10). Carved on a flat wall, each cherub shows two, the human and the young lion, one turned toward each palm.
- **v20 "the wall of the nave"** — in the Hebrew the scribes set dots over the letters of *ha-heikhal*, **the nave**, as they did over *kissed him* when Esau met Jacob (Genesis 33:4).
- **v22 "an altar of wood… the table that is before the LORD"** — the same piece is called an altar and a table. In the tent there was an altar for incense, with horns (Exodus 30:1–2), and a table for *the bread of the Presence, before Me always* (Exodus 25:30). The Greek gives this altar *horns*, where the Hebrew has *corners*.
- **vv23–24 "Each door had two leaves"** — as in Solomon's house: *The two leaves of the one door turned on pivots* (1 Kings 6:34).

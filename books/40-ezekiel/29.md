# Ezekiel 29

**1** In the tenth year, in the tenth month, on the twelfth of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, set your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against all Egypt.

**3** Speak and say: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am against you, Pharaoh king of Egypt, the great monster lying among his streams, who has said, 'My Nile is my own; I made it for myself.'

**4** I will put hooks in your jaws and make the fish of your streams stick to your scales. I will haul you up from among your streams, with all the fish of your streams sticking to your scales.

**5** I will fling you into the wilderness, you and all the fish of your streams. You will fall on the open field, and no one will gather you up or collect you. I have given you as food to the beasts of the earth and the birds of the heavens.

**6** All who live in Egypt will know that I am the LORD, because they have been a staff of reed to the house of Israel.

**7** When they took hold of you with the hand, you splintered and tore open every shoulder; when they leaned on you, you broke and crushed all their loins.

**8** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to bring a sword against you, and I will cut off man and beast from you.

**9** The land of Egypt will become a desolation and a ruin, and they will know that I am the LORD. Because he said, 'The Nile is mine; I made it,'

**10** therefore I am against you and against your streams, and I will make the land of Egypt an utter ruin and a desolation, from Migdol to Syene, as far as the border of Cush.

**11** No foot of man will pass through it, and no foot of beast will pass through it, and no one will live in it for forty years.

**12** I will make the land of Egypt a desolation among desolate lands, and her cities will be a desolation among ruined cities for forty years. I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them through the countries.

**13** For this is what the Lord GOD says: At the end of forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the peoples among whom they were scattered.

**14** I will restore the fortunes of Egypt and bring them back to the land of Pathros, the land of their origin, and there they will be a lowly kingdom.

**15** It will be the lowliest of the kingdoms and will never again lift itself up over the nations. I will make them so few that they will not rule over the nations.

**16** It will never again be the confidence of the house of Israel, bringing their iniquity to remembrance when they turn to them for help; and they will know that I am the Lord GOD."

## Egypt, the Wages for Tyre

**17** And it came to pass in the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, on the first of the month, that the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**18** "Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon worked his army hard against Tyre; every head was rubbed bald and every shoulder worn raw. Yet he and his army got no wages from Tyre for the work he did against it.

**19** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to give the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. He will carry off its multitude, take its spoil and seize its plunder, and it will be the wages for his army.

**20** As the pay for his labor I have given him the land of Egypt, because they acted for Me, declares the Lord GOD.

**21** On that day I will make a horn sprout for the house of Israel, and I will open your mouth among them; and they will know that I am the LORD."

## Notes

- **v1 "In the tenth year"** — counted from King Jehoiachin's exile (1:2). The oracle is dated a year before the Tyre oracle of 26:1, *the eleventh year*: the oracles against the nations are grouped by nation, not by date. The Greek dates it *the twelfth year… on the first of the month*.
- **v3 "the great monster"** — *ha-tannim ha-gadol*, the word of *the great sea-monsters* God created on the fifth day (Genesis 1:21), and of Aaron's staff, which *will become a serpent*, a *tannin*, before Pharaoh (Exodus 7:9). Now Pharaoh is the monster, lying in the Nile, and the LORD hauls him out with hooks like a fish.
- **vv3, 9 "My Nile is my own; I made it for myself… The Nile is mine; I made it"** — v3's verb is *asitini*, *made* with the ending *me*. It can be *I made it for myself*, the boast of v9 with *for myself* added, or *I made myself*: Pharaoh claiming to be his own maker, as the ruler of Tyre claimed *I am a god* (28:2). v9 repeats the boast without the ending. The Greek has *The rivers are mine, and I made them*.
- **vv6–7 "a staff of reed"** — the picture the Assyrian envoy used to Hezekiah: *the staff of this crushed reed, in Egypt, which will pierce the hand of any man who leans on it* (2 Kings 18:21; Isaiah 36:6). Judah's king turned to Egypt against Babylon as well (17:15), and Pharaoh did not help him (17:17).
- **v7 "you broke and crushed all their loins"** — the Hebrew has *ve-ha'amadta lahem kol motnayim*, **you made all their loins stand**, which leaves the second line without the injury the first has (*tore open every shoulder*). The rendering follows the Greek, *you broke all their loins*.
- **v10 "from Migdol to Syene, as far as the border of Cush"** — the whole land, end to end, out to Syene on the border of Cush. Migdol is also named among the camps before the sea (Exodus 14:2). The Greek has *the borders of the Ethiopians*, its name for Cush.
- **v14 "the land of Pathros, the land of their origin"** — Isaiah lists Pathros between Egypt and Cush (Isaiah 11:11), and the table of nations names the *Pathrusim* among the sons of Egypt (Genesis 10:14).
- **v16 "bringing their iniquity to remembrance"** — *mazkir avon*, the phrase of 21:23, *he brings their iniquity to remembrance*. Egypt as Israel's *confidence* kept that iniquity in view, the trust Isaiah condemned: *Woe to those who go down to Egypt for help* (Isaiah 31:1).
- **vv17–18 "in the twenty-seventh year… no wages from Tyre"** — the latest date in the book, sixteen years after the Tyre oracles of chapter 26. The book keeps the oracle that Nebuchadnezzar would come against Tyre (26:7) and records here that his army got nothing for its labor. Egypt is given as its pay, *because they acted for Me*: Jeremiah calls him *Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant* (Jeremiah 25:9).
- **v21 "I will make a horn sprout… I will open your mouth among them"** — *atsmiach qeren*, the words of Psalm 132:17, *There I will make a horn sprout for David*. *Pitchon peh*, **an opening of the mouth**: the prophet had been told he would be mute until a fugitive came with the news (24:26): *On that day your mouth will be opened* (24:27).

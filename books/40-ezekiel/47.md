# Ezekiel 47

## The River

**1** Then he brought me back to the entrance of the house, and water was flowing out from under the threshold of the house toward the east, for the house faced east. The water was flowing down from under the south side of the house, south of the altar.

**2** Then he brought me out by way of the north gate and led me around on the outside to the outer gate that faces east; and the water was trickling out from the south side.

**3** Going out eastward with a line in his hand, the man measured a thousand cubits and led me through the water: water up to the ankles.

**4** He measured a thousand more and led me through the water: water up to the knees. He measured a thousand more and led me through: water up to the waist.

**5** He measured a thousand more: a river I could not cross, for the water had risen, water deep enough to swim in, a river that could not be crossed.

**6** He said to me, "Son of man, have you seen this?" Then he led me back along the bank of the river.

**7** When I came back, there were very many trees on the bank of the river, on both sides.

**8** He said to me, "This water flows out toward the eastern region and goes down into the Arabah, and it enters the sea. When it comes to the sea, to the waters that flow out, the water will be healed.

**9** Wherever the river goes, every living creature that swarms will live, and there will be very many fish, because this water goes there and the sea is healed; everything will live wherever the river goes.

**10** Fishermen will stand beside it from En-gedi to En-eglaim; it will be a place for spreading nets. Their fish will be of every kind, like the fish of the Great Sea, very many.

**11** But its marshes and its swamps will not be healed; they will be left for salt.

**12** On the banks of the river, on both sides, every kind of tree for food will grow. Their leaves will not wither, and their fruit will not fail. Every month they will bear new fruit, because their water flows out from the sanctuary. Their fruit will be for food, and their leaves for healing."

## The Borders of the Land

**13** This is what the Lord GOD says: "These are the borders by which you shall divide the land as an inheritance among the twelve tribes of Israel; Joseph shall have two portions.

**14** You shall inherit it equally, each as his brother: the land I raised My hand to give to your fathers. This land shall fall to you as an inheritance.

**15** This shall be the border of the land: on the north side, from the Great Sea by the way of Hethlon, as one comes to Zedad;

**16** Hamath, Berothah and Sibraim, which lies between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath, and Hazer-hatticon, which is on the border of Hauran.

**17** So the border shall run from the sea to Hazar-enan, at the border of Damascus, with the border of Hamath to the north. This is the north side.

**18** On the east side, between Hauran and Damascus, and between Gilead and the land of Israel, the Jordan shall be the border; you shall measure to the eastern sea. This is the east side.

**19** On the south side, southward, it shall run from Tamar to the waters of Meribah-kadesh, along the Brook to the Great Sea. This is the south side, southward.

**20** On the west side the Great Sea shall be the border, to a point opposite Lebo-hamath. This is the west side.

**21** So you shall divide this land among yourselves by the tribes of Israel.

**22** You shall allot it as an inheritance for yourselves and for the guests living among you who have had children among you. They shall be to you as native-born among the sons of Israel; with you they shall be allotted an inheritance among the tribes of Israel.

**23** In whatever tribe the guest lives, there you shall give him his inheritance, declares the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **v1 "water was flowing out from under the threshold of the house"** — the house becomes a spring, as Eden was: *A river goes out from Eden to water the garden* (Genesis 2:10). The psalm had sung of it, *There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God* (Psalm 46:4). Revelation's river of the water of life flows from the throne (Revelation 22:1).
- **vv3–5 ankles, knees, waist** — at every thousand cubits the water is deeper, though no stream joins it. *Afsayim*, **ankles**, is used in this sense only here.
- **v8 "it enters the sea… the water will be healed"** — the sea at the end of the Arabah is *the sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea* (Joshua 3:16). *The waters that flow out* (*ha-mutsa'im*) is obscure. *Nirpe'u*, **healed**, is the word of Elisha at the spring of Jericho: *I have healed this water* (2 Kings 2:21).
- **v9 "every living creature that swarms"** — the words of the fifth day, *Let the waters swarm with swarms of living souls* (Genesis 1:20). *The river* is *nachalayim* in the Hebrew, a dual, **two rivers**.
- **v10 "a place for spreading nets… of every kind"** — what Tyre was to become, *a place for spreading nets* (26:5, 14), is here a blessing on the shore of the Salt Sea. *Of every kind*, *le-minah*, is the phrase of the creation of the sea creatures (Genesis 1:21).
- **v12 "their leaves for healing"** — *li-trufah*, a word found only here. The trees are those of the psalm, whose *leaf does not wither* (Psalm 1:3), and Revelation's tree of life bears fruit every month, its leaves for the healing of the nations (Revelation 22:2).
- **vv13–14 "Joseph shall have two portions… each as his brother"** — Jacob had taken Joseph's sons as his own: *Ephraim and Manasseh, like Reuben and Simeon, shall be mine* (Genesis 48:5). Under Moses the shares were by size: *To the larger you shall give a larger inheritance* (Numbers 26:54). Here every tribe gets an equal strip.
- **vv15–20 the borders** — nearly those of Numbers 34: Zedad and Hazar-enan in the north (Numbers 34:8–9), Kadesh and *the Brook of Egypt* to the sea in the south (Numbers 34:4–5), and the Jordan in the east, so that Gilead, east of the river, lies outside. See [the map after Joshua 24, stage 13](#/joshua/s1/13).
- **vv22–23 "the guests… as native-born"** — the law had said *The guest living with you shall be to you as a native among you* (Leviticus 19:34). Here the guest is also given an inheritance of land, in the tribe he lives among.

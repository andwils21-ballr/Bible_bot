# Ezekiel 43

## The Glory Fills the House

**1** Then he led me to the gate, the gate facing east.

**2** The glory of the God of Israel was coming from the east. His voice was like the sound of many waters, and the earth shone with His glory.

**3** The vision I saw was like the vision I had seen when I came to destroy the city, and like the vision I had seen by the river Chebar; and I fell on my face.

**4** The glory of the LORD entered the house by the gate facing east.

**5** The spirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner court, and the glory of the LORD filled the house.

**6** I heard someone speaking to me from the house, while the man stood beside me.

**7** He said to me, "Son of man, this is the place of My throne and the place of the soles of My feet, where I will dwell among the sons of Israel forever. The house of Israel will no longer make My holy name unclean, neither they nor their kings, by giving themselves to other gods, and by the corpses of their kings at their high places,

**8** setting their threshold by My threshold and their doorpost beside My doorpost, with only a wall between Me and them. They made My holy name unclean by the detestable things they did, so I consumed them in My anger.

**9** Now let them put away from Me their giving themselves to other gods and the corpses of their kings, and I will dwell among them forever.

**10** As for you, son of man, describe the house to the house of Israel, so that they may be ashamed of their iniquities; and let them measure its plan.

**11** If they are ashamed of all they have done, make known to them the design of the house, its arrangement, its exits and its entrances, all its design and all its statutes, all its design and all its laws. Write it down before their eyes, so that they may keep all its design and all its statutes and do them.

**12** This is the law of the house: on the top of the mountain, all its territory all around is most holy. This is the law of the house.

## The Altar

**13** These are the measurements of the altar in cubits, each cubit a cubit and a handbreadth: its gutter a cubit deep and a cubit wide, with a rim of one span around its edge. This is the base of the altar.

**14** From the gutter on the ground to the lower ledge was two cubits, and the ledge one cubit wide; from the small ledge to the large ledge was four cubits, and the ledge one cubit wide.

**15** The altar hearth was four cubits high, and from the hearth upward were four horns.

**16** The altar hearth was twelve cubits long and twelve cubits wide, square on its four sides.

**17** The ledge was fourteen cubits long and fourteen cubits wide on its four sides, with a rim around it half a cubit wide and a gutter of a cubit all around. Its steps faced east."

## Seven Days to Make the Altar Clean

**18** He said to me, "Son of man, this is what the Lord GOD says: These are the statutes for the altar on the day it is made, for offering burnt offerings on it and for dashing blood against it.

**19** You shall give to the Levitical priests of the seed of Zadok, who come near to Me to serve Me, declares the Lord GOD, a young bull for a sin offering.

**20** You shall take some of its blood and put it on the four horns of the altar, on the four corners of the ledge and on the rim all around; so you shall purify it and make atonement for it.

**21** You shall take the bull of the sin offering, and it shall be burned in the appointed place of the house, outside the sanctuary.

**22** On the second day you shall offer a male goat without defect for a sin offering, and they shall purify the altar as they purified it with the bull.

**23** When you have finished purifying it, you shall offer a young bull without defect and a ram from the flock without defect.

**24** You shall present them before the LORD, and the priests shall throw salt on them and offer them up as a burnt offering to the LORD.

**25** For seven days you shall provide a goat each day for a sin offering; a young bull and a ram from the flock, without defect, shall also be provided.

**26** For seven days they shall make atonement for the altar and cleanse it, and so consecrate it.

**27** They shall complete these days. And it shall come to pass from the eighth day on, that the priests will offer your burnt offerings and your peace offerings on the altar, and I will accept you, declares the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **vv1–5 the glory returns** — by the way it left. The glory went out through *the east gate of the house of the LORD* (10:19) and stood on the mountain east of the city (11:23); now it comes back from the east, through the same gate, and fills the house. *His voice was like the sound of many waters*, as the wings of the living creatures were (1:24).
- **v3 "when I came to destroy the city"** — Ezekiel speaks of his own prophesying as the destroying. The vision he means is the one of chapters 8–11, when the city's destroyers were called near (9:1). The Greek has *when I came to anoint the city*.
- **v7 "the place of My throne and the place of the soles of My feet"** — the house as God's throne and footstool: *I will make the place of My feet glorious* (Isaiah 60:13).
- **v7 "by the corpses of their kings at their high places"** — *u-ve-figrei malkheihem bamotam*. As the Hebrew is pointed, *bamotam* is **their high places**, and the words are those of the curse: *I will destroy your high places… and throw your corpses on the corpses of your idols* (Leviticus 26:30). The same letters read *be-motam*, **at their death**, and then the charge is that kings were buried beside the house, their threshold by God's threshold (v8). The Greek has *the murders of the leaders*.
- **vv10–12 "the law of the house"** — *torat ha-bayit*, opening and closing v12. What Ezekiel is to give the people is not only a plan but a law: the whole mountaintop is *most holy*, the term the law uses for the inner sanctuary (Exodus 26:33) and for the priests' share of the offerings (Leviticus 6:17).
- **vv15–16 "The altar hearth"** — *ha-har'el*, then *ha-ari'el*, the top of the altar where the fire burns. *Ariel* is the name Isaiah gives Jerusalem: *Woe, Ariel, Ariel, the city where David camped*, and *she will be to Me like an Ariel* (Isaiah 29:1–2), an altar hearth. The Greek spells it *ariēl*.
- **vv14, 17 "the ledge"** — *azarah*. The Greek renders it *hilastērion*, the word it uses for the gold *cover* on the ark (Exodus 25:17), and Paul's word for what God set forth in Christ (Romans 3:25).
- **v17 "Its steps faced east"** — the law of the first altar forbade steps: *You shall not go up by steps onto My altar* (Exodus 20:26). This altar, ten cubits from the gutter to the top of the hearth (vv14–15), is reached by them.
- **vv18–27 the seven days** — the consecration of Moses' altar is done again: blood *on the horns of the altar all around*, and so *he purified the altar… to make atonement for it* (Leviticus 8:15); seven days, as the priests' ordination took seven days *to fill your hand* (Leviticus 8:33); and on the eighth day the service begins (Leviticus 9:1). *And so consecrate it* (v26) is, word for word, *and fill its hands*. The salt is *the salt of the covenant* (Leviticus 2:13).
- **v27 "and I will accept you"** — *ve-ratsiti*, the word the law uses of the offering, *it will be accepted for him* (Leviticus 1:4). Here it is the people who are accepted.

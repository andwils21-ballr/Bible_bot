# Ezekiel 39

**1** "As for you, son of man, prophesy against Gog and say: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am against you, Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.

**2** I will turn you around and drive you on, bring you up from the far reaches of the north and bring you against the mountains of Israel.

**3** I will strike your bow from your left hand and make your arrows drop from your right hand.

**4** On the mountains of Israel you will fall, you and all your troops and the peoples with you. I will give you as food to birds of prey of every wing and to the beasts of the field.

**5** You will fall in the open field, for I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.

**6** I will send fire on Magog and on those who live securely in the coastlands; and they will know that I am the LORD.

**7** I will make My holy name known among My people Israel, and I will not let My holy name be profaned any more; and the nations will know that I am the LORD, the Holy One in Israel.

**8** It is coming, and it will be done, declares the Lord GOD. That is the day of which I have spoken.

## Seven Years and Seven Months

**9** Those who live in the cities of Israel will go out and make fires with the weapons and burn them: shields large and small, bows and arrows, war clubs and spears. They will make fires with them for seven years.

**10** They will not need to take wood from the field or cut it from the forests, because they will make their fires with the weapons. They will plunder those who plundered them and despoil those who despoiled them, declares the Lord GOD.

**11** And it shall come to pass in that day, that I will give Gog a place there for a grave in Israel, the Valley of the Travelers, east of the sea; it will block the way of the travelers. There they will bury Gog and all his multitude, and they will call it the Valley of Hamon-gog.

**12** For seven months the house of Israel will be burying them, to cleanse the land.

**13** All the people of the land will bury them, and it will bring them renown on the day I show My glory, declares the Lord GOD.

**14** They will set apart men to go through the land continually and bury the travelers, those left lying on the face of the land, to cleanse it. At the end of seven months they will make their search.

**15** As they go through the land, whoever sees a human bone will set up a marker beside it, until the buriers have buried it in the Valley of Hamon-gog.

**16** A city there will also be named Hamonah. So they will cleanse the land.

## The LORD's Sacrifice

**17** As for you, son of man, this is what the Lord GOD says: Say to the birds of every wing and to all the beasts of the field: Assemble and come; gather from all around to the sacrifice I am making for you, a great sacrifice on the mountains of Israel, and you will eat flesh and drink blood.

**18** You will eat the flesh of mighty ones and drink the blood of the princes of the earth — rams, lambs and goats, bulls, all of them fatlings of Bashan.

**19** You will eat fat until you are full and drink blood until you are drunk, at the sacrifice I have made for you.

**20** At My table you will eat your fill of horses and riders, mighty ones and all men of war, declares the Lord GOD.

**21** I will set My glory among the nations, and all the nations will see the judgment I have carried out and My hand that I have laid on them.

## I Will No Longer Hide My Face

**22** The house of Israel will know that I am the LORD their God, from that day on.

**23** The nations will know that the house of Israel went into exile for their iniquity, because they were unfaithful to Me; so I hid My face from them and gave them into the hand of their enemies, and they all fell by the sword.

**24** I dealt with them according to their uncleanness and their transgressions, and I hid My face from them.

**25** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Now I will restore the fortunes of Jacob and have compassion on the whole house of Israel, and I will be jealous for My holy name.

**26** They will bear their disgrace and all the unfaithfulness they committed against Me, when they live securely on their own soil with no one to make them afraid,

**27** when I bring them back from the peoples and gather them from the lands of their enemies, and show Myself holy through them in the sight of many nations.

**28** Then they will know that I am the LORD their God, because I sent them into exile among the nations and then gathered them into their own land. I will leave none of them there any longer.

**29** I will no longer hide My face from them, when I have poured out My Spirit on the house of Israel, declares the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **vv17–20 "a great sacrifice"** — Isaiah had used the picture of Edom: *the LORD has a sacrifice in Bozrah* (Isaiah 34:6). The guests are the birds and the beasts; the animals of the offering are warriors and princes; and the guests eat fat and drink blood, the two things Israel may never eat (Leviticus 3:17). Revelation takes up the scene (19:17–18).

# Ezekiel 3

**1** He said to me, "Son of man, eat what you find. Eat this scroll, and go, speak to the house of Israel."

**2** So I opened my mouth, and He gave me this scroll to eat.

**3** He said to me, "Son of man, feed your belly and fill your stomach with this scroll that I am giving you." So I ate it, and in my mouth it was as sweet as honey.

**4** He said to me, "Son of man, go to the house of Israel and speak My words to them.

**5** For you are not being sent to a people of speech too deep to understand and of heavy tongue, but to the house of Israel;

**6** not to many peoples of speech too deep to understand and of heavy tongue, whose words you cannot understand. Surely, if I sent you to them, they would listen to you.

**7** But the house of Israel will not be willing to listen to you, for they are not willing to listen to Me; for all the house of Israel are hard-browed and hard-hearted.

**8** I have made your face as hard as their faces, and your brow as hard as their brows.

**9** Like diamond, harder than flint, I have made your brow. Do not be afraid of them or dismayed by their looks, for they are a rebellious house."

**10** He said to me, "Son of man, all My words that I speak to you, take into your heart and hear with your ears.

**11** Go to the exiles, to the sons of your people, and speak to them and say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says,' whether they hear or refuse."

**12** Then the spirit lifted me up, and I heard behind me the sound of a great rumbling: "Blessed be the glory of the LORD from His place!"

**13** It was the sound of the wings of the living creatures brushing against one another, and the sound of the wheels beside them, the sound of a great rumbling.

**14** The spirit lifted me up and took me away, and I went bitter, in the heat of my spirit, and the hand of the LORD was strong on me.

**15** I came to the exiles at Tel-abib, who lived by the river Chebar. I sat where they were sitting, and I sat there seven days among them, stunned.

## The Watchman

**16** And it came to pass at the end of seven days that the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**17** "Son of man, I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel. When you hear a word from My mouth, you shall warn them from Me.

**18** When I say to the wicked, 'You shall surely die,' and you do not warn him or speak to warn the wicked from his wicked way to save his life, that wicked man shall die in his iniquity, but his blood I will require from your hand.

**19** But if you warn the wicked, and he does not turn from his wickedness or from his wicked way, he shall die in his iniquity, but you will have saved your life.

**20** When a righteous man turns from his righteousness and does wrong, and I lay a stumbling block before him, he shall die. Because you did not warn him, he shall die in his sin, and the righteous deeds he has done shall not be remembered; but his blood I will require from your hand.

**21** But if you warn the righteous man not to sin, and he does not sin, he shall surely live, because he took warning; and you will have saved your life."

## Bound and Silent

**22** The hand of the LORD was on me there, and He said to me, "Get up, go out into the plain, and there I will speak with you."

**23** So I got up and went out into the plain, and the glory of the LORD was standing there, like the glory I had seen by the river Chebar; and I fell on my face.

**24** Then the spirit entered me and set me on my feet, and He spoke with me and said to me, "Go, shut yourself inside your house.

**25** As for you, son of man, they will put cords on you and bind you with them, so that you cannot go out among them.

**26** I will make your tongue cling to the roof of your mouth, and you will be mute and unable to rebuke them, for they are a rebellious house.

**27** But when I speak with you, I will open your mouth, and you shall say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says.' Whoever will hear, let him hear; and whoever refuses, let him refuse; for they are a rebellious house."

## Notes

- **v3 "in my mouth it was as sweet as honey"** — the words of the psalm, *sweeter than honey to my mouth* (Psalm 119:103), though the scroll was written with *laments and mourning and woe* (2:10). In v14 he goes away *bitter*.
- **vv5–6 "a people of speech too deep to understand and of heavy tongue"** — *imqei safah ve-khivdei lashon*. Isaiah used the first for the foreign invader, *the people of a speech too deep to understand* (Isaiah 33:19); the second is Moses' word for himself, *heavy of mouth and heavy of tongue* (Exodus 4:10). Israel speaks Ezekiel's language, and that is the trouble: foreigners would have listened.
- **vv7–9, 14 "hard-browed… I have made your face as hard as their faces… the hand of the LORD was strong on me"** — *chazaq*, "hard, strong," four times in three verses and again in v14: the root of *Yechezqel*, "God makes strong." The people are hard; God makes the prophet harder, *like diamond, harder than flint*. *Shamir*, "diamond," is Jeremiah's word for the point that writes Judah's sin, *with a point of diamond it is engraved* (Jeremiah 17:1).
- **vv12–13 "the sound of a great rumbling: 'Blessed be the glory of the LORD from His place!'"** — *ra'ash*, the word for an earthquake. The Greek also reads *Blessed*. What he hears behind him is the vision of chapter 1 moving off: the wings of the living creatures and the wheels.
- **v15 "I sat there seven days among them, stunned"** — *mashmim*, appalled, struck silent, as Job's friends *sat with him on the ground seven days and seven nights, and no one spoke a word to him* (Job 2:13).
- **vv17–21 "I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel"** — *tsofeh*, the man on the wall who sees what is coming. God had said to Israel through Jeremiah, *I set watchmen over you* (Jeremiah 6:17); now one man is set, and his own life hangs on whether he gives warning. The charge is given again in the same words at 33:7–9.
- **v20 "I lay a stumbling block before him"** — *mikhshol*, Jeremiah's word, *I am about to lay stumbling blocks before this people* (Jeremiah 6:21). The stumbling block is God's; the warning is the prophet's.
- **vv25–27 "they will put cords on you… you will be mute… But when I speak with you, I will open your mouth"** — the prophet is bound and silent except when God speaks through him. The silence is lifted when word comes that the city has fallen (24:27; 33:22).

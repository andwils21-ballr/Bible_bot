# Ezekiel 38

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, set your face toward Gog, of the land of Magog, the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal, and prophesy against him.

**3** Say: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am against you, Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.

**4** I will turn you around, put hooks in your jaws, and bring you out with all your army, horses and horsemen, all of them clothed in full armor, a great company with large shield and small shield, all of them wielding swords;

**5** Persia, Cush and Put with them, all of them with shield and helmet;

**6** Gomer and all its troops, Beth-togarmah from the far reaches of the north and all its troops — many peoples with you.

**7** Be ready, and prepare yourself, you and all your company gathered around you, and take command of them.

**8** After many days you will be summoned. In the latter years you will come against a land restored from the sword, whose people were gathered from many peoples onto the mountains of Israel, which had long lain in ruins. Its people were brought out from the peoples, and all of them live securely.

**9** You will go up and come like a storm; you will be like a cloud covering the land, you and all your troops, and many peoples with you.

**10** This is what the Lord GOD says: And it shall come to pass in that day, that thoughts will come into your mind, and you will devise an evil plan.

**11** You will say, 'I will go up against a land of unwalled villages; I will come against a quiet people who live securely, all of them living without walls, with no bars or gates' —

**12** to take spoil and seize plunder, to turn your hand against the ruins now inhabited, and against a people gathered from the nations, who have gained livestock and goods, who live at the navel of the earth.

**13** Sheba and Dedan, the merchants of Tarshish and all its young lions will say to you, 'Have you come to take spoil? Have you assembled your company to seize plunder, to carry off silver and gold, to take away livestock and goods, to take great spoil?'

**14** Therefore prophesy, son of man, and say to Gog: This is what the Lord GOD says: On that day, when My people Israel are living securely, will you not know it?

**15** You will come from your place in the far reaches of the north, you and many peoples with you, all of them riding horses, a great company and a mighty army.

**16** You will come up against My people Israel like a cloud covering the land. In the end of days I will bring you against My land, so that the nations may know Me, when through you, Gog, I show Myself holy before their eyes.

**17** This is what the Lord GOD says: Are you the one of whom I spoke in former days through My servants the prophets of Israel, who prophesied in those days, for years, that I would bring you against them?

**18** And it shall come to pass in that day, the day Gog comes against the soil of Israel, declares the Lord GOD, that My wrath will flare up in My anger.

**19** In My jealousy, in the fire of My wrath, I have spoken: surely in that day there will be a great quaking on the soil of Israel.

**20** The fish of the sea, the birds of the heavens, the beasts of the field, every creeping thing that creeps on the ground, and every human being on the face of the ground will quake before Me. The mountains will be thrown down, the cliffs will fall, and every wall will fall to the ground.

**21** I will call for a sword against him on all My mountains, declares the Lord GOD; every man's sword will be against his brother.

**22** I will enter into judgment with him with pestilence and with blood. I will rain on him, on his troops and on the many peoples with him, flooding rain and hailstones, fire and sulfur.

**23** So I will show My greatness and My holiness, and make Myself known in the sight of many nations; and they will know that I am the LORD."

## Notes

- **v2 "the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal"** — *nesi rosh Meshekh ve-Tuval*. *Rosh* is **head**, and *nesi rosh* can be **chief prince**, the prince at the head of Meshech and Tubal. The Greek reads *Rōs* as a third people: *prince of Rōs, Meshech and Tubal*; and the accents of the Hebrew put a pause after *nesi*, **prince**, which leaves *rosh* free to stand as a name. No people called Rosh appears in the table of nations, where Magog, Meshech, Tubal, Gomer and Togarmah are all listed (Genesis 10:2–3), or anywhere else in the Bible; the only Rosh is a son of Benjamin (Genesis 46:21).
- **vv2–6 Gog and his armies** — Gog is named nowhere else as a ruler; Magog is a son of Japheth (Genesis 10:2). His allies come from the edges of the known world: Meshech, Tubal, Gomer and Beth-togarmah from the north, Persia from the east, Cush and Put from the south. Meshech and Tubal were Tyre's traders (27:13) and lie among the dead in the pit (32:26). *Gog and Magog* return at the end of Revelation (20:8).
- **v4 "put hooks in your jaws"** — what the LORD did to Pharaoh, the monster of the Nile (29:4). Gog comes because he is brought.
- **vv8, 16 "In the latter years… in the end of days"** — *be-acharit ha-yamim*, the phrase of Jacob's last words, *what will befall you in the end of days* (Genesis 49:1), and of Moses' promise that Israel would return to the LORD (Deuteronomy 4:30). The attack comes after the return of chapters 36–37, against a people living in peace.
- **v12 "the navel of the earth"** — *tabbur ha-arets*, a phrase found only here and in Judges, where Gaal sees people *coming down from the navel of the land* (Judges 9:37). The Greek has *omphalos*. Israel lives at the center, as Jerusalem was set *in the middle of the nations* (5:5).
- **v13 "all its young lions"** — *kefireha*, the word of Pharaoh's lament, *a young lion of the nations* (32:2): Tarshish's warriors or rulers. The Greek reads *kefarim*, **villages**.
- **v17 "Are you the one of whom I spoke in former days"** — no earlier prophet names Gog. What they spoke of was an enemy out of the north: *Out of the north disaster will break loose* (Jeremiah 1:14); *A people is coming from the land of the north* (Jeremiah 6:22). The Greek makes the question a statement: *You are the one*.
- **vv19–20 "a great quaking"** — *ra'ash gadol*, the *great rumbling* of 3:12, and the bones' rumbling (37:7). Here the earth itself shakes, and the creatures that tremble are those God named when He made humankind: *the fish of the sea… the birds of the heavens… every creeping thing that creeps upon the earth* (Genesis 1:26).
- **v21 "every man's sword will be against his brother"** — Gideon's victory, when *the LORD set every man's sword against his fellow throughout the whole camp* (Judges 7:22). The Greek has *I will call against it every fear*.
- **v22 "flooding rain and hailstones, fire and sulfur"** — the storm of 13:11, *a flooding rain… hailstones*, and the fire of Sodom: *The LORD rained upon Sodom and upon Gomorrah sulfur and fire* (Genesis 19:24).

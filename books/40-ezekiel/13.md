# Ezekiel 13

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel who are prophesying, and say to those who prophesy out of their own hearts: Hear the word of the LORD!

**3** This is what the Lord GOD says: Woe to the foolish prophets who follow their own spirit and have seen nothing!

**4** Your prophets, Israel, have been like foxes among ruins.

**5** You have not gone up into the breaches or built up a wall around the house of Israel, so that it might stand in the battle on the day of the LORD.

**6** They have seen emptiness and lying divination, those who say, 'Declares the LORD,' when the LORD has not sent them; yet they expect Him to fulfill their word.

**7** Have you not seen an empty vision and spoken a lying divination, when you say, 'Declares the LORD,' though I have not spoken?

**8** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have spoken emptiness and seen lies, therefore I am against you, declares the Lord GOD.

**9** My hand will be against the prophets who see emptiness and divine lies. They shall not be in the council of My people, nor be written in the register of the house of Israel, nor enter the land of Israel; and you will know that I am the Lord GOD.

**10** Because, yes, because they have led My people astray, saying, 'Peace,' when there is no peace, and when the people build a flimsy wall, these prophets plaster it with whitewash,

**11** say to those who plaster it with whitewash that it will fall. There will be a flooding rain, and you, hailstones, will fall, and a storm wind will break out.

**12** When the wall has fallen, will it not be said to you, 'Where is the plaster you plastered it with?'

**13** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: In My wrath I will make a storm wind break out; in My anger there will be a flooding rain, and hailstones in wrath to make an end of it.

**14** I will tear down the wall that you plastered with whitewash and bring it down to the ground, so that its foundation is laid bare. It will fall, and you will perish inside it; and you will know that I am the LORD.

**15** So I will spend My wrath on the wall and on those who plastered it with whitewash, and I will say to you: The wall is gone, and so are those who plastered it,

**16** the prophets of Israel who prophesied to Jerusalem and saw visions of peace for her when there was no peace, declares the Lord GOD.

**17** As for you, son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people who prophesy out of their own hearts, and prophesy against them,

**18** and say: This is what the Lord GOD says: Woe to the women who sew bands on every wrist and make veils for the heads of people of every height, to hunt souls! Will you hunt the souls of My people and keep your own souls alive?

**19** You have profaned Me among My people for handfuls of barley and scraps of bread, putting to death souls that should not die and keeping alive souls that should not live, by your lies to My people, who listen to lies.

**20** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I am against your bands, with which you hunt souls there like birds; I will tear them from your arms, and I will let the souls go, the souls you hunt like birds.

**21** I will tear off your veils and rescue My people from your hands, and they will no longer be prey in your hands; and you will know that I am the LORD.

**22** Because you have disheartened the righteous with lies, when I had not grieved him, and have strengthened the hands of the wicked, so that he does not turn from his evil way and save his life,

**23** therefore you will no longer see emptiness or practice divination. I will rescue My people from your hands, and you will know that I am the LORD."

---

## Notes

- **vv17–21 "the women who sew bands on every wrist and make veils… to hunt souls"** — *kesatot*, "bands," and *mispachot*, "veils," are found only in this passage; what was done with them is not known beyond what the verses say, that souls were caught and held by them *like birds*. *Atstsilei yadai*, "wrists," are the joints of the arm (*atsil*).

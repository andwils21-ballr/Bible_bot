# Ezekiel 36

## To the Mountains of Israel

**1** "As for you, son of man, prophesy to the mountains of Israel and say: Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the LORD!

**2** This is what the Lord GOD says: Because the enemy said of you, 'Aha!' and, 'The ancient heights have become our possession,'

**3** therefore prophesy and say: This is what the Lord GOD says: Because, yes, because they made you desolate and trampled you from every side, so that you became the possession of the rest of the nations, and you have become the talk of every tongue and the gossip of the people,

**4** therefore, mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD: This is what the Lord GOD says to the mountains and to the hills, to the ravines and to the valleys, to the desolate ruins and to the deserted cities that have become plunder and a mockery to the rest of the nations all around —

**5** therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: In the fire of My jealousy I have surely spoken against the rest of the nations, and against Edom, all of it, who gave My land to themselves as a possession with wholehearted joy and with contempt of soul, to take its pastureland as plunder.

**6** Therefore prophesy concerning the soil of Israel, and say to the mountains and to the hills, to the ravines and to the valleys: This is what the Lord GOD says: I have spoken in My jealousy and in My wrath, because you have borne the insults of the nations.

**7** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I have raised My hand: the nations all around you will bear their own insults.

**8** But you, mountains of Israel, will put out your branches and bear your fruit for My people Israel, for they will soon come.

**9** For I am for you, and I will turn to you, and you will be tilled and sown.

**10** I will multiply people on you, the whole house of Israel, all of it; the cities will be inhabited and the ruins rebuilt.

**11** I will multiply human and beast on you, and they will multiply and be fruitful. I will settle you as you were in your former times and do more good to you than at your beginning; and you will know that I am the LORD.

**12** I will make people walk on you, My people Israel. They will possess you, and you will be their inheritance, and you will no longer bereave them of their children.

**13** This is what the Lord GOD says: Because they say to you, 'You devour people, and you have bereaved your nations,'

**14** therefore you will no longer devour people or bereave your nations, declares the Lord GOD.

**15** I will no longer let you hear the insults of the nations; you will no longer bear the scorn of the peoples, and you will no longer make your nations stumble, declares the Lord GOD."

## For the Sake of My Holy Name

**16** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**17** "Son of man, when the house of Israel lived on their own soil, they made it unclean by their ways and their deeds. Their way before Me was like the uncleanness of a woman in her impurity.

**18** So I poured out My wrath on them for the blood they had poured out on the land, and for the idols with which they had made it unclean.

**19** I scattered them among the nations, and they were dispersed through the countries; I judged them according to their ways and their deeds.

**20** But when they came to the nations, wherever they came, they profaned My holy name, in that it was said of them, 'These are the people of the LORD, and they had to leave His land.'

**21** I had pity for My holy name, which the house of Israel had profaned among the nations where they went.

**22** Therefore say to the house of Israel: This is what the Lord GOD says: It is not for your sake that I am about to act, house of Israel, but for My holy name, which you have profaned among the nations where you went.

**23** I will make holy My great name, which has been profaned among the nations, which you have profaned among them; and the nations will know that I am the LORD, declares the Lord GOD, when I show Myself holy through you before their eyes.

## A New Heart and a New Spirit

**24** I will take you from the nations and gather you from all the countries, and bring you into your own land.

**25** I will sprinkle clean water on you, and you will be clean from all your uncleanness; I will cleanse you from all your idols.

**26** I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit within you. I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh.

**27** I will put My Spirit within you and cause you to walk in My statutes and keep My judgments and do them.

**28** You will live in the land I gave to your fathers; you will be My people, and I will be your God.

**29** I will save you from all your uncleanness. I will call for the grain and make it plentiful, and I will not bring famine on you.

**30** I will make the fruit of the tree and the produce of the field plentiful, so that you will no longer suffer the disgrace of famine among the nations.

**31** Then you will remember your evil ways and your deeds that were not good, and you will loathe yourselves for your iniquities and your detestable things.

**32** It is not for your sake that I am acting, declares the Lord GOD; let that be known to you. Be ashamed and humiliated for your ways, house of Israel.

**33** This is what the Lord GOD says: On the day I cleanse you from all your iniquities, I will make the cities inhabited, and the ruins will be rebuilt.

**34** The desolate land will be tilled, instead of lying desolate in the sight of all who passed by.

**35** They will say, 'This land that was desolate has become like the garden of Eden, and the cities that were ruined, desolate and torn down are fortified and inhabited.'

**36** Then the nations that are left around you will know that I, the LORD, have rebuilt what was torn down and planted what was desolate. I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it.

**37** This is what the Lord GOD says: This too I will let the house of Israel ask of Me, to do it for them: I will multiply their people like a flock.

**38** Like the flock for holy offerings, like the flock of Jerusalem at her appointed feasts, so the ruined cities will be filled with flocks of people. Then they will know that I am the LORD."

## Notes

- **vv1–15 "Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the LORD!"** — chapter 6 turned around. There the same mountains heard *I Myself am about to bring a sword on you, and I will destroy your high places* (6:3), addressed with the same list, *to the mountains and to the hills, to the ravines and to the valleys* (vv4, 6). Now they are promised branches, fruit and people. The enemy's boast in v2, *the ancient heights*, uses *bamot*, the word for those high places.
- **v2 "Aha!"** — *he'ach*, what the Ammonites said over the sanctuary when it was profaned (25:3). In v5 the enemy is named: *Edom, all of it*, the words that close chapter 35.
- **v5 "to take its pastureland as plunder"** — *le-ma'an migrashah la-vaz*, a phrase whose grammar is unclear; *migrash* is the open pasture around a town. The Greek reads it as a purpose, *to destroy in plunder*.
- **v9 "For I am for you"** — *hineni aleikhem*, the words that have meant *I am against you* all through the book (13:8; 35:3). Here *and I will turn to you* settles their sense, and it is Leviticus again: *I will turn to you, and make you fruitful and multiply you* (Leviticus 26:9). In v11 *they will multiply and be fruitful* gives the creation blessing, *Be fruitful and multiply* (Genesis 1:28), with its two verbs turned around.
- **vv13–14 "You devour people… bereave your nations"** — the spies' verdict on the land, *a land that devours those who live in it* (Numbers 13:32), finally answered. In v14 the written text has *tekhashli*, **make stumble**; the reading tradition has *teshakkeli*, **bereave**, which the Greek also has, and the rendering follows it. *Your nations* is the read form of vv13–15 (the written form is singular): the two nations, Israel and Judah, that Edom meant to take (35:10).
- **v17 "like the uncleanness of a woman in her impurity"** — *ke-tum'at ha-niddah*, the word of 18:6. The cure in v25 answers it: *zaraq*, **sprinkle**, is the verb of *the water for impurity* in Numbers, *mei niddah*, which cleanses the unclean (Numbers 19:13, 20), and of the blood dashed against the altar (Leviticus 1:5).
- **vv20–23 "they profaned My holy name"** — the profaning is what the nations say: *These are the people of the LORD, and they had to leave His land*. A god whose people are in exile looks like a god who could not keep them. In chapter 20 He spared them *for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned in the sight of the nations* (20:9, 14). Now He acts *not for your sake* (vv22, 32), and *I will make holy My great name* is, in the Greek, *hagiasō to onoma mou*, the verb of *hallowed be Your name* (Matthew 6:9).
- **vv26–27 "a new heart… My Spirit within you"** — twice before, the book has spoken of this. In 11:19 God promised *one heart* and *a new spirit*, *a heart of flesh* for *the heart of stone*; in 18:31 He commanded it: *make yourselves a new heart and a new spirit*. Here He gives it, and with it His own Spirit, so that the statutes are kept from the inside. *Heart of flesh* is *kardian sarkinēn* in the Greek, the phrase Paul uses for the tablets the Spirit writes on (2 Corinthians 3:3).
- **v35 "like the garden of Eden"** — the garden of God where the king of Tyre was set (28:13), and whose trees envied the cedar (31:9). The ruined land becomes the garden.
- **v37 "This too I will let the house of Israel ask of Me"** — *iddaresh*, the verb of 20:3: *As I live, I will not let Myself be consulted by you*. The refusal is withdrawn. What they will ask for is people, *a flock*, and v38 compares it to *the flock for holy offerings*, the crowds and animals that filled Jerusalem at the feasts, when *three times in the year all your males shall appear* (Deuteronomy 16:16). The flock of 34:31, *You are human*, has become a city full of people.

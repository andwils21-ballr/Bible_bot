# Ezekiel 23

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, there were two women, daughters of one mother.

**3** They prostituted themselves in Egypt; in their youth they prostituted themselves. There their breasts were fondled, and there their virgin bosoms were caressed.

**4** Their names were Oholah, the elder, and Oholibah her sister. They became Mine, and they bore sons and daughters. As for their names, Samaria is Oholah, and Jerusalem is Oholibah.

**5** Oholah prostituted herself while she was Mine; she lusted after her lovers, the Assyrians, warriors

**6** clothed in blue, governors and commanders, all of them desirable young men, horsemen riding on horses.

**7** She gave her prostitution to them, the choicest men of Assyria, all of them; and with all after whom she lusted, with all their idols, she made herself unclean.

**8** She did not give up the prostitution she had brought from Egypt; for in her youth they had lain with her and caressed her virgin bosom, and poured out their lust on her.

**9** Therefore I gave her into the hands of her lovers, into the hands of the Assyrians, after whom she lusted.

**10** They uncovered her nakedness; they took her sons and her daughters, and her they killed with the sword. She became a byword among women, and they carried out judgments on her.

**11** Her sister Oholibah saw this, yet she was more corrupt in her lust than she, and in her prostitution than her sister in hers.

**12** She lusted after the Assyrians, governors and commanders, warriors clothed in full splendor, horsemen riding on horses, all of them desirable young men.

**13** I saw that she was unclean; both of them took the same way.

**14** But she increased her prostitution. She saw men carved on the wall, images of Chaldeans carved in vermilion,

**15** with belts around their waists and flowing turbans on their heads, all of them looking like officers, the likeness of the Babylonians whose native land was Chaldea.

**16** As soon as her eyes saw them, she lusted after them, and sent messengers to them in Chaldea.

**17** The Babylonians came to her, to the bed of love, and defiled her with their prostitution; and when she had been defiled by them, she turned from them in disgust.

**18** When she uncovered her prostitution and uncovered her nakedness, I turned from her in disgust, as I had turned in disgust from her sister.

**19** Yet she multiplied her prostitution, remembering the days of her youth, when she prostituted herself in the land of Egypt.

**20** She lusted after their lovers there, whose flesh was like the flesh of donkeys and whose issue was like the issue of horses.

**21** So you longed for the lewdness of your youth, when the Egyptians caressed your bosom for the sake of your young breasts.

## The Cup of Your Sister

**22** Therefore, Oholibah, this is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to stir up your lovers against you, those from whom you turned in disgust, and I will bring them against you from every side:

**23** the Babylonians and all the Chaldeans, Pekod and Shoa and Koa, and all the Assyrians with them, desirable young men, governors and commanders all of them, officers and men of renown, all of them riding on horses.

**24** They will come against you with weapons, chariots and wheels, and a host of peoples; they will set themselves against you on every side with buckler, shield and helmet. I will hand judgment over to them, and they will judge you by their judgments.

**25** I will set My jealousy against you, and they will deal with you in fury. They will cut off your nose and your ears, and those of you who are left will fall by the sword. They will take your sons and your daughters, and those of you who are left will be consumed by fire.

**26** They will strip you of your clothes and take your beautiful jewels.

**27** So I will put an end to your lewdness and to the prostitution you brought from the land of Egypt; you will not lift your eyes to them or remember Egypt any more.

**28** For this is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to give you into the hands of those you hate, into the hands of those from whom you turned in disgust.

**29** They will deal with you in hatred, take away all you have worked for, and leave you naked and bare; and the nakedness of your prostitution will be uncovered, your lewdness and your prostitution.

**30** These things will be done to you because you prostituted yourself with the nations, because you made yourself unclean with their idols.

**31** You have walked in the way of your sister; therefore I will put her cup into your hand.

**32** This is what the Lord GOD says:  
You will drink your sister's cup,  
deep and wide;  
you will be laughed at and mocked;  
it holds so much.

**33** You will be filled with drunkenness and sorrow,  
a cup of horror and desolation,  
the cup of your sister Samaria.

**34** You will drink it and drain it,  
gnaw its broken pieces,  
and tear your breasts;  
for I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.

**35** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have forgotten Me and cast Me behind your back, you also must bear your lewdness and your prostitution."

## Judgment on Both

**36** The LORD said to me, "Son of man, will you judge Oholah and Oholibah? Then declare to them their detestable things.

**37** For they have committed adultery, and blood is on their hands; they have committed adultery with their idols, and they have even made their sons, whom they bore to Me, pass over to them to be devoured.

**38** This too they have done to Me: they have made My sanctuary unclean on that same day and profaned My sabbaths.

**39** For when they had slaughtered their children for their idols, on that same day they entered My sanctuary to profane it. This is what they did in the middle of My house.

**40** Moreover, they sent for men to come from far away, to whom a messenger was sent; and they came. For them you bathed, painted your eyes and put on your ornaments.

**41** You sat on a splendid couch with a table spread before it, on which you had set My incense and My oil.

**42** The sound of a carefree crowd was with her; and with men of the common sort, drunkards were brought from the wilderness. They put bracelets on the women's arms and beautiful crowns on their heads.

**43** Then I said of her who was worn out by adulteries, 'Now they will prostitute themselves with her, even her!'

**44** They went in to her as men go in to a prostitute; so they went in to Oholah and to Oholibah, the lewd women.

**45** But righteous men will judge them with the judgment of adulteresses and the judgment of women who shed blood, because they are adulteresses and blood is on their hands.

**46** For this is what the Lord GOD says: Bring up an assembly against them, and give them over to terror and plunder.

**47** The assembly will stone them and cut them down with their swords; they will kill their sons and their daughters and burn their houses with fire.

**48** So I will put an end to lewdness in the land, and all women will take warning and not commit lewdness as you have done.

**49** They will bring your lewdness back on you, and you will bear the sins of your idols; and you will know that I am the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **v4 "Oholah… Oholibah… Samaria is Oholah, and Jerusalem is Oholibah"** — *Oholah* is "her tent," and *Oholibah* "my tent is in her." The names turn on *ohel*, "tent," the word of the tent of meeting; the one with *my tent* in her name is Jerusalem, where the temple stood.
- **vv2–4 "two women, daughters of one mother… They became Mine"** — the figure of chapter 16 told again with two sisters, as Jeremiah told it of *faithless Israel* and *her treacherous sister Judah* (Jeremiah 3:6–8). Here the lovers are named by their alliances: Egypt in their youth, then Assyria, then Babylon.
- **vv5–6, 12, 23 "warriors clothed in blue, governors and commanders, all of them desirable young men, horsemen riding on horses"** — the same line of praise three times, until in v23 it describes the army that will come against her.
- **vv14–16 "men carved on the wall, images of Chaldeans carved in vermilion"** — Oholibah falls for pictures before she meets the men: the carvings on the walls, as at 8:10, now of foreign officers.
- **v20 "She lusted after their lovers there"** — *pilagsheihem*, the word for a secondary wife, here used of the Egyptian men she went after. The Greek has *the Chaldeans*.
- **vv31–34 "You will drink your sister's cup… a cup of horror and desolation"** — the cup of the LORD's wrath that Jeremiah was sent to give the nations, beginning with Jerusalem (Jeremiah 25:15–18). *Tear your breasts*: the breasts of v3 that her lovers caressed.
- **vv37–39 "on that same day they entered My sanctuary to profane it"** — the worst of the charges: from sacrificing children to their idols they came straight into the temple.
- **v42 "drunkards were brought from the wilderness"** — the read form, *sava'im*, "drunkards"; the written form, *sova'im*, can be read as "Sabeans," the people of Sheba. The English follows the read form.

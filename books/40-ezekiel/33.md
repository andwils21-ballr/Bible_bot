# Ezekiel 33

## The Watchman

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, speak to the sons of your people and say to them: When I bring the sword on a land, and the people of the land take one man from among them and make him their watchman,

**3** and he sees the sword coming on the land and blows the ram's horn and warns the people,

**4** then whoever hears the sound of the ram's horn and does not take warning, and the sword comes and takes him away, his blood will be on his own head.

**5** He heard the sound of the ram's horn and did not take warning; his blood will be on his own head. Had he taken warning, he would have saved his life.

**6** But if the watchman sees the sword coming and does not blow the ram's horn, and the people are not warned, and the sword comes and takes a life from among them, that man is taken away in his iniquity, but his blood I will require from the watchman's hand.

**7** As for you, son of man, I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel. When you hear a word from My mouth, you shall warn them from Me.

**8** When I say to the wicked, 'Wicked man, you shall surely die,' and you do not speak to warn the wicked from his way, that wicked man shall die in his iniquity, but his blood I will require from your hand.

**9** But if you warn the wicked to turn from his way, and he does not turn from his way, he shall die in his iniquity, but you will have saved your life.

## Why Will You Die?

**10** As for you, son of man, say to the house of Israel: This is what you have said: 'Our transgressions and our sins are on us, and we are rotting away in them. How then can we live?'

**11** Say to them: As I live, declares the Lord GOD, I take no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but rather that the wicked turn from his way and live. Turn back, turn back from your evil ways! Why will you die, house of Israel?

**12** As for you, son of man, say to the sons of your people: The righteousness of the righteous will not save him on the day he transgresses; and the wickedness of the wicked will not make him stumble on the day he turns from his wickedness; and the righteous will not be able to live by it on the day he sins.

**13** When I say to the righteous that he shall surely live, and he trusts in his righteousness and does wrong, none of his righteous deeds will be remembered; for the wrong he has done, he shall die.

**14** When I say to the wicked, 'You shall surely die,' and he turns from his sin and does what is just and right —

**15** if the wicked man gives back the pledge, repays what he took by robbery, and walks in the statutes of life, doing no wrong — he shall surely live; he shall not die.

**16** None of the sins he has committed will be remembered against him. He has done what is just and right; he shall surely live.

**17** Yet the sons of your people say, 'The way of the Lord is not fair,' when it is their own way that is not fair.

**18** When a righteous man turns from his righteousness and does wrong, he shall die for it.

**19** When a wicked man turns from his wickedness and does what is just and right, he shall live because of it.

**20** Yet you say, 'The way of the Lord is not fair.' I will judge you, house of Israel, each according to his ways."

## The City Has Been Struck Down

**21** And it came to pass in the twelfth year of our exile, in the tenth month, on the fifth of the month, that a fugitive from Jerusalem came to me and said, "The city has been struck down."

**22** The hand of the LORD had been on me in the evening, before the fugitive came, and He had opened my mouth by the time he came to me in the morning. So my mouth was opened, and I was no longer mute.

**23** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**24** "Son of man, those who live in these ruins on the soil of Israel are saying, 'Abraham was only one man, and he took possession of the land; but we are many. The land has been given to us as a possession.'

**25** Therefore say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: You eat meat with the blood in it, lift your eyes to your idols and shed blood. Shall you then possess the land?

**26** You rely on your sword, you do detestable things, and each of you defiles his neighbor's wife. Shall you then possess the land?

**27** Say this to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: As I live, those in the ruins will fall by the sword; whoever is in the open field I will give to the beasts to be eaten; and those in the strongholds and in the caves will die of pestilence.

**28** I will make the land a desolation and a waste; the pride of her strength will come to an end, and the mountains of Israel will be desolate, with no one passing through.

**29** Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I make the land a desolation and a waste because of all the detestable things they have done.

## A Love Song

**30** As for you, son of man, the sons of your people are talking about you beside the walls and in the doorways of the houses, saying to one another, each to his brother, 'Come, please, and hear what the word is that comes from the LORD.'

**31** They come to you as people come, and sit before you as My people, and hear your words, but they do not do them. With their mouths they make a show of love, but their heart goes after their gain.

**32** To them you are like a love song, sung with a beautiful voice and played well. They hear your words, but they do not do them.

**33** When it comes — and it is coming — they will know that a prophet has been among them."

## Notes

- **vv2–9 the watchman** — the charge of 3:17–21, given again, word for word in vv7–9, at the turn of the book. This time it opens with a parable from ordinary war: the people of a land choose one of their own to watch, and if he blows the horn, whoever ignores it carries his own blood. The *ram's horn* is the *shofar*, the horn of alarm (Jeremiah 6:17).
- **v10 "we are rotting away in them"** — *nemaqqim*, the verb of the last curse in Leviticus: *Those of you who are left will rot away in their guilt in the lands of your enemies* (Leviticus 26:39). The exiles say the curse back as their own verdict on themselves. The answer, in v11, is the question of 18:31: *Why will you die, house of Israel?*
- **vv12–20 the righteous and the wicked** — chapter 18 again, compressed, with the same complaint and the same reply: *The way of the Lord is not fair* (18:25, 29). It comes here because the people now accept that they are guilty; they no longer blame their fathers, they despair. The chapter answers both with the same thing: a man is judged by the way he is walking now.
- **v15 "the statutes of life"** — *chuqqot ha-chayyim*, a phrase found only here. It gathers up Leviticus 18:5, *the person who does them shall live by them*, which Ezekiel quoted in 20:11: statutes that give life to whoever keeps them.
- **v21 "in the twelfth year of our exile, in the tenth month"** — the years run from King Jehoiachin's exile (1:2). Jerusalem was breached *in the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month* (Jeremiah 39:2), and Ezra's journey from Babylon to Jerusalem took four months (Ezra 7:9). The Greek dates the fugitive's arrival to *the twelfth month*.
- **vv21–22 "The city has been struck down… I was no longer mute"** — what the LORD promised at the death of Ezekiel's wife: *On that day your mouth will be opened to the fugitive, and you will speak and no longer be mute* (24:27). Since 3:26 he has spoken only when the LORD opened his mouth. *Hukkethah ha-ir*, **the city has been struck**, is the phrase the book uses again to date its last vision, fourteen years after the city fell (40:1).
- **v24 "Abraham was only one man"** — the people left in the ruins argue from Abraham: if one man could hold the land, how much more the many. Isaiah draws the opposite conclusion from the same fact: *he was one when I called him, and I blessed him and made him many* (Isaiah 51:2). The land came to Abraham as a gift, and vv25–26 say these heirs have kept nothing of what the gift required.
- **v25 "You eat meat with the blood in it"** — *al ha-dam tokhelu*, the very words of Leviticus 19:26, *You shall not eat anything with the blood in it*. The Greek has nothing of vv25b–26; it goes from *Therefore say to them* straight to v27.
- **vv31–32 "a show of love… a love song"** — *agavim*, from *agav*, the word for Oholah's lust after the Assyrians (23:5). It is desire, not the covenant word *ahavah*. The prophet has become entertainment, a singer they come to hear and do not obey. The Greek reads *a lie in their mouth* in v31, and in v32 compares him to the sound of a sweet, well-tuned harp.
- **v33 "they will know that a prophet has been among them"** — the closing words of his commissioning (2:5), now that the word he spoke has come true.

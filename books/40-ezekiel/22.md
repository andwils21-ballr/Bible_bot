# Ezekiel 22

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "As for you, son of man, will you judge, will you judge the city of bloodshed? Then make known to her all her detestable things,

**3** and say: This is what the Lord GOD says: You city that sheds blood in your midst, so that your time comes, and makes idols for yourself, to make yourself unclean!

**4** You have become guilty by the blood you have shed, and unclean by the idols you have made. You have brought your days near and come to the end of your years. Therefore I have made you a reproach to the nations and a mockery to all the countries.

**5** Those near you and those far from you will mock you, you of unclean name, full of tumult.

**6** The princes of Israel in you, each by his own power, have been bent on shedding blood.

**7** In you father and mother are treated with contempt; in you they have oppressed the guest; in you they have wronged the orphan and the widow.

**8** You have despised My holy things and profaned My sabbaths.

**9** In you are slanderers, bent on shedding blood; in you they eat on the mountains; in your midst they commit lewdness.

**10** In you they uncover their fathers' nakedness; in you they force women who are unclean in their impurity.

**11** One commits a detestable act with his neighbor's wife; another lewdly defiles his daughter-in-law; another in you forces his sister, his father's daughter.

**12** In you they take bribes to shed blood. You take interest and increase, you make gain of your neighbors by extortion, and you have forgotten Me, declares the Lord GOD.

**13** Now I strike My hand at the dishonest gain you have made and at the blood that has been in your midst.

**14** Can your heart endure, or can your hands be strong, in the days when I deal with you? I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it.

**15** I will scatter you among the nations and disperse you through the countries, and I will remove your uncleanness from you.

**16** You will be profaned through yourself in the sight of the nations, and you will know that I am the LORD."

## Dross in the Furnace

**17** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**18** "Son of man, the house of Israel has become dross to Me. All of them are copper and tin and iron and lead in the furnace; they have become the dross of silver.

**19** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have all become dross, I am about to gather you into the middle of Jerusalem.

**20** As one gathers silver and copper and iron and lead and tin into a furnace, to blow fire on it to melt it, so I will gather you in My anger and in My wrath, and I will put you in and melt you.

**21** I will gather you and blow on you with the fire of My wrath, and you will be melted in it.

**22** As silver is melted in a furnace, so you will be melted in it; and you will know that I, the LORD, have poured out My wrath on you."

## No One in the Breach

**23** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**24** "Son of man, say to her: You are a land that is not cleansed, not rained upon in the day of indignation.

**25** There is a conspiracy of her prophets in her midst, like a roaring lion tearing prey. They have devoured souls; they take treasure and precious things; they have made many widows in her midst.

**26** Her priests have done violence to My law and profaned My holy things. They have not separated the holy from the ordinary, nor taught the difference between the unclean and the clean; they have hidden their eyes from My sabbaths, and I am profaned among them.

**27** Her princes in her midst are like wolves tearing prey, shedding blood and destroying lives to get dishonest gain.

**28** Her prophets have plastered for them with whitewash, seeing empty visions and divining lies for them, saying, 'This is what the Lord GOD says,' when the LORD has not spoken.

**29** The people of the land have practiced extortion and committed robbery; they have wronged the poor and needy and oppressed the guest without justice.

**30** I looked for a man among them who would build up the wall and stand in the breach before Me for the land, so that I would not destroy it; but I found no one.

**31** So I have poured out My indignation on them; I have consumed them with the fire of My wrath; I have brought their way on their own heads, declares the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "will you judge, will you judge the city of bloodshed?"** — *ir ha-damim*, "the city of bloods," the name the book gives Jerusalem again at 24:6, 9. The question is the one of 20:4: the prophet is made the judge, and the charge sheet follows.
- **vv7–12 "father and mother are treated with contempt… the guest… the orphan and the widow… their fathers' nakedness… interest and increase"** — the list follows the law, nearly line by line: the commands of honor, care for the guest and the widow, the forbidden unions, *You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's wife* (Leviticus 18:8), and *Take no interest from him and no increase* (Leviticus 25:36). The chapter ends where it began: *you have forgotten Me* (v12).
- **vv18–22 "the house of Israel has become dross… copper and tin and iron and lead in the furnace"** — Isaiah's charge, *Your silver has become dross* (Isaiah 1:22), made into a smelting. The furnace is Jerusalem itself, and no silver is said to come out of it.
- **v25 "There is a conspiracy of her prophets in her midst"** — *qesher nevi'eha*. The Greek reads *whose leaders* (*nesi'eha*, "her princes," a difference of one letter), and the verse fits rulers: they *take treasure* and *make many widows*, and the prophets have their own verse (v28).
- **v26 "They have not separated the holy from the ordinary, nor taught the difference between the unclean and the clean"** — the priests' charge at their ordination: *so that you can separate the holy from the ordinary, and the unclean from the clean* (Leviticus 10:10).
- **v30 "stand in the breach before Me for the land… but I found no one"** — what Moses did: *had not Moses His chosen one stood in the breach before Him* (Psalm 106:23). The prophets of 13:5 *have not gone up into the breaches*; here God looks for anyone at all.

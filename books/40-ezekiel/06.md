# Ezekiel 6

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, set your face toward the mountains of Israel, and prophesy against them,

**3** and say: Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD! This is what the Lord GOD says to the mountains and to the hills, to the ravines and to the valleys: I Myself am about to bring a sword on you, and I will destroy your high places.

**4** Your altars will be made desolate, your incense altars will be broken, and I will make your slain fall before your idols.

**5** I will lay the corpses of the sons of Israel before their idols, and I will scatter your bones around your altars.

**6** In all your dwelling places the cities will be laid waste and the high places made desolate, so that your altars will be laid waste and bear their guilt, your idols will be broken and brought to an end, your incense altars cut down, and your works wiped out.

**7** The slain will fall in your midst, and you will know that I am the LORD.

**8** Yet I will leave a remnant, when you have some who escape the sword among the nations, when you are scattered through the countries.

**9** Those of you who escape will remember Me among the nations where they are taken captive, how I was broken by their heart that gave itself to others and turned away from Me, and by their eyes that gave themselves to their idols. They will loathe themselves for the evils they have done, for all their detestable things.

**10** They will know that I am the LORD; I did not speak in vain of doing this harm to them.

**11** This is what the Lord GOD says: Strike with your hand and stamp with your foot, and say, 'Alas!' over all the evil detestable things of the house of Israel, who will fall by the sword, by famine and by pestilence.

**12** He who is far off will die of pestilence, he who is near will fall by the sword, and he who is left and is besieged will die of famine. So I will spend My wrath on them.

**13** You will know that I am the LORD, when their slain lie among their idols around their altars, on every high hill, on all the mountaintops, under every green tree and under every leafy oak, the places where they offered a soothing aroma to all their idols.

**14** I will stretch out My hand against them and make the land a desolation and a waste, from the wilderness to Diblah, in all their dwelling places; and they will know that I am the LORD."

## Notes

- **vv3–6 "I will destroy your high places… your incense altars will be broken… I will make your slain fall before your idols"** — the curse of the law, almost word for word: *I will destroy your high places and cut down your incense altars, and throw your corpses on the corpses of your idols* (Leviticus 26:30). *Gillulim*, "idols," is the law's word for them, and Ezekiel's word for them throughout the book.
- **vv7, 10, 13, 14 "you will know that I am the LORD"** — the sentence that runs through the book, four times in this chapter alone. What the judgment is for is that it be known who is speaking.
- **v9 "how I was broken by their heart that gave itself to others"** — *nishbarti*, "I was broken," God speaking of His own heart broken by theirs. The Greek has *I have sworn*, reading the same letters with one different (*nishba'ti*). The Hebrew is followed.
- **v9 "They will loathe themselves"** — *ve-naqottu bi-feneihem*, "they will loathe in their own faces." The survivors' turning begins not with the land restored but with disgust at what they did.
- **v13 "the places where they offered a soothing aroma to all their idols"** — *re'ach nichoach*, the law's phrase for the offering that pleases the LORD (Leviticus 1:9), given to idols on every hill. Where they offered it, their slain will lie.
- **v14 "from the wilderness to Diblah"** — *Diblatah*, a place named only here; the Greek has the same name. The phrase seems to mark the land from end to end, but where Diblah was is not known.

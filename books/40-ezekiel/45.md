# Ezekiel 45

## The Holy Portion of the Land

**1** "When you allot the land as an inheritance, you shall set apart a contribution to the LORD, a holy portion of the land, twenty-five thousand long and ten thousand wide. It shall be holy throughout its whole area.

**2** Of this, a square of five hundred by five hundred shall be for the sanctuary, with fifty cubits of open land around it.

**3** From this measured area you shall measure off twenty-five thousand long and ten thousand wide, and in it shall be the sanctuary, the most holy place.

**4** It is a holy portion of the land; it shall be for the priests who minister in the sanctuary, who come near to minister to the LORD. It shall be a place for their houses, and a holy place for the sanctuary.

**5** An area twenty-five thousand long and ten thousand wide shall be for the Levites who minister in the house, as their own possession: towns to live in.

**6** As the possession of the city you shall assign an area five thousand wide and twenty-five thousand long, alongside the holy contribution; it shall belong to the whole house of Israel.

## The Prince's Land, and Honest Measures

**7** The prince shall have land on both sides of the holy contribution and of the possession of the city, alongside them, on the west side westward and on the east side eastward, matching one of the tribal portions in length, from the west border to the east border.

**8** This land shall be his possession in Israel. My princes shall no longer wrong My people; they shall give the land to the house of Israel by their tribes.

**9** This is what the Lord GOD says: Enough, princes of Israel! Put away violence and plunder, and do what is just and right. Stop driving My people off their land, declares the Lord GOD.

**10** You shall have honest scales, an honest ephah and an honest bath.

**11** The ephah and the bath shall be the same measure: the bath a tenth of a homer, and the ephah a tenth of a homer; the homer shall be the standard.

**12** The shekel shall be twenty gerahs. Twenty shekels, twenty-five shekels and fifteen shekels shall make your mina.

## The Offerings and the Feasts

**13** This is the contribution you shall set apart: a sixth of an ephah from each homer of wheat, and a sixth of an ephah from each homer of barley;

**14** and the fixed portion of oil, measured by the bath: a tenth of a bath from each kor, which is ten baths, a homer, for ten baths make a homer;

**15** and one sheep from every flock of two hundred, from the watered pastures of Israel: for grain offerings, burnt offerings and peace offerings, to make atonement for them, declares the Lord GOD.

**16** All the people of the land shall give this contribution to the prince in Israel.

**17** It shall be the prince's duty to provide the burnt offerings, the grain offering and the drink offering at the feasts, the new moons and the sabbaths, at all the appointed feasts of the house of Israel. He shall provide the sin offering, the grain offering, the burnt offering and the peace offerings, to make atonement for the house of Israel.

**18** This is what the Lord GOD says: In the first month, on the first of the month, you shall take a young bull without defect and purify the sanctuary.

**19** The priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering and put it on the doorpost of the house, on the four corners of the ledge of the altar, and on the doorpost of the gate of the inner court.

**20** You shall do the same on the seventh of the month for anyone who sins by mistake or through ignorance; so you shall make atonement for the house.

**21** In the first month, on the fourteenth of the month, you shall have the Passover, a feast of seven days; unleavened bread shall be eaten.

**22** On that day the prince shall provide for himself and for all the people of the land a bull for a sin offering.

**23** During the seven days of the feast he shall provide as a burnt offering to the LORD seven bulls and seven rams without defect each day for the seven days, and a male goat each day for a sin offering.

**24** He shall provide as a grain offering an ephah for each bull and an ephah for each ram, and a hin of oil for each ephah.

**25** In the seventh month, on the fifteenth of the month, at the feast, he shall provide the same for seven days: the same sin offering, burnt offering, grain offering and oil."

## Notes

- **v1 "a contribution to the LORD"** — *terumah*, the word for the half-shekel every man gave, *half a shekel as a contribution to the LORD* (Exodus 30:13). Here what is set apart is a strip of the land itself. The Hebrew gives no unit for the large figures (vv1–7). The Greek makes the width *twenty thousand* in vv1, 3 and 5.
- **v2 "a square of five hundred by five hundred… fifty cubits"** — the area the wall of 42:20 encloses, *five hundred long and five hundred wide*, with an open margin measured in cubits around it.
- **v5 "towns to live in"** — the Hebrew has *esrim leshakhot*, **twenty chambers**; the Greek has *poleis tou katoikein*, **towns to live in**, which is *arim la-shevet* in Hebrew, nearly the same letters. The rendering follows the Greek: the verse is giving the Levites *their own possession*, and it is the phrase of the law for the Levites' share, *towns to live in* (Numbers 35:2). Choice in the report.
- **vv7–9 the prince's land** — the prince is given land of his own so that he will not take theirs: *My princes shall no longer wrong My people*, the verb of the law of the land, *you shall not wrong one another* (Leviticus 25:14). *Gerushot* in v9 is **driving out**, evictions. Jerusalem's princes had been *like wolves tearing prey* (22:27).
- **v10 "honest scales, an honest ephah and an honest bath"** — *tsedeq*, the law of Leviticus 19:36, *honest scales, honest weights, an honest ephah and an honest hin*.
- **v12 "Twenty shekels, twenty-five shekels and fifteen shekels"** — sixty shekels to the mina, stated as a sum of three weights. *The shekel is twenty gerahs* is Exodus 30:13. The Greek gives the mina as *fifty shekels*.
- **v15 "one sheep from every flock of two hundred"** — the Greek has *one from ten*.
- **vv18–20 cleansing the sanctuary** — a bull on the first day of the first month, and again *on the seventh of the month* for those who sin *by mistake* (*shogeh*, the word of Leviticus 4:2). The Greek has *in the seventh month, on the first of the month*: two cleansings a year, six months apart.
- **vv21–25 the feasts** — Passover with seven days of unleavened bread (Leviticus 23:5–6), and the feast of the seventh month on the fifteenth, the Festival of Booths (Leviticus 23:34), here not named. *A feast of seven days* is *chag shevu'ot yamim*, **a feast of weeks of days**, in the Hebrew; the Greek reads it as seven days. The list names no Day of Atonement (Leviticus 23:27); the house is cleansed in the first month (vv18–20).

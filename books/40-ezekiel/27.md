# Ezekiel 27

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "As for you, son of man, take up a lament over Tyre,

**3** and say to Tyre, you who dwell at the entrances to the sea, merchant of the peoples to many coastlands: This is what the Lord GOD says:  
Tyre, you have said,  
'I am perfect in beauty.'

**4** Your borders are in the heart of the seas;  
your builders made your beauty perfect.

**5** They made all your planks  
of fir from Senir;  
they took a cedar from Lebanon  
to make a mast for you.

**6** Of oaks from Bashan  
they made your oars;  
your deck they made of ivory set in cypress  
from the coastlands of Kittim.

**7** Fine linen with embroidery from Egypt  
was your sail,  
to be your banner;  
blue and purple from the coastlands of Elishah  
was your awning.

**8** The inhabitants of Sidon and Arvad  
were your rowers;  
your skilled men, Tyre, were in you;  
they were your sailors.

**9** The elders of Gebal and her skilled men were in you,  
caulking your seams;  
all the ships of the sea and their mariners were in you  
to trade for your goods.

**10** Persia and Lud and Put  
were in your army,  
your men of war;  
they hung shield and helmet in you;  
they gave you your splendor.

**11** The men of Arvad and your army  
were on your walls all around,  
and the Gammadim were in your towers;  
they hung their quivers on your walls all around;  
they made your beauty perfect.

**12** Tarshish traded with you because of your great wealth of every kind; silver, iron, tin and lead they gave for your wares.

**13** Javan, Tubal and Meshech traded with you; human beings and vessels of bronze they gave for your merchandise.

**14** From Beth-togarmah they gave horses, war horses and mules for your wares.

**15** The men of Dedan traded with you; many coastlands were your markets; they brought you tusks of ivory and ebony in payment.

**16** Aram traded with you because of the abundance of your products; turquoise, purple, embroidered work, fine linen, coral and rubies they gave for your wares.

**17** Judah and the land of Israel traded with you; wheat of Minnith, pannag, honey, oil and balm they gave for your merchandise.

**18** Damascus traded with you because of the abundance of your products and your great wealth of every kind, with wine of Helbon and white wool.

**19** Vedan and Javan from Uzal gave for your wares worked iron, cassia and calamus; they were among your merchandise.

**20** Dedan traded with you in saddlecloths for riding.

**21** Arabia and all the princes of Kedar were the merchants under your hand; in lambs, rams and goats they traded with you.

**22** The merchants of Sheba and Raamah traded with you; the best of all spices, every precious stone and gold they gave for your wares.

**23** Haran, Canneh and Eden, the merchants of Sheba, Asshur and Chilmad traded with you.

**24** They traded with you in choice garments, in cloaks of blue and embroidered work, and in carpets of many colors, bound with cords and firmly made, in your marketplace.

**25** The ships of Tarshish  
carried your merchandise;  
so you were filled and very heavy  
in the heart of the seas.

**26** Your rowers have brought you  
out into the great waters;  
the east wind has broken you  
in the heart of the seas.

**27** Your wealth, your wares, your merchandise,  
your mariners and your sailors,  
your caulkers, your dealers in merchandise,  
and all your men of war who are in you,  
with all your company that is in your midst,  
will fall into the heart of the seas  
on the day of your fall.

**28** At the sound of your sailors' cry  
the shores will shake.

**29** All who handle the oar,  
the mariners and all the sailors of the sea,  
will come down from their ships  
and stand on the land.

**30** They will raise their voices over you  
and cry out bitterly;  
they will throw dust on their heads  
and roll in ashes.

**31** They will make themselves bald for you  
and put on sackcloth,  
and weep over you in bitterness of soul  
with bitter mourning.

**32** In their wailing they will take up a lament for you  
and lament over you:  
'Who is like Tyre,  
like one silenced in the middle of the sea?'

**33** When your wares went out from the seas,  
you satisfied many peoples;  
with the abundance of your wealth and your merchandise  
you made the kings of the earth rich.

**34** Now you are broken by the seas,  
in the depths of the waters;  
your merchandise and all your company  
have fallen with you.

**35** All the inhabitants of the coastlands  
are appalled at you;  
their kings shudder with horror;  
their faces are troubled.

**36** The merchants among the peoples hiss at you;  
you have become a terror  
and will be no more forever."

## Notes

- **vv3–11 "Tyre, you have said, 'I am perfect in beauty'… your builders made your beauty perfect"** — the lament pictures the city as a ship, built of the best the world could give: fir from Senir (Mount Hermon), cedar from Lebanon, oaks from Bashan, linen from Egypt, and crewed and guarded by the peoples of the coast. *Perfect in beauty*, *kelilat yofi*, comes back at 28:12, said of Tyre's king.
- **vv12–24 the traders** — a list of the known world, many of its names from the table of nations: *Javan and Tubal and Meshech* (Genesis 10:2), *Togarmah* (10:3), *Elishah and Tarshish, Kittim* (10:4), *Sheba and Dedan* (10:7). Tyre's goods reach from Tarshish in the west to Sheba in the south and Asshur in the east.
- **v13 "human beings and vessels of bronze"** — *nefesh adam*, "souls of men," traded beside bronze pots: slaves.
- **v17 "Judah and the land of Israel traded with you; wheat of Minnith, pannag, honey, oil and balm"** — Israel's farm goods, some of them the gifts Jacob sent to Egypt, *a little balm and a little honey* (Genesis 43:11). *Pannag* is found only here, and what it was is not known; the Greek has *perfumes and cassia*.
- **v19 "Vedan and Javan from Uzal"** — the Hebrew of the opening words is uncertain; *Uzal* is named among the sons of Joktan (Genesis 10:27).
- **v26 "the east wind has broken you in the heart of the seas"** — the ship, loaded so heavy that it rides low, is wrecked by the wind that dried up the vine of 17:10 and 19:12.
- **vv28–36 "At the sound of your sailors' cry the shores will shake… Who is like Tyre?"** — the sailors who survive come ashore and mourn as for the dead: dust, ashes, shaved heads, sackcloth. *Who is like Tyre?* echoes the praise of God, *Who is like You among the gods, LORD?* (Exodus 15:11), now said of a sunken ship.
- **v36 "you have become a terror and will be no more forever"** — the refrain that ends each Tyre oracle (26:21; 27:36; 28:19).

# Ezekiel 16

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, make known to Jerusalem her detestable things,

**3** and say: This is what the Lord GOD says to Jerusalem: Your origin and your birth are of the land of the Canaanites; your father was an Amorite, and your mother a Hittite.

**4** As for your birth, on the day you were born your navel cord was not cut, you were not washed with water to cleanse you, you were not rubbed with salt, and you were not wrapped in cloths.

**5** No eye pitied you, to do any one of these things for you out of compassion; you were thrown out into the open field, so loathed were you on the day you were born.

**6** I passed by you and saw you kicking about in your blood, and I said to you in your blood, 'Live!' I said to you in your blood, 'Live!'

**7** I made you grow like a plant of the field, and you grew up and became tall and came to full beauty; your breasts were formed and your hair had grown, yet you were naked and bare.

**8** I passed by you again and saw you, and your time was the time for love. I spread My wing over you and covered your nakedness; I swore an oath to you and entered into a covenant with you, declares the Lord GOD, and you became Mine.

**9** Then I washed you with water, rinsed your blood off you, and anointed you with oil.

**10** I clothed you with embroidered cloth and put sandals of fine leather on your feet; I wrapped you in fine linen and covered you with silk.

**11** I adorned you with ornaments, put bracelets on your arms and a chain around your neck,

**12** a ring in your nose, earrings in your ears, and a beautiful crown on your head.

**13** So you were adorned with gold and silver, and your clothing was fine linen, silk and embroidered cloth. You ate fine flour, honey and oil; you became exceedingly beautiful and rose to be a queen.

**14** Your fame went out among the nations because of your beauty, for it was perfect through My splendor that I had put on you, declares the Lord GOD.

## The Bride Who Prostituted Herself

**15** But you trusted in your beauty and prostituted yourself on the strength of your fame, and poured out your prostitution on everyone who passed by; it was his.

**16** You took some of your garments and made for yourself high places decked with many colors, and prostituted yourself on them; such things have never been and never shall be.

**17** You took your beautiful jewels, of My gold and My silver that I had given you, and made for yourself images of men, and prostituted yourself with them.

**18** You took your embroidered garments and covered them, and set My oil and My incense before them.

**19** My bread that I gave you, the fine flour, oil and honey with which I fed you, you set before them as a soothing aroma; so it was, declares the Lord GOD.

**20** You took your sons and your daughters whom you had borne to Me and sacrificed them to these images to be devoured. Was your prostitution so small a thing,

**21** that you slaughtered My children and gave them up to them, making them pass over to them?

**22** In all your detestable things and your prostitution you did not remember the days of your youth, when you were naked and bare, kicking about in your blood.

**23** After all your wickedness — woe, woe to you, declares the Lord GOD —

**24** you built yourself a mound and made yourself a lofty place in every square.

**25** At the head of every road you built your lofty place and made your beauty detestable, spreading your legs to everyone who passed by, and multiplied your prostitution.

**26** You prostituted yourself with the Egyptians, your neighbors, great of flesh, and multiplied your prostitution to provoke Me.

**27** So I stretched out My hand against you, cut down your portion, and gave you over to the will of those who hate you, the daughters of the Philistines, who were ashamed of your lewd way.

**28** You prostituted yourself with the Assyrians, because you were never satisfied; you prostituted yourself with them, and still you were not satisfied.

**29** You multiplied your prostitution with the land of traders, Chaldea, and even with this you were not satisfied.

**30** How sick is your heart, declares the Lord GOD, that you did all these things, the deeds of a brazen prostitute,

**31** building your mound at the head of every road and making your lofty place in every square! Yet you were not like a prostitute, because you scorned payment.

**32** You adulterous wife, who takes strangers instead of her husband!

**33** All prostitutes are given gifts, but you gave your gifts to all your lovers, and bribed them to come to you from every side for your prostitution.

**34** So you were the opposite of other women in your prostitution: no one solicited you, and you gave payment when no payment was given to you. You were the opposite.

## The Judgment

**35** Therefore, prostitute, hear the word of the LORD.

**36** This is what the Lord GOD says: Because your copper was poured out and your nakedness uncovered in your prostitution with your lovers and with all your detestable idols, and because of the blood of your children whom you gave to them,

**37** therefore I am about to gather all your lovers with whom you took pleasure, all whom you loved and all whom you hated; I will gather them against you from every side and uncover your nakedness to them, and they will see all your nakedness.

**38** I will judge you with the judgments of women who commit adultery and shed blood, and bring on you the blood of wrath and jealousy.

**39** I will give you into their hands, and they will tear down your mound and break down your lofty places; they will strip you of your clothes, take your beautiful jewels and leave you naked and bare.

**40** They will bring up an assembly against you, and they will stone you and cut you to pieces with their swords.

**41** They will burn your houses with fire and carry out judgments on you in the sight of many women. I will put an end to your prostitution, and you will no longer give payment.

**42** So I will satisfy My wrath on you, and My jealousy will turn away from you; I will be quiet and no longer angry.

**43** Because you did not remember the days of your youth but enraged Me with all these things, I in turn will bring your way on your own head, declares the Lord GOD. Have you not committed lewdness on top of all your detestable things?

## Your Sisters

**44** Everyone who uses proverbs will use this proverb about you: 'Like mother, like daughter.'

**45** You are your mother's daughter, who loathed her husband and her children; and you are the sister of your sisters, who loathed their husbands and their children. Your mother was a Hittite and your father an Amorite.

**46** Your elder sister is Samaria, who lives with her daughters to the north of you; and your younger sister, who lives to the south of you, is Sodom with her daughters.

**47** You did not merely walk in their ways and do as their detestable things; as if that were too little, you were more corrupt than they in all your ways.

**48** As I live, declares the Lord GOD, your sister Sodom and her daughters have not done as you and your daughters have done.

**49** This was the iniquity of your sister Sodom: she and her daughters had pride, plenty of bread and careless ease, but did not strengthen the hand of the poor and needy.

**50** They were haughty and did detestable things before Me, so I removed them when I saw it.

**51** Samaria did not commit half your sins. You have multiplied your detestable things more than they, and by all the detestable things you have done you have made your sisters look righteous.

**52** You also must bear your disgrace, for you have pleaded for your sisters: because of your sins, in which you acted more detestably than they, they are more righteous than you. So be ashamed, you also, and bear your disgrace, for you have made your sisters look righteous.

**53** I will restore their fortunes, the fortunes of Sodom and her daughters and the fortunes of Samaria and her daughters, and your own fortunes along with them,

**54** so that you may bear your disgrace and be ashamed of all you have done, when you become a comfort to them.

**55** Your sisters, Sodom and her daughters, will return to what they were before, and Samaria and her daughters will return to what they were before, and you and your daughters will return to what you were before.

**56** Was not your sister Sodom a byword in your mouth in the day of your pride,

**57** before your wickedness was uncovered? Now you are like her, the reproach of the daughters of Aram and of all around her, the daughters of the Philistines, who despise you on every side.

**58** You bear your lewdness and your detestable things, declares the LORD.

## An Everlasting Covenant

**59** For this is what the Lord GOD says: I will deal with you as you have done, you who despised the oath by breaking the covenant.

**60** Yet I Myself will remember My covenant with you in the days of your youth, and I will establish an everlasting covenant with you.

**61** Then you will remember your ways and be ashamed, when you receive your sisters, those older and younger than you; and I will give them to you as daughters, but not by your covenant.

**62** I Myself will establish My covenant with you, and you will know that I am the LORD,

**63** so that you may remember and be ashamed, and never open your mouth again because of your disgrace, when I make atonement for you for all you have done, declares the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **v3 "your father was an Amorite, and your mother a Hittite"** — Jerusalem was the city of *the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land*, before David took it (2 Samuel 5:6–7), and the oracle gives her the parents of the land's old peoples. In v45 the same parents explain why the daughter is like them.
- **vv4–6 "your navel cord was not cut… you were thrown out into the open field… I said to you in your blood, 'Live!'"** — a newborn left to die, without the care every child was given at birth. *In your blood, 'Live!'* is said twice in the Hebrew; the Greek has it once.
- **v8 "I spread My wing over you and covered your nakedness"** — *kenafi*, "My wing," the corner of the garment: Ruth's words to Boaz, *Spread your wing over your servant, for you are a redeemer* (Ruth 3:9). It is the act of taking a wife, and *I entered into a covenant with you… and you became Mine* says it outright.
- **vv10–13 "sandals of fine leather… fine linen and silk… fine flour, honey and oil"** — *tachash*, the leather of the tabernacle's covering (Exodus 25:5), and *meshi*, found only here and in v13, taken as a fine cloth. She is dressed in the sanctuary's own materials, *fine leather* and *fine linen* (Exodus 25:4–5), and fed *fine flour* and *oil*, the stuff of the grain offering (Leviticus 2:1); in vv18–19 she sets them before idols.
- **vv15–34 "prostituted yourself… your prostitution"** — *zanah* and its noun, more than twenty times in the chapter. The figure is the one Jeremiah used, *you have prostituted yourself with many lovers* (Jeremiah 3:1): the covenant is a marriage, and worship of other gods is unfaithfulness in it. The lovers are named as nations: Egypt (v26), Assyria (v28), Chaldea (v29).
- **v26 "your neighbors, great of flesh"** — *gidlei basar*, a coarse phrase the English keeps as blunt as the Hebrew.
- **vv31–34 "you scorned payment… you gave payment when no payment was given to you"** — the turn of the charge: she is worse than a prostitute, who is at least paid. *Bribed them*, *tishchadi*, is the word Kings uses of Ahaz, who sent the temple's silver and gold *to the king of Assyria as a bribe* (2 Kings 16:8).
- **v36 "Because your copper was poured out"** — *nechushtekh*, from *nechoshet*, copper or bronze. The Greek also reads *your copper*, perhaps the payment of vv33–34; the word may also be a crude term for the body, and the sense is not certain.
- **vv46–52 "Samaria… Sodom… they are more righteous than you"** — Sodom's sin is named here not as Genesis 19 tells it but as *pride, plenty of bread and careless ease*, and failing *the poor and needy*. Jerusalem has done worse than both her sisters, and God's verdict is that they look righteous beside her.
- **vv60–63 "I Myself will remember My covenant… an everlasting covenant… when I make atonement for you"** — the chapter ends not with the stoning of vv40–41 but with God remembering *the days of your youth*, which she forgot (vv22, 43). *Kapper*, "make atonement," is the word of the altar, and here God does it Himself.

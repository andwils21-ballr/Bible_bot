# Ezekiel 25

## Ammon

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, set your face toward the Ammonites and prophesy against them.

**3** Say to the Ammonites: Hear the word of the Lord GOD! This is what the Lord GOD says: Because you said, 'Aha!' over My sanctuary when it was profaned, and over the land of Israel when it was made desolate, and over the house of Judah when they went into exile,

**4** therefore I am about to give you to the people of the east as a possession. They will set up their camps among you and pitch their tents among you; they will eat your fruit and drink your milk.

**5** I will make Rabbah a pasture for camels and the Ammonites a resting place for flocks; and you will know that I am the LORD.

**6** For this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you clapped your hands and stamped your feet and rejoiced with all the contempt of your soul over the land of Israel,

**7** therefore I have stretched out My hand against you; I will give you as plunder to the nations, cut you off from the peoples and make you perish from the countries. I will destroy you, and you will know that I am the LORD.

## Moab

**8** This is what the Lord GOD says: Because Moab and Seir said, 'The house of Judah is like all the nations,'

**9** therefore I am about to lay open the flank of Moab, stripping it of its cities, its cities on every side, the glory of the land, Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon and Kiriathaim.

**10** To the people of the east I will give it as a possession, along with the Ammonites, so that the Ammonites will not be remembered among the nations,

**11** and I will carry out judgments on Moab; and they will know that I am the LORD.

## Edom

**12** This is what the Lord GOD says: Because Edom took revenge on the house of Judah and has incurred great guilt by taking vengeance on them,

**13** therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I will stretch out My hand against Edom and cut off from it human and beast, and I will make it a ruin; from Teman to Dedan they will fall by the sword.

**14** I will lay My vengeance on Edom by the hand of My people Israel, and they will deal with Edom according to My anger and My wrath; and they will know My vengeance, declares the Lord GOD.

## The Philistines

**15** This is what the Lord GOD says: Because the Philistines acted in revenge and took vengeance with contempt of soul, to destroy, in never-ending hostility,

**16** therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to stretch out My hand against the Philistines; I will cut off the Cherethites and destroy the remnant of the seacoast.

**17** I will carry out great vengeance on them with furious rebukes; and they will know that I am the LORD, when I lay My vengeance on them."

---

## Notes

- **vv1–17 the nations** — the book turns from Jerusalem to its neighbors, and chapters 25–32 hold the oracles against the nations. The first four are the peoples around Judah, each judged for what it did or said when Jerusalem fell.

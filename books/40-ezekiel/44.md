# Ezekiel 44

## The Gate That Stays Shut

**1** Then he brought me back by the outer gate of the sanctuary, the one facing east, and it was shut.

**2** The LORD said to me, "This gate shall stay shut. It shall not be opened, and no one shall enter by it, for the LORD, the God of Israel, has entered by it; therefore it shall stay shut.

**3** As for the prince, he alone, as prince, may sit in it to eat bread before the LORD. He shall come in by way of the porch of the gate and go out by the same way."

**4** Then he brought me by way of the north gate to the front of the house. I looked, and the glory of the LORD filled the house of the LORD; and I fell on my face.

**5** The LORD said to me, "Son of man, set your heart, and look with your eyes and hear with your ears all that I tell you about all the statutes of the house of the LORD and all its laws. Set your heart on who may enter the house and on all who must go out of the sanctuary.

## Foreigners and the Levites

**6** Say to the rebellious house, to the house of Israel: This is what the Lord GOD says: Enough of all your detestable things, house of Israel!

**7** You brought in foreigners, uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, to be in My sanctuary and profane My house, when you offered My food, the fat and the blood; and they broke My covenant, on top of all your detestable things.

**8** You did not keep charge of My holy things; you set others to keep My charge in My sanctuary for you.

**9** This is what the Lord GOD says: No foreigner, uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, shall enter My sanctuary, of all the foreigners who are among the sons of Israel.

**10** But the Levites who went far from Me when Israel went astray, who went astray from Me after their idols, shall carry their guilt.

**11** They shall be ministers in My sanctuary, in charge of the gates of the house and ministering to the house. They shall slaughter the burnt offering and the sacrifice for the people, and they shall stand before the people to serve them.

**12** Because they served them before their idols and became a stumbling block of iniquity to the house of Israel, I have raised My hand against them, declares the Lord GOD, and they shall carry their guilt.

**13** They shall not come near Me to serve Me as priests, or come near any of My holy things, the most holy things; they shall bear their disgrace and the detestable things they have done.

**14** I will make them keepers of the charge of the house, for all its service and all that is to be done in it.

## The Sons of Zadok

**15** But the Levitical priests, the sons of Zadok, who kept the charge of My sanctuary when the sons of Israel went astray from Me, shall come near to Me to serve Me. They shall stand before Me to offer Me the fat and the blood, declares the Lord GOD.

**16** They shall enter My sanctuary, and they shall come near to My table to serve Me, and they shall keep My charge.

**17** And it shall come to pass, when they enter the gates of the inner court, that they shall wear linen garments. No wool shall be on them while they serve in the gates of the inner court and within the house.

**18** Linen turbans shall be on their heads and linen undergarments on their waists; they shall not gird themselves with anything that makes them sweat.

**19** When they go out into the outer court, into the outer court to the people, they shall take off the garments in which they have been serving and leave them in the holy chambers, and put on other garments, so that they do not make the people holy with their garments.

**20** They shall not shave their heads or let their hair grow long; they shall keep the hair of their heads trimmed.

**21** No priest shall drink wine when he enters the inner court.

**22** They shall not take as their wives a widow or a divorced woman, but only virgins of the seed of the house of Israel, or a widow who is the widow of a priest.

**23** They shall teach My people the difference between the holy and the ordinary, and make known to them the difference between the unclean and the clean.

**24** In a dispute they shall stand to judge, and judge it by My judgments. They shall keep My laws and My statutes at all My appointed feasts, and keep My sabbaths holy.

**25** A priest shall not go near a dead person and make himself unclean, except that for a father or a mother, a son or a daughter, a brother or a sister who has had no husband, they may make themselves unclean.

**26** After he is cleansed, seven days shall be counted for him.

**27** On the day he enters the holy place, the inner court, to serve in the holy place, he shall offer his sin offering, declares the Lord GOD.

**28** This shall be their inheritance: I am their inheritance. You shall give them no possession in Israel; I am their possession.

**29** They shall eat the grain offering, the sin offering and the guilt offering, and everything devoted in Israel shall be theirs.

**30** The first of all the firstfruits of every kind, and every contribution of every kind from all your contributions, shall be for the priests. You shall also give the priest the first of your dough, so that a blessing may rest on your house.

**31** The priests shall not eat anything, bird or animal, that died on its own or was torn by beasts.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "This gate shall stay shut"** — the gate by which the glory came back into the house (43:4) is closed after it: no one else walks where the LORD has walked. Only *the prince*, *nasi*, may sit in its porch to eat before the LORD: the title chapter 34 gave the coming David, *My servant David a prince among them* (34:24).
- **v6 "the rebellious house"** — *meri*, the name the LORD gave Israel when He called Ezekiel: *for they are a rebellious house* (2:5). Even in the vision of the new house, the old name is used once more.
- **vv7, 9 "uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh"** — the outward sign and the inward one together. Moses had said *Circumcise, then, the foreskin of your heart* (Deuteronomy 10:16), and Jeremiah *remove the foreskins of your hearts* (Jeremiah 4:4). *My food, the fat and the blood* is the law's own name for the offering: *food, an offering by fire to the LORD* (Leviticus 3:11).
- **vv10–14 the Levites who went astray** — the Levites who served at the high places are kept from the altar and set to guard the gates and slaughter for the people. Something like it is told of Josiah's reform: *the priests of the high places did not come up to the altar of the LORD in Jerusalem* (2 Kings 23:9). *They shall carry their guilt* is the law's phrase for the Levites' charge (Numbers 18:23).
- **v15 "the sons of Zadok, who kept the charge of My sanctuary"** — the line put *in the place of Abiathar* (1 Kings 2:35), after the word to Eli's house: *I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest* (1 Samuel 2:35).
- **vv17–18 linen, not wool** — the priests' clothes of Exodus, *linen undergarments to cover their naked flesh* (Exodus 28:42). *Ba-yeza*, **with sweat**, is a word found only here, from the root of *ze'ah*, the sweat of the curse, *By the sweat of your face you shall eat bread* (Genesis 3:19). The Greek has *they shall not gird themselves tightly*.
- **v19 "so that they do not make the people holy with their garments"** — holiness passes by touch, as the law says of the sin offering: *Whatever touches its flesh becomes holy* (Leviticus 6:27). The holy garments stay in the holy chambers.
- **vv20–22, 25 the priests' rules** — gathered from Leviticus: no hair hanging loose (10:6), no wine in the holy place (10:9), no unclean contact with the dead except the nearest kin (21:1–3). On marriage, Ezekiel asks of every priest what Leviticus asked of the high priest, *He shall take a virgin from his own people as a wife* (Leviticus 21:14), and adds the widow of a priest.
- **v28 "I am their inheritance… I am their possession"** — the priests' share since Moses: *I am your share and your inheritance among the sons of Israel* (Numbers 18:20). God Himself is their land.
- **v31 "that died on its own or was torn by beasts"** — the law for priests (Leviticus 22:8), and Ezekiel's own protest when he was told to bake his bread over dung: *I have never eaten an animal that died on its own or was torn by beasts* (4:14).

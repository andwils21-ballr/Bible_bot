# Ezekiel 32

**1** And it came to pass in the twelfth year, in the twelfth month, on the first of the month, that the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, take up a lament over Pharaoh king of Egypt and say to him:  
You thought yourself a young lion of the nations,  
but you are like the monster in the seas;  
you burst out in your rivers,  
muddied the water with your feet  
and fouled their rivers.

**3** This is what the Lord GOD says:  
I will spread My net over you  
with a company of many peoples,  
and they will haul you up in My dragnet.

**4** I will fling you on the land;  
I will hurl you onto the open field.  
I will make all the birds of the heavens settle on you,  
and fill the beasts of the whole earth with you.

**5** I will lay your flesh on the mountains  
and fill the valleys with your carcass.

**6** I will drench the land with your flowing blood,  
up to the mountains,  
and the ravines will be full of you.

**7** When I snuff you out, I will cover the heavens  
and darken their stars;  
I will cover the sun with a cloud,  
and the moon will not give its light.

**8** I will darken over you  
all the shining lamps in the heavens,  
and put darkness on your land,  
declares the Lord GOD.

**9** I will trouble the hearts of many peoples  
when I bring your ruin among the nations,  
to lands you have not known.

**10** I will make many peoples appalled at you,  
and their kings will shudder with horror over you  
when I brandish My sword before them;  
they will tremble every moment,  
each for his own life,  
on the day of your fall.

**11** For this is what the Lord GOD says:  
The sword of the king of Babylon  
will come upon you.

**12** By the swords of mighty ones  
I will make your multitude fall,  
all of them the most ruthless of nations.  
They will bring the pride of Egypt to ruin,  
and all her multitude will be destroyed.

**13** I will destroy all her cattle  
from beside the many waters;  
no human foot will muddy them again,  
nor will the hoofs of cattle muddy them.

**14** Then I will make their waters settle  
and make their rivers run like oil,  
declares the Lord GOD.

**15** When I make the land of Egypt a desolation,  
a land stripped of all that fills it,  
when I strike down all who live in it,  
they will know that I am the LORD.

**16** This is a lament, and they will chant it;  
the daughters of the nations will chant it;  
over Egypt and all her multitude they will chant it,  
declares the Lord GOD."

## Down to the Pit

**17** And it came to pass in the twelfth year, on the fifteenth of the month, that the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**18** "Son of man, wail over the multitude of Egypt and bring them down, her and the daughters of majestic nations, to the lowest parts of the earth, with those who go down to the pit.

**19** Whom do you surpass in beauty? Go down, and be laid with the uncircumcised!

**20** They will fall among those slain by the sword. The sword has been given; drag her away, with all her multitude!

**21** The mightiest of the mighty ones will speak to him from the middle of Sheol, with his helpers: 'They have come down; they lie still, the uncircumcised, slain by the sword.'

**22** Assyria is there with all her company, her graves all around her, all of them slain, fallen by the sword,

**23** whose graves are set in the far reaches of the pit, and her company is around her grave, all of them slain, fallen by the sword, who spread terror in the land of the living.

**24** Elam is there with all her multitude around her grave, all of them slain, fallen by the sword, who went down uncircumcised to the lowest parts of the earth, who spread their terror in the land of the living; they bear their disgrace with those who go down to the pit.

**25** Among the slain they have made a bed for her with all her multitude, her graves all around her; all of them uncircumcised, slain by the sword, for their terror was spread in the land of the living, and they bear their disgrace with those who go down to the pit; she is laid among the slain.

**26** Meshech and Tubal are there with all her multitude, her graves all around her; all of them uncircumcised, slain by the sword, for they spread their terror in the land of the living.

**27** They do not lie with the mighty ones who fell long ago, who went down to Sheol with their weapons of war, whose swords were laid under their heads and whose iniquities lay on their bones, for the terror of the mighty ones was in the land of the living.

**28** But you will be broken among the uncircumcised and lie with those slain by the sword.

**29** Edom is there, her kings and all her princes, who for all their might are laid with those slain by the sword; they lie with the uncircumcised, with those who go down to the pit.

**30** The chiefs of the north are there, all of them, and all the Sidonians, who went down with the slain, put to shame for all the terror their might inspired; they lie uncircumcised with those slain by the sword and bear their disgrace with those who go down to the pit.

**31** Pharaoh will see them and be comforted over all his multitude: Pharaoh and all his army, slain by the sword, declares the Lord GOD.

**32** For I spread My terror in the land of the living, and he will be laid among the uncircumcised, with those slain by the sword: Pharaoh and all his multitude, declares the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **v2 "You thought yourself a young lion of the nations"** — *kefir goyim nidmeita*. *Nidmeita* can be **you were likened**, the verb of 31:2, 18, *Whom are you like?*, as the Greek takes it; or, from a second verb with the same letters, **you are ruined**, the word of Isaiah 6:5, *Woe is me! For I am ruined*. A lament could open either way: *Young lion of the nations, you are ruined*. The monster is the *tannim* of 29:3.
- **v5 "your carcass"** — *ramutekha*, a word found only here. It seems to come from *rum*, **to be high**: the great body heaped up in the valleys. The Greek has *your blood*.
- **vv7–8 "When I snuff you out, I will cover the heavens… and put darkness on your land"** — the ninth plague again, *darkness over the land of Egypt* (Exodus 10:21), now as the end of Pharaoh's light. *The shining lamps in the heavens*, *me'orei or*, are the *lamps* of the fourth day (Genesis 1:14). The darkened sun and moon return in Isaiah 13:10 and in Jesus' words about the end: *the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light* (Matthew 24:29).
- **vv12, 21, 27 "mighty ones"** — *gibborim*, warriors. The Greek renders the word *gigantes*, **giants**, all three times, as it renders *the mighty ones who were of old* in Genesis 6:4: *the giants who were from of old*.
- **v17 "in the twelfth year, on the fifteenth of the month"** — the Hebrew gives no month. The Greek has *the first month*.
- **vv18–32 the nations in the pit** — Assyria, Elam, Meshech and Tubal, Edom, the chiefs of the north and the Sidonians, each laid with its multitude and its graves around it. The powers that *spread terror in the land of the living* lie *in the far reaches of the pit*, the place Isaiah gave the king of Babylon (Isaiah 14:15). Meshech and Tubal were Tyre's traders (27:13).
- **v27 "They do not lie with the mighty ones who fell long ago"** — the Hebrew has *nofelim me-arelim*, **fallen ones of the uncircumcised**. The rendering follows the Greek, which reads *me-olam*, **from of old**: *the giants who fell from of old*, the words of Genesis 6:4, *the mighty ones who were of old*; the pit already holds *the people of old* (26:20). They were buried as warriors, with their weapons and their swords under their heads, and Meshech and Tubal are not laid with them. The Greek has no *not*: in it, they lie together.
- **v31 "Pharaoh will see them and be comforted"** — *ve-nicham*, the comfort of the fallen trees in 31:16: that he is not alone among the dead.
- **v32 "For I spread My terror"** — the written text has *chittito*, **his terror**, which the Greek also has; the reading tradition, followed here, has *chittiti*, **My terror**.

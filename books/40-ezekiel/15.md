# Ezekiel 15

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, how is the wood of the vine better than any other wood,  
the vine branch that is among the trees of the forest?

**3** Is wood taken from it to make anything?  
Do people take a peg from it to hang any vessel on?

**4** It is thrown into the fire for fuel;  
the fire has consumed both its ends, and its middle is charred.  
Is it good for anything?

**5** Even when it was whole, it could not be made into anything;  
how much less, when the fire has consumed it and it is charred,  
can it ever be made into anything!

**6** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: As the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so I have given up the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**7** I will set My face against them; they have come out of the fire, but the fire will consume them; and you will know that I am the LORD, when I set My face against them.

**8** I will make the land a desolation, because they have broken faith, declares the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **v2 "the wood of the vine"** — the vine was the prophets' picture of Israel: *the vineyard of the LORD of hosts is the house of Israel* (Isaiah 5:7), and *I had planted you a choice vine* (Jeremiah 2:21). Ezekiel asks only about its wood. A vine is valued for its fruit; without fruit, its wood is good for nothing.
- **vv4–5 "the fire has consumed both its ends, and its middle is charred"** — a stick burnt at both ends: Jerusalem after the first exile, the one Ezekiel dates his visions by (1:2). *How much less*: if it was useless whole, it is useless now.
- **v7 "they have come out of the fire, but the fire will consume them"** — those left in Jerusalem after the first fire will not escape the next.
- **v8 "because they have broken faith"** — *ma'alu ma'al*, the verb and its own noun, the law's word for a breach of trust against the LORD: *the treachery they committed against Me* (Leviticus 26:40). Chapter 14 used the same words of a land that *sins against Me by breaking faith* (14:13).

# Ezekiel 19

**1** "As for you, take up a lament for the princes of Israel,

**2** and say:  
What a lioness was your mother  
among the lions!  
She lay down among young lions;  
she reared her cubs.

**3** She brought up one of her cubs;  
he became a young lion.  
He learned to tear prey;  
he devoured men.

**4** The nations heard about him;  
he was caught in their pit,  
and they brought him with hooks  
to the land of Egypt.

**5** When she saw that she had waited in vain,  
that her hope was lost,  
she took another of her cubs  
and made him a young lion.

**6** He prowled among the lions;  
he became a young lion.  
He learned to tear prey;  
he devoured men.

**7** He knew their widows  
and laid waste their cities;  
the land and all in it were appalled  
at the sound of his roaring.

**8** The nations set against him  
from the provinces all around;  
they spread their net over him;  
he was caught in their pit.

**9** With hooks they put him in a cage  
and brought him to the king of Babylon;  
they brought him in nets,  
so that his voice would no longer be heard  
on the mountains of Israel.

**10** Your mother was like a vine in your blood,  
planted by the waters;  
she was fruitful and full of branches  
because of many waters.

**11** She had strong stems  
fit for the staffs of rulers.  
Its height towered  
among the thick branches,  
and it was seen in its height  
with the mass of its boughs.

**12** But it was torn up in fury  
and thrown to the ground;  
the east wind dried up its fruit;  
its strong stems were broken off and withered;  
the fire consumed it.

**13** Now it is planted in the wilderness,  
in a dry and thirsty land.

**14** Fire has gone out from its stem  
and consumed its shoots and its fruit,  
so that there is no strong stem in it,  
no staff to rule.  
This is a lament, and it has become a lament."

## Notes

- **v1 "take up a lament"** — *qinah*, the song sung over the dead, here sung over the royal house of Judah. The chapter ends by naming itself the same way: *This is a lament, and it has become a lament*.
- **vv2–3 "What a lioness was your mother… one of her cubs… a young lion"** — Jacob's blessing gave Judah the lion: *A lion's cub is Judah… He crouched, he lay down like a lion, and like a lioness* (Genesis 49:9). The mother is the royal house of Judah, and the cubs her kings.
- **vv3–4 "he was caught in their pit, and they brought him with hooks to the land of Egypt"** — the cubs are not named. The first matches Jehoahaz, whom Pharaoh *took away, and he came to Egypt and died there* (2 Kings 23:34); the second, taken to the king of Babylon (v9), one of the kings carried there after him.
- **v7 "He knew their widows"** — the Hebrew as it stands, *va-yeda almenotav*, "he knew his widows"; the sense is not certain. The Greek reads differently, *he fed on his boldness*. Read with v3's *he devoured men*, the widows are those he made.
- **v10 "Your mother was like a vine in your blood"** — *be-damkha*, "in your blood," a phrase whose sense here is not settled; the same letters can also be heard as "in your likeness," from *damah*, "be like."
- **vv11, 14 "fit for the staffs of rulers… no staff to rule"** — *shevet*, the word of Jacob's promise, *The staff shall not turn aside from Judah* (Genesis 49:10). The lament ends with the staff gone: the promise to Judah sung as a funeral song.

# Ezekiel 12

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, you live in the middle of a rebellious house, who have eyes to see but do not see, and ears to hear but do not hear, for they are a rebellious house.

**3** As for you, son of man, prepare for yourself baggage for exile, and go into exile by day in their sight. Go into exile from your place to another place in their sight. Perhaps they will see, though they are a rebellious house.

**4** Bring out your baggage by day in their sight, as baggage for exile; and go out yourself at evening in their sight, as people go out into exile.

**5** In their sight dig through the wall and carry it out through it.

**6** In their sight lift it onto your shoulder and carry it out in the dark; cover your face so that you do not see the land, for I have made you a sign for the house of Israel."

**7** I did as I was commanded. I brought out my baggage by day, as baggage for exile, and at evening I dug through the wall with my hand; I carried it out in the dark, lifting it onto my shoulder in their sight.

**8** In the morning the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**9** "Son of man, has not the house of Israel, the rebellious house, said to you, 'What are you doing?'

**10** Say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: This burden concerns the prince in Jerusalem, and all the house of Israel who are in it.

**11** Say: I am your sign. As I have done, so it will be done to them; they will go into exile, into captivity.

**12** The prince who is among them will lift his baggage onto his shoulder in the dark and go out; they will dig through the wall to bring him out through it. He will cover his face, so that he will not see the land with his eyes.

**13** I will spread My net over him, and he will be caught in My snare; and I will bring him to Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans, yet he will not see it, and there he will die.

**14** All who are around him, his helpers and all his troops, I will scatter to every wind, and I will draw out a sword after them.

**15** They will know that I am the LORD, when I disperse them among the nations and scatter them through the countries.

**16** But I will leave a few of them from the sword, from famine and from pestilence, so that they may tell of all their detestable things among the nations where they go; and they will know that I am the LORD."

**17** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**18** "Son of man, eat your bread with trembling, and drink your water with quaking and anxiety.

**19** Say to the people of the land: This is what the Lord GOD says concerning the inhabitants of Jerusalem in the land of Israel: They will eat their bread with anxiety and drink their water in horror, because her land will be stripped of all it holds, because of the violence of all who live in it.

**20** The inhabited cities will be laid waste, and the land will become a desolation; and you will know that I am the LORD."

**21** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**22** "Son of man, what is this proverb you have about the land of Israel, saying, 'The days go on and on, and every vision comes to nothing'?

**23** Therefore say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: I will put an end to this proverb, and they will no longer quote it in Israel. Say to them instead: 'The days are near, and the word of every vision.'

**24** For there will no longer be any empty vision or flattering divination within the house of Israel.

**25** For I am the LORD; I will speak, and the word that I speak will be done. It will no longer be delayed; for in your days, rebellious house, I will speak the word and do it, declares the Lord GOD."

**26** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**27** "Son of man, the house of Israel is saying, 'The vision that he sees is for many days from now, and he prophesies of times far off.'

**28** Therefore say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: None of My words will be delayed any longer; the word that I speak will be done, declares the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **v2 "who have eyes to see but do not see, and ears to hear but do not hear"** — Jeremiah's words for the same people: *who have eyes but do not see, who have ears but do not hear* (Jeremiah 5:21).
- **vv3–7 "baggage for exile… dig through the wall… cover your face so that you do not see the land"** — Ezekiel acts out the escape: a pack by day, a hole in the wall at evening, the load on his shoulder in the dark, his face covered.
- **v10 "This burden concerns the prince in Jerusalem"** — the Hebrew is four words, *ha-nasi ha-massa ha-zeh*, "the prince, this burden." *Nasi*, "prince," and *massa*, "burden, oracle," both come from *nasa*, "lift, carry," and in v12 the prince *will lift* (*yissa*) his own load onto his shoulder.
- **vv12–13 "He will cover his face, so that he will not see the land… yet he will not see it, and there he will die"** — what happened to Zedekiah, told in Kings: *They slaughtered the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes, then put out the eyes of Zedekiah, bound him in bronze fetters and brought him to Babylon* (2 Kings 25:7). He was brought to Babylon and did not see it.
- **vv22–28 "The days go on and on, and every vision comes to nothing… The vision that he sees is for many days from now"** — two sayings of the people: one that prophecy fails, one that it will not come true in their time. God answers both with *the word that I speak will be done*, and *in your days*.
- **v23 "The days are near, and the word of every vision"** — *u-devar kol chazon*, the word, the thing spoken, of every vision: what was seen is about to be done.

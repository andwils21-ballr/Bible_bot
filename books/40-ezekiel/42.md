# Ezekiel 42

## The Chambers of the Priests

**1** Then he brought me out into the outer court, by the way toward the north, and brought me to the chambers opposite the separate yard and opposite the building, on the north.

**2** Along the length of a hundred cubits was the door on the north, and the width was fifty cubits.

**3** Opposite the twenty cubits that belonged to the inner court, and opposite the pavement of the outer court, were galleries facing galleries, in three stories.

**4** In front of the chambers was a passage ten cubits wide on the inner side, a way of one cubit; and their doors were on the north.

**5** The upper chambers were narrower, because the galleries took more space from them than from the lower and middle stories of the building.

**6** For they were in three stories and had no pillars like the pillars of the courts; so the upper ones were set back more than the lower and the middle ones, from the ground up.

**7** There was a wall outside, alongside the chambers, toward the outer court, in front of the chambers; its length was fifty cubits.

**8** For the length of the chambers on the outer court was fifty cubits, while those facing the nave were a hundred cubits.

**9** Below these chambers was the entrance on the east side, as one enters them from the outer court.

**10** In the thickness of the wall of the court, toward the east, facing the separate yard and the building, were chambers,

**11** with a passage in front of them. They looked like the chambers on the north: as long and as wide, with all their exits, their arrangements and their doors.

**12** Like the doors of the chambers on the south was a door at the head of the passage, the passage in front of the matching wall toward the east, as one enters them.

**13** He said to me, "The north chambers and the south chambers facing the separate yard are the holy chambers where the priests who come near to the LORD will eat the most holy offerings. There they will put the most holy offerings — the grain offering, the sin offering and the guilt offering — for the place is holy.

**14** When the priests enter, they shall not go out of the holy place into the outer court without leaving there the garments in which they minister, for these are holy. They shall put on other garments before they go near the area that is for the people."

## The Wall Around the Whole

**15** When he had finished measuring the inner house, he brought me out by the gate facing east and measured it all around.

**16** He measured the east side with the measuring reed: five hundred cubits by the measuring reed, all around.

**17** He measured the north side: five hundred cubits by the measuring reed, all around.

**18** He measured the south side: five hundred cubits by the measuring reed.

**19** He turned to the west side and measured: five hundred cubits by the measuring reed.

**20** He measured it on all four sides. It had a wall all around, five hundred long and five hundred wide, to separate the holy from the ordinary.

## Notes

- **vv1–12 the chambers** — two blocks of rooms for the priests, north and south of the separate yard, in three stories set back like terraces. Several details are obscure in the Hebrew and kept close to it: the door *along the length of a hundred cubits* (v2), *a way of one cubit* (v4), *the matching wall* (v12). The Greek differs widely: in v4 it has a passage *a hundred cubits long*, and in v10 it puts the second block *toward the south*, as v12 does.
- **vv16–19 "five hundred cubits by the measuring reed"** — the Hebrew reads *five hundred reeds*, each reed six long cubits (40:5), which would make each side three thousand cubits. The Greek has *five hundred cubits* (vv17, 20), and the written Hebrew of v16 still has the word *ammot*, **cubits**, which the reading tradition drops. Five hundred cubits is what chapters 40–41 add up to from east to west: the outer gate (50), the outer court (100), the inner gate (50), the inner court (100), the house (100) and the yard behind it (100).

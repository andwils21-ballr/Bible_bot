# Ezekiel 26

**1** And it came to pass in the eleventh year, on the first day of the month, that the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, because Tyre has said of Jerusalem, 'Aha! The gate of the peoples is broken; it has swung open to me. I will be filled, now that she is laid waste,'

**3** therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I am against you, Tyre, and I will bring up many nations against you, as the sea brings up its waves.

**4** They will destroy the walls of Tyre and tear down her towers; I will scrape her soil from her and make her a bare rock.

**5** She will be a place for spreading nets in the middle of the sea, for I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD; and she will become plunder for the nations.

**6** Her daughters on the mainland will be killed by the sword; and they will know that I am the LORD.

**7** For this is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to bring against Tyre from the north Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, king of kings, with horses and chariots and horsemen, and a great company and army.

**8** Your daughters on the mainland he will kill with the sword. He will build a siege wall against you, heap up a ramp against you and raise a shield against you.

**9** He will set the blows of his battering rams against your walls and break down your towers with his axes.

**10** His horses are so many that their dust will cover you. Your walls will shake at the noise of horsemen, wheels and chariots, when he enters your gates as men enter a city that has been breached.

**11** With the hooves of his horses he will trample all your streets; he will kill your people with the sword, and your mighty pillars will fall to the ground.

**12** They will plunder your wealth and loot your merchandise; they will break down your walls and destroy your fine houses, and your stones and your timber and your soil they will throw into the water.

**13** I will put an end to the noise of your songs, and the sound of your lyres will be heard no more.

**14** I will make you a bare rock; you will be a place for spreading nets; you will never be rebuilt, for I, the LORD, have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.

**15** This is what the Lord GOD says to Tyre: Will not the coastlands shake at the sound of your fall, when the wounded groan, when slaughter is done in your midst?

**16** Then all the princes of the sea will step down from their thrones, take off their robes and strip off their embroidered garments. They will clothe themselves with trembling; they will sit on the ground and tremble every moment, and be appalled at you.

**17** They will take up a lament over you and say to you:  
How you have perished,  
you who were inhabited from the seas,  
the city that was praised,  
which was mighty on the sea,  
she and those who lived in her,  
who laid their terror on all who lived there!

**18** Now the coastlands tremble  
on the day of your fall;  
the coastlands by the sea  
are dismayed at your passing.

**19** For this is what the Lord GOD says: When I make you a ruined city, like cities that are not inhabited, when I bring up the deep over you and the great waters cover you,

**20** then I will bring you down with those who go down to the pit, to the people of old, and make you live in the lowest parts of the earth, among ruins from of old, with those who go down to the pit, so that you will not be inhabited; and I will set glory in the land of the living.

**21** I will make you a terror, and you will be no more; though you are sought, you will never be found again, declares the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **v1 "in the eleventh year, on the first day of the month"** — the Hebrew gives the day but not the month; the Greek is the same. Jerusalem fell *in the eleventh year of Zedekiah* (Jeremiah 39:2), whose reign began with the exile of Jehoiachin that Ezekiel counts by.
- **v2 "Aha! The gate of the peoples is broken; it has swung open to me"** — Tyre's word is Ammon's, *Aha!* (25:3). Jerusalem is pictured as a gate through which the peoples passed; Tyre expects what went through it to come her way: *I will be filled*.
- **vv4, 14 "I will scrape her soil from her and make her a bare rock"** — *tsechiach sela*, the words of 24:7–8, where Jerusalem left blood *on the bare rock*. *Tsor*, "Tyre," sounds like *tsur*, "rock": the city will be left a rock with nothing on it.
- **vv5, 14 "a place for spreading nets in the middle of the sea"** — *in the middle of the sea*, as the city stood. The great trading city is to be no more than a place where fishermen dry their nets.
- **v7 "Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, king of kings"** — named here for the first time in the book. The book itself returns to the siege of Tyre later and says that Nebuchadnezzar's army *labored hard* against it and got no wages from it (29:17–18).
- **vv16–18 "all the princes of the sea will step down from their thrones… They will take up a lament"** — the other kings of the coast mourn as the people of chapter 24 were told not to: stripped of their robes, sitting on the ground.
- **vv19–21 "I will bring up the deep over you… with those who go down to the pit, to the people of old"** — *tehom*, the deep of Genesis 1:2. The sea that made Tyre rich covers it, and it goes down to *the pit*, the place of the dead, *the people of old*.

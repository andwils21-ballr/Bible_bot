# Ezekiel 35

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, set your face against Mount Seir, and prophesy against it,

**3** and say to it: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am against you, Mount Seir. I will stretch out My hand against you and make you a desolation and a waste.

**4** I will lay your cities in ruins, and you will be a desolation; and you will know that I am the LORD.

**5** Because you held a never-ending hostility, and handed the sons of Israel over to the power of the sword at the time of their calamity, the time of the final punishment,

**6** therefore, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will give you up to blood, and blood will pursue you. Since you did not hate blood, blood will pursue you.

**7** I will make Mount Seir a waste and a desolation, and cut off from it all who come and go.

**8** I will fill its mountains with its slain. On your hills and in your valleys and in all your ravines, those slain by the sword will fall.

**9** I will make you an everlasting desolation, and your cities will not be restored; and you will know that I am the LORD.

**10** Because you said, 'These two nations and these two lands will be mine, and we will possess them' — though the LORD was there —

**11** therefore, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will deal with you according to the anger and the jealousy you showed in your hatred of them; and I will make Myself known among them when I judge you.

**12** Then you will know that I, the LORD, have heard all the contempt you spoke against the mountains of Israel, saying, 'They are desolate; they have been given to us to devour.'

**13** You boasted against Me with your mouth and heaped up your words against Me. I have heard it.

**14** This is what the Lord GOD says: While the whole earth rejoices, I will make you a desolation.

**15** As you rejoiced over the inheritance of the house of Israel because it was desolate, so I will do to you: you will be a desolation, Mount Seir, and all Edom, all of it. Then they will know that I am the LORD."

## Notes

- **v2 "Mount Seir"** — the hill country of Edom: *Esau settled in the hill country of Seir. Esau — he is Edom* (Genesis 36:8). Edom was judged briefly in 25:12–14; it is taken up again here, at length, because of what it did when Jerusalem fell. The chapter is one half of a pair. Mount Seir is made *a desolation*; in the next chapter the mountains of Israel, which Edom called desolate (v12), are told they will bear fruit again (36:8).
- **v5 "a never-ending hostility"** — *eivat olam*, the charge brought against the Philistines (25:15). Behind it stands the brothers' quarrel: *Esau harbored hatred against Jacob* (Genesis 27:41). The psalm remembers Edom's part in the city's fall, *how they said, "Tear it down, tear it down"* (Psalm 137:7), and Lamentations taunts it: *Rejoice and be glad, daughter of Edom* (Lamentations 4:21).
- **v5 "the time of the final punishment"** — *et avon qets*, the words spoken over Zedekiah, *whose day has come, the time of the final punishment* (21:25). Edom struck Judah on the day God was already judging it.
- **v6 "I will give you up to blood, and blood will pursue you"** — *dam*, blood, four times in one verse, against *Edom*, a name that means **red**: *Therefore his name was called Edom*, after *that red, that red there* (Genesis 25:30). The red one is handed over to blood. *Im lo dam saneta* opens like an oath, where *im lo* means **surely**, but *surely you hated blood* does not fit; it is read as the reason, **since you did not hate blood**. The Greek keeps the oath and changes the verb: *surely you sinned unto blood*.
- **v9 "your cities will not be restored"** — the reading tradition has *tashovnah*, **will return**; the written text has *teshavnah*, **will be inhabited**, which the Greek also has. Either way Edom's cities stay empty.
- **v10 "though the LORD was there"** — Edom counted Israel and Judah as two vacant lands to be had. The land was never vacant: *the land is Mine* (Leviticus 25:23). The book ends by naming the rebuilt city with these words, *YHWH shammah*, **the LORD is there** (48:35).
- **v13 "You boasted against Me with your mouth"** — *va-tagdilu… be-fikhem*, **you made great with your mouth**: the words Obadiah uses of Edom on the day of Judah's distress (Obadiah 12). What Edom said about Israel's mountains, God hears as said against Himself.

# Ezekiel 46

## Sabbaths and New Moons

**1** This is what the Lord GOD says: The gate of the inner court facing east shall be shut on the six working days, but on the sabbath day it shall be opened, and on the day of the new moon it shall be opened.

**2** The prince shall enter by way of the porch of the gate from outside and stand by the doorpost of the gate. The priests shall offer his burnt offering and his peace offerings, and he shall bow down at the threshold of the gate and then go out; but the gate shall not be shut until evening.

**3** The people of the land shall bow down before the LORD at the entrance of that gate on the sabbaths and the new moons.

**4** The burnt offering the prince offers to the LORD on the sabbath day shall be six lambs without defect and a ram without defect;

**5** the grain offering shall be an ephah for the ram, and for the lambs as much as he chooses to give, and a hin of oil for each ephah.

**6** On the day of the new moon: a young bull without defect, and six lambs and a ram, all without defect.

**7** He shall provide as a grain offering an ephah for the bull and an ephah for the ram, and for the lambs as much as he can afford, and a hin of oil for each ephah.

**8** When the prince enters, he shall come in by way of the porch of the gate and go out by the same way.

**9** When the people of the land come before the LORD at the appointed feasts, whoever enters by the north gate to worship shall go out by the south gate, and whoever enters by the south gate shall go out by the north gate. No one shall return by the gate through which he came in; each shall go out straight ahead.

**10** The prince shall be among them: when they come in, he shall come in, and when they go out, they shall go out together.

**11** At the feasts and the appointed times the grain offering shall be an ephah for a bull and an ephah for a ram, and for the lambs as much as he chooses to give, and a hin of oil for each ephah.

**12** When the prince provides a freewill offering, a burnt offering or peace offerings as a freewill offering to the LORD, the gate facing east shall be opened for him, and he shall provide his burnt offering and his peace offerings as he does on the sabbath day. Then he shall go out, and the gate shall be shut after he goes out.

## The Daily Offering

**13** Each day you shall provide a lamb a year old without defect as a burnt offering to the LORD; morning by morning you shall provide it.

**14** With it you shall provide a grain offering morning by morning, a sixth of an ephah, with a third of a hin of oil to moisten the fine flour: a grain offering to the LORD, a lasting statute, continually.

**15** So they shall provide the lamb, the grain offering and the oil morning by morning, as a continual burnt offering.

## The Prince's Inheritance

**16** This is what the Lord GOD says: If the prince gives a gift to any of his sons, it is his inheritance; it shall belong to his sons; it is their possession by inheritance.

**17** But if he gives a gift from his inheritance to one of his servants, it shall be the servant's until the year of liberty, and then it shall return to the prince. His inheritance belongs to his sons alone; it shall be theirs.

**18** The prince shall not take any of the people's inheritance, wronging them out of their possession. He shall give his sons their inheritance out of his own possession, so that none of My people is scattered from his possession."

## The Kitchens

**19** Then he brought me through the entrance at the side of the gate to the holy chambers for the priests, which faced north, and there was a place at the far west end.

**20** He said to me, "This is the place where the priests shall boil the guilt offering and the sin offering and bake the grain offering, so that they do not carry them out into the outer court and make the people holy."

**21** Then he brought me out into the outer court and led me around to the four corners of the court, and in each corner of the court there was a court.

**22** In the four corners of the court were enclosed courts, forty long and thirty wide; the four in the corners had the same measurement.

**23** Around each of the four ran a row of stones, and hearths were made under the rows all around.

**24** He said to me, "These are the kitchens where those who minister in the house shall boil the sacrifices of the people."

## Notes

- **v1 "on the sabbath day it shall be opened"** — the outer east gate stays shut for good, because the LORD entered by it (44:2). The east gate of the inner court is shut on working days and opened on the sabbath and the new moon.
- **vv2–3 "he shall bow down at the threshold"** — the prince comes as far as the doorpost; the priests make his offerings. He bows at the threshold and the people bow at the entrance behind him. He worships with them and does not serve at the altar.
- **vv4–7 the offerings** — they are not the numbers of Numbers 28. There the sabbath has *two lambs a year old* (Numbers 28:9), and the new moon *two young bulls, one ram, and seven lambs* (Numbers 28:11). Here the sabbath has six lambs and a ram, and the new moon one bull, six lambs and a ram. *As much as he can afford* (v7) is the law's phrase for what a man's hand can reach (Leviticus 14:22).
- **vv9–10 "each shall go out straight ahead"** — in at the north gate, out at the south; in at the south, out at the north. No one turns around inside the house, and the prince goes in and out in the middle of the crowd.
- **vv13–15 "morning by morning"** — the law had *two lambs a year old, day by day*, one in the morning and one *between the evenings* (Exodus 29:38–39). Here there is only the morning lamb. The Greek has *he shall provide*, making it the prince's offering.
- **v17 "until the year of liberty"** — *shenat ha-deror*, the jubilee: *proclaim liberty in the land to all who live in it… each of you shall return to his holding* (Leviticus 25:10). Land given to a servant goes back in that year; the prince's land stays in his family.
- **v18 "The prince shall not take any of the people's inheritance"** — as King Ahab took Naboth's vineyard, after Naboth had said, *The LORD forbid that I should give you the inheritance of my fathers* (1 Kings 21:3). *Wronging them* is the verb of 45:8, *My princes shall no longer wrong My people*.
- **v20 "and make the people holy"** — as with the priests' garments (44:19): holy food is cooked where it cannot touch the people.
- **v22 "the four in the corners"** — *mehuqtsa'ot*, and in the Hebrew the scribes set dots over its letters, as over *the nave* in 41:20. *Qeturot*, **enclosed**, is uncertain; the Greek has *small*.

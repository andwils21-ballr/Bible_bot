# Ezekiel 48

## The Northern Tribes

**1** "These are the names of the tribes. Beginning at the northern end, beside the way of Hethlon to Lebo-hamath, as far as Hazar-enan at the border of Damascus, with Hamath to the north, and running from the east side to the west: Dan, one portion.

**2** Next to the border of Dan, from the east side to the west side: Asher, one portion.

**3** Next to the border of Asher, from the east side to the west side: Naphtali, one portion.

**4** Next to the border of Naphtali, from the east side to the west side: Manasseh, one portion.

**5** Next to the border of Manasseh, from the east side to the west side: Ephraim, one portion.

**6** Next to the border of Ephraim, from the east side to the west side: Reuben, one portion.

**7** Next to the border of Reuben, from the east side to the west side: Judah, one portion.

## The Holy Contribution

**8** Next to the border of Judah, from the east side to the west side, shall be the contribution you set apart, twenty-five thousand wide, and as long as one of the tribal portions from the east side to the west side; and the sanctuary shall be in the middle of it.

**9** The contribution you set apart for the LORD shall be twenty-five thousand long and ten thousand wide.

**10** This shall be the holy contribution for the priests: twenty-five thousand on the north, ten thousand wide on the west, ten thousand wide on the east, and twenty-five thousand long on the south; and the sanctuary of the LORD shall be in the middle of it.

**11** It shall be for the consecrated priests, the sons of Zadok, who kept My charge, who did not go astray when the sons of Israel went astray, as the Levites went astray.

**12** It shall be theirs as a special portion from the contribution of the land, most holy, beside the border of the Levites.

**13** Alongside the border of the priests, the Levites shall have twenty-five thousand long and ten thousand wide; the whole length twenty-five thousand and the width ten thousand.

**14** They shall not sell any of it or exchange it, and this choicest part of the land shall not pass to others, for it is holy to the LORD.

**15** The remaining five thousand in width, along the twenty-five thousand, shall be for ordinary use by the city, for houses and for open land; and the city shall be in the middle of it.

**16** These shall be its measurements: the north side four thousand five hundred, the south side four thousand five hundred, the east side four thousand five hundred, and the west side four thousand five hundred.

**17** The city shall have open land: on the north two hundred and fifty, on the south two hundred and fifty, on the east two hundred and fifty, and on the west two hundred and fifty.

**18** The rest of the length alongside the holy contribution shall be ten thousand on the east and ten thousand on the west. It shall lie alongside the holy contribution, and its produce shall be food for those who work in the city.

**19** Those who work in the city, from all the tribes of Israel, shall farm it.

**20** The whole contribution shall be twenty-five thousand by twenty-five thousand; you shall set apart the holy contribution as a square, together with the possession of the city.

**21** What remains, on both sides of the holy contribution and of the possession of the city, shall belong to the prince. It shall run from the twenty-five thousand of the contribution to the east border, and westward from the twenty-five thousand to the west border, alongside the tribal portions; it shall belong to the prince. The holy contribution and the sanctuary of the house shall be in the middle of it.

**22** Apart from the possession of the Levites and the possession of the city, which lie in the middle of what belongs to the prince, the land between the border of Judah and the border of Benjamin shall belong to the prince.

## The Southern Tribes

**23** As for the rest of the tribes: from the east side to the west side, Benjamin, one portion.

**24** Next to the border of Benjamin, from the east side to the west side: Simeon, one portion.

**25** Next to the border of Simeon, from the east side to the west side: Issachar, one portion.

**26** Next to the border of Issachar, from the east side to the west side: Zebulun, one portion.

**27** Next to the border of Zebulun, from the east side to the west side: Gad, one portion.

**28** Next to the border of Gad, on the south side, southward, the border shall run from Tamar to the waters of Meribah-kadesh, along the Brook to the Great Sea.

**29** This is the land you shall allot as an inheritance to the tribes of Israel, and these are their portions, declares the Lord GOD.

## The Gates of the City

**30** These are the exits of the city. On the north side, measuring four thousand five hundred,

**31** the gates of the city shall be named after the tribes of Israel, three gates on the north: the gate of Reuben, one; the gate of Judah, one; the gate of Levi, one.

**32** On the east side, four thousand five hundred, three gates: the gate of Joseph, one; the gate of Benjamin, one; the gate of Dan, one.

**33** On the south side, measuring four thousand five hundred, three gates: the gate of Simeon, one; the gate of Issachar, one; the gate of Zebulun, one.

**34** On the west side, four thousand five hundred, three gates: the gate of Gad, one; the gate of Asher, one; the gate of Naphtali, one.

**35** All around it shall be eighteen thousand. The name of the city from that day on shall be: The LORD Is There."

## Notes

- **vv1–7, 23–28 the tribes in strips** — each tribe has one equal band of land from the east border to the sea, as 47:14 said, *each as his brother*. Of the four sons of the servant girls (Genesis 30:4–13), Dan, Asher and Naphtali are the three northernmost and Gad the southernmost. Judah lies just north of the holy portion and Benjamin just south of it, the other way round from Joshua, where Benjamin's lot *went out between the sons of Judah and the sons of Joseph* (Joshua 18:11).
- **vv8–22 the holy contribution** — a square of twenty-five thousand each way (v20): the priests' strip with the sanctuary in its middle, the Levites' strip beside it, and the city's strip of five thousand. The prince's land runs out from it on both sides to the borders.
- **v14 "They shall not sell any of it or exchange it"** — the law for the Levites' land: *The pasture land around their cities shall not be sold, because it is their holding forever* (Leviticus 25:34).
- **v15 "for ordinary use by the city"** — *chol*, **ordinary**, the word the wall separates from the holy (42:20). The city is not the holy place; the house is. In nine chapters the city is never called Jerusalem; the only name it receives is the one in the last verse.
- **vv16–17 four thousand five hundred, and two hundred and fifty** — 4,500 with 250 of open land on each side makes 5,000, the width of the city's strip (v15). The city fills it exactly.
- **vv30–34 twelve gates** — three on each side, named for Jacob's sons. Levi, which has no tribal portion, has a gate, and Joseph has one gate for the two tribes of Ephraim and Manasseh. Revelation's city has twelve gates, three on each side, with the names of the twelve tribes of Israel on them (Revelation 21:12–13).
- **v35 "The LORD Is There"** — *YHWH shammah*, the book's last words. Ezekiel watched the glory leave: it *went out from the threshold of the house* (10:18) and *went up from the middle of the city* (11:23). The book ends with a city named for His presence. Edom had claimed the land *though the LORD was there* (35:10). The Greek gives no name: *the name of the city, from whatever day it comes to be, shall be its name*.

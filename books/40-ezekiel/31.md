# Ezekiel 31

**1** And it came to pass in the eleventh year, in the third month, on the first of the month, that the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, say to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to his multitude: Whom are you like in your greatness?

**3** Assyria was a cedar in Lebanon,  
fair in its branches, a thicket of shade,  
and tall in stature;  
its crown rose among the thick branches.

**4** The waters made it grow;  
the deep made it tall,  
running its rivers around the place where it was planted  
and sending out its channels to all the trees of the field.

**5** So it grew taller  
than all the trees of the field;  
its boughs multiplied, and its branches grew long  
from the abundant water as it spread them out.

**6** In its boughs  
all the birds of the heavens made their nests;  
under its branches all the beasts of the field gave birth,  
and all great nations lived in its shade.

**7** It was beautiful in its greatness,  
in the length of its branches,  
for its roots went down to abundant waters.

**8** The cedars in the garden of God could not overshadow it;  
the firs were not like its boughs,  
and the plane trees were nothing beside its branches;  
no tree in the garden of God  
was like it in its beauty.

**9** I made it beautiful  
with its many branches,  
and all the trees of Eden  
that were in the garden of God envied it.

**10** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you were so tall in stature, and it set its crown among the thick branches, and its heart grew proud of its height,

**11** I gave it into the hand of the mighty one of the nations, and he dealt with it; for its wickedness I drove it out.

**12** Strangers, the most ruthless of nations, cut it down and left it lying. Its branches fell on the mountains and in all the valleys, and its boughs lay broken in all the ravines of the land. All the peoples of the earth came down from its shade and left it.

**13** On its fallen trunk all the birds of the heavens settle, and among its boughs are all the beasts of the field,

**14** so that no trees by the waters may grow tall in their stature or set their crowns among the thick branches, and no tree that drinks water may rise up to them in its height. For they are all given over to death, to the lowest parts of the earth, among the sons of men, with those who go down to the pit.

**15** This is what the Lord GOD says: On the day it went down to Sheol I brought mourning; I covered the deep over it, I held back its rivers, and the great waters were stopped. I clothed Lebanon in gloom for it, and all the trees of the field fainted because of it.

**16** I made the nations shake at the sound of its fall, when I brought it down to Sheol with those who go down to the pit; and all the trees of Eden, the choicest and best of Lebanon, all that drink water, were comforted in the lowest parts of the earth.

**17** They too went down with it to Sheol, to those slain by the sword, and so did those who were its arm, who had lived in its shade among the nations.

**18** Whom are you like, in glory and in greatness, among the trees of Eden? You will be brought down with the trees of Eden to the lowest parts of the earth; you will lie among the uncircumcised, with those slain by the sword. This is Pharaoh and all his multitude, declares the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **vv2, 18 "Whom are you like in your greatness?… This is Pharaoh and all his multitude"** — the chapter is one question, a parable and its answer. *Hamon*, **multitude**, is Egypt's word in these oracles (29:19; 30:4, 10, 15): its crowds, its armies, its pomp. Chapter 32 says it over Egypt's dead.
- **v3 "Assyria was a cedar in Lebanon"** — Pharaoh is shown Assyria, the empire that fell before him, and Isaiah had already pictured its fall as a forest cut down: *Lebanon will fall by the Majestic One* (Isaiah 10:34). The Greek also has *Assur*, and calls the tree a cypress.
- **vv3, 10, 14 "among the thick branches"** — *bein avotim*, the words used of the vine in the lament of 19:11, *Its height towered among the thick branches*. The Greek reads *among the clouds*.
- **v4 "the deep made it tall"** — *tehom*, the deep of creation (Genesis 1:2). In v15 the same deep is covered over the fallen tree, and its rivers are held back.
- **vv6, 13 "all the birds of the heavens made their nests"** — the picture of Ezekiel's own cedar, the one the LORD plants on the mountain of Israel: *Under it will live every bird of every wing* (17:23). Jesus uses it for the kingdom, the mustard seed that becomes a tree *so that the birds of the sky come and nest in its branches* (Matthew 13:32). Fallen, this tree is only a perch.
- **vv8–9, 16, 18 "the garden of God… the trees of Eden"** — the garden where the king of Tyre was set (28:13). Here its trees envy the cedar, and in the end go down to the dead with it.
- **v10 "Because you were so tall… and it set its crown"** — the Hebrew moves from *you* to *it* inside one sentence. The tree is Assyria; the one being spoken to is Pharaoh.
- **v11 "I drove it out"** — *gerashtihu*, the verb of Eden: *He drove the human out* (Genesis 3:24). *The mighty one of the nations*, *el goyim*, is unnamed; 30:24–25 has given the sword to the king of Babylon.
- **vv15–16 "were comforted in the lowest parts of the earth"** — the trees already among the dead take comfort that the greatest of them has come down too, as in Isaiah's song over the king of Babylon: *Even the cypresses rejoice over you, the cedars of Lebanon* (Isaiah 14:8).
- **v17 "those who were its arm"** — *zero'o*, **its arm**: its allies, with the word of 30:21–25. The same letters read *zar'o*, **its seed**, which the Greek has.

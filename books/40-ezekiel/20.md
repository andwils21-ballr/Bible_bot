# Ezekiel 20

**1** And it came to pass in the seventh year, in the fifth month, on the tenth day of the month, that some of the elders of Israel came to inquire of the LORD, and sat before me.

**2** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**3** "Son of man, speak to the elders of Israel and say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: Have you come to inquire of Me? As I live, I will not let Myself be consulted by you, declares the Lord GOD.

**4** Will you judge them, will you judge them, son of man? Make known to them the detestable things of their fathers,

**5** and say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: On the day I chose Israel, I raised My hand to the descendants of the house of Jacob and made Myself known to them in the land of Egypt; I raised My hand to them, saying, 'I am the LORD your God.'

**6** On that day I raised My hand to them to bring them out of the land of Egypt into a land I had searched out for them, flowing with milk and honey, the most beautiful of all lands.

**7** I said to them, 'Each of you, throw away the loathsome things your eyes delight in, and do not make yourselves unclean with the idols of Egypt; I am the LORD your God.'

**8** But they rebelled against Me and would not listen to Me. They did not each throw away the loathsome things their eyes delighted in, nor did they forsake the idols of Egypt. So I said I would pour out My wrath on them and spend My anger on them in the middle of the land of Egypt.

**9** But I acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned in the sight of the nations among whom they were, in whose sight I had made Myself known to them by bringing them out of the land of Egypt.

**10** So I brought them out of the land of Egypt and led them into the wilderness.

**11** I gave them My statutes and made known to them My judgments, by which the person who does them shall live.

**12** I also gave them My sabbaths, to be a sign between Me and them, so that they would know that I am the LORD who sets them apart.

**13** But the house of Israel rebelled against Me in the wilderness. They did not walk in My statutes, and they rejected My judgments, by which the person who does them shall live, and they greatly profaned My sabbaths. So I said I would pour out My wrath on them in the wilderness to make an end of them.

**14** But I acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned in the sight of the nations in whose sight I had brought them out.

**15** I in turn raised My hand to them in the wilderness that I would not bring them into the land I had given them, flowing with milk and honey, the most beautiful of all lands,

**16** because they rejected My judgments and did not walk in My statutes, and profaned My sabbaths, for their heart went after their idols.

**17** Yet My eye spared them from destruction, and I did not make an end of them in the wilderness.

**18** I said to their children in the wilderness, 'Do not walk in the statutes of your fathers or keep their judgments, or make yourselves unclean with their idols.

**19** I am the LORD your God. Walk in My statutes, keep My judgments and do them,

**20** and keep My sabbaths holy, so that they will be a sign between Me and you, that you may know that I am the LORD your God.'

**21** But the children rebelled against Me. They did not walk in My statutes or keep My judgments by doing them, by which the person who does them shall live; they profaned My sabbaths. So I said I would pour out My wrath on them and spend My anger on them in the wilderness.

**22** But I held back My hand and acted for the sake of My name, so that it would not be profaned in the sight of the nations in whose sight I had brought them out.

**23** I in turn raised My hand to them in the wilderness that I would scatter them among the nations and disperse them through the countries,

**24** because they had not done My judgments but had rejected My statutes and profaned My sabbaths, and their eyes were set on their fathers' idols.

**25** I in turn gave them statutes that were not good, and judgments by which they could not live,

**26** and I made them unclean through their gifts, when they made every opener of the womb pass through the fire, so that I might devastate them, so that they would know that I am the LORD.

**27** Therefore speak to the house of Israel, son of man, and say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: In this also your fathers blasphemed Me, by breaking faith with Me.

**28** When I had brought them into the land that I had raised My hand to give them, then wherever they saw any high hill or any leafy tree, there they offered their sacrifices, there they presented the offerings that provoked Me, there they set out their soothing aromas, and there they poured out their drink offerings.

**29** I said to them, 'What is this high place that you go to?' So its name is called High Place to this day.

**30** Therefore say to the house of Israel: This is what the Lord GOD says: Will you make yourselves unclean in the way of your fathers, and prostitute yourselves after their loathsome things?

**31** When you offer your gifts and make your sons pass through the fire, you make yourselves unclean with all your idols to this day. Shall I then let Myself be consulted by you, house of Israel? As I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will not let Myself be consulted by you.

**32** What comes into your mind will never happen, when you say, 'Let us be like the nations, like the clans of the countries, serving wood and stone.'

**33** As I live, declares the Lord GOD, with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm and with wrath poured out I will be king over you.

**34** I will bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the countries where you are scattered, with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm and with wrath poured out.

**35** I will bring you into the wilderness of the peoples, and there I will enter into judgment with you face to face.

**36** As I entered into judgment with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so I will enter into judgment with you, declares the Lord GOD.

**37** I will make you pass under the rod, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant.

**38** I will purge out from among you the rebels and those who transgress against Me; I will bring them out of the land where they live as guests, but they will not enter the land of Israel; and you will know that I am the LORD.

**39** As for you, house of Israel, this is what the Lord GOD says: Go, each of you serve his idols, now and afterward, if you will not listen to Me; but you shall no longer profane My holy name with your gifts and your idols.

**40** For on My holy mountain, the high mountain of Israel, declares the Lord GOD, there all the house of Israel, all of them in the land, will serve Me. There I will accept them, and there I will require your contributions and the first of your gifts, with all your holy things.

**41** As a soothing aroma I will accept you, when I bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the countries where you have been scattered; and I will show Myself holy among you in the sight of the nations.

**42** You will know that I am the LORD, when I bring you into the land of Israel, into the land I raised My hand to give to your fathers.

**43** There you will remember your ways and all the deeds by which you made yourselves unclean, and you will loathe yourselves for all the evils you have done.

**44** You will know that I am the LORD, when I deal with you for the sake of My name, not according to your evil ways or your corrupt deeds, house of Israel, declares the Lord GOD."

## Fire in the Forest of the South

**45** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**46** "Son of man, set your face toward the south, preach against the south, and prophesy against the forest of the field in the Negev.

**47** Say to the forest of the Negev: Hear the word of the LORD! This is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to kindle a fire in you, and it will consume every green tree in you and every dry tree. The blazing flame will not be quenched, and every face from the Negev to the north will be scorched by it.

**48** All flesh will see that I, the LORD, have kindled it; it will not be quenched."

**49** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! They are saying of me, 'Is he not just a maker of parables?'"

## Notes

- **vv45–49 "set your face toward the south… the forest of the Negev"** — the Hebrew prints these five verses as the opening of chapter 21 (21:1–5). English Bibles end chapter 20 with them, as Swete's Greek also does, and the numbers here follow the English.
- **vv1, 3, 31 "some of the elders of Israel came to inquire of the LORD… I will not let Myself be consulted by you"** — a year after the vision of chapter 8 (the sixth year), the elders come again, and God refuses to be inquired of. What they get instead is the history of their fathers.
- **vv5–6, 15, 23, 28, 42 "I raised My hand"** — *nasa yad*, the gesture of an oath, as at the exodus: *I will bring you to the land that I raised My hand to give to Abraham* (Exodus 6:8). The chapter turns on it: God raised His hand to bring them out (vv5–6), to keep them out of the land (v15), to scatter them (v23), and finally to bring them in (v42).
- **vv9, 14, 22, 44 "But I acted for the sake of My name"** — four times the people rebel and God says He will pour out His wrath, and three times He holds back, not for their sake but so that His name *would not be profaned in the sight of the nations*. The last time (v44) He says it of the return.
- **vv11, 13, 21 "by which the person who does them shall live"** — the law's own words, *the person who does them shall live by them* (Leviticus 18:5), set against v25.
- **vv12, 20 "My sabbaths, to be a sign between Me and them, so that they would know that I am the LORD who sets them apart"** — Exodus 31:13 in the same words: *it is a sign between Me and you… so that you may know that I am the LORD who sets you apart*.
- **vv25–26 "I in turn gave them statutes that were not good… when they made every opener of the womb pass through the fire"** — the hardest sentence in the chapter, and the verse does not explain it. The law said *set apart to the LORD every opener of the womb* (Exodus 13:12) and *Every firstborn of man among your sons you shall redeem* (13:13). Here the fathers make *every opener of the womb* pass through the fire, and God says He *gave them statutes that were not good* and *made them unclean through their gifts*: their own worship became the judgment on them.
- **v29 "'What is this high place that you go to?' So its name is called High Place"** — a play on sounds: *mah ha-bamah asher attem ha-ba'im sham*, *bamah* "high place" and *ba'im* "you go."
- **vv33–38 "I will be king over you… I will make you pass under the rod, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant"** — the second exodus will have its own wilderness, *the wilderness of the peoples*, and its own judgment. Passing *under the rod* is the counting of the flock, as in the law of the tithe, *every tenth one that passes under the rod* (Leviticus 27:32); the Greek reads *I will bring you in by number*.

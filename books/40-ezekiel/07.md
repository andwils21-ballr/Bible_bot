# Ezekiel 7

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "As for you, son of man, this is what the Lord GOD says to the land of Israel:  
An end! The end has come  
on the four corners of the land.

**3** Now the end is on you,  
and I will send My anger against you;  
I will judge you according to your ways  
and bring on you all your detestable things.

**4** My eye will not spare you, and I will have no pity;  
I will bring your ways on you,  
and your detestable things will be in your midst;  
and you will know that I am the LORD.

**5** This is what the Lord GOD says:  
Disaster, one disaster! It is coming!

**6** An end has come; the end has come;  
it has awakened against you. It is coming!

**7** Your doom has come to you, you who live in the land.  
The time has come; the day is near,  
a day of tumult, and not of shouting on the mountains.

**8** Now soon I will pour out My wrath on you  
and spend My anger on you;  
I will judge you according to your ways  
and bring on you all your detestable things.

**9** My eye will not spare, and I will have no pity;  
I will bring on you according to your ways,  
and your detestable things will be in your midst;  
and you will know that I am the LORD who strikes.

**10** The day! It is coming!  
Your doom has gone out;  
the staff has put out blossoms; pride has sprouted.

**11** Violence has grown up into a staff of wickedness.  
None of them, none of their multitude,  
none of their wealth; no splendor among them.

**12** The time has come; the day has arrived.  
Let the buyer not rejoice, nor the seller mourn,  
for wrath is on all her multitude.

**13** For the seller will not return to what he sold,  
even if they are still among the living;  
for the vision concerning all her multitude will not be turned back,  
and no one, in his iniquity, will hold on to his life.

**14** They have blown the trumpet and made everything ready,  
but no one goes to battle,  
for My wrath is on all her multitude.

**15** The sword is outside,  
and pestilence and famine inside;  
whoever is in the field will die by the sword,  
and whoever is in the city, famine and pestilence will devour him.

**16** Even if their survivors escape,  
they will be on the mountains like doves of the valleys,  
all of them moaning, each for his iniquity.

**17** Every hand will hang limp,  
and every knee will turn to water.

**18** They will put on sackcloth,  
and horror will cover them;  
shame will be on every face,  
and baldness on all their heads.

**19** They will throw their silver into the streets,  
and their gold will be an unclean thing;  
their silver and their gold will not be able to rescue them  
in the day of the LORD's fury.  
They will not satisfy their hunger or fill their stomachs with it,  
for it was the stumbling block of their iniquity.

**20** His beautiful ornament they turned to pride,  
and made their detestable images, their loathsome things, out of it;  
therefore I will make it an unclean thing to them.

**21** I will give it into the hands of strangers as plunder,  
and to the wicked of the earth as spoil,  
and they will profane it.

**22** I will turn My face from them,  
and they will profane My treasured place;  
robbers will enter it and profane it.

**23** Make the chain,  
for the land is full of bloodshed  
and the city is full of violence.

**24** I will bring the worst of the nations,  
and they will take possession of their houses;  
I will put an end to the pride of the strong,  
and their holy places will be profaned.

**25** Anguish is coming;  
they will seek peace, but there will be none.

**26** Disaster upon disaster will come,  
and rumor upon rumor;  
they will seek a vision from the prophet,  
but the law will perish from the priest,  
and counsel from the elders.

**27** The king will mourn,  
the prince will be clothed in desolation,  
and the hands of the people of the land will tremble.  
According to their way I will deal with them,  
and by their own judgments I will judge them;  
and they will know that I am the LORD."

## Notes

- **vv2–9 "An end! The end has come"** — *qets*, five times in four verses, and in v6 a play on it: *ha-qets heqits*, "the end has awakened." The Greek gives these verses in another order and does not have the line about *doom*.
- **vv7, 10 "Your doom"** — *ha-tsefirah*, a word whose sense here is not settled. Its only other use in the Bible is Isaiah's *diadem of beauty* (Isaiah 28:5), which does not fit the context; the English takes it from what the verses say around it, something that has come round to the land and gone out against it.
- **v10 "the staff has put out blossoms; pride has sprouted"** — the words of Aaron's staff, which *had sprouted. It had put out blossoms* (Numbers 17:8): the same two verbs. There the blossoming staff proved the LORD's choice; here what blossoms is pride, and in v11 *a staff of wickedness*.
- **v11 "None of them, none of their multitude, none of their wealth; no splendor among them"** — four short phrases with no verb, whose exact sense is uncertain. The Greek has a different line here. What is clear is that nothing of theirs is left.
- **vv12–13 "Let the buyer not rejoice, nor the seller mourn… the seller will not return to what he sold"** — under the law, land sold went back to its family in the Jubilee: *In this year of jubilee you shall return, each man to his holding* (Leviticus 25:13). The end makes buying and selling meaningless; no one will be there to return.
- **vv19–20 "their gold will be an unclean thing… His beautiful ornament they turned to pride"** — *niddah*, the word Lamentations used for the fallen city, *Jerusalem has become an unclean thing among them* (Lamentations 1:17). *His ornament*, *edyo*, is the word of Exodus 33:4, where the people mourned and *no one put on his ornaments*; whose ornament is meant here, the people's or God's, the verse does not say.
- **v22 "they will profane My treasured place"** — *tsefuni*, "My hidden, treasured one." It is not named; with *robbers will enter it*, it is most naturally the temple.
- **v26 "but the law will perish from the priest, and counsel from the elders"** — the boast of Jeremiah's enemies turned around. They said, *the law will not perish from the priest, nor counsel from the wise, nor the word from the prophet* (Jeremiah 18:18). Now it does, and they seek a vision from the prophet in vain.

# Ezekiel 34

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of Israel. Prophesy, and say to them, to the shepherds: This is what the Lord GOD says: Woe to the shepherds of Israel who have been feeding themselves! Should not the shepherds feed the flock?

**3** You drink the milk, you clothe yourselves with the wool, you slaughter the fat ones, but you do not feed the flock.

**4** You have not strengthened the weak, healed the sick, bound up the injured, brought back the strays or sought the lost; you have ruled them with force and with crushing harshness.

**5** So they were scattered for lack of a shepherd; they became food for all the beasts of the field, and they were scattered.

**6** My sheep wandered over all the mountains and on every high hill. My sheep were scattered over all the face of the earth, with no one searching and no one seeking.

**7** Therefore, shepherds, hear the word of the LORD:

**8** As I live, declares the Lord GOD, because My sheep have become plunder, and My sheep have become food for all the beasts of the field for lack of a shepherd, and My shepherds have not searched for My sheep, but the shepherds have fed themselves and have not fed My sheep —

**9** therefore, shepherds, hear the word of the LORD:

**10** This is what the Lord GOD says: I am against the shepherds, and I will require My sheep from their hand. I will put a stop to their feeding the flock, and the shepherds will no longer feed themselves. I will rescue My sheep from their mouths, and they will no longer be food for them.

## I Myself Will Search for My Sheep

**11** For this is what the Lord GOD says: I Myself will search for My sheep and look after them.

**12** As a shepherd looks after his flock on the day he is among his scattered sheep, so I will look after My sheep, and I will rescue them from all the places where they were scattered on a day of cloud and thick darkness.

**13** I will bring them out from the peoples and gather them from the countries, and bring them into their own land. I will feed them on the mountains of Israel, in the ravines and in all the settled places of the land.

**14** I will feed them in good pasture, and their grazing ground will be on the high mountains of Israel. There they will lie down in a good grazing ground and feed on rich pasture on the mountains of Israel.

**15** I Myself will feed My sheep, and I Myself will make them lie down, declares the Lord GOD.

**16** I will seek the lost, bring back the strays, bind up the injured and strengthen the sick; but I will destroy the fat and the strong. I will feed them with justice.

## Between Sheep and Sheep

**17** As for you, My flock, this is what the Lord GOD says: I will judge between one sheep and another, between the rams and the male goats.

**18** Is it too little for you to feed on the good pasture, that you must trample the rest of your pasture with your feet? Or to drink the clear water, that you must foul the rest with your feet?

**19** My sheep must eat what your feet have trampled and drink what your feet have fouled.

**20** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says to them: I Myself will judge between the fat sheep and the lean sheep.

**21** Because you push with side and shoulder, and butt all the weak with your horns until you have scattered them abroad,

**22** I will save My flock, and they will no longer be plunder; and I will judge between one sheep and another.

## My Servant David

**23** I will set up over them one shepherd, and he will feed them: My servant David. He will feed them, and he will be their shepherd.

**24** I, the LORD, will be their God, and My servant David a prince among them. I, the LORD, have spoken.

**25** I will make a covenant of peace with them and rid the land of wild beasts, so that they will live securely in the wilderness and sleep in the forests.

**26** I will make them and the places around My hill a blessing. I will send down the rain in its season; they will be rains of blessing.

**27** The trees of the field will give their fruit, and the land will give its produce, and they will be secure on their own soil. They will know that I am the LORD, when I break the bars of their yoke and rescue them from the hand of those who enslaved them.

**28** They will no longer be plunder for the nations, and the beasts of the earth will not devour them; they will live securely, with no one to make them afraid.

**29** I will raise up for them a planting of renown, and they will no longer be swept away by famine in the land or bear the insults of the nations any more.

**30** Then they will know that I, the LORD their God, am with them, and that they, the house of Israel, are My people, declares the Lord GOD.

**31** You are My flock, the flock of My pasture. You are human, and I am your God, declares the Lord GOD."

## Notes

- **v2 "the shepherds of Israel"** — the kings and rulers. It is the title the LORD gave David: *You shall shepherd My people Israel* (2 Samuel 5:2). Jeremiah had pronounced the same woe: *Woe to the shepherds who destroy and scatter the sheep of My pasture!* (Jeremiah 23:1).
- **v3 "You drink the milk"** — the Hebrew letters *chlv* can be read *chalav*, **milk**, or *chelev*, **fat**. The reading tradition has *the fat*; the Greek has *the milk*, and so does the rendering here. Milk and wool are what a flock gives while it lives (Proverbs 27:26–27 pairs the lambs for clothing with *chalev izzim*, goats' milk, for food); slaughter comes third, and the fat ones are already named in that clause. Read *the fat*, the charge would be eating what Leviticus reserves for the LORD: *All fat belongs to the LORD* (Leviticus 3:16).
- **v4 "you have ruled them with force and with crushing harshness"** — *redittem… be-farekh*, the words of the law on the Israelite slave: *You shall not rule over him with crushing harshness* (Leviticus 25:43). *Parekh* is what Egypt did to Israel, *crushing labor* (Exodus 1:13–14). Israel's own shepherds have treated the flock the way Pharaoh did.
- **vv5–6 "for lack of a shepherd"** — Moses' fear for the people at his death, that they *may not be like sheep that have no shepherd* (Numbers 27:17), come true. Jesus sees the crowds the same way, *harassed and helpless, like sheep that have no shepherd* (Matthew 9:36).
- **vv11–16 "I Myself will search for My sheep"** — the LORD takes back the work He had given to the kings, item by item: the lost, the strays, the injured and the sick of v4, in reverse order. *I Myself will make them lie down* (v15) is the verb of the psalm: *He makes me lie down in green pastures* (Psalm 23:2). At the end of v16, *I will destroy the fat and the strong*, *ashmid*, the Greek has *I will guard* (*phylaxō*), as if reading *ashmor*; in the Hebrew script *d* and *r* look almost alike. The Hebrew fits what follows: vv17–22 are judgment on the fat sheep that push the weak aside.
- **v17 "between one sheep and another, between the rams and the male goats"** — the picture Jesus uses for the last judgment: *as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats* (Matthew 25:32).
- **vv18–19 "foul… what your feet have fouled"** — *tirposun*, *mirpas*, the verb used of Pharaoh the monster, who *muddied the water with your feet and fouled their rivers* (32:2). The strong in the flock do to the weak what Egypt's king did to the waters.
- **vv23–24 "one shepherd… My servant David"** — David had long been dead. The name stands for the king to come from his line, as in Jeremiah: *David their king, whom I will raise up for them* (Jeremiah 30:9). *One shepherd* returns in 37:24, over a people no longer divided, and Jesus takes it up for His own flock (John 10:16).
- **vv25–28 the covenant of peace** — nearly every line is a blessing from Leviticus 26: *I will give you your rains in their season, and the land will give its produce, and the trees of the field will give their fruit* (26:4); *I will rid the land of harmful beasts* (26:6), the same Hebrew as *wild beasts* here, *chayyah ra'ah*; *with no one to make you afraid* (26:6); *I broke the bars of your yoke* (26:13). The chapter of the curses that Israel is living through also held the blessings, and here they are promised again.
- **v31 "You are human, and I am your God"** — *adam attem*: the flock of the parable is people. The Greek does not have these two words.

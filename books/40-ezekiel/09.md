# Ezekiel 9

**1** Then He called out in my hearing with a loud voice, saying, "Come near, you who are appointed over the city, each with his weapon of destruction in his hand."

**2** Six men came from the direction of the upper gate, which faces north, each with his shattering weapon in his hand, and among them one man clothed in linen, with a writing case at his waist. They came in and stood beside the bronze altar.

**3** Now the glory of the God of Israel had gone up from the cherub on which it rested to the threshold of the house. He called to the man clothed in linen, who had the writing case at his waist,

**4** and the LORD said to him, "Go through the city, through Jerusalem, and put a mark on the foreheads of the men who sigh and groan over all the detestable things done in it."

**5** To the others He said in my hearing, "Go through the city after him and strike. Let your eye not spare, and have no pity.

**6** Old man, young man and young woman, little children and women: kill them to the last; but do not go near anyone who has the mark on him. Begin at My sanctuary." So they began with the elders who were in front of the house.

**7** He said to them, "Make the house unclean, and fill the courts with the slain. Go out!" So they went out and struck in the city.

**8** While they were striking and I was left alone, I fell on my face and cried out, "Ah, Lord GOD! Will You destroy all the remnant of Israel when You pour out Your wrath on Jerusalem?"

**9** He said to me, "The iniquity of the house of Israel and Judah is exceedingly great. The land is full of bloodshed and the city full of perversion, for they say, 'The LORD has forsaken the land, and the LORD does not see.'

**10** As for Me, My eye will not spare, and I will have no pity; I will bring their way on their own heads."

**11** Then the man clothed in linen, who had the case at his waist, brought back word, saying, "I have done all that You commanded me."

## Notes

- **v1 "you who are appointed over the city"** — *pequddot ha-ir*, from *paqad*, which can mean to put in charge and to punish; the word can name the city's overseers or its punishments. Those who answer the call come armed.
- **v2 "one man clothed in linen, with a writing case at his waist"** — linen is the priest's dress on the Day of Atonement, *a holy linen tunic* (Leviticus 16:4); the case is a scribe's, *qeset ha-sofer*. The Greek has a long robe and *a belt of sapphire* in its place. Six come with weapons, and the seventh with a pen.
- **v3 "the glory of the God of Israel had gone up from the cherub… to the threshold of the house"** — the first step of its leaving. It goes on to the east gate (10:19) and to the mountain east of the city (11:23).
- **vv4, 6 "put a mark on the foreheads… do not go near anyone who has the mark on him"** — *tav*, the name of the last letter of the Hebrew alphabet, used here for a mark. The Greek has *a sign*. The ones marked are those *who sigh and groan*, *ha-ne'enachim ve-ha-ne'enaqim*, two words that sound alike, over what was done in the city.
- **v6 "Begin at My sanctuary"** — the judgment starts where the detestable things of chapter 8 were done, with the elders in front of the house.
- **vv8–9 "Will You destroy all the remnant of Israel?… they say… 'the LORD does not see'"** — Ezekiel's first plea. God answers with the elders' own words from the dark room, *The LORD does not see us* (8:12).
- **v11 "I have done all that You commanded me"** — the scribe reports back, and the chapter ends on it. Who received the mark is not told.

# Ezekiel 40

## The Man with the Measuring Reed

**1** In the twenty-fifth year of our exile, at the beginning of the year, on the tenth of the month, in the fourteenth year after the city was struck down, on that very day the hand of the LORD was on me, and He brought me there.

**2** In visions of God He brought me into the land of Israel and set me on a very high mountain, and on it, to the south, was something like the structure of a city.

**3** He brought me there, and there was a man whose appearance was like the appearance of bronze, with a linen cord and a measuring reed in his hand; and he was standing in the gateway.

**4** The man said to me, "Son of man, look with your eyes and hear with your ears, and set your heart on everything I show you, for you were brought here so that I might show it to you. Tell the house of Israel everything you see."

**5** There was a wall all around the outside of the house. In the man's hand was a measuring reed six cubits long, each cubit a cubit and a handbreadth. He measured the thickness of the wall: one reed; and its height: one reed.

## The East Gate

**6** Then he went to the gate facing east, went up its steps and measured the threshold of the gate: one reed deep, the one threshold one reed deep.

**7** Each side room was one reed long and one reed wide, and between the side rooms were five cubits. The threshold of the gate beside the porch of the gate, on the inside, was one reed.

**8** He measured the porch of the gate on the inside: one reed.

**9** He measured the porch of the gate: eight cubits, and its jambs two cubits. The porch of the gate was on the inside.

**10** The side rooms of the east gate were three on one side and three on the other. The three had the same measurement, and the jambs on either side had the same measurement.

**11** He measured the width of the opening of the gate: ten cubits; and the length of the gate: thirteen cubits.

**12** There was a barrier in front of the side rooms, one cubit on one side and one cubit on the other; and the side rooms were six cubits on one side and six cubits on the other.

**13** He measured the gate from the roof of one side room to the roof of the other: twenty-five cubits across, from opening to opposite opening.

**14** He made the jambs sixty cubits, and the court reached to the jamb, all around the gate.

**15** From the front of the entrance gate to the front of the inner porch of the gate was fifty cubits.

**16** The side rooms and their jambs had recessed windows toward the inside of the gate all around, and so did the porches; there were windows all around on the inside, and on the jambs were palm trees.

## The Outer Court and Its Gates

**17** Then he brought me into the outer court, and there were chambers and a pavement made for the court all around; thirty chambers faced the pavement.

**18** The pavement ran beside the gates, as long as the gates were; this was the lower pavement.

**19** He measured the width from the front of the lower gate to the front of the inner court, outside: a hundred cubits, on the east and on the north.

**20** As for the gate of the outer court that faced north, he measured its length and its width.

**21** Its side rooms, three on one side and three on the other, its jambs and its porch had the same measurement as the first gate: fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.

**22** Its windows, its porch and its palm trees had the same measurements as the gate that faced east. Seven steps went up to it, and its porch was in front of them.

**23** The inner court had a gate opposite the north gate, as on the east. He measured from gate to gate: a hundred cubits.

**24** Then he led me toward the south, and there was a gate facing south. He measured its jambs and its porch; they had the same measurements as the others.

**25** It and its porch had windows all around, like the other windows: fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.

**26** Seven steps went up to it, and its porch was in front of them; it had palm trees on its jambs, one on each side.

**27** The inner court had a gate toward the south, and he measured from gate to gate toward the south: a hundred cubits.

## The Inner Court and Its Gates

**28** Then he brought me into the inner court through the south gate, and he measured the south gate; it had the same measurements as the others.

**29** Its side rooms, its jambs and its porch had the same measurements as the others, and it and its porch had windows all around: fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.

**30** There were porches all around, twenty-five cubits long and five cubits wide.

**31** Its porch faced the outer court, palm trees were on its jambs, and eight steps went up to it.

**32** Then he brought me into the inner court on the east side and measured the gate; it had the same measurements as the others.

**33** Its side rooms, its jambs and its porch had the same measurements as the others, and it and its porch had windows all around: fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.

**34** Its porch faced the outer court, with palm trees on its jambs on either side, and eight steps went up to it.

**35** Then he brought me to the north gate and measured it; it had the same measurements as the others,

**36** its side rooms, its jambs and its porch; and it had windows all around: fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.

**37** Its jambs faced the outer court, with palm trees on its jambs on either side, and eight steps went up to it.

## The Tables for the Offerings

**38** There was a chamber with its door by the jambs of the gates, where the burnt offering was washed.

**39** In the porch of the gate were two tables on one side and two tables on the other, on which the burnt offering, the sin offering and the guilt offering were to be slaughtered.

**40** On the outer side, as one goes up to the entrance of the north gate, were two tables, and on the other side of the porch of the gate were two tables.

**41** Four tables were on one side and four tables on the other, at the side of the gate: eight tables, on which they slaughtered.

**42** There were also four tables of dressed stone for the burnt offering, a cubit and a half long, a cubit and a half wide and one cubit high. On them they laid the instruments with which they slaughtered the burnt offering and the sacrifice.

**43** Hooks a handbreadth long were fastened all around inside, and the flesh of the offering was to be laid on the tables.

## The Chambers of the Priests

**44** Outside the inner gate, in the inner court, were two chambers: one at the side of the north gate, facing south, and one at the side of the east gate, facing north.

**45** He said to me, "This chamber facing south is for the priests who keep charge of the house,

**46** and the chamber facing north is for the priests who keep charge of the altar. These are the sons of Zadok, those of the sons of Levi who come near to the LORD to serve Him."

**47** He measured the court: a hundred cubits long and a hundred cubits wide, a square; and the altar was in front of the house.

**48** Then he brought me to the porch of the house and measured the jambs of the porch: five cubits on one side and five cubits on the other; and the width of the gate was three cubits on one side and three cubits on the other.

**49** The porch was twenty cubits long and eleven cubits deep. It was reached by steps, and there were pillars beside the jambs, one on each side.

## Notes

- **v1 "at the beginning of the year, on the tenth of the month"** — *be-rosh ha-shanah*, **at the head of the year**, a phrase found only here in the Bible. The Greek has *in the first month*. Exodus calls the month of the Passover *the head of months* (12:2), and on its tenth day each household chose its lamb (12:3). The year is the twenty-fifth of the exile, and the city fell fourteen years before (33:21).
- **v2 "a very high mountain"** — the *high mountain of Israel* where the LORD plants His cedar (17:23) and where all Israel will serve Him (20:40); Isaiah's *mountain of the LORD's house* raised above the hills (Isaiah 2:2).
- **vv3–5 "a measuring reed"** — the man *like the appearance of bronze*, like the living creatures that *sparkled like the gleam of burnished bronze* (1:7), measures everything with a reed of six cubits. Each cubit is *a cubit and a handbreadth*, the long cubit, a handbreadth more than the ordinary one; it sets the scale for the rest of the book. Revelation's angel measures the holy city with a golden reed (Revelation 21:15).
- **v6 "the gate facing east"** — the gate where the glory stood before it left the house: *They stopped at the entrance of the east gate of the house of the LORD* (10:19). The tour begins where the glory went out.
- **vv6–16 the gate** — *ta*, a **side room** or alcove, three on each side of the passage; *ayil*, a **jamb**, the thick wall-pillar at an opening; *ulam*, the **porch**, the word of Solomon's temple (1 Kings 6:3). Several measurements are obscure in the Hebrew and left as they stand: the doubled threshold in v6, the jambs of *sixty cubits* in v14, the *hooks* (*shefattayim*, a word of uncertain meaning) in v43. The Greek often only spells out the Hebrew words (*theē*, *ailam*), as if its translators knew them no better.
- **v16 "palm trees"** — *timorim*, the carved palms of Solomon's house, *cherubim, palm trees and open flowers* (1 Kings 6:29).
- **vv22, 31 "Seven steps… eight steps"** — the outer gates are reached by seven steps, the inner gates by eight; the Greek gives the porch of the house ten steps (v49), where the Hebrew has *steps* with no number. Each court stands above the last.
- **v44 "two chambers"** — the Hebrew has *lishkhot sharim*, **chambers for singers**, a phrase found only here. The Greek has *two chambers*, and the rendering follows it: the verse goes on to place *one*… *one*…, and vv45–46 give both to priests. Choice in the report.
- **v46 "the sons of Zadok"** — the priest Solomon put *in the place of Abiathar* (1 Kings 2:35). In the new house only his line serves at the altar.
- **v49 "pillars beside the jambs"** — the two pillars Solomon set at the porch and named Jachin and Boaz (1 Kings 7:21).

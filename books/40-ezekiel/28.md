# Ezekiel 28

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "Son of man, say to the ruler of Tyre: This is what the Lord GOD says: Because your heart is proud and you have said, 'I am a god; I sit in the seat of gods in the heart of the seas,' yet you are a man and not a god, though you make your heart like the heart of a god —

**3** you are wiser than Daniel; no secret is hidden from you;

**4** by your wisdom and your understanding you have gained wealth for yourself and gathered gold and silver into your treasuries;

**5** by your great wisdom in trade you have increased your wealth, and your heart has grown proud because of your wealth —

**6** therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you make your heart like the heart of a god,

**7** therefore I am about to bring strangers against you, the most ruthless of nations; they will draw their swords against the beauty of your wisdom and profane your splendor.

**8** They will bring you down to the pit, and you will die the death of the slain in the heart of the seas.

**9** Will you still say, 'I am a god,' before those who kill you, when you are a man and not a god in the hands of those who wound you?

**10** You will die the death of the uncircumcised by the hands of strangers, for I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD."

## A Lament for the King of Tyre

**11** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**12** "Son of man, take up a lament over the king of Tyre and say to him: This is what the Lord GOD says:  
You were the seal of perfection,  
full of wisdom and perfect in beauty.

**13** You were in Eden, the garden of God;  
every precious stone was your covering:  
carnelian, topaz and diamond,  
beryl, onyx and jasper,  
sapphire, turquoise and emerald;  
and of gold was the work of your settings and your sockets in you;  
on the day you were created they were prepared.

**14** You were an anointed cherub, the one who shelters;  
I set you there.  
You were on the holy mountain of God;  
you walked among the stones of fire.

**15** You were blameless in your ways  
from the day you were created,  
until wrongdoing was found in you.

**16** Through the abundance of your trade  
your inside was filled with violence,  
and you sinned;  
so I cast you out as profane from the mountain of God,  
and I destroyed you, sheltering cherub,  
from among the stones of fire.

**17** Your heart was proud because of your beauty;  
you corrupted your wisdom for the sake of your splendor.  
I threw you to the ground;  
I set you before kings  
for them to look at you.

**18** By the multitude of your iniquities,  
in the dishonesty of your trade,  
you profaned your sanctuaries;  
so I brought fire out from your midst;  
it consumed you,  
and I turned you to ashes on the earth  
in the sight of all who saw you.

**19** All who know you among the peoples  
are appalled at you;  
you have become a terror  
and will be no more forever."

## Sidon, and Israel Gathered

**20** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**21** "Son of man, set your face toward Sidon and prophesy against her,

**22** and say: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am against you, Sidon, and I will be glorified in your midst. They will know that I am the LORD, when I carry out judgments in her and show Myself holy in her.

**23** I will send pestilence into her and blood into her streets; the slain will fall in her midst by the sword that is against her on every side; and they will know that I am the LORD.

**24** No more will there be for the house of Israel a pricking brier or a painful thorn among all around them who treat them with contempt; and they will know that I am the Lord GOD.

**25** This is what the Lord GOD says: When I gather the house of Israel from the peoples among whom they are scattered, and show Myself holy among them in the sight of the nations, they will live in their own land that I gave to My servant Jacob.

**26** They will live in it securely; they will build houses and plant vineyards. They will live securely when I carry out judgments on all around them who treat them with contempt; and they will know that I am the LORD their God."

## Notes

- **vv2, 9 "I am a god… yet you are a man and not a god"** — *el ani… ve-attah adam ve-lo el*. The boast of the king of Babylon in Isaiah, *I will make myself like the Most High* (Isaiah 14:14), now in the mouth of Tyre's ruler, and answered in the same words twice: *a man and not a god*.
- **v3 "you are wiser than Daniel"** — the name is written *Dan'el* and read *Daniel*, as at 14:14, 20. The Greek makes it a question, *Are you wiser than Daniel?*
- **vv12–13 "You were the seal of perfection… You were in Eden, the garden of God"** — the lament moves the king into the first garden. *Perfect in beauty* was Tyre's own boast (27:3).
- **v13 "carnelian, topaz and diamond, beryl, onyx and jasper, sapphire, turquoise and emerald"** — nine of the twelve stones of the high priest's breastpiece, in Exodus's names (Exodus 28:17–20); the third row, *jacinth, agate and amethyst*, is missing. The Greek lists all twelve. *Your settings and your sockets*, *tuppekha u-neqavekha*, is uncertain: the words can also mean "your tambourines and your pipes."
- **vv14, 16 "You were an anointed cherub, the one who shelters… I destroyed you, sheltering cherub"** — *at keruv*, "you, a cherub," as the Hebrew is pointed. The Greek reads the same letters as *et keruv*, "with the cherub": *from the day you were created you were with the cherub*, and in v16, *the cherub drove you out from among the stones of fire*. *Who shelters*, *ha-sokhekh*, is the word of the cherubim over the ark, *sheltering the cover with their wings* (Exodus 25:20).
- **v16 "so I cast you out as profane from the mountain of God"** — the story of Eden: *He drove the human out, and He made dwell east of the garden of Eden the cherubim* (Genesis 3:24). Wealth from trade is what filled the king with violence, the charge Tyre's whole lament has prepared.
- **v24 "a pricking brier or a painful thorn"** — the law's warning about the peoples left in the land, *barbs in your eyes and thorns in your sides* (Numbers 33:55). With the neighbors judged, the thorn is removed.
- **vv25–26 "they will build houses and plant vineyards"** — what Jeremiah told the exiles to do in Babylon, *Build houses and live in them* (Jeremiah 29:5), promised now in their own land.

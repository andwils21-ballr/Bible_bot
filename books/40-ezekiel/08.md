# Ezekiel 8

**1** And it came to pass in the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth day of the month, as I was sitting in my house with the elders of Judah sitting before me, that the hand of the Lord GOD fell on me there.

**2** I looked: a likeness like the appearance of a man. From the appearance of His waist downward was fire, and from His waist upward something like the appearance of brightness, like the gleam of amber.

**3** He stretched out the form of a hand and took me by a lock of my head; and the spirit lifted me up between earth and heaven and brought me in visions of God to Jerusalem, to the entrance of the inner gate that faces north, where the seat of the image of jealousy was, which provokes jealousy.

**4** The glory of the God of Israel was there, like the vision I had seen in the plain.

**5** He said to me, "Son of man, lift up your eyes toward the north." So I lifted up my eyes toward the north, and north of the altar gate, at the entrance, was this image of jealousy.

**6** He said to me, "Son of man, do you see what they are doing, the great detestable things the house of Israel is committing here, to drive Me far from My sanctuary? But you will see still greater detestable things."

**7** He brought me to the entrance of the court, and I looked: there was a hole in the wall.

**8** He said to me, "Son of man, dig through the wall." So I dug through the wall, and there was a doorway.

**9** He said to me, "Go in, and see the evil detestable things they are doing here."

**10** So I went in and looked: carved on the wall all around were every form of creeping thing and loathsome beast, and all the idols of the house of Israel.

**11** Seventy men of the elders of the house of Israel were standing before them, with Jaazaniah son of Shaphan standing among them, each with his censer in his hand, and the fragrance of the cloud of incense was rising.

**12** He said to me, "Son of man, have you seen what the elders of the house of Israel are doing in the dark, each in his room of images? For they say, 'The LORD does not see us; the LORD has forsaken the land.'"

**13** He said to me, "You will see still greater detestable things that they are doing."

**14** He brought me to the entrance of the gate of the house of the LORD that faces north, and there sat women weeping for Tammuz.

**15** He said to me, "Have you seen this, son of man? You will see still greater detestable things than these."

**16** He brought me into the inner court of the house of the LORD, and at the entrance of the temple of the LORD, between the porch and the altar, were about twenty-five men with their backs to the temple of the LORD and their faces toward the east, and they were bowing down to the sun toward the east.

**17** He said to me, "Have you seen this, son of man? Is it too light a thing for the house of Judah to commit the detestable things they have committed here, that they must also fill the land with violence and provoke Me again and again? They are putting the branch to their nose.

**18** So I, too, will act in wrath; My eye will not spare, and I will have no pity. Though they cry in My ears with a loud voice, I will not listen to them."

## Notes

- **v1 "in the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth day"** — a year and two months after the call (1:1–2). The elders of Judah are sitting before him in his house in Babylon when he is taken to Jerusalem.
- **v2 "a likeness like the appearance of a man"** — the Hebrew reads *esh*, "fire": *a likeness like the appearance of fire*. The Greek reads *andros*, "a man," the Hebrew *ish*, which differs from *esh* only in its vowels. The English follows the Greek, because the rest of the verse speaks of *His waist*, and this is the figure of 1:26–27, *a likeness like the appearance of a human*, with fire below the waist and amber above.
- **v3 "took me by a lock of my head"** — *tsitsit*, the word for the tassels on the corners of the garment (Numbers 15:38), here the hair of his head.
- **vv3, 5 "the image of jealousy, which provokes jealousy"** — *semel*, "image," the law's word in *the form of any figure* (Deuteronomy 4:16). The Song of Moses says it: *They made Him jealous with strange gods* (Deuteronomy 32:16). It stands at the gate, where the glory of the God of Israel is also standing (v4).
- **vv7–12 "dig through the wall… carved on the wall all around… each in his room of images"** — seventy elders, the number of the elders who went up Sinai and *saw the God of Israel* (Exodus 24:9–10), burn incense in the dark to pictures on a wall. Among them is *Jaazaniah son of Shaphan*, of a family that stood by Jeremiah: *the hand of Ahikam son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah* (Jeremiah 26:24).
- **v12 "The LORD does not see us"** — they say it in a hidden room that the LORD is at that moment showing to His prophet.
- **v14 "women weeping for Tammuz"** — *ha-Tammuz*, with the article; the Greek has *Thammouz*. The verse says no more than that he was mourned.
- **v16 "with their backs to the temple of the LORD… bowing down to the sun toward the east"** — what the law forbade: *see the sun, the moon and the stars… and be drawn away and bow down to them* (Deuteronomy 4:19). They stand *between the porch and the altar*, at the door of the house itself, and turn their backs on it to do it.
- **v17 "They are putting the branch to their nose"** — *zemorah*, a vine branch. What the act was is not known; the Greek has *they are like people sneering*. It ends the list of what was done in the temple.

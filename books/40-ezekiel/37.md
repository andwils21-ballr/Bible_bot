# Ezekiel 37

## The Dry Bones

**1** The hand of the LORD was on me, and He brought me out by the Spirit of the LORD and set me down in the middle of the plain; it was full of bones.

**2** He led me around among them, all the way around. There were very many of them on the face of the plain, and they were very dry.

**3** He said to me, "Son of man, can these bones live?" I said, "Lord GOD, You know."

**4** He said to me, "Prophesy over these bones and say to them: Dry bones, hear the word of the LORD!

**5** This is what the Lord GOD says to these bones: I am about to bring breath into you, and you will live.

**6** I will put sinews on you, bring flesh up on you, cover you with skin and put breath in you, and you will live; and you will know that I am the LORD."

**7** So I prophesied as I had been commanded. As I prophesied there was a sound, then a rumbling, and the bones came together, bone to its bone.

**8** I looked, and there were sinews on them, flesh had come up, and skin had covered them over; but there was no breath in them.

**9** He said to me, "Prophesy to the breath; prophesy, son of man, and say to the breath: This is what the Lord GOD says: Come from the four winds, breath, and blow into these slain, so that they may live."

**10** So I prophesied as He commanded me, and the breath entered them, and they lived and stood on their feet, an exceedingly great army.

**11** Then He said to me, "Son of man, these bones are the whole house of Israel. They are saying, 'Our bones are dried up, and our hope is lost; we are cut off.'

**12** Therefore prophesy and say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to open your graves and bring you up from your graves, My people, and bring you into the land of Israel.

**13** You will know that I am the LORD, when I open your graves and bring you up from your graves, My people.

**14** I will put My Spirit in you, and you will live, and I will settle you on your own soil. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it, declares the LORD."

## The Two Sticks

**15** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**16** "As for you, son of man, take a stick and write on it, 'For Judah, and for the sons of Israel associated with him.' Then take another stick and write on it, 'For Joseph, the stick of Ephraim, and all the house of Israel associated with him.'

**17** Join them to each other into one stick, so that they become one in your hand.

**18** When the sons of your people say to you, 'Will you not tell us what you mean by these?'

**19** say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to take the stick of Joseph, which is in the hand of Ephraim, and the tribes of Israel associated with him, and join them to the stick of Judah, and make them one stick, and they will be one in My hand.

**20** The sticks you write on will be in your hand before their eyes.

**21** Say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to take the sons of Israel from among the nations where they have gone, gather them from all around, and bring them into their own land.

**22** I will make them one nation in the land, on the mountains of Israel, and one king will be king over them all. They will no longer be two nations, and no longer be divided into two kingdoms.

**23** They will no longer defile themselves with their idols and their loathsome things and all their transgressions. I will save them from all their dwelling places where they sinned, and cleanse them; they will be My people, and I will be their God.

**24** My servant David will be king over them, and there will be one shepherd for them all. They will walk in My judgments and keep My statutes and do them.

**25** They will live in the land I gave to My servant Jacob, where your fathers lived. They will live in it, they and their sons and their sons' sons forever, and David My servant will be their prince forever.

**26** I will make a covenant of peace with them; it will be an everlasting covenant with them. I will establish them and multiply them, and set My sanctuary among them forever.

**27** My dwelling place will be over them; I will be their God, and they will be My people.

**28** Then the nations will know that I am the LORD who makes Israel holy, when My sanctuary is among them forever."

## Notes

- **v1 "the plain"** — *ha-biq'ah*, with the article, as in 3:22–23 and 8:4: the plain where the glory stood when he went out to it, *and the glory of the LORD was standing there* (3:23). Now it is full of bones.
- **vv1–14 Spirit, breath, winds** — all one Hebrew word, *ruach*, ten times in fourteen verses. It is *the Spirit of the LORD* who carries him (v1); *breath* that the bones lack (vv5–10); *the four winds* the breath is called from (v9); and *My Spirit* put into the people (v14). English needs three words where the Hebrew uses one, and the vision turns on it: the wind from the four corners of the earth, the breath of the living, and God's own Spirit are not kept apart.
- **v7 "a rumbling"** — *ra'ash*, the sound he heard behind him when the living creatures' wings brushed together (3:12–13). The noise of the glory is the noise of the bones moving.
- **v9 "blow into these slain"** — *u-fechi*, from *nafach*, the verb of the first human: *and blew into his nostrils the breath of lives* (Genesis 2:7). Here, as there, a body is formed first and is not alive until breath is blown into it; the creation of the human is done a second time. *Harugim*, **slain**, says how these died: by violence, as an army falls.
- **v11 "we are cut off"** — *nigzarnu lanu*, the cry of Lamentations, *I said, "I am cut off"* (Lamentations 3:54). As in 33:10, the vision answers what the exiles were saying about themselves.
- **vv12–13 "I am about to open your graves"** — the picture changes from a battlefield to tombs, and the promise is to bring the people up out of them into their land. Isaiah had said it of Israel's dead: *Your dead will live; my dead ones will rise* (Isaiah 26:19). At Jesus' death Matthew records the same picture made literal: *the tombs were opened* (Matthew 27:52).
- **vv16–19 "take a stick and write on it"** — *ets*, wood: Judah, the southern kingdom, and Joseph, through his son Ephraim, the northern kingdom that broke away from David's house (1 Kings 12:16). The Greek calls them *rhabdoi*, **rods**, the word its Numbers uses for the twelve staffs: *Write each man's name on his staff* (Numbers 17:2).
- **vv22, 24 "one king… king over them"** — *melekh*, where 34:24 said *a prince*; v25 says *prince* again. The Greek has *archōn*, **ruler**, in vv22 and 24.
- **v23 "from all their dwelling places where they sinned"** — *moshvoteihem*, the word of 6:6, *In all your dwelling places the cities will be laid waste*, where the idols stood. With one letter moved, the same letters read *meshuvoteihem*, **their faithlessness**, the word of Jeremiah 3:22, *I will heal your faithlessness*. The Greek has *from all their lawless deeds*.
- **vv26–28 "My sanctuary among them… My dwelling place"** — *miqdash* and *mishkan*, the two names of the tent in the wilderness: *Let them make Me a sanctuary, and I will dwell among them* (Exodus 25:8). Verse 27 is Leviticus almost word for word: *I will set My dwelling among you… and I will be your God, and you will be My people* (Leviticus 26:11–12), and Paul quotes it of the church (2 Corinthians 6:16). After the oracles against Gog (chapters 38–39), the book's last nine chapters are the vision of that sanctuary.

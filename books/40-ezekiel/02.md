# Ezekiel 2

**1** He said to me, "Son of man, stand on your feet, and I will speak with you."

**2** As He spoke to me, the spirit entered me and set me on my feet, and I heard Him speaking to me.

**3** He said to me, "Son of man, I am sending you to the sons of Israel, to rebellious nations that have rebelled against Me. They and their fathers have transgressed against Me to this very day.

**4** The sons are hard-faced and hard-hearted. I am sending you to them; say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says.'

**5** As for them, whether they hear or refuse — for they are a rebellious house — they will know that a prophet has been among them.

**6** As for you, son of man, do not be afraid of them or afraid of their words, though briers and thorns are around you and you live among scorpions. Do not be afraid of their words or dismayed by their looks, for they are a rebellious house.

**7** Speak My words to them, whether they hear or refuse, for they are rebellious.

**8** But you, son of man, hear what I am saying to you: do not be rebellious like that rebellious house. Open your mouth and eat what I am giving you."

**9** I looked: a hand was stretched out to me, and in it was a scroll.

**10** He spread it out before me, and it was written on the front and on the back; and written on it were laments and mourning and woe.

## Notes

- **v1 "Son of man"** — *ben adam*, "son of a human," a member of humankind. It is God's name for Ezekiel throughout the book, and it comes first just after the vision of *a likeness like the appearance of a human* on the throne (1:26). The one on the throne only looks like a human; the one on the ground is one.
- **v4 "hard-faced and hard-hearted"** — *qeshei fanim ve-chizqei lev*. The second word is the root of Ezekiel's own name, *Yechezqel*, "God makes strong," and 3:7–9 turns on it.
- **vv8–10 "eat what I am giving you… a scroll"** — Jeremiah said, *Your words were found, and I ate them* (Jeremiah 15:16); for Ezekiel the eating is not a figure, and the next chapter tells it.

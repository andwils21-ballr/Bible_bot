# Ezekiel 1

**1** And it came to pass in the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, on the fifth day of the month, as I was among the exiles by the river Chebar, that the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God.

**2** On the fifth day of the month, in the fifth year of King Jehoiachin's exile,

**3** the word of the LORD came to Ezekiel son of Buzi, the priest, in the land of the Chaldeans by the river Chebar; and the hand of the LORD was on him there.

## The Living Creatures

**4** I looked: a storm wind was coming out of the north, a great cloud with fire flashing through it and brightness all around it, and from the middle of it, from the middle of the fire, something like the gleam of amber.

**5** From the middle of it came the likeness of four living creatures. This was their appearance: they had the likeness of a human.

**6** Each had four faces, and each of them had four wings.

**7** Their legs were straight, and the soles of their feet were like the sole of a calf's foot; and they sparkled like the gleam of burnished bronze.

**8** They had human hands under their wings on their four sides. As for the faces and wings of the four of them,

**9** their wings touched one another. They did not turn as they went; each went straight ahead.

**10** As for the likeness of their faces, each of the four had the face of a human, and on the right the face of a lion, and on the left the face of an ox, and each of the four had the face of an eagle.

**11** Such were their faces. Their wings were spread out above: each had two wings touching those of another, and two covering their bodies.

**12** Each went straight ahead. Wherever the spirit would go, they went, without turning as they went.

**13** As for the likeness of the living creatures, their appearance was like burning coals of fire, like the appearance of torches. Fire moved back and forth among the living creatures; the fire was bright, and lightning went out of the fire.

**14** The living creatures darted back and forth like the appearance of a flash of lightning.

## The Wheels

**15** As I looked at the living creatures, I saw a wheel on the ground beside each of the living creatures, for each of their four faces.

**16** The appearance of the wheels and their making was like the gleam of beryl, and all four had one likeness; their appearance and their making were as if a wheel were inside a wheel.

**17** When they went, they went in any of their four directions, without turning as they went.

**18** As for their rims, they were high and fearsome, and the rims of all four were full of eyes all around.

**19** When the living creatures went, the wheels went beside them; and when the living creatures rose from the ground, the wheels rose.

**20** Wherever the spirit would go, they went, wherever the spirit went, and the wheels rose along with them, for the spirit of the living creature was in the wheels.

**21** When those went, these went; when those stood still, these stood still; and when those rose from the ground, the wheels rose along with them, for the spirit of the living creature was in the wheels.

## The Throne

**22** Over the heads of the living creature was the likeness of an expanse, like the gleam of awe-inspiring crystal, spread out over their heads above.

**23** Under the expanse their wings were stretched out straight, one toward another. Each had two that covered its body on one side, and each had two that covered it on the other side.

**24** When they went, I heard the sound of their wings, like the sound of many waters, like the voice of the Almighty, a sound of tumult like the sound of an army. When they stood still, they let down their wings.

**25** A voice came from above the expanse that was over their heads; when they stood still, they let down their wings.

**26** Above the expanse that was over their heads was something like the appearance of sapphire stone, the likeness of a throne; and on the likeness of the throne, high above it, was a likeness like the appearance of a human.

**27** I saw something like the gleam of amber, like the appearance of fire enclosed all around, from the appearance of His waist upward; and from the appearance of His waist downward I saw something like the appearance of fire, and there was brightness all around Him.

**28** Like the appearance of the bow that is in the cloud on a day of rain, so was the appearance of the brightness all around. That was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the LORD. When I saw it, I fell on my face, and I heard the voice of One speaking.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "in the thirtieth year… the fifth year of King Jehoiachin's exile"** — two dates, and the first is not said to be of anything: the book never says what the thirtieth year counts from. The second is the exiles' own count, from the year the king was taken to Babylon (2 Kings 24:12–15). The prophet speaks as *I* in v1, and v3 names him in the third person, *Ezekiel son of Buzi, the priest*.
- **v3 "the hand of the LORD was on him there"** — Ezekiel's words for being seized by the LORD. The *hand* comes on him again at 3:14 and 3:22, each time before he is moved or shown something.
- **vv4, 27 "something like the gleam of amber"** — *chashmal*, found only in Ezekiel (here, v27 and 8:2). What it names is not known. The Greek has *ēlektron*, its word both for amber and for a shining alloy of gold and silver. The English takes the first; either way it is something that glows in the fire.
- **vv5, 10 "four living creatures… the face of a human… a lion… an ox… an eagle"** — *chayyot*, plain "living things." The book names them later: he sees them again by the river Chebar and *knew that they were cherubim* (10:20), and there the face of the ox is called *the face of a cherub* (10:14).
- **v14, v24 "darted back and forth… like the voice of the Almighty, a sound of tumult like the sound of an army"** — the Greek has neither: no v14, and in v24 only *like the sound of much water*. *Shaddai*, the Almighty, appears here for the first time in the book.
- **v22 "the likeness of an expanse… spread out over their heads"** — *raqia*, the word of the second day: *Let there be a beaten expanse in the middle of the waters* (Genesis 1:6). In Genesis it holds back the waters above; here it carries the throne.
- **v26 "something like the appearance of sapphire stone, the likeness of a throne"** — what the elders saw at Sinai, *under His feet was something like brickwork of sapphire* (Exodus 24:10). Ezekiel sees the stone again, and now a throne on it.
- **v28 "Like the appearance of the bow that is in the cloud on a day of rain"** — the bow of the covenant with Noah, *My bow I have set in the cloud* (Genesis 9:13).
- **v28 "the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the LORD"** — the chapter piles up two words, *mar'eh*, "appearance," and *demut*, "likeness," more than twenty times, and never once says that Ezekiel saw the LORD. At the top of it all is *the appearance of the likeness of the glory*, three steps removed, and that is where he falls on his face.

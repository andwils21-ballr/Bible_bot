# Ezekiel 5

**1** "As for you, son of man, take a sharp sword; use it as a barber's razor, and pass it over your head and your beard. Then take scales for weighing, and divide the hair.

**2** A third you shall burn in the fire inside the city when the days of the siege are completed. A third you shall take and strike with the sword all around it. A third you shall scatter to the wind, and I will draw out a sword after them.

**3** Take from there a few, a small number, and bind them in the hem of your garment.

**4** Of these again you shall take some, throw them into the fire and burn them in the fire; from it a fire will go out to all the house of Israel.

**5** This is what the Lord GOD says: This is Jerusalem. I have set her in the middle of the nations, with countries all around her.

**6** She has rebelled against My judgments more wickedly than the nations, and against My statutes more than the countries around her; for they have rejected My judgments and have not walked in My statutes.

**7** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have been more unruly than the nations around you, and have not walked in My statutes or kept My judgments, and have not even acted by the judgments of the nations around you,

**8** therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I Myself am against you, and I will carry out judgments in your midst in the sight of the nations.

**9** Because of all your detestable things I will do with you what I have never done, and the like of which I will never do again.

**10** Therefore fathers will eat their sons in your midst, and sons will eat their fathers. I will carry out judgments on you, and I will scatter all your remnant to every wind.

**11** Therefore, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, because you have made My sanctuary unclean with all your loathsome things and all your detestable things, I in turn will withdraw; My eye will not spare, and I in turn will have no pity.

**12** A third of you will die of pestilence and be consumed by famine in your midst; a third will fall by the sword around you; and a third I will scatter to every wind, and I will draw out a sword after them.

**13** So My anger will be spent, and I will satisfy My wrath on them and be eased; and they will know that I, the LORD, have spoken in My jealousy, when I have spent My wrath on them.

**14** I will make you a ruin and a reproach among the nations around you, in the sight of everyone who passes by.

**15** You will be a reproach and a taunt, a warning and a horror to the nations around you, when I carry out judgments on you in anger and in wrath and with furious rebukes. I, the LORD, have spoken.

**16** When I send against them the deadly arrows of famine, arrows for destruction, which I send to destroy you, I will bring more and more famine on you and break your staff of bread.

**17** I will send famine and wild beasts against you, and they will rob you of your children; pestilence and blood will pass through you, and I will bring the sword on you. I, the LORD, have spoken."

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "take a sharp sword; use it as a barber's razor… divide the hair"** — a priest was forbidden to shave his head or *the edge of his beard* (Leviticus 21:5). Ezekiel the priest shaves both with a sword, and the hair is the city: a third burned, a third cut down, a third scattered. Even the few kept back in his hem are not all safe.
- **v5 "I have set her in the middle of the nations"** — Jerusalem's place among the nations is what makes her sin public. Every judgment in the chapter is done *in the sight of the nations* (v8), *in the sight of everyone who passes by* (v14).
- **v7 "and have not even acted by the judgments of the nations around you"** — the Hebrew says *not*: Jerusalem has kept neither God's judgments nor even the laws of her neighbors. The Greek reads the same. At 11:12 the book says the opposite charge, that they *acted by the judgments of the nations*; each verse makes its own point.
- **v10 "fathers will eat their sons… and sons will eat their fathers"** — the siege curse of the law (Leviticus 26:29; Deuteronomy 28:53), here turned both ways.
- **v13 "I will satisfy My wrath on them and be eased"** — *ve-hinnechamti*, from *nicham*, the verb used for being comforted and for relenting. Here it is the relief of anger spent.
- **vv16–17 "break your staff of bread… wild beasts… rob you of your children… I will bring the sword on you"** — the curses of Leviticus 26, in its words: *When I break your staff of bread* (26:26), *they will rob you of your children* (26:22), *I will bring a sword on you* (26:25). Ezekiel the priest preaches the end of the covenant from the covenant's own text.

# Te'ezaz 1

In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, one God. This is the Sinodos of the fathers, the Apostles, which they ordered for the direction of the Church.

Greetings, our sons and our daughters, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

## The Apostles Divide the Earth

**1** John, Matthew, Peter, Philip, Simon, James, Nathanael, Thomas, Cephas, Andrew, Bartholomew, Judas, and James the brother of our Lord said: When we were assembled together with the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ, our Savior, He commanded us and said to us, "Divide the earth among yourselves, so that each of you may take his place according to your number. Take the order of bishops, the seat of presbyters, the works of deacons, the understanding of readers, and widows without blame; and the work on which the foundations of the Church may be made firm, by which they may make a pattern of what is in the heavens, and keep themselves from all wickedness. Know that they themselves will answer on the day of judgment for having heard and not kept." He commanded us to send these words into all the world. It is right that each of us should be as our Lord — to Him be glory — revealed to us by the will of God the Father through the Holy Spirit, and that, remembering His word, we should command you with it, as a reminder and an instruction, brothers.

## Notes

- **The whole book** — worked from the English of G. W. Horner, *The Statutes of the Apostles* (1904), made from the Ethiopic. No Ge'ez or other source language was used; Horner's printed Ethiopic, read by machine, was used only to check names. The chapters are his numbered statutes; each verse is one of his paragraphs. His opening lines before statute 1 are set above verse 1.
- **The opening, "the Sinodos"** — the Greek *synodos*, "a gathering, a council", the name the Ethiopian Church gives to its book of church law. Te'ezaz is the part of it that stands first.
- **v1 the names** — thirteen names, not twelve. Peter and Cephas are listed as two different men, though the Gospel says they are one: *You will be called Cephas, which is translated Peter* (John 1:42). Nathanael stands beside Bartholomew, and the last is *James the brother of our Lord*, the title Paul gives him (Galatians 1:19, *Iakōbon ton adelphon tou kyriou*). Matthias and Judas Iscariot are absent. The names are given here in their English forms; Horner keeps the Ethiopic spellings (*Yuhanes*, *Pētros*, *Kēfas*).
- **v1 "Divide the earth among yourselves"** — the commission is geographic: each apostle takes a region *according to your number*. The orders of the Church follow at once, bishops, presbyters, deacons, readers and widows, as the first thing to be set up in each place.
- **v1 "a pattern of what is in the heavens"** — the order of the Church on earth is to copy an order in heaven, as the dwelling was made by the pattern Moses was shown: *make them by their pattern, which you are being shown on the mountain* (Exodus 25:40; also 25:9).
- **v1 "they themselves will answer on the day of judgment for having heard and not kept"** — Jesus in Matthew 12:36: *for every careless word people speak, they will give an account on the day of judgment* (*apodōsousin peri autou logon en hēmera kriseōs*).
- **Source text** — G. W. Horner, *The Statutes of the Apostles, or Canones Ecclesiastici* (1904), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 2 Chronicles 5

## The Ark Brought Up

**1** So all the work that Solomon did for the house of the LORD was finished. Solomon brought in the things David his father had dedicated, the silver, the gold and all the vessels, and put them in the treasuries of the house of God.

**2** Then Solomon assembled the elders of Israel and all the heads of the tribes, the leaders of the fathers' houses of the sons of Israel, in Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the City of David, which is Zion.

**3** All the men of Israel assembled before the king at the feast, which is in the seventh month.

**4** All the elders of Israel came, and the Levites took up the ark.

**5** They brought up the ark, the tent of meeting and all the holy vessels that were in the tent; the Levitical priests brought them up.

**6** King Solomon and all the congregation of Israel who had gathered to him were before the ark, sacrificing sheep and oxen so many that they could not be counted or numbered.

**7** The priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD to its place, into the inner sanctuary of the house, into the Most Holy Place, under the wings of the cherubim.

**8** The cherubim spread their wings over the place of the ark, and the cherubim covered the ark and its poles from above.

**9** The poles were so long that the ends of the poles were seen from the ark in front of the inner sanctuary, but they could not be seen from outside. It is there to this day.

**10** There was nothing in the ark except the two tablets that Moses put there at Horeb, when the LORD made a covenant with the sons of Israel as they came out of Egypt.

## The Glory Fills the House

**11** When the priests came out of the holy place — for all the priests who were present had consecrated themselves, without keeping to their divisions,

**12** and the Levites who were singers, all of them, Asaph, Heman and Jeduthun, their sons and their kinsmen, clothed in fine linen, with cymbals, harps and lyres, stood east of the altar, and with them a hundred and twenty priests blowing trumpets —

**13** when the trumpeters and the singers were as one, to make one sound heard in praising and thanking the LORD, and when they raised the sound with trumpets and cymbals and instruments of song, and praised the LORD, "For He is good, for His loyal love endures forever," the house, the house of the LORD, was filled with a cloud,

**14** so that the priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud, for the glory of the LORD filled the house of God.

## Notes

- **vv2–10 and 1 Kings 8:1–9** — the same account. Chronicles leaves out the name of the month, *Ethanim* (1 Kings 8:2). The feast of the seventh month is the Festival of Booths (Leviticus 23:34).
- **v4 "the Levites took up the ark"** — Kings has *the priests took up the ark* (1 Kings 8:3). Chronicles holds to David's word after Uzza died: *No one but the Levites may carry the ark of God* (1 Chronicles 15:2).
- **v5 "the Levitical priests brought them up"** — *ha-kohanim ha-Leviyyim*, the phrase of Deuteronomy, *the Levitical priests* (Deuteronomy 17:9). Kings has *the priests and the Levites* (1 Kings 8:4), with *and*.
- **v9 "from the ark… It is there to this day"** — *min ha-aron*; Kings has *from the holy place* (1 Kings 8:8). *Va-yehi sham*, **it is there**, is singular, the ark itself; Kings has the plural, *va-yihyu sham*, **they are there**, the poles, and the Greek of Chronicles has the plural too.
- **vv11–13 the singers and the trumpets** — not in Kings. Chronicles sets the Levite singers of 1 Chronicles 25 and a hundred and twenty priests with trumpets between the priests coming out and the cloud coming in. The words they sing are the refrain David gave the singers: *for His loyal love endures forever* (1 Chronicles 16:34, 41).
- **v13 "as one, to make one sound heard"** — *ke-echad… qol echad*. The cloud comes when the voices are one.
- **vv13–14 "filled with a cloud… the priests could not stand to minister"** — as at the tabernacle: *Moses could not come into the tent of meeting, because the cloud had settled on it and the glory of the LORD filled the dwelling* (Exodus 40:35).

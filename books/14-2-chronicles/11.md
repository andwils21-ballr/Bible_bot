# 2 Chronicles 11

## Shemaiah's Word

**1** When Rehoboam came to Jerusalem, he assembled the house of Judah and Benjamin, a hundred and eighty thousand chosen warriors, to fight against Israel, to bring the kingdom back to Rehoboam.

**2** But the word of the LORD came to Shemaiah the man of God, saying,

**3** "Say to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin,

**4** 'This is what the LORD says: Do not go up or fight against your brothers. Return every man to his house, for this thing is from Me.'" So they listened to the words of the LORD and turned back from going against Jeroboam.

## The Fortified Cities

**5** Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem, and he built cities for defense in Judah.

**6** He built Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa,

**7** Beth-zur, Soco, Adullam,

**8** Gath, Mareshah, Ziph,

**9** Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah,

**10** Zorah, Aijalon and Hebron, fortified cities in Judah and in Benjamin.

**11** He strengthened the fortresses and put commanders in them, and stores of food, oil and wine.

**12** In every city he put shields and spears, and made them very strong. So Judah and Benjamin were his.

## The Priests and Levites Come to Judah

**13** The priests and the Levites who were in all Israel took their stand with him from all their territory.

**14** For the Levites left their pasture lands and their holdings and came to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had rejected them from serving as priests to the LORD,

**15** and he had appointed his own priests for the high places, for the goat-spirits and for the calves that he had made.

**16** After them, those from all the tribes of Israel who set their hearts to seek the LORD, the God of Israel, came to Jerusalem to sacrifice to the LORD, the God of their fathers.

**17** They strengthened the kingdom of Judah and made Rehoboam son of Solomon secure for three years, for they walked in the way of David and Solomon for three years.

## Rehoboam's Family

**18** Rehoboam took as his wife Mahalath, the daughter of Jerimoth son of David, and Abihail the daughter of Eliab son of Jesse.

**19** She bore him sons: Jeush, Shemariah and Zaham.

**20** After her he took Maacah the daughter of Absalom, and she bore him Abijah, Attai, Ziza and Shelomith.

**21** Rehoboam loved Maacah the daughter of Absalom more than all his wives and secondary wives, for he took eighteen wives and sixty secondary wives, and fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.

**22** Rehoboam appointed Abijah son of Maacah as chief, leader among his brothers, for he meant to make him king.

**23** He acted wisely and distributed some of all his sons through all the districts of Judah and Benjamin, in all the fortified cities. He gave them provisions in abundance, and sought many wives for them.

## Notes

- **v17 "for three years… for three years"** — the Hebrew says it twice. The next chapter begins when the three years are over: *he abandoned the instruction of the LORD* (12:1).
- **v18 "Mahalath, the daughter of Jerimoth son of David"** — written *ben*, **son**, read *bat*, **daughter**; the text follows the read form. Jerimoth is not among David's sons in 1 Chronicles 3. *Abihail the daughter of Eliab* may be a second wife or Mahalath's mother; the Hebrew does not say. Eliab is Jesse's firstborn (1 Chronicles 2:13).

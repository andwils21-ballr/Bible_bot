# 2 Chronicles 3

## The Site and the Measures

**1** Solomon began to build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where the LORD had appeared to David his father, at the place that David had prepared, on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**2** He began to build in the second month, on the second day, in the fourth year of his reign.

**3** These are the foundations Solomon laid for building the house of God: the length, in cubits of the old standard, was sixty cubits, and the width twenty cubits.

**4** The porch in front, across the width of the house, was twenty cubits long, and its height was a hundred and twenty. He overlaid it inside with pure gold.

## The Great House

**5** He paneled the great house with cypress wood, overlaid it with fine gold, and worked palm trees and chains on it.

**6** He adorned the house with precious stones for beauty, and the gold was gold of Parvaim.

**7** He overlaid the house with gold, its beams, its thresholds, its walls and its doors, and he engraved cherubim on the walls.

## The Most Holy Place

**8** He made the room of the Most Holy Place. Its length, across the width of the house, was twenty cubits, and its width twenty cubits. He overlaid it with fine gold, six hundred talents.

**9** The weight of the nails was fifty shekels of gold. He overlaid the upper rooms with gold.

**10** In the room of the Most Holy Place he made two cherubim of sculptured work, and they overlaid them with gold.

**11** The wings of the cherubim were twenty cubits long. One wing of the one, five cubits, touched the wall of the house, and its other wing, five cubits, touched the wing of the other cherub.

**12** One wing of the other cherub, five cubits, touched the wall of the house, and its other wing, five cubits, met the wing of the first cherub.

**13** The wings of these cherubim spread out twenty cubits. They stood on their feet, and their faces were toward the house.

**14** He made the veil of blue, purple, crimson and fine linen, and worked cherubim on it.

## The Pillars

**15** In front of the house he made two pillars, thirty-five cubits high, and the capital on the top of each was five cubits.

**16** He made chains, in the inner sanctuary, and put them on the tops of the pillars, and he made a hundred pomegranates and put them on the chains.

**17** He set up the pillars in front of the nave, one on the right and one on the left. He called the one on the right Jachin and the one on the left Boaz.

## Notes

- **v1 "on Mount Moriah, where the LORD had appeared to David"** — the name is found elsewhere only in Genesis 22:2, *the land of Moriah*, where Abraham was sent to offer Isaac. There the place was named for seeing: *On the mountain of the LORD it will be seen* (Genesis 22:14). Here, *nir'ah*, **He appeared**, from the same verb: at Ornan's threshing floor David saw the messenger of the LORD, and the LORD answered him with fire (1 Chronicles 21:16, 26).
- **v3 "cubits of the old standard"** — *ba-middah ha-rishonah*, **by the first measure**, an older standard than the writer's own. Ezekiel's measuring cubit is *a cubit and a handbreadth* (Ezekiel 40:5). The measures, sixty by twenty, are those of Kings (1 Kings 6:2).
- **v4 "its height was a hundred and twenty"** — the house itself was thirty cubits high (1 Kings 6:2); Kings gives the porch no height (1 Kings 6:3). The Greek also has *a hundred and twenty*. The text keeps the number.
- **v6 "gold of Parvaim"** — a place named only here. Where it was is not known.
- **v10 "cherubim of sculptured work"** — *ma'aseh tsa'tsu'im*, a word found only here, of uncertain meaning. The Greek has *a work of wood*, as Kings has the cherubim made of olivewood (1 Kings 6:23).
- **v13 "their faces were toward the house"** — the cherubim of the tabernacle faced *one toward the other… toward the cover* (Exodus 25:20). These stand on their feet and face the house.
- **v14 "the veil of blue, purple, crimson and fine linen… cherubim on it"** — the veil of the tabernacle, *of blue and purple and crimson and fine twisted linen… with cherubim* (Exodus 26:31). Kings has doors of olivewood at the entrance of the inner sanctuary (1 Kings 6:31) and no veil.
- **v15 "two pillars, thirty-five cubits high"** — Kings has *eighteen cubits* (1 Kings 7:15). The text keeps the Hebrew of each.
- **v16 "chains, in the inner sanctuary"** — *ba-devir*, which does not fit pillars standing at the porch; the Greek copies the word, *en tō dabeir*. The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **v17 "Jachin… Boaz"** — *Yakhin*, from *kun*, **to establish**, the verb of the promise to David, *I will establish his throne forever* (1 Chronicles 17:12). *Boaz* is the name of David's great-grandfather (Ruth 4:21).

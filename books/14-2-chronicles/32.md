# 2 Chronicles 32

## The Defense of Jerusalem

**1** After these things and this faithfulness, Sennacherib king of Assyria came and entered Judah. He encamped against the fortified cities and thought to break into them for himself.

**2** When Hezekiah saw that Sennacherib had come and meant to make war against Jerusalem,

**3** he took counsel with his officials and his mighty men to stop the water of the springs that were outside the city, and they helped him.

**4** Many people gathered and stopped all the springs and the brook that flowed through the land, saying, "Why should the kings of Assyria come and find much water?"

**5** He took courage and built up all the wall that was broken down, and raised towers on it, and built another wall outside it. He strengthened the Millo in the City of David, and made weapons and shields in abundance.

**6** He set commanders of war over the people, gathered them to him in the square at the gate of the city, and spoke encouragingly to them, saying,

**7** "Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid and do not lose heart because of the king of Assyria and all the multitude that is with him, for there is more with us than with him.

**8** With him is an arm of flesh, but with us is the LORD our God, to help us and to fight our battles." The people relied on the words of Hezekiah king of Judah.

## Sennacherib's Taunts

**9** After this Sennacherib king of Assyria, who was besieging Lachish with all his forces, sent his servants to Jerusalem, to Hezekiah king of Judah and to all Judah who were in Jerusalem, saying,

**10** "Thus says Sennacherib king of Assyria: On what are you trusting, that you remain under siege in Jerusalem?

**11** Is not Hezekiah misleading you, to give you over to die by hunger and thirst, when he says, 'The LORD our God will deliver us from the hand of the king of Assyria'?

**12** Is it not this same Hezekiah who has removed His high places and His altars, and told Judah and Jerusalem, 'You shall bow down before one altar, and on it you shall burn incense'?

**13** Do you not know what I and my fathers have done to all the peoples of the lands? Were the gods of the nations of those lands at all able to deliver their land from my hand?

**14** Who among all the gods of those nations that my fathers devoted to destruction was able to deliver his people from my hand, that your God should be able to deliver you from my hand?

**15** So now do not let Hezekiah deceive you or mislead you like this, and do not believe him, for no god of any nation or kingdom has been able to deliver his people from my hand or from the hand of my fathers. How much less will your God deliver you from my hand!"

**16** His servants said still more against the LORD God and against Hezekiah His servant.

**17** He also wrote letters to defy the LORD, the God of Israel, and to speak against Him, saying, "As the gods of the nations of the lands have not delivered their people from my hand, so the God of Hezekiah will not deliver His people from my hand."

**18** They called out with a loud voice in the language of Judah to the people of Jerusalem who were on the wall, to frighten and terrify them, so that they might take the city.

**19** They spoke of the God of Jerusalem as of the gods of the peoples of the earth, the work of human hands.

## The Deliverance

**20** Then Hezekiah the king and Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz, prayed about this and cried out to heaven.

**21** The LORD sent a messenger, who cut off every mighty man of valor, leader and commander in the camp of the king of Assyria. So he returned in shame to his own land. When he had gone into the house of his god, some of his own offspring struck him down there with the sword.

**22** So the LORD saved Hezekiah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem from the hand of Sennacherib king of Assyria and from the hand of all others, and guided them on every side.

**23** Many brought offerings to the LORD in Jerusalem and precious things to Hezekiah king of Judah, so that from then on he was exalted in the eyes of all the nations.

## Hezekiah's Sickness and Pride

**24** In those days Hezekiah became sick to the point of death. He prayed to the LORD, and the LORD spoke to him and gave him a sign.

**25** But Hezekiah did not repay according to the benefit done to him, for his heart was lifted up. So wrath came on him and on Judah and Jerusalem.

**26** Then Hezekiah humbled himself for the pride of his heart, both he and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so that the wrath of the LORD did not come on them in the days of Hezekiah.

**27** Hezekiah had very great riches and honor. He made himself treasuries for silver, gold, precious stones, spices, shields and all kinds of costly vessels,

**28** storehouses for the produce of grain, new wine and oil, and stalls for all kinds of cattle, and folds for the flocks.

**29** He also made cities for himself, and had flocks and herds in abundance, for God had given him very great possessions.

**30** This same Hezekiah stopped the upper outlet of the waters of Gihon and directed them down to the west side of the City of David. Hezekiah prospered in all his works.

**31** So too in the matter of the envoys of the officials of Babylon, who had been sent to him to inquire about the sign that had been done in the land, God left him, to test him, to know all that was in his heart.

**32** The rest of the acts of Hezekiah and his deeds of loyal love are written in the vision of Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz, in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

**33** Hezekiah lay down with his fathers, and they buried him on the ascent to the tombs of the sons of David. All Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem did him honor at his death. Manasseh his son reigned in his place.

## Notes

- **vv1–23 and 2 Kings 18:13–19:37** — the same invasion, told in a third of the space. Chronicles leaves out Hezekiah's payment of tribute (2 Kings 18:14–16) and gives the defense of the city (vv2–8) instead.
- **v1 "After these things and this faithfulness"** — *ve-ha-emet*, the word just said of Hezekiah, *good and right and true* (31:20). The invasion follows the faithfulness; it is not its punishment.
- **vv3–4, 30 "to stop the water of the springs… stopped the upper outlet of the waters of Gihon"** — Kings: *how he made the pool and the conduit and brought water into the city* (2 Kings 20:20).
- **vv7–8 "there is more with us than with him. With him is an arm of flesh"** — Elisha's word to his servant, *those who are with us are more than those who are with them* (2 Kings 6:16). *Zeroa basar*, **an arm of flesh**: Jeremiah curses *the man who trusts in man and makes flesh his arm* (Jeremiah 17:5). *The people relied*, *va-yissamkhu*, **leaned on**, the words of their king.
- **v12 "You shall bow down before one altar"** — the Assyrian's argument in Kings (2 Kings 18:22): he takes the reform for an insult to the LORD.
- **v18 "in the language of Judah"** — *Yehudit*, the language the king's men asked the Assyrians not to use *in the hearing of the people who are on the wall* (2 Kings 18:26).
- **v21 "The LORD sent a messenger, who cut off every mighty man… some of his own offspring struck him down"** — Kings gives the number, *a hundred and eighty-five thousand*, and the names of the sons, *Adrammelech and Sharezer* (2 Kings 19:35, 37). *Mi-tsi'ei me'av*, **those who came out of his own body**: the god who could not save the nations did not save the king in his own house.
- **vv24–26 "his heart was lifted up… Hezekiah humbled himself"** — *gavah libbo*, the pride of Uzziah (26:16), and *va-yikkana*, the humbling of 7:14. Kings tells the sickness and the sign of the shadow at length (2 Kings 20:1–11).
- **v31 "God left him, to test him, to know all that was in his heart"** — the words of the wilderness, *to humble you, to test you, to know what was in your heart* (Deuteronomy 8:2). Kings tells of the envoys and Isaiah's word that all would be carried to Babylon (2 Kings 20:12–19); Chronicles names only the test.
- **v32 "his deeds of loyal love"** — *chasadav*, the plural of *chesed*, said of Hezekiah, and of Josiah (35:26).

# 2 Chronicles 14

## Asa Seeks the LORD

**1** Abijah lay down with his fathers, and they buried him in the City of David, and Asa his son reigned in his place. In his days the land had rest for ten years.

**2** Asa did what was good and right in the eyes of the LORD his God.

**3** He removed the foreign altars and the high places, broke down the pillars and cut down the Asherim.

**4** He told Judah to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, and to do the instruction and the commandment.

**5** He removed the high places and the incense altars from all the cities of Judah, and the kingdom had rest under him.

**6** He built fortified cities in Judah, for the land had rest. There was no war against him in those years, for the LORD had given him rest.

**7** He said to Judah, "Let us build these cities and surround them with walls and towers, gates and bars. The land is still ours, because we have sought the LORD our God. We have sought Him, and He has given us rest on every side." So they built and prospered.

**8** Asa had an army that carried shield and spear: from Judah three hundred thousand, and from Benjamin two hundred and eighty thousand who carried shields and drew the bow. All these were mighty men of valor.

## The Battle at Mareshah

**9** Zerah the Cushite came out against them with an army of a million men and three hundred chariots, and came as far as Mareshah.

**10** Asa went out to meet him, and they drew up for battle in the Valley of Zephathah at Mareshah.

**11** Asa called to the LORD his God and said, "O LORD, there is none besides You to help, between the mighty and those who have no strength. Help us, O LORD our God, for we rely on You, and in Your name we have come against this multitude. O LORD, You are our God; do not let man prevail against You."

**12** So the LORD struck down the Cushites before Asa and before Judah, and the Cushites fled.

**13** Asa and the people with him pursued them as far as Gerar. So many Cushites fell that none of them was left alive, for they were shattered before the LORD and before His army. Judah carried away a very great amount of spoil.

**14** They struck all the cities around Gerar, for the fear of the LORD was on them. They plundered all the cities, for there was much plunder in them.

**15** They also struck the tents of the herdsmen, and carried off sheep in abundance and camels, and returned to Jerusalem.

## Notes

- **v1 the numbering** — in the Hebrew this verse is 13:23, and vv2–15 are 14:1–14. The text follows the English numbering.
- **v1 "the land had rest for ten years"** — *shaqtah ha-arets*, the refrain of Judges, *The land had rest forty years* (Judges 3:11). It is said again in v6. Kings has no such words for Asa (1 Kings 15:9–15).
- **vv3–5 "the foreign altars and the high places… the incense altars"** — *chammanim*, the word of the curse, *I will destroy your high places and cut down your incense altars* (Leviticus 26:30). Kings says the opposite of v5: *the high places were not taken away* (1 Kings 15:14), and Chronicles itself says in 15:17 that they were not removed *from Israel*.
- **v7 "because we have sought the LORD… He has given us rest on every side"** — *darashnu… va-yanach lanu*. The rest promised for Solomon's days, *I will give him rest from all his enemies around him* (1 Chronicles 22:9), comes to the king who seeks.
- **v9 "Zerah the Cushite… a million men"** — not in Kings. *Cush* is the land south of Egypt; Shishak's army had *Cushites* in it too (12:3).
- **v11 "between the mighty and those who have no strength… we rely on You"** — *bein rav le-ein koach*: for the LORD, the size of the army makes no difference. *Nish'annu*, **we rely**, the word of Abijah's victory (13:18), and the word on which Asa will fail (16:7).
- **v11 "do not let man prevail against You"** — *enosh*, **mortal man**. The battle is set as one between the LORD and a man, not between two armies.
- **v14 "the fear of the LORD was on them"** — *pachad YHWH*. It falls on the cities of Gerar here, on the kingdoms around Judah in Jehoshaphat's day (17:10), and Jehoshaphat asks it of his judges (19:7).

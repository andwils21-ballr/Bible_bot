# 2 Chronicles 22

## Ahaziah King of Judah

**1** The inhabitants of Jerusalem made Ahaziah his youngest son king in his place, for the band of raiders that came with the Arabians to the camp had killed all the older sons. So Ahaziah son of Jehoram king of Judah reigned.

**2** Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Athaliah the daughter of Omri.

**3** He too walked in the ways of the house of Ahab, for his mother was his counselor in doing wickedly.

**4** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, as the house of Ahab had done, for after his father's death they were his counselors, to his destruction.

**5** He also followed their counsel and went with Jehoram son of Ahab king of Israel to war against Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead, and the Arameans wounded Joram.

**6** He returned to be healed in Jezreel of the wounds that they had given him at Ramah, when he fought against Hazael king of Aram. Ahaziah son of Jehoram king of Judah went down to see Jehoram son of Ahab in Jezreel, because he was sick.

## The Death of Ahaziah

**7** But the downfall of Ahaziah came from God, through his going to Joram. When he arrived, he went out with Jehoram against Jehu son of Nimshi, whom the LORD had anointed to cut off the house of Ahab.

**8** And it came to pass, when Jehu was executing judgment on the house of Ahab, that he found the officials of Judah and the sons of Ahaziah's brothers, who were serving Ahaziah, and he killed them.

**9** He searched for Ahaziah, and they captured him while he was hiding in Samaria, brought him to Jehu and put him to death. They buried him, for they said, "He is the son of Jehoshaphat, who sought the LORD with all his heart." So the house of Ahaziah had no one able to hold the kingdom.

## Athaliah and Joash

**10** When Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she arose and destroyed all the royal seed of the house of Judah.

**11** But Jehoshabeath, the king's daughter, took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him away from among the king's sons who were being put to death, and put him and his nurse in the bedroom. So Jehoshabeath, the daughter of King Jehoram, the wife of Jehoiada the priest, because she was Ahaziah's sister, hid him from Athaliah, so that she did not put him to death.

**12** He was with them in the house of God, hidden for six years, while Athaliah reigned over the land.

## Notes

- **vv1–6 and 2 Kings 8:24–29; vv10–12 and 2 Kings 11:1–3** — the same accounts; the English follows Kings where the Hebrew is the same.
- **v1 "the band of raiders that came with the Arabians"** — the raid of 21:16–17, which left Jehoram only his youngest son.
- **v2 "twenty-two years old"** — the Hebrew has *forty-two*, but his father died at forty (21:20), so the son cannot have been older. Kings has *twenty-two* (2 Kings 8:26); the Greek of Chronicles has *twenty*. The text follows Kings.
- **v2 "Athaliah the daughter of Omri"** — *bat Omri*: Omri was Ahab's father, so *daughter* here means a woman of his house; she is called *the daughter of Ahab* in 21:6.
- **v6 "Ahaziah son of Jehoram"** — the Hebrew has *Azariah son of Jehoram*, against vv1, 2 and 7. The Greek has *Ochozias*, **Ahaziah**, as Kings does (2 Kings 8:29). The text follows the Greek.
- **v7 "the downfall of Ahaziah came from God"** — *tevusah*, a word found only here, from *bus*, **to trample**. Kings tells the visit without saying whose hand was in it.
- **v9 "they captured him while he was hiding in Samaria… They buried him"** — Kings tells it differently: Ahaziah fled, was struck *at the ascent of Gur*, *fled to Megiddo and died there*, and his servants carried him to Jerusalem to be buried with his fathers (2 Kings 9:27–28). In Chronicles Jehu's men bury him, out of respect for his grandfather, *who sought the LORD with all his heart*. The text keeps the Hebrew of each.
- **v11 "Jehoshabeath… the wife of Jehoiada the priest"** — Kings calls her *Jehosheba* and does not say she was the priest's wife (2 Kings 11:2). Chronicles gives the reason the child could be hidden *in the house of God* for six years.

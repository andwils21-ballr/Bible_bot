# 2 Chronicles 8

## Solomon's Building

**1** And it came to pass at the end of twenty years, in which Solomon had built the house of the LORD and his own house,

**2** that Solomon rebuilt the cities that Hiram had given him, and settled the sons of Israel there.

**3** Solomon went to Hamath-zobah and took it.

**4** He built Tadmor in the wilderness, and all the store cities that he built in Hamath.

**5** He built Upper Beth-horon and Lower Beth-horon, fortified cities with walls, gates and bars,

**6** and Baalath, and all the store cities that Solomon had, all the cities for his chariots and the cities for his horsemen, and whatever Solomon desired to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon and in all the land of his dominion.

## The Forced Labor

**7** All the people who were left of the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites and the Jebusites, who were not of Israel —

**8** their sons who were left after them in the land, whom the sons of Israel had not destroyed — these Solomon raised as forced labor, to this day.

**9** But of the sons of Israel Solomon made no slaves for his work. They were men of war, commanders of his officers, and commanders of his chariots and his horsemen.

**10** These were the chief officers of King Solomon, two hundred and fifty, who had charge of the people.

**11** Solomon brought Pharaoh's daughter up from the City of David to the house he had built for her, for he said, "No wife of mine shall live in the house of David king of Israel, for the places to which the ark of the LORD has come are holy."

## The Service of the House

**12** Then Solomon offered burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of the LORD that he had built before the porch,

**13** as the duty of each day required, offering according to the commandment of Moses on the sabbaths, the new moons and the appointed feasts three times a year: at the Festival of Unleavened Bread, the Festival of Weeks and the Festival of Booths.

**14** According to the rule of David his father, he appointed the divisions of the priests for their service, and the Levites for their duties, to praise and to minister before the priests as the duty of each day required, and the gatekeepers by their divisions at each gate, for so David the man of God had commanded.

**15** They did not turn aside from the king's command to the priests and the Levites in any matter, or concerning the treasuries.

**16** So all the work of Solomon was carried out, from the day the foundation of the house of the LORD was laid until it was finished. The house of the LORD was complete.

## Ships to Ophir

**17** Then Solomon went to Ezion-geber and to Eloth, on the shore of the sea in the land of Edom.

**18** Hiram sent him ships by his servants, and servants who knew the sea, and they went with Solomon's servants to Ophir. They took from there four hundred and fifty talents of gold and brought it to King Solomon.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "the cities that Hiram had given him"** — Kings tells it the other way: *King Solomon gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee*, and Hiram did not like them (1 Kings 9:11–13). In Chronicles the cities come from Hiram to Solomon, who settles Israelites in them. The Greek agrees with the Hebrew of Chronicles. The text keeps the Hebrew of each.
- **v3 "Hamath-zobah"** — Solomon's only campaign in either book. David had struck *Hadadezer king of Zobah, toward Hamath* (1 Chronicles 18:3).
- **vv7–9 the forced labor** — as in 1 Kings 9:20–22. The peoples not destroyed in the conquest work for the king; Israelites serve as soldiers and officers. Kings also says that *King Solomon raised forced labor from all Israel*, thirty thousand men (1 Kings 5:13).
- **v10 "two hundred and fifty"** — Kings has *five hundred and fifty* (1 Kings 9:23). The Greek of Chronicles also has two hundred and fifty.
- **v11 "the places to which the ark of the LORD has come are holy"** — the only time Chronicles mentions Pharaoh's daughter, and the reason for moving her is its own: a foreign wife may not live where the ark has been. Kings says only that she went up to her house (1 Kings 9:24), and later, that *King Solomon loved many foreign women, besides the daughter of Pharaoh* (1 Kings 11:1).
- **v13 "three times a year: at the Festival of Unleavened Bread, the Festival of Weeks and the Festival of Booths"** — the three feasts of Deuteronomy 16:16. Kings says *three times a year* and does not name them (1 Kings 9:25).
- **v14 "David the man of God"** — the title of Moses (Deuteronomy 33:1; 1 Chronicles 23:14), given to David here and again only in Nehemiah 12:24 and 36, both times for the order of the singers.
- **v18 "Hiram sent him ships… four hundred and fifty talents"** — Kings has Solomon build the fleet and Hiram send seamen, and *four hundred and twenty talents* (1 Kings 9:26–28).

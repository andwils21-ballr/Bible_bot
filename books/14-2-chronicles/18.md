# 2 Chronicles 18

## The Alliance with Ahab

**1** Jehoshaphat had riches and honor in abundance, and he made a marriage alliance with Ahab.

**2** After some years he went down to Ahab in Samaria. Ahab slaughtered sheep and oxen in abundance for him and for the people who were with him, and he enticed him to go up against Ramoth-gilead.

**3** Ahab king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, "Will you go with me to Ramoth-gilead?" He said to him, "I am as you are, and my people as your people; we will be with you in the battle."

**4** But Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "Inquire first for the word of the LORD."

**5** Then the king of Israel gathered the prophets, four hundred men, and said to them, "Shall we go to battle against Ramoth-gilead, or shall I refrain?" They said, "Go up, for God will give it into the hand of the king."

## Micaiah Son of Imlah

**6** But Jehoshaphat said, "Is there not here another prophet of the LORD, of whom we may inquire?"

**7** The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is yet one man by whom we may inquire of the LORD, but I hate him, for he never prophesies good concerning me, but always evil. He is Micaiah son of Imla." Jehoshaphat said, "Let not the king say so."

**8** Then the king of Israel called an officer and said, "Bring Micaiah son of Imla quickly."

**9** The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah were sitting each on his throne, dressed in their robes; they were sitting at the threshing floor at the entrance of the gate of Samaria, and all the prophets were prophesying before them.

**10** Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made for himself horns of iron and said, "Thus says the LORD: 'With these you shall gore the Arameans until they are destroyed.'"

**11** All the prophets prophesied so, saying, "Go up to Ramoth-gilead and prosper; the LORD will give it into the hand of the king."

**12** The messenger who went to call Micaiah said to him, "The words of the prophets with one mouth are good for the king. Let your word be like one of theirs, and speak good."

**13** But Micaiah said, "As the LORD lives, what my God says, that I will speak."

**14** When he came to the king, the king said to him, "Micah, shall we go to battle against Ramoth-gilead, or shall I refrain?" He said, "Go up and prosper; they will be given into your hand."

**15** But the king said to him, "How many times shall I make you swear that you speak to me nothing but the truth in the name of the LORD?"

**16** So he said, "I saw all Israel scattered on the mountains, like sheep that have no shepherd. Then the LORD said, 'These have no master; let each return to his home in peace.'"

**17** The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Did I not tell you that he would not prophesy good concerning me, but evil?"

**18** Micaiah said, "Therefore hear the word of the LORD: I saw the LORD sitting on His throne, and all the host of heaven standing on His right hand and on His left.

**19** The LORD said, 'Who will entice Ahab king of Israel, so that he may go up and fall at Ramoth-gilead?' One said one thing, and another said another.

**20** Then a spirit came forward and stood before the LORD and said, 'I will entice him.' The LORD said to him, 'By what means?'

**21** He said, 'I will go out and be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.' He said, 'You shall entice him, and you shall succeed. Go out and do so.'

**22** So now the LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouth of these your prophets, and the LORD has spoken evil concerning you."

**23** Then Zedekiah son of Chenaanah came near and struck Micaiah on the cheek and said, "Which way did the Spirit of the LORD pass from me to speak to you?"

**24** Micaiah said, "You shall see on that day, when you go into an inner room to hide yourself."

**25** The king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah and bring him back to Amon the governor of the city and to Joash the king's son,

**26** and say, 'Thus says the king: Put this man in prison, and feed him with a scant ration of bread and water until I return in peace.'"

**27** Micaiah said, "If you return in peace at all, the LORD has not spoken by me." Then he said, "Hear, all you peoples!"

## The Battle at Ramoth-gilead

**28** So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah went up to Ramoth-gilead.

**29** The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will disguise myself and go into battle, but you wear your robes." So the king of Israel disguised himself, and they went into battle.

**30** Now the king of Aram had commanded the captains of his chariots, "Fight with neither small nor great, but only with the king of Israel."

**31** And it came to pass, when the captains of the chariots saw Jehoshaphat, that they said, "It is the king of Israel." So they turned to fight against him, and Jehoshaphat cried out. The LORD helped him, and God drew them away from him.

**32** And it came to pass, when the captains of the chariots saw that it was not the king of Israel, that they turned back from pursuing him.

**33** But a certain man drew his bow at random and struck the king of Israel between the joints of his armor. So he said to the driver of his chariot, "Turn your hand and carry me out of the battle, for I am wounded."

**34** The battle grew hot that day, and the king of Israel propped himself up in his chariot facing the Arameans until evening. He died as the sun went down.

## Notes

- **vv1–34 and 1 Kings 22:1–35** — the same account; the English follows 1 Kings where the Hebrew is the same. Chronicles tells it as part of Jehoshaphat's story and ends with Ahab's death, without the dogs and his burial (1 Kings 22:36–40).
- **vv1–2 "he made a marriage alliance with Ahab… and he enticed him"** — *va-yitchatten*, the marriage of Jehoshaphat's son Jehoram to Ahab's daughter (21:6). *Va-yesitehu*, **enticed him**, is the verb of 1 Chronicles 21:1, where Satan *incited David*. The feast at Samaria is how Jehoshaphat is drawn in. Not in Kings.
- **v10 "horns of iron… With these you shall gore the Arameans"** — the picture of Moses' blessing on Joseph, *With them he gores the peoples* (Deuteronomy 33:17), acted out.
- **v16 "like sheep that have no shepherd"** — Moses' words when he asked for a leader (Numbers 27:17). The king dies at sunset (v34).
- **vv18–22 "a spirit came forward… a lying spirit"** — *ha-ruach*, **the spirit**, one of the host of v18, not the Spirit of the LORD; lowercase, as in 1 Kings 22:21. Zedekiah answers with *the Spirit of the LORD* (v23).
- **v27 "Hear, all you peoples!"** — *shim'u ammim kullam*, the first words of the book of Micah (Micah 1:2), whose name is a short form of *Micaiah*. The king calls him *Micah* in v14.
- **v31 "The LORD helped him, and God drew them away from him"** — not in Kings, where *Jehoshaphat cried out* and the captains simply see their mistake (1 Kings 22:32–33). *Va-yesitem*, **drew them away**, is the verb of v2: Ahab enticed Jehoshaphat into the battle, and God enticed the enemy away from him.
- **v34 "propped himself up… until evening. He died as the sun went down"** — Kings has *the blood of the wound ran into the bottom of the chariot* (1 Kings 22:35); Chronicles leaves it out.

# 2 Chronicles 17

## Jehoshaphat Strengthened

**1** Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his place and strengthened himself against Israel.

**2** He placed troops in all the fortified cities of Judah, and set garrisons in the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that Asa his father had taken.

**3** The LORD was with Jehoshaphat, because he walked in the earlier ways of David his father and did not seek the Baals,

**4** but sought the God of his father and walked in His commandments, and not as Israel did.

**5** So the LORD established the kingdom in his hand. All Judah brought tribute to Jehoshaphat, and he had riches and honor in abundance.

**6** His heart was lifted up in the ways of the LORD, and he again removed the high places and the Asherim from Judah.

## Teachers in the Cities

**7** In the third year of his reign he sent his officials, Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel and Micaiah, to teach in the cities of Judah.

**8** With them were the Levites Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah and Tob-adonijah, the Levites, and with them the priests Elishama and Jehoram.

**9** They taught in Judah, having the book of the instruction of the LORD with them. They went around through all the cities of Judah and taught among the people.

## Tribute and Troops

**10** The fear of the LORD fell on all the kingdoms of the lands around Judah, and they did not make war against Jehoshaphat.

**11** Some of the Philistines brought Jehoshaphat gifts and silver as tribute, and the Arabians also brought him flocks: seven thousand seven hundred rams and seven thousand seven hundred goats.

**12** Jehoshaphat grew steadily greater. He built fortresses and store cities in Judah,

**13** and he had much work in the cities of Judah, and soldiers, mighty men of valor, in Jerusalem.

**14** This was their muster by their fathers' houses. Of Judah, the commanders of thousands: Adnah the commander, and with him three hundred thousand mighty men of valor;

**15** next to him Jehohanan the commander, and with him two hundred and eighty thousand;

**16** and next to him Amasiah son of Zichri, who offered himself willingly to the LORD, and with him two hundred thousand mighty men of valor.

**17** Of Benjamin: Eliada, a mighty man of valor, and with him two hundred thousand armed with bow and shield;

**18** and next to him Jehozabad, and with him a hundred and eighty thousand equipped for war.

**19** These were the men who served the king, besides those the king had placed in the fortified cities throughout all Judah.

## Notes

- **vv1–19** — not in Kings, which gives Jehoshaphat's reign a summary of ten verses (1 Kings 22:41–50) and tells him mostly beside Ahab.
- **v3 "the earlier ways of David his father"** — *darkhei David aviv ha-rishonim*. The Greek has *the ways of his father, the earlier ones*, without *David*, and then the *father* is Asa, whose earlier years were faithful and his last were not (16:7–12). The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **v6 "His heart was lifted up in the ways of the LORD"** — *va-yigbah libbo*. Everywhere else in the book the phrase is pride: Uzziah's *heart was lifted up, to his destruction* (26:16), and Hezekiah's (32:25). Here alone it is good: his heart was raised high in the LORD's ways.
- **v6 "he again removed the high places"** — 20:33 says *the high places were not removed*, as Kings does (1 Kings 22:43). The text keeps both.
- **vv7–9 "having the book of the instruction of the LORD with them"** — *sefer torat YHWH*, carried from town to town by officials, Levites and priests together. The blessing of Levi: *They teach Your rules to Jacob* (Deuteronomy 33:10). The same phrase names the book found in Josiah's day (34:14).
- **v10 "The fear of the LORD fell on all the kingdoms"** — *pachad YHWH*, as on the cities around Gerar in Asa's war (14:14). Here it comes without a battle.
- **v16 "who offered himself willingly to the LORD"** — *ha-mitnaddev*, the word of Deborah's song, *when the people offer themselves willingly* (Judges 5:2, 9), and of the gifts for the house (1 Chronicles 29:5–9).

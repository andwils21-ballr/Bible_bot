# 2 Chronicles 23

## The Covenant in the House of God

**1** In the seventh year Jehoiada took courage and made a covenant with the commanders of the hundreds: Azariah son of Jeroham, Ishmael son of Jehohanan, Azariah son of Obed, Maaseiah son of Adaiah and Elishaphat son of Zichri.

**2** They went around through Judah and gathered the Levites from all the cities of Judah and the heads of the fathers' houses of Israel, and they came to Jerusalem.

**3** All the assembly made a covenant with the king in the house of God. Jehoiada said to them, "The king's son shall reign, as the LORD spoke concerning the sons of David.

**4** This is what you shall do: a third of you, priests and Levites who come on duty on the Sabbath, shall be gatekeepers at the thresholds;

**5** a third shall be at the king's house, and a third at the Gate of the Foundation; and all the people shall be in the courts of the house of the LORD.

**6** Let no one enter the house of the LORD except the priests and the Levites who minister. They may enter, for they are holy, but all the people shall keep the charge of the LORD.

**7** The Levites shall surround the king on every side, each man with his weapons in his hand, and whoever enters the house shall be put to death. Be with the king when he comes in and when he goes out."

**8** The Levites and all Judah did according to all that Jehoiada the priest commanded. Each took his men, those coming on duty on the Sabbath with those going off duty on the Sabbath, for Jehoiada the priest did not release the divisions.

**9** Jehoiada the priest gave the commanders of the hundreds the spears and the large and small shields that had been King David's, which were in the house of God.

**10** He stationed all the people, each man with his weapon in his hand, from the right side of the house to the left side of the house, by the altar and the house, around the king.

## Joash Crowned, Athaliah Killed

**11** Then they brought out the king's son and put the crown and the testimony on him, and made him king. Jehoiada and his sons anointed him, and they said, "Long live the king!"

**12** When Athaliah heard the noise of the people running and praising the king, she came to the people in the house of the LORD.

**13** She looked, and there was the king standing by his pillar at the entrance, with the commanders and the trumpeters beside the king, and all the people of the land rejoicing and blowing trumpets, and the singers with instruments of song leading the praise. Athaliah tore her clothes and cried, "Conspiracy! Conspiracy!"

**14** Then Jehoiada the priest brought out the commanders of the hundreds who were set over the army, and said to them, "Bring her out between the ranks, and put to death with the sword anyone who follows her." For the priest had said, "Do not put her to death in the house of the LORD."

**15** So they laid hands on her, and she went into the entrance of the Horse Gate of the king's house, and there they put her to death.

## The Covenant Renewed

**16** Jehoiada made a covenant between himself and all the people and the king, that they should be the LORD's people.

**17** All the people went to the house of Baal and tore it down. They smashed its altars and its images, and they killed Mattan the priest of Baal before the altars.

**18** Jehoiada placed the oversight of the house of the LORD in the hands of the Levitical priests whom David had organized over the house of the LORD, to offer the burnt offerings of the LORD, as it is written in the instruction of Moses, with rejoicing and with song, as David had directed.

**19** He stationed the gatekeepers at the gates of the house of the LORD, so that no one unclean in anything should enter.

**20** He took the commanders of the hundreds, the nobles, the rulers of the people and all the people of the land, and they brought the king down from the house of the LORD. They came through the Upper Gate to the king's house and set the king on the throne of the kingdom.

**21** All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet. They had put Athaliah to death with the sword.

## Notes

- **vv1–21 and 2 Kings 11:4–20** — the same account; the English follows Kings where the Hebrew is the same. Chronicles names the five commanders (v1), which Kings does not.
- **vv2, 4–8 the Levites** — in Kings the guard is *the Carites and the guard* (2 Kings 11:4, 19), the royal bodyguard, and they stand in the house of the LORD. In Chronicles the Levites take their place and the house is kept for those who may enter it: *Let no one enter the house of the LORD except the priests and the Levites… for they are holy* (v6).
- **v3 "as the LORD spoke concerning the sons of David"** — the promise of 1 Chronicles 17:11–14. With only one son of David left alive, the covenant is made to keep the promise.
- **v11 "the crown and the testimony"** — *ha-nezer ve-ha-edut*. The *testimony* is the word for the tablets of the covenant (Exodus 25:16); here something put on the king or given into his hand, perhaps the law of the king, *he shall write for himself a copy of this instruction* (Deuteronomy 17:18). The Hebrew does not say what it was.
- **v13 "the singers with instruments of song leading the praise"** — not in Kings. Chronicles sets the Levite singers in the crowd at the crowning.
- **v16 "a covenant between himself and all the people and the king"** — Kings has *between the LORD and the king and the people* (2 Kings 11:17). Chronicles has the priest himself stand as a party to the covenant.
- **vv18–19 "as it is written in the instruction of Moses… as David had directed… no one unclean"** — not in Kings. The order of the house is set back as Moses wrote it and David arranged it, and gatekeepers are posted against the unclean.

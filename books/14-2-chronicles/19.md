# 2 Chronicles 19

## Jehu Son of Hanani

**1** Jehoshaphat king of Judah returned in peace to his house in Jerusalem.

**2** Jehu son of Hanani the seer went out to meet him and said to King Jehoshaphat, "Should you help the wicked and love those who hate the LORD? Because of this, wrath has gone out against you from the LORD.

**3** Yet good things are found in you, for you have removed the Asherahs from the land and have set your heart to seek God."

## Judges in the Land

**4** Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem. He went out again among the people, from Beersheba to the hill country of Ephraim, and brought them back to the LORD, the God of their fathers.

**5** He appointed judges in the land, in all the fortified cities of Judah, city by city.

**6** He said to the judges, "Consider what you are doing, for you do not judge for man but for the LORD, and He is with you when you give judgment.

**7** Now let the fear of the LORD be on you. Take care what you do, for with the LORD our God there is no injustice, no partiality and no taking of bribes."

## The Court in Jerusalem

**8** In Jerusalem also Jehoshaphat appointed some of the Levites and the priests and the heads of the fathers' houses of Israel, to give judgment for the LORD and to decide disputes. They lived in Jerusalem.

**9** He charged them, saying, "This is how you shall act: in the fear of the LORD, faithfully and with a whole heart.

**10** Whenever a case comes to you from your brothers who live in their cities, between blood and blood, between instruction and commandment, statutes and rules, you shall warn them, so that they do not incur guilt before the LORD and wrath come on you and your brothers. Do this, and you will not incur guilt.

**11** Amariah the chief priest is over you in every matter of the LORD, and Zebadiah son of Ishmael, the leader of the house of Judah, in every matter of the king. The Levites will serve you as officials. Be strong and act, and may the LORD be with the good."

## Notes

- **vv1–11** — not in Kings. The king's name, *Yehoshafat*, is **the LORD judges**, from *shafat*, the verb of this chapter: *judges*, *shoftim* (v5), *you do not judge* (v6).
- **v2 "Jehu son of Hanani the seer"** — the son of the seer Asa put in the stocks for rebuking him (16:7–10). *Should you help the wicked and love those who hate the LORD?*: the alliance with Ahab of chapter 18.
- **v3 "good things are found in you"** — *devarim tovim*, the words said of Judah under Rehoboam (12:12). The wrath of v2 is not withdrawn; it is set beside them.
- **v4 "from Beersheba to the hill country of Ephraim"** — the old saying for the whole land is *from Dan to Beersheba*; the kingdom of Judah now reaches only to Ephraim.
- **vv5–7 "judges… in all the fortified cities… no injustice, no partiality and no taking of bribes"** — the law of Deuteronomy: *Judges and officials you shall appoint for yourself in all your towns* (Deuteronomy 16:18); *the judgment is God's* (1:17); the LORD *shows no partiality and takes no bribe* (10:17).
- **vv8–10 "between blood and blood"** — *bein dam le-dam*, the words of the law of the central court: *If a case is too hard for you in judgment — between blood and blood, between claim and claim… then you shall… go up to the place that the LORD your God will choose* (Deuteronomy 17:8). Jehoshaphat sets up that court in Jerusalem.
- **v11 "in every matter of the LORD… in every matter of the king"** — the division David made beyond the Jordan, *for every matter of God and every matter of the king* (1 Chronicles 26:32): a priest over the one, a leader of Judah over the other.

# 2 Chronicles 12

## Shishak's Invasion

**1** And it came to pass, when the kingdom of Rehoboam was established and he was strong, that he forsook the instruction of the LORD, and all Israel with him.

**2** And it came to pass in the fifth year of King Rehoboam that Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem, because they had broken faith with the LORD,

**3** with twelve hundred chariots and sixty thousand horsemen. The people who came with him from Egypt were without number: Libyans, Sukkiim and Cushites.

**4** He took the fortified cities of Judah and came as far as Jerusalem.

**5** Then Shemaiah the prophet came to Rehoboam and to the officials of Judah, who had gathered at Jerusalem because of Shishak, and said to them, "This is what the LORD says: You have forsaken Me, so I have forsaken you into the hand of Shishak."

**6** The officials of Israel and the king humbled themselves and said, "The LORD is righteous."

**7** When the LORD saw that they had humbled themselves, the word of the LORD came to Shemaiah, saying, "They have humbled themselves. I will not destroy them, but I will grant them some deliverance, and My wrath shall not be poured out on Jerusalem by the hand of Shishak.

**8** But they shall be his servants, so that they may know My service and the service of the kingdoms of the lands."

**9** So Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem. He took away the treasures of the house of the LORD and the treasures of the king's house; he took away everything. He also took away the shields of gold that Solomon had made.

**10** King Rehoboam made shields of bronze in their place, and committed them to the hands of the officers of the guard, who kept the door of the king's house.

**11** As often as the king went into the house of the LORD, the guard came and carried them, and brought them back to the guardroom.

**12** When he humbled himself, the anger of the LORD turned away from him, so as not to destroy him completely. In Judah, too, there were good things.

## The End of Rehoboam's Reign

**13** So King Rehoboam strengthened himself in Jerusalem and reigned. Rehoboam was forty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city that the LORD had chosen out of all the tribes of Israel to put His name there. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite.

**14** He did evil, for he did not set his heart to seek the LORD.

**15** The acts of Rehoboam, from first to last, are they not written in the words of Shemaiah the prophet and of Iddo the seer, by genealogy? There were wars between Rehoboam and Jeroboam continually.

**16** Rehoboam lay down with his fathers and was buried in the City of David, and Abijah his son reigned in his place.

## Notes

- **vv6–7, 12 "humbled themselves"** — *va-yikkane'u*, the first time the word of 7:14 is kept: *if My people… humble themselves… I will hear*. The LORD answers with a measure, *some deliverance*, *ki-me'at li-fleitah*.
- **v15 "by genealogy"** — *le-hityachesh*, the word for enrolling by descent (1 Chronicles 5:17; 9:1). Why it stands here after the names of two prophets is not clear.

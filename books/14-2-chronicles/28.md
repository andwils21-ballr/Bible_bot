# 2 Chronicles 28

## Ahaz King of Judah

**1** Ahaz was twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. He did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD, as David his father had done.

**2** He walked in the ways of the kings of Israel, and even made cast images for the Baals.

**3** He burned incense in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom and burned his sons in the fire, according to the detestable practices of the nations whom the LORD had driven out before the sons of Israel.

**4** He sacrificed and burned incense on the high places, on the hills and under every leafy tree.

## Defeat by Aram and Israel

**5** So the LORD his God gave him into the hand of the king of Aram, who struck him and carried away a great number of captives and brought them to Damascus. He was also given into the hand of the king of Israel, who struck him with a great slaughter.

**6** Pekah son of Remaliah killed a hundred and twenty thousand in Judah in one day, all of them men of valor, because they had forsaken the LORD, the God of their fathers.

**7** Zichri, a mighty man of Ephraim, killed Maaseiah the king's son, Azrikam the governor of the house and Elkanah, who was second to the king.

**8** The sons of Israel took captive from their kinsmen two hundred thousand women, sons and daughters, and also took much spoil from them and brought the spoil to Samaria.

## Oded and the Leaders of Ephraim

**9** A prophet of the LORD was there, whose name was Oded. He went out to meet the army that was coming to Samaria and said to them, "Because the LORD, the God of your fathers, was angry with Judah, He gave them into your hand; but you have killed them in a rage that has reached up to heaven.

**10** Now you intend to subjugate the sons of Judah and Jerusalem as your male and female slaves. But are there not guilty deeds of your own against the LORD your God?

**11** So now hear me, and send back the captives you have taken from your kinsmen, for the burning anger of the LORD is on you."

**12** Then some of the heads of the sons of Ephraim, Azariah son of Johanan, Berechiah son of Meshillemoth, Jehizkiah son of Shallum and Amasa son of Hadlai, stood up against those who were coming from the war,

**13** and said to them, "You shall not bring the captives in here, for you intend to bring on us guilt against the LORD in addition to our sins and our guilt. Our guilt is already great, and there is burning anger against Israel."

**14** So the armed men left the captives and the spoil before the officials and all the assembly.

**15** The men who had been named rose up and took the captives. From the spoil they clothed all who were naked among them. They clothed them, gave them sandals, fed them and gave them drink, and anointed them; they carried all the feeble on donkeys and brought them to Jericho, the city of palm trees, to their kinsmen. Then they returned to Samaria.

## Ahaz Seeks Help from Assyria

**16** At that time King Ahaz sent to the kings of Assyria for help.

**17** The Edomites had come again and struck Judah and carried away captives,

**18** and the Philistines had raided the cities of the lowland and the Negev of Judah, and had taken Beth-shemesh, Aijalon and Gederoth, Soco with its villages, Timnah with its villages and Gimzo with its villages, and they lived there.

**19** For the LORD brought Judah low because of Ahaz king of Israel, for he had let Judah loose and had broken faith with the LORD.

**20** Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria came against him and distressed him, and did not strengthen him.

**21** For Ahaz took a portion from the house of the LORD, from the house of the king and from the officials, and gave it to the king of Assyria, but it did not help him.

**22** In the time of his distress he broke faith with the LORD still more. This was King Ahaz.

**23** He sacrificed to the gods of Damascus, which had struck him, and said, "Because the gods of the kings of Aram help them, I will sacrifice to them, so that they may help me." But they were the ruin of him and of all Israel.

**24** Ahaz gathered the vessels of the house of God and cut the vessels of the house of God in pieces. He shut the doors of the house of the LORD, and he made himself altars in every corner of Jerusalem.

**25** In every city of Judah he made high places to burn incense to other gods, and he provoked the LORD, the God of his fathers, to anger.

**26** The rest of his acts and all his ways, from first to last, are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

**27** Ahaz lay down with his fathers, and they buried him in the city, in Jerusalem, for they did not bring him into the tombs of the kings of Israel. Hezekiah his son reigned in his place.

## Notes

- **vv1–4, 16–27 and 2 Kings 16** — the same reign, told with other events. Kings tells of the altar copied from Damascus (2 Kings 16:10–16); Chronicles tells of the war with Israel and the captives (vv5–15), and of Ahaz shutting the doors of the house (v24).
- **v3 "burned his sons in the fire"** — *va-yav'er et banav ba-esh*. Kings has *made his son pass through the fire* (2 Kings 16:3). *Detestable practices*, *to'avot*.
- **vv5–8 "a hundred and twenty thousand… two hundred thousand women, sons and daughters"** — not in Kings, which says only that Rezin and Pekah *besieged Ahaz but could not overcome him* (2 Kings 16:5).
- **v9 "a rage that has reached up to heaven"** — *be-za'af ad la-shamayim higgia*: Israel was the LORD's instrument, and went beyond what it was given to do.
- **vv12–15 "they clothed them… fed them… anointed them… carried all the feeble on donkeys… to Jericho"** — men of Samaria, who were not of Judah, take their enemies to Jericho with clothing, food, oil and donkeys. Jesus tells of a man going *down from Jerusalem to Jericho* who was stripped and left half dead, and of *a Samaritan* who *bound up his wounds, pouring on oil and wine, and set him on his own animal* (Luke 10:30–34).
- **vv16, 20–23 "for help… did not strengthen him… it did not help him… so that they may help me"** — *la'zor*, **to help**, four times: Ahaz seeks help from Assyria and from the gods of Damascus. *They were the ruin of him*, *le-hakhshilo*, **to make him stumble**, the word of 25:8, *God has power to help and to make fall*.
- **v19 "he had let Judah loose"** — *hifria*, the word of Exodus 32:25, *the people… were let loose, for Aaron had let them loose*, at the golden calf. *Ahaz king of Israel*: Chronicles uses *Israel* for Judah's king, as in 21:2.
- **v24 "He shut the doors of the house of the LORD"** — the first act of his son: *In the first year of his reign, in the first month, he opened the doors of the house of the LORD* (29:3).
- **v27 "they did not bring him into the tombs of the kings"** — Kings says he *was buried with his fathers in the City of David* (2 Kings 16:20). Chronicles keeps him out, as it kept Jehoram (21:20) and Joash (24:25); Uzziah lay in *the burial field that belonged to the kings* (26:23).

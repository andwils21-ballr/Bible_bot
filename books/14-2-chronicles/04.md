# 2 Chronicles 4

## The Altar, the Sea and the Basins

**1** He made an altar of bronze, twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide and ten cubits high.

**2** He made the sea of cast metal. It was round, ten cubits from brim to brim and five cubits high, and a line of thirty cubits measured its circumference.

**3** Under it, all around, were figures of oxen encircling it, ten to a cubit, going around the sea. The oxen were in two rows, cast with it when it was cast.

**4** It stood on twelve oxen, three facing north, three facing west, three facing south and three facing east. The sea was set on them, and all their hindquarters were toward the inside.

**5** Its thickness was a handbreadth, and its brim was made like the brim of a cup, like a lily blossom. It held three thousand baths.

**6** He made ten basins, and set five on the right and five on the left, for washing. In them they rinsed what was used for the burnt offering, and the sea was for the priests to wash in.

## The Lampstands, Tables and Courts

**7** He made ten lampstands of gold as prescribed for them, and set them in the nave, five on the right and five on the left.

**8** He made ten tables and placed them in the nave, five on the right and five on the left. He made a hundred bowls of gold.

**9** He made the court of the priests and the great court, and doors for the court, and overlaid their doors with bronze.

**10** He set the sea on the right side, to the southeast.

## Huram's Work

**11** Huram made the pots, the shovels and the bowls. So Huram finished the work that he did for King Solomon in the house of God:

**12** the two pillars, the bowls and the capitals on the tops of the pillars, and the two networks to cover the two bowls of the capitals on the tops of the pillars;

**13** the four hundred pomegranates for the two networks, two rows of pomegranates for each network, to cover the two bowls of the capitals on the pillars;

**14** the stands, and the basins on the stands;

**15** the one sea, and the twelve oxen under it;

**16** the pots, the shovels, the forks and all their utensils. Huram-abi made them for King Solomon for the house of the LORD, of burnished bronze.

**17** In the plain of the Jordan the king cast them, in the clay ground between Succoth and Zeredah.

**18** Solomon made all these utensils in great abundance, for the weight of the bronze could not be found out.

## The Gold Furnishings

**19** Solomon made all the furnishings that were in the house of God: the golden altar, the tables with the bread of the Presence on them,

**20** the lampstands and their lamps of pure gold, to burn before the inner sanctuary as prescribed;

**21** the flowers, the lamps and the tongs, of gold, of the purest gold;

**22** the snuffers, the bowls, the dishes for incense and the fire pans, of pure gold; and the entrance of the house, its inner doors to the Most Holy Place and the doors of the house to the nave, of gold.

## Notes

- **vv11, 16 "Huram… Huram-abi"** — in v11 the name is written *Chiram*, **Hiram**, and read *Churam*, **Huram**; the text follows the read form. In v16 he is *Churam aviv*, **Huram his father**, the title of 2:13.

# 2 Chronicles 1

## At Gibeon

**1** Solomon son of David established himself firmly over his kingdom, and the LORD his God was with him and made him exceedingly great.

**2** Solomon spoke to all Israel, to the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, to the judges and to every leader in all Israel, the heads of the fathers' houses.

**3** Then Solomon and all the assembly with him went to the high place at Gibeon, for the tent of meeting of God was there, which Moses the servant of the LORD had made in the wilderness.

**4** But David had brought up the ark of God from Kiriath-jearim to the place David had prepared for it, for he had pitched a tent for it in Jerusalem.

**5** The bronze altar that Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, had made was there before the dwelling of the LORD, and Solomon and the assembly sought Him there.

**6** Solomon went up there to the bronze altar before the LORD, which was at the tent of meeting, and offered a thousand burnt offerings on it.

**7** That night God appeared to Solomon and said to him, "Ask what I shall give you."

**8** Solomon said to God, "You have shown great loyal love to David my father, and have made me king in his place.

**9** Now, LORD God, let Your word to David my father be confirmed, for You have made me king over a people as many as the dust of the earth.

**10** Now give me wisdom and knowledge, so that I may go out and come in before this people, for who can judge this great people of Yours?"

**11** God said to Solomon, "Because this was in your heart, and you have not asked for riches, wealth or honor, or the life of those who hate you, and have not even asked for long life, but have asked for wisdom and knowledge for yourself, so that you may judge My people over whom I have made you king,

**12** wisdom and knowledge are granted to you. I will also give you riches, wealth and honor such as none of the kings before you had, and none after you will have."

**13** So Solomon came from the high place at Gibeon, from before the tent of meeting, to Jerusalem, and he reigned over Israel.

## Horses and Chariots

**14** Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen. He had one thousand four hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen, and he stationed them in the chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem.

**15** The king made silver and gold as common in Jerusalem as stones, and he made cedars as plentiful as the sycamore trees of the lowland.

**16** Solomon's horses were brought out of Egypt and from Kue; the king's traders bought them from Kue at a price.

**17** They brought up a chariot from Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver and a horse for a hundred and fifty, and so they brought them out by their hand for all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram.

## Notes

- **v3 "the high place at Gibeon, for the tent of meeting of God was there"** — Kings says only that Solomon went to Gibeon *for that was the great high place* (1 Kings 3:4). Chronicles gives the reason a high place was lawful: the tent Moses made and its altar stood there (1 Chronicles 16:39; 21:29), while the ark was in a tent in Jerusalem (v4).
- **v5 "The bronze altar that Bezalel… had made… sought Him there"** — the altar of Exodus 38:1, made by *Bezalel son of Uri son of Hur* (Exodus 31:2). *Va-yidreshehu* can be **sought Him**, the LORD, or **sought it**, the altar; the Greek has *it*. *Seek the LORD* is this book's word for faithfulness (1 Chronicles 22:19; 28:9).
- **v7 "That night God appeared to Solomon"** — Kings has *in a dream by night* (1 Kings 3:5); Chronicles does not mention a dream.
- **vv9–10 "as many as the dust of the earth… go out and come in before this people"** — the promise to Abraham, *I will make your seed like the dust of the earth* (Genesis 13:16), now a people to be judged. *Go out and come in before them* is what Moses asked for in a successor, *so that the congregation of the LORD may not be like sheep that have no shepherd* (Numbers 27:17).
- **v10 "wisdom and knowledge"** — *chokhmah u-madda*. In Kings Solomon asks for *a listening heart*, *lev shome'a* (1 Kings 3:9). *Madda*, **knowledge**, is found again only in Daniel 1:4, 17 and Ecclesiastes 10:20.
- **v13 "from the high place at Gibeon"** — the Hebrew has *la-bamah*, **to the high place**, which cannot stand with *from before the tent of meeting… to Jerusalem*. The Greek has *ek*, **from**. The text follows the Greek.
- **vv14–17 horses from Egypt** — the law of the king: *he shall not multiply horses for himself, nor make the people return to Egypt in order to multiply horses* (Deuteronomy 17:16). Chronicles sets the horse trade right after the gift of wisdom and riches; Kings tells it at the height of his glory (1 Kings 10:26–29).
- **v15 "silver and gold as common… as stones"** — Kings has *silver* alone (1 Kings 10:27); Chronicles adds *gold*.

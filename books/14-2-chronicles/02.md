# 2 Chronicles 2

## Solomon's Letter

**1** Solomon gave orders to build a house for the name of the LORD and a house for his kingdom.

**2** Solomon counted out seventy thousand men to carry loads, eighty thousand to cut stone in the hill country, and three thousand six hundred to oversee them.

**3** Solomon sent to Hiram king of Tyre, saying, "As you dealt with David my father, and sent him cedars to build himself a house to live in —

**4** I am about to build a house for the name of the LORD my God, to dedicate it to Him, to burn fragrant incense before Him, for the bread set out continually, and for burnt offerings morning and evening, on the sabbaths, the new moons and the appointed feasts of the LORD our God, as Israel must do forever.

**5** The house I am building will be great, for our God is greater than all gods.

**6** But who is able to build Him a house, since heaven, even the highest heaven, cannot contain Him? Who am I, that I should build Him a house, except as a place to burn incense before Him?

**7** So now send me a man skilled to work in gold, silver, bronze and iron, and in purple, crimson and blue, who knows how to engrave, to work with the skilled men who are with me in Judah and Jerusalem, whom David my father provided.

**8** Send me also cedar, cypress and algum timber from Lebanon, for I know that your servants know how to cut the timber of Lebanon. My servants will be with your servants

**9** to prepare timber for me in abundance, for the house I am building will be great and wonderful.

**10** For your servants, the woodcutters who fell the trees, I will give twenty thousand cors of crushed wheat, twenty thousand cors of barley, twenty thousand baths of wine and twenty thousand baths of oil."

## Hiram's Answer

**11** Hiram king of Tyre answered in a letter that he sent to Solomon: "Because the LORD loves His people, He has made you king over them."

**12** Hiram said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who made heaven and earth, who has given King David a wise son, endowed with insight and understanding, who will build a house for the LORD and a house for his kingdom.

**13** Now I am sending a skilled man, endowed with understanding, Huram-abi,

**14** the son of a woman of the daughters of Dan, and his father was a man of Tyre. He knows how to work in gold, silver, bronze and iron, in stone and wood, in purple, blue, fine linen and crimson, and to do every kind of engraving and carry out any design that is given him, with your skilled men and the skilled men of my lord David your father.

**15** Now let my lord send his servants the wheat and barley, the oil and wine, of which he has spoken.

**16** We will cut whatever timber you need from Lebanon and bring it to you as rafts by sea to Joppa, and you will take it up to Jerusalem."

## The Guests at Work

**17** Solomon counted all the guests who were in the land of Israel, after the census that David his father had taken of them, and they were found to be a hundred and fifty-three thousand six hundred.

**18** He made seventy thousand of them carry loads, eighty thousand cut stone in the hill country, and three thousand six hundred oversee the people at work.

## Notes

- **v1 the numbering** — in the Hebrew this verse is 1:18, and vv2–18 are 2:1–17. The text follows the English numbering.
- **v3 "As you dealt with David my father… —"** — the sentence has no second half: *as you did… [so do with me]*. The Greek leaves it unfinished too. In Kings the letter begins differently, with why David could not build (1 Kings 5:3).
- **v3 "Hiram"** — the Hebrew of Chronicles writes *Churam*, **Huram**, here and through the book; Kings writes *Chiram*, **Hiram** (1 Kings 5:1). The Greek has *Cheiram* in both books. He is the same king.

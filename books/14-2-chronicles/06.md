# 2 Chronicles 6

## Solomon Blesses the Assembly

**1** Then Solomon said,  
"The LORD has said that He would dwell in thick darkness.

**2** I have built You an exalted house,  
a place for You to dwell in forever."

**3** Then the king turned his face and blessed all the assembly of Israel, while all the assembly of Israel stood.

**4** He said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who spoke with His mouth to David my father, and with His hands has fulfilled it, saying,

**5** 'Since the day that I brought My people out of the land of Egypt, I have not chosen a city out of all the tribes of Israel in which to build a house, so that My name might be there, and I have not chosen a man to be leader over My people Israel.

**6** But I have chosen Jerusalem, that My name might be there, and I have chosen David to be over My people Israel.'

**7** It was in the heart of David my father to build a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**8** But the LORD said to David my father, 'Because it was in your heart to build a house for My name, you did well that it was in your heart.

**9** Only, you shall not build the house, but your son who shall come from your body, he shall build the house for My name.'

**10** Now the LORD has fulfilled His word that He spoke: I have risen in place of David my father, and I sit on the throne of Israel, as the LORD promised, and I have built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**11** There I have put the ark, in which is the covenant of the LORD that He made with the sons of Israel."

## The Prayer

**12** Then he stood before the altar of the LORD in the presence of all the assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands.

**13** For Solomon had made a bronze platform, five cubits long, five cubits wide and three cubits high, and had set it in the middle of the court. He stood on it, knelt on his knees in the presence of all the assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands toward the heavens,

**14** and said, "O LORD, God of Israel, there is no God like You in the heavens or on the earth, keeping covenant and loyal love with Your servants who walk before You with all their heart,

**15** who have kept with Your servant David my father what You promised him. You spoke with Your mouth, and with Your hand You have fulfilled it this day.

**16** And now, O LORD, God of Israel, keep with Your servant David my father what You promised him, saying, 'You shall not lack a man before Me to sit on the throne of Israel, if only your sons take heed to their way, to walk in My instruction as you have walked before Me.'

**17** And now, O LORD, God of Israel, let Your word be confirmed, which You spoke to Your servant David.

**18** But will God indeed dwell with man on the earth? The heavens, even the highest heavens, cannot contain You; how much less this house that I have built!

**19** Yet turn toward the prayer of Your servant and his plea, O LORD my God, to listen to the cry and the prayer that Your servant prays before You,

**20** that Your eyes may be open toward this house day and night, toward the place where You have said You would put Your name, to listen to the prayer that Your servant prays toward this place.

**21** Listen to the pleas of Your servant and of Your people Israel when they pray toward this place. Hear from the place of Your dwelling, from the heavens; hear and forgive.

**22** If a man sins against his neighbor and is made to take an oath, and comes and swears his oath before Your altar in this house,

**23** then hear from the heavens and act, and judge Your servants, repaying the guilty by bringing his conduct on his own head, and vindicating the righteous by rewarding him according to his righteousness.

**24** When Your people Israel are struck down before an enemy because they have sinned against You, and they turn back and confess Your name and pray and plead before You in this house,

**25** then hear from the heavens and forgive the sin of Your people Israel, and bring them back to the land that You gave to them and to their fathers.

**26** When the heavens are shut up and there is no rain because they have sinned against You, and they pray toward this place and confess Your name and turn from their sin because You afflict them,

**27** then hear in the heavens and forgive the sin of Your servants, Your people Israel, when You teach them the good way in which they should walk, and give rain on Your land that You have given to Your people as an inheritance.

**28** If there is famine in the land, if there is plague, scorching or mildew, locust or grasshopper, if their enemies besiege them in the land of their gates, whatever blow, whatever sickness there is,

**29** whatever prayer, whatever plea is made by any man or by all Your people Israel, each knowing his own blow and his own pain, and spreading out his hands toward this house,

**30** then hear from the heavens, the place of Your dwelling, and forgive, and give to each man according to all his ways, whose heart You know — for You, You alone, know the hearts of the sons of men —

**31** so that they may fear You and walk in Your ways all the days that they live on the face of the land that You gave to our fathers.

**32** Also concerning the foreigner, who is not of Your people Israel, but comes from a far country for the sake of Your great name and Your mighty hand and Your outstretched arm, when they come and pray toward this house,

**33** hear from the heavens, the place of Your dwelling, and do according to all that the foreigner calls to You for, so that all the peoples of the earth may know Your name and fear You, as do Your people Israel, and may know that this house that I have built is called by Your name.

**34** When Your people go out to battle against their enemies, by whatever way You send them, and they pray to You toward this city that You have chosen and the house that I have built for Your name,

**35** then hear from the heavens their prayer and their plea, and uphold their cause.

**36** When they sin against You — for there is no man who does not sin — and You are angry with them and give them to an enemy, so that their captors carry them away captive to a land far off or near,

**37** yet if they take it to heart in the land to which they have been carried captive, and turn back and plead with You in the land of their captivity, saying, 'We have sinned, we have done wrong and acted wickedly,'

**38** and if they turn back to You with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their captivity, to which they were carried captive, and pray toward their land, which You gave to their fathers, and the city that You have chosen and the house that I have built for Your name,

**39** then hear from the heavens, the place of Your dwelling, their prayer and their pleas, and uphold their cause, and forgive Your people who have sinned against You.

**40** Now, my God, let Your eyes be open and Your ears attentive to the prayer made in this place.

**41** So now arise, O LORD God, to Your resting place,  
You and the ark of Your strength.  
Let Your priests, O LORD God, be clothed with salvation,  
and let Your loyal ones rejoice in what is good.

**42** O LORD God, do not turn away the face of Your anointed ones.  
Remember the loyal love shown to David Your servant."

## Notes

- **vv1–42 and 1 Kings 8:12–50** — the same prayer, with changes noted below. Chronicles ends differently: Kings goes on to *the iron furnace* and Moses (1 Kings 8:51–53); Chronicles closes with a psalm (vv41–42).
- **vv1–2 "He would dwell in thick darkness"** — *ba-arafel*, the darkness Moses entered on Sinai: *Moses drew near to the thick darkness where God was* (Exodus 20:21).
- **vv5–6 "and I have not chosen a man to be leader… But I have chosen Jerusalem"** — Kings has neither clause (1 Kings 8:16). In Chronicles the two choosings stand side by side, a city and a man, as they do in the promise of 1 Chronicles 17.
- **v13 "a bronze platform, five cubits long, five cubits wide and three cubits high"** — not in Kings. The word is *kiyyor*, elsewhere a **basin** (4:6), here something he stands and kneels on. Its measures are those of the altar of the tabernacle, *five cubits long and five cubits wide… and three cubits its height* (Exodus 27:1).
- **v16 "to walk in My instruction as you have walked before Me"** — *la-lekhet be-torati*. Kings has *to walk before Me* (1 Kings 8:25). Chronicles names the *torah* as the way of walking before Him.
- **v18 "will God indeed dwell with man on the earth?"** — *et ha-adam*, **with humankind**, is not in Kings (1 Kings 8:27). The question is now whether God lives among people.
- **v29 "each knowing his own blow and his own pain"** — *nig'o u-makh'ovo*. Kings has *the blow of his own heart* (1 Kings 8:38).
- **vv41–42 "arise, O LORD God, to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength"** — Psalm 132:8–10, with changes: the psalm's priests are clothed with *righteousness* (*tsedeq*), these with *salvation* (*teshu'ah*); the psalm's *anointed one* is singular, here plural, *meshichekha*, **Your anointed ones**. The Greek reads *do not turn away Your face*, with no *anointed*.
- **v42 "Remember the loyal love shown to David"** — *chasdei David*, the phrase of Isaiah 55:3, *I will make with you an everlasting covenant, the loyal love shown to David, which is sure*. The prayer that began by asking God to keep His word to David (v16) ends by asking Him to remember it.

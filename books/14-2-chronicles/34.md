# 2 Chronicles 34

## Josiah King of Judah

**1** Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem.

**2** He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD and walked in the ways of David his father; he did not turn aside to the right or to the left.

## The Land Cleansed

**3** In the eighth year of his reign, while he was still a youth, he began to seek the God of David his father, and in the twelfth year he began to cleanse Judah and Jerusalem of the high places, the Asherim, the carved images and the cast images.

**4** They tore down the altars of the Baals in his presence, and he cut down the incense altars that stood above them. He broke the Asherim, the carved images and the cast images, crushed them to dust and scattered it over the graves of those who had sacrificed to them.

**5** He burned the bones of the priests on their altars, and cleansed Judah and Jerusalem.

**6** In the cities of Manasseh, Ephraim and Simeon, as far as Naphtali, in their ruins all around,

**7** he tore down the altars, beat the Asherim and the carved images into dust, and cut down all the incense altars throughout all the land of Israel. Then he returned to Jerusalem.

## The Repair of the House

**8** In the eighteenth year of his reign, when he had cleansed the land and the house, he sent Shaphan son of Azaliah, Maaseiah the governor of the city and Joah son of Joahaz the recorder to repair the house of the LORD his God.

**9** They came to Hilkiah the high priest and gave him the money that had been brought into the house of God, which the Levites, the keepers of the threshold, had collected from Manasseh and Ephraim, from all the remnant of Israel and from all Judah and Benjamin, and they returned to Jerusalem.

**10** They gave it into the hands of those who did the work, who were appointed over the house of the LORD, and they gave it to the workers who were working in the house of the LORD to restore and repair the house.

**11** They gave it to the carpenters and the builders to buy quarried stone, and timber for the couplings and for the beams of the buildings that the kings of Judah had let go to ruin.

**12** The men did the work faithfully. Over them were appointed Jahath and Obadiah the Levites, of the sons of Merari, and Zechariah and Meshullam, of the sons of the Kohathites, to direct it. The Levites, all who were skilled with instruments of song,

**13** were over the burden bearers and directed all who did the work in every kind of service, and some of the Levites were scribes, officers and gatekeepers.

## The Book Found

**14** While they were bringing out the money that had been brought into the house of the LORD, Hilkiah the priest found the book of the instruction of the LORD given through Moses.

**15** Hilkiah said to Shaphan the secretary, "I have found the book of the instruction in the house of the LORD." Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan.

**16** Shaphan brought the book to the king, and he also brought back word to the king, saying, "All that was given into the hands of your servants, they are doing.

**17** They have poured out the money that was found in the house of the LORD and have given it into the hands of those appointed and of those who do the work."

**18** Then Shaphan the secretary told the king, "Hilkiah the priest has given me a book." Shaphan read from it before the king.

**19** And it came to pass, when the king heard the words of the instruction, that he tore his clothes.

**20** The king commanded Hilkiah, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Abdon son of Micah, Shaphan the secretary and Asaiah the king's servant, saying,

**21** "Go, inquire of the LORD for me, and for those who are left in Israel and in Judah, concerning the words of the book that has been found. For great is the wrath of the LORD that is poured out on us, because our fathers have not kept the word of the LORD, to do according to all that is written in this book."

## Huldah the Prophetess

**22** So Hilkiah and those the king had sent went to Huldah the prophetess, the wife of Shallum son of Tokhath, son of Hasrah, keeper of the wardrobe. She lived in Jerusalem in the Second Quarter, and they spoke to her accordingly.

**23** She said to them, "Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: 'Tell the man who sent you to me,

**24** Thus says the LORD: I am about to bring evil on this place and on its inhabitants, all the curses that are written in the book that they have read before the king of Judah.

**25** Because they have forsaken Me and burned incense to other gods, to provoke Me to anger with all the works of their hands, My wrath will be poured out on this place, and it will not be quenched.'

**26** But to the king of Judah, who sent you to inquire of the LORD, say this to him: 'Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: As for the words that you have heard,

**27** because your heart was tender and you humbled yourself before God when you heard His words against this place and against its inhabitants, and you humbled yourself before Me and tore your clothes and wept before Me, I also have heard you, declares the LORD.

**28** I will gather you to your fathers, and you shall be gathered to your grave in peace, and your eyes shall not see all the evil that I will bring on this place and on its inhabitants.'" They brought back word to the king.

## The Covenant Renewed

**29** Then the king sent and gathered all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem.

**30** The king went up to the house of the LORD, and with him all the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the priests and the Levites and all the people, both great and small. He read in their hearing all the words of the book of the covenant that had been found in the house of the LORD.

**31** The king stood in his place and made the covenant before the LORD, to walk after the LORD and to keep His commandments, His testimonies and His statutes with all his heart and all his soul, to do the words of the covenant that were written in this book.

**32** He made all who were present in Jerusalem and Benjamin take their stand in it, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem did according to the covenant of God, the God of their fathers.

**33** Josiah took away all the detestable things from all the lands that belonged to the sons of Israel, and made all who were present in Israel serve the LORD their God. All his days they did not turn aside from following the LORD, the God of their fathers.

## Notes

- **vv3–7 "In the eighth year… in the twelfth year he began to cleanse Judah"** — Kings begins Josiah's reform after the book is found, in the eighteenth year (2 Kings 22:3; 23:4–20). Chronicles puts the cleansing first: he sought God at sixteen and purged the land at twenty, and the book is found while the house is being repaired.
- **v5 "He burned the bones of the priests on their altars"** — the word of the man of God against Jeroboam's altar at Bethel: *A son shall be born to the house of David, Josiah by name, and on you he shall sacrifice the priests of the high places… and human bones shall be burned on you* (1 Kings 13:2). Kings tells it of the altar at Bethel itself (2 Kings 23:15–16).
- **v6 "in their ruins all around"** — the read form, *be-charvoteihem*. What is written is two words, *be-har battehem*, **on the mountain, their houses**. The Greek has *in their places around*. The sense is that the cleansing reached the ruined towns of the north.
- **v9 "and they returned to Jerusalem"** — the read form, *va-yashuvu*. What is written, *ve-yoshvei*, reads **and the inhabitants of Jerusalem**, one more place the money came from.
- **vv17, 21, 25 "poured out the money… the wrath of the LORD that is poured out"** — one verb, *natakh*, used of metal melted for weighing and of wrath. Kings has *kindled* in vv21 and 25 (2 Kings 22:13, 17).
- **v24 "all the curses that are written in the book"** — Kings has *all the words of the book* (2 Kings 22:16). The phrase is Deuteronomy's: *all the curse that is written in this book… all the curses of the covenant written in this book of the instruction* (Deuteronomy 29:20–21).
- **v30 "the priests and the Levites"** — Kings has *the priests and the prophets* (2 Kings 23:2).
- **v31 "stood in his place"** — *al omdo*. Kings has *by the pillar*, *al ha-ammud* (2 Kings 23:3), the king's place in the house where Joash was crowned (23:13). The Greek of Chronicles also has *by the pillar*.
- **v33 "All his days they did not turn aside"** — said of the people, for as long as the king lived. Jeremiah, who prophesied *in the days of Josiah the king*, heard it otherwise: *Judah did not return to Me with her whole heart, but in falsehood* (Jeremiah 3:6, 10).

# 2 Chronicles 24

## The Repair of the House

**1** Joash was seven years old when he began to reign, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Zibiah of Beersheba.

**2** Joash did what was right in the eyes of the LORD all the days of Jehoiada the priest.

**3** Jehoiada took two wives for him, and he fathered sons and daughters.

**4** And it came to pass after this that Joash set his heart on restoring the house of the LORD.

**5** He gathered the priests and the Levites and said to them, "Go out to the cities of Judah and gather money from all Israel to repair the house of your God, year by year, and see that you act on it quickly." But the Levites did not act quickly.

**6** So the king called Jehoiada the chief and said to him, "Why have you not required the Levites to bring in from Judah and Jerusalem the tax that Moses the servant of the LORD laid on the assembly of Israel for the tent of the testimony?"

**7** For the sons of Athaliah, that wicked woman, had broken into the house of God, and had also used all the holy things of the house of the LORD for the Baals.

**8** So the king gave orders, and they made a chest and set it outside, at the gate of the house of the LORD.

**9** A proclamation was made through Judah and Jerusalem to bring to the LORD the tax that Moses the servant of God laid on Israel in the wilderness.

**10** All the officials and all the people rejoiced, and they brought it and threw it into the chest until it was full.

**11** Whenever the chest was brought to the king's officers by the hand of the Levites, and they saw that there was much money, the king's secretary and the officer of the chief priest would come and empty the chest, and take it and return it to its place. So they did day after day, and gathered money in abundance.

**12** The king and Jehoiada gave it to those who did the work of the service of the house of the LORD, and they hired stonecutters and carpenters to restore the house of the LORD, and also workers in iron and bronze to repair the house of the LORD.

**13** So the workmen worked, and the repair went forward in their hands. They set up the house of God according to its proper design and made it strong.

**14** When they had finished, they brought the rest of the money before the king and Jehoiada, and with it were made vessels for the house of the LORD, vessels for the service and for the burnt offerings, dishes, and vessels of gold and silver. They offered burnt offerings in the house of the LORD continually all the days of Jehoiada.

## The Death of Jehoiada

**15** Jehoiada grew old and full of days, and he died. He was a hundred and thirty years old at his death.

**16** They buried him in the City of David among the kings, because he had done good in Israel, and toward God and His house.

**17** After the death of Jehoiada the officials of Judah came and bowed down to the king, and the king listened to them.

**18** They forsook the house of the LORD, the God of their fathers, and served the Asherim and the idols. Wrath came on Judah and Jerusalem for this guilt of theirs.

**19** Yet He sent prophets among them to bring them back to the LORD. They testified against them, but they would not listen.

## Zechariah Son of Jehoiada

**20** Then the Spirit of God clothed Zechariah son of Jehoiada the priest, and he stood above the people and said to them, "Thus says God: Why do you transgress the commandments of the LORD, so that you cannot prosper? Because you have forsaken the LORD, He has forsaken you."

**21** But they conspired against him, and at the command of the king they stoned him with stones in the court of the house of the LORD.

**22** So Joash the king did not remember the loyal love that Jehoiada, Zechariah's father, had shown him, but killed his son. As he was dying, he said, "May the LORD see and require it!"

## The Death of Joash

**23** And it came to pass at the turn of the year that the army of Aram came up against him. They came to Judah and Jerusalem, destroyed all the officials of the people from among the people, and sent all their spoil to the king of Damascus.

**24** Though the army of Aram had come with few men, the LORD gave a very great army into their hand, because they had forsaken the LORD, the God of their fathers. So they executed judgment on Joash.

**25** When they had departed from him, leaving him in great sickness, his servants conspired against him because of the blood of the sons of Jehoiada the priest, and killed him on his bed. So he died, and they buried him in the City of David, but they did not bury him in the tombs of the kings.

**26** These are the men who conspired against him: Zabad son of Shimeath the Ammonite woman, and Jehozabad son of Shimrith the Moabite woman.

**27** As for his sons, the many oracles against him and the rebuilding of the house of God, they are written in the account of the book of the kings. Amaziah his son reigned in his place.

## Notes

- **vv1–14, 23–27 and 2 Kings 12** — the same reign. Chronicles adds what happened after Jehoiada died (vv15–22), and makes the Aramean war a judgment (v24). Kings has Hazael come up and Joash buy him off with the holy things (2 Kings 12:17–18).
- **v2 "all the days of Jehoiada the priest"** — Kings has *all his days, in which Jehoiada the priest instructed him* (2 Kings 12:2). Chronicles divides the reign at the priest's death (v17).
- **vv6, 9 "the tax that Moses the servant of the LORD laid on the assembly"** — the half-shekel of Exodus 30, *the atonement silver… for the service of the tent of meeting* (Exodus 30:16). Kings makes the chest the priest's doing and sets it *beside the altar* (2 Kings 12:9); here the king orders it and it stands *outside, at the gate*.
- **v16 "They buried him in the City of David among the kings"** — the priest is buried with the kings; Joash, the king, is not (v25), nor was Jehoram (21:20).
- **v20 "the Spirit of God clothed Zechariah"** — *lavshah*, the word of Judges 6:34, *The Spirit of the LORD clothed Gideon*. *You have forsaken the LORD, He has forsaken you*: Shemaiah's word to Rehoboam (12:5) and Azariah's to Asa (15:2).
- **vv21–22 "they stoned him… in the court of the house of the LORD… May the LORD see and require it!"** — Jesus names a Zechariah as the last of the righteous killed, *from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zechariah son of Berechiah, whom you murdered between the sanctuary and the altar* (Matthew 23:35). Matthew gives the father as *Berechiah*, the father of the prophet Zechariah (Zechariah 1:1).
- **v24 "with few men… a very great army into their hand"** — the reverse of Asa's prayer, *there is none besides You to help, between the mighty and those who have no strength* (14:11).
- **v25 "because of the blood of the sons of Jehoiada"** — *benei*, **sons**, plural; only Zechariah's death is told. The Greek has *son*.
- **v26 "Zabad son of Shimeath the Ammonite woman, and Jehozabad son of Shimrith the Moabite woman"** — Kings has *Jozabad son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shomer* (2 Kings 12:21). Chronicles names their mothers, and both foreign.

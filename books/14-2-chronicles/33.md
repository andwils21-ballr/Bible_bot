# 2 Chronicles 33

## Manasseh King of Judah

**1** Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem.

**2** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, according to the detestable practices of the nations whom the LORD had driven out before the sons of Israel.

**3** He rebuilt the high places that Hezekiah his father had torn down. He set up altars for the Baals and made Asherahs, and he bowed down to all the host of heaven and served them.

**4** He built altars in the house of the LORD, of which the LORD had said, "In Jerusalem shall My name be forever."

**5** He built altars for all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of the LORD.

**6** He made his sons pass through the fire in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, practiced soothsaying, read omens and practiced sorcery, and appointed those who deal with ghosts and spirits. He did much evil in the eyes of the LORD, provoking Him to anger.

**7** He set the carved image of the idol that he had made in the house of God, of which God had said to David and to Solomon his son, "In this house, and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, I will put My name forever.

**8** I will not again remove the foot of Israel from the land that I appointed for your fathers, if only they will be careful to do all that I have commanded them, all the instruction, the statutes and the rules given through Moses."

**9** So Manasseh led Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem astray, to do more evil than the nations whom the LORD had destroyed before the sons of Israel.

## Captivity and Return

**10** The LORD spoke to Manasseh and to his people, but they paid no attention.

**11** So the LORD brought on them the commanders of the army of the king of Assyria, who captured Manasseh with hooks, bound him with bronze shackles and took him to Babylon.

**12** In his distress he sought the favor of the LORD his God and humbled himself greatly before the God of his fathers.

**13** He prayed to Him, and God let Himself be pleaded with by him, heard his plea and brought him back to Jerusalem, to his kingdom. Then Manasseh knew that the LORD, He is God.

**14** After this he built an outer wall for the City of David, west of Gihon in the valley, as far as the entrance at the Fish Gate, and carried it around the Ophel and raised it very high. He put commanders of the army in all the fortified cities of Judah.

**15** He removed the foreign gods and the idol from the house of the LORD, and all the altars that he had built on the mountain of the house of the LORD and in Jerusalem, and threw them outside the city.

**16** He built the altar of the LORD and sacrificed on it peace offerings and thank offerings, and he told Judah to serve the LORD, the God of Israel.

**17** The people still sacrificed at the high places, but only to the LORD their God.

**18** The rest of the acts of Manasseh, his prayer to his God, and the words of the seers who spoke to him in the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, are in the records of the kings of Israel.

**19** His prayer, and how God let Himself be pleaded with by him, all his sin and his unfaithfulness, and the sites on which he built high places and set up the Asherim and the carved images before he humbled himself, are written in the records of Hozai.

**20** Manasseh lay down with his fathers, and they buried him in his house, and Amon his son reigned in his place.

## Amon King of Judah

**21** Amon was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem.

**22** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, as Manasseh his father had done. Amon sacrificed to all the carved images that Manasseh his father had made, and served them.

**23** He did not humble himself before the LORD, as Manasseh his father had humbled himself; this Amon only added to his guilt.

**24** His servants conspired against him and put him to death in his house.

**25** But the people of the land struck down all who had conspired against King Amon, and the people of the land made Josiah his son king in his place.

## Notes

- **vv1–20 and 2 Kings 21:1–18** — Kings tells Manasseh's reign as the evil that sealed the fall of Jerusalem (2 Kings 21:10–15; 23:26). Chronicles keeps the list of his sins and adds what Kings does not have: the captivity, the prayer, the return and the reform (vv10–17).
- **v7 "the carved image of the idol"** — *pesel ha-semel*. Kings has *the carved image of Asherah* (2 Kings 21:7). *Semel* is a figure or statue: the law forbids *a carved image in the form of any figure* (Deuteronomy 4:16), and Ezekiel sees *the image of jealousy* at the gate of this house (Ezekiel 8:3, 5).
- **v11 "captured Manasseh with hooks, bound him with bronze shackles and took him to Babylon"** — *ba-chochim*, the word of the LORD's threat to Sennacherib, *I will put My hook in your nose* (2 Kings 19:28). *Va-ya'asruhu ba-nechushtayim… Bavelah* are the words of the end of the last king, Zedekiah: *they bound him with bronze shackles and brought him to Babylon* (2 Kings 25:7). The worst king of Judah goes the way the kingdom will go, and comes back.
- **v12 "he sought the favor of the LORD"** — *chillah et penei YHWH*, **softened the face of the LORD**, the words of Moses after the golden calf (Exodus 32:11).
- **vv13, 19 "God let Himself be pleaded with by him"** — *va-ye'ater lo*, the rare verb of Isaac's prayer: *The LORD let Himself be pleaded with* (Genesis 25:21). *The LORD, He is God* is the cry of the people on Carmel (1 Kings 18:39).
- **v19 "the records of Hozai"** — *divrei Chozai*. The word differs by one letter from *ha-chozim*, **the seers**, in v18, and the Greek has *of the seers* in both verses. As written, it is a name, otherwise unknown.
- **v20 "they buried him in his house"** — Kings has *in the garden of his house, in the garden of Uzza* (2 Kings 21:18); the Greek of Chronicles also has *in the garden of his house*.
- **v23 "He did not humble himself… as Manasseh his father had humbled himself"** — *nikhna*, the word of the promise at the dedication, *if My people humble themselves* (7:14). Father and son are measured by it.

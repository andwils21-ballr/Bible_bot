# 2 Chronicles 31

## The High Places Torn Down

**1** When all this was finished, all Israel who were present went out to the cities of Judah and broke the pillars, cut down the Asherim, and tore down the high places and the altars throughout all Judah and Benjamin, and in Ephraim and Manasseh, until they had destroyed them all. Then all the sons of Israel returned, every man to his possession, to their cities.

## The Portions for the Priests and Levites

**2** Hezekiah appointed the divisions of the priests and the Levites by their divisions, each according to his service, the priests and the Levites, for burnt offerings and peace offerings, to minister, to give thanks and to praise in the gates of the camp of the LORD.

**3** The king's portion from his own possessions was for the burnt offerings: the morning and evening burnt offerings, and the burnt offerings for the sabbaths, the new moons and the appointed feasts, as it is written in the instruction of the LORD.

**4** He told the people who lived in Jerusalem to give the portion due to the priests and the Levites, so that they might hold fast to the instruction of the LORD.

**5** As soon as the word spread, the sons of Israel gave in abundance the first of their grain, new wine, oil, honey and all the produce of the field, and they brought in the tithe of everything in abundance.

**6** The sons of Israel and Judah who lived in the cities of Judah also brought the tithe of cattle and sheep, and the tithe of the holy things that were consecrated to the LORD their God, and laid them in heaps.

**7** In the third month they began to lay the foundation of the heaps, and in the seventh month they finished.

**8** When Hezekiah and the officials came and saw the heaps, they blessed the LORD and His people Israel.

**9** Hezekiah inquired of the priests and the Levites about the heaps.

**10** Azariah the chief priest, of the house of Zadok, answered him, "Since they began to bring the contributions into the house of the LORD, we have eaten and been satisfied and have plenty left over, for the LORD has blessed His people, and what is left is this great store."

## The Overseers

**11** Then Hezekiah told them to prepare chambers in the house of the LORD, and they prepared them.

**12** They brought in the contributions, the tithes and the consecrated things faithfully. Conaniah the Levite was the officer in charge of them, and Shimei his brother was second;

**13** and Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath and Benaiah were overseers under the hand of Conaniah and Shimei his brother, by the appointment of Hezekiah the king and Azariah the chief officer of the house of God.

**14** Kore son of Imnah the Levite, the keeper of the east gate, was over the freewill offerings to God, to give out the contribution of the LORD and the most holy things.

**15** Under him were Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah and Shecaniah in the cities of the priests, to give faithfully to their brothers by divisions, to great and small alike,

**16** apart from those enrolled by genealogy, males from three years old and upward, all who entered the house of the LORD as the duty of each day required, for their service in their charges by their divisions;

**17** and the priests were enrolled by their fathers' houses, and the Levites from twenty years old and upward, in their charges by their divisions.

**18** They were enrolled with all their little ones, their wives, their sons and their daughters, the whole assembly, for they were faithful in keeping themselves holy.

**19** For the sons of Aaron, the priests, who were in the fields of pasture land of their cities, in every city there were men designated by name to give portions to every male among the priests and to everyone enrolled among the Levites.

**20** So Hezekiah did throughout all Judah. He did what was good and right and true before the LORD his God.

**21** In every work that he began in the service of the house of God, and in the instruction and in the commandment, to seek his God, he acted with all his heart, and he prospered.

## Notes

- **v1 "broke the pillars, cut down the Asherim, and tore down the high places"** — Kings gives Hezekiah this act alone, *He removed the high places, broke the pillars and cut down the Asherah* (2 Kings 18:4). Here the people do it, in Ephraim and Manasseh as well as Judah.
- **v2 "the gates of the camp of the LORD"** — *machanot YHWH*, the word of the wilderness, where the tent *set out… in the middle of the camps* (Numbers 2:17). The house in Jerusalem is still spoken of as the camp around the dwelling.
- **vv4–6 "the portion due to the priests and the Levites… the first… the tithe"** — the law of Numbers 18: the first of *the oil… the new wine and the grain* to the priests (18:12), and *every tenth in Israel* to the Levites *in return for the service they do* (18:21). *So that they might hold fast to the instruction of the LORD*: provided for, they are free for it.
- **v7 "In the third month… and in the seventh month they finished"** — from the grain harvest to the ingathering of fruit: the Festival of Weeks falls fifty days after Passover, and the Festival of Booths in the seventh month (Leviticus 23:15–16, 34). The heaps grew through the whole harvest.
- **v10 "we have eaten and been satisfied and have plenty left over"** — *akhol ve-savoa ve-hoter*, the words of the promise, *You will eat and be satisfied, and you shall bless the LORD your God* (Deuteronomy 8:10). Hezekiah and the officials do bless Him (v8).
- **vv12, 15, 18 "faithfully"** — *be-emunah*, three times: the stores were brought in faithfully, given out faithfully, and the priests kept themselves holy *in their faithfulness*.
- **v16 "males from three years old and upward"** — an odd age for those *who entered the house of the LORD* for service; the Greek has it too. Read with *apart from*, it may count the boys of the priestly families who received a share. The Hebrew does not say more.
- **vv20–21 "good and right and true… with all his heart, and he prospered"** — *ha-tov ve-ha-yashar ve-ha-emet*. Of Hezekiah alone among the kings does Chronicles say all three.

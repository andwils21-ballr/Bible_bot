# 2 Chronicles 35

## The Passover

**1** Josiah kept a Passover to the LORD in Jerusalem, and they slaughtered the Passover lamb on the fourteenth day of the first month.

**2** He set the priests at their posts and encouraged them in the service of the house of the LORD.

**3** He said to the Levites who taught all Israel and who were holy to the LORD, "Put the holy ark in the house that Solomon son of David king of Israel built. You no longer have to carry it on your shoulders. Now serve the LORD your God and His people Israel.

**4** Prepare yourselves by your fathers' houses in your divisions, as David king of Israel wrote and as Solomon his son wrote.

**5** Stand in the holy place by the sections of the fathers' houses of your brothers, the lay people, so that each has a portion of a Levite family.

**6** Slaughter the Passover lamb, consecrate yourselves, and prepare it for your brothers, to do according to the word of the LORD given through Moses."

**7** Josiah contributed to the lay people, for all who were present, flocks of lambs and young goats, all for the Passover offerings, thirty thousand in number, and three thousand bulls; these were from the king's possessions.

**8** His officials contributed freely to the people, to the priests and to the Levites. Hilkiah, Zechariah and Jehiel, the chief officers of the house of God, gave the priests two thousand six hundred Passover offerings and three hundred bulls.

**9** Conaniah, with Shemaiah and Nethanel his brothers, and Hashabiah, Jeiel and Jozabad, the chiefs of the Levites, contributed to the Levites five thousand Passover offerings and five hundred bulls.

**10** So the service was prepared, and the priests stood at their posts and the Levites by their divisions, as the king had commanded.

**11** They slaughtered the Passover lamb, and the priests dashed the blood they received from them while the Levites flayed the animals.

**12** They set apart the burnt offerings to give them to the sections of the fathers' houses of the lay people, to offer to the LORD, as it is written in the book of Moses; and so with the bulls.

**13** They cooked the Passover lamb with fire according to the rule, and they boiled the holy offerings in pots, in cauldrons and in pans, and carried them quickly to all the lay people.

**14** Afterward they prepared for themselves and for the priests, because the priests, the sons of Aaron, were busy offering the burnt offerings and the fat until night. So the Levites prepared for themselves and for the priests, the sons of Aaron.

**15** The singers, the sons of Asaph, were at their posts according to the command of David, Asaph, Heman and Jeduthun the king's seer, and the gatekeepers were at each gate. They did not need to leave their service, because their brothers the Levites prepared for them.

**16** So all the service of the LORD was prepared that day, to keep the Passover and to offer burnt offerings on the altar of the LORD, as King Josiah had commanded.

**17** The sons of Israel who were present kept the Passover at that time, and the Festival of Unleavened Bread seven days.

**18** No Passover like it had been kept in Israel since the days of Samuel the prophet. None of the kings of Israel had kept such a Passover as Josiah kept, with the priests and the Levites, all Judah and Israel who were present, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**19** In the eighteenth year of Josiah's reign this Passover was kept.

## The Death of Josiah

**20** After all this, when Josiah had set the house in order, Neco king of Egypt came up to fight at Carchemish on the Euphrates, and Josiah went out to meet him.

**21** But Neco sent messengers to him, saying, "What have I to do with you, king of Judah? I am not coming against you today, but against the house with which I am at war, and God has told me to hurry. Stop opposing God, who is with me, so that He does not destroy you."

**22** But Josiah would not turn his face away from him. He disguised himself to fight him, and did not listen to the words of Neco from the mouth of God, and came to fight in the plain of Megiddo.

**23** The archers shot King Josiah, and the king said to his servants, "Take me away, for I am badly wounded."

**24** So his servants took him out of the chariot and put him in his second chariot and brought him to Jerusalem, and he died. He was buried in the tombs of his fathers, and all Judah and Jerusalem mourned for Josiah.

**25** Jeremiah lamented for Josiah, and all the singing men and singing women have spoken of Josiah in their laments to this day. They made them a statute in Israel; they are written in the Laments.

**26** The rest of the acts of Josiah, and his deeds of loyal love according to what is written in the instruction of the LORD,

**27** and his acts, first and last, are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.

## Notes

- **v3 "Put the holy ark in the house… You no longer have to carry it on your shoulders"** — the last time the ark is named in Kings or Chronicles. The verse does not say why it was out of the house. Carrying it on the shoulders was the Levites' charge in the wilderness (Numbers 7:9; 1 Chronicles 15:15); with the ark at rest, Josiah gives them a new one, the service of the people. In the same reign Jeremiah says a day is coming when *they will no longer say, "The ark of the covenant of the LORD"… nor will it be made again* (Jeremiah 3:16).
- **v21 "God has told me to hurry. Stop opposing God, who is with me"** — words of a Pharaoh, and v22 calls them *the words of Neco from the mouth of God*. The book that says the LORD stirred the spirit of the Philistines and Arabs against Jehoram (21:16), and will say He stirred the spirit of Cyrus (36:22), here gives His word to the king of Egypt, and Josiah does not hear it.

# 2 Chronicles 27

**1** Jotham was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jerushah the daughter of Zadok.

**2** He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, according to all that Uzziah his father had done, except that he did not enter the temple of the LORD. But the people still acted corruptly.

**3** He built the Upper Gate of the house of the LORD, and built much on the wall of Ophel.

**4** He built cities in the hill country of Judah, and in the wooded hills he built fortresses and towers.

**5** He fought with the king of the Ammonites and prevailed against them. The Ammonites gave him that year a hundred talents of silver, ten thousand cors of wheat and ten thousand of barley. The Ammonites paid him the same in the second year and the third.

**6** So Jotham became strong, because he ordered his ways before the LORD his God.

**7** The rest of the acts of Jotham, all his wars and his ways, are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.

**8** He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem.

**9** Jotham lay down with his fathers, and they buried him in the City of David, and Ahaz his son reigned in his place.

## Notes

- **vv1–3, 7–9 and 2 Kings 15:32–38** — the same reign. Chronicles leaves out Kings' last word on it, *the LORD began to send Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah against Judah* (2 Kings 15:37), and gives the Ammonite war instead (v5).
- **v2 "except that he did not enter the temple of the LORD"** — *raq lo va el heikhal YHWH*: he did all his father did, except the thing that brought his father's blight (26:16–21). *But the people still acted corruptly*: Kings has *the high places were not taken away* (2 Kings 15:35).
- **v6 "So Jotham became strong, because he ordered his ways before the LORD"** — *va-yitchazzeq*, the word of Uzziah's rise and fall: *he was marvelously helped, until he was strong. But when he was strong, his heart was lifted up* (26:15–16). Jotham's strength has a reason, and the chapter ends before it can turn.
- **v8 "He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign"** — the words of v1 again. The Greek does not repeat them.

# 2 Chronicles 21

## Jehoram Kills His Brothers

**1** Jehoshaphat lay down with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the City of David, and Jehoram his son reigned in his place.

**2** He had brothers, sons of Jehoshaphat: Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, Azariahu, Michael and Shephatiah. All these were sons of Jehoshaphat king of Israel.

**3** Their father gave them great gifts of silver, gold and precious things, with fortified cities in Judah, but he gave the kingdom to Jehoram, because he was the firstborn.

**4** When Jehoram had risen over his father's kingdom and established himself, he killed all his brothers with the sword, and some of the officials of Israel as well.

**5** Jehoram was thirty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem.

**6** He walked in the way of the kings of Israel, as the house of Ahab had done, for the daughter of Ahab was his wife. He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD.

**7** Yet the LORD was not willing to destroy the house of David, because of the covenant He had made with David, and since He had promised to give a lamp to him and to his sons forever.

## Edom and Libnah Revolt

**8** In his days Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah and set up a king of their own.

**9** Then Jehoram crossed over with his commanders and all his chariots with him. He rose by night and struck the Edomites who had surrounded him and the commanders of the chariots.

**10** So Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah to this day. Libnah revolted from under his hand at the same time, because he had forsaken the LORD, the God of his fathers.

**11** He also made high places in the hill country of Judah, and led the inhabitants of Jerusalem to whore, and drove Judah astray.

## The Letter from Elijah

**12** A letter came to him from Elijah the prophet, saying, "Thus says the LORD, the God of David your father: Because you have not walked in the ways of Jehoshaphat your father, or in the ways of Asa king of Judah,

**13** but have walked in the way of the kings of Israel, and have led Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem to whore, as the house of Ahab led Israel to whore, and have also killed your brothers, your father's house, who were better than you,

**14** the LORD is about to strike your people, your sons, your wives and all your possessions with a great blow.

**15** You yourself will have a severe sickness, a disease of your bowels, until your bowels come out because of the sickness, day after day."

**16** The LORD stirred up against Jehoram the spirit of the Philistines and of the Arabians who lived beside the Cushites.

**17** They came up against Judah, broke into it, and carried away all the possessions found in the king's house, and also his sons and his wives, so that no son was left to him except Jehoahaz, the youngest of his sons.

**18** After all this the LORD struck him in his bowels with a sickness that could not be healed.

**19** In the course of time, at the end of two years, his bowels came out because of his sickness, and he died in great pain. His people made no fire for him like the fire for his fathers.

**20** He was thirty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem. He departed with no one's regret. They buried him in the City of David, but not in the tombs of the kings.

## Notes

- **vv5–10 and 2 Kings 8:16–24** — the same account, with Chronicles' additions: the killing of the brothers (vv2–4), the reason for Libnah's revolt (v10), Elijah's letter (vv12–15) and the manner of his death (vv16–20).
- **v2 "Jehoshaphat king of Israel"** — the Greek has *king of Judah*. Chronicles uses *Israel* for the people faithful to David's house as well (11:3; 12:6). The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **v7 "because of the covenant He had made with David… a lamp to him and to his sons"** — Kings has *the LORD was not willing to destroy Judah, for the sake of David His servant* (2 Kings 8:19). Chronicles names the *covenant*, and the house of David instead of Judah.
- **vv11, 13 "led the inhabitants of Jerusalem to whore"** — *va-yizen*, the verb of *zanah* made causative: the king makes the people whore after other gods, *as the house of Ahab led Israel to whore*.
- **v12 "A letter came to him from Elijah the prophet"** — Elijah's only appearance in Chronicles, and the only writing of his anywhere in Scripture. Kings tells of his being taken up in 2 Kings 2, before it tells of Jehoram of Judah (2 Kings 8:16).
- **v14 "the LORD is about to strike… with a great blow"** — *hinneh YHWH nogef maggefah gedolah*, *hinneh* with a participle, rendered *is about to*.
- **v17 "Jehoahaz, the youngest of his sons"** — the king called *Ahaziah* in 22:1. *Yeho-achaz* and *Achaz-yahu* are the same two parts, the divine name and *achaz*, **he has grasped**, in reverse order.
- **v19 "His people made no fire for him like the fire for his fathers"** — the burning made for Asa at his burial, *a very great fire* (16:14). Jehoram is buried without it, and *not in the tombs of the kings* (v20).
- **v20 "He departed with no one's regret"** — *va-yelekh be-lo chemdah*, **he went without desire**: no one wanted him. The Greek has *he went without praise*.

# 2 Chronicles 15

## Azariah's Word

**1** The Spirit of God came on Azariah son of Oded,

**2** and he went out to meet Asa and said to him, "Hear me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin. The LORD is with you while you are with Him. If you seek Him, He will be found by you, but if you forsake Him, He will forsake you.

**3** For many days Israel was without the true God, without a priest to teach and without instruction.

**4** But when in their distress they turned to the LORD, the God of Israel, and sought Him, He was found by them.

**5** In those times there was no peace for anyone who went out or came in, for great turmoil was on all the inhabitants of the lands.

**6** They were crushed, nation by nation and city by city, for God troubled them with every kind of distress.

**7** But you, be strong, and do not let your hands go slack, for there is a reward for your work."

## The Reform and the Oath

**8** When Asa heard these words and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he took courage and put away the loathsome things from all the land of Judah and Benjamin and from the cities he had taken in the hill country of Ephraim. He restored the altar of the LORD that was in front of the porch of the LORD.

**9** He gathered all Judah and Benjamin, and those from Ephraim, Manasseh and Simeon who lived as guests among them, for many had deserted to him from Israel when they saw that the LORD his God was with him.

**10** They gathered at Jerusalem in the third month of the fifteenth year of Asa's reign.

**11** On that day they sacrificed to the LORD, from the spoil they had brought, seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep.

**12** They entered into a covenant to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, with all their heart and with all their soul,

**13** and that whoever would not seek the LORD, the God of Israel, should be put to death, whether small or great, whether man or woman.

**14** They swore an oath to the LORD with a loud voice, with shouting, with trumpets and with rams' horns.

**15** All Judah rejoiced over the oath, for they had sworn with all their heart and had sought Him with all their desire, and He was found by them. The LORD gave them rest on every side.

## Maacah Removed

**16** King Asa also removed Maacah his mother from being queen mother, because she had made a horrid image for Asherah. Asa cut down her horrid image, crushed it and burned it at the Kidron Valley.

**17** But the high places were not removed from Israel. Nevertheless, the heart of Asa was whole all his days.

**18** He brought into the house of God the things his father had dedicated and the things he himself had dedicated, silver and gold and vessels.

**19** There was no more war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa's reign.

## Notes

- **v2 "If you seek Him, He will be found by you, but if you forsake Him, He will forsake you"** — the words of David to Solomon, *If you seek Him, He will be found by you; but if you forsake Him, He will reject you forever* (1 Chronicles 28:9), now said to the whole people. Vv4 and 15 show it kept: *He was found by them*.
- **v8 "the prophecy of Oded the prophet"** — v1 gives the words to *Azariah son of Oded*; here the Hebrew has *Oded*, and the Greek *Adad*. The text keeps the Hebrew. *Ha-shiqqutsim*, **the loathsome things**.

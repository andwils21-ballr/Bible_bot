# 2 Chronicles 7

## The Fire and the Glory

**1** When Solomon had finished praying, fire came down from heaven and consumed the burnt offering and the sacrifices, and the glory of the LORD filled the house.

**2** The priests could not enter the house of the LORD, because the glory of the LORD filled the house of the LORD.

**3** All the sons of Israel saw the fire come down and the glory of the LORD on the house, and they bowed with their faces to the ground on the pavement. They bowed down and gave thanks to the LORD, "For He is good, for His loyal love endures forever."

## The Dedication

**4** Then the king and all the people offered sacrifice before the LORD.

**5** King Solomon offered a sacrifice of twenty-two thousand oxen and a hundred and twenty thousand sheep. So the king and all the people dedicated the house of God.

**6** The priests stood at their posts, and the Levites with the instruments for the song of the LORD that King David had made to give thanks to the LORD, "for His loyal love endures forever," when David offered praise by their hands. Opposite them the priests sounded trumpets, and all Israel stood.

**7** Solomon consecrated the middle of the court that was in front of the house of the LORD, for there he offered the burnt offerings and the fat of the peace offerings, because the bronze altar Solomon had made could not hold the burnt offering, the grain offering and the fat.

**8** So Solomon held the feast at that time for seven days, and all Israel with him, a very great assembly, from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of Egypt.

**9** On the eighth day they held a closing assembly, for they had kept the dedication of the altar seven days and the feast seven days.

**10** On the twenty-third day of the seventh month he sent the people away to their tents, joyful and glad of heart for the good the LORD had done for David, for Solomon and for Israel His people.

**11** So Solomon finished the house of the LORD and the king's house. All that Solomon had set his heart to do in the house of the LORD and in his own house, he carried out with success.

## The LORD Appears to Solomon

**12** The LORD appeared to Solomon by night and said to him, "I have heard your prayer and have chosen this place for Myself as a house of sacrifice.

**13** If I shut up the heavens so that there is no rain, or if I command the grasshopper to devour the land, or if I send plague among My people,

**14** and My people who are called by My name humble themselves, and pray and seek My face and turn from their evil ways, then I will hear from heaven, and forgive their sin and heal their land.

**15** Now My eyes will be open and My ears attentive to the prayer made in this place.

**16** I have chosen and consecrated this house, so that My name may be there forever. My eyes and My heart will be there for all time.

**17** As for you, if you walk before Me as David your father walked, doing all that I have commanded you, and keep My statutes and My rules,

**18** then I will establish the throne of your kingdom, as I covenanted with David your father, saying, 'You shall not lack a man to rule in Israel.'

**19** But if you turn away and forsake My statutes and My commandments that I have set before you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them,

**20** then I will uproot them from My land that I have given them, and this house that I have consecrated for My name I will cast out of My sight, and I will make it a proverb and a byword among all the peoples.

**21** As for this house, which was exalted, everyone who passes by it will be appalled and will say, 'Why has the LORD done this to this land and to this house?'

**22** Then they will say, 'Because they forsook the LORD, the God of their fathers, who brought them out of the land of Egypt, and took hold of other gods and bowed down to them and served them. That is why He has brought all this evil on them.'"

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "fire came down from heaven and consumed the burnt offering"** — not in Kings. As at the first offering of the tabernacle: *Fire came out from before the LORD and consumed the burnt offering… All the people saw, and they shouted, and they fell on their faces* (Leviticus 9:24); and as David was answered *with fire from heaven* on the same ground (1 Chronicles 21:26). The people's answer is the singers' refrain, *For He is good, for His loyal love endures forever* (5:13).
- **v6 "when David offered praise by their hands"** — *be-hallel David be-yadam*: the Levites play David's instruments, and the praise is counted as his.
- **vv8–10 "seven days… the eighth day… the twenty-third day"** — Kings has *seven days and seven days, fourteen days*, and sends the people home *on the eighth day* (1 Kings 8:65–66). Chronicles explains the fourteen: the dedication of the altar, then the feast, then *a closing assembly*, *atseret*, the eighth day of Booths (Leviticus 23:36).
- **v12 "a house of sacrifice"** — *beit zavach*, a phrase found only here.
- **vv13–15 "If I shut up the heavens… and My people… humble themselves… I will hear from heaven"** — not in Kings. The LORD answers the prayer in its own words: *the heavens are shut up and there is no rain* (6:26), *plague* (6:28), *hear from the heavens and forgive* (6:21). V15 answers 6:40 word for word. The insect differs: the prayer names *arbeh* and *chasil* (6:28), the answer *chagav*, **grasshopper**, the word of Leviticus 11:22 and Numbers 13:33.
- **v14 "humble themselves"** — *yikkane'u*, the word on which Chronicles weighs its kings: Rehoboam's officials *humbled themselves* (12:6–7), and Hezekiah (32:26), Manasseh (33:12) and Josiah (34:27); Amon *did not humble himself* (33:23), nor did Zedekiah (36:12).
- **v18 "as I covenanted with David… a man to rule in Israel"** — Kings has *as I promised* and *a man on the throne of Israel* (1 Kings 9:5). *Karatti*, **I cut a covenant**, and *moshel be-Yisrael*, **one who rules in Israel**, the words of Micah 5:2, *from you shall come out for Me one who is to rule in Israel*.
- **v20 "I will uproot them from My land"** — *u-netashtim*, the verb of the curse in Deuteronomy, *The LORD uprooted them from their ground* (Deuteronomy 29:28). Kings has *I will cut off Israel* (1 Kings 9:7).

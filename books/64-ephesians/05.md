# Ephesians 5

## Walk in Love

**1** So be imitators of God, as beloved children,

**2** and walk in love, as Christ loved us and gave Himself up for us, an offering and sacrifice to God as a soothing aroma.

**3** But sexual immorality and all impurity or greed must not even be named among you, as is fitting for saints,

**4** nor obscenity, foolish talk or crude joking, which are out of place, but instead thanksgiving.

**5** For you may be sure of this: no sexually immoral or impure person, or anyone greedy — that is, an idolater — has any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God.

**6** Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things the wrath of God comes on the sons of disobedience.

**7** So do not become partners with them;

**8** for at one time you were darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Walk as children of light

**9** (for the fruit of light is found in all goodness and righteousness and truth),

**10** testing what is pleasing to the Lord.

**11** Take no part in the fruitless works of darkness, but instead expose them.

**12** For it is shameful even to speak of the things they do in secret.

**13** But everything exposed by the light becomes visible,

**14** for everything that becomes visible is light. That is why it says:  
"Awake, sleeper,  
and rise from the dead,  
and Christ will shine on you."

## Filled with the Spirit

**15** Look carefully, then, how you walk, not as unwise but as wise,

**16** making the most of the time, because the days are evil.

**17** So do not be foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is.

**18** Do not get drunk with wine, which leads to reckless living, but be filled with the Spirit,

**19** speaking to one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making music to the Lord with your heart,

**20** giving thanks always for everything to God the Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ,

**21** submitting to one another in the fear of Christ.

## Wives and Husbands

**22** Wives, submit to your own husbands, as to the Lord.

**23** For the husband is the head of the wife as Christ also is the head of the church, He Himself being the Savior of the body.

**24** But as the church submits to Christ, so also wives should submit to their husbands in everything.

**25** Husbands, love your wives, as Christ also loved the church and gave Himself up for her,

**26** so that He might make her holy, cleansing her by the washing of water with the word,

**27** so that He might present the church to Himself in splendor, without spot or wrinkle or any such thing, but holy and without defect.

**28** In the same way husbands ought to love their wives as their own bodies. He who loves his wife loves himself.

**29** For no one ever hated his own flesh, but he nourishes and cherishes it, just as Christ does the church,

**30** because we are parts of His body.

**31** "Therefore a man will leave his father and mother and cling to his wife, and the two will become one flesh."

**32** This mystery is great, but I am speaking about Christ and the church.

**33** However, each one of you also must love his wife as himself, and the wife must see that she respects her husband.

## Notes

- **v2 "an offering and sacrifice to God as a soothing aroma"** — *eis osmēn euōdias*, the Greek Bible's words for the smoke of the burnt offering: *a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a soothing aroma to the LORD* (Leviticus 1:9), first said of Noah's offering after the flood, *The LORD smelled the soothing aroma* (Genesis 8:21). Christ's love, giving Himself up, is the offering God receives.
- **v14 "Awake, sleeper, and rise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you"** — *dio legei*, **that is why it says**, the words Paul used for Psalm 68 in 4:8, but these lines are not found in this form in the books of the Old Testament. The nearest are Isaiah's *Awake and sing for joy, you who dwell in the dust* (Isaiah 26:19) and *Arise, shine, for your light has come* (Isaiah 60:1). Where Paul found them in this form, he does not say.
- **v18 "Do not get drunk with wine"** — the Greek of Proverbs 23:31, *mē methyskesthe en oinois*, where the Hebrew has *Do not look at wine when it is red*.
- **vv21–22, 33 "submitting to one another in the fear of Christ. Wives, submit to your own husbands… the wife must see that she respects her husband"** — v22 has no verb of its own; it carries on v21's *submitting to one another*, and v24 supplies *submit*. The word in v33, *phobētai*, is the verb of *the fear of Christ* in v21, so the passage begins and ends on the same word.
- **vv31–32 "the two will become one flesh. This mystery is great"** — Genesis 2:24 in the Greek, which adds *the two*, as Jesus quotes it (Matthew 19:5). Paul reads the first marriage, the man and the woman made from him, as the pattern of Christ and the church, *we are parts of His body* (v30).

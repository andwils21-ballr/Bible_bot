# Ephesians 1

## Greeting

**1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, to the saints who are in Ephesus, the faithful in Christ Jesus:

**2** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

## Every Spiritual Blessing in Christ

**3** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places,

**4** even as He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world, to be holy and without defect before Him in love.

**5** He appointed us beforehand for adoption as sons through Jesus Christ, to be His own, according to the good pleasure of His will,

**6** to the praise of the glory of His grace, with which He has graced us in the Beloved.

**7** In Him we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of our trespasses, according to the riches of His grace,

**8** which He lavished on us in all wisdom and understanding,

**9** making known to us the mystery of His will, according to His good pleasure, which He purposed in Him

**10** as a plan for the fullness of the times: to sum up all things in Christ, the things in the heavens and the things on the earth. In Him

**11** we too were made His portion, having been appointed beforehand according to the purpose of Him who works all things according to the counsel of His will,

**12** so that we who were the first to hope in Christ might be to the praise of His glory.

**13** In Him you also, when you heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation, and believed in Him, were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit,

**14** who is the pledge of our inheritance, until the redemption of God's own possession, to the praise of His glory.

## Paul's Prayer

**15** For this reason I too, having heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints,

**16** do not stop giving thanks for you, remembering you in my prayers,

**17** that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give you the Spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of Him,

**18** having the eyes of your heart enlightened, so that you may know what is the hope of His calling, what are the riches of the glory of His inheritance in the saints,

**19** and what is the surpassing greatness of His power toward us who believe, according to the working of His mighty strength,

**20** which He worked in Christ when He raised Him from the dead and seated Him at His right hand in the heavenly places,

**21** far above every ruler and authority and power and dominion, and above every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the age to come.

**22** He put all things under His feet and gave Him as head over all things to the church,

**23** which is His body, the fullness of Him who fills all in all.

## Notes

- **v1 "who are in Ephesus"** — the edition prints *en Ephesō* in square brackets, its mark for words it is not sure belong to the text. The letter itself names no one in the city and sends no greetings.
- **v4 "holy and without defect before Him"** — *amōmos*, the word the Greek Bible uses for an offering: *it must be without defect to be accepted* (Leviticus 22:21), *amōmon* in the Greek. Paul uses it again of the church, presented *holy and without defect* (5:27), and it is the word for Christ, who *offered Himself without defect to God* (Hebrews 9:14).
- **vv4, 6 "He chose us in Him… with which He has graced us in the Beloved"** — *ho ēgapēmenos*, **the Beloved**, is Israel's name in the Greek Bible, where the Hebrew has *Jeshurun*: *Do not fear, Jacob My servant, and the beloved Israel whom I have chosen* (Isaiah 44:2; also Deuteronomy 32:15; 33:5, 26). Here the Beloved is Christ, and the ones *chosen* are chosen in Him. *Echaritōsen*, **graced**, is used once more in the New Testament: Gabriel's *Greetings, favored one!* to Mary (Luke 1:28).
- **v10 "to sum up all things in Christ"** — *anakephalaiōsasthai*, from *kephalaion*, the **sum** or main point, the word of Romans 13:9, where every commandment *is summed up* in *love your neighbor*. *Kephalaion* itself comes from *kephalē*, **head**, and twelve verses on God gives Christ *as head over all things* (v22): all things summed up in Him, or brought together under Him as head.
- **vv11, 14, 18 "we too were made His portion… God's own possession… His inheritance in the saints"** — *eklērōthēmen*, from *klēros*, a **lot** or allotted share, is passive: the believers are made God's share, or are given a share of their own (*our inheritance*, v14). The thought is Moses' song, *the LORD's portion is His people, Jacob the measured lot of His inheritance* (Deuteronomy 32:9), and *peripoiēsis*, **possession**, is Malachi's *They will be Mine… My own treasure* (Malachi 3:17), *eis peripoiēsin* in the Greek. So in v18 the inheritance is God's own, *in the saints*.
- **vv20–22 "seated Him at His right hand… He put all things under His feet"** — two psalms, as in 1 Corinthians 15:25–27: *Sit at My right hand* (Psalm 110:1) and *You have put all things under his feet* (Psalm 8:6), the psalm of what God made a human being to be.

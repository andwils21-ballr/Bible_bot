# Ephesians 4

## One Body, One Spirit

**1** So I, the prisoner in the Lord, urge you to walk in a way worthy of the calling to which you have been called,

**2** with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love,

**3** eager to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.

**4** There is one body and one Spirit, just as you were called in one hope of your calling;

**5** one Lord, one faith, one baptism;

**6** one God and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in all.

**7** But to each one of us grace was given according to the measure of Christ's gift.

**8** That is why it says:  
"When He went up on high, He led captives away,  
and He gave gifts to people."

**9** What does "He went up" mean, but that He had also gone down into the lower parts of the earth?

**10** He who went down is the same one who also went up far above all the heavens, so that He might fill all things.

**11** He Himself gave some as apostles, some as prophets, some as evangelists, some as shepherds and teachers,

**12** to equip the saints for the work of ministry, for building up the body of Christ,

**13** until we all reach the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, a full-grown man, the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ,

**14** so that we may no longer be children, tossed by the waves and carried about by every wind of teaching, by human trickery, by cunning in the schemes of deceit.

**15** Rather, speaking the truth in love, we are to grow up in every way into Him who is the head, into Christ,

**16** from whom the whole body, joined and held together by every joint that supplies it, as each part does its work, makes the body grow so that it builds itself up in love.

## The Old Self and the New

**17** So I say this and testify in the Lord: you must no longer walk as the Gentiles walk, in the emptiness of their minds.

**18** They are darkened in their understanding, shut out from the life of God because of the ignorance that is in them, because of the hardening of their hearts.

**19** Having lost all feeling, they have given themselves over to sensuality, to practice every kind of impurity with greed.

**20** But that is not the way you learned Christ,

**21** if indeed you heard about Him and were taught in Him, as the truth is in Jesus:

**22** to put off your old self, which belongs to your former way of life and is being corrupted by deceitful desires,

**23** to be renewed in the spirit of your minds,

**24** and to put on the new self, created in the likeness of God in true righteousness and holiness.

## Living as the New Self

**25** So, having put away falsehood, speak the truth each one with his neighbor, for we are parts of one another.

**26** "Be angry, and do not sin." Do not let the sun go down on your anger,

**27** and do not give the devil a foothold.

**28** Let the thief no longer steal, but rather let him labor, doing good work with his own hands, so that he may have something to share with anyone in need.

**29** Let no rotten word come out of your mouths, but only what is good for building up, as the need may be, so that it may give grace to those who hear.

**30** Do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.

**31** Let all bitterness and rage and anger and shouting and slander be put away from you, along with all malice.

**32** Be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, as God in Christ forgave you.

## Notes

- **v8 "When He went up on high, He led captives away, and He gave gifts to people"** — Psalm 68:18, where God goes up in triumph to *the mountain God has desired for His dwelling* (68:16): *You led captives away; You received gifts among people*. The Hebrew has *received*, *laqachta*, and so does the Greek, *elabes*. Paul writes *edōken*, **He gave**: the conqueror who took tribute gives it out, and v11 names the gifts, the apostles, prophets, evangelists, shepherds and teachers.
- **vv9–10 "He had also gone down into the lower parts of the earth"** — *ta katōtera merē tēs gēs*. The Greek Psalms use the phrase both ways: for the grave, *those who seek my life… will go down into the depths of the earth* (Psalm 63:9), and for the womb, *intricately woven in the depths of the earth* (Psalm 139:15). So Paul's words can mean that He came down to the earth itself, or that He went down beneath it, to the dead.
- **v26 "Be angry, and do not sin"** — Psalm 4:4 in the Greek, *orgizesthe kai mē hamartanete*. The Hebrew *rigzu* is wider, **tremble**, with fear or with anger: *Tremble, and do not sin*.

# Zephaniah 1

## I Will Sweep Away Everything

**1** The word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah.

**2** I will utterly sweep away everything  
from the face of the ground,  
declares the LORD.

**3** I will sweep away man and beast;  
I will sweep away the birds of the heavens  
and the fish of the sea,  
and the stumbling blocks with the wicked;  
I will cut off humankind  
from the face of the ground,  
declares the LORD.

**4** I will stretch out My hand against Judah  
and against all the inhabitants of Jerusalem;  
I will cut off from this place the remnant of Baal,  
the name of the idol priests with the priests,

**5** those who bow down on the rooftops  
to the host of the heavens,  
and those who bow down and swear to the LORD  
and swear by Milcom,

**6** those who turn back from following the LORD,  
who have not sought the LORD  
or inquired of Him.

## The Day of the LORD's Sacrifice

**7** Silence before the Lord GOD!  
For the day of the LORD is near;  
the LORD has prepared a sacrifice;  
He has consecrated those He invited.

**8** And it shall come to pass on the day of the LORD's sacrifice  
that I will punish the princes and the king's sons  
and all who dress in foreign clothing.

**9** On that day I will punish  
everyone who leaps over the threshold,  
who fill their master's house  
with violence and deceit.

**10** And it shall come to pass on that day, declares the LORD,  
that there will be the sound of a cry from the Fish Gate,  
a wail from the Second Quarter,  
and a great crash from the hills.

**11** Wail, you who live in the Hollow,  
for all the trading people are ruined;  
all who weigh out silver are cut off.

**12** And it shall come to pass at that time  
that I will search Jerusalem with lamps,  
and punish the men who are settled on their dregs,  
who say in their hearts,  
"The LORD will not do good, nor will He do harm."

**13** Their wealth will become spoil,  
and their houses a desolation;  
they will build houses but not live in them,  
and plant vineyards but not drink their wine.

## A Day of Fury

**14** The great day of the LORD is near,  
near and hurrying fast.  
The sound of the day of the LORD!  
The warrior cries out bitterly there.

**15** That day is a day of fury,  
a day of distress and anguish,  
a day of waste and desolation,  
a day of darkness and gloom,  
a day of cloud and thick darkness,

**16** a day of the horn and the battle cry  
against the fortified cities  
and against the high towers.

**17** I will bring distress on humankind,  
and they will walk like the blind,  
for they have sinned against the LORD;  
their blood will be poured out like dust,  
and their flesh like dung.

**18** Neither their silver nor their gold  
will be able to rescue them  
in the day of the LORD's fury;  
in the fire of His jealousy  
the whole earth will be consumed;  
for He will make a full end, a sudden one,  
of all the inhabitants of the earth.

## Notes

- **v1 "Zephaniah son of Cushi… son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon"** — the line goes back four generations, to a Hezekiah; the verse does not say whether he is King Hezekiah. *Tsefanyah*, **Zephaniah**, has the letters of *tsafan*, **hide** or **treasure**, with the LORD's name. Josiah began to reign as a boy, *eight years old* (2 Kings 22:1), and swept out the idol priests and the rooftop altars this chapter names (2 Kings 23:5, 12).
- **vv2–3 "I will utterly sweep away everything from the face of the ground… man and beast… the birds of the heavens and the fish of the sea"** — *asof asef*, word for word **sweeping away I will sweep away**. The list runs the creation backward, from man to the fish, and takes the flood's words, *I will wipe away the human whom I created from upon the face of the ground — from human to beast, to creeping thing, and to the bird of the heavens* (Genesis 6:7). Hosea uses the same verb of the land, *even the fish of the sea are taken away* (Hosea 4:3). *Adam… adamah*, **humankind… the ground**, sound alike. *Ha-makhshelot et ha-resha'im*, **the stumbling blocks with the wicked**, is obscure; *mikhshol* is the *stumbling block of their iniquity*, their idols (Ezekiel 14:3).
- **vv4–6 "the remnant of Baal, the name of the idol priests… swear to the LORD and swear by Milcom"** — *kemarim*, **idol priests**, are those Josiah *put down* (2 Kings 23:5) and those of the calf (Hosea 10:5). The rooftop worship is Jeremiah's *houses on whose roofs they burned incense to all the host of heaven* (Jeremiah 19:13). *Malkam* is read as **Milcom**, the god of the Ammonites, *Milcom the loathsome thing of the Ammonites* (1 Kings 11:5), as in Jeremiah 49:1; the same letters say **their king**, and the Greek has *their king*. They swear by the LORD and by Milcom at once.
- **vv7–8 "Silence before the Lord GOD!… the LORD has prepared a sacrifice; He has consecrated those He invited"** — *has*, **silence**, as in Habakkuk 2:20. The feast is turned around: the guests are called to a sacrifice that is themselves. *Hiqdish qeru'av*, **consecrated those He invited**, as Samuel *consecrated Jesse and his sons and invited them to the sacrifice* (1 Samuel 16:5), and *those who are invited* eat after the blessing (1 Samuel 9:13). The LORD's sacrifice is a slaughter, as in *the LORD has a sacrifice in Bozrah* (Isaiah 34:6) and *in the land of the north* (Jeremiah 46:10).
- **v9 "everyone who leaps over the threshold… their master's house"** — the priests of Dagon *do not tread on the threshold of Dagon* (1 Samuel 5:5); here those who leap over it are punished. *Beit adoneihem*, **their master's house**, may be a king's house, filled by servants with plunder, or a temple; the Greek has *the house of the Lord their God*.
- **vv10–11 "the Fish Gate… the Second Quarter… the Hollow… all the trading people"** — places of Jerusalem: *the Fish Gate* (Nehemiah 3:3), and *the Second Quarter*, where the prophetess Huldah lived in Josiah's day (2 Kings 22:14). *Ha-makhtesh*, **the Hollow**, word for word **the mortar**, the word of *the hollow place that is at Lehi* (Judges 15:19). *Am Kena'an* is word for word **the people of Canaan**; *Kena'an* is also **trader**, as in *A trader with false scales in his hand* (Hosea 12:7), and the next line names them, *all who weigh out silver*.
- **vv12–13 "I will search Jerusalem with lamps… settled on their dregs… The LORD will not do good, nor will He do harm"** — *achappes*, **I will search**, as *Esau has been searched out* (Obadiah 6). *Qof'im al shimreihem*, **settled on their dregs**, is wine left undisturbed, as Moab is *settled on his dregs; he has not been poured from vessel to vessel* (Jeremiah 48:11). Their creed is that the LORD does nothing, as the idols *can do no harm, and neither is it in them to do good* (Jeremiah 10:5). The curse is Amos's, *you have built houses of dressed stone, but you will not live in them; you have planted pleasant vineyards, but you will not drink their wine* (Amos 5:11).
- **vv14–16 "a day of fury… a day of darkness and gloom, a day of cloud and thick darkness… a day of the horn and the battle cry"** — *yom*, **day**, seven times in vv15–16. *Evrah*, **fury**, as in Habakkuk 3:8. *Tsarah u-metsuqah*, **distress and anguish**, as in *Distress and anguish terrify him* (Job 15:24), and *sho'ah u-mesho'ah*, **waste and desolation**, two words of one sound, as in Job 30:3. *A day of darkness and gloom, a day of cloud and thick darkness* is Joel 2:2 word for word, and the English is Joel's. *Tsoreach*, **cries out**, is found again only of the LORD Himself, *He raises the battle cry* (Isaiah 42:13). *Teru'ah*, **battle cry**, with the horn, as in Amos 2:2.
- **vv17–18 "they will walk like the blind… Neither their silver nor their gold will be able to rescue them in the day of the LORD's fury"** — the curse of the law, *You will grope at noon as the blind man gropes in darkness* (Deuteronomy 28:29). *Ke-gelalim*, **like dung**, as in *as one sweeps away dung* (1 Kings 14:10). The silver and gold line is Ezekiel's, *their silver and their gold will not be able to rescue them in the day of the LORD's fury* (Ezekiel 7:19). *The fire of His jealousy*: *the LORD your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God* (Deuteronomy 4:24). *Kol ha-arets*, **the whole earth**, may also be **the whole land**; v2 spoke of *the face of the ground*.

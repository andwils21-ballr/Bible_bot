# Zephaniah 2

## Seek the LORD

**1** Gather yourselves together, yes, gather,  
O nation without shame,

**2** before the decree gives birth —  
the day passes like chaff —  
before the burning anger of the LORD comes upon you,  
before the day of the LORD's anger comes upon you.

**3** Seek the LORD, all you humble of the land,  
who do His justice;  
seek righteousness, seek humility;  
perhaps you will be hidden  
on the day of the LORD's anger.

## Against the Philistines

**4** For Gaza will be forsaken,  
and Ashkelon a desolation;  
Ashdod will be driven out at noon,  
and Ekron uprooted.

**5** Woe to the inhabitants of the seacoast,  
the nation of the Cherethites!  
The word of the LORD is against you,  
Canaan, land of the Philistines:  
I will destroy you until no inhabitant is left.

**6** The seacoast will be pastures,  
with meadows for shepherds  
and folds for flocks.

**7** The coast will belong  
to the remnant of the house of Judah;  
they will graze on it;  
in the houses of Ashkelon  
they will lie down at evening;  
for the LORD their God will visit them  
and restore their fortunes.

## Against Moab and Ammon

**8** I have heard the scorn of Moab  
and the revilings of the sons of Ammon,  
how they scorned My people  
and boasted against their border.

**9** Therefore, as I live,  
declares the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel,  
Moab will become like Sodom,  
and the sons of Ammon like Gomorrah,  
a land possessed by nettles and salt pits,  
a desolation forever;  
the remnant of My people will plunder them,  
and the rest of My nation will inherit them.

**10** This they will have for their pride,  
because they scorned and boasted  
against the people of the LORD of hosts.

**11** The LORD will be fearsome against them,  
for He will starve all the gods of the earth;  
and they will bow down to Him, each from his place,  
all the coastlands of the nations.

## Against Cush and Assyria

**12** You too, Cushites —  
they are slain by My sword.

**13** He will stretch out His hand against the north  
and destroy Assyria,  
and He will make Nineveh a desolation,  
dry as the wilderness.

**14** Herds will lie down inside her,  
every kind of beast;  
the pelican and the hedgehog  
will lodge in her capitals;  
a voice will sing in the window;  
desolation will be on the threshold;  
for the cedar work is laid bare.

**15** This is the exultant city  
that sat secure,  
that said in her heart,  
"I am, and there is none besides me."  
How she has become a desolation,  
a lair for wild beasts!  
Everyone who passes by her  
will hiss and shake his hand.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "Gather yourselves together, yes, gather, O nation without shame… the day passes like chaff"** — *hitqoshshu va-qoshu*, **gather yourselves… gather**, is the verb of gathering stubble, *qash*: the nation is told to gather as straw is gathered. *Lo nikhsaf*, **without shame**, from *kasaf*, which is **long for**, as in *you deeply longed for your father's house* (Genesis 31:30): the nation may be one **not longed for**, or one that does not grow pale with shame. The Greek has *the undisciplined nation*. v2 is partly obscure: *be-terem ledet choq*, **before the decree gives birth**. The chaff is Hosea's, *like chaff that is whirled from the threshing floor* (Hosea 13:3).
- **vv14–15 "the pelican and the hedgehog… I am, and there is none besides me… Everyone who passes by her will hiss"** — *qa'at ve-qippod*, **the pelican and the hedgehog**, are the creatures of ruined Edom in Isaiah, *The pelican and the hedgehog will possess it* (Isaiah 34:11). *Kaftoreha*, **her capitals**, the tops of pillars, as in *Strike the capitals* (Amos 9:1). The rest of v14 is obscure: *chorev ba-saf*, **desolation on the threshold**, where the Greek has *ravens*, *orev*, nearly the same letters; *arzah*, **cedar work**, is found only here. Nineveh's boast is Babylon's in Isaiah, *who sits secure, who says in her heart, "I am, and there is none besides me"* (Isaiah 47:8). *Ha-alizah*, **exultant**, as in *an exultant town* (Isaiah 22:2). The hissing is that of ruins, *everyone who passes by it will be horrified and will hiss* (Jeremiah 49:17).

# Zephaniah 3

## Woe to the Oppressing City

**1** Woe to her who rebels and is defiled,  
the oppressing city!

**2** She has not listened to the voice;  
she has not taken correction;  
she has not trusted in the LORD;  
she has not drawn near to her God.

**3** Her princes within her are roaring lions;  
her judges are wolves of the evening  
that leave nothing for the morning.

**4** Her prophets are reckless,  
men of treachery;  
her priests have profaned what is holy;  
they have done violence to the law.

**5** The LORD within her is righteous;  
He does no injustice.  
Morning by morning He brings His justice to light;  
He does not fail.  
But the unjust knows no shame.

**6** I have cut off nations;  
their towers are desolate;  
I have laid waste their streets,  
with no one passing through;  
their cities are devastated,  
with no man, no inhabitant.

**7** I said, "Surely you will fear Me;  
you will take correction";  
then her dwelling would not be cut off  
for all that I had appointed against her.  
But they rose early to ruin all their deeds.

**8** Therefore wait for Me, declares the LORD,  
for the day I rise up to the prey;  
for My decision is to gather nations,  
to assemble kingdoms,  
to pour out on them My indignation,  
all My burning anger;  
for in the fire of My jealousy  
the whole earth will be consumed.

## A Pure Lip

**9** For then I will change the peoples to a pure lip,  
so that all of them may call on the name of the LORD  
and serve Him shoulder to shoulder.

**10** From beyond the rivers of Cush  
those who plead with Me, the daughter of My scattered ones,  
will bring My offering.

**11** On that day you will not be put to shame  
for all your deeds by which you rebelled against Me;  
for then I will remove from within you  
those who exult in your pride,  
and you will no longer be haughty  
on My holy mountain.

**12** I will leave within you  
a people humble and poor,  
and they will take refuge in the name of the LORD.

**13** The remnant of Israel will do no injustice  
and speak no lies,  
and no deceitful tongue will be found in their mouths;  
for they will graze and lie down,  
with no one to make them afraid.

## Sing, Daughter of Zion

**14** Sing aloud, daughter of Zion;  
shout, Israel!  
Rejoice and exult with all your heart,  
daughter of Jerusalem!

**15** The LORD has taken away the judgments against you;  
He has turned away your enemy.  
The King of Israel, the LORD, is within you;  
you will never again fear evil.

**16** On that day it will be said to Jerusalem:  
"Do not fear, Zion;  
let not your hands go limp.

**17** The LORD your God is within you,  
a warrior who saves;  
He will rejoice over you with gladness;  
He will be silent in His love;  
He will exult over you with singing."

**18** Those who grieve, away from the appointed feast, I will gather;  
they were from you,  
a burden on her, a reproach.

**19** I am about to deal with all who afflict you  
at that time;  
I will save the lame  
and gather the outcast,  
and I will make them a praise and a name  
in all the earth where they were put to shame.

**20** At that time I will bring you in,  
at the time when I gather you;  
for I will make you a name and a praise  
among all the peoples of the earth,  
when I restore your fortunes before your eyes,  
says the LORD.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "Woe to her who rebels and is defiled, the oppressing city!… she has not taken correction"** — the city is not named until v14: after the woes on the nations (chapter 2), Jerusalem is next. *Nig'alah*, **defiled**, as in *your hands are defiled with blood* (Isaiah 59:3). *Ha-ir ha-yonah*, **the oppressing city**, as in *the oppressing sword* (Jeremiah 46:16); *yonah* is also **dove**, the name of Jonah, and the Greek has *the dove*. *Lo laqechah musar*, **she has not taken correction**, are Jeremiah's words, *This is the nation that did not obey the voice of the LORD its God and did not take correction* (Jeremiah 7:28). In Swete's Greek this chapter begins one verse earlier, with 2:15.
- **vv3–4 "Her princes within her are roaring lions… her priests have profaned what is holy; they have done violence to the law"** — the same four, princes, judges, prophets and priests, stand in Ezekiel's charge against Jerusalem: *Her priests have done violence to My law and profaned My holy things* (Ezekiel 22:26), *Her princes in her midst are like wolves tearing prey* (Ezekiel 22:27). *Wolves of the evening* as in Habakkuk 1:8. *Lo garmu la-boqer*, **leave nothing for the morning**, word for word **gnaw no bone for the morning**: *garam* is the verb of *crush their bones* (Numbers 24:8). *Pochazim*, **reckless**, as in *worthless and reckless men* (Judges 9:4).
- **v5 "The LORD within her is righteous… Morning by morning He brings His justice to light… But the unjust knows no shame"** — *be-qirbah*, **within her**, the same word as *her princes within her* (v3): the LORD and the princes are in one city, and only one of them does justice. *Lo ya'aseh avlah*, **He does no injustice**, as in *A God of faithfulness and without wrong* (Deuteronomy 32:4), and the remnant will be the same (v13). *Lo ne'dar*, **He does not fail**, as in *not one is missing* (Isaiah 40:26). The unjust, like Jeremiah's people, *did not know how to blush* (Jeremiah 6:15).
- **vv6–8 "they rose early to ruin all their deeds… for the day I rise up to the prey"** — *hishkimu hishchitu*, **they rose early to ruin**: what they were eager for was ruin, as when *all flesh had ruined its way upon the earth* (Genesis 6:12). *Le-ad*, **to the prey**, as pointed, the word of Benjamin's blessing, *In the morning he eats the prey* (Genesis 49:27); the same letters read *le-ed*, **as a witness**, and the Greek has *for a witness*, as in *let the Lord GOD be a witness against you* (Micah 1:2). *In the fire of My jealousy the whole earth will be consumed* is 1:18 again, now in the LORD's own mouth.
- **vv9–10 "I will change the peoples to a pure lip… From beyond the rivers of Cush"** — *safah*, **lip**, is the word of Babel, *All the earth was one lip… let us confuse their lip there* (Genesis 11:1, 7): the confusion is undone, and the one lip is pure. *Ehpokh*, **I will change**, is *hafakh*, the overturning of Jonah 3:4. *Shekhem echad*, word for word **one shoulder**, **shoulder to shoulder**. *Me-ever le-naharei Khush*, **from beyond the rivers of Cush**, is Isaiah's phrase (Isaiah 18:1), where *a gift will be brought to the LORD of hosts* (Isaiah 18:7). *Atarai*, **those who plead with Me**, is the verb of Isaac, who *pleaded with the LORD* (Genesis 25:21).
- **vv11–13 "those who exult in your pride… a people humble and poor… with no one to make them afraid"** — *alizei ga'avatekh*, **those who exult in your pride**, are Isaiah's words for the LORD's own warriors, *those who exult in My majesty* (Isaiah 13:3), turned on Jerusalem's proud; *alizah*, **exultant**, was Nineveh's word (2:15). *Le-govhah*, **be haughty**, as in *Do not keep talking so very proudly* (1 Samuel 2:3). The remnant is the *humble* of 2:3, who take refuge in the LORD as in Nahum 1:7. *Leshon tarmit*, **a deceitful tongue**, as in *the deceit of their own hearts* (Jeremiah 14:14). *Ve-ein macharid*, **with no one to make them afraid**, is Micah 4:4.
- **vv14–15 "Sing aloud, daughter of Zion… The King of Israel, the LORD, is within you"** — *ronni*, **sing aloud**, as in *Sing, barren one* (Isaiah 54:1); *hari'u*, **shout**, is the verb of the alarm in Joel 2:1, now a shout of joy. The *judgments* taken away are the sentences of chapters 1 and 3. *The LORD… within you* answers the false boast, *Is not the LORD among us?* (Micah 3:11), and the LORD *within her* who saw her injustice (v5). *You will never again fear evil*, as in *I fear no evil, for You are with me* (Psalm 23:4).
- **vv16–17 "let not your hands go limp… a warrior who saves… He will be silent in His love"** — *al yirpu yadayikh*, **let not your hands go limp**, as Ishbosheth's *hands went limp* at Abner's death (2 Samuel 4:1). *Gibbor yoshia*, **a warrior who saves**: the *warrior* who cried out bitterly on the day of the LORD (1:14) is answered by the LORD as warrior. *Yasis alayikh*, **He will rejoice over you**, as in *so your God will rejoice over you* (Isaiah 62:5). *Yacharish be-ahavato*, **He will be silent in His love**, is the verb of Habakkuk's *keep silent* (Habakkuk 1:13), now love's stillness; the Greek has *He will renew you in His love*, *yechaddesh*, nearly the same letters.
- **vv18–20 "Those who grieve, away from the appointed feast… I will save the lame and gather the outcast… a name and a praise"** — v18 is obscure; its words are those of Lamentations, *no one comes to the appointed feasts… her virgins are grieving* (Lamentations 1:4). v19 is Micah's promise, *I will gather the lame and assemble the outcast* (Micah 4:6). *A praise and a name* is the law's, *for praise and for a name and for glory* (Deuteronomy 26:19). *Restore your fortunes* as in 2:7. The book that opened with the LORD sweeping away everything (1:2) ends with Him gathering.

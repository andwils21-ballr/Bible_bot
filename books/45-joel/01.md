# Joel 1

## What the Locust Left

**1** The word of the LORD that came to Joel son of Pethuel.

**2** Hear this, you elders;  
give ear, all inhabitants of the land!  
Has such a thing happened in your days,  
or in the days of your fathers?

**3** Tell your children of it,  
and let your children tell their children,  
and their children another generation.

**4** What the cutting locust left,  
the locust has eaten;  
what the locust left,  
the young locust has eaten;  
and what the young locust left,  
the grasshopper has eaten.

## Wake Up and Weep

**5** Wake up, you drunkards, and weep;  
wail, all you drinkers of wine,  
over the sweet wine,  
for it is cut off from your mouth.

**6** For a nation has come up against my land,  
mighty and without number;  
its teeth are the teeth of a lion,  
and it has the jaws of a lioness.

**7** It has made my vine a waste  
and my fig tree splinters;  
it has utterly stripped it bare and thrown it down;  
its tendrils have turned white.

**8** Lament like a virgin girded with sackcloth  
for the husband of her youth.

**9** Grain offering and drink offering are cut off  
from the house of the LORD;  
the priests mourn,  
the ministers of the LORD.

**10** The field is devastated,  
the ground mourns;  
for the grain is devastated,  
the new wine dries up,  
the oil wastes away.

**11** Be ashamed, you farmers;  
wail, you vinedressers,  
over the wheat and over the barley,  
for the harvest of the field has perished.

**12** The vine dries up,  
and the fig tree wastes away;  
pomegranate, palm too, and apple tree,  
all the trees of the field are dried up;  
for joy has dried up  
from the sons of men.

## Proclaim a Solemn Assembly

**13** Gird yourselves and lament, you priests;  
wail, you ministers of the altar;  
come, spend the night in sackcloth,  
you ministers of my God;  
for grain offering and drink offering are withheld  
from the house of your God.

**14** Consecrate a fast;  
proclaim a solemn assembly;  
gather the elders,  
all the inhabitants of the land,  
to the house of the LORD your God,  
and cry out to the LORD.

## The Day of the LORD Is Near

**15** Alas for the day!  
For the day of the LORD is near,  
and it will come like destruction from the Almighty.

**16** Is not the food cut off  
before our eyes,  
joy and gladness  
from the house of our God?

**17** The seeds shrivel under their clods;  
the storehouses are desolate;  
the granaries are torn down,  
for the grain has dried up.

**18** How the beasts groan!  
The herds of cattle wander in confusion,  
because they have no pasture;  
even the flocks of sheep bear the guilt.

**19** To You, LORD, I call,  
for fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness,  
and flame has burned up all the trees of the field.

**20** Even the beasts of the field long for You,  
for the streams of water are dried up,  
and fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness.

## Notes

- **v1 "The word of the LORD that came to Joel son of Pethuel"** — Joel names no king and no year, where Hosea and Amos name both (Hosea 1:1; Amos 1:1). *Yo'el*, **Joel**, is also the name of Samuel's firstborn (1 Samuel 8:2). For Pethuel the Greek has *Bathouel*, the Greek spelling of Bethuel, Rebekah's father (Genesis 24:15).
- **vv17–20 "The seeds shrivel… even the flocks of sheep bear the guilt… the beasts of the field long for You"** — v17 is obscure: *avshu*, **shrivel**, *megrefot*, **clods**, and *mammegurot*, **granaries**, are found only here; the Greek has *the heifers leaped at their mangers*. *Navokhu*, **wander in confusion**, is what Pharaoh said of Israel in the wilderness (Exodus 14:3). *Ne'shamu*, **bear the guilt**, is the verb of *now they must bear their guilt* (Hosea 10:2): the flocks suffer for the guilt of men. The Greek has *were destroyed*, as if from *nashammu*, **are desolate** (v17), nearly the same letters. *Ta'arog*, **long for**, is the deer of *As a deer longs for streams of water, so my soul longs for You, God* (Psalm 42:1), with the same *afiqei mayim*, **streams of water**: the beasts pray as the prophet prays (v19).

# Joel 3

## The Valley of Jehoshaphat

**1** For in those days and at that time,  
when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem,

**2** I will gather all the nations  
and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat,  
and I will enter into judgment with them there  
on behalf of My people and My inheritance Israel,  
whom they scattered among the nations;  
and they divided up My land.

**3** They cast lots for My people,  
and gave a boy for a prostitute,  
and sold a girl for wine, and drank it.

**4** What are you to Me, Tyre and Sidon,  
and all the regions of Philistia?  
Are you paying Me back for something?  
If you are doing something to Me,  
swiftly and speedily I will return your deed on your own head.

**5** For you have taken My silver and My gold,  
and carried My finest treasures into your temples.

**6** You sold the sons of Judah and the sons of Jerusalem  
to the sons of the Greeks,  
to remove them far from their own border.

**7** I am about to rouse them from the place  
where you sold them,  
and I will return your deed on your own head.

**8** I will sell your sons and your daughters  
into the hand of the sons of Judah,  
and they will sell them to the people of Sheba,  
to a nation far off;  
for the LORD has spoken.

## Beat Your Plowshares into Swords

**9** Proclaim this among the nations:  
Consecrate war!  
Rouse the warriors!  
Let all the men of war draw near and come up.

**10** Beat your plowshares into swords,  
and your pruning hooks into spears;  
let the weak say, "I am a warrior."

**11** Hurry and come, all you nations around,  
and gather yourselves there.  
Bring down Your warriors, LORD!

**12** Let the nations be roused and come up  
to the Valley of Jehoshaphat;  
for there I will sit to judge  
all the nations around.

**13** Put in the sickle,  
for the harvest is ripe.  
Come, tread,  
for the winepress is full;  
the vats overflow,  
for their evil is great.

**14** Multitudes, multitudes,  
in the valley of decision!  
For the day of the LORD is near  
in the valley of decision.

**15** The sun and the moon grow dark,  
and the stars withdraw their shining.

## The LORD Dwells in Zion

**16** The LORD roars from Zion  
and utters His voice from Jerusalem,  
and the heavens and the earth shake;  
but the LORD is a refuge for His people,  
a stronghold for the sons of Israel.

**17** You will know that I am the LORD your God,  
who dwells in Zion, My holy mountain;  
Jerusalem will be holy,  
and strangers will no longer pass through her.

**18** And it shall come to pass on that day  
that the mountains will drip sweet wine,  
and the hills will flow with milk,  
and all the streams of Judah will flow with water;  
a spring will come out from the house of the LORD  
and water the valley of Shittim.

**19** Egypt will become a desolation,  
and Edom a desolate wilderness,  
because of the violence done to the sons of Judah,  
for they shed innocent blood in their land.

**20** But Judah will be inhabited forever,  
and Jerusalem for generation after generation.

**21** I will hold their blood innocent,  
which I had not held innocent;  
and the LORD dwells in Zion.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "when I restore the fortunes… the Valley of Jehoshaphat… I will enter into judgment with them there"** — in the Hebrew text this chapter is chapter 4; the English numbering is followed. *Ashiv et shevut*, **restore the fortunes**, is the law's promise, *the LORD your God will restore your fortunes… and gather you again from all the peoples* (Deuteronomy 30:3), and Hosea's, *a harvest is appointed, when I restore the fortunes of My people* (Hosea 6:11). *Yehoshafat*, **Jehoshaphat**, is the LORD's name joined to *shafat*, **judge**, and the verb follows twice: *ve-nishpatti*, **I will enter into judgment** (v2), and *lishpot*, **to judge** (v12). The valley is named for what happens in it, as the valley of Jehoshaphat's own victory was named *the Valley of Beracah*, for there they blessed the LORD (2 Chronicles 20:26).
- **vv2–3 "they divided up My land… gave a boy for a prostitute, and sold a girl for wine"** — the charge is in the LORD's possessives: *My people*, *My inheritance*, *My land*. Lots were cast for captives, and a child went for one night's prostitute or one drink. *Zonah*, **prostitute**, is the plain word.
- **vv4–6 "What are you to Me, Tyre and Sidon… to the sons of the Greeks"** — *gemul*, **deed**, is what a man does to another and what comes back on him: they treat their raids as repayment, and the LORD will pay back the deed itself. *Galilot Pelashet*, **the regions of Philistia**, are *all the regions of the Philistines* of Joshua 13:2. *Bnei ha-Yevanim*, **the sons of the Greeks**, are Javan's descendants (Genesis 10:2), and Ezekiel names the same trade at Tyre: *Javan, Tubal and Meshech traded with you; human beings and vessels of bronze they gave for your merchandise* (Ezekiel 27:13). *Heikhaleikhem*, **your temples**, is the word for a palace and a temple; the Greek has *your temples*.
- **vv7–8 "I am about to rouse them… they will sell them to the people of Sheba"** — the slave trade is turned around: the captives come back, and the sellers' own children are sold *to a nation far off*, as Judah's were sold *far from their own border*. *Shevayim*, **the people of Sheba**, are the raiders of Job's story, *Sheba fell on them and took them* (Job 1:15). *For the LORD has spoken* closes Isaiah's promise in the same words (Isaiah 25:8).
- **vv9–10 "Consecrate war!… Beat your plowshares into swords"** — *qaddeshu milchamah*, **consecrate war**, the summons of a holy war, as in *Consecrate war against her!* (Jeremiah 6:4). Then the promise of Isaiah and Micah run backward: there *they shall beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruning hooks* (Micah 4:3; Isaiah 2:4); here the nations are told to beat their tools into weapons. Joel's word for *spears* is *romach*, the spear of Phinehas (Numbers 25:7), not the *chanit* of Isaiah and Micah. *Ha'iru*, **rouse**, is the verb of v7 and v12.
- **v11 "Hurry and come… Bring down Your warriors, LORD!"** — *ushu* is found only here, and its sense, **hurry** or **come to help**, is guessed from the line. The last words are the prophet's prayer, *hanchat YHWH gibborekha*; for them the Greek has *let the meek one be a warrior*.
- **vv13–14 "Put in the sickle… for their evil is great… Multitudes, multitudes, in the valley of decision!"** — harvest and vintage together, with the nations as the crop. *Rabbah ra'atam*, **their evil is great**, are the words of the flood, *the evil of humankind was great in the earth* (Genesis 6:5). *Redu*, **tread**, can be from *radah*, to tread down or rule, or *yarad*, **go down**; the Greek has *tread*. *Ha-charuts*, **decision**, is a thing cut or decided, as in *you yourself have decided it* (1 Kings 20:40), and also *a threshing sledge, new, sharp* (Isaiah 41:15), which fits the harvest; the Greek has *the valley of judgment*.
- **vv16–17 "The LORD roars from Zion… a refuge for His people… strangers will no longer pass through her"** — the first two lines are word for word Amos's first oracle, *The LORD roars from Zion and utters His voice from Jerusalem* (Amos 1:2); there the pastures mourned, here the LORD is a *machaseh*, **refuge**, as in *God is our refuge and strength* (Psalm 46:1), and a *ma'oz*, **stronghold**, as in *the LORD is the stronghold of my life* (Psalm 27:1). *You will know that I am the LORD your God* repeats 2:27. Isaiah gives Zion the same promise, *the uncircumcised and the unclean will no longer enter you* (Isaiah 52:1).
- **v18 "the mountains will drip sweet wine… a spring will come out from the house of the LORD"** — the closing words of Amos again, *the mountains will drip sweet wine* (Amos 9:13), and *asis*, the **sweet wine** the drunkards lost (1:5). The *afiqim*, **streams**, that dried up (1:20) run with water. The spring from the house is Ezekiel's river, *water was flowing out from under the threshold of the house* (Ezekiel 47:1). *Shittim* are acacias, the wood of the ark, *an ark of acacia wood* (Exodus 25:10), and the name of Israel's last camp before the Jordan (Micah 6:5); the Greek has *the torrent of the reeds*.
- **vv19–21 "they shed innocent blood… I will hold their blood innocent, which I had not held innocent"** — *dam naqi*, **innocent blood**, as in *so that innocent blood is not shed within your land* (Deuteronomy 19:10), and v21 answers with the same root: *niqqeiti*, **I hold innocent**, is the word of *I am innocent* (Jeremiah 2:35). As the Hebrew stands, the LORD declares innocent the blood of Judah that had gone unanswered. The Greek has *I will seek out their blood, and I will not hold guiltless*. *In their land* may be Judah's land or Egypt's and Edom's; the verse does not say.

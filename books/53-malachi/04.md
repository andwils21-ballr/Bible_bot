# Malachi 4

## The Day Is Coming

**1** "For the day is coming, burning like an oven, when all the arrogant and all who do wickedness will be stubble. The day that is coming will set them ablaze," says the LORD of hosts, "so that it will leave them neither root nor branch.

**2** But for you who fear My name the sun of righteousness will rise, with healing in its wings; you will go out and leap like calves from the stall.

**3** You will tread down the wicked, for they will be ashes under the soles of your feet on the day that I am preparing," says the LORD of hosts.

## Remember the Instruction of Moses

**4** "Remember the instruction of Moses My servant, which I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel, statutes and rules.

**5** I am sending you Elijah the prophet before the great and fearful day of the LORD comes.

**6** He will turn the heart of fathers to sons and the heart of sons to their fathers, so that I will not come and strike the land and doom it to destruction."

## Notes

- **v1 "For the day is coming, burning like an oven… neither root nor branch"** — in the Hebrew text vv1–6 are 3:19–24, the end of chapter 3; the English numbering is followed, and the Greek also has a chapter 4. *Tannur*, **oven**. *Qash*, **stubble**, as in Obadiah, *the house of Esau stubble* (Obadiah 1:18), and Isaiah, *They have become like stubble; the fire burns them* (Isaiah 47:14). *Neither root nor branch*: nothing left to grow again. *The arrogant*, *zedim*, are the ones called blessed in 3:15.
- **v2 "the sun of righteousness will rise, with healing in its wings… like calves from the stall"** — *shemesh tsedaqah*, **the sun of righteousness**; *its wings* are the sun's rays, and the Hebrew *its* is feminine, the sun's own. The Greek has *a sun of righteousness*, *with healing in its wings*. *Ufishtem*, **leap**, of calves let out of the stall.
- **v3 "ashes under the soles of your feet on the day that I am preparing"** — *the day that I am preparing*, the words of 3:17.
- **v4 "Remember the instruction of Moses My servant… at Horeb"** — *torat Moshe*, **the instruction of Moses**, as in *the book of the instruction of Moses* (Joshua 8:31). *Horeb* is Deuteronomy's name for Sinai, *the day you stood before the LORD your God at Horeb* (Deuteronomy 4:10). *Avdi*, **My servant**. The Greek puts this verse last, after v6.
- **vv5–6 "I am sending you Elijah the prophet before the great and fearful day of the LORD comes. He will turn the heart of fathers to sons"** — Elijah did not die but *went up in a whirlwind to heaven* (2 Kings 2:11). *The great and fearful day of the LORD* are Joel's words (Joel 2:31). The Greek has *Elijah the Tishbite* (1 Kings 17:1) and *the heart of a father to a son and the heart of a man to his neighbor*. Jesus says of John the Baptist, *he is Elijah who is to come* (Matthew 11:14), and *Elijah does come, and he will restore all things* (Matthew 17:11). *Cherem*, **doom it to destruction**, the word of Zechariah 14:11.

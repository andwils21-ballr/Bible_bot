# Malachi 1

## Jacob and Esau

**1** The oracle. The word of the LORD to Israel through Malachi.

**2** "I have loved you," says the LORD. But you say, "How have You loved us?" "Was not Esau Jacob's brother?" declares the LORD. "Yet I loved Jacob,

**3** but Esau I hated; I have made his mountains a desolation and given his inheritance to the jackals of the wilderness."

**4** If Edom says, "We are shattered, but we will rebuild the ruins," this is what the LORD of hosts says: "They may build, but I will tear down. They will be called the Territory of Wickedness, the people with whom the LORD is indignant forever."

**5** Your own eyes will see it, and you will say, "The LORD is great beyond the territory of Israel!"

## Blemished Offerings

**6** "A son honors his father, and a servant his master. If I am a father, where is My honor? If I am a master, where is the fear of Me?" says the LORD of hosts to you, priests, who despise My name. But you say, "How have we despised Your name?"

**7** "By offering defiled food on My altar." But you say, "How have we defiled You?" "By saying that the table of the LORD may be despised.

**8** When you offer the blind for sacrifice — 'Nothing wrong!' When you offer the lame and the sick — 'Nothing wrong!' Offer it, please, to your governor; will he be pleased with you, or show you favor?" says the LORD of hosts.

**9** "So now, plead with God to be gracious to us. With such offerings from your hands, will He show favor to any of you?" says the LORD of hosts.

**10** "Oh, that one of you would shut the doors, so that you would not light My altar for nothing! I have no pleasure in you," says the LORD of hosts, "and I will not accept an offering from your hands.

**11** For from the rising of the sun to its setting My name is great among the nations; in every place incense is offered to My name, and a pure offering; for My name is great among the nations," says the LORD of hosts.

**12** "But you profane it by saying that the table of the Lord is defiled, and its fruit, its food, may be despised.

**13** You say, 'What a weariness!' and you sniff at it," says the LORD of hosts. "You bring what was stolen, the lame and the sick; this is the offering you bring. Should I accept it from your hands?" says the LORD.

**14** "Cursed is the cheat who has a male in his flock and vows it, but sacrifices a ruined one to the Lord. For I am a great King," says the LORD of hosts, "and My name is feared among the nations."

## Notes

- **v1 "The oracle… through Malachi"** — *massa*, **oracle**, as in Zechariah 9:1 and 12:1. *Be-yad*, word for word **by the hand of**, is **through**. *Mal'akhi*, **Malachi**, means **My messenger**, the word the LORD uses in 3:1; the Greek has *by the hand of His messenger*, and adds *Set it on your hearts*. No father or date is given.
- **vv2–3 "I have loved you… Was not Esau Jacob's brother?… Yet I loved Jacob, but Esau I hated"** — the book is a set of disputes: the LORD says a thing, the people answer *How?* or *In what?*, and He answers them (1:2, 6, 7; 2:14, 17). The twins were two peoples from the womb, *the elder shall serve the younger* (Genesis 25:23). *Loved… hated* is a choice made between brothers, as the law speaks of *the son of the hated wife* (Deuteronomy 21:17). Edom's mountains are made a desolation, as Obadiah foretold for *the violence done to your brother Jacob* (Obadiah 1:10). *Tannot*, **jackals**, as in *a lair of jackals* (Jeremiah 9:11); the Greek has *dwellings*. Paul quotes the verse in Romans 9:13.
- **vv12–14 "its fruit, its food… you sniff at it… a ruined one… I am a great King"** — *nivo*, **its fruit**, is obscure. *Ve-hippachtem oto*, **you sniff at it**, word for word **you blow at it**, in contempt. *Moshchat*, **ruined**, the word the law gives for such animals, *their ruin is in them* (Leviticus 22:25). The law asks for *a male without defect* (Leviticus 1:3). *Melekh gadol*, **a great King**, the title the Assyrian took, *the great king, the king of Assyria* (2 Kings 18:19), given to the LORD.

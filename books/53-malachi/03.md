# Malachi 3

## The Messenger of the Covenant

**1** "I am sending My messenger, and he will clear the way before Me. Then suddenly the Lord whom you seek will come to His temple, and the messenger of the covenant, in whom you delight, is coming," says the LORD of hosts.

**2** But who can endure the day of His coming, and who can stand when He appears? For He is like a refiner's fire and like launderers' soap.

**3** He will sit as a refiner and purifier of silver; He will purify the sons of Levi and refine them like gold and silver, and they will bring offerings to the LORD in righteousness.

**4** Then the offering of Judah and Jerusalem will be pleasing to the LORD, as in the days of old and as in former years.

**5** "Then I will draw near to you for judgment. I will be a swift witness against the sorcerers, against the adulterers, against those who swear falsely, against those who cheat the hired worker of his wages, the widow and the fatherless, and against those who turn aside the guest and do not fear Me," says the LORD of hosts.

## Will a Man Rob God?

**6** "For I, the LORD, do not change; and you, sons of Jacob, have not come to an end.

**7** Since the days of your fathers you have turned aside from My statutes and have not kept them. Return to Me, and I will return to you," says the LORD of hosts. "But you say, 'How shall we return?'

**8** Will a man rob God? Yet you are robbing Me. But you say, 'How have we robbed You?' In the tithe and the contribution.

**9** You are cursed with a curse, for you are robbing Me, the whole nation of you.

**10** Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse, so that there may be food in My house, and test Me in this, please," says the LORD of hosts, "if I will not open for you the windows of the heavens and pour out for you a blessing until there is no more need.

**11** I will rebuke the devourer for you, so that it will not destroy the fruit of your ground, and the vine in your field will not fail to bear," says the LORD of hosts.

**12** "All the nations will call you blessed, for you will be a land of delight," says the LORD of hosts.

## A Book of Remembrance

**13** "Your words have been hard against Me," says the LORD. "But you say, 'What have we said against You?'

**14** You have said, 'It is useless to serve God. What do we gain by keeping His watch and walking as mourners before the LORD of hosts?

**15** So now we call the arrogant blessed. Those who do wickedness are built up; they test God, and they escape.'"

**16** Then those who feared the LORD spoke with one another. The LORD paid attention and heard, and a book of remembrance was written before Him for those who fear the LORD and think on His name.

**17** "They will be Mine," says the LORD of hosts, "My own treasure, on the day that I am preparing; I will spare them as a man spares his son who serves him.

**18** Then you will again see the difference between the righteous and the wicked, between one who serves God and one who does not serve Him.

## Notes

- **v1 "I am sending My messenger, and he will clear the way before Me… the Lord whom you seek… the messenger of the covenant"** — *hineni sholeach mal'akhi*, **I am sending My messenger**, as in *I am sending a messenger before you* (Exodus 23:20); *mal'akhi*, **My messenger**, is the book's name (1:1). *Pinnah derekh*, **clear the way**, as in Isaiah's *prepare the way of the LORD* (Isaiah 40:3). *Ha-adon*, **the Lord**, comes to *His temple*, so He is God; whether *the messenger of the covenant* is the same one, the verse does not say. The Gospels read the first messenger as John the Baptist (Matthew 11:10). The question of 2:17, *Where is the God of justice?*, is answered here.
- **vv2–4 "who can endure the day of His coming… a refiner's fire… launderers' soap… purify the sons of Levi"** — *metsaref*, **refiner**, as in *in vain the refiner refines* (Jeremiah 6:29). *Borit*, **soap**, lye for washing cloth. The priests whose offerings were rejected (1:10, 13) are made clean first, and then their offering *will be pleasing*.
- **v5 "the sorcerers… the adulterers… those who swear falsely… the hired worker… the widow and the fatherless… the guest"** — a list from the law: *A sorceress you shall not let live* (Exodus 22:18); the commandments against adultery and false oaths; *You shall give him his wages on his day* (Deuteronomy 24:15); and the helpless of Zechariah 7:10.
- **vv6–7 "I, the LORD, do not change… Return to Me, and I will return to you"** — the words of Zechariah 1:3, *Return to Me… and I will return to you*. The LORD does not change, and so Jacob's sons are not finished.
- **vv8–9 "Will a man rob God?… In the tithe and the contribution"** — *qava*, **rob**, as in *rob of life those who rob them* (Proverbs 22:23). Its letters are close to Jacob's name, *aqav*, **heel**, and the Greek has *Will a man trip up God?*, *pterniei*, the verb of Esau's complaint, *he has heeled me* (Genesis 27:36): the sons of Jacob act like Jacob. The tithe is the Levites' living, *every tenth in Israel as an inheritance* (Numbers 18:21); *terumah*, **contribution**, as in *let them take up a contribution for Me* (Exodus 25:2).
- **vv10–12 "test Me in this… the windows of the heavens… I will rebuke the devourer"** — the LORD tells His people to *test* Him, *bachan*, the verb of v15, where testing God is what the arrogant do. *Arubbot ha-shamayim*, **the windows of the heavens**, the word of the flood, *the lattices of the heavens were opened* (Genesis 7:11), now opened for blessing. *Ba-okhel*, **the devourer**, the locust.
- **vv13–15 "It is useless to serve God… keeping His watch… Those who do wickedness are built up"** — *mishmarto*, **His watch**, the priests' duty, as in Zechariah 3:7, *keep My watch*. *Qedorannit*, **as mourners**, in dark clothes, is found only here. *Nivnu*, **are built up**, the verb of Sarah's hope, *Perhaps I will be built up from her* (Genesis 16:2), here said of the wicked.
- **vv16–18 "a book of remembrance… My own treasure… as a man spares his son who serves him"** — *sefer zikkaron*, **a book of remembrance**, as Moses spoke of *Your book that You have written* (Exodus 32:32), and as the Persian king kept *the book of memorable deeds* (Esther 6:1). *Segullah*, **My own treasure**, the word of Sinai, *you will be My own treasure out of all the peoples* (Exodus 19:5). **Serving God** (vv14, 17, 18) is the word of the dispute: they said it was useless, and the LORD says He will show the difference.

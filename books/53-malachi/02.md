# Malachi 2

## A Command for the Priests

**1** "So now, priests, this command is for you.

**2** If you will not listen, and if you will not take it to heart to give glory to My name," says the LORD of hosts, "I will send the curse on you and curse your blessings. Indeed, I have already cursed them, because you do not take it to heart.

**3** I am about to rebuke your seed, and I will spread dung on your faces, the dung of your festivals, and you will be carried off with it.

**4** Then you will know that I have sent this command to you, so that My covenant with Levi may stand," says the LORD of hosts.

**5** "My covenant with him was life and peace, and I gave them to him; it was fear, and he feared Me and stood in awe of My name.

**6** True instruction was in his mouth, and no wrong was found on his lips; he walked with Me in peace and uprightness, and turned many away from iniquity.

**7** For the lips of a priest should guard knowledge, and people should seek instruction from his mouth, for he is the messenger of the LORD of hosts.

**8** But you have turned aside from the way; you have made many stumble by your instruction; you have corrupted the covenant of Levi," says the LORD of hosts.

**9** "So I in turn have made you despised and low before all the people, because you do not keep My ways but show partiality in the instruction."

## Faithless to One Another

**10** Do we not all have one father? Did not one God create us? Why then do we break faith with one another, profaning the covenant of our fathers?

**11** Judah has broken faith; a detestable thing has been done in Israel and in Jerusalem. For Judah has profaned the holy place of the LORD, which He loves, and has married the daughter of a foreign god.

**12** May the LORD cut off from the tents of Jacob the man who does this, whoever he may be, even one who brings an offering to the LORD of hosts.

**13** This is another thing you do: you cover the altar of the LORD with tears, with weeping and groaning, because He no longer looks at the offering or accepts it with favor from your hands.

**14** Yet you say, "Why?" Because the LORD was witness between you and the wife of your youth, with whom you have broken faith, though she is your companion and your wife by covenant.

**15** Did He not make one, with a remnant of the spirit belonging to him? What was the one seeking? Offspring from God. So guard yourselves in your spirit, and let no one break faith with the wife of his youth.

**16** "For the man who hates and sends her away," says the LORD, the God of Israel, "covers his garment with violence," says the LORD of hosts. "So guard yourselves in your spirit, and do not break faith."

## Where Is the God of Justice?

**17** You have wearied the LORD with your words. Yet you say, "How have we wearied Him?" By saying, "Everyone who does evil is good in the eyes of the LORD, and He delights in them," or, "Where is the God of justice?"

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "curse your blessings… I am about to rebuke your seed… dung on your faces, the dung of your festivals"** — the priests' *blessings* are the blessing they give (Numbers 6:23–26), now turned to curse. *Zera*, **seed**, the grain sown, or their offspring; the Greek reads *zeroa*, **the shoulder**, the priests' portion: *I am separating the shoulder for you*. *Peresh*, **dung**, the part of the sacrifice the law sends out, *its dung you shall burn in fire outside the camp* (Exodus 29:14): the priests will be carried out with it. *Chag*, **festival**, here the festival sacrifices.
- **vv4–7 "My covenant with Levi… life and peace… he is the messenger of the LORD of hosts"** — the covenant given to Phinehas, the priest of Levi's line, *I give him My covenant of peace* (Numbers 25:12), and the blessing of Levi, *They teach Your rules to Jacob and Your instruction to Israel* (Deuteronomy 33:10). *Torat emet*, **true instruction**. *Mal'akh YHWH tseva'ot*, **the messenger of the LORD of hosts**, said of a priest, as Haggai is *the LORD's messenger* (Haggai 1:13), and as the book's name means **My messenger** (1:1).
- **vv8–9 "you have made many stumble… show partiality in the instruction"** — *Nos'im panim*, **show partiality**, word for word **lift faces**, the words of 1:8–9: the priests want the LORD to lift His face to them while they lift faces in judgment. Nehemiah prays against those who *defiled the priesthood and the covenant of the priesthood and of the Levites* (Nehemiah 13:29).
- **vv10–12 "one father… one God… married the daughter of a foreign god… whoever he may be"** — *bagad*, **break faith**, five times in vv10–16. *One father* is God, *Is He not your Father, who acquired you, who made you* (Deuteronomy 32:6). *To'evah*, **a detestable thing**. *Qodesh YHWH*, **the holy place of the LORD**, may also be **the holiness of the LORD**, Judah itself set apart. *Bat el nekhar*, **the daughter of a foreign god**, a woman who worships another god, as in Ezra's time, *the holy seed has mixed itself with the peoples of the lands* (Ezra 9:2). *Er ve-oneh*, word for word **one who wakes and one who answers**, is obscure; it seems to mean anyone at all.
- **vv13–14 "you cover the altar of the LORD with tears… the wife of your youth… your wife by covenant"** — *eshet ne'urekha*, **the wife of your youth**, as in *rejoice in the wife of your youth* (Proverbs 5:18). *Chavertekha*, **your companion**, as in *who forsakes the companion of her youth and forgets the covenant of her God* (Proverbs 2:17): marriage is a covenant with God as witness.
- **vv15–16 "Did He not make one… Offspring from God… the man who hates and sends her away covers his garment with violence"** — v15 is obscure. *Ve-lo echad asah* may be **Did He not make one**, the two who become *one flesh* (Genesis 2:24), or **no one has done this** who has a remnant of the spirit. *Zera Elohim*, **offspring from God**, children of a faithful marriage. *Ki sane shallach*: *sane* is **he hates**, *shallach* is **send away**, as the law says of divorce, *he writes her a certificate of divorce… and sends her out of his house* (Deuteronomy 24:1). The Greek has *If, hating her, you send her away*.
- **v17 "You have wearied the LORD with your words… Where is the God of justice?"** — *hoga'tem*, **wearied**, as in *you have wearied Me with your iniquities* (Isaiah 43:24). The question is answered at once in 3:1.

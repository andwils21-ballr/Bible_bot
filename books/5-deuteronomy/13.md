# Deuteronomy 13

## The Prophet Whose Sign Comes True

**1** If a prophet or a dreamer of dreams arises among you and gives you a sign or a wonder,

**2** and the sign or the wonder that he spoke to you comes true, and he says, 'Let us go after other gods' — gods you have not known — 'and let us serve them,'

**3** you shall not listen to the words of that prophet or that dreamer of dreams. For the LORD your God is testing you, to know whether you love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

**4** After the LORD your God you shall walk, and Him you shall fear. His commandments you shall keep, and His voice you shall listen to. Him you shall serve, and to Him you shall hold fast.

**5** That prophet or that dreamer of dreams shall be put to death, because he has spoken rebellion against the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you from the house of slaves, to push you off the way in which the LORD your God commanded you to walk. So you shall purge the evil from among you.

## The One Who Entices You in Secret

**6** If your brother, your mother's son, or your son or your daughter, or the wife of your embrace, or your friend who is as your own soul entices you in secret, saying, 'Let us go and serve other gods' — gods that neither you nor your fathers have known,

**7** from the gods of the peoples who are around you, near you or far from you, from one end of the earth to the other end of the earth —

**8** you shall not consent to him or listen to him. Your eye shall not pity him; you shall not spare him and you shall not cover for him.

**9** You shall surely kill him. Your hand shall be the first against him to put him to death, and afterward the hand of all the people.

**10** You shall stone him, and he shall die, because he sought to push you away from the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slaves.

**11** All Israel shall hear and be afraid, and no one among you will again do anything like this evil thing.

## A Town That Turns Away

**12** If you hear it said of one of your towns that the LORD your God is giving you to live in,

**13** 'Worthless men have gone out from among you and pushed the inhabitants of their town away, saying, "Let us go and serve other gods," gods you have not known,'

**14** you shall inquire and search and ask thoroughly. If it is true and the thing is certain, that this detestable thing has been done among you,

**15** you shall surely strike the inhabitants of that town with the sword, devoting it to destruction, with all that is in it and its animals, with the sword.

**16** You shall gather all its spoil into the middle of its open square, and burn the town and all its spoil with fire, whole, to the LORD your God. It shall be a heap forever; it shall not be built again.

**17** None of what is devoted to destruction shall cling to your hand, so that the LORD may turn from the heat of His anger and give you compassion, and have compassion on you and multiply you, as He swore to your fathers,

**18** when you listen to the voice of the LORD your God, keeping all His commandments that I command you today, doing what is right in the eyes of the LORD your God.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 the sign that comes true** — a true sign does not make a true prophet. The test is not what he can do but where he leads: *after other gods*. A fulfilled wonder is itself called a test, from God.
- **v4 "After the LORD your God you shall walk"** — six verbs in a row, each with the LORD placed first for weight: walk, fear, keep, listen, serve, hold fast.
- **v5 "So you shall purge the evil from among you"** — *u-vi'arta ha-ra mi-qirbekha*, **you shall burn out the evil**. *Bi'er* is the verb of fire consuming. The formula returns nine times in Deuteronomy's laws, from here to 24:7.
- **v6 "the wife of your embrace… your friend who is as your own soul"** — the list moves inward: brother, child, wife, the friend closest of all. The nearer the voice, the stronger the pull, and the law names each.
- **v13 "Worthless men"** — *benei veliyya'al*, **sons of worthlessness**. The phrase becomes a stock insult in the historical books.
- **v14 "inquire and search and ask thoroughly"** — three verbs and an adverb before anything is done: the town is not destroyed on a rumor (v12, *if you hear it said*).
- **v16 "whole, to the LORD your God"** — *kalil*, the word for the priest's grain offering that goes up entire (Leviticus 6:22–23). The whole town is burned as if it were an offering.

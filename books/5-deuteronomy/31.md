# Deuteronomy 31

## Joshua Will Cross Over

**1** Moses went and spoke these words to all Israel.

**2** He said to them, "I am a hundred and twenty years old today. I can no longer go out and come in, and the LORD has said to me, 'You shall not cross this Jordan.'

**3** The LORD your God Himself will cross over before you. He will destroy these nations before you, and you will dispossess them. Joshua is the one who will cross over before you, as the LORD has spoken.

**4** The LORD will do to them as He did to Sihon and to Og, the kings of the Amorites, and to their land, when He destroyed them.

**5** The LORD will give them over before you, and you shall do to them according to all the commandment that I have commanded you.

**6** Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid or tremble before them, for the LORD your God is the one who goes with you. He will not let you go or forsake you."

**7** Then Moses called Joshua and said to him in the sight of all Israel, "Be strong and courageous, for you will go with this people into the land that the LORD swore to their fathers to give them, and you will cause them to inherit it.

**8** The LORD is the one who goes before you. He will be with you; He will not let you go or forsake you. Do not be afraid or lose heart."

## Read It Every Seven Years

**9** Moses wrote this instruction and gave it to the priests, the sons of Levi, who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD, and to all the elders of Israel.

**10** Moses commanded them, saying, "At the end of every seven years, at the set time of the year of release, at the Festival of Booths,

**11** when all Israel comes to appear before the face of the LORD your God in the place that He will choose, you shall read this instruction before all Israel in their hearing.

**12** Assemble the people, the men and the women and the little ones, and your guest who is inside your gates, so that they may hear and learn and fear the LORD your God, and be careful to do all the words of this instruction,

**13** and so that their sons, who have not known, may hear and learn to fear the LORD your God all the days that you live on the ground that you are crossing the Jordan to possess."

## The Song as a Witness

**14** The LORD said to Moses, "The days are near for you to die. Call Joshua, and present yourselves in the tent of meeting, so that I may commission him." Moses and Joshua went and presented themselves in the tent of meeting.

**15** The LORD appeared in the tent in a pillar of cloud, and the pillar of cloud stood over the entrance of the tent.

**16** The LORD said to Moses, "You are about to lie down with your fathers, and this people will rise and give themselves to the foreign gods of the land they are going into, among them. They will forsake Me and break My covenant that I made with them.

**17** My anger will burn against them in that day. I will forsake them and hide My face from them, and they will be devoured, and many evils and troubles will find them. They will say in that day, 'Is it not because our God is not among us that these evils have found us?'

**18** I will surely hide My face in that day because of all the evil they have done, for they have turned to other gods.

**19** So now write this song for yourselves, and teach it to the sons of Israel. Put it in their mouths, so that this song may be a witness for Me against the sons of Israel.

**20** For when I bring them into the ground that I swore to their fathers, flowing with milk and honey, and they eat and are satisfied and grow fat, they will turn to other gods and serve them, and despise Me and break My covenant.

**21** And it shall come to pass, when many evils and troubles have found them, this song will testify before them as a witness, for it will not be forgotten from the mouths of their seed. For I know the shaping of their thoughts, what they are doing today, before I have brought them into the land that I swore."

**22** Moses wrote this song that day and taught it to the sons of Israel.

**23** He commissioned Joshua son of Nun and said, "Be strong and courageous, for you will bring the sons of Israel into the land that I swore to them, and I will be with you."

## Beside the Ark

**24** When Moses had finished writing the words of this instruction in a book to the very end,

**25** Moses commanded the Levites who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD, saying,

**26** "Take this book of the instruction and put it beside the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, and let it be there as a witness against you.

**27** For I know your rebellion and your stiff neck. Even now, while I am still alive with you today, you have been rebellious against the LORD; how much more after my death!

**28** Assemble to me all the elders of your tribes and your officials, so that I may speak these words in their hearing and call the heavens and the earth to witness against them.

**29** For I know that after my death you will act corruptly and turn aside from the way that I have commanded you, and evil will meet you in the end of days, because you will do what is evil in the eyes of the LORD, provoking Him to anger with the work of your hands."

**30** Moses spoke in the hearing of all the assembly of Israel the words of this song, to the very end.

## Notes

- **v2 "a hundred and twenty years old"** — the limit of Genesis 6:3, *his days shall be a hundred and twenty years*. *I can no longer go out and come in*: yet 34:7 says *his eye was not dim*. The reason he stops is given in the same verse: *You shall not cross this Jordan*.
- **vv3, 8 "the one who goes before you"** — Joshua crosses before the people (v3), but the LORD crosses before them first. The leader changes; the One who leads does not.
- **vv6, 8 "He will not let you go or forsake you"** — *lo yarpekha ve-lo ya'azvekha*. Hebrews 13:5 quotes the promise: *I will never leave you nor forsake you*.
- **vv7, 23 "Be strong and courageous"** — *chazaq ve-emats*, the words God commanded Moses to say in 3:28, *strengthen him and encourage him*. God says them again to Joshua in Joshua 1:6–9.
- **vv10–13 the reading every seven years** — at the Festival of Booths of the year of release (15:1), everyone gathers, *the men and the women and the little ones, and your guest*, to hear the whole instruction read aloud.
- **v16 "You are about to lie down with your fathers"** — *hinnekha shokhev*, God's announcement of what is about to happen. *Lie down with your fathers* is the idiom for death. *Ve-zanah acharei*, **whore after**, rendered *give themselves to*, is the word for prostitution: the turning to other gods is told as a wife's infidelity.
- **vv17–18 "hide My face"** — *hester panim*, doubled in v18, *I will surely hide My face*. The people will conclude *our God is not among us*; the song will tell them why.
- **v21 "the shaping of their thoughts"** — *yitsro*, **his shaping**, the word of Genesis 6:5 and 8:21, *the shaping of the human heart is evil from his youth*.
- **v26 "beside the ark"** — the tablets are inside the ark (10:5); the book is kept beside it, *as a witness against you*.
- **v29 "in the end of days"** — *be-acharit ha-yamim*, as in 4:30.

# Deuteronomy 18

## The Portion of the Priests and Levites

**1** The Levitical priests, all the tribe of Levi, shall have no share or inheritance with Israel. They shall eat the LORD's offerings by fire, His inheritance.

**2** They shall have no inheritance among their brothers; the LORD is their inheritance, as He said to them.

**3** This shall be the priests' due from the people, from those who offer a sacrifice, whether ox or sheep: they shall give the priest the shoulder, the two cheeks and the stomach.

**4** The first of your grain, your new wine and your oil, and the first of the shearing of your flock, you shall give him.

**5** For the LORD your God has chosen him out of all your tribes, to stand to serve in the name of the LORD, him and his sons, all the days.

**6** If a Levite comes from any of your towns in all Israel where he lives as a guest, and comes with all the desire of his soul to the place that the LORD will choose,

**7** he may serve in the name of the LORD his God like all his brothers the Levites who stand there before the LORD.

**8** They shall eat equal portions, besides what he receives from the sale of his fathers' goods.

## Not Like the Nations

**9** When you enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you, you shall not learn to do according to the detestable things of those nations.

**10** There shall not be found among you anyone who makes his son or his daughter pass through the fire, a soothsayer, one who reads omens, one who practices divination, or a sorcerer,

**11** or one who casts spells, or one who asks a ghost or a spirit, or one who seeks out the dead.

**12** For whoever does these things is detestable to the LORD, and because of these detestable things the LORD your God is driving them out from before you.

**13** You shall be whole with the LORD your God.

**14** For these nations that you are dispossessing listen to those who read omens and to soothsayers. But as for you, the LORD your God has not given you this.

## A Prophet Like Me

**15** A prophet from among you, from your brothers, like me, the LORD your God will raise up for you. To him you shall listen —

**16** just as you asked of the LORD your God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, saying, 'Let me not hear again the voice of the LORD my God, and let me not see this great fire any more, or I will die.'

**17** The LORD said to me, 'They have spoken well in what they have spoken.

**18** A prophet I will raise up for them from among their brothers, like you, and I will put My words in his mouth, and he shall speak to them all that I command him.

**19** Whoever will not listen to My words that he speaks in My name, I Myself will require it of him.

**20** But the prophet who presumes to speak a word in My name that I have not commanded him to speak, or who speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet shall die.'

**21** If you say in your heart, 'How shall we know the word that the LORD has not spoken?' —

**22** when a prophet speaks in the name of the LORD, and the word does not happen and does not come true, that is the word that the LORD has not spoken. The prophet has spoken it presumptuously. You shall not be afraid of him.

## Notes

- **v13 "You shall be whole"** — *tamim*, the word said of Noah (Genesis 6:9) and asked of Abram (Genesis 17:1, *be whole*). Of an animal it is *without defect*; of a person it is wholeness toward God, with nothing held back for omens and diviners.
- **v15 "A prophet… like me"** — *navi… kamoni*. The promise answers v14: other nations listen to diviners; Israel will have a prophet. Peter reads it of Jesus in Acts 3:22, and Stephen quotes it in Acts 7:37.

# Deuteronomy 6

## The Commandment

**1** This is the commandment — the statutes and the rules — that the LORD your God commanded me to teach you, to do in the land that you are crossing over into, to possess it,

**2** so that you may fear the LORD your God, to keep all His statutes and His commandments that I command you — you, and your son, and your son's son — all the days of your life, and so that your days may be long.

**3** Hear, Israel, and be careful to do it, so that it may go well with you and so that you may multiply greatly, as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has spoken to you: a land flowing with milk and honey.

## Hear, Israel

**4** Hear, Israel: the LORD our God, the LORD is one.

**5** You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength.

**6** These words that I command you today shall be on your heart.

**7** Repeat them to your sons, and speak of them when you sit in your house and when you walk on the road, when you lie down and when you rise.

**8** Bind them as a sign on your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes.

**9** Write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates.

## When You Have Eaten and Are Satisfied

**10** When the LORD your God brings you into the land that He swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, to give you — great and good cities that you did not build,

**11** and houses full of every good thing that you did not fill, and hewn cisterns that you did not hew, vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant — and you eat and are satisfied,

**12** guard yourself so that you do not forget the LORD, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slaves.

**13** You shall fear the LORD your God, and Him you shall serve, and by His name you shall swear.

**14** You shall not go after other gods, from the gods of the peoples who are around you —

**15** for the LORD your God in your midst is a jealous God — or the anger of the LORD your God will burn against you, and He will wipe you off the face of the ground.

**16** You shall not test the LORD your God, as you tested Him at Massah.

**17** Keep, keep the commandments of the LORD your God, and His testimonies and His statutes that He has commanded you.

**18** Do what is right and good in the eyes of the LORD, so that it may go well with you, and you may go in and possess the good land that the LORD swore to your fathers,

**19** thrusting out all your enemies from before you, as the LORD has spoken.

## When Your Son Asks You

**20** When your son asks you tomorrow, saying, 'What are the testimonies and the statutes and the rules that the LORD our God has commanded you?'

**21** you shall say to your son, 'We were Pharaoh's slaves in Egypt, and the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a strong hand.

**22** The LORD gave signs and wonders, great and terrible, against Egypt, against Pharaoh and against all his house, before our eyes.

**23** Us He brought out from there, so that He might bring us in, to give us the land that He swore to our fathers.

**24** The LORD commanded us to do all these statutes, to fear the LORD our God, for our good all the days, to keep us alive, as it is this day.

**25** It will be righteousness for us, if we are careful to do all this commandment before the LORD our God, as He commanded us.'

## Notes

- **v4 "the LORD our God, the LORD is one"** — four words in Hebrew, *YHWH eloheinu YHWH echad*, with no verb. They can also be read *the LORD is our God, the LORD alone*. The first reading says what God is; the second says whom Israel serves. Both claim Him for Israel alone.
- **v5 "with all your strength"** — *me'od*, elsewhere an adverb, **very, greatly** (as in v3, *multiply greatly*, *me'od*). Only here and in 2 Kings 23:25 is it a noun: your *muchness*, all you have. When Jesus quotes this verse in Matthew 22:37 the list is *heart… soul… mind*.
- **v7 "Repeat them"** — *shinnantam*, from *shanan*, **to sharpen**: go over them until they are sharp. The four times of day that follow — sitting, walking, lying down, rising — cover a whole day.
- **v8 "frontlets"** — *totafot*, a rare word, used also of the Passover sign in Exodus 13:16, where this rendering has *a band between your eyes*. Jewish practice took vv8–9 literally in tefillin and the mezuzah; Matthew 23:5 mentions the *phylacteries*.
- **vv10–11 "that you did not build… did not fill… did not hew… did not plant"** — four times, *asher lo*. The gift is the danger: eating what others made, Israel may forget who gave it (v12).
- **v13 "You shall fear the LORD your God, and Him you shall serve"** — Jesus answers the tempter with this verse in Matthew 4:10, as the Greek has it: *worship* in place of *fear*, and *Him only*.
- **v16 "as you tested Him at Massah"** — Exodus 17:7: *they tested the LORD, saying, 'Is the LORD among us or not?'* Jesus answers the tempter with this verse in Matthew 4:7.
- **v17 "Keep, keep"** — *shamor tishmerun*, the verb doubled for force.
- **v20 "When your son asks you tomorrow"** — the same form as Exodus 13:14, *when your son asks you tomorrow, 'What is this?'* The answer (vv21–25) is not a rule but a story: *We were Pharaoh's slaves.*
- **v25 "It will be righteousness for us"** — *tsedaqah*. Keeping the commandment is counted as righteousness, as Abraham's trust was in Genesis 15:6.

# Deuteronomy 7

## Make No Covenant with Them

**1** When the LORD your God brings you into the land that you are going into to possess, and clears away many nations from before you — the Hittite and the Girgashite and the Amorite and the Canaanite and the Perizzite and the Hivite and the Jebusite, seven nations greater and mightier than you —

**2** and the LORD your God gives them over before you and you strike them, you shall devote them to destruction. You shall make no covenant with them and show them no favor.

**3** You shall not intermarry with them: your daughter you shall not give to his son, and his daughter you shall not take for your son,

**4** for he will turn your son away from following Me, and they will serve other gods. Then the anger of the LORD will burn against you, and He will wipe you out quickly.

**5** Rather, this is what you shall do to them: tear down their altars, shatter their standing stones, cut down their sacred poles, and burn their carved images with fire.

## Why the LORD Chose You

**6** For you are a holy people to the LORD your God. The LORD your God has chosen you to be a people for His own treasure, out of all the peoples who are on the face of the ground.

**7** Not because you were more numerous than all the peoples did the LORD set His heart on you and choose you — for you were the fewest of all the peoples —

**8** but because the LORD loved you, and because He kept the oath that He swore to your fathers, the LORD brought you out with a strong hand and redeemed you from the house of slaves, from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt.

**9** Know, then, that the LORD your God is God, the faithful God, who keeps covenant and loyal love to a thousand generations with those who love Him and keep His commandments,

**10** and repays to their face those who hate Him, to destroy them. He will not delay with the one who hates Him; to his face He will repay him.

**11** Keep the commandment and the statutes and the rules that I command you today, to do them.

## Blessed Above All Peoples

**12** And it will come to pass, because you listen to these rules and keep and do them, that the LORD your God will keep for you the covenant and the loyal love that He swore to your fathers.

**13** He will love you and bless you and multiply you. He will bless the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your ground, your grain and your new wine and your oil, the offspring of your cattle and the young of your flock, on the ground that He swore to your fathers to give you.

**14** You will be blessed above all the peoples. There will be no barren male or barren female among you, or among your animals.

**15** The LORD will take away from you every sickness, and none of the evil diseases of Egypt that you knew will He put on you; He will lay them on all who hate you.

**16** You shall consume all the peoples that the LORD your God is giving you. Your eye shall not pity them, and you shall not serve their gods, for that would be a snare to you.

## Do Not Be Afraid of Them

**17** If you say in your heart, 'These nations are greater than I; how can I drive them out?'

**18** do not be afraid of them. Remember, remember what the LORD your God did to Pharaoh and to all Egypt —

**19** the great trials that your eyes saw, the signs and the wonders, the strong hand and the outstretched arm by which the LORD your God brought you out. So the LORD your God will do to all the peoples you are afraid of.

**20** The LORD your God will also send the hornet among them, until those who are left and those who hide from you are destroyed.

**21** Do not tremble before them, for the LORD your God is in your midst, a great and awesome God.

**22** The LORD your God will clear away these nations from before you little by little. You will not be able to finish them off quickly, or the beasts of the field would multiply against you.

**23** The LORD your God will give them over before you and throw them into great confusion, until they are destroyed.

**24** He will give their kings into your hand, and you will wipe out their name from under the heavens. No one will be able to stand before you until you have destroyed them.

**25** The carved images of their gods you shall burn with fire. You shall not covet the silver or the gold on them and take it for yourself, or you will be snared by it, for it is detestable to the LORD your God.

**26** You shall not bring a detestable thing into your house, or you will be devoted to destruction like it. You shall utterly loathe it and utterly detest it, for it is devoted to destruction.

## Notes

- **v2 "you shall devote them to destruction"** — *hacharem tacharim*, the verb doubled. *Cherem* removes a thing from human use and gives it wholly to God; nothing of it may be kept (vv25–26).
- **v3 "You shall not intermarry with them"** — the reason given is not blood but worship: *he will turn your son away from following Me* (v4).
- **v6 "for His own treasure"** — *segullah*, the word of Exodus 19:5, *you will be My own treasure out of all the peoples*: a king's private wealth, set apart from what he governs.
- **vv7–8 "Not because you were more numerous… but because the LORD loved you"** — the choice is explained by nothing in Israel. *Chashaq*, **set His heart on**, is a word of desire, used of a man drawn to a woman in 21:11. Then the reason given is only love, and the oath to the fathers.
- **v10 "to their face"** — *el panav*, twice: God repays the one who hates Him directly and in person, not by waiting on his children (compare 5:9, *on the third and on the fourth generation*).
- **v12 "because you listen"** — *eqev*, **heel**, used as *because*: the reward that follows on the heel of the listening. The portion of the Torah read in synagogues from here takes its name from the word.
- **v20 "the hornet"** — *tzir'ah*, as in Exodus 23:28: one of three places the word occurs, all this same promise.
- **v22 "little by little… or the beasts of the field would multiply against you"** — the promise of Exodus 23:29–30, *I will not drive them out from before you in one year*.
- **vv25–26 "detestable… loathe… detest"** — *to'evah*, then the verbs *shaqqets teshaqqetzennu* and *ta'ev teta'avennu*, each doubled. Whoever takes a thing devoted to destruction becomes *cherem* with it: after Achan takes from the spoil of Jericho, God says of Israel, *they have become devoted to destruction* (Joshua 7:12).

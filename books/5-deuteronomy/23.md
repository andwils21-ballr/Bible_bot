# Deuteronomy 23

## Who May Enter the Assembly

**1** No one whose testicles are crushed or whose male organ is cut off shall enter the assembly of the LORD.

**2** No one born of a forbidden union shall enter the assembly of the LORD; even to the tenth generation none of his shall enter the assembly of the LORD.

**3** No Ammonite or Moabite shall enter the assembly of the LORD; even to the tenth generation none of theirs shall enter the assembly of the LORD, forever,

**4** because they did not meet you with bread and water on the way when you came out of Egypt, and because they hired against you Balaam son of Beor from Pethor of Aram-naharaim, to curse you.

**5** But the LORD your God would not listen to Balaam, and the LORD your God turned the curse into a blessing for you, because the LORD your God loved you.

**6** You shall not seek their peace or their good all your days, forever.

**7** You shall not detest an Edomite, for he is your brother. You shall not detest an Egyptian, because you were a guest in his land.

**8** Children born to them in the third generation may enter the assembly of the LORD.

## A Holy Camp

**9** When you go out as a camp against your enemies, you shall guard yourself from every bad thing.

**10** If there is among you a man who is not clean because of what happened in the night, he shall go outside the camp; he shall not come inside the camp.

**11** And it shall come to pass, toward evening, he shall bathe in water, and when the sun goes down he may come inside the camp.

**12** You shall have a place outside the camp, and you shall go out there.

**13** You shall have a spade among your gear, and when you sit down outside, you shall dig with it and turn back and cover your excrement.

**14** For the LORD your God walks about inside your camp, to deliver you and to give your enemies over before you. So your camp shall be holy, so that He does not see anything indecent among you and turn away from you.

## Various Laws

**15** You shall not hand over to his master a slave who has escaped from his master to you.

**16** He shall live with you, among you, in the place that he chooses in one of your towns, wherever it is good for him. You shall not wrong him.

**17** There shall be no consecrated woman among the daughters of Israel, and there shall be no consecrated man among the sons of Israel.

**18** You shall not bring the wages of a prostitute or the price of a dog into the house of the LORD your God for any vow, for both of them are detestable to the LORD your God.

**19** You shall not charge interest to your brother, interest on silver, interest on food, interest on anything that is lent at interest.

**20** To a foreigner you may lend at interest, but to your brother you shall not lend at interest, so that the LORD your God may bless you in all that you put your hand to in the land that you are going into to possess.

**21** When you make a vow to the LORD your God, you shall not delay to pay it, for the LORD your God will surely require it of you, and it will be sin in you.

**22** But if you refrain from vowing, it will not be sin in you.

**23** What has come out of your lips you shall keep and do, just as you have vowed to the LORD your God a freewill offering that you have spoken with your mouth.

**24** When you go into your neighbor's vineyard, you may eat grapes until you are satisfied, as much as you wish, but you shall not put any in your basket.

**25** When you go into your neighbor's standing grain, you may pick the heads with your hand, but you shall not put a sickle to your neighbor's standing grain.

## Notes

- **v2 "born of a forbidden union"** — *mamzer*, a word found only here and in Zechariah 9:6. What union it means is not said; Jewish law later defined it as the child of adultery or incest.
- **vv3–5 Ammonite and Moabite** — the reason is history, Numbers 22–24: Moab *hired… Balaam*. Nehemiah 13:1–2 reads this passage aloud to the returned exiles. Ruth, a Moabite woman, becomes David's great-grandmother (Ruth 4:17).
- **v5 "turned the curse into a blessing"** — the story of Numbers 23–24 in one line: every time Balaam opened his mouth to curse, blessing came out.
- **v7 "for he is your brother… because you were a guest in his land"** — Edom is Esau. Egypt enslaved Israel, yet Israel is not to detest it, because it first gave them a place to live.
- **v10 "what happened in the night"** — *miqreh laylah*, an emission of semen, as Leviticus 15:16: *he shall bathe his whole body in water, and be unclean until evening*.
- **v14 "the LORD your God walks about inside your camp"** — *mit'hallekh*, the verb of God *walking about in the garden* in Genesis 3:8. The camp must be clean because God is in it.
- **vv15–16 the escaped slave** — he is not returned. He chooses where to live, and *you shall not wrong him*: *tonennu*, the verb of the command about the guest in Exodus 22:21, *You shall not wrong a guest*.
- **v17 "consecrated woman… consecrated man"** — *qedeshah* and *qadesh*, the word Hirah uses in Genesis 38:21 for the woman Judah met. What the *qedeshah* did is disputed; the law here only bans it.
- **v18 "the price of a dog"** — *mechir kelev*. Set beside *the wages of a prostitute*, most take *dog* to mean a male prostitute, the *qadesh* of v17.
- **v25 "you may pick the heads with your hand"** — what the disciples do in Matthew 12:1, *they began to pick heads of grain and eat them*. The Pharisees' complaint there is the sabbath, not theft.

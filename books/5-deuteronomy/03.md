# Deuteronomy 3

## Og King of Bashan

**1** "Then we turned and went up by the road to Bashan, and Og king of Bashan came out against us, he and all his people, to battle at Edrei.

**2** The LORD said to me, 'Do not be afraid of him, for I have given him into your hand, with all his people and his land. You shall do to him as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon.'

**3** So the LORD our God gave into our hand Og king of Bashan as well, with all his people, and we struck him down until no survivor was left to him.

**4** We captured all his towns at that time. There was not a town that we did not take from them: sixty towns, the whole region of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

**5** All these were towns fortified with high walls, gates and bars, besides a great many unwalled villages.

**6** We devoted them to destruction, as we had done to Sihon king of Heshbon, devoting every town to destruction, men, women and little ones.

**7** But all the livestock and the spoil of the towns we took as plunder for ourselves.

**8** So at that time we took the land out of the hand of the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, from the Arnon valley to Mount Hermon

**9** (the Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, and the Amorites call it Senir),

**10** all the towns of the plateau, all Gilead and all Bashan, as far as Salecah and Edrei, towns of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

**11** (For only Og king of Bashan was left of the remnant of the Rephaim. His bed was a bed of iron; is it not in Rabbah of the sons of Ammon? Nine cubits was its length and four cubits its width, by the cubit of a man.)

## The Land Divided East of the Jordan

**12** "This land we took possession of at that time. From Aroer, which is by the Arnon valley, and half the hill country of Gilead with its towns, I gave to the Reubenites and the Gadites.

**13** The rest of Gilead, and all Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh — the whole region of Argob, all Bashan; it is called a land of Rephaim.

**14** Jair son of Manasseh took the whole region of Argob as far as the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and called them after his own name, Havvoth-jair — Bashan — to this day.

**15** To Machir I gave Gilead.

**16** To the Reubenites and the Gadites I gave from Gilead as far as the Arnon valley, with the middle of the valley as a border, and as far as the river Jabbok, the border of the sons of Ammon,

**17** and the Arabah, with the Jordan as a border, from Chinnereth as far as the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, under the slopes of Pisgah on the east.

**18** At that time I commanded you, saying, 'The LORD your God has given you this land to possess. All you men of valor shall cross over armed before your brothers, the sons of Israel.

**19** Only your wives, your little ones and your livestock — I know that you have much livestock — shall stay in the towns I have given you,

**20** until the LORD gives rest to your brothers as He has to you, and they too possess the land that the LORD your God is giving them beyond the Jordan. Then each of you may return to his possession that I have given you.'

**21** At that time I commanded Joshua, saying, 'Your eyes have seen all that the LORD your God has done to these two kings. So the LORD will do to all the kingdoms into which you are crossing.

**22** Do not be afraid of them, for the LORD your God is the one who fights for you.'

## Moses' Plea

**23** "At that time I pleaded with the LORD, saying,

**24** 'Lord GOD, You have begun to show Your servant Your greatness and Your strong hand. For what god is there in heaven or on earth who can do works and mighty deeds like Yours?

**25** Let me cross over, please, and see the good land beyond the Jordan, that good hill country and Lebanon.'

**26** But the LORD was furious with me on your account and would not listen to me. The LORD said to me, 'Enough! Speak to Me no more of this matter.

**27** Go up to the top of Pisgah and lift your eyes to the west and to the north and to the south and to the east, and look with your eyes, for you shall not cross this Jordan.

**28** But command Joshua, and strengthen him and encourage him, for he shall cross over before this people, and he shall cause them to inherit the land that you will see.'

**29** So we stayed in the valley opposite Beth-peor."

## Notes

- **vv1–3 Og** — the battle of Numbers 21:33–35 told again in almost the same words: *Do not be afraid of him, for I have given him into your hand… You shall do to him as you did to Sihon*. Here it is Moses remembering, *the LORD said to me*.
- **v9 "the Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, and the Amorites call it Senir"** — one mountain with three names, like the peoples of 2:10–12 and 2:20, each with its own name for the same giants.
- **v11 "His bed was a bed of iron"** — *eres barzel*. Og is *the remnant of the Rephaim*, the old giant peoples of 2:11, and his bed is given as proof: nine cubits by four, *by the cubit of a man*, the ordinary forearm. *Is it not in Rabbah?* speaks to readers who could go and see it.
- **v14 "Havvoth-jair"** — the name Numbers 32:41 gives to Jair's *tent villages*; here it is laid over all of Argob, *Bashan*, *to this day*.
- **v17 "the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea"** — two names for one lake, the second the name of Genesis 14:3 and Numbers 34:3.
- **v20 "until the LORD gives rest to your brothers"** — *yaniach*, **gives rest**, from *nuach*. The eastern tribes may go home only when the others have rest too.
- **v24 "Lord GOD"** — *Adonai YHWH*, the address Abram uses in Genesis 15:2, *Lord GOD, what will You give me?* Both men open a request to God with it.
- **vv25–27 "Let me cross over… the LORD was furious… you shall not cross"** — *e'berah*, **let me cross**, from *avar*; *va-yit'abber*, **was furious**, a verb built on the same three letters; and *lo ta'avor*, **you shall not cross**. The plea and the refusal are made of one word.
- **v26 "Enough!"** — *rav lakh*, **much for you**, the words of 1:6 and 2:3, *You have stayed long enough*, *You have gone around… long enough*, now said to Moses about his own prayer.
- **v27 "lift your eyes to the west and to the north and to the south and to the east"** — what the LORD told Abram after Lot left him: *Lift up, please, your eyes, and look from the place where you are — northward and southward and eastward and seaward* (Genesis 13:14). Abram was told the land he saw would be his; Moses may only see it.

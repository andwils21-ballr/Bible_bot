# Deuteronomy 29

## You Have Seen

**1** Moses called all Israel and said to them, "You have seen all that the LORD did before your eyes in the land of Egypt to Pharaoh and to all his servants and to all his land,

**2** the great trials that your eyes saw, the signs and those great wonders.

**3** Yet the LORD has not given you a heart to know, or eyes to see, or ears to hear, to this day.

**4** I have led you forty years in the wilderness. Your clothes have not worn out on you, and your sandal has not worn out on your foot.

**5** You have not eaten bread, and you have not drunk wine or strong drink, so that you may know that I am the LORD your God.

**6** When you came to this place, Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out against us to battle, and we struck them.

**7** We took their land and gave it as an inheritance to the Reubenites, to the Gadites and to the half-tribe of the Manassites.

**8** Keep the words of this covenant and do them, so that you may prosper in all that you do.

## All of You, Today

**9** You are standing today, all of you, before the LORD your God — your heads, your tribes, your elders and your officials, every man of Israel,

**10** your little ones, your wives, and your guest who is inside your camp, from the one who cuts your wood to the one who draws your water —

**11** to enter into the covenant of the LORD your God and into His oath, which the LORD your God is making with you today,

**12** so that He may establish you today as His people, and He will be your God, as He promised you and as He swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob.

**13** Not with you alone am I making this covenant and this oath,

**14** but with whoever is standing here with us today before the LORD our God, and with whoever is not here with us today.

## A Root Bearing Poison

**15** For you know how we lived in the land of Egypt, and how we passed through the nations that you passed through.

**16** You saw their loathsome things and their idols, wood and stone, silver and gold, that were among them.

**17** Let there not be among you a man or a woman, a family or a tribe, whose heart turns away today from the LORD our God to go and serve the gods of those nations. Let there not be among you a root bearing poison and wormwood,

**18** one who, when he hears the words of this oath, blesses himself in his heart, saying, 'I will have peace, though I walk in the stubbornness of my heart' — sweeping away the watered with the dry.

**19** The LORD will not be willing to forgive him. Then the anger of the LORD and His jealousy will smoke against that man, and every curse written in this book will settle on him, and the LORD will wipe out his name from under the heavens.

**20** The LORD will set him apart for evil out of all the tribes of Israel, according to all the curses of the covenant written in this book of the instruction.

## What the Nations Will Say

**21** The generation to come, your sons who rise up after you, and the foreigner who comes from a far land, will say, when they see the blows of that land and the sicknesses with which the LORD has made it sick —

**22** all its land sulfur and salt, a burning, not sown, not sprouting, no grass growing on it, like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which the LORD overthrew in His anger and His fury —

**23** all the nations will say, 'Why has the LORD done this to this land? What is this great burning anger?'

**24** They will say, 'Because they forsook the covenant of the LORD, the God of their fathers, which He made with them when He brought them out of the land of Egypt.

**25** They went and served other gods and bowed down to them, gods whom they had not known and whom He had not allotted to them.

**26** So the anger of the LORD burned against that land, to bring on it every curse written in this book.

**27** The LORD uprooted them from their ground in anger and in fury and in great wrath, and threw them into another land, as it is this day.'

**28** The hidden things belong to the LORD our God, but the revealed things belong to us and to our sons forever, to do all the words of this instruction.

## Notes

- **v1** — English Bibles number this verse 29:2; Hebrew 28:69 is their 29:1. English 29:2–29 is Hebrew 29:1–28 here.
- **v3 "Yet the LORD has not given you a heart to know"** — the people saw everything and still did not understand. Paul uses this verse, blended with Isaiah 29:10, in Romans 11:8.
- **vv4–5 clothing, sandals, bread** — 8:4 said the clothing did not wear out; here the sandals are added, and the speaker slips into God's own voice: *so that you may know that I am the LORD your God*.
- **v10 "from the one who cuts your wood to the one who draws your water"** — the lowest labor in the camp. Joshua 9:21 gives these jobs to the Gibeonites.
- **vv13–14 "with whoever is not here with us today"** — the covenant binds generations not yet born. The same point was made at Horeb in 5:3: *but with us, we who are all here alive today*.
- **v16 "their loathsome things and their idols"** — *shiqqutseihem*, the fixed word *loathsome*; *gillulim*, **idols**, as in Leviticus 26:30, a word that may mean **dung pellets** in contempt.
- **v17 "a root bearing poison and wormwood"** — *rosh ve-la'anah*, two bitter plants. Hebrews 12:15 takes up the image from the Greek: *that no root of bitterness springing up cause trouble*.
- **v18 "sweeping away the watered with the dry"** — an obscure phrase, perhaps a proverb: the one man's hidden idolatry will bring down everyone, the green with the dry.
- **v22 "Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim"** — the four cities of the plain named in Genesis 14:2. Genesis 19:24–25 tells how the LORD *rained… sulfur and fire* and *overturned those cities*; *overthrow* here is the same verb, *haphakh*.
- **v28 "The hidden things… the revealed things"** — in the Hebrew text the words *to us and to our sons* carry dots written over each letter, one of the places in the Torah so marked by the scribes (the rabbis counted ten). What the dots were meant to signal was already debated in antiquity.

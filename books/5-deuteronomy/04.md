# Deuteronomy 4

## Hear the Statutes

**1** "And now, Israel, listen to the statutes and the rules that I am teaching you to do, so that you may live, and go in and take possession of the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, is giving you.

**2** You shall not add to the word that I command you, and you shall not take away from it, so that you keep the commandments of the LORD your God that I command you.

**3** Your eyes have seen what the LORD did at Baal-peor, for the LORD your God destroyed from among you every man who followed the Baal of Peor.

**4** But you who held fast to the LORD your God are all alive today.

**5** See, I have taught you statutes and rules, as the LORD my God commanded me, for you to do in the land you are going in to possess.

**6** Keep them and do them, for that will be your wisdom and your understanding in the eyes of the peoples, who will hear all these statutes and say, 'Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people.'

**7** For what great nation is there that has a god so near to it as the LORD our God is whenever we call on Him?

**8** What great nation is there that has statutes and rules as righteous as all this instruction that I set before you today?

## The Day at Horeb

**9** "Only take care, and guard yourself closely, so that you do not forget the things your eyes have seen, and so that they do not depart from your heart all the days of your life. Make them known to your sons and your sons' sons —

**10** the day you stood before the LORD your God at Horeb, when the LORD said to me, 'Gather the people to Me, and I will let them hear My words, so that they may learn to fear Me all the days they live on the earth, and may teach their sons.'

**11** You came near and stood at the foot of the mountain, and the mountain burned with fire to the heart of the heavens, with darkness, cloud and thick darkness.

**12** The LORD spoke to you from the middle of the fire. You heard the sound of words, but you saw no form; there was only a voice.

**13** He declared to you His covenant, which He commanded you to do, the Ten Words, and He wrote them on two tablets of stone.

**14** The LORD commanded me at that time to teach you statutes and rules, for you to do them in the land you are crossing over to possess.

## No Image

**15** "So take great care for your lives, since you saw no form on the day the LORD spoke to you at Horeb from the middle of the fire,

**16** so that you do not act corruptly and make for yourselves a carved image in the form of any figure, the likeness of male or female,

**17** the likeness of any animal that is on the earth, the likeness of any winged bird that flies in the sky,

**18** the likeness of anything that creeps on the ground, the likeness of any fish that is in the waters under the earth;

**19** and so that you do not lift your eyes to the heavens and see the sun, the moon and the stars, all the host of heaven, and be drawn away and bow down to them and serve them, things the LORD your God has allotted to all the peoples under the whole heaven.

**20** But the LORD has taken you and brought you out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be a people of His own inheritance, as you are this day.

**21** The LORD was angry with me because of you, and He swore that I would not cross the Jordan and would not go into the good land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.

**22** For I am to die in this land. I am not crossing the Jordan; but you are crossing, and you shall possess this good land.

**23** Take care for yourselves, so that you do not forget the covenant of the LORD your God, which He made with you, and make for yourselves a carved image in the form of anything the LORD your God has forbidden you.

**24** For the LORD your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God.

## Scattered and Sought

**25** "When you have fathered sons and sons' sons, and have grown old in the land, and you act corruptly and make a carved image in the form of anything, and do what is evil in the eyes of the LORD your God, provoking Him to anger,

**26** I call heaven and earth to witness against you today that you will quickly perish from the land you are crossing the Jordan to possess. You will not live long on it; you will be utterly destroyed.

**27** The LORD will scatter you among the peoples, and you will be left few in number among the nations where the LORD will drive you.

**28** There you will serve gods made by human hands, wood and stone, which neither see nor hear nor eat nor smell.

**29** But from there you will seek the LORD your God, and you will find Him, if you search for Him with all your heart and with all your soul.

**30** When you are in distress and all these things have come upon you, in the end of days you will return to the LORD your God and listen to His voice.

**31** For the LORD your God is a merciful God. He will not let you go, and He will not destroy you, and He will not forget the covenant with your fathers that He swore to them.

## There Is No Other

**32** "For ask now about the former days, which were before you, from the day that God created humankind on the earth, and from one end of the heavens to the other: has anything as great as this ever happened, or has anything like it ever been heard of?

**33** Has any people ever heard the voice of God speaking from the middle of the fire, as you have heard, and lived?

**34** Or has any god ever tried to go and take for himself a nation from the middle of another nation, by trials, by signs and by wonders, by war, by a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and by great terrors, as all that the LORD your God did for you in Egypt before your eyes?

**35** You were shown it so that you might know that the LORD is God; there is no other besides Him.

**36** From heaven He let you hear His voice, to discipline you, and on earth He let you see His great fire, and you heard His words from the middle of the fire.

**37** Because He loved your fathers, He chose their seed after them, and He Himself brought you out of Egypt by His great strength,

**38** to drive out before you nations greater and mightier than you, to bring you in and give you their land as an inheritance, as it is this day.

**39** Know therefore today, and lay it to your heart, that the LORD is God in heaven above and on the earth below; there is no other.

**40** Keep His statutes and His commandments, which I command you today, so that it may go well with you and with your sons after you, and so that you may live long on the ground that the LORD your God is giving you for all time."

## Three Towns of Refuge

**41** Then Moses set apart three towns beyond the Jordan, toward the sunrise,

**42** for the one who kills his neighbor unintentionally, without having hated him before, to flee there, so that he may flee to one of these towns and live:

**43** Bezer in the wilderness on the plateau, for the Reubenites; Ramoth in Gilead, for the Gadites; and Golan in Bashan, for the Manassites.

## The Instruction

**44** This is the instruction that Moses set before the sons of Israel.

**45** These are the testimonies, the statutes and the rules that Moses spoke to the sons of Israel when they came out of Egypt,

**46** beyond the Jordan, in the valley opposite Beth-peor, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, whom Moses and the sons of Israel struck down when they came out of Egypt.

**47** They took possession of his land and the land of Og king of Bashan, the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise,

**48** from Aroer, which is on the edge of the Arnon valley, as far as Mount Sion, that is, Hermon,

**49** with all the Arabah beyond the Jordan on the east, as far as the Sea of the Arabah, under the slopes of Pisgah.

## Notes

- **v2 "You shall not add… and you shall not take away"** — *lo tosifu… ve-lo tigre'u*. *Gara*, **to take away**, is the verb of the daughters of Zelophehad and the heads of their clan (Numbers 27:4; 36:3). The instruction is to be handed on whole.
- **v4 "you who held fast to the LORD"** — *ha-deveqim*, from *davaq*, **to cling**, the verb of Genesis 2:24, *clings to his woman*. At Peor Israel *yoked itself to Baal* (Numbers 25:3); those who clung to the LORD instead are the ones still alive.
- **v12 "you saw no form; there was only a voice"** — *u-temunah einkhem ro'im zulati qol*. The whole argument of vv15–24 rests on this sentence: at Horeb nothing was seen, so nothing may be made. *Temunah*, **form, likeness**, is the word of the commandment, *any form of what is in the heavens* (Exodus 20:4).
- **v13 "the Ten Words"** — *aseret ha-devarim*, **the ten words**, the Bible's own name for what English calls the Ten Commandments.
- **vv16–18 male or female… animal… bird… creeping thing… fish** — the creatures of Genesis 1 in reverse order: God made the fish and birds, then the animals and creeping things, then humankind *male and female* (Genesis 1:20–27). Here the list starts with *male or female* and ends with fish. Nothing that God made may be made into an image of Him.
- **v19 "things the LORD your God has allotted to all the peoples"** — *asher chalaq*, **which He divided, apportioned** to every people under heaven. Israel is not to bow to them; what the other peoples were given them for, the verse does not say. Verse 20 sets Israel apart: *But the LORD has taken you*.
- **v20 "the iron furnace"** — *kur ha-barzel*, the smelting furnace in which iron is refined. Egypt was the fire Israel was brought out of.
- **v24 "a consuming fire, a jealous God"** — *esh okhlah hu, El qanna*: the *consuming fire* of Exodus 24:17, *the look of the LORD's glory was like a consuming fire on the top of the mountain*, and the *jealous God* of the commandment (Exodus 20:5).
- **v30 "in the end of days"** — *be-acharit ha-yamim*, the phrase of Jacob's blessing, *what will befall you in the end of days* (Genesis 49:1), and of Balaam's last oracle (Numbers 24:14). Here it looks past exile to a return.
- **v42 "without having hated him before"** — *mi-temol shilshom*, **from yesterday and the day before**: there was no old grudge. The towns of refuge of Numbers 35 are set apart for the eastern tribes; the three in Canaan come later.

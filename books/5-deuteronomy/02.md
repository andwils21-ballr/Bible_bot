# Deuteronomy 2

## Past Esau's Sons

**1** "Then we turned and set out into the wilderness by the way of the Sea of Reeds, as the LORD had told me, and we went around Mount Seir for many days.

**2** The LORD said to me,

**3** 'You have gone around this mountain long enough. Turn north.

**4** Command the people: You are about to pass through the territory of your brothers, the sons of Esau, who live in Seir. They will be afraid of you, so be very careful.

**5** Do not provoke them, for I will not give you any of their land, not even a footstep, because I have given Mount Seir to Esau as a possession.

**6** You shall buy food from them with silver, so that you may eat, and you shall also buy water from them with silver, so that you may drink.

**7** For the LORD your God has blessed you in all the work of your hands. He has known your going through this great wilderness. These forty years the LORD your God has been with you; you have lacked nothing.'

**8** So we passed by our brothers, the sons of Esau who live in Seir, away from the road of the Arabah, from Elath and Ezion-geber, and we turned and went by the way of the wilderness of Moab.

## Moab

**9** "The LORD said to me, 'Do not harass Moab or provoke them to war, for I will not give you any of their land as a possession, because I have given Ar to the sons of Lot as a possession.'

**10** (The Emim lived there formerly, a people great and many and tall as the Anakim.

**11** They too are counted as Rephaim, like the Anakim, but the Moabites call them Emim.

**12** The Horites also lived in Seir formerly, but the sons of Esau dispossessed them and destroyed them from before them and settled in their place, as Israel did to the land of its possession that the LORD gave them.)

**13** 'Now rise up and cross the Brook Zered.' So we crossed the Brook Zered.

**14** The time it took us to go from Kadesh-barnea until we crossed the Brook Zered was thirty-eight years, until the whole generation of the men of war had perished from the camp, as the LORD had sworn to them.

**15** Indeed, the hand of the LORD was against them, to wipe them out from the camp, until they were gone.

## Ammon

**16** "When all the men of war had died and were gone from among the people,

**17** the LORD spoke to me, saying,

**18** 'Today you are to pass through the territory of Moab, through Ar.

**19** When you come near the sons of Ammon, do not harass them or provoke them, for I will not give you any of the land of the sons of Ammon as a possession, because I have given it to the sons of Lot as a possession.'

**20** (It too is counted as a land of Rephaim. Rephaim lived there formerly, but the Ammonites call them Zamzummim,

**21** a people great and many and tall as the Anakim. But the LORD destroyed them from before them, and they dispossessed them and settled in their place,

**22** as He did for the sons of Esau who live in Seir, when He destroyed the Horites from before them, and they dispossessed them and settled in their place to this day.

**23** As for the Avvim, who lived in villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim, who came out of Caphtor, destroyed them and settled in their place.)

## Sihon King of Heshbon

**24** "'Rise up, set out and cross the Arnon valley. See, I have given into your hand Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land. Begin to take possession, and provoke him to war.

**25** This day I will begin to put the dread and fear of you on the peoples under the whole heaven, who will hear the report of you and tremble and be in anguish because of you.'

**26** So I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon, with words of peace, saying,

**27** 'Let me pass through your land. I will keep to the road; I will not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**28** You shall sell me food for silver so that I may eat, and give me water for silver so that I may drink. Only let me pass through on foot,

**29** as the sons of Esau who live in Seir and the Moabites who live in Ar did for me, until I cross the Jordan into the land that the LORD our God is giving us.'

**30** But Sihon king of Heshbon would not let us pass through his land, for the LORD your God made his spirit hard and his heart stubborn, so that He might give him into your hand, as He has this day.

**31** The LORD said to me, 'See, I have begun to give Sihon and his land over to you. Begin to take possession, to possess his land.'

**32** Sihon came out against us, he and all his people, to battle at Jahaz.

**33** The LORD our God gave him over to us, and we struck him down, with his sons and all his people.

**34** We captured all his towns at that time and devoted every town to destruction, men, women and little ones. We left no survivor.

**35** Only the livestock we took as plunder for ourselves, with the spoil of the towns we captured.

**36** From Aroer, which is on the edge of the Arnon valley, and the town in the valley, as far as Gilead, there was not a town too high for us. The LORD our God gave them all over to us.

**37** Only the land of the sons of Ammon you did not approach, all the bank of the river Jabbok and the towns of the hill country, and wherever the LORD our God had forbidden us."

## Notes

- **v1 "we went around Mount Seir for many days"** — *va-nasov*, **we circled**. The same verb returns in v3: *You have gone around this mountain long enough*, *rav lakhem sov*, the words spoken at Horeb in 1:6, *rav lakhem shevet*, *you have stayed long enough*.
- **vv4–6 "your brothers, the sons of Esau"** — Edom is *brothers* because Esau was Jacob's brother. In Numbers 20:14–21 the king of Edom refused passage, *Edom came out against them with a large army*; this chapter tells of buying food and water from *the sons of Esau who live in Seir* (vv6, 29) and passing by. The two accounts are given side by side without being joined.
- **v5 "not even a footstep"** — *ad midrakh kaf ragel*, **up to the treading of the sole of a foot**. Esau's land is as much a gift of the LORD as Israel's: *I have given Mount Seir to Esau as a possession*.
- **v7 "you have lacked nothing"** — *lo chasarta davar*. Forty years in the wilderness are summed up not as punishment but as provision.
- **vv9, 19 "the sons of Lot"** — Moab and Ammon, from Lot's two sons: *He is the father of Moab… He is the father of the sons of Ammon to this day* (Genesis 19:37–38). Edom, Moab and Ammon are all kin, and all three are left their land.
- **vv10–12, 20–23 the old peoples** — the speech breaks off (set in parentheses here) to say who held these lands before: the giants of Moab, *Emim*, and of Ammon, *Zamzummim*, both counted among the *Rephaim*. Genesis 14:5 names the Rephaim and the Emim among the peoples the eastern kings struck; the *Horites* of Seir are the sons of *Seir the Horite* (Genesis 36:20). Each people took its land from an older people, and the LORD gave it.
- **v14 "thirty-eight years"** — from Kadesh-barnea to the Brook Zered. With the time before Kadesh, it completes the forty years of 1:3, and the oath of 1:35 is kept: that generation is gone.
- **v30 "made his spirit hard and his heart stubborn"** — *hiqshah… et rucho ve-immets et levavo*, the words of Pharaoh's story: *I Myself will make Pharaoh's heart hard* (Exodus 7:3). Numbers 21:23 says only that *Sihon would not let Israel pass*; Deuteronomy says why.
- **v34 "devoted every town to destruction"** — *va-nacharem*, from *cherem*, the vow of Numbers 21:2: *I will devote their towns to destruction*. What is devoted is given over wholly and nothing is kept, except here the livestock and the spoil (v35).

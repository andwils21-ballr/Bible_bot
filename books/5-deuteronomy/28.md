# Deuteronomy 28

## The Blessings

**1** And it shall come to pass, if you listen, listen to the voice of the LORD your God, to be careful to do all His commandments that I command you today, the LORD your God will set you high above all the nations of the earth.

**2** All these blessings will come on you and overtake you, if you listen to the voice of the LORD your God.

**3** Blessed are you in the city, and blessed are you in the field.

**4** Blessed is the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your ground and the fruit of your animals, the offspring of your cattle and the young of your flock.

**5** Blessed is your basket and your kneading bowl.

**6** Blessed are you when you come in, and blessed are you when you go out.

**7** The LORD will make your enemies who rise against you be struck down before you. They will come out against you one way and flee before you seven ways.

**8** The LORD will command the blessing to be with you in your barns and in all that you put your hand to, and He will bless you in the land that the LORD your God is giving you.

**9** The LORD will establish you as a holy people for Himself, as He swore to you, if you keep the commandments of the LORD your God and walk in His ways.

**10** All the peoples of the earth will see that the name of the LORD is called over you, and they will be afraid of you.

**11** The LORD will make you abound in good, in the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your animals and the fruit of your ground, on the ground that the LORD swore to your fathers to give you.

**12** The LORD will open for you His good treasury, the heavens, to give the rain of your land in its season and to bless all the work of your hand. You will lend to many nations, but you will not borrow.

**13** The LORD will make you the head and not the tail; you will be only on top and not underneath, if you listen to the commandments of the LORD your God that I command you today, to keep and to do them,

**14** and do not turn aside from any of the words that I command you today, to the right or to the left, to go after other gods to serve them.

## The Curses

**15** And it shall come to pass, if you do not listen to the voice of the LORD your God, to be careful to do all His commandments and His statutes that I command you today, all these curses will come on you and overtake you.

**16** Cursed are you in the city, and cursed are you in the field.

**17** Cursed is your basket and your kneading bowl.

**18** Cursed is the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your ground, the offspring of your cattle and the young of your flock.

**19** Cursed are you when you come in, and cursed are you when you go out.

**20** The LORD will send on you the curse, the confusion and the rebuke in all that you put your hand to and do, until you are destroyed and perish quickly, because of the evil of your deeds, because you have forsaken Me.

**21** The LORD will make the plague cling to you until He has finished you off the ground that you are going into to possess.

**22** The LORD will strike you with wasting disease and fever, inflammation and burning heat, with the sword, with scorching and with mildew, and they will pursue you until you perish.

**23** The heavens over your head will be bronze, and the earth under you iron.

**24** The LORD will make the rain of your land powder and dust; from the heavens it will come down on you until you are destroyed.

**25** The LORD will make you be struck down before your enemies. You will go out one way against them and flee seven ways before them, and you will be a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth.

**26** Your dead body will be food for every bird of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth, and there will be no one to frighten them away.

**27** The LORD will strike you with the boils of Egypt, with tumors, with scabs and with itch, from which you cannot be healed.

**28** The LORD will strike you with madness and blindness and bewilderment of heart.

**29** You will grope at noon as the blind man gropes in darkness, and you will not prosper in your ways. You will be only oppressed and robbed all the days, and there will be no one to save you.

**30** You will be engaged to a woman, and another man will lie with her. You will build a house and not live in it. You will plant a vineyard and not use its fruit.

**31** Your ox will be slaughtered before your eyes, and you will not eat of it. Your donkey will be robbed from before your face and not come back to you. Your sheep will be given to your enemies, and there will be no one to save you.

**32** Your sons and your daughters will be given to another people, while your eyes look on and fail with longing for them all the day, and there will be nothing in the power of your hand.

**33** A people you have not known will eat the fruit of your ground and all your labor, and you will be only oppressed and crushed all the days.

**34** You will be driven mad by the sight your eyes see.

**35** The LORD will strike you with bad boils on the knees and on the legs, from which you cannot be healed, from the sole of your foot to the top of your head.

**36** The LORD will bring you and your king whom you set over you to a nation that neither you nor your fathers have known, and there you will serve other gods, wood and stone.

**37** You will become a horror, a proverb and a byword among all the peoples where the LORD drives you.

**38** You will carry much seed out to the field and gather in little, for the locust will consume it.

**39** You will plant vineyards and work them, but you will not drink the wine or gather the grapes, for the worm will eat them.

**40** You will have olive trees throughout all your territory, but you will not anoint yourself with oil, for your olives will drop off.

**41** You will father sons and daughters, but they will not be yours, for they will go into captivity.

**42** All your trees and the fruit of your ground the cricket will take over.

**43** The guest who is among you will rise above you higher and higher, and you will go down lower and lower.

**44** He will lend to you, but you will not lend to him. He will be the head, and you will be the tail.

**45** All these curses will come on you and pursue you and overtake you until you are destroyed, because you did not listen to the voice of the LORD your God, to keep His commandments and His statutes that He commanded you.

**46** They will be a sign and a wonder on you and on your seed forever.

## A Nation from Far Away

**47** Because you did not serve the LORD your God with joy and gladness of heart for the abundance of everything,

**48** you will serve your enemies whom the LORD will send against you, in hunger and in thirst, in nakedness and in lack of everything. He will put an iron yoke on your neck until He has destroyed you.

**49** The LORD will bring against you a nation from far away, from the end of the earth, swooping as the eagle swoops, a nation whose language you will not understand,

**50** a nation hard of face, who will not lift the face of the old or show favor to the young.

**51** It will eat the fruit of your animals and the fruit of your ground until you are destroyed. It will leave you no grain, new wine or oil, no offspring of your cattle or young of your flock, until it has made you perish.

**52** It will besiege you in all your towns until your high and fortified walls, in which you trust, come down throughout all your land. It will besiege you in all your towns throughout all your land that the LORD your God has given you.

**53** You will eat the fruit of your womb, the flesh of your sons and your daughters whom the LORD your God has given you, in the siege and in the distress with which your enemy will distress you.

**54** The man who is most tender and delicate among you will look with an evil eye on his brother and on the wife of his embrace and on the last of his sons who remain,

**55** so as not to give any of them the flesh of his sons that he is eating, because nothing is left to him in the siege and in the distress with which your enemy will distress you in all your towns.

**56** The woman who is most tender and delicate among you, who would not venture to set the sole of her foot on the ground for delicacy and tenderness, will look with an evil eye on the husband of her embrace and on her son and on her daughter,

**57** and on the afterbirth that comes out from between her feet and on her sons whom she bears, for she will eat them secretly for lack of everything, in the siege and in the distress with which your enemy will distress you in your towns.

## Back to Egypt

**58** If you are not careful to do all the words of this instruction that are written in this book, to fear this glorious and awesome name, the LORD your God,

**59** then the LORD will make your blows and the blows of your seed extraordinary, great and lasting blows, and bad and lasting sicknesses.

**60** He will bring back on you all the diseases of Egypt that you were afraid of, and they will cling to you.

**61** Also every sickness and every blow that is not written in the book of this instruction, the LORD will bring on you until you are destroyed.

**62** You will be left few in number, where you were like the stars of the heavens for multitude, because you did not listen to the voice of the LORD your God.

**63** And it shall come to pass that as the LORD delighted over you to do you good and to multiply you, so the LORD will delight over you to make you perish and to destroy you, and you will be torn from the ground that you are going into to possess.

**64** The LORD will scatter you among all the peoples, from one end of the earth to the other end of the earth, and there you will serve other gods that neither you nor your fathers have known, wood and stone.

**65** Among those nations you will find no ease, and there will be no resting place for the sole of your foot. The LORD will give you there a trembling heart, failing eyes and a languishing soul.

**66** Your life will hang in doubt before you. You will be in dread night and day, and have no assurance of your life.

**67** In the morning you will say, 'If only it were evening!' and in the evening you will say, 'If only it were morning!' because of the dread of your heart that you will feel and the sight of your eyes that you will see.

**68** The LORD will bring you back to Egypt in ships, by a way of which I said to you, 'You will never see it again.' There you will offer yourselves for sale to your enemies as male and female slaves, and there will be no buyer.

**69** These are the words of the covenant that the LORD commanded Moses to make with the sons of Israel in the land of Moab, besides the covenant that He made with them at Horeb.

## Notes

- **vv3–6 and 16–19 six blessings, six curses** — the same six lines, word for word, with *Blessed* turned to *Cursed*. City and field, womb and ground, basket and bowl, coming in and going out: the whole of life.
- **v10 "the name of the LORD is called over you"** — *shem YHWH niqra alekha*, the phrase for ownership: what bears a name belongs to its owner.
- **vv13, 44 "the head and not the tail"** — the blessing of v13 is turned against Israel in v44, where the guest becomes the head.
- **v22 "wasting disease and fever"** — *shachefet* and *qaddachat*, the pair of Leviticus 26:16, *wasting disease and fever*. *Scorching and mildew* strike the grain.
- **v23 "bronze… iron"** — Leviticus 26:19 has the pair the other way round: *your sky like iron and your earth like bronze*.
- **v27 "the boils of Egypt"** — *shechin*, the sixth plague, Exodus 9:9, *boils breaking out in blisters*. *With tumors*: the written text has *ofalim*, the reading tradition *techorim*, and 1 Samuel 5–6 has the same pair.
- **v30 the three reversals** — house, vineyard and bride, the three reasons for going home from war in 20:5–7, here each taken by another. The written text has a coarser verb for *lie with* (*yishgalennah*), and the reading tradition softens it.
- **v49 "swooping as the eagle swoops… whose language you will not understand"** — the invader is never named. Readers have seen Assyria and Babylon in it, and later Rome.
- **vv53–57 eating the children** — the curse of Leviticus 26:29, *You will eat the flesh of your sons*, drawn out in horror. 2 Kings 6:28–29 and Lamentations 4:10 record it happening under siege.
- **v58 "this glorious and awesome name"** — *ha-shem ha-nikhbad ve-ha-nora ha-zeh*: *the Name* standing for God Himself.
- **v63 "as the LORD delighted over you… so the LORD will delight"** — the same verb, *sas*, both times, the boldest line in the chapter.
- **v68 "back to Egypt in ships"** — the undoing of the exodus: back to the house of slaves, and not even wanted as slaves there.
- **v69 "besides the covenant that He made with them at Horeb"** — the covenant in Moab is a second covenant alongside the first. English Bibles number this verse 29:1.

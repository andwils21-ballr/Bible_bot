# Deuteronomy 21

## The Unsolved Killing

**1** If someone is found slain on the ground that the LORD your God is giving you to possess, lying in the field, and it is not known who struck him,

**2** your elders and your judges shall go out and measure the distance to the towns that are around the slain man.

**3** And it shall come to pass that the town nearest to the slain man — the elders of that town shall take a heifer from the herd that has not been worked and has not pulled in a yoke.

**4** The elders of that town shall bring the heifer down to a valley with a flowing stream, which is neither plowed nor sown, and there in the valley they shall break the heifer's neck.

**5** The priests, the sons of Levi, shall come forward, for the LORD your God has chosen them to serve Him and to bless in the name of the LORD, and by their word every dispute and every mark shall be settled.

**6** All the elders of that town nearest to the slain man shall wash their hands over the heifer whose neck was broken in the valley,

**7** and they shall answer and say, 'Our hands did not shed this blood, and our eyes did not see it.

**8** Make atonement, LORD, for Your people Israel, whom You redeemed, and do not set innocent blood among Your people Israel.' So the blood shall be atoned for them.

**9** So you shall purge the innocent blood from among you, when you do what is right in the eyes of the LORD.

## The Captive Woman

**10** When you go out to battle against your enemies, and the LORD your God gives them into your hand and you take them captive,

**11** and you see among the captives a woman beautiful in form, and you set your heart on her and would take her as your wife,

**12** you shall bring her into your house, and she shall shave her head and do her nails.

**13** She shall take off the clothing of her captivity and live in your house, and weep for her father and her mother a full month. After that you may go in to her and be her husband, and she shall be your wife.

**14** And it shall come to pass, if you no longer delight in her, you shall let her go wherever she wishes. You shall certainly not sell her for silver; you shall not treat her as a slave, because you have violated her.

## The Firstborn's Right

**15** If a man has two wives, one loved and the other hated, and both the loved and the hated have borne him sons, and the firstborn son is the hated one's,

**16** then on the day he gives his sons what he has as an inheritance, he may not make the son of the loved wife firstborn in place of the son of the hated wife, who is the firstborn.

**17** He shall acknowledge the firstborn, the son of the hated wife, by giving him a double portion of all that he has, for he is the first of his vigor; the right of the firstborn is his.

## The Rebellious Son

**18** If a man has a stubborn and rebellious son who does not listen to the voice of his father or the voice of his mother, and they discipline him but he will not listen to them,

**19** his father and his mother shall take hold of him and bring him out to the elders of his town, to the gate of his place.

**20** They shall say to the elders of his town, 'This son of ours is stubborn and rebellious. He will not listen to our voice. He is a glutton and a drunkard.'

**21** All the men of his town shall stone him with stones, and he shall die. So you shall purge the evil from among you, and all Israel shall hear and be afraid.

## The Body on the Tree

**22** If a man has committed a sin deserving death and he is put to death, and you hang him on a tree,

**23** his body shall not remain overnight on the tree. You shall surely bury him that same day, for one who is hanged is a curse of God. You shall not make unclean your ground that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.

## Notes

- **vv1–9 the unsolved killing** — no one can be punished, but the blood cannot be left (19:10, *so blood guilt is not on you*). The nearest town takes responsibility. The heifer *that has not been worked* dies in a valley *neither plowed nor sown*: untouched life, untouched land.
- **v4 "break the heifer's neck"** — *arafu*, the verb used for the unredeemed donkey in Exodus 13:13, *you shall break its neck*. It is not a sacrifice; there is no altar and no blood on one.
- **v6 "wash their hands"** — a sign of innocence spoken aloud in v7. Pilate does the same in Matthew 27:24, *he took water and washed his hands… saying, "I am innocent of this man's blood."*
- **vv10–14 the captive woman** — she mourns a full month before any marriage. If she is later sent away, she goes free: *you shall not treat her as a slave*.
- **v14 "because you have violated her"** — *'innitah*, the verb used of Shechem in Genesis 34:2, *violated her*. Having taken her, the man has lost the right to sell her.
- **v15 "one loved and the other hated"** — the words of Genesis 29:30–31, *Leah was hated*. Jacob gave Joseph, son of the loved Rachel, *one shoulder above your brothers* (Genesis 48:22). 1 Chronicles 5:1 says Reuben lost the birthright for his own sin, not his mother's place; this law forbids a father to choose by love alone.
- **v17 "the first of his vigor"** — *reshit ono*, as Jacob calls Reuben in Genesis 49:3: *my strength and the first of my vigor*. *A double portion*, *pi shenayim*, **a mouth of two**, is what Elisha asks of Elijah's spirit in 2 Kings 2:9.
- **v20 "a glutton and a drunkard"** — *zolel ve-sove*. Jesus says He was called *a glutton and a drunkard* in Matthew 11:19.
- **v23 "one who is hanged is a curse of God"** — *qilelat Elohim talui*. The body was hung up after death as a warning, and taken down before night. Paul quotes the verse in its Greek form in Galatians 3:13: *Cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree*.

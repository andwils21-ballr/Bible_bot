# Deuteronomy 5

## The Covenant at Horeb

**1** Moses called all Israel and said to them, "Hear, Israel, the statutes and the rules that I speak in your hearing today. Learn them and be careful to do them.

**2** The LORD our God made a covenant with us at Horeb.

**3** Not with our fathers did the LORD make this covenant, but with us, we who are all here alive today.

**4** Face to face the LORD spoke with you on the mountain from the middle of the fire,

**5** while I stood between the LORD and you at that time to declare to you the word of the LORD, for you were afraid because of the fire and did not go up the mountain. He said:

## The Ten Words

**6** 'I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slaves.

**7** You shall have no other gods before My face.

**8** You shall not make for yourself a carved image, any form of what is in the heavens above, or on the earth below, or in the waters under the earth.

**9** You shall not bow down to them and you shall not serve them — because I, the LORD your God, am a jealous God, calling the guilt of fathers to account on sons, on the third and on the fourth generation of those who hate Me,

**10** and doing loyal love to thousands, for those who love Me and keep My commandments.

**11** You shall not lift up the name of the LORD your God for emptiness, because the LORD will not leave unpunished the one who lifts up His name for emptiness.

**12** Keep the sabbath day, to keep it holy, as the LORD your God commanded you.

**13** Six days you shall work and do all your labor,

**14** but the seventh day is a sabbath to the LORD your God. You shall not do any labor — you, or your son, or your daughter, or your male slave, or your female slave, or your ox, or your donkey, or any of your animals, or the guest who is inside your gates — so that your male slave and your female slave may rest as you do.

**15** Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and the LORD your God brought you out from there with a strong hand and an outstretched arm. That is why the LORD your God commanded you to keep the sabbath day.

**16** Honor your father and your mother, as the LORD your God commanded you, so that your days may be long, and so that it may go well with you on the ground that the LORD your God is giving you.

**17** You shall not murder.

**18** You shall not commit adultery.

**19** You shall not steal.

**20** You shall not answer against your neighbor as an empty witness.

**21** You shall not covet your neighbor's wife. You shall not desire your neighbor's house, his field, or his male slave, or his female slave, his ox, or his donkey, or anything that is your neighbor's.'

## Speak to Us Yourself

**22** "These words the LORD spoke to all your assembly on the mountain from the middle of the fire, the cloud and the thick darkness, with a loud voice, and He added no more. He wrote them on two tablets of stone and gave them to me.

**23** And it came to pass, when you heard the voice from the middle of the darkness, while the mountain was burning with fire, that you came near to me, all the heads of your tribes and your elders,

**24** and said, 'The LORD our God has shown us His glory and His greatness, and we have heard His voice from the middle of the fire. Today we have seen that God may speak with a human being and he may live.

**25** But now, why should we die? For this great fire will consume us. If we hear the voice of the LORD our God any more, we will die.

**26** For who of all flesh has heard the voice of the living God speaking from the middle of the fire, as we have, and lived?

**27** You go near and hear all that the LORD our God says, and you speak to us all that the LORD our God speaks to you, and we will hear and do it.'

**28** The LORD heard the sound of your words when you spoke to me, and the LORD said to me, 'I have heard the sound of the words of this people, which they have spoken to you. All that they have said is good.

**29** If only they had such a heart as this always, to fear Me and keep all My commandments, so that it might go well with them and with their sons forever!

**30** Go, say to them, "Return to your tents."

**31** But you, stand here by Me, and I will speak to you all the commandment, the statutes and the rules that you shall teach them, so that they may do them in the land I am giving them to possess.'

**32** So be careful to do as the LORD your God has commanded you. Do not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**33** Walk in all the way that the LORD your God has commanded you, so that you may live, and it may go well with you, and you may live long in the land you shall possess."

## Notes

- **v3 "Not with our fathers… but with us, we who are all here alive today"** — most of those who stood at Horeb have died in the wilderness (2:14–16). Moses speaks to their children as if they had been there: the covenant is made with whoever hears it *today*.
- **v4 "Face to face"** — *panim be-fanim*, and yet v5 says Moses *stood between*, and 4:12 that they *saw no form*. The phrase is about the directness of the speaking, not about seeing.
- **v12 "Keep the sabbath day"** — *shamor*, where Exodus 20:8 has *zakhor*, **Remember**. Deuteronomy adds *as the LORD your God commanded you*, pointing back to Sinai; the command is being repeated, not given for the first time.
- **vv14–15 the reason for the sabbath** — in Exodus 20:11 the reason is creation: *in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth… and He rested on the seventh day*. Here it is Egypt: *Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt*, *so that your male slave and your female slave may rest as you do*. The same day is grounded once in God's rest and once in Israel's release.
- **v16 "and so that it may go well with you"** — words Exodus 20:12 does not have. Deuteronomy's own refrain returns in v29 and v33.
- **vv18–21 "You shall not… You shall not"** — in the Hebrew, each of these commands begins with *and*: *ve-lo tin'af, ve-lo tignov*, **and you shall not… and you shall not**, joining them into one chain. Exodus has no *and*.
- **v20 "as an empty witness"** — *ed shav*, where Exodus 20:16 has *ed shaqer*, **a false witness**. *Shav* is the word of v11, *for emptiness*: testimony with nothing behind it.
- **v21 the wife first, and two verbs** — Exodus 20:17 puts the house first and the wife among the household. Here the wife stands alone with her own verb, *lo tachmod*, **you shall not covet**, and the house, now with *his field*, gets another: *lo tit'avveh*, **you shall not desire**.
- **v22 "and He added no more"** — *ve-lo yasaf*: the ten were spoken aloud to all the people, and then the voice stopped. Everything after comes through Moses (v31).
- **v29 "If only they had such a heart as this always"** — *mi yitten*, **who will give**, the Hebrew form of a wish. God wishes the people's fear at the mountain would last.

# Deuteronomy 1

## These Are the Words

**1** These are the words that Moses spoke to all Israel beyond the Jordan in the wilderness, in the Arabah opposite Suph, between Paran and Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth and Di-zahab.

**2** It is eleven days' journey from Horeb by the way of Mount Seir to Kadesh-barnea.

**3** And it came to pass in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, that Moses spoke to the sons of Israel according to all that the LORD had commanded him for them,

**4** after he had struck down Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth, at Edrei.

**5** Beyond the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses undertook to explain this instruction, saying,

## Leaving Horeb

**6** "The LORD our God spoke to us at Horeb, saying, 'You have stayed long enough at this mountain.

**7** Turn and set out, and go to the hill country of the Amorites and to all their neighbors in the Arabah, in the hill country, in the lowland, in the Negev and on the seacoast, the land of the Canaanites, and Lebanon, as far as the great river, the river Euphrates.

**8** See, I have set the land before you. Go in and take possession of the land that the LORD swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, to give to them and to their seed after them.'

## Judges for the People

**9** "At that time I said to you, 'I cannot carry you by myself.

**10** The LORD your God has multiplied you, and today you are as many as the stars of the heavens.

**11** May the LORD, the God of your fathers, make you a thousand times as many as you are, and bless you as He has promised you!

**12** How can I bear by myself the weight of you, your burden and your quarrels?

**13** Choose for yourselves men who are wise, discerning and known among your tribes, and I will make them your heads.'

**14** You answered me, 'What you have said is good to do.'

**15** So I took the heads of your tribes, wise men and known, and made them heads over you, commanders of thousands, commanders of hundreds, commanders of fifties and commanders of tens, and officials for your tribes.

**16** At that time I charged your judges, 'Hear the cases between your brothers, and judge rightly between a man and his brother or the guest who is with him.

**17** Do not show partiality in judgment. Hear the small and the great alike. Do not be afraid of any man, for the judgment is God's. The case that is too hard for you, bring to me, and I will hear it.'

**18** At that time I commanded you all the things you should do.

## The Scouts at Kadesh

**19** "We set out from Horeb and went through all that great and terrible wilderness that you saw, by the way of the hill country of the Amorites, as the LORD our God had commanded us, and we came to Kadesh-barnea.

**20** I said to you, 'You have come to the hill country of the Amorites, which the LORD our God is giving us.

**21** See, the LORD your God has set the land before you. Go up and take possession, as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has told you. Do not be afraid and do not lose heart.'

**22** Then all of you came near to me and said, 'Let us send men ahead of us to search out the land for us and bring us back word of the way we should go up and the towns we shall come to.'

**23** The thing seemed good to me, and I took twelve men from among you, one man for each tribe.

**24** They turned and went up into the hill country and came to the valley of Eshcol, and scouted it out.

**25** They took some of the fruit of the land in their hands and brought it down to us, and they brought us back word and said, 'The land that the LORD our God is giving us is good.'

**26** But you would not go up, and you rebelled against the command of the LORD your God.

**27** You grumbled in your tents and said, 'Because the LORD hates us He has brought us out of the land of Egypt, to give us into the hand of the Amorites to destroy us.

**28** Where are we going up to? Our brothers have made our hearts melt, saying, "The people are greater and taller than we are; the cities are great and fortified up to the heavens; and we saw the sons of the Anakim there."'

**29** I said to you, 'Do not be terrified, and do not be afraid of them.

**30** The LORD your God, who goes before you, He Himself will fight for you, just as He did for you in Egypt before your eyes,

**31** and in the wilderness, where you saw how the LORD your God carried you, as a man carries his son, all the way you went until you came to this place.'

**32** Yet in this you did not trust the LORD your God,

**33** who went before you on the way to seek out a place for you to camp, in fire by night to show you the way you should go, and in the cloud by day.

## The Oath

**34** "The LORD heard the sound of your words, and He was angry and swore, saying,

**35** 'Not one of these men of this evil generation shall see the good land that I swore to give to your fathers,

**36** except Caleb son of Jephunneh. He shall see it, and to him and to his sons I will give the land he has walked on, because he has followed the LORD fully.'

**37** Even with me the LORD was angry on your account, saying, 'You also shall not go in there.

**38** Joshua son of Nun, who stands before you, he shall go in there. Strengthen him, for he will cause Israel to inherit it.

**39** Your little ones, who you said would become plunder, and your sons, who today do not know good and evil, they shall go in there. To them I will give it, and they shall possess it.

**40** But as for you, turn and set out into the wilderness by the way of the Sea of Reeds.'

**41** Then you answered and said to me, 'We have sinned against the LORD. We will go up and fight, just as the LORD our God commanded us.' Every man of you strapped on his weapons of war and thought it easy to go up into the hill country.

**42** The LORD said to me, 'Say to them: Do not go up and do not fight, for I am not among you; otherwise you will be struck down before your enemies.'

**43** So I spoke to you, but you would not listen. You rebelled against the command of the LORD, acted presumptuously and went up into the hill country.

**44** The Amorites who lived in that hill country came out against you and chased you as bees do, and beat you down in Seir as far as Hormah.

**45** You came back and wept before the LORD, but the LORD did not listen to your voice or give ear to you.

**46** So you stayed in Kadesh many days, as many days as you stayed there.

## Notes

- **v1 "These are the words"** — *elleh ha-devarim*, the book's opening words and its Hebrew name, *Devarim*, **Words**. The Greek name, *Deuteronomion*, **second law**, comes from 17:18, where the king is to write *mishneh ha-torah ha-zot*, **a copy of this instruction**; *mishneh* is **a second, a double**, and the Greek there reads *to deuteronomion touto*. The book is Moses speaking to Israel: *I said to you*.
- **v2 "eleven days' journey"** — from Horeb to Kadesh by the direct road is eleven days; v3 dates this speech *in the fortieth year*. The distance between the two numbers is the story of Numbers.
- **v5 "undertook to explain this instruction"** — *ho'il… be'er*, **set about to make plain**. *Be'er* is used of writing words *very plainly* on stones (27:8). *Torah* here is **instruction**, the whole teaching Moses is about to give, not yet a name for five books.
- **vv9–18 judges for the people** — Moses tells the choosing of the judges in his own words. In Exodus 18 the advice came from his father-in-law Jethro, *look among all the people for capable men* (Exodus 18:21); here it is Moses who says it to the people, and the burden of Numbers 11:14, *I cannot carry all this people by myself*, is behind it.
- **v10 "as many as the stars of the heavens"** — the promise to Abraham, *count the stars… So shall your seed be* (Genesis 15:5), spoken as already true: *today you are*.
- **v17 "the judgment is God's"** — *ha-mishpat l'Elohim hu*. The judge is not to fear a powerful man because the verdict he gives belongs to God.
- **v28 "fortified up to the heavens"** — *betsurot ba-shamayim*, the scouts' words given as the people heard them. In Numbers 13:28 the report was *the cities are fortified and very large*; by the time it reached the tents, the walls reached the sky.
- **v31 "as a man carries his son"** — *ka'asher yissa ish et beno*, set against v27, where the people say the LORD brought them out *because the LORD hates us*.
- **v37 "Even with me the LORD was angry on your account"** — Numbers 20:12 gives Moses' exclusion as his own failure at the rock, *because you did not trust Me*. Here Moses says it was *on your account*, *biglalkhem*. Both are in the Torah; Deuteronomy gives it from Moses' side.
- **v44 "as bees do"** — *ka'asher ta'asenah ha-devorim*. In Numbers 14:45 the Amalekites and Canaanites *struck them and beat them down as far as Hormah*; here the attackers are the Amorites and they swarm.

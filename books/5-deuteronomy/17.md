# Deuteronomy 17

## No Blemished Offering

**1** You shall not sacrifice to the LORD your God an ox or a sheep that has a blemish, any bad thing, for that is detestable to the LORD your God.

## On the Word of Two Witnesses

**2** If there is found among you, in any of your towns that the LORD your God is giving you, a man or a woman who does what is evil in the eyes of the LORD your God, transgressing His covenant,

**3** and has gone and served other gods and bowed down to them, or to the sun or the moon or any of the host of heaven, which I have not commanded,

**4** and it is told to you and you hear of it, then you shall inquire thoroughly. If it is true and the thing is certain, that this detestable thing has been done in Israel,

**5** you shall bring out that man or that woman who has done this evil thing to your gates — the man or the woman — and stone them, and they shall die.

**6** On the word of two witnesses or three witnesses the one who is to die shall be put to death. He shall not be put to death on the word of one witness.

**7** The hand of the witnesses shall be the first against him to put him to death, and afterward the hand of all the people. So you shall purge the evil from among you.

## The Case Too Hard for You

**8** If a case is too hard for you in judgment — between blood and blood, between claim and claim, between mark and mark — matters of dispute within your towns, then you shall get up and go up to the place that the LORD your God will choose.

**9** You shall come to the Levitical priests and to the judge who is in office in those days, and inquire, and they will declare to you the word of judgment.

**10** You shall do according to the word that they declare to you from that place that the LORD will choose, and you shall be careful to do all that they instruct you.

**11** According to the instruction that they teach you, and according to the judgment that they tell you, you shall do. You shall not turn aside from the word that they declare to you, to the right or to the left.

**12** The man who acts presumptuously, not listening to the priest who stands there to serve the LORD your God, or to the judge, that man shall die. So you shall purge the evil from Israel.

**13** All the people shall hear and be afraid, and will not act presumptuously again.

## The King

**14** When you come into the land that the LORD your God is giving you, and possess it and live in it, and you say, 'Let me set a king over me, like all the nations that are around me,'

**15** you shall surely set over you a king whom the LORD your God will choose. From among your brothers you shall set a king over you; you may not put over you a foreigner, who is not your brother.

**16** Only, he shall not multiply horses for himself, nor make the people return to Egypt in order to multiply horses, since the LORD has said to you, 'You shall never again return that way.'

**17** He shall not multiply wives for himself, so that his heart does not turn aside, and silver and gold he shall not greatly multiply for himself.

**18** And it shall come to pass, when he sits on the throne of his kingdom, he shall write for himself a copy of this instruction in a book, from what is before the Levitical priests.

**19** It shall be with him, and he shall read in it all the days of his life, so that he may learn to fear the LORD his God, to keep all the words of this instruction and these statutes, to do them,

**20** so that his heart is not lifted up above his brothers, and so that he does not turn aside from the commandment, to the right or to the left, so that he may prolong his days in his kingdom, he and his sons, in Israel.

## Notes

- **v1 "a blemish"** — *mum*, as in 15:21 and Leviticus 22:20. A defective animal offered to God is *to'evah*, the same word used of idols.
- **v4 "If it is true and the thing is certain"** — the words of 13:14, where a whole town was on trial. The same care is demanded for one person.
- **v6 "two witnesses or three witnesses"** — repeated as a general rule in 19:15. Jesus cites that verse in Matthew 18:16.
- **v7 "The hand of the witnesses shall be the first"** — the one who testifies must also be the first to act on his testimony. False witness would make him the killer.
- **v8 "between blood and blood… claim and claim… mark and mark"** — cases of killing, of property, and of *nega*, the **mark** of blight that the priest judges in Leviticus 13.
- **v11 "to the right or to the left"** — the phrase returns in v20 for the king: he too may not turn aside from the instruction.
- **v14 "like all the nations that are around me"** — the words the elders use in 1 Samuel 8:5: *set a king over us to judge us, like all the nations*.
- **vv16–17 horses, wives, silver and gold** — three things the king may not multiply. 1 Kings records Solomon importing horses from Egypt (10:28), and *seven hundred wives* who *turned his heart* (11:3).
- **v18 "a copy of this instruction"** — *mishneh ha-torah ha-zot*. The Greek reads *to deuteronomion touto*, **this second law**, which gave the book its Greek and English name (see the note on 1:1).
- **v20 "so that his heart is not lifted up above his brothers"** — the king is still a brother (v15), and the book in his hand is the same one the people keep.

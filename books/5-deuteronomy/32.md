# Deuteronomy 32

## The Song of Moses

**1** Give ear, heavens, and I will speak;  
let the earth hear the words of my mouth.

**2** Let my teaching drop like the rain,  
my speech distill like the dew,  
like showers on the new grass,  
like downpours on the plants.

**3** For I will proclaim the name of the LORD:  
ascribe greatness to our God!

**4** The Rock — His work is whole,  
for all His ways are justice.  
A God of faithfulness and without wrong,  
righteous and upright is He.

**5** They have acted corruptly toward Him;  
they are not His sons, it is their blemish —  
a crooked and twisted generation.

**6** Is this how you repay the LORD,  
foolish and unwise people?  
Is He not your Father, who acquired you,  
who made you and established you?

**7** Remember the days of old;  
consider the years of generation after generation.  
Ask your father, and he will tell you,  
your elders, and they will say to you.

**8** When the Most High gave the nations their inheritance,  
when He divided the sons of man,  
He set the boundaries of the peoples  
according to the number of the sons of Israel.

**9** For the LORD's portion is His people,  
Jacob the measured lot of His inheritance.

**10** He found him in a desert land,  
in the empty howling waste.  
He surrounded him, He cared for him,  
He guarded him as the pupil of His eye.

**11** Like an eagle that stirs up its nest,  
that hovers over its young,  
He spread His wings, He took him,  
He carried him on His pinions.

**12** The LORD alone led him,  
and no foreign god was with him.

**13** He made him ride on the heights of the earth,  
and he ate the produce of the field.  
He made him suck honey from the rock  
and oil from the flinty rock,

**14** curds of the herd and milk of the flock,  
with the fat of lambs,  
rams, the breed of Bashan, and goats,  
with the fat of the kidneys of wheat —  
and the blood of the grape you drank, foaming wine.

**15** Jeshurun grew fat and kicked —  
you grew fat, you grew thick, you were gorged —  
and he forsook God who made him  
and scorned the Rock of his salvation.

**16** They made Him jealous with strange gods;  
with detestable things they provoked Him.

**17** They sacrificed to demons, not God,  
to gods they had not known,  
new ones who came lately,  
whom your fathers did not dread.

**18** You neglected the Rock who bore you,  
and you forgot the God who brought you forth in labor.

**19** The LORD saw and spurned them,  
provoked by His sons and His daughters.

**20** He said, 'I will hide My face from them;  
I will see what their end will be,  
for they are a perverse generation,  
sons in whom there is no faithfulness.

**21** They made Me jealous with what is no god;  
they provoked Me with their empty things.  
So I will make them jealous with what is no people;  
with a foolish nation I will provoke them.

**22** For a fire is kindled in My anger,  
and it burns to the depths of Sheol;  
it devours the earth and its yield  
and sets on fire the foundations of the mountains.

**23** I will heap evils on them;  
I will spend My arrows on them.

**24** Wasted with hunger, devoured by plague  
and bitter destruction —  
the teeth of beasts I will send against them,  
with the venom of things that crawl in the dust.

**25** Outside the sword will bereave,  
and inside the rooms, terror,  
for young man and young woman alike,  
the nursing child with the man of gray hair.

**26** I said, "I will cut them to pieces;  
I will make their memory cease from among men,"

**27** had I not dreaded the provocation of the enemy,  
that their adversaries might misunderstand,  
that they might say, "Our hand is high,  
and it was not the LORD who did all this."'

**28** For they are a nation void of counsel,  
and there is no understanding in them.

**29** If they were wise, they would understand this;  
they would discern their end.

**30** How could one chase a thousand,  
and two put ten thousand to flight,  
unless their Rock had sold them  
and the LORD had given them up?

**31** For their rock is not like our Rock —  
our enemies themselves are judges of it.

**32** For their vine is from the vine of Sodom  
and from the fields of Gomorrah;  
their grapes are grapes of poison,  
their clusters are bitter.

**33** Their wine is the venom of serpents,  
the cruel poison of vipers.

**34** 'Is this not laid up in store with Me,  
sealed up in My treasuries?

**35** Vengeance is Mine, and recompense,  
for the time when their foot slips;  
for the day of their calamity is near,  
and what is prepared for them hurries on.'

**36** For the LORD will judge His people  
and have compassion on His servants,  
when He sees that their strength is gone  
and there is none left, bond or free.

**37** He will say, 'Where are their gods,  
the rock in which they took refuge,

**38** who ate the fat of their sacrifices  
and drank the wine of their drink offering?  
Let them rise up and help you;  
let them be your shelter!

**39** See now that I, I am He,  
and there is no god with Me.  
I put to death and I make alive;  
I wound and I heal,  
and there is no one who can deliver from My hand.

**40** For I lift up My hand to heaven  
and say: As I live forever,

**41** when I sharpen the lightning of My sword  
and My hand takes hold of judgment,  
I will return vengeance to My adversaries  
and repay those who hate Me.

**42** I will make My arrows drunk with blood,  
and My sword shall devour flesh —  
with the blood of the slain and the captives,  
from the long-haired heads of the enemy.'

**43** Rejoice, nations, with His people,  
for He avenges the blood of His servants.  
He returns vengeance to His adversaries  
and makes atonement for His land, His people.

**44** Moses came and spoke all the words of this song in the hearing of the people, he and Hoshea son of Nun.

**45** When Moses had finished speaking all these words to all Israel,

**46** he said to them, "Set your hearts on all the words by which I warn you today, which you shall command your sons to be careful to do, all the words of this instruction.

**47** For it is no empty word for you; it is your life, and by this word you will prolong your days on the ground that you are crossing the Jordan to possess."

## Go Up Mount Nebo

**48** The LORD spoke to Moses that very day, saying,

**49** "Go up this mountain of the Abarim, Mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab, opposite Jericho, and see the land of Canaan that I am giving to the sons of Israel as a possession.

**50** Die on the mountain that you are going up, and be gathered to your people, as Aaron your brother died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his people,

**51** because you broke faith with Me among the sons of Israel at the waters of Meribah of Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin, because you did not show Me holy among the sons of Israel.

**52** For you shall see the land from a distance, but you shall not go there, into the land that I am giving to the sons of Israel."

## Notes

- **v4 "The Rock"** — *ha-tsur*, God's name five times in the song (vv4, 15, 18, 30, 31), set against *their rock* (vv31, 37). *His work is whole*: *tamim*, the word used of a person whole toward God (18:13).
- **v5** — the Hebrew is broken and hard to construe: *corrupted to Him, not His sons, their blemish*. *Mum*, **blemish**, is the fixed word for a defect in an offering.
- **v6 "Is He not your Father, who acquired you"** — *qanekha*, the verb Eve uses in Genesis 4:1, *I have gotten a man*. One of the few places in the Torah where God is called Father.
- **v8 "according to the number of the sons of Israel"** — so the Hebrew. The Greek reads *according to the number of the angels of God*: each nation given to a heavenly being, while *the LORD's portion is His people* (v9). A Hebrew fragment from Qumran is reported to read *sons of God*. Both readings are old; this rendering follows the Hebrew it translates.
- **v10 "the pupil of His eye"** — *ishon*, **the little man**, the tiny figure one sees reflected in another's eye. *Empty*, *tohu*, is the word of Genesis 1:2, *wild and waste*.
- **v15 "Jeshurun"** — *Yeshurun*, a name for Israel from *yashar*, **upright**, used here with irony: the upright one grew fat and kicked. It appears again in 33:5 and 33:26.
- **v18 "the God who brought you forth in labor"** — *mecholelekha*, a verb of a mother's birth pains. The song gives God a father's name (v6) and a mother's labor.
- **v21 "no god… no people"** — *lo el… lo am*: jealousy for jealousy, measure for measure. Paul quotes the second half in Romans 10:19, *I will make you jealous by what is not a nation*.
- **v35 "Vengeance is Mine"** — *li naqam ve-shillem*. Paul quotes it in Romans 12:19 and Hebrews 10:30 has it with v36, *The Lord will judge His people*.
- **v43** — the Greek is much longer: *Rejoice, heavens, with Him, and let all the sons of God worship Him*. Hebrews 1:6 quotes it, *Let all the angels of God worship Him*, and Romans 15:10 quotes the line kept in both, *Rejoice, nations, with His people*.

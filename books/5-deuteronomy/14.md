# Deuteronomy 14

## Sons of the LORD

**1** You are sons of the LORD your God. You shall not gash yourselves or make a bald patch between your eyes for the dead,

**2** for you are a holy people to the LORD your God, and the LORD has chosen you to be a people for His own treasure, out of all the peoples who are on the face of the ground.

## What You May Eat

**3** You shall not eat any detestable thing.

**4** These are the animals you may eat: the ox, the sheep and the goat,

**5** the deer, the gazelle, the roebuck, the wild goat, the ibex, the antelope and the mountain sheep.

**6** Any animal that has a divided hoof, split in two, and brings up the cud among the animals, you may eat.

**7** But you shall not eat these, among those that bring up the cud or have a divided hoof, split: the camel, the hare and the rock badger, because they bring up the cud but do not have a divided hoof — they are unclean for you;

**8** and the pig, because it has a divided hoof but does not bring up the cud — it is unclean for you. You shall not eat their flesh, and you shall not touch their carcasses.

**9** Of all that is in the water you may eat these: anything that has fins and scales you may eat.

**10** Anything that does not have fins and scales you shall not eat. It is unclean for you.

**11** Every clean bird you may eat.

**12** These are the ones you shall not eat: the eagle, the bearded vulture, the black vulture,

**13** the buzzard, the falcon, and the kite of every kind,

**14** every kind of raven,

**15** the ostrich, the nighthawk, the gull, and the hawk of every kind,

**16** the owl, the great owl, the white owl,

**17** the pelican, the carrion vulture, the cormorant,

**18** the stork, the heron of every kind, the hoopoe, and the bat.

**19** Every winged swarming thing is unclean for you; they shall not be eaten.

**20** Every clean winged creature you may eat.

**21** You shall not eat any animal that has died on its own. You may give it to the guest who is inside your gates, and he may eat it, or you may sell it to a foreigner, for you are a holy people to the LORD your God. You shall not boil a young goat in its mother's milk.

## The Tithe

**22** You shall surely tithe all the yield of your seed that comes out of the field year by year.

**23** You shall eat before the LORD your God, in the place that He will choose to make His name dwell there, the tithe of your grain, your new wine and your oil, and the firstborn of your herd and of your flock, so that you may learn to fear the LORD your God all the days.

**24** If the way is too long for you, so that you are not able to carry it, because the place that the LORD your God will choose to put His name there is too far from you, when the LORD your God blesses you,

**25** then you shall turn it into silver, bind up the silver in your hand, and go to the place that the LORD your God will choose.

**26** Spend the silver on whatever you desire: on cattle or sheep, on wine or strong drink, on whatever your soul asks of you. Eat there before the LORD your God and rejoice, you and your household.

**27** The Levite who is inside your gates — do not forsake him, for he has no share or inheritance with you.

**28** At the end of three years you shall bring out all the tithe of your yield in that year and lay it up inside your gates.

**29** The Levite, because he has no share or inheritance with you, and the guest, the orphan and the widow who are inside your gates shall come and eat and be satisfied, so that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hand that you do.

## Notes

- **v1 "You are sons of the LORD your God"** — the reason given for not cutting the body in mourning. Leviticus 19:28 and 21:5 forbid the same gashes and bald patches; here the ground is sonship.
- **v3 "any detestable thing"** — *to'evah*, the word used of idols in 7:25–26, now of food. The list that follows spells out what counts.
- **vv4–5 ten clean animals** — three from the herd and flock, seven wild. Several of the seven Hebrew names (*yachmur*, *dishon*, *te'o*, *zemer*) occur only here or once more, and the English names are the usual identifications, not certainties.
- **vv7–8** — the list of Leviticus 11:4–8, in shorter form; the hare comes before the rock badger here.
- **v13 "the buzzard"** — *ha-ra'ah*, a word found only here. Leviticus 11:14 has *ha-da'ah* in the same place; in Hebrew letters ר and ד differ by one small stroke, and many take this for the same bird. Deuteronomy's list also has *ha-dayyah*, **the kite**, which Leviticus lacks.
- **v21 "You may give it to the guest… or sell it to a foreigner"** — Leviticus 17:15 held the *ger* who ate such meat to the same washing as the native. Deuteronomy's reason for Israel's abstaining is holiness: *for you are a holy people*.
- **v21 "You shall not boil a young goat in its mother's milk"** — the third time, after Exodus 23:19 and 34:26, and each time it closes a passage.
- **vv22–27 the tithe eaten at the feast** — Numbers 18:21 gave the tithe to the Levites. Here the family eats it at the chosen place, with the Levite as guest, and may even turn it into silver and buy *wine or strong drink* there. The two laws have been read as two different tithes.
- **vv28–29 the third-year tithe** — stored in the towns for those without land: the Levite, the *guest*, the orphan and the widow.

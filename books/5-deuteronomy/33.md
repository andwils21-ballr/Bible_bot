# Deuteronomy 33

## The LORD Came from Sinai

**1** This is the blessing with which Moses the man of God blessed the sons of Israel before his death.

**2** He said:

The LORD came from Sinai  
and dawned on them from Seir;  
He shone out from Mount Paran  
and came from the myriads of holy ones,  
from His right hand a fiery law for them.

**3** Yes, He loves the peoples;  
all His holy ones are in Your hand.  
They sat down at Your feet;  
each receives Your words.

**4** Moses commanded us an instruction,  
a possession for the assembly of Jacob.

**5** He became king in Jeshurun  
when the heads of the people gathered,  
the tribes of Israel together.

## The Tribes

**6** Let Reuben live and not die,  
though his men be few.

**7** This is for Judah. He said:  
Hear, LORD, the voice of Judah,  
and bring him to his people.  
With his hands he contends for them;  
be a help against his foes.

**8** Of Levi he said:  
Your Thummim and Your Urim belong to Your faithful man,  
whom You tested at Massah,  
with whom You strove at the waters of Meribah;

**9** who said of his father and his mother, "I have not seen them,"  
and did not acknowledge his brothers,  
and did not know his sons.  
For they kept Your word  
and guarded Your covenant.

**10** They teach Your rules to Jacob  
and Your instruction to Israel;  
they put incense before Your face  
and whole burnt offerings on Your altar.

**11** Bless, LORD, his strength,  
and accept the work of his hands.  
Strike through the loins of those who rise against him,  
and of those who hate him, so that they do not rise again.

**12** Of Benjamin he said:  
The beloved of the LORD —  
he dwells in safety by Him.  
He shields him all the day,  
and he dwells between His shoulders.

**13** Of Joseph he said:  
Blessed by the LORD be his land,  
with the choicest gifts of heaven, with the dew,  
and of the deep that crouches beneath,

**14** with the choicest fruits of the sun  
and the choicest yield of the months,

**15** with the finest of the ancient mountains  
and the choicest of the everlasting hills,

**16** with the choicest of the earth and its fullness,  
and the favor of the One who dwells in the bush.  
Let it come on the head of Joseph,  
on the crown of the one set apart from his brothers.

**17** His firstborn bull — majesty is his,  
and his horns are the horns of the wild ox.  
With them he gores the peoples,  
all together, to the ends of the earth.  
They are the myriads of Ephraim;  
they are the thousands of Manasseh.

**18** Of Zebulun he said:  
Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out,  
and Issachar, in your tents.

**19** They call peoples to the mountain;  
there they offer sacrifices of righteousness,  
for they suck the abundance of the seas  
and the hidden treasures of the sand.

**20** Of Gad he said:  
Blessed is He who enlarges Gad!  
He lies down like a lion  
and tears the arm, and the crown of the head.

**21** He chose the first part for himself,  
for there the ruler's portion was reserved.  
He came with the heads of the people;  
he did the righteousness of the LORD  
and His judgments with Israel.

**22** Of Dan he said:  
Dan is a lion's cub  
that leaps out from Bashan.

**23** Of Naphtali he said:  
Naphtali, satisfied with favor  
and full of the blessing of the LORD,  
take possession of the sea and the south.

**24** Of Asher he said:  
Most blessed of sons be Asher;  
let him be the favored of his brothers,  
and let him dip his foot in oil.

**25** Iron and bronze are your bolts,  
and as your days, so your strength.

## There Is None Like the God of Jeshurun

**26** There is none like God, Jeshurun,  
who rides the heavens to your help,  
and in His majesty the skies.

**27** The eternal God is a dwelling place,  
and underneath are the everlasting arms.  
He drove out the enemy before you  
and said, "Destroy!"

**28** So Israel dwells in safety,  
the fountain of Jacob alone,  
in a land of grain and new wine;  
yes, his heavens drip dew.

**29** Happy are you, Israel! Who is like you,  
a people saved by the LORD,  
the shield of your help  
and the sword of your majesty?  
Your enemies will cringe before you,  
and you will tread on their high places.

## Notes

- **v1 "Moses the man of God"** — *ish ha-Elohim*, the first time the title is used in the Bible. Psalm 90 is headed *A prayer of Moses, the man of God*.
- **v2 "from Sinai… from Seir… from Mount Paran"** — the LORD comes like the sunrise over the southern mountains. *A fiery law*: the written text has one word, *eshdat*, and the reading tradition splits it into *esh dat*, **fire, law**. What *eshdat* meant is not known.
- **v3** — difficult Hebrew; the pronouns shift between *He* and *You*, as often in these old poems.
- **v8 "Your Thummim and Your Urim"** — the order is reversed from Exodus 28:30, *the Urim and the Thummim*. Levi's place rests on testing at Massah and Meribah.
- **v9 "I have not seen them"** — the Levites who rallied to Moses at the calf and did not spare their own (Exodus 32:26–29). Loyalty to God is set above family.
- **v12 "between His shoulders"** — Benjamin's land held Jerusalem and the temple; the image may be of God carrying him, or of the sanctuary resting on Benjamin's hills.
- **vv13–16 Joseph** — the same words as Jacob's blessing in Genesis 49:26: *on the head of Joseph, on the crown of the one set apart from his brothers*. *The favor of the One who dwells in the bush*: *seneh*, the bush of Exodus 3.
- **v25 "as your days, so your strength"** — *dove'ekha*, a word found nowhere else. *Strength* is the old guess from context.
- **v26 "There is none like God, Jeshurun"** — *Jeshurun*, the name of 32:15, now in praise. Simeon is the one tribe not named in the blessing.
- **v27 "The eternal God is a dwelling place… underneath are the everlasting arms"** — *me'onah*, **dwelling**; Psalm 90, *A prayer of Moses, the man of God*, opens with the same root: *Lord, You have been our dwelling place*, *ma'on*. The last word of Moses' blessing about God is that He holds Israel up.

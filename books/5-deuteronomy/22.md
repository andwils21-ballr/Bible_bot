# Deuteronomy 22

## Do Not Look Away

**1** You shall not see your brother's ox or his sheep straying and hide yourself from them. You shall surely bring them back to your brother.

**2** If your brother is not near you, or you do not know him, you shall bring it into your house, and it shall stay with you until your brother seeks it, and you shall give it back to him.

**3** So you shall do with his donkey, so you shall do with his cloak, and so you shall do with anything your brother loses that you find. You may not hide yourself.

**4** You shall not see your brother's donkey or his ox fallen on the road and hide yourself from them. You shall surely help him lift them up.

## Laws of Order

**5** A woman shall not wear a man's gear, and a man shall not put on a woman's cloak, for whoever does these things is detestable to the LORD your God.

**6** If you come upon a bird's nest on the road, in any tree or on the ground, with young ones or eggs, and the mother sitting on the young or on the eggs, you shall not take the mother with the young.

**7** You shall surely let the mother go, and the young you may take for yourself, so that it may go well with you and you may prolong your days.

**8** When you build a new house, you shall make a parapet for your roof, so that you do not bring blood on your house if anyone falls from it.

**9** You shall not sow your vineyard with two kinds of seed, or the whole yield will become holy — the seed you have sown and the produce of the vineyard.

**10** You shall not plow with an ox and a donkey together.

**11** You shall not wear mixed cloth, wool and linen together.

**12** You shall make tassels for yourself on the four corners of your garment with which you cover yourself.

## The Slandered Bride

**13** If a man takes a wife and goes in to her, and then hates her,

**14** and makes charges against her and brings out a bad name on her, saying, 'I took this woman, and when I came near her I did not find in her the signs of virginity,'

**15** then the girl's father and her mother shall take the signs of the girl's virginity and bring them out to the elders of the town at the gate.

**16** The girl's father shall say to the elders, 'I gave my daughter to this man as his wife, and he hates her.

**17** He has made charges against her, saying, "I did not find in your daughter the signs of virginity." But these are the signs of my daughter's virginity.' They shall spread the cloth before the elders of the town.

**18** The elders of that town shall take the man and discipline him.

**19** They shall fine him a hundred pieces of silver and give them to the girl's father, because he has brought out a bad name on a virgin of Israel. She shall remain his wife; he may not send her away all his days.

**20** But if the thing is true, and the signs of virginity were not found in the girl,

**21** they shall bring the girl out to the entrance of her father's house, and the men of her town shall stone her with stones, and she shall die, because she has done an outrage in Israel, prostituting herself in her father's house. So you shall purge the evil from among you.

## Adultery and Rape

**22** If a man is found lying with a woman married to a husband, both of them shall die, the man who lay with the woman and the woman. So you shall purge the evil from Israel.

**23** If a girl who is a virgin is engaged to a man, and a man finds her in the town and lies with her,

**24** you shall bring them both out to the gate of that town and stone them with stones, and they shall die — the girl because she did not cry out in the town, and the man because he violated his neighbor's wife. So you shall purge the evil from among you.

**25** But if the man finds the engaged girl in the field, and the man seizes her and lies with her, then only the man who lay with her shall die.

**26** To the girl you shall do nothing; there is in the girl no sin deserving death. For this case is like a man who rises against his neighbor and murders him:

**27** he found her in the field; the engaged girl cried out, and there was no one to save her.

**28** If a man finds a girl who is a virgin, who is not engaged, and seizes her and lies with her, and they are found,

**29** the man who lay with her shall give the girl's father fifty pieces of silver, and she shall be his wife, because he has violated her. He may not send her away all his days.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "hide yourself"** — *hit'allamta*, **make yourself not see**. Exodus 23:4–5 commanded the same for *your enemy's ox* and *the one who hates you*; here the animal is *your brother's*, and the law names the temptation: looking away.
- **v6 "you shall not take the mother with the young"** — and the reward is the same as for honoring father and mother (5:16, *so that your days may be long, and so that it may go well with you*): the smallest command carries the promise of the greatest.
- **v8 "a parapet for your roof"** — flat roofs were used as living space. A householder who leaves the edge open is responsible for the fall: *blood on your house*.
- **vv9–11 two kinds** — Leviticus 19:19 forbade sowing a field with two kinds and wearing *a garment woven of two kinds of material*. Here *sha'atnez*, **mixed cloth**, is defined: *wool and linen together*. *Become holy* means forfeit to the sanctuary.
- **v12 "tassels"** — *gedilim*, **twisted cords**, a different word from the *tsitsit* of Numbers 15:38, *tassels on the corners of their garments*, but the same command.
- **v15 and on "the girl"** — *na'arah*. In this chapter the written Hebrew mostly spells the word *na'ar*, which elsewhere means **boy**, and the reading tradition says *na'arah*. The Five Books spell it this way throughout; this rendering follows what is read.
- **v21 "an outrage in Israel"** — *nevalah*, the word said of Shechem in Genesis 34:7, *he had done an outrage in Israel*.
- **vv23–27 the town and the field** — the law judges by what the girl could do. In the town a cry would have been heard; in the field *there was no one to save her*, and she is treated as one who was attacked, like a murder victim (v26).
- **vv28–29 the girl not engaged** — Exodus 22:16–17 has the same case: payment to the father and marriage, which the father may refuse. Deuteronomy fixes the payment and forbids the man ever to divorce her.

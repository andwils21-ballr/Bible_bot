# Deuteronomy 11

## What Your Eyes Have Seen

**1** Love the LORD your God, and keep His charge, His statutes and His rules and His commandments, all the days.

**2** Know today — for I am not speaking to your sons, who have not known and have not seen — the discipline of the LORD your God, His greatness, His strong hand and His outstretched arm,

**3** His signs and His works that He did in the middle of Egypt, to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to all his land,

**4** and what He did to the army of Egypt, to its horses and its chariots: how He made the waters of the Sea of Reeds flow over them as they pursued you, and the LORD destroyed them to this day;

**5** and what He did for you in the wilderness, until you came to this place;

**6** and what He did to Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab son of Reuben, when the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them, and their households and their tents and every standing thing that followed them, in the middle of all Israel.

**7** For your own eyes have seen all the great work of the LORD that He did.

## A Land the LORD Cares For

**8** Keep all the commandment that I command you today, so that you may be strong, and go in and possess the land that you are crossing over into, to possess it,

**9** and so that you may prolong your days on the ground that the LORD swore to your fathers to give to them and to their seed, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**10** For the land that you are going into to possess is not like the land of Egypt that you came out of, where you sowed your seed and watered it with your foot, like a vegetable garden.

**11** The land that you are crossing over into, to possess it, is a land of hills and valleys; it drinks water by the rain of the heavens,

**12** a land that the LORD your God cares for. The eyes of the LORD your God are always on it, from the beginning of the year to the end of the year.

## Rain in Its Season

**13** And it shall come to pass, if you listen, listen to My commandments that I command you today, to love the LORD your God and to serve Him with all your heart and with all your soul,

**14** I will give the rain of your land in its season, the early rain and the late rain, and you will gather in your grain and your new wine and your oil.

**15** I will give grass in your field for your animals, and you will eat and be satisfied.

**16** Guard yourselves so that your heart is not enticed, and you turn aside and serve other gods and bow down to them.

**17** Then the anger of the LORD will burn against you, and He will shut up the heavens so that there is no rain, and the ground will not give its produce, and you will perish quickly from the good land that the LORD is giving you.

## Lay Up These Words

**18** Put these words of Mine on your heart and on your soul. Bind them as a sign on your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes.

**19** Teach them to your sons, speaking of them when you sit in your house and when you walk on the road, when you lie down and when you rise.

**20** Write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates,

**21** so that your days and the days of your sons may be many on the ground that the LORD swore to your fathers to give them, as long as the heavens are above the earth.

**22** For if you are careful to keep all this commandment that I command you, to do it — to love the LORD your God, to walk in all His ways and to hold fast to Him —

**23** the LORD will drive out all these nations from before you, and you will dispossess nations greater and mightier than you.

**24** Every place on which the sole of your foot treads will be yours. From the wilderness and Lebanon, from the river, the river Euphrates, to the western sea will be your border.

**25** No one will be able to stand before you. The LORD your God will lay the dread of you and the fear of you on all the land that you tread on, as He has spoken to you.

## Gerizim and Ebal

**26** See, I am setting before you today a blessing and a curse:

**27** the blessing, if you listen to the commandments of the LORD your God that I command you today;

**28** and the curse, if you do not listen to the commandments of the LORD your God, but turn aside from the way that I command you today, to go after other gods that you have not known.

**29** And it shall come to pass, when the LORD your God brings you into the land that you are going into to possess, you shall set the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal.

**30** Are they not beyond the Jordan, past the road toward the setting of the sun, in the land of the Canaanite who lives in the Arabah, opposite Gilgal, beside the oaks of Moreh?

**31** For you are crossing the Jordan to go in and possess the land that the LORD your God is giving you. You will possess it and live in it,

**32** and you shall be careful to do all the statutes and the rules that I am setting before you today.

## Notes

- **v2 "for I am not speaking to your sons"** — the sentence in Hebrew breaks off and restarts, as speech does. Moses insists on eyewitnesses: *your own eyes have seen* (v7).
- **v6 "every standing thing that followed them"** — *kol ha-yequm asher be-ragleihem*, **every standing thing at their feet**. *Yequm* is the rare word of the flood, *every standing thing that I have made* (Genesis 7:4), and occurs only in the flood story (Genesis 7:4, 7:23) and here. Korah is not named, only the Reubenites of Numbers 16.
- **v10 "watered it with your foot"** — *hishqita ve-raglekha*. Egypt's fields drank from the Nile by channels opened and closed with the foot, or water wheels worked by foot. The land ahead depends on rain, which no one can command.
- **v12 "a land that the LORD your God cares for"** — *doresh otah*, **seeks it out**. The dependence on rain (v11) is also a closeness: God's eyes are on this land all year.
- **v14 "the early rain and the late rain"** — *yoreh u-malqosh*: the autumn rains that soften the ground for plowing, and the spring rains that fill the grain before harvest.
- **vv13–21** — the second passage of the Shema in Jewish prayer, after 6:4–9, and written with it in the tefillin and the mezuzah. Verses 18–20 repeat 6:6–9, now spoken to *you* in the plural.
- **v24 "Every place on which the sole of your foot treads"** — repeated to Joshua in Joshua 1:3.
- **vv29–30 Gerizim and Ebal** — two mountains facing each other above Shechem, *beside the oaks of Moreh*, where Abram first stopped in the land (Genesis 12:6) and God first promised it to his seed. The ceremony is laid out in chapter 27 and done in Joshua 8:30–35.

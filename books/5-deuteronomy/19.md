# Deuteronomy 19

## Three Towns in the Land

**1** When the LORD your God cuts off the nations whose land the LORD your God is giving you, and you dispossess them and live in their towns and in their houses,

**2** you shall set apart three towns for yourself within your land that the LORD your God is giving you to possess.

**3** You shall prepare the road for yourself, and divide into three parts the territory of your land that the LORD your God gives you as an inheritance, so that anyone who kills may flee there.

**4** This is the case of the one who kills who may flee there and live: whoever strikes his neighbor unintentionally, without having hated him before —

**5** as when a man goes into the forest with his neighbor to cut wood, and his hand swings the axe to cut down the tree, and the iron slips from the handle and strikes his neighbor so that he dies — he may flee to one of these towns and live.

**6** Otherwise the avenger of blood, pursuing the one who killed while his heart is hot, may overtake him because the way is long, and strike him dead, though he did not deserve to die, since he had not hated him before.

**7** That is why I command you, saying, 'You shall set apart three towns for yourself.'

**8** If the LORD your God enlarges your territory, as He swore to your fathers, and gives you all the land that He promised to give to your fathers —

**9** if you are careful to do all this commandment that I command you today, to love the LORD your God and to walk in His ways all the days — then you shall add three more towns for yourself to these three,

**10** so that innocent blood is not shed within your land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, and so blood guilt is not on you.

**11** But if a man hates his neighbor and lies in wait for him, and rises against him and strikes him dead, and he flees to one of these towns,

**12** the elders of his town shall send and take him from there and give him into the hand of the avenger of blood, and he shall die.

**13** Your eye shall not pity him. You shall purge the innocent blood from Israel, and it will go well with you.

## The Boundary Marker

**14** You shall not move your neighbor's boundary marker, which the men of old set, in your inheritance that you will inherit in the land that the LORD your God is giving you to possess.

## Witnesses

**15** A single witness shall not rise against a man for any guilt or any sin, in any sin that he sins. On the word of two witnesses or on the word of three witnesses a matter shall stand.

**16** If a violent witness rises against a man to testify against him of wrongdoing,

**17** then both men who have the dispute shall stand before the LORD, before the priests and the judges who are in office in those days.

**18** The judges shall inquire thoroughly, and if the witness is a false witness who has testified falsely against his brother,

**19** you shall do to him as he planned to do to his brother. So you shall purge the evil from among you.

**20** The rest shall hear and be afraid, and they will never again do anything like this evil thing among you.

**21** Your eye shall not pity: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.

## Notes

- **vv2–3 three towns in the land** — Moses has already set apart three beyond the Jordan (4:41–43); these are the three *in the land of Canaan* of Numbers 35:14. *Prepare the road* so that no one is too far from one.
- **v4 "without having hated him before"** — *mi-temol shilshom*, **from yesterday and the day before**: no history of hatred. The same test as 4:42.
- **v5 the axe** — the law gives one plain case instead of a definition: *the iron slips from the handle*. Anyone can see that no one meant it.
- **v6 "while his heart is hot"** — the avenger is not blamed for his anger; the town of refuge exists because anger will not stop to ask.
- **vv8–9 three more towns** — if the land grows to the full promise. Joshua 20:7–8 names six towns in all, three on each side of the Jordan; the further three are never named.
- **v13 "purge the innocent blood from Israel"** — the formula of 13:6, with *innocent blood* for *the evil*. Numbers 35:33: *blood pollutes the land*.
- **v14 "which the men of old set"** — *rishonim*, the first ones. Moving a boundary stone steals land quietly, from people who cannot see it happen; Proverbs 22:28 repeats the command.
- **v15 "a matter shall stand"** — the rule of 17:6 widened from capital cases to *any guilt or any sin*. Matthew 18:16 quotes it: *every matter may be established by the mouth of two or three witnesses*.
- **v16 "a violent witness"** — *ed chamas*, the word of the flood, *the earth was filled with violence* (Genesis 6:11). False testimony is violence done with words.
- **vv19, 21 "as he planned to do to his brother… eye for eye"** — the false witness receives what his lie would have done. The list here matches Exodus 21:23–24; Jesus quotes *Eye for eye, and tooth for tooth* in Matthew 5:38.

# Deuteronomy 30

## When You Return

**1** And it shall come to pass, when all these things come on you, the blessing and the curse that I have set before you, and you take them to heart among all the nations where the LORD your God has driven you,

**2** and you return to the LORD your God and listen to His voice according to all that I command you today, you and your sons, with all your heart and with all your soul,

**3** then the LORD your God will restore your fortunes and have compassion on you, and He will gather you again from all the peoples where the LORD your God has scattered you.

**4** If your outcasts are at the end of the heavens, from there the LORD your God will gather you, and from there He will take you.

**5** The LORD your God will bring you into the land that your fathers possessed, and you will possess it, and He will do you good and multiply you more than your fathers.

**6** The LORD your God will circumcise your heart and the heart of your seed, to love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul, so that you may live.

**7** The LORD your God will put all these curses on your enemies and on those who hate you, who persecuted you.

**8** You will return and listen to the voice of the LORD and do all His commandments that I command you today.

**9** The LORD your God will make you abound in all the work of your hand, in the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your animals and the fruit of your ground, for good. For the LORD will again delight over you for good, as He delighted over your fathers,

**10** when you listen to the voice of the LORD your God, to keep His commandments and His statutes that are written in this book of the instruction, when you return to the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

## Not Too Far Away

**11** For this commandment that I command you today is not too hard for you, and it is not far off.

**12** It is not in the heavens, that you should say, 'Who will go up for us to the heavens and bring it to us and let us hear it, so that we may do it?'

**13** Nor is it beyond the sea, that you should say, 'Who will cross over for us to the other side of the sea and bring it to us and let us hear it, so that we may do it?'

**14** For the word is very near you, in your mouth and in your heart, so that you can do it.

## Life and Death

**15** See, I have set before you today life and good, and death and evil,

**16** in that I command you today to love the LORD your God, to walk in His ways and to keep His commandments and His statutes and His rules, so that you may live and multiply, and the LORD your God will bless you in the land that you are going into to possess.

**17** But if your heart turns away and you will not listen, and you are drawn away and bow down to other gods and serve them,

**18** I declare to you today that you will perish, perish. You will not prolong your days on the ground that you are crossing the Jordan to go into and possess.

**19** I call the heavens and the earth to witness against you today: I have set before you life and death, the blessing and the curse. Choose life, so that you may live, you and your seed,

**20** loving the LORD your God, listening to His voice and holding fast to Him — for He is your life and the length of your days — so that you may live on the ground that the LORD swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, to give them.

## Notes

- **vv1–10 "return"** — the verb *shuv* runs through the passage seven times: *take them to heart* (v1, **bring back to your heart**), *you return* (vv2, 8, 10), *the LORD will restore your fortunes*, *shav et shevutkha* (v3), *gather you again*, **return and gather** (v3), and *will again delight* (v9). When Israel turns back, God turns back.
- **v4 "at the end of the heavens"** — *bi-qtseh ha-shamayim*: the horizon, the farthest edge anyone could be driven to.
- **v6 "The LORD your God will circumcise your heart"** — in 10:16 the people were told, *Circumcise, then, the foreskin of your heart*. Here God promises to do it Himself.
- **v9 "delight over you for good, as He delighted over your fathers"** — the verb *sas* of 28:63, where God would *delight… to make you perish*. The last word is not that one.
- **vv11–14 "not in the heavens… nor beyond the sea"** — no hero needs to climb to heaven or cross the sea to fetch the commandment; it has already been given. Paul reads the verses of Christ in Romans 10:6–8, putting *the abyss* where the Hebrew has *the sea*, and ending with *The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart*.
- **v15 "life and good, and death and evil"** — the pair of words from *the tree of the knowing of good and evil* (Genesis 2:9), now laid before Israel as a choice.
- **v19 "I call the heavens and the earth to witness"** — as in 4:26. The witnesses will outlast the people who hear the words.
- **v20 "for He is your life"** — *ki hu chayyekha*. What v19 asks Israel to choose is, in the end, God Himself.

# Deuteronomy 27

## Write the Instruction on Stones

**1** Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people, saying, "Keep all the commandment that I command you today.

**2** And it shall come to pass, on the day you cross the Jordan into the land that the LORD your God is giving you, you shall set up for yourself large stones and plaster them with plaster.

**3** You shall write on them all the words of this instruction when you cross over, so that you may enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you, a land flowing with milk and honey, as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has promised you.

**4** And it shall come to pass, when you have crossed the Jordan, you shall set up these stones, as I command you today, on Mount Ebal, and plaster them with plaster.

**5** There you shall build an altar to the LORD your God, an altar of stones. You shall not wield any iron tool on them.

**6** Of whole stones you shall build the altar of the LORD your God, and you shall offer burnt offerings on it to the LORD your God.

**7** You shall sacrifice peace offerings and eat there, and rejoice before the LORD your God.

**8** You shall write on the stones all the words of this instruction, making them very plain."

**9** Moses and the Levitical priests spoke to all Israel, saying, "Be silent and hear, Israel: this day you have become a people to the LORD your God.

**10** You shall listen to the voice of the LORD your God and do His commandments and His statutes that I command you today."

## Blessing and Curse

**11** Moses commanded the people that day, saying,

**12** "These shall stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people when you have crossed the Jordan: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph and Benjamin.

**13** These shall stand on Mount Ebal for the curse: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan and Naphtali.

**14** The Levites shall answer and say to every man of Israel in a loud voice:

**15** 'Cursed is the man who makes a carved or cast image, detestable to the LORD, the work of a craftsman's hands, and sets it up in secret.' All the people shall answer and say, 'Amen.'

**16** 'Cursed is he who dishonors his father or his mother.' All the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**17** 'Cursed is he who moves his neighbor's boundary marker.' All the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**18** 'Cursed is he who makes a blind man stray on the road.' All the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**19** 'Cursed is he who bends the judgment of the guest, the orphan and the widow.' All the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**20** 'Cursed is he who lies with his father's wife, for he has uncovered his father's skirt.' All the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**21** 'Cursed is he who lies with any animal.' All the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**22** 'Cursed is he who lies with his sister, his father's daughter or his mother's daughter.' All the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**23** 'Cursed is he who lies with his mother-in-law.' All the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**24** 'Cursed is he who strikes his neighbor in secret.' All the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**25** 'Cursed is he who takes a bribe to strike down a life, innocent blood.' All the people shall say, 'Amen.'

**26** 'Cursed is he who does not uphold the words of this instruction by doing them.' All the people shall say, 'Amen.'"

## Notes

- **v1 "Moses and the elders of Israel"** — here Moses commands together with the elders, and in v9 together with the priests. The speech is handing itself over to those who will remain after him.
- **vv2–4 plastered stones** — the writing is on plaster laid over the stones, not carved into them. Joshua 8:32 tells of writing *a copy of the instruction of Moses* on stones there.
- **vv5–6 "You shall not wield any iron tool on them… whole stones"** — the altar law of Exodus 20:25: *you shall not build them cut — because you have waved your sword over it and profaned it*. *Whole*, *shelemot*, is the root of the *peace offerings*, *shelamim*, of v7.
- **v8 "making them very plain"** — *ba'er heitev*, the verb of 1:5, where Moses *undertook to explain* the instruction. The book ends as it began: the words are meant to be understood.
- **vv12–13 the tribes on the two mountains** — the sons of Leah and Rachel stand for blessing; the sons of the servant women Bilhah and Zilpah, with Reuben and Zebulun, stand for the curse.
- **vv14–26 twelve curses, twelve Amens** — each curse names something done where no judge would see: *in secret* (vv15, 24), to the blind, to the powerless, within the family. The people bind themselves with their own *Amen* to what no court could enforce.
- **v16 "dishonors"** — *maqleh*, from *qalal*, **to be light**, the opposite of *kabbed*, **honor**, whose root means **to be heavy** (5:16).
- **v18 "makes a blind man stray"** — Leviticus 19:14: *you shall not put a stumbling block in front of a blind man*.
- **v26 "does not uphold the words of this instruction"** — Paul quotes the verse in Galatians 3:10 in its Greek form, which adds *all*: *Cursed is everyone who does not remain in all the things written in the book of the Law, to do them*.

# Deuteronomy 34

## Moses Sees the Land

**1** Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, the top of Pisgah, which is opposite Jericho. The LORD showed him all the land: Gilead as far as Dan,

**2** all Naphtali, the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, all the land of Judah as far as the western sea,

**3** the Negev, and the plain of the valley of Jericho, the city of palm trees, as far as Zoar.

**4** The LORD said to him, "This is the land that I swore to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, saying, 'To your seed I will give it.' I have let you see it with your eyes, but you shall not cross over there."

## The Death of Moses

**5** So Moses the servant of the LORD died there in the land of Moab, at the word of the LORD.

**6** He buried him in the valley in the land of Moab, opposite Beth-peor, and no one knows his burial place to this day.

**7** Moses was a hundred and twenty years old when he died. His eye was not dim, and his vigor had not fled.

**8** The sons of Israel wept for Moses in the plains of Moab thirty days, and the days of weeping and mourning for Moses were ended.

**9** Joshua son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom, for Moses had leaned his hands on him. The sons of Israel listened to him and did as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**10** There has not risen a prophet since in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face,

**11** for all the signs and the wonders that the LORD sent him to do in the land of Egypt, to Pharaoh and to all his servants and to all his land,

**12** and for all the mighty hand and all the great terror that Moses did before the eyes of all Israel.

## Notes

- **v4 "To your seed I will give it"** — the words of Genesis 12:7, *To your seed I will give this land*, first spoken to Abram at Shechem. The Torah ends where the promise began.
- **v6 "He buried him… no one knows his burial place"** — the subject is not named; the natural reading is that God Himself buried Moses. No grave could become a shrine. Jude 9 knows a later story of *Michael the archangel* disputing *about the body of Moses*.
- **vv10–12 "There has not risen a prophet since in Israel like Moses"** — the answer, from the far side of his death, to 18:15, *a prophet… like me*. *Face to face*: Exodus 33:11, and Numbers 12:8, *Mouth to mouth I speak with him*. The last words of the Torah are *before the eyes of all Israel*.

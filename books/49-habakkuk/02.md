# Habakkuk 2

## Write the Vision

**1** I will stand at my post  
and station myself on the rampart;  
I will watch to see what He will say to me,  
and what I will answer to my complaint.

**2** The LORD answered me and said:  
Write the vision;  
make it plain on the tablets,  
so that the one who reads it may run.

**3** For the vision is still for the appointed time;  
it testifies of the end, and it will not lie.  
If it delays, wait for it;  
for it will surely come; it will not be late.

**4** The presumptuous one —  
his soul is not upright in him;  
but the righteous will live by his faithfulness.

**5** Moreover, wine is treacherous;  
the arrogant man does not stay at home.  
He opens his throat wide like Sheol,  
and like death he is never satisfied;  
he gathers to himself all the nations  
and collects to himself all the peoples.

## Five Woes

**6** Will not all of these take up a taunt against him,  
a figure and riddles about him, and say:  
Woe to him who multiplies what is not his own —  
how long? —  
and loads himself with pledges!

**7** Will not your creditors suddenly rise up,  
and those who make you tremble wake up?  
Then you will be spoil for them.

**8** Because you have plundered many nations,  
all the rest of the peoples will plunder you,  
for the blood of man and the violence done to the land,  
to the city and all who live in it.

**9** Woe to him who is greedy for evil gain for his house,  
to set his nest on high,  
to be delivered from the hand of evil!

**10** You have counseled shame for your house,  
cutting off many peoples,  
and you sin against your own life.

**11** For the stone will cry out from the wall,  
and the beam from the woodwork will answer it.

**12** Woe to him who builds a city with blood  
and establishes a town with injustice!

**13** Is it not from the LORD of hosts  
that peoples labor only for fire,  
and nations weary themselves for nothing?

**14** For the earth will be filled  
with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD,  
as the waters cover the sea.

**15** Woe to him who makes his neighbor drink,  
mixing in your venom and making him drunk,  
in order to look on their nakedness!

**16** You are filled with shame instead of glory.  
Drink, you too, and show your foreskin!  
The cup in the LORD's right hand will come around to you,  
and utter shame will cover your glory.

**17** For the violence done to Lebanon will cover you,  
and the destruction of the beasts will terrify them,  
for the blood of man and the violence done to the land,  
to the city and all who live in it.

**18** What profit is a carved image,  
that its maker has carved it,  
a cast image, a teacher of lies?  
For its maker trusts in what he has made  
when he makes mute idols.

**19** Woe to him who says to wood, "Wake up!"  
to a mute stone, "Rouse yourself!"  
Can it teach?  
It is overlaid with gold and silver,  
and there is no breath at all inside it.

**20** But the LORD is in His holy temple;  
silence before Him, all the earth!

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "I will stand at my post… what I will answer to my complaint… Write the vision; make it plain on the tablets"** — the prophet as watchman, as in Isaiah's *On the watchtower, Lord, I stand continually by day, and at my post I am stationed every night* (Isaiah 21:8), and *atsappeh*, **I will watch**, as in Micah 7:7. *Tokhachti*, **my complaint**, is from the verb of 1:12, *to rebuke*: the prophet waits for the answer to his own charge. *Ba'er*, **make it plain**, as the law was to be written, *making them very plain* (Deuteronomy 27:8), and *luchot*, **tablets**, as in *write it before them on a tablet* (Isaiah 30:8). *So that the one who reads it may run*: the reader runs, perhaps to carry the news.
- **v3 "it testifies of the end, and it will not lie… it will surely come"** — *yafeach… ve-lo yekhazzev*, the words of the proverb, *A faithful witness does not lie, but a false witness breathes out lies* (Proverbs 14:5): *yafeach* is **breathe out**, and of a witness, **testify**. The vision is a witness who will not lie. *Bo yavo*, **it will surely come**. The Greek has *wait for him, for the one who comes will come*, and Hebrews gives the verse in that form (Hebrews 10:37).
- **v4 "The presumptuous one — his soul is not upright in him; but the righteous will live by his faithfulness"** — *uppelah*, **presumptuous**, is the verb of Israel at Hormah, *they presumed to go up* (Numbers 14:44). *Emunah*, **faithfulness**, is steadiness that holds: Moses' hands *stayed steady* (Exodus 17:12), and *great is Your faithfulness* (Lamentations 3:23). The Greek has *the righteous will live by My faith*, God's faithfulness, and has *if he shrinks back, My soul has no pleasure in him* for the first half. Paul gives the line with neither *his* nor *My* (Romans 1:17; Galatians 3:11), and Hebrews with *My* joined to *the righteous* (Hebrews 10:38).
- **v5 "wine is treacherous… He opens his throat wide like Sheol, and like death he is never satisfied"** — *boged*, **treacherous**, the word of 1:13. *Hirchiv ki-She'ol nafsho*, word for word **enlarges his throat like Sheol**, as Isaiah says of Sheol itself, *Sheol has opened wide its throat* (Isaiah 5:14). *Lo yinveh*, **does not stay at home**, is uncertain. For *wine is treacherous* the Greek has *the arrogant scoffer*.
- **vv6–8 "take up a taunt against him… loads himself with pledges… your creditors"** — *mashal yissa'u*, **take up a taunt**, as in Micah 2:4, and *melitsah chidot*, **a figure and riddles**, as in *a proverb and a figure, the words of the wise and their riddles* (Proverbs 1:6). Five woes follow (vv6, 9, 12, 15, 19). *Avtit*, **pledges**, is found only here; it sounds like *av tit*, **thick mud**. *Noshkhekha*, **your creditors**, word for word **those who bite you**: *nashakh* is to bite and to charge interest, as in *You shall not charge interest to your brother* (Deuteronomy 23:19). The plunderer is plundered, and the line *for the blood of man and the violence done to the land* comes back word for word in v17.
- **vv9–11 "greedy for evil gain… to set his nest on high… the stone will cry out from the wall"** — *botsea betsa*, **greedy for gain**, as in *everyone who is greedy for gain* (Proverbs 1:19). *His nest on high* is Edom's in Obadiah 4. *Chote nafshekha*, **you sin against your own life**, as in *whoever provokes him sins against his own life* (Proverbs 20:2). The house built by violence testifies against its builder: stone and beam cry out, as Abel's blood was *crying out to Me from the ground* (Genesis 4:10). *Kafis*, **beam**, is found only here.
- **vv12–14 "Woe to him who builds a city with blood… peoples labor only for fire… as the waters cover the sea"** — v12 has Micah's words of Jerusalem, *who build Zion with blood and Jerusalem with injustice* (Micah 3:10). v13 is Jeremiah's of Babylon with its halves changed round, *The peoples labor for nothing, and the nations weary themselves only for fire* (Jeremiah 51:58). v14 is Isaiah's, *for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the LORD as the waters cover the sea* (Isaiah 11:9), with *the glory* added: the cities of blood burn, and the earth is filled instead with the glory of the LORD.
- **vv15–16 "mixing in your venom… to look on their nakedness… show your foreskin!… The cup in the LORD's right hand"** — *chamatekha*, **your venom**, is *chemah*, both **wrath** and **venom**, as in *the venom of things that crawl in the dust* (Deuteronomy 32:24). Making a man drunk to see him naked recalls Ham, who *saw the nakedness of his father* (Genesis 9:22). *Ve-he'arel*, **show your foreskin**, from *orlah*; the Greek has *be shaken in heart and quake*, as if from *ra'al*, **reel**, the same letters in another order. The cup is the cup of wrath, *Take from My hand this cup of the wine of wrath* (Jeremiah 25:15). *Qiqalon*, **utter shame**, found only here, is *qalon*, **shame**, of the line before, made stronger.
- **vv17–19 "the violence done to Lebanon… a teacher of lies… there is no breath at all inside it"** — the violence done to Lebanon may be the cutting of its cedars, as Isaiah's cedars say of the king of Babylon, *Since you were laid low, no woodcutter comes up against us* (Isaiah 14:8). *Moreh shaqer*, **a teacher of lies**, as in *the prophet who teaches lies* (Isaiah 9:15), and v19 asks *hu yoreh*, **can it teach?** *Elilim illemim*, **mute idols**, two words of nearly one sound. The idols of the psalm, *They have mouths but do not speak* (Psalm 115:5), and of Jeremiah, *there is no breath in them* (Jeremiah 10:14). *Wake up!… Rouse yourself!* are the cries that Joel gave to the drunkards and the nations (Joel 1:5; 3:9).
- **v20 "But the LORD is in His holy temple; silence before Him, all the earth!"** — the words of the psalm, *The LORD is in His holy temple* (Psalm 11:4), against the mute idols: they cannot speak, and before Him all must be silent. *Has*, **silence**, is the hush of Amos's ruined houses, *Silence!* (Amos 6:10; 8:3).

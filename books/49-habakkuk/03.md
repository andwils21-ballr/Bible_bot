# Habakkuk 3

## Habakkuk's Prayer

**1** A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, on Shigionoth.

**2** LORD, I have heard the report of You;  
I fear, LORD, Your work.  
In the middle of the years revive it;  
in the middle of the years make it known;  
in wrath remember compassion.

## God Came from Teman

**3** God came from Teman,  
the Holy One from Mount Paran. *Selah*  
His splendor covered the heavens,  
and the earth was full of His praise.

**4** His brightness was like the light;  
rays came from His hand,  
and there His power was hidden.

**5** Before Him went plague,  
and fiery bolts went out at His feet.

**6** He stood and measured the earth;  
He looked and made the nations start;  
the everlasting mountains were shattered,  
the ancient hills sank low;  
His ways are as of old.

**7** I saw the tents of Cushan in distress;  
the curtains of the land of Midian trembled.

**8** Was it against the rivers, LORD, that You were angry?  
Was Your anger against the rivers,  
or Your fury against the sea,  
when You rode on Your horses,  
Your chariots of salvation?

**9** Your bow was laid bare;  
the shafts were sworn by Your word. *Selah*  
You split the earth with rivers.

**10** The mountains saw You and writhed;  
a downpour of waters swept by;  
the deep gave its voice;  
it lifted its hands on high.

**11** Sun and moon stood still in their dwelling,  
at the light of Your arrows as they went,  
at the gleam of the lightning of Your spear.

**12** In indignation You marched through the earth;  
in anger You threshed the nations.

**13** You went out for the salvation of Your people,  
for the salvation of Your anointed.  
You crushed the head of the house of the wicked,  
laying bare the foundation up to the neck. *Selah*

**14** You pierced with his own shafts the head of his warriors,  
who stormed out to scatter me,  
exulting as if to devour the poor in secret.

**15** You trampled the sea with Your horses,  
the foaming of many waters.

## Yet I Will Exult

**16** I heard, and my inward parts trembled;  
at the sound my lips quivered;  
rot entered my bones,  
and where I stand I tremble.  
Yet I will wait quietly for the day of distress  
to come up on the people who invade us.

**17** Though the fig tree does not blossom,  
and there is no fruit on the vines,  
though the yield of the olive fails  
and the fields produce no food,  
though the flock is cut off from the fold  
and there is no herd in the stalls,

**18** yet I will exult in the LORD;  
I will rejoice in the God of my salvation.

**19** GOD the Lord is my strength;  
He makes my feet like the deer's  
and makes me tread on my high places.  
For the director: with my stringed instruments.

## Notes

- **v1 "A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, on Shigionoth"** — the chapter is a psalm, with a psalm's heading, *Selah* three times (vv3, 9, 13), and a closing note for the musicians (v19), as in *For the director: with stringed instruments* (Psalm 4, title). *Shigyonot* is the plural of the *shiggayon* of Psalm 7's title, *A Shiggaion of David*, the only other place the word is found; what kind of song it names is not known.
- **v2 "I have heard the report of You… in wrath remember compassion"** — *shim'akha*, **the report of You**, what is told of the LORD's deeds; the prophet asks that they be done again *in the middle of the years*, now. *Be-rogez*, **in wrath**, is from *ragaz*, **tremble**: the same root comes back as the tents of Midian *trembled* (v7) and as the prophet trembles (v16). *Rachem*, **compassion**, as in *He will again have compassion on us* (Micah 7:19). The Greek has *in the middle of two living creatures You will be known*.
- **vv3–4 "God came from Teman, the Holy One from Mount Paran… rays came from His hand"** — the march of Moses' blessing, *The LORD came from Sinai and dawned on them from Seir; He shone out from Mount Paran* (Deuteronomy 33:2), and of Deborah's song, *LORD, when You went out from Seir, when You marched from the field of Edom, the earth shook* (Judges 5:4). Teman is in Edom (Obadiah 9). *Eloah*, **God**, a form found mostly in Job. *Qarnayim*, **rays**, is **horns**, the word of Moses' face, which *had grown horns* from speaking with Him (Exodus 34:29). *Chevyon*, **hiding**, is found only here.
- **vv5–7 "Before Him went plague, and fiery bolts… He stood and measured the earth… the tents of Cushan"** — *dever*, **plague**, as in *a very heavy plague* in Egypt (Exodus 9:3); the Greek has *a word*, *davar*, the same letters. *Reshef*, **fiery bolts**, is *bolts of fire* in Psalm 78:48 and *plague* in Deuteronomy 32:24. *Va-ymoded*, **measured**; the Greek has *the earth was shaken*. *Giv'ot olam*, **the ancient hills**, as in *the boundary of the everlasting hills* (Genesis 49:26). *Aven*, **distress**, is the *wrongdoing* of 1:3. Cushan is found only here; the king who oppressed Israel was *Cushan-rishathaim* (Judges 3:8). *Midian* is the land of Gideon's enemies (Judges 6–7).
- **vv8–10 "Was it against the rivers, LORD… Your chariots of salvation… The mountains saw You and writhed"** — the crossings of sea and river, as the psalm asks, *What ails you, sea, that you flee? Jordan, that you turn back?* (Psalm 114:5), and as Asaph sang, *The waters saw You, God; the waters saw You and writhed… The clouds poured out water* (Psalm 77:16–17). *You split the earth with rivers*, as in *You split open spring and torrent* (Psalm 74:15). v9 is obscure: *shevu'ot mattot omer* is word for word **oaths, shafts, word**; *mattot* is **rods**, **shafts** or **tribes** (as in Micah 6:9). The Greek has *You bent Your bow against scepters, says the Lord*.
- **v11 "Sun and moon stood still in their dwelling… the lightning of Your spear"** — the day of Joshua, *Sun, stand still at Gibeon… So the sun stood still, and the moon stopped* (Joshua 10:12–13). *Zevulah*, **in their dwelling**, from *zevul*, the word of Solomon's *exalted house* (1 Kings 8:13). *Beraq chanitekha*, **the lightning of Your spear**, is the *lightning of the spear* of Nahum 3:3, now in the LORD's hand.
- **vv12–14 "You threshed the nations… for the salvation of Your anointed… You crushed the head… laying bare the foundation"** — *titsad*, **You marched**, as in Judges 5:4; *tadush*, **threshed**, as in Amos 1:3. *Meshichekha*, **Your anointed**, the king, as in *He is the saving stronghold of His anointed* (Psalm 28:8) and *lifts high the horn of His anointed* (1 Samuel 2:10). *Machatsta rosh*, **You crushed the head**, as in *God will crush the head of His enemies* (Psalm 68:21). *Arot yesod*, **laying bare the foundation**, is the verb of Edom's cry, *Tear it down, tear it down, down to its foundations!* (Psalm 137:7). *Mattav*, **his own shafts**, the word of v9. *To devour the poor in secret* is the wicked man of Psalm 10, who *lies in wait in secret… to seize the poor* (Psalm 10:9). *Perazav*, **his warriors**, is uncertain; Deborah's *perazon* are *the villagers* (Judges 5:7).
- **v15 "You trampled the sea with Your horses, the foaming of many waters"** — the sea of the exodus, *Your way was through the sea, Your path through the great waters* (Psalm 77:19). *Chomer*, **foaming**, as the waters *roar and foam* (Psalm 46:3); it is also a **heap**, and the *mortar* of Nahum 3:14.
- **v16 "my inward parts trembled… rot entered my bones… Yet I will wait quietly for the day of distress"** — *ragaz*, **tremble**, twice, the root of v2's *wrath*. *Tsalelu*, **quivered**, the verb of *both ears… will tingle* (1 Samuel 3:11). *Raqav*, **rot**, as in *envy is rot to the bones* (Proverbs 14:30). *Anuach*, **I will wait quietly**, word for word **I will rest**: the prophet who stood on his watch for an answer (2:1) now waits for *the appointed time* (2:3). *Yegudennu*, **who invade us**, is from *gedud*, a raiding band, as in Micah 5:1.
- **vv17–19 "Though the fig tree does not blossom… yet I will exult in the LORD… He makes my feet like the deer's"** — every crop of the land fails, as the law warned, *your land will not give its produce* (Leviticus 26:20), and as in Joel 1:10–12; *kichesh*, **fails**, as in *the new wine will fail her* (Hosea 9:2). *The God of my salvation* is Micah's (Micah 7:7). v19 is nearly the psalm, *He makes my feet like the feet of a deer and sets me on my high places* (Psalm 18:33); *yadrikheni*, **makes me tread**, is the verb of the LORD who *treads on the heights of the earth* (Amos 4:13; Micah 1:3), now given to the prophet. *GOD the Lord*, *YHWH Adonai*, as in Psalm 68:20.

# Habakkuk 3

## Habakkuk's Prayer

**1** A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, on Shigionoth.

**2** LORD, I have heard the report of You;  
I fear, LORD, Your work.  
In the middle of the years revive it;  
in the middle of the years make it known;  
in wrath remember compassion.

## God Came from Teman

**3** God came from Teman,  
the Holy One from Mount Paran. *Selah*  
His splendor covered the heavens,  
and the earth was full of His praise.

**4** His brightness was like the light;  
rays came from His hand,  
and there His power was hidden.

**5** Before Him went plague,  
and fiery bolts went out at His feet.

**6** He stood and measured the earth;  
He looked and made the nations start;  
the everlasting mountains were shattered,  
the ancient hills sank low;  
His ways are as of old.

**7** I saw the tents of Cushan in distress;  
the curtains of the land of Midian trembled.

**8** Was it against the rivers, LORD, that You were angry?  
Was Your anger against the rivers,  
or Your fury against the sea,  
when You rode on Your horses,  
Your chariots of salvation?

**9** Your bow was laid bare;  
the shafts were sworn by Your word. *Selah*  
You split the earth with rivers.

**10** The mountains saw You and writhed;  
a downpour of waters swept by;  
the deep gave its voice;  
it lifted its hands on high.

**11** Sun and moon stood still in their dwelling,  
at the light of Your arrows as they went,  
at the gleam of the lightning of Your spear.

**12** In indignation You marched through the earth;  
in anger You threshed the nations.

**13** You went out for the salvation of Your people,  
for the salvation of Your anointed.  
You crushed the head of the house of the wicked,  
laying bare the foundation up to the neck. *Selah*

**14** You pierced with his own shafts the head of his warriors,  
who stormed out to scatter me,  
exulting as if to devour the poor in secret.

**15** You trampled the sea with Your horses,  
the foaming of many waters.

## Yet I Will Exult

**16** I heard, and my inward parts trembled;  
at the sound my lips quivered;  
rot entered my bones,  
and where I stand I tremble.  
Yet I will wait quietly for the day of distress  
to come up on the people who invade us.

**17** Though the fig tree does not blossom,  
and there is no fruit on the vines,  
though the yield of the olive fails  
and the fields produce no food,  
though the flock is cut off from the fold  
and there is no herd in the stalls,

**18** yet I will exult in the LORD;  
I will rejoice in the God of my salvation.

**19** GOD the Lord is my strength;  
He makes my feet like the deer's  
and makes me tread on my high places.  
For the director: with my stringed instruments.

## Notes

- **v1 "A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, on Shigionoth"** — the chapter is a psalm, with a psalm's heading, *Selah* three times (vv3, 9, 13), and a closing note for the musicians (v19), as in *For the director: with stringed instruments* (Psalm 4, title). *Shigyonot* is the plural of the *shiggayon* of Psalm 7's title, *A Shiggaion of David*, the only other place the word is found; what kind of song it names is not known.
- **vv8–10 "Was it against the rivers, LORD… Your chariots of salvation… The mountains saw You and writhed"** — the crossings of sea and river, as the psalm asks, *What ails you, sea, that you flee? Jordan, that you turn back?* (Psalm 114:5), and as Asaph sang, *The waters saw You, God; the waters saw You and writhed… The clouds poured out water* (Psalm 77:16–17). *You split the earth with rivers*, as in *You split open spring and torrent* (Psalm 74:15). v9 is obscure: *shevu'ot mattot omer* is word for word **oaths, shafts, word**; *mattot* is **rods**, **shafts** or **tribes** (as in Micah 6:9). The Greek has *You bent Your bow against scepters, says the Lord*.

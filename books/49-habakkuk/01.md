# Habakkuk 1

## How Long?

**1** The oracle that Habakkuk the prophet saw.

**2** How long, LORD, shall I cry for help,  
and You will not hear?  
I cry out to You, "Violence!"  
and You will not save.

**3** Why do You make me see wrongdoing,  
and look on trouble?  
Destruction and violence are before me;  
there is strife, and contention rises up.

**4** So the law goes numb,  
and justice never goes forth;  
for the wicked hem in the righteous;  
so justice goes forth crooked.

## I Am Raising Up the Chaldeans

**5** Look among the nations, and see;  
be utterly astounded;  
for I am doing a deed in your days  
that you would not believe if it were told.

**6** For I am about to raise up the Chaldeans,  
that bitter and hasty nation,  
who march through the breadth of the earth  
to seize dwellings not their own.

**7** He is dreadful and fearsome;  
his justice and his dignity  
go forth from himself.

**8** His horses are swifter than leopards,  
keener than wolves of the evening;  
his horsemen gallop on;  
his horsemen come from far away;  
they fly like an eagle swooping to devour.

**9** They all come for violence,  
the set of their faces forward;  
they gather captives like sand.

**10** He mocks kings,  
and rulers are a joke to him;  
he laughs at every fortress;  
he heaps up dirt and takes it.

**11** Then he sweeps on like the wind and passes through,  
and he is guilty,  
he whose strength is his god.

## Too Pure to See Evil

**12** Are You not from ancient times,  
LORD my God, my Holy One?  
We shall not die.  
LORD, You have set him for judgment;  
Rock, You have founded him to rebuke.

**13** You whose eyes are too pure to see evil,  
who cannot look on trouble —  
why do You look on the treacherous,  
and keep silent when the wicked swallows  
one more in the right than he?

**14** You make human beings like the fish of the sea,  
like creeping things with no ruler over them.

**15** He brings all of them up with a fishhook;  
he drags them off in his dragnet  
and gathers them in his net;  
so he rejoices and is glad.

**16** So he sacrifices to his dragnet  
and burns incense to his net;  
for by them his portion is rich  
and his food fat.

**17** Is he then to keep emptying his dragnet,  
and slaying nations continually, without pity?

## Notes

- **v1 "The oracle that Habakkuk the prophet saw"** — *massa*, **oracle**, as in Nahum 1:1, and *chazah*, **saw**, the verb of a vision. *Chavaqquq*, **Habakkuk**, is a name found only in this book (1:1; 3:1); it has the letters of *chavaq*, **embrace**. The book gives him no father, town or king. He begins not with the LORD's word to the people but with his own complaint to the LORD.
- **vv2–3 "How long, LORD… Violence!… Why do You make me see wrongdoing, and look on trouble?"** — *ad anah YHWH*, **how long, LORD**, the cry of the psalms, *How long, LORD? Will You forget me forever?* (Psalm 13:1). *Chamas*, **violence**, is the word Jeremiah shouts, *Violence and destruction!* (Jeremiah 20:8), and Amos's charge, *those who store up violence and destruction* (Amos 3:10). *Aven… amal*, **wrongdoing… trouble**, are the words of Balaam's oracle, *He has not looked on wrongdoing in Jacob, or seen trouble in Israel* (Numbers 23:21): Balaam said God does not look on them; the prophet asks why God makes him look.
- **v4 "So the law goes numb… justice goes forth crooked"** — *tafug*, **goes numb**, is the verb of Jacob's heart when he heard Joseph was alive, *His heart went numb, for he did not believe them* (Genesis 45:26). *Torah*, **the law**, as in *you have forgotten the law of your God* (Hosea 4:6). *Makhtir*, **hem in**, from *keter*, a ring or crown: the wicked surround the righteous. *Me'uqqal*, **crooked**, is found only here.
- **vv5–6 "Look among the nations… I am about to raise up the Chaldeans"** — the LORD's answer: the deed He is doing is the very thing the prophet fears. *Ve-hittammehu temahu*, word for word **be astounded, be astounded**. For *ba-goyim*, **among the nations**, the Greek has *you scoffers*, *bogedim*, nearly the same letters; Paul gives the verse in that form in the synagogue at Antioch (Acts 13:41). *Ha-mar ve-ha-nimhar*, **bitter and hasty**, two words of nearly one sound. *To seize dwellings not their own* is Israel's own entry turned around, *great and good cities that you did not build* (Deuteronomy 6:10).
- **vv7–8 "his justice and his dignity go forth from himself… swifter than leopards… like an eagle swooping to devour"** — *mimmenu mishpato*, **his justice… from himself**: the Chaldean makes his own law, while Israel's *justice never goes forth* (v4). His horses are as Jeremiah's invader's, *his horses are swifter than eagles* (Jeremiah 4:13), and he is the nation of the law's warning, *a nation from far away, from the end of the earth, swooping as the eagle swoops* (Deuteronomy 28:49). *Ze'evei erev*, **wolves of the evening**; Jeremiah has *a wolf of the desert* (Jeremiah 5:6), *aravot*, nearly the same letters, and the Greek has *wolves of Arabia*.
- **vv9–11 "the set of their faces forward… he heaps up dirt and takes it… he whose strength is his god"** — *megammat peneihem*, **the set of their faces**, is found only here, and *qadimah* is **forward** or **eastward**. *Yitqallas*, **mocks**, the verb of the boys who mocked Elisha (2 Kings 2:23); *rozenim*, **rulers**, as in *Hear, you kings; give ear, you rulers* (Judges 5:3). He *heaps up dirt*, the siege ramp, and every wall falls. v11 is obscure: *chalaf ruach va-ya'avor ve-ashem*, word for word **the wind sweeps on and passes through, and he is guilty**. *Zu kocho le-eloho*, **whose strength is his god**: his own power is what he worships; the Greek has *this is the strength to my God*.
- **v12 "Are You not from ancient times… We shall not die… Rock, You have founded him to rebuke"** — *mi-qedem*, **from ancient times**, as in Micah 5:2. *Lo namut*, **we shall not die**, and the Greek has the same. *Tsur*, **Rock**, the name of the song, *The Rock — His work is whole, for all His ways are justice* (Deuteronomy 32:4). The prophet takes the answer of vv5–11 and gives it back: the LORD has set the Chaldean *for judgment*, *to rebuke*.
- **v13 "too pure to see evil… why do You look on the treacherous… one more in the right than he?"** — *amal*, **trouble**, is the word of v3: there God made the prophet look on it, here God *cannot look* on it, so why does He? The question is Jeremiah's, *Why does the way of the wicked prosper? Why are all who deal treacherously at ease?* (Jeremiah 12:1). *Tsaddiq mimmennu*, **one more in the right than he**, are the words of Judah's confession, *She is more in the right than I* (Genesis 38:26).
- **vv14–17 "like the fish of the sea… his dragnet… he sacrifices to his dragnet"** — *the fish of the sea… creeping things* are the creatures over which humankind was to *hold sway* (Genesis 1:28); here humankind is made like them, with *no ruler*. *Chakkah*, **fishhook**, as in *Can you draw out Leviathan with a fishhook* (Job 41:1); *cherem*, **dragnet**, as in *they will haul you up in My dragnet* (Ezekiel 32:3), and *mikhmeret*, **net**, as in Isaiah's fishermen, *those who spread nets on the water* (Isaiah 19:8). *He sacrifices… and burns incense*, the words of Hosea's high places (Hosea 4:13): the conqueror worships his tools. *Lo yachmol*, **without pity**, as in Joel 2:18, where the LORD *had pity*. The chapter ends on a question.

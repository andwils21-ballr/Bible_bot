# Hosea 8

## The Calf of Samaria

**1** Set the horn to your lips!  
One comes like an eagle over the house of the LORD,  
because they have broken My covenant  
and rebelled against My law.

**2** To Me they cry,  
"My God, we know You — we, Israel!"

**3** Israel has rejected the good;  
the enemy will pursue him.

**4** They made kings, but not through Me;  
they made princes, but I did not know it.  
With their silver and their gold  
they made idols for themselves,  
to be cut off.

**5** Your calf is rejected, Samaria;  
My anger burns against them.  
How long will they be incapable of innocence?

**6** For it is from Israel:  
a craftsman made it,  
and it is not God;  
the calf of Samaria will be broken to splinters.

## Sowing the Wind

**7** For they sow the wind,  
and they will reap the whirlwind.  
The standing grain has no head;  
the sprout will yield no flour;  
and if it does yield,  
strangers will swallow it up.

**8** Israel is swallowed up;  
now they are among the nations  
like a jar no one wants.

**9** For they have gone up to Assyria,  
a wild donkey wandering alone;  
Ephraim has hired lovers.

**10** Even though they hire among the nations,  
I will now gather them up,  
and they will soon writhe  
under the burden of the king of princes.

## Altars for Sinning

**11** Because Ephraim has multiplied altars for sinning,  
they have become to him altars for sinning.

**12** Were I to write for him the many things of My law,  
they would be counted as something strange.

**13** As for the sacrifices of My gifts,  
they sacrifice flesh and eat it,  
but the LORD does not accept them.  
Now He will remember their iniquity  
and punish their sins;  
they will return to Egypt.

**14** Israel has forgotten his Maker  
and built palaces,  
and Judah has multiplied fortified cities;  
but I will send fire on his cities,  
and it will devour her strongholds.

## Notes

- **v10 "they will soon writhe under the burden of the king of princes"** — *va-yachellu me'at mi-massa*: obscure. *Yachellu* can be **begin**, or **writhe** as in labor; *massa* is a **burden** or a **tribute**. The Greek reads *they will cease a little from anointing king and princes*.

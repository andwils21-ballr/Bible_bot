# Hosea 14

## Take Words with You

**1** Return, Israel, to the LORD your God,  
for you have stumbled in your iniquity.

**2** Take words with you  
and return to the LORD;  
say to Him,  
"Take away all iniquity,  
and take what is good,  
and we will pay the bulls of our lips.

**3** Assyria will not save us;  
we will not ride on horses;  
and we will no longer say 'Our God'  
to the work of our hands;  
for in You the orphan is shown compassion."

## I Will Heal Them

**4** I will heal their faithlessness;  
I will love them freely,  
for My anger has turned from him.

**5** I will be like the dew to Israel;  
he will blossom like the lily  
and strike his roots like Lebanon.

**6** His shoots will spread out;  
his splendor will be like the olive tree,  
and his fragrance like Lebanon.

**7** Those who live in his shade will return;  
they will grow grain  
and blossom like the vine;  
his renown will be like the wine of Lebanon.

**8** Ephraim, what more have I to do with idols?  
It is I who answer and watch over him;  
I am like a luxuriant cypress;  
from Me your fruit is found.

**9** Whoever is wise, let him understand these things;  
whoever is discerning, let him know them;  
for the ways of the LORD are right,  
and the righteous walk in them,  
but transgressors stumble in them.

## Notes

- **v1 "Return, Israel… for you have stumbled in your iniquity"** — in the Hebrew text this verse is 14:2; the English numbering is followed. *Kashalta ba-avonekha* are the words of 5:5, *Israel and Ephraim stumble in their iniquity*. The chapter is built on *shuv*, **return, turn**: *Return* (v1), *return to the LORD* (v2), *their faithlessness*, *meshuvatam*, a turning away (v4), *My anger has turned* (v4), and *those who live in his shade will return* (v7).
- **v2 "Take away all iniquity… we will pay the bulls of our lips"** — *tissa avon*, **lift away iniquity**, the words the LORD called out as He passed before Moses, *carrying guilt and rebellion and sin* (Exodus 34:7). *Qechu*, **take** words, and *qach*, **take** what is good, are one verb. *Neshallemah farim sefateinu*, **we will pay bulls, our lips**: what they bring in place of bulls is their words. The Greek has *the fruit of our lips*, the phrase Hebrews uses of praise (Hebrews 13:15).
- **v3 "Assyria will not save us; we will not ride on horses… the work of our hands"** — the three things Israel trusted, given up in turn: Assyria (5:13; 7:11); horses, which meant Egypt, *nor make the people return to Egypt in order to multiply horses* (Deuteronomy 17:16); and the calf, of which *a craftsman made it, and it is not God* (8:6). *Yeruchham*, **is shown compassion**, is the verb in the name of Hosea's daughter, *Lo-ruhamah, for I will no longer have compassion on the house of Israel* (1:6).
- **v4 "I will heal their faithlessness; I will love them freely"** — Jeremiah has the same words: *Return, faithless sons; I will heal your faithlessness* (Jeremiah 3:22). *Nedavah*, **freely**, is the word for a gift no law requires, *a freewill offering to the LORD* (Exodus 35:29). In 6:1 the people said *He has torn, and He will heal us*; here the LORD says it Himself.
- **v5 "I will be like the dew to Israel"** — twice before, dew was what vanished: Israel's *loyal love… like the dew that goes early away* (6:4), and Israel itself (13:3). Now the LORD is the dew, as in Moses' blessing, *So Israel dwells in safety… his heavens drip dew* (Deuteronomy 33:28).
- **vv5–7 "like Lebanon… like the olive tree… like the wine of Lebanon"** — Lebanon three times, for roots, fragrance and wine. The bridegroom of the Song says it of the bride, *the fragrance of your garments is like the fragrance of Lebanon* (Song of Songs 4:11), and in Jeremiah the olive tree is the name the LORD gave His people, *A green olive tree, fair with goodly fruit: so the LORD named you* (Jeremiah 11:16).
- **v7 "Those who live in his shade will return; they will grow grain"** — *yashuvu yoshvei*, **they will return, those who live**, a play of sounds. The shade they once sought was the grove's, *under oak and poplar and terebinth, because their shade is good* (4:13); here it is Israel's own. *Yechayyu dagan*, **they will make grain live**, is the verb of 6:2, *He will revive us*; the Greek has *they will live and be drunk with grain*.
- **v8 "Ephraim, what more have I to do with idols?"** — *Efrayim mah li od la-atsabbim*, **Ephraim, what to me any more with idols**. The words can be the LORD's, done with the idols that stood between Him and Ephraim, or Ephraim's own, answering v3; the Greek has *What has Ephraim to do any more with idols?* *Ashurennu*, **I will watch over him**, is the verb of 13:7, *like a leopard I will lurk*, and has the letters of *Ashshur*, the Assyria that *will not save us* (v3).
- **v8 "I am like a luxuriant cypress; from Me your fruit is found"** — *ra'anan*, **luxuriant**, is the word of the law's *every leafy tree*, under which the nations served their gods (Deuteronomy 12:2). Here the LORD Himself is the evergreen. *Peri*, **fruit**, is the sound of Ephraim's name a last time (9:16; 13:15): the fruit Ephraim is named for comes from the LORD.
- **v9 "Whoever is wise, let him understand these things… transgressors stumble in them"** — the book closes as a psalm closes, *Whoever is wise, let him heed these things* (Psalm 107:43). *Yikkashlu*, **stumble**, is the verb of v1, *you have stumbled in your iniquity*: the same ways are walked by the righteous and stumbled in by transgressors.

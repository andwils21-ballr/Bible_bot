# Hosea 10

## The Calf Carried to Assyria

**1** Israel is a spreading vine  
that yields its fruit.  
The more his fruit increased,  
the more altars he built;  
the better his land,  
the better he made his standing stones.

**2** Their heart is false;  
now they must bear their guilt.  
He will break the neck of their altars  
and destroy their standing stones.

**3** For now they will say,  
"We have no king,  
for we did not fear the LORD;  
and the king — what could he do for us?"

**4** They speak words,  
swearing falsely in making covenants;  
so judgment springs up like poison  
in the furrows of the field.

**5** The inhabitants of Samaria will be in dread  
for the calf of Beth-aven;  
its people will mourn over it,  
and its idol priests, who rejoiced over it,  
over its glory,  
for it has gone into exile from it.

**6** The calf itself will be carried to Assyria  
as tribute to King Jareb.  
Ephraim will take shame,  
and Israel will be ashamed of his own counsel.

**7** Samaria and her king will perish  
like a twig on the face of the waters.

**8** The high places of Aven, the sin of Israel,  
will be destroyed;  
thorn and thistle will grow up on their altars.  
They will say to the mountains, "Cover us,"  
and to the hills, "Fall on us."

## Sow Righteousness

**9** Since the days of Gibeah you have sinned, Israel;  
there they have stood.  
Will not war overtake them in Gibeah  
against the sons of injustice?

**10** When I desire, I will discipline them;  
and peoples will be gathered against them  
when they are bound for their double iniquity.

**11** Ephraim was a trained heifer  
that loved to thresh,  
and I spared her fair neck;  
but I will harness Ephraim;  
Judah must plow;  
Jacob must harrow for himself.

**12** Sow for yourselves righteousness;  
reap according to loyal love;  
break up your fallow ground;  
for it is time to seek the LORD,  
until He comes and rains righteousness on you.

**13** You have plowed wickedness;  
you have reaped injustice;  
you have eaten the fruit of lies.  
Because you have trusted in your own way,  
in the multitude of your mighty men,

**14** the roar of battle will rise among your people,  
and all your fortresses will be destroyed,  
as Shalman destroyed Beth-arbel on the day of battle,  
when mothers were dashed to pieces on top of their children.

**15** So it will be done to you, Bethel,  
because of your great wickedness.  
At dawn the king of Israel  
will be utterly cut off.

## Notes

- **v1 "a spreading vine that yields its fruit"** — *gefen boqeq*. *Baqaq* elsewhere means **lay waste**, **empty**, as in Isaiah's *The LORD is about to empty the earth* (Isaiah 24:1), and *boqeq* can be read as **a plundered vine**; the Greek reads **a well-branched vine**, and the rest of the verse is about plenty. The psalm sings of the vine: *You brought a vine out of Egypt* (Psalm 80:8). *Peri*, **fruit**, is the sound of Ephraim's name again (9:16).
- **vv1–2 "their standing stones… He will break the neck of their altars"** — *ya'arof*, the verb of the law for a firstborn donkey not redeemed, *you shall break its neck* (Exodus 13:13). *Chalaq libbam*, **their heart is smooth**, is false, flattering; it can also be read **divided**.
- **v4 "judgment springs up like poison"** — *rosh*, a poisonous plant, the word of the law's warning against *a root bearing poison and wormwood* (Deuteronomy 29:18). Justice, which should feed the land, comes up as a weed in the plowed rows.
- **vv5–6 "its glory… has gone into exile from it"** — *kevodo ki galah mimmennu*, the words of the dying mother who named her son Ichabod: *The glory has gone into exile from Israel* (1 Samuel 4:21). There the glory was the ark of God; here it is a calf, carried to Assyria as tribute. *Kemarim*, **idol priests**, is the word for the priests Josiah put down (2 Kings 23:5).
- **v8 "thorn and thistle… Cover us… Fall on us"** — *qots ve-dardar*, the curse on the ground in Eden, *Thorn and thistle it shall sprout for you* (Genesis 3:18). Jesus quotes the cry to the mountains on the way to the cross (Luke 23:30), and Revelation has it again (Revelation 6:16).
- **vv9–10 "the days of Gibeah… their double iniquity"** — Gibeah again (9:9), where Israel's war against the town began (Judges 20). The lines are obscure: *there they have stood* may mean they have stayed in that sin ever since. What the *double iniquity* is, the text does not say.
- **v11 "a trained heifer that loved to thresh… Judah must plow"** — a threshing animal walks free over the grain and eats as it goes, *You shall not muzzle an ox while it is threshing* (Deuteronomy 25:4); now Ephraim is put under the yoke for the hard work of plowing.
- **v12 "break up your fallow ground… rains righteousness on you"** — Jeremiah says it to Judah, *Break up your fallow ground, and do not sow among thorns* (Jeremiah 4:3). *Ve-yoreh tsedeq*, **and rains righteousness**: *yoreh* is the early rain (6:3), and the same letters can be read **and teaches**, the root of *torah*.
- **v14 "as Shalman destroyed Beth-arbel… mothers dashed to pieces on top of their children"** — Shalman and Beth-arbel are named nowhere else in the Bible. *Em al banim*, **mother on top of children**, is Jacob's fear before he met Esau, *that he may come and strike me down — the mother on top of the children* (Genesis 32:11).
- **v15 "At dawn the king of Israel will be utterly cut off"** — *nidmoh nidmah*, the verb doubled, as of the mother in 4:5. *At dawn*, *ba-shachar*, the hour the people expected the LORD's coming to be sure (6:3).

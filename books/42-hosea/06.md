# Hosea 6

## Come, Let Us Return

**1** "Come, let us return to the LORD;  
for He has torn, and He will heal us;  
He has struck, and He will bind us up.

**2** He will revive us after two days;  
on the third day He will raise us up,  
that we may live before Him.

**3** Let us know, let us press on to know the LORD;  
His going out is as sure as the dawn.  
He will come to us like the rain,  
like the spring rain that waters the earth."

## Your Loyal Love Is Like a Morning Cloud

**4** What shall I do with you, Ephraim?  
What shall I do with you, Judah?  
Your loyal love is like a morning cloud,  
like the dew that goes early away.

**5** Therefore I have hewn them by the prophets;  
I have killed them by the words of My mouth;  
and My judgment goes out like light.

**6** For I desire loyal love and not sacrifice,  
the knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings.

**7** But like Adam they have broken the covenant;  
there they dealt faithlessly with Me.

**8** Gilead is a city of evildoers,  
tracked with blood.

**9** As bandits lie in wait for a man,  
so a band of priests  
murders on the road to Shechem;  
they have done what is vile.

**10** In the house of Israel I have seen a horrible thing:  
there is Ephraim's prostitution;  
Israel is defiled.

**11** For you also, Judah, a harvest is appointed,  
when I restore the fortunes of My people.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "Come, let us return to the LORD"** — the people's words, answering 5:14–15: He is the lion who *tore*, and they say He will heal. Whether the chapter accepts their words is the question of v4, *What shall I do with you, Ephraim?* Their *loyal love* lasts like *a morning cloud*.
- **v2 "on the third day He will raise us up"** — *ba-yom ha-shelishi yeqimenu*, said of the people, two days and then the third. The Gospels say Jesus would *on the third day be raised* (Matthew 16:21), and Paul names *the third day* as what the Scriptures said (1 Corinthians 15:4).
- **v5 "and My judgment goes out like light"** — this follows the Greek, *kai to krima mou hōs phōs exeleusetai*. The Hebrew letters are the same, divided differently: *u-mishpatekha or yetse*, **and your judgments, light goes out**. The verses around it are all *I* and *My*: *I have hewn… I have killed… the words of My mouth*.
- **v6 "For I desire loyal love and not sacrifice"** — *chesed*, the word of v4, where Israel's *loyal love* vanished like dew. Samuel said it before, *To listen is better than sacrifice* (1 Samuel 15:22). Jesus quotes the verse twice in Matthew from the Greek, which has *eleos*, **mercy**: *I desire mercy, not sacrifice* (Matthew 9:13; 12:7).
- **v7 "But like Adam they have broken the covenant; there"** — *ke-adam* is **like Adam** or **like mankind**. Job uses the same words, *if I have covered my transgressions as Adam did* (Job 31:33). The Greek has *like a man*. The *there* that follows asks for a place, and the next verses name places, Gilead and Shechem; a town called Adam stood on the Jordan, *Adam, the city beside Zarethan* (Joshua 3:16), but the Hebrew has *like*, not *at*.
- **v8 "tracked with blood"** — *aqubbah mi-dam*, **heel-marked with blood**, from *aqev*, **heel**, the word that gave Jacob his name (Genesis 25:26). Hosea comes back to it in 12:3.
- **v10 "a horrible thing"** — *sha'arurriyyah*, the word Jeremiah uses, *in the prophets of Jerusalem I have seen a horrible thing: they commit adultery* (Jeremiah 23:14).
- **v11 "when I restore the fortunes of My people"** — *be-shuvi shevut ammi*, the promise of Jeremiah, *I will restore your fortunes* (Jeremiah 29:14). The line runs straight on into 7:1, *when I would heal Israel*.

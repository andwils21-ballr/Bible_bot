# Hosea 13

## Like a Morning Cloud

**1** When Ephraim spoke, there was trembling;  
he was exalted in Israel;  
but he became guilty through Baal, and he died.

**2** Now they sin more and more,  
and make themselves a cast image from their silver,  
idols by their own skill,  
all of it the work of craftsmen.  
Of these it is said,  
"Those who sacrifice people kiss calves!"

**3** Therefore they will be like a morning cloud,  
like the dew that goes early away,  
like chaff that is whirled from the threshing floor,  
like smoke from a window.

## No Savior but Me

**4** But I have been the LORD your God  
ever since the land of Egypt;  
you shall know no god but Me,  
and besides Me there is no savior.

**5** It was I who knew you in the wilderness,  
in a land of drought.

**6** When they had pasture, they were satisfied;  
they were satisfied, and their heart was lifted up;  
therefore they forgot Me.

**7** So I will be to them like a lion;  
like a leopard I will lurk beside the road.

**8** I will meet them like a bear robbed of her cubs  
and tear open the enclosure of their heart;  
there I will devour them like a lioness;  
the wild beast of the field will rip them open.

**9** This is your ruin, Israel:  
you are against Me, against your help.

**10** Where now is your king,  
that he may save you in all your cities,  
and your judges, of whom you said,  
"Give me a king and princes"?

**11** I gave you a king in My anger,  
and I took him away in My wrath.

## The Mouth of the Womb

**12** The iniquity of Ephraim is bound up;  
his sin is stored away.

**13** The pains of a woman in labor come upon him;  
he is an unwise son,  
for when the time comes he does not take his place  
at the mouth of the womb.

**14** From the power of Sheol I will ransom them;  
from death I will redeem them.  
Death, where are your plagues?  
Sheol, where is your destruction?  
Relenting is hidden from My eyes.

**15** Though he is fruitful among his brothers,  
an east wind will come, the wind of the LORD,  
rising from the wilderness;  
his spring will dry up,  
and his fountain will be parched;  
it will plunder the treasury  
of every precious thing.

**16** Samaria must bear her guilt,  
because she has rebelled against her God;  
they will fall by the sword;  
their little ones will be dashed to pieces,  
and their pregnant women ripped open.

## Notes

- **v2 "Those who sacrifice people kiss calves!"** — *zovchei adam agalim yishaqun*. *Zovchei adam* is **sacrificers of man**: those who sacrifice people, as the north *made their sons and their daughters pass through the fire* (2 Kings 17:17); the Greek has the people say *Sacrifice men*. The same words can be **sacrificers among men**, people who come to sacrifice, and *yishaqun* can be **let them kiss**: *Let the men who sacrifice kiss the calves*. Kissing was worship, as of the seven thousand, *every mouth that has not kissed him* (1 Kings 19:18).
- **vv9–11 "against your help… Where now is your king… Give me a king and princes"** — v9 is obscure: *shichetkha… ki vi ve-ezrekha*, **it has ruined you… for against Me, against your help**. *Ezer*, **help**, is a name for God in Moses' blessing, *the shield of your help* (Deuteronomy 33:29). *Give me a king* is the people's demand to Samuel, *Give us a king to judge us* (1 Samuel 8:6), when the LORD said *they have rejected Me from being king over them* (8:7). *Ehi*, **where**, is found only in v10 and v14.
- **v14 "From the power of Sheol I will ransom them… Death, where are your plagues?"** — two readings of one verse. The first two lines have no word marking a question and read as a promise, as the Greek has them: *From the hand of Hades I will rescue them, and from death I will redeem them. Where is your penalty, O death? Where is your sting, O Hades?* Paul takes up those questions in 1 Corinthians 15:55. Read as questions, *Shall I ransom them from Sheol?*, the lines call death's plagues down on Ephraim, as vv13 and 15–16 do. *Dever* and *qetev*, **plagues** and **destruction**, are the pair of the psalm, *the plague that stalks in darkness, or the destruction that lays waste at noon* (Psalm 91:6). *Nocham*, **relenting**, is found only here, from the root of 11:8, *all My compassion is kindled*, and of *change His mind* (Numbers 23:19).
- **vv15–16 "Though he is fruitful among his brothers… their pregnant women ripped open"** — *yafri*, **is fruitful**, is the sound of Ephraim's name once more (9:16). The east wind of 12:1 is now *ruach YHWH*, **the wind of the LORD**, the words Isaiah uses of what withers the grass, *when the Spirit of the LORD blows on it* (Isaiah 40:7); here the verse names it a wind from the east. In the Hebrew text v16 is 14:1, and English 14:1–9 is Hebrew 14:2–10; the English numbering is followed. Elisha wept before Hazael over the same deeds: *dash their little ones in pieces and rip open their pregnant women* (2 Kings 8:12).

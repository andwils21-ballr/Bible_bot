# Hosea 5

## Judgment on Priests and King

**1** Hear this, priests!  
Pay attention, house of Israel!  
Listen, house of the king!  
For the judgment is for you,  
because you have been a snare at Mizpah  
and a net spread out on Tabor.

**2** They have gone deep in slaughter at Shittim,  
but I will discipline them all.

**3** I know Ephraim,  
and Israel is not hidden from Me;  
for now, Ephraim, you have prostituted yourself;  
Israel is defiled.

**4** Their deeds do not let them  
return to their God,  
for a spirit of prostitution is within them,  
and they do not know the LORD.

**5** The pride of Israel testifies against him to his face;  
Israel and Ephraim stumble in their iniquity,  
and Judah also stumbles with them.

**6** With their flocks and their herds  
they will go to seek the LORD,  
but they will not find Him;  
He has withdrawn from them.

**7** They have dealt faithlessly with the LORD,  
for they have borne strange children.  
Now the new moon will devour them with their portions.

## Sound the Alarm

**8** Blow the horn in Gibeah,  
the trumpet in Ramah!  
Sound the alarm at Beth-aven:  
"After you, Benjamin!"

**9** Ephraim will become a desolation  
on the day of rebuke;  
among the tribes of Israel  
I make known what is sure.

**10** The princes of Judah have become  
like those who move a boundary marker;  
on them I will pour out My wrath like water.

**11** Ephraim is oppressed, crushed in judgment,  
because he was determined to go after rule.

**12** But I am like a moth to Ephraim,  
and like rot to the house of Judah.

**13** When Ephraim saw his sickness,  
and Judah his wound,  
Ephraim went to Assyria  
and sent to King Jareb;  
but he cannot heal you  
or cure you of your wound.

**14** For I will be like a lion to Ephraim,  
and like a young lion to the house of Judah.  
I Myself will tear and go away;  
I will carry off, and no one will rescue.

**15** I will go and return to My place  
until they acknowledge their guilt and seek My face;  
in their distress they will seek Me at dawn.

## Notes

- **v2 "They have gone deep in slaughter at Shittim"** — *ve-shachatah setim he'emiqu*, three words that are hard to put together. *Setim* can be the place Shittim, where Israel *began to whore with the daughters of Moab* and ate at *the sacrifices of their gods* (Numbers 25:1–2), set beside Mizpah and Tabor; or the word for those who *turn aside*, *rebels*. *Shachatah* is **slaughter**, the killing of animals for sacrifice. The Greek reads the line differently: *which those who hunt the prey have fixed*.
- **v13 "King Jareb"** — *melekh yarev*, **King He-Contends**. The words read like a nickname, and no king of Assyria by that name is known in the Bible; the Greek also takes it as a name, *Iareim*. It comes again in 10:6.

# Hosea 11

## When Israel Was a Boy

**1** When Israel was a boy, I loved him,  
and out of Egypt I called My son.

**2** The more they were called,  
the more they went away from them;  
they sacrificed to the Baals  
and burned incense to carved images.

**3** Yet it was I who taught Ephraim to walk,  
taking them by their arms;  
but they did not know that I healed them.

**4** I led them with human cords,  
with bands of love;  
I was to them like those who lift the yoke from their jaws,  
and I bent down to them and fed them.

**5** He will not return to the land of Egypt,  
but Assyria will be his king,  
because they refused to return.

**6** The sword will whirl in his cities,  
consume his gate bars and devour,  
because of their schemes.

**7** My people are bent on turning from Me;  
though they are called upward,  
none at all will lift himself up.

## How Can I Give You Up?

**8** How can I give you up, Ephraim?  
How can I hand you over, Israel?  
How can I make you like Admah?  
How can I treat you like Zeboiim?  
My heart is overturned within Me;  
all My compassion is kindled.

**9** I will not carry out My burning anger;  
I will not again destroy Ephraim;  
for I am God and not a man,  
the Holy One among you,  
and I will not come in wrath.

**10** They will walk after the LORD;  
He will roar like a lion;  
when He roars,  
His sons will come trembling from the west.

**11** They will come trembling like birds from Egypt,  
and like doves from the land of Assyria;  
and I will settle them in their homes,  
declares the LORD.

**12** Ephraim has surrounded Me with lies,  
and the house of Israel with deceit;  
but Judah still roams with God  
and is faithful to the Holy One.

## Notes

- **v1 "out of Egypt I called My son"** — *u-mi-Mitsrayim qarati li-vni*. Israel is *My son, My firstborn* in the LORD's first message to Pharaoh (Exodus 4:22). Matthew reads the verse of Jesus brought back from Egypt: *This was to fulfill what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet, saying, "Out of Egypt I called My son"* (Matthew 2:15), the Hebrew's words; the Greek of Hosea has *I called his children*.
- **vv2–4 "The more they were called… taught Ephraim to walk… bent down to them and fed them"** — a father with a small child. *Tirgalti*, **I taught to walk**, from *regel*, **foot**, is found only here. *Healed* is the verb of 5:13 and 6:1. *Chavlei adam*, **cords of a man**, are human cords, the kind a person is led by, not a beast's harness.
- **v4 "like those who lift the yoke from their jaws"** — *ke-merimei ol al lecheihem*. *Ol* is **a yoke**; the same letters read *ul* are **a nursing child**, as in *an infant who lives but a few days* (Isaiah 65:20), which would make it *like those who lift an infant to their cheeks*. The father of vv1–3 makes the second reading fit; the vowels of the Hebrew text give the first.
- **v5 "He will not return to the land of Egypt"** — 8:13 and 9:3 said *they will return to Egypt*; here it is Assyria, not Egypt, that will rule. *Shuv*, **return**, is used twice: they will not *return* to Egypt, because they refused to *return* to the LORD. The Greek has *Ephraim settled in Egypt*.
- **vv6–7 "because of their schemes… none at all will lift himself up"** — v7 is obscure: *ve-el al yiqra'uhu*, **and to the Most High they call him**, or **to on-high**; whether the people call to God or the prophets call the people, the line ends with no one rising.
- **v8 "Admah… Zeboiim… My heart is overturned within Me"** — Admah and Zeboiim were destroyed with Sodom and Gomorrah, *which the LORD overthrew in His anger and His fury* (Deuteronomy 29:23). *Nehpakh alai libbi*, **My heart is overturned**, is the verb of that overthrow, *He overturned those cities* (Genesis 19:25): instead of overturning Ephraim, the LORD's own heart is overturned.
- **v9 "for I am God and not a man… I will not come in wrath"** — Balaam's words, *God is not a man, that He should lie, or a son of man, that He should change His mind* (Numbers 23:19). *Nichumai*, **My compassion** in v8, is from the same verb as *change His mind*, *nicham*: here, because He is God and not a man, His compassion is kindled. *Ve-lo avo be-ir*, **and I will not come in heat**, can also be read **I will not come into a city**, as the Greek has it.
- **vv10–11 "He will roar like a lion… like doves from the land of Assyria"** — the lion of 5:14, who tore and went away, roars now to call His sons home; the dove of 7:11, *silly and without sense*, flying between Egypt and Assyria, comes back trembling.
- **v12 "but Judah still roams with God"** — in the Hebrew text this verse is 12:1, and English 12:1–14 is Hebrew 12:2–15; the English numbering is followed. *Ve-Yehudah od rad im El* is obscure: *rad*, **roams**, may be loyal (*still walks with God*) or restless (*still roams free of God*), the sense it has in Jeremiah, *We roam free; we will come to You no more* (Jeremiah 2:31); and *qedoshim*, **holy ones**, can be *the Holy One*, as in 11:9, or the holy ones of heaven.

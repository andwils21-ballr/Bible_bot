# Hosea 1

**1** The word of the LORD that came to Hosea son of Beeri, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel.

## A Wife of Prostitution

**2** When the LORD first spoke through Hosea, the LORD said to Hosea, "Go, take for yourself a wife of prostitution and children of prostitution, for the land has given itself to prostitution, turning away from the LORD."

**3** So he went and took Gomer daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore him a son.

**4** The LORD said to him, "Call his name Jezreel, for in a little while I will punish the house of Jehu for the blood of Jezreel, and I will put an end to the kingdom of the house of Israel.

**5** And it shall come to pass on that day that I will break the bow of Israel in the Valley of Jezreel."

**6** She conceived again and bore a daughter, and the LORD said to him, "Call her name Lo-ruhamah, for I will no longer have compassion on the house of Israel, that I should ever forgive them.

**7** But I will have compassion on the house of Judah, and I will save them by the LORD their God. I will not save them by bow or sword or battle, by horses or horsemen."

**8** When she had weaned Lo-ruhamah, she conceived and bore a son.

**9** The LORD said, "Call his name Lo-ammi, for you are not My people, and I will not be yours."

## Sons of the Living God

**10** Yet the number of the sons of Israel will be like the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured or counted. And it shall come to pass that in the place where it was said to them, "You are not My people," it will be said to them, "Sons of the living God."

**11** The sons of Judah and the sons of Israel will be gathered together, and they will appoint for themselves one head and go up from the land, for great will be the day of Jezreel.

## Notes

- **v1 "Hosea son of Beeri"** — *Hoshea*, from *yasha*, **save**, the name Moses gave Joshua before he changed it (Numbers 13:16). The kings of Judah run to Hezekiah, but the only king of Israel named is Jeroboam, though the book is spoken to Israel, the north. In the Hebrew text vv10–11 are 2:1–2; the English numbering is followed, and the Greek follows it too.
- **v2 "a wife of prostitution… the land has given itself to prostitution"** — *eshet zenunim*, **a wife of prostitutions**, and *zanoh tizneh ha-arets*, the verb doubled, **the land prostitutes itself greatly**. The book's first command makes Hosea's marriage a picture of the land and its God. The text does not say whether Gomer was already a prostitute when he married her or became one after; *a wife of prostitution* can be read either way.
- **v4 "Jezreel… the blood of Jezreel… the house of Jehu"** — *Yizre'el*, **God sows**. Jezreel was where Jehu killed the house of Ahab, and Kings records God's words to him after it: *Because you have done well in doing what is right in My eyes… your sons to the fourth generation shall sit on the throne of Israel* (2 Kings 10:30). Here the same blood is laid to the account of Jehu's house. The name comes back as a promise: *I will sow her for Myself in the land* (2:23).
- **v6 "Lo-ruhamah… that I should ever forgive them"** — *Lo ruchamah*, **Not Shown Compassion**, from *racham*, the word for a mother's womb and her love. *Ki naso essa lahem*, **for lifting I will lift for them**, is short and unclear: *lift away* their sin, that is, forgive them, which the verse refuses; or *lift them away*, take them away. The Greek has *I will surely set myself against them*.
- **v9 "Lo-ammi… I will not be yours"** — *Lo ammi*, **Not My People**. *Ve-anokhi lo ehyeh lakhem*, **and I, I will not be to you**: *ehyeh*, *I will be*, is the name God gave Moses at the bush, *I AM has sent me to you* (Exodus 3:14). The words can be read *I will not be yours*, the covenant undone, or *I am not I AM to you*. The Greek has *I am not yours*.
- **v10 "like the sand of the sea… Sons of the living God"** — the promise to Jacob, *I will make your seed like the sand of the sea — too many to be counted* (Genesis 32:12), set beside the sentence of v9 and reversing it. Paul quotes this verse of the Gentiles (Romans 9:26).

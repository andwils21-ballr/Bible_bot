# Hosea 7

## Like an Oven

**1** When I would heal Israel,  
the iniquity of Ephraim is uncovered,  
and the evil deeds of Samaria;  
for they practice deceit:  
the thief breaks in,  
and a band of raiders plunders in the street.

**2** But they do not say to themselves  
that I remember all their evil.  
Now their deeds surround them;  
they are before My face.

**3** With their evil they make the king glad,  
and the princes with their lies.

**4** They are all adulterers;  
they are like an oven heated by a baker,  
who stops stirring the fire  
from the kneading of the dough until it is leavened.

**5** On the day of our king  
the princes made him sick with the heat of wine;  
he stretched out his hand with scoffers.

**6** For they come near with their hearts like an oven  
as they lie in wait;  
all night their baker sleeps;  
in the morning it blazes like a flaming fire.

**7** All of them are hot as an oven,  
and they devour their rulers;  
all their kings have fallen,  
and not one of them calls to Me.

## Ephraim Mixes with the Peoples

**8** Ephraim mixes himself among the peoples;  
Ephraim is a cake not turned.

**9** Strangers devour his strength,  
and he does not know it;  
gray hairs are sprinkled on him,  
and he does not know it.

**10** The pride of Israel testifies against him to his face;  
yet they do not return to the LORD their God,  
or seek Him, for all this.

**11** Ephraim has become like a dove,  
silly and without sense;  
they call to Egypt,  
they go to Assyria.

**12** As they go, I will spread My net over them;  
I will bring them down like the birds of the heavens;  
I will discipline them as their congregation has been told.

**13** Woe to them, for they have strayed from Me!  
Destruction to them, for they have rebelled against Me!  
I would redeem them,  
but they speak lies against Me.

**14** They do not cry to Me from their heart  
when they wail on their beds;  
for grain and new wine they gash themselves;  
they turn away from Me.

**15** Though I trained and strengthened their arms,  
they plot evil against Me.

**16** They turn, but not upward;  
they are like a deceitful bow.  
Their princes will fall by the sword  
for the fury of their tongue;  
this will be their derision in the land of Egypt.

## Notes

- **v1 "When I would heal Israel"** — the sentence begins at the end of chapter 6, *when I restore the fortunes of My people* (6:11). *Heal* is the verb of 5:13 and 6:1: Assyria could not heal, the people said the LORD would, and when He comes to heal, what He finds is their iniquity.
- **vv4–8 the oven and the cake** — one picture from the baker's trade: a heated oven left to smoulder all night while the dough rises, then blazing up in the morning, as the princes' plots smoulder and then burst out at the king. The lines are compressed and hard to follow word by word (vv4–6), and the Greek reads several of them differently. *Yitbolal*, **mixes himself**, is the verb for flour *mixed with oil* in the grain offering (Leviticus 2:4), and *a cake not turned* is burnt on one side and raw on the other.
- **v7 "all their kings have fallen"** — Kings records four kings of Israel struck down by the men who took their thrones: Shallum *conspired against him and struck him down* (2 Kings 15:10), Menahem *struck down Shallum* (15:14), Pekah *conspired against him* (15:25), and Hoshea *made a conspiracy against Pekah* (15:30).
- **v10 "The pride of Israel testifies against him to his face"** — the words of 5:5, said again.
- **v11 "like a dove, silly and without sense… Egypt… Assyria"** — *pothah*, **easily led**. The north turned to both powers in turn: the last king, Hoshea, *had sent messengers to So king of Egypt* and stopped the tribute he had paid Assyria *year by year* (2 Kings 17:4).
- **v13 "I would redeem them"** — *ve-anokhi efdem*, the verb of the exodus, *redeemed you from the house of slaves* (Deuteronomy 7:8).
- **v14 "for grain and new wine they gash themselves"** — this follows the Greek, *katetemnonto*, **they cut themselves**, which reads *yitgodadu*. The Hebrew has *yitgoraru*, one letter different, **they gather themselves** or **they stir themselves up**. Gashing is the rite of the prophets of Baal, who *gashed themselves… until the blood gushed out* (1 Kings 18:28), and the law forbids it, *You shall not gash yourselves* (Deuteronomy 14:1).
- **v16 "They turn, but not upward… a deceitful bow"** — *yashuvu lo al*, **they return, not on high**: obscure; *al* is **above**, and the words may mean they turn to what is not God. *A deceitful bow*, one that springs aside when it is drawn, is the psalm's picture of Israel in the wilderness, *they twisted like a deceitful bow* (Psalm 78:57).

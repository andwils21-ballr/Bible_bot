# Hosea 2

**1** Say to your brothers, "Ammi," and to your sisters, "Ruhamah."

## The Charge

**2** Contend with your mother, contend,  
for she is not my wife,  
and I am not her husband.  
Let her put away her prostitution from her face,  
and her adulteries from between her breasts,

**3** or I will strip her naked  
and set her out as on the day she was born,  
and make her like the wilderness,  
and turn her into a parched land,  
and kill her with thirst.

**4** On her children I will have no compassion,  
for they are children of prostitution.

**5** For their mother has prostituted herself;  
she who conceived them has acted shamefully.  
For she said, "I will go after my lovers,  
who give me my bread and my water,  
my wool and my flax, my oil and my drink."

**6** Therefore I am about to hedge up your way with thorns,  
and I will build a wall against her,  
so that she cannot find her paths.

**7** She will chase after her lovers but not overtake them;  
she will seek them but not find them.  
Then she will say, "I will go back to my first husband,  
for it was better for me then than now."

**8** She did not know  
that it was I who gave her the grain, the new wine and the oil,  
and multiplied for her silver and gold,  
which they used for Baal.

**9** Therefore I will take back My grain in its time  
and My new wine in its season,  
and I will snatch away My wool and My flax,  
which were to cover her nakedness.

**10** Now I will uncover her shame  
before the eyes of her lovers,  
and no one will rescue her from My hand.

**11** I will put an end to all her rejoicing,  
her feasts, her new moons, her sabbaths  
and all her appointed feasts.

**12** I will lay waste her vines and her fig trees,  
of which she said, "These are my wages,  
which my lovers have given me."  
I will make them a thicket,  
and the beasts of the field will devour them.

**13** I will punish her for the days of the Baals,  
when she burned incense to them  
and adorned herself with her ring and her jewelry,  
and went after her lovers,  
and forgot Me, declares the LORD.

## The Wilderness and the Door of Hope

**14** Therefore I am about to allure her,  
and lead her into the wilderness,  
and speak to her heart.

**15** There I will give her her vineyards,  
and make the Valley of Achor a door of hope.  
There she will answer as in the days of her youth,  
as on the day she came up out of the land of Egypt.

**16** And it shall come to pass on that day, declares the LORD,  
that you will call Me "My husband,"  
and you will no longer call Me "My Baal."

**17** I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth,  
and they will no longer be remembered by name.

**18** On that day I will make a covenant for them  
with the beasts of the field, the birds of the heavens  
and the creeping things of the ground;  
I will break bow and sword and war from the land,  
and make them lie down in safety.

**19** I will betroth you to Me forever;  
I will betroth you to Me in righteousness and in justice,  
in loyal love and in compassion.

**20** I will betroth you to Me in faithfulness,  
and you will know the LORD.

**21** And it shall come to pass on that day  
that I will answer, declares the LORD;  
I will answer the heavens,  
and they will answer the earth,

**22** and the earth will answer the grain, the new wine and the oil,  
and they will answer Jezreel.

**23** I will sow her for Myself in the land,  
and I will have compassion on Lo-ruhamah,  
and I will say to Lo-ammi, "You are My people,"  
and he will say, "My God."

## Notes

- **v1 "Ammi… Ruhamah"** — the names of 1:6 and 1:9 without their *Lo*, **not**: *Ammi*, **My People**, and *Ruhamah*, **Shown Compassion**. In the Hebrew text this verse is 2:3, and the chapter runs to 2:25; the English numbering is followed.
- **v2 "for she is not my wife, and I am not her husband"** — the husband disowns her, yet the speech that follows does not let her go. The marriage of Hosea and Gomer (1:2–3) and the marriage of the LORD and the land are spoken of in the same voice, and the chapter moves from one to the other without marking it.
- **vv5, 8, 12 "my lovers, who give me my bread… which they used for Baal… These are my wages"** — she thinks her food comes from her lovers; it came from the LORD (v8). *Etnah*, **wages**, a prostitute's pay, sounds like *te'enah*, **fig tree**: the fig trees she calls her wages are the ones He lays waste.
- **v14 "lead her into the wilderness, and speak to her heart"** — the wilderness of the exodus, where it began (v15). *Dibbarti al libbah*, **speak to her heart**, is what Shechem did to Dinah (*he spoke to the girl's heart*, Genesis 34:3) and Joseph to his brothers (*He comforted them and spoke to their heart*, Genesis 50:21); Isaiah uses it for the comfort of Jerusalem, *Speak to the heart of Jerusalem* (Isaiah 40:2).
- **v15 "the Valley of Achor a door of hope"** — *Akhor*, **trouble**, where Achan was stoned and *the LORD turned from the heat of His anger* (Joshua 7:26). The place named for Israel's first sin in the land becomes its entrance again. *She will answer* (*anetah*) begins the chain of *answer* in vv21–22.
- **v16 "My husband… My Baal"** — *ishi* and *ba'li*, both **my husband**. *Ba'al* is the ordinary word for a husband or owner, and the name of the god; the LORD will not be called by it, so that the god's name is no longer in her mouth (v17).
- **v18 "a covenant… with the beasts of the field, the birds of the heavens"** — as after the flood, God's covenant *with every living soul… in the bird, in the beasts* (Genesis 9:10), and *bow and sword and war* broken, where 1:5 broke only *the bow of Israel*.
- **vv19–20 "I will betroth you to Me"** — *erastikh*, three times. *Be-* with this verb names the price the husband pays, as David betrothed Michal *for a hundred foreskins of the Philistines* (2 Samuel 3:14, *be-me'ah*). Here the price is *righteousness and justice, loyal love and compassion, and faithfulness*. *Compassion*, *rachamim*, is the word Lo-ruhamah was denied (1:6).
- **vv21–23 "I will answer… they will answer Jezreel. I will sow her"** — heaven answers earth, earth answers the grain, the grain answers *Jezreel*, **God sows**, and God answers by sowing: *u-zera'tiha*, **I will sow her**. All three names of chapter 1 are undone in v23.

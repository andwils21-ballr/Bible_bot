# Hosea 9

## Unclean Bread in Exile

**1** Do not rejoice, Israel;  
do not exult like the peoples,  
for you have prostituted yourself, leaving your God;  
you have loved a prostitute's wages  
on every threshing floor of grain.

**2** Threshing floor and winepress will not feed them,  
and the new wine will fail her.

**3** They will not stay in the land of the LORD;  
Ephraim will return to Egypt,  
and in Assyria they will eat unclean food.

**4** They will not pour out wine to the LORD,  
and their sacrifices will not please Him.  
To them it will be like mourners' bread:  
all who eat it are defiled;  
for their bread will be for their hunger only;  
it will not go into the house of the LORD.

**5** What will you do on the day of the appointed feast,  
on the day of the LORD's feast?

**6** For they are going away from destruction;  
Egypt will gather them,  
Memphis will bury them.  
Nettles will take possession of their precious things of silver;  
thorns will be in their tents.

## The Prophet Is a Fool

**7** The days of punishment have come;  
the days of repayment have come;  
Israel will know it.  
"The prophet is a fool,  
the man of the spirit is mad" —  
because of the greatness of your iniquity  
and great hostility.

**8** Ephraim is a watchman with my God;  
the prophet — a fowler's snare is on all his ways,  
and hostility in the house of his God.

**9** They have gone deep in corruption  
as in the days of Gibeah;  
He will remember their iniquity;  
He will punish their sins.

## Ephraim Will Bear No Fruit

**10** Like grapes in the wilderness I found Israel;  
like the first fruit on the fig tree in its first season  
I saw your fathers.  
But they came to Baal-peor  
and consecrated themselves to the Shame,  
and they became loathsome like what they loved.

**11** Ephraim — their glory will fly away like a bird:  
no birth, no womb, no conception.

**12** Even if they bring up their children,  
I will bereave them until none is left.  
Woe to them indeed  
when I turn away from them!

**13** Ephraim, as I saw him, was like Tyre,  
planted in a meadow;  
but Ephraim must bring out his children to the slayer.

**14** Give them, O LORD —  
what will You give?  
Give them a miscarrying womb  
and dry breasts.

**15** All their evil is in Gilgal;  
there I came to hate them.  
Because of the evil of their deeds  
I will drive them out of My house;  
I will love them no more;  
all their princes are rebels.

**16** Ephraim is struck;  
their root is dried up;  
they will bear no fruit.  
Even if they give birth,  
I will kill the beloved fruit of their womb.

**17** My God will reject them,  
because they have not listened to Him;  
and they will be wanderers among the nations.

## Notes

- **v8 "Ephraim is a watchman with my God; the prophet"** — obscure. *Tsofeh*, **watchman**, is the prophet's title, *I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel* (Ezekiel 3:17); the line can mean that the prophet is the watchman and the people set snares for him.
- **vv11–16 Ephraim and fruit** — *Efrayim* was named for fruit: *God has made me fruitful* (Genesis 41:52). Now the name is undone: *no birth, no womb, no conception*; *they will bear no fruit*, *peri* (v16). The prayer of v14 takes back Jacob's blessing on Joseph, Ephraim's father, *blessings of breasts and womb* (Genesis 49:25).

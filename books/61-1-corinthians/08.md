# 1 Corinthians 8

## Knowledge and Love

**1** Now about food offered to idols: we know that "we all have knowledge." Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up.

**2** If anyone thinks he knows something, he does not yet know as he ought to know.

**3** But if anyone loves God, he is known by Him.

## One God and One Lord

**4** So, about eating food offered to idols: we know that "an idol is nothing in the world," and that "there is no God but one."

**5** For even if there are so-called gods, whether in heaven or on earth, as indeed there are many gods and many lords,

**6** yet for us there is one God, the Father, from whom are all things and for whom we exist, and one Lord, Jesus Christ, through whom are all things and through whom we exist.

## Do Not Make Your Brother Stumble

**7** But not everyone has this knowledge. Some, being used to idols until now, still eat the food as offered to an idol, and their conscience, being weak, is defiled.

**8** Food will not bring us near to God. We are no better off if we eat, and no worse off if we do not.

**9** But take care that this right of yours does not somehow become a stumbling block to the weak.

**10** For if someone sees you, who have knowledge, reclining at table in an idol's temple, will not his conscience, weak as it is, be built up to eat food offered to idols?

**11** So by your knowledge this weak man is destroyed, the brother for whom Christ died.

**12** When you sin against your brothers in this way and wound their conscience when it is weak, you sin against Christ.

**13** Therefore, if food makes my brother stumble, I will never eat meat again, so that I will not make my brother stumble.

## Notes

- **vv4–6 "there is no God but one… one God, the Father, from whom are all things… and one Lord, Jesus Christ, through whom are all things"** — the Shema, *Hear, Israel: the LORD our God, the LORD is one* (Deuteronomy 6:4), in its Greek words *theos* and *kyrios*, **God** and **Lord**. Paul keeps Israel's confession that God is one and sets the two words of it side by side: *one God, the Father*, and *one Lord, Jesus Christ*. All things are *from* the Father and *through* the Lord.

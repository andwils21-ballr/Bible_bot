# 1 Corinthians 7

## Husbands and Wives

**1** Now about the things you wrote: "It is good for a man not to touch a woman."

**2** But because of the temptation to sexual immorality, each man should have his own wife, and each woman her own husband.

**3** The husband should give his wife what is due her, and likewise the wife her husband.

**4** The wife does not have authority over her own body, but the husband does; likewise the husband does not have authority over his own body, but the wife does.

**5** Do not deprive one another, unless perhaps by agreement for a time, so that you may give yourselves to prayer; then come together again, so that Satan will not tempt you because of your lack of self-control.

**6** I say this as a concession, not as a command.

**7** I wish that everyone were as I am. But each has his own gift from God, one of one kind and one of another.

**8** To the unmarried and the widows I say: it is good for them to remain as I am.

**9** But if they cannot control themselves, they should marry, for it is better to marry than to burn with passion.

## The Lord's Command

**10** To the married I give this command — not I, but the Lord: the wife should not separate from her husband

**11** (but if she does separate, she must remain unmarried or else be reconciled to her husband), and the husband should not divorce his wife.

**12** To the rest I say — I, not the Lord: if any brother has a wife who is an unbeliever, and she is willing to live with him, he should not divorce her.

**13** If any woman has a husband who is an unbeliever, and he is willing to live with her, she should not divorce her husband.

**14** For the unbelieving husband is made holy through his wife, and the unbelieving wife is made holy through her husband. Otherwise your children would be unclean, but as it is, they are holy.

**15** But if the unbeliever separates, let him separate. In such cases the brother or the sister is not bound. God has called you to peace.

**16** For how do you know, wife, whether you will save your husband? Or how do you know, husband, whether you will save your wife?

## Remain as You Were Called

**17** Only, let each one live the life the Lord has assigned him, as God has called him. This is what I direct in all the churches.

**18** Was anyone called when he was already circumcised? He should not undo his circumcision. Was anyone called when uncircumcised? He should not be circumcised.

**19** Circumcision is nothing and uncircumcision is nothing; keeping the commandments of God is what counts.

**20** Each one should remain in the condition in which he was called.

**21** Were you a slave when you were called? Do not let it trouble you; but if you can gain your freedom, take the opportunity.

**22** For the one who was called in the Lord as a slave is the Lord's freedman; likewise the one who was free when called is Christ's slave.

**23** You were bought with a price; do not become slaves of men.

**24** Brothers, each one should remain before God in the condition in which he was called.

## The Unmarried

**25** Now about virgins: I have no command from the Lord, but I give my judgment as one who by the Lord's mercy is trustworthy.

**26** I think, then, that because of the present distress it is good for a person to remain as he is.

**27** Are you bound to a wife? Do not seek to be released. Are you released from a wife? Do not seek a wife.

**28** But if you do marry, you have not sinned, and if a virgin marries, she has not sinned. Yet those who marry will have troubles in this life, and I would spare you that.

**29** What I mean, brothers, is this: the time has been cut short. From now on, those who have wives should live as though they had none,

**30** those who weep as though they were not weeping, those who rejoice as though they were not rejoicing, those who buy as though they had no goods,

**31** and those who use the world as though they had no full use of it. For the present form of this world is passing away.

**32** I want you to be free from worry. The unmarried man is concerned about the things of the Lord, how to please the Lord.

**33** But the married man is concerned about the things of the world, how to please his wife,

**34** and his interests are divided. The unmarried woman or virgin is concerned about the things of the Lord, so as to be holy in body and in spirit; but the married woman is concerned about the things of the world, how to please her husband.

**35** I say this for your own good, not to put a noose on you, but to promote what is fitting and to help you serve the Lord without distraction.

## Giving a Daughter in Marriage

**36** If anyone thinks he is not acting properly toward his virgin daughter, if she is past the bloom of youth and it must be so, let him do as he wishes; he is not sinning. Let them marry.

**37** But whoever stands firm in his heart, being under no necessity but having control over his own will, and has decided in his heart to keep his virgin daughter, will do well.

**38** So the one who gives his virgin daughter in marriage does well, and the one who does not give her in marriage will do better.

## Widows

**39** A wife is bound for as long as her husband lives. But if her husband falls asleep, she is free to be married to whom she wishes, only in the Lord.

**40** Yet in my judgment she is happier if she remains as she is, and I think that I too have the Spirit of God.

## Notes

- **v1 "It is good for a man not to touch a woman"** — *peri de hōn egrapsate*, **about the things you wrote**: Paul is answering a letter from Corinth. The line that follows can be a saying from that letter, which Paul quotes and then qualifies (*But… each man should have his own wife*), as he quoted *All things are lawful for me* in 6:12; or it can be his own view, which the rest of the chapter limits. The Greek has no quotation marks.
- **v21 "if you can gain your freedom, take the opportunity"** — *mallon chrēsai*, word for word **rather use it**, with no object. *It* can be the freedom just named, **take the opportunity**, or the slave's present calling, **make the most of it instead**, in keeping with *each one should remain* (vv20, 24). V23, *do not become slaves of men*, leans toward freedom.
- **vv36–38 "his virgin daughter… the one who gives his virgin daughter in marriage"** — the Greek says only *his virgin*. *Gamizōn*, **giving in marriage**, is the verb of *neither marry nor are given in marriage* (Matthew 22:30), always so in the Gospels, and points to a father deciding about his unmarried daughter. Many English Bibles read instead a man and the young woman he is engaged to, with *gamizō* taken as **marry**. *Hyperakmos*, **past the bloom**, can be said of her age or of strong desire.

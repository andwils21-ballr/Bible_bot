# 1 Corinthians 6

## Lawsuits Among Believers

**1** When one of you has a dispute with another, does he dare to go to law before the unrighteous instead of before the saints?

**2** Or do you not know that the saints will judge the world? If the world is to be judged by you, are you not fit to try the smallest cases?

**3** Do you not know that we will judge angels? How much more, matters of this life!

**4** So if you have such cases, why do you seat as judges those who count for nothing in the church?

**5** I say this to your shame. Is there really no one among you wise enough to settle a dispute between brothers,

**6** but brother goes to law against brother, and that before unbelievers?

**7** To have lawsuits with one another at all is already a defeat for you. Why not rather be wronged? Why not rather be cheated?

**8** Instead, you yourselves do wrong and cheat, and your own brothers at that!

## Washed, Made Holy, Justified

**9** Or do you not know that the unrighteous will not inherit the kingdom of God? Do not be deceived: neither the sexually immoral, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor effeminate men, nor men who lie with men,

**10** nor thieves, nor the greedy, nor drunkards, nor slanderers, nor swindlers will inherit the kingdom of God.

**11** Such were some of you. But you were washed, you were made holy, you were justified in the name of the Lord Jesus and by the Spirit of our God.

## Your Body Is a Temple

**12** "All things are lawful for me" — but not all things are good for me. "All things are lawful for me" — but I will not be mastered by anything.

**13** "Food is for the stomach and the stomach for food" — and God will do away with both one and the other. The body is not for sexual immorality, but for the Lord, and the Lord for the body.

**14** God raised the Lord, and He will raise us up too, by His power.

**15** Do you not know that your bodies are parts of Christ? Shall I then take the parts of Christ and make them parts of a prostitute? By no means!

**16** Or do you not know that whoever is joined to a prostitute becomes one body with her? For it is said, "The two will become one flesh."

**17** But whoever is joined to the Lord becomes one spirit with Him.

**18** Flee sexual immorality. Every other sin a person commits is outside the body, but the sexually immoral person sins against his own body.

**19** Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you, whom you have from God? You are not your own,

**20** for you were bought with a price. So glorify God in your body.

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "the saints will judge the world… we will judge angels"** — Daniel's vision, *judgment was given to the holy ones of the Most High, and… the holy ones possessed the kingdom* (Daniel 7:22). Angels under judgment are the story of 1 Enoch, where the angels who sinned are bound *until the day of their judgment* (1 Enoch 10:12) and Enoch tells Azazel *A great judgment has gone out against you* (13:1). Paul does not say which angels.
- **v4 "why do you seat as judges those who count for nothing in the church?"** — *kathizete* can be a question, a rebuke for taking cases before outsiders the church does not esteem, or a command, **seat as judges even those who count for least in the church**: better the least of the believers than the courts of unbelievers.
- **v9 "effeminate men, nor men who lie with men"** — *malakoi*, word for word **soft ones**, the word of *soft clothing* in Jesus' question about John (Matthew 11:8). In this list it stands beside the next word, and whether it means effeminate men in general or the passive partner in sex between men is not settled. *Arsenokoitai* is made of the two words of the Greek of Leviticus 20:13, *meta arsenos koitēn*, **lying with a male**: *A man who lies with a male as one lies with a woman*.
- **vv16–17 "The two will become one flesh… whoever is joined to the Lord becomes one spirit with Him"** — Genesis 2:24, *clings to his woman, and they become one flesh*, in the words Jesus used (Mark 10:8). *Kollōmenos*, **joined**, is the Greek of *clings* in Genesis, and Paul uses it both of the man joined to a prostitute and of the one joined to the Lord.
- **vv19–20 "your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit… you were bought with a price"** — in 3:16 the temple was the church together; here it is each one's own body. *Ēgorasthēte*, **you were bought**, from *agora*, the market. Paul says it again in 7:23, *You were bought with a price; do not become slaves of men*.

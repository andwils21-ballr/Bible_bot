# 1 Corinthians 16

## The Collection

**1** Now about the collection for the saints: do as I directed the churches of Galatia.

**2** On the first day of every week, each of you should set aside and save something, as he prospers, so that no collections need be made when I come.

**3** When I arrive, I will send those whom you approve, with letters, to carry your gift to Jerusalem.

**4** If it seems right for me to go too, they will go with me.

## Paul's Plans

**5** I will come to you after I pass through Macedonia, for I am passing through Macedonia,

**6** and perhaps I will stay with you, or even spend the winter, so that you may send me on my way wherever I go.

**7** I do not want to see you now just in passing; I hope to spend some time with you, if the Lord permits.

**8** But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost,

**9** for a wide door for effective work has opened to me, and there are many who oppose me.

**10** When Timothy comes, see that he has nothing to fear among you, for he is doing the work of the Lord, as I am.

**11** So let no one look down on him. Send him on his way in peace, so that he may come to me, for I am expecting him with the brothers.

**12** As for our brother Apollos, I strongly urged him to visit you with the brothers, but it was not at all his will to come now. He will come when he has the opportunity.

## Final Words

**13** Be watchful, stand firm in the faith, be courageous, be strong.

**14** Let all that you do be done in love.

**15** I urge you, brothers — you know that the household of Stephanas were the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have devoted themselves to serving the saints —

**16** to submit yourselves to such people, and to everyone who works and labors with them.

**17** I rejoice at the coming of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus, because they have made up for your absence,

**18** for they refreshed my spirit as well as yours. Give recognition to such men.

**19** The churches of Asia greet you. Aquila and Prisca greet you warmly in the Lord, together with the church that meets in their house.

**20** All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss.

**21** I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand.

**22** If anyone does not love the Lord, let him be accursed. Marana tha!

**23** The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.

**24** My love be with all of you in Christ Jesus.

## Notes

- **v2 "On the first day of every week"** — *kata mian sabbatou*, the day the women came to the tomb, *on the first day of the week* (Mark 16:2), and the day the believers at Troas *gathered together to break bread* (Acts 20:7). The gift is set aside on the day of the resurrection.
- **v13 "be courageous, be strong"** — *andrizesthe, krataiousthe*, the two verbs of Psalm 31:24 in the Greek, *Be strong, and let your heart take courage, all you who wait for the LORD*, and the word given to Joshua, *Be strong and courageous* (Joshua 1:6). *Andrizomai* is from *anēr*, **man**: word for word, **act like men**.
- **v22 "Marana tha!"** — Aramaic, kept in Greek letters in a letter to Greeks, as *Abba* was (Romans 8:15). Divided as the edition has it, *marana tha*, it is a prayer, **Our Lord, come!**, the cry that ends the Bible, *Come, Lord Jesus!* (Revelation 22:20). Divided *maran atha*, it is a confession, **Our Lord has come**.

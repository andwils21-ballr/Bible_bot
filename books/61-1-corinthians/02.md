# 1 Corinthians 2

## Christ Crucified

**1** When I came to you, brothers, I did not come with lofty speech or wisdom as I proclaimed to you the testimony of God.

**2** For I decided to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ, and Him crucified.

**3** I came to you in weakness and in fear and with much trembling,

**4** and my speech and my preaching were not with the persuasion of wisdom, but with a demonstration of the Spirit and of power,

**5** so that your faith would not rest on the wisdom of men but on the power of God.

## The Hidden Wisdom

**6** Yet among the mature we do speak wisdom, though not a wisdom of this age or of the rulers of this age, who are being brought to nothing.

**7** We speak God's wisdom in a mystery, the hidden wisdom that God appointed beforehand, before the ages, for our glory.

**8** None of the rulers of this age understood it, for if they had, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.

**9** But, as it is written:  
"What no eye has seen and no ear has heard,  
and what has not come into the heart of man,  
what God has prepared for those who love Him" —

**10** these things God has revealed to us through the Spirit. For the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God.

**11** For who knows a person's thoughts except the spirit of that person, which is in him? So also no one has known the thoughts of God except the Spirit of God.

**12** We have not received the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God, so that we may understand what God has freely given us.

**13** These things we also speak, not in words taught by human wisdom but in words taught by the Spirit, explaining spiritual things in spiritual words.

**14** The natural person does not accept the things of the Spirit of God, for they are foolishness to him, and he cannot understand them, because they are judged spiritually.

**15** The spiritual person judges all things, but is himself judged by no one.

**16** "For who has known the mind of the Lord, so as to instruct Him?" But we have the mind of Christ.

## Notes

- **vv6–8 "the rulers of this age, who are being brought to nothing… they would not have crucified the Lord of glory"** — *hoi archontes tou aiōnos toutou* can be the men who put Jesus to death, the high priests and Pilate, or the unseen powers behind them, as Romans 8:38 counts *rulers*, *archai*, among the powers that cannot separate us from the love of God. *The Lord of glory* is a title of God in 1 Enoch, *the Holy and Great One, the Lord of glory, the Eternal King* (1 Enoch 25:3), and Psalm 24 calls Him *the King of glory*. Paul gives it to the one who was crucified.
- **v9 "What no eye has seen and no ear has heard"** — *as it is written*, but no verse reads exactly so. The nearest is Isaiah 64:4, *no ear has perceived, no eye has seen a God besides You, who acts for the one who waits for Him*; *what has not come into the heart* is in the Greek of Isaiah 65:17. Paul has *for those who love Him* where Isaiah has *for the one who waits for Him*.
- **vv13–15 "spiritual things in spiritual words… The natural person… The spiritual person"** — *pneumatikois pneumatika synkrinontes* can be read **explaining spiritual things in spiritual words**, **to spiritual people**, or **comparing spiritual things with spiritual**. *Psychikos*, **natural**, is from *psychē*, the life every human being has; *pneumatikos*, **spiritual**, from *pneuma*, the Spirit God gives. The same pair returns in 15:44, *a natural body… a spiritual body*.
- **v16 "who has known the mind of the Lord… But we have the mind of Christ"** — Isaiah 40:13 in the Greek, which Paul quoted in Romans 11:34 to say no one has. Here he answers it: the mind of *the Lord*, YHWH in Isaiah, is given in *the mind of Christ*.

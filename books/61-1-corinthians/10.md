# 1 Corinthians 10

## Patterns for Us

**1** For I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, that our fathers were all under the cloud, and all passed through the sea,

**2** and all were baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea,

**3** and all ate the same spiritual food,

**4** and all drank the same spiritual drink, for they drank from the spiritual rock that followed them, and the rock was Christ.

**5** Yet with most of them God was not pleased, for they were struck down in the wilderness.

**6** These things took place as patterns for us, so that we would not desire evil as they did.

**7** Do not be idolaters, as some of them were; as it is written, "The people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play."

**8** We must not commit sexual immorality, as some of them did, and twenty-three thousand fell in a single day.

**9** We must not put Christ to the test, as some of them did, and were destroyed by the serpents.

**10** Do not grumble, as some of them did, and were destroyed by the destroyer.

**11** These things happened to them as a pattern, and they were written down for our instruction, on whom the ends of the ages have come.

**12** So let anyone who thinks he stands take care that he does not fall.

**13** No testing has overtaken you that is not common to man. God is faithful, and He will not let you be tested beyond what you can bear; with the testing He will also provide the way out, so that you can endure it.

## The Table of the Lord

**14** Therefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry.

**15** I speak as to sensible people; judge for yourselves what I say.

**16** The cup of blessing that we bless, is it not a sharing in the blood of Christ? The bread that we break, is it not a sharing in the body of Christ?

**17** Because there is one bread, we who are many are one body, for we all partake of the one bread.

**18** Look at the people of Israel: are not those who eat the sacrifices sharers in the altar?

**19** What do I mean, then? That food offered to idols is anything, or that an idol is anything?

**20** No, but that what they sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons and not to God. I do not want you to be sharers with demons.

**21** You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons. You cannot partake of the table of the Lord and the table of demons.

**22** Or are we provoking the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than He is?

## Do All to the Glory of God

**23** "All things are lawful," but not all things are good for us. "All things are lawful," but not all things build up.

**24** Let no one seek his own good, but the good of the other.

**25** Eat whatever is sold in the meat market without raising any question for the sake of conscience,

**26** for "the earth is the Lord's, and all that fills it."

**27** If one of the unbelievers invites you to dinner and you want to go, eat whatever is set before you without raising any question for the sake of conscience.

**28** But if someone says to you, "This has been offered in sacrifice," do not eat it, for the sake of the one who told you, and for the sake of conscience —

**29** I mean not your own conscience but the other person's. For why should my freedom be judged by someone else's conscience?

**30** If I partake with thankfulness, why am I denounced over what I give thanks for?

**31** So whether you eat or drink, or whatever you do, do all to the glory of God.

**32** Give no offense to Jews or to Greeks or to the church of God,

**33** just as I try to please everyone in everything, not seeking my own advantage but that of the many, so that they may be saved.

## Notes

- **vv1–4, 9 "the cloud… the sea… the spiritual rock that followed them, and the rock was Christ… We must not put Christ to the test"** — the Exodus read as the church's own story: the cloud and the sea as a baptism, the manna and the water as spiritual food and drink. The water came from a rock at the start of the wandering (*You shall strike the rock, and water will come out of it*, Exodus 17:6) and again near its end (Numbers 20:11); Moses' song calls God Himself *The Rock* (Deuteronomy 32:4, 15). Paul names the rock *Christ*, and says Israel tested *Christ* in the wilderness (v9; Numbers 21:6).
- **vv6–11 "patterns for us… twenty-three thousand fell in a single day… destroyed by the destroyer"** — *typoi*, **patterns**, the word of Adam as *a pattern of the one who was to come* (Romans 5:14). V7 is the golden calf (Exodus 32:6); v8 is Baal of Peor, where Numbers 25:9, in the Hebrew and the Greek, counts *twenty-four thousand* who died in the plague. *The destroyer* is the one the LORD would not let *enter your houses to strike* on the night of Passover (Exodus 12:23).
- **vv16–17 "a sharing in the blood of Christ… one bread, one body"** — *koinōnia*, **sharing**, the word of *fellowship with His Son* (1:9). The many who eat the one loaf are made one body: the meal itself is the argument against eating at the table of idols.
- **vv20, 22 "they sacrifice to demons and not to God… are we provoking the Lord to jealousy?"** — Moses' song again, *They sacrificed to demons, not God* (Deuteronomy 32:17), in the Greek word for word as Paul has it, and *They made Me jealous with what is no god* (32:21), the verse Paul used in Romans 10:19 and 11:11. The song that told Israel's idolatry tells the Corinthians'.

# 1 Corinthians 5

## A Man and His Father's Wife

**1** It is actually reported that there is sexual immorality among you, and of a kind not found even among the Gentiles: a man has his father's wife.

**2** Yet you are puffed up! Should you not rather have mourned, so that the one who did this would be removed from among you?

**3** For though I am absent in body, I am present in spirit, and as if present I have already passed judgment on the one who did this.

**4** When you are assembled in the name of our Lord Jesus, and my spirit is with you, with the power of our Lord Jesus,

**5** you are to hand this man over to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, so that his spirit may be saved on the day of the Lord.

## Christ Our Passover

**6** Your boasting is not good. Do you not know that a little yeast leavens the whole lump of dough?

**7** Clean out the old yeast, so that you may be a new lump, as you really are unleavened. For Christ, our Passover lamb, has been sacrificed.

**8** So let us keep the feast, not with the old yeast, the yeast of malice and wickedness, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

## Judging Those Inside

**9** I wrote to you in my letter not to associate with the sexually immoral,

**10** not meaning the sexually immoral of this world at all, or the greedy and swindlers, or idolaters, since then you would have to go out of the world.

**11** But now I am writing to you not to associate with anyone who goes by the name of brother if he is sexually immoral or greedy, or an idolater, a slanderer, a drunkard or a swindler — not even to eat with such a man.

**12** For what business is it of mine to judge those outside? Is it not those inside whom you are to judge?

**13** God judges those outside. "Purge the evil from among you."

## Notes

- **v5 "hand this man over to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, so that his spirit may be saved"** — *paradounai*, the verb of *God handed them over* (Romans 1:24). *The flesh* can be the man's body, so that sickness or death follows, or *the flesh* as Romans uses it, the self that sins (Romans 8:13). The goal is rescue, not ruin: *that his spirit may be saved*.
- **vv6–8 "Clean out the old yeast… Christ, our Passover lamb, has been sacrificed"** — the first night of Passover, when Israel *put away leaven* from every house and ate *unleavened bread* seven days (Exodus 12:15), and Moses said *slaughter the Passover* (12:21). Paul reads the church as the house to be cleared, and Christ as the lamb already killed: the feast has begun.

# 1 Corinthians 4

## Stewards of the Mysteries

**1** This is how people should reckon us: as servants of Christ and stewards of the mysteries of God.

**2** What is required of stewards, moreover, is that each be found faithful.

**3** But to me it matters very little that I should be judged by you or by any human day of judgment. I do not even judge myself.

**4** For I know of nothing against myself, but I am not justified by that. The one who judges me is the Lord.

**5** So do not pronounce judgment before the time, before the Lord comes. He will bring to light what is hidden in darkness and make known the purposes of hearts, and then each one will receive his praise from God.

## Nothing Beyond What Is Written

**6** I have applied all this to myself and Apollos for your sake, brothers, so that you may learn from us the saying "Nothing beyond what is written," and none of you may be puffed up in favor of one man against another.

**7** For who makes you different from anyone else? What do you have that you did not receive? If you received it, why do you boast as if you had not?

**8** Already you have all you want! Already you have become rich! Without us you have become kings! I wish you really were kings, so that we might be kings with you.

**9** For I think God has put us apostles on display last of all, like men sentenced to death, because we have become a spectacle to the world, to angels and to men.

**10** We are fools for Christ's sake, but you are wise in Christ. We are weak, but you are strong. You are held in honor, but we in disgrace.

**11** To this very hour we go hungry and thirsty, we are poorly clothed and beaten and homeless,

**12** we labor, working with our own hands. When we are reviled, we bless; when we are persecuted, we endure;

**13** when we are slandered, we answer kindly. We have become like the scum of the world, the refuse of all things, to this day.

## My Beloved Children

**14** I do not write these things to shame you, but to warn you as my beloved children.

**15** For though you may have ten thousand guardians in Christ, you do not have many fathers. In Christ Jesus I became your father through the gospel.

**16** So I urge you, be imitators of me.

**17** That is why I sent you Timothy, my beloved and faithful child in the Lord, to remind you of my ways in Christ Jesus, as I teach them everywhere in every church.

**18** Some of you have become puffed up, as though I were not coming to you.

**19** But I will come to you soon, if the Lord wills, and I will find out not the talk of these puffed-up people but their power.

**20** For the kingdom of God is not a matter of talk but of power.

**21** Which do you want? Shall I come to you with a rod, or with love and a spirit of gentleness?

## Notes

- **vv6, 18–19 "Nothing beyond what is written… puffed up"** — four Greek words, *mē hyper ha gegraptai*, and the meaning has never been settled: keep within the Scriptures Paul has just quoted (1:19, 31; 3:19–20), or within what Scripture says at all. *Physioō*, **puff up**: six of its eight uses in the New Testament are in this letter, down to *love is not puffed up* (13:4).

# Exodus 33

## The Ornaments

**1** And the LORD said to Moses, "Go, go up from here — you and the people you brought up from the land of Egypt — to the land I swore to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, saying, 'To your seed I will give it.'

**2** And I will send a messenger before you, and I will drive out the Canaanite, the Amorite and the Hittite and the Perizzite, the Hivite and the Jebusite —

**3** to a land flowing with milk and honey. But I will not go up among you, because you are a stiff-necked people, or I would finish you on the way."

**4** And the people heard this bad word and they mourned, and no one put on his ornaments.

**5** And the LORD said to Moses, "Say to the sons of Israel: You are a stiff-necked people. If I went up among you for one moment, I would finish you. And now, take your ornaments off yourself, and I will know what to do with you."

**6** And the sons of Israel stripped themselves of their ornaments, from Mount Horeb onward.

## The Tent Outside the Camp

**7** And Moses would take the tent and pitch it outside the camp, far off from the camp, and he called it the tent of meeting. And everyone who sought the LORD would go out to the tent of meeting, which was outside the camp.

**8** And it would be, when Moses went out to the tent, that all the people would rise and stand, each at the entrance of his own tent, and watch Moses until he went into the tent.

**9** And it would be, when Moses went into the tent, that the pillar of cloud came down and stood at the entrance of the tent, and He spoke with Moses.

**10** And all the people would see the pillar of cloud standing at the entrance of the tent, and all the people would rise and bow down, each at the entrance of his own tent.

**11** And the LORD would speak to Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his companion. And he would return to the camp — but his attendant Joshua son of Nun, a young man, would not move out of the tent.

## If Your Face Does Not Go

**12** And Moses said to the LORD, "See — You say to me, 'Bring this people up,' and You have not let me know whom You will send with me. And You Yourself said, 'I know you by name, and you have also found favor in My eyes.'

**13** And now, if I have found favor in Your eyes, let me know Your ways, so that I may know You and find favor in Your eyes. And see, this nation is Your people."

**14** And He said, "My face will go, and I will give you rest."

**15** And he said to Him, "If Your face is not going, do not bring us up from here.

**16** For how will it be known that I have found favor in Your eyes, I and Your people, if not by Your going with us — so that we are marked out, I and Your people, from every people on the face of the ground?"

**17** And the LORD said to Moses, "This thing you have spoken, I will also do, because you have found favor in My eyes, and I know you by name."

## The Cleft of the Rock

**18** And he said, "Show me, please, Your glory."

**19** And He said, "I Myself will make all My goodness pass before your face, and I will call out the name of the LORD before you. And I will be gracious to whom I will be gracious, and I will have compassion on whom I will have compassion."

**20** And He said, "You cannot see My face, because no human being can see Me and live."

**21** And the LORD said, "Here is a place beside Me, and you shall stand on the rock.

**22** And it will be, when My glory passes by, that I will put you in the cleft of the rock and cover you with My palm until I have passed by.

**23** And I will take away My palm, and you will see My back. But My face will not be seen."

## Notes

- **v3 "I will not go up among you"** — the offer is the land without the giver. Everything promised to Abraham is still on the table — the country, the driving out of the nations, the milk and honey — and the one thing withdrawn is God's own company. And the reason given is not punishment but danger: *or I would finish you on the way*. He is saying that travelling with them would kill them.
- **vv4–6 "his ornaments"** — *edyo*, the jewellery that in 32:2 came off their ears and went into the fire. What is left of it comes off again here, and this time it is not melted down, it is simply not worn. The chapter's first act after the calf is a nation taking off its gold and leaving it off, "from Mount Horeb onward."
- **v7 "he called it the tent of meeting"** — *ohel mo'ed*, the name given to the tabernacle at 27:21. But the tabernacle has not been built; chapters 35–40 are still to come. This is a different tent, Moses' own, pitched deliberately **outside** the camp. So the book has two structures with one name, and the one that exists at this moment is the one God will not come into the camp to occupy.
- **v11 "face to face"** — *panim el panim*, and nine verses later: *you cannot see My face, because no human being can see Me and live* (v20). The chapter states both, in the same conversation, without a word of reconciliation. The plainest reading is that *face to face* is the Hebrew for speaking directly, the way *a man speaks to his companion* — which the verse itself supplies as the comparison — while v20 is about seeing. Whether that settles it is a question the text leaves open, and this rendering leaves it open too.
- **v11 "Joshua… would not move out of the tent"** — a single clause, dropped in without comment. Moses goes back to the camp each time. The young man stays. Nothing is made of it here, and the book never explains what he was doing in there.
- **v14 "My face will go"** — *panai yelekhu*. English Bibles print "My presence will go," which is the sense and hides the word: the whole argument from v11 to v23 is conducted in the noun *panim*, **face**. God's face will go; Moses says if Your face does not go, do not move us; and the argument ends with God covering Moses with His hand so that His face is not seen. One word, eight times, holding the chapter together.
- **v16 "so that we are marked out"** — *ve-niflinu*, from *pala*, the root behind *wonder* and *miracle* — to be separated off, made distinct, set apart as remarkable. Moses' argument is that there is no observable difference between Israel and anyone else except that God travels with them. Not the law, not the land, not the ancestry: the company.
- **v19 "I will be gracious to whom I will be gracious"** — *ve-channoti et asher achon*, the same construction as *ehyeh asher ehyeh*, **I will be what I will be**, at the burning bush (3:14). Hebrew builds these by repeating a verb around its own relative clause, and what they do is refuse to specify. Asked to show His glory, God answers with a sentence shaped exactly like the one He gave when asked His name.
- **v20 "no human being can see Me and live"** — *lo yir'ani ha-adam va-chai*. And nine chapters back, seventy-four elders *saw the God of Israel* and *ate and drank* (24:10–11). The book records both and never addresses the gap.
- **vv22–23 "the cleft of the rock… you will see My back"** — *achorai*, the back parts, what is behind. The arrangement is physical and almost domestic: a place to stand, a crack in a rock, a hand over the man's face until the moment has gone by, then the hand lifted and one look at what is already leaving. What Moses asked for was *kavod*, the weight and glory. What he is given is the sight of it from behind, after it has passed.

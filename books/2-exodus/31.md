# Exodus 31

## Bezalel and Oholiab

**1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "See, I have called by name Bezalel son of Uri son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.

**3** And I have filled him with the spirit of God — with wisdom and with understanding and with knowledge and with every kind of craft —

**4** to devise designs, to work in gold and in silver and in bronze,

**5** and in the cutting of stones for setting, and in the carving of wood, to work in every kind of craft.

**6** And I — here, I have given him Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan. And in the heart of everyone wise of heart I have put wisdom, and they shall make everything I have commanded you:

**7** the tent of meeting, and the ark for the Testimony, and the cover that is on it, and all the vessels of the tent;

**8** and the table and its vessels, and the pure lampstand and all its vessels, and the altar of incense;

**9** and the altar of burnt offering and all its vessels, and the basin and its stand;

**10** and the woven garments, and the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and his sons' garments, for serving as priests;

**11** and the anointing oil, and the fragrant incense for the holy place. According to all that I have commanded you, they shall do."

## The Sign

**12** And the LORD said to Moses, saying,

**13** "And you — speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'Above all, you shall keep My sabbaths. For it is a sign between Me and you, through your generations, so that you may know that I am the LORD who sets you apart.

**14** And you shall keep the sabbath, for it is holy to you. Whoever profanes it shall surely be put to death, for anyone who does work on it — that soul shall be cut off from among his people.

**15** Six days work shall be done, and on the seventh day is a sabbath of solemn rest, holy to the LORD. Whoever does work on the sabbath day shall surely be put to death.

**16** And the sons of Israel shall keep the sabbath, to make the sabbath through their generations — an everlasting covenant.

**17** Between Me and the sons of Israel it is a sign forever. For in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh day He stopped, and He took breath.'"

## Written by the Finger of God

**18** And when He had finished speaking with him on Mount Sinai, He gave Moses the two tablets of the Testimony — tablets of stone, written by the finger of God.

## Notes

- **v2 "Bezalel son of Uri son of Hur"** — *Betzal'el* is a sentence: *be-tzel El*, **in the shadow of God**. In a country where shade is what keeps a man alive, being in someone's shadow is being under their protection. And his grandfather is named: **Hur** is the man who held up one of Moses' arms at Rephidim while Aaron held the other (17:12), and who was left holding the court with Aaron when Moses went up the mountain (24:14). The builder of the sanctuary is the grandson of the man who kept Moses' hands steady.
- **v3 "filled him with the spirit of God"** — *ruach Elohim*, and this is what it is given for: gold, silver, bronze, stonecutting and joinery. The list that follows the spirit is a list of trades. Compare 28:3, where the weavers are *filled with a spirit of wisdom* — in this book, the only people said to be filled with a spirit are craftsmen.
- **v4 "to devise designs"** — *lachshov machashavot*, the verb with its own noun. The root is *chashav*, to think, calculate, reckon — the same root behind *ma'aseh choshev*, the **work of a designer**, on the inner curtains and the veil (26:1, 26:31), and behind the *choshen*, the breastpiece. The Hebrew for artistic design is a word for thinking hard.
- **v6 "Oholiab… of the tribe of Dan"** — the second craftsman is paired deliberately with the first, and the pairing is unbalanced. Bezalel is of **Judah**, the tribe that gets the kingship. Oholiab is of **Dan**, the tribe descended from Bilhah, Rachel's servant girl (Genesis 30:6) — the one Genesis 49 compares to a snake in the road. The two men who build the sanctuary come from the top and the bottom of the family.
- **v6 "in the heart of everyone wise of heart I have put wisdom"** — the sentence doubles back on itself: wisdom is put into those who are already wise. It is not a promise to make the unskilled able. The gift goes to people who could already do the work, and the text does not explain the difference between what they had and what was added.
- **vv12–17 the sabbath lands here, and the placement is the point** — six chapters of instructions for building, and the last word before the tablets are handed over is: not on the seventh day. The command is attached to the construction project, not to worship or to rest in general. Whatever else this sanctuary is worth, it is not worth working through a sabbath to finish it.
- **v13 "a sign"** — *ot*, the same noun as the rainbow after the flood (Genesis 9:12) and circumcision in the flesh (Genesis 17:11). The previous two signs were a thing in the sky and a mark on the body. This one is a **day** — a sign made out of time rather than matter, and the only one of the three that has to be renewed every week.
- **v15 "a sabbath of solemn rest"** — *shabbat shabbaton*, the noun doubled on itself, which is how Hebrew makes a superlative: a sabbath of sabbaths, the same construction as *qodesh ha-qodashim*, holy of holies. English has to reach for an adjective the Hebrew does not use.
- **v17 "He stopped, and He took breath"** — *shavat va-yinnafash*. The first verb is the one the noun *shabbat* is made from: to **stop**, to cease, not to sleep. The second is built on *nefesh*, throat and breath and life — the verb used at 23:12 for what the ox, the donkey and the foreigner get to do on the seventh day. It is said here of **God**. The same word for the animal catching its breath and for the Creator catching His.
- **v18 "written by the finger of God"** — *ketuvim be-etzba Elohim*. The phrase appeared once before in this book, in Pharaoh's court, when the magicians could not reproduce the gnats and said *this is the finger of God* (8:19). The first time it was an argument they lost; here it is a description of handwriting. And the next sentence in the book is the people at the bottom of the mountain telling Aaron to make them gods.

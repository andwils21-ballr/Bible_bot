# Exodus 25

## The Contribution

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, and let them take up a contribution for Me. From every man whose heart moves him you shall take My contribution.

**3** This is the contribution you shall take from them: gold and silver and bronze,

**4** and blue and purple and crimson, and fine linen and goats' hair,

**5** and rams' skins dyed red, and fine leather, and acacia wood,

**6** oil for the light, spices for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense,

**7** onyx stones and stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece.

**8** Let them make Me a sanctuary, and I will dwell among them.

**9** According to all that I show you — the pattern of the dwelling and the pattern of all its furnishings — so you shall make it.

## The Ark

**10** They shall make an ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits its length, and a cubit and a half its width, and a cubit and a half its height.

**11** You shall overlay it with pure gold — inside and outside you shall overlay it — and you shall make on it a molding of gold all around.

**12** You shall cast for it four rings of gold and put them on its four feet: two rings on its one side and two rings on its other side.

**13** You shall make poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold.

**14** You shall put the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, to carry the ark by them.

**15** The poles shall stay in the rings of the ark. They shall not be taken out of it.

**16** You shall put into the ark the Testimony that I will give you.

**17** You shall make a cover of pure gold, two and a half cubits its length and a cubit and a half its width.

**18** You shall make two cherubim of gold — hammered work you shall make them — at the two ends of the cover.

**19** Make one cherub at one end and one cherub at the other end. Of one piece with the cover you shall make the cherubim, at its two ends.

**20** The cherubim shall spread their wings upward, sheltering the cover with their wings, and their faces one toward the other. Toward the cover shall the faces of the cherubim be.

**21** You shall put the cover on the ark from above, and into the ark you shall put the Testimony that I will give you.

**22** I will meet with you there, and I will speak with you from above the cover, from between the two cherubim that are on the ark of the Testimony — everything that I command you for the sons of Israel.

## The Table

**23** You shall make a table of acacia wood, two cubits its length and a cubit its width and a cubit and a half its height.

**24** You shall overlay it with pure gold, and make for it a molding of gold all around.

**25** You shall make for it a rim a handbreadth wide all around, and make a molding of gold for its rim all around.

**26** You shall make for it four rings of gold, and put the rings on the four corners that are at its four legs.

**27** The rings shall be close to the rim, as holders for the poles to carry the table.

**28** You shall make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold, and the table shall be carried by them.

**29** You shall make its dishes and its pans and its jars and its bowls, for pouring out drink offerings. Of pure gold you shall make them.

**30** You shall set on the table the bread of the Presence, before Me always.

## The Lampstand

**31** You shall make a lampstand of pure gold. Hammered work the lampstand shall be made — its base and its shaft, its cups, its bulbs and its blossoms shall be of one piece with it.

**32** Six branches shall come out of its sides: three branches of the lampstand from its one side and three branches of the lampstand from its other side.

**33** Three cups shaped like almond blossoms on the one branch, a bulb and a blossom, and three cups shaped like almond blossoms on the next branch, a bulb and a blossom — so for the six branches coming out of the lampstand.

**34** On the lampstand itself, four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with its bulbs and its blossoms.

**35** A bulb under the first two branches of one piece with it, and a bulb under the next two branches of one piece with it, and a bulb under the next two branches of one piece with it — for the six branches coming out of the lampstand.

**36** Their bulbs and their branches shall be of one piece with it, all of it one hammered work of pure gold.

**37** You shall make its seven lamps, and its lamps shall be set up to give light in front of it.

**38** Its tongs and its trays shall be of pure gold.

**39** Of a talent of pure gold it shall be made, with all these furnishings.

**40** See, and make them by their pattern, which you are being shown on the mountain."

## Notes

- **v2 "a contribution… whose heart moves him"** — *terumah*, from *rum*, to **lift**: a portion lifted off a larger amount and set aside. And the qualification is *asher yiddvennu libbo*, whose heart **makes him willing**. The sanctuary is funded by nothing but what people choose to give, and the list that follows is the exact inventory of what a nation carried out of Egypt.
- **v8 "and I will dwell among them"** — not *in it*. The building is commanded and then the purpose is stated in the plural, about the people: *be-tokham*, **in their midst**. The verb is *shakhan*, to settle down and stay, which gives the sanctuary its Hebrew name in the next verse — *mishkan*, **the dwelling** — and much later gives Judaism the word *shekhinah* for God's settled presence.
- **vv9, 40 "the pattern"** — *tavnit*, from *banah*, to build: a model, the thing a builder works from. The instruction opens and closes on it, and v40 says Moses is **being shown** it on the mountain. The Greek renders it *typos*, and Hebrews quotes that verse from the Greek almost word for word — *see that you make everything according to the pattern shown you on the mountain* (8:5) — to argue that the whole sanctuary was a copy of something else.
- **v17 "a cover"** — *kapporet*, from the root *kipper*, to cover over and so to atone; the Day of Atonement is *Yom Kippur* from the same three letters. The Greek calls it the *hilastērion*, the place where atonement is made, and that is the word Paul uses of Jesus in Romans 3:25. English "mercy seat" comes down from Tyndale and is a translation of Luther's *Gnadenstuhl*, not of the Hebrew, which says only **cover**.
- **vv18–20 "two cherubim… hammered work"** — beaten out of the same sheet as the cover, so they are not attached to it; they are it. The last time this canon mentioned cherubim, they were posted at the east of the garden with a turning sword to guard the way to the tree of life (Genesis 3:24). Here they are made objects again, wings spread over the lid of a box, facing each other and looking down.
- **v22 "and I will meet with you there"** — *ve-no'adti lekha sham*, from the root behind *mo'ed*, an appointed meeting. This is the sentence the ark exists for. Everything before it is joinery and gold leaf; this is what the container is for — a place with an address, where God says He will be found and will speak, from the empty space between two golden figures.
- **v30 "the bread of the Presence"** — *lechem panim*, literally **bread of the face**. The same noun that carries God's face everywhere else in Exodus: *they shall not appear before My face empty-handed* (23:15), *no one can see My face and live* (33:20). Twelve loaves sit on a table in the dark, permanently, in front of a face.
- **vv31–36 the lampstand is a tree** — the pieces are named as botany: a *yarekh* (base, the same noun as the loin), a *qaneh* (shaft, also a reed or stalk), *qanim* (branches), *kaftorim* (bulbs or calyxes), and *perachim* (blossoms). Six branches come off a central stem, and the cups are *meshuqqadim*, **almond-blossomed**, three times over. What stands in the sanctuary giving light is a stylized almond tree in gold.
- **v33 "almond"** — *shaqed*, and the tree is the first to flower in that country, before its own leaves are out. The word is one letter from *shoqed*, **watching, staying awake**, and Jeremiah's call turns on exactly that: he sees *maqqel shaqed*, an almond branch, and God answers *shoqed ani al devari la-asoto* — **I am watching over My word to do it** (Jeremiah 1:11–12). The tree that wakes first is the tree the language uses for staying awake.
- **v31 "hammered work"** — *miqshah*, the lampstand and the cherubim both. Not cast in a mold and not assembled from parts: beaten out of a single mass of metal, so that no seam exists anywhere in either object. Verse 39 gives the budget for it as a talent of gold — roughly the weight of a grown man's load, all of it one piece.

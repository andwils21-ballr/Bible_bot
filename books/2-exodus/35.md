# Exodus 35

## The Sabbath, Before Anything Is Built

**1** And Moses assembled all the assembly of the sons of Israel and said to them, "These are the words that the LORD has commanded, to do them.

**2** Six days work shall be done, and on the seventh day there shall be a holy thing for you — a sabbath of solemn rest for the LORD. Anyone who does work on it shall be put to death.

**3** You shall not kindle a fire in any of your settlements on the sabbath day."

## The Call for the Contribution

**4** And Moses said to all the assembly of the sons of Israel, "This is the thing that the LORD has commanded, saying:

**5** Take from among you a contribution for the LORD. Everyone of a willing heart shall bring it — the LORD's contribution: gold and silver and bronze,

**6** and blue and purple and crimson, and fine linen and goats' hair,

**7** and rams' skins dyed red, and fine leather, and acacia wood,

**8** and oil for the light, and spices for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense,

**9** and onyx stones and stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece.

**10** And everyone with a wise heart among you shall come and make everything that the LORD has commanded:

**11** the dwelling, its tent and its covering, its clasps and its frames, its bars, its pillars and its sockets;

**12** the ark and its poles, the cover, and the veil of the screen;

**13** the table and its poles and all its vessels, and the bread of the Presence;

**14** and the lampstand for the light and its vessels and its lamps, and the oil for the light;

**15** and the altar of incense and its poles, and the anointing oil and the fragrant incense, and the screen for the entrance of the dwelling;

**16** the altar of burnt offering and the bronze grating that belongs to it, its poles and all its vessels; the basin and its stand;

**17** the hangings of the court, its pillars and its sockets, and the screen for the gate of the court;

**18** the pegs of the dwelling and the pegs of the court and their cords;

**19** the finely worked garments for serving in the holy place — the holy garments for Aaron the priest and the garments of his sons, for serving as priests."

## They Bring More Than Is Asked

**20** And all the assembly of the sons of Israel went out from before Moses.

**21** And everyone whose heart lifted him came, and everyone whose spirit moved him, and they brought the LORD's contribution for the work of the tent of meeting and for all its service and for the holy garments.

**22** And they came, the men along with the women. Everyone of a willing heart brought brooches and rings and signet rings and pendants, every article of gold — everyone who presented an offering of gold to the LORD.

**23** And everyone who had with him blue and purple and crimson, and fine linen and goats' hair, and rams' skins dyed red and fine leather, brought them.

**24** Everyone setting aside a contribution of silver or bronze brought the LORD's contribution, and everyone who had acacia wood with him for any of the work of the service brought it.

**25** And every woman with a wise heart spun with her own hands, and they brought what they had spun: the blue and the purple, the crimson and the fine linen.

**26** And all the women whose heart lifted them, in wisdom, spun the goats' hair.

**27** And the leaders brought the onyx stones and the stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece,

**28** and the spice and the oil for the light and for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense.

**29** Every man and woman whose heart moved them to bring for all the work that the LORD had commanded to be done through Moses — the sons of Israel brought it as a freewill offering to the LORD.

## The Two Men Who Will Build It

**30** And Moses said to the sons of Israel, "See — the LORD has called by name Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah,

**31** and has filled him with the spirit of God — with wisdom and with understanding and with knowledge and with every kind of craft —

**32** to devise designs, to work in gold and in silver and in bronze,

**33** and in the cutting of stones for setting, and in the carving of wood, to work in every kind of craft of design.

**34** And He has put it in his heart to teach — him and Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan.

**35** He has filled them with a wise heart to do all the work of a craftsman and a designer and an embroiderer in blue and in purple, in crimson and in fine linen, and of a weaver — doers of every kind of work and devisers of designs."

## Notes

- **vv1–3 the sabbath comes first, and it is the fence around the whole project** — the instructions in chapters 25–31 ended with the sabbath law (31:12–17); the building account here begins with it. The command to build is wrapped in it on both sides. And the one activity singled out, *you shall not kindle a fire*, is the one this particular work cannot proceed without — there is no casting of gold, no smelting of silver, no bronze grating without a fire. The prohibition names the craft that is about to start.
- **v3 "in any of your settlements"** — *moshevoteikhem*, from *yashav*, to sit or dwell. It is the same root as *mishkan* only by accident of subject matter, not of word: the dwelling God occupies is *mishkan*, from *shakhan*, to settle in or tent among. Two different verbs for dwelling run through these chapters, and English has one word for both.
- **vv5, 21, 24 "contribution"** — *terumah*, from *rum*, to be high, with the causative sense **to lift something up and set it apart**. It is not a tax and not a gift in the ordinary sense: the noun names the act of raising a portion out of a larger quantity and putting it beyond ordinary use. What is being described three times in this chapter is people separating their own metal from the rest of what they own.
- **v10 "everyone with a wise heart"** — *chakham lev*, and *chokhmah* is the word for wisdom throughout the Hebrew Bible — Solomon's wisdom, the wisdom of Proverbs. Here it means knowing how to cut a stone, weave a fabric, cast a socket. The language makes no distinction at all between the wisdom of a sage and the skill of a metalworker; both are *chokhmah*, and in this chapter only craftsmen have it. The same phrase is used of the women who spin in v25 and of Bezalel and Oholiab in v35.
- **v19 "the finely worked garments"** — *bigdei ha-serad*, and *serad* occurs four times in the Hebrew Bible, all four in Exodus and all four in this same phrase (31:10, here, 39:1, 39:41). Nothing else in the language constrains it. It has been read as *woven*, *plaited*, *finely worked*, and as a term for the coverings the furniture was wrapped in for transport. The word cannot be recovered; what it attaches to is clear.
- **vv21, 26 "whose heart lifted him"** — *nesa'o libbo*, the heart as the thing that picks a person up and carries him. The verb is *nasa*, the load verb: what Aaron does with the guilt of Israel in 28:38, what God does with guilt and rebellion and sin in 34:7. English says *moved*, which is a nudge. Hebrew says his heart took his weight.
- **v22 "brooches and rings and signet rings and pendants"** — four words for jewellery and two of them are guesses. *Chach* is a hook or clasp elsewhere in the Bible, including the hook put in a captive's jaw; *nezem* is a ring worn in the nose or the ear — it is the word for the ring Abraham's servant gives Rebekah; *tabba'at* is a signet ring. *Kumaz* occurs twice in the whole Bible, here and at Numbers 31:50, in both places in a list of gold ornaments, and it has never been identified.
- **v22 the gold that went the other way** — this is the second time in six chapters that Israel takes off its gold jewellery and hands it over. In 32:2–3 Aaron says *tear off the gold rings that are in the ears of your wives, your sons and your daughters* and they do it, and it becomes the calf. The same *nezem*, the same voluntary stripping, the same metal. Nothing in the text remarks on it.
- **v25 "spun with her own hands"** — the only place in the Torah where women are named as the makers of something in the sanctuary, and it is stated twice, in two different registers: the ones with a wise heart spin the dyed yarn and the linen, and the ones whose heart lifts them spin the goats' hair, which is the coarse outer covering. Both groups get the vocabulary of wisdom that Bezalel gets in v31.
- **v32 "to devise designs"** — *lachshov machashavot*, the verb with its own noun. *Chashav* is to think, calculate, reckon, plan; *machashavah* is a thought or a plan, and it is the word used of what humanity's heart produces before the flood — *every inclination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil, all day long* (Genesis 6:5). The word for the imagination that brought the flood is the word for the imagination that furnishes the sanctuary, and it is the highest praise the chapter gives.

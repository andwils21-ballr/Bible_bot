# Exodus 40

## Raise It Up

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "On the day of the first month, on the first of the month, you shall raise up the dwelling of the tent of meeting.

**3** You shall put the ark of the Testimony there, and screen the ark with the veil.

**4** You shall bring in the table and set it in order; and you shall bring in the lampstand and raise up its lamps.

**5** You shall put the gold altar for incense before the ark of the Testimony, and set up the screen of the entrance to the dwelling.

**6** You shall put the altar of burnt offering before the entrance of the dwelling of the tent of meeting.

**7** You shall put the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water there.

**8** You shall set up the court all around, and put up the screen of the gate of the court.

**9** You shall take the anointing oil and anoint the dwelling and everything in it, and set it apart with all its vessels — and it shall be holy.

**10** You shall anoint the altar of burnt offering and all its vessels, and set the altar apart — and the altar shall be most holy.

**11** You shall anoint the basin and its stand, and set it apart.

**12** You shall bring Aaron and his sons near to the entrance of the tent of meeting, and wash them with water.

**13** You shall clothe Aaron with the holy garments, and anoint him and set him apart, and he shall serve Me as priest.

**14** His sons you shall bring near, and clothe them with tunics.

**15** You shall anoint them as you anointed their father, and they shall serve Me as priests. Their anointing shall be for them a priesthood forever, through their generations."

**16** Moses did according to all that the LORD commanded him; so he did.

## And He Did It

**17** And it came to pass in the first month, in the second year, on the first of the month, that the dwelling was raised up.

**18** Moses raised up the dwelling: he laid its sockets and set up its frames and put in its bars and raised up its pillars.

**19** He spread the tent over the dwelling and put the covering of the tent over it, above, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**20** He took the Testimony and put it into the ark, and set the poles on the ark, and put the cover on the ark, above.

**21** He brought the ark into the dwelling and set up the veil of the screen and screened the ark of the Testimony, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**22** He put the table in the tent of meeting, on the north flank of the dwelling, outside the veil.

**23** He set the bread in order on it before the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**24** He put the lampstand in the tent of meeting, opposite the table, on the south flank of the dwelling.

**25** He raised up the lamps before the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**26** He put the gold altar in the tent of meeting, before the veil,

**27** and burned fragrant incense on it, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**28** He set up the screen of the entrance to the dwelling.

**29** The altar of burnt offering he set at the entrance of the dwelling of the tent of meeting, and offered up on it the burnt offering and the grain offering, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**30** He put the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water there for washing.

**31** Moses and Aaron and his sons washed their hands and their feet from it.

**32** When they came into the tent of meeting and when they came near the altar, they would wash — as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**33** He raised up the court all around the dwelling and the altar, and put up the screen of the gate of the court. Moses finished the work.

## The Cloud Settles

**34** The cloud covered the tent of meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the dwelling.

**35** Moses could not come into the tent of meeting, because the cloud had settled on it and the glory of the LORD filled the dwelling.

**36** Whenever the cloud was lifted from over the dwelling, the sons of Israel would set out, on all their journeys.

**37** If the cloud was not lifted, they would not set out, until the day it was lifted.

**38** For the cloud of the LORD was over the dwelling by day, and fire was in it by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel, on all their journeys.

## Notes

- **v35 "because the cloud had settled on it"** — *ki shakhan alav he-anan*, and *shakhan* is the verb the whole book has been building a noun out of. *Mishkan*, the **dwelling**, is a place of *shakhan*, of settling in. For sixteen chapters the word has named a thing made of boards and goat hair, and here, in the last five verses of Exodus, it finally does what its name says. And what settles is not described. Not a figure, not a voice — a cloud, and a weight called *kavod*, the glory, from the root meaning **heavy**.
- **v35 and Moses is the one left outside** — at 24:18 *va-yavo Mosheh be-tokh he-anan*, **Moses came into the middle of the cloud**, and went up the mountain, and stayed forty days. Here the same cloud is twenty feet away at ground level and he cannot get in. The book that began with a man turning aside to look at a fire ends with the same man standing outside a tent he built himself, and the text offers no comment on it at all.
- **v33 "Moses finished the work"** — *va-yekhal Mosheh et ha-melakhah*, and the clause is Genesis 2:2 with a man's name in it: *va-yekhal Elohim… melakhto asher asah*, **and God finished the work that he had made**. Same verb, same noun, same grammar. Chapter 39 already ran the other two verbs of the creation account — God *saw*, God *blessed*; Moses *saw*, Moses *blessed*. This is the third.
- **vv4, 23 "set it in order"** — *ve-arakhta et erko*, literally **and you shall arrange its arrangement**, and again at v23, *va-ya'arokh alav erekh lechem*, he arranged on it an arrangement of bread. Hebrew doubles a verb with its own noun to press it; English only stutters. The noun is *ma'arakhah*, the word for a battle line drawn up — bread laid out in rank.
- **vv22, 24 "the north flank of the dwelling"** — *yerekh ha-mishkan*, the **thigh** of the dwelling. The same noun is the hip the wrestler puts out at 32:25, and the part of the body an oath about descendants is sworn on at Genesis 24:2. Hebrew gives buildings bodies without marking it as a figure of speech: the tent has a thigh, and the table stands against it.
- **v10 "most holy"** — *qodesh qadashim*, **holiness of holinesses**, the construction Hebrew uses where English uses a superlative — the same shape as *shir ha-shirim*, song of songs, and *melekh melakhim*, king of kings. It is also the name of the inner room. What is being said of the altar out in the open courtyard is the grade of holiness the inmost chamber has.
- **vv2, 17 the date, and what it is two weeks short of** — the first day of the first month of the second year. The first month is the one named at 12:2, *this month shall be for you the head of months*, and the Passover lamb was killed on its fourteenth day (12:6). So the tent goes up a fortnight before the anniversary of the night they left. The book does not say this. It just gives the date twice, once as the command and once as the fact.
- **vv36–38 Exodus ends without arriving** — the last three verses are not an event but a standing arrangement: cloud by day, fire by night, and a people who move when it moves and stay when it stays. The pair goes back to 13:21, the pillar of cloud and the pillar of fire that led them out of Egypt; it is the same escort, now parked on top of a tent they carry with them. The book closes with Israel still in the wilderness, camped, waiting on a cloud — and the last two words are *be-khol mas'eihem*, **on all their journeys**, plural, none of them yet made.

# 2 Kings 21

## Manasseh King of Judah

**1** Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Hephzibah.

**2** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, according to the detestable practices of the nations whom the LORD had driven out before the sons of Israel.

**3** He rebuilt the high places that Hezekiah his father had destroyed. He set up altars for Baal and made an Asherah, as Ahab king of Israel had done, and he bowed down to all the host of heaven and served them.

**4** He built altars in the house of the LORD, of which the LORD had said, "In Jerusalem I will put My name."

**5** He built altars for all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of the LORD.

**6** He made his son pass through the fire, practiced soothsaying and read omens, and appointed those who deal with ghosts and spirits. He did much evil in the eyes of the LORD, provoking Him to anger.

**7** He set the carved image of Asherah that he had made in the house of which the LORD had said to David and to Solomon his son, "In this house, and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, I will put My name forever.

**8** I will not again make the feet of Israel wander from the land that I gave to their fathers, if only they will be careful to do according to all that I have commanded them, and according to all the instruction that My servant Moses commanded them."

**9** But they did not listen, and Manasseh led them astray to do more evil than the nations whom the LORD had destroyed before the sons of Israel.

## The Word against Jerusalem

**10** The LORD spoke by His servants the prophets, saying,

**11** "Because Manasseh king of Judah has done these detestable things, doing more evil than all that the Amorites did who were before him, and has made Judah also to sin with his idols,

**12** therefore thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: I am about to bring such evil on Jerusalem and Judah that both ears of everyone who hears of it will tingle.

**13** I will stretch over Jerusalem the measuring line of Samaria and the plumb line of the house of Ahab, and I will wipe Jerusalem as one wipes a dish, wiping it and turning it upside down.

**14** I will cast off the remnant of My inheritance and give them into the hand of their enemies, and they shall become plunder and spoil to all their enemies,

**15** because they have done what is evil in My eyes and have provoked Me to anger since the day their fathers came out of Egypt, even to this day."

**16** Moreover, Manasseh shed very much innocent blood, until he had filled Jerusalem from one end to the other, besides his sin with which he made Judah to sin, doing what was evil in the eyes of the LORD.

**17** The rest of the acts of Manasseh, all that he did and the sin that he committed, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**18** Manasseh lay down with his fathers and was buried in the garden of his house, in the garden of Uzza, and Amon his son reigned in his place.

## Amon King of Judah

**19** Amon was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Meshullemeth daughter of Haruz of Jotbah.

**20** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, as Manasseh his father had done.

**21** He walked in all the way in which his father walked, served the idols that his father served and bowed down to them.

**22** He forsook the LORD, the God of his fathers, and did not walk in the way of the LORD.

**23** The servants of Amon conspired against him and put the king to death in his house.

**24** But the people of the land struck down all who had conspired against King Amon, and the people of the land made Josiah his son king in his place.

**25** The rest of the acts of Amon that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**26** He was buried in his tomb in the garden of Uzza, and Josiah his son reigned in his place.

## Notes

- **v1 "Hephzibah"** — *Cheftsi-vah*, **my delight is in her**; Isaiah gives Jerusalem the name: *you shall be called Hephzibah… for the LORD delights in you* (Isaiah 62:4).
- **v3 "as Ahab king of Israel had done"** — the king of Judah is measured against the worst king of Israel, and in v13 Jerusalem against Samaria and the house of Ahab.
- **v6 "practiced soothsaying and read omens… ghosts and spirits"** — the same list the law forbids: *one who makes his son or his daughter pass through the fire, a soothsayer, one who reads omens… one who asks a ghost or a spirit* (Deuteronomy 18:10–11). Manasseh does five of its nine.
- **vv7–8 "I will put My name forever… if only they will be careful to do"** — the LORD's answer to Solomon's prayer (1 Kings 9:3–7), with its condition. The carved image stands in the house that bears the Name.
- **v11 "more evil than all that the Amorites did"** — the people whose land Israel was given once *the guilt of the Amorite* was complete (Genesis 15:16).
- **v12 "both ears of everyone who hears of it will tingle"** — the word the LORD spoke to the boy Samuel against the house of Eli (1 Samuel 3:11), and Jeremiah's word against this city (Jeremiah 19:3).
- **v13 "the measuring line of Samaria and the plumb line of the house of Ahab… as one wipes a dish"** — the builder's tools used for tearing down: Jerusalem will be measured by the standard Samaria was judged by. *Machah*, **to wipe**, is the verb of the flood: *I will wipe away the human whom I created* (Genesis 6:7).
- **v16 "from one end to the other"** — *peh la-feh*, **mouth to mouth**, as the house of Baal was filled (10:21).
- **vv1–18 and Chronicles** — Chronicles adds that the Assyrian commanders took Manasseh *with hooks*, bound him in bronze and carried him to Babylon, where he *humbled himself greatly* and prayed, and the LORD brought him back to Jerusalem (2 Chronicles 33:11–13). Kings says nothing of it, and Jeremiah names Manasseh as the cause of the coming judgment (Jeremiah 15:4).
- **v24 "the people of the land"** — *am ha-arets*, who crowned Joash (11:14, 18–20) and now put down the conspirators and make the eight-year-old Josiah king.

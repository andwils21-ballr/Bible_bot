# 2 Kings 3

## Jehoram King of Israel

**1** Jehoram son of Ahab began to reign over Israel in Samaria in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned twelve years.

**2** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, though not like his father and his mother, for he removed the pillar of Baal that his father had made.

**3** Nevertheless he clung to the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, with which he made Israel to sin; he did not turn away from them.

## Three Kings against Moab

**4** Now Mesha king of Moab was a sheep breeder, and he used to pay the king of Israel a hundred thousand lambs and the wool of a hundred thousand rams.

**5** And it came to pass, when Ahab died, that the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.

**6** So King Jehoram went out of Samaria at that time and mustered all Israel.

**7** He went and sent word to Jehoshaphat king of Judah: "The king of Moab has rebelled against me. Will you go with me to battle against Moab?" He said, "I will go up. I am as you are, my people as your people, my horses as your horses."

**8** He said, "Which way shall we go up?" He answered, "By the way of the wilderness of Edom."

**9** So the king of Israel went with the king of Judah and the king of Edom. When they had made a circuit of seven days' journey, there was no water for the army or for the animals that followed them.

**10** Then the king of Israel said, "Alas! The LORD has called these three kings to give them into the hand of Moab."

**11** But Jehoshaphat said, "Is there no prophet of the LORD here, through whom we may inquire of the LORD?" One of the servants of the king of Israel answered, "Elisha son of Shaphat is here, who poured water on the hands of Elijah."

**12** Jehoshaphat said, "The word of the LORD is with him." So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat and the king of Edom went down to him.

## Elisha's Word

**13** Elisha said to the king of Israel, "What have I to do with you? Go to the prophets of your father and the prophets of your mother." But the king of Israel said to him, "No, for the LORD has called these three kings to give them into the hand of Moab."

**14** Elisha said, "As the LORD of hosts lives, before whom I stand, were it not that I have regard for Jehoshaphat king of Judah, I would neither look at you nor see you.

**15** But now bring me a musician." When the musician played, the hand of the LORD came on him.

**16** He said, "Thus says the LORD: 'Make this valley full of pits.'

**17** For thus says the LORD: 'You shall not see wind or see rain, yet that valley shall be filled with water, so that you shall drink, you and your cattle and your animals.'

**18** This is a light thing in the eyes of the LORD; He will also give Moab into your hand.

**19** You shall strike every fortified city and every choice city, and fell every good tree, and stop up all springs of water, and ruin every good piece of land with stones."

**20** And it came to pass in the morning, about the time of offering the grain offering, that water came from the direction of Edom, and the country was filled with water.

## The Water Red as Blood

**21** When all the Moabites heard that the kings had come up to fight against them, all who were able to put on armor, from the youngest up, were called out, and they stood at the border.

**22** They rose early in the morning, and the sun shone on the water, and the Moabites saw the water opposite them as red as blood.

**23** They said, "This is blood! The kings have surely fought together and struck one another down. Now then, Moab, to the spoil!"

**24** But when they came to the camp of Israel, the Israelites rose up and struck the Moabites, so that they fled before them. They went forward, striking the Moabites as they went.

**25** They overthrew the cities, and on every good piece of land each man threw a stone until they filled it. They stopped up every spring of water and felled every good tree, until only the stones of Kir-hareseth were left, and the slingers surrounded it and struck it.

**26** When the king of Moab saw that the battle was going against him, he took with him seven hundred men who drew the sword, to break through to the king of Edom, but they could not.

**27** Then he took his firstborn son, who was to reign in his place, and offered him as a burnt offering on the wall. Then there came great wrath against Israel, and they withdrew from him and returned to their own land.

## Notes

- **v2 "he removed the pillar of Baal that his father had made"** — *matstsevet ha-Ba'al*. Later Jehu will break *the pillar of Baal* again and tear down its house (10:27).
- **v4 "Mesha king of Moab was a sheep breeder"** — *noqed*, the word Amos uses of himself, *who was among the sheep breeders of Tekoa* (Amos 1:1). The tribute is counted in lambs and wool.
- **v7 "I am as you are, my people as your people, my horses as your horses"** — the words Jehoshaphat said to Ahab before Ramoth-gilead (1 Kings 22:4).
- **v11 "who poured water on the hands of Elijah"** — the servant's task. Elisha is known by his service to his master.
- **v13 "Go to the prophets of your father and the prophets of your mother"** — the prophets of Baal and Asherah who ate at Jezebel's table (1 Kings 18:19). *Mah li va-lakh*, as in 1 Kings 17:18.
- **v15 "bring me a musician"** — *menaggen*. As the musician plays, *the hand of the LORD came on him*, the phrase used of Elijah before he ran to Jezreel (1 Kings 18:46).
- **vv19, 25 "fell every good tree… felled every good tree"** — the law of siege says, *you shall not destroy its trees by swinging an axe against them… you shall not cut them down* (Deuteronomy 20:19). Here the prophet's word commands it against Moab, and it is done.
- **vv20–22 "water came from the direction of Edom… the water… as red as blood"** — *adummim ka-dam*, **red as blood**; *adom*, **red**, and *Edom* are the same letters. The water comes *mi-derekh Edom*, and the Moabites see it *adom*.
- **v25 "until only the stones of Kir-hareseth were left"** — the capital of Moab, the last city standing.
- **v27 "offered him as a burnt offering on the wall. Then there came great wrath against Israel"** — *qetsef gadol*. Whose wrath is not said: God's, Moab's, or the other kings'. The Greek has *great remorse*. The text keeps the Hebrew.

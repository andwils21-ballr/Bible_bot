# 2 Kings 2

## From Gilgal to the Jordan

**1** And it came to pass, when the LORD was about to take Elijah up to heaven in a whirlwind, that Elijah and Elisha were going from Gilgal.

**2** Elijah said to Elisha, "Please stay here, for the LORD has sent me as far as Bethel." But Elisha said, "As the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So they went down to Bethel.

**3** The sons of the prophets who were at Bethel came out to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today the LORD will take your master from over your head?" He said, "Yes, I know it; keep silent."

**4** Elijah said to him, "Elisha, please stay here, for the LORD has sent me to Jericho." But he said, "As the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So they came to Jericho.

**5** The sons of the prophets who were at Jericho came near to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today the LORD will take your master from over your head?" He answered, "Yes, I know it; keep silent."

**6** Then Elijah said to him, "Please stay here, for the LORD has sent me to the Jordan." But he said, "As the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So the two of them went on.

**7** Fifty men of the sons of the prophets went and stood at a distance facing them, while the two of them stood by the Jordan.

**8** Then Elijah took his cloak, rolled it up and struck the water, and the water was divided to one side and to the other, so that the two of them crossed over on dry ground.

## The Chariot of Fire

**9** When they had crossed, Elijah said to Elisha, "Ask what I shall do for you before I am taken from you." Elisha said, "Please let a double portion of your spirit be on me."

**10** He said, "You have asked a hard thing. If you see me as I am being taken from you, it shall be so for you; but if not, it shall not be so."

**11** As they were still going on and talking, a chariot of fire and horses of fire separated the two of them, and Elijah went up in a whirlwind to heaven.

**12** Elisha saw it and cried out, "My father, my father! The chariots of Israel and its horsemen!" He saw him no more. Then he took hold of his own clothes and tore them in two pieces.

**13** He took up the cloak of Elijah that had fallen from him, and went back and stood on the bank of the Jordan.

**14** He took the cloak of Elijah that had fallen from him and struck the water and said, "Where is the LORD, the God of Elijah?" When he too had struck the water, it was divided to one side and to the other, and Elisha crossed over.

## Elisha at Jericho

**15** When the sons of the prophets who were at Jericho saw him from a distance, they said, "The spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha." They came to meet him and bowed down to the ground before him.

**16** They said to him, "Your servants have with them fifty strong men. Please let them go and look for your master. It may be that the Spirit of the LORD has taken him up and cast him on some mountain or into some valley." He said, "You shall not send them."

**17** But when they urged him until he was ashamed, he said, "Send them." So they sent fifty men, and they searched for three days but did not find him.

**18** They came back to him while he was staying at Jericho, and he said to them, "Did I not say to you, 'Do not go'?"

**19** The men of the city said to Elisha, "The situation of this city is pleasant, as my lord sees, but the water is bad and the land causes miscarriage."

**20** He said, "Bring me a new bowl, and put salt in it." So they brought it to him.

**21** Then he went to the spring of water and threw salt in it and said, "Thus says the LORD: 'I have healed this water; from now on neither death nor miscarriage shall come from it.'"

**22** So the water has been healed to this day, according to the word that Elisha spoke.

## The Boys of Bethel

**23** He went up from there to Bethel. As he was going up on the way, some small boys came out of the city and mocked him, saying to him, "Go up, baldhead! Go up, baldhead!"

**24** He turned around, looked at them and cursed them in the name of the LORD. Then two she-bears came out of the woods and tore forty-two of the boys.

**25** From there he went to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria.

## Notes

- **vv2, 4, 6 "As the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you"** — three times Elijah tells Elisha to stay, and three times Elisha swears the same oath. At Bethel and Jericho the sons of the prophets ask him the same question, and he gives the same answer.
- **v3 "the LORD will take your master from over your head"** — *me-al roshekha*, **from over your head**, from his place above you.
- **vv8, 14 "struck the water… crossed over on dry ground"** — *ba-charavah*, the word of the crossing of the sea, *the sons of Israel came into the middle of the sea on dry ground* (Exodus 14:22), and of the Jordan under Joshua (Joshua 3:17). Elijah crosses east as Joshua crossed west; Elisha comes back across as Joshua did.
- **v9 "a double portion of your spirit"** — *pi shenayim*, the firstborn's share of an inheritance, *a double portion of all that he has* (Deuteronomy 21:17). Elisha asks to be Elijah's heir, not to be twice Elijah.
- **v11 "a chariot of fire and horses of fire… Elijah went up in a whirlwind to heaven"** — *bi-se'arah*, **in a storm wind**. The text says the chariot came between them; it was the whirlwind that carried Elijah up.
- **v12 "My father, my father! The chariots of Israel and its horsemen!"** — the same cry King Joash will raise at Elisha's deathbed (13:14).
- **vv15–16 "The spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha… the Spirit of the LORD has taken him up"** — Elijah's own spirit, lowercase; *ruach YHWH*, capital, as fixed. The sons of the prophets feared the Spirit might carry Elijah off, as Obadiah had (1 Kings 18:12).
- **vv19–22 "the land causes miscarriage… neither death nor miscarriage"** — *meshakkelet*, from *shakhal*, **to be bereaved of children**. Salt in a *new* bowl, and the word *I have healed*.
- **v23 "some small boys… Go up, baldhead!"** — *ne'arim qetannim*; in v24 they are *yeladim*, **children** or **youths**. *Go up*, *aleh*, is the verb of Elijah's going *up* (vv1, 11).
- **v24 "two she-bears… tore forty-two of the boys"** — *va-tevaqqa'nah*, **tore open**. The text does not say that the boys died.

# 2 Kings 9

## The Young Prophet at Ramoth-gilead

**1** Elisha the prophet called one of the sons of the prophets and said to him, "Gird up your loins, take this flask of oil in your hand and go to Ramoth-gilead.

**2** When you arrive there, look for Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi. Go in and have him get up from among his brothers, and take him into an inner room.

**3** Then take the flask of oil and pour it on his head and say, 'Thus says the LORD: I anoint you king over Israel.' Then open the door and flee; do not wait."

**4** So the young man, the young prophet, went to Ramoth-gilead.

**5** When he came, the commanders of the army were sitting there. He said, "I have a word for you, commander." Jehu said, "For which of us all?" He said, "For you, commander."

**6** So he got up and went into the house, and the young man poured the oil on his head and said to him, "Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: 'I anoint you king over the people of the LORD, over Israel.

**7** You shall strike down the house of Ahab your master, so that I may avenge the blood of My servants the prophets, and the blood of all the servants of the LORD, at the hand of Jezebel.

**8** The whole house of Ahab shall perish, and I will cut off from Ahab every male, bond or free in Israel.

**9** I will make the house of Ahab like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah.

**10** The dogs shall eat Jezebel in the plot of ground of Jezreel, and none shall bury her.'" Then he opened the door and fled.

**11** When Jehu came out to the servants of his master, one said to him, "Is all well? Why did this madman come to you?" He said to them, "You know the man and his talk."

**12** They said, "That is not true. Tell us now." He said, "Thus and thus he spoke to me, saying, 'Thus says the LORD: I anoint you king over Israel.'"

**13** Then in haste every man took his garment and put it under him on the bare steps, and they blew the shofar and said, "Jehu is king!"

## Jehu Rides to Jezreel

**14** So Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi, conspired against Joram. (Joram had been keeping guard at Ramoth-gilead, he and all Israel, against Hazael king of Aram,

**15** but King Joram had returned to be healed in Jezreel of the wounds that the Arameans had given him when he fought with Hazael king of Aram.) Jehu said, "If this is your mind, let no one slip out of the city to go and tell it in Jezreel."

**16** Then Jehu mounted his chariot and went to Jezreel, for Joram was lying there. Ahaziah king of Judah had come down to see Joram.

**17** The watchman was standing on the tower in Jezreel, and he saw the company of Jehu as he came, and said, "I see a company." Joram said, "Take a horseman and send him to meet them, and let him say, 'Is it peace?'"

**18** So a man on horseback went to meet him and said, "Thus says the king: 'Is it peace?'" Jehu said, "What have you to do with peace? Turn around and follow me." The watchman reported, "The messenger reached them, but he is not coming back."

**19** Then he sent out a second horseman, who came to them and said, "Thus says the king: 'Is it peace?'" Jehu answered, "What have you to do with peace? Turn around and follow me."

**20** Again the watchman reported, "He reached them, but he is not coming back. The driving is like the driving of Jehu son of Nimshi, for he drives like a madman."

## The Death of Joram and Ahaziah

**21** Joram said, "Harness the chariot." When his chariot was harnessed, Joram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah set out, each in his chariot, and went out to meet Jehu, and they met him in the plot of ground of Naboth the Jezreelite.

**22** When Joram saw Jehu, he said, "Is it peace, Jehu?" He answered, "What peace, so long as the whoring of your mother Jezebel and her many sorceries go on?"

**23** Then Joram turned his hands and fled, and said to Ahaziah, "Treachery, Ahaziah!"

**24** Jehu drew his bow with his full strength and shot Joram between the shoulders, so that the arrow went out through his heart, and he sank down in his chariot.

**25** Jehu said to Bidkar his officer, "Pick him up and throw him into the plot of ground of Naboth the Jezreelite. For remember, when you and I rode side by side behind Ahab his father, the LORD laid this burden on him:

**26** 'As surely as I saw yesterday the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons, declares the LORD, I will repay you in this plot of ground, declares the LORD.' So now pick him up and throw him into the plot of ground, according to the word of the LORD."

**27** When Ahaziah king of Judah saw this, he fled by the way of Beth-haggan. Jehu pursued him and said, "Strike him too!" They struck him in the chariot at the ascent of Gur, which is by Ibleam. He fled to Megiddo and died there.

**28** His servants carried him in a chariot to Jerusalem and buried him in his tomb with his fathers in the City of David.

**29** In the eleventh year of Joram son of Ahab, Ahaziah began to reign over Judah.

## The Death of Jezebel

**30** When Jehu came to Jezreel, Jezebel heard of it. She painted her eyes and adorned her head and looked down from the window.

**31** As Jehu came in at the gate, she said, "Is it peace, Zimri, murderer of your master?"

**32** He lifted up his face to the window and said, "Who is on my side? Who?" Two or three eunuchs looked down at him.

**33** He said, "Throw her down." So they threw her down, and some of her blood spattered on the wall and on the horses, and they trampled her.

**34** Then he went in and ate and drank. Then he said, "See now to this cursed woman and bury her, for she is a king's daughter."

**35** But when they went to bury her, they found no more of her than the skull, the feet and the palms of her hands.

**36** They came back and told him. He said, "This is the word of the LORD, which He spoke by His servant Elijah the Tishbite: 'In the plot of ground of Jezreel the dogs shall eat the flesh of Jezebel,

**37** and the corpse of Jezebel shall be like dung on the face of the field in the plot of ground of Jezreel, so that no one can say, This is Jezebel.'"

## Notes

- **vv1, 3 "this flask of oil… pour it on his head"** — *pakh ha-shemen*, the flask Samuel poured on Saul's head (1 Samuel 10:1). Elijah was told to anoint Jehu (1 Kings 19:16); a young prophet sent by Elisha does it.
- **vv7–10 "every male, bond or free… like the house of Jeroboam… like the house of Baasha"** — the sentence Elijah passed on Ahab (1 Kings 21:21–24), now handed to the man who will carry it out.
- **vv11, 20 "this madman… he drives like a madman"** — *ha-meshugga* and *be-shigga'on*, the same root. Hosea says of Israel's scorn, *the prophet is a fool, the man of the spirit is mad* (Hosea 9:7).
- **v13 "every man took his garment and put it under him"** — the soldiers spread their garments for their new king; when Jesus entered Jerusalem, *most of the crowd spread their cloaks on the road* (Matthew 21:8).
- **vv17–22 "Is it peace?… What have you to do with peace?… What peace"** — *shalom*, eight times in vv17–31: asked by the king's riders, by Joram and by Jezebel (v31). Jehu answers that there is no peace while Jezebel's ways go on.
- **v22 "the whoring of your mother Jezebel and her many sorceries"** — *zenunei*, from *zanah*, **to whore**, the prophets' word for worship of other gods; *keshafeha*, **her sorceries**.
- **vv25–26 "the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons"** — Jehu remembers the word spoken over Ahab in Naboth's field. The account in 1 Kings 21 does not mention Naboth's sons. Joram's body is thrown into the same plot of ground.
- **vv27–29 "He fled to Megiddo and died there… In the eleventh year of Joram"** — Chronicles has Ahaziah found hiding in Samaria and brought to Jehu (2 Chronicles 22:9). 8:25 dates Ahaziah's accession to *the twelfth year* of Joram; here it is *the eleventh*. The text keeps both.
- **v31 "Is it peace, Zimri, murderer of your master?"** — Zimri killed King Elah and reigned seven days before he died in the burning palace (1 Kings 16:9–18). Jezebel greets Jehu with the name of a usurper who did not last.
- **vv36–37 "like dung on the face of the field… so that no one can say, This is Jezebel"** — *domen al penei ha-sadeh*, the phrase Jeremiah uses of the unburied dead, *they shall be dung on the face of the ground* (Jeremiah 8:2). Elijah said *by the rampart of Jezreel* (1 Kings 21:23); Jehu quotes it as *in the plot of ground*.

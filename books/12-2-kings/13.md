# 2 Kings 13

## Jehoahaz King of Israel

**1** In the twenty-third year of Joash son of Ahaziah, king of Judah, Jehoahaz son of Jehu began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned seventeen years.

**2** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD and followed the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, with which he made Israel to sin; he did not turn from them.

**3** The anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He gave them into the hand of Hazael king of Aram and into the hand of Ben-hadad son of Hazael, all the days.

**4** Then Jehoahaz entreated the LORD, and the LORD listened to him, for He saw the oppression of Israel, how the king of Aram oppressed them.

**5** The LORD gave Israel a deliverer, and they came out from under the hand of Aram, and the sons of Israel lived in their tents as before.

**6** Yet they did not turn from the sins of the house of Jeroboam, with which he made Israel to sin; they walked in them. The Asherah also remained standing in Samaria.

**7** For the king of Aram had left Jehoahaz no army but fifty horsemen, ten chariots and ten thousand foot soldiers, because he had destroyed them and made them like the dust at threshing.

**8** The rest of the acts of Jehoahaz, all that he did and his might, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**9** Jehoahaz lay down with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria, and Joash his son reigned in his place.

## Jehoash King of Israel

**10** In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Jehoash son of Jehoahaz began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned sixteen years.

**11** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD. He did not turn from all the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, with which he made Israel to sin; he walked in them.

**12** The rest of the acts of Joash, all that he did and his might with which he fought against Amaziah king of Judah, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**13** Joash lay down with his fathers, and Jeroboam sat on his throne. Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.

## The Arrow of Victory

**14** Now Elisha had fallen sick with the sickness of which he would die, and Joash king of Israel came down to him and wept over his face and said, "My father, my father! The chariots of Israel and its horsemen!"

**15** Elisha said to him, "Take a bow and arrows." So he took a bow and arrows.

**16** He said to the king of Israel, "Put your hand on the bow." So he put his hand on it, and Elisha laid his hands on the king's hands.

**17** He said, "Open the window eastward," and he opened it. Elisha said, "Shoot!" and he shot. He said, "The LORD's arrow of victory, the arrow of victory over Aram! You shall strike the Arameans at Aphek until you have made an end of them."

**18** He said, "Take the arrows," and he took them. He said to the king of Israel, "Strike the ground," and he struck three times and stopped.

**19** The man of God was angry with him and said, "You should have struck five or six times; then you would have struck the Arameans until you had made an end of them. But now you will strike the Arameans only three times."

## The Bones of Elisha

**20** Elisha died, and they buried him. Bands of Moabites would come into the land as the year came in.

**21** And it came to pass, as they were burying a man, that they saw a band, and they threw the man into the grave of Elisha. The man went down and touched the bones of Elisha, and he came to life and stood up on his feet.

**22** Hazael king of Aram oppressed Israel all the days of Jehoahaz.

**23** But the LORD was gracious to them and had compassion on them, and He turned toward them because of His covenant with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. He was not willing to destroy them, and He has not cast them from His presence until now.

**24** Hazael king of Aram died, and Ben-hadad his son reigned in his place.

**25** Then Jehoash son of Jehoahaz took back from the hand of Ben-hadad son of Hazael the cities that Ben-hadad had taken in war from the hand of Jehoahaz his father. Three times Joash struck him down, and he recovered the cities of Israel.

## Notes

- **v4 "Jehoahaz entreated the LORD"** — *va-yechal et penei YHWH*, **he softened the face of the LORD**, the words of Moses pleading for the people after the golden calf (Exodus 32:11). A king who keeps Jeroboam's calves (v2) uses Moses' words, and the LORD listens.
- **v5 "The LORD gave Israel a deliverer"** — *moshia*, the word of the judges: *the LORD raised up a deliverer* (Judges 3:9). The deliverer is not named; the Greek has *salvation*. The next chapter says the LORD *saved them by the hand of Jeroboam son of Joash* (14:27).
- **v5 "as before"** — *ki-tmol shilshom*, **as yesterday and the day before**, as in Pharaoh's order about the straw (Exodus 5:7).
- **v14 "My father, my father! The chariots of Israel and its horsemen!"** — Elisha's own cry when Elijah was taken up (2:12), now spoken over Elisha, by a king.
- **v17 "The LORD's arrow of victory"** — *chets teshu'ah la-YHWH*. *Teshu'ah* is **salvation**, **victory**, the noun of *moshia* in v5. *Aphek* is where Ahab defeated an earlier Ben-hadad (1 Kings 20:26–30). The Greek lacks *Elisha said, Shoot! and he shot.*
- **vv18–19, 25 "he struck three times and stopped… Three times Joash struck him down"** — the prophet's word kept to the number the king struck.
- **v20 "as the year came in"** — *ba shanah*, **a year came**, with no article; the Greek has *as the year came*. When in the year is meant is not said.
- **v21 "touched the bones of Elisha, and he came to life"** — Sirach praises Elisha for it: *in his sleep his body prophesied… in his death his works were marvelous* (Sirach 48:13–14).
- **v23 "He has not cast them from His presence until now"** — *ad attah*, **until now**, which the Greek lacks. The book records when it happened: *until He had cast them out from His presence* (17:20).

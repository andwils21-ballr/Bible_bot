# 2 Kings 12

## Jehoash King of Judah

**1** In the seventh year of Jehu, Jehoash began to reign, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Zibiah of Beersheba.

**2** Jehoash did what was right in the eyes of the LORD all his days, in which Jehoiada the priest instructed him.

**3** Only the high places were not taken away; the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.

## The Breaches of the House

**4** Jehoash said to the priests, "All the money of the holy gifts that is brought into the house of the LORD — the money of each man who passes the count, the money of each one's valuation of persons, and all the money that it comes into anyone's heart to bring into the house of the LORD —

**5** let the priests take it for themselves, each from the people he knows, and let them repair the breaches of the house, wherever any breach is found."

**6** And it came to pass in the twenty-third year of King Jehoash that the priests had not repaired the breaches of the house.

**7** Then King Jehoash called Jehoiada the priest and the other priests and said to them, "Why are you not repairing the breaches of the house? So now take no more money from the people you know, but hand it over for the breaches of the house."

**8** The priests agreed that they would take no more money from the people, and that they would not repair the breaches of the house.

**9** Then Jehoiada the priest took a chest, bored a hole in its lid, and set it beside the altar, on the right side as one comes into the house of the LORD. The priests who guarded the threshold put in it all the money that was brought into the house of the LORD.

**10** Whenever they saw that there was much money in the chest, the king's secretary and the high priest came up, and they tied up and counted the money that was found in the house of the LORD.

**11** They gave the money that had been weighed out into the hands of those who did the work, who were appointed over the house of the LORD, and they paid it out to the carpenters and the builders who worked on the house of the LORD,

**12** to the masons and the stonecutters, and to buy timber and quarried stone to repair the breaches of the house of the LORD, and for everything that was spent on the house to repair it.

**13** But no basins of silver, snuffers, bowls, trumpets, or any vessels of gold or of silver were made for the house of the LORD from the money that was brought into the house of the LORD,

**14** for they gave it to those who did the work, and with it they repaired the house of the LORD.

**15** They did not ask an accounting from the men into whose hands they gave the money to pay those who did the work, for they dealt faithfully.

**16** The money from the guilt offerings and the money from the sin offerings was not brought into the house of the LORD; it belonged to the priests.

## Hazael and the Death of Jehoash

**17** Then Hazael king of Aram went up and fought against Gath and took it, and Hazael set his face to go up against Jerusalem.

**18** Jehoash king of Judah took all the holy things that Jehoshaphat, Jehoram and Ahaziah, his fathers, kings of Judah, had consecrated, and his own holy things, and all the gold that was found in the treasuries of the house of the LORD and of the king's house, and sent them to Hazael king of Aram. So Hazael went away from Jerusalem.

**19** The rest of the acts of Joash and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**20** His servants arose and made a conspiracy, and struck down Joash at Beth-millo, which goes down to Silla.

**21** Jozabad son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shomer, his servants, struck him, and he died. They buried him with his fathers in the City of David, and Amaziah his son reigned in his place.

## Notes

- **vv1–21 in the Hebrew** — the Hebrew numbers these verses 2–22.
- **v2 "all his days, in which Jehoiada the priest instructed him"** — *kol yamav asher horahu*. *Asher* can mean **in which** or **because**: either all his life, because Jehoiada taught him, or only the days that Jehoiada taught him. The Greek has *all the days that Jehoiada enlightened him*. Chronicles says *all the days of Jehoiada the priest* (2 Chronicles 24:2), and tells how, after Jehoiada died, Joash had Jehoiada's son Zechariah stoned *in the court of the house of the LORD* (24:17–22).
- **v4 "the money of each man who passes the count"** — *kesef over ish*. *Over* is the word of the half-shekel: *everyone who passes over to those counted* (Exodus 30:13), and Chronicles calls this money *the tax of Moses the servant of the LORD* (2 Chronicles 24:6, 9). Abraham's silver was *over la-socher*, **current with the merchant** (Genesis 23:16), so the phrase can also mean simply **current money**. *Each one's valuation of persons* is the vow money of Leviticus 27.
- **vv5, 7 "each from the people he knows"** — *makkaro*, *makkareikhem*, found only in these two verses. Read from *nakar*, **to know**, it is a priest's own acquaintance; the Greek reads it from *makar*, **to sell**, *from his sale*. Uncertain.
- **vv5–12 "repair the breaches of the house"** — *chazzeq bedeq*, **make strong the breach**. Ezekiel uses the same words for the elders of Gebal who caulked the seams of the ship of Tyre (Ezekiel 27:9, 27).
- **v9 "set it beside the altar, on the right side"** — Chronicles sets the chest *at the gate of the house of the LORD, outside* (2 Chronicles 24:8).
- **v15 "for they dealt faithfully"** — *ki ve-emunah hem osim*. The same is said of the workmen when Josiah repairs the house (22:7).
- **v16 "it belonged to the priests"** — as the law gives it: *the sin offering and the guilt offering have one law… it shall belong to the priest who makes atonement with it* (Leviticus 7:7).
- **vv17–18 "sent them to Hazael king of Aram"** — Asa did the same with the treasures of the house of the LORD and sent them to Ben-hadad (1 Kings 15:18). Elisha had wept over what Hazael would do to Israel (8:12); now he reaches Judah.
- **vv20–21 "Jozabad son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shomer"** — Chronicles names them *Zabad son of Shimeath the Ammonite woman and Jehozabad son of Shimrith the Moabite woman*, and says they conspired *because of the blood of the sons of Jehoiada the priest* (2 Chronicles 24:25–26). *Beth-millo… Silla*: Silla is named nowhere else.

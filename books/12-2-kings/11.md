# 2 Kings 11

## Athaliah Seizes the Throne

**1** When Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she arose and destroyed all the royal seed.

**2** But Jehosheba, the daughter of King Joram and sister of Ahaziah, took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him away from among the king's sons who were being put to death, and put him and his nurse in the bedroom. They hid him from Athaliah, and he was not put to death.

**3** He was with her in the house of the LORD, hidden for six years, while Athaliah reigned over the land.

## Jehoiada's Plan

**4** In the seventh year Jehoiada sent and brought the commanders of the hundreds, of the Carites and of the guard, and had them come to him in the house of the LORD. He made a covenant with them and put them under oath in the house of the LORD, and he showed them the king's son.

**5** He commanded them, saying, "This is what you shall do: a third of you, those who come on duty on the Sabbath, shall keep watch over the king's house;

**6** a third shall be at the gate Sur, and a third at the gate behind the guard. You shall keep watch over the house as a barrier.

**7** The two divisions of you who go off duty on the Sabbath shall keep watch over the house of the LORD around the king.

**8** You shall surround the king on every side, each man with his weapons in his hand, and whoever comes within the ranks shall be put to death. Be with the king when he goes out and when he comes in."

**9** The commanders of the hundreds did according to all that Jehoiada the priest commanded. Each took his men, those coming on duty on the Sabbath with those going off duty on the Sabbath, and they came to Jehoiada the priest.

**10** The priest gave the commanders of the hundreds the spears and the shields that had been King David's, which were in the house of the LORD.

**11** The guard stood, each man with his weapons in his hand, from the right side of the house to the left side of the house, by the altar and the house, around the king.

## "Long Live the King!"

**12** Then he brought out the king's son and put the crown and the testimony on him. They made him king and anointed him, and they clapped their hands and said, "Long live the king!"

**13** When Athaliah heard the noise of the guard and of the people, she came to the people in the house of the LORD.

**14** She looked, and there was the king standing by the pillar, as the custom was, with the commanders and the trumpeters beside the king, and all the people of the land rejoicing and blowing trumpets. Athaliah tore her clothes and cried, "Conspiracy! Conspiracy!"

**15** Then Jehoiada the priest commanded the commanders of the hundreds who were set over the army, and said to them, "Bring her out between the ranks, and put to death with the sword anyone who follows her." For the priest had said, "Let her not be put to death in the house of the LORD."

**16** So they laid hands on her, and she went by the way of the horses' entrance to the king's house, and there she was put to death.

## The Covenant

**17** Jehoiada made the covenant between the LORD and the king and the people, that they should be the LORD's people, and between the king and the people.

**18** All the people of the land went to the house of Baal and tore it down. They smashed its altars and its images thoroughly, and they killed Mattan the priest of Baal before the altars. The priest appointed overseers over the house of the LORD.

**19** He took the commanders of the hundreds, the Carites, the guard and all the people of the land, and they brought the king down from the house of the LORD and came by the way of the gate of the guard to the king's house. He sat on the throne of the kings.

**20** All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet. They had put Athaliah to death with the sword at the king's house.

**21** Jehoash was seven years old when he began to reign.

## Notes

- **v21 in the Hebrew** — the Hebrew numbers this verse 12:1, and its chapter 12 runs one verse ahead of the English to the end.
- **v1 "destroyed all the royal seed"** — the LORD had promised David *to give him a lamp for his sons forever* (8:19). After this verse that line hangs on one infant, hidden for six years.
- **v2 "Jehosheba, the daughter of King Joram"** — Chronicles adds that she was *the wife of Jehoiada the priest* (2 Chronicles 22:11), which explains how the child could be hidden in the house of the LORD. The Greek calls Joash *the son of her brother*.
- **vv4, 19 "the Carites and the guard"** — *ha-kari ve-ha-ratsim*. *Kari* is the word written in 2 Samuel 20:23, where the read form is *Cherethites*, David's foreign bodyguard; here it stands as written, and the Greek simply spells both words out, *the Chorri and the Rasin*. *Ha-ratsim*, **the runners**, is *the guard* of 10:25.
- **v6 "the gate Sur… as a barrier"** — no gate called *Sur* appears anywhere else; Chronicles has *the gate of the foundation* (2 Chronicles 23:5). *Massach* is found only here, the Greek lacks it, and **as a barrier** is the sense the context gives, not a known meaning of the word.
- **v12 "the crown and the testimony"** — *ha-nezer ve-ha-edut*. *Edut* is the word of *the ark of the testimony*, where the tablets of the covenant lay (Exodus 40:21). What *testimony* was put on the young king the text does not say, and Chronicles repeats the same two words (2 Chronicles 23:11).
- **v13 "the noise of the guard and of the people"** — *qol ha-ratsin ha-am*, **the noise of the runners, the people**, with no *and*, and *ratsin* with an unusual ending. Chronicles reads *the noise of the people running and praising the king* (2 Chronicles 23:12).
- **v14 "standing by the pillar, as the custom was"** — *al ha-ammud ka-mishpat*. Josiah will stand *by the pillar* to make the covenant before the LORD, *to keep His commandments and His testimonies* (23:3).
- **v16 "they laid hands on her"** — *va-yasimu lah yadayim*, **they put hands to her**. *Yadayim* can also mean **room**, as in *the land is wide on both hands* (Genesis 34:21), so the clause can also be read *they made way for her*.
- **v17 "between the LORD and the king and the people… and between the king and the people"** — two covenants in one verse: the nation bound again to the LORD, and the king bound to the people.

# 2 Kings 15

## Azariah King of Judah

**1** In the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Azariah son of Amaziah king of Judah began to reign.

**2** He was sixteen years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jecoliah of Jerusalem.

**3** He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, according to all that Amaziah his father had done.

**4** Only the high places were not taken away; the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.

**5** The LORD struck the king, and he had blight until the day of his death, and he lived in a separate house. Jotham the king's son was over the household, governing the people of the land.

**6** The rest of the acts of Azariah and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**7** Azariah lay down with his fathers, and they buried him with his fathers in the City of David, and Jotham his son reigned in his place.

## Zechariah, Shallum and Menahem

**8** In the thirty-eighth year of Azariah king of Judah, Zechariah son of Jeroboam reigned over Israel in Samaria six months.

**9** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, as his fathers had done. He did not turn from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, with which he made Israel to sin.

**10** Shallum son of Jabesh conspired against him and struck him down before the people, and put him to death and reigned in his place.

**11** The rest of the acts of Zechariah are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

**12** This was the word of the LORD that He spoke to Jehu, saying, "Your sons to the fourth generation shall sit on the throne of Israel." So it came to be.

**13** Shallum son of Jabesh began to reign in the thirty-ninth year of Uzziah king of Judah, and he reigned one full month in Samaria.

**14** Then Menahem son of Gadi went up from Tirzah and came to Samaria, and he struck down Shallum son of Jabesh in Samaria and put him to death and reigned in his place.

**15** The rest of the acts of Shallum, and the conspiracy that he made, are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

**16** At that time Menahem struck Tiphsah and all who were in it and its territory from Tirzah, because they did not open to him. So he struck it, and he ripped open all its pregnant women.

**17** In the thirty-ninth year of Azariah king of Judah, Menahem son of Gadi began to reign over Israel, and he reigned ten years in Samaria.

**18** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD. All his days he did not turn from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, with which he made Israel to sin.

**19** Pul king of Assyria came against the land, and Menahem gave Pul a thousand talents of silver, so that his hand might be with him to strengthen the kingdom in his hand.

**20** Menahem exacted the silver from Israel, from all the men of worth, fifty shekels of silver from each man, to give to the king of Assyria. So the king of Assyria turned back and did not stay there in the land.

**21** The rest of the acts of Menahem and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**22** Menahem lay down with his fathers, and Pekahiah his son reigned in his place.

## Pekahiah and Pekah

**23** In the fiftieth year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekahiah son of Menahem began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned two years.

**24** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD. He did not turn from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, with which he made Israel to sin.

**25** Pekah son of Remaliah, his officer, conspired against him and struck him down in Samaria, in the citadel of the king's house, with Argob and with Arieh; with him were fifty men of the Gileadites. He put him to death and reigned in his place.

**26** The rest of the acts of Pekahiah and all that he did are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

**27** In the fifty-second year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekah son of Remaliah began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned twenty years.

**28** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD. He did not turn from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, with which he made Israel to sin.

**29** In the days of Pekah king of Israel, Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria came and took Ijon, Abel-beth-maacah, Janoah, Kedesh, Hazor, Gilead and Galilee, all the land of Naphtali, and he carried the people into exile to Assyria.

**30** Hoshea son of Elah made a conspiracy against Pekah son of Remaliah, struck him down and put him to death, and reigned in his place, in the twentieth year of Jotham son of Uzziah.

**31** The rest of the acts of Pekah and all that he did are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

## Jotham King of Judah

**32** In the second year of Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, Jotham son of Uzziah king of Judah began to reign.

**33** He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jerusha daughter of Zadok.

**34** He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD; he did according to all that Uzziah his father had done.

**35** Only the high places were not taken away; the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places. He built the upper gate of the house of the LORD.

**36** The rest of the acts of Jotham and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**37** In those days the LORD began to send Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah against Judah.

**38** Jotham lay down with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the City of David his father, and Ahaz his son reigned in his place.

## Notes

- **v5 "The LORD struck the king, and he had blight… in a separate house"** — *metsora*, from *tsara'at*, **blight**. *Beit ha-chofshit*, **the house of freedom**, found only here and in Chronicles, which gives the cause: Uzziah went into the house of the LORD to burn incense, and *the blight broke out on his forehead* before the priests (2 Chronicles 26:19–21). *Freedom* here is release from duty, the separation the law requires: *He shall live alone; his home shall be outside the camp* (Leviticus 13:46).
- **v10 "struck him down before the people"** — *qoval am*, with *qoval*, an Aramaic word for **before**. The Greek reads it as a place, *in Keblaam*.
- **v12 "Your sons to the fourth generation… So it came to be"** — the word of 10:30 kept: Jehoahaz, Joash, Jeroboam, Zechariah. Zechariah reigns six months.
- **v13 "one full month"** — *yerach yamim*, **a month of days**. Shallum reigns a month; Zimri reigned seven days (1 Kings 16:15).
- **v16 "Tiphsah… from Tirzah… ripped open all its pregnant women"** — *Tiphsah* is the Euphrates crossing named as the far edge of Solomon's rule (1 Kings 4:24), a long way from Tirzah; the Greek has *Tirzah* in both places. The text keeps the Hebrew. What Elisha wept to foresee from Hazael, *rip open their pregnant women* (8:12), an Israelite king does to Israelites.
- **vv19–20 "Pul… Tiglath-pileser"** — Chronicles names *Pul king of Assyria* and *Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria* together (1 Chronicles 5:26). *Men of worth*, *gibborei ha-chayil*, elsewhere fighting men (1 Chronicles 7:2); here the worth is measured in silver, fifty shekels a head.
- **v25 "with Argob and with Arieh"** — *Argov*, *ha-aryeh*, **the lion**: two names, or two men killed with the king; the text does not explain them. The Greek has *from the four hundred* for *of the Gileadites*.
- **v29 "Galilee, all the land of Naphtali… into exile to Assyria"** — the first exile of Israelites that Kings records. Isaiah speaks of this time: *in the former time He brought into contempt the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali; but in the latter He will make glorious… Galilee of the nations* (Isaiah 9:1), the words Matthew applies to Jesus (Matthew 4:15–16).
- **v30 "in the twentieth year of Jotham"** — Jotham reigns sixteen years (v33). The text keeps the number as it stands.
- **v37 "Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah"** — the two kings of Isaiah 7, *these two smoldering stumps of firebrands* (Isaiah 7:4).

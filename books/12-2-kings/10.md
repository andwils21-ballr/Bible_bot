# 2 Kings 10

## The Seventy Sons of Ahab

**1** Now Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. So Jehu wrote letters and sent them to Samaria, to the officials of Jezreel, the elders, and to the guardians of Ahab's sons, saying,

**2** "Now as soon as this letter comes to you, since your master's sons are with you, and you have chariots and horses, a fortified city and weapons,

**3** choose the best and most fitting of your master's sons, set him on his father's throne and fight for your master's house."

**4** But they were exceedingly afraid and said, "Two kings could not stand before him; how then can we stand?"

**5** So the one who was over the palace, the one who was over the city, the elders and the guardians sent word to Jehu, saying, "We are your servants, and all that you say to us we will do. We will not make anyone king. Do whatever is good in your eyes."

**6** Then he wrote them a second letter, saying, "If you are on my side, and if you will listen to my voice, take the heads of the men, your master's sons, and come to me at Jezreel by this time tomorrow." Now the king's sons, seventy men, were with the great men of the city, who were bringing them up.

**7** When the letter came to them, they took the king's sons and slaughtered them, seventy men, and put their heads in baskets and sent them to him at Jezreel.

**8** When the messenger came and told him, "They have brought the heads of the king's sons," he said, "Lay them in two heaps at the entrance of the gate until the morning."

**9** And it came to pass in the morning that he went out and stood and said to all the people, "You are innocent. It was I who conspired against my master and killed him. But who struck down all these?

**10** Know then that not a word of the LORD shall fall to the ground, which the LORD spoke concerning the house of Ahab. The LORD has done what He spoke by His servant Elijah."

**11** So Jehu struck down all who remained of the house of Ahab in Jezreel, all his great men, his close friends and his priests, until he left him not one survivor.

## The Brothers of Ahaziah

**12** Then he got up and set out and went to Samaria. On the way, at Beth-eked of the shepherds,

**13** Jehu met the brothers of Ahaziah king of Judah, and said, "Who are you?" They said, "We are the brothers of Ahaziah, and we have come down to greet the sons of the king and the sons of the queen mother."

**14** He said, "Take them alive." So they took them alive and slaughtered them at the pit of Beth-eked, forty-two men, and he left not one of them.

## Jehonadab Son of Rechab

**15** When he went on from there, he met Jehonadab son of Rechab coming to meet him. He greeted him and said to him, "Is your heart true to mine, as my heart is to yours?" Jehonadab answered, "It is." Jehu said, "If it is, give me your hand." So he gave him his hand, and Jehu took him up into the chariot with him.

**16** He said, "Come with me, and see my zeal for the LORD." So they had him ride in his chariot.

**17** When he came to Samaria, he struck down all who remained of Ahab in Samaria, until he had wiped them out, according to the word of the LORD that He spoke to Elijah.

## The Worshipers of Baal

**18** Then Jehu gathered all the people and said to them, "Ahab served Baal a little; Jehu will serve him much.

**19** So now call to me all the prophets of Baal, all his servants and all his priests. Let none be missing, for I have a great sacrifice for Baal. Whoever is missing shall not live." But Jehu did it with cunning, in order to destroy the servants of Baal.

**20** Jehu said, "Proclaim a solemn assembly for Baal." So they proclaimed it.

**21** Jehu sent throughout all Israel, and all the servants of Baal came, so that there was not a man left who did not come. They came into the house of Baal, and the house of Baal was filled from one end to the other.

**22** He said to the one in charge of the wardrobe, "Bring out garments for all the servants of Baal." So he brought out the garments for them.

**23** Then Jehu went into the house of Baal with Jehonadab son of Rechab, and he said to the servants of Baal, "Search and see that there is no servant of the LORD here with you, but only the servants of Baal."

**24** Then they went in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. Now Jehu had stationed eighty men outside and said, "The man who lets any of those whom I give into your hands escape shall give his life for his life."

**25** As soon as he had finished offering the burnt offering, Jehu said to the guard and to the officers, "Go in and strike them down; let no one come out." So they struck them down with the sword, and the guard and the officers threw them out and went into the inner room of the house of Baal.

**26** They brought out the pillars of the house of Baal and burned them.

**27** They broke down the pillar of Baal, and broke down the house of Baal, and made it a latrine to this day.

**28** Thus Jehu wiped out Baal from Israel.

## Jehu's Reign

**29** But Jehu did not turn aside from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, with which he made Israel to sin: the golden calves that were in Bethel and in Dan.

**30** The LORD said to Jehu, "Because you have done well in doing what is right in My eyes, and have done to the house of Ahab according to all that was in My heart, your sons to the fourth generation shall sit on the throne of Israel."

**31** But Jehu was not careful to walk in the law of the LORD, the God of Israel, with all his heart. He did not turn aside from the sins of Jeroboam, with which he made Israel to sin.

**32** In those days the LORD began to cut off parts of Israel. Hazael struck them throughout all the territory of Israel,

**33** from the Jordan eastward, all the land of Gilead, the Gadites, the Reubenites and the Manassites, from Aroer, which is by the Wadi Arnon, that is, Gilead and Bashan.

**34** The rest of the acts of Jehu, all that he did and all his might, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**35** Jehu lay down with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria. Jehoahaz his son reigned in his place.

**36** The time that Jehu reigned over Israel in Samaria was twenty-eight years.

## Notes

- **v1 "to the officials of Jezreel"** — the letters go to Samaria, and the Hebrew says *the officials of Jezreel*; the Greek has *the rulers of Samaria*. The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **vv9–10 "You are innocent… not a word of the LORD shall fall to the ground"** — Jehu owns the king's death; the seventy were killed by the elders of Samaria. *Not a word… shall fall to the ground*: as it was said of Samuel, the LORD *let none of his words fall to the ground* (1 Samuel 3:19).
- **vv13–14 "the brothers of Ahaziah… forty-two men"** — kin of the king of Judah, killed on the road. *Ha-gevirah*, **the queen mother** (1 Kings 15:13).
- **v15 "Jehonadab son of Rechab"** — the father of the Rechabites, who centuries later still keep his command: *Jonadab son of Rechab our father commanded us, You shall drink no wine* (Jeremiah 35:6).
- **v16 "see my zeal for the LORD"** — *be-qin'ati la-YHWH*, the word of Elijah at Horeb, *I have been very zealous for the LORD* (1 Kings 19:10).
- **v19 "Jehu did it with cunning"** — *be-oqbah*, from *aqav*, the root of Jacob's name, as Esau said, *he has heeled me these two times* (Genesis 27:36).
- **v27 "made it a latrine to this day"** — *le-machara'ot*, as the written text has it; the read form is *le-motsa'ot*, another word for the same thing.
- **vv29, 31 "the golden calves that were in Bethel and in Dan"** — Jehu ends the worship of Baal and keeps Jeroboam's calves (1 Kings 12:28–29).
- **v30 "your sons to the fourth generation shall sit on the throne of Israel"** — fulfilled in 15:12. Hosea names his son *Jezreel*, *for in a little while I will punish the house of Jehu for the blood of Jezreel* (Hosea 1:4): the bloodshed that won the throne is later counted against Jehu's house.
- **vv32–33 "the LORD began to cut off parts of Israel. Hazael struck them"** — *le-qatstsot*, **to trim off**. Elisha had wept over what Hazael would do (8:12).

# 2 Kings 4

## The Widow's Oil

**1** A certain woman of the wives of the sons of the prophets cried out to Elisha, "Your servant my husband is dead, and you know that your servant feared the LORD. Now the creditor has come to take my two children to be his slaves."

**2** Elisha said to her, "What shall I do for you? Tell me, what do you have in the house?" She said, "Your servant has nothing in the house except a jar of oil."

**3** He said, "Go outside, borrow vessels from all your neighbors, empty vessels, and not too few.

**4** Then go in and shut the door behind you and your sons, and pour into all these vessels. As each is filled, set it aside."

**5** So she went from him and shut the door behind her and her sons. They kept bringing vessels to her, and she kept pouring.

**6** And it came to pass, when the vessels were full, that she said to her son, "Bring me another vessel." He said to her, "There is not another vessel." Then the oil stopped.

**7** She came and told the man of God, and he said, "Go, sell the oil and pay your debt, and you and your sons can live on the rest."

## The Shunammite Woman

**8** And it came to pass one day that Elisha passed through Shunem, where a wealthy woman lived, and she urged him to eat bread. So whenever he passed that way, he would turn in there to eat bread.

**9** She said to her husband, "I know now that this is a holy man of God who is continually passing our way.

**10** Let us make a small room with walls on the roof, and put a bed there for him, a table, a chair and a lamp. And it shall come to pass, whenever he comes to us, that he can turn in there."

**11** And it came to pass one day that he came there, and he turned into the upper room and lay down there.

**12** He said to Gehazi his servant, "Call this Shunammite." When he had called her, she stood before him.

**13** He said to him, "Say now to her, 'You have taken all this trouble for us. What can be done for you? Would you have a word spoken for you to the king or to the commander of the army?'" She answered, "I live among my own people."

**14** He said, "What then can be done for her?" Gehazi answered, "Well, she has no son, and her husband is old."

**15** He said, "Call her." When he had called her, she stood in the doorway.

**16** He said, "At this appointed time, at the time of life, you shall embrace a son." She said, "No, my lord, man of God, do not lie to your servant."

**17** But the woman conceived and bore a son at that appointed time, at the time of life, as Elisha had said to her.

## The Shunammite's Son

**18** When the child had grown, it came to pass one day that he went out to his father among the reapers.

**19** He said to his father, "My head, my head!" The father said to his servant, "Carry him to his mother."

**20** When he had lifted him up and brought him to his mother, the child sat on her knees until noon, and then he died.

**21** She went up and laid him on the bed of the man of God, shut the door behind him and went out.

**22** Then she called to her husband and said, "Please send me one of the servants and one of the donkeys, so that I may hurry to the man of God and come back."

**23** He said, "Why are you going to him today? It is neither new moon nor sabbath." She said, "It is well."

**24** Then she saddled the donkey and said to her servant, "Drive on; do not slow the pace for me unless I tell you."

**25** So she set out and came to the man of God at Mount Carmel. And it came to pass, when the man of God saw her at a distance, that he said to Gehazi his servant, "There is the Shunammite.

**26** Run now to meet her and say to her, 'Is it well with you? Is it well with your husband? Is it well with the child?'" She answered, "It is well."

**27** When she came to the man of God on the mountain, she took hold of his feet. Gehazi came near to push her away, but the man of God said, "Let her alone, for her soul is bitter within her, and the LORD has hidden it from me and has not told me."

**28** She said, "Did I ask my lord for a son? Did I not say, 'Do not deceive me'?"

**29** He said to Gehazi, "Gird up your loins, take my staff in your hand and go. If you meet anyone, do not greet him, and if anyone greets you, do not answer him. Lay my staff on the face of the child."

**30** Then the mother of the child said, "As the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So he got up and followed her.

**31** Gehazi went on ahead of them and laid the staff on the face of the child, but there was no voice and no response. So he went back to meet him and told him, "The child has not awakened."

**32** When Elisha came into the house, the child was dead, laid on his bed.

**33** So he went in and shut the door behind the two of them and prayed to the LORD.

**34** Then he went up and lay on the child, putting his mouth on his mouth, his eyes on his eyes and his hands on his hands. As he stretched himself out on him, the flesh of the child became warm.

**35** Then he got up and walked once back and forth in the house, and went up and stretched himself out on him. The child sneezed seven times, and the child opened his eyes.

**36** He called Gehazi and said, "Call this Shunammite." So he called her, and when she came to him, he said, "Pick up your son."

**37** She came and fell at his feet, bowing to the ground. Then she picked up her son and went out.

## Death in the Pot

**38** Elisha came back to Gilgal when there was a famine in the land. As the sons of the prophets were sitting before him, he said to his servant, "Set on the large pot and boil stew for the sons of the prophets."

**39** One of them went out into the field to gather herbs, and found a wild vine and gathered from it wild gourds, his lap full, and came and cut them up into the pot of stew, for they did not know what they were.

**40** They poured it out for the men to eat. And it came to pass, as they were eating of the stew, that they cried out, "There is death in the pot, man of God!" And they could not eat it.

**41** He said, "Then bring flour." He threw it into the pot and said, "Pour it out for the people, so that they may eat." There was no harm in the pot.

## Bread for a Hundred Men

**42** A man came from Baal-shalishah and brought the man of God bread of the firstfruits, twenty loaves of barley, and fresh ears of grain in his sack. He said, "Give to the people, so that they may eat."

**43** But his servant said, "How can I set this before a hundred men?" He said, "Give to the people, so that they may eat, for thus says the LORD: 'They shall eat and have some left.'"

**44** So he set it before them, and they ate and had some left, according to the word of the LORD.

## Notes

- **v1 "the creditor has come to take my two children to be his slaves"** — the law allowed a poor Israelite to be sold to work off debt, but *you shall not make him work as a slave works* (Leviticus 25:39).
- **vv4–6 "shut the door… Then the oil stopped"** — the oil runs as long as there are vessels to hold it, as the jar of flour and jug of oil held out for the widow of Zarephath (1 Kings 17:14–16).
- **v8 "a wealthy woman"** — *ishah gedolah*, **a great woman**: of standing and means.
- **vv16–17 "At this appointed time, at the time of life, you shall embrace a son"** — *la-mo'ed ha-zeh ka-et chayyah*, the words the LORD spoke to Abraham about Sarah, *At the appointed time I will return to you, at the time of life, and Sarah shall have a son* (Genesis 18:14).
- **v23 "It is neither new moon nor sabbath… It is well"** — her husband's question implies that those were the days to visit a man of God. *Shalom*, she says, to her husband and again to Gehazi (v26), and tells the truth only at Elisha's feet.
- **v29 "If you meet anyone, do not greet him"** — Jesus gave the same charge to those He sent out: *greet no one on the road* (Luke 10:4).
- **v30 "As the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you"** — Elisha's own oath to Elijah (2:2, 4, 6), now spoken to him.
- **vv34–35 "his mouth on his mouth, his eyes on his eyes and his hands on his hands"** — as Elijah stretched himself out on the widow's son at Zarephath (1 Kings 17:21). The staff laid on the child's face did nothing (v31). *Va-yizorer*, **sneezed**, is found only here.
- **vv39–41 "wild gourds… There is death in the pot"** — *paqqu'ot sadeh*, from the root of the carved *gourds* of the temple (1 Kings 6:18). Flour is thrown in, and the danger is gone.
- **vv42–44 "twenty loaves of barley… They shall eat and have some left"** — *akhol ve-hoter*. In the Gospel of John a boy has *five barley loaves and two fish*, and after the crowd eats, *they filled twelve baskets with fragments from the five barley loaves* (John 6:9, 13).

# 2 Kings 17

## Hoshea, the Last King of Israel

**1** In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, Hoshea son of Elah began to reign in Samaria over Israel, and he reigned nine years.

**2** He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, yet not like the kings of Israel who were before him.

**3** Shalmaneser king of Assyria came up against him, and Hoshea became his servant and paid him tribute.

**4** But the king of Assyria found conspiracy in Hoshea, for he had sent messengers to So king of Egypt and had not brought tribute to the king of Assyria, as he had year by year. So the king of Assyria shut him up and bound him in prison.

**5** Then the king of Assyria came up through all the land, and he came up to Samaria and besieged it three years.

**6** In the ninth year of Hoshea the king of Assyria took Samaria and carried Israel into exile to Assyria. He settled them in Halah and on the Habor, the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes.

## Why Israel Fell

**7** This happened because the sons of Israel had sinned against the LORD their God, who brought them up out of the land of Egypt from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt. They feared other gods

**8** and walked in the statutes of the nations whom the LORD had driven out before the sons of Israel, and in the statutes the kings of Israel had made.

**9** The sons of Israel did in secret things that were not right against the LORD their God. They built for themselves high places in all their towns, from watchtower to fortified city.

**10** They set up for themselves pillars and Asherim on every high hill and under every leafy tree,

**11** and there they burned incense on all the high places, as the nations did whom the LORD carried away before them. They did evil things to provoke the LORD to anger,

**12** and they served idols, of which the LORD had said to them, "You shall not do this."

**13** Yet the LORD warned Israel and Judah by every prophet and every seer, saying, "Turn from your evil ways and keep My commandments and My statutes, according to all the law that I commanded your fathers and that I sent to you by My servants the prophets."

**14** But they would not listen, and they stiffened their necks, like the necks of their fathers, who did not believe in the LORD their God.

**15** They rejected His statutes and His covenant that He made with their fathers, and His testimonies with which He warned them. They went after emptiness and became empty, and after the nations that were around them, of whom the LORD had commanded them not to do as they did.

**16** They forsook all the commandments of the LORD their God and made for themselves molten images, two calves. They made an Asherah, bowed down to all the host of heaven and served Baal.

**17** They made their sons and their daughters pass through the fire, practiced divination and read omens, and sold themselves to do evil in the eyes of the LORD, to provoke Him to anger.

**18** So the LORD was very angry with Israel and removed them from His presence. None was left but the tribe of Judah alone.

**19** Judah also did not keep the commandments of the LORD their God, but walked in the statutes Israel had made.

**20** The LORD rejected all the seed of Israel. He afflicted them and gave them into the hand of plunderers, until He had cast them out from His presence.

**21** For He tore Israel from the house of David, and they made Jeroboam son of Nebat king. Jeroboam drove Israel away from following the LORD and made them sin a great sin.

**22** The sons of Israel walked in all the sins that Jeroboam did; they did not turn from them,

**23** until the LORD removed Israel from His presence, as He had spoken by all His servants the prophets. So Israel was carried into exile from its own land to Assyria, to this day.

## The Settlers in Samaria

**24** The king of Assyria brought people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath and Sepharvaim, and settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the sons of Israel. They took possession of Samaria and lived in its cities.

**25** And it came to pass, at the beginning of their living there, that they did not fear the LORD. So the LORD sent lions among them, which kept killing some of them.

**26** So they said to the king of Assyria, "The nations that you carried away and settled in the cities of Samaria do not know the rule of the god of the land. He has sent lions among them, and they are killing them, because they do not know the rule of the god of the land."

**27** Then the king of Assyria commanded, saying, "Take there one of the priests whom you carried away from there. Let him go and live there and teach them the rule of the god of the land."

**28** So one of the priests whom they had carried away from Samaria came and lived in Bethel, and he taught them how they should fear the LORD.

**29** But every nation went on making gods of its own and put them in the houses of the high places that the Samarians had made, every nation in the cities in which they lived.

**30** The men of Babylon made Succoth-benoth, the men of Cuth made Nergal, the men of Hamath made Ashima,

**31** and the Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak. The Sepharvites burned their sons in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.

**32** They also feared the LORD, and they appointed from among all their people priests of the high places, who sacrificed for them in the houses of the high places.

**33** They feared the LORD and served their own gods, according to the rule of the nations from which they had been carried away.

**34** To this day they do according to their former rules. They do not fear the LORD, and they do not do according to their statutes or their rules, or according to the law and the commandment that the LORD commanded the sons of Jacob, whom He named Israel.

**35** The LORD made a covenant with them and commanded them, saying, "You shall not fear other gods or bow down to them or serve them or sacrifice to them,

**36** but the LORD, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt with great power and with an outstretched arm, Him you shall fear, to Him you shall bow down, and to Him you shall sacrifice.

**37** The statutes and the rules, the law and the commandment that He wrote for you, you shall be careful to do all the days. You shall not fear other gods,

**38** and the covenant that I have made with you, you shall not forget. You shall not fear other gods,

**39** but the LORD your God you shall fear, and He will deliver you from the hand of all your enemies."

**40** But they would not listen, and they do according to their former rule.

**41** So these nations feared the LORD and served their carved images; their sons and their sons' sons also, as their fathers did, so they do to this day.

## Notes

- **v4 "So king of Egypt"** — *So* is named nowhere else; the Greek has *Segor*.
- **v6 "carried Israel into exile to Assyria"** — Samaria falls in the ninth year of Hoshea (18:9–11). Moses had warned it: *the LORD will scatter you among all the peoples* (Deuteronomy 28:64).
- **v9 "did in secret things that were not right"** — *va-yechappe'u*, found only here. **In secret** is the sense the context gives; it matches *chafah*, **to cover**, a verb that differs by its last letter, as David went up *with his head covered* (2 Samuel 15:30). The Greek has *they clothed themselves with words*.
- **v15 "went after emptiness and became empty"** — *va-yelkhu acharei ha-hevel va-yehbalu*, word for word in Jeremiah: *they went after emptiness and became empty* (Jeremiah 2:5). *Hevel* is **breath**, **vapor**, the word of Ecclesiastes and the name *Abel*; people become what they follow.
- **v17 "sold themselves to do evil"** — *va-yitmakkeru*, said earlier in Kings only of Ahab (1 Kings 21:20, 25). Moses used the same verb for the end of the curses: *there you will offer yourselves for sale to your enemies* (Deuteronomy 28:68).
- **v20 "until He had cast them out from His presence"** — the end of what 13:23 said had not happened *until now*.
- **v21 "He tore Israel from the house of David"** — *qara Yisra'el*, with no subject; the words can also be read *Israel tore away from the house of David*. The verb is the LORD's in Ahijah's sign: *I am about to tear the kingdom out of the hand of Solomon* (1 Kings 11:31), and the text reads it so. Written *va-yada*, read *va-yaddach*, **drove away**.
- **vv26–40 "the rule of the god of the land… their former rules"** — *mishpat*, one word throughout: the settlers do not know *the rule of the god of the land* (vv26–27) and follow *the rule of the nations* (v33), while the LORD's *statutes and rules* (vv34, 37) are what Israel was given. The god of the land is the LORD, and the priest teaches them *how they should fear the LORD* (v28).
- **v31 "burned their sons in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech"** — written *Sepharim*, read *Sepharvaim*, as in v24.
- **vv32–33, 41 "They feared the LORD and served their own gods"** — and then v34, *they do not fear the LORD*: fear of the LORD alongside other gods is counted as not fearing Him at all, as the commands of vv35–39 say three times, *You shall not fear other gods*.

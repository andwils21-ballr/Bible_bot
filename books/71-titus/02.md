# Titus 2

**1** But as for you, teach what fits sound teaching.

**2** Older men are to be sober-minded, dignified, self-controlled, sound in faith, in love and in endurance.

**3** Older women likewise are to be reverent in behavior, not slanderers or slaves to much wine. They are to teach what is good,

**4** and so train the young women to love their husbands and children,

**5** to be self-controlled, pure, working at home, kind, and submissive to their own husbands, so that the word of God may not be blasphemed.

**6** Likewise urge the younger men to be self-controlled.

**7** Show yourself in all respects a model of good works, and in your teaching show integrity and dignity,

**8** and sound speech that cannot be condemned, so that an opponent may be put to shame, having nothing bad to say about us.

**9** Slaves are to be submissive to their own masters in everything, to please them and not talk back,

**10** not stealing, but showing all good faith, so that in everything they may adorn the teaching of God our Savior.

## The Grace of God Has Appeared

**11** For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to all people,

**12** training us to renounce ungodliness and worldly desires, and to live self-controlled, upright and godly lives in the present age,

**13** waiting for the blessed hope, the appearing of the glory of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ,

**14** who gave Himself for us to redeem us from all lawlessness and to purify for Himself a people for His own possession, zealous for good works.

**15** Speak these things, and urge and rebuke with all authority. Let no one disregard you.

---

## Notes

- **v13 "our great God and Savior Jesus Christ"** — *tou megalou theou kai sōtēros hēmōn Iēsou Christou*, with one article over both nouns, which in Greek usually makes them one person: Jesus Christ is *our great God and Savior*. It can also be read as two, *the great God and our Savior Jesus Christ*.
- **v14 "a people for His own possession"** — *laon periousion*, the words of Sinai, *you will be My own treasure out of all the peoples* (Exodus 19:5), *laos periousios* in the Greek. What God said to Israel at the mountain is said here of the people Christ purifies for Himself.

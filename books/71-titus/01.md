# Titus 1

**1** Paul, a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, for the faith of God's chosen ones and their knowledge of the truth that accords with godliness,

**2** in hope of eternal life, which God, who cannot lie, promised before the ages began,

**3** and at the proper time revealed His word in the preaching entrusted to me by command of God our Savior,

**4** to Titus, my true son in our common faith: Grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior.

## Elders

**5** This is why I left you in Crete: so that you might put in order what remained to be done and appoint elders in every town as I directed you,

**6** if anyone is above reproach, the husband of one wife, with believing children who are not open to the charge of wild living or disobedience.

**7** For an overseer, as God's steward, must be above reproach. He must not be arrogant or quick-tempered or a drunkard or violent or greedy for gain,

**8** but hospitable, a lover of good, self-controlled, upright, holy and disciplined,

**9** holding firm to the trustworthy word as taught, so that he may be able both to encourage with sound teaching and to rebuke those who contradict it.

## Silencing the Deceivers

**10** For there are many who are insubordinate, empty talkers and deceivers, especially those of the circumcision.

**11** They must be silenced, for they are upsetting whole households by teaching what they ought not, for the sake of shameful gain.

**12** One of them, a prophet of their own, said, "Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, lazy gluttons."

**13** This testimony is true. So rebuke them sharply, so that they may be sound in the faith,

**14** not giving their minds to Jewish myths and the commandments of people who turn away from the truth.

**15** To the pure all things are pure, but to the defiled and unbelieving nothing is pure; both their minds and their consciences are defiled.

**16** They claim to know God, but by their works they deny Him. They are detestable, disobedient, and unfit for any good work.

# Hebrews 8

## The Minister of the True Tent

**1** Now the main point of what we are saying is this: we have such a high priest, one who sat down at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens,

**2** a minister in the holy places and in the true tent, which the Lord set up, not man.

**3** For every high priest is appointed to offer gifts and sacrifices, so it is necessary for this one also to have something to offer.

**4** If He were on earth, He would not be a priest at all, since there are those who offer the gifts according to law.

**5** They serve at a copy and shadow of the heavenly things, as Moses was instructed when he was about to finish the tent: "See," He says, "that you make everything by the pattern that was shown you on the mountain."

**6** But as it is, He has obtained a ministry as much more excellent as the covenant He mediates is better, a covenant enacted on better promises.

## The First Covenant Made Old

**7** For if that first covenant had been faultless, there would have been no place sought for a second.

**8** For He finds fault with them when He says:  
"Days are coming, says the Lord,  
when I will establish a new covenant  
with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah,

**9** not like the covenant that I made with their fathers  
on the day I took them by the hand  
to bring them out of the land of Egypt;  
for they did not continue in My covenant,  
and I turned away from them, says the Lord.

**10** For this is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel  
after those days, says the Lord:  
I will put My laws into their minds,  
and on their hearts I will write them;  
and I will be their God,  
and they will be My people.

**11** No one will teach his fellow citizen  
or his brother, saying, 'Know the Lord,'  
for they will all know Me,  
from the least of them to the greatest.

**12** For I will be merciful toward their wrongdoing,  
and I will remember their sins no more."

**13** When He says *new*, He has made the first one old; and what is becoming old and growing aged is near to vanishing.

## Notes

- **v5 "by the pattern that was shown you on the mountain"** — Exodus 25:40, *See, and make them by their pattern, which you are being shown on the mountain*, in the Greek *kata ton typon*, **by the pattern**. Hebrews reads it as the LORD's own word that the tent Moses built was made after something he was shown, so the earthly tent is *a copy and shadow*; in 9:24 the holy places made with hands are *antitypa*, **copies**, of the true ones. Stephen quotes the same command of the *tent of witness* (Acts 7:44).
- **v9 "they did not continue in My covenant, and I turned away from them"** — Jeremiah 31:32 in the Greek (38:32 there), *ēmelēsa autōn*, **I turned away from them**, or **I disregarded them**. The Hebrew reads *My covenant that they broke, though I was their husband*, *ba'alti bam*. Hebrews quotes the Greek throughout vv8–12, and its *I will put My laws into their minds* and *I will be merciful toward their wrongdoing* are the Greek's words as well.

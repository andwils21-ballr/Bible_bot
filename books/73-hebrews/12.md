# Hebrews 12

## Run the Race

**1** So then, since we also have so great a cloud of witnesses surrounding us, let us lay aside every weight and the sin that clings so closely, and let us run with endurance the race set before us,

**2** looking to Jesus, the founder and perfecter of faith, who for the joy set before Him endured the cross, despising its shame, and has sat down at the right hand of the throne of God.

**3** Consider Him who endured such hostility from sinners against themselves, so that you do not grow weary and lose heart.

## The Lord Disciplines the One He Loves

**4** In your struggle against sin you have not yet resisted to the point of shedding blood.

**5** You have forgotten the encouragement that speaks to you as sons:  
"My son, do not make light of the discipline of the Lord,  
and do not lose heart when you are reproved by Him;

**6** for the Lord disciplines the one He loves,  
and scourges every son whom He receives."

**7** It is for discipline that you endure. God is treating you as sons. For what son is there whom his father does not discipline?

**8** If you are left without discipline, in which all have shared, then you are illegitimate children and not sons.

**9** Besides, we have had the fathers of our flesh to discipline us, and we respected them. Shall we not much more be subject to the Father of spirits and live?

**10** For they disciplined us for a short time as seemed best to them, but He does it for our good, so that we may share His holiness.

**11** No discipline seems for the moment to be a joy, but painful; later, though, it yields the peaceful fruit of righteousness to those who have been trained by it.

**12** So lift your drooping hands and strengthen your weak knees,

**13** and make straight paths for your feet, so that what is lame may not be put out of joint, but rather be healed.

## Esau's Blessing

**14** Pursue peace with everyone, and the holiness without which no one will see the Lord.

**15** See to it that no one falls short of the grace of God; that no root of bitterness springs up and causes trouble, and many are defiled by it;

**16** that no one is sexually immoral, or godless like Esau, who sold his own birthright for a single meal.

**17** For you know that afterward, when he wanted to inherit the blessing, he was rejected, for he found no place for repentance, though he sought it with tears.

## Mount Sinai and Mount Zion

**18** For you have not come to what can be touched, to a blazing fire and darkness and gloom and a whirlwind,

**19** and to the sound of a trumpet and a voice speaking words, which those who heard begged that no further word be spoken to them.

**20** For they could not bear the command, "If even an animal touches the mountain, it shall be stoned."

**21** So terrifying was the sight that Moses said, "I am terrified and trembling."

**22** But you have come to Mount Zion and to the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem, and to myriads of angels in festal gathering,

**23** and to the assembly of the firstborn who are enrolled in heaven, and to God, the judge of all, and to the spirits of the righteous made perfect,

**24** and to Jesus, the mediator of a new covenant, and to the sprinkled blood that speaks better than Abel.

**25** See that you do not refuse Him who is speaking. For if they did not escape when they refused the One who warned them on earth, much less will we, if we turn away from Him who warns from heaven.

**26** His voice then shook the earth, but now He has promised, "Once more I will shake not only the earth but also the heaven."

**27** This phrase, "Once more," shows the removal of what is shaken, as of things that have been made, so that what cannot be shaken may remain.

**28** So, since we are receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken, let us be grateful, and through this let us serve God acceptably, with reverence and awe,

**29** for our God is a consuming fire.

## Notes

- **v2 "for the joy set before Him"** — *anti tēs prokeimenēs autō charas*. *Anti* can mean **for**, in exchange for, the joy that lay ahead as the prize of the race (v1, *the race set before us*, the same verb); or **instead of**, the joy that was in His reach and that He set aside for the cross.
- **v3 "hostility from sinners against themselves"** — *eis heautous*, as the edition prints it, with the mark that the copies differ here; most English Bibles read *against Himself*. In the edition's reading, those who opposed Him were working against their own lives.
- **vv5–6 "and scourges every son whom He receives"** — Proverbs 3:11–12 in the Greek. The Hebrew reads *for the LORD reproves the one He loves, as a father the son in whom he delights*. The Greek has *scourges*; both have the father and the son, on which vv7–10 are built.
- **v24 "the sprinkled blood that speaks better than Abel"** — Abel *still speaks* (11:4), and in Genesis it is his blood that speaks: *The voice of your brother's bloods is crying out to Me from the ground* (Genesis 4:10). Abel's blood cried out, and a curse followed (Genesis 4:11); the sprinkled blood is the blood of the covenant, which cleanses the conscience (9:14, 20; 10:22).

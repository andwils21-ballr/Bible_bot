# Hebrews 2

## Do Not Drift Away

**1** For this reason we must pay much closer attention to what we have heard, so that we do not drift away from it.

**2** For if the message spoken through angels proved firm, and every transgression and disobedience received its just penalty,

**3** how will we escape if we neglect so great a salvation? It was first spoken by the Lord, and it was confirmed to us by those who heard Him,

**4** while God also bore witness by signs and wonders and various mighty works and by gifts of the Holy Spirit distributed according to His will.

## The Son Made Lower for a Little While

**5** For it was not to angels that God subjected the world to come, of which we are speaking.

**6** But someone has testified somewhere,  
"What is man, that You remember him,  
or the son of man, that You visit him?

**7** You made him for a little while lower than the angels;  
You crowned him with glory and honor,

**8** putting all things under his feet."  
In putting everything under him, He left nothing outside his control. At present, we do not yet see everything under him.

**9** But we see Jesus, who for a little while was made lower than the angels, crowned with glory and honor because of the suffering of death, so that apart from God He might taste death for everyone.

**10** For it was fitting that He, for whom and through whom all things exist, in bringing many sons to glory, should make the founder of their salvation perfect through sufferings.

**11** For He who makes holy and those who are made holy all have one origin. That is why He is not ashamed to call them brothers,

**12** saying,  
"I will tell of Your name to My brothers;  
among the congregation I will sing Your praise."

**13** Again,  
"I will put My trust in Him."  
Again,  
"Here am I and the children God has given Me."

**14** Since therefore the children share in blood and flesh, He Himself likewise shared in the same things, so that through death He might destroy the one who has the power of death, that is, the devil,

**15** and set free all those who through fear of death were held in slavery all their lives.

**16** For surely it is not angels that He helps, but He helps the seed of Abraham.

**17** Therefore He had to be made like His brothers in every way, so that He might become a merciful and faithful high priest in the service of God, to make atonement for the sins of the people.

**18** Because He Himself suffered when He was tested, He is able to help those who are being tested.

## Notes

- **vv6–9 "You made him for a little while lower than the angels"** — Psalm 8:4–6 in the Greek. The Hebrew has *You have made him little less than God*, *me'at me-Elohim*; the Greek has *brachy ti par' angelous*, which can mean **a little lower** or **for a little while lower**. The psalm speaks of humankind; the writer reads it of Jesus, who was lower *for a little while* and is now *crowned with glory and honor*.
- **v9 "apart from God"** — *chōris theou*, the reading of the Greek edition (SBLGNT), which marks the place as one where manuscripts differ; the other reading is *chariti theou*, **by the grace of God**. *Apart from God* can mean that He tasted death for all things except God, or that He tasted it forsaken, as He cried, *My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me?* (Mark 15:34).

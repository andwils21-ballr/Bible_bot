# Hebrews 4

## The Rest That Remains

**1** So, while the promise of entering His rest still stands, let us fear that any of you should seem to have fallen short of it.

**2** For we have had the good news proclaimed to us just as they did; but the message they heard did them no good, because they were not united in faith with those who listened.

**3** For we who have believed enter that rest, as He has said,  
"So I swore in My anger,  
'They shall not enter My rest,'"  
although His works were finished from the foundation of the world.

**4** For somewhere He has spoken of the seventh day in this way: "God rested on the seventh day from all His works."

**5** Again, in this passage: "They shall not enter My rest."

**6** Since therefore it remains for some to enter it, and those who formerly had the good news proclaimed to them did not enter because of disobedience,

**7** He again sets a certain day, "Today," saying through David so long afterward, in the words already quoted,  
"Today, if you hear His voice,  
do not harden your hearts."

**8** For if Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken of another day later on.

**9** So then, there remains a sabbath rest for the people of God,

**10** for whoever has entered God's rest has also rested from his works, as God did from His.

**11** Let us therefore strive to enter that rest, so that no one may fall by the same kind of disobedience.

## The Living Word

**12** For the word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing to the division of soul and spirit, of joints and marrow, and able to judge the thoughts and intentions of the heart.

**13** No creature is hidden from His sight, but all are naked and laid bare to the eyes of Him to whom we must give account.

## Our Great High Priest

**14** Since then we have a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus, the Son of God, let us hold fast our confession.

**15** For we do not have a high priest who is unable to sympathize with our weaknesses, but one who has been tested in every way as we are, yet without sin.

**16** Let us then draw near with confidence to the throne of grace, so that we may receive mercy and find grace to help in time of need.

## Notes

- **vv3–10 "My rest… God rested on the seventh day… a sabbath rest"** — the argument turns on one Greek word. Psalm 95 says *They shall not enter My rest*, *katapausis*, and the Greek of Genesis 2:2 says *God rested*, *katepausen*, on the seventh day. Where the Hebrew of Genesis has *He ceased*, *shavat*, the root of *sabbath*, and v9 names what is left for God's people with a word found nowhere else, *sabbatismos*, **a sabbath rest**.
- **v8 "if Joshua had given them rest"** — *Iēsous*, the Greek of the name *Joshua* and the name of Jesus. Joshua brought the people into the land, but the psalm, written long after, still says *Today*: the rest was not yet given.

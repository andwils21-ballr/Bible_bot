# Isaiah 51

**1** Listen to Me, you who pursue righteousness,  
you who seek the LORD:  
look to the rock from which you were hewn,  
and to the quarry from which you were dug.

**2** Look to Abraham your father,  
and to Sarah who bore you;  
for he was one when I called him,  
and I blessed him and made him many.

**3** For the LORD comforts Zion;  
He comforts all her waste places;  
He makes her wilderness like Eden,  
and her desert like the garden of the LORD;  
joy and gladness will be found in her,  
thanksgiving and the sound of song.

**4** Pay attention to Me, My people;  
give ear to Me, My nation;  
for instruction will go out from Me,  
and I will set My justice as a light for the peoples.

**5** My righteousness is near;  
My deliverance has gone out,  
and My arms will judge the peoples;  
the coastlands wait for Me,  
and for My arm they hope.

**6** Lift up your eyes to the heavens,  
and look at the earth beneath;  
for the heavens will vanish like smoke,  
the earth will wear out like a garment,  
and its inhabitants will die in the same way;  
but My deliverance will be forever,  
and My righteousness will not be broken.

**7** Listen to Me, you who know righteousness,  
the people with My instruction in their heart:  
do not fear the reproach of men,  
and do not be dismayed at their revilings.

**8** For the moth will eat them up like a garment,  
and the worm will eat them like wool;  
but My righteousness will be forever,  
and My deliverance to all generations.

**9** Awake, awake, put on strength,  
arm of the LORD!  
Awake, as in the days of old,  
the generations of long ago.  
Was it not You who cut Rahab in pieces,  
who pierced the dragon?

**10** Was it not You who dried up the sea,  
the waters of the great deep,  
who made the depths of the sea a way  
for the redeemed to cross over?

**11** The ransomed of the LORD will return  
and come to Zion with singing;  
everlasting joy will be on their heads;  
they will obtain gladness and joy,  
and sorrow and sighing will flee away.

**12** I, I am He who comforts you;  
who are you that you are afraid of man, who dies,  
of the son of man, who is made like grass,

**13** and have forgotten the LORD your Maker,  
who stretched out the heavens  
and laid the foundations of the earth,  
and you live in dread continually, all day long,  
because of the fury of the oppressor  
when he sets himself to destroy?  
Where is the fury of the oppressor?

**14** The one bowed down will soon be set free;  
he will not die and go down to the pit,  
nor will his bread be lacking.

**15** For I am the LORD your God,  
who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar —  
the LORD of hosts is His name.

**16** I have put My words in your mouth  
and covered you in the shadow of My hand,  
to plant the heavens  
and lay the foundations of the earth,  
and to say to Zion, "You are My people."

## The Cup of Staggering

**17** Rouse yourself, rouse yourself,  
stand up, Jerusalem,  
you who have drunk from the hand of the LORD  
the cup of His wrath,  
who have drunk the bowl, the cup of reeling,  
and drained it.

**18** There is none to guide her  
among all the sons she has borne;  
there is none to take her by the hand  
among all the sons she has brought up.

**19** These two things have come on you —  
who will grieve with you? —  
devastation and destruction, famine and sword;  
by whom shall I comfort you?

**20** Your sons have fainted;  
they lie at the head of every street  
like an antelope in a net;  
they are full of the wrath of the LORD,  
the rebuke of your God.

**21** So hear this, you who are afflicted,  
who are drunk, but not with wine:

**22** This is what your Lord, the LORD, says,  
your God who pleads the cause of His people:  
"I have taken from your hand the cup of reeling;  
the bowl, the cup of My wrath,  
you will drink no more.

**23** I will put it into the hand of your tormentors,  
who said to you, 'Bow down, that we may pass over,'  
and you made your back like the ground,  
like the street for those who pass over."

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "the rock from which you were hewn… Abraham your father"** — the quarry is named in the next line: the people were cut from Abraham and Sarah. *He was one when I called him* — the LORD's promise to the one man, *I will bless you, and I will make your name great* (Genesis 12:2). *Va-arbehu*, "and made him many": one man became a people.
- **v3 "her desert like the garden of the LORD"** — the words of Genesis 13:10, where the plain of the Jordan was *like the garden of the LORD* before it was ruined. Here the ruin is turned back.
- **v4 "instruction will go out from Me"** — *torah… tetse*, as in 2:3, *out of Zion shall go forth instruction*; there from Zion, here from the LORD Himself, and *a light for the peoples*, as the servant is *a light for the nations* (42:6; 49:6).
- **vv6, 8 "My deliverance will be forever"** — the heavens *vanish like smoke* and the earth wears out *like a garment*, the picture of 50:9; the LORD's deliverance and righteousness outlast them both. The two verses say it twice, in reverse order.
- **v9 "Awake, awake… who cut Rahab in pieces, who pierced the dragon"** — *Rahab* is the sea monster, a name also given to Egypt (30:7); *tannin* is *the dragon that is in the sea* of 27:1. "Pierced" — *mecholelet*, the verb of 53:5, *he was pierced for our transgressions*.
- **v10 "who dried up the sea… a way for the redeemed to cross over"** — the Sea of Reeds, told with *tehom rabbah*, *the great deep* whose fountains split open at the flood (Genesis 7:11). The arm that dried the sea once is called to wake and do it again.
- **v11 "The ransomed of the LORD will return"** — the same verse as 35:10, almost word for word, set here at the end of the cry *Awake*.
- **vv9, 17; 52:1 "Awake, awake… Rouse yourself, rouse yourself… Awake, awake"** — three doubled calls. The first is to the LORD's arm; the second and third turn the call back to Jerusalem, who must stand up from the cup and put on her own strength.
- **vv17, 22 "the cup of His wrath… the cup of reeling"** — *kos ha-tar'elah*, the cup that makes one stagger, as *wine to drink that makes us reel* (Psalm 60:3, *tar'elah*). Psalm 75:8 puts the cup in the LORD's hand, for *the wicked of the earth* to drain; here Jerusalem has drained it, and the LORD takes it from her hand to give to her tormentors.
- **v19 "by whom shall I comfort you?"** — *mi anachamekh*, "who — I will comfort you," the LORD's own question: there is no one in Jerusalem to comfort her (v18), and He names none. The Greek has *who will comfort you?*

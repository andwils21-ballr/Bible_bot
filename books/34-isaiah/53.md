# Isaiah 53

**1** Who has believed what we have heard,  
and to whom has the arm of the LORD been revealed?

**2** He grew up before Him like a young plant,  
and like a root out of dry ground;  
he had no form or majesty that we should look at him,  
and no appearance that we should desire him.

**3** He was despised and forsaken by men,  
a man of pains, and acquainted with sickness;  
and like one from whom people hide their faces  
he was despised, and we did not esteem him.

**4** Surely he carried our sicknesses  
and bore our pains;  
yet we considered him stricken,  
struck down by God, and afflicted.

**5** But he was pierced for our transgressions;  
he was crushed for our iniquities;  
the chastisement that brought us peace was on him,  
and by his wounds we are healed.

**6** All we like sheep have gone astray;  
we have turned, each one, to his own way;  
and the LORD has laid on him  
the iniquity of us all.

**7** He was oppressed, and he was afflicted,  
yet he did not open his mouth;  
like a lamb that is led to the slaughter,  
and like a sheep that is silent before its shearers,  
he did not open his mouth.

**8** By oppression and judgment he was taken away;  
and as for his generation, who considered  
that he was cut off from the land of the living,  
stricken for the transgression of my people?

**9** His grave was made with the wicked,  
and with a rich man in his death,  
although he had done no violence,  
and there was no deceit in his mouth.

**10** Yet the LORD was pleased to crush him;  
He made him sick.  
When his soul makes a guilt offering,  
he will see offspring; he will prolong his days,  
and the purpose of the LORD will prosper in his hand.

**11** Out of the anguish of his soul he will see and be satisfied;  
by his knowledge the righteous one, My servant,  
will make the many righteous,  
and he will bear their iniquities.

**12** Therefore I will give him a portion among the many,  
and he will divide the spoil with the strong,  
because he poured out his soul to death  
and was counted with the transgressors;  
yet he carried the sin of many,  
and intercedes for the transgressors.

## Notes

- **v1 "Who has believed what we have heard?"** — *shemu'atenu*, "our report," the thing heard. John gives the verse as the reason many did not believe in Jesus though they saw His signs (John 12:38), and Paul for those who did not obey the good news (Romans 10:16); both quote the Greek, which begins *Lord, who has believed…*
- **v2 "He grew up before Him like a young plant, and like a root"** — *yoneq*, a sucker, a shoot from the base of a plant; *shoresh*, "root," is the word of 11:1 and 11:10, *a branch from his roots*, *the root of Jesse*. *Lefanav*, "before him," does not say whose sight; the capital reads it as the LORD's, whose arm is named in v1.
- **vv3–4 "a man of pains, and acquainted with sickness… Surely he carried our sicknesses and bore our pains"** — *makh'ovot* and *choli* in v3 are the same two words in v4: the pains and sickness he knew were ours. *Nasa*, "carry," and *saval*, "bear," are the verbs of 46:4, where the LORD carries and bears His people. Matthew gives the verse as fulfilled when Jesus healed the sick: *He took our weaknesses and carried our diseases* (Matthew 8:17).
- **v5 "pierced… crushed"** — *mecholal*, the verb of 51:9, where the arm of the LORD *pierced the dragon*; *medukka*, from *dakka*, the verb of v10, *the LORD was pleased to crush him*. "By his wounds" — *ba-chavurato*, "by his wound" (singular, a welt or stripe). Peter: *by whose wound you were healed* (1 Peter 2:24).
- **vv6, 12 "the LORD has laid on him… and intercedes for the transgressors"** — *hifgia* and *yafgia*, the same verb (*paga*, to meet, to fall on, to plead). The LORD made the iniquity of all *fall on him*; he in turn *pleads* for those whose iniquity it was. The Gospel gives *he was counted with the transgressors* as fulfilled in Jesus: *And He was counted with the lawless* (Luke 22:37).
- **v7 "like a lamb that is led to the slaughter"** — the passage the Ethiopian, a eunuch and treasurer of the queen of the Ethiopians, was reading in his chariot on the road home from Jerusalem. He asked Philip, *About whom does the prophet say this, about himself or about someone else?*, and Philip, *beginning from this scripture*, told him the good news of Jesus (Acts 8:27–35).
- **vv8, 11 "cut off from the land of the living… he will bear their iniquities"** — *nigzar*, and *avonotam yisbol*: the day of atonement's goat *shall carry on itself all their guilt to a land cut off* (Leviticus 16:22, *avonotam*, *gezerah*). "Stricken for the transgression of my people" — *nega lamo*, "a blow to them"; *lamo* is usually plural, and as a poetic form it can be singular, *to him*. The Greek has *he was led to death*.
- **v9 "His grave was made with the wicked, and with a rich man in his death"** — *va-yitten*, "he gave," with no subject named; *be-motav*, "in his deaths," plural. Peter: *who committed no sin, nor was deceit found in His mouth* (1 Peter 2:22).
- **v10 "When his soul makes a guilt offering"** — *im tasim asham nafsho*. *Tasim* can be "she makes," with *nafsho*, "his soul," as subject, or "you make," *when you make his soul a guilt offering*. *Asham* is the guilt offering of Leviticus 5:6. "The LORD was pleased… the purpose of the LORD" — *chafets* and *chefets*, the verb and its noun, the purpose of 44:28.
- **v11 "he will see and be satisfied"** — the Hebrew has no object for *see*; the Greek has *to show him light*. "Will make the many righteous" — *yatsdiq*, the verb of 50:8, *He who declares me in the right*: the one declared in the right there declares many in the right here.

# Isaiah 57

**1** The righteous man perishes,  
and no one lays it to heart;  
men of loyal love are taken away,  
and no one understands  
that the righteous is taken away from the evil to come.

**2** He enters into peace;  
they rest in their beds,  
each who walks in uprightness.

**3** But you, come here,  
sons of a sorceress,  
offspring of the adulterer and the prostitute.

**4** Whom are you mocking?  
Against whom do you open wide your mouth  
and stick out your tongue?  
Are you not children of transgression,  
offspring of deceit,

**5** you who burn with lust among the oaks,  
under every green tree,  
who slaughter the children in the valleys,  
under the clefts of the rocks?

**6** Among the smooth stones of the valley is your portion;  
they, they are your lot;  
to them you have poured out a drink offering,  
you have brought a grain offering.  
Shall I relent over these things?

**7** On a high and lifted up mountain  
you have set your bed;  
there too you went up  
to offer sacrifice.

**8** Behind the door and the doorpost  
you have set your memorial;  
for, turning from Me, you uncovered your bed and went up;  
you made it wide;  
you made a covenant for yourself with them;  
you loved their bed;  
you gazed on a hand.

**9** You journeyed to Molech with oil  
and multiplied your perfumes;  
you sent your envoys far off,  
and sent them down even to Sheol.

**10** You were wearied with the length of your way,  
but you did not say, "It is hopeless";  
you found new life for your strength,  
so you did not grow faint.

**11** Whom did you dread and fear,  
that you lied,  
and did not remember Me,  
did not lay it to heart?  
Have I not been silent, and for a long time,  
so that you do not fear Me?

**12** I will declare your righteousness and your deeds,  
but they will not profit you.

**13** When you cry out,  
let your collection of idols deliver you!  
The wind will carry them all off;  
a breath will take them away.  
But whoever takes refuge in Me will possess the land  
and inherit My holy mountain.

**14** One will say,  
"Build up, build up, prepare the way;  
remove every obstacle from My people's way."

**15** For this is what He says, the One who is high and lifted up,  
who dwells forever, whose name is Holy:  
"I dwell in the high and holy place,  
and with the one who is crushed and lowly in spirit,  
to revive the spirit of the lowly  
and to revive the heart of the crushed.

**16** For I will not contend forever,  
nor will I always be angry;  
for the spirit would grow faint before Me,  
and the breath of those I made.

**17** Because of the iniquity of his gain I was angry,  
and I struck him;  
I hid My face and was angry;  
but he went on turning away  
in the way of his own heart.

**18** I have seen his ways, but I will heal him;  
I will lead him and restore comfort to him  
and to his mourners,

**19** creating the fruit of the lips.  
Peace, peace, to the far and to the near,"  
says the LORD,  
"and I will heal him."

**20** But the wicked are like the tossing sea,  
for it cannot be still,  
and its waters toss up mire and mud.

**21** "There is no peace," says my God, "for the wicked."

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "The righteous man perishes… He enters into peace"** — no one sees why the righteous die; the answer is that they are *taken away from the evil to come* (*ne'esaf*, "gathered," the verb of Abraham's death, *he was gathered to his people*, Genesis 25:8) and rest. "Men of loyal love" — *anshei chesed*.
- **v5 "you who burn with lust among the oaks"** — *ba-elim*, the *oaks* of 1:29, *the oaks that you desired*; the same letters are *elim*, "gods," and the Greek has *who call on idols under leafy trees*. "Who slaughter the children in the valleys" — the sacrifice of children that the law forbids, *You shall not give any of your children to pass over to Molech* (Leviticus 18:21).
- **v6 "Among the smooth stones of the valley is your portion"** — *be-chalqei nachal chelqekh*, "in the smooth things of the valley is your smooth share": *chelek*, "portion," and *chalaq*, "smooth," sound alike.
- **vv7, 15 "On a high and lifted up mountain you have set your bed… the One who is high and lifted up"** — *gavoah ve-nissa* and *ram ve-nissa*. She climbs a high and lifted up mountain to her lovers; the LORD who is high and lifted up (6:1; 52:13) comes down to the crushed.
- **v8 "Behind the door and the doorpost you have set your memorial"** — *mezuzah*, the doorpost on which Israel was told, *Write them on the doorposts of your house* (Deuteronomy 6:9). Behind it she set up a reminder of her own. "You gazed on a hand" — *yad chazit*: *yad*, "hand," is also a monument (56:5, *a monument and a name*); what she gazed on is not said more plainly.
- **v9 "You journeyed to Molech with oil"** — the letters are *mlk*. The Hebrew vowels read them *la-melekh*, "to the king"; the same letters spell *Molekh*, the god of child sacrifice (*You shall not give any of your children to pass over to Molech*, Leviticus 18:21), and the text reads them so: the children slaughtered in v5, and the envoys sent *down even to Sheol* in the next line. The Greek has no king or god here.
- **v13 "let your collection of idols deliver you!… a breath will take them away"** — *qibbutsayikh*, "your gatherings." *Hevel*, "breath," is the *vapor* of 49:4.
- **v14 "Build up, build up, prepare the way"** — *pannu derekh*, the words of 40:3, *In the wilderness prepare the way of the LORD*; there the way is for the LORD, here for His people.
- **v15 "with the one who is crushed and lowly in spirit… the heart of the crushed"** — *dakka*, *nidka'im*, the verb of 53:5 and 53:10, *he was crushed for our iniquities*. The LORD who dwells on high dwells also with the crushed.
- **vv19, 21 "Peace, peace, to the far and to the near… There is no peace… for the wicked"** — Paul says Christ *came and proclaimed peace to you who were far off and peace to those who were near* (Ephesians 2:17). The chapter ends with the line of 48:22, here *says my God*.

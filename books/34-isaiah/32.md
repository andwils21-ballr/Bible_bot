# Isaiah 32

**1** A king will reign in righteousness,  
and princes will rule in justice.

**2** Each will be like a hiding place from the wind,  
a shelter from the storm,  
like streams of water in a dry place,  
like the shade of a great rock in a weary land.

**3** Then the eyes of those who see will not be closed,  
and the ears of those who hear will listen.

**4** The heart of the rash will understand and know,  
and the tongue of the stammerers will hasten to speak clearly.

**5** The fool will no longer be called noble,  
nor the scoundrel be said to be generous.

**6** For the fool speaks folly,  
and his heart plans iniquity:  
to practice ungodliness,  
to speak error concerning the LORD,  
to leave the hungry empty,  
and to deprive the thirsty of drink.

**7** As for the scoundrel, his schemes are evil;  
he devises wicked plans  
to ruin the poor with lying words,  
even when the plea of the needy is right.

**8** But the noble plans noble things,  
and on noble things he stands.

## The Women at Ease

**9** Women at ease, rise up, hear my voice;  
complacent daughters, give ear to my speech.

**10** In little more than a year  
you will shudder, you complacent ones;  
for the vintage will fail;  
the harvest will not come.

**11** Tremble, you women at ease;  
shudder, you complacent ones;  
strip and make yourselves bare,  
and put sackcloth on your waist.

**12** Beat your breasts  
for the pleasant fields,  
for the fruitful vine,

**13** for the soil of my people  
growing up in thorns and briers,  
yes, for all the joyful houses  
in the exultant city.

**14** For the palace is forsaken,  
the crowded city deserted;  
the hill and the watchtower  
will be dens forever,  
a joy of wild donkeys,  
a pasture for flocks;

**15** until the Spirit is poured out on us from on high,  
and the wilderness becomes a fruitful field,  
and the fruitful field is regarded as a forest.

**16** Then justice will dwell in the wilderness,  
and righteousness abide in the fruitful field.

**17** The work of righteousness will be peace,  
and the result of righteousness, quietness and trust forever.

**18** My people will dwell in a peaceful home,  
in secure dwellings,  
and in quiet resting places.

**19** But hail will fall when the forest comes down,  
and the city will be brought low, utterly low.

**20** Blessed are you who sow beside all waters,  
who let the ox and the donkey range free.

## Notes

- **v3 "the eyes of those who see will not be closed"** — *lo tish'enah*, the verb of Isaiah's commission, *shut their eyes* (6:10, *hasha*). What was closed then is opened.
- **vv5–6 "The fool… the fool speaks folly"** — *naval*, the word that was a man's name: *Nabal is his name, and folly is with him* (1 Samuel 25:25). *Naval nevalah yedabber*, "the fool speaks folly," turns the noun into its own verb's object.
- **v7 "the scoundrel, his schemes"** — *kelai kelav*, the scoundrel and his tools, two words that sound alike. The read form *aniyyim*, "the poor," is followed.
- **vv9, 11, 18 "Women at ease… in quiet resting places"** — *sha'anannot*, "at ease," twice of the women whose ease is false, and once more in v18 of the people's true rest, *menuchot sha'anannot*: the same word, the false ease taken away and the true one given.
- **v12 "Beat your breasts for the pleasant fields"** — *al shadayim… al sedei chemed*, "breasts" and "fields," two words that sound alike.
- **v15 "until the Spirit is poured out on us from on high"** — *ruach mi-marom*, the LORD's Spirit, as in 44:3, *I will pour My Spirit on your seed*. Then the reversal of 29:17: wilderness to fruitful field, fruitful field to forest.
- **v17 "quietness and trust"** — *hashqet va-vetach*, the words of 30:15, *in quietness and trust will be your strength*, which the people refused. Here it is the fruit of righteousness.

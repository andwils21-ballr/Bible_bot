# Isaiah 28

**1** Woe to the proud crown of the drunkards of Ephraim,  
and to the fading flower of its glorious beauty,  
which is on the head of the rich valley,  
of those struck down by wine!

**2** The Lord has one who is mighty and strong:  
like a storm of hail, a destroying tempest,  
like a storm of mighty waters overflowing,  
he casts it down to the earth with his hand.

**3** The proud crown of the drunkards of Ephraim  
will be trampled underfoot;

**4** and the fading flower of its glorious beauty,  
which is on the head of the rich valley,  
will be like a first-ripe fig before the summer:  
whoever sees it  
swallows it as soon as it is in his hand.

**5** In that day the LORD of hosts will be  
a crown of glory  
and a diadem of beauty  
to the remnant of His people,

**6** and a spirit of justice  
to him who sits in judgment,  
and strength to those  
who turn back the battle at the gate.

**7** These also reel with wine  
and stagger with strong drink;  
priest and prophet reel with strong drink;  
they are swallowed up by wine;  
they stagger with strong drink;  
they reel in vision;  
they stumble in giving judgment.

**8** For all the tables are full of vomit and filth,  
with no place left.

**9** "Whom will he teach knowledge,  
and to whom will he explain the message?  
Those weaned from milk,  
those taken from the breast?

**10** For it is rule upon rule, rule upon rule,  
line upon line, line upon line,  
a little here, a little there."

**11** Indeed, with stammering lips  
and another tongue  
He will speak to this people,

**12** to whom He said,  
"This is rest; give rest to the weary;  
this is repose";  
yet they would not listen.

**13** So the word of the LORD will be to them  
rule upon rule, rule upon rule,  
line upon line, line upon line,  
a little here, a little there,  
so that they will go, and fall backward,  
and be broken, and snared, and taken.

## A Covenant with Death

**14** Therefore hear the word of the LORD, you scoffers,  
who rule this people in Jerusalem!

**15** Because you have said,  
"We have made a covenant with death,  
and with Sheol we have an agreement;  
when the overwhelming scourge passes through,  
it will not come to us;  
for we have made lies our refuge,  
and in falsehood we have taken shelter";

**16** therefore this is what the Lord GOD says:  
"I am laying in Zion a stone,  
a tested stone,  
a precious cornerstone, a sure foundation:  
whoever believes will not be in haste.

**17** I will make justice the line,  
and righteousness the plumb line;  
hail will sweep away the refuge of lies,  
and waters will overflow the hiding place."

**18** Then your covenant with death will be wiped out,  
and your agreement with Sheol will not stand;  
when the overwhelming scourge passes through,  
you will be trampled down by it.

**19** As often as it passes through it will take you;  
for morning by morning it will pass through,  
by day and by night;  
and it will be sheer terror  
to understand the message.

**20** For the bed is too short to stretch out on,  
and the blanket too narrow to wrap oneself in.

**21** For the LORD will rise up as at Mount Perazim;  
as in the Valley of Gibeon He will be stirred up,  
to do His deed — strange is His deed! —  
and to work His work — alien is His work!

**22** So now, do not scoff,  
or your bonds will be made stronger;  
for I have heard a full destruction decreed  
by the Lord GOD of hosts  
on the whole earth.

## The Farmer

**23** Give ear, and hear my voice;  
listen, and hear my speech.

**24** Does the plowman plow all day for sowing?  
Does he keep breaking up and harrowing his ground?

**25** When he has leveled its surface,  
does he not scatter dill and sow cumin,  
and put in wheat in rows  
and barley in its plot,  
and spelt as its border?

**26** His God instructs him in the right way;  
He teaches him.

**27** Dill is not threshed with a threshing sledge,  
nor is a cart wheel rolled over cumin;  
dill is beaten out with a stick,  
and cumin with a rod.

**28** Grain for bread is crushed,  
but one does not thresh it forever;  
when he drives his cart wheel and his horses over it,  
he does not crush it.

**29** This also comes from the LORD of hosts;  
He is wonderful in counsel  
and great in wisdom.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "the proud crown of the drunkards of Ephraim… on the head of the rich valley"** — Samaria, Ephraim's capital, a city built on a hill (*He bought the hill of Samaria from Shemer*, 1 Kings 16:24): the crown on the valley's head. *Halumei yayin*, "struck down by wine," is the phrase the note on 16:8 cites.
- **vv1, 5 "the proud crown… a crown of glory"** — *ateret*, twice: the crown of Ephraim's drunken pride fades, and the LORD Himself will be the crown of His remnant.
- **vv9–13 "rule upon rule, line upon line"** — *tsav la-tsav… qav la-qav*. *Qav* is a measuring line (v17; 18:2), and *tsav* is from the root of *command*; but the syllables are short and doubled, and v9 sets them beside infants just weaned, so the mockers may be imitating a child's babble or a teacher's drill. The LORD answers in kind: since they will not hear plain words, He will speak *with stammering lips and another tongue*, the speech of a foreign army. Paul quotes v11 of speaking in tongues (1 Corinthians 14:21). The Greek has *affliction upon affliction, hope upon hope*.
- **v13 "fall backward, and be broken, and snared, and taken"** — the verbs of 8:15, *they shall fall and be broken; they shall be snared and taken*, of those who stumble over the LORD.
- **v15 "a covenant with death… an agreement with Sheol"** — *chozeh*, literally "a seer, a vision," here a pact; v18 has *chazut*, from the same root. The rulers have bought safety with lies.
- **v16 "I am laying in Zion a stone… whoever believes will not be in haste"** — *ha-ma'amin lo yachish*, "will not hurry, will not flee in panic." The Greek has *will not be put to shame*, and Peter and Paul quote that: *whoever believes in Him will not be put to shame* (1 Peter 2:6; Romans 9:33). Paul joins it to *a stone of stumbling* from 8:14.
- **vv17–18 "justice the line… your covenant with death will be wiped out"** — *qav*, the word the mockers babbled in v10, is now the builder's line the LORD lays. *Kuppar*, "wiped out," is the verb of atonement (6:7; 22:14; 27:9): the same covering that takes away sin cancels their pact.
- **v21 "as at Mount Perazim… the Valley of Gibeon… strange is His deed"** — the places where the LORD fought for Israel: *The LORD has broken through my enemies before me* (2 Samuel 5:20), and at Gibeon, where He threw the Amorites into panic before Israel (Joshua 10:10). Now He rises the same way against His own people: *zar*, "strange," and *nokhriyyah*, "foreign," the words for strangers and foreigners.
- **v22 "a full destruction decreed… on the whole earth"** — *kalah ve-necheratsah*, the words of 10:23.
- **vv26, 29 "His God instructs him… He is wonderful in counsel"** — *hifli etsah*, *pele* and *etsah*, the two words of 9:6, *Wonderful Counselor*, as in 25:1. The farmer's sense of when to plow and how to thresh each crop is called teaching from God (*yorennu*, the root of *torah*).

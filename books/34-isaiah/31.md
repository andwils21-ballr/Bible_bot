# Isaiah 31

**1** Woe to those who go down to Egypt for help,  
who rely on horses,  
who trust in chariots because they are many,  
and in horsemen because they are very strong,  
but do not look to the Holy One of Israel  
or seek the LORD!

**2** Yet He too is wise, and brings disaster;  
He does not take back His words;  
He will rise against the house of evildoers,  
and against the helpers of those who work iniquity.

**3** The Egyptians are man, and not God;  
their horses are flesh, and not spirit.  
When the LORD stretches out His hand,  
the helper will stumble, and the one helped will fall,  
and they will all perish together.

**4** For thus the LORD said to me:  
"As a lion or a young lion growls over its prey,  
and when a band of shepherds is called out against it,  
it is not terrified by their shouting  
or daunted by their noise,  
so the LORD of hosts will come down  
to fight upon Mount Zion and upon its hill.

**5** Like birds hovering,  
so the LORD of hosts will shield Jerusalem;  
He will shield and rescue it;  
He will pass over and deliver it."

**6** Return to Him from whom you have so deeply turned away, sons of Israel.

**7** For in that day each of you will reject his idols of silver and his idols of gold, which your hands have made for you in sin.

**8** "Assyria will fall by a sword, not of man,  
and a sword, not of a human, will devour him;  
he will flee from the sword,  
and his young men will be put to forced labor.

**9** His rock will pass away in terror,  
and his commanders will be dismayed at the signal,"  
declares the LORD,  
whose fire is in Zion,  
and whose furnace is in Jerusalem.

## Notes

- **v1 "who go down to Egypt… who rely on horses"** — the law forbade the king to *multiply horses for himself* or to *make the people return to Egypt* to get them (Deuteronomy 17:16). The note on 2:7 counts the horses among the three things forbidden.
- **v3 "The Egyptians are man, and not God; their horses are flesh, and not spirit"** — *adam* against *El*, *basar* against *ruach*: flesh is what man is, and spirit what God gives and takes back (*My breath shall not remain in the human forever, in that he also is flesh*, Genesis 6:3).
- **v4 "to fight upon Mount Zion"** — *litsbo al*. In 29:7–8 the same words are used of the nations that *fight against* Mount Zion, and *al* can be "against" as well as "upon." Here v5 settles which: the LORD comes down as the lion that will not give up its prey, to shield the city, not to take it.
- **v5 "He will shield and rescue it; He will pass over and deliver it"** — *pasoach*, the verb of the Passover, and *hitsil*, "rescue," the two verbs of Exodus 12:27, *the LORD, who passed over the houses of the sons of Israel in Egypt when He struck Egypt, and He rescued our houses*. Jerusalem is spared as Israel's houses were.
- **v8 "a sword, not of man"** — the city is delivered by no army; Isaiah tells the night it happened: *the messenger of the LORD went out and struck down a hundred and eighty-five thousand in the camp of Assyria* (37:36).
- **v9 "whose fire is in Zion, and whose furnace is in Jerusalem"** — the altar fire of *Ariel*, the hearth-city of 29:1–2, here a furnace for the enemy.

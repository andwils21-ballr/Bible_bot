# Isaiah 59

**1** The LORD's hand is not too short to save,  
nor His ear too heavy to hear;

**2** but your iniquities have made a separation  
between you and your God,  
and your sins have hidden His face from you,  
so that He does not hear.

**3** For your hands are defiled with blood,  
and your fingers with iniquity;  
your lips have spoken lies;  
your tongue mutters wickedness.

**4** No one calls for justice,  
and no one goes to law honestly;  
they trust in emptiness and speak falsehood;  
they conceive trouble and give birth to iniquity.

**5** They hatch adders' eggs  
and weave the spider's web;  
whoever eats their eggs dies,  
and from one that is crushed a viper breaks out.

**6** Their webs will not become clothing;  
they will not cover themselves with what they make;  
their works are works of iniquity,  
and deeds of violence are in their hands.

**7** Their feet run to evil,  
and they hurry to shed innocent blood;  
their thoughts are thoughts of iniquity;  
devastation and destruction are in their highways.

**8** The way of peace they do not know,  
and there is no justice in their paths;  
they have made their roads crooked;  
no one who walks in them knows peace.

**9** Therefore justice is far from us,  
and righteousness does not overtake us;  
we hope for light, and there is darkness;  
for brightness, and we walk in gloom.

**10** We grope for the wall like the blind;  
we grope like those who have no eyes;  
we stumble at noon as in the twilight;  
among the strong we are like the dead.

**11** We all growl like bears;  
we moan and moan like doves;  
we hope for justice, and there is none;  
for deliverance, and it is far from us.

**12** For our transgressions are many before You,  
and our sins testify against us;  
for our transgressions are with us,  
and we know our iniquities:

**13** transgressing, and denying the LORD,  
and turning back from following our God,  
speaking oppression and revolt,  
conceiving and uttering from the heart words of falsehood.

**14** Justice is turned back,  
and righteousness stands far off;  
for truth has stumbled in the public square,  
and uprightness cannot enter.

**15** Truth is lacking,  
and whoever turns from evil makes himself a prey.  
The LORD saw it, and it was evil in His eyes  
that there was no justice.

**16** He saw that there was no man,  
and was appalled that there was no one to intercede;  
then His own arm brought Him deliverance,  
and His righteousness upheld Him.

**17** He put on righteousness as a breastplate,  
and a helmet of deliverance on His head;  
He put on garments of vengeance for clothing,  
and wrapped Himself in zeal as a cloak.

**18** According to their deeds, so will He repay:  
wrath to His adversaries, recompense to His enemies;  
to the coastlands He will repay recompense.

**19** So they will fear the name of the LORD from the west,  
and His glory from the rising of the sun;  
for He will come like a rushing stream  
that the Spirit of the LORD drives on.

**20** "A Redeemer will come to Zion,  
to those in Jacob who turn from transgression,"  
declares the LORD.

**21** "As for Me, this is My covenant with them," says the LORD: "My Spirit that is on you, and My words that I have put in your mouth, will not depart from your mouth, or from the mouth of your offspring, or from the mouth of your offspring's offspring," says the LORD, "from now on and forever."

## Notes

- **v10 "among the strong we are like the dead"** — *ba-ashmannim*, a word found only here, and its sense is not certain; "the stout, the strong" sets it against *the dead*.
- **v19 "like a rushing stream that the Spirit of the LORD drives on"** — *ki yavo kha-nahar tsar, ruach YHWH nosesah vo*. *Tsar* can be "narrow, rushing," said of the stream, or "an adversary": *when the adversary comes like a river, the Spirit of the LORD will lift a banner against him*, reading *nosesah* from *nes*, "banner." The Greek has *the wrath of the Lord will come like a violent river*.

# Isaiah 16

**1** Send the lamb to the ruler of the land,  
from Sela, across the wilderness,  
to the mount of the daughter of Zion.

**2** Like fluttering birds,  
like a nest scattered,  
so are the daughters of Moab  
at the fords of the Arnon.

**3** "Give counsel;  
grant a ruling;  
make your shade like night  
at the height of noon;  
hide the outcasts;  
do not betray the fugitive.

**4** Let the outcasts of Moab live as guests among you;  
be a shelter to them from the destroyer."

When the oppressor is no more,  
and destruction has ceased,  
and the one who tramples has vanished from the land,

**5** then a throne will be established in loyal love,  
and on it will sit in faithfulness,  
in the tent of David,  
one who judges and seeks justice  
and is swift to do righteousness.

**6** We have heard of the pride of Moab —  
how very proud he is! —  
of his arrogance, his pride, and his fury;  
his boasting is empty.

**7** So Moab wails for Moab;  
all of them wail.  
For the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth you will moan,  
utterly stricken.

**8** For the fields of Heshbon languish,  
and the vine of Sibmah;  
the lords of the nations have struck down its choice branches,  
which reached as far as Jazer  
and strayed into the wilderness;  
its shoots spread out  
and crossed over the sea.

**9** So I weep with the weeping of Jazer  
for the vine of Sibmah;  
I drench you with my tears,  
Heshbon and Elealeh;  
for on your summer fruit and on your harvest  
the shout has fallen.

**10** Joy and gladness are taken away from the fruitful field;  
in the vineyards no one sings,  
no one shouts for joy;  
no one treads out wine in the presses;  
I have put an end to the shout.

**11** So my inner parts moan for Moab like a harp,  
and my inmost being for Kir-heres.

**12** And it shall come to pass, when Moab presents himself, when he wears himself out on the high place and comes to his sanctuary to pray, that he will not prevail.

**13** This is the word that the LORD spoke concerning Moab long ago.

**14** But now the LORD has spoken, saying: "Within three years, like the years of a hired man, the glory of Moab will be brought into contempt, with all his great multitude, and the remnant will be very small and feeble."

## Notes

- **v1 "Send the lamb to the ruler of the land"** — *kar*, the tribute Moab once paid in lambs: *Mesha king of Moab was a sheep breeder, and he used to pay the king of Israel a hundred thousand lambs* (2 Kings 3:4, *karim*). Moab's refugees are told to send it again, now to Zion.
- **vv3–4 "Let the outcasts of Moab live as guests among you"** — *nidchei Mo'av*, as the Greek reads it: *the fugitives of Moab will live as guests with you*. The Hebrew vowels read the same letters *nidachai*, "my outcasts," with *Moab* as the one spoken to: *Let my outcasts live as guests among you; Moab, be a shelter to them*. Read that way, Israel's outcasts ask Moab for shelter. Read the Greek's way, Moab's refugees (v2) ask the daughter of Zion (v1): most of the commands in v3 and the *you* of v4 are feminine, and the answer in v5 is a throne in Zion; only *be* in v4 is masculine. "Live as guests" — *yaguru*, from *gur*. Moab once sheltered David's family: *Please let my father and my mother come and stay with you* (1 Samuel 22:3).
- **v5 "a throne will be established in loyal love… in the tent of David"** — *chesed* and *emet*, loyal love and faithfulness, the throne of 9:7, *on the throne of David… with justice and with righteousness*. The answer to the refugees is a king in Zion who judges rightly.
- **v6 "the pride of Moab… his arrogance, his pride"** — *ge'on*, *ge*, *ga'avato*, *ge'ono*: four words from one root, *ga'ah*, "to rise up." Jeremiah 48:29 has the same line and adds more: *his loftiness, his pride, his arrogance, and the haughtiness of his heart*.
- **v7 "the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth"** — *ashishei*, pressed cakes of raisins. Jeremiah's version of the line has *the men of Kir-heres* (Jeremiah 48:31), and v11 here has the shorter name too. In v11 *qirbi*, "my inmost being," chimes with *Qir*.
- **v8 "the lords of the nations have struck down its choice branches"** — *ba'alei goyim halemu seruqqeha*. The words can also be read the other way round: *its choice vines struck down the lords of the nations*, that is, its wine laid them low, as *halumei yayin*, "those struck down by wine," says of Ephraim's drunkards (28:1). The Greek reads it as rendered: the nations trample its vines.
- **vv9–10 "the shout has fallen… I have put an end to the shout"** — *heidad*, the shout of the men treading grapes (*a shout like those who tread grapes*, Jeremiah 25:30). In v10 the harvest shout is silenced; in v9 a shout falls on the harvest, the enemy's.
- **v14 "like the years of a hired man"** — counted to the day, no more and no less: in the law a sold man's time *shall be counted like the days of a hired man* (Leviticus 25:50). The same measure is set for Kedar in 21:16.

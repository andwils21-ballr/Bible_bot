# Isaiah 30

**1** "Woe to the rebellious sons," declares the LORD,  
"who carry out a plan, but not Mine,  
and make an alliance, but not of My Spirit,  
so as to add sin to sin;

**2** who set out to go down to Egypt  
without asking My mouth,  
to take refuge in the protection of Pharaoh  
and to shelter in the shadow of Egypt!

**3** The protection of Pharaoh will be your shame,  
and the shelter in the shadow of Egypt your disgrace.

**4** For though his princes are at Zoan  
and his envoys reach Hanes,

**5** everyone will be put to shame  
by a people that cannot profit them,  
that brings no help, no profit,  
but shame and also reproach."

**6** The oracle concerning the beasts of the Negev.

Through a land of trouble and anguish,  
of lioness and lion,  
of adder and flying fiery serpent,  
they carry their riches on the backs of donkeys  
and their treasures on the humps of camels  
to a people that cannot profit them.

**7** Egypt's help is vapor and emptiness;  
therefore I have called her  
"Rahab who sits still."

**8** Now go, write it before them on a tablet,  
and inscribe it in a book,  
that it may be for the time to come,  
forever and ever.

**9** For they are a rebellious people,  
lying sons,  
sons who will not hear  
the instruction of the LORD;

**10** who say to the seers, "Do not see,"  
and to the visionaries, "Do not envision for us what is right;  
speak to us smooth things;  
envision illusions;

**11** leave the way;  
turn aside from the path;  
make the Holy One of Israel cease from before us."

**12** Therefore thus says the Holy One of Israel:  
"Because you despise this word,  
and trust in oppression and crookedness,  
and lean on them,

**13** therefore this iniquity will be to you  
like a breach in a high wall, bulging out,  
about to fall,  
whose crash comes suddenly, in an instant;

**14** and its breaking is like the breaking of a potter's jar,  
smashed without mercy,  
so that among its pieces not a shard is found  
to take fire from the hearth,  
or to scoop water from the cistern."

**15** For thus said the Lord GOD, the Holy One of Israel:  
"In returning and rest you will be saved;  
in quietness and trust will be your strength."  
But you were not willing,

**16** and you said, "No! We will flee on horses";  
therefore you will flee!  
"We will ride on swift steeds";  
therefore your pursuers will be swift!

**17** A thousand will flee at the threat of one;  
at the threat of five you will flee,  
until you are left like a pole on the top of a mountain,  
like a signal on a hill.

## The LORD Waits to Be Gracious

**18** Therefore the LORD waits to be gracious to you,  
and therefore He rises to show you compassion.  
For the LORD is a God of justice;  
blessed are all who wait for Him.

**19** For a people will dwell in Zion, in Jerusalem; you will weep no more. He will surely be gracious to you at the sound of your cry; as soon as He hears it, He will answer you.

**20** Though the Lord gives you the bread of adversity and the water of affliction, your Teacher will hide Himself no more, but your eyes will see your Teacher.

**21** Your ears will hear a word behind you, saying, "This is the way; walk in it," when you turn to the right or when you turn to the left.

**22** You will defile your silver-covered idols and your gold-plated images; you will scatter them like an unclean thing; "Out!" you will say to it.

**23** He will give rain for the seed with which you sow the ground, and bread, the produce of the ground, rich and plentiful. In that day your livestock will graze in wide pastures,

**24** and the oxen and the donkeys that work the ground will eat seasoned fodder, winnowed with shovel and fork.

**25** On every high mountain and every lofty hill there will be channels running with water, in the day of the great slaughter, when the towers fall.

**26** The light of the moon will be like the light of the sun, and the light of the sun will be sevenfold, like the light of seven days, in the day when the LORD binds up the brokenness of His people and heals the wounds His blow inflicted.

## The Name of the LORD Comes from Afar

**27** The name of the LORD is coming from afar,  
burning with His anger, in thick rising smoke;  
His lips are full of fury,  
and His tongue is like a devouring fire;

**28** His breath is like an overflowing stream  
that reaches up to the neck,  
to sift the nations with the sieve of destruction,  
and to put on the jaws of the peoples  
a bridle that leads them astray.

**29** You will have a song as in the night  
when a holy feast is kept,  
and gladness of heart, as when one sets out to the sound of the flute  
to go to the mountain of the LORD,  
to the Rock of Israel.

**30** The LORD will make His majestic voice heard,  
and show the descending blow of His arm,  
in furious anger and the flame of a devouring fire,  
with cloudburst and storm and hailstones.

**31** At the voice of the LORD Assyria will be shattered,  
when He strikes with the rod.

**32** Every stroke of the appointed staff  
that the LORD lays on him  
will be to the sound of tambourines and harps;  
in battles of brandishing He will fight against them.

**33** For a Topheth has long been prepared;  
it too is made ready for the king;  
its pit made deep and wide,  
its pyre with fire and wood in plenty;  
the breath of the LORD, like a stream of sulfur,  
kindles it.

## Notes

- **v1 "the rebellious sons"** — *banim sorerim*, the word of the law's *stubborn and rebellious son* who *does not listen to the voice of his father* (Deuteronomy 21:18). "An alliance" — *linsokh massekhah*, literally "to pour a pouring," a libation poured to seal a pact, or "to weave a web"; either way, made *not of My Spirit*.
- **v7 "Rahab who sits still"** — *Rahav*, the sea monster of the old poems, *You crushed Rahab like one who is slain* (Psalm 89:10), used as a name for Egypt. The great monster sits and does nothing. *Hevel*, "vapor," is the word that was Abel's name.
- **vv11–12 "make the Holy One of Israel cease from before us… Therefore thus says the Holy One of Israel"** — they ask to hear no more of Him, and the next word comes in that very name.
- **v15 "In returning and rest you will be saved"** — *be-shuvah va-nachat*, from *shuv*, "return," the verb in the name of Isaiah's son Shear-jashub, *a remnant will return* (7:3; 10:21). The same promise of quiet trust was given to Ahaz (7:4, *Take care and be calm; do not fear*).
- **vv16–17 "We will flee on horses; therefore you will flee… A thousand will flee at the threat of one"** — *nanus… tenusun*, *qal… yiqqallu*: they get exactly what they chose, turned on them. The Song of Moses asked *How could one chase a thousand… unless their Rock had sold them?* (Deuteronomy 32:30); here it has happened.
- **vv20–23 "your Teacher… He will give rain"** — *moreikha*, "your Teacher," with a singular verb (*will hide Himself*): the LORD, hidden in the adversity, is seen. *Moreh* also means "the early rain," and Joel uses it both ways in one verse: *He has given you the early rain (ha-moreh) for righteousness* (Joel 2:23). Here the Teacher is promised, and then the rain (v23). The Greek reads differently: *those who lead you astray will no longer come near you*.
- **v28 "an overflowing stream that reaches up to the neck"** — the words used of Assyria's flood in 8:8, *it will reach up to the neck*. Now the flood is the LORD's breath, and it comes against the nations. "A bridle" — what the LORD says to Assyria's king: *I will put My hook in your nose and My bit in your lips* (37:29).
- **v31 "when He strikes with the rod"** — *ba-shevet*, the word of 10:5, *Woe to Assyria, the rod of My anger*. The rod is turned on Assyria.
- **v33 "a Topheth… for the king"** — *Topheth* was the burning place *in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom* where children were made to *pass through the fire to Molech* (2 Kings 23:10). *La-melekh*, "for the king," is spelled with the letters of *Molech*. The valley's Hebrew name, *Ge Hinnom*, is the source of *Gehenna*, the Gospels' word for the place of fire (Matthew 5:22).

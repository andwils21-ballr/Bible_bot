# Isaiah 6

**1** In the year that King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord sitting on a throne, high and lifted up, and the hem of His robe filled the temple.

**2** Seraphim stood above Him; each had six wings: with two he covered his face, with two he covered his feet, and with two he flew.

**3** One called to another and said:

"Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of hosts;  
the whole earth is full of His glory."

**4** The posts of the thresholds shook at the voice of the one who called, and the house was filled with smoke.

**5** I said: "Woe is me! For I am ruined, because I am a man of unclean lips, and I live among a people of unclean lips; for my eyes have seen the King, the LORD of hosts."

**6** Then one of the seraphim flew to me, with a burning coal in his hand that he had taken with tongs from the altar.

**7** He touched my mouth with it and said: "This has touched your lips; your iniquity is taken away, and your sin is atoned for."

**8** Then I heard the voice of the Lord saying: "Whom shall I send, and who will go for us?" I said: "Here I am; send me."

**9** He said: "Go, and say to this people:

'Keep on listening, but do not understand;  
keep on looking, but do not perceive.'

**10** Make the heart of this people fat,  
make their ears heavy,  
and shut their eyes;  
otherwise they might see with their eyes,  
and hear with their ears,  
and understand with their heart,  
and turn and be healed."

**11** Then I said, "How long, Lord?" He said:

"Until cities lie waste without inhabitant,  
and houses without people,  
and the land is left a desolate waste,

**12** and the LORD has sent the people far away,  
and the forsaken places are many in the middle of the land.

**13** Even if a tenth remains in it,  
it will be burned again,  
like a terebinth or an oak  
whose stump remains when it is felled.  
The holy seed is its stump."

## Notes

- **v1 "In the year that King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord"** — *Adonai*, "the Lord." The king of Judah dies, and Isaiah sees the King (v5). John says of this vision, *Isaiah said these things because he saw His glory and spoke about Him* (John 12:41, *eiden tēn doxan autou*).
- **v2 "Seraphim"** — *serafim*, from *saraf*, "to burn": the "burning ones." The same word names the *fiery serpents* of the wilderness (Numbers 21:6) and the bronze serpent made in their likeness.
- **v3 "Holy, holy, holy"** — *qadosh qadosh qadosh*: Hebrew repeats a word to make a superlative, and nowhere else in the Hebrew Bible is *holy* said three times; the living creatures of Revelation say it again (*hagios hagios hagios*, Revelation 4:8). It is the source of the title Isaiah gives God throughout the book, *the Holy One of Israel* (1:4).
- **v5 "For I am ruined"** — *nidmeiti*, from a root meaning both "be silenced" and "be cut off." The man whose lips are unclean is struck silent before the King.
- **v7 "your sin is atoned for"** — *tekhuppar*, the verb of the altar's atonement (*kipper*), and the coal comes from the altar. The lips that made him ruined are where he is cleansed.
- **v8 "who will go for us?"** — the plural, as in *Let us make humankind in our image* (Genesis 1:26); and the answer is Abraham's, *Here I am* (*hinneni*, Genesis 22:1).
- **vv9–10 "Keep on listening, but do not understand"** — *shim'u shamoa… re'u ra'o*, each verb doubled. Jesus quotes these lines of His hearers (Matthew 13:14–15), and John and Paul quote them again (John 12:40; Acts 28:26–27). "Otherwise they might… turn and be healed" — *pen*, the word usually given as "lest."
- **v13 "The holy seed is its stump"** — *zera qodesh matsavtah*: what is left after the felling is a stump, and the stump is a seed. The Greek of Isaiah ends the verse without this line.

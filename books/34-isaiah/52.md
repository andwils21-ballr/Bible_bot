# Isaiah 52

**1** Awake, awake,  
put on your strength, Zion;  
put on your beautiful garments,  
Jerusalem, the holy city;  
for the uncircumcised and the unclean  
will no longer enter you.

**2** Shake yourself from the dust,  
rise up, sit enthroned, Jerusalem;  
loose the bonds from your neck,  
captive daughter of Zion.

**3** For thus says the LORD: "You were sold for nothing, and you will be redeemed without money."

**4** For thus says the Lord GOD: "My people went down at first into Egypt to live there as guests, and Assyria oppressed them for nothing.

**5** So now, what do I have here," declares the LORD, "seeing that My people are taken away for nothing? Their rulers howl," declares the LORD, "and continually, all day long, My name is despised.

**6** Therefore My people will know My name; therefore in that day they will know that I am He who speaks: here I am."

**7** How beautiful on the mountains  
are the feet of him who brings good news,  
who proclaims peace,  
who brings good news of good things,  
who proclaims deliverance,  
who says to Zion, "Your God reigns!"

**8** The voice of your watchmen!  
They lift up their voice;  
together they sing for joy;  
for eye to eye they see  
the LORD return to Zion.

**9** Break out together into singing,  
you waste places of Jerusalem;  
for the LORD has comforted His people;  
He has redeemed Jerusalem.

**10** The LORD has bared His holy arm  
before the eyes of all the nations,  
and all the ends of the earth will see  
the deliverance of our God.

**11** Depart, depart, go out from there;  
touch nothing unclean;  
go out from the middle of her;  
purify yourselves,  
you who carry the vessels of the LORD.

**12** For you will not go out in haste,  
nor will you go in flight;  
for the LORD will go before you,  
and the God of Israel will be your rear guard.

## My Servant

**13** My servant will act wisely;  
he will rise high and be lifted up,  
and be very exalted.

**14** As many were appalled at you —  
so marred was his appearance, beyond that of a man,  
and his form beyond the sons of men —

**15** so many nations will marvel at him;  
kings will shut their mouths because of him;  
for what they had not been told they will see,  
and what they had not heard they will understand.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "Shake yourself from the dust, rise up, sit enthroned… captive daughter of Zion"** — the reverse of Babylon's sentence, *Come down and sit in the dust… sit on the ground without a throne* (47:1). Zion rises from the dust to the seat Babylon lost. *Shevi*, "sit," and *sheviyah*, "captive," sound alike: the captive is told to sit enthroned.
- **v3 "You were sold for nothing, and you will be redeemed without money"** — *chinnam*, "for nothing," as in 50:1: no creditor was paid when they were sold, and no money will be paid to buy them back. Cyrus sets the exiles free *not for price or reward* (45:13).
- **v4 "to live there as guests"** — *la-gur*, the word of Jacob's sons before Pharaoh, *We have come to live in the land as guests* (Genesis 47:4). Israel went down to Egypt by choice and was oppressed; Assyria took them *for nothing*, *be-efes*, with no cause.
- **v5 "what do I have here"** — the read form *mah li*, "what is it to Me"; the written form is *mi li*, "who do I have here." "My name is despised" — the Greek has *because of you My name is blasphemed among the nations*, and Paul quotes that form against those who teach the law and break it: *the name of God is blasphemed among the nations because of you* (Romans 2:24).
- **v7 "the feet of him who brings good news"** — *mevasser*, the word of 40:9, *Zion, herald of good news*; there Zion carries the news, here a runner brings it to her. Paul quotes the line for those sent to preach: *How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news!* (Romans 10:15). "Your God reigns" — *malakh Elohayikh*.
- **v8 "eye to eye they see the LORD return to Zion"** — *ayin be-ayin*, the watchmen on the walls see His return face to face.
- **v11 "touch nothing unclean… you who carry the vessels of the LORD"** — *the vessels of the house of the LORD* that Cyrus brought out of the house of Babylon's gods (Ezra 1:7). Paul joins the line to other verses: *Come out from among them and be separate, says the Lord, and touch nothing unclean* (2 Corinthians 6:17).
- **v12 "you will not go out in haste"** — *be-chippazon*, the word of the first Passover, *You shall eat it in haste* (Exodus 12:11). This exodus is not a flight; the LORD goes before them and behind them, as at the sea *the pillar of cloud moved from before them and stood behind them* (Exodus 14:19).
- **v13 "he will rise high and be lifted up"** — *yarum ve-nissa*, the words of 6:1, *the Lord sitting on a throne, high and lifted up*, and of 33:10, *now I will rise high; now I will be lifted up*. Words used of the LORD are said of His servant.
- **v15 "so many nations will marvel at him"** — the reading of the Greek, which answers *as many were appalled* in v14 and the kings who shut their mouths. The Hebrew is *yazzeh*, the verb of the priest who *sprinkles* the blood before the LORD (Leviticus 4:6; 16:14): *so he will sprinkle many nations*. Paul quotes the end of the verse from the Greek: *those who were not told about him will see* (Romans 15:21).

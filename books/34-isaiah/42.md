# Isaiah 42

**1** Here is My servant, whom I uphold,  
My chosen one, in whom My soul delights;  
I have put My Spirit on him;  
he will bring forth justice to the nations.

**2** He will not cry out or lift up his voice,  
or make it heard in the street;

**3** a crushed reed he will not break,  
and a dimly burning wick he will not quench;  
he will faithfully bring forth justice.

**4** He will not grow dim or be crushed  
until he has set justice in the earth;  
and the coastlands wait for his instruction.

**5** Thus says God, the LORD,  
who created the heavens and stretched them out,  
who spread out the earth and what comes from it,  
who gives breath to the people on it  
and spirit to those who walk in it:

**6** "I, the LORD, have called you in righteousness;  
I will take you by the hand and keep you;  
I will give you as a covenant for the people,  
a light for the nations,

**7** to open the eyes that are blind,  
to bring out prisoners from the dungeon,  
from the prison those who sit in darkness.

**8** I am the LORD; that is My name;  
My glory I give to no other,  
nor My praise to carved idols.

**9** The former things have come to pass,  
and new things I now declare;  
before they spring up  
I tell you of them."

## A New Song

**10** Sing to the LORD a new song,  
His praise from the end of the earth,  
you who go down to the sea, and all that fills it,  
the coastlands and their inhabitants.

**11** Let the wilderness and its cities lift up their voice,  
the villages where Kedar lives;  
let the inhabitants of Sela sing for joy;  
let them shout from the tops of the mountains.

**12** Let them give glory to the LORD,  
and declare His praise in the coastlands.

**13** The LORD goes out like a mighty man;  
like a man of war He stirs up His zeal;  
He shouts, He raises the battle cry;  
He shows Himself mighty against His enemies.

**14** For a long time I have held My peace;  
I have kept still and restrained Myself;  
now I will cry out like a woman in labor;  
I will gasp and pant together.

**15** I will lay waste mountains and hills,  
and dry up all their green growth;  
I will turn rivers into dry land,  
and dry up the pools.

**16** I will lead the blind by a way they do not know;  
in paths they do not know I will guide them.  
I will turn the darkness before them into light,  
the rough places into level ground.  
These are the things I will do,  
and I will not forsake them.

**17** They will be turned back and utterly put to shame,  
those who trust in carved idols,  
who say to metal images,  
"You are our gods."

## The Blind Servant

**18** Hear, you deaf;  
and look, you blind, that you may see!

**19** Who is blind but My servant,  
or deaf like My messenger whom I send?  
Who is blind like the one at peace with Me,  
or blind like the servant of the LORD?

**20** You see many things, but do not keep them;  
ears are open, but no one hears.

**21** The LORD was pleased, for the sake of His righteousness,  
to make His instruction great and glorious.

**22** But this is a people plundered and looted;  
they are all trapped in holes  
and hidden in prisons;  
they have become plunder with no one to rescue,  
spoil with no one to say, "Give it back!"

**23** Who among you will give ear to this,  
will listen and hear for the time to come?

**24** Who gave up Jacob to the looter,  
and Israel to the plunderers?  
Was it not the LORD,  
against whom we have sinned,  
in whose ways they would not walk,  
and whose instruction they did not obey?

**25** So He poured out on him the heat of His anger  
and the fury of battle;  
it set him on fire all around, but he did not understand;  
it burned him, but he did not take it to heart.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "Here is My servant… a crushed reed he will not break"** — Matthew quotes the passage of Jesus, healing quietly and telling the crowds not to make Him known: *Here is My servant whom I have chosen, My beloved in whom My soul is well pleased. I will put My Spirit upon Him… A bruised reed He will not break* (Matthew 12:18–21). The Greek of Isaiah names the servant: *Jacob My servant… Israel My chosen*; and in 41:8 and 42:19 the LORD calls Israel *My servant*.
- **vv3–4 "a crushed reed… a dimly burning wick… He will not grow dim or be crushed"** — *qaneh ratsuts*, the *crushed reed* the Rabshakeh called Egypt (36:6), which pierces the hand that leans on it. This servant will not break such a reed. And the two words return of the servant himself: *lo yikhheh ve-lo yaruts*, he will not grow *dim* (*kehah*, the wick's word) or be *crushed* (*ratsats*, the reed's word).
- **v4 "the coastlands wait for his instruction"** — *le-torato*. The Greek has *in his name the nations will hope*, and Matthew quotes that (Matthew 12:21).
- **v5 "who spread out the earth"** — *roqa*, the verb behind *raqia*, the *beaten expanse* of Genesis 1:6, used of metal hammered flat. "Breath… and spirit" — *neshamah* and *ruach*, the breath God *blew into his nostrils* (Genesis 2:7) given to all who walk the earth.
- **vv6–7 "a light for the nations, to open the eyes that are blind"** — Simeon, holding the child in the temple, calls Him *a light for revelation to the nations* (Luke 2:32). The eyes Isaiah was sent to shut (6:10) are to be opened.
- **v13 "like a man of war"** — *ke-ish milchamot*, as in the Song of the Sea, *The LORD is a man of war* (Exodus 15:3).
- **v14 "now I will cry out like a woman in labor"** — the LORD, silent through the years of judgment, speaks of Himself as a mother in birth pangs, the picture used of the people's own fruitless labor in 26:17–18.
- **vv18–20 "Who is blind but My servant… ears are open, but no one hears"** — the servant Israel is blind and deaf, the people of 6:9–10, *Keep on listening, but do not understand*. "The one at peace with Me" — *meshullam*, from the root of *shalom*: one made whole, or bound by a covenant of peace; the sense is uncertain.
- **v22 "plundered and looted… plunder with no one to rescue"** — *bazuz*, *baz*: the plunder of Maher-shalal-hash-baz (8:1), now taken from Israel.

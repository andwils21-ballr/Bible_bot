# Isaiah 24

**1** The LORD is about to empty the earth and lay it waste;  
He will twist its surface  
and scatter its inhabitants.

**2** It will be the same for the people as for the priest,  
for the servant as for his master,  
for the maid as for her mistress,  
for the buyer as for the seller,  
for the lender as for the borrower,  
for the creditor as for the debtor.

**3** The earth will be utterly emptied  
and utterly plundered,  
for the LORD has spoken this word.

**4** The earth mourns and withers;  
the world languishes and withers;  
the high ones of the people of the earth languish.

**5** The earth is defiled under its inhabitants,  
for they have transgressed the instructions,  
violated the statute,  
broken the everlasting covenant.

**6** Therefore a curse devours the earth,  
and those who live on it bear their guilt;  
therefore the inhabitants of the earth are burned,  
and few men are left.

**7** The new wine mourns;  
the vine languishes;  
all the merry-hearted sigh.

**8** The joy of the tambourines has ceased;  
the noise of the revelers has ended;  
the joy of the harp has ceased.

**9** They no longer drink wine with singing;  
strong drink is bitter to those who drink it.

**10** The city of chaos is broken down;  
every house is shut, so that no one can enter.

**11** There is an outcry in the streets for lack of wine;  
all joy has grown dark;  
the gladness of the earth is banished.

**12** Desolation is left in the city;  
the gate is battered into ruins.

**13** For so it will be in the middle of the earth,  
among the peoples,  
as when an olive tree is beaten,  
as at the gleaning when the grape harvest is done.

**14** They lift up their voices; they sing for joy;  
over the majesty of the LORD they shout from the west.

**15** Therefore in the east give glory to the LORD;  
in the coastlands of the sea,  
to the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**16** From the ends of the earth we hear songs:  
"Glory to the Righteous One!"  
But I say, "I waste away, I waste away!  
Woe is me!  
The traitors betray;  
with betrayal the traitors betray."

**17** Terror and the pit and the snare  
are upon you, inhabitant of the earth!

**18** Whoever flees at the sound of the terror  
will fall into the pit,  
and whoever climbs out of the pit  
will be caught in the snare.  
For the lattices on high are opened,  
and the foundations of the earth shake.

**19** The earth is utterly broken;  
the earth is split apart;  
the earth is violently shaken.

**20** The earth staggers like a drunkard;  
it sways like a hut;  
its transgression lies heavy on it,  
and it falls, and will not rise again.

**21** And it shall come to pass in that day, that the LORD will punish  
the host of heaven in heaven,  
and the kings of the earth on the earth.

**22** They will be gathered together  
as prisoners in a pit;  
they will be shut up in a prison,  
and after many days they will be punished.

**23** Then the moon will be confounded  
and the sun ashamed,  
for the LORD of hosts reigns  
on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem,  
and before His elders, glory.

## Notes

- **v1 "to empty the earth and lay it waste"** — *boqeq ha-arets u-voleqah*, two verbs that sound alike, as v3 doubles each: *hibboq tibboq… hibboz tibboz*, "utterly emptied… utterly plundered." *Baz*, "plunder," is the last word of the child's name in 8:1.
- **v2 "the same for the people as for the priest"** — *ka-am ka-kohen*, the words of Hosea 4:9. Every rank and trade is named in pairs, and none escapes.
- **vv5, 18 "broken the everlasting covenant… the lattices on high are opened"** — *berit olam*, the covenant God made with every living soul after the flood (*to remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living soul*, Genesis 9:16). And the earth is undone as it was then: *arubbot*, the *lattices of the heavens* that were opened for the flood (Genesis 7:11).
- **v10 "The city of chaos"** — *qiryat tohu*, the *tohu* of Genesis 1:2, the earth *formless* before the first word. The city is unmade.
- **v13 "as when an olive tree is beaten, as at the gleaning"** — the picture of 17:6, now for the whole earth: a few left at the top of the tree.
- **v16 "I waste away, I waste away"** — *razi li, razi li*, a word found only here. *Razah* is "to grow lean" (10:16, *razon*, *leanness*); in Aramaic *raz* is "a secret" (Daniel 2:18), and read so the line is *My secret is mine*. "The traitors betray; with betrayal the traitors betray" — the root *bagad* five times in two lines, as in 21:2.
- **v17 "Terror and the pit and the snare"** — *pachad va-fachat va-fach*, three words that sound alike. Jeremiah uses the line of Moab (Jeremiah 48:43).
- **v20 "it sways like a hut"** — *ka-melunah*, the watchman's *hut in a cucumber field* of 1:8, where Zion was left alone.
- **vv21–22 "the host of heaven in heaven… shut up in a prison, and after many days they will be punished"** — the powers above are judged with the kings below. 1 Enoch tells of the same: *a prison for the stars of heaven and for the host of heaven* (1 Enoch 18:14), and the watchers bound *for seventy generations… until the day of their judgment* (10:12).
- **v23 "before His elders, glory"** — as at Sinai: *Moses went up, and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel. They saw the God of Israel* (Exodus 24:9–10). "The moon… the sun" — *ha-levanah*, "the white one," and *ha-chammah*, "the hot one," the poetic names.

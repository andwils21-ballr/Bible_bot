# Isaiah 43

**1** But now, this is what the LORD says,  
the One who created you, Jacob,  
who formed you, Israel:  
"Do not fear, for I have redeemed you;  
I have called you by name; you are Mine.

**2** When you pass through the waters, I will be with you;  
and through the rivers, they will not sweep over you;  
when you walk through fire, you will not be burned,  
and the flame will not consume you.

**3** For I am the LORD your God,  
the Holy One of Israel, your Savior;  
I give Egypt as your ransom,  
Cush and Seba in your place.

**4** Because you are precious in My eyes,  
and honored, and I love you,  
I give men in your place,  
and peoples in exchange for your life.

**5** Do not fear, for I am with you;  
I will bring your offspring from the east,  
and from the west I will gather you.

**6** I will say to the north, 'Give them up!'  
and to the south, 'Do not hold them back!'  
Bring My sons from afar,  
and My daughters from the end of the earth,

**7** everyone who is called by My name,  
whom I created for My glory,  
whom I formed, yes, whom I made."

**8** Bring out the people who are blind, yet have eyes,  
who are deaf, yet have ears!

**9** All the nations gather together,  
and the peoples assemble.  
Who among them can declare this,  
and show us the former things?  
Let them bring their witnesses to prove them right,  
and let others hear and say, "It is true."

**10** "You are My witnesses," declares the LORD,  
"and My servant whom I have chosen,  
so that you may know and believe Me  
and understand that I am He.  
Before Me no god was formed,  
nor will there be any after Me.

**11** I, I am the LORD,  
and besides Me there is no savior.

**12** I declared and saved and proclaimed,  
when there was no strange god among you;  
and you are My witnesses," declares the LORD,  
"and I am God.

**13** From this day on I am He;  
there is no one who can deliver from My hand;  
I act, and who can turn it back?"

**14** This is what the LORD says,  
your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel:  
"For your sake I have sent to Babylon  
and will bring them all down as fugitives,  
the Chaldeans, in the ships they rejoiced in.

**15** I am the LORD, your Holy One,  
the Creator of Israel, your King."

**16** This is what the LORD says,  
the One who makes a way in the sea,  
a path in the mighty waters,

**17** who brings out chariot and horse,  
army and warrior;  
they lie down together, they cannot rise;  
they are extinguished, quenched like a wick:

**18** "Do not remember the former things,  
or consider the things of old.

**19** I am about to do a new thing;  
now it springs up; do you not perceive it?  
I will make a way in the wilderness  
and rivers in the desert.

**20** The wild beasts will honor Me,  
the jackals and the ostriches,  
for I give water in the wilderness,  
rivers in the desert,  
to give drink to My chosen people,

**21** the people whom I formed for Myself,  
that they might declare My praise.

**22** Yet you did not call on Me, Jacob;  
you have grown weary of Me, Israel.

**23** You have not brought Me your sheep for burnt offerings,  
or honored Me with your sacrifices.  
I have not made you serve with offerings,  
or wearied you with frankincense.

**24** You have not bought Me fragrant cane with money,  
or satisfied Me with the fat of your sacrifices.  
But you have made Me serve with your sins;  
you have wearied Me with your iniquities.

**25** I, I am He  
who blots out your transgressions for My own sake,  
and I will not remember your sins.

**26** Remind Me; let us argue our case together;  
set forth your case, that you may be proved right.

**27** Your first father sinned,  
and your spokesmen rebelled against Me.

**28** So I profaned the princes of the sanctuary,  
and gave Jacob over to destruction  
and Israel to reviling."

## Notes

- **vv1, 7, 21 "who created you… who formed you… whom I created… whom I formed… whom I made"** — *bara*, *yatsar*, *asah*, the three verbs of the making of the world and of man (Genesis 1:1, 2:7, 1:26), used here of the making of a people.

# Isaiah 2

**1** The word that Isaiah son of Amoz saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem.

## The Mountain of the LORD

**2** And it shall come to pass in the last days  
that the mountain of the LORD's house  
shall be established as the highest of the mountains,  
and shall be lifted up above the hills;  
and all the nations shall stream to it.

**3** Many peoples shall go and say,  
"Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD,  
to the house of the God of Jacob,  
that He may teach us His ways,  
and that we may walk in His paths."  
For out of Zion shall go forth instruction,  
and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.

**4** He shall judge between the nations,  
and decide for many peoples;  
they shall beat their swords into plowshares,  
and their spears into pruning hooks;  
nation shall not lift up sword against nation,  
nor shall they learn war anymore.

**5** House of Jacob,  
come, let us walk in the light of the LORD.

## The Day of the LORD

**6** For You have forsaken Your people,  
the house of Jacob,  
because they are full of what comes from the east,  
and of fortune-tellers like the Philistines,  
and they strike hands with the children of foreigners.

**7** Their land is filled with silver and gold,  
and there is no end to their treasures;  
their land is filled with horses,  
and there is no end to their chariots.

**8** Their land is filled with idols;  
they bow down to the work of their hands,  
to what their own fingers have made.

**9** So man is humbled,  
and each one brought low —  
do not forgive them!

**10** Go into the rock,  
and hide in the dust,  
from the terror of the LORD  
and the splendor of His majesty.

**11** The haughty eyes of man shall be brought low,  
and the pride of men shall be humbled,  
and the LORD alone shall be exalted in that day.

**12** For the LORD of hosts has a day  
against all that is proud and lofty,  
against all that is lifted up — and it shall be brought low;

**13** against all the cedars of Lebanon,  
lofty and lifted up,  
and against all the oaks of Bashan;

**14** against all the lofty mountains,  
and against all the hills that are lifted up;

**15** against every high tower,  
and against every fortified wall;

**16** against all the ships of Tarshish,  
and against all the beautiful craft.

**17** The haughtiness of man shall be humbled,  
and the pride of men brought low,  
and the LORD alone shall be exalted in that day.

**18** The idols shall utterly pass away.

**19** Men shall go into the caves of the rocks  
and into the holes of the ground,  
from the terror of the LORD  
and the splendor of His majesty,  
when He rises to make the earth tremble.

**20** In that day man will throw away  
his idols of silver and his idols of gold,  
which they made for him to bow down to,  
to the moles and to the bats,

**21** to go into the clefts of the rocks  
and the crevices of the crags,  
from the terror of the LORD  
and the splendor of His majesty,  
when He rises to make the earth tremble.

**22** Stop trusting in man,  
who has only breath in his nostrils;  
for of what account is he?

## Notes

- **v1 "The word that Isaiah… saw"** — *ha-davar asher chazah*: a word that is seen, as the book's title calls the whole a *vision* (1:1).
- **vv2–4 "the mountain of the LORD's house… all the nations shall stream to it"** — *naharu*, "flow like a river": the nations run uphill to a mountain. The same oracle stands, with small differences, in Micah 4:1–3.
- **v3 "that He may teach us… instruction"** — *yorenu… torah*: the verb and the noun are one root, *yarah*, "to point the way." What goes out from Zion is what the LORD teaches.
- **v4 "He shall judge… and decide"** — *ve-hokhiach*, the verb of 1:18, *let us settle this*: the case the LORD offered Judah, He will settle for the nations. "Swords into plowshares" — Joel turns the words around for the day of battle: *Beat your plowshares into swords* (Joel 3:10, Hebrew 4:10).
- **vv7–8 "silver and gold… horses… idols"** — the three things the law forbade a king to multiply, *he shall not multiply horses… and silver and gold he shall not greatly multiply* (Deuteronomy 17:16–17), and the third is the end of it. "Idols" — *elilim*, "nothings," a mocking twin of *elohim*, "God."
- **v9 "do not forgive them!"** — *ve-al tissa lahem*, "and do not lift [it] from them": *nasa*, "lift," is the verb of forgiving, as in *lift the offense of your brothers* (Genesis 50:17). The prophet's own cry breaks into the oracle.
- **vv10–21 "from the terror of the LORD and the splendor of His majesty"** — a refrain three times (vv10, 19, 21); and *the LORD alone shall be exalted in that day* twice (vv11, 17). The *day of the LORD* is a day against everything high: cedars, oaks, mountains, towers, walls, ships.
- **v19 "when He rises to make the earth tremble"** — *la'arots ha-arets*, a play of sounds: to *terrify the terrain*.
- **v22 "who has only breath in his nostrils"** — *neshamah be-appo*, the breath God blew into the first man's nostrils (Genesis 2:7): what man has is lent.

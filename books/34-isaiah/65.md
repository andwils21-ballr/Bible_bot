# Isaiah 65

**1** I let Myself be sought by those who did not ask;  
I let Myself be found by those who did not seek Me.  
I said, "Here I am, here I am,"  
to a nation not called by My name.

**2** I have spread out My hands all day long  
to a rebellious people,  
who walk in a way that is not good,  
following their own thoughts;

**3** a people who provoke Me  
to My face continually,  
sacrificing in the gardens  
and burning incense on the bricks;

**4** who sit among the graves  
and spend the night in hidden places;  
who eat the flesh of pigs,  
with broth of foul meat in their pots;

**5** who say, "Keep to yourself;  
do not come near me, for I am too holy for you."  
These are smoke in My nostrils,  
a fire that burns all day.

**6** It stands written before Me:  
I will not keep silent, but I will repay;  
I will repay into their lap

**7** your iniquities and your fathers' iniquities together,  
says the LORD,  
because they burned incense on the mountains  
and insulted Me on the hills;  
I will measure into their lap  
payment for their former deeds.

**8** Thus says the LORD:  
As when new wine is found in the cluster,  
and someone says, "Do not destroy it,  
for there is a blessing in it,"  
so I will do for the sake of My servants,  
and not destroy them all.

**9** I will bring out offspring from Jacob,  
and from Judah an heir to My mountains;  
My chosen ones will possess it,  
and My servants will live there.

**10** Sharon will become a pasture for flocks,  
and the Valley of Achor a resting place for herds,  
for My people who have sought Me.

**11** But you who forsake the LORD,  
who forget My holy mountain,  
who set a table for Fortune  
and fill cups of mixed wine for Destiny,

**12** I will destine you to the sword,  
and all of you will bow down to the slaughter;  
because when I called, you did not answer;  
when I spoke, you did not listen;  
but you did what was evil in My eyes,  
and chose what I did not delight in.

**13** Therefore thus says the Lord GOD:  
My servants will eat,  
but you will be hungry;  
My servants will drink,  
but you will be thirsty;  
My servants will rejoice,  
but you will be put to shame;

**14** My servants will sing for gladness of heart,  
but you will cry out for pain of heart,  
and wail for a broken spirit.

**15** You will leave your name  
to My chosen ones for a curse,  
and the Lord GOD will put you to death;  
but His servants He will call by another name,

**16** so that whoever blesses himself in the land  
will bless himself by the God of truth,  
and whoever swears in the land  
will swear by the God of truth;  
for the former troubles are forgotten  
and are hidden from My eyes.

**17** For I am about to create new heavens  
and a new earth;  
the former things will not be remembered  
or come to mind.

**18** But be glad and rejoice forever  
in what I create;  
for I am about to create Jerusalem to be a joy,  
and her people to be a gladness.

**19** I will rejoice in Jerusalem  
and be glad in My people;  
no more will the sound of weeping be heard in her,  
or the sound of crying.

**20** No more will there be in it  
an infant who lives but a few days,  
or an old man who does not fill out his days;  
for the youth will die a hundred years old,  
and the sinner a hundred years old will be accursed.

**21** They will build houses and live in them;  
they will plant vineyards and eat their fruit.

**22** They will not build for another to live in;  
they will not plant for another to eat;  
for like the days of a tree  
will be the days of My people,  
and My chosen ones will long enjoy  
the work of their hands.

**23** They will not labor in vain,  
or bear children for sudden terror;  
for they are the offspring of those blessed by the LORD,  
and their descendants with them.

**24** And it shall come to pass, before they call, that I will answer;  
while they are still speaking, I will hear.

**25** The wolf and the lamb shall graze together,  
and the lion shall eat straw like the ox,  
and dust shall be the serpent's food.  
They shall not harm or destroy  
in all My holy mountain,  
says the LORD.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "I let Myself be sought… to a nation not called by My name"** — *nidrashti*, "I let Myself be sought." *A nation not called by My name* is what the people said of themselves in 63:19, *like those not called by Your name*; and *Here I am* (*hinneni*) is the servant's answer, given by the LORD as in 58:9. Paul quotes the Greek of these two verses and divides them: v1, *I was found by those who did not seek Me*, of the nations, and v2, *All day long I have held out My hands to a disobedient and contrary people*, of Israel (Romans 10:20–21).
- **vv3–4 "sacrificing in the gardens… who eat the flesh of pigs"** — the *gardens* of 1:29 and of 66:17, where the same people again *eat the flesh of pigs*; the pig is unclean in the law (Leviticus 11:7). "Spend the night in hidden places" — the Greek says they sleep *in the caves for dreams*. "Broth of foul meat" — *piggulim*, the word for an offering eaten on the third day, *It is foul* (Leviticus 7:18). *Broth* is the read form, *u-meraq*; the written form is *u-feraq*, "and pieces."
- **v5 "I am too holy for you"** — *qedashtikha*, "I am holy to you." The warning not to come near rests on the law's rule for the holiest offerings, *Whatever touches them becomes holy* (Leviticus 6:18). To the LORD such holiness is *smoke in My nostrils*.
- **v10 "the Valley of Achor"** — *achor* is "trouble": Joshua said to Achan, *Why have you brought trouble on us? The LORD will bring trouble on you today*, and the place was named for it, *the Valley of Achor to this day* (Joshua 7:25–26). The valley named for that trouble becomes a place where herds lie down.
- **vv11–12 "for Fortune… for Destiny… I will destine you"** — *Gad* and *Meni*, gods of luck. *Gad* is the word Leah said at her son's birth, *Fortune has come* (Genesis 30:11). *Meni* is from *manah*, "to count, to allot," and the LORD answers with the same verb, *u-maniti*, *I will destine you to the sword*. The Greek has *a table for the demon* and *mixed wine for Fortune*.
- **vv15–16 "another name… the God of truth"** — the servants receive a new name, as Zion did (62:2). "The God of truth" is *Elohei amen*, "the God of Amen," twice: *amen* is the word that seals an oath, as the woman says *Amen, amen* in Numbers 5:22. In Revelation Jesus speaks as *the Amen, the faithful and true witness* (Revelation 3:14). The Greek has *the true God*.
- **vv17–19 "new heavens and a new earth… no more will the sound of weeping be heard"** — *bore*, "create," three times in two verses, the verb of *In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth* (Genesis 1:1). John sees *a new heaven and a new earth*, and God *will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and death will be no more; neither mourning nor crying nor pain will be any more* (Revelation 21:1, 4). Peter waits for *new heavens and a new earth, in which righteousness dwells* (2 Peter 3:13).
- **v20 "the sinner a hundred years old will be accursed"** — *ha-chote*, from *chata*, whose first sense is "to miss": the left-handed men of Benjamin could *sling a stone at a hair and not miss* (Judges 20:16). Read as "the sinner," as the Greek reads it, the line says sin is still under the curse, even in a life of a hundred years. Read as "the one who misses," it carries on the line before: *the one who falls short of a hundred will be thought accursed*, so long will life be.
- **vv21–22 "They will build houses and live in them"** — the curse of Deuteronomy 28:30 undone: *You will build a house and not live in it. You will plant a vineyard and not use its fruit.* "Like the days of a tree" — the Greek has *like the days of the tree of life* (Genesis 2:9).
- **v25 "The wolf and the lamb… They shall not harm or destroy in all My holy mountain"** — the picture of 11:6–9, with two of its lines word for word: *the lion shall eat straw like the ox* (11:7) and *They shall not harm or destroy in all My holy mountain* (11:9). One line is new, *dust shall be the serpent's food*: the serpent's sentence, *dust you shall eat all the days of your life* (Genesis 3:14), still stands.

# Isaiah 1

**1** The vision of Isaiah son of Amoz, which he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.

## The Rebel Sons

**2** Hear, heavens, and give ear, earth,  
for the LORD has spoken:  
"Sons I have raised and brought up,  
but they have rebelled against Me.

**3** The ox knows its owner,  
and the donkey its master's manger;  
Israel does not know,  
My people do not understand."

**4** Woe, sinful nation,  
a people heavy with iniquity,  
offspring of evildoers,  
sons who deal corruptly!  
They have forsaken the LORD,  
they have spurned the Holy One of Israel,  
they have turned their backs on Him.

**5** Where will you be struck again,  
as you keep on rebelling?  
The whole head is sick,  
and the whole heart faint.

**6** From the sole of the foot to the head  
there is no soundness in it:  
bruises and welts and raw wounds,  
not pressed out, not bound up,  
not softened with oil.

**7** Your land is a desolation,  
your cities are burned with fire;  
your soil — strangers devour it before your eyes,  
and it is a desolation, like an overthrow by strangers.

**8** The daughter of Zion is left  
like a shelter in a vineyard,  
like a hut in a cucumber field,  
like a city under siege.

**9** Unless the LORD of hosts  
had left us a few survivors,  
we would have been like Sodom,  
we would have resembled Gomorrah.

## Your Sacrifices

**10** Hear the word of the LORD,  
you rulers of Sodom!  
Give ear to the instruction of our God,  
you people of Gomorrah!

**11** "What are your many sacrifices to Me?"  
says the LORD.  
"I have had my fill of burnt offerings of rams  
and the fat of fattened animals;  
I take no delight in the blood of bulls,  
of lambs or of goats.

**12** When you come to appear before My face,  
who asked this of you,  
this trampling of My courts?

**13** Bring no more empty offerings;  
incense is a detestable thing to Me.  
New moon and sabbath, the calling of assemblies —  
I cannot bear iniquity with the sacred assembly.

**14** Your new moons and your appointed feasts  
My soul hates;  
they have become a burden to Me;  
I am weary of bearing them.

**15** When you spread out your hands,  
I will hide My eyes from you;  
even when you make many prayers,  
I am not listening.  
Your hands are full of blood.

**16** Wash yourselves; make yourselves clean;  
remove the evil of your deeds from before My eyes;  
stop doing evil.

**17** Learn to do good;  
seek justice;  
set the oppressor right;  
defend the orphan;  
plead for the widow."

## Come, Let Us Settle This

**18** "Come now, let us settle this,"  
says the LORD:  
"Though your sins are like scarlet,  
they shall be white as snow;  
though they are red like crimson,  
they shall become like wool.

**19** If you are willing and listen,  
you shall eat the good of the land;

**20** but if you refuse and rebel,  
you shall be eaten by the sword";  
for the mouth of the LORD has spoken.

## The Faithful City

**21** How the faithful city  
has become a prostitute!  
She was full of justice;  
righteousness lodged in her,  
but now murderers.

**22** Your silver has become dross,  
your wine is cut with water.

**23** Your rulers are rebels  
and companions of thieves.  
Every one of them loves a bribe  
and runs after payoffs.  
They do not defend the orphan,  
and the widow's case never comes before them.

**24** Therefore the Lord, the LORD of hosts,  
the Mighty One of Israel, declares:  
"Ah, I will get relief from My foes,  
and avenge Myself on My enemies.

**25** I will turn My hand against you;  
I will smelt away your dross as with lye,  
and remove all your alloy.

**26** I will restore your judges as at the first,  
and your counselors as at the beginning.  
After this you shall be called  
the City of Righteousness, the Faithful City."

**27** Zion shall be redeemed by justice,  
and those in her who return, by righteousness.

**28** But rebels and sinners shall be broken together,  
and those who forsake the LORD shall come to an end.

**29** For they shall be ashamed of the oaks  
that you desired,  
and you shall blush for the gardens  
that you chose.

**30** For you shall be like an oak  
whose leaf withers,  
and like a garden that has no water.

**31** The strong one shall become tinder,  
and his work a spark;  
both shall burn together,  
with no one to quench them.

## Notes

- **v1 "Isaiah son of Amoz"** — *Yesha'yahu*, "the LORD saves," from *yasha*, "save," the root of Joshua's name. The prophet's name says what the book will say.
- **v2 "Hear, heavens, and give ear, earth"** — the two witnesses of the Song of Moses, *Give ear, heavens, and I will speak; let the earth hear the words of my mouth* (Deuteronomy 32:1), with the verbs reversed. The chapter is a lawsuit: the witnesses are called (v2), the charge is laid (vv2–9), the plea is rejected (vv10–15), and the LORD offers terms (v18, *let us settle this*, *nivvakhechah*, the word for arguing a case).
- **v4 "the Holy One of Israel"** — *qedosh Yisrael*, Isaiah's own name for God: twenty-four times in this book, and only six times in the rest of the Hebrew Bible (three in the Psalms, two in Jeremiah, and 2 Kings 19:22, which is Isaiah speaking).
- **vv5–6 "Where will you be struck again"** — *al meh tukku od* can be "Why will you be struck again?" or "On what will you be struck again?" The next line answers the second: *from the sole of the foot to the head there is no soundness*. There is no unbruised place left to strike.
- **vv7–10 "like an overthrow by strangers… like Sodom… you rulers of Sodom"** — *mahpekhah*, "overthrow," is used everywhere else of the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah (Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 13:19; Jeremiah 49:18; 50:40; Amos 4:11). In v9 the survivors say they *would have been like Sodom*; in v10 the prophet calls them Sodom. Paul quotes v9 from the Greek, which has *a seed* for "survivors" (Romans 9:29).
- **v12 "to appear before My face"** — *lera'ot panai*, as in Exodus 34:24, *to appear before the face of the LORD your God three times in the year*. The pilgrim feasts God commanded, He now calls *trampling of My courts*. What He rejects is not the rite but the hands that bring it: *Your hands are full of blood* (v15).
- **v17 "set the oppressor right"** — *ashsheru chamots*. *Chamots* is found only here: "the ruthless one," whom the people are to correct, or "the oppressed," whom they are to relieve. The verb means to set straight.
- **v18 "Though your sins are like scarlet, they shall be white as snow"** — *im yihyu chata'eikhem kashshanim kashsheleg yalbinu*, "if your sins are like scarlet, as snow they will be white." It can be read as the promise it has always been taken to be, or as the question of a judge: "if your sins are scarlet, are they to pass for white?" Verses 19–20 set the terms either way.
- **vv19–20 "you shall eat… you shall be eaten"** — *tokhelu… te'ukkelu*, the same verb active and passive: eat the land's good, or be eaten by the sword.
- **vv21, 26 "the faithful city"** — *qiryah ne'emanah* opens the lament and closes the promise: what she was, she will be called again. "The oaks… the gardens" (vv29–30): places of other worship, as later in the book, *sacrificing in the gardens* (65:3) and *burning with lust among the oaks* (57:5). *Eilim*, "oaks," is spelled as *eilim*, "rams," the burnt offerings of v11.

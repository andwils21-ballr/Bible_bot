# Isaiah 48

**1** Hear this, house of Jacob,  
who are called by the name of Israel,  
and who came from the waters of Judah,  
who swear by the name of the LORD  
and call on the God of Israel,  
but not in truth or in righteousness.

**2** For they call themselves after the holy city  
and lean on the God of Israel;  
the LORD of hosts is His name.

**3** The former things I declared long ago;  
they went out from My mouth, and I announced them;  
suddenly I did them, and they came to pass.

**4** Because I knew that you are stubborn,  
and your neck is an iron sinew  
and your forehead bronze,

**5** I declared them to you long ago;  
before they came to pass I announced them to you,  
so that you would not say, "My idol did them;  
my carved image and my cast image commanded them."

**6** You have heard; see it all.  
Will you not declare it?  
From now on I announce new things to you,  
hidden things that you have not known.

**7** They are created now, not long ago;  
before today you never heard of them,  
so that you would not say, "I knew them already."

**8** You have not heard, you have not known;  
from long ago your ear has not been opened.  
For I knew that you would deal treacherously,  
and that you were called a rebel from the womb.

**9** For My name's sake I defer My anger;  
for the sake of My praise I hold it back for you,  
so as not to cut you off.

**10** I have refined you, but not as silver;  
I have chosen you in the furnace of affliction.

**11** For My own sake, for My own sake, I do it;  
for how should it be profaned?  
My glory I give to no other.

**12** Listen to Me, Jacob,  
and Israel, whom I called:  
I am He; I am the first,  
and I am also the last.

**13** My own hand laid the foundation of the earth,  
and My right hand spread out the heavens;  
when I call to them,  
they stand up together.

**14** Assemble, all of you, and listen!  
Who among them has declared these things?  
The LORD loves him;  
he will carry out His purpose on Babylon,  
and His arm will be against the Chaldeans.

**15** I, I have spoken; I have called him;  
I have brought him, and he will prosper in his way.

**16** Draw near to Me, hear this:  
from the beginning I have not spoken in secret;  
from the time it came to be, I have been there.  
And now the Lord GOD has sent me, and His Spirit.

**17** This is what the LORD says,  
your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel:  
"I am the LORD your God,  
who teaches you what is for your good,  
who leads you in the way you should go.

**18** If only you had paid attention to My commandments!  
Then your peace would have been like a river,  
and your righteousness like the waves of the sea;

**19** your seed would have been like the sand,  
and the offspring of your body like its grains;  
its name would never be cut off  
or destroyed from before Me."

**20** Go out from Babylon, flee from the Chaldeans!  
Declare this with a shout of joy, proclaim it,  
send it out to the end of the earth;  
say, "The LORD has redeemed His servant Jacob!"

**21** They did not thirst  
when He led them through the deserts;  
He made water flow for them from the rock;  
He split the rock, and water gushed out.

**22** "There is no peace," says the LORD, "for the wicked."

## Notes

- **v1 "who came from the waters of Judah"** — *u-mimmei Yehudah*, the people pictured as a stream flowing from its spring, as Psalm 68:26 calls them *you who come from the fountain of Israel*. The Greek has only *who came out of Judah*.
- **v4 "you are stubborn, and your neck is an iron sinew"** — *qasheh*, "hard," the word of Exodus 32:9, *a stiff-necked people* (*qesheh oref*). Isaiah spells the old phrase out: the neck has a sinew of iron and the forehead is bronze.
- **v8 "a rebel from the womb"** — *poshea mi-beten*. Israel was *formed from the womb* (44:2, 24) and *carried from the womb* (46:3); it has also been rebelling from the womb. The LORD knew both.
- **v10 "I have chosen you in the furnace of affliction"** — *bechartikha*, from *bachar*, "choose," the verb of *Israel whom I have chosen* (41:8; 44:1, 2). Beside *I have refined you* the reader may expect "tested," but that is *bachan* (28:16, *a tested stone*), one letter different, and the Hebrew has *bachar*. The Greek has *I have rescued you from the furnace of poverty*. *Kur*, "furnace," is the word of Egypt, *the iron furnace* (Deuteronomy 4:20).
- **v11 "for how should it be profaned?"** — the Hebrew has no subject; the thing that would be profaned is the *name* of v9. The Greek supplies it, *My name is profaned*. "My glory I give to no other" — the words of 42:8.
- **vv14–15 "The LORD loves him; he will carry out His purpose on Babylon"** — Cyrus, called and brought by the LORD (41:2, 25; 45:1–4). *Cheftso*, "His purpose," is the word of 44:28, where Cyrus *shall carry out all My purpose*. The Greek reads it as God speaking to Israel: *Loving you, I have done your will on Babylon*.
- **v16 "And now the Lord GOD has sent me, and His Spirit"** — a new voice speaks, *me*, sent by the Lord GOD; it is not said whose. The Hebrew order allows two readings: the Lord GOD and His Spirit have sent me, or the Lord GOD has sent me and His Spirit with me. The English keeps the Hebrew order and both readings.
- **vv18–19 "your seed would have been like the sand"** — the promise to Abraham, *your seed… as the sand that is on the lip of the sea* (Genesis 22:17), as it would have been kept had Israel listened. "Its grains" — *ke-me'otav*, a word found only here.
- **v21 "He split the rock, and water gushed out"** — *va-yivqa tsur va-yazuvu mayim*, the exodus told in the Psalms' words: *He opened the rock, and water gushed out* (Psalm 105:41; also 78:20). The way out of Babylon is a second exodus.
- **v22 "There is no peace… for the wicked"** — the line comes again at 57:21, there with *says my God*.

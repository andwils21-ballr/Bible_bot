# Isaiah 50

**1** Thus says the LORD:  
"Where is your mother's certificate of divorce,  
with which I sent her away?  
Or which of My creditors is it  
to whom I sold you?  
It was for your iniquities that you were sold,  
and for your transgressions your mother was sent away.

**2** Why, when I came, was there no one?  
When I called, was there no one to answer?  
Is My hand too short to redeem,  
or have I no power to deliver?  
By My rebuke I dry up the sea;  
I make the rivers a desert;  
their fish stink for lack of water  
and die of thirst.

**3** I clothe the heavens with blackness  
and make sackcloth their covering."

## The Lord GOD Has Opened My Ear

**4** The Lord GOD has given me  
the tongue of disciples,  
to know how to sustain the weary with a word.  
Morning by morning He wakens,  
He wakens my ear  
to hear as disciples hear.

**5** The Lord GOD has opened my ear,  
and I was not rebellious;  
I did not turn back.

**6** I gave my back to those who strike,  
and my cheeks to those who pull out the beard;  
I did not hide my face  
from insults and spitting.

**7** But the Lord GOD helps me;  
therefore I have not been disgraced;  
therefore I have set my face like flint,  
and I know that I will not be put to shame.

**8** He who declares me in the right is near.  
Who will contend with me?  
Let us stand up together.  
Who has a case against me?  
Let him come near to me.

**9** The Lord GOD helps me;  
who will declare me guilty?  
All of them will wear out like a garment;  
the moth will eat them up.

**10** Who among you fears the LORD  
and obeys the voice of His servant?  
Let him who walks in darkness  
and has no light  
trust in the name of the LORD  
and rely on his God.

**11** But all of you who kindle a fire,  
who arm yourselves with firebrands:  
walk by the light of your fire,  
and by the firebrands you have lit!  
This you will have from My hand:  
you will lie down in torment.

## Notes

- **v1 "Where is your mother's certificate of divorce… which of My creditors"** — *sefer keritut*, the document of Deuteronomy 24:1, *he writes her a certificate of divorce, puts it in her hand and sends her out of his house*. A man in debt might sell his children to a creditor (*the creditor has come to take my two children*, 2 Kings 4:1). The LORD has no certificate to show and no creditor to pay: the mother was sent away, and the children sold, for their own sins.
- **v2 "Is My hand too short to redeem?"** — *ha-qatsor qatsrah yadi*, the LORD's question to Moses in the wilderness, *Is the LORD's hand too short?* (Numbers 11:23). Isaiah returns to it in 59:1. "By My rebuke I dry up the sea" — *ge'arah* and *charav*, the words of Psalm 106:9, *He rebuked the Sea of Reeds, and it dried up*.
- **v4 "the tongue of disciples… to hear as disciples hear"** — *limmudim*, "those who are taught," the word of 8:16, *seal the instruction among my disciples*. The servant speaks as one taught before he teaches. "To sustain" — *la'ut*, a verb found only here; its sense is taken from what follows, *the weary* and *a word*. The Greek has *to know when to speak a word*.
- **v6 "I gave my back to those who strike… I did not hide my face from insults and spitting"** — the Greek has *my cheeks to slaps* (*rhapismata*) and *the shame of spitting* (*emptysmata*). The Gospel tells the night before the crucifixion in the same words: *they spat in His face… and some slapped Him* (Matthew 26:67, *eneptysan*, *erapisan*).
- **v7 "I have set my face like flint"** — *challamish*, the *flint rock* the LORD brought water out of in the wilderness (Deuteronomy 8:15). The servant does not turn back (v5) and does not flinch.
- **vv8–9 "He who declares me in the right is near… who will declare me guilty?"** — *matsdiqi*, *yarshi'eni*, the words of a court: one who acquits, one who condemns. Paul uses the scene for the believer: *Who will bring a charge against God's elect? God is the one who justifies; who is the one who condemns?* (Romans 8:33–34).
- **v9 "the moth will eat them up"** — *ash yokhlem*, said again in 51:8, *the moth will eat them up like a garment*.
- **v10 "Let him who walks in darkness and has no light"** — *nogah*, "brightness," the word of 9:2, *light has shone on them* (*nagah*), there given to *the people who walked in darkness*.
- **vv10–11 "trust in the name of the LORD… walk by the light of your fire"** — two ways through the dark. One trusts the LORD with no light of his own; the others make their own light and walk by it, and lie down *in torment* (*ma'atsevah*, from the root of *etsev*, the *pain* of Genesis 3:16).

# Isaiah 9

**1** But there will be no gloom for her who was in anguish. In the former time He brought contempt on the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, but in the latter time He has made glorious the way of the sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations.

**2** The people who walked in darkness  
have seen a great light;  
those who lived in the land of the shadow of death,  
light has shone on them.

**3** You have multiplied the nation;  
You have increased its joy;  
they rejoice before You as with joy at the harvest,  
as men rejoice when they divide the spoil.

**4** For the yoke of his burden,  
and the staff on his shoulder,  
the rod of his oppressor,  
You have broken as on the day of Midian.

**5** For every boot of the tramping warrior in the tumult,  
and every garment rolled in blood,  
will be for burning, fuel for the fire.

**6** For to us a child is born,  
to us a son is given;  
and the government shall be on his shoulder;  
and his name shall be called  
Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God,  
Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace.

**7** Of the increase of his government and of peace  
there will be no end,  
on the throne of David and over his kingdom,  
to establish it and to uphold it  
with justice and with righteousness  
from this time forth and forever.  
The zeal of the LORD of hosts will do this.

## His Hand Is Still Stretched Out

**8** The Lord has sent a word against Jacob,  
and it has fallen on Israel;

**9** all the people will know it,  
Ephraim and the inhabitants of Samaria,  
who say in pride and arrogance of heart:

**10** "The bricks have fallen,  
but we will build with dressed stone;  
the sycamores have been cut down,  
but we will put cedars in their place."

**11** So the LORD raised up the adversaries of Rezin against him,  
and stirred up his enemies:

**12** the Arameans on the east and the Philistines on the west,  
and they devoured Israel with open mouth.  
For all this His anger has not turned away,  
and His hand is still stretched out.

**13** But the people did not turn to Him who struck them,  
nor did they seek the LORD of hosts.

**14** So the LORD cut off from Israel head and tail,  
palm branch and reed, in one day —

**15** the elder and the man of rank, he is the head;  
and the prophet who teaches lies, he is the tail;

**16** for those who guide this people lead them astray,  
and those who are guided by them are swallowed up.

**17** Therefore the Lord does not rejoice over their young men,  
and has no compassion on their orphans and widows;  
for every one is godless and an evildoer,  
and every mouth speaks folly.  
For all this His anger has not turned away,  
and His hand is still stretched out.

**18** For wickedness burns like fire;  
it devours briers and thorns;  
it kindles the thickets of the forest,  
and they roll upward in a column of smoke.

**19** Through the fury of the LORD of hosts the land is scorched,  
and the people are like fuel for the fire;  
no one spares his brother.

**20** They slice on the right, and are still hungry;  
they devour on the left, and are not satisfied;  
each devours the flesh of his own arm,

**21** Manasseh Ephraim, and Ephraim Manasseh,  
and together they are against Judah.  
For all this His anger has not turned away,  
and His hand is still stretched out.

## Notes

- **v1** — Hebrew 8:23; the Hebrew numbers the rest of the chapter one lower (English 9:2 = Hebrew 9:1). The English numbering is followed. Zebulun and Naphtali, the northern tribes first taken by Assyria, are the first promised glory: Matthew quotes vv1–2 of Jesus making His home in Capernaum, *Land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali, the way of the sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations* (Matthew 4:15–16).
- **v2 "the land of the shadow of death"** — *tsalmavet*, the word of Psalm 23:4, *the valley of the shadow of death*; the darkness of 8:22 is answered.
- **v3 "You have increased its joy"** — the read form, *lo* spelled with *vav*, "to it"; the written form is *lo* spelled with *aleph*, "not," which would make the line *You have not increased the joy*. The two differ in meaning, and the read form fits the rejoicing that follows.
- **v4 "as on the day of Midian"** — when Gideon's three hundred broke a vast army without a fight, *the LORD set every man's sword against his fellow* (Judges 7:22).
- **vv4, 6 "the staff on his shoulder… the government shall be on his shoulder"** — *shikhmo* both times: the oppressor's rod is broken off the people's shoulder, and the rule is laid on the child's.
- **v6 "Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace"** — *pele yo'ets, el gibbor, avi-ad, sar-shalom*. *El gibbor*, "Mighty God," is used of the LORD Himself in the next chapter: *A remnant will return, the remnant of Jacob, to the Mighty God* (10:21).
- **v7 "The zeal of the LORD of hosts will do this"** — *qin'at YHWH tseva'ot*: what the people cannot bring about, the LORD's own ardor will.
- **vv12, 17, 21 "For all this His anger has not turned away, and His hand is still stretched out"** — the refrain of 5:25, three times here, and once more at 10:4. Each stanza ends where the last one did: the blow has fallen and the people have not turned.
- **v14 "head and tail, palm branch and reed"** — v15 explains the first pair; the second is the high and the low of the marsh: everyone, from top to bottom.

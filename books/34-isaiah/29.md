# Isaiah 29

**1** Woe, Ariel, Ariel,  
the city where David camped!  
Add year to year;  
let the feasts run their round.

**2** Yet I will distress Ariel,  
and there will be moaning and lamentation,  
and she will be to Me like an Ariel.

**3** I will camp against you all around;  
I will besiege you with towers  
and raise siege works against you.

**4** Brought low, you will speak from the earth,  
and your words will come low from the dust;  
your voice will be like a ghost's from the earth,  
and your words will chirp from the dust.

**5** But the multitude of your foes will be like fine dust,  
and the multitude of the ruthless like chaff blowing away;  
and in an instant, suddenly,

**6** you will be visited by the LORD of hosts  
with thunder and earthquake and a great noise,  
with whirlwind and tempest  
and the flame of a devouring fire.

**7** The multitude of all the nations that fight against Ariel,  
all that fight against her and her stronghold and distress her,  
will be like a dream, a vision of the night.

**8** As when a hungry man dreams he is eating,  
and wakes with his hunger unsatisfied,  
or a thirsty man dreams he is drinking,  
and wakes faint, with his thirst unquenched,  
so will the multitude of all the nations be  
that fight against Mount Zion.

**9** Be astonished and astounded;  
blind yourselves and be blind!  
Be drunk, but not with wine;  
stagger, but not with strong drink!

**10** For the LORD has poured out on you  
a spirit of deep sleep,  
and has shut your eyes, the prophets,  
and covered your heads, the seers.

**11** All this vision has become to you like the words of a sealed book. When they give it to one who can read, saying, "Read this," he says, "I cannot, for it is sealed."

**12** When the book is given to one who cannot read, saying, "Read this," he says, "I cannot read."

**13** The Lord said:  
"Because this people draw near with their mouth  
and honor Me with their lips,  
while their heart is far from Me,  
and their fear of Me  
is a commandment of men learned by rote,

**14** therefore I will again do wonders with this people,  
wonder upon wonder;  
the wisdom of their wise men will perish,  
and the understanding of their discerning men will be hidden."

**15** Woe to those who hide their plans deep from the LORD,  
whose deeds are in the dark,  
and who say, "Who sees us? Who knows us?"

**16** You turn things upside down!  
Shall the potter be regarded as the clay,  
that the thing made should say of its maker,  
"He did not make me,"  
or the thing formed say of him who formed it,  
"He has no understanding"?

**17** Is it not yet a very little while  
until Lebanon is turned into a fruitful field,  
and the fruitful field is regarded as a forest?

**18** In that day the deaf will hear the words of a book,  
and out of gloom and darkness  
the eyes of the blind will see.

**19** The humble will increase their joy in the LORD,  
and the poorest of mankind will rejoice in the Holy One of Israel.

**20** For the ruthless will come to nothing,  
and the scoffer will cease,  
and all who watch to do evil will be cut off,

**21** who make a man a sinner by a word,  
and lay a snare for the one who reproves at the gate,  
and turn aside the righteous with empty arguments.

**22** Therefore this is what the LORD, who redeemed Abraham, says concerning the house of Jacob:  
"Jacob will no longer be ashamed;  
his face will no longer grow pale.

**23** For when he sees his children,  
the work of My hands, in his midst,  
they will sanctify My name;  
they will sanctify the Holy One of Jacob,  
and will stand in awe of the God of Israel.

**24** Those who go astray in spirit will come to understanding,  
and those who grumble will accept instruction."

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "Ariel… she will be to Me like an Ariel"** — a name for Jerusalem, *the city where David camped* (*chanah*, the verb of v3, *I will camp against you*). *Ariel* reads as "lion of God," and the same word names the altar hearth in Ezekiel's temple (*ha-ari'el*, Ezekiel 43:15). The second sense makes v2 a threat: the city will become a hearth where fire burns.
- **v4 "your voice will be like a ghost's… your words will chirp from the dust"** — *ov* and *tsiftsef*, the words of 8:19, *Consult the ghosts and the spirits that chirp and mutter*. The city that consulted the dead will speak like them.
- **v10 "a spirit of deep sleep"** — *tardemah*, the sleep the LORD God *made fall upon the human* (Genesis 2:21). Paul quotes the line, joined to Deuteronomy 29:4: *God gave them a spirit of stupor, eyes that would not see and ears that would not hear* (Romans 11:8).
- **v13 "a commandment of men learned by rote"** — Jesus quotes the verse from the Greek: *This people honors Me with their lips, but their heart is far from Me; in vain do they worship Me, teaching as teachings the commandments of men* (Matthew 15:8–9). *In vain*, *matēn*, is the Greek's; the Hebrew has *learned by rote*.
- **v14 "wonder upon wonder; the wisdom of their wise men will perish"** — *hafle va-fele*, the *pele* of 9:6 and 25:1, here a wonder of judgment. Paul quotes the second line: *I will destroy the wisdom of the wise* (1 Corinthians 1:19).
- **v16 "Shall the potter be regarded as the clay"** — *yotser*, the potter, the word for the LORD who *formed* (27:11). Paul uses the picture: *Will what is formed say to the one who formed it, "Why did you make me like this?"* (Romans 9:20).
- **vv11–12, 18 "a sealed book… the deaf will hear the words of a book"** — the book no one could read, sealed and unlettered alike, is heard in that day; the eyes shut in v10 see. Jesus answers John's disciples with these signs: *the blind see again… and the deaf hear* (Matthew 11:5).
- **v24 "those who grumble will accept instruction"** — *rogenim*, the verb of Israel in the wilderness, *You grumbled in your tents* (Deuteronomy 1:27).

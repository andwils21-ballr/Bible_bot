# Isaiah 58

**1** Cry aloud, do not hold back;  
lift up your voice like a ram's horn;  
declare to My people their transgression,  
and to the house of Jacob their sins.

**2** Yet they seek Me day after day  
and delight to know My ways,  
as if they were a nation that did righteousness  
and did not forsake the justice of its God;  
they ask Me for just judgments;  
they delight to draw near to God.

**3** "Why have we fasted, and You do not see?  
Why have we afflicted our souls, and You take no notice?"  
But on the day of your fast you seek your own pleasure,  
and drive hard all your workers.

**4** You fast only to quarrel and fight  
and to strike with a wicked fist.  
Fasting as you do today  
will not make your voice heard on high.

**5** Is such the fast that I choose,  
a day for a man to afflict his soul?  
Is it to bow down his head like a reed,  
and to spread sackcloth and ashes under him?  
Will you call this a fast,  
and a day of favor to the LORD?

**6** Is not this the fast that I choose:  
to loose the bonds of wickedness,  
to undo the straps of the yoke,  
to let the oppressed go free,  
and to break every yoke?

**7** Is it not to share your bread with the hungry  
and bring the homeless poor into your house;  
when you see the naked, to cover him,  
and not to hide yourself from your own flesh?

**8** Then your light will break out like the dawn,  
and your healing will spring up quickly;  
your righteousness will go before you;  
the glory of the LORD will be your rear guard.

**9** Then you will call, and the LORD will answer;  
you will cry for help, and He will say, "Here I am."  
If you take away from among you the yoke,  
the pointing of the finger, and speaking wickedness,

**10** if you pour yourself out for the hungry  
and satisfy the desire of the afflicted,  
then your light will rise in the darkness,  
and your gloom will be like noon.

**11** The LORD will guide you continually  
and satisfy your desire in scorched places  
and strengthen your bones;  
you will be like a watered garden,  
like a spring of water  
whose waters do not fail.

**12** Those from among you will rebuild the ancient ruins;  
you will raise up the foundations of many generations;  
you will be called the repairer of the breach,  
the restorer of streets to live in.

**13** If you turn back your foot from the sabbath,  
from seeking your own pleasure on My holy day,  
and call the sabbath a delight,  
the holy day of the LORD honorable;  
if you honor it, not going your own ways,  
nor seeking your own pleasure,  
nor talking idly,

**14** then you will delight yourself in the LORD,  
and I will make you ride on the heights of the earth;  
I will feed you with the heritage of Jacob your father,  
for the mouth of the LORD has spoken.

## Notes

- **vv3, 5 "Why have we afflicted our souls?… a day for a man to afflict his soul"** — *innu nafshenu*, the words of the day of atonement, *you shall afflict yourselves* (Leviticus 16:29). The people keep the form of that fast and ask why God does not notice.
- **vv3, 13 "you seek your own pleasure"** — *chefets*, three times (v3; twice in v13), the word for the LORD's own *purpose* in 44:28, 53:10 and 55:11. On the fast day and on the sabbath the people pursue their own *chefets* and not His.
- **v5 "a day of favor to the LORD"** — *yom ratson*, as *a time of favor* in 49:8.
- **v6 "to let the oppressed go free"** — *ratsutsim*, "the crushed," the word of the *crushed reed* of 42:3. In the synagogue at Nazareth Jesus reads from Isaiah 61:1–2 and joins to it the Greek of this line, *to send away the crushed in release* (Luke 4:18).
- **v8 "the glory of the LORD will be your rear guard"** — *ya'asfekha*, the word of 52:12, *the God of Israel will be your rear guard*: the LORD goes before and behind those who set the oppressed free, as He went before and behind those who came out of Babylon.
- **v9 "He will say, 'Here I am'"** — *hinneni*, the answer of a servant to his master (Abraham's *Here I am*, Genesis 22:1). Here God gives that answer to the one who calls.
- **v12 "the repairer of the breach"** — *goder perets*, one who builds up a gap in a wall.
- **vv13–14 "call the sabbath a delight… then you will delight yourself in the LORD"** — *oneg* and *titanag*, one root. The delight given up for His day comes back as delight in Him. "I will make you ride on the heights of the earth" — the words of the Song of Moses, *He made him ride on the heights of the earth* (Deuteronomy 32:13).

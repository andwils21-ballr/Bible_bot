# Isaiah 4

**1** In that day seven women will take hold of one man, saying, "We will eat our own bread and wear our own clothing; only let us be called by your name; take away our disgrace."

**2** In that day the branch of the LORD shall be beautiful and glorious,  
and the fruit of the land shall be the pride and splendor  
of the survivors of Israel.

**3** Whoever is left in Zion  
and remains in Jerusalem will be called holy,  
everyone who is recorded for life in Jerusalem,

**4** when the Lord has washed away  
the filth of the daughters of Zion,  
and rinsed the bloodstains of Jerusalem from within her  
by a spirit of judgment and a spirit of burning.

**5** Then the LORD will create  
over the whole site of Mount Zion and over her assemblies  
a cloud by day, and smoke,  
and the shining of a flaming fire by night;  
for over all the glory there will be a canopy.

**6** There will be a shelter for shade from the heat by day,  
and a refuge and a hiding place from storm and rain.

## Notes

- **v2 "the branch of the LORD"** — *tsemach YHWH*, "what the LORD makes sprout." Set beside *the fruit of the land*, it reads as the growth God gives to the land after the judgment. Elsewhere *tsemach* becomes a title for the promised king: *I will raise up for David a righteous Branch* (Jeremiah 23:5), *My servant the Branch* (Zechariah 3:8). Here it stands lowercase, in step with *the fruit of the land*.
- **v5 "the LORD will create… a cloud by day… a flaming fire by night"** — *bara*, the verb of Genesis 1:1, used only with God as subject; and the pillar of the exodus, *by day in a pillar of cloud… and by night in a pillar of fire* (Exodus 13:21), now resting over all of Mount Zion.

# Isaiah 26

**1** In that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah:

"We have a strong city;  
He sets deliverance  
as its walls and rampart.

**2** Open the gates,  
that the righteous nation may enter,  
the nation that keeps faith.

**3** The mind that leans on You  
You keep in peace, peace,  
because it trusts in You.

**4** Trust in the LORD forever,  
for in Yah, the LORD,  
is an everlasting Rock.

**5** For He has brought low  
those who live on the height, the lofty city;  
He lays it low, lays it low to the ground,  
casts it to the dust.

**6** The foot tramples it,  
the feet of the poor,  
the steps of the needy."

**7** The path of the righteous is level;  
You make smooth the way of the righteous.

**8** In the path of Your judgments, LORD,  
we wait for You;  
Your name and Your remembrance  
are the desire of our soul.

**9** My soul longs for You in the night;  
my spirit within me seeks You at dawn;  
for when Your judgments are on the earth,  
the inhabitants of the world learn righteousness.

**10** If favor is shown to the wicked,  
he does not learn righteousness;  
in the land of uprightness he deals wrongly  
and does not see the majesty of the LORD.

**11** LORD, Your hand is lifted up,  
but they do not see it.  
Let them see Your zeal for the people, and be ashamed;  
let the fire for Your adversaries consume them.

**12** LORD, You will establish peace for us,  
for all that we have done  
You have done for us.

**13** LORD our God,  
other masters besides You have ruled over us,  
but Your name alone we call to mind.

**14** The dead do not live;  
the shades do not rise;  
so You have punished and destroyed them,  
and wiped out all memory of them.

**15** You have increased the nation, LORD;  
You have increased the nation; You are glorified;  
You have extended all the borders of the land.

**16** LORD, in distress they sought You;  
they poured out a whispered prayer  
when Your discipline was on them.

**17** Like a woman with child  
who writhes and cries out in her pangs  
when she is near to giving birth,  
so were we before You, LORD.

**18** We were with child; we writhed;  
we gave birth, as it were, to wind.  
We have brought no deliverance to the earth,  
and no inhabitants of the world have been born.

**19** Your dead will live;  
my dead ones will rise.  
Awake and sing for joy,  
you who dwell in the dust!  
For your dew is a dew of lights,  
and the earth will give birth to the shades.

**20** Come, my people, enter your rooms,  
and shut your door behind you;  
hide yourselves for a little while,  
until the fury has passed by.

**21** For the LORD is about to come out from His place  
to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity;  
the earth will uncover the blood shed on it,  
and will no longer cover its slain.

## Notes

- **vv14, 19 "The dead do not live; the shades do not rise… Your dead will live; my dead ones will rise"** — the same words, *metim*, *repha'im*, *qum*, said first of the oppressors (v14) and then turned for the LORD's own: *the earth will give birth to the shades*. "Awake" — *heqitsu*, the verb of Daniel 12:2, *many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth will awake*. "My dead ones" — *nevelati*, "my corpse," a singular with a plural verb, read as the speaker's own dead together; the Greek has *those in the tombs will be raised*.
- **vv17–19 "we gave birth, as it were, to wind… the earth will give birth to the shades"** — *naphal*, "fall," is used for being born: in v18 no inhabitants *fell*, were born; in v19 the earth *makes fall*, gives birth to, the dead. Israel's labor brought forth nothing; the LORD's dew brings forth the dead.
- **v20 "enter your rooms, and shut your door… until the fury has passed by"** — as on the night of the first Passover: *None of you shall go out from the doorway of his house until morning. The LORD will pass through to strike Egypt* (Exodus 12:22–23); *ya'avor*, "pass by," is the same verb.

# Isaiah 27

**1** In that day the LORD will punish,  
with His hard and great and strong sword,  
Leviathan the fleeing serpent,  
Leviathan the twisting serpent,  
and He will kill the dragon that is in the sea.

## The Song of the Vineyard Again

**2** In that day:  
"A pleasant vineyard, sing of it!

**3** I, the LORD, am its keeper;  
every moment I water it;  
so that no one may harm it,  
I keep it night and day.

**4** I have no wrath.  
If only I had briers and thorns to battle!  
I would march against them;  
I would burn them up together.

**5** Or let them take hold of My stronghold;  
let them make peace with Me;  
let them make peace with Me."

**6** In days to come Jacob will take root;  
Israel will blossom and put out shoots,  
and fill the face of the world with fruit.

**7** Has He struck him  
as He struck those who struck him?  
Or has he been slain  
as his slayers were slain?

**8** By driving her out, by sending her away, You contended with her;  
He removed her with His fierce wind  
in the day of the east wind.

**9** Therefore by this the iniquity of Jacob will be atoned for,  
and this will be the full fruit of the removal of his sin:  
when he makes all the stones of the altar  
like chalk stones crushed to pieces,  
no Asherim or incense altars will remain standing.

**10** For the fortified city is alone,  
a home deserted and forsaken like the wilderness;  
there the calf grazes,  
there it lies down and strips its branches.

**11** When its boughs are dry, they are broken off;  
women come and make fires of them.  
For this is a people without understanding;  
therefore He who made them will not have compassion on them;  
He who formed them will show them no favor.

**12** And it shall come to pass in that day, that the LORD will thresh out the grain from the flowing Euphrates to the Brook of Egypt, and you will be gathered one by one, sons of Israel.

**13** And it shall come to pass in that day, that a great ram's horn will be blown, and those who were lost in the land of Assyria and those who were driven out to the land of Egypt will come and worship the LORD on the holy mountain at Jerusalem.

## Notes

- **v1 "Leviathan the fleeing serpent… the dragon that is in the sea"** — *livyatan nachash bariach*, the creature of Job 26:13, *His hand pierced the fleeing serpent*, and of Psalm 74:14, *You crushed the heads of Leviathan*. *Tannin*, "dragon," is the word of *the great sea-monsters* of Genesis 1:21. The Greek renders *drakonta ophin*, "dragon, serpent," and Revelation joins the same two words for the devil: *the great dragon, the ancient serpent* (Revelation 12:9).
- **vv2–5 "A pleasant vineyard, sing of it!… If only I had briers and thorns"** — the song of the vineyard in 5:1–7 sung again with the ending reversed. There the LORD let the vineyard go to *briers and thorns* (*shamir va-shayit*, 5:6) and forbade the clouds to rain on it; here He waters it every moment, and the briers and thorns are what He would fight for it.
- **v5 "let them make peace with Me; let them make peace with Me"** — *ya'aseh shalom li, shalom ya'aseh li*, the same words twice with the order turned round.
- **v9 "the iniquity of Jacob will be atoned for"** — *yekhuppar*, the word refused to Jerusalem in 22:14, *Surely this iniquity will not be atoned for you until you die*. Here it is given, when the altars of other gods are ground to chalk.
- **v11 "He who made them… He who formed them"** — *osehu… yotsro*, the LORD as Maker and as potter (*yatsar*, the verb of Genesis 2:7, *The LORD God shaped the human of dust*).
- **v12 "the flowing Euphrates"** — *shibbolet ha-nahar*. *Shibbolet* is an ear of grain, and also a stream in flood (*the flood sweeps over me*, Psalm 69:2); it is the password of Judges 12:6, *Shibboleth*. Here both senses meet: the LORD threshes from the river's flood, and gathers Israel one by one like gleaned ears. The span is the land promised to Abram, *from the river of Egypt to the great river, the river Euphrates* (Genesis 15:18).
- **v13 "a great ram's horn will be blown"** — *shofar gadol*. Jesus speaks of the gathering in the same way: *He will send out His angels with a great trumpet, and they will gather His chosen from the four winds* (Matthew 24:31).

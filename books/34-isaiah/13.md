# Isaiah 13

**1** The oracle concerning Babylon, which Isaiah son of Amoz saw.

**2** On a bare hill raise a signal;  
lift up your voice to them;  
wave the hand  
for them to enter the gates of the nobles.

**3** I Myself have commanded My consecrated ones;  
I have called My mighty men for My anger,  
those who exult in My majesty.

**4** The sound of a tumult on the mountains,  
like that of a great people!  
The sound of an uproar of kingdoms,  
of nations gathered together!  
The LORD of hosts is mustering  
a host for battle.

**5** They come from a distant land,  
from the end of the heavens,  
the LORD and the weapons of His fury,  
to destroy the whole earth.

**6** Wail, for the day of the LORD is near;  
it will come like destruction from the Almighty.

**7** Therefore all hands will go limp,  
and every human heart will melt.

**8** They will be terrified;  
pangs and agony will seize them;  
they will writhe like a woman in labor.  
They will look at one another aghast;  
their faces will be aflame.

**9** The day of the LORD is coming,  
cruel, with fury and burning anger,  
to make the earth a desolation  
and to destroy its sinners from it.

**10** For the stars of the heavens and their constellations  
will not flash their light;  
the sun will be dark at its rising,  
and the moon will not shine its light.

**11** I will punish the world for its evil,  
and the wicked for their iniquity;  
I will put an end to the pride of the arrogant,  
and lay low the haughtiness of the ruthless.

**12** I will make man rarer than fine gold,  
and mankind than the gold of Ophir.

**13** Therefore I will make the heavens tremble,  
and the earth will be shaken out of its place,  
at the fury of the LORD of hosts  
on the day of His burning anger.

**14** Like a hunted gazelle,  
like sheep that no one gathers,  
each will turn to his own people,  
and each will flee to his own land.

**15** Whoever is found will be thrust through,  
and whoever is caught will fall by the sword.

**16** Their infants will be dashed to pieces before their eyes;  
their houses will be plundered,  
and their wives raped.

**17** I am about to stir up the Medes against them,  
who have no regard for silver  
and take no delight in gold.

**18** Their bows will dash the young men to pieces;  
they will have no mercy on the fruit of the womb;  
their eyes will not spare children.

**19** Babylon, the glory of kingdoms,  
the splendor and pride of the Chaldeans,  
will be like Sodom and Gomorrah  
when God overthrew them.

**20** It will never be inhabited  
or lived in for all generations;  
no Arab will pitch his tent there;  
no shepherds will rest their flocks there.

**21** But wild beasts will lie down there,  
and their houses will be full of howling creatures;  
there ostriches will live,  
and there goat-spirits will dance.

**22** Hyenas will cry in its strongholds,  
and jackals in its palaces of pleasure;  
its time is close at hand,  
and its days will not be prolonged.

## Notes

- **v6 "like destruction from the Almighty"** — *ke-shod mi-Shaddai*, a play of sounds: *shod*, "destruction," from *Shaddai*, the old name of God rendered *the Almighty*, the only time the name appears in Isaiah. Joel has the same line (Joel 1:15).
- **v10 "their constellations"** — *u-khsileihem*, the plural of *kesil*, Orion (Amos 5:8; Job 9:9; 38:31): one constellation's name used for them all. Jesus takes up the verse: *Immediately after the affliction of those days the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light* (Matthew 24:29).
- **v21 "goat-spirits"** — *se'irim*, "hairy ones," the word of Leviticus 17:7 for what Israel had been sacrificing to. It is also the ordinary word for he-goats, so the line may mean wild goats leaping in the ruins. The Greek has *daimonia*, demons, and Isaiah pairs *se'ir* with Lilith in another ruined land (34:14). "Howling creatures" — *ochim*, a word found only here; what creature it names is not known.

# Isaiah 17

**1** The oracle concerning Damascus.

Damascus is about to cease to be a city;  
it will become a heap of ruins.

**2** The cities of Aroer are deserted;  
they will be for flocks,  
which will lie down with no one to make them afraid.

**3** The fortress will vanish from Ephraim,  
and the kingdom from Damascus;  
and the remnant of Aram will be  
like the glory of the sons of Israel,  
declares the LORD of hosts.

**4** And it shall come to pass in that day,  
that the glory of Jacob will be brought low,  
and the fat of his flesh will grow lean.

**5** It will be as when the reaper gathers the standing grain,  
and his arm harvests the ears,  
and as when one gleans the ears of grain  
in the Valley of Rephaim.

**6** Gleanings will be left in it,  
as when an olive tree is beaten:  
two or three berries  
at the top of the highest bough,  
four or five  
on the branches of a fruitful tree,  
declares the LORD, the God of Israel.

**7** In that day man will look to his Maker,  
and his eyes will look to the Holy One of Israel.

**8** He will not look to the altars,  
the work of his hands,  
and he will not look at what his fingers have made,  
the Asherim and the incense altars.

**9** In that day his strong cities will be  
like the deserted places of the woods and the hilltops,  
which they deserted before the sons of Israel;  
and there will be desolation.

**10** For you have forgotten the God of your deliverance,  
and have not remembered the Rock of your stronghold;  
so you plant pleasant plants,  
and set them out with a stranger's vine-slips.

**11** On the day you plant them, you make them grow,  
and in the morning you make your seed blossom;  
yet the harvest will be a heap  
on the day of possession,  
and the pain past cure.

**12** Woe! The uproar of many peoples —  
they roar like the roaring seas;  
the thunder of nations —  
they thunder like mighty waters!

**13** The nations thunder like the thunder of many waters,  
but He rebukes them, and they flee far away,  
chased like chaff on the mountains before the wind,  
like whirling dust before the storm.

**14** At evening, sudden terror;  
before morning, they are gone.  
This is the portion of those who plunder us,  
the lot of those who rob us.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "Damascus… Ephraim"** — the two kingdoms that marched on Jerusalem together in 7:1–9, Aram with its capital Damascus and Ephraim, the northern kingdom of Israel. They fall together. "The remnant of Aram will be like the glory of the sons of Israel": like a glory that is fading (v4).
- **v4 "the fat of his flesh will grow lean"** — *mishman… yerazeh*, the two words of 10:16, where the LORD sends *leanness among his fat ones*, Assyria's. Now the same wasting comes on Jacob.
- **v5 "the Valley of Rephaim"** — near Jerusalem, where the Philistines came up against David (2 Samuel 5:18, 22). Its name is the word for *the shades* of the dead in 14:9, *repha'im*.
- **v6 "Gleanings will be left in it, as when an olive tree is beaten"** — *olelot*, what the law left on the tree and the vine for the poor: *When you beat your olive trees, you shall not go over the branches after you. It shall be for the guest, the orphan and the widow* (Deuteronomy 24:20–21). Israel's remnant is the few olives left at the top. The picture returns in 24:13.
- **vv7–8 "his Maker… what his fingers have made"** — *osehu* and *asu*, the same verb, *asah*, "to make." Man will look to the One who made him, not to what he made.
- **v9 "the deserted places of the woods and the hilltops, which they deserted before the sons of Israel"** — the Greek reads different words: *as the Amorites and the Hivites deserted them before the sons of Israel*, naming the peoples of the land who left their cities when Israel came in. The Hebrew's *amir*, "hilltop," is the word for *the top of the highest bough* in v6, where the last olives hang.
- **vv9–10 "his strong cities… the Rock of your stronghold"** — *ma'uzzo* and *ma'uzzekh*, the same word. Her strongholds are deserted because she forgot the Rock who was her stronghold. "The God of your deliverance" — *yish'ekh*, from the root of *yeshu'ah* in 12:2–3.
- **v11 "the harvest will be a heap on the day of possession"** — *ned qatsir be-yom nachalah*. *Ned* is a heap, a standing mass, the word for the waters that *stood like a wall* at the sea (Exodus 15:8). *Nachalah* is "possession, inheritance," as the Greek also reads it; with other vowels the line reads *the harvest flees in a day of sickness*. The line is uncertain; either way, the plants that grew in a day bring their planter pain past cure.
- **vv12–14 "before morning, they are gone"** — the nations roar like the sea, and one rebuke scatters them overnight. Isaiah tells the night it happened to Assyria's army: *the messenger of the LORD went out and struck down a hundred and eighty-five thousand in the camp of Assyria; and when people rose early in the morning, there were all the dead bodies* (37:36). "Like whirling dust… like chaff" — the prayer of Psalm 83:13, *make them like whirling dust, like chaff before the wind*.

# Isaiah 19

**1** The oracle concerning Egypt.

The LORD is riding on a swift cloud  
and is coming to Egypt;  
the idols of Egypt tremble before Him,  
and the heart of Egypt melts within it.

**2** "I will stir up Egyptians against Egyptians,  
and they will fight, each against his brother  
and each against his neighbor,  
city against city, kingdom against kingdom.

**3** The spirit of Egypt will be emptied out within it,  
and I will swallow up its counsel;  
they will consult the idols and the whisperers,  
the ghosts and the spirits.

**4** I will hand Egypt over  
to a hard master,  
and a fierce king will rule over them,"  
declares the Lord, the LORD of hosts.

**5** The waters of the sea will dry up,  
and the river will be parched and dry;

**6** the canals will stink;  
the streams of Egypt will dwindle and dry up;  
reed and rush will wither.

**7** The bare places by the Nile, at the mouth of the Nile,  
and everything sown by the Nile  
will dry up, be driven away, and be gone.

**8** The fishermen will mourn and lament,  
all who cast a hook into the Nile;  
those who spread nets on the water will languish.

**9** Those who work combed flax will be ashamed,  
and the weavers of white cloth.

**10** Its foundations will be crushed,  
and all who work for wages will be grieved in soul.

**11** The princes of Zoan are nothing but fools;  
the wisest of Pharaoh's counselors give senseless counsel.  
How can you say to Pharaoh,  
"I am a son of the wise,  
a son of ancient kings"?

**12** Where then are your wise men?  
Let them tell you  
and make known what the LORD of hosts  
has planned against Egypt.

**13** The princes of Zoan have become fools;  
the princes of Memphis are deceived;  
those who are the cornerstone of its tribes  
have led Egypt astray.

**14** The LORD has mixed within her  
a spirit of confusion,  
and they have led Egypt astray in all it does,  
as a drunkard staggers in his vomit.

**15** There will be nothing Egypt can do,  
head or tail, palm branch or reed.

## Egypt, My People

**16** In that day Egypt will be like women, and will tremble with fear before the hand that the LORD of hosts shakes over it.

**17** The land of Judah will become a terror to Egypt; everyone to whom it is mentioned will be afraid, because of what the LORD of hosts has planned against it.

**18** In that day there will be five cities in the land of Egypt that speak the language of Canaan and swear allegiance to the LORD of hosts. One of them will be called the City of Destruction.

**19** In that day there will be an altar to the LORD in the middle of the land of Egypt, and a standing stone to the LORD at its border.

**20** It will be a sign and a witness to the LORD of hosts in the land of Egypt. When they cry out to the LORD because of those who crush them, He will send them a deliverer and a defender, and he will rescue them.

**21** The LORD will make Himself known to Egypt, and Egypt will know the LORD in that day; they will worship with sacrifice and offering, and make vows to the LORD and keep them.

**22** The LORD will strike Egypt, striking and healing; they will return to the LORD, and He will hear their pleas and heal them.

**23** In that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria; Assyria will come into Egypt, and Egypt into Assyria, and Egypt will worship with Assyria.

**24** In that day Israel will be the third with Egypt and Assyria, a blessing in the middle of the earth,

**25** whom the LORD of hosts has blessed, saying: "Blessed be Egypt My people, and Assyria the work of My hands, and Israel My inheritance."

## Notes

- **v1 "The LORD is riding on a swift cloud"** — the LORD comes to Egypt Himself, and the land's gods and its heart give way. "The heart of Egypt melts" — *yimmas*, the verb used of Canaan's heart when Israel came (*our hearts melted*, Joshua 2:11).
- **v4 "a hard master"** — *adonim qasheh*. *Qasheh*, "hard," is the word of the *hard service* Egypt laid on Israel (Exodus 1:14). Now Egypt is handed to a hard master.
- **v15 "head or tail, palm branch or reed"** — the words of 9:14, where the LORD cut them off from Israel. Here Egypt has no one, high or low, who can do anything.
- **v18 "the City of Destruction"** — *ir ha-heres*. *Heres* is "tearing down," the verb of the command to Israel about the gods of Canaan: *You shall utterly tear them down and utterly break their standing stones* (Exodus 23:24); read so, it is the city where the idols are torn down. The Greek has *polis asedek*, "the City of Righteousness," the name given Jerusalem in 1:26.
- **v19 "a standing stone to the LORD"** — *matsevah*, what the law forbade in Israel: *You shall not set up for yourself a standing stone, which the LORD your God hates* (Deuteronomy 16:22). In Egypt one stands at the border, for the LORD.
- **vv20–22 "because of those who crush them… Egypt will know the LORD… striking and healing"** — the exodus told in reverse. Israel cried out because of *the crushing with which Egypt is crushing them* (Exodus 3:9, *lachats*); now Egypt cries out because of *lochatsim*, "those who crush," and the LORD sends a *deliverer*, as He did for Israel in Judges (*the LORD raised up a deliverer*, Judges 3:9). *Egypt will know that I am the LORD* (Exodus 7:5) was said of the plagues; here it is said of worship. *Nagaf*, "strike," is the verb of the last plague (*the LORD will pass through to strike Egypt*, Exodus 12:23), and here the stroke is followed by healing, as *I am the LORD your healer* was promised Israel (Exodus 15:26).
- **vv24–25 "Blessed be Egypt My people, and Assyria the work of My hands, and Israel My inheritance"** — *ammi*, "My people," the name the LORD gave Israel when He sent Moses to Pharaoh (Exodus 3:7), is given to Egypt, and Israel is the third, *a blessing in the middle of the earth*, as Abraham was told, *In you all the families of the ground shall be blessed* (Genesis 12:3). The Greek softens it: *Blessed be My people who are in Egypt and who are among the Assyrians*.

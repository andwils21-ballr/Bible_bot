# Isaiah 8

**1** The LORD said to me, "Take a large tablet and write on it in ordinary letters: For Maher-shalal-hash-baz."

**2** I called reliable witnesses to attest it for me, Uriah the priest and Zechariah son of Jeberechiah.

**3** Then I went to the prophetess, and she conceived and bore a son. The LORD said to me, "Call his name Maher-shalal-hash-baz;

**4** for before the boy knows how to cry 'My father' and 'My mother,' the wealth of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria will be carried off before the king of Assyria."

**5** The LORD spoke to me again:

**6** "Because this people has refused  
the waters of Shiloah that flow gently,  
and rejoices with Rezin and the son of Remaliah,

**7** therefore the Lord is about to bring up against them  
the waters of the River, mighty and many —  
the king of Assyria and all his glory;  
it will rise over all its channels  
and go over all its banks;

**8** it will sweep on into Judah,  
overflowing and passing through;  
it will reach up to the neck;  
and its outspread wings will fill  
the breadth of your land, Immanuel."

**9** Be broken, you peoples, and be shattered;  
give ear, all you distant parts of the earth;  
put on your armor, and be shattered;  
put on your armor, and be shattered.

**10** Make a plan, and it will come to nothing;  
speak a word, and it will not stand;  
for God is with us.

**11** For the LORD spoke thus to me, while His hand was strong upon me, and warned me not to walk in the way of this people, saying:

**12** "Do not call conspiracy  
all that this people calls conspiracy,  
and do not fear what they fear,  
or be in dread.

**13** The LORD of hosts — Him you shall regard as holy;  
let Him be your fear,  
and let Him be your dread.

**14** He will become a sanctuary,  
and a stone to strike against and a rock to stumble over  
for both houses of Israel,  
a trap and a snare  
for the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**15** Many shall stumble over them;  
they shall fall and be broken;  
they shall be snared and taken."

**16** Bind up the testimony;  
seal the instruction among my disciples.

**17** I will wait for the LORD,  
who is hiding His face from the house of Jacob,  
and I will hope in Him.

**18** Here am I and the children the LORD has given me, as signs and portents in Israel from the LORD of hosts, who dwells on Mount Zion.

**19** When they say to you, "Consult the ghosts and the spirits that chirp and mutter," should not a people consult their God? Should they consult the dead on behalf of the living?

**20** To the instruction and to the testimony! If they do not speak according to this word, it is because there is no dawn for them.

**21** They will pass through the land, hard-pressed and hungry. And it shall come to pass, when they are hungry, that they will be enraged and curse their king and their God, and turn their faces upward;

**22** they will look to the earth: distress and darkness, the gloom of anguish; and they will be driven into deep darkness.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "Maher-shalal-hash-baz"** — "Swift is the spoil, speedy the plunder": the name is written on a public tablet before the child is conceived, with two witnesses, so that no one can say afterward the prophecy was made up. One of them, Uriah the priest, is the priest who built King Ahaz an altar on the pattern of the one at Damascus (2 Kings 16:10).
- **v4 "before the boy knows how to cry 'My father'"** — the clock of 7:16, now shorter: before the child can talk, Damascus and Samaria will be plundered.
- **vv6–8 "the waters of Shiloah that flow gently… the waters of the River"** — the small stream that fed Jerusalem against the Euphrates in flood: the people who would not trust the quiet water will get the flood. "Your land, Immanuel" — the land belongs to the child of 7:14.
- **vv9–10 "for God is with us"** — *ki immanu el*: the name of 7:14 becomes the answer to every plan the nations make.
- **v11 "while His hand was strong upon me"** — *ke-chezqat ha-yad*, "with the strength of the hand": the prophet speaks under compulsion.
- **vv12–13 "do not fear what they fear… The LORD of hosts — Him you shall regard as holy"** — Peter applies the Greek of these lines to Christ: *do not fear what they fear… but in your hearts regard Christ the Lord as holy* (1 Peter 3:14–15, *ton de phobon autōn mē phobēthēte… kyrion de ton Christon hagiasate*).
- **v14 "a stone to strike against and a rock to stumble over"** — Paul and Peter join this verse to 28:16 and apply both to Christ: *a stone of stumbling and a rock of offense* (Romans 9:33; 1 Peter 2:8, *lithos proskommatos kai petra skandalou*). The same LORD is *a sanctuary* to some and a stumbling stone to others.
- **vv16–18 "seal the instruction among my disciples… Here am I and the children the LORD has given me"** — the prophet, his disciples and his sons (Shear-jashub, Maher-shalal-hash-baz) are themselves *signs and portents*. Hebrews puts vv17–18 in the mouth of Christ: *I will put my trust in Him… Here am I and the children God has given me* (Hebrews 2:13).
- **v19 "the ghosts and the spirits that chirp and mutter"** — *ovot… yidde'onim*, the pair of 2 Kings 23:24, *the ghosts and the spirits*, which Josiah would later put away.
- **v20 "there is no dawn for them"** — *ein lo shachar*: those who will not speak by the instruction walk into the darkness of vv21–22, and 9:2 answers it, *The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light*.

# Isaiah 21

**1** The oracle concerning the wilderness of the sea.

Like whirlwinds sweeping through the Negev,  
it comes from the wilderness,  
from a terrible land.

**2** A hard vision has been told to me:  
the traitor betrays,  
and the destroyer destroys.  
Go up, Elam!  
Lay siege, Media!  
All the sighing she caused  
I bring to an end.

**3** So my loins are filled with anguish;  
pangs have seized me,  
like the pangs of a woman in labor;  
I am bent double at what I hear;  
I am terrified at what I see.

**4** My heart staggers;  
horror has overwhelmed me;  
the twilight I longed for  
has been turned to trembling for me.

**5** They set the table,  
they spread the rugs,  
they eat, they drink.  
Rise up, commanders;  
oil the shield!

**6** For thus the Lord said to me:  
"Go, post a watchman;  
let him report what he sees.

**7** When he sees riders,  
horsemen in pairs,  
riders on donkeys,  
riders on camels,  
let him listen closely,  
very closely."

**8** Then he cried out like a lion:  
"On the watchtower, Lord,  
I stand continually by day,  
and at my post I am stationed  
every night.

**9** Here comes a man in a chariot,  
with horsemen in pairs!"  
Then he answered:  
"Fallen, fallen is Babylon,  
and all the carved images of her gods  
He has shattered to the ground."

**10** My threshed one, the grain of my threshing floor,  
what I have heard from the LORD of hosts,  
the God of Israel,  
I have told you.

## Dumah

**11** The oracle concerning Dumah.

One calls to me from Seir:  
"Watchman, what of the night?  
Watchman, what of the night?"

**12** The watchman says:  
"Morning comes, and also the night.  
If you would ask, ask;  
come back again."

## Arabia

**13** The oracle concerning Arabia.

In the thickets of Arabia you will spend the night,  
you caravans of Dedanites.

**14** To the thirsty bring water,  
you who live in the land of Tema;  
meet the fugitive with bread.

**15** For they have fled from the swords,  
from the drawn sword,  
from the bent bow,  
and from the weight of battle.

**16** For thus the Lord said to me: "Within a year, like the years of a hired man, all the glory of Kedar will come to an end;

**17** and the remnant of the archers, the mighty men of the sons of Kedar, will be few; for the LORD, the God of Israel, has spoken."

## Notes

- **v1 "the wilderness of the sea"** — a riddling name; v9 says what it is: Babylon. "Go up, Elam! Lay siege, Media!" (v2): the Medes, named in 13:17 as the power that takes Babylon, with Elam beside them.
- **v5 "They set the table… they eat, they drink. Rise up, commanders"** — a feast broken by a call to arms. Daniel tells Babylon's last night in the same way: Belshazzar's feast, and *that very night Belshazzar the Chaldean king was killed* (Daniel 5:1, 30). "Spread the rugs" — *tsafoh ha-tsafit*; *tsafit* is found only here. The verb *tsafah* is also "to watch," the root of *watchman* (v6) and *watchtower* (v8), and read so the line is *keep the watch*. The Greek has only *Prepare the table, eat, drink*. "Oil the shield" — leather shields were rubbed with oil, as Saul's was *no longer anointed with oil* (2 Samuel 1:21).
- **v8 "he cried out like a lion"** — *va-yiqra aryeh*, literally "he cried, a lion." Who cries, and why a lion, is not clear: the watchman shouting with a lion's voice is the plain reading. The Greek reads different words, *call Uriah to the watchtower*.
- **v9 "Fallen, fallen is Babylon"** — *nafelah nafelah Bavel*. Revelation takes up the cry twice, *Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great* (Revelation 14:8; 18:2).
- **v10 "My threshed one, the grain of my threshing floor"** — *medushati u-ven gorni*, literally "my threshed one and son of my threshing floor": the prophet's own people, beaten on the floor like grain, are the ones he tells.
- **vv11–12 "Dumah… Seir"** — Seir is Edom's mountain (Genesis 36:8), and *dumah* is "silence" (*my soul would soon have dwelt in silence*, Psalm 94:17). The watchman's answer is in words close to Aramaic, *tiv'ayun… be'ayu… eteyu*, "ask… come": morning comes, but night as well; they may come back and ask again.
- **vv16–17 "like the years of a hired man"** — the measure set for Moab in 16:14: counted exactly, no more. Kedar is one of Ishmael's sons (Genesis 25:13).

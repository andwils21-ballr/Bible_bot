# Isaiah 25

**1** LORD, You are my God;  
I will exalt You; I will praise Your name;  
for You have done wonders,  
plans formed long ago,  
faithful and sure.

**2** For You have made the city a heap,  
the fortified town a ruin;  
the strangers' palace is a city no more;  
it will never be rebuilt.

**3** Therefore a strong people will glorify You;  
the city of ruthless nations will fear You.

**4** For You have been a stronghold to the poor,  
a stronghold to the needy in his distress,  
a shelter from the storm, a shade from the heat;  
for the breath of the ruthless is like a storm against a wall,

**5** like heat in a dry land.  
You subdue the uproar of strangers;  
as heat is cooled by the shade of a cloud,  
the song of the ruthless is brought low.

**6** On this mountain the LORD of hosts will make for all peoples  
a feast of rich food, a feast of aged wine,  
of rich food full of marrow,  
of aged wine well refined.

**7** On this mountain He will swallow up  
the covering that is cast over all peoples,  
the veil that is spread over all nations.

**8** He will swallow up death forever;  
the Lord GOD will wipe away tears from all faces,  
and the reproach of His people He will take away from all the earth,  
for the LORD has spoken.

**9** It will be said in that day:  
"This is our God;  
we have waited for Him, and He will save us.  
This is the LORD; we have waited for Him;  
let us be glad and rejoice in His deliverance."

**10** For the hand of the LORD will rest on this mountain,  
and Moab will be trampled down where he stands,  
as straw is trampled down in a dung pit.

**11** He will spread out his hands in it,  
as a swimmer spreads his hands to swim,  
but his pride will be brought low  
with every stroke of his hands.

**12** The high fortifications of your walls  
He will bring down, lay low,  
cast to the ground, to the dust.

## Notes

- **v1 "You have done wonders, plans formed long ago"** — *pele*, "wonder," and *etsot*, "plans, counsels": the two words of the child's first name in 9:6, *Wonderful Counselor*, *pele yo'ets*. "Faithful and sure" — *emunah omen*, two forms of the root of *amen*.
- **vv6–8 "a feast… for all peoples… He will swallow up death forever"** — the meal is set on Zion for every nation. Jesus speaks of it: *many will come from east and west and recline at table with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven* (Matthew 8:11).
- **vv7–8 "He will swallow up the covering… He will swallow up death"** — *bila*, the same verb twice. Death is the great swallower, *let us swallow them alive like Sheol* (Proverbs 1:12); here it is swallowed. "Forever" — *la-netsach*; *netsach* can also mean "victory," as in *the glory, the victory and the majesty* (1 Chronicles 29:11), and Paul's Greek reads it so: *Death is swallowed up in victory* (1 Corinthians 15:54). The Greek of Isaiah reads the line the other way, *Death, having prevailed, swallowed*.
- **v8 "the Lord GOD will wipe away tears from all faces"** — Revelation takes up the line: *He will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and death shall be no more* (Revelation 21:4).
- **v9 "His deliverance"** — *yeshu'ato*, the word of 12:2–3, *God is my deliverance*.
- **v10 "a dung pit"** — *madmenah*, the dung pit, and also the name of a town in 10:31, *Madmenah is in flight*.
- **v11 "with every stroke of his hands"** — *im arbot yadav*, a word found only here; it is taken as the swimmer's strokes, and the range runs to "the cunning of his hands."

# Isaiah 44

**1** But now hear, Jacob My servant,  
Israel whom I have chosen.

**2** This is what the LORD says, the One who made you,  
who formed you from the womb and will help you:  
"Do not fear, Jacob My servant,  
Jeshurun whom I have chosen.

**3** For I will pour water on the thirsty land,  
and streams on the dry ground;  
I will pour My Spirit on your seed,  
and My blessing on your descendants.

**4** They will spring up among the grass  
like willows by flowing streams.

**5** One will say, 'I am the LORD's';  
another will call himself by the name of Jacob;  
another will write on his hand, 'The LORD's,'  
and take the name of Israel as a title."

**6** This is what the LORD says, the King of Israel,  
and his Redeemer, the LORD of hosts:  
"I am the first and I am the last;  
besides Me there is no god.

**7** Who is like Me? Let him proclaim it;  
let him declare it and set it in order before Me,  
since I appointed an ancient people.  
Let them declare what is to come,  
and what will happen.

**8** Do not tremble, and do not be afraid.  
Have I not told you from of old and declared it?  
You are My witnesses!  
Is there a God besides Me?  
There is no Rock; I know of none."

## The Idol Maker

**9** All who form idols are emptiness,  
and the things they delight in do not profit;  
their witnesses neither see nor know,  
so that they will be put to shame.

**10** Who forms a god or casts an idol  
that profits nothing?

**11** All his companions will be put to shame,  
and the craftsmen are only human.  
Let them all assemble, let them stand up;  
they will be terrified; they will be put to shame together.

**12** The ironsmith takes a cutting tool  
and works it over the coals;  
he forms it with hammers  
and works it with his strong arm;  
he becomes hungry, and his strength fails;  
he drinks no water and grows faint.

**13** The carpenter stretches a line;  
he marks it out with a stylus;  
he shapes it with planes  
and marks it with a compass;  
he shapes it into the figure of a man,  
with the beauty of a human,  
to dwell in a house.

**14** He cuts down cedars for himself,  
or takes a cypress or an oak  
and lets it grow strong among the trees of the forest;  
he plants a pine, and the rain makes it grow.

**15** Then it becomes fuel for a man;  
he takes part of it and warms himself;  
he kindles a fire and bakes bread;  
he also makes a god and worships it;  
he makes it an idol and bows down to it.

**16** Half of it he burns in the fire;  
over that half he eats meat;  
he roasts a roast and is satisfied;  
he also warms himself and says,  
"Aha, I am warm; I see the fire!"

**17** The rest of it he makes into a god, his idol;  
he bows down to it and worships;  
he prays to it and says,  
"Deliver me, for you are my god!"

**18** They do not know, nor do they understand;  
for their eyes are smeared over so that they cannot see,  
and their hearts so that they cannot understand.

**19** No one stops to think,  
nor is there knowledge or understanding to say,  
"Half of it I burned in the fire;  
I also baked bread on its coals;  
I roasted meat and have eaten.  
Shall I make the rest of it a detestable thing?  
Shall I bow down to a block of wood?"

**20** He feeds on ashes;  
a deluded heart has led him astray,  
and he cannot deliver himself or say,  
"Is there not a lie in my right hand?"

## I Have Redeemed You

**21** Remember these things, Jacob,  
and Israel, for you are My servant;  
I formed you; you are My servant;  
Israel, you will not be forgotten by Me.

**22** I have blotted out your transgressions like a cloud,  
and your sins like mist;  
return to Me, for I have redeemed you.

**23** Sing, you heavens, for the LORD has done it;  
shout, you depths of the earth;  
break out into singing, you mountains,  
you forest, and every tree in it!  
For the LORD has redeemed Jacob,  
and will be glorified in Israel.

**24** This is what the LORD says, your Redeemer,  
who formed you from the womb:  
"I am the LORD, who made all things,  
who alone stretched out the heavens,  
who spread out the earth by Myself;

**25** who frustrates the signs of the babblers,  
and makes fools of diviners;  
who turns wise men back,  
and makes their knowledge foolish;

**26** who confirms the word of His servant,  
and fulfills the counsel of His messengers;  
who says of Jerusalem, 'She shall be inhabited,'  
and of the cities of Judah, 'They shall be built,  
and I will raise up their ruins';

**27** who says to the deep, 'Be dry;  
I will dry up your rivers';

**28** who says of Cyrus, 'He is My shepherd,  
and he shall carry out all My purpose,'  
saying of Jerusalem, 'She shall be built,'  
and of the temple, 'Your foundation shall be laid.'"

## Notes

- **v2 "Jeshurun whom I have chosen"** — *Yeshurun*, from *yashar*, "upright," a name for Israel in the Song and Blessing of Moses: *Jeshurun grew fat and kicked* (Deuteronomy 32:15); *There is none like God, Jeshurun* (33:26). The Greek has *the beloved Israel*.
- **v3 "I will pour My Spirit on your seed"** — the promise of 32:15, *until the Spirit is poured out on us from on high*, here poured on the children.
- **v6 "I am the first and I am the last"** — as in 41:4 and 48:12; Revelation 1:17 gives the words to the risen Christ.
- **v8 "There is no Rock; I know of none"** — *tsur*, the name the Song of Moses gives the LORD: *The Rock — His work is whole* (Deuteronomy 32:4).
- **vv9–10 "All who form idols… Who forms a god"** — *yotsrei*, *yatsar*, the verb the LORD uses of forming Israel (vv2, 21, 24). In v9 the printed Hebrew has dots set over the word *hemmah*, "they" (*their witnesses, they neither see*), a mark of the scribes; the text gives no reason for it.
- **v12 "he grows faint"** — *va-yi'af*, the word of 40:28–31: the LORD *does not grow faint or weary*; the man who makes a god does.
- **v13 "the figure of a man, with the beauty of a human"** — *tavnit ish*, *tif'eret adam*: man was made in the likeness of God (Genesis 1:26), and the carpenter makes his god in the likeness of a man.
- **vv15–19 "Half of it he burns… the rest of it he makes into a god"** — one log does for the oven, the dinner and the god. "A detestable thing" — *to'evah*.
- **vv22–24 "blotted out… I have redeemed you… your Redeemer"** — *machiti*, as in 43:25; *ge'altikha*, *go'alekha*: the kinsman who buys back (41:14). "By Myself" (v24) — the read form *me-itti*, "from with Me," is followed; the written form is *mi itti*, "who was with Me?"
- **v28 "who says of Cyrus, 'He is My shepherd'"** — the conqueror of 41:2, 25, named at last. Chronicles and Ezra close and open with his decree: *A message from Cyrus king of Persia: The LORD, the God of heaven… has appointed me to build Him a house in Jerusalem* (2 Chronicles 36:23; Ezra 1:2).

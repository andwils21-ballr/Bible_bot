# Isaiah 46

**1** Bel bows down; Nebo stoops;  
their idols are on beasts and cattle;  
the things you carry are loaded  
as a burden on weary beasts.

**2** They stoop, they bow down together;  
they cannot save the burden,  
but they themselves go into captivity.

**3** Listen to Me, house of Jacob,  
and all the remnant of the house of Israel,  
you who have been loaded on Me from birth,  
carried from the womb;

**4** even to your old age I am He,  
and to gray hairs I will bear you.  
I have made, and I will carry;  
I will bear, and I will save.

**5** To whom will you liken Me and make Me equal,  
and compare Me, that we may be alike?

**6** Those who lavish gold from the purse,  
and weigh out silver on the scale,  
hire a goldsmith, and he makes it into a god;  
then they fall down and worship!

**7** They lift it on their shoulders, they bear it;  
they set it in its place, and it stands there;  
from its place it does not move.  
If one cries to it, it does not answer,  
or save him from his trouble.

**8** Remember this and stand firm;  
take it to heart, you transgressors.

**9** Remember the former things of old;  
for I am God, and there is no other;  
I am God, and there is none like Me,

**10** declaring the end from the beginning,  
and from ancient times things not yet done,  
saying, "My counsel shall stand,  
and I will do all My purpose,"

**11** calling a bird of prey from the east,  
the man of My counsel from a far country.  
I have spoken, and I will bring it to pass;  
I have planned it, and I will do it.

**12** Listen to Me, you stubborn of heart,  
you who are far from righteousness:

**13** I bring near My righteousness; it is not far off,  
and My deliverance will not delay;  
I will put deliverance in Zion,  
for Israel My glory.

---

## Notes

- **v11 "a bird of prey from the east, the man of My counsel"** — Cyrus, named in 44:28 and 45:1. The read form *atsati*, "My counsel," is followed.

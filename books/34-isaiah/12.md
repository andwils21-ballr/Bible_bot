# Isaiah 12

**1** In that day you will say:  
"I will give thanks to You, LORD,  
for though You were angry with me,  
Your anger has turned away,  
and You have comforted me.

**2** God is my deliverance;  
I will trust, and will not be afraid;  
for my strength and my song is Yah, the LORD,  
and He has become my deliverance."

**3** With joy you will draw water  
from the springs of deliverance.

**4** In that day you will say:  
"Give thanks to the LORD; call on His name;  
make His deeds known among the peoples;  
proclaim that His name is exalted.

**5** Sing praise to the LORD, for He has done gloriously;  
let this be known in all the earth.

**6** Cry out and shout for joy, you who live in Zion,  
for great among you is the Holy One of Israel."

## Notes

- **v1 "Your anger has turned away"** — *yashov appekha*. The refrain of the chapters before was *His anger has not turned away* (5:25; 9:12, 17, 21; 10:4), *lo shav appo*: the same verb and the same noun. The song begins where the refrain ended. "You will say" is singular here and plural in v4: one voice first, then all of them.
- **v2 "my strength and my song is Yah… and He has become my deliverance"** — word for word the Song of the Sea (Exodus 15:2), sung again in Psalm 118:14. Isaiah adds the name of the LORD to *Yah*: *Yah YHWH*. The chapter before ended on the sea struck and a people crossing it (11:15–16); the song that follows is the song Israel sang on the far shore.
- **vv2–3 "deliverance"** — *yeshu'ah*, three times in two verses: God is my deliverance, He has become my deliverance, the springs of deliverance. It is the root of the prophet's own name, *Yesha'yahu* (1:1), "the LORD delivers."
- **v6 "you who live in Zion… great among you is the Holy One of Israel"** — *yoshevet Tsiyyon*, feminine and singular: the city's people spoken to as one woman, as in *the daughter of Zion* (1:8; 10:32). The One the seraphim called *holy, holy, holy* (6:3) is *among you*, *be-qirbekh*.

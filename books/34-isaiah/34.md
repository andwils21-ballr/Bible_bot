# Isaiah 34

**1** Draw near, you nations, to hear;  
and listen, you peoples!  
Let the earth hear, and all that fills it;  
the world, and all that comes from it.

**2** For the LORD is enraged against all the nations,  
and furious against all their host;  
He has doomed them,  
He has given them over for slaughter.

**3** Their slain will be cast out,  
and the stench of their corpses will rise;  
the mountains will melt with their blood.

**4** All the host of heaven will rot away,  
and the skies roll up like a scroll;  
all their host will wither  
as a leaf withers from the vine,  
like a withered fig from the fig tree.

**5** For My sword has drunk its fill in the heavens;  
it descends on Edom,  
on the people I have doomed, for judgment.

**6** The LORD has a sword; it is full of blood;  
it is gorged with fat,  
with the blood of lambs and goats,  
with the fat of the kidneys of rams.  
For the LORD has a sacrifice in Bozrah,  
a great slaughter in the land of Edom.

**7** Wild oxen will fall with them,  
and young bulls with the mighty bulls.  
Their land will drink its fill of blood,  
and their dust will be gorged with fat.

**8** For the LORD has a day of vengeance,  
a year of recompense for the cause of Zion.

**9** Edom's streams will be turned into pitch,  
and its dust into sulfur;  
its land will become burning pitch.

**10** Night and day it will not be quenched;  
its smoke will go up forever.  
From generation to generation it will lie waste;  
none will pass through it forever and ever.

**11** The pelican and the hedgehog will possess it;  
the great owl and the raven will dwell in it.  
He will stretch over it the line of the wild  
and the stones of the waste.

**12** Its nobles — there is no one there to call it a kingdom,  
and all its princes will be nothing.

**13** Thorns will grow over its palaces,  
nettles and thistles in its fortresses;  
it will be a haunt of jackals,  
an abode for ostriches.

**14** Wild beasts will meet with hyenas;  
the goat-spirit will cry to his fellow;  
there too Lilith will settle  
and find herself a place of rest.

**15** There the owl will nest and lay her eggs,  
and hatch them and gather her young in her shadow;  
there too the kites will gather,  
each one with her mate.

**16** Seek in the book of the LORD, and read:  
not one of these will be missing;  
none will lack her mate.  
For My mouth has commanded,  
and His Spirit has gathered them.

**17** He has cast the lot for them;  
His hand has portioned it out to them by the line;  
they will possess it forever;  
from generation to generation they will dwell in it.

## Notes

- **vv2, 5 "He has doomed them… the people I have doomed"** — *hecherim*, *cheremi*: the ban that was laid on the cities of Canaan (11:15 note; Joshua 6:21) is laid on the nations, and on Edom by name.
- **v11 "the line of the wild and the stones of the waste"** — *qav tohu ve-avnei vohu*, the *tohu va-vohu* of Genesis 1:2, *wild and waste*, split across a builder's tools: the measuring line (*qav*, 28:17) and the plumb stones are used to unmake. "The pelican" — *qa'at*, the bird of Leviticus 11:18. "The hedgehog" — *qippod*, as in 14:23.
- **v14 "the goat-spirit… Lilith"** — *sa'ir*, as in 13:21; *lilit*, a word found only here, built like *layil*, "night." Read as a name, it is Lilith, a creature of the night; the Greek has *onokentauroi*, "donkey-centaurs." The wild things settle where Edom's kings were.
- **v16 "For My mouth has commanded"** — *ki fi hu tsivvah*, "for my mouth, it has commanded." The mouth is the LORD's (the line goes on *and His Spirit has gathered them*) or the prophet's, who has just told the reader to *seek in the book of the LORD*; the Hebrew does not say which. The Greek reads *the Lord commanded them*.

# Isaiah 11

**1** A shoot will come out from the stump of Jesse,  
and a branch from his roots will bear fruit.

**2** The Spirit of the LORD will rest on him,  
the Spirit of wisdom and understanding,  
the Spirit of counsel and might,  
the Spirit of knowledge and of the fear of the LORD.

**3** The fear of the LORD will be his delight;  
he will not judge by what his eyes see,  
or decide by what his ears hear,

**4** but with righteousness he will judge the poor,  
and decide with fairness for the humble of the earth;  
he will strike the earth with the rod of his mouth,  
and with the breath of his lips he will kill the wicked.

**5** Righteousness will be the belt around his waist,  
and faithfulness the belt around his loins.

**6** The wolf shall live as a guest with the lamb,  
and the leopard shall lie down with the young goat;  
the calf and the young lion and the fatling together,  
and a little child shall lead them.

**7** The cow and the bear shall graze;  
their young shall lie down together;  
and the lion shall eat straw like the ox.

**8** The nursing child shall play over the hole of the cobra,  
and the weaned child shall put his hand on the adder's den.

**9** They shall not harm or destroy  
in all My holy mountain;  
for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the LORD  
as the waters cover the sea.

## The Second Exodus

**10** And it shall come to pass in that day, that the nations will seek the root of Jesse, who stands as a signal for the peoples, and his resting place will be glorious.

**11** And it shall come to pass in that day, that the Lord will extend His hand a second time to recover the remnant of His people, those who are left, from Assyria, from Egypt, from Pathros, from Cush, from Elam, from Shinar, from Hamath, and from the coastlands of the sea.

**12** He will raise a signal for the nations,  
and gather the banished of Israel,  
and bring together the scattered of Judah  
from the four corners of the earth.

**13** The jealousy of Ephraim shall depart,  
and those who harass Judah shall be cut off;  
Ephraim shall not be jealous of Judah,  
and Judah shall not harass Ephraim.

**14** They shall swoop down on the slopes of the Philistines to the west;  
together they shall plunder the sons of the east;  
Edom and Moab shall be in their grasp,  
and the sons of Ammon shall obey them.

**15** The LORD will devote to destruction  
the tongue of the sea of Egypt,  
and wave His hand over the River  
with His scorching wind,  
and strike it into seven streams,  
and lead people across in sandals.

**16** There will be a highway for the remnant of His people  
who are left from Assyria,  
as there was for Israel  
on the day they came up from the land of Egypt.

## Notes

- **v1 "a shoot will come out from the stump of Jesse"** — *choter* and *geza*. The chapter before ends with a forest felled, its tall trees *hewn down* and Lebanon fallen (10:33–34). This one opens on a stump that sprouts. The line is named for Jesse, David's father, not for David: the tree has been cut back to its stump. "Will bear fruit" — *yifreh*; the Greek has *a flower will come up from the root*.
- **v2 "the Spirit of the LORD will rest on him"** — *ve-nachah*, "settle, rest," the verb of Numbers 11:25, *when the Spirit rested on them, they prophesied*; in v10 the same root gives his *resting place*. Then three pairs: wisdom and understanding, counsel and might, knowledge and the fear of the LORD. The Greek makes the last *knowledge and godliness*, and gives *the fear of God* to v3, so that it names seven where the Hebrew names six.
- **v3 "the fear of the LORD will be his delight"** — *va-hariho*, literally "his smelling." It is the verb of *the LORD smelled the soothing aroma* (Genesis 8:21), and of *I will not smell your solemn assemblies* (Amos 5:21), where to smell is to take pleasure. The judge who will not go by what his eyes see or his ears hear is given a third sense.
- **v4 "the rod of his mouth… the breath of his lips"** — *shevet*, the word of 10:5, where Assyria is *the rod of My anger*. This king's rod is his word. Paul applies the line to the Lord Jesus: *whom the Lord Jesus will kill with the breath of His mouth* (2 Thessalonians 2:8).
- **v6 "The wolf shall live as a guest with the lamb"** — *gar*, from *gur*, the verb of the guest who lives on land that is another's, *A guest and a resident I am among you* (Genesis 23:4). The wolf is the lamb's guest. Isaiah returns to vv6–9 at the book's end: *The wolf and the lamb shall graze together… They shall not harm or destroy in all My holy mountain, says the LORD* (65:25).
- **v9 "the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the LORD as the waters cover the sea"** — Habakkuk has the same line with one word more: *the knowledge of the glory of the LORD* (Habakkuk 2:14).
- **vv10, 12 "a signal for the peoples… He will raise a signal for the nations"** — *nes*, the signal of 5:26, where the LORD raises it to call the enemy *from the end of the earth*. Here it is raised to gather His own scattered people from its four corners. Paul quotes v10 from the Greek, which reads differently: *the root of Jesse, the one who rises to rule the nations; in Him the nations will hope* (Romans 15:12).
- **vv11, 15–16 "a second time to recover… as there was for Israel on the day they came up from the land of Egypt"** — *liqnot*, "to acquire, make one's own," the verb of the Song of the Sea, *the people You made Your own* (Exodus 15:16). The return is told as the first exodus told again: a sea struck, a crossing on foot, a highway.
- **v13 "The jealousy of Ephraim shall depart"** — Ephraim, the northern kingdom, had joined Aram against Judah (7:1–2), and *together they are against Judah* (9:21). The split ends.
- **v15 "devote to destruction"** — *hecherim*, the verb of the ban, used of the cities of Canaan (*They devoted to destruction all that was in the city*, Joshua 6:21). Here it is laid on a sea. The Greek has *will lay waste*. "His scorching wind" — *ba'yam*, a word found only here; "scorching" is the sense the context gives, and the Greek has *a violent wind*.

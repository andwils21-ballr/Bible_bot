# Isaiah 56

**1** Thus says the LORD:  
"Keep justice and do righteousness,  
for My deliverance is near to come,  
and My righteousness to be revealed.

**2** Blessed is the man who does this,  
and the son of man who holds it fast,  
who keeps the sabbath, not profaning it,  
and keeps his hand from doing any evil."

**3** Let not the foreigner who has joined himself to the LORD say,  
"The LORD will separate me from His people";  
and let not the eunuch say,  
"I am a dry tree."

**4** For thus says the LORD:  
"To the eunuchs who keep My sabbaths,  
who choose what pleases Me  
and hold fast My covenant,

**5** I will give, in My house and within My walls,  
a monument and a name  
better than sons and daughters;  
to each I will give an everlasting name  
that will not be cut off.

**6** The foreigners who join themselves to the LORD,  
to minister to Him, to love the name of the LORD,  
and to be His servants,  
everyone who keeps the sabbath, not profaning it,  
and holds fast My covenant —

**7** these I will bring to My holy mountain  
and make them joyful in My house of prayer;  
their burnt offerings and their sacrifices  
will be accepted on My altar;  
for My house will be called a house of prayer  
for all the peoples."

**8** The Lord GOD declares,  
who gathers the banished of Israel:  
"I will gather yet others to him  
besides those already gathered."

## Blind Watchmen

**9** All you beasts of the field, come to devour,  
all you beasts in the forest!

**10** His watchmen are blind;  
they are all without knowledge;  
they are all mute dogs;  
they cannot bark;  
dreaming, lying down,  
loving to slumber.

**11** The dogs have a greedy appetite;  
they never know when they have had enough.  
They are shepherds who cannot understand;  
they have all turned to their own way,  
each to his own gain, every last one.

**12** "Come," they say, "let me get wine,  
and let us drink our fill of strong drink;  
and tomorrow will be like this day,  
great beyond measure."

## Notes

- **vv3–5 "the foreigner… the eunuch… a monument and a name"** — the law kept the eunuch out of *the assembly of the LORD* (Deuteronomy 23:1), and certain foreigners with him (23:3). Here the foreigner and the eunuch are named first and given a place *in My house and within My walls*. "A monument and a name" — *yad va-shem*, "a hand and a name": a standing stone, as Absalom raised a pillar because *I have no son to keep my name in remembrance*, and it was called *Absalom's Monument* (*yad Avshalom*, 2 Samuel 18:18). The eunuch has no son; the LORD gives him what sons would have given, and better.
- **v3 "I am a dry tree"** — no branch will grow from him. The Ethiopian whom Philip met on the road was such a man: *a man of Ethiopia, a eunuch*, who had come *to worship in Jerusalem* and was reading Isaiah (Acts 8:27–28).
- **vv2, 4, 6 "who keeps the sabbath, not profaning it… hold fast My covenant"** — the sabbath is *a sign between Me and you* (Exodus 31:13); keeping it is the mark of belonging, and it is open to the foreigner and the eunuch as to Israel.
- **v5 "an everlasting name that will not be cut off"** — *shem olam… asher lo yikkaret*, the words of 55:13, *an everlasting sign that will not be cut off*. "To each" — the Hebrew turns from *them* to *him*, *I will give him*.
- **v7 "a house of prayer for all the peoples"** — Jesus quotes the line when He drives the sellers out of the temple: *My house shall be called a house of prayer* (Matthew 21:13). Mark gives the whole of it, *for all the nations* (Mark 11:17).
- **v8 "who gathers the banished of Israel"** — *nidchei Yisra'el*, as in 11:12, *gather the banished of Israel*. Beyond Israel's own exiles the LORD gathers *yet others*.
- **vv10–11 "His watchmen are blind… mute dogs… shepherds who cannot understand"** — the leaders who should warn the people (*watchmen*, the word of 52:8, *the voice of your watchmen*) sleep, and the guard dogs do not bark. "Dreaming" — *hozim*, a word found only here. "They have all turned to their own way, each to his own gain" — the words of 53:6, *we have turned, each one, to his own way*, now said of the shepherds.
- **v11 "The dogs have a greedy appetite"** — *azzei nefesh*, "strong of soul": their *nefesh*, their appetite, is fierce and never satisfied, the *soul* of 55:2 that is told to *delight itself in rich food*.

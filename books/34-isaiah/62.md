# Isaiah 62

**1** For Zion's sake I will not keep silent,  
and for Jerusalem's sake I will not rest,  
until her righteousness goes out like brightness,  
and her deliverance like a burning torch.

**2** The nations will see your righteousness,  
and all kings your glory;  
and you will be called by a new name  
that the mouth of the LORD will give.

**3** You will be a crown of beauty in the hand of the LORD,  
and a royal diadem in the palm of your God.

**4** You will no longer be called Forsaken,  
and your land will no longer be called Desolate;  
but you will be called My Delight Is in Her,  
and your land Married;  
for the LORD delights in you,  
and your land will be married.

**5** For as a young man marries a virgin,  
so your sons will marry you;  
and as the bridegroom rejoices over the bride,  
so your God will rejoice over you.

**6** On your walls, Jerusalem,  
I have set watchmen;  
all day and all night  
they will never be silent.  
You who remind the LORD,  
take no rest,

**7** and give Him no rest  
until He establishes Jerusalem  
and makes it a praise in the earth.

**8** The LORD has sworn by His right hand  
and by His mighty arm:  
"I will no longer give your grain  
as food to your enemies,  
and foreigners will not drink your new wine  
for which you have labored;

**9** but those who gather it will eat it  
and praise the LORD,  
and those who collect it will drink it  
in the courts of My sanctuary."

**10** Go through, go through the gates;  
prepare the way for the people;  
build up, build up the highway;  
clear it of stones;  
lift up a signal over the peoples.

**11** The LORD has proclaimed  
to the end of the earth:  
Say to the daughter of Zion,  
"Your deliverance is coming;  
His reward is with Him,  
and His recompense before Him."

**12** They will be called The Holy People,  
The Redeemed of the LORD;  
and you will be called Sought Out,  
A City Not Forsaken.

## Notes

- **v1 "like brightness… like a burning torch"** — *nogah*, the *brightness* of 60:3, *kings to the brightness of your rising*. Zion's deliverance is seen from far off, like a torch at night.
- **vv2, 4, 12 "a new name… My Delight Is in Her… Married… Sought Out"** — the names Zion is given answer the names she had: *Azuvah*, "Forsaken," becomes *Cheftsi-vah*, "My delight is in her," and *Shemamah*, "Desolate," becomes *Be'ulah*, "Married" (the *married one* of 54:1). Written as names they are *Hephzibah* and *Beulah*; *Hephzibah* was the name of the mother of Manasseh (2 Kings 21:1). The chapter ends with two more: *Derushah*, "Sought Out," and *A City Not Forsaken*.
- **v4 "for the LORD delights in you"** — *chafets*, the verb of the name *Cheftsi-vah*; the LORD whose *purpose* (*chefets*) the servant carried out (53:10) now *delights* in Zion.
- **v5 "so your sons will marry you"** — *yiv'alukh banayikh*. Read as the Hebrew vowels have it, the sons of the city take her as a bride: the people settle the land and possess it, as *your land will be married* in v4. The same letters read *bonayikh*, "your Builder" (plural in form, as *your Maker* and *your husband* in 54:5), which would make the LORD the one who marries her, as the next line says, *so your God will rejoice over you*; the Greek has *so your sons will live with you*.
- **v6 "You who remind the LORD"** — *ha-mazkirim*, the title of Joah son of Asaph, *the recorder* (36:3). The watchmen keep the LORD's promise before Him, day and night.
- **vv8–9 "foreigners will not drink your new wine for which you have labored"** — the oath undoes the curse of Deuteronomy 28:33, *A people you have not known will eat the fruit of your ground and all your labor*.
- **v10 "prepare the way… build up, build up the highway"** — *pannu derekh*, the words of 40:3, and *sollu sollu*, the words of 57:14. "Lift up a signal" — *nes*, as in 11:12 and 49:22.
- **v11 "Your deliverance is coming; His reward is with Him, and His recompense before Him"** — the last line is 40:10 word for word, said there of the Lord GOD. Here *deliverance* (*yesha*) is spoken of as a person; the Greek has *Your Savior has come*. Matthew's account of the entry into Jerusalem begins with this verse's words, *Say to the daughter of Zion* (Matthew 21:5).

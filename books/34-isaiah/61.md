# Isaiah 61

**1** The Spirit of the Lord GOD is on me,  
because the LORD has anointed me  
to bring good news to the poor;  
He has sent me to bind up the brokenhearted,  
to proclaim liberty to the captives,  
and the opening of the prison to those who are bound;

**2** to proclaim the year of the LORD's favor,  
and the day of vengeance of our God;  
to comfort all who mourn;

**3** to grant to those who mourn in Zion,  
to give them a garland instead of ashes,  
the oil of gladness instead of mourning,  
the garment of praise instead of a faint spirit;  
and they will be called oaks of righteousness,  
the planting of the LORD, that He may be glorified.

**4** They will build up the ancient ruins;  
they will raise up the former desolations;  
they will renew the ruined cities,  
the desolations of many generations.

**5** Strangers will stand and shepherd your flocks;  
foreigners will be your plowmen and vinedressers.

**6** But you will be called priests of the LORD;  
they will speak of you as ministers of our God;  
you will eat the wealth of the nations,  
and in their glory you will boast.

**7** Instead of your shame there will be a double portion;  
instead of disgrace they will rejoice in their portion;  
therefore in their land they will possess a double portion;  
everlasting joy will be theirs.

**8** For I, the LORD, love justice;  
I hate robbery and wrongdoing;  
I will faithfully give them their reward,  
and make an everlasting covenant with them.

**9** Their offspring will be known among the nations,  
and their descendants among the peoples;  
all who see them will acknowledge  
that they are the offspring the LORD has blessed.

**10** I will greatly rejoice in the LORD;  
my soul will exult in my God;  
for He has clothed me with garments of deliverance;  
He has wrapped me in a robe of righteousness,  
as a bridegroom wears a garland like a priest,  
and as a bride adorns herself with her jewels.

**11** For as the earth brings forth its sprouts,  
and as a garden makes what is sown in it spring up,  
so the Lord GOD will make righteousness and praise  
spring up before all the nations.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "The Spirit of the Lord GOD is on me, because the LORD has anointed me"** — *mashach*, "anointed," the verb behind *mashiach*, the title given to Cyrus in 45:1. In the synagogue at Nazareth Jesus reads these lines from the Greek, stops at *to proclaim the year of the Lord's favor*, rolls up the scroll and says, *Today this scripture has been fulfilled in your hearing* (Luke 4:16–21). He stops before *the day of vengeance*.
- **v1 "to proclaim liberty to the captives"** — *deror*, the liberty of the jubilee, *proclaim liberty in the land to all who live in it* (Leviticus 25:10). "The opening of the prison" — *peqach-qoach*, "opening, opening," of eyes or of doors; the Greek reads it of the eyes, *recovery of sight to the blind*, and Luke 4:18 follows the Greek.
- **vv2–3 "the year of the LORD's favor… to comfort all who mourn"** — *ratson*, as *a time of favor* (49:8) and *a day of favor* (58:5); *to comfort*, the word of 40:1, *Comfort, comfort My people*.
- **v3 "a garland instead of ashes"** — *pe'er tachat efer*, two words with the same letters in another order: the mourner's ashes on the head are exchanged for a festal headdress. "Oaks of righteousness" — *elei ha-tsedeq*, the *oaks* of 1:29 and 57:5, where idols were worshipped, now a name for the people. "The planting of the LORD, that He may be glorified" — the words of 60:21.
- **v6 "you will be called priests of the LORD"** — what Israel was called to be at Sinai, *a kingdom of priests and a holy nation* (Exodus 19:6). Peter gives the name to the church, *a royal priesthood, a holy nation* (1 Peter 2:9).
- **v7 "a double portion"** — *mishneh*, "a second, a double share." The verse moves from *your shame* to *they will rejoice*, *you* and *they* in the same line, as the Hebrew has it.
- **v8 "I hate robbery and wrongdoing"** — *gazel be-avlah*, following the Greek, *robbery from injustice*: the LORD who loves justice hates what was done to His people, and so repays them (*I will faithfully give them their reward*). The Hebrew vowels read the same letters *be-olah*, "with a burnt offering": *I hate robbery with a burnt offering*, a sacrifice brought from stolen goods.
- **v10 "as a bridegroom wears a garland like a priest"** — *yekhahen pe'er*, "acts the priest with a garland": the verb is *kohen*, "priest," and the *pe'er* is the garland of v3. The LORD put on righteousness and deliverance Himself in 59:17; here He clothes the one who speaks with *garments of deliverance* and *a robe of righteousness*.

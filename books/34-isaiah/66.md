# Isaiah 66

**1** This is what the LORD says:  
Heaven is My throne,  
and the earth is My footstool;  
where is the house you would build for Me,  
and where is the place of My rest?

**2** All these things My hand has made,  
and so all these things came to be,  
declares the LORD.  
But this is the one I will look to:  
the one who is poor and broken in spirit,  
and trembles at My word.

**3** Whoever slaughters an ox is like one who kills a man;  
whoever sacrifices a lamb, like one who breaks a dog's neck;  
whoever presents a grain offering, like one who offers pig's blood;  
whoever burns frankincense as a memorial, like one who blesses an idol.  
They have chosen their own ways,  
and their soul delights in their loathsome things;

**4** so I also will choose harsh treatment for them,  
and bring on them what they dread;  
because when I called, no one answered;  
when I spoke, they did not listen;  
but they did what was evil in My eyes,  
and chose what I did not delight in.

**5** Hear the word of the LORD,  
you who tremble at His word:  
Your brothers who hate you  
and cast you out for My name's sake  
have said, "Let the LORD be glorified,  
so that we may see your joy";  
but they will be put to shame.

**6** A sound of uproar from the city!  
A sound from the temple!  
The sound of the LORD,  
repaying His enemies what they deserve!

**7** Before she was in labor, she gave birth;  
before her pain came upon her,  
she delivered a male child.

**8** Who has heard such a thing?  
Who has seen such things?  
Can a land be born in one day?  
Can a nation be brought forth at once?  
Yet as soon as Zion was in labor,  
she gave birth to her sons.

**9** "Shall I bring to the mouth of the womb  
and not bring to birth?" says the LORD.  
"Shall I, who bring to birth, shut the womb?"  
says your God.

**10** Rejoice with Jerusalem,  
and be glad for her, all you who love her;  
rejoice with her in joy,  
all you who mourn over her,

**11** so that you may nurse and be satisfied  
at her comforting breast,  
so that you may drink deeply and delight  
in her abundant glory.

**12** For this is what the LORD says:  
I am about to extend peace to her like a river,  
and the glory of the nations like an overflowing stream;  
and you will nurse; you will be carried on the hip  
and bounced on the knees.

**13** As one whom his mother comforts,  
so I will comfort you;  
you will be comforted in Jerusalem.

**14** You will see, and your heart will rejoice;  
your bones will flourish like the grass;  
the hand of the LORD will be known to His servants,  
and His indignation to His enemies.

**15** For the LORD will come in fire,  
and His chariots like the whirlwind,  
to pay back His anger in fury,  
and His rebuke in flames of fire.

**16** For by fire the LORD will enter into judgment,  
and by His sword, with all flesh;  
and those slain by the LORD will be many.

**17** Those who consecrate and purify themselves  
to go into the gardens,  
following one in the middle,  
who eat the flesh of pigs,  
loathsome things and mice,  
will come to an end together, declares the LORD.

**18** As for Me, I know their works and their thoughts;  
the time is coming to gather all nations and tongues;  
and they will come and see My glory.

**19** I will set a sign among them, and I will send survivors from them to the nations: to Tarshish, Pul and Lud, who draw the bow, to Tubal and Javan, to the far coastlands that have not heard of My fame or seen My glory; and they will declare My glory among the nations.

**20** They will bring all your brothers from all the nations as an offering to the LORD, on horses, in chariots, in covered wagons, on mules and on swift camels, to My holy mountain Jerusalem, says the LORD, just as the sons of Israel bring their offering in a clean vessel to the house of the LORD.

**21** I will also take some of them for priests and for Levites, says the LORD.

**22** For as the new heavens and the new earth  
that I am making  
will remain before Me, declares the LORD,  
so your offspring and your name will remain.

**23** From new moon to new moon,  
and from sabbath to sabbath,  
all flesh will come to worship before Me,  
says the LORD.

**24** They will go out and look  
on the corpses of the men  
who have rebelled against Me;  
for their worm will not die,  
and their fire will not be quenched,  
and they will be an abhorrence to all flesh.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "Heaven is My throne, and the earth is My footstool; where is the house you would build for Me?"** — Solomon said it at the dedication of the temple: *The heavens, even the highest heavens, cannot contain You; how much less this house that I have built!* (1 Kings 8:27). Stephen, before the council, says *the Most High does not dwell in houses made by hands*, and quotes these verses from the Greek (Acts 7:48–50).
- **vv2, 5 "trembles at My word"** — *chared*, "trembling." The same word names the faithful in Ezra's day: *everyone who trembled at the words of the God of Israel* (Ezra 9:4; also 10:3). Here those who tremble are cast out by their own brothers (v5).
- **v3 "Whoever slaughters an ox is like one who kills a man"** — the Hebrew sets each pair side by side with no word between: *slaughtering the ox, striking a man; sacrificing the lamb, breaking a dog's neck*. Read with *as*, which the Greek has, the offering of one who is not *poor and broken in spirit* (v2) counts as murder and as an unclean sacrifice. Without *as*, the same people do both, bringing the LORD's offerings and the rites of 65:3–4 together. *Breaking the neck* is the verb of the law for the firstling donkey that is not redeemed (Exodus 13:13).
- **v4 "because when I called, no one answered"** — the charge of 65:12, said there to them, *you did not answer*, and here said of them.
- **vv7–9 "she delivered a male child… Shall I bring to the mouth of the womb and not bring to birth?"** — *ashbir*, from *mashber*, the word of Hezekiah's message in the siege, *The children have come to the mouth of the womb, and there is no strength to give birth* (37:3). The LORD answers with the same picture: what He brings to the point of birth, He delivers. John sees a woman who *gave birth to a son, a male child, who is to shepherd all the nations with a rod of iron* (Revelation 12:5).
- **vv11–13 "carried on the hip… As one whom his mother comforts"** — the daughters *carried on the hip* (60:4), and the *peace like a river* that Israel would have had (48:18), now come. The LORD, who said a mother may forget her nursing child, *yet I will not forget you* (49:15), here is the mother; *comfort*, three times in v13, is the word of 40:1, *Comfort, comfort My people*.
- **v17 "following one in the middle… the flesh of pigs, loathsome things and mice"** — the read form is *achat*, "one" (a woman); the written form is *achad*, "one" (a man): someone leads the rite in the middle of the garden. The pig and the mouse are both unclean in the law (Leviticus 11:7, 29).
- **v18 "As for Me, I know their works and their thoughts"** — the Hebrew has no verb: *And I — their works and their thoughts — it is coming, to gather all nations*. *I know* is supplied; the Greek has no verb there either.
- **vv19–21 "Tarshish, Pul and Lud… Tubal and Javan… covered wagons… some of them"** — the far peoples of Genesis 10, where *Javan and Tubal* are sons of Japheth (Genesis 10:2); *Javan* is Greece, and the Greek has *Hellas*. Where the Hebrew has *Pul* and *who draw the bow*, the Greek has *Put* and *Meshech*. The *covered wagons* (*tsabbim*) are those the chiefs brought for the dwelling (Numbers 7:3). *Some of them* may be the brothers brought home or the nations who bring them; the Hebrew does not say which.
- **v24 "their worm will not die, and their fire will not be quenched"** — Jesus takes these words for the place *where their worm does not die and the fire is not quenched* (Mark 9:48). "An abhorrence" — *dera'on*, found elsewhere only in Daniel 12:2, *some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting abhorrence*. The book that began with sons who *have rebelled against Me* (1:2) ends with *the men who have rebelled against Me*, the same verb.

# Isaiah 5

## The Song of the Vineyard

**1** Let me sing for my beloved  
my love song about his vineyard:  
My beloved had a vineyard  
on a very fertile hill.

**2** He dug it and cleared it of stones,  
and planted it with choice vines;  
he built a watchtower in the middle of it,  
and also hewed out a winepress in it;  
and he looked for it to yield grapes,  
but it yielded rotten grapes.

**3** So now, inhabitants of Jerusalem  
and men of Judah,  
judge between me and my vineyard.

**4** What more was there to do for my vineyard  
that I have not done in it?  
Why, when I looked for it to yield grapes,  
did it yield rotten grapes?

**5** And now, let me tell you  
what I am about to do to my vineyard:  
I will remove its hedge,  
and it shall be grazed bare;  
I will break down its wall,  
and it shall be trampled.

**6** I will make it a waste;  
it shall not be pruned or hoed,  
and briers and thorns shall come up;  
I will also command the clouds  
to send no rain upon it.

**7** For the vineyard of the LORD of hosts  
is the house of Israel,  
and the men of Judah  
are the planting He delighted in;  
He looked for justice,  
but there was bloodshed;  
for righteousness,  
but there was a cry.

## Six Woes

**8** Woe to those who join house to house,  
who add field to field,  
until there is no room left,  
and you are made to dwell alone in the middle of the land!

**9** In my hearing the LORD of hosts has sworn:  
Surely many houses shall be desolate,  
large and fine ones, without inhabitant.

**10** For ten acres of vineyard shall yield one bath,  
and a homer of seed shall yield an ephah.

**11** Woe to those who rise early in the morning  
to chase strong drink,  
who stay late into the evening  
as wine inflames them!

**12** They have lyre and harp,  
tambourine and flute and wine at their feasts,  
but they do not regard the deeds of the LORD,  
or see the work of His hands.

**13** Therefore My people go into exile  
for lack of knowledge;  
their honored men are starving,  
and their multitude is parched with thirst.

**14** Therefore Sheol has opened wide its throat,  
and opened its mouth beyond measure;  
and down go her splendor and her multitude,  
her din, and whoever exults in her.

**15** So man is humbled,  
and each one brought low,  
and the eyes of the haughty are brought low.

**16** But the LORD of hosts is exalted in justice,  
and the holy God is shown holy in righteousness.

**17** Lambs shall graze as in their pasture,  
and guests shall eat among the ruins of the fat ones.

**18** Woe to those who drag iniquity with cords of falsehood,  
and sin as with cart ropes,

**19** who say, "Let Him hurry,  
let Him speed His work, so that we may see it;  
let the plan of the Holy One of Israel  
draw near and come, so that we may know it!"

**20** Woe to those who call evil good  
and good evil,  
who put darkness for light  
and light for darkness,  
who put bitter for sweet  
and sweet for bitter!

**21** Woe to those who are wise in their own eyes,  
and clever in their own sight!

**22** Woe to those who are heroes at drinking wine,  
and valiant men at mixing strong drink,

**23** who acquit the guilty for a bribe,  
and take from the righteous what is right!

**24** Therefore, as the tongue of fire devours the stubble,  
and dry grass sinks down in the flame,  
so their root will be like rot,  
and their blossom will go up like dust;  
for they have rejected the instruction of the LORD of hosts,  
and spurned the word of the Holy One of Israel.

**25** Therefore the anger of the LORD burned against His people,  
and He stretched out His hand against them and struck them;  
the mountains quaked,  
and their corpses were like refuse in the middle of the streets.  
For all this His anger has not turned away,  
and His hand is still stretched out.

## The Nation from Afar

**26** He will raise a signal for nations far away,  
and whistle for them from the end of the earth;  
swiftly, speedily they come!

**27** None of them is weary, none stumbles;  
none slumbers or sleeps;  
not a waistband is loosened,  
not a sandal strap broken.

**28** Their arrows are sharp,  
all their bows are bent;  
their horses' hooves are counted as flint,  
and their wheels as the whirlwind.

**29** Their roar is like a lioness;  
they roar like young lions;  
they growl and seize the prey,  
and carry it off, and no one can rescue.

**30** They will growl over it in that day  
like the growling of the sea;  
and if one looks to the land:  
darkness and distress,  
and the light darkened by its clouds.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "my beloved… my love song… on a very fertile hill"** — *yedidi… shirat dodi*, the language of the Song of Songs; the prophet sings as the friend of the bridegroom. The hill is *qeren ben shamen*, "a horn, son of oil": a hilltop of rich soil.
- **vv2, 4 "rotten grapes"** — *be'ushim*, from *ba'ash*, "to stink": not wild grapes but grapes gone bad on the vine, after every care.
- **vv3–6 "judge between me and my vineyard"** — the hearers are made the judges of a case they do not yet know is their own, as Nathan made David judge in the story of the ewe lamb, *You are the man!* (2 Samuel 12:7). The owner is still a man in the song; only in v6, *I will command the clouds to send no rain*, does the speaker show who He is, and v7 names Him.
- **v5 "it shall be grazed bare… it shall be trampled"** — *le-va'er… le-mirmas*: the verb of 3:14, *you have devoured the vineyard*, and the noun of 1:12, *this trampling of My courts*.
- **v7 "He looked for justice, but there was bloodshed; for righteousness, but there was a cry"** — *mishpat… mispach; tsedaqah… tse'aqah*: each pair differs by a sound or a letter. What the LORD looked for and what He found sound almost alike.
- **vv8–23 "Woe"** — six woes (vv8, 11, 18, 20, 21, 22), on land-grabbing, drunkenness, mocking God, calling evil good, self-made wisdom, and justice for sale (*who acquit the guilty for a bribe*; compare Exodus 23:8, *a bribe blinds the clear-sighted*).
- **v14 "Sheol has opened wide its throat"** — *hirchivah… nafshah*: *nefesh* here is the throat, the appetite, as in Leviticus 17:11; the grave swallows the city's noise and pride.
- **v16 "the holy God is shown holy in righteousness"** — *niqdash*, as the LORD said at the death of Nadab and Abihu, *Through those near Me I will be shown holy* (Leviticus 10:3).
- **v25 "For all this His anger has not turned away, and His hand is still stretched out"** — a refrain that returns four times (9:12, 17, 21; 10:4).
- **v27 "none slumbers or sleeps"** — *lo yanum ve-lo yishan*, the words Psalm 121:4 says of the LORD, *He who keeps Israel will neither slumber nor sleep*: the army He summons has His tirelessness.

# Isaiah 60

**1** Arise, shine, for your light has come,  
and the glory of the LORD has risen on you.

**2** For darkness covers the earth,  
and thick darkness the peoples;  
but the LORD will rise on you,  
and His glory will be seen on you.

**3** Nations will come to your light,  
and kings to the brightness of your rising.

**4** Lift up your eyes all around and see:  
they all gather together, they come to you;  
your sons will come from afar,  
and your daughters will be carried on the hip.

**5** Then you will see and be radiant;  
your heart will tremble and swell,  
because the abundance of the sea will be turned to you;  
the wealth of the nations will come to you.

**6** A multitude of camels will cover you,  
the young camels of Midian and Ephah;  
all those from Sheba will come;  
they will bring gold and frankincense,  
and proclaim the praises of the LORD.

**7** All the flocks of Kedar will be gathered to you;  
the rams of Nebaioth will minister to you;  
they will come up with favor on My altar,  
and I will beautify My beautiful house.

**8** Who are these that fly like a cloud,  
and like doves to their windows?

**9** For the coastlands wait for Me,  
and the ships of Tarshish first,  
to bring your sons from afar,  
their silver and gold with them,  
for the name of the LORD your God,  
and for the Holy One of Israel,  
because He has glorified you.

**10** Foreigners will build up your walls,  
and their kings will minister to you;  
for in My wrath I struck you,  
but in My favor I have had compassion on you.

**11** Your gates will be open continually;  
day and night they will not be shut,  
so that people may bring to you the wealth of the nations,  
with their kings led in procession.

**12** For the nation and the kingdom that will not serve you will perish;  
those nations will be utterly laid waste.

**13** The glory of Lebanon will come to you,  
the cypress, the plane and the pine together,  
to beautify the place of My sanctuary,  
and I will make the place of My feet glorious.

**14** The sons of those who afflicted you  
will come bending low to you,  
and all who despised you  
will bow down at the soles of your feet;  
they will call you the City of the LORD,  
the Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

**15** Instead of being forsaken and hated,  
with no one passing through,  
I will make you majestic forever,  
a joy from generation to generation.

**16** You will drink the milk of nations;  
you will nurse at the breast of kings;  
and you will know that I, the LORD, am your Savior,  
and your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.

**17** Instead of bronze I will bring gold,  
and instead of iron I will bring silver,  
instead of wood, bronze,  
instead of stones, iron.  
I will make peace your overseers  
and righteousness your slave drivers.

**18** Violence will no longer be heard in your land,  
devastation or destruction within your borders;  
you will call your walls Deliverance,  
and your gates Praise.

**19** The sun will no longer be your light by day,  
nor will the brightness of the moon give you light;  
but the LORD will be your everlasting light,  
and your God your beauty.

**20** Your sun will no longer go down,  
nor your moon withdraw;  
for the LORD will be your everlasting light,  
and the days of your mourning will be ended.

**21** Your people will all be righteous;  
they will possess the land forever,  
the branch of My planting,  
the work of My hands, that I may be glorified.

**22** The least one will become a thousand,  
and the smallest a mighty nation.  
I am the LORD; in its time I will hasten it.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "Arise, shine… the brightness of your rising"** — *zarach*, the verb of the sun rising, three times (vv1, 2, 3): the LORD rises on Zion like the sun, and the nations walk to her light. *The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light* (9:2); here the darkness covers the peoples, and Zion is where the light is.
- **v6 "gold and frankincense"** — camels from Midian, Ephah and Sheba bring gifts and praise. The magi from the east bring *gold, frankincense and myrrh* to the child (Matthew 2:11).
- **v7 "Kedar… Nebaioth"** — the sons of Ishmael, *Nebaioth; and Kedar* (Genesis 25:13): the tribes of the desert bring their flocks to the altar.
- **vv7, 9, 13, 21 "I will beautify… He has glorified you… to beautify… that I may be glorified"** — *pa'ar*, the verb of 49:3 and 55:5, four times in the chapter: the LORD makes His house and His city beautiful, and is glorified in His people.
- **v10 "in My wrath I struck you, but in My favor I have had compassion"** — as 54:7–8, *For a brief moment I forsook you, but with great compassion I will gather you*.
- **v17 "righteousness your slave drivers"** — *nogesayikh*, the word for Egypt's *slave drivers* (Exodus 3:7) and for those Israel will one day *rule over* (14:2). Those who drove Zion will be replaced by peace and righteousness.
- **vv19–20 "the LORD will be your everlasting light"** — sun and moon give way to the LORD Himself. The city John sees *has no need of the sun or of the moon to shine on it, for the glory of God gave it light* (Revelation 21:23).
- **v21 "the branch of My planting"** — *netser*, the *branch* that comes from the roots of Jesse (11:1), here said of the whole people. "My planting" is the read form *matta'ai*; the written form is *matta'o*, "His planting."
- **v22 "The least one will become a thousand"** — as Abraham was *one when I called him* and was made many (51:2). "In its time I will hasten it" — *be-ittah achishennah*: the LORD sets the time, and when it comes He does not delay.

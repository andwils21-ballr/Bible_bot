# Isaiah 35

**1** The wilderness and the dry land will be glad;  
the desert will rejoice and blossom;  
like the flower

**2** it will blossom abundantly,  
and rejoice with joy and singing.  
The glory of Lebanon will be given to it,  
the splendor of Carmel and Sharon.  
They will see the glory of the LORD,  
the splendor of our God.

**3** Strengthen the weak hands,  
and make firm the feeble knees.

**4** Say to those whose hearts are anxious,  
"Be strong; do not fear!  
Here is your God! He will come with vengeance,  
with the recompense of God.  
He will come and save you."

**5** Then the eyes of the blind will be opened,  
and the ears of the deaf unstopped;

**6** then the lame will leap like a deer,  
and the tongue of the mute sing for joy.  
For waters will break out in the wilderness,  
and streams in the desert;

**7** the burning sand will become a pool,  
and the thirsty ground springs of water;  
in the haunt of jackals, where they lay,  
grass will become reeds and rushes.

**8** A highway will be there, a road,  
and it will be called the Way of Holiness;  
the unclean will not pass over it.  
It will be for those who walk the way;  
fools will not wander on it.

**9** No lion will be there,  
nor will any ravenous beast go up on it;  
they will not be found there;  
but the redeemed will walk there.

**10** The ransomed of the LORD will return  
and come to Zion with singing;  
everlasting joy will be on their heads;  
they will obtain gladness and joy,  
and sorrow and sighing will flee away.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "like the flower… the splendor of Carmel and Sharon"** — *chavatselet*, the flower of *I am a flower of Sharon* (Song of Songs 2:1); here too Sharon is named. In 33:9 *Lebanon is ashamed and withers; Sharon is like a desert; Bashan and Carmel shake off their leaves*; here the same three places are given back.
- **v3 "Strengthen the weak hands, and make firm the feeble knees"** — the letter to the Hebrews quotes it to people worn out by discipline: *lift your drooping hands and strengthen your weak knees* (Hebrews 12:12).
- **v4 "Here is your God!… He will come and save you"** — *hinneh Eloheikhem*, the words the herald is told to cry to the towns of Judah in 40:9. *Save* is *yasha*, the root of *yeshu'ah*, "deliverance" (12:2–3).
- **vv5–6 "the eyes of the blind will be opened… the lame will leap"** — the signs Jesus names to John's disciples: *the blind see again and the lame walk… and the deaf hear* (Matthew 11:5). The eyes and ears shut in 6:10 are opened.
- **v8 "the Way of Holiness"** — *derekh ha-qodesh*, the highway of 11:16 and 19:23, now named. Only the clean walk it, and it is so plain that *fools will not wander on it*.
- **vv9–10 "the redeemed… the ransomed of the LORD"** — *ge'ulim*, from *ga'al*, the next of kin who buys back, and *peduyim*, from *padah*, "ransom." Verse 10 stands again, almost word for word, at 51:11.

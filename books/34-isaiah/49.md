# Isaiah 49

**1** Listen to me, you coastlands;  
pay attention, you peoples from afar.  
The LORD called me from the womb;  
from my mother's body He named my name.

**2** He made my mouth like a sharp sword;  
in the shadow of His hand He hid me;  
He made me a polished arrow;  
in His quiver He hid me away.

**3** He said to me, "You are My servant,  
Israel, in whom I will be glorified."

**4** But I said, "I have labored in vain;  
I have spent my strength for emptiness and vapor;  
yet surely my right is with the LORD,  
and my reward with my God."

**5** But now the LORD says,  
who formed me from the womb to be His servant,  
to bring Jacob back to Him,  
and that Israel might be gathered to Him —  
for I am honored in the eyes of the LORD,  
and my God has become my strength —

**6** He says:  
"It is too small a thing that you should be My servant  
to raise up the tribes of Jacob  
and to bring back the preserved of Israel;  
I will give you as a light for the nations,  
that My deliverance may reach to the end of the earth."

**7** This is what the LORD says,  
the Redeemer of Israel, his Holy One,  
to one deeply despised, abhorred by the nation,  
the servant of rulers:  
"Kings will see and rise up;  
princes, and they will bow down,  
because of the LORD, who is faithful,  
the Holy One of Israel, who has chosen you."

**8** This is what the LORD says:  
"In a time of favor I have answered you,  
and in a day of deliverance I have helped you;  
I will keep you and give you  
as a covenant for the people,  
to restore the land,  
to give out the desolate inheritances,

**9** saying to the prisoners, 'Go out,'  
to those who are in darkness, 'Show yourselves.'  
They will feed along the roads,  
and on all the bare heights will be their pasture.

**10** They will not hunger or thirst,  
and neither burning heat nor sun will strike them,  
for He who has compassion on them will lead them,  
and by springs of water He will guide them.

**11** I will make all My mountains a road,  
and My highways will be raised up.

**12** These will come from afar,  
and these from the north and from the west,  
and these from the land of Sinim."

**13** Sing for joy, you heavens, and rejoice, earth;  
break out into singing, you mountains!  
For the LORD has comforted His people  
and will have compassion on His afflicted.

**14** But Zion said, "The LORD has forsaken me;  
the Lord has forgotten me."

**15** "Can a woman forget her nursing child,  
or have no compassion on the son of her womb?  
Even these may forget,  
yet I will not forget you.

**16** I have engraved you on the palms of My hands;  
your walls are continually before Me.

**17** Your sons hurry back;  
those who tore you down and laid you waste  
go out from you.

**18** Lift up your eyes all around and see:  
they all gather together, they come to you.  
As I live," declares the LORD,  
"you will put them all on like ornaments;  
you will bind them on as a bride does.

**19** Surely your waste and desolate places  
and your ruined land —  
surely now you will be too narrow for those who live in you,  
and those who swallowed you up will be far away.

**20** The children born in your bereavement  
will yet say in your hearing,  
'The place is too narrow for me;  
make room for me to live.'

**21** Then you will say in your heart,  
'Who bore me these?  
I was bereaved and barren,  
exiled and turned away;  
who brought these up?  
I was left alone;  
where have these come from?'"

**22** This is what the Lord GOD says:  
"I am about to lift up My hand to the nations  
and raise My signal to the peoples;  
they will bring your sons in their arms,  
and your daughters will be carried on their shoulders.

**23** Kings will be your foster fathers,  
and their queens your nursing mothers;  
with their faces to the ground they will bow down to you  
and lick the dust of your feet.  
Then you will know that I am the LORD;  
those who wait for Me will not be put to shame."

**24** Can the prey be taken from the mighty,  
or the captives of the righteous be rescued?

**25** For this is what the LORD says:  
"Even the captives of the mighty will be taken,  
and the prey of the tyrant will be rescued;  
I will contend with those who contend with you,  
and I will save your children.

**26** I will make your oppressors eat their own flesh,  
and they will be drunk on their own blood as with sweet wine.  
Then all flesh will know  
that I am the LORD your Savior,  
and your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob."

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "The LORD called me from the womb… You are My servant, Israel"** — the servant of 42:1 now speaks for himself. He is given the name *Israel* (v3), and in v5 he is sent *to bring Jacob back*: the servant bears the people's name and is sent to the people. "In whom I will be glorified" — *etpa'ar*, the verb of 44:23, *the LORD… will be glorified in Israel*.
- **v4 "my right is with the LORD, and my reward with my God"** — *mishpati*, the word of Jacob's complaint in 40:27, *my right is passed over by my God*. The servant says the same of himself and answers it: his right is with God. *Pe'ullati*, "my reward," is the word of 40:10, *His reward is with Him*. "Emptiness and vapor" — *tohu va-hevel*: *tohu* as in 44:9, *hevel* the word that is Abel's name (Genesis 4:2).
- **v5 "and that Israel might be gathered to Him"** — the read form *lo*, "to Him," is followed. The written form is *lo* with another letter, "not": *and Israel is not gathered*. The Greek has *to gather Jacob to Him, and Israel*, with no *not*.
- **v6 "a light for the nations, that My deliverance may reach to the end of the earth"** — *or goyim*, as in 42:6. Paul and Barnabas take it as a command to themselves in Antioch of Pisidia: *I have set you as a light for the nations, that you may be for salvation to the end of the earth* (Acts 13:47).
- **v8 "In a time of favor I have answered you"** — Paul quotes the line in the Greek and adds, *Now is the favorable time; now is the day of salvation* (2 Corinthians 6:2). "A covenant for the people" — as in 42:6.
- **v12 "the land of Sinim"** — a land named only here, set against *the north* and *the west* (*yam*, the sea); which land is meant is not known. The Greek has *the land of the Persians*.
- **vv10, 13, 15 "He who has compassion… will have compassion… have no compassion on the son of her womb"** — *merachamam*, *yerachem*, *merachem*, from the letters of *rechem*, "womb." God's compassion is set against a mother's for the child of her body, and it outlasts hers.
- **v17 "Your sons hurry back"** — *banayikh*, "your sons," as the vowels read it; the sons return in vv20–22. The same letters read *bonayikh*, "your builders," which stands against *those who tore you down* in the next line, and the Greek has *you will quickly be built by those who tore you down*.
- **vv20–21 "the children born in your bereavement… I was bereaved and barren"** — *shikkulayikh*, *shekhulah*, the word Babylon said she would never know (*I will not… know the loss of children*, 47:8). Babylon loses her children in one day; Zion, who thought herself bereaved, finds herself with more than the place can hold.
- **v24 "the captives of the righteous"** — *shevi tsaddiq*. The next verse answers with *the captives of the mighty… the prey of the tyrant*, so the captor of v24 is a strong man; the Hebrew calls him *tsaddiq*, which can mean one who is in the right, a captor who holds his captives lawfully. The Greek has *if one takes captive unjustly, will it be saved?*

# Isaiah 22

**1** The oracle concerning the Valley of Vision.

What is the matter with you,  
that you have all gone up to the housetops,

**2** you who are full of shouting,  
a tumultuous city, an exultant town?  
Your slain were not slain by the sword,  
nor did they die in battle.

**3** All your rulers fled together;  
without a bow they were captured.  
All of you who were found were captured together,  
though they had fled far away.

**4** So I said: "Look away from me;  
let me weep bitterly;  
do not try to comfort me  
over the destruction of the daughter of my people."

**5** For the Lord GOD of hosts has a day  
of tumult and trampling and confusion  
in the Valley of Vision,  
a battering down of walls  
and a crying out to the mountain.

**6** Elam took up the quiver,  
with chariots of men and horsemen,  
and Kir bared the shield.

**7** Your choicest valleys were full of chariots,  
and the horsemen took their stand at the gate.

**8** He stripped away the covering of Judah.

In that day you looked to the weapons of the House of the Forest,

**9** and you saw that the breaches in the City of David were many. You collected the waters of the lower pool,

**10** and you counted the houses of Jerusalem, and tore down the houses to fortify the wall.

**11** You made a reservoir between the two walls for the water of the old pool. But you did not look to Him who did it, or see Him who planned it long ago.

**12** In that day the Lord GOD of hosts called  
for weeping and mourning,  
for baldness and putting on sackcloth;

**13** but instead there was joy and gladness,  
killing oxen and slaughtering sheep,  
eating meat and drinking wine:  
"Let us eat and drink,  
for tomorrow we die."

**14** The LORD of hosts has revealed Himself in my hearing: "Surely this iniquity will not be atoned for you until you die," says the Lord GOD of hosts.

## Shebna and Eliakim

**15** Thus says the Lord GOD of hosts: "Go, come to this steward, to Shebna, who is over the household, and say:

**16** 'What have you here, and whom have you here, that you have cut out a tomb for yourself here — cutting out his tomb on the height, carving a dwelling for himself in the rock?

**17** The LORD is about to hurl you away, hurl you, you strong man, and grip you hard;

**18** He will wind you round and round and throw you like a ball into a wide land. There you will die, and there will be your splendid chariots, you disgrace to your master's house.

**19** I will thrust you from your office, and from your post he will pull you down.

**20** And it shall come to pass in that day, that I will call My servant Eliakim son of Hilkiah,

**21** and I will clothe him with your robe, and bind your sash on him, and put your authority in his hand; and he will be a father to the inhabitants of Jerusalem and to the house of Judah.

**22** I will set the key of the house of David on his shoulder; he will open, and no one will shut; he will shut, and no one will open.

**23** I will drive him like a peg into a firm place, and he will become a throne of honor for his father's house.

**24** They will hang on him the whole weight of his father's house, the offspring and the offshoots, every small vessel, from the bowls to all the jars.

**25** In that day, declares the LORD of hosts, the peg that was driven into a firm place will give way; it will be cut down and fall, and the load that was on it will be cut off, for the LORD has spoken.'"

## Notes

- **v1 "the Valley of Vision"** — the oracles on the nations come home: this one is against Jerusalem itself (vv9–10), the place of Isaiah's visions. The city is on its rooftops, celebrating, while the prophet weeps (v4).
- **vv5–6 "a battering down of walls… and Kir bared the shield"** — *meqarqar qir*, "a tearing down of walls," and then *Qir*, the land of Kir: the same sound, the wall and the people who come to break it.
- **vv8–11 "you looked to the weapons… you did not look to Him who did it"** — *va-tabbet* and *lo hibbattem*, the same verb, *nabat*, "look to." They looked to the armory of the House of the Forest (Solomon's *House of the Forest of Lebanon*, where he put his gold shields, 1 Kings 7:2; 10:17), counted houses, collected water, built up the wall, the work Hezekiah did against Assyria (*to stop the water of the springs… built up all the wall that was broken down*, 2 Chronicles 32:3–5). What they did not look to was the One who made it all, as in 17:7, *man will look to his Maker*.
- **v13 "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die"** — Paul quotes it in the Greek, of those who deny the resurrection: *if the dead are not raised, Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die* (1 Corinthians 15:32).
- **v14 "Surely this iniquity will not be atoned for you"** — *yekhuppar*, the word spoken over Isaiah's own lips, *your sin is atoned for* (6:7). The coal was given to him; to the feasting city the same word is refused.
- **vv15, 20 "Shebna, who is over the household… My servant Eliakim son of Hilkiah"** — *al ha-bayit*, the title of the king's chief steward (1 Kings 4:6). When Assyria comes to Jerusalem's walls, it is *Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was over the household*, who goes out, and *Shebna the secretary* with him (36:3): the office has passed as this chapter says.
- **v22 "the key of the house of David on his shoulder; he will open, and no one will shut"** — *al shikhmo*, the words of 9:6, *the government shall be on his shoulder*. Revelation gives the line to Christ: *the Holy One, the True One, who has the key of David, who opens and no one will shut* (Revelation 3:7).
- **vv23–25 "a throne of honor… the whole weight of his father's house… the load"** — *kavod*, "honor," is also "weight": the peg is made a seat of honor, and the family hangs its whole weight on it until it gives way. *Ha-massa*, "the load," is the word the chapter opened with, *the oracle*; the burden laid on the peg falls with it.

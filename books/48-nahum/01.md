# Nahum 1

## The LORD Takes Vengeance

**1** The oracle concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

**2** The LORD is a jealous and avenging God;  
the LORD is avenging and full of wrath;  
the LORD takes vengeance on His adversaries  
and keeps His wrath for His enemies.

**3** The LORD is slow to anger and great in power,  
and not leaving it unpunished.  
His way is in the whirlwind and in the storm,  
and the clouds are the dust of His feet.

**4** He rebukes the sea and dries it up,  
and makes all the rivers run dry;  
Bashan and Carmel waste away,  
and the flower of Lebanon wastes away.

**5** The mountains quake before Him,  
and the hills melt;  
the earth heaves at His presence,  
the world and those who live in it.

**6** Who can stand before His indignation?  
Who can rise up against His burning anger?  
His wrath is poured out like fire,  
and the rocks are broken apart by Him.

**7** The LORD is good,  
a stronghold on the day of distress;  
He knows those who take refuge in Him.

**8** But with an overflowing flood passing through  
He will make a full end of her place,  
and He will pursue His enemies into darkness.

## Distress Will Not Rise Twice

**9** What do you devise against the LORD?  
He will make a full end;  
distress will not rise up twice.

**10** For though they are like tangled thorns,  
and drunk as with their drink,  
they will be consumed like stubble, fully dried.

**11** From you has come out one who devises evil against the LORD,  
a worthless counselor.

**12** This is what the LORD says:  
Though they are at full strength and many,  
even so they will be sheared off and pass away.  
Though I have afflicted you,  
I will afflict you no more.

**13** So now I will break his yoke bar from off you  
and tear off your bonds.

**14** The LORD has commanded concerning you:  
No more descendants will bear your name.  
From the house of your gods I will cut off  
the carved image and the cast image.  
I will make your grave,  
for you are of no account.

**15** There on the mountains, the feet of one who brings good news,  
who proclaims peace!  
Keep your feasts, Judah;  
pay your vows;  
for the worthless one will no longer pass through you;  
he is cut off completely.

## Notes

- **v1 "The oracle concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum"** — *massa*, **oracle**, as in *The oracle concerning Tyre* (Isaiah 23:1). *Sefer chazon*, **the book of the vision**, is found only here: no other prophet's heading calls itself a book. *Nachum*, **Nahum**, is the root of *nacham*, **comfort**, as in *Comfort, comfort My people* (Isaiah 40:1). *Elkoshite* names a town not mentioned elsewhere. In the Hebrew text v15 is 2:1; the English numbering is followed, and the Greek follows it too. The city is the Nineveh that turned and was spared in Jonah (Jonah 3:10; 4:11).
- **v2 "a jealous and avenging God… takes vengeance… keeps His wrath for His enemies"** — *El qanno*, **a jealous God**, is Joshua's word, *He is a jealous God* (Joshua 24:19), and *noqem*, **avenging**, is said three times. *Noqem… ve-noter* are the two verbs the law forbids to an Israelite, *You shall not take revenge and you shall not hold a grudge against the sons of your people* (Leviticus 19:18); here they belong to the LORD, against His enemies. The psalm says of His people, *nor will He keep His anger forever* (Psalm 103:9), with the same verb. *Ba'al chemah*, word for word **master of wrath**, is **full of wrath**.
- **vv2–8 the order of the lines** — the poem begins its lines, with gaps, with the letters of the alphabet in order: *El* (aleph, v2), *be-sufah* (bet, v3), *go'er* (gimel, v4), *harim* (he, v5), *va-tissa* (vav, v5), *chamato* (chet, v6), *tov* (tet, v7), and *kalah* (kaf) inside v8. Some letters are missing or out of place, and the pattern is not carried past v8.
- **v3 "The LORD is slow to anger and great in power, and not leaving it unpunished"** — the LORD's name from Sinai, as Moses gave it back in his prayer, *The LORD is slow to anger and abundant in loyal love… and not leaving it unpunished* (Numbers 14:18), with *great in power* where Moses had *abundant in loyal love*. *Ve-naqqeh lo yenaqqeh* is the doubled verb of *and not leaving it unpunished* (Exodus 34:7). Jonah gave the mercy of the name as his reason for fleeing (Jonah 4:2); Nahum gives the other half. *Sufah*, **whirlwind**, as in *they will reap the whirlwind* (Hosea 8:7), and *se'arah*, **storm**, the storm out of which the LORD answered Job (Job 38:1).
- **vv4–6 "He rebukes the sea and dries it up… Who can stand before His indignation?"** — the sea of the exodus, *He rebuked the Sea of Reeds, and it dried up* (Psalm 106:9), and Isaiah's *By My rebuke I dry up the sea* (Isaiah 50:2). Bashan, Carmel and Lebanon *umlal*, **waste away**, as in Hosea 4:3. *The hills melt* is Amos 9:13, and *the world and those who live in it* is *The earth is the LORD's… the world and those who live in it* (Psalm 24:1). *Mi ya'amod*, **who can stand**, as in *who can stand before You once Your anger is roused?* (Psalm 76:7); *nittekhah*, **is poured out**, as in *My anger and My wrath will be poured out* (Jeremiah 7:20).
- **vv7–8 "a stronghold on the day of distress… with an overflowing flood passing through"** — *ma'oz*, **stronghold**, as in Joel 3:16, and *chosei vo*, **those who take refuge in Him**, as in *Blessed are all who take refuge in him* (Psalm 2:12). The flood is Isaiah's picture of Assyria itself, *it will sweep on into Judah, overflowing and passing through* (Isaiah 8:8), turned against Nineveh. *Kalah*, **a full end**, is what the LORD would not make of Judah, *yet I will not make a full end* (Jeremiah 4:27). *Meqomah*, **her place**, is Nineveh's. *Ve-oyevav yeraddef choshekh* is **He will pursue His enemies into darkness**, or **darkness will pursue His enemies**, as the Greek has it.
- **vv9–11 "What do you devise against the LORD?… tangled thorns… a worthless counselor"** — *chashav*, **devise**, the verb of Micah 2:1, now said of Nineveh; *yo'ets beliyya'al*, **a worthless counselor**, as the law's *worthless men* (Deuteronomy 13:13). *Lo taqum pa'amayim tsarah*, **distress will not rise up twice**: the LORD will not need a second blow. v10 is obscure: *ad sirim sevukhim u-khe-sov'am sevu'im*, a run of like sounds, **tangled thorns** and **drunk… drunk**; its sense is uncertain, and the Greek has *for to its foundation it will be laid bare, and like twisted bindweed it will be devoured*.
- **vv12–14 "they will be sheared off… I will break his yoke bar… you are of no account"** — *nagozzu*, **sheared off**, is the shearing of sheep, *Laban had gone to shear his flock* (Genesis 31:19). The *you* changes: *I have afflicted you* is Judah, as is *your bonds*, from the promise *I will break his yoke from off your neck and tear off your bonds* (Jeremiah 30:8); the yoke bar is the LORD's own word in the law, *I broke the bars of your yoke* (Leviticus 26:13). In v14 *you* is masculine, Nineveh's king: *the carved image and the cast image* of the law's curse (Deuteronomy 27:15). *Qalota*, **you are of no account**, word for word **you are light**, as in *those who despise Me shall be lightly esteemed* (1 Samuel 2:30).
- **v15 "the feet of one who brings good news, who proclaims peace!… the worthless one will no longer pass through you"** — Isaiah has the same words, *How beautiful on the mountains are the feet of him who brings good news, who proclaims peace* (Isaiah 52:7). The good news here is that Nineveh has fallen. *Chaggi… chaggayikh*, word for word **feast your feasts**, is **keep your feasts**, and *pay your vows* is Jonah's *what I have vowed I will pay* (Jonah 2:9). *Beliyya'al*, **the worthless one**, is the counselor of v11, and *will no longer pass through you* is Joel's word for strangers (Joel 3:17).

# Nahum 2

## The Attack on Nineveh

**1** The scatterer has come up against you.  
Guard the rampart!  
Watch the road!  
Brace your loins!  
Gather all your strength!

**2** For the LORD is restoring the pride of Jacob,  
like the pride of Israel,  
for plunderers have emptied them  
and ruined their vine branches.

**3** The shield of his warriors is red;  
the men of valor are clothed in scarlet;  
the chariots flash with steel  
on the day he makes ready,  
and the cypress spears are brandished.

**4** The chariots drive madly in the streets;  
they rush to and fro in the squares;  
their appearance is like torches;  
they dart like lightning.

**5** He calls his nobles to mind;  
they stumble as they go;  
they hurry to her wall,  
and the shelter is set up.

**6** The gates of the rivers are opened,  
and the palace melts away.

**7** It is fixed: she is stripped, she is carried off,  
and her slave women moan like the sound of doves,  
beating on their breasts.

**8** Nineveh is like a pool of water,  
all her days;  
and they flee.  
"Stand! Stand!"  
But no one turns back.

**9** Plunder the silver!  
Plunder the gold!  
There is no end to the treasure,  
the weight of every precious thing.

**10** Empty, emptied, and laid waste!  
Hearts melt and knees give way;  
anguish is in all loins,  
and all faces grow pale.

## The Lions' Den

**11** Where is the lions' den,  
the feeding place of the young lions,  
where the lion and the lioness walked,  
and the lion's cub,  
with no one to make them afraid?

**12** The lion tore enough for his cubs  
and strangled prey for his lionesses;  
he filled his caves with prey  
and his dens with torn flesh.

**13** I am against you, declares the LORD of hosts.  
I will burn her chariots in smoke,  
and the sword will devour your young lions;  
I will cut off your prey from the earth,  
and the voice of your messengers  
will no longer be heard.

## Notes

- **v1 "The scatterer has come up against you. Guard the rampart!"** — in the Hebrew text vv1–13 are 2:2–14; the English numbering is followed. *Mefits*, **the scatterer**, is the one who breaks a people apart; a word with nearly the same letters, *mappets*, is the **war club** of Jeremiah 51:20, *You are My war club, My weapon of war*. *Natsor metsurah*, **guard the rampart**, and the commands that follow are said to Nineveh as the attack begins.
- **vv3–4 "the shield of his warriors is red… the cypress spears are brandished… The chariots drive madly in the streets"** — the attackers come on in red and scarlet; *metulla'im*, **clothed in scarlet**, from *tola*, the scarlet of *red like crimson* (Isaiah 1:18). *Be-esh peladot*, **flash with steel**, is uncertain: *peladot* is found only here. *Ve-ha-beroshim*, **the cypresses**, are the spear shafts; the Greek has *the horsemen*, *parashim*, nearly the same letters. *Yit-holelu ha-rekhev*, **the chariots drive madly**, are Jeremiah's words, *Drive madly, you chariots!* (Jeremiah 46:9), and *yishtaqshequn*, **rush to and fro**, is from *shaqaq*, the verb of the locusts that *leap on the city* (Joel 2:9).
- **v7 "It is fixed: she is stripped, she is carried off… beating on their breasts"** — obscure. *Ve-hutstsav*, **it is fixed**, can also be read as a name; *gulletah*, **she is stripped**, is from *galah*, to uncover and to go into exile; *ho'alatah*, **she is carried off**, word for word **she is brought up**. The Greek has *the foundation is uncovered, and she was going up*. *Metofefot*, **beating**, is the verb of the tambourine, *young women playing tambourines* (Psalm 68:25): the slave women beat their breasts like drums. The doves are Ezekiel's, *like doves of the valleys, all of them moaning* (Ezekiel 7:16).
- **vv8–9 "Nineveh is like a pool of water… Stand! Stand! But no one turns back… There is no end to the treasure"** — *mimei hi*, **all her days**, is obscure; the Greek has *her waters*. *Imdu amodu*, **Stand! Stand!**, the officers' cry to men already running. *Ve-ein qetseh la-tekhunah*, **there is no end to the treasure**, as Isaiah said of Judah's riches, *there is no end to their treasures* (Isaiah 2:7). *Kavod*, **weight**, is also the word for **glory**.

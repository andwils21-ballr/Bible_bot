# 3 Meqabyan 10

## Life from the Rain

**1** Hear, if you do not believe in the resurrection of the dead: how many living things rise in the rainy season without being born! For the Spirit of God rests on them, as He breathed at the first.

**2** First He commands them by His word to die.

**3** They rise as He wills, though their flesh has decayed, and are made better a second time.

**4** Again, when the rain comes down and waters the earth, they live and go about as they were ordered at the first.

**5** For it says, "Let there be every living soul that moves on the earth, and every living soul that the water brings forth"; for the Spirit of God hovers over the water, and by His Spirit and His word the spirit of life is given them.

**6** See, you who are blind of heart, who say the dead do not rise! By His Spirit and His word they come to be without mother or father. If you had understanding and wisdom, how could you say the dead do not rise by the word of God their Creator?

**7** But you, repent and return to your faith, for by the word of God the dead will live who have become dust and ashes in the earth.

**8** They will rise by the dew of His mercy, which comes from God, as His word spoke at the first; that word surrounds all things, and raises the dead as He wills.

## The Day of Repayment

**9** Know that you will rise and stand before Him. Do not imagine, as you think, that you will stay in the grave.

**10** It is not so: you will rise and be repaid for what you did on earth, whether good or evil, for it is the day of repayment.

**11** On the day of resurrection you will be repaid for all the sins you did, and the record of your debt, written from your youth until then, will hold sway. You will have no excuse there, as you have in the dealings of this world, where you make excuses in your sin so as to deny your sin,

**12** setting up your lying word before you as if it were true, and making the lie you spoke look like the truth.

**13** There is no excuse there, for the Spirit of God speaks against you; for it knows all the evil deeds you have done and lays them bare for you before God its Creator.

**14** There you will be ashamed for the sin you did. Before that day reaches you, hurry to repent here, so that you are not put to shame before angels and men on the day of judgment, but are praised with those who are praised for the beauty of their deeds.

**15** They will receive their wage without shame from Him who created them, with the angels who praise God, and they will rejoice. But if you did no good in your life, while you were in your body, you have no portion with the righteous.

**16** Your repentance will come where it does not help you. For while you had the skill of buying and selling, and while you had wealth, you did not feed the hungry;

**17** while you had clothing, you did not clothe the naked; while you had power, you did not rescue the oppressed;

**18** while you had understanding, you did not teach the sinner, so that he would turn back and repent and God would forgive him the sin he had committed before in ignorance; while you had strength, you did not fight against those who war against you, so that you could win.

**19** While you had vigor, you did not fast or pray, to wear down the strength of your bodily youth and to bend yourself to righteousness, and not to the favor of the flesh in the world,

**20** not to the pleasures of the world, to sweet foods and fine drinks, not to fine garments and adornment of gold and silver,

**21** with precious stones of India, topaz and emerald. That is not what befits the adornment of a man.

## The True Adornment

**22** The adornment of a man is purity, wisdom and understanding, loving in purity without rivalry, caring without doubt or hatred, and loving your neighbor as yourself,

**23** and not repaying evil to the one who did you evil, so that you may enter the dwelling given to the patient, and He may reward you with those who inherited the holy land through patience, understanding and wisdom, and through hope of the kingdom of heaven at the resurrection.

**24** Do not say, "We will not rise from the dead." Those who say and think this, the devil makes them lose hope, so that they are not saved at the resurrection. They will know it when it reaches them, and will grieve and mourn for what they did in ignorance, so that evil is counted against them at the resurrection, because they did not believe that they would rise on that day.

**25** Because of this they will be reproved for the evil deeds they did on earth, and they will see, all together, the resurrection of the body which they denied.

**26** On that day they will weep because they did not make their way good. Had they wept on earth, it would have been better for them, if they could, so as not to be weeping there.

**27** If we do not weep by our own will here, they will make us weep there against our will; when we do not prepare repentance here, we prepare weeping and groaning there, where it does not help us.

**28** Prepare to do good, so that you may pass from death into life, from this passing earth to the heavenly one above, from this one on the earth to the light in the heavens.

**29** Refuse the delights of the earth that are beyond measure, so that there you may be filled with joy that does not end in the kingdom of heaven, with those who believe in the resurrection of the dead, from now and forever and ever. Amen.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 life from the rain** — creatures that seem to come alive with the rains, after dying away in the dry season, as a sign of the resurrection; *What you sow does not come to life unless it dies* (1 Corinthians 15:36). In Ethiopia *kərämt*, the rainy season, runs from June to September.
- **v5** — *Let the waters swarm with swarms of living creatures… Let the earth bring forth living creatures* (Genesis 1:20, 24); *the Spirit of God was hovering over the face of the waters* (Genesis 1:2).
- **v8 "by the dew of His mercy"** — *Your dead shall live; their bodies shall rise… for your dew is a dew of light, and the earth will give birth to the dead* (Isaiah 26:19).
- **v11 "the record of your debt"** — *the record of debt that stood against us* (Colossians 2:14); *books were opened* (Daniel 7:10).
- **v13 "it knows all the evil deeds you have done… before God its Creator"** — the Ge'ez has "she," which may point back to the soul rather than the Spirit; the subject is left open as "it."
- **v16** — after "For" the Ge'ez has *lita dalawka*, not understood; it is left out.
- **vv16–18** — *I was hungry and you gave Me no food… naked and you did not clothe Me* (Matthew 25:42–43).
- **vv20–22 "not to fine garments and adornment of gold… The adornment of a man is purity"** — *not with braided hair and gold or pearls or costly attire, but with what is proper… with good works* (1 Timothy 2:9–10); *the imperishable beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit* (1 Peter 3:4).
- **v23 "those who inherited the holy land through patience"** — *the meek shall inherit the land* (Psalm 37:11; Matthew 5:5).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

# 3 Meqabyan 4

## The Throne Taken Away

**1** "Adam's Lord is there to redeem him; He will put you to shame and rescue the lamb from the hand of the wolf.

**2** But those you have made your own will rise and be drawn with you into perdition.

**3** Those who kept the law of the Lord their God will rejoice with their Creator, those whom God hid from your evil deeds, to make them His portion, so that they may praise Him with His holy angels, who did not transgress the law of their God as you did.

**4** But because of your pride the Most High God took from you the throne He had given you above all who were like you, so that you might praise Him with His angels.

**5** You refused, and you were named, and they were named, demons. Those who love God will be a people to Him like the holy angels, the cherubim and seraphim who praise Him, spreading their wings without ceasing.

**6** Those who love God will be a people to Him like the holy angels, the cherubim and seraphim who praise Him, spreading their wings without ceasing.

**7** But by your trickery and your pride you lost your praise, which He commanded you to give Him at all times with your tribe and all your company, who were created with you in your fellowship.

**8** When you forgot the praise of Him who created you, you thought He could not make another like you, so that the praise of God who created you would not cease: a tenth tribe, when you were cut off from the company of your brothers, so that the praise of God your Creator would not cease.

**9** In the pride of your heart you grew slack in the praise of Him who created you; He was angry with you, laughed at you, and bound you in the terror of Gehenna with your demons.

## Adam Made in Your Place

**10** He took earth from the ground in His holy hands, mixed in water, fire and wind, and created Adam in His own image and likeness.

**11** He set him over all the work of His hands, to fill up His praise where you had fallen short; the praise of the earthly one was joined to the praise of the heavenly ones and became equal to it.

**12** But through your pride and your stiff neck you were driven out, and cut yourself off from the praise of God who created you.

**13** Know that His praise did not grow less, for He made one to praise Him by the counsel of His will, so that the praise of God would not grow less.

**14** He knows everything before it is. He knew before He created you that you would transgress His commandment, and that you would lead Adam to transgress, His servant made in His image and likeness; for before the world there was a hidden counsel with Him.

**15** As Solomon said, 'Before the hills He brought me forth; before the world was formed He created me, before the foundations of the earth were laid,

**16** before He set the mountains and hills in their places, before the paths of the world were made firm, before the light of the sun and moon shone, before the circling of the stars and the seasons were,

**17** before night and day took their turns, before the waves of the sea were, before all that has come to be came to be,

**18** before what now appears appeared, before every name now named was named.' It was not you, nor those like you, who were in the thought of God, but Adam His servant.

**19** When you transgressed, He acted so that His holy name would be praised; when you grew proud, He created Adam His servant, so that He would be praised by a lowly one, His own work, whom He created from earth.

**20** For God hears the prayer of the poor from afar, and God loves the praise of the lowly.

**21** God loathes the talk of the proud, for He does not delight in the strength of the horse, nor take pleasure in the legs of a man; God takes pleasure in those who fear Him.

## He Repented; You Did Not

**22** They weep and wail for the sins they have done.

**23** But you could not bow down in repentance.

**24** He, though he was earth, turned back in repentance, weeping and lamenting for his sin before God his Creator.

**25** But you, in the pride of your heart and the stiffness of your neck, did not know the way of peace or of repentance, and could not bow down before God your Creator in repentance, weeping and tears.

**26** He, earth and dust and poor, turned with weeping and tears to repentance, to the way of peace and to humility.

**27** But you did not humble yourself, nor bend your heart to Him who created you.

**28** He humbled himself, bowed down for the sin he had committed, and was not proud.

**29** Nor was the error his own making; it came from you. For you created rebellion and planted its root, and in your pride you drew him with you into perdition.

**30** He Himself knows that it came about through the pride of your heart, for before He created the two of you He knew you and knew your ways.

**31** As for him, who had no pride of his own devising, He brought him back in repentance, weeping and tears.

**32** But in the pride of your heart you could not bow down in repentance. For one who sins and bows down has made his pride worse than his first offense; but one who sins, bows down and weeps before God his Creator,

**33** he is the one who has truly repented and found the way of his salvation, so as to soften the heart of his Lord. He has bowed down before his Master, and his Lord turns from His earlier resolve to be angry with His servant, for he has bowed before Him with much repentance and prostration; He forgives him his former sin,

**34** if he does not go back to his former offense. This is his complete repentance, if he does this. Adam did not forget to think of his God, and bowed down in repentance to Him who created him.

**35** You too, bow down to Him and repent before Him who created you, and do not bring guilt on him and on the work of His hands. For their Lord who created them knows their weakness, for they are flesh and blood.

**36** Once their soul goes out of their body, it becomes dust until the day He has chosen.

## Notes

- **v1 "rescue the lamb from the hand of the wolf"** — *I will rescue My flock; they shall no longer be a prey* (Ezekiel 34:22).
- **v6** — repeats the second half of verse 5 word for word in the Ge'ez; it is rendered as written.
- **v8 "a tenth tribe"** — Ethiopian tradition counts the angels in ten orders; when the deceiver's order fell, Adam's race was made to fill the tenth place. The verse is hard to follow in the Ge'ez and is rendered close to its order.
- **v10 "mixed in water, fire and wind"** — Adam made of the four elements, earth, water, fire and wind, as in the Ethiopian teaching on creation; *the LORD God formed the man of dust from the ground* (Genesis 2:7).
- **vv15–18 "Before the hills He brought me forth"** — Wisdom's words in Proverbs 8:23–29. The verb rendered **formed** in v15 (*yəṭāʿṭāʿ*) is not otherwise known.
- **v18 "but Adam His servant"** — the Ge'ez has "and Adam His servant"; read as "but," which the verse needs.
- **vv20–21** — *He does not forget the cry of the afflicted* (Psalm 9:12); *His delight is not in the strength of the horse, nor His pleasure in the legs of a man, but the LORD takes pleasure in those who fear Him* (Psalm 147:10–11).
- **v29** — after "into perdition" the Ge'ez has *ʾiʾaka*, a word not understood; it is left out.
- **v32 "one who sins and bows down has made his pride worse"** — the sense needs "does not bow down," as the rest of the verse shows, but there is no "not" in the Ge'ez; it is rendered as written.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

# 3 Meqabyan 5

## Earth Returns to Earth

**1** Know Him who created you, and do not forget Him who strengthened and saved you, the Holy One of Israel. For though you were earth, He made you in His image and likeness and placed you in the garden, to delight in it and to work the land.

**2** When you transgressed His commandment, He drove you out from there into this dry land, a land of thorns and thistles, which He cursed because of you.

**3** For you are earth and it is earth; you are dust and it is dust; you are soil and it is soil. You are fed from it, you return to it and become dust, until the time He wills to raise you and question you about all you have done, transgression and sin.

**4** Know now what you will answer at that time. Remember here what you have done, evil and good; weigh it and consider whether the evil is more or the good is more.

**5** If you have done more good, it is well with you, for you will rejoice and be glad in the resurrection of the dead.

**6** If you have done more evil, woe to you, for you will be repaid by the work of your hands: as your thoughts were evil, as you wronged your brother, and as you did not fear God, you will be repaid according to your deeds.

## False Words and False Scales

**7** As you slander your neighbor and swear falsely by the name of God, you will be repaid according to your deeds.

**8** You make your word a lie and make it look true to your neighbor, though you know that you speak falsely.

**9** To those who stand with you, you hold that your word is true while you win their belief, and you multiply words where your word is not trustworthy and true. You will be repaid according to your deeds, as when you say, 'I will give you,' and do not give it to your neighbor, and slander your fellow.

**10** When you say with a pure heart, 'I will give,' demons lie in wait for you like dogs and make you forget it all; even if you took it and meant to give, they make you covet the goods of the world, to hoard what does not profit you and what you will not eat. For it says, 'They heap up, and do not know for whom they gather.'

**11** Again it says, 'The sons of men are liars; they falsify the scales; all of them go from vanity into vanity.'

**12** O people, do not go over into wrongdoing by false scales and false measures, by seizing another's goods and adding them to your own unjustly, by trespassing on your neighbor's property, and by all that you gain for yourselves and not for your neighbor.

**13** If you do this, you will be repaid according to your deeds.

**14** O people, do not trust in robbery, but live by what is right, by the work of your hands. Do not covet another's toil, to eat by robbery and plunder, without justice and right,

**15** which will not satisfy you when you eat it, nor profit you when you hoard it; you will leave it to another when you die.

**16** If your wealth grows, do not let your heart be proud. A little with righteousness is better than the great wealth of sinners, for the wealth of sinners is like smoke that goes out of the mouth of a furnace, and the wind takes it away.

## Notes

- **v2 "a land of thorns and thistles, which He cursed because of you"** — *cursed is the ground because of you… thorns and thistles it shall bring forth for you* (Genesis 3:17–18).
- **v3 "you return to it and become dust"** — *you are dust, and to dust you shall return* (Genesis 3:19).
- **vv4–6 weighing the evil and the good** — *the dead were judged by what was written in the books, according to what they had done* (Revelation 20:12).
- **v10 "They heap up, and do not know for whom they gather"** — Psalm 39:6.
- **v11 "they falsify the scales"** — *Those of low estate are but a breath… in the balances they go up* (Psalm 62:9); the Greek and Ge'ez Psalm reads "the sons of men are liars in the scales."
- **v12 "false scales and false measures"** — *You shall not have in your bag two kinds of weights… a full and fair weight you shall have* (Deuteronomy 25:13–15).
- **v16 "A little with righteousness is better than the great wealth of sinners"** — Psalm 37:16; *if riches increase, set not your heart on them* (Psalm 62:10). *The wicked will perish… like smoke they vanish away* (Psalm 37:20).

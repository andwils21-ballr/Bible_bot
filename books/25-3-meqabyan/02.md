# 3 Meqabyan 2

## God's Word and the Deceiver's Reply

**1** "But those you could not overcome, like My servant Job, I will make inherit your throne in honor; to those you could not lead astray I will give the kingdom of heaven as a reward," said God, who rules all.

**2** "But I will press the children of Adam hard in everything, and will not let them stand in good deeds, if I am able to lead them astray. For I press the children of Adam in everything, and make the desire of the world sweet to them,

**3** whether by the desire for food, the desire for drink and the love of garments, or by the love of talk, of giving and of taking;

**4** or by the love of looking, the love of hearing, the love of walking and wandering about; or by much talk in pride, and the love of sleep and dreams;

**5** or by much drinking and drunkenness; or by the sight of the beauty of the women of this world and the scent of perfumes, I lead them astray;

**6** or by slandering their neighbor in hatred; or by the sight of the beauty of the women of this world and the scent of perfumes, I lead them astray.

**7** By all this I oppose them, so that they cannot be saved, and I drive them from the way of God, so that they go with me into perdition, for whose sake I was driven from my throne."

## The Prophet's Rebuke

**8** The prophet said, "You perdition and destroyer, do you exalt yourself so over the creature of God, when you transgressed the commandment of God in your pride, in the hardness of your heart, in angering your Creator and not praising your God?

**9** When your Creator was angry with you, He cast you down because of your evil deeds. Why then do you oppress the poor one whom his God created from earth and dust, made as He willed and set there for His praise?

**10** You, a spirit, grew proud at the word of God, who created you from flame of fire and wind.

**11** When you boasted, God saw your evil deeds and created a singer in your place, to praise His name without weariness; for you, with your demons, transgressed the commandment of God.

**12** He made Adam too, with his children, to praise the name of God in place of your praise and that of your host, whom God rejected because of your pride. For you exalted yourself above all the angels, above the hosts of angels who were like you.

**13** Because of this God tore you away from all the archangels who were like you, and your hosts too, who were created with you; with one counsel you strayed from the praise of God, through the hardness of your hearts and the pride of your thoughts, which is of no use. You exalted yourselves over your Creator, and not over another.

## Adam and Eve

**14** Because of this, so that He might be praised by the lowly, He created Adam from the dust of the earth, and gave him a law and a commandment not to eat of the fruit of the tree.

**15** He set him over all that He had made and taught him, saying, 'Eat of every tree in the garden; but of one tree, the sting of death, do not eat of it, so that you do not bring death on your own head.'

**16** When you heard this word, you spat venom from your mouth on Eve, the bone of Adam's side.

**17** You stole the pure lamb by evil cunning, so that she was made like you, a transgressor of the commandment.

**18** You led astray Eve, who was created gentle as a dove and did not know your ways; you led her astray with your crooked word and your cunning speech. After you led astray that first creature, she went and led astray the creature of God, the first man, who was created from earth.

**19** In your pride you entangled him in what was not fitting, in turmoil, and made him transgress the word of his Creator; in your pride you scorched the poor man.

**20** By your wickedness you drew him far from the love of his God; by your pretext you drove him out of the garden of delight; by your snare you made him leave the food of the garden.

**21** By your lie you made him thirst, cut off from the drink of the garden. For you were hostile from the beginning to the gentle creature, to bring him down into your death, into Sheol, and to take him from the love of his God, who brought him from where he was not into true being.

**22** Though he was of earth, He made him spiritual and able to speak, one who praises and sings to his God with his soul, his body and his mind.

**23** He created for him *[words lost]*

## Notes

- **v1 "those you could not overcome, like My servant Job"** — *Have you considered My servant Job, that there is none like him on the earth… he still holds fast his integrity* (Job 1:8; 2:3). The second clause has *la-ʾənki*, a copying slip; read with the sense of the first, **those you could not lead astray**.
- **v2 "if I am able"** — the Ge'ez has *kəhəlka*, "if you are able," in the deceiver's own speech; read as *kəhəlku*, "if I am able."
- **v6** — the second half repeats the end of verse 5 word for word in the Ge'ez; it is rendered as written.
- **v10 "flame of fire and wind"** — *He makes His angels winds, His ministers a flaming fire* (Psalm 104:4). The words rendered **at the word of God** could also be read "in calling himself God."
- **v15 "the sting of death"** — the Ge'ez *šok mot* is "the thorn of death"; *O death, where is your sting?* (1 Corinthians 15:55). *You shall not eat of it, for in the day that you eat of it you shall surely die* (Genesis 2:17).
- **v17 "the pure lamb"** — Eve, as the next verse makes plain. The verb rendered **stole** is uncertain.
- **v18** — *The serpent was more crafty than any other beast… she took of its fruit and ate, and she also gave some to her husband* (Genesis 3:1, 6); *be wise as serpents and innocent as doves* (Matthew 10:16).
- **v20** — two of the three verbs have the form "she," as though Eve did these things through the deceiver; they are rendered with **you** like the middle one.
- **v23** — the text breaks off here; the rest of the verse is lost.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

# 3 Meqabyan 6

## Those Who Meet the Soul

**1** Remember the day of your death, and think of what will come to you when your soul goes out of your body, when you leave your wealth to another and go on a road you do not know.

**2** Those who receive you are harsh, hideous to look at and fearful in their bearing. They will not hear your voice, and you will not understand theirs.

**3** Even if you plead with them, they will not answer you, because you did not do the will of the Lord your God; because of this they will terrify you greatly.

**4** Those who did the will of God have no fear, for the demons fear them; but the souls of sinners the demons mock.

**5** The souls of the righteous rejoice at the shout of the angels, for the angels gladden them greatly because they despised this world; but the souls of sinners are received by harsh angels.

**6** The souls of the righteous and the good are received by angels of mercy, for these are sent from God to comfort the souls of the righteous; but for the souls of sinners, harsh angels are sent from the devil to mock them.

## Repent While There Is Time

**7** Woe to you sinners! Weep over yourselves before the day of your death reaches you, for after your death the days that have passed will not come back.

**8** Rather, before your days pass, repent while you are here, so that you may be free of sickness and pain when you come before God in joy.

**9** Let there be on you no evil shame, no *ḥənqʷāqʷe*, no love of luxury and food, so that it does not drag you into empty desire, which takes you far from God. For a soul filled beyond measure does not remember the name of God; the Spirit of life does not rest on it, but the spirit of the devil rests on it.

**10** As Moses said, 'Jacob ate and was filled; he grew fat, grew thick and grew broad, and forsook God his Creator.'

**11** His life grew far from God. For filling the belly beyond measure is like the neighing of a horse and like the wild beasts of the wilderness: eating and drinking beyond measure, and fornication.

**12** But whoever does not fill his belly stands firm on the foundation of God, like the wall of the horizon, like a strong building with a rampart. For it says, 'The wicked flee when no one pursues,

**13** but the righteous are bold as a lion.'

**14** Those who do not love God do not keep His word, and their heart is not upright.

**15** God gives them dismay and sorrow while they are on the earth, so that they have no rest, through fear and trembling, the plundering of their goods, troubles beyond number, and the chains on their hands from the hands of their masters,

**16** and through their own afflictions, which shake them, so that they may have no rest and may not live in peace.

## Notes

- **vv2–6 the angels who receive the soul** — *the poor man died and was carried by the angels to Abraham's side* (Luke 16:22). The **harsh** ones (*ṣawwāgān*) are the angels of the devil, as v6 says.
- **v3 "they will terrify you"** — the Ge'ez *yəfarrəhukəmu* is "they will fear you," a slip of one letter for "make you afraid," which the verse needs.
- **v7 "Weep over yourselves"** — *Weep not for Me, but weep for yourselves* (Luke 23:28).
- **v9 *ḥənqʷāqʷe*** — a word not otherwise known; from the words around it, some kind of excess.
- **v10 "Jacob ate and was filled"** — *Jeshurun grew fat, and kicked… he forsook God who made him* (Deuteronomy 32:15); the Greek reads "Jacob ate and was filled."
- **v11 "like the neighing of a horse"** — *They were well-fed, lusty stallions, each neighing for his neighbor's wife* (Jeremiah 5:8).
- **vv12–13 "The wicked flee when no one pursues, but the righteous are bold as a lion"** — Proverbs 28:1.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

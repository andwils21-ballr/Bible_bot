## Out of the Garden

**6** She came and led astray the first creature of God by eating of the tree, and brought death on herself and on her children, because she transgressed the commandment of her Creator.

**7** By the judgment of God's justice they went out of the garden; yet He did not keep them far from the garden in enmity, but comforted them in the land into which He drove them, with their children, the fruit of their womb and the fruit of their land.

**8** When you drove them out of the garden in envy, God gave them green shoots, *sabawwota*, from the trees of the garden, so that they might plant them and be comforted by the fruit of the garden and the fruit of the earth that God gave them,

**9** so that they would be comforted and renew their hearts greatly by seeing children, by seeing the garden, and by the fruit of the earth, which the earth made sprout from the dust. When they eat it, they are greatly comforted from the sorrow of your driving them out of the garden.

**10** They are comforted in their hearts by their children and by the fruit of the earth, for God knows how to comfort His creature.

**11** He renews their hearts with food and water, for they had been driven out into this land of thorns and thistles."

## Notes

- **vv1, 2, 5** — the text breaks off in each of these verses; the rest is lost. Verse 2 keeps one more word, *təwəhbu*, "given," whose sense cannot be recovered without what came before it.
- **v3 "like the waves of the sea"** — *the one who doubts is like a wave of the sea that is driven and tossed by the wind* (James 1:6).
- **v7 "they went out of the garden"** — *the LORD God sent him out from the garden of Eden to work the ground from which he was taken* (Genesis 3:23).
- **v8 *sabawwota*** — a word not otherwise known; it may be a form of "seven," but that is not certain.
- **v11 "a land of thorns and thistles"** — *thorns and thistles it shall bring forth for you… By the sweat of your face you shall eat bread* (Genesis 3:18–19).

# 3 Meqabyan 7

## No Fear for Those Who Trust

**1** Those who trust in God have no dismay or fear, as David said, "In God I have trusted; I will not fear what man can do to me."

**2** Again he said, "Though war rise against me, in Him I have trusted. One thing I have asked of the Lord, and that I will seek." Whoever trusts in Him will live forever and will not fear evil tidings.

**3** Who has trusted in God and been put to shame? Who has called on Him and been ignored?

**4** Who has hoped in Him and been put to shame? For He said, "Those who love Me I will love; those who honor Me I will honor; the one who turns to Me I will take under My protection."

**5** Save the life of the widow and rescue them, so that God may save you from all who rise against you with evil, and save your descendants after you. For the generation of the righteous is blessed; they do not lack bread, but have plenty and give.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "I will not fear what man can do to me"** — Psalm 56:11; 118:6.
- **v2 "Though war rise against me… One thing I have asked of the LORD"** — Psalm 27:3–4. The opening words in the Ge'ez, *ʾamakro dunī*, are garbled; read with the Psalm. *He is not afraid of bad news; his heart is firm, trusting in the LORD* (Psalm 112:7).
- **v3 "Who has trusted in God and been put to shame?"** — *Who ever trusted in the Lord and was put to shame? Or who ever called upon Him and was overlooked?* (Sirach 2:10).
- **v4 "those who honor Me I will honor"** — 1 Samuel 2:30; *I love those who love me* (Proverbs 8:17).
- **v5 "they do not lack bread, but have plenty and give"** — *I have not seen the righteous forsaken or his children begging for bread. He is ever lending generously* (Psalm 37:25–26).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

# 3 Meqabyan 8

## Job Trusted in God

**1** Job trusted in God, and He saved him from all the affliction that the devil, the enemy of mankind, brought on him. For he did not grow slack: he blessed the Lord his God and did not let his heart grieve at the affliction that came on him, as he said, "The Lord — and the Lord has taken; as it was the Lord's will, so it has been. In all things blessed be the name of the Lord in heaven and on earth."

**2** When God saw that Job's heart was pure, God received him with great honor.

**3** He gave him wealth greater than his former wealth and healed him of his sores, because of his patience in all that came on him, for he was very patient.

**4** So you too, if you are patient with the enemies sent against you, will be blessed.

**5** Be patient, then, and do not let your hearts shrink at the affliction that comes on you, so that God may be a refuge for you from those who oppose you, and for your children and your children's children after you.

**6** Trust in Him, and He will be a refuge for you; call on Him, and He will hear you; hope in Him, and He will have mercy on you; ask Him, and He will be a father to you.

## Others Who Trusted

**7** Remember Esther and Mordecai, Judith and Gideon, Barak and Deborah, Samson and Jephthah,

**8** and others like them, who were valiant, trusting in God, and their enemies did not overcome them.

**9** Those who want to do evil to them He repays with evil, for God is just and does not show partiality. All who fear Him and do justice, He keeps their souls and gives them honor and favor.

**10** He gladdens them in their coming in and their going out, in their death and their life, in their dwelling and their rising, for He gives life and puts to death.

**11** He strikes and has mercy.

**12** He makes poor and makes rich; He brings low and gives honor.

## Notes

- **v1 "The Lord — and the Lord has taken"** — *The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away; blessed be the name of the LORD* (Job 1:21). The Ge'ez has lost the verb "gave," and the gap is left as it stands.
- **v3 "wealth greater than his former wealth"** — *the LORD gave Job twice as much as he had before* (Job 42:10).
- **v6 "He will be a father to you"** — the Ge'ez *ʾabi* is read as *ʾab*, "father"; it could also be a slip for *ʿabiy*, "great."
- **v7** — Esther and Mordecai (Esther 4–9), Judith (Judith 8–16), Gideon (Judges 6–8), Barak and Deborah (Judges 4–5), Samson (Judges 13–16), Jephthah (Judges 11). *Time would fail me to tell of Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah* (Hebrews 11:32).
- **v9 "does not show partiality"** — *God shows no partiality* (Acts 10:34).
- **vv10–12 "He gives life and puts to death… He brings low and gives honor"** — *The LORD kills and brings to life… The LORD makes poor and makes rich; He brings low and He exalts* (1 Samuel 2:6–7); *your going out and your coming in* (Psalm 121:8).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

# 3 Meqabyan 9

## All Things Keep Their Bounds

**1** Whether in heaven or on earth, whether of spirit or of flesh, all is His, and all stands by His order.

**2** Nothing passes beyond its bound or beyond the command of the Creator of all. The track of the eagle in the sky, He directs its flight where He wills;

**3** the way of the beast of the earth on the rock, He directs it where He wills; and the way of a ship in the sea, no one knows its way but God alone.

**4** Who knows the path of the soul once it has gone out of its body, but God alone, whether the soul of a righteous one or the soul of a sinner,

**5** and where it wanders, whether in the wilderness or in the mountains, whether like a bird, or like the dew of Hermon that comes down on the slope of the mountain,

**6** whether like a storm wind, or like lightning that makes its paths straight,

**7** whether like the stars that shine in the deep, or like the sand of the seashore that is washed into the deep,

**8** whether like a stone of adamant set in the depths of the sea, or like a tree planted by a stream of water that gives its good fruit,

**9** or like a tree of *baḥrus* that the heat of the sun scorches, and the wind takes it and carries it to another place where it did not grow, and no trace of it is found?

**10** Like the mist of a cloud, no trace of it is found. Who knows the way of God? Who has been His counselor, and with whom did He take counsel?

**11** Who can follow His track? For the thought of God is hidden from mankind.

## The Gifts of the Creator

**12** No one can think out the wisdom and counsel of God. For He founded the earth on the waters and made it firm without a pillar; by His wisdom He set up the heavens on the wind, and spread them like sackcloth over the male waters.

**13** He commanded the clouds to send rain on the earth, to make grass spring up, and fruits beyond number, as food for mankind, so that we might rejoice together in God.

**14** All the blessings, delights and plenty God pours out for the children of men are so that they may be satisfied and give thanks to God, who gave them of the fruit of the earth.

**15** He clothed them in fine garments, with all that is desired, blessings, pleasure and delight, given to those who have done the will of God.

**16** In heaven too He gives them honor, favor and joy, where He has prepared the dwelling of their fathers, for those who keep the commandment of God.

**17** They walked in His judgment and His law, and did not transgress the commandment of Him who kept them, fed them, raised them and made them great, so that they would keep His law and seek His statutes. I have seen on the earth what God does for those who love Him, wearing down their enemy and keeping their souls.

**18** He gives them all that they ask of Him and does their will. Do not transgress the commandment of God, and do the will of God.

**19** Do not turn aside from His law and His commandment, so that He is not angry with you, does not destroy you in a moment or strike you in His wrath, so that you are not driven out of the inheritance of your fathers where you lived before, and your dwelling is not in the fire of Gehenna, from which you have no way out forever.

**20** Before your soul goes out of your body, keep the will of the Lord your God, so that your deeds may be fair when you stand before God.

**21** For the kingdom of heaven and earth is His alone; power and kingship are His; mercy and pardon are His alone.

**22** For He makes rich and makes poor; He brings low and gives honor.

## His Kingdom Does Not Pass

**23** David prophesied about Him, saying, "Man is like a breath; his days pass like a shadow.

**24** But You, O Lord, remain forever, and Your remembrance to generation after generation."

**25** Again he said, "Your kingdom is a kingdom over all the world, and Your dominion to generation after generation." You took the kingdom from Saul and gave it to David.

**26** No one appoints You, and no one can see You; You alone see all.

**27** Your kingdom will not pass away to generation after generation, forever and ever. There is none who judges Him; He judges all and sees all.

**28** He tests the heart and the mind, for He Himself made man in His image and likeness, to be for His praise, so that they would know how to worship Him without doubting, with an upright heart, and not bow down to foreign gods, but to God alone, who created them, fed them, raised them and made them great.

**29** But they refused to worship Him who created them, and bow down to wood and stone, to gold and silver, the work of men's hands.

**30** They sacrifice to them until the smoke of their sacrifice goes up to heaven, so that it stands before God and accuses them for all the sins they committed in worshiping their idols.

**31** They did not keep the commandment of God that they were taught, but learned to bow down to idols, and all filthy deeds that are not to be desired, reading omens in the stars, divination, idols, lust and everything that does not please God.

**32** This they do for lack of the good, because they did not want to praise God, to save their souls from wrongdoing and sin by giving thanks before God and His angels.

## The Rising of the Dead

**33** Their souls will stand naked when all of them rise together from the dust where they lay and where their bones were broken. The souls of the good have dwelt in houses of light,

**34** but those of sinners in darkness. When the houses are opened, the bodies will rise, and the souls will return into the bodies from which they first went out.

**35** They will stand naked before God as they were born, and their deeds will be laid bare, from their youth until the time they did them.

**36** They will carry their sins on their shoulders and be repaid according to the deeds they did, whether small or great.

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "The track of the eagle in the sky… the way of a ship in the sea"** — *the way of an eagle in the sky, the way of a serpent on a rock, the way of a ship on the high seas* (Proverbs 30:19).
- **v5 "the dew of Hermon"** — *like the dew of Hermon, which falls on the mountains of Zion* (Psalm 133:3).
- **vv8–9 "a tree planted by a stream of water… the wind takes it"** — *He is like a tree planted by streams of water that yields its fruit in its season… The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away* (Psalm 1:3–4). *Baḥrus* is a word not otherwise known.
- **v10 "Who has been His counselor?"** — *Who has measured the Spirit of the LORD, or what man shows Him His counsel?* (Isaiah 40:13; Romans 11:34).
- **v12 "the male waters"** — the waters above the heavens, called male, and those below, called female, as in 1 Enoch 54:8. *He stretches out the heavens like a curtain* (Isaiah 40:22); *He hangs the earth on nothing* (Job 26:7).
- **vv23–25** — *Man is like a breath; his days are like a passing shadow* (Psalm 144:4); *You, O LORD, are enthroned forever; You are remembered throughout all generations* (Psalm 102:12); *Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and Your dominion endures throughout all generations* (Psalm 145:13).
- **v28 "He tests the heart and the mind"** — the Ge'ez is "heart and kidneys," as in *the righteous God who tests the minds and hearts* (Psalm 7:9).
- **v30 "so that it stands before God"** — the Ge'ez has *kama bahyəb*, not understood, before "stands"; it is left out.
- **vv33–35 the rising of the dead** — *Many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake* (Daniel 12:2); *Naked I came from my mother's womb* (Job 1:21).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

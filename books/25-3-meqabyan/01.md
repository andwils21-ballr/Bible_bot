# 3 Meqabyan 1

## The One Who Will Come

**1** The coasts of Egypt will rejoice in the last days, because of the Good and Gentle One who comes to them, who will take vengeance for the oppression and the deceit of Sablyanos, who opposes the way of his Creator.

**2** He will take vengeance on him and bring his honor down into the earth and into shame, for he exalted himself in his thoughts.

## What the Deceiver Says

**3** He said, "Who is higher than I? I go into the heart of the seas and go up to the heights, I look on the depths, and I strike down the children of Adam like young birds.

**4** No one keeps them from my hand, for I do not spare those who live on the earth if they do the will of God. I devise pretexts against them, to turn them aside from the straight way of God,

**5** and to bring them into my wicked way, so that they go with me into perdition.

**6** Because of this, those who love Him and keep His commandment hate me; but those who stray from their Lord come to me, love me, keep my covenant and do my commandment as I command them. For I harm their hearts and twist their thoughts, so that they do not turn back to God their Creator.

**7** When I show them the goods of the world, I lead their hearts astray from the straight way; when I show them lovely women and virgins, by these too I turn them aside from the straight way.

**8** When I show them the gleaming stones of India, and stones of gold and silver, by this too I turn them aside from the straight way, so that they turn to my deeds.

**9** When I show them garments of fine linen and byssus, silk and brocade and *ʾagāyāt*, by this too I turn them from the straight way and bring them into my thoughts; and when I show them possessions and cattle like the sand, by this too I turn them to my deeds.

**10** When I show them the rivalries of pride, of women, of rank and of wrath, by all this I turn them to my way.

**11** When I show them omens, I enter into the hearts of their companions and mingle into their hearts the words of their own omens; I show them the omen of their own word, and so I make fools of them.

**12** To those whom I have made my dwelling I show signs, whether by the courses of the stars, the movements of the clouds, the burning of fire, or the cries of birds and beasts. By all this I set a sign in their hearts, for they are my dwelling.

**13** They speak and give signs to their companions, and I go before them and become the sign, as their soothsayers told them.

**14** I make the sign of their word come true for them, so that those who consulted them go astray and give them a reward, and tell their companions, saying, 'There are no seers like so-and-so, man or woman; it happens to them as they spoke. They know prophecy and can tell good from evil; everything happens as they said, and it is done as they spoke.'

**15** When they say this, I rejoice that those who perish multiply and those who go astray through me increase, so that the children of Adam perish with me. For because of their father Adam, God threw me down from my throne, because I said, 'I will not bow down to one lower than me.'

## The Deceiver's Plea

**16** I too draw into perdition all his children who walk in my commandment; and I have a grant from Him who created me, that those I lead astray will be cast with me into the fire.

**17** I pleaded with my Lord when He brought down His wrath on me and commanded them to bind me and cast me into Gehenna and the depths of Sheol. When my Creator commanded this, I pleaded before Him, saying, 'My Lord, since You are angry with me, have rebuked me with Your punishment and struck me in Your wrath, allow me to speak one word before You.'

**18** My Lord answered me and said to me, 'Speak, and I will hear you.' At once I took hold and begged Him, saying, 'Since I have been driven from my throne, let those I have led astray be with me in what I suffer.

**19** But those who refused me and did not go astray through me, and did not keep my commandment, so that they do Your commandment, fulfill Your will and keep Your word, when they refused what I taught them and did not go astray through me as I led them astray, let them be for Your kingdom, and let them receive the crown You gave me when You loved me.

**20** To all the powers who were driven out with me, who are called satans, give their crowns, and seat them at Your right hand on my throne, which is left empty by me and my angels.

**21** Let them praise You as You wish, and be as I was and as my angels were; let them praise You as You wish. For my authority has been overthrown and theirs raised up, for You hated me and loved those created from earth and dust.'

**22** My Lord answered me and said to me, 'As you wish: those who do not want what is Mine, let them be yours, according to your word; for though they saw and heard, you led them astray.

**23** Their perishing does not grieve Me, when they come to you, having left My commandment and the word of My Scriptures; if you led them astray, they will be condemned with you in punishment.

**24** You have no way out forever and ever, you and those who went astray through you, in the judgment of punishment; you will be tormented in the fire forever and ever.'"

## Notes

- **The text** — the Ge'ez of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo printed Bible, and the verse numbers follow it. No other ancient text of the book is known, and it is **not** the Greek 3 Maccabees. Much of it is the deceiver's own speech and the prophet's answer to him.
- **v1 "The coasts of Egypt will rejoice… the Good and Gentle One who comes to them"** — *Behold, the LORD is riding on a swift cloud and comes to Egypt* (Isaiah 19:1). *Sablyanos* is the teacher of evil of 1 Meqabyan 13:3 and 17:3–5 and 2 Meqabyan 9:2.
- **v3 "Who is higher than I? I go into the heart of the seas"** — the boast of the king of Babylon, *I will ascend above the heights of the clouds* (Isaiah 14:14), and of the prince of Tyre, *I sit in the seat of the gods, in the heart of the seas* (Ezekiel 28:2).
- **v9 the garments** — *fine linen, purple cloth, silk and scarlet cloth* among the wares of Babylon (Revelation 18:12). *ʾAgāyāt* is a cloth not otherwise known.
- **vv11–14 omens and soothsayers** — *There shall not be found among you… anyone who practices divination or tells fortunes or interprets omens… a medium or a necromancer* (Deuteronomy 18:10–11).
- **v15 "I will not bow down to one lower than me"** — the fall of the devil for refusing to worship Adam, as in 2 Meqabyan 9:3 and the Life of Adam and Eve 13–14.
- **v19** — the Ge'ez says twice, *wa-ʾi-saḥtu bəya za-kama ʾasḥatkəwwomu*, **and did not go astray through me as I led them astray**; the repeated clause is rendered once.
- **vv19–21 "let them receive the crown You gave me… seat them on my throne"** — the thrones left empty by the fallen angels given to the righteous; God's answer at 2:1, *I will make them inherit your throne*.
- **v22 "those who do not want what is Mine"** — the Ge'ez has *la-mbārya*, a word not otherwise known; read with the sense of the verse.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

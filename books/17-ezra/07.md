# Ezra 7

## Ezra the Scribe

**1** After these things, in the reign of Artaxerxes king of Persia, Ezra son of Seraiah, son of Azariah, son of Hilkiah,

**2** son of Shallum, son of Zadok, son of Ahitub,

**3** son of Amariah, son of Azariah, son of Meraioth,

**4** son of Zerahiah, son of Uzzi, son of Bukki,

**5** son of Abishua, son of Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the chief priest,

**6** this Ezra went up from Babylon. He was a scribe skilled in the instruction of Moses, which the LORD, the God of Israel, had given, and the king granted him all he asked, for the hand of the LORD his God was on him.

**7** Some of the sons of Israel, and of the priests, the Levites, the singers, the gatekeepers and the temple servants, also went up to Jerusalem in the seventh year of Artaxerxes the king.

**8** He came to Jerusalem in the fifth month, in the seventh year of the king.

**9** On the first day of the first month he began the journey up from Babylon, and on the first day of the fifth month he came to Jerusalem, for the good hand of his God was on him.

**10** For Ezra had set his heart to seek the instruction of the LORD, and to do it, and to teach statute and rule in Israel.

## The Letter of Artaxerxes

**11** This is a copy of the letter that King Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe, a scribe of the words of the commandments of the LORD and of His statutes for Israel:

**12** "Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, the scribe of the law of the God of heaven. Greetings. Now,

**13** I make a decree that anyone of the people of Israel, or of its priests or Levites, in my kingdom who freely offers to go to Jerusalem may go with you,

**14** because you are sent by the king and his seven counselors to inquire about Judah and Jerusalem according to the law of your God, which is in your hand,

**15** and to carry the silver and gold that the king and his counselors have freely offered to the God of Israel, whose dwelling is in Jerusalem,

**16** with all the silver and gold that you find in the whole province of Babylon, and the freewill offerings of the people and the priests, freely offered for the house of their God that is in Jerusalem.

**17** With this money, then, you shall buy with diligence bulls, rams and lambs, with their grain offerings and their drink offerings, and you shall offer them on the altar of the house of your God that is in Jerusalem.

**18** Whatever seems good to you and your brothers to do with the rest of the silver and gold, you may do according to the will of your God.

**19** The vessels that have been given to you for the service of the house of your God, deliver in full before the God of Jerusalem.

**20** Whatever else is needed for the house of your God that falls to you to provide, you may provide it from the king's treasure house.

**21** I, Artaxerxes the king, make a decree to all the treasurers in the province Beyond the River: whatever Ezra the priest, the scribe of the law of the God of heaven, requires of you, let it be done with diligence,

**22** up to a hundred talents of silver, a hundred kors of wheat, a hundred baths of wine, a hundred baths of oil, and salt without limit.

**23** Whatever is decreed by the God of heaven, let it be done in full for the house of the God of heaven, so that there may be no wrath against the realm of the king and his sons.

**24** We also inform you that it shall not be lawful to impose tribute, custom or toll on any of the priests, the Levites, the singers, the gatekeepers, the temple servants or the other servants of this house of God.

**25** As for you, Ezra, according to the wisdom of your God that is in your hand, appoint magistrates and judges who may judge all the people in the province Beyond the River, all who know the laws of your God; and those who do not know them, you shall teach.

**26** Whoever will not obey the law of your God and the law of the king, let judgment be executed on him with diligence, whether for death, for banishment, for confiscation of goods or for imprisonment."

## Ezra's Blessing

**27** Blessed be the LORD, the God of our fathers, who has put such a thing as this into the heart of the king, to beautify the house of the LORD that is in Jerusalem,

**28** and who has extended loyal love to me before the king and his counselors and all the king's mighty officials. I took courage, for the hand of the LORD my God was on me, and I gathered leading men from Israel to go up with me.

## Notes

- **vv1–5 "Ezra son of Seraiah… son of Aaron the chief priest"** — the high priests' line of 1 Chronicles 6:3–15, which ends with *Seraiah*, killed by the king of Babylon at Riblah (2 Kings 25:18–21), and his son *Jehozadak*, who went into exile. Ezra is of that house, and *son of* here reaches over generations. The list also passes over six names that Chronicles has between *Meraioth* and *Azariah*.
- **v6 "a scribe skilled in the instruction of Moses"** — *sofer mahir*, the phrase of the psalm, *my tongue is the pen of a skilled scribe* (Psalm 45:1). *Mahir* is **quick, practiced**.
- **vv6, 9, 28 "the hand of the LORD his God was on him"** — the book's refrain for Ezra, said six times (also 8:18, 22, 31). In v9 it is *the good hand of his God*.
- **v9 "On the first day of the first month… on the first day of the fifth month"** — four months on the road from Babylon, from the month of Passover to the month in which the house had been burned (2 Kings 25:8–9).
- **v10 "to seek the instruction of the LORD, and to do it, and to teach"** — in that order: study, practice, then teaching.
- **v12 "king of kings"** — the title Ezekiel gives Nebuchadnezzar (Ezekiel 26:7) and Daniel gives him to his face (Daniel 2:37).
- **v14 "the king and his seven counselors"** — the seven princes of Persia and Media *who saw the king's face* (Esther 1:14).
- **v25 "according to the wisdom of your God that is in your hand"** — the law itself, called wisdom by a Persian king, as Moses called it: *that is your wisdom and your understanding in the eyes of the peoples* (Deuteronomy 4:6).
- **v27 "to beautify the house of the LORD"** — *le-fa'er*, the verb of Isaiah's promise for the house: *I will beautify the house of My beauty* (Isaiah 60:7), *to beautify the place of My sanctuary* (60:13). From here the book is in Hebrew again, and Ezra speaks in the first person.

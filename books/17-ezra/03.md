# Ezra 3

## The Altar Rebuilt

**1** When the seventh month came, and the sons of Israel were in their towns, the people gathered as one man to Jerusalem.

**2** Then Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brothers the priests, and Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and his brothers, arose and built the altar of the God of Israel, to offer burnt offerings on it, as it is written in the instruction of Moses the man of God.

**3** They set the altar on its base, for fear of the peoples of the lands was on them, and they offered burnt offerings on it to the LORD, burnt offerings morning and evening.

**4** They kept the Festival of Booths, as it is written, and offered the daily burnt offerings by number, according to the rule for each day,

**5** and after that the continual burnt offering, the offerings for the new moons and for all the appointed feasts of the LORD that were consecrated, and the offerings of everyone who made a freewill offering to the LORD.

**6** From the first day of the seventh month they began to offer burnt offerings to the LORD, but the foundation of the temple of the LORD had not yet been laid.

**7** So they gave money to the masons and the carpenters, and food, drink and oil to the Sidonians and the Tyrians to bring cedar timber from Lebanon by sea to Joppa, according to the grant they had from Cyrus king of Persia.

## The Foundation Laid

**8** In the second year after they came to the house of God at Jerusalem, in the second month, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak began the work, with the rest of their brothers, the priests and the Levites and all who had come to Jerusalem from the captivity. They appointed the Levites from twenty years old and upward to oversee the work of the house of the LORD.

**9** Jeshua with his sons and his brothers, and Kadmiel and his sons, the sons of Judah, stood together to oversee those who did the work in the house of God, with the sons of Henadad, their sons and their brothers the Levites.

**10** When the builders laid the foundation of the temple of the LORD, the priests in their vestments were stationed with trumpets, and the Levites, the sons of Asaph, with cymbals, to praise the LORD according to the directions of David king of Israel.

**11** They sang to one another, praising and giving thanks to the LORD: "For He is good, for His loyal love toward Israel endures forever." All the people shouted with a great shout, praising the LORD, because the foundation of the house of the LORD had been laid.

**12** But many of the priests and the Levites and the heads of the fathers' houses, the old men who had seen the first house, wept with a loud voice when they saw the foundation of this house laid before their eyes, while many shouted aloud for joy.

**13** The people could not tell the sound of the shout of joy from the sound of the people's weeping, for the people shouted with a great shout, and the sound was heard far away.

## Notes

- **v1 "When the seventh month came"** — the month of the trumpet blast on its first day, the Day of Atonement on the tenth and the Festival of Booths from the fifteenth (Leviticus 23:24, 27, 34). The altar is up for all three.
- **v2 "Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel"** — so he is called here and in Haggai (Haggai 1:1). The genealogy of the house of David makes him the son of Pedaiah, Shealtiel's brother (1 Chronicles 3:17–19).
- **v3 "for fear of the peoples of the lands was on them"** — the reason the altar is built first: before walls or house, the daily offering, *morning and evening*, as the law commands (Numbers 28:3–4).
- **v4 "by number, according to the rule for each day"** — the Festival of Booths has its own count for every day: thirteen bulls on the first, twelve on the second, down to seven on the seventh (Numbers 29:12–32).
- **v7 "food, drink and oil to the Sidonians and the Tyrians… cedar timber from Lebanon by sea to Joppa"** — Solomon's arrangement with Hiram for the first house: *We will cut whatever timber you need from Lebanon and bring it to you as rafts by sea to Joppa* (2 Chronicles 2:16), paid for in wheat, wine and oil (2:10, 15).
- **v8 "in the second month"** — the month in which Solomon began to build (2 Chronicles 3:2).
- **v9 "the sons of Judah"** — *benei Yehudah*, and the Greek agrees. In the list of the Levites, Jeshua and Kadmiel are *of the sons of Hodaviah* (2:40), and the two names are close in Hebrew (*Yehudah*, *Hodavyah*). The verse may name the same family.
- **v11 "For He is good, for His loyal love toward Israel endures forever"** — the song at the dedication of Solomon's house, when the glory filled it (2 Chronicles 5:13; 7:3). Here it is sung over a foundation.
- **vv12–13 "the old men who had seen the first house wept"** — the prophet's word to the same people: *Who is left among you who saw this house in its former glory? How do you see it now? Is it not as nothing in your eyes?* (Haggai 2:3).

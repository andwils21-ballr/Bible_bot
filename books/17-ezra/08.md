# Ezra 8

## Those Who Went Up with Ezra

**1** These are the heads of their fathers' houses, and this is the genealogy of those who went up with me from Babylon in the reign of Artaxerxes the king:

**2** of the sons of Phinehas, Gershom; of the sons of Ithamar, Daniel; of the sons of David, Hattush,

**3** of the sons of Shecaniah; of the sons of Parosh, Zechariah, and with him a hundred and fifty men enrolled by genealogy;

**4** of the sons of Pahath-moab, Eliehoenai son of Zerahiah, and with him two hundred men;

**5** of the sons of Shecaniah, the son of Jahaziel, and with him three hundred men;

**6** of the sons of Adin, Ebed son of Jonathan, and with him fifty men;

**7** of the sons of Elam, Jeshaiah son of Athaliah, and with him seventy men;

**8** of the sons of Shephatiah, Zebadiah son of Michael, and with him eighty men;

**9** of the sons of Joab, Obadiah son of Jehiel, and with him two hundred and eighteen men;

**10** of the sons of Shelomith, the son of Josiphiah, and with him a hundred and sixty men;

**11** of the sons of Bebai, Zechariah son of Bebai, and with him twenty-eight men;

**12** of the sons of Azgad, Johanan son of Hakkatan, and with him a hundred and ten men;

**13** of the sons of Adonikam, the last ones, whose names are Eliphelet, Jeiel and Shemaiah, and with them sixty men;

**14** of the sons of Bigvai, Uthai and Zaccur, and with them seventy men.

## Levites for the House

**15** I gathered them at the river that runs to Ahava, and there we camped three days. I looked over the people and the priests, and found none of the sons of Levi there.

**16** So I sent for Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah and Meshullam, who were leading men, and for Joiarib and Elnathan, who were teachers,

**17** and I sent them to Iddo, the leading man at the place Casiphia. I told them what to say to Iddo and his brothers the temple servants at the place Casiphia, to bring us ministers for the house of our God.

**18** By the good hand of our God on us, they brought us a man of insight from the sons of Mahli son of Levi, son of Israel, namely Sherebiah, with his sons and his brothers, eighteen;

**19** and Hashabiah, and with him Jeshaiah of the sons of Merari, with his brothers and their sons, twenty;

**20** and of the temple servants, whom David and the officials had given for the service of the Levites, two hundred and twenty temple servants. All of them were designated by name.

## The Fast at Ahava

**21** Then I proclaimed a fast there at the river Ahava, that we might humble ourselves before our God, to seek from Him a straight way for us, our little ones and all our goods.

**22** For I was ashamed to ask the king for a troop of soldiers and horsemen to help us against the enemy on the way, because we had told the king, "The hand of our God is for good on all who seek Him, and His power and His anger are against all who forsake Him."

**23** So we fasted and sought our God for this, and He let Himself be pleaded with by us.

## The Gifts Entrusted

**24** Then I set apart twelve of the leading priests, with Sherebiah, Hashabiah and ten of their brothers.

**25** I weighed out to them the silver and the gold and the vessels, the contribution for the house of our God that the king, his counselors, his officials and all Israel who were present had offered.

**26** I weighed out into their hands six hundred and fifty talents of silver, silver vessels worth a hundred talents, a hundred talents of gold,

**27** twenty bowls of gold worth a thousand darics, and two vessels of fine bright bronze, as precious as gold.

**28** I said to them, "You are holy to the LORD, and the vessels are holy, and the silver and the gold are a freewill offering to the LORD, the God of your fathers.

**29** Guard them and keep them until you weigh them before the leading priests and the Levites and the heads of the fathers' houses of Israel at Jerusalem, in the chambers of the house of the LORD."

**30** So the priests and the Levites took over the weight of the silver and the gold and the vessels, to bring them to Jerusalem to the house of our God.

## Arrival in Jerusalem

**31** We set out from the river Ahava on the twelfth day of the first month to go to Jerusalem. The hand of our God was on us, and He delivered us from the hand of the enemy and of those who lay in wait along the way.

**32** We came to Jerusalem and stayed there three days.

**33** On the fourth day the silver and the gold and the vessels were weighed in the house of our God into the hands of Meremoth son of Uriah the priest, and with him was Eleazar son of Phinehas, and with them were the Levites Jozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui.

**34** Everything was counted and weighed, and the whole weight was recorded at that time.

**35** The exiles who had come from the captivity offered burnt offerings to the God of Israel: twelve bulls for all Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs, and as a sin offering twelve male goats; all of it was a burnt offering to the LORD.

**36** They delivered the king's laws to the king's satraps and to the governors of the province Beyond the River, and they supported the people and the house of God.

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "of the sons of David, Hattush, of the sons of Shecaniah"** — the line of David after the exile has *the sons of Shecaniah: Shemaiah, and the sons of Shemaiah: Hattush* (1 Chronicles 3:22). A son of David's house goes up with Ezra.
- **vv5, 10 "of the sons of Shecaniah, the son of Jahaziel… of the sons of Shelomith, the son of Josiphiah"** — every other line names a family and then its leader, *of the sons of Adin, Ebed son of Jonathan*. In these two the family's name is missing. 1 Esdras, which tells the same return, has *of the sons of Zattu, Shecaniah son of Jahaziel* and *of the sons of Bani, Shelomith son of Josiphiah* (1 Esdras 8:32, 36); both families are in the first list (2:8, 10). The Greek of Ezra agrees with the Hebrew in v10.
- **v15 "found none of the sons of Levi there"** — of the families of the first return, the Levites were the fewest: seventy-four (2:40).
- **v18 "a man of insight… namely Sherebiah"** — *ish sekhel*. The Greek reads it as a name, *Sachol*.
- **v21 "that we might humble ourselves"** — *le-hit'annot*, the word of the Day of Atonement, *you shall humble yourselves* (Leviticus 16:29), used for fasting.
- **v22 "I was ashamed to ask the king for a troop of soldiers and horsemen"** — Nehemiah, coming later to the same city, came with them: *the king sent with me officers of the army and horsemen* (Nehemiah 2:9). Ezra had told the king that God's hand would be enough, and he would not ask for more.
- **v23 "He let Himself be pleaded with by us"** — *va-ye'ater lanu*, the verb of Isaac's prayer (Genesis 25:21) and of Manasseh's (2 Chronicles 33:13).
- **v28 "You are holy to the LORD, and the vessels are holy"** — the priests and what they carry are named with the same word: the holy is carried by the holy.
- **v35 "twelve bulls for all Israel, ninety-six rams… twelve male goats"** — twelve bulls, eight times twelve rams and twelve goats, as at the dedication (6:17): the few who returned offer for all twelve tribes.

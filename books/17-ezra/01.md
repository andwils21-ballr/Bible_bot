# Ezra 1

## The Decree of Cyrus

**1** In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, to fulfill the word of the LORD from the mouth of Jeremiah, the LORD stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, and he sent a proclamation throughout all his kingdom, and also put it in writing, saying,

**2** "Thus says Cyrus king of Persia: The LORD, the God of heaven, has given me all the kingdoms of the earth, and He has appointed me to build Him a house in Jerusalem, which is in Judah.

**3** Whoever is among you of all His people, may his God be with him, and let him go up to Jerusalem, which is in Judah, and build the house of the LORD, the God of Israel. He is the God who is in Jerusalem.

**4** Every survivor, in whatever place he lives as a guest, let the men of his place support him with silver and gold, with goods and with livestock, besides the freewill offering for the house of God that is in Jerusalem."

## The Return Prepared

**5** Then the heads of the fathers' houses of Judah and Benjamin rose up, with the priests and the Levites, everyone whose spirit God had stirred up to go up and build the house of the LORD that is in Jerusalem.

**6** All who were around them strengthened their hands with vessels of silver, with gold, with goods, with livestock and with costly gifts, besides all that was freely offered.

**7** King Cyrus also brought out the vessels of the house of the LORD that Nebuchadnezzar had carried away from Jerusalem and put in the house of his gods.

**8** Cyrus king of Persia brought them out by the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, who counted them out to Sheshbazzar the prince of Judah.

**9** This was their number: thirty basins of gold, a thousand basins of silver, twenty-nine censers,

**10** thirty bowls of gold, four hundred and ten bowls of silver of a second kind, and a thousand other vessels.

**11** All the vessels of gold and of silver were five thousand four hundred. Sheshbazzar brought them all up when the exiles were brought up from Babylon to Jerusalem.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 and 2 Chronicles 36:22–23** — the same decree. Chronicles ends on its last words, *let him go up*; Ezra begins with them and finishes the sentence: *to Jerusalem… and build the house of the LORD*.
- **vv1, 5 "the LORD stirred up the spirit of Cyrus… everyone whose spirit God had stirred up"** — *he'ir… et ruach*, twice in five verses. The same God moves the king who gives the order and the families who go.
- **vv4, 6 "silver and gold… vessels of silver, with gold"** — the going out from Egypt again: *they asked of the Egyptians vessels of silver and vessels of gold*, and the LORD gave the people favor in their eyes (Exodus 12:35–36). The neighbors of the exiles give, as the Egyptians gave.
- **v4 "Every survivor, in whatever place he lives as a guest"** — *ha-nish'ar*, **the one left**, and *gar*, the verb of the guest. The word to Abraham was *your offspring shall be guests in a land not theirs* (Genesis 15:13); in Babylon they are guests again.
- **v8 "Sheshbazzar the prince of Judah"** — *ha-nasi*. Later he is called governor and is said to have laid the foundations of the house (5:14, 16), which 3:8–10 tells of Zerubbabel. The book does not say how the two men are related.
- **v9 "twenty-nine censers"** — *machalaphim*, a word found only here. Its root, *chalaph*, means to pass on or to change; the Greek of Ezra has *changed* or *spare* vessels, and 1 Esdras, which tells the same return, has *censers* (1 Esdras 2:13). The kind of vessel is uncertain.
- **vv9–11 "five thousand four hundred"** — the numbers listed add up to 2,499, not 5,400. The Greek of Ezra has the same total. 1 Esdras gives a list that does add up to its total, 5,469 (1 Esdras 2:13–14).

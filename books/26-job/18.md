# Job 18

**1** Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

## Why Are We Counted as Cattle?

**2** How long will you set snares for words?  
Consider, and then we will speak.

**3** Why are we counted as cattle,  
stupid in your sight?

**4** You who tear yourself in your anger,  
shall the earth be forsaken for your sake,  
or the rock be moved from its place?

## The Light of the Wicked Is Put Out

**5** Yes, the light of the wicked is put out,  
and the flame of his fire does not shine.

**6** The light is dark in his tent,  
and his lamp above him is put out.

**7** His strong steps are shortened,  
and his own counsel throws him down.

**8** For he is cast into a net by his own feet,  
and he walks onto a mesh.

**9** A trap seizes him by the heel;  
a snare lays hold of him.

**10** A rope is hidden for him in the ground,  
a trap for him on the path.

**11** Terrors frighten him on every side  
and chase him at his heels.

**12** His strength is starved,  
and disaster is ready for his stumbling.

**13** It eats away the parts of his skin;  
the firstborn of death eats away his limbs.

**14** He is torn from the tent in which he trusted  
and marched off to the king of terrors.

**15** In his tent dwells what is not his;  
sulfur is scattered over his dwelling.

**16** His roots dry up beneath,  
and his branches wither above.

**17** His memory perishes from the earth,  
and he has no name in the street.

**18** He is thrust from light into darkness,  
and driven out of the world.

**19** He has no offspring or descendant among his people,  
and no survivor where he lived as a guest.

**20** Those who come after are appalled at his day,  
and those who went before are seized with horror.

**21** Surely such are the dwellings of the unrighteous,  
and this is the place of one who does not know God.

---

## Notes

- **v14 "the king of terrors"** — death, the ruler of the dead; in the old Canaanite poems Death (*Mot*) is a king of the underworld.

# Job 31

## A Covenant with My Eyes

**1** I made a covenant with my eyes;  
how then could I gaze at a young woman?

**2** What would be my portion from God above,  
my heritage from the Almighty on high?

**3** Is not disaster for the unjust,  
and calamity for those who do iniquity?

**4** Does He not see my ways  
and count all my steps?

**5** If I have walked with falsehood,  
and my foot has hurried after deceit,

**6** let Him weigh me in a just balance,  
and let God know my integrity!

**7** If my step has turned aside from the way,  
and my heart has gone after my eyes,  
and any stain has clung to my hands,

**8** then let me sow, and another eat,  
and let my crops be rooted out.

**9** If my heart has been enticed by a woman,  
and I have lain in wait at my neighbor's door,

**10** then let my wife grind for another,  
and let other men kneel over her.

**11** For that would be a shameful crime;  
that would be an iniquity for the judges.

**12** For it is a fire that consumes down to Abaddon,  
and it would uproot all my harvest.

## Did Not One Fashion Us Both?

**13** If I have rejected the cause of my servant or my maidservant,  
when they brought a complaint against me,

**14** what then would I do when God rises up?  
When He calls me to account, what would I answer Him?

**15** Did not He who made me in the womb make him?  
Did not One fashion us both in the womb?

**16** If I have withheld from the poor what they desired,  
or made the eyes of the widow fail,

**17** or have eaten my bread alone,  
and the fatherless has not eaten of it

**18** (for from my youth he grew up with me as with a father,  
and from my mother's womb I guided her),

**19** if I have seen anyone perishing for lack of clothing,  
or the needy without covering,

**20** if his loins have not blessed me,  
and he was not warmed by the fleece of my sheep,

**21** if I have raised my hand against the fatherless,  
because I saw I had help in the gate,

**22** then let my shoulder fall from its blade,  
and my arm be broken from its socket.

**23** For disaster from God was a terror to me,  
and I could not stand before His majesty.

## If I Have Made Gold My Trust

**24** If I have made gold my trust,  
or called fine gold my confidence,

**25** if I have rejoiced because my wealth was great,  
because my hand had gained much,

**26** if I have looked at the sun when it shone,  
or the moon moving in splendor,

**27** and my heart has been secretly enticed,  
and my mouth has kissed my hand,

**28** this too would be an iniquity for the judges,  
for I would have denied God above.

**29** If I have rejoiced at the ruin of the one who hated me,  
or exulted when evil found him

**30** (I have not let my mouth sin  
by asking for his life with a curse),

**31** if the men of my tent have not said,  
"Who is there who has not been filled with his meat?"

**32** (the guest has not spent the night in the street;  
I have opened my doors to the traveler),

**33** if I have covered my transgressions as Adam did,  
hiding my iniquity in my bosom,

**34** because I feared the great crowd,  
and the contempt of families terrified me,  
so that I kept silent and did not go out of doors —

## Let the Almighty Answer Me

**35** Oh that I had one to hear me!  
Here is my mark; let the Almighty answer me!  
Oh for the document my adversary has written!

**36** Surely I would carry it on my shoulder;  
I would bind it on me as a crown.

**37** I would give Him an account of all my steps;  
like a prince I would come near to Him.

**38** If my land has cried out against me,  
and its furrows have wept together,

**39** if I have eaten its produce without payment  
and made its owners breathe their last,

**40** let thorns grow instead of wheat,  
and stinkweed instead of barley.  
The words of Job are ended.

## Notes

- **The oath** — Job's last speech ends with an oath of clearance, the form a man accused of a crime could take: *if I have done this, let this happen to me*. Some of the curses are spoken (vv8, 10, 22, 40), others left unsaid. By law, an oath like this put the case in God's hands.
- **v1 "a covenant with my eyes"** — Job's innocence reaches to the look as well as the act; *everyone who looks at a woman with lustful intent has already committed adultery with her in his heart* (Matthew 5:28).
- **v6 "let Him weigh me in a just balance"** — Job asks for a true scale, *a just balance and scales are the LORD's* (Proverbs 16:11). Compare his wish in 6:2 that his grief were weighed.
- **v10 "let my wife grind for another"** — become another man's slave, and his wife; grinding grain was the work of a slave woman (Exodus 11:5; Isaiah 47:2).
- **v15 "Did not One fashion us both in the womb?"** — master and slave are made alike by the one God; *Have we not all one Father? Has not one God created us?* (Malachi 2:10; Proverbs 22:2).
- **v18 "I guided her"** — the widow of v16: from his youth Job cared for the fatherless as a father and the widow as a son.
- **vv26–27 "if I have looked at the sun… and my mouth has kissed my hand"** — worship of the sun and moon, done by blowing a kiss toward them (Deuteronomy 4:19; Ezekiel 8:16).
- **v32 "the guest"** — *ger*, the one who lives as a guest; *Abraham ran to meet them* (Genesis 18:2–5; Judges 19:20).
- **v33 "as Adam did"** — *ke-adam*, which can also mean **as men do**. Adam hid himself and covered his sin (Genesis 3:8–12).
- **v35 "Here is my mark"** — *tavi*, **my tav**, the last letter of the Hebrew alphabet, written as an X: Job signs his statement and rests his case. **The document my adversary has written** is the formal charge he has asked God for since 13:22–23.

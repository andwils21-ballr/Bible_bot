# Job 2

## Skin for Skin

**1** And it came to pass one day that the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and Satan also came among them to present himself before the LORD.

**2** The LORD said to Satan, "Where have you come from?" Satan answered the LORD and said, "From roaming over the earth and from walking about on it."

**3** The LORD said to Satan, "Have you set your heart on My servant Job? For there is no one like him on the earth, a man blameless and upright, who fears God and turns away from evil. He still holds fast to his integrity, though you incited Me against him to swallow him up without cause."

**4** Satan answered the LORD and said, "Skin for skin! All that a man has he will give for his life.

**5** But stretch out Your hand now and touch his bone and his flesh, and he will surely curse You to Your face."

**6** The LORD said to Satan, "He is in your hand; only keep his life."

**7** Satan went out from the presence of the LORD and struck Job with evil sores from the sole of his foot to the crown of his head.

**8** He took a piece of broken pottery to scrape himself with, as he sat among the ashes.

**9** His wife said to him, "Do you still hold fast to your integrity? Curse God and die."

**10** He said to her, "You speak as one of the foolish women speaks. Shall we receive good from God, and not receive evil?" In all this Job did not sin with his lips.

## The Three Friends

**11** When Job's three friends heard of all this evil that had come on him, they came each from his own place, Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite and Zophar the Naamathite. They had agreed together to come to show him sympathy and to comfort him.

**12** When they lifted up their eyes from far off, they did not recognize him. They lifted up their voices and wept; each tore his robe, and they threw dust on their heads toward the sky.

**13** They sat with him on the ground seven days and seven nights, and no one spoke a word to him, for they saw that his pain was very great.

## Notes

- **vv1–3** — the scene of 1:6–8 told again in the same words, with one line added: **He still holds fast to his integrity**. *Tummah*, **integrity**, is the noun of *tam*, **blameless**, and Job's wife throws it back at him in v9.
- **v3 "without cause"** — *chinnam*, the word of Satan's question in 1:9, *Is it for nothing that Job fears God?* The LORD now turns it back: the suffering was **for nothing**.
- **v4 "Skin for skin!"** — a saying of trade, like-for-like: a man will give up any skin, any possession, to save his own.
- **v6 "only keep his life"** — *et nafsho shemor*, **guard his life**: Satan may strike, but he must keep Job alive.
- **v7 "evil sores from the sole of his foot to the crown of his head"** — *shechin ra*, the very curse of Deuteronomy 28:35, *The LORD will strike you… with grievous boils of which you cannot be healed, from the sole of your foot to the crown of your head*.
- **v9 "Curse God and die"** — the Hebrew is *barekh*, **bless**, as at 1:5 and 1:11 and 2:5. The Greek gives Job's wife a longer speech here, telling of her own wanderings and labor since the losses; the Hebrew has only these words.
- **v10 "one of the foolish women"** — *nevalot*, from *naval*, the fool who says in his heart, *There is no God* (Psalm 14:1); not a lack of brains but a lack of reverence.
- **v11 the three friends** — **Teman** is in Edom, named for a grandson of Esau (Genesis 36:11); *Is wisdom no more in Teman?* (Jeremiah 49:7). **Shuah** was a son of Abraham by Keturah, sent east (Genesis 25:2, 6). **Naamah** is not otherwise known as a place.
- **v13 "seven days and seven nights"** — the time of mourning for the dead; Joseph *made a mourning for his father seven days* (Genesis 50:10).

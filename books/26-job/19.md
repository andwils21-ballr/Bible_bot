# Job 19

**1** Job answered and said:

## God Has Put Me in the Wrong

**2** How long will you torment me  
and crush me with words?

**3** These ten times you have reproached me;  
are you not ashamed to wrong me?

**4** Even if it is true that I have erred,  
my error stays with me.

**5** If indeed you make yourselves great against me  
and use my disgrace as an argument against me,

**6** know then that God has put me in the wrong  
and closed His net around me.

**7** I cry out, "Violence!" but I am not answered;  
I call for help, but there is no justice.

**8** He has walled up my way, so that I cannot pass,  
and He has set darkness on my paths.

**9** He has stripped my glory from me  
and taken the crown from my head.

**10** He breaks me down on every side, and I am gone,  
and He has pulled up my hope like a tree.

**11** He has kindled His anger against me  
and counts me as one of His enemies.

**12** His troops come on together;  
they have built up their road against me  
and camp around my tent.

## Forsaken by All

**13** He has put my brothers far from me,  
and those who knew me are wholly estranged from me.

**14** My relatives have failed me,  
and my close friends have forgotten me.

**15** The guests in my house and my maidservants count me a stranger;  
I have become a foreigner in their eyes.

**16** I call to my servant, but he gives me no answer;  
I must plead with him with my own mouth.

**17** My breath is strange to my wife,  
and I am repulsive to the children of my own womb.

**18** Even young children despise me;  
when I rise, they talk against me.

**19** All my intimate friends abhor me,  
and those I loved have turned against me.

**20** My bones cling to my skin and to my flesh,  
and I have escaped by the skin of my teeth.

**21** Have pity on me, have pity on me, you my friends,  
for the hand of God has touched me!

**22** Why do you pursue me as God does?  
Why are you not satisfied with my flesh?

## My Redeemer Lives

**23** Oh that my words were written down!  
Oh that they were inscribed in a book,

**24** with an iron pen and lead,  
cut in the rock forever!

**25** For I know that my Redeemer lives,  
and at the last He will stand upon the dust.

**26** After my skin has been thus torn away,  
yet from my flesh I will see God,

**27** whom I will see for myself,  
and my eyes will see, and not a stranger.  
My heart faints within me!

**28** If you say, "How we will pursue him!"  
and, "The root of the matter is found in him,"

**29** be afraid of the sword,  
for wrath brings the punishments of the sword,  
so that you may know there is a judgment.

## Notes

- **v3 "ten times"** — a round number, **again and again** (Genesis 31:7).
- **v6 "God has put me in the wrong"** — *ivvetani*, the verb of Bildad's question, *Does God pervert justice?* (8:3). Job answers: in my case, He has.
- **v12 "they have built up their road against me"** — a siege ramp, cast up against the walls of a city; Job's tent is besieged by an army.
- **v17 "the children of my own womb"** — *benei vitni*, the sons of the womb that bore me, that is, **my brothers**; Job's own children are dead (1:19).
- **v20 "by the skin of my teeth"** — the origin of the English saying; the sense of the Hebrew is uncertain, perhaps that nothing of him is left whole but his gums.
- **v25 "my Redeemer lives"** — *go'ali chai*. The *go'el* is the next of kin who buys back his kinsman's land, avenges his blood and takes up his cause (Leviticus 25:25; Numbers 35:19; Ruth 4:4–6); *their Redeemer is strong; He will plead their cause* (Proverbs 23:11). Job looks for the witness in heaven of 16:19. The Hebrew does not say who the *go'el* is; the Church has heard in it the risen Christ, and the capital letter follows that reading.
- **v25 "at the last He will stand upon the dust"** — *acharon*, **the last**, as God calls Himself *the first and the last* (Isaiah 44:6); **stand** is the word for a witness rising in court.
- **v26 "from my flesh I will see God"** — *mi-besari*, which can mean **in my flesh**, from within his body, or **apart from my flesh**, after it is gone. The Hebrew of vv26–27 is very hard, and every translation is partly a guess.
- **v27 "and not a stranger"** — *ve-lo zar*: Job's own eyes will see God, and God will not be a stranger to him, as his household now is (v15).
- **v28 "found in him"** — the Hebrew text has *bi*, **in me**; some Hebrew manuscripts and the Greek have *bo*, **in him**, followed here, since the friends are speaking of Job.

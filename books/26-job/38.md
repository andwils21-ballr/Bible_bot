# Job 38

**1** Then the LORD answered Job out of the storm and said:

**2** Who is this who darkens counsel  
by words without knowledge?

**3** Gird up your loins like a man;  
I will question you, and you make it known to Me.

## The Foundations of the Earth

**4** Where were you when I laid the foundation of the earth?  
Tell Me, if you have understanding.

**5** Who set its measurements? Surely you know!  
Or who stretched the line over it?

**6** On what were its bases sunk,  
or who laid its cornerstone,

**7** when the morning stars sang together  
and all the sons of God shouted for joy?

**8** Who shut in the sea with doors  
when it burst out from the womb,

**9** when I made clouds its garment  
and thick darkness its swaddling band,

**10** and broke off My limit for it,  
and set bars and doors,

**11** and said, "This far you may come, and no farther,  
and here your proud waves shall stop"?

## The Morning and the Deep

**12** Have you ever in your days commanded the morning,  
and shown the dawn its place,

**13** to take hold of the edges of the earth,  
so that the wicked are shaken out of it?

**14** It is changed like clay under the seal,  
and its features stand out like a garment.

**15** From the wicked their light is withheld,  
and the arm raised high is broken.

**16** Have you gone into the springs of the sea,  
or walked in the recesses of the deep?

**17** Have the gates of death been shown to you,  
or have you seen the gates of deep shadow?

**18** Have you understood the breadth of the earth?  
Tell Me, if you know all this.

**19** Where is the way to the dwelling of light,  
and darkness, where is its place,

**20** that you may take it to its territory  
and know the paths to its home?

**21** You know, for you were born then,  
and the number of your days is great!

## The Storehouses of the Snow

**22** Have you gone into the storehouses of the snow,  
or have you seen the storehouses of the hail,

**23** which I have kept for the time of trouble,  
for the day of battle and war?

**24** What is the way to where the light is divided,  
or where the east wind is scattered over the earth?

**25** Who has cut a channel for the downpour  
and a way for the thunderbolt,

**26** to bring rain on a land where no one lives,  
on the wilderness in which there is no man,

**27** to satisfy the waste and desolate ground  
and make the grass sprout?

**28** Does the rain have a father,  
or who has fathered the drops of dew?

**29** From whose womb did the ice come out,  
and who gave birth to the frost of heaven?

**30** The waters hide themselves as under stone,  
and the face of the deep is frozen.

## The Stars and the Clouds

**31** Can you bind the chains of the Pleiades  
or loosen the cords of Orion?

**32** Can you lead out the Mazzaroth in their season,  
or guide the Bear with her children?

**33** Do you know the laws of the heavens?  
Can you set their rule over the earth?

**34** Can you lift up your voice to the clouds,  
so that a flood of waters covers you?

**35** Can you send out lightnings, so that they go  
and say to you, "Here we are"?

**36** Who has put wisdom in the ibis,  
or given understanding to the rooster?

**37** Who can number the clouds by wisdom,  
or tip over the waterskins of the heavens,

**38** when the dust runs into a mass  
and the clods stick together?

## The Lion and the Raven

**39** Can you hunt prey for the lioness,  
or satisfy the appetite of the young lions,

**40** when they crouch in their dens,  
or lie in wait in the thicket?

**41** Who provides for the raven its food,  
when its young cry to God  
and wander about for lack of food?

## Notes

- **v1 "the LORD answered Job out of the storm"** — *YHWH*, the name not heard since the prologue except once (12:9). **The storm**, *ha-se'arah*, is the whirlwind of Elijah (2 Kings 2:1, 11) and of the coming of God (Ezekiel 1:4; Nahum 1:3). God answers Job, not Elihu or the friends.
- **v2 "Who is this who darkens counsel by words without knowledge?"** — said of Job, who repeats the question in 42:3. Elihu had used the same phrase of Job just before (35:16).
- **v3 "Gird up your loins like a man"** — tuck up your robe for action, as for a wrestling match or a trial. Job had asked to be questioned (13:22); now he is.
- **vv4–7** — the earth as a building: measured, its line stretched, its bases sunk and its cornerstone laid, while the stars sang. *The morning stars* and *the sons of God* are the heavenly beings of 1:6.
- **vv8–11 the sea born from the womb** — God as midwife and nurse to the sea, the power of chaos, swaddling it in cloud and setting it a limit (Psalm 104:9; Jeremiah 5:22; Proverbs 8:29).
- **vv12–15 the dawn** — the light seizes the earth by its edges, like a cloth, and shakes the wicked out of it; the land takes shape **like clay under the seal**, as the dawn shows its contours.
- **v15 "their light is withheld"** — for the wicked, darkness is their light (24:17); the dawn ends their work.
- **v32 "the Mazzaroth"** — a word found only here, perhaps the signs of the zodiac or a single star group; *mazzalot* in 2 Kings 23:5. The other star groups are those of 9:9.
- **v36 "the ibis… the rooster"** — *tuchot* and *sekhvi*, both found only here. The ibis was thought to foretell the rising of the Nile, and the rooster the dawn. Other readings: **the inward parts… the mind**.
- **vv39–41** — the questions turn from the world to the living creatures that God feeds and Job cannot: *He gives to the beasts their food, and to the young ravens that cry* (Psalm 147:9; Luke 12:24).

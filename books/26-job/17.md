# Job 17

## My Spirit Is Broken

**1** My spirit is broken; my days are snuffed out;  
the graves are ready for me.

**2** Surely there are mockers around me,  
and my eye dwells on their provocation.

**3** Lay down a pledge for me with Yourself;  
who else will strike hands with me?

**4** Since You have closed their hearts to understanding,  
therefore You will not let them triumph.

**5** One who informs against his friends for a share of their goods,  
the eyes of his children will fail.

**6** He has made me a byword of the peoples,  
and I am one in whose face they spit.

**7** My eye has grown dim from grief,  
and all my limbs are like a shadow.

**8** The upright are appalled at this,  
and the innocent stirs himself up against the godless.

**9** Yet the righteous holds to his way,  
and the one who has clean hands grows stronger and stronger.

## Where Then Is My Hope?

**10** But you, come back, all of you, and come on;  
I will not find a wise man among you.

**11** My days are past; my plans are broken off,  
the desires of my heart.

**12** They make night into day:  
"The light is near," in the face of darkness.

**13** If I hope for Sheol as my house,  
if I spread out my bed in darkness,

**14** if I say to the pit, "You are my father,"  
and to the worm, "My mother," and "My sister,"

**15** where then is my hope?  
Who will see my hope?

**16** Will it go down to the bars of Sheol?  
Shall we go down together into the dust?

---

## Notes

- **v3 "Lay down a pledge for me with Yourself… strike hands"** — the gesture of standing surety for another's debt (Proverbs 6:1; 17:18). Job asks God to stand surety for him against God; no one else will.

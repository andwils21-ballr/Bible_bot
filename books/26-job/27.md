# Job 27

**1** Job again took up his discourse and said:

## As God Lives

**2** As God lives, who has taken away my right,  
and the Almighty, who has made my soul bitter,

**3** as long as my breath is in me,  
and the breath of God is in my nostrils,

**4** my lips will not speak injustice,  
and my tongue will not utter deceit.

**5** Far be it from me to say that you are right;  
till I breathe my last I will not put away my integrity from me.

**6** I hold fast my righteousness and will not let it go;  
my heart does not reproach me for any of my days.

**7** Let my enemy be as the wicked,  
and the one who rises against me as the unjust.

**8** For what is the hope of the godless when He cuts him off,  
when God takes away his life?

**9** Will God hear his cry  
when distress comes on him?

**10** Will he take delight in the Almighty?  
Will he call on God at all times?

## The Portion of the Wicked

**11** I will teach you about the hand of God;  
what is with the Almighty I will not hide.

**12** You yourselves have all seen it;  
why then do you speak such empty breath?

**13** This is the portion of a wicked man with God,  
the heritage the ruthless receive from the Almighty:

**14** If his children are many, it is for the sword,  
and his offspring will not have enough bread.

**15** Those who survive him will be buried by the plague,  
and his widows will not weep.

**16** Though he heaps up silver like dust  
and lays up clothing like clay,

**17** he may lay it up, but the righteous will wear it,  
and the innocent will divide the silver.

**18** He builds his house like a moth's,  
like a booth that a watchman makes.

**19** He lies down rich, but will not do so again;  
he opens his eyes, and it is gone.

**20** Terrors overtake him like a flood;  
in the night a storm steals him away.

**21** The east wind lifts him up, and he is gone;  
it sweeps him out of his place.

**22** It hurls itself at him without pity;  
he flees headlong from its power.

**23** It claps its hands at him  
and hisses at him from its place.

## Notes

- **v1 "Job again took up his discourse"** — *mashal*, a solemn utterance, as of Balaam (Numbers 23:7). The new heading, unlike *Job answered and said*, marks that the friends have finished: no one answers Bildad's last speech but Job.
- **v2 "As God lives, who has taken away my right"** — Job swears by the very God who, he says, has wronged him: his faith and his complaint stand together.
- **v3 "the breath of God is in my nostrils"** — *the LORD God… breathed into his nostrils the breath of life* (Genesis 2:7).
- **v5 "I will not put away my integrity"** — *tummati*, the word God used of Job (2:3) and his wife threw at him (2:9). Job keeps it to the end.
- **v8 "when God takes away his life"** — *yeshel*, a rare verb, **draws out**, as one draws out a sword or pulls off a sandal.
- **v12 "empty breath"** — *hevel tehbalu*, **you breathe out breath**: their speeches are vanity.
- **vv13–23** — this account of the wicked man's ruin echoes Zophar's words of 20:29, and many readers take it as Zophar's missing third speech, or as Job quoting the friends' teaching. It is rendered as it stands.
- **v15 "buried by the plague"** — *ba-mavet*, **by death**, the word used for pestilence (Jeremiah 15:2). **His widows will not weep**, as no proper mourning is held (Psalm 78:64).
- **v18 "like a moth's… like a booth"** — a cocoon, and a watchman's hut in a vineyard (Isaiah 1:8): flimsy and short-lived.
- **v23 "claps its hands… hisses"** — gestures of scorn over the ruined (Lamentations 2:15); the subject may be the wind of v21, or every onlooker.

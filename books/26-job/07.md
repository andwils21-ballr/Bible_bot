# Job 7

## Like a Hired Worker

**1** Is there not hard service for man on earth,  
and are not his days like the days of a hired worker?

**2** Like a slave who longs for the shade,  
and like a hired worker who waits for his wages,

**3** so I have been given months of emptiness,  
and nights of misery are appointed to me.

**4** When I lie down I say, "When shall I rise?"  
But the night is long, and I am full of tossing until the dawn.

**5** My flesh is clothed with worms and crusts of dust;  
my skin hardens, then runs again.

**6** My days are swifter than a weaver's shuttle;  
they come to their end without hope.

**7** Remember that my life is a breath;  
my eye will not again see good.

**8** The eye of the one who sees me will see me no more;  
Your eyes will be on me, but I will be gone.

**9** As a cloud fades and goes,  
so the one who goes down to Sheol does not come up.

**10** He does not return again to his house,  
and his place does not know him anymore.

## Am I the Sea?

**11** Therefore I will not hold back my mouth;  
I will speak in the anguish of my spirit;  
I will complain in the bitterness of my soul.

**12** Am I the sea, or the sea monster,  
that You set a guard over me?

**13** When I say, "My bed will comfort me,  
my couch will ease my complaint,"

**14** then You frighten me with dreams  
and terrify me with visions,

**15** so that my soul would choose strangling,  
death rather than these bones of mine.

**16** I despise it; I will not live forever.  
Leave me alone, for my days are a breath.

**17** What is man, that You make so much of him,  
that You set Your heart on him,

**18** visit him every morning  
and test him every moment?

**19** How long will You not look away from me,  
nor let me alone till I swallow my spit?

**20** If I have sinned, what do I do to You, watcher of mankind?  
Why have You made me Your target,  
so that I am a burden to myself?

**21** Why do You not pardon my transgression  
and take away my iniquity?  
For now I will lie down in the dust;  
You will seek me, but I will not be.

## Notes

- **v1 "hard service"** — *tsava*, military service, a soldier's term of duty; *Her warfare is ended* (Isaiah 40:2).
- **v6 "swifter than a weaver's shuttle… without hope"** — *tiqvah*, **hope**, is also **thread** (Joshua 2:18): the shuttle runs out of thread, and the days run out of hope.
- **v9 "Sheol"** — the place of the dead, under the earth, from which no one comes back up (10:21–22).
- **v12 "the sea, or the sea monster"** — *yam* and *tannin*, the powers of chaos that God holds in check (38:8–11; Psalm 74:13). Job asks whether he is so dangerous that God must keep him under guard.
- **v16 "I despise it"** — *ma'asti*, with no object in the Hebrew; his life is meant. **A breath**, *hevel*, the word of Ecclesiastes, *vanity of vanities*.
- **vv17–18 "What is man, that You make so much of him?"** — the words of Psalm 8:4, *What is man that You are mindful of him?* There the question wonders at God's care; here it begs to be left alone.
- **v19 "till I swallow my spit"** — a saying for the shortest moment.
- **v20 "watcher of mankind"** — *notser ha-adam*, the one who keeps watch over man; the word is used of God's care (Psalm 12:7), and Job feels it as a guard.
- **v20 "a burden to myself"** — the Jewish scribes' tradition lists this among the places where they softened the text; it read **a burden to You**. The Greek has *a burden to You*.
- **v21 "You will seek me, but I will not be"** — *shichartani*, **seek me early**, earnestly; God will come looking, and it will be too late.

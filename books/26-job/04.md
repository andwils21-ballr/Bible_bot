# Job 4

**1** Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said:

## You Have Strengthened Others

**2** If one tries a word with you, will you be weary?  
Yet who can hold back from speaking?

**3** You have instructed many,  
and you have strengthened weak hands.

**4** Your words have raised up the one who stumbled,  
and you have made firm the knees that give way.

**5** But now it has come to you, and you are weary;  
it touches you, and you are dismayed.

**6** Is not your fear of God your confidence,  
and the blamelessness of your ways your hope?

**7** Remember now: who that was innocent ever perished?  
Where were the upright cut off?

**8** As I have seen, those who plow iniquity  
and sow trouble reap it.

**9** By the breath of God they perish,  
and by the blast of His anger they are consumed.

**10** The roar of the lion, the voice of the fierce lion,  
and the teeth of the young lions are broken.

**11** The lion perishes for lack of prey,  
and the cubs of the lioness are scattered.

## A Word in the Night

**12** A word came to me in secret,  
and my ear caught a whisper of it.

**13** Among troubled thoughts from visions of the night,  
when deep sleep falls on men,

**14** dread came on me, and trembling,  
and made all my bones shake.

**15** A spirit glided past my face;  
the hair of my flesh stood up.

**16** It stood still, but I could not make out its appearance;  
a form was before my eyes;  
there was silence, and I heard a voice:

**17** "Can mortal man be righteous before God?  
Can a man be pure before his Maker?

**18** Even in His servants He puts no trust,  
and His messengers He charges with error;

**19** how much more those who live in houses of clay,  
whose foundation is in the dust,  
who are crushed like the moth.

**20** Between morning and evening they are beaten to pieces;  
with no one taking note, they perish forever.

**21** Is not their tent cord pulled up within them?  
They die, and without wisdom."

## Notes

- **v1 Eliphaz** — the first of the three friends to speak, and the eldest, it seems (15:10). The debate runs in three rounds, each friend speaking in turn and Job answering each.
- **v6 "your confidence"** — *kislah*, the trust a man rests on. Eliphaz begins gently: Job's own piety should be his hope.
- **v7 "who that was innocent ever perished?"** — the friends' teaching in one line: the innocent do not perish, so the one who perishes cannot be innocent. *I have not seen the righteous forsaken* (Psalm 37:25).
- **v8 "those who plow iniquity and sow trouble reap it"** — *Whoever sows injustice will reap calamity* (Proverbs 22:8); *whatever one sows, that will he also reap* (Galatians 6:7).
- **vv10–11 the lions** — five Hebrew words for lion in two verses (*aryeh, shachal, kefir, layish, lavi*): even the strongest of the wicked are broken.
- **v15 "A spirit glided past my face"** — *ruach*, which can also be **a breath of wind**; the next verse shows a form standing before him.
- **v17 "righteous before God"** — *me-Eloah*, which can also be read **more righteous than God**. The night voice's question returns in 9:2 and 25:4.
- **v18 "His messengers He charges with error"** — *mal'akhav*, here the heavenly ones who serve Him. This book keeps **messenger** for *mal'akh*, as the rest of the Hebrew books do. *He puts no trust in His holy ones* (15:15).
- **v19 "houses of clay"** — the body made from the dust (Genesis 2:7; 3:19); *if the tent that is our earthly home is destroyed* (2 Corinthians 5:1).
- **v21 "their tent cord"** — *yitram*, the cord that holds the tent up, pulled out and the tent collapses; the word can also mean **their excellence**, what remains of them.

# Job 29

**1** Job again took up his discourse and said:

## When God Watched Over Me

**2** Oh that I were as in the months of old,  
as in the days when God watched over me,

**3** when His lamp shone on my head,  
and by His light I walked through darkness,

**4** as I was in the days of my prime,  
when the friendship of God was over my tent,

**5** when the Almighty was still with me,  
and my children were all around me,

**6** when my steps were washed in curds,  
and the rock poured out streams of oil for me!

## Honored at the Gate

**7** When I went out to the gate of the city,  
when I took my seat in the square,

**8** the young men saw me and drew back,  
and the aged rose and stood;

**9** the princes held back their words  
and laid their hand on their mouth;

**10** the voice of the nobles was hushed,  
and their tongue clung to the roof of their mouth.

**11** When the ear heard, it called me blessed,  
and when the eye saw, it bore witness for me,

**12** because I delivered the poor who cried for help,  
and the fatherless who had no one to help him.

**13** The blessing of the one about to perish came on me,  
and I made the widow's heart sing for joy.

**14** I put on righteousness, and it clothed me;  
my justice was like a robe and a turban.

**15** I was eyes to the blind  
and feet to the lame.

**16** I was a father to the needy,  
and I searched out the case of the one I did not know.

**17** I broke the jaws of the unjust  
and made him drop his prey from his teeth.

**18** Then I said, "I will die in my nest,  
and I will multiply my days as the sand,

**19** my roots spread out to the waters,  
with the dew all night on my branches,

**20** my glory fresh with me,  
and my bow ever new in my hand."

## Like a King Among His Troops

**21** Men listened to me and waited  
and kept silent for my counsel.

**22** After I spoke, they did not speak again,  
and my word dropped on them like dew.

**23** They waited for me as for the rain,  
and opened their mouths wide as for the spring rain.

**24** I smiled on them when they had no confidence,  
and they did not cast down the light of my face.

**25** I chose their way and sat as chief,  
and I lived like a king among his troops,  
like one who comforts mourners.

## Notes

- **v1** — the last speech of Job, three chapters long: what he was (29), what he is (30), and his oath of innocence (31).
- **v4 "the friendship of God"** — *sod Eloah*, the close counsel of God, the word Eliphaz used for the heavenly council (15:8); *the friendship of the LORD is for those who fear Him* (Psalm 25:14).
- **v6 "my steps were washed in curds… streams of oil"** — pictures of overflowing plenty: *He made him suck honey out of the rock, and oil out of the flinty rock, curds from the herd* (Deuteronomy 32:13–14).
- **v7 "the gate of the city"** — the place of the elders' council and the law court (Ruth 4:1–2; Proverbs 31:23).
- **vv12–17** — Job's answer to Eliphaz's charges of 22:6–9: he did not send widows away empty, but made them sing for joy; he did not crush the fatherless, but helped them.
- **v14 "I put on righteousness, and it clothed me"** — *He put on righteousness as a breastplate* (Isaiah 59:17); *Let Your priests be clothed with righteousness* (Psalm 132:9).
- **v18 "as the sand"** — *ka-chol*; an old Jewish reading takes *chol* here as **the phoenix**, the bird that dies in its nest and rises again, which fits **die in my nest**.
- **v20 "my bow ever new in my hand"** — the bow is strength (Genesis 49:24; 1 Samuel 2:4).
- **v23 "the spring rain"** — *malqosh*, the late rain of spring that ripens the crop (Deuteronomy 11:14; Proverbs 16:15).
- **v24** — the sense is uncertain; it may also mean **when I smiled on them they could hardly believe it**.

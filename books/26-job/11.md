# Job 11

**1** Zophar the Naamathite answered and said:

## Oh That God Would Speak

**2** Shall a multitude of words go unanswered,  
and a man full of talk be judged right?

**3** Should your babble silence men,  
and when you mock, shall no one put you to shame?

**4** For you say, "My teaching is pure,  
and I am clean in Your eyes."

**5** But oh that God would speak  
and open His lips against you,

**6** and tell you the secrets of wisdom!  
For sound wisdom has two sides.  
Know then that God exacts of you less than your iniquity deserves.

**7** Can you find out the deep things of God?  
Can you find out the limit of the Almighty?

**8** It is as high as the heavens; what can you do?  
Deeper than Sheol; what can you know?

**9** Its measure is longer than the earth  
and broader than the sea.

**10** If He passes through and imprisons,  
and calls the court together, who can turn Him back?

**11** For He knows worthless men;  
He sees iniquity without needing to look into it.

**12** But a hollow man will get understanding  
when a wild donkey's colt is born a man.

## If You Set Your Heart Aright

**13** If you set your heart aright  
and stretch out your hands toward Him,

**14** if iniquity is in your hand, put it far away,  
and let no injustice dwell in your tents.

**15** Surely then you will lift up your face without blemish;  
you will be firmly set and will not fear.

**16** You will forget your misery;  
you will remember it as waters that have passed away.

**17** Your life will rise brighter than noonday;  
its darkness will be like the morning.

**18** You will trust, because there is hope;  
you will look around and lie down in safety.

**19** You will lie down, and no one will make you afraid;  
many will seek your favor.

**20** But the eyes of the wicked will fail;  
all way of escape will be lost to them,  
and their hope is to breathe their last.

---

## Notes

- **v6 "God exacts of you less than your iniquity deserves"** — *yashsheh lekha mi-avonekha*, **He causes some of your iniquity to be forgotten for you**: Job is suffering less than he has earned. It is the harshest word any of the friends has yet spoken.

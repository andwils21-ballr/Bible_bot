# Job 8

**1** Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

## Does God Pervert Justice?

**2** How long will you say these things,  
and the words of your mouth be a mighty wind?

**3** Does God pervert justice,  
or does the Almighty pervert what is right?

**4** If your children sinned against Him,  
He has sent them away into the hand of their transgression.

**5** If you will seek God earnestly  
and plead with the Almighty for mercy,

**6** if you are pure and upright,  
surely then He will rouse Himself for you  
and restore your rightful dwelling.

**7** Though your beginning was small,  
your end will grow very great.

## Ask the Former Generation

**8** For ask, please, of the former generation,  
and set your mind to what their fathers searched out.

**9** For we are but of yesterday and know nothing,  
for our days on earth are a shadow.

**10** Will they not teach you and tell you,  
and bring out words from their heart?

**11** Can papyrus grow tall where there is no marsh?  
Can reeds flourish without water?

**12** While it is still in flower, and not cut down,  
it withers before any other grass.

**13** Such are the paths of all who forget God;  
the hope of the godless will perish.

**14** His confidence is cut off,  
and his trust is a spider's house.

**15** He leans on his house, but it does not stand;  
he holds fast to it, but it does not last.

**16** He is green before the sun,  
and his shoots spread over his garden.

**17** His roots twine around the stone heap;  
he looks on a house of stones.

**18** If he is swallowed up from his place,  
it will deny him: "I never saw you."

**19** That is the joy of his way,  
and out of the dust others will spring up.

**20** God will not reject a blameless man,  
nor take hold of the hand of evildoers.

**21** He will yet fill your mouth with laughter,  
and your lips with shouts of joy.

**22** Those who hate you will be clothed with shame,  
and the tent of the wicked will be no more.

## Notes

- **v1 Bildad** — the second friend. Where Eliphaz spoke of a vision (4:12–16), Bildad speaks from the tradition of the fathers (vv8–10).
- **v3 "Does God pervert justice?"** — *mishpat* and *tsedeq*, **justice** and **what is right**, the two words Job will turn back on God (9:19–24; 19:7).
- **v4 "If your children sinned against Him"** — Bildad says plainly what Eliphaz only hinted: Job's children died for their own sins. Compare Job's sacrifices for them in 1:5.
- **v6 "your rightful dwelling"** — *nevat tsidqekha*, **the pasture of your righteousness**: the home that a righteous man deserves.
- **v7 "your end will grow very great"** — the friend speaks better than he knows: *the LORD blessed the latter days of Job more than his beginning* (42:12).
- **v9 "we are but of yesterday"** — *our days on the earth are like a shadow* (1 Chronicles 29:15).
- **vv11–12 papyrus and reeds** — *gome* and *achu*, plants of the Nile marshes (Exodus 2:3; Genesis 41:2); without water they wither while still green.
- **v14 "His confidence is cut off"** — *yaqot*, a word found only here; some read it as **gossamer**, a thread as frail as the spider's web in the next line.
- **vv16–19** — a second plant, flourishing in a garden and rooted among stones, then torn out so that even its place denies it.
- **v20 "a blameless man"** — *tam*, the word of 1:1 for Job. Bildad holds out the promise on the condition he set in v6.

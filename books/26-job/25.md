# Job 25

**1** Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

**2** Dominion and dread are with Him;  
He makes peace in His high places.

**3** Is there any number to His troops?  
On whom does His light not rise?

**4** How then can man be in the right with God?  
How can one born of a woman be pure?

**5** Even the moon is not bright,  
and the stars are not pure in His eyes;

**6** how much less man, who is a maggot,  
and the son of man, who is a worm!

---

## Notes

- **v1** — Bildad's third speech is the shortest in the book, six verses. The third round of the debate breaks down here: Zophar does not speak again, and some of the lines given to Job in chapters 24 and 27 sound like the friends.

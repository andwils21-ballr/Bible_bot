# Job 13

## Worthless Physicians

**1** My eye has seen all this;  
my ear has heard and understood it.

**2** What you know, I also know;  
I am not inferior to you.

**3** But I would speak to the Almighty,  
and I desire to argue my case with God.

**4** As for you, you smear over with lies;  
worthless physicians are you all.

**5** Oh that you would keep silent,  
and it would be your wisdom!

**6** Hear now my argument,  
and listen to the pleadings of my lips.

**7** Will you speak falsely for God  
and speak deceitfully for Him?

**8** Will you show partiality toward Him?  
Will you plead the case for God?

**9** Will it be well with you when He searches you out?  
Or can you deceive Him, as one deceives a man?

**10** He will surely rebuke you  
if in secret you show partiality.

**11** Will not His majesty terrify you,  
and the dread of Him fall on you?

**12** Your maxims are proverbs of ashes;  
your defenses are defenses of clay.

## I Will Argue My Ways to His Face

**13** Be silent before me, and let me speak,  
and let come on me what may.

**14** Why should I take my flesh in my teeth  
and put my life in my hand?

**15** Though He slay me, I will hope in Him;  
yet I will argue my ways to His face.

**16** This too will be my salvation,  
that the godless will not come before Him.

**17** Listen closely to my words,  
and let my declaration be in your ears.

**18** I have set my case in order;  
I know that I will be in the right.

**19** Who is there who will contend with me?  
For then I would be silent and breathe my last.

**20** Only two things do not do to me,  
then I will not hide myself from Your face:

**21** take Your hand far from me,  
and let not dread of You terrify me.

**22** Then call, and I will answer;  
or let me speak, and You reply to me.

**23** How many are my iniquities and my sins?  
Make me know my transgression and my sin.

**24** Why do You hide Your face  
and count me as Your enemy?

**25** Will You frighten a driven leaf  
and pursue dry chaff?

**26** For You write bitter things against me  
and make me inherit the iniquities of my youth.

**27** You put my feet in the stocks  
and watch all my paths;  
You mark a line around the soles of my feet.

**28** He wastes away like a rotten thing,  
like a garment that the moth has eaten.

## Notes

- **v4 "you smear over with lies… worthless physicians"** — *tofelei sheqer*, plasterers of falsehood, who cover the crack instead of mending it (Ezekiel 13:10–12); *rofe'ei elil*, healers of nothing.
- **v8 "Will you show partiality toward Him?"** — *ha-panav tissa'un*, **will you lift His face**, the phrase for favoring one side in court (Leviticus 19:15). Job warns that God Himself hates a false witness, even one on His own side.
- **v12 "proverbs of ashes… defenses of clay"** — sayings that crumble, and shields (*gabbim*, **bosses**, **mounds**) made of mud.
- **v14 "take my flesh in my teeth and put my life in my hand"** — two sayings for risking one's life (1 Samuel 19:5; 28:21).
- **v15 "Though He slay me, I will hope in Him"** — the written text has *lo*, **not**: *He will slay me; I have no hope*; the read form, followed here, has *lo*, **in Him**. The two are opposite in sense; both are old. The second line holds either way: Job will argue his case to God's face.
- **v16 "the godless will not come before Him"** — Job's daring to appear before God is itself the proof that he is not godless (*chanef*, the word Bildad used of him in 8:13).
- **v18 "I have set my case in order"** — *arakhti mishpat*, the words of a man who has drawn up his case for court (23:4).
- **v24 "count me as Your enemy"** — *oyev*, **enemy**, sounds like *Iyyov*, **Job**, and the rabbis drew the link: God treats Job as His foe.
- **v27 "You mark a line around the soles of my feet"** — *titchaqqeh*, **You carve**: a mark cut on a slave's or prisoner's feet, or a line he may not cross.
- **v28 "He wastes away"** — *he*, man; the line leads into the next chapter, *Man who is born of a woman* (14:1).

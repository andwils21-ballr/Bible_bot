# Job 12

**1** Job answered and said:

## A Laughingstock to My Friends

**2** No doubt you are the people,  
and wisdom will die with you.

**3** But I have understanding as well as you;  
I am not inferior to you.  
Who does not know such things as these?

**4** I am a laughingstock to my friends,  
I who called to God and He answered me,  
a laughingstock, the righteous and blameless man.

**5** In the thought of one at ease there is contempt for misfortune;  
it is ready for those whose feet slip.

**6** The tents of robbers are at peace,  
and those who provoke God are secure,  
those who carry their god in their hand.

## The Hand of the LORD Has Done This

**7** But ask the beasts, and they will teach you,  
the birds of the heavens, and they will tell you;

**8** or speak to the earth, and it will teach you,  
and the fish of the sea will declare it to you.

**9** Who among all these does not know  
that the hand of the LORD has done this?

**10** In His hand is the life of every living thing  
and the breath of all mankind.

**11** Does not the ear test words  
as the palate tastes its food?

**12** Wisdom is with the aged,  
and understanding in length of days.

**13** With Him are wisdom and might;  
He has counsel and understanding.

**14** If He tears down, it will not be rebuilt;  
if He shuts a man in, he will not be let out.

**15** If He holds back the waters, they dry up;  
if He sends them out, they overturn the land.

**16** With Him are strength and sound wisdom;  
the one led astray and the one who leads astray are His.

**17** He leads counselors away stripped,  
and makes fools of judges.

**18** He loosens the bonds of kings  
and binds a waistcloth on their hips.

**19** He leads priests away stripped  
and overthrows the long established.

**20** He takes away the speech of those who are trusted  
and removes the discernment of the elders.

**21** He pours contempt on nobles  
and loosens the belt of the strong.

**22** He uncovers the deeps out of darkness  
and brings deep shadow to light.

**23** He makes nations great, and He destroys them;  
He spreads nations out, and He leads them away.

**24** He takes away the understanding of the chiefs of the people of the earth  
and makes them wander in a trackless waste.

**25** They grope in the dark without light,  
and He makes them stagger like a drunken man.

## Notes

- **v2 "No doubt you are the people, and wisdom will die with you"** — Job's sarcasm: the friends talk as if all wisdom were theirs alone.
- **v4 "the righteous and blameless man"** — *tsaddiq tamim*, the man God answered, now mocked; *tamim* is the fuller form of *tam* (1:1).
- **v5 "contempt for misfortune"** — *lappid buz*, which the old translations read as **a torch despised**; most now read *le-fid*, **for disaster**. The line is hard either way.
- **v6 "those who carry their god in their hand"** — the sense is uncertain: those whose only god is their own strong hand (Habakkuk 1:11), or those who carry an idol.
- **v9 "the hand of the LORD has done this"** — the only place in the speeches of Job and his friends (chapters 3–37) where the name *YHWH* appears. The line is word for word Isaiah 41:20.
- **v12 "Wisdom is with the aged"** — taken by most as Job quoting the friends' boast (8:8–10), to answer it in v13: wisdom is with **Him**.
- **v14 "if He shuts a man in"** — the hedge of 3:23 again.
- **v16 "the one led astray and the one who leads astray are His"** — both the deceived and the deceiver are under His power.
- **vv17–25** — a hymn of God overturning the great: counselors, judges, kings, priests, elders, nations. Lines of it return in Psalm 107:40, *He pours contempt on princes and makes them wander in trackless wastes*.
- **v24 "a trackless waste"** — *tohu*, the word of Genesis 1:2, *without form*, the world before God ordered it.

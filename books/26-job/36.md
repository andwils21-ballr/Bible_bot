# Job 36

**1** Elihu went on and said:

## On God's Behalf

**2** Bear with me a little, and I will show you,  
for there are still words to say on God's behalf.

**3** I will bring my knowledge from afar  
and ascribe righteousness to my Maker.

**4** For truly my words are not false;  
one perfect in knowledge is with you.

**5** God is mighty, and does not despise anyone;  
He is mighty in strength of heart.

**6** He does not keep the wicked alive,  
but gives the afflicted their right.

**7** He does not take His eyes off the righteous;  
with kings on the throne  
He seats them forever, and they are exalted.

**8** If they are bound in chains  
and caught in the cords of affliction,

**9** then He declares to them what they have done,  
their transgressions, that they have behaved proudly.

**10** He opens their ears to instruction  
and tells them to turn back from iniquity.

**11** If they listen and serve Him,  
they complete their days in prosperity,  
and their years in pleasantness.

**12** But if they do not listen, they cross the river of death  
and die without knowledge.

**13** The godless in heart store up anger;  
they do not cry for help when He binds them.

**14** They die in their youth,  
and their life ends among the temple prostitutes.

**15** He rescues the afflicted by their affliction  
and opens their ears by distress.

## He Drew You Out of Distress

**16** He also drew you out of the mouth of distress  
into a broad place where there was no constraint,  
and your table was set full of rich food.

**17** But you are full of the judgment due the wicked;  
judgment and justice take hold of you.

**18** Take care that anger does not entice you into scoffing,  
and do not let the greatness of the ransom turn you aside.

**19** Will your cry keep you from distress,  
or all the force of your strength?

**20** Do not long for the night,  
when peoples are taken away in their place.

**21** Take care; do not turn to iniquity,  
for you have chosen this rather than affliction.

## Remember to Exalt His Work

**22** God is exalted in His power;  
who is a teacher like Him?

**23** Who has appointed Him His way,  
or who can say, "You have done wrong"?

**24** Remember to exalt His work,  
of which men have sung.

**25** All mankind has looked on it;  
man gazes on it from afar.

**26** God is great, and we do not know Him;  
the number of His years is past searching out.

**27** For He draws up the drops of water;  
they distill rain from His mist,

**28** which the skies pour down  
and shower on mankind in abundance.

**29** Can anyone understand the spreading of the clouds,  
the thunder of His pavilion?

**30** He spreads His light around Him  
and covers the roots of the sea.

**31** For by these He judges peoples;  
He gives food in abundance.

**32** He covers His hands with the lightning  
and commands it to strike the mark.

**33** Its thunder tells of Him;  
the cattle also, of what is rising.

## Notes

- **v4 "one perfect in knowledge is with you"** — Elihu speaks of himself; in 37:16 the same words are used of God.
- **v7 "with kings on the throne He seats them"** — *He raises up the poor from the dust… to make them sit with princes* (1 Samuel 2:8; Psalm 113:7–8).
- **vv8–10 "If they are bound in chains"** — Elihu's teaching: suffering is God's instruction, to turn a man from pride before it destroys him (33:16–18).
- **v12 "they cross the river of death"** — *be-shelach ya'avoru*, as in 33:18.
- **v14 "among the temple prostitutes"** — *qedeshim*, men who served in the shrines of foreign gods (Deuteronomy 23:17; 1 Kings 14:24); they died young and in shame.
- **v15 "He rescues the afflicted by their affliction"** — *ani be-onyo*, the heart of Elihu's answer: the suffering itself is the means of rescue.
- **v16 "a broad place where there was no constraint"** — *He brought me out into a broad place* (Psalm 18:19). Elihu speaks of Job's former life; the Hebrew of vv16–20 is very hard.
- **v18 "the greatness of the ransom"** — perhaps the suffering Job pays, or a bribe he might think to offer; *kofer*, as in 33:24.
- **vv27–33** — the hymn of the storm begins: rain, cloud, thunder and lightning, leading into chapter 37 and toward the voice from the storm (38:1).
- **v33 "the cattle also, of what is rising"** — the line is obscure; it may mean that even the cattle sense the storm coming. With other vowels it reads *zealous with anger against iniquity*.

# Job 10

## Why Do You Contend with Me?

**1** My soul is weary of my life;  
I will give free rein to my complaint;  
I will speak in the bitterness of my soul.

**2** I will say to God, Do not condemn me;  
let me know why You contend against me.

**3** Does it seem good to You to oppress,  
to despise the work of Your hands  
and shine on the counsel of the wicked?

**4** Do You have eyes of flesh?  
Do You see as man sees?

**5** Are Your days like the days of man,  
or Your years like a man's years,

**6** that You seek out my iniquity  
and search for my sin,

**7** though You know that I am not guilty,  
and there is no one to deliver out of Your hand?

## You Made Me like Clay

**8** Your hands shaped me and made me,  
all of me, all around, and now You swallow me up.

**9** Remember that You made me like clay;  
will You return me to dust?

**10** Did You not pour me out like milk  
and curdle me like cheese?

**11** You clothed me with skin and flesh  
and knit me together with bones and sinews.

**12** Life and loyal love You have granted me,  
and Your care has kept my spirit.

**13** Yet these things You hid in Your heart;  
I know that this was in Your mind:

**14** if I sinned, You would watch me  
and not acquit me of my iniquity.

**15** If I am guilty, woe to me!  
If I am in the right, I cannot lift up my head,  
filled with disgrace and looking on my affliction.

**16** Should it lift itself up, You hunt me like a lion,  
and again show Your wonders against me.

**17** You renew Your witnesses against me  
and increase Your anger toward me;  
fresh troops and a host are against me.

## Leave Me Alone

**18** Why did You bring me out from the womb?  
I should have breathed my last, and no eye would have seen me.

**19** I would have been as though I had not been,  
carried from the belly to the grave.

**20** Are not my days few? Cease,  
and leave me alone, that I may have a little cheer

**21** before I go, never to return,  
to the land of darkness and deep shadow,

**22** a land of gloom like thick darkness,  
of deep shadow without order,  
where the light is like thick darkness.

---

## Notes

- **v20 "Cease, and leave me alone"** — the written text has **Let Him cease… let Him set**; the read form, followed here, speaks to God directly. The Greek has *Let me alone, that I may rest a little*.

# Job 24

## The Poor of the Earth

**1** Why are times not kept by the Almighty,  
and why do those who know Him not see His days?

**2** Some move boundary stones;  
they seize flocks and pasture them.

**3** They drive away the donkey of the fatherless;  
they take the widow's ox as a pledge.

**4** They push the needy off the road;  
the poor of the earth all hide themselves.

**5** Like wild donkeys in the wilderness  
they go out to their work, searching for food;  
the desert gives bread for their children.

**6** They gather fodder in the field  
and glean the vineyard of the wicked man.

**7** They spend the night naked, without clothing,  
and have no covering in the cold.

**8** They are wet with the rain of the mountains  
and cling to the rock for lack of shelter.

**9** There are those who snatch the fatherless child from the breast  
and take a pledge from the poor.

**10** They go about naked, without clothing;  
hungry, they carry the sheaves;

**11** among the olive rows they press out oil;  
they tread the winepresses, and are thirsty.

**12** From the city the dying groan,  
and the soul of the wounded cries for help;  
yet God does not count it wrong.

## Rebels Against the Light

**13** There are those who rebel against the light;  
they do not know its ways,  
and do not stay in its paths.

**14** The murderer rises before daylight  
to kill the poor and needy,  
and in the night he is like a thief.

**15** The eye of the adulterer also waits for the twilight,  
saying, "No eye will see me,"  
and he puts a cover over his face.

**16** In the dark they dig through houses;  
by day they shut themselves in;  
they do not know the light.

**17** For deep shadow is morning to all of them;  
they are friends with the terrors of deep shadow.

## Swift on the Waters

**18** They are swift on the face of the waters;  
their portion is cursed in the land;  
no one turns toward their vineyards.

**19** Drought and heat snatch away the snow waters;  
so does Sheol those who have sinned.

**20** The womb forgets him; the worm feeds sweetly on him;  
he is remembered no more,  
and injustice is broken like a tree.

**21** He wrongs the barren woman who does not bear,  
and does no good to the widow.

**22** Yet He drags off the mighty by His power;  
they rise up, and no one is sure of life.

**23** He gives them security, and they are supported,  
and His eyes are on their ways.

**24** They are lifted up a little while, and then they are gone;  
they are brought low and gathered up like all others;  
they are cut off like the heads of grain.

**25** If it is not so, who will prove me a liar  
and make my words worth nothing?

## Notes

- **v1 "Why are times not kept by the Almighty?"** — times of judgment set aside for the wicked; **His days** are the days when He brings justice (Isaiah 2:12; Amos 5:18).
- **v2 "Some move boundary stones"** — forbidden by the law, *You shall not move your neighbor's landmark* (Deuteronomy 19:14; 27:17; Proverbs 22:28).
- **vv2–12** — the sins Eliphaz charged Job with (22:6–9) are done every day by others, and God does not act.
- **v5 "Like wild donkeys in the wilderness"** — the poor, driven from their land, live like wild animals foraging (30:3–8).
- **v11 "among the olive rows"** — *shurotam*, **their rows**, the terraces or walls of the olive groves. The poor press oil and tread grapes for others and go thirsty themselves (Deuteronomy 25:4).
- **v12 "God does not count it wrong"** — *lo yasim tiflah*, the word of 1:22, where Job did not charge God with wrong; now he says God does not charge the wicked.
- **vv13–17 rebels against the light** — the murderer, the adulterer and the thief work in darkness; *everyone who does wicked things hates the light* (John 3:20).
- **vv18–24** — these verses describe the swift end of the wicked, as the friends do (18:5–21; 20:5–29), and sit oddly in Job's mouth. They may be Job quoting the friends' view, or they may belong to a speech of one of the friends, perhaps Zophar's missing third speech. The text is rendered as it stands.
- **v20 "the worm feeds sweetly on him"** — *metaqo rimmah*, **the worm finds him sweet**; compare 21:33, where *the clods of the valley are sweet to him*.
- **v22 "no one is sure of life"** — *lo ya'amin ba-chayyin*, **he does not trust in life**; the line may mean that even the mighty, when God drags them off, despair of living.

# Job 3

**1** After this Job opened his mouth and cursed his day.

**2** Job answered and said:

## Let the Day Perish

**3** Let the day perish on which I was born,  
and the night that said, "A man is conceived."

**4** That day, let it be darkness;  
let God above not seek it,  
nor light shine on it.

**5** Let darkness and deep shadow claim it;  
let a cloud settle on it;  
let the blackness of the day terrify it.

**6** That night, let gloom seize it;  
let it not rejoice among the days of the year;  
let it not come into the number of the months.

**7** Let that night be barren;  
let no shout of joy come into it.

**8** Let those who curse the day damn it,  
those who are ready to rouse Leviathan.

**9** Let the stars of its twilight be dark;  
let it wait for light, and have none,  
and not see the eyelids of the dawn,

**10** because it did not shut the doors of my mother's womb,  
nor hide trouble from my eyes.

## Why Did I Not Die?

**11** Why did I not die from the womb,  
come out from the belly and breathe my last?

**12** Why did knees receive me,  
or why breasts, that I should nurse?

**13** For now I would have lain down and been quiet;  
I would have slept; then I would be at rest,

**14** with kings and counselors of the earth,  
who rebuilt ruins for themselves,

**15** or with princes who had gold,  
who filled their houses with silver.

**16** Or like a hidden stillborn child I would not be,  
like infants who never saw light.

**17** There the wicked cease from raging,  
and there the weary of strength are at rest.

**18** The prisoners are at ease together;  
they do not hear the voice of the taskmaster.

**19** The small and the great are there,  
and the slave is free from his master.

## Why Give Light?

**20** Why does He give light to the one in misery,  
and life to the bitter of soul,

**21** who wait for death, and it does not come,  
and dig for it more than for hidden treasure,

**22** who rejoice to the point of exulting,  
and are glad when they find the grave?

**23** Why give light to a man whose way is hidden,  
whom God has hedged in?

**24** For my sighing comes before my bread,  
and my groans pour out like water.

**25** For the thing I feared has come on me,  
and what I dreaded has come to me.

**26** I have no ease, I have no quiet, I have no rest,  
and turmoil has come.

## Notes

- **v1 "cursed his day"** — *qillel*, the plain verb for cursing. Job does not curse God, as Satan said he would (1:11; 2:5), but the day of his birth. *Cursed be the day on which I was born* (Jeremiah 20:14–18).
- **v4 "let it be darkness"** — *yehi choshekh*, the reverse of the first word of creation, *yehi or*, *Let there be light* (Genesis 1:3). **God** here is *Eloah*, the singular of *Elohim*, the name for God most used in the poetry of Job.
- **v5 "deep shadow"** — *tsalmavet*, as in *the valley of the shadow of death* (Psalm 23:4).
- **v8 "those who curse the day… ready to rouse Leviathan"** — those who were thought able to lay curses on days and to stir up the sea monster of chaos (41:1; Isaiah 27:1). With one letter changed, *yom*, **day**, would be *yam*, **sea**, the partner of Leviathan (7:12).
- **v10 "my mother's womb"** — the Hebrew is *bitni*, **my womb**, the womb Job came from.
- **v14 "who rebuilt ruins for themselves"** — kings who raised up ruined cities, or who built the great tombs that are now ruins; the phrase can bear both.
- **v16 "a hidden stillborn child"** — *nefel*, as in *it is better for the stillborn child than for him* (Ecclesiastes 6:3).
- **v22 "to the point of exulting"** — *elei gil*; with other vowels, *elei gal*, **at the grave mound**, which would match the second line.
- **v23 "whom God has hedged in"** — the image of 1:10, where Satan said God had put a hedge around Job to protect him; now the hedge is a prison.
- **v26 "turmoil has come"** — *rogez*, the **raging** of the wicked in v17 that ceases in the grave; for Job it has come in life.

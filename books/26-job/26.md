# Job 26

**1** Job answered and said:

## How You Have Helped!

**2** How you have helped the one without power!  
How you have saved the arm without strength!

**3** How you have counseled the one without wisdom,  
and made known sound wisdom in abundance!

**4** With whose help have you uttered words,  
and whose breath has come out from you?

## The Power of God

**5** The dead tremble  
beneath the waters and those who live in them.

**6** Sheol is naked before Him,  
and Abaddon has no covering.

**7** He stretches out the north over the void  
and hangs the earth on nothing.

**8** He binds up the waters in His thick clouds,  
and the cloud does not burst under them.

**9** He covers the face of the full moon  
and spreads His cloud over it.

**10** He has drawn a circle on the face of the waters,  
at the boundary of light and darkness.

**11** The pillars of heaven tremble  
and are astounded at His rebuke.

**12** By His power He stilled the sea;  
by His understanding He shattered Rahab.

**13** By His wind the heavens were made fair;  
His hand pierced the fleeing serpent.

**14** These are only the edges of His ways,  
and how faint a whisper we hear of Him!  
But the thunder of His power, who can understand?

## Notes

- **vv2–4** — Job mocks Bildad: the singular *you* shows he speaks to him alone. **Whose breath**: is Bildad repeating Eliphaz's spirit of the night (4:15)?
- **v5 "The dead"** — *ha-refa'im*, the shades in the underworld (Psalm 88:10; Isaiah 14:9). Even they, below the waters under the earth, tremble before God.
- **v6 "Abaddon"** — **destruction**, a name for the place of the dead (28:22; Proverbs 15:11); in Revelation 9:11 it is the name of the angel of the abyss.
- **v7 "He stretches out the north over the void and hangs the earth on nothing"** — *tohu*, the formless void of Genesis 1:2. **The north**, *tsafon*, is the high northern sky, and the mountain where God dwells (Isaiah 14:13; Psalm 48:2).
- **v9 "the full moon"** — *kisseh*, read as *keseh*, the full moon (Psalm 81:3); as written it is **throne**: *He covers the face of His throne*.
- **v10 "a circle on the face of the waters"** — the horizon, where the sky meets the sea, the edge of the world between light and darkness (Proverbs 8:27).
- **vv12–13 "He stilled the sea… shattered Rahab… pierced the fleeing serpent"** — the powers of chaos, *Leviathan the fleeing serpent, Leviathan the twisting serpent* (Isaiah 27:1; 51:9).
- **v14 "only the edges of His ways"** — all that has been said is the outer fringe; the full **thunder** of His power is beyond knowing. God's own speech will begin from the storm (38:1).

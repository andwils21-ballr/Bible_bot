# Job 32

## Elihu's Anger

**1** So these three men ceased to answer Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes.

**2** Then the anger of Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram, burned. His anger burned against Job because he made himself out to be in the right rather than God.

**3** His anger burned also against Job's three friends, because they had found no answer and yet had declared Job guilty.

**4** Elihu had waited to speak to Job, because they were older than he.

**5** When Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of the three men, his anger burned.

## I Am Young in Years

**6** Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said:

I am young in years, and you are old;  
therefore I held back and was afraid to declare my knowledge to you.

**7** I said, "Let days speak,  
and many years make wisdom known."

**8** But it is the spirit in man,  
the breath of the Almighty, that gives him understanding.

**9** It is not the many in years who are wise,  
nor the old who understand what is right.

**10** Therefore I say, "Listen to me;  
let me also declare my knowledge."

**11** I waited for your words;  
I listened for your reasoning,  
while you searched for what to say.

**12** I gave you my full attention,  
and there was no one among you to refute Job,  
none of you who answered his words.

**13** Do not say, "We have found wisdom;  
God may rout him, not man."

**14** He has not set out his words against me,  
and I will not answer him with your speeches.

## Full of Words

**15** They are dismayed; they answer no more;  
words have gone from them.

**16** Shall I wait, because they do not speak,  
because they stand there and answer no more?

**17** I too will answer my part;  
I too will declare my knowledge.

**18** For I am full of words;  
the spirit within me presses me.

**19** My belly is like wine that has no vent;  
like new wineskins, it is ready to burst.

**20** I must speak, so that I may find relief;  
I must open my lips and answer.

**21** I will not show partiality to any man  
or give flattering titles to anyone.

**22** For I do not know how to flatter;  
else my Maker would soon take me away.

## Notes

- **v1 "righteous in his own eyes"** — the Greek and Syriac read **in their eyes**: the friends stopped because they could not prove Job wrong.
- **v2 "Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite"** — a new speaker, named nowhere before, with a full line of descent, unlike the three friends. *Elihu* is **He is my God**; *Barachel* is **God blesses**. **Buz** was a nephew of Abraham, brother of Uz (Genesis 22:21); **Ram** may be the Aram of the same list.
- **v2 "rather than God"** — *me-Elohim*: Job's defense of himself had become an accusation of God (40:8).
- **v3 "and yet had declared Job guilty"** — the scribes' tradition counts this among the places where they softened the text; it read **and so had declared God guilty**: by failing to answer Job, the friends had let God appear in the wrong.
- **vv2–5 "his anger burned"** — four times in four verses. Elihu speaks out of anger, as Job did and the friends did.
- **vv6–9 "I am young in years"** — Elihu turns the friends' appeal to age (8:8–10; 15:10) on its head: wisdom comes from **the breath of the Almighty**, not from years.
- **v8 "the spirit in man, the breath of the Almighty"** — *ruach* and *nishmat Shaddai*, the breath God breathed into Adam (Genesis 2:7; 27:3). Elihu repeats it in 33:4.
- **v13 "God may rout him, not man"** — the friends' last excuse, that only God can answer Job; Elihu says he will.
- **v19 "new wineskins, ready to burst"** — *no one puts new wine into old wineskins* (Matthew 9:17); here even the new skins cannot hold him.
- **v21 "give flattering titles"** — *akhanneh*, to call a man by an honorific name instead of his own.

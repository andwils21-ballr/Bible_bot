# Job 23

**1** Job answered and said:

## I Would Lay My Case Before Him

**2** Even today my complaint is bitter;  
my hand is heavy on my groaning.

**3** Oh that I knew where I might find Him,  
that I might come even to His seat!

**4** I would lay my case before Him  
and fill my mouth with arguments.

**5** I would know the words He would answer me  
and understand what He would say to me.

**6** Would He contend with me in the greatness of His power?  
No; He would surely pay attention to me.

**7** There an upright man could argue with Him,  
and I would be delivered forever by my judge.

## When He Has Tested Me

**8** But I go forward, and He is not there,  
and backward, but I do not perceive Him;

**9** on the left, when He is at work, I do not catch sight of Him;  
He turns to the right, and I do not see Him.

**10** Yet He knows the way that I take;  
when He has tested me, I will come out as gold.

**11** My foot has held fast to His steps;  
I have kept His way and not turned aside.

**12** I have not departed from the commandment of His lips;  
I have treasured the words of His mouth more than my portion.

**13** But He is one, and who can turn Him back?  
What His soul desires, that He does.

**14** For He will complete what He has decreed for me,  
and many such things are with Him.

**15** Therefore I am dismayed at His presence;  
when I consider, I am in dread of Him.

**16** God has made my heart faint;  
the Almighty has dismayed me.

**17** Yet I am not silenced by the darkness,  
nor by the thick darkness that covers my face.

## Notes

- **v2 "my hand is heavy on my groaning"** — the Greek and Syriac read **His hand is heavy**, God's hand on Job, as in 19:21. The Hebrew may mean that Job holds back his groaning with his own hand.
- **v3 "His seat"** — *tekhunato*, His fixed place, His dwelling; Job longs to find God's court and plead there.
- **v7 "delivered forever by my judge"** — Job no longer fears the trial he dreaded in 9:14–20; he is sure that a fair hearing would clear him.
- **vv8–9 forward, backward, left, right** — *qedem*, *achor*, *semol* and *yamin* are also **east**, **west**, **north** and **south**, for a man who faces the sunrise. Job has searched the four quarters of the world.
- **v10 "when He has tested me, I will come out as gold"** — gold refined by fire (Zechariah 13:9; 1 Peter 1:7). Job does not know that he is being tested (1:11–12), but he says it.
- **v12 "more than my portion"** — *me-chuqqi*, **more than my allotted food**; the Greek reads **in my bosom**. *Your words were found, and I ate them* (Jeremiah 15:16); *Man shall not live by bread alone* (Deuteronomy 8:3).
- **v13 "He is one"** — *hu ve-echad*, **He is in one**: one in mind, unchangeable; the same words as *the LORD is one* (Deuteronomy 6:4), but here a source of dread.
- **v17 "Yet I am not silenced by the darkness"** — the Hebrew is hard; it may also be read **I am undone because of the darkness**. As read here, Job is still speaking in spite of it.

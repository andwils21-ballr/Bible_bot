# Job 42

**1** Then Job answered the LORD and said:

## Job's Answer

**2** I know that You can do all things,  
and that no purpose of Yours can be held back.

**3** "Who is this who hides counsel without knowledge?"  
Therefore I have spoken of what I did not understand,  
things too wonderful for me, which I did not know.

**4** "Hear, and I will speak;  
I will question you, and you make it known to Me."

**5** I had heard of You by the hearing of the ear,  
but now my eye sees You;

**6** therefore I despise myself,  
and repent in dust and ashes.

## The LORD's Word to the Friends

**7** And it came to pass, after the LORD had spoken these words to Job, that the LORD said to Eliphaz the Temanite, "My anger burns against you and against your two friends, for you have not spoken of Me what is right, as My servant Job has.

**8** Now take seven bulls and seven rams, go to My servant Job and offer up a burnt offering for yourselves. My servant Job will pray for you, for I will accept him, so that I do not deal with you as your folly deserves; for you have not spoken of Me what is right, as My servant Job has."

**9** So Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite and Zophar the Naamathite went and did as the LORD had told them, and the LORD accepted Job.

## The Latter Days of Job

**10** The LORD restored the fortunes of Job when he prayed for his friends, and the LORD gave Job twice as much as he had before.

**11** Then all his brothers and all his sisters and all who had known him before came to him and ate bread with him in his house. They showed him sympathy and comforted him for all the evil that the LORD had brought on him, and each of them gave him a piece of money and a ring of gold.

**12** The LORD blessed the latter days of Job more than his beginning. He had fourteen thousand sheep, six thousand camels, a thousand yoke of oxen and a thousand female donkeys.

**13** He also had seven sons and three daughters.

**14** He called the name of the first Jemimah, the name of the second Keziah, and the name of the third Keren-happuch.

**15** In all the land there were no women as beautiful as Job's daughters, and their father gave them an inheritance among their brothers.

**16** After this Job lived a hundred and forty years, and saw his sons and his sons' sons, four generations.

**17** Job died, old and full of days.

## Notes

- **vv3–4** — Job quotes God's own words back to Him: the question of 38:2 and the command of 38:3 and 40:7, and answers them.
- **v5 "now my eye sees You"** — what Job longed for in 19:26–27, *my eyes will see*, has come to pass, though not as he expected: God has answered from the storm, and has not answered his questions.
- **v6 "therefore I despise myself"** — *em'as*, with no object in the Hebrew, as in 7:16; it may mean **I reject what I said** or **I retract**. **Repent in dust and ashes**, *nichamti al afar va-efer*, can also mean **I am comforted concerning dust and ashes**, that is, about being mortal (30:19). The traditional reading is followed, and the line stays open.
- **v7 "you have not spoken of Me what is right, as My servant Job has"** — *elai*, **of Me** or **to Me**. God sides with Job, who spoke to God in protest, against the friends, who spoke about God in His defense. **My servant Job** returns from 1:8 and 2:3, four times in two verses.
- **v8 "seven bulls and seven rams"** — a large burnt offering, as Balaam's (Numbers 23:1). Job, who offered for his children (1:5), now prays for those who accused him. **I will accept him** is *panav essa*, **I will lift his face**.
- **v10 "The LORD restored the fortunes of Job"** — *shav et shevut*, the phrase of the return from exile (Deuteronomy 30:3; Psalm 126:4). **Twice as much**: the numbers in v12 are exactly double those of 1:3.
- **v11 "a piece of money"** — *qesitah*, an old unit of value, found elsewhere only in Genesis 33:19 and Joshua 24:32, from the days of the patriarchs.
- **v13 "seven sons and three daughters"** — the same number as before (1:2), not doubled; the rabbis said the first children were not lost but waited for him.
- **v14 Jemimah, Keziah, Keren-happuch** — **dove**, **cassia** (a fragrant spice) and **horn of eye paint**: names of beauty. The daughters, and not the sons, are named, and they receive an inheritance with their brothers, as Zelophehad's daughters did (Numbers 27:1–8).
- **v17 "old and full of days"** — the end of Abraham, Isaac and David (Genesis 25:8; 35:29; 1 Chronicles 29:28). The Greek adds that Job *will rise again with those whom the Lord raises up*, and identifies him with Jobab, king of Edom (Genesis 36:33).

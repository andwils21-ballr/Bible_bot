# Job 20

**1** Zophar the Naamathite answered and said:

## The Joy of the Wicked Is Short

**2** Therefore my thoughts answer me,  
because of the haste within me.

**3** I hear a reproof that insults me,  
and out of my understanding a spirit answers me.

**4** Do you not know this from of old,  
since man was placed on the earth,

**5** that the exulting of the wicked is short,  
and the joy of the godless but for a moment?

**6** Though his height goes up to the heavens,  
and his head reaches to the clouds,

**7** he will perish forever like his own dung;  
those who have seen him will say, "Where is he?"

**8** He will fly away like a dream, and they will not find him;  
he will be chased away like a vision of the night.

**9** The eye that saw him will not see him again,  
and his place will see him no more.

**10** His children will seek the favor of the poor,  
and his hands will give back his wealth.

**11** His bones are full of his youth,  
but it will lie down with him in the dust.

## Evil Sweet in His Mouth

**12** Though evil is sweet in his mouth,  
though he hides it under his tongue,

**13** though he spares it and will not let it go,  
and holds it back in his mouth,

**14** his food is turned in his stomach;  
it is the venom of cobras within him.

**15** He swallows down riches and vomits them up again;  
God casts them out of his belly.

**16** He will suck the poison of cobras;  
the tongue of a viper will kill him.

**17** He will not look on the rivers,  
the streams flowing with honey and curds.

**18** He gives back the fruit of his toil and will not swallow it;  
from the profit of his trading he will have no joy.

**19** For he has crushed and abandoned the poor;  
he has seized a house that he did not build.

**20** Because he knew no rest in his belly,  
he will not save anything he desired.

**21** Nothing was left after he had eaten;  
therefore his prosperity will not last.

**22** In the fullness of his plenty he will be in distress;  
the hand of everyone in misery will come against him.

## The Portion of the Wicked

**23** To fill his belly,  
He will send His burning anger against him  
and rain it on him as his food.

**24** He will flee from an iron weapon;  
a bronze bow will strike him through.

**25** He draws it out, and it comes out of his back;  
the glittering point comes out of his gall;  
terrors come on him.

**26** All darkness is laid up for his treasures;  
a fire not fanned will devour him;  
it will consume what is left in his tent.

**27** The heavens will reveal his iniquity,  
and the earth will rise up against him.

**28** The produce of his house will be carried away,  
swept off in the day of His anger.

**29** This is the wicked man's portion from God,  
the heritage decreed for him by God.

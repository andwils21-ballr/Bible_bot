# Job 5

## The Fool Taking Root

**1** Call now — is there anyone who will answer you?  
To which of the holy ones will you turn?

**2** For vexation kills the fool,  
and jealousy slays the simple.

**3** I have seen the fool taking root,  
but suddenly I cursed his dwelling.

**4** His children are far from safety;  
they are crushed in the gate, with no one to rescue them.

**5** The hungry eat his harvest,  
and take it even from among the thorns,  
and the snare gapes for their wealth.

**6** For trouble does not come out of the dust,  
nor does misery sprout from the ground;

**7** but man is born to misery,  
as the sparks fly upward.

## I Would Seek God

**8** But as for me, I would seek God,  
and to God I would commit my cause,

**9** who does great things beyond searching out,  
wonders without number,

**10** who gives rain on the face of the earth  
and sends water on the face of the fields,

**11** to set the lowly on high,  
and those who mourn are lifted to safety.

**12** He frustrates the plans of the crafty,  
so that their hands achieve no success.

**13** He catches the wise in their own craftiness,  
and the counsel of the twisted is swept away.

**14** By day they meet darkness,  
and grope at noon as in the night.

**15** But He saves the needy from the sword of their mouth,  
and from the hand of the mighty.

**16** So the poor have hope,  
and injustice shuts its mouth.

## Blessed Is the One God Reproves

**17** Blessed is the man whom God reproves;  
do not reject the discipline of the Almighty.

**18** For He wounds, and He binds up;  
He strikes, and His hands heal.

**19** In six troubles He will deliver you;  
in seven no evil will touch you.

**20** In famine He will redeem you from death,  
and in war from the power of the sword.

**21** You will be hidden from the lash of the tongue,  
and will not fear destruction when it comes.

**22** At destruction and hunger you will laugh,  
and you will not fear the beasts of the earth.

**23** For you will have a covenant with the stones of the field,  
and the beasts of the field will be at peace with you.

**24** You will know that your tent is at peace;  
you will look over your fold and find nothing missing.

**25** You will know that your offspring are many,  
and your descendants like the grass of the earth.

**26** You will come to the grave in ripe old age,  
as a stack of sheaves goes up in its season.

**27** This we have searched out; so it is.  
Hear it, and know it for yourself.

---

## Notes

- **v13 "He catches the wise in their own craftiness"** — quoted by Paul in 1 Corinthians 3:19.
- **v17 "the Almighty"** — *Shaddai*, the old name of God, used thirty-one times in Job, more than in all the rest of the Old Testament together. *My son, do not despise the LORD's discipline* (Proverbs 3:11–12; Hebrews 12:5–6).

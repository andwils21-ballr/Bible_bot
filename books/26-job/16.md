# Job 16

**1** Job answered and said:

## Miserable Comforters

**2** I have heard many such things;  
miserable comforters are you all.

**3** Is there an end to windy words?  
Or what provokes you, that you answer?

**4** I too could speak as you do,  
if you were in my place;  
I could string words together against you  
and shake my head at you.

**5** I could strengthen you with my mouth,  
and the comfort of my lips would ease your pain.

**6** If I speak, my pain is not eased,  
and if I hold back, how much of it leaves me?

## He Set Me Up as His Target

**7** But now He has worn me out;  
You have made desolate all my company.

**8** You have shriveled me up, and it has become a witness;  
my leanness has risen up against me; it testifies to my face.

**9** His anger has torn me, and He has hated me;  
He has gnashed His teeth at me;  
my adversary sharpens his eyes against me.

**10** They have gaped at me with their mouth;  
they have struck my cheek in scorn;  
they mass themselves together against me.

**11** God hands me over to the unjust  
and throws me into the hands of the wicked.

**12** I was at ease, and He broke me apart;  
He seized me by the neck and shattered me;  
He set me up as His target.

**13** His archers surround me;  
He slashes open my kidneys and does not spare;  
He pours out my gall on the ground.

**14** He breaks me with breach on breach;  
He runs at me like a warrior.

**15** I have sewn sackcloth on my skin  
and laid my horn in the dust.

**16** My face is red with weeping,  
and on my eyelids is deep shadow,

**17** although there is no violence in my hands,  
and my prayer is pure.

## O Earth, Do Not Cover My Blood

**18** O earth, do not cover my blood,  
and let my cry find no resting place.

**19** Even now, my witness is in heaven,  
and the one who testifies for me is on high.

**20** My friends scorn me;  
my eye pours out tears to God,

**21** that he would argue the case of a man with God,  
as a son of man does for his friend.

**22** For when a few years have come,  
I will go the way from which I will not return.

## Notes

- **v2 "miserable comforters"** — *menachamei amal*, **comforters of trouble**, who bring trouble instead of comfort; the friends came *to comfort him* (2:11).
- **v4 "shake my head at you"** — the gesture of scorn, *all who see me mock me… they wag their heads* (Psalm 22:7).
- **v7 "He has worn me out; You have made desolate"** — Job moves from speaking of God to speaking to Him and back, in one line; the Hebrew does the same.
- **v9 "my adversary sharpens his eyes against me"** — God pictured as a beast of prey and as an enemy in battle. **Adversary** is *tsar*, not the *satan* of chapters 1–2; Job does not know of the scene in heaven.
- **v10 "they have struck my cheek in scorn"** — *I gave my back to those who strike, and my cheeks to those who pull out the beard* (Isaiah 50:6; Lamentations 3:30).
- **v15 "laid my horn in the dust"** — the horn is a man's strength and honor (1 Samuel 2:1; Psalm 75:10).
- **v18 "O earth, do not cover my blood"** — blood that the earth does not cover goes on crying out for justice, as Abel's did (Genesis 4:10; Isaiah 26:21).
- **v19 "my witness is in heaven"** — *sahadi*, an Aramaic word, **my testifier**. Job has said there is no arbiter (9:33); now he looks for one in heaven, and in 19:25 he names his **redeemer**.
- **v20 "My friends scorn me"** — *melitsai re'ai*; *melits* can also be **interpreter**, **advocate**, as in 33:23, so the line can be read **my advocate is my friend**.
- **v21 "as a son of man does for his friend"** — *ben adam*, the same phrase as in Psalm 8:4 and 80:17.

# Job 34

**1** Elihu answered and said:

## Let Us Choose What Is Right

**2** Hear my words, you wise men,  
and listen to me, you who know;

**3** for the ear tests words  
as the palate tastes food.

**4** Let us choose for ourselves what is right;  
let us know among ourselves what is good.

**5** For Job has said, "I am in the right,  
and God has taken away my right;

**6** in spite of my right I am counted a liar;  
my wound is incurable, though I am without transgression."

**7** What man is like Job,  
who drinks up scorn like water,

**8** who goes in company with those who do iniquity  
and walks with wicked men?

**9** For he has said, "It profits a man nothing  
to take delight in God."

## Far Be It from God to Do Wickedness

**10** Therefore hear me, you men of understanding:  
far be it from God to do wickedness,  
and from the Almighty to do wrong.

**11** For He repays a man for his work,  
and makes it come on each according to his ways.

**12** Truly, God will not act wickedly,  
and the Almighty will not pervert justice.

**13** Who gave Him charge over the earth,  
and who set Him over the whole world?

**14** If He should set His heart on it,  
and gather to Himself His Spirit and His breath,

**15** all flesh would perish together,  
and man would return to dust.

**16** If you have understanding, hear this;  
listen to the sound of my words.

**17** Shall one who hates justice govern?  
Will you condemn Him who is righteous and mighty,

**18** who says to a king, "Worthless one,"  
and to nobles, "Wicked man,"

**19** who shows no partiality to princes  
and does not favor the rich over the poor,  
for they are all the work of His hands?

**20** In a moment they die;  
at midnight the people are shaken and pass away,  
and the mighty are taken away by no human hand.

**21** For His eyes are on the ways of a man,  
and He sees all his steps.

**22** There is no darkness or deep shadow  
where those who do iniquity may hide.

**23** For He does not need to set a man a time  
to come before God in judgment.

**24** He shatters the mighty without inquiry  
and sets others in their place.

**25** Thus, knowing their works,  
He overturns them in the night, and they are crushed.

**26** He strikes them down as wicked men  
in the place where all can see,

**27** because they turned aside from following Him  
and paid no heed to any of His ways,

**28** so that they brought the cry of the poor before Him,  
and He hears the cry of the afflicted.

**29** When He keeps quiet, who can condemn Him?  
When He hides His face, who can see Him,  
whether a nation or a man,

**30** so that the godless may not reign,  
and there be none to ensnare the people?

## Job Speaks Without Knowledge

**31** For has anyone said to God,  
"I have borne my punishment; I will not offend again;

**32** teach me what I cannot see;  
if I have done iniquity, I will do it no more"?

**33** Shall He repay on your terms, because you reject it?  
For you must choose, and not I;  
so tell what you know.

**34** Men of understanding will say to me,  
and the wise man who hears me:

**35** "Job speaks without knowledge;  
his words are without insight."

**36** Would that Job were tested to the end,  
because he answers like men of iniquity.

**37** For he adds rebellion to his sin;  
he claps his hands among us  
and multiplies his words against God.

## Notes

- **v2 "you wise men"** — Elihu speaks to the friends and to any wise hearers; he turns to Job only in v33.
- **v3 "the ear tests words as the palate tastes food"** — Job's own words of 12:11, turned on him.
- **vv5–6** — Elihu sums up Job's claims: *I am in the right* (13:18); *God has taken away my right* (27:2).
- **v9 "It profits a man nothing to take delight in God"** — Job never says this in so many words; it is Elihu's reading of 9:22 and 21:15.
- **v10 "far be it from God to do wickedness"** — *Shall not the Judge of all the earth do what is just?* (Genesis 18:25).
- **vv14–15 "His Spirit and His breath… all flesh would perish"** — the life of every creature depends on God's breath; *when You take away their breath, they die and return to their dust* (Psalm 104:29; Genesis 6:3).
- **vv18–19 "who says to a king, 'Worthless one'"** — God judges kings and princes, the rich and the poor alike (Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34).
- **v23 "He does not need to set a man a time"** — the answer to Job's demand for a day in court (23:3–7; 24:1): God needs no hearing to know.
- **vv29–33** — among the hardest lines in the book; the words are clear but how they hang together is not, and every translation reads them differently.
- **v36 "Would that Job were tested to the end"** — *avi*, which may be **my father**, as in the Latin, *Pater mi*; read here as a wish, **would that**.

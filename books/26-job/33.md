# Job 33

## I Too Was Formed from Clay

**1** But now, Job, hear my speech,  
and listen to all my words.

**2** I have opened my mouth;  
my tongue speaks in my mouth.

**3** My words come from the uprightness of my heart,  
and what my lips know they speak sincerely.

**4** The Spirit of God has made me,  
and the breath of the Almighty gives me life.

**5** Answer me, if you can;  
set your case in order before me; take your stand.

**6** I am as you are before God;  
I too was pinched off from clay.

**7** No dread of me need terrify you;  
my pressure will not be heavy on you.

## You Have Said in My Hearing

**8** Surely you have spoken in my hearing,  
and I have heard the sound of your words:

**9** "I am pure, without transgression;  
I am clean, and there is no iniquity in me.

**10** He finds occasions against me;  
He counts me as His enemy;

**11** He puts my feet in the stocks  
and watches all my paths."

**12** In this you are not right, I answer you,  
for God is greater than man.

**13** Why do you contend against Him,  
saying He answers none of man's words?

## How God Speaks

**14** For God speaks in one way,  
and in two, though man does not perceive it.

**15** In a dream, in a vision of the night,  
when deep sleep falls on men,  
while they slumber on their beds,

**16** then He opens the ears of men  
and seals their instruction,

**17** to turn man aside from his deed  
and to cover pride from a man;

**18** He keeps back his soul from the pit,  
his life from crossing over the river of death.

**19** Man is also rebuked with pain on his bed,  
with constant pain in all his bones,

**20** so that his life finds bread repulsive,  
and his soul the food he craved.

**21** His flesh wastes away out of sight,  
and his bones that were not seen stick out.

**22** His soul draws near the pit,  
and his life to those who bring death.

**23** If there is for him a messenger,  
an advocate, one among a thousand,  
to declare to man what is right for him,

**24** and he is gracious to him, and says,  
"Spare him from going down into the pit;  
I have found a ransom;

**25** let his flesh become fresher than a child's;  
let him return to the days of his youth,"

**26** then he prays to God, and He accepts him;  
he sees His face with a shout of joy,  
and He restores to man his righteousness.

**27** He sings before men and says,  
"I sinned and perverted what was right,  
and it was not repaid to me."

**28** He has redeemed his soul from going down into the pit,  
and his life will look on the light.

**29** All these things God does,  
twice, three times, with a man,

**30** to bring back his soul from the pit,  
so that he may be lit with the light of life.

**31** Pay attention, Job; listen to me;  
be silent, and I will speak.

**32** If you have words, answer me;  
speak, for I desire to declare you in the right.

**33** If not, listen to me;  
be silent, and I will teach you wisdom.

## Notes

- **v4 "The Spirit of God has made me"** — *ruach El*, **the Spirit**, capitalized as the word table has it; with **the breath of the Almighty**, the words of 32:8.
- **v6 "pinched off from clay"** — *qoratsti*, the potter's pinch of clay; Elihu is a creature like Job, and Job need not fear him as he feared God (9:34; 13:21).
- **vv9–11** — Elihu quotes Job closely: *I am blameless* (9:21); *count me as Your enemy* (13:24); *You put my feet in the stocks and watch all my paths* (13:27).
- **v14 "in one way, and in two"** — God does speak, in more ways than one; the first is the dream (vv15–18), the second is sickness (vv19–22).
- **v15 "when deep sleep falls on men"** — the words of Eliphaz's night vision, 4:13.
- **v18 "crossing over the river of death"** — *shelach*, which may be a **weapon** or a **channel**; read here as the stream the dead cross.
- **v19 "with constant pain in all his bones"** — the read form, *rov*, **all**, followed here; the written form is *riv*, **strife**: *continual strife in his bones*.
- **v23 "a messenger, an advocate, one among a thousand"** — *mal'akh*, **messenger**, as the word table has it; here a heavenly one. *Melits* is the **advocate** or **interpreter** of 16:20. Elihu answers Job's longing for an arbiter (9:33) and a witness in heaven (16:19).
- **v24 "I have found a ransom"** — *kofer*, the price paid to release a life (Exodus 21:30; Psalm 49:7–8).
- **v28 "He has redeemed his soul… his life"** — the read form has **his**, followed here; the written form has **my soul… my life**, continuing the man's song of v27.

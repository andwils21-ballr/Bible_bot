# Job 40

**1** The LORD answered Job and said:

**2** Shall one who finds fault contend with the Almighty?  
He who argues with God, let him answer it.

## Job Answers

**3** Then Job answered the LORD and said:

**4** I am of small account; what shall I answer You?  
I lay my hand on my mouth.

**5** I have spoken once, and I will not answer;  
twice, and I will go no further.

## Will You Put Me in the Wrong?

**6** Then the LORD answered Job out of the storm and said:

**7** Gird up your loins like a man;  
I will question you, and you make it known to Me.

**8** Will you even put My justice in the wrong?  
Will you condemn Me, so that you may be in the right?

**9** Do you have an arm like God's,  
and can you thunder with a voice like His?

**10** Adorn yourself with majesty and dignity;  
clothe yourself with glory and splendor.

**11** Pour out the overflowing of your anger,  
and look on everyone who is proud, and bring him low.

**12** Look on everyone who is proud, and humble him,  
and tread down the wicked where they stand.

**13** Hide them all in the dust together;  
bind their faces in the hidden place.

**14** Then I too will praise you,  
that your own right hand can save you.

## Behemoth

**15** Here is Behemoth, which I made as I made you;  
he eats grass like an ox.

**16** His strength is in his loins,  
and his power in the muscles of his belly.

**17** He makes his tail stiff like a cedar;  
the sinews of his thighs are knit together.

**18** His bones are tubes of bronze,  
his limbs like bars of iron.

**19** He is the first of the ways of God;  
let his Maker bring near His sword.

**20** For the mountains bring him their produce,  
where all the beasts of the field play.

**21** Under the lotus plants he lies,  
in the shelter of the reeds and the marsh.

**22** The lotus plants cover him with their shade;  
the willows of the brook surround him.

**23** If the river rages, he is not alarmed;  
he is confident though the Jordan rushes against his mouth.

**24** Can anyone take him by his eyes,  
or pierce his nose with a snare?

## Notes

- **v24 and the Hebrew numbering** — this chapter follows the English verses 1–24. The Hebrew continues the chapter with eight more verses (40:25–32), which the English numbers 41:1–8, the opening of the speech on Leviathan.
- **v2 "Shall one who finds fault contend with the Almighty?"** — God takes up Job's own word, *riv*, **contend**, **bring a case** (9:3; 13:8; 23:6).
- **vv4–5 "I lay my hand on my mouth… once… twice"** — Job does not take back his case; he stops speaking. **Once… twice** is the counting of 33:14.
- **v8 "Will you even put My justice in the wrong?"** — the heart of God's second speech: Job has defended his own righteousness by condemning God's (32:2; 34:5).
- **vv10–14** — God invites Job to put on the royal robes and judge the proud of the earth; if he can, God will praise him.
- **v15 "Behemoth"** — the plural of *behemah*, **beast**, **cattle**, used as the name of one great beast: **the beast of beasts**. It has been taken as the hippopotamus, or as a creature of chaos like Leviathan; in 1 Enoch 60:7–8 Behemoth is the male monster of the land.
- **v15 "which I made as I made you"** — *asher asiti immakh*, **which I made with you**: Behemoth is a fellow creature of Job's.
- **v19 "the first of the ways of God"** — *reshit darkhei El*, the words Wisdom uses of herself, *The LORD possessed me at the beginning of His way* (Proverbs 8:22).
- **v21 "the lotus plants"** — *tse'elim*, a thorny shrub of the river banks, found only here and in v22.
- **v23 "the Jordan"** — perhaps any rushing river; Behemoth is unmoved by the flood.

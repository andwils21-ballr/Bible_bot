# Job 21

**1** Job answered and said:

## Listen and Be Appalled

**2** Listen closely to my words,  
and let this be the comfort you give me.

**3** Bear with me, and I will speak,  
and after I have spoken, mock on.

**4** As for me, is my complaint against man?  
Why should my spirit not be short?

**5** Look at me and be appalled,  
and lay your hand over your mouth.

**6** When I remember it, I am dismayed,  
and shuddering seizes my flesh.

## The Prosperity of the Wicked

**7** Why do the wicked live,  
grow old and become mighty in power?

**8** Their offspring are established in their presence,  
and their descendants before their eyes.

**9** Their houses are safe from fear,  
and no rod of God is on them.

**10** Their bull breeds and does not fail;  
their cow calves and does not miscarry.

**11** They send out their little ones like a flock,  
and their children dance.

**12** They sing to the tambourine and the lyre  
and rejoice at the sound of the pipe.

**13** They spend their days in prosperity,  
and in a moment they go down to Sheol.

**14** They say to God, "Depart from us!  
We do not desire the knowledge of Your ways.

**15** What is the Almighty, that we should serve Him?  
What profit do we get if we pray to Him?"

**16** Their prosperity is not in their own hand;  
the counsel of the wicked is far from me.

**17** How often is the lamp of the wicked put out?  
How often does their disaster come on them,  
or does He deal out pains in His anger?

**18** How often are they like straw before the wind,  
like chaff that the storm carries away?

**19** You say, "God stores up a man's iniquity for his children."  
Let Him repay it to the man himself, so that he knows it.

**20** Let his own eyes see his ruin,  
and let him drink of the wrath of the Almighty.

**21** For what does he care for his house after him,  
when the number of his months is cut off?

## One Dies, Another Dies

**22** Will anyone teach God knowledge,  
when He judges those on high?

**23** One dies in his full strength,  
wholly at ease and secure,

**24** his pails full of milk  
and the marrow of his bones moist.

**25** Another dies in bitterness of soul,  
never having tasted of good.

**26** They lie down alike in the dust,  
and the worms cover them.

**27** I know your thoughts,  
and the schemes with which you would wrong me.

**28** For you say, "Where is the house of the prince?  
Where is the tent in which the wicked lived?"

**29** Have you not asked those who travel the roads,  
and do you not accept their signs,

**30** that the evil man is spared in the day of disaster,  
that he is led away in the day of wrath?

**31** Who declares his way to his face,  
and who repays him for what he has done?

**32** When he is carried to the grave,  
watch is kept over his tomb.

**33** The clods of the valley are sweet to him;  
all mankind follows after him,  
and those who went before him are without number.

**34** How then will you comfort me with empty breath?  
Nothing is left of your answers but faithlessness.

## Notes

- **v4 "Why should my spirit not be short?"** — *lo tiqtsar ruchi*, the Hebrew for impatience. Job's complaint is against God, not man, and so his patience has run out.
- **v7 "Why do the wicked live?"** — Job answers Zophar's teaching of 20:5, *the exulting of the wicked is short*; *Why does the way of the wicked prosper?* (Jeremiah 12:1; Psalm 73:3–12).
- **vv14–15 "Depart from us… What is the Almighty, that we should serve Him?"** — the words of the wicked, quoted by Eliphaz in 22:17.
- **v16 "the counsel of the wicked is far from me"** — Job distances himself from the wicked even while describing their success. Eliphaz repeats the line word for word in 22:18.
- **vv17–18 "How often…?"** — Job's challenge to Bildad's *the light of the wicked is put out* (18:5): it does not happen often. **Like chaff that the storm carries away** is the picture of Psalm 1:4.
- **vv19–21** — the Hebrew moves to the singular, *a man… his children*: it is not justice if a man's sons pay for his sins after he is dead and cannot know it (Jeremiah 31:29–30; Ezekiel 18:2–4).
- **v24 "his pails full of milk"** — *atinav*, a word found only here; it may mean his **body** or **loins**, **full of fat**.
- **vv23–26** — *the same event happens to the righteous and the wicked* (Ecclesiastes 9:2–3).
- **v29 "those who travel the roads"** — men who have seen the world, and can tell that the wicked are not punished as the friends say.
- **v34 "empty breath… faithlessness"** — *hevel* and *ma'al*, the word for unfaithfulness to God (Leviticus 5:15; Joshua 7:1): the friends' answers are not only empty but false.

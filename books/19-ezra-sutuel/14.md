# Ezra Sutuel 14

## The Voice from the Tree

**1** On the third day, while I was sitting under a tree,

**2** a voice came from opposite that tree and said, "Ezra, Ezra." I said, "Here I am," and I rose and stood up.

**3** He said to me, "I revealed Myself plainly to Moses, and spoke with him at the bush, when My people were slaves in Egypt.

**4** I sent him, and brought My people out of Egypt, and led them to Mount Sinai, and kept them with Me many days.

**5** I told them many wonders, and made known to them the secret of the times, and made known to them the end of the age.

**6** I commanded him, saying, 'These words you are to make known, and these you are to hide.'

**7** Now I say to you:

**8** keep in your heart the signs I have told you, the dream you have seen, and the meaning you have heard.

**9** For you will be taken from among men, and from then on you will live with My Son, where those like you are, until the world comes to its end.

**10** For the world has passed its youth, and its days have grown old.

**11** The world has been divided into ten parts, and it has reached the tenth,

**12** and half of the tenth remains.

**13** Now set your house in order, comfort those who grieve, teach their wise, and shake off now the life that dies.

**14** Put away mortal thoughts, cast from you the burdens of human life, and put on what does not die; put away the thoughts of grief, and hasten to cross over from this world.

**15** For the evil you have seen has come now, and an evil worse than this will come again.

**16** For as the world grows old and weak, so the evils of those who live in it increase.

**17** For truth will perish and falsehood will rise; for the eagle you saw is hurrying to come."

## The Lamp of Understanding

**18** I answered Him, "Let me speak before You.

**19** I will go, Lord, as You have commanded me, and teach the people who are alive now. But who will teach those who are born after?

**20** For the world lies in darkness, and those who live in it have no light.

**21** For Your law has been burned, and no one knows what You have done, or what You are about to do.

**22** If I have found favor before You, send the Holy Spirit upon me, and I will write all that has happened in the world from its beginning, and all that is written in Your law, so that people may find the way of life, and those who wish to live may live."

**23** He answered me, "Go, gather the people, and tell them not to look for you for forty days.

**24** Prepare many tablets for yourself, and take with you Sarea, Dabria, Selemia, Ecanus and Asiel, these five men, for they are skilled in writing.

**25** Then come here, and I will light the lamp of wisdom in your heart, and it will not go out until you have finished all you are to write.

**26** When you have finished, some things you are to make public, and some you are to hide and give to the wise. Tomorrow at this hour you will begin to write."

## Ezra Speaks to the People

**27** I went as He had commanded me, and gathered all the people,

**28** and said to them, "Hear these words, Israel.

**29** Your fathers lived at first in the land of Egypt, and He redeemed them from there.

**30** They received the law of life and did not keep it, and you who came after them have denied it.

**31** He gave us a land, and made us inherit the land of Zion; but you and your fathers sinned, and did not keep the ways of the Most High that He commanded you.

**32** Because He is a righteous Judge, He took from you in His time what He had given you.

**33** Now you are here, and your brothers are farther in exile than you.

**34** If you rule your hearts, discipline your thoughts and guard your lives, you will not die.

**35** For after death the judgment comes, and it will not give us life. On that day the names of the righteous will appear, and the deeds of sinners will be revealed.

**36** Let none of you come to me, or look for me, for forty days."

## The Cup and the Books

**37** I took the five men, as He had commanded me, and we went into the field and stayed there.

**38** The next day a voice came and called me, and said, "Ezra, open your mouth and drink what I give you to drink."

**39** I opened my mouth, and He handed me a cup full of water, and its color was like fire.

**40** I took it and drank, and my heart poured out wisdom, understanding filled my breast, and my spirit held fast and remembered.

**41** My mouth was opened, and it was not shut again.

**42** The Most High gave wisdom to the five men, and they wrote it all down in order, in signs they did not know. They stayed there forty days. They wrote by day,

**43** and in the evening ate bread; but I spoke by day, and was not silent by night.

**44** In those forty days ninety-four books were written.

**45** When the forty days were completed, the Most High spoke to me and said, "What you wrote first, make public, and let all read it, the worthy and the unworthy;

**46** but keep these, to give to the wise of the people.

**47** For in them is the lamp of light, the spring of wisdom, and knowledge like a river."

**48** I did so in the fourth year of the week of years, in the five thousand and ninety-second year after the creation of the world, on the tenth night of the third month. On that day Ezra was taken up, and carried to the land of those who are like him, after he had written all this. He was called the scribe of the wisdom of the Most High, to whom be glory forever and ever.

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "Ezra, Ezra." I said, "Here I am"… at the bush** — the call of Moses: *God called to him from the middle of the bush and said, "Moses, Moses." He said, "Here I am"* (Exodus 3:4). The voice that gave Moses the law now calls the man who will write it again (vv21–22).
- **vv11–12 "ten parts… half of the tenth remains"** — so the Ethiopic. The Latin has *twelve parts*, of which *ten parts… have already passed, as well as half of the tenth*, so that two and a half remain.
- **v14 "put on what does not die"** — so the Ethiopic, with Paul's image: *this mortal body must put on immortality* (1 Corinthians 15:53). The Latin and the Syriac turn it the other way: *take off now your weak nature*.
- **v35 "it will not give us life"** — so the Ethiopic: for those who do not rule their hearts (v34), the judgment after death brings no life. The Latin and the Syriac read *For judgment comes after death, when we will live again*.
- **vv45–46 "What you wrote first, make public… but keep these"** — the Syriac counts them: *The twenty-four books that you have written first, place openly… but keep these seventy*. The Latin has *the seventy* as well, and the Ethiopic gives no count; with the ninety-four of v44, seventy are kept back.
- **v48 "On that day Ezra was taken up"** — the book's last verse is the Ethiopic's alone; the Latin and the Syriac end at v47. What the voice promised in v9, *you will be taken from among men*, is done, as it was for Enoch: *He was not, for God took him* (Genesis 5:24).

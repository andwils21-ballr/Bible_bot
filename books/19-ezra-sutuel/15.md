## Notes

- **The chapter** — Ezra Sutuel is the book other Bibles number 2 Esdras 3–14 (4 Ezra). Chapters 15–16 of 2 Esdras are an addition found in the Latin (called 6 Ezra); the Ethiopic book does not have them, and the Syriac also ends at chapter 14. The Ethiopic book ends at 14:48, *On that day Ezra was taken up*.

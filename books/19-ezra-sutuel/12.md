# Ezra Sutuel 12

## The End of the Eagle

**1** Then, while the Merciful One was speaking these words to the eagle,

**2** the head that remained perished. The wings that had gone over to it rose up and stood to rule, and their talons were restless.

**3** Then they too perished, and the whole body was burned, and the earth was greatly astonished. I too was astonished at all this searching, and I woke in great fear.

**4** I said to my soul, "You have brought all this on me, by searching out the ways of the Most High.

**5** My soul is weary and my spirit very sick; there is no strength in me, none at all, because of the great fear that shook me this night.

**6** Now I will pray to the Most High to strengthen me to the end."

**7** I said, "O Lord, my Lord, if I have found favor in Your sight, if I am greatly blessed before You, and if my prayer has gone up before Your face,

**8** save me and strengthen me, and tell Your servant the meaning of this fearful dream, so that my soul may rejoice fully.

**9** For You have made me blessed by showing me what comes in the last days, and the end of the days of the world."

## The Fourth Kingdom

**10** He said to me, "This is the meaning of the dream you saw.

**11** The eagle coming up from the sea is the fourth kingdom, which appeared in a dream to your brother Daniel;

**12** but I did not explain it to him as I now explain it to you.

**13** The days are coming when a kingdom will arise on the earth that will be more fearsome than all the kingdoms before it.

**14** Twelve kings will reign in it.

**15** The second to reign will hold power longer than any of the twelve.

**16** This is the meaning of the twelve wings you saw.

**17** As for the voice you heard speaking, which came not from its heads but from the middle of its body,

**18** this is its meaning: in the middle of that kingdom's body great turmoil will arise, and it will be in danger of falling; but it will not fall then, and it will stand and keep its rule.

**19** As for the heads you saw coming out of its wings,

**20** this is its meaning: eight kings will arise in it, whose years will be evil and whose days short; and two of them will quickly perish in the middle of their time.

**21** Four will be kept until the time of its end draws near, and two will be kept longer still.

**22** As for the three heads you saw that were silent,

**23** this is its meaning: in the last days the Most High will raise up three kings, and they will renew many things in it, and afflict the earth

**24** and those who live on it with great fear, more than all who were before them. That is why they are called the heads of the eagle,

**25** for these heads of the kingdom are its end.

**26** As for the one head you saw perish, the greatest, this is its meaning: one of them alone will die on his bed, yet in torment.

**27** The two that remained will die by the spear;

**28** and the last will die by the spear too.

**29** As for the two heads you saw that went over to the head on the right side,

**30** this is its meaning: these are the ones the Most High has kept for the last, through whom the end will begin; and there will be great turmoil, as you saw.

**31** As for the lion you saw rising out of the field, roaring and speaking to the eagle and rebuking it for its sins, all the words you heard,

**32** this is the one the Most High has kept for the last days, from the seed of David. He is the one who will go out and come, and tell them their sins, and rebuke them for their wickedness, and set their recompense before them.

**33** First He will set them alive before the judgment, and then, when He has rebuked them, He will destroy them.

**34** Those of the people who remain He will redeem in mercy, those who have been saved within My borders, and He will make them glad until the day of judgment comes, of which I spoke to you at the beginning.

**35** This is the dream you saw, and this is its meaning.

**36** To you alone the Most High has given to know this mystery.

**37** But write all you have seen in a book, and put it in a hidden place;

**38** and teach it to the wise of the people, those you know can keep this mystery in their hearts.

**39** But you, stay here seven more days, so that you may see what the Most High wishes to show you."

## The People Come to the Field

**40** Then he went from me. When all the people heard that the seven days had passed and I had not come back into the city, they all gathered, from the least to the greatest, and came to me and said,

**41** "How have we sinned against you, and what wrong have we done you, that you should forsake us and sit in this place?

**42** For of all the prophets you alone are left to us, like one cluster out of all the vintage, like a lamp in a dark place, like a harbor for a ship saved from the deep.

**43** Or are the evils that have come upon us not enough?

**44** If you forsake us too, it would have been better for us to have stayed in the burning of Zion.

**45** For we are no better than those who died there." They wept with a loud voice.

**46** I answered them, "Take heart and do not grieve, house of Jacob.

**47** For our remembrance is before the Most High, and He who has not forgotten us is mighty.

**48** As for me, I will not forsake you or go far from you. I came to this place to pray over the ruin of Zion, and to seek mercy for the grief that has come on our joy.

**49** Now go, all of you, to your homes, and I will come to you after these days."

**50** The people went to their homes, as I had told them.

**51** But I stayed in that place, in the field, seven days, as he had commanded me. In those days I fed only on the fruit of the field, and ate the plants of the field.

## Notes

- **v1 "while the Merciful One was speaking these words to the eagle"** — so the Ethiopic; the Latin and the Syriac have *while the lion was speaking*. The lion spoke the words of the Most High (11:38), and in v32 He is the one *from the seed of David*.
- **v11 "the fourth kingdom, which appeared in a dream to your brother Daniel"** — Daniel's night vision: *Four great beasts came up out of the sea*, and the fourth was *terrifying and dreadful* (Daniel 7:3, 7); *The fourth beast shall be a fourth kingdom on earth* (Daniel 7:23). Here the beast from the sea is an eagle.
- **vv31–32 "the lion… from the seed of David"** — Jacob's blessing, *A lion's cub is Judah* (Genesis 49:9), and David was of Judah. The Ethiopic names him by his line; the Latin and the Syriac call him *the Messiah*, the Anointed, *whom the Most High has kept for the end of days*.
- **v48 "the grief that has come on our joy"** — so the Ethiopic; the Latin and the Syriac have *the humiliation of our sanctuary*. Ezekiel calls the sanctuary *their joy and glory* (Ezekiel 24:21, 25).

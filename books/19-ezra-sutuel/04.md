# Ezra Sutuel 4

## Uriel's Three Questions

**1** Then the angel who had been sent to me, whose name was Uriel, answered me

**2** and said to me, "Your soul is utterly bewildered. Do you think you can find out the counsel of the Most High?"

**3** I said, "Yes, my lord." He answered me, "I have been sent to show you three ways and to set three likenesses before you.

**4** If you can explain one of them to me, I too will show you the way you long to see, and teach you why this evil exists."

**5** I said, "Speak, my lord." He said to me, "Go, weigh the fire in a balance, measure the wind with a measure, or call back for me the day that has passed."

**6** I answered, "Who born of a woman can do this, that you should ask it of me?"

**7** He said to me, "If I had asked you, 'How many dwellings are in the heart of the sea, or how many springs at the face of the deep, or how many ways above the heavens? Which is the way to Sheol, or which the way to paradise?'

**8** you would surely have said to me, 'I have never gone down into the deep, I have never gone down into Sheol, and I have never gone up into heaven.'

**9** But now I have asked you only about the fire and the wind and the day that has passed, and you have no answer to give me about them."

**10** He said to me, "If you cannot understand what passes away,

**11** how can you understand the ways of the Most High? The ways of the Most High are formed beyond all reckoning, and you who are corruptible cannot understand the ways of Him who is incorruptible." When I heard this, I fell on my face

**12** and said, "It would have been better for us never to have been created than, once created, to live in sin and suffering and not know why we suffer."

## The Forest and the Sea

**13** He answered me, "The forest and the trees of the plain went and took counsel together,

**14** saying, 'Come, let us go and make war on the sea, so that it may give way before us, and we may make ourselves another forest.'

**15** In the same way the waves of the sea took counsel together and said, 'Come, let us go up and make war on the forest of the plain, so that there too we may make ourselves another country.'

**16** But the counsel of the forest came to nothing, for a fire came and consumed it.

**17** So did the counsel of the waves of the sea, for the sand rose up and stopped them.

**18** If you are wise in judgment, tell me: which of them would you acquit, and which would you condemn?"

**19** I answered, "Each of them took a foolish counsel, for the land was given to the forest, and the sea a place to bear its waves."

**20** He answered me, "You have judged rightly. Why then have you not judged so in your own case?

**21** For as the land was given to the forest and the sea to its waves, so those who live on the earth can understand only what is on the earth, and not Him who is in heaven, nor what is in heaven."

**22** Then I answered, "I beg you, my lord, why then were we given a mind to understand?

**23** I did not want to ask about the ways above, but about what passes before us every day: why Israel has been given over to the nations, the people He loved to a sinful people; why the law of our fathers has been brought to nothing, and the written covenant is nowhere.

**24** We pass away from the world like locusts, our life is like smoke, and we are not worthy to obtain mercy.

**25** What then will become of His holy name, by which we are called? These are the things I have asked."

## The Harvest Has Not Come

**26** He answered me, "If you remain, you will see, and if you live, you will know the times; for the world is hastening to pass away.

**27** It cannot bear what is hoped for the righteous, for it is a world full of sorrow and sickness.

**28** The evil you asked me about has been sown, but its harvest has not yet come.

**29** Unless what was sown is reaped, and the place where the evil was sown passes away, the field where the good is sown will not come.

**30** For a grain of evil seed was sown in the heart of Adam from the beginning, and the fruit of sin has grown from it and increased until now, and will grow until its harvest comes.

**31** Consider for yourself: if one grain of evil seed has brought so great a crop of sin,

**32** when good seed is sown beyond counting, how great a harvest it will yield!"

**33** I answered him, "How long, and when will this be? For our days are few and evil."

**34** He answered me, "Do not be in more haste than the Most High. You are in haste for your own sake alone, but the Most High for the sake of many.

**35** Did not the souls of the righteous ask about this in their chambers, saying, 'How long are we to be here? When will the harvest of our reward come?'

**36** Jeremiel the angel answered them, 'When the number of those like you is complete. For He has weighed the world in the balance,

**37** and measured the sea with a measure; and He will neither command silence nor speak until the measure set for it is fulfilled.'"

**38** I answered, "O Lord, my Lord, we are all full of sin.

**39** Perhaps it is because of us that the harvest of the righteous is held back, because of the sins of those who live in the world."

## The Womb and the Earth

**40** He answered me, "Go and ask a pregnant woman whether, when she has completed her nine months, her womb can keep what is in it any longer."

**41** I said, "It cannot, my lord." He said to me, "Sheol and the dwellings of the spirits of the righteous are like the womb.

**42** For as the womb hastens to give birth in its pain, so the earth hastens to give back from its womb those who have been in it since the creation of the world.

**43** Then all that you long to see will be shown to you."

**44** I answered, "If I have found favor in your sight, and if it is possible,

**45** tell me whether the days still to come in this world are more than those that have passed, or whether more have passed;

**46** for what has passed I know, but what is to come I do not know."

**47** He said to me, "Stand at the right side, and I will show you the meaning of a likeness."

**48** I stood and looked, and a blazing furnace passed before me; and when the flame had gone by, I looked, and smoke remained.

**49** After this a cloud full of water passed before me and poured down a heavy and violent rain; and when the force of the rain had passed, drops remained.

**50** He said to me, "Consider for yourself: as the rain is more than the drops, and the fire more than the smoke, so the measure of what has passed is far greater; what is left is drops and smoke."

**51** Then I pleaded with him, "Will I live until those days? What will happen in those days?"

**52** He answered me, "About what you ask me, I can tell you in part; but about your life I was not sent to tell you, and I do not know it."

## Notes

- **v1 "the angel… whose name was Uriel"** — the angel 1 Enoch names first among *the holy angels who watch: Uriel, one of the holy angels, who is over the world and over Tartarus* (1 Enoch 20:1–2), the angel of its book of the heavenly lights. Here he answers Ezra's questions about the ways of the Most High.
- **vv29, 37, 41, the witnesses** — Laurence's manuscript and Dillmann's edition break off in v29 before *the field where the good is sown will not come*, which the Latin and the Syriac have; it is kept. In v41 *Sheol*, in Dillmann's edition, the Latin and the Syriac, is kept, though Laurence's manuscript lacks it. In v37 the Ethiopic has *measured the sea*, where the Latin and the Syriac have *measured the times*.
- **v33 "our days are few and evil"** — Jacob's words to Pharaoh, *Few and evil have been the days of the years of my life* (Genesis 47:9).
- **vv41–42 "the earth hastens to give back from its womb those who have been in it"** — the resurrection pictured as birth. 1 Enoch says the same of the last day: *the earth will give back what was entrusted to it, and Sheol will give back what was entrusted to it* (1 Enoch 51:1).

# Ezra Sutuel 6

## Before the World Was Made

**1** He said to me, "Its beginning was through the Son of Man, and its end will be by Me alone. For before the earth and its regions were made, before the ways of the world stood firm, before the winds blew,

**2** before the sound of the thunder was heard, before the flash of the lightning shone, before the foundation of paradise was laid,

**3** before the fair flowers were seen, before the powers of movement were established, before the hosts of angels were numbered,

**4** before the heights of the air were raised up, before the measures of the heavens were named,

**5** before the trace of the years to come was known, before the schemes of those who now sin were set aside, and before those who store up faith were sealed,

**6** then I took thought, and it was by Me alone, and not by another."

**7** I answered, "What will be the sign of its time? When will the first age end, and when will the other world begin?"

**8** He said to me, "From Abraham to Isaac, for from him were born Jacob and Esau.

**9** Esau is the end of this age, and Jacob is the beginning of the world that follows.

**10** For the end of a man is his heel, and his beginning is his hand. Seek nothing further, Ezra."

## The Voice Like Many Waters

**11** I answered, "O Lord, my Lord, if I have found favor in Your sight,

**12** show Your servant the end of Your signs, part of which You showed me last night."

**13** He answered me, "Rise and stand on your feet, and you will hear a mighty, sounding voice.

**14** If the place where you stand shakes violently

**15** while it speaks with you, do not be terrified, for the word concerns the end. The foundations of the earth will understand

**16** that it speaks of them, and they will tremble and shake, for they know that their end must be changed."

**17** When I heard this, I rose and stood on my feet and listened, and a voice spoke, and its sound was like the sound of many waters.

**18** It said, "The days are coming when I will draw near to visit all who live on the earth,

**19** when I will search out those who have done wrong by their wickedness, and when the humiliation of Zion is complete,

**20** and when the age that is passing away is sealed. This is the sign I will give: the books will be opened before the face of heaven, and all will see Me together.

**21** Children a year old will speak with their voices, and pregnant women will give birth to infants of three and four months, and they will live and be raised up.

**22** Land that seemed unsown will appear sown, and full storehouses will be found empty.

**23** The trumpet will sound, and all who hear it will suddenly be terrified.

**24** In those days friends will fight against friends like enemies; the earth and those who live on it will be terrified, and the springs of water will stand still and not flow for three hours.

**25** Whoever is left of all those I have told you of will live, and will see My salvation and the end of My world.

**26** On that day they will see the men who were taken up and have not tasted death since their birth. The heart of those who live in the world will be changed, and they will be given another heart.

**27** For evil will be blotted out, and deceit will be extinguished.

**28** Faith will flourish, corruption will be overcome, and the truth, which has gone so long without fruit, will be made known."

**29** While he was speaking to me, little by little the place where I stood began to shake.

**30** He said to me, "I have come to show you these things, as on the night before.

**31** If you pray again and fast again seven days,

**32** I will tell you again greater things than these; for your voice has been heard before the Most High, and He has seen the uprightness you have kept from your youth.

**33** That is why He sent me to show you all these things, and to say to you, 'Take courage, and do not be afraid,

**34** and do not hurry to dwell on vain thoughts about the former times, that you were not born in the last days.'"

## The Six Days

**35** After this I wept again in the same way and fasted seven days, to complete the three weeks he had told me of.

**36** That night my heart was troubled within me again, and I began once more to speak before the Most High,

**37** for my spirit burned within me, and my soul was in distress.

**38** I said, "O Lord, my Lord, You spoke at the beginning of creation, on the first day, and said, 'Let heaven and earth be made,' and Your word did the work.

**39** Your Spirit was hovering over all, and within, all was darkness and silence; the sound of a human voice was not yet.

**40** Then You commanded that light come out of Your treasuries, so that Your works might appear.

**41** On the second day You created the spirit of the firmament and commanded it to divide the waters, so that one part might go up and the other part remain below.

**42** On the third day You commanded the waters to be gathered into the seventh part of the earth, and six parts You left dry, so that in them people might plow and sow and live before You.

**43** Your word went out, and at once the work was done.

**44** Fruit came forth without number, of every taste and kind; flowers of many colors, trees of many sorts and many fragrant scents. This was done on the third day.

**45** On the fourth day You commanded the light of the sun and the light of the moon to be, and the stars in their order,

**46** and You commanded them to serve man, who was about to be formed.

**47** On the fifth day You told the seventh part, where the waters were gathered, to bring forth living creatures, birds and fish.

**48** The mute and lifeless water brought forth living creatures, so that the generations might tell of Your glory.

**49** Then You kept two living creatures that You had created; the one You named Behemoth, and the other You named Leviathan.

**50** You separated them from each other, for the seventh part, where the water was gathered, could not hold them both.

**51** To Behemoth You gave one of the parts dried up on the third day, to live in it, where the mountains are.

**52** To Leviathan You gave the seventh part, the watery one; and You have kept them to be food for whom You will.

**53** On the sixth day You commanded the earth to bring forth before You cattle, beasts and creeping things,

**54** and over all these You set Adam as ruler over all the works You had made before; and because of him we, Your people whom You chose, are overwhelmed.

**55** All this I have spoken before You, O Lord, because You said that You created the world for our sake.

**56** As for the other nations, born of Adam, You said that they are nothing; they are like froth, like a drop from a bucket, and their joy is nothing.

**57** And now, O Lord, these nations that are counted as nothing rule over us and trample us.

**58** But we, Your people, whom You called 'My firstborn, My only one, My beloved,' have been handed over into their hands.

**59** If the world was created for our sake, why do we not possess our world as our inheritance? How long will this go on?"

## Notes

- **v1 "Its beginning was through the Son of Man, and its end will be by Me alone"** — so the Ethiopic, as Laurence's English and the shape of Dillmann's edition give it, and the Syriac, *The beginning was through the Son of Man, but the end is through my own hands*. The Latin lacks the clause. It answers Ezra's question in 5:56, *through whom You will visit Your world*. Laurence's English reads *on account of the Son of man*. The title is Daniel's *one like a son of man* who came *with the clouds of heaven* (Daniel 7:13), and 1 Enoch's *Son of Man who has righteousness* (1 Enoch 46:3).
- **vv8–10 "Esau is the end of this age, and Jacob is the beginning… his heel… his hand"** — the twins' birth, *his hand holding the heel of Esau* (Genesis 25:26): Esau came out first, and Jacob followed with his hand on Esau's heel, so the age that ends and the age that begins touch. The Ethiopic says *From Abraham to Isaac*, the Latin and the Syriac *From Abraham to Abraham*.
- **vv49–52 "Behemoth… Leviathan"** — the two great beasts that close the book of Job (Job 40–41). 1 Enoch tells of the same separation: *a female monster named Leviathan, to live in the depths of the sea… and the male, named Behemoth*, in a wilderness *east of the garden* (1 Enoch 60:7–8).
- **v58 "whom You called 'My firstborn, My only one, My beloved'"** — the word Moses was to say to Pharaoh, *Israel is My son, My firstborn* (Exodus 4:22).

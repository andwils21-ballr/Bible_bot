## Notes

- **The chapter** — Ezra Sutuel is the book other Bibles number 2 Esdras 3–14 (4 Ezra). Chapters 1–2 of 2 Esdras are an addition found in the Latin (called 5 Ezra); the Ethiopic book does not have them, and the Syriac also begins at chapter 3. The Ethiopic book begins at chapter 3, *In the thirtieth year after the ruin of our city*.

# Ezra Sutuel 13

## The Man with the Clouds of Heaven

**1** After seven days I dreamed a dream by night.

**2** I saw a great wind coming up out of the sea, and all its waves were stirred.

**3** I saw that wind coming out of the sea in the likeness of a man, and the man flew with the clouds of heaven. Wherever he turned his face and looked, at once everything before him turned back;

**4** and all against whom his voice went out, all who heard his voice, melted, as a honeycomb melts when it comes near the fire.

**5** After this I saw a multitude of people beyond number gathering from the four winds of heaven to make war on the man who had come out of the sea.

**6** Then he built himself a great mountain, and flew up on it.

**7** I tried to find out where that mountain had been built from, and I could not.

**8** After this all those who had gathered against him to make war on him were greatly afraid, and yet they dared to fight him.

**9** When they rushed at him and came upon him, he did not lift his hand against them, or hold a spear, or any weapon of war;

**10** but from his mouth he sent out waves of fire, from his lips a flame of fire, and from his tongue coals of fire, like a storm. They were all mingled together, the waves of fire, the flame of fire and the coals of fire,

**11** and came down on the multitude that had rushed at him to kill him, and burned them all, until nothing was left of them but the dust of their ashes and the smoke of their burning. Then I awoke.

**12** After this I saw that man come down from the mountain and call to himself another multitude, a peaceable one.

**13** Many people came to him; some of them were glad, some were grieving, and some were bound. Then, because it was too much for me, I was troubled and awoke, and I prayed to the Most High and said,

**14** "From the first You have shown Your servant this glory of Yours, and granted that You hear my prayer.

**15** Now tell me also the meaning of this dream.

**16** As I consider it, woe to those who will be alive in those days, and still more to those who will not be!

**17** For they will grieve, not knowing what is laid up in the last days, which has not yet reached them.

**18** As for those who are alive then, they will know it already;

**19** and for this, woe to them, for they will see great anguish and much torment, as I have seen in this dream.

**20** Yet it is better to suffer and to come to this than to pass from the world like a cloud, and not know what will meet them at the last."

## The Meaning of the Man

**21** He answered me, "I will tell you the meaning of your dream, and explain to you what you have said.

**22** As for what you said about those who are left and are alive, this is its meaning:

**23** they are the ones who will see that suffering in those days, and He Himself will keep those whom that suffering overtakes, those who have good works and faith with the Most High and Mighty.

**24** Know, then, that the living are more blessed than the dead.

**25** This is the meaning of your dream. The man you saw coming out of the middle of the sea

**26** is the one the Most High has kept for many days, to redeem the world through Him; and He will set in order those who are left.

**27** As for what you saw coming out of His mouth, fire and flame and coals, like a storm,

**28** and that He lifted no spear and no weapon of war, and yet killed the multitude that rushed at Him and came to kill Him, this is its meaning:

**29** the days are coming when the Most High will redeem those who live on the earth.

**30** On that day they will be bewildered,

**31** and fight among themselves, city against city, country against country, people against people, and kingdom against kingdom.

**32** When this comes about, and the signs I showed you before have come, on that day the man you saw coming out of the sea will be revealed.

**33** When all the peoples hear His voice, they will leave, each in his own country, the wars they wage among themselves;

**34** and they will gather together, a multitude beyond number, as you saw, wishing to come and kill Him.

**35** But He will stand on the top of Mount Zion.

**36** Zion will come and be revealed to all, prepared and built, as you saw the mountain come out of itself and be made, not by human hands.

**37** He is the Son, who will rebuke the nations for their sins, those who were like the storm. He will lay out before them the evil of their deeds, and the great judgment with which they will be judged,

**38** those who were like the flame of fire; and He will destroy them with their sins, without toil, those who were like the coals of fire.

## The Peaceable Multitude

**39** As for what you saw, that a peaceable multitude gathered to Him,

**40** these are the nine tribes who were taken captive from their land in the days of Shalmaneser king of Assyria, whom he carried captive into his kingdom and settled across the river, and they were taken into another land.

**41** They took this counsel among themselves, to leave the multitude of their people and go into a land where no human race had ever lived,

**42** so that there they might keep their law, which they had not kept in their own land.

**43** They went in by the narrow entrance of the Euphrates.

**44** On that day the Most High did wonders for them, and stopped the springs of the river until they had crossed.

**45** That land is a journey of a year and a half, and the land is called Azaph.

**46** There they have lived until the last days. Then, when they are to return,

**47** the Most High will stop the springs of the river again, so that they can cross.

**48** That is why you saw the multitude of the people gathered and found within My blessed borders.

**49** When He destroys the many peoples who gathered against Him, on that day He will strengthen the people who remain.

**50** On that day He will show them His wonders."

**51** I said to him, "O Lord, my Lord, tell me this too: why did I see a man coming out of the middle of the sea?"

**52** He said to me, "As no one can know what is in the depths of the sea, so no one on the earth can know the Son, or those who are with Him, until His time and His day have come.

**53** This is the meaning of the dream you saw, and that is why I have shown it to you alone.

**54** For you have left your own concerns and followed Mine, and searched out My law;

**55** you have given your life to knowledge, and made wisdom your mother.

**56** That is why I have shown you this, for your reward is with the Most High. Come again after a few days, for there are other things I will tell you, and wonders I will explain to you."

**57** So I went out and passed through the field, praising and glorifying God for the glory He works in His times,

**58** and for how He orders His years, and what comes to pass in each year. I stayed there three days.

## Notes

- **v3 "the man flew with the clouds of heaven"** — Daniel's vision: *with the clouds of heaven there came one like a son of man* (Daniel 7:13). Here the figure comes up out of the sea, the place Daniel's four beasts came from (Daniel 7:3).
- **vv6–7, 36 "he built himself a great mountain… not by human hands"** — so the Ethiopic; the Latin and the Syriac say he *carved out* the mountain. The likeness is Daniel's: *a stone was cut out, not by hands*, and *became a great mountain and filled the whole earth* (Daniel 2:34–35). Here the mountain is Zion.
- **vv37, 52 "the Son"** — so the Ethiopic; the Latin and the Syriac have *my Son*, here and in v32, where the Ethiopic has *the man you saw*.
- **v40 "the nine tribes… in the days of Shalmaneser king of Assyria"** — the Syriac has *nine and a half tribes*, and the Latin and the Syriac date it *in the days of King Josiah*. Kings tells it of Hoshea, king of Israel: *Shalmaneser king of Assyria came up against him*, and *the king of Assyria took Samaria and carried Israel into exile to Assyria* (2 Kings 17:3, 6).
- **v45 "Azaph"** — so the Ethiopic; the Syriac has *Arzaph*, the Latin *Arzareth*, which the King James Version prints as *Arsareth*.

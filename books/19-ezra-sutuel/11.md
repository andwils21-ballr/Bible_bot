# Ezra Sutuel 11

## The Eagle from the Sea

**1** Then, on the second night, I saw in a dream an eagle coming up from the sea, and it had twelve wings and three heads.

**2** It spread its wings over all the earth, and all the winds of heaven blew on it, and the clouds gathered around it.

**3** From its wings heads sprouted, and those heads became small, thin wings.

**4** Its heads were silent. The middle head was greater than the other two, but it too was silent with them.

**5** The eagle flew with its wings to rule over the earth and over those who live on it,

**6** so that everything under heaven should be subject to it; and no one opposed it, not one of all that was created on the earth.

**7** Then the eagle rose and stood on its talons, cried out with a loud voice, and said to its wings,

**8** "Do not all keep watch together. Sleep, each of you, in your own place, and keep watch each in your turn;

**9** and the heads will become great at the last."

**10** As I looked, the voice did not come from its heads, but from the middle of its body.

**11** I counted the small heads, and there were eight.

**12** Then on the right side a wing went out and ruled over all the earth.

**13** When the time of its end came, it perished, so that its place was no longer seen. The second went out, and it too ruled a long time and held power.

**14** Then, when its end came, it too perished like the first.

**15** A voice came to it, and said,

**16** "Listen, you who have held the earth so long. I tell you this before you perish:

**17** after you no one will hold it as you have, not even for half your time."

**18** The third went out, and it too held power and ruled like the first; then it too perished.

**19** So all its wings went out and ruled, one by one, and in turn perished.

**20** Then its wings rose up, each in its time, on the right side, to hold power and to rule; but they quickly perished.

**21** Some of them rose up, but did not rule.

**22** After this the twelve wings perished, and two of the small heads.

**23** Nothing was left on the eagle's body but its three heads that were silent, and six small heads.

**24** Then two of those six separated and stayed under the head on the right side, and four remained in their place.

**25** These four planned to rise up and rule.

**26** One of them rose and stood, and quickly perished,

**27** and the second likewise, and it perished more quickly than the first.

**28** Then the two that remained planned in the same way to rise up and rule.

**29** While they were planning, one of the three heads that were silent woke, the middle one, which was greater than the other heads.

**30** It took the other two heads with it,

**31** and the head turned, with those that were with it, and devoured the two small heads that had planned to rule.

**32** This head held power over all the earth and oppressed those who live on it with great hardship, and it wronged the world more than all the wings that had stood before.

**33** After this the middle head perished, like the wings.

**34** Two heads remained, and they too ruled in the same way over the earth and over those who live on it.

**35** Then the head on the right side devoured the one on the left.

## The Lion from the Field

**36** I heard a voice saying to me, "Look in front of you, and understand what you see."

**37** When I looked, a lion rose up out of the field, roaring, and I heard it speak with a human voice. It said to the eagle,

**38** "Listen, and I will speak to you. The Most High says to you:

**39** Are you not the one who is left of the four beasts I made to rule the world, so that through them the end of the times might come?

**40** The fourth came and overcame all the beasts that had gone before, and oppressed the world with great hardship and pain. So many years you lived in the world, and lived in it with deceit,

**41** and you did not judge the world in truth.

**42** For you have oppressed the righteous and struck the meek, hated the upright and loved liars; you have torn down the strongholds of the righteous, and thrown down the walls of those who did you no harm.

**43** Your sin has gone up to the Most High, and your pride to the Mighty One.

**44** The Most High has looked on His people, and the world is complete and has come to its end.

**45** So you will perish, you eagle, and your sinful wings, your wicked heads, your evil talons and your lawless body,

**46** so that the earth may rest and be eased of all its pain, freed from you, and may hope for the judgment and the mercy of Him who made her."

## Notes

- **v3 "heads sprouted, and those heads became small, thin wings"** — so the Ethiopic, which calls them heads all through the vision; they are the small heads of vv11, 22, 23 and 31, apart from the eagle's three heads that were silent. The Latin and the Syriac call them *opposing wings* and *little wings*.
- **v9 "the heads will become great at the last"** — so the Ethiopic; the Latin and the Syriac have *let the heads be kept for the end*.
- **v44 "The Most High has looked on His people"** — so the Ethiopic. The Latin and the Syriac have *looked upon his times, and behold, they are ended*.

# Ezra Sutuel 7

## The Narrow Way

**1** After I had finished speaking these words, the angel who had been with me on the nights before was sent to me again.

**2** He said to me, "Rise, Ezra, and hear the words I have come to speak to you."

**3** I said, "Speak, my lord." He said to me, "There is a sea set in a wide place, broad and boundless;

**4** but its entrance is set in a narrow place, no wider than a river.

**5** If anyone wants to see the sea, how can he reach its broad expanse unless he passes through the narrow entrance?

**6** Here is another likeness: a city is built on a plain, full of every good thing;

**7** but its entrance is narrow and set on a cliff, with fire on the right hand and deep water on the left,

**8** and only one path between them, between the fire and the water, so narrow that it holds the footsteps of only one person.

**9** If this city is given to someone as an inheritance, how will he receive his inheritance unless he passes through the danger set before it?"

**10** I said, "So it is, my lord." He said to me, "So it is with the land of My Israel and their inheritance,

**11** for I made the world for their sake. When Adam transgressed My statutes, the ways of this world became rough and narrow;

**12** they became few and evil, full of every kind of trouble, toil and hardship.

**13** But the ways of the other world are great, wide and glorious, and they bear immortal fruit.

**14** Unless the living pass through this rough and futile road, they cannot receive what is stored up for them.

**15** Why then are you troubled, you who are dust? Why are you so disturbed, you who are mortal?

**16** Why have you not set your mind on what is to come, as well as on what is now?"

**17** I answered, "O Lord, You have ordained in Your law that the righteous will inherit these things, but the ungodly will perish.

**18** The righteous endure what is narrow, hoping for what is wide; but the ungodly trust in what is narrow, and they will never see what is wide."

**19** He said to me, "There is no judge above God, and no one with understanding above the Most High.

**20** Let the many perish who disregard the law He set before them.

**21** For God strictly commanded those who came into the world what they should do to live, and what they should keep to escape punishment.

**22** But they would not be persuaded, and they spoke against Him; they devised vain things for themselves,

**23** and set deceit and wickedness before themselves; and on top of all this they said, 'There is no God.'

**24** They forsook His ways, scorned His law, despised His covenant, disbelieved His statutes and neglected His works.

**25** That is why empty things are for the empty, and full things for the full.

## The Messiah and the Judgment

**26** For the time is coming when the signs I have foretold to you will appear: the city that now appears will be hidden, and the land that is now hidden will appear.

**27** Everyone who has been delivered from the evils I foretold will see My glory.

**28** For My Messiah will be revealed with those who are with Him, and He will make those who have been raised up rejoice.

**29** After this My Child, My Messiah, will die, and all people who have breath.

**30** The world will be turned back to its ancient silence for seven days, as it was at the beginning, so that no one is left.

**31** After seven days it will be roused, and the perishable world will pass away.

**32** The earth will give up the bodies laid in it, and the dust those who rest in it, and then they will give back the souls entrusted to them.

**33** On that day the Most High will appear on the seat of judgment. His mercy will come, His compassion will cease, and His patience will come to an end.

**34** Only judgment will remain; truth will stand, faith will flourish,

**35** the work will follow, and the reward will be shown; righteousness will awake, and unrighteousness will not sleep.

**36** Then the pit of judgment will appear, and opposite it the place of rest; the furnace of Gehenna will be shown, and opposite it the paradise of joy.

**37** On that day the Most High will say to the people who have been raised, 'Look and understand whom you have denied, whom you would not serve, and whose commandments you have despised.

**38** Look on this side and on that: here is joy and rest, and there is judgment and fire.' So He will speak to them on the day of judgment.

**39** This is how the day of judgment will be: there will be neither sun nor moon nor stars,

**40** nor cloud nor thunder nor lightning, nor wind nor water nor sky, nor darkness nor day nor night,

**41** nor winter nor summer nor harvest, nor cold nor heat, nor mist nor hail nor frost, nor rain nor dew,

**42** nor evening nor dawn, nor brightness nor splendor nor blazing light; only the radiance of the glory of God, so that those who wait for Him may see all things in Him.

**43** That day will last as long as seven years.

**44** This is His judgment and His decree; to you alone have I shown these things."

## Few Saved, Many Lost

**45** I answered, "Now I will call blessed those who keep Your commandments.

**46** But as for what I asked: who is there who does not sin, and who that is born has not transgressed Your statutes?

**47** I see now that the world to come will bring joy to few, but torment to many.

**48** For an evil heart has grown up in us, which leads us astray, brings us into ruin, and draws us into the way of death and the path of destruction, and takes us far from life; and not a few, but all who have been born."

**49** He answered me, "Listen to me, and I will speak to you and explain again

**50** why the Most High has made not one world but two.

**51** For you have said that the righteous are few, not many. Listen, then:

**52** make for yourself a vessel of lead and of potter's clay."

**53** I said, "How, my lord, can this be?"

**54** He said to me, "Not only this; ask the earth, and it will tell you and teach you.

**55** Say to it, 'You have brought forth gold and silver, bronze, iron, lead and potter's clay;

**56** but more silver than gold, more bronze than silver, more lead than iron, and more clay than lead.'

**57** Judge for yourself which is precious and desirable: what is plentiful, or what is rare?"

**58** I said, "O Lord, my Lord, what is rare is more precious, and what is plentiful is despised."

**59** He answered me, "You have judged rightly, for whoever has what is rare rejoices more than whoever has what is common.

**60** So also is the hope of the righteous, who are Mine. I will rejoice over the few who will live, because they will come to My glory, for in them My name has been glorified.

**61** I do not grieve for those who perish, for they are now like fire, like a flame and like smoke: they burn, they blaze, and they go out."

**62** I answered him, "O earth, what has become of the dust that shares your nature?

**63** It would have been better for us if an understanding heart had not been formed in us

**64** than to have it formed and grow with us, and to be condemned because of it; for we know that we will perish.

**65** Let the human race mourn and the beasts of the field rejoice; let all who are born of woman weep, and the flocks and the cattle leap for joy;

**66** for it is much better for them than for us. No judgment awaits them, they know no torment, and they hope for no life after death.

**67** What good is our life to us?

**68** All who are born are sunk in sins, full of wickedness and laden with transgressions.

**69** It would have been better for us if we were not to face judgment after death."

**70** He answered me, "When the Most High made the world, and Adam and all who came from him, He first prepared the judgment and the punishment.

**71** Now learn wisdom from your own words. You said that an understanding heart grows with us.

**72** That is why those who live on the earth will be punished: having understanding, they did wrong; having received knowledge, they did not keep His commandments; and having learned His law, they forsook the statutes they had received.

**73** What then will they be able to say when they are judged?

**74** How long the Most High has borne with those who live in the world, not for their own sake, but for the sake of the times He appointed!"

## The Souls After Death

**75** I answered him, "If I have found favor before you, explain this to your servant: when we die and our soul goes out from each of us, will we be kept in a place of rest until the time comes when He establishes His judgment, or will we be judged from that very hour?"

**76** He answered me, "I will explain this to you as well. But you are not joined with those who have shown contempt, nor counted among those who will be punished,

**77** for you have a treasure of works stored up with the Most High; but you will not see it until the last days.

**78** As for death, this is the teaching: when the decree has gone out from the Most High that a person must die, the spirit leaves the body to return to Him who gave it, and first of all it bows down before the glory of the Most High.

**79** If it is one of those who have shown contempt, who have not kept the way of the Most High, have despised His law and forgotten the fear of Him,

**80** those spirits will not enter dwellings, but will wander about, and will be punished, tormented and grieved in seven ways.

**81** The first way: because they scorned the law of the Most High.

**82** The second way: because they cannot turn back to live.

**83** The third way: they will see the reward stored up for those who believed the statutes of the Most High.

**84** The fourth way: they will see the punishment that awaits them in the last days.

**85** The fifth way: they will see the angels guarding the souls of the righteous in their dwellings in great quiet.

**86** The sixth way: they must wander about and see the punishment that will come upon them from then on.

**87** The seventh way, which is greater than all I have told you: they will melt away in shame, faint in confusion and wither in fear when they see the glory of the Most High before them, against whom they sinned while they lived, and before whom they are to be judged in the last times.

**88** It will be otherwise with those who have kept the statutes of the Most High, when they leave their mortal body.

**89** All the time they lived in it, they served Him in affliction and endured pain every hour, to keep the law of their Lawgiver perfectly.

**90** This is what will happen to them:

**91** first of all, they will see with great joy the glory of Him who receives them, and He will lead them through seven orders of consolation.

**92** The first order: they strove with great toil to overcome the evil inclination formed with them, so that it would not lead them astray from life into death.

**93** The second order: they see the souls of sinners wandering from place to place, and the punishment that awaits them.

**94** The third order: they see the witness their Maker bears to them, that in their lifetime they kept faithfully the law that was given them.

**95** The fourth order: they understand the rest they will enjoy, gathered in their dwellings in great quiet under the guard of angels, and the glory that awaits them.

**96** The fifth order: they rejoice that they have escaped what is mortal and will inherit what is to come; they see that, as they endured a narrow life full of suffering, so now they will receive a wide life full of joy that does not die.

**97** The sixth order: it is shown them that their faces will shine like the sun and they will be made like the light of the stars, for they will never die.

**98** The seventh order, which is greater than all: they will rejoice openly without shame and trust without confusion, for they hasten to see the face of Him whom they served in their lifetime, from whom they will receive honor and reward.

**99** These are the orders of consolation of the souls of the righteous, which they will receive from then on; and these are the ways of punishment with which those who have shown contempt will be afflicted."

**100** I answered him, "Will the soul be given time after it leaves the body to see what you have spoken of?"

**101** He said to me, "They will be free for seven days, to see during those days what I have told you; afterward they will go to their dwellings."

## No One Can Plead for Another

**102** I answered him, "My lord, if I have found favor in your sight, show your servant this also: on the day of judgment, will the righteous be able to plead with the Most High for the ungodly,

**103** fathers for their children, or children for their fathers, kin for their kin, or friends for their friends?"

**104** He answered me, "Since you have found favor in My sight, I will show you this also. The day of judgment is decisive, and it sets the seal of truth on all My decrees. As now a father does not send his son, nor a son his father, nor a servant his master, nor a friend his friend, to be ill in his place, or sleep, or eat, or be healed for him,

**105** so no one will ever be able to plead for another, nor lay his own burden on his companion; for each one will bear his own righteousness or unrighteousness."

**106** I answered him, "How then do we find that Abraham first prayed for the people of Sodom, and Moses for those who sinned in the wilderness,

**107** Joshua for Israel in the days of Achan, and Samuel in the days of Saul,

**108** David for the plague, and Solomon for the sanctuary,

**109** Elijah for rain, and for the dead to live again,

**110** and Hezekiah for the people in the days of Sennacherib, and many for many?

**111** If now, when corruption has grown and wickedness has multiplied, the righteous have prayed for the ungodly, why will it not be so on that day too?"

**112** He answered me, "Because the end of this world has not yet come, and the glory of God does not stay in it continually. That is why the strong have prayed for the weak.

**113** But the day of judgment will be the end of this world and the beginning of the world to come, in which mortality will cease and immortality begin.

**114** After that, weakness will be done away with, strife will cease, righteousness will flourish and uprightness will be established.

**115** Then no one will be able to have mercy on him who has been condemned, nor to harm him who has been acquitted."

## O Adam, What Have You Done?

**116** I answered him, "This is my first word and my last: it would have been better if the earth had not brought forth Adam than to bring him forth and teach him to sin.

**117** What good is it to all of us to live in misery, and to look for punishment when we die?

**118** O Adam, what have you done? If you had not sinned, this evil would not have come upon us.

**119** What good is it to us that an immortal world has been promised to us, when we have done the works that bring death?

**120** that a good hope has been foretold to us, when we have pursued evil?

**121** that dwellings free of sickness and sorrow are stored up for us, when we have lived wickedly?

**122** that the glory of the Most High will protect the righteous who have suffered, when we have walked in transgression?

**123** that a paradise will be shown, whose fruit never fades, in which there is life and joy,

**124** when we will never enter it, because we have done what is not worthy?

**125** that the faces of those who endured with a steadfast mind will shine, when our faces will be blacker than darkness?

**126** We live without knowing what will happen to us after death."

**127** He answered me, "This is the meaning of the contest that everyone born on the earth must fight:

**128** if he is defeated, he will suffer what you have said; but if he conquers, he will receive what I have said.

**129** This is the way Moses spoke of to the people, 'Choose life'; and the prophets spoke of it to those who lived after him.

**130** But they denied him, and the prophets after him,

**131** and Me, who have spoken to them; and I do not grieve over their destruction, as I rejoice over the life of those who believe."

## The Names of His Mercy

**132** I answered him, "I know, O Lord, that the Most High is now called merciful, because He has mercy on those who have not yet come into the world;

**133** and compassionate, because He has compassion on those who turn to His law;

**134** and patient, because He bears patiently with those who have sinned, as with His own children;

**135** and bountiful, because He is gracious to those who are worthy by their works;

**136** and abundant in mercy, because He multiplies His mercies on those who are now, on those who obey Him, and on those who are worthy of praise.

**137** For if He did not multiply His mercy, the world and those who live in it could not remain alive.

**138** He is a giver, for if He did not give out of His goodness, to relieve sinners of their sins, the families of mankind could not live;

**139** and He is a judge, for if He did not keep the work of His hands, and blot out the multitude of those who deny Him, very few indeed would be left of a countless multitude."

## Notes

- **The chapter and its numbering** — vv36–105, from the pit of judgment to *no one will ever be able to plead for another*, are missing from the Latin text the King James Version was made from, so the King James has 70 verses here: it goes from v35 straight to Abraham's prayer (v106 here) and numbers on from 36. The Ethiopic and the Syriac have the whole chapter, and it is numbered as they and the restored Latin number it.
- **vv28–29 "My Messiah will be revealed… My Child, My Messiah, will die"** — so the Ethiopic, as Laurence's English and the shape of Dillmann's edition give it. The Syriac has *my son the Messiah*, who *shall rejoice those who remain for thirty years*; the Latin has *my Son Jesus* and *four hundred years*, then *my Son Christ shall die*. The Ethiopic gives no number of years: the Messiah *will make those who have been raised up rejoice*, and after that He dies, with all who have breath, before the seven days of silence and the resurrection.
- **v118 "O Adam, what have you done? If you had not sinned, this evil would not have come upon us"** — the Latin and the Syriac have *your fall was not yours alone, but ours also who are descended from you*. Both read the evil heart of 3:21 back to the first man; Paul says the same: *sin entered the world through one man, and death through sin* (Romans 5:12).
- **v129 "Choose life"** — Moses' words, *I have set before you life and death, the blessing and the curse. Choose life, so that you may live* (Deuteronomy 30:19).
- **vv132–139 "merciful… compassionate… patient… abundant in mercy"** — the names of the LORD proclaimed to Moses, *God merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abundant in loyal love and truth* (Exodus 34:6), taken one by one, each with its reason.

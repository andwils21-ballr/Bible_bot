# Ezra Sutuel 8

## Many Created, Few Saved

**1** He answered me, "The Most High made this world for the many, but the world to come for the few.

**2** I will tell you a likeness, Ezra. Ask the earth, and it will tell you that it gives the clay from which earthen pots are made, and the dust from which gold comes; so it is with the course of this world.

**3** Many are created in it, but few will be saved."

**4** I answered him, "Let my soul rejoice in understanding, and let my ear drink in wisdom.

**5** For the ear comes to hear and goes away against its will, and nothing is given us but a short life.

**6** O Lord, if You had granted Your servants, when You gave us understanding and thought by which we till the ground, that wherever we plowed and sowed we would gain fruit, we could have lived. But we all die, so that the world can hold the children of men.

**7** For we are all alike the work of Your hands, as You have said.

**8** The body and the limbs of a man are fashioned in the womb, when You give him a heart and keep him in warmth and moisture; nine months You bear him whom You have created, and he is kept by Your word.

**9** When his mother has given birth to him, after he is born and formed,

**10** You command milk, the fruit of the breasts, to flow,

**11** to nourish what You have formed for an appointed time. Then You make him grow by Your mercy,

**12** You raise him in Your righteousness, You teach him in Your law, and You correct him in Your wisdom.

**13** Afterward You bring death on Your servant, whom You created and gave life.

**14** If You destroy him whom You spent so long creating and forming, why did You create him at all?

**15** Now I have spoken; but You Yourself know all things best.

**16** Yet for Your people and for Your inheritance I grieve.

**17** This is why I mourn, and why I have begun to pray to You for myself and for them,

**18** because I see our falling away, we who are to live in the world to come.

**19** Hear my voice, then, and listen to my petition, and I will speak before You."

## The Prayer of Ezra

**20** The beginning of the prayer of Ezra, before he was taken up. He said, "O Lord, who dwell forever, who look out from the heights, who are in heaven,

**21** whose throne is beyond measure and whose glory is beyond knowing, before whom Your angels stand trembling,

**22** at whose word they are turned to wind and fire; whose word is true and whose sayings endure,

**23** whose command is strong and whose ordinance is fearful, whose look dries up the deep and whose anger makes the mountains melt, and whose truth bears witness:

**24** hear the prayer of Your servant, and listen to the petition of Your creature;

**25** give ear to my words. For while I live I will speak, and while I have understanding I will answer.

**26** Do not look on the sins of Your people, but on the righteousness of those who serve You.

**27** Do not regard the works of sinners, but the sufferings of those who keep Your statutes.

**28** Do not be angry with those who do wrong before You, but remember those who have believed in Your law with all their heart.

**29** Do not will to destroy those who have lived like beasts, but remember and look on those who have truly embraced Your statutes.

**30** Do not be indignant with those who are worse than beasts, but love those who have always trusted in Your glory.

**31** We and our fathers have done deeds worthy of death;

**32** but have mercy on us, for our sake and for the sake of those who have transgressed. If You have mercy on us, who have no good works, then You will be called merciful.

**33** As for the righteous, whose good works are stored up with You, they will receive mercy because of their own works.

**34** For what is man, that You should be angry with him? What is a corruptible race, that You should be so bitter toward it?

**35** Truly I say that there is no one born who has not sinned.

**36** In this above all Your goodness will be shown, if You have mercy on those who have no good works."

## The Farmer's Seed

**37** He answered me, "You have spoken rightly and truly, and so it will be.

**38** I will not think about those who have sinned, nor their death, nor their judgment and their misery;

**39** but I will rejoice over the righteous, for they are the ones who will live and receive their reward.

**40** As you have spoken, so it will be.

**41** For as the farmer sows many seeds and plants many trees, and yet not all that are sown live in their season, nor do all that are planted take root, so not all who are in this world will live."

**42** I answered him, "If I have found favor in Your sight, let me speak.

**43** If the farmer's seed does not come up, it is said that it did not receive rain, and so it perishes.

**44** But man, whom You formed with Your hands, You have likened to Your own image; if he is Your own image, and You made all things for his sake, how can You liken him to the farmer's seed?

**45** Far be it from You, O Lord! Spare Your people and have mercy on Your inheritance, for You have mercy on Your own creation."

## Paradise Is Opened for You

**46** He answered me, "What belongs to this world is for this world, and what belongs to the other world is for the other world.

**47** You come far short of being able to love My creation more than I do. You have often counted yourself among sinners, though you are not one.

**48** In this you will be praised before the Most High,

**49** because you have humbled yourself as is fitting for you, and have not placed yourself among the righteous; so you will be all the more worthy of praise.

**50** Many miseries will come upon those who live in the world in the last days, because they have walked in great pride.

**51** But you, understand for yourself, and seek out the works of those who are like you.

**52** For you paradise is opened, the tree of life is planted, the world to come is prepared, and joy is decreed and built; rest is given, blessing and goodness remain, and the root of wisdom is gathered.

**53** Sickness is sealed up, death is put out, Sheol is hidden, and corruption is destroyed;

**54** pain is forgotten, and the treasure of immortal life is shown.

**55** Ask no more, then, about the number of those who perish.

**56** For they were given freedom, and they despised the Most High, scorned His law and forsook His ways.

**57** They trampled His righteous ones,

**58** and said in their hearts, 'There is no God,' though they knew that they must die.

**59** So as this good is yours, that destruction is theirs.

**60** For those who were created defiled the name of Him who made them, and were ungrateful to Him who prepared life for them.

**61** Now their judgment is drawing near,

**62** which I have not shown to many, but only to you and to a few like you."

**63** I answered him, "O Lord, You have now shown me the many wonders You will do in the last days, but You have not told me when."

## Notes

- **v20 "The beginning of the prayer of Ezra, before he was taken up"** — a title inside the book, in the Ethiopic and the Latin; the Syriac lacks it. It looks ahead to the book's end, which in the Ethiopic closes, *Then Ezra was taken up* (14:48), as Enoch was, *for God took him* (Genesis 5:24).
- **v44 "You have likened to Your own image"** — *God created the human in His image* (Genesis 1:27). Ezra's argument is that a creature made in God's image cannot be treated like seed that fails for want of rain.
- **vv52–53 "For you paradise is opened, the tree of life is planted… Sheol is hidden"** — the way that was shut at the beginning, when the cherubim were set *to keep the way to the tree of life* (Genesis 3:24), is opened again for the righteous.

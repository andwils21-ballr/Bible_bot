# Ezra Sutuel 9

## The Time of the Visitation

**1** He answered me, "Measure carefully in yourself; and when you see that part of the signs I have told you of has passed,

**2** then you will understand that the time has come near when the Most High will visit the world He has made.

**3** When earthquakes appear in every land of the world, when the nations are in turmoil and the peoples defiled, when their rulers destroy one another and their leaders are thrown into confusion,

**4** then you will understand that the Most High spoke of these things before their appointed time.

**5** For as everything that happens in the world has its beginning in the word that foretells it and its end in the deed that fulfills it,

**6** so the Most High begins with prophecy, wonder and power, and ends with the deed foretold and with signs.

**7** Whoever is saved, and can escape by his works or by the faith in which he has believed,

**8** will be kept from the sufferings I have spoken of, and will see life in the land and the borders that I have set apart for Myself from the world.

**9** Then those who have now neglected My precepts will be afflicted, and those who have forsaken My statutes and despised Me will be tormented in judgment.

**10** All who did not know Me in their lifetime, though they received good things from Me,

**11** all who scorned My law while they still had freedom, while I still bore with them,

**12** and did not understand, but despised Me, these will know Me only after death.

**13** Do not ask, then, how the wicked will be tormented, but ask how the righteous will be saved, whose world it is, and for whose sake it was created."

**14** I answered,

**15** "As I said before, so I say now again: many more will perish than will be saved,

**16** as a wave is greater than a drop." He answered me,

**17** "As is the field, so is the seed; as is the work, so is the judgment of it; as is the farmer, so is the harvest. The world was formed in its appointed time,

**18** which I prepared before the age of those who were to live in it was established, and no one opposed Me,

**19** for no one existed then. Now they were created in a world prepared for them, with a table always spread and a law with no trace of death; and when I looked at their works and at the world,

**20** it had sunk into ruin, the world I formed, and they will be tormented for their wicked deeds spread over the earth.

**21** Then I went and saved a very small part: I kept for Myself one grape out of a cluster, and one plant out of a great forest.

**22** So let the multitude perish who were created in vain, but let My grape and My plant be kept, which I have tended with so much labor.

**23** Now, if you will pray again and fast seven days,

**24** go into a field where no house has been built, and eat only what grows in that field; taste no meat and drink no wine, but only the fruit of its trees;

**25** and pray to the Most High without ceasing, and I will come and speak with you."

## The Law Sown in Israel

**26** So I went, as he had commanded me, into the field called Arphad, and sat down on the grass, and ate of the fruit of the field until I was satisfied.

**27** After seven days, as I lay on the grass, my heart was troubled again, as before.

**28** Then I opened my mouth and began to speak before the Most High.

**29** I said, "O Lord, my Lord, You appeared to our fathers in the wilderness, in a barren land where there were neither trees nor grass,

**30** when You said, 'Israel, hear Me and obey My word.

**31** I am sowing My law in you, and it will bear fruit in you, and you will be glorified in it forever.'

**32** But our fathers, who received Your law, did not keep it, nor did they observe Your ordinances. Yet the fruit of Your law did not perish, for it could not perish,

**33** because it was Yours. But those who received it perished, because they did not keep what had been sown in them; for that was the law.

**34** It is not so with the earth when it has received seed, nor with the sea when a ship has been launched on it, nor with a vessel when food has been put in it;

**35** for what is sown or set in any of them perishes when its time comes, and they that received it remain. But with us it is not so.

**36** We who received the law and sinned perish, and our heart that received it;

**37** yet the law does not perish, but remains in its glory."

## The Mourning Woman

**38** While I was thinking these things in my heart, I looked with my eyes and saw a woman on my right, weeping and wailing aloud, deeply grieved in her soul. Her garments were torn, and there were ashes on her head.

**39** Then I put aside the thoughts that filled my mind and turned to her,

**40** and said to her, "Why are you weeping, and why is your soul so grieved?"

**41** She said, "Let me be, my lord, that I may weep for myself and go on mourning, for I am very bitter in spirit and deeply troubled."

**42** I said to her, "What has happened to you? Tell me." She said,

**43** "I, your servant, was barren, though I had a husband for thirty years.

**44** Every hour and every day of those thirty years I prayed to the Most High, day and night.

**45** After thirty years God heard His servant, looked on my sorrow, considered my distress and gave me a son. I rejoiced greatly over him, I and my husband and all my neighbors, and I gave glory to God.

**46** I raised him with great care.

**47** When he grew up, I chose a wife for him and made a day of feasting."

## Notes

- **vv21–22 "I kept for Myself one grape out of a cluster"** — the likeness of Isaiah 65:8: *As when new wine is found in the cluster, and someone says, "Do not destroy it, for there is a blessing in it," so I will do for the sake of My servants, and not destroy them all.*
- **v26 "the field called Arphad"** — so the Ethiopic and the Syriac; the Latin has *Ardat*, which the King James Version prints as *Ardath*.
- **vv35–37, the witnesses** — from *and they that received it remain* to the end of v37, the words are kept from the Latin and the Syriac: Laurence's manuscript ends v35 at *perishes when its time comes*, and Dillmann's edition breaks off there and has no vv36–37.
- **vv38–47 "a woman… weeping and wailing aloud"** — a new vision begins in the field. Who the woman is, Uriel tells Ezra in the next chapter.

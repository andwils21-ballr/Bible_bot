# Ezra Sutuel 5

## The Signs of the End

**1** "As for the signs: the days are coming when great terror will seize those who live on the earth. The way of truth will be hidden, and the land of faith will be barren.

**2** Wickedness will increase beyond what you have seen and what you have heard.

**3** A land that you now see ruined and laid waste will rule, and the earth will become a desert.

**4** If the Most High lets you live, you will see the earth thrown into confusion after the third time, and the sun will suddenly shine by night and the moon by day.

**5** Blood will drip from wood, and the stone will utter its voice, and the peoples will be in turmoil,

**6** and the stars will fall from their courses. One whom many do not expect will reign,

**7** and all will hear his voice.

**8** There will be an uproar in many regions, fire will burst out again and again, the wild beasts will leave their own country, and women will give birth to monsters.

**9** Water that is sweet will be found bitter, and friends will suddenly fight one another. Wisdom will hide herself in that day, and understanding will return to her chamber,

**10** and many will seek her and not find her; and wickedness and lawlessness will increase on the earth.

**11** One region will ask its neighbor, 'Has righteousness, or anyone who does what is right, passed through you?' And it will answer, 'No.'

**12** In those days people will hope and not obtain, they will marry and not rejoice, they will toil and labor, and their way will not prosper.

**13** These are the signs I have been sent to tell you. If you pray again and weep as you do now, and fast seven days, you will hear greater things than these."

**14** Then I awoke. My body shook violently, and my soul was so troubled that it fainted.

**15** But the angel who had come to speak with me took hold of me, strengthened me and set me on my feet.

## Phaltiel and the Fast

**16** On the second night Phaltiel, the leader of the people, came to me and said, "Where have you been, and why is your face so sad?

**17** Do you not know that Israel has been entrusted to you in the land of their exile?

**18** Get up, then, and eat some bread, and do not abandon us, like a shepherd who leaves his flock in the power of cruel wolves."

**19** I said to him, "Go away from me, and do not come near me for seven days; then come back to me." He listened to what I said and left me.

**20** I fasted seven days, mourning and weeping, as the angel Uriel had commanded me.

## The Second Vision: The One and the Many

**21** After the seven days the thoughts of my heart troubled me greatly again,

**22** and a spirit of understanding lifted up my soul, and I began once more to speak before the Most High.

**23** I said, "O Lord, my Lord, from all the forests of the earth and from all its trees You have chosen for Yourself one vine;

**24** from all the lands of the world You have chosen for Yourself one land;

**25** from all the depths of the sea You have filled for Yourself one river; from all the cities that have been built You have set apart Zion for Yourself alone;

**26** from all the birds that were created You have set apart for Yourself one dove; from all the flocks that were made You have chosen for Yourself one sheep;

**27** and from all the many nations of the earth You have chosen for Yourself one people, and to this people, whom You loved above all, You gave the law You approved out of all laws.

**28** Now, O Lord, why have You given up the one to the many, and dishonored the one root more than the others, and scattered Your few among the many?

**29** Those who believe in Your law are trampled by those who oppose Your covenants.

**30** If You hated Your people, they should have been punished by Your own hand."

## A Circle of Judgment

**31** When I had spoken these words, the angel who had come to me the night before was sent to me,

**32** and he said to me, "Listen to me, and I will teach you; pay attention to me, and I will tell you more."

**33** I said, "Speak, my lord." He said to me, "Are you greatly troubled for Israel? Or do you love Israel more than his Maker does?"

**34** I said, "No, my lord. I spoke out of grief, for my inmost being torments me every hour as I try to find the way of the Most High and to understand the path of His judgment."

**35** He said to me, "You cannot." I said, "Why, my lord? Why then was I born? Why did my mother's womb not become my grave, so that I would not have seen the travail of Jacob and the exhaustion of the race of Israel?"

**36** He said to me, "Count for Me the days that have not yet come; gather for Me the scattered drops; make the withered flowers green again for Me;

**37** open for Me the shut chambers, and bring out for Me the souls shut up in them; show Me the faces of those you have never seen, and let Me hear their voices; and then I will tell you about the suffering that has rightly come upon them."

**38** I said, "O Lord, my Lord, who can know these things, except one whose dwelling is not with mortals?

**39** As for me, I am simple and lowly. How could I speak of what You ask me?"

**40** He said to me, "As you cannot do one of the things I have named, so you cannot find out My judgment, or the end of My love, for which I wait patiently for the sake of My people."

**41** I said, "O Lord, my Lord, You have waited patiently for their sake. But what will become of those who were before us, and of us, and of those who come after us?"

**42** He said to me, "I liken My judgment to a circle: as there is no slowness for the last, so there is no haste for the first."

**43** I answered, "Could You not have created at one time those who have been, those who are, and those who are to come, so that You might show Your judgment sooner?"

**44** He answered me, "The creation cannot hasten ahead of its Creator, and the world cannot hold all at once those who are created in it."

**45** I said, "How then did You tell Your servant that You will give life all at once to those You have created? If You give them life all at once and the world will hold them, it could hold them all at once now."

**46** He said to me, "Ask the womb of a woman, and say to her, 'If you bear ten children, why one at a time?' Ask her, then, to give birth to ten at once."

**47** I said, "She cannot; only one after another."

**48** He said to me, "So I have made the earth a womb for those who are sown in it, each in its time.

**49** For as an infant cannot give birth, nor a woman who has grown old, so I have set its times for the world I created."

## The World Grows Old

**50** I asked Him, "Since You have given me leave, let me speak before You. Our mother, of whom You told me, was young; is she now old?"

**51** He answered me, "Ask a woman who bears children, and she will tell you.

**52** Say to her, 'Why are those you have borne now not like those born before, but smaller in strength?'

**53** She will answer you, 'Those born in the strength of youth are one thing, and those born when the womb is old are another.'

**54** Consider, then, that you are less in strength than those before you,

**55** and those who come after you will be less than you. So it is with everything that is created and lives, once the strength of its youth has passed."

**56** I said, "I beg You, my Lord, if I have found favor in Your sight, tell Your servant through whom You will visit Your world."

## Notes

- **v3 "A land that you now see ruined and laid waste will rule"** — so the Ethiopic, as both Laurence's English and the shape of Dillmann's edition give it. The Latin and the Syriac say the reverse: *the land that you now see ruling* will be left desolate.
- **vv6–7 "One whom many do not expect will reign, and all will hear his voice"** — the Ethiopic is shorter here. The Latin and the Syriac add *the birds shall fly away, and the Sea of Sodom shall cast forth fish*, and give the voice to *one whom many do not know*, which *shall utter its voice by night*.
- **vv36–55, the angel's "I" and "My"** — the angel speaks as God Himself: *My judgment… My people* (v40), *the world I created* (v49), and Ezra answers him *O Lord, my Lord* (vv38, 41). The pronouns are capitalized where the words are God's.
- **v56 "through whom You will visit Your world"** — so the Latin, the Syriac and the shape of Dillmann's edition. Laurence's English of his manuscript reads *on account of whom didst thou contemplate thy world*. The answer comes in 6:1: *its end will be by Me alone*.

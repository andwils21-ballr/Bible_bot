# Ezra Sutuel 10

## The Woman's Son

**1** "Then, when he went into his wedding chamber, he fell down and died.

**2** We put out the lamps and sat weeping, and all the people of my city rose up to comfort me; and I kept silent until the night of the next day.

**3** When they had all fallen silent and stopped comforting me, I rose up in the night and fled, and came here, to this field, as you see.

**4** I have made up my mind not to go back to the city, but to stay here, and neither eat nor drink, but fast and mourn without ceasing until I die."

**5** Then I put aside the thoughts I had been thinking, and answered her,

**6** "You are more foolish than any woman! Do you not see our grief, and what has come upon us?

**7** Because of Zion, the mother of us all, all of us are in grief and in deep pain.

**8** Now indeed you grieve and mourn; but all of us are mourning, and you grieve for one son.

**9** Ask the earth, and she will tell you: it is she who has the right to grieve, for the multitude born on her,

**10** all who were made on her from the beginning; others have come after them, and they have all been carried off to corruption and ruin, every one of them.

**11** Who, then, has more right to mourn: she who has lost so great a multitude, or you, who grieve for one?

**12** Or will you say, 'My mourning is not like the earth's, for I have lost the fruit of my womb, which I bore in labor and pain;

**13** but the earth is not so: as many as came to her, so many have gone'?

**14** Yet I tell you: as you gave birth in pain, so the earth too has given her fruit, humankind, from the beginning, out of the fruit her Creator gave her.

**15** Now put this sorrow away from you, and be patient in the suffering and the judgment that have come upon you.

**16** For if you acknowledge that God is just, you will receive your son back in his time, and be named among women for your patience.

**17** Now go into the city, to your husband."

**18** She said to me, "I will not do so. I will not go into the city; here I will die."

**19** I went on speaking to her, and said,

**20** "Do not do this; let yourself be persuaded. Set your sorrow beside the judgment of Zion, and take comfort with the city of Jerusalem.

**21** Do you not see that our sanctuary has been torn down,

**22** our psalms have ceased, our praise is silent, our boasting has fallen, our lamp and our light are put out, the ark of our law has been plundered, our holy things are defiled, our name is profaned, our freeborn are disgraced, our priests are burned, our Levites have gone into captivity, our virgins are killed, our wives are violated, our righteous are dragged away, our young men are enslaved, our little ones are carried off, and our strong men are made weak?

**23** Greatest of all is Zion's seal: her glory has gone out from her, and we have been given into the hands of those who hate us.

**24** So cast off your great grief, and put away the multitude of your sorrows, so that the Most High, the Mighty One, may have mercy on you again, and God may give you rest from your labors."

## The City in the Field

**25** While I was speaking to her, her face suddenly shone exceedingly, and her appearance became like the flash of lightning. I was very much afraid to go near her, and my heart was astonished. While I was wondering what this was,

**26** she suddenly cried out with a loud voice, so great and fearful that the earth shook at her cry.

**27** I looked, and the woman was no longer there, but a city, built, and I saw a place of great foundations. I was afraid, and cried out with a loud voice,

**28** "Where is Uriel the angel, who came to me at the first? Why has he done this to me, bringing me into all this searching, so that my end has become ruin, and my prayer a reproach?"

**29** While I was saying this, the angel who had come to me at the first came to me.

**30** He found me lying like a corpse, and my senses had left me. He took me by my right hand, strengthened me, and set me upright on my feet,

**31** and said to me, "What is the matter with you? What troubles you? Why have your senses left you?"

**32** I said to him, "Because you have left me and forsaken me. I did as you told me and went out into the field, and I have seen what I cannot understand."

**33** He said to me, "Stand up like a man, and I will tell you."

**34** I said to him, "Speak, my lord; only do not leave me, so that I do not die suddenly.

**35** For I have seen what I never saw, and heard what I do not understand.

**36** Or has my mind deceived me, and is my soul dreaming?

**37** Now, I beg you, my lord, tell your servant what this means."

## Zion

**38** He answered me, "Listen to me, and I will teach you about what you fear; for the Most High God has revealed a hidden mystery to you,

**39** since He has seen your righteousness, that you grieve greatly for your people and mourn deeply for Zion.

**40** This is the meaning. The woman you saw at first,

**41** who was mourning so, and whom you began to comfort,

**42** you see no longer as a woman; she has become to you a city, built.

**43** As for her telling you of what befell her son, this is the interpretation:

**44** the woman you saw is Zion, whom you now see as a city, built.

**45** As for what she told you, 'I was barren thirty years': it is because the world stood three thousand years with no one bringing an offering in her.

**46** After three thousand years Solomon built the city and brought the offering in; that was when the barren woman bore a son.

**47** As for her saying, 'I raised him with great labor': that was the time when Jerusalem was lived in.

**48** As for her saying to you, 'When my son went into his wedding chamber he died, and this misfortune came upon me': that was the fall of Jerusalem.

**49** You saw her likeness, how she mourned for her son, and you began to comfort her in her pain.

**50** Now, when the Most High God saw that you grieve with all your soul and suffer with all your heart for her, He showed you the brightness of her glory and the loveliness of her beauty.

**51** That is why I told you to stay in the field where no house has been built,

**52** for I knew that God was about to show you all this.

**53** That is why I told you to come here, where no foundation of any building has been laid;

**54** for no building made by human hands could stand in the place where the Most High was about to reveal His city.

**55** So do not be afraid, and do not let your heart be terrified; go in and see her brightness and the strength of her buildings, as much as your eyes can see,

**56** and hear with your ears as much as you are able to hear.

**57** For you are blessed above many, and you have been named before the Most High as few have been.

**58** Tomorrow night, stay here again,

**59** and the Most High God will show you in a dream visions of what He will do, each in its time, to those who live in the world."

**60** So I slept there that night and the next, as he had commanded me.

## Notes

- **v7 "Zion, the mother of us all"** — Paul's words of the city above: *the Jerusalem above is free, and she is our mother* (Galatians 4:26). Here the mother is the woman in the field, and in v44 the angel names her: she is Zion.
- **v14 "out of the fruit her Creator gave her"** — so the Ethiopic: the earth bears humankind from what God gave her. The Latin and the Syriac turn it the other way: the earth *has given its fruit, humanity… to him who made it*.
- **v22 "the ark of our law… our virgins are killed"** — so the Ethiopic; the Latin and the Syriac have *the ark of our covenant* and *our virgins defiled*.
- **vv27, 42, 44 "a city, built… a place of great foundations"** — so the Ethiopic; the Latin and the Syriac have *a city being built*. In the Ethiopic the city is already standing when the woman is gone, as in Hebrews: *the city that has foundations, whose designer and builder is God* (Hebrews 11:10).
- **v59 "what He will do, each in its time"** — so the Ethiopic; the Latin and the Syriac have *in the last days*.

# Nehemiah 13

## Separation from the Foreigners

**1** On that day they read from the book of Moses in the hearing of the people, and it was found written in it that no Ammonite or Moabite should ever enter the assembly of God,

**2** because they did not meet the sons of Israel with bread and water, but hired Balaam against them to curse them. Yet our God turned the curse into a blessing.

**3** When they heard the instruction, they separated from Israel all who were of mixed descent.

## Tobiah Thrown Out

**4** Before this, Eliashib the priest, who was appointed over the chambers of the house of our God and was related to Tobiah,

**5** had prepared for him a large chamber where they had formerly put the grain offering, the frankincense, the vessels, and the tithes of grain, new wine and oil, which were commanded for the Levites, the singers and the gatekeepers, and the contributions for the priests.

**6** While all this was going on, I was not in Jerusalem, for in the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes king of Babylon I went to the king. After some time I asked leave of the king

**7** and came to Jerusalem, and I learned of the evil Eliashib had done for Tobiah, preparing a chamber for him in the courts of the house of God.

**8** It displeased me greatly, and I threw all of Tobiah's household furniture out of the chamber.

**9** Then I gave orders, and they purified the chambers, and I brought back there the vessels of the house of God, with the grain offering and the frankincense.

## The Levites' Portions

**10** I also learned that the portions of the Levites had not been given to them, so that the Levites and the singers who did the work had fled, each to his field.

**11** So I brought charges against the officials and said, "Why is the house of God neglected?" I gathered them together and set them at their posts.

**12** Then all Judah brought the tithe of the grain, the new wine and the oil into the storehouses.

**13** I put in charge of the storehouses Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe and Pedaiah of the Levites, and with them Hanan son of Zaccur, son of Mattaniah, for they were counted faithful, and their duty was to distribute to their brothers.

**14** Remember me, my God, for this, and do not wipe out the loyal deeds that I have done for the house of my God and for its service.

## The Sabbath Kept

**15** In those days I saw in Judah people treading winepresses on the sabbath, bringing in heaps of grain and loading them on donkeys, and also wine, grapes, figs and every kind of load, which they brought into Jerusalem on the sabbath day. I warned them on the day they sold food.

**16** Men of Tyre who lived in the city brought in fish and every kind of merchandise and sold it on the sabbath to the people of Judah, and in Jerusalem.

**17** Then I brought charges against the nobles of Judah and said to them, "What is this evil thing you are doing, profaning the sabbath day?

**18** Did not your fathers act in this way, and did not our God bring all this evil on us and on this city? Yet you are bringing more wrath on Israel by profaning the sabbath."

**19** When the gates of Jerusalem began to grow dark before the sabbath, I commanded that the doors be shut, and I ordered that they not be opened until after the sabbath. I stationed some of my servants at the gates, so that no load would be brought in on the sabbath day.

**20** Once or twice the merchants and sellers of every kind of goods spent the night outside Jerusalem.

**21** But I warned them and said to them, "Why are you spending the night in front of the wall? If you do it again, I will lay hands on you." From that time on they did not come on the sabbath.

**22** Then I told the Levites to purify themselves and come and guard the gates, to keep the sabbath day holy. Remember me, my God, for this also, and spare me according to the greatness of Your loyal love.

## The Foreign Wives

**23** In those days also I saw Jews who had married women of Ashdod, Ammon and Moab.

**24** Half of their children spoke the language of Ashdod, or the language of one people or another, and could not speak the language of Judah.

**25** I brought charges against them and cursed them, beat some of their men and pulled out their hair, and made them swear by God, "You shall not give your daughters to their sons, or take their daughters for your sons or for yourselves.

**26** Did not Solomon king of Israel sin on account of such women? Among the many nations there was no king like him, and he was loved by his God, and God made him king over all Israel. Yet even him the foreign women made to sin.

**27** Shall we then listen to you and do all this great evil, breaking faith with our God by marrying foreign women?"

**28** One of the sons of Joiada, son of Eliashib the high priest, was the son-in-law of Sanballat the Horonite, so I drove him away from me.

**29** Remember them, my God, because they have defiled the priesthood and the covenant of the priesthood and of the Levites.

**30** So I cleansed them from everything foreign, and I established the duties of the priests and the Levites, each in his work,

**31** and provided for the wood offering at appointed times, and for the firstfruits. Remember me, my God, for good.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "no Ammonite or Moabite should ever enter the assembly of God… our God turned the curse into a blessing"** — the law of Deuteronomy 23:3–5: *the LORD your God turned the curse into a blessing for you, because the LORD your God loved you*.
- **vv4–9 "a large chamber… I threw all of Tobiah's household furniture out"** — the store of the offerings had become the lodging of Tobiah the Ammonite (2:10), whom the law just read shuts out of the assembly.
- **vv10–11 "Why is the house of God neglected?"** — *ne'ezav*, the verb of the people's own pledge, *We will not neglect the house of our God* (10:39). The agreement broke at its last line first.
- **vv14, 22, 31 "Remember me, my God"** — Nehemiah's short prayer, as at 5:19, here three times in one chapter, and the last words of the book. In v14 he asks God not to *wipe out* his *loyal deeds*, *chasadai*; in v22 he asks to be spared *according to the greatness of Your loyal love*, *chasdekha*. In v29 the same words are turned against the priests: *Remember them, my God*.
- **vv15–22 "bringing… into Jerusalem on the sabbath day"** — the word of Jeremiah: *Do not carry a load on the sabbath day or bring it in by the gates of Jerusalem… if you do not listen… I will kindle a fire in its gates* (Jeremiah 17:21, 27). *Did not your fathers act in this way?* (v18).
- **v24 "could not speak the language of Judah"** — *Yehudit*, the language the king's men begged the Assyrian not to use on the wall (2 Kings 18:26). The children of the city cannot speak it.
- **v25 "beat some of their men and pulled out their hair"** — when Ezra heard of the marriages, he pulled out his own hair (Ezra 9:3). Nehemiah pulls out theirs.
- **v26 "he was loved by his God… Yet even him the foreign women made to sin"** — Solomon's other name was *Jedidiah*, **beloved of the LORD** (2 Samuel 12:25), and *his wives turned his heart away after other gods* (1 Kings 11:4).

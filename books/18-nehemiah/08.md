# Nehemiah 8

## The Reading at the Water Gate

**1** All the people gathered as one man into the square before the Water Gate, and they told Ezra the scribe to bring the book of the instruction of Moses, which the LORD had commanded Israel.

**2** So Ezra the priest brought the instruction before the assembly, men and women and all who could listen with understanding, on the first day of the seventh month.

**3** He read from it in the square before the Water Gate from daybreak until midday, in the presence of the men and the women and those who could understand, and the ears of all the people were on the book of the instruction.

**4** Ezra the scribe stood on a wooden platform that they had made for the purpose. Beside him on his right stood Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah and Maaseiah, and on his left Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hashbaddanah, Zechariah and Meshullam.

**5** Ezra opened the book in the sight of all the people, for he was above all the people, and as he opened it all the people stood up.

**6** Ezra blessed the LORD, the great God, and all the people answered, "Amen, Amen," lifting up their hands. They bowed down and worshiped the LORD with their faces to the ground.

**7** Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodiah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan and Pelaiah, the Levites, helped the people to understand the instruction, while the people stood in their places.

**8** They read from the book, from the instruction of God, clearly, and gave the sense, so that the people understood the reading.

## "The Joy of the LORD Is Your Strength"

**9** Nehemiah, who was the governor, and Ezra the priest, the scribe, and the Levites who taught the people said to all the people, "This day is holy to the LORD your God. Do not mourn or weep." For all the people were weeping as they heard the words of the instruction.

**10** He said to them, "Go, eat rich food and drink sweet drinks, and send portions to anyone who has nothing prepared, for this day is holy to our Lord. Do not be grieved, for the joy of the LORD is your strength."

**11** The Levites calmed all the people, saying, "Be quiet, for this day is holy. Do not be grieved."

**12** All the people went away to eat and drink, to send portions and to celebrate with great joy, because they had understood the words that had been made known to them.

## The Festival of Booths

**13** On the second day the heads of the fathers' houses of all the people, with the priests and the Levites, gathered to Ezra the scribe to study the words of the instruction.

**14** They found written in the instruction, which the LORD had commanded through Moses, that the sons of Israel should live in booths during the festival in the seventh month,

**15** and that they should proclaim and spread the word in all their towns and in Jerusalem, saying, "Go out to the hills and bring branches of olive, of wild olive, of myrtle, of palm and of leafy trees, to make booths, as it is written."

**16** So the people went out and brought them, and made booths for themselves, each on his roof, in their courtyards, in the courts of the house of God, in the square of the Water Gate and in the square of the Gate of Ephraim.

**17** All the assembly of those who had returned from the captivity made booths and lived in the booths, for from the days of Jeshua son of Nun to that day the sons of Israel had not done so. There was very great joy.

**18** Day by day, from the first day to the last day, he read from the book of the instruction of God. They kept the festival seven days, and on the eighth day there was a solemn assembly, according to the rule.

## Notes

- **v2 "on the first day of the seventh month"** — the day of the trumpet blast, *a holy assembly* (Leviticus 23:24). Ezra had come to Jerusalem *to teach statute and rule in Israel* (Ezra 7:10); this is the day he does it before all the people.
- **vv2–3 "men and women and all who could listen with understanding"** — *kol mevin lishmoa*. Children old enough to follow are counted with the assembly; the same word, *mevinim*, is used of the Levites who *helped the people to understand* (v7).
- **v8 "clearly, and gave the sense"** — *meforash ve-som sekhel*. *Meforash* is the word the king of Persia used of the letter *read plainly before me* (Ezra 4:18). It may mean read distinctly, or explained, or put into the Aramaic the people spoke; *gave the sense* says that the reading came with explanation.
- **v10 "the joy of the LORD is your strength"** — *chedvat YHWH hi ma'uzzekhem*. *Chedvah* is a rare word: in Hebrew it is found again only in the psalm David gave to Asaph, *strength and joy are in His place* (1 Chronicles 16:27), and in Aramaic at the dedication of the rebuilt house, kept *with joy* (Ezra 6:16). *Ma'oz* is a stronghold, a place to take refuge.
- **v15 "branches of olive, of wild olive, of myrtle, of palm and of leafy trees"** — Leviticus names *the fruit of splendid trees, branches of palm trees, boughs of leafy trees, and willows of the brook* (Leviticus 23:40). The list here is not the same; what is *written* is the booths themselves.
- **v17 "from the days of Jeshua son of Nun"** — *Yeshua*, the later form of the name Joshua, as the high priest Jeshua bears it. The booths were kept on the return (Ezra 3:4), but not like this, by *all the assembly*, since Joshua's day.
- **v18 "Day by day… he read from the book"** — the law of Moses: *at the end of every seven years… at the Festival of Booths… you shall read this instruction before all Israel in their hearing* (Deuteronomy 31:10–11).

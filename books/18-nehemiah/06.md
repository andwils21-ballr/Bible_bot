# Nehemiah 6

## The Plots against Nehemiah

**1** And it came to pass, when it was reported to Sanballat and Tobiah and to Geshem the Arab and to the rest of our enemies that I had built the wall and that no breach was left in it, though up to that time I had not set up the doors in the gates,

**2** that Sanballat and Geshem sent to me, saying, "Come, let us meet together at Kephirim in the plain of Ono." But they were planning to do me harm.

**3** So I sent messengers to them, saying, "I am doing a great work and cannot come down. Why should the work stop while I leave it and come down to you?"

**4** They sent to me four times in this way, and I answered them in the same way.

**5** The fifth time Sanballat sent his servant to me in the same way, with an open letter in his hand.

**6** In it was written: "It is reported among the nations, and Geshem says it too, that you and the Jews are planning to rebel; that is why you are building the wall. According to these reports, you are to become their king,

**7** and you have also set up prophets to proclaim about you in Jerusalem, 'There is a king in Judah.' Now this will be reported to the king according to these words. So now come, and let us take counsel together."

**8** I sent to him, saying, "No such things as you say have been done; you are inventing them out of your own heart."

**9** For they all wanted to frighten us, thinking, "Their hands will drop from the work, and it will not be done." But now, strengthen my hands.

## The Hired Prophet

**10** I went into the house of Shemaiah son of Delaiah, son of Mehetabel, who was shut in at home, and he said, "Let us meet at the house of God, inside the temple, and let us shut the doors of the temple, for they are coming to kill you. By night they are coming to kill you."

**11** But I said, "Should a man like me run away? Would a man like me go into the temple and live? I will not go in."

**12** Then I understood that God had not sent him, but that he had spoken the prophecy against me because Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him.

**13** He was hired for this purpose, that I should be afraid and act in this way and sin, and so they would have a bad name to taunt me with.

**14** Remember, my God, Tobiah and Sanballat according to these deeds of theirs, and also Noadiah the prophetess and the rest of the prophets who wanted to make me afraid.

## The Wall Finished

**15** So the wall was finished on the twenty-fifth day of the month of Elul, in fifty-two days.

**16** When all our enemies heard of it, all the nations around us were afraid and fell greatly in their own eyes, for they knew that this work had been done by our God.

**17** In those days also the nobles of Judah sent many letters to Tobiah, and Tobiah's letters came to them.

**18** For many in Judah were bound to him by oath, because he was the son-in-law of Shecaniah son of Arah, and his son Jehohanan had married the daughter of Meshullam son of Berechiah.

**19** They also kept speaking of his good deeds in my presence and reporting my words to him, and Tobiah sent letters to make me afraid.

## Notes

- **v3 "I sent messengers to them, saying, 'I am doing a great work'"** — *mal'akhim*, **messengers**, and *melakhah*, **work**, from the same root, *la'akh*, to send on an errand. They send for him; his errand is the wall.
- **v5 "an open letter"** — not sealed, so that the servant and anyone along the way could read it. The charge of rebellion is spread as it is delivered.
- **v7 "There is a king in Judah"** — the charge Ezra's enemies brought: a city *rebellious and harmful to kings* (Ezra 4:15).
- **vv10–11 "inside the temple… would a man like me go into the temple and live?"** — *ha-heikhal*, the house itself, where only the priests may enter. The law says *the outsider who comes near shall be put to death* (Numbers 18:7), and King Uzziah was struck with blight for going in (2 Chronicles 26:16–21). Nehemiah is not a priest; to hide there would itself be the sin (v13).
- **v14 "Noadiah the prophetess"** — *ha-nevi'ah*, the title of Miriam, Deborah and Huldah. Here it is given to a prophetess who works against him.
- **v15 "the twenty-fifth day of the month of Elul, in fifty-two days"** — Elul is the sixth month. The wall that had lain broken since Babylon burned it was closed in less than two months.
- **v16 "fell greatly in their own eyes"** — *va-yippelu me'od be-eineihem*: their confidence fell, as the wall rose.
- **v18 "the son-in-law of Shecaniah… the daughter of Meshullam son of Berechiah"** — Meshullam built two sections of the wall (3:4, 30). Tobiah the Ammonite is married into the families doing the work.

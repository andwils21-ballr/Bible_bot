# Nehemiah 11

## One in Ten for the Holy City

**1** The leaders of the people lived in Jerusalem, and the rest of the people cast lots to bring one in ten to live in Jerusalem, the holy city, while nine tenths stayed in the towns.

**2** The people blessed all the men who willingly offered to live in Jerusalem.

**3** These are the chiefs of the province who lived in Jerusalem; but in the towns of Judah each lived on his own property in their towns: Israel, the priests, the Levites, the temple servants and the sons of Solomon's servants.

## In Jerusalem

**4** In Jerusalem lived some of the sons of Judah and of the sons of Benjamin. Of the sons of Judah: Athaiah son of Uzziah, son of Zechariah, son of Amariah, son of Shephatiah, son of Mahalalel, of the sons of Perez;

**5** and Maaseiah son of Baruch, son of Col-hozeh, son of Hazaiah, son of Adaiah, son of Joiarib, son of Zechariah, son of the Shilonite.

**6** All the sons of Perez who lived in Jerusalem were four hundred and sixty-eight men of valor.

**7** These are the sons of Benjamin: Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Joed, son of Pedaiah, son of Kolaiah, son of Maaseiah, son of Ithiel, son of Jeshaiah,

**8** and after him Gabbai and Sallai, nine hundred and twenty-eight.

**9** Joel son of Zichri was their overseer, and Judah son of Hassenuah was second over the city.

**10** Of the priests: Jedaiah son of Joiarib, Jachin,

**11** Seraiah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, the chief officer of the house of God,

**12** and their brothers who did the work of the house, eight hundred and twenty-two; and Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pelaliah, son of Amzi, son of Zechariah, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah,

**13** and his brothers, heads of fathers' houses, two hundred and forty-two; and Amashsai son of Azarel, son of Ahzai, son of Meshillemoth, son of Immer,

**14** and their brothers, mighty men of valor, a hundred and twenty-eight. Their overseer was Zabdiel son of Haggedolim.

**15** Of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, son of Bunni;

**16** Shabbethai and Jozabad, of the chiefs of the Levites, who were over the outside work of the house of God;

**17** Mattaniah son of Mica, son of Zabdi, son of Asaph, the leader who began the thanksgiving in prayer, and Bakbukiah, the second among his brothers; and Abda son of Shammua, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun.

**18** All the Levites in the holy city were two hundred and eighty-four.

**19** The gatekeepers: Akkub, Talmon and their brothers, who kept watch at the gates, a hundred and seventy-two.

**20** The rest of Israel, the priests and the Levites were in all the towns of Judah, each on his own inheritance.

**21** The temple servants lived on the Ophel, and Ziha and Gishpa were over the temple servants.

**22** The overseer of the Levites in Jerusalem was Uzzi son of Bani, son of Hashabiah, son of Mattaniah, son of Mica, of the sons of Asaph, the singers, over the work of the house of God.

**23** For there was a command from the king concerning them, and a fixed provision for the singers, as each day required.

**24** Pethahiah son of Meshezabel, of the sons of Zerah son of Judah, was at the king's hand in every matter concerning the people.

## In the Villages

**25** As for the villages with their fields, some of the sons of Judah lived in Kiriath-arba and its towns, in Dibon and its towns, in Jekabzeel and its villages,

**26** in Jeshua, in Moladah, in Beth-pelet,

**27** in Hazar-shual, in Beersheba and its towns,

**28** in Ziklag, in Meconah and its towns,

**29** in En-rimmon, in Zorah, in Jarmuth,

**30** Zanoah, Adullam and their villages, Lachish and its fields, Azekah and its towns. So they camped from Beersheba to the Valley of Hinnom.

**31** The sons of Benjamin lived from Geba onward, at Michmash, Aija, Bethel and its towns,

**32** Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah,

**33** Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim,

**34** Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat,

**35** Lod and Ono, the Valley of Craftsmen.

**36** Some divisions of the Levites of Judah were joined to Benjamin.

## Notes

- **v1 "to bring one in ten to live in Jerusalem, the holy city"** — a tenth of the people, given to the city as a tithe is given. *Ir ha-qodesh*, **the holy city**, Isaiah's name for it: *Put on your beautiful garments, Jerusalem, the holy city* (Isaiah 52:1). The wall was finished, but the city was nearly empty (7:4).
- **v2 "all the men who willingly offered"** — *ha-mitnaddevim*, the word of the freewill offering. Some went by lot; some gave themselves.
- **vv3–24 and 1 Chronicles 9:2–34** — Chronicles gives a list of *the first inhabitants who lived on their property in their towns* in Jerusalem, many of the same names and families, with its own numbers.
- **v9 "second over the city"** — *al ha-ir mishneh*, which may mean *second in command of the city* or *over the Second Quarter* of the city, where Huldah lived (2 Kings 22:14).
- **v14 "Zabdiel son of Haggedolim"** — *ha-gedolim*, **the great ones**. It may be a family name, or say that he came of a leading family.
- **v17 "the leader who began the thanksgiving in prayer"** — the line of Asaph, whom David set *to give thanks to the LORD* (1 Chronicles 16:7), still leads the praise.
- **vv25–30 "Kiriath-arba… from Beersheba to the Valley of Hinnom"** — the towns of Judah's old allotment, from Hebron, once *Kiriath-arba* (Joshua 14:15), to the valley that was Judah's border below Jerusalem (Joshua 15:8). The returned people *camped* across the land their fathers had held.

# Nehemiah 5

## The Outcry

**1** There was a great outcry of the people and their wives against their brothers the Jews.

**2** Some were saying, "With our sons and our daughters, we are many. Let us get grain, so that we may eat and live."

**3** Some were saying, "We are mortgaging our fields, our vineyards and our houses to get grain in the famine."

**4** Some were saying, "We have borrowed money for the king's tax on our fields and our vineyards.

**5** Our flesh is like the flesh of our brothers, and our children are like their children, yet we are forcing our sons and our daughters into slavery. Some of our daughters have already been forced into it, and it is not in our power to help it, for our fields and our vineyards belong to others."

## Nehemiah Rebukes the Nobles

**6** I was very angry when I heard their outcry and these words.

**7** I took counsel with myself and brought charges against the nobles and the officials. I said to them, "You are exacting interest, each from his brother!" Then I held a great assembly against them.

**8** I said to them, "We, as far as we were able, have bought back our brothers the Jews who had been sold to the nations, but you are selling your own brothers, so that they may be sold to us!" They were silent and could find nothing to say.

**9** I said, "What you are doing is not good. Should you not walk in the fear of our God, because of the taunts of the nations, our enemies?

**10** I too, and my brothers and my servants, are lending them money and grain. Let us stop this interest.

**11** Give back to them this very day their fields, their vineyards, their olive groves and their houses, and the hundredth part of the money, the grain, the new wine and the oil that you are exacting from them."

**12** They said, "We will give it back and require nothing from them. We will do as you say." I called the priests and made them swear to do as they had promised.

**13** I also shook out the fold of my garment and said, "So may God shake out every man from his house and from his labor who does not keep this promise. So may he be shaken out and emptied." All the assembly said, "Amen," and praised the LORD. The people did as they had promised.

## Nehemiah's Example

**14** Moreover, from the day I was appointed to be their governor in the land of Judah, from the twentieth year to the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes the king, twelve years, neither I nor my brothers ate the governor's food allowance.

**15** The former governors who were before me laid heavy burdens on the people and took from them food and wine, besides forty shekels of silver. Even their servants lorded it over the people. But I did not do so, because of the fear of God.

**16** I also held fast to the work on this wall, and we bought no land, and all my servants were gathered there for the work.

**17** At my table were a hundred and fifty men, Jews and officials, besides those who came to us from the nations around us.

**18** What was prepared for one day was one ox and six choice sheep, and birds were prepared for me, and every ten days all kinds of wine in abundance. Yet for all this I did not ask for the governor's food allowance, because the service was heavy on this people.

**19** Remember me, my God, for good, for all that I have done for this people.

## Notes

- **vv1, 6 "a great outcry… their outcry"** — *tsa'aqah*, *za'aqah*, the cry the LORD heard from Israel in Egypt: *I have heard their outcry because of their taskmasters* (Exodus 3:7). Now the cry is against brothers.
- **v5 "Our flesh is like the flesh of our brothers"** — *ki-vsar acheinu besarenu*. The words Judah used of Joseph when he proposed to sell him: *he is our brother, our flesh* (Genesis 37:27). The poor use them against brothers who are selling them.
- **v7 "You are exacting interest, each from his brother"** — *massa… nosh'im*, from *nasha*, **to lend at interest**, the word of the law: *you shall not be a creditor to him* (Exodus 22:25); *You shall not charge interest to your brother* (Deuteronomy 23:19).
- **v8 "We… have bought back our brothers… but you are selling your own brothers"** — the law of redemption: a brother sold to a guest living among you may be bought back by his kin (Leviticus 25:47–49), and a brother who sells himself to you must not be made to serve as a slave (25:39).
- **v13 "I also shook out the fold of my garment"** — *chotsni*, the fold at the chest where things were carried. Emptied before them, it shows what will happen to any man who breaks the oath.
- **v14 "from the twentieth year to the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes"** — the year Nehemiah came (2:1) to the year he went back to the king (13:6).
- **v19 "Remember me, my God, for good"** — Nehemiah's short prayer, his own signature. It returns, in nearly the same words, at the end of the book (13:14, 22, 31).

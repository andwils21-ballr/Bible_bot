# Nehemiah 1

## News from Jerusalem

**1** The words of Nehemiah son of Hacaliah.

And it came to pass in the month of Chislev, in the twentieth year, as I was in Susa the citadel,

**2** that Hanani, one of my brothers, came with some men from Judah. I asked them about the Jews who had escaped, those who were left from the captivity, and about Jerusalem.

**3** They said to me, "The remnant there in the province who are left from the captivity are in great trouble and disgrace. The wall of Jerusalem is broken down, and its gates have been burned with fire."

**4** When I heard these words, I sat down and wept and mourned for days, and I was fasting and praying before the God of heaven.

## The Prayer

**5** I said, "Please, LORD, God of heaven, the great and awesome God, who keeps covenant and loyal love with those who love Him and keep His commandments,

**6** let Your ear be attentive and Your eyes open, to hear the prayer of Your servant that I am praying before You today, day and night, for the sons of Israel Your servants, confessing the sins of the sons of Israel, which we have sinned against You. Both I and my father's house have sinned.

**7** We have acted very corruptly against You and have not kept the commandments, the statutes and the rules that You commanded Moses Your servant.

**8** Remember the word that You commanded Moses Your servant, saying, 'If you break faith, I will scatter you among the peoples;

**9** but if you return to Me and keep My commandments and do them, even if your outcasts are at the end of the heavens, I will gather them from there and bring them to the place that I have chosen to make My name dwell there.'

**10** They are Your servants and Your people, whom You redeemed by Your great power and by Your strong hand.

**11** Please, Lord, let Your ear be attentive to the prayer of Your servant and to the prayer of Your servants who delight to fear Your name. Give success to Your servant today, and grant him mercy before this man."

I was cupbearer to the king.

## Notes

- **v1 "The words of Nehemiah son of Hacaliah"** — the book opens as a man's own account, and most of it is told in the first person.
- **v1 "in the month of Chislev, in the twentieth year"** — the twentieth year of Artaxerxes (2:1), the ninth month of the year; the request to the king comes four months later, in Nisan.
- **v5 "the great and awesome God, who keeps covenant and loyal love with those who love Him"** — the words of Moses: *the faithful God, who keeps covenant and loyal love with those who love Him and keep His commandments* (Deuteronomy 7:9). Daniel's prayer opens with the same words (Daniel 9:4).
- **v6 "let Your ear be attentive and Your eyes open"** — Solomon's prayer at the dedication of the house: *let Your eyes be open and Your ears attentive to the prayer made in this place* (2 Chronicles 6:40). *Both I and my father's house have sinned*: he confesses with the people, not about them.
- **vv8–9 "If you break faith, I will scatter you… even if your outcasts are at the end of the heavens, I will gather them from there"** — not one verse but the sense of several: *I will scatter you among the nations* (Leviticus 26:33), and *if your outcasts are at the end of the heavens, from there the LORD your God will gather you* (Deuteronomy 30:4). *Break faith* is *ma'al*, Ezra's word for the sin (Ezra 9:2).
- **v10 "by Your great power and by Your strong hand"** — Moses' plea after the golden calf: *whom You brought out of the land of Egypt with great power and with a strong hand* (Exodus 32:11).
- **v11 "before this man… I was cupbearer to the king"** — *mashqeh*, the office of Pharaoh's *chief cupbearer* in Joseph's story (Genesis 40:1–2). In the prayer, the king of Persia is *this man*.

# Nehemiah 10

**7** Meshullam, Abijah, Mijamin,

**8** Maaziah, Bilgai and Shemaiah. These were the priests.

**11** Mica, Rehob, Hashabiah,

**12** Zaccur, Sherebiah, Shebaniah,

## The Terms of the Agreement

**28** The rest of the people, the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, the temple servants and all who had separated themselves from the peoples of the lands to the instruction of God, their wives, their sons and their daughters, all who had knowledge and understanding,

**29** join their brothers, their nobles, and enter into a curse and an oath to walk in the instruction of God, which was given through Moses the servant of God, and to keep and do all the commandments of the LORD our Lord and His rules and His statutes:

**30** We will not give our daughters to the peoples of the land or take their daughters for our sons.

**31** If the peoples of the land bring goods or any grain to sell on the sabbath day, we will not buy from them on the sabbath or on a holy day. We will let the seventh year lie fallow, and cancel every debt.

**32** We also lay on ourselves the obligation to give a third of a shekel each year for the service of the house of our God:

**33** for the bread set in rows, the continual grain offering and the continual burnt offering, the sabbaths, the new moons, the appointed feasts, the holy things and the sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and for all the work of the house of our God.

**34** We, the priests, the Levites and the people, have also cast lots for the wood offering, to bring it into the house of our God by our fathers' houses at appointed times year by year, to burn on the altar of the LORD our God, as it is written in the instruction.

**35** We will bring the firstfruits of our ground and the firstfruits of every fruit of every tree year by year to the house of the LORD,

**36** and the firstborn of our sons and of our livestock, as it is written in the instruction, and the firstborn of our herds and our flocks, to bring them to the house of our God, to the priests who minister in the house of our God.

**37** We will bring the first of our dough, our contributions, the fruit of every tree, the new wine and the oil to the priests, to the chambers of the house of our God, and the tithe of our ground to the Levites, for it is the Levites who collect the tithes in all our towns where we work.

**38** A priest, a son of Aaron, shall be with the Levites when the Levites collect the tithes, and the Levites shall bring up a tenth of the tithes to the house of our God, to the chambers of the storehouse.

**39** For the sons of Israel and the sons of Levi shall bring the contribution of grain, new wine and oil to the chambers, where the vessels of the sanctuary are, and the priests who minister, and the gatekeepers and the singers. We will not neglect the house of our God.

## Notes

- **vv1–39** — the Hebrew numbers these verses 10:2–40; its 10:1 is the English 9:38. The rendering follows the English numbering.
- **v1 "Nehemiah the governor"** — the man who tells the story is named first among those who set their seal, in the third person, as a document names its signers.
- **v29 "enter into a curse and an oath"** — *ba'im be-alah u-vi-shvu'ah*, as Moses made the people *enter into the covenant of the LORD your God, and into His oath* (Deuteronomy 29:12): the people bind themselves to be cursed if they break it.
- **v31 "We will let the seventh year lie fallow, and cancel every debt"** — two laws joined: *in the seventh year you shall let it rest and lie fallow* (Exodus 23:11), *ve-nittosh*, the same verb; and *every creditor shall release what he has lent* (Deuteronomy 15:2).
- **v32 "a third of a shekel each year"** — the law of the census asked *half a shekel* of each man (Exodus 30:13), once. The agreement makes a smaller gift a yearly one.
- **v34 "the wood offering… as it is written in the instruction"** — what is written is that *the fire on the altar shall be kept burning; it shall not go out. Every morning the priest shall burn wood on it* (Leviticus 6:12). The law says the fire must not go out; the people cast lots for who keeps it fed.
- **v38 "a tenth of the tithes"** — *ma'sar ha-ma'aser*, the law of Numbers: the Levites give *a tithe of the tithe* to the LORD (Numbers 18:26).
- **v39 "We will not neglect the house of our God"** — *lo na'azov*, the last words of the agreement. When Nehemiah comes back, his charge to the officials will be *Why is the house of God neglected?* (13:11), the same verb.

# Judges 8

## Ephraim

**1** The men of Ephraim said to him, "What is this you have done to us, not calling us when you went to fight against Midian?" They quarreled with him fiercely.

**2** He said to them, "What have I done now compared with you? Is not the gleaning of Ephraim better than the grape harvest of Abiezer?

**3** God gave the princes of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb, into your hand. What have I been able to do compared with you?" When he said this, their anger against him subsided.

## Succoth and Penuel

**4** Gideon came to the Jordan and crossed over, he and the three hundred men who were with him, exhausted but still pursuing.

**5** He said to the men of Succoth, "Please give loaves of bread to the people who follow me, for they are exhausted, and I am pursuing Zebah and Zalmunna, the kings of Midian."

**6** The officials of Succoth said, "Are the hands of Zebah and Zalmunna already in your hand, that we should give bread to your army?"

**7** Gideon said, "For that, when the LORD has given Zebah and Zalmunna into my hand, I will thresh your flesh with the thorns of the wilderness and with briers."

**8** He went up from there to Penuel and spoke to them the same way, and the men of Penuel answered him as the men of Succoth had answered.

**9** He said also to the men of Penuel, "When I come back in peace, I will tear down this tower."

**10** Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor, and their army with them, about fifteen thousand men, all who were left of the whole army of the sons of the east; for a hundred and twenty thousand men who drew the sword had fallen.

**11** Gideon went up by the road of the tent-dwellers east of Nobah and Jogbehah, and struck the army while the army was off guard.

**12** Zebah and Zalmunna fled, and he pursued them. He captured the two kings of Midian, Zebah and Zalmunna, and threw the whole army into terror.

**13** Gideon son of Joash returned from the battle by the ascent of Heres.

**14** He caught a young man of the men of Succoth and questioned him, and the young man wrote down for him the officials of Succoth and its elders, seventy-seven men.

**15** He came to the men of Succoth and said, "Here are Zebah and Zalmunna, about whom you taunted me, saying, 'Are the hands of Zebah and Zalmunna already in your hand, that we should give bread to your exhausted men?'"

**16** He took the elders of the city, and thorns of the wilderness and briers, and with them he taught the men of Succoth a lesson.

**17** He tore down the tower of Penuel and killed the men of the city.

## Zebah and Zalmunna

**18** Then he said to Zebah and Zalmunna, "Where are the men you killed at Tabor?" They said, "They were like you, every one of them; each looked like the son of a king."

**19** He said, "They were my brothers, the sons of my mother. As the LORD lives, if you had let them live, I would not kill you."

**20** He said to Jether his firstborn, "Up, kill them!" But the boy did not draw his sword, for he was afraid, because he was still a boy.

**21** Zebah and Zalmunna said, "Get up yourself and strike us down, for as the man is, so is his strength." So Gideon rose and killed Zebah and Zalmunna, and he took the crescent ornaments that were on the necks of their camels.

## The Ephod

**22** The men of Israel said to Gideon, "Rule over us, you and your son and your son's son also, for you have saved us from the hand of Midian."

**23** Gideon said to them, "I will not rule over you, and my son will not rule over you. The LORD will rule over you."

**24** Gideon said to them, "Let me make a request of you: every one of you give me an earring from his spoil." For they had gold earrings, because they were Ishmaelites.

**25** They said, "We will give them." They spread out a cloak, and every man threw on it an earring from his spoil.

**26** The weight of the gold earrings that he requested was a thousand seven hundred shekels of gold, besides the crescent ornaments, the pendants and the purple garments worn by the kings of Midian, and besides the chains that were on the necks of their camels.

**27** Gideon made an ephod of it and set it up in his city, in Ophrah. All Israel gave themselves to it there, and it became a snare to Gideon and to his house.

**28** So Midian was subdued before the sons of Israel, and they did not raise their heads again. The land had rest forty years in the days of Gideon.

## Gideon's Death

**29** Jerubbaal son of Joash went and lived in his own house.

**30** Gideon had seventy sons, his own offspring, for he had many wives.

**31** His secondary wife who was in Shechem also bore him a son, and he named him Abimelech.

**32** Gideon son of Joash died at a good gray age and was buried in the tomb of Joash his father, in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.

**33** As soon as Gideon was dead, the sons of Israel turned again and gave themselves to the Baals, and made Baal-berith their god.

**34** The sons of Israel did not remember the LORD their God, who had delivered them from the hand of all their enemies all around,

**35** and they did not show loyal love to the house of Jerubbaal, that is, Gideon, for all the good he had done for Israel.

## Notes

- **vv7, 16 "I will thresh your flesh… he taught the men of Succoth a lesson"** — in v7 Gideon promises to *thresh*, *dashti*; in v16 the Hebrew has *va-yoda*, **he made them know**. The Greek has *he threshed* in both, reading *va-yadosh*, one letter different, which is what Gideon promised. The text follows the Hebrew.
- **vv22–23 "Rule over us… The LORD will rule over you"** — the first time Israel asks for a ruling house, a father, a son and a grandson. Gideon refuses in the words Samuel will hear from the LORD: *they have rejected Me from being king over them* (1 Samuel 8:7). Yet in v31 he names a son *Abimelech*, **my father is king**, and chapter 9 follows him.

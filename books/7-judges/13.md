# Judges 13

## The Promise

**1** The sons of Israel again did evil in the eyes of the LORD, and the LORD gave them into the hand of the Philistines forty years.

**2** There was a certain man of Zorah, of the clan of the Danites, whose name was Manoah. His wife was barren and had borne no children.

**3** The messenger of the LORD appeared to the woman and said to her, "You are barren and have borne no children, but you shall conceive and bear a son.

**4** So now, be careful: do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean,

**5** for you are pregnant and will bear a son. No razor shall go up on his head, for the boy shall be a Nazirite of God from the womb, and he shall begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines."

**6** The woman went and told her husband, "A man of God came to me, and his appearance was like the appearance of the messenger of God, very fearsome. I did not ask him where he was from, and he did not tell me his name.

**7** He said to me, 'You are pregnant and will bear a son. So now do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean, for the boy shall be a Nazirite of God from the womb to the day of his death.'"

## The Messenger Returns

**8** Manoah entreated the LORD and said, "Please, my Lord, let the man of God whom You sent come to us again and teach us what we are to do for the boy who will be born."

**9** God listened to the voice of Manoah, and the messenger of God came again to the woman as she was sitting in the field. But Manoah her husband was not with her.

**10** The woman ran quickly and told her husband, "The man who came to me the other day has appeared to me."

**11** Manoah rose and followed his wife. He came to the man and said to him, "Are you the man who spoke to the woman?" He said, "I am."

**12** Manoah said, "Now, when your words come true, what shall be the boy's way of life, and what shall he do?"

**13** The messenger of the LORD said to Manoah, "Let the woman be careful about all that I said to her.

**14** She shall not eat anything that comes from the grapevine, or drink wine or strong drink, or eat anything unclean. Let her keep all that I have commanded her."

## The Offering

**15** Manoah said to the messenger of the LORD, "Please let us detain you and prepare a young goat for you."

**16** The messenger of the LORD said to Manoah, "If you detain me, I will not eat your food. But if you prepare a burnt offering, offer it to the LORD." For Manoah did not know that he was the messenger of the LORD.

**17** Manoah said to the messenger of the LORD, "What is your name, so that we may honor you when your words come true?"

**18** The messenger of the LORD said to him, "Why do you ask my name? It is wonderful."

**19** Manoah took the young goat with the grain offering and offered it on the rock to the LORD, and He worked a wonder while Manoah and his wife looked on.

**20** When the flame went up from the altar toward heaven, the messenger of the LORD went up in the flame of the altar, while Manoah and his wife looked on. They fell on their faces to the ground.

**21** The messenger of the LORD did not appear again to Manoah and his wife. Then Manoah knew that he was the messenger of the LORD.

**22** Manoah said to his wife, "We shall surely die, for we have seen God."

**23** His wife said to him, "If the LORD had meant to kill us, He would not have accepted a burnt offering and a grain offering from our hands, or shown us all these things, or let us hear such things now."

## Samson

**24** The woman bore a son and called his name Samson. The boy grew, and the LORD blessed him.

**25** The Spirit of the LORD began to stir him in Mahaneh-dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.

## Notes

- **v1 "gave them into the hand of the Philistines forty years"** — every earlier cycle with a named deliverer has the sons of Israel *cry out* before a deliverer is raised (3:9, 3:15, 4:3, 6:6, 10:10). Here there is no cry. The deliverer is announced before anyone asks.
- **v2 "His wife was barren"** — the mother is never named, while Manoah is. She joins Sarai (Genesis 11:30), Rebekah (25:21) and Rachel (29:31), barren wives who are promised a son. *Zorah* and *Eshtaol* are towns of Dan (Joshua 19:41).
- **v5 "a Nazirite of God from the womb"** — Numbers 6 lays down the Nazirite vow as one a man or woman takes *for the days* of a set period. Here it is not taken at all: it is laid on the child before birth, and v7 has it last *to the day of his death*. *No razor shall go up on his head*, *morah*, is the razor of Numbers 6:5, *ta'ar*, under another name; the Greek has *iron*. The mother carries the first part of the restriction for him (vv4, 7, 14).
- **v5 "he shall begin to save"** — *yachel lehoshia*, **he shall begin**. The verse promises a start, not an end.
- **v14 "anything that comes from the grapevine"** — the messenger's second speech is wider than his first: v4 forbids wine and strong drink; v14 adds every product of the vine, the law of Numbers 6:4, *he shall eat nothing that is made from the grapevine*. The messenger tells Manoah nothing new about the boy; he repeats what the woman already had (v13).
- **v18 "Why do you ask my name? It is wonderful."** — *lamah zeh tish'al lishmi*, the same words the man at the ford speaks to Jacob in Genesis 32:29, and there too no name is given. *Peli* is from *pele*, **wonder, a thing beyond understanding**, the word in *pele yo'ets*, a title in Isaiah 9:5 (English 9:6). It can be read **wonderful** or **beyond understanding**; the Greek has *thaumaston*, **wonderful**.
- **v19 "He worked a wonder"** — *u-mafli la'asot*, from the same root as *peli*. The sentence has no named subject; the closest is the LORD. The Greek reads differently, *he set apart to do*, and does not have a wonder here.
- **vv22–23 "We shall surely die… If the LORD had meant to kill us"** — the fear of Gideon, who also thought he would die after seeing the messenger face to face (6:22–23). There the LORD answers *you shall not die*; here the woman answers, and she reasons from the offering: a God who meant to kill would not have accepted it. She is the one who understands the sign.
- **v24 "Samson"** — *Shimshon*, built on *shemesh*, **sun**. The text gives the name and does not explain it.
- **v25 "began to stir him"** — *lefa'amo*, from *pa'am*, **to strike, to beat**, the root of *his spirit was beaten* in Genesis 41:8. The Spirit does not fill Samson or clothe him (6:34); it beats at him. The Greek has *began to go out with him*. *Mahaneh-dan*, **camp of Dan**, gets its name in 18:12, from a camp of the Danites near Kiriath-jearim.

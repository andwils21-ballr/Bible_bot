# Judges 16

## Gaza

**1** Samson went to Gaza and saw a prostitute there, and he went in to her.

**2** It was told to the Gazites, "Samson has come here." They surrounded the place and lay in wait for him all night at the city gate. They kept quiet all night, saying, "Let us wait until the light of morning; then we will kill him."

**3** Samson lay until midnight. At midnight he rose and took hold of the doors of the city gate and the two posts, pulled them up with the bar, put them on his shoulders and carried them to the top of the hill that faces Hebron.

## Delilah

**4** And it came to pass afterward that he loved a woman in the Valley of Sorek, whose name was Delilah.

**5** The lords of the Philistines came up to her and said to her, "Coax him and see where his great strength lies, and by what means we can overpower him, so that we may bind him to subdue him. Then each of us will give you eleven hundred pieces of silver."

**6** Delilah said to Samson, "Please tell me where your great strength lies, and how you could be bound so that one could subdue you."

**7** Samson said to her, "If they bind me with seven fresh bowstrings that have not dried, I will become weak and be like any other man."

**8** The lords of the Philistines brought her seven fresh bowstrings that had not dried, and she bound him with them.

**9** The men lying in wait were sitting in an inner room. She said to him, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" He snapped the bowstrings as a thread of tow snaps when it touches fire. So the secret of his strength was not known.

**10** Delilah said to Samson, "You have mocked me and told me lies. Now please tell me how you could be bound."

**11** He said to her, "If they bind me with new ropes that have never been used for work, I will become weak and be like any other man."

**12** Delilah took new ropes and bound him with them and said to him, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" The men lying in wait were sitting in the inner room. He snapped them off his arms like thread.

**13** Delilah said to Samson, "Until now you have mocked me and told me lies. Tell me how you could be bound." He said to her, "If you weave the seven locks of my head into the web and fasten it with the pin, I will become weak and be like any other man."

**14** She fastened it with the pin and said to him, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" He woke from his sleep and pulled up the pin, the loom and the web.

**15** She said to him, "How can you say, 'I love you,' when your heart is not with me? Three times you have mocked me and have not told me where your great strength lies."

**16** Because she pressed him hard with her words day after day and urged him, he was worn out to the point of death.

**17** He told her all his heart and said to her, "No razor has ever gone up on my head, for I have been a Nazirite of God from my mother's womb. If I am shaved, my strength will leave me, and I will become weak and be like anyone else."

**18** Delilah saw that he had told her all his heart. She sent and called the lords of the Philistines, saying, "Come up this once, for he has told me all his heart." Then the lords of the Philistines came up to her and brought the silver in their hands.

**19** She lulled him to sleep on her knees and called a man and had him shave off the seven locks of his head. Then she began to subdue him, and his strength left him.

**20** She said, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" He woke from his sleep and said, "I will go out as at other times and shake myself free." But he did not know that the LORD had left him.

## The Prison

**21** The Philistines seized him and gouged out his eyes and brought him down to Gaza. They bound him with bronze shackles, and he was a grinder in the prison.

**22** But the hair of his head began to grow again after it had been shaved.

## Dagon's House

**23** The lords of the Philistines gathered to offer a great sacrifice to Dagon their god and to rejoice. They said, "Our god has given Samson our enemy into our hand."

**24** When the people saw him, they praised their god, for they said, "Our god has given our enemy into our hand, the one who laid waste our land and multiplied our slain."

**25** And it came to pass, when their hearts were merry, that they said, "Call Samson, and let him entertain us." They called Samson out of the prison, and he entertained them. They made him stand between the pillars.

**26** Samson said to the young man who held his hand, "Let me feel the pillars that support the house, so that I may lean on them."

**27** The house was full of men and women, and all the lords of the Philistines were there, and on the roof about three thousand men and women, watching while Samson entertained them.

**28** Samson called to the LORD and said, "Lord GOD, remember me, please, and strengthen me, please, just this once, O God, that with one act of revenge I may take revenge on the Philistines for my two eyes."

**29** Samson took hold of the two middle pillars on which the house rested, and braced himself against them, one with his right hand and one with his left.

**30** Samson said, "Let me die with the Philistines." He pushed with all his strength, and the house fell on the lords and on all the people in it. So the dead whom he killed at his death were more than those he had killed in his life.

**31** His brothers and all his family went down and took him and brought him up and buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol in the tomb of Manoah his father. He had judged Israel twenty years.

## Notes

- **vv1, 4 "a prostitute… a woman in the Valley of Sorek"** — *ishah zonah*, the words used of Rahab (Joshua 2:1). Delilah is not called a Philistine or anything else: the text gives her a name and a valley, and says only that Samson *loved* her. The Philistine lords come to her, and she does what they ask.
- **v5 "eleven hundred pieces of silver"** — each of the lords offers this sum, and there are five (3:3). The same sum, *eleven hundred pieces of silver*, is what Micah takes from his mother in 17:2, at the start of the next story. *Coax*, *patti*, is the verb the Philistines used on Samson's wife in 14:15.
- **vv7, 11, 17 "I will become weak and be like any other man"** — the same words in all three answers. The first two are false; the third is true and adds the reason.
- **v13 "into the web and fasten it with the pin, I will become weak…"** — the Hebrew of v13 stops at *with the web*. It gives no pin and no result, though v14 says *she fastened it with the pin* and vv7 and 11 both give a result. The Greek fills both gaps: *and drive it with the peg into the wall, and I shall be, like one of men, weak*. The text follows the Greek for the pin and the result; *into the wall* is not in v14 and is left out.
- **v16 "worn out to the point of death"** — *va-tiqtsar nafsho la-mut*, **his soul grew short**, the idiom used of the people in Numbers 21:4 and of the LORD in 10:16 (*He could bear the misery of Israel no longer*). Delilah wears Samson out as Israel's misery wore out God.
- **v17 "No razor has ever gone up on my head"** — *morah*, as in 13:5, the words of the messenger before he was born. The hair is the mark of the Nazirite vow (Numbers 6:5, *he shall let the locks of the hair of his head grow long*), and he tells her that the vow is *from my mother's womb*, not one he took.
- **vv19–20 "his strength left him… the LORD had left him"** — the same verb, *sur*, both times: *va-yasar kocho me'alav* in v19 and *YHWH sar me'alav* in v20. He wakes and does not know which has gone.
- **v21 "gouged out his eyes"** — the man who *saw* a woman in Timnah (14:1) and one in Gaza (16:1), and for whom Timnah was *right in my eyes* (14:3, 7), loses his eyes in Gaza, the town where he first went in to a prostitute.
- **v25 "he entertained them"** — *va-yetsacheq*, from *tsachaq*, **to laugh, to play**, the root of Isaac's name. The Greek has *he played*, and adds that they struck him.
- **v28 "for my two eyes"** — Samson's second prayer, after the one at Lehi (15:18), asks for *one* act of revenge for *two* eyes. Israel is not mentioned; the promise of 13:5, *he shall begin to save Israel*, is not spoken by him here.

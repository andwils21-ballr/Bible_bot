# Judges 21

## The Oath at Mizpah

**1** The men of Israel had sworn at Mizpah, "None of us shall give his daughter to Benjamin as a wife."

**2** The people came to Bethel and sat there before God until evening, and they lifted up their voices and wept bitterly.

**3** They said, "LORD, God of Israel, why has this happened in Israel, that today one tribe should be missing from Israel?"

**4** And it came to pass the next day that the people rose early and built an altar there, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings.

**5** The sons of Israel said, "Who from all the tribes of Israel did not come up in the assembly to the LORD?" For they had taken a great oath concerning anyone who did not come up to the LORD at Mizpah: "He shall surely be put to death."

**6** The sons of Israel were sorry for Benjamin their brother and said, "Today one tribe is cut off from Israel.

**7** What shall we do for wives for those who are left, since we have sworn by the LORD not to give them any of our daughters as wives?"

## Jabesh-gilead

**8** They said, "Which one of the tribes of Israel did not come up to the LORD at Mizpah?" No one had come to the camp from Jabesh-gilead, to the assembly.

**9** When the people were counted, not one of the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead was there.

**10** So the congregation sent twelve thousand of the men of valor there and commanded them, "Go and strike the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead with the sword, with the women and the little ones.

**11** This is what you shall do: every male, and every woman who has lain with a male, you shall devote to destruction."

**12** Among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead they found four hundred young virgins who had not known a man by lying with a male, and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.

**13** Then the whole congregation sent word and spoke to the sons of Benjamin who were at the Rock of Rimmon, and proclaimed peace to them.

**14** Benjamin came back at that time, and they gave them the women whom they had kept alive of the women of Jabesh-gilead. But there were not enough for them.

## The Daughters of Shiloh

**15** The people were sorry for Benjamin, because the LORD had made a breach in the tribes of Israel.

**16** The elders of the congregation said, "What shall we do for wives for those who are left, since the women of Benjamin have been destroyed?"

**17** They said, "There must be an inheritance for the survivors of Benjamin, so that a tribe is not wiped out from Israel.

**18** But we cannot give them wives from our daughters, for the sons of Israel have sworn, 'Cursed be anyone who gives a wife to Benjamin.'"

**19** They said, "There is the feast of the LORD at Shiloh year by year, which is north of Bethel, on the east of the highway that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and south of Lebonah."

**20** They commanded the sons of Benjamin, "Go and lie in wait in the vineyards,

**21** and watch. When the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance in the dances, come out of the vineyards and each of you seize a wife for himself from the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.

**22** And it shall come to pass, when their fathers or their brothers come to complain to us, that we will say to them, 'Be gracious to us about them, because we did not take a wife for each man in the war, and you did not give them to them yourselves; otherwise you would now be guilty.'"

**23** The sons of Benjamin did so, and took wives according to their number from the dancers whom they carried off. Then they went and returned to their inheritance, rebuilt the cities and lived in them.

**24** The sons of Israel went from there at that time, each to his tribe and his clan, and each went out from there to his inheritance.

**25** In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes.

## Notes

- **v1 "The men of Israel had sworn at Mizpah"** — the oath was not told in chapter 20. It comes to light only now, when it has become a problem. A second oath follows in v5, and the chapter is spent getting around both without breaking either in words.
- **v3 "why has this happened in Israel"** — the question is put to the LORD, and v15 answers it on His behalf: *the LORD had made a breach in the tribes of Israel*. The people who fought the war now speak of it as something done to them.
- **v6 "were sorry for Benjamin their brother"** — *va-yinnachamu*, the verb the book used of the LORD being *moved to pity* (2:18). *Cut off*, *nigda*, is the verb for hewing down a tree or an Asherah (Deuteronomy 7:5).
- **vv10–12 Jabesh-gilead** — the order repeats Moses' command against Midian almost word for word: *kill every woman who has known a man by lying with a male… the girls who have not known a man by lying with a male, keep alive* (Numbers 31:17–18). Israel now gives that order against a town of Israel. Jabesh-gilead will be the town Saul rescues first (1 Samuel 11:1) and the town that buries him (1 Samuel 31:11).
- **v14 "there were not enough for them"** — four hundred women for six hundred men (20:47).
- **v19 "the feast of the LORD at Shiloh year by year"** — the house of God is at Shiloh (18:31), yet the directions to it are given at length as if the place needed finding. *Year by year*, *mi-yamim yamimah*, is the phrase for Elkanah's yearly journey to Shiloh in 1 Samuel 1:3 and for the yearly lament for Jephthah's daughter (11:40).
- **v21 "seize a wife"** — *chataf*, **to snatch, to catch as prey**. Their fathers did not *give* the daughters of Shiloh, so the oath of v1 is kept.
- **v22 "Be gracious to us about them… otherwise you would now be guilty"** — the plan for answering the fathers is compressed in the Hebrew and hard to follow. The sense is that since the fathers did not give their daughters, they have not broken the oath; the Greek is no clearer.
- **v23 "whom they carried off"** — *gazalu*, the word for robbery.
- **v25 "In those days there was no king in Israel"** — the line of 17:6, 18:1 and 19:1, and the full refrain of 17:6 ends the book. The last five chapters show what *everyone did what was right in his own eyes* looks like, and the book closes on the words without comment.

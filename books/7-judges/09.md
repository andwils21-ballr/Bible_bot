# Judges 9

## Abimelech Made King

**1** Abimelech son of Jerubbaal went to Shechem, to his mother's brothers, and spoke to them and to the whole clan of his mother's father's house, saying,

**2** "Please say in the hearing of all the lords of Shechem, 'Which is better for you: that seventy men, all the sons of Jerubbaal, rule over you, or that one man rule over you?' Remember also that I am your bone and your flesh."

**3** His mother's brothers spoke all these words about him in the hearing of all the lords of Shechem, and their hearts inclined to follow Abimelech, for they said, "He is our brother."

**4** They gave him seventy pieces of silver from the house of Baal-berith, and with it Abimelech hired worthless and reckless men, who followed him.

**5** He went to his father's house at Ophrah and killed his brothers, the sons of Jerubbaal, seventy men, on one stone. But Jotham, the youngest son of Jerubbaal, was left, because he hid himself.

**6** All the lords of Shechem and all Beth-millo gathered together, and they went and made Abimelech king by the oak of the pillar at Shechem.

## Jotham's Fable

**7** When they told Jotham, he went and stood on the top of Mount Gerizim, and lifted up his voice and called out. He said to them, "Listen to me, lords of Shechem, so that God may listen to you.

**8** The trees once went out to anoint a king over themselves. They said to the olive tree, 'Reign over us.'

**9** But the olive tree said to them, 'Should I give up my richness, by which gods and men are honored, and go to sway over the trees?'

**10** Then the trees said to the fig tree, 'You come and reign over us.'

**11** But the fig tree said to them, 'Should I give up my sweetness and my good fruit, and go to sway over the trees?'

**12** Then the trees said to the vine, 'You come and reign over us.'

**13** But the vine said to them, 'Should I give up my new wine, which cheers gods and men, and go to sway over the trees?'

**14** Then all the trees said to the bramble, 'You come and reign over us.'

**15** The bramble said to the trees, 'If in truth you are anointing me king over you, come and take refuge in my shade. But if not, let fire come out of the bramble and devour the cedars of Lebanon.'

**16** "And now, if you have acted in truth and wholeness in making Abimelech king, and if you have dealt well with Jerubbaal and his house, and have done to him as his deeds deserved —

**17** for my father fought for you and risked his life and delivered you from the hand of Midian,

**18** but you have risen against my father's house today and killed his sons, seventy men, on one stone, and made Abimelech, the son of his slave woman, king over the lords of Shechem, because he is your brother —

**19** if, then, you have acted in truth and wholeness with Jerubbaal and his house this day, rejoice in Abimelech, and let him also rejoice in you.

**20** But if not, let fire come out from Abimelech and devour the lords of Shechem and Beth-millo, and let fire come out from the lords of Shechem and from Beth-millo and devour Abimelech."

**21** Then Jotham ran away and fled, and went to Beer and lived there, because of Abimelech his brother.

## An Evil Spirit

**22** Abimelech ruled over Israel three years.

**23** Then God sent an evil spirit between Abimelech and the lords of Shechem, and the lords of Shechem dealt treacherously with Abimelech,

**24** so that the violence done to the seventy sons of Jerubbaal might come, and their blood be laid on Abimelech their brother, who killed them, and on the lords of Shechem, who strengthened his hands to kill his brothers.

**25** The lords of Shechem set men in ambush against him on the tops of the mountains, and they robbed everyone who passed by them along the road. It was told to Abimelech.

**26** Gaal son of Ebed came with his brothers and moved into Shechem, and the lords of Shechem put their trust in him.

**27** They went out into the field, harvested their vineyards, trod the grapes and held a festival. They went into the house of their god, ate and drank, and cursed Abimelech.

**28** Gaal son of Ebed said, "Who is Abimelech, and who is Shechem, that we should serve him? Is he not the son of Jerubbaal, and is not Zebul his officer? Serve the men of Hamor, the father of Shechem! Why should we serve him?

**29** If only this people were under my hand! Then I would remove Abimelech." Then he said to Abimelech, "Increase your army and come out."

**30** When Zebul the ruler of the city heard the words of Gaal son of Ebed, his anger burned.

**31** He sent messengers to Abimelech secretly, saying, "Gaal son of Ebed and his brothers have come to Shechem, and they are stirring up the city against you.

**32** So rise by night, you and the people who are with you, and lie in ambush in the field.

**33** And it shall come to pass in the morning, as soon as the sun rises, that you shall rise early and rush upon the city. When he and the people who are with him come out against you, you may do to him whatever you can."

## The Fall of Shechem

**34** So Abimelech and all the people who were with him rose by night and lay in ambush against Shechem in four companies.

**35** Gaal son of Ebed went out and stood at the entrance of the city gate, and Abimelech and the people who were with him rose from the ambush.

**36** When Gaal saw the people, he said to Zebul, "People are coming down from the tops of the mountains!" Zebul said to him, "You are seeing the shadow of the mountains as if they were men."

**37** Gaal spoke again and said, "People are coming down from the navel of the land, and one company is coming by the road of the Diviners' Oak."

**38** Zebul said to him, "Where is your mouth now, you who said, 'Who is Abimelech, that we should serve him?' Is not this the people you despised? Go out now and fight against them."

**39** So Gaal went out at the head of the lords of Shechem and fought against Abimelech.

**40** Abimelech chased him, and he fled before him, and many fell wounded up to the entrance of the gate.

**41** Abimelech stayed at Arumah, and Zebul drove out Gaal and his brothers so that they could not live in Shechem.

**42** And it came to pass on the next day that the people went out into the field, and it was told to Abimelech.

**43** He took the people, divided them into three companies and lay in ambush in the field. When he saw the people coming out of the city, he rose against them and struck them.

**44** Abimelech and the company that was with him rushed forward and stood at the entrance of the city gate, while the two other companies rushed on all who were in the field and struck them.

**45** Abimelech fought against the city all that day. He took the city and killed the people who were in it, tore down the city and sowed it with salt.

**46** When all the lords of the tower of Shechem heard of it, they went into the stronghold of the house of El-berith.

**47** It was told to Abimelech that all the lords of the tower of Shechem had gathered together.

**48** Abimelech went up Mount Zalmon, he and all the people who were with him. Abimelech took an axe in his hand, cut down a branch from the trees, lifted it and put it on his shoulder. He said to the people who were with him, "What you have seen me do, hurry and do as I have done."

**49** So all the people also cut down each one his branch and followed Abimelech. They put them against the stronghold and set the stronghold on fire over them, so that all the people of the tower of Shechem also died, about a thousand men and women.

## The Millstone at Thebez

**50** Then Abimelech went to Thebez, camped against Thebez and took it.

**51** But there was a strong tower in the middle of the city, and all the men and women and all the lords of the city fled there. They shut themselves in and went up to the roof of the tower.

**52** Abimelech came to the tower and fought against it, and came near the entrance of the tower to burn it with fire.

**53** A certain woman threw an upper millstone on Abimelech's head and crushed his skull.

**54** He called quickly to the young man who carried his armor and said to him, "Draw your sword and kill me, or they will say of me, 'A woman killed him.'" So his young man ran him through, and he died.

**55** When the men of Israel saw that Abimelech was dead, they went each to his place.

**56** So God paid back the evil of Abimelech, which he had done to his father by killing his seventy brothers.

**57** God also brought all the evil of the men of Shechem back on their heads, and the curse of Jotham son of Jerubbaal came upon them.

## Notes

- **v2 "the lords of Shechem"** — *ba'alei Shekhem*, **the owners of Shechem**, its landholders. *Ba'al* is the word for owner and master, the same as the god whose house pays for the murders (v4, Baal-berith). The chapter says *ba'alei Shekhem* sixteen times.
- **v2 "I am your bone and your flesh"** — what Laban said to Jacob (Genesis 29:14). Abimelech's claim on Shechem is his mother's blood, and the Shechemites answer in kind: *He is our brother* (v3).
- **v6 "the oak of the pillar at Shechem"** — *mutsav*, **something set up**, a standing stone. At Shechem Joshua *set up a great stone… under the terebinth* as a witness against forsaking the LORD (Joshua 24:26). Abimelech is crowned at Shechem beside an oak and a standing stone.
- **vv8–15 Jotham's fable** — the olive, the fig and the vine each refuse to *sway over the trees*, *lanua*, **to wave, to totter**: ruling is pictured as swaying uselessly above the others. Only the bramble accepts, and offers *shade* it does not have. *To anoint* is *limshoach*, the verb behind *mashiach*, **anointed one**; this is its first use for a king.
- **v18 "the son of his slave woman"** — 8:31 calls his mother Gideon's *concubine*; Jotham uses the harsher *amah*, a slave woman.
- **v23 "God sent an evil spirit"** — *ruach ra'ah*. The fire Jotham called for in v20 comes as a spirit of distrust between the king and the men who made him.
- **v28 "Serve the men of Hamor, the father of Shechem!"** — Hamor was the city's founding father in Genesis 34:2. Gaal, **son of Ebed**, **son of a slave**, calls the old Canaanite line the rightful masters of Shechem, not the son of Jerubbaal.
- **v37 "the navel of the land"** — *tabbur ha-arets*, a height at the center of the country; Ezekiel 38:12 uses the same words of Israel's land. *The Diviners' Oak*, *elon me'onenim*, is named for the *me'onen*, the *one who reads omens* forbidden in Deuteronomy 18:10.
- **v46 "the house of El-berith"** — **God of the covenant**, the same house called *Baal-berith* in 8:33 and 9:4. Its tower becomes a furnace.
- **vv53–54 "A certain woman threw an upper millstone"** — *pelach rekhev*, the top stone of a hand mill. Abimelech dies as Sisera did, by a woman's hand, and asks for the sword to hide it; the story was still told in David's day (2 Samuel 11:21, *Did not a woman throw an upper millstone on him from the wall?*).

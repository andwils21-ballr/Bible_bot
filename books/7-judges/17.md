# Judges 17

## The Silver

**1** There was a man of the hill country of Ephraim whose name was Micah.

**2** He said to his mother, "The eleven hundred pieces of silver that were taken from you, about which you uttered a curse and also spoke it in my hearing — the silver is with me. I took it." His mother said, "Blessed be my son by the LORD."

**3** He gave the eleven hundred pieces of silver back to his mother, and his mother said, "I solemnly consecrate the silver to the LORD from my hand for my son, to make a carved image and a cast image. So now I will give it back to you."

**4** When he gave the silver back to his mother, his mother took two hundred pieces of silver and gave it to the silversmith, and he made it into a carved image and a cast image, and it was in the house of Micah.

**5** The man Micah had a house of God. He made an ephod and household gods, and ordained one of his sons, who became his priest.

**6** In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes.

## The Levite

**7** There was a young man from Bethlehem in Judah, of the clan of Judah. He was a Levite, and he was living there as a guest.

**8** The man left the town of Bethlehem in Judah to live as a guest wherever he could find a place. He came to the hill country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, as he went on his way.

**9** Micah said to him, "Where do you come from?" He said to him, "I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah, and I am going to live as a guest wherever I can find a place."

**10** Micah said to him, "Stay with me, and be a father and a priest to me, and I will give you ten pieces of silver a year, a set of clothes and your food." So the Levite went in.

**11** The Levite agreed to stay with the man, and the young man became to him like one of his sons.

**12** Micah ordained the Levite, and the young man became his priest, and he was in the house of Micah.

**13** Micah said, "Now I know that the LORD will do me good, because I have a Levite as my priest."

## Notes

- **vv1, 5 "Micah"** — v1 spells his name in full, *Mikhayehu*, **who is like the LORD?**, and from v5 on it is the short form, *Mikhah*. The man whose name asks *who is like the LORD?* makes an image of Him.
- **v3 "I solemnly consecrate the silver to the LORD… to make a carved image and a cast image"** — *haqdesh hiqdashti*, a doubled verb. The silver is given to the LORD for the very thing Deuteronomy 27:15 curses: *Cursed is the man who makes a carved or cast image.* She means it as devotion.
- **v5 "an ephod and household gods"** — *ephod u-terafim*. Gideon made an ephod that became a snare (8:27); *terafim* are the *household gods* Rachel stole from Laban (Genesis 31:19). *Ordained* is *va-yemalle et yad*, **filled the hand**, the words of Exodus 28:41 for Aaron's sons.
- **v6 "everyone did what was right in his own eyes"** — Moses warned against this in Deuteronomy 12:8: *You shall not do as we are doing here today, each man whatever is right in his own eyes.* The line returns at 21:25 and closes the book; Samson chose his wife the same way (14:3).

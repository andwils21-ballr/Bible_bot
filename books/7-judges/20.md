# Judges 20

## The Assembly at Mizpah

**1** Then all the sons of Israel came out, from Dan to Beersheba and the land of Gilead, and the congregation assembled as one man to the LORD at Mizpah.

**2** The leaders of all the people, of all the tribes of Israel, presented themselves in the assembly of the people of God, four hundred thousand foot soldiers who drew the sword.

**3** The sons of Benjamin heard that the sons of Israel had gone up to Mizpah. The sons of Israel said, "Tell us, how did this evil happen?"

**4** The Levite, the husband of the murdered woman, answered and said, "I came to Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin, I and my secondary wife, to spend the night.

**5** The lords of Gibeah rose against me and surrounded the house against me by night. They meant to kill me, and they violated my secondary wife, and she died.

**6** I took my secondary wife and cut her in pieces and sent her throughout all the country of Israel's inheritance, because they have committed lewdness and outrage in Israel.

**7** All of you are sons of Israel. Give your word and counsel here."

**8** All the people rose as one man, saying, "None of us will go to his tent, and none of us will turn back to his house.

**9** Now this is what we will do to Gibeah: we will go up against it by lot.

**10** We will take ten men of a hundred from all the tribes of Israel, and a hundred of a thousand, and a thousand of ten thousand, to bring provisions for the people, so that when they come to Gibeah of Benjamin they may do to it for all the outrage it has committed in Israel."

**11** So all the men of Israel gathered against the city, united as one man.

## Benjamin Refuses

**12** The tribes of Israel sent men through all the tribe of Benjamin, saying, "What is this evil that has happened among you?

**13** Now give up the men, the worthless men in Gibeah, so that we may put them to death and purge the evil from Israel." But the sons of Benjamin would not listen to the voice of their brothers, the sons of Israel.

**14** The sons of Benjamin gathered from the cities to Gibeah to go out to battle against the sons of Israel.

**15** On that day the sons of Benjamin mustered from the cities twenty-six thousand men who drew the sword, besides the inhabitants of Gibeah, who mustered seven hundred chosen men.

**16** Among all these people were seven hundred chosen men who were left-handed; every one could sling a stone at a hair and not miss.

**17** The men of Israel, apart from Benjamin, mustered four hundred thousand men who drew the sword; all of these were men of war.

## Two Defeats

**18** They rose and went up to Bethel and inquired of God. The sons of Israel said, "Who shall go up first for us to battle against the sons of Benjamin?" The LORD said, "Judah first."

**19** The sons of Israel rose in the morning and camped against Gibeah.

**20** The men of Israel went out to battle against Benjamin, and the men of Israel drew up for battle against them at Gibeah.

**21** The sons of Benjamin came out of Gibeah and struck down twenty-two thousand men of Israel to the ground that day.

**22** But the people, the men of Israel, took courage and again drew up for battle in the place where they had drawn up on the first day.

**23** The sons of Israel went up and wept before the LORD until evening, and they inquired of the LORD, saying, "Shall we again go near to battle against the sons of Benjamin my brother?" The LORD said, "Go up against him."

**24** So the sons of Israel came near against the sons of Benjamin on the second day.

**25** Benjamin went out against them from Gibeah on the second day and struck down eighteen thousand more of the sons of Israel to the ground; all of these drew the sword.

**26** Then all the sons of Israel, the whole people, went up and came to Bethel and wept. They sat there before the LORD and fasted that day until evening, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD.

**27** The sons of Israel inquired of the LORD — the ark of the covenant of God was there in those days,

**28** and Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron, stood before it in those days — saying, "Shall I go out once more to battle against the sons of Benjamin my brother, or shall I stop?" The LORD said, "Go up, for tomorrow I will give him into your hand."

## The Ambush

**29** So Israel set men in ambush all around Gibeah.

**30** The sons of Israel went up against the sons of Benjamin on the third day and drew up against Gibeah as at the other times.

**31** The sons of Benjamin went out against the people and were drawn away from the city. As at the other times, they began to strike down some of the people on the highways, one of which goes up to Bethel and the other to Gibeah in the open country, about thirty men of Israel.

**32** The sons of Benjamin said, "They are being struck down before us as at first." But the sons of Israel said, "Let us flee and draw them away from the city to the highways."

**33** All the men of Israel rose from their place and drew up at Baal-tamar, and the men of Israel in ambush burst out of their place, from the open ground of Geba.

**34** Ten thousand chosen men from all Israel came against Gibeah, and the battle was hard, but the Benjaminites did not know that disaster was close upon them.

**35** The LORD struck Benjamin before Israel, and the sons of Israel destroyed twenty-five thousand one hundred men of Benjamin that day; all of these drew the sword.

**36** The sons of Benjamin saw that they were struck down. The men of Israel had given ground to Benjamin, because they trusted the ambush they had set against Gibeah.

**37** The men in ambush hurried and rushed upon Gibeah; the ambush spread out and struck the whole city with the sword.

**38** Now the appointed signal between the men of Israel and the men in ambush was that they should make a great cloud of smoke rise up from the city.

**39** When the men of Israel turned in the battle, Benjamin had begun to strike down about thirty of the men of Israel, for they said, "They are surely struck down before us, as in the first battle."

**40** But the signal began to rise from the city, a column of smoke, and Benjamin looked behind them, and the whole city was going up to heaven.

**41** Then the men of Israel turned, and the men of Benjamin were terrified, for they saw that disaster had come upon them.

**42** They turned before the men of Israel toward the way of the wilderness, but the battle overtook them, and those who came out of the cities destroyed them in the middle of them.

**43** They surrounded Benjamin, pursued them and trod them down at their resting place, as far as opposite Gibeah toward the sunrise.

**44** Eighteen thousand men of Benjamin fell; all of these were men of valor.

**45** They turned and fled toward the wilderness to the Rock of Rimmon, and Israel gleaned five thousand men of them on the highways, and pursued them hard as far as Gidom and struck down two thousand men of them.

**46** So all who fell of Benjamin that day were twenty-five thousand men who drew the sword; all of these were men of valor.

**47** But six hundred men turned and fled toward the wilderness to the Rock of Rimmon, and they stayed at the Rock of Rimmon four months.

**48** The men of Israel turned back against the sons of Benjamin and struck them with the sword, the whole city, people and animals, everything that was found. All the cities that were found they also set on fire.

## Notes

- **v1 "from Dan to Beersheba"** — the first use of the phrase for the whole land, north to south. Dan is the city just founded in 18:29. *As one man* returns in vv8 and 11: Israel is never so united in the book as against one of its own tribes.
- **vv4–6 the Levite's account** — he says the men of Gibeah *meant to kill me* (19:22 says they asked *to know* him), and he does not say that he put his secondary wife out to them himself (19:25). *Lewdness and outrage*, *zimmah u-nevalah*: *nevalah* is the old man's word in 19:23.
- **v13 "purge the evil from Israel"** — the formula of Deuteronomy, *So you shall purge the evil from among you* (13:5, 17:7). Benjamin refuses to give up the guilty, and the whole tribe is drawn in.
- **vv15–16 the numbers of Benjamin** — the Hebrew has 26,000 besides 700 of Gibeah; v35 counts 25,100 dead, v46 about 25,000, and v47 leaves 600. The totals do not agree exactly, and the Greek of v15 has 23,000. The text gives the Hebrew figures as they stand.
- **v16 "left-handed… and not miss"** — *itter yad-yemino*, the phrase used of Ehud the Benjaminite (3:15). *Not miss* is *lo yachati*, from *chata*, **to miss the mark**, the ordinary Hebrew word for sin. The Greek has *ambidextrous*.
- **v18 "Who shall go up first… Judah first"** — the question and answer of 1:1–2, where the enemy was the Canaanites. Now the enemy is Benjamin.
- **vv27–28 "the ark… Phinehas son of Eleazar"** — the priest who led the embassy of Joshua 22 is still serving. His presence places this story early in the time of the judges, though the book tells it last.
- **vv29–41 the ambush** — the plan that took Ai (Joshua 8): a feigned flight, the enemy drawn from the city, the ambush breaking in, and smoke that tells the fleeing side the city is lost (*the smoke of the city was going up to the heavens*, Joshua 8:20). Israel now uses it on Israel.
- **v40 "the whole city was going up to heaven"** — *kelil ha-ir*, **the city whole**, the word of Deuteronomy 13:16 for a town under the ban burned *whole, to the LORD your God*.
- **vv43, 45 "at their resting place… gleaned"** — *menuchah* can be **resting place** or a place name, *Menuhah*; the Greek has *from Noua*. *Va-ye'olleluhu*, **they gleaned them**, is the word for picking over a harvested field.

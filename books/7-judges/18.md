# Judges 18

## The Scouts

**1** In those days there was no king in Israel. In those days the tribe of the Danites was seeking an inheritance to live in, for until that day no inheritance had fallen to them among the tribes of Israel.

**2** The sons of Dan sent five men from their clan, from their whole number, men of valor from Zorah and Eshtaol, to spy out the land and explore it. They said to them, "Go, explore the land." They came to the hill country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, and spent the night there.

**3** When they were at the house of Micah, they recognized the voice of the young Levite, and they turned aside there and said to him, "Who brought you here? What are you doing in this place? What do you have here?"

**4** He said to them, "Micah did this and that for me, and he has hired me, and I have become his priest."

**5** They said to him, "Please inquire of God, so that we may know whether the journey we are on will succeed."

**6** The priest said to them, "Go in peace. The journey you are on is before the LORD."

**7** The five men went on and came to Laish. They saw the people who were in it living in security, in the manner of the Sidonians, quiet and unsuspecting. There was no one in the land who put them to shame in anything or held power over them. They were far from the Sidonians and had no dealings with anyone.

**8** They came to their brothers at Zorah and Eshtaol, and their brothers said to them, "What have you found?"

**9** They said, "Arise, let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and it is very good. Will you sit still? Do not be slow to go and enter and take possession of the land.

**10** When you go, you will come to an unsuspecting people, and the land is spacious. God has given it into your hand, a place where there is no lack of anything that is on the earth."

## Micah's Gods Taken

**11** Six hundred men of the clan of the Danites set out from there, from Zorah and Eshtaol, armed with weapons of war.

**12** They went up and camped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah. That is why that place is called Mahaneh-dan to this day; it is west of Kiriath-jearim.

**13** From there they passed on to the hill country of Ephraim and came to the house of Micah.

**14** Then the five men who had gone to spy out the land of Laish said to their brothers, "Do you know that in these houses there are an ephod, household gods, a carved image and a cast image? So now consider what you will do."

**15** They turned aside there and came to the house of the young Levite, at the house of Micah, and greeted him.

**16** The six hundred men of the sons of Dan, armed with their weapons of war, stood at the entrance of the gate.

**17** The five men who had gone to spy out the land went up and went in and took the carved image, the ephod, the household gods and the cast image, while the priest stood at the entrance of the gate with the six hundred men armed with weapons of war.

**18** When these went into Micah's house and took the carved image, the ephod, the household gods and the cast image, the priest said to them, "What are you doing?"

**19** They said to him, "Be quiet. Put your hand over your mouth and come with us, and be a father and a priest to us. Is it better for you to be priest to the house of one man, or to be priest to a tribe and a clan in Israel?"

**20** The priest's heart was glad. He took the ephod, the household gods and the carved image, and went in among the people.

**21** They turned and went, and put the little ones, the livestock and the goods in front of them.

**22** When they were a good way from the house of Micah, the men who were in the houses near Micah's house were called out, and they overtook the sons of Dan.

**23** They shouted to the sons of Dan, who turned around and said to Micah, "What is the matter with you, that you have called out your men?"

**24** He said, "You have taken my gods that I made, and the priest, and gone away. What more do I have? How can you say to me, 'What is the matter with you?'"

**25** The sons of Dan said to him, "Do not let your voice be heard among us, or angry men will fall on you, and you will lose your life and the lives of your household."

**26** Then the sons of Dan went on their way. When Micah saw that they were too strong for him, he turned and went back to his house.

## Laish Becomes Dan

**27** They took what Micah had made and the priest he had, and came to Laish, to a people quiet and unsuspecting, and struck them with the sword and burned the city with fire.

**28** There was no one to save them, because it was far from Sidon and they had no dealings with anyone. It was in the valley that belongs to Beth-rehob. They rebuilt the city and lived in it.

**29** They called the name of the city Dan, after the name of Dan their father, who was born to Israel. But the name of the city was formerly Laish.

**30** The sons of Dan set up the carved image for themselves, and Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Manasseh, he and his sons were priests to the tribe of the Danites until the day the land went into exile.

**31** So they set up Micah's carved image that he had made for themselves, all the days that the house of God was at Shiloh.

## Notes

- **vv7, 10, 27 "quiet and unsuspecting"** — *shoqet u-voteach*, said three times of Laish. The people of Laish are *far from the Sidonians* and have no allies; that is what makes them an easy target. *No one who put them to shame… or held power over them* is obscure in the Hebrew (*yoresh etser*), and the Greek is no clearer.
- **v30 "Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Manasseh"** — Moses' son was named Gershom (Exodus 2:22), and *Manasseh*, *Mnshh*, differs from *Moses*, *Mshh*, by one letter, *n*. The Hebrew and the Greek both read *Manasseh*, and the text follows them. *Until the day the land went into exile* reaches far past the book's own time.

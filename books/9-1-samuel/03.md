# 1 Samuel 3

## The Call

**1** The boy Samuel was ministering to the LORD before Eli. The word of the LORD was rare in those days; there was no vision breaking through.

**2** And it came to pass at that time that Eli, whose eyes had begun to grow dim so that he could not see, was lying down in his place.

**3** The lamp of God had not yet gone out, and Samuel was lying down in the temple of the LORD, where the ark of God was.

**4** Then the LORD called Samuel, and he said, "Here I am."

**5** He ran to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you called me." But he said, "I did not call; lie down again." So he went and lay down.

**6** The LORD called again, "Samuel!" Samuel got up and went to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you called me." But he said, "I did not call, my son; lie down again."

**7** Samuel did not yet know the LORD, and the word of the LORD had not yet been revealed to him.

**8** The LORD called Samuel again, the third time. He got up and went to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you called me." Then Eli understood that the LORD was calling the boy.

**9** Eli said to Samuel, "Go, lie down. And it shall come to pass, if He calls you, that you shall say, 'Speak, LORD, for Your servant is listening.'" So Samuel went and lay down in his place.

**10** The LORD came and stood there and called as at the other times, "Samuel! Samuel!" Samuel said, "Speak, for Your servant is listening."

## The Word Against Eli's House

**11** The LORD said to Samuel, "I am about to do a thing in Israel at which both ears of everyone who hears it will tingle.

**12** On that day I will carry out against Eli all that I have spoken concerning his house, from beginning to end.

**13** I have told him that I am about to judge his house forever, for the iniquity that he knew, because his sons were bringing a curse on themselves, and he did not restrain them.

**14** Therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli that the iniquity of Eli's house shall not be atoned for by sacrifice or offering forever."

**15** Samuel lay until morning; then he opened the doors of the house of the LORD. Samuel was afraid to tell the vision to Eli.

**16** But Eli called Samuel and said, "Samuel, my son." He said, "Here I am."

**17** Eli said, "What is the word that He spoke to you? Please do not hide it from me. May God do so to you and more also, if you hide from me anything of all the word that He spoke to you."

**18** So Samuel told him everything and hid nothing from him. Eli said, "It is the LORD. Let Him do what is good in His eyes."

## A Prophet of the LORD

**19** Samuel grew, and the LORD was with him and let none of his words fall to the ground.

**20** All Israel from Dan to Beersheba knew that Samuel was established as a prophet of the LORD.

**21** The LORD appeared again at Shiloh, for the LORD revealed Himself to Samuel at Shiloh by the word of the LORD.

## Notes

- **v1 "The word of the LORD was rare"** — *yaqar*, **precious, scarce**. *No vision breaking through*, *niferats*, from *parats*, to break out, as water or a crowd does.
- **vv2–3 "whose eyes had begun to grow dim… The lamp of God had not yet gone out"** — Eli's sight is failing, as Isaac's was (Genesis 27:1), and the lamp that was to burn *from evening until morning before the LORD* (Exodus 27:21) is still lit: it is the night's last hours, and not yet dark in either sense.
- **vv4, 10 "Here I am… Samuel! Samuel!"** — *hinneni*, the answer of Abraham at the binding of Isaac and of Moses at the bush, and the name called twice, as they were called (Genesis 22:11, Exodus 3:4).
- **v7 "Samuel did not yet know the LORD"** — said without blame. Eli's sons *did not know the LORD* (2:12) and were priests; the boy does not know Him yet, because the word has not come to him.
- **v11 "both ears… will tingle"** — *titsallenah*, the ringing of the ears at news too heavy to hear. 4:12–22 tells the news: the ark taken, both sons dead, Eli dead.
- **v13 "his sons were bringing a curse on themselves… he did not restrain them"** — *meqallelim lahem*. The Greek reads *his sons were speaking evil of God*, and *lahem*, **themselves**, differs from *Elohim*, **God**, by two letters. The text follows the Hebrew. *Did not restrain*, *lo kihah*, is from the root of Eli's *dim* eyes in v2: he did not see, and he did not check them.
- **v14 "shall not be atoned for by sacrifice or offering"** — the sons abused the sacrifices (2:13–17), and sacrifice will not cover them.
- **v18 "It is the LORD. Let Him do what is good in His eyes"** — Eli accepts the word against his own house without protest.
- **v19 "let none of his words fall to the ground"** — every word Samuel spoke came true. The same was said of the LORD's promises to Israel: *not one word failed* (Joshua 21:45, 23:14).
- **v20 "established as a prophet"** — *ne'eman*, **faithful, made sure**, the word of the man of God's promise, *I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest* (2:35). The Greek adds a closing summary: Samuel was trusted as a prophet *to all Israel from one end of the earth to the other*, while Eli's sons kept to their evil way.

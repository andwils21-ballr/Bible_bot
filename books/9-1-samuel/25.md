# 1 Samuel 25

## The Death of Samuel

**1** Samuel died. All Israel gathered and mourned for him, and they buried him at his house in Ramah. Then David arose and went down to the wilderness of Paran.

## Nabal

**2** There was a man in Maon whose business was at Carmel. The man was very rich; he had three thousand sheep and a thousand goats. He was shearing his sheep at Carmel.

**3** The name of the man was Nabal, and the name of his wife Abigail. The woman was of good understanding and beautiful, but the man was harsh and evil in his dealings. He was a Calebite.

**4** David heard in the wilderness that Nabal was shearing his sheep.

**5** So David sent ten young men. David said to the young men, "Go up to Carmel, go to Nabal and greet him in my name.

**6** Say this: 'Long life to you! Peace to you, peace to your house, and peace to all that you have.

**7** Now I have heard that you have shearers. Your shepherds have been with us, and we did not humiliate them, and nothing of theirs was missing all the time they were in Carmel.

**8** Ask your young men, and they will tell you. So let my young men find favor in your eyes, for we have come on a feast day. Please give whatever you have at hand to your servants and to your son David.'"

**9** When David's young men came, they said all these words to Nabal in David's name, and they waited.

**10** Nabal answered David's servants, "Who is David? Who is the son of Jesse? There are many servants these days who are breaking away, each from his master.

**11** Shall I take my bread and my water and the meat I have slaughtered for my shearers and give it to men who come from I do not know where?"

**12** So David's young men turned on their way and went back, and came and told him all these words.

**13** David said to his men, "Every man strap on his sword!" Every man strapped on his sword, and David also strapped on his sword. About four hundred men went up after David, and two hundred stayed with the baggage.

## Abigail

**14** One of the young men told Abigail, Nabal's wife, "David sent messengers from the wilderness to greet our master, and he flew at them.

**15** Yet the men were very good to us. We were not humiliated, and we missed nothing all the time we went about with them when we were in the field.

**16** They were a wall to us both by night and by day, all the time we were with them keeping the sheep.

**17** So know and consider what you should do, for evil has been resolved against our master and against all his house. He is such a worthless man that no one can speak to him."

**18** Then Abigail hurried and took two hundred loaves, two skins of wine, five sheep ready dressed, five seahs of roasted grain, a hundred clusters of raisins and two hundred cakes of figs, and loaded them on donkeys.

**19** She said to her young men, "Go on ahead of me; I am coming after you." But she did not tell her husband Nabal.

**20** As she rode on the donkey and came down under cover of the mountain, David and his men were coming down toward her, and she met them.

**21** Now David had said, "It was all for nothing that I guarded everything this man has in the wilderness, so that nothing of all he has was missing. He has repaid me evil for good.

**22** May God do so to the enemies of David, and more also, if by morning I leave so much as one male of all who belong to him."

**23** When Abigail saw David, she hurried and got down from the donkey, fell on her face before David and bowed to the ground.

**24** She fell at his feet and said, "On me alone, my lord, be the guilt. Please let your servant speak in your ears, and hear the words of your servant.

**25** Please let my lord not set his heart on this worthless man, Nabal, for as his name is, so is he: Nabal is his name, and folly is with him. But I, your servant, did not see the young men of my lord whom you sent.

**26** And now, my lord, as the LORD lives and as your soul lives, since the LORD has kept you from coming into bloodguilt and from saving yourself with your own hand, and now let your enemies and those who seek evil against my lord be like Nabal.

**27** Now let this gift that your servant has brought to my lord be given to the young men who follow my lord.

**28** Please forgive the offense of your servant. For the LORD will surely make my lord a sure house, because my lord is fighting the battles of the LORD, and evil shall not be found in you all your days.

**29** If a man rises up to pursue you and to seek your life, the life of my lord shall be bound in the bundle of the living with the LORD your God, and the lives of your enemies He shall sling out as from the hollow of a sling.

**30** And it shall come to pass, when the LORD has done for my lord all the good He has spoken concerning you, and has appointed you leader over Israel,

**31** that this will not be a stumbling block to you, or a burden on my lord's heart, that you shed blood without cause or that my lord saved himself. When the LORD has dealt well with my lord, remember your servant."

**32** David said to Abigail, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who sent you this day to meet me!

**33** Blessed be your discernment, and blessed be you, who have kept me this day from coming into bloodguilt and from saving myself with my own hand!

**34** For truly, as the LORD, the God of Israel, lives, who has kept me from harming you, if you had not hurried and come to meet me, surely by morning light there would not have been left to Nabal so much as one male."

**35** Then David received from her hand what she had brought him, and he said to her, "Go up in peace to your house. See, I have listened to your voice and granted your request."

## The Death of Nabal

**36** Abigail came to Nabal, and there he was, holding a feast in his house like the feast of a king. Nabal's heart was merry within him, and he was very drunk, so she told him nothing at all until morning light.

**37** And it came to pass in the morning, when the wine had gone out of Nabal, that his wife told him these things, and his heart died within him, and he became like a stone.

**38** And it came to pass about ten days later that the LORD struck Nabal, and he died.

**39** When David heard that Nabal was dead, he said, "Blessed be the LORD, who has pleaded the cause of my disgrace at the hand of Nabal, and has kept His servant back from evil. The LORD has returned the evil of Nabal on his own head." Then David sent and spoke to Abigail, to take her as his wife.

**40** David's servants came to Abigail at Carmel and said to her, "David has sent us to you to take you to him as his wife."

**41** She arose and bowed with her face to the ground and said, "Your servant is a maidservant to wash the feet of my lord's servants."

**42** Abigail hurried and arose and rode on a donkey, with her five young women who attended her. She went after David's messengers and became his wife.

**43** David had also taken Ahinoam of Jezreel, and both of them became his wives.

**44** But Saul had given Michal his daughter, David's wife, to Palti son of Laish, who was from Gallim.

## Notes

- **v1 "Samuel died… the wilderness of Paran"** — the man who anointed both kings dies between the two sparings of Saul (chapters 24 and 26). *Paran* is the wilderness where Israel camped after leaving Sinai (Numbers 10:12). The Greek has *the wilderness of Maon*, the place named in the next verse.
- **v3 "He was a Calebite"** — the written text has *kelibbo*, **as his heart**, and the reading is *kalibbi*, **a Calebite**, of the clan of Caleb, who was given his portion in Judah (Joshua 15:13). *Kelev* is **dog**, and the Greek reads it so: *the man was doglike*.
- **vv3, 25, 37 "Nabal… folly is with him… when the wine had gone out of Nabal"** — *naval* is **fool**, as in *The fool says in his heart, "There is no God"* (Psalm 14:1), and *nevalah* is **folly**. The same letters make *nevel*, the **skin** of wine Abigail loads in v18. The wine goes out of Nabal, and his heart dies.
- **v10 "Who is David? Who is the son of Jesse?"** — Saul's contempt, *the son of Jesse*, in the mouth of a rich man of Judah, David's own tribe. The form of the question is Pharaoh's: *Who is the LORD, that I should listen to His voice?* (Exodus 5:2).
- **v22 "May God do so to the enemies of David… so much as one male"** — an oath of this kind calls the curse down on the speaker (*May the LORD do so to me*, Ruth 1:17). The Hebrew has *to the enemies of David*, turning the curse away from David; the Greek has *to David*. *One male* is literally *one who urinates against the wall*, the Hebrew idiom for a man or boy (also v34).
- **vv26, 31, 33, 39 "from saving yourself with your own hand"** — the danger Gideon was warned of, that Israel would say *My own hand has saved me* (Judges 7:2). Abigail keeps David from it, and when the LORD strikes Nabal, David says He *has pleaded the cause of my disgrace*, the words he had used to Saul, *may He see and plead my cause* (24:15).
- **v28 "the LORD will surely make my lord a sure house"** — *bayit ne'eman*, what the LORD promised the faithful priest in place of Eli, *I will build him a sure house* (2:35), and what Nathan will promise David, *your house and your kingdom shall be made sure forever* (2 Samuel 7:16). Abigail says it first.
- **v29 "bound in the bundle of the living… sling out as from the hollow of a sling"** — *tseror ha-chayyim*, the bundle in which something precious is tied and kept. The enemies' lives are flung away like stones; the sling is the weapon that killed Goliath (17:49–50).
- **v33 "Blessed be your discernment"** — *ta'mekh*, **your taste, your judgment**, the word of 21:13, when David *changed his behavior* before Achish.
- **v44 "Saul had given Michal his daughter, David's wife, to Palti"** — David later demands her back, and Paltiel *went with her, weeping behind her*, until Abner sent him home (2 Samuel 3:15–16).

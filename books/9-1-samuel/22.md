# 1 Samuel 22

## The Cave of Adullam

**1** David left there and escaped to the cave of Adullam. When his brothers and all his father's house heard of it, they went down to him there.

**2** Everyone who was in distress, everyone who was in debt, and everyone who was bitter of soul gathered to him, and he became commander over them. There were about four hundred men with him.

**3** From there David went to Mizpeh of Moab and said to the king of Moab, "Please let my father and my mother come and stay with you until I know what God will do for me."

**4** He brought them before the king of Moab, and they stayed with him all the time that David was in the stronghold.

**5** Then Gad the prophet said to David, "Do not stay in the stronghold. Go, get into the land of Judah." So David went and came to the forest of Hereth.

## Saul Accuses His Servants

**6** Saul heard that David and the men with him had been discovered. Saul was sitting at Gibeah under the tamarisk tree on the height, with his spear in his hand, and all his servants were standing around him.

**7** Saul said to his servants standing around him, "Hear now, you Benjaminites! Will the son of Jesse give every one of you fields and vineyards? Will he make you all commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds,

**8** that all of you have conspired against me? No one tells me when my son makes a covenant with the son of Jesse. None of you is sorry for me or tells me that my son has stirred up my servant against me to lie in wait, as he does this day."

**9** Then Doeg the Edomite, who was standing over Saul's servants, answered, "I saw the son of Jesse coming to Nob, to Ahimelech son of Ahitub.

**10** He inquired of the LORD for him and gave him provisions, and he gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine."

## The Death of the Priests

**11** The king sent to summon Ahimelech the priest, son of Ahitub, and all his father's house, the priests who were at Nob, and all of them came to the king.

**12** Saul said, "Hear now, son of Ahitub." He said, "Here I am, my lord."

**13** Saul said to him, "Why have you conspired against me, you and the son of Jesse, in that you gave him bread and a sword and inquired of God for him, so that he has risen against me to lie in wait, as he does this day?"

**14** Ahimelech answered the king, "Who among all your servants is as faithful as David, the king's son-in-law, captain of your bodyguard and honored in your house?

**15** Is today the first time I have inquired of God for him? Far be it from me! Let the king not charge his servant or all my father's house with anything, for your servant knew nothing of all this, little or great."

**16** The king said, "You shall die, Ahimelech, you and all your father's house."

**17** The king said to the runners standing around him, "Turn and kill the priests of the LORD, because their hand also is with David, and they knew that he fled and did not tell me." But the king's servants would not put out their hands to strike the priests of the LORD.

**18** Then the king said to Doeg, "You turn and strike the priests." So Doeg the Edomite turned and struck the priests, and he killed that day eighty-five men who wore the linen ephod.

**19** Nob, the city of the priests, he struck with the edge of the sword: man and woman, child and infant, ox and donkey and sheep, with the edge of the sword.

**20** But one son of Ahimelech son of Ahitub, whose name was Abiathar, escaped and fled after David.

**21** Abiathar told David that Saul had killed the priests of the LORD.

**22** David said to Abiathar, "I knew that day, when Doeg the Edomite was there, that he would surely tell Saul. I am the cause of the death of every soul of your father's house.

**23** Stay with me; do not be afraid. The one who seeks my life seeks your life. With me you will be kept safe."

## Notes

- **v2 "everyone who was in distress… in debt… bitter of soul"** — *mar nefesh*, the words said of Hannah, *she was bitter of soul* (1:10).
- **vv3–4 "Mizpeh of Moab… my father and my mother"** — David's great-grandmother was Ruth the Moabite: Obed was *the father of Jesse, the father of David* (Ruth 4:17). He takes his parents to the land she came from.
- **v7 "Will the son of Jesse give every one of you fields and vineyards? Will he make you all commanders of thousands"** — Samuel's warning about the king: *He will take the best of your fields and vineyards… and give them to his servants* (8:14), and *He will appoint for himself commanders of thousands* (8:12). Saul now holds out that very bargain to his own tribe.
- **vv8, 13 "all of you have conspired against me"** — *qeshartem*, from *qashar*, **to bind**, the verb of 18:1, *the soul of Jonathan was bound to the soul of David*. What was a bond of love Saul calls a conspiracy.
- **vv10, 15 "He inquired of the LORD for him… Is today the first time I have inquired of God for him?"** — chapter 21 does not tell of an inquiry at Nob. Doeg reports one, and Ahimelech does not deny it: he says it was not the first.
- **v14 "as faithful as David"** — *ne'eman*, the word of the LORD's promise to Eli, *I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest* (2:35), and of Samuel, *established as a prophet* (3:20). A priest of Eli's house uses it of David. *Captain of your bodyguard*, *sar el mishma'tekha*: David later sets Benaiah *over his bodyguard*, *mishma'to* (2 Samuel 23:23).
- **v17 "the king's servants would not put out their hands to strike the priests of the LORD"** — the words David will use of Saul himself: *far be it from me… to put out my hand against him, for he is the LORD's anointed* (24:6). Only the Edomite does it.
- **v18 "eighty-five men who wore the linen ephod"** — the *ephod bad* Samuel wore as a boy serving at Shiloh (2:18). The Greek has *three hundred and five*.
- **v19 "man and woman, child and infant, ox and donkey and sheep"** — the list of the ban on Amalek, *man and woman, child and infant, ox and sheep, camel and donkey* (15:3). Saul did not carry it out on Amalek; he carries it out on a city of the LORD's priests.
- **vv20–23 "one son of Ahimelech… escaped"** — the word to Eli: *The man of yours whom I do not cut off from My altar shall be spared to wear out your eyes* (2:33). Abiathar serves David, and Solomon later removes him from the priesthood *to fulfill the word of the LORD that He had spoken concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh* (1 Kings 2:27).

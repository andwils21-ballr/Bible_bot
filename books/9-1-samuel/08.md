# 1 Samuel 8

## Samuel's Sons

**1** And it came to pass, when Samuel was old, that he made his sons judges over Israel.

**2** The name of his firstborn son was Joel, and the name of his second, Abijah. They were judges in Beersheba.

**3** But his sons did not walk in his ways. They turned aside after gain, took bribes and perverted justice.

## "Give Us a King"

**4** Then all the elders of Israel gathered together and came to Samuel at Ramah.

**5** They said to him, "You are old, and your sons do not walk in your ways. Now appoint for us a king to judge us like all the nations."

**6** But the thing was evil in Samuel's eyes when they said, "Give us a king to judge us." Samuel prayed to the LORD.

**7** The LORD said to Samuel, "Listen to the voice of the people in all that they say to you, for they have not rejected you; they have rejected Me from being king over them.

**8** According to all the deeds they have done from the day I brought them up from Egypt until this day, forsaking Me and serving other gods, so they are doing to you also.

**9** So now listen to their voice. Only you shall solemnly warn them and tell them the ways of the king who will reign over them."

## The Ways of the King

**10** So Samuel told all the words of the LORD to the people who were asking him for a king.

**11** He said, "These will be the ways of the king who will reign over you: he will take your sons and put them to his chariots and among his horsemen, and they will run before his chariots.

**12** He will appoint for himself commanders of thousands and commanders of fifties, and some to plow his ground and to reap his harvest, and to make his weapons of war and the equipment of his chariots.

**13** He will take your daughters to be perfumers and cooks and bakers.

**14** He will take the best of your fields and vineyards and olive groves and give them to his servants.

**15** He will take a tenth of your grain and of your vineyards and give it to his officials and his servants.

**16** He will take your male servants and your female servants, and the best of your young men, and your donkeys, and put them to his work.

**17** He will take a tenth of your flocks, and you yourselves will be his slaves.

**18** In that day you will cry out because of your king, whom you have chosen for yourselves, but the LORD will not answer you in that day."

**19** But the people refused to listen to the voice of Samuel. They said, "No! There shall be a king over us,

**20** so that we too may be like all the nations, and our king may judge us and go out before us and fight our battles."

**21** When Samuel had heard all the words of the people, he repeated them in the ears of the LORD.

**22** The LORD said to Samuel, "Listen to their voice and make them a king." Then Samuel said to the men of Israel, "Go, each to his town."

## Notes

- **v3 "turned aside after gain, took bribes and perverted justice"** — each of the three is forbidden in the law: *You shall not bend judgment… and you shall not take a bribe* (Deuteronomy 16:19). Samuel's sons repeat Eli's sons (2:12–17), in the courts instead of at the altar.
- **v5 "a king to judge us like all the nations"** — the words Deuteronomy 17:14 foresaw: *Let me set a king over me, like all the nations that are around me.* The law allows a king, with limits; the elders ask for one for the reason the law names.
- **v7 "they have rejected Me from being king over them"** — Gideon had refused to rule: *The LORD will rule over you* (Judges 8:23). The request for a king is heard by the LORD as a request for a replacement.
- **v9 "the ways of the king"** — *mishpat ha-melekh*, **the justice** or **the rule of the king**. *Mishpat* is what the elders say they want from him, *to judge us*, *le-shoftenu* (v5); what Samuel describes is what the king will *take*.
- **vv11–17 "he will take"** — *yiqqach*, four times, and *ya'sor*, **he will take a tenth**, twice. The tenth the law gives to the LORD and the Levites the king will take for himself and his servants. The list ends where Israel began: *you yourselves will be his slaves* (v17), *avadim*, the word of Egypt.
- **v12 "commanders of thousands and commanders of fifties"** — the officers Moses set over the people (Exodus 18:21), now made the king's.
- **v16 "the best of your young men"** — *bachureikhem*. The Greek has *your cattle*, *beqarkhem*, which fits a list of servants and donkeys; the Hebrew letters differ by one. The text follows the Hebrew.
- **v18 "you will cry out… but the LORD will not answer you"** — in Judges the pattern was *the sons of Israel cried out to the LORD, and the LORD raised up a deliverer* (Judges 3:9). Here the cry is foreseen, and the answer refused.
- **v20 "go out before us and fight our battles"** — Israel's battles in Judges and at Mizpah (7:10) were the LORD's. The people now ask for a man to fight them.
- **v22 "Listen to their voice"** — the LORD grants the request He has called a rejection of Himself.

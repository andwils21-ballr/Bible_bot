# 1 Samuel 21

## The Holy Bread

**1** David came to Nob, to Ahimelech the priest. Ahimelech came trembling to meet David and said to him, "Why are you alone, and no one with you?"

**2** David said to Ahimelech the priest, "The king has charged me with a matter and said to me, 'Let no one know anything of the matter on which I am sending you and what I have commanded you.' I have told the young men to meet me at such and such a place.

**3** Now what do you have on hand? Give me five loaves of bread, or whatever can be found."

**4** The priest answered David, "There is no common bread on hand, only holy bread, if only the young men have kept themselves from women."

**5** David answered the priest, "Women have indeed been kept from us, as always when I set out. The young men's vessels are holy even on an ordinary journey; how much more today will they be holy in their vessels!"

**6** So the priest gave him holy bread, for there was no bread there but the bread of the Presence, which had been taken away from before the LORD, to put hot bread in its place on the day it was taken away.

**7** Now one of Saul's servants was there that day, detained before the LORD. His name was Doeg the Edomite, the chief of Saul's shepherds.

**8** David said to Ahimelech, "Is there not a spear or a sword here on hand? For I did not bring my sword or my weapons with me, because the king's business was urgent."

**9** The priest said, "The sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom you struck down in the Valley of Elah, is here, wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod. If you will take it, take it, for there is no other here but that one." David said, "There is none like it. Give it to me."

## David Feigns Madness

**10** David rose and fled that day from Saul, and came to Achish king of Gath.

**11** The servants of Achish said to him, "Is this not David, the king of the land? Is this not the one they sang of to one another in the dances:  
'Saul has struck down his thousands,  
and David his ten thousands'?"

**12** David took these words to heart and was very afraid of Achish king of Gath.

**13** So he changed his behavior before them and acted like a madman in their hands. He scratched marks on the doors of the gate and let his spittle run down his beard.

**14** Achish said to his servants, "You see the man is mad. Why have you brought him to me?

**15** Do I lack madmen, that you have brought this one to act the madman before me? Shall this one come into my house?"

## Notes

- **v1 "David came to Nob, to Ahimelech the priest"** — in the Hebrew this is 21:2; from here to the end of the chapter the Hebrew numbering is one verse ahead of the English. Ahimelech is *son of Ahitub* (22:9), as Ahijah the priest was (14:3): the house of Eli, still serving. *Came trembling to meet*, *va-yecherad… li-qrat*, as the elders of Bethlehem met Samuel (16:4).
- **v2 "The king has charged me with a matter… such and such a place"** — David lies to the priest, and the cost comes in 22:18–19. *Peloni almoni*, **such and such**, is the pair of words Boaz uses for the redeemer he will not name (Ruth 4:1).
- **vv4–5 "if only the young men have kept themselves from women"** — the rule of Sinai, *Do not go near a woman* (Exodus 19:15), applied to men on campaign. David claims more than the priest asks: the men's *vessels*, their gear or their bodies, are holy on every march.
- **v6 "the bread of the Presence… hot bread in its place"** — *lechem ha-panim* (Exodus 25:30), set out before the LORD and replaced fresh; the old bread *shall be Aaron's and his sons', and they shall eat it in a holy place* (Leviticus 24:9). Jesus cites this day when His disciples are charged with breaking the sabbath: David *entered the house of God in the time of Abiathar the high priest and ate the bread of the Presence* (Mark 2:26). Abiathar is Ahimelech's son, who escapes the slaughter of the priests (22:20).
- **v7 "Doeg the Edomite, the chief of Saul's shepherds"** — *ne'tsar lifnei YHWH*, **held back before the LORD**, kept at the sanctuary for a reason the text does not give. An Edomite, of Esau's line, in Saul's service. What he sees here he reports (22:9), and he kills the priests (22:18). The Greek has *Doeg the Syrian, who tended Saul's mules*.
- **v9 "The sword of Goliath… wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod"** — the sword David took from Goliath to cut off his head (17:51) and laid up (17:54) has been kept at the sanctuary. *There is none like it*: David had said the LORD saves not by sword and spear (17:47); now he flees with the Philistine's sword.
- **v10 "came to Achish king of Gath"** — Gath is Goliath's town (17:4). David goes there carrying Goliath's sword.
- **v11 "Is this not David, the king of the land?"** — the Philistines call him king before Israel does. The song of the women (18:7) has reached Gath.
- **v13 "he changed his behavior before them"** — *va-yeshanno et ta'mo*, **he changed his taste**, his sense or judgment. Psalm 34 has the heading *Of David, when he changed his behavior before Abimelech, who drove him out, and he went away*, and the psalm says *Taste and see that the LORD is good* (34:8): the same *ta'am*. The Greek has *he drummed on the doors of the city*.
- **vv14–15 "Do I lack madmen?"** — *meshugga'im*. The ruse works: Achish sees only one more madman, not the king of the land.

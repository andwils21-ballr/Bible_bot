# 1 Samuel 18

## Jonathan's Covenant

**1** And it came to pass, when he had finished speaking to Saul, that the soul of Jonathan was bound to the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul.

**2** Saul took him that day and would not let him go back to his father's house.

**3** Jonathan made a covenant with David, because he loved him as his own soul.

**4** Jonathan stripped off the robe he was wearing and gave it to David, and his garments too, even his sword, his bow and his belt.

**5** David went out wherever Saul sent him, and he succeeded. Saul set him over the men of war, and it was good in the eyes of all the people, and in the eyes of Saul's servants too.

## Thousands and Ten Thousands

**6** And it came to pass, as they were coming, when David returned from killing the Philistine, that the women came out of all the towns of Israel, singing and dancing, to meet King Saul, with tambourines, with joy and with cymbals.

**7** The women sang to one another as they celebrated:  
"Saul has struck down his thousands,  
and David his ten thousands."

**8** Saul was very angry, and this saying was evil in his eyes. He said, "They have given David ten thousands, and to me they have given thousands. What more can he have but the kingdom?"

**9** Saul kept his eye on David from that day on.

**10** And it came to pass the next day that an evil spirit from God rushed on Saul, and he prophesied inside the house while David was playing with his hand, as he did day by day. Saul had the spear in his hand.

**11** Saul hurled the spear, thinking, "I will pin David to the wall." But David evaded him twice.

**12** Saul was afraid of David, because the LORD was with him and had departed from Saul.

**13** So Saul removed him from his presence and made him commander of a thousand, and he went out and came in before the people.

**14** David succeeded in all his ways, and the LORD was with him.

**15** When Saul saw that he was succeeding greatly, he stood in dread of him.

**16** But all Israel and Judah loved David, for he went out and came in before them.

## Saul's Daughters

**17** Saul said to David, "Here is my older daughter Merab. I will give her to you as a wife; only be a man of valor for me and fight the LORD's battles." For Saul thought, "Let my hand not be against him, but let the hand of the Philistines be against him."

**18** David said to Saul, "Who am I, and what is my life, or my father's clan in Israel, that I should be son-in-law to the king?"

**19** And it came to pass, at the time when Merab, Saul's daughter, should have been given to David, that she was given to Adriel the Meholathite as a wife.

**20** Michal, Saul's daughter, loved David. They told Saul, and the thing was right in his eyes.

**21** Saul thought, "I will give her to him, so that she may be a snare to him, and so that the hand of the Philistines may be against him." So Saul said to David, "Through the second you shall become my son-in-law today."

**22** Saul commanded his servants, "Speak to David in secret and say, 'The king delights in you, and all his servants love you. Now become the king's son-in-law.'"

**23** Saul's servants spoke these words in David's hearing. David said, "Is it a light thing in your eyes to become the king's son-in-law, when I am a poor man and lightly esteemed?"

**24** Saul's servants told him, "These are the words David spoke."

**25** Saul said, "Say this to David: 'The king desires no bride-price except a hundred foreskins of the Philistines, to take vengeance on the king's enemies.'" For Saul planned to make David fall by the hand of the Philistines.

**26** When his servants told David these words, the thing was right in David's eyes, to become the king's son-in-law. Before the days were fulfilled,

**27** David arose and went, he and his men, and struck down two hundred men of the Philistines. David brought their foreskins, and they gave them in full number to the king, so that he might become the king's son-in-law. So Saul gave him Michal his daughter as a wife.

**28** Saul saw and knew that the LORD was with David, and that Michal, Saul's daughter, loved him.

**29** Saul was still more afraid of David, and Saul was David's enemy all his days.

**30** The commanders of the Philistines came out to battle, and as often as they came out, David succeeded more than all Saul's servants, so that his name was held in high honor.

## Notes

- **v1 "the soul of Jonathan was bound to the soul of David"** — *nefesh… niqsherah be-nefesh*, the words Judah used of Jacob and Benjamin: *his life is bound up with the boy's life* (Genesis 44:30). *As his own soul*, *ke-nafsho*, comes twice (vv1, 3).
- **vv1–5 and the Greek** — the Greek has none of vv1–5, vv10–11, vv17–19 or v30, and it lacks the end of vv21, 26 and 29. Without them there is no covenant with Jonathan here, no spear thrown, and no offer of Merab. The Hebrew has them, and the text follows it.
- **v4 "the robe he was wearing… even his sword, his bow and his belt"** — *me'il*, the robe. When the Philistines kept the smiths, *neither sword nor spear was found* in Israel except with Saul and Jonathan (13:22). David could not walk in Saul's armor (17:39); Jonathan gives him his own sword.
- **vv5, 14, 15, 30 "he succeeded"** — *maskil*, **acting wisely, succeeding**: the word joins good sense and good outcome. It comes four times in the chapter, and Saul's fear grows beside it (vv12, 15, 29).
- **v6 "with tambourines, with joy and with cymbals"** — *shalishim*, an instrument built on *shalosh*, **three**; what it was is not known. The Greek has *cymbals*, and the text follows it. The Greek also has the women come out *to meet David*, not King Saul.
- **v7 "Saul has struck down his thousands, and David his ten thousands"** — *thousands* and *ten thousands* stand as a pair in Hebrew poetry: *How could one chase a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight* (Deuteronomy 32:30). Saul hears the pair as a ranking (v8). The song follows David: the servants of Achish quote it at Gath (21:11), and the Philistine commanders quote it again (29:5).
- **v10 "an evil spirit from God rushed on Saul, and he prophesied"** — *va-titslach*, the verb of the Spirit of God on Saul at his anointing (10:6, 10:10), and on David (16:13). *Va-yitnabbe*, **he prophesied**, is the word of 10:10 too; what came on him then from the Spirit of God now comes from an evil spirit.
- **vv17, 21, 25 "let the hand of the Philistines be against him… a snare to him"** — three times Saul's plan is given in his own mind. *Moqesh*, **snare**, is the word Israel was warned about in the land (Judges 2:3).
- **v18 "Who am I… that I should be son-in-law to the king?"** — Moses' question at the bush, *Who am I, that I should go to Pharaoh?* (Exodus 3:11).
- **vv25, 27 "a hundred foreskins… two hundred men"** — *mohar*, the **bride-price** that Shechem offered to raise as high as Jacob's sons liked (Genesis 34:12). David pays double. The Greek has *a hundred* in v27, and David names the price later as *a hundred foreskins of the Philistines* (2 Samuel 3:14).

# 1 Samuel 24

## The Cave

**1** And it came to pass, when Saul returned from pursuing the Philistines, that he was told, "David is in the wilderness of En-gedi."

**2** Saul took three thousand chosen men from all Israel and went to seek David and his men in front of the Rocks of the Wild Goats.

**3** He came to the sheepfolds by the road, where there was a cave, and Saul went in to relieve himself. David and his men were sitting in the far recesses of the cave.

**4** David's men said to him, "This is the day of which the LORD said to you, 'I am giving your enemy into your hand, and you shall do to him as seems good in your eyes.'" Then David arose and secretly cut off the edge of Saul's robe.

**5** And it came to pass afterward that David's heart struck him because he had cut off the edge of Saul's robe.

**6** He said to his men, "The LORD forbid that I should do this thing to my lord, the LORD's anointed, to put out my hand against him, for he is the LORD's anointed."

**7** So David held his men back with these words and did not let them rise against Saul. Saul got up from the cave and went on his way.

## "My Father, See"

**8** Afterward David also arose and went out of the cave and called after Saul, "My lord the king!" When Saul looked behind him, David bowed with his face to the ground and paid him homage.

**9** David said to Saul, "Why do you listen to the words of men who say, 'David is seeking to harm you'?

**10** This very day your eyes have seen how the LORD gave you into my hand in the cave. Some said to kill you, but I spared you. I said, 'I will not put out my hand against my lord, for he is the LORD's anointed.'

**11** My father, see, yes, see the edge of your robe in my hand. Since I cut off the edge of your robe and did not kill you, know and see that there is no evil or rebellion in my hand. I have not sinned against you, yet you are hunting my life to take it.

**12** May the LORD judge between me and you, and may the LORD avenge me on you; but my hand shall not be against you.

**13** As the proverb of the ancients says, 'From the wicked comes wickedness.' But my hand shall not be against you.

**14** After whom has the king of Israel come out? After whom are you chasing? After a dead dog! After a single flea!

**15** May the LORD be judge and judge between me and you, and may He see and plead my cause and deliver me from your hand."

## Saul Weeps

**16** And it came to pass, when David had finished speaking these words to Saul, that Saul said, "Is this your voice, my son David?" Saul lifted up his voice and wept.

**17** He said to David, "You are more in the right than I, for you have repaid me with good, and I have repaid you with evil.

**18** You have shown today how you have dealt well with me: the LORD gave me into your hand, and you did not kill me.

**19** For if a man finds his enemy, will he let him go on his way safely? May the LORD repay you with good for what you have done to me this day.

**20** And now I know that you will surely be king, and that the kingdom of Israel will be established in your hand.

**21** So swear to me by the LORD that you will not cut off my seed after me, and that you will not destroy my name from my father's house."

**22** So David swore to Saul. Saul went home, but David and his men went up to the stronghold.

## Notes

- **v1 "when Saul returned from pursuing the Philistines"** — in the Hebrew this is 24:2; the whole chapter runs one verse ahead of the English, because the English puts 24:1 at the end of chapter 23.
- **v3 "Saul went in to relieve himself"** — *le-hasekh et raglav*, **to cover his feet**, the phrase Eglon's servants used when they supposed *he must be relieving himself* (Judges 3:24). The king David is fleeing walks alone into David's hiding place.
- **v4 "This is the day of which the LORD said to you"** — the LORD told David *I am giving the Philistines into your hand* (23:4); no such word about Saul has been told. His men make one out of it.
- **vv4–5 "cut off the edge of Saul's robe… David's heart struck him"** — *kenaf ha-me'il*, the edge Saul tore from Samuel's robe when Samuel said, *The LORD has torn the kingdom of Israel from you today* (15:27–28). David cuts it from Saul's robe, and his own heart strikes him for it.
- **vv6, 10 "to put out my hand against him, for he is the LORD's anointed"** — *mashiach YHWH*. David will ask the Amalekite who claims to have killed Saul, *How were you not afraid to put out your hand to destroy the LORD's anointed?* (2 Samuel 1:14).
- **v7 "David held his men back with these words"** — *va-yeshassa*, a verb of **tearing apart**, used of Samson, who *tore the lion as one tears a young goat* (Judges 14:6).
- **vv11, 16 "My father, see… Is this your voice, my son David?"** — the son-in-law calls the king *my father*. Saul, who has called him only *the son of Jesse* (20:27–31, 22:7–13), answers *my son David*.
- **v14 "After a dead dog! After a single flea!"** — the Philistine asked, *Am I a dog?* (17:43). David calls himself a dead dog; Mephibosheth will say *a dead dog such as I* to King David (2 Samuel 9:8).
- **v17 "You are more in the right than I"** — *tsaddiq attah mimmenni*, the words of Judah over Tamar, *She is more in the right than I* (Genesis 38:26): the one in power admits the one he wronged was right.
- **vv20–21 "you will surely be king… you will not cut off my seed after me"** — Jonathan had said, *Saul my father also knows this* (23:17), and asked the same oath for his house (20:15). David spares Jonathan's son *because of the oath of the LORD* (2 Samuel 21:7), but in the same chapter he hands over seven other sons and grandsons of Saul (2 Samuel 21:8–9).

# 1 Samuel 28

## Philistines at Shunem

**1** And it came to pass in those days that the Philistines gathered their forces for war, to fight against Israel. Achish said to David, "Know for certain that you and your men shall go out with me in the army."

**2** David said to Achish, "Very well, you shall know what your servant will do." Achish said to David, "Very well, I will make you my bodyguard for life."

**3** Now Samuel had died, and all Israel had mourned for him and buried him in Ramah, his own town. Saul had removed the ghosts and the spirits from the land.

**4** The Philistines gathered and came and camped at Shunem, and Saul gathered all Israel, and they camped at Gilboa.

**5** When Saul saw the camp of the Philistines, he was afraid, and his heart trembled greatly.

**6** Saul inquired of the LORD, but the LORD did not answer him, neither by dreams nor by Urim nor by prophets.

## The Woman at En-dor

**7** Then Saul said to his servants, "Find me a woman who has a ghost, so that I may go to her and inquire of her." His servants said to him, "There is a woman who has a ghost at En-dor."

**8** So Saul disguised himself and put on other clothes, and he went with two men, and they came to the woman by night. He said, "Practice soothsaying for me by the ghost, and bring up for me the one I name to you."

**9** The woman said to him, "Surely you know what Saul has done, how he has cut off the ghosts and the spirits from the land. Why then are you laying a snare for my life, to bring about my death?"

**10** Saul swore to her by the LORD, "As the LORD lives, no punishment shall come upon you for this."

**11** Then the woman said, "Whom shall I bring up for you?" He said, "Bring up Samuel for me."

**12** When the woman saw Samuel, she cried out with a loud voice, and the woman said to Saul, "Why have you deceived me? You are Saul!"

**13** The king said to her, "Do not be afraid. What do you see?" The woman said to Saul, "I see a god coming up out of the earth."

**14** He said to her, "What does he look like?" She said, "An old man is coming up, and he is wrapped in a robe." Then Saul knew that it was Samuel, and he bowed with his face to the ground and paid homage.

## Samuel's Word

**15** Samuel said to Saul, "Why have you disturbed me by bringing me up?" Saul said, "I am in great distress. The Philistines are fighting against me, and God has departed from me and no longer answers me, either by prophets or by dreams. So I have called you to tell me what I should do."

**16** Samuel said, "Why then do you ask me, since the LORD has departed from you and has become your adversary?

**17** The LORD has done as He spoke by me. The LORD has torn the kingdom out of your hand and given it to your neighbor, to David.

**18** Because you did not listen to the voice of the LORD and did not carry out His fierce anger against Amalek, the LORD has done this thing to you today.

**19** The LORD will also give Israel with you into the hand of the Philistines, and tomorrow you and your sons will be with me. The LORD will also give the army of Israel into the hand of the Philistines."

**20** At once Saul fell full length on the ground, terrified by Samuel's words. There was no strength left in him, for he had eaten no food all day and all night.

**21** The woman came to Saul and saw that he was badly shaken, and she said to him, "Your servant has listened to your voice. I took my life in my hand and listened to the words you spoke to me.

**22** So now, please, listen also to the voice of your servant. Let me set a morsel of bread before you, and eat, so that you may have strength when you go on your way."

**23** He refused and said, "I will not eat." But his servants, together with the woman, pressed him, and he listened to their voice. He rose from the ground and sat on the bed.

**24** The woman had a fattened calf in the house, and she quickly slaughtered it. She took flour, kneaded it and baked unleavened bread.

**25** She set it before Saul and his servants, and they ate. Then they rose and went away that night.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "you shall know what your servant will do… I will make you my bodyguard for life"** — David's answer can be heard two ways, and Achish hears it as loyalty. The next chapter decides it without David having to choose.
- **vv3, 9 "Saul had removed the ghosts and the spirits from the land"** — *ha-ovot ve-ha-yidde'onim*, the words of the law, *Do not turn to ghosts, and do not seek out spirits* (Leviticus 19:31). The words name both the spirit consulted and the one who consults it. Saul had kept this law.
- **v6 "neither by dreams nor by Urim nor by prophets"** — every lawful way of asking. David has the ephod and the priest (23:6, 23:9), and the LORD answers him. Saul had been left unanswered before (14:37). Chronicles names this night among the causes of his death: *Saul died for his unfaithfulness… and also for asking a ghost, to inquire of it, and he did not inquire of the LORD* (1 Chronicles 10:13–14).
- **v7 "a woman who has a ghost"** — *eshet ba'alat ov*, **a woman, mistress of a ghost**, as the law says *a man or a woman who has a ghost or a spirit in them shall surely be put to death* (Leviticus 20:27).
- **v8 "Practice soothsaying for me by the ghost… the one I name to you"** — *qosomi*, the practice Samuel set beside rebellion: *rebellion is the sin of soothsaying* (15:23). *The one I name to you* are the LORD's words to Samuel when He sent him to anoint David (16:3).
- **v13 "I see a god coming up out of the earth"** — *elohim*, with a plural verb, *olim*: **a divine being**, or **gods**. The Greek has *gods*. The word is the ordinary word for God; here it names what the woman sees rising.
- **v14 "an old man… wrapped in a robe"** — *me'il*, the robe Hannah made Samuel as a boy (2:19) and the robe Saul tore when the kingdom was torn from him (15:27–28). Saul knows him by it.
- **vv16–19 "has become your adversary… torn the kingdom… tomorrow you and your sons will be with me"** — Samuel repeats his word at Gilgal, *The LORD has torn the kingdom of Israel from you* (15:28), and the cause, Amalek (15:18). The Greek of v16 has *has become with your neighbor*. *Tomorrow* comes in 31:6: *Saul died, and his three sons… on that day together*. *Arekha*, **your adversary**, is a rare word; the same form stands in Psalm 139:20.
- **vv21–25 "I took my life in my hand… a fattened calf… unleavened bread"** — the woman risks her life for the king who outlawed her, and then feeds him. The meal is made in haste, as Abraham made one for his guests (Genesis 18:6–7).

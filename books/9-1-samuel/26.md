# 1 Samuel 26

## The Spear and the Water Jar

**1** The Ziphites came to Saul at Gibeah and said, "Is not David hiding on the hill of Hachilah, facing Jeshimon?"

**2** So Saul arose and went down to the wilderness of Ziph with three thousand chosen men of Israel, to seek David in the wilderness of Ziph.

**3** Saul camped on the hill of Hachilah, which faces Jeshimon, by the road. David was staying in the wilderness, and he saw that Saul had come after him into the wilderness.

**4** David sent out spies and learned that Saul had indeed come.

**5** Then David arose and came to the place where Saul had camped. David saw the place where Saul lay, with Abner son of Ner, the commander of his army. Saul was lying inside the encampment, and the army was camped around him.

**6** David said to Ahimelech the Hittite and to Abishai son of Zeruiah, Joab's brother, "Who will go down with me to Saul in the camp?" Abishai said, "I will go down with you."

**7** So David and Abishai came to the army by night. Saul lay sleeping inside the encampment, with his spear stuck in the ground at his head, and Abner and the army were lying around him.

**8** Abishai said to David, "God has delivered your enemy into your hand today. Now please let me pin him to the ground with the spear in one stroke; I will not need to strike him twice."

**9** But David said to Abishai, "Do not destroy him, for who can put out his hand against the LORD's anointed and be guiltless?"

**10** David said, "As the LORD lives, the LORD will strike him; or his day will come and he will die; or he will go down into battle and perish.

**11** The LORD forbid that I should put out my hand against the LORD's anointed. But now take the spear that is at his head and the water jar, and let us go."

**12** So David took the spear and the water jar from Saul's head, and they went away. No one saw it, no one knew it, and no one woke, for they were all asleep, because a deep sleep from the LORD had fallen on them.

## "Is This Your Voice, My Son David?"

**13** Then David crossed over to the other side and stood on top of the mountain far off, with a great space between them.

**14** David called to the army and to Abner son of Ner, "Will you not answer, Abner?" Abner answered, "Who are you, calling to the king?"

**15** David said to Abner, "Are you not a man? Who is like you in Israel? Why then have you not kept watch over your lord the king? For one of the people went in to destroy the king your lord.

**16** This thing that you have done is not good. As the LORD lives, you deserve to die, because you have not kept watch over your lord, the LORD's anointed. Now see where the king's spear is, and the water jar that was at his head."

**17** Saul recognized David's voice and said, "Is this your voice, my son David?" David said, "It is my voice, my lord the king."

**18** He said, "Why is my lord pursuing his servant? What have I done? What evil is in my hand?

**19** Now let my lord the king hear the words of his servant. If it is the LORD who has stirred you up against me, may He accept an offering; but if it is men, cursed be they before the LORD, for they have driven me out this day from having a share in the inheritance of the LORD, saying, 'Go, serve other gods.'

**20** So do not let my blood fall to the ground away from the presence of the LORD, for the king of Israel has come out to seek a single flea, as one hunts a partridge in the mountains."

**21** Saul said, "I have sinned. Return, my son David, for I will not harm you again, because my life was precious in your eyes this day. I have acted foolishly and have erred very greatly."

**22** David answered, "Here is the king's spear! Let one of the young men come over and take it.

**23** The LORD will repay each man for his righteousness and his faithfulness. The LORD gave you into my hand today, but I would not put out my hand against the LORD's anointed.

**24** As your life was highly valued this day in my eyes, so may my life be highly valued in the eyes of the LORD, and may He deliver me from all trouble."

**25** Saul said to David, "Blessed are you, my son David! You will do great things and will surely prevail." So David went on his way, and Saul returned to his place.

## Notes

- **v19 "may He accept an offering… Go, serve other gods"** — *yarach minchah*, **may He smell an offering**, as *the LORD smelled the soothing aroma* after the flood (Genesis 8:21). To be driven from the LORD's inheritance is to be sent where one must serve other gods, the exile the law foresaw: *There you will serve gods made by human hands* (Deuteronomy 4:28).

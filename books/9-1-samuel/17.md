# 1 Samuel 17

## The Champion of Gath

**1** The Philistines gathered their armies for battle. They gathered at Socoh, which belongs to Judah, and camped between Socoh and Azekah, in Ephes-dammim.

**2** Saul and the men of Israel gathered and camped in the Valley of Elah, and drew up in battle lines to meet the Philistines.

**3** The Philistines stood on the mountain on one side, and Israel stood on the mountain on the other side, with the valley between them.

**4** A champion came out from the camp of the Philistines, named Goliath, of Gath. His height was six cubits and a span.

**5** He had a bronze helmet on his head, and he wore a coat of scale armor; the weight of the coat was five thousand shekels of bronze.

**6** He had bronze greaves on his legs, and a bronze javelin slung between his shoulders.

**7** The shaft of his spear was like a weaver's beam, and the head of his spear weighed six hundred shekels of iron. His shield-bearer went before him.

**8** He stood and called out to the ranks of Israel, "Why have you come out to draw up for battle? Am I not the Philistine, and are you not servants of Saul? Choose a man for yourselves, and let him come down to me.

**9** If he is able to fight with me and kills me, we will be your slaves. But if I prevail against him and kill him, you shall be our slaves and serve us."

**10** The Philistine said, "I defy the ranks of Israel this day. Give me a man, and let us fight together."

**11** When Saul and all Israel heard these words of the Philistine, they lost heart and were greatly afraid.

## David Comes to the Camp

**12** David was the son of that Ephrathite of Bethlehem in Judah whose name was Jesse. He had eight sons, and in the days of Saul the man was old, well on in years.

**13** The three oldest sons of Jesse had gone after Saul to the battle. The names of his three sons who went to the battle were Eliab the firstborn, next to him Abinadab, and the third Shammah.

**14** David was the youngest. The three oldest followed Saul,

**15** but David went back and forth from Saul to tend his father's sheep at Bethlehem.

**16** For forty days the Philistine came forward morning and evening and took his stand.

**17** Jesse said to David his son, "Take for your brothers an ephah of this roasted grain and these ten loaves, and hurry to the camp to your brothers.

**18** Take these ten cheeses to the commander of their thousand. See how your brothers are, and bring back some token from them.

**19** Saul and they and all the men of Israel are in the Valley of Elah, fighting the Philistines."

**20** David rose early in the morning, left the sheep with a keeper, took the provisions and went, as Jesse had commanded him. He came to the encampment as the army was going out to the battle line, shouting the war cry.

**21** Israel and the Philistines drew up, line facing line.

**22** David left his baggage in the care of the keeper of the baggage, ran to the battle line, and came and asked his brothers how they were.

**23** As he was speaking with them, the champion, Goliath the Philistine of Gath by name, came up out of the ranks of the Philistines and spoke the same words as before, and David heard.

**24** All the men of Israel, when they saw the man, fled from him and were greatly afraid.

**25** The men of Israel said, "Have you seen this man who is coming up? He comes up to defy Israel. The man who kills him, the king will make very rich; he will give him his daughter and make his father's house free in Israel."

**26** David said to the men standing with him, "What will be done for the man who kills this Philistine and takes away the disgrace from Israel? For who is this uncircumcised Philistine, that he should defy the ranks of the living God?"

**27** The people answered him in the same way: "So shall it be done for the man who kills him."

**28** Eliab his oldest brother heard him speaking to the men, and Eliab's anger burned against David. He said, "Why have you come down? With whom have you left those few sheep in the wilderness? I know your insolence and the evil of your heart; you have come down to see the battle."

**29** David said, "What have I done now? Was it not only a word?"

**30** He turned from him to another and spoke the same way, and the people answered him as before.

**31** When the words David had spoken were heard, they reported them to Saul, and he sent for him.

## "The Battle Is the LORD's"

**32** David said to Saul, "Let no one's heart fail because of him. Your servant will go and fight with this Philistine."

**33** Saul said to David, "You are not able to go against this Philistine to fight with him, for you are a youth, and he has been a man of war from his youth."

**34** David said to Saul, "Your servant used to tend his father's sheep. When a lion came, or a bear, and took a lamb from the flock,

**35** I went out after it and struck it and rescued the lamb from its mouth. If it rose against me, I caught it by its beard, struck it and killed it.

**36** Your servant has struck down both lion and bear, and this uncircumcised Philistine shall be like one of them, for he has defied the ranks of the living God."

**37** David said, "The LORD who rescued me from the paw of the lion and from the paw of the bear, He will rescue me from the hand of this Philistine." Saul said to David, "Go, and the LORD be with you."

**38** Saul clothed David in his own garments. He put a bronze helmet on his head and clothed him in a coat of mail.

**39** David fastened Saul's sword over the garments and tried to walk, for he had not tested them. David said to Saul, "I cannot walk in these, for I have not tested them." So David took them off.

**40** He took his staff in his hand and chose five smooth stones from the stream, and put them in the shepherd's bag he had, in the pouch. With his sling in his hand, he drew near to the Philistine.

**41** The Philistine came on, drawing near to David, with the shield-bearer before him.

**42** When the Philistine looked and saw David, he despised him, for he was a youth, ruddy and handsome in appearance.

**43** The Philistine said to David, "Am I a dog, that you come to me with sticks?" Then the Philistine cursed David by his gods.

**44** The Philistine said to David, "Come to me, and I will give your flesh to the birds of the heavens and the beasts of the field."

**45** David said to the Philistine, "You come to me with sword and spear and javelin, but I come to you in the name of the LORD of hosts, the God of the ranks of Israel, whom you have defied.

**46** This day the LORD will deliver you into my hand, and I will strike you down and cut off your head. I will give the corpses of the camp of the Philistines this day to the birds of the heavens and the wild animals of the earth, so that all the earth may know that there is a God in Israel,

**47** and all this assembly may know that the LORD does not save by sword and spear. For the battle is the LORD's, and He will give you into our hand."

**48** When the Philistine rose and came on and drew near to meet David, David hurried and ran toward the battle line to meet the Philistine.

**49** David put his hand into the bag, took out a stone, slung it and struck the Philistine on his forehead. The stone sank into his forehead, and he fell on his face to the ground.

**50** So David prevailed over the Philistine with a sling and a stone. He struck the Philistine and killed him, and there was no sword in David's hand.

**51** David ran and stood over the Philistine, took his sword, drew it out of its sheath and killed him, and cut off his head with it. When the Philistines saw that their champion was dead, they fled.

**52** The men of Israel and Judah rose with a shout and pursued the Philistines as far as the approach to the valley and to the gates of Ekron. The Philistine dead fell on the road from Shaaraim as far as Gath and Ekron.

**53** The sons of Israel came back from chasing the Philistines and plundered their camp.

**54** David took the head of the Philistine and brought it to Jerusalem, and he put his weapons in his tent.

## "Whose Son Is This Youth?"

**55** When Saul saw David going out to meet the Philistine, he said to Abner, the commander of the army, "Abner, whose son is this youth?" Abner said, "As your soul lives, O king, I do not know."

**56** The king said, "Find out whose son the young man is."

**57** When David returned from killing the Philistine, Abner took him and brought him before Saul, with the Philistine's head in his hand.

**58** Saul said to him, "Whose son are you, young man?" David said, "The son of your servant Jesse the Bethlehemite."

## Notes

- **v4 "A champion… six cubits and a span"** — *ish ha-benayim*, **the man of the space between**: the one who steps into the ground between the two lines to fight for his side. The Greek has *four cubits and a span*.
- **vv5–7 "a coat of scale armor… bronze greaves… like a weaver's beam"** — *qasqassim*, the word for the **scales** of a fish (Leviticus 11:9). The greaves, *mitschat*, are built on *metsach*, **forehead**: he wears a forehead-piece on his legs, and the stone finds the forehead (v49). *A weaver's beam* is said again of the spear of *Goliath the Gittite* in 2 Samuel 21:19, where Elhanan of Bethlehem kills him; 1 Chronicles 20:5 has Elhanan kill *Lahmi the brother of Goliath the Gittite*, with the same spear.
- **vv10, 26 "I defy the ranks of Israel… takes away the disgrace from Israel"** — *cheraf*, **defy, taunt**, and its noun *cherpah*, **disgrace**, the word of Nahash, who wanted to *bring disgrace on all Israel* (11:2). David hears the taunt as aimed at *the ranks of the living God*.
- **vv12–31 "David was the son of that Ephrathite"** — the Greek has none of vv12–31, nor v41, v50 or vv55–58. Without them, David is already Saul's armor-bearer (16:21) and simply goes out against the Philistine. With them, he is introduced a second time, as a shepherd who brings food to his brothers. The Hebrew keeps both, and the text follows it.
- **v12 "the man was old, well on in years"** — the Hebrew is *ba va-anashim*, **come among men**. The usual phrase is *ba ba-yamim*, **coming on in days**, said of Abraham (Genesis 24:1); the sense is the same.
- **v28 "I know your insolence and the evil of your heart"** — Eliab is the brother the LORD passed over with *the LORD looks at the heart* (16:7). Now Eliab claims to see his youngest brother's heart.
- **vv34–37 "the paw of the lion… the hand of this Philistine"** — *yad*, **hand**, in both places: David puts the Philistine among the beasts he has already fought (v36). Saul's *the LORD be with you* echoes the servant's word about David, *the LORD is with him* (16:18).
- **vv45–47 "the LORD does not save by sword and spear"** — in Saul's army *neither sword nor spear was found* except with Saul and Jonathan (13:22). David turns the lack into the point, as Jonathan did: *nothing restrains the LORD from saving by many or by few* (14:6). The fight is fought *so that all the earth may know that there is a God in Israel*.
- **v54 "brought it to Jerusalem, and he put his weapons in his tent"** — Jerusalem was still held by the Jebusites; David takes it as king (2 Samuel 5:6–9). The sword turns up later with the priest at Nob, *wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod* (21:9).
- **vv55–58 "Whose son is this youth?"** — Saul already *loved him greatly* and made him his armor-bearer (16:21), and sent to Jesse by name (16:22). Here neither Saul nor Abner knows him. This scene is one of those the Greek does not have.

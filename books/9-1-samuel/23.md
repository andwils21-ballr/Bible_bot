# 1 Samuel 23

## David Saves Keilah

**1** David was told, "The Philistines are fighting against Keilah and are plundering the threshing floors."

**2** So David inquired of the LORD, "Shall I go and strike these Philistines?" The LORD said to David, "Go, strike the Philistines and save Keilah."

**3** But David's men said to him, "We are afraid here in Judah. How much more if we go to Keilah against the ranks of the Philistines!"

**4** David inquired of the LORD again, and the LORD answered him, "Arise, go down to Keilah, for I am giving the Philistines into your hand."

**5** So David and his men went to Keilah and fought the Philistines. He drove off their livestock and struck them with a great slaughter. So David saved the inhabitants of Keilah.

**6** When Abiathar son of Ahimelech fled to David at Keilah, he came down with an ephod in his hand.

**7** Saul was told that David had come to Keilah. Saul said, "God has handed him over to me, for he has shut himself in by entering a town with gates and bars."

**8** Saul summoned all the people to war, to go down to Keilah and besiege David and his men.

**9** David knew that Saul was plotting evil against him, and he said to Abiathar the priest, "Bring the ephod here."

**10** David said, "O LORD God of Israel, Your servant has heard for certain that Saul seeks to come to Keilah to destroy the town because of me.

**11** Will the lords of Keilah surrender me into his hand? Will Saul come down, as Your servant has heard? O LORD God of Israel, please tell Your servant." The LORD said, "He will come down."

**12** David said, "Will the lords of Keilah surrender me and my men into Saul's hand?" The LORD said, "They will surrender you."

**13** So David and his men, about six hundred, arose and left Keilah and went wherever they could go. When Saul was told that David had escaped from Keilah, he gave up the expedition.

## Jonathan at Horesh

**14** David stayed in the wilderness, in the strongholds, in the hill country of the wilderness of Ziph. Saul sought him every day, but God did not give him into his hand.

**15** David saw that Saul had come out to seek his life. David was in the wilderness of Ziph, at Horesh.

**16** Jonathan, Saul's son, arose and went to David at Horesh and strengthened his hand in God.

**17** He said to him, "Do not be afraid, for the hand of Saul my father will not find you. You will be king over Israel, and I will be second to you. Saul my father also knows this."

**18** The two of them made a covenant before the LORD. David stayed at Horesh, and Jonathan went home.

## The Ziphites

**19** Then the Ziphites went up to Saul at Gibeah and said, "Is not David hiding among us in the strongholds at Horesh, on the hill of Hachilah, which is south of Jeshimon?

**20** So, O king, come down whenever your soul desires to come down, and it will be our part to surrender him into the king's hand."

**21** Saul said, "Blessed are you by the LORD, for you have had pity on me.

**22** Go now, make sure once more. Find out and see the place where his foot rests, and who has seen him there, for I am told he is very shrewd.

**23** See and learn all the hiding places where he hides, and come back to me with sure word, and I will go with you. And it shall come to pass, if he is in the land, that I will search him out among all the clans of Judah."

**24** They arose and went to Ziph ahead of Saul. David and his men were in the wilderness of Maon, in the Arabah south of Jeshimon.

**25** Saul and his men went to seek him. When David was told, he went down to the rock and stayed in the wilderness of Maon. When Saul heard of it, he pursued David into the wilderness of Maon.

**26** Saul went on one side of the mountain, and David and his men on the other side of the mountain. David was hurrying to get away from Saul, while Saul and his men were closing in on David and his men to seize them.

**27** But a messenger came to Saul, saying, "Hurry and come, for the Philistines have made a raid on the land."

**28** So Saul turned back from pursuing David and went to meet the Philistines. That is why that place was called Sela-hammahlekoth.

**29** David went up from there and stayed in the strongholds of En-gedi.

## Notes

- **vv2, 4 "David inquired of the LORD… and the LORD answered him"** — twice, and the answer comes both times. When Saul asked, *He did not answer him that day* (14:37), and at the end *the LORD did not answer him, neither by dreams nor by Urim nor by prophets* (28:6).
- **vv6, 9 "he came down with an ephod in his hand… Bring the ephod here"** — the one priest who escaped Nob (22:20) brings the ephod to David. Saul killed the priests; the means of asking the LORD is now in David's camp.
- **vv7, 14 "God has handed him over to me… but God did not give him into his hand"** — *nikkar*, **made strange, handed over**, a rare use of the word; the Greek has *sold*. Saul reads the LORD's will from the gates of Keilah. The narrator answers him in v14.
- **vv11–12 "Will the lords of Keilah surrender me?… They will surrender you"** — *ba'alei Qe'ilah*, as *the lords of Shechem*. The town David has just saved would hand him over. The Greek has no v12 and gives the answer *He will be shut in* to the first question.
- **vv16–18 "strengthened his hand in God… I will be second to you"** — *le-mishneh*, **second**: the king's son gives up the throne in words. This is the last time Jonathan and David meet. Jonathan dies with his father on Mount Gilboa (31:2).
- **v19 "Is not David hiding among us?"** — the words of the heading of Psalm 54, *when the Ziphites came and said to Saul, "Is not David hiding among us?"*
- **v21 "for you have had pity on me"** — *chamaltem*, the verb of 15:9, when *Saul and the people spared Agag*. Saul's pity went to the king of Amalek; now he asks it for himself.
- **v22 "I am told he is very shrewd"** — *arom ya'rim*, from *arum*, the word of the serpent, *shrewder than every living thing of the field* (Genesis 3:1).
- **v28 "Sela-hammahlekoth"** — *sela*, **rock**, and *machleqot*, from *chalaq*, **to divide**: the rock where the two sides parted. The same letters also mean **smooth**. The Greek has *the Divided Rock*.
- **v29 "the strongholds of En-gedi"** — in the Hebrew this verse is 24:1, and chapter 24 runs one verse ahead of the English.

# 1 Samuel 15

## The Command Against Amalek

**1** Samuel said to Saul, "The LORD sent me to anoint you king over His people, over Israel. So now listen to the words of the LORD.

**2** Thus says the LORD of hosts: 'I have taken note of what Amalek did to Israel, how he set himself against him on the way when he came up from Egypt.

**3** Now go and strike Amalek, and devote to destruction all that he has. Do not spare him, but put to death man and woman, child and infant, ox and sheep, camel and donkey.'"

**4** Saul summoned the people and mustered them at Telaim: two hundred thousand foot soldiers, and ten thousand men of Judah.

**5** Saul came to the city of Amalek and lay in wait in the valley.

**6** Saul said to the Kenites, "Go, turn aside, go down from among the Amalekites, in case I sweep you away with them. For you showed loyal love to all the sons of Israel when they came up from Egypt." So the Kenites turned aside from among the Amalekites.

**7** Saul struck Amalek from Havilah as you come to Shur, which faces Egypt.

**8** He took Agag king of Amalek alive, and all the people he devoted to destruction with the edge of the sword.

**9** But Saul and the people spared Agag, and the best of the sheep and the cattle, the next best and the lambs, and all that was good, and would not devote them to destruction. Everything that was despised and worthless they devoted to destruction.

## The LORD Is Sorry

**10** The word of the LORD came to Samuel:

**11** "I am sorry that I made Saul king, for he has turned back from following Me and has not carried out My words." Samuel was angry, and he cried out to the LORD all night.

**12** Samuel rose early to meet Saul in the morning. Samuel was told, "Saul came to Carmel and set up a monument for himself there, and he has turned and passed on and gone down to Gilgal."

**13** Samuel came to Saul, and Saul said to him, "Blessed are you by the LORD! I have carried out the word of the LORD."

**14** Samuel said, "Then what is this bleating of sheep in my ears, and the lowing of cattle that I hear?"

**15** Saul said, "They have brought them from the Amalekites, for the people spared the best of the sheep and the cattle to sacrifice to the LORD your God. The rest we have devoted to destruction."

**16** Samuel said to Saul, "Stop, and let me tell you what the LORD said to me tonight." He said to him, "Speak."

**17** Samuel said, "Though you are small in your own eyes, are you not the head of the tribes of Israel? The LORD anointed you king over Israel.

**18** The LORD sent you on a mission and said, 'Go, devote to destruction the sinners, the Amalekites, and fight against them until you have wiped them out.'

**19** Why then did you not listen to the voice of the LORD, but pounced on the spoil and did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD?"

**20** Saul said to Samuel, "But I did listen to the voice of the LORD. I went on the mission on which the LORD sent me. I brought back Agag king of Amalek, and I devoted Amalek to destruction.

**21** But the people took sheep and cattle from the spoil, the best of what was devoted to destruction, to sacrifice to the LORD your God at Gilgal."

**22** Samuel said,  
"Does the LORD delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices  
as much as in listening to the voice of the LORD?  
To listen is better than sacrifice,  
to pay attention than the fat of rams.

**23** For rebellion is the sin of soothsaying,  
and insistence is wickedness and household gods.  
Because you have rejected the word of the LORD,  
He has rejected you from being king."

## The Torn Robe

**24** Saul said to Samuel, "I have sinned, for I have transgressed the command of the LORD and your words, because I feared the people and listened to their voice.

**25** Now please forgive my sin and return with me, so that I may bow down to the LORD."

**26** Samuel said to Saul, "I will not return with you, for you have rejected the word of the LORD, and the LORD has rejected you from being king over Israel."

**27** As Samuel turned to go, Saul took hold of the edge of his robe, and it tore.

**28** Samuel said to him, "The LORD has torn the kingdom of Israel from you today and has given it to a neighbor of yours who is better than you.

**29** Also the Enduring One of Israel will not lie or change His mind, for He is not a man, that He should change His mind."

**30** Saul said, "I have sinned. Yet honor me now, please, before the elders of my people and before Israel, and return with me, so that I may bow down to the LORD your God."

**31** So Samuel returned after Saul, and Saul bowed down to the LORD.

## Agag

**32** Samuel said, "Bring Agag king of Amalek to me." Agag came to him cheerfully, and Agag said, "Surely the bitterness of death has passed."

**33** Samuel said,  
"As your sword has made women childless,  
so shall your mother be childless among women."  
Samuel hewed Agag to pieces before the LORD at Gilgal.

**34** Then Samuel went to Ramah, and Saul went up to his house at Gibeah of Saul.

**35** Samuel did not see Saul again until the day of his death, for Samuel mourned over Saul. The LORD was sorry that He had made Saul king over Israel.

## Notes

- **v2 "what Amalek did to Israel… on the way when he came up from Egypt"** — Amalek attacked Israel at Rephidim, and the LORD said, *I will utterly blot out the memory of Amalek from under the heavens* (Exodus 17:14). The law repeats it: *Remember what Amalek did to you on the way when you came out of Egypt… you shall blot out the memory of Amalek* (Deuteronomy 25:17–19). Samuel gives Saul that command.
- **v3 "devote to destruction"** — *ve-hacharamtem*, the *cherem* of Jericho, where Israel *devoted to destruction all that was in the city, man and woman… ox and sheep and donkey* (Joshua 6:21). What is devoted belongs to the LORD, and no one may keep it; that is the point on which Saul fails.
- **v6 "you showed loyal love to all the sons of Israel"** — *chesed*. The Kenites were the family of Moses' father-in-law, who went up with Judah into the land (Judges 1:16). Saul keeps faith with them before he strikes Amalek.
- **v9 "the next best and the lambs"** — *ha-mishnim*, **the second ones**, as the Levites *of the second rank* (1 Chronicles 15:18). The Greek has *the foods*, and for *karim*, **lambs**, it has *vineyards*. *Would not*, *lo avu*: the sparing was chosen.
- **vv11, 29, 35 "I am sorry… He is not a man, that He should change His mind… The LORD was sorry"** — one verb, *nicham*, three times. The LORD *was sorry* over Saul as He was over humankind before the flood (Genesis 6:6). Between the two, v29 says He *will not change His mind*, close to Balaam's oracle, *God is not a man… that He should change His mind* (Numbers 23:19): the word just spoken, that the kingdom is torn from Saul (v28), stands. *Netsach*, **the Enduring One**, is the word in *la-netsach*, **forever** (2 Samuel 2:26). The Greek of v29 has *Israel will be divided in two*.
- **v12 "set up a monument for himself"** — *matsiv lo yad*, **setting up a hand for himself**. *Yad*, **hand**, is a monument, as Absalom's pillar was called *Absalom's hand* (2 Samuel 18:18). The Greek goes on in v13 with Saul *offering a burnt offering to the Lord, the first of the spoils he had brought from Amalek*.
- **v19 "pounced on the spoil"** — *va-ta'at*, the word of 14:32, when the people *pounced on the spoil* and ate with the blood. In chapter 14 Saul stopped the people from eating with the blood; here the same rush is charged to him.
- **vv22–23 "To listen is better than sacrifice… rebellion is the sin of soothsaying"** — *shemoa*, **to listen**, is the verb of *listen to the words of the LORD* (v1) and of Saul's *I listened to their voice* (v24). *Qesem* is the practice of the *qosem*, the **soothsayer** the law forbids (Deuteronomy 18:10). *Haftsar*, **insistence**, is the verb of Lot, who *pressed them greatly* (Genesis 19:3). The Greek has *omen-taking* for *soothsaying*.
- **vv27–28 "Saul took hold of the edge of his robe, and it tore… The LORD has torn the kingdom"** — the Hebrew says only *he took hold*; the Greek names Saul. *His robe* is Samuel's, since Samuel is the one turning to go: the *me'il*, the garment his mother made him each year (2:19), and the robe in which Saul will know him at Endor (28:14). David will cut off *the edge of Saul's robe* (24:4), and Samuel will say again, *the LORD has torn the kingdom from your hand and given it to your neighbor, to David* (28:17).
- **vv32–33 "Agag came to him cheerfully… Samuel hewed Agag to pieces"** — *ma'adannot*, a form of the word in *a king's delicacies* (Genesis 49:20), fits Agag's *surely the bitterness of death has passed*. The same word is *the bonds* of the Pleiades in Job 38:31, which would put Agag in chains. The Greek has *trembling*. *Va-yeshassef*, **hewed to pieces**, occurs only here.

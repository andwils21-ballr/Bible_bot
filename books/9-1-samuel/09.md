# 1 Samuel 9

## The Search

**1** There was a man of Benjamin whose name was Kish son of Abiel, son of Zeror, son of Becorath, son of Aphiah, a Benjaminite, a man of worth.

**2** He had a son whose name was Saul, a choice young man and handsome. There was no man among the sons of Israel more handsome than he; from his shoulders upward he was taller than any of the people.

**3** The donkeys of Kish, Saul's father, were lost. Kish said to Saul his son, "Take one of the young men with you, and arise, go and look for the donkeys."

**4** He passed through the hill country of Ephraim and through the land of Shalishah, but they did not find them. They passed through the land of Shaalim, but they were not there. Then he passed through the land of Benjamin, but they did not find them.

**5** When they came to the land of Zuph, Saul said to his young man who was with him, "Come, let us go back, or my father will stop worrying about the donkeys and become anxious about us."

**6** But he said to him, "There is a man of God in this town, and the man is held in honor; all that he says comes true. Let us go there now. Perhaps he can tell us about the journey we have set out on."

**7** Saul said to his young man, "But if we go, what shall we bring the man? For the bread in our sacks is gone, and there is no gift to bring to the man of God. What do we have?"

**8** The young man answered Saul again and said, "I have a quarter of a shekel of silver with me. I will give it to the man of God, and he will tell us our way."

**9** (Formerly in Israel, when a man went to inquire of God, he would say, "Come, let us go to the seer," for the one who is now called a prophet was formerly called a seer.)

**10** Saul said to his young man, "Well said. Come, let us go." So they went to the town where the man of God was.

## Samuel

**11** As they went up the ascent to the town, they met young women coming out to draw water, and said to them, "Is the seer here?"

**12** They answered them, "He is; he is just ahead of you. Hurry now, for he has come to the town today, because the people have a sacrifice today on the high place.

**13** As soon as you enter the town you will find him, before he goes up to the high place to eat, for the people will not eat until he comes, because he blesses the sacrifice; afterward those who are invited eat. So go up now, for you will find him about this time."

**14** So they went up to the town. As they were entering the town, Samuel was coming out toward them on his way up to the high place.

**15** Now a day before Saul came, the LORD had revealed to Samuel:

**16** "About this time tomorrow I will send you a man from the land of Benjamin, and you shall anoint him to be leader over My people Israel. He will save My people from the hand of the Philistines, for I have seen My people, because their cry has come to Me."

**17** When Samuel saw Saul, the LORD told him, "There is the man of whom I spoke to you! He shall restrain My people."

**18** Saul came up to Samuel in the gateway and said, "Please tell me where the seer's house is."

**19** Samuel answered Saul, "I am the seer. Go up before me to the high place, for you shall eat with me today, and in the morning I will let you go and will tell you all that is in your heart.

**20** As for your donkeys that were lost three days ago, do not set your heart on them, for they have been found. On whom is all the desire of Israel? Is it not on you and on all your father's house?"

**21** Saul answered, "Am I not a Benjaminite, from the smallest of the tribes of Israel? Is not my clan the least of all the clans of the tribe of Benjamin? Why then have you spoken to me like this?"

## The Meal at the High Place

**22** Samuel took Saul and his young man and brought them into the hall and gave them a place at the head of those who had been invited, about thirty men.

**23** Samuel said to the cook, "Bring the portion I gave you, of which I said to you, 'Put it aside.'"

**24** So the cook took up the thigh and what was on it and set it before Saul. Samuel said, "See, what was kept is set before you. Eat, for it has been kept for you until the appointed time, since I said, 'I have invited the people.'" So Saul ate with Samuel that day.

**25** When they came down from the high place into the town, he spoke with Saul on the roof.

**26** They rose early. And it came to pass at the break of dawn that Samuel called to Saul on the roof, "Get up, and I will send you on your way." Saul got up, and both of them, he and Samuel, went outside.

**27** As they were going down to the edge of the town, Samuel said to Saul, "Tell the young man to go on ahead of us." When he had gone on, Samuel said, "But you stand still for a while, so that I may make you hear the word of God."

## Notes

- **v1 "a man of worth"** — *gibbor chayil*, as Boaz is described in Ruth 2:1: a man of wealth and standing. The written text has *mi-ben yamin*, **from a son of the right hand**, and the reading *mi-Binyamin*, **from Benjamin**; the meaning is the same.
- **v2 "a choice young man and handsome… taller than any of the people"** — *bachur va-tov*. The chapter introduces Saul by his looks and height; 16:7 will set that measure aside, *the LORD looks on the heart*. *Saul*, *Sha'ul*, is **asked for**: the people asked Samuel for a king (8:10), and this is the one asked for.
- **v6 "all that he says comes true"** — *bo yavo*, the report of 3:19: the LORD let none of Samuel's words fall to the ground.
- **v9 "the one who is now called a prophet was formerly called a seer"** — the narrator explains an old word for his readers, as Ruth 4:7 explained an old custom. *Ro'eh*, **seer**, is from *ra'ah*, **to see**.
- **v16 "anoint him to be leader… for I have seen My people, because their cry has come to Me"** — *nagid*, **leader**, not *melekh*, **king**; the LORD does not use the people's word. *Their cry has come to Me* are the words of the LORD at the bush: *the cry of the sons of Israel has come to Me* (Exodus 3:9). The king is sent as a deliverer, as Moses was.
- **v17 "He shall restrain My people"** — *ya'tsor*, **hold back, restrain**, an unusual word for ruling. The Greek has *he shall rule*.
- **v20 "on whom is all the desire of Israel?"** — *chemdat Yisra'el*, what Israel longs for. Samuel answers a question about donkeys with a hint of a kingdom.
- **v21 "Am I not a Benjaminite, from the smallest of the tribes of Israel?"** — Benjamin was nearly destroyed in Judges 20–21. The answer is Gideon's, *My clan is the weakest in Manasseh* (Judges 6:15).
- **v24 "the thigh and what was on it"** — *ha-shoq*, the portion the law gives the priest from a peace offering (Leviticus 7:32). Samuel sets the priest's portion before Saul. *Since I said, 'I have invited the people'* is compressed in the Hebrew; the Greek has *it was set before you as a witness, above the others*.
- **v25 "he spoke with Saul on the roof"** — *va-yedabber*. The Greek has *they spread a bed for Saul on the roof, and he slept*, which fits v26, where Samuel calls to Saul *on the roof* at dawn. The text follows the Hebrew.

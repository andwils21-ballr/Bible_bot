# 1 Samuel 2

## Hannah's Song

**1** Then Hannah prayed and said:

My heart exults in the LORD;  
my horn is lifted high in the LORD.  
My mouth is wide open over my enemies,  
for I rejoice in Your salvation.

**2** There is none holy like the LORD,  
for there is none besides You;  
there is no rock like our God.

**3** Do not keep talking so very proudly;  
let no arrogance come from your mouth,  
for the LORD is a God of knowledge,  
and by Him actions are weighed.

**4** The bows of the mighty are broken,  
and those who stumbled are girded with strength.

**5** Those who were full hire themselves out for bread,  
and those who were hungry hunger no more.  
The barren has borne seven,  
and she who has many children languishes.

**6** The LORD puts to death and makes alive;  
He brings down to Sheol and brings up.

**7** The LORD makes poor and makes rich;  
He brings low; He also lifts up.

**8** He raises the poor from the dust;  
He lifts the needy from the ash heap,  
to seat them with nobles  
and make them inherit a seat of honor.  
For the pillars of the earth are the LORD's,  
and He has set the world on them.

**9** He guards the feet of His faithful ones,  
but the wicked are silenced in darkness,  
for not by strength shall a man prevail.

**10** The LORD — those who contend with Him are shattered;  
against them He thunders in the heavens.  
The LORD judges the ends of the earth.  
He gives strength to His king  
and lifts high the horn of His anointed.

## The Sons of Eli

**11** Then Elkanah went home to Ramah, and the boy ministered to the LORD before Eli the priest.

**12** The sons of Eli were worthless men; they did not know the LORD.

**13** The custom of the priests with the people was this: whenever anyone offered a sacrifice, the priest's servant would come while the meat was boiling, with a three-pronged fork in his hand,

**14** and he would thrust it into the pan or kettle or cauldron or pot. All that the fork brought up the priest would take for himself. This is what they did to all Israel who came there to Shiloh.

**15** Even before the fat was burned, the priest's servant would come and say to the man who was sacrificing, "Give meat for the priest to roast. He will not take boiled meat from you, only raw."

**16** If the man said to him, "Let them burn the fat first, and then take as much as you wish," he would say, "No, you must give it now, and if not, I will take it by force."

**17** So the sin of the young men was very great before the LORD, for the men treated the offering of the LORD with contempt.

## Samuel at Shiloh

**18** Samuel was ministering before the LORD, a boy clothed with a linen ephod.

**19** His mother would make him a little robe and bring it up to him year by year, when she went up with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice.

**20** Eli would bless Elkanah and his wife and say, "May the LORD give you children by this woman in place of the one she asked for and lent to the LORD." Then they would go back to their home.

**21** The LORD visited Hannah, and she conceived and bore three sons and two daughters. The boy Samuel grew up before the LORD.

**22** Eli was very old. He heard all that his sons were doing to all Israel, and how they lay with the women who served at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**23** He said to them, "Why do you do such things? For I hear of your evil dealings from all these people.

**24** No, my sons; it is not a good report that I hear the people of the LORD spreading.

**25** If one man sins against another, God will mediate for him; but if a man sins against the LORD, who can intercede for him?" But they would not listen to the voice of their father, for the LORD wished to put them to death.

**26** The boy Samuel kept growing in stature and in favor, both with the LORD and with men.

## A Man of God

**27** A man of God came to Eli and said to him, "Thus says the LORD: 'Did I not reveal Myself to the house of your father when they were in Egypt, subject to the house of Pharaoh?

**28** I chose him out of all the tribes of Israel to be My priest, to go up to My altar, to burn incense, to wear an ephod before Me, and I gave to the house of your father all the offerings by fire of the sons of Israel.

**29** Why then do you kick at My sacrifice and My offering, which I commanded for My dwelling, and honor your sons above Me by fattening yourselves on the choicest parts of every offering of Israel My people?'

**30** Therefore the LORD, the God of Israel, declares: 'I did say that your house and the house of your father would walk before Me forever.' But now the LORD declares: 'Far be it from Me! For those who honor Me I will honor, and those who despise Me shall be lightly esteemed.

**31** The days are coming when I will cut off your strength and the strength of your father's house, so that there will not be an old man in your house.

**32** You will see distress in My dwelling, in all the good that He does for Israel, and there shall not be an old man in your house forever.

**33** The man of yours whom I do not cut off from My altar shall be spared to wear out your eyes and grieve your soul, and all the increase of your house shall die as men.

**34** This shall be the sign to you, which shall come on your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas: on one day both of them shall die.

**35** I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest, who shall do according to what is in My heart and in My soul. I will build him a sure house, and he shall walk before My anointed forever.

**36** And it shall come to pass that everyone who is left in your house shall come to bow down to him for a piece of silver or a loaf of bread, and shall say, "Please put me in one of the priest's places, so that I may eat a piece of bread."'"

## Notes

- **vv1, 10 "my horn is lifted high… the horn of His anointed"** — *qeren*, the horn of an animal, the Bible's picture of strength. The song opens with Hannah's horn and closes with the horn of *His anointed*, *meshicho*, the first time the word *mashiach* is used of a king, though Israel has no king yet. The Greek has *christou autou*.
- **v2 "there is no rock like our God"** — *tsur*, the name of God in the Song of Moses: *The Rock — His work is whole* (Deuteronomy 32:4).
- **v3 "by Him actions are weighed"** — the written text has *lo*, **not**: *actions are not weighed*. The reading tradition has *lo*, **by Him**, and the Greek, *a God preparing His ways*, agrees with it; the two words sound alike. The text follows the reading.
- **v5 "The barren has borne seven"** — Hannah will have six children (v21); *seven* is the full number, as in *better to you than seven sons* (Ruth 4:15).
- **v6 "The LORD puts to death and makes alive"** — the words of Deuteronomy 32:39, *I put to death and I make alive*. *Sheol* is the place of the dead.
- **vv7–8 "He brings low; He also lifts up… He raises the poor from the dust"** — Mary's song in Luke 1:52–53 takes up the same reversal: *He has brought down rulers from thrones and lifted up the lowly; the hungry He has filled with good things.* The Greek of 1 Samuel 2:10 adds a passage found in Jeremiah 9:23–24, *Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom… but let him who boasts boast in this, that he understands and knows the Lord.*
- **vv13–16 "the custom of the priests"** — the law gave the priest *the breast that is waved and the thigh* after the fat was burned (Leviticus 7:31, 34), for *all fat belongs to the LORD* (3:16). Eli's sons take their share with a fork before the LORD's part is burned, and by force.
- **v25 "for the LORD wished to put them to death"** — *ki chafets YHWH lahamitam*. Their refusal to listen is tied to the LORD's purpose, as Pharaoh's hardened heart was. *God will mediate*, *u-fillelo Elohim*, and *who can intercede*, *yitpallel*, are the same root, the root of *prayer*.
- **v26 "in stature and in favor, both with the LORD and with men"** — Luke 2:52 says the same of the boy Jesus: *in wisdom and stature and in favor with God and men*.
- **vv29–30 "honor… lightly esteemed"** — *kavod* is **weight**, and *qalal* is **to be light**. Eli *honors*, gives weight to, his sons over God, and the sons are *fattening* themselves. 4:18 will say that Eli died because he was *old and heavy*, *kaved*.

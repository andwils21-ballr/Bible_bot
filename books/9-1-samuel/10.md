# 1 Samuel 10

## The Anointing

**1** Then Samuel took the flask of oil and poured it on his head and kissed him and said, "Has not the LORD anointed you leader over His people Israel? You shall reign over the people of the LORD and save them from the hand of their enemies all around. This shall be the sign to you that the LORD has anointed you leader over His inheritance:

**2** When you leave me today, you will find two men by Rachel's tomb in the territory of Benjamin at Zelzah. They will say to you, 'The donkeys you went to look for have been found. Your father has stopped caring about the donkeys and is anxious about you, saying, "What shall I do about my son?"'

**3** Then you will go on from there and come to the oak of Tabor. Three men going up to God at Bethel will meet you there, one carrying three young goats, another carrying three loaves of bread, and another carrying a skin of wine.

**4** They will greet you and give you two loaves of bread, which you shall accept from their hand.

**5** After that you will come to Gibeath-elohim, where the Philistine garrison is. And it shall come to pass, as you come there to the town, that you will meet a band of prophets coming down from the high place, with harp, tambourine, flute and lyre before them, and they will be prophesying.

**6** Then the Spirit of the LORD will rush on you, and you will prophesy with them and be turned into another man.

**7** And it shall come to pass, when these signs come to you, that you shall do whatever you find to do, for God is with you.

**8** Then go down before me to Gilgal. I will come down to you to offer burnt offerings and to sacrifice peace offerings. Seven days you shall wait, until I come to you and show you what you are to do."

## The Signs

**9** When he turned his back to go from Samuel, God gave him another heart, and all these signs came to pass that day.

**10** When they came there to Gibeah, a band of prophets met him, and the Spirit of God rushed on him, and he prophesied among them.

**11** When all who had known him before saw him prophesying with the prophets, the people said to one another, "What has happened to the son of Kish? Is Saul also among the prophets?"

**12** A man from there answered, "But who is their father?" So it became a proverb: "Is Saul also among the prophets?"

**13** When he had finished prophesying, he came to the high place.

**14** Saul's uncle said to him and to his young man, "Where did you go?" He said, "To look for the donkeys. When we saw that they were nowhere to be found, we went to Samuel."

**15** Saul's uncle said, "Please tell me what Samuel said to you."

**16** Saul said to his uncle, "He told us plainly that the donkeys had been found." But about the matter of the kingdom, of which Samuel had spoken, he did not tell him.

## The Lot at Mizpah

**17** Samuel called the people together to the LORD at Mizpah.

**18** He said to the sons of Israel, "Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: 'I brought Israel up from Egypt, and I delivered you from the hand of the Egyptians and from the hand of all the kingdoms that oppressed you.'

**19** But today you have rejected your God, who saves you from all your troubles and distresses, and you have said to Him, 'No, set a king over us.' So now present yourselves before the LORD by your tribes and by your thousands."

**20** Samuel brought all the tribes of Israel near, and the tribe of Benjamin was taken.

**21** He brought the tribe of Benjamin near by its clans, and the clan of the Matrites was taken. Then he brought the clan of the Matrites near man by man, and Saul son of Kish was taken. But when they looked for him, he could not be found.

**22** So they inquired of the LORD again, "Has the man come here yet?" The LORD said, "He is hiding among the baggage."

**23** They ran and took him from there, and when he stood among the people, he was taller than any of the people from his shoulders upward.

**24** Samuel said to all the people, "Do you see the one whom the LORD has chosen? There is none like him among all the people." All the people shouted and said, "Long live the king!"

**25** Then Samuel told the people the rule of the kingship and wrote it in a book and laid it up before the LORD. Then Samuel sent all the people away, each to his home.

**26** Saul also went home to Gibeah, and with him went the men of valor whose hearts God had touched.

**27** But some worthless men said, "How can this man save us?" They despised him and brought him no gift. But he kept silent.

## Notes

- **v1 "You shall reign over the people of the LORD… And this shall be the sign to you"** — these words are in the Greek and not in the Hebrew, whose verse ends with *Has not the LORD anointed you leader over His inheritance?* The Greek names the sign that vv2–7 go on to give (*when these signs come to you*, v7); the Hebrew never introduces it. The phrase *anointed you… leader* stands twice in the Greek, and the Hebrew has only the second, as if the eye had passed from the first to the second. The text follows the Greek.
- **v1 "the flask of oil… anointed you leader"** — *nagid*, as in 9:16. This is the first anointing of a king in Israel; the flask, *pakh*, is the same word used when Elisha sends one of the sons of the prophets with *this flask of oil* to anoint Jehu (2 Kings 9:1).
- **v2 "Rachel's tomb in the territory of Benjamin at Zelzah"** — Genesis 35:19–20 puts the grave *on the road to Ephrath*, where Jacob set up a pillar *to this day*. Saul, a son of Rachel's son Benjamin, begins his road at her grave.
- **v5 "Gibeath-elohim, where the Philistine garrison is"** — **the hill of God**, held by a Philistine *netsiv*, a garrison or an officer. The Greek reads *Nasib the Philistine*, as a man's name. The new king's first task is in sight on the first day.
- **vv6, 10 "the Spirit of the LORD will rush on you… be turned into another man"** — *tsalach*, the verb of the Spirit on Samson (Judges 14:6). In v9 *God gave him another heart*: the change is given before he has done anything.
- **vv11–12 "Is Saul also among the prophets?… But who is their father?"** — the proverb is given twice in the book, here and at 19:24, with two different stories. *Who is their father?* seems to mean that prophets have no pedigree, so a wealthy man's son is out of place among them. The Greek has *his father*.
- **v16 "But about the matter of the kingdom… he did not tell him"** — the first of Saul's silences; v22 finds him hiding, and v27 has him keep silent before his mockers.
- **v19 "you have rejected your God, who saves you"** — Samuel repeats the LORD's word of 8:7 to the people's faces. The lot then falls exactly as the LORD has already chosen (9:16).
- **v21 "Then he brought the clan of the Matrites near man by man"** — this step is in the Greek; the Hebrew passes from the clan to Saul at once. The same order of tribe, clan, and man is followed when Achan is taken by lot (Joshua 7:16–18). The text follows the Greek.
- **v25 "the rule of the kingship… wrote it in a book and laid it up before the LORD"** — *mishpat ha-melukhah*, set against *the ways of the king* of 8:11, which describe what a king takes. The written law of the king recalls Deuteronomy 17:18, where the king writes *a copy of this instruction in a book*.

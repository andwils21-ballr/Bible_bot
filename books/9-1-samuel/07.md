# 1 Samuel 7

## Kiriath-jearim

**1** The men of Kiriath-jearim came and took up the ark of the LORD and brought it into the house of Abinadab on the hill. They consecrated Eleazar his son to keep the ark of the LORD.

**2** And it came to pass, from the day that the ark stayed at Kiriath-jearim, that a long time passed, twenty years, and all the house of Israel lamented after the LORD.

## Mizpah

**3** Samuel said to all the house of Israel, "If you are returning to the LORD with all your heart, put away the foreign gods and the Ashtaroth from among you, direct your hearts to the LORD and serve Him only, and He will deliver you from the hand of the Philistines."

**4** So the sons of Israel put away the Baals and the Ashtaroth, and served the LORD only.

**5** Samuel said, "Gather all Israel to Mizpah, and I will pray to the LORD for you."

**6** They gathered at Mizpah, drew water and poured it out before the LORD, and fasted on that day, and said there, "We have sinned against the LORD." Samuel judged the sons of Israel at Mizpah.

**7** When the Philistines heard that the sons of Israel had gathered at Mizpah, the lords of the Philistines went up against Israel. When the sons of Israel heard it, they were afraid of the Philistines.

**8** The sons of Israel said to Samuel, "Do not stop crying out to the LORD our God for us, so that He may save us from the hand of the Philistines."

**9** Samuel took a suckling lamb and offered it as a whole burnt offering to the LORD. Samuel cried out to the LORD for Israel, and the LORD answered him.

**10** As Samuel was offering up the burnt offering, the Philistines drew near to fight against Israel. But the LORD thundered with a great voice that day against the Philistines and threw them into panic, and they were struck down before Israel.

**11** The men of Israel went out from Mizpah and pursued the Philistines and struck them down as far as below Beth-car.

## Ebenezer

**12** Then Samuel took a stone and set it up between Mizpah and Shen, and called its name Ebenezer, saying, "Till now the LORD has helped us."

**13** So the Philistines were subdued and did not again enter the territory of Israel. The hand of the LORD was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel.

**14** The cities that the Philistines had taken from Israel were restored to Israel, from Ekron to Gath, and Israel delivered their territory from the hand of the Philistines. There was peace also between Israel and the Amorites.

**15** Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life.

**16** He would go year by year on a circuit to Bethel, Gilgal and Mizpah, and judge Israel in all these places.

**17** Then he would return to Ramah, for his home was there, and there he judged Israel. He built an altar there to the LORD.

## Notes

- **v2 "twenty years… lamented after the LORD"** — *va-yinnahu*, from *nahah*, **to wail, to mourn**, found rarely. The Greek has *looked after the Lord*. Either way, the twenty years end with Israel turning toward the LORD.
- **v3 "put away the foreign gods"** — as Jacob said before Bethel, *Remove the foreign gods that are among you* (Genesis 35:2), and Joshua at the covenant, *put away the foreign gods that are among you* (Joshua 24:23). Each time the call comes before a new start.
- **v6 "drew water and poured it out before the LORD"** — a rite found only here. *Poured out*, *va-yishpekhu*, is Hannah's verb: *I have been pouring out my soul before the LORD* (1:15). *Samuel judged the sons of Israel*: the first time the word *judge* is used of him.
- **v8 "Do not stop crying out"** — *al tacharesh mimmennu*, **do not be silent from us**. Israel has learned in chapter 4 that the ark carried into battle does not save; now they ask for prayer.
- **v9 "a whole burnt offering"** — *olah kalil*, the offering burned entire. The written text has *va-ya'aleh*, the reading *va-ya'alehu*; the meaning is the same.
- **v10 "the LORD thundered with a great voice… threw them into panic"** — *va-yar'em*, the word of Hannah's song, *against them He thunders in the heavens* (2:10). *Va-yehummem* is the verb of Exodus 14:24 and Judges 4:15: the LORD throws the enemy into panic, and Israel only pursues.
- **v12 "Ebenezer… Till now the LORD has helped us"** — *even ha-ezer*, **stone of help**. The name was used in 4:1 for the place of Israel's defeat; here it is given, at the site of a victory. *Shen* is **tooth**, a crag; the Greek reads *the old one*, *yeshanah*.
- **v13 "did not again enter the territory of Israel"** — the verse speaks of Samuel's days. Saul will fight the Philistines through his whole reign (14:52), and Goliath is a Philistine.
- **v14 "peace also between Israel and the Amorites"** — the peoples of the hill country, not the Philistines of the coast.
- **vv15–17 "a circuit to Bethel, Gilgal and Mizpah"** — the three places of Israel's old assemblies (Judges 20:1, 20:18; Joshua 5:9–10). *He built an altar there*: Shiloh is no longer spoken of after chapter 4, and Samuel worships at Ramah.

# 1 Samuel 30

## Ziklag Burned

**1** And it came to pass, when David and his men came to Ziklag on the third day, that the Amalekites had made a raid on the Negev and on Ziklag. They had struck Ziklag and burned it with fire,

**2** and had taken captive the women who were in it, from the least to the greatest. They killed no one, but carried them off and went their way.

**3** When David and his men came to the town, it had been burned with fire, and their wives and their sons and their daughters had been taken captive.

**4** David and the people with him lifted up their voices and wept until they had no strength left to weep.

**5** David's two wives had been taken captive, Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail the widow of Nabal of Carmel.

**6** David was in great distress, for the people spoke of stoning him, because the soul of all the people was bitter, each for his sons and his daughters. But David strengthened himself in the LORD his God.

## The Pursuit

**7** David said to Abiathar the priest, son of Ahimelech, "Bring me the ephod." Abiathar brought the ephod to David.

**8** David inquired of the LORD, "Shall I pursue this raiding band? Shall I overtake them?" He said to him, "Pursue, for you will overtake them and you will rescue."

**9** So David went, he and the six hundred men with him, and they came to the Wadi Besor, where those who were left behind stayed.

**10** David pursued, he and four hundred men; two hundred men stayed behind, who were too exhausted to cross the Wadi Besor.

**11** They found an Egyptian in the open country and brought him to David. They gave him bread, and he ate, and they gave him water to drink.

**12** They gave him a piece of a cake of figs and two clusters of raisins, and when he had eaten, his spirit came back to him, for he had not eaten bread or drunk water for three days and three nights.

**13** David said to him, "To whom do you belong, and where are you from?" He said, "I am a young Egyptian, the slave of an Amalekite. My master left me behind because I fell sick three days ago.

**14** We made a raid on the Negev of the Cherethites, on what belongs to Judah and on the Negev of Caleb, and we burned Ziklag with fire."

**15** David said to him, "Will you take me down to this band?" He said, "Swear to me by God that you will not kill me or hand me over to my master, and I will take you down to this band."

**16** When he had taken him down, there they were, spread out over all the land, eating and drinking and feasting on all the great spoil they had taken from the land of the Philistines and from the land of Judah.

**17** David struck them from twilight until the evening of the next day, and not a man of them escaped except four hundred young men who rode off on camels and fled.

**18** David recovered all that the Amalekites had taken, and David rescued his two wives.

**19** Nothing of theirs was missing, small or great, sons or daughters, spoil or anything they had taken for themselves. David brought everything back.

**20** David took all the flocks and the herds. They drove them ahead of the other livestock and said, "This is David's spoil."

## Equal Shares

**21** Then David came to the two hundred men who had been too exhausted to follow David, whom they had left at the Wadi Besor. They came out to meet David and the people who were with him, and David came near to the people and greeted them.

**22** Then all the wicked and worthless men among those who had gone with David answered, "Because they did not go with us, we will not give them any of the spoil we have recovered, except for each man's wife and children. Let them lead them away and go."

**23** But David said, "You shall not do so, my brothers, with what the LORD has given us. He has kept us and has given into our hand the band that came against us.

**24** Who would listen to you in this? For as is the share of the one who goes down into the battle, so shall be the share of the one who stays with the baggage; they shall share alike."

**25** And it came to pass from that day forward that he made it a statute and a rule for Israel, to this day.

**26** When David came to Ziklag, he sent some of the spoil to the elders of Judah, his friends, saying, "Here is a gift for you from the spoil of the enemies of the LORD":

**27** to those in Bethel, in Ramoth of the Negev and in Jattir,

**28** to those in Aroer, in Siphmoth and in Eshtemoa,

**29** to those in Racal, in the towns of the Jerahmeelites and in the towns of the Kenites,

**30** to those in Hormah, in Bor-ashan and in Athach,

**31** to those in Hebron, and to all the places where David and his men had gone about.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "the Amalekites had made a raid… They killed no one"** — the people David raided from Ziklag (27:8), leaving *neither man nor woman alive* (27:9, 27:11). The Amalekites raid Ziklag in return and take everyone alive.
- **v6 "the soul of all the people was bitter… But David strengthened himself in the LORD his God"** — *marah nefesh*, the bitterness of the men who first gathered to David (22:2). *Va-yitchazzeq*, **strengthened himself**, the verb of Jonathan, who *strengthened his hand in God* (23:16). Now there is no Jonathan, and David does it himself.
- **vv7–8 "Bring me the ephod… Pursue, for you will overtake them"** — the night before, Saul asked and *the LORD did not answer him* (28:6). David asks and is answered.
- **vv11–13 "an Egyptian… My master left me behind because I fell sick"** — the Amalekite abandons his sick slave; David's men feed him, and he leads them to his master's camp. *His spirit came back to him*, *va-tashov rucho*: food and water restore him.
- **v17 "except four hundred young men who rode off on camels"** — four hundred, the number who went with David (v10). Amalek, whom Saul was to destroy (15:3), is struck again but not ended.
- **v22 "all the wicked and worthless men"** — *ish ra u-veliyya'al*. *Beliyya'al*, **worthless**, is the word used of Nabal (25:17, 25:25) and of the men who despised Saul (10:27).
- **vv24–25 "they shall share alike… a statute and a rule for Israel"** — the law of the plunder of Midian divided it *between those who took part in the battle… and the whole congregation* (Numbers 31:27). *Choq u-mishpat*, **a statute and a rule**, as at Marah, *There He made for them a statute and a rule* (Exodus 15:25). David, not yet king, makes law for Israel.
- **v26 "a gift for you from the spoil of the enemies of the LORD"** — *berakhah*, **blessing**, the word Abigail used for her gift to David (25:27). *The enemies of the LORD*: David calls his wars what Saul asked of him, *fight the LORD's battles* (18:17).
- **vv27–31 "to those… in Hebron"** — the towns of Judah that sheltered David and his men. Hebron is where *the men of Judah… anointed David king over the house of Judah* (2 Samuel 2:4).

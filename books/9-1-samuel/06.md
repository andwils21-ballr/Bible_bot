# 1 Samuel 6

## The Guilt Offering

**1** The ark of the LORD was in the country of the Philistines seven months, and their land swarmed with mice.

**2** The Philistines called for the priests and the soothsayers and said, "What shall we do with the ark of the LORD? Tell us how we should send it back to its place."

**3** They said, "If you send away the ark of the God of Israel, do not send it empty, but be sure to return to Him a guilt offering. Then you will be healed, and it will be known to you why His hand has not turned away from you."

**4** They said, "What is the guilt offering that we shall return to Him?" They said, "Five golden tumors and five golden mice, according to the number of the lords of the Philistines, for one plague was on all of you and on your lords.

**5** So you shall make images of your tumors and images of your mice that ravage the land, and give glory to the God of Israel. Perhaps He will lighten His hand from you and from your gods and from your land.

**6** Why should you harden your hearts as the Egyptians and Pharaoh hardened their hearts? When He had dealt harshly with them, did they not send the people away, and they went?

**7** And now take and prepare a new cart and two milk cows on which no yoke has come. Hitch the cows to the cart, but take their calves home, away from them.

**8** Take the ark of the LORD and put it on the cart, and put the golden objects that you are returning to Him as a guilt offering in a box beside it. Then send it off, and let it go.

**9** Watch: if it goes up by the road to its own territory, to Beth-shemesh, then it is He who has done us this great harm. But if not, then we will know that it was not His hand that struck us; it happened to us by chance."

## Beth-shemesh

**10** The men did so. They took two milk cows and hitched them to the cart, and shut up their calves at home.

**11** They put the ark of the LORD on the cart, and the box with the golden mice and the images of their tumors.

**12** The cows went straight along the road toward Beth-shemesh. They kept to one highway, lowing as they went, and did not turn aside to the right or to the left. The lords of the Philistines went after them as far as the border of Beth-shemesh.

**13** The people of Beth-shemesh were reaping the wheat harvest in the valley. They lifted up their eyes and saw the ark, and they rejoiced to see it.

**14** The cart came into the field of Joshua of Beth-shemesh and stopped there. A great stone was there. They split up the wood of the cart and offered the cows as a burnt offering to the LORD.

**15** The Levites took down the ark of the LORD and the box that was with it, in which were the golden objects, and put them on the great stone. The men of Beth-shemesh offered burnt offerings and made sacrifices that day to the LORD.

**16** When the five lords of the Philistines had seen it, they returned to Ekron that day.

**17** These are the golden tumors that the Philistines returned as a guilt offering to the LORD: one for Ashdod, one for Gaza, one for Ashkelon, one for Gath, one for Ekron.

**18** The golden mice were according to the number of all the cities of the Philistines belonging to the five lords, both fortified cities and unwalled villages, as far as the great stone on which they set down the ark of the LORD, which is in the field of Joshua of Beth-shemesh to this day.

**19** He struck some of the men of Beth-shemesh, because they looked into the ark of the LORD. He struck seventy men of the people, fifty thousand men. The people mourned, because the LORD had struck the people with a great slaughter.

**20** The men of Beth-shemesh said, "Who is able to stand before the LORD, this holy God? To whom shall He go up from us?"

**21** So they sent messengers to the inhabitants of Kiriath-jearim, saying, "The Philistines have brought back the ark of the LORD. Come down and take it up to you."

## Notes

- **v1 "and their land swarmed with mice"** — the clause is in the Greek; the Hebrew has only the seven months. As at 5:6, the text keeps the detail the Greek has, because v5 speaks of *your mice that ravage the land* and the Hebrew nowhere tells of them.
- **v3 "a guilt offering"** — *asham*, the offering for a wrong done to what is holy (Leviticus 5:6). The Philistine priests know that the ark must not go back *empty*, *reqam*, the word of the law for Israel's pilgrims: *They shall not appear before the face of the LORD empty-handed* (Deuteronomy 16:16).
- **v5 "give glory to the God of Israel… lighten His hand"** — *kavod*, **glory, weight**, and *yaqel*, **make light**. The LORD's hand has been *heavy* on them (5:6, 11); by giving Him weight they hope He will make it light.
- **v6 "Why should you harden your hearts as the Egyptians and Pharaoh hardened their hearts?"** — *tekhabbedu*, **make heavy**, the verb of Pharaoh, who *made his own heart heavy* (Exodus 8:15, 8:32). *Dealt harshly*, *hit'allel*, is the verb of Exodus 10:2, *what I did in Egypt*. The Philistine priests know the Exodus better than the Philistine soldiers did (4:8).
- **vv7–12 the test of the cows** — two cows never yoked, like *the red heifer… on which a yoke has never come* (Numbers 19:2), and with their calves shut up at home. By nature they would turn back toward their calves. They go straight to Israel, *lowing as they went*, and do not turn aside *to the right or to the left*.
- **v9 "by chance"** — *miqreh*, the word of Ruth 2:3, *as it happened*. The Philistines set a test to tell the hand of God from chance.
- **v14 "the field of Joshua of Beth-shemesh"** — Beth-shemesh was a town of the Levites (Joshua 21:16), and v15 has the Levites handle the ark.
- **v17 "one for Ashdod, one for Gaza…"** — the five cities of the five lords (Joshua 13:3; Judges 3:3).
- **v19 "because they looked into the ark… seventy men of the people, fifty thousand men"** — to look on the holy things was death even for the Levites who carried them (Numbers 4:20). The Hebrew gives two numbers side by side, *seventy men, fifty thousand men*, without joining them; the Greek has the same, and adds that *the sons of Jeconiah did not rejoice with the men of Beth-shemesh*. The text gives the Hebrew as it stands.
- **v20 "Who is able to stand before the LORD, this holy God?"** — the question that the ark's journey has been asking of the Philistines is now asked in Israel.

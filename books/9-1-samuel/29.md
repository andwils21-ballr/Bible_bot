# 1 Samuel 29

## "What Are These Hebrews?"

**1** The Philistines gathered all their forces at Aphek, and Israel was camped by the spring in Jezreel.

**2** The lords of the Philistines were passing on by hundreds and by thousands, and David and his men were passing on in the rear with Achish.

**3** The commanders of the Philistines said, "What are these Hebrews?" Achish said to the commanders of the Philistines, "Is this not David, the servant of Saul king of Israel, who has been with me these days, or these years? I have found nothing against him from the day he came over to me until this day."

**4** But the commanders of the Philistines were angry with him, and the commanders of the Philistines said to him, "Send the man back, so that he may return to the place you assigned him. He shall not go down with us into battle, or he will become an adversary to us in the battle. For how could this man win back his lord's favor? Would it not be with the heads of these men?

**5** Is this not David, of whom they sang to one another in the dances:  
'Saul has struck down his thousands,  
and David his ten thousands'?"

## Achish Sends David Away

**6** Then Achish called David and said to him, "As the LORD lives, you have been upright, and your going out and coming in with me in the army is good in my eyes, for I have found no evil in you from the day you came to me until this day. But in the eyes of the lords you are not good.

**7** So now go back, and go in peace, so that you do nothing that is evil in the eyes of the lords of the Philistines."

**8** David said to Achish, "But what have I done? What have you found in your servant from the day I entered your service until this day, that I may not go and fight against the enemies of my lord the king?"

**9** Achish answered David, "I know that you are as good in my eyes as a messenger of God. But the commanders of the Philistines have said, 'He shall not go up with us into the battle.'

**10** So now rise early in the morning with the servants of your lord who came with you. Rise early in the morning, and as soon as you have light, go."

**11** So David rose early, he and his men, to go in the morning and return to the land of the Philistines, while the Philistines went up to Jezreel.

## Notes

- **v1 "at Aphek"** — where the Philistines camped when they took the ark (4:1). The book's first Philistine victory and its last are gathered from the same place.
- **v3 "What are these Hebrews?"** — the Philistines' name for Israel (4:6, 13:19, 14:11). Achish answers with a name the commanders know better: *David, the servant of Saul*.
- **v4 "or he will become an adversary to us"** — *satan*, **adversary**, the word used of the messenger of the LORD who *took his stand in the road as an adversary* against Balaam (Numbers 22:22). *The heads of these men* recalls the head David carried from the Valley of Elah (17:54, 17:57).
- **v5 "Saul has struck down his thousands, and David his ten thousands"** — the song of the women (18:7), quoted by the servants of Achish when David first came to Gath (21:11), and now by the Philistine commanders. It follows David everywhere among the Philistines.
- **v6 "As the LORD lives"** — the Philistine king swears by the LORD's name to David.
- **v8 "that I may not go and fight against the enemies of my lord the king"** — which king David means is not said. Achish hears himself; *my lord the king* is also what David called Saul at En-gedi (24:8). The commanders' fear in v4 is the other reading.
- **v9 "as good in my eyes as a messenger of God"** — *ke-mal'akh Elohim*. The same comparison is made of David as king by the woman of Tekoa (2 Samuel 14:17) and by Mephibosheth (2 Samuel 19:27).
- **v11 "while the Philistines went up to Jezreel"** — David is sent away before the battle in which Saul dies (31:1–6). The Philistine lords' distrust keeps him from fighting Israel and from fighting for it.

# Micah 2

## Woe to Those Who Devise Wickedness

**1** Woe to those who devise wickedness  
and work evil on their beds!  
In the morning light they do it,  
for their hand has the power.

**2** They covet fields and seize them,  
and houses, and take them away;  
they oppress a man and his house,  
a man and his inheritance.

**3** Therefore this is what the LORD says:  
I am about to devise disaster against this family,  
from which you cannot remove your necks;  
you will not walk with your heads high,  
for it is an evil time.

**4** On that day they will take up a taunt against you  
and raise a lament:  
"It is over; we are utterly ruined;  
He changes the portion of my people.  
How He takes it away from me!  
To the faithless He divides our fields."

**5** Therefore you will have no one  
to cast the measuring line by lot  
in the assembly of the LORD.

## Do Not Preach

**6** "Do not preach," they preach.  
"One should not preach of such things;  
disgrace will not overtake us."

**7** Should this be said, house of Jacob?  
Is the Spirit of the LORD cut short?  
Are these His deeds?  
Do not My words do good  
to the one who walks uprightly?

**8** But lately My people have risen up as an enemy:  
you strip the cloak off the garment  
from those who pass by trusting,  
like men returning from war.

**9** The women of My people you drive out  
from their pleasant houses;  
from their young children you take away  
My splendor forever.

**10** Get up and go,  
for this is not the place of rest;  
because of uncleanness it will destroy  
with a grievous destruction.

**11** If a man walking after wind and lies should deceive:  
"I will preach to you of wine and strong drink,"  
he would be the preacher for this people!

## I Will Gather Jacob

**12** I will surely gather all of you, Jacob;  
I will surely assemble the remnant of Israel;  
I will put them together like sheep in a fold,  
like a flock in the middle of its pasture;  
they will hum with people.

**13** The one who breaks through will go up before them;  
they will break through and pass the gate  
and go out by it;  
their king will pass on before them,  
the LORD at their head.

## Notes

- **v6 "'Do not preach,' they preach"** — *al tattifu yattifun*. *Nataf* is **drip**, and of speech **preach**, the verb Amaziah used to Amos, *do not preach against the house of Isaac* (Amos 7:16). Who says it, the people to the prophets or one prophet to another, the line does not mark. *Lo yissag kelimmot* is **disgrace will not overtake us**, or **disgrace will not turn back**; the Greek has *do not weep with tears*.
- **vv8–9 "My people have risen up as an enemy… from their young children you take away My splendor forever"** — obscure. As the lines stand, the powerful treat their own people as an enemy, stripping cloaks from travelers who expect no harm, as if they were spoil of war, and driving women and children from their houses. *Hadari*, **My splendor**, is the LORD's: what He gave the children is taken from them for good.
- **v13 "The one who breaks through will go up before them… their king… the LORD at their head"** — *ha-porets*, **the one who breaks through**, has the name of Perez, *How you have breached — a breach upon you!… He called his name Perez* (Genesis 38:29), from whom David came (Ruth 4:18), and the words of David's victory, *The LORD has broken through my enemies before me* (2 Samuel 5:20). Whether *their king* and *the LORD* are one leader or two, the verse does not say.

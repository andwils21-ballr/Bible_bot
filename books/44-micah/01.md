# Micah 1

## The LORD Comes Out of His Place

**1** The word of the LORD that came to Micah of Moresheth in the days of Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.

**2** Hear, you peoples, all of you;  
listen, earth, and all that fills it;  
let the Lord GOD be a witness against you,  
the Lord from His holy temple.

**3** For the LORD is about to come out from His place;  
He will come down and tread on the heights of the earth.

**4** The mountains will melt under Him,  
and the valleys split open,  
like wax before the fire,  
like water poured down a slope.

**5** All this is for the transgression of Jacob  
and for the sins of the house of Israel.  
What is the transgression of Jacob?  
Is it not Samaria?  
What are the high places of Judah?  
Are they not Jerusalem?

**6** So I will make Samaria a heap in the open field,  
a place for planting vineyards;  
I will pour her stones down into the valley  
and lay bare her foundations.

**7** All her carved images will be smashed to pieces,  
all her wages will be burned with fire,  
and I will make all her idols a desolation;  
for she gathered them from a prostitute's wages,  
and to a prostitute's wages they will return.

## I Will Lament and Howl

**8** For this I will lament and howl;  
I will go stripped and naked;  
I will make a lament like the jackals  
and a mourning like the ostriches.

**9** For her wounds are incurable;  
it has come to Judah;  
it has reached the gate of my people,  
to Jerusalem.

**10** Tell it not in Gath;  
do not weep at all;  
in Beth-leaphrah  
roll yourself in the dust.

**11** Pass on, inhabitant of Shaphir,  
in shameful nakedness;  
the inhabitant of Zaanan has not come out;  
the wailing of Beth-ezel  
will take from you its standing place.

**12** For the inhabitant of Maroth  
writhes, waiting for good,  
because disaster has come down from the LORD  
to the gate of Jerusalem.

**13** Harness the chariot to the steeds,  
inhabitant of Lachish;  
she was the beginning of sin  
for the daughter of Zion,  
for in you were found  
the transgressions of Israel.

**14** Therefore you will give parting gifts  
to Moresheth-gath;  
the houses of Achzib will be a deceitful brook  
to the kings of Israel.

**15** I will yet bring the conqueror upon you,  
inhabitant of Mareshah;  
the glory of Israel  
will come as far as Adullam.

**16** Make yourself bald and shave your hair  
for the children you delight in;  
make your baldness as wide as the eagle's,  
for they have gone from you into exile.

## Notes

- **v1 "Micah of Moresheth… Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah"** — *Mikhah* is short for *Mikhayahu*, **who is like the LORD?**, and Jeremiah's text writes it long, *Mikhayah*, where it is read *Mikhah* (Jeremiah 26:18). The Greek calls him *Meichaias*, the name it gives Micaiah son of Imlah (1 Kings 22:8). He is remembered by name in Jeremiah's trial, when *some of the elders of the land* said, *Micah of Moresheth prophesied in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah* (Jeremiah 26:17–18). The kings are those of Hosea 1:1 after Uzziah. Moresheth is the *Moresheth-gath* of v14.
- **v2 "Hear, you peoples, all of you"** — *shim'u ammim kullam*, word for word the last words of Micaiah son of Imlah, *Hear, all you peoples!* (1 Kings 22:28). *The earth and all that fills it* is the psalm's *The earth is the LORD's, and all that fills it* (Psalm 24:1); here it is called to court.
- **vv3–4 "the LORD is about to come out from His place… tread on the heights of the earth… like wax before the fire"** — *ki hinneh YHWH yotse mi-meqomo*, the words of Isaiah 26:21, *For the LORD is about to come out from His place to punish the inhabitants of the earth*. *Treads on the heights of the earth* is the line of Amos's hymn (Amos 4:13). The melting is the psalm's, *The mountains melt like wax before the LORD* (Psalm 97:5).
- **v5 "What is the transgression of Jacob? Is it not Samaria? What are the high places of Judah?"** — the sin is named as the two capitals themselves. *Bamot*, **high places**, is the word of v3, *bamotei arets*, the heights the LORD treads: He comes down on the heights, and Judah's heights are Jerusalem. The Greek has *what is the sin of the house of Judah?*
- **vv6–7 "a heap in the open field… a prostitute's wages"** — Micah says it of Jerusalem too, in the words Jeremiah's elders quote: *Zion shall be plowed as a field; Jerusalem shall become a heap of ruins* (Jeremiah 26:18). *Etnan*, **wages**, three times, is the prostitute's pay the law would not let into the LORD's house (Deuteronomy 23:18), and Hosea's word, *you have loved a prostitute's wages* (Hosea 9:1).
- **vv8–9 "I will go stripped and naked… like the jackals… like the ostriches… her wounds are incurable"** — *sholal*, **stripped**, the read form (written *shilal*), as in *He leads counselors away stripped* (Job 12:17); Isaiah walked *naked and barefoot* as a sign (Isaiah 20:2). Job mourned in the same pair, *I am a brother of jackals and a companion of ostriches* (Job 30:29). The Greek has *she will lament*: Samaria, not the prophet. *Incurable* is Jeremiah's word, *my wound incurable* (Jeremiah 15:18).
- **vv10–12 "Tell it not in Gath… Beth-leaphrah… Shaphir… Zaanan… Beth-ezel… Maroth"** — *Tell it not in Gath* is David's lament for Saul (2 Samuel 1:20). Then each town hears its name turned against it: *Beit le-Afrah*, **house of dust**, rolls in *afar*, **dust** (the read form, *roll yourself*; written, *I have rolled*); *Shafir*, **beautiful**, goes in shameful nakedness; *Tsa'anan* sounds like *yatsa*, **come out**, and does not come out; *Marot*, **bitterness**, waits for good. V11's last line is obscure: *Beit ha-Etsel*, **house beside**, takes its *emdah*, **standing place**, away. The Greek reads several names as words.
- **vv13–14 "Lachish… the chariot to the steeds… parting gifts to Moresheth-gath… Achzib"** — *Lakhish* chimes with *rekhesh*, **steeds**. *Shilluchim*, **parting gifts**, are a bride's dowry, as Pharaoh gave Gezer *as a dowry to his daughter* (1 Kings 9:16), and *Moreshet* sounds like *me'orasah*, **engaged**, the word of *a virgin… engaged to a man* (Deuteronomy 22:23): Micah's own town is given away like a bride. *Akhziv… le-akhzav*, **a deceitful brook**, a wadi that runs dry, Jeremiah's *Will You be to me like a deceitful brook, like waters that fail?* (Jeremiah 15:18).
- **v15 "the conqueror… Mareshah… Adullam"** — *ha-yoresh*, **the one who takes possession**, and *Mareshah* sound alike. *The glory of Israel* will come to Adullam as David came when he fled, *David left there and escaped to the cave of Adullam* (1 Samuel 22:1).
- **v16 "Make yourself bald… as wide as the eagle's"** — the mourner's shaved head (Amos 8:10), the *bald patch… for the dead* the law forbade (Deuteronomy 14:1). *Nesher*, the eagle of Hosea 8:1, is also the vulture, and the line compares the shaved head to its bare one.

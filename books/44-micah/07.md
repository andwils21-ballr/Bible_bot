# Micah 7

## Woe to Me

**1** Woe to me! For I have become  
like the gatherings of summer fruit,  
like the gleanings of the grape harvest:  
there is no cluster to eat,  
no first-ripe fig that my soul desires.

**2** The faithful one has perished from the land,  
and there is no one upright among men;  
they all lie in wait for blood;  
each hunts his brother with a net.

**3** Their hands are on evil, to do it well:  
the prince asks, and the judge, for a payment,  
and the great man speaks the desire of his soul;  
so they weave it together.

**4** The best of them is like a briar,  
the most upright worse than a hedge of thorns.  
The day of your watchmen, your punishment, has come;  
now will be their confusion.

**5** Do not trust a neighbor;  
do not rely on a close friend;  
from her who lies in your arms  
guard the doors of your mouth.

**6** For son treats father with contempt,  
daughter rises against her mother,  
daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;  
a man's enemies are the men of his own house.

## I Will Watch for the LORD

**7** But as for me, I will watch for the LORD;  
I will wait for the God of my salvation;  
my God will hear me.

**8** Do not rejoice over me, my enemy;  
though I have fallen, I will rise;  
though I sit in darkness,  
the LORD is my light.

**9** I will bear the rage of the LORD,  
because I have sinned against Him,  
until He pleads my cause  
and carries out justice for me.  
He will bring me out to the light;  
I will look on His righteousness.

**10** Then my enemy will see,  
and shame will cover her,  
she who said to me,  
"Where is the LORD your God?"  
My eyes will look on her;  
now she will be trampled down  
like the mud of the streets.

## A Day for Building Your Walls

**11** A day for building your walls!  
On that day the boundary will be far off.

**12** On that day they will come to you,  
from Assyria and the cities of Egypt,  
and from Egypt to the River,  
from sea to sea and from mountain to mountain.

**13** But the earth will become a desolation  
because of its inhabitants,  
for the fruit of their deeds.

## Who Is a God Like You?

**14** Shepherd Your people with Your rod,  
the flock of Your inheritance,  
who dwell alone, a forest  
in the middle of Carmel;  
let them graze in Bashan and Gilead  
as in the days of old.

**15** As in the days when you came out of the land of Egypt,  
I will show him wonders.

**16** The nations will see and be ashamed  
of all their might;  
they will lay their hand on their mouth;  
their ears will be deaf.

**17** They will lick the dust like a snake,  
like things that crawl on the earth;  
they will come quaking out of their strongholds;  
they will come trembling to the LORD our God,  
and they will fear You.

**18** Who is a God like You,  
carrying guilt and passing over transgression  
for the remnant of His inheritance?  
He does not hold on to His anger forever,  
for He delights in loyal love.

**19** He will again have compassion on us;  
He will tread our iniquities under foot.  
You will cast all their sins  
into the depths of the sea.

**20** You will give truth to Jacob,  
loyal love to Abraham,  
as You swore to our fathers  
from ancient days.

## Notes

- **v1 "Woe to me!… like the gleanings of the grape harvest… no first-ripe fig"** — *alelai li*, **woe to me**, are Job's words, *If I am guilty, woe to me!* (Job 10:15). The prophet is a field after the harvest: *qayits*, **summer fruit**, is what Amos saw in the basket, *a basket of summer fruit* (Amos 8:1), and *olelot batsir*, **the gleanings of the grape harvest**, the little left on the vine, *as at the gleaning when the grape harvest is done* (Isaiah 24:13). *Bikkurah* is *a first-ripe fig before the summer* (Isaiah 28:4). He goes looking for one good person and finds none (v2).
- **v2 "The faithful one has perished… each hunts his brother with a net"** — *chasid*, **the faithful one**, is from *chesed*, the loyal love the LORD requires (6:8), as in *Save, LORD, for the faithful one is gone* (Psalm 12:1). *Cherem*, **a net**, as in *a place for spreading nets* (Ezekiel 26:5), has the letters of *cherem*, a thing devoted to the LORD, the word of *I will devote their gain to the LORD* (4:13).
- **vv3–4 "the judge, for a payment… so they weave it together… worse than a hedge of thorns"** — partly obscure. *Ba-shillum*, **for a payment**, is the word of Hosea's *the days of repayment have come* (Hosea 9:7). *Va-ye'abbetuha*, **they weave it together**, is found only here; it is the root of *avot*, a cord, as in *bands of love* (Hosea 11:4): the prince, the judge and the great man twist their wants into one rope. *Mesukhah*, **a hedge of thorns**, is the word of *the way of a sluggard is like a hedge of thorns* (Proverbs 15:19), spelled there with *sin* for *samekh*; and *chedeq*, **briar**, is the thorn of that verse; for *like a briar* the Greek has *like a moth devouring*. *Your watchmen* are the prophets, as the LORD said to Ezekiel, *I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel* (Ezekiel 3:17): the day they warned of has come. *Mevukhatam*, **their confusion**, is Isaiah's *a day of tumult and trampling and confusion* (Isaiah 22:5).
- **vv5–6 "Do not trust a neighbor… a man's enemies are the men of his own house"** — Jeremiah says the same of his city, *Let everyone beware of his neighbor, and put no trust in any brother* (Jeremiah 9:4). *Alluf*, **close friend**, is the word of *a whisperer separates close friends* (Proverbs 16:28). Matthew gives v6 in Jesus' words: *For I came to set a man against his father, a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law; and a man's enemies will be the members of his own household* (Matthew 10:35–36).
- **vv7–10 "I will watch for the LORD… though I have fallen, I will rise… Where is the LORD your God?"** — *atsappeh*, **I will watch**, is the verb of the watchmen of v4: they watched for the day of punishment, and the prophet watches for the LORD, as in *in the morning I set it out before You, and watch* (Psalm 5:3). *Elohayikh*, **your God** (v10), is said to a woman: the one who speaks now is the city, and *oyavti*, **my enemy**, is a woman too, another city. *I have fallen, I will rise* answers Amos's lament, *Fallen, no more to rise, is virgin Israel* (Amos 5:2). *Yariv rivi*, **He pleads my cause**, as in *plead my cause against an unfaithful nation* (Psalm 43:1): the LORD who had a case against His people (6:2) takes up their case. The enemy's taunt is the psalm's, *Where is your God?* (Psalm 42:3), and her end is the word of Isaiah, *to trample them down like the mud of the streets* (Isaiah 10:6).
- **vv11–13 "the boundary will be far off… from Assyria and the cities of Egypt… from sea to sea"** — *yirchak choq* is obscure. *Choq* is a decree, and a boundary set, as in *when He assigned to the sea its limit* (Proverbs 8:29); so the boundary moves far out, or the decree is taken far away. The Greek has *that day will rub away your statutes*. *Matsor*, **Egypt**, is the name in *the streams of Egypt* (Isaiah 19:6), and it is also the word *siege* (5:1): those who once laid siege send the people home. The Greek has *from Tyre*. *From sea to sea… the River* is the reach of the king's rule in *May he rule from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth* (Psalm 72:8). *Ha-arets* is the land and the earth; here it follows the lands of v12, and is given as **the earth**. *The fruit of their deeds* is Jeremiah's phrase (Jeremiah 17:10).
- **v14 "Shepherd Your people with Your rod… who dwell alone… as in the days of old"** — the prayer turns to the LORD. *Shevet*, **rod**, is the shepherd's rod of *Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me* (Psalm 23:4), and the rod that struck the judge on the cheek (5:1). *Shokhni levadad*, **who dwell alone**, are Balaam's words, *Here is a people that lives alone* (Numbers 23:9), and Moses', *Israel dwells in safety, the fountain of Jacob alone* (Deuteronomy 33:28). Bashan and Gilead are the grazing lands across the Jordan, *the place was a place for livestock* (Numbers 32:1). *Kimei olam*, **as in the days of old**, are the words of the ruler's beginnings (5:2).
- **vv15–17 "I will show him wonders… they will lick the dust like a snake… come trembling to the LORD our God"** — the LORD answers: *I will do wonders* was His word to Moses (Exodus 34:10). The nations are struck silent, as Job was, *I lay my hand on my mouth* (Job 40:4), and brought low as the snake, *dust you shall eat* (Genesis 3:14), and as the king's enemies *lick the dust* (Psalm 72:9). *Ke-zochalei arets*, **like things that crawl**, is the word of *things that crawl in the dust* (Deuteronomy 32:24). *Out of their strongholds* is the psalm's *come trembling out of their strongholds* (Psalm 18:45), and *yifchadu el YHWH*, **they will come trembling to the LORD**, is Hosea's promise for Israel, *they will come trembling to the LORD* (Hosea 3:5), here given to the nations.
- **vv18–19 "Who is a God like You, carrying guilt and passing over transgression… into the depths of the sea"** — *mi el kamokha* is the song at the sea, *Who is like You among the gods, LORD?* (Exodus 15:11), and the question in the prophet's own name: *Mikhah* is written *Mikhayah* in Jeremiah 26:18, **who is like YH?**, and Micaiah son of Imlah (1 Kings 22:8) bears the same name. *Nose avon*, **carrying guilt**, is the name the LORD proclaimed, *carrying guilt and rebellion and sin* (Exodus 34:7). *Over al pesha*, **passing over transgression**, is the phrase of *it is his glory to overlook an offense* (Proverbs 19:11); Amos heard *I will never again pass them by* (Amos 7:8), and here the LORD passes over the sin instead. *La-ad*, **forever**, has the letters of *le-ed*, **for a witness**, and the Greek has *He did not keep His anger as a witness*. *Yikhbosh*, **He will tread under foot**, is the verb of *fill the earth and take it under foot* (Genesis 1:28). *Metsulot*, **depths**, is the sea where Pharaoh's army sank, *They went down into the depths like a stone* (Exodus 15:5). The Hebrew has *their sins* where the Greek has *our sins*.
- **v20 "You will give truth to Jacob, loyal love to Abraham, as You swore to our fathers"** — *emet* and *chesed*, the pair of the LORD's name, *abundant in loyal love and truth* (Exodus 34:6), and of the blessing of Abraham's servant, *who has not forsaken His kindness and His truth from my master* (Genesis 24:27). The oath is the one sworn to Abraham, *By Myself I have sworn* (Genesis 22:16). *Mi-mei qedem*, **from ancient days**: *qedem* is the word of the ruler's *goings out… from ancient times* (5:2).

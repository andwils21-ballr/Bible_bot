# Micah 4

## The Mountain of the LORD's House

**1** And it shall come to pass in the last days  
that the mountain of the LORD's house shall be established  
as the highest of the mountains;  
it shall be lifted up above the hills,  
and peoples shall stream to it.

**2** Many nations shall go and say,  
"Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD,  
and to the house of the God of Jacob,  
that He may teach us His ways,  
and that we may walk in His paths."  
For out of Zion shall go forth instruction,  
and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.

**3** He shall judge between many peoples,  
and decide for strong nations far away;  
they shall beat their swords into plowshares,  
and their spears into pruning hooks;  
nation shall not lift up sword against nation,  
nor shall they learn war anymore.

**4** But they shall sit, each man under his vine  
and under his fig tree,  
and no one shall make them afraid;  
for the mouth of the LORD of hosts has spoken.

**5** For all the peoples walk,  
each in the name of its god,  
but we will walk in the name of the LORD our God  
forever and ever.

## The Lame and the Outcast

**6** On that day, declares the LORD,  
I will gather the lame  
and assemble the outcast,  
and those I have harmed;

**7** I will make the lame a remnant,  
and the cast-off a strong nation;  
and the LORD will reign over them on Mount Zion  
from now on and forever.

**8** As for you, tower of the flock,  
hill of the daughter of Zion,  
to you it will come;  
the former dominion will come,  
the kingdom of the daughter of Jerusalem.

## Writhe, Daughter of Zion

**9** Now why do you cry aloud?  
Is there no king in you?  
Has your counselor perished,  
that pain has seized you like a woman in labor?

**10** Writhe and burst forth, daughter of Zion,  
like a woman in labor;  
for now you will go out of the city  
and live in the open field;  
you will go to Babylon;  
there you will be rescued;  
there the LORD will redeem you  
from the power of your enemies.

**11** Now many nations are gathered against you,  
who say, "Let her be polluted,  
and let our eyes gaze on Zion."

**12** But they do not know the thoughts of the LORD,  
and they do not understand His counsel,  
for He has gathered them like sheaves to the threshing floor.

**13** Rise and thresh, daughter of Zion,  
for I will make your horn iron  
and your hoofs bronze;  
you will crush many peoples,  
and I will devote their gain to the LORD,  
their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "the mountain of the LORD's house… swords into plowshares"** — the same oracle stands in Isaiah 2:2–4, nearly word for word, and the English here follows Isaiah's where the Hebrew is the same. The two differ in small things that show: here *peoples shall stream to it*, where Isaiah has *all the nations*; *many nations shall go*, where Isaiah has *many peoples*; and Micah alone has *strong nations far away*. Isaiah goes on, *House of Jacob, come, let us walk in the light of the LORD* (Isaiah 2:5); Micah goes on with v4. *Be-acharit ha-yamim*, **in the last days**, is the phrase Hosea gives as *in the end of days* (Hosea 3:5).
- **v4 "each man under his vine and under his fig tree… no one shall make them afraid"** — the words of Solomon's peace, *each man under his vine and under his fig tree* (1 Kings 4:25), and of the law's blessing, *you will lie down with no one to make you afraid* (Leviticus 26:6). *For the mouth of the LORD has spoken* is Isaiah's seal on a word (Isaiah 1:20).
- **v5 "each in the name of its god, but we will walk in the name of the LORD"** — v1 saw the peoples streaming to Zion in the last days; v5 speaks of the present, when each still walks after its own god, and says what Israel will do now.
- **vv6–7 "I will gather the lame… the cast-off a strong nation"** — *ha-tsole'ah*, **the lame**, is the word for Jacob after the night at Penuel, *he was limping on his hip* (Genesis 32:31): the people are gathered limping, as their father walked away. *Va-asher hare'oti*, **those I have harmed**: the LORD names the harm as His own (2:3). *Will reign* is the song's *The LORD will reign forever and ever* (Exodus 15:18).
- **v8 "tower of the flock, hill of the daughter of Zion"** — *Migdal Eder*, **Tower of the Flock**, is the place where Jacob camped after Rachel was buried *on the road to Ephrath — that is Bethlehem*: *Israel pulled up and pitched his tent beyond Migdal-eder* (Genesis 35:19, 21). *Ophel*, **hill**, is the name of a height of Jerusalem, *the wall of Ophel* (2 Chronicles 27:3). *The former dominion* is the kingdom as it once was; the Greek adds *from Babylon*.
- **vv9–10 "Is there no king in you?… you will go to Babylon; there the LORD will redeem you"** — the king may be Jerusalem's own, beside *your counselor*, or the LORD, who has just been named as reigning on Mount Zion (v7), as Jeremiah hears the exiles ask, *Is the LORD not in Zion? Is her King not in her?* (Jeremiah 8:19). *Gochi*, **burst forth**, is the verb of the sea born from the womb, *when it burst out from the womb* (Job 38:8). Micah names Babylon here, though the enemy at the door in his own oracles is Assyria (5:5–6); and the way out runs through the exile itself: *there* she is rescued. *Yig'alekh*, **will redeem you**, is the verb of the kinsman who buys back.
- **vv11–12 "Let her be polluted… like sheaves to the threshing floor"** — *techenaf*, **polluted**, is the law's word, *You shall not pollute the land where you are* (Numbers 35:33). *Machshevot*, **thoughts**, is from the verb of 2:1 and 2:3, *devise*: the nations devise against Zion, and the LORD's thoughts are not theirs, as in *My thoughts are not your thoughts* (Isaiah 55:8). They gather themselves for war, and He has gathered them as a farmer gathers sheaves for threshing.
- **v13 "I will make your horn iron… I will devote their gain to the LORD"** — *horns of iron* were the sign of the false prophet Zedekiah (1 Kings 22:11); here the LORD gives them. *Ve-hacharamti*, **I will devote**, is the word for what is given wholly to the LORD: Zion threshes, and the LORD takes the spoil for Himself. The Greek has *you will devote*, keeping the subject of *you will crush*. *Betsa*, **gain**, is the word of *men… who hate dishonest gain* (Exodus 18:21), and *the Lord of all the earth* is the title of the ark at the Jordan, *the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth* (Joshua 3:11).

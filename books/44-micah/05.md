# Micah 5

## The Ruler from Bethlehem

**1** Now gather in your troops, daughter of troops;  
a siege is laid against us;  
with a rod they strike the judge of Israel  
on the cheek.

**2** But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah,  
too small to be among the clans of Judah,  
from you shall come out for Me  
one who is to be ruler in Israel,  
whose goings out are from ancient times,  
from the days of old.

**3** Therefore He will give them up  
until the time when she who is in labor has given birth;  
then the rest of his brothers will return  
to the sons of Israel.

**4** He will stand and shepherd in the strength of the LORD,  
in the majesty of the name of the LORD his God;  
and they will live secure,  
for now he will be great  
to the ends of the earth.

**5** This one will be peace.  
When Assyria comes into our land  
and treads on our strongholds,  
we will raise against him seven shepherds  
and eight princes of men.

**6** They will shepherd the land of Assyria with the sword,  
and the land of Nimrod at its gates;  
and he will rescue us from Assyria  
when it comes into our land  
and treads within our border.

## The Remnant of Jacob

**7** The remnant of Jacob will be  
among many peoples  
like dew from the LORD,  
like downpours on the plants,  
which do not wait for man  
or delay for the sons of men.

**8** The remnant of Jacob will be among the nations,  
among many peoples,  
like a lion among the beasts of the forest,  
like a young lion among the flocks of sheep,  
which, when it goes through, tramples and tears,  
and there is no one to rescue.

**9** Your hand will be lifted up over your adversaries,  
and all your enemies will be cut off.

## I Will Cut Off

**10** And it shall come to pass on that day,  
declares the LORD,  
that I will cut off your horses from among you  
and destroy your chariots;

**11** I will cut off the cities of your land  
and tear down all your fortresses;

**12** I will cut off sorceries from your hand,  
and you will have no more soothsayers;

**13** I will cut off your carved images  
and your standing stones from among you,  
and you will no longer bow down  
to the work of your hands;

**14** I will uproot your sacred poles from among you  
and destroy your cities.

**15** In anger and wrath I will execute vengeance  
on the nations that have not listened.

## Notes

- **v1 "Now gather in your troops, daughter of troops… strike the judge of Israel on the cheek"** — in the Hebrew text this verse is 4:14, and vv2–15 are 5:1–14; the English numbering is followed. *Titgodedi bat gedud* plays on one sound: *gadad* is to gather in troops, as Jeremiah's people *trooped to the house of a prostitute* (Jeremiah 5:7), and to gash oneself in grief, *You shall not gash yourselves* (Deuteronomy 14:1). The Greek has *the daughter will be walled in with a wall*. The judge struck on the cheek is how the prophet before Micah was treated: *Zedekiah… struck Micaiah on the cheek* (1 Kings 22:24). From the *shofet*, **judge**, struck down, the next verse turns to the *moshel*, **ruler**, to come.
- **v2 "But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, too small to be among the clans of Judah"** — Ephrathah is David's town, *that Ephrathite of Bethlehem in Judah whose name was Jesse* (1 Samuel 17:12), and the road where Rachel was buried, *the road to Ephrath — that is Bethlehem* (Genesis 35:19). *Tsa'ir*, **small**, is the word for the younger son in the oracle over Rebekah's twins, *the elder shall serve the younger* (Genesis 25:23). Matthew gives the verse in other words: *You, Bethlehem, land of Judah, are by no means least among the rulers of Judah; for from you will come a ruler who will shepherd My people Israel* (Matthew 2:6). *Alfei*, **the clans**, has the letters of *allufei*, **chiefs**; *by no means least* turns *too small* around; and *who will shepherd My people Israel* are the words said to David, *You shall shepherd My people Israel* (2 Samuel 5:2).
- **v2 "whose goings out are from ancient times, from the days of old"** — *motsa'otav*, **his goings out**, the word of Israel's *starting points* in the wilderness (Numbers 33:2): where he comes from. *Mi-yemei olam*, **from the days of old**, are the words of Amos's promise to rebuild David's fallen booth *as in the days of old* (Amos 9:11). *Olam* runs from **long ago** to **forever**: Micah has it as *forever and ever* (4:5) and *from now on and forever* (4:7).
- **v3 "until the time when she who is in labor has given birth"** — *yoledah yaladah*, **she who bears has borne**. The daughter of Zion was just told to *writhe… like a woman in labor* (4:10); she may be the one, or the ruler's own mother; the verse does not name her. *The rest of his brothers* are the scattered ones gathered in 4:6–7.
- **v4 "He will stand and shepherd in the strength of the LORD, in the majesty of the name of the LORD"** — the shepherd-king of the promise to David (2 Samuel 5:2), as in Ezekiel's *one shepherd… My servant David* (Ezekiel 34:23). *Ge'on*, **majesty**, is the word of *the pride of Jacob* (Amos 6:8; 8:7): there the pride was Jacob's, here it belongs to the LORD's name. *To the ends of the earth*, *ad afsei arets*, is the reach of Joseph's horns in Moses' blessing (Deuteronomy 33:17).
- **vv5–6 "This one will be peace… seven shepherds and eight princes of men… the land of Nimrod"** — *ve-hayah zeh shalom*: *zeh*, **this one**, the ruler of vv2–4, or **this** of what follows; the Greek has *this will be peace*. Seven, then eight, is the counting of Amos's *for three… and for four* (Amos 1:3): enough, and one more. *Nesikhei*, **princes**, is the word of *the princes of Sihon* (Joshua 13:21). Assyria is called the land of Nimrod, from whose kingdom *went out Asshur* (Genesis 10:11). *Shepherd… with the sword* is the shepherd's word turned to war. *Bi-ftacheha*, **at its gates**; the Greek has *in its trench*.
- **vv7–8 "like dew from the LORD… like a lion among the beasts of the forest"** — two pictures of one remnant. The first is Moses' song, *my speech distill like the dew, like showers on the new grass, like downpours on the plants* (Deuteronomy 32:2), and Hosea's *I will be like the dew to Israel* (Hosea 14:5): rain that waits for no one. The second is Jacob's blessing of Judah, *like a lion, and like a lioness — who will rouse him?* (Genesis 49:9), and Hosea's lion turned around: there the LORD said *I Myself will tear… and no one will rescue* against Israel (Hosea 5:14); here the remnant tears, *and there is no one to rescue*.
- **vv10–14 "I will cut off your horses… sorceries… your standing stones… your sacred poles"** — *ve-hikhratti*, **I will cut off**, four times, and what is cut off is what Israel trusted: horses, which the king was not to *multiply* (Deuteronomy 17:16); walled cities; *a soothsayer… or a sorcerer* (Deuteronomy 18:10); *standing stones* and *sacred poles*, *You shall not plant for yourself a sacred pole* (Deuteronomy 16:21). It is the list Hosea's penitents give up in their own mouths, *we will not ride on horses; and we will no longer say 'Our God' to the work of our hands* (Hosea 14:3).
- **v15 "on the nations that have not listened"** — *asher lo shame'u*, **who have not heard**: the book opened with *Hear, you peoples, all of you* (1:2), and the nations who go up to be taught (4:2) are set against those who would not hear.

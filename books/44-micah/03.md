# Micah 3

## The Heads of Jacob

**1** I said:  
Hear, please, you heads of Jacob  
and rulers of the house of Israel!  
Is it not for you to know justice?

**2** You who hate good and love evil,  
who tear the skin off them  
and the flesh off their bones,

**3** who eat the flesh of my people,  
strip their skin off them,  
break their bones,  
and chop them up like meat for the pot,  
like flesh in a cauldron —

**4** then they will cry out to the LORD,  
but He will not answer them;  
He will hide His face from them at that time,  
because they have made their deeds evil.

## The Prophets Who Lead My People Astray

**5** This is what the LORD says concerning the prophets  
who lead My people astray,  
who, when they have something to bite with their teeth,  
cry "Peace!",  
but consecrate war against whoever puts nothing into their mouths:

**6** Therefore it will be night for you, without vision,  
and darkness for you, without divination;  
the sun will go down on the prophets,  
and the day will grow dark over them.

**7** The seers will be ashamed,  
and the diviners disgraced;  
they will all cover their upper lips,  
for there is no answer from God.

**8** But as for me, I am filled with power,  
with the Spirit of the LORD,  
and with justice and might,  
to declare to Jacob his transgression  
and to Israel his sin.

## Because of You

**9** Hear this, please, you heads of the house of Jacob  
and rulers of the house of Israel,  
who detest justice  
and make crooked all that is straight,

**10** who build Zion with blood  
and Jerusalem with injustice.

**11** Her heads judge for a bribe,  
her priests teach for a price,  
and her prophets divine for money;  
yet they lean on the LORD and say,  
"Is not the LORD among us?  
No disaster will come upon us."

**12** Therefore, because of you,  
Zion shall be plowed as a field;  
Jerusalem shall become a heap of ruins,  
and the mountain of the house a wooded height.

## Notes

- **v1 "I said: Hear, please, you heads of Jacob… Is it not for you to know justice?"** — *va-omar*, **I said**: Micah speaks of himself for the first time. The heads and rulers are the ones whose work was to know *mishpat*, **justice**, the right judgment of a case, and v9 says they *detest* it.
- **vv2–3 "You who hate good and love evil… like meat for the pot"** — the reverse of Amos's *Hate evil and love good* (Amos 5:15). The people are butchered and cooked: skin, flesh, bones, pot. In Ezekiel the rulers of Jerusalem boast, *This city is the pot, and we are the meat* (Ezekiel 11:3); here they are the cooks, and the people are the meat. *My people* is Micah's own word, as in *the gate of my people* (1:9).
- **v4 "He will hide His face from them"** — the words of the law's warning, *I will forsake them and hide My face from them* (Deuteronomy 31:17). They cried no prayer while they ate; they will cry and not be answered.
- **v5 "who, when they have something to bite with their teeth, cry 'Peace!'"** — *ha-noshkhim be-shinneihem*, word for word **who bite with their teeth**; *nashakh* is the verb of the snake's bite (Amos 5:19; 9:3). A meal buys *shalom*, the word Jeremiah's prophets sold, *saying, "Peace, peace," when there is no peace* (Jeremiah 6:14). No meal, and they *consecrate war*, the holy war of *Consecrate war against her!* (Jeremiah 6:4).
- **vv6–7 "night for you, without vision… they will all cover their upper lips"** — night comes on the prophets with no vision in it, and the sun goes down on them as in Amos 8:9. To cover the upper lip is the sign of one cut off: *he shall cover his upper lip and call out, "Unclean! Unclean!"* (Leviticus 13:45), and of mourning, which Ezekiel was told to refuse, *do not cover your upper lip* (Ezekiel 24:17). *Ma'aneh*, **answer**, is from the verb of v4: the LORD will not answer the rulers, and He will not answer the prophets.
- **v8 "But as for me, I am filled with power, with the Spirit of the LORD"** — *et ruach YHWH*, the Spirit set between *power* and *justice*; the Greek has *in the Spirit of the Lord*. Against prophets who speak for food (v5) and for money (v11), Micah's fullness is the Spirit, and what it fills him for is to name sin. Another prophet was asked the same question with a slap, *Which way did the Spirit of the LORD pass from me to speak to you?* (1 Kings 22:24), the Micaiah whose words open this book (1:2).
- **vv9–10 "who detest justice and make crooked all that is straight… who build Zion with blood"** — *meta'avim*, **who detest**, is the verb of *they detest the one who speaks with integrity* (Amos 5:10), the root of *detestable*. *Ye'aqqeshu*, **make crooked**, is the law's word for *a crooked and twisted generation* (Deuteronomy 32:5). *Boneh*, **who builds**, is singular, as in Jeremiah's woe, *Woe to him who builds his house without righteousness* (Jeremiah 22:13).
- **v11 "Her heads judge for a bribe, her priests teach for a price… Is not the LORD among us?"** — the law said *you shall not take a bribe* (Deuteronomy 16:19), and *yoru*, **teach**, is the verb of *torah*: instruction sold. Their boast reverses the cry the law foretold, *Is it not because our God is not among us that these evils have found us?* (Deuteronomy 31:17); they say the LORD is among them, so no disaster will find them.
- **v12 "Zion shall be plowed as a field… a wooded height"** — this verse is quoted word for word in Jeremiah's trial: *Micah of Moresheth prophesied in the days of Hezekiah… Zion shall be plowed as a field* (Jeremiah 26:18). The elders go on: *Did he not fear the LORD and plead with the LORD, so that the LORD relented of the disaster that He had pronounced against them?* (Jeremiah 26:19). *Bamot ya'ar*, **a wooded height**, says *bamot* again, the heights of 1:3 and 1:5.

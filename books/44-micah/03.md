# Micah 3

## The Heads of Jacob

**1** I said:  
Hear, please, you heads of Jacob  
and rulers of the house of Israel!  
Is it not for you to know justice?

**2** You who hate good and love evil,  
who tear the skin off them  
and the flesh off their bones,

**3** who eat the flesh of my people,  
strip their skin off them,  
break their bones,  
and chop them up like meat for the pot,  
like flesh in a cauldron —

**4** then they will cry out to the LORD,  
but He will not answer them;  
He will hide His face from them at that time,  
because they have made their deeds evil.

## The Prophets Who Lead My People Astray

**5** This is what the LORD says concerning the prophets  
who lead My people astray,  
who, when they have something to bite with their teeth,  
cry "Peace!",  
but consecrate war against whoever puts nothing into their mouths:

**6** Therefore it will be night for you, without vision,  
and darkness for you, without divination;  
the sun will go down on the prophets,  
and the day will grow dark over them.

**7** The seers will be ashamed,  
and the diviners disgraced;  
they will all cover their upper lips,  
for there is no answer from God.

**8** But as for me, I am filled with power,  
with the Spirit of the LORD,  
and with justice and might,  
to declare to Jacob his transgression  
and to Israel his sin.

## Because of You

**9** Hear this, please, you heads of the house of Jacob  
and rulers of the house of Israel,  
who detest justice  
and make crooked all that is straight,

**10** who build Zion with blood  
and Jerusalem with injustice.

**11** Her heads judge for a bribe,  
her priests teach for a price,  
and her prophets divine for money;  
yet they lean on the LORD and say,  
"Is not the LORD among us?  
No disaster will come upon us."

**12** Therefore, because of you,  
Zion shall be plowed as a field;  
Jerusalem shall become a heap of ruins,  
and the mountain of the house a wooded height.

## Notes

- **v5 "who, when they have something to bite with their teeth, cry 'Peace!'"** — *ha-noshkhim be-shinneihem*, word for word **who bite with their teeth**; *nashakh* is the verb of the snake's bite (Amos 5:19; 9:3). A meal buys *shalom*, the word Jeremiah's prophets sold, *saying, "Peace, peace," when there is no peace* (Jeremiah 6:14). No meal, and they *consecrate war*, the holy war of *Consecrate war against her!* (Jeremiah 6:4).
- **v12 "Zion shall be plowed as a field… a wooded height"** — this verse is quoted word for word in Jeremiah's trial: *Micah of Moresheth prophesied in the days of Hezekiah… Zion shall be plowed as a field* (Jeremiah 26:18). The elders go on: *Did he not fear the LORD and plead with the LORD, so that the LORD relented of the disaster that He had pronounced against them?* (Jeremiah 26:19). *Bamot ya'ar*, **a wooded height**, says *bamot* again, the heights of 1:3 and 1:5.

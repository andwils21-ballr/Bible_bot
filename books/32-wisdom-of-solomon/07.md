# Wisdom of Solomon 7

**1** I also am mortal, like everyone,  
a descendant of the first-formed man, born of the earth;  
and in my mother's womb I was molded into flesh,

**2** within the time of ten months,  
compacted in blood from the seed of a man  
and the pleasure that comes with sleep.

**3** When I was born, I too drew in the common air,  
and fell upon the earth that we all share,  
and my first sound was a cry, like that of all.

**4** I was nursed in swaddling cloths, and with cares.

**5** For no king has had any other beginning of existence;

**6** there is one entrance into life for all,  
and one way out alike.

**7** Therefore I prayed, and understanding was given me;  
I called, and the spirit of wisdom came to me.

**8** I preferred her to scepters and thrones,  
and I counted wealth as nothing beside her.

**9** Nor did I liken to her any priceless stone,  
because all gold is a little sand in her sight,  
and silver will be counted as clay before her.

**10** I loved her more than health and beauty,  
and chose to have her rather than light,  
because her radiance never sleeps.

**11** All good things came to me together with her,  
and countless wealth in her hands.

**12** I rejoiced in them all, because wisdom leads them,  
but I did not know that she was their source.

**13** I learned without guile and I share without grudging;  
I do not hide her wealth.

**14** For she is an unfailing treasure for men;  
those who use it gain friendship with God,  
commended to Him by the gifts that come from discipline.

**15** May God grant me to speak with judgment,  
and to think thoughts worthy of what has been given,  
for He is the guide even of wisdom  
and the corrector of the wise.

**16** For both we and our words are in His hand,  
and all understanding and skill in crafts.

**17** For it is He who gave me unerring knowledge of what exists,  
to know the structure of the world and the working of the elements;

**18** the beginning and end and middle of times,  
the turns of the solstices and the changes of the seasons,

**19** the cycles of the year and the positions of the stars,

**20** the natures of living creatures and the tempers of wild beasts,  
the forces of winds and the reasonings of men,  
the varieties of plants and the powers of roots.

**21** I learned both what is hidden and what is plain,

**22** for wisdom, the fashioner of all things, taught me.  
For in her is a spirit that is intelligent, holy,  
unique, manifold, subtle,  
mobile, clear, unpolluted,  
distinct, invulnerable, loving the good, keen,  
unhindered, beneficent,

**23** loving mankind, steadfast, sure, free from care,  
all-powerful, overseeing all,  
and passing through all spirits  
that are intelligent, pure and most subtle.

**24** For wisdom is more mobile than any motion;  
because of her pureness she pervades and passes through all things.

**25** For she is a breath of the power of God,  
and a pure outflowing of the glory of the Almighty;  
therefore nothing defiled finds its way into her.

**26** For she is a reflection of eternal light,  
a spotless mirror of the working of God,  
and an image of His goodness.

**27** Though she is one, she can do all things,  
and while remaining in herself, she makes all things new;  
in every generation she passes into holy souls  
and makes them friends of God and prophets;

**28** for God loves nothing so much as the one who lives with wisdom.

**29** For she is more beautiful than the sun,  
and excels every constellation of the stars;  
compared with light she is found to be superior,

**30** for night follows the light,  
but against wisdom evil does not prevail.

## Notes

- **vv1–6 "I also am mortal"** — the king begins as every person begins: from Adam, "the first-formed man, born of the earth" (Genesis 2:7), carried in the womb "ten months" counted by the moon, crying at birth. What he has, he received by asking (v7).
- **v7 "Therefore I prayed"** — Solomon's request at Gibeon (1 Kings 3:5–12); the prayer itself comes in chapter 9.
- **vv8–11 "I preferred her to scepters and thrones"** — the choice of 1 Kings 3:11–13, and the riches that came with it unasked; compare Proverbs 8:10–11 and Job 28:15–19.
- **v20 "the forces of winds"** — *pneumatōn*, winds or spirits; in this list of the natural world it is the winds. The list matches what Solomon spoke of in 1 Kings 4:33: trees, beasts, birds, creeping things and fish.
- **v22 "the fashioner of all things"** — *technitis*, a craftswoman: Proverbs 8:30, *I was beside Him as a master workman*. "Unique" — *monogenes*, "the only one of its kind," the word John uses of the Son (John 1:14, 18; 3:16). The spirit in wisdom is given twenty-one marks, three times seven.
- **v25 "a breath… a pure outflowing"** — *atmis*, vapor, and *aporroia*, what streams out from a source: wisdom comes from God as breath from a mouth.
- **v26 "a reflection of eternal light… an image of His goodness"** — *apaugasma* and *eikōn*. Hebrews says the Son is *the radiance (apaugasma) of His glory* (Hebrews 1:3), and Paul, *the image (eikōn) of the invisible God* (Colossians 1:15).
- **v27 "friends of God and prophets"** — Abraham is called God's friend (2 Chronicles 20:7; James 2:23).
- **v30 "against wisdom evil does not prevail"** — compare John 1:5, *the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it*.

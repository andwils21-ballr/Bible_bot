# Wisdom of Solomon 11

**1** She prospered their works by the hand of a holy prophet.

**2** They journeyed through an uninhabited wilderness,  
and pitched their tents in trackless places.

**3** They stood against their foes,  
and fought off their enemies.

**4** When they were thirsty, they called upon You,  
and water was given them out of the flint rock,  
and relief from thirst out of hard stone.

**5** For by the very things through which their enemies were punished,  
they themselves were helped in their need.

**6** Instead of the spring of an ever-flowing river,  
stirred up and defiled with blood

**7** in rebuke for the decree to kill the infants,  
You gave them abundant water when they had no hope of it,

**8** showing by their thirst at that time  
how You punished their adversaries.

**9** For when they were tried,  
though they were being disciplined in mercy,  
they learned how the ungodly were tormented  
when judged in wrath.

**10** For these You tested as a father does in warning,  
but those You examined as a stern king does in condemning.

**11** Whether absent or present, they were equally distressed,

**12** for a double grief seized them,  
and a groaning at the memory of what had passed.

**13** For when they heard that through their own punishments  
the others had been helped,  
they perceived the Lord.

**14** For the one they had long before cast out and exposed,  
and then rejected with scorn,  
they marveled at in the end,  
when they had thirsted as the righteous had not.

**15** In return for their foolish and wicked thoughts,  
which led them astray to worship  
irrational reptiles and worthless creatures,  
You sent upon them a multitude of irrational creatures to punish them,

**16** so that they might learn  
that one is punished by the very things by which he sins.

**17** For Your all-powerful hand,  
which created the world out of formless matter,  
did not lack the means to send upon them  
a multitude of bears or bold lions,

**18** or newly created beasts, unknown and full of rage,  
breathing out a fiery blast,  
or puffing out roaring smoke,  
or flashing terrible sparks from their eyes,

**19** whose harm could not only destroy them,  
but whose very sight could kill them with fright.

**20** Even apart from these, they could have fallen at a single breath,  
pursued by justice,  
and scattered by the breath of Your power.  
But You have arranged all things  
by measure and number and weight.

**21** For it is always in Your power to show great strength,  
and who can withstand the might of Your arm?

**22** Because the whole world before You is like a speck that tips the scales,  
and like a drop of morning dew that falls on the ground.

**23** But You have mercy on all, because You can do all things,  
and You overlook the sins of men, so that they may repent.

**24** For You love all things that exist,  
and abhor none of the things You have made,  
for You would not have made anything if You had hated it.

**25** How would anything have endured if You had not willed it?  
Or how would anything not called by You have been preserved?

**26** You spare all things, because they are Yours,  
Master, lover of life.

## Notes

- **v1 "a holy prophet"** — Moses, the prophet like no other (Deuteronomy 34:10). From v4 on, the book speaks to God directly, and does so to its end.
- **v4 "out of the flint rock"** — Exodus 17:6 and Numbers 20:11; the Greek of Deuteronomy 8:15 has the same words, *ek petras akrotomou*.
- **v5** — the rule that governs the rest of the book: the same things that punished Egypt helped Israel. A series of such comparisons runs through chapter 19, beginning here with water: the Nile turned to blood (Exodus 7:20), the rock that gave water.
- **v7 "the decree to kill the infants"** — Pharaoh's command that every son be thrown into the Nile (Exodus 1:22): the river that took the children was turned to blood.
- **v10 "as a father… as a stern king"** — *as a man disciplines his son, the LORD your God disciplines you* (Deuteronomy 8:5).
- **v14 "the one they had long before cast out and exposed"** — Moses, set out on the Nile as a baby (Exodus 2:3) and later rejected (2:14).
- **vv15–16 "one is punished by the very things by which he sins"** — Egypt worshipped animals, and was struck by frogs, gnats, flies and locusts.
- **v20 "by measure and number and weight"** — compare Job 28:25 and Isaiah 40:12. "The breath of Your power" — a blast of God's breath, lowercase here as a wind that scatters (5:23), as in Isaiah 11:4, *with the breath of his lips he shall kill the wicked*.
- **v22 "like a speck that tips the scales"** — Isaiah 40:15, *the nations are like a drop from a bucket, and are counted as the dust on the scales*.
- **v26 "Master, lover of life"** — *Despota philopsyche*, "lover of souls": the God who made all things spares them because they are His.

# Wisdom of Solomon 19

**1** But upon the ungodly pitiless anger came to the end,  
for He knew beforehand even what they would do:

**2** that, though they themselves had let Your people go  
and hurriedly sent them on their way,  
they would change their minds and pursue them.

**3** For while they were still busy with mourning,  
and lamenting at the graves of their dead,  
they took up another foolish plan,  
and pursued as fugitives  
those whom they had begged to leave and driven out.

**4** For the fate they deserved drew them on to this end,  
and made them forget what had happened,  
so that they might fill up the punishment  
still lacking from their torments,

**5** and that Your people might experience an incredible journey,  
while they themselves met a strange death.

**6** For the whole creation, each part in its own kind,  
was fashioned anew,  
obeying Your commands,  
so that Your children might be kept unharmed.

**7** The cloud was seen overshadowing the camp,  
and dry land rising where water had stood before,  
an unhindered way out of the Red Sea,  
and a grassy plain out of the raging waves,

**8** through which the whole nation passed,  
protected by Your hand,  
after seeing marvelous wonders.

**9** For they ranged like horses,  
and leaped like lambs,  
praising You, Lord, who delivered them.

**10** For they still remembered the things of their stay as guests,  
how instead of producing animals the earth brought forth gnats,  
and instead of fish the river spewed out a multitude of frogs.

**11** Afterward they saw also a new kind of birds,  
when desire led them to ask for luxurious food;

**12** for to comfort them quails came up from the sea.

**13** The punishments came upon the sinners  
not without signs given beforehand in the violence of the thunder;  
for they suffered justly for their own wickedness,  
since they practiced a more bitter hatred of strangers.

**14** Others had refused to receive strangers when they came,  
but these made slaves of guests who were their benefactors.

**15** Not only so, but there will be a visitation on those others,  
because they received foreigners with hostility;

**16** but these, after receiving with festivities  
those who had already shared the same rights,  
afflicted them with terrible labors.

**17** They were struck also with blindness,  
as those others were at the door of the righteous man,  
when, surrounded by yawning darkness,  
each tried to find the way through his own door.

**18** For the elements changed places with one another,  
as on a harp the notes change the character of the rhythm  
while each keeps its own sound;  
this may be clearly inferred from the sight of what took place.

**19** For land animals were changed into water creatures,  
and creatures that swim went over onto the land;

**20** fire kept its own power even in water,  
and water forgot its power to quench;

**21** flames, on the other hand, did not consume  
the flesh of perishable creatures that walked among them,  
nor melt the icelike, easily melted kind of heavenly food.

**22** For in everything, Lord, You have exalted and glorified Your people,  
and You have not neglected to stand by them  
at every time and in every place.

## Notes

- **v1 "He knew beforehand"** — the Greek gives the verb no named subject; God is meant, as the address of vv9 and 22 shows.
- **v12 "quails came up from the sea"** — *A wind went out from the LORD and swept quail in from the sea* (Numbers 11:31).

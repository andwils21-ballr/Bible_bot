# Wisdom of Solomon 16

**1** Therefore they were deservedly punished through such creatures,  
and were tormented by a multitude of vermin.

**2** Instead of this punishment, You showed kindness to Your people,  
and to satisfy the desire of their appetite  
You prepared quails to eat, a food of strange taste,

**3** so that those others, when they desired food,  
might lose even the appetite they needed  
because of the hideous creatures sent among them,  
while Your people, after a short time of want,  
might share in a food of strange taste.

**4** For it was necessary that upon those oppressors  
inescapable want should come,  
while to these it was only shown  
how their enemies were being tormented.

**5** For when the terrible rage of wild beasts came upon Your people,  
and they were being destroyed by the bites of writhing serpents,  
Your wrath did not continue to the end;

**6** they were troubled for a little while as a warning,  
and they had a sign of salvation  
to remind them of the commandment of Your law.

**7** For the one who turned toward it was saved,  
not by what he saw,  
but by You, the Savior of all.

**8** By this also You convinced our enemies  
that it is You who deliver from every evil.

**9** For they were killed by the bites of locusts and flies,  
and no healing was found for their life,  
because they deserved to be punished by such things;

**10** but Your sons were not conquered even by the fangs of venomous serpents,  
for Your mercy came to meet them and healed them.

**11** To remind them of Your sayings they were bitten,  
and then quickly delivered,  
so that they might not fall into deep forgetfulness  
and become cut off from Your kindness.

**12** For neither herb nor poultice cured them,  
but Your word, Lord, which heals all things.

**13** For You have power over life and death;  
You lead down to the gates of Hades, and lead up again.

**14** A man in his wickedness can kill,  
but he cannot bring back the spirit that has gone out,  
nor free the soul that has been taken.

**15** To escape from Your hand is impossible;

**16** for the ungodly, refusing to know You,  
were scourged by the strength of Your arm,  
pursued by strange rains and hail and relentless storms,  
and utterly consumed by fire.

**17** For — most incredible of all —  
in the water, which quenches all things,  
the fire had still greater effect,  
for the world fights for the righteous.

**18** At one time the flame was restrained,  
so that it might not burn up the creatures sent against the ungodly,  
but that seeing this they might know  
that they were being pursued by the judgment of God;

**19** at another time, even in the middle of water,  
it burned more fiercely than fire,  
to destroy the crops of an unrighteous land.

**20** Instead of these things, You fed Your people with the food of angels,  
and without their toil You sent them from heaven bread ready to eat,  
able to give every pleasure and suited to every taste.

**21** For Your sustenance showed Your sweetness toward Your children;  
and serving the desire of the one who took it,  
it changed to whatever each one wished.

**22** Snow and ice withstood fire and did not melt,  
so that they might know that the crops of their enemies  
were being destroyed by fire blazing in the hail  
and flashing in the rain;

**23** while this fire, again, so that the righteous might be fed,  
even forgot its own power.

**24** For creation, serving You who made it,  
tightens itself to punish the unrighteous,  
and slackens to do good to those who trust in You.

**25** Therefore at that time also, changing into all things,  
it served Your gift that feeds all,  
as those in need desired,

**26** so that Your sons, whom You loved, Lord, might learn  
that it is not the producing of crops that feeds man,  
but Your word preserves those who trust in You.

**27** For what was not destroyed by fire  
was melted when simply warmed by a brief ray of the sun,

**28** to make it known that one must rise before the sun to give You thanks,  
and meet You at the dawning of the light;

**29** for the hope of an ungrateful man will melt like wintry frost,  
and flow away like useless water.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "Instead of this punishment… quails"** — the second comparison: Egypt plagued by vermin (frogs, gnats, flies), Israel fed with quails in the wilderness (Exodus 16:13; Numbers 11:31).
- **vv5–7 "writhing serpents… a sign of salvation… not by what he saw, but by You"** — the bronze serpent of Numbers 21:6–9. The book is careful: the sign did not save; God did. Jesus uses the same scene of Himself (John 3:14–15).
- **v12 "Your word, Lord, which heals all things"** — Psalm 107:20, *He sent His word and healed them*.
- **v13 "You lead down to the gates of Hades, and lead up again"** — Hannah's song: *He brings down to Sheol and brings up* (1 Samuel 2:6).
- **vv16–19 "fire… in the water"** — the hail of Exodus 9:23–24, *fire flashing in the middle of the hail*. "The world fights for the righteous" — *hypermachos*, the word of 10:20, "Your hand that fought for them," and the battle of 5:20.
- **v20 "the food of angels… bread ready to eat"** — the manna. Psalm 78:25 in the Hebrew says *the bread of the mighty*; the Greek has *bread of angels*, and the book follows the Greek. John 6:31 quotes *He gave them bread from heaven to eat*.
- **v21 "it changed to whatever each one wished"** — the manna tasted as each one desired: an old tradition, here told as a sign of God's sweetness.
- **vv22, 27 "Snow and ice withstood fire… melted… by a brief ray of the sun"** — the manna, *fine as frost on the ground* (Exodus 16:14), which could be baked and boiled (16:23) but melted when the sun grew hot (16:21).
- **v24 "tightens… slackens"** — *epiteinetai… anietai*, the words for tuning a string: creation is an instrument in God's hand, tightened to punish and loosened to bless.
- **v26 "it is not the producing of crops that feeds man, but Your word"** — Deuteronomy 8:3, *a person does not live on bread alone*, which Jesus quotes in the wilderness (Matthew 4:4).

# Wisdom of Solomon 9

**1** God of the fathers and Lord of mercy,  
who made all things by Your word,

**2** and by Your wisdom formed man  
to have dominion over the creatures You have made,

**3** and rule the world in holiness and righteousness,  
and pronounce judgment in uprightness of soul,

**4** give me the wisdom that sits beside Your throne,  
and do not reject me from among Your servants.

**5** For I am Your servant and the son of Your maidservant,  
a man who is weak and short-lived,  
with little understanding of judgment and laws;

**6** for even if one is perfect among the sons of men,  
without the wisdom that comes from You  
he will be counted as nothing.

**7** You chose me to be king of Your people  
and judge of Your sons and daughters.

**8** You told me to build a temple on Your holy mountain,  
and an altar in the city where You dwell,  
a copy of the holy tent that You prepared from the beginning.

**9** With You is wisdom, who knows Your works  
and was present when You made the world,  
and understands what is pleasing in Your sight  
and what is right in Your commandments.

**10** Send her forth from the holy heavens,  
and from the throne of Your glory send her,  
so that she may be with me and toil,  
and that I may learn what is pleasing to You.

**11** For she knows and understands all things,  
and she will guide me wisely in my actions,  
and guard me with her glory.

**12** Then my works will be acceptable,  
and I will judge Your people justly,  
and I will be worthy of the throne of my father.

**13** For what man can know the counsel of God?  
Or who can discern what the Lord wills?

**14** For the reasonings of mortals are timid,  
and our designs are likely to fail;

**15** for a perishable body weighs down the soul,  
and this earthly tent burdens the mind full of thoughts.

**16** We can hardly guess at what is on earth,  
and what is at hand we find with labor;  
but who has traced out what is in the heavens?

**17** Who has learned Your counsel,  
unless You gave wisdom  
and sent Your holy Spirit from on high?

**18** So the paths of those on earth were set right,  
and men were taught what pleases You,  
and were saved by wisdom.

## Notes

- **vv1–18** — the prayer that 7:7 and 8:21 promised, the prayer at Gibeon (1 Kings 3:6–9; 2 Chronicles 1:8–10) in the book's own words.
- **v1 "who made all things by Your word"** — *en logō sou*: the word by which the heavens were made (Psalm 33:6), and the Word of John 1:3, *all things came to be through Him*. Here the word and wisdom (v2) stand side by side.
- **v4 "the wisdom that sits beside Your throne"** — *paredros*, "one seated beside," as an assessor sits beside a judge. "Your servants" — *paidōn*, the word of 2:13 ("a child of the Lord"); here, beside v5, it means those who serve.
- **v5 "I am Your servant and the son of Your maidservant"** — the words of Psalm 116:16.
- **v8 "a copy of the holy tent that You prepared from the beginning"** — the pattern shown to Moses on the mountain (Exodus 25:9, 40); Hebrews calls the earthly sanctuary *a copy and shadow of the heavenly things* (Hebrews 8:5).
- **v13 "what man can know the counsel of God?"** — Isaiah 40:13; Paul quotes it in Romans 11:34.
- **v15 "this earthly tent burdens the mind"** — *skēnos*, "tent," the body: Paul, *while we are still in this tent, we groan, being burdened* (2 Corinthians 5:4), uses the same two words.
- **v17 "sent Your holy Spirit from on high"** — *to hagion sou pneuma*, "Your holy Spirit," capital S since it is God's (as in 1:7), and beside wisdom as God's gift (1:5 and its note).

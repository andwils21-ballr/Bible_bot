# Wisdom of Solomon 5

**1** Then the righteous man will stand with great boldness  
to face those who afflicted him  
and made light of his labors.

**2** When they see him, they will be shaken with dreadful fear,  
and they will be amazed at his unexpected salvation.

**3** They will say to themselves, repenting,  
and groaning in anguish of spirit:  
"This is the man whom we once held in derision  
and made a byword of reproach —

**4** we fools! We counted his life madness,  
and his end without honor.

**5** How has he been numbered among the sons of God,  
and how is his lot among the holy ones?

**6** So we strayed from the way of truth,  
and the light of righteousness did not shine on us,  
and the sun did not rise on us.

**7** We took our fill of the paths of lawlessness and destruction,  
and we journeyed through trackless deserts,  
but the way of the Lord we did not know.

**8** What has our arrogance profited us?  
What good has wealth with boasting brought us?

**9** All those things have passed away like a shadow,  
and like a rumor that hurries by;

**10** like a ship that passes through the surging water,  
and when it has gone by no trace can be found,  
nor the track of its keel in the waves;

**11** or as, when a bird flies through the air,  
no sign of its passage is found,  
but the light air, lashed by the beat of its wings  
and torn by the force of its rushing,  
is passed through by the moving of its wings,  
and afterward no sign of its coming is found there;

**12** or as, when an arrow is shot at a target,  
the air, divided, at once comes back together,  
so that no one knows its path.

**13** So we also, as soon as we were born, ceased to be;  
we had no sign of virtue to show,  
but were consumed in our wickedness."

**14** For the hope of the ungodly man is like chaff carried by the wind,  
and like a light frost driven away by a storm;  
it is scattered like smoke by the wind,  
and it passes like the remembrance of a guest who stays one day.

**15** But the righteous live forever,  
and their reward is with the Lord,  
and the Most High takes care of them.

**16** Therefore they will receive the glorious kingdom  
and the crown of beauty from the hand of the Lord,  
because with His right hand He will cover them,  
and with His arm He will shield them.

**17** He will take His zeal as His full armor,  
and will arm creation to repel His enemies;

**18** He will put on righteousness as a breastplate,  
and wear impartial judgment as a helmet;

**19** He will take holiness as an invincible shield,

**20** and sharpen stern wrath for a sword,  
and the world will fight with Him against the senseless.

**21** Well-aimed shafts of lightning will fly,  
and from the clouds, as from a well-drawn bow,  
they will leap to the mark,

**22** and hailstones full of wrath will be hurled as from a catapult;  
the water of the sea will rage against them,  
and rivers will relentlessly overwhelm them;

**23** a mighty wind will rise against them,  
and like a whirlwind it will winnow them away.  
Lawlessness will lay waste the whole earth,  
and evildoing will overturn the thrones of rulers.

## Notes

- **v1 "Then the righteous man will stand"** — the judgment scene answering 2:12–20: the man the ungodly tested to death now faces them, and they speak again (vv3–13), this time in regret.
- **v5 "among the sons of God… among the holy ones"** — the company of heaven, the "sons of God" of Job 38:7; he whom they mocked for calling God his father (2:16) is counted with them.
- **v6 "the sun did not rise on us"** — the sun of righteousness that rises for those who fear God's name (Malachi 4:2).
- **vv9–12 "a ship… a bird… an arrow"** — three things that pass and leave no trace, like the sayings of Reproof 6:19 (Proverbs 30:19), *the way of an eagle in the sky… the way of a ship on the high seas*.
- **v14 "a guest who stays one day"** — remembered for a day and gone.
- **vv17–20 "full armor… breastplate… helmet… shield… sword"** — God armed for battle, as in Isaiah 59:17, *He put on righteousness as a breastplate*. Paul gives the same armor to believers: *the full armor (panoplia) of God*, *the breastplate of righteousness* (Ephesians 6:11–17).
- **vv20–22 "the world will fight with Him"** — lightning, hail, sea and rivers take God's side against the wicked; the book returns to this in the plagues of Egypt and the sea (16:17; 19:6).
- **v23 "a mighty wind"** — *pneuma dynameōs*, a wind of power: the storm of vv21–22, lowercase.

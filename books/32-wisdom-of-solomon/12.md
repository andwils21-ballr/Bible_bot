# Wisdom of Solomon 12

**1** For Your incorruptible Spirit is in all things.

**2** Therefore You correct little by little those who go astray,  
and You remind and warn them of the things by which they sin,  
so that they may be freed from wickedness  
and believe in You, Lord.

**3** For the old inhabitants of Your holy land

**4** You hated for their most hateful practices,  
their works of sorcery and unholy rites,

**5** their merciless slaughter of children,  
their feasting on human flesh and blood,  
initiates from among a frenzied band,

**6** parents who murdered helpless souls —  
these You willed to destroy by the hands of our fathers,

**7** so that the land most precious of all to You  
might receive a worthy colony of the children of God.

**8** Yet even these You spared, since they were men,  
and sent hornets as forerunners of Your army,  
to destroy them little by little,

**9** though You were not unable to give the ungodly  
into the hands of the righteous in battle,  
or to destroy them at one blow  
by dread wild beasts or a stern word.

**10** But judging them little by little, You gave them a place for repentance,  
though You knew that their origin was evil  
and their wickedness inborn,  
and that their way of thinking would never change.

**11** For they were a seed accursed from the beginning;  
nor was it out of fear of anyone  
that You left them unpunished for their sins.

**12** For who will say, "What have You done?"  
Or who will resist Your judgment?  
Who will accuse You for the destruction of nations that You made?  
Or who will come before You to plead as an avenger  
for unrighteous men?

**13** For there is no god besides You,  
whose care is for all,  
to whom You must show that You have not judged unjustly;

**14** nor can any king or monarch confront You  
about those whom You have punished.

**15** You are righteous and rule all things righteously,  
counting it foreign to Your power  
to condemn one who does not deserve to be punished.

**16** For Your strength is the source of righteousness,  
and because You are Master of all, You spare all.

**17** For You show Your strength  
when men doubt that Your power is complete,  
and among those who know it, You rebuke their insolence.

**18** But You, who are master of strength, judge with gentleness,  
and with great forbearance You govern us;  
for You have power to act whenever You choose.

**19** Through such works You have taught Your people  
that the righteous man must love mankind,  
and You have made Your sons full of good hope,  
because You give repentance for sins.

**20** For if You punished the enemies of Your children,  
who deserved to die,  
with such great care and pleading,  
granting them time and opportunity  
to be freed from their wickedness,

**21** with what strictness You have judged Your sons,  
to whose fathers You gave oaths and covenants  
full of good promises!

**22** So while You discipline us,  
You scourge our enemies ten thousand times more,  
so that when we judge, we may think of Your goodness,  
and when we are judged, we may expect mercy.

**23** Therefore those who lived unrighteously, in the folly of their life,  
You tormented through their own detestable things.

**24** For they went far astray on the paths of error,  
taking for gods those animals  
that even their enemies despised,  
deceived like foolish infants.

**25** Therefore, as to children who cannot reason,  
You sent Your judgment to mock them.

**26** But those who would not be warned by mocking rebukes  
will experience a judgment worthy of God.

**27** For when they suffered, they were indignant at the creatures  
they had thought to be gods,  
and were punished by means of them;  
and they saw and recognized as the true God  
Him whom they had long refused to know.  
Therefore the last of condemnation came upon them.

## Notes

- **v1 "Your incorruptible Spirit is in all things"** — *to aphtharton sou pneuma*, capital S since it is God's (as 1:7 and 9:17): the Spirit that fills the world (1:7) holds every creature in being, and so God spares what He made (11:24–26).
- **vv3–7 "the old inhabitants of Your holy land"** — the Canaanites; the practices listed are those Deuteronomy names as the reason for their removal (Deuteronomy 12:31; 18:9–12).
- **v5 "initiates from among a frenzied band"** — *ek mesou mystas thiasou*: *thiasos* is a band of worshippers in an ecstatic rite. The Greek of the line is uneven, and its exact sense uncertain.
- **v7 "the children of God"** — *theou paidōn*; *pais* is "child" or "servant" (2:13; 9:4). Here, beside "Your sons" (vv19, 21), it is "children."
- **v8 "hornets as forerunners of Your army"** — *I will send the hornet before you* (Exodus 23:28; Deuteronomy 7:20; Joshua 24:12).
- **v10 "a place for repentance"** — *topon metanoias*, the phrase of Hebrews 12:17, where Esau *found no place for repentance*.
- **v11 "a seed accursed from the beginning"** — *Cursed be Canaan* (Genesis 9:25).
- **v12 "who will say, 'What have You done?'"** — Job 9:12; Ecclesiastes 8:4; Paul, *who are you, man, to answer back to God?* (Romans 9:20).
- **v19 "the righteous man must love mankind"** — *philanthrōpon*, the word used of wisdom's own spirit (1:6; 7:23): God's patience teaches His people to be like Him.
- **v20 "with such great care and pleading"** — *deēsis*, "entreaty": even God's punishment of Canaan came with a call to turn.

# Wisdom of Solomon 3

**1** But the souls of the righteous are in the hand of God,  
and no torment will touch them.

**2** In the eyes of the foolish they seemed to have died,  
and their departure was thought to be an affliction,

**3** and their going from us to be their ruin;  
but they are at peace.

**4** For though in the sight of men they were punished,  
their hope is full of immortality.

**5** Having been disciplined a little, they will receive great good,  
because God tested them and found them worthy of Himself;

**6** like gold in the furnace He tried them,  
and like a whole burnt offering He accepted them.

**7** In the time of their visitation they will shine out,  
and will run like sparks through the stubble.

**8** They will judge nations and rule over peoples,  
and the Lord will reign over them forever.

**9** Those who trust in Him will understand truth,  
and the faithful will stay with Him in love,  
because grace and mercy are with His chosen ones.

**10** But the ungodly will be punished as their reasoning deserves,  
those who neglected the righteous man and rebelled against the Lord;

**11** for whoever despises wisdom and discipline is miserable.  
Their hope is empty, their labors unprofitable,  
and their works useless.

**12** Their wives are foolish, and their children evil;  
their offspring are accursed.

**13** For blessed is the barren woman who is undefiled,  
who has not known a sinful bed;  
she will have fruit at the visitation of souls.

**14** So too the eunuch whose hand has done no lawless deed,  
and who has not plotted wicked things against the Lord;  
for a special favor will be given him for his faithfulness,  
and a share in the temple of the Lord more pleasing to the heart.

**15** For the fruit of good labors is glorious,  
and the root of understanding does not fail.

**16** But the children of adulterers will not come to maturity,  
and the offspring of an unlawful bed will vanish.

**17** Even if they live long, they will be counted as nothing,  
and at the last their old age will be without honor.

**18** If they die early, they have no hope,  
and no comfort on the day of decision.

**19** For the end of an unrighteous generation is grievous.

## Notes

- **v1 "the souls of the righteous are in the hand of God"** — the answer to 2:1–20: the righteous man whom the ungodly killed to see whether God would help him is safe in God's hand.
- **vv5–6 "disciplined… tested… like gold in the furnace"** — *paideuthentes*, from *paideia*, "discipline" (1:5); the refining of Proverbs 17:3, *the crucible is for silver and the furnace for gold, but the LORD tests hearts*.
- **v7 "they will shine out"** — compare Daniel 12:3, *those who are wise shall shine like the brightness of the expanse*. "Like sparks through the stubble" — fire that runs through a dry field.
- **v11 "whoever despises wisdom and discipline"** — Proverbs 1:7 in the Greek, *the ungodly will despise wisdom and discipline*, with the same verb (*exouthenein*).
- **vv13–14 "the barren woman… the eunuch"** — the two who had no children, and whom Isaiah comforts: *Sing, barren one* (54:1), and the eunuchs who keep God's covenant, to whom He gives *in My house and within My walls a monument and a name better than sons and daughters* (56:3–5).
- **v13 "the visitation of souls"** — *episkopē*, God's coming to look upon and judge, as in v7 ("the time of their visitation").

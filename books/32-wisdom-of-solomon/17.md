# Wisdom of Solomon 17

**1** For Your judgments are great and hard to describe;  
therefore undisciplined souls went astray.

**2** For when lawless men supposed that they held a holy nation in their power,  
they themselves lay as captives of darkness and prisoners of a long night,  
shut in under their roofs,  
exiles from eternal providence.

**3** For thinking that they were unseen in their secret sins,  
they were scattered behind a dark curtain of forgetfulness,  
terribly alarmed, and troubled by phantoms.

**4** For not even the inner room that held them kept them from fear,  
but sounds that troubled them rang all around,  
and gloomy apparitions with unsmiling faces appeared.

**5** No power of fire was able to give light,  
nor could the brilliant flames of the stars  
bear to light up that hateful night.

**6** Nothing shone through to them but a fire, self-kindled and full of fear,  
and in their terror, when that sight was gone,  
they thought the things they had seen worse than they were.

**7** The tricks of their magic art lay humbled,  
and their boasting in wisdom was scornfully exposed.

**8** For those who promised to drive away fears and troubles from a sick soul  
were themselves sick with ridiculous fear.

**9** For even if nothing frightening alarmed them,  
yet, startled by the passing of vermin and the hissing of snakes,

**10** they perished trembling,  
refusing even to look at the air,  
though it could nowhere be escaped.

**11** For wickedness is a cowardly thing, condemned by its own witness;  
pressed by conscience, it has always imagined the worst.

**12** For fear is nothing but the surrender of the help that comes from reason;

**13** and the hope within, being weak,  
counts ignorance of the cause of its torment worse than the torment.

**14** But all through that night, which was truly powerless,  
and which came upon them from the depths of powerless Hades,  
they slept the same sleep,

**15** and now were driven by monstrous apparitions,  
and now were paralyzed by their souls' surrender;  
for sudden and unexpected fear came upon them.

**16** So whoever was there fell down,  
and was kept shut up in a prison not made of iron;

**17** for whether he was a farmer or a shepherd  
or a laborer at work in the wilderness,  
he was overtaken, and endured the inescapable fate;

**18** for with one chain of darkness they were all bound.  
Whether there was a whistling wind,  
or the melodious sound of birds in wide-spreading branches,  
or the rhythm of water rushing violently,

**19** or the harsh crash of rocks hurled down,  
or the unseen running of leaping animals,  
or the sound of the most savage roaring beasts,  
or an echo thrown back from a hollow of the mountains,  
it paralyzed them with terror.

**20** For the whole world was lit with brilliant light,  
and went about its work unhindered,

**21** while over those men alone heavy night was spread,  
an image of the darkness that was to receive them;  
but heavier than darkness were they to themselves.

## Notes

- **vv1–21** — the ninth plague, the darkness over Egypt (Exodus 10:21–23), told from inside the darkness. From here to the end the book sets the plagues and the Exodus side by side, each punishment of Egypt matched by a mercy to Israel.
- **v2 "exiles from eternal providence"** — *pronoia*, the forethought of God that steered Noah's raft (14:3); the Egyptians are shut out from it as from the light.
- **v7 "The tricks of their magic art lay humbled"** — Pharaoh's magicians, who matched the first signs and then could not (Exodus 7:11, 22; 8:18–19).
- **v11 "pressed by conscience"** — *syneidēsis*, the word Paul uses for the inner witness (Romans 2:15).
- **vv12–13** — a definition of fear: the mind gives up its own help, and not knowing the cause becomes worse than the pain.
- **v18 "with one chain of darkness they were all bound"** — compare 2 Peter 2:4 and Jude 6, the rebels *kept in chains under gloomy darkness*.
- **vv18–19** — every ordinary sound of the world, wind and birds and water and echo, becomes a terror in the dark.
- **v20 "the whole world was lit with brilliant light"** — *for all the sons of Israel there was light in their dwellings* (Exodus 10:23).

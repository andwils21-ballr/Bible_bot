# Wisdom of Solomon 2

**1** For they reasoned unsoundly, saying to themselves:  
"Short and sorrowful is our life,  
and there is no remedy when a man comes to his end,  
and no one has been known to return from Hades.

**2** For we were born by chance,  
and afterward we shall be as though we had never been;  
for the breath in our nostrils is smoke,  
and reason is a spark at the beating of our hearts;

**3** when it is put out, the body will turn to ashes,  
and the spirit will dissolve like empty air.

**4** Our name will be forgotten in time,  
and no one will remember our works;  
our life will pass away like the traces of a cloud,  
and be scattered like mist  
chased by the rays of the sun  
and weighed down by its heat.

**5** For our life is the passing of a shadow,  
and there is no turning back from our end,  
because it is sealed, and no one returns.

**6** Come, then, let us enjoy the good things that are here,  
and make use of the creation eagerly, as in youth.

**7** Let us take our fill of costly wine and perfumes,  
and let no flower of the air pass us by.

**8** Let us crown ourselves with rosebuds before they wither.

**9** Let none of us go without a share in our revelry;  
everywhere let us leave signs of our merriment,  
because this is our portion, and this our lot.

**10** Let us oppress the righteous poor man;  
let us not spare the widow,  
nor respect the gray hair of the aged, grown old in years.

**11** But let our strength be our law of righteousness,  
for what is weak proves itself useless.

**12** Let us lie in wait for the righteous man,  
because he is a nuisance to us and opposes our actions;  
he reproaches us for sins against the law,  
and charges us with sins against our upbringing.

**13** He professes to have knowledge of God,  
and calls himself a child of the Lord.

**14** He has become a reproof of our thoughts;  
even the sight of him is a burden to us,

**15** because his life is unlike the life of others,  
and his ways are strange.

**16** We are counted by him as counterfeit,  
and he keeps away from our ways as from uncleanness;  
he calls the end of the righteous happy,  
and boasts that God is his father.

**17** Let us see if his words are true,  
and let us test what will happen at his end.

**18** For if the righteous man is God's son, He will help him,  
and rescue him from the hand of his adversaries.

**19** Let us test him with insult and torture,  
so that we may learn how gentle he is,  
and put his patience to the proof.

**20** Let us condemn him to a shameful death,  
for, by his own words, he will be watched over."

**21** So they reasoned, and they went astray,  
for their wickedness blinded them,

**22** and they did not know the mysteries of God,  
nor hope for the wages of holiness,  
nor discern the prize for blameless souls;

**23** for God created man for incorruption,  
and made him the image of His own nature,

**24** but through the envy of the devil death entered the world,  
and those who belong to his party experience it.

## Notes

- **vv1–9 "Short and sorrowful is our life"** — the ungodly speak like the Preacher read with God left out: *this is our portion* (Ecclesiastes 9:9), *eat your bread with joy* (9:7). The Preacher ends *know that for all these things God will bring you into judgment* (11:9); these men end in vv10–20.
- **v7 "flower of the air"** — *anthos aeros*: the bloom of the season, carried on the air, which they mean to pick before it passes.
- **v12 "he is a nuisance to us"** — *dyschrēstos*, the word of the Greek of Isaiah 3:10: *Let us bind the righteous man, for he is a nuisance to us*. The plot of vv12–20 grows from that verse.
- **v13 "a child of the Lord"** — *pais Kyriou*: *pais* is a child or a servant. It is the word of the Greek of Isaiah 52:13, *my servant (pais) shall understand*, and v16 ("God is his father") and v18 ("God's son") take it as "child."
- **vv17–18 "if the righteous man is God's son, He will help him, and rescue him"** — the mockers at the cross speak these words: *He trusts in God; let God rescue Him now, if He wants Him. For He said, "I am the Son of God"* (Matthew 27:43).
- **v23 "the image of His own nature"** — *eikona tēs idias idiotētos*: God's own selfhood, the image of Genesis 1:27 given to man for incorruption.
- **v24 "through the envy of the devil death entered the world"** — *diabolos*, "the slanderer," the word the Greek uses for the satan of Job 1:6. Paul's *through one man sin entered into the world, and death through sin* (Romans 5:12) uses the same "entered into the world."

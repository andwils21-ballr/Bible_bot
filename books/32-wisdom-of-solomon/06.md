# Wisdom of Solomon 6

**1** Listen, then, kings, and understand;  
learn, you judges of the ends of the earth.

**2** Give ear, you who rule over multitudes  
and pride yourselves on crowds of nations.

**3** For your dominion was given you by the Lord,  
and your sovereignty by the Most High,  
who will examine your works and search out your plans.

**4** Because, as servants of His kingdom, you did not judge rightly,  
nor keep the law,  
nor walk according to the purpose of God,

**5** He will come upon you terribly and swiftly,  
because severe judgment falls on those in high places.

**6** For the lowliest may be pardoned in mercy,  
but the mighty will be mightily tested.

**7** For the Master of all will not show partiality,  
nor stand in awe of greatness,  
because He Himself made both small and great,  
and He cares for all alike;

**8** but a strict inquiry is in store for the mighty.

**9** To you, then, monarchs, my words are directed,  
so that you may learn wisdom and not fall away.

**10** For those who keep holy things in holiness will be made holy,  
and those who have been taught them will find a defense.

**11** So set your desire on my words;  
long for them, and you will be instructed.

**12** Wisdom is radiant and unfading,  
and she is easily seen by those who love her.

**13** She is quick to make herself known to those who desire her.

**14** He who rises early for her will not grow weary,  
for he will find her sitting at his gates.

**15** To think about her is the perfection of understanding,  
and he who lies awake for her sake will soon be free from care,

**16** because she goes about seeking those worthy of her,  
and graciously shows herself to them on their paths,  
and meets them in every thought.

**17** For her truest beginning is the desire for discipline,

**18** and care for discipline is love,  
and love is the keeping of her laws,  
and attention to her laws is the assurance of incorruption,

**19** and incorruption brings one near to God;

**20** so the desire for wisdom leads to a kingdom.

**21** If, then, you delight in thrones and scepters, monarchs of the peoples,  
honor wisdom, so that you may reign forever.

**22** What wisdom is, and how she came to be, I will tell you,  
and I will not hide mysteries from you,  
but I will trace her out from the beginning of her coming to be,  
and make the knowledge of her plain,  
and I will not pass by the truth;

**23** nor will I travel in the company of wasting envy,  
for such a man will have no share in wisdom.

**24** A multitude of wise men is the salvation of the world,  
and a sensible king is the stability of his people.

**25** Therefore be instructed by my words, and you will profit.

## Notes

- **v1 "Listen, then, kings"** — the call of 1:1 to "you who judge the earth," taken up again before the book turns to wisdom herself.
- **v3 "your dominion was given you by the Lord"** — as Paul says, *there is no authority except from God* (Romans 13:1), and Jesus to Pilate, *you would have no authority over Me at all unless it had been given you from above* (John 19:11).
- **v6 "the mighty will be mightily tested"** — *dynatoi dynatōs*, the word doubled: the greater the power, the stricter the account (compare Luke 12:48).
- **v7 "will not show partiality"** — literally "will not shrink back before a face"; compare Deuteronomy 10:17, *who shows no partiality and takes no bribe*. "He Himself made both small and great" — as *the LORD is the Maker of them all* (Proverbs 22:2).
- **v9 "monarchs"** — *tyrannoi*, rulers who hold power alone; the word names the office, not cruelty, and stands beside "kings" and "judges" (vv1–2).
- **v10 "keep holy things in holiness will be made holy"** — *hosiōs ta hosia hosiōthēsontai*, one root three times.
- **v14 "he will find her sitting at his gates"** — Proverbs 8:34 blesses the one *watching daily at my gates*; here wisdom sits at the seeker's gate before he wakes.
- **vv17–20** — a chain in which each step takes up the last word of the one before: desire for discipline, care for discipline, love, keeping her laws, incorruption, nearness to God, and so to a kingdom.
- **v24 "the salvation of the world"** — *sōtēria*, the word of the Greek of Proverbs 11:14, *in an abundance of counselors there is victory*.

# Numbers 17

## Twelve Staffs

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and take from them a staff for each fathers' house, from all their chiefs by their fathers' houses, twelve staffs. Write each man's name on his staff.

**3** Write Aaron's name on the staff of Levi, for there shall be one staff for the head of each fathers' house.

**4** Lay them in the tent of meeting before the Testimony, where I meet with you.

**5** The staff of the man I choose will sprout, and so I will quiet before Me the grumblings of the sons of Israel, which they grumble against you."

**6** Moses spoke to the sons of Israel, and all their chiefs gave him a staff, one for each chief, by their fathers' houses, twelve staffs. Aaron's staff was among their staffs.

**7** Moses laid the staffs before the LORD in the tent of the Testimony.

## The Staff That Blossomed

**8** The next day Moses went into the tent of the Testimony, and here — the staff of Aaron for the house of Levi had sprouted. It had put out blossoms, bloomed with flowers, and ripened almonds.

**9** Moses brought out all the staffs from before the LORD to all the sons of Israel. They looked, and each man took his staff.

**10** The LORD said to Moses, "Put Aaron's staff back before the Testimony, to be kept as a sign for the rebels, so that you put an end to their grumblings against Me, and they do not die."

**11** Moses did so. As the LORD had commanded him, so he did.

**12** The sons of Israel said to Moses, "Look, we are dying! We are lost, all of us lost!

**13** Everyone who comes near, who comes near the dwelling of the LORD, dies. Are we all to die?"

## Notes

- **The whole chapter** — in the Hebrew, these thirteen verses are 17:16–28.
- **v2 "a staff for each fathers' house"** — *matteh*, **staff**, is also the word for a **tribe**. Twelve tribes bring twelve staffs, and the question of which tribe God has chosen is put to the staffs themselves.
- **v4 "where I meet with you"** — *iwwa'ed*, from *ya'ad*, the root of *mo'ed* in *the tent of meeting*.
- **v5 "I will quiet… the grumblings"** — *hashikkoti*, the verb of the flood's end: *the waters grew quiet* (Genesis 8:1). The grumbling has run from the scouts' report (14:2) to the day after Korah (16:41), and God puts the end of it in the terms of the flood going down.
- **v8 "put out blossoms, bloomed with flowers, and ripened almonds"** — bud, flower and ripe fruit at once, on a dead stick overnight. *Blossoms* and almonds are the pattern of the lampstand, whose cups are *shaped like almond blossoms* (Exodus 25:33). *Flower* is *tsits*, the word for the gold plate on Aaron's turban engraved *Holy to the LORD* (Exodus 28:36). The Greek reads *nuts* for *almonds*. The letter to the Hebrews lists *Aaron's staff that budded* among the things kept with the ark (Hebrews 9:4).
- **v10 "the rebels"** — *benei meri*, **sons of rebellion**. The Greek has *the sons of the disobedient*.
- **vv12–13 "we are dying… are we all to die?"** — the people draw the wrong conclusion from the right fact. Coming near the dwelling does kill (16:35, 16:49). The next chapter answers them: the priests and Levites stand in between, *so that there will no longer be wrath on the sons of Israel* (18:5).

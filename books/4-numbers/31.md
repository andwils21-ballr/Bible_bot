# Numbers 31

## The War against Midian

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Take vengeance for the sons of Israel on the Midianites. Afterward you will be gathered to your people."

**3** Moses spoke to the people, saying, "Arm men from among you for the army, so that they may go against Midian to carry out the LORD's vengeance on Midian.

**4** A thousand from each tribe, from all the tribes of Israel, you shall send to the army."

**5** So from the thousands of Israel a thousand were supplied from each tribe, twelve thousand armed for war.

**6** Moses sent them to the army, a thousand from each tribe, them and Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest, to the army, with the holy vessels and the trumpets for the blast in his hand.

**7** They waged war against Midian, as the LORD had commanded Moses, and killed every male.

**8** They killed the kings of Midian along with the rest of their slain: Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur and Reba, the five kings of Midian. Balaam son of Beor they also killed with the sword.

**9** The sons of Israel took the women of Midian and their little ones captive, and all their cattle, all their flocks and all their goods they took as plunder.

**10** All their towns where they lived, and all their encampments, they burned with fire.

**11** They took all the spoil and all the plunder, both people and animals,

**12** and brought the captives, the plunder and the spoil to Moses, to Eleazar the priest and to the congregation of the sons of Israel, at the camp on the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho.

## Moses and the Officers

**13** Moses, Eleazar the priest and all the chiefs of the congregation went out to meet them outside the camp.

**14** Moses was angry with the officers of the army, the commanders of thousands and the commanders of hundreds, who had come back from the campaign.

**15** Moses said to them, "Have you let all the women live?

**16** These are the very ones who, on Balaam's word, led the sons of Israel into unfaithfulness to the LORD in the matter of Peor, so that the plague came on the congregation of the LORD.

**17** Now kill every male among the little ones, and kill every woman who has known a man by lying with a male.

**18** But all the girls among the women who have not known a man by lying with a male, keep alive for yourselves.

## Purifying the Army

**19** As for you, camp outside the camp seven days. Whoever has killed a person and whoever has touched one of the slain, purify yourselves on the third day and on the seventh day, you and your captives.

**20** You shall purify every garment, every article of leather, all work of goats' hair and every article of wood."

**21** Eleazar the priest said to the men of the army who had gone to the war, "This is the statute of the instruction that the LORD has commanded Moses:

**22** only the gold, the silver, the bronze, the iron, the tin and the lead —

**23** everything that can stand the fire — you shall pass through the fire, and it will be clean. It must also be purified with the water for impurity. Whatever cannot stand the fire you shall pass through the water.

**24** You shall wash your clothes on the seventh day, and you will be clean. Afterward you may come into the camp."

## Dividing the Plunder

**25** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**26** "Take a count of the plunder that was captured, both people and animals, you and Eleazar the priest and the heads of the fathers' houses of the congregation.

**27** Divide the plunder in half between those who took part in the battle, who went out to the army, and the whole congregation.

**28** Levy a tribute for the LORD from the men of war who went out to the army: one life out of five hundred, from the people, the cattle, the donkeys and the flocks.

**29** Take it from their half and give it to Eleazar the priest as a contribution to the LORD.

**30** From the half belonging to the sons of Israel you shall take one out of every fifty, from the people, the cattle, the donkeys and the flocks, from all the animals, and give them to the Levites, who keep the charge of the dwelling of the LORD."

**31** Moses and Eleazar the priest did as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**32** The plunder, what was left of the spoil that the army had taken, was six hundred and seventy-five thousand sheep,

**33** seventy-two thousand cattle,

**34** sixty-one thousand donkeys,

**35** and people, women who had not known a man by lying with a male, thirty-two thousand persons in all.

**36** The half, the share of those who had gone out to the army, was: the number of sheep, three hundred and thirty-seven thousand five hundred,

**37** and the tribute for the LORD from the sheep was six hundred and seventy-five;

**38** the cattle, thirty-six thousand, and their tribute for the LORD, seventy-two;

**39** the donkeys, thirty thousand five hundred, and their tribute for the LORD, sixty-one;

**40** and the people, sixteen thousand, and their tribute for the LORD, thirty-two persons.

**41** Moses gave the tribute, the contribution to the LORD, to Eleazar the priest, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**42** From the half belonging to the sons of Israel, which Moses had separated from that of the men who fought —

**43** the congregation's half was three hundred and thirty-seven thousand five hundred sheep,

**44** thirty-six thousand cattle,

**45** thirty thousand five hundred donkeys,

**46** and sixteen thousand people —

**47** from the half belonging to the sons of Israel Moses took one out of every fifty, of people and of animals, and gave them to the Levites, who keep the charge of the dwelling of the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## The Officers' Offering

**48** The officers over the thousands of the army, the commanders of thousands and the commanders of hundreds, came to Moses

**49** and said to Moses, "Your servants have taken a count of the men of war under our command, and not one man of us is missing.

**50** So we have brought the LORD's offering, what each man found of gold ornaments, armlets and bracelets, signet rings, earrings and pendants, to make atonement for our lives before the LORD."

**51** Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from them, all of it worked ornaments.

**52** All the gold of the contribution that they offered to the LORD, from the commanders of thousands and the commanders of hundreds, was sixteen thousand seven hundred and fifty shekels.

**53** The men of the army had each taken plunder for himself.

**54** Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from the commanders of thousands and of hundreds and brought it into the tent of meeting as a remembrance for the sons of Israel before the LORD.

## Notes

- **v2 "Take vengeance… Afterward you will be gathered to your people"** — *neqom niqmat*, the verb with its own noun. The order answers 25:17–18, *Harass the Midianites and strike them, for they have harassed you with their tricks… in the matter of Peor*. It is also Moses' last campaign: the words *gathered to your people* are the ones used when the LORD showed him the land and told him he would die (27:13).
- **v3 "the LORD's vengeance"** — God says *the vengeance of the sons of Israel* (v2); Moses tells the people it is *the vengeance of the LORD*. The same war is named from both sides.
- **v6 "Phinehas… with the holy vessels and the trumpets"** — the priest who stopped the plague at Peor with a spear (25:7–8) goes out with the army, not the commander. The trumpets are the ones the Law gave for war: *you shall sound a blast on the trumpets, and you will be remembered before the LORD your God* (10:9).
- **v8 "Balaam son of Beor they also killed with the sword"** — the man who would not curse Israel (chapters 22–24) dies among the Midianites. He had told his donkey, *If only there were a sword in my hand, I would kill you now* (22:29).
- **v16 "on Balaam's word… in the matter of Peor"** — *bi-dvar Bil'am*. Chapter 25 tells the sin at Peor and never names Balaam in it; this verse is the first to say the counsel was his. The Hebrew opens with *hen hennah*, **they, these**: *These are the very ones*.
- **vv17–18 "kill every male among the little ones… keep alive for yourselves"** — the text states the order and does not soften it. The line it draws is the one of v16: the women who *led the sons of Israel into unfaithfulness* at Peor.
- **vv19–24 purifying the army** — the law of the dead body in chapter 19, applied to a whole army: *Whoever touches the dead body of any person shall be unclean seven days. He shall purify himself with the water on the third day and on the seventh day* (19:11–12), the *water for impurity*. Metal goes through fire and water; everything else through water.
- **vv27–47 the division** — half to the fighters and half to the congregation, then one in five hundred of the fighters' half to the priest and one in fifty of the people's half to the Levites. The figures agree exactly: half of 675,000 sheep is 337,500, and one five-hundredth of that is 675; the cattle, donkeys and persons work out the same way (72, 61, 32). David later makes the even division a rule: *as the share of the one who goes down to battle, so the share of the one who stays with the baggage* (1 Samuel 30:24).
- **v49 "not one man of us is missing"** — *ve-lo nifqad mimmennu ish*, the verb *paqad* of the census, **to count**: counted, and none came up short.
- **v50 "to make atonement for our lives"** — the words of the census ransom: *each shall give a ransom for his life to the LORD when they are numbered — so there will be no plague among them* (Exodus 30:12), and the silver *shall be a remembrance for the sons of Israel before the LORD* (30:16), as the gold is here (v54). The officers have just counted their men.

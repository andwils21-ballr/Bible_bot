# Numbers 6

## The Vow of the Nazirite

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them: When a man or a woman makes a special vow, the vow of a Nazirite, to keep himself apart for the LORD,

**3** he shall keep apart from wine and strong drink. He shall drink no vinegar made from wine or vinegar made from strong drink, and he shall drink no grape juice of any kind, and he shall eat no grapes, fresh or dried.

**4** All the days of his separation he shall eat nothing that is made from the grapevine, from the seeds to the skin.

**5** All the days of his vow of separation no razor shall pass over his head. Until the days are complete for which he keeps himself apart for the LORD, he shall be holy. He shall let the locks of the hair of his head grow long.

**6** All the days that he keeps himself apart for the LORD, he shall not go near a dead body.

**7** Not for his father or for his mother, for his brother or for his sister — he shall not make himself unclean for them when they die, because the crown of his God is on his head.

**8** All the days of his separation he is holy to the LORD.

**9** If someone dies beside him very suddenly, and he makes his consecrated head unclean, then he shall shave his head on the day of his cleansing. On the seventh day he shall shave it.

**10** On the eighth day he shall bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest, to the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**11** The priest shall offer one as a sin offering and one as a burnt offering, and make atonement for him, because he sinned by reason of the dead. He shall make his head holy that same day.

**12** He shall keep himself apart for the LORD for the days of his separation, and bring a male lamb a year old as a guilt offering. The earlier days shall fall away, because his separation was made unclean.

## When the Days Are Complete

**13** This is the instruction for the Nazirite: On the day the days of his separation are complete, he shall be brought to the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**14** He shall present his offering to the LORD: one unblemished male lamb a year old as a burnt offering, and one unblemished ewe lamb a year old as a sin offering, and one unblemished ram as a peace offering,

**15** and a basket of unleavened bread, loaves of fine flour mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers spread with oil, with their grain offering and their drink offerings.

**16** The priest shall present them before the LORD and offer his sin offering and his burnt offering.

**17** He shall offer the ram as a sacrifice of peace offering to the LORD, with the basket of unleavened bread. The priest shall offer its grain offering and its drink offering.

**18** The Nazirite shall shave his consecrated head at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and take the hair of his consecrated head and put it on the fire that is under the sacrifice of the peace offering.

**19** The priest shall take the boiled shoulder of the ram, and one unleavened loaf from the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and put them on the palms of the Nazirite after he has shaved off his consecrated hair.

**20** The priest shall wave them as a wave offering before the LORD. It is holy for the priest, together with the breast that is waved and the thigh that is lifted off. After that the Nazirite may drink wine.

**21** This is the instruction for the Nazirite who vows his offering to the LORD for his separation, besides whatever else he can afford. As his vow is that he vows, so he shall do, according to the instruction for his separation."

## The Blessing

**22** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**23** "Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying: This is how you shall bless the sons of Israel. Say to them:

**24** The LORD bless you and keep you.  
**25** The LORD make His face shine on you and be gracious to you.  
**26** The LORD lift up His face toward you and give you peace.

**27** So they shall put My name on the sons of Israel, and I will bless them."

## Notes

- **v2 "the vow of a Nazirite"** — *nazir*, from *nazar*, to keep apart, the verb of the priests who must *keep themselves apart* from the holy things when unclean (Leviticus 22:2). The vow is open to **a man or a woman**, and it is made by choice, for a set time (vv5, 13). The verb for making it is *yafli*, the special vow of Leviticus 27:2, from the root of *pele*, wonder.
- **v4 "from the seeds to the skin"** — *chartsannim* and *zag* occur here and nowhere else. The usual reading is seeds and skins, the two parts of the grape left after pressing. The Greek has them the other way round: *from the pressed skins to the seed*. Either way the line covers the whole fruit, inside and out.
- **v5 "no razor shall pass over his head"** — the words of the angel to Samson's mother: *no razor shall go up on his head, for the boy shall be a Nazirite of God from the womb* (Judges 13:5). Samson is the one Nazirite made so for life, before birth, by God's word, not his own vow. In the New Testament, John is announced with the other half of this law: *wine and strong drink he shall not drink* (Luke 1:15), where *strong drink* is *sikera*, the Greek form of this verse's *shekhar*.
- **v7 "the crown of his God is on his head"** — *nezer*, from the same root as *nazir*, and it is the word for the gold plate on the high priest's turban, *the holy crown* (Exodus 29:6). The Nazirite keeps the high priest's own rule: *he shall not make himself unclean even for his father or for his mother, because the crown of the anointing oil of his God is on him* (Leviticus 21:11–12). The high priest's crown is gold; the Nazirite's is his uncut hair.
- **v9 "very suddenly"** — *be-feta pit'om*, suddenly, suddenly: two words for the same thing. The vow can be broken by no act of his own at all, only by being near someone when he dies.
- **v12 "the earlier days shall fall away"** — *yippelu*, they fall. Days kept under the vow before the death do not count, and he begins again from the first day.
- **vv24–26 the blessing** — in Hebrew the three lines are three, five and seven words long, each line longer than the one before it. Every line opens with the name of the LORD. The *you* is singular throughout: the priests bless the whole people as if it were one person.
- **vv25–26 "make His face shine… lift up His face"** — *panav*, His face, twice. In Leviticus 26:17 God warned, *I will set My face against you*. The blessing asks for the reverse. *Lift up the face* toward someone is to look on them with favor. *Peace* is *shalom*, which is more than the absence of war: whole, complete, lacking nothing.
- **v27 "they shall put My name on the sons of Israel"** — the priests speak the words, and God says what they do: the name *YHWH*, spoken three times, is put on the people. And the last word is His: *and **I** will bless them*. The priests speak, and God is the one who blesses.

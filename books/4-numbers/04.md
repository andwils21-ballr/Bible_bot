# Numbers 4

## Kohath

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**2** "Take a count of the sons of Kohath from among the sons of Levi, by their clans, by their fathers' house,

**3** from thirty years old and upward to fifty years old, all who enter the service to do work in the tent of meeting.

**4** This is the work of the sons of Kohath in the tent of meeting: the most holy things.

**5** When the camp sets out, Aaron and his sons shall go in and take down the veil of the screen, and cover the ark of the Testimony with it.

**6** They shall put on it a covering of fine leather, and spread over it a cloth all of blue, and put in its poles.

**7** Over the table of the Presence they shall spread a cloth of blue, and put on it the dishes and the pans and the bowls and the jars for the drink offering. The continual bread shall be on it.

**8** They shall spread over them a cloth of crimson, and cover it with a covering of fine leather, and put in its poles.

**9** They shall take a cloth of blue and cover the lampstand for the light, and its lamps and its tongs and its trays, and all its vessels for oil, with which they serve it.

**10** They shall put it and all its vessels into a covering of fine leather, and put it on the carrying frame.

**11** Over the gold altar they shall spread a cloth of blue, and cover it with a covering of fine leather, and put in its poles.

**12** They shall take all the vessels of service with which they serve in the holy place, and put them in a cloth of blue, and cover them with a covering of fine leather, and put them on the carrying frame.

**13** They shall clear the ashes from the altar and spread a cloth of purple over it.

**14** They shall put on it all its vessels with which they serve at it: the fire pans, the forks, the shovels and the basins, all the vessels of the altar. They shall spread over it a covering of fine leather, and put in its poles.

**15** When Aaron and his sons have finished covering the holy place and all the vessels of the holy place, when the camp sets out, after that the sons of Kohath shall come to carry them. But they shall not touch the holy things, or they will die. These are the load of the sons of Kohath in the tent of meeting.

**16** The oversight of Eleazar son of Aaron the priest is the oil for the light and the fragrant incense and the regular grain offering and the anointing oil — the oversight of all the dwelling and all that is in it, the holy place and its vessels."

**17** The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**18** "Do not let the tribe of the clans of the Kohathites be cut off from among the Levites.

**19** But do this for them, so that they live and do not die when they approach the most holy things: Aaron and his sons shall go in and assign them, each man to his work and to his load.

**20** But they shall not go in to see the holy things even for an instant, or they will die."

## Gershon

**21** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**22** "Take a count of the sons of Gershon also, by their fathers' house, by their clans.

**23** From thirty years old and upward to fifty years old you shall count them, all who enter to serve in the service, to do the work in the tent of meeting.

**24** This is the work of the clans of the Gershonites, in serving and in carrying:

**25** they shall carry the curtains of the dwelling and the tent of meeting, its covering and the covering of fine leather that is on top of it, and the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting,

**26** and the hangings of the court, and the screen for the entrance of the gate of the court that is around the dwelling and the altar, and their cords, and all the equipment for their service. Whatever is to be done with them, they shall do.

**27** At the command of Aaron and his sons shall be all the work of the sons of the Gershonites, in all their load and in all their work. You shall appoint them to the charge of all their load.

**28** This is the work of the clans of the sons of the Gershonites in the tent of meeting, and their charge is under the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.

## Merari

**29** "The sons of Merari — by their clans, by their fathers' house you shall count them.

**30** From thirty years old and upward to fifty years old you shall count them, all who enter the service to do the work of the tent of meeting.

**31** This is the charge of their load, for all their work in the tent of meeting: the frames of the dwelling and its bars and its pillars and its sockets,

**32** and the pillars of the court all around, and their sockets and their pegs and their cords, with all their equipment and all their work. By name you shall assign the items of the charge of their load.

**33** This is the work of the clans of the sons of Merari, for all their work in the tent of meeting, under the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest."

## The Count

**34** Moses and Aaron and the chiefs of the congregation counted the sons of the Kohathites by their clans and by their fathers' house,

**35** from thirty years old and upward to fifty years old, all who enter the service for work in the tent of meeting.

**36** Those of them counted by their clans were two thousand seven hundred and fifty.

**37** These are those counted of the clans of the Kohathites, all who work in the tent of meeting, whom Moses and Aaron counted at the word of the LORD by the hand of Moses.

**38** Those counted of the sons of Gershon, by their clans and by their fathers' house,

**39** from thirty years old and upward to fifty years old, all who enter the service for work in the tent of meeting —

**40** those of them counted, by their clans, by their fathers' house, were two thousand six hundred and thirty.

**41** These are those counted of the clans of the sons of Gershon, all who work in the tent of meeting, whom Moses and Aaron counted at the word of the LORD.

**42** Those counted of the clans of the sons of Merari, by their clans, by their fathers' house,

**43** from thirty years old and upward to fifty years old, all who enter the service for work in the tent of meeting —

**44** those of them counted by their clans were three thousand two hundred.

**45** These are those counted of the clans of the sons of Merari, whom Moses and Aaron counted at the word of the LORD by the hand of Moses.

**46** All those counted of the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron and the chiefs of Israel counted, by their clans and by their fathers' house,

**47** from thirty years old and upward to fifty years old, all who enter to do the work of serving and the work of carrying in the tent of meeting —

**48** those of them counted were eight thousand five hundred and eighty.

**49** At the word of the LORD he counted them by the hand of Moses, each man to his work and to his load. They were counted by him, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Notes

- **v3 "all who enter the service"** — *kol ba la-tsava*, and *tsava* is the word for an **army**, the word of the war count in 1:3. Verse 23 doubles it: *litsbo tsava*, to serve in the service, to muster for duty. The work of carrying the dwelling is counted as a muster, and the women who *served in ranks* at the entrance of the tent (Exodus 38:8) are described with the same verb.
- **v3 "from thirty years old"** — the Greek reads *twenty-five* here and in vv23, 30, 35, 39, 43 and 47. The Hebrew says thirty in all seven places. In 8:24 the Hebrew itself gives twenty-five as the age a Levite begins. The two ages stand in the Hebrew text as written, and the chapters do not explain the difference.
- **v6 "a covering of fine leather… a cloth all of blue"** — on every other item the fine leather goes on the outside. On the ark the order is reversed: the leather goes under, and the blue cloth goes on top, so on the march the ark is the one object seen in blue. The Greek reads *tachash*, the uncertain word behind *fine leather*, as a color too: its *hyacinth-blue skin*.
- **v14 the altar's load** — the Greek adds a sentence at the end of this verse: *and they shall take a purple cloth and cover the basin and its stand*, and put them in a leather covering. The Hebrew has no instructions for the bronze basin anywhere in the chapter.
- **v15 "they shall not touch the holy things, or they will die"** — the Kohathites carry the most holy objects and may never see or touch them. The priests wrap everything first, and only then do the carriers come in. When David moves the ark on a cart, Uzzah puts out his hand to steady it and dies (2 Samuel 6:6–7).
- **v18 "do not let the tribe… be cut off"** — *al takhritu*, the verb of the penalty *cut off*. The command is addressed to Moses and Aaron. If the wrapping is done carelessly, it is the priests who will have cut the Kohathites off.
- **v20 "even for an instant"** — *ke-valla*, literally **as a swallowing**, the length of a gulp. It occurs nowhere else. The Greek has *suddenly*.
- **v48 "eight thousand five hundred and eighty"** — 2,750 + 2,630 + 3,200. The three clans add up exactly.

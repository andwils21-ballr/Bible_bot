# Numbers 9

## The Passover at Sinai

**1** And the LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the first month of the second year after they came out of the land of Egypt, saying,

**2** "Let the sons of Israel keep the Passover at its appointed time.

**3** On the fourteenth day of this month, between the evenings, you shall keep it at its appointed time. You shall keep it according to all its statutes and all its rulings."

**4** So Moses told the sons of Israel to keep the Passover.

**5** And they kept the Passover in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, between the evenings, in the wilderness of Sinai. Just as the LORD had commanded Moses, so the sons of Israel did.

## Men Who Could Not Keep It

**6** But there were men who were unclean because of a human corpse, and they could not keep the Passover on that day. So they came before Moses and Aaron that same day,

**7** and those men said to him, "We are unclean because of a human corpse. Why should we be kept back from presenting the LORD's offering at its appointed time among the sons of Israel?"

**8** And Moses said to them, "Wait, and I will hear what the LORD commands concerning you."

**9** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**10** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying: If any one of you or of your generations is unclean because of a corpse, or is away on a long journey, he shall still keep the Passover to the LORD.

**11** In the second month, on the fourteenth day, between the evenings, they shall keep it. They shall eat it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs.

**12** They shall leave none of it until morning, and they shall not break a bone of it. They shall keep it according to every statute of the Passover.

**13** But the man who is clean and is not on a journey, and fails to keep the Passover — that person shall be cut off from his people, because he did not present the LORD's offering at its appointed time. That man shall carry his sin.

**14** And if a guest lives among you and would keep the Passover to the LORD, he shall keep it according to the statute of the Passover and its ruling. You shall have one statute, for the guest and for the native of the land."

## The Cloud over the Dwelling

**15** And on the day the dwelling was set up, the cloud covered the dwelling, the tent of the Testimony; and from evening until morning it was over it like the appearance of fire.

**16** So it was continually: the cloud covered it, and at night the appearance of fire.

**17** And whenever the cloud lifted from over the tent, the sons of Israel would set out after it; and in the place where the cloud settled, there the sons of Israel would camp.

**18** At the command of the LORD the sons of Israel set out, and at the command of the LORD they camped. As long as the cloud rested on the dwelling, they stayed in camp.

**19** And when the cloud lingered over the dwelling many days, the sons of Israel kept the LORD's charge and did not set out.

**20** Sometimes the cloud was over the dwelling only a few days; at the command of the LORD they camped, and at the command of the LORD they set out.

**21** And sometimes the cloud stayed from evening until morning; and when the cloud lifted in the morning, they set out. Or it stayed a day and a night, and when the cloud lifted, they set out.

**22** Whether it was two days, or a month, or longer, while the cloud lingered over the dwelling and rested on it, the sons of Israel stayed in camp and did not set out; but when it lifted, they set out.

**23** At the command of the LORD they camped, and at the command of the LORD they set out. They kept the LORD's charge at the command of the LORD, through Moses.

## Notes

- **v1 "in the first month of the second year"** — a month before the count that opens the book (1:1, *the second month*). Like 7:1, this chapter reaches back in time. It is the first Passover kept since the night in Egypt, and the only one the five books record in the wilderness.
- **vv6–7 "Why should we be kept back?"** — *lamah nigara*, why should we be made less, cut short. The men are not asking to be excused from the Passover; they are asking not to miss it. Nothing in the law so far covered them, and Moses does not answer from what he knows. He says *wait, and I will hear* (v8).
- **v10 "a long journey"** — in the Hebrew text the last letter of *rechoqah*, **long** (literally *far*), has a dot written over it. It is one of ten places in the five books where the scribes marked a word or letter this way (3:39 is another). The dot flags the letter as questioned. The text does not say why.
- **vv10–11 the second Passover** — a month later, on the fourteenth of the second month, for anyone who was unclean or far away on the first date. It is the only festival in the Torah that is given a second date.
- **v12 "they shall not break a bone of it"** — the rule of the first Passover (Exodus 12:46), repeated for the second. John quotes it at the cross, when the soldiers do not break Jesus' legs: *these things happened so that the Scripture would be fulfilled: not a bone of him shall be broken* (John 19:36).
- **v14 "one statute, for the guest and for the native"** — the guest who keeps the Passover keeps it by the same law. Exodus 12:48 had set the condition, that his males be circumcised first.
- **vv18–23 "at the command of the LORD"** — *al pi YHWH*, literally **at the mouth of the LORD**, seven times in six verses (twice in v18, twice in v20, three times in v23). Whether the cloud stayed one night or a year (v22, *yamim*, "days", which can mean a year, as in Leviticus 25:29), the camp moved when it moved and not before.
- **v18 "as long as the cloud rested on the dwelling"** — the verb is *shakhan*, to settle, to dwell, the root of *mishkan*, the dwelling itself. The cloud *dwells on the dwelling*.

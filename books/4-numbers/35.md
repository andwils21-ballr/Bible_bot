# Numbers 35

## Towns for the Levites

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses on the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho, saying,

**2** "Command the sons of Israel to give the Levites, from the inheritance of their holding, towns to live in. You shall also give the Levites pasture land around the towns.

**3** The towns shall be theirs to live in, and their pasture land shall be for their cattle, their goods and all their animals.

**4** The pasture land of the towns that you give the Levites shall reach from the wall of the town outward a thousand cubits all around.

**5** You shall measure outside the town two thousand cubits on the east side, two thousand cubits on the south side, two thousand cubits on the west side and two thousand cubits on the north side, with the town in the middle. This shall be the pasture land of their towns.

**6** The towns that you give the Levites shall be the six towns of refuge, which you shall give for the one who kills a person to flee to, and besides them you shall give forty-two towns.

**7** All the towns that you give the Levites shall be forty-eight towns, together with their pasture land.

**8** As for the towns that you give from the holding of the sons of Israel, from the larger tribe you shall take more, and from the smaller you shall take fewer. Each shall give some of its towns to the Levites in proportion to the inheritance it receives."

## Towns of Refuge

**9** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**10** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them: When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan,

**11** you shall choose towns to be towns of refuge for you, so that the one who kills a person by mistake may flee there.

**12** The towns shall be a refuge for you from the avenger, so that the one who has killed does not die before he has stood before the congregation for judgment.

**13** The towns that you give shall be six towns of refuge for you.

**14** Three towns you shall give beyond the Jordan, and three towns you shall give in the land of Canaan. They shall be towns of refuge.

**15** For the sons of Israel, and for the guest and the resident among them, these six towns shall be a refuge, so that anyone who kills a person by mistake may flee there.

## Murder and Manslaughter

**16** "But if he struck him with an iron object, so that he died, he is a murderer. The murderer shall be put to death.

**17** If he struck him with a stone in the hand that could kill, and he died, he is a murderer. The murderer shall be put to death.

**18** Or if he struck him with a wooden object in the hand that could kill, and he died, he is a murderer. The murderer shall be put to death.

**19** The avenger of blood shall put the murderer to death himself; when he meets him, he shall put him to death.

**20** If out of hatred he pushed him, or threw something at him while lying in wait, so that he died,

**21** or in enmity struck him with his hand, so that he died, the one who struck him shall be put to death. He is a murderer. The avenger of blood shall put the murderer to death when he meets him.

**22** But if he pushed him suddenly, without enmity, or threw any object at him without lying in wait,

**23** or with any stone that could kill, without seeing him, and let it fall on him so that he died, though he was not his enemy and was not seeking his harm,

**24** then the congregation shall judge between the one who struck him and the avenger of blood, according to these rules.

**25** The congregation shall rescue the one who killed from the hand of the avenger of blood, and the congregation shall return him to the town of refuge to which he fled. He shall live there until the death of the high priest who was anointed with the holy oil.

**26** But if the one who killed ever goes outside the border of the town of refuge to which he fled,

**27** and the avenger of blood finds him outside the border of his town of refuge, and the avenger of blood kills the one who killed, there is no bloodguilt on him.

**28** For he must stay in his town of refuge until the death of the high priest. After the death of the high priest, the one who killed may return to the land of his holding.

**29** These shall be a statute and a rule for you throughout your generations, in all the places where you live.

**30** Whoever kills a person, the murderer shall be put to death on the word of witnesses. But one witness shall not testify against a person so that he dies.

**31** You shall not take a ransom for the life of a murderer who is guilty and must die. He shall surely be put to death.

**32** Nor shall you take a ransom for one who has fled to his town of refuge, so that he may return to live in the land before the death of the priest.

**33** You shall not pollute the land where you are, for blood pollutes the land, and no atonement can be made for the land for the blood that is shed on it, except by the blood of the one who shed it.

**34** You shall not make the land unclean where you live, in the middle of which I dwell, for I, the LORD, dwell among the sons of Israel."

## Notes

- **v2 "pasture land"** — *migrash*, from *garash*, **to drive out**: the open ground where the animals were driven out to graze, the word of Leviticus 25:34, *the pasture land around their cities shall not be sold*.
- **vv4–5 a thousand cubits… two thousand cubits** — the two figures do not measure the same thing, and the text does not say how they fit together. The thousand is measured *from the wall of the town outward*; the two thousand is a side measured *outside the town* on each of the four sides, *with the town in the middle*. Read together, the two thousand may be each side of the whole square around the town, but the verses give both numbers without saying how they fit.
- **v6 "towns of refuge"** — *arei ha-miqlat*. The Law promised them at Sinai: *if he did not lie in wait, and God let it meet his hand, then I will appoint for you a place where he may flee* (Exodus 21:13).
- **v11 "the one who kills a person by mistake"** — *rotseach*, the verb of the commandment *You shall not murder* (*lo tirtsach*), here used even of someone who kills without intent. *Bi-shgagah*, **by mistake**, is the word of the unintended sins of Leviticus 4.
- **vv12, 19 "the avenger of blood"** — *go'el ha-dam*, literally **the redeemer of the blood**, the same *go'el* who buys back a poor brother's land (Leviticus 25:25). The nearest kinsman restores what the family lost; here what was lost is a life.
- **v15 "the guest and the resident"** — *la-ger ve-la-toshav*, the two words of Leviticus 25:35. The refuge is not for Israel alone.
- **vv16–23 iron, stone, wood** — the law does not ask what the killer intended; it asks what he used and how. An iron object, a stone or wooden tool *that could kill*, a blow *out of hatred* or *while lying in wait*: murder. A push *suddenly, without enmity*, a stone dropped *without seeing him*: manslaughter.
- **v25 "until the death of the high priest"** — the only release is a death: the anointed priest's. Nothing in the chapter explains why, beyond v32, *no ransom*; the one who killed goes home when the priest dies.
- **v30 "one witness shall not testify against a person so that he dies"** — the rule Deuteronomy will state for every charge (19:15), stated first here for a life.
- **v33 "no atonement can be made for the land… except by the blood of the one who shed it"** — *chanaf*, **to pollute**: blood left unanswered defiles the ground itself. It goes back to the first murder, *The voice of your brother's bloods is crying out to Me from the ground* (Genesis 4:10), and to the rule after the flood: *One who sheds the blood of the human, by the human shall his blood be shed* (Genesis 9:6).

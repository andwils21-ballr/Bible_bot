# Numbers 26

## The Count on the Plains of Moab

**1** And it came to pass after the plague that the LORD said to Moses and to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest,

**2** "Take a count of the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, from twenty years old and up, by their fathers' houses, all in Israel who can go out to war."

**3** So Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke with them in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho, saying,

**4** "From twenty years old and up," as the LORD had commanded Moses. The sons of Israel who came out of the land of Egypt were these:

**5** Reuben, the firstborn of Israel. The sons of Reuben: of Hanoch, the clan of the Hanochites; of Pallu, the clan of the Palluites;

**6** of Hezron, the clan of the Hezronites; of Carmi, the clan of the Carmites.

**7** These are the clans of the Reubenites, and those counted of them were forty-three thousand seven hundred and thirty.

**8** The son of Pallu: Eliab.

**9** The sons of Eliab: Nemuel and Dathan and Abiram. These are the Dathan and Abiram, called to the congregation, who fought against Moses and Aaron in the company of Korah when they fought against the LORD,

**10** and the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them up together with Korah, when that company died, when the fire consumed two hundred and fifty men, and they became a warning sign.

**11** But the sons of Korah did not die.

**12** The sons of Simeon by their clans: of Nemuel, the clan of the Nemuelites; of Jamin, the clan of the Jaminites; of Jachin, the clan of the Jachinites;

**13** of Zerah, the clan of the Zerahites; of Shaul, the clan of the Shaulites.

**14** These are the clans of the Simeonites, twenty-two thousand two hundred.

**15** The sons of Gad by their clans: of Zephon, the clan of the Zephonites; of Haggi, the clan of the Haggites; of Shuni, the clan of the Shunites;

**16** of Ozni, the clan of the Oznites; of Eri, the clan of the Erites;

**17** of Arod, the clan of the Arodites; of Areli, the clan of the Arelites.

**18** These are the clans of the sons of Gad as they were counted, forty thousand five hundred.

**19** The sons of Judah were Er and Onan, and Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan.

**20** The sons of Judah by their clans were: of Shelah, the clan of the Shelanites; of Perez, the clan of the Perezites; of Zerah, the clan of the Zerahites.

**21** The sons of Perez were: of Hezron, the clan of the Hezronites; of Hamul, the clan of the Hamulites.

**22** These are the clans of Judah as they were counted, seventy-six thousand five hundred.

**23** The sons of Issachar by their clans: of Tola, the clan of the Tolaites; of Puvah, the clan of the Punites;

**24** of Jashub, the clan of the Jashubites; of Shimron, the clan of the Shimronites.

**25** These are the clans of Issachar as they were counted, sixty-four thousand three hundred.

**26** The sons of Zebulun by their clans: of Sered, the clan of the Seredites; of Elon, the clan of the Elonites; of Jahleel, the clan of the Jahleelites.

**27** These are the clans of the Zebulunites as they were counted, sixty thousand five hundred.

**28** The sons of Joseph by their clans: Manasseh and Ephraim.

**29** The sons of Manasseh: of Machir, the clan of the Machirites. Machir fathered Gilead; of Gilead, the clan of the Gileadites.

**30** These are the sons of Gilead: of Iezer, the clan of the Iezerites; of Helek, the clan of the Helekites;

**31** of Asriel, the clan of the Asrielites; of Shechem, the clan of the Shechemites;

**32** of Shemida, the clan of the Shemidaites; of Hepher, the clan of the Hepherites.

**33** Zelophehad son of Hepher had no sons, only daughters, and the names of the daughters of Zelophehad were Mahlah and Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah.

**34** These are the clans of Manasseh, and those counted of them were fifty-two thousand seven hundred.

**35** These are the sons of Ephraim by their clans: of Shuthelah, the clan of the Shuthelahites; of Becher, the clan of the Becherites; of Tahan, the clan of the Tahanites.

**36** These are the sons of Shuthelah: of Eran, the clan of the Eranites.

**37** These are the clans of the sons of Ephraim as they were counted, thirty-two thousand five hundred. These are the sons of Joseph by their clans.

**38** The sons of Benjamin by their clans: of Bela, the clan of the Belaites; of Ashbel, the clan of the Ashbelites; of Ahiram, the clan of the Ahiramites;

**39** of Shephupham, the clan of the Shuphamites; of Hupham, the clan of the Huphamites.

**40** The sons of Bela were Ard and Naaman: of Ard, the clan of the Ardites; of Naaman, the clan of the Naamites.

**41** These are the sons of Benjamin by their clans, and those counted of them were forty-five thousand six hundred.

**42** These are the sons of Dan by their clans: of Shuham, the clan of the Shuhamites. These are the clans of Dan by their clans.

**43** All the clans of the Shuhamites, as they were counted, were sixty-four thousand four hundred.

**44** The sons of Asher by their clans: of Imnah, the clan of the Imnites; of Ishvi, the clan of the Ishvites; of Beriah, the clan of the Beriites.

**45** Of the sons of Beriah: of Heber, the clan of the Heberites; of Malchiel, the clan of the Malchielites.

**46** The name of Asher's daughter was Serah.

**47** These are the clans of the sons of Asher as they were counted, fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**48** The sons of Naphtali by their clans: of Jahzeel, the clan of the Jahzeelites; of Guni, the clan of the Gunites;

**49** of Jezer, the clan of the Jezerites; of Shillem, the clan of the Shillemites.

**50** These are the clans of Naphtali by their clans, and those counted of them were forty-five thousand four hundred.

**51** These are those counted of the sons of Israel: six hundred and one thousand seven hundred and thirty.

## Dividing the Land

**52** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**53** "Among these the land shall be divided as an inheritance, by the number of names.

**54** To the larger you shall give a larger inheritance, and to the smaller you shall give a smaller inheritance. Each shall be given its inheritance by the number of those counted in it.

**55** But the land shall be divided by lot. They shall inherit by the names of their fathers' tribes.

**56** Each inheritance shall be divided by lot, between the larger and the smaller."

## The Levites

**57** These are those counted of the Levites by their clans: of Gershon, the clan of the Gershonites; of Kohath, the clan of the Kohathites; of Merari, the clan of the Merarites.

**58** These are the clans of Levi: the clan of the Libnites, the clan of the Hebronites, the clan of the Mahlites, the clan of the Mushites, the clan of the Korahites. Kohath fathered Amram.

**59** The name of Amram's wife was Jochebed daughter of Levi, who was born to Levi in Egypt. She bore to Amram Aaron and Moses and Miriam their sister.

**60** To Aaron were born Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**61** But Nadab and Abihu died when they offered strange fire before the LORD.

**62** Those counted of them were twenty-three thousand, every male from a month old and up. They were not counted among the sons of Israel, because no inheritance was given to them among the sons of Israel.

## Not One Was Left

**63** These are those counted by Moses and Eleazar the priest, who counted the sons of Israel in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho.

**64** Among them there was not a man of those counted by Moses and Aaron the priest, who counted the sons of Israel in the wilderness of Sinai.

**65** For the LORD had said of them, "They shall surely die in the wilderness." Not a man of them was left, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.

## Notes

- **v1 "after the plague"** — in the Hebrew these words close chapter 25 (25:19), and the count begins at 26:1. The Greek, like the English, puts them together.
- **v4 "who came out of the land of Egypt"** — the sentence has no main verb in the Hebrew: *from twenty years old and up, as the LORD commanded Moses, and the sons of Israel who came out of the land of Egypt*. It joins the command to the list that follows, and it names this generation, born in the wilderness, by the exodus of their fathers.
- **v10 "they became a warning sign"** — *le-nes*, the word for the pole the bronze serpent was set on (21:8): something set up for everyone to see.
- **vv12–50 the clans and Genesis 46** — the clans are the sons of Jacob's sons, and most names match the list of those who went down to Egypt (Genesis 46:8–27). Some are spelled differently: *Nemuel* for *Jemuel*, *Zerah* for *Zohar*, *Zephon* for *Ziphion*, *Ozni* for *Ezbon*, *Jashub* for *Job*, *Shuham* for *Hushim*, *Shephupham* and *Hupham* for *Muppim* and *Huppim*. Some are missing: Ohad of Simeon, Ishvah of Asher. Ard and Naaman, sons of Benjamin in Genesis 46:21, are here sons of Bela, and Becher, another son of Benjamin there, is here a clan of Ephraim (v35).
- **v14 "twenty-two thousand two hundred"** — Simeon had 59,300 in the first count (1:23). No tribe loses more. Zimri, the man killed with Cozbi at Peor, was *chief of a fathers' house of the Simeonites* (25:14).
- **v33 "Zelophehad… had no sons, only daughters"** — five women, named one by one in a list of men's clans. The next chapter is their case.
- **v46 "Asher's daughter was Serah"** — the only daughter of Jacob's sons named in the clan lists, as in Genesis 46:17. She is listed, but no clan is counted from her.
- **v51 "six hundred and one thousand seven hundred and thirty"** — the twelve tribal totals add up exactly. The first count was 603,550 (1:46). A whole generation has died and been replaced, and the nation is 1,820 smaller. On *thousand*, *elef*, see the note on 1:16.
- **v59 "who was born to Levi in Egypt"** — the Hebrew is *asher yaldah otah le-Levi*, **whom she bore to Levi**, with no subject for *she*. The Greek supplies one: *who bore these to Levi in Egypt*.
- **vv64–65 "not a man of them was left"** — the sentence of 14:29–30, carried out: *In this wilderness your bodies shall fall… except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun*.

# Numbers 7

## Wagons for the Levites

**1** And it came to pass, on the day Moses finished setting up the dwelling and had anointed it and set it apart with all its vessels, and the altar with all its vessels,

**2** that the chiefs of Israel, the heads of their fathers' houses, presented offerings. They were the chiefs of the tribes, the men who stood over those who were counted.

**3** They brought their offering before the LORD: six covered wagons and twelve oxen, a wagon for every two chiefs and an ox for each one. And they presented them before the dwelling.

**4** And the LORD said to Moses,

**5** "Accept these from them, and let them be used for the work of the tent of meeting. Give them to the Levites, to each according to his work."

**6** So Moses took the wagons and the oxen and gave them to the Levites.

**7** He gave two wagons and four oxen to the sons of Gershon, as their work required,

**8** and four wagons and eight oxen to the sons of Merari, as their work required, under the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.

**9** But to the sons of Kohath he gave none, because theirs was the service of the holy things, which they carried on their shoulders.

## One Chief Each Day

**10** And the chiefs brought their offerings for the dedication of the altar on the day it was anointed, and presented them before the altar.

**11** And the LORD said to Moses, "Let them present their offerings for the dedication of the altar one chief each day."

## The First Day: Judah

**12** The one who presented his offering on the first day was Nahshon son of Amminadab, of the tribe of Judah.

**13** And his offering was one silver dish weighing a hundred and thirty shekels and one silver basin of seventy shekels, by the shekel of the sanctuary, both of them full of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering;

**14** one gold pan of ten shekels, full of incense;

**15** one young bull, one ram, one male lamb a year old, for a burnt offering;

**16** one male goat for a sin offering;

**17** and for the sacrifice of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five he-goats, five male lambs a year old. This was the offering of Nahshon son of Amminadab.

**84** This was the dedication offering for the altar from the chiefs of Israel, on the day it was anointed: twelve silver dishes, twelve silver basins, twelve gold pans,

**85** each silver dish weighing a hundred and thirty shekels and each basin seventy. All the silver of the vessels was two thousand four hundred shekels, by the shekel of the sanctuary.

**86** The twelve gold pans full of incense weighed ten shekels each, by the shekel of the sanctuary. All the gold of the pans was a hundred and twenty shekels.

**87** All the cattle for the burnt offering: twelve bulls, twelve rams, twelve male lambs a year old, with their grain offering; and twelve male goats for a sin offering.

**88** And all the cattle for the sacrifice of peace offerings: twenty-four bulls, sixty rams, sixty he-goats, sixty male lambs a year old. This was the dedication offering for the altar after it was anointed.

## The Voice from Above the Cover

**89** And when Moses went into the tent of meeting to speak with Him, he heard the voice speaking to him from above the cover that was on the ark of the Testimony, from between the two cherubim; and He spoke to him.

## Notes

- **v1 "on the day Moses finished setting up the dwelling"** — the chapter goes back in time. The dwelling was set up on the first day of the first month of the second year (Exodus 40:17); Numbers began a month later, in the second month (1:1). These gifts arrived in the days after the dwelling was raised, and the book tells of them here, after the camp has been counted and ordered.
- **vv3, 9 "covered wagons… on their shoulders"** — *agalah*, a wagon. The Kohathites got none, because the most holy things are carried by hand (4:15). When David first moved the ark, it was put *on a new cart*, the same word (2 Samuel 6:3), and Uzzah died. The second time, *the Levites carried the ark of God on their shoulders with the poles, as Moses had commanded* (1 Chronicles 15:15), and David said of the first attempt, *we did not seek Him in the way He ruled* (15:13).
- **v10 "the dedication of the altar"** — *chanukkat ha-mizbeach*. *Chanukkah* is the word that later named the festival of the rededicated temple. The Greek here has *enkainismos*, and the Gospel of John calls that festival by a word from the same root, *ta enkainia*: *it was the feast of Dedication at Jerusalem; it was winter* (John 10:22).
- **vv12–83 the same gift twelve times** — each chief brings exactly what the others bring, and the text writes it out in full every time, six verses a day for twelve days, rather than saying *and so did the rest*. No tribe's gift is summarized into another's. Only the first is introduced differently: Nahshon of Judah is not called *chief* (v12), and every other man is.
- **vv17, 88 "he-goats… male goat"** — two Hebrew words. The sin offering is a *se'ir izzim*, a male goat (v16); the peace offering includes *attudim*, he-goats, the word Jacob used of the flock's breeding males (Genesis 31:10).
- **vv84–88 the totals** — twelve dishes of 130 shekels and twelve basins of 70 come to 2,400 shekels of silver; twelve pans of 10 come to 120 of gold; and 2 oxen, 5 rams, 5 he-goats and 5 lambs a day for twelve days make 24, 60, 60 and 60. Every total in these verses adds up.
- **v89 "from above the cover… from between the two cherubim"** — the promise of Exodus 25:22, *I will meet with you there, and I will speak with you from above the cover, from between the two cherubim*, kept here for the first time in the words it was made in. The chapter of gifts ends with the voice.

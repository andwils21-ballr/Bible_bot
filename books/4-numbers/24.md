# Numbers 24

## The Third Saying

**1** When Balaam saw that it pleased the LORD to bless Israel, he did not go, as at the other times, to look for divinings, but set his face toward the wilderness.

**2** Balaam lifted up his eyes and saw Israel camping tribe by tribe, and the Spirit of God came on him.

**3** He took up his saying and said:  
"The word of Balaam son of Beor,  
the word of the man whose eye is opened,

**4** the word of one who hears the sayings of God,  
who sees the vision of the Almighty,  
falling down, with his eyes uncovered:

**5** How good are your tents, O Jacob,  
your dwellings, O Israel!

**6** Like valleys they stretch out,  
like gardens beside a river,  
like aloes that the LORD has planted,  
like cedars beside the waters.

**7** Water shall flow from his buckets,  
and his seed shall be in many waters.  
His king shall be higher than Agag,  
and his kingdom shall be lifted up.

**8** God brings him out of Egypt;  
he has the horns of the wild ox.  
He shall eat up the nations, his foes,  
and crush their bones,  
and strike them through with his arrows.

**9** He crouched, he lay down like a lion,  
and like a lioness — who will rouse him?  
Blessed are those who bless you,  
and cursed are those who curse you."

**10** Balak's anger burned against Balaam, and he struck his hands together. Balak said to Balaam, "I called you to put a curse on my enemies, and here — you have done nothing but bless them these three times!

**11** Now flee to your own place. I said I would honor you greatly, but here — the LORD has kept you back from honor."

**12** Balaam said to Balak, "Did I not tell your messengers whom you sent to me,

**13** 'If Balak were to give me his house full of silver and gold, I could not go beyond the command of the LORD, to do good or bad from my own heart. What the LORD speaks, that I will speak'?

**14** Now I am going to my people. Come, I will tell you what this people will do to your people in the end of days."

## The Fourth Saying

**15** He took up his saying and said:  
"The word of Balaam son of Beor,  
the word of the man whose eye is opened,

**16** the word of one who hears the sayings of God,  
and knows what the Most High knows,  
who sees the vision of the Almighty,  
falling down, with his eyes uncovered:

**17** I see him, but not now;  
I watch him, but not near.  
A star shall march out of Jacob,  
and a staff shall rise out of Israel.  
It shall crush the brow of Moab  
and tear down all the sons of Sheth.

**18** Edom shall be a possession,  
Seir, his enemies, shall be a possession,  
while Israel does valiantly.

**19** One from Jacob shall rule,  
and destroy what survives of the city."

## The Last Sayings

**20** He looked on Amalek, and took up his saying and said:  
"Amalek was first among the nations,  
but his end is destruction."

**21** He looked on the Kenite, and took up his saying and said:  
"Enduring is your dwelling place,  
and your nest is set in the rock.

**22** Yet Kain shall be burned.  
How long? Asshur will take you captive."

**23** He took up his saying and said:  
"Alas, who shall live when God does this?

**24** Ships shall come from the coast of Kittim,  
and they shall afflict Asshur and afflict Eber,  
and he too shall come to destruction."

**25** Then Balaam rose and went back to his place, and Balak also went his way.

## Notes

- **v1 "to look for divinings"** — *li-qrat nechashim*, **to meet divinings**, the *nachash* that 23:23 said was not in Jacob. The two earlier sayings began with Balaam going off to *meet* the LORD (23:3, 23:15). This time he goes nowhere. He looks at Israel, and *the Spirit of God came on him* (v2).
- **vv3, 15 "the word of Balaam"** — *ne'um*, the word that marks a prophet's utterance, almost always *the word of the LORD* (*ne'um YHWH*). Here Balaam uses it of himself.
- **vv3, 15 "whose eye is opened"** — *shetum ha-ayin*. The verb is found only here, and it has been read both ways: **opened** or **shut**. *Opened* fits v4, *with his eyes uncovered*, the words of 22:31, where the LORD *uncovered Balaam's eyes*. The Greek has *who sees truly*.
- **v4 "the Almighty"** — *Shaddai*, the name God gave Himself to Abraham as *God Almighty* (Genesis 17:1). This is its first appearance in Numbers, and it comes from a foreign seer.
- **v6 "like aloes"** — *ka-ahalim*, a fragrant tree, and a play on *ohalekha*, **your tents**, in v5. The Greek reads it as **tents**: *like tents that the Lord pitched*.
- **v7 "higher than Agag"** — Agag is the name of the king of Amalek whom Saul spared (1 Samuel 15:8). The Greek has *Gog*, and in place of the buckets, *a man shall come out of his seed and rule over many nations*.
- **v9 "He crouched, he lay down like a lion"** — Jacob's words over Judah in Genesis 49:9, almost exactly. *Blessed are those who bless you, and cursed are those who curse you* is Isaac's blessing of Jacob (Genesis 27:29). The seer hired to curse ends by quoting the fathers' blessings back over their sons.
- **v17 "a star… a staff"** — *kokhav* and *shevet*. *Shevet* is the ruler's staff of Genesis 49:10, *the staff shall not turn aside from Judah*, and the word for **tribe** in v2. *The brow of Moab… the sons of Sheth* is quoted in Jeremiah's judgment on Moab: *fire… consumed the brow of Moab, the crown of the sons of tumult* (Jeremiah 48:45). Jeremiah has *qodqod*, **crown**, for *qarqar*, **tear down**, and *sha'on*, **tumult**, for *Sheth*. The Greek has *a man shall rise out of Israel*.
- **v21 "your nest is set in the rock"** — *qinnekha*, **your nest**, sounds like *Qayin*, **Kain**, the Kenites' name (v22). Verse 22 is the most uncertain line in the chapter: *ki im yihyeh le-va'er Qayin* may be *yet Kain shall be burned* or *Kain shall be for grazing*. Asshur here may be the Assyrians, or the Asshurim, a tribe of Genesis 25:3.
- **v24 "ships… from Kittim"** — *tsim miyyad Kittim*. Kittim is a people of the western sea, a son of Javan in Genesis 10:4. Daniel 11:30 uses the same words for the ships that come against the king of the north.

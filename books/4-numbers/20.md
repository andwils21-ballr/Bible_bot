# Numbers 20

## Miriam Dies

**1** The sons of Israel, the whole congregation, came into the wilderness of Zin in the first month, and the people stayed in Kadesh. Miriam died there and was buried there.

## Water from the Rock

**2** There was no water for the congregation, and they gathered against Moses and against Aaron.

**3** The people quarreled with Moses and said, "If only we had died when our brothers died before the LORD!

**4** Why have you brought the assembly of the LORD into this wilderness, for us and our livestock to die here?

**5** Why did you bring us up out of Egypt, to bring us to this terrible place? It is no place for grain, or figs, or vines, or pomegranates, and there is no water to drink."

**6** Moses and Aaron went from the assembly to the entrance of the tent of meeting and fell on their faces, and the glory of the LORD appeared to them.

**7** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**8** "Take the staff, and gather the congregation, you and Aaron your brother, and speak to the rock before their eyes, and it will give its water. So you will bring water out of the rock for them and give the congregation and their livestock drink."

**9** So Moses took the staff from before the LORD, as He had commanded him.

**10** Moses and Aaron gathered the assembly in front of the rock, and he said to them, "Listen now, you rebels: must we bring water out of this rock for you?"

**11** Moses raised his hand and struck the rock with his staff twice, and much water came out, and the congregation and their livestock drank.

**12** The LORD said to Moses and to Aaron, "Because you did not trust Me, to show Me holy before the eyes of the sons of Israel, therefore you shall not bring this assembly into the land that I have given them."

**13** These are the waters of Meribah, where the sons of Israel quarreled with the LORD, and He showed Himself holy among them.

## Edom Refuses

**14** Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom: "Thus says your brother Israel: You know all the hardship that has come on us —

**15** how our fathers went down to Egypt, and we lived in Egypt many days, and the Egyptians treated us and our fathers badly.

**16** When we cried out to the LORD, He heard our voice and sent a messenger and brought us out of Egypt. Here we are in Kadesh, a town at the edge of your border.

**17** Please let us pass through your land. We will not pass through field or vineyard, or drink water from a well. We will go by the King's Highway. We will not turn aside to the right or to the left until we have passed through your border."

**18** Edom said to him, "You shall not pass through me, or I will come out against you with the sword."

**19** The sons of Israel said to him, "We will go up by the highway, and if we drink your water, I and my livestock, I will pay for it. It is nothing — let me only pass through on foot."

**20** He said, "You shall not pass through." Edom came out against them with a large army and a strong hand.

**21** So Edom refused to let Israel pass through his border, and Israel turned away from him.

## Aaron Dies

**22** They set out from Kadesh, and the sons of Israel, the whole congregation, came to Mount Hor.

**23** The LORD said to Moses and to Aaron at Mount Hor, on the border of the land of Edom,

**24** "Aaron shall be gathered to his people, for he shall not enter the land that I have given to the sons of Israel, because you rebelled against My command at the waters of Meribah.

**25** Take Aaron and Eleazar his son and bring them up Mount Hor.

**26** Strip Aaron of his garments and put them on Eleazar his son. Aaron shall be gathered to his people and die there."

**27** Moses did as the LORD commanded. They went up Mount Hor before the eyes of the whole congregation.

**28** Moses stripped Aaron of his garments and put them on Eleazar his son, and Aaron died there on the top of the mountain. Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain.

**29** When the whole congregation saw that Aaron had died, all the house of Israel wept for Aaron thirty days.

## Notes

- **v1 "the first month"** — the text gives no year. The next date in Numbers puts Aaron's death on Mount Hor in *the fortieth year* (33:38), so the story has passed over almost the whole forty years of 14:33 in silence, from the scouts to this month. Miriam's death gets seven words, and no mourning is recorded, as it is for Aaron (v29).
- **vv2–13 the rock at Meribah, a second time** — at Horeb, near the start of the journey, the LORD told Moses to **strike** the rock (Exodus 17:6), and that place was also named *Meribah*, for the people's quarreling (Exodus 17:7). Here he is told to take the staff and **speak** to the rock (v8). He strikes it, twice.
- **v10 "you rebels"** — *ha-morim*, from the root of *benei meri*, *the rebels* of 17:10, for whom Aaron's staff was kept as a sign. Moses takes the staff from that place (v9) and uses the word the LORD used. In v24 the same verb comes back on Moses and Aaron: *you rebelled against My command*.
- **v10 "must we bring water… for you?"** — *we*, not the LORD. The text names no single fault, and v12 names only one: *you did not trust Me, to show Me holy*. Psalm 106:32–33 says of this place that *it went badly for Moses because of them, for they rebelled against his spirit, and he spoke rashly with his lips*. The rebels' verb there is *himru*, the root of *morim*.
- **v13 "He showed Himself holy among them"** — *va-yiqqadesh*, from *qadosh*, holy, the root of the place name **Kadesh** (v1). At Holy-place the LORD showed Himself holy, even though His two servants had not shown Him so (v12).
- **v14 "your brother Israel"** — Edom is Esau, and Israel is Jacob (Genesis 36:1; 32:28). Moses' message opens on the brothers of Genesis, who parted in peace (Genesis 33).
- **v16 "sent a messenger"** — *mal'akh*, the same word as the *messengers* Moses sends to Edom in v14. It is also the word for the angel of God who went before Israel's camp at the sea (Exodus 14:19).
- **v20 "with a large army and a strong hand"** — *be-yad chazakah*, the phrase of the LORD's power against Pharaoh (Exodus 6:1). Edom turns it against Israel, and Israel turns away (v21).
- **vv26–28 Aaron's garments on Eleazar** — *the holy garments of Aaron shall belong to his sons after him, to be anointed in them* (Exodus 29:29). The office passes to Eleazar on the mountain before Aaron dies, and the high priest dies out of his vestments. The Greek says in v29 that Aaron *was released*, *apelythē*, where the Hebrew has *died*.

# Numbers 10

## Two Silver Trumpets

**1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Make yourself two trumpets of silver; you shall make them of hammered work. They shall be for you to summon the congregation and to set the camps moving.

**3** When both are blown, the whole congregation shall gather to you at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**4** If only one is blown, the chiefs, the heads of the thousands of Israel, shall gather to you.

**5** When you sound a blast, the camps on the east side shall set out.

**6** When you sound a second blast, the camps on the south side shall set out. A blast shall be sounded for their setting out.

**7** But when the assembly is to be gathered, you shall blow, but not sound a blast.

**8** And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow the trumpets. They shall be a lasting statute for you through your generations.

**9** And when you go to war in your land against the enemy who oppresses you, you shall sound a blast on the trumpets, and you will be remembered before the LORD your God and saved from your enemies.

**10** And on your days of gladness, at your appointed times and at the beginnings of your months, you shall blow the trumpets over your burnt offerings and over your sacrifices of peace offerings, and they shall be a remembrance for you before your God. I am the LORD your God."

## Setting Out

**11** And it came to pass in the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth of the month, that the cloud lifted from over the dwelling of the Testimony.

**12** And the sons of Israel set out on their journeys from the wilderness of Sinai, and the cloud settled in the wilderness of Paran.

**13** They set out for the first time at the command of the LORD through Moses.

**14** The banner of the camp of the sons of Judah set out first, by their divisions; over its army was Nahshon son of Amminadab.

**15** Over the army of the tribe of the sons of Issachar was Nethanel son of Zuar,

**16** and over the army of the tribe of the sons of Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon.

**17** Then the dwelling was taken down, and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari, who carried the dwelling, set out.

**18** Then the banner of the camp of Reuben set out, by their divisions; over its army was Elizur son of Shedeur.

**19** Over the army of the tribe of the sons of Simeon was Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai,

**20** and over the army of the tribe of the sons of Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel.

**21** Then the Kohathites set out, carrying the holy things; and the dwelling was set up before they arrived.

**22** Then the banner of the camp of the sons of Ephraim set out, by their divisions; over its army was Elishama son of Ammihud.

**23** Over the army of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh was Gamaliel son of Pedahzur,

**24** and over the army of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni.

**25** Then the banner of the camp of the sons of Dan set out, the rear guard of all the camps, by their divisions; over its army was Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.

**26** Over the army of the tribe of the sons of Asher was Pagiel son of Ocran,

**27** and over the army of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan.

**28** This was the order of march of the sons of Israel by their divisions when they set out.

## Hobab

**29** And Moses said to Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses' father-in-law, "We are setting out for the place of which the LORD said, 'I will give it to you.' Come with us, and we will do you good, for the LORD has promised good to Israel."

**30** But he said to him, "I will not go. I will go back to my own land and my own kindred."

**31** And he said, "Please do not leave us, because you know where we should camp in the wilderness, and you will be eyes for us.

**32** And if you go with us, then whatever good the LORD does for us, we will do the same for you."

## The Ark Goes Ahead

**33** So they set out from the mountain of the LORD a three days' journey, and the ark of the covenant of the LORD went before them those three days, to seek out a resting place for them.

**34** And the cloud of the LORD was over them by day when they set out from the camp.

**35** And whenever the ark set out, Moses would say,  
"Rise up, O LORD, and let Your enemies be scattered,  
and let those who hate You flee before You."

**36** And when it came to rest, he would say,  
"Return, O LORD,  
to the countless thousands of Israel."

## Notes

- **vv5–7 "sound a blast… blow, but not sound a blast"** — two verbs. *Taqa* is to blow the trumpet; *heri'a* is to sound the *teru'ah*, the blast or great shout of Leviticus 23:24 and 25:9. The assembly is called by the one, and the camp is set moving by the other.
- **v9 "you will be remembered before the LORD"** — *ve-nizkartem*, the verb *zakhar*. The trumpets are blown in war and at the feasts alike, and both times the stated purpose is that God remember (v10, *a remembrance before your God*).
- **v11 "in the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth"** — Israel reached Sinai in the third month after leaving Egypt (Exodus 19:1). They leave it now, nineteen days after the count of 1:1: almost a year at the mountain. Exodus 19 through Numbers 10 all happens in that one year.
- **v21 "carrying the holy things"** — the Hebrew says *nos'ei ha-miqdash*, **carrying the sanctuary**: the Kohathites carried the ark and the most holy things (4:4–15). The Gershonites and Merarites went ahead with the structure (v17), so that the dwelling was already standing when the holy things arrived.
- **v29 "Hobab son of Reuel… Moses' father-in-law"** — the Hebrew allows either man to be the father-in-law. Exodus calls the priest of Midian *Reuel* (2:18) and *Jethro* (3:1). Judges 4:11 calls *Hobab* the father-in-law of Moses. The verse can be read either way, and the text does not settle which man is meant.
- **v31 "you will be eyes for us"** — he knows the wilderness and can show them where to camp. The Greek reads differently: *and you will be an elder among us*.
- **v33 "to seek out a resting place"** — *la-tur*, to scout, the verb used for the twelve men sent to spy out Canaan (13:2). The ark goes ahead of the people as a scout.
- **vv35–36 "Rise up, O LORD, and let Your enemies be scattered"** — Psalm 68 opens with the same words, turned to the third person: *let God rise up, let His enemies be scattered, and let those who hate Him flee before Him* (68:1). *Countless thousands* is *rivevot alfei*, the ten thousands of thousands; on *thousands*, see the note on 1:16.

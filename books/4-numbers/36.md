# Numbers 36

## The Heads of Gilead's Clan

**1** The heads of the fathers' houses of the clan of the sons of Gilead son of Machir, son of Manasseh, of the clans of the sons of Joseph, came forward and spoke before Moses and before the chiefs, the heads of the fathers' houses of the sons of Israel.

**2** They said, "The LORD commanded my lord to give the land as an inheritance by lot to the sons of Israel, and my lord was commanded by the LORD to give the inheritance of Zelophehad our brother to his daughters.

**3** But if they become wives to any of the sons of the other tribes of the sons of Israel, their inheritance will be taken away from the inheritance of our fathers and added to the inheritance of the tribe into which they marry. So it will be taken away from the lot of our inheritance.

**4** When the jubilee of the sons of Israel comes, their inheritance will be added to the inheritance of the tribe into which they marry, and their inheritance will be taken away from the inheritance of the tribe of our fathers."

## The Ruling

**5** Moses commanded the sons of Israel at the LORD's word, saying, "The tribe of the sons of Joseph is right in what it says.

**6** This is what the LORD has commanded concerning the daughters of Zelophehad: Let them marry whom they think best, only they must marry within the clan of their father's tribe.

**7** No inheritance of the sons of Israel shall pass from tribe to tribe, for each of the sons of Israel shall hold on to the inheritance of the tribe of his fathers.

**8** Every daughter who inherits an inheritance in any tribe of the sons of Israel shall become the wife of one from the clan of her father's tribe, so that each of the sons of Israel may possess the inheritance of his fathers.

**9** No inheritance shall pass from one tribe to another tribe, for each of the tribes of the sons of Israel shall hold on to its own inheritance."

**10** As the LORD had commanded Moses, so the daughters of Zelophehad did.

**11** Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, became wives to the sons of their father's brothers.

**12** They became wives within the clans of the sons of Manasseh son of Joseph, and their inheritance remained in the tribe of their father's clan.

**13** These are the commandments and the rules that the LORD commanded the sons of Israel through Moses on the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 the second hearing** — chapter 27 gave the daughters of Zelophehad their father's holding so that his *name* would not be *taken away from his clan* (27:4). The same verb, *gara*, **to take away, to diminish**, now comes back from the other side: if the daughters marry outside the tribe, the land will be *taken away from the inheritance of our fathers* (v3). The first ruling protected a father's name; this one protects a tribe's land.
- **v4 "When the jubilee… comes"** — the jubilee returns sold land to its family: *each of you shall return to his holding and to his family* (Leviticus 25:10). An inheritance that passes by marriage has not been sold, and the heads of the clan fear the jubilee would only confirm it in the other tribe (v4).
- **v5 "is right in what it says"** — *ken… dovrim*, the words Moses used of the daughters in 27:7, *The daughters of Zelophehad are right in what they say*. Both sides are right, and the law is made from both.
- **v6 "Let them marry whom they think best"** — *la-tov be-eineihem*, **as is good in their eyes**. The choice stays theirs, within the clan.
- **vv7, 9 "shall hold on to"** — *yidbequ*, the verb of Genesis 2:24, *clings to his woman*: each tribe clings to its land.
- **v11 Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah and Noah** — the names in a new order. In 26:33 and 27:1 they are *Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah*; here Tirzah is second and Noah last. The text gives no reason.
- **v13 "These are the commandments and the rules"** — the closing line of Numbers, set against the closing line of Leviticus: *These are the commandments that the LORD commanded Moses for the sons of Israel on Mount Sinai* (Leviticus 27:34). Leviticus ends at Sinai; Numbers ends *on the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho*, across the river from the land.

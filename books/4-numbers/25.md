# Numbers 25

## Yoked to Baal of Peor

**1** While Israel was staying at Shittim, the people began to prostitute themselves with the daughters of Moab.

**2** The women invited the people to the sacrifices of their gods, and the people ate and bowed down to their gods.

**3** So Israel yoked itself to Baal of Peor, and the anger of the LORD burned against Israel.

**4** The LORD said to Moses, "Take all the chiefs of the people and hang them up before the LORD in the sun, so that the fierce anger of the LORD may turn away from Israel."

**5** Moses said to the judges of Israel, "Each of you kill those of his men who have yoked themselves to Baal of Peor."

## Phinehas

**6** And here — one of the sons of Israel came and brought a Midianite woman to his brothers, before the eyes of Moses and before the eyes of the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, while they were weeping at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**7** When Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw it, he rose from among the congregation and took a spear in his hand.

**8** He went after the man of Israel into the tent chamber and pierced both of them, the man of Israel and the woman, through her belly. So the plague was stopped from the sons of Israel.

**9** Those who died in the plague were twenty-four thousand.

**10** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**11** "Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, has turned back My wrath from the sons of Israel, because he was jealous with My jealousy among them, so that I did not consume the sons of Israel in My jealousy.

**12** Therefore say: Here — I give him My covenant of peace.

**13** It shall be for him and for his offspring after him a covenant of lasting priesthood, because he was jealous for his God and made atonement for the sons of Israel."

**14** The name of the man of Israel who was struck down with the Midianite woman was Zimri son of Salu, chief of a fathers' house of the Simeonites.

**15** The name of the Midianite woman who was struck down was Cozbi daughter of Zur, who was head of the clans of a fathers' house in Midian.

## Against Midian

**16** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**17** "Harass the Midianites and strike them,

**18** for they have harassed you with their tricks, the tricks they played on you in the matter of Peor, and in the matter of Cozbi, the daughter of a chief of Midian, their sister, who was struck down on the day of the plague over the matter of Peor."

## Notes

- **v1 "prostitute themselves"** — *liznot*, the word of 15:39 for going after one's own eyes. The women invite the men to their gods' sacrifices (v2), and the text does not separate the two. Balaam's part comes out later: *these women, by Balaam's word, caused the sons of Israel to break faith with the LORD in the matter of Peor* (31:16). The Revelation to John remembers him for it: *Balaam, who taught Balak to put a stumbling block before the sons of Israel, to eat food sacrificed to idols and to practice sexual immorality* (Revelation 2:14).
- **v3 "Israel yoked itself to Baal of Peor"** — *va-yitsamed*, from *tsemed*, **a yoke** of oxen: harnessed to him. *Baal* means **lord** or **owner**, and *Baal of Peor* is the god named for Peor, the mountain where Balak had taken Balaam (23:28). Psalm 106:28 adds that they *ate sacrifices of the dead*.
- **v4 "hang them up… in the sun"** — *hoqa*, a rare verb for a public execution: hanging, impaling or exposing the bodies. It is the verb used when the Gibeonites put seven of Saul's sons to death *before the LORD* (2 Samuel 21:6, 9). The Greek has *make an example of them*.
- **v8 "into the tent chamber… through her belly"** — *el ha-qubbah… el qovatah*, two words that sound alike. *Qubbah* is a vaulted tent, found only here. The Greek has *into the furnace* and *through her womb*.
- **v9 "twenty-four thousand"** — Paul, warning the Corinthians against sexual immorality, says *twenty-three thousand fell in one day* (1 Corinthians 10:8). The Hebrew and the Greek of Numbers both have twenty-four thousand.
- **v11 "he was jealous with My jealousy"** — *be-qan'o et qin'ati*, the word of the *jealous God* of Exodus 20:5, a husband's jealousy for his wife. Phinehas feels what God feels, and so God does not have to act on it.
- **v12 "My covenant of peace"** — *beriti shalom*. Malachi recalls it of Levi: *My covenant was with him, life and peace* (Malachi 2:5). The psalm says of Phinehas, *it was counted to him as righteousness from generation to generation* (Psalm 106:31), the words of Abraham's faith in Genesis 15:6.
- **v17 "Harass… for they have harassed you"** — *tsaror… tsorerim*, the same verb, given back. *Tricks* is *nekhel*, twice in one verse: they *tricked you with their tricks*.

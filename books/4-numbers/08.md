# Numbers 8

## The Seven Lamps

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Speak to Aaron and say to him: When you set up the lamps, the seven lamps shall give their light toward the front of the lampstand."

**3** Aaron did so. He set up its lamps to face the front of the lampstand, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**4** This is how the lampstand was made: hammered work of gold, hammered work from its base to its flowers. Just as the LORD had shown Moses, so he made the lampstand.

## The Levites Made Clean

**5** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**6** "Take the Levites from among the sons of Israel and cleanse them.

**7** This is what you shall do to them to cleanse them: sprinkle the water of purification on them, and have them shave their whole body and wash their clothes, and so cleanse themselves.

**8** Then they shall take a young bull and its grain offering, fine flour mixed with oil, and you shall take a second young bull for a sin offering.

**9** You shall bring the Levites before the tent of meeting and assemble the whole congregation of the sons of Israel.

**10** When you bring the Levites before the LORD, the sons of Israel shall lean their hands on the Levites.

**11** Aaron shall present the Levites before the LORD as a wave offering from the sons of Israel, so that they may do the service of the LORD.

**12** The Levites shall lean their hands on the heads of the bulls, and you shall offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering to the LORD, to make atonement for the Levites.

**13** You shall stand the Levites before Aaron and before his sons, and present them as a wave offering to the LORD.

**14** So you shall set the Levites apart from among the sons of Israel, and the Levites shall be Mine.

**15** After that the Levites shall go in to serve at the tent of meeting, once you have cleansed them and presented them as a wave offering.

## Given in Place of the Firstborn

**16** For they are wholly given to Me from among the sons of Israel. In place of all who open the womb, the firstborn of all the sons of Israel, I have taken them for Myself.

**17** For every firstborn among the sons of Israel is Mine, of man and of beast. On the day I struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I set them apart for Myself,

**18** and I have taken the Levites in place of every firstborn among the sons of Israel.

**19** I have given the Levites, wholly given, to Aaron and to his sons from among the sons of Israel, to do the service of the sons of Israel at the tent of meeting and to make atonement for the sons of Israel, so that there will be no plague among the sons of Israel when the sons of Israel come near the holy place."

**20** Moses and Aaron and the whole congregation of the sons of Israel did so to the Levites. As the LORD had commanded Moses concerning the Levites, so the sons of Israel did to them.

**21** The Levites purified themselves and washed their clothes, and Aaron presented them as a wave offering before the LORD and made atonement for them to cleanse them.

**22** After that the Levites went in to do their service at the tent of meeting before Aaron and before his sons. As the LORD had commanded Moses concerning the Levites, so they did to them.

## Twenty-Five to Fifty

**23** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**24** "This is what applies to the Levites: from twenty-five years old and upward a man shall come to serve in the service of the tent of meeting.

**25** From the age of fifty he shall leave the service of the work and serve no more.

**26** He may assist his brothers at the tent of meeting in keeping the charge, but he shall do no work. This is how you shall deal with the Levites in their duties."

## Notes

- **v2 "when you set up the lamps"** — *be-ha'alotekha*, literally **when you make them go up**: the verb *alah*, which also names the burnt offering, the *olah*, the offering that goes up (Leviticus 1:3). The flame is spoken of as something raised.
- **v4 "just as the LORD had shown Moses"** — Exodus 25:40 closes the instructions for the lampstand with *see, and make them by their pattern, which you are being shown on the mountain*. Here the finished lampstand is said to match what Moses was shown.
- **v7 "the water of purification"** — *mei chattat*, literally **water of sin**: the noun that names the sin and also the sin offering. It is the water that takes sin away, as the verb *chitte* means to purge sin out of something (Leviticus 6:26, 8:15).
- **v10 "the sons of Israel shall lean their hands on the Levites"** — *ve-samkhu*, the verb of the worshipper leaning his hand on the head of his animal (Leviticus 1:4). Here the people lean their hands on men, and in the next verse those men are presented *as a wave offering*. The Levites are given to God the way an offering is given, standing in for the firstborn of every family (vv16–18).
- **v11 "as a wave offering"** — *tenufah*, the word for the breast of the peace offering waved before the LORD and given to the priests (Leviticus 7:30, 34). It is used of the Levites four times in this chapter (vv11, 13, 15, 21), and they are *given* to Aaron (3:9, and v19 here): an offering that goes to the priests, as the breast does.
- **v19 "the sons of Israel," five times** — the phrase is repeated five times in one verse, where one mention and pronouns would have done. The verse is about who is served and protected, and it names them every time.
- **v24 "from twenty-five years old"** — 4:3 gave the age as **thirty**, in the Hebrew of all seven verses there. Here the Hebrew itself says twenty-five. The Greek has twenty-five in both places.
- **vv25–26 "he shall leave the service… he may assist his brothers"** — *yashuv*, he shall turn back from the service, and then *sheret et echav*, he shall serve alongside his brothers. The man over fifty is not dismissed. He stops the carrying (4:15, 24) and stays at the tent to keep the charge.

# Numbers 15

## Grain, Oil and Wine with Every Offering

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them: When you come into the land where you will live, which I am giving you,

**3** and you present an offering by fire to the LORD — a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to fulfill a special vow, or as a freewill offering, or at your appointed times — to make a soothing aroma for the LORD from the herd or from the flock,

**4** the one who brings his offering shall present to the LORD a grain offering of a tenth of fine flour mixed with a fourth of a hin of oil.

**5** You shall also prepare a fourth of a hin of wine for the drink offering, with the burnt offering or the sacrifice, for each lamb.

**6** For a ram you shall prepare a grain offering of two tenths of fine flour mixed with a third of a hin of oil,

**7** and for the drink offering you shall present a third of a hin of wine, a soothing aroma to the LORD.

**8** When you prepare a young bull as a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to fulfill a special vow, or as peace offerings to the LORD,

**9** you shall present with the young bull a grain offering of three tenths of fine flour mixed with half a hin of oil,

**10** and you shall present half a hin of wine for the drink offering, an offering by fire, a soothing aroma to the LORD.

**11** This is how it shall be done for each bull or each ram, or for each lamb or young goat.

**12** However many you prepare, so you shall do for each one, according to their number.

**13** Every native shall do these things in this way, in presenting an offering by fire, a soothing aroma to the LORD.

**14** If a guest lives with you, or anyone is among you throughout your generations, and he would present an offering by fire, a soothing aroma to the LORD, he shall do as you do.

**15** For the assembly, there shall be one statute for you and for the guest who lives with you, a lasting statute throughout your generations: as you are, so shall the guest be before the LORD.

**16** One instruction and one ruling shall be for you and for the guest who lives with you."

## The First of the Dough

**17** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**18** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them: When you come into the land where I am bringing you,

**19** and you eat of the bread of the land, you shall lift off a contribution to the LORD.

**20** From the first of your dough you shall lift off a loaf as a contribution; you shall lift it off like the contribution from the threshing floor.

**21** From the first of your dough you shall give a contribution to the LORD throughout your generations.

## Sins by Mistake

**22** If you go astray and do not keep all these commands that the LORD has spoken to Moses —

**23** all that the LORD has commanded you through Moses, from the day the LORD gave commands and onward throughout your generations —

**24** then, if it was done by mistake, without the congregation knowing, the whole congregation shall offer one young bull as a burnt offering, a soothing aroma to the LORD, with its grain offering and its drink offering according to the rule, and one male goat as a sin offering.

**25** The priest shall make atonement for the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, and they shall be forgiven, because it was a mistake, and they have brought their offering, an offering by fire to the LORD, and their sin offering before the LORD for their mistake.

**26** The whole congregation of the sons of Israel shall be forgiven, and the guest who lives among them, because the whole people was involved in the mistake.

**27** If one person sins by mistake, he shall present a female goat a year old as a sin offering.

**28** The priest shall make atonement before the LORD for the person who went astray, who sinned by mistake, to make atonement for him, and he shall be forgiven.

**29** For the native among the sons of Israel and for the guest who lives among them, you shall have one instruction for anyone who does something by mistake.

## In Open Defiance

**30** But the person who acts in open defiance, whether native or guest, is reviling the LORD, and that person shall be cut off from among his people.

**31** Because he has despised the word of the LORD and broken His command, that person shall surely be cut off. His guilt is on him."

**32** While the sons of Israel were in the wilderness, they found a man gathering wood on the sabbath day.

**33** Those who found him gathering wood brought him to Moses and Aaron and to the whole congregation.

**34** They put him in custody, because it had not been made clear what should be done to him.

**35** The LORD said to Moses, "The man shall surely be put to death. The whole congregation shall stone him outside the camp."

**36** So the whole congregation took him outside the camp and stoned him, and he died, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Tassels

**37** The LORD said to Moses,

**38** "Speak to the sons of Israel and tell them to make tassels on the corners of their garments throughout their generations, and to put a cord of blue on the tassel of each corner.

**39** It shall be a tassel for you. When you see it, you will remember all the commands of the LORD and do them, and you will not go about after your own heart and your own eyes, which you follow like a prostitute.

**40** So you will remember and do all My commands, and be holy to your God.

**41** I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God. I am the LORD your God."

## Notes

- **vv4–10 grain, oil and wine by the size of the animal** — for a lamb, a tenth of flour, a fourth of a hin of oil and a fourth of wine; for a ram, two tenths and a third and a third; for a bull, three tenths and a half and a half. Every animal offering in the land comes with bread and wine. The meal on the altar is meant to be a whole one.
- **vv14–16 "as you are, so shall the guest be before the LORD"** — *kakhem ka-ger*, like you, like the guest. The law repeats it three ways in three verses: one statute, one instruction, one ruling. The guest (*ger*) brings the same offering by the same measure.
- **v20 "a loaf"** — *challah*, the word for a ring-shaped loaf, used of the bread in the ordination basket (Leviticus 8:26). The first of every batch of dough is lifted off for the LORD, as the first of the grain is lifted off at the threshing floor.
- **v30 "in open defiance"** — *be-yad ramah*, **with a high hand**: the phrase of Exodus 14:8, where Israel marched out of Egypt in open defiance of Pharaoh. The same posture that carried them out of Egypt, turned against God, is the one sin this chapter says no offering covers. Every offering in vv22–29 is for a sin done *by mistake*.
- **vv32–36 the man gathering wood** — the second time in the Torah that a man is held *because it had not been made clear* what to do with him. The first is the man who cursed the Name in Leviticus 24:12. Both cases are put before the LORD, both end outside the camp, and both come just after a law about acting against God on purpose.
- **v38 "tassels… a cord of blue"** — *tsitsit*, and *tekhelet*, the blue of the dwelling's curtains and the high priest's robe (Exodus 28:31). The Greek word for the tassel is *kraspedon*, and it is what the woman with the flow of blood touched on Jesus' garment (Matthew 9:20). Jesus also criticizes those who *make their tassels long* to be seen (Matthew 23:5).
- **v39 "you will not go about after your own heart and your own eyes"** — *lo taturu*, the verb for the scouts, who *went about* the land (13:2) and brought back a report shaped by what their eyes feared. The tassel is given so that what the eye sees reminds it of the commands.

# Numbers 5

## Outside the Camp

**1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Command the sons of Israel to send out of the camp everyone with blight, and everyone with a discharge, and everyone unclean by a dead person.

**3** Male and female alike you shall send out; outside the camp you shall send them, so that they do not make their camps unclean, where I dwell among them."

**4** And the sons of Israel did so, and sent them outside the camp. As the LORD had spoken to Moses, so the sons of Israel did.

## Wrong Done to a Neighbor

**5** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**6** "Speak to the sons of Israel: When a man or a woman commits any of the sins people commit, breaking faith with the LORD, and that person is guilty,

**7** they shall confess the sin they have done. And he shall pay back what he is guilty of in full, and add a fifth to it, and give it to the one he wronged.

**8** But if the man has no redeemer to whom the payment can be made, the payment made to the LORD goes to the priest, besides the ram of atonement with which atonement is made for him.

**9** And every contribution of all the holy things of the sons of Israel that they bring to the priest shall be his.

**10** Each man's holy things shall be his own; whatever a man gives to the priest shall be the priest's."

## The Water of Bitterness

**11** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**12** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them: If any man's wife goes astray and breaks faith with him,

**13** and a man lies with her and it is hidden from her husband's eyes, and she keeps it secret and has made herself unclean, and there is no witness against her, and she was not caught —

**14** and a spirit of jealousy comes over him and he is jealous of his wife, and she has made herself unclean; or a spirit of jealousy comes over him and he is jealous of his wife, and she has not made herself unclean —

**15** then the man shall bring his wife to the priest, and bring her offering for her: a tenth of an ephah of barley flour. He shall pour no oil on it and put no frankincense on it, because it is a grain offering of jealousy, a grain offering of remembrance, bringing guilt to remembrance.

**16** And the priest shall bring her near and stand her before the LORD.

**17** And the priest shall take holy water in a clay vessel, and the priest shall take some of the dust that is on the floor of the dwelling and put it into the water.

**18** And the priest shall stand the woman before the LORD, and let the hair of the woman's head hang loose, and put on her palms the grain offering of remembrance, which is the grain offering of jealousy. And in the priest's hand shall be the water of bitterness that brings the curse.

**19** And the priest shall make her swear, and say to the woman, 'If no man has lain with you, and if you have not gone astray into uncleanness while under your husband, be free of this water of bitterness that brings the curse.

**20** But if you have gone astray while under your husband, and if you have made yourself unclean, and a man other than your husband has lain with you —'

**21** then the priest shall make the woman swear the oath of the curse, and the priest shall say to the woman, 'the LORD make you a curse and an oath among your people, when the LORD makes your thigh fall away and your belly swell.

**22** And this water that brings the curse shall go into your insides, to make the belly swell and the thigh fall away.' And the woman shall say, 'Amen, amen.'

**23** And the priest shall write these curses in a scroll and wash them off into the water of bitterness.

**24** And he shall make the woman drink the water of bitterness that brings the curse, and the water that brings the curse shall go into her and become bitter.

**25** And the priest shall take the grain offering of jealousy from the woman's hand, and wave the grain offering before the LORD, and bring it near to the altar.

**26** And the priest shall take a handful of the grain offering, its memorial portion, and turn it into smoke on the altar, and afterward make the woman drink the water.

**27** And when he has made her drink the water, then, if she has made herself unclean and has broken faith with her husband, the water that brings the curse shall go into her and become bitter, and her belly shall swell and her thigh shall fall away, and the woman shall become a curse among her people.

**28** But if the woman has not made herself unclean, and she is clean, then she shall be free and shall conceive children.

**29** This is the instruction for jealousy, when a wife goes astray while under her husband and makes herself unclean,

**30** or when a spirit of jealousy comes over a man and he is jealous of his wife. He shall stand the woman before the LORD, and the priest shall carry out for her all this instruction.

**31** And the man shall be free of guilt, but that woman shall carry her guilt."

## Notes

- **v3 "where I dwell among them"** — *shokhen*, the verb behind *mishkan*, the dwelling. The camp is clean not because the people are, but because God lives in the middle of it. The three kinds sent out are the three Leviticus laid down: blight (chapters 13–14), discharges (15), and contact with the dead (21:1–4, 22:4).
- **vv6–8 "breaking faith with the LORD"** — *li-m'ol ma'al*, the phrase of Leviticus 6:2, where wronging a neighbor is called breaking faith with God. This law adds what Leviticus left open: if the wronged man has died and has no *go'el*, no relative to receive the payment (Leviticus 25:25), the debt is not cancelled. It is paid to the LORD, through the priest.
- **v12 "goes astray"** — *tisteh*, from *satah*, to turn aside from the path. Proverbs uses it of the adulteress: *let your heart not **turn aside** to her ways* (7:25). And *ma'al*, **break faith**, is the word for betraying God in v6 and for betraying a husband here.
- **v14 "and she has not made herself unclean"** — the law is written for both cases, in the same words: the husband's jealousy, and a wife who is guilty, or a wife who is innocent. It does not assume she is guilty. It takes the matter out of the husband's hands and puts it before God.
- **v17 "holy water"** — *mayim qedoshim* occurs here and nowhere else in the Hebrew Bible. The dust is from the floor of the dwelling itself.
- **v22 "Amen, amen"** — the first time the word *amen* appears in the Bible. It is from *aman*, to be firm, the root of *he believed* in Genesis 15:6. Saying it, the woman accepts the oath and puts herself in God's hands.
- **v23 "write these curses in a scroll and wash them off"** — the curse contains the divine name (v21). The priest writes it and dissolves the ink into the water she drinks. The words themselves go into her.
- **vv21, 27–28 "your thigh fall away and your belly swell"** — no one can say exactly what physical condition this names. The outcome for the innocent woman supplies the sense: *she shall be free and shall conceive children* (v28, literally *be sown with seed*). The penalty for the guilty is to be unable to bear. No human punishment is prescribed, and none is carried out by the husband or the priest.

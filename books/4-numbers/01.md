# Numbers 1

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tent of meeting, on the first of the second month, in the second year after they came out of the land of Egypt, saying,

**2** "Take a count of the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, every male, head by head,

**3** from twenty years old and up, all in Israel who can go out to war. You and Aaron shall count them by their divisions.

**4** With you there shall be a man from each tribe, each one the head of his fathers' house.

**5** These are the names of the men who shall stand with you: from Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur;

**6** from Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;

**7** from Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab;

**8** from Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar;

**9** from Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon;

**10** from the sons of Joseph: from Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud; from Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur;

**11** from Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni;

**12** from Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;

**13** from Asher, Pagiel son of Ocran;

**14** from Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel;

**15** from Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan."

**16** These were the ones called from the congregation, the chiefs of their fathers' tribes. They were the heads of the thousands of Israel.

**17** Moses and Aaron took these men who had been designated by name,

**18** and they assembled the whole congregation on the first of the second month, and they registered their descent by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, from twenty years old and up, head by head,

**19** as the LORD had commanded Moses. He counted them in the wilderness of Sinai.

## The Count by Tribes

**20** The sons of Reuben, Israel's firstborn — their descendants, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, head by head, every male twenty years old and up, all who could go out to war:

**21** those of them counted, of the tribe of Reuben, were forty-six thousand five hundred.

**22** Of the sons of Simeon — their descendants, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, those of them counted, by the number of names, head by head, every male twenty years old and up, all who could go out to war:

**23** those of them counted, of the tribe of Simeon, were fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

**24** Of the sons of Gad — their descendants, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, twenty years old and up, all who could go out to war:

**25** those of them counted, of the tribe of Gad, were forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.

**26** Of the sons of Judah — their descendants, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, twenty years old and up, all who could go out to war:

**27** those of them counted, of the tribe of Judah, were seventy-four thousand six hundred.

**28** Of the sons of Issachar — their descendants, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, twenty years old and up, all who could go out to war:

**29** those of them counted, of the tribe of Issachar, were fifty-four thousand four hundred.

**30** Of the sons of Zebulun — their descendants, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, twenty years old and up, all who could go out to war:

**31** those of them counted, of the tribe of Zebulun, were fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

**32** Of the sons of Joseph: of the sons of Ephraim — their descendants, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, twenty years old and up, all who could go out to war:

**33** those of them counted, of the tribe of Ephraim, were forty thousand five hundred.

**34** Of the sons of Manasseh — their descendants, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, twenty years old and up, all who could go out to war:

**35** those of them counted, of the tribe of Manasseh, were thirty-two thousand two hundred.

**36** Of the sons of Benjamin — their descendants, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, twenty years old and up, all who could go out to war:

**37** those of them counted, of the tribe of Benjamin, were thirty-five thousand four hundred.

**38** Of the sons of Dan — their descendants, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, twenty years old and up, all who could go out to war:

**39** those of them counted, of the tribe of Dan, were sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

**40** Of the sons of Asher — their descendants, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, twenty years old and up, all who could go out to war:

**41** those of them counted, of the tribe of Asher, were forty-one thousand five hundred.

**42** Of the sons of Naphtali — their descendants, by their clans, by their fathers' houses, by the number of names, twenty years old and up, all who could go out to war:

**43** those of them counted, of the tribe of Naphtali, were fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**44** These are the ones counted, whom Moses and Aaron counted with the chiefs of Israel, twelve men, each one for his fathers' house.

**45** All those counted of the sons of Israel, by their fathers' houses, from twenty years old and up, all in Israel who could go out to war —

**46** all those counted were six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.

## The Levites

**47** But the Levites were not counted among them by their fathers' tribe.

**48** For the LORD had spoken to Moses, saying,

**49** "Only the tribe of Levi you shall not count, and you shall not take a count of them among the sons of Israel.

**50** But you — appoint the Levites over the dwelling of the Testimony, and over all its vessels, and over everything that belongs to it. They shall carry the dwelling and all its vessels, and they shall serve it, and they shall camp around the dwelling.

**51** When the dwelling sets out, the Levites shall take it down, and when the dwelling camps, the Levites shall set it up. The outsider who comes near shall be put to death.

**52** The sons of Israel shall camp each in his own camp and each by his own banner, by their divisions.

**53** But the Levites shall camp around the dwelling of the Testimony, so that there will be no wrath on the congregation of the sons of Israel. The Levites shall keep the charge of the dwelling of the Testimony."

**54** The sons of Israel did so. According to all that the LORD commanded Moses, so they did.

## Notes

- **v1 "in the wilderness"** — *be-midbar*, the Hebrew name of this book, taken from its first line. The English name *Numbers* comes through Latin from the Greek *Arithmoi*, named for the two counts of the people (chapters 1 and 26). The date is exact: one month after the dwelling was raised on the first of the first month (Exodus 40:17). Israel has been at Sinai nearly a year.
- **v2 "take a count"** — *se'u et rosh*, literally **lift the head**. It is the idiom of Joseph's two dreams in prison: Pharaoh will *lift your head* and restore you, the cupbearer is told, and the baker, *lift your head from off you* (Genesis 40:13, 19). *Head by head* is *le-gulgelotam*, by their skulls, the word behind *Golgotha*.
- **v3 "all who can go out to war"** — *kol yotse tsava*, everyone who goes out with the army. The count is a muster. The verb, *paqad*, is to count and also to attend to, to take charge of, and to visit. The count is God taking stock of what He brought out of Egypt.
- **v16 "the heads of the thousands of Israel"** — *elef* means a **thousand**, and it also means a **clan**, a family unit: *my clan is the weakest in Manasseh*, Gideon says (Judges 6:15), using this word. The census totals in this chapter are written with *elef*. Read as *thousands*, the fighting men number 603,550, the same figure as the half-shekel count in Exodus 38:26. Read as *clans* or units, the numbers would be far smaller. Both senses of the word are real; the totals the text itself adds up (v46) follow the *thousand* reading.
- **v17 "designated by name"** — *nikkevu*, from *naqav*, the verb used of the man who **blasphemed** the Name in Leviticus 24:11. The same verb that marks a name out for honor here marks it out for abuse there.
- **v18 "registered their descent"** — *va-yityaldu*, a form of *yalad*, to give birth, that occurs here and nowhere else: they *had themselves born*, each one stating whose son he was. The count of an army is also a roll of ancestry.
- **v20 "Reuben, Israel's firstborn"** — the list opens with the firstborn, as Genesis 49 did, and names Joseph's two sons as tribes (vv32–35), as Jacob did when he adopted them (Genesis 48:5). Levi is missing from the twelve; Ephraim and Manasseh keep the count at twelve.
- **vv47–53 the Levites are not counted** — the tribe that carries the dwelling is not mustered for war. It camps around the dwelling as a living wall, *so that there will be no wrath on the congregation*: the Levites stand between the people and the holy place.

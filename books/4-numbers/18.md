# Numbers 18

## Guarding the Holy Place

**1** The LORD said to Aaron, "You and your sons and your father's house with you shall carry the guilt of the sanctuary, and you and your sons with you shall carry the guilt of your priesthood.

**2** Bring near with you your brothers also, the tribe of Levi, your father's tribe, so that they may be joined to you and serve you, while you and your sons with you are before the tent of the Testimony.

**3** They shall keep your charge and the charge of the whole tent, but they shall not come near the vessels of the holy place or the altar, or both they and you will die.

**4** They shall be joined to you and keep the charge of the tent of meeting, for all the service of the tent. No outsider shall come near you.

**5** You shall keep the charge of the holy place and the charge of the altar, so that there will no longer be wrath on the sons of Israel.

**6** I Myself have taken your brothers the Levites from among the sons of Israel. They are a gift to you, given to the LORD, to do the service of the tent of meeting.

**7** But you and your sons with you shall keep your priesthood for everything at the altar and inside the veil, and you shall serve. I give you your priesthood as a service that is a gift. The outsider who comes near shall be put to death."

## The Priests' Share

**8** The LORD spoke to Aaron: "I Myself have given you charge of My contributions, all the holy things of the sons of Israel. I have given them to you as the share that comes with your anointing, and to your sons, as a lasting due.

**9** This shall be yours from the most holy things, kept back from the fire: every offering of theirs, every grain offering and every sin offering and every guilt offering that they return to Me. It is most holy for you and for your sons.

**10** You shall eat it in a most holy place. Every male may eat it. It shall be holy to you.

**11** This also is yours: the contribution of their gifts, all the wave offerings of the sons of Israel. I have given them to you, and to your sons and daughters with you, as a lasting due. Everyone in your house who is clean may eat it.

**12** All the best of the oil, and all the best of the new wine and the grain, the first of them that they give to the LORD, I have given to you.

**13** The firstfruits of all that is in their land, which they bring to the LORD, shall be yours. Everyone in your house who is clean may eat it.

**14** Everything devoted in Israel shall be yours.

**15** Every first to open the womb, of all flesh, whether human or animal, that they present to the LORD, shall be yours. But you shall redeem the firstborn of man, and you shall redeem the firstborn of unclean animals.

**16** You shall redeem them from a month old, at your valuation, for five shekels of silver by the shekel of the sanctuary, which is twenty gerahs.

**17** But the firstborn of a cow, or the firstborn of a sheep, or the firstborn of a goat, you shall not redeem. They are holy. You shall throw their blood against the altar and turn their fat into smoke as an offering by fire, a soothing aroma to the LORD.

**18** Their meat shall be yours, as the breast that is waved and the right thigh are yours.

**19** All the holy contributions that the sons of Israel lift off for the LORD I have given to you, and to your sons and daughters with you, as a lasting due. It is a covenant of salt forever before the LORD, for you and for your offspring with you."

**20** The LORD said to Aaron, "You shall have no inheritance in their land, and no share among them. I am your share and your inheritance among the sons of Israel.

## The Levites' Tenth

**21** To the sons of Levi I have given every tenth in Israel as an inheritance, in return for the service they do, the service of the tent of meeting.

**22** The sons of Israel shall no longer come near the tent of meeting, or they will carry sin and die.

**23** The Levites shall do the service of the tent of meeting, and they shall carry their guilt. It is a lasting statute throughout your generations. Among the sons of Israel they shall have no inheritance,

**24** for the tenth of the sons of Israel, which they lift off as a contribution to the LORD, I have given to the Levites as an inheritance. That is why I have said to them, 'Among the sons of Israel they shall have no inheritance.'"

**25** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**26** "Speak to the Levites and say to them: When you take from the sons of Israel the tenth that I have given you from them as your inheritance, you shall lift off from it a contribution to the LORD, a tenth of the tenth.

**27** Your contribution shall be counted to you as if it were grain from the threshing floor and the full yield of the winepress.

**28** So you also shall lift off a contribution to the LORD from all the tenths you take from the sons of Israel, and from it you shall give the LORD's contribution to Aaron the priest.

**29** From all your gifts you shall lift off every contribution due to the LORD, from all the best of them, the part of them that is holy.

**30** Say to them: When you have lifted off the best of it, the rest shall be counted to the Levites like the yield of the threshing floor and the yield of the winepress.

**31** You may eat it in any place, you and your households, for it is your wages in return for your service in the tent of meeting.

**32** You will not carry sin because of it, once you have lifted off the best of it. You shall not profane the holy things of the sons of Israel, or you will die."

## Notes

- **v2 "so that they may be joined to you"** — *ve-yillavu*, a play on the name **Levi**. When Leah named him, she said, *this time my husband will be joined to me* (Genesis 29:34). The tribe named for being joined is now joined to Aaron. The verb comes back in v4.
- **v5 "no longer be wrath on the sons of Israel"** — the answer to the people's cry at the end of chapter 17, *are we all to die?* The priests and Levites stand between the people and the holy things and carry the danger (v1, v23) so that the people do not.
- **v7 "a service that is a gift"** — *avodat mattanah*. The priesthood is not something Aaron's house took, as Korah tried to (16:10). It is given.
- **v12 "the best of the oil… of the new wine"** — *chelev*, literally the **fat**: the part of an animal that always belongs on the altar (Leviticus 3:16), used here of the finest of the harvest, as in *the fat of the land* (Genesis 45:18). In vv29–32 the Levites must lift off *its fat*, the best of their tenth, for the LORD.
- **v19 "a covenant of salt forever"** — *berit melach*. Leviticus 2:13 required *the salt of the covenant* on every offering without saying what it meant, and this verse does not explain it either. The phrase appears once more, for the kingdom given to David and his sons *by a covenant of salt* (2 Chronicles 13:5). Both times it is attached to something given forever.
- **v20 "I am your share and your inheritance"** — Aaron's house receives no land when Canaan is divided. The LORD Himself is their portion. The psalms take up the words for anyone: *The LORD is my share and my cup* (Psalm 16:5).
- **v21 "every tenth in Israel"** — *kol ma'aser*, the tenth of Leviticus 27:30. There it is declared holy to the LORD. Here it is given to the Levites as their wages (v31), and the Levites in turn give a tenth of their tenth to the priests (v26).
- **v27 "the full yield of the winepress"** — *mele'ah*, **the fullness**, the word of Exodus 22:29, *your full harvest*.

# Numbers 14

## Let Us Go Back to Egypt

**1** The whole congregation raised their voices and cried out, and the people wept that night.

**2** All the sons of Israel grumbled against Moses and against Aaron, and the whole congregation said to them, "If only we had died in the land of Egypt, or in this wilderness, if only we had died!

**3** Why is the LORD bringing us into this land to fall by the sword? Our wives and our little ones will become plunder. Would it not be better for us to go back to Egypt?"

**4** They said to one another, "Let us appoint a leader and go back to Egypt."

**5** Then Moses and Aaron fell on their faces before the whole assembly of the congregation of the sons of Israel.

**6** Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, who were among those who had scouted out the land, tore their clothes,

**7** and said to the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, "The land we passed through to scout it out is a very, very good land.

**8** If the LORD delights in us, He will bring us into this land and give it to us, a land that flows with milk and honey.

**9** Only do not rebel against the LORD, and do not fear the people of the land, for they are bread for us. Their shade has turned away from them, and the LORD is with us. Do not fear them."

**10** But the whole congregation said to stone them. The glory of the LORD appeared at the tent of meeting to all the sons of Israel.

## Moses Pleads

**11** The LORD said to Moses, "How long will this people despise Me? How long will they not trust Me, in spite of all the signs I have done among them?

**12** I will strike them with pestilence and disinherit them, and I will make you into a nation greater and mightier than they are."

**13** But Moses said to the LORD, "Then the Egyptians will hear of it, for by Your power You brought this people up from among them,

**14** and they will tell the people who live in this land. They have heard that You, LORD, are among this people, that You, LORD, are seen eye to eye, that Your cloud stands over them, and that You go before them in a pillar of cloud by day and in a pillar of fire by night.

**15** If You put this people to death as one man, the nations who have heard Your fame will say,

**16** 'It was because the LORD was not able to bring this people into the land He swore to give them that He slaughtered them in the wilderness.'

**17** So now, let the power of the Lord be great, as You have spoken, saying,

**18** 'The LORD is slow to anger and abundant in loyal love, carrying guilt and rebellion, and not leaving it unpunished, calling the guilt of fathers to account on sons, on the third and on the fourth generation.'

**19** Please forgive the guilt of this people according to the greatness of Your loyal love, just as You have carried this people from Egypt until now."

**20** The LORD said, "I have forgiven, as you asked.

**21** But as I live, and as all the earth shall be filled with the glory of the LORD,

**22** none of the men who have seen My glory and My signs that I did in Egypt and in the wilderness, and yet have tested Me these ten times and have not listened to My voice,

**23** shall see the land that I swore to their fathers. None of those who despised Me shall see it.

**24** But My servant Caleb, because there is a different spirit in him and he has followed Me fully — I will bring him into the land where he went, and his offspring shall possess it.

**25** Now the Amalekites and the Canaanites live in the valley. Tomorrow turn and set out into the wilderness by the way of the Sea of Reeds."

## The Sentence

**26** The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**27** "How long shall this evil congregation go on grumbling against Me? I have heard the grumblings of the sons of Israel against Me.

**28** Say to them: As I live, declares the LORD, I will do to you exactly what you have said in My hearing.

**29** In this wilderness your bodies shall fall — all of you who were counted in your full number from twenty years old and upward, who have grumbled against Me.

**30** Not one of you shall come into the land where I raised My hand to settle you, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.

**31** But your little ones, who you said would become plunder, I will bring in, and they shall know the land you have rejected.

**32** But as for you, your bodies shall fall in this wilderness.

**33** Your sons shall be shepherds in the wilderness forty years, and shall carry your unfaithfulness until the last of your bodies lies in the wilderness.

**34** By the number of the days you scouted out the land, forty days — a year for each day — you shall carry your guilt forty years, and you shall know what it is to have Me against you.

**35** I, the LORD, have spoken. I will surely do this to all this evil congregation that has gathered together against Me. In this wilderness they shall come to an end, and there they shall die."

**36** The men whom Moses sent to scout out the land, who came back and made the whole congregation grumble against him by spreading a bad report about the land —

**37** those men who spread the bad report about the land died by the plague before the LORD.

**38** But Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh remained alive, of the men who went to scout out the land.

## Too Late

**39** Moses told these words to all the sons of Israel, and the people mourned greatly.

**40** They rose early in the morning and went up toward the top of the mountain, saying, "Here we are. We will go up to the place the LORD has spoken of, for we have sinned."

**41** But Moses said, "Why are you going against the command of the LORD? It will not succeed.

**42** Do not go up, for the LORD is not among you, or you will be struck down before your enemies.

**43** For the Amalekites and the Canaanites are there ahead of you, and you will fall by the sword. Because you have turned back from following the LORD, the LORD will not be with you."

**44** But they presumed to go up toward the top of the mountain, while neither the ark of the covenant of the LORD nor Moses moved out of the camp.

**45** Then the Amalekites and the Canaanites who lived in that hill country came down and struck them and beat them down as far as Hormah.

## Notes

- **v4 "Let us appoint a leader and go back to Egypt"** — *nittenah rosh*, let us give ourselves a head. The people who were brought out of Egypt propose to choose their own leader and undo the Exodus. Nehemiah recalls this line in his prayer: *they appointed a leader to return to their slavery* (Nehemiah 9:17).
- **v9 "they are bread for us… their shade has turned away from them"** — two pictures. The people of the land will be eaten up like bread, the answer to the scouts' *a land that devours those who live in it* (13:32). And their *shade*, their protection, as shade protects in desert heat, has left them.
- **vv17–18 "as You have spoken, saying"** — Moses quotes God's own words from Exodus 34:6–7 back to Him, the words spoken when God passed before him on the mountain. He quotes them as the ground of his plea, and leaves some out: *merciful and gracious*, *and truth*, *keeping loyal love for thousands*.
- **v22 "these ten times"** — *zeh eser pe'amim*. It may be a round number, *again and again*, or a count of the tests since the sea. The text does not list them.
- **v24 "a different spirit… he has followed Me fully"** — *va-yemalle acharai*, literally **he filled up after Me**: he followed all the way. Joshua is named with Caleb in v30 and v38; here Caleb alone is singled out.
- **v33 "shall carry your unfaithfulness"** — *zenutekhem*, from *zanah*, the word for prostitution that the Bible uses for unfaithfulness to God. The children bear the cost of it for forty years.
- **v34 "a year for each day"** — *yom la-shanah yom la-shanah*, a day for the year, a day for the year. Forty days of looking at the land become forty years of not entering it.
- **v44 "they presumed"** — *va-ya'pilu*, from the root of Habakkuk's *here is one who is **puffed up**; his soul is not upright in him, but the righteous shall live by his faith* (Habakkuk 2:4). They went up without the ark and without Moses.
- **v45 "as far as Hormah"** — *ha-chormah*, from *charam*, to devote to destruction, the root of *cherem*, **devoted** (Leviticus 27:28). The place of their defeat bears the name of destruction.

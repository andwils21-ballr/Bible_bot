# Numbers 13

## One Man from Each Tribe

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Send men to scout out the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the sons of Israel. You shall send one man from each tribe of their fathers, every one a chief among them."

**3** So Moses sent them from the wilderness of Paran at the command of the LORD, all of them men who were heads of the sons of Israel.

**4** These were their names: from the tribe of Reuben, Shammua son of Zaccur;

**5** from the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat son of Hori;

**6** from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;

**7** from the tribe of Issachar, Igal son of Joseph;

**8** from the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Nun;

**9** from the tribe of Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu;

**10** from the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel son of Sodi;

**11** from the tribe of Joseph, that is, from the tribe of Manasseh, Gaddi son of Susi;

**12** from the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gemalli;

**13** from the tribe of Asher, Sethur son of Michael;

**14** from the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi son of Vophsi;

**15** from the tribe of Gad, Geuel son of Machi.

**16** These are the names of the men Moses sent to scout out the land. Moses called Hoshea son of Nun Joshua.

## What to Look For

**17** So Moses sent them to scout out the land of Canaan, and said to them, "Go up this way into the Negev, and go up into the hill country,

**18** and see what the land is like, and whether the people who live in it are strong or weak, few or many,

**19** and whether the land they live in is good or bad, and whether the cities they live in are open camps or fortified,

**20** and whether the land is rich or poor, and whether there are trees in it or not. Be brave, and bring back some of the fruit of the land." Now it was the season of the first ripe grapes.

## Forty Days

**21** So they went up and scouted out the land from the wilderness of Zin as far as Rehob, near Lebo-hamath.

**22** They went up into the Negev, and he came to Hebron. Ahiman, Sheshai and Talmai, the descendants of Anak, were there. Now Hebron had been built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.

**23** They came to the valley of Eshcol, and cut down from there a branch with a single cluster of grapes, and they carried it on a pole between two of them, with some of the pomegranates and the figs.

**24** That place was called the valley of Eshcol because of the cluster that the sons of Israel cut down there.

**25** They came back from scouting out the land at the end of forty days.

## The Report

**26** They came to Moses and Aaron and to the whole congregation of the sons of Israel in the wilderness of Paran, at Kadesh. They brought back word to them and to the whole congregation, and showed them the fruit of the land.

**27** They told him, "We came to the land where you sent us, and it does flow with milk and honey, and this is its fruit.

**28** But the people who live in the land are strong, and the cities are fortified and very large. We saw the descendants of Anak there too.

**29** The Amalekites live in the land of the Negev; the Hittites, the Jebusites and the Amorites live in the hill country; and the Canaanites live by the sea and along the Jordan."

**30** Caleb quieted the people before Moses and said, "We must go up at once and take possession of it, because we can surely overcome it."

**31** But the men who had gone up with him said, "We cannot go up against the people, because they are stronger than we are."

**32** They spread among the sons of Israel a bad report of the land they had scouted, saying, "The land we passed through to scout it out is a land that devours those who live in it, and all the people we saw in it are men of great size.

**33** There we saw the Nephilim — the sons of Anak are from the Nephilim — and we were like grasshoppers in our own eyes, and so we were in theirs."

## Notes

- **v2 "to scout out"** — *ve-yaturu*, from *tur*, to go about and look over. The ark did this for the people in 10:33, *to seek out a resting place*. And in 15:39 the same verb warns against the scouts' failure: *you shall not **go about** after your own heart and your own eyes*.
- **v16 "Moses called Hoshea son of Nun Joshua"** — *Hoshea* is **he saves**; *Yehoshua* adds the name of God: **the LORD saves**. Moses adds one letter, and the man who will lead Israel into the land carries the LORD's name in his own. The Greek of *Yehoshua* is *Iēsous*, Jesus (see the note on Exodus 17:9).
- **v22 "and he came to Hebron"** — the verb is singular in the middle of a plural story: *they went up… and **he** came*. The text does not say who. Caleb, who later asks for Hebron as his own (Joshua 14:12–14), is the one it is usually taken to mean.
- **v22 "seven years before Zoan in Egypt"** — a builder's date: Hebron was older than a city of Egypt by seven years.
- **vv23–24 "the valley of Eshcol… because of the cluster"** — *eshkol* is **a cluster**. The valley was called Cluster Valley after what the scouts cut there, a single bunch of grapes that took two men and a pole to carry.
- **v32 "a land that devours those who live in it"** — the land they were sent to see as *flowing with milk and honey* (v27) they report as a land that eats. The same scouts brought back the fruit that proved the first report.
- **v33 "the Nephilim"** — the word of Genesis 6:4, *the Nephilim were in the earth in those days*, and the word occurs only there and here. The clause *the sons of Anak are from the Nephilim* explains the name for the reader. The Greek has only *we saw the giants*, and not that clause.
- **v33 "and so we were in theirs"** — the scouts report how they looked to the giants, which they had no way of knowing.

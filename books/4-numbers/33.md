# Numbers 33

## From Egypt to Sinai

**1** These are the stages of the journey of the sons of Israel, when they went out of the land of Egypt by their armies under the hand of Moses and Aaron.

**2** Moses wrote down their starting points, stage by stage, at the LORD's command. These are their stages by their starting points.

**3** They set out from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month. On the day after the Passover the sons of Israel went out in open defiance, in the sight of all Egypt,

**4** while the Egyptians were burying all their firstborn, whom the LORD had struck down among them. On their gods, too, the LORD had carried out judgments.

**5** The sons of Israel set out from Rameses and camped at Succoth.

**6** They set out from Succoth and camped at Etham, which is at the edge of the wilderness.

**7** They set out from Etham and turned back to Pi-hahiroth, which faces Baal-zephon, and they camped before Migdol.

**8** They set out from before Hahiroth and passed through the middle of the sea into the wilderness. They went three days' journey in the wilderness of Etham and camped at Marah.

**9** They set out from Marah and came to Elim. At Elim there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees, and they camped there.

**10** They set out from Elim and camped by the Sea of Reeds.

**11** They set out from the Sea of Reeds and camped in the wilderness of Sin.

**12** They set out from the wilderness of Sin and camped at Dophkah.

**13** They set out from Dophkah and camped at Alush.

**14** They set out from Alush and camped at Rephidim, where there was no water for the people to drink.

**15** They set out from Rephidim and camped in the wilderness of Sinai.


## From Sinai to Ezion-geber

**16** They set out from the wilderness of Sinai and camped at Kibroth-hattaavah.

**17** They set out from Kibroth-hattaavah and camped at Hazeroth.

**18** They set out from Hazeroth and camped at Rithmah.

**19** They set out from Rithmah and camped at Rimmon-perez.

**20** They set out from Rimmon-perez and camped at Libnah.

**21** They set out from Libnah and camped at Rissah.

**22** They set out from Rissah and camped at Kehelathah.

**23** They set out from Kehelathah and camped at Mount Shepher.

**24** They set out from Mount Shepher and camped at Haradah.

**25** They set out from Haradah and camped at Makheloth.

**26** They set out from Makheloth and camped at Tahath.

**27** They set out from Tahath and camped at Terah.

**28** They set out from Terah and camped at Mithkah.

**29** They set out from Mithkah and camped at Hashmonah.

**30** They set out from Hashmonah and camped at Moseroth.

**31** They set out from Moseroth and camped at Bene-jaakan.

**32** They set out from Bene-jaakan and camped at Hor-haggidgad.

**33** They set out from Hor-haggidgad and camped at Jotbathah.

**34** They set out from Jotbathah and camped at Abronah.

**35** They set out from Abronah and camped at Ezion-geber.

## From Kadesh to the Plains of Moab

**36** They set out from Ezion-geber and camped in the wilderness of Zin, that is, Kadesh.

**37** They set out from Kadesh and camped at Mount Hor, at the edge of the land of Edom.

**38** Aaron the priest went up Mount Hor at the LORD's command and died there, in the fortieth year after the sons of Israel came out of the land of Egypt, in the fifth month, on the first day of the month.

**39** Aaron was a hundred and twenty-three years old when he died on Mount Hor.

**40** The Canaanite, the king of Arad, who lived in the Negev in the land of Canaan, heard that the sons of Israel were coming.

**41** They set out from Mount Hor and camped at Zalmonah.

**42** They set out from Zalmonah and camped at Punon.

**43** They set out from Punon and camped at Oboth.

**44** They set out from Oboth and camped at Iye-abarim, on the border of Moab.

**45** They set out from Iyim and camped at Dibon-gad.

**46** They set out from Dibon-gad and camped at Almon-diblathaim.

**47** They set out from Almon-diblathaim and camped in the mountains of Abarim, before Nebo.

**48** They set out from the mountains of Abarim and camped on the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho.

**49** They camped by the Jordan from Beth-jeshimoth as far as Abel-shittim on the plains of Moab.

## When You Cross the Jordan

**50** The LORD spoke to Moses on the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho, saying,

**51** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them: When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan,

**52** you shall drive out all the inhabitants of the land from before you. You shall destroy all their carved stones, destroy all their images of cast metal and tear down all their high places.

**53** You shall take possession of the land and settle in it, for I have given you the land to possess it.

**54** You shall inherit the land by lot according to your clans. To the larger you shall give a larger inheritance, and to the smaller a smaller inheritance. Wherever the lot falls for anyone, that shall be his. You shall inherit by the tribes of your fathers.

**55** But if you do not drive out the inhabitants of the land from before you, those of them you let remain will be barbs in your eyes and thorns in your sides, and they will harass you in the land where you live.

**56** And it shall come to pass that what I planned to do to them, I will do to you."

## Notes

- **v2 "Moses wrote down their starting points"** — Moses writes the list himself, *at the LORD's command*, as he wrote down the words of the LORD at Sinai (Exodus 24:4). The word for a stage, *massa*, is from *nasa*, **to pull up tent pegs**, the verb of every line that follows: *they set out*.
- **v3 "in open defiance, in the sight of all Egypt"** — *be-yad ramah*, **with a high hand**, the phrase of Exodus 14:8, *the sons of Israel were marching out in open defiance*. The date is the Passover's: the fifteenth of the first month, the morning after.
- **v4 "On their gods, too, the LORD had carried out judgments"** — the promise of the Passover night: *Against all the gods of Egypt I will carry out judgments. I am the LORD* (Exodus 12:12). The Egyptians bury their firstborn while Israel walks out.
- **v8 "passed through the middle of the sea"** — the crossing of Exodus 14 in six words, set in a list between two campsites.
- **vv12–13 Dophkah and Alush** — two stops between the wilderness of Sin and Rephidim that Exodus does not name (Exodus 17:1 says only that they set out *by their stages*).
- **v38 "in the fortieth year… in the fifth month, on the first day"** — the date of Aaron's death is given here. Chapter 20 tells the death on Mount Hor (20:28) but not the day.
- **v52 "carved stones… images of cast metal… high places"** — *maskiyyot*, stones with figures cut in them (the word of Leviticus 26:1, *a carved stone*); *tsalmei massekhot*, images poured from molten metal; *bamot*, the raised places of worship.
- **v54 "To the larger you shall give a larger inheritance"** — the rule already given at the census: *To the larger you shall give a larger inheritance, and to the smaller you shall give a smaller inheritance* (26:54), and *the land shall be divided by lot* (26:55).
- **v55 "barbs in your eyes and thorns in your sides"** — *sikkim* and *tseninim*, sharp points in the eye and in the side. What is left of the old inhabitants will not stay quiet.
- **v56 "what I planned to do to them, I will do to you"** — the warning turns the whole list of stages around: the people who came out of Egypt can be driven out as well.

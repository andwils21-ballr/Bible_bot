# Numbers 3

## The Sons of Aaron

**1** These are the begettings of Aaron and Moses, on the day the LORD spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai.

**2** These are the names of the sons of Aaron: the firstborn Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**3** These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests, whose hand he filled to serve as priests.

**4** Nadab and Abihu died before the LORD when they brought strange fire near before the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no sons. So Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests in the presence of Aaron their father.

## The Levites Given to Aaron

**5** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**6** "Bring the tribe of Levi near, and set it before Aaron the priest, and they shall serve him.

**7** They shall keep his charge and the charge of the whole congregation before the tent of meeting, doing the work of the dwelling.

**8** They shall keep all the vessels of the tent of meeting, and the charge of the sons of Israel, doing the work of the dwelling.

**9** You shall give the Levites to Aaron and to his sons. Wholly given they are to him, from the sons of Israel.

**10** You shall appoint Aaron and his sons, and they shall keep their priesthood. The outsider who comes near shall be put to death."

**11** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**12** "I — here, I have taken the Levites from among the sons of Israel in place of every firstborn, every opener of the womb among the sons of Israel. The Levites shall be Mine.

**13** For every firstborn is Mine. On the day I struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I set apart for Myself every firstborn in Israel, from man to beast. They shall be Mine. I am the LORD."

## The Count of the Levites

**14** The LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying,

**15** "Count the sons of Levi by their fathers' houses, by their clans. Every male from a month old and upward you shall count."

**16** Moses counted them at the word of the LORD, as he had been commanded.

**17** These were the sons of Levi by their names: Gershon and Kohath and Merari.

**18** These are the names of the sons of Gershon by their clans: Libni and Shimei.

**19** The sons of Kohath by their clans: Amram and Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**20** The sons of Merari by their clans: Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of the Levites by their fathers' houses.

## Gershon: West

**21** To Gershon belonged the clan of the Libnites and the clan of the Shimeites. These are the clans of the Gershonites.

**22** Those of them counted, by the number of every male from a month old and upward — those of them counted were seven thousand five hundred.

**23** The clans of the Gershonites shall camp behind the dwelling, on the west.

**24** The chief of the fathers' house of the Gershonites is Eliasaph son of Lael.

**25** The charge of the sons of Gershon in the tent of meeting is the dwelling and the tent, its covering, and the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting,

**26** and the hangings of the court, and the screen for the entrance of the court that is around the dwelling and the altar, and its cords — all the work of it.

## Kohath: South

**27** To Kohath belonged the clan of the Amramites and the clan of the Izharites and the clan of the Hebronites and the clan of the Uzzielites. These are the clans of the Kohathites.

**28** By the number of every male from a month old and upward, eight thousand six hundred, keeping the charge of the holy place.

**29** The clans of the sons of Kohath shall camp on the side of the dwelling, on the south.

**30** The chief of the fathers' house of the clans of the Kohathites is Elizaphan son of Uzziel.

**31** Their charge is the ark and the table and the lampstand and the altars, and the vessels of the holy place with which they serve, and the screen — all the work of it.

**32** The chief over the chiefs of the Levites is Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, with oversight of those who keep the charge of the holy place.

## Merari: North

**33** To Merari belonged the clan of the Mahlites and the clan of the Mushites. These are the clans of Merari.

**34** Those of them counted, by the number of every male from a month old and upward, were six thousand two hundred.

**35** The chief of the fathers' house of the clans of Merari is Zuriel son of Abihail. They shall camp on the side of the dwelling, on the north.

**36** The appointed charge of the sons of Merari is the frames of the dwelling and its bars and its pillars and its sockets and all its equipment — all the work of it —

**37** and the pillars of the court all around, and their sockets and their pegs and their cords.

## Moses and Aaron: East

**38** Those camping before the dwelling on the east, before the tent of meeting toward the sunrise, are Moses and Aaron and his sons, keeping the charge of the sanctuary, the charge for the sons of Israel. The outsider who comes near shall be put to death.

**39** All those counted of the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron counted at the word of the LORD by their clans, every male from a month old and upward, were twenty-two thousand.

## The Firstborn Redeemed

**40** The LORD said to Moses, "Count every firstborn male of the sons of Israel from a month old and upward, and take the number of their names.

**41** You shall take the Levites for Me — I am the LORD — in place of every firstborn among the sons of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of every firstborn among the livestock of the sons of Israel."

**42** Moses counted, as the LORD had commanded him, every firstborn among the sons of Israel.

**43** All the firstborn males, by the number of names, from a month old and upward, of those counted, were twenty-two thousand two hundred and seventy-three.

**44** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**45** "Take the Levites in place of every firstborn among the sons of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of their livestock. The Levites shall be Mine. I am the LORD.

**46** For the redemption of the two hundred and seventy-three of the firstborn of the sons of Israel who are more than the Levites,

**47** you shall take five shekels apiece, head by head. By the shekel of the sanctuary you shall take it; twenty gerahs make a shekel.

**48** You shall give the money to Aaron and to his sons, the redemption of those who are more than their number."

**49** Moses took the redemption money from those who were more than the ones redeemed by the Levites.

**50** From the firstborn of the sons of Israel he took the money, one thousand three hundred and sixty-five shekels, by the shekel of the sanctuary.

**51** Moses gave the redemption money to Aaron and to his sons at the word of the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Notes

- **v1 "the begettings of Aaron and Moses"** — *toledot*, the word that divides Genesis into its families: *these are the begettings of the heavens and the earth* (Genesis 2:4), *of Noah* (6:9), *of Terah* (11:27), *of Jacob* (37:2). This is the only time the formula appears in the rest of the five books. Then it names Aaron's sons and no one else. Moses's sons are never listed with the priests; 1 Chronicles 23:14 says of them, *the sons of Moses the man of God were named among the tribe of Levi*. The family line of Moses, in the sense that matters here, is the priesthood of his brother.
- **v3 "whose hand he filled"** — *mille yadam*, the idiom for ordaining a priest (Leviticus 8:33). At their ordination Moses put the portions of the offering into their hands (8:27).
- **v4 "in the presence of Aaron their father"** — *al penei*, literally on the face of. It can mean *in his sight* or *during his lifetime*, as when Haran died *before the face of Terah his father* (Genesis 11:28), while his father still lived. Both are true here: the two younger sons took up the work while their father was still the high priest, in front of him.
- **v9 "wholly given"** — *netunim netunim*, given, given: the word doubled for completeness. Here the Levites are given *to him*, to Aaron. At their dedication the same doubled word is used, but God says *they are given, given **to Me*** (8:16), and the Greek already reads *to Me* in this verse. They belong to God, and He hands them to the priests.
- **vv12–13 "in place of every firstborn"** — the firstborn were claimed on the night Egypt's firstborn died: *set apart to Me every firstborn* (Exodus 13:2). Now one tribe stands in for all of them, and the firstborn of every other tribe go back to their families.
- **v38 "the outsider who comes near shall be put to death"** — *ha-zar*, the word for anyone not entitled to the holy things (Leviticus 22:10). Moses and Aaron camp in front of the only entrance, on the east. Anyone entering has to pass them.
- **vv22, 28, 34, 39 the Levite total** — the three clans add up to 7,500 + 8,600 + 6,200 = 22,300, but v39 says 22,000. The Greek does not reconcile it: it has 6,050 for Merari, which makes 22,150. The 22,000 is the number the rest of the chapter uses, set against 22,273 firstborn to leave 273 over (v46). The Hebrew words for *six* (*shesh*) and *three* (*shalosh*) differ by one letter, so v28 may once have read 8,300, which would make the sum exact. No witness here has that reading.
- **v39 "and Aaron"** — in the Hebrew text the word *ve-Aharon* has a dot written over every letter. It is one of ten places in the five books where the scribes marked a word this way (Genesis 33:4 is another). The dots flag the word as questioned. Aaron is not named in the command to count (v15) or in the count itself (v16), so the dots may mark his name as added.
- **v47 "five shekels apiece, head by head"** — *la-gulgolet*, by the skull, the word of the census in 1:2. The firstborn left over after each Levite stands for one of them are bought back at five shekels each, the price set for a boy from a month to five years old in the valuations of Leviticus 27:6. 273 × 5 = 1,365 (v50).

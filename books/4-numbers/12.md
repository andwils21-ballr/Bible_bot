# Numbers 12

## Against Moses

**1** Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses because of the Cushite woman he had married — for he had married a Cushite woman.

**2** They said, "Has the LORD spoken only through Moses? Has He not spoken through us too?" The LORD heard it.

**3** Now the man Moses was very humble, more than any person on the face of the earth.

**4** Suddenly the LORD said to Moses and to Aaron and to Miriam, "Come out, the three of you, to the tent of meeting." The three of them came out.

**5** The LORD came down in a pillar of cloud and stood at the entrance of the tent, and called Aaron and Miriam, and the two of them came forward.

**6** He said, "Hear My words:  
If there is a prophet among you,  
I the LORD make Myself known to him in a vision;  
I speak with him in a dream.

**7** Not so with My servant Moses.  
He is trusted in all My house.

**8** Mouth to mouth I speak with him,  
plainly and not in riddles,  
and he sees the form of the LORD.  
Why then were you not afraid  
to speak against My servant Moses?"

**9** The anger of the LORD burned against them, and He went away.

## White as Snow

**10** The cloud turned away from over the tent, and here — Miriam was blighted, white as snow. Aaron turned toward Miriam, and here — she was blighted.

**11** Aaron said to Moses, "Please, my lord, do not hold against us the sin we committed in our folly.

**12** Do not let her be like a dead thing, whose flesh is half eaten away when it comes out of its mother's womb."

**13** Moses cried out to the LORD, saying, "O God, please heal her, please."

**14** The LORD said to Moses, "If her father had spit in her face, would she not be put to shame seven days? Let her be shut away outside the camp seven days, and afterward she may be brought back in."

**15** So Miriam was shut away outside the camp seven days, and the people did not set out until Miriam was brought back in.

**16** Afterward the people set out from Hazeroth and camped in the wilderness of Paran.

## Notes

- **v1 "the Cushite woman"** — *ha-ishah ha-kushit*. Cush is the land south of Egypt, the country of the Gihon in Genesis 2:13 and a son of Ham in Genesis 10:6. The Greek reads *the **Ethiopian** woman*, *Aithiopissa*, twice in this verse. Whether she is Zipporah, the Midianite wife of Exodus 2:21, or a second wife, the text does not say. Habakkuk puts the two names side by side, *the tents of Cushan… the curtains of the land of Midian* (3:7).
- **v1 "Miriam and Aaron spoke"** — the verb is feminine singular, *va-tedabber*, **she spoke**, with Aaron added after it. Miriam leads, and in v10 she alone is struck.
- **v3 "very humble"** — *anav*. The written text spells it without the letter *yod*, and the reading tradition adds it: one of the places where the written and the read forms differ. The word is from the root of *ani*, the poor and lowly: a man who does not defend himself, and here the LORD defends him.
- **v7 "He is trusted in all My house"** — *ne'eman*, **faithful, trusted**, from *aman*, the root of *amen*. The letter to the Hebrews quotes it: *Moses was faithful in all His house as a servant* (Hebrews 3:5).
- **v8 "Mouth to mouth… he sees the form of the LORD"** — *temunat YHWH*. At Horeb the people *heard the sound of words, but saw no form*, the same word (Deuteronomy 4:12). Moses sees what the people did not. The Greek has *he saw the **glory** of the Lord*.
- **v10 "white as snow"** — *metsora'at ka-shaleg*, the words of Exodus 4:6, where the LORD made Moses' own hand *blighted, white as snow* as a sign, and then healed it.
- **v13 "O God, please heal her, please"** — *El na refa na lah*, five words in Hebrew, and the only thing Moses says in the chapter. The man who was spoken against prays for the one who spoke.
- **v14 "If her father had spit in her face"** — *ve-aviha yaroq yaraq be-faneha*. A father's public contempt would shame a daughter seven days, and so her shutting away is seven days: the seven days of Leviticus 13:4–5. The camp waits for her (v15).

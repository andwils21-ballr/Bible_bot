# Numbers 29

## The Day of Horn Blasts

**1** In the seventh month, on the first of the month, you shall have a holy assembly. You shall do no ordinary work. It shall be a day of horn blasts for you.

**2** You shall offer a burnt offering as a soothing aroma to the LORD: one young bull, one ram, and seven lambs a year old without defect,

**3** with their grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil, three tenths for the bull, two tenths for the ram,

**4** and one tenth for each of the seven lambs,

**5** and one male goat as a sin offering, to make atonement for you,

**6** besides the burnt offering of the new moon and its grain offering, and the continual burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings according to the rule for them, as a soothing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.

## The Tenth Day

**7** On the tenth of this seventh month you shall have a holy assembly, and you shall afflict yourselves. You shall do no work.

**8** You shall present a burnt offering to the LORD as a soothing aroma: one young bull, one ram, and seven lambs a year old. They shall be without defect.

**9** Their grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for the bull, two tenths for the one ram,

**10** and a tenth for each of the seven lambs,

**11** and one male goat as a sin offering, besides the sin offering of atonement, and the continual burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings.

## The Festival of Booths

**12** On the fifteenth day of the seventh month you shall have a holy assembly. You shall do no ordinary work, and you shall keep a festival to the LORD for seven days.

**13** You shall present a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a soothing aroma to the LORD: thirteen young bulls, two rams, and fourteen lambs a year old. They shall be without defect.

**14** Their grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for each of the thirteen bulls, two tenths for each of the two rams,

**15** and a tenth for each of the fourteen lambs,

**16** and one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

**17** On the second day: twelve young bulls, two rams, and fourteen lambs a year old without defect,

**18** with their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, by their number, according to the rule,

**19** and one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

**20** On the third day: eleven bulls, two rams, and fourteen lambs a year old without defect,

**21** with their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, by their number, according to the rule,

**22** and one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

**23** On the fourth day: ten bulls, two rams, and fourteen lambs a year old without defect,

**24** with their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, by their number, according to the rule,

**25** and one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

**26** On the fifth day: nine bulls, two rams, and fourteen lambs a year old without defect,

**27** with their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, by their number, according to the rule,

**28** and one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

**29** On the sixth day: eight bulls, two rams, and fourteen lambs a year old without defect,

**30** with their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, by their number, according to the rule,

**31** and one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

**32** On the seventh day: seven bulls, two rams, and fourteen lambs a year old without defect,

**33** with their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, by their number, according to the rule,

**34** and one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

## The Eighth Day

**35** On the eighth day you shall have a closing assembly. You shall do no ordinary work.

**36** You shall present a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a soothing aroma to the LORD: one bull, one ram, and seven lambs a year old without defect,

**37** with their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bull, the ram and the lambs, by their number, according to the rule,

**38** and one male goat as a sin offering, besides the continual burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

**39** These you shall offer to the LORD at your appointed times, besides your vows and your freewill offerings, for your burnt offerings, your grain offerings, your drink offerings and your peace offerings."

**40** Moses told the sons of Israel everything that the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Notes

- **v1 "a day of horn blasts"** — *yom teru'ah*, a day of **shouting** or **blasting**, the word for the trumpet alarm of 10:5–6 and for *the shout of a king* in 23:21. Leviticus 23:24 calls the same day *a remembrance with horn blasts*. Neither text says what the blasts are for.
- **v6 "besides the burnt offering of the new moon"** — the first of the seventh month is also a new moon, so it carries three sets of offerings at once: the daily, the monthly (28:11–15), and its own.
- **v7 "you shall afflict yourselves"** — the Day of Atonement of Leviticus 16:29–31, where the ritual of the two goats is set out. Here only its public offerings are listed, and v11 mentions *the sin offering of atonement* as already known.
- **vv13–32 thirteen bulls, then twelve… then seven** — the Festival of Booths is by far the largest offering of the year. The bulls go down by one each day, from thirteen to seven, and add up to **seventy** over the seven days. Every other number stays fixed: two rams and fourteen lambs a day.
- **v15 "a tenth"** — the Hebrew scribes put a dot over one letter of *issaron*, **a tenth**, the second of the two times the word is written. The dot marks the letter as questioned, as at 21:30. The meaning of the verse does not change.
- **v35 "a closing assembly"** — *atseret*, from *atsar*, **to hold back, to close**, the word of Leviticus 23:36. After seven days of seventy bulls, the eighth day has a single bull, as on the first of the month and the Day of Atonement. The Greek calls it *exodion*, **the going out**, the day the festival ends.
- **v39 "besides your vows and your freewill offerings"** — the calendar sets the offerings the nation owes. What each person promises is added on top, and a vow is the subject of the next chapter.
- **v40** — in the Hebrew and the Greek this verse is 30:1, the first line of the next chapter. The English ends chapter 29 with it.

# Numbers 28

## Every Day

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Command the sons of Israel and say to them: My offering, My bread for My offerings by fire, a soothing aroma to Me, you shall take care to present to Me at its appointed time.

**3** Say to them: This is the offering by fire that you shall present to the LORD: two lambs a year old without defect, day by day, as a continual burnt offering.

**4** You shall offer the one lamb in the morning, and the other lamb you shall offer between the evenings,

**5** with a tenth of an ephah of fine flour for a grain offering, mixed with a fourth of a hin of beaten oil.

**6** It is a continual burnt offering, the one made at Mount Sinai, as a soothing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.

**7** Its drink offering shall be a fourth of a hin for each lamb. In the holy place you shall pour out a drink offering of strong drink to the LORD.

**8** The other lamb you shall offer between the evenings. Like the grain offering of the morning and like its drink offering you shall offer it, an offering by fire, a soothing aroma to the LORD.

## Every Sabbath

**9** On the sabbath day: two lambs a year old without defect, and two tenths of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering, and its drink offering.

**10** This is the burnt offering of every sabbath, besides the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.

## Every New Moon

**11** At the beginnings of your months you shall present a burnt offering to the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, and seven lambs a year old without defect,

**12** and three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for each bull, and two tenths of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for the one ram,

**13** and a tenth of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for each lamb, a burnt offering, a soothing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.

**14** Their drink offerings shall be half a hin of wine for a bull, a third of a hin for the ram, and a fourth of a hin for a lamb. This is the burnt offering of every new moon, month by month through the months of the year.

**15** One male goat shall be offered as a sin offering to the LORD, besides the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.

## Passover and Unleavened Bread

**16** In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, is the LORD's Passover.

**17** On the fifteenth day of this month is a festival. For seven days unleavened bread shall be eaten.

**18** On the first day there shall be a holy assembly. You shall do no ordinary work.

**19** You shall present an offering by fire, a burnt offering to the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, and seven lambs a year old. They shall be without defect.

**20** Their grain offering shall be fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for a bull and two tenths for the ram you shall offer,

**21** and a tenth you shall offer for each of the seven lambs,

**22** and one male goat as a sin offering, to make atonement for you.

**23** You shall offer these besides the burnt offering of the morning, which is for the continual burnt offering.

**24** In this way you shall offer daily for seven days the bread of the offering by fire, a soothing aroma to the LORD. It shall be offered besides the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.

**25** On the seventh day you shall have a holy assembly. You shall do no ordinary work.

## The Day of Firstfruits

**26** On the day of the firstfruits, when you present a new grain offering to the LORD at your Festival of Weeks, you shall have a holy assembly. You shall do no ordinary work.

**27** You shall present a burnt offering as a soothing aroma to the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, and seven lambs a year old,

**28** with their grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil, three tenths for each bull, two tenths for the one ram,

**29** a tenth for each of the seven lambs,

**30** and one male goat to make atonement for you.

**31** You shall offer them besides the continual burnt offering and its grain offering. They shall be without defect for you, with their drink offerings.

## Notes

- **v2 "My offering, My bread for My offerings by fire"** — *qorbani lachmi le-ishai*, three nouns each marked **My**. *Lechem*, **bread**, and more broadly **food**, is the word Leviticus uses for what goes up on the altar, *the bread of their God* (Leviticus 21:6). Chapters 28 and 29 set out that bread for the whole year, day by day, sabbath by sabbath, month by month and feast by feast.
- **v3 "day by day, as a continual burnt offering"** — *olat tamid*, the offering of Exodus 29:38–42, one lamb in the morning and one between the evenings. Every other offering in these two chapters is added *besides the continual burnt offering* (vv10, 15, 23, 24, 31). The *tamid* never stops for a feast; the feast is laid on top of it.
- **v6 "the one made at Mount Sinai"** — the daily offering was first commanded at Sinai (Exodus 29:42) and has been offered since the dwelling was set up. The law here repeats it for the generation about to enter the land.
- **v7 "a drink offering of strong drink"** — *shekhar*. Exodus 29:40 and v14 here specify *wine* for the drink offering; this verse alone names *shekhar*, the word forbidden to priests on duty in Leviticus 10:9. It is poured out, not drunk.
- **vv9–10 "the burnt offering of every sabbath"** — *olat shabbat be-shabbato*, **the burnt offering of a sabbath on its sabbath**. The Torah commands the sabbath many times; this is the first place it sets a burnt offering for it.
- **vv11–15 "at the beginnings of your months"** — the new moon, *rosh chodesh*, the first law in the Torah to give it an offering. The trumpets of 10:10 were blown *at the beginnings of your months* over these same burnt offerings.
- **vv16–25 Passover and Unleavened Bread** — the Passover itself (v16) has no burnt offering listed here: it is the lamb eaten in each house (Exodus 12). The seven days that follow each carry the same large offering as a new moon.
- **v26 "the day of the firstfruits… your Festival of Weeks"** — *bi-shvu'oteikhem*, **in your weeks**, the *Festival of Weeks* of Exodus 34:22, counted seven weeks from the offering of the first sheaf (Leviticus 23:15–16). The Greek calls the day *the day of the new things*.

# Numbers 27

## Five Daughters

**1** The daughters of Zelophehad son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, of the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph, came forward. These are the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah.

**2** They stood before Moses and before Eleazar the priest and before the chiefs and the whole congregation, at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and said,

**3** "Our father died in the wilderness. He was not among the company who gathered together against the LORD in the company of Korah. He died for his own sin, and he had no sons.

**4** Why should the name of our father be taken away from his clan because he had no son? Give us a holding among our father's brothers."

**5** Moses brought their case before the LORD.

**6** The LORD said to Moses,

**7** "The daughters of Zelophehad are right in what they say. You shall give them a holding as an inheritance among their father's brothers, and pass their father's inheritance on to them.

**8** Say to the sons of Israel as well: If a man dies and has no son, you shall pass his inheritance on to his daughter.

**9** If he has no daughter, you shall give his inheritance to his brothers.

**10** If he has no brothers, you shall give his inheritance to his father's brothers.

**11** If his father has no brothers, you shall give his inheritance to his nearest relative in his clan, and he shall take possession of it. It shall be a statute and a ruling for the sons of Israel, as the LORD commanded Moses."

## Joshua

**12** The LORD said to Moses, "Go up this mountain of the Abarim, and see the land that I have given to the sons of Israel.

**13** When you have seen it, you also shall be gathered to your people, as Aaron your brother was gathered,

**14** because in the wilderness of Zin, when the congregation quarreled, you rebelled against My command to show Me holy at the waters before their eyes." These are the waters of Meribah of Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin.

**15** Moses spoke to the LORD, saying,

**16** "Let the LORD, the God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the congregation

**17** who will go out before them and come in before them, who will lead them out and bring them in, so that the congregation of the LORD may not be like sheep that have no shepherd."

**18** The LORD said to Moses, "Take Joshua son of Nun, a man in whom is the spirit, and lean your hand on him.

**19** Make him stand before Eleazar the priest and before the whole congregation, and give him his charge before their eyes.

**20** Put some of your authority on him, so that the whole congregation of the sons of Israel may obey.

**21** He shall stand before Eleazar the priest, who shall ask for him by the judgment of the Urim before the LORD. At his word they shall go out, and at his word they shall come in, he and all the sons of Israel with him, the whole congregation."

**22** Moses did as the LORD commanded him. He took Joshua and made him stand before Eleazar the priest and before the whole congregation,

**23** and he leaned his hands on him and gave him his charge, as the LORD had spoken through Moses.

## Notes

- **v3 "he died for his own sin"** — *be-chet'o met*. The daughters draw the line carefully: their father did not die with Korah, whose company was cut off with their households (16:32). He died as the rest of his generation died in the wilderness (14:29, 26:64–65), and his name should not die with him.
- **v4 "why should the name of our father be taken away"** — *yiggara*, **be cut short, be subtracted**. The daughters tie the name to the land: with no son to hold a portion, their father's name would be lost from his clan. In the count of 26:33 he is already listed only through his five daughters.
- **v7 "The daughters of Zelophehad are right in what they say"** — *ken benot Tselofchad dovrot*, **rightly the daughters speak**. The ruling comes from the LORD, and it opens by agreeing with them. The law of inheritance that follows (vv8–11) is written because they asked. Their case returns in chapter 36, and they receive their land in Joshua 17:3–6.
- **v14 "you rebelled against My command"** — *meritem pi*, the same words as 20:24, where Aaron's death was announced. Moses is told of his own death in the words used for his brother's.
- **v17 "like sheep that have no shepherd"** — *ka-tso'n asher ein lahem ro'eh*. Micaiah sees Israel *scattered on the mountains like sheep that have no shepherd* after the king falls (1 Kings 22:17). Matthew uses the phrase of the crowds Jesus had compassion on (Matthew 9:36). *Go out before them and come in before them* is said of David leading Israel's army: *all Israel and Judah loved David, for he went out and came in before them* (1 Samuel 18:16).
- **v18 "Joshua"** — *Yehoshua*, **the LORD saves**, the name Moses gave him (13:16). The Greek spells it *Iēsous*, the same name as Jesus, and the letter to the Hebrews uses it of Joshua: *if Jesus* (that is, Joshua) *had given them rest* (Hebrews 4:8).
- **v18 "lean your hand on him"** — *samakhta et yadkha alav*, the gesture of Leviticus 1:4, where the worshipper leans his hand on his offering. The LORD says *hand*; Moses leans both *hands* (v23), and Deuteronomy 34:9 says that because of it Joshua *was full of the spirit of wisdom*.
- **v20 "some of your authority"** — *me-hodkha*, **from your splendor, your majesty**. Moses is to give some of it, not all. The Greek has *of your glory*.
- **v21 "the judgment of the Urim"** — *mishpat ha-Urim*, the Urim in the *breastpiece of judgment* on Aaron's heart (Exodus 28:30). Moses spoke with the LORD mouth to mouth (12:8). Joshua will ask through the priest.

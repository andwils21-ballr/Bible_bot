# Numbers 34

## The Borders of Canaan

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Command the sons of Israel and say to them: When you come into the land of Canaan, this is the land that will fall to you as an inheritance, the land of Canaan with its borders.

**3** Your south side shall be from the wilderness of Zin along the side of Edom, and your south border shall run from the end of the Salt Sea on the east.

**4** Your border shall turn south of the Ascent of Akrabbim and cross to Zin, and its end shall be south of Kadesh-barnea. It shall go on to Hazar-addar and cross to Azmon.

**5** The border shall turn from Azmon to the Brook of Egypt, and its end shall be at the sea.

**6** For the west border you shall have the Great Sea and its coast. This shall be your west border.

**7** This shall be your north border: from the Great Sea you shall mark out your line to Mount Hor.

**8** From Mount Hor you shall mark it out to Lebo-hamath, and the end of the border shall be at Zedad.

**9** The border shall go on to Ziphron, and its end shall be at Hazar-enan. This shall be your north border.

**10** For your east border you shall mark out your line from Hazar-enan to Shepham.

**11** The border shall go down from Shepham to Riblah on the east side of Ain, and the border shall go down and strike the slope of the Sea of Chinnereth on the east.

**12** The border shall go down to the Jordan, and its end shall be at the Salt Sea. This shall be your land by its borders all around."

**13** Moses commanded the sons of Israel, saying, "This is the land that you shall inherit by lot, which the LORD has commanded to give to the nine tribes and the half-tribe.

**14** For the tribe of the sons of Reuben by their fathers' houses and the tribe of the sons of Gad by their fathers' houses have taken their inheritance, and the half-tribe of Manasseh has taken its inheritance.

**15** The two tribes and the half-tribe have taken their inheritance beyond the Jordan at Jericho, eastward, toward the sunrise."

## The Men Who Will Divide the Land

**16** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**17** "These are the names of the men who shall divide the land for you as an inheritance: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun.

**18** You shall take one chief from each tribe to divide the land as an inheritance.

**19** These are the names of the men: from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;

**20** from the tribe of the sons of Simeon, Shemuel son of Ammihud;

**21** from the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon;

**22** from the tribe of the sons of Dan a chief, Bukki son of Jogli;

**23** of the sons of Joseph: from the tribe of the sons of Manasseh a chief, Hanniel son of Ephod,

**24** and from the tribe of the sons of Ephraim a chief, Kemuel son of Shiphtan;

**25** from the tribe of the sons of Zebulun a chief, Elizaphan son of Parnach;

**26** from the tribe of the sons of Issachar a chief, Paltiel son of Azzan;

**27** from the tribe of the sons of Asher a chief, Ahihud son of Shelomi;

**28** from the tribe of the sons of Naphtali a chief, Pedahel son of Ammihud."

**29** These are the ones the LORD commanded to divide the inheritance for the sons of Israel in the land of Canaan.

## Notes

- **v2 "the land that will fall to you as an inheritance"** — *tippol lakhem*, **will fall to you**, the language of the lot (26:55; 33:54): the land is not taken by choice but falls to each tribe as the lot falls.
- **v3 "the Salt Sea"** — *yam ha-melach*, the name Genesis gives it: *the Valley of Siddim — that is the Salt Sea* (Genesis 14:3). The border begins at its southern end and ends at it again (v12), a closed line.
- **v4 "the Ascent of Akrabbim"** — *ma'aleh aqrabbim*, **the Scorpions' Ascent**, a pass named for the creature: *aqrav* is **scorpion**.
- **v5 "the Brook of Egypt"** — *nachlah Mitsrayim*, a *nachal*, a wadi, a streambed that runs in the rainy season; not *ye'or*, the word for the Nile.
- **v6 "the Great Sea"** — *ha-yam ha-gadol*, the Mediterranean, the western edge.
- **v7 "Mount Hor"** — *Hor ha-har*, **Hor the mountain**, the name of the mountain *at the edge of the land of Edom* where Aaron died (33:37–38). Here it marks the northern border, at the far end of the land from Edom.
- **v11 "the Sea of Chinnereth"** — *yam Kinneret*, the lake the Gospels call the Sea of Galilee (Matthew 4:18).
- **vv13–15 "the nine tribes and the half-tribe"** — the division east of the Jordan in chapter 32 is now fixed: Reuben, Gad and half of Manasseh have *taken* their inheritance, and the land described here is for the rest.
- **v19 "Caleb son of Jephunneh"** — the only one of the twelve scouts (13:6) named among the men who divide the land, with Joshua above them (v17). The two who *followed the LORD fully* (32:12) are the ones who hand it out.

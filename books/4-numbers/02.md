# Numbers 2

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**2** "The sons of Israel shall camp each by his own banner, under the signs of their fathers' houses. They shall camp facing the tent of meeting, at a distance, all around it.

## East: Judah

**3** Those camping on the east side, toward the sunrise, shall be the banner of the camp of Judah by their divisions. The chief of the sons of Judah is Nahshon son of Amminadab,

**4** and his army, those of them counted, seventy-four thousand six hundred.

**5** Those camping next to him shall be the tribe of Issachar. The chief of the sons of Issachar is Nethanel son of Zuar,

**6** and his army, those of it counted, fifty-four thousand four hundred.

**7** Then the tribe of Zebulun. The chief of the sons of Zebulun is Eliab son of Helon,

**8** and his army, those of it counted, fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

**9** All those counted for the camp of Judah, by their divisions, one hundred and eighty-six thousand four hundred. They shall set out first.

## South: Reuben

**10** The banner of the camp of Reuben shall be on the south, by their divisions. The chief of the sons of Reuben is Elizur son of Shedeur,

**11** and his army, those of it counted, forty-six thousand five hundred.

**12** Those camping next to him shall be the tribe of Simeon. The chief of the sons of Simeon is Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai,

**13** and his army, those of them counted, fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

**14** Then the tribe of Gad. The chief of the sons of Gad is Eliasaph son of Reuel,

**15** and his army, those of them counted, forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.

**16** All those counted for the camp of Reuben, by their divisions, one hundred and fifty-one thousand four hundred and fifty. They shall set out second.

## The Tent in the Middle

**17** The tent of meeting shall set out, the camp of the Levites, in the middle of the camps. As they camp, so they shall set out, each in his place, by their banners.

## West: Ephraim

**18** The banner of the camp of Ephraim shall be on the west, by their divisions. The chief of the sons of Ephraim is Elishama son of Ammihud,

**19** and his army, those of them counted, forty thousand five hundred.

**20** Next to him the tribe of Manasseh. The chief of the sons of Manasseh is Gamaliel son of Pedahzur,

**21** and his army, those of them counted, thirty-two thousand two hundred.

**22** Then the tribe of Benjamin. The chief of the sons of Benjamin is Abidan son of Gideoni,

**23** and his army, those of them counted, thirty-five thousand four hundred.

**24** All those counted for the camp of Ephraim, by their divisions, one hundred and eight thousand one hundred. They shall set out third.

## North: Dan

**25** The banner of the camp of Dan shall be on the north, by their divisions. The chief of the sons of Dan is Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai,

**26** and his army, those of them counted, sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

**27** Those camping next to him shall be the tribe of Asher. The chief of the sons of Asher is Pagiel son of Ocran,

**28** and his army, those of them counted, forty-one thousand five hundred.

**29** Then the tribe of Naphtali. The chief of the sons of Naphtali is Ahira son of Enan,

**30** and his army, those of them counted, fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**31** All those counted for the camp of Dan, one hundred and fifty-seven thousand six hundred. They shall set out last, by their banners."

**32** These are those of the sons of Israel counted by their fathers' houses. All those counted in the camps, by their divisions, were six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.

**33** But the Levites were not counted among the sons of Israel, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**34** The sons of Israel did according to all that the LORD had commanded Moses. So they camped by their banners, and so they set out, each by his clans, by his fathers' house.

## Notes

- **v2 "facing the tent of meeting, at a distance"** — *mi-neged saviv*, opposite and all around. Every tent in Israel faces the dwelling, and none comes close. Four camps of three tribes make a square, the Levites form an inner ring (1:53), and the tent of meeting stands in the middle (v17). When the camp moves, it keeps the same shape.
- **v3 "on the east side, toward the sunrise"** — *qedmah mizrachah*. The entrance to the court of the dwelling faces the sunrise (Exodus 27:13), and Judah camps in front of it and marches first. The largest tribe holds the door. East is also where the way back into Eden was guarded (Genesis 3:24).
- **v10, 18, 25 the four directions** — Hebrew names them by where a person stands facing the sunrise: *qedem*, **east**, is also *before, in front*; *teiman*, **south**, is from *yamin*, the right hand; the **west** is *yam*, the sea; the **north**, *tsafon*, is from a root meaning hidden. The camp is laid out as a man facing east would see the world.
- **v14 "Eliasaph son of Reuel"** — in 1:14 and three more times (7:42, 7:47, 10:20) his father is *Deuel*. The Hebrew letters *d* and *r* are nearly the same shape, and one of the two spellings is a copyist's slip. The Greek has *Reuel* in every place. The text stands as the Hebrew has it in each verse.
- **v17 "as they camp, so they shall set out"** — the tent of meeting marches in the middle, with two camps ahead of it and two behind. The order of the march is the order of the camp, and God's dwelling is at the center of both.
- **v32 "six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty"** — the total of chapter 1, now broken into four camps of 186,400, 151,450, 108,100 and 157,600. The four add up exactly. On *thousand* and *clan*, see the note on 1:16.

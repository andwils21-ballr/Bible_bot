# Numbers 32

## A Land for Livestock

**1** The sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad had a great deal of livestock, a very large amount. They saw the land of Jazer and the land of Gilead, and the place was a place for livestock.

**2** The sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben came and spoke to Moses, to Eleazar the priest and to the chiefs of the congregation, saying,

**3** "Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo and Beon —

**4** the land that the LORD struck down before the congregation of Israel — is a land for livestock, and your servants have livestock."

**5** They said, "If we have found favor in your eyes, let this land be given to your servants as a holding. Do not take us across the Jordan."

## Moses' Rebuke

**6** Moses said to the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben, "Shall your brothers go to war while you sit here?

**7** Why would you hold back the heart of the sons of Israel from crossing over into the land the LORD has given them?

**8** Your fathers did this when I sent them from Kadesh-barnea to see the land.

**9** They went up as far as the valley of Eshcol and saw the land, and they held back the heart of the sons of Israel from going into the land the LORD had given them.

**10** The anger of the LORD burned on that day, and He swore, saying,

**11** 'None of the men who came up out of Egypt, from twenty years old and upward, shall see the ground that I swore to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, because they have not followed Me fully,

**12** except Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite and Joshua son of Nun, because they have followed the LORD fully.'

**13** The anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He made them wander in the wilderness forty years, until the whole generation that had done evil in the eyes of the LORD was gone.

**14** Now you have risen up in your fathers' place, a brood of sinful men, to add still more to the burning anger of the LORD against Israel.

**15** If you turn away from following Him, He will again leave them in the wilderness, and you will destroy this whole people."

## The Agreement

**16** They came near to him and said, "We will build sheepfolds here for our livestock, and towns for our little ones.

**17** But we ourselves will arm and go quickly before the sons of Israel until we have brought them to their place. Our little ones will stay in the fortified towns because of the inhabitants of the land.

**18** We will not return to our homes until each of the sons of Israel has taken possession of his inheritance.

**19** We will not take an inheritance with them across the Jordan and beyond, because our inheritance has come to us on this side of the Jordan, to the east."

**20** Moses said to them, "If you will do this, if you will arm yourselves before the LORD for the war,

**21** and every armed man of you will cross the Jordan before the LORD until He has driven out His enemies from before Him,

**22** and the land is subdued before the LORD, then afterward you may return and be free of obligation to the LORD and to Israel, and this land shall be your holding before the LORD.

**23** But if you do not do this, you will have sinned against the LORD, and know that your sin will find you.

**24** Build towns for your little ones and folds for your flocks, and do what has come out of your mouth."

**25** The sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben said to Moses, "Your servants will do as my lord commands.

**26** Our little ones, our wives, our livestock and all our cattle will stay there in the towns of Gilead,

**27** but your servants, every man armed for war, will cross over before the LORD to the battle, as my lord says."

**28** So Moses gave command concerning them to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua son of Nun and to the heads of the fathers' houses of the tribes of the sons of Israel.

**29** Moses said to them, "If the sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben, every man armed for battle before the LORD, cross the Jordan with you, and the land is subdued before you, then you shall give them the land of Gilead as a holding.

**30** But if they do not cross over with you armed, they shall receive their holding among you in the land of Canaan."

**31** The sons of Gad and the sons of Reuben answered, saying, "What the LORD has said to your servants, we will do.

**32** We will cross over armed before the LORD into the land of Canaan, and the holding of our inheritance shall remain with us on this side of the Jordan."

## The Towns East of the Jordan

**33** Moses gave to them, to the sons of Gad, to the sons of Reuben and to half the tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph, the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan: the land with its towns within their borders, the towns of the land all around.

**34** The sons of Gad built Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer,

**35** Atroth-shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah,

**36** Beth-nimrah and Beth-haran, fortified towns, and folds for sheep.

**37** The sons of Reuben built Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim,

**38** Nebo and Baal-meon (their names were changed) and Sibmah. They gave other names to the towns they built.

**39** The sons of Machir son of Manasseh went to Gilead, captured it and drove out the Amorites who were in it.

**40** Moses gave Gilead to Machir son of Manasseh, and he settled there.

**41** Jair son of Manasseh went and captured their tent villages, and called them Havvoth-jair.

**42** Nobah went and captured Kenath and its villages, and called it Nobah, after his own name.

## Notes

- **v1 "the place was a place for livestock"** — *ve-hinneh ha-maqom meqom miqneh*: they looked, and what they saw was grazing land. The word *miqneh*, **livestock**, runs through the whole request (vv1, 4, 16, 26).
- **v7 "hold back the heart"** — *teni'un et lev*, from *heni*, **to restrain, to hold back**, the verb of a father forbidding his daughter's vow (30:5). Moses hears in the request the same thing the spies did at Kadesh: they *held back the heart of the sons of Israel* (v9).
- **vv8–13 the spies** — the story of chapters 13–14 told in brief: *the valley of Eshcol* (13:23), the oath that none of that generation would see the land *except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun* (14:30), and forty years of wandering (14:33–34). Here Caleb is called *the Kenizzite*, the first time Numbers gives his clan.
- **vv11–12 "they have not followed Me fully… they have followed the LORD fully"** — *millu acharai*, literally **they filled after Me**: to follow all the way. It is what the LORD said of Caleb at Kadesh, *he has followed Me fully* (14:24).
- **v14 "a brood of sinful men"** — *tarbut*, from *ravah*, **to multiply**: an increase, a new growth of the same stock. A new generation standing where the old one stood.
- **v17 "arm and go quickly"** — *nechalets chushim*: *chalats*, to equip for war (the word of 31:3), and *chush*, to hurry. They offer to go ahead of the army.
- **v22 "free of obligation to the LORD and to Israel"** — *neqiyyim*, **clear**, the word Abraham uses to his servant: *then you are clear from this oath of mine* (Genesis 24:8). Having fought, they owe nothing.
- **v23 "know that your sin will find you"** — *u-de'u chattatkhem asher timtsa etkhem*. The sin is the subject: not *you will be found out*, but the sin itself will come looking for them and find them.
- **v38 "their names were changed"** — *musabbot shem*, **turned as to name**. Nebo is also the name of a god (*Bel bows down, Nebo stoops*, Isaiah 46:1), and Baal-meon carries the name *Baal*. The verse does not say what the new names were.
- **v41 "Havvoth-jair"** — *chavvot Ya'ir*, usually taken as **the tent villages of Jair**; the English keeps the Hebrew as the name.

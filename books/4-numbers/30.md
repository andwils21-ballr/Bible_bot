# Numbers 30

## A Man's Vow

**1** Moses spoke to the heads of the tribes of the sons of Israel, saying, "This is what the LORD has commanded:

**2** When a man makes a vow to the LORD, or swears an oath to bind himself with a pledge, he shall not break his word. He shall do everything that has come out of his mouth.

## A Daughter's Vow

**3** When a woman makes a vow to the LORD and binds herself with a pledge while in her father's house in her youth,

**4** and her father hears her vow and the pledge by which she has bound herself, and her father says nothing to her, then all her vows shall stand, and every pledge by which she has bound herself shall stand.

**5** But if her father forbids her on the day he hears of it, none of her vows or pledges by which she has bound herself shall stand. The LORD will forgive her, because her father forbade her.

## A Wife's Vow

**6** If she marries a husband while her vows are on her, or a rash word from her lips by which she has bound herself,

**7** and her husband hears of it and says nothing to her on the day he hears, then her vows shall stand, and her pledges by which she has bound herself shall stand.

**8** But if on the day her husband hears of it he forbids her, he annuls the vow that is on her and the rash word from her lips by which she bound herself, and the LORD will forgive her.

**9** But the vow of a widow or of a divorced woman, everything by which she has bound herself, shall stand against her.

**10** If she vowed in her husband's house, or bound herself with a pledge under oath,

**11** and her husband heard of it and said nothing to her and did not forbid her, then all her vows shall stand, and every pledge by which she bound herself shall stand.

**12** But if her husband annuls them on the day he hears of them, nothing that has come out of her lips concerning her vows or the pledge on herself shall stand. Her husband has annulled them, and the LORD will forgive her.

**13** Every vow and every sworn pledge to afflict herself, her husband may let stand, and her husband may annul.

**14** But if her husband says nothing to her from day to day, he lets stand all her vows or all her pledges that are on her. He has let them stand, because he said nothing to her on the day he heard of them.

**15** But if he annuls them after he has heard of them, he shall carry her guilt."

**16** These are the statutes that the LORD commanded Moses, between a man and his wife, and between a father and his daughter while in her youth in her father's house.

## Notes

- **The whole chapter** — in the Hebrew and the Greek these verses are 30:2–17. The Hebrew's 30:1 is English 29:40.
- **v2 "he shall not break his word"** — *lo yachel devaro*, literally **he shall not profane his word**, from *chalal*, the verb for profaning what is holy. A spoken vow to the LORD is treated as a holy thing. *Everything that has come out of his mouth* is the principle of Deuteronomy 23:23: *what has gone out of your lips you shall keep and do*. Jephthah says it of his own vow: *I have opened my mouth to the LORD, and I cannot go back* (Judges 11:35).
- **v2 "a pledge"** — *issar*, a word found only in this chapter, from *asar*, **to bind, to tie**: an obligation a person lays on themselves, *to bind himself*. The chapter uses the root more than twenty times.
- **v5 "if her father forbids her"** — *heni*, **to hold back, to restrain**, the verb Moses uses in 32:7, *why would you hold back the heart of the sons of Israel?* A father or husband may cancel the vow only on the day he hears of it. Silence past that day lets the vow stand (v14).
- **v6 "a rash word from her lips"** — *mivta sefateha*, from *bata*, **to speak rashly**, the word of Leviticus 5:4 for someone who *swears rashly with his lips*, and of Psalm 106:33, where Moses *spoke rashly with his lips*.
- **v9 "the vow of a widow or of a divorced woman… shall stand"** — a woman with no father or husband over her is bound by her word exactly as a man is (v2).
- **v13 "to afflict herself"** — *le-annot nafesh*, the phrase of the Day of Atonement, *you shall afflict yourselves* (29:7), usually meaning fasting.
- **v15 "he shall carry her guilt"** — *ve-nasa et avonah*. A husband who lets a vow stand past the first day and then cancels it is the one who has broken it; the guilt passes from her to him. The Greek reads *he shall bear his sin*.

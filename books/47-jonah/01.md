# Jonah 1

## Jonah Flees

**1** The word of the LORD came to Jonah son of Amittai, saying,

**2** "Rise, go to Nineveh, the great city, and call out against it, for their evil has come up before Me."

**3** Jonah rose to flee to Tarshish, away from the presence of the LORD. He went down to Joppa and found a ship going to Tarshish. He paid its fare and went down into it, to go with them to Tarshish, away from the presence of the LORD.

## The Storm

**4** But the LORD hurled a great wind onto the sea, and there was a great storm on the sea, and the ship thought it would break apart.

**5** The mariners were afraid, and each cried out to his god, and they hurled the cargo that was in the ship into the sea to lighten it for them. But Jonah had gone down into the inner part of the vessel, and had lain down and fallen fast asleep.

**6** The captain came to him and said to him, "What are you doing asleep? Rise, call to your God! Perhaps God will give a thought to us, and we will not perish."

**7** They said to one another, "Come, let us cast lots, so that we may know on whose account this evil has come to us." They cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah.

**8** They said to him, "Tell us, please, you on whose account this evil has come to us: What is your work? Where do you come from? What is your country? From what people are you?"

**9** He said to them, "I am a Hebrew, and I fear the LORD, the God of the heavens, who made the sea and the dry ground."

**10** Then the men were greatly afraid, and said to him, "What is this you have done?" For the men knew that he was fleeing from the presence of the LORD, because he had told them.

**11** They said to him, "What shall we do to you, so that the sea will quiet down for us?" For the sea was growing more and more stormy.

**12** He said to them, "Pick me up and hurl me into the sea, and the sea will quiet down for you; for I know that it is because of me that this great storm has come upon you."

**13** The men rowed hard to bring the ship back to the dry ground, but they could not, for the sea was growing more and more stormy against them.

**14** Then they called to the LORD and said, "Please, LORD, do not let us perish for this man's life, and do not lay innocent blood on us; for You, LORD, have done as You pleased."

**15** They picked up Jonah and hurled him into the sea, and the sea ceased from its rage.

**16** Then the men greatly feared the LORD; they offered a sacrifice to the LORD and made vows.

**17** The LORD appointed a great fish to swallow Jonah, and Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights.

## Notes

- **v1 "Jonah son of Amittai"** — the prophet of Jeroboam's day, *His servant Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet, who was from Gath-hepher* (2 Kings 14:25), who foretold Israel's border restored. *Yonah* is the word **dove**, as in *He sent out the dove* (Genesis 8:8), and Hosea's *Ephraim has become like a dove, silly and without sense* (Hosea 7:11). *Amittai* has the letters of *emet*, **truth**. In the Hebrew text v17 is 2:1, and the Greek follows the Hebrew; the English numbering is followed.
- **v2 "Nineveh, the great city… their evil has come up before Me"** — *the great city* are the words of Genesis, where Asshur *built Nineveh… that is the great city* (Genesis 10:11–12). The evil that *has come up* is the outcry of Sodom, *her outcry that has come to Me* (Genesis 18:21); the Greek has *the cry of its evil has come up to Me*. *Gadol*, **great**, is the book's word: a great city, a great wind, a great storm (v4), great fear (vv10, 16), a great fish (v17).
- **vv3, 5 "away from the presence of the LORD… went down to Joppa… went down into it… had gone down"** — *mi-lifnei YHWH*, word for word **from before the face of the LORD**, the words of Cain, who *went out from before the face of the LORD* (Genesis 4:16). Jonah sets off for Tarshish instead, the land of *silver… brought from Tarshish* (Jeremiah 10:9). And at each step *va-yered*, **he went down**: to Joppa, the port of the cedar rafts (2 Chronicles 2:16), into the ship, into its inner part, and asleep, *va-yeradam*, a word that sounds like it. The descent goes on into the sea (2:6).
- **vv4–5 "the LORD hurled a great wind… the ship thought it would break apart… they hurled the cargo"** — *hetil*, **hurl**, four times: the LORD hurls the wind (v4), the mariners the cargo (v5), Jonah asks to be hurled (v12), and they hurl him (v15). *Chishvah le-hishaver*, word for word **the ship thought to be broken**: the ship is given a thought, as the captain hopes God will give one (v6). *Nirdam*, **fast asleep**, is Sisera's sleep, *He was fast asleep and exhausted* (Judges 4:21).
- **v6 "Rise, call to your God!"** — *qum qera*, the same two words the LORD spoke in v2, *Rise… call out*: a foreign captain says to the prophet what God said to him. *Yit'ashet*, **will give a thought**, is found only here in the Hebrew Bible. *Ve-lo noved*, **and we will not perish**, is said again by the sailors (v14) and by the king of Nineveh (3:9).
- **vv7–10 "on whose account this evil has come to us… I fear the LORD… What is this you have done?"** — *ra'ah*, **evil**, is the word of v2: Nineveh's evil came up before God, and now an evil comes down on the ship. The lot is the LORD's way of finding the one, as in *the tribe that the LORD takes* (Joshua 7:14). The four questions come in a rush; Jonah answers the last, and adds whom he fears. *Ivri*, **a Hebrew**: the Greek has *a servant of the Lord*, *eved*, nearly the same letters. *Who made the sea and the dry ground* names the God of *let the dry ground be seen* (Genesis 1:9), and Jonah has fled from Him onto the sea. *What is this you have done?* is God's question to the woman in the garden (Genesis 3:13), now asked by the sailors.
- **vv10, 16 "the men were greatly afraid… the men greatly feared the LORD"** — *va-yir'u… yir'ah gedolah*, word for word **they feared a great fear**, twice, and the doubling is the plain *greatly* in English. Their fear grows: they *were afraid* of the storm (v5); they *greatly* feared when they learned whose storm it was (v10); and last they *greatly feared the LORD* (v16), with sacrifice and vows. Jonah says *I fear the LORD* (v9); the sailors are the ones who do.
- **vv13–15 "The men rowed hard… do not lay innocent blood on us… the sea ceased from its rage"** — *va-yachteru*, **rowed hard**, word for word **dug**, the verb of *dig through the wall* (Ezekiel 8:8). *Dam naqi*, **innocent blood**, the law's word (Deuteronomy 19:10). *Ka'asher chafatsta asita*, **You have done as You pleased**, is what the psalm says of the LORD, *He does whatever He pleases* (Psalm 115:3). *Za'af*, **rage**, is the word of *I will bear the rage of the LORD* (Micah 7:9). The sailors' story is Psalm 107's, *Some went down to the sea in ships… He made the storm a whisper* (Psalm 107:23, 29).
- **v17 "The LORD appointed a great fish… three days and three nights"** — *va-yeman*, **appointed**, God's verb four times in the book: the fish, the plant (4:6), the worm (4:7) and the east wind (4:8). *Dag*, **fish**; the Greek has *ketos*, a great sea creature, and so does Matthew, in Jesus' words: *For just as Jonah was in the belly of the sea creature three days and three nights, so the Son of Man will be in the heart of the earth three days and three nights* (Matthew 12:40).

# Jonah 3

## Jonah Goes to Nineveh

**1** The word of the LORD came to Jonah a second time, saying,

**2** "Rise, go to Nineveh, the great city, and call out to it the message that I am telling you."

**3** Jonah rose and went to Nineveh, according to the word of the LORD. Nineveh was a great city to God, a walk of three days.

**4** Jonah began to go into the city, a walk of one day, and he called out and said, "Forty more days, and Nineveh is overturned!"

## Nineveh Turns

**5** The people of Nineveh believed in God; they proclaimed a fast and put on sackcloth, from the greatest of them to the least of them.

**6** When the word reached the king of Nineveh, he rose from his throne, took off his cloak, covered himself with sackcloth, and sat on the ashes.

**7** He had it proclaimed in Nineveh: "By the decree of the king and his nobles: Let neither man nor beast, herd nor flock, taste anything; let them not graze, and let them not drink water.

**8** Let man and beast be covered with sackcloth, and let them call to God with all their strength; let them turn, each from his evil way and from the violence that is in their hands.

**9** Who knows? God may turn and relent, and turn from His burning anger, and we will not perish."

**10** God saw their deeds, that they turned from their evil way; and God relented of the evil He had said He would do to them, and He did not do it.

## Notes

- **v3 "Nineveh was a great city to God, a walk of three days"** — *ir gedolah le-Elohim*, word for word **a great city to God**. *To God* or *of God* can make a word as great as it can be, as in *a prince of God* (Genesis 23:6) and *the mountains of God* (Psalm 36:6), so the phrase may mean **an exceedingly great city**; it may also mean great in God's eyes, which is where the book ends (4:11). *A walk of three days* is the time it takes to go through it, and v4 has Jonah one day in.
- **v4 "Forty more days, and Nineveh is overturned!"** — five words in the Hebrew, *od arba'im yom ve-Nineveh nehpakhet*, the whole of what Jonah is reported to say. *Hafakh*, **overturn**, is Sodom's word, *He overturned those cities* (Genesis 19:25), *the cities that the LORD overturned without relenting* (Jeremiah 20:16). It also means **be turned** into something else, as the water of the Nile *will turn to blood* (Exodus 7:17), so the five words can be read both ways: Nineveh overthrown, or Nineveh changed. It is changed (v10). The Greek has *Three more days*.

# Jonah 3

## Jonah Goes to Nineveh

**1** The word of the LORD came to Jonah a second time, saying,

**2** "Rise, go to Nineveh, the great city, and call out to it the message that I am telling you."

**3** Jonah rose and went to Nineveh, according to the word of the LORD. Nineveh was a great city to God, a walk of three days.

**4** Jonah began to go into the city, a walk of one day, and he called out and said, "Forty more days, and Nineveh is overturned!"

## Nineveh Turns

**5** The people of Nineveh believed in God; they proclaimed a fast and put on sackcloth, from the greatest of them to the least of them.

**6** When the word reached the king of Nineveh, he rose from his throne, took off his cloak, covered himself with sackcloth, and sat on the ashes.

**7** He had it proclaimed in Nineveh: "By the decree of the king and his nobles: Let neither man nor beast, herd nor flock, taste anything; let them not graze, and let them not drink water.

**8** Let man and beast be covered with sackcloth, and let them call to God with all their strength; let them turn, each from his evil way and from the violence that is in their hands.

**9** Who knows? God may turn and relent, and turn from His burning anger, and we will not perish."

**10** God saw their deeds, that they turned from their evil way; and God relented of the evil He had said He would do to them, and He did not do it.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "a second time… call out to it the message that I am telling you"** — the call of 1:2 again, *Rise, go to Nineveh, the great city*, word for word, and only the end is new: there it was *call out against it*, here *call out to it* the message, *u-qera eleha et ha-qeri'ah*, word for word **call to it the call**.
- **v3 "Nineveh was a great city to God, a walk of three days"** — *ir gedolah le-Elohim*, word for word **a great city to God**. *To God* or *of God* can make a word as great as it can be, as in *a prince of God* (Genesis 23:6) and *the mountains of God* (Psalm 36:6), so the phrase may mean **an exceedingly great city**; it may also mean great in God's eyes, which is where the book ends (4:11). *A walk of three days* is the time it takes to go through it, and v4 has Jonah one day in.
- **v4 "Forty more days, and Nineveh is overturned!"** — five words in the Hebrew, *od arba'im yom ve-Nineveh nehpakhet*, the whole of what Jonah is reported to say. *Hafakh*, **overturn**, is Sodom's word, *He overturned those cities* (Genesis 19:25), *the cities that the LORD overturned without relenting* (Jeremiah 20:16). It also means **be turned** into something else, as the water of the Nile *will turn to blood* (Exodus 7:17), so the five words can be read both ways: Nineveh overthrown, or Nineveh changed. It is changed (v10). The Greek has *Three more days*.
- **v5 "The people of Nineveh believed in God; they proclaimed a fast"** — *va-ya'aminu be-Elohim*, **believed in God**, the words said of Israel at the sea, *they believed in the LORD and in Moses His servant* (Exodus 14:31), and the verb of Abraham, *He trusted in the LORD* (Genesis 15:6). *Qir'u tsom*, **proclaimed a fast**, as in *Proclaim a fast* (1 Kings 21:9). They do it before the king has spoken, *from the greatest of them to the least of them*, the order of Jeremiah's *from the least of them to the greatest* turned round (Jeremiah 6:13).
- **v6 "he rose from his throne, took off his cloak… and sat on the ashes"** — four acts: he rises from the throne, puts off the *adderet*, the **cloak** that Elijah *cast… on* Elisha (1 Kings 19:19), puts on sackcloth, and sits where Job sat, *among the ashes* (Job 2:8).
- **vv7–8 "By the decree of the king… taste anything… Let man and beast be covered with sackcloth"** — *mi-ta'am ha-melekh*, **by the decree of the king**, uses *ta'am*, the word of royal decrees, as in *You, O king, have made a decree* (Daniel 3:10, where it is Aramaic); and the decree is that no one *yit'amu*, **taste**, from the same root. Man and beast together, as in *man and beast You save, LORD* (Psalm 36:6). *Chamas*, **violence**, is the sin of the flood, *the earth was filled with violence* (Genesis 6:11). *Turn, each from his evil way* is Jeremiah's call to Judah, *Return, each of you, from his evil way* (Jeremiah 18:11), here obeyed by Assyria.
- **v9 "Who knows? God may turn and relent, and turn from His burning anger"** — *mi yode'a yashuv ve-nicham*, the words of Joel 2:14, and the English follows Joel's. *Ve-shav me-charon appo*, **turn from His burning anger**, is Moses' prayer after the calf, *Turn from Your burning anger and relent of the evil against Your people* (Exodus 32:12). *Ve-lo noved*, **and we will not perish**, is the captain's hope (1:6).
- **v10 "God relented of the evil He had said He would do to them, and He did not do it"** — the answer to Moses' prayer, in its words: *The LORD relented of the evil He had said He would do to His people* (Exodus 32:14). *Ra'ah*, **evil**, is the book's word on both sides: Nineveh turns *from its evil way*, and God relents *of the evil* He had said. Jeremiah gives it as a rule, *if that nation… turns from its evil, I will relent of the disaster that I intended to do to it* (Jeremiah 18:8).

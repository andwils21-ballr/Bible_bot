# Jeremiah 40

**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD after Nebuzaradan the chief of the slaughterers had let him go from Ramah, when he had taken him bound in chains among all the exiles of Jerusalem and Judah who were being carried into exile to Babylon.

**2** The chief of the slaughterers took Jeremiah and said to him, "The LORD your God pronounced this disaster against this place.

**3** The LORD has brought it about and has done as He said, because you sinned against the LORD and did not obey His voice; so this thing has come upon you.

**4** But now, I have freed you today from the chains on your hands. If it seems good to you to come with me to Babylon, come, and I will set my eyes on you; but if it seems bad to you to come with me to Babylon, then stay. See, all the land is before you; wherever it seems good and right to you to go, go there."

**5** While Jeremiah had not yet turned to go, he said, "Go back to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon has appointed over the cities of Judah, and stay with him among the people; or go wherever it seems right to you to go." Then the chief of the slaughterers gave him an allowance and a gift, and let him go.

**6** So Jeremiah went to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah and stayed with him among the people who were left in the land.

## Gedaliah at Mizpah

**7** When all the commanders of the forces who were in the open country, they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam over the land, and had committed to him men, women and children, some of the poorest of the land who had not been carried into exile to Babylon,

**8** they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah: Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan and Jonathan the sons of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth, the sons of Ephai the Netophathite, and Jezaniah son of the Maacathite, they and their men.

**9** Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, swore to them and to their men, saying, "Do not be afraid to serve the Chaldeans. Live in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it will go well with you.

**10** As for me, I will stay at Mizpah to stand before the Chaldeans who come to us; but you, gather wine and summer fruit and oil, and put them in your jars, and live in your cities that you have taken."

**11** Likewise all the Jews who were in Moab, among the Ammonites, in Edom and in all the lands heard that the king of Babylon had left a remnant for Judah, and that he had appointed over them Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan.

**12** Then all the Jews returned from all the places to which they had been driven, and came to the land of Judah, to Gedaliah at Mizpah; and they gathered wine and summer fruit in great abundance.

**13** Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces who were in the open country came to Gedaliah at Mizpah

**14** and said to him, "Do you know that Baalis king of the Ammonites has sent Ishmael son of Nethaniah to take your life?" But Gedaliah son of Ahikam did not believe them.

**15** Then Johanan son of Kareah spoke secretly to Gedaliah at Mizpah, saying, "Let me go, please, and strike down Ishmael son of Nethaniah, and no one will know. Why should he take your life, so that all the Jews who are gathered to you are scattered, and the remnant of Judah perishes?"

**16** But Gedaliah son of Ahikam said to Johanan son of Kareah, "Do not do this thing, for you are telling a lie about Ishmael."

## Notes

- **vv1–6 "The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD after Nebuzaradan… had let him go from Ramah"** — the heading of a word from the LORD, but none follows in these verses, and the next word of the LORD to Jeremiah comes at 42:7. The release is told another way at 39:14, from the court of the guard straight to Gedaliah; here he is found in chains among the captives at Ramah, where Rachel weeps for her children (31:15).
- **vv2–3 "The LORD your God pronounced this disaster against this place… because you sinned against the LORD and did not obey His voice"** — a Babylonian officer speaks the prophet's own message, in the words of the confession of 3:25, *for we have sinned against the LORD our God… and we have not obeyed the voice of the LORD our God*. *You* in v3 is plural: the people, not Jeremiah.
- **v4 "I will set my eyes on you… See, all the land is before you; wherever it seems good and right to you to go, go there"** — the order of 39:12 kept. The words are Abram's to Lot, *Is not all the land before you?* (Genesis 13:9), and the foreign king Abimelech's to Abraham, *Here is my land before you. Settle where it is good in your eyes* (Genesis 20:15).
- **v5 "While Jeremiah had not yet turned to go, he said, 'Go back to Gedaliah'"** — the Hebrew, *ve-odennu lo yashuv*, "and he still not turning back," is obscure, and has no *he said*; the English follows its sense. *Yashuv*, "turn back," and *shuvah*, "go back," are the same verb. The Greek has *But if not, run off; go back to Gedaliah*. *Aruchah*, "allowance," is the word for Jehoiachin's daily portion from the king of Babylon, *For his allowance, a continual allowance was given him* (2 Kings 25:30).
- **vv7–9 "the commanders of the forces… Do not be afraid to serve the Chaldeans. Live in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it will go well with you"** — Kings tells it more briefly (2 Kings 25:22–24), with *Johanan son of Kareah* and no Jonathan, *Seraiah son of Tanhumeth the Netophathite and Jaazaniah son of the Maacathite*, and *Do not be afraid of the servants of the Chaldeans*. The Greek also has no Jonathan, and *the servants of the Chaldeans*. Gedaliah gives the counsel of 27:17, *Serve the king of Babylon, and live*.
- **vv11–12 "all the Jews returned from all the places to which they had been driven"** — a small return, in the words of the promise, *I Myself will gather the remnant of My flock out of all the lands where I have driven them* (23:3).
- **vv14–16 "Baalis king of the Ammonites has sent Ishmael son of Nethaniah to take your life… you are telling a lie about Ishmael"** — *sheqer*, "lie," Jeremiah's answer to the guard who called him a deserter (37:14). Johanan's warning was true: Ishmael *came with ten men, and they struck down Gedaliah, and he died* (2 Kings 25:25).

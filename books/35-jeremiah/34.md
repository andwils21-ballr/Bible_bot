# Jeremiah 34

**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD, when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and all his army, all the kingdoms of the earth under his rule and all the peoples were fighting against Jerusalem and all its cities:

**2** This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Go and speak to Zedekiah king of Judah, and say to him: This is what the LORD says: I am about to give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire.

**3** You will not escape from his hand, but will surely be captured and given into his hand; your eyes will see the eyes of the king of Babylon, and he will speak with you mouth to mouth, and you will go to Babylon.

**4** Yet hear the word of the LORD, Zedekiah king of Judah: This is what the LORD says concerning you: You will not die by the sword.

**5** You will die in peace; and as they burned for your fathers, the former kings who were before you, so they will burn for you, and lament for you, "Alas, lord!" For I Myself have spoken the word, declares the LORD.

**6** Then Jeremiah the prophet spoke all these words to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem,

**7** while the army of the king of Babylon was fighting against Jerusalem and against all the cities of Judah that were left, against Lachish and Azekah; for these were the only fortified cities left among the cities of Judah.

## The Slaves Set Free

**8** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD, after King Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people in Jerusalem to proclaim liberty to them:

**9** that each should set free his male servant and his female servant, a Hebrew man or a Hebrew woman, so that no one should keep a fellow Jew in servitude.

**10** All the princes and all the people who had entered into the covenant obeyed: each would set free his male servant and his female servant, so as not to keep them in servitude any longer. They obeyed and let them go.

**11** But afterward they turned around and took back the male and female servants whom they had set free, and brought them into subjection as male and female servants.

**12** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying,

**13** This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I Myself made a covenant with your fathers on the day I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slaves, saying,

**14** "At the end of seven years each of you shall set free his brother, the Hebrew who has been sold to you and has served you six years; you shall set him free from you." But your fathers did not listen to Me or incline their ear.

**15** You yourselves had turned today and done what is right in My eyes, proclaiming liberty each to his neighbor, and you had made a covenant before Me in the house that is called by My name;

**16** but then you turned back and profaned My name, and each of you took back his male servant and his female servant whom you had set free to go where they wished, and you brought them into subjection to be your male and female servants.

**17** Therefore this is what the LORD says: You have not obeyed Me by proclaiming liberty each to his brother and each to his neighbor. I am about to proclaim liberty for you, declares the LORD — to the sword, to pestilence and to famine; and I will make you a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth.

**18** I will make the men who transgressed My covenant, who did not keep the words of the covenant that they made before Me, like the calf that they cut in two and passed between its halves —

**19** the princes of Judah and the princes of Jerusalem, the eunuchs, the priests and all the people of the land who passed between the halves of the calf.

**20** I will give them into the hand of their enemies and into the hand of those who seek their life, and their corpses will be food for the birds of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth.

**21** Zedekiah king of Judah and his princes I will give into the hand of their enemies, into the hand of those who seek their life, and into the hand of the army of the king of Babylon, which has withdrawn from you.

**22** I am about to give the command, declares the LORD, and I will bring them back to this city; they will fight against it, take it and burn it with fire; and I will make the cities of Judah a desolation, without inhabitant.

## Notes

- **vv8–10 "to proclaim liberty"** — *deror*, the word of the jubilee, *proclaim liberty in the land to all who live in it* (Leviticus 25:10), and of Isaiah's anointed one, *to proclaim liberty to the captives* (Isaiah 61:1).
- **vv18–20 "the calf that they cut in two and passed between its halves"** — the rite of the covenant with Abram, who *cut them in two through the middle, and set each piece opposite its fellow* (*bitro*, Genesis 15:10); the fire *passed between those pieces* (15:17). *Beter*, "half," is found only there and here. *Ha-overim et beriti*, "who transgressed My covenant," and *va-ya'avru*, "they passed," are the same verb: they crossed between the halves, and then crossed the covenant. *Ve-natatti et ha-anashim… ha-egel*, "I will make the men… the calf," has no word for *like*: the verb takes the men and the calf as its two objects, as in *father of a roar of nations I have made you* (Genesis 17:5, *netattikha*). Read so, what was done to the calf is done to them, and *their corpses will be food for the birds* (v20).

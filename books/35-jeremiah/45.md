# Jeremiah 45

**1** The word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah, when he wrote these words in a book at Jeremiah's dictation, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah:

**2** This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says to you, Baruch:

**3** You said, "Woe is me, for the LORD has added sorrow to my pain! I am weary with my sighing, and I find no rest."

**4** This is what you shall say to him: This is what the LORD says: What I have built I am overthrowing, and what I have planted I am uprooting — that is, the whole land.

**5** As for you, are you seeking great things for yourself? Do not seek them; for I am about to bring disaster on all flesh, declares the LORD, but I will give you your life as plunder in all the places where you go.

## Notes

- **v1 "when he wrote these words in a book at Jeremiah's dictation, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim"** — the year and the scroll of 36:1–4. The word belongs to Baruch's first writing, though it stands here after the flight to Egypt, where Baruch was last named (43:6).
- **v3 "Woe is me, for the LORD has added sorrow to my pain! I am weary with my sighing"** — the words of the psalm, *I am weary with my sighing* (Psalm 6:6), and of the prophet's own laments, *Woe is me, my mother, that you bore me* (15:10), and *to see toil and sorrow* (20:18). The scribe complains in his master's words.
- **v4 "What I have built I am overthrowing, and what I have planted I am uprooting"** — the verbs of Jeremiah's call (1:10) and of the promise *I will build them up and not overthrow them; I will plant them and not uproot them* (24:6; 42:10), here turned on what the LORD Himself has made. The Greek has no *that is, the whole land*.
- **v5 "are you seeking great things for yourself?… I will give you your life as plunder"** — *gedolot*, "great things," what the LORD promised to show the prophet, *great and inaccessible things that you have not known* (33:3); the scribe is to seek nothing great for himself. His life *as plunder* is the promise made to all who went out to the Chaldeans (21:9; 38:2) and to Ebed-melech (39:18).

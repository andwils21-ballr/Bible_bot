# Jeremiah 18

**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:

**2** "Arise, and go down to the potter's house, and there I will let you hear My words."

**3** So I went down to the potter's house, and he was working at the wheel.

**4** Whenever the vessel he was making of clay was spoiled in the potter's hand, he would make it again into another vessel, as it seemed good to the potter to make.

**5** Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**6** "Can I not do with you as this potter has done, house of Israel? declares the LORD. Like the clay in the potter's hand, so are you in My hand, house of Israel.

**7** At one moment I may speak concerning a nation or a kingdom, to uproot it, to pull it down and to destroy it;

**8** but if that nation, against which I have spoken, turns from its evil, I will relent of the disaster that I intended to do to it.

**9** At another moment I may speak concerning a nation or a kingdom, to build and to plant it;

**10** but if it does what is evil in My eyes, not listening to My voice, I will relent of the good that I had said I would do for it.

**11** So now, say to the men of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem: Thus says the LORD: I am about to shape disaster against you and devise a plan against you. Return, each of you, from his evil way, and amend your ways and your deeds."

**12** But they say, "It is hopeless! We will follow our own plans, and each of us will act according to the stubbornness of his evil heart."

**13** Therefore thus says the LORD:  
Ask among the nations:  
Who has heard such things?  
The virgin Israel  
has done a very horrible thing.

**14** Does the snow of Lebanon  
leave the rock of the field?  
Do the cold flowing waters from afar  
run dry?

**15** But My people have forgotten Me;  
they burn incense to emptiness;  
they have been made to stumble in their ways,  
in the ancient paths,  
to walk in side tracks,  
on a road not built up,

**16** making their land a horror,  
a thing to be hissed at forever;  
everyone who passes by it is horrified  
and shakes his head.

**17** Like the east wind  
I will scatter them before the enemy;  
I will show them My back and not My face  
in the day of their disaster.

**18** Then they said, "Come, let us devise plans against Jeremiah, for the law will not perish from the priest, nor counsel from the wise, nor the word from the prophet. Come, let us strike him with the tongue, and let us pay no attention to any of his words."

**19** Pay attention to me, LORD,  
and listen to the voice of those who contend with me.

**20** Should evil be repaid for good?  
Yet they have dug a pit for my life.  
Remember how I stood before You  
to speak good for them,  
to turn Your wrath away from them.

**21** Therefore give their children over to famine;  
hand them over to the power of the sword;  
let their wives be bereaved of children and widowed;  
let their men be slain by death,  
their young men struck down by the sword in battle.

**22** Let a cry be heard from their houses,  
when You suddenly bring raiders upon them;  
for they have dug a pit to take me,  
and hidden snares for my feet.

**23** Yet You, LORD, know  
all their plotting against me to kill me.  
Do not atone for their iniquity,  
and do not blot out their sin from before You;  
let them be overthrown before You;  
deal with them in the time of Your anger.

## Notes

- **v3 "he was working at the wheel"** — *al ha-ovnayim*, "on the two stones." The same word names the birthstool in Exodus 1:16, *look at the two stones*: the place where a vessel is shaped and the place where a child is born.
- **vv4, 6 "he would make it again into another vessel… Like the clay in the potter's hand, so are you in My hand"** — the picture of Isaiah, *we are the clay, and You are our potter* (Isaiah 64:8; also 29:16; 45:9). Paul asks, *Has the potter no right over the clay, to make from the same lump one vessel for honor and another for dishonor?* (Romans 9:21). Here the potter remakes a spoiled vessel, and vv7–10 say the clay's turning changes what he makes.
- **vv7–10 "to uproot it, to pull it down… to build and to plant… I will relent"** — the verbs of Jeremiah's call (1:10). When Nineveh turned, *God relented of the disaster that He had said He would do to them* (Jonah 3:10).
- **v11 "I am about to shape disaster against you"** — *yotser*, the word for the potter in vv2–6: the LORD speaks as the potter, and the vessel He is shaping is disaster. *Return* is *shuvu*, the call of chapter 3.
- **v15 "in the ancient paths"** — *shevilei olam*, the *ancient paths* the people were told to ask for (6:16, *netivot olam*); they stumble in them and leave them for side tracks.
- **v17 "I will show them My back and not My face"** — *oref ve-lo fanim ar'em*, the letters read with the vowels of "I will show them," as the Greek reads them (*I will show them*, though the Greek goes on *the day of their destruction*). The Hebrew vowels give *er'em*, "I will see them": *I will look on their backs and not their faces*. The people *turned their backs to Me, and not their faces* (2:27), and the LORD gives them the same. Moses was shown only the LORD's back: *you will see My back. But My face will not be seen* (Exodus 33:23).
- **v18 "the law will not perish from the priest, nor counsel from the wise, nor the word from the prophet"** — three offices, three gifts. Ezekiel turns the saying around: *they will seek a vision from a prophet, but the law will perish from the priest, and counsel from the elders* (Ezekiel 7:26).
- **vv20, 22 "Remember how I stood before You… to turn Your wrath away from them… they have dug a pit"** — what Moses did, who *stood in the breach before Him to turn away His wrath* (Psalm 106:23), and what the LORD would no longer accept even from him (15:1). *Shuchah*, "a pit," in both verses; in v22 it is the read form, and the written form is *sichah*.
- **v23 "Do not atone for their iniquity, and do not blot out their sin from before You"** — *al tekhapper*, the verb of atonement. Nehemiah prayed the same against his enemies: *Do not cover their iniquity, and do not let their sin be blotted out from before You* (Nehemiah 4:5).

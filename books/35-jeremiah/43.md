# Jeremiah 43

**1** And it came to pass, when Jeremiah had finished speaking to all the people all the words of the LORD their God, with which the LORD their God had sent him to them — all these words —

**2** that Azariah son of Hoshaiah and Johanan son of Kareah and all the arrogant men said to Jeremiah, "You are telling a lie! The LORD our God has not sent you to say, 'Do not go to Egypt to live there as guests.'

**3** Baruch son of Neriah is inciting you against us, to give us into the hand of the Chaldeans, so that they may put us to death or carry us into exile to Babylon."

**4** So Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces and all the people did not obey the voice of the LORD, to stay in the land of Judah.

**5** Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces took all the remnant of Judah who had returned from all the nations to which they had been driven, to live as guests in the land of Judah:

**6** the men, the women, the children, the king's daughters, and every person whom Nebuzaradan the chief of the slaughterers had left with Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan; also Jeremiah the prophet and Baruch son of Neriah.

**7** They went into the land of Egypt, for they did not obey the voice of the LORD, and they arrived at Tahpanhes.

## The Stones at Tahpanhes

**8** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes, saying,

**9** "Take some large stones in your hand and hide them in the mortar of the brickwork at the entrance of Pharaoh's house in Tahpanhes, in the sight of the men of Judah,

**10** and say to them: This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am about to send for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant, and I will set his throne above these stones that I have hidden, and he will spread his royal canopy over them.

**11** He will come and strike the land of Egypt:  
those who are for death, to death;  
those who are for captivity, to captivity;  
and those who are for the sword, to the sword.

**12** I will kindle a fire in the houses of the gods of Egypt, and he will burn them and carry them away captive. He will wrap himself in the land of Egypt as a shepherd wraps himself in his garment, and he will go out from there in peace.

**13** He will shatter the standing stones of Beth-shemesh, which is in the land of Egypt, and burn the houses of the gods of Egypt with fire."

---

## Notes

- **v9 "hide them in the mortar of the brickwork… in the sight of the men of Judah"** — *taman*, "hide," the verb of the loincloth hidden by the Euphrates (13:4–5): another sign carried out before the people. *Melet*, "mortar," is found only here, and *malben* elsewhere is the brick mold or kiln (2 Samuel 12:31; Nahum 3:14).

# Jeremiah 49

## Ammon

**1** Concerning the Ammonites. This is what the LORD says:  
Has Israel no sons?  
Has he no heir?  
Why then has Milcom dispossessed Gad,  
and his people settled in its cities?

**2** Therefore, days are coming, declares the LORD,  
when I will make the alarm of war heard  
against Rabbah of the Ammonites;  
it will become a desolate mound,  
and its villages will be burned with fire;  
then Israel will dispossess those who dispossessed him,  
says the LORD.

**3** Wail, Heshbon, for Ai is laid waste!  
Cry out, daughters of Rabbah!  
Put on sackcloth and lament,  
and run to and fro among the walls;  
for Milcom will go into exile,  
his priests and his princes together.

**4** Why do you boast of your valleys,  
your valley flowing with plenty,  
faithless daughter,  
who trusted in her treasures:  
"Who will come against me?"

**5** I am about to bring terror upon you,  
declares the Lord GOD of hosts,  
from all who are around you;  
you will be driven out, each straight ahead,  
with no one to gather the fugitives.

**6** But afterward I will restore the fortunes of the Ammonites,  
declares the LORD.

## Edom

**7** Concerning Edom. This is what the LORD of hosts says:  
Is there no longer wisdom in Teman?  
Has counsel perished from the prudent?  
Has their wisdom gone bad?

**8** Flee! Turn back! Hide in the depths,  
you who live in Dedan!  
For I will bring the calamity of Esau upon him,  
the time that I punish him.

**9** If grape-gatherers came to you,  
they would leave no gleanings;  
if thieves came by night,  
they would ruin all they wanted.

**10** But I Myself have stripped Esau bare;  
I have uncovered his hiding places,  
and he cannot hide.  
His seed is destroyed, his brothers and his neighbors,  
and he is no more.

**11** Leave your orphans; I will keep them alive;  
and let your widows trust in Me.

**12** For this is what the LORD says: Those who did not deserve to drink the cup must drink it; and will you go unpunished? You will not go unpunished, for you will drink it.

**13** For by Myself I have sworn, declares the LORD, that Bozrah will become a horror, a reproach, a waste and a curse, and all her cities will be everlasting ruins.

**14** I have heard a report from the LORD,  
and an envoy has been sent among the nations:  
"Gather yourselves and come against her,  
and rise up for battle!"

**15** For I will make you small among the nations,  
despised among men.

**16** The terror you cause has deceived you,  
and the arrogance of your heart,  
you who live in the clefts of the rock,  
who hold the height of the hill.  
Though you make your nest as high as the eagle's,  
I will bring you down from there,  
declares the LORD.

**17** Edom will become a horror;  
everyone who passes by it will be horrified  
and will hiss because of all its wounds.

**18** Like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighbors, says the LORD, no one will live there, and no son of man will live there as a guest.

**19** Like a lion coming up from the thickets of the Jordan  
against a pasture that never fails,  
I will suddenly make them run from it,  
and I will appoint over it whomever I choose.  
For who is like Me? Who will summon Me?  
What shepherd can stand before Me?

**20** Therefore hear the counsel of the LORD  
that He has planned against Edom,  
and the plans He has devised  
against the inhabitants of Teman:  
Surely the smallest of the flock will drag them away;  
surely their pasture will be horrified over them.

**21** At the sound of their fall the earth quakes;  
a cry — its sound is heard at the Sea of Reeds.

**22** One will go up and swoop like an eagle  
and spread his wings over Bozrah.  
The heart of Edom's warriors in that day  
will be like the heart of a woman in labor.

## Damascus

**23** Concerning Damascus.  
Hamath and Arpad are put to shame,  
for they have heard bad news;  
they melt away;  
there is anxiety on the sea;  
it cannot be still.

**24** Damascus has grown feeble; she has turned to flee,  
and panic has seized her;  
anguish and pains have taken hold of her,  
like a woman in labor.

**25** How is the city of praise not forsaken,  
the town of my joy?

**26** Therefore her young men will fall in her squares,  
and all the men of war will be silenced in that day,  
declares the LORD of hosts.

**27** I will kindle a fire in the wall of Damascus,  
and it will devour the palaces of Ben-hadad.

## Kedar and Hazor

**28** Concerning Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon struck down. This is what the LORD says:  
Rise up, go up against Kedar,  
and lay waste the sons of the east!

**29** Their tents and their flocks will be taken,  
their tent curtains and all their goods;  
their camels will be carried off for themselves,  
and they will call out against them,  
"Terror on every side!"

**30** Flee, wander far away,  
hide in the depths, you who live in Hazor,  
declares the LORD;  
for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has made a plan against you  
and devised a purpose against you.

**31** Rise up, go up against a nation at ease,  
that lives in security, declares the LORD,  
that has no gates or bars,  
that lives alone.

**32** Their camels will become booty,  
and their many herds plunder.  
I will scatter to every wind  
those who clip the hair at the sides of their head,  
and from every side of them  
I will bring their calamity,  
declares the LORD.

**33** Hazor will become a lair of jackals,  
a desolation forever;  
no one will live there,  
and no son of man will live there as a guest.

## Elam

**34** The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning Elam, at the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah:

**35** This is what the LORD of hosts says:  
I am about to break the bow of Elam,  
the first of their might.

**36** I will bring against Elam the four winds  
from the four ends of the heavens,  
and I will scatter them to all these winds;  
there will be no nation  
to which the outcasts of Elam will not come.

**37** I will dismay Elam before their enemies  
and before those who seek their life;  
I will bring disaster upon them,  
My fierce anger, declares the LORD;  
and I will send the sword after them  
until I have consumed them.

**38** I will set My throne in Elam  
and destroy from there king and princes,  
declares the LORD.

**39** And it shall come to pass in the end of days  
that I will restore the fortunes of Elam,  
declares the LORD.

## Notes

- **v4 "your valley flowing with plenty, faithless daughter"** — *zav*, "flowing," as the land *flowing with milk and honey*; the Hebrew has only *your valley flows*. *Ha-bat ha-shovevah*, "faithless daughter," is what the LORD called Israel (31:22). The Greek has *the plains of the Anakim*, as it did for the Philistines' valley (47:5).

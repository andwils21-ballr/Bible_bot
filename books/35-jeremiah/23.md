# Jeremiah 23

**1** Woe to the shepherds who destroy and scatter the sheep of My pasture! declares the LORD.

**2** Therefore thus says the LORD, the God of Israel, concerning the shepherds who tend My people: You have scattered My flock and driven them away, and you have not cared for them. I am about to punish you for the evil of your deeds, declares the LORD.

**3** I Myself will gather the remnant of My flock out of all the lands where I have driven them, and bring them back to their fold, and they will be fruitful and multiply.

**4** I will raise up shepherds over them who will tend them; they will fear no more, nor be dismayed, nor will any be missing, declares the LORD.

## The Righteous Branch

**5** Days are coming, declares the LORD,  
when I will raise up for David a righteous Branch;  
he will reign as king and act wisely,  
and do justice and righteousness in the land.

**6** In his days Judah will be saved,  
and Israel will dwell in safety;  
and this is the name by which he will be called:  
The LORD Is Our Righteousness.

**7** Therefore, days are coming, declares the LORD, when they will no longer say, "As the LORD lives, who brought up the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt,"

**8** but, "As the LORD lives, who brought up and led the seed of the house of Israel out of the land of the north and out of all the lands where He had driven them." Then they will dwell on their own soil.

## Concerning the Prophets

**9** Concerning the prophets:  
My heart is broken within me;  
all my bones shake;  
I am like a drunken man,  
like a man overcome by wine,  
because of the LORD  
and because of His holy words.

**10** For the land is full of adulterers;  
because of the curse the land mourns;  
the pastures of the wilderness are dried up.  
Their course is evil,  
and their might is not right.

**11** For both prophet and priest are godless;  
even in My house I have found their evil,  
declares the LORD.

**12** Therefore their way will be to them  
like slippery paths in the darkness;  
they will be driven on and fall in it;  
for I will bring disaster on them,  
the year of their punishment,  
declares the LORD.

**13** In the prophets of Samaria  
I saw an unseemly thing:  
they prophesied by Baal  
and led My people Israel astray.

**14** But in the prophets of Jerusalem  
I have seen a horrible thing:  
they commit adultery and walk in falsehood;  
they strengthen the hands of evildoers,  
so that no one turns from his evil.  
All of them have become like Sodom to Me,  
and its inhabitants like Gomorrah.

**15** Therefore thus says the LORD of hosts concerning the prophets:  
I am about to feed them with wormwood  
and give them poisoned water to drink;  
for from the prophets of Jerusalem  
godlessness has gone out into all the land.

**16** Thus says the LORD of hosts:  
Do not listen to the words of the prophets  
who prophesy to you;  
they are filling you with emptiness;  
they speak a vision of their own heart,  
not from the mouth of the LORD.

**17** They keep saying to those who despise Me,  
"The LORD has said, 'You will have peace'";  
and to everyone who walks in the stubbornness of his heart they say,  
"No disaster will come on you."

**18** For who has stood in the council of the LORD,  
to see and to hear His word?  
Who has paid attention to His word and listened?

**19** The storm of the LORD has gone out in wrath,  
a whirling tempest;  
it will whirl down on the head of the wicked.

**20** The anger of the LORD will not turn back  
until He has done and carried out  
the purposes of His heart.  
In the end of days  
you will understand it clearly.

**21** I did not send the prophets,  
yet they ran;  
I did not speak to them,  
yet they prophesied.

**22** But if they had stood in My council,  
they would have made My people hear My words,  
and turned them back from their evil way  
and from the evil of their deeds.

**23** Am I a God who is near, declares the LORD,  
and not a God far off?

**24** Can a man hide himself in secret places  
so that I cannot see him? declares the LORD.  
Do I not fill heaven and earth?  
declares the LORD.

**25** I have heard what the prophets have said who prophesy lies in My name, saying, "I have dreamed, I have dreamed!"

**26** How long will this be in the heart of the prophets who prophesy lies, who prophesy the deceit of their own hearts?

**27** They plan to make My people forget My name by their dreams that they tell one another, just as their fathers forgot My name for Baal.

**28** Let the prophet who has a dream tell his dream, but let him who has My word speak My word faithfully. What has straw to do with wheat? declares the LORD.

**29** Is not My word like fire, declares the LORD, and like a hammer that shatters rock?

**30** Therefore I am against the prophets, declares the LORD, who steal My words from one another.

**31** I am against the prophets, declares the LORD, who use their own tongues and say, "He declares."

**32** I am against those who prophesy lying dreams, declares the LORD, who tell them and lead My people astray by their lies and their recklessness, when I did not send them or command them. They do not profit this people at all, declares the LORD.

## The Burden of the LORD

**33** When this people, or a prophet or a priest, asks you, "What is the burden of the LORD?" you shall say to them, "You are the burden, and I will cast you off, declares the LORD."

**34** As for the prophet, the priest or the people who say, "The burden of the LORD," I will punish that man and his household.

**35** This is what you shall say, each to his neighbor and each to his brother: "What has the LORD answered?" and "What has the LORD spoken?"

**36** But you shall no longer mention "the burden of the LORD," for each man's own word will be his burden, and you have perverted the words of the living God, the LORD of hosts, our God.

**37** This is what you shall say to the prophet: "What has the LORD answered you?" and "What has the LORD spoken?"

**38** But if you say, "The burden of the LORD," then thus says the LORD: Because you have said this word, "The burden of the LORD," when I sent to you, saying, "You shall not say, 'The burden of the LORD,'"

**39** therefore I am about to lift you up and cast you off, you and the city that I gave to you and to your fathers, away from My presence.

**40** I will bring on you an everlasting reproach and an everlasting disgrace that will not be forgotten.

## Notes

- **vv2, 4 "you have not cared for them. I am about to punish you… nor will any be missing"** — the verb *paqad*, "to attend to," three times: the shepherds *lo feqadtem*, did not attend to the flock, so the LORD is *poqed*, attending to them for their evil; and under the new shepherds none *yippaqedu*, the word of the count, *not one man of us is missing* (Numbers 31:49). The gathered flock will *be fruitful and multiply*, the first blessing (Genesis 1:28).
- **v5 "a righteous Branch… and do justice and righteousness in the land"** — *tsemach*, what sprouts from the ground: Isaiah's *branch of the LORD* (Isaiah 4:2). Zechariah makes it a name, *My servant the Branch* (Zechariah 3:8), *a man whose name is Branch* (6:12). The king will do what was asked of the house of David, *Do justice and righteousness* (22:3), and what Josiah did (22:15).
- **v6 "The LORD Is Our Righteousness"** — *YHWH tsidqenu*. This run of oracles began with King Zedekiah, *Tsidqiyahu* (21:1), whose name is made of the same two words: *tsedeq* and the LORD's name. The coming king will be what that name says. The Hebrew accents join the LORD's name to *Our Righteousness*, as in *The LORD Is My Banner* (Exodus 17:15) and *The LORD Is Peace* (Judges 6:24). The Greek reads the words otherwise: *this is his name, which the Lord will call him: Iosedek*. At 33:16 the same name is given to Jerusalem.
- **vv7–8 "As the LORD lives, who brought up… out of the land of the north"** — 16:14–15 again, with small changes. *Where He had driven them* follows the Greek and the same oath in 16:15; the Hebrew here has *where I had driven them*, the LORD's own voice breaking into the people's oath.
- **v9 "all my bones shake"** — *rachafu*, a verb found elsewhere only in *the breath of God was hovering over the face of the waters* (Genesis 1:2) and the eagle *that hovers over its young* (Deuteronomy 32:11). In the prophet's body the same movement is a trembling.
- **vv13–14 "In the prophets of Samaria I saw an unseemly thing… in the prophets of Jerusalem I have seen a horrible thing"** — *tiflah*, the word of Job 1:22, *nor did he charge God with wrong*; *sha'arurah*, the word of 5:30, *an appalling and horrible thing*. Samaria's prophets *prophesied by Baal* (as 2:8), and Samaria fell; Jerusalem's are worse, as 3:11 said of the two kingdoms. Isaiah also called Jerusalem's rulers *rulers of Sodom* (Isaiah 1:10).
- **vv18, 22 "who has stood in the council of the LORD… if they had stood in My council"** — *sod*, the council where the LORD's word is heard. Micaiah, the prophet who stood against four hundred prophets, *saw the LORD sitting on His throne, and all the host of heaven standing beside Him* (1 Kings 22:19). Amos says the Lord GOD does nothing *without revealing His sod to His servants the prophets* (Amos 3:7). Eliphaz asks Job, *Have you listened in the council of God?* (Job 15:8).
- **v23 "Am I a God who is near… and not a God far off?"** — a question, and v24 gives its point: no one hides from Him, near or far. The Greek has no question, and denies the far: *I am a God who draws near, and not a God from far off*.
- **v31 "who use their own tongues and say, 'He declares'"** — *va-yin'amu ne'um*, "and declare a declaration," using *ne'um*, the word of every *declares the LORD* in this chapter. The formula comes off their tongues with no word from the LORD behind it.
- **vv33–40 "the burden of the LORD… You are the burden"** — *massa*, from *nasa*, "to lift, to carry": a load, and also a prophet's oracle, as in *The oracle concerning Babylon* (Isaiah 13:1). The people ask mockingly for the LORD's *burden*, and the answer turns the word on them. *You are the burden* is the Greek, *attem ha-massa*: the same Hebrew letters divided otherwise. The Hebrew vowels give *et mah massa*, "What burden?" In v39 *nasho* has the letters of *nasa*: *I will lift you up* (the Greek has *I take*). The Hebrew vowels give *nashiti*, from *nashah*, "to forget": *I will forget you completely*.

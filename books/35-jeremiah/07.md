# Jeremiah 7

**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:

**2** Stand in the gate of the LORD's house and proclaim there this word, and say: Hear the word of the LORD, all you of Judah who enter these gates to worship the LORD.

**3** This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Amend your ways and your deeds, and I will let you dwell in this place.

**4** Do not trust in these deceptive words: "The temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD is this!"

**5** For if you truly amend your ways and your deeds, if you truly do justice between a man and his neighbor,

**6** if you do not oppress the guest, the fatherless and the widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place, and do not go after other gods to your own harm,

**7** then I will let you dwell in this place, in the land that I gave to your fathers from of old and forever.

**8** But you are trusting in deceptive words that cannot profit.

**9** Will you steal, murder, commit adultery, swear falsely, burn incense to Baal and go after other gods that you have not known,

**10** and then come and stand before Me in this house, which is called by My name, and say, "We are delivered!" — only to go on doing all these detestable things?

**11** Has this house, which is called by My name, become a den of robbers in your eyes? I Myself have seen it, declares the LORD.

**12** Go now to My place that was in Shiloh, where I made My name dwell at first, and see what I did to it because of the wickedness of My people Israel.

**13** So now, because you have done all these things, declares the LORD, and I spoke to you again and again, but you did not listen, and I called you, but you did not answer,

**14** I will do to the house that is called by My name, in which you trust, and to the place that I gave to you and to your fathers, as I did to Shiloh.

**15** I will cast you out of My sight, as I cast out all your brothers, all the offspring of Ephraim.

## Do Not Pray for This People

**16** As for you, do not pray for this people, or lift up a cry or a prayer for them, or intercede with Me, for I will not hear you.

**17** Do you not see what they are doing in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?

**18** The children gather wood, the fathers kindle the fire, and the women knead dough to make cakes for the queen of heaven; and they pour out drink offerings to other gods, to provoke Me.

**19** Is it Me they provoke? declares the LORD. Is it not themselves, to their own shame?

**20** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: My anger and My wrath will be poured out on this place, on man and on beast, on the trees of the field and on the fruit of the ground; it will burn and not be quenched.

## Obey My Voice

**21** This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Add your burnt offerings to your sacrifices, and eat the meat.

**22** For I did not speak to your fathers or command them, in the day I brought them out of the land of Egypt, about burnt offerings and sacrifices.

**23** But this is what I commanded them: "Obey My voice, and I will be your God, and you will be My people; and walk in all the way that I command you, so that it may go well with you."

**24** But they did not listen or incline their ear; they walked in their own counsels, in the stubbornness of their evil heart, and went backward and not forward.

**25** From the day your fathers came out of the land of Egypt to this day, I have sent you all My servants the prophets, sending them again and again, day after day.

**26** Yet they did not listen to Me or incline their ear, but stiffened their neck; they did worse than their fathers.

**27** You shall speak all these words to them, but they will not listen to you; you shall call to them, but they will not answer you.

**28** You shall say to them: "This is the nation that did not obey the voice of the LORD its God and did not take correction; faithfulness has perished; it is cut off from their mouth."

## The Valley of Slaughter

**29** Cut off your crown and throw it away; raise a lament on the bare heights; for the LORD has rejected and forsaken the generation of His wrath.

**30** For the sons of Judah have done what is evil in My eyes, declares the LORD; they have set their loathsome things in the house that is called by My name, to defile it.

**31** They have built the high places of Topheth, which is in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, to burn their sons and their daughters in the fire, which I did not command, nor did it come into My mind.

**32** Therefore, days are coming, declares the LORD, when it will no longer be called Topheth or the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, but the Valley of Slaughter; for they will bury in Topheth because there is no room elsewhere.

**33** The corpses of this people will be food for the birds of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth, and there will be no one to frighten them away.

**34** I will silence from the cities of Judah and from the streets of Jerusalem the voice of mirth and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride; for the land will become a ruin.

## Notes

- **v9 "Will you steal, murder, commit adultery, swear falsely… and go after other gods"** — five of the Ten Words, given as bare verbs: *You shall not murder… commit adultery… steal… answer against your neighbor as a false witness* (Exodus 20:13–16), and *You shall have no other gods before My face* (20:3). Then the people come to the temple and say, *We are delivered!*
- **v11 "a den of robbers"** — *me'arat paritsim*, a cave where robbers hide between raids. Jesus, clearing the temple, joins Isaiah to this verse: *My house shall be called a house of prayer* (Isaiah 56:7), *but you are making it a den of robbers* (Matthew 21:13).
- **vv12, 14 "My place that was in Shiloh"** — where the tent of meeting stood when the land was first taken (Joshua 18:1), and from which the ark was carried into battle and taken (1 Samuel 4:11). The psalm says, *He abandoned the dwelling at Shiloh* (Psalm 78:60). The house in Jerusalem has no promise Shiloh did not have.
- **vv13, 25 "I spoke to you again and again… sending them again and again"** — *hashkem*, "rising early": Jeremiah's word for the LORD's persistence (7:13, 25; 11:7; 25:4; 26:5), and the word of the Chronicler's last chapter, *sent to them by His messengers, sending again and again, because He had compassion on His people* (2 Chronicles 36:15).
- **v16 "do not… intercede with Me"** — *al tifga bi*, the verb of the servant who *intercedes for the transgressors* (Isaiah 53:12). The prophet is forbidden to do what the prophet's office was for.
- **v18 "cakes for the queen of heaven"** — the whole family serves her: children, fathers, women. *Kavvanim*, "cakes," is found only here and in 44:19, where the women answer Jeremiah that they made them with their husbands' knowledge. The Greek has *the host of heaven*.
- **vv21–23 "Add your burnt offerings to your sacrifices, and eat the meat… Obey My voice"** — a burnt offering was wholly burned, *the priest shall turn all of it into smoke on the altar* (Leviticus 1:9); since they mean nothing, the people may as well eat them. What came first was the voice: *if you will truly listen to My voice and keep My covenant* (Exodus 19:5), and *I will be your God, and you will be My people* (Leviticus 26:12). Samuel said it to Saul: *To listen is better than sacrifice* (1 Samuel 15:22).
- **v29 "Cut off your crown and throw it away"** — *nezer*, the Nazirite's hair, *the crown of his God is on his head* (Numbers 6:7). Jerusalem, the city set apart, is told to shave off what marked her as the LORD's.
- **vv31–32 "Topheth, which is in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom"** — the place Josiah *defiled… so that no one might make his son or his daughter pass through the fire to Molech* (2 Kings 23:10). The valley's name, *ge-Hinnom*, becomes the Greek *geenna*, Gehenna: *liable to the fire of Gehenna* (Matthew 5:22).
- **v34 "the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride"** — silenced again in 16:9 and 25:10, and given back in 33:11, *the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the voice of those who say, Give thanks to the LORD of hosts, for the LORD is good*.

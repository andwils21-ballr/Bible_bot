# Jeremiah 30

**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:

**2** Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: Write in a book all the words that I have spoken to you.

**3** For days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will restore the fortunes of My people Israel and Judah, says the LORD, and I will bring them back to the land that I gave to their fathers, and they will possess it.

**4** These are the words that the LORD spoke concerning Israel and Judah:

**5** Thus says the LORD:  
We have heard a cry of trembling,  
of terror, and no peace.

**6** Ask now and see:  
can a male give birth?  
Why then do I see every man  
with his hands on his loins like a woman in labor,  
and every face turned pale?

**7** Alas! For that day is great;  
there is none like it.  
It is a time of distress for Jacob,  
yet out of it he will be saved.

**8** And it shall come to pass in that day, declares the LORD of hosts, that I will break his yoke from off your neck and tear off your bonds, and strangers will no longer make him their slave.

**9** They will serve the LORD their God and David their king, whom I will raise up for them.

**10** But you, do not fear, Jacob My servant, declares the LORD,  
and do not be dismayed, Israel;  
for I am about to save you from far away,  
and your seed from the land of their captivity.  
Jacob will return and have quiet and ease,  
with no one to make him afraid.

**11** For I am with you, declares the LORD, to save you.  
I will make a full end of all the nations  
among which I have scattered you,  
but of you I will not make a full end.  
I will correct you with justice,  
and I will not leave you unpunished.

**12** For thus says the LORD:  
Your hurt is incurable,  
your wound is grievous.

**13** There is no one to plead your cause;  
for your sore there is no healing,  
no new flesh for you.

**14** All your lovers have forgotten you;  
they do not seek you;  
for I have struck you as an enemy strikes,  
with the correction of a cruel one,  
because your guilt is great,  
because your sins are many.

**15** Why do you cry out over your hurt?  
Your pain is incurable.  
Because your guilt is great,  
because your sins are many,  
I have done these things to you.

**16** Therefore all who devour you will be devoured,  
and all your adversaries, every one of them, will go into captivity;  
those who plunder you will be plundered,  
and all who prey on you I will give up as prey.

**17** For I will make new flesh grow for you,  
and heal you of your wounds, declares the LORD,  
because they have called you an outcast:  
"It is Zion; no one seeks her."

**18** Thus says the LORD:  
I am about to restore the fortunes of the tents of Jacob,  
and have compassion on his dwellings;  
the city will be rebuilt on its mound,  
and the palace will stand in its rightful place.

**19** Out of them will come thanksgiving  
and the voice of revelers.  
I will multiply them, and they will not decrease;  
I will honor them, and they will not be made small.

**20** Their sons will be as of old,  
and their congregation will be established before Me,  
and I will punish all who oppress them.

**21** Their prince will be one of their own,  
and their ruler will come from their midst;  
I will bring him near, and he will approach Me;  
for who is he who would pledge his heart  
to approach Me? declares the LORD.

**22** You will be My people,  
and I will be your God.

**23** The storm of the LORD has gone out in wrath,  
a sweeping tempest;  
it will whirl down on the head of the wicked.

**24** The fierce anger of the LORD will not turn back  
until He has done and carried out  
the purposes of His heart.  
In the end of days  
you will understand it.

## Notes

- **vv6–7 "can a male give birth?… It is a time of distress for Jacob, yet out of it he will be saved"** — the men double over like women in labor. *Et tsarah*, "a time of distress," is Daniel's phrase for the last trouble, *a time of distress such as has not been since there was a nation* (Daniel 12:1). And the name is chosen: Jacob himself built an altar *to the God who answered me in the day of my distress* (Genesis 35:3).
- **vv8–9 "I will break his yoke from off your neck and tear off your bonds… David their king"** — the yoke Jeremiah wore and Hananiah broke too soon (27:2; 28:10–11): what Hananiah promised within two years the LORD will do on His own day. *Tear off your bonds* is what the LORD had done once already, *long ago I broke your yoke and tore off your bonds* (2:20). Hosea: *the sons of Israel will return and seek the LORD their God and David their king… in the end of days* (Hosea 3:5).
- **vv10–11 "do not fear, Jacob My servant… I will correct you with justice, and I will not leave you unpunished"** — the two verses stand again at 46:27–28; the Greek does not have them here. *Do not fear, Jacob My servant* is Isaiah's word (Isaiah 44:2). The prophet had prayed, *Correct me, LORD, but with justice* (10:24); *not leaving it unpunished* is part of the LORD's name proclaimed at Sinai (Exodus 34:7).
- **vv12–17 "Your hurt is incurable… For I will make new flesh grow for you"** — *anush*, the word of the prophet's own complaint, *my wound incurable* (15:18). *Arukhah*, "new flesh," is what he asked about: *Why then has the daughter of my people not been healed?* (8:22). The same verb runs from v13, *no new flesh for you* (*te'alah*), to v17, *I will make new flesh grow* (*a'aleh*). V16 begins *Therefore* where judgment is expected, and v17 gives the reason: *because they have called you an outcast*.
- **v18 "the city will be rebuilt on its mound"** — *tel*, the heap a ruined city leaves. The law made a town that turned to other gods *a heap forever; it shall not be built again* (Deuteronomy 13:16). This heap will be built again.
- **v21 "who is he who would pledge his heart to approach Me?"** — *arav*, to stand as a pledge, the word of Judah for Benjamin, *I myself will stand as the pledge for him* (Genesis 43:9). To come near was the priests' right: *the outsider who comes near shall be put to death* (Numbers 18:7). The ruler is one the LORD Himself brings near.
- **vv23–24 "The storm of the LORD has gone out in wrath"** — 23:19–20 again, with three small changes: *a sweeping tempest* (*mitgorer*) for *a whirling tempest*, *fierce* added to *anger*, and *clearly* gone from *you will understand it*.

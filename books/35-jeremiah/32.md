# Jeremiah 32

**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, which was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar.

**2** At that time the army of the king of Babylon was besieging Jerusalem, and Jeremiah the prophet was shut up in the court of the guard, which was in the house of the king of Judah,

**3** where Zedekiah king of Judah had shut him up, saying, "Why do you prophesy, saying, 'Thus says the LORD: I am about to give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will take it;

**4** Zedekiah king of Judah will not escape from the hand of the Chaldeans, but will surely be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and will speak with him mouth to mouth and see him eye to eye;

**5** he will take Zedekiah to Babylon, and there he will stay until I attend to him, declares the LORD. Though you fight against the Chaldeans, you will not succeed'?"

**6** Jeremiah said: The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**7** "Hanamel son of Shallum your uncle is about to come to you and say, 'Buy my field that is at Anathoth, for the right of redemption is yours to buy it.'"

**8** Then Hanamel my uncle's son came to me in the court of the guard, as the LORD had said, and said to me, "Buy my field that is at Anathoth in the land of Benjamin, for the right of possession is yours, and the redemption is yours; buy it for yourself." Then I knew that this was the word of the LORD.

**9** So I bought the field at Anathoth from Hanamel my uncle's son, and weighed out the silver for him, seventeen shekels of silver.

**10** I signed the deed, sealed it, called witnesses, and weighed the silver on scales.

**11** Then I took the deed of purchase, both the sealed copy, containing the terms and conditions, and the open copy,

**12** and I gave the deed of purchase to Baruch son of Neriah son of Mahseiah, in the sight of Hanamel my uncle's son and of the witnesses who had signed the deed of purchase, and in the sight of all the Jews who were sitting in the court of the guard.

**13** In their sight I charged Baruch, saying,

**14** "Thus says the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Take these deeds, this deed of purchase, both the sealed copy and this open copy, and put them in an earthenware jar, so that they may last for many days.

**15** For thus says the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Houses and fields and vineyards will again be bought in this land."

## Jeremiah's Prayer

**16** After I had given the deed of purchase to Baruch son of Neriah, I prayed to the LORD, saying:

**17** "Ah, Lord GOD! It is You who made the heavens and the earth by Your great power and Your outstretched arm. Nothing is too wondrous for You.

**18** You show loyal love to thousands, and repay the guilt of fathers into the lap of their children after them, the great and mighty God, whose name is the LORD of hosts,

**19** great in counsel and mighty in deed, whose eyes are open on all the ways of the sons of men, to give to each man according to his ways and according to the fruit of his deeds.

**20** You set signs and wonders in the land of Egypt, and to this day in Israel and among mankind, and have made a name for Yourself, as it is this day.

**21** You brought Your people Israel out of the land of Egypt with signs and wonders, with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and with great terror,

**22** and You gave them this land, which You swore to their fathers to give them, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**23** They went in and took possession of it, but they did not obey Your voice or walk in Your law; they did nothing of all that You commanded them to do. So You have made all this disaster come upon them.

**24** The siege ramps have reached the city to take it, and the city is given into the hand of the Chaldeans who are fighting against it, because of the sword, famine and pestilence. What You spoke has come to pass, and You see it.

**25** Yet You have said to me, Lord GOD, 'Buy the field for money and call witnesses' — though the city is given into the hand of the Chaldeans."

## The Answer

**26** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying,

**27** "I am the LORD, the God of all flesh. Is anything too wondrous for Me?

**28** Therefore thus says the LORD: I am about to give this city into the hand of the Chaldeans and into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will take it.

**29** The Chaldeans who are fighting against this city will come and set this city on fire and burn it, with the houses on whose roofs they burned incense to Baal and poured out drink offerings to other gods, to provoke Me to anger.

**30** For the sons of Israel and the sons of Judah have done nothing but evil in My eyes from their youth; the sons of Israel have done nothing but provoke Me to anger with the work of their hands, declares the LORD.

**31** For this city has roused My anger and My wrath from the day they built it to this day, so that I must remove it from before My face,

**32** because of all the evil that the sons of Israel and the sons of Judah have done to provoke Me to anger, they, their kings, their princes, their priests and their prophets, the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**33** They have turned their backs to Me, and not their faces; though I taught them again and again, they would not listen and take correction.

**34** They set their loathsome things in the house that is called by My name, to defile it.

**35** They built the high places of Baal in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, to make their sons and their daughters pass over to Molech, which I did not command them, nor did it come into My mind, that they should do this detestable thing, to make Judah sin.

**36** But now thus says the LORD, the God of Israel, concerning this city of which you say, 'It is given into the hand of the king of Babylon by the sword, by famine and by pestilence':

**37** I am about to gather them from all the lands to which I have driven them in My anger and in My fury and in great wrath, and I will bring them back to this place and make them dwell in safety.

**38** They will be My people, and I will be their God.

**39** I will give them one heart and one way, to fear Me always, for their own good and the good of their children after them.

**40** I will make with them an everlasting covenant, that I will not turn back from them, from doing them good; and I will put the fear of Me in their hearts, so that they will not turn away from Me.

**41** I will rejoice over them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land in faithfulness, with all My heart and with all My soul.

**42** For thus says the LORD: Just as I have brought all this great disaster on this people, so I will bring on them all the good that I am promising them.

**43** Fields will be bought in this land of which you say, 'It is a desolation, without man or beast; it is given into the hand of the Chaldeans.'

**44** Men will buy fields for money, sign deeds, seal them and call witnesses, in the land of Benjamin, around Jerusalem, in the cities of Judah, in the cities of the hill country, in the cities of the lowland and in the cities of the Negev; for I will restore their fortunes, declares the LORD."

## Notes

- **vv1–5 "the tenth year of Zedekiah… shut up in the court of the guard… see him eye to eye"** — the siege had begun in Zedekiah's ninth year (2 Kings 25:1); how Jeremiah came to the court of the guard is told in 37:21. Zedekiah did see the king of Babylon eye to eye, and then *they slaughtered the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes, then put out the eyes of Zedekiah* (2 Kings 25:7).
- **vv7–8 "the right of redemption is yours to buy it"** — the law of the redeemer: *If your brother becomes poor and sells some of his holding, his redeemer who is closest to him shall come and redeem what his brother has sold* (Leviticus 25:25). Boaz took up the same right for Elimelech's field: *If you will redeem it, redeem it* (Ruth 4:4). Jeremiah is told to buy land the Babylonians already hold.
- **vv10–15 "the sealed copy… and the open copy… an earthenware jar, so that they may last for many days"** — an earthenware flask was broken in the Valley of Hinnom *as one breaks a potter's vessel, so that it can never be mended* (19:11). This earthenware jar keeps the deed whole for the day of v15, *Houses and fields and vineyards will again be bought in this land*.
- **v12 "Hanamel my uncle's son"** — so the Greek, as vv7–9 have it; the Hebrew here has *Chanam'el dodi*, "Hanamel my uncle."
- **vv17, 27 "Nothing is too wondrous for You… Is anything too wondrous for Me?"** — *pala*, the word the LORD spoke over Sarah's laughter, *Is any word too wondrous for the LORD?* (Genesis 18:14). Jeremiah says it in his prayer, which ends on a field bought in a city already lost (v25); the LORD gives his own words back to him as a question. *It is You who made the heavens and the earth by Your great power and Your outstretched arm* is 27:5 in the prophet's mouth.
- **v18 "You show loyal love to thousands, and repay the guilt of fathers into the lap of their children"** — the words of Sinai, *doing loyal love to thousands* (Exodus 20:6) and *calling the guilt of fathers to account on sons* (34:7); *into their lap* as in Isaiah 65:6.
- **vv33–35 "They have turned their backs to Me, and not their faces… their loathsome things in the house that is called by My name… nor did it come into My mind"** — the charges of 2:27 and 7:30–31, nearly word for word, now given as the reason the city falls. *To make their sons and their daughters pass over to Molech* is the act the law forbade (Leviticus 18:21).
- **vv37, 39 "in My anger and in My fury and in great wrath… one heart and one way"** — the words with which the LORD fought against the city (21:5) are the anger that scattered them, and the same LORD gathers them. For *one heart and one way* the Greek has *another way and another heart*: *echad*, "one," and *acher*, "another," differ by one letter (*d* and *r*).
- **vv40–41 "an everlasting covenant… with all My heart and with all My soul"** — the new covenant of 31:31–34 under another name. *With all My heart and with all My soul* is the great command, *You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul* (Deuteronomy 6:5), said by the LORD of Himself, and nowhere else.

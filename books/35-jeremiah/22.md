# Jeremiah 22

**1** Thus says the LORD: Go down to the house of the king of Judah, and speak this word there.

**2** Say: Hear the word of the LORD, king of Judah, you who sit on the throne of David — you, your servants and your people who enter by these gates.

**3** Thus says the LORD: Do justice and righteousness, and rescue the one who has been robbed from the hand of the oppressor. Do not wrong the guest, the fatherless and the widow, do no violence, and do not shed innocent blood in this place.

**4** For if you diligently do this, then there will enter by the gates of this house kings who sit on the throne of David, riding in chariots and on horses, the king and his servants and his people.

**5** But if you do not listen to these words, by Myself I have sworn, declares the LORD, that this house shall become a desolation.

**6** For thus says the LORD concerning the house of the king of Judah:  
You are Gilead to Me,  
the summit of Lebanon;  
yet I will surely make you a wilderness,  
cities that are not inhabited.

**7** I will consecrate destroyers against you,  
each with his weapons;  
they will cut down your choicest cedars  
and throw them on the fire.

**8** Many nations will pass by this city, and each will say to his neighbor, "Why has the LORD done this to this great city?"

**9** They will answer, "Because they forsook the covenant of the LORD their God, and bowed down to other gods and served them."

## Shallum

**10** Do not weep for the dead,  
or grieve for him;  
weep bitterly for the one who goes away,  
for he shall return no more  
nor see the land of his birth.

**11** For thus says the LORD concerning Shallum son of Josiah, king of Judah, who reigned in place of Josiah his father, and who went out from this place: He shall not return here again;

**12** he shall die in the place where they have carried him into exile, and he shall not see this land again.

## Jehoiakim

**13** Woe to him who builds his house without righteousness,  
and his upper rooms without justice;  
who makes his neighbor work for nothing  
and does not give him his wages;

**14** who says, "I will build myself a spacious house  
with airy upper rooms,"  
and cuts out windows for it,  
paneling it with cedar  
and painting it with vermilion.

**15** Are you a king  
because you compete in cedar?  
Did not your father eat and drink,  
and do justice and righteousness?  
Then it was well with him.

**16** He judged the cause of the poor and needy;  
then it was well.  
Is not this to know Me?  
declares the LORD.

**17** But your eyes and your heart  
are only on your own gain,  
on shedding innocent blood,  
and on practicing oppression and extortion.

**18** Therefore thus says the LORD concerning Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah:  
They shall not mourn for him,  
"Alas, my brother!" or "Alas, sister!"  
They shall not mourn for him,  
"Alas, lord!" or "Alas, his majesty!"

**19** He will be buried with the burial of a donkey,  
dragged off and thrown out  
beyond the gates of Jerusalem.

**20** Go up to Lebanon and cry out,  
and lift up your voice in Bashan;  
cry out from Abarim,  
for all your lovers are crushed.

**21** I spoke to you in your ease,  
but you said, "I will not listen."  
This has been your way from your youth:  
you have not obeyed My voice.

**22** The wind will shepherd all your shepherds,  
and your lovers will go into captivity;  
then you will be ashamed and disgraced  
because of all your evil.

**23** You who dwell in Lebanon,  
nested among the cedars,  
how you will be pitied when pangs come upon you,  
pain like a woman in labor!

## Coniah

**24** As I live, declares the LORD, though Coniah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, were the signet ring on My right hand, yet I would tear you off from there,

**25** and give you into the hand of those who seek your life, into the hand of those you dread, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and into the hand of the Chaldeans.

**26** I will hurl you and the mother who bore you into another land, where you were not born, and there you will die.

**27** But to the land they long to return to, there they shall not return.

**28** Is this man Coniah a despised, broken pot,  
a jar no one wants?  
Why are he and his seed hurled out  
and cast into a land they do not know?

**29** Land, land, land,  
hear the word of the LORD!

**30** Thus says the LORD:  
Write this man down as childless,  
a man who will not prosper in his days;  
for no man of his seed will prosper,  
sitting on the throne of David  
and ruling again in Judah.

## Notes

- **v5 "by Myself I have sworn… this house shall become a desolation"** — the oath the LORD swore to Abraham, *By Myself I have sworn* (Genesis 22:16), here sworn against the house of David's sons. *Le-chorbah*, "a desolation": Jesus said to Jerusalem, *Your house is left to you desolate* (Matthew 23:38).
- **vv6–7 "I will consecrate destroyers against you… they will cut down your choicest cedars"** — *qiddashti*, the word that made war a holy act, *Consecrate war against her!* (6:4). The palace is a mountain forest, Gilead and Lebanon, and its cedars are timber for the fire. In v14 the king is still paneling with cedar.
- **vv10–12 "Do not weep for the dead… weep bitterly for the one who goes away"** — the dead is Josiah, killed by Pharaoh Neco at Megiddo (2 Kings 23:29). The one who goes away is his son Shallum, *the fourth Shallum* (1 Chronicles 3:15), crowned as Jehoahaz and taken by Neco to Egypt, *and he came to Egypt and died there* (2 Kings 23:34). The verse grieves more for the son who lives on in exile than for the father who died.
- **vv13–16 "Woe to him who builds his house without righteousness… Is not this to know Me?"** — the king is Jehoiakim (v18). The law said, *The wages of a hired man shall not stay with you overnight* (Leviticus 19:13); he pays none. His father Josiah *ate and drank* like any man and did justice; that, not cedar, made him a king. To know the LORD is defined as judging the cause of the poor, as at 9:24, *that he understands and knows Me, that I am the LORD who practices loyal love, justice and righteousness*.
- **vv18–19 "Alas, my brother!… He will be buried with the burial of a donkey"** — the cry raised over the man of God at Bethel, *Alas, my brother!* (1 Kings 13:30). No one will raise it for Jehoiakim. A donkey is not buried: it is dragged off and thrown away. Kings says of his death only that he *lay down with his fathers* (2 Kings 24:6), and records no burial.
- **v22 "The wind will shepherd all your shepherds"** — *kol ro'ayikh tir'eh ruach*: the noun and the verb are from the same root. The shepherds are the rulers (2:8); the next chapter opens with a woe on them (23:1).
- **v23 "how you will be pitied when pangs come upon you"** — *mah nechant*, from *chanan*, "to show favor," in a form found only here: the one who nested among the cedars will be pitied, as a woman in labor is. The Greek has *you will groan*.
- **v24 "Coniah… the signet ring on My right hand"** — Coniah is Jehoiachin (2 Kings 24:8), called Jeconiah in 24:1. Even the ring on the LORD's own hand would be torn off. Haggai turns the word around for Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel: *I will make you like a signet ring, for I have chosen you* (Haggai 2:23).
- **v28 "a despised, broken pot, a jar no one wants"** — *keli*, the word of the potter's house, where a spoiled pot was made again (18:4), of the jar broken *so that it can never be mended* (19:11), and of the rulers who *fall like a precious jar* (25:34). The Greek has only *Jeconiah was dishonored like a vessel that is not needed*.
- **vv29–30 "Land, land, land… Write this man down as childless"** — the land is called three times to hear the verdict (the Greek has it twice). *Ariri*, the word of Abram, *I am going stripped* (Genesis 15:2), and of the law's sentence, *they shall die childless* (Leviticus 20:20). Coniah had sons, *The sons of Jeconiah the captive: Shealtiel his son* (1 Chronicles 3:17), and Matthew's line runs through him (Matthew 1:12). The verse says what *childless* means here: none of his seed will sit on David's throne.

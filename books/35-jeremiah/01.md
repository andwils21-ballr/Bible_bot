# Jeremiah 1

**1** The words of Jeremiah son of Hilkiah, one of the priests who were in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin,

**2** to whom the word of the LORD came in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.

**3** It came also in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the exile of Jerusalem in the fifth month.

## The Call

**4** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**5** "Before I formed you in the belly I knew you,  
and before you came out of the womb I consecrated you;  
I appointed you a prophet to the nations."

**6** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! I do not know how to speak, for I am a youth."

**7** But the LORD said to me, "Do not say, 'I am a youth'; for to all to whom I send you, you shall go, and all that I command you, you shall speak.

**8** Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you to deliver you, declares the LORD."

**9** Then the LORD put out His hand and touched my mouth, and the LORD said to me, "I have put My words in your mouth.

**10** See, I have appointed you this day over the nations and over the kingdoms, to uproot and to pull down, to destroy and to overthrow, to build and to plant."

## The Almond Branch and the Boiling Pot

**11** The word of the LORD came to me, saying, "What do you see, Jeremiah?" I said, "I see a branch of an almond tree."

**12** The LORD said to me, "You have seen well, for I am watching over My word to do it."

**13** The word of the LORD came to me a second time, saying, "What do you see?" I said, "I see a boiling pot, tilting from the north."

**14** Then the LORD said to me, "Out of the north disaster will break loose on all who live in the land.

**15** For I am about to call all the clans of the kingdoms of the north, declares the LORD; and they will come, and each will set his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, against all its walls all around, and against all the cities of Judah.

**16** I will speak My judgments against them for all their wickedness, because they have forsaken Me, burned incense to other gods, and bowed down to the works of their own hands.

**17** But you, gird up your loins; arise, and speak to them all that I command you. Do not be dismayed before them, or I will dismay you before them.

**18** I Myself have made you today a fortified city, an iron pillar and bronze walls against the whole land: against the kings of Judah, its princes, its priests and the people of the land.

**19** They will fight against you, but they will not overcome you, for I am with you, declares the LORD, to deliver you."

## Notes

- **v1 "one of the priests who were in Anathoth"** — Anathoth was one of the towns given *to the sons of Aaron the priest* (Joshua 21:13, 18), and the place Solomon sent Abiathar the priest: *Go to Anathoth, to your fields* (1 Kings 2:26). The Greek begins *The word of God which came to Jeremiah*.
- **v3 "until the exile of Jerusalem in the fifth month"** — the month the city fell: *In the fifth month, on the seventh day of the month… Nebuzaradan… came to Jerusalem* (2 Kings 25:8). The book's first verses already name its end.
- **v5 "Before I formed you in the belly I knew you"** — *yatsar*, "formed," the potter's verb, used of the man *shaped* from dust (Genesis 2:7) and of the servant, *who formed me from the womb* (Isaiah 49:5). "Consecrated" is *hiqdashtikha*, "made you holy": set apart before birth. Paul says the same of himself: *He who set me apart from my mother's womb and called me by His grace* (Galatians 1:15).
- **vv6–8 "I do not know how to speak… I am with you"** — the objection Moses made, *I am not a man of words* (Exodus 4:10), and the same answer: *I Myself will be with your mouth* (Exodus 4:12); here *I am with you to deliver you*, said again at the end of the chapter (v19).
- **vv7, 9 "all that I command you, you shall speak… I have put My words in your mouth"** — the two halves of the promise of a prophet like Moses: *I will put My words in his mouth, and he shall speak to them all that I command him* (Deuteronomy 18:18). Isaiah's lips were touched with a coal (Isaiah 6:7); Jeremiah's mouth is touched by the LORD's own hand.
- **v10 "to uproot and to pull down, to destroy and to overthrow, to build and to plant"** — four verbs of tearing down and two of building. They come back through the book (18:7, 9; 31:28), and the tearing down comes first.
- **vv11–12 "a branch of an almond tree… I am watching over My word"** — *shaqed*, "almond," and *shoqed*, "watching," the same three letters. In 31:28 the almond's verb and the verbs of v10 meet: *As I watched over them to uproot and to pull down… so I will watch over them to build and to plant*.
- **v15 "each will set his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem"** — at the fall, *all the princes of the king of Babylon came and sat in the Middle Gate* (39:3).
- **vv17–19 "Do not be dismayed before them, or I will dismay you… a fortified city… bronze walls"** — *al techat… pen achittekha*, the same verb twice: the prophet who fears the people is given something worse to fear. The promise is made again in 15:20, *I will make you to this people a fortified wall of bronze; they will fight against you, but they will not overcome you*.

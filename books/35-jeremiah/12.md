# Jeremiah 12

**1** You are righteous, LORD,  
when I bring my case to You;  
yet I would speak with You about Your judgments:  
Why does the way of the wicked prosper?  
Why are all who deal treacherously at ease?

**2** You plant them, and they take root;  
they grow and bear fruit;  
You are near in their mouth  
but far from their hearts.

**3** But You, LORD, know me;  
You see me and test my heart toward You.  
Drag them off like sheep for the slaughter,  
and set them apart for the day of slaughter.

**4** How long will the land mourn,  
and the grass of every field wither?  
Because of the wickedness of those who live in it,  
the beasts and the birds are swept away;  
for they said, "He will not see our end."

**5** If you have run with men on foot and they have wearied you,  
how will you compete with horses?  
If in a land of peace you are secure,  
how will you do in the thickets of the Jordan?

**6** For even your brothers and the house of your father,  
even they have dealt treacherously with you;  
even they are in full cry after you.  
Do not believe them,  
though they speak fair words to you.

**7** I have forsaken My house;  
I have abandoned My inheritance;  
I have given the beloved of My soul  
into the hands of her enemies.

**8** My inheritance has become to Me  
like a lion in the forest;  
she has raised her voice against Me;  
therefore I hate her.

**9** Is My inheritance to Me a speckled bird of prey,  
with birds of prey against her all around?  
Go, gather all the beasts of the field;  
bring them to devour.

**10** Many shepherds have destroyed My vineyard;  
they have trampled down My portion;  
they have made My pleasant portion  
a desolate wilderness.

**11** They have made it a desolation;  
desolate, it mourns to Me;  
the whole land is made desolate,  
because no man lays it to heart.

**12** On all the bare heights in the wilderness  
destroyers have come,  
for the sword of the LORD devours  
from one end of the land to the other;  
no flesh has peace.

**13** They have sown wheat and reaped thorns;  
they have worn themselves out but profit nothing.  
They will be ashamed of their harvests  
because of the fierce anger of the LORD.

**14** This is what the LORD says concerning all My evil neighbors who touch the inheritance that I gave My people Israel to inherit: I am about to uproot them from their land, and I will uproot the house of Judah from among them.

**15** After I have uprooted them, I will again have compassion on them, and I will bring them back, each to his inheritance and each to his land.

**16** If they will diligently learn the ways of My people, to swear by My name, "As the LORD lives," as they taught My people to swear by Baal, then they will be built up among My people.

**17** But if any nation will not listen, I will utterly uproot that nation and destroy it, declares the LORD.

## Notes

- **v1 "You are righteous, LORD, when I bring my case to You… Why does the way of the wicked prosper?"** — *ariv*, the verb of a case at law. The LORD brought His case against the people (2:9); now the prophet brings his against the LORD, and asks the psalmist's question, *I saw the prosperity of the wicked* (Psalm 73:3).

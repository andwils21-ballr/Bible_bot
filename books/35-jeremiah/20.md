# Jeremiah 20

**1** Pashhur son of Immer, the priest, who was chief officer in the house of the LORD, heard Jeremiah prophesying these things.

**2** Then Pashhur struck Jeremiah the prophet and put him in the stocks that were in the Upper Benjamin Gate in the house of the LORD.

**3** And it came to pass the next day that Pashhur released Jeremiah from the stocks, and Jeremiah said to him, "The LORD does not call your name Pashhur, but Terror on Every Side.

**4** For this is what the LORD says: I am about to make you a terror to yourself and to all your friends. They will fall by the sword of their enemies while your eyes look on. I will give all Judah into the hand of the king of Babylon; he will carry them into exile to Babylon and strike them down with the sword.

**5** I will give all the wealth of this city, all its gains, all its precious things and all the treasures of the kings of Judah into the hand of their enemies, who will plunder them, seize them and carry them to Babylon.

**6** As for you, Pashhur, you and all who live in your house will go into captivity; you will go to Babylon, and there you will die, and there you will be buried, you and all your friends to whom you have prophesied falsely."

**7** You enticed me, LORD, and I was enticed;  
You were stronger than I, and You prevailed.  
I have become a laughingstock all day long;  
everyone mocks me.

**8** For whenever I speak, I cry out;  
I shout, "Violence and destruction!"  
For the word of the LORD has become for me  
a reproach and derision all day long.

**9** If I say, "I will not mention Him,  
or speak any more in His name,"  
then in my heart it becomes like a burning fire  
shut up in my bones;  
I am weary of holding it in,  
and I cannot.

**10** For I hear the whispering of many:  
"Terror on every side!  
Denounce him! Let us denounce him!"  
All my friends watch for my fall:  
"Perhaps he will be enticed,  
and we can prevail against him  
and take our revenge on him."

**11** But the LORD is with me as a dread warrior;  
therefore my persecutors will stumble;  
they will not prevail.  
They will be greatly put to shame,  
for they will not succeed;  
their everlasting disgrace will never be forgotten.

**12** LORD of hosts, who tests the righteous,  
who sees the mind and the heart,  
let me see Your vengeance on them,  
for to You I have committed my cause.

**13** Sing to the LORD;  
praise the LORD!  
For He has delivered the life of the needy  
from the hand of evildoers.

**14** Cursed be the day  
on which I was born!  
The day my mother bore me,  
let it not be blessed!

**15** Cursed be the man  
who brought the news to my father,  
"A son is born to you, a male child,"  
making him very glad.

**16** Let that man be like the cities  
that the LORD overturned without relenting;  
let him hear a cry in the morning  
and an alarm at noon,

**17** because he did not kill me in the womb;  
so my mother would have been my grave,  
and her womb pregnant forever.

**18** Why did I come out of the womb  
to see toil and sorrow,  
and spend my days in shame?

## Notes

- **v1 "Pashhur son of Immer, the priest"** — of the priestly course of Immer, *the sixteenth to Immer* in David's order of the priests (1 Chronicles 24:14). The temple's officers had charge *over every madman who prophesies, to put him in the stocks* (29:26), and Jeremiah had just preached in its court (19:14).
- **vv3, 10 "Terror on Every Side"** — *Magor-missabib*, the phrase of 6:25, *terror is on every side*, given to Pashhur as a name; in v10 Jeremiah hears it whispered against himself, in the words of the psalm, *I hear the whispering of many, terror on every side* (Psalm 31:13). The Greek has *Exile* for the name.
- **vv4–6 "the king of Babylon… to Babylon"** — the first time the book names Babylon. Until now the enemy has been *from the north* (1:14; 4:6; 6:22).
- **vv7, 10 "You enticed me, LORD, and I was enticed; You were stronger than I, and You prevailed… Perhaps he will be enticed, and we can prevail against him"** — *pittitani*, from *patah*, "to entice, to persuade," the word of the heavenly council, *Who will entice Ahab?* (1 Kings 22:20); the Greek has *You deceived me*. In v10 his enemies hope to do to him what he says the LORD did: the same two verbs, *entice* and *prevail*.
- **v9 "like a burning fire shut up in my bones; I am weary of holding it in"** — *nil'eiti*, the words of 6:11, *I am full of the wrath of the LORD; I am weary of holding it in*. The LORD had said, *I am about to make My words in your mouth a fire* (5:14).
- **v12 "who tests the righteous, who sees the mind and the heart"** — 11:20 again, nearly word for word: *who tests the mind and the heart, let me see Your vengeance on them, for to You I have committed my cause*.
- **vv14–18 "Cursed be the day on which I was born!… Why did I come out of the womb"** — Job curses his birth in the same way, *Let the day perish on which I was born* (Job 3:3). *The cities that the LORD overturned* are Sodom and Gomorrah, *He overturned those cities* (Genesis 19:25). The last verse turns his call around: *before you came out of the womb I consecrated you* (1:5); now, *Why did I come out of the womb?*

# Jeremiah 33

**1** The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah a second time, while he was still shut up in the court of the guard, saying,

**2** This is what the LORD says, the One who made it,  
the LORD who formed it to establish it —  
the LORD is His name:

**3** Call to Me, and I will answer you,  
and I will tell you great and inaccessible things  
that you have not known.

**4** For this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says concerning the houses of this city and the houses of the kings of Judah that are torn down to hold off the siege ramps and the sword:

**5** Men come to fight against the Chaldeans, only to fill those houses with the corpses of the men whom I have struck down in My anger and My wrath; for I have hidden My face from this city because of all their evil.

**6** I am about to bring it new flesh and healing; I will heal them and reveal to them an abundance of peace and truth.

**7** I will restore the fortunes of Judah and the fortunes of Israel, and build them as they were at first.

**8** I will cleanse them from all their iniquity by which they have sinned against Me, and I will forgive all their iniquities by which they have sinned against Me and rebelled against Me.

**9** This city will be to Me a name of joy, a praise and a glory before all the nations of the earth, who will hear of all the good that I am doing for them; they will fear and tremble because of all the good and all the peace that I am doing for it.

**10** This is what the LORD says: Once more there will be heard in this place, of which you say, "It is a ruin, without man or beast," in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem that are desolate, without man, without inhabitant and without beast,

**11** the voice of mirth and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the voice of those who say,  
"Give thanks to the LORD of hosts,  
for the LORD is good,  
for His loyal love endures forever,"  
as they bring thank offerings to the house of the LORD. For I will restore the fortunes of the land as at first, says the LORD.

**12** This is what the LORD of hosts says: Once more there will be in this place, which is a ruin without man or beast, and in all its cities, pastures where shepherds rest their flocks.

**13** In the cities of the hill country, in the cities of the lowland and in the cities of the Negev, in the land of Benjamin, around Jerusalem and in the cities of Judah, flocks will again pass under the hands of the one who counts them, says the LORD.

## A Branch of Righteousness

**14** Days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will fulfill the good word that I have spoken concerning the house of Israel and the house of Judah.

**15** In those days and at that time  
I will make a Branch of righteousness sprout for David,  
and he will do justice and righteousness in the land.

**16** In those days Judah will be saved,  
and Jerusalem will dwell in safety;  
and this is the name by which she will be called:  
The LORD Is Our Righteousness.

**17** For this is what the LORD says: David will never lack a man to sit on the throne of the house of Israel,

**18** and the Levitical priests will never lack a man before Me to offer burnt offerings, to burn grain offerings and to make sacrifices all the days.

**19** The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying,

**20** This is what the LORD says: If you can break My covenant with the day and My covenant with the night, so that day and night do not come at their time,

**21** then My covenant with David My servant can also be broken, so that he would have no son to reign on his throne, and My covenant with the Levitical priests, My ministers.

**22** As the host of heaven cannot be counted and the sand of the sea cannot be measured, so I will multiply the seed of David My servant and the Levites who minister to Me.

**23** The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying,

**24** Have you not seen what this people have said: "The two clans that the LORD chose, He has rejected"? So they despise My people, as though they were no longer a nation in their sight.

**25** This is what the LORD says: If I have not established My covenant with day and night, and the fixed order of heaven and earth,

**26** then I will also reject the seed of Jacob and of David My servant, and not take from his seed rulers over the seed of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. For I will restore their fortunes and have compassion on them.

## Notes

- **v2 "the One who made it, the LORD who formed it to establish it"** — *yotser*, "who formed," is the potter at his wheel (18:4–6): the LORD shapes what He has made. The Hebrew does not say what *it* is; the Greek has *who makes the earth*.
- **v3 "great and inaccessible things that you have not known"** — *betsurot*, the word for walled cities: the spies came back with *the cities are great and fortified up to the heavens* (Deuteronomy 1:28), *gedolot u-vetsurot*, the same pair. What was walled off the LORD will open. Isaiah has a near word, *hidden things that you have not known* (*netsurot*, Isaiah 48:6).
- **vv4–5 "the houses… torn down to hold off the siege ramps and the sword"** — as Isaiah said of Jerusalem under threat, *you tore down the houses to fortify the wall* (Isaiah 22:10). The Hebrew of these two verses is broken in places, and the English follows the sense.
- **v6 "I am about to bring it new flesh and healing"** — *arukhah*, the healing the prophet asked for, *Why then has the daughter of my people not been healed?* (8:22), and promised in 30:17, *I will make new flesh grow for you*.
- **v9 "a name of joy, a praise and a glory… they will fear and tremble because of all the good"** — the words of the loincloth: the LORD made Israel cling to Him *to be for Me a people, a name, a praise and a glory; but they did not listen* (13:11). The nations will tremble at the LORD's goodness, as Hosea said Israel would come *trembling to the LORD and to His goodness in the end of days* (Hosea 3:5).
- **vv10–11 "the voice of mirth… the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride… Give thanks to the LORD of hosts, for the LORD is good"** — the voices silenced at 7:34, 16:9 and 25:10 come back, with the song of the temple added: *Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good, for His loyal love endures forever* (Psalm 136:1).
- **v13 "flocks will again pass under the hands of the one who counts them"** — the shepherd's evening count. The law used the same picture for the tithe of the flock, *every tenth one that passes under the rod* (Leviticus 27:32).
- **vv14–16 "a Branch of righteousness… The LORD Is Our Righteousness"** — 23:5–6 again, with changes: *a Branch of righteousness* (*tsemach tsedaqah*) for *a righteous Branch*, *I will make… sprout* (*atsmiach*, the verb of *tsemach*), and the name given now to Jerusalem, *by which she will be called*. The Greek has nothing of vv14–26.
- **vv17–22 "David will never lack a man to sit on the throne… as the sand of the sea cannot be measured"** — the promise to David, *you shall not lack a man on the throne of Israel* (1 Kings 2:4), and to Abraham, *I will multiply your seed as the stars of the heavens and as the sand that is on the lip of the sea* (Genesis 22:17). The covenant with day and night is the one made after the flood, *day and night shall not cease* (Genesis 8:22).
- **vv24–26 "The two clans that the LORD chose, He has rejected"** — Israel and Judah, the two kingdoms of v14; v26 answers with *the seed of Jacob and of David My servant*.

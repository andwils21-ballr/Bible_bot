# Jeremiah 31

**1** At that time, declares the LORD, I will be the God of all the clans of Israel, and they will be My people.

**2** Thus says the LORD:  
The people who survived the sword  
found favor in the wilderness,  
when Israel went to find its rest.

**3** From far away the LORD appeared to me:  
I have loved you with an everlasting love;  
therefore I have drawn you with loyal love.

**4** Again I will build you, and you will be built,  
virgin Israel;  
again you will adorn yourself with your tambourines  
and go out in the dance of the revelers.

**5** Again you will plant vineyards  
on the mountains of Samaria;  
the planters will plant  
and use their fruit.

**6** For there will be a day when watchmen will call  
on Mount Ephraim:  
"Arise, and let us go up to Zion,  
to the LORD our God."

**7** For thus says the LORD:  
Sing with gladness for Jacob,  
and shout at the head of the nations;  
proclaim, give praise, and say,  
"Save Your people, LORD,  
the remnant of Israel."

**8** I am about to bring them from the land of the north,  
and gather them from the farthest parts of the earth;  
among them the blind and the lame,  
the woman with child and the one in labor, together;  
a great company, they will return here.

**9** With weeping they will come,  
and with pleas for mercy I will lead them;  
I will bring them to streams of water,  
on a straight road where they will not stumble;  
for I am a father to Israel,  
and Ephraim is My firstborn.

**10** Hear the word of the LORD, you nations,  
and declare it in the coastlands far away;  
say: He who scattered Israel will gather him,  
and keep him as a shepherd keeps his flock.

**11** For the LORD has ransomed Jacob,  
and redeemed him from the hand of one stronger than he.

**12** They will come and sing on the height of Zion,  
and stream to the goodness of the LORD,  
to the grain, the new wine and the oil,  
and to the young of the flock and the herd;  
their soul will be like a watered garden,  
and they will never languish again.

**13** Then the virgin will rejoice in the dance,  
and the young men and the old together;  
I will turn their mourning into joy;  
I will comfort them, and give them gladness after their sorrow.

**14** I will saturate the soul of the priests with richness,  
and My people will be satisfied with My goodness,  
declares the LORD.

## Rachel Weeping

**15** Thus says the LORD:  
A voice is heard in Ramah,  
lamentation and bitter weeping.  
Rachel is weeping for her children;  
she refuses to be comforted for her children,  
because they are no more.

**16** Thus says the LORD:  
Keep your voice from weeping,  
and your eyes from tears;  
for there is a reward for your work, declares the LORD,  
and they will return from the land of the enemy.

**17** There is hope for your future, declares the LORD,  
and your children will return to their own border.

**18** I have heard Ephraim grieving:  
"You corrected me, and I was corrected,  
like an untrained calf.  
Bring me back, that I may return,  
for You are the LORD my God.

**19** For after I turned, I repented;  
and after I was made to understand, I struck my thigh;  
I was ashamed, and humiliated too,  
because I bore the disgrace of my youth."

**20** Is Ephraim My dear son?  
Is he My darling child?  
For as often as I speak against him,  
I still remember him.  
Therefore My heart yearns for him;  
I will surely have compassion on him,  
declares the LORD.

**21** Set up road markers for yourself;  
make yourself guideposts;  
set your heart on the highway,  
the road by which you went.  
Return, virgin Israel,  
return to these your cities.

**22** How long will you waver,  
faithless daughter?  
For the LORD has created a new thing in the land:  
a woman encompasses a man.

**23** Thus says the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Once more they will say this word in the land of Judah and in its cities, when I restore their fortunes:  
"The LORD bless you, abode of righteousness,  
holy mountain!"

**24** Judah and all its cities will live there together, the farmers and those who go about with the flocks.

**25** For I have saturated the weary soul,  
and every languishing soul I have filled.

**26** At this I awoke and looked, and my sleep was sweet to me.

**27** Days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will sow the house of Israel and the house of Judah with the seed of man and the seed of beast.

**28** And it shall come to pass that as I have watched over them to uproot and to pull down, to overthrow, to destroy and to bring disaster, so I will watch over them to build and to plant, declares the LORD.

**29** In those days they will no longer say,  
"The fathers have eaten sour grapes,  
and the children's teeth are set on edge."

**30** But each will die for his own iniquity: every man who eats sour grapes, his own teeth will be set on edge.

## The New Covenant

**31** Days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah,

**32** not like the covenant that I made with their fathers on the day I took them by the hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt, My covenant that they broke, though I was their husband, declares the LORD.

**33** For this is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel after those days, declares the LORD: I will put My law within them, and on their heart I will write it; and I will be their God, and they will be My people.

**34** No longer will each man teach his neighbor and each his brother, saying, "Know the LORD," for they will all know Me, from the least of them to the greatest, declares the LORD; for I will forgive their iniquity, and their sin I will remember no more.

**35** Thus says the LORD,  
who gives the sun for light by day,  
and the fixed order of the moon and the stars for light by night,  
who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar —  
the LORD of hosts is His name:

**36** If this fixed order departs from before Me, declares the LORD,  
then the seed of Israel also will cease  
from being a nation before Me forever.

**37** Thus says the LORD:  
If the heavens above can be measured,  
and the foundations of the earth below be searched out,  
then I too will reject all the seed of Israel  
for all that they have done,  
declares the LORD.

**38** Days are coming, declares the LORD, when the city will be rebuilt for the LORD from the Tower of Hananel to the Corner Gate.

**39** The measuring line will go out farther, straight ahead, to the hill Gareb, and then turn to Goah.

**40** The whole valley of the corpses and the ashes, and all the fields as far as the Brook Kidron, to the corner of the Horse Gate toward the east, will be holy to the LORD. It will never again be uprooted or overthrown.

## Notes

- **v2 "found favor in the wilderness, when Israel went to find its rest"** — the wilderness after the golden calf, where Moses was told *you have also found favor in My eyes* and *I will give you rest* (Exodus 33:12, 14). The Greek reads *I found warmth in the wilderness*.
- **v3 "I have loved you with an everlasting love; therefore I have drawn you with loyal love"** — *meshakhtikh*, the verb of Hosea, *I drew them with cords of a man, with bands of love* (Hosea 11:4). *Chesed*, loyal love, is the love of a covenant kept.
- **vv8–9 "the blind and the lame… Ephraim is My firstborn"** — *pisseach*, "the lame," has the same letters as *pesach*, Passover, and the Greek reads it so: *I will gather them from the end of the earth at the feast of Passover*. The return is told as a second exodus: *Israel is My son, My firstborn* was the word to Pharaoh (Exodus 4:22), and here the firstborn is Ephraim.
- **vv15–17, 21 "Rachel is weeping for her children… There is hope for your future… make yourself guideposts"** — Rachel, mother of Joseph and Benjamin, grandmother of Ephraim, weeps at Ramah, a town of Benjamin (Joshua 18:25). It was at Ramah that the captives were gathered for Babylon, and Jeremiah was released there in chains (40:1). Matthew hears her weeping again at Bethlehem (Matthew 2:18). *Tamrurim*, "bitter" (v15), is spelled the same as *tamrurim*, "guideposts" (v21): the bitter weeping is answered by markers on the road home.
- **vv18–22 "Bring me back, that I may return… Return, virgin Israel… faithless daughter"** — *shuv*, "turn," runs through the passage: *hashiveni ve-ashuvah* (v18), *acharei shuvi* (v19), *shuvi… shuvi* (v21), *ha-shovevah*, "the faithless" (v22), the wordplay of chapter 3. Lamentations ends with Ephraim's prayer in the people's mouth: *Bring us back to You, LORD, and we will return* (Lamentations 5:21).
- **v22 "the LORD has created a new thing in the land: a woman encompasses a man"** — *bara*, the verb of Genesis 1, said only of God; and *neqevah*, "female," the word of *male and female He created them* (Genesis 1:27), here set beside *gever*, "a strong man." What *encompasses* means is uncertain: to surround, to protect, to go around. The Greek has *the LORD has created salvation for a new planting; in salvation men will go about*.
- **v28 "as I have watched over them to uproot and to pull down… so I will watch over them to build and to plant"** — the verbs of Jeremiah's call (1:10) and the almond branch: *I am watching over My word to do it* (1:12), *shoqed*, the word in both places.
- **vv29–30 "The fathers have eaten sour grapes"** — the proverb Ezekiel also hears and answers (Ezekiel 18:2–4). The law already said, *each shall be put to death for his own sin* (Deuteronomy 24:16).
- **vv31–34 "a new covenant… though I was their husband… on their heart I will write it"** — *ve-anokhi ba'alti vam*, the words of 3:14, *for I am your husband*. The first covenant was given on *tablets of stone* (Exodus 31:18), and Judah's sin was cut *on the tablet of their heart* (17:1); the new one is written there. The letter to the Hebrews quotes vv31–34 from the Greek, which has *and I disregarded them* for *though I was their husband* (Hebrews 8:8–12). Jesus: *This cup is the new covenant in My blood* (Luke 22:20).
- **vv35–37 "who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar — the LORD of hosts is His name"** — the last two lines are Isaiah's, letter for letter (Isaiah 51:15). The Greek gives the three verses in another order: v37, then vv35–36.

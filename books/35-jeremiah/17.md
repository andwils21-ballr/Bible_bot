# Jeremiah 17

**1** The sin of Judah is written with a pen of iron;  
with a point of diamond it is engraved  
on the tablet of their heart  
and on the horns of your altars,

**2** while their children remember their altars  
and their Asherim beside the green trees,  
on the high hills,

**3** My mountain in the open country.  
Your wealth and all your treasures  
I will give as plunder,  
your high places, for sin,  
throughout all your territory.

**4** You will let go, through your own fault,  
of your inheritance that I gave you,  
and I will make you serve your enemies  
in a land you do not know;  
for you have kindled a fire in My anger  
that will burn forever.

**5** Thus says the LORD:  
Cursed is the man who trusts in man  
and makes flesh his arm,  
whose heart turns away from the LORD.

**6** He will be like a shrub in the desert,  
and will not see when good comes;  
he will live in parched places in the wilderness,  
in a salt land where no one lives.

**7** Blessed is the man who trusts in the LORD,  
whose trust is the LORD.

**8** He will be like a tree planted by water,  
that sends out its roots by the stream,  
and does not see when heat comes;  
its leaves stay green,  
and in the year of drought it is not anxious,  
and it does not cease to bear fruit.

**9** The heart is more deceitful than all else,  
and desperately sick;  
who can understand it?

**10** I the LORD search the heart  
and test the mind,  
to give to each man according to his ways,  
according to the fruit of his deeds.

**11** Like a partridge that hatches eggs it did not lay  
is he who gets riches, but not by justice;  
in the middle of his days they will leave him,  
and at his end he will be a fool.

**12** A glorious throne, on high from the beginning,  
is the place of our sanctuary.

**13** Hope of Israel, LORD,  
all who forsake You will be put to shame;  
those who turn away from Me will be written in the earth,  
for they have forsaken the LORD,  
the fountain of living waters.

**14** Heal me, LORD, and I shall be healed;  
save me, and I shall be saved;  
for You are my praise.

**15** They keep saying to me,  
"Where is the word of the LORD?  
Let it come!"

**16** But I have not run away from being a shepherd after You,  
nor have I longed for the day of disaster;  
You know;  
what came out of my lips  
was before Your face.

**17** Do not be a terror to me;  
You are my refuge in the day of disaster.

**18** Let my persecutors be put to shame,  
but let me not be put to shame;  
let them be dismayed,  
but let me not be dismayed;  
bring on them the day of disaster;  
destroy them with double destruction.

## The Sabbath

**19** Thus the LORD said to me: Go and stand in the gate of the sons of the people, by which the kings of Judah enter and by which they go out, and in all the gates of Jerusalem,

**20** and say to them: Hear the word of the LORD, kings of Judah, and all Judah, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem who enter by these gates.

**21** Thus says the LORD: Take care, for your lives' sake, and do not carry a load on the sabbath day or bring it in by the gates of Jerusalem.

**22** Do not carry a load out of your houses on the sabbath day or do any work, but keep the sabbath day holy, as I commanded your fathers.

**23** Yet they did not listen or incline their ear, but stiffened their neck so as not to hear or take correction.

**24** If you diligently listen to Me, declares the LORD, and bring no load in through the gates of this city on the sabbath day, but keep the sabbath day holy and do no work on it,

**25** then there will enter by the gates of this city kings and princes who sit on the throne of David, riding in chariots and on horses, they and their princes, the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem; and this city will be inhabited forever.

**26** People will come from the cities of Judah and from around Jerusalem, from the land of Benjamin, from the lowland, from the hill country and from the Negev, bringing burnt offerings and sacrifices, grain offerings and frankincense, and bringing thank offerings to the house of the LORD.

**27** But if you do not listen to Me, to keep the sabbath day holy and not to carry a load as you enter the gates of Jerusalem on the sabbath day, I will kindle a fire in its gates, and it will devour the palaces of Jerusalem and will not be quenched.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "written with a pen of iron… on the tablet of their heart"** — the covenant was given on *tablets of stone, written by the finger of God* (Exodus 31:18); Judah's sin is cut into the tablet of the heart. The new covenant answers it in the same words: *I will put My law within them, and on their heart I will write it* (31:33). The Greek does not have vv1–4.
- **v4 "You will let go… of your inheritance… I will make you serve your enemies"** — *shamat*, the verb of the seventh year, *The seventh you shall let go and leave it* (Exodus 23:11), and of *the LORD's release* (Deuteronomy 15:2). What Israel did not let go, it will lose: the Chronicler says the exile lasted *until the land had been paid its sabbaths* (2 Chronicles 36:21). The last lines repeat 15:13–14.
- **vv5–8 "Cursed is the man who trusts in man… Blessed is the man who trusts in the LORD… like a tree planted by water"** — the two ways of Psalm 1, where the righteous man *will be like a tree planted by streams of water… its leaf does not wither* (Psalm 1:3). *Does not see when heat comes* is the read form, *yir'eh*, matching *will not see when good comes* in v6; the written form is *yira*, "does not fear."
- **v9 "The heart is more deceitful than all else"** — *aqov*, the root of Jacob's name, as at 9:4, *every brother is a supplanter*. The Greek reads the line otherwise: *The heart is deep above all things, and it is man; who can know him?*
- **v11 "Like a partridge that hatches eggs it did not lay"** — *qore*, the bird David named when Saul hunted him, *as one hunts a partridge in the mountains* (1 Samuel 26:20). Wealth gathered unjustly is a brood not one's own: it leaves.
- **v13 "those who turn away from Me will be written in the earth… the fountain of living waters"** — the charge of 2:13, *they have forsaken Me, the fountain of living waters*, now in the prophet's own prayer.
- **v16 "I have not run away from being a shepherd after You"** — *me-ro'eh acharekha*; the sense of the line is uncertain. The Greek has *I have not grown weary following after You*.
- **vv19–27 "keep the sabbath day holy, as I commanded your fathers"** — the words of the commandment, *Keep the sabbath day, to keep it holy, as the LORD your God commanded you* (Deuteronomy 5:12). After the exile Nehemiah shut the gates against the sabbath trade and said, *Did not your fathers act in this way, and did not our God bring all this evil on us and on this city?* (Nehemiah 13:18). *I will kindle a fire in its gates, and it will devour the palaces of Jerusalem* is Amos's word against Judah (Amos 2:5).

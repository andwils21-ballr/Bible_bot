# Jeremiah 35

**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah:

**2** "Go to the house of the Rechabites and speak to them, and bring them to the house of the LORD, into one of the chambers, and give them wine to drink."

**3** So I took Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah, son of Habazziniah, and his brothers and all his sons, and the whole house of the Rechabites,

**4** and I brought them into the house of the LORD, to the chamber of the sons of Hanan son of Igdaliah, the man of God, which was next to the chamber of the princes, above the chamber of Maaseiah son of Shallum, the keeper of the threshold.

**5** Then I set before the men of the house of the Rechabites bowls full of wine, and cups, and I said to them, "Drink wine."

**6** But they said, "We will drink no wine, for Jonadab son of Rechab our father commanded us, 'You shall drink no wine, neither you nor your sons, forever.

**7** You shall not build a house or sow seed or plant a vineyard, or have any; but you shall live in tents all your days, so that you may live many days on the face of the ground where you live as guests.'

**8** We have obeyed the voice of Jehonadab son of Rechab our father in all that he commanded us: to drink no wine all our days, we, our wives, our sons and our daughters,

**9** and not to build houses to live in. We have no vineyard or field or seed.

**10** We have lived in tents, and have obeyed and done all that Jonadab our father commanded us.

**11** But when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up against the land, we said, 'Come, let us go to Jerusalem, away from the army of the Chaldeans and the army of Aram.' So we are living in Jerusalem."

**12** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying,

**13** This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Go and say to the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem: Will you not take correction and listen to My words? declares the LORD.

**14** The words of Jehonadab son of Rechab, which he commanded his sons, to drink no wine, have been kept; they have drunk none to this day, for they have obeyed their father's command. But I Myself have spoken to you again and again, and you have not listened to Me.

**15** I have sent to you all My servants the prophets again and again, saying, "Turn now, each of you, from his evil way, and amend your deeds, and do not go after other gods to serve them; then you will live on the land that I gave to you and to your fathers." But you did not incline your ear or listen to Me.

**16** The sons of Jehonadab son of Rechab have kept the command of their father which he commanded them, but this people has not listened to Me.

**17** Therefore this is what the LORD, the God of hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am about to bring on Judah and on all the inhabitants of Jerusalem all the disaster that I have pronounced against them, because I spoke to them and they did not listen, and I called to them and they did not answer.

**18** To the house of the Rechabites Jeremiah said, "This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Because you have obeyed the command of Jehonadab your father, and kept all his commands, and done all that he commanded you,

**19** therefore this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Jonadab son of Rechab will never lack a man to stand before Me all the days."

## Notes

- **v2 "the house of the Rechabites"** — the line of Jehonadab son of Rechab, who rode in Jehu's chariot when Jehu wiped out the house of Ahab and the worshippers of Baal (2 Kings 10:15–23). Chronicles counts *the house of Rechab* among the Kenites (1 Chronicles 2:55), the people of *the Kenite, Moses' father-in-law*, who came up with Judah into the wilderness (Judges 1:16).
- **vv6–7 "You shall drink no wine… you shall live in tents all your days, so that you may live many days on the face of the ground where you live as guests"** — a long life on the ground, the promise the law attaches to honoring father and mother, *so that your days may be long on the ground* (Exodus 20:12); these sons honor their father's word. They are guests (*garim*) in the land and live like it, with no house, field or vineyard of their own. To the exiles in Babylon Jeremiah said the opposite: *Build houses… plant gardens* (29:5).
- **v11 "the army of the Chaldeans and the army of Aram"** — the raiders Kings names in Jehoiakim's day, *bands of Chaldeans, bands of Arameans, bands of Moabites and bands of Ammonites* (2 Kings 24:2). The Greek has *the army of the Assyrians*. The tent-dwellers have come inside the walls of Jerusalem and still keep their father's rule.
- **vv13–16 "Will you not take correction… The words of Jehonadab… have been kept… But I Myself have spoken to you again and again"** — the argument runs from the lesser to the greater: a man's command to his sons is kept for generations, and the LORD's word, sent *again and again* (*hashkem*), is not kept at all. *Turn now, each of you, from his evil way* is 25:5 word for word.
- **v17 "I spoke to them and they did not listen, and I called to them and they did not answer"** — the charge of the temple sermon, *I spoke to you again and again, but you did not listen, and I called you, but you did not answer* (7:13).
- **v19 "Jonadab son of Rechab will never lack a man to stand before Me"** — the words of the promises to David and to the Levitical priests, *will never lack a man* (33:17–18). *To stand before the LORD* is the service of the Levites (Deuteronomy 10:8), and the prophet's place: *you shall stand before Me* (15:19), and Elijah's oath, *As the LORD… lives, before whom I stand* (1 Kings 17:1).

# Jeremiah 50

**1** The word that the LORD spoke concerning Babylon, concerning the land of the Chaldeans, through Jeremiah the prophet:

**2** Declare it among the nations and proclaim it;  
set up a banner and proclaim it;  
hide nothing; say:  
"Babylon is taken,  
Bel is put to shame,  
Merodach is shattered;  
her images are put to shame,  
her idols shattered."

**3** For a nation has come up against her out of the north;  
it will make her land a desolation,  
and no one will live in it;  
both man and beast have fled and gone.

**4** In those days and at that time, declares the LORD,  
the sons of Israel will come,  
they and the sons of Judah together;  
weeping as they go, they will go  
and seek the LORD their God.

**5** They will ask the way to Zion,  
with their faces turned toward it:  
"Come, let us join ourselves to the LORD  
in an everlasting covenant that will not be forgotten."

**6** My people have been lost sheep;  
their shepherds have led them astray,  
turning them loose on the mountains;  
they have gone from mountain to hill;  
they have forgotten their resting place.

**7** All who found them devoured them,  
and their enemies said, "We are not guilty,  
because they have sinned against the LORD,  
the abode of righteousness,  
the LORD, the hope of their fathers."

**8** Flee from the middle of Babylon,  
go out from the land of the Chaldeans,  
and be like the he-goats in front of the flock.

**9** For I am about to stir up and bring against Babylon  
an assembly of great nations from the land of the north;  
they will set themselves in array against her;  
from there she will be taken.  
Their arrows are like a skilled warrior's,  
who does not come back empty-handed.

**10** Chaldea will become plunder;  
all who plunder her will be satisfied,  
declares the LORD.

**11** Though you rejoice, though you exult,  
you who plunder My inheritance,  
though you frisk like a heifer at the threshing  
and neigh like stallions,

**12** your mother will be utterly put to shame;  
she who bore you will be disgraced.  
She will be the last of the nations,  
a wilderness, a dry land and a desert.

**13** Because of the wrath of the LORD she will not be inhabited,  
but will be wholly a desolation;  
everyone who passes by Babylon will be horrified  
and will hiss because of all her wounds.

**14** Set yourselves in array against Babylon all around,  
all you who bend the bow;  
shoot at her, spare no arrows,  
for she has sinned against the LORD.

**15** Raise the battle cry against her all around!  
She has given her hand;  
her bulwarks have fallen,  
her walls are torn down.  
For this is the vengeance of the LORD:  
take vengeance on her;  
as she has done, do to her.

**16** Cut off from Babylon the sower  
and the one who handles the sickle at harvest time;  
because of the oppressing sword  
each will turn to his own people,  
and each will flee to his own land.

**17** Israel is a scattered sheep,  
driven away by lions.  
First the king of Assyria devoured him,  
and now at last Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has gnawed his bones.

**18** Therefore this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says:  
I am about to punish the king of Babylon and his land,  
as I punished the king of Assyria.

**19** I will bring Israel back to his pasture,  
and he will feed on Carmel and in Bashan,  
and his soul will be satisfied  
on the hill country of Ephraim and in Gilead.

**20** In those days and at that time, declares the LORD,  
the iniquity of Israel will be sought,  
and there will be none,  
and the sins of Judah, and they will not be found;  
for I will forgive those whom I leave as a remnant.

**21** Go up against the land of Merathaim,  
against it and against the inhabitants of Pekod!  
Put them to the sword and destroy them completely, declares the LORD,  
and do all that I have commanded you.

**22** The sound of battle is in the land,  
and great ruin!

**23** How the hammer of the whole earth  
is cut down and broken!  
How Babylon has become a horror  
among the nations!

**24** I set a snare for you, and you were caught, Babylon,  
and you did not know it;  
you were found and seized,  
because you contended with the LORD.

**25** The LORD has opened His armory  
and brought out the weapons of His fury,  
for the Lord GOD of hosts has work to do  
in the land of the Chaldeans.

**26** Come against her from every quarter;  
open her granaries;  
pile her up like heaps of grain,  
and destroy her completely;  
let nothing be left of her.

**27** Put all her bulls to the sword;  
let them go down to the slaughter.  
Woe to them, for their day has come,  
the time of their punishment.

**28** Listen! Fugitives and survivors from the land of Babylon  
declare in Zion the vengeance of the LORD our God,  
the vengeance for His temple.

**29** Summon archers against Babylon,  
all who bend the bow;  
camp against her all around;  
let no one escape.  
Repay her according to her deeds;  
as she has done, do to her;  
for she has acted proudly against the LORD,  
against the Holy One of Israel.

**30** Therefore her young men will fall in her squares,  
and all her men of war will be silenced in that day,  
declares the LORD.

**31** I am against you, you Pride,  
declares the Lord GOD of hosts;  
for your day has come,  
the time that I punish you.

**32** Pride will stumble and fall,  
with no one to raise him up;  
I will kindle a fire in his cities,  
and it will devour all that is around him.

**33** This is what the LORD of hosts says:  
The sons of Israel are oppressed,  
and the sons of Judah with them;  
all who took them captive have held them fast;  
they refuse to let them go.

**34** Their Redeemer is strong —  
the LORD of hosts is His name;  
He will plead their cause,  
so that He may give rest to the earth  
and unrest to the inhabitants of Babylon.

**35** A sword against the Chaldeans, declares the LORD,  
and against the inhabitants of Babylon,  
and against her princes and her wise men!

**36** A sword against the diviners,  
and they will become fools!  
A sword against her warriors,  
and they will be shattered!

**37** A sword against his horses and against his chariots,  
and against all the foreign troops in her midst,  
and they will become like women!  
A sword against her treasures,  
and they will be plundered!

**38** A drought against her waters,  
and they will dry up!  
For it is a land of carved images,  
and they go mad over their idols of terror.

**39** Therefore wild beasts will live there with hyenas,  
and ostriches will live in her;  
she will never again be inhabited,  
nor lived in from generation to generation.

**40** As when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighbors, declares the LORD, no one will live there, and no son of man will live there as a guest.

**41** A people is coming from the north;  
a great nation and many kings are stirring  
from the farthest parts of the earth.

**42** They lay hold of bow and javelin;  
they are cruel and have no mercy;  
their voice roars like the sea;  
they ride on horses,  
set in array as a man for battle,  
against you, daughter of Babylon.

**43** The king of Babylon has heard the report of them,  
and his hands fall limp;  
anguish has seized him,  
pain as of a woman in labor.

**44** Like a lion coming up from the thickets of the Jordan  
against a pasture that never fails,  
I will suddenly make them run from it,  
and I will appoint over it whomever I choose.  
For who is like Me? Who will summon Me?  
What shepherd can stand before Me?

**45** Therefore hear the counsel of the LORD  
that He has planned against Babylon,  
and the plans He has devised  
against the land of the Chaldeans:  
Surely the smallest of the flock will drag them away;  
surely the pasture will be horrified over them.

**46** At the cry, "Babylon is seized!" the earth quakes,  
and a cry is heard among the nations.

## Notes

- **v2 "Bel is put to shame, Merodach is shattered"** — Babylon's gods, as in Isaiah, *Bel bows down; Nebo stoops* (Isaiah 46:1). *Gillulim*, "idols," is the law's word, *the corpses of your idols* (Leviticus 26:30). The Greek has *Bel the fearless… the delicate Maiodak*.
- **vv4–5, 20 "the sons of Israel… and the sons of Judah together… an everlasting covenant… I will forgive those whom I leave"** — the promises of 31:31–34 and 32:40, *an everlasting covenant*, set beside the fall of Babylon: the two kingdoms return as one, weeping, and their sins are looked for and not found.
- **vv6–7 "their shepherds have led them astray… the abode of righteousness, the LORD, the hope of their fathers"** — the shepherds of 23:1. The enemies speak like believers: they know whom Israel sinned against, and use it to excuse themselves. *Neveh tsedeq*, "abode of righteousness," was Jerusalem's name in 31:23; here it is the LORD Himself, as is *the hope of Israel* (14:8; 17:13).
- **vv15, 29 "as she has done, do to her"** — the prayer of the exiles by Babylon's rivers, *blessed is the one who repays you for what you have done to us* (Psalm 137:8).
- **v21 "Merathaim… Pekod… destroy them completely"** — two names that are also words: *Merathaim*, "double rebellion," of *marah*, "rebel," and *Pekod*, of *paqad*, "punish." *Charem*, "destroy completely," is the law's command against the cities of Canaan, *You shall destroy them completely* (Deuteronomy 20:17), now given against Babylon, with *do all that I have commanded you* (also v26).
- **v29 "the Holy One of Israel"** — Isaiah's name for God, found in Jeremiah only here and at 51:5, in the oracles against Babylon.
- **v34 "Their Redeemer is strong… He will plead their cause"** — the proverb about the orphan's field, *for their Redeemer is strong; He will plead their cause against you* (Proverbs 23:11), here for the captives. *Go'el*, "redeemer," is the kinsman who buys back a relative and takes up his cause.
- **vv35–38 "A sword… A sword… A drought against her waters"** — *cherev*, "sword," five times; the sixth is *chorev*, "drought," with the same letters. The sword turns into a drought that dries up the river city.
- **vv39–40 "wild beasts… hyenas… ostriches… As when God overthrew Sodom"** — Isaiah's words for fallen Babylon, *there ostriches will live… Hyenas will cry in its strongholds* (Isaiah 13:21–22), and its comparison, *like Sodom and Gomorrah when God overthrew them* (13:19). Verse 40 is 49:18, said there of Edom.
- **vv41–46 "A people is coming from the north… daughter of Babylon"** — 6:22–24, the enemy from the north that came against *the daughter of Zion*, now sent against *the daughter of Babylon*: *our hands fall limp* has become *his hands fall limp*. Verses 44–46 are 49:19–21, said there of Edom.

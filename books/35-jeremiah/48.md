# Jeremiah 48

**1** Concerning Moab. This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says:  
Woe to Nebo, for it is laid waste!  
Kiriathaim is put to shame; it is taken;  
the high fortress is put to shame and shattered.

**2** The praise of Moab is no more.  
In Heshbon they have planned evil against her:  
"Come, let us cut her off from being a nation."  
You too, Madmen, will be silenced;  
the sword will go after you.

**3** The sound of a cry from Horonaim:  
"Destruction and great ruin!"

**4** Moab is broken;  
her little ones have made their cry heard.

**5** For up the ascent of Luhith  
they go weeping, weeping as they go;  
for on the descent of Horonaim  
they have heard the anguished cry of ruin.

**6** Flee! Save your lives!  
Be like a shrub in the wilderness.

**7** Because you trusted in your works and in your treasures,  
you too will be taken;  
and Chemosh will go into exile,  
his priests and his princes together.

**8** The destroyer will come against every city,  
and no city will escape;  
the valley will perish,  
and the plateau will be destroyed,  
as the LORD has said.

**9** Give wings to Moab,  
for she must fly away;  
her cities will become a desolation,  
with no one living in them.

**10** Cursed is he who does the LORD's work negligently,  
and cursed is he who keeps back his sword from blood.

**11** Moab has been at ease from his youth,  
settled on his dregs;  
he has not been poured from vessel to vessel,  
nor has he gone into exile;  
so his taste has remained in him,  
and his scent has not changed.

**12** Therefore, days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will send him men who tip wine, and they will tip him over; they will empty his vessels and smash their jars.

**13** Then Moab will be ashamed of Chemosh,  
as the house of Israel was ashamed of Bethel, their trust.

**14** How can you say, "We are warriors,  
men of valor for battle"?

**15** Moab is laid waste; the enemy has gone up into her cities;  
the choicest of his young men have gone down to the slaughter,  
declares the King, whose name is the LORD of hosts.

**16** The calamity of Moab is near at hand,  
and his disaster hurries on swiftly.

**17** Grieve for him, all you around him,  
and all who know his name;  
say, "How the mighty staff is broken,  
the glorious rod!"

**18** Come down from your glory,  
and sit on the parched ground,  
you who live there, daughter of Dibon;  
for the destroyer of Moab has come up against you;  
he has ruined your strongholds.

**19** Stand by the road and watch,  
you who live in Aroer!  
Ask the man who flees and the woman who escapes;  
say, "What has happened?"

**20** Moab is put to shame, for it is shattered;  
wail and cry out!  
Tell it by the Arnon  
that Moab is laid waste.

**21** Judgment has come upon the land of the plateau,  
upon Holon, Jahzah and Mephaath,

**22** upon Dibon, Nebo and Beth-diblathaim,

**23** upon Kiriathaim, Beth-gamul and Beth-meon,

**24** upon Kerioth and Bozrah, and upon all the cities of the land of Moab, far and near.

**25** The horn of Moab is cut off,  
and his arm is broken,  
declares the LORD.

**26** Make him drunk,  
for he has made himself great against the LORD;  
Moab will wallow in his vomit,  
and he too will become a laughingstock.

**27** Was not Israel a laughingstock to you?  
Was he found among thieves,  
that whenever you spoke of him  
you shook your head?

**28** Leave the cities and live among the rocks,  
you who live in Moab!  
Be like the dove that nests  
on the walls of the pit's mouth.

**29** We have heard of the pride of Moab —  
how very proud he is! —  
of his loftiness, his pride, his arrogance,  
and the haughtiness of his heart.

**30** I Myself know his fury, declares the LORD;  
it is empty;  
his boasting has done nothing.

**31** Therefore I wail for Moab;  
for all Moab I cry out;  
for the men of Kir-heres there is moaning.

**32** More than the weeping of Jazer I weep for you,  
vine of Sibmah!  
Your shoots crossed over the sea;  
they reached as far as the sea of Jazer;  
on your summer fruit and on your vintage  
the destroyer has fallen.

**33** Joy and gladness are taken away  
from the fruitful field and from the land of Moab;  
I have put an end to the wine in the presses;  
no one treads with a shout;  
the shout is no shout.

**34** From the cry of Heshbon to Elealeh,  
as far as Jahaz they raise their voice,  
from Zoar to Horonaim and Eglath-shelishiyah;  
for even the waters of Nimrim  
have become desolate.

**35** I will put an end in Moab, declares the LORD,  
to the one who offers on the high place  
and burns incense to his gods.

**36** Therefore my heart moans for Moab like flutes;  
my heart moans like flutes for the men of Kir-heres;  
therefore the wealth they have gained has perished.

**37** For every head is shaved bald,  
and every beard is cut off;  
on every hand there are gashes,  
and on the loins sackcloth.

**38** On all the housetops of Moab and in her squares  
there is nothing but lamentation;  
for I have broken Moab like a jar no one wants,  
declares the LORD.

**39** How it is shattered! How they wail!  
How Moab has turned his back in shame!  
So Moab will become a laughingstock  
and a terror to all around him.

**40** For this is what the LORD says:  
One will swoop like an eagle  
and spread his wings over Moab.

**41** Kerioth is taken,  
and the strongholds are seized.  
The heart of Moab's warriors in that day  
will be like the heart of a woman in labor.

**42** Moab will be destroyed and be a people no more,  
for he has made himself great against the LORD.

**43** Terror and the pit and the snare  
are upon you, inhabitant of Moab!  
declares the LORD.

**44** Whoever flees from the terror  
will fall into the pit,  
and whoever climbs out of the pit  
will be caught in the snare.  
For I will bring these upon her, upon Moab,  
the year of their punishment,  
declares the LORD.

**45** In the shadow of Heshbon  
the fugitives stand without strength;  
for fire has gone out from Heshbon,  
a flame from the midst of Sihon;  
it has devoured the brow of Moab,  
the crown of the sons of tumult.

**46** Woe to you, Moab!  
The people of Chemosh are lost;  
for your sons are taken into captivity,  
and your daughters into captivity.

**47** Yet I will restore the fortunes of Moab  
in the end of days, declares the LORD.  
Here ends the judgment on Moab.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "In Heshbon they have planned evil against her… You too, Madmen, will be silenced"** — two plays on names: in *Cheshbon* they *chashvu*, "planned," and *Madmen* will *tiddommi*, "be silenced."
- **vv5, 29–38, 43–44 "the ascent of Luhith… We have heard of the pride of Moab… Terror and the pit and the snare"** — much of this chapter is also in Isaiah's oracle on Moab (Isaiah 15–16) and in Isaiah 24:17–18, often word for word, and where the Hebrew is the same the English is the same. Where Jeremiah changes a word, the English shows it: Isaiah's *harp* (Isaiah 16:11) is *flutes* here (v36); Isaiah's *inhabitant of the earth* is *inhabitant of Moab* (v43); and where Isaiah has *the shout has fallen* on the harvest (Isaiah 16:9), Jeremiah has *the destroyer has fallen* (v32).
- **v7 "Chemosh will go into exile, his priests and his princes together"** — the god of Moab is carried off like a captive king, with his court. The same words are said of Milcom, the god of Ammon, at 49:3. The law called Moab *the people of Chemosh* (Numbers 21:29), and v46 repeats it.
- **v9 "Give wings to Moab, for she must fly away"** — *tsits* is elsewhere a blossom (Isaiah 40:7) or the gold plate on the high priest's turban (Exodus 28:36), and *natso*, "fly," is found elsewhere only of fugitives (Lamentations 4:15); *wings* is the sense the verse asks for, and it is uncertain. The Greek has *Give signs to Moab*.
- **vv11–12 "settled on his dregs… not poured from vessel to vessel… men who tip wine… they will empty his vessels and smash their jars"** — Moab is wine left on its sediment and never decanted, so it has kept its old taste; that is, it has never gone into exile. Now the cellarmen come, and they do not decant it; they empty it out and break the jars.
- **v13 "as the house of Israel was ashamed of Bethel, their trust"** — Bethel, where Jeroboam *set the one* calf (1 Kings 12:29), stands beside Chemosh as a god that failed its people.
- **vv26–27 "Make him drunk… Moab will wallow in his vomit"** — the cup of the nations: *Drink, get drunk and vomit, fall and rise no more* (25:27). Moab laughed at Israel, and becomes the one laughed at.
- **v33 "no one treads with a shout; the shout is no shout"** — *heidad*, the shout of the grape-treaders, which the LORD Himself raises against the earth at 25:30, *like those who tread grapes*. The shout heard in Moab's vineyards is no longer the vintage.
- **v40 "One will swoop like an eagle"** — the law's warning of the enemy *swooping as the eagle swoops* (Deuteronomy 28:49). The same words are used of Edom at 49:22.
- **vv45–47 "fire has gone out from Heshbon… the brow of Moab, the crown of the sons of tumult… Woe to you, Moab!"** — an old song from the conquest, *For fire went out from Heshbon, a flame from the town of Sihon… Woe to you, Moab! You are lost, people of Chemosh!* (Numbers 21:28–29), joined to Balaam's oracle, *It shall crush the brow of Moab and tear down all the sons of Sheth* (Numbers 24:17), with *sha'on*, "tumult," standing where Numbers has *Sheth*. The Greek has nothing of vv45–47, and so no restoring of Moab's fortunes.

# Jeremiah 10

**1** Hear the word that the LORD speaks to you, house of Israel.

**2** Thus says the LORD:  
Do not learn the way of the nations,  
and do not be dismayed at the signs of the heavens,  
though the nations are dismayed at them;

**3** for the customs of the peoples are emptiness.  
A tree is cut from the forest,  
the work of a craftsman's hands with an axe.

**4** They adorn it with silver and gold;  
they fasten it with nails and hammers  
so that it will not totter.

**5** They are like a scarecrow in a cucumber field,  
and they cannot speak;  
they have to be carried,  
for they cannot walk.  
Do not be afraid of them,  
for they can do no harm,  
and neither is it in them to do good.

**6** There is none like You, LORD;  
You are great,  
and Your name is great in might.

**7** Who would not fear You, King of the nations?  
For this is Your due;  
for among all the wise of the nations  
and in all their kingdoms  
there is none like You.

**8** They are all senseless and foolish;  
the teaching of emptiness is wood.

**9** Beaten silver is brought from Tarshish,  
and gold from Uphaz,  
the work of the craftsman and of the goldsmith's hands;  
their clothing is blue and purple;  
they are all the work of skilled men.

**10** But the LORD is the true God;  
He is the living God and the everlasting King.  
At His wrath the earth quakes,  
and the nations cannot endure His indignation.

**11** Thus you shall say to them: The gods who did not make the heavens and the earth will perish from the earth and from under these heavens.

**12** It is He who made the earth by His power,  
who established the world by His wisdom,  
and by His understanding stretched out the heavens.

**13** When He utters His voice,  
there is a roar of waters in the heavens;  
He makes the clouds rise from the ends of the earth;  
He makes lightning for the rain  
and brings out the wind from His storehouses.

**14** Every man is senseless, without knowledge;  
every goldsmith is put to shame by his idol;  
for his images are falsehood,  
and there is no breath in them.

**15** They are emptiness, a work of mockery;  
in the time of their punishment they will perish.

**16** Not like these is the Portion of Jacob,  
for He is the One who formed all things,  
and Israel is the tribe of His inheritance;  
the LORD of hosts is His name.

**17** Gather up your bundle from the ground,  
you who live under siege!

**18** For thus says the LORD:  
I am about to sling out the inhabitants of the land  
at this time,  
and I will bring distress on them,  
so that they may find it so.

**19** Woe is me because of my wound!  
My wound is grievous.  
But I said, "Surely this is a sickness,  
and I must bear it."

**20** My tent is destroyed,  
and all my cords are broken;  
my sons have gone from me,  
and they are no more;  
there is no one to spread my tent again  
and to set up my curtains.

**21** For the shepherds have become senseless  
and have not sought the LORD;  
therefore they have not prospered,  
and all their flock is scattered.

**22** The sound of a report: it is coming,  
a great commotion from the land of the north,  
to make the cities of Judah a desolation,  
a lair of jackals.

**23** I know, LORD, that a man's way is not his own;  
it is not in a man who walks  
to direct his steps.

**24** Correct me, LORD, but with justice;  
not in Your anger,  
or You will bring me to nothing.

**25** Pour out Your wrath on the nations that do not know You,  
and on the clans that do not call on Your name;  
for they have devoured Jacob;  
they have devoured him and consumed him,  
and have laid waste his pasture.

## Notes

- **v4 "they fasten it with nails and hammers so that it will not totter"** — Isaiah's picture of the idol-maker, who *fastens it with nails so that it will not totter* (Isaiah 41:7; also 40:20). A god that must be nailed down so as not to fall over.
- **v5 "like a scarecrow in a cucumber field"** — *ke-tomer miqshah*. *Miqshah* is the *cucumber field* of Isaiah 1:8, *like a hut in a cucumber field*, and the Letter of Jeremiah has the same picture: *like a scarecrow in a cucumber field that guards nothing, so are their gods of wood, overlaid with gold and silver* (Letter of Jeremiah 1:69, Swete's Greek). *Miqshah* is also the word for *hammered work*, the hammered gold of the lampstand (Exodus 25:31), and *tomer* can be a post: *like a post of hammered work*. The Greek has *it is chased silver*.
- **vv6–8, 10 "There is none like You, LORD… He is the living God and the everlasting King"** — the Greek does not have these verses; it goes from the idols of v5 to the silver of v9 and on to v11. The song John hears in heaven takes up v7: *Who will not fear, Lord, and glorify Your name?… All the nations will come and worship before You* (Revelation 15:4).
- **v9 "their clothing is blue and purple"** — *tekhelet ve-argaman*, two of the colors of the dwelling's curtains, *blue and purple and crimson* (Exodus 26:1), and of the priests' garments (28:5). The idol is dressed as the LORD's house was.
- **v11 "Thus you shall say to them: The gods who did not make the heavens and the earth will perish"** — this one verse is in Aramaic, the language Hezekiah's officials asked the Assyrian envoy to use (*Please speak to your servants in Aramaic*, 2 Kings 18:26): a sentence given to be said to the nations. It turns on a pair of sounds, *avadu*, "made," and *ye'vadu*, "will perish": the gods who did not make will be unmade.
- **vv12–16 "It is He who made the earth by His power"** — these five verses are given again, nearly word for word, in the oracle against Babylon (51:15–19). v13's last lines are those of Psalm 135:7, *He makes the clouds rise from the ends of the earth; He makes lightning for the rain and brings out the wind from His storehouses*.
- **v16 "Not like these is the Portion of Jacob"** — the Song of Moses says *the LORD's portion is His people, Jacob the measured lot of His inheritance* (Deuteronomy 32:9); here it is turned around, and the LORD is Jacob's portion, as He said to Aaron, *I am your share and your inheritance* (Numbers 18:20).
- **v20 "My tent is destroyed, and all my cords are broken… no one to spread my tent again"** — the opposite of Isaiah's promise to Zion, *Enlarge the place of your tent… lengthen your cords* (Isaiah 54:2).
- **vv23–24 "a man's way is not his own… Correct me, LORD, but with justice; not in Your anger"** — the wisdom of *A man's steps are from the LORD* (Proverbs 20:24), and the prayer of *LORD, do not rebuke me in Your anger* (Psalm 6:1). The Greek has *Correct us*.
- **v25 "Pour out Your wrath on the nations that do not know You"** — Psalm 79:6–7 has the same prayer, *and on the kingdoms that do not call on Your name, for they have devoured Jacob and laid waste his homeland*; Jeremiah has *clans* for *kingdoms*, and doubles the devouring.

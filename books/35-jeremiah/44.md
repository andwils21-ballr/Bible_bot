# Jeremiah 44

**1** The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the Jews who were living in the land of Egypt, living at Migdol, at Tahpanhes, at Memphis and in the land of Pathros:

**2** This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: You yourselves have seen all the disaster that I brought on Jerusalem and on all the cities of Judah. They are a ruin this day, and no one lives in them,

**3** because of the evil they did to provoke Me to anger, going to burn incense and to serve other gods whom they had not known, neither they, nor you, nor your fathers.

**4** Yet I sent you all My servants the prophets again and again, saying, "Please do not do this detestable thing that I hate."

**5** But they did not listen or incline their ear to turn from their evil, to stop burning incense to other gods.

**6** So My wrath and My anger were poured out and burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, and they became a ruin and a desolation, as they are this day.

**7** So now, this is what the LORD, the God of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Why are you bringing great disaster on yourselves, cutting off from yourselves man and woman, child and infant, from among Judah, leaving yourselves no remnant,

**8** provoking Me to anger with the works of your hands, burning incense to other gods in the land of Egypt where you have gone to live as guests, so that you will cut yourselves off and become a curse and a reproach among all the nations of the earth?

**9** Have you forgotten the evils of your fathers, the evils of the kings of Judah, the evils of their wives, your own evils and the evils of your wives, which they committed in the land of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?

**10** They have not been humbled to this day, nor have they feared, nor walked in My law and My statutes that I set before you and before your fathers.

**11** Therefore this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am about to set My face against you for harm, to cut off all Judah.

**12** I will take the remnant of Judah who set their faces to go to the land of Egypt to live there as guests, and they will all come to an end. In the land of Egypt they will fall; by the sword and by famine they will come to an end; from the least to the greatest, by the sword and by famine they will die; and they will become a name to curse by, a horror, a curse and a reproach.

**13** I will punish those who live in the land of Egypt as I punished Jerusalem, with the sword, with famine and with pestilence,

**14** so that none of the remnant of Judah who have gone to live as guests in the land of Egypt will escape or survive to return to the land of Judah, to which they long to return to live there; for they will not return, except a few who escape.

## The People Answer

**15** Then all the men who knew that their wives were burning incense to other gods, and all the women who stood by, a great assembly, and all the people who lived in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, answered Jeremiah:

**16** "As for the word that you have spoken to us in the name of the LORD, we will not listen to you.

**17** We will do everything that has come out of our mouth: we will burn incense to the queen of heaven and pour out drink offerings to her, as we did, we and our fathers, our kings and our princes, in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. Then we had our fill of bread, and we were well and saw no disaster.

**18** But since we stopped burning incense to the queen of heaven and pouring out drink offerings to her, we have lacked everything and have come to an end by the sword and by famine.

**19** When we burned incense to the queen of heaven and poured out drink offerings to her, was it without our husbands that we made cakes for her in her image and poured out drink offerings to her?"

**20** Then Jeremiah said to all the people, to the men and the women and all the people who had given him this answer:

**21** "As for the incense that you burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, you and your fathers, your kings and your princes and the people of the land — did the LORD not remember them? Did it not come into His mind?

**22** The LORD could no longer bear it, because of the evil of your deeds and because of the detestable things you have done; so your land has become a ruin, a horror and a curse, without inhabitant, as it is this day.

**23** Because you burned incense, and because you sinned against the LORD and did not obey the voice of the LORD, or walk in His law, His statutes and His testimonies, this disaster has happened to you, as it is this day."

**24** Jeremiah said to all the people and to all the women, "Hear the word of the LORD, all Judah who are in the land of Egypt.

**25** This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: You and your wives have spoken with your mouths and fulfilled it with your hands, saying, 'We will perform our vows that we have vowed, to burn incense to the queen of heaven and to pour out drink offerings to her.' Go on, then: let your vows stand, and perform your vows!

**26** Therefore hear the word of the LORD, all Judah who live in the land of Egypt: I have sworn by My great name, says the LORD, that My name will no longer be invoked by the mouth of any man of Judah in all the land of Egypt, saying, 'As the Lord GOD lives.'

**27** I will watch over them for harm and not for good, and all the men of Judah who are in the land of Egypt will come to an end by the sword and by famine, until they are finished.

**28** Those who escape the sword will return from the land of Egypt to the land of Judah, few in number; and all the remnant of Judah who went to the land of Egypt to live there as guests will know whose word will stand, Mine or theirs.

**29** This will be the sign to you, declares the LORD, that I will punish you in this place, so that you may know that My words will stand against you for harm:

**30** This is what the LORD says: I am about to give Pharaoh Hophra king of Egypt into the hand of his enemies and into the hand of those who seek his life, as I gave Zedekiah king of Judah into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, his enemy, who sought his life."

## Notes

- **v1 "at Migdol, at Tahpanhes, at Memphis and in the land of Pathros"** — Migdol is the name of the place where Israel camped on the way out of Egypt, *between Migdol and the sea* (Exodus 14:2). Pathros is among the lands Isaiah names for the remnant's return, *from Assyria, from Egypt, from Pathros* (Isaiah 11:11).
- **v7 "Why are you bringing great disaster on yourselves"** — *ra'ah gedolah*, the words of the elders who spoke up for Jeremiah when his life was at stake: *we are about to bring great disaster on ourselves* (26:19).
- **vv15–17, 19 "all the men who knew that their wives were burning incense… everything that has come out of our mouth… was it without our husbands"** — the answer is built on the law of vows. A man *shall do everything that has come out of his mouth* (Numbers 30:2), and a wife's vows *shall stand* when *her husband hears of it and says nothing to her on the day he hears* (30:7). The women speak in v19, as *our husbands* shows, and their defense is that their husbands knew.
- **vv17–18 "to the queen of heaven… Then we had our fill of bread… since we stopped… we have come to an end"** — the worship of 7:18, *the women knead dough to make cakes for the queen of heaven*. They read the history the other way round: the disaster began when the offerings stopped. *Tamam*, "come to an end," is the LORD's word for their end in Egypt (vv12, 27), and theirs for what has happened since they stopped (v18).
- **v21 "did the LORD not remember them? Did it not come into His mind?"** — the idiom of 7:31, where the burning of children was what *I did not command, nor did it come into My mind*. Their incense did come into His mind.
- **vv25, 28–29 "let your vows stand… whose word will stand, Mine or theirs… My words will stand against you"** — *qum*, the word of the vow law, *shall stand against her* (Numbers 30:9). In v25 most of the verbs are feminine, addressed to the women; the Greek has *You women have spoken with your mouth*.
- **v26 "As the Lord GOD lives"** — the oath Judah was to swear *in truth, in justice and in righteousness* (4:2). The book once promised that *it will no longer be said, 'As the LORD lives, who brought up the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt'*, because a greater return would replace it (16:14–15). In Egypt the oath itself will end.
- **v27 "I will watch over them for harm and not for good"** — *shaqad*, the word behind the almond branch of the call, *I am watching over My word to do it* (1:11–12), and of the promise, *as I have watched over them to uproot… so I will watch over them to build* (31:28).
- **v28 "few in number"** — the law's word for exile: *you will be left few in number among the nations* (Deuteronomy 4:27).
- **v30 "Pharaoh Hophra… as I gave Zedekiah"** — the Greek names him *Vaphres*. Zedekiah was given into the hand of the king of Babylon at Riblah (39:5–7).

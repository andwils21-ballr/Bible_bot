# Jeremiah 4

**1** If you return, Israel, declares the LORD,  
return to Me;  
if you put your loathsome things out of My sight,  
and do not waver,

**2** and swear, "As the LORD lives,"  
in truth, in justice and in righteousness,  
then nations will bless themselves in Him,  
and in Him they will glory.

**3** For this is what the LORD says to the men of Judah and to Jerusalem:  
Break up your fallow ground,  
and do not sow among thorns.

**4** Circumcise yourselves to the LORD;  
remove the foreskins of your hearts,  
men of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem;  
or My wrath will go out like fire  
and burn with no one to quench it,  
because of the evil of your deeds.

## Disaster from the North

**5** Declare in Judah, proclaim in Jerusalem, and say:  
Blow the ram's horn in the land!  
Cry aloud and say:  
"Gather together, and let us go  
into the fortified cities!"

**6** Raise a signal toward Zion;  
flee for safety; do not stand still;  
for I am bringing disaster from the north,  
and great destruction.

**7** A lion has gone up from his thicket;  
a destroyer of nations has set out;  
he has gone out from his place  
to make your land a desolation;  
your cities will be ruins,  
without inhabitant.

**8** For this, put on sackcloth;  
lament and wail;  
for the fierce anger of the LORD  
has not turned back from us.

**9** And it shall come to pass in that day, declares the LORD, that the courage of the king and the courage of the princes will fail; the priests will be appalled, and the prophets astounded.

**10** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! Surely You have utterly deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, 'You will have peace,' when the sword has reached the throat."

**11** At that time it will be said to this people and to Jerusalem:  
A scorching wind from the bare heights in the wilderness  
comes toward the daughter of My people,  
not to winnow and not to cleanse;

**12** a wind too strong for that  
comes at My command.  
Now I Myself will speak judgments against them.

**13** He comes up like clouds,  
his chariots like the whirlwind;  
his horses are swifter than eagles.  
Woe to us, for we are ruined!

**14** Wash your heart from evil, Jerusalem,  
so that you may be saved.  
How long will your wicked thoughts  
lodge within you?

**15** For a voice declares from Dan,  
and proclaims disaster from Mount Ephraim.

**16** Warn the nations;  
proclaim against Jerusalem:  
Besiegers are coming from a distant land;  
they raise their voice against the cities of Judah.

**17** Like keepers of a field they surround her,  
because she has rebelled against Me,  
declares the LORD.

**18** Your way and your deeds  
have brought this on you.  
This is your disaster; how bitter it is!  
How it reaches your very heart!

**19** My insides, my insides! I writhe in pain!  
The walls of my heart!  
My heart is pounding within me;  
I cannot keep silent;  
for you have heard, my soul,  
the sound of the ram's horn,  
the alarm of war.

**20** Disaster on disaster is proclaimed,  
for the whole land is devastated;  
suddenly my tents are devastated,  
my curtains in a moment.

**21** How long must I see the signal  
and hear the sound of the ram's horn?

**22** For My people are foolish;  
they do not know Me;  
they are senseless sons;  
they have no understanding.  
They are wise in doing evil,  
but how to do good they do not know.

**23** I looked at the earth, and it was wild and waste;  
and at the heavens, and their light was gone.

**24** I looked at the mountains, and they were quaking,  
and all the hills were swaying.

**25** I looked, and there was no man,  
and all the birds of the heavens had fled.

**26** I looked, and the fruitful land was a wilderness,  
and all its cities were pulled down  
before the LORD,  
before His fierce anger.

**27** For this is what the LORD says:  
The whole land will be a desolation;  
yet I will not make a full end.

**28** For this the earth will mourn,  
and the heavens above grow dark,  
because I have spoken, I have purposed;  
I have not relented, and I will not turn back from it.

**29** At the noise of horseman and archer  
every city flees;  
they go into the thickets  
and climb among the rocks;  
every city is forsaken,  
and no man lives in them.

**30** As for you, devastated one, what will you do?  
Though you dress in scarlet,  
though you adorn yourself with ornaments of gold,  
though you enlarge your eyes with paint,  
in vain you make yourself beautiful.  
Your lovers despise you;  
they seek your life.

**31** For I heard a cry as of a woman in labor,  
anguish as of one bearing her first child,  
the cry of the daughter of Zion gasping for breath,  
stretching out her hands:  
"Woe is me! My soul faints  
before murderers."

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "then nations will bless themselves in Him"** — *ve-hitbarekhu*, the form of the verb in the promise to Abraham, *In your seed all the nations of the earth shall bless themselves* (Genesis 22:18). Israel's return is the way the blessing reaches the nations.
- **vv3–4 "Break up your fallow ground… remove the foreskins of your hearts"** — *niru lakhem nir*, the words of Hosea 10:12, *Break up your fallow ground; it is time to seek the LORD*; and the command of Deuteronomy 10:16, *Circumcise, then, the foreskin of your heart*. Paul: *circumcision is of the heart, by the Spirit, not by the letter* (Romans 2:29).
- **v10 "Surely You have utterly deceived this people… saying, 'You will have peace'"** — *hashe hishe'ta*, the verb doubled. The *peace* is the message of the prophets who speak in the LORD's name, *Peace, peace, when there is no peace* (6:14), and Jeremiah lays it before the LORD as a charge. The Greek also has *I said*.
- **vv23–26 "I looked at the earth, and it was wild and waste"** — *tohu va-vohu*, the words of Genesis 1:2, *the earth was wild and waste*, found together elsewhere only in Isaiah 34:11, *the line of the wild and the stones of the waste*. Then the creation of Genesis 1 runs backward: the light of the first day is gone, there is no man (the sixth day), the birds have fled (the fifth), and the fruitful land is a wilderness (the third). Four times the prophet says *I looked*.
- **v27 "yet I will not make a full end"** — *ve-khalah lo e'eseh*. The same limit is set twice more in the next chapter, *do not make a full end* (5:10) and *I will not make a full end of you* (5:18).
- **v30 "though you enlarge your eyes with paint"** — *puk*, the eye paint of Jezebel, who *painted her eyes and adorned her head and looked down from the window* (2 Kings 9:30) as her killer arrived. Jerusalem dresses for lovers who now want her life.
- **v31 "as of one bearing her first child"** — at the end of Isaiah, Zion gave birth *before she was in labor* (Isaiah 66:7). Here the daughter of Zion is in labor, and what comes is the murderers.

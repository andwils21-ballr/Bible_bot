# Jeremiah 15

**1** Then the LORD said to me: "Even if Moses and Samuel stood before Me, My heart would not turn toward this people. Send them away from My presence, and let them go!

**2** And it shall come to pass, when they say to you, 'Where shall we go?' that you shall say to them, 'This is what the LORD says:  
Those who are for death, to death;  
those who are for the sword, to the sword;  
those who are for famine, to famine;  
and those who are for captivity, to captivity.'

**3** I will appoint over them four kinds of destroyers, declares the LORD: the sword to kill, the dogs to drag away, and the birds of the heavens and the beasts of the earth to devour and destroy.

**4** I will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth because of Manasseh son of Hezekiah, king of Judah, for what he did in Jerusalem.

**5** Who will have pity on you, Jerusalem?  
Who will mourn for you?  
Who will turn aside  
to ask about your welfare?

**6** You have rejected Me, declares the LORD;  
you keep going backward;  
so I have stretched out My hand against you and destroyed you;  
I am weary of relenting.

**7** I have winnowed them with a winnowing fork  
in the gates of the land;  
I have bereaved them; I have destroyed My people;  
they did not turn from their ways.

**8** Their widows have become more numerous before Me  
than the sand of the seas;  
I have brought against the mothers of young men  
a destroyer at noonday;  
I have made anguish and terror  
fall on them suddenly.

**9** She who bore seven languishes;  
she has breathed out her soul;  
her sun has gone down while it was still day;  
she is shamed and disgraced.  
The rest of them I will give to the sword  
before their enemies,  
declares the LORD."

**10** Woe is me, my mother, that you bore me,  
a man of strife and contention to the whole land!  
I have not lent, nor have they lent to me,  
yet every one of them curses me.

**11** The LORD said: Surely I will set you free for good;  
surely I will make the enemy plead with you  
in the time of disaster and the time of distress.

**12** Can one break iron,  
iron from the north, and bronze?

**13** Your wealth and your treasures  
I will give as plunder, without price,  
for all your sins,  
throughout all your territory.

**14** I will make you serve your enemies  
in a land you do not know;  
for a fire is kindled in My anger;  
it will burn against you.

**15** You know, LORD;  
remember me and visit me,  
and avenge me on my persecutors.  
In Your slowness to anger do not take me away;  
know that for Your sake I bear reproach.

**16** Your words were found, and I ate them,  
and Your word became to me a joy  
and the delight of my heart;  
for I am called by Your name,  
LORD God of hosts.

**17** I did not sit in the company of revelers,  
nor did I rejoice;  
because of Your hand I sat alone,  
for You had filled me with indignation.

**18** Why is my pain unending,  
my wound incurable,  
refusing to be healed?  
Will You be to me like a deceitful brook,  
like waters that fail?

**19** Therefore this is what the LORD says:  
If you return, I will restore you,  
and you shall stand before Me;  
if you bring out what is precious from what is worthless,  
you shall be as My mouth.  
They shall turn to you,  
but you shall not turn to them.

**20** I will make you to this people  
a fortified wall of bronze;  
they will fight against you,  
but they will not overcome you,  
for I am with you to save you and deliver you,  
declares the LORD.

**21** I will deliver you out of the hand of the wicked,  
and redeem you from the grasp of the ruthless.

## Notes

- **v1 "Even if Moses and Samuel stood before Me"** — the two great intercessors. Moses *pleaded with the LORD his God* after the golden calf (Exodus 32:11), and Samuel said, *far be it from me that I should sin against the LORD by ceasing to pray for you* (1 Samuel 12:23); the psalm names them together, *Moses and Aaron were among His priests, Samuel among those who called on His name* (Psalm 99:6).
- **v2 "Those who are for death, to death… for captivity, to captivity"** — Revelation takes up the words: *If anyone is for captivity, into captivity he goes; if anyone is to be killed with the sword, with the sword he must be killed* (Revelation 13:10).
- **v4 "a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth because of Manasseh"** — the curse of Deuteronomy 28:25, *you will be a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth*, and the reason the book of Kings gives for the fall, *for the sins of Manasseh, according to all that he had done* (2 Kings 24:3).
- **vv8–9 "more numerous… than the sand of the seas… She who bore seven languishes"** — the measure of the promise to Abraham, *as the sand that is on the lip of the sea* (Genesis 22:17), now counts widows. Hannah sang, *The barren has borne seven, and she who has many children languishes* (1 Samuel 2:5); here the mother of seven *languishes*, the same verb, and her sun goes down at noon.
- **vv11–12 "Surely I will set you free for good… Can one break iron, iron from the north, and bronze?"** — both verses are hard in the Hebrew. *Sheritikha*, "I will set you free," is the read form; the written form *sherotikha* is uncertain, and the Greek has *So be it, Master*. Iron from the north is the enemy of 1:14, or the prophet made *an iron pillar and bronze walls* (1:18); the verse does not say which.
- **vv13–14 "Your wealth and your treasures I will give as plunder… I will make you serve your enemies"** — these lines come again in 17:3–4, where the Hebrew has *I will make you serve your enemies in a land you do not know*. Here the Hebrew has *ha'avarti*, "I will make pass," one letter from *ha'avadti*, "I will make serve"; the text follows 17:4 and the Greek, which has *serve* here as well. It is the curse of the covenant, *you will serve your enemies* (Deuteronomy 28:48).
- **v15 "In Your slowness to anger do not take me away"** — *le-erekh appekha*, the LORD's name proclaimed to Moses, *slow to anger, and abundant in loyal love* (Exodus 34:6). The prophet asks that the patience shown to his enemies not cost him his life.
- **v16 "Your words were found, and I ate them"** — Ezekiel is given a scroll to eat, *and it was in my mouth as sweet as honey* (Ezekiel 3:3), and so is John (Revelation 10:10). In Josiah's reign the high priest Hilkiah said, *I have found the book of the instruction in the house of the LORD* (2 Kings 22:8); Jeremiah's father bore the name Hilkiah (1:1), and nothing in the text says whether they were the same man.
- **v18 "like a deceitful brook, like waters that fail"** — *akhzav*, a stream that runs dry when it is needed. The LORD called Himself *the fountain of living waters* (2:13); the prophet asks whether He has become the opposite.
- **v19 "If you return… you shall stand before Me… you shall be as My mouth"** — *shuv* four times: *If you return, I will restore you… They shall turn to you, but you shall not turn to them*. *Stand before Me* is the prophet's service, as Elijah said, *the LORD, the God of Israel… before whom I stand* (1 Kings 17:1); *as My mouth* is what Aaron was to Moses, *he will be a mouth for you* (Exodus 4:16).

# Jeremiah 14

**1** The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah concerning the drought:

**2** Judah mourns,  
and her gates languish;  
they sit in black on the ground,  
and the cry of Jerusalem goes up.

**3** Their nobles send their servants for water;  
they come to the cisterns;  
they find no water;  
they return with their jars empty;  
they are ashamed and dismayed,  
and cover their heads.

**4** Because of the ground, which is cracked,  
for there has been no rain in the land,  
the farmers are ashamed;  
they cover their heads.

**5** Even the doe in the field  
gives birth and abandons her fawn,  
because there is no grass.

**6** The wild donkeys stand on the bare heights;  
they pant for air like jackals;  
their eyes fail  
because there is no vegetation.

**7** Though our iniquities testify against us,  
act, LORD, for Your name's sake;  
for our faithlessness is great;  
we have sinned against You.

**8** Hope of Israel,  
its Savior in time of trouble,  
why should You be like a guest in the land,  
like a traveler who turns aside to lodge for a night?

**9** Why should You be like a man dismayed,  
like a mighty man who cannot save?  
Yet You, LORD, are in our midst,  
and we are called by Your name;  
do not leave us.

**10** This is what the LORD says to this people:  
They have loved to wander like this;  
they have not restrained their feet;  
so the LORD does not accept them;  
now He will remember their iniquity  
and punish their sins.

**11** The LORD said to me: "Do not pray for the good of this people.

**12** Though they fast, I will not hear their cry; and though they offer burnt offering and grain offering, I will not accept them; but I will consume them by the sword, by famine and by pestilence."

**13** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! The prophets are telling them, 'You will not see the sword, and you will not have famine; but I will give you true peace in this place.'"

**14** The LORD said to me: "The prophets are prophesying lies in My name. I did not send them, or command them, or speak to them. They are prophesying to you a lying vision, divination, a worthless thing and the deceit of their own hearts.

**15** Therefore this is what the LORD says concerning the prophets who prophesy in My name although I did not send them, and who say, 'Sword and famine will not come on this land': By sword and famine those prophets will be consumed.

**16** The people to whom they prophesy will be thrown out into the streets of Jerusalem because of famine and sword, with no one to bury them — them, their wives, their sons and their daughters; for I will pour out their evil on them.

**17** You shall say this word to them:  
'Let my eyes run down with tears  
night and day, and let them not cease,  
for the virgin daughter of my people  
is broken with a great breaking,  
with a very grievous wound.

**18** If I go out into the field,  
there are those slain by the sword!  
If I enter the city,  
there are the diseases of famine!  
For both prophet and priest  
go about in a land they do not know.'"

**19** Have You utterly rejected Judah?  
Does Your soul loathe Zion?  
Why have You struck us down  
so that there is no healing for us?  
We hoped for peace, but no good came;  
for a time of healing, but there is terror.

**20** We acknowledge our wickedness, LORD,  
the iniquity of our fathers,  
for we have sinned against You.

**21** Do not spurn us, for Your name's sake;  
do not dishonor Your glorious throne;  
remember, do not break Your covenant with us.

**22** Are there any among the emptinesses of the nations who bring rain?  
Or can the heavens give showers?  
Are You not He, LORD our God?  
We hope in You,  
for You have made all these things.

## Notes

- **vv3–4 "they cover their heads"** — the sign of grief and shame: David went up the Mount of Olives *with his head covered*, and *all the people who were with him covered their heads* (2 Samuel 15:30).
- **v8 "why should You be like a guest in the land"** — *ger*, the word the law uses for the foreigner who lives among Israel, *The guest living with you shall be to you as a native among you* (Leviticus 19:34). The people ask why the LORD acts like a stranger passing through His own land. The Greek has *a sojourner*.
- **v10 "now He will remember their iniquity and punish their sins"** — the words of Hosea 8:13, said there of the northern kingdom's sacrifices: *the LORD does not accept them; now He will remember their iniquity and punish their sins*.
- **vv11–12 "Do not pray for the good of this people… by the sword, by famine and by pestilence"** — the third time the prophet is told not to pray (7:16; 11:14). *The sword, famine and pestilence* is a triad that runs through the book (21:9; 27:8; 29:18; 38:2; 44:13), first here.
- **vv13–15 "The prophets are prophesying lies in My name. I did not send them"** — the law of the false prophet, *the prophet who presumes to speak a word in My name that I have not commanded him to speak… that prophet shall die* (Deuteronomy 18:20). They promised that sword and famine would not come; by sword and famine they will die.
- **v19 "We hoped for peace, but no good came; for a time of healing, but there is terror"** — the words of 8:15, said again.
- **v21 "do not dishonor Your glorious throne"** — *kisse khevodekha*. Jerusalem is *the throne of the LORD* (3:17), and the sanctuary *a glorious throne, on high from the beginning* (17:12).
- **v22 "Are there any among the emptinesses of the nations who bring rain?"** — *havlei ha-goyim*, the *emptiness* (*hevel*) of 2:5 and 10:15, the idols. The LORD is the One *who gives the rain in its season* (5:24) and *makes lightning for the rain* (10:13).

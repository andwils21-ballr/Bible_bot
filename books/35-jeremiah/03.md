# Jeremiah 3

**1** If a man sends away his wife,  
and she goes from him  
and becomes another man's wife,  
will he return to her again?  
Would not that land be greatly polluted?  
But you have prostituted yourself with many lovers;  
and would you return to Me?  
declares the LORD.

**2** Lift up your eyes to the bare heights and see:  
where have you not been lain with?  
By the roads you sat waiting for them  
like an Arab in the wilderness.  
You have polluted the land  
with your prostitution and your wickedness.

**3** Therefore the showers have been withheld,  
and the late rain has not come;  
yet you have a prostitute's forehead;  
you refuse to be ashamed.

**4** Have you not just now called to Me,  
"My Father, You are the companion of my youth!

**5** Will He keep His anger forever?  
Will He hold on to it to the end?"  
This is how you have spoken,  
yet you have done all the evil you could.

## Faithless Israel and Treacherous Judah

**6** The LORD said to me in the days of King Josiah, "Have you seen what faithless Israel did? She went up on every high mountain and under every green tree, and prostituted herself there.

**7** I said, 'After she has done all this, she will return to Me'; but she did not return. Her treacherous sister Judah saw it.

**8** I saw that, though I had sent faithless Israel away and given her a certificate of divorce for all her adulteries, her treacherous sister Judah did not fear; she too went and prostituted herself.

**9** Because her prostitution was so light a thing to her, she polluted the land, committing adultery with stone and wood.

**10** Yet for all this her treacherous sister Judah did not return to Me with her whole heart, but in falsehood, declares the LORD."

**11** The LORD said to me, "Faithless Israel has shown herself more righteous than treacherous Judah.

**12** Go and proclaim these words toward the north, and say:  
Return, faithless Israel, declares the LORD;  
I will not look on you in anger,  
for I am loyal, declares the LORD;  
I will not keep My anger forever.

**13** Only acknowledge your iniquity,  
that you have rebelled against the LORD your God  
and scattered your ways to strangers  
under every green tree,  
and have not obeyed My voice,  
declares the LORD.

**14** Return, faithless sons, declares the LORD, for I am your husband; I will take you, one from a city and two from a clan, and bring you to Zion.

**15** I will give you shepherds after My own heart, who will feed you with knowledge and understanding.

**16** And it shall come to pass, when you have multiplied and been fruitful in the land, in those days, declares the LORD, that they will no longer say, 'The ark of the covenant of the LORD.' It will not come to mind; they will not remember it or miss it, and it will not be made again.

**17** At that time they will call Jerusalem the throne of the LORD, and all the nations will gather to it, to the name of the LORD at Jerusalem; and they will no longer walk after the stubbornness of their evil heart.

**18** In those days the house of Judah will walk with the house of Israel, and together they will come from the land of the north to the land I gave your fathers as an inheritance.

**19** I said, 'How I would set you among the sons, and give you a pleasant land, the most beautiful inheritance of the nations!' And I said, 'You will call Me My Father, and will not turn back from following Me.'

**20** Surely, as a woman betrays her companion, so you have betrayed Me, house of Israel, declares the LORD."

**21** A voice is heard on the bare heights,  
the weeping and pleading of the sons of Israel,  
because they have perverted their way;  
they have forgotten the LORD their God.

**22** Return, faithless sons;  
I will heal your faithlessness.  
"Here we are; we come to You,  
for You are the LORD our God.

**23** Surely the hills are a lie,  
the uproar on the mountains;  
surely in the LORD our God  
is the deliverance of Israel.

**24** But the Shame has devoured  
all that our fathers labored for from our youth:  
their flocks and their herds,  
their sons and their daughters.

**25** Let us lie down in our shame,  
and let our disgrace cover us;  
for we have sinned against the LORD our God,  
we and our fathers,  
from our youth to this day,  
and we have not obeyed the voice of the LORD our God."

## Notes

- **v1 "will he return to her again?… and would you return to Me?"** — the law of Deuteronomy 24:1–4: a man who has sent his wife away with *a certificate of divorce* *may not take her again to be his wife after she has been made unclean, for that is detestable before the LORD. You shall not bring sin on the land*. The verse asks the law's question. *Ve-shov elai* has no mark of a question, and can also be read as a call, *yet return to Me*, the call the chapter makes three times (vv12, 14, 22). The Greek has the question.
- **v3 "the late rain has not come"** — *malqosh*, the rain the LORD promised, *the early rain and the late rain* (Deuteronomy 11:14), and said He would withhold if the people served other gods: *He will shut up the heavens so that there is no rain* (11:16–17).
- **v4 "the companion of my youth"** — *alluf ne'urai*, the words of Proverbs 2:17, the woman *who forsakes the companion of her youth*. Here the faithless wife still calls the LORD by that name.
- **vv6, 12, 14, 22 "faithless… Return"** — *meshuvah*, "turning away," and *shuvah*, "return," are one root, *shuv*: *Return, turned-away Israel*. The play runs through the chapter, *Return, faithless sons* (vv14, 22), *I will heal your faithlessness* (v22), and began at 2:19. *Treacherous* (*bogedah*) is from *bagad*, "betray," the verb of v20.
- **v8 "a certificate of divorce"** — *sefer keritut*, the words of Deuteronomy 24:1. Isaiah asked, *Where is your mother's certificate of divorce, with which I sent her away?* (Isaiah 50:1). Here it was given, to Israel, the northern kingdom; Judah saw it and did not fear.
- **v10 "did not return to Me with her whole heart"** — said in the days of Josiah (v6), the king of whom it is written that *there was no king like him, who turned to the LORD with all his heart* (2 Kings 23:25), the same verb and the same phrase, *with all his heart*. The king turned; the people turned in falsehood.
- **v14 "for I am your husband"** — *ba'alti*, from *ba'al*, "master, husband," the name of the god of 2:8 and 2:23. Hosea has the same play: *you will call Me "My husband," and no longer call Me "My Baal"* (Hosea 2:16). The Greek has *I will be lord over you*.
- **vv16–17 "The ark of the covenant of the LORD… will not be made again… the throne of the LORD"** — the only place Jeremiah names the ark. It was the seat of *the LORD of hosts, who is enthroned on the cherubim* (1 Samuel 4:4); here the throne is Jerusalem itself.
- **v19 "How I would set you among the sons"** — *ashitekh*, "set you," feminine: the daughter is given a son's place and inheritance, as the daughters of Zelophehad were given *a holding as an inheritance among their father's brothers* (Numbers 27:7).
- **v24 "the Shame"** — *ha-boshet*, Jeremiah's name for Baal: *altars to the Shame, altars to burn incense to Baal* (11:13). It is the god that devoured the flocks, the herds and the children.

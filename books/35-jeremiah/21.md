# Jeremiah 21

**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD when King Zedekiah sent to him Pashhur son of Malchiah and Zephaniah son of Maaseiah, the priest, saying,

**2** "Please inquire of the LORD for us, for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon is making war against us. Perhaps the LORD will deal with us according to all His wonderful works, and make him withdraw from us."

**3** Jeremiah said to them, "This is what you shall say to Zedekiah:

**4** This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I am about to turn back the weapons of war that are in your hands, with which you are fighting the king of Babylon and the Chaldeans who are besieging you outside the wall, and I will gather them into the middle of this city.

**5** I Myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and a strong arm, in anger and in fury and in great wrath.

**6** I will strike the inhabitants of this city, both man and beast; they will die of a great pestilence.

**7** After that, declares the LORD, I will give Zedekiah king of Judah, his servants and the people, those in this city who survive the pestilence, the sword and the famine, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, into the hand of their enemies and into the hand of those who seek their life. He will strike them down with the sword; he will not pity them or spare them or have compassion."

**8** To this people you shall say: This is what the LORD says: I am setting before you the way of life and the way of death.

**9** Whoever stays in this city will die by the sword, by famine and by pestilence; but whoever goes out and surrenders to the Chaldeans who are besieging you will live, and his life will be his as plunder.

**10** For I have set My face against this city for harm and not for good, declares the LORD. It will be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire.

## The House of David

**11** To the house of the king of Judah: Hear the word of the LORD,

**12** house of David! This is what the LORD says:  
Execute justice in the morning,  
and rescue the one who has been robbed  
from the hand of the oppressor;  
or My wrath will go out like fire  
and burn with no one to quench it,  
because of the evil of your deeds.

**13** I am against you, inhabitant of the valley,  
rock of the plain, declares the LORD,  
you who say, "Who will come down against us?  
Who will enter our dwellings?"

**14** I will punish you according to the fruit of your deeds,  
declares the LORD;  
I will kindle a fire in her forest,  
and it will devour all that is around her.

## Notes

- **v1 "Pashhur son of Malchiah and Zephaniah son of Maaseiah, the priest"** — not the Pashhur son of Immer who put Jeremiah in the stocks (20:1). This Pashhur is among the officials who later want him dead (38:1, 4). Zephaniah is sent to him again (37:3) and is taken by the Babylonians as *the second priest* (52:24).
- **v2 "Perhaps the LORD will deal with us according to all His wonderful works"** — *nifle'otav*, the word of the exodus, *I will strike Egypt with all My wonders* (Exodus 3:20). Once before, with an Assyrian army at the wall, a king of Judah sent the elders of the priests to a prophet (2 Kings 19:2), and the LORD's messenger struck the camp that night (19:35). Zedekiah asks for the same.
- **vv4–5 "I Myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and a strong arm, in anger and in fury and in great wrath"** — the exodus is *with a strong hand and an outstretched arm* (Deuteronomy 26:8); here the two words change places, and the hand is raised against Jerusalem. At the sea Moses said, *The LORD will fight for you* (Exodus 14:14); now the LORD fights against them. *In anger and in fury and in great wrath* is how Deuteronomy says the LORD would uproot the people from their land (Deuteronomy 29:28).
- **v7 "he will not pity them or spare them or have compassion"** — the three verbs of 13:14, where the LORD said them of Himself. Here the king of Babylon carries them out.
- **vv8–9 "I am setting before you the way of life and the way of death… his life will be his as plunder"** — Moses set before Israel *life and death, the blessing and the curse. Choose life* (Deuteronomy 30:19). There life was to keep the covenant; here it is to walk out of the city and give up to the enemy. *Shalal* is a soldier's share of the spoil: when the city falls, the one who went out takes away nothing but his life. The book gives the same promise to all who go out (38:2), to Ebed-melech (39:18) and to Baruch (45:5).
- **v10 "I have set My face against this city for harm and not for good"** — *samti fanai*, the words of the law against the man who gives his child to Molech, *I Myself will set My face against that man* (Leviticus 20:5), now spoken against a city. Amos has the full sentence: *I will set My eyes on them for harm and not for good* (Amos 9:4). Jeremiah 24:6 turns it around for the exiles: *I will set My eyes on them for good*.
- **v12 "Execute justice in the morning… or My wrath will go out like fire and burn with no one to quench it, because of the evil of your deeds"** — the last three lines are 4:4, said there to the men of Judah, here to the house of David. A king's justice was done in the morning: *Morning by morning I will destroy all the wicked of the land* (Psalm 101:8).
- **v14 "I will kindle a fire in her forest"** — the forest is cedar. In the next chapter the king's house is *Gilead… the summit of Lebanon*, and its *choicest cedars* go on the fire (22:6–7); the king is the one *nested among the cedars* (22:23). Solomon's great hall was the House of the Forest of Lebanon, built on rows of cedar pillars (1 Kings 7:2).

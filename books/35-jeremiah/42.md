# Jeremiah 42

**1** Then all the commanders of the forces, Johanan son of Kareah, Jezaniah son of Hoshaiah and all the people from the least to the greatest came up

**2** and said to Jeremiah the prophet, "Please let our plea come before you, and pray for us to the LORD your God, for all this remnant; for we are left, a few out of many, as your eyes see us.

**3** Let the LORD your God tell us the way we should go and what we should do."

**4** Jeremiah the prophet said to them, "I have heard you. I will pray to the LORD your God as you have asked, and whatever word the LORD answers you, I will tell you; I will hold nothing back from you."

**5** They said to Jeremiah, "May the LORD be a true and faithful witness against us if we do not do everything that the LORD your God sends you to tell us.

**6** Whether it is good or bad, we will obey the voice of the LORD our God, to whom we are sending you, so that it may go well with us when we obey the voice of the LORD our God."

## The Answer after Ten Days

**7** And it came to pass at the end of ten days that the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah.

**8** He called Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces who were with him, and all the people from the least to the greatest,

**9** and said to them, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says, the One to whom you sent me to bring your plea before Him:

**10** If you will indeed stay in this land, then I will build you up and not overthrow you; I will plant you and not uproot you; for I relent of the disaster that I have done to you.

**11** Do not be afraid of the king of Babylon, of whom you are afraid; do not be afraid of him, declares the LORD, for I am with you to save you and to deliver you from his hand.

**12** I will grant you compassion, so that he will have compassion on you and let you return to your own land.

**13** But if you say, 'We will not stay in this land,' and so disobey the voice of the LORD your God,

**14** saying, 'No, we will go to the land of Egypt, where we will not see war or hear the sound of the ram's horn or hunger for bread, and there we will stay' —

**15** then hear the word of the LORD, remnant of Judah. This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: If you set your faces to go to Egypt, and go to live there as guests,

**16** the sword you fear will overtake you there in the land of Egypt, and the famine you are anxious about will follow close behind you there in Egypt, and there you will die.

**17** All the men who set their faces to go to Egypt to live there as guests will die by the sword, by famine and by pestilence; none of them will survive or escape the disaster that I am bringing on them.

**18** For this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: As My anger and My wrath were poured out on the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so My wrath will be poured out on you when you go to Egypt. You will become a name to curse by, a horror, a curse and a reproach, and you will not see this place again.

**19** The LORD has spoken to you, remnant of Judah: Do not go to Egypt. Know for certain that I have warned you today.

**20** For you have gone astray at the cost of your lives. You yourselves sent me to the LORD your God, saying, 'Pray for us to the LORD our God; tell us everything the LORD our God says, and we will do it.'

**21** I have told you today, but you have not obeyed the voice of the LORD your God in anything that He has sent me to tell you.

**22** So now know for certain that you will die by the sword, by famine and by pestilence in the place where you desire to go to live as guests."

---

## Notes

- **v20 "you have gone astray at the cost of your lives"** — *be-nafshoteikhem*, "with your lives," as Adonijah *has spoken this word at the cost of his own life* (1 Kings 2:23). It can also be read *you have deceived yourselves*, with *nefesh* as the self; the Greek has *you acted wickedly in your souls*.

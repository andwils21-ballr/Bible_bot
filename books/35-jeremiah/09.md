# Jeremiah 9

**1** Oh, that my head were waters,  
and my eyes a fountain of tears,  
so that I might weep day and night  
for the slain of the daughter of my people!

**2** Oh, that I had in the wilderness  
a travelers' lodging place,  
that I might leave my people  
and go away from them!  
For they are all adulterers,  
a company of traitors.

**3** They bend their tongue like their bow;  
falsehood and not faithfulness  
has grown strong in the land;  
for they go from evil to evil,  
and they do not know Me,  
declares the LORD.

**4** Let everyone beware of his neighbor,  
and put no trust in any brother;  
for every brother is a supplanter,  
and every neighbor goes about with slander.

**5** Each one deceives his neighbor,  
and no one speaks the truth;  
they have taught their tongue to speak falsehood;  
they weary themselves doing wrong.

**6** You live in the middle of deceit;  
through deceit they refuse to know Me,  
declares the LORD.

**7** Therefore this is what the LORD of hosts says:  
I am about to refine them and test them,  
for what else can I do  
because of the daughter of My people?

**8** Their tongue is a sharpened arrow;  
it speaks deceit;  
with his mouth each speaks peace to his neighbor,  
but in his heart he plans an ambush.

**9** Shall I not punish them for these things?  
declares the LORD;  
shall I not avenge Myself  
on a nation such as this?

**10** I will take up weeping and wailing for the mountains,  
and a lament for the pastures of the wilderness,  
because they are burned up so that no one passes through,  
and the lowing of cattle is not heard;  
both the birds of the heavens and the beasts  
have fled and are gone.

**11** I will make Jerusalem a heap of ruins,  
a lair of jackals;  
and I will make the cities of Judah a desolation,  
without inhabitant.

**12** Who is the man wise enough to understand this? To whom has the mouth of the LORD spoken, that he may declare it? Why is the land ruined and burned like a wilderness, so that no one passes through?

**13** The LORD said: Because they have forsaken My law that I set before them, and have not obeyed My voice or walked in it,

**14** but have walked after the stubbornness of their own heart and after the Baals, as their fathers taught them,

**15** therefore this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am about to feed this people with wormwood and give them poisoned water to drink.

**16** I will scatter them among nations that neither they nor their fathers have known, and I will send the sword after them until I have consumed them.

**17** This is what the LORD of hosts says:  
Consider, and call for the mourning women to come;  
send for the skilled women to come;

**18** let them hurry and raise a wailing over us,  
so that our eyes may run down with tears,  
and our eyelids flow with water.

**19** For a sound of wailing is heard from Zion:  
"How we are ruined!  
We are utterly put to shame,  
for we have left the land,  
for they have cast down our dwellings."

**20** Hear, you women, the word of the LORD,  
and let your ear receive the word of His mouth;  
teach your daughters a wailing,  
and each her neighbor a lament.

**21** For death has come up through our windows;  
it has entered our palaces,  
to cut off the children from the street,  
and the young men from the squares.

**22** Speak: This is what the LORD declares:  
The corpses of men will fall  
like dung on the open field,  
like sheaves after the reaper,  
with no one to gather them.

**23** This is what the LORD says:  
Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom;  
let not the mighty man boast in his might;  
let not the rich man boast in his riches;

**24** but let him who boasts boast in this,  
that he understands and knows Me,  
that I am the LORD who practices loyal love,  
justice and righteousness in the earth;  
for in these things I delight,  
declares the LORD.

**25** Days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will punish all who are circumcised in the flesh alone:

**26** Egypt, Judah, Edom, the sons of Ammon, Moab, and all who clip the hair at the sides of their head, who live in the wilderness; for all these nations are uncircumcised, and all the house of Israel is uncircumcised in heart.

## Notes

- **v1 "Oh, that my head were waters"** — in the Hebrew this verse is 8:23, the last line of the lament that began at 8:18, and the Hebrew numbers the rest of this chapter one lower (9:2 is Hebrew 9:1). The English numbering is followed.
- **vv3, 8 "They bend their tongue like their bow… Their tongue is a sharpened arrow"** — the tongue is the weapon in both verses: bent like a bow in v3, loosed as an arrow in v8. *Sharpened* is the read form, *shachut*; the written form is *shochet*, "slaughtering."
- **v4 "every brother is a supplanter"** — *aqov ya'qov*, "supplanting he supplants": the verb is Jacob's name. Esau said it first, *Is it because his name is called Jacob that he has heeled me these two times?* (Genesis 27:36). Every brother in Judah has become a Jacob.
- **v7 "I am about to refine them and test them"** — the refiner of 6:27–30, where the silver did not come clean; here the LORD takes up the work Himself.
- **v15 "I am about to feed this people with wormwood and give them poisoned water to drink"** — the words of Deuteronomy 29:18, *Let there not be among you a root bearing poison and wormwood*: what Moses warned would grow among them is now their food and drink.
- **v16 "I will scatter them among nations… and I will send the sword after them"** — the curse of the covenant, *I will scatter you among the nations, and I will draw the sword after you* (Leviticus 26:33).
- **vv17–20 "call for the mourning women… teach your daughters a wailing"** — the women whose trade was the lament; *ha-chakhamot*, "the skilled women," are the wise in their craft. The Chronicler says *all the singing men and singing women have spoken of Josiah in their laments to this day*, and that Jeremiah lamented for him (2 Chronicles 35:25). Here the mothers must teach the lament to their daughters.
- **vv23–24 "let him who boasts boast in this, that he understands and knows Me"** — Paul gives the sense of the verse twice, *Let the one who boasts, boast in the Lord* (1 Corinthians 1:31; 2 Corinthians 10:17). What the LORD delights in is *chesed*, loyal love, with justice and righteousness.
- **vv25–26 "all who are circumcised in the flesh alone… uncircumcised in heart"** — *kol mul be-orlah*, "all circumcised in foreskin": circumcised outwardly, with the heart untouched. It can also be read *the circumcised together with the uncircumcised*. Judah is listed among the nations. *Who clip the hair at the sides of their head* is the custom the law forbade, *You shall not round off the hair at the sides of your head* (Leviticus 19:27). The heart is what 4:4 asked to be circumcised.

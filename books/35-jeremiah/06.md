# Jeremiah 6

**1** Flee for safety, sons of Benjamin,  
out of the middle of Jerusalem!  
Blow the ram's horn in Tekoa,  
and raise a fire signal on Beth-haccherem;  
for disaster looks down from the north,  
and great destruction.

**2** The lovely and delicate one,  
the daughter of Zion, I will destroy.

**3** Shepherds with their flocks will come against her;  
they will pitch their tents all around her;  
each will graze his own part.

**4** Consecrate war against her!  
"Arise, let us go up at noon!"  
"Woe to us, for the day is turning,  
for the shadows of evening lengthen!"

**5** "Arise, let us go up by night,  
and destroy her palaces!"

**6** For this is what the LORD of hosts says:  
Cut down her trees,  
and cast up a siege ramp against Jerusalem.  
This is the city to be punished;  
there is nothing but oppression within her.

**7** As a well keeps its water fresh,  
so she keeps her wickedness fresh;  
violence and destruction are heard in her;  
sickness and wounds are ever before Me.

**8** Be warned, Jerusalem,  
or My soul will be torn from you,  
or I will make you a desolation,  
a land without inhabitant.

**9** This is what the LORD of hosts says:  
They will strip the remnant of Israel bare  
as one strips a vine;  
pass your hand again over its branches,  
like a grape gatherer.

**10** To whom shall I speak and give warning,  
that they may hear?  
Their ears are uncircumcised,  
and they cannot listen;  
the word of the LORD has become a reproach to them;  
they take no pleasure in it.

**11** But I am full of the wrath of the LORD;  
I am weary of holding it in.  
Pour it out on the children in the street,  
and on the gatherings of young men together;  
for both husband and wife will be taken,  
the old with the very aged.

**12** Their houses will be turned over to others,  
their fields and their wives together;  
for I will stretch out My hand  
against the inhabitants of the land,  
declares the LORD.

**13** For from the least of them to the greatest,  
everyone is greedy for unjust gain;  
from prophet to priest,  
everyone deals falsely.

**14** They have healed the wound of My people lightly,  
saying, "Peace, peace,"  
when there is no peace.

**15** They acted shamefully, committing detestable things;  
yet they were not at all ashamed;  
they did not know how to blush.  
Therefore they will fall among those who fall;  
at the time I punish them they will stumble,  
says the LORD.

**16** This is what the LORD says:  
Stand by the roads and look,  
and ask for the ancient paths,  
where the good way is,  
and walk in it,  
and you will find rest for your souls.  
But they said, "We will not walk in it."

**17** I set watchmen over you:  
"Listen to the sound of the ram's horn!"  
But they said, "We will not listen."

**18** Therefore hear, you nations,  
and know, you congregation,  
what is among them.

**19** Hear, earth:  
I am about to bring disaster on this people,  
the fruit of their own schemes,  
because they have not listened to My words,  
and My law, they have rejected it.

**20** What use to Me is frankincense that comes from Sheba,  
or fragrant cane from a distant land?  
Your burnt offerings are not acceptable,  
and your sacrifices do not please Me.

**21** Therefore this is what the LORD says:  
I am about to lay stumbling blocks before this people;  
fathers and sons together will stumble on them;  
neighbor and friend will perish.

**22** This is what the LORD says:  
A people is coming from the land of the north;  
a great nation is stirring  
from the farthest parts of the earth.

**23** They lay hold of bow and javelin;  
they are cruel and have no mercy;  
their voice roars like the sea;  
they ride on horses,  
set in array as a man for battle,  
against you, daughter of Zion.

**24** We have heard the report of it;  
our hands fall limp;  
anguish has seized us,  
pain as of a woman in labor.

**25** Do not go out into the field,  
or walk on the road;  
for the enemy has a sword;  
terror is on every side.

**26** Daughter of my people,  
put on sackcloth  
and roll in ashes;  
make mourning as for an only son,  
a most bitter lament;  
for suddenly the destroyer  
will come upon us.

**27** I have made you a tester among My people,  
a fortress,  
that you may know and test their way.

**28** They are all stubborn rebels,  
going about with slander;  
they are bronze and iron;  
all of them act corruptly.

**29** The bellows blow fiercely;  
the lead is consumed by the fire;  
in vain the refiner refines,  
for the wicked are not removed.

**30** They are called rejected silver,  
for the LORD has rejected them.

## Notes

- **v1 "Blow the ram's horn in Tekoa"** — *bi-Tqoa tiq'u*, the town's name and the verb, *taqa*, "blow," share their letters. Tekoa was the home of the prophet Amos (*among the shepherds of Tekoa*, Amos 1:1). *Sons of Benjamin* are Jeremiah's own tribe (1:1).
- **v2 "The lovely and delicate one, the daughter of Zion, I will destroy"** — *ha-navah ve-ha-me'unnagah damiti*. *Me'unnagah* is the *delicate* woman of the curse, *most tender and delicate among you* (Deuteronomy 28:56), and *damiti* is "I will cut off." The same letters can be read *navah*, "pasture," and *damiti*, "I have likened": *To a lovely pasture I liken the daughter of Zion*, which runs into the shepherds and flocks of v3. The Greek has *your pride will be taken away, daughter of Zion*.
- **v9 "They will strip the remnant of Israel bare as one strips a vine"** — *olel ye'olelu*, the verb of the law, *You shall not strip your vineyard bare… You shall leave them for the poor and for the guest* (Leviticus 19:10). The enemy will leave nothing.
- **v10 "Their ears are uncircumcised"** — as their hearts are (4:4). Stephen to the council: *you stiff-necked people, uncircumcised in hearts and ears* (Acts 7:51).
- **vv13–15 "saying, 'Peace, peace,' when there is no peace"** — the *peace* of 4:10. These verses come again almost word for word in 8:10–12, where the Greek does not have them.
- **v16 "ask for the ancient paths… and find rest for your souls"** — *margo'a le-nafshekhem*. Jesus says, *Take My yoke upon you… and you will find rest for your souls* (Matthew 11:29), in the words of the Hebrew; the Greek of Jeremiah has *purification for your souls*.
- **vv22–24 "A people is coming from the land of the north… against you, daughter of Zion"** — the same words are turned on Babylon at the end of the book: *against you, daughter of Babylon*, and *the king of Babylon has heard the report of them, and his hands fall limp; anguish has seized him, pain as of a woman in labor* (50:41–43).
- **v25 "terror is on every side"** — *magor mi-saviv*, Jeremiah's phrase. He gives it as a name to the priest Pashhur, who had him beaten and put in the stocks (20:3), and hears it whispered against himself (20:10), as the psalmist did: *terror on every side, as they plot together against me* (Psalm 31:13).
- **v26 "make mourning as for an only son"** — *evel yachid*, the grief for a son who leaves no other; *yachid* is the word God used of Isaac, *your son — your beloved one* (Genesis 22:2).
- **vv27–30 "a tester… the refiner refines… rejected silver"** — the prophet is set to test the people as silver is tested, and the metal does not come clean. Isaiah used the same picture: *Your silver has become dross… I will smelt away your dross as with lye* (Isaiah 1:22, 25).

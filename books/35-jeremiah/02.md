# Jeremiah 2

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** Go and proclaim in the ears of Jerusalem: Thus says the LORD:  
I remember the loyal love of your youth,  
your love as a bride,  
how you followed Me in the wilderness,  
in a land not sown.

**3** Israel was holy to the LORD,  
the firstfruits of His harvest;  
all who ate of it were held guilty;  
disaster came on them,  
declares the LORD.

**4** Hear the word of the LORD, house of Jacob,  
and all the clans of the house of Israel.

**5** Thus says the LORD:  
What wrong did your fathers find in Me,  
that they went far from Me,  
and went after emptiness and became empty?

**6** They did not say, "Where is the LORD,  
who brought us up from the land of Egypt,  
who led us through the wilderness,  
through a land of deserts and pits,  
through a land of drought and the shadow of death,  
through a land that no one passes through,  
where no man lives?"

**7** I brought you into a fruitful land  
to eat its fruit and its good things;  
but you came in and defiled My land,  
and made My inheritance a detestable thing.

**8** The priests did not say, "Where is the LORD?"  
Those who handle the law did not know Me;  
the shepherds rebelled against Me;  
the prophets prophesied by Baal,  
and went after things that do not profit.

**9** Therefore I will still contend with you, declares the LORD,  
and with your sons' sons I will contend.

**10** Cross over to the coastlands of Kittim and see;  
send to Kedar and consider carefully;  
see whether there has ever been such a thing.

**11** Has a nation exchanged its gods,  
though they are not gods?  
But My people have exchanged their Glory  
for what does not profit.

**12** Be appalled, heavens, at this;  
shudder, be utterly desolate,  
declares the LORD.

**13** For My people have committed two evils:  
they have forsaken Me,  
the fountain of living waters,  
to hew out cisterns for themselves,  
broken cisterns  
that cannot hold water.

**14** Is Israel a slave?  
Is he a servant born in the house?  
Why then has he become plunder?

**15** Young lions have roared against him;  
they have raised their voice;  
they have made his land a desolation;  
his cities are burned,  
without inhabitant.

**16** The men of Memphis and Tahpanhes  
have also broken the crown of your head.

**17** Have you not brought this on yourself  
by forsaking the LORD your God  
when He led you in the way?

**18** So now, what are you doing on the road to Egypt,  
to drink the waters of the Shihor?  
Or what are you doing on the road to Assyria,  
to drink the waters of the River?

**19** Your own evil will discipline you,  
and your faithlessness will rebuke you.  
Know and see that it is evil and bitter  
to forsake the LORD your God;  
the fear of Me is not in you,  
declares the Lord GOD of hosts.

**20** For long ago I broke your yoke  
and tore off your bonds,  
and you said, "I will not transgress";  
yet on every high hill  
and under every green tree  
you lay down as a prostitute.

**21** Yet I had planted you a choice vine,  
wholly of true seed;  
how then have you turned against Me  
into the wild shoots of a foreign vine?

**22** Though you wash yourself with lye  
and use much soap,  
the stain of your iniquity is before Me,  
declares the Lord GOD.

**23** How can you say, "I am not defiled;  
I have not gone after the Baals"?  
See your way in the valley;  
know what you have done:  
a swift young camel  
running here and there,

**24** a wild donkey used to the wilderness,  
sniffing the wind in her desire.  
In her heat who can turn her back?  
None who seek her need weary themselves;  
in her month they will find her.

**25** Keep your feet from going bare,  
and your throat from thirst.  
But you said, "It is hopeless!  
No, for I have loved strangers,  
and after them I will go."

**26** As a thief is put to shame when he is caught,  
so the house of Israel is put to shame:  
they, their kings, their princes,  
their priests and their prophets,

**27** who say to wood, "You are my father,"  
and to stone, "You gave birth to us."  
For they have turned their backs to Me,  
and not their faces;  
but in the time of their trouble they say,  
"Arise and save us!"

**28** But where are your gods  
that you made for yourself?  
Let them arise, if they can save you  
in the time of your trouble;  
for as many as your cities  
are your gods, Judah.

**29** Why do you contend with Me?  
You have all rebelled against Me,  
declares the LORD.

**30** In vain I have struck your sons;  
they took no correction;  
your own sword has devoured your prophets  
like a ravening lion.

**31** You of this generation, see the word of the LORD!  
Have I been a wilderness to Israel,  
or a land of thick darkness?  
Why do My people say, "We roam free;  
we will come to You no more"?

**32** Can a virgin forget her ornaments,  
or a bride her sash?  
Yet My people have forgotten Me  
days without number.

**33** How well you direct your way  
to seek love!  
So you have taught your ways  
even to wicked women.

**34** On your skirts too is found  
the lifeblood of the innocent poor;  
you did not find them breaking in.  
Yet in spite of all these things

**35** you say, "I am innocent;  
surely His anger has turned from me."  
I am about to enter into judgment with you  
for saying, "I have not sinned."

**36** Why do you go about so lightly,  
changing your way?  
You will be put to shame by Egypt  
as you were put to shame by Assyria.

**37** From there too you will come away  
with your hands on your head;  
for the LORD has rejected those you trust,  
and you will not prosper by them.

## Notes

- **v2 "the loyal love of your youth… how you followed Me in the wilderness"** — *chesed*, the word for the LORD's own loyal love, here said of Israel's love for Him. The wilderness years, which Numbers tells as years of complaint (*The people became complainers*, Numbers 11:1), are remembered here as a bride following her husband. The Greek has *I remembered the mercy of your youth*.
- **v3 "Israel was holy to the LORD, the firstfruits of His harvest; all who ate of it were held guilty"** — *ye'shamu*, "became guilty," the law of holy things: *No outsider shall eat a holy thing* (Leviticus 22:10), and one who eats it by mistake must make it good (22:14). Israel is the LORD's portion, and the nations that devoured her answered for it.
- **v5 "went after emptiness and became empty"** — *hevel*, the word Ecclesiastes uses for *vanity* and the name of Abel (Genesis 4:2). 2 Kings 17:15 says it of the northern kingdom in the same words, *They went after emptiness and became empty*.
- **v11 "My people have exchanged their Glory for what does not profit"** — the verb and noun of Psalm 106:20, said of the golden calf: *They exchanged their Glory for the image of an ox that eats grass*. Paul: *they exchanged the glory of the immortal God for the likeness of an image* (Romans 1:23). "What does not profit" — *be-lo yo'il*, the *things that do not profit* of v8.
- **v13 "the fountain of living waters… broken cisterns"** — *mayim chayyim*, "living water," is the law's word for *fresh running water* (Leviticus 14:5): water that flows by itself, set against water stored by hand in a cistern that cracks. The LORD calls Himself the fountain again in 17:13. Jesus offers the Samaritan woman *living water* (John 4:10).
- **v18 "the waters of the Shihor… the waters of the River"** — *Shichor* stands beside the Nile in Isaiah 23:3, *the grain of Shihor, the harvest of the Nile*; *the River* is the Euphrates. The Greek has *Gihon*, the name of the river of Eden *winding through all the land of Cush* (Genesis 2:13).
- **v20 "Long ago I broke your yoke… 'I will not transgress'"** — the Hebrew as read: *shavarti*, "I broke," the LORD's work at the Exodus, *I broke the bars of your yoke* (Leviticus 26:13), and the read form *e'evor*, "I will not transgress," Israel's promise. The written form is *e'evod*, "I will not serve," and the Greek has *you broke your yoke… and said, I will not serve You*. In Jeremiah a written *-ti* is often read "you" (*limmadti*, v33; *qarati*, 3:4; *dibbarti*, 3:5; *shama'ti*, 4:19), so *shavarti* can be "you broke"; and 5:5 says it of the people in the same words, *they have broken the yoke, they have burst the bonds*.
- **v21 "a choice vine"** — *soreq*, the vine of Isaiah's song of the vineyard, *planted it with choice vines* (Isaiah 5:2), which also yielded what it should not.
- **v34 "you did not find them breaking in"** — *va-machteret*, the word of the law: *If the thief is found breaking in and is struck so that he dies, there is no bloodguilt for him* (Exodus 22:2). The poor whose blood is on Judah's skirts were no thieves caught in the act. The Greek has *not in breakings-in did I find them, but on every oak*.
- **v37 "with your hands on your head"** — the sign of grief and disgrace: Tamar *put her hand on her head and went away, crying out* (2 Samuel 13:19).

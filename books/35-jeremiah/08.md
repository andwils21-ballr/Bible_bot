# Jeremiah 8

**1** At that time, declares the LORD, they will bring out of their graves the bones of the kings of Judah, the bones of its princes, the bones of the priests, the bones of the prophets and the bones of the inhabitants of Jerusalem,

**2** and spread them before the sun and the moon and all the host of heaven, which they have loved and served, which they have gone after, which they have sought and worshipped. They will not be gathered or buried; they will be dung on the face of the ground.

**3** Death will be chosen rather than life by all the remnant that remains of this evil family, in all the places where I have driven them, declares the LORD of hosts.

## Turning Away

**4** You shall say to them, Thus says the LORD:  
When men fall, do they not get up?  
If one turns away, does he not turn back?

**5** Why then has this people, Jerusalem,  
turned away in lasting faithlessness?  
They hold fast to deceit;  
they refuse to return.

**6** I have listened and heard;  
they do not speak what is right;  
no one repents of his wickedness,  
saying, "What have I done?"  
Each one turns to his own course,  
like a horse plunging headlong into battle.

**7** Even the stork in the heavens  
knows her appointed times;  
and the turtledove, the swift and the crane  
keep the time of their coming;  
but My people do not know  
the ordinance of the LORD.

**8** How can you say, "We are wise,  
and the law of the LORD is with us"?  
But the lying pen of the scribes  
has made it into a lie.

**9** The wise will be put to shame;  
they will be dismayed and taken;  
they have rejected the word of the LORD,  
and what wisdom is in them?

**10** Therefore I will give their wives to others,  
and their fields to new owners;  
for from the least to the greatest  
everyone is greedy for unjust gain;  
from prophet to priest  
everyone deals falsely.

**11** They have healed the wound of the daughter of My people lightly,  
saying, "Peace, peace,"  
when there is no peace.

**12** They acted shamefully, committing detestable things;  
yet they were not at all ashamed;  
they did not know how to blush.  
Therefore they will fall among those who fall;  
in the time of their punishment they will stumble,  
says the LORD.

**13** I will gather them up and sweep them away,  
declares the LORD;  
there are no grapes on the vine,  
no figs on the fig tree,  
and the leaf has withered;  
what I gave them will pass away from them.

**14** Why are we sitting still?  
Gather together, and let us go into the fortified cities  
and perish there;  
for the LORD our God has doomed us to perish,  
and given us poisoned water to drink,  
because we have sinned against the LORD.

**15** We hoped for peace, but no good came;  
for a time of healing, but there is terror.

**16** The snorting of his horses is heard from Dan;  
at the sound of the neighing of his stallions  
the whole land quakes.  
They come and devour the land and all that fills it,  
the city and those who live in it.

**17** For I am about to send among you serpents,  
adders that cannot be charmed,  
and they will bite you,  
declares the LORD.

## Is There No Balm in Gilead?

**18** There is no cheer for me in my grief;  
my heart is sick within me.

**19** Hear the cry of the daughter of my people  
from a land far away:  
"Is the LORD not in Zion?  
Is her King not in her?"  
"Why have they provoked Me with their carved images,  
with their foreign emptiness?"

**20** "The harvest is past, the summer is ended,  
and we are not saved."

**21** For the wound of the daughter of my people I am wounded;  
I mourn; horror has seized me.

**22** Is there no balm in Gilead?  
Is there no physician there?  
Why then has the daughter of my people  
not been healed?

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "they will bring out of their graves the bones… and spread them before the sun and the moon and all the host of heaven"** — what Josiah did to the graves at Bethel, *He sent and took the bones out of the graves and burned them on the altar* (2 Kings 23:16), is done to Judah's own kings and priests. Josiah had put down the priests who burned incense *to the sun and the moon… and to all the host of heaven* (23:5); here five verbs of devotion are given to them, *loved… served… gone after… sought… worshipped*, and the bones are laid out before them.
- **v3 "Death will be chosen rather than life"** — Moses set the choice before the people: *I have set before you life and death… Choose life* (Deuteronomy 30:19). The remnant will choose the other.
- **vv4–5 "If one turns away, does he not turn back?… they refuse to return"** — *shuv*, "turn," five times in two verses, as through 3:6–22: *yashuv… yashuv… shovevah… meshuvah… lashuv*.
- **v7 "the stork… the turtledove, the swift and the crane keep the time of their coming"** — the birds keep their seasons; the people do not keep the LORD's. Isaiah began the same way: *The ox knows its owner… Israel does not know* (Isaiah 1:3). *Swift* is the read form, *sis*; the written form is *sus*, "horse."
- **vv10–12 "from prophet to priest everyone deals falsely… 'Peace, peace'"** — 6:12–15, nearly word for word. The Greek has only the first line of v10 and nothing of vv11–12.
- **v13 "I will gather them up and sweep them away… there are no grapes on the vine, no figs on the fig tree"** — *asof asifem*, two verbs that sound alike: *asaf*, "gather," the word for bringing in a harvest, and *suf*, "come to an end." The same pair stands in Zephaniah 1:2, *I will utterly sweep away everything from the face of the ground*. Read with *asaf* alone, the line is a harvest that finds nothing: *When I would gather them, there are no grapes on the vine*. The Greek has *they will gather their produce*. Jesus came to a fig tree *and found nothing on it but leaves* (Matthew 21:19).
- **v14 "Gather together, and let us go into the fortified cities and perish there"** — the cry of 4:5, *Gather together, and let us go into the fortified cities*, said again with its end added. *Poisoned water*, *mei rosh*, comes back in 9:15 and 23:15.
- **v17 "I am about to send among you serpents… and they will bite you"** — the words of the wilderness, *the LORD sent the fiery serpents among the people, and they bit the people* (Numbers 21:6). There, Moses made a bronze serpent, and whoever looked at it lived (21:9); here the adders *cannot be charmed*.
- **vv18–22 "Is there no balm in Gilead?"** — *tsori*, the balm the Ishmaelites were carrying from Gilead when Joseph was sold (Genesis 37:25). The lament goes on in one more verse, numbered 8:23 in the Hebrew and 9:1 in the English, and the English numbering is followed.

# Jeremiah 51

**1** This is what the LORD says:  
I am about to stir up a destroying spirit  
against Babylon  
and against the inhabitants of Leb-kamai.

**2** I will send strangers to Babylon,  
and they will winnow her  
and empty her land;  
for they will be against her from every side  
on the day of disaster.

**3** Let not the archer bend his bow,  
nor rise up in his coat of mail.  
Do not spare her young men;  
destroy her whole army completely.

**4** They will fall slain in the land of the Chaldeans,  
run through in her streets.

**5** For Israel and Judah have not been widowed  
by their God, the LORD of hosts,  
though their land is full of guilt  
against the Holy One of Israel.

**6** Flee from the middle of Babylon;  
let each of you save his life!  
Do not be cut off in her punishment,  
for this is the time of the LORD's vengeance;  
He is repaying her what she deserves.

**7** Babylon was a golden cup in the LORD's hand,  
making all the earth drunk;  
the nations drank of her wine;  
therefore the nations have gone mad.

**8** Suddenly Babylon has fallen and is broken;  
wail over her!  
Take balm for her pain;  
perhaps she may be healed.

**9** We would have healed Babylon,  
but she was not healed.  
Leave her, and let us each go to his own land;  
for her judgment reaches to heaven  
and is lifted up to the skies.

**10** The LORD has brought out our vindication;  
come, let us declare in Zion  
the work of the LORD our God.

**11** Sharpen the arrows!  
Take up the shields!  
The LORD has stirred up the spirit of the kings of the Medes,  
because His purpose against Babylon is to destroy it;  
for this is the vengeance of the LORD,  
the vengeance for His temple.

**12** Set up a banner against the walls of Babylon;  
strengthen the watch;  
post the watchmen;  
prepare the ambushes;  
for the LORD has both planned and done  
what He spoke concerning the inhabitants of Babylon.

**13** You who live by many waters,  
rich in treasures,  
your end has come,  
the measure of your gain.

**14** The LORD of hosts has sworn by Himself:  
Surely I will fill you with men, as with locusts,  
and they will raise the shout over you.

**15** It is He who made the earth by His power,  
who established the world by His wisdom,  
and by His understanding stretched out the heavens.

**16** When He utters His voice,  
there is a roar of waters in the heavens;  
He makes the clouds rise from the ends of the earth;  
He makes lightning for the rain  
and brings out the wind from His storehouses.

**17** Every man is senseless, without knowledge;  
every goldsmith is put to shame by his idol;  
for his images are falsehood,  
and there is no breath in them.

**18** They are emptiness, a work of mockery;  
in the time of their punishment they will perish.

**19** Not like these is the Portion of Jacob,  
for He is the One who formed all things,  
and the tribe of His inheritance;  
the LORD of hosts is His name.

## My War Club

**20** You are My war club, My weapon of war:  
with you I shatter nations;  
with you I destroy kingdoms;

**21** with you I shatter the horse and its rider;  
with you I shatter the chariot and its rider;

**22** with you I shatter man and woman;  
with you I shatter old man and youth;  
with you I shatter young man and virgin;

**23** with you I shatter the shepherd and his flock;  
with you I shatter the farmer and his team;  
with you I shatter governors and officials.

**24** I will repay Babylon and all the inhabitants of Chaldea for all the evil they have done in Zion before your eyes, declares the LORD.

**25** I am against you, Mount of Destruction,  
declares the LORD,  
you who destroy the whole earth;  
I will stretch out My hand against you,  
roll you down from the crags,  
and make you a burned-out mountain.

**26** No stone will be taken from you for a corner,  
nor a stone for foundations,  
for you will be a desolation forever,  
declares the LORD.

**27** Set up a banner in the land;  
blow the ram's horn among the nations;  
consecrate the nations against her;  
summon against her the kingdoms  
of Ararat, Minni and Ashkenaz;  
appoint a marshal against her;  
bring up horses like bristling locusts.

**28** Consecrate the nations against her:  
the kings of the Medes,  
their governors and all their officials,  
and every land of their dominion.

**29** The land quakes and writhes,  
for the LORD's purposes against Babylon stand,  
to make the land of Babylon a desolation,  
without inhabitant.

**30** The warriors of Babylon have stopped fighting;  
they sit in their strongholds;  
their strength has failed;  
they have become like women;  
her dwellings are set on fire;  
her bars are broken.

**31** Runner runs to meet runner,  
and messenger to meet messenger,  
to tell the king of Babylon  
that his city is taken from end to end;

**32** the fords are seized,  
the reed marshes are burned with fire,  
and the men of war are terrified.

**33** For this is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says:  
The daughter of Babylon is like a threshing floor  
at the time it is trodden;  
yet a little while,  
and the time of her harvest will come.

**34** "Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has devoured me;  
he has crushed me;  
he has set me aside like an empty jar;  
he has swallowed me like a sea monster;  
he has filled his belly with my delicacies;  
he has cast me out.

**35** May the violence done to me and to my flesh be upon Babylon,"  
the inhabitant of Zion will say;  
"and my blood be upon the inhabitants of Chaldea,"  
Jerusalem will say.

**36** Therefore this is what the LORD says:  
I am about to plead your cause  
and take vengeance for you;  
I will dry up her sea  
and make her spring run dry.

**37** Babylon will become a heap of ruins,  
a lair of jackals,  
a horror and a thing to be hissed at,  
without inhabitant.

**38** They will roar together like young lions;  
they will growl like lions' cubs.

**39** When they are heated, I will set out their feast  
and make them drunk, so that they may revel,  
and sleep an everlasting sleep and not wake,  
declares the LORD.

**40** I will bring them down like lambs to the slaughter,  
like rams with he-goats.

**41** How Sheshach is taken,  
the praise of the whole earth seized!  
How Babylon has become a horror  
among the nations!

**42** The sea has come up over Babylon;  
she is covered with the tumult of its waves.

**43** Her cities have become a desolation,  
a dry land and a desert,  
a land where no one lives,  
and through which no son of man passes.

**44** I will punish Bel in Babylon,  
and take out of his mouth what he has swallowed;  
the nations will no longer stream to him;  
even the wall of Babylon has fallen.

**45** Go out from the middle of her, My people!  
Let each of you save his life  
from the fierce anger of the LORD!

**46** Do not let your heart grow faint,  
or be afraid at the report heard in the land,  
when a report comes one year,  
and after it another report the next year,  
and violence is in the land,  
ruler against ruler.

**47** Therefore, days are coming  
when I will punish the carved images of Babylon;  
her whole land will be put to shame,  
and all her slain will fall in her midst.

**48** Then the heavens and the earth  
and all that is in them  
will sing for joy over Babylon,  
for the destroyers will come against her from the north,  
declares the LORD.

**49** Babylon must fall for the slain of Israel,  
as for Babylon the slain of all the earth have fallen.

**50** You who have escaped the sword,  
go! Do not stand still!  
Remember the LORD from far away,  
and let Jerusalem come into your mind.

**51** We are put to shame, for we have heard reproach;  
disgrace has covered our faces,  
for strangers have entered  
the holy places of the LORD's house.

**52** Therefore, days are coming, declares the LORD,  
when I will punish her carved images,  
and through all her land  
the wounded will groan.

**53** Though Babylon should mount up to the heavens,  
and though she should fortify her strong height,  
from Me destroyers will come against her,  
declares the LORD.

**54** Listen! A cry from Babylon,  
and great ruin from the land of the Chaldeans!

**55** For the LORD is laying Babylon waste  
and silencing her loud voice;  
their waves roar like many waters;  
the noise of their voice is raised.

**56** For a destroyer has come against her, against Babylon;  
her warriors are taken;  
their bows are shattered;  
for the LORD is a God of recompense;  
He will repay in full.

**57** I will make her princes and her wise men drunk,  
her governors, her officials and her warriors;  
they will sleep an everlasting sleep and not wake,  
declares the King, whose name is the LORD of hosts.

**58** This is what the LORD of hosts says:  
The broad wall of Babylon  
will be leveled to the ground,  
and her high gates  
will be burned with fire.  
The peoples labor for nothing,  
and the nations weary themselves only for fire.

## The Book Sunk in the Euphrates

**59** The word that Jeremiah the prophet commanded Seraiah son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah, when he went with Zedekiah king of Judah to Babylon in the fourth year of his reign. Seraiah was the quartermaster.

**60** Jeremiah wrote in one book all the disaster that would come upon Babylon, all these words that are written concerning Babylon.

**61** Jeremiah said to Seraiah, "When you arrive in Babylon, see that you read all these words,

**62** and say, 'LORD, You have spoken concerning this place, to cut it off, so that nothing will live in it, neither man nor beast, for it will be a desolation forever.'

**63** And it shall come to pass, when you have finished reading this book, that you shall tie a stone to it and throw it into the middle of the Euphrates,

**64** and say, 'So shall Babylon sink and not rise, because of the disaster that I am bringing upon her; and they will be weary.'" Here end the words of Jeremiah.

## Notes

- **vv1, 41 "Leb-kamai… Sheshach"** — two names in the letter-swap of 25:26, where each letter is replaced by the one standing as far from the other end of the alphabet. *Sheshach* is *Bavel*, Babylon, as v41 shows by setting the two names side by side; *Leb-kamai*, "the heart of those who rise against Me," is *Kasdim*, the Chaldeans, as the Greek has it.
- **vv1, 11 "I am about to stir up a destroying spirit… The LORD has stirred up the spirit of the kings of the Medes"** — *he'ir… ruach*, the same verb and noun: the spirit stirred against Babylon is in the kings who come against it.
- **v3 "Let not the archer bend his bow… destroy her whole army completely"** — the Hebrew writes *yidrokh*, "bend," twice, and the second is not read. Read as it stands, the line forbids Babylon's archers to shoot; the Greek has *Let the one who bends bend his bow*, an order to the attackers. *Charem*, "destroy completely," as at 50:21, 26.
- **vv15–19 "It is He who made the earth by His power…"** — 10:12–16 again, word for word, except that v19 has *and the tribe of His inheritance* without *Israel*.
- **vv20–23 "You are My war club… with you I shatter"** — *nippatsti*, "I shatter," nine times. The verse does not name the club; the shattering it describes is what Babylon did to the nations, and v24 turns to repay it. *The horse and its rider* is the song at the sea, *horse and its rider He has thrown into the sea* (Exodus 15:1).
- **v25 "Mount of Destruction… you who destroy the whole earth"** — *har ha-mashchit*, the name of the hill east of Jerusalem where Solomon built high places for Chemosh and Milcom, *the Mount of Destruction* (2 Kings 23:13). Here it is Babylon itself.
- **vv34, 44 "he has swallowed me like a sea monster… take out of his mouth what he has swallowed"** — *tannin*, the creature of *the great sea-monsters* (Genesis 1:21). Zion is swallowed by Nebuchadnezzar, and Babylon's god Bel is made to give it back. In v34 the written forms say *us*; the read forms say *me*. *The nations will no longer stream to him* (v44) takes the word of the promise, *all the nations shall stream to it* (Isaiah 2:2), from Babylon's god.
- **v50 "let Jerusalem come into your mind"** — the idiom of 3:16, where the ark *will not come to mind*: what must not be forgotten now is the city.
- **v53 "Though Babylon should mount up to the heavens"** — Babel's own boast, *a tower with its head in the heavens* (Genesis 11:4).
- **vv59–64 "Seraiah son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah… throw it into the middle of the Euphrates… and they will be weary. Here end the words of Jeremiah"** — Seraiah is Baruch's brother, *Baruch son of Neriah son of Mahseiah* (32:12). *Sar menuchah* is literally "officer of the resting place"; the Greek has *ruler of gifts*. The sign is the loincloth's river again (13:4–7). *And they will be weary* is the last word of v58, repeated to close the book's words before chapter 52; the Greek has neither it nor the closing line.

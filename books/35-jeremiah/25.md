# Jeremiah 25

**1** The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, which was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon,

**2** which Jeremiah the prophet spoke to all the people of Judah and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem:

**3** "From the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, to this day, these twenty-three years, the word of the LORD has come to me, and I have spoken to you again and again, but you have not listened.

**4** The LORD has sent to you all His servants the prophets again and again, but you have not listened or inclined your ear to hear,

**5** saying, 'Turn now, each of you, from his evil way and from the evil of your deeds, and live on the land that the LORD has given to you and to your fathers from of old and forever.

**6** Do not go after other gods to serve them and to worship them, and do not provoke Me to anger with the work of your hands; then I will do you no harm.'

**7** Yet you have not listened to Me, declares the LORD, and so you have provoked Me to anger with the work of your hands, to your own harm.

**8** Therefore this is what the LORD of hosts says: Because you have not listened to My words,

**9** I am about to send for all the clans of the north, declares the LORD, and for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant, and I will bring them against this land and its inhabitants, and against all these nations around it. I will destroy them completely, and make them a horror and a thing to be hissed at, and everlasting ruins.

**10** I will banish from them the voice of mirth and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the sound of the millstones and the light of the lamp.

**11** This whole land will become a ruin and a horror, and these nations will serve the king of Babylon seventy years.

**12** And it shall come to pass, when seventy years are completed, that I will punish the king of Babylon and that nation for their iniquity, declares the LORD, and the land of the Chaldeans; and I will make it an everlasting desolation.

**13** I will bring on that land all My words that I have spoken against it, all that is written in this book, which Jeremiah prophesied against all the nations.

**14** For many nations and great kings will make slaves of them too, and I will repay them according to their deeds and the work of their hands."

## The Cup

**15** For this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, said to me: Take from My hand this cup of the wine of wrath, and make all the nations to whom I send you drink it.

**16** They will drink and stagger and go mad because of the sword that I am sending among them.

**17** So I took the cup from the LORD's hand and made all the nations to whom the LORD sent me drink it:

**18** Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, its kings and its princes, to make them a ruin, a horror, a thing to be hissed at and a curse, as it is this day;

**19** Pharaoh king of Egypt, his servants, his princes and all his people;

**20** all the mixed peoples; all the kings of the land of Uz; all the kings of the land of the Philistines: Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron and the remnant of Ashdod;

**21** Edom, Moab and the sons of Ammon;

**22** all the kings of Tyre, all the kings of Sidon, and the kings of the coastlands across the sea;

**23** Dedan, Tema, Buz, and all who clip the hair at the sides of their head;

**24** all the kings of Arabia, and all the kings of the mixed peoples who live in the wilderness;

**25** all the kings of Zimri, all the kings of Elam and all the kings of Media;

**26** all the kings of the north, near and far, one after another; and all the kingdoms of the world on the face of the earth. After them the king of Sheshach will drink.

**27** Then you shall say to them: This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Drink, get drunk and vomit, fall and rise no more, because of the sword that I am sending among you.

**28** And it shall come to pass, when they refuse to take the cup from your hand to drink, that you shall say to them: This is what the LORD of hosts says: You must drink!

**29** For I am beginning to bring disaster on the city that is called by My name, and will you go unpunished? You will not go unpunished, for I am calling for a sword against all the inhabitants of the earth, declares the LORD of hosts.

**30** As for you, prophesy against them all these words, and say to them:  
The LORD will roar from on high,  
and utter His voice from His holy dwelling;  
He will roar mightily against His fold;  
He will raise a shout like those who tread grapes  
against all the inhabitants of the earth.

**31** The tumult has reached the end of the earth,  
for the LORD has a dispute with the nations;  
He is entering into judgment with all flesh;  
the wicked He has given to the sword,  
declares the LORD.

**32** This is what the LORD of hosts says:  
Disaster is going out  
from nation to nation,  
and a great storm is stirring  
from the farthest parts of the earth.

**33** Those slain by the LORD on that day will lie from one end of the earth to the other. They will not be mourned or gathered or buried; they will be dung on the face of the ground.

**34** Wail, you shepherds, and cry out;  
roll in the dust, you leaders of the flock,  
for the days of your slaughter have come,  
and of your scatterings;  
you will fall like a precious jar.

**35** Flight will fail the shepherds,  
and escape the leaders of the flock.

**36** The sound of the shepherds' cry,  
and the wailing of the leaders of the flock!  
For the LORD is laying waste their pasture,

**37** and the peaceful folds are silenced  
because of the fierce anger of the LORD.

**38** Like a young lion He has left His lair;  
for their land has become a horror  
because of the oppressing sword  
and because of His fierce anger.

## Notes

- **vv1, 3 "the fourth year of Jehoiakim… the first year of Nebuchadnezzar… these twenty-three years"** — the year Nebuchadnezzar defeated Pharaoh Neco at Carchemish (46:2), and the year Jeremiah was told to write all his words in a scroll (36:1–2). Twenty-three years earlier, in the thirteenth year of Josiah, the word first came to him (1:2). The Greek does not have *the first year of Nebuchadnezzar*.
- **v9 "Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant… I will destroy them completely"** — *avdi*, "My servant," the title of Moses and of David, given to the king of Babylon (also 27:6; 43:10). *Hacharamtim* is the verb of Jericho, *They destroyed everything in the city* (Joshua 6:21): the ban Israel once carried out in Canaan is now carried out on Judah. The Greek names neither Nebuchadnezzar nor *My servant* here, only *a clan from the north*.
- **v10 "the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the sound of the millstones and the light of the lamp"** — 7:34 and 16:9 silence the wedding voices; here the grinding of the day's grain and the lamp in the house at night are added. The same voices are promised back in 33:11. The lament over Babylon in Revelation takes up three of them: *the sound of the millstone… the light of a lamp… the voice of bridegroom and bride* (Revelation 18:22–23).
- **vv11–12 "seventy years… when seventy years are completed"** — the count is given again at 29:10. The Chronicler ends his book with it, *to fulfill seventy years* (2 Chronicles 36:21), and Daniel reads it *in the books… the word of the LORD to Jeremiah the prophet* (Daniel 9:2). When it is done, the same punishment falls on Babylon.
- **vv13–15 "all that is written in this book, which Jeremiah prophesied against all the nations"** — here the Greek takes another order. Its sentence ends at *written in this book*; *What Jeremiah prophesied against the nations* becomes a heading, and the oracles against the nations follow at once, Elam first. The cup of vv15–38 comes after them. The Hebrew keeps those oracles for chapters 46–51 (Elam at 49:34–39). The Greek has no v14.
- **v15 "this cup of the wine of wrath"** — the cup of Isaiah 51:17, *you who have drunk from the hand of the LORD the cup of His wrath*. Jerusalem drinks first (v18), then the nations. The Greek has *the cup of this unmixed wine*.
- **v26 "the king of Sheshach"** — Babylon's name written in a letter-swap. Each letter of *Bavel* (b, b, l), counted from the start of the alphabet, is replaced by the letter that stands as far from the end (sh, sh, k). Jeremiah 51:41 sets the two names side by side: *How Sheshach is taken… How Babylon has become a horror among the nations!* Babylon, the LORD's servant against the nations (v9), drinks last, as v12 said it would be punished. The Greek does not have the line.
- **v29 "I am beginning to bring disaster on the city that is called by My name"** — the LORD's own city is first, so no other will be spared. Peter: *it is time for the judgment to begin from the house of God; and if it begins first from us, what will be the end of those who do not obey the gospel of God?* (1 Peter 4:17).
- **v30 "The LORD will roar from on high… He will raise a shout like those who tread grapes"** — Amos opens with *The LORD roars from Zion and utters His voice from Jerusalem* (Amos 1:2; also Joel 3:16). Here the roar comes from heaven, against the whole earth. *Heidad* is the harvest shout of the men treading the winepress (Isaiah 16:10), and the LORD treads the nations as grapes: *I have trodden the winepress alone* (Isaiah 63:3).
- **vv34, 38 "and of your scatterings… because of the oppressing sword"** — in v34, *u-tefotsotikhem* is found only here; its sense is uncertain, and the Greek does not have it. For *like a precious jar* the Greek has *like the choice rams*, the flock picture of vv34–36. In v38 the Greek reads *sword* (*the great sword*), and *mippenei cherev ha-yonah*, "because of the oppressing sword," is the book's own phrase at 46:16 and 50:16; the sword is what the LORD sends through this whole chapter (vv16, 27, 29, 31). The Hebrew here has *charon ha-yonah*, "the fierceness of the oppressor," with *charon* again in the next line, *His fierce anger*.

# Jeremiah 36

**1** And it came to pass in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, that this word came to Jeremiah from the LORD:

**2** "Take a scroll and write on it all the words that I have spoken to you concerning Israel and Judah and all the nations, from the day I first spoke to you, in the days of Josiah, until this day.

**3** Perhaps the house of Judah will hear all the disaster that I intend to do to them, so that each will turn from his evil way, and I will forgive their iniquity and their sin."

**4** Then Jeremiah called Baruch son of Neriah, and Baruch wrote on the scroll at Jeremiah's dictation all the words of the LORD that He had spoken to him.

**5** Jeremiah commanded Baruch, saying, "I am barred; I cannot go into the house of the LORD.

**6** So you go, and on a fast day read the words of the LORD from the scroll that you have written at my dictation, in the hearing of the people in the house of the LORD. Read them also in the hearing of all Judah who come in from their cities.

**7** Perhaps their plea will come before the LORD, and each will turn from his evil way; for great is the anger and the wrath that the LORD has pronounced against this people."

**8** Baruch son of Neriah did all that Jeremiah the prophet commanded him, reading from the book the words of the LORD in the house of the LORD.

**9** And it came to pass in the fifth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, in the ninth month, that all the people in Jerusalem and all the people who came from the cities of Judah to Jerusalem proclaimed a fast before the LORD.

**10** Then Baruch read from the book the words of Jeremiah in the house of the LORD, in the chamber of Gemariah son of Shaphan the scribe, in the upper court, at the entrance of the New Gate of the LORD's house, in the hearing of all the people.

**11** When Micaiah son of Gemariah, son of Shaphan, heard all the words of the LORD from the book,

**12** he went down to the king's house, to the scribe's chamber; and all the princes were sitting there: Elishama the scribe, Delaiah son of Shemaiah, Elnathan son of Achbor, Gemariah son of Shaphan, Zedekiah son of Hananiah and all the princes.

**13** Micaiah told them all the words that he had heard when Baruch read the book in the hearing of the people.

**14** Then all the princes sent Jehudi son of Nethaniah, son of Shelemiah, son of Cushi, to Baruch, saying, "Take in your hand the scroll that you read in the hearing of the people, and come." So Baruch son of Neriah took the scroll in his hand and came to them.

**15** They said to him, "Sit down, please, and read it in our hearing." So Baruch read it in their hearing.

**16** When they had heard all the words, they turned to one another in fear, and they said to Baruch, "We must surely tell the king all these words."

**17** They asked Baruch, "Tell us, please, how did you write all these words? Was it at his dictation?"

**18** Baruch said to them, "He dictated all these words to me, and I wrote them in the book with ink."

**19** Then the princes said to Baruch, "Go and hide, you and Jeremiah, and let no one know where you are."

**20** They went in to the king in the court, having left the scroll in the chamber of Elishama the scribe, and they told all the words in the hearing of the king.

**21** The king sent Jehudi to get the scroll, and he took it from the chamber of Elishama the scribe. Jehudi read it in the hearing of the king and of all the princes who stood beside the king.

**22** The king was sitting in the winter house in the ninth month, with a fire burning in the brazier before him.

**23** As Jehudi read three or four columns, the king would cut them off with the scribe's knife and throw them into the fire in the brazier, until the whole scroll was consumed in the fire that was in the brazier.

**24** Yet neither the king nor any of his servants who heard all these words was afraid, nor did they tear their garments.

**25** Even when Elnathan, Delaiah and Gemariah pleaded with the king not to burn the scroll, he would not listen to them.

**26** The king commanded Jerahmeel the king's son, Seraiah son of Azriel and Shelemiah son of Abdeel to seize Baruch the scribe and Jeremiah the prophet; but the LORD hid them.

## The Second Scroll

**27** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, after the king had burned the scroll with the words that Baruch had written at Jeremiah's dictation, saying,

**28** "Take another scroll, and write on it all the former words that were on the first scroll, which Jehoiakim king of Judah burned.

**29** Concerning Jehoiakim king of Judah you shall say: This is what the LORD says: You burned this scroll, saying, 'Why have you written on it that the king of Babylon will surely come and destroy this land, and cut off from it man and beast?'

**30** Therefore this is what the LORD says concerning Jehoiakim king of Judah: He will have no one to sit on the throne of David, and his dead body will be thrown out to the heat by day and to the frost by night.

**31** I will punish him and his seed and his servants for their iniquity, and I will bring on them and on the inhabitants of Jerusalem and on the men of Judah all the disaster that I have pronounced against them, and they did not listen."

**32** Then Jeremiah took another scroll and gave it to Baruch son of Neriah, the scribe, and he wrote on it at Jeremiah's dictation all the words of the book that Jehoiakim king of Judah had burned in the fire; and many similar words were added to them.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "the fourth year of Jehoiakim… Take a scroll… Perhaps the house of Judah will hear… and I will forgive"** — the year of 25:1, when Nebuchadnezzar defeated Pharaoh at Carchemish (46:2). *Megillat sefer*, "a scroll," the words of the psalm, *in the scroll of the book it is written of me* (Psalm 40:7). *Perhaps* is the hope of 26:3, given again.
- **vv4, 17–18, 32 "at Jeremiah's dictation… He dictated all these words to me, and I wrote them in the book with ink… many similar words were added"** — *mi-pi Yirmeyahu*, "from Jeremiah's mouth," six times in the chapter: the book is made from the prophet's mouth by the scribe's hand. The scroll the king burned was written again, with more besides; the book of Jeremiah as it stands is that second scroll and what was added to it.
- **v9 "in the fifth year… in the ninth month… a fast"** — a year after the scroll was written (v1). The Greek has *the eighth year*.
- **vv10–12 "the chamber of Gemariah son of Shaphan the scribe… Elishama the scribe"** — Shaphan was the secretary who read the book of the law found in the temple to King Josiah (2 Kings 22:8–10). Now the scroll is read in his son's chamber, and his grandson Micaiah carries it to the princes.
- **vv23–24 "the king would cut them off with the scribe's knife… neither the king nor any of his servants… was afraid, nor did they tear their garments"** — *When the king heard the words of the book of the instruction, he tore his clothes* (2 Kings 22:11): so Josiah. His son cuts the scroll column by column with the scribe's knife and feeds it to the fire. *Pachadu*, "afraid": the princes were afraid when they heard it (v16); the king was not.
- **v25 "pleaded with the king not to burn the scroll"** — Elnathan, the king's agent against Uriah (26:22), here pleads for the scroll. The Greek reads it the other way: *Elnathan and Gedaliah urged the king to burn the scroll*.
- **v26 "but the LORD hid them"** — the princes told them to hide (v19); the Hebrew credits the hiding to the LORD. The Greek has only *and they were hidden*.
- **v30 "He will have no one to sit on the throne of David, and his dead body will be thrown out to the heat by day and to the frost by night"** — the sentence of 22:18–19, *the burial of a donkey… thrown out beyond the gates of Jerusalem*. His son Jehoiachin reigned three months (2 Kings 24:8) and was taken to Babylon, and of him 22:30 says, *no man of his seed will prosper, sitting on the throne of David*.

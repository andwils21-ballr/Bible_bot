# Jeremiah 16

**1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**2** "You shall not take a wife, and you shall not have sons or daughters in this place.

**3** For thus says the LORD concerning the sons and daughters who are born in this place, and concerning the mothers who bear them and the fathers who father them in this land:

**4** They will die of deadly diseases. They will not be mourned or buried; they will be dung on the face of the ground. They will be consumed by the sword and by famine, and their corpses will be food for the birds of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth.

**5** For thus says the LORD: Do not enter the house of mourning, or go to lament, or grieve for them; for I have taken away My peace from this people, declares the LORD, My loyal love and My compassion.

**6** Great and small will die in this land; they will not be buried, and no one will lament for them, or gash himself, or shave his head for them.

**7** No one will break bread for the mourner to comfort him for the dead, or give him the cup of consolation to drink for his father or his mother.

**8** You shall not go into the house of feasting to sit with them, to eat and drink.

**9** For thus says the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: I am about to silence from this place, before your eyes and in your days, the voice of mirth and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride.

**10** And it shall come to pass, when you tell this people all these words, and they say to you, 'Why has the LORD pronounced all this great disaster against us? What is our iniquity? What is the sin that we have committed against the LORD our God?'

**11** that you shall say to them: 'Because your fathers have forsaken Me, declares the LORD, and have gone after other gods and served them and bowed down to them, and have forsaken Me and not kept My law;

**12** and you have done worse than your fathers, for each of you walks after the stubbornness of his evil heart, refusing to listen to Me.

**13** So I will hurl you out of this land into a land that neither you nor your fathers have known, and there you will serve other gods day and night, for I will show you no favor.'

**14** Therefore, days are coming, declares the LORD, when it will no longer be said, 'As the LORD lives, who brought up the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt,'

**15** but, 'As the LORD lives, who brought up the sons of Israel out of the land of the north and out of all the lands where He had driven them.' For I will bring them back to their land that I gave to their fathers.

**16** I am about to send for many fishers, declares the LORD, and they will fish for them; and afterward I will send for many hunters, and they will hunt them from every mountain and every hill and out of the clefts of the rocks.

**17** For My eyes are on all their ways; they are not hidden from Me, nor is their iniquity concealed from My eyes.

**18** I will first repay double for their iniquity and their sin, because they have polluted My land with the carcasses of their loathsome things and have filled My inheritance with their detestable things."

**19** LORD, my strength and my stronghold,  
my refuge in the day of trouble,  
to You the nations will come  
from the ends of the earth and say:  
"Our fathers have inherited nothing but lies,  
emptiness in which there is no profit.

**20** Can a man make gods for himself?  
Such are not gods!"

**21** "Therefore I am about to make them know;  
this time I will make them know  
My hand and My might,  
and they will know that My name is the LORD."

## Notes

- **v2 "You shall not take a wife"** — Hosea was told, *Go, take for yourself a wife* (Hosea 1:2), and Ezekiel was forbidden to mourn the wife who died (Ezekiel 24:16). Jeremiah is told to have no wife and no children at all: the prophet's own house is the message.
- **v5 "the house of mourning… My loyal love and My compassion"** — *beit marzeach*, here a house of mourning; the word is found elsewhere only in Amos 6:7, of a feast, *the revelry of those who sprawl*. What the LORD takes away is named: *My peace… My loyal love* (*chesed*) *and My compassion*.
- **v6 "or gash himself, or shave his head for them"** — the rites of grief the law forbade, *You shall not gash yourselves or make a bald patch between your eyes for the dead* (Deuteronomy 14:1). Even these will not be done.
- **v9 "the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride"** — the silence of 7:34, now *before your eyes and in your days*.
- **v13 "there you will serve other gods day and night"** — the curse of Deuteronomy 28:64, *there you will serve other gods that neither you nor your fathers have known, wood and stone*.
- **vv14–15 "no longer… out of the land of Egypt, but… out of the land of the north"** — the oath by the LORD who brought Israel out of Egypt will give way to an oath by the LORD who brought them back from exile, a second exodus greater than the first. The two verses come again in 23:7–8.
- **v16 "I am about to send for many fishers… and afterward… many hunters"** — *dayyagim*, "fishers," is the read form; the written form is *dugim*, "fish." Here the fishers and hunters gather people for judgment, and none can hide (v17). Jesus uses the same picture for His call: *Come, follow Me, and I will make you fishers of people* (Matthew 4:19).
- **v18 "I will first repay double… the carcasses of their loathsome things"** — *mishneh*, "double," the word Isaiah uses when the debt is paid, *she has received from the LORD's hand double for all her sins* (Isaiah 40:2). The idols are called carcasses, as the covenant curse called them corpses: *I will… throw your corpses on the corpses of your idols* (Leviticus 26:30).
- **v21 "they will know that My name is the LORD"** — *ki shemi YHWH*, as in Isaiah 42:8, *I am the LORD; that is My name*.

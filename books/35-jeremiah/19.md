# Jeremiah 19

**1** This is what the LORD says: Go and buy a potter's earthenware flask, and take some of the elders of the people and some of the elders of the priests,

**2** and go out to the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, at the entrance of the Potsherd Gate, and proclaim there the words that I will speak to you.

**3** Say: Hear the word of the LORD, kings of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem. This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am about to bring disaster on this place, so that the ears of everyone who hears of it will tingle,

**4** because they have forsaken Me and made this place foreign, and have burned incense in it to other gods whom neither they nor their fathers nor the kings of Judah have known; and they have filled this place with the blood of the innocent,

**5** and have built the high places of Baal to burn their sons in the fire as burnt offerings to Baal, which I did not command or speak, nor did it come into My mind.

**6** Therefore, days are coming, declares the LORD, when this place will no longer be called Topheth or the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, but the Valley of Slaughter.

**7** In this place I will make void the plans of Judah and Jerusalem; I will make them fall by the sword before their enemies and by the hand of those who seek their life; and I will give their corpses as food to the birds of the heavens and to the beasts of the earth.

**8** I will make this city a horror and a thing to be hissed at; everyone who passes by it will be horrified and will hiss because of all its wounds.

**9** I will make them eat the flesh of their sons and the flesh of their daughters, and each will eat the flesh of his neighbor, in the siege and in the distress with which their enemies and those who seek their life will distress them.

**10** Then break the flask before the eyes of the men who go with you,

**11** and say to them: This is what the LORD of hosts says: So will I break this people and this city, as one breaks a potter's jar so that it can never be mended. They will bury in Topheth because there is no other place to bury.

**12** This is what I will do to this place, declares the LORD, and to its inhabitants, making this city like Topheth.

**13** The houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah will be unclean like the place of Topheth — all the houses on whose roofs they burned incense to all the host of heaven and poured out drink offerings to other gods.

**14** Then Jeremiah came from Topheth, where the LORD had sent him to prophesy, and he stood in the court of the LORD's house and said to all the people,

**15** "This is what the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am about to bring on this city and on all its towns all the disaster that I have pronounced against it, because they have stiffened their neck, refusing to hear My words."

## Notes

- **vv1, 7, 10 "a potter's earthenware flask… I will make void the plans… break the flask"** — *baqbuq*, "flask," and *u-vaqqoti*, "I will make void," from *baqaq*, the verb of *The LORD is about to empty the earth* (Isaiah 24:1): the same letters. Where the flask is broken, the LORD empties out Judah's plans.

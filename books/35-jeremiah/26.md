# Jeremiah 26

**1** In the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, this word came from the LORD:

**2** Thus says the LORD: Stand in the court of the LORD's house, and speak to all who come from the cities of Judah to worship in the LORD's house all the words that I command you to speak to them; do not take away a word.

**3** Perhaps they will listen and turn, each from his evil way, and I will relent of the disaster that I intend to do to them because of the evil of their deeds.

**4** Say to them: Thus says the LORD: If you will not listen to Me, to walk in My law that I have set before you,

**5** and to listen to the words of My servants the prophets whom I send to you again and again, though you have not listened,

**6** then I will make this house like Shiloh, and I will make this city a curse to all the nations of the earth.

**7** The priests and the prophets and all the people heard Jeremiah speaking these words in the house of the LORD.

**8** When Jeremiah had finished speaking all that the LORD had commanded him to speak to all the people, the priests and the prophets and all the people seized him, saying, "You must die!

**9** Why have you prophesied in the name of the LORD, saying, 'This house will be like Shiloh, and this city will be laid waste, without an inhabitant'?" All the people crowded around Jeremiah in the house of the LORD.

**10** When the officials of Judah heard these things, they came up from the king's house to the house of the LORD and sat at the entrance of the New Gate of the LORD's house.

**11** Then the priests and the prophets said to the officials and to all the people, "This man deserves to die, because he has prophesied against this city, as you have heard with your own ears."

**12** Jeremiah said to all the officials and to all the people, "The LORD sent me to prophesy against this house and against this city all the words that you have heard.

**13** So now, amend your ways and your deeds, and obey the voice of the LORD your God, and the LORD will relent of the disaster that He has pronounced against you.

**14** As for me, I am in your hands; do with me as seems good and right to you.

**15** Only know for certain that if you put me to death, you will bring innocent blood on yourselves and on this city and its inhabitants; for in truth the LORD has sent me to you to speak all these words in your hearing."

**16** Then the officials and all the people said to the priests and the prophets, "This man does not deserve to die, for he has spoken to us in the name of the LORD our God."

**17** Some of the elders of the land rose and said to all the assembled people,

**18** "Micah of Moresheth prophesied in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah, and said to all the people of Judah: Thus says the LORD of hosts:  
Zion shall be plowed as a field;  
Jerusalem shall become a heap of ruins,  
and the mountain of the house a wooded height.

**19** Did Hezekiah king of Judah and all Judah put him to death? Did he not fear the LORD and plead with the LORD, so that the LORD relented of the disaster that He had pronounced against them? But we are about to bring great disaster on ourselves."

**20** There was also a man who prophesied in the name of the LORD, Uriah son of Shemaiah from Kiriath-jearim. He prophesied against this city and against this land in words just like Jeremiah's.

**21** When King Jehoiakim, with all his warriors and all the officials, heard his words, the king sought to put him to death. But Uriah heard of it and was afraid, and fled and went to Egypt.

**22** King Jehoiakim sent men to Egypt, Elnathan son of Achbor and others with him.

**23** They brought Uriah out of Egypt and took him to King Jehoiakim, who struck him down with the sword and threw his body into the graves of the common people.

**24** But the hand of Ahikam son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah, so that he was not given into the hand of the people to be put to death.

## Notes

- **vv1–6 "Stand in the court of the LORD's house… I will make this house like Shiloh"** — the temple sermon of chapter 7 (7:2, 12–14), told here as an event, with what followed. *Do not take away a word* is the law's command about itself, *you shall not take away from it* (Deuteronomy 4:2): the prophet may not soften the message.
- **vv7–16 "the priests and the prophets"** — in vv7, 8, 11 and 16 the Greek calls them *the false prophets*; the Hebrew says only *the prophets*. It is the prophets who demand Jeremiah's death, and the officials and the people who refuse it.
- **vv8, 11, 16 "You must die!… This man deserves to die… This man does not deserve to die"** — the law's sentence on the prophet who speaks a word the LORD has not commanded, *that prophet shall die* (Deuteronomy 18:20). *Mishpat mavet*, "a judgment of death," is the legal phrase of Deuteronomy 19:6, *he did not deserve to die*, and 21:22, *a sin deserving death*. The officials sit as judges at the gate, and the verdict turns on the law's own test: he has spoken *in the name of the LORD our God* (v16).
- **vv18–19 "Zion shall be plowed as a field… Did he not fear the LORD and plead with the LORD"** — the elders quote Micah 3:12 word for word; Micah prophesied in the days of Hezekiah (Micah 1:1). *Va-yechal et penei YHWH*, "he pleaded with the LORD," is what Moses did after the golden calf, *Moses pleaded with the LORD his God* (Exodus 32:11), and there too *the LORD relented* (32:14). The elders argue from Hezekiah's day: a prophet of doom was heard, not killed, and the city was spared.
- **v22 "Elnathan son of Achbor"** — the king's agent who brings Uriah back. He is among the officials who later hear Jeremiah's scroll read, and he pleads with Jehoiakim not to burn it (36:12, 25).
- **v23 "threw his body into the graves of the common people"** — *qivrei benei ha-am*, a phrase found only here and where Josiah threw the dust of the burned Asherah *on the graves of the common people* (2 Kings 23:6). Josiah's son throws a prophet's body where his father threw the idol's ashes.
- **v24 "Ahikam son of Shaphan"** — one of the men Josiah sent to inquire of the LORD when the book of the law was found (2 Kings 22:12–13). His son Gedaliah will take Jeremiah into his care after Jerusalem falls (39:14; 40:5–6).

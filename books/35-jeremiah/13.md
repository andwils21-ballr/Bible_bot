# Jeremiah 13

**1** Thus the LORD said to me: "Go and buy yourself a linen loincloth, and put it around your waist, but do not put it in water."

**2** So I bought the loincloth as the LORD had said, and put it around my waist.

**3** The word of the LORD came to me a second time, saying,

**4** "Take the loincloth that you bought, which is around your waist, and arise, go to the Euphrates, and hide it there in a cleft of the rock."

**5** So I went and hid it by the Euphrates, as the LORD had commanded me.

**6** And it came to pass after many days that the LORD said to me, "Arise, go to the Euphrates, and take from there the loincloth that I commanded you to hide there."

**7** So I went to the Euphrates and dug, and I took the loincloth from the place where I had hidden it; it was ruined, good for nothing.

**8** Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

**9** "Thus says the LORD: Just so will I ruin the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem.

**10** This evil people, who refuse to hear My words, who walk in the stubbornness of their heart and have gone after other gods to serve them and to bow down to them, will be like this loincloth, which is good for nothing.

**11** For as the loincloth clings to a man's waist, so I made the whole house of Israel and the whole house of Judah cling to Me, declares the LORD, to be for Me a people, a name, a praise and a glory; but they did not listen.

**12** You shall speak this word to them: Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: Every jar is to be filled with wine. They will say to you, 'Do we not know very well that every jar is to be filled with wine?'

**13** Then you shall say to them, Thus says the LORD: I am about to fill all the inhabitants of this land — the kings who sit on David's throne, the priests, the prophets and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem — with drunkenness.

**14** I will dash them one against another, fathers and sons together, declares the LORD. I will not pity or spare or have compassion; nothing will keep Me from destroying them."

**15** Hear and give ear; do not be proud,  
for the LORD has spoken.

**16** Give glory to the LORD your God  
before He brings darkness,  
before your feet stumble  
on the mountains at twilight;  
and while you look for light,  
He turns it into the shadow of death  
and makes it deep darkness.

**17** But if you will not listen,  
my soul will weep in secret for your pride;  
my eyes will weep bitterly and run down with tears,  
because the LORD's flock has been taken captive.

**18** Say to the king and to the queen mother:  
"Take a lowly seat,  
for your beautiful crown  
has come down from your heads."

**19** The cities of the Negev are shut up,  
and there is no one to open them;  
all Judah is taken into exile,  
wholly taken into exile.

**20** Lift up your eyes and see  
those who come from the north.  
Where is the flock that was given to you,  
your beautiful flock?

**21** What will you say when He sets over you as head  
those you yourself taught to be your friends?  
Will not pangs take hold of you,  
like a woman in labor?

**22** If you say in your heart,  
"Why have these things come upon me?" —  
for the greatness of your iniquity  
your skirts are lifted  
and you are violated.

**23** Can the Cushite change his skin,  
or the leopard his spots?  
Then you also can do good,  
who are accustomed to doing evil.

**24** I will scatter them like chaff  
driven by the wind of the desert.

**25** This is your lot,  
the portion I have measured out to you,  
declares the LORD,  
because you have forgotten Me  
and trusted in falsehood.

**26** I Myself will lift up your skirts over your face,  
and your shame will be seen.

**27** Your adulteries and your neighings,  
your lewd prostitution,  
on the hills in the field —  
I have seen your loathsome things.  
Woe to you, Jerusalem!  
You will not be made clean —  
how long yet?

## Notes

- **vv1–7 "a linen loincloth… go to the Euphrates, and hide it"** — *ezor pishtim*, a waistcloth of linen, the cloth of the priests' holy garments (*linen undergarments… a linen sash*, Leviticus 16:4), worn by a priest's son (1:1). *Perat* is the Euphrates, the river of Eden (Genesis 2:14), and the river into which the scroll against Babylon is thrown at the end of the book (51:63); the Greek has *the Euphrates*. Buried there, unwashed, the cloth rots.
- **v11 "as the loincloth clings to a man's waist, so I made the whole house of Israel… cling to Me… to be for Me a people, a name, a praise and a glory"** — *davaq*, the word of Genesis 2:24, *a man… clings to his woman*, and of the command *to Him you shall hold fast* (Deuteronomy 10:20). *A name, a praise and a glory* is the promise of Deuteronomy 26:19, *for praise and for a name and for glory*.
- **vv12–14 "Every jar is to be filled with wine… I will dash them one against another"** — a saying so plain the people mock it, and the LORD gives it its meaning: the jars are the people, the wine is drunkenness, and full jars knocked together break.
- **v18 "Say to the king and to the queen mother"** — *ha-gevirah*, the king's mother. Jehoiachin went out to the king of Babylon *he and his mother* (2 Kings 24:12, 15), and Jeremiah's letter to the exiles names *Jeconiah the king and the queen mother* (29:2).
- **v23 "Can the Cushite change his skin, or the leopard his spots?"** — *kushi*, a man of Cush. Moses married *a Cushite woman* (Numbers 12:1), and in this book it is *Ebed-melech the Cushite* who hears that Jeremiah has been thrown into the cistern and pulls him out (38:7–13).
- **vv22, 26 "your skirts are lifted… I Myself will lift up your skirts over your face"** — the shaming of a woman taken in adultery; Nahum says the same words to Nineveh, *I will lift up your skirts over your face* (Nahum 3:5). In v22 the Hebrew says *your heels suffer violence*.

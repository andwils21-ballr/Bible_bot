# Jeremiah 27

**1** In the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from the LORD:

**2** Thus the LORD said to me: Make yourself straps and yoke bars, and put them on your neck.

**3** Then send them to the king of Edom, the king of Moab, the king of the sons of Ammon, the king of Tyre and the king of Sidon, by the envoys who have come to Jerusalem to Zedekiah king of Judah.

**4** Give them this charge for their masters: Thus says the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: This is what you shall say to your masters:

**5** It is I who made the earth, and the man and the beast that are on the face of the earth, by My great power and My outstretched arm, and I give it to whomever seems right in My eyes.

**6** So now I have given all these lands into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant; I have given him even the beasts of the field to serve him.

**7** All the nations will serve him, and his son and his son's son, until the time of his own land comes; then many nations and great kings will make him their slave.

**8** But the nation or kingdom that will not serve him, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and will not put its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon, I will punish that nation by the sword, by famine and by pestilence, declares the LORD, until I have consumed them by his hand.

**9** As for you, do not listen to your prophets, your soothsayers, your dreams, those who read omens for you or your sorcerers, who keep saying to you, "You will not serve the king of Babylon."

**10** For they are prophesying a lie to you, so that you will be removed far from your land; I will drive you out, and you will perish.

**11** But the nation that brings its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and serves him, I will leave on its own land, declares the LORD, and it will till it and live in it.

## To Zedekiah and the Priests

**12** To Zedekiah king of Judah I spoke in the same way: Bring your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon, and serve him and his people, and live.

**13** Why will you die, you and your people, by the sword, by famine and by pestilence, as the LORD has spoken concerning the nation that will not serve the king of Babylon?

**14** Do not listen to the words of the prophets who keep saying to you, "You will not serve the king of Babylon," for they are prophesying a lie to you.

**15** I have not sent them, declares the LORD, but they are prophesying falsely in My name, so that I will drive you out and you will perish, you and the prophets who are prophesying to you.

**16** Then I spoke to the priests and to all this people, saying: Thus says the LORD: Do not listen to the words of your prophets who are prophesying to you, "The vessels of the LORD's house will now shortly be brought back from Babylon," for they are prophesying a lie to you.

**17** Do not listen to them. Serve the king of Babylon, and live. Why should this city become a ruin?

**18** If they are prophets, and if the word of the LORD is with them, let them intercede with the LORD of hosts, so that the vessels that are left in the house of the LORD, in the house of the king of Judah and in Jerusalem do not go to Babylon.

**19** For thus says the LORD of hosts concerning the pillars, the Sea, the stands and the rest of the vessels that are left in this city,

**20** which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon did not take when he carried Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon, with all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem —

**21** thus says the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, concerning the vessels that are left in the house of the LORD, in the house of the king of Judah and in Jerusalem:

**22** They will be carried to Babylon, and there they will stay until the day I attend to them, declares the LORD. Then I will bring them up and restore them to this place.

## Notes

- **v1 "In the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim"** — the same words as 26:1, but the chapter is set under Zedekiah: the envoys come *to Zedekiah king of Judah* (v3), Jeremiah speaks *to Zedekiah* (v12), and the next chapter begins *in that same year, at the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah* (28:1). The Greek has no v1. The Hebrew stands as it is.
- **vv2, 8, 11–12 "straps and yoke bars… put its neck under the yoke"** — *motot*, the bars of a yoke. At the exodus the LORD broke them: *I broke the bars of your yoke and made you walk upright* (Leviticus 26:13). Now the prophet wears them, and tells five kings and his own king to bend their necks. Hananiah will break them off him (28:10).
- **vv5–6 "It is I who made the earth… I have given him even the beasts of the field"** — the Creator gives the earth *to whomever seems right in My eyes*, and for now that is *Nebuchadnezzar… My servant*, as at 25:9. Daniel tells the same king, *the beasts of the field and the birds of the heavens He has given into your hand* (Daniel 2:38).
- **v7 "his son and his son's son, until the time of his own land comes"** — Babylon's rule has an end, as 25:12, 14 said. The Greek does not have the verse.
- **v9 "your prophets, your soothsayers, your dreams, those who read omens for you or your sorcerers"** — the law's list of what Israel must not have, *a soothsayer, one who reads omens… or a sorcerer* (Deuteronomy 18:10), with Judah's own prophets at its head. For *your dreams* the Greek has *those who dream for you*.
- **v18 "let them intercede with the LORD of hosts"** — *yifge'u*, the verb forbidden to Jeremiah himself, *do not intercede with Me* (7:16). The test he sets them is the work of a prophet, as the LORD said of Abraham: *he is a prophet, and he will pray for you* (Genesis 20:7).
- **vv19–22 "the pillars, the Sea, the stands… until the day I attend to them. Then I will bring them up"** — Nebuchadnezzar had already *cut in pieces all the vessels of gold that Solomon king of Israel had made* (2 Kings 24:13). What was left the Chaldeans *broke in pieces and carried the bronze to Babylon* (2 Kings 25:13). The rest came back: *King Cyrus also brought out the vessels of the house of the LORD that Nebuchadnezzar had carried away* (Ezra 1:7). The Greek of vv16–22 is much shorter and has no promise of return.

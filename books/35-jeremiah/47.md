# Jeremiah 47

**1** The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the Philistines, before Pharaoh struck Gaza.

**2** This is what the LORD says:  
Waters are rising from the north  
and will become an overflowing torrent;  
they will overflow the land and all that fills it,  
the city and those who live in it.  
People will cry out,  
and every inhabitant of the land will wail.

**3** At the noise of the stamping hoofs of his stallions,  
at the rumbling of his chariots and the clatter of their wheels,  
fathers will not turn back for their children,  
so limp are their hands,

**4** because of the day that is coming  
to destroy all the Philistines,  
to cut off from Tyre and Sidon  
every helper who survives;  
for the LORD is destroying the Philistines,  
the remnant of the coastland of Caphtor.

**5** Baldness has come upon Gaza;  
Ashkelon is silenced.  
Remnant of their valley,  
how long will you gash yourself?

**6** Ah, sword of the LORD!  
How long until you are quiet?  
Go back into your sheath;  
rest and be still!

**7** How can you be quiet,  
when the LORD has given it orders?  
Against Ashkelon and against the seashore,  
there He has appointed it.

## Notes

- **v1 "before Pharaoh struck Gaza"** — the Hebrew does not say which Pharaoh. The Greek has only *Against the foreigners*, its word for the Philistines, with no date.
- **vv2–3 "Waters are rising from the north… the land and all that fills it, the city and those who live in it… his stallions"** — Egypt rose like the Nile (46:7–8); here the flood comes out of the north, where the book's first vision placed the disaster (1:14). The words are those of the enemy from the north in 8:16, *the neighing of his stallions… the land and all that fills it, the city and those who live in it*.
- **v3 "fathers will not turn back for their children, so limp are their hands"** — *rifyon yadayim*, the slack hands of Judah's own terror, *our hands fall limp* (6:24).
- **v4 "Tyre and Sidon… the remnant of the coastland of Caphtor"** — the verse calls the Philistines the remnant of Caphtor; Deuteronomy tells how the land around Gaza was taken by *the Caphtorim, who came out of Caphtor* (Deuteronomy 2:23).
- **v5 "Baldness has come upon Gaza… Remnant of their valley, how long will you gash yourself?"** — the mourning of 16:6 and 41:5, the shaved head and the gashed body. The Greek has *the remnant of the Anakim* for *the remnant of their valley*, and the Anakim had last been left in this very coast: *only in Gaza, in Gath and in Ashdod did some remain* (Joshua 11:22).
- **vv6–7 "Ah, sword of the LORD! How long until you are quiet?… How can you be quiet, when the LORD has given it orders?"** — a cry for the sword to rest, and the answer: the sword is not its own. *The sword of the LORD devours from one end of the land to the other* (12:12). The Hebrew turns from *you* to *it* in the middle of v7.

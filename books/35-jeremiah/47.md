# Jeremiah 47

**1** The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the Philistines, before Pharaoh struck Gaza.

**2** This is what the LORD says:  
Waters are rising from the north  
and will become an overflowing torrent;  
they will overflow the land and all that fills it,  
the city and those who live in it.  
People will cry out,  
and every inhabitant of the land will wail.

**3** At the noise of the stamping hoofs of his stallions,  
at the rumbling of his chariots and the clatter of their wheels,  
fathers will not turn back for their children,  
so limp are their hands,

**4** because of the day that is coming  
to destroy all the Philistines,  
to cut off from Tyre and Sidon  
every helper who survives;  
for the LORD is destroying the Philistines,  
the remnant of the coastland of Caphtor.

**5** Baldness has come upon Gaza;  
Ashkelon is silenced.  
Remnant of their valley,  
how long will you gash yourself?

**6** Ah, sword of the LORD!  
How long until you are quiet?  
Go back into your sheath;  
rest and be still!

**7** How can you be quiet,  
when the LORD has given it orders?  
Against Ashkelon and against the seashore,  
there He has appointed it.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "before Pharaoh struck Gaza"** — the Hebrew does not say which Pharaoh. The Greek has only *Against the foreigners*, its word for the Philistines, with no date.

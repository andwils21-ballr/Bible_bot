# Romans 12

## A Living Sacrifice

**1** So I appeal to you, brothers, by the compassion of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God, which is your reasonable worship.

**2** Do not be conformed to this age, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you may test and approve what the will of God is: what is good and pleasing and perfect.

## One Body, Many Gifts

**3** For by the grace given to me I tell every one of you not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think, but to think with sober judgment, each according to the measure of faith that God has assigned.

**4** For as in one body we have many parts, and the parts do not all have the same work,

**5** so we, though many, are one body in Christ, and each of us parts of one another.

**6** We have gifts that differ according to the grace given to us: if prophecy, in proportion to our faith;

**7** if service, in serving; the one who teaches, in teaching;

**8** the one who encourages, in encouragement; the one who gives, with generosity; the one who leads, with diligence; the one who shows mercy, with cheerfulness.

## Love Without Pretense

**9** Let love be without pretense. Hate what is evil; hold fast to what is good.

**10** Love one another with brotherly affection; outdo one another in showing honor.

**11** Do not be slack in zeal; be fervent in spirit; serve the Lord.

**12** Rejoice in hope, be patient in suffering, keep on in prayer.

**13** Share in the needs of the saints; pursue hospitality.

**14** Bless those who persecute you; bless and do not curse.

**15** Rejoice with those who rejoice, weep with those who weep.

**16** Be of one mind with one another. Do not set your mind on high things, but go along with the lowly. Do not be wise in your own eyes.

**17** Repay no one evil for evil. Give thought to what is good in the sight of all people.

**18** If it is possible, so far as it depends on you, live at peace with all people.

**19** Beloved, never take revenge yourselves, but leave room for the wrath; for it is written, "Vengeance is Mine, I will repay, says the Lord."

**20** On the contrary: "If your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him something to drink; for by doing this you will heap burning coals on his head."

**21** Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "present your bodies… your reasonable worship… be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you may test and approve"** — the end of chapter 1 turned around. There the nations' bodies were dishonored (1:24); here bodies are *presented*, the verb of 6:13. There they *worshiped and served the creature* (1:25, *elatreusan*); here is *latreia*, **worship**. There, because they *did not think God worth acknowledging* (*ouk edokimasan*), God handed them over to *a worthless mind* (*adokimon noun*, 1:28); here the mind is renewed so as *to test and approve*, *dokimazein*, the will of God. *Metamorphousthe*, **be transformed**, is the word of Jesus *transfigured* on the mountain (Mark 9:2).
- **v1 "your reasonable worship"** — *tēn logikēn latreian*. *Logikos* is from *logos*, the word of John 1:1: what belongs to reason and to the word. Other English Bibles have **spiritual worship** or **reasonable service**; *spiritual* in Paul is another word, *pneumatikos* (1:11; 7:14). Peter uses *logikos* of *the pure milk* the newborn long for (1 Peter 2:2).
- **vv14, 17–21 "Bless those who persecute you… Vengeance is Mine… heap burning coals on his head"** — Jesus' teaching, *love your enemies… bless those who curse you* (Luke 6:27–28). *Vengeance is Mine* is from Moses' song (Deuteronomy 32:35), the song Paul quoted at 10:19 and took up again at 11:11, 14. V20 is Proverbs 25:21–22, in this Bible Reproof 1:21–22, which goes on *and the LORD will reward you*. The *burning coals* have been read as the shame that leads an enemy to repent, or as the judgment left to God; either way, the one who was wronged is to feed him.

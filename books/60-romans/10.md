# Romans 10

## The End of the Law

**1** Brothers, my heart's desire and my prayer to God for them is that they may be saved.

**2** For I bear them witness that they have a zeal for God, but not according to knowledge.

**3** Not knowing the righteousness of God, and seeking to establish their own, they did not submit to the righteousness of God.

**4** For Christ is the end of the Law for righteousness to everyone who believes.

## The Word of Faith

**5** For Moses writes about the righteousness that comes from the Law, that the person who does it shall live by it.

**6** But the righteousness that comes from faith says: "Do not say in your heart, 'Who will go up into heaven?'" (that is, to bring Christ down)

**7** "or 'Who will go down into the abyss?'" (that is, to bring Christ up from the dead).

**8** But what does it say? "The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart" (that is, the word of faith that we proclaim):

**9** because if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved.

**10** For with the heart one believes, and it leads to righteousness; with the mouth one confesses, and it leads to salvation.

**11** For the Scripture says, "Everyone who believes in Him will not be put to shame."

**12** For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek: the same Lord is Lord of all, rich toward all who call on Him.

**13** For "everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved."

## How Beautiful Are the Feet

**14** How, then, will they call on Him in whom they have not believed? How will they believe in Him of whom they have not heard? How will they hear without someone to proclaim Him?

**15** How will they proclaim Him unless they are sent? As it is written: "How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news of good things!"

**16** But not all have obeyed the gospel. For Isaiah says, "Lord, who has believed what we have heard?"

**17** So faith comes from what is heard, and what is heard comes through the word of Christ.

**18** But I ask, have they not heard? Indeed they have:  
"Their voice has gone out into all the earth,  
and their words to the ends of the world."

**19** But I ask, did Israel not understand? First Moses says:  
"I will make you jealous with what is no nation;  
with a foolish nation I will provoke you."

**20** Then Isaiah is bold enough to say:  
"I was found by those who did not seek Me;  
I showed Myself to those who did not ask for Me."

**21** But of Israel he says, "All day long I have spread out My hands to a disobedient and contrary people."

## Notes

- **v4 "Christ is the end of the Law"** — *telos*, which is both **end** and **goal**: the point where a thing stops, and the point it was running toward. Read the first way, Christ brings the Law's role in righteousness to a close; read the second, the Law was always heading to Christ. Chapter 9 has just told the story as a race, Israel *pursuing* a law of righteousness and not *reaching* it, stumbling over the stone (9:31–32); the English *end*, like the Greek, holds both.
- **vv9–13 "Jesus is Lord… Everyone who believes in Him will not be put to shame… everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved"** — v13 is Joel 2:32, where the name called on is *the LORD*, YHWH; Paul gives it to the *Lord* confessed in v9, Jesus, as Peter did at Pentecost (Acts 2:21). V11 is Isaiah 28:16 in the Greek, the stone laid in Zion (9:33), where the Hebrew has *whoever believes will not be in haste*.

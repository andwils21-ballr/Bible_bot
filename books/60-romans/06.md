# Romans 6

## Buried and Raised with Christ

**1** What shall we say, then? Are we to go on in sin so that grace may increase?

**2** By no means! How can we who died to sin still live in it?

**3** Or do you not know that all of us who were baptized into Christ Jesus were baptized into His death?

**4** We were buried with Him, then, through baptism into death, so that, just as Christ was raised from the dead through the glory of the Father, we too might walk in newness of life.

**5** For if we have grown together with Him in the likeness of His death, we will certainly also grow together with Him in the likeness of His resurrection.

**6** We know that our old self was crucified with Him, so that the body of sin might be done away with, and we would no longer be slaves to sin.

**7** For the one who has died has been set free from sin.

**8** If we died with Christ, we believe that we will also live with Him,

**9** knowing that Christ, once raised from the dead, will never die again; death no longer has dominion over Him.

**10** The death He died, He died to sin once for all; but the life He lives, He lives to God.

**11** So you too must reckon yourselves dead to sin and alive to God in Christ Jesus.

## Weapons of Righteousness

**12** So do not let sin reign in your mortal body, to make you obey its desires.

**13** Do not present the parts of your body to sin as weapons of unrighteousness, but present yourselves to God as people alive from the dead, and the parts of your body to God as weapons of righteousness.

**14** For sin will not have dominion over you, because you are not under the Law but under grace.

## Slaves of Righteousness

**15** What then? Are we to sin because we are not under the Law but under grace? By no means!

**16** Do you not know that if you present yourselves to anyone as obedient slaves, you are slaves of the one you obey, either of sin, which leads to death, or of obedience, which leads to righteousness?

**17** But thanks be to God that you, who were once slaves of sin, have obeyed from the heart the pattern of teaching to which you were handed over,

**18** and, set free from sin, have become slaves of righteousness.

**19** I am speaking in human terms because of the weakness of your flesh. Just as you once presented the parts of your body as slaves to impurity and to lawlessness, leading to more lawlessness, so now present them as slaves to righteousness, leading to holiness.

**20** When you were slaves of sin, you were free from righteousness.

**21** What fruit did you have then from the things you are now ashamed of? Their end is death.

**22** But now that you have been set free from sin and have become slaves of God, the fruit you have leads to holiness, and its end is eternal life.

**23** For the wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.

## Notes

- **v11 "reckon yourselves dead to sin and alive to God"** — *logizesthe*, **reckon**, the word of chapter 4: God *reckoned* Abraham's faith as righteousness. Now the believer is told to do the same accounting about himself.

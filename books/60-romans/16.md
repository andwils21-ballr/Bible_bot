# Romans 16

## Phoebe

**1** I commend to you our sister Phoebe, a servant of the church at Cenchreae,

**2** so that you may welcome her in the Lord in a way worthy of the saints, and help her in whatever she may need from you, for she has been a patron of many, and of me as well.

## Greetings to the Believers in Rome

**3** Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus,

**4** who risked their necks for my life. Not only I but all the churches of the Gentiles give thanks for them.

**5** Greet also the church that meets in their house. Greet my beloved Epaenetus, who was the firstfruits of Asia for Christ.

**6** Greet Mary, who has worked hard for you.

**7** Greet Andronicus and Junia, my fellow Jews and my fellow prisoners. They are outstanding among the apostles, and they were in Christ before I was.

**8** Greet Ampliatus, my beloved in the Lord.

**9** Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and my beloved Stachys.

**10** Greet Apelles, who is approved in Christ. Greet those who belong to the household of Aristobulus.

**11** Greet my fellow Jew Herodion. Greet those in the household of Narcissus who are in the Lord.

**12** Greet Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who work hard in the Lord. Greet the beloved Persis, who has worked hard in the Lord.

**13** Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother, who has been a mother to me as well.

**14** Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers who are with them.

**15** Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints who are with them.

**16** Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the churches of Christ greet you.

## A Last Warning

**17** I appeal to you, brothers, to watch out for those who cause divisions and put obstacles in the way, contrary to the teaching you have learned. Keep away from them.

**18** For such people do not serve our Lord Christ but their own appetites, and by smooth talk and flattery they deceive the hearts of the unsuspecting.

**19** Your obedience is known to everyone, so I rejoice over you; but I want you to be wise as to what is good and innocent as to what is evil.

**20** The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.

## Greetings from Paul's Companions

**21** Timothy, my fellow worker, greets you, and so do Lucius and Jason and Sosipater, my fellow Jews.

**22** I, Tertius, who wrote this letter down, greet you in the Lord.

**23** Gaius, who is host to me and to the whole church, greets you. Erastus, the city treasurer, greets you, and our brother Quartus.

**24** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.

## Notes

- **v1 "Phoebe, a servant of the church at Cenchreae"** — *diakonos*, the word for the ruler as *God's servant* (13:4) and for Christ as *a servant of the circumcised* (15:8). In Philippians 1:1 and 1 Timothy 3:8 the same word names an office in the church, **deacon**, and Phoebe's title can be read so.
- **v7 "Andronicus and Junia… outstanding among the apostles"** — the edition reads *Iounian*, **Junia**, a woman's name, and marks the place as one where manuscripts differ. *Episēmoi en tois apostolois* can mean **outstanding among the apostles**, counted as apostles themselves, or **well known to the apostles**, held in honor by them. *Syngeneis*, **fellow Jews**, is the word of 9:3, *my own people according to the flesh*.
- **v24 "The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen."** — the edition prints this verse in brackets, as one manuscripts differ on, and ends the letter here. Many English Bibles end Romans with a blessing to God numbered 16:25–27; it is not in this edition's text.

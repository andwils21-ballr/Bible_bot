# Romans 14

## Do Not Judge Your Brother

**1** As for the one who is weak in faith, welcome him, but not to argue over opinions.

**2** One person believes he may eat anything, while the one who is weak eats only vegetables.

**3** The one who eats must not look down on the one who does not, and the one who does not eat must not judge the one who does, for God has welcomed him.

**4** Who are you to judge someone else's servant? It is before his own master that he stands or falls. He will stand, for the Lord is able to make him stand.

**5** One person values one day above another, while another values every day alike. Each should be fully convinced in his own mind.

**6** The one who observes the day observes it for the Lord. The one who eats, eats for the Lord, since he gives thanks to God; the one who does not eat abstains for the Lord, and he too gives thanks to God.

**7** For none of us lives for himself, and none of us dies for himself.

**8** If we live, we live for the Lord; if we die, we die for the Lord. So whether we live or die, we are the Lord's.

**9** For this is why Christ died and lived again: to be Lord of both the dead and the living.

**10** Why do you judge your brother? Or you, why do you look down on your brother? For we will all stand before the judgment seat of God,

**11** for it is written:  
"As I live, says the Lord, every knee will bow to Me,  
and every tongue will confess to God."

**12** So each of us will give an account of himself.

## Do Not Make Your Brother Stumble

**13** So let us stop judging one another, and judge this instead: never to put a stumbling block or a snare in a brother's way.

**14** I know and am persuaded in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean in itself; but it is unclean for anyone who reckons it unclean.

**15** For if your brother is grieved by what you eat, you are no longer walking in love. Do not, by what you eat, destroy the one for whom Christ died.

**16** So do not let what is good for you be spoken of as evil.

**17** For the kingdom of God is not eating and drinking but righteousness and peace and joy in the Holy Spirit.

**18** Whoever serves Christ in this way is pleasing to God and approved by people.

**19** So then let us pursue what makes for peace and for building one another up.

**20** Do not, for the sake of food, tear down the work of God. Everything is indeed clean, but it is wrong for anyone to make another stumble by what he eats.

**21** It is good not to eat meat or drink wine or do anything that makes your brother stumble, or fall, or grow weak.

**22** The faith that you have, keep between yourself and God. Blessed is the one who does not condemn himself in what he approves.

**23** But whoever has doubts is condemned if he eats, because he does not eat from faith; and whatever does not come from faith is sin.

## Notes

- **vv10–11 "the judgment seat of God… every knee will bow to Me, and every tongue will confess to God"** — Isaiah 45:23, where the Hebrew and the Greek both have *every tongue shall swear*; Paul has *will confess*, *exomologēsetai*. In Philippians 2:10–11 he gives the same line to Jesus: *at the name of Jesus every knee should bow… and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord*.

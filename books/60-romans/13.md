# Romans 13

## The Governing Authorities

**1** Let every person be subject to the governing authorities. For there is no authority except from God, and those that exist have been put in place by God.

**2** So whoever opposes the authority resists what God has put in place, and those who resist will bring judgment on themselves.

**3** For rulers are not a terror to good conduct, but to bad. Do you want to have no fear of the one in authority? Then do what is good, and you will receive his praise,

**4** for he is God's servant for your good. But if you do wrong, be afraid, for he does not carry the sword for nothing. He is God's servant, an avenger who brings wrath on the one who does wrong.

**5** So one must be subject, not only because of the wrath but also for the sake of conscience.

**6** This is also why you pay taxes, for the authorities are ministers of God, attending to this very thing.

**7** Pay everyone what you owe them: tax to whom tax is owed, revenue to whom revenue, respect to whom respect, honor to whom honor.

## The Debt of Love

**8** Owe no one anything, except to love one another; for the one who loves another has fulfilled the Law.

**9** For the commandments, "You shall not commit adultery, you shall not murder, you shall not steal, you shall not covet," and any other commandment there is, are summed up in this word: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself."

**10** Love does no wrong to a neighbor; so love is the fulfilling of the Law.

## The Day Is Near

**11** Do this, knowing the time: the hour has come for you to wake from sleep, for our salvation is nearer now than when we first believed.

**12** The night is far gone; the day is near. So let us put off the works of darkness and put on the weapons of light.

**13** Let us walk decently, as in the daytime, not in carousing and drunkenness, not in sexual immorality and debauchery, not in strife and jealousy.

**14** But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh, to gratify its desires.

## Notes

- **vv1–7 "an avenger who brings wrath… Pay everyone what you owe them"** — the words of the verses just before. Believers are told *never take revenge… leave room for the wrath* (12:19); here the ruler is *ekdikos eis orgēn*, **an avenger for wrath**, *God's servant*. What is forbidden to the believer is given, within its bounds, to the one who bears the sword. *Apodote*, **pay**, word for word **give back**, is Jesus' word, *Give back to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's* (Mark 12:17).
- **vv8–10 "summed up in this word: You shall love your neighbor as yourself"** — Leviticus 19:18, the verse that begins *You shall not take revenge… and you shall love your neighbor as yourself*: both halves of Paul's teaching from 12:19 to here stand in that one verse. Jesus named it the second commandment (Mark 12:31). *Anakephalaioutai*, **summed up**, from *kephalē*, **head**: the commandments are gathered under one heading.
- **vv12, 14 "put on the weapons of light… put on the Lord Jesus Christ"** — *hopla*, the **weapons** of 6:13, *weapons of righteousness*. What the believer puts on in the end is not a set of weapons but a person.

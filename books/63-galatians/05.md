# Galatians 5

## Stand Firm in Freedom

**1** For freedom Christ has set us free. Stand firm, then, and do not be held again under a yoke of slavery.

**2** I, Paul, tell you that if you have yourselves circumcised, Christ will be of no benefit to you.

**3** I testify again to every man who has himself circumcised that he is bound to keep the whole Law.

**4** You who would be justified by the Law have been cut off from Christ; you have fallen away from grace.

**5** For through the Spirit, by faith, we eagerly wait for the hope of righteousness.

**6** In Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision counts for anything, but faith working through love.

**7** You were running well. Who cut in on you, to keep you from obeying the truth?

**8** This persuasion does not come from Him who calls you.

**9** A little yeast leavens the whole lump of dough.

**10** I am confident in the Lord about you that you will take no other view. But the one who is troubling you will bear his judgment, whoever he is.

**11** But if I, brothers, am still preaching circumcision, why am I still being persecuted? In that case the stumbling block of the cross has been removed.

**12** I wish those who are unsettling you would go on and emasculate themselves!

**13** For you were called to freedom, brothers; only do not use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but through love be slaves to one another.

**14** For the whole Law is fulfilled in one word: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself."

**15** But if you bite and devour one another, watch out that you are not consumed by one another.

## Walk by the Spirit

**16** I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will not carry out the desire of the flesh.

**17** For the flesh desires against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh; these are opposed to each other, to keep you from doing whatever you want.

**18** But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the Law.

**19** The works of the flesh are plain: sexual immorality, impurity, sensuality,

**20** idolatry, sorcery, hostilities, strife, jealousy, fits of anger, rivalries, dissensions, factions,

**21** envy, drunkenness, carousing, and things like these. I warn you, as I warned you before, that those who practice such things will not inherit the kingdom of God.

**22** But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness,

**23** gentleness, self-control; against such things there is no law.

**24** Those who belong to Christ have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires.

**25** If we live by the Spirit, let us also walk in line with the Spirit.

**26** Let us not become conceited, provoking one another, envying one another.

## Notes

- **v4 "you have been cut off from Christ"** — *katērgēthēte apo Christou*, the verb of Romans 7: the wife *released from the law concerning her husband* when he dies (Romans 7:2), and *we have been released from the Law* (7:6). In Romans the one released from the Law belongs to Christ (7:4); here those who go back to the Law for righteousness are released from Christ.
- **v12 "would go on and emasculate themselves"** — *apokopsontai*, **cut themselves off**, the knife of circumcision carried all the way. The Law shut such a man out: *No one… whose male organ is cut off shall enter the assembly of the LORD* (Deuteronomy 23:1), *apokekommenos… eis ekklēsian Kyriou* in the Greek, the word Paul uses for the church.

# Galatians 2

## Accepted by the Apostles

**1** Then after fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus along also.

**2** I went up because of a revelation, and I set before them the gospel I preach among the Gentiles, privately, before those who were held in high regard, to make sure I was not running, and had not run, in vain.

**3** But not even Titus, who was with me, was compelled to be circumcised, though he was a Greek.

**4** The question arose because of false brothers secretly brought in, who slipped in to spy out the freedom we have in Christ Jesus, so that they might enslave us.

**5** We did not yield to them in submission even for a moment, so that the truth of the gospel might remain with you.

**6** As for those who were held in high regard — whatever they once were makes no difference to me; God shows no partiality — those men added nothing to me.

**7** On the contrary, they saw that I had been entrusted with the gospel to the uncircumcised, just as Peter had been with the gospel to the circumcised,

**8** for He who worked through Peter for his apostleship to the circumcised worked also through me for the Gentiles;

**9** and when James and Cephas and John, who were held to be pillars, recognized the grace that had been given to me, they gave Barnabas and me the right hand of fellowship, so that we would go to the Gentiles and they to the circumcised.

**10** They asked only that we remember the poor, the very thing I was eager to do.

## Paul Opposes Cephas

**11** But when Cephas came to Antioch, I opposed him to his face, because he stood condemned.

**12** Before certain men came from James, he used to eat with the Gentiles; but when they came, he drew back and kept himself apart, fearing those of the circumcision.

**13** The rest of the Jews joined him in playing the hypocrite, so that even Barnabas was carried away by their hypocrisy.

**14** But when I saw that they were not walking straight by the truth of the gospel, I said to Cephas in front of them all, "If you, a Jew, live like a Gentile and not like a Jew, how can you compel the Gentiles to live like Jews?"

## Justified by Faith

**15** We ourselves are Jews by birth and not sinners from among the Gentiles,

**16** yet we know that a person is not justified by works of the Law but through faith in Jesus Christ. So we also have believed in Christ Jesus, in order to be justified by faith in Christ and not by works of the Law, because by works of the Law no flesh will be justified.

**17** But if, in seeking to be justified in Christ, we ourselves were also found to be sinners, is Christ then a minister of sin? By no means!

**18** For if I build up again what I tore down, I show myself to be a transgressor.

**19** For through the Law I died to the Law, so that I might live to God. I have been crucified with Christ.

**20** It is no longer I who live, but Christ who lives in me. The life I now live in the flesh I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave Himself for me.

**21** I do not set aside the grace of God, for if righteousness comes through the Law, then Christ died for nothing.

## Notes

- **v16 "through faith in Jesus Christ… we also have believed in Christ Jesus… by faith in Christ"** — *dia pisteōs Iēsou Christou* and *ek pisteōs Christou*, word for word **through the faith of Jesus Christ** and **by the faith of Christ**: faith *in* Christ, or Christ's own *faithfulness*. Paul says the phrase twice and puts *we have believed in Christ Jesus* between them; it comes back in v20 and 3:22. The last line, *by works of the Law no flesh will be justified*, is Psalm 143:2 in the Greek, *no one living will be justified before You*; the Hebrew has *no one living is righteous before You*.

# Galatians 3

## By Faith, Not by Works of the Law

**1** Foolish Galatians! Who has bewitched you? Before your eyes Jesus Christ was publicly portrayed as crucified.

**2** Let me ask you only this: did you receive the Spirit by works of the Law, or by hearing with faith?

**3** Are you so foolish? Having begun by the Spirit, are you now finishing by the flesh?

**4** Did you suffer so much for nothing — if it really was for nothing?

**5** Does He who supplies the Spirit to you and works mighty works among you do it by works of the Law, or by hearing with faith?

**6** Just as Abraham "believed God, and it was reckoned to him as righteousness,"

**7** know then that it is those of faith who are the sons of Abraham.

**8** The Scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the Gentiles by faith, preached the gospel beforehand to Abraham: "In you all the nations shall be blessed."

**9** So those who are of faith are blessed along with Abraham, the man of faith.

## The Curse of the Law

**10** For all who depend on works of the Law are under a curse, for it is written: "Cursed is everyone who does not continue in all the things written in the book of the Law, to do them."

**11** It is plain that no one is justified before God by the Law, for "The righteous will live by faith."

**12** But the Law is not of faith; rather, "The person who does them shall live by them."

**13** Christ redeemed us from the curse of the Law by becoming a curse for us, for it is written: "Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree,"

**14** so that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles, so that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.

## The Law and the Promise

**15** Brothers, let me give an example from everyday life: even a man's covenant, once it has been ratified, no one sets aside or adds to.

**16** The promises were spoken to Abraham and to his seed. It does not say "and to seeds," as of many, but as of one, "and to your seed," who is Christ.

**17** This is what I mean: the Law, which came four hundred and thirty years afterward, does not annul a covenant previously ratified by God, so as to make the promise void.

**18** For if the inheritance comes from the Law, it no longer comes from the promise; but God gave it freely to Abraham through a promise.

**19** Why then the Law? It was added because of transgressions, until the seed should come to whom the promise had been made, and it was ordained by angels through a mediator.

**20** Now a mediator does not stand for one party only, but God is one.

**21** Is the Law then against the promises of God? By no means! For if a law had been given that could give life, then righteousness would indeed be by the Law.

**22** But the Scripture has shut up everything under sin, so that the promise by faith in Jesus Christ might be given to those who believe.

**23** Before faith came, we were held in custody under the Law, shut up until the faith that was to come would be revealed.

**24** So the Law was our guardian until Christ came, so that we might be justified by faith.

**25** But now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian.

## One in Christ

**26** For in Christ Jesus you are all sons of God, through faith.

**27** As many of you as were baptized into Christ have put on Christ.

**28** There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is no male and female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus.

**29** If you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham's seed, heirs according to the promise.

## Notes

- **v13 "becoming a curse for us… Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree"** — Deuteronomy 21:23, of an executed man's body left on a tree: *one who is hanged is a curse of God*, in the Greek *cursed by God is everyone who is hanged on a tree*. Paul quotes it without *by God*, and with the word *Cursed*, *epikataratos*, of the verse he has just quoted (v10), so that both sayings open alike.
- **v16 "and to your seed," who is Christ** — the promise *to you and to your seed* (Genesis 13:15; 17:8; 24:7). *Zera* in Hebrew and *sperma* in Greek are singular and usually mean a whole line of descendants. Paul reads the singular as one person, Christ, and in v29 widens it again to all who belong to Him: *you are Abraham's seed*.
- **vv19–20 "ordained by angels through a mediator… God is one"** — the angels are in the Greek of Moses' blessing, *at His right hand angels with Him*, where the Hebrew has *a fiery law for them* (Deuteronomy 33:2); Stephen says the same (Acts 7:53). The mediator is Moses: the laws the LORD gave *on Mount Sinai, through Moses* (Leviticus 26:46), *en cheiri Mōysē*, Paul's phrase. *God is one* is the Shema, *the LORD is one* (Deuteronomy 6:4). A mediator stands between two parties; the promise God gave Abraham by Himself (v18).
- **vv24–25 "our guardian until Christ came"** — *paidagōgos*, **child-leader**, the guardian Paul set against a father in 1 Corinthians 4:15, like the *trustees and managers* who have charge of the heir *until the date set by his father* (4:2). *Eis Christon* can mean **until Christ** or **to lead us to Christ**. The verses around it measure time: *before faith came… until the faith that was to come… now that faith has come*.
- **v28 "there is no male and female"** — *ouk eni arsen kai thēly*. The first two pairs are joined by *nor*; the third by *and*, in the words of creation, *male and female He created them* (Genesis 1:27), *arsen kai thēly* in the Greek. What God made two at the beginning are, with Jew and Greek, slave and free, *all one in Christ Jesus*.

# 1 Kings 16

## Jehu's Word against Baasha

**1** The word of the LORD came to Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha, saying,

**2** "Because I raised you up from the dust and made you leader over My people Israel, and you have walked in the way of Jeroboam and have made My people Israel sin, provoking Me to anger with their sins,

**3** I am about to sweep away Baasha and his house, and I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat.

**4** Anyone belonging to Baasha who dies in the city the dogs shall eat, and anyone of his who dies in the open country the birds of the heavens shall eat."

**5** The rest of the acts of Baasha, what he did and his might, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**6** Baasha lay down with his fathers and was buried in Tirzah, and Elah his son reigned in his place.

**7** Moreover, the word of the LORD came by the hand of the prophet Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha and his house, both because of all the evil that he did in the eyes of the LORD, provoking Him to anger with the work of his hands, in being like the house of Jeroboam, and also because he struck it down.

## Elah and Zimri

**8** In the twenty-sixth year of Asa king of Judah, Elah son of Baasha began to reign over Israel in Tirzah, and he reigned two years.

**9** His servant Zimri, commander of half his chariots, conspired against him. When he was in Tirzah, drinking himself drunk in the house of Arza, who was over the household in Tirzah,

**10** Zimri came in and struck him down and killed him, in the twenty-seventh year of Asa king of Judah, and reigned in his place.

**11** And it came to pass, when he began to reign, as soon as he sat on his throne, that he struck down all the house of Baasha. He did not leave him a single male, of his kinsmen or of his friends.

**12** So Zimri destroyed all the house of Baasha, according to the word of the LORD that He spoke against Baasha by Jehu the prophet,

**13** for all the sins of Baasha and the sins of Elah his son, which they sinned and which they made Israel to sin, provoking the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger with their empty things.

**14** The rest of the acts of Elah and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**15** In the twenty-seventh year of Asa king of Judah, Zimri reigned seven days in Tirzah. The people were camped against Gibbethon, which belonged to the Philistines,

**16** and the people who were camped there heard it said, "Zimri has conspired, and he has also struck down the king." So all Israel made Omri, the commander of the army, king over Israel that day in the camp.

**17** Omri went up from Gibbethon, and all Israel with him, and they besieged Tirzah.

**18** And it came to pass, when Zimri saw that the city was taken, that he went into the citadel of the king's house and burned the king's house over himself with fire, and he died,

**19** because of the sins that he sinned, doing evil in the eyes of the LORD, walking in the way of Jeroboam and in his sin that he committed, making Israel to sin.

**20** The rest of the acts of Zimri and the conspiracy that he carried out, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

## Omri

**21** Then the people of Israel were divided into two parts: half of the people followed Tibni son of Ginath, to make him king, and half followed Omri.

**22** But the people who followed Omri prevailed over the people who followed Tibni son of Ginath. So Tibni died, and Omri became king.

**23** In the thirty-first year of Asa king of Judah, Omri began to reign over Israel, and he reigned twelve years; six years he reigned in Tirzah.

**24** He bought the hill of Samaria from Shemer for two talents of silver. He built on the hill and called the name of the city that he built Samaria, after the name of Shemer, the owner of the hill.

**25** Omri did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, and did more evil than all who were before him.

**26** He walked in all the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat and in his sins with which he made Israel to sin, provoking the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger with their empty things.

**27** The rest of the acts of Omri that he did, and the might that he showed, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

**28** Omri lay down with his fathers and was buried in Samaria, and Ahab his son reigned in his place.

## Ahab

**29** Ahab son of Omri began to reign over Israel in the thirty-eighth year of Asa king of Judah, and Ahab son of Omri reigned over Israel in Samaria twenty-two years.

**30** Ahab son of Omri did evil in the eyes of the LORD, more than all who were before him.

**31** As if it had been a light thing for him to walk in the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, he took as his wife Jezebel the daughter of Ethbaal king of the Sidonians, and went and served Baal and bowed down to him.

**32** He set up an altar for Baal in the house of Baal that he built in Samaria.

**33** Ahab also made the Asherah. Ahab did more to provoke the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger than all the kings of Israel who were before him.

**34** In his days Hiel the Bethelite built Jericho. He laid its foundation at the cost of Abiram his firstborn, and set up its gates at the cost of Segub his youngest, according to the word of the LORD that He spoke by Joshua son of Nun.

## Notes

- **v2 "Because I raised you up from the dust"** — as Hannah sang, *He raises the poor from the dust* (1 Samuel 2:8), and as the LORD had said to Jeroboam, *I raised you up from among the people* (14:7).
- **v7 "and also because he struck it down"** — Baasha destroyed Jeroboam's house as Ahijah had foretold (15:29), and is judged both for walking in Jeroboam's ways and for the killing. The verse gives both reasons.
- **v9 "drinking himself drunk in the house of Arza"** — the king is drunk in his steward's house while the army is at Gibbethon (v15).
- **v11 "a single male, of his kinsmen or of his friends"** — *mashtin be-qir* (14:10). *Go'alav*, **his redeemers**, the kinsmen who would have avenged his blood (Numbers 35:19).
- **vv13, 26 "with their empty things"** — *be-havleihem*, *hevel*, **breath**, **vapor**: the idols are nothing. The word of the Song of Moses, *they provoked Me with their empty things* (Deuteronomy 32:21).
- **vv15–18 "Zimri reigned seven days… burned the king's house over himself"** — the shortest reign in the book.
- **vv23, 29 "In the thirty-first year of Asa… twelve years… Ahab… in the thirty-eighth year of Asa"** — thirty-one and twelve would pass the thirty-eighth year. The dates fit if Omri's twelve years are counted from his acclamation in the camp in Asa's twenty-seventh year (vv15–16), with part-years counted at each end (27 to 38 is twelve years counted that way), and the thirty-first year marks when he reigned alone, after Tibni died (v22). The Greek calls Omri *Zambri* and dates Ahab to *the second year of Jehoshaphat*, on a different reckoning. The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **v24 "He bought the hill of Samaria from Shemer… Samaria"** — *Shomeron*, named after *Shemer*. From here to the end of the northern kingdom Samaria is its capital; earlier mentions, as in 13:32, use the name it came to have.
- **v31 "Jezebel the daughter of Ethbaal king of the Sidonians"** — *Etba'al*, **with Baal**. The Sidonian marriage brings the worship of Baal into Israel's capital (vv32–33).
- **v34 "at the cost of Abiram his firstborn… at the cost of Segub his youngest"** — the curse Joshua laid on Jericho: *At the cost of his firstborn he shall lay its foundation, and at the cost of his youngest he shall set up its gates* (Joshua 6:26). The written text has *Segib*, the read form *Segub*.

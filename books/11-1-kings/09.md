# 1 Kings 9

## The Second Appearance

**1** And it came to pass, when Solomon had finished building the house of the LORD and the king's house and all that Solomon desired to do,

**2** that the LORD appeared to Solomon a second time, as He had appeared to him at Gibeon.

**3** The LORD said to him, "I have heard your prayer and your plea that you made before Me. I have consecrated this house that you have built, by putting My name there forever. My eyes and My heart will be there for all time.

**4** As for you, if you walk before Me as David your father walked, in integrity of heart and in uprightness, doing all that I have commanded you, and keep My statutes and My judgments,

**5** then I will establish the throne of your kingdom over Israel forever, as I promised David your father, saying, 'You shall not lack a man on the throne of Israel.'

**6** But if you or your sons turn back from following Me, and do not keep My commandments and My statutes that I have set before you, but go and serve other gods and bow down to them,

**7** then I will cut off Israel from the face of the land that I have given them, and the house that I have consecrated for My name I will cast out of My sight, and Israel will become a proverb and a byword among all the peoples.

**8** As for this house, which is exalted, everyone who passes by it will be appalled and will hiss, and they will say, 'Why has the LORD done this to this land and to this house?'

**9** Then they will say, 'Because they forsook the LORD their God, who brought their fathers out of the land of Egypt, and took hold of other gods and bowed down to them and served them. That is why the LORD has brought all this evil on them.'"

## Hiram and the Cities of Galilee

**10** And it came to pass at the end of twenty years, in which Solomon had built the two houses, the house of the LORD and the king's house,

**11** Hiram king of Tyre having supplied Solomon with cedar timber and cypress timber and gold, as much as he desired, that King Solomon gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee.

**12** Hiram came out from Tyre to see the cities that Solomon had given him, and they did not please him.

**13** He said, "What are these cities that you have given me, my brother?" So they are called the land of Cabul to this day.

**14** Hiram had sent to the king a hundred and twenty talents of gold.

## Solomon's Forced Labor and Buildings

**15** This is the account of the forced labor that King Solomon raised to build the house of the LORD and his own house, the Millo and the wall of Jerusalem, and Hazor, Megiddo and Gezer.

**16** (Pharaoh king of Egypt had gone up and captured Gezer and burned it with fire, and had killed the Canaanites who lived in the city, and had given it as a dowry to his daughter, Solomon's wife.)

**17** So Solomon rebuilt Gezer and Lower Beth-horon,

**18** and Baalath and Tadmor in the wilderness, in the land,

**19** and all the store cities that Solomon had, the cities for his chariots and the cities for his horsemen, and whatever Solomon desired to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon and in all the land of his dominion.

**20** All the people who were left of the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Hivites and the Jebusites, who were not of the sons of Israel —

**21** their sons who were left after them in the land, whom the sons of Israel were not able to devote to destruction — these Solomon raised as forced labor of slaves, to this day.

**22** But of the sons of Israel Solomon made no slaves. They were the men of war, his servants, his officials, his officers, and the commanders of his chariots and his horsemen.

**23** These were the chief officers who were over Solomon's work, five hundred and fifty, who had charge of the people doing the work.

**24** But Pharaoh's daughter went up from the City of David to her own house that Solomon had built for her. Then he built the Millo.

**25** Three times a year Solomon offered burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar that he built to the LORD, burning incense with them before the LORD. So he completed the house.

## The Fleet

**26** King Solomon built a fleet of ships at Ezion-geber, which is near Eloth on the shore of the Sea of Reeds, in the land of Edom.

**27** Hiram sent with the fleet his servants, seamen who knew the sea, together with the servants of Solomon.

**28** They went to Ophir and brought from there four hundred and twenty talents of gold, and brought it to King Solomon.

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "a second time, as He had appeared to him at Gibeon… My eyes and My heart will be there"** — the first appearance gave gifts (3:5–14); this one answers the prayer of 8:29, *that Your eyes may be open toward this house*.
- **vv6–7 "if you or your sons turn back… Israel will become a proverb and a byword"** — the words of the covenant curses: *You will become a horror, a proverb and a byword among all the peoples* (Deuteronomy 28:37).
- **v8 "this house, which is exalted — everyone who passes by it will be appalled"** — *elyon*, **high**. The Greek has the same, and Chronicles reads *this house, which was exalted* (2 Chronicles 7:21). *Will hiss*: *sharaq*, the whistle of one who sees a ruin.
- **v13 "the land of Cabul"** — Cabul is a town on the border of Asher (Joshua 19:27). *My brother* is how one king addresses another who is bound to him, as Ahab will say of Ben-hadad, *He is my brother* (20:32). Chronicles tells of cities that *Huram had given to Solomon*, which Solomon rebuilt and settled (2 Chronicles 8:2).
- **vv15–25 in the Greek** — the Greek does not have these verses at this point; its chapter 9 goes from v14 to v26.
- **v16 "had given it as a dowry to his daughter"** — *shilluchim*, **parting gifts**, the word for what a father sends with a bride.
- **v18 "Tadmor in the wilderness"** — the written text has *Tamar*, the read form *Tadmor*; the text follows the read form. Chronicles also has *Tadmor in the wilderness* (2 Chronicles 8:4).
- **vv20–22 "these Solomon raised as forced labor of slaves… But of the sons of Israel Solomon made no slaves"** — the Canaanites of Gezer had long been forced labor (Joshua 16:10). The forced labor of Israelites in 5:13–14 was by monthly turns; the text here distinguishes it from slavery.
- **v25 "Three times a year"** — the three pilgrim feasts, when *all your males shall appear before the face of the LORD your God* (Deuteronomy 16:16).
- **vv26, 28 "the Sea of Reeds… four hundred and twenty talents of gold"** — *Yam Suf*, the sea Israel crossed out of Egypt, here its gulf at Eloth. Chronicles has *four hundred and fifty talents* (2 Chronicles 8:18), and the Greek *a hundred and twenty*. The text keeps the Hebrew.

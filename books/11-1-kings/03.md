# 1 Kings 3

## The Dream at Gibeon

**1** Solomon made a marriage alliance with Pharaoh king of Egypt. He took Pharaoh's daughter and brought her into the City of David until he had finished building his own house and the house of the LORD and the wall around Jerusalem.

**2** The people, however, were sacrificing at the high places, because no house had yet been built for the name of the LORD until those days.

**3** Solomon loved the LORD, walking in the statutes of David his father; only, he sacrificed and burned incense at the high places.

**4** The king went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, for that was the great high place. Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings on that altar.

**5** At Gibeon the LORD appeared to Solomon in a dream by night, and God said, "Ask what I shall give you."

**6** Solomon said, "You have shown great loyal love to Your servant David my father, because he walked before You in faithfulness, in righteousness and in uprightness of heart toward You. You have kept for him this great loyal love and have given him a son to sit on his throne, as it is today.

**7** And now, O LORD my God, You have made Your servant king in place of David my father, but I am only a little child; I do not know how to go out or come in.

**8** Your servant is in the middle of Your people whom You have chosen, a great people, too many to be numbered or counted.

**9** So give Your servant a listening heart to judge Your people, to discern between good and evil. For who is able to judge this great people of Yours?"

**10** It pleased the Lord that Solomon had asked this.

**11** God said to him, "Because you have asked this, and have not asked for yourself long life or riches, or asked for the life of your enemies, but have asked for yourself understanding to discern justice,

**12** I now do according to your word. I give you a wise and discerning heart, so that there has been no one like you before you, and none like you shall arise after you.

**13** I also give you what you have not asked, both riches and honor, so that no king shall be like you all your days.

**14** If you walk in My ways, keeping My statutes and My commandments, as David your father walked, then I will lengthen your days."

**15** Solomon awoke, and it was a dream. He came to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings, and made a feast for all his servants.

## The Judgment of Solomon

**16** Then two women who were prostitutes came to the king and stood before him.

**17** One woman said, "Please, my lord, this woman and I live in the same house, and I gave birth to a child while she was in the house.

**18** And it came to pass on the third day after I gave birth that this woman also gave birth. We were together; there was no one else with us in the house, only the two of us.

**19** This woman's son died in the night, because she lay on him.

**20** She got up in the middle of the night and took my son from beside me while your servant was asleep, and laid him at her breast, and her dead son she laid at my breast.

**21** When I got up in the morning to nurse my son, he was dead. But when I looked at him closely in the morning, he was not my son whom I had borne."

**22** But the other woman said, "No, the living one is my son, and the dead one is your son." The first said, "No, the dead one is your son, and the living one is my son." So they argued before the king.

**23** Then the king said, "This one says, 'This is my son who is alive, and your son is dead,' and that one says, 'No, your son is dead, and my son is the living one.'"

**24** The king said, "Bring me a sword." So they brought a sword before the king.

**25** The king said, "Divide the living child in two, and give half to the one and half to the other."

**26** Then the woman whose son was alive said to the king, because her compassion grew hot for her son, "Please, my lord, give her the living child, and by no means put him to death!" But the other said, "He shall be neither mine nor yours; divide him."

**27** Then the king answered, "Give the living child to the first woman, and by no means put him to death. She is his mother."

**28** All Israel heard of the judgment the king had rendered, and they stood in awe of the king, because they saw that the wisdom of God was in him to do justice.

## Notes

- **v1 "He took Pharaoh's daughter"** — the law of the king says, *He shall not multiply wives for himself, so that his heart does not turn aside* (Deuteronomy 17:17). The book will return to Pharaoh's daughter among the *many foreign women* who turned Solomon's heart (11:1–4).
- **vv2–3 "however… only, he sacrificed… at the high places"** — *raq*, twice: the people's sacrifice and the king's are both qualified. *Bamot*, **high places**, local altars, most often on hills.
- **v7 "I am only a little child; I do not know how to go out or come in"** — *lo eda tset va-vo*, the phrase for leading a people: Moses asked for a man *who will go out before them and come in before them* (Numbers 27:17), and said at the end, *I can no longer go out and come in* (Deuteronomy 31:2).
- **v9 "a listening heart… to discern between good and evil"** — *lev shomea*, **a hearing heart**, from *shama*, the word of *Shema Yisra'el*. *To discern good and evil* is what the woman of Tekoa said of David, *like the messenger of God, to discern good and evil* (2 Samuel 14:17).
- **vv11–13 "not asked for yourself long life or riches… I also give you… both riches and honor"** — the law warns the king against piling up silver and gold (Deuteronomy 17:17); here God gives riches unasked.
- **v12 "a wise and discerning heart"** — *lev chakham ve-navon*, what Joseph told Pharaoh to find, *a man discerning and wise* (Genesis 41:33), and what Pharaoh then said of Joseph, *there is none discerning and wise like you* (41:39).
- **v14 "then I will lengthen your days"** — the promise at the end of the law of the king, *so that he may prolong his days in his kingdom* (Deuteronomy 17:20). This promise, unlike the gifts of vv12–13, has a condition.
- **v15 "Solomon awoke, and it was a dream"** — *va-yiqats… ve-hinneh chalom*, the words used of Pharaoh, *Pharaoh awoke, and it was a dream* (Genesis 41:7).
- **v16 "two women who were prostitutes"** — *zonot*. There was *no one else with us in the house* (v18): no witness, only the two women's words.
- **v26 "her compassion grew hot for her son"** — *nikhmeru rachamehah*. *Rachamim*, **compassion**, is from *rechem*, **womb**. The same words are used of Joseph when he saw Benjamin, *his compassion grew hot toward his brother* (Genesis 43:30).

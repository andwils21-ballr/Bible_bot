# 1 Kings 17

## Elijah at the Brook Cherith

**1** Elijah the Tishbite, of the residents of Gilead, said to Ahab, "As the LORD, the God of Israel, lives, before whom I stand, there shall be neither dew nor rain these years, except by my word."

**2** Then the word of the LORD came to him, saying,

**3** "Go from here and turn eastward, and hide yourself by the brook Cherith, which is east of the Jordan.

**4** You shall drink from the brook, and I have commanded the ravens to feed you there."

**5** So he went and did according to the word of the LORD. He went and lived by the brook Cherith, which is east of the Jordan.

**6** The ravens brought him bread and meat in the morning, and bread and meat in the evening, and he drank from the brook.

**7** And it came to pass after a while that the brook dried up, because there was no rain in the land.

## The Widow of Zarephath

**8** Then the word of the LORD came to him, saying,

**9** "Arise, go to Zarephath, which belongs to Sidon, and live there. I have commanded a widow there to feed you."

**10** So he arose and went to Zarephath. When he came to the gate of the city, a widow was there gathering sticks. He called to her and said, "Please bring me a little water in a vessel, so that I may drink."

**11** As she was going to bring it, he called to her and said, "Please bring me a morsel of bread in your hand."

**12** She said, "As the LORD your God lives, I have nothing baked, only a handful of flour in a jar and a little oil in a jug. I am gathering a couple of sticks, so that I may go in and prepare it for myself and my son, that we may eat it and die."

**13** Elijah said to her, "Do not be afraid. Go and do as you have said. But first make me a little cake of it and bring it out to me, and afterward make some for yourself and your son.

**14** For thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: 'The jar of flour shall not be spent, and the jug of oil shall not run short, until the day that the LORD sends rain on the face of the land.'"

**15** She went and did according to the word of Elijah. He and she and her household ate for many days.

**16** The jar of flour was not spent, and the jug of oil did not run short, according to the word of the LORD that He spoke by Elijah.

## The Widow's Son

**17** And it came to pass after these things that the son of the woman, the mistress of the house, fell sick. His illness was so severe that there was no breath left in him.

**18** She said to Elijah, "What have I to do with you, man of God? Have you come to me to bring my sin to remembrance and to put my son to death?"

**19** He said to her, "Give me your son." He took him from her bosom and carried him up to the upper room where he was staying, and laid him on his own bed.

**20** He cried out to the LORD and said, "O LORD my God, have You brought evil even on the widow with whom I live as a guest, by putting her son to death?"

**21** Then he stretched himself out on the child three times, and cried out to the LORD and said, "O LORD my God, please let this child's life come back into him."

**22** The LORD listened to the voice of Elijah, and the life of the child came back into him, and he lived.

**23** Elijah took the child and brought him down from the upper room into the house and gave him to his mother. Elijah said, "See, your son is alive."

**24** The woman said to Elijah, "Now I know that you are a man of God, and that the word of the LORD in your mouth is truth."

## Notes

- **v1 "Elijah the Tishbite, of the residents of Gilead"** — *Eliyyahu*, **my God is YHWH**, a name that is itself the argument of chapter 18. *Mi-toshavei Gil'ad*, **of the residents**; the Greek reads *from Thesbon of Gilead*, taking the word as a town name. *Before whom I stand*: the stance of a servant before his master.
- **v1 "there shall be neither dew nor rain"** — the drought is announced after Ahab has built a house for Baal in Samaria (16:32). The New Testament gives its length as *three years and six months* (Luke 4:25; James 5:17).
- **vv4–6 "I have commanded the ravens to feed you"** — *ha-orvim*. The raven is among the birds the law calls unclean (Leviticus 11:15). The same word, *I have commanded*, is used of the widow in v9.
- **v9 "Zarephath, which belongs to Sidon"** — the country of Jezebel's father, *king of the Sidonians* (16:31). Jesus pointed to this widow: Elijah *was sent to none of them except to Zarephath in the land of Sidon, to a widow* (Luke 4:26).
- **v12 "As the LORD your God lives"** — the Sidonian widow swears by Elijah's God, *your God*, not her own.
- **vv14–16 "The jar of flour shall not be spent"** — the promise is repeated as fulfilled, word for word, in v16.
- **v18 "What have I to do with you… to bring my sin to remembrance"** — *mah li va-lakh*, the words David used to refuse the sons of Zeruiah (2 Samuel 16:10). She reads her son's death as punishment brought by the prophet's presence.
- **vv20–21 "the widow with whom I live as a guest… he stretched himself out on the child three times"** — *mitgorer*, from *gur*, **to live as a guest**: the prophet is a guest in a foreign widow's house. *Va-yitmoded*, found only here; Elisha will lie on the Shunammite's son, *his mouth on his mouth, his eyes on his eyes* (2 Kings 4:34). The Greek has *he breathed on the child three times*.
- **vv22, 24 "and he lived… the word of the LORD in your mouth is truth"** — the first account in the Hebrew Bible of a dead person brought back to life. The widow's words close the chapter: what Elijah says, happens.

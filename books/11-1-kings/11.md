# 1 Kings 11

## Solomon's Wives

**1** King Solomon loved many foreign women, besides the daughter of Pharaoh: Moabite, Ammonite, Edomite, Sidonian and Hittite women,

**2** from the nations of which the LORD had said to the sons of Israel, "You shall not go in among them, and they shall not come in among you, for surely they will turn your hearts away after their gods." Solomon clung to these in love.

**3** He had seven hundred wives who were princesses, and three hundred concubines, and his wives turned his heart away.

**4** And it came to pass, when Solomon was old, that his wives turned his heart away after other gods, and his heart was not whole with the LORD his God, as the heart of David his father had been.

**5** Solomon went after Ashtoreth the goddess of the Sidonians, and after Milcom the loathsome thing of the Ammonites.

**6** So Solomon did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD and did not follow the LORD fully, as David his father had done.

**7** Then Solomon built a high place for Chemosh the loathsome thing of Moab, on the mountain east of Jerusalem, and for Molech the loathsome thing of the sons of Ammon.

**8** He did the same for all his foreign wives, who burned incense and sacrificed to their gods.

**9** The LORD was angry with Solomon, because his heart had turned away from the LORD, the God of Israel, who had appeared to him twice

**10** and had commanded him concerning this very thing, that he should not go after other gods. But he did not keep what the LORD commanded.

**11** So the LORD said to Solomon, "Because this has been your way, and you have not kept My covenant and My statutes that I commanded you, I will surely tear the kingdom from you and give it to your servant.

**12** Yet I will not do it in your days, for the sake of David your father; I will tear it out of the hand of your son.

**13** However, I will not tear away all the kingdom; I will give one tribe to your son for the sake of David My servant and for the sake of Jerusalem, which I have chosen."

## Solomon's Adversaries

**14** Then the LORD raised up an adversary against Solomon, Hadad the Edomite; he was of the royal seed in Edom.

**15** For when David was in Edom, and Joab the commander of the army went up to bury the slain, he struck down every male in Edom.

**16** (For Joab and all Israel stayed there six months, until he had cut off every male in Edom.)

**17** But Hadad fled to Egypt, he and certain Edomites of his father's servants with him, while Hadad was still a little boy.

**18** They set out from Midian and came to Paran, and took men with them from Paran and came to Egypt, to Pharaoh king of Egypt, who gave him a house and assigned him food and gave him land.

**19** Hadad found great favor in the eyes of Pharaoh, so that he gave him as his wife the sister of his own wife, the sister of Tahpenes the queen.

**20** The sister of Tahpenes bore him Genubath his son, whom Tahpenes weaned in Pharaoh's house. Genubath was in Pharaoh's house among the sons of Pharaoh.

**21** When Hadad heard in Egypt that David had lain down with his fathers and that Joab the commander of the army was dead, Hadad said to Pharaoh, "Let me go, so that I may go to my own land."

**22** Pharaoh said to him, "What have you lacked with me, that you are now seeking to go to your own land?" He said, "Nothing; only let me go."

**23** God also raised up another adversary against him, Rezon son of Eliada, who had fled from his master Hadadezer king of Zobah.

**24** He gathered men around him and became leader of a raiding band, after David killed them. They went to Damascus and lived there, and they made him king in Damascus.

**25** He was an adversary to Israel all the days of Solomon, besides the harm that Hadad did. He loathed Israel and reigned over Aram.

## Jeroboam and Ahijah

**26** Jeroboam son of Nebat, an Ephraimite of Zeredah, a servant of Solomon, whose mother's name was Zeruah, a widow, also lifted up his hand against the king.

**27** This was the reason he lifted up his hand against the king: Solomon was building the Millo and closing up the breach of the City of David his father.

**28** The man Jeroboam was a man of worth. When Solomon saw that the young man was capable in his work, he put him in charge of all the forced labor of the house of Joseph.

**29** And it came to pass at that time, when Jeroboam went out of Jerusalem, that the prophet Ahijah the Shilonite found him on the road. Ahijah had clothed himself with a new cloak, and the two of them were alone in the open country.

**30** Ahijah took hold of the new cloak that was on him and tore it into twelve pieces.

**31** He said to Jeroboam, "Take for yourself ten pieces, for thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: 'I am about to tear the kingdom out of the hand of Solomon and give you ten tribes.

**32** But he shall have one tribe, for the sake of My servant David and for the sake of Jerusalem, the city that I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel,

**33** because they have forsaken Me and bowed down to Ashtoreth the goddess of the Sidonians, Chemosh the god of Moab and Milcom the god of the sons of Ammon, and have not walked in My ways, doing what is right in My eyes and keeping My statutes and My judgments, as David his father did.

**34** Yet I will not take the whole kingdom out of his hand; I will make him ruler all the days of his life, for the sake of David My servant whom I chose, who kept My commandments and My statutes.

**35** But I will take the kingdom out of his son's hand and give it to you, ten tribes.

**36** To his son I will give one tribe, so that David My servant may always have a lamp before Me in Jerusalem, the city where I have chosen to put My name.

**37** I will take you, and you shall reign over all that your soul desires, and you shall be king over Israel.

**38** And it shall come to pass, if you listen to all that I command you, and walk in My ways and do what is right in My eyes, keeping My statutes and My commandments as David My servant did, that I will be with you and build you a sure house, as I built for David, and I will give Israel to you.

**39** I will humble the seed of David because of this, but not forever.'"

**40** Solomon sought to put Jeroboam to death, but Jeroboam got up and fled to Egypt, to Shishak king of Egypt, and he stayed in Egypt until the death of Solomon.

## The Death of Solomon

**41** The rest of the acts of Solomon, all that he did and his wisdom, are they not written in the book of the acts of Solomon?

**42** The time that Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel was forty years.

**43** Solomon lay down with his fathers and was buried in the city of David his father, and Rehoboam his son reigned in his place.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "many foreign women… You shall not go in among them… Solomon clung to these in love"** — the law: *You shall not intermarry with them… for he will turn your son away from following Me* (Deuteronomy 7:3–4), and the law of the king, *He shall not multiply wives for himself, so that his heart does not turn aside* (Deuteronomy 17:17). *Davaq*, **clung**, is the word of a man who *clings to his woman* (Genesis 2:24).
- **vv4, 6 "his heart was not whole with the LORD… did not follow the LORD fully"** — *lo hayah levavo shalem*, the wholeness Solomon asked of the people: *Let your heart therefore be whole with the LORD our God* (8:61). *Shalem*, **whole**, sounds in *Shelomoh*.
- **vv5, 7 "Milcom… Molech… the loathsome thing"** — *shiqquts*. The god of the Ammonites is *Milcom* in v5 and v33, and *Molech* in v7. The Greek has *their king* in v7.
- **v11 "I will surely tear the kingdom from you"** — *qaroa eqra*. Samuel said to Saul when his robe tore, *The LORD has torn the kingdom of Israel from you today* (1 Samuel 15:28). The same verb returns in vv30–31, when Ahijah tears his cloak.
- **vv14, 23, 25 "an adversary… another adversary"** — *satan*, three times. Early in the reign Solomon said, *there is no adversary* (5:4). Hadad's escape to Egypt as a boy follows David's war in Edom (2 Samuel 8:13–14).
- **v28 "a man of worth… the forced labor of the house of Joseph"** — *gibbor chayil*, as Kish is called (1 Samuel 9:1). *Sevel*, the labor gangs of Ephraim and Manasseh, Jeroboam's own tribes.
- **vv29–30 "a new cloak… tore it into twelve pieces"** — *salmah chadashah*. *Salmah*, **cloak**, is written with the same letters as *Shelomoh*, Solomon (שלמה). The Hebrew says only that *he* had clothed himself with it; the Greek names Ahijah.
- **vv31–32 "ten pieces… one tribe"** — twelve pieces: ten for Jeroboam and one for David's line; the text does not say what becomes of the twelfth. The Greek has *two scepters* for David's line in v32.
- **v36 "so that David My servant may always have a lamp before Me"** — *nir*, as David's men called him *the lamp of Israel* (2 Samuel 21:17).
- **v38 "build you a sure house, as I built for David"** — *bayit ne'eman*, the promise to the faithful priest (1 Samuel 2:35) and to David (2 Samuel 7:16), now offered to Jeroboam on condition.

# 1 Kings 2

## David's Charge to Solomon

**1** When David's time to die drew near, he charged Solomon his son, saying,

**2** "I am going the way of all the earth. Be strong, and show yourself a man.

**3** Keep the charge of the LORD your God, walking in His ways and keeping His statutes, His commandments, His judgments and His testimonies, as it is written in the law of Moses, so that you may prosper in all that you do and wherever you turn,

**4** so that the LORD may establish His word that He spoke concerning me: 'If your sons take heed to their way, to walk before Me in faithfulness with all their heart and with all their soul, you shall not lack a man on the throne of Israel.'

**5** Moreover, you know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me, what he did to the two commanders of the armies of Israel, to Abner son of Ner and to Amasa son of Jether, whom he killed. He shed the blood of war in peace, and put the blood of war on the belt around his waist and on the sandals on his feet.

**6** Act according to your wisdom, and do not let his gray head go down to Sheol in peace.

**7** But show loyal love to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite, and let them be among those who eat at your table, for so they came near to me when I fled from Absalom your brother.

**8** There is also with you Shimei son of Gera, the Benjaminite from Bahurim, who cursed me with a grievous curse on the day I went to Mahanaim. But he came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the LORD, saying, 'I will not put you to death with the sword.'

**9** Now therefore do not hold him guiltless, for you are a wise man, and you will know what you ought to do to him, and you shall bring his gray head down to Sheol with blood."

**10** Then David lay down with his fathers and was buried in the City of David.

**11** The days that David reigned over Israel were forty years: he reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.

**12** So Solomon sat on the throne of David his father, and his kingdom was firmly established.

## Adonijah's Request

**13** Then Adonijah son of Haggith came to Bathsheba the mother of Solomon. She said, "Do you come in peace?" He said, "In peace."

**14** Then he said, "I have something to say to you." She said, "Speak."

**15** He said, "You know that the kingdom was mine, and all Israel set their faces toward me to be king. But the kingdom has turned about and become my brother's, for it was his from the LORD.

**16** Now I have one request to make of you; do not turn me away." She said to him, "Speak."

**17** He said, "Please speak to King Solomon — he will not turn you away — that he may give me Abishag the Shunammite as my wife."

**18** Bathsheba said, "Very well; I will speak to the king for you."

**19** So Bathsheba went to King Solomon to speak to him for Adonijah. The king rose to meet her and bowed down to her. Then he sat down on his throne and had a throne set for the king's mother, and she sat at his right hand.

**20** Then she said, "I have one small request to make of you; do not turn me away." The king said to her, "Make your request, my mother, for I will not turn you away."

**21** She said, "Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to Adonijah your brother as his wife."

**22** King Solomon answered his mother, "Why do you ask Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? Ask for him the kingdom as well, for he is my older brother — for him, and for Abiathar the priest, and for Joab son of Zeruiah!"

**23** Then King Solomon swore by the LORD, saying, "May God do so to me and more also, if Adonijah has not spoken this word at the cost of his own life.

**24** So now, as the LORD lives, who has established me and set me on the throne of David my father, and who has made me a house as He promised, Adonijah shall be put to death today."

**25** So King Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he struck him down, and he died.

## Abiathar and Joab

**26** To Abiathar the priest the king said, "Go to Anathoth, to your fields, for you deserve to die. But I will not put you to death today, because you carried the ark of the Lord GOD before David my father, and because you shared in all that my father suffered."

**27** So Solomon drove Abiathar out from being priest to the LORD, to fulfill the word of the LORD that He had spoken concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh.

**28** When the news came to Joab — for Joab had turned after Adonijah, though he had not turned after Absalom — Joab fled to the tent of the LORD and took hold of the horns of the altar.

**29** King Solomon was told, "Joab has fled to the tent of the LORD; he is there beside the altar." Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, saying, "Go, strike him down."

**30** Benaiah came to the tent of the LORD and said to him, "Thus says the king: 'Come out.'" But he said, "No, I will die here." Benaiah brought word back to the king, saying, "Thus Joab spoke, and thus he answered me."

**31** The king said to him, "Do as he has said. Strike him down and bury him, and take away from me and from my father's house the blood that Joab shed without cause.

**32** The LORD will bring his blood back on his own head, because he struck down two men more righteous and better than he, and killed them with the sword without my father David knowing it: Abner son of Ner, commander of the army of Israel, and Amasa son of Jether, commander of the army of Judah.

**33** So their blood shall come back on the head of Joab and on the head of his seed forever. But for David and his seed and his house and his throne there shall be peace from the LORD forever."

**34** Then Benaiah son of Jehoiada went up and struck him down and killed him, and he was buried in his own house in the wilderness.

**35** The king put Benaiah son of Jehoiada over the army in his place, and the king put Zadok the priest in the place of Abiathar.

## Shimei

**36** Then the king sent and called Shimei and said to him, "Build yourself a house in Jerusalem and live there, and do not go out from there anywhere at all.

**37** On the day you go out and cross the Kidron Valley, know for certain that you shall surely die. Your blood shall be on your own head."

**38** Shimei said to the king, "What you say is good. As my lord the king has said, so will your servant do." So Shimei lived in Jerusalem many days.

**39** And it came to pass at the end of three years that two of Shimei's servants ran away to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath. Shimei was told, "Your servants are in Gath."

**40** Shimei got up, saddled his donkey and went to Gath, to Achish, to look for his servants. Shimei went and brought his servants back from Gath.

**41** Solomon was told that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath and had come back.

**42** The king sent and called Shimei and said to him, "Did I not make you swear by the LORD, and warn you, saying, 'On the day you go out and go anywhere at all, know for certain that you shall surely die'? You said to me, 'What I have heard is good.'

**43** Why then have you not kept the oath of the LORD and the command that I laid on you?"

**44** The king also said to Shimei, "You know in your own heart all the evil that you did to David my father. So the LORD will bring your evil back on your own head.

**45** But King Solomon shall be blessed, and the throne of David shall be established before the LORD forever."

**46** Then the king commanded Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he went out and struck him down, and he died. So the kingdom was established in the hand of Solomon.

## Notes

- **v2 "I am going the way of all the earth. Be strong, and show yourself a man"** — the words of Joshua at the end of his life, *Today I am going the way of all the earth* (Joshua 23:14), and of the LORD to Joshua at the start of his, *be strong… so that you may prosper wherever you go* (Joshua 1:7), which v3 echoes.
- **v4 "you shall not lack a man on the throne of Israel"** — the promise of Nathan (2 Samuel 7:12–16), here with a condition: *If your sons take heed to their way*.
- **vv5–6 "He shed the blood of war in peace… do not let his gray head go down to Sheol in peace"** — *shalom* three times: Joab made war in time of peace, and shall not die in peace. *Gray head… to Sheol*, as Jacob said, *you will bring my gray head down in sorrow to Sheol* (Genesis 42:38). The Greek lacks *He shed the blood of war in peace*.
- **vv8–9 "I swore to him by the LORD… Now therefore do not hold him guiltless"** — David's oath (2 Samuel 19:23) bound David; he leaves Shimei to Solomon.
- **vv17, 22 "Abishag the Shunammite as my wife… Ask for him the kingdom as well"** — to take the late king's woman was to claim his place: Absalom went in to his father's concubines (2 Samuel 16:21–22), and Abner was accused over Saul's (2 Samuel 3:7). Abishag had served David in his bed (1:4).
- **v26 "you deserve to die… you shared in all that my father suffered"** — *ish mavet*, near to *ben mavet*. Abiathar alone escaped when Saul killed the priests of Nob, and fled to David (1 Samuel 22:20–23). *Anathoth*: the priests' town in Benjamin.
- **v27 "to fulfill the word of the LORD… concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh"** — *I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest… I will build him a sure house* (1 Samuel 2:35). Abiathar was the son of *Ahimelech son of Ahitub* (1 Samuel 22:9, 20), and an Ahitub was Eli's grandson (1 Samuel 14:3).
- **vv28–34 "took hold of the horns of the altar… No, I will die here"** — Adonijah held the altar and was spared (1:50–53); Joab is not, as the law allows for one who has killed *with intent… by cunning* (Exodus 21:14).
- **v35 "put Zadok the priest in the place of Abiathar"** — here and after v46 the Greek has long additions about Solomon's wisdom, his marriage to Pharaoh's daughter and his rule, matters the Hebrew tells in later chapters. The text stays with the Hebrew.
- **v37 "Your blood shall be on your own head"** — *damkha yihyeh ve-roshekha*, the terms of the spies' oath to Rahab, *his blood will be on his own head* (Joshua 2:19). *Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath* (v39): David once fled to *Achish son of Maoch* (1 Samuel 27:2).

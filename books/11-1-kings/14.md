# 1 Kings 14

## Jeroboam's Wife Goes to Ahijah

**1** At that time Abijah the son of Jeroboam fell sick.

**2** Jeroboam said to his wife, "Get up, please, and disguise yourself, so that no one will know you are the wife of Jeroboam, and go to Shiloh. Ahijah the prophet is there, who told me that I would be king over this people.

**3** Take with you ten loaves, some crumbled cakes and a jar of honey, and go to him. He will tell you what will happen to the boy."

**4** Jeroboam's wife did so. She got up and went to Shiloh and came to the house of Ahijah. Now Ahijah could not see, for his eyes were fixed because of his age.

**5** But the LORD had said to Ahijah, "The wife of Jeroboam is coming to ask you about her son, for he is sick. Thus and thus you shall say to her." When she came, she was pretending to be another woman.

**6** And it came to pass, when Ahijah heard the sound of her feet as she came in at the door, that he said, "Come in, wife of Jeroboam. Why do you pretend to be another? I am sent to you with hard news.

**7** Go, tell Jeroboam, 'Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: Because I raised you up from among the people and made you leader over My people Israel,

**8** and tore the kingdom away from the house of David and gave it to you, yet you have not been like My servant David, who kept My commandments and followed Me with all his heart, doing only what was right in My eyes,

**9** but you have done evil above all who were before you, and have gone and made for yourself other gods and molten images, provoking Me to anger, and have cast Me behind your back,

**10** therefore I am about to bring evil on the house of Jeroboam. I will cut off from Jeroboam every male, bond or free in Israel, and I will sweep away the house of Jeroboam as one sweeps away dung, until it is all gone.

**11** Anyone belonging to Jeroboam who dies in the city the dogs shall eat, and anyone who dies in the open country the birds of the heavens shall eat, for the LORD has spoken.'

**12** As for you, get up, go to your house. When your feet enter the city, the child shall die.

**13** All Israel shall mourn for him and bury him, for he alone of Jeroboam's family shall come to a grave, because in him there is found something good toward the LORD, the God of Israel, in the house of Jeroboam.

**14** Moreover, the LORD will raise up for Himself a king over Israel who shall cut off the house of Jeroboam this day — and what? even now.

**15** The LORD will strike Israel, as a reed is shaken in the water, and root up Israel out of this good land that He gave to their fathers, and scatter them beyond the River, because they have made their Asherim, provoking the LORD to anger.

**16** He will give Israel up because of the sins of Jeroboam, which he sinned and which he made Israel to sin."

**17** Then Jeroboam's wife got up and went away and came to Tirzah. As she came to the threshold of the house, the child died.

**18** They buried him, and all Israel mourned for him, according to the word of the LORD, which He spoke by His servant Ahijah the prophet.

**19** The rest of the acts of Jeroboam, how he warred and how he reigned, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.

**20** The time that Jeroboam reigned was twenty-two years. He lay down with his fathers, and Nadab his son reigned in his place.

## Rehoboam King of Judah

**21** Rehoboam son of Solomon reigned in Judah. Rehoboam was forty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city that the LORD had chosen out of all the tribes of Israel to put His name there. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite.

**22** Judah did evil in the eyes of the LORD, and they provoked Him to jealousy with their sins that they committed, more than all that their fathers had done.

**23** For they also built for themselves high places, pillars and Asherim on every high hill and under every leafy tree.

**24** There were also consecrated men in the land. They did according to all the detestable things of the nations that the LORD had driven out before the sons of Israel.

**25** And it came to pass in the fifth year of King Rehoboam that Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem.

**26** He took away the treasures of the house of the LORD and the treasures of the king's house; he took away everything. He also took away all the shields of gold that Solomon had made.

**27** King Rehoboam made shields of bronze in their place, and committed them to the hands of the officers of the guard, who kept the door of the king's house.

**28** As often as the king went into the house of the LORD, the guard carried them and brought them back to the guardroom.

**29** The rest of the acts of Rehoboam and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

**30** There was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam continually.

**31** Rehoboam lay down with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the City of David. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonite. Abijam his son reigned in his place.

## Notes

- **vv1–20 in the Greek** — the Greek lacks these verses here; it tells the sickness of Jeroboam's son in its long addition after 12:24.
- **v3 "crumbled cakes"** — *niqqudim*, the word for the Gibeonites' bread, *dry and crumbled* (Joshua 9:5, 12).
- **v4 "his eyes were fixed because of his age"** — *qamu einav*, the words used of Eli, *his eyes were fixed, and he could not see* (1 Samuel 4:15). The blind prophet sees through the disguise.
- **v9 "and have cast Me behind your back"** — *ve-oti hishlakhta acharei gavekha*, a figure for contempt, as the LORD says to Jerusalem, *you have forgotten Me and cast Me behind your back* (Ezekiel 23:35).
- **v10 "every male, bond or free… as one sweeps away dung"** — *mashtin be-qir*, **one who urinates against a wall**, the idiom of *so much as one male* (1 Samuel 25:22). *Atsur ve-azuv*, **bond or free**, as in *there is none left, bond or free* (Deuteronomy 32:36). *Galal*, **dung**.
- **v11 "the dogs shall eat… the birds of the heavens shall eat"** — the same sentence will be passed on the house of Baasha (16:4) and on the house of Ahab (21:24).
- **v14 "this day — and what? even now"** — *zeh ha-yom u-meh gam attah*, a broken phrase whose sense is uncertain. The Greek has no text here to compare.
- **v15 "as a reed is shaken in the water… beyond the River"** — *beyond the River*, the Euphrates. *Asherim*, the wooden poles of the goddess Asherah.
- **v24 "There were also consecrated men in the land"** — *qadesh*, the word of the law, *there shall be no consecrated man among the sons of Israel* (Deuteronomy 23:17). The Greek has *a conspiracy*. *Detestable things*: *to'evot*, fixed.
- **vv25–27 "Shishak… took away all the shields of gold that Solomon had made… shields of bronze in their place"** — the shields of 10:16–17. The written text has *Shushaq*, the read form *Shishaq*. Gold becomes bronze within five years of Solomon's death.

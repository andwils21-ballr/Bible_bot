# 1 Kings 19

## Elijah Flees

**1** Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah had done, and how he had killed all the prophets with the sword.

**2** Then Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah, saying, "So may the gods do to me, and more also, if by this time tomorrow I do not make your life as the life of one of them."

**3** He was afraid, and he got up and ran for his life, and came to Beersheba, which belongs to Judah, and left his servant there.

**4** But he himself went a day's journey into the wilderness, and came and sat down under a broom tree. He asked that he might die, and said, "It is enough; now, O LORD, take my life, for I am no better than my fathers."

**5** He lay down and slept under the broom tree, and a messenger touched him and said to him, "Get up and eat."

**6** He looked, and there at his head was a cake baked on hot stones and a jug of water. He ate and drank and lay down again.

**7** The messenger of the LORD came again a second time and touched him and said, "Get up and eat, for the journey is too great for you."

**8** He got up and ate and drank, and went in the strength of that food forty days and forty nights to Horeb, the mountain of God.

## The Sound of Thin Silence

**9** There he came to the cave and spent the night there. The word of the LORD came to him, and He said to him, "What are you doing here, Elijah?"

**10** He said, "I have been very zealous for the LORD, the God of hosts, for the sons of Israel have forsaken Your covenant, thrown down Your altars and killed Your prophets with the sword. I, even I only, am left, and they seek my life, to take it away."

**11** He said, "Go out and stand on the mountain before the LORD." The LORD was passing by, and a great and strong wind was tearing the mountains and breaking the rocks in pieces before the LORD, but the LORD was not in the wind. After the wind an earthquake, but the LORD was not in the earthquake.

**12** After the earthquake a fire, but the LORD was not in the fire. After the fire, a sound of thin silence.

**13** And it came to pass, when Elijah heard it, that he wrapped his face in his cloak and went out and stood at the entrance of the cave. A voice came to him and said, "What are you doing here, Elijah?"

**14** He said, "I have been very zealous for the LORD, the God of hosts, for the sons of Israel have forsaken Your covenant, thrown down Your altars and killed Your prophets with the sword. I, even I only, am left, and they seek my life, to take it away."

**15** The LORD said to him, "Go, return on your way to the wilderness of Damascus. When you arrive, you shall anoint Hazael to be king over Aram.

**16** Jehu son of Nimshi you shall anoint to be king over Israel, and Elisha son of Shaphat of Abel-meholah you shall anoint to be prophet in your place.

**17** And it shall come to pass that whoever escapes from the sword of Hazael, Jehu shall put to death, and whoever escapes from the sword of Jehu, Elisha shall put to death.

**18** Yet I will leave seven thousand in Israel, all the knees that have not bowed to Baal, and every mouth that has not kissed him."

## The Call of Elisha

**19** So he went from there and found Elisha son of Shaphat, who was plowing with twelve yoke of oxen before him, and he was with the twelfth. Elijah passed by him and cast his cloak on him.

**20** He left the oxen and ran after Elijah and said, "Let me kiss my father and my mother, and then I will follow you." He said to him, "Go back, for what have I done to you?"

**21** He turned back from following him, took the yoke of oxen and slaughtered them, and boiled their flesh with the oxen's gear and gave it to the people, and they ate. Then he got up and went after Elijah and served him.

## Notes

- **v3 "He was afraid"** — the Hebrew letters *vyr'* can be read *va-yira*, **he was afraid**, or *va-yar*, **he saw**; the scribes' vowels read *he saw*. The Greek has *Elijah was afraid*, and the flight that follows, *for his life*, bears it out. The text follows the Greek.
- **v4 "take my life, for I am no better than my fathers"** — Moses asked the same in the wilderness, *kill me now* (Numbers 11:15), and Jonah will ask it, *take my life from me* (Jonah 4:3).
- **vv5–7 "a messenger touched him… The messenger of the LORD"** — *mal'akh*, fixed as **messenger**. Jezebel's *messenger* in v2 brought a threat; this one brings bread.
- **v8 "forty days and forty nights to Horeb, the mountain of God"** — as Moses was on the mountain *forty days and forty nights* and *ate no bread and drank no water* (Exodus 34:28). Elijah goes back to where the covenant was given.
- **v9 "There he came to the cave"** — *ha-me'arah*, **the cave**, with the article. On this mountain the LORD set Moses *in the cleft of the rock… when My glory passes by* (Exodus 33:22).
- **v11 "The LORD was passing by"** — *ve-hinneh YHWH over*, the word of the LORD's passing before Moses (Exodus 33:22).
- **v12 "a sound of thin silence"** — *qol demamah daqqah*. *Qol* is **sound** or **voice**; *demamah* is **silence** or **stillness** (the calm after a storm, Psalm 107:29); *daqqah* is **thin**, **fine**, the word for fine dust (Isaiah 29:5). The Greek has *a sound of a light breeze*. Older English has *a still small voice*. The text keeps the three words as they stand.
- **vv10, 14 "I have been very zealous… I, even I only, am left"** — Elijah gives the same answer twice, word for word, before and after the wind, earthquake and fire.
- **vv15–16 "anoint Hazael… Jehu… Elisha"** — Elijah himself calls Elisha, with his cloak (v19); Hazael becomes king through Elisha's word (2 Kings 8), and Jehu is anointed by a prophet Elisha sends (2 Kings 9).
- **v18 "seven thousand in Israel… every mouth that has not kissed him"** — Paul cites this reply: *I have kept for Myself seven thousand men, who have not bowed the knee to Baal* (Romans 11:4). Kissing the image was an act of worship (Hosea 13:2).

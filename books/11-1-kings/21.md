# 1 Kings 21

## The Vineyard

**1** And it came to pass after these things that Naboth the Jezreelite had a vineyard in Jezreel, beside the palace of Ahab king of Samaria.

**2** Ahab spoke to Naboth, saying, "Give me your vineyard, so that I may have it for a vegetable garden, because it is near my house. I will give you a better vineyard for it, or, if it seems good to you, I will give you its value in silver."

**3** But Naboth said to Ahab, "The LORD forbid that I should give you the inheritance of my fathers."

**4** Ahab went into his house sullen and vexed because of the word that Naboth the Jezreelite had spoken to him, for he had said, "I will not give you the inheritance of my fathers." He lay down on his bed, turned away his face and would not eat bread.

**5** But Jezebel his wife came to him and said to him, "Why is your spirit so sullen that you are not eating bread?"

**6** He said to her, "Because I spoke to Naboth the Jezreelite and said to him, 'Give me your vineyard for silver, or, if you prefer, I will give you another vineyard for it.' But he said, 'I will not give you my vineyard.'"

**7** Jezebel his wife said to him, "Is it you who now rule over Israel? Get up, eat bread, and let your heart be glad. I will give you the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite."

## Jezebel's Letters

**8** So she wrote letters in Ahab's name and sealed them with his seal, and she sent the letters to the elders and the nobles who lived in his city with Naboth.

**9** She wrote in the letters, "Proclaim a fast, and seat Naboth at the head of the people.

**10** Seat two worthless men opposite him, and let them bear witness against him, saying, 'You cursed God and the king.' Then take him out and stone him, so that he dies."

**11** The men of his city, the elders and the nobles who lived in his city, did as Jezebel had sent word to them, as it was written in the letters that she had sent them.

**12** They proclaimed a fast and seated Naboth at the head of the people.

**13** The two worthless men came in and sat opposite him, and the worthless men bore witness against Naboth before the people, saying, "Naboth cursed God and the king." So they took him outside the city and stoned him, and he died.

**14** Then they sent to Jezebel, saying, "Naboth has been stoned; he is dead."

**15** As soon as Jezebel heard that Naboth had been stoned and was dead, Jezebel said to Ahab, "Get up, take possession of the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, which he refused to give you for silver, for Naboth is not alive, but dead."

**16** As soon as Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, Ahab got up to go down to the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, to take possession of it.

## Elijah Meets Ahab

**17** Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,

**18** "Arise, go down to meet Ahab king of Israel, who is in Samaria. He is in the vineyard of Naboth, where he has gone down to take possession of it.

**19** You shall speak to him, saying, 'Thus says the LORD: Have you murdered and also taken possession?' Then you shall speak to him, saying, 'Thus says the LORD: In the place where the dogs licked up the blood of Naboth, the dogs shall lick up your blood, yours too.'"

**20** Ahab said to Elijah, "Have you found me, O my enemy?" He answered, "I have found you, because you have sold yourself to do what is evil in the eyes of the LORD.

**21** I am about to bring evil on you, and I will sweep you away, and I will cut off from Ahab every male, bond or free in Israel.

**22** I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah, because of the anger to which you have provoked Me, and because you have made Israel to sin.

**23** Also of Jezebel the LORD has spoken, saying, 'The dogs shall eat Jezebel by the rampart of Jezreel.'

**24** Anyone belonging to Ahab who dies in the city the dogs shall eat, and anyone who dies in the open country the birds of the heavens shall eat."

**25** (Indeed there was none like Ahab, who sold himself to do what was evil in the eyes of the LORD, whom Jezebel his wife stirred up.

**26** He acted very detestably in going after idols, as the Amorites had done, whom the LORD drove out before the sons of Israel.)

**27** When Ahab heard these words, he tore his clothes and put sackcloth on his flesh and fasted, and lay in sackcloth and went about softly.

**28** Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,

**29** "Have you seen how Ahab has humbled himself before Me? Because he has humbled himself before Me, I will not bring the evil in his days; in his son's days I will bring the evil on his house."

## Notes

- **v3 "The LORD forbid that I should give you the inheritance of my fathers"** — *nachalat avotai*. The law holds the land as the LORD's: *The land shall not be sold for good, because the land is Mine* (Leviticus 25:23), and *each… shall hold on to the inheritance of the tribe of his fathers* (Numbers 36:7). Naboth's refusal stands on the law.
- **vv4–7 "sullen and vexed… Is it you who now rule over Israel?"** — Ahab goes home as he did after the prophet's rebuke (20:43).
- **v8 "letters in Ahab's name and sealed them with his seal"** — the king's name and seal carry the order; Ahab is not said to know of it.
- **vv10, 13 "You cursed God and the king"** — the Hebrew says *berakhta*, **you blessed**, standing for *cursed*, as in the words of Job's wife, *barekh Elohim va-mut*, **Curse God and die** (Job 2:9). The charge answers the law, *You shall not curse God, and you shall not curse a leader among your people* (Exodus 22:28), and *two worthless men* meet the rule of *two witnesses* (Deuteronomy 17:6).
- **v19 "Have you murdered and also taken possession?… the dogs shall lick up your blood"** — *ha-ratsachta ve-gam yarashta*, two words. The dogs licked Ahab's blood when his chariot was washed at the pool of Samaria (22:38). The Greek has *the pigs and the dogs*.
- **v20 "you have sold yourself to do what is evil"** — *hitmakkerkha*, as if into slavery. The same is said of Ahab in v25.
- **v21 "every male, bond or free"** — the sentence passed on Jeroboam (14:10) and Baasha (16:3–4), now on Ahab's house (v22).
- **v23 "The dogs shall eat Jezebel by the rampart of Jezreel"** — *be-chel*, **the rampart**. When it happens, Jehu repeats the word as *be-cheleq*, **in the plot of ground** of Jezreel (2 Kings 9:36). Jehu also speaks of *the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons* (2 Kings 9:26), which this chapter does not mention.
- **v26 "He acted very detestably in going after idols"** — *va-yat'ev*, from *to'evah*, **a detestable thing**; *gillulim*, **idols** (15:12).
- **vv27–29 "went about softly… Because he has humbled himself before Me"** — *at*, **gently**. The judgment is not taken back but delayed to his son's days.

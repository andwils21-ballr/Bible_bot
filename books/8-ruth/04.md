# Ruth 4

## At the Gate

**1** Boaz went up to the gate and sat down there, and just then the redeemer of whom Boaz had spoken came by. He said, "Turn aside, friend, sit down here." He turned aside and sat down.

**2** He took ten men of the elders of the town and said, "Sit down here." So they sat down.

**3** He said to the redeemer, "Naomi, who has come back from the country of Moab, is selling the piece of land that belonged to our brother Elimelech.

**4** I thought I would let you know, and say, 'Buy it in the presence of those sitting here and in the presence of the elders of my people.' If you will redeem it, redeem it. But if you will not redeem it, tell me, so that I may know, for there is no one besides you to redeem it, and I am after you." He said, "I will redeem it."

**5** Then Boaz said, "On the day you buy the field from the hand of Naomi, you also buy Ruth the Moabite, the wife of the dead man, to raise up the name of the dead man on his inheritance."

**6** The redeemer said, "I cannot redeem it for myself, or I would ruin my own inheritance. Take my right of redemption yourself, for I cannot redeem it."

**7** Now this was the custom in former times in Israel concerning redeeming and exchanging, to confirm any matter: a man drew off his sandal and gave it to the other. This was the way of attesting in Israel.

**8** So the redeemer said to Boaz, "Buy it for yourself," and he drew off his sandal.

**9** Boaz said to the elders and all the people, "You are witnesses today that I have bought all that belonged to Elimelech, and all that belonged to Chilion and Mahlon, from the hand of Naomi.

**10** I have also acquired Ruth the Moabite, the wife of Mahlon, to be my wife, to raise up the name of the dead man on his inheritance, so that the name of the dead man may not be cut off from among his brothers and from the gate of his place. You are witnesses today."

**11** All the people who were at the gate, and the elders, said, "We are witnesses. May the LORD make the woman who is coming into your house like Rachel and Leah, the two who built the house of Israel. May you do worthily in Ephrathah and make a name in Bethlehem.

**12** May your house also be like the house of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah, through the offspring the LORD will give you by this young woman."

## Obed

**13** So Boaz took Ruth, and she became his wife. He went in to her, and the LORD gave her conception, and she bore a son.

**14** The women said to Naomi, "Blessed be the LORD, who has not left you without a redeemer today. May his name be renowned in Israel.

**15** He shall be to you a restorer of life and a nourisher of your old age, for your daughter-in-law who loves you, who is better to you than seven sons, has borne him."

**16** Naomi took the child and laid him in her lap and became his nurse.

**17** The women of the neighborhood gave him a name, saying, "A son has been born to Naomi." They called his name Obed. He was the father of Jesse, the father of David.

## The Line of Perez

**18** These are the generations of Perez: Perez fathered Hezron,

**19** Hezron fathered Ram, Ram fathered Amminadab,

**20** Amminadab fathered Nahshon, Nahshon fathered Salmah,

**21** Salmon fathered Boaz, Boaz fathered Obed,

**22** Obed fathered Jesse, and Jesse fathered David.

## Notes

- **v1 "friend"** — *peloni almoni*, the Hebrew for **so-and-so**, a name withheld. The man who would not raise up a name for the dead (v6) is left without one in the story.
- **v4 "But if you will not redeem it"** — the Hebrew has *if he will not redeem*, as though Boaz turns to the elders mid-sentence. The Greek has *you*, and the whole speech is addressed to the redeemer; the text follows the Greek.
- **v5 "you also buy Ruth the Moabite"** — the written text has *qaniti*, **I buy**; the reading tradition has *qanitah*, **you buy**, and the Greek agrees with the reading. The written form would have Boaz declare his own claim to Ruth; the read form tells the redeemer that the widow comes with the field. The Hebrew also has *u-me'et Rut*, **and from Ruth**, as though Ruth were a second seller; the Greek has *and from Ruth… you must also acquire her*. The text follows the reading tradition and the Greek in giving the sense both point to.
- **v6 "or I would ruin my own inheritance"** — a son born to Ruth would carry Mahlon's name and inherit the field (v5, v10). The redeemer would pay for land that would go to another's line.
- **v7 "Now this was the custom in former times"** — the narrator explains a practice his readers no longer knew. It is not the ceremony of Deuteronomy 25:9, where the widow herself pulls off the brother's sandal and spits in his face; here the man draws off his own sandal and hands it over, the sign of a transfer.
- **v10 "to raise up the name of the dead man"** — the words of Deuteronomy 25:7, *to raise up a name for his brother in Israel*. Boaz is not a brother, but he takes the duty as one.
- **v11 "Rachel and Leah, the two who built the house of Israel"** — *banu*, **built**, and *ben*, **son**, sound alike in Hebrew: a house is built with sons. *Do worthily*, *chayil*, the word of 2:1 and 3:11.
- **v12 "the house of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah"** — Tamar, like Ruth, was a widow whose husband's family owed her a son; she got him by her own boldness (Genesis 38), and Perez was born of it (38:29). Boaz descends from Perez (vv18–21).
- **vv14–15 "a redeemer… better to you than seven sons"** — the redeemer the women bless is the child. *Seven sons*, the full number, sets Ruth above them; Elkanah asks Hannah, *Am I not better to you than ten sons?* (1 Samuel 1:8). *Restorer of life*, *meshiv nefesh*, answers *the LORD has brought me back empty* (1:21) with the same verb.
- **vv17–22 Obed… David** — *Obed* is **servant**. The book that began *in the days when the judges ruled* ends with the name of David, and the ten names from Perez to David stand again in Matthew 1:3–6, where Boaz's mother is Rahab and Obed's mother is Ruth. The father of Boaz is *Salmah* in v20 and *Salmon* in v21, and *Salma* in 1 Chronicles 2:11; the Greek has *Salman* in both verses of Ruth.

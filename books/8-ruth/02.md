# Ruth 2

## Gleaning

**1** Naomi had a relative of her husband's, a man of worth, of the clan of Elimelech, and his name was Boaz.

**2** Ruth the Moabite said to Naomi, "Let me go to the field and glean among the ears of grain after someone in whose eyes I may find favor." She said to her, "Go, my daughter."

**3** So she went and came and gleaned in the field after the reapers, and as it happened, she came to the part of the field belonging to Boaz, who was of the clan of Elimelech.

**4** Just then Boaz came from Bethlehem and said to the reapers, "The LORD be with you." They said to him, "The LORD bless you."

**5** Boaz said to his young man who was in charge of the reapers, "Whose young woman is this?"

**6** The young man in charge of the reapers answered, "She is the Moabite young woman who came back with Naomi from the country of Moab.

**7** She said, 'Please let me glean and gather among the sheaves after the reapers.' So she came, and she has stood from the morning until now; she has sat in the house only a little."

## Boaz and Ruth

**8** Boaz said to Ruth, "Listen, my daughter. Do not go to glean in another field, and do not leave this one, but keep close to my young women.

**9** Keep your eyes on the field they are reaping, and go after them. Have I not commanded the young men not to touch you? When you are thirsty, go to the vessels and drink what the young men have drawn."

**10** She fell on her face, bowed down to the ground and said to him, "Why have I found favor in your eyes, that you should take notice of me, when I am a foreigner?"

**11** Boaz answered her, "All that you have done for your mother-in-law since your husband's death has been fully told to me: how you left your father and your mother and the land of your birth, and came to a people you did not know before.

**12** May the LORD repay your work, and may your reward be full from the LORD, the God of Israel, under whose wings you have come to take refuge."

**13** She said, "May I find favor in your eyes, my lord, for you have comforted me and spoken to the heart of your servant, though I am not like one of your servants."

**14** At mealtime Boaz said to her, "Come here and eat some of the bread, and dip your piece in the vinegar." So she sat beside the reapers, and he passed her roasted grain, and she ate and was satisfied and had some left over.

**15** When she rose to glean, Boaz commanded his young men, "Let her glean even among the sheaves, and do not shame her.

**16** Also pull out some for her from the bundles and leave it for her to glean, and do not rebuke her."

**17** So she gleaned in the field until evening. Then she beat out what she had gleaned, and it was about an ephah of barley.

## The Redeemer

**18** She took it up and went into the town, and her mother-in-law saw what she had gleaned. She also brought out and gave her what she had left over after being satisfied.

**19** Her mother-in-law said to her, "Where did you glean today? Where have you worked? Blessed be the man who took notice of you." So she told her mother-in-law with whom she had worked, and said, "The name of the man with whom I worked today is Boaz."

**20** Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, "Blessed be he by the LORD, whose loyal love has not forsaken the living or the dead." Naomi also said to her, "The man is a close relative of ours; he is one of our redeemers."

**21** Ruth the Moabite said, "He also said to me, 'Keep close to my young men until they have finished all my harvest.'"

**22** Naomi said to Ruth her daughter-in-law, "It is good, my daughter, that you go out with his young women, so that you are not harmed in another field."

**23** So she kept close to the young women of Boaz, gleaning until the end of the barley harvest and the wheat harvest. She lived with her mother-in-law.

## Notes

- **v1 "a man of worth"** — *ish gibbor chayil*, the phrase used of Gideon (Judges 6:12) and Jephthah (11:1), there *a mighty man of valor*. *Chayil* is strength, wealth and worth together; here it is a landowner's standing. Boaz will call Ruth *eshet chayil*, **a woman of worth** (3:11), the same word. The written text has *meyudda*, the reading *moda*, **a relative**; the meaning is the same.
- **v3 "as it happened"** — *va-yiqer miqreha*, **her chance chanced**. The narrator calls it chance; Naomi will call it the LORD's loyal love (v20).
- **v7 "she has sat in the house only a little"** — the Hebrew is hard to follow here, and *the house* may be a shelter in the field. The Greek reads *she did not rest in the field even a little*.
- **v11 "you left your father and your mother and the land of your birth"** — the call of Abram in Genesis 12:1, *from your land, and from your kindred, and from your father's house*. Ruth did it with no promise.
- **v12 "under whose wings you have come to take refuge"** — *kenafav*. In 3:9 Ruth asks Boaz to spread his *kanaf*, the **wing** or edge of his garment, over her. He prays for the LORD's wing; she asks for his.
- **v20 "whose loyal love has not forsaken the living or the dead"** — the Hebrew can be read of the LORD or of Boaz. The blessing asks the LORD to bless Boaz, and the *loyal love* may be either's; the Greek reads it the same way. *One of our redeemers*, *go'alenu*: the *go'el* is the nearest relative, who buys back a family's land (Leviticus 25:25).

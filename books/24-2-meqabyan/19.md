# 2 Meqabyan 19

## O Earth

**1** O earth, you who have gathered in kings and rulers, the great and the honored, lovely women and beautiful virgins,

**2** strong men who had beauty and fine legs, those who had knowledge and understanding, and those whose voices were pleasant like the sound of a sounding organ, like the striking of the lyre and the harp;

**3** those who sang sweetly and gave gladness like the drinking of wine, lovely, their eyes bright like the morning star;

**4** their right hands strong to give and to take and to handle, their feet beautiful to see, and running like straight wheels.

**5** O death, you took the souls of fine men; for you were sent by the will of God.

**6** You too, earth, have gathered in many bodies, whom God brought out from you and brought back to you. From you we came out and to you we returned; by the will of God we rejoiced on you, by the will of God.

**7** You became a bed for our bodies. We walked on you and slept in your lap; we ate your fruit, and you ate our flesh.

**8** We drank from the dew of your springs, and you drank from the spring of our blood; we ate from the marrow of your land, and you ate from the marrow of our bones.

**9** We ate of the taste of your dust and your good dew, as God commanded you for our food; and you took the beauty of our flesh and made it dust, as God commanded you for your food.

## O Death

**10** O death, you who have gathered in many mighty kings and rulers, and did not fear their majesty and their greatness, as He who created them commanded you; and you did not despise the poor.

**11** You had no pity on those whose looks were fair. You did not leave the mighty and the strong, did not leave the rich and the poor, the ugly and the handsome, old men and children, men and women.

**12** You did not leave those who think good and did not transgress His commandment, nor did you leave those who think evil, who became like beasts in their deeds, nor those who were perfect in the beauty of their looks, their voices and the sweetness of their speech.

**13** You did not leave those whose speech was bitter and whose mouths were full of cursing; you gathered their souls into your storehouses, whether of light or of darkness.

**14** The earth too gathered in their bodies, whether in rock or in earth, until the blowing of the horn, when the dead will rise.

**15** The doers will be repaid according to the deeds they did; for at the blowing of the horn and by the command of God the dead will rise in the twinkling of an eye, and those who did good will rejoice.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 the dead whom the earth has gathered** — the lament of 18:12–14 continued: the earth receives the beautiful and the strong alike. *Remember your Creator… before the silver cord is snapped… and the dust returns to the earth as it was* (Ecclesiastes 12:1, 6–7).
- **v2 "like the sound of a sounding organ"** — *ʾargānon nababit*, from the Greek *organon*, an instrument.
- **v6 "From you we came out and to you we returned"** — *you are dust, and to dust you shall return* (Genesis 3:19).
- **vv7–9 "we ate your fruit, and you ate our flesh"** — the book's own exchange between man and the earth, each feeding the other *as God commanded*.
- **vv10–11 "did not fear their majesty… did not despise the poor"** — *the wise dies just like the fool!* (Ecclesiastes 2:16); *Death and Destruction are never satisfied* (Proverbs 27:20).
- **v13 "your storehouses, whether of light or of darkness"** — the houses of light and of darkness of 1 Meqabyan 8:10, and *the storehouses of the souls* of 14:33.

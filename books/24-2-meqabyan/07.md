# 2 Meqabyan 7

## Woe to You

**1** "Woe to you, you and those like you, who do not know God who created you, and do not know His commandment and His word! For you will repent where repentance does no good, when you are punished in the torment of Sheol.

**2** There is no way out of it for you forever, you and your gods, and those who sacrifice and bow down to them as if they were God your Creator, to things that have no soul and no breath, that do not avenge those who harm them or do good to those who do good to them.

**3** For they are the work of men's hands, in which Satan speaks to lead astray the hearts of fools like you, to bring you down to the Gehenna of fire, you and your deceiving gods and the demons you obey.

**4** For you do not know that they profit you nothing; you sin and go astray.

**5** The cattle that God created for your food are better than you, and the beasts too, and the dogs; for they have no second judgment, only one death.

**6** But you will die and be judged a second time, in the terror of Gehenna, where there is no way out forever."

## The Bodies Hidden

**7** When they had said this, they passed on and vanished from him.

**8** Tsirutsaydan was in great fear and trembling, and it did not leave him until morning.

**9** After that he went to where he had thrown the bodies of the righteous, and did not find them. He wanted to bury them and could not, for God had hidden them, so that their bodies could not be found.

## Notes

- **v1 "you will repent where repentance does no good… the torment of Sheol"** — the rich man *in Hades, being in torment*, who asks too late for his brothers to be warned (Luke 16:23–28).
- **vv5–6 "only one death… judged a second time"** — *This is the second death, the lake of fire* (Revelation 20:14).
- **v9 "God had hidden them, so that their bodies could not be found"** — of Moses: *He buried him in the valley in the land of Moab… but no one knows the place of his burial to this day* (Deuteronomy 34:6).

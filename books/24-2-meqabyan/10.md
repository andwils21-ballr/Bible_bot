# 2 Meqabyan 10

## Balak and Balaam

**1** You too, kings and rulers, do not walk in his way, but fear God, who created you and has kept you until now.

**2** Walk in the commandment and the judgment of God, who rules all.

**3** For Balak son of Zippor, when the sons of Israel came toward Amalek to inherit the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites and the Perizzites,

**4** said to Balaam, "Come, curse them for me; for whom you curse is cursed, and whom you bless is blessed. I will give you much gold and silver to honor you, if you curse them for me."

**5** Balaam came, hoping for the reward of his wrongdoing, and Balak son of Zippor showed him where the sons of Israel were encamped.

**6** He practiced divination and offered sacrifice, slaughtering sheep and cattle from the fattest of them, and wanted to curse the sons of Israel.

**7** But God did not want him to curse them, and by His word He turned the curse into a blessing.

**8** Balaam said, "Those who bless you shall be blessed, and those who curse you shall be cursed; for you are a people God has chosen, and you are the dwelling of the Beloved who comes from on high."

**9** Then Balak son of Zippor grieved and was angry and enraged, because Balaam had blessed them, blessed them before him; and he ordered him to curse them.

**10** Balaam said to him, "I will not curse those whom God has blessed, for the people God has chosen have come into this land."

**11** Balak son of Zippor said to Balaam, "I wanted you to curse them for me, and you did not curse them; you have blessed them, blessed them before me. I would have given you my house full of gold and silver, if you had said, 'Give me,' and cursed them for me. But you have blessed them, blessed them, and have not done well by me, and I will not do well by you."

**12** Balaam said, "I cannot disregard and transgress the word of God; the word God puts in my mouth, that is what I speak.

**13** I do not prize goods above my soul; for if I loved goods, God would be angry with me, for I am not to curse a blessed people.

**14** For God said to Jacob their father, 'Those who bless you, let them be blessed, and those who curse you, let them be cursed'; for everyone who blesses you is blessed,

**15** and whoever curses you without justice is cursed." Make your deeds and your way good, so that God may be pleased with you.

## Jerusalem Plowed

**16** Do not be like the first people, who provoked God with their evil, and He disregarded them. Some He wiped out with the waters of the flood,

**17** and some He destroyed by the hand of their enemies. He brought an enemy, an evil people, who made them suffer and took their kings captive, with their prophets and their Levites,

**18** brought them to a foreign country they did not know, took them captive, plundered them, punished them and ruined their country.

**19** They made Jerusalem like a plowed field, for they pulled down the ramparts and the wall of the holy city.

**20** The priests were taken captive, the law was abolished, and the men fell by the spear.

**21** The widows were taken captive and did not weep for their husbands who had died, for they themselves were taken captive and were on their way.

**22** The children wept, the old were put to shame, and the enemy had no pity on the face of the old man and the gray-haired,

**23** nor on those in the *sabsab*, nor on the virgins. They ruined all they found in their cities and carried them off to a foreign country they did not know; for when God wills to be angry with His people, He first pulls down His sanctuary.

**24** Because of this, when God disregarded the sons of Israel, God made Jerusalem to be plowed like a field, for they always provoked their Creator.

**25** But He did not destroy them all at once, for He pitied them for their fathers' sake. It was not for their own sake that He pitied them, but because He loves their fathers Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, who reigned in righteousness and walked before their Creator in uprightness.

**26** He made them a double honor, in heaven and on earth, and they won two kingdoms.

**27** So you too, kings and rulers who are in this passing world, remember those before you who walked in purity, so that they inherited the kingdom of heaven and their name was good from generation to generation.

**28** You too, so that your kingdom may stand upright and your name be good, like the good kings before you who pleased God by the beauty of their life.

## Notes

- **vv3–15 Balak and Balaam** — told from Numbers 22–24. *Come now, curse this people for me… for I know that he whom you bless is blessed, and he whom you curse is cursed* (Numbers 22:6); Balaam *loved gain from wrongdoing* (2 Peter 2:15); *the LORD your God turned the curse into a blessing for you* (Deuteronomy 23:5).
- **v8 "the dwelling of the Beloved who comes from on high"** — the book's own words in Balaam's blessing. Numbers has *Blessed are those who bless you, and cursed are those who curse you* (Numbers 24:9), and Balaam's later oracle, *a star shall come out of Jacob* (24:17).
- **v14 "God said to Jacob their father"** — the words are Isaac's to Jacob (Genesis 27:29), and God's to Abraham (Genesis 12:3).
- **vv19, 24 "Jerusalem like a plowed field"** — *Zion shall be plowed as a field; Jerusalem shall become a heap of ruins* (Micah 3:12, quoted in Jeremiah 26:18).
- **v21 "did not weep for their husbands"** — *their widows made no lamentation* (Psalm 78:64).
- **v23 "the *sabsab*"** — a word not otherwise known.
- **v26 "they won two kingdoms"** — the Ge'ez has *2 mangəśta*.

# 2 Meqabyan 5

## The Sons of Meqabis

**1** Meqabis left young children behind him, and they grew up as their father had ordered them. They kept the order of their house and all that was theirs, and did not make the poor, the orphan and the widow groan.

**2** They feared God and gave their goods as alms to the poor, and held to all their father had entrusted to them. They comforted the widow and the orphan, became father and mother to them, rescued them from the hand of their oppressors, and comforted them in all the trouble and grief that came upon them.

**3** They lived this way for five years.

## Tsirutsaydan

**4** After that Tsirutsaydan, king of the Chaldeans, came and destroyed all their country, took the sons of Meqabis captive, and destroyed all their land.

**5** He plundered all their goods; and the Chaldeans seized the sons of Meqabis too and took them to their own country. They were worshipers of idols, who do not walk in the commandment and the judgment of God, but in all evil, fornication, uncleanness, greed and blasphemy, and do not remember their God.

**6** They eat everything that has died, blood, what a beast has eaten and what is torn, and all that God does not delight in; they have no law from all the commandments of righteousness written in the Law.

**7** They do not know God, that He is their Creator and their Savior, who brought them out of their mother's womb and fed them as was fitting.

**8** They have no law in judgment. They walk in robbery and wickedness, fornication and uncleanness, taking their father's wife and their mother's sister; they do every evil, taking their sisters and their kinswomen as wives, and they have no law.

**9** All their way is stumbling and darkness, uncleanness and fornication.

**10** But the sons of Meqabis kept themselves in all their abstinence, and did not eat what had died or what was torn, nor any of the uncleanness and sin of the Chaldeans; for their deeds are evil and many, not written in this book, which the sons of the *ʾArabāmāyān* do, sinners and rebels, heretics and perverse, full of uncleanness and wickedness.

**11** There is nothing in them that pleases God their Creator.

## Bel Phegor

**12** Again, they worship an idol named Bel Phegor; they serve it and trust it as if it were God their Creator. For it is deaf and dumb and neither sees nor hears, for it is a carved image, the work of men's hands, which the smith cast of gold and silver, the work of the sons of men, with no understanding and no breath.

**13** It does not eat or drink,

**14** does not kill or make alive,

**15** does not plant or pluck up,

**16** does no harm and does no good,

**17** does not make poor or make rich,

**18** does not punish or have mercy; it is only a snare to lead astray the foolish people of the Chaldeans.

## Notes

- **v3 "five years"** — the Ge'ez has the digit *5*; in this text a larger number may have been cut to a digit (1 Meqabyan 1:21).
- **v4 "Tsirutsaydan, king of the Chaldeans"** — the persecutor of 1 Meqabyan 1–5, whose soldiers' fame reached *all the land of Moab and the Chaldeans* (1 Meqabyan 2:10).
- **v6 "They eat everything that has died"** — the Ge'ez has *wa-ʾi-yəballəʿu*, **and they do not eat**, which the rest of the verse and v10 contradict; the negative is read as a slip.
- **v8 "taking their father's wife and their mother's sister"** — *You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's wife… You shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister* (Leviticus 18:8, 13).
- **v10 "the sons of Meqabis kept themselves"** — like Daniel in Babylon: *Daniel resolved that he would not defile himself with the king's food* (Daniel 1:8). *ʾArabāmāyān* is a people not otherwise known.
- **v12 "Bel Phegor"** — *Bəʾel Fegor*, the Baal of Peor: *Israel yoked himself to Baal of Peor. And the anger of the LORD was kindled against Israel* (Numbers 25:3).
- **vv12–18 "deaf and dumb… does not eat or drink"** — *Their idols are silver and gold, the work of human hands. They have mouths, but do not speak; eyes, but do not see* (Psalm 115:4–7); compare 1 Meqabyan 4:22.

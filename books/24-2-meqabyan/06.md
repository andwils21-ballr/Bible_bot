# 2 Meqabyan 6

## False Priests

**1** The proud Tsirutsaydan, the enemy of God, made false priests, deceivers for his gods.

**2** They sacrificed to them and poured out drink offerings to them,

**3** and he thought that the gods were the ones who ate and drank.

**4** He gave them cattle and sheep, fattened beasts and young goats, and they sacrificed evening and morning, and he ate of that unclean sacrifice.

**5** Not only did they do this themselves, but they enticed others too and compelled them to sacrifice to their gods.

## The Sons of Meqabis Refuse

**6** When those deceivers saw the sons of Meqabis, that they were good and worshiped God their Creator, they wanted to lead them astray, so that they would sacrifice and eat of that unclean sacrifice. But these blessed men refused them.

**7** They could not make them say yes, for they kept the commandment of their father, held firm in doing good, and feared God greatly.

**8** Then they reviled them, spoke against them and wronged them,

**9** and told King Tsirutsaydan that they refused to bow down and sacrifice to his gods.

**10** Because of this the king was angry and grieved. He ordered them brought; they brought them and stood them before him, and he said to them, "Sacrifice and bow down to my gods."

**11** They answered him, "We will not yield to you in this, and we will not sacrifice to your unclean gods."

**12** He threatened them in many ways but could not overcome them, for they had made their hearts firm toward God.

**13** He kindled a fire and threw them in, and they gave up their souls to God.

## They Appear to the King

**14** They appeared to him alive after their death, holding drawn swords, while he slept on his royal bed at night, and he was very afraid.

**15** He said to them, "What shall I do for you, my lords? Help me, and do not take my life, and I will do all you command me." They frightened him greatly.

**16** They told him all that was fitting, saying, "Remember that God is your Creator. There is One who will overthrow you from this pride of yours and bring you down to Gehenna below, with the devil your father; and there you will complete all your punishment, as you burned us with fire, though we had done you no wrong, while we worshiped God our Creator and bowed down to Him in the fear of God.

**17** For He is the Creator of all, who made heaven and earth and the sea and all that is in it,

**18** the sun and the moon and the stars and every creature that God Himself created.

**19** There is no other god besides Him in heaven above or on the earth below, who can do all, and nothing is too hard for Him, who kills and makes alive, who punishes and has mercy.

**20** No one escapes from His hand, for He is the ruler of heaven and earth.

**21** Of all He created, none transgresses His commandment but you, the wicked, and the wicked like you, whose hearts your father Satan has blinded; you and your gods will go down together into Gehenna, where there is no way out forever.

**22** For it is not you alone who do this, but Satan your teacher, who taught you this evil deed, to do us harm.

**23** You did not know God your Creator, and you make yourself out to be like God your Creator.

**24** You exalt yourself in the work of your hands and in your idols, until God brings you low and takes you down to the depths of Gehenna, and takes vengeance on you for all the offense and sin you have done on the earth.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 the priests and the sacrifice** — the deceit of Tsirutsaydan's priests in 1 Meqabyan 1:7–9, and of the priests of Bel in the Greek Daniel (Daniel 14:15).
- **v13 "He kindled a fire and threw them in"** — the death of the sons of Maccabeus in 1 Meqabyan 3:35–37, under the same king. The book does not name these sons or say how many they were.
- **vv14–15 "They appeared to him alive… holding drawn swords"** — as the five martyrs appeared to him in 1 Meqabyan 4:8–10.
- **v19 "who kills and makes alive"** — *I kill and I make alive* (Deuteronomy 32:39).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

# 2 Meqabyan 8

## Tsirutsaydan's Pride

**1** He went on in pride and in the deceit of his heart.

**2** He went around the regions of the nations around him; for it was said of his kingdom that it was like the iron that Daniel the prophet saw.

**3** He walks in all folly, evil and tumult.

**4** As we have told before, it crushes, breaks in pieces and devours,

**5** and what is left it tramples with its feet; for he is an oppressor, like his father the devil, stiff of neck.

**6** He does not know God, that He is his Creator, he who says, "My days have become like the days of heaven."

**7** He thinks in his heart that the sun rises from him.

**8** He rises up in Gad and settles in the land of Zebulun, raises war in Macedonia, takes food from Samaria, and they give him tribute from Syria.

**9** He settles as far as the region of Kedar and spreads as far as Sidon, imposes labor in Achaia, raises his neck as far as the river Takkaze, and turns back and spreads his rule as far as the sea of India.

**10** So he raises his neck to heaven.

**11** He does not humble himself, but goes in all evil and arrogance.

**12** All his way is in stumbling and darkness, arrogance and rebellion, destruction and the shedding of blood.

**13** All the ways that God hates he follows, as Sablanyos, his teacher of evil, taught him wickedness and robbery; he makes the orphan groan and has no pity on the poor.

**14** The kings of the nations he brought under his hand and killed.

**15** The commanders of the enemy he judged, and many nations he subdued and made them serve as he wished.

**16** He did not stop, and there was none he did not plunder, from the sea of Tarshish to the sea of Jericho.

**17** He bows down to idols and eats what has died, blood, what is torn and what is sacrificed to the gods. There is no justice in him, but in all his ways there is lawlessness, wrath, anger and tumult; for he became a terror to the nations under his hand, and he forced them to work as he wished.

**18** There is no fear of God before their eyes, for all that pleases him he does before Him, and he walks in deceit before God his Creator.

**19** He does not regard Him as his God; and God will repay him as he has done evil to his neighbor, on the day He seizes him in His wrath.

## God Repays the Proud

**20** He will take vengeance on him in its time, as He uprooted the sinners who were before them; for He has said, "I am God, who takes vengeance on the proud who do not walk in My commandment, and I will uproot their memory from the earth."

**21** The evil will be repaid as they did evil.

**22** But goodness from God will follow the doers of good.

**23** As Joshua destroyed the kings of Canaan at one time, the five kings in one cave, and by his prayer made the sun stand still at Gibeon so as to destroy their army, and killed at one time about *[number lost]*; for the sun stood in the middle of heaven until he had destroyed the army of the Canaanites, the Hivites, the Hittites, the Perizzites and the Jebusites. Their kings too he killed: he put their feet on their necks, killed them with the edge of the sword, and covered them with stones in the cave.

**24** So it overtakes those who provoked God with the evil of their deeds.

## Notes

- **vv2–5 "like the iron that Daniel the prophet saw… crushes, breaks in pieces and devours"** — *a fourth kingdom, strong as iron, because iron breaks to pieces and shatters all things* (Daniel 2:40); *it devoured and broke in pieces and stamped what was left with its feet* (Daniel 7:7, 19).
- **vv6–7 "My days have become like the days of heaven… the sun rises from him"** — Nebuchadnezzar's boast in 1 Meqabyan 5:3, *I am the one who makes the sun rise on the earth*.
- **v9 "the river Takkaze… the sea of India"** — *Takkaze* is the Tekezé, the great river of northern Ethiopia, flowing into the Nile; the king's reach runs from Kedar in Arabia to Ethiopia and India.
- **v13 "Sablanyos, his teacher of evil"** — *Sablānyos*, the teacher of the wicked in 1 Meqabyan 13:3 and 17:3–5.
- **v16 "from the sea of Tarshish to the sea of Jericho"** — the far west and the Dead Sea by Jericho.
- **v23 Joshua and the five kings** — *Sun, stand still at Gibeon… And the sun stopped in the midst of heaven* (Joshua 10:12–13); the five kings hid in the cave at Makkedah, and Joshua's captains *put their feet on the necks of these kings*; he killed them, and *they set large stones against the mouth of the cave* (Joshua 10:16–27). The Ge'ez has *5* for the kings, and *2))* for those killed, a number lost in the conversion of the text.

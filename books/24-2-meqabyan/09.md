# 2 Meqabyan 9

## O Man, and Not God

**1** You too, O man, and not God, why do you exalt yourself, you who are a man today and tomorrow earth and ashes, and will become worms and maggots in your grave?

**2** Sheol will follow you and those who do your deeds; for your teacher Sablyanos brought on his own head the sin of all the world, because by his pride he led our father Adam astray.

**3** For he refused to bow down to the work of his God, exalting himself and stiffening his neck.

**4** You too, like your teacher, have refused to bow down to the Lord your God.

**5** You too walk as those before you walked, who did not know the Lord their God.

**6** He took vengeance on them, and they went down into Sheol for the evil of the deeds they did on the earth.

**7** You too, like them, will go down into Gehenna.

**8** Do you think you will escape from God's hand? For you have kindled His wrath and grown slack in the worship of God, who gave you authority over five kingdoms.

**9** He tested you, whether you would do His will or not. If you make your way good on the earth, God will make your ways good; all you put your hand to He will make good and bless for you, and He will subdue all your enemies and adversaries for you.

**10** You will rejoice in your going out and your coming in, in the fruit of your womb, your storehouses and your flocks. Wherever you point your fingers and whatever you think in your heart, all will obey you, for it is granted you by God to do so, to build and to plant and to pull down.

**11** But if you refuse to hear the word and the law of God and do not walk in His judgment, you have nowhere to escape from the hand of God, like all the rebels before you, who did not worship God in purity and did not trust in His upright judgment; for all His judgment is righteousness.

**12** Nothing is hidden from His eyes; all is uncovered and laid bare before Him.

**13** He holds the authority of kings and overthrows the thrones of the mighty.

**14** He lifts up the humble and raises the fallen.

**15** He sets the prisoners free and raises the dead; those whose flesh has decayed and become like dust He wakes when He wills, for the dew of mercy is from Him.

**16** Those who did evil He raises at that resurrection of judgment, for they provoked Him, and He takes vengeance on them.

**17** On earth too He destroys their seed, for they are rebels against the law and the ordinance of God.

## The Two Ways

**18** Sinners do not want to be in the counsel of the righteous, for the way of the righteous is hard compared with the way of sinners.

**19** As high as heaven is above the earth, so far is the way of the righteous from the way of sinners.

**20** The way of sinners is the doing of robbery and wickedness, wrongdoing and fornication, perversity and greed, seizing others' goods, and drunkenness in wrongdoing,

**21** running to shed blood, ruining for nothing, making the orphan weep, eating what has died and blood, eating the flesh of swine and camel, and going in to a woman in her impurity or after childbirth, before she is cleansed.

**22** All these deeds of sinners are a broad way, the spread net of Satan, which leads to perdition and eternal judgment.

**23** But the way of the righteous is narrow and hard, and it leads to life: humility and gentleness, love and peace, fasting and prayer, purity of body, and keeping from foods that do no good, what has died and what is torn, and from fornication and going to another man's wife.

**24** They keep themselves from every filthy deed, from unclean foods not commanded in the law, and from the filthy deeds God does not delight in; for all this the sinners do.

**25** But the righteous turn away from all the evil ways that God does not delight in.

**26** He loves them, takes them into His keeping, and saves them from all their distress.

**27** But Satan makes the sinners his own, for they keep his law and his ordinance and all that he loves.

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "he refused to bow down to the work of his God"** — the devil's fall told in the Life of Adam and Eve: commanded to worship the image of God in Adam, he refused, saying that Adam was younger than he and below him (Life of Adam and Eve 13–14). *Sablyanos* is the teacher of evil of 1 Meqabyan 13:3 and 17:3–5.
- **v8 "authority over five kingdoms"** — the Ge'ez has the digit *5*; 1 Meqabyan 5:24 gives the king *four kingdoms*.
- **v10 "in your going out and your coming in, in the fruit of your womb"** — *Blessed shall be the fruit of your womb… Blessed shall you be when you come in, and blessed shall you be when you go out* (Deuteronomy 28:4, 6).
- **v12 "all is uncovered and laid bare before Him"** — *no creature is hidden from His sight, but all are naked and exposed to the eyes of Him to whom we must give account* (Hebrews 4:13).
- **vv13–15 "overthrows the thrones of the mighty… sets the prisoners free"** — *He has brought down the mighty from their thrones and exalted those of humble estate* (Luke 1:52); *the LORD sets the prisoners free; the LORD opens the eyes of the blind; the LORD lifts up those who are bowed down* (Psalm 146:7–8). *The dew of mercy*: *your dew is a dew of light, and the earth will give birth to the dead* (Isaiah 26:19).
- **vv18–19 "the counsel of the righteous… As high as heaven is above the earth"** — *Blessed is the man who walks not in the counsel of the wicked* (Psalm 1:1); *as the heavens are higher than the earth, so are My ways higher than your ways* (Isaiah 55:9).
- **v21 "the flesh of swine and camel… a woman in her impurity or after childbirth"** — the law forbids *the camel* and *the pig* (Leviticus 11:4, 7), and a man coming near a woman *while she is in her menstrual uncleanness* (18:19), or before the days of her purifying after childbirth are complete (12:2–4).
- **vv22–23 "a broad way… narrow and hard, and it leads to life"** — *the gate is wide and the way is easy that leads to destruction… the gate is narrow and the way is hard that leads to life* (Matthew 7:13–14).

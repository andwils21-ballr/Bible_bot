# 2 Meqabyan 12

## Woe to the Mighty of Israel

**1** "Woe to you, mighty ones of Israel, who did not keep what I commanded you in the tent, and did not do My will, only your own will: pride and arrogance, fornication and greed, drunkenness and carousing and swearing falsely!

**2** Because of this My wrath will burn against you like stubble before the fire, like fire that burns the mountains, and like a stormy wind that lifts the fine dust from the earth and scatters it high in the air, where no trace of it is found.

**3** So I will destroy from before My face all who do evil," says God, the Holy One of Israel. Remember God, who rules all, and nothing is too hard for Him.

**4** He loves those who love Him, and forgives the sins and offenses of those who walk in His commandment. Do not be stubborn and dull of heart, unbelieving.

**5** Set your souls right before God and hold to Him, so that you may save your souls. "I will save you from the hand of your enemy on the day of your distress.

**6** When you call Me, I will say to you, 'Here I am, with you,' and I will save you from the hand of your enemy and will not disregard you on the day of your distress; for you trusted in Me, did My commandment, did not transgress My law and loved what I love," says God, who rules all.

**7** Those who love Him He loves, and those who keep His commandment He takes into His keeping, for He is merciful and compassionate.

**8** He turns back His wrath again and again and does not kindle all His punishment, for He is merciful; He knows them, that they are flesh and blood, and once their soul goes out from their body they return to their earth.

**9** They do not know where they are until the time God wills to bring them from where they are not; for He created them from where they were not, and again He took their souls and returned the earth to its place.

**10** Again He will bring them, from where they are not, into being by His will.

## The Angel of Death

**11** But Tsirutsaydan, the rebel against God, greatly increased his pride before God; and when God let him be until the time He chose, he exalted himself.

**12** He said, "My days have become like the days of heaven. I am the one who makes the sun rise; I will not die forever."

**13** He had not yet finished saying this when the angel of death, whose name is *Ṭəlməyākos*, came down to him and struck his heart, and he died at once. He was cut off from the beauty of his life, from his great pride and from the evil of his deeds, for he did not give thanks to his Creator.

**14** The armies that were outside his city and his camp, wanting to fight him, came out when he died, destroyed his country and plundered all his cattle, and left none of it standing within the walls.

**15** They plundered all his goods, took his vessels, burned his city with fire, and returned to their own country.

## Notes

- **v2 "like stubble before the fire… like a stormy wind"** — *As the tongue of fire devours the stubble… their root will be as rottenness, and their blossom go up like dust* (Isaiah 5:24); *As fire consumes the forest, as the flame sets the mountains ablaze* (Psalm 83:14).
- **v6 "When you call Me, I will say to you, 'Here I am'"** — *Then you shall call, and the LORD will answer; you shall cry, and He will say, "Here I am"* (Isaiah 58:9).
- **vv8–9 "they return to their earth… returned the earth to its place"** — *When his breath departs, he returns to the earth; on that very day his plans perish* (Psalm 146:4); *the dust returns to the earth as it was* (Ecclesiastes 12:7).
- **vv12–13 "I will not die forever." He had not yet finished saying this** — *While the words were still in the king's mouth, there fell a voice from heaven* (Daniel 4:31). Of Herod: *an angel of the Lord struck him down, because he did not give God the glory* (Acts 12:23). *Ṭəlməyākos*, the angel's name, is not otherwise known.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

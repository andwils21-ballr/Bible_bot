# 2 Meqabyan 15

## The Day of God

**1** Those who held fast to good deeds will rejoice then; but those who grew slack, saying, "The dead will not rise," will grieve then, when they see that they have risen from the dead with their evil deeds that are of no use.

**2** Their own deeds will judge them; though no one accuses them, they themselves will know how they are judged.

**3** They will stand in the place of the wicked on the day of weeping and judgment, on the day of punishment, on the day of God,

**4** on the day of darkness, on the day of gloom, on the day of fog, on the day of *ḍaʿāʿ*, on the day of lightning,

**5** on the day of fear and trembling, on the day of sleet and frost, on the day of ice,

**6** on the day when the evil man is repaid as he did evil, the pure as he did purity, and the harsh as he did robbery,

**7** when the master is not honored above the slave, nor the mistress above the maidservant,

**8** when the king is not honored above the poor man, the ruler above the child, the father above his son, or the mother above her daughter,

**9** when the rich is not honored above the poor, the proud above the humble, or the great above the small. For it is the day of judgment, the day of repayment and of sentence, the day on which each is repaid as he has done,

**10** the day on which the doers of good are rewarded, and those who did evil are repaid with evil,

**11** the day when those rewarded with good rejoice and those repaid with evil grieve; and those who made the Scriptures a lie, saying, "The dead will not rise," will see it.

## Joy without End and Grief without Comfort

**12** Then all the sinners of the earth will weep for the sins they did, because grief without comfort has come upon them, because they did no good on the earth.

**13** So too the joy of the good, who did good, will not end forever and ever; for while they were on the earth they held fast to doing good,

**14** and did not transgress the commandment of their Creator, for they knew that they would rise from the dead.

**15** Because they did not transgress His commandment, they will inherit *[words lost]*

**16** In the heavens too He gave them as an inheritance the land of life that He swore to their fathers, on the day of the resurrection, when the rich are made poor.

**17** The doers of evil who did not believe in the resurrection of the dead and the law of God, and did not remember the day of resurrection, will wail.

**18** Then they will see the evil that comes on them, which has no end, no healing and no comfort; in their hearts grief without comfort, and no rest;

**19** the worm that does not sleep and the fire that is not quenched;

**20** and outside their bodies, fire and *tay*, a stormy wind, ice, hail and sleet raining down on them,

**21** the lash of fire for those who do not believe in the resurrection of the dead.

## Notes

- **v2 "though no one accuses them, they themselves will know"** — *their conscience also bears witness, and their conflicting thoughts accuse or even excuse them on that day when… God judges the secrets of men* (Romans 2:15–16).
- **vv3–5 "on the day of darkness, on the day of gloom…"** — *A day of wrath is that day, a day of distress and anguish, a day of ruin and devastation, a day of darkness and gloom, a day of clouds and thick darkness* (Zephaniah 1:15). *Ḍaʿāʿ* is a word not otherwise known; *sleet*, *frost* and *ice* (*dadak*, *marqi*, *ḥasatyā*) are taken as the cold of v20.
- **vv7–9 "the master is not honored above the slave"** — *it shall be, as with the people, so with the priest; as with the slave, so with his master; as with the maid, so with her mistress* (Isaiah 24:2); *who shows no partiality to princes, nor regards the rich more than the poor* (Job 34:19).
- **v15 "they will inherit *[words lost]*"** — the Ge'ez breaks off at *wa-yəwarrəsu*, **and they will inherit**; `SOURCES.md` lists the verse among those cut short.
- **v19 "the worm that does not sleep and the fire that is not quenched"** — *their worm shall not die, their fire shall not be quenched* (Isaiah 66:24; Mark 9:48), as in 1 Meqabyan 12:22.
- **v20 "fire and *tay*"** — a word not otherwise known.

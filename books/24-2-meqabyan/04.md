# 2 Meqabyan 4

## The Judges

**1** Some were in the days of Hoshea,

**2** some in the days of Gideon,

**3** some in the days of Samson, Barak, Deborah and Judith. Whether by women or by men, He raises up judges for them, to save them from those who afflict them and from the hand of all their enemies,

**4** as God wills to save them from those who afflict them.

**5** They rejoice greatly in all the good He has done them, in the produce of their land and the increase of their cattle, and in all the flocks in the field and in the wilderness.

**6** He blesses their cattle and their plants, and all they hold, at home and in the wilderness He blesses for them, and does not let their cattle grow few; for He watches over them with His eyes and loves them greatly, for they are the generation of the righteous.

**7** But when they themselves become evil in their deeds, He gives them into the hand of their enemies.

**8** When He kills them, then they seek Him, and turn and rise early to seek God their Creator.

**9** When they turn with all their heart, He forgives them and does not remember their former offense; for He knows them, that they are flesh and blood, and have in them the misleading thoughts of this world and of the demons.

## Meqabis Keeps the Law

**10** Once Meqabis heard this word, which God had established on His holy mountain, he strove in repentance.

**11** He did not cease to do what he had seen and heard of this word, and of all the good that the sons of Israel do, for God had compassion on them; when God punished them because they transgressed His commandment, they wept and cried out, and again He had compassion on them, and they kept His commandment.

**12** So too Meqabis set his ways straight, kept His commandment and walked in the commandment of God, the God of Israel.

**13** From the day he heard, in all that the sons of Israel boast of, he too boasted in keeping the commandment of God.

**14** He ordered his house and his children to walk in all the ways and commandments of God.

**15** What the Jews held forbidden he too held forbidden; the commandments the Jews keep, as he heard them, he too did; and the foods the Jews abstain from he abstained from, though he was of another people, a Moabite.

**16** He gave the tithes too, and the firstborn of his sheep, his cattle and his donkeys, and of all he acquired; and the sacrifices the Jews offer he too offered, facing toward Jerusalem:

**17** for vows and for sin, freewill offerings, the sacrifice of peace and the continual sacrifice,

**18** and the firstfruits. The drink offerings the Jews pour out he too poured out, and gave to the Levite who was appointed; all the Jews did, he did likewise, and the burning of incense.

**19** He made *marānātā* and *milonāt*, tables and tents, basins and *ḫoṣādāt*, beaten oil for the lamp of the holy place, and curtains, as they do in the holy of holies of Israel when they please God.

**20** For when they lived by His law and His ordinance, God did not disregard them or give them into the hand of their enemies; so Meqabis did well in all his ways.

**21** He prayed at all times to God, the God of Israel, to be his guide and not to cut him off from the assembly of Israel, whom He had chosen and who did His will.

**22** Again he prayed that God would give him seed in Zion and houses in Jerusalem, save him from the trial He had spoken through the mouth of the prophet, and accept his repentance in all he had repented of.

**23** He wept before God with tears, that He would not destroy his seed from the earth, and would keep him in his going out and his coming in.

## A Just Ruler

**24** Of the nations of Moab under the hand of Meqabis, some were persuaded to believe, for their ruler walked a straight way. They saw his justice and did his will; they weighed the justice of their cities and the bounds of their country, and saw that what Meqabis did was more upright and far better.

**25** They came to him and heard the righteous judgment and the goodness of Meqabis.

**26** He had great wealth, male and female slaves, donkeys and camels; and he had horses that were clad in iron *[words lost]*

**27** From the time he worshiped God, when he went to war, none defeated him; he alone defeated.

**28** They came in the power of their gods to fight him and cursed him in the name of their gods, but none defeated him, for he had put his trust in God his God.

**29** He lived doing so, defeating his enemy and bringing nations under his hand.

**30** He took vengeance for the oppressed and did justice for the orphan.

**31** He took in widows and orphans and comforted them, fed the hungry from his own food, and clothed the naked from his own clothing.

**32** He rejoiced in his labor and the work of his hands, and gave from what he had without holding back, and gave tithes to the house of God. Doing this, Meqabis died in peace.

## Notes

- **v1 "Hoshea"** — *Hośeʿ*. In a list of judges and saviors it is likely Joshua, whose name was first Hoshea: *Moses called Hoshea the son of Nun Joshua* (Numbers 13:16).
- **v9 "He knows… that they are flesh and blood"** — *He remembered that they were but flesh, a wind that passes and comes not again* (Psalm 78:39); *He knows our frame; He remembers that we are dust* (Psalm 103:14).
- **v15 "though he was of another people, a Moabite"** — the law said *No Ammonite or Moabite may enter the assembly of the LORD* (Deuteronomy 23:3); Ruth the Moabite said, *Your people shall be my people, and your God my God* (Ruth 1:16).
- **v16 "facing toward Jerusalem"** — *if they pray to the LORD toward the city that You have chosen* (1 Kings 8:44); Daniel *had windows in his upper chamber open toward Jerusalem* (Daniel 6:10).
- **v19 the furnishings** — *marānātā*, *milonāt* and *ḫoṣādāt* are not otherwise known. *Pure beaten olive oil for the light, that a lamp may regularly be set up to burn* (Exodus 27:20).
- **v22 "seed in Zion and houses in Jerusalem"** — *let not the foreigner who has joined himself to the LORD say, "The LORD will surely separate me from His people"… I will give in My house and within My walls a monument and a name better than sons and daughters* (Isaiah 56:3, 5).
- **v23 "keep him in his going out and his coming in"** — *The LORD will keep your going out and your coming in from this time forth and forevermore* (Psalm 121:8).
- **v26 "horses that were clad in iron *[words lost]*"** — the Ge'ez breaks off after *ʾafrās ʾəlla yāləbbəsu ḫaṣina*. `SOURCES.md` records that the Church's Amharic has a number here, *five hundred* horses; the words after it are lost with it.
- **vv30–31 "did justice for the orphan… clothed the naked"** — Job: *I delivered the poor who cried for help, and the fatherless who had none to help him… I was a father to the needy* (Job 29:12, 16); *if I have seen anyone perish for lack of clothing* (31:19).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

# 2 Meqabyan 14

## What They Say

**1** As for me, I grieve greatly, I am sorrowful, and my heart burns within me because of the Jews and the Samaritans, the Pharisees and the Sadducees, who do not believe in the resurrection of the dead. The Jews say, "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die, and there we will see nothing."

**2** The Samaritans say, "Our flesh will not rise, for it becomes dust.

**3** If there is a resurrection, we believe in the resurrection of our souls; for the soul does not die even if the flesh dies. It is like the wind, which is not seen, and like the sound of thunder, of which no one says, 'Here it is,' and which does not appear.

**4** But our flesh is seen by all, and becomes ashes and dust; beasts eat it, and worms eat it in the grave.

**5** Those that ate it also become ashes, and no trace of them is found; they become like grass, and become ashes as though they had never been created.

**6** That is where the bodies that have decayed are found." Thus the Pharisees say: "We believe in the resurrection of the dead; but in heaven He will clothe the souls with another body, not the earthly one."

**7** Thus the Sadducees say: "Once our soul has gone out of our body we will not rise from the dead." They have no resurrection of the dead, neither for the soul nor for the body: "Once we have died, we will not rise."

**8** Because of this they are utterly foolish, and they grieve me, for they speak blasphemy against God and His glory.

**9** They have no hope of salvation, for they did not believe in God, who makes them righteous; they have no hope of salvation or of the resurrection of the dead.

## The Rope of Sheol

**10** O blind Jew, do you think God a fool, who brought you from where you were not, and while you were spittle made you a man? Is it too hard for Him who sowed you to make your soul and your body sprout again in His image and His likeness?

**11** Do not think so; you will not escape from the hand of God. You will rise even if you do not wish it, and be judged even if you are not willing; for the rope of Sheol is on your neck, by which you will be seized on the day of your death.

**12** In your mother's womb that rope sprouts for you from the stock of Sheol, the rope they put on your neck; as you grow, so it grows.

**13** When you die they put it on your neck, and when they pull it, its stock draws them in.

**14** It pulls its branches, for all the sinners are held in it, like ropes on their necks.

**15** It draws all the souls of sinners from where they are, from the ends of heaven; so your rope pulls you, to bring your soul from where you are and return you to the river of Sheol.

**16** Your flesh too, after it has passed away, the dew of God's mercy will raise up sevenfold, like the body of our father Adam; and there you will be repaid as you have done offense and sin, according to your unbelief.

**17** Do not think that because you lead another astray and go astray yourself, you will stay in the grave in your error, saying, "There is no resurrection of the dead," so that people go astray from the commandment of God.

**18** Who will leave you in the dust and the earth? He will raise you up, to repay you according to the deeds you have done.

**19** Then they will come: your spirits from the winds, your dust from the earth, your flame from the fire,

**20** and the breath of life that was breathed into you, from the court of darkness,

**21** where it is shut in. But the souls of the righteous are kept in the garden, in joy.

**22** As for you, O Jew and Samaritan, Pharisee and Sadducee, until the time of your judgment you will be bound in darkness.

**23** Then you will see how God repays you according to your deeds, because your heart led you astray.

**24** You said to them, "The dead do not live; let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die"; and with your own voice, while you sit on the seat of Moses, you say, "There is no resurrection of the dead."

**25** Because of this you went astray, though you know the book of the Law and teach the words of the Scriptures; and you lead people astray. It would have been better for you not to teach and not to know

**26** the words of the Scriptures than to lead the people of God astray by your evil teaching and your useless word.

**27** You will be repaid according to your deeds and the fruit of your lips; for God shows no favor to persons, and those who teach good He repays from the good things He has prepared for those who love Him.

**28** You have nowhere to escape from the hand of Him who judges you; He will repay you according to your deeds, for you and those you taught will be judged together.

## The Dead Will Rise

**29** I know that the dead will rise. Those who kept His commandment will rise, and standing will not be hard for them, for His commandment brings them out, as the earth brings out grass when the rain strikes it,

**30** as a green tree puts out leaves when the rain has watered the earth, and as it bears the fruit of wheat or a crop of grain, and its fruit cannot be held back when God is pleased.

**31** As a pregnant woman, when her labor comes, cannot hold her womb closed once she has heard the word of God, but gives birth at once, for the dew from God comes down on her,

**32** so the earth too, on the day, brings out those stored up in her; she cannot hold her womb closed once she has heard the word of God, but gives birth to them at once, for the dew from God comes down on her.

**33** The bodies gather in their places, where their corpses fell; the storehouses of the souls are opened, and the souls return into the bodies from which they went out before.

**34** At the blowing of the horn the dead will rise in the twinkling of an eye and stand before God, and He will repay them according to the work of their hands.

**35** Then you will see that you rise from the dead, and you will marvel at all the deeds you did on the earth, when you see them written before your eyes; and on that day you will repent where it does you no good.

**36** You will know that you rise from the dead and are repaid according to the deeds you have done.

## Notes

- **v1 "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die"** — *Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die* (Isaiah 22:13), as Paul quotes it: *If the dead are not raised, "Let us eat and drink"* (1 Corinthians 15:32).
- **vv6–7 the Pharisees and the Sadducees** — *the Sadducees say that there is no resurrection, nor angel, nor spirit, but the Pharisees acknowledge them all* (Acts 23:8). *Another body, not the earthly one*: Paul, *There are heavenly bodies and earthly bodies* (1 Corinthians 15:40).
- **v10 "while you were spittle"** — *mərāt*, as *mərāq*, **spittle**, the word of 1 Meqabyan 4:18.
- **vv11–15 the rope of Sheol** — *The cords of Sheol entangled me; the snares of death confronted me* (Psalm 18:5). The likeness of a rope that grows with a man from the womb is the book's own.
- **v16 "after it has passed away… sevenfold"** — *taʾatito wa-wino məsbəʿita*; the sense of the clause is uncertain.
- **vv19–21 "your spirits from the winds, your dust from the earth, your flame from the fire"** — the parts of the body returning from the four elements, as in 1 Meqabyan 8:6–15.
- **v24 "while you sit on the seat of Moses"** — *The scribes and the Pharisees sit on Moses' seat* (Matthew 23:2).
- **vv31–33 the womb and the storehouses** — vv31 and 32 repeat the same words of the woman and of the earth. *The earth shall give back those who are asleep in it… and the chambers shall give back the souls which have been committed to them* (2 Esdras 7:32); compare 1 Meqabyan 7:24 and 1 Enoch 51:1.
- **v34 "At the blowing of the horn… in the twinkling of an eye"** — *in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet. For the trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised* (1 Corinthians 15:52).

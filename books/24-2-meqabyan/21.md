# 2 Meqabyan 21

## Where Are They?

**1** Where are those who gathered others' goods by wrongdoing, goods that were not theirs and not the work of their hands?

**2** They take others' goods without paying and gather them in, not knowing the day of their death that will overtake them, and they leave their wealth to others.

**3** Their children do not rejoice in their fathers' wealth, for they are houses of sinners, who squander it by theft or wickedness, sinners like their fathers, or by robbery.

**4** Their fathers' goods do not profit them, for they gathered them by wrongdoing. Like a passing cloud, like smoke that the wind carries away, like the flower of the grass that falls, and like wax that melts before the fire, so the honor of sinners perishes.

**5** As David said, "I saw the sinner exalted and lifted up like the cedar of Lebanon; and when I passed by again he was gone. I searched for him, and I did not find his place."

**6** So the destruction of sinners comes all at once, so that those who wrong their neighbors may not boast, thinking that they will not die because they have gathered others' goods by wrongdoing.

**7** O fools, remember that you will pass away, and your wealth will pass away with you. Even if your gold is much, your silver too will rust.

**8** If you have many children, there will be many graves; if you have many houses, they will become ruins.

**9** If you have many cattle, they will be plunder for your enemy, for you did not do the will of the Lord your God; and nothing you put your hand to will prosper, for it is not blessed,

**10** at home or in the field, in the enclosures of the field, the *ḥatl*, the threshing floor or the winepress.

**11** Nor will you rejoice in the fruit of your womb, but grief will come to you from all your enemies; for God will not let you grow old with all the people of your house, because you did not keep the commandments of God.

## The Blessing of the Righteous

**12** But those who kept His law, His commandment and His ordinance He does not deprive of His blessing; He gives them all they ask, and blesses for them the fruit of their land and the fruit of their womb.

**13** He sets them above all the nations around them, to judge and not to be judged, and gives them every blessing in the enclosures of their fields.

**14** All they put their hands to He blesses for them, the fruit of their labor and the folds of their cattle, and He makes them rejoice in the fruit of their womb.

**15** He does not let their cattle grow few, and saves them from all their distress, from disease and weariness and trial, from what they know and what they do not know.

**16** He stands up for them in judgment, and saves them from distress, from evil talk and from all that opposes them. For formerly, in the work of the tent, if there was a Levite who was entrusted with the work of the tent and kept it, and walked in the will of God, by the former law they gave him tithes of everything according to His law, and every firstborn, of man and of beast,

**17** and cities of refuge among all their cities, as Moses commanded Joshua son of Nun, so that the killer who killed unknowingly might be saved, or even one who killed knowingly,

**18** until they bring his case before those who judge and acquit.

**19** "So inquire among yourselves," he said to them, "whether there was hatred between them before. If he says, 'Unknowingly iron or stone or wood fell from my hand, and the one it fell on died,' when you know this, save him; for he did it unknowingly, and he will be saved.

**20** But if he did it knowingly, he will be repaid according to his offense, and no one will pity him. If he did it unknowingly and killed, once you have inquired, let him go so that he does not die, for he did it unknowingly."

**21** So Moses ordered the sons of Israel, so that they would not transgress the commandment of God but turn away from all evil; so he ordered them.

**22** He commanded them to walk in all His judgment and righteousness, and to keep far from *ʾagʿəwo*, from what is torn and what has died, and from all that is not fitting for the children of men to do who walk in the commandment of God and keep away from all evil, as he ordered them.

**23** He commanded them not to transgress His commandment, which He ordained for them after the likeness of the heavenly, that is, the tent, so that He might save their souls and they might find their dwelling with their fathers who did good.

## The Generation of the Righteous

**24** For those who believed the word of God and walked in His commandment are called the generation of the righteous; for from Adam and Seth on, those who did the will of God He does not reject.

**25** For we are the seed of Adam; in His image He formed us and in His likeness He created us, to do good and all that pleases God.

**26** If we do good, we will inherit the land of life with those who did good; for He does not separate or set apart those who love Him.

**27** He loves them greatly. He hears the prayer of those who call on Him in purity, accepts the repentance of those who repent firmly, and gives strength and might to those who keep His law, His ordinance and His commandment.

**28** Those who did His will, whether the first or the last, will rejoice with Him in His eternal kingdom. To Him they raise praise from now and forever and ever. Amen.

## Notes

- **v2 "not knowing the day of their death… they leave their wealth to others"** — *man heaps up wealth and does not know who will gather!* (Psalm 39:6); *Fool! This night your soul is required of you, and the things you have prepared, whose will they be?* (Luke 12:20).
- **v4 "Like a passing cloud, like smoke… like the flower of the grass"** — *they shall be like the morning mist… like smoke from a window* (Hosea 13:3); *The grass withers, the flower fades* (Isaiah 40:7).
- **v5 "I saw the sinner exalted… like the cedar of Lebanon"** — *I have seen a wicked, ruthless man, spreading himself like a green laurel tree. But he passed away, and behold, he was no more; though I sought him, he could not be found* (Psalm 37:35–36); the Greek has *like the cedars of Lebanon*.
- **v7 "your silver too will rust"** — *Your gold and silver have corroded, and their corrosion will be evidence against you* (James 5:3). The Ge'ez has *za-ḫala*, taken as **rust**.
- **vv9–14 the curse and the blessing** — *Cursed shall you be in the city, and cursed in the field. Cursed shall be your basket and your kneading bowl. Cursed shall be the fruit of your womb* (Deuteronomy 28:16–18); *the LORD will make you the head and not the tail* (28:13). *Ḥatl* (v10) is a place on the farm not otherwise known.
- **vv16–20 the Levite and the cities of refuge** — *To the Levites I have given every tithe in Israel for an inheritance* (Numbers 18:21); *the cities shall be for you a refuge from the avenger, that the manslayer may not die until he stands before the congregation for judgment* (Numbers 35:12); *if he pushed him suddenly without enmity… or used a stone that could cause death, without seeing him* (35:22–23); *the axe head slips from the handle and strikes his neighbor so that he dies — he may flee to one of these cities* (Deuteronomy 19:5).
- **v22 "keep far from *ʾagʿəwo*"** — a word not otherwise known, beside what is torn and what has died.
- **v23 "after the likeness of the heavenly, that is, the tent"** — *They serve a copy and shadow of the heavenly things. For when Moses was about to erect the tent, he was instructed by God, saying, "See that you make everything according to the pattern"* (Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 25:40).
- **v28 "whether the first or the last"** — *many who are first will be last, and the last first* (Matthew 19:30). The book ends, like 1 Meqabyan, with *Amen*.

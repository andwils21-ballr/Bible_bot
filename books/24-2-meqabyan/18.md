# 2 Meqabyan 18

## The Error of Those Who Deny

**1** How great is the error you have fallen into, you who do not believe in the resurrection of the dead! When they take you where you do not know, at that time you will repent where it does you no good, when they cast you into judgment because you did not believe in the resurrection of the dead, in body and soul together.

**2** You will be rewarded as you have done, whether good or evil; for you harmed the hearts of your companions, saying, "We believe that the dead who have become ashes and earth will not rise."

**3** Because of this their companions grow foolish; for there is no reprieve from their death and no strength against their punishment, and they are not spared the toil, for they must stand before the judgment seat of God.

**4** They will be greatly ashamed when He rebukes them in His wrath and is angry with them for all the evil they did; for though they do not know the way of God, they speak, and they themselves do not know where they came from,

**5** nor do they know where they are going, into the judgment of burning fire. According to the crookedness of their heart they teach their companions, for they are bitter and rough in their deeds and harsh, and they teach a crooked word, saying, "There is no resurrection of the dead."

**6** On that day they will know that the dead rise, and they will be judged for not believing in the resurrection of the dead, which is for all the creation of mankind.

## The Fruit of Adam

**7** For we are all the fruit of Adam, and because of Adam we died; for because of the error of our father Adam, the judgment of death from God came upon us all.

**8** Again, so that we may be repaid for what we have done, we will rise with that Adam our father; for by the ignorance of our father Adam he was made subject in the world to death.

**9** Because of this we were repaid for Adam's transgression: our flesh melted in the grave, and our bones were broken.

**10** The earth drank our marrow, and it perished; our beauty melted in the dust, our flesh was covered in the earth, and our pleasant voice was covered in the earth.

**11** The light went out of our bright eyes, and our beauty perished in the tomb and became dust.

**12** Where is the beauty of the handsome young men, whose stature was lovely and whose voices were sweet? Where is the greatness of the strong?

**13** Where is the might of kings and the honor of rulers, who rode on horses, adorned themselves with gold and silver, and were armed with gleaming weapons of war?

**14** Where is the sweetness of food, and of sweet wine?

## Notes

- **v7 "we are all the fruit of Adam, and because of Adam we died"** — *as in Adam all die, so also in Christ shall all be made alive* (1 Corinthians 15:22); *sin came into the world through one man, and death through sin* (Romans 5:12).
- **v11 "The light went out of our bright eyes"** — the Ge'ez has *ʿəda*, a word not otherwise known in this place; *the light* is the plain sense with *bright eyes*.
- **vv12–14 "Where is the beauty… Where is the might of kings"** — *Where are the princes of the nations, and those who rule over the beasts on earth… those who heap up silver and gold?… They have vanished and gone down to Hades* (Baruch 3:16–19).

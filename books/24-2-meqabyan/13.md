# 2 Meqabyan 13

## What the Sons of Meqabis Knew

**1** But these five Maccabees, the sons of Meqabis, believed and gave themselves up to death, refusing to eat the sacrifice of idols,

**2** and gave themselves up to the fire, so as to be saved from the fire in heaven, knowing that this world passes away

**3** and that pleasure does not last forever. For they knew that the reproach of God is worse than the reproach of the sons of men, and the wrath of God worse than the wrath of the king.

**4** One day of rejoicing in the gardens of paradise is better than thousands of years of life on the earth; and better than long days is one hour of Your mercy, O Lord.

**5** What are the days of man? Do they not pass like a shadow, and like wax before the fire?

**6** But You, O Lord, live forever; Your years do not end, and Your remembrance is from generation to generation.

**7** All this and the like the sons of Meqabis thought; they chose, they chose to trust in God, and refused to eat the unclean sacrifice.

**8** Because of this they gave up their bodies to bear witness for God's sake, knowing that the dead will rise, and that there will be judgment after the resurrection.

## Learn from These Five

**9** You who do not know and do not believe in the resurrection of the dead, learn now from these five good men of one family, who together gave themselves up to death, knowing that their latter life would be better than this earthly one that passes away.

**10** They saw that all passes away, and gave themselves up to death, so as to find praise from God because they believed in Him, did not bow down to idols, and did not eat the sacrifice of the dead and of uncleanness.

**11** Because of this, knowing that He would make them rejoice on the last day in soul and body, though they had wives and children, they did not regard the sweetness of this world or the bitterness of death, and gave themselves up to death, knowing that there will be a resurrection on the last day in soul and in body.

**12** They knew that those who kept the ordinance of God will reign in the world to come, from generation to generation and for long days, where there is no sickness or sorrow.

**13** Where there is no sickness or sorrow and no death, with the good kings and rulers who believed in the word of God, there they gave themselves up to death like wax in the fire, seeing in the thought of their hearts what is to come,

**14** and hoping that their faces would shine seven times brighter than the sun, and that they would be filled with His love at the resurrection of all, in soul and in body.

## Notes

- **v1 "these five Maccabees"** — the Ge'ez has the digit *5* (and again in v9). The sons of Meqabis burned by Tsirutsaydan in 6:13 were not counted there; the martyrs of 1 Meqabyan 2–4 were five as well.
- **v4 "One day of rejoicing in the gardens of paradise is better than thousands of years"** — *For a day in Your courts is better than a thousand elsewhere* (Psalm 84:10).
- **vv5–6 "like a shadow… like wax before the fire… Your years do not end"** — *his days are like a passing shadow* (Psalm 144:4); *as wax melts before fire* (Psalm 68:2); *You are the same, and Your years have no end* (Psalm 102:27).
- **v10 "the sacrifice of the dead"** — *they yoked themselves to the Baal of Peor, and ate sacrifices offered to the dead* (Psalm 106:28).
- **v14 "shine seven times brighter than the sun"** — *the light of the sun will be sevenfold* (Isaiah 30:26); *the righteous will shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father* (Matthew 13:43).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

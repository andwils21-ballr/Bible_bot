# 2 Meqabyan 2

## Rejoice a Little

**1** Thus the prophet whom they call the Seer said to him: "Rejoice now a little, in the light of an hour; but God, the Holy One of Israel, will take vengeance on you, because you showed no regard.

**2** You say, 'My horses are swift runners, and so I will escape by flight.'

**3** But I will bring those who pursue you, running swifter than eagles, and you will not escape the trial and the wrath of God that is on you.

**4** Do you say, 'I will put on iron, and the shot of the bow and the thrust of the spear will not harm me, and so too with my coat of iron,' and that I will not take vengeance on you?" says God, the Holy One of Israel. "But I will bring an evil sickness into your heart, worse than the shot of the bow and the thrust of the spear, a sickness of sores and boils.

**5** I will bring an evil sickness into your heart, and you will look for one to help you and not find one, for you have kindled My wrath; and you will not escape from My hand until I wipe out the memory of you from the earth.

**6** You will know Me, that I am God your Creator, when I do this thing quickly, in the twinkling of an eye; for you stiffened your neck and lifted your head over My city. You are like grass before the wind, which fire eats before My eyes, and like ashes that the winds and the storm carry from the face of the earth; so are you before Me.

**7** For you have kindled My wrath and did not know your Creator; I too will not spare anything of yours, and I will leave none of yours on all your mountains.

**8** Now repent of all the evil you have done. If you repent and humble yourself before God with tears and weeping, and ask Him with a pure heart, God will pass over your sin in all you have sinned before Him."

## Meqabis Repents

**9** At that time Meqabis wept before God for his sin and put on ashes, for God was angry with him.

**10** He knew that God does not delay the evil He has spoken through the mouth of His prophet; for His eyes are watchful and do not sleep, His ears are open and are not deaf, the word He has spoken does not lie, and He does it at once, in the twinkling of an eye.

**11** He threw off his clothes and put on sackcloth of hair, put ashes on his head, and wept and cried out before God his God for the evil he had done.

## Notes

- **v1 "the prophet whom they call the Seer"** — *nabiy raʿāy za-yəbələwwo*. The prophet is not named. *Formerly in Israel… he who is now called a prophet was formerly called a seer* (1 Samuel 9:9).
- **vv2–3 "My horses are swift runners… swifter than eagles"** — *You said, "No! We will flee upon horses"; therefore you shall flee away; and, "We will ride upon swift steeds"; therefore your pursuers shall be swift* (Isaiah 30:16); *Our pursuers were swifter than the eagles in the heavens* (Lamentations 4:19).
- **v6 "like grass before the wind… like ashes that the winds carry"** — *like the chaff that the wind drives away* (Psalm 1:4); *O my God, make them like whirling dust, like chaff before the wind. As fire consumes the forest…* (Psalm 83:13–14).
- **v10 "His eyes are watchful and do not sleep"** — *He who keeps Israel will neither slumber nor sleep* (Psalm 121:4).
- **v11 "put on sackcloth… ashes on his head"** — the king of Nineveh, at Jonah's word: *he arose from his throne, removed his robe, covered himself with sackcloth, and sat in ashes* (Jonah 3:6).

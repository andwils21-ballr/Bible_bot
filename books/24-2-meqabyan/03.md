# 2 Meqabyan 3

## In the Pit

**1** The prophet spoke to him, for Ramath of Moab is near Syria.

**2** Meqabis dug a pit in the ground and went into it up to his neck, and wept bitterly and repented of the sin he had done before God.

**3** Thus God said to the prophet in the land of Judah: "Go back to the land of Ramath, to Meqabis the ruler of Moab, and say: Thus says God: It was not you who did this. Do not say, 'By the strength of my might and with my army I destroyed the holy city.' I am God, your Lord; by My own will I sent you to destroy My city,

**4** for she provoked Me with all her greed, her fornication and her deceit.

**5** I disregarded her and gave her into your hand. It was not for your sake; yet you stiffened your neck and said, 'I trampled the city by the strength of my hand.' Now, because of your children whom you fathered, God has passed over your sin.

**6** Now repent with all your heart and be steadfast, and do not be two-faced; for the two-faced do not hold firm in repentance.

**7** Blessed are those who repent with all their heart and do not turn back to robbery and sin in anything they have repented of their evil.

**8** They hold firm with weeping and tears, with much asking and with bowing down to God who created them; for He says to those who repent, 'You who went astray and then repented, you are Mine.'

**9** To the proud man too, after he went astray, when he returned to Him in repentance, He said: 'Because of your dismay and your fear I will pass over your sin; for I am God your Lord, who visits the sin of the father on the children to the third and fourth generation, and to those who love Me and keep My commandment I show mercy to the *[number lost]* generation.'

**10** Now, because of these children you fathered, I will keep My covenant with you, and accept the repentance you have made for the evil of your deeds," says God, the Holy One of Israel, who rules all.

## Meqabis and the Prophet

**11** At that time he came out of the pit and bowed down to the prophet, saying, "May God do so and more to me if I part from you! But do with me whatever you wish. For I provoked God and did not walk in His commandment, as my fathers did not; we have no law, and you yourself know that we worship idols as our fathers taught us.

**12** As for me, until now I had not heard the word of the prophets of God; for I am a sinner who walked in my sin, in the pride of my heart and the stiffness of my neck, who provoked the commandment of God and did not walk in the commandment and the judgment He commanded me."

**13** The prophet answered him, "I know that your repentance has now been accepted, for before you there has been none of your kin who confessed his sin.

**14** From now on, leave the worship of idols and turn to the knowledge of God, so that your repentance may be true." Meqabis bowed down at the prophet's feet, and the prophet raised him up and commanded him every good deed that is fitting.

**15** He did as God commanded him, and went back to his house.

## The Children of the Captivity

**16** Meqabis set his soul right, and removed from his house all divination and idols, sorcerers, sinners and soothsayers.

**17** The children of the captives whom he had brought from Jerusalem he questioned evening and morning about all the commandment of God, His law and His ordinance, as their fathers kept them.

**18** He appointed wise men from the children of the captivity in his house.

**19** Again, from the young ones, from their wise men, he appointed attendants in his bedchamber to keep his young children, their companions, and to teach them the way of God, as the sons of Israel did His law and His ordinance, His judgment and His justice; and about all the sacred places that the Moabites held sacred he would hear from the children of the captivity.

**20** He would abolish their sacred places, all their divinations, all their idols, all their altars of drink offering and their altars of sacrifice, the sacrifices from their fat beasts, the choice of his flocks and the young goats that he used to sacrifice to his gods evening and morning.

**21** For he had sacrificed to them and served them in every work, at noon and at every hour; he had asked them and trusted them and done their will in all that his gods, the deceivers, told him.

**22** He had not departed from their word in all they told him, for it seemed to him that they had saved him in everything.

**23** But Meqabis left their way.

## The Way of Israel

**24** Once he heard the word of the Seer whom they call the prophet, he made his way good in repentance, and all his house made their way good, more than the sons of Israel in those days. For the sons of Israel provoke Him at one time, and at the time He punishes them they know Him and cry out to God.

**25** When He sees that they are brought low under the hands of those who afflict them, and they groan before Him, on that day He has mercy on them, remembering the covenant of their fathers when they cry out to Him, for the sake of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob their fathers.

**26** When He has saved them, they forget the One who saved them and go back to the worship of idols.

**27** On that day He sends against them nations who afflict them. When these have afflicted them and made them suffer, they cry out to God; at that time He has mercy and compassion on them, for He loves them, for they are the work of His hands.

**28** Again, when He bears with them, they go back to provoking Him with the worship of the idols around them and the evil work of their hands.

**29** He too raises up against them the people of the foreigners, Moab, Midian, Syria and Egypt; and they weep and cry out when their enemies have defeated them, made them suffer, made them serve and enslaved them. Then He raises up judges for them, to save them.

## Notes

- **v2 "dug a pit… up to his neck"** — the book's own picture of his repentance; v11 brings him out.
- **vv3–5 "It was not you who did this… by My own will I sent you to destroy My city"** — Isaiah on Assyria: *Woe to Assyria, the rod of My anger… Against a godless nation I send him… For he says: "By the strength of my hand I have done it"… Shall the axe boast over him who hews with it?* (Isaiah 10:5–6, 13, 15).
- **v5 "I trampled the city"** — the Ge'ez has *rodkəwwā*, read as *ragaṣkəwwā*, **I trampled it**.
- **v6 "do not be two-faced"** — *nəfuq*, a hypocrite. *A double-minded man, unstable in all his ways* (James 1:8).
- **v9 "to the third and fourth generation… mercy to the *[number lost]* generation"** — the ten words: *visiting the iniquity of the fathers on the children to the third and the fourth generation of those who hate Me, but showing steadfast love to thousands of those who love Me* (Exodus 20:5–6). The Ge'ez has *))*, a numeral lost in the conversion of the text.
- **v10 "the repentance you have made"** — the Ge'ez has *za-gabarku*, **that I made**, read as *za-gabarka*.
- **v11 "May God do so and more to me"** — *kamaz wa-kamaz la-yərassayani ʾƎgziʾabəḥer*, the oath of Ruth: *May the LORD do so to me and more also if anything but death parts me from you* (Ruth 1:17).
- **vv24–29 the round of Israel** — told as in 1 Meqabyan 28:17–35: sin, oppression, crying out, a savior, and sin again (Judges 2:11–19).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

# 2 Meqabyan 17

## The Grain of Wheat

**1** A grain of wheat, if it does not die, does not sprout or bear fruit. But if the grain of wheat dies, it makes a root in the earth, puts out leaves, becomes a stalk and bears fruit; and you know

**2** yourselves that one grain of wheat becomes many grains.

**3** So this grain sprouts from wind and water and earth, and the sun in place of fire; for without the sun the wheat cannot bear fruit.

**4** The wind is in place of the soul, and without wind the wheat cannot bear fruit; and the water is what waters it and soaks the earth.

**5** Once the dust and the earth are soaked, it makes a root and rises up, and bears fruit as much as God has blessed it.

## The Vine

**6** For the grain of wheat is in the likeness of Adam, into whom God's speaking spirit was breathed. The vine too is watered with water; it makes roots and takes up water through the fine cords of its roots,

**7** and lifts it up above, into the crown of the leaves; for the dew of mercy from God nurses them at the tops of the tall branches. It ripens in the heat of the sun and bears fruit from God.

**8** It gives a good fragrance that gladdens the heart. When they eat it, it satisfies like water and bread, so that one is not hungry or thirsty; and when they press it, it gives the blood of the cluster.

**9** When they drink it, it gladdens the heart of man, as it says in the Psalm, "Wine gladdens the heart of man." But a man who takes pleasure in it gets drunk when he opens his mouth and swallows and fills his belly, and his blood is poured into his heart.

**10** It makes him senseless, for drunkenness with wine makes a man very foolish; it makes the cliffs and ravines seem to him like level ground, and he does not know the stumbling block or the thorn in his hands and his feet.

**11** So God has done with the vine and its fruit, so that His name may be praised by those who do His will and those who believe in the resurrection of the dead.

**12** He makes those who believe in the resurrection of the dead rejoice in the dwelling of life.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "A grain of wheat, if it does not die"** — *unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains alone; but if it dies, it bears much fruit* (John 12:24).
- **vv3–4 "wind and water and earth, and the sun in place of fire… the wind in place of the soul"** — the four parts of 1 Meqabyan 8:6–8 and 2 Meqabyan 14:19, found again in the grain.
- **v6 "God's speaking spirit was breathed"** — *manfasa ʾƎgziʾabəḥer nababit*: *the LORD God… breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and the man became a living creature* (Genesis 2:7).
- **v8 "the blood of the cluster"** — *you drank foaming wine made from the blood of the grape* (Deuteronomy 32:14); *he washes… his vesture in the blood of grapes* (Genesis 49:11).
- **vv9–10 "Wine gladdens the heart of man… makes the cliffs seem like level ground"** — *wine to gladden the heart of man* (Psalm 104:15); *Who has woe?… Those who tarry long over wine… You will be like one who lies down in the midst of the sea* (Proverbs 23:29–34).

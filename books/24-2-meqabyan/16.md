# 2 Meqabyan 16

## Your Own Body

**1** Consider now for yourself what is in your body: the nails of your hands and your feet, and the hair of your head. Each time you cut them, they grow out again. By this know it, if you have understanding and knowledge to believe.

**2** Where, you say, do these come from, the nails of your hands and your feet and the hair of your head? Is it not God who ordered them to grow? So you may know that the resurrection of the dead will be of your own body, not in another body, so that you may know for yourself that you will rise from the dead.

**3** You will be repaid according to the deeds you have done, offense and sin, at the resurrection of the dead, because you led the people astray, saying, "There is no resurrection of the dead."

## Each Seed of Its Own Kind

**4** Now you will see it, when the day of your repayment comes. What you sowed does not refuse to sprout, whether grain or wheat.

**5** Again, the tree you planted does not refuse to sprout, whether vine or fig; its leaves and its fruit do not change.

**6** If you plant a vine, it does not change into a fig; if you plant a fig, it does not change into a vine; if you sow wheat, it does not change into barley;

**7** and if you sow barley, it does not change into wheat. Each, by its seed and of its own kind, in its fruit and its wood, its leaves and its sap, receives a blessing by the dew of mercy from God, and brings out fruit.

**8** So too the earth brings out what God has sown. It makes the soul and body of the doers of good sprout, not changed into doers of evil, and the doers of evil not changed into doers of good; what God has sown sprouts, soul and body.

**9** When the time comes for the blowing of the horn, the dead will rise by the dew of mercy from God: those who did good, to the resurrection of life, and their reward is the garden of joy, the dwellings for the pure that He prepared for the good, where there is no disease or sickness, where they never die again;

**10** but those who did evil, to the resurrection of judgment, with the devil who led them astray

**11** and his evil demons, who do not want a single one of all mankind to be saved, into eternal judgment,

**12** below, beneath, to the end of darkness, where there is weeping and gnashing of teeth, where there is no compassion or mercy and no way out forever and ever; for they did no good in their life on the earth while they were in their bodies.

**13** Because of this they are judged at the resurrection, soul and body.

**14** Woe to those who do not believe in the resurrection of soul and body together, in which God shows the greatness of His wonders!

**15** Each one will be repaid according to his deeds and the labor of his hand.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "the nails of your hands… the hair of your head"** — *not a hair of your head will perish* (Luke 21:18). The book's likeness: what grows again from the same body shows that the body rises as itself.
- **vv4–7 "a vine… does not change into a fig"** — the likeness of 1 Meqabyan 8:29–31. *Can a fig tree, my brothers, bear olives, or a grapevine produce figs?* (James 3:12); *to each kind of seed its own body* (1 Corinthians 15:38).
- **vv9–10 "to the resurrection of life… to the resurrection of judgment"** — *those who have done good to the resurrection of life, and those who have done evil to the resurrection of judgment* (John 5:29).

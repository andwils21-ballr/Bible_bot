# 2 Meqabyan 1

## The Moabite against the Holy City

**1** About Meqabis, who came upon the Jews in Mesopotamia of Syria and killed them in their valleys, from the Jabbok as far as the region of Jerusalem, and destroyed the holy city.

**2** For they made an agreement together, from Samaria to the region of Jerusalem and all its borders, and killed with the edge of the sword until they left none but a few; for the Edomites, the Syrians and the Amalekites joined with Meqabis the Moabite, who destroyed the city of Jerusalem.

**3** When the sons of Israel sinned, God sent Meqabis the Moabite against them, and he killed them all with the sword.

**4** Because of this the enemies of God shouted against His holy city and joined together in their feasting.

**5** The Edomites and the foreigners made an agreement and began to take vengeance on the city of God; for He Himself had sent them, because the people had despised the word of God.

**6** This Meqabis, whose city was Ramath of Moab, rose up in power from his city and joined forces with those who were with him.

**7** They encamped in the valleys of Gilboa of Mesopotamia as far as Syria, to destroy the city of God. From there he asked the foreigners and the Amalekites to be with him at the feasting, and gave them much gold and silver, horses and chariots.

**8** They came together and besieged the city, and poured out the blood of those who lived in it like water.

**9** They made Jerusalem like a watchman's hut in a field of gourds. They did filth and dung in it and all that God does not delight in, deeds of robbery, and they defiled the city of God, which was full of righteousness and holiness.

**10** They made the bodies of Your servants food for the birds of the sky, and the flesh of Your righteous ones food for the beasts of the field.

**11** They oppressed the widow and the orphan and ripped out what was in the wombs of the pregnant women, until God, who tests the heart and the kidneys, was angry; for they acted without the fear of God, as Satan had taught them.

**12** They went back to their borders rejoicing in the evil they had done to the people of God, and took the captives and the plunder they had seized from the holy city.

**13** When they came back to their dwellings, they held joy and merriment and feasting.

## Notes

- **The text** — the Ge'ez of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo printed Bible, and the verse numbers follow it. No other ancient text of the book is known. Like 1 Meqabyan, it is **not** the Greek 2 Maccabees. It opens with *Meqabis*, *Maqābis*, a king of Moab, a different man from the Maccabeus of 1 Meqabyan 2:1, whose name is written *Maqābyos*.
- **v1 "About Meqabis"** — *baʾənta za-rakabomu Maqābis*, a title for what follows rather than a sentence.
- **vv4, 7, 13 "feasting"** — *māʿlet*, the word for the celebration of v13. In v4 and v6 *tasakātayu*, **joined together**, is a word whose exact sense is uncertain.
- **v6 "Ramath of Moab"** — *Remāt za-Moʾāb*, Meqabis's city, not otherwise known; *Ramoth* and *Ramah* are towns of Gilead and of Benjamin.
- **v8 "poured out the blood of those who lived in it like water"** — *They have poured out their blood like water all around Jerusalem* (Psalm 79:3).
- **v9 "like a watchman's hut in a field of gourds"** — *ləgata ʿāqābe qamḥ*. *The daughter of Zion is left like a booth in a vineyard, like a lodge in a cucumber field, like a besieged city* (Isaiah 1:8).
- **v10 "the bodies of Your servants food for the birds of the sky"** — Psalm 79:2, word for word: *They have given the bodies of Your servants to the birds of the heavens for food, the flesh of Your faithful to the beasts of the earth*. The sudden *Your* is the psalm's.
- **v11 "ripped out what was in the wombs of the pregnant women"** — the cruelty foretold of Hazael (2 Kings 8:12) and condemned in the Ammonites (Amos 1:13).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

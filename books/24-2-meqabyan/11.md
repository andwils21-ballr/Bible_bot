# 2 Meqabyan 11

## The Gentleness of Moses

**1** Remember Moses the man of God, in his prayer and his humility. Though he saw what his people were, he was not angry and did not destroy them. For those who reviled him, his brother among them, and for those whom God wanted to destroy at once, he made atonement before God in gentleness, saying, "Have mercy, O God, on Your people, and do not disregard them, for Your people have sinned before You.

**2** As for me, Your sinful servant, have compassion on me, for I have sinned before You; and forgive them too, for You are merciful and compassionate."

**3** So he made atonement for those who reviled him.

**4** Because of this he was known as gentle.

**5** God loved him greatly, more than all the sons of the Levites, his brothers, and made him like God to them, for he also appointed the priests.

**6** Those who transgressed, the sons of Korah, God sank beneath the earth and brought down to Sheol alive, soul and body, with their tents and their cattle. Every word Moses spoke was done for him as for God, for he loved the Lord his God and did not transgress His commandment.

**7** So you too, if you do not transgress the commandment of God, God will do your will, be pleased with your word and keep your kingdom.

## The Censers

**8** The sons of Korah and Asaph transgressed the commandment of Moses and murmured against him, because he told them, "Set your souls right before God."

**9** They said to him, "Are we not the sons of Levi, who do the work of the priesthood in the holy tent?"

**10** They took their censers to burn incense, and went and burned incense; but God did not accept their prayer. By the fire of their own censers they were burned and melted like wax that melts in fire, and nothing of them was left, not their bones or their clothes, only the bronze censers, which they brought into the house of God at God's command; for He said, "Their censers have become holy by the burning of their flesh."

**11** Because of this God said to Moses and Aaron, "Gather their censers into the tent, and let them be a covering for My house and for all I have ordained for it, from outside to inside."

**12** He made good the covering of the holy tent: the mercy seat and the *ḫoṣādāt*, the cherubim and the basins,

**13** the bowls, the carrying poles and the *mawādəd*, the court and the screens of the tent, the dishes, and the sacrifices they offered in the holy tent:

**14** the freewill offering, the sacrifice of salvation, the vow offering, the offering for sin and the sacrifice by His law.

**15** All He had commanded Moses to do in the holy tent, Moses commanded them.

## The Dwelling of His Name

**16** They did not neglect to serve their God, so that His name would be praised among them in the tent of the law of the Lord their God, who promised to give them the inheritance of their fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey, which He swore to Abraham.

**17** He made a covenant with Isaac and established a testimony for Jacob,

**18** and for Moses and Aaron in the tent of witness,

**19** and for Samuel and Elijah in Jerusalem, in the tent in the temple, where Solomon built, until it became the house of God, the resting place of the name of the Most High and the dwelling of the Holy One of Israel.

**20** For it is the place of asking and of the forgiveness of sin, where He appears to His priests who walk in gentleness,

**21** where He hears the prayer of those who do His will,

**22** where the law of God, the Holy One of Israel, is ordained,

**23** and where sacrifice and incense are burned for a soothing aroma to God, the Holy One of Israel.

**24** He speaks above the mercy seat in the holy tent, and the glory of God appears to His chosen ones, the sons of Jacob, and His holy ones who walk in the commandment and the judgment of God.

**25** But those who reproach Him will be like the sons of Korah, whom the earth swallowed; so too the sinners are bound to be cast into the judgment of burning fire, where there is no way out forever.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "Have mercy, O God, on Your people… forgive them"** — Moses after the calf: *But now, if You will forgive their sin — but if not, please blot me out of Your book* (Exodus 32:32). *Those who reviled him, his brother among them*: Miriam and Aaron *spoke against Moses* (Numbers 12:1), and Moses prayed for Miriam (12:13).
- **v4 "he was known as gentle"** — *Now the man Moses was very meek, more than all people who were on the face of the earth* (Numbers 12:3).
- **v5 "made him like God to them"** — *you shall be as God to him* (Exodus 4:16); *I have made you like God to Pharaoh* (Exodus 7:1).
- **vv6, 8–10 Korah** — *the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them up, with their households… So they and all that belonged to them went down alive into Sheol* (Numbers 16:32–33); *fire came out from the LORD and consumed the 250 men offering the incense* (16:35). Numbers names Korah with Dathan and Abiram; the book names *Asaph*, a Levite singer of David's time (1 Chronicles 6:39).
- **vv10–11 "Their censers have become holy… let them be a covering"** — *the censers of these men who have sinned at the cost of their lives. Let them be made into hammered plates as a covering for the altar, for they offered them before the LORD, and they became holy* (Numbers 16:38).
- **vv12–13 the furnishings** — *ḫoṣādāt* (as in 4:19) and *mawādəd* are not otherwise known.
- **v24 "He speaks above the mercy seat"** — *There I will meet with you, and from above the mercy seat, from between the two cherubim… I will speak with you* (Exodus 25:22).

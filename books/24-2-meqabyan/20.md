# 2 Meqabyan 20

## Without Provisions

**1** Believe me, none of the deeds we have done on the earth will be left out or hidden when we stand before Him in fear and trembling,

**2** when we have not filled ourselves with provisions for our journey and have no clothing for our nakedness,

**3** when we have no staff for our hands and no sandals for our feet,

**4** and we do not know the road to where they are taking us, whether stumbling or rough places, darkness or thorns and thistles, the depth of the sea or the depth of Sheol;

**5** and we do not know those who take us and do not understand their speech,

**6** and we do not see their faces, for they are darkness, and into darkness they lead us.

**7** As David the prophet said, "When my spirit fainted within me, You, O Lord, knew my path. On the way where I walked they hid a snare for me. I turned to my right hand and looked, and there was no one who knew me, and there was nowhere for me to flee."

**8** He says this because he knows that the demons mock him and lead him where he does not know the way; he turns right and left and finds no one who knows him.

**9** There is no one who knows him either, but he is alone among the angels of darkness.

## The Angels of Light and the Angels of Darkness

**10** The spiritual angels of light are sent to the good, to receive the souls of the righteous and carry them to the court of light, of life;

**11** and the angels of darkness and the demons are sent to the souls of sinners, to receive them and carry them into the eternal judgment prepared for them, so that they may be repaid according to the deeds they did.

**12** Woe to the souls of sinners, whom they carry into perdition, where there is no rest and no salvation, no escape and no way out forever from the distress that has found them!

**13** Woe to the sinners, for they walked in the way of Cain and perished for the reward of Balaam's wrongdoing, and found none to help them; for by taking bribes they went down, making excuses to take others' goods by wrongdoing, goods that were not theirs.

**14** They will be repaid according to the deeds they did, in the judgment of burning fire.

## Notes

- **v7 "When my spirit fainted within me, You knew my path"** — *When my spirit faints within me, You know my way! In the path where I walk they have hidden a trap for me. Look to the right and see: there is none who takes notice of me; no refuge remains to me* (Psalm 142:3–4).
- **v10 "angels of light… to receive the souls of the righteous"** — *The poor man died and was carried by the angels to Abraham's side* (Luke 16:22).
- **v13 "the way of Cain… the reward of Balaam's wrongdoing"** — *Woe to them! For they walked in the way of Cain and abandoned themselves for the sake of gain to Balaam's error* (Jude 11); compare 10:5.

# Amos 7

## Locusts and Fire

**1** This is what the Lord GOD showed me: He was forming locusts when the late crop was beginning to come up, the late crop after the king's mowing.

**2** When they had finished eating the grass of the land, I said, "Lord GOD, forgive, please! How can Jacob stand? He is so small."

**3** The LORD relented concerning this. "It shall not be," said the LORD.

**4** This is what the Lord GOD showed me: the Lord GOD was calling to contend by fire, and it devoured the great deep and was devouring the portion.

**5** I said, "Lord GOD, stop, please! How can Jacob stand? He is so small."

**6** The LORD relented concerning this. "This too shall not be," said the Lord GOD.

## The Plumb Line

**7** This is what He showed me: the Lord was standing beside a wall made by a plumb line, with a plumb line in His hand.

**8** The LORD said to me, "What do you see, Amos?" I said, "A plumb line." Then the Lord said, "I am about to set a plumb line among My people Israel; I will never again pass them by.

**9** The high places of Isaac will be made desolate,  
and the sanctuaries of Israel laid waste,  
and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword."

## Amaziah the Priest of Bethel

**10** Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent word to Jeroboam king of Israel: "Amos has conspired against you among the house of Israel; the land cannot bear all his words.

**11** For this is what Amos says: 'Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel will surely go into exile from its land.'"

**12** Amaziah said to Amos, "Seer, go, run away to the land of Judah. Earn your bread there, and prophesy there.

**13** But never prophesy again at Bethel, for it is the king's sanctuary and a royal house."

**14** Amos answered Amaziah, "I was no prophet, nor a prophet's son; I was a herdsman and a tender of sycamore figs.

**15** But the LORD took me from following the flock, and the LORD said to me, 'Go, prophesy to My people Israel.'

**16** Now hear the word of the LORD. You say, 'Do not prophesy against Israel, and do not preach against the house of Isaac.'

**17** Therefore this is what the LORD says:  
'Your wife will become a prostitute in the city,  
and your sons and your daughters will fall by the sword,  
and your land will be divided up with a measuring line;  
you yourself will die on unclean ground,  
and Israel will surely go into exile from its land.'"

## Notes

- **v1 "the late crop after the king's mowing"** — *leqesh*, **the late crop**, and *gizzei ha-melekh*, **the king's mowing**, a phrase found only here: the cut taken for the king, and the locusts come for the growth after it. The Greek reads *gizzei* as a name: *one young locust, Gog the king*.
- **v4 "calling to contend by fire… devoured the great deep and was devouring the portion"** — *la-riv*, **to contend**, the word of a case at law (Hosea 4:1). *Tehom rabbah*, **the great deep**, is the deep of the flood, when *all the fountains of the great deep were split open* (Genesis 7:11): a fire that eats up the deep. *Ha-cheleq*, **the portion**, is the allotted land, each family's share.
- **vv7–8 "a wall made by a plumb line, with a plumb line in His hand… I will never again pass them by"** — *anakh*, four times in two verses and nowhere else. *Plumb line* follows the picture of a wall tested for true; the Greek has *adamas*, **a hard metal**: *a wall of adamant, and adamant in His hand*. *Lo osif od avor lo*, **I will never again pass them by**, is the verb of overlooking, *it is his glory to overlook an offense* (Proverbs 19:11). In 5:17 the LORD said *I will pass through among you*; now He will no longer pass them by.
- **v14 "I was no prophet, nor a prophet's son"** — *lo navi anokhi ve-lo ven navi anokhi*, with no verb: **not a prophet I, and not a prophet's son I**. It can be past, *I was no prophet* until the LORD took me (v15), as the Greek has it, *I was not a prophet*; or present, *I am no prophet*, refusing to be one who earns his bread by prophecy (v12). *A prophet's son* is one of the prophets' guilds, like *the sons of the prophets who were at Bethel* (2 Kings 2:3). *Boles shiqmim*, **a tender of sycamore figs**, is found only here; the sycamore grew in the lowland, *as plentiful as the sycamore trees of the lowland* (1 Kings 10:27). The Greek has *a goatherd*.

# Amos 1

## The LORD Roars from Zion

**1** The words of Amos, one of the sheep breeders from Tekoa: what he saw concerning Israel in the days of Uzziah king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.

**2** He said:  
The LORD roars from Zion  
and utters His voice from Jerusalem;  
the pastures of the shepherds mourn,  
and the top of Carmel dries up.

## Damascus

**3** This is what the LORD says:  
For three transgressions of Damascus,  
and for four, I will not turn it back,  
because they threshed Gilead  
with threshing sledges of iron.

**4** I will send fire on the house of Hazael,  
and it will devour the strongholds of Ben-hadad.

**5** I will break the bar of Damascus,  
and cut off the one enthroned from the Valley of Aven,  
and the one who holds the scepter from Beth-eden;  
and the people of Aram will go into exile to Kir,  
says the LORD.

## Gaza

**6** This is what the LORD says:  
For three transgressions of Gaza,  
and for four, I will not turn it back,  
because they took a whole people into exile  
to hand them over to Edom.

**7** I will send fire on the wall of Gaza,  
and it will devour her strongholds.

**8** I will cut off the one enthroned from Ashdod,  
and the one who holds the scepter from Ashkelon;  
I will turn My hand against Ekron,  
and the remnant of the Philistines will perish,  
says the Lord GOD.

## Tyre

**9** This is what the LORD says:  
For three transgressions of Tyre,  
and for four, I will not turn it back,  
because they handed over a whole people to Edom  
and did not remember the covenant of brothers.

**10** I will send fire on the wall of Tyre,  
and it will devour her strongholds.

## Edom

**11** This is what the LORD says:  
For three transgressions of Edom,  
and for four, I will not turn it back,  
because he pursued his brother with the sword  
and destroyed his compassion;  
his anger tore without end,  
and he kept his wrath forever.

**12** I will send fire on Teman,  
and it will devour the strongholds of Bozrah.

## Ammon

**13** This is what the LORD says:  
For three transgressions of the Ammonites,  
and for four, I will not turn it back,  
because they ripped open the pregnant women of Gilead  
to enlarge their territory.

**14** I will kindle a fire on the wall of Rabbah,  
and it will devour her strongholds,  
with a war cry on the day of battle,  
with a storm on the day of the whirlwind.

**15** Their king will go into exile,  
he and his princes together,  
says the LORD.

## Notes

- **v1 "one of the sheep breeders from Tekoa… two years before the earthquake"** — *noqedim*, the word used of a king: *Now Mesha king of Moab was a sheep breeder* (2 Kings 3:4). Tekoa was a town of Judah, one of the cities Rehoboam built *for defense in Judah* (2 Chronicles 11:5–6), but the words concern Israel, the north. The kings are those of Hosea 1:1, Uzziah of Judah and *Jeroboam son of Joash*. The earthquake is remembered again in Zechariah 14:5. The Greek reads *noqedim* as a place, *in Akkarim in Thekoue*, and has *concerning Jerusalem*.
- **v2 "The LORD roars from Zion"** — *yish'ag*, the roar of a lion. In Hosea 11:10 *He will roar like a lion* to call His sons home; here the roar dries up the land. Jeremiah has the same picture in his own words, *The LORD will roar from on high, and utter His voice from His holy dwelling* (Jeremiah 25:30), and Joel has the two lines word for word (Joel 3:16). *Avlu*, **mourn**, is said of land as well as people, as in *How long will the land mourn, and the grass of every field wither?* (Jeremiah 12:4).
- **v3 "For three transgressions of Damascus, and for four, I will not turn it back"** — the refrain of eight oracles, word for word each time (1:3, 6, 9, 11, 13; 2:1, 4, 6). Three, then four, is a way of counting to the full number, as in *In six troubles He will deliver you; in seven no evil will touch you* (Job 5:19); yet for Damascus, Gaza, Ammon and Moab the charge that follows names a single deed. *Pesha*, **transgression**, is rebellion, the verb of *Moab rebelled against Israel* (2 Kings 1:1). *Lo ashivennu*, **I will not turn it back**: *it* is never named. The words are Balaam's of a blessing, *He has blessed, and I cannot turn it back* (Numbers 23:20). The Greek has *I will not turn away from him*.
- **vv3–4 "they threshed Gilead with threshing sledges of iron… the house of Hazael… Ben-hadad"** — Hazael of Damascus *struck them throughout all the territory of Israel, from the Jordan eastward, all the land of Gilead* (2 Kings 10:32–33), and left Israel's army *like the dust at threshing* (2 Kings 13:7); Ben-hadad was his son. The Greek reads the crime as *they sawed with iron saws the pregnant women of Gilead*. *I will send fire on… and it will devour… strongholds* is the line Hosea uses of Judah's cities (Hosea 8:14).
- **v5 "the Valley of Aven… Beth-eden… Kir"** — *Aven* is **wickedness**, the word of Beth-aven (Hosea 4:15); *Eden* is **delight**. *Yoshev*, **the one enthroned**, is the one who sits: an inhabitant, or a king on his throne, as in *He who sits in the heavens* (Psalm 2:4); the next line has the scepter. Kir is where the king of Assyria *carried its people into exile* when he took Damascus (2 Kings 16:9).
- **vv6, 9 "a whole people… to hand them over to Edom"** — *galut shelemah*, **a whole exile**, a people taken entire and sold on. The Greek reads *shelemah* as *Shelomoh*: *the captivity of Solomon*. Gaza took them captive (v6); Tyre handed them over (v9).
- **v8 "Ashdod… Ashkelon… Ekron"** — with Gaza, four of the five Philistine cities of 1 Samuel 6:17; Gath is not named. *I will turn My hand against* is Isaiah's *I will turn My hand against you* (Isaiah 1:25). *The Lord GOD*, *Adonai YHWH*, closes this oracle; Damascus, Ammon and Moab close with *says the LORD*.
- **v9 "did not remember the covenant of brothers"** — *berit achim*. Hiram of Tyre and Solomon *made a covenant* (1 Kings 5:12), and Hiram called Solomon *my brother* (1 Kings 9:13).
- **v11 "he pursued his brother with the sword and destroyed his compassion"** — Edom is Esau's people, and the law calls him brother: *A message from your brother Israel* (Numbers 20:14); *You shall not detest an Edomite, for he is your brother* (Deuteronomy 23:7). *Rachamav*, **his compassion**, is from *rechem*, **womb**: the feeling of those born of one womb, and these two were twins, *Two nations are in your belly* (Genesis 25:23). *Va-yitrof*, **tore**, is the verb of a beast with its prey, *Torn, Joseph is torn* (Genesis 37:33). Teman and Bozrah are named with Edom in Jeremiah (49:7, 13).
- **vv13–15 "ripped open the pregnant women of Gilead… Their king will go into exile"** — the deed Elisha foresaw for Hazael, *rip open their pregnant women* (2 Kings 8:12). *Sufah*, **whirlwind**, is the word of *they will reap the whirlwind* (Hosea 8:7). *Malkam*, **their king**: Jeremiah, speaking against Ammon in nearly the same words, has *for Milcom will go into exile, his priests and his princes together* (Jeremiah 49:3), Milcom being *the god of the sons of Ammon* (1 Kings 11:33). Here the Hebrew names no priests, only princes, and the Greek has *their kings… their priests and their princes*.

# Amos 8

## A Basket of Summer Fruit

**1** This is what the Lord GOD showed me: a basket of summer fruit.

**2** He said, "What do you see, Amos?" I said, "A basket of summer fruit." Then the LORD said to me, "The end has come upon My people Israel; I will never again pass them by.

**3** The songs of the temple will turn to howling on that day,  
declares the Lord GOD.  
So many corpses!  
They are flung out everywhere.  
Silence!"

## When Will the Sabbath Be Over?

**4** Hear this, you who trample the needy  
and put an end to the poor of the land,

**5** saying, "When will the new moon be over,  
so that we may sell grain,  
and the sabbath,  
so that we may open up the grain,  
making the ephah small and the shekel great,  
and cheating with false scales,

**6** buying the poor for silver  
and the needy for a pair of sandals,  
and selling the chaff of the grain?"

**7** The LORD has sworn by the pride of Jacob:  
I will never forget any of their deeds.

**8** Shall not the land tremble for this,  
and everyone who lives in it mourn?  
All of it will rise like the Nile,  
be tossed about, and sink again,  
like the Nile of Egypt.

## A Famine of the Word

**9** And it shall come to pass on that day,  
declares the Lord GOD,  
that I will make the sun go down at noon  
and darken the earth in broad daylight.

**10** I will turn your feasts into mourning  
and all your songs into a lament;  
I will put sackcloth on every waist  
and baldness on every head;  
I will make it like the mourning for an only son,  
and its end like a bitter day.

**11** Days are coming,  
declares the Lord GOD,  
when I will send a famine on the land,  
not a famine for bread or a thirst for water,  
but for hearing the words of the LORD.

**12** They will stagger from sea to sea  
and from the north to the east;  
they will roam about to seek the word of the LORD,  
and they will not find it.

**13** On that day the fair young women  
and the young men will faint for thirst,

**14** those who swear by the guilt of Samaria,  
and say, "As your god lives, Dan,"  
and, "As the way of Beersheba lives";  
they will fall and never rise again.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "a basket of summer fruit… The end has come"** — *kelub qayits*, and the answer, *ba ha-qets*: *qayits*, the fruit of the last harvest, and *qets*, **end**, sound nearly alike. Jeremiah's first vision turns the same way, on *a branch of an almond tree*, *shaqed*, and *I am watching*, *shoqed* (Jeremiah 1:11–12), and Ezekiel cries *An end! The end has come* (Ezekiel 7:2). *Kelub*, **basket**, is the *cage* of Jeremiah 5:27, *Like a cage full of birds*. *I will never again pass them by* repeats 7:8.
- **v3 "The songs of the temple will turn to howling… Silence!"** — *shirot heikhal*, **the songs of the temple**; with other vowels the same letters are **the singing women of the palace**, and *heikhal* is both temple and palace. The Greek has *the ceiling panels of the temple will wail*. *Has*, **Silence!**, is the hush of 6:10, among the dead.
- **vv4–5 "put an end to the poor… When will the new moon be over… and the sabbath"** — *lashbit*, **put an end to**, is from *shavat*, the root of *shabbat*: they would make the poor cease, and cannot wait for the sabbath to cease. *Ha-sho'afim*, **you who trample**, is the word of 2:7. *Sell grain… open up the grain* are the verbs of Joseph in the famine, who *opened all that was in them and sold grain* (Genesis 41:56); these sellers open to cheat.
- **vv5–6 "the ephah small and the shekel great… a pair of sandals"** — a small measure for the grain and a heavy weight for the buyer's silver: the *two kinds of weights, a large and a small* of the law (Deuteronomy 25:13), and the *false scales* of Hosea 12:7. In 2:6 they *sell the righteous for silver, and the needy for a pair of sandals*; here they buy them at the same price.
- **v7 "The LORD has sworn by the pride of Jacob"** — *bi-g'on Ya'aqov*, the words of 6:8, where He swore by Himself, *I abhor the pride of Jacob*. Here He swears by it: by the pride He will not forget, or by Himself under a name, as Samuel calls Him *the Enduring One of Israel* (1 Samuel 15:29) and as He swore to Abraham, *By Myself I have sworn* (Genesis 22:16).
- **v8 "rise like the Nile… and sink again, like the Nile of Egypt"** — the Nile rises over its land each year and falls again; the whole land will heave like it. *Ka-or*, **like the Nile**, is written without the *y* of *ye'or*; 9:5 gives the same line with the word in full. The read form, *ve-nishqe'ah*, **and sink**, is followed.
- **vv9–10 "the sun go down at noon… like the mourning for an only son"** — the law's curse, *You will grope at noon as the blind man gropes in darkness* (Deuteronomy 28:29), and Jeremiah's mother of seven, *her sun has gone down while it was still day* (Jeremiah 15:9): the day of the LORD as *darkness, and not light* (5:18). *Evel yachid*, **the mourning for an only son**, is Jeremiah's *make mourning as for an only son, a most bitter lament* (Jeremiah 6:26). The Greek has *the mourning for a beloved one*, its word for Isaac in *your son — your beloved one* (Genesis 22:2). *Baldness* is the mourner's shaved head the law forbade, *a bald patch between your eyes for the dead* (Deuteronomy 14:1).
- **vv11–12 "a famine… for hearing the words of the LORD… They will stagger"** — the law said *a person does not live on bread alone, but on everything that comes out of the mouth of the LORD* (Deuteronomy 8:3), and in Eli's days *The word of the LORD was rare* (1 Samuel 3:1). *Na'u*, **stagger**, is the verb of 4:8, where cities staggered to one city for water; *yeshotetu*, **roam about**, is Jeremiah's *Roam through the streets of Jerusalem* (Jeremiah 5:1).
- **v14 "the guilt of Samaria… As your god lives, Dan… the way of Beersheba"** — *ashmat Shomron*, **the guilt of Samaria**, its calf, as Hosea says, *Your calf is rejected, Samaria* (Hosea 8:5). *Ashima*, the god the men of Hamath made in Samaria's towns after its fall (2 Kings 17:30), has nearly the same letters; the Greek has *the propitiation of Samaria*. The other calf stood at Dan: *He set the one in Bethel, and the other he put in Dan* (1 Kings 12:29). *Derekh*, **the way**, is the pilgrim road to Beersheba (5:5); the Greek has *As your god lives, Beersheba*. *They will fall and never rise again* is the lament of 5:2.

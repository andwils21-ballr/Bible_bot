# Amos 3

## Only You Have I Known

**1** Hear this word that the LORD has spoken against you, sons of Israel, against the whole family that I brought up from the land of Egypt:

**2** Only you have I known  
of all the families of the ground;  
therefore I will punish you  
for all your iniquities.

## The Lion Has Roared

**3** Do two walk together  
unless they have agreed to meet?

**4** Does a lion roar in the forest  
when it has no prey?  
Does a young lion utter its voice from its den  
unless it has caught something?

**5** Does a bird fall into a trap on the ground  
when there is no lure for it?  
Does a trap spring up from the ground  
and catch nothing?

**6** If a horn is blown in a city,  
do the people not tremble?  
If disaster comes to a city,  
has the LORD not done it?

**7** For the Lord GOD does nothing  
unless He has revealed His counsel  
to His servants the prophets.

**8** The lion has roared;  
who will not fear?  
The Lord GOD has spoken;  
who will not prophesy?

## The Mountains of Samaria

**9** Proclaim it over the strongholds in Ashdod  
and over the strongholds in the land of Egypt,  
and say: Gather on the mountains of Samaria,  
and see the great tumults in her  
and the oppressions within her.

**10** They do not know how to do what is right,  
declares the LORD,  
those who store up violence and destruction  
in their strongholds.

**11** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says:  
An enemy will surround the land;  
he will bring down your strength from you,  
and your strongholds will be plundered.

**12** This is what the LORD says:  
As the shepherd rescues from the lion's mouth  
two legs or a piece of an ear,  
so the sons of Israel who live in Samaria will be rescued,  
with the corner of a bed  
and a piece of a couch.

**13** Hear, and testify against the house of Jacob,  
declares the Lord GOD, the God of hosts:

**14** On the day I punish Israel for its transgressions,  
I will punish the altars of Bethel;  
the horns of the altar will be cut off  
and fall to the ground.

**15** I will strike the winter house  
together with the summer house;  
the houses of ivory will perish,  
and the many houses will come to an end,  
declares the LORD.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "the whole family that I brought up… Only you have I known of all the families of the ground"** — *mishpechot ha-adamah*, the words of the promise to Abraham, *In you all the families of the ground shall be blessed* (Genesis 12:3). *Yada'ti*, **I have known**, is the knowing of a choice, as of Abraham, *For I have known him, so that he may charge his sons* (Genesis 18:19). The Hebrew puts *only you* first, and draws from it the opposite of a privilege: *therefore I will punish you*. The verse begins *the LORD has spoken* and turns, without a break, into the LORD's own *I brought up*.
- **vv3–6 "Do two walk together unless they have agreed to meet?"** — seven questions, each with an answer everyone knows, before the answer that matters in vv7–8. *No'adu*, **agreed to meet**, is the verb of *I will meet with you there* (Exodus 25:22), the root of the tent of meeting; the Greek has *unless they know each other*. The two who walk together are not named.
- **v4 "Does a lion roar… Does a young lion utter its voice"** — the words of the book's first oracle, *The LORD roars from Zion and utters His voice from Jerusalem* (1:2). The riddle is answered in v8: *The lion has roared*. *Kefir*, **young lion**, is Hosea's word as well (Hosea 5:14).
- **v6 "If disaster comes to a city, has the LORD not done it?"** — *ra'ah*, **disaster**, the word of Isaiah's *I make peace and create disaster* (Isaiah 45:7). The horn is the alarm Hosea sounds too, *Blow the horn in Gibeah* (Hosea 5:8).
- **v7 "His counsel to His servants the prophets"** — *sod*, the counsel of a closed circle, the word of Jeremiah's *who has stood in the council of the LORD, to see and to hear His word?* (Jeremiah 23:18). *His servants the prophets* is the phrase of *I have sent you all My servants the prophets* (Jeremiah 7:25). The Greek has *instruction* for *sod*.
- **vv9–10 "the strongholds in Ashdod… store up violence and destruction in their strongholds"** — *armenot*, the strongholds the fire devoured in every oracle of 1:4–2:5, three times here: the strongholds of the nations are called to look at the strongholds of Samaria. The Greek has *Assyria* for *Ashdod*, the pair that Hosea gives, Egypt and Assyria (Hosea 7:11). *Chamas va-shod*, **violence and destruction**, is Jeremiah's cry, *violence and destruction are heard in her* (Jeremiah 6:7). *Ha-otsrim*, **those who store up**, is the verb of a treasury.
- **v11 "An enemy will surround the land"** — *tsar u-sevivh ha-arets*, word for word **an enemy, and around the land**, with no verb. The Greek reads *tsar* as *Tsor*, **Tyre**: *Tyre! your land will be laid waste all around*.
- **v12 "As the shepherd rescues from the lion's mouth two legs or a piece of an ear"** — the shepherd's law: *If it was torn by a beast, he shall bring it as evidence* (Exodus 22:13), as Jacob told Laban, *Torn flesh I did not bring to you* (Genesis 31:39). What the shepherd rescues proves the sheep is dead. *U-vi-dmeshek arash*, **and a piece of a couch**: *demeshek* is found only here, spelled with the letters of Damascus but with other vowels and a *shin* where Damascus has a *sin* (1:3; 5:27), and its meaning is not known; the line beside it, *the corner of a bed*, asks for a part of a couch. The Greek reads it as the city: *in Damascus*, which Jeroboam had *restored… to Judah in Israel* (2 Kings 14:28).
- **v14 "I will punish the altars of Bethel; the horns of the altar will be cut off"** — the altar of Bethel was condemned before, when *a man of God came out of Judah by the word of the LORD to Bethel*: *the altar shall be torn apart* (1 Kings 13:1, 3). Amos too is from Judah (1:1). The horns of the altar were the place of refuge, where Adonijah *took hold of the horns of the altar* (1 Kings 1:50); cut off, they leave none.
- **v15 "the winter house… the houses of ivory… the many houses"** — kings kept both: *The king was sitting in the winter house* (Jeremiah 36:22), and Ahab built *the ivory house* (1 Kings 22:39). *Battim rabbim*, **many houses**, is Isaiah's oath, *Surely many houses shall be desolate, large and fine ones* (Isaiah 5:9).

# Amos 5

## A Lament for the House of Israel

**1** Hear this word, which I take up over you as a lament, house of Israel:

**2** Fallen, no more to rise,  
is virgin Israel;  
forsaken on her own ground,  
with no one to raise her up.

**3** For this is what the Lord GOD says:  
The city that went out a thousand strong  
will have a hundred left,  
and the one that went out a hundred strong  
will have ten left,  
for the house of Israel.

## Seek Me and Live

**4** For this is what the LORD says to the house of Israel:  
Seek Me and live.

**5** Do not seek Bethel,  
do not go to Gilgal,  
and do not cross over to Beersheba;  
for Gilgal will surely go into exile,  
and Bethel will come to nothing.

**6** Seek the LORD and live,  
or He will rush like fire on the house of Joseph,  
and it will devour,  
with no one to quench it for Bethel.

**7** You who turn justice into wormwood  
and throw righteousness down to the ground!

**8** He who made the Pleiades and Orion,  
who turns the shadow of death into morning  
and darkens day into night,  
who calls for the waters of the sea  
and pours them out on the face of the earth:  
the LORD is His name;

**9** who makes destruction flash out against the strong,  
so that destruction comes upon the fortress.

**10** They hate the one who reproves at the gate,  
and they detest the one who speaks with integrity.

**11** Therefore, because you trample on the poor  
and take a levy of grain from him,  
you have built houses of dressed stone,  
but you will not live in them;  
you have planted pleasant vineyards,  
but you will not drink their wine.

**12** For I know how many are your transgressions  
and how mighty your sins,  
you who afflict the righteous, who take a bribe,  
and turn aside the needy at the gate.

**13** Therefore the prudent will keep silent at that time,  
for it is an evil time.

**14** Seek good and not evil,  
so that you may live;  
and so the LORD, the God of hosts, will be with you,  
as you have said.

**15** Hate evil and love good,  
and establish justice at the gate;  
perhaps the LORD, the God of hosts,  
will be gracious to the remnant of Joseph.

**16** Therefore this is what the LORD, the God of hosts, the Lord, says:  
In all the squares there will be wailing,  
and in all the streets they will say, "Alas! Alas!"  
They will call the farmer to mourning,  
and those skilled in lament to wailing.

**17** In all the vineyards there will be wailing,  
for I will pass through among you,  
says the LORD.

## The Day of the LORD

**18** Woe to you who long for the day of the LORD!  
What will the day of the LORD be to you?  
It is darkness, and not light,

**19** as when a man flees from a lion,  
and a bear meets him;  
he goes into the house and leans his hand against the wall,  
and a snake bites him.

**20** Is not the day of the LORD darkness, and not light,  
gloom with no brightness in it?

**21** I hate, I reject your feasts,  
and I take no pleasure in your sacred assemblies.

**22** Even if you offer Me burnt offerings  
and your grain offerings,  
I will not accept them;  
I will not look at the peace offerings  
of your fattened animals.

**23** Take away from Me the noise of your songs;  
I will not listen to the music of your harps.

**24** But let justice roll down like waters,  
and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream.

**25** Did you bring Me sacrifices and offerings  
in the wilderness forty years, house of Israel?

**26** You will take up Sikkuth your king  
and Kiyyun your images,  
the star of your god,  
which you made for yourselves,

**27** and I will send you into exile beyond Damascus,  
says the LORD, whose name is the God of hosts.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "a lament… Fallen, no more to rise, is virgin Israel… ten left"** — *qinah*, the funeral song, as *David sang this lament over Saul* (2 Samuel 1:17); here it is sung over a nation still living. Jeremiah answers it: *Again I will build you, and you will be built, virgin Israel* (Jeremiah 31:4). A city loses nine in ten, as Isaiah heard of a land where *a tenth remains in it* (Isaiah 6:13).
- **vv4–6 "Seek Me and live… Gilgal will surely go into exile, and Bethel will come to nothing"** — *dirshuni*, **seek Me**, the verb of Moses' promise, *you will seek the LORD your God, and you will find Him* (Deuteronomy 4:29). *Ha-Gilgal galoh yigleh*: the name of the town sounds out its sentence. *Beit-El yihyeh le-aven*: the house of God becomes *aven*, **nothing**, **wickedness**, the word Hosea uses to call it Beth-aven (Hosea 4:15). Beersheba lay in the south, *Beersheba, which belongs to Judah* (1 Kings 19:3), where Abraham *called on the name of the LORD* (Genesis 21:33) and Isaac built an altar (Genesis 26:25). *Yitslach*, **rush**, is the verb of *The Spirit of the LORD rushed on him* (Judges 14:6), and *with no one to quench it* is Jeremiah's *burn with no one to quench it* (Jeremiah 4:4).
- **vv7–9 "You who turn justice into wormwood… who turns the shadow of death into morning"** — one verb, *hafakh*, **turn**, the verb of the overthrow of Sodom (4:11): they turn justice into *la'anah*, **wormwood**, the bitter plant of *a root bearing poison and wormwood* (Deuteronomy 29:18); the LORD turns *tsalmavet*, the dark of *the valley of the shadow of death* (Psalm 23:4), into morning. The hymn breaks into the charge between v7 and v10. *Kimah u-khesil*, **the Pleiades and Orion**, are the stars of Job, *He made the Bear and Orion, the Pleiades* (Job 9:9). *Ha-mavlig*, **who makes flash**, is uncertain; the Greek has *who apportions ruin*.
- **vv10–11 "the one who reproves at the gate… you will not live in them"** — the gate was the court. Isaiah too condemns those who *lay a snare for the one who reproves at the gate* (Isaiah 29:21). *Tamim*, **integrity**, is the word for an offering *without defect*: speech that is whole. *Boshaskhem*, **you trample**, is found only here; the Greek has *you struck the poor with your fists*. The sentence is the law's curse: *You will build a house and not live in it* (Deuteronomy 28:30), and *you will not drink the wine* (28:39). *Dressed stone* is the boast of *we will build with dressed stone* (Isaiah 9:10).
- **v12 "who take a bribe, and turn aside the needy at the gate"** — *kofer*, a **ransom**, the price paid for a life: *You shall not take a ransom for the life of a murderer* (Numbers 35:31), and the bribe Samuel swore he never took, *From whose hand have I taken a bribe* (1 Samuel 12:3). *Turn aside* is the law's word, *You shall not turn aside the judgment of your needy in his dispute* (Exodus 23:6), and the verb of 2:7, *turn aside the way of the humble*.
- **vv14–15 "the LORD… will be with you, as you have said… perhaps"** — they say *the LORD is with us*, the words of Joshua and Caleb (Numbers 14:9), which Gideon threw back, *if the LORD is with us, why has all this found us?* (Judges 6:13). *Hate evil and love good* is the psalm's *You who love the LORD, hate evil!* (Psalm 97:10). *Ulay*, **perhaps**, is all the hope offered, Moses' word on the mountain, *Perhaps I can make atonement for your sin* (Exodus 32:30).
- **vv16–17 "Alas! Alas!… for I will pass through among you"** — *ho ho*, the cry of mourners. The skilled mourners are Jeremiah's too, *send for the skilled women to come* (Jeremiah 9:17), and the farmer is called from the field to mourn. *E'evor be-qirbekha*, **I will pass through among you**, is the word of the first Passover, *I will pass through the land of Egypt that night* (Exodus 12:12): this time Israel is the land He passes through.
- **vv18–20 "Woe to you who long for the day of the LORD… darkness, and not light"** — *hoy*, **woe**, is the cry over the dead, *Alas, my brother!* (1 Kings 13:30), as *ho ho* is in v16. The day they long for is turned against them, as in Isaiah, *the LORD of hosts has a day against all that is proud* (Isaiah 2:12). One man meets three beasts: the lion, the bear, and the snake inside his own house.
- **vv21–24 "I take no pleasure in your sacred assemblies… let justice roll down like waters"** — *lo ariach*, **I will not smell**, the word of the law's warning, *I will not smell your soothing aroma* (Leviticus 26:31). *Yiggal*, **roll**, is from *galal*, the verb that named Gilgal, *Today I have rolled away the disgrace of Egypt from you* (Joshua 5:9). *Nachal eitan*, **an ever-flowing stream**, is a wadi that does not run dry, *a valley with a flowing stream* (Deuteronomy 21:4).
- **vv25–27 "Did you bring Me sacrifices… forty years… You will take up Sikkuth your king and Kiyyun"** — Jeremiah asks the same, *I did not speak to your fathers… about burnt offerings and sacrifices* (Jeremiah 7:22). As pointed, *Sikkut* and *Kiyyun* are names found nowhere else, and both carry the vowels of *shiqquts*, **loathsome**; *Kiyyun* stands beside *the star of your god*. The Greek has *the tent of Moloch and the star of your god Raiphan*, the words Stephen quotes (Acts 7:42–43); *sukkat*, **the tent of**, has the letters of *Sikkut*. *U-nesatem*, **you will take up**, runs on into v27, *and I will send you into exile*; the Greek has *you took up*, in the wilderness.

# Amos 9

## The Lord Beside the Altar

**1** I saw the Lord standing beside the altar, and He said:  
Strike the capitals so that the thresholds shake,  
and shatter them on the heads of them all;  
the last of them I will kill with the sword.  
Not one of them will get away;  
not one of them will escape.

**2** If they dig down into Sheol,  
from there My hand will take them;  
if they climb up to the heavens,  
from there I will bring them down.

**3** If they hide on the top of Carmel,  
from there I will search them out and take them;  
if they hide from My eyes on the floor of the sea,  
from there I will command the serpent, and it will bite them.

**4** If they go into captivity before their enemies,  
from there I will command the sword, and it will kill them;  
I will set My eyes on them  
for harm and not for good.

**5** The Lord GOD of hosts,  
He who touches the earth and it melts,  
and all who live in it mourn;  
all of it rises like the Nile  
and sinks like the Nile of Egypt;

**6** who builds His upper rooms in the heavens  
and founds His vault upon the earth;  
who calls for the waters of the sea  
and pours them out on the face of the earth:  
the LORD is His name.

## The Sinful Kingdom

**7** Are you not like the Cushites to Me,  
sons of Israel?  
declares the LORD.  
Did I not bring Israel up from the land of Egypt,  
and the Philistines from Caphtor,  
and the Arameans from Kir?

**8** The eyes of the Lord GOD are on the sinful kingdom,  
and I will destroy it from the face of the ground;  
except that I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob,  
declares the LORD.

**9** For I am about to command,  
and I will shake the house of Israel among all the nations  
as one shakes with a sieve,  
and not a pebble will fall to the ground.

**10** All the sinners of My people will die by the sword,  
those who say,  
"Disaster will not come near us or meet us."

## I Will Raise It Up

**11** On that day I will raise up  
the fallen booth of David,  
and wall up its breaches,  
and raise up its ruins,  
and rebuild it as in the days of old,

**12** so that they may possess the remnant of Edom  
and all the nations over whom My name is called,  
declares the LORD, who does this.

**13** Days are coming,  
declares the LORD,  
when the plowman will overtake the reaper,  
and the one who treads grapes the one who sows the seed;  
the mountains will drip sweet wine,  
and all the hills will melt.

**14** I will restore the fortunes of My people Israel;  
they will rebuild the ruined cities and live in them;  
they will plant vineyards and drink their wine;  
they will make gardens and eat their fruit.

**15** I will plant them on their land,  
and they will never again be uprooted  
from the land I have given them,  
says the LORD your God.

## Notes

- **v1 "I saw the Lord standing beside the altar… Strike the capitals"** — the fifth vision, and the only one that does not begin *This is what… showed me* (7:1, 4, 7; 8:1), and here Amos does not plead. Who is told to strike is not said. *Kaftor*, **capital**, the top of a pillar, is the word of the lampstand's *bulbs* (Exodus 25:31) and the name of *Caphtor* in v7. The altar is not named; 3:14 named *the altars of Bethel*. The Greek has *Strike the mercy seat*.
- **vv2–4 "If they dig down into Sheol… for harm and not for good"** — the psalm's comfort turned around: *If I go up to the heavens, You are there; if I make my bed in Sheol, You are there* (Psalm 139:8). The serpent is the one that bit the man in his own house (5:19). *I will set My eyes on them for harm and not for good* is the reverse of Jeremiah's promise, *I will set My eyes on them for good… I will plant them and not uproot them* (Jeremiah 24:6), and Jeremiah has the threat too, *I will watch over them for harm and not for good* (Jeremiah 44:27).
- **vv5–6 "He who touches the earth and it melts… the LORD is His name"** — the hymn takes up two lines already heard: the earth rising and sinking *like the Nile* (8:8), and *who calls for the waters of the sea and pours them out on the face of the earth* (5:8). *Aguddato*, **His vault**, is uncertain: *agudah* is a thing bound together, *a bunch of hyssop* (Exodus 12:22); the Greek has *His promise*.
- **v7 "Are you not like the Cushites to Me… the Philistines from Caphtor, and the Arameans from Kir?"** — Cush is the land of Genesis 2:13 and of Moses' wife, *the Cushite woman he had married* (Numbers 12:1); the Greek has *the sons of the Ethiopians*. The Philistines are *the Caphtorim, who came out of Caphtor* (Deuteronomy 2:23), and the Arameans, brought from Kir, are sent back to it (1:5). The exodus, the ground of *Only you have I known* (3:2), is set beside the LORD's other moves of peoples.
- **v8 "except that I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob"** — *efes ki*, **except that**, and *hashmed ashmid*, the verb doubled, **utterly destroy**. After the *perhaps* of 5:15, *perhaps the LORD… will be gracious to the remnant of Joseph*, the line turns: the kingdom will go, and the house of Jacob will not go with it entirely.
- **vv9–10 "as one shakes with a sieve, and not a pebble will fall… Disaster will not come near us"** — *kevarah*, **sieve**, is found only here. *Tseror* is a **pebble**, as in *not even a pebble is found there* (2 Samuel 17:13), and also a **bundle**, as in *the bundle of the living* (1 Samuel 25:29): the sieve holds back every stone, or lets no grain of the people fall lost; the Greek has *no fragment will fall*. *Taggish*, **come near**, is the verb of 6:3: they *bring near the seat of violence* and say disaster will not come near.
- **v11 "the fallen booth of David"** — *sukkat David ha-nofelet*. *Sukkah* is a shelter of branches, Isaiah's *shelter in a vineyard* (Isaiah 1:8), the booths of the feast (Leviticus 23:42); *sukkat* has the letters of *Sikkuth* in 5:26. *Ha-nofelet… aqim*, **fallen… I will raise up**, answers the lament, *Fallen, no more to rise, is virgin Israel… with no one to raise her up* (5:2). The Hebrew's pronouns change three times: *their* breaches, *his* ruins, *rebuild it*. The Greek has *the tent of David*.
- **v12 "so that they may possess the remnant of Edom and all the nations over whom My name is called"** — *over whom My name is called* is the law's *the name of the LORD is called over you* (Deuteronomy 28:10). The Greek reads differently: *so that the rest of mankind may seek, and all the nations over whom My name is called*. *Edom* and *adam*, **mankind**, have nearly the same letters, as do *yirshu*, **possess**, and *yidreshu*, **seek**. James quotes the Greek at the gathering in Jerusalem, of the nations coming in (Acts 15:16–17). The Hebrew, the text here, has Edom, the brother of 1:11.
- **v13 "the plowman will overtake the reaper… the mountains will drip sweet wine"** — the blessing of the law, *Your threshing will last until the grape harvest, and the grape harvest until the sowing* (Leviticus 26:5): one harvest runs into the next. Joel has the dripping mountains as well (Joel 3:18). *Titmogagnah*, **will melt**, is the verb of v5, where the earth melted at His touch.
- **vv14–15 "rebuild the ruined cities and live in them… drink their wine… says the LORD your God"** — 5:11 undone: *you have built houses of dressed stone, but you will not live in them… but you will not drink their wine*. *Shavti et shevut*, **I will restore the fortunes**, is Jeremiah's *I will restore your fortunes* (Jeremiah 29:14), and *I will plant them… never again be uprooted* is his *I will plant them and not uproot them* (Jeremiah 24:6). The book's last words are *Elohekha*, **your God**, as in *prepare to meet your God* (4:12).

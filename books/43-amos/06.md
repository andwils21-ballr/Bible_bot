# Amos 6

## Woe to Those at Ease

**1** Woe to those at ease in Zion,  
and to those who feel secure on the mountain of Samaria,  
the notable men of the first among the nations,  
to whom the house of Israel comes!

**2** Cross over to Calneh and see;  
go from there to Hamath the great;  
then go down to Gath of the Philistines.  
Are they better than these kingdoms?  
Is their territory greater than your territory?

**3** You who put far off the day of disaster  
and bring near the seat of violence,

**4** who lie on beds of ivory  
and sprawl on their couches,  
and eat lambs from the flock  
and calves from the fattening stall,

**5** who strum to the sound of the harp,  
and like David devise for themselves instruments of song,

**6** who drink wine from basins  
and anoint themselves with the finest oils,  
but are not grieved over the ruin of Joseph!

**7** Therefore they will now go into exile  
at the head of the exiles,  
and the feasting of those who sprawl will pass away.

## I Abhor the Pride of Jacob

**8** The Lord GOD has sworn by Himself,  
declares the LORD, the God of hosts:  
I abhor the pride of Jacob  
and hate his strongholds;  
I will hand over the city  
and all that fills it.

**9** And it shall come to pass, if ten men are left in one house, that they will die.

**10** When a man's uncle, the one who burns for him, lifts him up to carry the bones out of the house, he will say to the one in the innermost part of the house, "Is there anyone else with you?" He will say, "No one." Then he will say, "Silence! The name of the LORD must not be mentioned."

**11** For the LORD is about to command,  
and He will strike the great house into fragments  
and the small house into splinters.

**12** Do horses run on the rock?  
Does one plow there with oxen?  
Yet you have turned justice into poison  
and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood.

**13** You who rejoice in nothing,  
who say, "Have we not taken horns for ourselves  
by our own strength?"

**14** For I am about to raise up against you a nation,  
house of Israel,  
declares the LORD, the God of hosts,  
and they will oppress you from Lebo-hamath  
to the Wadi of the Arabah.

## Notes

- **v1 "those at ease in Zion… the notable men of the first among the nations"** — Zion is named with Samaria: Judah is in the woe too. *Sha'anannim*, **at ease**, is Isaiah's word, *Women at ease, rise up* (Isaiah 32:9). *Neqvei*, **notable men**, is from the verb of *men who had been designated by name* (Numbers 1:17). *Reshit ha-goyim*, **the first among the nations**, is Balaam's saying, *Amalek was first among the nations, but his end is destruction* (Numbers 24:20).
- **v2 "Calneh… Hamath the great… Gath of the Philistines"** — Isaiah has the Assyrian boast over the same names, *Is not Calno like Carchemish? Is not Hamath like Arpad?* (Isaiah 10:9). Gath, missing from the Philistine cities of 1:6–8, had been taken by Hazael (2 Kings 12:17). *These kingdoms* are Israel and Judah of v1, asked whether the fallen cities were any better or any larger.
- **v3 "the seat of violence"** — *shevet chamas*, **the sitting of violence**: a throne, or a rule. They push away the day of disaster and bring the reign of violence near. The Greek has *who draw near and lay hold of false sabbaths*: *shevet* has the letters of *shabbat*.
- **vv4–6 "beds of ivory… couches… wine from basins… the finest oils"** — the bed and the couch of 3:12, where all that is rescued is *the corner of a bed and a piece of a couch*. *Mizraq*, **basin**, is the vessel of the altar, *one silver basin of seventy shekels* (Numbers 7:13); here it is filled with wine. *Marbeq*, **the fattening stall**, is where the woman of Endor kept *a fattened calf* (1 Samuel 28:24). *Reshit shemanim*, **the first of oils**, says *reshit* again, as in v1.
- **v5 "like David devise for themselves instruments of song"** — David spoke of *the instruments I made for praise* (1 Chronicles 23:5), and the Levites *stood with the instruments of David* (2 Chronicles 29:26). *Chashvu lahem* can be **devise for themselves** or **count themselves**, as David at his instruments. *Ha-poretim*, **who strum**, is found only here.
- **vv6–7 "not grieved over the ruin of Joseph… the feasting of those who sprawl will pass away"** — *shever Yosef*, **the breaking of Joseph**, the north. *Mirzach*, **feasting**, is the word of *Do not enter the house of mourning* (Jeremiah 16:5): there a gathering for the dead, here a feast for the living, while Joseph breaks. *Be-rosh golim*, **at the head of the exiles**: the first among the nations, anointed with the first of the oils, go into exile first.
- **v8 "sworn by Himself… I abhor the pride of Jacob… I will hand over the city"** — *be-nafsho*, **by Himself**, as in *The LORD of hosts has sworn by Himself* (Jeremiah 51:14). *Ge'on Ya'aqov*, **the pride of Jacob**, is what the psalm sings, *the pride of Jacob, whom He loves* (Psalm 47:4). *Ve-hisgarti*, **I will hand over**, is the verb of Gaza and Tyre, who handed whole peoples over to Edom (1:6, 9).
- **vv9–10 "his uncle, the one who burns for him… Silence! The name of the LORD must not be mentioned"** — *dodo*, **his uncle**, the kinsman who carries out the dead, as Aaron's uncle's sons carried out Nadab and Abihu (Leviticus 10:4). *Mesarefo*, **the one who burns for him**: the burning made in honor of the dead, *a very great fire in his honor* (2 Chronicles 16:14), or the burning of the body, as at Jabesh, *They came to Jabesh and burned them there* (1 Samuel 31:12). *Has*, **Silence!**, is Eglon's word (Judges 3:19). The law said *The name of other gods you shall not mention* (Exodus 23:13); here it is the LORD's name that must not be said.
- **v12 "Do horses run on the rock? Does one plow there with oxen?"** — the second question has no *rock* of its own; *there* takes it from the first: no one plows rock with oxen. The Greek has *will they keep silent among mares?* *Rosh* and *la'anah*, **poison** and **wormwood**, are the pair of *a root bearing poison and wormwood* (Deuteronomy 29:18); Hosea has justice spring up *like poison* (Hosea 10:4), and 5:7 here has it turned into wormwood.
- **vv13–14 "who rejoice in nothing… taken horns… from Lebo-hamath to the Wadi of the Arabah"** — *lo davar*, **nothing**, and *qarnayim*, **horns**, are also the names of towns: Lo-debar (2 Samuel 9:4) and Ashteroth-karnaim (Genesis 14:5). The Hebrew points *lo davar* as words, not as the town, and the Greek reads both as words. Horns were Joseph's in Moses' blessing, *his horns are the horns of the wild ox* (Deuteronomy 33:17). The nation's march runs the length of what Jeroboam won, who *restored the border of Israel from Lebo-hamath to the Sea of the Arabah* (2 Kings 14:25).

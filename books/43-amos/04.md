# Amos 4

## The Cows of Bashan

**1** Hear this word, you cows of Bashan  
who are on the mountain of Samaria,  
who oppress the poor,  
who crush the needy,  
who say to their husbands,  
"Bring, so that we may drink!"

**2** The Lord GOD has sworn by His holiness:  
days are coming upon you  
when they will carry you away with hooks,  
and the last of you with fishhooks.

**3** You will go out through the breaches,  
each woman straight ahead,  
and you will be thrown out toward Harmon,  
declares the LORD.

## Yet You Did Not Return to Me

**4** Come to Bethel and transgress;  
to Gilgal, and multiply transgression;  
bring your sacrifices every morning,  
your tithes every three days.

**5** Turn a thanksgiving offering of leavened bread into smoke,  
and proclaim freewill offerings; announce them;  
for that is what you love, sons of Israel,  
declares the Lord GOD.

**6** I in turn gave you clean teeth in all your cities  
and lack of bread in all your places;  
yet you did not return to Me,  
declares the LORD.

**7** I also withheld the rain from you  
when there were still three months to the harvest;  
I would send rain on one city  
and send no rain on another city;  
one field would be rained on,  
and the field that had no rain would wither.

**8** Two or three cities would stagger to one city  
to drink water, and would not be satisfied;  
yet you did not return to Me,  
declares the LORD.

**9** I struck you with scorching and mildew;  
the locust devoured your many gardens and vineyards,  
your fig trees and your olive trees;  
yet you did not return to Me,  
declares the LORD.

**10** I sent among you a plague, as in Egypt;  
I killed your young men with the sword,  
along with your captured horses;  
I made the stench of your camps rise  
into your nostrils;  
yet you did not return to Me,  
declares the LORD.

**11** I overthrew some of you,  
as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah,  
and you were like a brand snatched from the burning;  
yet you did not return to Me,  
declares the LORD.

## Prepare to Meet Your God

**12** Therefore this is what I will do to you, Israel;  
because I will do this to you,  
prepare to meet your God, Israel!

**13** For He is the One who forms the mountains  
and creates the wind,  
who declares to man what His thought is,  
who makes the dawn into darkness  
and treads on the heights of the earth:  
the LORD, the God of hosts, is His name.

## Notes

- **v1 "you cows of Bashan… who say to their husbands"** — Bashan, east of the Jordan, was known for its herds: *rams, the breed of Bashan* (Deuteronomy 32:14); *strong bulls of Bashan* (Psalm 22:12). The cows are the women of Samaria, and *adoneihem*, **their lords**, are their husbands, as Sarah says *my husband is old* (Genesis 18:12). *Oppress* and *crush*, *ashaq* and *ratsats*, are the two verbs of Samuel's defense, *Whom have I cheated? Whom have I oppressed?* (1 Samuel 12:3).
- **v2 "sworn by His holiness… with hooks, and the last of you with fishhooks"** — the oath of *Once for all I have sworn by My holiness* (Psalm 89:35). *Tsinnot* and *sirot dugah* are uncertain: hooks and fishhooks, or baskets and fish-pots; *sirot* is elsewhere **pots**, and the Greek has *they will take you with weapons, and those with you they will throw into cauldrons*.
- **v3 "through the breaches… toward Harmon"** — *peratsim*, the gaps broken in a taken city's wall, each woman going straight out through the one in front of her. *Ha-Harmonah*, **toward Harmon**, is a place named nowhere else, and where it was is not known; the Greek has *the mountain Romman*.
- **vv4–5 "Come to Bethel and transgress… your tithes every three days… leavened bread into smoke"** — a call to worship, turned: *pish'u*, **transgress**, is the verb of the *transgressions* of 1:3–2:6. The law asked a tithe *at the end of three years* (Deuteronomy 14:28); here it is every three days. A thanksgiving offering came with *loaves of leavened bread* (Leviticus 7:13), but *you shall not turn any sourdough… into smoke* (Leviticus 2:11). They bring more than the law asks and the wrong thing, and *that is what you love*.
- **vv6–9 "yet you did not return to Me"** — five times, after each blow (vv6, 8, 9, 10, 11). *Ve-lo shavtem adai*: *ad*, **all the way to**, the word of Hosea's call, *Return, Israel, to the LORD your God* (Hosea 14:1). *Clean teeth* are teeth with nothing to chew. The blows are those of Solomon's prayer at the temple, *When the heavens are shut up and there is no rain… and turn from their sin* (1 Kings 8:35), and *if there is plague, scorching or mildew, locust* (8:37); there the people are to turn, and here they do not. *Na'u*, **stagger**, is the drunkard's reeling of Psalm 107:27.
- **v10 "a plague, as in Egypt"** — *be-derekh Mitsrayim*, Isaiah's *as in Egypt* (Isaiah 10:24). *Dever*, **plague**, struck Egypt's herds, *a very heavy plague* (Exodus 9:3), and the law threatened *all the diseases of Egypt* (Deuteronomy 28:60): the blows that brought Israel out now fall on Israel.
- **v11 "as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah… a brand snatched from the burning"** — *ke-mahpekhat Elohim et Sedom ve-et Amorah*, the formula Jeremiah uses, *As when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah* (Jeremiah 50:40); it keeps *God* even in the LORD's own speech. The brand pulled from the fire is named again in Zechariah 3:2.
- **v12 "this is what I will do to you… prepare to meet your God"** — what *this* is, the verse never says. *Hikkon liqrat Elohekha* are the words of Sinai: *Be ready for three days* (Exodus 19:15), when *Moses brought the people out of the camp to meet God* (Exodus 19:17). The meeting Israel was once made ready for is set again, after five refusals.
- **v13 "who declares to man what His thought is… the LORD, the God of hosts, is His name"** — *mah secho*, **what his thought is**, names no one: God declaring His own thought, as He reveals *His counsel to His servants the prophets* (3:7), or showing a man his own. The Greek has *announcing to men His anointed*; *meshicho*, **His anointed**, has nearly the same letters. *Oseh shachar efah*, **maker of dawn, darkness**, has no *and* and no *into*; the Greek has *who makes dawn and mist*. *The heights of the earth* are those Moses sang of, *He made him ride on the heights of the earth* (Deuteronomy 32:13). The same ending closes 5:8, and again 9:6.

# Joshua 20

## Appoint the Towns of Refuge

**1** The LORD spoke to Joshua, saying,

**2** "Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, 'Appoint for yourselves the towns of refuge, of which I spoke to you through Moses,

**3** so that anyone who kills a person by mistake, unintentionally, may flee there. They shall be a refuge for you from the avenger of blood.

**4** He shall flee to one of these towns and stand at the entrance of the city gate and state his case in the hearing of the elders of that town. They shall take him into the town and give him a place, and he shall live with them.

**5** If the avenger of blood pursues him, they shall not give up the one who killed into his hand, because he struck his neighbor unintentionally and had not hated him before.

**6** He shall live in that town until he stands before the congregation for judgment, until the death of the high priest who is in office in those days. Then the one who killed may return and come to his own town and his own house, to the town from which he fled.'"

## The Six Towns

**7** So they set apart Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali, Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, in the hill country of Judah.

**8** Beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho, they appointed Bezer in the wilderness on the plateau, from the tribe of Reuben; Ramoth in Gilead, from the tribe of Gad; and Golan in Bashan, from the tribe of Manasseh.

**9** These were the appointed towns for all the sons of Israel and for the guest who lives as a guest among them, so that anyone who kills a person by mistake may flee there, and not die by the hand of the avenger of blood until he stands before the congregation.

## Notes

- **v2 "of which I spoke to you through Moses"** — the law of Numbers 35:9–34 and Deuteronomy 19:1–13, now carried out.
- **v3 "by mistake, unintentionally"** — *bi-shegagah*, the word of Numbers 35:11, *the one who kills a person by mistake*, and *bi-veli da'at*, **without knowing**, the phrase of Deuteronomy 19:4.
- **v4 "at the entrance of the city gate"** — the fugitive first pleads before the elders of the refuge town; the full trial comes later *before the congregation* (v6).
- **v5 "had not hated him before"** — *mi-temol shilshom*, the test of Deuteronomy 19:4 and 4:42.
- **v6 "until the death of the high priest"** — the rule of Numbers 35:25, *until the death of the high priest who was anointed with the holy oil*. The high priest's death closes the matter.
- **v7 "they set apart"** — *va-yaqdishu*, **made holy**. The three western towns stand one in each region, north, center and south, so that none is too far away (Deuteronomy 19:3).
- **v8 Bezer, Ramoth, Golan** — the three east of the Jordan that Moses had already named in Deuteronomy 4:43, *Bezer in the wilderness on the plateau… Ramoth in Gilead… Golan in Bashan*.
- **v9 "for the guest who lives as a guest among them"** — refuge is not only for Israelites, as Numbers 35:15 said: *for the guest and the resident among them*.

# Joshua 12

## Beyond the Jordan

**1** These are the kings of the land whom the sons of Israel struck down and whose land they possessed beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise, from the valley of the Arnon to Mount Hermon, and all the Arabah eastward:

**2** Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived at Heshbon and ruled from Aroer, which is on the edge of the valley of the Arnon, and from the middle of the valley, and half of Gilead, as far as the river Jabbok, the border of the sons of Ammon,

**3** and the Arabah as far as the Sea of Chinneroth eastward, and as far as the sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, eastward, by the way to Beth-jeshimoth, and on the south below the slopes of Pisgah;

**4** and the territory of Og king of Bashan, one of the remnant of the Rephaim, who lived at Ashtaroth and at Edrei,

**5** and ruled over Mount Hermon and Salecah and all Bashan, as far as the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and half of Gilead, to the border of Sihon king of Heshbon.

**6** Moses the servant of the LORD and the sons of Israel struck them down, and Moses the servant of the LORD gave their land as a possession to the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

## West of the Jordan

**7** These are the kings of the land whom Joshua and the sons of Israel struck down beyond the Jordan westward, from Baal-gad in the Valley of Lebanon to the bare mountain that rises toward Seir, and Joshua gave it to the tribes of Israel as a possession according to their divisions,

**8** in the hill country and in the lowland, in the Arabah and on the slopes, in the wilderness and in the Negev — the Hittite, the Amorite and the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite and the Jebusite:

**9** the king of Jericho, one; the king of Ai, which is beside Bethel, one;

**10** the king of Jerusalem, one; the king of Hebron, one;

**11** the king of Jarmuth, one; the king of Lachish, one;

**12** the king of Eglon, one; the king of Gezer, one;

**13** the king of Debir, one; the king of Geder, one;

**14** the king of Hormah, one; the king of Arad, one;

**15** the king of Libnah, one; the king of Adullam, one;

**16** the king of Makkedah, one; the king of Bethel, one;

**17** the king of Tappuah, one; the king of Hepher, one;

**18** the king of Aphek, one; the king of Lasharon, one;

**19** the king of Madon, one; the king of Hazor, one;

**20** the king of Shimron-meron, one; the king of Achshaph, one;

**21** the king of Taanach, one; the king of Megiddo, one;

**22** the king of Kedesh, one; the king of Jokneam in Carmel, one;

**23** the king of Dor on the heights of Dor, one; the king of Goiim at Gilgal, one;

**24** the king of Tirzah, one. All the kings: thirty-one.

## Notes

- **vv1–6 the two kings east of the Jordan** — Sihon and Og, whose defeat Moses recounted in Deuteronomy 2:26–3:11. The list begins with what Moses did before it tells what Joshua did.
- **v4 "one of the remnant of the Rephaim"** — the words of Deuteronomy 3:11, *only Og king of Bashan was left of the remnant of the Rephaim*.
- **v6 "Moses the servant of the LORD"** — twice in one verse: the conquest east of the Jordan is his, the conquest west is Joshua's (v7).
- **vv9–24 "one… one"** — each king is counted like a line in a ledger, *echad*, **one**, and the total given at the end: *thirty-one*.
- **v9 "the king of Jericho… the king of Ai"** — the order follows the book: Jericho (chapter 6), Ai (8), the five kings of the south (10), and then the north (11).
- **v14 "Hormah… Arad"** — cities of the Negev; Numbers 21:1–3 tells of the king of Arad and the place named Hormah.
- **v20 "Shimron-meron"** — the Hebrew has *Shimron Meron* as one entry; the king of Shimron of 11:1.
- **v23 "the king of Goiim at Gilgal"** — *Goiim* is **nations**; the name may mean a ruler over mixed peoples. This Gilgal is not Israel's camp by the Jordan.

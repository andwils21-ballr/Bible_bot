# Joshua 9

## The Kings Gather

**1** And it came to pass, when all the kings who were beyond the Jordan heard of it — in the hill country and in the lowland and all along the coast of the Great Sea toward Lebanon, the Hittite and the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite and the Jebusite —

**2** that they gathered together with one mouth to fight against Joshua and against Israel.

## The Gibeonites' Trick

**3** But the inhabitants of Gibeon heard what Joshua had done to Jericho and to Ai,

**4** and they too acted with cunning. They went and disguised themselves as envoys, and took worn-out sacks for their donkeys, and wineskins worn out and split and tied up,

**5** and worn-out, patched sandals on their feet, and worn-out clothes on them. All the bread of their provisions was dry and crumbled.

**6** They went to Joshua at the camp at Gilgal and said to him and to the men of Israel, "We have come from a far country. And now make a covenant with us."

**7** The men of Israel said to the Hivites, "Perhaps you live among us. How then can we make a covenant with you?"

**8** They said to Joshua, "We are your servants." Joshua said to them, "Who are you, and where do you come from?"

**9** They said to him, "From a very far country your servants have come, because of the name of the LORD your God. For we have heard the report of Him, and all that He did in Egypt,

**10** and all that He did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, to Sihon king of Heshbon and to Og king of Bashan, who was at Ashtaroth.

**11** Our elders and all the inhabitants of our country said to us, 'Take provisions in your hand for the journey and go to meet them, and say to them, "We are your servants. And now make a covenant with us."'

**12** This bread of ours was warm when we took it from our houses as our provision on the day we set out to come to you, and now it is dry and crumbled.

**13** These wineskins were new when we filled them, and they have split. These clothes of ours and our sandals are worn out from the very long journey."

**14** The men took some of their provisions, but they did not ask the counsel of the LORD.

**15** Joshua made peace with them and made a covenant with them to let them live, and the leaders of the congregation swore to them.

## Found Out

**16** And it came to pass at the end of three days, after they had made a covenant with them, that they heard that they were their neighbors and were living among them.

**17** The sons of Israel set out and came to their cities on the third day. Their cities were Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeroth and Kiriath-jearim.

**18** The sons of Israel did not strike them, because the leaders of the congregation had sworn to them by the LORD, the God of Israel. All the congregation grumbled against the leaders.

**19** All the leaders said to all the congregation, "We have sworn to them by the LORD, the God of Israel, and now we cannot touch them.

**20** This is what we will do to them: let them live, so that wrath does not come on us because of the oath that we swore to them."

**21** The leaders said to them, "Let them live." So they became cutters of wood and drawers of water for all the congregation, as the leaders had told them.

**22** Joshua called for them and spoke to them, saying, "Why did you deceive us, saying, 'We are very far from you,' when you live among us?

**23** And now you are cursed. There shall never cease to be from among you slaves, cutters of wood and drawers of water for the house of my God."

**24** They answered Joshua and said, "Because it was told, told to your servants that the LORD your God commanded Moses His servant to give you all the land and to destroy all the inhabitants of the land before you, we were very afraid for our lives because of you, and we did this thing.

**25** And now we are in your hand. Do to us whatever seems good and right in your eyes."

**26** He did so to them, and delivered them from the hand of the sons of Israel, and they did not kill them.

**27** That day Joshua made them cutters of wood and drawers of water for the congregation and for the altar of the LORD, to this day, in the place that He would choose.

## Notes

- **v2 "with one mouth"** — *peh echad*, one voice, as all the earth at Babel was *one lip* (Genesis 11:1). Against them stands one city that chooses otherwise.
- **v4 "acted with cunning"** — *be-ormah*, the root of Genesis 3:1, where the serpent was *shrewder than every living thing of the field*. *They too*: as Joshua had used cunning at Ai (8:2, the ambush).
- **vv6, 9 "from a far country"** — the law of Deuteronomy 20:10–15 let Israel make peace with *cities that are very far from you*, not with the nations of the land. The Gibeonites know the law and dress for it.
- **v7 "the Hivites"** — Gibeon's people are among the seven nations (3:10), the ones Deuteronomy 7:2 forbade Israel to make a covenant with.
- **v9 "because of the name of the LORD your God"** — they say what Rahab said (2:10), *we have heard*, but leave out what happened at Jericho and Ai, since a far people could not yet know it.
- **v14 "they did not ask the counsel of the LORD"** — *et pi YHWH*, **the mouth of the LORD**. They tasted the bread and trusted their own judgment.
- **vv18–20 the oath kept** — an oath sworn in God's name binds even when it was won by deceit. 2 Samuel 21:1–2 tells how Saul later broke it, and Israel suffered famine for it.
- **v21 "cutters of wood and drawers of water"** — the lowest work of the camp, named in Deuteronomy 29:11 for *your guest who is inside your camp, from the one who cuts your wood to the one who draws your water*. The Gibeonites are given the guest's place.
- **v27 "for the altar of the LORD… in the place that He would choose"** — the phrase of Deuteronomy 12. The deceivers end up serving at God's altar.

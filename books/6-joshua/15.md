# Joshua 15

## Judah's Borders

**1** The lot for the tribe of the sons of Judah according to their clans reached to the border of Edom, to the wilderness of Zin at the far south.

**2** Their south border ran from the end of the Salt Sea, from the tongue of water that faces south,

**3** and went out south of the Ascent of Akrabbim, crossed to Zin, went up south of Kadesh-barnea, crossed to Hezron, went up to Addar and turned around to Karka,

**4** crossed to Azmon, went out to the Brook of Egypt, and the border ended at the sea. This shall be your south border.

**5** The east border was the Salt Sea, up to the mouth of the Jordan. The border on the north side ran from the tongue of the sea at the mouth of the Jordan.

**6** The border went up to Beth-hoglah and crossed north of Beth-arabah, and the border went up to the Stone of Bohan son of Reuben.

**7** The border went up to Debir from the Valley of Achor, and turned north toward Gilgal, which is opposite the Ascent of Adummim, south of the ravine. The border crossed to the waters of En-shemesh and ended at En-rogel.

**8** The border went up by the Valley of the Son of Hinnom at the southern slope of the Jebusite — that is, Jerusalem — and the border went up to the top of the mountain that lies west of the Valley of Hinnom, at the northern end of the Valley of Rephaim.

**9** The border was drawn from the top of the mountain to the spring of the waters of Nephtoah, went out to the cities of Mount Ephron, and was drawn to Baalah, that is, Kiriath-jearim.

**10** The border turned west from Baalah to Mount Seir, crossed to the northern slope of Mount Jearim, that is, Chesalon, went down to Beth-shemesh and crossed to Timnah.

**11** The border went out to the northern slope of Ekron, was drawn to Shikkeron, crossed to Mount Baalah, went out to Jabneel, and the border ended at the sea.

**12** The west border was the Great Sea and its coast. This is the border of the sons of Judah all around, according to their clans.

## Caleb and Achsah

**13** To Caleb son of Jephunneh he gave a portion among the sons of Judah, at the command of the LORD to Joshua: Kiriath-arba, Arba being the father of Anak, that is, Hebron.

**14** Caleb drove out from there the three sons of Anak: Sheshai, Ahiman and Talmai, the descendants of Anak.

**15** From there he went up against the inhabitants of Debir. The name of Debir before was Kiriath-sepher.

**16** Caleb said, "Whoever strikes Kiriath-sepher and takes it, I will give him Achsah my daughter as a wife."

**17** Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's brother, took it, and he gave him Achsah his daughter as a wife.

**18** And it came to pass, when she came to him, that she urged him to ask her father for a field. She got down from her donkey, and Caleb said to her, "What do you want?"

**19** She said, "Give me a blessing. Since you have given me land in the Negev, give me also springs of water." So he gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.

## The Cities of Judah

**20** This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Judah according to their clans.

**21** The cities at the far end of the tribe of the sons of Judah, toward the border of Edom in the Negev, were Kabzeel, Eder, Jagur,

**22** Kinah, Dimonah, Adadah,

**23** Kedesh, Hazor, Ithnan,

**24** Ziph, Telem, Bealoth,

**25** Hazor-hadattah, Kerioth-hezron, that is, Hazor,

**26** Amam, Shema, Moladah,

**27** Hazar-gaddah, Heshmon, Beth-pelet,

**28** Hazar-shual, Beersheba, Biziothiah,

**29** Baalah, Iim, Ezem,

**30** Eltolad, Chesil, Hormah,

**31** Ziklag, Madmannah, Sansannah,

**32** Lebaoth, Shilhim, Ain and Rimmon: in all, twenty-nine cities with their villages.

**33** In the lowland: Eshtaol, Zorah, Ashnah,

**34** Zanoah, En-gannim, Tappuah, Enam,

**35** Jarmuth, Adullam, Socoh, Azekah,

**36** Shaaraim, Adithaim, Gederah and Gederothaim: fourteen cities with their villages.

**37** Zenan, Hadashah, Migdal-gad,

**38** Dilean, Mizpeh, Joktheel,

**39** Lachish, Bozkath, Eglon,

**40** Cabbon, Lahmas, Chitlish,

**41** Gederoth, Beth-dagon, Naamah and Makkedah: sixteen cities with their villages.

**42** Libnah, Ether, Ashan,

**43** Iphtah, Ashnah, Nezib,

**44** Keilah, Achzib and Mareshah: nine cities with their villages.

**45** Ekron, with its towns and its villages;

**46** from Ekron to the sea, all that were near Ashdod, with their villages;

**47** Ashdod, its towns and its villages; Gaza, its towns and its villages, as far as the Brook of Egypt and the Great Sea and its coast.

**48** In the hill country: Shamir, Jattir, Socoh,

**49** Dannah, Kiriath-sannah, that is, Debir,

**50** Anab, Eshtemoh, Anim,

**51** Goshen, Holon and Giloh: eleven cities with their villages.

**52** Arab, Dumah, Eshan,

**53** Janum, Beth-tappuah, Aphekah,

**54** Humtah, Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, and Zior: nine cities with their villages.

**55** Maon, Carmel, Ziph, Juttah,

**56** Jezreel, Jokdeam, Zanoah,

**57** Kain, Gibeah and Timnah: ten cities with their villages.

**58** Halhul, Beth-zur, Gedor,

**59** Maarath, Beth-anoth and Eltekon: six cities with their villages.

**60** Kiriath-baal, that is, Kiriath-jearim, and Rabbah: two cities with their villages.

**61** In the wilderness: Beth-arabah, Middin, Secacah,

**62** Nibshan, the City of Salt and En-gedi: six cities with their villages.

**63** But the Jebusites, the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the sons of Judah could not drive out, and the Jebusites live with the sons of Judah in Jerusalem to this day.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 the south border** — the line of Numbers 34:3–5, *from the wilderness of Zin along the side of Edom… to the Brook of Egypt*, now Judah's own border. Judah's lot comes first, as Judah was first in the march (Numbers 10:14).
- **v6 "the Stone of Bohan son of Reuben"** — a landmark, perhaps a standing stone; *bohen* is **thumb**. Nothing else is known of Bohan.
- **v7 "the Valley of Achor"** — where Achan was stoned (7:26).
- **v8 "the Valley of the Son of Hinnom"** — *gei ven Hinnom*, south of Jerusalem. *Gei Hinnom* passed into Greek as *geenna*, the *Gehenna* of Matthew 5:22, *the fire of Gehenna*. The city itself is still called *the Jebusite* here.
- **vv13–19 Caleb, Othniel and Achsah** — told again, almost word for word, in Judges 1:10–15. Othniel will be the first of the judges (Judges 3:9).
- **v14 "Sheshai, Ahiman and Talmai"** — the three *descendants of Anak* whom the spies saw at Hebron (Numbers 13:22). The giants who frightened ten of the spies are driven out by the one who was not afraid.
- **v18 "she urged him"** — Achsah asks through her husband, then speaks for herself. *What do you want?* is *mah lakh*, **what is to you**.
- **v19 "Give me a blessing… springs of water"** — *berakhah*, a gift; in the dry Negev the gift that matters is water.
- **v32 "twenty-nine cities"** — the names listed in vv21–32 come to more than twenty-nine. Some may be double names for one place, or the count and the list may come from different times; the text is given as it stands.
- **v63 "could not drive out"** — Jerusalem stays Jebusite until David takes it (2 Samuel 5:6–7).

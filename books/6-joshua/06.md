# Joshua 6

## Seven Days Around the City

**1** Jericho was shut up tight because of the sons of Israel; no one went out and no one came in.

**2** The LORD said to Joshua, "See, I have given Jericho into your hand, with its king and its mighty men of valor.

**3** You shall march around the city, all the men of war, going around the city once. So you shall do for six days.

**4** Seven priests shall carry seven rams' horns before the ark. On the seventh day you shall march around the city seven times, and the priests shall blow the horns.

**5** And it shall come to pass, when they make a long blast with the ram's horn, when you hear the sound of the horn, that all the people shall give a great shout, and the wall of the city will fall down in its place, and the people shall go up, each man straight ahead."

**6** So Joshua son of Nun called the priests and said to them, "Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests carry seven rams' horns before the ark of the LORD."

**7** He said to the people, "Go forward and march around the city, and let the armed men go on before the ark of the LORD."

**8** And it came to pass, when Joshua had spoken to the people, that the seven priests carrying the seven rams' horns before the LORD went forward and blew the horns, and the ark of the covenant of the LORD followed them.

**9** The armed men went before the priests who blew the horns, and the rear guard went after the ark, with the horns blowing all the while.

**10** But Joshua commanded the people, saying, "You shall not shout or let your voice be heard, and no word shall go out of your mouth until the day I say to you, 'Shout!' Then you shall shout."

**11** So he had the ark of the LORD go around the city, circling it once, and they came into the camp and spent the night in the camp.

**12** Joshua rose early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of the LORD.

**13** The seven priests carrying the seven rams' horns before the ark of the LORD went on, blowing the horns as they went, and the armed men went before them, and the rear guard went after the ark of the LORD, with the horns blowing all the while.

**14** They marched around the city once on the second day and returned to the camp. So they did for six days.

## The Seventh Day

**15** And it came to pass on the seventh day that they rose early at the dawning of the day and marched around the city in the same manner seven times. Only on that day did they march around the city seven times.

**16** And it came to pass the seventh time, when the priests blew the horns, that Joshua said to the people, "Shout, for the LORD has given you the city!

**17** The city and all that is in it shall be devoted to the LORD for destruction. Only Rahab the prostitute shall live, she and all who are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers whom we sent.

**18** But as for you, keep yourselves from what is devoted to destruction, so that you do not devote it and then take from what is devoted, and make the camp of Israel something devoted to destruction and bring trouble on it.

**19** All silver and gold, and vessels of bronze and iron, are holy to the LORD; they shall go into the treasury of the LORD."

**20** So the people shouted, and the horns were blown. And it came to pass, when the people heard the sound of the horn, that the people gave a great shout, and the wall fell down in its place, and the people went up into the city, each man straight ahead, and they took the city.

**21** They devoted to destruction all that was in the city, man and woman, young and old, ox and sheep and donkey, with the sword.

## Rahab Saved

**22** To the two men who had spied out the land Joshua said, "Go into the prostitute's house, and bring out the woman and all who belong to her from there, as you swore to her."

**23** The young men who had been spies went in and brought out Rahab, her father and her mother, her brothers and all who belonged to her. They brought out all her family and put them outside the camp of Israel.

**24** They burned the city with fire, and all that was in it. Only the silver and the gold, and the vessels of bronze and iron, they put into the treasury of the house of the LORD.

**25** But Rahab the prostitute, her father's household and all who belonged to her, Joshua let live, and she has lived among Israel to this day, because she hid the messengers whom Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.

**26** Joshua made them swear an oath at that time, saying, "Cursed before the LORD is the man who rises up and rebuilds this city, Jericho. At the cost of his firstborn he shall lay its foundation, and at the cost of his youngest he shall set up its gates."

**27** The LORD was with Joshua, and his fame was in all the land.

## Notes

- **v1 "shut up tight"** — *sogeret u-mesuggeret*, the verb twice, closed and closed up. The city that let the spies out through a window (2:15) now opens nothing.
- **vv4–16 sevens** — seven priests, seven horns, seven days, seven circuits on the seventh day. The conquest begins as a liturgy.
- **v4 "rams' horns"** — *shofrot ha-yovelim*. *Yovel*, **ram's horn**, is the horn of Exodus 19:13, *When the ram's horn sounds long, they may come up the mountain*, and it gives its name to the year of *jubilee* (Leviticus 25).
- **v5 "a long blast"** — *bi-meshokh*, the verb of Exodus 19:13, *sounds long*.
- **v10 "You shall not shout"** — six days of silence, and then one shout.
- **v17 "devoted to the LORD for destruction"** — *cherem*: the city is given entire to God, as Deuteronomy 20:16–17 commanded for the cities of the land. *Rahab the prostitute shall live* — the one exception, by oath (2:14).
- **v18 "bring trouble on it"** — *va-akhartem*, the verb that will give the valley of Achor its name after Achan does exactly this (7:25–26).
- **v20 "the wall fell down in its place"** — *tachteiha*, **under itself**. Hebrews 11:30: *By faith the walls of Jericho fell, after they had been encircled for seven days*.
- **v23 "outside the camp of Israel"** — at first. By v25 *she has lived among Israel to this day*.
- **v26 the curse on Jericho** — 1 Kings 16:34 records its fulfillment: *In his days Hiel of Bethel built Jericho. With Abiram his firstborn he laid its foundation, and with Segub his youngest he set up its gates, according to the word of the LORD that He spoke through Joshua son of Nun.*

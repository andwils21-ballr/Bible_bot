# Joshua 24

## What the LORD Has Done

**1** Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shechem and called for the elders of Israel, its heads, its judges and its officials, and they presented themselves before God.

**2** Joshua said to all the people, "Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: 'Long ago your fathers lived beyond the River — Terah, the father of Abraham and the father of Nahor — and they served other gods.

**3** I took your father Abraham from beyond the River and led him through all the land of Canaan. I multiplied his offspring and gave him Isaac,

**4** and to Isaac I gave Jacob and Esau. To Esau I gave Mount Seir to possess, but Jacob and his sons went down to Egypt.

**5** I sent Moses and Aaron, and I struck Egypt with what I did in its midst, and afterward I brought you out.

**6** I brought your fathers out of Egypt, and you came to the sea, and the Egyptians pursued your fathers with chariots and horsemen to the Sea of Reeds.

**7** They cried out to the LORD, and He put darkness between you and the Egyptians, and brought the sea upon them and covered them. Your eyes saw what I did in Egypt. Then you lived in the wilderness a long time.

**8** I brought you to the land of the Amorites who lived beyond the Jordan. They fought with you, and I gave them into your hand. You took possession of their land, and I destroyed them before you.

**9** Then Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, rose up and fought against Israel. He sent and called Balaam son of Beor to curse you,

**10** but I would not listen to Balaam. He blessed you instead, and I delivered you out of his hand.

**11** You crossed the Jordan and came to Jericho, and the lords of Jericho fought against you — the Amorite, the Perizzite, the Canaanite, the Hittite, the Girgashite, the Hivite and the Jebusite — and I gave them into your hand.

**12** I sent the hornet before you, and it drove them out before you, the two kings of the Amorites — not by your sword and not by your bow.

**13** I gave you a land you had not labored for, and cities you had not built, and you live in them. You eat from vineyards and olive groves you did not plant.'

## Choose Today

**14** "And now fear the LORD and serve Him in wholeness and in truth. Put away the gods your fathers served beyond the River and in Egypt, and serve the LORD.

**15** But if it seems wrong in your eyes to serve the LORD, choose for yourselves today whom you will serve: whether the gods your fathers served beyond the River, or the gods of the Amorites in whose land you are living. But as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD."

**16** The people answered, "Far be it from us to forsake the LORD to serve other gods!

**17** For it is the LORD our God who brought us and our fathers up from the land of Egypt, out of the house of slaves, who did these great signs before our eyes, and kept us on all the way we went and among all the peoples through whom we passed.

**18** The LORD drove out before us all the peoples, and the Amorites who lived in the land. We too will serve the LORD, for He is our God."

**19** Joshua said to the people, "You cannot serve the LORD, for He is a holy God. He is a jealous God; He will not forgive your transgressions or your sins.

**20** If you forsake the LORD and serve foreign gods, He will turn and do you harm and consume you, after having done you good."

**21** The people said to Joshua, "No! We will serve the LORD."

**22** Joshua said to the people, "You are witnesses against yourselves that you have chosen the LORD, to serve Him." They said, "We are witnesses."

**23** "And now put away the foreign gods that are among you, and turn your hearts to the LORD, the God of Israel."

**24** The people said to Joshua, "The LORD our God we will serve, and His voice we will obey."

## The Stone of Witness

**25** So Joshua made a covenant with the people that day, and set for them a statute and a rule at Shechem.

**26** Joshua wrote these words in the book of the instruction of God. He took a great stone and set it up there under the terebinth that was in the sanctuary of the LORD.

**27** Joshua said to all the people, "This stone shall be a witness against us, for it has heard all the words the LORD spoke to us. It shall be a witness against you, so that you do not deny your God."

**28** Then Joshua sent the people away, each to his inheritance.

## Three Burials

**29** And it came to pass after these things that Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died, a hundred and ten years old.

**30** They buried him within the border of his inheritance at Timnath-serah, which is in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.

**31** Israel served the LORD all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders who outlived Joshua and had known all the work the LORD did for Israel.

**32** The bones of Joseph, which the sons of Israel brought up from Egypt, they buried at Shechem, in the portion of the field that Jacob bought from the sons of Hamor, the father of Shechem, for a hundred qesitah. It became an inheritance of the sons of Joseph.

**33** Eleazar son of Aaron died, and they buried him at Gibeah of Phinehas his son, which had been given him in the hill country of Ephraim.

## Notes

- **v1 "to Shechem"** — where Abram first built an altar in the land (Genesis 12:6–7) and where the blessing and curse were read at Mount Ebal (8:30–35). The Greek has *to Shiloh*, here and in v25, where it adds *before the tent of the God of Israel*.
- **v2 "beyond the River… they served other gods"** — the River is the Euphrates. Terah's household is the start of the story (Genesis 11:31), and Joshua does not hide that it began in idolatry.
- **v7 "He put darkness between you and the Egyptians"** — *ma'afel*, found only here; the event is Exodus 14:20, *cloud and darkness*. The speech moves from *I* to *He* and back: Joshua is speaking the LORD's words.
- **v10 "He blessed you instead"** — *va-yevarekh barokh*, a doubled verb, **he blessed, blessing**. Deuteronomy 23:5 tells the same story: *the LORD your God turned the curse into a blessing for you*.
- **v12 "the hornet… the two kings of the Amorites"** — the hornet of Exodus 23:28 and Deuteronomy 7:20; the two kings are Sihon and Og, east of the Jordan. The Greek has *twelve kings of the Amorites*.
- **v14 "in wholeness and in truth"** — *be-tamim u-ve-emet*. *Tamim* of a person is wholeness toward God, as in Deuteronomy 18:13, *You shall be whole with the LORD your God*.
- **v15 "as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD"** — the choice is put to the people, but Joshua has already made his.
- **v19 "You cannot serve the LORD, for He is a holy God"** — *Elohim qedoshim hu*, with the adjective in the plural to match *Elohim*. Joshua's refusal is meant to make the people's yes a considered one. *A jealous God* is *El qanno*, a form of the *El qanna* of Exodus 20:5.
- **vv26–27 "a great stone… a witness against us"** — as the altar by the Jordan was a witness in 22:34. The terebinth recalls Genesis 35:4, where Jacob hid the foreign gods *under the terebinth that is by Shechem*; here too the people are told to put away foreign gods (v23, Genesis 35:2).
- **vv29–33 three burials** — Joshua, Joseph and Eleazar. Joseph's bones, carried out of Egypt at his own request (Genesis 50:25, Exodus 13:19), are laid in the field Jacob bought (Genesis 33:19). The Greek adds that the flint knives of the circumcision at Gilgal (5:2) were buried with Joshua, and after v33 it adds that Phinehas served as priest after Eleazar, and that Israel then served Astarte and was given into the hand of Eglon king of Moab for eighteen years — the story Judges 3 tells.

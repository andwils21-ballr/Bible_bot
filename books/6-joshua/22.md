# Joshua 22

## The Eastern Tribes Go Home

**1** Then Joshua called the Reubenites and the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh,

**2** and said to them, "You have kept all that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you, and you have listened to my voice in all that I commanded you.

**3** You have not forsaken your brothers these many days, to this day, and you have kept the charge of the commandment of the LORD your God.

**4** And now the LORD your God has given your brothers rest, as He promised them. So now turn and go to your tents, to the land of your possession, which Moses the servant of the LORD gave you beyond the Jordan.

**5** Only be very careful to do the commandment and the instruction that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you: to love the LORD your God, to walk in all His ways, to keep His commandments, to hold fast to Him, and to serve Him with all your heart and with all your soul."

**6** Joshua blessed them and sent them away, and they went to their tents.

**7** To the one half-tribe of Manasseh Moses had given a possession in Bashan, and to the other half Joshua had given a possession with their brothers beyond the Jordan to the west. When Joshua sent them away to their tents, he blessed them,

**8** and said to them, "Go back to your tents with great wealth and with very much livestock, with silver and gold, with bronze and iron, and with very many clothes. Divide the spoil of your enemies with your brothers."

**9** So the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh returned, leaving the sons of Israel at Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan, to go to the land of Gilead, the land of their possession, which they had taken possession of at the command of the LORD through Moses.

## The Altar

**10** When they came to the region of the Jordan that is in the land of Canaan, the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh built there an altar by the Jordan, a great altar to look at.

**11** The sons of Israel heard it said, "The sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh have built the altar at the frontier of the land of Canaan, in the region of the Jordan, on the side belonging to the sons of Israel."

**12** When the sons of Israel heard it, the whole congregation of the sons of Israel gathered at Shiloh to go up against them to war.

**13** The sons of Israel sent to the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh, in the land of Gilead, Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest,

**14** and ten leaders with him, one leader for each father's house from all the tribes of Israel, each of them the head of his father's house among the thousands of Israel.

**15** They came to the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh, in the land of Gilead, and spoke with them, saying,

**16** "Thus says the whole congregation of the LORD: 'What is this breach of faith that you have committed against the God of Israel, turning away today from following the LORD by building yourselves an altar, to rebel today against the LORD?

**17** Is the guilt of Peor too little for us, from which we have not cleansed ourselves to this day, though the plague came on the congregation of the LORD?

**18** You would turn away today from following the LORD! And it shall come to pass, if you rebel today against the LORD, that tomorrow He will be angry with the whole congregation of Israel.

**19** But if the land of your possession is unclean, cross over into the land of the LORD's possession, where the LORD's dwelling stands, and take your possession among us. Only do not rebel against the LORD, and do not rebel against us, by building yourselves an altar other than the altar of the LORD our God.

**20** Did not Achan son of Zerah break faith in the matter of what was devoted to destruction, and wrath fell on all the congregation of Israel? He was not the only man who perished for his guilt.'"

## The Answer of the Eastern Tribes

**21** Then the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh answered and said to the heads of the thousands of Israel,

**22** "The Mighty One, God, the LORD! The Mighty One, God, the LORD! He knows, and let Israel itself know: if it was in rebellion or in breach of faith against the LORD, do not save us this day!

**23** If we have built ourselves an altar to turn away from following the LORD, or to offer burnt offerings or grain offerings on it, or to make peace offerings on it, may the LORD Himself require it.

**24** No — we did it out of anxiety over a matter, thinking, 'Tomorrow your sons may say to our sons, "What have you to do with the LORD, the God of Israel?

**25** The LORD has made the Jordan a border between us and you, you sons of Reuben and sons of Gad. You have no portion in the LORD." So your sons may make our sons stop fearing the LORD.'

**26** So we said, 'Let us now build ourselves an altar, not for burnt offering and not for sacrifice,

**27** but to be a witness between us and you, and between our generations after us, that we do serve the LORD before Him with our burnt offerings and our sacrifices and our peace offerings, so that your sons do not say to our sons tomorrow, "You have no portion in the LORD."'

**28** We said, 'And it shall come to pass, if they say this to us or to our generations tomorrow, that we will say, "See the pattern of the altar of the LORD, which our fathers made, not for burnt offering and not for sacrifice, but to be a witness between us and you."'

**29** Far be it from us to rebel against the LORD and turn away today from following the LORD by building an altar for burnt offering, for grain offering or for sacrifice, other than the altar of the LORD our God that stands before His dwelling."

## A Witness Between Us

**30** When Phinehas the priest and the leaders of the congregation and the heads of the thousands of Israel who were with him heard the words that the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the sons of Manasseh spoke, it was good in their eyes.

**31** Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest said to the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad and the sons of Manasseh, "Today we know that the LORD is among us, because you have not committed this breach of faith against the LORD. Now you have delivered the sons of Israel from the hand of the LORD."

**32** Then Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest and the leaders returned from the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad, from the land of Gilead, to the land of Canaan, to the sons of Israel, and brought back word to them.

**33** The report was good in the eyes of the sons of Israel. The sons of Israel blessed God and spoke no more of going up against them to war, to destroy the land where the sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad lived.

**34** The sons of Reuben and the sons of Gad called the altar Witness: "For it is a witness between us that the LORD is God."

## Notes

- **vv1–6 the eastern tribes released** — the promise of Numbers 32 and Joshua 1:12–18 kept. They *have not forsaken* their brothers, and Joshua sends them home with a blessing and a charge that repeats Deuteronomy's heart: *to love the LORD your God… with all your heart and with all your soul*.
- **v10 "a great altar to look at"** — *mizbeach gadol le-mar'eh*, built to be seen. The whole quarrel turns on what it is for.
- **v16 "this breach of faith"** — *ma'al*, the word used of Achan in 7:1. The western tribes suspect the easterners of a second Achan's sin, and v20 says so outright.
- **v17 "the guilt of Peor"** — the worship of the Baal of Peor in Numbers 25, where *those who died in the plague were twenty-four thousand*. Phinehas, who leads this embassy, is the priest who stopped that plague (Numbers 25:11).
- **v19 "if the land of your possession is unclean"** — the offer is generous: if the east bank is not fit for worship, come and share the west.
- **v22 "The Mighty One, God, the LORD!"** — *El Elohim YHWH*, three names of God, twice: the strongest oath the easterners can swear. *He knows* — the answer is put first to God, then to Israel.
- **v28 "See the pattern of the altar of the LORD"** — *tavnit*, the word of Exodus 25:9, *the pattern of the dwelling*. The altar is a copy, a model of the real one at Shiloh, built to be looked at, not used.
- **v31 "Today we know that the LORD is among us"** — the danger of chapter 7 is not repeated; the peace between the tribes is itself a sign of God's presence.
- **v34 "called the altar Witness"** — *Ed* is not written in the Hebrew as the altar's name; the sentence reads *called the altar: for it is a witness between us*. Many translations supply the name *Witness*, as this one does, because the explanation that follows gives it.

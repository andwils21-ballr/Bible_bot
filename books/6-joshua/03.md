# Joshua 3

## Follow the Ark

**1** Joshua rose early in the morning, and they set out from Shittim and came to the Jordan, he and all the sons of Israel, and they spent the night there before they crossed.

**2** And it came to pass at the end of three days that the officials passed through the camp

**3** and commanded the people, saying, "When you see the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, and the Levitical priests carrying it, then you shall set out from your place and go after it.

**4** But there shall be a distance between you and it of about two thousand cubits by measure. Do not come near it, so that you may know the way you are to go, for you have not passed this way before."

**5** Joshua said to the people, "Make yourselves holy, for tomorrow the LORD will do wonders among you."

**6** Joshua said to the priests, "Take up the ark of the covenant and cross over before the people." They took up the ark of the covenant and went before the people.

## The Waters Will Stand

**7** The LORD said to Joshua, "Today I will begin to make you great in the eyes of all Israel, so that they may know that as I was with Moses, so I will be with you.

**8** As for you, command the priests who carry the ark of the covenant, saying, 'When you come to the edge of the waters of the Jordan, stand still in the Jordan.'"

**9** Joshua said to the sons of Israel, "Come here and hear the words of the LORD your God."

**10** Joshua said, "By this you will know that the living God is among you, and that He will surely drive out before you the Canaanite and the Hittite and the Hivite and the Perizzite and the Girgashite and the Amorite and the Jebusite.

**11** The ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is crossing over before you into the Jordan.

**12** Now take for yourselves twelve men from the tribes of Israel, one man from each tribe.

**13** And it shall come to pass, when the soles of the feet of the priests who carry the ark of the LORD, the Lord of all the earth, rest in the waters of the Jordan, that the waters of the Jordan will be cut off, the waters coming down from above, and they will stand as a single wall."

## The People Cross on Dry Ground

**14** And it came to pass, when the people set out from their tents to cross the Jordan, with the priests carrying the ark of the covenant before the people,

**15** and when those carrying the ark came to the Jordan, and the feet of the priests carrying the ark dipped into the edge of the water — the Jordan overflows all its banks all the days of harvest —

**16** that the waters coming down from above stood still. They rose up as a single wall, very far away, from Adam, the city beside Zarethan, and those going down to the sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, were completely cut off. The people crossed opposite Jericho.

**17** The priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD stood firm on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan, while all Israel was crossing on dry ground, until the whole nation had finished crossing the Jordan.

## Notes

- **v4 "about two thousand cubits"** — roughly half a mile. The ark leads, and the people keep far enough back to see it: *so that you may know the way you are to go, for you have not passed this way before*, *mi-temol shilshom*, the phrase of Deuteronomy 19:4.
- **v5 "Make yourselves holy"** — *hitqaddeshu*, as the people were made holy before Sinai in Exodus 19:10.
- **v7 "as I was with Moses, so I will be with you"** — the promise of 1:5, now to be seen by all Israel. The crossing is Joshua's Sea of Reeds.
- **v10 "the living God"** — *el chai*, a title rare in the Torah; Deuteronomy 5:26 has *the voice of the living God speaking from the middle of the fire*.
- **vv11, 13 "the Lord of all the earth"** — *adon kol ha-arets*. The ark that goes into the river belongs to the Lord of the whole earth, not a local god of Israel.
- **vv13, 16 "a single wall"** — *ned echad*, the word of the Song of the Sea, Exodus 15:8, *The flowing waters stood like a wall*.
- **v15 "the Jordan overflows all its banks all the days of harvest"** — the crossing comes at the spring flood, when the river is at its widest (4:19, the first month).
- **v16 "from Adam, the city beside Zarethan"** — the waters stop far upstream, so the riverbed runs dry for miles. The written text has *be-Adam*, **at Adam**; the reading tradition, followed here, has *me-Adam*, **from Adam**: the pile-up reached from Adam down.
- **v17 "on dry ground"** — *be-charavah*, the word of Exodus 14:21, where the LORD *made the sea into dry ground*.

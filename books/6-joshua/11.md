# Joshua 11

## The Waters of Merom

**1** And it came to pass, when Jabin king of Hazor heard of it, that he sent to Jobab king of Madon, and to the king of Shimron, and to the king of Achshaph,

**2** and to the kings who were in the north, in the hill country and in the Arabah south of Chinneroth, and in the lowland and on the heights of Dor on the west,

**3** to the Canaanite in the east and the west, the Amorite, the Hittite, the Perizzite and the Jebusite in the hill country, and the Hivite under Hermon in the land of Mizpah.

**4** They went out, they and all their armies with them, a people as many as the sand on the lip of the sea for multitude, with very many horses and chariots.

**5** All these kings met by appointment, and came and camped together at the waters of Merom to fight against Israel.

**6** The LORD said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid of them, for tomorrow at this time I will give all of them slain before Israel. You shall hamstring their horses and burn their chariots with fire."

**7** So Joshua and all the fighting men with him came upon them suddenly at the waters of Merom and fell upon them.

**8** The LORD gave them into the hand of Israel, and they struck them and pursued them as far as Great Sidon and Misrephoth-maim, and eastward as far as the Valley of Mizpeh. They struck them until they had left them no survivor.

**9** Joshua did to them as the LORD had told him: he hamstrung their horses and burned their chariots with fire.

## Hazor Burned

**10** Joshua turned back at that time and took Hazor, and struck its king with the sword, for Hazor formerly was the head of all these kingdoms.

**11** They struck every person in it with the sword, devoting them to destruction. Nothing that breathed was left, and he burned Hazor with fire.

**12** All the cities of these kings, and all their kings, Joshua took, and struck them with the sword. He devoted them to destruction, as Moses the servant of the LORD had commanded.

**13** But the cities standing on their mounds Israel did not burn, except Hazor alone, which Joshua burned.

**14** All the spoil of these cities and the livestock the sons of Israel took as plunder for themselves. But every human being they struck with the sword until they had destroyed them; they left nothing that breathed.

**15** As the LORD had commanded Moses His servant, so Moses commanded Joshua, and so Joshua did. He left nothing undone of all that the LORD had commanded Moses.

## The Whole Land Taken

**16** So Joshua took all this land: the hill country and all the Negev and all the land of Goshen, and the lowland and the Arabah, and the hill country of Israel and its lowland,

**17** from the bare mountain that rises toward Seir as far as Baal-gad in the Valley of Lebanon under Mount Hermon. He took all their kings and struck them and put them to death.

**18** Joshua made war many days with all these kings.

**19** There was not a city that made peace with the sons of Israel except the Hivites, the inhabitants of Gibeon. They took all of them in battle.

**20** For it was from the LORD to make their hearts strong to meet Israel in battle, so that they might be devoted to destruction and might receive no favor, but be destroyed, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**21** At that time Joshua came and cut off the Anakim from the hill country, from Hebron, from Debir, from Anab, and from all the hill country of Judah and from all the hill country of Israel. Joshua devoted them to destruction with their cities.

**22** None of the Anakim was left in the land of the sons of Israel; only in Gaza, in Gath and in Ashdod did some remain.

**23** So Joshua took the whole land, according to all that the LORD had spoken to Moses, and Joshua gave it for an inheritance to Israel according to their divisions by their tribes. Then the land had rest from war.

## Notes

- **v1 "Jabin king of Hazor"** — a king of the same name and city appears again in Judges 4:2, *Jabin king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor*.
- **v4 "as many as the sand on the lip of the sea"** — the image of God's promise to Abraham, Genesis 22:17, *as the sand that is on the lip of the sea*, here used for Israel's enemies.
- **vv6, 9 "hamstring their horses and burn their chariots"** — Israel is not to keep the weapons of the kings. Deuteronomy 17:16 forbade the king to *multiply horses*.
- **v10 "Hazor… the head of all these kingdoms"** — the leading city of the northern alliance; it alone is burned (v13).
- **v13 "standing on their mounds"** — *al tillam*: the *tel*, the mound of earlier ruins on which a city was rebuilt. The rest were left standing for Israel to live in, as Deuteronomy 6:10 promised: *great and good cities that you did not build*.
- **v15 "He left nothing undone"** — *lo hesir davar*, **he did not remove a word**: Joshua did all Moses commanded, as Moses did all God commanded.
- **v18 "many days"** — the campaigns of chapters 10–11, told as if at once, took a long time. Caleb's age in 14:7–10 puts it at about seven years.
- **v20 "to make their hearts strong"** — *le-chazzeq et libbam*, the verb of Exodus 4:21, *I Myself will make his heart strong*. The kings of Canaan are hardened as Pharaoh was.
- **vv21–22 the Anakim** — the giants whose size frightened the spies (Numbers 13:33, *the sons of Anak*). A remnant is left in Gaza, Gath and Ashdod, Philistine cities; Goliath of Gath will come from them (1 Samuel 17:4).
- **v23 "Then the land had rest from war"** — *ve-ha-arets shaqetah*, the refrain that later closes the stories of the judges (Judges 3:11). The summary looks ahead; 13:1 will say *very much of the land remains to be possessed*.

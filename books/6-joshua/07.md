# Joshua 7

## Defeat at Ai

**1** But the sons of Israel broke faith in the matter of what was devoted to destruction. Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took some of what was devoted, and the anger of the LORD burned against the sons of Israel.

**2** Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, which is near Beth-aven, east of Bethel, and said to them, "Go up and spy out the land." The men went up and spied out Ai.

**3** They returned to Joshua and said to him, "Do not let all the people go up. Let about two or three thousand men go up and strike Ai. Do not make all the people toil up there, for they are few."

**4** So about three thousand men of the people went up there, and they fled before the men of Ai.

**5** The men of Ai struck down about thirty-six of them and chased them from before the gate as far as Shebarim, and struck them down on the descent. The hearts of the people melted and became like water.

**6** Joshua tore his clothes and fell on his face to the ground before the ark of the LORD until evening, he and the elders of Israel, and they put dust on their heads.

**7** Joshua said, "Ah, Lord GOD, why did You ever bring this people across the Jordan, to give us into the hand of the Amorites, to destroy us? If only we had been content to stay beyond the Jordan!

**8** Please, Lord, what can I say, now that Israel has turned its back before its enemies?

**9** The Canaanites and all the inhabitants of the land will hear of it and surround us and cut off our name from the earth. What will You do for Your great name?"

## Israel Has Sinned

**10** The LORD said to Joshua, "Get up. Why are you lying on your face?

**11** Israel has sinned. They have also transgressed My covenant that I commanded them. They have also taken some of what was devoted; they have also stolen, and also lied, and also put it among their own belongings.

**12** That is why the sons of Israel cannot stand before their enemies; they turn their backs before their enemies, because they have become devoted to destruction. I will not be with you anymore unless you destroy what is devoted from among you.

**13** Get up, make the people holy, and say, 'Make yourselves holy for tomorrow, for thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: There is something devoted among you, Israel. You cannot stand before your enemies until you remove what is devoted from among you.'

**14** In the morning you shall come near by your tribes. And it shall come to pass that the tribe that the LORD takes shall come near by clans, and the clan that the LORD takes shall come near by households, and the household that the LORD takes shall come near man by man.

**15** And it shall come to pass that the one who is taken with what is devoted shall be burned with fire, he and all that he has, because he has transgressed the covenant of the LORD and because he has done an outrage in Israel."

## Achan Is Taken

**16** Joshua rose early in the morning and brought Israel near by tribes, and the tribe of Judah was taken.

**17** He brought near the clans of Judah, and he took the clan of the Zerahites. He brought near the clan of the Zerahites man by man, and Zabdi was taken.

**18** He brought near his household man by man, and Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was taken.

**19** Joshua said to Achan, "My son, please give glory to the LORD, the God of Israel, and give Him praise. Tell me now what you have done; do not hide it from me."

**20** Achan answered Joshua and said, "Truly, I have sinned against the LORD, the God of Israel. This and this is what I did:

**21** I saw among the spoil a beautiful cloak from Shinar, and two hundred shekels of silver, and a bar of gold weighing fifty shekels, and I coveted them and took them. They are hidden in the ground inside my tent, with the silver underneath."

**22** Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to the tent, and it was hidden in his tent, with the silver underneath.

**23** They took them from inside the tent and brought them to Joshua and to all the sons of Israel, and they poured them out before the LORD.

**24** Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan son of Zerah, and the silver and the cloak and the bar of gold, and his sons and his daughters, his ox and his donkey and his sheep, and his tent and all that he had, and brought them up to the Valley of Achor.

**25** Joshua said, "Why have you brought trouble on us? The LORD will bring trouble on you today." All Israel stoned him. They burned them with fire and stoned them.

**26** They raised over him a great heap of stones, which is there to this day. Then the LORD turned from the heat of His anger. That is why the name of that place is called the Valley of Achor to this day.

## Notes

- **v1 "the sons of Israel broke faith"** — *va-yim'alu… ma'al*: one man takes, and the text says Israel sinned. God says the same in v11, *Israel has sinned*, and v12, *they have become devoted to destruction*. The warning of 6:18 has come true: *make the camp of Israel something devoted to destruction*.
- **vv3–5 the second spies** — this time the report is confident and the attack fails. *Thirty-six* men fall; *the hearts of the people melted*, the phrase used of the Canaanites in 2:11 and 5:1, now said of Israel.
- **v7 "Ah, Lord GOD"** — *ahah Adonai YHWH*, a cry of dismay. *If only we had been content to stay beyond the Jordan* is the complaint of the wilderness generation, now in Joshua's mouth.
- **v9 "What will You do for Your great name?"** — the argument Moses used in Deuteronomy 9:28: what will the nations say?
- **v11 "also… also… also"** — *ve-gam*, five times, piling up the charges: sinned, transgressed, taken, stolen, lied, hidden.
- **vv14–18 taken by lot** — tribe, clan, household, man. The verb *lakhad*, **take, capture**, is the verb of taking a city; now it is used of Israel's own.
- **v19 "give glory to the LORD… and give Him praise"** — *ten lo todah*: confess, and so acknowledge that God has judged rightly.
- **v21 "I saw… I coveted… I took"** — *va-ere… va-echmedem… va-eqqachem*. *Chamad* is the verb of the Ten Words, *You shall not covet* (Exodus 20:17). The order is Eve's in Genesis 3:6: she *saw*, it was *desirable*, she *took*.
- **v25 "Why have you brought trouble on us? The LORD will bring trouble on you"** — *akhartanu… ya'kor'kha*, the verb of 6:18, *bring trouble on it*, and the root of *Achor*. 1 Chronicles 2:7 even calls him *Achar, the troubler of Israel, who broke faith with what was devoted*. Hosea 2:17 (2:15 in English) turns the name around: *the Valley of Achor as a door of hope*, *tiqvah*, the word of Rahab's cord (2:18).
- **v26 "a great heap of stones"** — the second heap in the book, after the twelve stones of Gilgal: one to remember a crossing, one to remember a sin.

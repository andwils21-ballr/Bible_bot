# Joshua 2

## Rahab Hides the Spies

**1** Joshua son of Nun sent two men secretly from Shittim as spies, saying, "Go, look over the land, and Jericho." They went and came into the house of a prostitute whose name was Rahab, and they lay down there.

**2** It was told to the king of Jericho, "Men of the sons of Israel have come here tonight to search out the land."

**3** The king of Jericho sent to Rahab, saying, "Bring out the men who came to you, who entered your house, for they have come to search out all the land."

**4** But the woman had taken the two men and hidden them. She said, "Yes, the men came to me, but I did not know where they were from.

**5** When the gate was about to be shut at dark, the men went out. I do not know where the men went. Pursue them quickly, for you will overtake them."

**6** But she had brought them up to the roof and hidden them under the stalks of flax that she had laid out on the roof.

**7** The men pursued them on the road to the Jordan, as far as the fords, and the gate was shut as soon as the pursuers had gone out after them.

## The Oath on the Roof

**8** Before the men lay down, she came up to them on the roof

**9** and said to the men, "I know that the LORD has given you the land, and that terror of you has fallen on us, and that all the inhabitants of the land melt before you.

**10** For we have heard how the LORD dried up the waters of the Sea of Reeds before you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, to Sihon and to Og, whom you devoted to destruction.

**11** We heard, and our hearts melted, and no spirit remained in anyone because of you, for the LORD your God — He is God in the heavens above and on the earth below.

**12** And now, please swear to me by the LORD, since I have shown you loyal love, that you also will show loyal love to my father's house, and give me a true sign,

**13** that you will let my father and my mother, my brothers and my sisters, and all who belong to them live, and deliver our lives from death."

**14** The men said to her, "Our life for yours, to die — if you do not tell this business of ours. And it shall come to pass, when the LORD gives us the land, that we will deal with you in loyal love and truth."

**15** Then she let them down by a rope through the window, for her house was in the wall of the city, and she lived in the wall.

**16** She said to them, "Go to the hill country, so that the pursuers do not come upon you. Hide there three days until the pursuers return, and afterward go your way."

**17** The men said to her, "We will be free of this oath of yours that you have made us swear,

**18** unless, when we come into the land, you tie this cord of scarlet thread in the window through which you let us down, and gather your father and your mother, your brothers and all your father's house to you into the house.

**19** And it shall come to pass that anyone who goes out of the doors of your house into the street, his blood will be on his own head, and we will be free. But anyone who is with you in the house, his blood will be on our head if a hand is laid on him.

**20** If you tell this business of ours, we will be free of your oath that you have made us swear."

**21** She said, "According to your words, so be it." She sent them away, and they went, and she tied the scarlet cord in the window.

## The Spies Return

**22** They went and came to the hill country and stayed there three days, until the pursuers returned. The pursuers searched all along the road and did not find them.

**23** Then the two men came back down from the hill country, crossed over, and came to Joshua son of Nun and told him all that had happened to them.

**24** They said to Joshua, "Truly the LORD has given all the land into our hands, and all the inhabitants of the land melt before us."

## Notes

- **v1 "two men… as spies"** — forty years earlier Moses sent twelve, and ten brought back fear (Numbers 13–14). Joshua, one of the two who did not, sends two, *secretly*.
- **v1 "a prostitute whose name was Rahab"** — *ishah zonah*. Hebrews 11:31 and James 2:25 both call her *Rahab the prostitute*, and both praise her. Matthew 1:5 puts her in the line of David: *Salmon fathered Boaz by Rahab*.
- **v6 "stalks of flax"** — *pishtei ha-ets*, **flax of the tree**: the long stalks, laid out on the flat roof to dry before their fibers were beaten out for linen.
- **v9 "terror of you has fallen on us… all the inhabitants of the land melt"** — the Song of the Sea, Exodus 15:15–16: *All who live in Canaan melted in fear. Terror and dread fell on them.* Rahab reports that the song has come true.
- **v11 "He is God in the heavens above and on the earth below"** — the confession of Deuteronomy 4:39, *the LORD is God in heaven above and on the earth below*, spoken by a Canaanite woman.
- **vv12, 14 "loyal love"** — *chesed*, the covenant word. She asks for it, and the men promise *chesed ve-emet*, **loyal love and truth**, the pair of God's own name in Exodus 34:6, *abundant in loyal love and truth*.
- **v18 "this cord of scarlet thread"** — *tiqvat chut ha-shani*. *Tiqvah* means **cord** here and in v21; elsewhere it is the ordinary word for **hope**. The scarlet in the window marks the house to be spared, as the blood on the doorposts did (Exodus 12:13).
- **v19 "his blood will be on his own head"** — the house is a place of safety only for those inside it, as on the night of the Passover no one was to *go out from the doorway of his house until morning* (Exodus 12:22).
- **v24 "Truly the LORD has given all the land into our hands"** — the report the spies of Numbers 13 did not bring.

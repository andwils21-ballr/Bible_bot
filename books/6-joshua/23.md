# Joshua 23

## The LORD Fought for You

**1** A long time afterward, when the LORD had given Israel rest from all their enemies around them, and Joshua was old, coming on in days,

**2** Joshua called for all Israel, for their elders and their heads, their judges and their officials, and said to them, "I am old, coming on in days.

**3** You have seen all that the LORD your God has done to all these nations for your sake, for it is the LORD your God who has fought for you.

**4** See, I have allotted to you as an inheritance for your tribes these nations that remain, along with all the nations I have cut off, from the Jordan to the Great Sea in the west.

**5** The LORD your God will push them back before you and drive them out of your sight, and you shall possess their land, as the LORD your God promised you.

## Hold Fast to the LORD

**6** Be very strong, then, to keep and to do all that is written in the book of the instruction of Moses, not turning aside from it to the right or to the left,

**7** so that you do not mix with these nations that remain among you. Do not speak the names of their gods, do not swear by them, do not serve them and do not bow down to them.

**8** Hold fast to the LORD your God, as you have done to this day.

**9** The LORD has driven out great and strong nations before you, and as for you, no one has stood against you to this day.

**10** One man of you chases a thousand, for it is the LORD your God who fights for you, as He promised you.

**11** Take great care for your own lives to love the LORD your God.

**12** For if you ever turn back and cling to what is left of these nations that remain among you, and marry among them, and you go in to them and they to you,

**13** know for certain that the LORD your God will no longer drive these nations out before you. They will be a snare and a trap for you, a whip on your sides and thorns in your eyes, until you perish from this good land that the LORD your God has given you.

## Not One Word Has Failed

**14** Today I am going the way of all the earth. You know with all your heart and with all your soul that not one word has failed of all the good words that the LORD your God spoke concerning you. All have come to pass for you; not one word of them has failed.

**15** And it shall come to pass that just as every good word the LORD your God spoke to you has come upon you, so the LORD will bring upon you every evil word, until He has destroyed you from this good land that the LORD your God has given you,

**16** when you break the covenant of the LORD your God, which He commanded you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them. Then the anger of the LORD will burn against you, and you will perish quickly from the good land that He has given you."

## Notes

- **v1 "old, coming on in days"** — *zaqen ba ba-yamim*, the words said of Joshua in 13:1 and of Abraham in Genesis 24:1. The chapter is Joshua's farewell, as Deuteronomy is Moses'.
- **v3 "it is the LORD your God who has fought for you"** — the promise of Deuteronomy 1:30 and 3:22, *the LORD your God is the one who fights for you*. Joshua repeats it in v10.
- **v6 "not turning aside from it to the right or to the left"** — the charge given to Joshua himself in 1:7, now passed on to the people.
- **v7 "Do not speak the names of their gods"** — *lo tazkiru*, **do not call to mind, do not mention**, as in Exodus 23:13, *The name of other gods you shall not mention; it shall not be heard on your mouth*.
- **v8 "Hold fast"** — *tidbaqu*, the verb of Deuteronomy 10:20 and 13:4. In v12 the same verb turns the other way: *cling* to the nations that remain.
- **v10 "One man of you chases a thousand"** — the reverse of the curse in Deuteronomy 32:30, *How could one chase a thousand… unless their Rock had sold them?*
- **v12 "if you ever turn back"** — *shov tashuvu*, **turn back, you turn back**, a doubled verb for emphasis. So in v13, *yadoa ted'u*, **know, you will know**: *know for certain*.
- **v13 "a snare and a trap… a whip on your sides and thorns in your eyes"** — the warning of Numbers 33:55, *barbs in your eyes and thorns in your sides*, with the thorns moved to the eyes. Judges 2:3 recalls it when the warning comes true.
- **v14 "the way of all the earth"** — death. David says the same words to Solomon in 1 Kings 2:2. *Not one word has failed* repeats 21:45.
- **vv15–16** — the good words and the evil words are both the LORD's; the blessing and the curse of Deuteronomy 28 stand side by side.

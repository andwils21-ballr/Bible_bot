# Joshua 18

## The Tent of Meeting at Shiloh

**1** The whole congregation of the sons of Israel assembled at Shiloh, and they set up the tent of meeting there. The land was subdued before them.

**2** There remained among the sons of Israel seven tribes whose inheritance had not yet been divided.

**3** Joshua said to the sons of Israel, "How long will you be slack about going in to possess the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, has given you?

**4** Appoint for yourselves three men from each tribe, and I will send them out. They shall get up and walk through the land and write a description of it according to their inheritance, and come back to me.

**5** They shall divide it into seven portions. Judah shall stay in its territory on the south, and the house of Joseph shall stay in its territory on the north.

**6** You shall write a description of the land in seven portions and bring it here to me, and I will cast lots for you here before the LORD our God.

**7** For the Levites have no portion among you, for the priesthood of the LORD is their inheritance. Gad and Reuben and the half-tribe of Manasseh have received their inheritance beyond the Jordan to the east, which Moses the servant of the LORD gave them."

**8** So the men got up and went, and Joshua commanded those who went to write a description of the land, saying, "Go and walk through the land and write a description of it, and come back to me, and I will cast lots for you here before the LORD in Shiloh."

**9** The men went and passed through the land and wrote a description of it by cities, in seven portions, in a book, and came to Joshua at the camp at Shiloh.

**10** Joshua cast lots for them in Shiloh before the LORD, and there Joshua divided the land to the sons of Israel according to their divisions.

## Benjamin

**11** The lot of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin came up according to their clans, and the territory of their lot went out between the sons of Judah and the sons of Joseph.

**12** Their border on the north side was from the Jordan; the border went up to the slope north of Jericho, went up through the hill country westward, and ended at the wilderness of Beth-aven.

**13** From there the border crossed to Luz, to the slope of Luz southward, that is, Bethel, and the border went down to Ataroth-addar, on the mountain south of Lower Beth-horon.

**14** The border was drawn and turned on the west side southward from the mountain that faces Beth-horon on the south, and it ended at Kiriath-baal, that is, Kiriath-jearim, a city of the sons of Judah. This was the west side.

**15** The south side was from the edge of Kiriath-jearim, and the border went out westward and went out to the spring of the waters of Nephtoah.

**16** The border went down to the edge of the mountain that faces the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, which is at the north end of the Valley of Rephaim. It went down the Valley of Hinnom, to the slope of the Jebusite on the south, and went down to En-rogel.

**17** It was drawn on the north, went out to En-shemesh, went out to Geliloth, which is opposite the Ascent of Adummim, and went down to the Stone of Bohan son of Reuben.

**18** It crossed to the slope opposite the Arabah on the north and went down to the Arabah.

**19** The border crossed to the slope of Beth-hoglah on the north, and the border ended at the north tongue of the Salt Sea, at the south end of the Jordan. This was the south border.

**20** The Jordan was its border on the east side. This is the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin, by its borders all around, according to their clans.

**21** The cities of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin according to their clans were Jericho, Beth-hoglah, Emek-keziz,

**22** Beth-arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel,

**23** Avvim, Parah, Ophrah,

**24** Chephar-haammonah, Ophni and Geba: twelve cities with their villages;

**25** Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth,

**26** Mizpeh, Chephirah, Mozah,

**27** Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah,

**28** Zela, Haeleph, the Jebusite, that is, Jerusalem, Gibeath and Kiriath: fourteen cities with their villages. This is the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin according to their clans.

## Notes

- **v1 "Shiloh… the tent of meeting"** — the center of worship moves from Gilgal to Shiloh, where it stays until the days of Eli and Samuel (1 Samuel 1:3). *The land was subdued before them*, *nikhbeshah*, the verb of Numbers 32:22, *the land is subdued before the LORD*.
- **v3 "How long will you be slack"** — *mitrappim*, **letting your hands drop**. Seven tribes have not taken their share, and Joshua rebukes them.
- **vv4–9 the survey** — three men from each tribe walk the land and *write a description of it… in a book*, *al sefer*. The land is divided by writing and by lot, before the LORD.
- **v7 "the priesthood of the LORD is their inheritance"** — a third way of saying it, after *the offerings by fire* (13:14) and *the LORD* Himself (13:33).
- **v11 "between the sons of Judah and the sons of Joseph"** — Benjamin, Rachel's younger son, lies between the two great houses; Jerusalem sits on his border with Judah (v16, 15:8).
- **vv15–19 the south border** — the same line as Judah's north border in 15:5–9, walked in the other direction.
- **v28 "the Jebusite, that is, Jerusalem"** — listed among Benjamin's cities, though 15:63 says Judah could not drive the Jebusites out; Judges 1:21 says the same of Benjamin.

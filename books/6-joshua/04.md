# Joshua 4

## Stones from the Riverbed

**1** And it came to pass, when the whole nation had finished crossing the Jordan, that the LORD said to Joshua,

**2** "Take for yourselves twelve men from the people, one man from each tribe,

**3** and command them, saying, 'Take up for yourselves twelve stones from here, from the middle of the Jordan, from the place where the priests' feet stood firm, and carry them over with you and set them down in the lodging place where you spend the night.'"

**4** Joshua called the twelve men whom he had appointed from the sons of Israel, one man from each tribe.

**5** Joshua said to them, "Cross over before the ark of the LORD your God into the middle of the Jordan, and each of you lift a stone on his shoulder, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Israel,

**6** so that this may be a sign among you. When your sons ask tomorrow, saying, 'What are these stones to you?'

**7** you shall say to them, 'The waters of the Jordan were cut off before the ark of the covenant of the LORD. When it crossed the Jordan, the waters of the Jordan were cut off.' These stones shall be a memorial for the sons of Israel forever."

**8** The sons of Israel did so, as Joshua commanded. They took up twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, as the LORD had told Joshua, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Israel, and they carried them over with them to the lodging place and set them down there.

**9** Joshua also set up twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan, in the place where the feet of the priests who carried the ark of the covenant had stood, and they are there to this day.

## The Ark Comes Up

**10** The priests carrying the ark stood in the middle of the Jordan until everything was finished that the LORD commanded Joshua to tell the people, according to all that Moses had commanded Joshua. The people hurried and crossed over.

**11** And it came to pass, when all the people had finished crossing, that the ark of the LORD and the priests crossed over before the people.

**12** The sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh crossed over in ranks before the sons of Israel, as Moses had told them.

**13** About forty thousand armed for war crossed over before the LORD to battle, to the plains of Jericho.

**14** On that day the LORD made Joshua great in the eyes of all Israel, and they feared him as they had feared Moses, all the days of his life.

**15** The LORD said to Joshua,

**16** "Command the priests who carry the ark of the Testimony to come up out of the Jordan."

**17** Joshua commanded the priests, saying, "Come up out of the Jordan."

**18** And it came to pass, when the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD came up from the middle of the Jordan, and the soles of the priests' feet were lifted up onto dry land, that the waters of the Jordan returned to their place and flowed over all its banks as before.

## The Stones at Gilgal

**19** The people came up out of the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month, and they camped at Gilgal on the east edge of Jericho.

**20** Those twelve stones that they had taken from the Jordan Joshua set up at Gilgal.

**21** He said to the sons of Israel, "When your sons ask their fathers tomorrow, saying, 'What are these stones?'

**22** you shall let your sons know, saying, 'Israel crossed this Jordan on dry land.'

**23** For the LORD your God dried up the waters of the Jordan before you until you had crossed, as the LORD your God did to the Sea of Reeds, which He dried up before us until we had crossed,

**24** so that all the peoples of the earth may know the hand of the LORD, that it is strong, so that you may fear the LORD your God all the days."

## Notes

- **vv3, 9 two sets of twelve stones** — one set carried to the camp at Gilgal (v20), the other set up in the riverbed where the priests stood (v9), *there to this day*: one visible to all, one under the water.
- **vv6–7, 21–22 "When your sons ask tomorrow"** — the question of the Passover (Exodus 13:14, *when your son asks you tomorrow, 'What is this?'*) and of the commandments (Deuteronomy 6:20). The answer is again a story: *Israel crossed this Jordan on dry land*.
- **v12 "in ranks"** — *chamushim*, as in 1:14 and Exodus 13:18; the two and a half tribes keep their promise to Moses (Numbers 32).
- **v14 "they feared him as they had feared Moses"** — the promise of 3:7 kept: *Today I will begin to make you great*.
- **v16 "the ark of the Testimony"** — the name used in Exodus for the ark holding the tablets of the Testimony. Elsewhere in these chapters it is *the ark of the covenant*.
- **v19 "the tenth day of the first month"** — the day the Passover lamb was chosen in Egypt (Exodus 12:3, *On the tenth of this month*). Forty years later to the season, Israel comes into the land, and keeps Passover four days later (5:10).
- **v19 "Gilgal"** — from *galal*, **to roll**; 5:9 gives the name its meaning.
- **v23 "as the LORD your God did to the Sea of Reeds"** — the Jordan is the second sea. The first crossing ended the slavery; the second begins the inheritance.
- **v24 "so that all the peoples of the earth may know"** — Rahab had already heard (2:10). The sign is for the nations as well as for Israel's sons.

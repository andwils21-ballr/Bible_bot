# Joshua 14

## Dividing the Land by Lot

**1** These are the inheritances that the sons of Israel received in the land of Canaan, which Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun and the heads of the fathers' houses of the tribes of the sons of Israel distributed to them.

**2** Their inheritance was by lot, as the LORD had commanded through Moses, for the nine tribes and the half-tribe.

**3** For Moses had given the inheritance of the two tribes and the half-tribe beyond the Jordan, but to the Levites he gave no inheritance among them.

**4** For the sons of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim. They gave no portion to the Levites in the land, except cities to live in, with their pasture lands for their livestock and their possessions.

**5** As the LORD had commanded Moses, so the sons of Israel did, and they divided the land.

## Give Me This Hill Country

**6** The sons of Judah came to Joshua at Gilgal, and Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite said to him, "You know the word that the LORD spoke to Moses the man of God concerning me and concerning you at Kadesh-barnea.

**7** I was forty years old when Moses the servant of the LORD sent me from Kadesh-barnea to spy out the land, and I brought him back word as it was in my heart.

**8** My brothers who went up with me made the heart of the people melt, but I followed the LORD my God fully.

**9** Moses swore on that day, saying, 'Surely the land on which your foot has walked shall be an inheritance for you and for your sons forever, because you have followed the LORD my God fully.'

**10** And now the LORD has kept me alive, as He said, these forty-five years since the LORD spoke this word to Moses, while Israel walked in the wilderness. And now, today I am eighty-five years old.

**11** I am still as strong today as I was on the day Moses sent me. As my strength was then, so my strength is now, for war and for going out and coming in.

**12** And now give me this hill country of which the LORD spoke on that day, for you heard on that day that the Anakim were there, with great fortified cities. Perhaps the LORD will be with me, and I will drive them out, as the LORD said."

**13** Joshua blessed him, and he gave Hebron to Caleb son of Jephunneh as an inheritance.

**14** That is why Hebron became the inheritance of Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite to this day, because he followed the LORD, the God of Israel, fully.

**15** The name of Hebron before was Kiriath-arba; Arba was the greatest man among the Anakim. Then the land had rest from war.

## Notes

- **v1 "Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun and the heads of the fathers' houses"** — the three named in Numbers 34:17–18 for dividing the land. Eleazar comes first; the land is given before the LORD.
- **v2 "by lot"** — *be-goral*, as Numbers 26:55 commanded. The lot leaves the choice to God.
- **v4 "cities to live in, with their pasture lands"** — *migresheihem*, the pasture land around the Levites' towns commanded in Numbers 35:2–5. Chapter 21 lists them.
- **v6 "Caleb… the Kenizzite"** — Kenaz is also a name among the clans of Edom (Genesis 36:11). Caleb comes with the sons of Judah, yet carries a clan name found outside Israel, and he is the one held up as having *followed the LORD fully*.
- **vv7–9** — Caleb tells the story of Numbers 13–14 in his own words. *As it was in my heart*: his report was honest. The ten *made the heart of the people melt*, the phrase of 2:11, 5:1 and 7:5.
- **vv8, 9, 14 "followed the LORD… fully"** — *millé acharei YHWH*, three times in the chapter, the words of Numbers 14:24, *he has followed Me fully*, and Deuteronomy 1:36.
- **v9 "the land on which your foot has walked"** — Deuteronomy 1:36: *to him and to his sons I will give the land he has walked on*.
- **v10 "these forty-five years… eighty-five years old"** — forty at Kadesh, thirty-eight years of wandering, and about seven years of conquest since the crossing.
- **v12 "Perhaps the LORD will be with me"** — at eighty-five, Caleb asks for the hardest ground in the land: the hill country of the Anakim who frightened the spies.
- **v15 "Kiriath-arba"** — **town of Arba**, the older name of Hebron, as in Genesis 23:2, *Kiriath-arba — that is Hebron*, where Sarah died.

# Joshua 8

## The Ambush

**1** The LORD said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid and do not lose heart. Take all the fighting men with you, and get up, go up to Ai. See, I have given into your hand the king of Ai, and his people, his city and his land.

**2** You shall do to Ai and its king as you did to Jericho and its king, except that you may take its spoil and its livestock as plunder for yourselves. Set an ambush against the city behind it."

**3** So Joshua and all the fighting men got up to go up to Ai. Joshua chose thirty thousand mighty men of valor and sent them out by night.

**4** He commanded them, saying, "See, you are to lie in ambush against the city, behind the city. Do not go very far from the city, and all of you be ready.

**5** I and all the people who are with me will approach the city. And it shall come to pass, when they come out against us as at the first, that we will flee before them.

**6** They will come out after us until we have drawn them away from the city, for they will say, 'They are fleeing before us as at the first.' So we will flee before them,

**7** and you shall rise up from the ambush and seize the city, and the LORD your God will give it into your hand.

**8** And it shall come to pass, when you have taken the city, that you shall set the city on fire. You shall do according to the word of the LORD. See, I have commanded you."

**9** So Joshua sent them out, and they went to the place of ambush and stayed between Bethel and Ai, west of Ai. Joshua spent that night among the people.

**10** Joshua rose early in the morning and mustered the people, and went up, he and the elders of Israel, before the people to Ai.

**11** All the fighting men who were with him went up and drew near and came opposite the city, and camped north of Ai, with the valley between them and Ai.

**12** He took about five thousand men and set them in ambush between Bethel and Ai, west of the city.

**13** So they stationed the people, the whole camp that was north of the city, and its rear guard west of the city. Joshua went that night into the middle of the valley.

**14** And it came to pass, when the king of Ai saw it, that the men of the city hurried and rose early and went out to meet Israel in battle, he and all his people, at the appointed place facing the Arabah. But he did not know that there was an ambush against him behind the city.

**15** Joshua and all Israel let themselves be struck before them and fled by the way of the wilderness.

**16** All the people who were in Ai were called out to pursue them, and they pursued Joshua and were drawn away from the city.

**17** Not a man was left in Ai or Bethel who did not go out after Israel. They left the city open and pursued Israel.

## The Javelin Held Out

**18** The LORD said to Joshua, "Stretch out the javelin in your hand toward Ai, for I will give it into your hand." Joshua stretched out the javelin that was in his hand toward the city.

**19** The ambush rose quickly from its place, and they ran as soon as he stretched out his hand, and entered the city and took it, and hurried to set the city on fire.

**20** The men of Ai turned back and looked, and the smoke of the city was going up to the heavens, and they had no power to flee this way or that. The people who had fled to the wilderness turned back against the pursuers.

**21** When Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush had taken the city and that the smoke of the city was going up, they turned back and struck down the men of Ai.

**22** The others came out of the city against them, so that they were in the middle of Israel, some on this side and some on that. They struck them down until no survivor or fugitive was left.

**23** The king of Ai they took alive and brought him to Joshua.

**24** And it came to pass, when Israel had finished killing all the inhabitants of Ai in the field, in the wilderness where they had pursued them, and all of them had fallen by the sword until they were finished, that all Israel returned to Ai and struck it with the sword.

**25** All who fell that day, men and women, were twelve thousand, all the people of Ai.

**26** Joshua did not draw back his hand with which he stretched out the javelin until he had devoted all the inhabitants of Ai to destruction.

**27** Only the livestock and the spoil of that city Israel took as plunder for themselves, according to the word of the LORD that He commanded Joshua.

**28** Joshua burned Ai and made it a heap forever, a desolation, to this day.

**29** The king of Ai he hanged on a tree until evening. At sunset Joshua commanded, and they took his body down from the tree and threw it at the entrance of the city gate, and raised over it a great heap of stones, which is there to this day.

## The Altar on Mount Ebal

**30** Then Joshua built an altar to the LORD, the God of Israel, on Mount Ebal,

**31** as Moses the servant of the LORD had commanded the sons of Israel, as it is written in the book of the instruction of Moses: an altar of whole stones, on which no one had wielded any iron tool. They offered burnt offerings on it to the LORD and sacrificed peace offerings.

**32** There, on the stones, he wrote a copy of the instruction of Moses, which he had written before the sons of Israel.

**33** All Israel, with their elders and officials and judges, stood on either side of the ark, opposite the Levitical priests who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD, the guest as well as the native, half of them in front of Mount Gerizim and half of them in front of Mount Ebal, as Moses the servant of the LORD had commanded at the first, to bless the people Israel.

**34** Afterward he read all the words of the instruction, the blessing and the curse, according to all that is written in the book of the instruction.

**35** There was not a word of all that Moses commanded that Joshua did not read before all the assembly of Israel, with the women and the little ones and the guests who were going among them.

## Notes

- **v1 "Do not be afraid and do not lose heart"** — the words of Deuteronomy 1:21 and 31:8, after the defeat of chapter 7.
- **v2 "you may take its spoil and its livestock"** — what Achan died for taking at Jericho is given freely at Ai. Had he waited, it would have been his.
- **vv3, 12 thirty thousand… five thousand** — two numbers for the ambush. Either there were two ambushes, or the text has kept two accounts side by side; it does not say which.
- **v17 "Ai or Bethel"** — Bethel's men are mentioned only here in the battle; the two towns lay close together, as in Genesis 12:8, *Bethel on the west and Ai on the east*.
- **vv18, 26 "Stretch out the javelin"** — *kidon*, a short spear. Joshua holds it out until the battle is over, as Moses held up his hands against Amalek *until the sun went down* (Exodus 17:12), with Joshua fighting below.
- **v28 "a heap forever"** — *tel olam*, the words of Deuteronomy 13:16 for a town devoted to destruction. *Ai*, *ha-Ai*, means **the ruin**.
- **v29 the king on a tree** — taken down *at sunset*, as Deuteronomy 21:23 commands: *his body shall not remain overnight on the tree*.
- **vv30–35 the altar on Mount Ebal** — the command of Deuteronomy 27:2–8 carried out: *an altar of whole stones… You shall not wield any iron tool on them*, and the instruction written on stones. The blessing and the curse are read, as Deuteronomy 11:29 and 27:12–13 required.
- **v32 "a copy of the instruction of Moses"** — *mishneh torat Moshe*, the phrase of Deuteronomy 17:18, the king's copy.
- **v35 "the women and the little ones and the guests"** — the gathering of Deuteronomy 31:12, *the men and the women and the little ones, and your guest*. No one is left out of the hearing.

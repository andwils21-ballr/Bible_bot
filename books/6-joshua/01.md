# Joshua 1

## The LORD Speaks to Joshua

**1** And it came to pass after the death of Moses the servant of the LORD that the LORD said to Joshua son of Nun, Moses' attendant,

**2** "Moses My servant is dead. And now get up, cross this Jordan, you and all this people, into the land that I am giving to them, to the sons of Israel.

**3** Every place on which the sole of your foot treads I have given to you, as I promised Moses.

**4** From the wilderness and this Lebanon as far as the great river, the river Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites, to the Great Sea toward the setting of the sun, will be your border.

**5** No one will be able to stand before you all the days of your life. As I was with Moses, so I will be with you. I will not let you go or forsake you.

**6** Be strong and courageous, for you will cause this people to inherit the land that I swore to their fathers to give them.

**7** Only be strong and very courageous, to be careful to do according to all the instruction that Moses My servant commanded you. Do not turn from it to the right or to the left, so that you may prosper wherever you go.

**8** This book of the instruction shall not depart from your mouth. You shall meditate on it day and night, so that you may be careful to do according to all that is written in it, for then you will make your way succeed, and then you will prosper.

**9** Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not tremble and do not lose heart, for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go."

## Three Days

**10** Then Joshua commanded the officials of the people, saying,

**11** "Pass through the camp and command the people, saying, 'Prepare provisions for yourselves, for in three days you are crossing this Jordan to go in and possess the land that the LORD your God is giving you to possess.'"

**12** To the Reubenites, to the Gadites and to the half-tribe of Manasseh Joshua said,

**13** "Remember the word that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you, saying, 'The LORD your God is giving you rest and will give you this land.'

**14** Your wives, your little ones and your livestock shall stay in the land that Moses gave you beyond the Jordan, but you shall cross over in ranks before your brothers, all the mighty men of valor, and help them,

**15** until the LORD gives your brothers rest as He has given you, and they too possess the land that the LORD your God is giving them. Then you shall return to the land of your possession and possess it, the land that Moses the servant of the LORD gave you beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise."

**16** They answered Joshua, saying, "All that you have commanded us we will do, and wherever you send us we will go.

**17** Just as we listened to Moses in all things, so we will listen to you. Only may the LORD your God be with you, as He was with Moses.

**18** Anyone who rebels against your command and does not listen to your words in all that you command him shall be put to death. Only be strong and courageous."

## Notes

- **v1 "after the death of Moses the servant of the LORD"** — the book opens where Deuteronomy ends (Deuteronomy 34:5, *Moses the servant of the LORD died there*). Joshua is *Moses' attendant*, *meshart Moshe*, the young man of Exodus 33:11 who *would not move out of the tent*.
- **v2 "Moses My servant is dead. And now get up"** — no time is given to grief beyond the thirty days of Deuteronomy 34:8. The promise now passes to the one who is left.
- **vv3–5** — God repeats to Joshua the words of Deuteronomy 11:24–25, *Every place on which the sole of your foot treads*, and of Deuteronomy 31:6, *He will not let you go or forsake you*, now in the first person: *I will not let you go*.
- **vv6, 7, 9, 18 "Be strong and courageous"** — *chazaq ve-emats*, four times in the chapter, as Moses said it to Joshua in Deuteronomy 31:7 and 23. The last time it is the people who say it to him.
- **v7 "all the instruction that Moses My servant commanded you"** — the courage asked of Joshua is not first for battle but for keeping the instruction, *to the right or to the left* (Deuteronomy 17:11, 20).
- **v8 "meditate on it day and night"** — *ve-hagita bo*, **murmur it**, the verb of reading aloud under the breath. Psalm 1:2 says the same of the happy man: *on His instruction he meditates day and night*.
- **v11 "in three days"** — the crossing will come in 3:2, *at the end of three days*, after the spies of chapter 2 return.
- **vv12–15 the two and a half tribes** — the agreement of Numbers 32:16–18, *we ourselves will arm and go quickly before the sons of Israel*, now called in. *In ranks*, *chamushim*, the word of Exodus 13:18, where *the sons of Israel went up out of the land of Egypt in ranks*.
- **v18 "Only be strong and courageous"** — the people give Joshua back the words God gave him.

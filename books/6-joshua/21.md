# Joshua 21

## The Levites Ask for Their Towns

**1** The heads of the fathers' houses of the Levites came near to Eleazar the priest and to Joshua son of Nun and to the heads of the fathers' houses of the tribes of the sons of Israel.

**2** They spoke to them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, saying, "The LORD commanded through Moses that we be given towns to live in, with their pasture lands for our livestock."

**3** So the sons of Israel gave to the Levites from their inheritance, at the command of the LORD, these towns and their pasture lands.

## The Lots of the Clans

**4** The lot came out for the clans of the Kohathites. To the sons of Aaron the priest, of the Levites, came thirteen towns by lot from the tribe of Judah, the tribe of the Simeonites and the tribe of Benjamin.

**5** The rest of the sons of Kohath received ten towns by lot from the clans of the tribe of Ephraim, the tribe of Dan and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**6** The sons of Gershon received thirteen towns by lot from the clans of the tribe of Issachar, the tribe of Asher, the tribe of Naphtali and the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.

**7** The sons of Merari according to their clans received twelve towns from the tribe of Reuben, the tribe of Gad and the tribe of Zebulun.

**8** The sons of Israel gave these towns and their pasture lands to the Levites by lot, as the LORD had commanded through Moses.

## The Sons of Aaron

**9** From the tribe of the sons of Judah and the tribe of the sons of Simeon they gave these towns, which are here mentioned by name.

**10** They went to the sons of Aaron, of the clans of the Kohathites, of the sons of Levi, for the lot fell to them first.

**11** They gave them Kiriath-arba, Arba being the father of Anak, that is, Hebron, in the hill country of Judah, with the pasture lands around it.

**12** But the fields of the town and its villages they had given to Caleb son of Jephunneh as his possession.

**13** To the sons of Aaron the priest they gave Hebron, the town of refuge for the one who killed, with its pasture lands, Libnah with its pasture lands,

**14** Jattir with its pasture lands, Eshtemoa with its pasture lands,

**15** Holon with its pasture lands, Debir with its pasture lands,

**16** Ain with its pasture lands, Juttah with its pasture lands, and Beth-shemesh with its pasture lands: nine towns from these two tribes.

**17** From the tribe of Benjamin: Gibeon with its pasture lands, Geba with its pasture lands,

**18** Anathoth with its pasture lands and Almon with its pasture lands: four towns.

**19** All the towns of the sons of Aaron, the priests: thirteen towns with their pasture lands.

## The Rest of the Kohathites

**20** As for the clans of the sons of Kohath, the Levites who remained of the sons of Kohath, the towns of their lot were from the tribe of Ephraim.

**21** They gave them Shechem, the town of refuge for the one who killed, with its pasture lands in the hill country of Ephraim, Gezer with its pasture lands,

**22** Kibzaim with its pasture lands and Beth-horon with its pasture lands: four towns.

**23** From the tribe of Dan: Elteke with its pasture lands, Gibbethon with its pasture lands,

**24** Aijalon with its pasture lands and Gath-rimmon with its pasture lands: four towns.

**25** From the half-tribe of Manasseh: Taanach with its pasture lands and Gath-rimmon with its pasture lands: two towns.

**26** All the towns for the clans of the rest of the sons of Kohath: ten, with their pasture lands.

## The Gershonites

**27** To the sons of Gershon, of the clans of the Levites, from the half-tribe of Manasseh: Golan in Bashan, the town of refuge for the one who killed, with its pasture lands, and Beeshterah with its pasture lands: two towns.

**28** From the tribe of Issachar: Kishion with its pasture lands, Daberath with its pasture lands,

**29** Jarmuth with its pasture lands and En-gannim with its pasture lands: four towns.

**30** From the tribe of Asher: Mishal with its pasture lands, Abdon with its pasture lands,

**31** Helkath with its pasture lands and Rehob with its pasture lands: four towns.

**32** From the tribe of Naphtali: Kedesh in Galilee, the town of refuge for the one who killed, with its pasture lands, Hammoth-dor with its pasture lands and Kartan with its pasture lands: three towns.

**33** All the towns of the Gershonites according to their clans: thirteen towns with their pasture lands.

## The Merarites

**34** To the clans of the sons of Merari, the rest of the Levites, from the tribe of Zebulun: Jokneam with its pasture lands, Kartah with its pasture lands,

**35** Dimnah with its pasture lands and Nahalal with its pasture lands: four towns.

**36** From the tribe of Reuben: Bezer with its pasture lands, Jahaz with its pasture lands,

**37** Kedemoth with its pasture lands and Mephaath with its pasture lands: four towns.

**38** From the tribe of Gad: Ramoth in Gilead, the town of refuge for the one who killed, with its pasture lands, Mahanaim with its pasture lands,

**39** Heshbon with its pasture lands and Jazer with its pasture lands: four towns in all.

**40** All the towns for the sons of Merari according to their clans, the rest of the clans of the Levites: their lot was twelve towns.

**41** All the towns of the Levites within the holding of the sons of Israel were forty-eight towns with their pasture lands.

**42** These towns each had its pasture lands around it; so it was with all these towns.

## Not One Word Failed

**43** So the LORD gave Israel all the land that He had sworn to give to their fathers, and they possessed it and settled in it.

**44** The LORD gave them rest all around, according to all that He had sworn to their fathers. Not one of all their enemies stood before them; the LORD gave all their enemies into their hand.

**45** Not one word failed of all the good word that the LORD had spoken to the house of Israel. It all came to pass.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 the Levites ask** — as Caleb (14:6) and the daughters of Zelophehad (17:4) did, the Levites come and claim what Moses was commanded to give them (Numbers 35:2).
- **vv4–7 the three clans** — Kohath, Gershon and Merari, the sons of Levi (Numbers 26:57). The priests, Aaron's sons, are a branch of Kohath and receive their towns in Judah, Simeon and Benjamin, near where the temple will stand.
- **v10 "the lot fell to them first"** — Aaron's line is chosen first.
- **vv11–12 Hebron** — the town goes to the priests, but *the fields of the town and its villages* stay Caleb's (14:13–14).
- **v18 "Anathoth"** — the priestly town from which the prophet Jeremiah came (Jeremiah 1:1).
- **v25 "Gath-rimmon"** — named also in v24, for Dan; 1 Chronicles 6:55 has *Bileam* in this second place. The repetition may be a copying slip.
- **vv13, 21, 27, 32, 38 the towns of refuge** — all six are Levite towns, each marked *the town of refuge for the one who killed*: Hebron, Shechem, Golan, Kedesh, Ramoth, and Bezer (v36) without the phrase.
- **v41 "forty-eight towns"** — the number of Numbers 35:7, *All the towns that you give the Levites shall be forty-eight towns*.
- **vv43–45 "Not one word failed"** — *lo nafal davar*, **not a word fell** to the ground. The conquest summary ends in praise: *It all came to pass*. Joshua will say the same in 23:14, *not one word has failed*, and yet in 23:4–5 he speaks of *these nations that remain*: the promise is kept, and what remains is Israel's part.

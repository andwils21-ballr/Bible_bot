# Joshua 16

## The Sons of Joseph

**1** The lot for the sons of Joseph went out from the Jordan at Jericho, east of the waters of Jericho, into the wilderness, going up from Jericho through the hill country to Bethel.

**2** It went out from Bethel to Luz and crossed to the border of the Archites at Ataroth,

**3** and went down westward to the border of the Japhletites, as far as the border of Lower Beth-horon and as far as Gezer, and it ended at the sea.

**4** So the sons of Joseph, Manasseh and Ephraim, received their inheritance.

## Ephraim

**5** The border of the sons of Ephraim according to their clans was this: the border of their inheritance on the east was Ataroth-addar as far as Upper Beth-horon,

**6** and the border went out to the sea. Michmethath was on the north; the border turned east to Taanath-shiloh and passed along it east of Janoah,

**7** went down from Janoah to Ataroth and to Naarah, touched Jericho and ended at the Jordan.

**8** From Tappuah the border went west to the Brook of Kanah, and it ended at the sea. This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Ephraim according to their clans,

**9** together with the cities set apart for the sons of Ephraim within the inheritance of the sons of Manasseh, all the cities with their villages.

**10** But they did not drive out the Canaanites who lived in Gezer, and the Canaanites have lived among Ephraim to this day, and have become forced labor.

## Notes

- **v1 "the lot for the sons of Joseph"** — one lot for two tribes. Jacob had given Joseph a double share by adopting his sons, *Ephraim and Manasseh, like Reuben and Simeon, shall be mine* (Genesis 48:5). The complaint of 17:14 grows from this single lot.
- **v2 "from Bethel to Luz"** — Genesis 28:19 says *Luz was the name of the city at first*, before Jacob called it Bethel. Here the two seem to be distinguished, perhaps the town and the nearby holy place.
- **vv5–8 Ephraim first** — the younger son is placed before the elder, as Jacob crossed his hands in Genesis 48:14 and put Ephraim ahead of Manasseh.
- **v9 "cities set apart for the sons of Ephraim within the inheritance of… Manasseh"** — the two brother tribes' land is intertwined; 17:8–9 says the same from Manasseh's side.
- **v10 "they did not drive out the Canaanites who lived in Gezer"** — after Judah's failure at Jerusalem (15:63), Ephraim's at Gezer. 1 Kings 9:16 tells that Pharaoh later took Gezer, *burned it with fire, and killed the Canaanites who lived in the city*, and gave it to his daughter, Solomon's wife. *Forced labor*, *mas oved*, is what Deuteronomy 20:10–15 allowed only for cities far away.

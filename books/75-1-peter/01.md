# 1 Peter 1

## Greeting

**1** Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the chosen exiles of the Dispersion in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia and Bithynia,

**2** chosen according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, by the Spirit who makes you holy, for obedience and for sprinkling with the blood of Jesus Christ: grace and peace be multiplied to you.

## Born Again to a Living Hope

**3** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who in His great mercy has caused us to be born again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,

**4** to an inheritance that cannot perish, be defiled or fade away, kept in heaven for you,

**5** who by the power of God are being guarded through faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.

**6** In this you rejoice, though now for a little while, if need be, you have been grieved by various trials,

**7** so that the testing of your faith, more precious than gold, which perishes though it is tested by fire, may be found to result in praise and glory and honor at the revelation of Jesus Christ.

**8** Though you have not seen Him, you love Him. Though you do not see Him now, you believe in Him and rejoice with joy that is inexpressible and filled with glory,

**9** obtaining the outcome of your faith, the salvation of your souls.

**10** Concerning this salvation, the prophets who prophesied about the grace that was to be yours searched and inquired carefully,

**11** inquiring what time, or what kind of time, the Spirit of Christ in them was pointing to when He foretold the sufferings of Christ and the glories that would follow.

**12** It was revealed to them that they were serving not themselves but you, in the things that have now been announced to you through those who preached the good news to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven, things into which angels long to look.

## Be Holy

**13** So prepare your minds for action, be fully sober, and set your hope on the grace that will be brought to you at the revelation of Jesus Christ.

**14** As obedient children, do not be shaped by the desires you had before, in your ignorance,

**15** but as He who called you is holy, you also be holy in all your conduct,

**16** since it is written, "You shall be holy, because I am holy."

**17** If you call on Him as Father who judges impartially according to each one's work, conduct yourselves with fear throughout the time you live here as guests,

**18** knowing that you were ransomed from the empty way of life handed down from your fathers, not with perishable things such as silver or gold,

**19** but with the precious blood of Christ, like that of a lamb without defect or spot.

**20** He was foreknown before the foundation of the world, but was revealed in the last times for your sake,

**21** who through Him believe in God, who raised Him from the dead and gave Him glory, so that your faith and hope are in God.

**22** Having purified your souls by your obedience to the truth, for a sincere brotherly love, love one another earnestly from the heart,

**23** since you have been born again, not of perishable seed but of imperishable, through the living and abiding word of God.

**24** For  
"All flesh is like grass,  
and all its glory like the flower of grass.  
The grass withers,  
and the flower falls,

**25** but the word of the Lord remains forever."  
This word is the good news that was preached to you.

## Notes

- **vv13–19 "prepare your minds for action… the time you live here as guests… a lamb without defect"** — v13 reads literally *girding up the loins of your mind*, the dress of the first Passover, *your waists belted… You shall eat it in haste* (Exodus 12:11). The lamb of that night was *a lamb without defect* (Exodus 12:5); the word of v19, *amōmos*, is the Greek law's word for an offering without defect (*amōmon*, Leviticus 22:21), where the Greek of Exodus 12:5 has *teleion*, **perfect**. Peter writes to *exiles* (v1) who live as guests (v17) and were ransomed with a lamb's blood, as Israel was on the way out of Egypt.
- **vv24–25 "All flesh is like grass… but the word of the Lord remains forever"** — Isaiah 40:6–8 in the Greek. The Hebrew reads *all its loyalty*, *chasdo*, where the Greek has *all the glory of man*, and *the word of our God will stand forever*, where Peter has *the word of the Lord*.

# 1 Peter 2

## The Living Stone

**1** So put away all malice and all deceit, hypocrisy and envy, and all slander.

**2** Like newborn babies, long for the pure milk of the word, so that by it you may grow up to salvation,

**3** if indeed you have tasted that the Lord is good.

**4** Come to Him, a living stone, rejected by men but chosen and precious in God's sight,

**5** and you yourselves, like living stones, are being built up as a spiritual house, to be a holy priesthood, to offer spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.

**6** For it stands in Scripture:  
"I am laying in Zion a stone,  
a chosen and precious cornerstone,  
and whoever believes in Him will not be put to shame."

**7** So the honor is for you who believe; but for those who do not believe,  
"The stone the builders rejected  
has become the cornerstone,"

**8** and  
"a stone to strike against and a rock to stumble over."  
They stumble because they disobey the word; to this they were also appointed.

## A Chosen People

**9** But you are a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for His own possession, so that you may proclaim the excellencies of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light.

**10** Once you were not a people, but now you are the people of God; once you had not received mercy, but now you have received mercy.

## Living as Guests

**11** Beloved, I urge you as guests and exiles to keep away from the desires of the flesh, which wage war against the soul.

**12** Keep your conduct among the Gentiles good, so that when they speak against you as evildoers, they may see your good works and glorify God on the day of visitation.

**13** Submit yourselves for the Lord's sake to every human institution, whether to the king as supreme,

**14** or to governors as sent by him to punish those who do evil and to praise those who do good.

**15** For this is the will of God, that by doing good you should silence the ignorance of foolish people.

**16** Live as free people, not using your freedom as a cover for evil, but as servants of God.

**17** Honor everyone. Love the brotherhood. Fear God. Honor the king.

## The Example of Christ's Suffering

**18** Household servants, be subject to your masters with all fear, not only to the good and gentle but also to the crooked.

**19** For this is grace, when someone, because he is mindful of God, endures sorrows while suffering unjustly.

**20** For what credit is it if, when you sin and are beaten for it, you endure? But if you do good and suffer for it and endure, this is grace before God.

**21** For to this you were called, because Christ also suffered for you, leaving you an example, so that you might follow in His steps.

**22** He committed no sin,  
and no deceit was found in His mouth.

**23** When He was insulted, He did not insult in return; when He suffered, He did not threaten, but entrusted Himself to Him who judges justly.

**24** He Himself bore our sins in His body on the tree, so that we might die to sins and live to righteousness. By His wounds you have been healed.

**25** For you were straying like sheep, but now you have returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls.

## Notes

- **v2 "the pure milk of the word"** — *to logikon adolon gala*. *Logikos* is built on *logos*, **word**, the *living and abiding word of God* that has just been named (1:23–25), and it also means **of the mind**, **spiritual**, as in Paul's *reasonable worship* (Romans 12:1). *Adolon*, **pure**, is literally **without deceit**, against *all deceit*, *dolos*, put away in v1.
- **vv6–8 "a stone… the cornerstone… a stone to strike against"** — three stones from three books: Isaiah 28:16 in the Greek, Psalm 118:22, and Isaiah 8:14. Isaiah's Hebrew ends *whoever believes will not be in haste*; the Greek, which Peter quotes, *will not be put to shame*. The stone that is a foundation for those who believe is the one others stumble over.
- **vv9–10 "a royal priesthood, a holy nation… once you were not a people"** — the words of Sinai in the Greek, *you will be for Me a royal priesthood and a holy nation* (Exodus 19:6; the Hebrew has *a kingdom of priests*), and of Isaiah 43:20–21, *My chosen people… that they might declare My praise*. *Not a people… not received mercy* are Hosea's children, *Lo-ammi* and *Lo-ruhamah*, renamed *My people* (Hosea 1:9; 2:23).
- **vv22–25 "no deceit was found in His mouth… He Himself bore our sins… by His wounds… like sheep"** — Isaiah 53 in the Greek, in order: *no deceit in his mouth* (v9); *he bears our sins* (v4); *by his wound we were healed*, *tō mōlōpi*, the word of v24 (v5); *All we like sheep have gone astray* (v6).

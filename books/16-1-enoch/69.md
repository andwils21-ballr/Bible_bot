# 1 Enoch 69

**1** After this judgment they will terrify them and make them angry, because they showed this to those who live on the dry land.

**2** Here are the names of those angels, and these are their names: the first of them Semyaza, the second Arstiqefa, the third Armen, the fourth Kokabiel, the fifth Turel, the sixth Rumyal, the seventh Danel, the eighth Nuqael, the ninth Baraqiel, the tenth Azazel, the eleventh Armaros, the twelfth Batarael, the thirteenth Basasael, the fourteenth Ananel, the fifteenth Turyal, the sixteenth Simasipiel, the seventeenth Yetarel, the eighteenth Tumael, the nineteenth Tarel, the twentieth Rumael, the twenty-first Azazel.

**3** These are the chiefs of their angels, and their names, their chiefs of hundreds, their chiefs of fifties and their chiefs of tens.

**4** The name of the first is Yeqon. He is the one who led astray all the sons of the angels and brought them down to the dry land, and led them astray through the daughters of men.

**5** The second is named Asbeel. He gave evil counsel to the sons of the holy angels and led them astray, so that they ruined their bodies with the daughters of men.

**6** The third is named Gadreel. He is the one who showed the sons of men the blows of death, and he led Eve astray. He showed the sons of men the weapons of death — the shield and the breastplate and the sword for killing — all the weapons of death, to the sons of men.

**7** From his hand they have gone out against those who live on the dry land, from that time and forever.

**8** The fourth is named Penemue. He showed the sons of men the bitter and the sweet, and he showed them all the secrets of their wisdom.

**9** He taught men to write with ink and paper, and because of this many go astray, from eternity to eternity and to this day.

**10** For men were not born for this, to confirm their faith in this way, with pen and ink.

**11** For men were created no different from the angels, to remain righteous and pure, and death, which ruins everything, would not have touched them. But through this knowledge of theirs they perish, and because of this the power is consuming me.

**12** The fifth is named Kadeya. He showed the sons of men all the evil blows of souls and of demons, and the blows that strike the child in the womb so that it miscarries, and the blows against the soul, the bite of the serpent, and the blows that come at midday, the son of the serpent whose name is Taba.

**13** This is the task of Kasbeel, the chief of the oath, which he showed to the holy ones when he dwelt on high in glory, and its name is Beqa.

**14** He told holy Michael to show them the hidden name, so that they might see that hidden name and speak it in the oath, so that those who showed the sons of men everything hidden would tremble at that name and oath.

**15** This is the power of this oath, for it is powerful and strong, and he placed this oath, Akae, in the hand of holy Michael.

**16** These are the secrets of this oath, and they are made firm by his oath: the heaven was hung before the world was created, and forever.

**17** By it the earth was founded on the water, and from the hidden places of the mountains come beautiful waters for the living, from the creation of the world and forever.

**18** By that oath the sea was created, and He set sand as its foundation against the time of its anger, and it does not pass beyond it, from the creation of the world and forever.

**19** By that oath the deeps are made firm. They stand and do not move from their place, from eternity and forever.

**20** By that oath the sun and the moon complete their course and do not stray from their command, from eternity and forever.

**21** By that oath the stars complete their course. He calls their names, and they answer Him, from eternity and forever.

**22** So too the souls of the waters and the souls of the winds, and all their spirits, and their paths, from all the companies of the spirits.

**23** There the storehouses of the voice of the thunder and the light of the lightning are kept, and there the storehouses of the hail and the frost are kept, and the storehouses of the mist, and the storehouses of the rain and the dew.

**24** All of them believe and give thanks before the Lord of Spirits and praise Him with all their strength. Their food is in every thanksgiving: they give thanks and praise and exalt the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever.

**25** Over them this oath has been made firm, and they are kept by it. Their paths are kept, and their courses do not fail.

**26** There was great joy among them, and they blessed and praised and exalted, because the name of the Son of Man had been revealed to them.

**27** He sat on the throne of his glory, and the whole of judgment was given to him, the Son of Man. He will make the sinners pass away and perish from the face of the earth, and those who led the world astray.

**28** They will be bound with chains and shut up in their gathering place of destruction, and all their works will pass away from the face of the earth.

**29** From now on there will be nothing that perishes, for that Son of Man has appeared and sat on the throne of his glory, and all evil will pass away and depart from before his face. They will speak to that Son of Man, and he will be strong before the Lord of Spirits. This is the third parable of Enoch.

## Notes

- **v2 the twenty-one names** — a list that overlaps the one at 6:7 but is not the same: the order differs, and many names appear only here. Where a name has already appeared (6:7, 8:1, 8:3), the spelling used there is kept. The EOTC names Azazel twice, tenth and twenty-first; Knibb's Ge'ez has *ʾIzēzēʾēl* at twenty-one.
- **v3 "their chiefs of hundreds, their chiefs of fifties and their chiefs of tens"** — the ranks Jethro told Moses to set over Israel: *officers of thousands, officers of hundreds, officers of fifties, and officers of tens* (Exodus 18:21). The fallen angels are ranked as Israel was under Moses.
- **v4 "led astray all the sons of the angels… through the daughters of men"** — Genesis 6:2, *the sons of God saw the daughters of humankind, that they were good*. vv4–12 are a second list of five, each with a name and a charge.
- **v6 "he led Eve astray"** — in Genesis it is the serpent: *The serpent deceived me, and I ate* (Genesis 3:13). Here one of the fallen angels is named as the deceiver. *The shield and the breastplate and the sword* were Azazel's teaching at 8:1.
- **vv9–10 "He taught men to write with ink and paper"** — the same book calls Enoch *the scribe* (12:3) and has him hand the parables on to Noah in a book (68:1).
- **v11 "men were created no different from the angels… death… would not have touched them"** — the Wisdom of Solomon says the same: *God created man for incorruption… but through the envy of the devil death entered the world* (Wisdom 2:23–24). The verse ends with a *me* that the text does not explain.
- **v12 "the blows that come at midday"** — Psalm 91:6: *the destruction that lays waste at noon*. The name of the serpent's son is *Tabāʿ* in the EOTC and *Tabāʿət* in Knibb's Ge'ez.
- **vv13–15 the oath** — its names are *Beqa* and *Akae*, and it is placed in Michael's hand. By it the angels who revealed the secrets are made to tremble, and by it creation holds (vv16–25).
- **v18 "He set sand as its foundation… and it does not pass beyond it"** — Jeremiah 5:22: *I placed the sand as the boundary for the sea, an eternal decree, and it cannot pass it*.
- **v21 "He calls their names, and they answer Him"** — Psalm 147:4, *He counts the number of the stars; He calls them all by name*. The book said it at 43:1: *He calls them all by their names, and they listen to Him*.
- **v27 "the whole of judgment was given to him, the Son of Man"** — *ʾarʾəsu la-kʷənnanē*, **the head of judgment**. Jesus says of himself, *He gave Him authority to carry out judgment, because He is the Son of Man* (John 5:27).
- **v29 "that Son of Man"** — here the title takes its third Ge'ez form, *walda bəʾəsi*, **son of a man** (see 46:2, 62:5, 62:7). It returns at 71:14.
- **v29 "They will speak to that Son of Man, and he will be strong"** — *wa-yənaggəru la-wəʾətu walda bəʾəsi*, so both Ge'ez witnesses. Both English renderings give *the word of that Son of Man will go out and be strong*. The EOTC calls this *the third parable*; Knibb's Ge'ez has *the parable*.

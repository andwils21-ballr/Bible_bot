# 1 Enoch 15

**1** He answered and said to me with His voice, "Hear, and do not be afraid, Enoch, righteous man and scribe of righteousness. Come here and hear My word.

**2** Go, say to the Watchers of heaven who sent you to petition on their behalf: 'You ought to be petitioning on behalf of men, not men on your behalf.

**3** Why have you left the high, holy, eternal heaven, and lain with women, and defiled yourselves with the daughters of men, and taken wives for yourselves, and done as the sons of the earth do, and fathered sons who are giants?

**4** Though you were spirits, holy and living an eternal life, you defiled yourselves with women, and fathered children with the blood of flesh, and lusted with the blood of men, and did as they do — flesh and blood, who die and perish.

**5** That is why I gave them wives: so that they might sow seed in them, and children be born of them, and so nothing on the earth would be left undone.

**6** But you were spirits from the beginning, living an eternal life that does not die through all the generations of the world.

**7** That is why I did not make wives for you. The spirits of heaven — heaven is their dwelling.

## The Giants After They Die

**8** And now, the giants who were born of spirit and flesh will be called evil spirits on the earth, and on the earth will be their dwelling.

**9** Evil spirits came out from their bodies, because they were created from above. From the holy Watchers is their beginning and their first foundation. They will be evil spirits on the earth, and evil spirits they will be called.

**10** The spirits of heaven — in heaven is their dwelling. But the spirits born on the earth — on the earth is their dwelling.

**11** The spirits of the giants are clouds that harm: they oppress and corrupt and attack and fight and crush, upon the earth, and cause grief. They eat no food, and they do not thirst, and they are not seen.

**12** These spirits will rise up against the sons of men and against the women, because they came out from them.

## Notes

- **v2 "You ought to be petitioning on behalf of men, not men on your behalf"** — the sentence the whole episode has been built to reach. The angels' job was to carry human prayers up. They have inverted the order of creation so completely that a man is now carrying their prayers, and God's first response to the petition is to point at the direction it is traveling.
- **vv4–7 the argument** — and it is an argument, not a decree. Humans were given marriage **because they die**: children are how a mortal thing continues, and without them the work of the earth would stop. Spirits do not die, so they were not given wives, because they do not need the mechanism. The crime is not lust. It is beings that cannot die using the apparatus of death.
- **v4 "though you were spirits"** — the Ge'ez and the Greek both press the contradiction: holy, living, eternal — and then *the blood of flesh, the blood of men*. The word *blood* appears three times in one verse, of beings who have none.
- **vv8–9 where demons come from** — **this is the passage.** The giants die; their spirits do not. Born of a spirit father and a human mother, they are unclassifiable: heaven will not take them because they came from flesh, and earth will not release them because they came from above. So they stay here. Everything later literature says about unclean spirits roaming the earth starts in these two verses, and the Hebrew Bible says nothing of the kind anywhere.
- **v8 "will be called evil spirits"** — the Ge'ez says **evil**; the **Greek** says **strong** spirits. One letter of difference in the character of the thing being defined.
- **v10 "the spirits of heaven... the spirits born on the earth"** — the sorting principle, stated flatly: where you were born is where you belong. It is the same rule that condemns the giants, and it is the only reason given.
- **v11 "they eat no food, and they do not thirst"** — the Ge'ez. The **Greek** says the opposite: *eating nothing, but **fasting and thirsting***. One witness has beings with no appetite; the other has beings in permanent hunger. It is a real disagreement about what a demon is, and the second reading explains the hostility of v12 while the first does not.
- **v12 "these spirits will rise up against the sons of men"** — and here the two witnesses contradict each other outright. The **Greek** reads *and they **will** rise up against the sons of men and against the women*. The Ge'ez reads *these spirits shall **not** rise up*. A negative particle stands in one and not the other, and the sense of the passage turns on it. This rendering follows the Greek, because v11 has just described what the spirits do to people and chapter 16 goes on to say they will destroy until the last day — the surrounding verses assume the attack.

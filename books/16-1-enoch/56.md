# 1 Enoch 56

**1** There I saw hosts of the angels of punishment going, holding chains of iron and bronze.

**2** I asked the angel of peace who went with me, "To whom are these who are holding them going?"

**3** He said to me, "Each of them to their own chosen and beloved ones, so that they may be thrown into the deep chasms of the valley.

**4** Then that valley will be filled with their chosen and beloved ones. The day of their life will come to an end, and from then on the day of their going astray will no longer be counted.

**5** In those days the angels will gather and turn their heads to the east, toward the Parthians and the Medes. They will stir up the kings, and a spirit of unrest will come on them, and they will rouse them from their thrones. They will break out like lions from their lairs, and like hungry wolves among their flocks.

**6** They will go up and trample the land of His chosen ones, and the land of His chosen ones will become a threshing floor and a beaten path before them.

**7** But the city of My righteous ones will be a stumbling block to their horses. They will stir up slaughter among themselves, and their own right hand will be strong against them. A man will not know his neighbor or his brother, nor a son his father or his mother, until their corpses are counted up through their death and their punishment. It will not be in vain.

**8** In those days Sheol will open its mouth, and they will sink into it, to their destruction. Sheol will swallow the sinners from the presence of the chosen."

## Notes

- **v5 "the Parthians and the Medes"** — *sabʾa Pārte wa-Mēd*, **the people of Parthia and Media**. They head the list of nations in Jerusalem at Pentecost: *Parthians and Medes and Elamites* (Acts 2:9).
- **v8 "Sheol will open its mouth, and they will sink into it"** — the end of Korah and his company: *The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them… They and all that belonged to them went down alive to Sheol* (Numbers 16:32–33).

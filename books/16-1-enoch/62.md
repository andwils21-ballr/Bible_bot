# 1 Enoch 62

**1** This is what the Lord commanded the kings and the mighty and the exalted and the strong, and those who live on the earth. He said, "Open your eyes and lift up your horns, if you are able to recognize the Chosen One."

**2** The Lord of Spirits sat on the throne of His glory, and the spirit of righteousness was poured out on him, and the word of his mouth kills all the sinners and all the unrighteous, and they perish before his face.

**3** On that day all the kings and the mighty and the exalted and those who hold the earth will stand up, and they will see him and recognize him, how he sits on the throne of his glory. The righteous are judged before him in righteousness, and no idle word is spoken before him.

**4** Pain will come on them like a woman in labor, for whom giving birth is hard when her child comes to the mouth of her womb and she labors hard to give birth.

**5** One group of them will look at the other, and they will be terrified, and their faces will fall, and pain will seize them when they see that Son of a woman sitting on the throne of his glory.

**6** The kings and the mighty and all who hold the earth will praise and bless and exalt him who rules over all that is hidden.

**7** For from the beginning the Son of Man was hidden, and the Most High kept him in the presence of His host and revealed him to the chosen.

**8** The congregation of the holy and the chosen will be sown, and all the chosen will stand before him on that day.

**9** All the kings and the mighty and the exalted and those who rule the dry land will fall on their faces and worship. They will set their hope on that Son of Man, and plead with him, and ask him for mercy.

**10** But the Lord of Spirits will then press them to hurry out from His presence. Their faces will be filled with shame, and darkness will grow deeper on their faces.

**11** The angels of punishment will take them, to repay them for the wrong they did to His children and His chosen.

**12** They will be a spectacle for His chosen, and His righteous will rejoice over them, because the wrath of the Lord of Spirits rests on them, and the punishment of the Lord of Spirits is drunk with them.

**13** The righteous and the chosen will be saved on that day, and from then on they will never see the faces of the sinners and the unrighteous.

**14** The Lord of Spirits will rest on them, and they will live with that Son of Man. They will eat and drink, and lie down and rise up, forever and ever.

**15** The righteous and the chosen will rise from the earth and stop casting down their faces, and they will put on the garment of life.

**16** That will be the garment of life from the Lord of Spirits. Your garments will not wear out, and your glory will not fail before the Lord of Spirits.

## Notes

- **v1 "lift up your horns"** — the horn is strength and pride: *Do not lift up your horn on high, do not speak with a stiff neck* (Psalm 75:5). Here the kings are dared to do it.
- **v2 "The Lord of Spirits sat on the throne of His glory, and the spirit of righteousness was poured out on him"** — so both Ge'ez witnesses: the Lord of Spirits takes His throne, and the spirit is poured on the Chosen One of v1, who sits *on the throne of his glory* in vv3 and 5. Charles changes *sat* to *seated him*, as at 61:8. *The word of his mouth kills all the sinners* is Isaiah on the shoot of Jesse: *with the breath of his lips he will kill the wicked* (Isaiah 11:4).
- **v4 "like a woman in labor"** — *ka-bəʾəsit*. Paul uses the same picture for the day of the Lord: *sudden destruction comes on them, like labor pains on a woman with child, and they will not escape* (1 Thessalonians 5:3).
- **v5 "that Son of a woman"** — *zəku walda bəʾəsit*, in both Ge'ez witnesses. *Bəʾəsit* is **woman**, the word of v4's woman in labor, one verse before. Both English renderings give *that Son of Man*, as if the word were *bəʾəsi*, **man**; the two differ only by the last letter, and the EOTC has *walda bəʾəsi* where the title returns at 69:29 and 71:14. Paul says of Christ, *God sent His Son, born of a woman* (Galatians 4:4).
- **v7 "the Son of Man"** — here the title takes a second Ge'ez form for the first time: *walda ʿəgʷāla ʾəmmaḥəyāw*, **son of the offspring of the mother of the living**. *ʾƏmm* is **mother** (*his mother*, 56:7) and *ḥəyāw* **living** (*the books of the living*, 47:3). *The mother of all living* is the name Adam gave Eve (Genesis 3:20). The first form, *walda sabʾ*, is at 46:2–4 and 48:2; this one returns at vv9 and 14, 63:11, 69:26–27, 70:1 and 71:17.
- **v7 "from the beginning the Son of Man was hidden"** — see 48:6, *he was chosen and hidden before Him before the world was created*.
- **vv9–10 "ask him for mercy… But the Lord of Spirits will then press them"** — the time for mercy has ended, as 50:5 said it would: *From now on I will have no mercy on them*.
- **v12 "the punishment of the Lord of Spirits is drunk with them"** — the EOTC, *maqśaftu*. Knibb's Ge'ez has *maṭbāḥtu*, **His sword**, the image of Deuteronomy 32:42: *I will make My arrows drunk with blood, and My sword will devour flesh*. The EOTC also opens the verse with *They will be a spectacle for His chosen*, which Knibb's Ge'ez lacks.
- **v14 "They will eat and drink"** — the EOTC adds *drink*. Jesus to the twelve: *that you may eat and drink at My table in My kingdom* (Luke 22:30).
- **v15 "stop casting down their faces"** — the verb of v5, where the kings' *faces will fall*. The downcast faces pass from the righteous to the kings.
- **v16 "Your garments will not wear out"** — Moses to Israel: *your clothes have not worn out on you* in forty years in the wilderness (Deuteronomy 29:5).

# 1 Enoch 10

**1** Then the Most High, the Great and Holy One, spoke, and sent a messenger to the son of Lamech, saying to him:

**2** "Say to him in My name, 'Hide yourself,' and reveal to him the end that is coming — that the whole earth is perishing, and a flood of water is about to come over the whole earth and destroy everything on it.

**3** And teach him how he may escape, so that his seed may remain for all the generations of eternity."

**4** And to Raphael He said, "Bind Azazel hand and foot, and cast him into the darkness. And open the desert that is in Dudael, and cast him there.

**5** And lay beneath him rough and sharp stones, and cover him with darkness, and let him stay there forever, and cover his face so that he may not see light.

**6** And on the day of the great judgment he will be led away into the burning fire.

**7** And heal the earth, which the angels have ruined, and proclaim the healing of the earth — that I may heal it, and that not all the sons of men may perish through the whole mystery that the Watchers disclosed and taught to their sons.

**8** And the whole earth was laid waste, corrupted by the teaching of the works of Azazel. Ascribe all sin to him."

**9** And to Gabriel the Lord said, "Go against the bastards and the half-breeds and the sons of fornication, and destroy the sons of the Watchers from among men. Send them against one another in a war of destruction, for they will not have length of days.

**10** And they will all plead with you, but nothing will be granted to their fathers on their behalf — though they hope to live an eternal life, and that each of them may live five hundred years."

**11** And to Michael He said, "Go, make known to Shemihazah and to the rest with him, who joined themselves to the women to defile themselves with them in their uncleanness,

**12** and when their sons have slaughtered one another and they have seen the destruction of their beloved ones, bind them for seventy generations under the hills of the earth, until the day of their judgment and their consummation, until the judgment that is forever and ever is finished.

**13** And in those days they will be led away into the abyss of fire, into the torment and into the prison of eternal confinement.

**14** And whoever is burned and destroyed from then on will be bound together with them until the end of all generations.

**15** And destroy all the spirits of the half-breeds and the sons of the Watchers, because they have wronged mankind.

**16** Destroy all violence from the face of the earth, and let every wicked deed come to an end, and let the plant of righteousness and truth appear — and it will be a blessing, and the works of righteousness and truth will be planted forever with joy.

**17** And now all the righteous will escape, and they will live until they father thousands, and all the days of their youth and their sabbaths they will complete in peace.

**18** And in those days the whole earth will be worked in righteousness, and all of it planted with trees, and it will be full of blessing.

**19** And all the trees of joy will be planted on it, and they will plant vines on it, and the vine planted on it will yield fruit in abundance. And of all the seed sown on it, one measure will produce ten thousand, and one measure of olives will produce ten presses of oil.

**20** And you — cleanse the earth from all oppression and from all injustice and from all sin and from all godlessness, and wipe out all the uncleanness that is done on the earth.

**21** And let all the sons of men become righteous, and let all the nations worship and praise Me, and all of them bow down to Me.

**22** And the earth will be cleansed from all corruption and from all sin and from all plague and from all torment, and I will never again send a flood upon it, for all generations and forever."

## Notes

- **v1 "a messenger"** — the witnesses name him differently, and one of the names carries meaning. The Greek has **Ἰστραήλ, Istrael**, which ends in *-ēl* — *El*, **God**, the ending every angel in this book is built on: Micha**el**, Gabri**el**, Rapha**el**, Uri**el**, Baraqi**el**, Kokabi**el**. And it is one letter from Ἰσραήλ, **Israel**: a single *tau* inserted. Israel is the name Jacob is given in Genesis 32:28, explained there as *you have striven with God and with men and have prevailed*. The Ge'ez has **Arsyalalyur**, which has no *-el* ending and no recoverable sense; it looks like a name that has drifted in copying. Whether the Greek preserves a real name or has pulled an unfamiliar one toward a familiar one, nothing in the witnesses decides.
- **v1 "the son of Lamech"** — Noah, and the book does not name him. Genesis 5:28–29 has Lamech father a son and call him Noah. Here the man who will survive the flood is identified only by his father, and he never speaks.
- **v4 "Bind Azazel... and open the desert that is in Dudael"** — and this is the verse that connects the book to the Day of Atonement. **Leviticus 16** has two goats: one for the LORD, and one *for **Azazel***, which is *sent away to Azazel, into the wilderness* (16:10). The Hebrew Bible never says who or what Azazel is. This book says he is a bound angel in a desert, and puts him there by name.
- **v5 "cover his face so that he may not see light"** — the punishment is precisely reversed from the crime. He revealed what was hidden; he is hidden where nothing is revealed to him.
- **v7 "heal the earth"** — Raphael's assignment, and his name means *God heals*. The book gives each of the four a task matching what he is called: Raphael heals, Gabriel (*God is my strength*) goes to war, Michael (*who is like God?*) carries the sentence to the chief who overreached.
- **v8 "Ascribe all sin to him"** — the entire corruption is assigned to one name, in a chapter that has just listed a dozen teachers. The verse reads like a legal finding rather than a description, and it is the sentence the scapegoat ritual enacts: everything laid on one head and sent out.
- **v9 "send them against one another"** — the giants are not killed by heaven. They are set to destroy each other, and Gabriel's job is to arrange it. The flood, when it comes, is not what ends them.
- **v10 "each of them may live five hundred years"** — set this beside **Genesis 6:3**, where God says of the same generation *his days shall be a hundred and twenty years*. Both texts respond to the same crisis with a number, and the numbers do not match.
- **v12 "bind them for seventy generations under the hills"** — **Jude 6** is this verse: *the angels who did not keep their own domain but left their own dwelling, He has kept in eternal chains under darkness for the judgment of the great day.* **2 Peter 2:4** says the same and names the place *Tartarus*. Two New Testament letters describe the punishment of the Watchers, and this is where it is written down.
- **v16 "the plant of righteousness"** — the image the book will keep returning to. What replaces the ruined world is not a rescue but a planting, and vv18–19 take it literally: trees, vines, seed, oil.
- **v19 the yields** — the Ge'ez says one measure of seed produces **ten thousand**; the Greek says **a thousand**. The Ge'ez says one measure of olives gives **ten presses**; the Greek says **ten baths**. Both are describing an impossible harvest, and neither agrees with the other on how impossible.
- **v22 "I will never again send a flood upon it"** — the promise of **Genesis 9:11**, spoken here before the flood has happened. The whole chapter is set in the days of Noah and speaks from after them.

# 1 Enoch 2

**1** Observe all the work in heaven, how the lights in heaven do not change their paths, how each one rises and sets, each appointed in its season, and they do not overstep their command.

**2** Look at the earth, and think carefully about the work done on it, from the beginning to the end — how no work of God changes as it appears.

**3** Look at the summer and the winter, how the whole earth is filled with water, and cloud and dew and rain settle on it.

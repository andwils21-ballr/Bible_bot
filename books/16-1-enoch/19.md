# 1 Enoch 19

**1** Uriel said to me, "Here the angels who mingled with women will stand, and their spirits, taking many different forms, defile mankind and will lead them astray to sacrifice to demons as gods — until the day of the great judgment, in which they will be judged until they are finished.

**2** Their wives, the wives of the angels who went astray, will become sirens."

**3** I, Enoch, alone saw the vision, the ends of all things. No one among men will see as I have seen.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "to sacrifice to demons as gods"** — the book's explanation of where idolatry comes from. Not carved wood and not mistake: the spirits of the fallen angels take shapes and are worshiped in them. Every god of the nations, on this account, is a Watcher in costume.
- **v2 "will become sirens"** — the Greek word is **σειρῆνας**, *seirēnas*, and it is exactly the word Homer uses. There is no Semitic creature behind it; the sirens are Greek, from the Odyssey, women whose singing draws sailors onto rocks. The Ge'ez keeps the word too. A Jewish book about the sons of Seth and the daughters of men ends its account of the Watchers' wives by turning them into a Greek myth, and every surviving witness preserves it. Whatever else this tells us, it dates the world the book was written in.

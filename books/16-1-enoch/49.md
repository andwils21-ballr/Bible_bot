# 1 Enoch 49

**1** For wisdom is poured out like water, and glory does not fail before him forever and ever.

**2** For he is mighty in all the secrets of righteousness, and wrongdoing will pass like a shadow and have no standing, because the Chosen One stands before the Lord of Spirits, and his glory is forever and ever, and his might for all generations.

**3** In him dwells the spirit of wisdom, and the spirit that gives understanding, and the spirit of instruction and of might, and the spirit of those who have fallen asleep in righteousness.

**4** He will judge the hidden things, and no one will be able to speak an idle word before him, because he is the Chosen One before the Lord of Spirits, as He has willed.

---

## Notes

- **v3 "the spirit of wisdom… of instruction and of might"** — Isaiah 11:2 on the shoot from the stump of Jesse: *the Spirit of the LORD will rest on him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might*. The Parables add a spirit Isaiah does not have: *the spirit of those who have fallen asleep in righteousness*.

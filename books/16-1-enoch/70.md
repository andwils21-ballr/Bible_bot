# 1 Enoch 70

**1** And it came to pass after this that his name, while he was living, was lifted up to the presence of that Son of Man and to the Lord of Spirits, from among those who live on the dry land.

**2** He was lifted up on the chariots of the spirit, and his name went out from among them.

**3** From that day I was no longer counted among them. He set me between the two winds, between the north and the west, where the angels took the cords to measure for me the place for the chosen and the righteous.

**4** There I saw the first fathers and the righteous, who have lived in that place from of old.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "his name, while he was living, was lifted up"** — *səmu ḥəyāw*, **his name, living**. The OCP's English of the Ge'ez gives *his living name*; Charles, *during his lifetime*. Either way Enoch is taken without dying, as Genesis tells it: *Enoch walked with God. He was not, for God took him* (Genesis 5:24). The chapter speaks of Enoch as *he* in vv1–2, and in v3 he speaks as *I*.
- **v2 "on the chariots of the spirit"** — Elijah is taken the same way: *a chariot of fire and horses of fire… and Elijah went up in a whirlwind to heaven* (2 Kings 2:11).
- **v3 "the angels took the cords to measure for me the place"** — the measuring cords of 61:1–5, where the angels went out *to take the measurements of the righteous*.

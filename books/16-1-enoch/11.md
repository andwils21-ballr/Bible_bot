# 1 Enoch 11

**1** "In those days I will open the storehouses of blessing that are in heaven, to send them down upon the earth, upon the work and upon the labor of the sons of men.

**2** Peace and truth will be joined together for all the days of the world and for all the generations of eternity."

---

## Notes

- **v1 "the storehouses of blessing"** — treasuries, granaries: rooms in heaven where something is kept until it is released. The book uses the same picture later for the winds, the snow and the hail, each with its own store. Here what is warehoused is blessing, and the flood chapter ends by opening it.
- **v1 "upon the work and upon the labor of the sons of men"** — what is blessed is the working, not the workers. In 7:3 the giants *consumed all the labor of men*, using the same word for it. The chapter closes the account: what was eaten is repaid.
- **v2 "peace and truth"** — the Ge'ez has peace first, the Greek has *truth and peace*. They are also the two things denied to the Watchers, in this book's standing refrain: *there will be no peace for them* (5:4, 12:5, 13:1). The two-verse chapter exists to say that what the guilty are refused is given to everyone else.

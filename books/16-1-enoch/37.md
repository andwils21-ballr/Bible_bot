# 1 Enoch 37

**1** The vision he saw, the second vision of wisdom, which Enoch saw, the son of Jared, son of Mahalalel, son of Kenan, son of Enosh, son of Seth, son of Adam.

**2** This is the beginning of the words of wisdom that I took up to speak and to say to those who live on the dry land: Hear, you who were first, and see, you who come after, the holy words that I speak before the Lord of Spirits.

**3** It would be better to say these things to those who were first, but even from those who come after we will not hold back the beginning of wisdom.

**4** Until now such wisdom as I have received has not been given by the Lord of Spirits: according to my insight, according to the good pleasure of the Lord of Spirits, from whom the portion of eternal life has been given to me.

**5** Three parables came to me, and I speak them to those who live on the dry land.

## Notes

- **The whole chapter** — this opens the second part of the book, chapters 37–71, called *the Parables* from v5. From here to the end of chapter 71 the Ge'ez is the only witness: no Greek, Aramaic or Latin of these chapters survives.
- **v1 "son of Jared… son of Adam"** — seven names, Adam to Enoch, the line of Genesis 5. Jude counts the same way when he quotes this book: *Enoch, the seventh from Adam* (Jude 14). Luke traces the same line upward to *Adam, the son of God* (Luke 3:37–38).
- **v2 "the Lord of Spirits"** — *ʾƎgziʾa Manāfəst*, the name for God in the Parables, 106 times in chapters 37–71 of the EOTC text and never before this verse. Chapter 39:12 shows where it comes from.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. Knibb's Ge'ez and its English: Online Critical Pseudepigrapha, CC BY 4.0. English translation by this project.

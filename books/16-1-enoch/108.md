# 1 Enoch 108

**1** Another book, which Enoch wrote for his son Methuselah and for those who will come after him and keep the law in the last days.

**2** You who have done good and wait in these days until those who do evil are brought to an end, and the might of the transgressors is brought to an end —

**3** as for you, wait until sin passes away. For their name will be wiped out of the books of the holy ones, and their seed will perish forever, and their spirits will be killed. They will cry out and lament in a waste place that cannot be seen, and burn in fire, for there is no earth there.

**4** There I saw something like a cloud that could not be seen through, for because of its depth I could not look over it. I saw a flame of fire burning brightly, and things like bright mountains circling and swirling this way and that.

**5** I asked one of the holy angels who was with me and said to him, "What is this bright thing? For it is not a heaven, but only a flame of blazing fire, and the sound of crying and weeping and lament and fierce pain."

**6** He said to me, "This place that you see: here are thrown the spirits of the sinners and the blasphemers, and of those who do evil, and of those who twist all that the Lord has spoken through the mouth of His prophets, the things that are to be done on the earth.

**7** For some of them are written and engraved above in heaven, so that the angels may read them and know what will come on the sinners, and on the spirits of the humble, and on those who afflicted their bodies and were repaid by God, and on those who were put to shame by evil men,

**8** who love God and did not love gold or silver or any of the good things in the world, who gave their bodies over to torment,

**9** and who from the time they came into being did not long for the food of the earth, but counted themselves as a breath that passes away, and held to this. The Lord tested them much, and their spirits were found pure, so that they might bless His name.

**10** All their blessings I have told in the books, and He has assigned them their reward, for they were found to love heaven more than their breath in this world. While they were trampled by evil men, and heard insult and reviling from them, and were put to shame, they blessed Me.

**11** And now I will call the spirits of the good from the generation of light, and I will change those who were born in darkness, who in their flesh were not repaid with the honor their faithfulness deserved.

**12** I will bring out in shining light those who love My holy name, and I will seat each one on the throne of his honor.

**13** They will shine for times without number, for the judgment of God is righteousness; for to the faithful He will give faithfulness in the dwelling of upright paths.

**14** They will see those who were born in darkness thrown into darkness, while the righteous shine.

**15** The sinners will cry out and see them shining, and they too will go where days and times are written down for them."

## Notes

- **v1 "Another book, which Enoch wrote"** — a last title, like 92:1, *What was written by Enoch*. It closes the book. The Greek has nothing of this chapter.
- **v2 "You who have done good"** — the Ge'ez has *ʾəlla gabarkəmu*, **you who have done**, without an object; *good* is supplied from the sense, as Charles does. The sentence breaks off at the end of v2 and starts again in v3, *as for you*.
- **v3 "their name will be wiped out of the books of the holy ones"** — the psalm: *Let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and not be written with the righteous* (Psalm 69:28). The risen Christ to Sardis: *I will not blot out his name from the book of life* (Revelation 3:5). Charles adds *the book of life*; the EOTC has only *the books of the holy ones*. **"for there is no earth there"** — the place of punishment lies beyond the earth's edge, as the prison of the stars did in 18:12–16 and 21:1–6.
- **v6 "the spirits of the sinners and the blasphemers"** — *ṣəruʿān*; Charles, *blasphemers*. *ʾƎgziʾabəḥer*, **the Lord**.
- **vv7–9 "those who afflicted their bodies… gave their bodies over to torment"** — Hebrews on the faithful: *They were stoned, they were sawn in two, they were killed with the sword… destitute, afflicted, mistreated, of whom the world was not worthy* (Hebrews 11:37–38). **"counted themselves as a breath that passes away"** — the psalm: *Man is like a breath; his days are like a passing shadow* (Psalm 144:4); James: *You are a vapor that appears for a little while and then vanishes* (James 4:14).
- **vv10–12 "they blessed Me… I will call… My holy name"** — within the angel's words, from the end of v10, the speaker is God Himself, and He speaks in the first person to the end of v12. The EOTC gives no word to mark the change.
- **v12 "I will seat each one on the throne of his honor"** — the risen Christ: *To him who overcomes I will grant to sit with Me on My throne, as I also overcame and sat with My Father on His throne* (Revelation 3:21). Jesus to the twelve: *when the Son of Man sits on the throne of His glory, you also will sit on twelve thrones* (Matthew 19:28).
- **vv13–15 "They will shine… while the righteous shine"** — the last words of the book are the promise of 104:2, *you will shine like the lights of heaven*, now seen by the sinners themselves.

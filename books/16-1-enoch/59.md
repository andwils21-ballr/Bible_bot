# 1 Enoch 59

**1** In those days my eyes saw the secrets of the lightnings and of the lights, and their judgments. They flash for blessing and for curse, as the Lord of Spirits wills.

**2** There I saw the secrets of the thunder: when it crashes in the heights of heaven, its sound is heard. He showed me the dwellings of the dry land, and the sound of the thunder is for peace and for blessing, or for a curse, by the word of the Lord of Spirits.

**3** After this all the secrets of the lights and of the lightnings were shown to me: they flash for blessing and for plenty.

---

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "for blessing and for curse… by the word of the Lord of Spirits"** — the same storm serves either end. Elihu says it of the clouds and lightning: *whether for a rod, or for His land, or for loyal love, He causes it to come* (Job 37:13).
- **v2 "He showed me the dwellings of the dry land"** — both Ge'ez witnesses have it; Charles changes it to *he caused me to see the judgements executed on the earth*.

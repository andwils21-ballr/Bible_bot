# 1 Enoch 89

**1** One of those four went to that white bull and taught him a secret while he trembled. He was born a bull and became a man, and he built himself a great vessel and lived on it, and three bulls lived with him in that vessel, and it was covered over them.

**2** Again I raised my eyes toward heaven and saw a high roof, and seven torrents on it, and those torrents poured much water into an enclosure.

**3** I looked again, and here — fountains were opened on the ground in that great enclosure, and that water began to boil up and rise over the ground, and it did not let the enclosure be seen until all its ground was covered with water.

**4** Water and darkness and mist increased on it. I watched the height of that water, and that water rose above the height of that enclosure and poured over that enclosure and stood on the earth.

**5** All the bulls of that enclosure were gathered together, until I saw them sink and be swallowed up and perish in that water.

**6** That vessel floated on the water, but all the bulls and elephants and camels and donkeys sank to the bottom with all the animals, and I could not see them. They could not get out; they perished and sank into the deep.

**7** Again I saw in the vision, until those torrents were taken away from that high roof, and the chasms of the earth were leveled, and other depths were opened.

**8** The water began to go down into them, until the earth appeared. That vessel came to rest on the earth, and the darkness withdrew, and there was light.

**9** That white bull that had become a man came out of that vessel, and the three bulls with him. One of those three bulls was white like that bull, one of them red as blood, and one black. Then that white bull passed away from them.

**10** They began to father wild beasts and birds, and from them came every kind of nation: lions, leopards, wolves, dogs, hyenas, wild boars, foxes, conies, swine, falcons, vultures, kites, *hobay* and *foqanas*, and ravens. Among them a white bull was born.

**11** They began to bite one another. That white bull born among them fathered a wild donkey and a white bull with it, and the wild donkeys multiplied.

**12** That bull born from him fathered a black wild boar and a white sheep. That wild boar fathered many boars, and that sheep fathered twelve sheep.

**13** When those twelve sheep had grown, they handed one of them over to the wild donkeys, and those wild donkeys in turn handed that sheep over to the wolves, and that sheep grew up among the wolves.

**14** The Lord brought the eleven sheep to live with it and to pasture with it among the wolves, and they multiplied and became many flocks of sheep.

**15** The wolves began to fear them and oppressed them until their little ones perished, and they threw their little ones into a river of much water. Those sheep began to cry out for their little ones and to complain to their Lord.

**16** A sheep that had been saved from the wolves fled and went over to the wild donkeys. I saw the sheep wailing and crying out and pleading with their Lord with all their strength, until that Lord of the sheep came down at the voice of the sheep from a high place, and came to them and saw them.

**17** He called that sheep that had escaped from the wolves and spoke with it about the wolves, that it should warn them not to touch the sheep.

**18** That sheep went to the wolves at the word of the Lord, and another sheep met that sheep and went with it. The two of them went together into the assembly of those wolves, spoke to them, and warned them not to touch the sheep from then on.

**19** After that I saw the wolves, and how they pressed the sheep all the harder with all their strength, and the sheep cried out.

**20** Their Lord came to the sheep and began to strike those wolves. The wolves began to wail, but the sheep became quiet, and from then on they did not cry out.

**21** I watched the sheep until they went out from among the wolves. The eyes of the wolves were blinded, and those wolves went out after the sheep with all their strength.

**22** The Lord of the sheep went with them, leading them, and all His sheep followed Him. His face was glorious and honored, and His appearance terrible and honored.

**23** The wolves began to pursue those sheep until they caught up with them at a sea of water.

**24** That sea of water was split, and the water stood on this side and that before their face. Their Lord led them and stood between them and the wolves.

**25** Those wolves had not yet seen the sheep, and the sheep went into the middle of that sea, and the wolves followed the sheep; those wolves ran after them into that sea.

**26** When they saw the Lord of the sheep, they turned to flee from His face, but that sea came together and quickly became as it was made. The water filled and rose until it covered those wolves.

**27** I watched until all the wolves who pursued those sheep perished and drowned.

**28** The sheep passed out of that water and went into a wilderness where there was no water and no grass, and they began to open their eyes and see. I saw the Lord of the sheep pasturing them and giving them water and grass, and that sheep going and leading them.

**29** It went up to the top of that high rock, and the Lord of the sheep sent it to them.

**30** After that I saw the Lord of the sheep standing before them, and His appearance was great and terrible and mighty, and all those sheep saw Him and were afraid of His face.

**31** All of them were afraid and trembled before Him, and they cried out after that sheep who was with Him, to the other sheep who was among them, "We cannot stand before our Lord or look at Him."

**32** That sheep who led them turned back and went up to the top of that rock, and the sheep began to be blinded and to stray from the path he had shown them, and that sheep did not know it.

**33** The Lord of the sheep was very angry with them. That sheep learned of it and came down from the top of the rock and came to the sheep, and found most of them blinded and strayed from his path.

**34** When they saw him, they were afraid and trembled before his face, and wanted to go back to their own fold.

**35** That sheep took other sheep with him and went to those sheep that had strayed, and then began to kill them. The sheep were afraid of his face, and that sheep brought back those sheep that had strayed, and they went back to their folds.

**36** There I saw in the vision until that sheep became a man and built a house for the Lord of the sheep, and he made all the sheep stand in that house.

**37** I watched until the sheep that had met the sheep who led them fell asleep. I watched until all the great sheep perished, and little ones rose up in their place and came into a pasture and drew near to a river of water.

**38** That sheep who led them, who had become a man, was separated from them and fell asleep, and all the sheep looked for him and cried over him with a great cry.

**39** I watched until they stopped crying for that sheep, and they crossed that river of water. All the sheep who led them stood up in the place of those who had fallen asleep, and led them.

**40** I watched the sheep until they came into a good place, a pleasant and glorious land, and I watched those sheep until they were full, and that house was among them in the pleasant land.

**41** Sometimes their eyes were opened and sometimes they were blinded, until another sheep rose up and led them and brought them all back, and their eyes were opened.

**42** Dogs and foxes and wild boars began to devour those sheep, until another sheep rose up, a ram from among them, who led them.

**43** That ram began to butt those dogs and foxes and wild boars on this side and on that, until he had destroyed them all.

**44** That sheep whose eyes were opened saw that ram among the sheep, who left his glory and began to butt those sheep, and trampled them, and went on without dignity.

**45** The Lord of the sheep sent the sheep to another sheep and raised it up to be a ram and lead the sheep in place of that ram who had left his glory.

**46** He went to him and spoke to him alone, and raised up that ram and made him judge and leader of the sheep. Through all this those dogs oppressed the sheep.

**47** The first ram pursued that second ram, and that second ram rose up and fled from his face. I watched until those dogs brought down the first ram.

**48** That second ram rose up and led the little sheep. That second ram fathered many sheep and fell asleep, and a little sheep became ram in his place and became judge and leader of those sheep.

**49** Those sheep grew and multiplied, and all those dogs and foxes and wild boars were afraid and fled from him. That ram struck and killed all the beasts, and those beasts could no longer come among the sheep and took nothing at all from them.

**50** That house became great and wide, and it was built for those sheep. A high tower was built on that house for the Lord of the sheep. That house was low, but the tower was raised up and high, and the Lord of the sheep stood on that tower, and they set a full table before Him.

**51** Again I saw those sheep, how they strayed again and went many ways and left that house of theirs. The Lord of the sheep called sheep from among them and sent them to the sheep, and the sheep began to kill them.

**52** One of them was saved and was not killed. It leaped away and cried out against the sheep, and they wanted to kill it, but the Lord of the sheep saved it from the hands of the sheep and brought it up to me and made it stay.

**53** Many other sheep He sent to those sheep, to bear witness and to wail over them.

**54** After that I saw that when they left the house of the Lord of the sheep and His tower, they strayed in everything, and their eyes were blinded. I saw the Lord of the sheep, how He did much killing among them in their flocks, until those sheep called for that killing and gave up His place.

**55** He left them in the hands of lions and leopards and wolves and hyenas, and in the hands of foxes and of all the beasts, and those wild beasts began to tear those sheep to pieces.

**56** I saw that they had left that house of theirs and their tower, and He threw them all into the hands of the lions to tear them and devour them, into the hands of all the beasts.

**57** I began to cry out with all my strength and to call on the Lord of the sheep and show Him about the sheep, that they were being devoured and swallowed by all the wild beasts.

**58** He was silent, though He saw it, and rejoiced that they were devoured and swallowed and carried off, and He left them in the hands of all the beasts for food.

**59** He called seventy shepherds and threw those sheep to them to pasture them. He said to the shepherds and their companions, "Each one of you from now on is to pasture the sheep, and do everything I command you.

**60** I will hand them over to you by number, and I will tell you which of them are to perish, and destroy those." So He handed those sheep over to them.

**61** He called another and said to him, "Understand and see everything the shepherds do to these sheep, for they will destroy more of them than I have commanded them.

**62** Write down every excess and destruction done by the shepherds: how many they destroy at my command, and how many they destroy on their own authority. Write it down against each shepherd, one by one.

**63** Read out before Me by number how many they destroy and how many they hand over to destruction, so that this may be a witness for Me against them, so that I may know every deed of the shepherds, and weigh them, and see what they do, whether they keep to My command that I commanded them or not.

**64** They are not to know it, and you are not to show them or rebuke them, but write down every destruction by the shepherds, each one, and bring it all up to Me."

**65** I watched until those shepherds pastured, each in his time, and they began to kill and destroy many more than they were commanded, and they left those sheep in the hands of the lions.

**66** The lions and leopards ate and swallowed most of those sheep, and the wild boars ate with them. They burned that tower and dug up that house of the sheep.

**67** I grieved very much over the tower, because that house of the sheep was dug up, and after that I could not see those sheep, whether they went into that house or did not.

**68** The shepherds and their companions handed those sheep over to all the wild beasts to devour them. Each one of them in his time received them by number, and each one wrote for the next in a book how many of them he destroyed.

**69** Each one killed and destroyed beyond their order, and I began to weep and wail very much because of those sheep.

**70** So in the vision I saw the one who writes, how he writes down every one destroyed by those shepherds, every day, and goes up and lays down and shows that whole book to the Lord of the sheep: all they did, all that each one of them took away, and all they handed over to destruction.

**71** The book was read before the Lord of the sheep, and He took the book in His hand and read it and sealed it and laid it down.

**72** After that I saw the shepherds pasture for twelve hours, and here — three of those sheep turned back and came and went in, and began to build up everything that had fallen from that house; and the wild boars hindered them, but they could not.

**73** They began again to build as before, and they raised up that tower, and it was called the high tower. Again I saw them set a table before the tower, but all the bread on it was defiled and not pure.

**74** Besides all this, these sheep were blinded in their eyes and could not see, and their shepherds likewise. They handed them over to their shepherds for destruction still more, and they trampled the sheep with their feet and devoured them.

**75** The Lord of the sheep was silent until all the sheep were scattered over the field and mixed with the beasts, and they did not save them from the hands of the beasts.

**76** The one who writes the book carried it up and showed it and read it in the dwellings of the Lord of the sheep, and pleaded with Him for them and asked Him, as he showed Him all the deeds of their shepherds, and bore witness before Him against all the shepherds.

**77** He took that book and laid it down beside Him and went out.

## Notes

- **The dream as history** — chapters 89–90 tell the story from Noah to the end in animals. Men are bulls and then sheep; the nations are wild beasts and birds; angels are *white men* (87:2). Only Noah (v1) and Moses (v36) are said to *become a man*. The notes give the Genesis-to-Kings reading that the story itself follows.
- **vv1–9 the flood** — the white bull who *became a man* and built *a great vessel* is Noah (Genesis 6:14); the three bulls with him, white, red and black, are Shem, Ham and Japheth. *Seven torrents* from *a high roof* and *fountains* opened below are the two halves of Genesis 7:11.
- **v1 "while he trembled"** — the EOTC, *ʾənza yərʿəd*. Charles has *without his being terrified*.
- **v10 the nations** — after Noah the story's people are the bulls and then the sheep; everyone else is a beast or a bird. *Hobay* and *foqanas* are left as names: what animals they are is uncertain, and Charles's list has *eagles* in their place.
- **vv10–14 Abraham to Joseph** — the white bull born among the beasts is Abraham; the wild donkey and white bull, Ishmael and Isaac; the black boar and white sheep, Esau and Jacob; the twelve sheep, Jacob's sons; the one handed to the donkeys and then to the wolves, Joseph (Genesis 37:28, 36); the wolves, Egypt.
- **vv15–27 the exodus** — the little ones thrown into the river (Exodus 1:22); the sheep who escaped to the wild donkeys, Moses among the Midianites; *the other sheep* who met him, Aaron (Exodus 4:27); the sea split and closed again on the pursuers (Exodus 14).
- **vv28–35 Sinai** — the high rock is the mountain; the sheep going blind and straying while Moses is on it, the golden calf; the killing of the strayed, Exodus 32:26–28.
- **v36 "that sheep became a man and built a house for the Lord of the sheep"** — Moses and the tabernacle. Of all the sheep only Moses *becomes a man*, as Noah did at v1.
- **vv37–41 into the land** — Aaron and Moses fall asleep (die); the stream crossed is the Jordan; the sheep who lead are Joshua and the judges; *sometimes their eyes were opened and sometimes they were blinded*, the book of Judges in one sentence.
- **vv42–50 the kingdom** — Saul, the ram who *left his glory*; David, raised in his place and anointed *alone* (1 Samuel 16:13); Solomon, the *little sheep* who became ram, and the house made great with a tower on it, the temple. The Greek of vv42–49 survives; at v44 it has Saul *abandon his way and walk in a pathless way* where the EOTC has *left his glory… and went on without dignity*.
- **v52 "brought it up to me and made it stay"** — Elijah, taken up alive (2 Kings 2:11). In the dream he is brought to Enoch, who was taken the same way.
- **vv59–64 the seventy shepherds** — from the fall of the kingdom the sheep are put in the hands of seventy shepherds who destroy more than they are told, while another keeps a written record against them. *Seventy* is the count of the exile in Jeremiah (Jeremiah 25:11) and of the weeks in Daniel 9:24.
- **vv66–73 the fall of the temple and the return** — the tower burned and the house dug up (2 Kings 25:9); the three sheep who return and build, the leaders of the return from exile (Ezra 3:2); the bread on the table *defiled and not pure* (Malachi 1:7).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. Greek (vv42–49): Online Critical Pseudepigrapha, CC BY 4.0. English translation by this project.

# 1 Enoch 41

**1** After this I saw all the secrets of the heavens, and how the kingdom is divided, and how the deeds of men are weighed in the balance.

**2** There I saw the dwellings of the holy and the dwelling of the chosen. My eyes saw there all the sinners who deny the name of the Lord of Spirits being driven out from there and dragged away. They could not stand, because of the punishment that goes out from the Lord of Spirits.

**3** There my eyes saw the secrets of the lightning and the thunder, and the secrets of the winds, how they are divided to blow on the earth, and the secrets of the clouds and the dew. There I saw the place they come out from, and from there they satisfy the dust of the earth.

**4** There I saw closed storehouses, from which the winds are divided, and the storehouse of the hail and the storehouse of the mist and of the clouds, and its cloud hovers over the earth from before the world.

**5** I saw the storehouses of the sun and the moon, where they go out and where they return, and their glorious return, and how one is more honored than the other, and their magnificent course. They do not leave their course; they neither add to it nor take from it, and they keep faith with each other by the oath they have sworn.

**6** The sun goes out first and travels its path at the command of the Lord of Spirits, and His name stands firm forever and ever.

**7** After this I saw the hidden and the visible path of the moon. It completes its course in that place by day and by night, the one facing the other before the Lord of Spirits. They give thanks and praise and do not rest, for their thanksgiving is their rest.

**8** For the bright sun makes many turnings, for blessing and for curse, and the course of the moon's path is light for the righteous and darkness for sinners, in the name of the Lord of Spirits, who made a divide between the light and between the darkness, and divided the spirits of men, and strengthened the spirits of the righteous in the name of His righteousness.

**9** For no angel hinders, and no authority can hinder, because the Judge sees them all, and He judges them all before Him.

## Notes

- **v1 "weighed in the balance"** — the verdict written on Belshazzar's wall, *you have been weighed in the balances* (Daniel 5:27), here seen as the ordinary working of heaven: every human deed is weighed.

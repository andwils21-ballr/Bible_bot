# 1 Enoch 107

**1** "I saw written on them that generation after generation will do evil, until a generation of righteousness arises, and transgression is destroyed, and sin is wiped away from the earth, and every good thing comes on it.

**2** And now, my son, go and make known to your son Lamech that this son who was born is truly his son, and that this is no lie."

**3** When Methuselah heard the words of his father Enoch, for he had shown him everything in secret, he returned, having seen it, and named that son Noah, for he will gladden the earth after all the destruction.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "generation after generation will do evil, until a generation of righteousness arises"** — *təʾebbəs*; the Greek has *generation will be worse than generation*. The last word Enoch reads on the tablets looks past the flood (106:19) to the end, when *sin is wiped away from the earth*.
- **v3 "named that son Noah, for he will gladden the earth"** — *yāstafeśśəḥā la-mədr*, so the Greek, *gladdening the earth from destruction*; Charles gives *comfort*. In Genesis Lamech names him: *This one will comfort us from our work and from the toil of our hands, from the ground that the LORD has cursed* (Genesis 5:29). Here Methuselah gives the name, as Enoch told him to (106:18); in the Greek the name *was called*.

# 1 Enoch 84

**1** I lifted up my hands in righteousness and blessed the Holy and Great One, and I spoke with the breath of my mouth and with the tongue of flesh that God made for the sons of men to speak with; He gave them breath and a tongue and a mouth to speak with.

**2** "Blessed are You, O Lord, King, great and mighty in Your greatness, Lord of all the creation of heaven, King of kings and God of the whole world. Your godhead and Your kingdom and Your greatness remain forever and ever, and Your authority for all generations. All the heavens are Your dwelling forever, and the whole earth the footstool of Your feet forever and ever.

**3** For You made everything and You rule everything, and no work is too hard for You. Not one wisdom escapes You, and it does not turn away from the seat of Your dwelling or from Your face. You know and see and hear everything, and nothing is hidden from You, for You see everything.

**4** And now the angels of Your heavens are sinning, and Your wrath rests on the flesh of men until the great day of judgment.

**5** And now, God and Lord and great King, I plead and ask that You grant me my request: that You leave me a posterity on the earth, and do not destroy all human flesh, and do not strip the earth bare, so that there is destruction forever.

**6** And now, my Lord, wipe from the earth the flesh that has angered You, but establish the flesh of righteousness and uprightness as a plant of seed forever. Do not hide Your face from the prayer of Your servant, O Lord."

---

## Notes

- **v2 "All the heavens are Your dwelling… the whole earth the footstool of Your feet"** — Isaiah: *Heaven is My throne, and the earth is the footstool of My feet* (Isaiah 66:1).
- **v3 "no work is too hard for You"** — Jeremiah: *nothing is too hard for You* (Jeremiah 32:17).
- **v6 "a plant of seed forever"** — the *plant of righteousness and truth* that is to appear after the flood (10:16).

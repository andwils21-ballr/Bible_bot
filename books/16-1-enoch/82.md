# 1 Enoch 82

**1** And now, my son Methuselah, I am telling you all these things and writing them for you. I have revealed everything to you and given you the books of all these things. Keep, my son Methuselah, the books from your father's hand, and give them to the generations of the world.

**2** I have given wisdom to you and to your children, and to the children you will have, so that they may give it to their children for generations forever, this wisdom that is beyond their thought.

**3** Those who understand it will not sleep, but will listen with their ears to learn this wisdom, and it will please those who eat it more than good food.

**4** Blessed are all the righteous, and blessed are all who walk in the way of righteousness and do not sin as the sinners do, in the count of all their days in which the sun goes through heaven, entering and going out by the gates for thirty days, with the heads of thousands of the order of the stars, together with the four that are added, which divide the four parts of the year, which lead them, and with which the four days enter.

**5** Because of them people go wrong and do not count them in the reckoning of the whole world, for they go wrong about them, and people do not know them exactly.

**6** For they belong to the reckoning of the year and are truly recorded forever: one in the first gate, one in the third gate, one in the fourth gate and one in the sixth gate, and the year is completed in three hundred and sixty-four days.

**7** Its account is true and its recorded reckoning exact, for Uriel the angel showed me and breathed on me the lights and the months, the feasts, the rainy seasons and the days, Uriel whom the Lord of all creation commanded for me with the host of heaven.

**8** He has authority over day and night in heaven, to make light shine on people: the sun and the moon and the stars, and all the authorities of heaven that turn in their circles.

**9** This is the order of the stars that set in their places, in their seasons, in their feasts and in their months.

**10** These are the names of those who lead them, who watch that they enter at their times, in their orders, in their seasons, in their months, in their authorities and in their positions.

**11** Their four leaders, who divide the four parts of the year, enter first. After them come the twelve leaders of the orders, who divide the months and the year of three hundred and sixty-four days, with the heads of thousands who separate the days; and for the four that are added to them there are the leaders who divide the four parts of the years.

**12** These heads of thousands are between leader and led, one added behind each station, and their leaders divide them.

**13** These are the names of the leaders who divide the four appointed parts of the year: Melkeel, Helmelmelek, Mileel and Narel.

**14** The names of those who lead them are Adnarel, Iyasusael and Iyelumiel. These three follow behind the leaders of the orders, and there is one who follows behind the three leaders of the orders, who follow behind those leaders of the stations that divide the four parts of the year.

**15** At the beginning of the year Melkeel rises first and rules, who is called Tamaane and Sun, and all the days of his authority, when he rules, are ninety-one days.

**16** These are the signs of the days that are to be seen on the earth in the days of his authority: sweat and heat and sorrow. All the trees bear fruit, and leaves come out on all the trees, and the harvest of wheat, and the rose, and all the flowers bloom in the field, and the trees of the rainy season wither.

**17** These are the names of the leaders under them: Berkeel, Zelebsael, and another who is added, a head of a thousand, named Heluyasef. The days of this one's authority are ended.

**18** The next leader after them is Helmelmelek, whom they call the Shining Sun, and all the days of his authority are ninety-one days.

**19** These are the signs of the days on the earth: burning heat and dryness. The trees bring out their fruit ripe and cooked, and give their fruit dried; the sheep pair and conceive; and they gather in all the fruit of the earth, and everything in the fields, and the winepress. This takes place in the days of his authority.

**20** These are the names and the orders and the leaders under those heads of thousands: Gidael, Kiel and Heel, and the name of the head of a thousand who is added with them is Asfael. The days of his authority are ended.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "Keep, my son Methuselah, the books from your father's hand"** — the Astronomical Book ends as it began, handed down in writing: Enoch writes, Methuselah keeps, and the generations receive it. *It will please those who eat it more than good food*: the psalm says of God's judgments, *sweeter than honey and the drippings of the honeycomb* (Psalm 19:10).
- **vv4–6 "the four that are added… people go wrong and do not count them"** — the four thirty-first days of 72:13, 19, 25 and 31, in the first, third, fourth and sixth gates, as at 75:1–2. The book says it a second time: the 364-day year is only right if they are counted.
- **v7 "breathed on me"** — *wa-nafḫa diběya*, the EOTC; Uriel gives Enoch the knowledge by breathing on him.
- **v11 "the year of three hundred and sixty-four days"** — the EOTC. Charles has *three hundred and sixty*, the days apart from the four added ones.
- **vv13–20 the leaders of the seasons** — four leaders divide the year into four parts of ninety-one days (v15, v18). The chapter describes the first two, spring and summer, with their signs on the earth, and ends there; the third and fourth leaders named in v13, Mileel and Narel, are not described.
- **v16 "sweat and heat and sorrow"** — the sense of *ḥazan*, **sorrow**, here is uncertain; Charles has *calms*.

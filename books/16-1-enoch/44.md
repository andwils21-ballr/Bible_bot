# 1 Enoch 44

**1** Another thing I saw concerning the lightnings: how they rise from the stars and become lightning, and cannot leave them.

---

## Notes

- **The whole chapter** — one verse in both Ge'ez witnesses, finishing the thought of 43:2, *lightning begets lightning*.
- **"they rise from the stars and become lightning"** — the flash is born from the star, the way the book has the stars born as names of the righteous (43:4).
- **"and cannot leave them"** — the lightning stays bound to its star, as the sun and moon *keep faith with each other* (41:5) and the stars *keep faith among themselves* (43:2). Nothing in heaven breaks its bond.

# 1 Enoch 88

**1** I saw one of those four who had come out first. He seized that first star that had fallen from heaven, bound it hand and foot, and threw it into an abyss; and that abyss was narrow and deep, dreadful and dark.

**2** One of them drew his sword and gave it to those elephants and camels and donkeys, and they began to gore one another, and the whole earth shook because of them.

**3** As I watched in the vision, here — one of those four who had come out threw stones from heaven, and gathered and took all the great stars whose members were like the members of horses, and bound them all hand and foot, and threw them into the chasms of the earth.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "bound it hand and foot, and threw it into an abyss"** — the sentence of 10:4 carried out in the dream: *Bind Azazel hand and foot, and cast him into the darkness*.
- **v2 "gave it to those elephants and camels and donkeys, and they began to gore one another"** — 10:9: *Send them against one another in a war of destruction*.
- **v3 "bound them all hand and foot"** — 10:12, where Michael is to *bind them for seventy generations under the hills of the earth*.

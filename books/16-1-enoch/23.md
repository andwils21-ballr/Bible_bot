# 1 Enoch 23

**1** And from there I went to another place, toward the west, to the ends of the earth.

**2** And I saw a burning fire that ran without resting, and did not stop from its course day or night, but kept going, always the same.

**3** And I asked, "What is this that has no rest?"

**4** Then Raguel answered me, one of the holy angels who was with me, and said, "This course of fire that you saw, running toward the west, is the fire that pursues all the luminaries of heaven."

---

## Notes

- **v4 "the fire that pursues all the luminaries"** — that is the **Greek**. The **Ge'ez** says instead that the fire **is** all the luminaries of heaven. One witness has a fire chasing the lights across the sky; the other has the fire and the lights being the same thing. It is a four-word clause and the two readings describe different machinery.
- **v2 "ran without resting... day or night"** — the chapter's whole subject is a thing that never stops. Set against chapter 18, where seven stars were imprisoned for being late, the book's admiration here is for something that keeps perfect time.
- **v1 "toward the west, to the ends of the earth"** — west is where this book puts the end of things: the fire of the west (17:4), the river of fire running west (17:5), the mountain of the dead in the west (22:1), and now this. The sun sets there, and the geography follows the sun.
- **On the shortest chapter of the journey** — four verses, one question, one answer. The pattern of the whole tour in miniature: he arrives, he sees, he asks, an angel with a name explains. Chapters 17 through 36 are built out of this unit.

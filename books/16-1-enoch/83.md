# 1 Enoch 83

**1** And now, my son Methuselah, I will show you everything, and I will tell you all the visions I have seen.

**2** I saw two visions before I took a wife, and neither of them was like the other. The first was when I was learning to write, and the second, before I took your mother, when I saw a terrible vision. Because of them I prayed to the Lord.

**3** I was lying down in the house of my grandfather Mahalalel when I saw in a vision the heaven being swept away and carried off, and falling on the earth.

**4** When it fell on the earth, I saw the earth swallowed up in a great abyss. Mountains were hung on mountains, hills sank on hills, and tall trees were torn from their stumps, thrown down, and sank into the abyss.

**5** Then a word fell into my mouth, and I began to cry out, and said, "The earth is destroyed!"

**6** My grandfather Mahalalel woke me as I lay there, and said to me, "Why are you crying out like this, my son, and why are you wailing like this?"

**7** I told him the whole vision I had seen, and he said to me, "You have seen a terrible thing, my son. Your dream vision is grave, about the secrets of all the sin of the earth: it must sink into the abyss and be destroyed with a great destruction.

**8** And now, my son, rise and plead with the Lord of glory, for you are faithful, that a remnant may be left on the earth and that He may not wipe out the whole earth.

**9** My son, all this will come on the earth from heaven, and on the earth there will be a great destruction."

**10** Then I rose and prayed and pleaded, and I wrote down my prayer for the generations of the world, and I will show you everything, my son Methuselah.

**11** When I had gone out below and seen the heaven, and the sun rising in the east and the moon setting in the west, and a few stars, and the whole earth, and everything as He had known it from the beginning, I blessed the Lord of judgment and gave Him greatness, because He brought the sun out of the windows of the east, and it went up and rose over the face of heaven, and set out, and goes on the path that was shown to it.

## Notes

- **v2 "when I was learning to write"** — the man who becomes *the scribe* (12:3) has his first vision as a boy at his lessons.
- **v3 "my grandfather Mahalalel"** — Enoch's father was Jared, son of Mahalalel (Genesis 5:15–18). *Mahalalel* is the project's spelling in Genesis.
- **vv7–8 "it must sink into the abyss… that a remnant may be left"** — the flood, seen before it comes. Enoch's plea is the first intercession for a remnant in the book, and it is answered in Noah (65:12, 67:2).
- **v11 "everything as He had known it from the beginning"** — after the dream of the world undone, Enoch goes outside and finds the sun rising on its path, as chapters 72–82 described it.

# 1 Enoch 63

**1** In those days the kings and the mighty who hold the dry land will plead with the angels of His punishment, to whom they have been handed over, to give them a little rest, so that they may fall down and worship before the Lord of Spirits and confess their sins before Him.

**2** They will bless and praise the Lord of Spirits and say, "Blessed is the Lord, the Lord of Spirits, the Lord of kings, the Lord of the mighty, the Lord of the rich, the Lord of glory and the Lord of wisdom. By Him every hidden thing comes to light.

**3** Your power is from generation to generation, and Your glory forever and ever. All Your secrets are deep and without number, and Your righteousness is beyond reckoning.

**4** Now we know that we should praise and bless the Lord of kings and Him who reigns over all kings."

**5** They will say, "If only someone would give us rest, so that we might praise Him and give thanks to Him and bless Him and confess His glory!

**6** Now we long for a little rest and do not find it. We are driven off and cannot lay hold of it. The light has passed from before us, and darkness is our dwelling forever and ever.

**7** For we did not believe before Him, and we did not praise the name of the Lord of kings, nor did we praise the Lord in all His works. Our hope was in the scepter of the throne of our kingdom and in our glory.

**8** In the day of our suffering and distress He does not save us, and we find no rest to confess that our Lord is faithful in all His deeds and in all His judgment and His righteousness, and His judgments show no partiality.

**9** We pass from His presence because of our deeds, and all our sins have been counted in righteousness."

**10** Now they will say to them, "Our souls are full of the wealth of wrongdoing, but it does not keep us from going down into the flame of the heaviness of Sheol."

**11** After this their faces will be filled with darkness and shame before that Son of Man, and they will be driven from his presence, and the sword will stay before his face among them.

**12** This is what the Lord of Spirits said: "This is the law and the judgment for the mighty and the kings and the exalted and those who hold the dry land, before the Lord of Spirits."

## Notes

- **v2 "the Lord of kings… By Him every hidden thing comes to light"** — Nebuchadnezzar says it to Daniel: *your God is God of gods and Lord of kings, and a revealer of secrets* (Daniel 2:47). The kings of the Parables come to the same confession, too late.
- **v8 "His judgments show no partiality"** — *wa-gaṣṣa kʷənnanēhu ʾiyənaśśəʾu*, literally **His judgments do not lift up a face**: they do not favor anyone for who he is. Moses says it of the LORD, *who does not lift up faces and takes no bribe* (Deuteronomy 10:17).
- **v10 "the wealth of wrongdoing… the flame"** — *nəwāya ʿamaḍā*. Jesus uses the phrase, *make friends for yourselves by means of the wealth of unrighteousness* (Luke 16:9), in the chapter where the rich man cries out from Hades, *I am in agony in this flame* (Luke 16:24).
- **v10 "into the flame of the heaviness of Sheol"** — *ʾəm-lāhbā la-kəbada Siʾol*, as the Ge'ez has it. The OCP's English of the Ge'ez gives *the flame of the torment of Sheol*; Charles, *the burden of Sheol*.

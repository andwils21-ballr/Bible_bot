# 1 Enoch 91

**1** "And now, my son Methuselah, call all your brothers to me, and gather all the sons of your mother to me, for a word calls me, and the spirit has been poured out on me, so that I may show you everything that will come on you forever."

**2** Then Methuselah went and called all his brothers to him and gathered his relatives.

**3** He spoke righteousness to all his children and said, "Hear, my children, every word of your father, and listen rightly to the voice of my mouth, for I bear witness to you and tell you, my beloved: love uprightness and walk in it.

**4** Do not come near uprightness with a double heart, and do not join with those of a double heart, but walk in righteousness, my children. It will lead you on good paths, and righteousness will be your companion.

**5** For I know that the state of violence will grow strong on the earth, and a great punishment will be carried out on the earth, and all wrongdoing will be ended; it will be cut off from its roots, and its whole building will pass away.

**6** Wrongdoing will come back again and be completed on the earth, and every deed of wrongdoing and every deed of violence and transgression will take hold a second time.

**7** When wrongdoing and sin and blasphemy and violence grow in every deed, and apostasy and transgression and uncleanness grow, a great punishment will come from heaven on all of these, and the holy Lord will come out in wrath and great punishment to carry out judgment on the earth.

**8** In those days violence will be cut off from its roots, and the roots of wrongdoing with deceit, and they will perish from under heaven.

**9** Everything will be handed over with the nations, and the tower will burn with fire. They will be taken out from the whole earth, and thrown into the judgment of fire, and perish in wrath and in mighty judgment forever.

**10** The righteous one will rise from sleep, and wisdom will rise and be given to them.

**11** After that the roots of wrongdoing will be cut off, and the sinners will perish by the sword; from among the blasphemers they will be cut off in every place, and those who plan violence and those who commit blasphemy will perish by the sword.

**12** After this there will be another week, the eighth, the week of righteousness, and a sword will be given to it, so that righteous judgment may be carried out on those who oppress, and the sinners will be handed over to the righteous.

**13** At its end they will acquire houses by their righteousness, and a house will be built for the Great King in glory forever.

**14** After this, in the ninth week, righteous judgment will be revealed to the whole world. All the works of the wicked will vanish from the earth, the world will be written down for destruction, and all people will look to the path of uprightness.

**15** After this, in the tenth week, in its seventh part, there will be the eternal judgment, and it will be carried out on the Watchers, and the great eternal heaven that will spring up from among the angels.

**16** The first heaven will depart and pass away, and a new heaven will appear, and all the powers of the heavens will shine forever, sevenfold.

**17** After this there will be many weeks without number, forever, in goodness and in righteousness, and sin will not be spoken of there, forever.

**18** And now I tell you, my children, and show you the paths of righteousness and the paths of violence. I will show you again, so that you may know what is coming.

**19** And now listen to me, my children, and walk in the paths of righteousness, and do not walk in the paths of violence, for all who walk in the path of wrongdoing will perish forever."

## Notes

- **vv12–17 the eighth, ninth and tenth weeks** — the end of the *Apocalypse of Weeks*, a history of the world in ten weeks. The first seven weeks come in 93:3–10. Charles moves these verses to follow 93:10; the EOTC keeps them here, and the numbering follows it.
- **v9 "Everything will be handed over with the nations, and the tower will burn with fire"** — the EOTC. Charles has *all the idols of the heathen shall be abandoned, and the temples burned with fire*.
- **v10 "The righteous one will rise from sleep"** — the same words open 92:3.
- **v15 "carried out on the Watchers, and the great eternal heaven that will spring up from among the angels"** — the EOTC as it stands; the sense of the second half is uncertain. Charles has *in which He will execute vengeance amongst the angels*.
- **v16 "The first heaven will depart and pass away, and a new heaven will appear"** — *I saw a new heaven and a new earth, for the first heaven and the first earth had passed away* (Revelation 21:1).

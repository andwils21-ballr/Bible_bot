# 1 Enoch 26

**1** And from there I went to the middle of the earth, and I saw a blessed and watered place, where there were branches still sprouting from a tree that had been cut down.

**2** And there I saw a holy mountain, and beneath the mountain, water on the east, and its flow ran toward the south.

**3** And I saw toward the east another mountain higher than this one, and between them a deep ravine with no breadth, and through it water ran beneath the mountain.

**4** And toward the west of this was another mountain, lower than it and without height, and a deep and dry ravine between them, and another deep and dry ravine at the ends of the three of them.

**5** And all of them had deep ravines of hard rock, and no tree was planted on them.

**6** And I marveled at the rock, and I marveled at the ravine — I marveled greatly.

## Notes

- **v1 "the middle of the earth"** — and what he describes from here to the end of chapter 27 is **Jerusalem**, without naming it. A holy mountain, a spring on its east side, a deep ravine on one side and another on the west, meeting at the corner of three. That is the city's actual geography: Mount Zion, the Gihon spring, the Kidron valley east, the Hinnom valley west and south, joining below.
- **v1 "branches still sprouting from a tree that had been cut down"** — the Ge'ez; the Greek has only *a blessed place with cuttings*. The image the Ethiopic preserves is the one **Isaiah 11:1** uses for the line of David: *a shoot will come out from the **stump** of Jesse, and a branch will grow from his roots*. A felled tree that is still putting out shoots is how the Hebrew Bible pictures a cut-down dynasty that is not finished.
- **v2 the water runs south** — the Greek. The Ge'ez says **north**. The Gihon runs south, down the Kidron; but the book has already used north for the direction of the holy place in 25:5, so both readings have a pull on them.
- **v5 "no tree was planted on them"** — the Greek. The Ge'ez says the opposite: *trees were planted upon them*. This is the third place in the book where the two witnesses differ by a negative, after 15:11 and 15:12. Here the context decides it: chapter 27 opens by contrasting a land *full of trees* with the cursed valley between, which only works if the ravines are bare.
- **v6 "I marveled greatly"** — he has been shown the throne of God, the tree of life and the storehouses of the winds without comment. What stops him is a rock formation.

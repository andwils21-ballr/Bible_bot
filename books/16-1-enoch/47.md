# 1 Enoch 47

**1** In those days the prayer of the righteous and the blood of the righteous went up from the earth before the Lord of Spirits, and it was required.

**2** In those days the holy ones who live above the heavens will join together, and with one voice they will petition and pray and praise and give thanks and bless the name of the Lord of Spirits, because of the blood of the righteous that has been shed, so that the prayer of the righteous may not be set aside before the Lord of Spirits, so that judgment may be done for them, and their patience may not have to last forever.

**3** In those days I saw the Head of Days when He sat on the throne of His glory, and the books of the living were opened before Him, and all His host that is above the heavens and His council around Him stood before Him.

**4** The hearts of the holy were filled with joy, because the number of righteousness had arrived, and the prayer of the righteous had been heard, and the blood of the righteous had been required before the Lord of Spirits.

## Notes

- **v1 "the blood of the righteous went up from the earth"** — the voice of Abel's blood, *crying out to Me from the ground* (Genesis 4:10). In Revelation the souls under the altar cry out, *How long, holy and true Master, until You judge and avenge our blood on those who live on the earth?* (Revelation 6:10).
- **v1 "the blood of the righteous"** — the Ge'ez here and in v4 is singular, *ṣādəq*, the same word as *the Righteous One* of 38:2. Both English renderings of the Ge'ez take it as the righteous in general, each one of them, and the plural *prayer of the righteous* beside it supports that. The singular is there for any reader who hears in it the Righteous One himself.
- **vv1, 4 "it was required"** — *tafaqda*: the blood is demanded back, an account is called for. God says it of all shed blood after the flood: *your blood… I will require* (Genesis 9:5). The EOTC has the word in both verses; Knibb's Ge'ez only in v4.
- **v3 "the books of the living were opened"** — the court of Daniel 7:10, *the court sat, and the books were opened*. *The book of the living* is Psalm 69:28, and in Revelation *the books were opened, and another book was opened, which is the book of life* (Revelation 20:12).
- **v4 "the number of righteousness had arrived"** — the EOTC, *baṣḥa ʾəlqʷā la-ṣədq*. Knibb's Ge'ez has only *it had arrived*. The same answer is given to the souls under the altar in Revelation: they must rest a little longer, *until their fellow servants and their brothers… were made complete* (Revelation 6:11).

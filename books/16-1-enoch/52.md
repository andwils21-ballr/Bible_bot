# 1 Enoch 52

**1** After those days, in the place where I had seen all the hidden visions — for I had been snatched away by a whirlwind, and they had carried me to the west —

**2** there my eyes saw all the secret things of heaven, everything that will come to pass on the earth: a mountain of iron, a mountain of copper, a mountain of silver, a mountain of gold, a mountain of soft metal, and a mountain of lead.

**3** I asked the angel who went with me, "What are these things I have seen in secret?"

**4** He said to me, "All these things you have seen will serve the authority of His Anointed, so that he may command and be mighty on the earth."

**5** That angel of peace answered me, "Wait a little, and you will see, and everything hidden that the Lord of Spirits has established will be revealed to you.

**6** These mountains your eyes have seen — the mountain of iron, the mountain of copper, the mountain of silver, the mountain of gold, the mountain of soft metal, and the mountain of lead — all of them will be before the Chosen One like wax before the fire, and like water that comes down from above on those mountains, and they will become weak under his feet.

**7** And it shall come to pass in those days that no one will be saved by gold or by silver, and no one will be able to escape or flee.

**8** There will be no iron for war, and no cloth for a breastplate. Bronze will be of no use, and tin will be of no use and will count for nothing, and lead will not be wanted.

**9** All these will be trampled, and they must perish from the face of the earth, when the Chosen One appears before the face of the Lord of Spirits."

## Notes

- **vv2, 6 "a mountain of iron, a mountain of copper…"** — in Daniel's dream a statue of gold, silver, bronze, iron and clay is struck by a stone that *became a great mountain*, and the metals are broken to pieces together (Daniel 2:35).
- **v4 "His Anointed"** — the second and last time the title is used in the book (see 48:10). The mountains serve *the authority of His Anointed* (v4) and melt *before the Chosen One* (v6): the two titles name one figure.
- **v6 "like wax before the fire, and like water that comes down"** — Micah 1:4 has the same two pictures: *the mountains will melt under him… like wax before the fire, like water poured down a slope*. Psalm 97:5: *the mountains melted like wax before the LORD*.
- **v7 "no one will be saved by gold or by silver"** — Zephaniah 1:18: *neither their silver nor their gold will be able to deliver them on the day of the LORD's wrath*. Ezekiel 7:19 has the same words.
- **v8 "no iron for war… no cloth for a breastplate. Bronze will be of no use"** — the craft Azazel taught at the start: *swords of iron… and breastplates of bronze* (8:1). What the Watchers put in men's hands is worth nothing on the day the Chosen One appears.
- **v9 "All these will be trampled"** — the EOTC, *yətkayyadu*. Knibb's Ge'ez has *yətkaḥadu*, **they will be denied**, and both English renderings follow it. The two words differ by one letter. The EOTC's verb is the one the OCP English renders *trample* at 56:6, and it follows v6, where the mountains grow weak *under his feet*.

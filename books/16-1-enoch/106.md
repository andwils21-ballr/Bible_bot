# 1 Enoch 106

**1** After some days my son Methuselah took a wife for his son Lamech, and she conceived by him and bore a son.

**2** His body was white as snow and red as a rose in bloom, and the hair of his head and his locks were white like white wool, and his eyes were beautiful. When he opened his eyes, he lit up the whole house like the sun, and the whole house was very bright.

**3** When he rose from the hands of the midwife, he opened his mouth and spoke the praise of the Lord of righteousness.

**4** His father Lamech was afraid of him, and fled, and came to his father Methuselah.

**5** He said to him, "I have fathered a strange son. He is not like a man; he looks like the children of the angels of heaven. His nature is different, and he is not like us. His eyes are like the rays of the sun, and his face is glorious.

**6** It seems to me that he is not from me but from the angels, and I am afraid that some wonder will be done on the earth in his days.

**7** So I am here, my father, to plead with you and ask you to go to Enoch our father and hear the truth from him, for his dwelling is with the angels."

**8** When Methuselah heard his son's words, he came to me at the ends of the earth, for he had heard that I was there. He cried out, and I heard his voice and came to him, and said to him, "Here I am, my son. Why have you come to me?"

**9** He answered me and said, "I have come to you because of a great matter, and I have drawn near because of a terrible vision.

**10** And now, my father, hear me: to Lamech my son a son has been born whose likeness and nature are not like the nature of men. His color is whiter than snow and redder than a rose in bloom, and the hair of his head is whiter than white wool, and his eyes are like the rays of the sun. When he opened his eyes, he lit up the whole house.

**11** He rose from the hands of the midwife, and opened his mouth, and blessed the Lord of heaven.

**12** His father Lamech, my son, was afraid, and fled to me, and does not believe that he is from him, but that his likeness is from the angels of heaven. Here I have come to you, so that you may make known to me the truth."

**13** I, Enoch, answered him and said to him, "The Lord will do new things on the earth. This I have already seen in a vision, and I made it known to you: in the generation of my father Jared, some from the height of heaven transgressed the word of the Lord.

**14** See, they commit sin and transgress the law. They have joined themselves with women and commit sin with them, and have married some of them, and have fathered children by them.

**15** There will be a great destruction over the whole earth. There will be a flood, and a great destruction for one year.

**16** This son who has been born to you will be left on the earth, and his three sons will be saved with him. When all the people on the earth die, he and his sons will be saved.

**17** They will father giants on the earth, not of the spirit but of the flesh, and there will be a great punishment on the earth, and the earth will be washed clean of all corruption.

**18** And now make known to your son Lamech that the one who was born is truly his son, and call his name Noah, for he will be left to you as a remnant. He and his sons will be saved from the destruction that will come on the earth because of all the sin and all the wrongdoing that will be completed on the earth in his days.

**19** After this there will be more wrongdoing than what was first completed on the earth. For I know the mysteries of the holy ones, for He, the Lord, showed them to me and made them known to me, and I read them on the tablets of heaven and understood."

## Notes

- **v1 "my son Methuselah took a wife for his son Lamech"** — Genesis names the father and son: *Lamech lived one hundred and eighty-two years and fathered a son* (Genesis 5:28). The Greek begins further back, with Lamech's own birth and marriage; the Latin says Lamech was *three hundred and fifty years old*.
- **v2 "white as snow… the hair of his head… white like white wool… he lit up the whole house like the sun"** — the child is described in the words used of the Head of Days (46:1, 71:10) and of the risen Christ: *His head and His hair were white like white wool, like snow, and His eyes like a flame of fire* (Revelation 1:14). At the transfiguration *His face shone like the sun* (Matthew 17:2). The Latin says *no human being is able to look at his body*.
- **v3 "spoke the praise of the Lord of righteousness"** — the Greek has *blessed the Lord*, the Latin *worshipped the Lord living forever, and praised him*.
- **v5 "like the children of the angels of heaven"** — so the Greek; Charles gives *the sons of the God of heaven*. Lamech fears his wife has borne a child to one of the Watchers, as the women of chapters 6–7 did.
- **v8 "he came to me at the ends of the earth"** — as Noah himself will later go *to the ends of the earth* and cry out to Enoch (65:2). Enoch, taken from among men, is found at the edge of the world.
- **v13 "The Lord will do new things on the earth"** — *Here I am doing a new thing* (Isaiah 43:19). The Greek has *The Lord will renew a decree upon the earth*. **"in the generation of my father Jared"** — the time of the Watchers' descent (see 6:6). **"some from the height of heaven"** — the Ge'ez reads *they transgressed the word of the Lord from the height of heaven*; Charles has *some of the angels of heaven*, and the Greek *from the covenant of heaven*.
- **v14 "have married some of them, and have fathered children by them"** — Genesis: *the sons of God saw that the daughters of men were beautiful, and they took wives for themselves* (Genesis 6:2), and *the Nephilim were on the earth in those days* (Genesis 6:4).
- **v15 "a great destruction for one year"** — the flood came in Noah's six hundredth year, *in the second month, on the seventeenth day* (Genesis 7:11), and the earth was dry in the six hundred and first year, *in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day* (Genesis 8:13–14): a year and ten days. The Latin has *forty days* and *after five hundred years*.
- **v17 "They will father giants on the earth, not of the spirit but of the flesh"** — *they* are the Watchers of v14. The Greek has these words in v14, right after the marriages (numbered 17a); the EOTC places them after the flood is announced. **"the earth will be washed clean of all corruption"** — the Greek has *he will soothe the earth from the corruption that is in it*: a play on Noah's name.
- **v18 "call his name Noah"** — Lamech in Genesis: *This one will comfort us from our work and from the toil of our hands* (Genesis 5:29). The Latin: *Noah, which is interpreted "rest," because he will provide rest in the ark*. **"a remnant"** — *tarafa*, **what is left**, the word of v16, *he will be left*.
- **v19 "I read them on the tablets of heaven"** — as at 81:1–2 and 103:2.

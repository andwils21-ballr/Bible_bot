# 1 Enoch 50

**1** In those days there will be a change for the holy and the chosen. The light of days will dwell on them, and glory and honor will turn to the holy.

**2** On the day of distress, evil will be stored up for the sinners, and the righteous will conquer in the name of the Lord of Spirits. He will show this to the others, so that they may repent and give up the works of their hands.

**3** They will have honor before the Lord of Spirits, and in His name they will be saved, and the Lord of Spirits will have mercy on them, because His mercy is great.

**4** He is righteous in His judgment, and before His glory wrongdoing will not stand in His judgment. Whoever does not repent before Him will perish.

**5** "From now on I will have no mercy on them," says the Lord of Spirits.

## Notes

- **v1 "the light of days"** — *bərhāna mawāʿəl*, with the same word for *days* as the title *the Head of Days*, *Rəʾsa Mawāʿəl* (46:2, 47:3). The *change*, *miṭat*, is the one the verse goes on to name: glory and honor *will turn* to the holy.
- **v2 "the others, so that they may repent"** — a third group, neither the righteous nor the condemned sinners: those who see the righteous win and turn.
- **v2 "evil will be stored up for the sinners"** — Paul's words to the unrepentant: *you are storing up wrath for yourself on the day of wrath* (Romans 2:5).
- **v3 "They will have honor before the Lord of Spirits"** — the EOTC. Knibb's Ge'ez has the negative, *they will have **no** honor*, and Charles follows it: the others are saved, but without the honor of the righteous. The two witnesses differ by the negative prefix *ʾi-*, **not**. In the EOTC the repentant are honored as well as saved.
- **vv4–5 "Whoever does not repent before Him will perish… From now on I will have no mercy"** — repentance has a time limit. Jesus: *unless you repent, you will all likewise perish* (Luke 13:3).

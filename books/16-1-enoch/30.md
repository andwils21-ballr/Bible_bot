# 1 Enoch 30

**1** Beyond it, above these, above the mountain of the east, and not far off, I saw another place, ravines of water that never runs dry.

**2** I saw a beautiful tree, and its fragrance was like mastic.

**3** Along the sides of these ravines I saw fragrant cinnamon. Beyond them I went on toward the east.

---

## Notes

- **v2 "mastic"** — Greek *schinos*, the mastic tree, whose resin was prized for its scent; the Ge'ez word, *sakinon*, is borrowed from the same Greek. Chapters 29 to 32 name spice after spice: frankincense, myrrh, mastic, cinnamon, galbanum, nard, pepper. The route to the garden of righteousness (32:3) is laid out as a road of incense.

# 1 Enoch 79

**1** And now, my son Methuselah, I have shown you everything, and the whole law of the stars of heaven is complete.

**2** He showed me all their laws, for every day and for every season of each authority, for every year and for its going out and its command, for every month and for every week:

**3** the waning of the moon that takes place in the sixth gate, for in this sixth gate its light is completed, and from it comes the beginning of the month and the waning;

**4** the waning that takes place in the first gate in its season, until one hundred and seventy-seven days are completed, by the reckoning of weeks, twenty-five weeks and two days.

**5** It falls behind the sun and the order of the stars by exactly five days in one period, when this place that you see is completed.

**6** Such is the picture and the likeness of every light that Uriel the great angel, who is their leader, showed me.

---

## Notes

- **v4 "one hundred and seventy-seven days… twenty-five weeks and two days"** — the half-year of 78:15, counted in weeks: 25 × 7 = 175, and two days more.
- **v6 "the picture and the likeness"** — *ʾarʾayā wa-ʾamsāl*: what Enoch has written is a copy of what Uriel showed him.

# 1 Enoch 7

**1** All the others with them took wives for themselves, each choosing one for himself, and they began to go in to them and to defile themselves with them. They taught them sorceries and spells, and the cutting of roots, and showed them plants.

**2** They became pregnant and bore great giants, whose height was three thousand cubits.

**3** These consumed all the labor of men, until men could no longer sustain them.

**4** The giants turned against them, to devour mankind.

**5** They began to sin against the birds and the beasts and the creeping things and the fish, and to devour one another's flesh, and to drink the blood from it.

**6** Then the earth brought accusation against the lawless ones.

---

## Notes

- **v5 "and to drink the blood from it"** — the one prohibition Genesis lays on all humanity after the flood is exactly this: *flesh with its life, its blood, you shall not eat* (Genesis 9:4). The giants are doing beforehand the single thing the new world will be forbidden.

# 1 Enoch 67

**1** In those days the word of the Lord came to me, and He said to me, "Noah, here — your portion has come up to Me, a portion without blame, a portion of love and uprightness.

**2** Now the angels are working on timber, and when they have gone out to that task, I will put My hand on it and keep it safe. From it will come the seed of life, and a change will come, so that the dry land will not remain empty.

**3** I will establish your seed before Me forever and ever, and I will sow those who live with you over the face of the dry land. It will multiply and be blessed before the dry land in the name of the Lord of Spirits."

**4** They will shut up those angels who showed wrongdoing in that burning valley that my forefather Enoch had shown me before, in the west, near the mountains of gold and silver and iron and soft metal and tin.

**5** I saw that valley, in which there was a great upheaval and a churning of the waters.

**6** When all this happened, from that fiery molten metal and from the upheaval that churned them in that place, a smell of sulfur arose, and it mixed with those waters. That valley of the angels who led men astray burns beneath that land.

**7** Through its valleys flow rivers of fire, where those angels are punished who led astray those who live on the dry land.

**8** In those days those waters will serve the kings and the mighty and the exalted and those who live on the dry land for the healing of soul and body, but for the judgment of the spirit. Their spirit is full of lust, so that they will be punished in their flesh, because they denied the Lord of Spirits. They see their judgment every day and do not believe in His name.

**9** As greatly as their flesh burns, so great will be the change in their spirit forever and ever, for no one before the Lord of Spirits speaks an idle word.

**10** For judgment will come on them, because they trust in the lust of their flesh and deny the Spirit of the Lord.

**11** Those same waters will undergo a change at that time. When those angels are punished in those days, the appearance of those springs of water will change, and when the angels come up, the water of the springs will change and become cold.

**12** I heard holy Michael answering and saying, "This judgment by which the angels are judged is a witness to the kings and the mighty and those who hold the dry land."

**13** For these waters of judgment serve for the healing of the bodies of the angels and for the death of their flesh. But they do not see, and do not believe, that those waters will be changed and become a fire that burns forever.

## Notes

- **v1 "a portion without blame"** — *kəfl za-ʾalbotu ḫis*, the word of 65:11, *free of this reproach*. Genesis says it of him: *Noah was a righteous man, whole in his generations* (Genesis 6:9).
- **v2 "the angels are working on timber"** — in Genesis God says to Noah, *Make yourself an ark of gopher wood* (Genesis 6:14); here the angels do the work. *The seed of life* that comes out of it is the purpose Genesis gives for the ark: *to keep seed alive upon the face of all the earth* (Genesis 7:3).
- **v3 "I will sow those who live with you"** — *ʾəzarrəʾomu*, the verb of 62:8, *the congregation of the holy and the chosen will be sown*. Genesis tells the same end: *from these the whole earth was scattered* (Genesis 9:19). Knibb's Ge'ez has *on the dry land* where the EOTC has *before the dry land*, and adds *it will not be barren on the face of the earth*.
- **v4 "that burning valley… near the mountains of gold and silver"** — the mountains of metal of 52:2 and the valley of fire of 54:1, now seen by Noah.
- **v6 "that fiery molten metal"** — *naṭabṭāba ʾəsāt*. *Naṭabṭāb* is the word rendered *soft metal* in v4 and at 52:2; here, *of fire*, it is molten.
- **v9 "no one before the Lord of Spirits speaks an idle word"** — *nagara bak*, as at 49:4 and 62:3.
- **v13 "the healing of the bodies of the angels"** — *la-malāʾəkt*, **of the angels**, in both Ge'ez witnesses. Charles changes it to *of the kings*, as in v8. The waters that the kings use for healing are the waters where the angels are punished.

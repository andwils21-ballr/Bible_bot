# 1 Enoch 104

**1** I swear to you, you righteous, that in heaven the angels remember you for good before the glory of the Great One, and your names are written before the glory of the Great One.

**2** Be hopeful. Before, you were put to shame through evil and hardship, but now you will shine like the lights of heaven, and you will be seen, and the gate of heaven will be opened to you.

**3** Cry out for judgment in your cry, and it will appear to you; for all your distress will be searched out from the angels, and from all who helped those who rob you.

**4** Be hopeful, and do not give up your hope, for you will have great joy, like the angels in heaven.

**5** This is what you will have to do: you will not have to hide on the day of the great judgment, and you will not be found as sinners, and the eternal judgment will be far from you for all the generations of the world.

**6** And now do not fear, you righteous, when you see the sinners grow strong and prosper in their desires. Do not be their companions, but keep far from their violence, for you are to be companions of the host of heaven.

**7** For you sinners say, "None of our sins will be searched out or written down." They will write down all your sins every day.

**8** And now I show you that light and darkness, day and night, see all your sins.

**9** Do not be godless in your heart, and do not lie, and do not change the word of uprightness, and do not call the word of the Holy and Great One a lie, and do not praise your idols; for all your lying and all your godlessness lead not to righteousness but to great sin.

**10** And now I know this mystery: many sinners will change and twist the word of uprightness, and speak evil words, and lie, and make great fabrications, and write books about their own words.

**11** But when they write all my words truthfully in their languages, and do not change or leave out any of my words, but write everything truthfully, all that I first testified concerning them —

**12** then I know another mystery: books will be given to the righteous and the wise, for joy and uprightness and much wisdom.

**13** To them the books will be given, and they will believe in them and rejoice in them, and all the righteous who have learned from them all the paths of uprightness will be glad.

## Notes

- **v1 "your names are written before the glory of the Great One"** — Jesus to the seventy: *rejoice that your names are written in heaven* (Luke 10:20). The Greek lacks this second clause.
- **v2 "you will shine like the lights of heaven"** — Daniel: *Those who are wise will shine like the brightness of the firmament, and those who turn many to righteousness like the stars forever and ever* (Daniel 12:3). Jesus: *Then the righteous will shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father* (Matthew 13:43). **"Before, you were put to shame"** — the Greek has *you have grown old in evils and in tribulations*. **"the gate of heaven"** — *ḫoḫta samāy*; the Greek has *the windows of heaven*.
- **v3 "searched out from the angels"** — *ʾəmna malāʾəkt*, as at 100:10, and the angels of the complaint at 103:14. Charles gives *visited on the rulers*.
- **v4 "great joy, like the angels in heaven"** — Jesus: *in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels in heaven* (Matthew 22:30). The Greek has a gap at this verse.
- **v6 "do not fear… when you see the sinners grow strong and prosper"** — the psalm: *Do not fret because of evildoers; do not envy those who do wrong* (Psalm 37:1). **"companions of the host of heaven"** — the righteous who refuse to be *companions of the sinners* (97:4) will be companions of the angels.
- **v8 "light and darkness, day and night, see all your sins"** — the psalm turns it round: *even the darkness is not dark to You, and the night shines like the day* (Psalm 139:12).
- **vv10–11 "write books about their own words… write all my words truthfully in their languages"** — the Greek has *write scriptures in their own names*, and *would that they would write all my words in truth in their names*. The warning against changing the words is Moses': *You shall not add to the word that I command you, nor take away from it* (Deuteronomy 4:2). Revelation ends the same way: *if anyone takes away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God will take away his part from the tree of life* (Revelation 22:19).
- **vv12–13 "books will be given to the righteous and the wise"** — the Greek has *my books*: Enoch's own writings, handed down to the righteous.

# 1 Enoch 103

**1** And now I swear to you, the righteous, by His great glory and His honor, and by His honored kingdom, and by His greatness I swear to you.

**2** For I know this mystery. I have read the tablets of heaven and seen the writing of the holy ones, and I found written and engraved in them concerning them

**3** that all good and joy and honor is prepared for them and written down for the spirits of those who died in righteousness. Much good will be given to you in return for your labors, and your portion is greater than the portion of the living.

**4** The spirits of you who died in righteousness will live, and their spirits will rejoice and be glad, and their memory will be before the Great One for all the generations of the world. So now do not fear their insults.

**5** Woe to you, sinners, when you die in your sins, and those who are like you say of you, "Blessed are the sinners: they have seen all their days.

**6** Now they have died in prosperity and in wealth, and they did not see distress or slaughter in their life. They died in glory, and no judgment was carried out on them in their life."

**7** Do you not know that their souls will be brought down into Sheol, and that their distress will be evil and great?

**8** Into darkness and a net and a burning flame, where the great judgment is, your spirit will enter, and the great judgment will be for every generation forever. Woe to you, for you will have no peace.

**9** Do not say of the righteous and good who are alive, "In the days of our trouble we toiled hard, and saw every trouble, and met many evils, and we were consumed and became few, and our spirit became small.

**10** We were destroyed, and there was no one to help us in word or in deed. We were powerless and found nothing. We were tormented and destroyed, and did not hope to see life from one day to the next.

**11** We hoped to be the head and have become the tail. We toiled as we labored, and had no power over our toil. We became food for the sinners, and the unrighteous made their yoke heavy on us.

**12** Those who hate us and those who goad us have ruled over us, and to those who hate us we bowed our necks, but they had no mercy on us.

**13** We wanted to get away from them, to escape and rest, but found no place to flee to and be safe from them.

**14** We complained of them to the angels in our distress, and cried out against those who devour us, but they did not regard our cry and did not want to hear our voice.

**15** They helped those who rob us and devour us, and those who made us few. They hid their violence, and did not lift from us the yoke of those who devour us and scatter us and kill us. They hid our killing, and did not remember that they had raised their hands against us."

## Notes

- **vv3–4 "written down for the spirits of those who died in righteousness"** — Enoch's answer to the grief of 102:4–5. John hears a voice from heaven: *Write: Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord from now on… so that they may rest from their labors* (Revelation 14:13). *The righteous will be remembered forever* (Psalm 112:6). The Greek adds that the spirits of the righteous *will not perish*.
- **v11 "We hoped to be the head and have become the tail"** — the blessing and the curse of the law turned upside down: *The LORD will make you the head and not the tail* (Deuteronomy 28:13); *he will be the head, and you will be the tail* (Deuteronomy 28:44).

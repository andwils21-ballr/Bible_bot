# 1 Enoch 101

**1** Look at the heaven, all you children of heaven, and at every work of the Most High, and fear Him, and do no evil before Him.

**2** If He shuts the windows of heaven and holds back the rain and the dew from coming down on the earth because of you, what will you do?

**3** If He sends His anger on you and on all your deeds, will you not be the ones pleading with Him? For you speak great and hard words against His righteousness, and you will have no peace.

**4** Do you not see the masters of the ships, how they are tossed by the waves and shaken by the winds, and are in distress?

**5** Because of this they are afraid, for all their good possessions go out on the sea with them, and they have no good thoughts in their heart, for the sea will swallow them and they will perish in it.

**6** Are not the whole sea and all its waters and all its movement the work of the Most High? He has sealed all its doings and its waters, and bound the whole of it with sand.

**7** At His rebuke it dries up and is afraid, and all its fish die, and all that is in it. But you sinners who are on the earth do not fear Him.

**8** Did He not make heaven and earth and all that is in them? Who gave teaching and wisdom to all that move on the earth and to those in the sea?

**9** Do not the masters of the ships fear the sea? Yet the sinners do not fear the Most High.

## Notes

- **v1 "Look at the heaven, all you children of heaven"** — the Greek has *Consider, then, children of men, the works of the Most High*. Isaiah: *Lift up your eyes on high and see: who created these?* (Isaiah 40:26). As in chapters 2–5, creation keeps its order and man does not.
- **v2 "If He shuts the windows of heaven"** — the curse of the law: *He will shut up the heavens, so that there will be no rain* (Deuteronomy 11:17). The rain of 100:11–12 again.
- **v3 "will you not be the ones pleading with Him?"** — *ʾakkonu ʾantəmu za-tāstabaqʷʿəwwo*, so the Greek, *will you not be entreating him?* Charles reads it the other way: they *cannot petition Him*. **"great and hard words against His righteousness"** — the Greek has *against his majesty*. The words that Jude quotes from 1:9 end the same way: *all the harsh things which ungodly sinners have spoken against Him* (Jude 15).
- **vv4–5 "the masters of the ships"** — *nagaśta ʾaḥmār*; Charles, *sailors*; the Greek, *shipmasters*. The psalm: *Those who go down to the sea in ships… He spoke and raised the stormy wind, which lifted up its waves… their soul melted away in their trouble* (Psalm 107:23–26). The Greek has the sailors *cast their possessions into the sea*, as Jonah's shipmates did: *they threw the cargo that was in the ship into the sea to lighten it* (Jonah 1:5). **"they have no good thoughts in their heart"** — *wa-śannāya ʾiyəḫelləyu ba-ləbbomu*; Charles, *they have evil forebodings of heart*.
- **v6 "bound the whole of it with sand"** — Jeremiah 5:22, as at 69:18. God to Job: *Thus far you may come and no farther, and here your proud waves shall stop* (Job 38:11).
- **v7 "At His rebuke it dries up"** — Nahum: *He rebukes the sea and makes it dry* (Nahum 1:4).
- **v8 "heaven and earth and all that is in them"** — the fourth commandment: *in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that is in them* (Exodus 20:11).

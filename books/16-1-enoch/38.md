# 1 Enoch 38

**1** The first parable. When the congregation of the righteous appears, and sinners are judged for their sins and driven from the face of the dry land,

**2** and when the Righteous One appears before the eyes of the righteous, whose chosen works hang on the Lord of Spirits, and light appears to the righteous and the chosen who live on the dry land — where then will the sinners live, and where will those rest who denied the Lord of Spirits? It would have been better for them if they had not been born.

**3** When the secrets of the righteous are revealed, the sinners will be judged, and the wicked will be driven from the presence of the righteous and the chosen.

**4** From then on those who hold the earth will no longer be mighty and exalted. They will not be able to look at the face of the holy, because the Lord of Spirits has made His light appear on the face of the holy, the righteous and the chosen.

**5** The kings and the mighty will perish at that time, and they will be given into the hand of the righteous and the holy.

**6** From then on no one will seek mercy for them from the Lord of glory, because their life has come to an end.

## Notes

- **v1 "the congregation of the righteous… sinners are judged"** — the parable opens with the two phrases of Psalm 1:5: *the wicked will not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the **congregation of the righteous***. The psalm says the sinners will not stand there; the parable describes the day it happens.
- **v2 "the Righteous One"** — *ṣādəq*, singular: the first appearance of the figure at the center of the Parables, who will also be called the Chosen One (40:5) and the Son of Man (46:2). Stephen uses the same title in Acts: the prophets *announced beforehand the coming of the **Righteous One***, whom you betrayed (Acts 7:52).
- **v2 "whose chosen works hang on the Lord of Spirits"** — *sǝqul*, **hung, suspended**. Their works depend on Him the way a thing hangs from a peg. The phrase comes back at 40:5 and 46:8.
- **v2 "It would have been better for them if they had not been born"** — the same sentence Jesus says of the one who betrays the Son of Man: *it would have been good for that man if he had not been born* (Matthew 26:24).
- **v5 "The kings and the mighty"** — the Parables name their enemy by rank: kings, the mighty, the exalted, *those who hold the earth* (v4). The word *kings*, *nagaśt*, occurs 20 times in chapters 37–71. In chapters 1–36 the judgment fell on the Watchers; here it falls on rulers.
- **v6 "from the Lord of glory"** — the EOTC. Knibb's Ge'ez reads *the Lord of Spirits*. At 40:3 the two change places.

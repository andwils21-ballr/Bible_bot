# 1 Enoch 81

**1** He said to me, "Enoch, look at the book of the tablets of heaven, and read what is written on them, and learn every one of them."

**2** I looked at everything on the tablets of heaven and read everything written, and I understood everything. I read the book and everything written in it, all the deeds of men and of all the children of flesh on the earth, to the generations of the world.

**3** Then at once I blessed the great Lord, the King of glory forever, because He has made every work of the world. I praised the Lord for His patience, and I blessed, and I wept over the sons of Adam.

**4** Then I said, "Blessed is the man who dies righteous and good, against whom no book of wrongdoing has been written, and in whom no error is found on the day of judgment."

**5** Those three holy ones brought me and set me down on the earth before the door of my house, and said to me, "Tell everything to Methuselah your son, and show all your children that no flesh is righteous before the Lord, for He created them.

**6** For one year we will leave you with your children, until you give your commands again, so that you may teach your children and write for them and bear witness to all your children. In the second year they will take you from among them.

**7** Let your heart be strong, for the good will announce righteousness to the good; the righteous will rejoice with the righteous, and they will greet one another.

**8** The sinner will die with the sinner, and the one who turns away will sink with the one who turns away.

**9** Those who do righteousness will die because of the deeds of men, and they will be gathered in because of the deeds of the wicked."

**10** In those days they finished speaking with me, and I came to my people, blessing the Lord of the ages.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "the tablets of heaven"** — *ṣafṣafa samāy*: a written record in heaven of *all the deeds of men*. Compare the books opened before the Head of Days at 47:3.
- **v3 "I wept over the sons of Adam"** — the EOTC, *wa-bakayku*. Charles has *blessed Him because of the children of men*.
- **v4 "Blessed is the man who dies righteous"** — in Revelation a voice from heaven says, *Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord* (Revelation 14:13).
- **v5 "Those three holy ones"** — the EOTC; Charles has *seven*.
- **v5 "no flesh is righteous before the Lord"** — the psalm: *do not enter into judgment with Your servant, for no one living is righteous before You* (Psalm 143:2).
- **v6 "In the second year they will take you from among them"** — the book's own account of how Enoch was taken; Genesis says only *he was not, for God took him* (Genesis 5:24).
- **v10 "the Lord of the ages"** — *ʾƎgziʾa ʿālamāt*, the plural of the word in *the Lord of the ages* at 58:4.

# 1 Enoch 18

**1** I saw the storehouses of all the winds, and I saw that with them He ordered the whole creation and the foundations of the earth. I saw the cornerstone of the earth.

**2** I saw the four winds that bear the earth and the firmament of heaven.

**3** I saw how the winds stretch out the height of heaven, and they stand between heaven and earth. They are the pillars of heaven.

**4** I saw the winds that turn the heaven, that bring the disk of the sun and all the stars to their setting.

**5** I saw the winds upon the earth bearing the clouds, and I saw the paths of the angels. I saw at the edge of the earth the firmament of heaven above.

**6** I passed on toward the south, and it burns day and night, where the seven mountains are of precious stone — three facing east and three facing south.

**7** Those toward the east were of colored stone, and one was of pearl, and one of healing stone. Those toward the south were of red stone.

**8** The middle one reached up to heaven, like the throne of God, of alabaster stone, and the summit of the throne was of sapphire.

**9** I saw a burning fire. Beyond these mountains

**10** is a place at the end of the great earth. There the heavens come to an end.

**11** I saw a great chasm among the pillars of heavenly fire, and I saw pillars of fire descending among them, without measure, neither toward the height nor toward the depth.

**12** Beyond that chasm I saw a place where there was no firmament of heaven above it, and no foundation of earth beneath it, and no water on it, and no birds — but it was a desolate and terrible place.

**13** I saw there seven stars, like great burning mountains. When I asked about them,

**14** the angel said, "This is the place of the end of heaven and earth. It has become a prison for the stars of heaven and for the host of heaven.

**15** The stars that roll over the fire — these are the ones that transgressed the command of the Lord at the beginning of their rising, because they did not come out at their appointed times."

**16** He was angry with them, and bound them until the time when their sin is completed.

## Notes

- **v1 "the cornerstone of the earth"** — the building term. Job 38:6 asks who laid *the cornerstone* of the earth and gets no answer. Enoch says he saw it.
- **v8 "like the throne of God, of alabaster... the summit of sapphire"** — the middle mountain of seven, reaching to heaven, described as a throne. **Exodus 24:10** has the elders see under God's feet *something like a pavement of sapphire*, and Ezekiel 1:26 puts a throne of sapphire above the firmament. The stone is consistent across three books.
- **vv12–16 the prison for stars** — **and this is the book's strangest judgment.** Seven stars are locked up, and the charge in v15 is that *they did not come out at their appointed times*. Not rebellion, not pride. **Lateness.** They broke schedule.

# 1 Enoch 4

**1** Again observe the days of summer, how the sun is above the earth and directly over it. You look for shade and shelter from the heat of the sun, and the earth burns with scorching heat, and you cannot walk on the ground or on the rock because of its heat.

---

## Notes

- **v1** — there is no Greek for this chapter. The witnesses are the Ge'ez and a torn **Aramaic** fragment, and they agree closely: the searching for shade, the inability to walk on ground or rock, the heat. Where two witnesses this far apart in age and language line up phrase by phrase, the wording behind both is likely close to original.
- **v1 "observe the days of summer"** — the Aramaic again has *the **signs*** of summer, as it did in 2:3, where the Ge'ez has *days*. The same difference twice in two chapters is a pattern, not a slip: one tradition is reading the seasons as signs to be interpreted, the other as periods of time.
- **v1 "you cannot walk on the ground or on the rock"** — the Aramaic reads *the du[st] and on its rocks*. Dust, not ground. It is the word from Genesis 2:7, the stuff the first man was made of, and the sun makes it untouchable.

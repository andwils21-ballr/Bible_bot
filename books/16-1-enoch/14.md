# 1 Enoch 14

**1** This book is the word of righteousness and the rebuke of the Watchers who are from eternity, as the Holy and Great One commanded in that vision.

**2** I saw in my sleep what I will now speak with a tongue of flesh and with the breath of my mouth, which the Great One has given to men, to speak with it and to understand with the heart.

**3** As He created and gave to men the understanding of the word of knowledge, so He created and gave to me the rebuking of the Watchers, the sons of heaven.

**4** "I wrote down your petition, and in my vision it appeared like this: your petition will not be granted you through all the days of eternity, and the judgment against you is complete and decided.

**5** And from now on you will not go up into heaven for all eternity, and on earth it has been decreed that you are to be bound for all the days of eternity.

**6** And before that, you will see the destruction of your beloved sons, and you will have no pleasure in them, and they will fall before you by the sword.

**7** And your petition on their behalf will not be granted, nor on your own — even while you weep and make supplication, and speak not one word of the writing I wrote."

## The Wall of Hail

**8** And the vision appeared to me like this. Here — clouds called me, and a mist called me, and the courses of the stars and the lightnings hurried me and drove me on, and winds in the vision made me fly and lifted me up and carried me into heaven.

**9** And I went in until I came near a wall built of stones of hail, and tongues of fire surrounded it, and it began to terrify me.

**10** And I went into the tongues of fire and came near a great house built of stones of hail. And the walls of that house were like slabs of stone, and all of them were snow, and its floor was snow.

**11** Its ceiling was like the courses of the stars and the lightnings, and between them were fiery cherubim, and their sky was water.

**12** And a burning fire was around its walls, and its door burned with fire.

**13** And I went into that house, and it was hot as fire and cold as ice, and there was nothing of life in it. Fear covered me, and trembling seized me.

**14** And as I was shaking and trembling, I fell on my face. And I was looking in the vision —

**15** and here, another house, greater than this one, and its door stood open before me, and it was built entirely of tongues of fire.

**16** And in every way it surpassed the other in glory and honor and greatness, so that I cannot describe to you its glory and its greatness.

**17** Its floor was fire, and above it were lightnings and the courses of the stars, and its ceiling also was burning fire.

**18** And I looked and saw in it a high throne. Its appearance was like crystal, and its wheels like the shining sun, and there was the sound of cherubim.

**19** And from beneath the throne came streams of flaming fire, and I could not look at it.

**20** And the Great Glory sat on it, and His robe was brighter than the sun and whiter than any snow.

**21** And no angel could come in, and no one of flesh could look on the face of the Honored and Glorious One.

**22** A burning fire was around Him, and a great fire stood before Him, and no one of those around Him came near. Ten thousand times ten thousand were before Him, and He needed no counsel.

**23** And the holiest of those who drew near to Him did not leave by night or by day, and did not go away from Him.

**24** And until then I was on my face, covered and trembling. And the Lord called me with His own mouth and said to me, "Come here, Enoch, and hear My word."

**25** And He raised me up and brought me as far as the door, and I was looking with my face downward.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 the book announces itself** — a preface in the middle of the story, in Enoch's own voice, saying what the following chapters are. And the claim it makes about itself is modest and strange: *a tongue of flesh and the breath of my mouth*. He is saying the vision was not speakable, and he is going to speak it anyway.
- **v4 "I wrote down your petition"** — the answer to chapter 13. The document Enoch drafted came back refused. Verses 4–7 are him reading the verdict aloud to the angels who hired him.
- **v7 "speak not one word of the writing I wrote"** — the petition is not only rejected; they are forbidden to use it. The lawyer is told to instruct his clients that the brief is void.
- **v8 "clouds called me... winds made me fly"** — the weather carries him. Nothing in the ascent is an angel or a chariot: cloud, mist, shooting stars, lightning, wind. The same *ruach*, wind or spirit, that opens seas in Exodus.
- **vv9–13 the first house, of hail and snow and fire** — a wall, then a great house, then an inner house. **That is a temple plan**: outer court, holy place, holy of holies. The building materials are impossible on purpose — it is made of hailstones and snow, and it burns; it is *hot as fire and cold as ice* in the same sentence.
- **v13 "there was nothing of life in it"** — said of the outer house, the one a man can enter. What lies beyond it is where the life is, and he cannot go there yet.
- **v18 "a high throne... its wheels like the shining sun"** — **Ezekiel 1** has the same furniture: a throne, the appearance of crystal, wheels, cherubim, fire. Whichever came first, the two visions are furnished from the same imagination.
- **vv18–22 and Daniel 7** — set this beside **Daniel 7:9–10**, which can be checked word for word. Daniel: *his garment **white as snow***; Enoch 14:20: *whiter than any snow*. Daniel: *a **river of fire** flowing out from before him*; Enoch 14:19: *streams of flaming fire from beneath the throne*. Daniel: *a thousand thousands served him, and **ten thousand times ten thousand** stood before him*; Enoch 14:22: *ten thousand times ten thousand were before Him*. Three specific images, in the same order, in two books.
- **v20 "the Great Glory"** — the title used instead of a name. The book will not say *God sat on the throne*; it says the Glory sat. It is the same move as calling the presence in the wilderness *kavod*, weight.
- **v22 "He needed no counsel"** — that is the Ge'ez. The **Greek** has something else entirely: *his every word was an act*. One witness says God takes no advice; the other says God's speech is deed. Both are single short clauses in the same slot, and they are not variants of one another.
- **v25 "And He raised me up"** — in the Ge'ez, God does it. In the **Greek**, *one of the holy ones approached me, raised me up, made me stand*. Whether a man is lifted to the door of the holy of holies by God's own hand or by an attendant is the sort of thing a tradition decides, and the two traditions decided differently.

# 1 Enoch 28

**1** From there I went toward the east, into the middle of the mountain range of the desert, and I saw a wilderness, alone and empty, yet full of trees.

**2** They were from this seed, and water gushed out over it from above, and a torrent appeared, running abundantly toward the north-west, and from every side water and dew rose up.

---

## Notes

- **The verses** — the EOTC Ge'ez divides this chapter into two verses. Knibb's Ge'ez, the Greek and Charles's English divide the same words into three, and other English editions number it 28:1–3.

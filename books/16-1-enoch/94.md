# 1 Enoch 94

**1** And now I say to you, my children: love righteousness and walk in it. For the paths of righteousness are worthy to be accepted, but the paths of wrongdoing will quickly be destroyed and fail.

**2** To certain men of a generation the paths of violence and of death will be revealed, and they will keep far from them and will not follow them.

**3** And now I say to you, the righteous: do not walk in the paths of evil and violence, nor in the paths of death. Do not come near them, so that you are not destroyed.

**4** Seek and choose for yourselves righteousness and a chosen life, and walk in the paths of peace, so that you may live and prosper.

**5** Hold my words fast in the thoughts of your heart, and do not let my word be wiped out of your heart. For I know that sinners will counsel men to treat wisdom wickedly, so that no place is found for her, and no trial grows less.

**6** Woe to those who build wrongdoing and violence and lay deceit as a foundation, for they will quickly be overthrown, and they will have no peace.

**7** Woe to those who build their houses with sin, for they will be overthrown from all their foundations, and they will fall by the sword. Those who gain gold and silver in judgment will quickly perish.

**8** Woe to you, the rich, for you have trusted in your riches, and from your riches you will go out, because you did not remember the Most High in the days of your riches.

**9** You have done blasphemy and wrongdoing, and you have become ready for the day of the shedding of blood, and for the day of darkness, and for the day of the great judgment.

**10** This is what I say and make known to you: He who created you will overthrow you, and over your fall there will be no mercy, and your Creator will rejoice at your destruction.

**11** In those days your righteous ones will be a reproach to the sinners and the godless.

## Notes

- **v4 "choose for yourselves righteousness and a chosen life"** — Moses at the end of the law: *I have set before you life and death, blessing and curse. Choose life, so that you may live, you and your offspring* (Deuteronomy 30:19).
- **v8 "Woe to you, the rich"** — Jesus in Luke: *But woe to you who are rich, for you have received your comfort* (Luke 6:24). *Trusted in your riches* is the psalm's man who *trusted in the abundance of his riches* (Psalm 52:7).

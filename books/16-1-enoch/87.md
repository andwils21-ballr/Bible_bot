# 1 Enoch 87

**1** Again I saw them, and they began to gore one another and devour one another, and the earth began to cry out.

**2** I raised my eyes again to heaven and saw in the vision, and there came out of heaven beings in the likeness of white men. Four came out from that place, and three with them.

**3** Those three who came out last took me by my hand and lifted me up from the generations of the earth, and raised me to a high place, and showed me a tower high above the earth, and all the hills were lower.

**4** They said to me, "Stay here until you see everything that comes on these elephants and camels and donkeys, and on the stars, and on all the bulls."

---

## Notes

- **v2 "Four came out"** — the EOTC has *ʾaḥadu*, **one**, where the next chapter speaks of *those four who came out* (88:1, 3), and 90:21 calls all of them *those seven white ones*. The English follows the count the text itself requires, and Charles has *four* (see choice #1 of the 72 report).

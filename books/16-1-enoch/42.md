# 1 Enoch 42

**1** Wisdom found no place where she could live, and her dwelling was in the heavens.

**2** Wisdom went out to live among the sons of men and found no dwelling. Wisdom went back to her place and took her seat among the angels.

**3** Iniquity went out of her storehouses. She found those she did not seek, and she lived among them like rain in the desert and like dew on thirsty ground.

---

## Notes

- **The whole chapter** — three verses on Wisdom and her opposite, set between Enoch's view of the heavenly lights (41) and the stars called by name (43). Both Ge'ez witnesses have it here.
- **vv1–2 "Wisdom… found no dwelling"** — the reverse of Sirach 24, where Wisdom seeks a resting place among the nations and is told by her Creator, *Make your dwelling in Jacob, and in Israel receive your inheritance* (Sirach 24:7–8). In Sirach she finds a home on earth. Here she finds none and goes back to heaven. John's Gospel says it of the Word: *He came to His own, and His own did not receive Him* (John 1:11).
- **v3 "like rain in the desert and like dew on thirsty ground"** — the pictures of blessing, used for its opposite. At 39:5 *righteousness flowed like water, and mercy like dew* among the holy. Isaiah uses the same pictures for God's gift: *I will pour water on the thirsty* (Isaiah 44:3). Iniquity is welcomed the way rain is welcomed in a dry land.
- **v3 "She found those she did not seek"** — Wisdom sought men and was refused; Iniquity did not need to seek, and was taken in.

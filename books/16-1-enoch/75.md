# 1 Enoch 75

**1** The leaders of the heads of thousands, who are over all creation and over all the stars, are also with the four days that are added. They are not separated from their positions, by the whole reckoning of the year, and they serve on the four days that are not counted in the reckoning of the year.

**2** Because of them people go wrong, for those lights truly serve at the stations of the world: one in the first gate, one in the third gate, one in the fourth gate, and one in the sixth gate. The exactness of the world is completed in each three hundred and sixty-four stations of the world.

**3** For Uriel the angel, whom the Lord of glory set forever over all the lights of heaven, showed me the signs and the seasons and the years and the days, in heaven and in the world, so that they rule on the face of heaven and are seen on the earth, and are leaders for the day and the night: the sun and the moon and the stars, and all the serving powers that go around in all the chariots of heaven.

**4** In the same way Uriel showed me twelve open gates in the circle of the sun's chariot in heaven, from which the rays of the sun come out, and from them heat comes out over the earth when they are opened at the times appointed for them.

**5** So too for the winds and for the spirit of the dew, when they are opened at their times: they stand open in heaven at the ends.

**6** I saw twelve gates in heaven at the ends of the earth, from which the sun and the moon and the stars and all the works of heaven go out, in the east and in the west.

**7** There are many open windows to their right and left, and one window at its time gives off heat, like those gates from which the stars go out as He commanded them, and in which they set according to their number.

**8** I saw chariots in heaven, running through the world above and below those gates in which the stars that never set turn around.

**9** One is greater than all of them, and it goes around the whole world.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "the four days that are added… one in the first gate, one in the third… the fourth… the sixth"** — the four thirty-first days of 72:13, 19, 25 and 31, one at the end of each quarter of the year, when the sun stays a day longer in the sixth, fourth, first and third gates. *They are not counted in the reckoning of the year*, and *because of them people go wrong*.
- **v3 "the signs and the seasons and the years and the days… leaders for the day and the night"** — the purpose God gave the lights on the fourth day: *let them be for signs, and for appointed times, and for days and years… and to rule over the day and over the night* (Genesis 1:14, 18).
- **v4 "the rays of the sun"** — *ʾəgarihā la-ḍaḥāy*, literally **the feet of the sun**, the word for the feet Noah lifted to set out at 65:2.

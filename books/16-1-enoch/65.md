# 1 Enoch 65

**1** In those days Noah saw that the earth had tilted and that its destruction was near.

**2** He set out from there and went to the ends of the earth, and he cried out to his forefather Enoch. Noah said in a bitter voice, three times, "Hear me, hear me, hear me."

**3** He said to him, "Tell me what is being done on the earth, for the earth is in such distress and is shaken. Perhaps I will perish with it."

**4** After that there was a great uproar on the earth, and a voice was heard from heaven, and I fell on my face.

**5** Enoch my forefather came and stood by me and said to me, "Why have you cried out to me with a bitter cry and weeping?

**6** A command has gone out from the presence of the Lord of Spirits concerning those who live on the dry land, that their end must come, because they have learned all the secrets of the angels, and all the violence of the satans, and all their hidden power, and the power of those who practice sorcery, and the power of spells, and the power of those who cast molten images for the whole earth,

**7** and how silver is produced from the dust of the earth, and how soft metal comes to be on the earth.

**8** For lead and tin are not produced from the earth as the first is. It is a spring that produces them, and an angel stands in it, and that angel is preeminent."

**9** After this my forefather Enoch took me by the hand and raised me up and said to me, "Go, for I have asked the Lord of Spirits about this uproar on the earth.

**10** He said to me, 'Because of their wrongdoing their judgment has been completed, and it will not be counted before Me. Because of the months they searched out, they know that the earth will perish, and those who live on it.'

**11** For these there will be no return forever, because they have shown them what was hidden, and they are condemned. But not you, my son: the Lord of Spirits knows that you are pure and good, free of this reproach about the secrets.

**12** He has established your name among the holy ones, and He will keep you safe from among those who live on the dry land. He has established your seed in righteousness, for kings and for great glories, and from your seed will come a spring of the righteous and the holy, without number forever."

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "Noah saw… and I fell on my face"** — the chapter begins by speaking of Noah and in v4 turns to *I*. From there Noah tells it himself, and calls Enoch *my forefather*, as in 60:8.
- **v6 "all the secrets of the angels"** — the Watchers' teachings of chapters 7–8: spells, the cutting of roots, the signs of the stars. *The violence of the satans* uses the plural of 40:7.
- **vv7–8 "silver… soft metal… lead and tin"** — the metals of the mountains of 52:2 and 52:8, the same Ge'ez words. What men learned to dig and cast is part of the charge against them.
- **v10 "Because of the months they searched out"** — *ʾawrāḫ*, **months**, in both Ge'ez witnesses. The singular *warḫ* is both **month** and **moon** (60:1, *the seventh month*; 60:12, *the light of the moon*), and among the Watchers' teachings was *the course of the moon* (8:3). Both English renderings give *sorceries* here.
- **v11 "pure and good, free of this reproach"** — Genesis: *Noah was a righteous man, whole in his generations* (Genesis 6:9). Noah is set apart from a condemned generation.

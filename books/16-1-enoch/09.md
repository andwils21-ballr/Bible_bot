# 1 Enoch 9

**1** Then Michael and Uriel and Raphael and Gabriel looked down from heaven and saw much blood being shed on the earth, and all the lawlessness being done on the earth.

**2** They said to one another, "The sound of their crying — the wasted earth cries out as far as the gates of heaven."

**3** "And now to you, holy ones of heaven, the souls of men bring their case, saying, 'Bring our judgment before the Most High.'"

**4** They said to the Lord, "You are Lord of lords and God of gods and King of kings. The throne of Your glory is for all the generations of eternity, and Your name is holy and glorious for all the generations of eternity, blessed and glorious.

**5** You made all things, and power over all is with You, and everything is uncovered and open before You, and You see all things, and there is nothing that can be hidden from You.

**6** See then what Azazel has done — how he taught all unrighteousness on the earth, and revealed the eternal secrets that are kept in heaven, which men were striving to learn.

**7** Shemihazah has made known spells — he to whom You gave authority to rule over those who are with him.

**8** They went in to the daughters of the men of the earth and slept with them and defiled themselves, and revealed to them all these sins.

**9** The women bore giants, and by them the whole earth has been filled with blood and unrighteousness.

**10** And now — the souls of those who have died cry out and bring their case as far as the gates of heaven, and their groaning has gone up and cannot stop, because of the lawless deeds done on the earth.

**11** You know all things before they come to pass, and You see this and their deeds, and You say nothing to us. What are we to do with them about this?"

## Notes

- **v1 the four names** — the **Greek** gives Michael, Uriel, Raphael and Gabriel, and this rendering follows it. The **Ge'ez** has Michael, Gabriel, **Suriel** and Uriel. The two lists agree on three names and differ on the fourth, and Suriel and Raphael are not variants of one name — they are different angels. The set of four archangels is not fixed even inside this one book.

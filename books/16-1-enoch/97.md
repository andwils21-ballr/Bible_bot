# 1 Enoch 97

**1** Believe, you righteous: the sinners will become a reproach, and they will perish on the day of wrongdoing.

**2** Be sure of this, you sinners: the Most High remembers your destruction, and the angels rejoice over your destruction.

**3** What will you do, sinners, and where will you flee on that day of judgment, when you hear the voice of the prayer of the righteous?

**4** You will not be like them, but this word will be a witness against them: you were companions of the sinners.

**5** In those days the prayer of the holy ones will reach the Lord, and for you the days of your judgment will come.

**6** All the words of their wrongdoing will be read out before the Great and Holy One. Your faces will be put to shame, and every work that was grounded in wrongdoing will be cast away.

**7** Woe to you, sinners, who are in the middle of the sea and on the dry land, whose record is evil against you.

**8** Woe to you who gain silver and gold, not by righteousness, and say, "We have grown rich with riches and have possessions, and we have gained everything we wanted.

**9** Now let us do what we have planned, for we have gathered silver and filled our storehouses, and the workers of our houses are as many as water."

**10** Your lies will flow away like water, for your riches will not stay with you but will quickly go up from you, because you gained it all by wrongdoing; and you will be handed over to a great curse.

## Notes

- **v1 "the sinners will become a reproach"** — *ṣəʾlat*, the word of 94:11.
- **v2 "the angels rejoice over your destruction"** — the reverse of Luke: *there is joy before the angels of God over one sinner who repents* (Luke 15:10).
- **v3 "What will you do… and where will you flee on that day"** — Isaiah: *What will you do on the day of punishment, in the ruin that will come from far away? To whom will you flee for help, and where will you leave your wealth?* (Isaiah 10:3).
- **v4 "a witness against them: you were companions of the sinners"** — the EOTC has *against them*, *dibehomu*, and then *you*; the same sentence at 96:3 has *against you*. Charles reads the first sentence the other way: the sinners *shall fare like* those against whom the word is a witness.
- **v5 "the prayer of the holy ones will reach the Lord"** — *ʾƎgziʾ*. Charles has *the righteous*. John sees *the smoke of the incense, with the prayers of the saints*, go up *before God* (Revelation 8:4).
- **v6 "All the words of their wrongdoing will be read out"** — the EOTC has *their*; the Greek and Charles have *your*. The books opened at the judgment: *the dead were judged by what was written in the books, according to their works* (Revelation 20:12). The Greek has *read before the Great Holy One to your face* where the EOTC has *your faces will be put to shame*.
- **vv8–9 "We have grown rich with riches"** — the Greek has the same boast: *We have become rich with wealth and have possessed property*. The church at Laodicea says, *I am rich, and have become wealthy, and need nothing* (Revelation 3:17), and the rich fool says to his soul, *you have many goods laid up for many years* (Luke 12:19).
- **v9 "the workers of our houses are as many as water"** — *ḥarasta ʾabyātina*, **the farmers of our houses**; Charles gives *many are the husbandmen in our houses*. The Greek has *many goods in our houses*.
- **v10 "Your lies will flow away like water"** — the Greek has *like water… you are deceived*. It agrees with the rest of the verse: the riches will not remain, *because you have acquired everything unjustly*.

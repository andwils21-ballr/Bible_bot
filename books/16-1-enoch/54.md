# 1 Enoch 54

**1** I turned and saw another part of the earth, and there I saw a deep valley with fire burning in it.

**2** They brought the kings and the mighty and threw them into that deep valley.

**3** There my eyes saw them making their instruments, chains of iron too heavy to be weighed.

**4** I asked the angel of peace who went with me, "These chains, these instruments — for whom are they being prepared?"

**5** He said to me, "They are being prepared for the hosts of Azazel, so that they may take them and throw them into the lowest depths of condemnation. They will cover their jaws with rough stones, as the Lord of Spirits commanded.

**6** Michael, Gabriel, Raphael and Phanuel will seize them on that great day and throw them into the furnace of burning fire on that day, so that the Lord of Spirits may take vengeance on them for their wrongdoing, because they became servants of Satan and led astray those who live on the dry land.

**7** In those days the punishment of the Lord of Spirits will go out. All the storehouses of the waters above the heavens will be opened, and the fountains that are beneath the heavens and above the earth.

**8** All the waters will be joined with the waters above the heavens. The water above the heavens is the male, and the water beneath the earth is the female.

**9** All who live on the dry land and all who live under the ends of heaven will be wiped out.

**10** Because of this they will recognize the wrongdoing they have done on the earth, and because of this they will perish."

## Notes

- **vv4–5 "for whom are they being prepared… for the hosts of Azazel"** — Jesus speaks of *the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels* (Matthew 25:41).
- **v5 "rough stones, as the Lord of Spirits commanded"** — *ʾəbna ṭawwāyāta*, the words of the command at 10:5, *Lay beneath him rough and sharp stones*, given to Raphael against Azazel. Chapter 10 also named the day: *on the day of the great judgment he will be led away into the burning fire* (10:6). Verse 6 here is that day.
- **v6 "Michael, Gabriel, Raphael and Phanuel"** — the four presences of chapter 40, named at 40:9.
- **v6 "the furnace of burning fire"** — *ʾətona ʾəsāt za-yənaddəd*, the phrase of Daniel 3:6, *a furnace of burning fire*. Jesus says the angels *will throw them into the furnace of fire* (Matthew 13:42).
- **v7 "the storehouses of the waters above the heavens will be opened"** — from here the angel speaks of the flood, as 55:1–2 makes clear. Genesis tells it in the same two halves: *all the fountains of the great deep were split open, and the lattices of the heavens were opened* (Genesis 7:11). The EOTC places the fountains *beneath the heavens and above the earth*; Knibb's Ge'ez has *beneath the earth*, as v8 does.
- **v8 "The water above the heavens is the male, and the water beneath the earth is the female"** — on the second day God *divided between the waters that were beneath the expanse and the waters that were above the expanse* (Genesis 1:7). In the flood the two are joined again, and the joining is pictured as male and female.

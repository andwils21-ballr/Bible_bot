# 1 Enoch 85

**1** After this I saw another dream, and I will show you all of it, my son.

**2** Enoch raised his voice and said to his son Methuselah, "To you I speak, my son. Hear my words, and turn your ear to the dream vision of your father.

**3** Before I took your mother Edna, I saw in a vision on my bed, and here — a bull came out of the earth, and that bull was white. After it came out a heifer calf, and with her came two more calves, one of them black and the other red.

**4** That black calf gored the red one and pursued him over the earth, and from then on I could no longer see that red calf.

**5** That black calf grew, and that heifer went with him, and I saw many bulls come out of him that were like him and followed him.

**6** That heifer, the first one, went out from the presence of that first bull to look for that red calf, but she did not find him. She wailed over him with great wailing and looked for him.

**7** I watched until that first bull came to her and quieted her, and from that time she did not cry out.

**8** After this she bore another white bull, and after him she bore many bulls and black heifers.

**9** I saw in my sleep that white bull grow in the same way and become a great white bull, and from him came many white bulls that were like him.

**10** They began to father many white bulls that were like them, one following the other.

## Notes

- **vv3–10 the second dream** — the history of the world told with animals, which runs to chapter 90. Read against Genesis, it begins with Adam, the white bull from the earth; Eve, the heifer; Cain and Abel, the black calf who gores the red and pursues him (Genesis 4:8); Eve's search and grief, which Genesis does not tell; and Seth, the other white bull (Genesis 4:25).
- **v3 "your mother Edna"** — *ʾƎdnā*, the name of Enoch's wife, which Genesis does not give.
- **v3 "and here —"** — *wa-nāhu*, the dreamer's own moment of seeing.
- **v10** — a scrap of the Greek survives here, a few letters only.

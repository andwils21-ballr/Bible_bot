# 1 Enoch 77

**1** They call the first quarter the east, because it is first. They call the second the south, because the Most High comes down there, and there above all He who is blessed forever comes down.

**2** The quarter of the west is named the Diminished, because there all the lights of heaven diminish and go down.

**3** The fourth quarter, named the north, is divided into three parts. One of them is a dwelling for people; the second is for the seas of water and the deeps, the forests and the rivers, the darkness and the mist; and the third part is in the garden of righteousness.

**4** I saw seven high mountains, higher than all the mountains on the earth. From them the frost comes out, and days and seasons and years pass and go.

**5** I saw seven rivers on the earth, greater than all the rivers. One of them comes from the west and pours its water into the Great Sea.

**6** Those two come from the north to the sea and pour their water into the Red Sea in the east.

**7** The remaining four go out on the side of the north to their own sea, the Red Sea, and two pour into the Great Sea, and there, they say, is the desert.

**8** I saw seven great islands in the sea and on the land: two on the land and five in the Great Sea.

## Notes

- **v1 "the east… the south… because the Most High comes down there"** — the Ge'ez gives each quarter a reason for its name, but only the reasons, not the play on words behind them; *ʾAzēb*, **south**, carries no *down* or *high* in it. Charles has the same reasons.
- **v3 "the third part is in the garden of righteousness"** — the garden Enoch came to in the east at 32:3, *the garden of righteousness*.
- **vv6–7 "the Red Sea"** — *Bāḥra ʾErtrā*, the Greek name *Erythra*, **red**. Two scraps of the Greek survive in vv7–8, and both have *the Red Sea*, *ἐρυθρὰ θάλασσα*.
- **v7 "and there, they say, is the desert"** — *wa-ba-həya yəbēlu madbarā*: the sense is uncertain. Charles has *and some say: into the desert*.

# 1 Enoch 48

**1** In that place I saw the fountain of righteousness, which never runs out, and around it were many fountains of wisdom. All the thirsty drank from it and were filled with wisdom, and their dwelling was with the righteous and the holy and the chosen.

**2** At that hour that Son of Man was named in the presence of the Lord of Spirits, and his name before the Head of Days.

**3** Before the sun and the signs were created, before the stars of heaven were made, his name was named before the Lord of Spirits.

**4** He will be a staff for the righteous and the holy, so that they may lean on him and not fall. He is the light of the nations, and he will be the hope of those whose hearts are grieved.

**5** All who live on the dry land will fall down and worship before him, and they will bless and praise and sing to the name of the Lord of Spirits.

**6** For this reason he was chosen and hidden before Him before the world was created.

**7** The wisdom of the Lord of Spirits has revealed him to the righteous and the chosen, for he has kept the portion of the righteous, because they have hated and rejected this world of wrongdoing, and hated all its works and its ways in the name of the Lord of Spirits. For in his name they are saved, and he has been the keeper of their life.

**8** In those days the kings of the earth, and the strong who hold the dry land, will be downcast in face because of the works of their hands, for on the day of their anguish and distress they will not save their souls.

**9** I will give them into the hands of My chosen ones. Like straw in the fire they will burn before the righteous, and like lead in water they will sink before the holy, and no trace of them will be found.

**10** On the day of their distress there will be fear on the earth. They will fall before him and not rise, and there will be no one to take them by the hand and raise them, because they have denied the Lord of Spirits and His Anointed. Blessed be the name of the Lord of Spirits.

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "his name was named… before the sun"** — Psalm 72:17 says of the king: *May his name endure forever; before the sun may his name flourish*. The Parables make it a statement about time: the Son of Man was named before the sun was made. Peter says of Christ that he was *foreknown before the foundation of the world, but revealed in the last times* (1 Peter 1:20).
- **v4 "the light of the nations"** — the servant's calling in Isaiah 49:6, *I will make you a **light of the nations**, that My salvation may reach to the end of the earth*. Simeon says it over the infant Jesus: *a light for revelation to the Gentiles* (Luke 2:32).
- **v8 "the kings of the earth"** — and in v10, *the Lord of Spirits and His Anointed*. Both phrases are from Psalm 2:2: *the **kings of the earth** set themselves… against the LORD and against **His anointed***. The kings of the Parables are the kings of the psalm.
- **v10 "His Anointed"** — *masiḥu*, **His Messiah**: the first time the title appears in the book. It comes once more, at 52:4.

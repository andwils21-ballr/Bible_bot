# 1 Enoch 34

**1** From there I went north, to the ends of the earth, and there at the ends of the whole earth I saw a great and glorious wonder.

**2** There I saw three gates of heaven open in the sky. Through each of them winds come out toward the north, and when they blow there is cold, hail, frost, snow, dew and rain.

**3** From one gate the wind blows gently, but when it blows through the other two, it is with violence and hardship, and it comes on the earth, and they blow with violence.

---

## Notes

- **v2 "three gates… winds come out"** — the weather has doors. Job asks the same of the north: *Have you entered the storehouses of the snow, or seen the storehouses of the hail?* (Job 38:22). Enoch is shown them.
- **v2 "cold, hail, frost, snow, dew and rain"** — six things, all from the north, and none of them described as punishment. The winds come out when they come out; the chapter is a survey, not a judgment.
- **v3 "when it blows through the other two"** — the Ge'ez says *with violence* twice and runs its clauses together. The sense is clear, one gate gentle and two harsh, but the sentence is rough in the only witness that has it.

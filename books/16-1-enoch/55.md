# 1 Enoch 55

**1** After this the Head of Days repented and said, "In vain have I destroyed all who live on the dry land."

**2** He swore by His great name: "From now on I will not do this to all who live on the dry land. I will set a sign in the heavens, and it will be a pledge of faith between Me and them forever, as long as the days of heaven are over the earth.

**3** After this, it is by My command, when I choose to seize them by the hands of the angels on the day of distress and pain. Before this, My wrath and My punishment will rest on them," says the Lord, the Lord of Spirits.

**4** "You mighty kings who live on the dry land, you will have to see My Chosen One when he sits on the throne of his glory and judges Azazel and all his company and all his hosts in the name of the Lord of Spirits."

## Notes

- **v1 "the Head of Days repented"** — *nassəḥa*, the verb used of the others who *repent* at 50:2 and 50:4. In Genesis the sorrow comes before the flood, *The LORD was sorry that He had made humankind in the earth* (Genesis 6:6); here it comes after, *In vain have I destroyed*. *In vain* is *bak*, the word of 49:4, *an idle word*. It returns at 56:7, where the destruction of the kings *will not be in vain*.
- **v2 "I will set a sign in the heavens"** — the bow of Genesis 9:13: *My bow I have set in the cloud, and it shall be for a sign of covenant between Me and the earth*. God's promise there is *I will not go on striking every living thing again, as I have done* (Genesis 8:21).
- **v2 "a pledge of faith"** — *hāymānot*, the word for **faith** in *of righteousness and of faith* (39:6). Here it names God's own faithfulness, pledged between Him and those who live on the dry land.
- **v2 "as long as the days of heaven are over the earth"** — the phrase of Deuteronomy 11:21, *as the days of the heavens above the earth*, said there of Israel's years in the land.
- **v3 "it is by My command… by the hands of the angels"** — the oath does not end judgment; it changes the way it comes: by command, through the angels, not by water. The rest of the verse is compressed in the Ge'ez. The OCP's English of the Ge'ez keeps *before this*; Charles has *because of this*. Knibb's Ge'ez repeats *My punishment and My wrath* at the end; the EOTC has them once.
- **v4 "the throne of his glory"** — the EOTC, *ba-manbara səbḥatihu*. Knibb's Ge'ez has *səbḥatəya*, **My glory**. In the EOTC the throne is the Chosen One's own, as in Matthew: *the Son of Man… will sit on the throne of His glory* (Matthew 25:31).
- **v4 "judges Azazel"** — the sentence of chapter 10 kept Azazel bound in the dark until *the day of the great judgment* (10:6). The one who judges him on that day is the Chosen One.

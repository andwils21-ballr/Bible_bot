# 1 Enoch 93

**1** After this Enoch began to speak from the books.

**2** Enoch said, "About the children of righteousness, and about the chosen of the world, and about the plant of righteousness and uprightness, I tell you these things and make them known to you, my children, I, Enoch: what was shown to me from the vision of heaven, and what I learned from the word of the holy angels, and understood from the tablets of heaven."

**3** Then Enoch began to speak from the books and said, "I was born the seventh in the first week, while judgment and righteousness still waited.

**4** After me, in the second week, great wickedness will arise, and deceit will have sprung up. In it will be the first end, and in it a man will be saved. After it is ended, wrongdoing will grow, and a law will be made for the sinners.

**5** After this, in the third week, at its end, a man will be chosen as the plant of righteous judgment, and after him will come the plant of righteousness and uprightness forever.

**6** After this, in the fourth week, at its end, visions of the holy and righteous will be seen, and a law for all generations, and an enclosure will be made for them.

**7** After this, in the fifth week, at its end, a house of glory and of kingdom will be built forever.

**8** After this, in the sixth week, all who are in it will be blinded, and the hearts of all of them will forget wisdom. In it a man will go up, and at its end the house of the kingdom will be burned with fire, and in it the whole race of the chosen root will be scattered.

**9** After this, in the seventh week, a rebellious generation will rise, and its deeds will be many, and all its deeds will be rebellion.

**10** At its end the chosen of righteousness will be chosen from the eternal plant of righteousness, to whom will be given sevenfold instruction for all His creation.

**11** For who is there of the sons of men who can hear the voice of the Holy One and not be shaken? Who can think His thoughts? Who can look at the heaven and all its works?

**12** What is there that can understand the work of heaven, and see a soul or a spirit and be able to tell of it, or go up and see all their ends and consider them or do as they do?

**13** Who is there of all men who can know how great is the breadth and the length of the earth, and to whom has the measure of all of them been shown?

**14** Is there any man who can know the length of heaven, how high it is, and on what it is set, and how great the number of the stars is, and where all the lights rest?

## Notes

- **vv3–10 the first seven weeks** — the *Apocalypse of Weeks*, whose last three weeks came earlier, at 91:12–17. Read against scripture, each week ends at a turning point: Enoch, *the seventh* from Adam (Jude 14); in the second week the flood, *the first end*, and a man saved, Noah; in the third, a man chosen, Abraham; in the fourth, the law and *an enclosure*, Sinai and the tabernacle; in the fifth, *a house of glory*, the temple; in the sixth, *a man will go up*, Elijah (2 Kings 2:11), and the house burned and the people scattered, the fall of Jerusalem (2 Kings 25:9); in the seventh, a rebellious generation, and at its end the chosen given *sevenfold instruction*.
- **v3 "I was born the seventh in the first week"** — Jude calls him *Enoch, the seventh from Adam* (Jude 14).
- **vv11–14 "who can…?"** — the questions God puts to Job: *Where were you when I laid the foundation of the earth?… Who set its measurements?* (Job 38:4–5). Enoch, who has seen these things, asks who else can.

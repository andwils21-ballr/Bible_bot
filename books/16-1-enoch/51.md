# 1 Enoch 51

**1** In those days the earth will give back what was entrusted to it, and Sheol will give back what was entrusted to it, what it received, and destruction will pay back what it owes.

**2** He will choose the righteous and the holy from among them, for the day has drawn near for them to be saved.

**3** In those days the Chosen One will sit on his throne, and he will bring out all the counsels of wisdom from the counsel of his mouth, for the Lord of Spirits has given them to him and glorified him.

**4** In those days the mountains will leap like rams, and the hills will skip like lambs filled with milk, and all will become angels in heaven.

**5** Their faces will shine with joy, for in those days the Chosen One will arise. The earth will rejoice, and the righteous will live on it, and the chosen will walk and go about on it.

## Notes

- **v1 "the earth will give back what was entrusted to it"** — *maḥḍantā*, **its deposit**: the dead are held in trust, and the trust is returned. Isaiah says it of the earth: *the earth will cast out the dead* (Isaiah 26:19). Revelation makes the same list of places: *the sea gave up the dead who were in it, and Death and Hades gave up the dead who were in them* (Revelation 20:13). *Sheol* and *destruction* stand side by side as in Proverbs 15:11, *Sheol and Abaddon are before the LORD*, where *Abaddon* means **destruction**.
- **v2 "He will choose"** — the verse does not name him. The verb is *yaḫerri*, used of the Chosen One at 45:3, and he is named in v3 and v5. Charles moves the first line of v5, *the Chosen One will arise*, up to v1 to give the verb a subject; both Ge'ez witnesses have it in v5.
- **v3 "all the counsels of wisdom from the counsel of his mouth"** — the EOTC uses one word twice, *ḫəllināta ṭəbab… ḫəllina ʾafuhu*. Knibb's Ge'ez has *all the secrets of wisdom* and has them *go out* on their own; in the EOTC the Chosen One *brings them out*.
- **v4 "the mountains will leap like rams"** — Psalm 114:4, *the mountains skipped like rams, the hills like lambs*, said of the exodus.
- **v4 "all will become angels in heaven"** — *wa-yəkawwənu kʷəllomu malāʾəkt ba-samāy*, **and they will all be angels in heaven**. In the EOTC the verse ends there, and v5 opens with *and their faces will shine*. Knibb's Ge'ez has the same words run on without the *and*, and both English renderings read them as one clause: *the faces of all the angels in heaven will shine with joy*. Read as the EOTC divides it, the raised righteous of v2 become angels. Jesus says of those who rise from the dead: *they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels in the heavens* (Mark 12:25); in Luke they are *equal to angels… being sons of the resurrection* (Luke 20:36).

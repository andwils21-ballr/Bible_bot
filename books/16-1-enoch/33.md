# 1 Enoch 33

**1** From there I went to the ends of the earth, and there I saw great beasts, each different from the other, and the birds too differed in their form and their beauty, and their voices differed one from another.

**2** East of these beasts I saw the ends of the earth, where heaven rests, and the gates of heaven were open.

**3** I saw how the stars of heaven come out, and I counted the gates they come out of, and wrote down all their exits, each one by its number, and their names in their order, their positions, their times and their months, as the angel Uriel who was with me showed me.

**4** He showed me everything and wrote it down for me. He also wrote down their names for me, and their laws and their tasks.

---

## Notes

- **The whole chapter** — from here to the end of chapter 36, 1 Enoch survives only in the Ge'ez. The Greek manuscript of these chapters stops at 32:6.
- **v2 "where heaven rests"** — Ge'ez *yāʿrəf*, **rests, lies down**. The sky is pictured as a dome whose edge sits on the rim of the earth, as in 18:5, where Enoch saw *at the edge of the earth the firmament of heaven above*.
- **v3 "as the angel Uriel… showed me"** — this is the subject of the book's next major part, the *Book of the Luminaries* (chapters 72–82), where Uriel shows Enoch the course of every light in the sky. Chapters 33 to 36 are a short preview of it.
- **v4 "He… wrote it down for me"** — here the angel writes. Elsewhere Enoch is *the scribe of righteousness* (12:4, 15:1). In v3 Enoch writes; in v4 Uriel does. The Ge'ez keeps both.

# 1 Enoch 86

**1** Again I saw with my eyes as I slept, and I saw the heaven above, and here — one star fell from heaven, and it rose and ate and was seen among those bulls.

**2** After this I saw the great bulls and the black ones, and here — they all changed their stalls and their pastures and their calves, and began to wail one with another.

**3** Again I saw in the vision and looked at the heaven, and here — I saw many stars come down and throw themselves from heaven to that first star, and among those calves and bulls they were with them, seen among them.

**4** I looked at them, and here — they all brought out their members like horses and began to mount the heifers of the bulls, and all of them conceived and bore elephants and camels and donkeys.

**5** All the bulls were afraid of them and were terrified by them, and began to bite with their teeth and devour, and to gore with their horns.

**6** Then they began to devour those bulls, and here — all the children of the earth began to tremble and shake before them and to flee.

## Notes

- **v1 "one star fell from heaven"** — in the dream the fallen angels are stars, as in Revelation: *I saw a star fallen from heaven to the earth* (Revelation 9:1). The first star to fall is the leader of the Watchers of chapters 6–10; the many who follow are the rest (v3).
- **v2 "began to wail one with another"** — *yaʿawwəyəwu*, the EOTC. Charles has *began to live with each other*.
- **vv4–6 "elephants and camels and donkeys"** — the giants of chapters 7–10, born to the Watchers and the daughters of men; *the Nephilim were in the earth in those days* (Genesis 6:4). They devour the bulls, as the giants devoured men at 7:4.
- **vv1–2** — scraps of the Greek survive, a few words each.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. Greek: Online Critical Pseudepigrapha, CC BY 4.0. English translation by this project.

# 1 Enoch 98

**1** And now I swear to you, the wise and the foolish, that you will see many things on the earth.

**2** For you men will put on more beauty than women, and more color than a virgin, in kingship and in greatness and in authority, and in silver and gold and purple, and in honor and in food; like water it flows.

**3** Because of this they have no teaching and no wisdom, and through it they will perish, together with their possessions and with all their glory and their honor. In reproach and in want, in slaughter and in great want, their spirit will be thrown into the furnace of fire.

**4** I have sworn to you, sinners: as a mountain has not become a slave, and a hill will not become a woman's servant, so sin was not sent on the earth, but men created it of themselves, and those who commit it will come under a great curse.

**5** Barrenness was not given to the woman, but because of the work of her own hands she dies without children.

**6** I have sworn to you, sinners, by the Holy and Great One, that all your evil work is revealed in the heavens, and you have no work of violence that is covered and hidden.

**7** Do not think in your spirit or say in your heart that you do not know and do not see that all your sin is written in heaven every day before the Most High.

**8** From now on, know that all your violence that you do is written down every day until the day of your judgment.

**9** Woe to you, fools, for you will perish through your folly. You do not listen to the wise, and good will not come to you.

**10** And now know that you are made ready for the day of destruction. Do not hope to live, sinners; you will go and die, for you know no ransom, since you are made ready for the day of the great judgment, and for the day of distress and great shame for your spirit.

**11** Woe to you, hard of heart, who do evil and eat blood! From where do you get good things to eat, and eat and drink and are filled? It is all the good that our Lord the Most High has given in abundance on the earth, and you will have no peace.

**12** Woe to you who love the work of wrongdoing! Why do you hope for good? Know that you will be handed over into the hands of the righteous, and they will cut off your heads and kill you, and have no mercy on you.

**13** Woe to you who rejoice in the distress of the righteous, for no grave will be dug for you.

**14** Woe to you who set aside the words of the righteous, for you will have no hope of life.

**15** Woe to you who write words of lies and words of the wicked, for they write their lies so that people may hear them and act wickedly in folly. They will have no peace, but will surely die, and die quickly.

## Notes

- **v1 "the wise and the foolish"** — the Greek has *to the wise, and not to the foolish*, and *you will see many lawless deeds on the earth* where the EOTC has *many things*.
- **v2 "you men will put on more beauty than women"** — the Greek adds that silver and gold will be *for food* among them, and *in their houses they will be poured out like water*.
- **v3 "the furnace of fire"** — Jesus in the parable of the weeds: the angels *will throw them into the furnace of fire; there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth* (Matthew 13:42).
- **v4 "sin was not sent on the earth, but men created it of themselves"** — the Epistle lays the blame on men, where the Book of the Watchers laid much of it on the X. James: *each one is tempted when he is drawn away and enticed by his own desire. Then desire, when it has conceived, gives birth to sin* (James 1:14–15). Where the EOTC has a mountain and a hill, the Greek is broken.
- **v5 "Barrenness was not given to the woman"** — the Greek verse is longer. It says that slavery was not given from above but came about through oppression, that lawlessness came from transgression, and that a barren woman is punished with childlessness for her own wrongs.
- **v6 "you have no work of violence that is covered and hidden"** — Jesus: *There is nothing covered that will not be revealed, and nothing hidden that will not be known* (Luke 12:2).
- **v7 "that you do not know and do not see"** — so the EOTC; the Greek has *that they do not know or see*: the sinners suppose that no one in heaven notices. The psalm: *they say, "The LORD does not see, and the God of Jacob does not understand"* (Psalm 94:7).
- **v10 "you know no ransom"** — *bēzā*; Charles, *ransom*. The psalm: *No man can by any means redeem his brother, or give to God a ransom for him* (Psalm 49:7).
- **v11 "who… eat blood"** — the command to Noah: *you shall not eat flesh with its life, its blood* (Genesis 9:4). **"hard of heart"** — *gəzufāna ləbb*; the Greek has *stiff-necked in heart*.
- **v12 "they will cut off your heads"** — the Ge'ez says *they will cut your necks*. The righteous carry out the judgment, as at 95:3.
- **v13 "no grave will be dug for you"** — Jeremiah on King Jehoiakim: *He will be buried with the burial of a donkey, dragged off and thrown out beyond the gates of Jerusalem* (Jeremiah 22:19).
- **v15 "so that people may hear them and act wickedly in folly"** — the Ge'ez here is hard, and the English follows the sense Charles gives, *that men may hear them and act godlessly towards (their) neighbour*. The Greek has *they write and will lead many astray with their lies; you yourselves go astray*. Outside the city are *everyone who loves and practices a lie* (Revelation 22:15). The EOTC's v15 holds what Charles and the Greek number vv15–16.

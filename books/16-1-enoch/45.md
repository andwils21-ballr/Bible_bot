# 1 Enoch 45

**1** This is the second parable, concerning those who deny the name of the dwelling of the holy ones and of the Lord of Spirits.

**2** They will not go up to heaven, and they will not come onto the earth. Such will be the portion of the sinners who deny the name of the Lord of Spirits, who are kept like this for the day of suffering and distress.

**3** On that day the Chosen One will sit on the throne of glory and will test their deeds, and their resting places will be without number. Their spirit will grow strong within them when they see My Chosen One, and those who call on My holy and glorious name.

**4** On that day I will make him dwell among them, and I will transform heaven and make it a blessing and a light forever.

**5** I will transform the dry land and make it a blessing, and I will make My chosen ones live on it. But those who commit sin and wrongdoing will not set foot on it.

**6** For I have seen My righteous ones and satisfied them with peace, and I have set them before Me. But My judgment of the sinners has drawn near to Me, so that I may destroy them from the face of the earth.

---

## Notes

- **v3 "the Chosen One will sit on the throne of glory"** — the scene Jesus describes: *when the Son of Man comes in His glory… He will sit on the throne of His glory* (Matthew 25:31), and again in Matthew 19:28.
- **vv4–5 "I will transform heaven… I will transform the dry land"** — the promise of Isaiah 65:17, *I am creating new heavens and a new earth*. The chosen live on the renewed land, as in Psalm 37:29, *the righteous will inherit the land and dwell on it forever*, and in the beatitude, *the meek… will inherit the earth* (Matthew 5:5).

# 1 Enoch 90

**1** I watched until the time when thirty-five shepherds had pastured in this way. They all completed their times as the first had, and others received them into their hands to pasture them in their times, each shepherd in his own time.

**2** After this I saw in the vision all the birds of heaven coming: eagles and kites and *hobay* and ravens, and the eagles led all the birds. They began to devour those sheep, to dig out their eyes and devour their flesh.

**3** The sheep cried out because their flesh was devoured by the birds, and I cried out and wailed in my sleep over that shepherd who pastured the sheep.

**4** I watched until the sheep were devoured by the dogs and the eagles and the *hobay*, and they left them no flesh at all, nor skin nor sinew, until only their bones stood; and their bones too fell to the earth, and the sheep became few.

**5** I watched until the time when twenty-three shepherds had pastured, and they completed, each in his time, fifty-eight times.

**6** Little lambs were born to those white sheep, and they began to open their eyes and see, and to cry out to the sheep.

**7** The sheep did not cry out to them and did not listen to what they told them, but were deafer still, and their eyes were blinded more and more.

**8** I saw in the vision how the ravens flew down on those lambs and seized one of those lambs, and dashed the sheep to pieces and devoured them.

**9** I watched until horns came up on those lambs, and the ravens were bringing down their horns. I watched until one great horn sprouted on one of those sheep, and their eyes were opened.

**10** It looked at them, and their eyes were opened, and it cried out to the sheep, and the rams saw it and all ran to it.

**11** With all this, those eagles and kites and ravens and *hobay* kept seizing the sheep and swooping down on them and devouring them. The sheep kept silent, but the rams wailed and cried out.

**12** Those ravens fought and struggled with it and wanted to take away its horn, but they could not overcome it.

**13** I watched them until the shepherds came, and the eagles, those eagles and the *hobay*, and they cried out to the ravens to break the horn of that ram. They struggled with it and fought, and it struggled with them and cried out for its help to come.

**14** I watched until that man came who wrote down the names of the shepherds and carried them up before the Lord of the sheep. He helped it and showed it everything, and its help came down to that ram.

**15** I watched until the Lord of the sheep came to them in wrath, and all who saw Him fled, and they all fell into the shadow before His face.

**16** All the eagles and kites and ravens and *hobay* gathered, and they brought with them all the sheep of the field, and they all came together and helped one another to break that ram's horn.

**17** I watched that man who writes the book at the word of the Lord until he opened that book of the destruction that those last twelve shepherds had done, and showed before the Lord of the sheep that they had destroyed more than those before them.

**18** I watched until the Lord of the sheep came to them and took in His hand the staff of His wrath and struck the earth. The earth split open, and all the beasts and the birds of heaven fell away from those sheep and sank into the earth, and it closed over them.

**19** I watched until a great sword was given to the sheep, and the sheep went out against those wild beasts to kill them, and all the beasts and the birds of heaven fled from their face.

**20** I watched until a throne was built in the pleasant land, and the Lord of the sheep sat on it, and He took all the sealed books and opened those books before the Lord of the sheep.

**21** The Lord called those seven first white ones and commanded them to bring before Him, beginning with the first star that went before those stars whose members were like the members of horses, and the first star that fell first, and they brought them all before Him.

**22** He said to that man who writes before Him, who is one of the seven white ones, "Take these seventy shepherds to whom I handed over the sheep, and who, when they took them, killed many more than I commanded them."

**23** And here — I saw them all bound, and they all stood before Him.

**24** The judgment was held first on the stars. They were judged and found guilty and went to the place of judgment, and they threw them into a deep place, full of fire that flames and full of pillars of fire.

**25** Those seventy shepherds were judged and found guilty, and they too were thrown into that deep place of fire.

**26** At that time I saw a deep place like it opened in the middle of the earth, full of fire, and they brought those blinded sheep, and they were all judged and found guilty and thrown into that fiery deep, and they burned. That deep place was to the right of that house.

**27** I saw those sheep burning, and their bones burning.

**28** I stood watching until He folded up that old house. They took out all the pillars, and all its beams and ornaments were folded up with it, and they took it out and put it in a place to the right of the land.

**29** I watched until the Lord of the sheep brought a new house, greater and higher than that first one, and set it up in the place of the first, which had been folded up. All its pillars were new, and its ornaments new and greater than those of the first, the old one He had taken out, and all the sheep were inside it.

**30** I saw all the sheep that were left, and all the animals on the earth and all the birds of heaven, falling down and bowing to those sheep, pleading with them and obeying them in every word.

**31** After that those three who were clothed in white, who had lifted me up before, took me by my hand, with the hand of that ram holding me, and lifted me up and set me down among those sheep, before the judgment took place.

**32** Those sheep were all white, and their wool thick and clean.

**33** All that had been destroyed and scattered, and all the wild beasts and all the birds of heaven, gathered in that house, and the Lord of the sheep rejoiced with great joy, because they had all become good and had come back to His house.

**34** I watched until they laid down that sword that had been given to the sheep and brought it back into His house, and it was sealed before the face of the Lord. All the sheep were shut in that house, and it could not hold them.

**35** The eyes of all of them were opened, and they saw what is good, and there was not one among them who did not see.

**36** I saw that that house was great and wide and very full.

**37** I saw that a white bull was born, and its horns were great, and all the wild beasts and the birds of heaven feared it and pleaded with it all the time.

**38** I watched until all their kinds were changed, and they all became white bulls. The first among them became a word, and that word became a great beast with great black horns on its head, and the Lord of the sheep rejoiced over them and over all the bulls.

**39** I slept among them, and I woke and saw everything.

**40** This is the vision I saw while I slept. I woke and blessed the Lord of righteousness and gave Him glory.

**41** Then I wept with great weeping, and my tears did not stop until I could not bear it, when I saw what would come down on what I had seen; for everything will come and be fulfilled, and all the deeds of men, each in its part, were shown to me.

**42** That night I remembered my first dream, and because of it I wept and was troubled, because I had seen that vision.

## Notes

- **v1 "thirty-five shepherds"** — the EOTC has *thirty-seven*. The seventy shepherds of 89:59 are counted in parts: thirty-five here, twenty-three in v5 (the two together *fifty-eight*, as v5 says), and the *last twelve* of v17, seventy in all. With thirty-seven the count comes to seventy-two, so the English follows the count the chapter itself requires, which Charles also has (see choice #1 in the 72 report).
- **vv6–16 the lambs and the great horn** — after the return, lambs are born who open their eyes, and a great horn rises among them, fought by all the birds and helped from heaven. The dream now reaches the writer's own times; the notes do not name who is meant.
- **v20 "a throne was built in the pleasant land… and He opened those books"** — Daniel's court: *thrones were set… the court sat, and the books were opened* (Daniel 7:9–10).
- **vv24–27 the fiery deep** — the stars first, then the seventy shepherds, then the blinded sheep. *To the right of that house*: the place of burning stands beside the city, as at the end of Isaiah, where they *go out and look on the corpses of the men who rebelled against Me* (Isaiah 66:24).
- **vv28–29 "that old house… a new house, greater and higher"** — the house is folded up like a tent and carried off, and a new one set in its place. John sees *the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God* (Revelation 21:2).
- **v31 "set me down among those sheep, before the judgment took place"** — Enoch is placed among the flock at the end.
- **v37 "a white bull was born"** — the story began with a white bull from the earth (85:3), and the white bulls ran from Adam to Isaac (89:11). Here one is born again, and all the kinds become white bulls after him (v38).
- **v38 "The first among them became a word, and that word became a great beast"** — the EOTC, *kona… nagara, wa-wəʾətu nagar kona ʾarwē ʿabiya*: *nagar* is **word**. Charles, judging the text corrupt, prints *a lamb*. John writes of *the Word* that *became flesh* (John 1:14). The EOTC's reading is given as it stands.
- **v42 "I remembered my first dream"** — the flood of 83:3–5, which this second dream ended by passing far beyond.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

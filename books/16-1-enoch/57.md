# 1 Enoch 57

**1** And it came to pass after this that I saw another host of chariots, with men riding in them, and they came on the winds from the east and from the west until midday.

**2** The sound of their chariots was heard. When this uproar took place, the holy ones in heaven took notice of it, and the pillar of the earth was shaken from its place, and the sound was heard from one end of heaven to the other in one day.

**3** They will all fall down and worship the Lord of Spirits. This is the end of the second parable.

---

## Notes

- **v2 "from one end of heaven to the other"** — the EOTC, *ʾəm-ʾaṣnāfa samāy ʾəska ʾaṣnāfa samāy*; Knibb's Ge'ez has *from the ends of the earth to the ends of heaven*. Jesus uses the EOTC's phrase of the last gathering: the angels *will gather His chosen from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other* (Matthew 24:31).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. Knibb's Ge'ez and its English: Online Critical Pseudepigrapha, CC BY 4.0. English translation by this project.

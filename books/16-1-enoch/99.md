# 1 Enoch 99

**1** Woe to you who do godless deeds, who praise and honor the word of lies! You have perished, and you have no life and no good.

**2** Woe to you who twist the words of uprightness and transgress the eternal law, and count themselves not sinners! On the earth they will be trodden down.

**3** In those days make ready, you righteous, to raise your prayers as a reminder, and set them as a witness before the angels, so that they may set the sin of the sinners before the Most High as a reminder.

**4** In those days the nations will be thrown into confusion, and the families of the nations will rise up on the day of destruction.

**5** In those days those in want will go out and carry off their children and abandon them, and their children will fall from them. They will abandon them while they are still nursing, and will not return to them, and will have no mercy on their loved ones.

**6** Again I swear to you, sinners, that sin is made ready for a day of blood that does not cease.

**7** They will worship stone, and those who carve images of gold and silver and wood and clay, and those who worship unclean spirits and evil demons and all kinds of idols, will find no help from them.

**8** They will become godless through the folly of their heart, and their eyes will be darkened through the fear of their heart and through the visions of their dreams.

**9** Through these they will become godless and afraid, because they did all their work in a lie and worshiped stone, and they will perish in an instant.

**10** In those days blessed are all who accept the words of wisdom and know them, and keep the paths of the Most High, and walk in the path of righteousness, and do not become godless with the godless, for they will be saved.

**11** Woe to you who spread evil to your neighbor, for you will be killed in Sheol.

**12** Woe to you who lay the foundation of sin and deceit, and who cause bitterness on the earth, for through it they will be brought to an end.

**13** Woe to you who build your houses with the toil of others, when all their building is of bricks and stones of sin! I tell you, you will have no peace.

**14** Woe to those who reject the measure and the eternal inheritance of their fathers, and make their souls follow after idols, for they will have no rest.

**15** Woe to you who do wrongdoing and help violence, and kill your neighbors, until the day of the great judgment.

**16** He will cast down your glory, and put evil into your heart, and rouse the spirit of His anger to destroy you all with the sword, and all the righteous and holy will remember your sins.

# 1 Enoch 66

**1** After this he showed me the angels of punishment, who are ready to come and let loose all the power of the water beneath the earth, so that it may bring judgment and destruction on all who stay and live on the dry land.

**2** The Lord of Spirits commanded the angels who were going out not to raise their hands but to keep watch, for those angels were over the power of the waters.

**3** Then I went out from the presence of Enoch.

---

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "ready to come and let loose… not to raise their hands but to keep watch"** — the flood is ready and held back at the same time: the angels over the waters wait for the command. In 54:7–8 the same waters were seen let loose.

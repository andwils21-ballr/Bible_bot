# 1 Enoch 100

**1** In those days, in one place, fathers will be struck down together with their children, and brothers will fall in death with their companions, until their blood flows like a river.

**2** For a man will not hold back his hand from his children, or from his children's children, even the beloved, but will kill them; and the sinner will not hold back his hand from his honored brother. From dawn until the sun sets they will kill one another.

**3** The horse will walk up to its breast in the blood of the sinners, and the chariot will sink to its top.

**4** In those days the angels will go down into the hidden places and gather into one place all who helped sin. The Most High will rise on that day to carry out a great judgment on all the sinners.

**5** Over all the righteous and holy He will set guardians from the holy angels, and they will guard them like the apple of the eye until all evil and all sin come to an end. Even if the righteous sleep a long sleep, they have nothing to fear.

**6** The wise among men will see truly, and the children of the earth will understand all the words of this book, and know that their riches cannot save them when their sins fall.

**7** Woe to you, sinners, when you afflict the righteous on the day of hard distress and burn them with fire: you will be repaid according to your deeds.

**8** Woe to you, stubborn of heart, who stay awake to think out evil: fear will overtake you, and there will be no one to help you.

**9** Woe to you, sinners! Because of the words of your mouth and the deeds of your hands, you who have done godless deeds, you will burn in the burning of blazing fire.

**10** And now know that your deeds will be searched out in heaven from the angels, from the sun and from the moon and from the stars, because of your sins, for on the earth you pass judgment on the righteous.

**11** He will call every cloud and mist and dew and rain to witness against you, for all of them will be held back from you so that they do not come down on you, and they will not be mindful of you because of your sins.

**12** And now give gifts to the rain, so that it is not held back from coming down on you, and to the dew, once it has received gold and silver from you, so that it will come down. When the frost and the snow with their cold, and all the winds of the snow, and all their torments fall on you, in those days you will not be able to stand before them.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "fathers… with their children… they will kill one another"** — Jesus: *Brother will hand over brother to death, and a father his child, and children will rise up against parents and have them put to death* (Mark 13:12). **"even the beloved"** — *məḥira*; the Greek has *from his son, nor from his beloved*.
- **v3 "The horse will walk up to its breast in the blood"** — Revelation: *blood came out of the winepress, up to the horses' bridles, for a distance of sixteen hundred stadia* (Revelation 14:20). Where the EOTC has *to its top*, the Greek has *to the axles*.
- **v4 "all who helped sin"** — *ʾəlla yāraddəʾəwwā la-ḫaṭiʾat*, so the Greek, *those who aided unrighteousness*. Charles has *all those who brought down sin*.
- **v5 "guard them like the apple of the eye"** — the song of Moses: *He kept him as the apple of His eye* (Deuteronomy 32:10); David: *Keep me as the apple of the eye; hide me in the shadow of Your wings* (Psalm 17:8). *He will command His angels concerning you, to guard you in all your ways* (Psalm 91:11). **"a long sleep"** — the Greek has *a sweet sleep*, as in Proverbs: *when you lie down, your sleep will be sweet* (Proverbs 3:24).
- **v6 "all the words of this book"** — the Greek has *these words of this epistle*. The name *the Epistle of Enoch*, for chapters 91–108, comes from that Greek line. **"The wise among men will see truly"** — so the Greek, *the wise among people will see*; Charles gives *shall see the wise in security*.
- **v8 "who stay awake to think out evil"** — Micah: *Woe to those who plan wickedness and work out evil on their beds! In the morning light they do it* (Micah 2:1).
- **vv11–12 the rain held back** — *Give gifts to the rain* is mockery: the rich, who have bought everything else, cannot buy the rain. **"they will not be mindful of you because of your sins"** — the EOTC has *wa-ʾiyəḫelləyu diba ḫaṭiʾatkəmu*, with *not*; Charles has *they shall be mindful of your sins*, and the Greek line is broken. The EOTC is kept.
- **v12 "you will not be able to stand before them"** — the EOTC's v12 holds what Charles and the Greek number vv12–13.

# 1 Enoch 39

**1** And it shall come to pass in those days that chosen and holy children will come down from the high heavens, and their seed will become one with the sons of men.

**2** In those days Enoch received books of zeal and wrath, and books of haste and turmoil. "There will be no mercy for them," says the Lord of Spirits.

**3** At that time a cloud and a whirlwind snatched me away from the face of the earth and set me down at the end of the heavens.

**4** There I saw another vision: the dwellings of the righteous and the resting places of the holy.

**5** There my eyes saw their dwellings with the angels and their resting places with the holy ones. They were asking and interceding and praying for the sons of men, and righteousness flowed before them like water, and mercy like dew on the earth. So it is among them forever and ever.

**6** In those days my eyes saw the place of the chosen, of righteousness and of faith. Righteousness will be in their days, and the righteous and the chosen will be without number before Him forever and ever.

**7** I saw their dwelling under the wings of the Lord of Spirits. All the righteous and the chosen before Him shine like the light of fire. Their mouths are full of blessing, and their lips praise the name of the Lord of Spirits. Righteousness before Him never fails, and uprightness before Him never fails.

**8** There I wanted to live, and my soul longed for that dwelling. My portion has been there from before, for so it has been established for me before the Lord of Spirits.

**9** In those days I praised and exalted the name of the Lord of Spirits with blessing and praise, because He has established me in blessing and praise, according to the good pleasure of the Lord of Spirits.

**10** For a long time my eyes looked at that place, and I blessed Him and praised Him, saying, "Blessed is He, and may He be blessed from before the world and forever.

**11** Before Him there is no end. He knows, before the world was created, what the world is, and what will be from generation to generation.

**12** Those who do not sleep bless You. They stand before Your glory and bless and praise and exalt, saying, 'Holy, holy, holy, Lord of Spirits; He fills the earth with spirits.'"

**13** There my eyes saw all those who do not sleep. They stand before Him and bless and say, "Blessed are You, and blessed is the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever."

**14** My face was changed, because I could not look.

## Notes

- **v1 "chosen and holy children will come down from the high heavens"** — the words of the Watchers' story told again: at 6:2 *the sons of heaven* saw the daughters of men, and here *children* come down *from the high heavens* and *their seed will become one with the sons of men*. But these children are called **chosen and holy**, the words the Parables use for the righteous. The verse does not say whether it looks back to the fall of chapter 6 or forward to a holy descent, and the chapter goes straight on to Enoch.
- **v12 "Holy, holy, holy, Lord of Spirits; He fills the earth with spirits"** — the song of the seraphim in Isaiah 6:3, *Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of hosts; the whole earth is full of His glory*, with both halves changed: *hosts* becomes **spirits**, and *glory* becomes **spirits**. This is where the Parables' name for God comes from. The Lord of Spirits is the LORD of hosts, and the hosts are the spirits who fill heaven and earth.

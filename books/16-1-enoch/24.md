# 1 Enoch 24

**1** From there I went to another place of the earth, and he showed me a mountain of fire that burned day and night.

**2** I went toward it and saw seven magnificent mountains, each different from the others, with precious and beautiful stones, all of them splendid and glorious in appearance and beautiful in form — three toward the east, set one upon another, and three toward the south, one upon another, and deep and rugged ravines, none of them running into another.

**3** The seventh mountain was in the middle of these, and it rose above them, like the seat of a throne, and fragrant trees surrounded it.

**4** Among them was a tree such as I had never smelled — no other among them or any other tree was like it. It had a fragrance beyond all spices, and its leaves and its blossom and its wood never wither, forever. Its fruit is beautiful, and its fruit is like the clusters of a date palm.

**5** Then I said, "Look at this beautiful tree — beautiful to look at, and lovely are its leaves, and its fruit is a delight to the eye."

**6** Then Michael answered me, one of the holy and honored angels who was with me, and who was set over them.

---

## Notes

- **v4 "a tree such as I had never smelled"** — the first thing said about it is its smell, and the second is that nothing is like it. The book is describing the tree of life and will not name it; that comes in the next chapter. Here it is only a tree nobody may touch, which is what it was in Genesis 3:22.

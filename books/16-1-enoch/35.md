# 1 Enoch 35

**1** From there I went west, to the ends of the earth, and there I saw three gates open, just as I had seen in the east, the same number of gates and the same number of exits.

---

## Notes

- **The whole chapter** — one verse, and only in the Ge'ez. It completes the four-sided survey: east (33), north (34), west (35), south (36).
- **v1 "just as I had seen in the east"** — the east gates of 33:2–3, where the stars come out. The west has the same number, and the stars that rise through one side set through the other (36:3).
- **v1 "the same number of exits"** — Ge'ez *muḍāʾāt*, **goings out**, the word of 33:3, *I wrote down all their exits*. The numbers themselves are not given until chapters 72–82.

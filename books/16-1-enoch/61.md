# 1 Enoch 61

**1** In those days I saw long cords given to those angels. They took wings for themselves and flew, and went toward the north.

**2** I asked the angel, "Why have these taken the long cords and gone?"

**3** The angel who was with me said to me, "They are going to take the measurements of the righteous and bring them, so that the righteous may lean on the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever.

**4** The chosen will begin to live with the chosen. These are the measurements that will be given to faith, and they will strengthen the word of righteousness.

**5** These measurements will reveal all the secrets of the depths of the earth, and those who were destroyed by the wilderness, and those who were devoured by the fish of the sea and by the wild beasts, so that they may be brought back and may lean on the day of the Chosen One. For no one will be destroyed before the Lord of Spirits, and no one can be destroyed."

**6** All who are above the heavens received a command, and power, and one voice and one light like fire.

**7** With their first word they bless Him, and exalt Him and praise Him with wisdom, and they show wisdom in speech and in the spirit of life.

**8** The Lord of Spirits seated the Chosen One on the throne of His glory, and he will judge all the works of the holy ones above the heavens, and the deeds of every one of them will be weighed in the balance.

**9** When he lifts up his face to judge their hidden ways by the word of the name of the Lord of Spirits, and their paths by the way of the righteous judgment of the Lord Most High, they will all speak with one voice, and bless and praise and exalt and glorify the name of the Lord of Spirits.

**10** He will call all the host of the heavens, and all the holy ones above, and the host of the Lord — the cherubim, the seraphim and the ophanim — and all the angels of power, and all the angels of the dominions, and the Chosen One, and the other host that is on the dry land and over the water.

**11** On that day they will raise one voice, and bless and praise and glorify and exalt in the spirit of faith, in the spirit of wisdom and in patience, in the spirit of mercy, in the spirit of judgment and peace, and in the spirit of goodness. They will all say with one voice, "Blessed is He, and blessed be the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever."

**12** All the holy ones who do not sleep above the heaven will bless Him, and all the chosen who live in the garden of life, and every spirit of light that is able to bless and praise and exalt and hallow Your holy name, and all flesh will praise and bless Your name beyond measure, forever and ever.

**13** For great is the mercy of the Lord of Spirits, and He is slow to anger. All His work and all His power, all that He has made, He has revealed to the righteous and the chosen in the name of the Lord of Spirits.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "long cords… to take the measurements of the righteous"** — Zechariah sees *a man with a measuring line in his hand*, going *to measure Jerusalem* (Zechariah 2:1–2). In Revelation John is told, *Rise and measure the temple of God and the altar, and those who worship in it* (Revelation 11:1). Here what is measured is the righteous themselves.
- **v10 "the cherubim, the seraphim and the ophanim"** — *Kirubēl, Surāfēl* and *ʾAfnin*. The *ophanim* are the **wheels** of Ezekiel's vision, *ʾofannim*, one beside each cherub (Ezekiel 10:9). The Chosen One is named in the same list, among those called to praise.

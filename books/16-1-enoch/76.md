# 1 Enoch 76

**1** At the ends of the earth I saw twelve gates open to all the winds, from which the winds go out and blow over the earth.

**2** Three of them are open on the face of heaven, three in the west, three on the right of heaven, and three on the left.

**3** The first three are those toward the east, three are toward the north, three behind, on the left, toward the south, and three in the west.

**4** Through four of them come winds of blessing and peace, and from those eight come winds of punishment. When they are sent, they destroy the whole earth and the water on it, and all who live on it, and everything in the water and on the dry land.

**5** The first wind from those gates, called the east wind, comes out through the first gate toward the east, which leans toward the south. From it come desolation, drought, heat and destruction.

**6** Through the second gate, the middle one, comes what is right, and from it come rain and fruit, peace and dew. Through the third gate, toward the north, come cold and drought.

**7** After these the winds toward the south come out through three gates. Through the first of them, which leans toward the east, comes a hot wind.

**8** Through the gate next to it, the middle one, come fragrance, dew and rain, peace and life.

**9** Through the third gate, toward the west, come dew and rain, locusts and destruction.

**10** After these the winds toward the north, whose name is Sea. From the seventh gate, toward the east, which leans toward the south, come dew and rain, locusts and destruction.

**11** From the middle gate, the straight one, come rain and dew, life and peace. Through the third gate, toward the west, which leans toward the north, come mist and frost, snow and rain, dew and locusts.

**12** After these, the fourth: the winds toward the west. Through the first gate, which leans toward the north, come dew and rain, frost and cold, snow and Dadak.

**13** From the middle gate come dew and rain, peace and blessing. Through the last gate, toward the south, come drought and desolation, burning and destruction.

**14** So the twelve gates of the four quarters of heaven are complete. All their laws, all their punishments and all their peace I have shown you, my son Methuselah.

## Notes

- **v4 "four of them… winds of blessing and peace, and… eight… winds of punishment"** — in each quarter the middle gate brings the good winds (vv6, 8, 11, 13), and the two gates on either side bring the harmful ones. The count of four and eight holds.
- **v10 "whose name is Sea"** — *za-səmu bāḥr*, in the EOTC; Charles does not have it.
- **v12 "snow and Dadak"** — *Dadak*, the name given at 60:18 to what rises from the snow like smoke. Charles gives *frost*.
- **v14 "my son Methuselah"** — the first time the Astronomical Book names the one it is written for. *Enoch lived sixty-five years, and he fathered Methuselah* (Genesis 5:21).

# 1 Enoch 73

**1** After this law I saw another law, for the lesser light, whose name is the moon.

**2** Its circle is like the circle of heaven, and the wind drives the chariot in which it rides, and light is given to it by measure.

**3** Every month its rising and its setting change, and its days are like the days of the sun. When its light is full, its light is a seventh part of the light of the sun.

**4** This is how it rises. Its beginning, toward the east, comes out every thirty mornings. On that day it appears, and for you it becomes the beginning of the month, thirty mornings, together with the sun in the gate from which the sun comes out.

**5** Half of it stands out by one seventh part, and its whole circle is empty, without light, except for its seventh part, of the fourteen parts of its light.

**6** On the day it receives a seventh part of half its light, its light is one seventh part and a half.

**7** It sets with the sun, and when the sun rises, the moon rises with it and receives half a part of light. In that night, at the beginning of its morning, before the moon's day, the moon sets with the sun, and in that night it is dark in thirteen parts and a half.

**8** On that day it rises with exactly a seventh part, and it comes out and moves away from the rising of the sun, and in the rest of its days it shines seven and seven parts.

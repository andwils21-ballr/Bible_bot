# 1 Enoch 72

**1** The book of the turnings of the lights of heaven, each one as they are, by their classes, each by its authority and its season, each by its name and its places of origin and its months. Uriel the holy angel, who was with me and who is their guide, showed them to me. He showed me their whole record as it is, and how it is for all the years of the world and forever, until the new creation is made that will last forever.

**2** This is the first law of the lights: the light, the sun, rises in the gates of heaven toward the east and sets in the gates of heaven in the west.

**3** I saw six gates from which the sun rises and six gates where the sun sets. The moon rises and sets in those gates, and the leaders of the stars of heaven with those they lead: six in the east and six in the west, all of them in exact order, one after the other, and many windows to the right and left of each gate.

**4** First the great light goes out, whose name is the sun. Its circle is like the circle of heaven, and it is entirely full of fire that gives light and heat.

**5** The wind drives the chariot on which it goes up. The sun sets from heaven and returns by way of the north to go east, and it is guided to enter that gate and shine on the face of heaven.

**6** In this way it rises in the first month in the great gate, which is the fourth of those six gates toward the rising of the sun.

**7** In that fourth gate, from which the sun rises in the first month, there are twelve open windows, from which a flame comes out when they are opened at their time.

**8** When the sun rises in heaven, it comes out through that fourth gate for thirty mornings, and it sets exactly in the fourth gate in the west of heaven.

**9** In those days the day grows longer day by day and the night shorter night by night, until the thirtieth morning.

**10** On that day the day is longer than the night by two parts: the day is exactly ten parts, and the night eight parts.

**11** The sun rises from that fourth gate and sets in the fourth gate, and it returns to the fifth gate of the east for thirty mornings, and rises from it and sets in the fifth gate.

**12** Then the day grows longer by two portions: the day is eleven parts, and the night grows shorter and is seven parts.

**13** The sun returns to the east and enters the sixth gate, and it rises and sets in the sixth gate for thirty-one mornings, because of its sign.

**14** On that day the day grows longer than the night and becomes double the night: the day is twelve parts, and the night is shortened to six parts.

**15** The sun sets out to make the day shorter and the night longer. The sun returns to the east and enters the sixth gate, and rises from it and sets for thirty mornings.

**16** When thirty mornings are completed, the day decreases by exactly one part: the day is eleven parts and the night seven parts.

**17** The sun goes out from the west, from that sixth gate, and goes east, and rises in the fifth gate for thirty mornings, and sets again in the west in the fifth gate of the west.

**18** On that day the day decreases by two parts: the day is ten parts and the night eight parts.

**19** The sun goes out from that fifth gate and sets in the fifth gate of the west, and it rises in the fourth gate for thirty-one mornings, because of its sign, and sets in the west.

**20** On that day the day is equal to the night; they are the same length: the night is nine parts and the day nine parts.

**21** The sun goes out from that gate and sets in the west, and returns to the east, and rises in the third gate for thirty mornings, and sets in the west in the third gate.

**22** On that day the night grows longer night by night for thirty mornings, and the day shorter day by day for thirty days: the night is exactly ten parts and the day eight parts.

**23** The sun goes out from that third gate and sets in the third gate in the west, and returns to the east. The sun rises in the second gate of the east for thirty mornings, and in the same way sets in the second gate in the west of heaven.

**24** On that day the night is eleven parts and the day seven parts.

**25** On that day the sun goes out from that second gate and sets in the west in the second gate, and returns to the east and rises in the first gate for thirty-one mornings, because of its sign, and sets in the west in the first gate.

**26** On that day the night grows longer and becomes double the day: the night is exactly twelve parts and the day six parts.

**27** The sun has completed its turning points, and it goes around again over those turning points, and enters that gate for thirty mornings, and sets in the west opposite it.

**28** On that day the night grows shorter by one portion, that is, one part: the night is eleven parts and the day seven parts.

**29** The sun has returned and entered the second gate in the east, and it goes back over those turning points for thirty mornings, rising and setting.

**30** On that day the night grows shorter: the night is ten parts and the day eight parts.

**31** On that day the sun goes out from that second gate and sets in the west, and returns to the east and rises in the third gate for thirty-one mornings, because of its sign, and sets in the west of heaven.

**32** On that day the night grows shorter and is nine parts, and the day is nine parts, and the night is equal to the day. The year is exactly three hundred and sixty-four days.

**33** The length of the day and of the night, and the shortness of the day and of the night: by the course of the sun they are made different.

**34** Because of it its course grows longer, and day draws near to day, and night to night.

**35** This is the law and the course of the sun, and its return. When it returns, it returns sixty times and rises. This is the great light, forever, which is called the sun, forever and ever.

**36** This is what goes out, the great light, which is named for its appearance, as the Lord commanded.

**37** As it goes out, so it goes in. It does not grow less and does not rest, but runs day and night in the chariot. Its light shines seven times brighter than the moon's, and in size the two are equal.

## Notes

- **v1 "The book of the turnings of the lights of heaven"** — the Astronomical Book, chapters 72–82, begins here, with its own title. Its guide is Uriel, who spoke to Enoch at 19:1. *Until the new creation is made* is Isaiah's promise: *Here — I create new heavens and a new earth* (Isaiah 65:17).
- **v2 "the gates of heaven"** — *ḫawāḫəwa samāy*, **doors, gates**. Six in the east and six in the west (v3) mark where the sun rises and sets through the year. Charles calls them *portals*.
- **v4 "the great light… whose name is the sun"** — The creation account names neither sun nor moon: *the greater lamp for the rule of the day* (Genesis 1:16). This chapter names the sun and lays out its year.
- **v5 "returns by way of the north to go east"** — Ecclesiastes: *The sun rises and the sun goes down, and hurries to its place, where it rises there* (Ecclesiastes 1:5).
- **v10 "by two parts… ten parts, and the night eight parts"** — the chapter divides a day and a night together into eighteen parts. At the longest day the day has twelve and the night six (v14); at the equal day, nine and nine (v20). *By two parts* is *kāʿbata*, **double**: the day now has two parts more than the night. Charles gives *by a ninth part*, the same amount, since two parts of eighteen are one ninth.
- **vv13, 19, 25, 31 "thirty-one mornings, because of its sign"** — every third month the sun stays one more day in its gate: 30 + 30 + 31 days, four times over. That is how the year of v32 comes out at 364.
- **vv25, 26, 28 three numbers in the EOTC** — at v25 the EOTC reads *because of my sign*, *ziʾaya*, where vv13, 19 and 31 read *ziʾahā*, **its**. At v26 it gives the day *seven* parts beside a night of *twelve*, though the same verse calls the night *double the day*. At v28 it gives the day *one* part beside a night of eleven. The chapter's own sums (eighteen parts, nights and days that mirror each other) require *its*, *six* and *seven*, and Charles has them; the English follows those.
- **v32 "The year is exactly three hundred and sixty-four days"** — fifty-two weeks exactly, so in this calendar every date falls on the same day of the week every year.
- **v37 "seven times brighter than the moon"** — Isaiah says of the day the LORD binds up His people, *the light of the moon will be like the light of the sun, and the light of the sun will be sevenfold* (Isaiah 30:26).

# 1 Enoch 31

**1** I saw another mountain with trees on it, and water flowed out, and from it came something like nectar, whose name is sarara and galbanum.

**2** Beyond that mountain I saw another mountain, and on it were aloe trees, and those trees were full, like almond bark, and strong.

**3** When they take that fruit, it is better than any fragrance.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "galbanum"** — Greek *chalbanē*, a gum resin with a sharp smell. It is one of the four spices of the holy incense in Exodus 30:34, *stacte and onycha and galbanum… and pure frankincense*, the same Greek word in the Greek of Exodus. *Sarara* (Greek *sarran*) is not known from anywhere else.
- **v2 "aloe trees"** — the Ge'ez. The **Greek** has no aloes: *another mountain toward the east of the ends of the earth, and all the trees were full*, and it puts the almonds in a phrase about rubbing. The Ge'ez *wa-ṣənuʿ*, **and strong**, has no match in the Greek.
- **v3 "when they take that fruit"** — the Ge'ez. The **Greek** reads *when they are rubbed; wherefore it was more fragrant than all spices*: bark rubbed to release its smell, not fruit gathered. The Ge'ez *yənaśśəʾəwwo*, **they take it**, is the only point where the two part; both end on a fragrance better than every other.

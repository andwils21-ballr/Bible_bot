# 1 Enoch 20

**1** These are the names of the holy angels who watch:

**2** Uriel, one of the holy angels, who is over the world and over Tartarus.

**3** Raphael, one of the holy angels, who is over the spirits of men.

**4** Raguel, one of the holy angels, who takes vengeance on the world of the luminaries.

**5** Michael, one of the holy angels, who is set over the best part of mankind, and over the nation.

**6** Saraqael, one of the holy angels, who is set over the spirits that sin in the spirit.

**7** Gabriel, one of the holy angels, who is over paradise and the serpents and the cherubim.

---

## Notes

- **v2 "over Tartarus"** — the Greek is **ταρτάρου**, *Tartarus*, the deepest pit of the Greek underworld, below Hades. It is not a Hebrew idea and there is no Hebrew word behind it. It is also the word **2 Peter 2:4** reaches for — the only place in the New Testament that uses it — in the verse about angels who sinned being cast down and *kept for judgment*. The letter describing the Watchers' punishment uses this book's vocabulary for the place.
- **v5 Michael "over the nation"** — the Ge'ez. The Greek has him *over the chaos*. Michael as the guardian of Israel is how Daniel 12:1 knows him — *Michael, the great prince who stands over the sons of your people* — so the Ge'ez reading is the one the rest of the Bible recognizes.
- **the names themselves** — every one ends in *-el*, **God**. *Uri-el*, my light is God. *Rapha-el*, God heals. *Micha-el*, who is like God. *Gabri-el*, God is my strength. The names are not labels; they are sentences, and each angel's task in this list matches the sentence he is called by.

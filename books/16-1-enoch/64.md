# 1 Enoch 64

**1** I saw other faces hidden in that place.

**2** I heard the voice of the angel saying, "These are the angels who came down from heaven to the earth and revealed what was hidden to the sons of men, and led the sons of men astray into committing sin."

---

## Notes

- **v2 "the angels who came down from heaven to the earth and revealed what was hidden"** — the Watchers of chapters 6–9, who *came down onto Ardis, which is the summit of Mount Hermon* (6:6), and of whom the archangels said, *See then what Azazel has done… and revealed the eternal secrets that are kept in heaven* (9:6).

# 1 Enoch 3

**1** Observe and see how all the trees look withered, and all their leaves are stripped off — except fourteen trees, which do not shed their leaves, but wait with the old until the new comes, through two or three winters.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "fourteen trees"** — the number is in the Ge'ez. The **Aramaic** from Qumran says instead *all the tr[ees], **all of them**, appear withered*, with no exception made, and the Greek here is a torn line reading only *observe and see all the trees*. Whether the exception is original or later cannot be decided from what survives.
- **v1 "wait with the old until the new comes"** — the Ge'ez and the Aramaic agree on this clause, and the Aramaic supplies the reckoning: *[their] lea[ves] remain [... until] two or three [years pass]*. Where two witnesses this far apart in age and language line up, the wording behind both is likely close to the original.
- **On the shortest chapter** — one verse, and the witnesses disagree about how many trees it is about.

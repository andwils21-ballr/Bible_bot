# 1 Enoch 3

**1** Observe and see how all the trees look withered, and all their leaves are stripped off — except fourteen trees, which do not shed, but keep the old leaves for two or three years until the new ones come.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "fourteen trees"** — the number is in the Ge'ez alone. The Greek here is a torn line that reads only *observe and see all the trees*, and the **Aramaic** says *all the tr[ees], **all of them**, appear withered* — with no exception made for fourteen of them. The oldest witness may simply be broken at the point where the exception stood, or the exception may be later. The count is not checkable from the earliest text.
- **v1 "keep the old leaves for two or three years"** — this clause is **not in the Ge'ez**, which ends at *do not shed their leaves*. It is in the Aramaic: *[their] lea[ves] remain [... until] two or three [years pass]*. A detail about evergreens that the complete Ethiopic text lost and a Dead Sea fragment kept.
- **On the shortest chapter** — one verse, and all three witnesses disagree about its content. It is a fair sample of what reading this book is like.

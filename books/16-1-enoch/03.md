# 1 Enoch 3

**1** Observe and see how all the trees look withered, and all their leaves are stripped off — except fourteen trees, which do not shed, but keep the old leaves for two or three years until the new ones come.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "fourteen trees"** — the number is in the Ge'ez. The **Aramaic** says *all the tr[ees], **all of them**, appear withered*, with no exception made for fourteen of them, and the Greek here is a torn line reading only *observe and see all the trees*. Whether the exception is original or later cannot be decided from what survives.
- **v1 "keep the old leaves for two or three years"** — from the **Aramaic**: *[their] lea[ves] remain [... until] two or three [years pass]*. A detail about evergreens preserved in a Dead Sea fragment.
- **On the shortest chapter** — one verse, and the witnesses do not agree about its content. It is a fair sample of what reading this book is like.

# 1 Enoch 5

**1** Observe how the trees cover themselves with green leaves and bear fruit. Understand about all of it, and know that He who lives forever made them all for you, and that all their fruit is for honor and glory.

**2** His works are before Him year by year as they come, and all His works serve Him and do not change — but just as God ordained, so everything is done.

**3** See how the seas and the rivers alike carry out their tasks, and do not change their works from His word.

**4** But you — you have not held fast, and you have not kept the commandment of the Lord. You have turned aside, and you have spoken against Him slanderously, great and hard words, with your unclean mouth, against His greatness. You hard-hearted: there will be no peace for you.

**5** Because of this you will curse your days, and the years of your life will perish, and the everlasting curse will grow, and you will find no mercy.

**6** In those days you will make your peace an everlasting curse to all the righteous, and you will be cursed by sinners without end — you, together with the sinners.

**7** For the chosen there will be light and joy and peace, and they will inherit the earth. But for you, the wicked, there will be a curse.

**8** Then wisdom will be given to the chosen, and they will all live and will never sin again, not through godlessness and not through pride. Those who have wisdom will be humble.

**9** They will not sin again, and they will not be condemned all the days of their life, and they will not die by plague or by wrath. They will complete the number of the days of their life, and their life will grow in peace, and the years of their joy will increase in gladness and in peace forever, through all the days of their life.

---

## Notes

- **v4 "there is no peace for you"** — a refrain in this book, and it lands on the accused rather than on their circumstances. Nothing is said about what will happen to them. It is said about what they are.

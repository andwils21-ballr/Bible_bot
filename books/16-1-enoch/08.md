# 1 Enoch 8

**1** Azazel taught men to make swords of iron and daggers and shields and breastplates of bronze, and showed them the metals of the earth and how to work them, and bracelets and ornaments, and the use of antimony, and the painting of the eyelids, and every kind of costly and choice stone, and all the coloring dyes — and the altering of the world.

**2** There was great godlessness, and much fornication, and they went astray, and all their ways became corrupt.

**3** Shemihazah taught spell-binding and the cutting of roots. Armaros, the undoing of spells. Baraqiel, astrology. Kokabiel, the signs of the stars. Tamiel, the watching of the stars. Asradel, the course of the moon. Shamshiel, the omens of the sun — and they revealed mysteries to their wives.

**4** As men were being destroyed, they cried out, and their voice went up to heaven.

## Notes

- **v1 the two halves of the list** — weapons, and then cosmetics. Swords, daggers, shields, breastplates — and then bracelets, antimony, eye-paint, dyes. The book puts arms and beauty in one breath as the same kind of gift, and the connecting thread is named at the end: metal, dug out of the earth and worked.
- **v1 "swords of iron... breastplates of bronze"** — the metals are specified in the **Aramaic** only. The Ge'ez and Greek both say simply swords and breastplates. The Qumran fragment is the fullest witness here and the oldest, and it also has the gold and the silver being made ready, and *stibium and eye-paint* by name.
- **v1 "the altering of the world"** — the phrase is in the **Ge'ez** and in neither of the others. It is the verdict the Ethiopic text puts on the whole list.
- **v1 "antimony... the painting of the eyelids"** — the black eye-paint of Egyptian and Near Eastern cosmetics, ground from stibium. It is named in all three witnesses, which is a strange thing for three traditions to agree on keeping, and it sits in the same sentence as armor.
- **v3 "Shemihazah taught spell-binding"** — the Ge'ez names this teacher **Amazarak**; the Greek and the Aramaic both name him **Shemihazah**, the leader from chapter 6. Two witnesses put the chief of the Watchers at the head of the teaching list. One does not.
- **v3 "Shamshiel, the omens of the sun"** — from the **Aramaic** alone, and the name is built on *shemesh*, the sun. So is the teaching. The Ge'ez and Greek lists stop at the moon.
- **v3 "they revealed mysteries to their wives"** — also **Aramaic** alone, and it closes the loop with 7:1. The wives are not incidental to the corruption. They are who the knowledge was given to.
- **v3 the sciences of the sky** — astrology, the signs of the stars, the watching of the stars, the course of the moon, the omens of the sun. Four or five separate specialists, each with a name, all for reading the heavens. For a book that will spend chapters 72–82 on an exact solar calendar, this list is the illegitimate version of its own subject.
- **v4 "their voice went up to heaven"** — no one prays. The cry simply rises, the way the earth's accusation simply happened in 7:6. Nothing in these two chapters is addressed to God by anyone on the ground, and heaven answers anyway.

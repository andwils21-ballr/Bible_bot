# 1 Enoch 8

**1** Azazel taught men to make swords of iron and daggers and shields and breastplates of bronze, and showed them the metals of the earth and how to work them, and bracelets and ornaments, and the use of antimony, and the painting of the eyelids, and every kind of costly and choice stone, and all the coloring dyes — and the altering of the world.

**2** There was great godlessness, and much fornication, and they went astray, and all their ways became corrupt.

**3** Shemihazah taught spell-binding and the cutting of roots. Armaros, the undoing of spells. Baraqiel, astrology. Kokabiel, the signs of the stars. Tamiel, the watching of the stars. Asradel, the course of the moon. Shamshiel, the omens of the sun — and they revealed mysteries to their wives.

**4** As men were being destroyed, they cried out, and their voice went up to heaven.

---

## Notes

- **v3 "Shemihazah taught spell-binding"** — the Ge'ez names this teacher **Amazarak**; the Greek and the Aramaic both name him **Shemihazah**, the leader from chapter 6. Two witnesses put the chief of the Watchers at the head of the teaching list. One does not.

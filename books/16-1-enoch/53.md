# 1 Enoch 53

**1** There my eyes saw a deep valley with its mouth open wide. All who live on the dry land and the sea and the islands will bring it gifts and presents and tribute, but that deep valley will not be filled.

**2** They commit the wrongs of their hands, and the sinners devour everything they wrongfully toil for. The sinners will perish from the presence of the Lord of Spirits, and they will be banished from the face of His earth, without end, forever and ever.

**3** For I saw the angels of punishment going about and preparing all the instruments for Satan.

**4** I asked the angel of peace who went with me, "For whom are they preparing these instruments?"

**5** He said to me, "They are preparing these for the kings and the mighty of this earth, so that they may perish by them.

**6** After this the Righteous and Chosen One will make the house of his assembly appear. From then on they will no longer be hindered in the name of the Lord of Spirits.

**7** These mountains will be like the ground before him, and the hills will become like a spring of water, and the righteous will have rest from the oppression of the sinners."

## Notes

- **v1 "a deep valley with its mouth open wide… will not be filled"** — the valley is drawn like Sheol: *Sheol has enlarged its appetite and opened its mouth beyond measure* (Isaiah 5:14), and *Sheol and Abaddon are never satisfied* (Proverbs 27:20). *Will bring it gifts* is *lotu*, which can be **to it** or **to him**; Charles reads *to him*. The EOTC lists *gifts and presents and tribute*; Knibb's Ge'ez lacks *gifts*.

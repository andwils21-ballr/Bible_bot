# 1 Enoch 105

**1** In those days the Lord said that they should call out to the children of men and bear witness to them with their wisdom: "Show it to them, for you are their guides, and a reward over all the earth.

**2** For I and My Son will be joined with them forever in the paths of uprightness in their lives, and you will have peace. Rejoice, you children of uprightness, truly."

---

## Notes

- **v1 "you are their guides"** — the Lord speaks to those who hold Enoch's books (104:12–13): the righteous who received them are to show their wisdom to the children of men. *ʾƎgziʾ*, **the Lord**.
- **v2 "I and My Son"** — *ʾana wa-waldəya*. The Lord speaks, and the Son is His; Charles prints *My son*. The Greek has nothing of this chapter. For a Christian reader the words recall Jesus: *If anyone loves Me, he will keep My word; and My Father will love him, and We will come to him and make Our home with him* (John 14:23). The psalm has the Lord say to His anointed, *You are My Son; today I have begotten You* (Psalm 2:7). **"truly"** — *ba-ʾamān*; Charles gives *Amen*. With this verse the Epistle's exhortations end, and what follows is the story of Noah's birth.

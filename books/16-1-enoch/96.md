# 1 Enoch 96

**1** Be hopeful, you righteous, for the sinners will quickly perish before you, and you will have authority over them as you wish.

**2** On the day of the sinners' distress your young will rise and be lifted up like eagles, and your nest will be higher than the vultures'. You will go up and enter the clefts of the earth and the crevices of the rock forever, like the rock badger before the unrighteous, and they will groan over you.

**3** You will not be like them, but this will be a witness against you, that you were companions of the sinners; and they will weep like sirens. But you who have suffered, do not fear, for you will have healing, and a bright light will shine on you, and you will hear a voice of rest from heaven.

**4** Woe to you, sinners, for your riches make you look righteous, but your heart rebukes you that you are sinners, and this word will be a witness against you, a record of your evil deeds.

**5** Woe to you who eat the finest of the wheat and drink the strength of the root of the spring, and trample the sinners with your strength.

**6** Woe to you who drink water at every hour, for you will quickly be repaid, and you will be consumed and wither, because you have forsaken the spring of life.

**7** Woe to you who practice wrongdoing and deceit and blasphemy: this will be a record against you for evil.

**8** Woe to you, sinners, who oppress the righteous with strength, for the day of your destruction is coming. In those days many good days will come to the righteous, on the day of your judgment.

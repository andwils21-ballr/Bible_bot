# 1 Enoch 96

**1** Be hopeful, you righteous, for the sinners will quickly perish before you, and you will have authority over them as you wish.

**2** On the day of the sinners' distress your young will rise and be lifted up like eagles, and your nest will be higher than the vultures'. You will go up and enter the clefts of the earth and the crevices of the rock forever, like the rock badger before the unrighteous, and they will groan over you.

**3** You will not be like them, but this will be a witness against you, that you were companions of the sinners; and they will weep like sirens. But you who have suffered, do not fear, for you will have healing, and a bright light will shine on you, and you will hear a voice of rest from heaven.

**4** Woe to you, sinners, for your riches make you look righteous, but your heart rebukes you that you are sinners, and this word will be a witness against you, a record of your evil deeds.

**5** Woe to you who eat the finest of the wheat and drink the strength of the root of the spring, and trample the sinners with your strength.

**6** Woe to you who drink water at every hour, for you will quickly be repaid, and you will be consumed and wither, because you have forsaken the spring of life.

**7** Woe to you who practice wrongdoing and deceit and blasphemy: this will be a record against you for evil.

**8** Woe to you, sinners, who oppress the righteous with strength, for the day of your destruction is coming. In those days many good days will come to the righteous, on the day of your judgment.

## Notes

- **v2 "lifted up like eagles… higher than the vultures'"** — Isaiah: *those who wait for the LORD will renew their strength; they will mount up with wings like eagles* (Isaiah 40:31). Obadiah says it of proud Edom: *Though you soar like the eagle, and though your nest is set among the stars, from there I will bring you down* (Obadiah 4). Here the nest on high is the reward of the righteous.
- **v2 "like the rock badger"** — *gəḥe*; Charles, *coneys*. The psalm: *the rocks are a refuge for the rock badgers* (Psalm 104:18).
- **v3 "You will not be like them… companions of the sinners"** — in the EOTC this verse begins with a sentence found again, almost word for word, at 97:4, where it is spoken to the sinners. Charles does not have it here. It is rendered as the EOTC gives it; the verse then turns to the righteous, *But you who have suffered*. **"like sirens"** — *ṣedenātāt*, so Charles; he places the weeping at the end of v2.
- **v3 "a bright light will shine on you"** — Isaiah: *The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light; those who lived in the land of the shadow of death, on them light has shone* (Isaiah 9:2).
- **v5 "the finest of the wheat… the strength of the root of the spring"** — *the finest of the wheat* is God's gift in the psalms: *He would feed you with the finest of the wheat* (Psalm 81:16). Where the EOTC has *drink the strength of the root of the spring*, Charles gives *drink wine in large bowls*, and where it has *the sinners*, he gives *the lowly*. The English follows the EOTC.
- **v6 "you have forsaken the spring of life"** — Jeremiah: *they have forsaken Me, the spring of living water, to hew out cisterns for themselves, broken cisterns that can hold no water* (Jeremiah 2:13). **"at every hour"** is *ba-kʷəllu gize*; Charles gives *from every fountain*.

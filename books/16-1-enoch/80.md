# 1 Enoch 80

**1** In those days Uriel the angel answered me and said, "Here — I have shown you everything, Enoch, and I have revealed everything to you, so that you may see this sun and this moon, and those who lead the stars of heaven, and all who turn them, their work, their seasons and their going out.

**2** In the days of the sinners the rainy seasons will be shortened, and their seed will be late in their land and in their fields. Everything on the earth will change and will not appear in its season. The rain will be held back, and heaven will stand still.

**3** In those times the fruit of the earth will be late and will not grow in its season, and the fruit of the trees will be held back in its season.

**4** The moon will change its order and will not be seen in its season.

**5** In those days heaven will be seen, and drought will come at the edge of the great chariot in the west, and it will shine more brightly than the order of light.

**6** Many of the heads of the stars of the command will go astray. They will change their paths and their work, and will not turn at the seasons commanded them.

**7** The whole order of the stars will be shut away from the sinners, and the thoughts of those who live on the earth will go astray about them. They will turn from all their ways, go astray, and take them for gods.

**8** Evil will multiply on them, and punishment will come on them to destroy them all."

## Notes

- **v2 "the rainy seasons will be shortened"** — *kərāmāt*, the rainy seasons. Charles has *the years*.
- **vv2–7 the order breaks** — until now the Astronomical Book has described a creation that keeps its law to the day (74:12). Here, in the days of the sinners, the rain, the harvest, the moon and the stars all fall out of their seasons. Jesus: *the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light, and the stars will fall from heaven* (Matthew 24:29).
- **v5 "heaven will be seen, and drought will come"** — the sense of the EOTC is uncertain. Charles has *the sun shall be seen and he shall journey in the evening on the extremity of the great chariot*.
- **v7 "take them for gods"** — Moses warns against the same: *when you lift up your eyes to the heavens and see the sun and the moon and the stars… do not be drawn away to bow down to them* (Deuteronomy 4:19).

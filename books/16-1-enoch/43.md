# 1 Enoch 43

**1** I saw other lightnings and the stars of heaven, and I saw how He calls them all by their names, and they listen to Him.

**2** I saw the balance of righteousness, how they are weighed by their light, by the breadth of their spaces and the day of their appearing, and their revolution: lightning begets lightning, and their revolution is by the number of their angels, and they keep faith among themselves.

**3** I asked the angel who went with me, who showed me what was hidden, "What are these?"

**4** He said to me, "The Lord of Spirits has shown you their likeness. These are the names of the righteous who live on the dry land and believe in the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever."

---

## Notes

- **v1 "He calls them all by their names"** — the words of Isaiah 40:26 about the stars: *He brings out their host by number; He calls them all by name*.
- **v2 "lightning begets lightning… by the number of their angels"** — the verse is hard in both Ge'ez witnesses, and it is kept close to the words. The stars are weighed like deeds (41:1), each has an angel, and the lightning is born from them (44:1).
- **v4 "their likeness"** — *məsl*, the word behind **parable** (37:5). The stars are a parable: each one is the name of a righteous person on earth. Daniel says the same of the wise: *those who lead many to righteousness will shine like the stars forever and ever* (Daniel 12:3).

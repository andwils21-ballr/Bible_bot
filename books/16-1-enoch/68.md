# 1 Enoch 68

**1** After this my forefather Enoch gave me the signs of all the secrets in a book, and the parables that had been given to him, and he gathered them for me in the words of the book of the parables.

**2** On that day holy Michael answered Raphael, saying, "The power of the spirit seizes me and stirs me to anger because of the severity of the judgment on the secrets of the angels. Who can endure the severity of the judgment that has been carried out and remains, before which they melt away?"

**3** Holy Michael answered again and said to Raphael, "Who is there whose heart is not softened over it, and whose inmost being is not troubled by this word of judgment that has gone out against them, because of those who led them out like this?"

**4** And it came to pass, when he stood before the Lord of Spirits, that holy Michael said to Raphael, "They will have no mercy in the eye of the Lord, for the Lord of Spirits is angry with them, because they act as if they were the Lord.

**5** Because of this the hidden judgment will come on them forever and ever, for neither angel nor man will receive its portion. They alone have received their judgment forever and ever."

## Notes

- **v1 "the book of the parables"** — *maṣḥaf za-məsālē*: the book names itself. The parables Enoch received (37:5) are here handed on in writing to Noah.
- **v2 "Who can endure… before which they melt away?"** — Malachi of the day of the LORD: *who can endure the day of his coming?* (Malachi 3:2).
- **v4 "They will have no mercy in the eye of the Lord"** — the EOTC has *məḥrat*, **mercy**. Knibb's Ge'ez lacks the word, and both English renderings read *I will not take their part*.
- **v4 "because they act as if they were the Lord"** — *ba-ʾamsāla ʾƎgziʾ yəgabbəru*, **they act in the likeness of the Lord**. It is the boast of the king of Babylon in Isaiah: *I will make myself like the Most High* (Isaiah 14:14).

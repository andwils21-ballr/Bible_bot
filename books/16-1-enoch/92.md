# 1 Enoch 92

**1** What was written by Enoch. He wrote all this teaching of wisdom, praised by all men and judge of all the earth, for all my children who live on the earth, and for the later generations who will do uprightness and peace.

**2** Do not let your spirit be grieved because of the times, for the Holy and Great One has given days to everything.

**3** The righteous one will rise from sleep; he will rise and walk in the path of righteousness, and all his path and his going will be in goodness and in grace forever.

**4** He will be gracious to the righteous one and give him eternal uprightness, and give him authority, and he will be in goodness and in righteousness, and walk in eternal light.

**5** Sin will perish in darkness forever, and from that day it will not be seen again, forever.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "What was written by Enoch"** — a new title: the *Epistle of Enoch*, chapters 91–108, the last of the book's parts. *Praised by all men and judge of all the earth* describes the teaching (or its writer); the Ge'ez does not make clear which.
- **v2 "the Holy and Great One has given days to everything"** — Ecclesiastes: *For everything there is a season, and a time for every matter under heaven* (Ecclesiastes 3:1).

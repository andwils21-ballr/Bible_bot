# 1 Enoch 40

**1** After this I saw thousands of thousands and ten thousand times ten thousand, beyond number and reckoning, those who stand before the Lord of Spirits.

**2** I looked, and on the four sides of the Lord of Spirits I saw four presences, different from those who stand there. I learned their names, for the angel who came with me told me them and showed me all the hidden things.

**3** I heard the voices of those four presences as they sang praise before the Lord of Spirits.

**4** The first voice blesses the Lord of Spirits forever and ever.

**5** The second voice I heard blessing the Chosen One and the chosen who hang on the Lord of Spirits.

**6** The third voice I heard asking and praying for those who live on the dry land, and interceding in the name of the Lord of Spirits.

**7** The fourth voice I heard driving away the satans and not allowing them to come before the Lord of Spirits to accuse those who live on the dry land.

**8** After this I asked the angel of peace who went with me, who showed me everything that is hidden, "Who are these four presences I have seen, whose voices I have heard and written down?"

**9** He said to me, "The first is the merciful and slow to anger, holy Michael. The second, who is over every sickness and every wound of the sons of men, is Raphael. The third, who is over every power, is holy Gabriel. The fourth, who is over repentance, for the hope of those who will inherit eternal life, is Phanuel."

**10** These are the four angels of the Lord Most High, and the four voices I heard in those days.

## Notes

- **v1 "thousands of thousands and ten thousand times ten thousand"** — the court of Daniel 7:10, where *a thousand thousands served Him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before Him*. Revelation counts the angels around the throne the same way (Revelation 5:11).
- **v5 "the Chosen One"** — *ḫəruy*, singular: the first time the title appears. Older English versions say *the Elect One*. It is the word of Isaiah 42:1, *My servant… My **chosen one**, in whom My soul delights*. At the transfiguration the voice from the cloud says, *This is My Son, the **Chosen One**; listen to Him* (Luke 9:35).

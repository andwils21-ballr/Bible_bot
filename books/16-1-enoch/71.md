# 1 Enoch 71

**1** And it came to pass after this that my spirit was hidden away and went up into the heaven of heavens. I saw the sons of the holy angels walking on flames of fire. Their garments were white, and their robes, and the light of their faces was like snow.

**2** I saw two rivers of fire, and the light of that fire shone like jacinth, and I fell on my face before the Lord of Spirits.

**3** Michael the angel, one of the archangels, took me by my right hand and lifted me up, and brought me out to all the secrets of mercy and the secrets of righteousness.

**4** He showed me all the secrets of the ends of heaven, and all the storehouses of the stars and of all the lights, from where they go out before the face of the holy ones.

**5** The spirit hid Enoch away in the heaven of heavens. There, in the middle of that light, I saw what looked like something built of stones of crystal, and among those stones were tongues of living fire.

**6** My spirit saw the ring that surrounds that house of fire. On its four sides was a river full of living fire, and they surrounded that house.

**7** Around it were the cherubim, the seraphim and the ophanim. These are the ones who do not sleep, and they guard the throne of His glory.

**8** I saw angels who could not be counted, thousands of thousands and ten thousand times ten thousand, surrounding that house. Michael and Gabriel, Phanuel and Raphael, and the holy angels who are above the heavens, go in and out of that house.

**9** Out of that house came Michael and Gabriel, Raphael and Phanuel, and many holy angels without number.

**10** With them was the Head of Days. His head was white and pure like wool, and His clothing was beyond description.

**11** I fell on my face, and all my flesh melted, and my spirit was changed. I cried out with a loud voice, with the spirit of power, and I blessed and praised and exalted.

**12** These blessings that came out of my mouth were pleasing before that Head of Days.

**13** That Head of Days came with Michael and Gabriel, Raphael and Phanuel, and thousands and ten thousands of angels without number.

**14** That angel came to me and greeted me with his voice, and said to me, "You are the Son of Man who was born for righteousness. Righteousness has dwelt on you, and the righteousness of the Head of Days will not forsake you."

**15** He said to me, "He proclaims peace to you in the name of the everlasting world to come, for from there peace has gone out since the creation of the world, and so it will be for you forever and forever and ever.

**16** All will walk in your way, since righteousness will never forsake you. With you will be their dwelling, and with you their portion, and they will not be separated from you forever and forever and ever.

**17** So there will be length of days with that Son of Man, and the righteous will have peace, and the righteous an upright way, in the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever."

## Notes

- **vv1, 5 "my spirit was hidden away… The spirit hid Enoch away"** — *tətkabbat*, *kabato*, the same verb both times: Enoch is taken out of sight. Genesis says only *He was not, for God took him* (Genesis 5:24). In v5 the chapter names *Enoch* in the third person, in the middle of his own account.
- **vv2, 10 "two rivers of fire… His head was white and pure like wool"** — Daniel's vision of the Ancient of Days: *His clothing was white as snow, and the hair of His head like pure wool… a river of fire flowed and came out from before Him* (Daniel 7:9–10). *Jacinth*, *yākənt*, is the eleventh foundation stone of the new Jerusalem (Revelation 21:20).
- **v5 "stones of crystal… tongues of living fire"** — the house is described as in chapter 14, *built of stones of hail*, with *tongues of fire* around it (14:9–10).
- **v7 "the ones who do not sleep"** — the Watchers (39:12), here the cherubim, seraphim and ophanim of 61:10, guarding the throne.
- **v14 "You are the Son of Man who was born for righteousness"** — *ʾanta wəʾətu walda bəʾəsi*, so both Ge'ez witnesses: the angel speaks to Enoch, *you*. The form of the title is the one of 69:29, *that Son of Man has appeared and sat on the throne of his glory*; when an angel called Noah *son of man* at 60:10, the Ge'ez was *walda sabʾ*. The promises made of the Son of Man are here made to Enoch: *they will live with that Son of Man* (62:14) becomes *with you will be their dwelling* (v16). Charles changes the verse to *This is the Son of Man*, and adds a note that a passage has been lost; neither Ge'ez witness has a gap.
- **v17 "length of days with that Son of Man"** — the last words of the Parables speak of the Son of Man again as *he*, with the form *walda ʿəgʷāla ʾəmmaḥəyāw* of 62:7. *Length of days forever and ever* is the gift to the king in Psalm 21:4.

# 1 Enoch 95

**1** Oh that my eyes were clouds of heaven, so that I might weep over you and pour out my tears like a cloud of water, and rest from the grief of my heart!

**2** Who gave you leave to practice hatred and evil? Judgment will overtake you, sinners.

**3** Do not fear the sinners, you righteous, for the Lord will again hand them over into your hands, so that you may carry out judgment on them as you wish.

**4** Woe to you who pronounce curses that cannot be undone: healing will be far from you because of your sins.

**5** Woe to you who repay your neighbor with evil, for you will be repaid according to your deeds.

**6** Woe to you, false witnesses, and to those who weigh out injustice, for you will quickly perish.

**7** Woe to you, sinners, for you persecute the righteous. You will be handed over and persecuted because of injustice, and their yoke will be heavy on you.

## Notes

- **v1 "Oh that my eyes were clouds of heaven"** — *Who will give me my eyes*, the Ge'ez way of saying *Oh that*. Jeremiah: *Oh that my head were waters, and my eyes a fountain of tears, so that I might weep day and night for the slain of the daughter of my people* (Jeremiah 9:1).
- **v3 "the Lord will again hand them over into your hands"** — *ʾƎgziʾabəḥer*. The righteous carry out the judgment, as in the eighth week at 91:12, when *a sword will be given to it*.
- **v4 "curses that cannot be undone"** — *ʾəlla tāwaggəzu gəzatāta kama ʾitəftəḥu*; Charles, *anathemas which cannot be reversed*.
- **v5 "Woe to you who repay your neighbor with evil"** — Paul: *Repay no one evil for evil* (Romans 12:17).
- **v6 "false witnesses… you will quickly perish"** — Proverbs: *A false witness will not go unpunished, and he who breathes out lies will perish* (Proverbs 19:9).
- **v7 "their yoke will be heavy on you"** — the EOTC has *their* yoke, *ʾarʿuta ziʾahomu*: the yoke of the righteous, whom the sinners persecuted. Charles has *its yoke*, the yoke of injustice.

# 1 Enoch 58

**1** I began to speak the third parable, about the righteous and the chosen.

**2** Blessed are you, the righteous and the chosen, for your portion is glorious.

**3** The righteous will be in the light of the sun, and the chosen in the light of eternal life. There will be no end to the days of their life, and the days of the holy will be without number.

**4** They will seek the light and find righteousness with the Lord of Spirits. There will be peace for the righteous with the Lord of the ages.

**5** After this the holy ones in heaven will be told to seek out the secrets of righteousness, the portion of faith, for it has risen like the sun on the dry land, and the darkness has passed.

**6** There will be light that never runs out, and their days will not be counted, for the darkness will have been destroyed first. The light will grow strong before the Lord of Spirits, and the light of uprightness will stand firm forever before the Lord of Spirits.

## Notes

- **v3 "The righteous will be in the light of the sun"** — Jesus: *Then the righteous will shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father* (Matthew 13:43).
- **v4 "the Lord of the ages"** — *ʾƎgziʾa ʿālam*, the only time the title appears in the book. *ʿĀlam* is **world** and also **eternity**, the word in *la-ʿālam*, **forever**. The OCP's English of the Ge'ez gives *the Lord of the world*; Charles, *the Eternal Lord*. *The King of the ages* at 12:3 is the same word.
- **v4 "There will be peace for the righteous"** — the EOTC has the verb, *yəkawwən*, **will be**; Knibb's Ge'ez has only *peace to the righteous*, a greeting.
- **v5 "the darkness has passed"** — the words of the first letter of John: *the darkness is passing away, and the true light is already shining* (1 John 2:8).
- **v6 "light that never runs out, and their days will not be counted"** — *za-ʾiyətḫwallaqʷ*, **that is not counted**, the word of 48:1, *the fountain of righteousness, which never runs out*. Neither the light nor the days of the righteous are counted (v3). Isaiah has the same unending light: *the LORD will be your everlasting light, and the days of your mourning will be ended* (Isaiah 60:20).

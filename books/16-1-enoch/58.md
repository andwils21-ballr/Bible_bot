# 1 Enoch 58

**1** I began to speak the third parable, about the righteous and the chosen.

**2** Blessed are you, the righteous and the chosen, for your portion is glorious.

**3** The righteous will be in the light of the sun, and the chosen in the light of eternal life. There will be no end to the days of their life, and the days of the holy will be without number.

**4** They will seek the light and find righteousness with the Lord of Spirits. There will be peace for the righteous with the Lord of the ages.

**5** After this the holy ones in heaven will be told to seek out the secrets of righteousness, the portion of faith, for it has risen like the sun on the dry land, and the darkness has passed.

**6** There will be light that never runs out, and their days will not be counted, for the darkness will have been destroyed first. The light will grow strong before the Lord of Spirits, and the light of uprightness will stand firm forever before the Lord of Spirits.

# 1 Enoch 74

**1** I saw another course and another law for it, by which law it makes its monthly course.

**2** Uriel the holy angel, who is the leader of them all, showed me everything, and I wrote down their positions as he showed me. I wrote down their months as they are, and the appearance of their light, until fifteen days are completed.

**3** One seventh at a time it completes all its light in the east, and one seventh at a time it completes all its darkness in the west.

**4** In appointed months it completes its settings, and in appointed months it goes on its own course, one by one.

**5** In two months it sets with the sun in those two middle gates, in the third and the fourth gate.

**6** It goes out for seven days and turns around and returns again by the gate from which the sun comes out, and in it completes all its light. Then it moves away from the sun and enters in eight days the sixth gate, from which the sun comes out.

**7** When the sun comes out from the fourth gate, it goes out for seven days until it comes out from the fifth, and again returns in seven days to the fourth gate and completes all its light. Then it moves away and enters the first gate in eight days.

**8** Again it returns in seven days to the fourth gate, from which the sun comes out.

**9** In this way I saw their positions: as the order of their months runs, the sun rises and sets.

**10** In those days, over five years, thirty days are added to the sun, and all the days of any one of those five years, when they are full, come to three hundred and sixty-four days.

**11** The excess of the sun and the stars comes to six days: in five years, six each year, it comes to thirty days, and the moon falls behind the sun and the stars by thirty days.

**12** The moon brings in the years exactly, and all of them, by their positions, do not go ahead or fall behind by a single day, but change the year exactly, in righteousness, every three hundred and sixty-four days.

**13** In three years the days come to one thousand and ninety-two, and in five years to one thousand eight hundred and twenty, so that in eight years there are two thousand nine hundred and twelve days.

**14** For the moon alone, its days in three years come to one thousand and sixty-two, and in five years it falls fifty days behind, because it is increased in its going out by sixty-two days.

**15** For five years there are one thousand seven hundred and seventy days, so that for the moon in eight years its days come to two thousand eight hundred and thirty-two.

**16** For its shortfall in eight years is eighty days; all the days it falls short in eight years are eighty.

**17** The year is completed exactly, in righteousness, by their stations and the stations of the sun, which rise from the gates from which it rises and sets, thirty days.

## Notes

- **v2 "I wrote down their positions as he showed me"** — Enoch the scribe (12:3) writes the book as Uriel shows it to him.
- **vv10–16 the years of the sun and the moon** — the sun's year is 364 days: 1,092 in three years, 1,820 in five, 2,912 in eight (v13). The moon's year is 354: 1,062 in three years and 1,770 in five, 2,832 in eight (vv14–15). The moon falls ten days a year behind, fifty in five years and eighty in eight (vv14, 16). Every figure the EOTC gives here agrees with the others. v11 counts the excess another way, six days a year, thirty in five years; Charles has the same.
- **v12 "The moon brings in the years exactly"** — so the EOTC. Charles has *the sun and the stars*, which fits the rest of the verse: they do not go ahead or fall behind by a single day, and they complete the year in 364 days, where vv11 and 14 have the moon falling behind.
- **v14 "because it is increased in its going out by sixty-two days"** — the sense is uncertain. Charles supplies words to make it a sum (*to the sum (of 1770) there is to be added (1000 and) 62 days*).

# 1 Enoch 36

**1** From there I went south, to the ends of the earth, and there I saw three gates of heaven open, and from there come the south wind, dew, rain and wind.

**2** From there I went east, to the ends of heaven, and there I saw three gates of heaven open toward the east, and above them small gates.

**3** Through each of these small gates pass the stars of heaven, and they go west on the path that was shown to them.

**4** When I saw it, I blessed, and I will always bless the Lord of glory, who has made great and glorious wonders, to show the greatness of His work to His angels and to the souls of men, so that they may praise His work and all His creation, so that they may see the work of His power and praise the great work of His hands, and bless Him forever.

---

## Notes

- **v4 "I will always bless the Lord of glory"** — the title of 22:14 and 25:3. The chapter, and the whole first part of the book (chapters 1–36, the *Book of the Watchers*), ends on a blessing: the long journey through prisons, mountains, trees and gates closes with Enoch praising what he has been shown.

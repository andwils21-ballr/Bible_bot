# 1 Enoch 46

**1** There I saw One who had a head of days, and His head was like white wool. With Him was another, whose face had the appearance of a man, and his face was full of grace, like one of the holy angels.

**2** I asked one of the holy angels who went with me and showed me all the hidden things about that Son of Man: "Who is he, and where is he from, and why does he go with the Head of Days?"

**3** He answered me and said, "This is the Son of Man who has righteousness, and righteousness lives with him. He will reveal all the treasures of what is hidden, because the Lord of Spirits has chosen him, and his portion has prevailed before the Lord of Spirits in uprightness forever.

**4** This Son of Man whom you have seen will rouse the kings and the mighty from their couches, and the strong from their thrones. He will loosen the reins of the strong and break the teeth of sinners.

**5** He will throw the kings down from their thrones and from their kingdoms, because they do not exalt him or praise him, or humbly acknowledge where their kingship was given them from.

**6** He will throw down the faces of the strong and fill them with shame. Darkness will be their dwelling, and worms will be their bed, and they will have no hope of rising from their beds, because they do not exalt the name of the Lord of Spirits.

**7** These are the ones who judge the stars of heaven and raise their hands against the Most High, and tread on the dry land and live on it. All their deeds show wrongdoing, and all their deeds are wrongdoing. Their power rests on their wealth, and their faith is in the gods they have made with their hands, and they deny the name of the Lord of Spirits.

**8** They drive the faithful out of His houses of assembly, those who hang on the name of the Lord of Spirits."

## Notes

- **v1 "One who had a head of days, and His head was like white wool"** — the Ancient of Days of Daniel 7:9, *the hair of His head was like pure wool*. Here He is described by His age, *a head of days*, and from v2 on it becomes His title. In Revelation the same white wool belongs to the one *like a son of man*: *His head and His hair were white like wool* (Revelation 1:13–14).
- **v1 "another, whose face had the appearance of a man"** — Daniel 7:13: *with the clouds of heaven came one **like a son of man**, and he came to the Ancient of Days*. Daniel sees him come to the throne. Enoch sees him already beside it.
- **v2 "that Son of Man"** — *walda sabʾ*, the first of the Ge'ez expressions the Parables use for this figure; the other main one, *walda ʿəgwāla ʾəmmaḥəyāw*, begins at 62:7. Daniel wrote *like a son of man*, a comparison. Here *that Son of Man* points at the one Enoch has just seen, and the rest of the Parables use it as his title. In the Gospels it is the name Jesus uses for himself.
- **v2 "the Head of Days"** — the EOTC, *Rəʾsa Mawāʿəl*. Knibb's Ge'ez has *the **Ancient** of Days*, *Bəluya Mawāʿəl*, Daniel's own title.
- **v3 "He will reveal all the treasures of what is hidden"** — Paul says of Christ, *in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge* (Colossians 2:3).
- **vv4–5 "throw the kings down from their thrones"** — Mary's song: *He has brought down the mighty from their thrones and lifted up the lowly* (Luke 1:52). *Break the teeth of sinners* is Psalm 3:7, *You break the teeth of the wicked*.
- **vv6–7 "worms will be their bed… they judge the stars of heaven"** — both from Isaiah 14, the taunt over the king of Babylon: *maggots are spread beneath you, and worms are your covering* (14:11), the king who said, *I will raise my throne above the stars of God* (14:13). The kings of the Parables are that king, many times over.
- **v8 "They drive the faithful out of His houses of assembly"** — the EOTC, *wa-yəsaddədu*: the kings persecute the faithful. Knibb's Ge'ez has the passive, *wa-yəssaddadu*, **they will be driven out** of His houses: the kings' punishment rather than their crime. One letter's vowel separates the two.

# 1 Enoch 16

**1** From the day of the slaughter and destruction and death of the giants, the spirits that went out from the souls of their flesh will be destroying without judgment. So they will destroy until the day of the great consummation, when the great age is completed, over the Watchers and the godless.'

**2** And now, to the Watchers who sent you to petition on their behalf, who were formerly in heaven, say:

**3** 'You were in heaven, and not every mystery had yet been revealed to you — and a mystery that came from God you knew, and this you made known to the women in the hardness of your heart, and through this mystery women and men multiply evil on the earth.'

**4** Say to them, then: 'You have no peace.'"

---

## Notes

- **v3 "a mystery that came from God you knew"** — that is the **Greek**. The Ge'ez says something close to the reverse: *a **rejected** mystery you knew*. On the Greek reading the knowledge was genuine and divine, and the crime was passing it on. On the Ge'ez reading the knowledge was worthless to begin with. The charge against the Watchers changes shape depending on which witness is read.
- **v4 "You have no peace"** — the fourth and last time. It closes the Watchers section, and after this chapter the book leaves the subject and Enoch begins to travel. The whole trial, from the earth's accusation in 7:6 to this sentence, runs ten chapters and ends on four words.

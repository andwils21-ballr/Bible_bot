# Leviticus 26

**1** "You shall not make worthless gods for yourselves, and you shall not set up a carved image or a standing stone for yourselves, and you shall not put a carved stone in your land to bow down to it, because I am the LORD your God.

**2** You shall keep My sabbaths and revere My sanctuary. I am the LORD.

## If You Walk in My Statutes

**3** If you walk in My statutes and keep My commandments and do them,

**4** I will give you your rains in their season, and the land will give its produce, and the trees of the field will give their fruit.

**5** Your threshing will reach to the grape harvest, and the grape harvest will reach to the sowing. You will eat your bread to the full and live in your land in security.

**6** And I will give peace in the land, and you will lie down with no one to make you afraid. I will rid the land of harmful beasts, and no sword will pass through your land.

**7** You will chase your enemies, and they will fall before you by the sword.

**8** Five of you will chase a hundred, and a hundred of you will chase ten thousand, and your enemies will fall before you by the sword.

**9** And I will turn to you, and make you fruitful and multiply you, and I will establish My covenant with you.

**10** You will eat old stored grain, and you will clear out the old to make room for the new.

**11** And I will set My dwelling among you, and My soul will not abhor you.

**12** And I will walk among you, and I will be your God, and you will be My people.

**13** I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt so that you would no longer be their slaves. I broke the bars of your yoke and made you walk upright.

## If You Will Not Listen

**14** But if you will not listen to Me and will not do all these commandments,

**15** if you reject My statutes, and your soul abhors My judgments, so that you do not do all My commandments, and you break My covenant,

**16** then I in turn will do this to you: I will bring sudden terror on you, wasting disease and fever that wear out the eyes and drain the life away. You will sow your seed for nothing, because your enemies will eat it.

**17** I will set My face against you, and you will be struck down before your enemies. Those who hate you will rule over you, and you will flee when no one is chasing you.

**18** And if after all this you will not listen to Me, I will discipline you seven times more for your sins.

**19** I will break the pride of your strength, and I will make your sky like iron and your earth like bronze.

**20** Your strength will be spent for nothing: your land will not give its produce, and the trees of the land will not give their fruit.

**21** And if you walk with Me in hostility and will not listen to Me, I will add blows to you seven times over, as your sins deserve.

**22** I will send the wild animals among you, and they will rob you of your children, and wipe out your livestock, and make you few, and your roads will be deserted.

**23** And if by these things you will not be corrected by Me, but walk with Me in hostility,

**24** then I too will walk with you in hostility, and I Myself will strike you seven times for your sins.

**25** I will bring a sword on you that avenges the covenant. And when you gather into your cities, I will send pestilence among you, and you will be handed over to the enemy.

**26** When I break your staff of bread, ten women will bake your bread in one oven and hand out your bread by weight, and you will eat and not be satisfied.

**27** And if in spite of this you will not listen to Me, but walk with Me in hostility,

**28** then I will walk with you in furious hostility, and I Myself will discipline you seven times for your sins.

**29** You will eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters you will eat.

**30** I will destroy your high places and cut down your incense altars, and throw your corpses on the corpses of your idols, and My soul will abhor you.

**31** I will make your cities a ruin and lay waste your sanctuaries, and I will not smell your soothing aroma.

**32** I Myself will lay waste the land, so that your enemies who come to live in it will be appalled at it.

**33** And you I will scatter among the nations, and I will draw the sword after you. Your land will be a waste, and your cities will be a ruin.

**34** Then the land will be paid its sabbaths, all the days it lies waste while you are in the land of your enemies. Then the land will rest and be paid its sabbaths.

**35** All the days it lies waste it will rest, the rest it did not have on your sabbaths while you lived on it.

**36** And as for those of you who are left, I will bring faintness into their hearts in the lands of their enemies. The sound of a driven leaf will chase them, and they will flee as one flees from the sword, and fall when no one is chasing.

**37** They will stumble over one another as if before the sword, when no one is chasing. You will have no power to stand before your enemies.

**38** You will perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies will eat you up.

**39** And those of you who are left will rot away in their guilt in the lands of your enemies, and also in the guilt of their fathers they will rot away with them.

## If They Confess

**40** But if they confess their guilt and the guilt of their fathers, in the treachery they committed against Me, and also that they walked with Me in hostility,

**41** so that I too walked with them in hostility and brought them into the land of their enemies — if then their uncircumcised heart is humbled, and they then pay for their guilt,

**42** then I will remember My covenant with Jacob, and also My covenant with Isaac, and also My covenant with Abraham I will remember, and the land I will remember.

**43** For the land will be left empty of them, and will be paid its sabbaths while it lies waste without them, and they will pay for their guilt, because — and only because — they rejected My judgments and their soul abhorred My statutes.

**44** And yet for all that, when they are in the land of their enemies, I will not reject them and I will not abhor them so as to destroy them and break My covenant with them, because I am the LORD their God.

**45** I will remember for them the covenant with the first generation, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt before the eyes of the nations, to be their God. I am the LORD."

**46** These are the statutes and the judgments and the laws that the LORD gave between Himself and the sons of Israel on Mount Sinai, through Moses.

## Notes

- **v1 "a carved stone"** — *even maskit*. *Maskit* is from *sakhah*, to look: a stone made to be looked at, carved with a figure. Ezekiel uses the same word for the pictured walls of the room where the elders worship images in the dark (Ezekiel 8:12). *Worthless gods*, *elilim*, is the word of 19:4, which sounds like *elohim*, God, and means *nothings*.
- **v9 "make you fruitful and multiply you"** — *ve-hifreti… ve-hirbeti*, the two verbs of the first blessing on humanity, *be fruitful and multiply* (Genesis 1:28), and of the promise to Abraham (17:6). The blessing that began creation is what obedience in the land brings back.
- **vv11–12 "I will set My dwelling among you… and I will walk among you"** — *mishkan*, the word for the tabernacle, and *ve-hithallakhti*, the form of *halakh* that Genesis uses for God *walking about in the garden* (3:8). The last promise of the blessing is Eden: God living among people and walking there. Paul quotes this verse as God's promise to the church (2 Corinthians 6:16).
- **v13 "made you walk upright"** — *qomemiyut*, a word that occurs here and nowhere else in the Bible, from *qum*, to stand up. The yoke's crossbars kept the neck bent; God broke them, and the people stood up straight.
- **vv18, 21, 24, 28 "seven times"** — the warning comes in four rounds, and each round ends *seven times* more. Seven is the number of the sabbath and of completion; this chapter, which ends with the land's unpaid sabbaths, measures the punishment in it.
- **vv21–41 "in hostility"** — *qeri*, seven times in this chapter and nowhere else in the Hebrew Bible. It is from *qarah*, to meet or happen, and it has always been read two ways: **against** (meeting head-on, opposition), or **by chance** (living as though what happens to them has nothing to do with God). The second reading makes God's answer, *I will walk with you in qeri*, mean: then I will leave you to the chance you believe in. The Hebrew allows both; *hostility* is the usual English reading.
- **v26 "your staff of bread"** — *matteh lechem*, bread as the staff a person leans on to stay alive. When it breaks, ten households share one oven, and the bread is weighed out.
- **v30 "your idols"** — *gillulim*, its first appearance in the Bible. It is built on *galal*, dung, and sounds like it; Ezekiel uses it in more than thirty verses. The word is an insult, not a description.
- **vv34–43 "be paid its sabbaths… pay for their guilt"** — one verb, *ratsah*, to pay off or make good, for both: the land *is paid its sabbaths* (vv34, 43) and the people *pay for their guilt* (vv41, 43). The land is owed the sabbath years of chapter 25 and collects them in the exile. 2 Chronicles 36:21 cites this chapter to explain the seventy years of exile: *until the land had been paid its sabbaths*.
- **v41 "their uncircumcised heart"** — the first time the Bible speaks of the heart as uncircumcised. Deuteronomy will command the cure: *circumcise the foreskin of your heart* (10:16).
- **v42 "Jacob… Isaac… Abraham"** — the covenant is remembered in reverse order, starting with the latest father and reaching back to the first. *Jacob* here is spelled with an extra letter, *Ya'aqov* with a *vav*, one of only five places in the Bible with that spelling, and the only one in the five books of Moses.
- **v44 "I will not reject them and I will not abhor them"** — *ga'al*, **abhor**, is the chapter's turning word: God's soul will not abhor them (v11); their soul abhors His judgments (v15); His soul abhors them (v30); they abhorred His statutes (v43); and at the end, even in exile, *I will not abhor them*. The last use undoes the ones before it.

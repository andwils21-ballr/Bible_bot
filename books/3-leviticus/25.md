# Leviticus 25

## A Sabbath for the Land

**1** And the LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, saying,

**2** "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them: When you come into the land that I am giving you, the land shall keep a sabbath to the LORD.

**3** Six years you shall sow your field, and six years you shall prune your vineyard and gather in its produce.

**4** But in the seventh year there shall be a sabbath of complete rest for the land, a sabbath to the LORD. Your field you shall not sow, and your vineyard you shall not prune.

**5** What grows by itself from your harvest you shall not reap, and the grapes of your untrimmed vine you shall not gather. It shall be a year of complete rest for the land.

**6** And the sabbath of the land shall be food for you — for you, and for your male slave and your female slave, and for your hired man and your resident, who live with you as guests,

**7** and for your livestock and for the wild animals that are in your land. All its produce shall be food.

## The Jubilee

**8** And you shall count for yourself seven sabbaths of years, seven years seven times, so that the days of the seven sabbaths of years come to forty-nine years.

**9** Then you shall send the horn's blast through the land, in the seventh month, on the tenth of the month. On the Day of Atonement you shall send the horn through all your land.

**10** And you shall make the fiftieth year holy, and proclaim liberty in the land to all who live in it. It shall be a jubilee for you, and you shall return, each man to his holding, and each man to his family you shall return.

**11** That fiftieth year shall be a jubilee for you. You shall not sow, and you shall not reap what grows by itself, and you shall not gather the grapes of its untrimmed vines.

**12** Because it is a jubilee, it shall be holy to you. You shall eat its produce straight from the field.

**13** In this year of jubilee you shall return, each man to his holding.

**14** And if you sell something to your neighbor, or buy from your neighbor's hand, you shall not wrong one another.

**15** By the number of years since the jubilee you shall buy from your neighbor; by the number of years of harvests he shall sell to you.

**16** The more the years, the higher you shall make its price; the fewer the years, the lower you shall make its price, because it is a number of harvests that he is selling you.

**17** And you shall not wrong one another, but you shall fear your God, because I am the LORD your God.

**18** And you shall do My statutes, and keep My judgments and do them, and you will live in the land in security.

**19** And the land will give its fruit, and you will eat your fill and live in it in security.

**20** And if you say, 'What shall we eat in the seventh year, if we may not sow or gather in our produce?' —

**21** I will command My blessing for you in the sixth year, and it will produce enough for three years.

**22** And you will sow in the eighth year and eat of the old produce until the ninth year. Until its produce comes in, you will eat the old.

## Redeeming the Land

**23** And the land shall not be sold for good, because the land is Mine. For you are guests and residents with Me.

**24** And in all the land you hold, you shall allow the land to be redeemed.

**25** If your brother becomes poor and sells some of his holding, his redeemer who is closest to him shall come and redeem what his brother has sold.

**26** And if a man has no one to redeem it, and he himself prospers and finds enough to redeem it,

**27** he shall count the years since he sold it, and pay back what remains to the man he sold it to, and return to his holding.

**28** But if he has not found enough to pay him back, then what he sold shall remain in the hand of the one who bought it until the year of jubilee. And it shall go out in the jubilee, and he shall return to his holding.

**29** And if a man sells a house to live in, in a walled city, his right to redeem it shall last until a full year after its sale. For a year he may redeem it.

**30** And if it is not redeemed before a full year has passed, the house in the walled city shall belong for good to the one who bought it, throughout his generations. It shall not go out in the jubilee.

**31** But the houses of villages that have no wall around them shall be counted as the fields of the land. They may be redeemed, and they shall go out in the jubilee.

**32** And as for the cities of the Levites, the houses in the cities they hold — the Levites shall always have the right to redeem them.

**33** And if one of the Levites redeems it, then the house that was sold in the city he holds shall go out in the jubilee, because the houses in the cities of the Levites are their holding among the sons of Israel.

**34** And the pasture land around their cities shall not be sold, because it is their holding forever.

## A Brother Who Becomes Poor

**35** And if your brother becomes poor and his hand fails beside you, you shall hold him up. As a guest and a resident, he shall live with you.

**36** Take no interest from him and no increase, but fear your God, so that your brother may live with you.

**37** You shall not give him your money at interest, and you shall not give him your food for increase.

**38** I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan, to be your God.

**39** And if your brother becomes poor beside you and sells himself to you, you shall not make him work as a slave works.

**40** As a hired man, as a resident, he shall be with you. Until the year of jubilee he shall work with you.

**41** Then he shall go out from you, he and his sons with him, and he shall return to his family, and to the holding of his fathers he shall return.

**42** For they are My servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt. They shall not be sold as a slave is sold.

**43** You shall not rule over him with crushing harshness, but you shall fear your God.

**44** As for your male and female slaves whom you may have — from the nations around you, from them you may buy male and female slaves.

**45** And also from the sons of the residents who live with you as guests, from them you may buy, and from their families that are with you, whom they have fathered in your land. They may be your property.

**46** And you may leave them to your sons after you, to inherit as property. You may make them work for you forever. But over your brothers, the sons of Israel, one over another, you shall not rule with crushing harshness.

**47** And if a guest or a resident with you prospers, and your brother beside him becomes poor and sells himself to the guest or resident with you, or to an offshoot of the guest's family,

**48** after he is sold he shall have the right to be redeemed. One of his brothers may redeem him,

**49** or his uncle or his uncle's son may redeem him, or anyone of his own flesh from his family may redeem him, or if he prospers he may redeem himself.

**50** And he shall reckon with the one who bought him from the year he sold himself to him until the year of jubilee, and the price of his sale shall be according to the number of years. As the days of a hired man it shall be with him.

**51** If many years remain, he shall pay back for his redemption a matching part of the price he was bought for.

**52** And if few years remain until the year of jubilee, he shall reckon it with him; according to his years he shall pay back his redemption.

**53** As a man hired year by year he shall be with him. He shall not rule over him with crushing harshness in your sight.

**54** And if he is not redeemed by these means, then he shall go out in the year of jubilee, he and his sons with him.

**55** For the sons of Israel are servants to Me. They are My servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God."

## Notes

- **v1 "on Mount Sinai"** — the mountain is named here for the first time since 7:38, and again at the close of the next two chapters (26:46, 27:34). The laws of land, debt and release are marked as given at Sinai, alongside the covenant itself.
- **v5 "your untrimmed vine"** — *nezirekha*, your *nazir*: the word for a Nazirite, the man who lets no razor touch his head (Numbers 6:5). The vine left unpruned in the sabbath year is called a Nazirite vine, set apart and left to grow as it will.
- **v9 "send the horn's blast through the land"** — *ve-ha'avarta shofar teru'ah*, and the verb is *avar*, to pass through: the verb of the Passover night, *I will pass through the land of Egypt* (Exodus 12:12). The sound is made to cross the whole land. The *shofar* is a ram's horn, and the *teru'ah* is the blast of 23:24. The year of release is announced on the Day of Atonement.
- **v10 "proclaim liberty… a jubilee"** — *deror*, release, the freeing of those held. Isaiah takes up the phrase for the one anointed *to proclaim liberty to the captives* (61:1), and Jesus reads that line aloud in the synagogue at Nazareth (Luke 4:18). The Liberty Bell in Philadelphia carries this verse. *Jubilee* is *yovel*, the ram, and so the ram's horn: the horns blown at Jericho are *yovel* horns (Joshua 6:5). The year is named for the sound that starts it.
- **vv15–16 "it is a number of harvests he is selling"** — land is not sold at all, only the use of it until the next jubilee. The price is the number of crops left, and the law makes the buyer count them.
- **v21 "I will command My blessing"** — *ve-tsivviti et birkhati*, the verb of God's commandments. God does not ask the land for a harvest; He commands the blessing, and it yields three years' food in one.
- **v23 "for you are guests and residents with Me"** — *gerim ve-toshavim*, the same two words Abraham used of himself to the Hittites when he had no land to bury Sarah in: *a guest and a resident I am among you* (Genesis 23:4). Israel is to hold the land the way Abraham lived in it: as a guest of the owner, who is God.
- **v25 "his redeemer"** — *go'alo*, from *ga'al*, to buy back what belongs to the family. The *go'el* is the nearest relative, whose duty is to redeem the land, or the man, before they are lost. Boaz is the *go'el* who redeems Naomi's field and marries Ruth (Ruth 4:4), and Job says *I know that my **redeemer** lives* (Job 19:25). It is the same word.
- **v30 "the house in the walled city"** — the Hebrew text as written says the city *which has **not** a wall*, *lo*, spelled with an *aleph*. The reading marked in the margin is *lo* spelled with a *vav*, **which has** a wall, and v29 shows that is the sense. It is one of five places in Leviticus where the written word and the read word differ.
- **v35 "his hand fails… you shall hold him up"** — *u-matah yado*, his hand totters, *ve-hechezaqta bo*, you shall take hold of him and make him strong. The law pictures a man about to fall and a neighbor who grabs him.
- **v36 "interest"** — *neshekh*, literally a **bite**, from *nashakh*, the verb for a serpent biting (Genesis 49:17; Numbers 21:6). Interest is named for what it does to the man who owes it.
- **vv42, 55 "My servants"** — *avadai*. Hebrew has one word, *eved*, for both **servant** and **slave**: it is the slave sold in the same sentence and the slaves of Egypt. Israel belongs to God, and so may be sold to no human master. At the sea the people said it would be better *to serve Egypt* (Exodus 14:12). The law answers that they already serve someone else.
- **v43 "with crushing harshness"** — *be-farekh*, the word for how Egypt made Israel work: *with crushing labor* (Exodus 1:13–14). It occurs three times in this chapter (vv43, 46, 53), and every time the command is that no Israelite may do it to another.

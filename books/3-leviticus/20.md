# Leviticus 20

## Molech, and the Ones Who Look Away

**1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "And to the sons of Israel you shall say: Any man of the sons of Israel, or of the foreigners living in Israel, who gives any of his children to Molech shall surely be put to death. The people of the land shall stone him with stones.

**3** And I Myself will set My face against that man, and cut him off from among his people, because he gave of his children to Molech, to make My sanctuary unclean and to profane My holy name.

**4** And if the people of the land deliberately shut their eyes to that man when he gives his children to Molech, and do not put him to death,

**5** then I Myself will set My face against that man and against his family, and cut him off from among their people — him and all who go whoring after him, to whore after Molech.

**6** And the person who turns to ghosts and to spirits, to whore after them — I will set My face against that person and cut him off from among his people.

**7** And you shall set yourselves apart and be holy, because I am the LORD your God.

**8** And you shall keep My statutes and do them. I am the LORD who makes you holy.

## The Penalties

**9** Any man who curses his father or his mother shall surely be put to death. He has cursed his father and his mother; his blood is on him.

**10** And a man who commits adultery with another man's wife — who commits adultery with his neighbor's wife — the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be put to death.

**11** And a man who lies with his father's wife has uncovered his father's nakedness. Both of them shall surely be put to death; their blood is on them.

**12** And a man who lies with his daughter-in-law — both of them shall surely be put to death. They have done a mixing; their blood is on them.

**13** And a man who lies with a male as one lies with a woman — both of them have done a detestable thing. They shall surely be put to death; their blood is on them.

**14** And a man who takes a woman and her mother — it is depravity. They shall burn him and them in the fire, so that there shall be no depravity among you.

**15** And a man who gives his emission to an animal shall surely be put to death, and you shall kill the animal.

**16** And a woman who comes near any animal to mate with it — you shall kill the woman and the animal. They shall surely be put to death; their blood is on them.

**17** And a man who takes his sister, his father's daughter or his mother's daughter, and sees her nakedness and she sees his nakedness — it is a disgrace. They shall be cut off in the sight of the sons of their people. He has uncovered his sister's nakedness; he bears his guilt.

**18** And a man who lies with a woman in her sickness and uncovers her nakedness — he has laid bare her source, and she has uncovered the source of her blood. Both of them shall be cut off from among their people.

**19** And you shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister or of your father's sister, because that is laying bare his own flesh. They shall bear their guilt.

**20** And a man who lies with his uncle's wife has uncovered his uncle's nakedness. They shall bear their sin; they shall die childless.

**21** And a man who takes his brother's wife — it is an impurity. He has uncovered his brother's nakedness. They shall be childless.

## Why You Are Not Like Them

**22** And you shall keep all My statutes and all My judgments and do them, so that the land I am bringing you into to live in does not vomit you out.

**23** And you shall not walk in the statutes of the nation I am driving out before you, because they did all these things, and I was sick of them.

**24** And I said to you: You shall take possession of their ground, and I will give it to you to possess — a land flowing with milk and honey. I am the LORD your God, who has separated you from the peoples.

**25** And you shall separate between the clean animal and the unclean, and between the unclean bird and the clean, and you shall not make yourselves loathsome with any animal or bird or anything that creeps on the ground, which I have separated out for you as unclean.

**26** And you shall be holy to Me, because I the LORD am holy, and I have separated you from the peoples to be Mine.

**27** And a man or a woman who has a ghost or a spirit in them shall surely be put to death. They shall stone them with stones; their blood is on them."

## Notes

- **v17 "it is a disgrace"** — the Hebrew word is *chesed*, and this is one of the strangest sentences in the Bible. *Chesed* is the great covenant word: loyal love, steadfast kindness, the thing God is said to keep for a thousand generations, the word in *I desire mercy and not sacrifice* (Hosea 6:6) and all through the Psalms. Here it names an act punished by being cut off. The spelling is identical and so is the vowel pointing, checked against Hosea 6:6 and Psalm 25:10 — this is not a look-alike. Either one root carried two opposite senses, shame and devotion, or a second root has been swallowed by the first. Nothing in the verse decides it, and the English cannot show the collision, so it is recorded here.
- **v9 "his blood is on him"** — *damav bo*, said six times in this chapter. It is not a statement that the man deserves it. It is a liability formula: the blood is accounted to him and not to the people carrying out the sentence. Israelite law returns to this repeatedly — who is answerable for a death — and the phrase settles the question in three words each time.
- **v4 "deliberately shut their eyes"** — *ha'alem ya'limu*, the verb doubled with itself, which is how Hebrew leans on a word: not a failure to notice but a decision not to see. And v5 makes the consequence fall on the man **and his family**, and on everyone who followed him. The community that looks away is legislated for in the same breath as the offender.
- **v2 "the people of the land shall stone him"** — *am ha-arets*, and there is no court, no priest and no king in this sentence. Execution here is the work of the general population, and the chapter assigns it to them twice (vv2, 27). What the previous chapter asked of them was leaving grain in a field; this one asks this.
- **chapter 18 and chapter 20 are the same list twice** — the first time as prohibitions, the second time with what it costs. Almost every offense in 18 reappears here in the same order, now attached to a penalty, and a few drop out. A reader who wants to know what the Torah thought was worst does not get it from the prohibitions; the weighting is only visible here, where death, being cut off, bearing guilt and dying childless are handed out at different levels.
- **vv20–21 "they shall die childless"** — *aririm*, stripped or bare, used of a person with no descendants. It is a different kind of sentence from the ones around it: nobody executes it and no court is involved. For two of the offenses on this list the punishment is simply that the line ends.
- **v21 "it is an impurity"** — *niddah*, the same word used in chapter 15 for a woman's monthly separation, which is a **status** and involves no wrongdoing at all. Here it is used of an act. The word does not mean uncleanness of a moral kind in chapter 15 and does seem to in this verse, and the Hebrew uses one noun for both.
- **v23 "I was sick of them"** — *va-aquts bam*, from *quts*, a verb for loathing, the feeling of being unable to stand something any longer. It is the word used of the Egyptians when the Israelites multiplied (Exodus 1:12). This is God saying it about the previous occupants of Canaan, in the first person, with no legal vocabulary attached.
- **vv24–26 "separated"** — *badal* four times in three verses, and it is the verb of Genesis 1, where God separates light from darkness and waters from waters. The chapter's closing argument is built out of it: **I separated you** from the peoples (v24), so **you separate** clean animals from unclean (v25), from things **I separated out** for you (v25), because **I separated you** to be Mine (v26). The dietary law and the election of Israel are stated as one act, in one word, four times over. It is the same verb handed to the priesthood at 10:10 and used to close chapter 11.
- **v27 "who has a ghost or a spirit in them"** — the Hebrew says *ki yihyeh vahem*, **if there is in them**, which describes the medium as containing the thing rather than consulting it. The same two words, *ov* and *yidde'oni*, appeared at 19:31 as things not to turn to; here they are inside a person. The text never explains what either is.

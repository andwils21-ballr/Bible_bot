# Leviticus 3

## From the Herd

**1** If his offering is a peace offering — if he is bringing it from the herd, whether male or female, he shall bring it without defect before the LORD.

**2** He shall lean his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall dash the blood against the altar all around.

**3** He shall bring from the peace offering an offering by fire to the LORD: the fat covering the entrails and all the fat on the entrails,

**4** and the two kidneys with the fat on them at the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.

**5** The sons of Aaron shall turn it into smoke on the altar, on top of the burnt offering on the burning wood — an offering by fire, a soothing aroma to the LORD.

## From the Flock

**6** If his offering for a peace offering to the LORD is from the flock, male or female, he shall bring it without defect.

**7** If he is bringing a sheep as his offering, he shall bring it before the LORD,

**8** and he shall lean his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it before the tent of meeting, and the sons of Aaron shall dash its blood against the altar all around.

**9** He shall bring from the peace offering an offering by fire to the LORD: its fat — the whole fat tail, which he shall remove close to the backbone — and the fat covering the entrails and all the fat on the entrails,

**10** and the two kidneys with the fat on them at the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.

**11** The priest shall turn it into smoke on the altar — food, an offering by fire to the LORD.

## A Goat

**12** If his offering is a goat, he shall bring it before the LORD,

**13** and he shall lean his hand on its head and slaughter it before the tent of meeting, and the sons of Aaron shall dash its blood against the altar all around.

**14** He shall bring from it his offering, an offering by fire to the LORD: the fat covering the entrails and all the fat on the entrails,

**15** and the two kidneys with the fat on them at the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.

**16** The priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar — food, an offering by fire, for a soothing aroma. All fat belongs to the LORD.

**17** A lasting statute through your generations, in all your settlements: you shall eat no fat and no blood.

## Notes

- **v1 "peace offering"** — *zevach shelamim*, and the second word is built on the root behind *shalom*. That root is not primarily about the absence of conflict; it is about a thing being **whole, complete, paid up, settled**. So the name has been rendered *peace offering*, *offering of well-being*, and *fellowship offering*, and each catches part of it. What separates this sacrifice from the two before it is what happens to the meat: the burnt offering goes up entirely, the grain offering feeds the priests, and this one is the offering the worshipper and his household sit down and **eat** (7:15–16). The fat and the blood go to the altar; the rest is dinner.

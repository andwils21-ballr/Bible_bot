# Leviticus 8

## Washed and Dressed

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Take Aaron and his sons with him, and the garments, and the anointing oil, and the bull of the sin offering, and the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread,

**3** and assemble the whole congregation at the entrance of the tent of meeting."

**4** Moses did as the LORD commanded him, and the congregation assembled at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**5** Moses said to the congregation, "This is what the LORD has commanded to be done."

**6** Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them with water.

**7** He put the tunic on Aaron and tied the sash around him, and clothed him with the robe, and put the ephod on him, and tied the woven band of the ephod around him, fastening it to him.

**8** He set the breastpiece on him, and put the Urim and the Thummim into the breastpiece.

**9** He set the turban on his head, and set the gold plate, the holy crown, on the front of the turban, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**10** Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the dwelling and everything in it, and set them apart.

**11** He sprinkled some of it on the altar seven times, and anointed the altar and all its vessels, and the basin and its stand, to set them apart.

**12** He poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron's head and anointed him, to set him apart.

**13** Moses brought Aaron's sons forward and clothed them with tunics and tied sashes around them and bound caps on them, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## The Bull and the Two Rams

**14** He brought the bull of the sin offering, and Aaron and his sons leaned their hands on its head.

**15** It was slaughtered, and Moses took the blood and put it with his finger on the horns of the altar all around, and purified the altar; and he poured out the blood at the base of the altar. So he set the altar apart, to make atonement for it.

**16** He took all the fat that was on the entrails, and the lobe on the liver, and the two kidneys and their fat, and Moses turned them into smoke on the altar.

**17** He burned the bull — its hide, its flesh and its dung — in the fire outside the camp, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**18** He brought the ram of the burnt offering, and Aaron and his sons leaned their hands on the head of the ram.

**19** It was slaughtered, and Moses dashed the blood against the altar all around.

**20** He cut the ram into pieces, and Moses turned the head, the pieces and the suet into smoke.

**21** He washed the entrails and the legs with water, and Moses turned the whole ram into smoke on the altar. It was a burnt offering for a soothing aroma; it was an offering by fire to the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Blood on the Ear, the Thumb, the Toe

**22** He brought the second ram, the ram of ordination, and Aaron and his sons leaned their hands on the head of the ram.

**23** It was slaughtered, and Moses took some of its blood and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot.

**24** He brought Aaron's sons forward, and Moses put some of the blood on the lobe of their right ear, and on the thumb of their right hand, and on the big toe of their right foot. Moses dashed the blood against the altar all around.

**25** He took the fat — the fat tail and all the fat on the entrails, and the lobe on the liver, and the two kidneys and their fat — and the right thigh.

**26** From the basket of unleavened bread before the LORD he took one unleavened loaf, one loaf of oiled bread and one wafer, and put them on the fat and on the right thigh,

**27** and he put it all on Aaron's palms and on his sons' palms, and waved them before the LORD.

**28** Moses took them from their palms and turned them into smoke on the altar, on top of the burnt offering. They were an ordination offering for a soothing aroma; it was an offering by fire to the LORD.

**29** Moses took the breast and waved it before the LORD. It was Moses' share of the ram of ordination, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**30** Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood that was on the altar and sprinkled it on Aaron and his garments, and on his sons and their garments; so he set apart Aaron and his garments, and his sons and their garments with him.

## Seven Days at the Door

**31** Moses said to Aaron and to his sons, "Boil the flesh at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and eat it there with the bread that is in the basket of ordination, as I commanded: Aaron and his sons shall eat it.

**32** You shall burn in the fire whatever is left of the flesh and the bread.

**33** You shall not go out from the entrance of the tent of meeting for seven days, until the day your days of ordination are complete; for it will take seven days to fill your hand.

**34** The LORD commanded what has been done today, to make atonement for you.

**35** You shall stay at the entrance of the tent of meeting day and night for seven days, and keep the LORD's watch, so that you do not die; for so I was commanded."

**36** Aaron and his sons did everything the LORD had commanded through Moses.

## Notes

- **v8 the Urim and the Thummim** — they are put into the breastpiece here with no more explanation than Exodus 28:30 gave, and the Bible never provides one. The names are usually connected to *or*, light, and *tam*, complete or whole, and they are handled as objects: put **in**, and later carried, never described. What they did can be partly reconstructed from how they are used elsewhere — a question is asked and an answer comes back one way or the other, and at 1 Samuel 14:37 no answer comes back at all. This verse is the installation of a device whose operation the text assumes everyone knows.
- **v27 "on Aaron's palms"** — this is the idiom acted out. Ordination in Hebrew is *filling the hand*, and here Moses piles fat, the right thigh, a loaf, an oiled loaf and a wafer onto the palms of men who are standing there holding them. Then he takes it all back off and burns it. Their hands are filled with exactly what they will handle for the rest of their lives, and they do not keep any of it.

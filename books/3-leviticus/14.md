# Leviticus 14

## Outside the Camp, and the Two Birds

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "This shall be the instruction for the person with the blight on the day of his cleansing. He shall be brought to the priest,

**3** and the priest shall go outside the camp. The priest shall look. If the mark of blight has healed in the blighted man,

**4** the priest shall order that two live clean birds be taken for the one being cleansed, with cedar wood, scarlet yarn and hyssop.

**5** The priest shall order that one of the birds be slaughtered into a clay pot, over fresh running water.

**6** He shall take the live bird with the cedar wood, the scarlet yarn and the hyssop, and dip them all in the blood of the bird slaughtered over the fresh running water,

**7** and sprinkle it seven times on the one being cleansed from the blight, and declare him clean; and he shall let the live bird go free over the open field.

**8** The one being cleansed shall wash his clothes and shave off all his hair and bathe in water, and he is clean. After that he may enter the camp — but he shall stay outside his tent for seven days.

**9** On the seventh day he shall shave off all his hair — his head, his beard and his eyebrows, every hair. He shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and he is clean.

## The Eighth Day

**10** On the eighth day he shall take two male lambs without defect, and one ewe lamb a year old without defect, and three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, and one log of oil.

**11** The priest who does the cleansing shall set the man being cleansed, and these things, before the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**12** The priest shall take one of the lambs and present it as a guilt offering, with the log of oil, and wave them before the LORD.

**13** He shall slaughter the lamb in the place where the sin offering and the burnt offering are slaughtered, in the holy place — because the guilt offering, like the sin offering, belongs to the priest. It is most holy.

**14** The priest shall take some of the blood of the guilt offering and put it on the lobe of the right ear of the one being cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot.

**15** The priest shall take some of the log of oil and pour it into his own left palm,

**16** and dip his right finger in the oil on his left palm and sprinkle some of it with his finger seven times before the LORD.

**17** From the oil left in his palm the priest shall put some on the lobe of the right ear of the one being cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot — on top of the blood of the guilt offering.

**18** He shall put what is left of the oil in his palm on the head of the one being cleansed, and the priest shall make atonement for him before the LORD.

**19** The priest shall offer the sin offering and make atonement for the one being cleansed from his uncleanness, and after that he shall slaughter the burnt offering.

**20** The priest shall offer up the burnt offering and the grain offering on the altar, and make atonement for him, and he is clean.

## If He Cannot Afford It

**21** If he is poor and cannot afford so much, he shall take one lamb as a guilt offering to be waved, to make atonement for him, and one tenth of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, and a log of oil,

**22** and two turtledoves or two young pigeons, whichever he can afford — one shall be a sin offering and the other a burnt offering.

**23** On the eighth day he shall bring them for his cleansing to the priest, to the entrance of the tent of meeting, before the LORD.

**24** The priest shall take the lamb of the guilt offering and the log of oil, and wave them before the LORD.

**25** He shall slaughter the lamb of the guilt offering, and the priest shall take some of the blood of the guilt offering and put it on the lobe of the right ear of the one being cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot.

**26** The priest shall pour some of the oil into his own left palm,

**27** and with his right finger sprinkle some of the oil in his left palm seven times before the LORD.

**28** From the oil in his palm the priest shall put some on the lobe of the right ear of the one being cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot — on the place where the blood of the guilt offering was.

**29** He shall put what is left of the oil in his palm on the head of the one being cleansed, to make atonement for him before the LORD.

**30** He shall offer the turtledoves or the young pigeons, whichever he can afford —

**31** one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering, with the grain offering. The priest shall make atonement for the one being cleansed, before the LORD.

**32** This is the instruction for the person who has a mark of blight, who cannot afford the full offering for his cleansing."

## Blight in a House

**33** The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

**34** "When you enter the land of Canaan, which I am giving you as a holding, and I put a mark of blight in a house in the land you hold,

**35** the owner of the house shall come and tell the priest, 'Something like a mark has appeared in my house.'

**36** The priest shall order the house emptied before he goes in to look at the mark, so that nothing in the house becomes unclean. After that the priest shall go in to look at the house.

**37** He shall look at the mark. If the mark is in the walls of the house, in greenish or reddish hollows that look lower than the wall,

**38** the priest shall go out of the house to its doorway and shut it up for seven days.

**39** The priest shall come back on the seventh day and look. If the mark has spread in the walls of the house,

**40** the priest shall order that the stones with the mark in them be pulled out and thrown outside the city, to an unclean place.

**41** He shall have the house scraped all around inside, and they shall pour out the plaster they scraped off outside the city, to an unclean place.

**42** They shall take other stones and put them in place of the old ones, and he shall take fresh plaster and replaster the house.

**43** If the mark comes back and breaks out in the house after the stones have been pulled out, and after the house has been scraped and plastered,

**44** the priest shall come and look. If the mark has spread in the house, it is a malignant blight in the house. It is unclean.

**45** He shall tear down the house — its stones and its timbers and all the plaster of the house — and carry it outside the city, to an unclean place.

**46** Anyone who goes into the house during all the days it is shut up shall be unclean until evening.

**47** Anyone who sleeps in the house shall wash his clothes, and anyone who eats in the house shall wash his clothes.

**48** But if the priest comes and looks, and the mark has not spread in the house after the house was plastered, then the priest shall declare the house clean, because the mark has healed.

**49** To purify the house he shall take two birds, and cedar wood, and scarlet yarn, and hyssop.

**50** He shall slaughter one of the birds into a clay pot, over fresh running water,

**51** and take the cedar wood and the hyssop and the scarlet yarn and the live bird, and dip them in the blood of the slaughtered bird and in the fresh running water, and sprinkle the house seven times.

**52** He shall purify the house with the blood of the bird and with the fresh running water and with the live bird and with the cedar wood and with the hyssop and with the scarlet yarn.

**53** He shall let the live bird go free outside the city, over the open field, and make atonement for the house; and it is clean.

## This Is the Instruction

**54** This is the instruction for every mark of blight, and for a scaly patch,

**55** and for blight in a garment and in a house,

**56** and for a swelling and a scab and a bright spot,

**57** to teach when something is unclean and when it is clean. This is the instruction for blight.

## Notes

- **vv4–7 the two birds** — the first is killed into a clay pot held over fresh running water; the second is dipped in its blood and released over open country. Nothing is burned, nothing is eaten, nothing goes on the altar, and the whole thing happens outside the camp before the man is clean. It is not a sacrifice. The nearest thing to it in the Torah is the goat of chapter 16, sent away alive into the wilderness — and the verb is the same, *shillach*, **to send off**. One living creature carries something away and is not killed.
- **v34 "and I put a mark of blight in a house"** — the only place in these two chapters where a cause is given for any of this, and the cause is **God**, speaking in the first person, about houses that do not exist yet in a land they have not entered. The sentence sits in the middle of a procedure manual: the same paragraph that explains how to scrape plaster says who puts the blight in the wall. Chapter 13 refused to say where a mark on skin comes from. This one verse says, of walls only, that it comes from Him.

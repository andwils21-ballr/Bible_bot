# Leviticus 24

## The Lamp and the Bread

**1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Command the sons of Israel to bring you clear oil of beaten olives for the light, to set up a lamp continually.

**3** Outside the veil of the Testimony, in the tent of meeting, Aaron shall set it in order from evening until morning before the LORD continually. It is a lasting statute throughout your generations.

**4** On the pure lampstand he shall set the lamps in order before the LORD continually.

**5** And you shall take fine flour and bake twelve loaves from it. Each loaf shall be two tenths.

**6** And you shall set them in two rows, six to a row, on the pure table before the LORD.

**7** And you shall put pure frankincense on each row, and it shall be a memorial portion for the bread, an offering by fire to the LORD.

**8** Every sabbath day he shall set it in order before the LORD continually. It is from the sons of Israel, an everlasting covenant.

**9** And it shall be Aaron's and his sons', and they shall eat it in a holy place, because it is most holy to him, from the offerings by fire of the LORD — a lasting due."

## The Man Who Cursed

**10** And the son of an Israelite woman, whose father was an Egyptian man, went out among the sons of Israel. And the son of the Israelite woman and an Israelite man fought in the camp.

**11** And the son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the Name, and cursed. And they brought him to Moses. And his mother's name was Shelomith, daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.

**12** And they put him in custody until the answer should be made clear to them by the mouth of the LORD.

**13** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**14** "Take the one who cursed outside the camp, and let all who heard lay their hands on his head, and let all the congregation stone him.

**15** And speak to the sons of Israel, saying: Any man at all who curses his God shall carry his sin.

**16** And whoever blasphemes the name of the LORD shall surely be put to death. All the congregation shall surely stone him. The guest as well as the native, when he blasphemes the Name, shall be put to death.

**17** And a man who strikes down any human life shall surely be put to death.

**18** And whoever strikes down the life of an animal shall make it good, life for life.

**19** And a man who puts a blemish on his neighbor — as he has done, so it shall be done to him:

**20** break for break, eye for eye, tooth for tooth. As he has put a blemish on a person, so it shall be put on him.

**21** And whoever strikes down an animal shall make it good, and whoever strikes down a human shall be put to death.

**22** You shall have one law, for the guest and for the native alike, because I am the LORD your God."

**23** And Moses spoke to the sons of Israel, and they took the one who had cursed outside the camp and stoned him with stones. And the sons of Israel did as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Notes

- **vv2–8 "continually"** — *tamid*, four times in seven verses: the lamp burns *continually* (vv2, 3, 4) and the bread is set out *continually* (v8). Exodus 27:20 gave the lamp command to Moses; here the people are told to supply the oil. The light and the bread before God are never to lapse, and Israel keeps them going.
- **vv5–9 the twelve loaves** — this is the bread Exodus 25:30 calls *the bread of the Presence*, *lechem panim*, bread of the face. It is set out every sabbath and eaten afterward by the priests *in a holy place*. It is the bread David is given at Nob when he is fleeing Saul (1 Samuel 21:7, *the bread of the Presence*), and the bread Jesus points to when the Pharisees object to his disciples picking grain on the sabbath (Matthew 12:4).
- **v8 "from the sons of Israel, an everlasting covenant"** — *berit olam*, the phrase of the covenant with Noah (Genesis 9:16) and with Abraham (Genesis 17:7). The weekly bread is counted as one of the covenant's standing signs.
- **v10 "whose father was an Egyptian man"** — the only story in Leviticus outside the priests' ordination (chapters 8–10), and it turns on a man of mixed birth, the kind Exodus 12:38 calls the *great mixed crowd* that came out with Israel. His mother is named; he is not.
- **v11 "blasphemed the Name"** — *va-yiqqov*, from *naqav*. The verb means to pierce, and also to mark out by name: the men *who were **designated** by name* in Numbers 1:17. Balaam uses it for cursing: *how can I **curse** whom God has not cursed?* (Numbers 23:8). **The Name**, *ha-shem*, stands alone, without *of the LORD*, the first time in the Bible that God's name is called simply *the Name*. Verse 16 then gives it in full.
- **v14 "lay their hands on his head"** — *ve-samkhu*, the verb of the offerer laying his hand on the animal's head (1:4), and of Aaron laying both hands on the goat for Azazel (16:21). Those who heard the curse put their hands on the man who spoke it before he is put to death.
- **vv19–20 "a blemish"** — *mum*, the word of the last three chapters: the **blemish** that keeps a priest from the altar (21:17) and an animal off it (22:20). Here it is the injury one man does to another. *Eye for eye, tooth for tooth* is the formula of Exodus 21:24. Here it sits inside a law stated twice to be the same for the foreigner and the native (vv16, 22).
- **v23 "they did as the LORD had commanded"** — the sentence that closes the building of the tabernacle (Exodus 39–40). It closes this story too. The law of vv15–22 comes out of a case that happened.

# Leviticus 24

## The Lamp and the Bread

**1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**2** "Command the sons of Israel to bring you clear oil of beaten olives for the light, to set up a lamp continually.

**3** Outside the veil of the Testimony, in the tent of meeting, Aaron shall set it in order from evening until morning before the LORD continually. It is a lasting statute throughout your generations.

**4** On the pure lampstand he shall set the lamps in order before the LORD continually.

**5** You shall take fine flour and bake twelve loaves from it. Each loaf shall be two tenths.

**6** You shall set them in two rows, six to a row, on the pure table before the LORD.

**7** You shall put pure frankincense on each row, and it shall be a memorial portion for the bread, an offering by fire to the LORD.

**8** Every sabbath day he shall set it in order before the LORD continually. It is from the sons of Israel, an everlasting covenant.

**9** It shall be Aaron's and his sons', and they shall eat it in a holy place, because it is most holy to him, from the LORD's offerings by fire — a lasting due."

## The Man Who Cursed

**10** The son of an Israelite woman, whose father was an Egyptian man, went out among the sons of Israel. The son of the Israelite woman and an Israelite man fought in the camp.

**11** The son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the Name, and cursed. They brought him to Moses. His mother's name was Shelomith, daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.

**12** They put him in custody until the LORD's decision should be made clear to them.

**13** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

**14** "Take the one who cursed outside the camp, and let all who heard lay their hands on his head, and let all the congregation stone him.

**15** Speak to the sons of Israel, saying: Any man at all who curses his God shall carry his sin.

**16** Whoever blasphemes the name of the LORD shall surely be put to death. All the congregation shall surely stone him. The guest as well as the native, when he blasphemes the Name, shall be put to death.

**17** A man who strikes down any human life shall surely be put to death.

**18** Whoever strikes down the life of an animal shall make it good, life for life.

**19** A man who puts a blemish on his neighbor — as he has done, so it shall be done to him:

**20** break for break, eye for eye, tooth for tooth. As he has put a blemish on a person, so it shall be put on him.

**21** Whoever strikes down an animal shall make it good, and whoever strikes down a human shall be put to death.

**22** You shall have one law, for the guest and for the native alike, because I am the LORD your God."

**23** Moses spoke to the sons of Israel, and they took the one who had cursed outside the camp and stoned him. The sons of Israel did as the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Notes

- **v11 "blasphemed the Name"** — *va-yiqqov*, from *naqav*. The verb means to pierce, and also to mark out by name: the men *who were **designated** by name* in Numbers 1:17. Balaam uses it for cursing: *How can I **put a curse on** one God has not cursed?* (Numbers 23:8). **The Name**, *ha-shem*, stands alone, without *of the LORD*, the first time in the Bible that God's name is called simply *the Name*. Verse 16 then gives it in full.

# 2 Thessalonians 2

**1** Now about the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our being gathered together to Him, we ask you, brothers,

**2** not to be quickly shaken in mind or alarmed, whether by a spirit or by a word or by a letter seeming to be from us, saying that the day of the Lord has already come.

**3** Let no one deceive you in any way. For that day will not come unless the falling away comes first, and the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of destruction,

**4** who opposes and exalts himself above every so-called god or object of worship, so that he takes his seat in the temple of God, proclaiming himself to be God.

**5** Do you not remember that when I was still with you I told you these things?

**6** You know what is restraining him now, so that he may be revealed in his own time.

**7** For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work; only he who now restrains it will do so until he is taken out of the way.

**8** Then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus will kill with the breath of His mouth and bring to nothing by the appearing of His coming.

**9** The coming of the lawless one is by the working of Satan, with every kind of power and false signs and wonders,

**10** and with every kind of wicked deception for those who are perishing, because they did not receive the love of the truth so as to be saved.

**11** For this reason God sends them a working of delusion, so that they believe the lie,

**12** so that all may be judged who did not believe the truth but took pleasure in wickedness.

## Stand Firm

**13** But we ought always to thank God for you, brothers loved by the Lord, because God chose you as firstfruits for salvation, through the holiness the Spirit gives and through belief in the truth.

**14** To this He called you through our gospel, so that you may obtain the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**15** So then, brothers, stand firm and hold to the traditions you were taught by us, whether by word of mouth or by our letter.

**16** Now may our Lord Jesus Christ Himself, and God our Father, who loved us and gave us eternal comfort and good hope by grace,

**17** comfort your hearts and strengthen them in every good work and word.

## Notes

- **vv4, 8 "exalts himself above every so-called god… whom the Lord Jesus will kill with the breath of His mouth"** — two prophecies. The king of Daniel's last vision *will exalt himself and magnify himself above every god* (Daniel 11:36). And the shoot of Jesse *with the breath of his lips… will kill the wicked* (Isaiah 11:4), in the Greek *en pneumati… anelei*, the verb Paul uses here, *anelei*. What Isaiah said of the coming king, Paul says of the Lord Jesus.
- **vv6–7 "what is restraining him… he who now restrains it"** — *to katechon*, a thing, and *ho katechōn*, a person. The Thessalonians *know* what it is, and Paul does not say. The letter gives no more than that something, and someone, holds the lawless one back until a set time.

# Ecclesiastes 8

**1** Who is like the wise man? Who knows the meaning of a thing? A man's wisdom makes his face shine, and the hardness of his face is changed.

**2** As for me: keep the king's command, because of the oath of God.

**3** Do not be hasty to go from his presence; do not take your stand in an evil cause, for he does whatever he pleases.

**4** Since the word of a king is power, who may say to him, "What are you doing?"

**5** Whoever keeps the commandment will know no evil thing, and the heart of a wise man knows the time and the way.

**6** For there is a time and a way for every matter, though the evil of man lies heavy on him.

**7** For he does not know what will be, for who can tell him how it will be?

**8** No man has power over the breath to hold back the breath, and there is no power over the day of death; there is no discharge in war, and wickedness will not rescue its masters.

**9** All this I saw as I applied my heart to all the work that is done under the sun, a time when man has power over man to his hurt.

**10** Then I saw the wicked buried. They used to come and go from the holy place, and they were forgotten in the city where they had done such things. This also is vanity.

**11** Because the sentence against an evil deed is not carried out quickly, the heart of the children of man is fully set in them to do evil.

**12** Though a sinner does evil a hundred times and lives long, yet I know that it will be well with those who fear God, who fear before Him.

**13** But it will not be well with the wicked, nor will he prolong his days like a shadow, because he does not fear before God.

**14** There is a vanity that is done on the earth: there are righteous people to whom it happens according to the deeds of the wicked, and there are wicked people to whom it happens according to the deeds of the righteous. I said that this also is vanity.

**15** So I commended joy, for man has nothing better under the sun than to eat and drink and be glad, and this will go with him in his toil through the days of his life that God has given him under the sun.

**16** When I applied my heart to know wisdom and to see the business that is done on earth — for neither by day nor by night do one's eyes see sleep —

**17** then I saw all the work of God, that man cannot find out the work that is done under the sun. However much man toils in seeking, he will not find it out; even if the wise man claims to know, he cannot find it out.

## Notes

- **v2 "As for me"** — *ani*, "I," stands alone at the head of the verse; many supply "I say." "The oath of God" — an oath of loyalty to the king sworn before God (2 Kings 11:17; 1 Chronicles 29:24).
- **v8 "the breath"** — *ruach*, breath or wind or spirit; beside "the day of death" it is likely the breath of life (3:19; 12:7). "No discharge in war" — no one is released from this battle (compare the releases of Deuteronomy 20:5–8).
- **v10** — the Hebrew is hard. "Forgotten" is *yishtakkechu*; the Greek reads "praised" (*epēnethēsan*, as from Hebrew *yishtabbechu*): the wicked were honored in the city where they had done such things.

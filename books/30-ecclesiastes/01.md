# Ecclesiastes 1

**1** The words of the Preacher, the son of David, king in Jerusalem.

**2** "Vanity of vanities," says the Preacher, "vanity of vanities! All is vanity."

**3** What does a man gain from all his toil at which he toils under the sun?

**4** A generation goes, and a generation comes, but the earth remains forever.

**5** The sun rises, and the sun goes down, and pants back to its place, where it rises.

**6** The wind goes to the south and turns around to the north; around and around goes the wind, and on its rounds the wind returns.

**7** All the streams run to the sea, but the sea is not full; to the place where the streams flow, there they flow again.

**8** All things are full of weariness; no one can utter it. The eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear filled with hearing.

**9** What has been is what will be, and what has been done is what will be done, and there is nothing new under the sun.

**10** Is there a thing of which it is said, "See, this is new"? It has been already, in the ages before us.

**11** There is no remembrance of those who came before, and of those who are yet to come there will be no remembrance among those who come after them.

**12** I the Preacher have been king over Israel in Jerusalem.

**13** I set my heart to seek and to search out by wisdom all that is done under heaven. It is an unhappy business that God has given to the children of man to be busy with.

**14** I have seen all the works that are done under the sun, and indeed, all is vanity and a chasing after wind.

**15** What is crooked cannot be made straight, and what is lacking cannot be counted.

**16** I said in my heart, "I have grown great and added wisdom beyond all who were over Jerusalem before me, and my heart has seen much wisdom and knowledge."

**17** So I set my heart to know wisdom and to know madness and folly. I came to know that this also is a chasing after wind.

**18** For in much wisdom is much vexation, and he who increases knowledge increases sorrow.

## Notes

- **v1 "the Preacher"** — *Qohelet*, "one who gathers," the speaker to an assembly (*qahal*); the Greek *Ekklesiastes* gives the book its name. "Son of David, king in Jerusalem" points to Solomon, though the book never names him.
- **v2 "Vanity"** — *hevel*, the word that was Abel's name (Genesis 4:2). There it names a man; here it is a word in the sentence.
- **v2 "Vanity of vanities"** — the Hebrew way of saying "the utmost vanity," like "holy of holies." *Hevel* is literally a breath or vapor, and it is "vanity" every time in this book.
- **v3 "under the sun"** — the book's phrase for life as seen from this world, about thirty times.
- **vv4–7** — the sun, the wind and the streams go round and round and arrive nowhere.
- **v5 "pants back to its place"** — *sho'ef*, panting like a runner.
- **v14 "a chasing after wind"** — *re'ut ruach*, wind, not spirit; the phrase returns through chapter 6. In v17 it is *ra'yon ruach*, the same sense.
- **v15 "What is crooked cannot be made straight"** — as in 7:13.

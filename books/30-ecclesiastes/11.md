# Ecclesiastes 11

**1** Send your bread out on the waters, for after many days you will find it.

**2** Give a portion to seven, or even to eight, for you do not know what disaster may happen on the earth.

**3** If the clouds are full of rain, they empty it on the earth; and if a tree falls to the south or to the north, in the place where the tree falls, there it will lie.

**4** He who watches the wind will not sow, and he who looks at the clouds will not reap.

**5** As you do not know the way of the wind, or of the bones in the womb of a woman with child, so you do not know the work of God, who makes everything.

**6** In the morning sow your seed, and in the evening do not withhold your hand, for you do not know which will prosper, this or that, or whether both alike will be good.

**7** Light is sweet, and it is good for the eyes to see the sun.

**8** So if a man lives many years, let him rejoice in them all, and let him remember the days of darkness, for they will be many. All that comes is vanity.

**9** Rejoice, young man, in your youth, and let your heart cheer you in the days of your youth. Walk in the ways of your heart and in the sight of your eyes, but know that for all these things God will bring you into judgment.

**10** Remove vexation from your heart, and put away evil from your flesh, for youth and the dawn of life are vanity.

## Notes

- **v1 "Send your bread out on the waters"** — bread sent by sea in trade, or given freely with no thought of return; either way it comes back "after many days."
- **v2 "to seven, or even to eight"** — the counting pattern of Micah 5:5 and the numerical sayings of Reproof 6 (Proverbs 30): a full number, and then one more.
- **v5 "the way of the wind"** — *ruach*, wind or breath: the same word for the wind no one can track and the breath of life that comes into the child in the womb. Jesus sets wind and Spirit side by side in the same way in John 3:8.
- **v6 "do not withhold your hand"** — the same words as 7:18.
- **v8 "the days of darkness"** — the days after death (Job 10:21–22), or the hard days of old age that 12:1–2 describes.
- **v9 "Walk in the ways of your heart and in the sight of your eyes"** — Numbers 15:39 warns Israel not to follow heart and eyes; here the young man may, *but know that God will bring you into judgment*. The Greek reads "walk in the ways blameless, and not in the sight of your eyes."
- **v10 "the dawn of life"** — *shacharut*, found only here: from *shachar*, "dawn," or from *shachor*, "black," the black hair of youth.

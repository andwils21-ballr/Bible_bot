# Jubilees 50

**1** After this law I made known to you the days of the Sabbaths in the wilderness of Sin, which is between Elim and Sinai.

**2** I told you of the Sabbaths of the land on Mount Sinai, and of the jubilee years in the Sabbaths of years; but its year we have not told you, until you enter the land that you are to possess.

**3** The land too will keep its Sabbaths while they live on it, and they will know the year of jubilee.

**4** For this reason I have ordained for you the weeks of years and the jubilees: forty-nine jubilees from the days of Adam until this day, and one week and two years; and there are yet forty years to come for learning the commandments of the Lord, until they cross over into the land of Canaan, crossing the Jordan to the west.

**5** The jubilees will pass by until Israel is cleansed from all the guilt of fornication and uncleanness and defilement and sin and error, and lives in all the land in safety; and there will no longer be any Satan or any evil one, and the land will be clean from that time for all days.

**6** The commandment of the Sabbaths too I have written for you, and all the judgments of its laws.

**7** Six days you shall do work, and on the seventh day is the Sabbath of the Lord your God; you shall not do any work on it — you and your sons, and your male servants and your female servants, and all your cattle, and the stranger who is with you.

**8** The man who does any work on it shall die: every man who profanes that day, who lies with a woman, or who speaks of any work on it — that he will set out on a journey on it — or of any buying or selling; and whoever draws water on it that he had not prepared for himself on the sixth day; and whoever lifts any burden to carry it out of his tent or out of his house shall die.

**9** You shall not do any work on the Sabbath but what you have prepared for yourselves on the sixth day, to eat and to drink, and to rest and keep Sabbath from all work on that day, and to bless the Lord your God, who has given you a day of festival and a holy day; and a day of the holy kingdom for all Israel is this day among their days, forever.

**10** For great is the honor the Lord has given Israel: to eat and drink and be satisfied on this festival day, and to rest on it from all the work that belongs to the work of the sons of men, except burning incense and bringing offerings and sacrifices before the Lord, for the days and for the Sabbaths.

**11** This work alone shall be done on the Sabbath days in the sanctuary of the Lord your God: to atone for Israel with offering continually, day after day, for a memorial acceptable before the Lord, and that He may receive them forever, day after day, as you have been commanded.

**12** Every man who does work, or goes on a journey, or works a farm, whether in his house or in any place, and whoever lights a fire, or rides on any beast, or travels by ship on the sea, and whoever strikes or kills anything, or slaughters a beast or a bird, or whoever catches an animal or a bird or a fish, or whoever fasts or makes war on the Sabbaths —

**13** the man who does any of these things on the Sabbath day shall die, so that the sons of Israel keep the Sabbaths according to the commandments of the Sabbaths of the land, as it is written on the tablets that He gave into my hands, that I should write out for you the laws of the seasons, and the seasons according to the division of their days. Here is completed the account of the division of the days.

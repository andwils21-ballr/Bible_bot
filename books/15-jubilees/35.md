# Jubilees 35

**1** In the first year of the first week of the forty-fifth jubilee Rebekah called Jacob her son and commanded him about his father and about his brother, that he should honor them all the days of Jacob's life.

**2** Jacob said, "I will do everything as you have commanded me, for this thing will be honor and greatness to me, and righteousness before the Lord, that I should honor them.

**3** You too, mother, know from the day I was born until this day all my deeds and all that is in my heart, that all the days I think good toward all.

**4** How should I not do this thing that you have commanded me, to honor my father and my brothers?

**5** Tell me, mother, what perversity you have seen in me, and I will turn away from it, and mercy will be on me."

**6** She said to him, "My son, in all my days I have seen in you no perverse deed at all, but only upright ones. Yet I will tell you the truth, my son: I will die this year, and I will not live through this year[; for I have seen in a dream the day of my death, that I would not live beyond a hundred and fifty-five years; and here — I have completed all the days of my life that I am to live]."

**7** Jacob laughed at his mother's words, because his mother had told him that she would die, and she was sitting opposite him with her strength on her, and she had not grown weak in her strength; for she went in and out and could see, and her teeth were strong, and no sickness had touched her all the days of her life.

**8** Jacob said to her, "Blessed am I, mother, if my days come near to the days of your life, and my strength stays with me as your strength does; and you will not die, for you are speaking with me idly about your death."

**9** She went in to Isaac and said to him, "One request I ask of you: make Esau swear that he will not harm Jacob, and will not pursue him with hatred[; for you know Esau's mind, that it is evil from his youth, and there is nothing upright in him; for he wants to kill him after your death].

**10** You know all he has done from the day Jacob his brother went to Harran until this day, how with all his heart he has forsaken us[, and has done evil to us; your flocks he has taken for himself, and carried off all your possessions from before your face].

**11** When we begged and asked him for what was ours, he acted like a man who was doing us a kindness.

**12** He is bitter against you because you blessed Jacob your whole and upright son, for there is no evil in him, only goodness[; and since he came from Harran until this day he has not robbed us of anything, for he brings us everything in its season, always, and rejoices with all his heart when we take from his hands, and he blesses us; and he has not parted from us since he came from Harran until this day, and he stays with us always at home, honoring us]."

**13** Isaac said to her, "I too know and see the deeds of Jacob who is with us, how with all his heart he honors us[; but before, I loved Esau more than Jacob, because he was the firstborn; but now I love Jacob more than Esau, for he has done many evil deeds, and there is no righteousness in him, for all his ways are unrighteousness and violence, and there is no righteousness around him].

**14** Now my heart is troubled because of his deeds, and neither he nor his seed is to be saved, for they are those who will be destroyed from the earth and who will be rooted out from under heaven[; for he has forsaken the God of Abraham and gone after his wives and after their uncleanness and after their error, he and his children].

**15** You ask me to make him swear that he will not kill Jacob his brother[; even if he swears, he will not keep his oath, and he will not do good but only evil].

**16** But if he wants to kill Jacob his brother, he will be given into Jacob's hand, and he will not escape from his hand, for he will fall into his hand.

**17** Do not fear for Jacob, for Jacob's guardian is great and mighty and honored, and more praised than Esau's guardian."

**18** Rebekah sent and called Esau, and he came to her, and she said to him, "I have a request, my son, that I ask of you; say that you will do it, my son."

**19** He said, "I will do everything you tell me, and I will not refuse your request."

**20** She said to him, "I ask of you that on the day I die you will bring me in and bury me near Sarah, your father's mother; and that you and Jacob will love each other, and neither will seek evil for his brother, but only love; and so you will prosper, my sons, and be honored in the middle of the land, and no enemy will rejoice over you, and you will be a blessing and a mercy in the eyes of all who love you."

**21** He said, "I will do all that you have told me, and I will bury you on the day you die near Sarah, my father's mother, as you have wished, that her bones may be near your bones.

**22** Jacob my brother I too will love above all flesh, for I have no brother in all the earth but him alone[; and it is no great merit for me if I love him, for he is my brother, and we were sown together in your body, and together we came out of your womb; and if I do not love my brother, whom shall I love?]

**23** I myself ask you to urge Jacob concerning me and my sons, for I know that he will surely reign over me and over my sons[; for on the day my father blessed him, he made him the higher and me the lower].

**24** I swear to you that I will love him, and will not seek evil for him all the days of my life, but only good."

**25** [He swore to her about all this.] She called Jacob before Esau's eyes and commanded him according to the words she had spoken with Esau.

**26** He said, "I will do your will. Believe me that no evil will come from me or from my sons against Esau; I will go before him in nothing but love."

**27** They ate and drank, she and her sons, that night[; and she died, three jubilees and one week and one year old, on that night; and her two sons, Esau and Jacob, buried her in the double cave near Sarah, their father's mother].

## Notes

- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. In v9 the first part of the bracket follows the Latin, which covers this chapter from v3 to v12 in broken form; everything else in brackets follows Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts: the end of v9 and vv6, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 22, 23, 25 and 27. **v6** — the Latin covers the verse but is broken at the point of Rebekah's age (*… fifty years am I completing*); the bracket follows Charles. Without v27's bracket, the Ge'ez never tells of Rebekah's death.
- **v1 the date** — the first year of the forty-fifth jubilee is 44 × 49 + 1 = 2157. **v27 "three jubilees and one week and one year"** — 147 + 7 + 1 = 155, the age of her dream in v6.
- **vv1–8 Rebekah and Jacob** — *Honor your father and your mother, so that your days may be long* (Exodus 20:12). **v7 "Jacob laughed"** — she is still strong and her teeth are sound; he takes her foreknowledge of death for a jest.
- **vv9–17 Rebekah and Isaac** — Isaac at last says that he loves Jacob more than Esau (v13, in Charles's text), reversing Genesis 25:28, *Isaac loved Esau*. **v17 "Jacob's guardian… Esau's guardian"** — each brother has a heavenly guardian. The Greek sums up the chapter: *Rebecca asked Isaac in his old age to exhort Esau and Jacob to love one another*.
- **vv18–26 Rebekah and Esau** — Esau swears to love Jacob. **v23 "he will surely reign over me"** — the oracle at the twins' birth: *One people will be stronger than the other, and the elder shall serve the younger* (Genesis 25:23).
- **v27 the double cave** — Genesis does not tell of Rebekah's death; Jacob names her grave at his own: *There they buried Isaac and Rebekah his wife* (Genesis 49:31).

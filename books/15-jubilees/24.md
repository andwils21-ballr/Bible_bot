# Jubilees 24

**1** And it came to pass after Abraham died that the Lord blessed Isaac his son; and he rose from Hebron and went and lived at the Well of the Vision, in the first year of the third week of this jubilee, seven years.

**2** In the fourth week a famine came on the land, besides the first famine that was in the days of Abraham.

**3** Jacob cooked a stew of lentils, and Esau came in from the field hungry, and said to Jacob his brother, "Give me some of this red stew." Jacob said to him, "Hand over to me your birthright, the right of the firstborn, and I will give you bread, and some of this stew too."

**4** Esau said in his heart, "I am going to die. What is this birthright to me?" He said to Jacob, "I give it to you."

**5** Jacob said to him, "Swear to me today," and he swore to him.

**6** Jacob gave his brother Esau bread and stew, and he ate until he was full, and Esau despised the birthright. For this reason his name was called Esau and Edom, because of the red stew that Jacob gave him for his birthright.

**7** Jacob grew great, and Esau was brought down from his greatness.

**8** The famine was on the land, and Isaac set out to go down to Egypt, in the second year of this week, and went to the king of the Philistines, to Gerar, to Abimelech.

**9** The Lord appeared to him and said to him, "Do not go down to Egypt. Settle in the land that I tell you of, and live as a guest in that land, and I will be with you and bless you.

**10** For to you and to your seed I will give all this land, and I will raise up the oath that I swore to Abraham your father, and I will multiply your seed as the stars of heaven, and I will give to your seed all this land.

**11** In your seed all the nations of the earth will be blessed, because your father listened to My voice and kept My charge, and My commandments and My laws and My ordinances and My covenant; and now listen to My voice and My command, and live in this land."

**12** He lived in Gerar three weeks of years.

**13** Abimelech gave a command about him and about all that was his, saying, "Any man who touches him or anything that is his shall surely die."

**14** Isaac grew great among the Philistines, and he had many possessions: cattle and sheep and camels and donkeys, and many possessions.

**15** They sowed in the land of the Philistines, and reaped a hundredfold; and Isaac became very great, and the Philistines envied him.

**16** All the wells that Abraham's servants had dug in Abraham's lifetime the Philistines had stopped up after Abraham's death, and filled them with earth.

**17** Abimelech said to Isaac, "Go from us, for you have become much greater than we." Isaac went from there in the first year of the seventh week, and moved to the valleys of Gerar.

**18** They went back and dug the wells of water that the servants of Abraham his father had dug, which the Philistines had stopped up after the death of Abraham his father; and he called them by the names his father had called them.

**19** Isaac's servants dug wells in the valley and found living water, and the shepherds of Gerar quarreled with Isaac's shepherds, saying, "This water is ours." Isaac called the name of the well Hardship, because they were hard with us.

**20** They dug another well, and they quarreled over that one too, and he called its name Narrow. He moved on, and they dug another well, and they did not quarrel over it, and he called its name Room; and Isaac said, "Now the Lord has made room for us, and we have grown great in the land."

**21** He went up from there to the Well of the Oath, in the first year of the first week of the forty-fourth jubilee.

**22** The Lord appeared to him that night, on the new moon of the first month, and said to him, "I am the God of Abraham your father. Do not fear, for I am with you, and I will bless you, and I will surely multiply your seed as the sand of the earth, for the sake of Abraham My servant."

**23** He built an altar there, the one that Abraham his father had first built, and he called on the name of the Lord and offered sacrifice to the God of Abraham his father.

**24** They dug a well and found living water.

**25** Isaac's servants dug another well and did not find water; and they came and told Isaac that they had not found water, and Isaac said, "Because I swore an oath this day to the Philistines, this thing has happened to us."

**26** He called the name of that place the Well of the Oath, for there he had sworn to Abimelech, and to Ahuzzath his companion, and to Phicol the commander of his army.

**27** Isaac knew that day that he had sworn to them under constraint, to make peace with them.

**28** Isaac cursed the Philistines that day and said, "Cursed be the Philistines for the day of wrath and indignation, from among all the nations; may God make them a mockery and a curse, for wrath and indignation, in the hands of sinful nations and in the hand of the Kittim, who will root them out.

**29** Whoever escapes the sword of the enemy and the Kittim, may the righteous nation root out in judgment from under heaven; for they will be enemies and foes to my sons in their days on the earth.

**30** No remnant will be left to them, nor one who is saved on the day of the wrath of judgment; but they will be for destruction and extermination, so that all the seed of the Philistines may be removed from the face of the earth, and no name at all will remain to them on the earth.

**31** For even if he goes up to heaven, from there he will come down; and even if he makes himself strong on the earth, from there he will be dragged out; and even if he hides among the nations, from there he will be rooted out; and even if he goes down into Sheol, there his judgment will be great, and there will be no peace for him there.

**32** If he goes into captivity, by the hands of those who seek his life they will kill him on the way, and neither name nor seed will be left to him in all the earth; for he will go into an eternal curse."

**33** So it is written and engraved concerning him on the tablets of heaven, to be done to him on the day of judgment, so that he may be rooted out of the earth.

## Notes

- **vv3, 10, 11, 15, 17, 18, 19, 23, 25, 28, 30, 31, 32** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter from v13 on, has most of them too. Without the end of v3 Jacob never asks for the birthright.
- **v1 "the Well of the Vision"** — Genesis 25:11: *Isaac settled by Beer-lahai-roi*, the well Hagar named, *the Well of the Living One who sees me*.
- **vv1–21 the dates** — this jubilee is the forty-fourth, the jubilee of Abraham's death (22:1). Its third week begins in 2122, and the first year of its seventh week (v17) is 2150. Then v21 has Isaac come to the Well of the Oath *in the first year of the first week of the forty-fourth jubilee*, 2108, before all of these. Charles's English has *forty-fourth* too. The text is left as it stands. **v2 "In the fourth week"** — the EOTC has *in the year of the fourth week*, with no number; Charles has *the first year*.
- **vv3–7 the birthright** — Genesis 25:29–34: *Let me gulp down some of that red, that red there… Therefore his name was called Edom… Jacob gave Esau bread and a boiling of lentils… Esau despised the birthright.* **"red"** — in vv3 and 6 the EOTC has *sərnāy*, wheat, where Charles has *red*; the name *Edom* (v6) is explained by the red stew, so the English follows Charles and the note says so. **v7 "Jacob grew great, and Esau was brought down"** — Jubilees' own summary; Charles has *Jacob became the elder*.
- **vv8–13 Isaac in Gerar** — Genesis 26:1–11. **v11 "your father listened to My voice and kept My charge"** — Genesis 26:5: *because Abraham listened to My voice, and kept My charge: My commands, My statutes, and My instructions.* Jubilees leaves out Genesis 26:7–11, where Isaac says of Rebekah *She is my sister*; only Abimelech's order is kept (v13).
- **vv16–26 the wells** — Genesis 26:15–33. The names differ: Genesis has *Esek* (contention), *Sitnah* (enmity) and *Rehoboth* (room). **v19 "Hardship"** — the Latin; Charles has *Perversity*. **v20 "Narrow"** — the EOTC's *Ṣabāb*; Charles has *Enmity*. **"Room"** — the EOTC's *Səfuḥ*, from the same word as *made room*, as in Genesis 26:22, *For now the LORD has made room for us*. **v25 "Because I swore an oath this day to the Philistines, this thing has happened to us"** — the Latin; in Genesis 26:32–33 the servants find water and the place is named Beersheba. In Jubilees the dry well is a sign against the oath Isaac made under constraint (v27).
- **vv28–33 the curse on the Philistines** — **"the Kittim"** — the name Daniel gives the ships sent against the king of the north: *ships of Kittim shall come against him* (Daniel 11:30). **v31 "even if he goes up to heaven, from there he will come down"** — Amos 9:2: *If they dig into Sheol, from there My hand shall take them; and if they go up to the heavens, from there I will bring them down.* Obadiah 1:4: *If you build high like the eagle, and if you set your nest among the stars, from there I will bring you down.*

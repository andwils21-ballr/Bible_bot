# Jubilees 44

**1** Israel set out from Harran, from his house, on the new moon of the third month, and went by the way of the Well of the Oath, and offered a sacrifice to the God of his father Isaac on the seventh of this month.

**2** Jacob remembered the dream he had seen at Bethel, and was afraid to go down into Egypt.

**3** He was thinking of sending to Joseph to come to him, so that he himself would not go down; and he stayed there seven days, in case he might see a vision about whether he should stay or go down.

**4** He kept the festival of the harvest, the first fruits of wheat, with old wheat, for in all the land of Canaan there was not a handful of seed in the land; for the famine was on all the beasts and the cattle and the birds, and on men too.

**5** On the sixteenth the Lord appeared to him and said to him, "Jacob, Jacob"; and he said, "Here I am." He said to him, "I am the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham and Isaac. Do not be afraid to go down into Egypt, for I will make you a great nation there.

**6** I will go down with you, and I will bring you up, and in this land you will be buried, and Joseph will put his hand on your eyes. Do not be afraid; go down into Egypt."

**7** His sons and his sons' sons rose, and set their father and their goods on wagons.

**8** Israel set out from the Well of the Oath on the sixteenth day of this third month, and went to the land of Egypt.

**9** Israel sent Judah his son ahead of him to Joseph, to look over the land of Goshen; for Joseph had told his brothers to come and live there, so that they would be near him.

**10** It was the best in the land of Egypt, and near to him, for all of them and for the cattle too.

**11** These are the names of the sons of Jacob who went into Egypt with Jacob their father.

**12** Reuben, Israel's firstborn; and these are the names of his sons: Hanoch and Pallu and Hezron and Carmi — five.

**13** Simeon and his sons; and these are the names of his sons: Jemuel and Jamin and Ohad and Jachin and Zohar, and Shaul the son of Finisawet — seven.

**14** Levi and his sons; and these are the names of his sons: Gershon and Kohath and Merari — four.

**15** Judah and his sons; and these are the names of his sons: Shelah and Perez and Zerah — four.

**16** Issachar and his sons; and these are the names of his sons: Tola and Puvah and Jashub and Shimron — five.

**17** Zebulun and his sons; and these are the names of his sons: Sered and Elon and Jahleel — four.

**18** These are the sons of Jacob and their sons, whom Leah bore to Jacob in Mesopotamia, six, and their one sister Dinah; and all the souls of the sons of Leah and their sons who went with Jacob their father into Egypt were twenty-nine, and with Jacob their father, thirty.

**19** The sons of Zilpah, the maid of Leah, Jacob's wife, whom she bore to Jacob: Gad and Asher.

**20** These are the names of their sons who went with him into Egypt. The sons of Gad: Ziphion and Haggi and Shuni and Ezbon and Eri and Areli and Arodi — eight.

**21** The sons of Asher: Imnah and Ishvah and Ishvi and Beriah, and Serah their one sister — five.

**22** All the souls were fourteen, and all of Leah's were forty-four.

**23** The sons of Rachel, Jacob's wife: Joseph and Benjamin.

**24** There were born to Joseph in Egypt, before his father came into Egypt, whom Asenath daughter of Poti-phera, priest of On, bore to him: Manasseh and Ephraim.

**25** The sons of Benjamin: Bela and Becher and Ashbel, Gera and Naaman, Ehi and Rosh, Muppim and Huppim and Ard — eleven.

**26** All the souls of Rachel were fourteen.

**27** The sons of Bilhah, the maid of Rachel, Jacob's wife, whom she bore to Jacob: Dan and Naphtali.

**28** These are the names of their sons who went with them into Egypt. The sons of Dan: Hushim and Samon and Asudi and Iyaka and Salomon — six.

**29** They died in Egypt in the year they came in, and there was left to Dan Hushim alone.

**30** These are the names of the sons of Naphtali: Jahzeel and Guni and Jezer and Shillem and Ew.

**31** Ew, who was born after the years of famine, died in Egypt.

**32** All those of Rachel were twenty-six.

**33** All the souls of Jacob that went into Egypt were seventy souls. These are his children and his children's children, seventy in all; but five died in Egypt before Joseph, and had no children.

**34** In the land of Canaan two sons of Judah died, Er and Onan, and they had no children; and the sons of Israel buried those who perished, and they were set among the seventy nations.

## Notes

- **vv3, 5, 18, 33** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63). **v18 "six"** — so the EOTC; the Asmara printing reads *seven*.
- **v1 "from Harran"** — so the EOTC, and Charles has *Haran*; but Jacob had been living at Hebron, in the tower of Abraham (36:20; 37:14). **vv1–8 the dates** — Jacob leaves on the new moon of the third month and sacrifices on the seventh; he keeps the festival of first fruits (v4), the Festival of Weeks of the middle of the third month (6:17–21; 15:1); the Lord appears to him on the sixteenth, and he sets out the same day.
- **vv2–6 the vision at the Well of the Oath** — Genesis 46:1–4: *Do not be afraid to go down to Egypt, for I will make you a great nation there. I Myself will go down with you to Egypt, and I Myself will surely bring you up again. Joseph will set his hand on your eyes.* **v2 "the dream he had seen at Bethel"** — the seven tablets, and the word *in Egypt you will die* (32:21–23).
- **v9 Judah sent ahead** — Genesis 46:28: *He sent Judah ahead of him to Joseph, to point the way before him to Goshen.*
- **vv11–34 the seventy souls** — Genesis 46:8–27, with the names as in this project's Genesis where the Ge'ez has the same person. The Ge'ez differs from Genesis in these places:
  - **v13 "Shaul the son of Finisawet"** — Genesis 46:10 has *Shaul son of the Canaanite woman*; Charles has *the Zephathite woman*.
  - **v15** — Er and Onan are not counted here but set apart in v34.
  - **v16 "Jashub"** — so Numbers 26:24; Genesis 46:13 has *Job*.
  - **vv20–21** — the EOTC puts *Eri* and *Ishvi* in square brackets of its own; they are rendered as plain text. **v21 "five"** — Charles has *six*.
  - **v24** — the EOTC writes *Heliopolis*, the Greek name of On (Genesis 41:45).
  - **v28 "six"** — the Ge'ez lists five names under that count. Genesis 46:23 gives Dan one son, *Hushim*; v29 explains that the others died the year they came in.
  - **v30 "Ew"**, **v31** — a fifth son of Naphtali, not in Genesis 46:24, who died in Egypt.
  - **v33 "seventy souls"** — Genesis 46:27: *All the souls of the house of Jacob coming to Egypt: seventy.*
  - **v34 "set among the seventy nations"** — Er and Onan, who died childless in Canaan, are not counted in Israel's seventy; Charles has *reckoned among the seventy Gentile nations*.

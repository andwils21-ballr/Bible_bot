# Jubilees 34

**1** In the sixth year of this week of this forty-fourth jubilee[, Jacob sent his sons to pasture his sheep, and his servants with them, to the field of Shechem].

**2** The seven kings of the Amorites gathered against them, hiding under the trees, to kill them and to carry off their cattle as plunder.

**3** Jacob and Levi and Judah and Joseph were in the house with Isaac their father, for his spirit was grieved, and they could not leave him; and Benjamin was the youngest, and for this reason he stayed with him.

**4** There came the kings of Taphu, and the kings of Ares, and the kings of Seragan, and the kings of Selo, and the king of Gaaz, and the king of Beth-horon, and the king of Maanisakir, and all who live in this mountain country, who live in the forest of the land of Canaan.

**5** They told Jacob, saying, "Here — the kings of the Amorites have surrounded your sons, and their flocks have been plundered."

**6** He rose from his house, he and his three sons and all his father's servants and his own servants, and went against them with six thousand men who carried swords.

**7** He killed them in the pastures of Shechem, and pursued those who fled, and killed them with the edge of the sword[; and he killed Ares and Taphu and Seragan and Selo and Maanisakir and Gaaz].

**8** He gathered in his flocks[; and he prevailed over them and laid tribute on them, that they should pay him tribute, five fruits of their land; and he built Robel and Tamnatares].

**9** He returned in peace[, and made peace with them, and they became his servants until the day that he and his sons went down into Egypt].

**10** In the seventh year of this week he sent Joseph [from his house to the land of Shechem, to learn how his brothers were, and he found them in the land of Dothan].

**11** They dealt treacherously with him and made a plan against him to kill him[; but changing their minds, they sold him to Ishmaelite merchants, and they brought him down into Egypt and sold him to Potiphar, the eunuch of Pharaoh, the chief of the cooks, the priest of the city of Elew].

**12** The sons of Jacob slaughtered a kid of the goats, and dipped Joseph's garment in its blood, and sent it to Jacob their father on the tenth of the seventh month.

**13** He mourned all that night, for they had brought it to him in the evening, and he became feverish with mourning for his death, and he said, "A fierce beast has devoured Joseph"; and all the people of his house mourned with him that day, and they were grieving and mourning with him all that day.

**14** His sons and his daughter rose to comfort him, and he would not be comforted for his son.

**15** On that day Bilhah heard that Joseph had perished, and she died mourning for him; she was living in Qafratef. Dinah his daughter also died after Joseph had perished. These three mournings came upon Israel in one month.

**16** They buried Bilhah opposite the tomb of Rachel, and Dinah his daughter they also buried there.

**17** He mourned for Joseph one year and did not cease, for he said, "Let me go down to the grave mourning for my son."

**18** For this reason it is ordained for the sons of Israel that they should afflict themselves on the tenth of the seventh month — on the day the news came to Jacob his father that made him weep for Joseph — to make atonement for themselves on it with a kid of the goats[, on the tenth of the seventh month, once a year, for their sins; for they had grieved the affection of their father over Joseph his son].

**19** This day has been ordained, that they should grieve on it for their sins, and for all their transgressions, and for all their errors, so that they may cleanse themselves on this day once a year.

**20** After Joseph perished, the sons of Jacob took wives for themselves[. The name of Reuben's wife is Ada; and the name of Simeon's wife is Adlbaa, a Canaanite; and the name of Levi's wife is Melka, of the daughters of Aram, of the seed of the sons of Terah; and the name of Judah's wife, Betasuel, a Canaanite; and the name of Issachar's wife, Hezaqa; and the name of Zebulun's wife, Niiman; and the name of Dan's wife, Egla; and the name of Naphtali's wife, Rasuu, of Mesopotamia; and the name of Gad's wife, Maka; and the name of Asher's wife, Ijona; and the name of Joseph's wife, Asenath the Egyptian; and the name of Benjamin's wife, Ijasaka].

**21** Simeon repented, and took a second wife from Mesopotamia, like his brothers.

## Notes

- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. In v1 they follow the Latin, which covers this chapter as far as v5; in vv7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 18 and 20 they follow Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts. Without the brackets, the Ge'ez never says where Jacob's sons were, never tells how Joseph came to Egypt, and never names the wives.
- **vv1–9 the war with the Amorite kings** — not in Genesis. Genesis 48:22 has Jacob speak of *one shoulder above your brothers, which I took from the hand of the Amorite with my sword and with my bow*; this war tells how. **v4 the kings** — the Ge'ez names are given as it writes them; the Latin reads *Shiloh* for *Selo* and *Gaash* for *Gaaz*. **v1 the date** — the sixth year of this week, the sixth of the forty-fourth jubilee, is 2148.
- **vv10–17 Joseph sold** — Genesis 37. **v10 the date** — the seventh year of this week is 2149; Joseph, born in 2134 (28:24), is fifteen. Genesis 37:2 has *Joseph, a son of seventeen years*. **v11 "the chief of the cooks"** — Genesis 37:36 has *chief of the slaughterers*. **v12** — Genesis 37:31: *They took Joseph's coat and slaughtered a male goat and dipped the coat in the blood.* **vv14, 17** — Genesis 37:35: *All his sons and all his daughters rose to comfort him, and he refused to be comforted, and said, "For I will go down to my son, mourning, to Sheol."* **v15 Bilhah and Dinah** — their deaths are not in Genesis.
- **vv18–19 the Day of Atonement** — Leviticus 16:29–30: *in the seventh month, on the tenth of the month, you shall afflict yourselves… because on this day atonement shall be made for you, to cleanse you.* Jubilees roots the day in the tenth of the seventh month, when Jacob's sons brought him the goat-bloodied coat: the kid of the goats that deceived their father becomes the kid of the goats of atonement.
- **vv20–21 the wives** — **"Betasuel, a Canaanite"** — Genesis 38:2: *Judah saw there the daughter of a Canaanite man whose name was Shua*; *bat-Shua*, "daughter of Shua," has become her name. **"Asenath the Egyptian"** — Genesis 41:45. **v21 "Simeon repented"** — Genesis 46:10 names *Shaul son of the Canaanite woman* among Simeon's sons.

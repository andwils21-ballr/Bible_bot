# Jubilees 40

**1** In those days Pharaoh dreamed two dreams in one night, about the famine that was to come on all the land; and he woke from his sleep and called all the interpreters of dreams who were in Egypt, and the magicians, and told them his two dreams, and they could not understand them.

**2** Then the chief cupbearer remembered Joseph and spoke of him to the king; and they brought him out of the prison, and he told his two dreams before him.

**3** He said before Pharaoh that his two dreams were one: "Seven years of abundance will come throughout all the land of Egypt, and after them seven years of famine, such as has not been throughout all the land.

**4** Now let Pharaoh appoint overseers in all the land of Egypt, and let them store up food in every city in the days of the years of plenty, and there will be food for the seven years of famine, and the land will not perish before the famine, for it will be very severe."

**5** The Lord gave Joseph favor and mercy in Pharaoh's eyes, and Pharaoh said to his servants, "We will not find a man as wise and understanding as this man, for the spirit of the Lord is with him."

**6** He appointed him second in all his kingdom, and gave him authority over all Egypt, and had him ride in Pharaoh's second chariot.

**7** He clothed him in garments of fine linen, and put a gold chain on his neck; and they proclaimed before him, "Elel and Habirel," and he put a ring on his hand, and set him over all his house, and made him great, and said, "Only in my throne will I be greater than you."

**8** Joseph ruled over all the land of Egypt, and all Pharaoh's princes and all his servants and all who did the king's work loved him, because he walked in truth, and there was no pride or arrogance in him; and he had no respect of persons and did not accept gifts, but he judged all the people of the land in uprightness.

**9** The land of Egypt was at peace before Pharaoh because of Joseph, for the Lord was with him, and gave him favor and mercy for all his kindred before all who knew him and who heard news of him; and Pharaoh's kingdom was upright, and there was no Satan and no evil one.

**10** The king called Joseph's name Zaphenath-paneah, and gave Joseph as a wife the daughter of Potiphar, the daughter of the priest of On, the chief of the cooks.

**11** On the day Joseph stood before Pharaoh he was thirty years old, when he stood before Pharaoh.

**12** In that year Isaac died. As Joseph had said in the interpretation of his two dreams, as he had told him, there were seven years of plenty over all the land of Egypt, and the land bore abundantly, one measure yielding eighteen hundred measures.

**13** Joseph gathered food in every city, until it was full of grain, until they could not count it or measure it for its abundance.

## Notes

- **vv3, 7, 8, 10** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter as far as v8, has vv3–8 too. Joseph's words begin at the end of v3 and go on in v4, which is in the EOTC.
- **vv1–13 Joseph before Pharaoh** — Genesis 41, retold in brief. **v5 "for the spirit of the Lord is with him"** — Genesis 41:38: *Shall we find one like this, a man in whom is the spirit of God?* **vv6–7** — Genesis 41:42–43: *Pharaoh took off his signet ring… and clothed him in garments of fine linen, and put the gold collar on his neck. He had him ride in the second chariot… and they called out before him, "Abrek!"* **"Elel and Habirel"** — the Latin's form of the cry; Charles's text has *El El wa Abirer*. **"Only in my throne will I be greater than you"** — Genesis 41:40. **v8 "he had no respect of persons and did not accept gifts"** — the marks of a just judge, not in Genesis.
- **v9 "there was no Satan and no evil one"** — the words used of the last days in 23:29, here of Egypt under Joseph.
- **v10 "Zaphenath-paneah"** — Genesis 41:45; the EOTC writes *Sefānṭifānəs*. **"the daughter of Potiphar, the daughter of the priest of On"** — Genesis 41:45 has *Asenath daughter of Poti-phera, priest of On*; Charles's text makes Joseph's father-in-law the same man as his master Potiphar. Charles writes *Heliopolis*, the Greek name for On.
- **v11 "thirty years old"** — Genesis 41:46. **v12 "In that year Isaac died"** — by this book's dates Isaac died in 2162 (36:1, 18) and Joseph was born in 2134 (28:24), which makes him twenty-eight, not thirty. The text is left as it stands. **"one measure yielding eighteen hundred measures"** — Genesis 41:47 has *the land produced by handfuls*. **v13** — Genesis 41:49: *until he stopped counting, for there was no number.*

# Jubilees 14

**1** After these things, in the fourth year of this week, on the new moon of the third month, the word of the Lord came to Abram in a dream, saying, "Do not fear, Abram. I am your defender, and your reward will be very great."

**2** He said, "Lord, Lord, what will You give me, when I am going childless, and the son of Maseq, my maidservant, the Damascene Eliezer — he will inherit me? To me You have given no seed. Give me seed."

**3** He said to him, "This one will not inherit you; the one who comes out of your own body, he will inherit you."

**4** He brought him outside and said to him, "Look toward heaven and count the stars, if you are able to count them."

**5** He looked at the heavens and saw the stars, and He said to him, "So shall your seed be."

**6** He trusted the Lord, and it was counted to him as righteousness.

**7** He said to him, "I am the Lord who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans, to give you the land of the Canaanites to hold forever, and to be God to you and to your seed after you."

**8** He said, "Lord, Lord, how shall I know that I will inherit it?"

**9** He said to him, "Take for Me a heifer three years old, and a goat three years old, and a sheep three years old, and a turtledove and a pigeon."

**10** He took all these in the middle of the month, while he was living at the oak of Mamre, which is near Hebron.

**11** He built an altar there and slaughtered all these, and poured their blood on the altar, and divided them down the middle and laid each half opposite the other; but the birds he did not divide.

**12** Birds came down on the pieces, and Abram drove them away and did not let the birds touch them.

**13** When the sun had set, a trance fell on Abram, and a great terror of darkness fell on him. It was said to Abram, "Know for certain that your seed will be strangers in a foreign land, and they will enslave them and afflict them four hundred years.

**14** The people whom they serve I will judge, and after that they will come out from there with great possessions.

**15** You will go to your fathers in peace and be buried in a good old age.

**16** In the fourth generation they will return here, for the sin of the Amorites is not yet complete."

**17** He woke from sleep and rose, and the sun had set, and there was a flame; a furnace was smoking, and a flame of fire passed between the pieces.

**18** On that day the Lord made a covenant with Abram, saying, "To your seed I will give this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the river Euphrates: the Kenites and the Kenizzites and the Kadmonites and the Perizzites and the Rephaim and the Phakorites and the Hivites and the Amorites and the Canaanites and the Girgashites and the Jebusites."

**19** The day passed, and he offered them up: the pieces and the birds, and their grain offerings and their drink offerings, and the fire consumed them.

**20** That night we made a covenant with Abram, like the covenant we had made in this same month with Noah; and Abram renewed the festival and the ordinance for himself forever.

**21** Abram rejoiced and told all this to Sarai his wife; and he believed that he would have seed, but she did not bear.

**22** Sarai advised Abram her husband and said to him, "Go in to Hagar, my Egyptian maid; perhaps I will build up seed for you from her."

**23** Abram listened to the voice of Sarai his wife and said to her, "Do so." Sarai took Hagar her maid, the Egyptian, and gave her to Abram her husband to be his wife.

**24** He went in to her, and she conceived and bore a son, and he called his name Ishmael, in the fifth year of this week; this was the eighty-sixth year of Abram's life.

## Notes

- **v14** — the end of the verse is rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the EOTC text as held here breaks off before it. **vv18, 19, 20, 21, 23, 24** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from Charles's edition of the Ge'ez (1895); the Asmara file held here lacks 14:16–16:13. Without the end of v24 the EOTC never names Ishmael.
- **vv1–18 the covenant between the pieces** — Genesis 15, retold almost verse for verse: *Do not fear, Abram… count the stars, if you are able to count them… So shall your seed be* (15:1, 5). Jubilees dates it: the dream on the new moon of the third month (v1), the sacrifice *in the middle of the month* (v10), the time of the Festival of Weeks, the day of Noah's covenant (6:17). That is why the angel says in v20, *like the covenant we had made in this same month with Noah*.
- **v6 "He trusted the Lord, and it was counted to him as righteousness"** — Genesis 15:6, the verse Paul builds on: *Abraham believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness* (Romans 4:3). James quotes it too (James 2:23).
- **v2 "Give me seed"** — the EOTC adds these words to Abram's complaint; Genesis has *to me You have given no seed* (Genesis 15:3). **"the son of Maseq, my maidservant"** — Genesis 15:2 has *the son of the possession of my house*, *ben-mesheq*; Jubilees reads the word as the name of Eliezer's mother.
- **v13 "a trance fell on Abram, and a great terror of darkness"** — Genesis 15:12: *a deep sleep fell upon Abram, and a dread, a darkness, a great one*. **"four hundred years"** — Genesis 15:13; Paul reckons the law as coming *four hundred and thirty years* after the promise (Galatians 3:17).
- **v18 "the Phakorites"** — a people Genesis 15:19–21 does not list.
- **vv22–24 Hagar** — Genesis 16:1–4, 15–16. **"the eighty-sixth year of Abram's life"** — Charles's figure, the same as Genesis 16:16, *Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael*. By the book's own dates the fifth year of this week (the forty-first jubilee, first week) is 1965, and Abram was born in 1876 (11:15), which makes him 89. The figure is left as the Ge'ez gives it.

# Jubilees 39

**1** Jacob lived in the land where his father had lived as a guest, the land of Canaan.

**2** These are the generations of Jacob. Joseph was seventeen years old when they took him down into the land of Egypt, after his brothers sold him, and Potiphar, a eunuch of Pharaoh, the chief of the cooks, bought him.

**3** He set Joseph over all his house; and the blessing of the Lord came on the Egyptian's house because of Joseph, and the Lord prospered him in all that he did.

**4** The Egyptian left everything in Joseph's charge, for he saw that the Lord was with him, and the Lord prospered all that he did.

**5** Joseph was beautiful to look at, and very lovely to look at; and his master's wife lifted her eyes and saw Joseph, and she loved him and begged him to lie with her.

**6** He did not give up his soul, and he remembered the Lord and the words that Jacob his father used to read from the words of Abraham: that no man should commit fornication with a woman who has a husband; that for him the judgment of death has been ordained in heaven before God Most High, and the sin will be written against him in the eternal books, always, before the Lord.

**7** Joseph remembered this word and would not lie with her.

**8** She begged him for one year and a second, and he refused to listen.

**9** She embraced him and held him fast in the house, to force him to lie with her; and she shut the door of her house and held him openly; but he escaped from her hands, and left his garment in her hands, and broke the door, and fled outside from her presence.

**10** The woman saw that he would not lie with her, and she slandered him before his master, saying, "Your Hebrew servant, whom you love, wanted to force me, to lie with me; and it came to pass, when I lifted up my voice, that he fled and left his garment in my hands when I held him, and he broke through the door."

**11** The Egyptian saw Joseph's garment and the broken door, and heard the words of his wife, and put Joseph into the prison, into the place where the king's prisoners were kept.

**12** He was there in the prison; and the Lord gave him favor in the sight of the chief of the prison guards, and mercy before him, because he saw that the Lord was with him, and that all Joseph did, the Lord prospered.

**13** He left everything in his charge, and the chief of the prison guards knew nothing of what was with him, for Joseph did everything and the Lord completed it; and he stayed there two years.

**14** In those days Pharaoh king of Egypt was angry with his two eunuchs, the chief cupbearer and the chief baker, and put them in custody in the house of the chief of the cooks, in the prison where Joseph was held.

**15** The chief of the prison guards appointed Joseph to serve them, and he served before them.

**16** They both dreamed a dream, the chief cupbearer and the chief baker, and they told it to Joseph.

**17** As he interpreted to them, so it happened to them: Pharaoh restored the chief cupbearer to his office, and hanged the chief baker, just as Joseph had told them.

**18** The chief cupbearer forgot Joseph in the prison, though he had told him what would happen to him, and did not remember to tell Pharaoh what Joseph had told him, for he forgot.

## Notes

- **vv2, 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 17** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter from v9 on, has most of vv9–17 too. Without the end of v5 the EOTC never says who wanted Joseph, and without the end of v6 it never says what he remembered.
- **vv1–13 Joseph and Potiphar's wife** — Genesis 39, retold in brief: *He left all he had in Joseph's hand… Joseph was beautiful of form and beautiful of appearance* (39:6). **v2 "seventeen years old"** — as Genesis 37:2, *Joseph, a son of seventeen years*; by this book's own dates (34:10) he was fifteen. **v6 "the words that Jacob his father used to read from the words of Abraham"** — in Genesis Joseph refuses because he will not wrong his master or *sin against God* (39:9); in Jubilees he remembers the law against adultery, handed down in writing from Abraham, with its penalty written in heaven. **v8 "one year and a second"** — so the Ge'ez; Charles has *a year*.
- **vv12–13 the prison** — Genesis 39:21–23: *The LORD was with Joseph… The keeper of the round house did not look at anything in his hand, because the LORD was with him.* **"two years"** — Jubilees counts two years in prison before the dreams; Genesis 41:1 counts *two full years* after them.
- **vv14–18 the dreams** — Genesis 40, in four verses. **v18** — Genesis 40:23: *The chief of the cupbearers did not remember Joseph, and he forgot him.*

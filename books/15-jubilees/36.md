# Jubilees 36

**1** In the sixth year of this week Isaac called his two sons, Esau and Jacob, and they came to him, and he said to them, "My sons, I am going the way of my fathers, to the eternal house where my fathers are.

**2** Bury me near Abraham my father, in the double cave in the field of Ephron the Hittite, which Abraham acquired to bury in; in the grave that I dug for myself there, there bury me.

**3** This I command you, my sons: do righteousness and uprightness on the earth, so that the Lord may bring on you all that the Lord said He would do for Abraham and his seed.

**4** Be, my sons, loving your brothers among yourselves as a man loves his own soul, and let each seek what is good for his brother, and act together on the earth, and let them love each other as their own souls.

**5** About idols, I charge you to despise them and hate them and not love them, for they are full of error for those who serve them and those who bow down to them.

**6** Remember, my sons, the Lord, the God of Abraham your father, and how I too worshiped Him and served Him in righteousness and in joy, so that He may multiply you and make your seed grow as the stars of heaven in number, and plant you on the earth as a plant of righteousness that will not be rooted out, for all the eternal generations.

**7** Now I will make you swear a great oath — for there is no oath greater than it — by the name glorious and honored and great, splendid and wonderful and mighty, who made the heavens and the earth and all things together[, that you will fear Him and worship Him];

**8** and that each will love his brother with mercy and righteousness, and no man will want evil for his brother, from now on and forever, all the days of your life, so that you may prosper in all your deeds and not perish.

**9** If any of you seeks evil for his brother, know from now on that everyone who seeks evil for his brother will fall into his hand and be rooted out of the land of the living, and his seed will perish from under heaven.

**10** On the day of turmoil and curse and wrath and anger, with devouring fire, as He burned Sodom, so He will burn his land and his city and all that is his[; and he will be blotted out of the book of the instruction of the sons of men, and not be written in the book of life, but in the one appointed for destruction, and he will go into an eternal curse, so that their condemnation may be always renewed, in hate and in curse and in wrath, in torment and in indignation, in plagues and in sickness, forever].

**11** I tell you and testify to you, my sons, according to the judgment that will come on the man who wants to do harm to his brother."

**12** He divided all his possessions between the two of them that day, and gave the larger portion to the man who was born first, and the tower and all that was around it[, and all that Abraham had acquired at the Well of the Oath].

**13** He said, "This larger portion I will give to the man who was born first."

**14** Esau said, "I sold it to Jacob and gave my birthright to Jacob; let it be given to him, and I have not a single word to say about it, for it is his."

**15** Isaac said, "May a blessing rest on you, my sons, and on your seed this day, for you have given me rest, and my heart is not grieved over the birthright, that you might do wickedness on account of it.

**16** May the Lord Most High bless the man who does righteousness, him and his seed forever."

**17** He finished commanding them and blessing them, and they ate and drank together before him, and he rejoiced because there was one mind between them; and they went out from him and rested that day and slept.

**18** Isaac slept on his bed that day rejoicing, and he slept the sleep of eternity[, and died one hundred and eighty years old. He completed twenty-five weeks and five years; and his two sons, Esau and Jacob, buried him].

**19** Esau went to the land of Edom, to Mount Seir, and lived there.

**20** Jacob lived on the mountain of Hebron, in the tower of the land where his father Abraham had lived as a guest[, and he served God with all his heart and with all his soul, and according to the visible commandments, according to the division of the times of his generations].

**21** Leah his wife died in the fourth year of the second week of the forty-fifth jubilee, and he buried her in the double cave near Rebekah his mother, to the left of the grave of Sarah, his father's mother.

**22** All her sons and his sons came to mourn Leah his wife with him and to comfort him over her, for he was lamenting her.

**23** For he loved her very much from the time Rachel her sister died, for she was whole and upright in all her ways, and honored Jacob [all the days he lived with her; and he did not hear a harsh word from her mouth, for she was quiet and peaceable and upright and honorable].

**24** He remembered all the deeds she had done in her life, and lamented her greatly, for he loved her with all his heart and with all his soul.

## Notes

- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. In vv20 and 23 they follow the Latin, which covers this chapter from v20 on (in v23 the Latin has a gap, and Charles supplies *upright*); in vv7, 10, 12 and 18 they follow Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts. Without v18's bracket the Ge'ez never says that Isaac died or that his sons buried him.
- **vv1–11 Isaac's charge** — **v2 "in the grave that I dug for myself"** — the words Jacob will use of his own grave: *In my grave that I dug for myself in the land of Canaan, there you shall bury me* (Genesis 50:5). **v4 "as a man loves his own soul"** — Leviticus 19:18: *you shall love your neighbor as yourself.* **v9 "will fall into his hand"** — the Greek has it as a prophecy: *if Esau rises up against Jacob, he will fall into his hands*; it comes true in chapters 37–38.
- **vv12–16 the birthright** — Isaac means to give the larger share to the firstborn, and Esau gives it up: *I sold it to Jacob* (Genesis 25:33, *he sold his birthright to Jacob*).
- **v18 Isaac's death** — Genesis 35:28–29: *The days of Isaac were a hundred years and eighty years. Isaac breathed his last and died… Esau and Jacob his sons buried him.* **"twenty-five weeks and five years"** — 175 + 5 = 180. **The date** — the sixth year of this week, the first of the forty-fifth jubilee, is 2162; by this book's date for his birth, 1987 (16:15), Isaac is 175. The text is left as it stands.
- **vv21–24 Leah** — the fourth year of the second week is 2167. Genesis says only *There I buried Leah* (Genesis 49:31); Jubilees gives Jacob's grief and her praise.

# Jubilees 47

**1** In the seventh week, in the seventh year, in the forty-seventh jubilee, your father came from the land of Canaan; and you were born in the fourth week, in its sixth year, in the forty-eighth jubilee, which was the time of distress on the sons of Israel.

**2** Pharaoh king of Egypt gave a command about them, that they should throw their children, every male that was born, into the river.

**3** They went on throwing them in for seven months, until the day you were born. Your mother hid you for three months, but they informed on her.

**4** She made you an ark, and smeared it with pitch and asphalt, and set it in the grass by the bank of the river, and put you in it seven days; and your mother would come by night and nurse you, and by day your sister Miriam guarded you from the birds.

**5** In those days Tharmuth, Pharaoh's daughter, came out to bathe in the river, and she heard your voice as you cried, and told her Hebrew women to bring you, and they brought you to her.

**6** She took you out of the ark and had compassion on you.

**7** Your sister said to her, "Shall I go and call you one of the Hebrew women, to nurse and suckle this child for you?" [She said to her, "Go."]

**8** She went and called your mother, Jochebed, and she gave her wages, and she nursed you.

**9** After that, when you had grown, they brought you to Pharaoh's house, and you became her son; and Amram your father taught you writing; and when you had completed three weeks, they brought you into the royal court.

**10** You were in the court three weeks of years, until the time when you went out from the royal court, and saw an Egyptian striking your companion, of the sons of Israel; and you killed him and hid him in the sand.

**11** On the second day you found two of the sons of Israel fighting, and you said to the one doing wrong, "Why do you strike your brother?"

**12** He was angry and indignant, and said, "Who made you a prince and a judge over us? Do you want to kill me as you killed the Egyptian?" You were afraid, and fled because of this word.

## Notes

- **vv3, 4, 10** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter, has them too. Without the end of v10 the EOTC never tells of the Egyptian that Moses killed, though v12 speaks of him. **Words in [brackets]** (v7) are in no Ge'ez text here; they follow the Latin.
- **v1 the dates** — Amram comes from Canaan in the seventh year of the seventh week of the forty-seventh jubilee, 46 × 49 + 6 × 7 + 7 = 2303 (compare 46:10). Moses is born in the sixth year of the fourth week of the forty-eighth jubilee, 47 × 49 + 3 × 7 + 6 = 2330. Three weeks later he enters the royal court (v9), 2351, and three weeks after that he leaves it (v10), 2372, at forty-two; Stephen says Moses was *forty years old* when he went out to his brothers (Acts 7:23).
- **vv2–8 the ark on the river** — Exodus 1:22–2:9: *she hid him three months. She could hide him no longer, and she took for him a basket of papyrus and daubed it with tar and with resin… His sister said to Pharaoh's daughter, "Shall I go and call you a nursing woman from the Hebrews?"* **v3 "seven months"** — the Greek has *only ten months*. **v4** — Jubilees adds the seven days in the reeds, the mother nursing by night, and Miriam keeping off the birds by day. **v5 "Tharmuth"** — Pharaoh's daughter is not named in Exodus; the Latin and Greek have *Thermuthis*. **"her Hebrew women"** — so the Ge'ez; Charles has *her maidens*, and Exodus 2:5 *her young women*. **v8 "Jochebed"** — named in Exodus 6:20.
- **v9 "Amram your father taught you writing"** — Moses learns to read and write from his own father, not only from the Egyptians. Acts 7:22: *Moses was instructed in all the wisdom of the Egyptians.*
- **vv10–12 the Egyptian** — Exodus 2:11–15: *He saw an Egyptian man striking a Hebrew man… and he struck down the Egyptian and hid him in the sand… Who made you a chief and a judge over us? Are you saying you will kill me as you killed the Egyptian?… Moses fled from before Pharaoh.*

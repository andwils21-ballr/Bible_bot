# Jubilees 20

**1** In the forty-second jubilee, in the first year of the seventh week, Abraham called Ishmael and his twelve sons, and Isaac and his two sons, and the six sons of Keturah and their sons.

**2** He commanded them to keep the way of the Lord, to do righteousness, and to love each one his neighbor, and to be so in every strife, each walking toward the others so as to do justice and righteousness on the earth;

**3** that they should circumcise their sons according to the covenant He had made with them, and not turn aside to the right or the left from all the ways the Lord commanded us; and that we should keep ourselves from all fornication and uncleanness, and put away from among us all uncleanness and fornication.

**4** If any woman or girl among you commits fornication, burn her with fire; and let them not go whoring after their eyes and their hearts, and let them not take wives for themselves from the daughters of Canaan, for the seed of Canaan will be uprooted from the land.

**5** He told them of the judgment of the giants and the judgment of Sodom, how they were judged for their wickedness, and died for fornication and uncleanness and corruption among themselves through fornication.

**6** "You too, keep yourselves from all fornication and uncleanness, and from all the defilement of sin, so that you do not make our name a curse, and all your life a hissing, and all your sons a ruin by the sword, and become cursed like Sodom, and all your remnant like the sons of Gomorrah.

**7** I testify to you, my sons: love the God of heaven and cling to all His commandments, and do not go after their idols and after their uncleanness.

**8** Do not make for yourselves molten gods or carved images, for they are empty, and there is no spirit in them, for they are the work of hands, and all who trust in them trust in nothing. Do not serve them, and do not bow down to them.

**9** But serve the Most High God and bow down to Him always, and hope for His face at all times, and do righteousness and uprightness before Him, so that He may be pleased with you and give you His mercy and send down rain for you morning and evening, and bless all your works that you do on the earth, and bless your bread and your water, and bless the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your land, the herds of your cattle and the flocks of your sheep.

**10** You will be a blessing on the earth, and all the nations of the earth will desire you, and they will bless your sons in my name, so that they may be a blessing as I am."

**11** He gave gifts to Ishmael and his sons and to the sons of Keturah, and sent them away from Isaac his son, and he gave everything to Isaac his son.

**12** Ishmael and his sons, and the sons of Keturah and their sons, went together and lived from Paran to the entrance of Babylon, in all the land toward the east, facing the desert.

**13** These mixed with one another, and their name was called Arabs and Ishmaelites[, to this day].

## Notes

- **vv8, 9, 10, 13** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which begins partway through v5, has them too. **Words in [brackets]** (v13) are in no Ge'ez text here; they follow the Latin.
- **v1 the date** — the first year of the seventh week of the forty-second jubilee is 41 × 49 + 6 × 7 + 1 = 2052. By the book's date for Abraham's birth (1876, 11:15) he is 176; Genesis 25:7 gives him *a hundred years and seventy years and five years* in all. His death comes in chapter 23.
- **v2 "to keep the way of the Lord, to do righteousness"** — Genesis 18:19: *he may charge his sons and his house after him, and they shall keep the way of the LORD, to do righteousness and judgment*. Jubilees shows him doing it. **"in every strife"** — the Ge'ez has *ṣabʾ*, war or strife; Charles reads *among all men*.
- **v4 "burn her with fire"** — the law of Leviticus 21:9 for a priest's daughter who whores: *She shall be burned in the fire*. **"go whoring after their eyes and their hearts"** — Numbers 15:39: *you will not go about after your own heart and your own eyes, which you go whoring after*. **"the daughters of Canaan"** — Abraham's charge in Genesis 24:3, here given to all his sons.
- **v5 "the giants"** — the sons of the Watchers (5:1; 7:22), destroyed with Sodom as the two great examples of judgment.
- **v6 "a hissing"** — so the Latin and Charles; the EOTC has *la-təmkəḥt*, the word rendered *glory* at 19:17, which does not fit a curse.
- **v9 "rain… morning and evening"** — the Latin has *the early and the late rains*, as Deuteronomy 11:14: *I will give the rain of your land in its season, the early rain and the late rain*. **"the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your land"** — Deuteronomy 28:4: *Blessed is the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your ground*.
- **vv11–13 the sons sent east** — Genesis 25:5–6: *Abraham gave all that was his to Isaac. To the sons of the secondary wives… Abraham gave gifts, and he sent them away from Isaac his son… eastward, to the land of the east.* Jubilees adds that the sons of Ishmael and of Keturah mixed together and became the Arabs. **"Paran"** — the EOTC writes *Farmon*; the Latin has *Faramon*.

# Jubilees 29

**1** And it came to pass, after Rachel had borne Joseph, that Laban went to shear his sheep, for they were three days' journey away from him.

**2** Jacob saw that Laban was going to shear his sheep; and Jacob called Leah and Rachel and spoke kindly to them, that they should come with him to the land of Canaan.

**3** For he told them how he had seen everything in a dream, and all that He had told him, that he should return to his father's house; and they said, "Wherever you go, we will go with you."

**4** Jacob blessed the God of Isaac his father and the God of Abraham his father's father; and he rose and set his wives and his children on the animals, and took all his possessions and crossed the river, and came to the land of Gilead; and Jacob hid his purpose from Laban and did not tell him.

**5** In the seventh year of the fourth week Jacob turned toward Gilead, in the first month, on the twenty-first of it; and Laban pursued him and overtook Jacob on the mountain of Gilead in the third month, on the thirteenth of it.

**6** The Lord did not let him harm Jacob, for He appeared to him in a dream by night; and Laban spoke to Jacob.

**7** On the fifteenth of those days Jacob made a feast for Laban and for all who came with him; and Jacob swore to Laban that day, and Laban to Jacob, that neither would cross over to the other with evil on the mountain of Gilead.

**8** He made a heap there for a witness; for this reason the name of that place is called the Heap of Witness, after this heap.

**9** Before, they used to call the land of Gilead the land of the Rephaim, for it is the land of the Rephaim; and the Rephaim were born there, giants whose height was ten cubits, and nine, and eight, down to seven cubits.

**10** Their dwellings were from the land of the sons of Ammon to Mount Hermon, and the seats of their kingdom were Karnaim and Ashtaroth and Edrei and Misur and Beon.

**11** The Lord destroyed them for the evil of their deeds, for they were very fierce; and in their place He made the Amorites live, evil and sinful, so that no nation is so hateful; for they filled up all their sins, and they no longer have length of life on the earth.

**12** Jacob sent Laban away, and he went to Mesopotamia, the land of the east[; and Jacob turned back to the land of Gilead].

**13** He crossed the Jabbok in the ninth month, on the eleventh of it, he and his eleven sons; and on that day Esau his brother came to him and was reconciled to him, and went away from him to the land of Seir; and Jacob lived in tents.

**14** In the first year of the fifth week of this jubilee he crossed the Jordan and lived beyond the Jordan, and he pastured his sheep from the Sea of the Heap to Beth-shan, and to Dothan, and to the forest of Akrabbim.

**15** He sent to his father Isaac from all his possessions: clothing and food, meat and drink, milk and butter and cheese, and some dates of the valley;

**16** and to his mother Rebekah too, four times a year, between the seasons of the months — between plowing and reaping, between autumn and the rains, and between winter and spring — to the tower of Abraham.

**17** For Isaac had returned from the Well of the Oath and gone up to the tower of his father Abraham, and lived there, apart from Esau his son.

**18** For in the days when Jacob went to Mesopotamia, Esau took for himself a wife, Mahalath the daughter of Ishmael, and gathered all his father's flocks and his wives, and went up and lived on Mount Seir, and left Isaac his father at the Well of the Oath alone.

**19** Isaac went up from the Well of the Oath and lived in the tower of Abraham his father, on the mountain of Hebron.

**20** From there Jacob sent all that he sent to his father and his mother, from time to time, all they needed; and they blessed Jacob with all their heart and with all their soul.

## Notes

- **vv2, 3, 4, 9, 11, 13, 18, 20** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter from v8 on, has most of them too. Without the end of v13 the EOTC never tells of Jacob's meeting with Esau. **Words in [brackets]** (v12) are in no Ge'ez text here; they follow the Latin.
- **vv1–8 the flight from Laban** — Genesis 31. Jubilees tells in a few verses what Genesis tells at length, and leaves out Rachel's theft of the household gods (31:19, 30–35). **v5 the dates** — the seventh year of the fourth week is 2135, the year after Joseph's birth (28:24). **v8 "the Heap of Witness"** — Genesis 31:48: *Laban said, "This heap is a witness between me and you today." Therefore its name was called Galeed.* The EOTC has *fəśśəḥat*, "joy," where Charles and the Latin have *heap*; the English follows them.
- **vv9–11 the land of the Rephaim** — Genesis 14:5 names *the Rephaim in Ashteroth-karnaim*; Deuteronomy 3:11 their last king: *Only Og king of Bashan was left of the remnant of the Rephaim; his bed was a bed of iron… nine cubits its length*. **v9 "the land of the Rephaim"** — the EOTC writes the first as *the land of Raphael*, then *the land of the Rephaim*.
- **vv12–13 Esau** — Genesis 32–33 tells of Jacob's fear, the wrestling at Peniel, the new name Israel, and the meeting with Esau; Jubilees keeps only the crossing of the Jabbok and, in the Latin, the reconciliation on the same day. **v12** — the Greek dates Jacob's return to *the 153rd year of Isaac*.
- **v14 the date and the places** — the first year of the fifth week is 2136. **"the Sea of the Heap"** — so Charles; the Latin has *the Salt Sea*.
- **vv15–20 the tower of Abraham** — Jacob, though living apart, provides for both parents four times a year; Esau takes his father's flocks and leaves him. **v18 "Mahalath the daughter of Ishmael"** — Genesis 28:9: *Esau went to Ishmael, and took Mahalath daughter of Ishmael son of Abraham*.

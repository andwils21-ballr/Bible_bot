# Jubilees 31

**1** On the new moon of the month Jacob spoke to all the people of his house, saying, "Purify yourselves and change your clothes, and let us rise and go up to Bethel, where I made my vow to Him on the day I fled from the face of Esau my brother, because He has been with me and brought me back into this land in peace; and put away the foreign gods that are among you."

**2** They gave up the foreign gods, and what was in their ears and on their necks, and the idols that Rachel had stolen from Laban her father she gave, all of them, to Jacob; and he burned them and broke them to pieces and destroyed them, and hid them under an oak that is in the land of Shechem.

**3** On the new moon of the seventh month he went up to Bethel, and built an altar at the place where he had slept, and set up a pillar there[; and he sent word to his father Isaac to come to him to his sacrifice, and to his mother Rebekah].

**4** Isaac said, "Let my son Jacob come, and let me see him before I die."

**5** Jacob went to his father Isaac and to his mother Rebekah, to the house of his father Abraham, and took two of his sons with him, Levi and Judah; and he came to his father Isaac and to his mother Rebekah.

**6** Rebekah came out of the tower to the gate of the tower to kiss Jacob and embrace him[; for her spirit had revived when she heard, "Here — Jacob your son has come"; and she kissed him].

**7** She saw his two sons[, and she recognized them, and said to him, "Are these your sons, my son?" She embraced them and kissed them and blessed them, saying, "In you the seed of Abraham will become famous, and you will be a blessing on the earth."]

**8** Jacob went in to Isaac his father, into his chamber where he lay[; and his two sons were with him; and he took his father's hand and bent down and kissed him, and Isaac clung to the neck of Jacob his son and wept on his neck].

**9** The darkness passed from Isaac's eyes, and he saw the two sons of Jacob, Levi and Judah[, and he said, "Are these your sons, my son? For they are like you."]

**10** He told him that they were truly his sons: "Truly you have seen that they are truly my sons."

**11** They came near to him, and he turned and kissed them and embraced them both together.

**12** The spirit of prophecy came down into his mouth, and he took Levi by his right hand and Judah by his left.

**13** He turned to Levi first and began to bless him first, and said to him, "May the Lord of all bless you, He, the Lord of all the ages[, you and your sons forever and ever].

**14** May the Lord give to you and to your seed greatness and great glory, and bring you and your seed near to Himself from all flesh, to serve in His sanctuary like the angels of the presence and like the holy ones[; and like them the seed of your sons will be, for glory and greatness and holiness, and He has made them holy forever and ever].

**15** They will be princes and judges and chiefs to all the seed of the sons of Jacob; they will speak the word of the Lord in righteousness, and they will judge all His judgments in righteousness[; and they will declare My ways to Jacob and My paths to Israel. The blessing of God will be given to their seed, that they may bless all the beloved seed].

**16** Your mother called your name Levi, and truly she called your name: you will be joined to the Lord, and be the companion of all the sons of Jacob[. Your table will be yours, and you and your sons will eat of it; and your table will be full forever, and your food will not fail forever and ever].

**17** All who hate you will fall down before you, and all your enemies will be rooted out and perish. Blessed is he who blesses you, and every nation that curses you will be cursed."

**18** To Judah he said, "May the Lord give you power and strength to trample all who hate you. Be a prince, you and one of your sons, over the sons of Jacob; may your name and the name of your sons go out and travel through all the earth and the cities[. Then the nations will fear before your face, and all the nations will tremble, and all the peoples will tremble].

**19** In you will be the help of Jacob, and in you the salvation of Israel will be found.

**20** On the day you sit on the throne of the honor of your righteousness, there will be great peace for all the seed of the sons of the beloved[. Blessed is he who blesses you; and all who hate you and afflict you and curse you will be rooted out and destroyed from the earth, and be cursed]."

**21** He turned and kissed him again and embraced him, and rejoiced greatly, for he had seen the sons of Jacob his son in truth.

**22** He went out from between his feet, and fell down and bowed down to him, and he blessed them[; and he rested there with Isaac his father that night, and they ate and drank with joy].

**23** He laid the two sons of Jacob to sleep, one on his right and one on his left, and it was counted to him as righteousness.

**24** Jacob told his father everything in the night, how the Lord had shown him great mercy[, and had prospered him in all his ways and protected him from all evil].

**25** Isaac blessed the God of his father Abraham, who had not withdrawn His mercy and His righteousness from the son of His servant Isaac.

**26** In the morning Jacob told his father Isaac the vow he had vowed to the Lord, and the vision he had seen, and that he had built an altar, and that everything was ready for the sacrifice to be made before the Lord as he had vowed, and that he had come to set him on a donkey.

**27** Isaac said to Jacob his son, "I cannot come with you, for I am old and cannot bear the way. Go, my son, in peace, for I am one hundred and sixty-five years old [this day; I am no longer able to journey. Set your mother on a donkey, and let her go with you].

**28** I know, my son, that you have come on my account[; and blessed be this day on which you have seen me alive, and I also have seen you, my son].

**29** Prosper, and fulfill the vow you have vowed, and do not put off your vow, for the vow will be required of you[; now hurry to perform it, and may He who made all things, to whom you have vowed the vow, be pleased]."

**30** He said to Rebekah, "Go with Jacob your son." [Rebekah went with her son Jacob, and Deborah her nurse with her, and they came to Bethel.]

**31** Jacob remembered the prayer with which his father had blessed him and his two sons, Levi and Judah, and he rejoiced and blessed the God of his fathers, Abraham and Isaac.

**32** He said, "Now I know that I have an eternal hope, and my sons too, before the God of all." So it is ordained concerning the two of them, and they bring it up for them as an eternal testimony on the tablets of heaven, how Isaac blessed them.

## Notes

- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. In vv13, 14, 15, 16 and 30 they follow the Latin, which covers this chapter from v9 to v18 and from v29 on; in v9 the Latin begins partway, and the bracket follows Charles for its opening words and the Latin for *For they are like you*. In vv3, 6, 7, 8, 18, 20, 22, 24, 27, 28 and 29 they follow Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts; in v18 the Latin covers the words but breaks off.
- **vv1–3 back to Bethel** — Genesis 35:1–4: *Remove the foreign gods that are among you, and purify yourselves, and change your clothes… They gave Jacob all the foreign gods that were in their hand, and the rings that were in their ears, and Jacob hid them under the terebinth that is by Shechem.* Jubilees has Jacob burn and break them first.
- **vv4–32 the visit to Isaac** — Genesis tells nothing of it. In Jubilees Isaac's blindness lifts (v9), the spirit of prophecy comes into his mouth (v12), and he blesses the two sons from whom Israel's priests and kings will come; the Greek sums it up, *he blessed Levi as high priest, and Judah as king and ruler*.
- **vv13–17 Levi** — **v14 "like the angels of the presence"** — the priests of Israel serve on earth as the highest angels serve in heaven. **v15** — as Moses blesses Levi: *They shall teach Your judgments to Jacob and Your law to Israel* (Deuteronomy 33:10). **v16 "you will be joined to the Lord"** — Genesis 29:34: *Now, this time, my husband will be joined to me… Therefore he called his name Levi.* Isaac turns the joining from husband to God.
- **vv18–20 Judah** — **"a prince, you and one of your sons"** — Genesis 49:10: *The staff shall not turn aside from Judah, nor the ruler's rod from between his feet.*
- **v27 "one hundred and sixty-five years old"** — by this book's dates Isaac was born in 1987 (16:13, 15) and this is 2143 (30:1), which makes him 156; the Greek at 29:12 puts Jacob's return in Isaac's 153rd year. Genesis 35:28 has Isaac live *a hundred years and eighty years*. The text is left as it stands.
- **v29 "do not put off your vow"** — Deuteronomy 23:22: *When you vow a vow to the LORD your God, you shall not delay to pay it, for the LORD your God will surely require it of you.* **v30 "Deborah her nurse"** — she dies at Bethel in Genesis 35:8, *below Bethel, under the oak*.

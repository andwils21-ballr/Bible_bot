# Jubilees 12

**1** And it came to pass in the sixth week, in its seventh year, that Abram said to Terah his father, "Father!" He said, "Here I am, my son."

**2** He said, "What help or profit do we have from these idols that you serve and bow down before?

**3** For there is no spirit in them at all; they are dumb forms and an error of the heart. Do not serve them.

**4** Serve the God of heaven, who sends down the rain and the dew on the earth, and does everything on the earth, and created everything by His word, and all life is from before His face.

**5** Why do you serve things that have no spirit in them? They are the work of hands, and you carry them on your shoulders, and you have no help from them, only great shame for those who make them and an error of the heart for those who serve them. Do not serve them."

**6** His father said to him, "I know it too, my son, but what shall I do with the people who have made me serve before them?

**7** If I tell them the truth, they will kill me, for their soul clings to them, to serve them and praise them. Keep silent, my son, or they will kill you."

**8** He told these words to his two brothers, and they were angry with him, and he kept silent.

**9** In the fortieth jubilee, in the second week, in its seventh year, Abram took a wife named Sarai, the daughter of his father, and she became his wife.

**10** Haran his brother took a wife in the third year of the third week, and she bore him a son in the seventh year of this week, and he called his name Lot.

**11** Nahor his brother also took a wife.

**12** In the sixtieth year of Abram's life, that is, the fourth week, in its fourth year, Abram rose by night and burned the house of the idols, and he burned all that was in the house, and no one knew it.

**13** They rose in the night and tried to save their gods from the middle of the fire.

**14** Haran rushed to save them, and the fire flared over him, and he was burned in the fire and died in Ur of the Chaldeans before Terah his father, and they buried him in Ur of the Chaldeans.

**15** Terah went out from Ur of the Chaldeans, he and his sons, to go into the land of Lebanon and into the land of Canaan, and he lived in Harran; and Abram lived with Terah his father in Harran two weeks of years.

**16** In the sixth week, in its fifth year, Abram sat up through the night, on the new moon of the seventh month, to watch the stars from evening to morning, to see what the year would be like for rain; and he sat alone and watched.

**17** A word came into his heart, and he said, "All the signs of the stars, and the signs of the moon and of the sun, are all in the hand of the Lord. Why do I search them out?

**18** If He wishes, He sends rain morning and evening; and if He wishes, He does not send it down. Everything is in His hand."

**19** He prayed that night and said, "My God, my God, God Most High, You alone are my God. You created everything, and all that is and has been is the work of Your hands, and You and Your rule I have chosen.

**20** Save me from the hands of evil spirits who rule over the thoughts of men's hearts, and do not let them lead me astray from You, my God; and establish me and my seed forever, so that we do not go astray from now on and forever."

**21** He said, "Shall I return to Ur of the Chaldeans, who seek my face so that I will return to them, or shall I stay here in this place? The right path before You — prosper it in the hand of Your servant, so that he may walk it, and let me not walk in the error of my heart, my God."

**22** When he had finished speaking and praying, here — the word of the Lord was sent to him through me, saying, "Go out from your land, and from your kindred, and from your father's house, to a land that I will show you, and I will make you a great and numerous nation.

**23** I will bless you and make your name great, and you will be blessed on the earth; and in you all the nations of the earth will be blessed, and I will bless those who bless you, and curse those who curse you.

**24** I will be God to you, and to your son, and to your son's son, and to all your seed. Do not fear. From now on and to all the generations of the earth, I am your God."

**25** The Lord God said to me, "Open his mouth and his ears, so that he may hear and speak with his tongue in the language that is revealed"; for it had ceased from the mouths of all the sons of men from the day of the overthrow.

**26** I opened his mouth and his ears and his lips, and I began to speak with him in Hebrew, in the language of creation.

**27** He took the books of his fathers, which were written in Hebrew, and copied them, and from then on he began to study them, and I made known to him everything he could not understand; and he studied them during the six rainy months.

**28** In the seventh year of the sixth week he spoke with his father and told him that he would leave Harran to go to the land of Canaan, to see it, and then return to him.

**29** Terah his father said to him, "Go in peace. May the eternal God make your path straight, and may the Lord be with you and protect you from all evil, and give you grace, mercy and favor before those who see you, and may none of the sons of men have power over you to harm you. Go in peace.

**30** If you see a land pleasant to your eyes to live in, come and take me to you; and take Lot with you, the son of Haran your brother, as your own son. The Lord be with you.

**31** Leave Nahor your brother with me until you return in peace, and then we will all go with you together."

## Notes

- **vv4, 7, 12, 15, 17, 20, 22, 23, 27, 29** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63). Two of them carry the story. In v12 the EOTC gives the date and stops; the burning of the idols is in the Asmara printing, and the Greek has it too: *when Abraham was sixty-one years old, Abraham set fire to the idols of his father*. In v22 the EOTC says only *when he had finished speaking and praying*; the call itself, Genesis 12:1–2, is in the Asmara printing.
- **vv2–5 "they are dumb forms… you carry them on your shoulders"** — the prophets' mockery of idols: *They lift it on the shoulder, they carry it, and set it in its place, and it stands there; from its place it will not move* (Isaiah 46:7).
- **v9 "Sarai, the daughter of his father"** — so Abraham says of her in Genesis: *she is my sister — the daughter of my father, but not the daughter of my mother — and she became my wife* (Genesis 20:12). The Greek makes her *the daughter of Harran, sister of Melcha and of Lot*.
- **vv12–14 "Haran… died in Ur of the Chaldeans before Terah his father"** — Genesis 11:28 gives the death and the place, *Haran died before the face of Terah his father… in Ur of the Chaldeans*, but not the cause. Jubilees gives the cause: Haran died in the fire trying to save the idols Abram had set alight. The sixtieth year of Abram is the fourth year of the fourth week of the fortieth jubilee: 39 × 49 + 3 × 7 + 4 = 1936, sixty years after his birth in 1876 (11:15).
- **v15 "Harran"** — the town, spelled apart from Haran, Abram's brother, as in this project's Genesis.
- **vv16–18 "All the signs of the stars… are all in the hand of the Lord"** — the watcher of the stars gives up reading them for rain and turns to the one who sends it; the Chaldean lore his fathers were taught (11:8) is set aside.
- **vv22–24 "I will make you a great and numerous nation… I will be God to you"** — Genesis 12:1–3: *Go, for yourself, from your land, and from your kindred, and from your father's house… In you all the families of the ground shall be blessed.* In Jubilees the word comes *through me*, the angel of the presence.
- **vv25–27 "I began to speak with him in Hebrew, in the language of creation"** — the language lost at Babel (10:24) is given back to Abram, and with it the old books of his fathers. The Greek quotes the same line: *I taught Abraham the Hebrew language according to that from the beginning of creation*.

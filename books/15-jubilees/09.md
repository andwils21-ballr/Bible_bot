# Jubilees 9

**1** Ham divided among his sons, and the first portion came out for Cush, toward the east, and to the west of him for Egypt, and to the west of him for Put[, and to the west of him, and to the west of that, on the sea, for Canaan].

**2** Shem too divided among his sons, and the first portion came out for Elam and his sons, to the east of the river Tigris until it comes near the east: the whole land of India, and on the Red Sea, on its shore, and the waters of Dedan, and all the mountains of Mebri and Ela, and all the land of Susan, and all that is on the side of Pharnak, to the Red Sea and to the river Tina.

**3** For Asshur the second portion came out: all the land of Asshur and Nineveh and Shinar and Sek, to the border of India, and it goes up and runs along the river.

**4** For Arpachshad the third portion came out: all the land of the region of the Chaldeans, to the east of the Euphrates, near the Red Sea, and all the waters of the wilderness, near the tongue of the sea that looks toward Egypt; all the land of Lebanon and Senir and Amana, to the border of the Euphrates.

**5** For Aram the fourth portion came out: all the land of Mesopotamia, between the Tigris and the Euphrates, to the north of the Chaldeans, to the border of the mountains of Asshur and the land of Arara.

**6** For Lud the fifth portion came out: the mountain of Asshur and all that belongs to it, until it reaches the great sea and reaches the east of Asshur his brother.

**7** Japheth too divided the land among his sons for an inheritance.

**8** The first portion came out for Gomer, to the east, from the north side, to the river Tina[; and in the north there came out for Magog all the inner parts of the north, until it reaches the sea of Meat].

**9** For Madai a portion came out, for him to hold, from the west of his two brothers to the islands and to the coasts of the islands.

**10** For Javan the fourth portion came out: every island, and the islands toward the border of Lud.

**11** For Tubal the fifth portion came out: in the middle of the tongue that comes near the border of Lud's portion, to the second tongue, and beyond the second tongue to the third tongue.

**12** For Meshech the sixth portion came out: all the region beyond the third tongue, until it comes near the east of Gadir.

**13** For Tiras the seventh portion came out: four great islands in the middle of the sea, which come near the portion of Ham[; and the islands of Kamaturi came out by lot for the sons of Arpachshad as his inheritance].

**14** In this way the sons of Noah divided the land among their sons in the presence of Noah their father[, and he bound them all by an oath, calling down a curse on anyone who tried to seize a portion that had not fallen to him by his lot].

**15** They all said, "So be it, so be it," for themselves and their sons forever, through their generations, until the day of judgment, on which the Lord God will judge them with the sword and with fire for all the unclean wickedness of their errors, with which they have filled the earth with guilt and uncleanness and fornication and sin.

## Notes

- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. They follow Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts: vv1, 8, 13 and 14. Without v8's bracket, Magog, the second son of Japheth in Genesis 10:2, would get no land at all; the portions are numbered, and Madai's (v9) is the third.
- **The sons** — the names are those of the table of nations: *The sons of Ham: Cush and Egypt and Put and Canaan* (Genesis 10:6); *The sons of Shem: Elam and Asshur and Arpachshad and Lud and Aram* (10:22); *The sons of Japheth: Gomer and Magog and Madai and Javan and Tubal and Meshech and Tiras* (10:2). Jubilees gives each his land. Canaan's lies in the far west, *on the sea*; in the next chapter he refuses it and settles in the land of Lebanon, which fell to Shem (10:29–33).
- **v2 "for Elam"** — so the Ge'ez. Charles's English has *for Ham* here, a slip: Elam is Shem's firstborn in Genesis 10:22.
- **v5 "to the north of the Chaldeans"** — *dabub*, rendered as in chapter 8 (see the note there on north and south).
- **vv14–15 "So be it, so be it"** — the oath and the curse bind the division, as the people answer *Amen* to the curses of Deuteronomy 27. It is this oath that Canaan breaks in the next chapter.

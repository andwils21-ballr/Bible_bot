# Jubilees 15

**1** In the fifth year of the fourth week of this jubilee, in the third month, in the middle of the month, Abram kept the festival of the first fruits of the wheat harvest.

**2** He offered a new offering on the altar, the first fruits of the produce, to the Lord: a heifer and a goat and a sheep on the altar, as a burnt offering to the Lord; and their grain offerings and their drink offerings he offered on the altar with frankincense.

**3** The Lord appeared to Abram, and the Lord said to Abram, "I am God Almighty. Be pleasing before Me, and be whole.

**4** I will set My covenant between Me and you, and I will increase you greatly."

**5** Abram fell on his face, and the Lord spoke with him and said,

**6** "My ordinance is with you, and I will make you a father of many nations.

**7** Your name will no longer be called Abram, but from now on and forever your name will be Abraham, for I have made you a father of many nations.

**8** I will make you very great, and I will make you into nations, and kings will come out of you.

**9** I will set My covenant between Me and you, and between your seed after you through their generations, for an eternal ordinance, so that I may be God to you and to your seed after you.

**10** I will give to you and to your seed after you the land where you have lived as a guest, the land of Canaan, to hold forever, and I will be their God."

**11** The Lord said to Abraham, "And you — keep My covenant, you and your seed after you. Circumcise every male among you, and circumcise your foreskins, and it will be a sign of My eternal ordinance between Me and you.

**12** The son you shall circumcise on the eighth day, every male through your generations, one born of the house, and one bought with money from any foreigner, whom you have acquired, who is not of your seed.

**13** One born of your house must surely be circumcised, and those you have bought with money must be circumcised; and My covenant will be in your flesh for an eternal ordinance.

**14** The male who is not circumcised, who has not circumcised the flesh of his foreskin on the eighth day — that soul will be cut off from its kindred, for he has broken My covenant."

**15** The Lord said to Abraham, "Sarai your wife — her name will no longer be called Sarai, for Sarah is her name.

**16** I will bless her, and I will give you a son from her, and I will bless him, and he will become a people, and kings of nations will come from him."

**17** Abraham fell on his face and rejoiced, and said in his heart, "Shall a son be born to one who is a hundred years old? Shall Sarah, who is ninety years old, bear?"

**18** Abraham said to the Lord, "If only Ishmael might live before You!"

**19** The Lord said, "Yes; and Sarah also will bear you a son, and you shall call his name Isaac, and I will raise up My covenant with him, an eternal covenant, and with his seed after him.

**20** As for Ishmael also, I have heard you. I will bless him and make him great and multiply him exceedingly; he will father twelve princes, and I will make him a great nation.

**21** But My covenant I will raise up with Isaac, whom Sarah will bear to you at this season, in the next year."

**22** When He had finished speaking with him, the Lord went up from Abraham.

**23** Abraham did as the Lord had told him: he took Ishmael his son, and all born of his house, and those he had bought with money, every male in his house, and circumcised the flesh of their foreskin.

**24** On that same day Abraham was circumcised; and those born of his house, and the men of his house, and all whom he had bought with money, even from the sons of foreigners, were circumcised with him.

**25** This law is for all generations forever, and there is no cutting short of the days, and no passing over one day of the eight days; for it is an eternal ordinance, ordained and written on the tablets of heaven.

**26** Everyone who is born whose flesh is not circumcised by the eighth day is not of the sons of the ordinance that the Lord made with Abraham, for he is of the sons of destruction; and there is no longer any sign on him that he belongs to the Lord, but he is for destruction and for slaughter on the earth, to be rooted out of the earth, for he has broken the covenant of the Lord our God.

**27** For all the angels of the presence and all the angels of sanctification have been so created from the day of their creation; and before the angels of the presence and the angels of sanctification He sanctified Israel, to be with Him and with His holy angels.

**28** As for you, command the sons of Israel, and let them keep the sign of this covenant through their generations as an eternal ordinance, and they will not be uprooted from the land.

**29** For the command has been ordained for the covenant, that they should keep it forever among all the sons of Israel.

**30** For Ishmael and his sons and his brothers and Esau the Lord did not bring near to Himself, and He did not choose them, though they are of the sons of Abraham, for He knew them; but Israel He chose to be His people.

**31** He sanctified it and gathered it from among all the sons of men; for there are many nations and many peoples, and all are His, and over all He has set spirits in authority to lead them astray from Him.

**32** But over Israel He set no one, neither angel nor spirit, for He alone is their ruler, and He will keep them and require them at the hand of His angels and His spirits, and at the hand of [all His powers], so that He may keep them and bless them, and they may be His and He theirs from now on and forever.

**33** Now I tell you that the sons of Israel will be false to this ordinance, and will not circumcise their sons according to all this law; for they will leave some of the flesh of the circumcision uncut when they circumcise their sons; and all of them, sons of Beliar, will leave their sons uncircumcised, as they were born.

**34** There will be great wrath from the Lord on the sons of Israel, because they have forsaken His covenant and turned aside from His word, and provoked and blasphemed, since they have not kept the ordinance of this sign; for they have made their members like the nations, so that they may be removed and uprooted from the land. There will be no more pardon or forgiveness for them, that they should be pardoned and forgiven for all the sin of this eternal error.

## Notes

- **vv2, 8, 12, 13, 19, 20, 25, 26, 27, 29, 31, 32, 34** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from Charles's edition of the Ge'ez (1895); the Asmara file held here lacks 14:16–16:13. Without the end of v19 the EOTC first names Isaac in v21. **v32 [all His powers]** — Charles's correction, in none of his manuscripts: three of the four have *all His commandments*, and the fourth leaves the words out.
- **vv1–2 "the festival of the first fruits of the wheat harvest"** — the middle of the third month, the Festival of Weeks (6:17–21), and the same day as the covenant between the pieces (14:10). Exodus 34:22: *the Festival of Weeks… the firstfruits of the wheat harvest*. **The date** — the fifth year of the fourth week of the forty-first jubilee is 40 × 49 + 3 × 7 + 5 = 1986; with Abram born in 1876 (11:15), that makes him 110, not the ninety-nine of Genesis 17:1. The text is left as it stands (compare 14:24).
- **vv3–22 the covenant of circumcision** — Genesis 17:1–22, retold in order, in this project's wording where the Ge'ez allows. **v3 "God Almighty"** — the Ge'ez keeps the Hebrew name, *ʾAmlāka Saday*, *El Shaddai*; see the note at Genesis 17:1. **"Be pleasing before Me"** — as the Greek of Genesis 17:1, *euarestei enantion emou*; the Hebrew has *Walk about before Me*.
- **vv11–14 "a sign… on the eighth day"** — Genesis 17:10–14. Paul calls it *the sign of circumcision, a seal of the righteousness of the faith* Abraham had while uncircumcised (Romans 4:11). **"money"** — the Ge'ez word is *warq*, gold; Genesis has *silver*. Both mean the price paid.
- **v17 "rejoiced"** — Genesis 17:17 has *he laughed*, the laugh that gives Isaac his name. Jubilees has Abraham rejoice.
- **v24 and v10, the EOTC's own brackets** — the printed EOTC text sets *those born of his house* (v24) and *I will give to you and to your seed after you* (v10) in square brackets. They are rendered here as plain text.
- **v25 "no cutting short of the days"** — literally *no circumcising of the days*: the eighth day may not be trimmed.
- **v27 "have been so created"** — Charles's reading: the highest angels were made so from their creation, and Israel is sanctified to be with them.
- **v30 "though they are of the sons of Abraham"** — so the Latin; the Ge'ez joins the clause with *for*, *ʾəsma*.
- **vv31–32 "over all He has set spirits… over Israel He set no one"** — Deuteronomy 32:8–9: *When the Most High gave the nations their inheritance, when He divided the sons of man, He set the boundaries of the peoples… For the LORD's portion is His people; Jacob is the measured lot of His inheritance.*
- **v33 "sons of Beliar"** — the name of the adversary Paul sets against Christ: *What agreement has Christ with Beliar?* (2 Corinthians 6:15). **"leave some of the flesh… uncut"** — the Ge'ez *matrəfāna*, a remainder: the angel foretells a circumcision done only in part.

Latin: Online Critical Pseudepigrapha, CC BY 4.0. Charles's edition of the Ge'ez (1895): public domain. English translation by this project.

# Jubilees 18

**1** The Lord said to him, "Abraham, Abraham!" He said, "Here I am."

**2** He said to him, "Take your son, your beloved one, whom you love — Isaac — and go to the high land, and bring him up on one of the mountains that I will show you."

**3** He rose early in the morning and saddled his donkey, and took his two young men with him, and Isaac his son, and split the wood for the offering, and went to the place on the third day; and he saw the place from far off.

**4** He came to a well of water, and said to his young men, "Stay here with the donkey, and I and the boy will go; and when we have bowed down, we will return to you."

**5** He took the wood of the offering and set it on Isaac his son, and he took in his hand the fire and the knife; and the two of them went together to that place.

**6** Isaac said to his father, "Father!" He said, "Here I am, my son." He said to him, "Here is the fire and the knife and the wood — but where is the lamb for the burnt offering, father?"

**7** He said, "The Lord will see to the lamb for the burnt offering for Himself, my son." He came near to the place of the mountain of God.

**8** He built an altar and set the wood on the altar, and bound Isaac his son and set him on the wood that was on top of the altar, and stretched out his hand to take the knife to slaughter Isaac his son.

**9** I stood before him and before the prince Mastema, and the Lord said, "Tell him not to lay his hand on the boy or do anything to him, for I know that he fears the Lord."

**10** I called to him from heaven and said to him, "Abraham!" He was terrified and said, "Here I am."

**11** I said to him, "Do not lay your hand on the boy or do anything to him, for now I know that you fear the Lord, and you have not withheld your son, your firstborn, from Me."

**12** The prince Mastema was put to shame. Abraham lifted up his eyes and looked, and here — a ram caught by its horns; and Abraham went and took the ram and brought it up as a burnt offering in place of his son.

**13** Abraham called that place "The Lord Has Seen," so that it is said, "The Lord has seen." That is Mount Zion.

**14** The Lord called Abraham by his name a second time from heaven, as He made us appear to speak to him in the name of the Lord.

**15** He said, "By Myself I have sworn, says the Lord: because you have done this thing, and have not withheld your son, your firstborn, whom you loved, from Me, blessing, I will bless you, and multiplying, I will multiply your seed as the stars of heaven and as the sand that is on the seashore; and your seed will inherit the cities of its enemies.

**16** In your seed all the nations of the earth will be blessed, because you listened to My voice; and I have shown to all that you are faithful to Me in all that I have said to you. Go in peace."

**17** Abraham went to his young men, and they rose and went together to Beersheba; and Abraham lived at the Well of the Oath.

**18** He kept this festival every year, seven days with joy, and he named it the festival of the Lord, after the seven days in which he went and returned in peace.

**19** So it is ordained and written on the tablets of heaven concerning Israel and its seed, to keep this festival seven days with the joy of a festival.

## Notes

- **vv3, 5, 6, 7, 15, 16, 17** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which begins at v10 of this chapter, has vv15–17 too. The EOTC of v6 stops at Isaac's *Father!*; his question about the lamb, and the answer the EOTC keeps in v7, are the heart of Genesis 22:7–8.
- **vv1–19 the binding of Isaac** — Genesis 22:1–19, retold in order, in this project's wording where the Ge'ez allows: *Take, please, your son — your beloved one — whom you have loved — Isaac* (22:2); *God will see to the lamb for a burnt offering for Himself, my son* (22:8). Jubilees adds what Genesis does not tell: Mastema stands watching (v9), and he is put to shame (v12).
- **v1 "Abraham, Abraham!"** — Genesis 22:1 calls him once; the double call is Genesis 22:11, the angel's. Here the order is turned about: v10, the angel's call, has the name once in the EOTC (Charles and the Latin have *Abraham, Abraham*).
- **v2 "the high land"** — Genesis 22:2 has *the land of Moriah*.
- **vv9, 11 "I know"** — so the Ge'ez, as in Genesis 22:12, *now I know that you fear God*. Charles and the Latin have *I have shown*: in their text the test is not for God to learn but for the watching Mastema to see, as in v16, *I have shown to all that you are faithful to Me*.
- **vv11, 15 "your firstborn"** — so the EOTC, of Isaac, though Ishmael is older; the Latin has *firstborn* in v11 and *only son* in v15. Genesis 22:12, 16 has *your beloved one* (see the note there).
- **v13 "That is Mount Zion"** — Genesis 22:14 ends *On the mountain of the LORD it will be seen*. Jubilees names the mountain. So does Chronicles: *Solomon began to build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem, on Mount Moriah* (2 Chronicles 3:1). The Greek has *where David later established the sanctuary*.
- **vv15–16 "By Myself I have sworn"** — Genesis 22:16–18. **"will inherit the cities of its enemies"** — the Latin; Genesis has *Your seed shall seize the gate of their enemies*.
- **vv18–19 "this festival… seven days"** — the Ge'ez does not name it. The heavenly council met on the twelfth of the first month (17:15); counted from there, three days' journey out and seven days in all would fall in the week of the Festival of Unleavened Bread, which Leviticus sets on *the fifteenth day of this month… Seven days you shall eat unleavened bread* (Leviticus 23:6). **v18 "every year"** — the Latin has *three times each year*.

# Jubilees 7

**1** In the seventh week, in its first year, in this jubilee, Noah planted vines on the mountain on which the ark had come to rest, named Lubar, one of the mountains of Ararat. They bore fruit in the fourth year, and he guarded their fruit and gathered it that year, in the seventh month.

**2** He made wine from it and put it in a vessel, and kept it until the fifth year, until the first day, on the new moon of the first month.

**3** He kept the day of this festival with joy, and he made a burnt offering to the Lord: one young bull and one ram, and seven sheep a year old, and a kid of the goats, to make atonement with it for himself and his sons.

**4** He prepared the kid first, and put some of its blood on the flesh on the altar he had made, and all the fat he offered up on the altar where he made the burnt offering, and the bull and the ram and the sheep; and he laid all their flesh on the altar.

**5** He placed all their offerings, mixed with oil, on it, and afterward he sprinkled wine on the fire he had made on the altar beforehand, and he put frankincense on the altar and made a soothing aroma rise, pleasing before the Lord his God.

**6** He rejoiced, and he and his children drank of this wine with joy.

**7** It was evening, and he went into his tent, and lay down drunk and slept, and he was uncovered in his tent as he slept.

**8** Ham saw Noah his father naked, and went out and told his two brothers outside.

**9** Shem took a garment, and he rose, he and Japheth, and they put the garment on their shoulders with their faces turned back, and covered their father's shame, with their faces turned back.

**10** Noah woke from his sleep and knew all that his younger son had done to him, and he cursed his son and said, "Cursed be Canaan. A servant, a slave, shall he be to his brothers."

**11** He blessed Shem and said, "Blessed be the Lord, the God of Shem, and let Canaan be his servant.

**12** May the Lord make wide for Japheth, and may the Lord dwell in the dwelling of Shem, and let Canaan be their servant."

**13** Ham knew that his father had cursed his younger son, and he was grieved that he had cursed his son, and he parted from his father, he and his sons with him, Cush and Egypt and Put and Canaan.

**14** He too built himself a city, and called its name after the name of his wife, Neelatamauk.

**15** Japheth saw it and was jealous of his brother, and he too built himself a city, and called its name after the name of his wife, Adataneses.

**16** Shem lived with his father Noah, and he built a city near his father, on the mountain, and he too called its name after the name of his wife, Sedeqetelebab.

**17** Here are these three cities near Mount Lubar: Sedeqetelebab in front of the mountain, on its east; Naeltamauk on its south side; Adataneses toward the sea.

**18** These are the sons of Shem: Elam and Asshur and Arpachshad — this son was born two years after the flood — [and Lud] and Aram.

**19** The sons of Japheth: Gomer and Magog and Madai and Javan, Tubal, Meshech and Tiras. These are the sons of Noah.

**20** In the twenty-eighth jubilee Noah began to command his sons' sons the ordinances and the commandments, all the judgment that he knew. He testified to his sons that they should do righteousness, and cover the shame of their flesh, and bless the One who created them, and honor father and mother, and love each his neighbor, and guard their souls from fornication and uncleanness and from all wrongdoing.

**21** For because of these three things the flood came on the earth: because of the fornication the Watchers committed, against the command of their ordinances, after the daughters of men. They took wives for themselves from all whom they chose, and made the beginning of uncleanness.

**22** They fathered sons, the Naphidim, and none of them were alike, and they devoured one another: the Giants killed the Naphil, and the Naphil killed the Elyo, and the Elyo killed mankind, and one man another.

**23** Everyone sold himself to do wrongdoing and to shed innocent blood, and the earth was filled with wrongdoing.

**24** After these, [they sinned against] all the wild animals and the birds, and what moves and what walks on the earth; and much blood was shed on the earth, and every thought and desire of men imagined vanity and evil all the time.

**25** The Lord wiped out everything from the face of the earth, because of their deeds and because of the blood in the middle of the earth.

**26** "We were left, I and you, my sons, and all that went in with us into the ark; and I see your works before me, that you do not walk in righteousness: you have begun to walk in the path of destruction, and you are parting from one another and are jealous of one another, and so you are not at one, my sons, each with his brother.

**27** For I see, and here — the demons have begun to lead you and your children astray; and now I fear for you, that after my death you will shed the blood of men on the earth, and that you too will be wiped out from the face of the earth.

**28** For everyone who sheds the blood of man, and everyone who eats the blood of any flesh, will all be wiped out from the earth.

**29** No man who eats blood or sheds blood on the earth will be left, nor will any seed or descendants be left to him living under heaven; for they will go into Sheol, and go down into the place of condemnation, and all be taken away into the darkness of the deep by a violent death.

**30** No blood shall be seen on you, of all the blood there is, all the days that you slaughter any wild animal or cattle or what flies on the earth. Do a good work for your souls by covering what is shed on the face of the earth.

**31** Do not be like one who eats with the blood, but guard yourselves so that no one eats blood in your presence. Cover the blood, for so I have been commanded to testify to you and your children, together with all flesh.

**32** Do not eat the life with the flesh, so that your blood, which is your life, will not be required at the hand of any flesh that sheds it on the earth.

**33** For the earth will not be made clean of the blood that has been shed on it; only through the blood of the one who shed it will the earth be made clean, through all its generations.

**34** And now, my children, listen: do judgment and righteousness, so that you may be planted in righteousness over the face of the whole earth, and your honor may be lifted up before my God, who saved me from the waters of the flood.

**35** Here — you will go and build yourselves cities, and plant in them every plant that is on the earth, and all the fruit-bearing trees too.

**36** For three years its fruit shall not be gathered by anyone for eating, and in the fourth year its fruit shall be made holy, and they shall offer the first fruits, acceptable before the Lord Most High, the Creator of heaven and earth and all things. Let them offer in abundance the first of the wine and oil as first fruits on the altar of the Lord, who receives it; and what is left, let the servants of the house of the Lord eat before the altar that receives it.

**37** In the fifth year make its release, so that you release it in righteousness and uprightness; and you will be righteous, and all your planting will prosper.

**38** For so your forefather Enoch commanded Methuselah his son, and Methuselah commanded Lamech his son, and Lamech commanded me all that his fathers had commanded him.

**39** I too command you, my sons, as Enoch commanded his son in the first jubilees. While he was alive, the seventh in his generation, he commanded and testified to his son and to his sons' sons until the day of his death."

## Notes

- **vv3, 4, 8, 13, 15, 18, 22, 24, 26, 27, 29, 31, 35, 36** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63). The end of **v11**, which the Asmara file lacks too, is rendered from Charles's edition of the Ge'ez (1895). **Words in [brackets]** are Charles's own corrections and are in none of his manuscripts: *and Lud* (v18), made from Genesis 10:22, where no manuscript names Lud; *they sinned against* (v24), made from 1 Enoch 7:5.
- **vv1–9 the vineyard** — Genesis 9:20–23, told with more: Noah waits the years of the law for the fruit (see vv35–37), and before he drinks he offers the sacrifices of a festival. Genesis says only *he drank from the wine and became drunk, and he uncovered himself inside his tent* (Genesis 9:21).
- **vv10–12 "Cursed be Canaan… may the Lord dwell in the dwelling of Shem"** — Genesis 9:25–27. Genesis says *May God make wide for Japheth, and let him dwell in the tents of Shem*, and leaves it open whether *he* is Japheth or God. Jubilees reads it as God: *may the Lord dwell in the dwelling of Shem*.
- **vv13–17 the three cities** — each son names his city after his wife. Genesis names none of Noah's daughters-in-law. **"on its south side"** — *samen*, as Charles renders it. In the division of the earth (chapter 8) the same word marks the side of Ham's hot land and its partner *dabub* the side of Japheth's cold one (8:22–30), so the two words are rendered there, and here, as Charles gives them.
- **vv21–25 "because of these three things the flood came"** — fornication, uncleanness and wrongdoing (v20). *They took wives for themselves from all whom they chose* is Genesis 6:2; *the earth was filled with wrongdoing* is Genesis 6:11, *the earth was filled with violence*.
- **v22 "the Naphidim"** — the *Nephilim* of Genesis 6:4, in the form the Ge'ez gives. Charles's fuller text names three kinds, the Giants, the *Naphil* and the *Elyo*, each killing the next, as the giants of 1 Enoch 7 turn on men and on one another.
- **vv28–33 the blood** — the blood law given to Noah in Genesis 9:4–6, now with its reasons. Covering the blood (vv30–31) is the law of Leviticus 17:13: *he shall pour out its blood and cover it with earth*. *The earth will not be made clean… only through the blood of the one who shed it* is Numbers 35:33: *no atonement can be made for the land for the blood shed in it, except by the blood of the one who shed it*.
- **vv35–37 "For three years its fruit shall not be gathered… in the fourth year… In the fifth year"** — the law of new trees: *For three years it shall be forbidden to you… In the fourth year all its fruit shall be holy… In the fifth year you may eat its fruit* (Leviticus 19:23–25). Noah keeps it with his own vineyard (vv1–2): planted in the first year, fruit in the fourth, the wine opened in the fifth.
- **vv38–39 "Enoch… the seventh in his generation"** — *Enoch, the seventh from Adam* (Jude 14). Noah passes on what came down to him from Enoch through Methuselah and Lamech.

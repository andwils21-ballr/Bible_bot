# Jubilees 33

**1** Jacob went and lived to the south of Migdal-eder; and he went to his father Isaac, he and Leah his wife, on the new moon of the tenth month.

**2** Reuben saw Bilhah, Rachel's maid, his father's secondary wife, bathing in water in a hidden place, and he loved her.

**3** He hid himself at night, and went into Bilhah's house by night, and found her sleeping alone on her bed in her house.

**4** He lay with her, and she woke and saw Reuben lying with her in the bed; and she uncovered the corner of her garment and seized him and cried out, and recognized that it was Reuben, and was ashamed.

**5** She was ashamed because of him, and let go her hand from him, and he fled.

**6** She grieved over this thing very much, and told no one.

**7** When Jacob came and sought her, she said to him, "I am not clean for you, for I have been defiled as regards you; for Reuben has defiled me and lain with me in the night, and I was asleep and did not know until he uncovered my skirt and lay with me."

**8** Jacob was very angry with Reuben, because he had lain with Bilhah, because he had uncovered his father's skirt.

**9** Jacob did not come near her again, because Reuben had defiled her. As for any man who uncovers his father's skirt, his deed is very evil, for he is abominable before the Lord.

**10** For this reason it is written and ordained on the tablets of heaven that a man shall not lie with his father's wife, and shall not uncover his father's skirt, for it is unclean: they shall surely die together, the man who lies with his father's wife and the woman too, for they have done an unclean thing on the earth.

**11** Let there be nothing unclean before our God in the people He has chosen for Himself as a possession.

**12** Again it is written a second time: "Cursed be he who lies with his father's wife, for he has uncovered his father's shame"; and all the holy ones of the Lord said, "So be it, so be it."

**13** You too, Moses, command the sons of Israel to keep this word, for it is a judgment of death, and it is unclean, and there is no atonement ever to atone for the man who has done this, but to put him to death, to kill him, to stone him, and to root him out from among the people of our God.

**14** For no man who does it in Israel shall live a single day on the earth, for he is abominable and unclean.

**15** Let them not say, "Reuben had life and forgiveness after he lay with his father's secondary wife, and she too, though she had a husband, and her husband, Jacob his father, was alive."

**16** For until then the ordinance and the judgment and the law had not been revealed in full to all; but in your days it has been revealed as a law of times and of days, and an eternal law for the eternal generations.

**17** To this law there is no end of days, and no atonement for it, except that both of them be rooted out from among the people; on the day they did it they shall kill them.

**18** You too, Moses, write it for Israel, and let them keep it and not do anything like this thing, and not go astray into a sin to death; for the Lord our God is the judge, who does not respect persons and does not take gifts.

**19** Tell them these words of the ordinance, that they may hear and keep them and guard themselves concerning them, and not perish and not be rooted out of the land; for it is uncleanness and abomination and hatred and defilement to all who do them on the earth before our God.

**20** There is no greater sin than the fornication they commit on the earth, for Israel is a holy people to the Lord its God, and a people of inheritance, and a priestly and royal people, and set apart; and let no uncleanness appear among the holy people.

**21** In the third year of the sixth week Jacob went, with all his sons, and they lived in the house of Abraham, near Isaac his father and Rebekah his mother.

**22** These are the names of the sons of Jacob: the firstborn Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, the sons of Leah; and the sons of Rachel, Joseph and Benjamin; and the sons of Bilhah, Dan and Naphtali; and the sons of Zilpah, Gad and Asher; and Dinah, the daughter of Leah, Jacob's only daughter.

**23** They came and bowed down to Isaac and Rebekah; and when they saw them, they blessed Jacob and all his sons; and Isaac rejoiced greatly, because he saw the sons of Jacob his younger son, and he blessed them.

## Notes

- **vv4, 16, 19, 20, 22, 23** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter from v1 to v9 and from v18 on, has most of them too. **v9** — the EOTC stops at *because Reuben had defiled her*; the second sentence is in the second Ge'ez text and the Asmara printing. **v6 "told no one"** — the Latin has *told everyone*.
- **vv1–9 Reuben and Bilhah** — Genesis 35:21–22: *Israel pulled up and pitched his tent beyond Migdal-eder. And it came to pass… that Reuben went and lay with Bilhah his father's secondary wife. Israel heard.* Genesis says no more until Jacob's last words: *you went up on your father's bed. Then you defiled it* (Genesis 49:4). Jubilees tells the night from Bilhah's side. **"Migdal-eder"** — the EOTC writes *Magdaladrāʾef*; the Latin has *Migdal-Eder*.
- **vv10–20 the law** — Leviticus 18:8: *You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's wife*; Leviticus 20:11: *A man who lies with his father's wife has uncovered his father's nakedness. Both of them shall surely be put to death.* **v12 "written a second time"** — Deuteronomy 27:20: *Cursed is he who lies with his father's wife, for he has uncovered his father's skirt. And all the people shall say, Amen.* In Jubilees it is the holy ones of heaven who answer.
- **vv15–16 "Let them not say, 'Reuben had life and forgiveness'"** — Jubilees meets the obvious objection: Reuben was not put to death because the law had not yet been revealed in full. The law revealed to Moses leaves no such room.
- **vv21–23 the date and the sons** — the third year of the sixth week is 2145. The list is Genesis 35:23–26, with Dinah added.

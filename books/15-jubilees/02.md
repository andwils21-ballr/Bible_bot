# Jubilees 2

**1** The angel of the presence spoke to Moses by the word of the Lord, saying: Write down the whole account of creation: how in six days the Lord God finished all His work and all that He created, and kept sabbath on the seventh day, and made it holy for all ages, and set it as a sign for all His works.

**2** For on the first day He created the heavens above, and the earth, and the waters, and every spirit that serves before Him: the angels of the presence, and the angels of holiness, and the angels of the spirit of fire, and the angels of the spirit of the winds, and the angels of the spirit of the clouds, for darkness and for all things, for hail and for snow; and the angels of the voices and the thunders and the lightnings; and the angels of the spirits of cold and of heat, of winter and of spring, of autumn and of summer; and all the spirits of His works in the heavens and on the earth and in all the deeps; and darkness and light, dawn and evening, which He prepared in the knowledge of His heart.

**3** Then we saw His works, and we blessed Him and praised Him before Him for all His works, for He made seven great works on the first day.

**4** On the second day He made the expanse in the middle of the waters, and on that day the waters were divided: half of them went up above, and half of them went down below the expanse, in the middle, over the face of the whole earth. This was the only work He made on the second day.

**5** On the third day He did as He had said: He told the waters to pass from the face of the whole earth into one place, and the dry ground to appear.

**6** The waters did as He told them: they withdrew from the face of the earth into one place outside this expanse, and the dry ground appeared.

**7** On that day He created for it all the seas, each in its own gathering, and all the rivers, and the gatherings of the waters in the mountains and over all the earth, and all the pools, and all the dew of the earth, and the seed that is sown, each with its own seed, and everything that is eaten, and the fruit-bearing trees, and the forests, and the garden of Eden in Eden, for delight. These were the four great kinds He made on the third day.

**8** On the fourth day the Lord made the sun and the moon and the stars, and set them in the expanse of heaven to give light on all the earth, to rule over the day and the night, and to divide the light from the darkness.

**9** The Lord made the sun a great sign on the earth for days and for sabbaths, for months and for festivals, for years and for sabbaths of years, for jubilees, and for all the seasons of the years.

**10** It divides between light and darkness, and it is for well-being, so that everything that sprouts and grows on the earth may prosper. These were the three kinds He made on the fourth day.

**11** On the fifth day He created the great sea-monsters in the middle of the depths of the waters, for these were the first flesh made by His hands; and everything that moves in the waters, the fish; and everything that flies, the birds, and all their kinds.

**12** The sun rose over them for their well-being, and over everything that is on the earth, everything that sprouts from the earth, and all the fruit-bearing trees, and all flesh. These were the three kinds He made on the fifth day.

**13** On the sixth day He made all the wild animals of the earth, and all the cattle, and everything that moves on the earth.

**14** After all this He made humankind: a man and a woman He made them. He gave him rule over all that is on the earth and in the seas, over what flies, over the wild animals, over the cattle, over everything that moves on the earth, and over the whole earth: over all this He gave him rule. These were the four kinds He made on the sixth day.

**15** In all there were twenty-two kinds.

**16** He finished all His work on the sixth day: all that is in heaven and on the earth, in the seas and in the deeps, in the light and in the darkness, and in everything.

**17** He gave us a great sign, the sabbath day, that we should work six days and keep sabbath on the seventh day from all work.

**18** All the angels of the presence and all the angels of holiness, these two great kinds: He told us to keep sabbath with Him in heaven and on earth.

**19** He said to us, "I will set apart for Myself a people from among My nations, and they too will keep sabbath. I will make them holy for Myself as a people, and I will bless them. As I have made the sabbath day holy and keep it holy for Myself, so I will bless them, and they will be My people, and I will be their God.

**20** I have chosen the seed of Jacob out of all that I have seen, and I have written him down for Myself as a firstborn son, and made him holy for Myself forever and ever. I will teach them the sabbath days, so that they keep sabbath on them from all work."

**21** In this way He made in it a sign, by which they too keep sabbath with us on the seventh day, to eat and to drink and to bless the One who created all things, as He blessed and made holy for Himself a people that stands out from all the nations, so that they keep sabbath together with us.

**22** He made His will rise as a soothing aroma, accepted before Him all the days.

**23** There were twenty-two heads of mankind from Adam to Jacob, and twenty-two kinds of work were made until the seventh day. This one is blessed and holy, and that one too is blessed and holy; this one belongs with that one, for holiness and for blessing.

**24** It was granted to this seed to be blessed and holy for all days, by the testimony and the first law, as the sabbath day was made holy and blessed on the seventh day.

**25** He created the heavens and the earth and all that was created in six days, and God gave a holy festival day for all His works. So He commanded concerning it that whoever does any work on it shall die, and whoever defiles it shall surely die.

**26** As for you, command the sons of Israel to keep this day: to keep it holy, and not to do any work on it, and not to defile it, for it is holier than all days.

**27** Everyone who profanes it shall surely die, and everyone who does any work on it shall surely die forever, so that the sons of Israel may keep this day through their generations and not be uprooted from the land; for it is a holy day and a blessed day.

**28** Every man who keeps it and keeps sabbath on it from all his work will be holy and blessed all the days, like us.

**29** Make known and tell the sons of Israel the law of this day: that they keep sabbath on it and do not forsake it in the error of their heart; that they must not do on it any work that is not fitting, doing their own will on it; and that they must not prepare on it anything to eat or drink, or draw water, or bring in or take out through their gates any load that they had not prepared on the sixth day in their dwellings.

**30** They shall not take anything out or bring anything in, from house to house, on this day, for it is holier and more blessed than any jubilee day of the jubilees. On it we kept sabbath in the heavens before it was made known to any flesh to keep sabbath on it on the earth.

**31** The Creator of all blessed it, but He did not make all peoples and nations holy to keep sabbath on it, only Israel: to him alone He gave to eat and drink and keep sabbath on it on the earth.

**32** The Creator of all, who created this day, blessed it for blessing and for holiness and for glory above all days.

**33** This law and testimony was given to the sons of Israel as a law forever, for their generations.

## Notes

- **v1 "The angel of the presence spoke to Moses"** — from here on the angel of 1:27 speaks, and *we* in this chapter are the angels. The Greek, in a line that speaks of Moses in the third person, calls this angel *the archangel Gabriel*; the Ge'ez gives no name.
- **vv2–3 "every spirit that serves before Him… Then we saw His works, and we blessed Him"** — Genesis names no angels at creation. Jubilees makes them the first day's work, and they watch the rest and praise. God asks Job where he was *when the morning stars sang together and all the sons of God shouted for joy* (Job 38:7). *The voices and the thunders and the lightnings* are what John sees coming from the throne: *lightnings and voices and thunders* (Revelation 4:5).
- **vv7, 8, 10, 12, 14, 20, 23, 24, 25, 27, 29, the ends of the verses** — from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63). The EOTC text as held here breaks off before them. The Greek has the counts at the ends of vv10, 12 and 14.
- **v7 "These were the four great kinds He made on the third day"** — the counts matter to the chapter. Verse 15 adds up to twenty-two kinds, which is 7 + 1 + 4 + 3 + 3 + 4, and v10 speaks of *everything that sprouts and grows on the earth*, made here on the third day. Genesis also puts the plants on the third day (Genesis 1:11–12).
- **v9 "The Lord made the sun a great sign… for days and for sabbaths, for months and for festivals"** — Genesis gives the signs to both lamps: *let them be for signs, and for appointed times, and for days and years* (Genesis 1:14). Jubilees gives them to the sun alone. The reason comes at 6:36, where those who follow the moon make the seasons slip, *ten days too soon* each year.
- **vv15, 23 "twenty-two kinds… twenty-two heads of mankind from Adam to Jacob"** — the two numbers are made to match, as v23 goes on to say: twenty-two works in the six days, twenty-two fathers from Adam to Jacob. The names in Genesis 5 and 11, from Adam through Isaac to Jacob, come to twenty-two. The EOTC has *to him*; the Greek names *Jacob*, the *seed of Jacob* of v20.
- **v17 "a great sign, the sabbath day"** — the words of Exodus 31: *it is a sign between Me and you, through your generations* (31:13). Here the angels are given the sign first, and keep sabbath in heaven before any man on earth (v30).
- **vv19–20 "I will set apart for Myself a people… as a firstborn son"** — God's word to Pharaoh: *Israel is My son, My firstborn* (Exodus 4:22). *From among My nations*, *ʾaḥzābəya*: all the nations are His, and one is set apart.
- **v27 "Everyone who profanes it shall surely die"** — Exodus 31:14–15: *Whoever profanes it shall surely be put to death… Whoever does work on the sabbath day shall surely be put to death*.
- **v29 "doing their own will on it"** — Isaiah: *if you turn back your foot from the sabbath, from doing your pleasure on My holy day* (Isaiah 58:13).
- **v30 "They shall not take anything out or bring anything in, from house to house"** — Jeremiah: *Do not carry a load out of your houses on the sabbath day* (Jeremiah 17:22).

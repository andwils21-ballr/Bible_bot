# Jubilees 23

**1** He laid two of Jacob's fingers on his eyes, and blessed the God of gods, and covered his face and stretched out his feet, and slept the sleep of eternity, and was gathered to his fathers.

**2** Through all this Jacob was lying in his bosom, and did not know that Abraham, his father's father, had died.

**3** Jacob woke from his sleep, and Abraham was cold as ice, and he said, "Father, father"; but no one spoke, and he knew that he was dead.

**4** He rose from his bosom and ran and told Rebekah his mother; and Rebekah went to Isaac in the night and told him, and they went together, and Jacob with them, with a lamp in his hand; and when they went in they found Abraham lying dead.

**5** Isaac fell on his father's face, and wept and kissed him.

**6** A voice was heard in the house of Abraham; and Ishmael his son rose and went to Abraham his father, and wept over Abraham his father, he and all the house of Abraham, and they wept with a great weeping.

**7** His sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him in the double cave, near Sarah his wife; and they wept for him forty days, all the men of his house, and Isaac and Ishmael and all their sons, and all the sons of Keturah in their places; and the days of weeping for Abraham were ended.

**8** He lived three jubilees and four weeks of years, one hundred and seventy-five years, and completed the days of his life, old and full of days.

**9** For the days of the ancients, of their life, were nineteen jubilees; and after the flood they began to fall short of nineteen jubilees, and to grow old more quickly, and the days of their lives were shortened by the affliction of many things and the wickedness of their ways — except for Abraham.

**10** For Abraham was whole in all his deeds with the Lord, and pleasing in truth all the days of his life; yet he did not complete four jubilees in his life before he grew old because of the wickedness of men, and he was full of his days.

**11** All the generations that arise from now until the day of the great judgment will grow old quickly, before they complete two jubilees, and their understanding will leave them because of their old age, and all their knowledge will pass away.

**12** In those days, if a man lives a jubilee and a half of years, they will say of him, "He has lived long"; and the greater part of his days are sickness and toil and affliction, and there is no peace.

**13** For there is blow upon blow, and wound upon wound, and affliction upon affliction, and evil report upon evil report, and sickness upon sickness, and every evil judgment like these, one with another: sickness and overthrow, and snow and frost and ice, and fever and chills and torpor, and famine and death, and sword and captivity, and all kinds of plagues and pains.

**14** All this will come on an evil generation, which makes the earth sin with the uncleanness of fornication, and with defilement, and with the abominations of their deeds.

**15** Then they will say, "The days of the ancients were many, up to a thousand years, and good; but the days of our life, if a man lives long, are seventy years, and if he is very strong, eighty, and these are evil, and there is no peace at all in the days of this evil generation."

**16** In that generation the sons will rebuke their fathers and their elders for sin and for unrighteousness, and for the words of their mouth, and for the great wickedness that they do, because they have forsaken the covenant that God made between Himself and them, to keep and do all His commandments and His ordinances and all His laws, without turning aside to the right or to the left.

**17** For all have done evil, and every mouth speaks transgression, and all their works are uncleanness and hatred, and all their ways are defilement and abomination and destruction.

**18** The earth will be destroyed because of all their deeds, and there will be no seed of the vine and no oil, for all their deeds are faithless; and they will all perish together, wild animals and cattle and birds, and all the fish of the sea, because of the sons of men.

**19** They will quarrel with one another, the young with the old and the old with the young, the poor with the rich, the lowly with the great, and the beggar with the ruler, over the law and over the covenant; for they have forgotten commandment and covenant, and festival and month, and Sabbath and jubilee, and every judgment.

**20** They will stand with swords and with war to turn them back into the way, and they will not turn back until much blood is shed on the earth, one by another.

**21** Those who have escaped will not turn back from their wickedness to the way of righteousness, for they will all lift themselves up to deceit and to wealth, so that each may take all that is his neighbor's; and they will name the great name, but not in truth and not in righteousness; and they will defile the holy of holies with the uncleanness of the corruption of their defilement.

**22** There will be a great punishment from the Lord on the deeds of that generation, and He will give them over to the sword and to judgment, to captivity and to plunder and to being devoured.

**23** He will rouse against them the sinners of the nations, who have no mercy or compassion on them, and who will show favor to no one, neither old nor young nor anyone, for they are more wicked and strong to do evil than all the sons of men; and they will do violence in Israel and transgression against Jacob, and much blood will be shed on the earth, and there will be none to gather and none to bury.

**24** In those days they will cry out and call and pray to be saved from the hand of the sinners, the nations, and there will be none to save.

**25** The heads of the children will be white with gray hair, and a child three weeks old will look old like one a hundred years old, and their stature will be destroyed by affliction and distress.

**26** In those days the children will begin to search the laws, and to seek the commandment, and to return to the path of righteousness.

**27** The days will begin to grow many and increase, among the sons of men, generation after generation and day after day, until their days come near to a thousand years, and to more years than the many days before.

**28** There will be no old man, and none who is full of days, for all of them will be as children and boys.

**29** They will complete all their days and live in peace and joy, and there will be no Satan and no evil one who destroys; for all their days will be days of blessing and healing.

**30** Then the Lord will heal His servants, and they will rise and see great peace, and He will drive out His enemies; and the righteous will see and give thanks, and rejoice with joy forever and ever, and will see all His judgments and all His curses on their enemies.

**31** Their bones will rest in the earth, and their spirits will have much joy, and they will know that it is the Lord who executes judgment and shows mercy to hundreds and thousands, and to all who love Him.

**32** You too, Moses, write these words, for so they are written, and they are brought up into the testimony of the tablets of heaven for the eternal generations.

## Notes

- **vv1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 13, 15, 16, 17, 18, 30, 31** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter from v8 to v23, has vv9–18 too. Without the ends of vv3–7 the EOTC never says that anyone found Abraham dead or mourned him.
- **vv1–8 Abraham's death** — Genesis 25:7–9: *Abraham expired and died at a good gray age, old and satisfied, and he was gathered to his people. Isaac and Ishmael his sons buried him in the cave of Machpelah.* **v5 "Isaac fell on his father's face"** — as Joseph did at Jacob's death: *Joseph fell on his father's face, and wept over him, and kissed him* (Genesis 50:1); the forty days of weeping in v7 are the forty days of Genesis 50:3. **v8 "three jubilees and four weeks of years"** — 147 + 28 = 175.
- **vv9–15 the shortening of days** — the ancients, says v9, lived nineteen jubilees (931 years); Abraham, the most righteous since, did not reach four (196). **v15 "seventy years… eighty"** — Psalm 90:10: *The days of our years in them are seventy years, and if by strength, eighty years*.
- **vv16–25 the evil generation** — the sons rebuke the fathers, the covenant, the festivals, the months and the Sabbaths are forgotten, and the nations are sent against Israel. **v17 "every mouth speaks transgression"** — Paul, quoting the Psalms: *All have turned aside, together they have become worthless; there is no one who does good, not even one* (Romans 3:12). **v25 "a child three weeks old"** — elsewhere in Jubilees a *week* is seven years; Charles leaves it as *three weeks*.
- **vv26–31 the lengthening of days** — the turn comes when *the children will begin to search the laws* (v26), and life lengthens back toward a thousand years. Isaiah 65:20: *No more will there be from there an infant of days, or an old man who does not fill his days; for the youth shall die a hundred years old.* **v31 "Their bones will rest in the earth, and their spirits will have much joy"** — the body at rest in the ground, the spirit in joy; the second half is in Charles's bracket. **"to thousands… to all who love Him"** — Exodus 20:6: *doing loyal love to thousands, for those who love Me and keep My commandments*.
- **v30 "He will drive out His enemies"** — so the EOTC; Charles has *they shall… drive out their adversaries*.

# Jubilees 8

**1** In the twenty-ninth jubilee, in the first week, at its beginning, Arpachshad took a wife named Rasueya, the daughter of Susan, the daughter of Elam, and she bore him a son in the third year of this week, and he called his name Kainam.

**2** The son grew, and his father taught him writing, and he went to look for a place where he could take a city for himself.

**3** He found writing that earlier generations had carved on a rock, and he read what was on it and copied it, and went astray because of it; for it held the teaching of the Watchers, by which they observed the omens of the sun and moon and stars, and all the signs of heaven.

**4** He wrote it down and said nothing about it, for he was afraid to tell Noah about it, in case he would be angry with him because of it.

**5** In the thirtieth jubilee, in the second week, in its first year, he took a wife named Melka, the daughter of Madai, the son of Japheth[; and he fathered a son and called his name Shelah, for he said, "Truly I have been sent"].

**6** In the fourth year he was born; and Shelah grew up and took a wife named Muak, the daughter of Kesed, his father's brother[, in the thirty-first jubilee, in the fifth week, in its first year].

**7** She bore him a son in its fifth year, and he called his name Eber[; and he took a wife named Azurad, the daughter of Nebrod, in the thirty-second jubilee, in the seventh week, in its third year].

**8** In the sixth year she bore him a son, and he called his name Peleg[; for in the days when he was born the children of Noah began to divide the earth among themselves, and for this reason he called his name Peleg].

**9** They divided it wrongly among themselves, and told Noah.

**10** And it came to pass at the beginning of the thirty-third jubilee that they divided the earth into three portions, for Shem and Ham and Japheth, each by his inheritance[, in the first year of the first week, when one of us who had been sent was with them].

**11** He called his sons, and they came near to him, they and their children, and he divided the earth into the lots that his three sons would take[; and they reached out their hands and took the writing from the bosom of Noah their father].

**12** On the writing came out Shem's lot: the middle of the earth, which he would take as an inheritance for himself and his sons for the eternal generations[, from the middle of the mountain range of Rafa, from the mouth of the water of the river Tina. His portion goes toward the west through the middle of this river, and it goes as far as the water of the deeps out of which this river comes and pours its waters into the sea of Meat, and this river flows into the great sea. All that is toward the north is Japheth's, and all that is toward the south belongs to Shem].

**13** It goes as far as Karaso: this is in the bosom of the tongue that looks toward the south.

**14** His portion goes along the great sea, and it goes straight until it comes to the west of the tongue that looks toward the south[; for this sea is named the tongue of the Egyptian Sea].

**15** From there it turns toward the south, toward the mouth of the great sea, on the shore of its waters[, and it goes toward the west to Afra, and it goes until it reaches the waters of the river Gihon, and to the south of the waters of the Gihon, to the banks of this river].

**16** It goes toward the east until it comes to the garden of Eden, on its southern side [— to the south and the east of the whole land of Eden and of the whole east — and it turns to the east and goes until it reaches the east of the mountain named Rafa, and goes down to the bank of the mouth of the river Tina].

**17** This portion came out by lot for Shem and his sons, for them to hold forever, for his generations forever.

**18** Noah rejoiced that this portion came out for Shem and his sons[, and he remembered all that he had spoken with his mouth in prophecy, for he had said, "Blessed be the Lord, the God of Shem, and may the Lord dwell in the dwelling of Shem"].

**19** He knew that the garden of Eden is the holy of holies and the dwelling of the Lord, and Mount Sinai the center of the wilderness, and Mount Zion the center of the navel of the earth[: these three were created as holy places facing each other].

**20** He blessed the God of gods, who had put the word of the Lord in his mouth, and the Lord forever.

**21** He knew that a portion of blessing, a blessed one, had come to Shem and his sons for the eternal generations: the whole land of Eden, and the whole land of the Red Sea, and the whole land of the east, and India, and on the Red Sea and its mountains, and all the land of Bashan, and all the land of Lebanon, and the islands of Caphtor[, and all the mountains of Senir and Amana, and the mountains of Asshur in the north, and all the land of Elam, Asshur and Babel, and Susa and Madai, and all the mountains of Ararat, and all the region beyond the sea, beyond the mountains of Asshur toward the north: a blessed and spacious land, and all that is in it is very good].

**22** For Ham the second portion came out, beyond the Gihon, toward the south, to the right of the garden, and it goes toward the south, and it goes along all the mountains of fire[, and it goes toward the west to the sea of Atel, and it goes toward the west until it reaches the sea of Mauk, the sea into which everything that is not destroyed goes down].

**23** It goes toward the north to the edge of Gadir, and it comes to the shore of the waters of the sea, to the waters of the great sea, until it comes near the river Gihon; and it goes along the river Gihon until it comes to the right of the garden of Eden.

**24** This is the land that came out for Ham as the portion he would hold forever, for himself and his sons, for their generations forever.

**25** For Japheth the third portion came out, beyond the river Tina, to the north of the outflow of its waters, and it goes to the northeast, across the whole region of Gog and all the country east of them.

**26** It goes north, to the far north, and it goes toward the mountains of Qelt, toward the north, and toward the sea of Mauk, and it comes to the east of Gadir, as far as the region of the waters of the sea.

**27** It goes until it comes near the west of Fara[, and it turns toward Aferag, and it goes east to the waters of the sea of Meat].

**28** It goes toward the region of the river Tina, to the northeast, until it comes near the edge of its waters toward the mountain Rafa, and it turns around toward the north.

**29** This is the land that came out for Japheth and his sons as the portion of his inheritance, which he would hold for himself and his sons for their generations forever[: five great islands, and a great land in the north].

**30** But it is cold, and the land of Ham is hot, and the land of Shem is neither hot nor cold, for it is a mixture of cold and heat.

## Notes

- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. They follow Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts: vv5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 18, 19, 21, 22, 27 and 29. The Greek, a list of wives, agrees with vv5–7: *Melcha, the daughter of Madai, the son of Japheth*; *Moacha, the daughter of Keedam*; *Azurah, the daughter of Nimrod*.
- **North and south in this chapter** — the Ge'ez words are *samen* and *dabub*, which Charles renders **south** and **north**. The chapter bears him out: Japheth's land, on the *dabub* side (vv25–28), is the cold one, and Ham's, on the *samen* side (v22), is the hot one (v30). The English follows the chapter.
- **vv1–4 "Kainam"** — a son of Arpachshad whom the Hebrew of Genesis 11:12 does not have: there Arpachshad fathers Shelah. The Greek of Genesis 11:12–13 has him, *Arpachshad lived a hundred and thirty-five years, and fathered Kainan*, and so does Luke's genealogy of Jesus: *the son of Kainam, the son of Arpachshad, the son of Shem* (Luke 3:36). The teaching he copies from the rock is what the Watchers taught in 1 Enoch 8:3: *astrology… the signs of the stars… the omens of the sun*.
- **v8 "Peleg"** — Genesis 10:25: *the name of the one was Peleg, for in his days the earth was divided*. **v9 "They divided it wrongly"** — *ba-ʾəkuy*, **in evil**; Charles has *secretly*.
- **v19 "Mount Zion the center of the navel of the earth"** — Ezekiel speaks of the people *who dwell at the navel of the earth* (Ezekiel 38:12), and says of Jerusalem, *I have set her in the middle of the nations* (Ezekiel 5:5). Eden, Sinai and Zion are three of the Lord's four places named at 4:26.
- **v20 "the God of gods"** — *the LORD your God is God of gods and Lord of lords* (Deuteronomy 10:17).
- **v21 "the Red Sea"** — *baḥra ʾErtrā*, **the sea of Eritrea**, from the Greek name for it, the *Erythraean* (**red**) sea. *India* is *ʾƎndake*.
- **v30 "neither hot nor cold"** — Shem's land is the middle land in climate as in place.

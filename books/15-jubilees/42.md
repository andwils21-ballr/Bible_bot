# Jubilees 42

**1** In the first year of the third week of the forty-fifth jubilee the famine began to come on the land, and the rain refused to be given to the earth, for none at all came down.

**2** The earth grew barren[; but in the land of Egypt there was bread, for Joseph had gathered the grain of the land in the seven years of abundance and was keeping it].

**3** The Egyptians came to Joseph, for him to give them food, and he opened the storehouses where the grain of the first year was, and sold it to the peoples of the land for gold.

**4** [The famine grew very severe in the land of Canaan.] Jacob heard that there was food in Egypt, and he sent his ten sons to get food for him in Egypt; but Benjamin he did not send[; and the ten sons of Jacob came into Egypt].

**5** Joseph recognized them, but they did not recognize him[; and he spoke harshly to them and said to them, "You have come to spy out the land"; and he put them in custody].

**6** After that he let them go, and kept back Simeon alone, and sent away his nine brothers.

**7** He filled their sacks with grain, and put their gold back in their sacks, and they did not know.

**8** He commanded them to bring their younger brother, for they had told him that their father was alive, and their younger brother.

**9** They went up from the land of Egypt and came to the land of Canaan, and told their father all that had happened to them, and how the lord of the land had spoken harshly with them and had seized Simeon until they should bring Benjamin.

**10** Jacob said, "You have bereaved me! Joseph is not, and Simeon is not, and Benjamin you would take from me. On me your evil has come."

**11** He said, "My son will not go with you, in case he falls sick[; for their mother bore these two, and one has perished; and if you take this one, and some sickness befalls him on the way, you will bring down my gray hairs with sorrow to Sheol]."

**12** For he saw that all their gold had come back, each in his sack, and for this reason he was afraid to send him.

**13** The famine grew and became severe in the land of Canaan, and in all the land except the land of Egypt[; for many of the sons of the Egyptians had stored up their seed ever since they saw Joseph gathering grain and laying it up in storehouses, so that it might be kept for the years of famine].

**14** The people of Egypt fed on it in the first year of the famine.

**15** When Israel saw that the famine was very severe in the land, and there was no deliverance, he said to his sons, "Go back, and get food for us, so that we do not die."

**16** They said, "We will not go. Unless our youngest brother comes with us, we will not go."

**17** Israel saw that if he did not send him with them, they would all perish before the famine.

**18** Reuben said, "Give him into my hand, and if I do not bring him back to you, kill my two sons for his life."

**19** [He said to him, "He will not go with you."] Judah came near and said, "Send him with us, and if I do not bring him back to you, let me bear the guilt before you all the days of my life."

**20** He sent him with them in the second year of this week, on the new moon of the month, and they came to the land of Egypt with all who were going, with gifts in their hands: stacte and almonds and terebinth nuts and honeycombs.

**21** They came and stood before Joseph, and he saw Benjamin his brother and recognized him, and said to them, "Is this your youngest brother?" They said to him, "It is he." [He said, "The Lord be gracious to you, my son."]

**22** He sent him into his house, and brought Simeon out to them[; and he made a feast for them, and they presented to him the gift they had brought in their hands].

**23** They ate before him, and he gave them all a portion, and Benjamin's portion was seven times larger than the portion of any of them.

**24** They ate and drank, and rose and stayed with their donkeys.

**25** Joseph thought of a plan by which he might know their thoughts, whether there were thoughts of peace among them[; and he said to the steward who was over his house, "Fill all their sacks with food, and put their money back in their vessels; and my cup, the silver cup that I drink from, put it in the sack of the youngest, and send them away."]

## Notes

- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. In vv2, 4, 5, 11 and 13 they follow the Latin, which covers this chapter from v2 to v14; in vv19, 21, 22 and 25 they follow Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts. Without v25's bracket the Ge'ez never says what Joseph's plan was.
- **vv1–14 the first journey** — Genesis 42, in brief. **v1 the date** — the first year of the third week of the forty-fifth jubilee is 2171, the year after Perez and Zerah (41:21) and the end of the years of plenty (41:22). **v5 "You have come to spy out the land!"** — the Latin; Genesis 42:9 has *You are spies*. **v11** — Genesis 42:38: *If harm befalls him on the road you go, you will bring my gray head down in sorrow to Sheol.* **v13 "many of the sons of the Egyptians had stored up their seed"** — Jubilees explains why Egypt could eat in the first year of famine; Genesis does not say.
- **vv15–25 the second journey** — Genesis 43–44. **v18 Reuben's offer** — Genesis 42:37. **v19 Judah's pledge** — Genesis 43:9: *I myself will stand as the pledge for him… If I do not bring him to you and set him before you, then I will have sinned against you all my days.* **v20 the date and the gifts** — the second year of this week is 2172. Genesis 43:11 has *a little balm and a little honey, gum and ladanum, pistachios and almonds*; Jubilees has *stacte and almonds and terebinth nuts and honeycombs*. **v21** — Genesis 43:29: *God be gracious to you, my son.* **v23 "seven times larger"** — Genesis 43:34 has *five times*.

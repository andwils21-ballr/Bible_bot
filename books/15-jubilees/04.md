# Jubilees 4

**1** In the third week of the second jubilee she bore Cain, and in the fourth she bore Abel[, and in the fifth she bore her daughter Awan].

**2** In the first year of the third jubilee Cain killed [Abel, because God accepted the sacrifice of Abel and did not accept the offering of Cain].

**3** He killed him in the field, and his blood cried out from the ground to heaven, lamenting because he had been killed.

**4** The Lord rebuked Cain because of Abel, because he had killed him, and He made him a fugitive on the earth because of his brother's blood, and He cursed him on the earth.

**5** For this reason it is written on the tablets of heaven, "Cursed is he who strikes his neighbor with evil intent," and all who have seen it shall say, "So be it"; and the man who has seen it and not told it, let him be cursed like him.

**6** For this reason, when we come before the Lord our God, we make known all the sin that is done in heaven and on earth, in light and in darkness and everywhere.

**7** Adam and his wife mourned for Abel four weeks of years[, and in the fourth year of the fifth week they became glad, and Adam knew his wife again, and she bore him a son, and he called his name Seth, for he said, "The Lord has raised up a second seed for us on the earth in place of Abel, because Cain killed him"].

**8** In the sixth week she bore his daughter Azura.

**9** Cain took Awan his sister as his wife, and she bore him Enoch at the end of the fourth jubilee[. In the first year of the first week of the fifth jubilee houses were built on the earth, and Cain built a city and called its name after the name of his son Enoch].

**10** Adam knew Eve his wife, and she bore nine more children.

**11** In the fifth week of the fifth jubilee Seth took Azura his sister as his wife, and in its fourth year she bore him Enosh.

**12** He was the first to call on the name of the Lord on the earth.

**13** In the seventh jubilee, in the third week, Enosh took Noam his sister as his wife, and she bore him a son in the third year of the fifth week, and he called his name Kenan.

**14** At the end of the eighth jubilee Kenan took Mualeleth his sister as his wife, and she bore him a son in the ninth jubilee, in the first week, in the third year of this week, and he called his name Mahalalel.

**15** In the second week of the tenth jubilee Mahalalel took for himself a wife, Dinah, the daughter of Barakiel, the daughter of his father's sister[, and she bore him a son in the third week, in the sixth year, and he called his name Jared; for in his days the angels of the Lord came down to the earth, those who are named the Watchers, to teach the children of men and to do judgment and uprightness on the earth].

**16** In the eleventh jubilee Jared took for himself a wife named Baraka, the daughter of Rasuyal, the daughter of his father's sister, in the fourth week of this jubilee[, and she bore him a son in the fifth week, in the fourth year of the jubilee, and he called his name Enoch].

**17** He was the first of the sons of men born on the earth to learn writing and knowledge and wisdom[, and to write down the signs of heaven by the order of their months in a book, so that men might know the seasons of the years by the order of each of their months].

**18** He was the first to write a testimony, and he testified to the sons of men among the generations of the earth. He told the weeks of the jubilees and made known the days of the years; he set the months in order and told the sabbaths of the years, as we made them known to him.

**19** What had been and what will be he saw in a vision in his sleep, how it will be for the sons of men through their generations until the day of judgment. He saw and understood everything[, and he wrote his testimony and placed the testimony on the earth for all the children of men and for their generations].

**20** In the twelfth jubilee, in its seventh week, he took for himself a wife named Edni, the daughter of Daniel, the daughter of his father's sister[, and in the sixth year of this week she bore him a son, and he called his name Methuselah].

**21** He was with the angels of God for six jubilees of years[, and they showed him everything on earth and in the heavens, the rule of the sun, and he wrote down everything].

**22** He testified to the Watchers, who had sinned with the daughters of men[; for these had begun to join themselves with the daughters of men and be defiled, and Enoch testified against them all].

**23** He was taken up from among the sons of men, and we brought him into the garden of Eden in greatness and honor[, and there he writes down the condemnation and judgment of the world, and all the wickedness of the children of men].

**24** Because of him the waters of the flood did not come over all the land of Eden, for he was set there as a sign, to testify against all the sons of men and to tell all the deeds of the generations until the day of judgment.

**25** He burned the evening incense of the sanctuary, which is acceptable before the Lord, on the Mountain of Midday.

**26** For the Lord has four places on the earth: the garden of Eden, and the Mountain of the East, and this mountain on which you are today, Mount Sinai, and Mount Zion, which will be made holy in the new creation for the hallowing of the earth. Through it the earth will be made holy from all guilt and from uncleanness through the generations of the world.

**27** In the fourteenth jubilee Methuselah took as his wife Edna, the daughter of Ezrael, the daughter of his father's sister, in the third week, in the first year of this week; and he fathered a son and called his name Lamech.

**28** In the fifteenth jubilee, in the third week, Lamech took a wife named Betenos, the daughter of Barakiel, the daughter of his father's sister, and in this week she bore him a son, and he called his name Noah, saying, "This one will comfort me for my sorrow and for all my work, and for the ground that the Lord has cursed."

**29** At the end of the nineteenth jubilee, in the seventh week, in its sixth year, Adam died, and all his sons buried him in the land of his creation[, and he was the first to be buried in the earth].

**30** He was seventy years short of a thousand years, for a thousand years are one day in the testimony of the heavens[; and so it was written about the tree of knowledge, "On the day you eat from it you will die." For this reason he did not complete the years of this day, for he died within it].

**31** At the end of this jubilee Cain was killed, after him, in the same year. His house fell on him, and he died in the middle of his house and was killed by its stones[, for he too had killed Abel with stones].

**32** For this reason it was ordained on the tablets of heaven: with the instrument with which a man kills his neighbor, with it he shall be killed; as he wounded him, so they shall do to him.

**33** In the twenty-fifth jubilee Noah took for himself a wife named Emzara, the daughter of Rakiel, the daughter of his [father's] sister[, in the first year of the fifth week; and in its third year she bore him Shem, in its fifth year she bore him Ham, and in the first year of the sixth week she bore him Japheth].

## Notes

- **Verses 3–14 and v2** — the EOTC text here has verses 1–2 and then 15–33. Its v2 runs from *Cain killed* straight into the last words of v14 (*…and he called his name Mahalalel*). Verses 3–14 are rendered from the second Ge'ez text, which has them (its v11 holds v12 as well), and the stray end of v2 is rendered where it belongs, in v14.
- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. In v31 they follow the Greek: *for he too had killed Abel with stones*. The rest follow Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts: vv1, 2, 7, 9, 15, 16, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 29, 30 and 33. The Greek, a list of dates and wives, agrees in substance where it overlaps: a daughter born after Abel (v1), Abel killed and mourned *four week-periods, that is, twenty-eight years* (vv2, 7).
- **The dates** — a jubilee is 49 years and a week 7, so Adam's death *at the end of the nineteenth jubilee, in the seventh week, in its sixth year* (v29) is 18 × 49 + 6 × 7 + 6 = 930, Adam's age in Genesis: *All the days of Adam that he lived were nine hundred and thirty years* (Genesis 5:5). Abel dies in the first year of the third jubilee, year 99; four weeks of mourning bring it to 127; Seth is born three years later, in year 130 (v7), as in Genesis 5:3, *Adam lived a hundred and thirty years… and called his name Seth*.
- **v5 "Cursed is he who strikes his neighbor with evil intent," and all… shall say, "So be it"** — Deuteronomy's curses: *Cursed is he who strikes his neighbor in secret. All the people shall say, "Amen"* (Deuteronomy 27:24). The one who saw and did not tell is the witness of Leviticus 5:1: *if he does not tell it, he carries his guilt*.
- **vv15–33 "the daughter of his father's sister"** — so the Ge'ez, for every wife from Dinah on. The Greek has *the daughter of Barachiel, his father's brother*, and Charles the same. At v33 the Ge'ez has only *the daughter of his sister*; *father's* is supplied from Charles, as the rest of the chapter has it. The early fathers marry their sisters (vv9–14); from Mahalalel on, their cousins.
- **v15 "for in his days the angels of the Lord came down"** — *Jared* is Hebrew *Yered*, **coming down**; see 1 Enoch 6:6. In Jubilees the Watchers come down to teach and to do justice, and go wrong only afterward (v22), where 1 Enoch has them come down already sworn to sin.
- **vv17–19 "the first… to learn writing… the signs of heaven… a vision in his sleep"** — the books of 1 Enoch: the Astronomical Book, on the months and the signs of heaven (1 Enoch 72–82), and the Dream Visions (83–90). Jude calls him *Enoch, the seventh from Adam* (Jude 14).
- **vv21–23 "with the angels of God for six jubilees… we brought him into the garden of Eden"** — Genesis: *Enoch walked with God after he fathered Methuselah three hundred years… He was not, for God took him* (Genesis 5:22–24). Six jubilees are 294 years.
- **v24 "the waters of the flood did not come over all the land of Eden"** — so both Ge'ez texts, with *not*. Charles reads the opposite: *on account of it (God) brought the waters of the flood upon all the land of Eden*.
- **v25 "the evening incense of the sanctuary… on the Mountain of Midday"** — *dabra qatr*, **the mountain of noon**; Charles has only *the Mount*. Enoch offers the incense that Aaron will burn *between the evenings* (Exodus 30:8), as Adam did on leaving the garden (3:27).
- **vv29–30 "a thousand years are one day"** — the psalm: *a thousand years in Your eyes are like yesterday when it passes* (Psalm 90:4); Peter: *with the Lord one day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years like one day* (2 Peter 3:8). So *on the day you eat from it you will die* (Genesis 2:17) came true: Adam died within his first thousand-year day.
- **vv31–32 "with the instrument with which a man kills his neighbor, with it he shall be killed"** — the law of like for like: *as he has done, so it shall be done to him* (Leviticus 24:19).

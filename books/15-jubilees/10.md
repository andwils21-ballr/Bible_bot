# Jubilees 10

**1** In the third week of this jubilee the unclean demons began to lead astray the children of Noah's sons, and to make them foolish and destroy them.

**2** Noah's sons came to Noah their father and told him about the demons who were leading astray and blinding and killing his sons' sons.

**3** He prayed before the Lord his God and said, "God of the spirits that are in all flesh, You who have shown me mercy and saved me and my sons from the waters of the flood, and did not make me perish as You did the children of destruction — for great has been Your grace toward me, and great Your mercy on my soul: let Your grace be lifted up over my sons, and do not let wicked spirits rule over them, or they will destroy them from the earth.

**4** Bless me and my children, so that we may grow and multiply and fill the earth.

**5** You know what Your Watchers, the fathers of these spirits, did in my days; and as for these spirits that are alive, imprison them and hold them fast in the place of condemnation, and do not let them bring destruction on the sons of Your servant, my God, for they are cruel and were created to destroy.

**6** Do not let them rule over the spirits of the living, for You alone know their judgment; and let them have no power over the children of the righteous from now on and forever."

**7** Our God told us to bind them all.

**8** The chief of the spirits, Mastema, came and said, "Lord, Creator, leave some of them before me, and let them listen to my voice and do all that I tell them. For if some of them are not left to me, I will not be able to exercise the authority of my will among the sons of men; for these are for corruption and for leading astray before my judgment, for great is the wickedness of the sons of men."

**9** He said, "Let a tenth of them remain before him, and let him send nine parts down into the place of condemnation."

**10** He told one of us that we should teach Noah all their healings, for He knew that they would not walk in uprightness or strive in righteousness.

**11** We did all that He said: all the evil ones who are cruel we bound in the place of condemnation, and a tenth of them we left to be subject before Satan on the earth.

**12** We told Noah all the healing of their diseases, together with their ways of leading astray, so that he could heal with the plants of the earth.

**13** Noah wrote down in a book everything we taught him, every kind of healing, and the evil spirits were shut out from following the sons of Noah.

**14** He gave all the books he had written to Shem, his eldest son, for he loved him most of all his sons.

**15** Noah slept with his fathers and was buried on Mount Lubar in the land of Ararat.

**16** He completed nine hundred and fifty years in his life: nineteen jubilees and two weeks and five years.

**17** In his life on the earth he surpassed the sons of men, because of his righteousness, in which he was perfect — all except Enoch; for Enoch's office was ordained as a testimony to the generations of the world, so that he should tell all the deeds of generation after generation until the day of judgment.

**18** In the thirty-third jubilee, in the first year of this second week, Peleg took a wife named Lomna, the daughter of Sinaor, and she bore him a son in the fourth year of this week, and he called his name Reu; for he said, "Here — the sons of men have become evil through the counsel to build themselves a city and a tower in the land of Shinar."

**19** For they had moved from the land of Ararat eastward to Shinar, and in his days they built the city and the tower, saying, "Let us go up by it into heaven."

**20** They began to build, and in the fourth week they baked bricks with fire, and the bricks served them as stone; and the clay with which they plastered them was asphalt, which comes out of the sea and out of the springs of water in the land of Shinar.

**21** They built it for forty-three years, with whole bricks, thirteen within it, and a third of one for its height; its height rose to five thousand four hundred and thirty-three cubits.

**22** The Lord our God said to us, "Here — they are one people, and they have begun to act, and now nothing will be held back from them. Come, let us go down and confuse their language, so that they do not understand one another's speech, and they are scattered into cities and nations, and one purpose will no longer remain with them until the day of judgment."

**23** The Lord came down, and we came down with Him, to see the city and the tower that the sons of men had built.

**24** He confused every sound of their languages, and no one understood the speech of another any longer, and they stopped building the city and the tower.

**25** For this reason the whole land of Shinar is called Babel, because there God confused all the languages of the sons of men, and from there they were scattered into their cities, each by his language and his nation.

**26** The Lord sent a great wind against their tower and threw it down to the earth; and here it is, between Asshur and Babylon in the land of Shinar, and He called its name "Overthrow."

**27** In the fourth week, in the first year, at its beginning, in the thirty-fourth jubilee, they were scattered from the land of Shinar.

**28** Ham and his sons went into the land he was to hold, which he had received as his portion, in the land of the south.

**29** Canaan saw the land of Lebanon, as far as the river of Egypt, that it was very good, and he did not go into the land of his inheritance, to the west, to the sea; and he lived in the land of Lebanon, east and west, from the border of the Jordan and from the border of the sea.

**30** Ham his father and Cush and Egypt his brothers said to him, "You have settled in a land that is not yours and did not come out for us by lot. Do not do this; for if you do, you and your sons will fall in the land and be cursed through rebellion. By rebellion you have settled, and by rebellion your children will fall, and you will be uprooted forever.

**31** Do not live in the dwelling of Shem, for it came to Shem and to his sons by their lot.

**32** Cursed are you, and cursed you will be above all the sons of Noah, by the curse to which we bound ourselves with an oath before the holy Judge and before Noah our father."

**33** He did not listen to them, and he lived in the land of Lebanon, from Hamath to the entrance of Egypt, he and his sons, to this day.

**34** For this reason that land is called the land of Canaan.

**35** Japheth and his sons went toward the sea and lived in the land of their portion. Madai saw the land of the sea, and it did not please him, and he begged a portion from Elam and Asshur and Arpachshad, his wife's brother, and he has lived in the land of Media, near his wife's brother, to this day.

**36** He called his dwelling and the dwelling of his sons Media, after the name of their father Madai.

## Notes

- **vv3, 5, 8, 16, 17, 22, 25, 26, 29, 30, 31** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63). In v16 the EOTC stops at *two weeks*; the Asmara printing's *five years* complete the sum: nineteen jubilees and two weeks are 945 years, and the five years bring them to the 950 of v16 and of Genesis 9:29, *All the days of Noah were nine hundred and fifty years*.
- **v3 "God of the spirits that are in all flesh"** — Moses and Aaron's cry in the wilderness: *God, the God of the spirits of all flesh* (Numbers 16:22). Noah asks the God of spirits to deal with spirits.
- **vv5–11 "Your Watchers, the fathers of these spirits… a tenth of them we left"** — the demons are the spirits of the Watchers' dead sons, as in 1 Enoch 15:9: *Evil spirits came out from their bodies*. Nine tenths are bound; one tenth is left to *Mastema*, who is also called *Satan* (v11). The Greek has the same division: *a tenth of them was given to him… so as to tempt humankind… while the remaining nine parts were cast into the abyss*. **"The chief of the spirits"** — *malʾaka manāfəst*, which Charles renders *chief*.
- **vv12–14 "healing… with the plants of the earth… He gave all the books he had written to Shem"** — the book of healing is Noah's gift against the demons. The lore passes down through Shem, as Enoch's did through Methuselah (7:38).
- **v18 "Reu"** — Genesis 11:18. Jubilees explains the name from the building of Babel, as Peleg's was explained from the division (8:8).
- **vv19–26 the tower** — Genesis 11:2–9: *Come, let us build for ourselves a city, and a tower with its head in the heavens… The LORD came down to see the city and the tower… Therefore its name was called Babel*. In Jubilees the angels come down with Him (v23), and a wind throws the tower down (v26), which Genesis does not tell. The Greek has the same forty-three years of building and the same height.
- **v21 the measures** — *thirteen within it, and a third of one for its height* is all the Ge'ez gives. Charles's text and the Greek have the fuller measure: a breadth of 203 bricks, the height of a brick a third of one, a height of 5,433 cubits and two palms, and walls of thirteen and thirty stadia. The Ge'ez keeps the thirteen and the third, and the height.
- **v28 "in the land of the south"** — *dabub*, which here, with Charles, is **south**: Ham's is the hot land (8:22–30). In chapter 8 Charles renders the same word *north*. The Ge'ez directions do not keep one meaning throughout, and the English follows the geography each time.
- **vv29–34 Canaan** — Canaan breaks the oath of 9:14–15 and takes Shem's land, from *Hamath to the entrance of Egypt*. The land bears his name, but by the lot it was Shem's.
- **v35 "Elam and Asshur and Arpachshad"** — so the Ge'ez; Charles again has *Ham* for *Elam* (as at 9:2). **"Media"** — the Ge'ez writes *Medqin* and *Mediqin*; v36 derives it from *Madai*.

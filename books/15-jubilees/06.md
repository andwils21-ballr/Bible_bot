# Jubilees 6

**1** On the new moon of the third month he went out of the ark[, and built an altar on that mountain].

**2** He appeared on the earth, and took a young goat, and made atonement with its blood for all the guilt of the earth, for everything that had been on it had been wiped out except those who were in the ark with Noah.

**3** He offered up the fat on the altar, and took an ox and a goat and a sheep and kids, and salt, and a turtledove and a young dove[, and placed a burnt offering on the altar, and poured on it an offering mixed with oil, and sprinkled wine and scattered frankincense over everything, and made a soothing aroma rise, acceptable before the Lord].

**4** The Lord smelled the soothing aroma, and He made a covenant with him that there should never again be a flood to ruin the earth: all the days of the earth, seed-time and harvest shall not cease; cold and heat, summer and winter, and day and night shall not change their order, and shall never cease.

**5** "As for you, be fruitful and multiply on the earth, and increase on it, and be a blessing on it. The fear of you and the dread of you [I will put on everything that is on the earth and in the sea].

**6** Here — I have given you all the wild animals, and all the cattle, and all that flies, and all that moves on the earth, and the fish in the waters, and everything for food. Like the green plants, I have given you everything to eat.

**7** But flesh with its life, with its blood, you shall not eat, for the life of all flesh is in the blood; otherwise your blood, your lives, will be required. From the hand of every man, from the hand of every living thing, I will require the blood of man.

**8** Whoever sheds the blood of man, by man his blood shall be shed, for in the image of the Lord He made Adam.

**9** As for you, be fruitful and multiply on the earth."

**10** Noah and his sons swore that they would not eat any blood that is in any flesh[, and he made a covenant before the Lord God forever, through all the generations of the earth, in this month].

**11** For this reason He told you, too, to make a covenant with the sons of Israel in this month on the mountain, with an oath[, and to sprinkle blood on them because of all the words of the covenant that the Lord made with them forever].

**12** This testimony is written concerning you, that you keep it all the days, so that you do not eat on any day any blood of wild animals or birds, all the days of the earth[; and the man who eats the blood of wild animals or cattle or birds during all the days of the earth, he and his seed shall be uprooted from the land].

**13** As for you, command the sons of Israel not to eat any blood, so that their name and their seed may be before the Lord our God all the days.

**14** For this law there is no limit of days, for it is forever. They shall keep it through their generations, so that they continue to plead for themselves with blood before the altar; every day, morning and evening, they make atonement for themselves continually before the Lord, so that they keep it and are not uprooted.

**15** He gave Noah and his sons a sign that there would not again be a flood on the earth.

**16** He set His bow in the cloud as a sign of the eternal covenant[, that there should not again be a flood on the earth to ruin it, all the days of the earth].

**17** For this reason it is ordained and written on the tablets of heaven that they should keep the Festival of Weeks in this month, once a year, to renew the covenant every year.

**18** This whole festival was kept in heaven from the day of creation until the days of Noah, twenty-six jubilees and five weeks of years; and Noah and his sons kept it seven jubilees and one week of years, until the day of Noah's death. From the day of Noah's death his sons corrupted it, until the days of Abraham, and they ate blood.

**19** Abraham alone kept it, and Isaac and Jacob, his sons, kept it until your days[; and in your days the sons of Israel forgot it, until you kept it anew on this mountain].

**20** As for you, command the sons of Israel to keep this festival in all their generations as a commandment for them: one day in the year, in this month, they shall keep the festival.

**21** For it is the Festival of Weeks and the Festival of First Fruits. This festival is twofold and of two kinds; keep it as it is written and engraved concerning it.

**22** For I have written it in the book of the first law, in what I wrote for you, that you should keep it in its season, one day in the year[; and I explained to you its sacrifices, so that the sons of Israel should remember it and keep it through their generations in this month, one day every year].

**23** On the new moon of the first month, and on the new moon of the fourth month, [and on the new moon of the seventh month,] and on the new moon of the tenth month: these are days of remembrance, and they are the days of the seasons [in the four divisions of the year. They are written and ordained as a testimony forever].

**24** Noah ordained them for himself as festivals for the eternal generations, so that they became a memorial for him.

**25** On the new moon of the first month he was told to make the ark, and on it the earth dried, and he opened the ark and saw the earth.

**26** On the new moon of the fourth month the mouths of the depths of the abyss below were closed, and on the new moon of the seventh month all the mouths of the depths of the earth were opened, and the waters began to go down into them.

**27** On the new moon of the tenth month the tops of the mountains appeared, and Noah was glad.

**28** For this reason he ordained them for himself as festivals of remembrance forever.

**29** They placed them on the tablets of heaven[; each had thirteen weeks, and their memorial passed from one to another, from the first to the second, and from the second to the third, and from the third to the fourth].

**30** All the days of the commandments will be fifty-two weeks of days[, and these will make the whole year complete].

**31** So it is engraved and ordained on the tablets of heaven[. There is no neglecting it for a single year, or from year to year].

**32** As for you, command the sons of Israel to keep the years by this number, three hundred and sixty-four days, and it will be a complete year[; and they will not disturb its time from its days and from its festivals, for everything will happen in them according to their testimony, and they will not leave out any day or disturb any festival].

**33** If they transgress and do not keep them as He commanded, then they will all corrupt their seasons, and the years will be shifted from this order. They will corrupt the seasons, and the years will be shifted, and they will transgress their ordinances.

**34** All the sons of Israel will forget, and will not find the path of the years[, and will forget the new moons and seasons and sabbaths, and will go wrong in all the order of the years].

**35** I know it. [From now on I will make it known to you; it is not of my own devising, for the book lies written before me, and on the tablets of heaven the division of days is ordained, so that they do not forget the festivals of the covenant and walk by the festivals of the nations, after their error and after their ignorance.]

**36** There will be those who watch the moon closely[, how it disturbs the seasons and comes in ten days too soon from year to year].

**37** For this reason years will come upon them in which they will corrupt the order and make the day of testimony a worthless day[, and an unclean day a festival, and they will confuse all the days, the holy with the unclean and the unclean day with the holy; for they will go wrong as to the months and sabbaths and festivals and jubilees].

**38** For this reason I command you and testify to you, so that you may testify to them. For after your death your children will corrupt it, so that they do not make the year three hundred and sixty-four days only[; and for this reason they will go wrong as to the new moons and seasons and sabbaths and festivals, and they will eat all kinds of blood with all kinds of flesh].

## Notes

- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. They follow Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts: vv1, 3, 5, 10, 11, 12, 16, 19, 22, 23, 29, 30, 31, 32, 34, 35, 36, 37 and 38. In v23 the Ge'ez lists only three new moons, the first, fourth and tenth; the seventh, which vv26 and 29 need for *the four divisions of the year*, is supplied from Charles.
- **v2 "He appeared on the earth… and made atonement with its blood for all the guilt of the earth"** — *ʾastarʾaya*, **appeared**, in both Ge'ez texts. Charles has *he made atonement for the earth*, the verb that follows in the same verse. Before any burnt offering, Noah's first sacrifice in Jubilees is atonement for the land the flood has cleansed.
- **vv3–4 "The Lord smelled the soothing aroma"** — Genesis 8:20–22: *The LORD smelled the soothing aroma… From now on, all the days of the earth — seed and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night — shall not cease.* The offerings, salt and oil and wine and frankincense, are those of Leviticus.
- **vv5–9 "The fear of you and the dread of you… for in the image of the Lord He made Adam"** — God's words to Noah in Genesis 9:1–7, retold closely. Genesis ends v6 *in the image of God He made the human*; the Ge'ez has *Adam*, the same Hebrew word read as a name.
- **vv10–11 "Noah and his sons swore… He told you, too, to make a covenant… in this month on the mountain"** — the covenant at Sinai is dated to the same month as Noah's: *Moses took the blood and threw it on the people, and said, "Here — the blood of the covenant that the LORD has cut with you"* (Exodus 24:8). Jubilees makes Sinai a renewal of what Noah swore.
- **vv17–21 "the Festival of Weeks… the Festival of First Fruits"** — Exodus 34:22: *The Festival of Weeks you shall make for yourself, the firstfruits of the wheat harvest*. In Jubilees it is also the day the covenant is renewed each year, kept in heaven since creation.
- **v18 "twenty-six jubilees and five weeks of years… seven jubilees and one week of years"** — 26 × 49 + 5 × 7 = 1309, the year Noah left the ark (5:22–23 has him enter it in 1308). Seven jubilees and a week are 350 years: *Noah lived after the flood three hundred and fifty years* (Genesis 9:28).
- **vv23–29 the four new moons** — the first days of the first, fourth, seventh and tenth months divide the year into four quarters of thirteen weeks. Jubilees ties each to a day of the flood: the ark ordered and the earth dry (v25), the deep closed (v26), the deep opened (v26), the mountain tops seen (v27; Genesis 8:5).
- **vv30–32 "fifty-two weeks of days… three hundred and sixty-four days"** — 52 × 7 = 364: a year of whole weeks, in which every date falls on the same day of the week each year. It is the year of 1 Enoch's Astronomical Book: *every three hundred and sixty-four days* (1 Enoch 74:12).
- **v36 "those who watch the moon closely… ten days too soon"** — a year of twelve moons is about 354 days, ten days short of 364. The warning answers 2:9, where the signs of the calendar are given to the sun alone.

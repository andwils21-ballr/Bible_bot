# Jubilees 49

**1** Remember the commandment that the Lord commanded you about the Passover, that you should keep it in its season, on the fourteenth of the first month, that you should slaughter it before evening, and that they should eat it by night, on the evening of the fifteenth, from the time the sun sets.

**2** For on this night — the beginning of the festival and the beginning of joy — you were eating the Passover in Egypt, when all the powers of Mastema had been let loose to kill every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of Pharaoh to the firstborn of the captive maid at the mill, and to the cattle.

**3** This is the sign the Lord gave them: into every house on whose doorway they saw the blood of a year-old lamb, they should not go in to kill, but should pass over, so that all who were in the house might be saved, because the sign of the blood was on its doorway.

**4** The powers of the Lord did all that the Lord commanded them, and they passed over all the sons of Israel, and the plague did not come on them to destroy any soul from among them, from cattle to man, even to dog.

**5** The plague in Egypt was very great, and there was no house in Egypt without a dead body in it, and weeping and wailing.

**6** All Israel was eating the flesh of the Passover and drinking wine, and praising and blessing and giving thanks to the Lord, the God of their fathers, and was ready to go out from under the yoke of Egypt and from the evil bondage.

**7** You too, remember this day all the days of your life, and keep it from year to year, all the days of your life, once a year, on its day, according to all its law; and do not move it from day to day, or from month to month.

**8** For it is an eternal ordinance, and engraved on the tablets of heaven concerning the sons of Israel, that they should keep it every year on its day, once a year, through all their generations; and there is no limit of days, for it is ordained forever.

**9** The man who is clean and does not come to keep it at the time of its day, to bring an acceptable offering before the Lord and to eat and drink before the Lord on the day of its festival — that man who is clean and near at hand shall be cut off, for he did not bring the Lord's offering in its season; that man shall take the sin on himself.

**10** Let the sons of Israel come and keep the Passover on the day of its season, on the fourteenth of the first month, between the evenings, from the third part of the day to the third part of the night; for two parts of the day are given to the light, and a third to the evening.

**11** This is what the Lord commanded you, that you should keep it between the evenings.

**12** It is not to be slaughtered at any time of the light, but only at the time bordering on the evening; and let them eat it in the time of the evening, until the third part of the night; and whatever is left of all its flesh from the third part of the night on, let them burn it with fire.

**13** They shall not boil it in water, nor eat it raw, but roasted with fire; cooked with fire, with care, the head with its inner parts and with its feet; and no bone of it shall be broken, for among the sons of Israel no bone of it shall be crushed.

**14** For this reason the Lord commanded the sons of Israel to keep the Passover on the day of its season, and that no bone of it is to be broken, for it is a festival day and a day of commandment; it is not to be passed over from day to day or from month to month, but kept in its season.

**15** You too, command the sons of Israel to keep the Passover in their days, every year, once a year, on the day of its season; and it will come as a memorial, pleasing before the Lord, and no plague will come on them to kill or to strike in that year, when they have kept the Passover in its season, in everything as it is commanded.

**16** It will no longer be eaten outside the sanctuary of the Lord, but before the sanctuary of the Lord; and all the people of the congregation of Israel shall keep it in its season.

**17** Every man who has come on its day shall eat it in the sanctuary of your God, before the Lord, from twenty years old and upward; for so it is written and ordained, that they should eat it in the sanctuary of the Lord.

**18** When the sons of Israel come into the land they are to possess, into the land of Canaan, and set up the tabernacle of the Lord in the middle of the land, in one of their tribes, until the sanctuary of the Lord is built on the earth, they shall come and keep the Passover in the middle of the tabernacle of the Lord, and slaughter it before the Lord from year to year.

**19** In the days when the house has been built in the name of the Lord in the land of their inheritance, they will go there and slaughter the Passover in the evening, when the sun is setting, at the third part of the day.

**20** They will offer its blood on the base of the altar, and set its fat on the fire that is on the altar, and eat its flesh roasted with fire in the court of the house of the sanctuary, in the name of the Lord.

**21** They may not keep the Passover in their cities, or in any place, except before the tabernacle of the Lord, or else before the house where His name has dwelt; and they shall not go astray from the Lord.

**22** You too, Moses, command the sons of Israel to keep the ordinance of the Passover as it was commanded to you, and tell them its year, year by year, and its day among the days; and the Festival of Unleavened Bread, that they eat unleavened bread seven days, and keep its festival, and bring its offering day by day during those seven days of joy before the Lord, on the altar of your God.

**23** For you kept this festival in haste when you went out of Egypt, until you crossed the sea into the wilderness of Shur; for on the shore of the sea you completed it.

## Notes

- **vv2, 13, 14, 18** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter from v7 on, has vv13–18 too.
- **vv1–6 the first Passover** — Exodus 12. **v2 "all the powers of Mastema had been let loose"** — in Exodus it is *the destroyer* whom the LORD does not let *come into your houses to strike* (12:23); Jubilees names the destroying powers as Mastema's, though in v4 they are *the powers of the Lord* and do only what He commands. **"from the firstborn of Pharaoh to the firstborn of the captive"** — Exodus 12:29. **v4 "even to dog"** — Exodus 11:7: *against all the sons of Israel not a dog will sharpen its tongue.*
- **vv7–15 the law of the day** — **v9** — Numbers 9:13: *the man who is clean and is not on a journey, and fails to keep the Passover — that person shall be cut off from his people… That man shall carry his sin.* **vv10–12 "between the evenings… from the third part of the day"** — Exodus 12:6, *between the evenings*; Jubilees fixes it as the last third of the daylight. **v13** — Exodus 12:8–9: *roasted with fire… Do not eat any of it raw, or boiled at all in water, but roasted with fire — its head with its legs and with its inner parts.* **"no bone of it shall be broken"** — Exodus 12:46; John 19:36: *these things happened so that the Scripture would be fulfilled: "Not a bone of Him shall be broken."*
- **vv16–21 the place** — Deuteronomy 16:5–6: *You may not sacrifice the Passover in any of your gates… but at the place that the LORD your God chooses to make His name dwell, there you shall sacrifice the Passover, in the evening, as the sun goes down.* Jubilees adds the tabernacle, before the temple is built, and the age of twenty.
- **vv22–23 the Festival of Unleavened Bread** — seven days, kept first in haste on the way out of Egypt (Exodus 12:11, *You shall eat it in haste*) and finished on the shore of the sea, on the way into *the wilderness of Shur* (Exodus 15:22).

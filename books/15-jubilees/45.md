# Jubilees 45

**1** Israel came into the country of Egypt, into the land of Goshen, on the new moon of the fourth month, in the second year of the third week of the forty-fifth jubilee.

**2** Joseph came to meet his father Jacob in the land of Goshen, and he fell on his father's neck and wept.

**3** Israel said to Joseph, "Now let me die, since I have seen you. Now blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac, who has not withheld His mercy and His grace from His servant Jacob.

**4** It is great for me that I have seen your face while I am alive; for true indeed is the vision I saw at Bethel. Blessed be the Lord my God forever and ever, and blessed be His name."

**5** Joseph and his brothers ate bread before their father and drank wine, and Jacob rejoiced with very great joy[, because he saw Joseph eating with his brothers and drinking before him; and he blessed the Creator of all things, who had kept him and had kept his twelve sons for him].

**6** Joseph gave to his father and his brothers, as a gift, the right to live in the land of Goshen and in Rameses and all its region, which he ruled before Pharaoh[. Israel and his sons lived in the land of Goshen, the best of the land of Egypt; and Israel was one hundred and thirty years old when he came into Egypt].

**7** Joseph fed his father and his brothers, and their possessions too, with bread, as much as was enough for them for the seven years of famine.

**8** The land of Egypt suffered before the famine[; and Joseph acquired all the land of Egypt for Pharaoh in return for food, for he bought the people and the cattle and everything for Pharaoh].

**9** The years of famine were completed[; and Joseph gave the people of the land seed and food, so that they might sow the land in the eighth year, for the river had overflowed all the land of Egypt].

**10** For in the seven years of famine [it had not overflowed, and had watered only a few places along the bank of the river; but in the eighth year the Egyptians sowed their land and gathered good fruit that year].

**11** This was the first year of the fourth week of the forty-fifth jubilee.

**12** Joseph took from the grain they sowed a fifth for the king, and four parts he left to them for food and for seed; and Joseph made it an ordinance for the land of Egypt to this day.

**13** Israel lived in the land of Egypt seventeen years[; and all the years of his life that he lived were three jubilees, one hundred and forty-seven years; and he died in the forty-fifth jubilee, in the fourth year of the fifth week].

**14** Israel blessed his sons before he died, and told them all that would happen to them in the land of Egypt; and he made known to them what would come on them in the last days, and blessed them[; and Joseph he blessed twofold in the land].

**15** He slept with his fathers, and was buried in the double cave in the land of Canaan, near Abraham his father, in the grave he had dug for himself in the double cave in the land of Hebron.

**16** He gave all his books and the books of his fathers to Levi his son, to keep them and to renew them for his sons to this day.

## Notes

- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. In vv8, 9, 10, 13 and 14 they follow the Latin, which covers this chapter from v8 on; in vv5 and 6 they follow Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts. Without v13's bracket the Ge'ez never tells Jacob's age or the year of his death.
- **vv1–7 the meeting in Goshen** — Genesis 46:29–30: *Joseph… went up to meet Israel his father at Goshen… and fell on his neck, and wept on his neck a long while. Israel said to Joseph, "Let me die this time, after seeing your face."* **v4 "true indeed is the vision I saw at Bethel"** — the seven tablets (32:21–26). **v6 "Rameses"** — Genesis 47:11; **"one hundred and thirty years old"** — Genesis 47:9.
- **vv8–12 the famine** — Genesis 47:13–26: *Joseph bought all the ground of Egypt for Pharaoh… Joseph made it a statute to this day over the ground of Egypt — a fifth for Pharaoh* (47:20, 26). Jubilees adds that the Nile did not flood in the famine years.
- **The dates** — Jacob arrives in the second year of the third week of the forty-fifth jubilee, 2172 (v1). The famine ends with the first year of the fourth week, 2178 (v11; the Latin has *fifth week*); seven years from 2171 (42:1). Jacob dies in the fourth year of the fifth week, 2188 (v13). By this book's own dates he was born in 2046 (19:13), which makes him 126 on arrival and 142 at death, where v6 and v13 give 130 and 147, as Genesis 47:9 and 47:28 do. The text is left as it stands.
- **v14 "what would come on them in the last days"** — Genesis 49:1: *Gather, and I will tell you what will befall you in the end of days.* **"Joseph he blessed twofold"** — Genesis 48:22: *I have given you one shoulder above your brothers.*
- **v16 "all his books and the books of his fathers to Levi"** — the line of written lore that began with Enoch and passed through Noah, Shem and Abraham (10:14; 12:27; 21:10) now goes to the priestly tribe.

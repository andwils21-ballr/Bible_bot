# Jubilees 13

**1** Abram went from Harran, and he took Sarai his wife, and Lot, his brother Haran's son, to the land of Canaan; and he came into Asshur and went on to Shechem, and lived near a tall oak.

**2** He looked, and the land was very pleasant, from the entrance of Hamath to the tall oak.

**3** The Lord said to him, "To you and to your seed I will give this land."

**4** He built an altar there, and offered on it a burnt offering to the Lord who had appeared to him.

**5** He moved from there to the mountain of Bethel, toward the sea, with Ai toward the east, and pitched his tent there.

**6** He looked, and the land was wide and very good, and everything grew on it: vines and figs and pomegranates, oaks and holm oaks, and terebinths and olive trees, and cedars and cypresses and frankincense trees, and all the trees of the field, and there was water on the mountains.

**7** He blessed the Lord who had brought him out of Ur of the Chaldeans and brought him to this mountain.

**8** And it came to pass in the first year, in the seventh week, on the new moon of the first month, that he built an altar on this mountain and called on the name of the Lord: "You are my God, the eternal God."

**9** He offered on the altar a burnt offering to the Lord, that He would be with him and not forsake him all the days of his life.

**10** He moved from there and went toward the south and came to Hebron, and Hebron was built at that time, and he lived there two years; and he went into the land of the south as far as Bealoth, and a famine arose over the land.

**11** Abram went into Egypt in the third year of the week, and he lived in Egypt five years before his wife was taken from him.

**12** Tanis in Egypt was built at that time, seven years after Hebron.

**13** And it came to pass, when Pharaoh took Sarai, Abram's wife, that the Lord struck Pharaoh and his house with great plagues because of Sarai, Abram's wife.

**14** Abram was very rich in possessions: in sheep and cattle and donkeys and horses and camels and male and female servants, and in silver and gold, very much; and Lot, his brother's son, also had riches.

**15** Pharaoh gave back Sarai, Abram's wife, and sent him out of the land of Egypt; and he went to the place where he had pitched his tent at first, to the place of the altar, with Ai to the east and Bethel to the west; and he blessed the Lord his God, who had brought him back in peace.

**16** And it came to pass in this forty-first jubilee, in the third year of the first week, that he returned to this place and offered a burnt offering on it, and called on the name of the Lord: "God Most High, my God forever and ever."

**17** In the fourth year of this week Lot parted from him, and Lot lived in Sodom, and the men of Sodom were very great sinners [and wicked in their hearts].

**18** It grieved him in his heart that his brother's son had parted from him, for he had no children.

**19** In that year Lot was taken captive. In the fourth year of this week the Lord said to Abram, after Lot had parted from him, "Lift up your eyes from the place where you are living, to the west and to the south and to the east and to the north.

**20** For all the land that you see I will give to you and to your seed forever. I will make your seed like the sand of the sea, so that if a man could count the sand of the sea, then your seed also could be counted.

**21** Arise, walk through its length and its breadth and see it all, for to your seed I will give it." Abram went to Hebron and lived there.

**22** In this year Chedorlaomer king of Elam came, and Amraphel king of Shinar, and Arioch king of Ellasar, and Tidal king of Goiim, and they killed the king of Gomorrah, and the king of Sodom fled; and many fell wounded in the Valley of Siddim, by the Salt Sea.

**23** They took captive Sodom and Admah and Zeboiim, and they took captive Lot too, the son of Abram's brother, with all his possessions, and they went as far as Dan.

**24** One who had escaped came and told Abram that his brother's son had been taken captive.

**25** He armed the servants born in his house. Abram went and killed Chedorlaomer and came back, and he gave a tenth of everything, and gave it to Melchizedek; … for Abram and for his seed, a tenth of the first fruits to the Lord. The Lord ordained it as an ordinance forever, that they should give it to the priests who serve before Him, for them to possess forever.

**26** For this law there is no limit of days, for He ordained it for the eternal generations, that they should give to the Lord a tenth of everything: of the seed and of the wine and of the oil and of the cattle and of the sheep.

**27** He gave it to His priests to eat and drink with joy before Him.

**28** The king of Sodom came to him and bowed down before him and said, "Our lord Abram, give us the souls you have rescued, and let the spoil be yours."

**29** Abram said to him, "I lift up my hands to God Most High: from a thread to a sandal strap, I will not take anything that is yours, so that you will not say, 'I made Abram rich' — except only what the young men have eaten, and the portion of the men who went with me, Aner, Eshcol and Mamre: they shall take their portion."

## Notes

- **vv1, 10, 14, 19, 20, 21, 22, 25, 29** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63). In v19 the EOTC has only *In that year Lot was taken captive*; the Lord's promise to Abram, Genesis 13:14–17, is in the Asmara printing and in the Latin, which begins at v10 of this chapter. **Words in [brackets]** (v17) are in no Ge'ez text here; they follow the Latin.
- **vv1–9 Abram's arrival** — Genesis 12:6–8, retold: the tall oak near Shechem, the promise *To your seed I will give this land*, the altar, and the tent *with Bethel on the west and Ai on the east* (Genesis 12:8). **"frankincense trees"** — *libanos*, the Greek name of the frankincense tree; Charles has *date trees*.
- **v10 "as far as Bealoth"** — the EOTC writes it as two words, *bo'a Lot*, which could be read *Lot came*; the Latin has the place name, *Bealoth*.
- **v12 "Tanis… seven years after Hebron"** — the note Numbers makes when the spies reach Hebron: *Hebron had been built seven years before Zoan in Egypt* (Numbers 13:22). Zoan is Tanis.
- **vv13–15 Pharaoh and Sarai** — Genesis 12:14–20. Jubilees adds the years: Abram had been in Egypt five years before she was taken (v11).
- **vv22–29 the war of the kings** — Genesis 14, with the kings as named there: *Amraphel king of Shinar, Arioch king of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Tidal king of Goiim* (Genesis 14:1).
- **v25 "Abram went and killed Chedorlaomer… and gave it to Melchizedek"** — the two Beta Masaheft Ge'ez texts carry these words after an opening bracket, a mark that sets them apart from what comes before. The Asmara printing has no such words: like Charles's manuscripts it has a gap here, and it breaks off at *armed his household servants* and picks up mid-sentence with the tithe. Genesis: *He gave him a tenth of everything* (Genesis 14:20); Hebrews: *to whom Abraham apportioned a tenth of everything* (Hebrews 7:2). In Jubilees the tithe becomes a law forever, for the priests (vv25–27), as in Leviticus 27:30: *Every tenth of the land… is the LORD's*.
- **v29 "from a thread to a sandal strap"** — Genesis 14:23.

# Jubilees 25

**1** In the second year of this week, in this jubilee, Rebekah called Jacob her son and spoke to him, saying, "My son, do not take a wife for yourself from the daughters of Canaan, as Esau your brother did, who took two wives from the seed of Canaan; and they have made my soul bitter with all their unclean deeds, for all their deeds are fornication and lust, and there is no righteousness at all with them, for it is evil.

**2** I, my son, love you very much, and my heart and my affection bless you every hour of the day and every watch of the night.

**3** Now, my son, listen to my voice and do the will of your mother, and do not take a wife for yourself from the daughters of this land, but only from my father's house and from my father's kindred. Take a wife for yourself from my father's house, and God Most High will bless you, and your children will be a righteous generation and your seed holy."

**4** Then Jacob spoke with Rebekah his mother and said to her, "Here — I, mother, am nine weeks of years old, and I have not known or touched any woman, nor have I betrothed myself, nor do I even think of taking a wife for myself from any seed of the daughters of Canaan.

**5** For I remember, mother, the words of Abraham our father, what he commanded me: not to take a wife from any seed of the house of Canaan, for I will take a wife for myself from the seed of my father's house and from my kindred.

**6** I heard long ago, mother, that daughters had been born to Laban your brother, and I have set my heart on them, to take a wife from among them.

**7** For this reason I have kept myself in my spirit from sinning or being corrupted in any of my ways, all the days of my life; for about lust and fornication Abraham my father gave me many commands.

**8** With all that he commanded me, these twenty-two years my brother has quarreled with me, and he speaks to me often and says, 'My brother, take as a wife a sister of my two wives'; but I do not want to do as he has done.

**9** I swear before you, mother, that all the days of my life I will not take a wife for myself from any seed of the daughters of Canaan, and I will not do evil as my brother has done.

**10** Do not fear, mother; be sure that I will do your will and walk in uprightness, and I will not corrupt my ways forever."

**11** Then she lifted her face to heaven and spread out the fingers of her hands, and opened her mouth and blessed God Most High, who created heaven and earth, and she gave Him thanks and praise.

**12** She said, "Blessed be the Lord God, and blessed be His name forever and ever[, who has given me Jacob as a pure son and a holy seed; for he is Yours, and Yours his seed will be always, through all the generations forever].

**13** Bless him, O Lord, and put in my mouth the blessing of righteousness, that I may bless him."

**14** At that hour the spirit of righteousness came down into her mouth, and she laid both her hands on Jacob's head and said,

**15** "Blessed are You, O righteous Lord, God of the ages; and may He bless you above all the generations of men. May He give you, my son, the way of righteousness, and reveal righteousness to your seed.

**16** May He make your sons many in your lifetime, and may they rise up to the number of the months of the year; and may their sons become many and great, beyond the stars of heaven, and may their number be greater than the sand of the sea.

**17** May He give them this good land, as He said He would give it to Abraham and to his seed after him for all days, and may they hold it as a possession forever.

**18** May I see born to you, my son, blessed children in my lifetime, and may all your seed be a blessed and holy seed.

**19** As you have refreshed your mother's spirit in her lifetime, so the womb of her who bore you blesses you; my affection and my breasts bless you, and my mouth and my tongue praise you greatly.

**20** Grow and spread out on the earth, and may your seed be perfect in all the world, in the joy of heaven and earth[; and may your seed rejoice, and on the great day of peace may it have peace].

**21** May your name and your seed endure to all the ages[; and may God Most High be their God, and may the God of righteousness dwell with them, and by them may His sanctuary be built for all the ages].

**22** Blessed is he who blesses you, and all flesh that curses you falsely, may it be cursed."

**23** She kissed him and said to him, "May the Lord of the world love you as your mother's heart does, and as her affection rejoices in you and blesses you." Then she ceased from blessing.

## Notes

- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. They follow Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts: vv12, 20 and 21. The Latin has only the opening words of v1.
- **v1 the date** — the second year of the first week of the forty-fourth jubilee (24:21) is 2109, the year of Abraham's death by 22:1. Jacob, born in 2046 (19:13), is sixty-three: *nine weeks of years*, as he says in v4.
- **vv1–3 "do not take a wife… from the daughters of Canaan"** — in Genesis Rebekah says it to Isaac, not to Jacob: *I am disgusted with my life because of the daughters of Heth* (Genesis 27:46); and Isaac sends Jacob away with the charge, *You shall not take a wife from the daughters of Canaan. Rise, go to Paddan-aram… from the daughters of Laban your mother's brother* (Genesis 28:1–2). Esau's two wives: *Judith daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Basemath daughter of Elon the Hittite. They were a bitterness of spirit to Isaac and to Rebekah* (Genesis 26:34–35). **v8 "these twenty-two years"** — Esau married at forty (Genesis 26:34).
- **vv4–10 Jacob's answer** — Jacob keeps Abraham's charge (20:4; 22:20) and swears to his mother. **v6 "the daughters of Laban"** — Leah and Rachel, whom he will marry in chapter 28.
- **vv11–23 Rebekah's blessing** — Genesis gives Rebekah no blessing of her own; here the *spirit of righteousness* comes into her mouth (v14), as a spirit comes on a prophet, and she lays both hands on Jacob's head. **v16 "to the number of the months of the year"** — twelve, the twelve sons of Jacob. **v22 "Blessed is he who blesses you, and all flesh that curses you… may it be cursed"** — Isaac's words over Jacob in Genesis 27:29: *Those who curse you, cursed; and those who bless you, blessed.*

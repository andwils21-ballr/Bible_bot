# Jubilees 32

**1** He stayed that night at Bethel, and Levi dreamed that they had appointed and made him priest of God Most High, him and his sons forever; and he woke from his sleep and blessed the Lord.

**2** Jacob rose early in the morning, on the fourteenth of this month, and tithed everything that had come with him, from men to every living creature, and from gold to every vessel and garment; he tithed everything.

**3** In those days Rachel was pregnant with Benjamin her son, and Jacob counted his sons from him; and the count went up, and Levi fell to the portion of the Lord; and his father clothed him in the garments of the priesthood and consecrated him.

**4** On the fifteenth of this month he brought to the altar fourteen bulls from the cattle, and twenty-eight rams, and forty-nine sheep, and sixty lambs, and twenty-nine kids of the goats, as a burnt offering on the altar, an acceptable offering for a soothing aroma before God.

**5** This was his offering from the vow he had vowed, to give a tenth, with their grain offerings and their drink offerings.

**6** When the fire had consumed it, he burned incense on it, over the fire; and as a peace offering, four calves and four rams and four male goats, and two yearling sheep and two kids of the goats; and he did this every morning for the seven days.

**7** He and all his sons and his men were eating there with joy for seven days, and blessing and thanking the Lord, who had delivered him from all his distress and had granted him his vow.

**8** He tithed all the clean animals and made a burnt offering of them; and the unclean animals he gave to Levi his son, and all the souls of men he gave him.

**9** Levi served as priest at Bethel before Jacob his father, above his ten brothers, and he was priest there; and Jacob paid his vow: so he tithed the tithe again to the Lord and made it holy, and it became holy to Him.

**10** For this reason it is ordained on the tablets of heaven as a law, to tithe the tithe again, to eat it before the Lord in the place chosen for His name to dwell on it, from year to year; and to this law there is no limit of days forever.

**11** This ordinance is written, to be done from year to year: to eat the second tithe before the Lord in the place that has been chosen; and nothing of it shall be left over from this year to the next.

**12** For in its year the seed shall be eaten, until the days of the harvest of the seed of the year; and the wine until the days of the wine; and the oil until the days of its season.

**13** All that is left of it and grows old, let it be counted defiled; let it be burned with fire, for it has become unclean.

**14** So let them eat it together in the sanctuary, and not let it grow old.

**15** All the tithes of cattle and sheep are holy to the Lord, and let them be for His priests to eat before Him from year to year; for so it is ordained and engraved concerning the tithe on the tablets of heaven.

**16** On the second night, on the twenty-second day of this month, Jacob resolved to build that place and to surround the court with a wall, and to make it holy, and to make it holy forever, for himself and for his sons after him forever.

**17** The Lord appeared to him by night and blessed him and said to him, "Your name shall not be called Jacob only, but they will call your name Israel."

**18** He said to him again, "I am the Lord who created heaven and earth, and I will make you grow and multiply you very greatly; and kings will come from you, and they will rule everywhere the foot of the sons of men has trodden.

**19** I will give to your seed all the earth that is under heaven, and they will rule over all the nations as they wish; and after this they will gather in all the earth and inherit it forever."

**20** He finished speaking with him and went up from him, and Jacob watched until He had gone up into heaven.

**21** He saw in a vision of the night an angel coming down from heaven with seven tablets in his hands; and he gave them to Jacob, and he read them, and read all that was written in them, what would happen to him and to his sons in all the ages.

**22** He showed him all that was written on the tablets, and said to him, "Do not build this place, and do not make it an eternal sanctuary, and do not live here, for this is not the place. Go to the house of Abraham your father, and live with Isaac your father until the day of your father's death.

**23** For in Egypt you will die in peace, and in this land you will be buried with honor, in the tomb of your fathers, with Abraham and Isaac.

**24** Do not fear, for as you have seen and read, so it will all be; and you, write down all that you have seen and read."

**25** Jacob said, "Lord, how shall I remember all that I have read and seen?" He said to him, "I will bring all things to your remembrance."

**26** He went up from him; and he woke from his sleep, and remembered everything he had read and seen, and wrote down all the words he had read and seen.

**27** He kept one more day there, and sacrificed on it according to all that he had sacrificed on the former days, and called its name Addition, for that day was added; and the former days he called the festival.

**28** So it was shown that it should be, and it is written on the tablets of heaven; for this reason it was revealed to him to keep it, and to add it to the seven days of the festival.

**29** Its name was called Addition, because it was recorded in the testimony of the festival days, according to the number of the days of the year.

**30** In the night, on the twenty-third of this month, Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, died, and they buried her below the city, under the oak of the river; and he called the name of that place the River of Deborah, and the oak, the Oak of the Mourning of Deborah.

**31** Rebekah went and returned to her house, to his father Isaac; and Jacob sent by her hand rams and sheep and male goats, so that she might prepare a meal for his father such as he desired.

**32** He went after his mother until he came near the land of Kabratan, and he lived there.

**33** Rachel bore a son in the night, and called his name Son of My Sorrow, for she had hard labor bearing him; but his father called his name Benjamin.

**34** On the tenth of the eighth month, in the first year of the sixth week of this jubilee, Rachel died there and was buried in the land of Ephrath, which is Bethlehem; and Jacob built a pillar on Rachel's grave, on the way, over her grave.

## Notes

- **vv1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 10, 11, 15, 22, 25, 26, 28, 31, 34** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter from v1 to v8 and from v18 on, has many of them too. **v26** — the Latin says that Jacob *concealed* the words he had read; the Asmara printing, like Charles, has *wrote down*, which is what the angel commands in v24. **v31** — the Latin has Jacob, not Rebekah, return to Isaac; the Asmara printing has Rebekah.
- **vv1–9 Levi made priest** — on the fifteenth of the seventh month, the Festival of Booths (16:29), Jacob tithes everything he has, counts his sons upward from Benjamin still in the womb, and the tenth, Levi, falls to the Lord. **v6 the peace offering** — so the Asmara printing: four calves, four rams, four male goats, two yearling sheep, two kids; the Latin and Charles have *two calves, four rams, two yearling lambs and two male goats*. **v4 the numbers** — so the EOTC: fourteen bulls, twenty-eight rams, forty-nine sheep, sixty lambs, twenty-nine kids. Charles and the Latin have *seven* and *twenty-one* for the last two, and so every number a multiple of seven. **v8 "the unclean animals he gave to Levi"** — Charles's text reads *he gave (not)*; compare Numbers 18:15, where the firstborn of unclean animals is redeemed, not given to the priest.
- **vv10–15 the second tithe** — Deuteronomy 14:23: *You shall eat before the LORD your God, in the place that He chooses to make His name dwell there, the tithe of your grain, your new wine and your oil*; Leviticus 27:32: *Every tenth of the herd and the flock… shall be holy to the LORD.* Jubilees roots both laws in Jacob's vow at Bethel (27:27).
- **vv16–26 the name Israel, and the seven tablets** — Genesis 35:9–15. **v17 "Your name shall not be called Jacob only"** — Genesis 35:10: *Your name shall no longer be called Jacob — Israel shall be your name.* The EOTC's *only* keeps both names, as the Bible goes on using both. **vv21–26 "seven tablets"** — Jacob is shown the future of his sons; the Latin calls them *boxwood tablets*. **v22 "Do not build this place"** — the sanctuary will be built elsewhere, in the place God chooses (v10).
- **vv27–29 "Addition"** — the eighth day after the seven of Booths, as in Leviticus 23:36: *On the eighth day you shall have a holy assembly… It is a closing assembly.* The Latin calls it *Retention*.
- **v30 Deborah** — Genesis 35:8: *Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, died, and she was buried below Bethel, under the oak. He called its name Allon-bacuth*, "oak of weeping."
- **vv32–34 Benjamin and Rachel's death** — Genesis 35:16–20: *There was still a stretch of ground to come to Ephrath, and Rachel gave birth, and her labor was hard… she called his name Ben-oni. His father called him Benjamin… Jacob set up a pillar over her grave.* **"Kabratan"** — apparently the Hebrew *kivrat*, *a stretch of ground*, read as a place name. **v34 the date** — the first year of the sixth week is 2143, the year of 30:1 and 31:1. The Asmara printing sets the date with Rachel's death, the Latin with Benjamin's birth, which was the same night. The Asmara printing and the Latin have *the tenth* of the eighth month, Charles *the eleventh*.

# Jubilees 46

**1** And it came to pass, after Jacob died, that the sons of Israel multiplied in the land of Egypt and became a great people; and they were all of one heart, so that brother loved brother and each man helped his brother; and they increased and multiplied very greatly, ten weeks of years, all the days of Joseph's life.

**2** There was no Satan and nothing evil at all, all the days of the life of Joseph that he lived after his father Jacob; for all the Egyptians honored the sons of Israel all the days of Joseph's life.

**3** Joseph died at one hundred and ten years old: seventeen years he lived in the land of Canaan, and ten years he was a servant, and three years in prison, and eighty years he was under the king, ruling all the land of Egypt.

**4** He died, and all his brothers, and all that generation.

**5** Before he died he commanded the sons of Israel to carry his bones with them in the days when they went out of the land of Egypt.

**6** He made them swear about his bones, for he knew that the Egyptians would not again bring him out and bury him in the land of Canaan; for Makamaron, king of Canaan, while he was living in the land of Assyria, fought in the valley with the king of Egypt and killed him there, and pursued the Egyptians to the gates of Ermon.

**7** He could not enter, for another, a new king, reigned over Egypt, and he was stronger than he; and he went back to the land of Canaan, and the gates of Egypt were shut, and no one went out and no one came into Egypt.

**8** Joseph died in the forty-sixth jubilee, in the sixth week, in its second year, and they buried him in the land of Egypt; and all his brothers died after him.

**9** The king of Egypt went out to fight with the king of Canaan in the forty-seventh jubilee, in the second week, in its second year; and the sons of Israel brought out all the bones of the sons of Jacob except the bones of Joseph, and buried them in the field, in the double cave in the mountain.

**10** Most of them went back to Egypt, and a few of them stayed on the mountain of Hebron, and Amram your father stayed with them.

**11** The king of Canaan defeated the king of Egypt, and shut the gates of Egypt.

**12** He devised an evil plan against the sons of Israel, to afflict them, and he said to the people of Egypt,

**13** "The people of the sons of Israel have grown and multiplied more than we. Come, let us deal shrewdly with them before they multiply, and afflict them with slavery before war comes on us, and before they too fight against us; otherwise they will join the enemy and go out of our land, for their hearts and their faces are toward the land of Canaan."

**14** He set taskmasters over them, to afflict them with slavery; and they commanded them, and they built fortified cities for Pharaoh, Pithom and Rameses, and they built every wall and every rampart that had fallen in the cities of Egypt.

**15** They made them serve with oppression, and the more they did evil to them, the more they multiplied and the more they increased.

**16** The people of Egypt held the sons of Israel to be unclean.

## Notes

- **vv1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 14** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter in part, has v14 too. Without the end of v6 the EOTC never says who *could not enter* Egypt in v7: it is Makamaron, king of Canaan.
- **vv1–2 "no Satan and nothing evil"** — as in Egypt under Joseph (40:9), and as in the last days (23:29).
- **v3 Joseph's years** — *seventeen… ten… three… eighty*, 110 in all, as Genesis 50:26: *Joseph died, a son of a hundred and ten years*. The Greek has *three years as a slave, and three years in the prison*.
- **vv5–6 Joseph's bones** — Genesis 50:25: *God will surely visit you, and you shall carry my bones up from here*; Exodus 13:19: *Moses took the bones of Joseph with him.* Jubilees gives the reason they were not carried up at once: the war with Canaan and the shut gates of Egypt.
- **The dates** — Joseph dies in the second year of the sixth week of the forty-sixth jubilee, 45 × 49 + 5 × 7 + 2 = 2242. By this book's date for his birth, 2134 (28:24), he is 108, where v3 has 110. The war of v9 is in the second year of the second week of the forty-seventh jubilee, 2263. The Asmara printing dates Joseph's death to the second year of the *third* week of the *forty-seventh* jubilee, 2270, which makes him 136 and puts his death after the war. The text is left as it stands.
- **v9 the bones of Jacob's sons** — buried in the double cave at Hebron, except Joseph's, which wait for the exodus.
- **v10 "Amram your father"** — the angel is speaking to Moses; Amram is the father of Moses (Exodus 6:20). The EOTC writes *ʾAbrām*; the second Ge'ez text has the same.
- **vv11–16 the bondage** — Exodus 1:8–14: *A new king rose over Egypt, who did not know Joseph… Come, let us deal shrewdly with them — otherwise they will multiply, and it will come to pass that if war breaks out they will be added to those who hate us… They built store cities for Pharaoh: Pithom and Rameses. But the more they afflicted them, the more they multiplied.* **v12 "He devised an evil plan"** — the Ge'ez does not name the king; the Latin says it was *the king of Canaan*. **v14 "Pithom and Rameses"** — so the Asmara printing, as Exodus 1:11; the Latin adds *On*. The Greek adds that the Hebrews were worn out digging canals, building embankments against the Nile, and raising pyramids.

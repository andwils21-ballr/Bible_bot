# Jubilees 22

**1** And it came to pass in the first week of the forty-fourth jubilee, in the second year, the year in which Abraham died, that Isaac and Ishmael came from the Well of the Oath to keep the Festival of Weeks, the festival of the first fruits of the harvest, with Abraham their father; and Abraham rejoiced because both his sons had come.

**2** For Isaac had many possessions in Beersheba, and Isaac would go and look over his possessions and return to his father.

**3** In those days Ishmael came to see his father, and they all came together; and Isaac slaughtered a sacrifice for a burnt offering and offered it on the altar of his father that he had made in Hebron.

**4** He offered a peace offering and made a feast of joy before Ishmael his brother; and Rebekah made new cakes from the new grain and gave them to Jacob her son, to bring to Abraham his father from the first fruits of the land, so that he might eat and bless the Creator of all things before he died.

**5** Isaac too sent by the hand of Jacob the best peace offering to Abraham, that he might eat and drink.

**6** He ate and drank, and blessed God Most High, who created heaven and earth, who made all the fat of the earth and gave it to the sons of men to eat and drink and bless their Creator.

**7** "Now I give thanks to You, my God, because You have let me see this day. I am one hundred and seventy-five years old, old and full of days, and all my days have been peace to me.

**8** The sword of the enemy has not overcome me in anything You gave me, or in my sons, all the days of my life until this day.

**9** May Your mercy and Your peace be on Your servant and on the seed of his sons, that they may be to You a chosen people and an inheritance from all the nations of the earth, from now on to all the days of the generations of the earth, for all the ages."

**10** He called Jacob and said to him, "My son Jacob, may the God of all bless you and strengthen you to do righteousness and His will before Him, and may He choose you and your seed, that you may be to Him a people for His inheritance according to His will, all the days. You, my son Jacob, come near to me and kiss me."

**11** He came near and kissed him. He said, "Blessed are you, my son Jacob, and all the sons of God Most High, in all the ages. May God give you a seed of righteousness from your sons, and may He make it holy in the middle of the whole earth; nations will serve you, and all the nations will bow down before your seed.

**12** Be strong before men, and rule over all the seed of Seth; then your ways and the ways of your sons will be justified, so that they become a holy people.

**13** May God Most High give you all the blessings with which He blessed me and with which He blessed Noah and Adam; may they rest on the holy head of your seed, for every generation of generations and forever.

**14** May He cleanse you from all the filth of uncleanness, so that you may be forgiven every transgression you have committed in ignorance; and may He strengthen you and bless you, and may you inherit all the earth.

**15** May He renew His covenant with you, that you may be to Him a people for His inheritance for all the ages, and that He may be God to you and to your seed in truth and righteousness all the days of the earth.

**16** You too, my son Jacob, remember my words and keep the commandments of Abraham your father: separate yourself from the nations, and do not eat with them, and do not act according to their deeds, and do not become their companion; for their works are defilement, and all their way is uncleanness and abomination and filth.

**17** They sacrifice their sacrifices to the dead and bow down to demons, and they eat over graves, and all their deeds are empty and nothing.

**18** They have no heart to understand, and their eyes do not see what their deeds are, and how they go astray, saying to wood, 'You are my god,' and to stone, 'You are my lord and my deliverer'; and they have no heart.

**19** As for you, my son Jacob, may God Most High help you, and the God of heaven bless you, and may He turn you away from their uncleanness and from all their error.

**20** Take care, my son Jacob, not to take a wife from any seed of the daughters of Canaan[, for all his seed is to be rooted out of the earth].

**21** For through the transgression of Ham, Canaan went astray, and all his seed will be wiped out from the earth, and all its remnant, and none of him will be saved on the day of judgment.

**22** For all who serve idols, and for the profane, there is no hope at all in the land of the living; for they will go down into Sheol, and into the place of condemnation they will go, and there will be no remembrance of them on the earth. As the sons of Sodom were taken away from the earth, so all who serve idols will be taken away.

**23** Do not fear, my son Jacob, and do not be dismayed, son of Abraham. May God Most High keep you from destruction, and from every path of error may He deliver you.

**24** This house I have built for myself, to put my name on it on the earth: it is given to you and to your seed forever, and it will be called the house of Abraham; it is given to you and to your seed forever, for you will build my house and establish my name before God forever. Your seed and your name will stand through all the generations of the earth."

**25** He finished commanding him and blessing him.

**26** The two of them lay together on one bed, and Jacob slept in the bosom of Abraham, his father's father; and he kissed him seven times, and his affection and his heart rejoiced over him.

**27** He blessed him with all his heart and said, "God Most High, the God of all and Creator of all, who brought me out of Ur of the Chaldeans to give me this land to inherit forever, and to raise up a holy seed, that it may be blessed forever—"

**28** He blessed Jacob: "My son, in whom I rejoice with all my heart and with all my affection — may Your grace and Your mercy be lifted high over him and over his seed all the days.

**29** Do not forsake him or despise him, from now on to the days of eternity; and may Your eyes be open on him and on his seed, to keep him and bless him and make him holy as a people for Your inheritance.

**30** Bless him with all Your blessings, from now on to all the days of eternity, and renew Your covenant and Your grace with him and with his seed according to all Your good pleasure, to all the generations of the earth."

## Notes

- **vv4, 8, 10, 11, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 22, 29, 30** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter from v2 to v19, has most of them too. **v11** — the Latin blessing is broken in places and has *a seed of truth* and *before your name*; the Asmara printing, like Charles, has *a seed of righteousness* and *before your seed*. **Words in [brackets]** (v20) are in no Ge'ez text here; they follow Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts.
- **v1 the date** — the second year of the first week of the forty-fourth jubilee is 43 × 49 + 2 = 2109. Abraham, born in 1876 (11:15), dies at 175 (v7; 23:8), which would be 2051. Charles's English has *forty-fourth* too. The text is left as it stands (compare 21:1).
- **vv1–5 the last Festival of Weeks** — Abraham's sons come to him on the festival of first fruits, the day of the covenants with Noah and with Abram (6:17; 14:10; 15:1). In Genesis Isaac and Ishmael meet once more, at his burial (Genesis 25:9).
- **vv7–9 "old and full of days"** — Genesis 25:8: *Abraham expired and died at a good gray age, old and satisfied*.
- **vv10–24 the blessing of Jacob** — the blessing that in Genesis passes from Isaac to Jacob by Rebekah's plan (Genesis 27) is here given openly by Abraham, as 19:16–29 foretold.
- **v16 "separate yourself from the nations, and do not eat with them"** — the rule Peter names in Acts: *You know how unlawful it is for a Jewish man to join himself to a foreigner or to come to him* (Acts 10:28).
- **v17 "They sacrifice… to the dead and bow down to demons"** — Psalm 106:28: *they ate the sacrifices of the dead*; Deuteronomy 32:17: *They sacrificed to demons, not God*. **v18 "saying to wood, 'You are my god'"** — Jeremiah 2:27: *saying to wood, "You are my father," and to stone, "You gave birth to me."*
- **v19 "the God of heaven"** — the Latin has *the God of Adam*.
- **vv20–22 the daughters of Canaan** — as Abraham charged all his sons (20:4). Canaan's curse goes back to Ham's sin (Genesis 9:22–25) and to Canaan's seizing of Shem's land (10:29–34).
- **v24 "This house I have built for myself"** — Abraham's house in Hebron, given to Jacob; it will be named *the house of Abraham*.
- **v26 "Jacob slept in the bosom of Abraham"** — the last night of Abraham's life. In Luke the poor man is carried *by the angels to Abraham's bosom* (Luke 16:22).
- **v27 "that it may be blessed forever—"** — the EOTC's sentence ends here without a main verb; Charles has *blessed be the Most High for ever*. **v28 "He blessed Jacob: 'My son…'"** — the Ge'ez turns from speaking of Jacob to God in mid-sentence, as the English does.

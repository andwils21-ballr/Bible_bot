# Jubilees 26

**1** In the seventh year of this week Isaac called Esau, his elder son, and said to him, "My son, I am old, and here — my eyes are too dim to see, and I do not know the day of my death.

**2** Now take your hunting gear, your quiver and your bow, and go out to the field and hunt, and catch game for me, my son; and make me food such as my soul loves, and bring it to me, so that I may eat, and my soul may bless you before I die."

**3** Rebekah was listening as Isaac spoke to Esau.

**4** Esau went out early to the field to hunt and catch game and bring it to his father.

**5** Rebekah called Jacob her son and said to him, "Here — I heard Isaac your father speaking with Esau your brother, saying, 'Hunt for me and make me food, and bring it to me, and let me eat, and I will bless you before the Lord before I die.'

**6** Now, my son, listen to my voice in what I command you: go to your flock and take me two good kids of the goats, and I will make them into food for your father, such as he loves; and you will bring it to your father and he will eat, so that he may bless you before the Lord before he dies."

**7** Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "Mother, I will not hold back anything my father would eat and that would please him; but I am afraid, mother, that he will know my voice and want to touch me.

**8** You know that I am smooth, and Esau my brother is hairy, and I will be in his eyes like one who does evil, and I will do a deed he did not command me, and he will be angry with me, and I will bring on myself a curse and not a blessing."

**9** Rebekah his mother said to him, "Upon me be your curse, my son. Only listen to my voice."

**10** Jacob listened to the voice of Rebekah his mother, and he went and took two tender and good kids of the goats and brought them to his mother; and his mother made them into food, such as he loved.

**11** Rebekah took the fine garments of Esau, her elder son, which were with her in her house, and clothed Jacob, her younger son; and she put the skins of the kids on his hands, and on the bare parts of his body, and on his neck.

**12** She gave the stew and the bread that she had made into the hand of Jacob her son.

**13** He went in to his father and said, "I am your son. I have done as you told me. Rise, sit and eat of what I have caught, father, so that your soul may bless me."

**14** Isaac said to his son, "How is it that you found it so quickly, my son?"

**15** Jacob said, "Because your God made it happen before me."

**16** Isaac said to him, "Come near, and let me feel you, my son, whether you are my son Esau or not."

**17** Jacob came near to Isaac his father, and he felt him and said,

**18** "The voice is the voice of Jacob, and the hands are the hands of Esau." He did not recognize him, for it was a turning from heaven to lead his spirit aside; and Isaac did not recognize him, because his hands were hairy like the hands of Esau, so that he might bless him.

**19** He said, "Are you my son Esau?" He said, "I am your son." He said, "Bring it near to me, and let me eat of what you have caught, my son, so that my soul may bless you."

**20** He brought it near to him, and he ate; and he brought him wine, and he drank.

**21** Isaac his father said to him, "Come near and kiss me, my son." He came near and kissed him.

**22** He smelled the smell of his garments, and blessed him and said, "Here — the smell of my son is like the smell of a field that the Lord has blessed.

**23** May the Lord give to you, and multiply you, from the dew of heaven and from the dew of the earth, and may He multiply for you plenty of grain and oil; let nations serve you, and peoples bow down to you.

**24** Be lord over your brothers, and let your mother's sons bow down to you; and may all the blessings with which the Lord has blessed me, and blessed Abraham my father, be yours and your seed's forever. Cursed be he who curses you, and blessed be he who blesses you."

**25** And it came to pass, after Isaac had finished blessing his son Jacob, and when Jacob had gone out from Isaac his father, that he hid himself; and Esau his brother came in from his hunting.

**26** He too made food and brought it to his father, and said to his father, "Let my father rise and eat of my game, so that your soul may bless me."

**27** Isaac his father said to him, "Who are you?" He said to him, "I am your firstborn, Esau your son. I have done as you commanded me."

**28** Isaac was very greatly astonished, and said, "Who is it, then, who hunted and caught game for me and brought it, and I ate of it all before you came, and blessed him? He shall be blessed, and all his seed forever."

**29** And it came to pass, when Esau heard the words of his father Isaac, that he cried out with a great and very bitter cry, and said to his father, "Bless me — me also, father!"

**30** He said to him, "Your brother came with guile and took your blessings." He said, "Now I know why his name is called Jacob. Here — he has supplanted me these two times: he took away my birthright, and now he has taken away my blessing."

**31** He said, "Have you not kept back a blessing for me, father?" Isaac answered and said to Esau, "Here — I have made him your lord, and all his brothers I have given to him as servants, and with plenty of grain and wine and oil I have strengthened him. What now shall I do for you, my son?"

**32** Esau said to Isaac his father, "Is your blessing only one, father? Bless me — me also, father!" Esau lifted up his voice and wept.

**33** Isaac answered and said to him, "Here — away from the dew of the earth will be your dwelling, and away from the dew of heaven above.

**34** By your sword you will live, and you will serve your brother, and he will be your lord; and when you refuse and loosen his yoke from your neck, then you will sin a complete sin to death, and all your seed will be rooted out from under heaven."

**35** Esau kept threatening Jacob because of the blessing with which his father had blessed him, and he said in his heart, "Now let the days of mourning for my father come, and I will kill my brother Jacob."

## Notes

- **vv10, 11, 21, 23, 25, 26, 30, 31, 33, 34** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter from v8 to v23, has vv10, 11 and 21 too. **v33** — the whole verse is missing from both Beta Masaheft texts; without it they never give Isaac's answer to Esau's tears. **"away from the dew of the earth"** — *ʾəm-ṭala*, **from the dew**: the Ge'ez preposition, like the Hebrew *min* of Genesis 27:39, can mean *out of* or *away from*; see the note on Genesis 27:39. **v34 "when you refuse and loosen his yoke"** — the Greek has *whenever you cast down and loosen his yoke from your neck*.
- **vv1–35 the stolen blessing** — Genesis 27:1–41, retold in order, in this project's wording where the Ge'ez allows: *The voice is the voice of Jacob, and the hands are the hands of Esau* (27:22); *Upon me be your curse, my son* (27:13); *Let the days of mourning for my father draw near, and I will kill Jacob my brother* (27:41).
- **vv13, 19 "I am your son"** — in Genesis 27:19 Jacob says *I am Esau your firstborn*, and in 27:24, to *Are you really my son Esau?*, he answers *I am*. In Jubilees he says only *I am your son* both times: he does not speak the lie in so many words.
- **v18 "a turning from heaven to lead his spirit aside"** — Genesis 27:23 says only that *he did not recognize him, because his hands were hairy*. Jubilees adds that heaven itself turned Isaac's mind aside, so that the blessing went where it was meant to go (19:16–29; 22:10–24). The Latin has the same, *a turning away from heaven to distract his spirit*.
- **v1 the date** — the seventh year of this week, the first week of the forty-fourth jubilee (24:21; 25:1), is 2114. Jacob, born in 2046, is sixty-eight.
- **v23 "plenty of grain and oil"** — Genesis 27:28 has *abundance of grain and new wine*.
- **v34 "you will sin a complete sin to death"** — Genesis 27:40 ends *you will tear his yoke off your neck*. Jubilees, in the Asmara printing and in Charles, adds that Esau's breaking free will be his final sin (compare 21:22).

Asmara printing (1962/63): Ge'ez Frontier Foundation, CC BY-SA 4.0. Latin and Greek: Online Critical Pseudepigrapha, CC BY 4.0. English translation by this project.

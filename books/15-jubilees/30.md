# Jubilees 30

**1** In the first year of the sixth week he went up to Salem, east of Shechem, in peace, in the fourth month.

**2** There they carried off Dinah, the daughter of Jacob, into the house of Shechem, the son of Hamor the Hivite, the prince of the land, and he lay with her and defiled her; and she was a young girl, a daughter of twelve years.

**3** He begged her father that she be given to him as a wife, and her brothers, as a wife for him; and Jacob and his sons were indignant against the men of Shechem, because they had defiled Dinah their sister, and they spoke to them deceitfully and mocked them with guile.

**4** Simeon and Levi came to Shechem unexpectedly and executed judgment on all the men of Shechem, and killed every man they found in it, and did not leave a single one in it; they killed them all in torment, because they had dishonored Dinah their sister.

**5** Let it not be done so again from now on, that a daughter of Israel is defiled; for judgment was ordained against them in heaven, that they should destroy all the men of Shechem with the sword, because they had done a shameful thing in Israel.

**6** The Lord gave them into the hands of the sons of Jacob, to root them out with the sword and to execute judgment on them, and that it might no longer be in Israel that a virgin of Israel is defiled.

**7** If there is a man in Israel who wants to give his daughter or his sister to any man of the seed of the nations, he shall surely die, and they shall stone him, for he has done a sin and a shameful thing in Israel; and the woman shall be burned with fire, because she has defiled the name of her father's house; she shall be rooted out of Israel.

**8** Let no prostitute and no uncleanness be found in Israel, all the days of the generations of the earth, for Israel is holy to the Lord; and any man who defiles it shall die by stoning.

**9** For so it is ordained and written on the tablets of heaven about all the seed of Israel: whoever defiles it shall surely die, and they shall stone him.

**10** To this law there is no limit of days, and no remission, and no atonement at all; but the man who has defiled his daughter shall be rooted out in the middle of all Israel, because he has given of his seed to Molech and sinned so as to defile it.

**11** You, Moses, command the sons of Israel and testify to them that they should not give any of their daughters to the nations, and should not take any of the daughters of the nations, for it is abominable before the Lord.

**12** For this reason I have written for you in the words of the law all the deeds of the Shechemites, which they did against Dinah, and how the sons of Jacob spoke, saying, "We will not give our daughter to a man who has a foreskin, for that is a disgrace to us."

**13** It is a disgrace to Israel, to those who give and to those who take from the daughters of the nations, for it is unclean and abominable to Israel.

**14** Israel will not be clean of this uncleanness while one has a wife from the daughters of the nations, or has given one of his daughters to a man of any of the nations.

**15** For it is plague upon plague and curse upon curse, and every judgment and plague and curse will come on anyone who does this deed; and if he transgresses and despises it, committing abomination and defiling the sanctuary of God, and those who defile His holy name, the whole people will be judged together for all his abominations.

**16** There will be no respect of persons and no partiality, and no receiving from his hand of fruit and offering and burnt offering and fat, or of the fragrance of a soothing aroma, so as to accept it; and so it will be with every man or woman in Israel who defiles the sanctuary.

**17** For this reason I have commanded you, saying, "Testify this testimony to Israel: see what happened to the Shechemites and their sons, how they were given into the hands of two sons of Jacob, and they killed them in judgment; and it was counted to them for truth, and written down for them as righteousness."

**18** The seed of Levi was chosen for the priesthood and to be Levites, to minister before the Lord as we do, all the days; and Levi and his sons are blessed forever, for he was zealous for the truth, to execute judgment and vengeance on all who rose up against Israel.

**19** So they bring up for him, as a testimony on the tablets of heaven, blessing and righteousness before the God of all.

**20** We remember the righteousness that a man has done in his life, at all the times of the year; up to a thousand generations they bring it up, and it comes to him and to his generations after him; and he has been written on the tablets of heaven as a friend and a righteous man.

**21** All this account I have written for you, and have commanded you to tell the sons of Israel not to sin, nor transgress the ordinances, nor break the covenant ordained for them, but to do it and be written down as friends.

**22** But if they transgress and do any of the ways of uncleanness, they will be written on the tablets of heaven as enemies, and they will be blotted out of the book of the living and written in the book of those who will be destroyed, and with those who will be rooted out of the earth.

**23** On the day the sons of Jacob killed Shechem, a writing went up for them in heaven, that they had done righteousness and uprightness and vengeance on the sinners, and it was written for a blessing.

**24** They brought Dinah their sister out of the house of Shechem, and they took captive all that was in Shechem, their sheep and their cattle and their donkeys, and all their goods and all their flocks, and brought it all to Jacob their father.

**25** He spoke with them about their having killed the city, for he feared those who lived in the land, the Canaanites and the Perizzites.

**26** The dread of the Lord was on all the cities around Shechem, and they did not rise to pursue the sons of Jacob, for terror had fallen on them.

## Notes

- **vv2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers the whole chapter, has them too.
- **vv1–4 Dinah and Shechem** — Genesis 34. **v1 "to Salem… in peace"** — Genesis 33:18: *Jacob came in one piece to the city of Shechem*; the Hebrew *shalem*, "whole, in one piece," is read here as a place, Salem. The date is 2143. **v2 "a daughter of twelve years"** — the Latin; by this book's dates, Dinah was born in 2134 (28:23) and is nine. **v12 "a man who has a foreskin"** — Genesis 34:14: *We cannot do this thing, to give our sister to a man who has a foreskin, for that is a disgrace to us.* Jubilees leaves out the circumcision of the men of Shechem (Genesis 34:15–24), the deceit that let the brothers kill them.
- **vv5–17 the law against marrying outside Israel** — the angel makes the story a law on the tablets of heaven. **v10 "given of his seed to Molech"** — Leviticus 20:2: *Any man of the sons of Israel… who gives any of his children to Molech shall surely be put to death. The people of the land shall stone him.* The Latin has *to a foreigner* for *to Molech*.
- **vv17–23 Levi's zeal** — in Genesis Jacob condemns the deed: *You have muddied me, to make me stink among those who dwell in the land* (34:30); and at his death, *Simeon and Levi are brothers; instruments of violence are their trade… Cursed be their anger* (49:5, 7). Jubilees counts it as righteousness and makes it the reason Levi is chosen for the priesthood, as Phinehas was for his zeal: *because he was jealous for his God and made atonement for the sons of Israel* (Numbers 25:13), and *it was counted to him as righteousness, from generation to generation, forever* (Psalm 106:31). **v22 "blotted out of the book of the living"** — Psalm 69:29: *Let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and not be written with the righteous.*
- **vv25–26 Jacob's fear** — Genesis 34:30 and 35:5: *A terror of God was on the cities around them, and they did not pursue after the sons of Jacob.*

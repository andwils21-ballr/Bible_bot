# Jubilees 43

**1** He did as Joseph had told him, and filled all their sacks with food for them, and put their money in their sacks, and put the cup in Benjamin's sack.

**2** Early in the morning they went; and it came to pass, when they had gone out from there, that Joseph said to the man of his house, "Pursue them, run, and contend with them, saying, 'You have repaid me evil for good; you have stolen from me the silver cup from which my lord drinks.' Bring back to me their youngest brother, and bring him quickly, before I go out to my judgment seat."

**3** He ran after them and spoke to them according to these words.

**4** They said to him, "Far be it from your servants to do this thing, and to steal any vessel from the house of your lord; and the money too, which we found in our sacks the first time, we your servants brought back from the land of Canaan.

**5** How then would we steal any vessel? Here we are with our sacks; search, and wherever you find the cup, in the sack of any man among us, let him be put to death, and we and our donkeys will serve your lord."

**6** He said to them, "Not so. The man with whom I find it, him alone I will take as a servant, and you will go back to your house in peace."

**7** As he was searching in their vessels, beginning with the eldest and ending with the youngest, it was found in Benjamin's sack.

**8** They tore their garments, and loaded their donkeys, and went back to the city, and came to Joseph's house, and they all bowed down to him with their faces to the ground.

**9** Joseph said to them, "You have done evil." They said, "What shall we say, and how shall we defend ourselves? Our lord has found out the guilt of his servants. Here we are, the servants of our lord, and our donkeys too."

**10** Joseph said to them, "I fear the Lord. As for you, go to your homes, and let your brother be a servant, for you have done evil. Do you not know that a man delights in his cup, as I do in this cup? Yet you have stolen it from me."

**11** Judah said, "Let your servant, my lord, speak a word in my lord's ear: two brothers did your servant's mother bear to our father; one went away and was lost and has not been found, and he alone is left of his mother, and your servant our father loves him, and his life is bound up with the life of this boy.

**12** It will come to pass, when we go to your servant our father and the boy is not with us, that he will die, and we will bring our father down with sorrow to death.

**13** Rather, let me, your servant, stay instead of the boy as a servant to my lord, and let the boy go with his brothers; for I became his pledge at the hand of your servant our father, and if I do not bring him back, your servant will bear the guilt before our father forever."

**14** Joseph saw that the hearts of all of them were one, each with the other, for good; and he could not hold himself in, and he told them that he was Joseph.

**15** He spoke with them in the Hebrew tongue, and fell on their necks and wept; but they did not know him, and they too began to weep.

**16** He said to them, "Do not weep over me. Hurry and bring my father to me, so that I may see him before I die, and the eyes of my brother Benjamin see it too.

**17** For this is the second year of the famine, and there are still five years, with no harvest, and no fruit of trees, and no plowing.

**18** Come down quickly, you and your households, so that you do not perish in the famine; and do not be troubled about your possessions, for the Lord sent me ahead of you to set things in order, so that many people may live.

**19** Tell my father that I am still alive; and you see that the Lord has made me as a father to Pharaoh, and to rule in his house and over all the land of Egypt.

**20** Tell my father of all my honor, and all the wealth and honor the Lord has given me."

**21** By the word of Pharaoh's mouth he gave them chariots and provisions for the way, and he gave them all garments of many colors, and silver.

**22** To their father he sent garments and silver, and ten donkeys carrying grain, and he sent them away.

**23** They went up and told their father that Joseph was alive, and was measuring out grain to all the nations of the earth, and that he was ruler over all the land of Egypt.

**24** Their father did not believe it, for he was stunned in his mind; but when he saw the wagons Joseph had sent, his spirit was renewed and revived, and he said, "It is a great thing for me that Joseph is alive; I will go down and see him before I die."

## Notes

- **vv3, 4, 5, 11, 13, 15, 23, 24** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63). Without the ends of vv23–24 the EOTC never says what the brothers told their father, or that he went.
- **vv1–13 the silver cup** — Genesis 44. **v10 "a man delights in his cup"** — in Genesis the cup is the one Joseph *reads the omens with* (44:5), and he asks, *Did you not know that a man like me certainly divines?* (44:15). Jubilees leaves out the divining. **v11 "his life is bound up with the life of this boy"** — Genesis 44:30. **v13** — Genesis 44:33: *let your servant stay instead of the boy as a slave to my lord, and let the boy go up with his brothers.*
- **vv14–20 Joseph makes himself known** — Genesis 45:1–13. **v14 "the hearts of all of them were one… for good"** — the test Joseph set in 42:25, *whether there were thoughts of peace among them*, is passed. **v15 "in the Hebrew tongue"** — the language restored to Abraham (12:25–26). **v17** — Genesis 45:6: *For these two years the famine has been within the land, and there are five more years in which there is no plowing and no harvest.* By this book's dates the famine began in 2171 (42:1), and this is 2172 (42:20). **v18 "so that many people may live"** — Genesis 45:5, 7: *it was to preserve life that God sent me ahead of you.* **v19 "a father to Pharaoh"** — Genesis 45:8.
- **vv21–24 the wagons** — Genesis 45:21–28: *His heart went numb, for he did not believe them… he saw the wagons Joseph had sent to carry him. The spirit of Jacob their father revived. Israel said, "Enough. Joseph my son is still alive. Let me go and see him before I die."*

# Jubilees 41

**1** In the forty-fifth jubilee, in the second week, in the second year, Judah took a wife for Er his firstborn from the daughters of Aram, and her name was Tamar.

**2** He hated her and did not lie with her, because his mother was of the daughters of Canaan, and he wanted to take a wife from his mother's kin; but Judah his father would not let him.

**3** This Er, Judah's firstborn, was evil, and the Lord put him to death.

**4** Judah said to Onan his brother, "Go in to your brother's wife and do the brother's duty by her, and raise up seed for your brother."

**5** Onan knew that the seed would not be his, but his brother's only; and he went into the house of his brother's wife and spilled the seed on the ground, and he was evil in the eyes of the Lord, and He put him to death.

**6** Judah said to Tamar his daughter-in-law, "Stay in your father's house as a widow until Shelah my son grows up, and I will give you to him as a wife."

**7** He grew up, but Bedsuel, Judah's wife, did not let Shelah her son marry; and Bedsuel, Judah's wife, died in the fifth year of this week.

**8** In its sixth year Judah went up to shear his sheep at Timnah; and it was told to Tamar, "Here — your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his sheep."

**9** She put off her widow's garments and put on a veil and adorned herself, and sat at the gate by the road to Timnah.

**10** As Judah was going, he found her and took her for a harlot, and said to her, "Let me come in to you." She said to him, "Come in," and he went in.

**11** She said to him, "Give me my hire." He said to her, "I have nothing in my hand but the ring on my finger, and my cord, and my staff that is in my hand."

**12** She said to him, "Give them to me until you send me my hire." Judah said, "I will send you a young goat." He gave them to her, and was with her, and she conceived by him.

**13** Judah went to his sheep, and she went to her father's house.

**14** Judah sent a kid by the hand of his shepherd, the Adullamite, and he did not find her; and he asked the people of the place, saying, "Where is the harlot who was here?" They said to him, "There is no harlot here, and we have no harlot among us."

**15** He returned and told him that he had not found her, and said to him, "I asked the people of the place too, and they said to me, 'There is no harlot here.'" He said, "Get up, let us go, so that we do not become a laughingstock."

**16** When three months had passed, it became known that she was with child; and they told Judah, saying, "Here — Tamar your daughter-in-law is with child by harlotry."

**17** Judah went to her father's house and said to her father and her brothers, "Bring her out and let them burn her, for she has done an unclean thing in Israel."

**18** And it came to pass, when they brought her out to burn her, that she sent to her father-in-law the ring and the cord and the staff, and said, "Recognize whose these are, for by him I am with child."

**19** Judah recognized them and said, "Tamar is more righteous than I," and they did not burn her after all.

**20** For this reason she was not given to Shelah, and he did not come near her again.

**21** After that she bore two sons, Perez and Zerah, in the seventh year of this second week.

**22** Then the seven years of plenty that Joseph had told Pharaoh of were completed.

**23** Judah knew that the deed he had done was evil, for he had lain with his daughter-in-law, and it was hateful in his eyes; and he knew that he had sinned and gone astray, for he had uncovered his son's skirt; and he began to lament and to plead before the Lord for his transgression.

**24** We told him in a dream that it was forgiven him, because he pleaded earnestly and lamented, and did not do it again.

**25** He had forgiveness, because he turned from his sin and from his ignorance, for the transgression before our God was great; and everyone who does so, everyone who lies with his mother-in-law, let them burn him with fire, that he may burn in it, for there is uncleanness and defilement on them; with fire let them burn them.

**26** You too, command the sons of Israel, and let there be no uncleanness among them; for everyone who lies with his daughter-in-law or with his mother-in-law has done an unclean thing. With fire let them burn the man who has lain with her, and the woman too, and He will turn away wrath and punishment from Israel.

**27** To Judah we said that his two sons had not lain with her; and for this reason his seed was established for a second generation.

**28** For in the singleness of his eye he had gone and sought judgment; for according to the judgment of Abraham, which he had commanded his sons, Judah wanted to burn her with fire.

## Notes

- **vv5, 7, 8, 12, 15, 16, 25, 27** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter from v6 to v18, has most of vv7–16 too. Without the end of v12 the EOTC never says that Tamar conceived by Judah. **v15** — in the Latin Judah says *Let her keep them*; in the Asmara printing he says *Get up, let us go*.
- **vv1–21 Judah and Tamar** — Genesis 38, retold in order, in this project's wording where the Ge'ez allows. **v1 "from the daughters of Aram"** — Genesis 38:6 does not say where Tamar came from; Jubilees makes her an Aramean, of Abraham's kin, and not a Canaanite. **v2 Er's hatred** — Genesis 38:7 says only that *Er, Judah's firstborn, was evil in the eyes of the LORD*; Jubilees gives the reason: he wanted a Canaanite wife like his mother. **v7 "Bedsuel"** — so the EOTC here; the Latin has *Bathshua*, and the bracket at 34:20, from Charles, has *Betasuel*. All are *bat-Shua*, "daughter of Shua" (Genesis 38:2). **v11 "the ring on my finger"** — Genesis 38:18 has *Your seal and your cord, and your staff*. **v19 "Tamar is more righteous than I"** — Genesis 38:26: *She is more in the right than I, because I did not give her to Shelah my son.*
- **v1 and v21 the dates** — the second year of the second week of the forty-fifth jubilee is 2165; Perez and Zerah are born in its seventh year, 2170. **v22** — the seven years of plenty began in the year of Isaac's death (40:12).
- **vv23–28 Judah's repentance** — Jubilees has just said that there is no atonement for such a sin (33:13, of Reuben). The text gives two reasons Judah is forgiven: he turned *from his sin and from his ignorance* (v25), and his two sons had not lain with Tamar (v27). **v26 "with fire let them burn"** — Leviticus 20:12 sets death for *a man who lies with his daughter-in-law*, and 20:14 burning for *a man who takes a woman and her mother*. **v28 "the judgment of Abraham"** — Abraham's charge, *If any woman or girl among you commits fornication, burn her with fire* (20:4).

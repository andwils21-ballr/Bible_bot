# Jubilees 38

**1** After that Judah spoke to Jacob his father and said to him, "Bend your bow, father, and send your arrow, and shoot the adversary and kill the enemy, and may you have the strength; for we will not kill your brother, for he is close to you and like you, and to us he is owed honor."

**2** Then Jacob bent his bow and sent an arrow and struck Esau his brother [in his right breast] and killed him.

**3** Again he sent an arrow and struck Adoran the Aramean in the left breast, and drove him back and killed him.

**4** Then the sons of Jacob went out, they and their servants, dividing themselves on the four sides of the tower.

**5** Judah went out in front, and Naphtali and Gad with him, and fifty servants with him, on the south side of the tower, and they killed all they found before them, and not one of them escaped.

**6** Levi and Dan and Asher went out on the east side of the tower, and fifty with them, and they killed the fighting men of Moab and of Ammon.

**7** Reuben and Issachar and Zebulun went out on the north side of the tower, and fifty with them, and they killed the fighting men of the Philistines.

**8** Simeon and Benjamin and Hanoch, Reuben's son, went out on the west side of the tower, and fifty with them, and they killed of Edom and of the Horites four hundred men, strong warriors; and six hundred fled[, and the four sons of Esau fled with them, and left the body of their father cast out on the hill that is in Aduram].

**9** The sons of Jacob pursued them as far as Mount Seir[; and Jacob buried Esau his brother on the hill that is in Aduram, and returned to the tower].

**10** The sons of Jacob pressed hard on the sons of Esau on Mount Seir and bowed their necks, so that they became servants of the sons of Jacob.

**11** They sent to their father, to ask whether they should make peace with them or kill them.

**12** Jacob sent word to his sons to make peace, and they made peace with them, and put the yoke of servitude on them, so that they paid tribute to Jacob and to all his sons.

**13** They went on paying tribute to Jacob until the day Jacob went down into Egypt.

**14** The sons of Edom have not got free of the yoke of servitude that the twelve sons of Jacob laid on them, to this day.

**15** These are the kings who reigned in Edom before a king reigned over the sons of Israel, to this day, in the land of Edom.

**16** Bela son of Beor reigned in Edom, and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

**17** Bela died, and Jobab son of Zerah of Bozrah reigned in his place.

**18** Jobab died, and Husham of the mountain of Teman reigned in his place.

**19** Husham died, and Hadad son of Bedad, who struck Midian in the field of Moab, reigned in his place, and the name of his city was Avith.

**20** Hadad died, and Samlah of Masrekah reigned in his place.

**21** Samlah died, and Shaul of Rehoboth-on-the-River reigned in his place.

**22** Shaul died, and Baal-hanan son of Achbor reigned in his place.

**23** Baal-hanan son of Achbor died, and Hadar reigned in his place, and his wife's name was Mehetabel daughter of Matred daughter of Me-zahab.

**24** These are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom.

## Notes

- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. They follow the Latin, which covers this chapter from v1 to v16: vv2, 8 and 9. Without v9's bracket the Ge'ez never says that Jacob buried his brother.
- **vv1–3 the death of Esau** — not in Genesis, which has Esau alive and at peace with Jacob at their father's burial (Genesis 35:29) and says nothing of his death. Here Jacob shoots him only when Judah presses him; the Greek: *Jacob, being pressed by Judah… bent his bow, and striking Esau upon his right breast, he brought him down.* So the word of Isaac's last charge comes true: *everyone who seeks evil for his brother will fall into his hand* (36:9).
- **vv4–9 the battle** — the sons of Jacob go out by threes on the four sides of the tower, as the tribes will later camp by threes on the four sides of the tabernacle (Numbers 2). **v8 "Hanoch, Reuben's son"** — Genesis 46:9: *The sons of Reuben: Hanoch and Pallu and Hezron and Carmi.* The EOTC writes *ʾEnok*.
- **vv10–14 Edom under the yoke** — Isaac's words over Jacob, *Be lord over your brothers* (26:24), and over Esau, *you will serve your brother* (26:34). Kings tells when the yoke broke: *In his days Edom rebelled from under the hand of Judah, and they made a king over themselves* (2 Kings 8:20).
- **vv15–24 the kings of Edom** — Genesis 36:31–39, with the names as in this project's Genesis. The EOTC's forms differ: *Balāq* for Bela, *Danābā* for Dinhabah, *ʾAsām* for Husham, *ʾAdāt son of Bārd* for Hadad son of Bedad, *Salomān* for Samlah, *ʾAdāt* again for Hadar, and *Mayṭabit daughter of Maṭrit* for Mehetabel daughter of Matred. Jubilees leaves out Hadar's city, *Pau*.

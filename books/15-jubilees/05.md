# Jubilees 5

**1** And it came to pass, when the sons of men began to multiply on the face of all the earth and daughters were born to them, [that the angels of God saw them in a certain year of this jubilee, that they were beautiful to look at; and they took wives for themselves from all whom they chose, and these bore them sons, and they were giants].

**2** Wrongdoing grew on the earth, and all flesh corrupted its way, from man to the cattle and to the wild animals and to the birds and to everything that walks on the earth: all of them corrupted their ways and their order[, and they began to devour one another; and wrongdoing grew on the earth, and every thought of the minds of all men was only evil all the time].

**3** The Lord looked at the earth, and here — it was corrupt. All flesh had corrupted its order, and all that were on the earth had done evil before His eyes.

**4** He said that He would wipe out man and all flesh on the face of the earth that He had created.

**5** Noah alone found favor in His eyes.

**6** Against His angels whom He had sent to the earth He was very angry, and He uprooted them from all their dominion[; and He told us to bind them in the depths of the earth, and there they are bound in the middle of them, and are kept apart].

**7** Against their children a word went out from before His face, to strike them with the sword and remove them from under heaven.

**8** He said, "My spirit shall not remain on man forever, for they are flesh, and their days shall be a hundred and twenty years."

**9** He sent His sword among them, so that each should kill his neighbor[, and they began to kill one another until they all fell by the sword and were wiped out from the earth].

**10** Their fathers watched, and after this they were bound in the depths of the earth until the day of the great judgment, when judgment is carried out on all who have corrupted their ways and their works before the Lord.

**11** He wiped them all out from their places, and not one of them was left whom He did not judge for all their wickedness.

**12** He made for all His works a new and righteous nature, so that they should never again sin in all their nature[, but should all be righteous, each in its kind, always].

**13** The judgment of all of them is ordained and written on the tablets of heaven, and there is no injustice[: even the judgment of all who leave the path that is ordained for them to walk in; if they do not walk in it, judgment is written down for every creature and for every kind].

**14** There is nothing in heaven or on earth, in light or in darkness, in Sheol or in the deep[, or in the place of darkness, that is not judged; and all their judgments are ordained and written and engraved].

**15** He will judge concerning all: the great according to his greatness, the small according to his smallness, and each one according to his way.

**16** He is not one who shows partiality, and He is not one who takes gifts, when He says He will carry out judgment on each one. If one gave Him everything that is on the earth, He would not show partiality or accept it from him, for He is a righteous judge.

**17** Of the sons of Israel it has been written and ordained: if they turn to Him in righteousness, He will forgive all their guilt and pardon all their sins.

**18** It is written and ordained that He will show mercy to all who turn from all their error[, once each year].

**19** As for all who corrupted their ways and their counsel before the flood, He showed favor to no one except Noah alone. He showed him favor for the sake of his sons, whom He saved from the waters of the flood because of him, for his heart was righteous in all his ways[, as was commanded concerning him, and he had not left anything that was ordained for him].

**20** The Lord said that He would wipe out everything on the dry ground, from man to the cattle, to the wild animals, to the birds, to what moves on the earth.

**21** He commanded Noah to make himself an ark, to save himself from the waters of the flood.

**22** Noah made the ark in every respect as He had commanded him, in the twenty-seventh jubilee of years, in the fifth week, in its fifth year.

**23** He went in, in its sixth year, in the second month, from the new moon of the second month until the sixteenth: he went in, and all that we brought to him, into the ark[, and the Lord shut it from outside on the evening of the seventeenth].

**24** The Lord opened seven floodgates of heaven, and opened the mouths of the springs of the great deep, seven mouths in number.

**25** The floodgates began to pour down water from heaven forty days and forty nights[, and the springs of the deep also sent up waters, until the whole world was full of water].

**26** The waters grew on the earth. The waters rose fifteen cubits above every high mountain, and the ark was lifted up from the earth and moved on the face of the waters.

**27** The waters stood on the face of the earth five months, a hundred and fifty days.

**28** The ark went and came to rest on the top of Lubar, one of the mountains of Ararat.

**29** In the fourth month the springs of the great deep were stopped, and the floodgates of heaven were held back[; and on the new moon of the seventh month all the mouths of the depths of the earth were opened, and the water began to go down into the deep below].

**30** On the new moon of the tenth month the tops of the mountains appeared[, and on the new moon of the first month the earth appeared].

**31** The waters dried up from the earth [in the fifth week, in its seventh year, and on the seventeenth day of the second month the earth was dry].

**32** On its twenty-seventh day he opened the ark and sent out of it the wild animals and the birds and what moves.

## Notes

- **Words in [brackets]** — not in either Ge'ez text here, which is cut short in these verses. They follow Charles's English, made from other Ge'ez manuscripts: vv1, 2, 6, 9, 12, 13, 14, 18, 19, 23, 25, 29, 30 and 31. In v1 the Ge'ez stops at *daughters were born to them*, before the angels ever see them; the words that make the chapter make sense are all in the bracket.
- **vv1–2 "the angels of God saw them… every thought of the minds of all men was only evil"** — Genesis 6:1–5: *the sons of God saw the daughters of humankind… every shaping of the thoughts of his heart was only evil all the day*. Where Genesis has *the sons of God*, Jubilees has *the angels of God*, and the Watchers of 4:15 are meant.
- **vv6–10 "bind them in the depths of the earth… until the day of the great judgment"** — the sentence of 1 Enoch 10:12: *bind them for seventy generations under the hills of the earth, until the day of their judgment*. Jude: *the angels who did not keep their own domain, but left their proper dwelling, He has kept in eternal chains under darkness for the judgment of the great day* (Jude 6). The sons kill one another with the sword God sends among them (v9), as Gabriel is told in 1 Enoch 10:9: *send them against one another in a war of destruction*.
- **v16 "He is not one who shows partiality, and He is not one who takes gifts"** — Moses: *the great God, mighty and fearsome, who does not show partiality and does not take a bribe* (Deuteronomy 10:17). The Ge'ez says *lift up the face*, the Hebrew idiom for favoring someone; v19 uses it too.
- **vv17–18 "He will show mercy to all who turn… once each year"** — the Day of Atonement: *to make atonement for the sons of Israel for all their sins once a year* (Leviticus 16:34). The *once each year* is Charles's; the Ge'ez here ends at *all their error*.
- **v19 "He showed him favor for the sake of his sons"** — Ezekiel names Noah as a righteous man whose righteousness could save no one else: *even if Noah, Daniel and Job were in it… they would save neither son nor daughter* (Ezekiel 14:20). In the flood, Jubilees says, it did: his sons were saved *because of him*.
- **v22 "in the twenty-seventh jubilee"** — the EOTC reads *in the two jubilees*, *ba-kəlʾetu ʾiyobelwu*, in both Ge'ez texts. The chapter's own count needs the twenty-seventh, as Charles has it: Noah was born in the fifteenth jubilee (4:28), and 26 × 49 + 4 × 7 + 5 = 1307, about six hundred years after his birth, the age Genesis gives for the flood (Genesis 7:11). The English follows the count.
- **vv23–32 the dates of the flood** — Genesis: the flood began *in the second month, on the seventeenth day* (Genesis 7:11), and *the LORD shut him in* (7:16); the waters prevailed *a hundred and fifty days* (7:24); *in the tenth month, on the first of the month, the heads of the mountains were seen* (8:5); *in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dried out* (8:14). Jubilees keeps every date and adds its own new-moon markers. Five months of thirty days make the hundred and fifty days of v27.
- **v28 "the top of Lubar, one of the mountains of Ararat"** — Genesis names only *the mountains of Ararat* (Genesis 8:4). Jubilees names the peak.

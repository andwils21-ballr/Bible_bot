# Jubilees 21

**1** In the sixth year of the seventh week of this jubilee Abraham called Isaac his son and commanded him, saying, "I have grown old, and I do not know the day of my death, for I am full of my days.

**2** I am one hundred and seventy-five years old, and all the days of my life I have remembered the Lord, and sought with all my heart to do His will and to walk uprightly in all His ways.

**3** My soul has hated idols[, and I have despised those who serve them, and I have given my heart and my spirit], so that I might keep myself to do the will of Him who created me.

**4** For He is the living God, and He is holy, and faithful and righteous above all, and with Him there is no respect of persons and no taking of gifts; for God is righteous, and He executes judgment on all who transgress His commandments and despise His covenant.

**5** You too, my son, keep His commandments and His ordinances and His judgments, and do not go after the unclean things, nor after carved images, nor after molten ones.

**6** Eat no blood at all, of wild animal or of cattle, or of any bird that flies in the sky.

**7** If you slaughter a sacrifice as an acceptable peace offering, slaughter it, and pour out its blood on the altar; and all the fat of the offering offer on the altar, with fine flour and the grain offering mixed with oil, with its drink offering — offer them all together on the altar of burnt offering, a soothing aroma before the Lord.

**8** The fat of the sacrifice of peace offering you shall set on the fire that is on the altar: the fat that is on the belly, and all the fat that is on the inner parts, and the two kidneys and all the fat that is on them and on the loins, and the liver, which you shall remove with the kidneys.

**9** Offer all this for a soothing aroma acceptable before the Lord, with its grain offering and its drink offering, for a soothing aroma, the bread of the offering to the Lord.

**10** Eat its meat on that day and on the next, and do not let the sun go down on it on the next day until it is eaten; and let nothing be left for the third day, for it is not acceptable, for it is not approved, and let it no longer be eaten; and all who eat of it will bring sin on themselves. For so I have found it written in the books of my forefathers, and in the words of Enoch, and in the words of Noah.

**11** On all your offerings you shall scatter salt, and let not the covenant of salt be missing from all your offerings before the Lord.

**12** Take care with the wood of the sacrifice, that you bring no other wood onto the altar than these: cypress and bay and almond, fir and pine and cedar, savin and fig and the olive tree, myrrh and laurel, and the cedar called *arbat*, and *besem*.

**13** Of these kinds of wood lay under the offering on the altar such as have been tested by their look; and do not lay on it any split or dark wood, but wood that is hard and firm, without any blemish, whole and of new growth. Do not lay on it old wood, for its fragrance has gone out, for there is no longer fragrance in it as at first.

**14** Besides these kinds of wood you shall lay no other[; for the fragrance is scattered, and the smell of its fragrance does not go up to heaven].

**15** Keep this command and do it, my son, so that you may be upright in all your works.

**16** At all times be clean in your body, and wash with water before you go to offer on the altar; and wash your hands and your feet before you draw near to the altar; and when you have finished offering, wash your hands and your feet again.

**17** Let no blood be seen on you or on your clothes; be on your guard, my son, against blood, be very much on your guard; cover it with earth.

**18** Do not eat blood, for blood is the soul; eat no blood at all.

**19** Take no gifts for any human blood, so that it is not shed with impunity, without judgment; for it is the blood that is shed that makes the earth sin, and the earth cannot be cleansed from human blood except by the blood of the one who shed it.

**20** Take no present or gift for human blood: blood for blood, and you will be acceptable before God Most High; for He is the defense of the good, and so you will be kept from all evil, and He will save you from every kind of death.

**21** I see, my son, all the works of the sons of men, that they are sin and wickedness, and all their deeds are uncleanness and abomination and defilement, and there is no righteousness with them.

**22** Take care not to walk in their ways and tread in their paths, and do not sin a sin to death before God Most High; otherwise He will hide His face from you and give you back into the hands of your transgression, and root you out of the land, and your seed likewise from under heaven, and your name and your seed will perish from the whole earth.

**23** Turn away from all their deeds and all their uncleanness, and keep the charge of God Most High, and do His will, and be upright in all things.

**24** He will bless you in all your deeds, and will raise up from you a plant of righteousness in all the earth, through all the generations of the earth; and my name and your name will not be forgotten under heaven, for all days.

**25** Go, my son, in peace. May God Most High, my God and your God, strengthen you to do His will, and may He bless all your seed and the remnant of your seed for the eternal generations with every blessing of righteousness, so that you may be a blessing on all the earth."

**26** He went out from him rejoicing.

## Notes

- **vv10, 20, 21, 22** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63). **Words in [brackets]** are in no whole Ge'ez text here: in v3 they follow the Latin, which covers this chapter as far as v10; in v14 they follow Charles's English, where the Asmara printing is broken (*for its fragrance… to heaven*).
- **vv1–2 the date** — the sixth year of the seventh week of the forty-second jubilee is 41 × 49 + 6 × 7 + 6 = 2057, which by 11:15 makes Abraham 181; he says he is 175, the age at which he dies in Genesis 25:7. The text is left as it stands (compare 15:1; 20:1).
- **vv6–10 the peace offering** — the law of Leviticus 3 and 19, given here by Abraham to Isaac long before Sinai: *the fat covering the entrails… and the two kidneys with the fat on them at the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys* (Leviticus 3:3–4); *It shall be eaten on the day you sacrifice it and on the next day… If it is eaten at all on the third day, it is foul. It will not be accepted* (Leviticus 19:6–7). **v9 "the bread of the offering"** — Leviticus 3:11, *food, an offering by fire to the LORD*. **v10 "the books of my forefathers… Enoch… Noah"** — in Charles's text Abraham names his sources. The book has already told of *the books of his fathers*, which he copied in Hebrew (12:27), and of the book Noah wrote (10:13).
- **v11 "the covenant of salt"** — Leviticus 2:13: *You shall not let the salt of the covenant of your God be missing from your grain offering. With all your offerings you shall bring salt.*
- **v12 the woods** — the list follows Charles's English for the first eleven, *cypress, bay, almond, fir, pine, cedar, savin, fig, olive, myrrh, laurel*; he gives *aspalathus* for the twelfth. The Ge'ez has thirteen names, the last two left here as it writes them: *the cedar called arbat*, and *besem*. Several are Greek names in Ge'ez letters: *qeṗarson*, *ṗiṭin*, *qedron*.
- **v16 washing hands and feet** — Exodus 30:20–21: *When they come into the tent of meeting they shall wash with water, and they shall not die… They shall wash their hands and their feet.*
- **vv17–18 "cover it with earth… blood is the soul"** — Leviticus 17:13–14: *shall pour out its blood and cover it with earth. For the life of all flesh — its blood is its life.* Genesis 9:4: *flesh with its soul — its blood — you shall not eat.*
- **vv19–20 "blood for blood"** — Numbers 35:33: *the blood pollutes the land, and for the land no atonement can be made for the blood shed in it except by the blood of the one who shed it.* Genesis 9:6: *One who sheds the blood of the human, by the human shall his blood be shed.*
- **v22 "a sin to death"** — 1 John 5:16: *There is a sin to death; I do not say that he should ask about that.*
- **v24 "a plant of righteousness"** — as at 16:26.

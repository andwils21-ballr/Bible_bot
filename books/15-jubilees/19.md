# Jubilees 19

**1** In the first year of the first week of the forty-second jubilee Abraham returned and lived opposite Hebron, that is Kiriath-arba, two weeks of years.

**2** In the first year of the third week of this jubilee the days of Sarah's life were completed, and she died in Hebron.

**3** Abraham went to weep for her and to bury her. We tested him, to see whether his spirit was patient and not faint in the words of his mouth; and he was found very patient in this too, and was not troubled.

**4** For with patience of spirit he spoke with the sons of Heth, that they might give him a place to bury his dead in.

**5** The Lord gave him favor before all who saw him; and he entreated the sons of Heth in meekness, and they gave him the field of the double cave, which is opposite Mamre — that is Hebron — for forty pieces of silver.

**6** They pleaded with him, saying, "We will give it to you for nothing"; but he would not take it from their hands for nothing, for he gave the price of the place, the silver in full; and he bowed down before them twice, and after this he buried his dead in the double cave.

**7** All the days of Sarah's life were one hundred and twenty-seven years, that is, two jubilees and four weeks and one year; these are the days of the years of Sarah's life.

**8** This is the tenth trial with which Abraham was tried, and he was found faithful, patient in spirit.

**9** He did not say a word about the promise of the land, that the Lord had said He would give it to him and to his seed after him; and he begged a place from them to bury his dead, for he was found faithful, and he was written on the tablets of heaven as the friend of God.

**10** In its fourth year he took a wife for Isaac his son, and her name was Rebekah, the daughter of Bethuel, the son of Nahor, Abraham's brother, the sister of Laban and daughter of Bethuel; and Bethuel was the son of Milcah, the wife of Nahor, Abraham's brother.

**11** Abraham took for himself a third wife, named Keturah, from the daughters of his household servants, for Hagar had died before Sarah.

**12** She bore him six sons, Zimran and Jokshan and Medan and Midian and Ishbak and Shuah, in two weeks of years.

**13** In the sixth week, in its second year, Rebekah bore Isaac two sons, Jacob and Esau. Jacob was whole and upright, and Esau was a fierce man, a man of the field, and hairy; and Jacob lived in tents.

**14** The young men grew, and Jacob learned writing; but Esau did not learn, for he was a man of the field and a hunter, and he learned war, and all his deeds were fierce.

**15** Abraham loved Jacob, and Isaac loved Esau.

**16** Abraham saw Esau's deeds, and he knew that through Jacob name and seed would be called for him; and he called Rebekah and gave her commands about Jacob, for she loved Jacob more than Esau.

**17** He said to her, "My daughter, watch over my son Jacob, for he will be in my place on the earth, and for a blessing among the sons of men, and for the glory of all the seed of Shem.

**18** For I know that the Lord will choose him to be a people for His own possession, above all who are on the face of the earth.

**19** Isaac my son loves Esau more than Jacob, but I see that you love Jacob in truth.

**20** Add still more kindness to him, and let your eyes be on him in love; for he will be a blessing for us on the earth, from now on and to every generation of the earth.

**21** Let your hands be strong, and let your heart rejoice in your son Jacob; for I have loved him above all my sons. He will be blessed forever, and his seed will fill all the earth.

**22** If a man can number the sand of the earth, so also will his seed be numbered.

**23** All the blessings with which the Lord has blessed me and my seed will belong to Jacob and to his seed all the days.

**24** In his seed my name will be blessed, and the name of my fathers, Shem and Noah and Enoch and Mahalalel and Enosh and Seth and Adam.

**25** These will serve to lay the foundations of heaven, and to strengthen the earth, and to renew all the lights that are in the expanse."

**26** He called Jacob before the eyes of Rebekah his mother, and kissed him and blessed him and said,

**27** "Jacob, my beloved son, whom my soul loves, may God bless you from above the expanse, and give you all the blessings with which He blessed Adam and Enoch and Noah and Shem, and all that He has told me, and all that He promised to give me; may He make them cleave to you and to your seed forever, according to the days of heaven above the earth.

**28** May the spirits of Mastema not rule over you or over your seed, to turn you away from following the Lord, who is your God, from now on and forever.

**29** May the Lord God be a father to you, and you His firstborn son and His people, all the days. Go in peace, my son."

**30** The two of them went out together from Abraham.

**31** Rebekah loved Jacob with all her heart and with all her spirit, very much more than Esau; and Isaac loved Esau much more than Jacob.

## Notes

- **vv3, 5, 6, 9, 16, 20, 21, 27, 29** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter as far as v26, has most of them too. Without the ends of vv5–6 the EOTC never says where Sarah was buried. **v5 "forty pieces of silver"** — so the Asmara printing, *ʾarbāʿā*; the Latin and Genesis 23:16 have four hundred.
- **vv1–7 the dates** — the first year of the forty-second jubilee is 41 × 49 + 1 = 2010; the first year of its third week, when Sarah dies, is 2024. **v7 "two jubilees and four weeks and one year"** — 98 + 28 + 1 = 127, the years of Genesis 23:1: *The life of Sarah was a hundred years and twenty years and seven years*. The Latin has *four weeks minus one year*, which comes to 125.
- **vv3–9 the tenth trial** — Genesis 23 tells the purchase of the cave at length; Jubilees makes it a test of patience. Abraham, who has been promised the whole land, buys a grave in it and says nothing. **"the double cave"** — the name, in the Latin and in the Asmara printing, for the cave Genesis calls *Machpelah*, which it takes as *double*: *the cave of the field of Machpelah, facing Mamre — that is Hebron* (Genesis 23:19).
- **v9 "the friend of God"** — Isaiah 41:8: *the seed of Abraham My friend*; James 2:23: *he was called friend of God*. Here the name is written on the tablets of heaven.
- **v10 "Bethuel was the son of Milcah"** — the Ge'ez runs together here (*wa-Batuʾel waladomu la-Batuʾelāwiyān*, "and Bethuel fathered the Bethuelites"); the sense follows the Latin and Charles. Genesis 22:20: *Milcah has borne sons, she also, to Nahor your brother*. **"In its fourth year"** — 2027; Isaac, born in 1987, is forty, as in Genesis 25:20, *Isaac was forty years old when he took Rebekah*.
- **vv11–12 Keturah** — Genesis 25:1–2. Jubilees makes her a daughter of the household servants, and has Hagar die before Sarah; Genesis says neither.
- **vv13–14 Jacob and Esau** — Genesis 25:27: *Esau was a man who knew hunting, a man of the field; and Jacob was a whole man, dwelling in tents.* Jubilees adds that Jacob learned to write and Esau learned war. **The date** — the second year of the sixth week is 2046, when Isaac is fifty-nine; Genesis 25:26 has *Isaac was sixty years old when she bore them*, and the Greek of this verse says the same.
- **vv15–31 Abraham chooses Jacob** — in Genesis Abraham's death is told before the twins are born (Genesis 25:7–8), and the blessing reaches Jacob through Rebekah's plan, when Isaac's eyes are dim (Genesis 27:1). In Jubilees Abraham sees the boys grow up and sets his blessing on Jacob himself, with Rebekah as its keeper. **v16 "through Jacob name and seed would be called for him"** — the words of 16:16 and Genesis 21:12, *through Isaac shall seed be called for you*, carried on to the next generation.
- **v24 "Shem and Noah and Enoch and Mahalalel and Enosh and Seth and Adam"** — Abraham's line, skipping many names; the Latin leaves out Seth.
- **v25 "to lay the foundations of heaven, and to strengthen the earth"** — as the LORD says to His people in Isaiah 51:16: *to plant the heavens and to lay the foundations of the earth, and to say to Zion, "You are My people."*
- **v29 "a father to you, and you His firstborn son"** — Exodus 4:22: *Israel is My son, My firstborn*.

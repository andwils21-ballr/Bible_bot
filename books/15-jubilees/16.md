# Jubilees 16

**1** On the new moon of the fourth month we appeared to Abraham at the oak of Mamre, and we talked with him, and we told him that a son would be given to him by Sarah his wife.

**2** Sarah laughed, for she heard that we had spoken these words with Abraham; and we rebuked her, and she became afraid and denied that she had laughed at the words.

**3** We told her the name of her son, as it is ordained and written on the tablets of heaven — Isaac —

**4** and that when we returned to her at the set time, she would have conceived a son.

**5** In this month the Lord executed His judgment on Sodom and Gomorrah and Zeboiim and all the region of the Jordan, and He burned them with fire and sulfur and destroyed them to this day, even as I have told you all their works: how they were wicked and very great sinners, and defiled themselves and committed fornication in their flesh, and did what is unclean on the earth.

**6** In the same way the Lord will execute judgment on the places where they do as the uncleanness of Sodom, like the judgment of Sodom.

**7** But Lot we saved, for the Lord remembered Abraham, and brought him out from the middle of the overthrow.

**8** He and his daughters also committed a sin on the earth such as had not been on the earth from the days of Adam until then, for the man lay with his daughter.

**9** It has been commanded and engraved concerning all his seed on the tablets of heaven, to remove them and root them out, and to execute judgment on them like the judgment of Sodom, and to leave him no seed of man on the earth on the day of judgment.

**10** In this month Abraham moved from Hebron, and went and lived between Kadesh and Shur, in the mountains of Gerar.

**11** In the middle of the fifth month he moved from there and lived at the Well of the Oath.

**12** In the middle of the sixth month the Lord visited Sarah and did for her as He had said.

**13** She conceived and bore a son in the third month, in the middle of the month, at the time the Lord had told Abraham; on the festival of the first fruits of the harvest Isaac was born.

**14** Abraham circumcised his son at eight days old; he was the first to be circumcised according to the covenant that is ordained forever.

**15** In the sixth year of the fourth week we came to Abraham at the Well of the Oath, and we appeared to him, as we had told Sarah that we would return to her, and she would have conceived a son.

**16** We returned in the seventh month and found Sarah with child before us, and we blessed her and told her all that had been decreed for him: that he would not die until he had fathered six more sons, and would see them before he died; and in Isaac a name and seed would be called for him.

**17** All the seed of his sons would become nations and be counted with the nations; but from the sons of Isaac one would become a holy seed, and would not be counted among the nations.

**18** For he would become the portion of the Most High, and all his seed had fallen into the possession of God, to be for the Lord a people treasured above all peoples, and to be a kingdom and a priesthood and a holy people.

**19** We went on our way, and we told Sarah all that we had told him, and they both rejoiced with very great joy.

**20** He built an altar there to the Lord, who had delivered him and was making him rejoice in the land where he lived as a guest; and he kept a festival of joy in this month for seven days beside the altar he had built at the Well of the Oath.

**21** He built booths for himself and for his servants at this festival, and he was the first to keep the Festival of Booths on the earth.

**22** During these seven days he offered every day, day by day, on the altar a burnt offering to the Lord: two bulls and two rams, seven sheep, one male goat for sin, to make atonement with it for himself and for his seed.

**23** As a peace offering: seven rams and seven kids, seven sheep and seven male goats, and their grain offerings and their drink offerings; and he burned all their fat on the altar, a chosen offering for a soothing aroma.

**24** Morning and evening he burned fragrant incense: frankincense and galbanum and stacte, nard and myrrh, spice and costus. All seven of these he offered, crushed and mixed in equal parts, and pure.

**25** He kept this festival seven days, rejoicing with all his heart and with all his soul, he and all who were in his house; and there was no foreigner with him, nor anyone who was uncircumcised.

**26** He blessed his Creator, who had created him in his generation, for He had created him for His good pleasure; for he knew and perceived that from him would come the plant of righteousness for the eternal generations, and from him a holy seed, to be like the One who made all things.

**27** He blessed and rejoiced, and he called the name of this festival the festival of the Lord, a joy acceptable to God Most High.

**28** We blessed him forever, and all his seed after him through all the generations of the earth, because he kept this festival in its season, according to the testimony of the tablets of heaven.

**29** For this reason it is ordained on the tablets of heaven concerning Israel that they shall keep the Festival of Booths seven days with joy, in the seventh month, acceptable before the Lord, a law forever through their generations, every year.

**30** To this there is no limit of days, for it is ordained forever concerning Israel that they should keep it and live in booths, and set wreaths on their heads, and take leafy boughs and willows of the brook.

**31** Abraham took branches of palm trees and the fruit of splendid trees, and every day he went around the altar with the branches seven times; in the morning he rose early and praised and gave thanks to his God for everything with joy.

## Notes

- **vv16, 17, 20, 23** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63). **vv2, 5** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from Charles's edition of the Ge'ez (1895); the Asmara file held here lacks 14:16–16:13.
- **vv1–4 the visit at Mamre** — Genesis 18:10–15: *At the appointed time I will return to you, at the time of life, and Sarah shall have a son… Sarah denied it, saying, "I did not laugh" — for she was afraid.* In Genesis the three visitors eat with Abraham; in Jubilees they are the angels of the presence, and they name the child from the tablets of heaven.
- **vv5–9 Sodom and Lot** — Genesis 19:24–29: *The LORD rained upon Sodom and upon Gomorrah sulfur and fire… God remembered Abraham, and He sent Lot out from the midst of the overturning.* **v8 "with his daughter"** — so the EOTC; Charles and the Latin have *daughters*. **v9 "all his seed"** — Lot's seed, Moab and Ammon (Genesis 19:37–38). The Greek sums it up: *from Lot came the Moabites and Ammonites, an accursed seed from an unlawful union*.
- **vv10–11 "between Kadesh and Shur… the Well of the Oath"** — Genesis 20:1, *he settled between Kadesh and Shur, and he lived as a guest in Gerar*. The Well of the Oath is Beersheba: *Therefore that place was called Beersheba, for there the two of them swore* (Genesis 21:31).
- **vv12–14 Isaac's birth** — Genesis 21:1–4: *The LORD attended to Sarah as He had said… Abraham circumcised Isaac his son at eight days old*. Jubilees dates it to the festival of first fruits, the Festival of Weeks, the day of the covenants with Noah and Abram (6:17; 14:10; 15:1). **v13 "in the third month"** — so the Ge'ez and Charles; the Latin has *the seventh month*.
- **vv15–16 the dates** — the sixth year of the fourth week is 40 × 49 + 3 × 7 + 6 = 1987, the year after the covenant of 15:1 (1986), and so the year of Isaac's birth. Yet v16 finds Sarah *with child* in its seventh month, after a birth in the third. The text is left as it stands.
- **v16 "we blessed her and told her"** — so the EOTC; Charles and the Latin have *him*, Abraham, and v19 says the angels then told Sarah *all that we had told him*. **"six more sons"** — the sons of Keturah, Genesis 25:1–2. The Asmara printing reads *seven*, *sabʿata*. **"in Isaac a name and seed would be called for him"** — so the Asmara printing; Genesis 21:12 has *through Isaac shall seed be called for you*.
- **vv17–18 "a people treasured above all peoples… a kingdom and a priesthood and a holy people"** — Exodus 19:5–6: *you will be My own treasure out of all the peoples… You will be for Me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation.* Of Abraham's sons only Isaac's line, and of Isaac's sons only one, is kept out of *the nations*.
- **vv20–31 the first Festival of Booths** — Abraham keeps it at the Well of the Oath in the seventh month, before the law is given. Leviticus 23:40–42: *you shall take for yourselves the fruit of splendid trees, branches of palm trees, boughs of leafy trees, and willows of the brook… You shall live in booths for seven days.* Jubilees adds the wreaths on the head (v30) and the sevenfold circling of the altar (v31); the Greek has the circling with *branches of palm trees and olive trees*.
- **v24 the seven spices** — Exodus 30:34 names four of them for the incense of the tabernacle: *stacte and onycha and galbanum… and pure frankincense. Each shall be in equal measure.* **"stacte"** — the Ge'ez *māya ləbn*; **"spice"** — *sanbalt*, which the Latin gives as *spica*, spikenard.
- **v26 "for he knew"** — the Latin takes it as Abraham; Charles has *He*, God. **"the plant of righteousness"** — the name 1 Enoch gives the chosen line: *the plant of righteousness and uprightness forever* (1 Enoch 93:5).

# Jubilees 1

This is the account of the division of the days of the law and of the testimony, of the events of the years, of their weeks and their jubilees, through all the years of the world, as He spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai when he went up to receive the tablets of stone, the law and the commandment, by the word of the Lord, as He had said to him, "Go up to the top of the mountain."

**1** And it came to pass in the first year after the sons of Israel went out of Egypt, in the third month, on the sixteenth day of that month, that the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, "Come up to Me here on the mountain, and I will give you two tablets of stone, the law and the commandment, which I have written, so that you may teach them."

**2** Moses went up the mountain of God, and the glory of the Lord settled on Mount Sinai, and a cloud overshadowed it for six days.

**3** He called Moses on the seventh day from the middle of the cloud, and Moses saw the glory of the Lord like a fire burning on the top of the mountain.

**4** Moses was on the mountain forty days and forty nights, and the Lord taught him what had been and what was to come, the account of the division of all the days of the law and of the testimony.

**5** He said to him, "Set your heart on every word that I speak to you on this mountain, and write it in a book, so that their generations may see that I have not forsaken them for all the evil they have done in straying from the ordinance that I set between Me and you today, for their generations, on Mount Sinai.

**6** They will tell of it when all this comes upon them, and they will know that I am more righteous than they in all their judgments and in all their deeds, and they will know that I have truly been with them.

**7** As for you, write down for yourself all the words that I make known to you today. For I know their rebellion and their stiff neck, before I bring them into the land that I swore to Abraham and to Isaac and to Jacob, saying, 'To your seed I will give a land flowing with milk and honey.' They will eat and be satisfied, and they will turn to a foreign god, to gods who cannot save them from any of their distress.

**8** This testimony will be heard as a witness against them. For they will forget all My commandments that I command them, and they will walk after the nations, after their uncleanness and after their shame, and they will serve their gods, and these will become for them a stumbling block, and distress and anguish and a snare.

**9** Many will perish, and they will be taken captive and fall into the hand of the enemy, because they have forsaken My ordinances and My commandments, and the festivals of My covenant, and My sabbaths, and the holy things that I made holy for Myself among them, and My tabernacle and My sanctuary that I made holy for Myself in the middle of the land, to set My name on it, so that it might dwell there.

**10** They will make for themselves high places and groves and carved images, and each will worship his own and go astray. They will sacrifice their children to demons and to every work of the error of their heart. I will send witnesses to them, to testify against them, but they will not listen, and they will kill the witnesses too.

**11** Those who search out the law they will kill and persecute. They will set everything aside and begin to do evil before My eyes.

**12** I will hide My face from them and hand them over into the hand of the nations, to be taken captive and plundered and devoured. I will remove them from the middle of the land and scatter them among the nations.

**13** They will forget all My law and all My commandments and all My judgments, and they will set aside new moon and sabbath and festival and jubilee and ordinance.

**14** After this they will turn to Me from among the nations with all their heart and with all their soul and with all their strength, and I will gather them from among all the nations.

**15** They will seek Me, and I will let Myself be found by them. When they seek Me with all their heart and with all their soul, I will reveal to them abundant peace in righteousness, and I will plant them anew as a plant of uprightness, with all My heart and with all My soul.

**16** They will be a blessing and not a curse; they will be the head and not the tail. I will build My sanctuary among them and dwell with them. I will be their God, and they will be My people in truth and in righteousness. I will not forsake them or turn away from them, for I am the Lord their God."

**17** Moses fell on his face and prayed and said, "O Lord my God, do not forsake Your people and Your inheritance to walk in the error of their heart, and do not hand them over into the hand of the nations, to rule over them and make them sin against You.

**20** Let Your mercy, O Lord, be lifted up over Your people, and create in them an upright spirit. Do not let the spirit of Beliar rule over them, to accuse them before You and to trap them away from every path of righteousness, so that they perish from before Your face.

**21** They are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You rescued by Your great power from the hand of the Egyptians. Create in them a clean heart and a holy spirit, and do not let them be trapped in their sins from now on and forever."

**22** The Lord said to Moses, "I know their contrariness and their thoughts and their stiff neck, and they will not listen until they acknowledge their sin and the sins of their fathers.

**23** After this they will turn to Me in all uprightness, with all their heart and with all their soul. I will circumcise the foreskin of their heart and the foreskin of the heart of their seed, and I will create in them a holy spirit, and I will cleanse them, so that they will not turn away from Me from that day on and forever.

**24** Their souls will cling to Me and to all My commandments, and they will keep My commandments. I will be their Father, and they will be My children.

**25** They will all be called children of the living God, and every angel and every spirit will know them. They will know that they are My children, and that I am their Father in uprightness and in righteousness, and that I love them.

**26** As for you, write down for yourself all these words that I make known to you on this mountain: what was first and what is last and what is to come, in all the divisions of the days, in the law and in the testimony, and in their weeks and their jubilees, forever, until I come down and dwell with them for all the ages."

**27** He said to the angel of the presence, "Write for Moses from the beginning of creation until My sanctuary is built among them for all the ages.

**28** The Lord will appear to the eyes of all, and all will know that I am the God of Israel, and the Father of all the children of Jacob, and King on Mount Zion forever and ever. Zion and Jerusalem will be holy."

**29** The angel of the presence, who went before the camps of Israel, took the tablets of the division of the years, from the time of creation, of the law and of the testimony, of the weeks of the jubilees, year by year in their full number, and of the jubilees, from the day of the new creation, when the heavens and the earth and all their creatures are renewed, according to the powers of heaven and according to all the creation of the earth, until the sanctuary of the Lord is made in Jerusalem on Mount Zion, and all the lights are renewed for healing and for peace and for blessing for all the chosen of Israel, so that it may be so from that day and for all the days of the earth.

## Notes

- **The opening and the verse numbers** — the lines above verse 1 are the book's own title: *the account of the division*, *nagara kufāle*. From that word the book takes its Ge'ez name, *Maṣḥafa Kufāle*, **the Book of Division**: it divides the history of the world into jubilees of forty-nine years and weeks of seven. Verse numbers follow the EOTC printed Bible. It fits into vv1–17 what other editions number 1–19, then goes on from v20, so there are no verses 18 and 19 here and nothing is missing. From v20 on, the numbers agree with other editions.
- **v1 "which I have written"** — the EOTC reads *ṣaḥafka*, **you have written**; the second Ge'ez text reads *ṣaḥafku*, **I have written**, as Exodus 24:12 does: *I will give you the tablets of stone, the instruction and the commandment, which I have written to teach them*. The English follows the second Ge'ez text. Exodus dates the arrival at Sinai to *the third month* (Exodus 19:1); Jubilees gives the day, the sixteenth.
- **vv2–4 "the glory of the Lord settled on Mount Sinai"** — Exodus 24:16–18, retold almost word for word: six days of cloud, the call on the seventh, the fire on the top of the mountain, forty days and forty nights. Jubilees adds what Moses was taught there: *what had been and what was to come*. The second Ge'ez text ends v4 at *forty nights*.
- **v5 "that I have not forsaken them"** — the EOTC lacks the *not*: *ka-ma ḫadagwomu*. The second Ge'ez text has it, *ka-ma ʾi-ḫadagwomu*, and so does Charles. The English follows them, because v6 says the same from the other side: *they will know that I have truly been with them*.
- **vv7–8 "They will eat and be satisfied, and they will turn to a foreign god… This testimony will be heard as a witness against them"** — Deuteronomy 31, where the Lord tells Moses the same at the end of his life: *they will eat and be satisfied and grow fat, and turn to other gods… this song will answer before them as a witness* (Deuteronomy 31:20–21); *I know your rebellion and your stiff neck* (31:27). Jubilees moves the warning to the beginning, to Sinai. *A snare* is the warning of Exodus 23:33: *you would serve their gods, and it would become a snare to you*.
- **v9 "to set My name on it, so that it might dwell there"** — the words of Deuteronomy for the temple: *the place that the LORD your God chooses, to make His name dwell there* (Deuteronomy 12:11).
- **v10 "They will sacrifice their children to demons… they will kill the witnesses too"** — the psalm: *they sacrificed their sons and their daughters to demons* (Psalm 106:37). Nehemiah's confession: *they killed Your prophets, who testified against them to turn them back to You* (Nehemiah 9:26).
- **v12 "I will hide My face from them… to be devoured"** — Deuteronomy 31:17: *I will forsake them and hide My face from them, and they will be devoured*.
- **v13 "they will set aside new moon and sabbath and festival and jubilee"** — the EOTC reads *yəsəʿəru*, **set aside**. The second Ge'ez text reads *yəsəḥətu*, **go astray**, and Charles *go astray as to new moons, and sabbaths*. The second reading is the book's own fear about the calendar, stated again at 6:34: they *will forget the new moons, and seasons, and sabbaths*.
- **vv14–16 "with all their heart and with all their soul… with all My heart and with all My soul"** — Deuteronomy: *you will seek the LORD your God, and you will find Him, if you search for Him with all your heart and with all your soul* (Deuteronomy 4:29). Jeremiah gives the promise and its answer: *you will seek Me and find Me… I will let Myself be found by you* (Jeremiah 29:13–14), and *I will plant them in this land in faithfulness, with all My heart and with all My soul* (Jeremiah 32:41). *In truth and in righteousness* is Zechariah 8:8, *they will be My people and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness*; *the head and not the tail* is Deuteronomy 28:13.
- **vv17, 21 "do not forsake Your people and Your inheritance… whom You rescued by Your great power"** — Moses' prayer in Deuteronomy 9: *do not destroy Your people and Your inheritance* (9:26); *they are Your people and Your inheritance, whom You brought out by Your great power* (9:29).
- **v20 "the spirit of Beliar… to accuse them before You"** — *Belḥor*. Paul uses the name: *What agreement has Christ with Beliar?* (2 Corinthians 6:15). In Revelation the one cast down is *the accuser of our brothers, who accuses them before our God day and night* (Revelation 12:10). *Create in them an upright spirit* is David's prayer, from the same verse as v21's *clean heart*: *create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me* (Psalm 51:10).
- **vv21, 23, 26, 29, the ends of the verses** — from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63). The EOTC text as held here breaks off before them.
- **v23 "I will circumcise the foreskin of their heart"** — the promise of Deuteronomy: *the LORD your God will circumcise your heart and the heart of your seed, to love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul* (Deuteronomy 30:6).
- **vv24–25 "I will be their Father, and they will be My children… children of the living God"** — the promise to David's son, *I will be his father, and he will be My son* (2 Samuel 7:14), and Paul's *I will be a Father to you, and you will be My sons and daughters* (2 Corinthians 6:18). *In the place where it was said to them, "You are not My people," it will be said to them, "Children of the living God"* (Hosea 1:10), which Paul quotes in Romans 9:26.
- **vv27–29 "the angel of the presence"** — *malʾaka gaṣṣ*, **the angel of the face**: the angel who stands before God's face. Isaiah: *the angel of His presence saved them* (Isaiah 63:9). God tells this angel to write the history for Moses (v27), and the rest of the book is that history. **"who went before the camps of Israel"** — the angel of the exodus: *the messenger of God who was going before the camp of Israel* (Exodus 14:19).
- **v28 "King on Mount Zion forever"** — Micah: *the LORD will reign over them on Mount Zion from now and forever* (Micah 4:7).
- **v29 "from the time of creation… from the day of the new creation"** — so the Ge'ez, and Charles gives it the same way. With v27, *from the beginning of creation until My sanctuary is built among them*, the tablets cover the whole span: from the first creation to the new one, when the heavens and the earth are renewed.

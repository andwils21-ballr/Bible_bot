# Jubilees 27

**1** The words of Esau her elder son were told to Rebekah in a dream; and Rebekah sent and called Jacob her younger son, and said to him,

**2** "Esau your brother is bearing a grudge against you, to kill you.

**3** Now, my son, listen to my voice: rise and flee to Laban my brother, to Harran, and stay with him a few days, until your brother's anger turns and he puts away his anger from you and forgets all that you have done to him; then I will send and bring you from there."

**4** Jacob said, "I am not afraid. If he wants to kill me, I will kill him."

**5** She said to him, "Let me not be bereaved of both my sons in one day."

**6** Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "You know that my father has grown old, and I see that his eyes are dim; and if I leave him, it will be evil in his eyes, because I leave him and go away from you; and my father will be angry and will curse me. I will not go; only when he sends me will I go."

**7** Rebekah said to Jacob, "I will go in and speak to him, and he will send you away."

**8** Rebekah went in and said to Isaac, "I loathe my life because of the two daughters of Heth whom Esau has taken as wives; and if Jacob takes a wife from the daughters of the land such as these, why should I live any longer? For the daughters of Canaan are evil."

**9** Isaac called Jacob his son, and blessed him and charged him, and said to him,

**10** "Do not take a wife for yourself from any of the daughters of Canaan. Rise, go to Mesopotamia, to the house of Bethuel, your mother's father, and take a wife for yourself from there, from the daughters of Laban, your mother's brother.

**11** May God Almighty bless you, and make you grow and multiply you, and may you become a company of nations; and may He give the blessings of my father Abraham to you and to your seed after you, so that you may inherit the land where you have lived as a guest, and all the land that the Lord gave to Abraham. Go, my son, in peace."

**12** Isaac sent Jacob away, and he went to Mesopotamia, to Laban the son of Bethuel the Aramean, the brother of Rebekah, Jacob's mother.

**13** After Jacob had set out to go to Mesopotamia, Rebekah's spirit grieved after her son, and she wept.

**14** Isaac said to Rebekah, "My sister, do not weep for my son, for he goes in peace, and in peace he will return.

**15** God Most High will keep him from all evil and will be with him, and will not forsake him all his days.

**16** For I know that his ways will prosper in everything, wherever he goes, until he returns to us in peace and we see him in peace.

**17** Do not fear for him, my sister, for he is upright in his way, and he is a whole man; he is faithful, and he will not perish. Do not weep."

**18** Isaac comforted Rebekah about Jacob her son, and he blessed him.

**19** Jacob went out from the Well of the Oath to go to Harran, in the first year of the second week of the forty-fourth jubilee, and he came to Luz, which is on the mountain, that is Bethel, on the new moon of the first month of this week. He came to the place at evening, and turned from the road to the west of the path that night, and slept there, for the sun had set.

**20** He took one of the stones of that place and set it under the tree, and he was traveling alone.

**21** He dreamed that night: a stairway was set up on the earth, and its top reached to heaven; the angels of God were going up and coming down on it, and the Lord was standing on it.

**22** He spoke with Jacob and said, "I am the God of Abraham your father, and the God of Isaac. The land on which you are sleeping — to you I will give it, and to your seed after you.

**23** Your seed will be like the sand of the earth, and you will increase to the west and the east and the north and the south; and in you and in your seed all the tribes of the earth will be blessed.

**24** I will be with you and keep you wherever you go, and I will bring you back to this land in peace; for I will not leave you until I have done all that I have told you."

**25** Jacob woke from his sleep and said, "Truly this place is the house of the Lord, and I did not know it." He was afraid and said, "How fearful is this place! It is nothing but the house of the Lord, and this is the gate of heaven."

**26** Jacob rose early in the morning, and took the stone he had set at his head, and set it up as a pillar for a sign, and poured oil on its top; and he called the name of that place Bethel, but at first the name of the land was Luz.

**27** Jacob prayed a prayer to the Lord, saying, "If the Lord is with me and keeps me on this way that I am going, and gives me bread to eat and clothing to wear, and I return to my father's house in peace, then the Lord will be my God; and this stone, which I have set up as a pillar for a sign in this place, will be the house of the Lord; and of all that You give me, I will give the tenth to You, my God."

## Notes

- **vv1, 6, 8, 15, 21, 23, 27** — the words the EOTC text as held here lacks are rendered from the Asmara printing of the Ge'ez (1962/63); the Latin, which covers this chapter from v11 to v23, has vv15, 21 and 23 too. **v21 "the Lord was standing on it"** — so the Asmara printing, *yəqawwəm*; the Latin has *resting*. Genesis 28:13: *Here, the LORD standing over him*.
- **vv1–18 Jacob sent away** — Genesis 27:42–28:5: *until your brother's anger turns from you, and he forgets what you have done to him. Then I will send and take you from there. Why should I be bereaved of both of you in one day?* (27:45). Jubilees adds Jacob's answer, *If he wants to kill me, I will kill him* (v4), and his refusal to leave his blind father unless his father sends him (v6). **v10 "Mesopotamia"** — so the Ge'ez; Genesis 28:2 has *Paddan-aram*. **v11 "God Almighty… a company of nations"** — Genesis 28:3–4: *May God Almighty bless you, and make you fruitful and multiply you, and you shall become an assembly of peoples. May He give you the blessing of Abraham.* **vv14–17 "My sister"** — Isaac's name for his wife; Genesis gives Isaac no words of comfort to Rebekah.
- **v19 the date** — the first year of the second week of the forty-fourth jubilee is 43 × 49 + 7 + 1 = 2115; the Latin has *the second year*. Jacob, born in 2046, is sixty-nine.
- **vv19–27 the stairway at Bethel** — Genesis 28:10–22: *here, a stairway set on the earth, and its head reaching to the heavens. Here, messengers of God going up and coming down on it… How fearful is this place! This is nothing but the house of God, and this is the gate of the heavens.* Jesus takes up the scene: *you will see heaven opened, and the angels of God going up and coming down on the Son of Man* (John 1:51). **v23 "the sand of the earth"** — so the Ge'ez and the Latin; Genesis 28:14 has *the dust of the earth*. **v25 "Jacob woke"** — the EOTC has *wa-noma*, "and he slept"; the English follows Charles and the sense. **v27 "I will give the tenth to You"** — the vow Jacob pays at Bethel in 32:2.

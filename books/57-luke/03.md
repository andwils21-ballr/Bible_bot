# Luke 3

## The Word Comes to John

**1** In the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar, when Pontius Pilate was governor of Judea, and Herod was tetrarch of Galilee, and his brother Philip tetrarch of the region of Ituraea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias tetrarch of Abilene,

**2** in the high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas, the word of God came to John the son of Zechariah in the wilderness.

**3** He went into all the region around the Jordan, preaching a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins,

**4** as it is written in the book of the words of Isaiah the prophet: "A voice of one crying out in the wilderness: 'Prepare the way of the Lord; make His paths straight.

**5** Every ravine shall be filled, and every mountain and hill shall be made low; the crooked shall become straight, and the rough places smooth roads;

**6** and all flesh shall see the salvation of God.'"

**7** So he said to the crowds who came out to be baptized by him, "Brood of vipers! Who warned you to flee from the wrath to come?

**8** Bear fruits worthy of repentance, then, and do not begin to say to yourselves, 'We have Abraham as our father.' For I tell you that God is able from these stones to raise up children for Abraham.

**9** The axe is already laid at the root of the trees. Every tree, then, that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire."

**10** The crowds asked him, "What then shall we do?"

**11** He answered them, "Whoever has two tunics, let him share with the one who has none; and whoever has food, let him do the same."

**12** Tax collectors also came to be baptized, and they said to him, "Teacher, what shall we do?"

**13** He said to them, "Collect no more than you have been ordered."

**14** Soldiers also asked him, "What about us? What shall we do?" He said to them, "Do not extort money from anyone by force or by false accusation, and be content with your wages."

**15** As the people were waiting, and all were wondering in their hearts about John, whether he might be the Messiah,

**16** John answered them all, "I baptize you with water; but one is coming who is stronger than I, the strap of whose sandals I am not worthy to untie. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire.

**17** His winnowing fork is in His hand, to clear His threshing floor and to gather the wheat into His barn; but the chaff He will burn with fire that cannot be put out."

**18** So with many other exhortations he preached the good news to the people.

**19** But Herod the tetrarch, who had been rebuked by him about Herodias, his brother's wife, and about all the evil things that Herod had done,

**20** added this to them all: he shut John up in prison.

## The Baptism of Jesus

**21** When all the people were being baptized, and Jesus also had been baptized and was praying, the heaven was opened,

**22** and the Holy Spirit came down on Him in bodily form, like a dove; and a voice came from heaven: "You are My Son, the beloved; in You I am well pleased."

## The Line of Jesus

**23** Jesus Himself was about thirty years old when He began. He was the son, as was supposed, of Joseph, the son of Heli,

**24** the son of Matthat, the son of Levi, the son of Melchi, the son of Jannai, the son of Joseph,

**25** the son of Mattathias, the son of Amos, the son of Nahum, the son of Esli, the son of Naggai,

**26** the son of Maath, the son of Mattathias, the son of Semein, the son of Josech, the son of Joda,

**27** the son of Joanan, the son of Rhesa, the son of Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel, the son of Neri,

**28** the son of Melchi, the son of Addi, the son of Cosam, the son of Elmadam, the son of Er,

**29** the son of Joshua, the son of Eliezer, the son of Jorim, the son of Matthat, the son of Levi,

**30** the son of Simeon, the son of Judah, the son of Joseph, the son of Jonam, the son of Eliakim,

**31** the son of Melea, the son of Menna, the son of Mattatha, the son of Nathan, the son of David,

**32** the son of Jesse, the son of Obed, the son of Boaz, the son of Salmon, the son of Nahshon,

**33** the son of Amminadab, the son of Admin, the son of Arni, the son of Hezron, the son of Perez, the son of Judah,

**34** the son of Jacob, the son of Isaac, the son of Abraham, the son of Terah, the son of Nahor,

**35** the son of Serug, the son of Reu, the son of Peleg, the son of Eber, the son of Shelah,

**36** the son of Cainan, the son of Arpachshad, the son of Shem, the son of Noah, the son of Lamech,

**37** the son of Methuselah, the son of Enoch, the son of Jared, the son of Mahalalel, the son of Kenan,

**38** the son of Enosh, the son of Seth, the son of Adam, the son of God.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "In the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar… the word of God came to John"** — Luke dates John's call by emperor, governor, tetrarchs and high priests, as the prophets dated theirs by kings, *the word of the LORD came to Zechariah son of Berechiah* (Zechariah 1:1). *Tetraarchēs*, **tetrarch**, word for word **ruler of a fourth**, a lesser ruler under Rome. Two high priests are named, *Annas and Caiaphas*, with a singular *high priesthood*.
- **vv4–6 "Every ravine shall be filled… and all flesh shall see the salvation of God"** — Luke quotes Isaiah 40 further than Matthew or Mark, to *all flesh shall see*, the Greek of *all flesh shall see it together* (Isaiah 40:5), where the Hebrew has *the glory of the LORD shall be revealed*. *To sōtērion*, **the salvation**, the word of Simeon's *my eyes have seen Your salvation* (2:30).
- **vv10–14 "What then shall we do?… Collect no more… be content with your wages"** — only Luke has John's answers to the crowds, the tax collectors and the soldiers. *Diaseisēte*, **extort by force**, word for word **shake down**; *sykophantēsēte*, **by false accusation**. *Opsōnia*, **wages**, a soldier's pay.
- **vv15–17 "whether he might be the Messiah… with the Holy Spirit and fire… fire that cannot be put out"** — nearly the words of Matthew 3:11–12. *Ptyon*, **winnowing fork**, the tool that throws grain into the wind to part it from the chaff.
- **vv19–20 "Herod the tetrarch… he shut John up in prison"** — Luke tells of John's imprisonment before the baptism of Jesus, and does not name John at the baptism (v21). *Prosethēken kai touto epi pasin*, **added this to them all**.
- **vv21–22 "Jesus also had been baptized and was praying… in bodily form, like a dove"** — only Luke says Jesus *was praying*; prayer comes at each turn in His story in this Gospel (5:16; 6:12). *Sōmatikō eidei*, **in bodily form**, only in Luke. The voice is the same as in Mark 1:11.
- **vv23–38 "about thirty years old… the son, as was supposed, of Joseph… the son of Adam, the son of God"** — *hōs enomizeto*, **as was supposed**: Luke has told of the virgin birth (1:34–35). Matthew's line runs down from Abraham through Solomon (Matthew 1:2–16); Luke's runs up to Adam through Nathan, another son of David (2 Samuel 5:14), and the names between David and Joseph differ. The names are given in the forms already used where the same man is meant: *Arpachshad*, *Shelah*, *Peleg*, *Reu*, *Serug* (Genesis 11:10–24), *Kenan*, *Mahalalel*, *Jared*, *Methuselah* (Genesis 5:9–25), *Nahshon*, *Salmon*, *Boaz*, *Obed* (Ruth 4:20–22). *Cainan* (v36), between Arpachshad and Shelah, is not in the Hebrew of Genesis 11 but is in its Greek, which has *Kainan* (Genesis 11:12). Where Matthew and Ruth have *Ram*, Luke has *Admin* and *Arni*. The list ends with God: Adam had no human father, and the One just called *My Son* (v22) is traced to Him.

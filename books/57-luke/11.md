# Luke 11

## The Prayer

**1** Once He was praying in a certain place, and when He had finished, one of His disciples said to Him, "Lord, teach us to pray, as John also taught his disciples."

**2** He said to them, "When you pray, say:

Father,  
hallowed be Your name.  
Your kingdom come.

**3** Give us each day our daily bread,

**4** and forgive us our sins,  
for we ourselves also forgive everyone who is indebted to us;  
and lead us not into temptation."

**5** He said to them, "Which of you who has a friend will go to him at midnight and say to him, 'Friend, lend me three loaves,

**6** for a friend of mine has arrived from a journey, and I have nothing to set before him';

**7** and he will answer from inside, 'Do not bother me; the door is already shut, and my children are with me in bed; I cannot get up and give you anything'?

**8** I tell you, even if he will not get up and give him anything because he is his friend, yet because of his shamelessness he will get up and give him whatever he needs.

**9** So I tell you, ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you.

**10** For everyone who asks receives, and the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks it will be opened.

**11** Which father among you, if his son asks for a fish, will give him a snake instead of a fish?

**12** Or if he asks for an egg, will give him a scorpion?

**13** If you, then, evil as you are, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will the Father from heaven give the Holy Spirit to those who ask Him!"

## The Finger of God

**14** He was driving out a demon that was mute. When the demon had gone out, the mute man spoke, and the crowds were amazed.

**15** But some of them said, "He drives out the demons by Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons."

**16** Others, to test Him, were demanding from Him a sign from heaven.

**17** But He knew their thoughts and said to them, "Every kingdom divided against itself is laid waste, and house falls on house.

**18** If Satan also is divided against himself, how will his kingdom stand? For you say that I drive out the demons by Beelzebul.

**19** If I drive out the demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your sons drive them out? So they will be your judges.

**20** But if it is by the finger of God that I drive out the demons, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.

**21** When a strong man, fully armed, guards his own courtyard, his possessions are safe;

**22** but when one stronger than he attacks him and overpowers him, he takes away the armor in which he trusted and divides his plunder.

**23** Whoever is not with Me is against Me, and whoever does not gather with Me scatters.

**24** "When the unclean spirit has gone out of a person, it passes through waterless places looking for rest; and finding none, it says, 'I will go back to my house that I came out of.'

**25** When it comes, it finds it swept and put in order.

**26** Then it goes and brings seven other spirits more evil than itself, and they go in and live there; and the last state of that person becomes worse than the first."

**27** As He was saying these things, a woman in the crowd raised her voice and said to Him, "Blessed is the womb that carried You and the breasts that nursed You!"

**28** But He said, "Rather, blessed are those who hear the word of God and keep it!"

## The Sign of Jonah

**29** As the crowds were increasing, He began to say, "This generation is an evil generation; it looks for a sign, but no sign will be given it except the sign of Jonah.

**30** For just as Jonah became a sign to the people of Nineveh, so the Son of Man will be to this generation.

**31** The queen of the South will rise at the judgment with the men of this generation and condemn them, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and something greater than Solomon is here.

**32** The men of Nineveh will rise up at the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because they repented at the preaching of Jonah; and something greater than Jonah is here.

**33** "No one lights a lamp and puts it in a cellar or under a basket, but on the lampstand, so that those who come in may see the light.

**34** The lamp of your body is your eye. When your eye is sound, your whole body is full of light; but when it is evil, your body is full of darkness.

**35** See to it, then, that the light in you is not darkness.

**36** If your whole body, then, is full of light, with no part of it dark, it will be wholly full of light, as when a lamp gives you light with its brightness."

## Woes to Pharisees and Lawyers

**37** While He was speaking, a Pharisee asked Him to dine with him; so He went in and reclined at table.

**38** The Pharisee was surprised to see that He did not first wash before the meal.

**39** But the Lord said to him, "Now you Pharisees clean the outside of the cup and the dish, but inside you are full of plunder and wickedness.

**40** Fools! Did not the One who made the outside make the inside also?

**41** But give as alms what is inside, and then everything is clean for you.

**42** "But woe to you Pharisees! For you tithe mint and rue and every herb, and pass over justice and the love of God. These you ought to have done, without neglecting the others.

**43** Woe to you Pharisees! For you love the best seat in the synagogues and greetings in the marketplaces.

**44** Woe to you! For you are like unmarked graves, and people walk over them without knowing it."

**45** One of the lawyers answered Him, "Teacher, in saying these things You insult us too."

**46** He said, "Woe to you lawyers also! For you load people with burdens hard to carry, and you yourselves do not touch the burdens with one of your fingers.

**47** Woe to you! For you build the tombs of the prophets, and it was your fathers who killed them.

**48** So you are witnesses, and you approve of the deeds of your fathers; for they killed them, and you build their tombs.

**49** For this reason also the Wisdom of God said, 'I will send them prophets and apostles, and some of them they will kill and persecute,'

**50** so that the blood of all the prophets, shed since the foundation of the world, may be required of this generation,

**51** from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zechariah, who perished between the altar and the house. Yes, I tell you, it will be required of this generation.

**52** Woe to you lawyers! For you have taken away the key of knowledge; you did not go in yourselves, and you hindered those who were going in."

**53** When He went out from there, the scribes and the Pharisees began to press Him hard and to question Him closely about many things,

**54** lying in wait for Him, to catch Him in something He might say.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "Lord, teach us to pray… Father, hallowed be Your name… our daily bread… forgive us our sins"** — Luke's prayer is shorter than Matthew's (Matthew 6:9–13): *Father*, without *Our… in heaven*; no *Your will be done*; no *but deliver us from evil*. *Ton epiousion*, **daily**, is found in the Bible only in this prayer (here and Matthew 6:11); it may be **for the coming day** or **needed for existence**. *Hamartias*, **sins**, where Matthew has *debts*, and then *everyone who is indebted to us*: Luke keeps the debt in the second line.
- **vv5–13 "at midnight… because of his shamelessness… how much more will the Father from heaven give the Holy Spirit"** — only Luke has the friend at midnight. *Anaideia*, **shamelessness**, the friend's boldness in asking. Matthew has *give good things* (Matthew 7:11); Luke has *give the Holy Spirit*.
- **vv14–23 "by the finger of God… one stronger than he… divides his plunder"** — *en daktylō theou*, **by the finger of God**, the words of Pharaoh's magicians, *This is the finger of God* (Exodus 8:19); Matthew has *by the Spirit of God* (Matthew 12:28). The stronger one is the one John promised, *stronger than I* (3:16).
- **vv27–28 "Blessed is the womb that carried You… Rather, blessed are those who hear the word of God and keep it"** — only Luke has this. *Menoun*, **Rather**, which both agrees and corrects. Mary herself is called blessed *who believed* (1:45) and *kept* His words (2:19, 51).
- **vv29–32 "the sign of Jonah… the queen of the South… the men of Nineveh"** — Luke has no *three days and three nights* (Matthew 12:40): Jonah himself and his preaching are the sign, as *the people of Nineveh believed in God* (Jonah 3:5). *The queen of the South*, the queen of Sheba, who *came to test him with hard questions* (1 Kings 10:1).
- **vv37–44 "He did not first wash… mint and rue… justice and the love of God… unmarked graves"** — *ebaptisthē*, **wash**, the word of baptism, a washing before meals (Mark 7:4). Matthew has *mint and dill and cumin… justice and mercy and faithfulness* (Matthew 23:23). Matthew's tombs are *whitewashed* (Matthew 23:27); Luke's are *unmarked*, so that people are made unclean by walking over them without knowing, as the law says whoever touches *a grave* is unclean (Numbers 19:16).
- **vv45–54 "the Wisdom of God said… from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zechariah… the key of knowledge"** — *hē sophia tou theou*, **the Wisdom of God**, speaking as a person, where Matthew has *I am sending you* (Matthew 23:34). Abel's blood cried *from the ground* (Genesis 4:10); Zechariah was stoned *in the court of the house of the LORD* (2 Chronicles 24:21). Matthew names him *son of Berechiah* (Matthew 23:35); Luke gives no father.

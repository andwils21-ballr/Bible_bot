# Luke 22

## Judas Agrees to Hand Him Over

**1** The Feast of Unleavened Bread, which is called the Passover, was drawing near.

**2** The chief priests and the scribes were looking for a way to put Him to death, for they were afraid of the people.

**3** Then Satan entered into Judas, called Iscariot, who was one of the twelve.

**4** He went away and talked with the chief priests and officers about how he might hand Him over to them.

**5** They were glad, and agreed to give him money.

**6** So he consented, and began looking for an opportunity to hand Him over to them away from the crowd.

## The Passover Prepared

**7** Then came the day of Unleavened Bread, on which the Passover lamb had to be sacrificed.

**8** Jesus sent Peter and John, saying, "Go and prepare the Passover for us, so that we may eat it."

**9** They said to Him, "Where do You want us to prepare it?"

**10** He said to them, "When you have entered the city, a man carrying a jar of water will meet you. Follow him into the house that he enters,

**11** and say to the master of the house, 'The Teacher says to you, Where is the guest room, where I may eat the Passover with My disciples?'

**12** He will show you a large upper room, furnished; prepare it there."

**13** They went and found it as He had told them, and they prepared the Passover.

## The Lord's Supper

**14** When the hour came, He reclined at table, and the apostles with Him.

**15** He said to them, "I have longed to eat this Passover with you before I suffer;

**16** for I tell you, I will not eat it until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God."

**17** He took a cup, and when He had given thanks, He said, "Take this and share it among yourselves;

**18** for I tell you, from now on I will not drink of the fruit of the vine until the kingdom of God comes."

**19** He took bread, and when He had given thanks, He broke it and gave it to them, saying, "This is My body, which is given for you; do this in remembrance of Me."

**20** In the same way He took the cup after supper, saying, "This cup is the new covenant in My blood, which is poured out for you.

**21** But the hand of the one who is handing Me over is with Me on the table.

**22** For the Son of Man goes as it has been determined; but woe to that man by whom He is handed over!"

**23** They began to question one another, which of them it could be who was going to do this.

## Who Is the Greatest?

**24** A dispute also arose among them as to which of them was to be regarded as the greatest.

**25** He said to them, "The kings of the Gentiles lord it over them, and those in authority over them are called benefactors.

**26** But not so with you; rather, let the greatest among you become as the youngest, and the leader as the one who serves.

**27** For who is greater, the one who reclines at table or the one who serves? Is it not the one who reclines at table? But I am among you as the one who serves.

**28** "You are the ones who have stood by Me in My trials;

**29** and I confer on you, as My Father conferred on Me, a kingdom,

**30** so that you may eat and drink at My table in My kingdom, and sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel.

## Peter's Denial Foretold

**31** "Simon, Simon, Satan has demanded to have you all, to sift you like wheat;

**32** but I have prayed for you that your faith may not fail. When you have turned back, strengthen your brothers."

**33** He said to Him, "Lord, I am ready to go with You both to prison and to death."

**34** He said, "I tell you, Peter, the rooster will not crow today until you have denied three times that you know Me."

**35** He said to them, "When I sent you out without purse and bag and sandals, did you lack anything?" They said, "Nothing."

**36** He said to them, "But now, whoever has a purse, let him take it, and likewise a bag; and whoever has none, let him sell his cloak and buy a sword.

**37** For I tell you that this Scripture must be fulfilled in Me: 'He was counted with the transgressors.' For what is written about Me is reaching its end."

**38** They said, "Lord, here are two swords." He said to them, "It is enough."

## On the Mount of Olives

**39** He came out and went, as was His custom, to the Mount of Olives, and the disciples followed Him.

**40** When He came to the place, He said to them, "Pray that you may not enter into temptation."

**41** He withdrew from them about a stone's throw, knelt down and prayed,

**42** saying, "Father, if You are willing, take this cup away from Me; yet not My will, but Yours be done."

**43** An angel from heaven appeared to Him, strengthening Him.

**44** Being in anguish, He prayed more earnestly; and His sweat became like great drops of blood falling down on the ground.

**45** When He rose from prayer and came to the disciples, He found them sleeping from grief,

**46** and He said to them, "Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray, so that you do not enter into temptation."

## The Arrest

**47** While He was still speaking, a crowd came, and the one called Judas, one of the twelve, was leading them. He came near to Jesus to kiss Him;

**48** but Jesus said to him, "Judas, are you handing over the Son of Man with a kiss?"

**49** When those around Him saw what was going to happen, they said, "Lord, shall we strike with the sword?"

**50** One of them struck the slave of the high priest and cut off his right ear.

**51** But Jesus answered, "No more of this!" He touched his ear and healed him.

**52** Then Jesus said to the chief priests and officers of the temple and elders who had come out against Him, "Have you come out with swords and clubs as if against a robber?

**53** When I was with you day after day in the temple, you did not stretch out your hands against Me. But this is your hour, and the power of darkness."

## Peter Denies Him

**54** They seized Him, led Him away and brought Him into the house of the high priest. Peter followed at a distance.

**55** When they had kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard and sat down together, Peter sat among them.

**56** A servant girl, seeing him as he sat in the light of the fire, looked closely at him and said, "This man was with Him too."

**57** But he denied it, saying, "Woman, I do not know Him."

**58** A little later someone else saw him and said, "You also are one of them." But Peter said, "Man, I am not."

**59** About an hour later another kept insisting, "Certainly this man also was with Him, for he is a Galilean."

**60** But Peter said, "Man, I do not know what you are talking about." At once, while he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.

**61** The Lord turned and looked at Peter. Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how He had said to him, "Before a rooster crows today, you will deny Me three times."

**62** He went outside and wept bitterly.

## Before the Council

**63** The men who were holding Jesus mocked Him and beat Him.

**64** They blindfolded Him and kept asking Him, "Prophesy! Who is it that struck You?"

**65** They said many other things against Him, blaspheming.

**66** When day came, the council of elders of the people gathered, both chief priests and scribes, and they led Him away to their Sanhedrin,

**67** saying, "If You are the Messiah, tell us." He said to them, "If I tell you, you will not believe;

**68** and if I ask you, you will not answer.

**69** But from now on the Son of Man will be seated at the right hand of the power of God."

**70** They all said, "Are You the Son of God, then?" He said to them, "You say that I am."

**71** They said, "What further need do we have of testimony? We have heard it ourselves from His own mouth."

## Notes

- **vv1–6 "Satan entered into Judas… officers… away from the crowd"** — the devil had left Jesus *until an opportune time* (4:13); *eukairia*, **opportunity**, is the word of that time come back. *Stratēgoi*, **officers**, the captains of the temple guard (v52). *Exōmologēsen*, **he consented**.
- **vv7–13 "the Passover lamb had to be sacrificed… a man carrying a jar of water… a large upper room"** — as Mark 14:12–16, but Jesus sends Peter and John. *To pascha*, **the Passover**, is both the feast and its lamb, killed on the fourteenth of the month (Exodus 12:6). *Kataluma*, **guest room**, the word of *no place for them in the guest room* at His birth (2:7).
- **vv14–18 "I have longed to eat this Passover with you… until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God… the fruit of the vine"** — *epithymia epethymēsa*, word for word **with longing I have longed**, a doubled verb. Only Luke has a cup before the bread (v17) as well as after (v20).
- **vv19–20 "This is My body, which is given for you; do this in remembrance of Me… This cup is the new covenant in My blood"** — the edition sets the words from *which is given for you* (v19) to the end of v20 in brackets, and marks them as a place where manuscripts differ. *The new covenant* is Jeremiah's, *I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel* (Jeremiah 31:31), and *the blood of the covenant* is Moses' (Exodus 24:8). *Anamnēsis*, **remembrance**: the Passover was itself *a memorial for you* (Exodus 12:14).
- **vv24–30 "lord it over them… benefactors… as the one who serves… I confer on you… a kingdom… judging the twelve tribes"** — *euergetai*, **benefactors**, a title kings took for themselves. *Diatithemai*, **I confer**, the verb of making a covenant or a will, from the same root as *diathēkē*, **covenant** (v20). Matthew has the twelve thrones elsewhere, *you… will also sit on twelve thrones* (Matthew 19:28).
- **vv31–34 "Simon, Simon, Satan has demanded to have you all, to sift you like wheat… strengthen your brothers"** — *hymas*, **you all**, is plural: Satan asks for all of them; Jesus prays for Simon, *sou*, singular. As Satan asked for Job, *All that he has is in your hand* (Job 1:12). *Siniasai*, **to sift**, as Amos, *as one shakes with a sieve* (Amos 9:9). *Epistrepsas*, **when you have turned back**, the word of repentance.
- **vv35–38 "without purse and bag and sandals… buy a sword… He was counted with the transgressors… It is enough"** — Jesus recalls 10:4, *Carry no purse, no bag, no sandals*. Isaiah 53:12, *was counted with the transgressors*; the Greek of Isaiah and Luke both have *anomoi*, **the lawless**. *Hikanon estin*, **It is enough**, may close the talk rather than count the swords.
- **vv39–53 "Father, if You are willing, take this cup away from Me… An angel from heaven appeared to Him… like great drops of blood"** — Mark has Gethsemane and *Abba, Father* (Mark 14:32, 36); Luke has neither. The edition marks vv43–44 as a place where manuscripts differ. *Agōnia*, **anguish**, found only here in the New Testament. *Thromboi*, **great drops**, clots or thick drops; the sweat is *like* blood. Only Luke says they slept *from grief*. At the arrest (vv47–53), only Luke has the right ear, the healing, and *the power of darkness*. *Eate heōs toutou*, **No more of this!**, word for word **let it be as far as this**.
- **vv54–62 "Woman, I do not know Him… a rooster crowed… The Lord turned and looked at Peter… wept bitterly"** — as Mark 14:66–72, with one crowing; only Luke has *The Lord turned and looked at Peter*. *He went outside and wept bitterly*, word for word as Matthew 26:75.
- **vv63–71 "Prophesy! Who is it that struck You?… the right hand of the power of God… You say that I am"** — as Matthew 26:68. Here the trial is at daybreak (v66); Mark has it at night (Mark 14:53). *The right hand of the power of God*, where Mark has *of the Power*, and Psalm 110:1, *Sit at My right hand*. *Hymeis legete hoti egō eimi*, **You say that I am**: either *You say it, and I am*, or *You say it, not I*.

# Luke 23

## Before Pilate and Herod

**1** Then the whole company of them rose and led Him to Pilate.

**2** They began to accuse Him, saying, "We found this man misleading our nation, forbidding us to pay tribute to Caesar, and saying that He Himself is Messiah, a king."

**3** Pilate asked Him, "Are You the King of the Jews?" He answered him, "You say so."

**4** Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, "I find no guilt in this man."

**5** But they kept insisting, "He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee even to this place."

**6** When Pilate heard this, he asked whether the man was a Galilean.

**7** When he learned that He belonged to Herod's jurisdiction, he sent Him to Herod, who was himself in Jerusalem in those days.

**8** When Herod saw Jesus, he was very glad, for he had long wanted to see Him, because he had heard about Him, and he was hoping to see some sign done by Him.

**9** He questioned Him at some length, but He gave him no answer.

**10** The chief priests and the scribes stood there, accusing Him vehemently.

**11** Herod with his soldiers treated Him with contempt and mocked Him; he put a splendid robe on Him and sent Him back to Pilate.

**12** Herod and Pilate became friends with each other that very day, for before this they had been enemies.

**13** Pilate called together the chief priests and the rulers and the people,

**14** and said to them, "You brought me this man as one who was turning the people away. I have examined Him before you and found no guilt in this man in any of the things you charge Him with.

**15** Neither has Herod, for he sent Him back to us. He has done nothing deserving death.

**16** So I will have Him whipped and release Him."

**18** But they all shouted out together, "Away with this man! Release Barabbas for us!"

**19** He had been thrown into prison for an uprising that had taken place in the city, and for murder.

**20** Pilate called out to them again, wanting to release Jesus,

**21** but they kept shouting, "Crucify, crucify Him!"

**22** A third time he said to them, "Why, what evil has this man done? I have found in Him no guilt deserving death. So I will have Him whipped and release Him."

**23** But they pressed him with loud cries, demanding that He be crucified, and their cries prevailed.

**24** So Pilate decided that their demand should be granted.

**25** He released the man who had been thrown into prison for uprising and murder, whom they asked for; but Jesus he handed over to their will.

## The Way to the Cross

**26** As they led Him away, they seized one Simon of Cyrene, who was coming in from the country, and laid the cross on him, to carry it behind Jesus.

**27** A great crowd of the people followed Him, and of women who were beating their breasts and wailing for Him.

**28** But Jesus turned to them and said, "Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for Me, but weep for yourselves and for your children.

**29** For the days are coming when they will say, 'Blessed are the barren, the wombs that never bore and the breasts that never nursed!'

**30** Then they will begin to say to the mountains, 'Fall on us,' and to the hills, 'Cover us.'

**31** For if they do these things when the wood is green, what will happen when it is dry?"

**32** Two others also, criminals, were led away with Him to be put to death.

## The Crucifixion

**33** When they came to the place that is called The Skull, there they crucified Him and the criminals, one on the right and one on the left.

**34** Jesus said, "Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing." They divided His clothes, casting lots.

**35** The people stood by, watching. The rulers also sneered at Him, saying, "He saved others; let Him save Himself, if this is the Messiah of God, the Chosen One!"

**36** The soldiers also mocked Him, coming up and offering Him sour wine,

**37** and saying, "If You are the King of the Jews, save Yourself!"

**38** There was also an inscription over Him: "This is the King of the Jews."

**39** One of the criminals who were hanged there blasphemed Him, saying, "Are You not the Messiah? Save Yourself and us!"

**40** But the other answered, rebuking him, "Do you not even fear God, since you are under the same sentence?

**41** We indeed justly, for we are receiving what our deeds deserve; but this man has done nothing wrong."

**42** He said, "Jesus, remember me when You come in Your kingdom."

**43** He said to him, "Truly I tell you, today you will be with Me in paradise."

**44** It was now about the sixth hour, and darkness came over all the land until the ninth hour,

**45** while the sun's light failed; and the curtain of the sanctuary was torn in two.

**46** Jesus called out with a loud voice and said, "Father, into Your hands I commit My spirit." Having said this, He breathed His last.

**47** When the centurion saw what had happened, he glorified God, saying, "Certainly this man was righteous."

**48** All the crowds who had gathered for this sight, when they saw what had happened, returned home beating their breasts.

**49** All His acquaintances, and the women who had followed Him from Galilee, stood at a distance, watching these things.

## The Burial

**50** There was a man named Joseph, a member of the council, a good and righteous man —

**51** he had not agreed to their plan and action — from Arimathea, a town of the Jews, who was waiting for the kingdom of God.

**52** This man went to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus.

**53** He took it down, wrapped it in a linen cloth and laid Him in a tomb cut in the rock, where no one had ever yet been laid.

**54** It was the day of Preparation, and the sabbath was beginning.

**55** The women who had come with Him from Galilee followed, and saw the tomb and how His body was laid.

**56** Then they returned and prepared spices and ointments. On the sabbath they rested according to the commandment.

## Notes

- **vv13–25 "I will have Him whipped and release Him… Away with this man!… Crucify, crucify Him!… handed over to their will"** — *paideusas*, **whipped**, word for word **having disciplined**. v17 is not in the edition, so the numbering here skips from 16 to 18. Barabbas was held *for an uprising… and for murder*, the very charge brought against Jesus, *He stirs up the people* (v5).
- **vv33–34 "The Skull… Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing… casting lots"** — *Kranion*, **The Skull**, Luke's Greek for the Aramaic *Golgotha* (Mark 15:22). *They divide my garments among them, and for my clothing they cast lots* (Psalm 22:18). The edition marks the prayer of v34 as a place where manuscripts differ. He is *counted with the transgressors… and intercedes for the transgressors* (Isaiah 53:12).
- **vv35–43 "He saved others; let Him save Himself… sour wine… remember me when You come in Your kingdom… today you will be with Me in paradise"** — three times *save Yourself* (vv35, 37, 39), from rulers, soldiers and a criminal. *Oxos*, **sour wine**, as *for my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink* (Psalm 69:21). Only Luke has the second criminal. *Paradeisos*, **paradise**, a Persian word for a garden, the word the Greek of Genesis uses for the garden God planted in Eden (Genesis 2:8).
- **vv44–49 "the sun's light failed… the curtain of the sanctuary was torn… into Your hands I commit My spirit… Certainly this man was righteous"** — Amos saw *the sun go down at noon* (Amos 8:9). The curtain is the *veil* that divided *the Holy Place and the Holy of Holies* (Exodus 26:33). The last words are Psalm 31:5, *Into Your hand I commit my spirit*, with *Father* before them; the Greek of the Psalm has *hands*. Mark's centurion says *the Son of God* (Mark 15:39); Luke's says *dikaios*, **righteous**, innocent.

# Luke 17

## Stumbling, Forgiveness and Faith

**1** He said to His disciples, "Causes of stumbling are sure to come, but woe to the one through whom they come!

**2** It would be better for him to have a millstone hung around his neck and to be thrown into the sea than for him to cause one of these little ones to stumble.

**3** Watch yourselves. If your brother sins, rebuke him; and if he repents, forgive him.

**4** If he sins against you seven times in a day, and seven times turns back to you, saying, 'I repent,' you must forgive him."

**5** The apostles said to the Lord, "Increase our faith!"

**6** The Lord said, "If you had faith like a mustard seed, you could say to this mulberry tree, 'Be uprooted and be planted in the sea,' and it would obey you.

**7** "Which of you, who has a slave plowing or tending sheep, will say to him when he comes in from the field, 'Come at once and recline at table'?

**8** Will he not rather say to him, 'Prepare something for me to eat, and fasten your belt and serve me while I eat and drink; and after that you may eat and drink'?

**9** Does he thank the slave because he did what he was commanded?

**10** So you too, when you have done all that you were commanded, say, 'We are unworthy slaves; we have only done what we ought to have done.'"

## The Ten Lepers

**11** On the way to Jerusalem, He was passing between Samaria and Galilee.

**12** As He entered a village, ten men with leprosy met Him. They stood at a distance

**13** and raised their voices, saying, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!"

**14** When He saw them, He said to them, "Go and show yourselves to the priests." As they went, they were made clean.

**15** One of them, when he saw that he was healed, turned back, glorifying God with a loud voice;

**16** he fell on his face at His feet, giving Him thanks. He was a Samaritan.

**17** Jesus answered, "Were not ten made clean? Where are the nine?

**18** Was no one found to turn back and give glory to God except this foreigner?"

**19** He said to him, "Get up and go; your faith has made you well."

## The Coming of the Kingdom

**20** Being asked by the Pharisees when the kingdom of God was coming, He answered them, "The kingdom of God is not coming in a way that can be watched for;

**21** nor will they say, 'Here it is!' or 'There!' For the kingdom of God is in your midst."

**22** He said to the disciples, "Days are coming when you will long to see one of the days of the Son of Man, and you will not see it.

**23** They will say to you, 'There!' or 'Here!' Do not go off or chase after them.

**24** For as the lightning flashes and lights up the sky from one side to the other, so will the Son of Man be in His day.

**25** But first He must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation.

**26** As it was in the days of Noah, so will it be also in the days of the Son of Man.

**27** They were eating, drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day Noah entered the ark, and the flood came and destroyed them all.

**28** It was the same as in the days of Lot: they were eating, drinking, buying, selling, planting, building;

**29** but on the day Lot went out from Sodom, fire and sulfur rained from heaven and destroyed them all.

**30** It will be just the same on the day the Son of Man is revealed.

**31** On that day, let the one on the housetop, whose goods are in the house, not go down to take them; and likewise let the one in the field not turn back.

**32** Remember Lot's wife.

**33** Whoever seeks to keep his life will lose it, but whoever loses it will preserve it alive.

**34** I tell you, on that night there will be two in one bed; one will be taken and the other left.

**35** There will be two women grinding together; one will be taken and the other left."

**37** They answered Him, "Where, Lord?" He said to them, "Where the body is, there the vultures will gather."

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "Causes of stumbling are sure to come… a millstone hung around his neck"** — as Matthew 18:6–7 and Mark 9:42. *Skandala*, **causes of stumbling**, traps or snares that make one fall. *Lithos mylikos*, **a millstone**, where Matthew and Mark have the great stone turned by a donkey.
- **vv3–4 "If your brother sins, rebuke him… seven times in a day"** — Matthew has the brother shown his fault *between you and him alone* (Matthew 18:15), and Peter's question, *As many as seven times?* (Matthew 18:21). The law says *You shall be sure to correct your neighbor, and not carry guilt because of him* (Leviticus 19:17).
- **vv5–6 "Increase our faith… faith like a mustard seed… this mulberry tree"** — Matthew has a mountain moved (Matthew 17:20). *Sykaminos*, **mulberry tree**, the black mulberry.
- **vv7–10 "fasten your belt and serve me… We are unworthy slaves"** — *perizōsamenos*, **fasten your belt**, tucking up the robe to work. Jesus turns it about in 12:37, where the master *will fasten his belt… and come and serve them*. *Achreioi*, **unworthy**, word for word **of no profit**: the slave has earned no thanks for doing his duty.
- **vv11–14 "between Samaria and Galilee… They stood at a distance… show yourselves to the priests"** — the law kept those with leprosy apart, *He shall live alone; his home shall be outside the camp* (Leviticus 13:46), and sent the one healed to the priest, *He shall be brought to the priest* (Leviticus 14:2). Jesus sent one man so in 5:14; here they are sent before they are healed. *Epistata*, **Master**, the word Luke alone uses, as Simon in 5:5.
- **vv15–19 "He was a Samaritan… this foreigner… your faith has made you well"** — *allogenēs*, **foreigner**, word for word **of another race**, the word on the stone notice of the temple that warned foreigners out of the inner court. As in Jesus' word about *Naaman the Syrian* (4:27), and the Samaritan of 10:33, the outsider is the one who turns back. *Sesōken se*, **has made you well**, word for word **has saved you**, as 7:50 and 8:48.
- **vv20–21 "not coming in a way that can be watched for… the kingdom of God is in your midst"** — *meta paratērēseōs*, **in a way that can be watched for**, with signs to be observed. *Entos hymōn* may be **in your midst** or **within you**. Jesus is speaking to the Pharisees.
- **vv22–30 "as the lightning flashes… in the days of Noah… in the days of Lot… fire and sulfur rained from heaven"** — as Matthew 24:27, 37–39. The days of Lot are only in Luke: *The LORD rained upon Sodom and upon Gomorrah sulfur and fire* (Genesis 19:24). Both times the people go on with their days, *eating, drinking*, until the end comes. *Dei auton polla pathein*, **He must suffer many things**, as in 9:22.
- **vv31–33 "on the housetop… Remember Lot's wife… will preserve it alive"** — Mark and Matthew have the housetop and the field in the teaching on the Mount of Olives (Mark 13:15–16; Matthew 24:17–18). *His wife looked back from behind him, and she became a pillar of salt* (Genesis 19:26). *Zōogonēsei*, **will preserve it alive**, as in 9:24 with another verb.
- **vv34–37 "two in one bed… one will be taken and the other left… Where the body is, there the vultures will gather"** — as Matthew 24:40–41, 28. v36 is not in the edition, so the numbering here skips from 35 to 37. *Aetoi* may be **eagles** or **vultures**; birds that gather at a body are vultures, as in Job, *where the slain are, there he is* (Job 39:30).

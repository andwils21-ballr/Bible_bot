# Sirach 16

**1** Do not desire a multitude of worthless children,  
nor rejoice in ungodly sons.

**2** If they multiply, do not rejoice in them,  
unless the fear of the Lord is with them.

**3** Do not trust in their life,  
and do not rely on their standing;  
for one is better than a thousand,  
and to die childless is better than to have ungodly children.

**4** For through one man of understanding a city will be peopled,  
but a tribe of lawless men will be laid waste.

**5** Many such things my eyes have seen,  
and my ear has heard things stronger than these.

**6** In an assembly of sinners a fire will be kindled,  
and against a disobedient nation wrath has blazed up.

**7** He did not forgive the giants of old,  
who rebelled in their strength.

**8** He did not spare the neighbors among whom Lot lived as a guest,  
whom He abhorred for their pride.

**9** He showed no pity to a nation doomed to destruction,  
who were taken away in their sins;

**10** so it was with the six hundred thousand on foot  
who gathered together in the hardness of their hearts.

**11** Even if there is only one stiff-necked man,  
it would be a wonder if he went unpunished.  
For mercy and wrath are with Him;  
He is mighty to forgive, and He pours out wrath.

**12** As great as His mercy, so great also is His reproof;  
He judges a man according to his works.

**13** The sinner will not escape with his plunder,  
and the patience of the godly will not go unrewarded.

**14** He will make room for every act of charity;  
each will find according to his works.

**17** Do not say, "I will hide from the Lord;  
who from on high will remember me?  
Among so many people I will not be noticed;  
for what is my soul in a boundless creation?

**18** The heaven, and the highest heaven of God,  
the abyss and the earth will shake when He visits them;

**19** the mountains and the foundations of the earth together  
tremble and quake when He looks upon them;

**20** but no heart will think about these things,  
and who will consider His ways?

**21** There is a storm that no man will see,  
and most of His works are hidden.

**22** Who will announce works of justice?  
Or who will wait for them? For the covenant is far off."

**23** These are the thoughts of one who lacks understanding;  
a foolish man who has gone astray thinks foolish things.

**24** Listen to me, my child, and gain knowledge,  
and set your heart on my words.

**25** I will set out discipline by weight,  
and declare knowledge with precision.

**26** When the Lord made His works in the beginning by His judgment,  
from their making He set apart their portions.

**27** He set His works in order forever,  
and their dominions for their generations;  
they neither hunger nor grow weary,  
and they do not leave off from their tasks.

**28** None of them crowds its neighbor,  
and they never disobey His word.

**29** After this the Lord looked upon the earth,  
and filled it with His good things.

**30** With every kind of living thing He covered its face,  
and into it they return.

## Notes

- **v3 "one is better than a thousand"** — one righteous child outweighs a crowd of ungodly ones.
- **v6 "In an assembly of sinners a fire will be kindled"** — as fire came out from the LORD on Korah's company (Numbers 16:35; Psalm 106:18, *Fire blazed among their company*).
- **vv7–10** — four examples: the giants before the Flood (Genesis 6:4; Wisdom 14:6); Sodom, where Lot lived as a guest (Genesis 19), abhorred *for their pride*; the nations of Canaan; and the *six hundred thousand on foot* who came out of Egypt and died in the wilderness (Numbers 11:21; 14:22–35).
- **v11 "mercy and wrath are with Him"** — the words of 5:6.
- **v12 "He judges a man according to his works"** — Psalm 62:12, *You repay a man according to his work*; Romans 2:6.
- **vv15–16** — Swete's Greek gives these verse numbers and no words (see the note on 1:5).
- **vv17–22** — the doubter's own words: God is too high to notice one soul, and His judgment too far off to fear. "The covenant is far off" — *diathēkē*, the decree of judgment, as in 14:12. Verse 23 answers him.
- **v18 "the heaven, and the highest heaven of God"** — as Solomon prayed, *The heavens, even the highest heavens, cannot contain You* (1 Kings 8:27).
- **vv26–30** — the order of creation in Genesis 1: the lights in their courses, then the earth filled and its living things, who return to it (Psalm 104:29).

# Sirach 1

**1** All wisdom is from the Lord,  
and with Him it remains forever.

**2** The sand of the seas, the drops of rain,  
and the days of eternity — who can count them?

**3** The height of heaven, the breadth of the earth,  
the abyss, and wisdom — who can search them out?

**4** Wisdom was created before all things,  
and prudent understanding from eternity.

**5** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**6** The root of wisdom — to whom has it been revealed?  
Her subtle ways — who knows them?

**7** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**8** There is One who is wise, greatly to be feared,  
seated upon His throne: the Lord.

**9** He Himself created her;  
He saw her and numbered her,  
and poured her out upon all His works.

**10** She is with all flesh according to His giving,  
and He has supplied her to those who love Him.

**11** The fear of the Lord is glory and exultation,  
and gladness and a crown of rejoicing.

**12** The fear of the Lord delights the heart,  
and gives gladness and joy and long life.

**13** With him who fears the Lord it will go well at the end;  
on the day of his death he will find favor.

**14** To fear God is the beginning of wisdom;  
she was created with the faithful in the womb.

**15** She has made her nest among men, an everlasting foundation,  
and among their descendants she will be trusted.

**16** To fear the Lord is the fullness of wisdom;  
she makes men drunk with her fruits;

**17** she fills her whole house with desirable things,  
and her storehouses with her produce.

**18** The fear of the Lord is the crown of wisdom,  
making peace and perfect health flourish.

**19** He saw her and numbered her;  
He rained down knowledge and discerning understanding,  
and He exalted the glory of those who hold her fast.

**20** To fear the Lord is the root of wisdom,  
and her branches are long life.

**21** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**22** Unrighteous anger cannot be justified,  
for the sway of his anger is his downfall.

**23** A patient man will hold out until the right time,  
and afterward joy will spring up for him.

**24** He will hide his words until the right time,  
and the lips of the faithful will tell of his understanding.

**25** In the treasuries of wisdom are wise sayings,  
but godliness is detestable to a sinner.

**26** If you desire wisdom, keep the commandments,  
and the Lord will supply her to you.

**27** For the fear of the Lord is wisdom and discipline,  
and faithfulness and meekness are His delight.

**28** Do not disobey the fear of the Lord,  
and do not approach Him with a divided heart.

**29** Do not play the hypocrite before men,  
and keep watch over your lips.

**30** Do not exalt yourself, or you may fall  
and bring dishonor on yourself;  
the Lord will reveal your secrets  
and cast you down before the whole assembly,  
because you did not come in the fear of the Lord,  
and your heart was full of deceit.

## Notes

- **v1 "All wisdom is from the Lord"** — rendered from the Greek (Swete), the form in which the book was handed down; the author names himself in 50:27, Jesus son of Sirach of Jerusalem. Verse numbers follow the English.
- **vv5, 7, 21** — Swete's Greek gives these verse numbers and no words: some English Bibles have lines here from other copies. The numbers are kept so that the numbering matches, and the lines are left out.
- **v4 "Wisdom was created before all things"** — the Greek of Proverbs 8:22 uses the same verb, *The Lord created (ektisen) me as the beginning of His ways*, where the Hebrew has *got me*.
- **vv14, 16, 18, 20 "the beginning… the fullness… the crown… the root of wisdom"** — four sayings on the fear of the Lord, building on Proverbs 1:7 and 9:10.
- **v16 "she makes men drunk with her fruits"** — *methyskei*: wisdom's feast, as in Proverbs 9:5, *drink of the wine I have mixed*.
- **v25 "godliness is detestable to a sinner"** — *bdelygma*, as in Reproof 5:27 (Proverbs 29:27), *one whose way is upright is detestable to the wicked*.
- **v28 "with a divided heart"** — *kardia dissē*, "a double heart": compare James 1:8, *a double-minded man, unstable in all his ways*.

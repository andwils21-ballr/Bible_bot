# Sirach 6

**1** and do not become an enemy instead of a friend;  
for a bad name inherits shame and reproach;  
so does the double-tongued sinner.

**2** Do not exalt yourself in the plans of your soul,  
or your soul will be torn in pieces like a bull;

**3** you will devour your leaves and destroy your fruit,  
and will be left like a dry tree.

**4** An evil soul destroys the one who has it,  
and makes him the laughingstock of his enemies.

**5** A sweet voice multiplies friends,  
and a gracious tongue multiplies kind greetings.

**6** Let those who are at peace with you be many,  
but let your counselors be one in a thousand.

**7** If you would gain a friend, gain him by testing him,  
and do not be quick to trust him.

**8** For there is a friend when it suits him,  
who will not stand by you in the day of your trouble.

**9** There is a friend who turns into an enemy,  
and will bring a quarrel into the open to your shame.

**10** There is a friend who is a companion at table,  
who will not stand by you in the day of your trouble.

**11** In your prosperity he will be like your own self,  
and will speak boldly to your servants;

**12** but if you are brought low, he will turn against you,  
and will hide himself from your presence.

**13** Keep yourself apart from your enemies,  
and be on guard with your friends.

**14** A faithful friend is a strong shelter;  
whoever has found one has found a treasure.

**15** Nothing can be given in exchange for a faithful friend,  
and no scale can weigh his worth.

**16** A faithful friend is a medicine of life,  
and those who fear the Lord will find him.

**17** Whoever fears the Lord directs his friendship rightly,  
for as he is, so is his neighbor.

**18** My child, from your youth choose discipline,  
and until your hair is gray you will keep finding wisdom.

**19** Come to her like one who plows and sows,  
and wait for her good fruits;  
for in working for her you will toil a little,  
and soon you will eat of her produce.

**20** How very swift she is with the undisciplined!  
The senseless man will not stay with her;

**21** she will lie on him like a heavy testing stone,  
and he will not be slow to cast her off.

**22** For wisdom is like her name,  
and she is not plain to many.

**23** Listen, my child, and accept my judgment,  
and do not reject my counsel.

**24** Put your feet into her fetters,  
and your neck into her collar.

**25** Put your shoulder under her and carry her,  
and do not chafe at her bonds.

**26** Come to her with all your soul,  
and keep her ways with all your might.

**27** Search out and seek, and she will become known to you;  
and when you have hold of her, do not let her go.

**28** For at last you will find the rest she gives,  
and she will be turned into joy for you.

**29** Then her fetters will become for you a strong shelter,  
and her branches a glorious robe.

**30** For there is a golden ornament on her,  
and her bonds are a cord of blue.

**31** You will put her on as a glorious robe,  
and set her on your head as a crown of rejoicing.

**32** If you are willing, my child, you will be taught,  
and if you give your soul to it, you will become shrewd.

**33** If you love to listen, you will gain knowledge,  
and if you incline your ear, you will be wise.

**34** Stand in the company of the elders;  
who is wise? Cling to him.

**35** Be willing to listen to every godly discourse,  
and do not let wise proverbs escape you.

**36** If you see a man of understanding, rise early to go to him;  
let your foot wear out his doorstep.

**37** Reflect on the statutes of the Lord,  
and meditate on His commandments at all times.  
It is He who will strengthen your heart,  
and your desire for wisdom will be granted.

## Notes

- **v2 "your soul will be torn in pieces like a bull"** — the Greek line is obscure: pride tears a man apart as a bull is torn, and leaves him (v3) a dry tree with its leaves eaten.
- **vv5–17** — on friendship: the many and the one counselor (v6); a friend tested before he is trusted (v7); three false friends, for his own occasion, the turncoat, and the friend at table (vv8–12); the faithful friend (vv14–16). Compare Proverbs 18:24, *there is a friend who sticks closer than a brother*.
- **v16 "a medicine of life"** — *pharmakon zōēs*: a friend who keeps one alive.
- **v18 "choose discipline"** — the Greek held here spells it *paidia*, as in 4:17; the sense is *paideia*, "discipline."
- **v20 "How very swift she is with the undisciplined"** — *tacheia*, "swift," as Swete prints it: wisdom is quickly gone from those who will not be taught. *Tracheia*, "rough," would differ by one letter.
- **v22 "wisdom is like her name"** — the play on words behind this line does not show in the Greek; what is clear is the second half: she is not plain to many.
- **vv24–31 "fetters… collar… bonds… a cord of blue"** — the marks of a slave become a robe and a crown. "A cord of blue" — *klōsma hyakinthinon*, the words of the Greek of Numbers 15:38, the blue cord on the tassels that reminded Israel of the commandments.
- **v29 "her branches"** — *kladoi*, as Swete prints it; v24 has *kloios*, "collar," and the likeliest sense is that her collar becomes a glorious robe.
- **v37 "meditate on His commandments at all times"** — Psalm 1:2, *on His law he meditates day and night*.

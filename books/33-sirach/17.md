# Sirach 17

**1** The Lord created man out of earth,  
and turned him back to it again.

**2** He gave them a fixed number of days and a time,  
and gave them authority over the things on the earth.

**3** He clothed them with strength fit for them,  
and made them in His own image.

**4** He put the fear of them upon all flesh,  
and gave them dominion over beasts and birds.

**5** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**6** Counsel and tongue and eyes,  
ears and a heart for thinking, He gave them.

**7** He filled them with knowledge and understanding,  
and showed them good and evil.

**8** He set His eye upon their hearts,  
to show them the majesty of His works,

**9** so that they might tell of the majesty of His works,

**10** and praise His holy name.

**11** He gave them knowledge besides,  
and gave them the law of life as an inheritance.

**12** He established an everlasting covenant with them,  
and showed them His judgments.

**13** Their eyes saw the majesty of His glory,  
and their ears heard the glory of their voice.

**14** He said to them, "Beware of all unrighteousness,"  
and He gave each of them a command concerning his neighbor.

**15** Their ways are always before Him;  
they will not be hidden from His eyes.

**16** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**17** For every nation He appointed a ruler,  
but Israel is the Lord's portion.

**18** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**19** All their works are as clear as the sun before Him,  
and His eyes are always on their ways.

**20** Their iniquities are not hidden from Him,  
and all their sins are before the Lord.

**21** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**22** A man's charity is like a signet ring with Him,  
and He will keep a man's kindness like the apple of His eye.

**23** Afterward He will rise up and repay them,  
and bring their recompense on their own heads.

**24** Yet to those who repent He grants a way back,  
and He encourages those whose endurance is failing.

**25** Turn back to the Lord and forsake your sins;  
pray before His face and lessen your offense.

**26** Return to the Most High and turn away from iniquity,  
and utterly hate what is detestable.

**27** Who will sing praise to the Most High in Hades,  
in place of the living who give thanks?

**28** From the dead, as from one who does not exist, thanksgiving has ceased;  
he who is alive and well will praise the Lord.

**29** How great is the mercy of the Lord,  
and His forgiveness for those who turn to Him!

**30** For not everything is possible for men,  
since a son of man is not immortal.

**31** What is brighter than the sun? Yet it fails;  
and an evil man will set his mind on flesh and blood.

**32** He watches over the host of the heights of heaven,  
but all men are earth and ashes.

## Notes

- **vv5, 16, 18, 21** — Swete's Greek gives these verse numbers and no words (see the note on 1:5).
- **v13 "the glory of their voice"** — so Swete's Greek (*phōnēs autōn*); the voice heard at Sinai was God's, and "His voice" is what the line means.
- **v17 "For every nation He appointed a ruler, but Israel is the Lord's portion"** — Deuteronomy 32:8–9: the Greek there has the nations bounded *according to the number of the angels of God*, and *the LORD's portion is His people*.

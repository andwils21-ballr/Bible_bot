# Sirach 23

**1** Lord, Father and Master of my life,  
do not abandon me to their counsel;  
do not let me fall by them.

**2** Who will set whips over my thoughts,  
and the discipline of wisdom over my heart,  
so that they may not spare me for my errors,  
and my sins may not pass unnoticed,

**3** so that my errors may not multiply,  
and my sins abound,  
and I fall before my adversaries,  
and my enemy rejoice over me?

**4** Lord, Father and God of my life,  
do not give me haughty eyes,

**5** and turn desire away from me;

**6** let neither gluttony nor lust take hold of me,  
and do not give me over to a shameless soul.

**7** *Discipline of the mouth.*  
Listen, children, to the discipline of the mouth;  
whoever keeps it will never be caught.  
By his own lips

**8** the sinner will be overtaken,  
the reviler and the proud will stumble by them.

**9** Do not accustom your mouth to oaths,  
nor make a habit of naming the Holy One.

**10** For as a servant who is constantly examined  
will not be without bruises,  
so also the man who always swears and names the Holy One  
will not be cleansed from sin.

**11** A man who swears many oaths will be filled with lawlessness,  
and the scourge will not leave his house;  
if he offends, his sin remains on him,  
and if he disregards it, he has sinned twice;  
if he has sworn in vain, he will not be justified,  
for his house will be filled with calamities.

**12** There is a way of speaking that is clothed in death;  
may it never be found in the inheritance of Jacob!  
For all these things will be far from the godly,  
and they will not wallow in sins.

**13** Do not accustom your mouth to coarse and foul talk,  
for there is sinful speech in it.

**14** Remember your father and mother  
when you sit among great men,  
or you may forget yourself before them,  
and through habit behave like a fool;  
then you will wish you had never been born,  
and you will curse the day of your birth.

**15** A man accustomed to abusive words  
will never be disciplined all his days.

**16** Two kinds of men multiply sins,  
and a third brings on wrath:  
a hot soul like a blazing fire,  
which will not be quenched until it is consumed;  
a man who commits fornication in the body of his flesh,  
who will not stop until the fire burns him up.

**17** To the man who commits fornication all bread is sweet;  
he will not give up until he dies.

**18** A man who strays from his marriage bed  
says to himself, "Who sees me?  
Darkness is around me, and the walls hide me,  
and no one sees me. Why should I fear?  
The Most High will not remember my sins."

**19** His fear is the eyes of men,  
and he does not know that the eyes of the Lord  
are ten thousand times brighter than the sun,  
looking upon all the ways of men  
and seeing into hidden places.

**20** Before all things were created they were known to Him,  
and so it is after they were finished.

**21** This man will be punished in the streets of the city,  
and where he least suspects it, he will be seized.

**22** So too the woman who leaves her husband  
and provides an heir by a stranger.

**23** For first, she has disobeyed the law of the Most High;  
second, she has offended against her husband;  
and third, she has committed adultery through fornication  
and brought forth children by another man.

**24** She will be brought out before the assembly,  
and her children will be called to account.

**25** Her children will not spread their roots,  
and her branches will bear no fruit.

**26** She will leave her memory as a curse,  
and her disgrace will not be blotted out.

**27** Those who are left will know  
that nothing is better than the fear of the Lord,  
and nothing sweeter than heeding the commandments of the Lord.

## Notes

- **vv1–6 "Lord, Father and Master of my life"** — the prayer begun in 22:27: a guard on the mouth, then on the eyes and the appetites. God addressed as Father, as in Isaiah 63:16, *You, LORD, are our Father*.
- **v7 "Discipline of the mouth."** — a heading written into the Greek text, as at 18:30 and 20:26.
- **vv9–11 "Do not accustom your mouth to oaths"** — the third commandment, *You shall not take the name of the LORD your God in vain* (Exodus 20:7); compare Jesus, *Do not swear at all* (Matthew 5:34).
- **v12 "a way of speaking that is clothed in death"** — blasphemy, for which the Law set the penalty of death (Leviticus 24:16).
- **v14 "you will curse the day of your birth"** — as Job did (Job 3:1–3).
- **v16 "Two kinds of men… and a third"** — the counting pattern of the sayings in Reproof 6 (Proverbs 30).
- **v17 "To the man who commits fornication all bread is sweet"** — compare Proverbs 9:17, *Stolen water is sweet, and bread eaten in secret is pleasant*.
- **v18 "Who sees me? Darkness is around me"** — Job 24:15, *The eye of the adulterer also waits for the twilight, saying, "No eye will see me"*.
- **v19 "the eyes of the Lord are ten thousand times brighter than the sun"** — compare Proverbs 15:3, *The eyes of the LORD are in every place*.

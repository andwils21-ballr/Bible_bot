# Sirach 47

**1** After him Nathan rose up  
to prophesy in the days of David.

**2** As the fat is set apart from the peace offering,  
so was David from the sons of Israel.

**3** He played with lions as with young goats,  
and with bears as with lambs of the flock.

**4** In his youth did he not kill a giant,  
and take away the people's disgrace,  
when he lifted his hand with the stone in the sling  
and brought down the pride of Goliath?

**5** For he called on the Lord, the Most High,  
and He gave strength to his right hand  
to remove a man of war,  
and to lift up the horn of his people.

**6** So he was glorified for his ten thousands,  
and praised for the blessings of the Lord,  
when the crown of glory was brought to him.

**7** For he wiped out the enemies on every side,  
and brought to nothing the Philistines, his adversaries;  
to this day he has broken their horn.

**8** In every work he gave thanks  
to the Holy One, the Most High, with words of glory;  
with all his heart he sang hymns,  
and loved the One who made him.

**9** He set singers before the altar,  
to make sweet melody with their voices;

**10** he gave splendor to the feasts,  
and set the seasons in perfect order,  
while they praised His holy name,  
and from early morning the sanctuary resounded.

**11** The Lord took away his sins,  
and exalted his horn forever;  
He gave him a covenant of kings,  
and a throne of glory in Israel.

**12** After him rose up a wise son,  
who lived in security because of him.

**13** Solomon reigned in days of peace,  
and God gave him rest on every side,  
so that he might build a house for His name,  
and prepare a sanctuary forever.

**14** How wise you were in your youth!  
You were filled with understanding like a river.

**15** Your soul covered the earth,  
and you filled it with parables and riddles.

**16** Your name reached far-off islands,  
and you were loved for your peace.

**17** For your songs and proverbs and parables,  
and for your interpretations, the countries marveled at you.

**18** In the name of the Lord God,  
who is called the God of Israel,  
you gathered gold like tin,  
and piled up silver like lead.

**19** You laid your loins beside women,  
and gave them power over your body.

**20** You put a blemish on your glory,  
and defiled your offspring,  
bringing wrath on your children;  
and I was grieved at your folly,

**21** that the monarchy was split in two,  
and a rebellious kingdom arose out of Ephraim.

**22** But the Lord will never give up His mercy,  
nor let any of His works perish;  
He will never blot out the descendants of His chosen one,  
nor destroy the offspring of the one who loved Him.  
He gave a remnant to Jacob,  
and to David a root from him.

**23** Solomon rested with his fathers,  
and left behind him one of his offspring,  
the folly of the people, lacking in understanding:  
Rehoboam, who turned the people away by his counsel;  
and Jeroboam son of Nebat, who made Israel sin,  
and gave Ephraim a way of sin.

**24** Their sins grew exceedingly many,  
so that they were removed from their land.

**25** They sought out every kind of wickedness,  
until vengeance came upon them.

## Notes

- **v2 "As the fat is set apart from the peace offering"** — the fat was the LORD's portion of every peace offering, *All fat belongs to the LORD* (Leviticus 3:16): David is the part of Israel set apart for God.
- **vv3–5 "He played with lions… a man of war"** — David's own account to Saul, *When a lion came, or a bear* (1 Samuel 17:34), and Saul's warning, *he has been a man of war from his youth* (17:33). "Take away the people's disgrace" — 17:26, *takes away the disgrace from Israel*.
- **v5 "to lift up the horn of his people"** — Hannah's song, *He gives strength to His king and lifts high the horn of His anointed* (1 Samuel 2:10).
- **v6 "glorified for his ten thousands"** — the women's song, *Saul has struck down his thousands, and David his ten thousands* (1 Samuel 18:7).
- **v11 "The Lord took away his sins"** — Nathan's word to David, *The LORD also has put away your sin* (2 Samuel 12:13).
- **v13 "Solomon reigned in days of peace"** — his name is built on *shalom*: *Solomon shall be his name, and I will give peace and quiet to Israel in his days* (1 Chronicles 22:9).
- **v16 "Your name reached far-off islands"** — *eis nēsous*, "to islands," the word that, with one letter changed, stands as *Iēsous* in Swete's 43:23.
- **vv19–20 "You laid your loins beside women… I was grieved at your folly"** — *his wives turned his heart away after other gods* (1 Kings 11:4). *Katenygēn*, "I was pierced": the writer speaks his own grief.
- **v22 "and to David a root from him"** — *rhizan*. Revelation calls Christ *the Lion from the tribe of Judah, the Root of David* (*hē rhiza Dauid*, 5:5).
- **v23 "Jeroboam son of Nebat, who made Israel sin"** — the refrain of Kings, *the sins of Jeroboam, which he sinned and which he made Israel to sin* (1 Kings 14:16).

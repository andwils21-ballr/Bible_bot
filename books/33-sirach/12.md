# Sirach 12

**1** If you do a kindness, know to whom you do it,  
and you will be thanked for your good deeds.

**2** Do good to a godly man, and you will be repaid —  
if not by him, then by the Most High.

**3** No good comes to one who persists in evil,  
or to one who does not give charity.

**4** Give to the godly man,  
but do not help the sinner.

**5** Do good to the humble, but do not give to the ungodly;  
hold back his bread and do not give it to him,  
or by it he may overpower you;  
for you will receive twice as much evil  
for all the good you do him.

**6** For the Most High also hates sinners,  
and will repay the ungodly with punishment.

**7** Give to the good man,  
but do not help the sinner.

**8** A friend is not proved in prosperity,  
and an enemy will not stay hidden in adversity.

**9** When a man prospers, his enemies are grieved,  
and in his adversity even his friend will part from him.

**10** Never trust your enemy,  
for as bronze corrodes, so does his wickedness.

**11** Even if he humbles himself and goes about bowed down,  
take heed to yourself and be on guard against him;  
you will be to him like one who has polished a mirror,  
and you will know that his rust has not been wiped away for good.

**12** Do not stand him beside you,  
or he may overturn you and take your place;  
do not seat him at your right hand,  
or he may seek your seat,  
and in the end you will understand my words,  
and be stung by what I said.

**13** Who will pity a snake charmer who is bitten,  
or anyone who goes near wild beasts?

**14** So no one will pity a man who keeps company with a sinner  
and becomes caught up in his sins.

**15** He will stay with you for a while,  
but if you falter, he will not stand by you.

**16** An enemy speaks sweetly with his lips,  
but in his heart he plans to throw you into a pit;  
an enemy will weep with his eyes,  
but if he finds an opportunity, he will never have his fill of blood.

**17** If misfortune meets you, you will find him there before you,  
and while seeming to help, he will trip your heel.

**18** He will shake his head and clap his hands,  
and whisper much, and change his expression.

## Notes

- **vv4–7 "do not give to the ungodly; hold back his bread"** — counsel of caution, and hard beside Reproof 1:21 (Proverbs 25:21), *If your enemy is hungry, give him bread to eat*, which Paul quotes (Romans 12:20), and Jesus' *love your enemies, and do good* (Luke 6:35). Sirach speaks of feeding a man who will use the gift against you; the Proverb, of answering hatred with bread.
- **v8 "A friend is not proved in prosperity"** — *ouk ekdikēthēsetai*, "will not be vindicated": good times do not show who is a true friend; bad times show who is an enemy.
- **vv10–11 "as bronze corrodes… like one who has polished a mirror"** — mirrors were of bronze: wiping one clears it for a moment, but the tarnish returns. The enemy's humility is a polish.
- **v13 "a snake charmer who is bitten"** — as in Ecclesiastes 10:11, *there is no gain for the charmer* when the snake bites first.
- **v16 "An enemy speaks sweetly with his lips"** — Psalm 55:21, *His speech was smoother than butter, but war was in his heart*.
- **v17 "he will trip your heel"** — compare Psalm 41:9, *who ate my bread, has lifted up his heel against me*.
- **v18 "He will shake his head and clap his hands"** — the gestures of mockery at another's ruin (Lamentations 2:15).

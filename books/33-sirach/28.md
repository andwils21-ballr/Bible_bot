# Sirach 28

**1** Whoever takes vengeance will meet vengeance from the Lord,  
and He will keep strict account of his sins.

**2** Forgive your neighbor the wrong he has done,  
and then, when you pray, your sins will be forgiven.

**3** Does a man harbor anger against another,  
and yet seek healing from the Lord?

**4** Does he show no mercy to a man like himself,  
and yet pray about his own sins?

**5** If he, being flesh, keeps up his wrath,  
who will make atonement for his sins?

**6** Remember the end of your life, and stop hating;  
remember decay and death, and keep to the commandments.

**7** Remember the commandments, and do not be angry with your neighbor;  
remember the covenant of the Most High, and overlook ignorance.

**8** Keep away from strife, and you will lessen sins;  
for a hot-tempered man kindles strife,

**9** and a sinful man stirs up trouble among friends,  
and sows slander among those at peace.

**10** As the fuel is, so the fire will burn;  
as a man's strength is, so will his anger be;  
as his wealth is, so will his wrath rise;  
and as the strife is stubborn, so will it blaze.

**11** A hasty quarrel kindles a fire,  
and urgent strife sheds blood.

**12** If you blow on a spark, it will blaze;  
if you spit on it, it will be put out;  
and both come out of your mouth.

**13** Curse the whisperer and the double-tongued,  
for they have destroyed many who were at peace.

**14** A third tongue has shaken many,  
and scattered them from nation to nation;  
it has torn down strong cities,  
and overturned the houses of great men.

**15** A third tongue has driven out capable wives,  
and robbed them of the fruit of their labors.

**16** Whoever listens to it will find no rest,  
nor will he settle down in peace.

**17** The blow of a whip raises a welt,  
but the blow of the tongue breaks bones.

**18** Many have fallen by the sword,  
but not so many as have fallen because of the tongue.

**19** Happy is the man who is sheltered from it,  
who has not gone through its wrath,  
who has not borne its yoke,  
and has not been bound with its fetters;

**20** for its yoke is a yoke of iron,  
and its fetters are fetters of bronze;

**21** its death is an evil death,  
and Hades is better than it.

**22** It will not rule over the godly,  
and they will not be burned in its flame.

**23** Those who forsake the Lord will fall into its power;  
it will burn among them and not be put out;  
it will be sent out against them like a lion,  
and like a leopard it will tear them.

**24** Fence your property with thorns;  
lock up your silver and gold;

**25** make a balance and a weight for your words,  
and a door and a bolt for your mouth.

**26** Take care not to slip by it,  
or you may fall before one who lies in wait.

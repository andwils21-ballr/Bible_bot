# Sirach 36

**1** Have mercy on us, Master, God of all,  
and look upon us,

**2** and send the fear of You on all the nations.

**3** Lift up Your hand against foreign nations,  
and let them see Your might.

**4** As before their eyes You have shown Yourself holy among us,  
so before our eyes show Yourself great among them;

**5** let them know You, as we too have known You,  
that there is no God but You, Lord.

**6** Renew signs and work other wonders;  
glorify Your hand and Your right arm.

**7** Rouse anger and pour out wrath;  
remove the adversary and wipe out the enemy.

**8** Hasten the time and remember the oath,  
and let them tell of Your mighty deeds.

**9** In fiery wrath let whoever escapes be consumed,  
and may those who harm Your people find destruction.

**10** Crush the heads of the enemy rulers,  
who say, "There is no one but us."

**11** Gather all the tribes of Jacob,  
and give them their inheritance as at the beginning.

**12** Have mercy, Lord, on the people called by Your name,  
on Israel, whom You likened to a firstborn.

**13** Have pity on the city of Your sanctuary,  
Jerusalem, the city of Your rest.

**14** Fill Zion with the telling of Your mighty deeds,  
and Your people with Your glory.

**15** Bear witness to those You created in the beginning,  
and bring to pass the prophecies spoken in Your name.

**16** Reward those who wait for You,  
and let Your prophets be found trustworthy.

**17** Hear, Lord, the prayer of those who plead with You,  
according to Aaron's blessing over Your people,  
and all who are on the earth will know  
that You are the Lord, the God of the ages.

**18** The stomach will eat any food,  
yet one food is better than another.

**19** As the palate tastes game,  
so an understanding heart tastes lying words.

**20** A crooked heart causes grief,  
but a man of experience will pay him back.

**21** A woman will accept any man,  
but one daughter is better than another.

**22** A woman's beauty brightens the face,  
and surpasses every desire of a man.

**23** If there is mercy and gentleness on her tongue,  
her husband is not like other men.

**24** Whoever gains a wife makes a beginning of possessions,  
a helper fit for him and a pillar to rest on.

**25** Where there is no fence, property is plundered,  
and where there is no wife, a man wanders and groans.

**26** Who will trust a nimble robber  
who darts from city to city?  
So it is with a man who has no nest,  
and lodges wherever night overtakes him.

## Notes

- **vv1–26** — vv1–10 and the first words of v11 are Swete's 33:1–13, divided into verses differently; the rest of v11 is the end of his 36:16, and vv12–26 are his 36:17–31 (see the note on 30:25).

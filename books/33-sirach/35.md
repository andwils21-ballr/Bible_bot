# Sirach 35

**1** Whoever keeps the law makes many offerings;  
whoever pays attention to the commandments sacrifices a peace offering.

**2** Whoever repays a kindness offers fine flour,  
and whoever gives charity sacrifices a thank offering.

**3** To turn away from evil is what pleases the Lord,  
and to turn away from injustice is atonement.

**4** Do not appear before the Lord empty-handed,

**5** for all these things are done because of the commandment.

**6** The offering of the righteous enriches the altar,  
and its fragrance is before the Most High.

**7** The sacrifice of a righteous man is acceptable,  
and its memorial will not be forgotten.

**8** Glorify the Lord with a good eye,  
and do not stint the firstfruits of your hands.

**9** In every gift show a cheerful face,  
and set apart your tithe with gladness.

**10** Give to the Most High as He has given to you,  
and with a good eye, as much as your hand has found.

**11** For the Lord is One who repays,  
and He will repay you sevenfold.

**12** Do not offer bribes, for He will not accept them,  
and do not rely on an unjust sacrifice;  
for the Lord is the judge,  
and with Him there is no partiality.

**13** He will not show partiality against a poor man,  
and He will listen to the prayer of one who is wronged.

**14** He will not ignore the plea of the orphan,  
or the widow when she pours out her complaint.

**15** Do not a widow's tears run down her cheek,  
and is not her cry against the one who made them fall?

**16** Whoever serves willingly will be accepted,  
and his prayer will reach the clouds.

**17** The prayer of the humble passes through the clouds,  
and until it draws near it will not be comforted;  
it will not depart until the Most High looks upon it,  
and judges justly, and executes judgment.

**18** The Lord will not delay,  
nor will He be patient with them,  
until He crushes the loins of the merciless  
and repays the nations with vengeance;  
until He removes the multitude of the insolent  
and breaks the scepters of the unjust;

**19** until He repays each man according to his deeds,  
and the works of men according to their intentions;  
until He judges the cause of His people  
and makes them glad in His mercy.

**20** Mercy is welcome in the time of his affliction,  
like rain clouds in a time of drought.

## Notes

- **vv1–20** — Swete's 32:1–26. The English joins pairs of his verses in vv1, 2, 12, 15 and 19, and divides his 32:21–23 between vv17 and 18; see the note on 30:25.
- **vv1–2 "a peace offering… fine flour… a thank offering"** — *thysiazōn sōtēriou… semidalin… thysiazōn aineseōs*, the offerings of Leviticus by their Greek names (3:1; 2:2; 7:12): keeping the law, repaying a kindness and giving charity are each counted as one. Psalm 50:14, *Offer to God a sacrifice of thanksgiving*, has the same words in its Greek, *thysian aineseōs*.
- **v4 "Do not appear before the Lord empty-handed"** — *mē ophthēs… kenos*, the command of Exodus 23:15 (*ouk ophthēsē… kenos*), *They shall not appear before My face empty-handed*.
- **vv6–7 "its fragrance… its memorial"** — *euōdia* and *mnēmosynon*, the words of the grain offering in Leviticus 2:2, where the priest burns *its memorial portion… a soothing aroma*. What rises to God from the righteous man is remembered.
- **vv8–10 "with a good eye"** — *en agathō ophthalmō*, the generous eye, against the *evil eye* of the greedy guest (31:13).
- **v12 "Do not offer bribes… with Him there is no partiality"** — the two things the LORD does not do in Deuteronomy 10:17, *who shows no partiality and takes no bribe*.
- **vv14–15 "the widow… is not her cry against the one who made them fall?"** — Exodus 22:23, *if he cries out to Me, I will surely hear his cry*.
- **vv17–18 "it will not depart… nor will He be patient with them"** — *oude mē makrothymēsei ep' autois*. Jesus tells of a widow who would not stop coming to a judge, and asks, *Will not God bring about justice for His chosen ones who cry out to Him day and night? Will He delay long over them?* — the same words, *makrothymei ep' autois* (Luke 18:7).
- **v20 "Mercy is welcome in the time of his affliction"** — *hōraion*, from *hōra*, "season": mercy that comes in its season, like rain in a dry year.

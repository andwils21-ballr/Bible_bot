# Sirach 43

**1** The pride of the heights is the pure firmament,  
the appearance of heaven in a vision of glory.

**2** The sun, when it appears, proclaims as it comes out:  
a marvelous instrument, the work of the Most High.

**3** At its noon it dries up the land,  
and who can stand before its heat?

**4** A man tending a furnace works in the heat,  
but the sun burns the mountains three times as much;  
it breathes out fiery vapors,  
and its shining rays dim the eyes.

**5** Great is the Lord who made it,  
and at His words it hurries on its course.

**6** The moon too keeps to its season in everything,  
marking the times and a sign for the age.

**7** From the moon comes the sign of the feast,  
a light that wanes as it reaches its end.

**8** The month is named after her,  
growing marvelously as she changes,  
a vessel of parables on high,  
shining in the firmament of heaven.

**9** The beauty of heaven, the glory of the stars,  
a shining ornament: in the heights is the Lord.

**10** At holy words they stand as decreed,  
and never grow faint at their watches.

**11** Look at the rainbow and bless the One who made it;  
it is very beautiful in its brightness.

**12** It rings the sky with a circle of glory;  
the hands of the Most High have stretched it out.

**13** By His command He lays down the snow,  
and speeds the lightnings of His judgment.

**14** Therefore the storehouses are opened,  
and clouds fly out like birds.

**15** In His majesty He makes the clouds strong,  
and the hailstones are broken in pieces.

**16** At His appearing the mountains will shake;  
at His will the south wind blows.

**17** The voice of His thunder rebukes the earth,  
and so do the storm from the north and the whirlwind.  
Like birds alighting He scatters the snow,  
and its coming down is like locusts settling.

**18** The eye marvels at the beauty of its whiteness,  
and the heart is amazed at its falling.

**19** He pours frost over the earth like salt,  
and when it freezes it becomes points of thorns.

**20** The cold north wind blows,  
and ice freezes over the water;  
it settles on every gathering of water,  
and the water puts it on like a breastplate.

**21** It devours the mountains and burns up the wilderness,  
and withers the green growth like fire.

**22** A quick healing for all is a mist;  
a dew that comes after the heat brings refreshment.

**23** By His counsel He calmed the deep,  
and Joshua planted it.

**24** Those who sail the sea tell of its dangers,  
and we marvel at what our ears hear;

**25** for there are strange and marvelous works,  
every kind of living thing, and the race of sea monsters.

**26** Through Him its end is success,  
and by His word all things hold together.

**27** We could say much and never reach the end;  
the sum of our words is: He is the all.

**28** Where shall we find strength to glorify Him?  
For He is the Great One, above all His works.

**29** The Lord is awesome and very great,  
and His power is marvelous.

**30** When you glorify the Lord, exalt Him as much as you can,  
for He will surpass even that;  
and when you exalt Him, put forth all your strength;  
do not grow weary, for you will never reach the end.

**31** Who has seen Him and can describe Him?  
Who can magnify Him as He is?

**32** Many things greater than these are hidden,  
for we have seen few of His works.

**33** For the Lord has made all things,  
and to the godly He has given wisdom.

## Notes

- **v1 "the pure firmament"** — *stereōma kathariotētos*, "a firmament of purity": the word of the Greek of Exodus 24:10, where under God's feet was *something like… the very body of the sky for purity* (*tē kathariotēti*).
- **vv2–5 "The sun… proclaims as it comes out"** — Psalm 19, *The heavens declare the glory of God*, and the sun *comes out like a bridegroom from his chamber* (vv1, 5).
- **vv6–8 "a sign for the age… the sign of the feast"** — the moon set *for signs, and for appointed times* (Genesis 1:14); Psalm 104:19, *He made the moon to mark the seasons*. Psalm 81:3, *Blow the ram's horn at the new moon, at the full moon, on our feast day*.
- **v8 "a vessel of parables on high"** — *skeuos parabolōn*, as Swete prints it. With one syllable more, *parembolōn*, it is "a vessel of the camps," a beacon for the hosts of heaven.
- **vv11–12 "Look at the rainbow"** — *toxon*, the *bow* of Genesis 9:13 (Greek *to toxon mou*), *My bow I have set in the cloud, and it shall be for a sign of covenant*.
- **v23 "and Joshua planted it"** — *kai ephyteusen autēn Iēsous*, as Swete prints it: *Iēsous* is Joshua's name in Greek (46:1), and the name Jesus. With one letter changed, *nēsous*, the line reads "and planted islands in it," which fits the calmed deep.
- **v26 "Through Him its end is success"** — Swete prints *euōdia*, "fragrance"; *euodia*, with *o* for *ō*, is "a good journey, success," which the line needs. "By His word all things hold together" — *synkeitai*; Paul says of Christ, *in Him all things hold together* (*ta panta en autō synestēken*, Colossians 1:17).
- **v27 "He is the all"** — *to pan estin autos*: the sum of all that can be said of creation is Him. The next line keeps Him *above all His works* (v28).
- **vv27, 30 "never reach the end"** — *ou mē aphikōmetha… ou gar mē aphikēsthe*: Psalm 145:3, *His greatness is unsearchable*.
- **v31 "Who has seen Him and can describe Him?"** — John 1:18, *No one has ever seen God* (*theon oudeis heōraken pōpote*).

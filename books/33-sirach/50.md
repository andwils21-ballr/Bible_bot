# Sirach 50

**1** Simon son of Onias, the great priest,  
who in his life repaired the house,  
and in his days made the temple strong.

**2** By him the high double wall was founded,  
the high retaining wall of the temple enclosure.

**3** In his days the reservoir for water was made less,  
a basin of bronze, like the sea in its compass.

**4** He took thought for his people, to save them from falling,  
and strengthened the city against siege.

**5** How glorious he was as the people gathered round him,  
when he came out from behind the curtain of the house!

**6** Like the morning star among the clouds,  
like the full moon in its days,

**7** like the sun shining on the temple of the Most High,  
like the rainbow gleaming in clouds of glory,

**8** like roses in bloom in the days of spring,  
like lilies by streams of water,  
like a green shoot of Lebanon in the days of summer,

**9** like fire and incense in the censer,  
like a vessel of beaten gold  
adorned with every kind of precious stone,

**10** like an olive tree bursting with fruit,  
like a cypress towering into the clouds —

**11** when he put on his glorious robe,  
and clothed himself with the perfection of glory,  
going up to the holy altar,  
he made the court of the sanctuary glorious.

**12** When he received the portions from the hands of the priests,  
standing by the hearth of the altar,  
with a crown of brothers around him,  
like young cedars in Lebanon,  
they surrounded him like the trunks of palm trees,

**13** all the sons of Aaron in their glory,  
with the Lord's offering in their hands,  
before the whole assembly of Israel.

**14** Finishing the service at the altars,  
to adorn the offering of the Most High, the Almighty,

**15** he stretched out his hand to the cup,  
and poured out the blood of the grape;  
he poured it out at the foot of the altar,  
a soothing aroma to the Most High, the King of all.

**16** Then the sons of Aaron shouted;  
they sounded the trumpets of beaten metal,  
and made a great sound heard,  
as a reminder before the Most High.

**17** Then all the people together hurried,  
and fell on their faces to the ground  
to worship their Lord,  
the Almighty, God Most High.

**18** The singers praised with their voices;  
in the full house the melody was sweet.

**19** The people pleaded with the Lord Most High  
in prayer before the Merciful One,  
until the order of the Lord's worship was complete,  
and they had finished His service.

**20** Then he came down and lifted up his hands  
over the whole assembly of the sons of Israel,  
to give the blessing of the Lord with his lips,  
and to glory in His name;

**21** and they bowed down in worship a second time,  
to receive the blessing from the Most High.

**22** Now bless the God of all,  
who does great things everywhere,  
who exalts our days from the womb,  
and deals with us according to His mercy.

**23** May He give you gladness of heart,  
and may there be peace in our days in Israel,  
as in the days of old.

**24** May He entrust His mercy to us,  
and in His days may He redeem us.

**25** Two nations my soul detests,  
and the third is no nation:

**26** those who live on the mountain of Samaria,  
the Philistines,  
and the foolish people who live in Shechem.

**27** Instruction in understanding and knowledge  
I have written in this book,  
I, Jesus son of Sirach, son of Eleazar, of Jerusalem,  
who poured out wisdom from his heart like rain.

**28** Happy is he who spends his time with these things;  
whoever lays them to heart will become wise.

**29** For if he does them, he will be strong for everything,  
for the light of the Lord is his path.

## Notes

- **v1 "Simon son of Onias, the great priest"** — the last of the fathers praised: the hymn that began with *Let us now praise glorious men* (44:1) ends with a high priest at the altar.
- **v3 "the reservoir for water was made less"** — *ēlattōthē*, "was made less," as Swete prints it, in a line that praises his building works.
- **v5 "when he came out from behind the curtain of the house"** — *en exodō oikou katapetasmatos*: the high priest went behind the veil once a year, on the Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16:2, *he must not come at just any time into the holy place inside the veil*).
- **v11 "his glorious robe… the perfection of glory"** — *stolēn doxēs… synteleian kauchēmatos*, the words used of Aaron's vestments (45:7–8): Simon wears what Aaron wore.
- **v15 "the blood of the grape… a soothing aroma"** — the drink offering, poured at the altar's base; "the blood of the grape" as in 39:26. *Osmēn euōdias* is the Greek of the formula *a soothing aroma*.
- **v16 "the trumpets… as a reminder before the Most High"** — Numbers 10:10, *you shall blow the trumpets over your burnt offerings… and they shall be a remembrance for you before your God*.
- **vv20–21 "to give the blessing of the Lord with his lips, and to glory in His name"** — the blessing of Aaron, *So they shall put My name on the sons of Israel, and I will bless them* (Numbers 6:24–27).
- **vv25–26 "Two nations… and the third is no nation"** — a counting saying (as 25:1–2; 26:5, 28): the people of the mountain of Samaria, the Philistines, and *the foolish people who live in Shechem*.
- **v27 "I, Jesus son of Sirach, son of Eleazar, of Jerusalem"** — the writer signs the book. *Iēsous* is Joshua's name (46:1) and the name Jesus; the English has long called the book by his father's name, Sirach. "Poured out wisdom… like rain" — *anōmbrēsen*, the word used of the scribe in 39:6, *he will rain down words of wisdom*.

# Sirach 4

**1** My child, do not cheat the poor of his living,  
and do not keep needy eyes waiting.

**2** Do not grieve a hungry soul,  
nor anger a man in his want.

**3** Do not add to the troubles of an angry heart,  
nor delay your gift to one in need.

**4** Do not reject a suppliant in his distress,  
nor turn your face away from the poor.

**5** Do not turn your eye away from the needy,  
nor give a man cause to curse you;

**6** for if he curses you in the bitterness of his soul,  
his Maker will hear his prayer.

**7** Make yourself loved in the assembly,  
and bow your head to a great man.

**8** Incline your ear to the poor,  
and answer him peaceably and with meekness.

**9** Rescue the wronged from the hand of the wrongdoer,  
and do not be fainthearted in giving judgment.

**10** Be like a father to orphans,  
and like a husband to their mother;  
you will then be like a son of the Most High,  
and He will love you more than your mother does.

**11** Wisdom exalts her sons,  
and takes hold of those who seek her.

**12** Whoever loves her loves life,  
and those who rise early for her will be filled with joy.

**13** Whoever holds her fast will inherit glory,  
and wherever he goes, the Lord will bless.

**14** Those who serve her minister to the Holy One,  
and the Lord loves those who love her.

**15** Whoever obeys her will judge the nations,  
and whoever comes to her will dwell secure.

**16** If you trust her, you will inherit her,  
and your descendants will keep possession of her.

**17** For at first she walks with him on winding ways;  
she brings fear and dread upon him,  
and torments him with her discipline  
until she trusts his soul,  
and tests him with her ordinances;

**18** then she comes straight back to him and gladdens him,  
and reveals her secrets to him.

**19** If he goes astray, she will forsake him,  
and hand him over to his own ruin.

**20** Watch for the right time, and beware of evil,  
and do not be ashamed for your soul's sake;

**21** for there is a shame that brings sin,  
and there is a shame that is glory and favor.

**22** Do not favor another at the cost of your own soul,  
nor let respect for him bring you down.

**23** Do not hold back a word when it could save;

**24** for wisdom is known through speech,  
and discipline through the words of the tongue.

**25** Do not speak against the truth,  
but be ashamed of your lack of discipline.

**26** Do not be ashamed to confess your sins,  
and do not try to force back the current of a river.

**27** Do not lay yourself down before a fool,  
nor show partiality to a ruler.

**28** Fight to the death for the truth,  
and the Lord God will fight for you.

**29** Do not be harsh with your tongue,  
or sluggish and slack in your deeds.

**30** Do not be like a lion in your house,  
nor capricious with your servants.

**31** Do not let your hand be stretched out to receive,  
and closed when it is time to give back.

## Notes

- **vv1–10** — the poor, the suppliant, the orphan and the widow: what the Law commands for them (Exodus 22:22–23; Deuteronomy 15:7–11) turned into counsel for one person.
- **v6 "his Maker will hear his prayer"** — *If you do afflict him — if he cries out to Me, I will surely hear his cry* (Exodus 22:23).
- **v10 "you will then be like a son of the Most High"** — Jesus: *love your enemies, and do good… and you will be sons of the Most High* (Luke 6:35).
- **v12 "those who rise early for her"** — *orthrizontes*, as in Wisdom 6:14, "he who rises early for her."
- **v14 "minister to the Holy One"** — *leitourgēsousin hagiō*, the words of priestly service: to serve wisdom is to serve God.
- **vv17–18 "at first she walks with him on winding ways… then she comes straight back to him"** — wisdom tests her pupil before she trusts him, as discipline seems painful for the moment and later yields fruit (Hebrews 12:11). "Her discipline" — the Greek held here spells the word *paidia*, "play," for *paideia*, "discipline"; the context is discipline.
- **vv20–21 "a shame that brings sin… a shame that is glory"** — the shame that keeps a man from what is right, and the shame that keeps him from what is wrong.
- **v26 "do not try to force back the current of a river"** — confession is as natural as a river's flow; to hold it back is labor for nothing. Compare Proverbs 28:13 (Reproof 4:13).
- **v31 "stretched out to receive, and closed when it is time to give back"** — compare Acts 20:35, *It is more blessed to give than to receive*.

# Sirach 24

**1** *The praise of wisdom.*  
Wisdom will praise herself,  
and will glory among her people.

**2** In the assembly of the Most High she will open her mouth,  
and before His host she will glory:

**3** "I came out of the mouth of the Most High,  
and covered the earth like a mist.

**4** I dwelt in the heights,  
and my throne was in a pillar of cloud.

**5** Alone I went around the circle of heaven,  
and walked in the depths of the abyss.

**6** Over the waves of the sea, and over all the earth,  
and over every people and nation I have held sway.

**7** Among all these I sought a resting place;  
in whose inheritance should I lodge?

**8** Then the Creator of all things gave me a command,  
and He who created me gave my tent a resting place,  
and said, 'Pitch your tent in Jacob,  
and receive your inheritance in Israel.'

**9** Before the ages, from the beginning, He created me,  
and to the ages I shall not cease.

**10** In the holy tent I ministered before Him,  
and so I was established in Zion.

**11** In the beloved city likewise He gave me rest,  
and in Jerusalem was my authority.

**12** I took root among an honored people,  
in the portion of the Lord, His inheritance.

**13** I grew tall like a cedar in Lebanon,  
and like a cypress on the mountains of Hermon.

**14** I grew tall like a palm tree on the shores,  
and like rose bushes in Jericho;  
like a fair olive tree in the plain,  
and I grew tall like a plane tree.

**15** Like cinnamon and camel's thorn I gave forth the scent of spices,  
and like choice myrrh I spread a sweet fragrance,  
like galbanum and onycha and stacte,  
and like the smoke of frankincense in the tent.

**16** Like a terebinth I spread out my branches,  
and my branches are branches of glory and grace.

**17** Like a vine I put forth grace,  
and my blossoms became the fruit of glory and wealth.

**18** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**19** Come to me, you who desire me,  
and eat your fill of my fruits.

**20** For the memory of me is sweeter than honey,  
and my inheritance sweeter than the honeycomb.

**21** Those who eat me will hunger for more,  
and those who drink me will thirst for more.

**22** Whoever obeys me will not be put to shame,  
and those who work with me will not sin."

**23** All these things are the book of the covenant of the Most High God,  
the law that Moses commanded,  
an inheritance for the assemblies of Jacob.

**24** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**25** It fills men with wisdom like the Pishon,  
and like the Tigris in the days of first fruits;

**26** it makes understanding overflow like the Euphrates,  
and like the Jordan in the days of harvest;

**27** it makes discipline shine like the light,  
like the Gihon in the days of vintage.

**28** The first man did not know her fully,  
nor will the last trace her out;

**29** for her thought is fuller than the sea,  
and her counsel deeper than the great abyss.

**30** As for me, I came out like a canal from a river,  
and like a water channel into a garden.

**31** I said, "I will water my garden,  
and drench my flower bed";  
and my canal became a river,  
and my river became a sea.

**32** I will again make discipline shine like the dawn,  
and make it shine far and wide.

**33** I will again pour out teaching like prophecy,  
and leave it to all future generations.

**34** See that I have not labored for myself alone,  
but for all who seek her.

## Notes

- **v1 "The praise of wisdom."** — a heading written into the Greek text. The chapter stands at the center of the book: wisdom speaks in her own voice, as in Proverbs 8, and is found to be the Law given to Israel (v23).
- **v3 "I came out of the mouth of the Most High"** — wisdom as God's spoken word: *By the word of the LORD the heavens were made* (Psalm 33:6).
- **vv4, 8 "I dwelt… Pitch your tent in Jacob"** — *kataskēnoun*, "to pitch a tent," the word of God's dwelling in the tabernacle; John uses the same verb of the Word: *the Word became flesh and pitched His tent among us* (John 1:14, *eskēnōsen*). "A pillar of cloud" — the cloud that led Israel (Exodus 13:21).
- **v9 "Before the ages, from the beginning, He created me"** — the Greek of Proverbs 8:22–23, as at 1:4.
- **v12 "the portion of the Lord"** — Israel, as in 17:17 and Deuteronomy 32:9.
- **v15 "galbanum and onycha and stacte… frankincense in the tent"** — the spices of the holy incense (Exodus 30:34) and, with cinnamon, of the anointing oil (30:23): wisdom is the fragrance of the sanctuary.
- **v18, v24** — Swete's Greek gives these verse numbers and no words (see the note on 1:5).
- **vv19–21 "Come to me… Those who eat me will hunger for more"** — wisdom's invitation, as in Proverbs 9:5. Jesus says the opposite of Himself: *I am the bread of life; whoever comes to Me shall not hunger* (John 6:35).
- **v23 "the law that Moses commanded, an inheritance for the assemblies of Jacob"** — almost word for word the Greek of Deuteronomy 33:4 (*nomon hon eneteilato… Mōusēs, klēronomian synagōgais Iakōb*); the Hebrew there reads *Moses commanded us an instruction, a possession for the assembly of Jacob*. Here the Law and wisdom are one.
- **vv25–27 "the Pishon… the Tigris… the Euphrates… the Gihon"** — the rivers of Eden (Genesis 2:10–14), with the Jordan, which *overflows all its banks all the days of harvest* (Joshua 3:15): the Law overflows with wisdom like the rivers of paradise. In vv30–31 the teacher's own small channel grows into a river and a sea.

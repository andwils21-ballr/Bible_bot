# Sirach 10

**1** A wise judge will discipline his people,  
and the rule of a man of understanding will be well ordered.

**2** As the judge of a people is, so are his officials;  
and as the ruler of a city is, so are all who live in it.

**3** An undisciplined king will ruin his people,  
but a city will be built up by the understanding of its rulers.

**4** The government of the earth is in the hand of the Lord,  
and over it He will raise up the right man for the time.

**5** A man's success is in the hand of the Lord,  
and He will lay His honor upon the scribe.

**6** Do not be angry with your neighbor for every wrong,  
and do nothing by acts of insolence.

**7** Arrogance is hateful before the Lord and before men,  
and wrongdoing is an offense to both.

**8** Sovereignty passes from nation to nation  
because of injustice and insolence and wealth.

**9** How can earth and ashes be proud?  
For even while he lives I have cast out his inner parts.

**10** A long illness — the physician makes light of it;  
today a king, and tomorrow he will die.

**11** For when a man dies,  
he will inherit creeping things and wild beasts and worms.

**12** The beginning of a man's pride is to depart from the Lord;  
his heart has turned away from his Maker.

**13** For the beginning of pride is sin,  
and the man who clings to it will pour out detestable things.  
Therefore the Lord brought unheard-of calamities on them,  
and overthrew them utterly.

**14** The Lord has cast down the thrones of rulers,  
and seated the meek in their place.

**15** The Lord has plucked up the roots of nations,  
and planted the humble in their place.

**16** The Lord has overthrown the lands of nations,  
and destroyed them to the foundations of the earth.

**17** He has withered some of them and destroyed them,  
and made the memory of them cease from the earth.

**18** Pride was not created for men,  
nor fierce anger for those born of women.

**19** What seed is held in honor? The seed of man.  
What seed is held in honor? Those who fear the Lord.  
What seed is held in dishonor? The seed of man.  
What seed is held in dishonor? Those who break the commandments.

**20** Among brothers their leader is honored,  
and those who fear the Lord are honored in His eyes.

**21** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**22** The rich, the noble and the poor —  
their glory is the fear of the Lord.

**23** It is not right to dishonor a poor man who has understanding,  
nor is it fitting to honor a sinful man.

**24** The great man, the judge and the ruler will be honored,  
but none of them is greater than the one who fears the Lord.

**25** Free men will serve a wise servant,  
and a man of knowledge will not grumble.

**26** Do not be too clever to do your work,  
nor glorify yourself in the time of your need.

**27** Better is a man who works at everything  
than one who strolls about,  
or one who glorifies himself and lacks bread.

**28** My child, glorify your soul with meekness,  
and give it the honor it deserves.

**29** Who will justify the man who sins against his own soul?  
Who will honor the man who dishonors his own life?

**30** A poor man is honored for his knowledge,  
and a rich man is honored for his wealth.

**31** But one honored in poverty, how much more in wealth;  
one dishonored in wealth, how much more in poverty!

## Notes

- **v5 "the scribe"** — *grammateus*, the man of the written law; Sirach praises the scribe's calling at length in 38:24–39:11.
- **v8 "Sovereignty passes from nation to nation"** — compare Daniel 2:21, *He removes kings and sets up kings*.
- **v9 "earth and ashes"** — *gē kai spodos*, Abraham's words before God in the Greek of Genesis 18:27, *I am dust and ashes*. "I have cast out his inner parts" — the verb is first person in the Greek held here, abruptly, as if God speaks: man's body decays even while he lives.
- **v10 "the physician makes light of it"** — *skōptei*, "jests": the line is obscure; it may mean that a long illness is made light of until the king who has it dies.
- **v11 "creeping things and wild beasts and worms"** — compare Isaiah 14:11, *worms are your covering*.
- **vv14–17 "The Lord has cast down the thrones of rulers, and seated the meek in their place"** — Mary's song uses the same verb, *He has brought down (kathelen) the mighty from their thrones and lifted up the humble* (Luke 1:52); Hannah's song before it, *He raises the poor from the dust… to seat them with nobles* (1 Samuel 2:8).
- **v19 "What seed is held in honor?"** — question and answer twice over: the same human race is honored or dishonored by what it does with the commandments.
- **v21** — Swete's Greek gives this verse number and no words (see the note on 1:5).
- **v25 "Free men will serve a wise servant"** — Proverbs 17:2, *A servant who acts wisely will rule over a son who brings shame*.
- **v27** — compare Proverbs 12:9, *Better to be lowly and have a servant than to play the great man and lack bread*.

# Sirach 48

**1** Then the prophet Elijah arose like fire,  
and his word burned like a torch.

**2** He brought famine upon them,  
and by his zeal made them few.

**3** By the word of the Lord he shut up the heavens,  
and so three times brought down fire.

**4** How glorious you were, Elijah, in your wonders!  
Who can boast as you can?

**5** You raised a dead man from death and from Hades,  
by the word of the Most High.

**6** You brought kings down to destruction,  
and honored men from their beds.

**7** You heard rebuke at Sinai,  
and judgments of vengeance at Horeb.

**8** You anointed kings to bring retribution,  
and prophets as successors after him.

**9** You were taken up in a whirlwind of fire,  
in a chariot with horses of fire.

**10** You were written down, in reproofs for their times,  
to calm anger before it breaks into fury,  
to turn the heart of the father to the son,  
and to restore the tribes of Jacob.

**11** Happy are those who saw you,  
and those adorned with love;  
for we too will surely live.

**12** It was Elijah who was covered by the whirlwind,  
and Elisha was filled with his spirit;  
in his days he was not shaken by any ruler,  
and no one overpowered him.

**13** No word was too hard for him,  
and even in death his body prophesied.

**14** In his life he did wonders,  
and in death his works were marvelous.

**15** For all this the people did not repent,  
and did not turn away from their sins,  
until they were carried off as plunder from their land  
and scattered over all the earth;  
the people were left very few,  
with a ruler from the house of David.

**16** Some of them did what was pleasing,  
but others multiplied sins.

**17** Hezekiah fortified his city,  
and brought Gog in among them;  
he dug through the sheer rock with iron,  
and built pools for water.

**18** In his days Sennacherib came up,  
and sent Rabshakeh, and set out;  
he lifted his hand against Zion,  
and boasted in his arrogance.

**19** Then their hearts and hands trembled,  
and they were in anguish like women in labor.

**20** They called on the Lord who is merciful,  
spreading out their hands to Him;  
and the Holy One quickly heard them from heaven,  
and redeemed them by the hand of Isaiah.

**21** He struck the camp of the Assyrians,  
and His angel wiped them out.

**22** For Hezekiah did what pleased the Lord,  
and held firmly to the ways of David his father,  
as Isaiah the prophet commanded,  
who was great and trustworthy in his vision.

**23** In his days the sun went back,  
and he added life to the king.

**24** By a great spirit he saw the last things,  
and comforted those who mourned in Zion.

**25** He revealed what was to be, to the end of time,  
and hidden things before they came to pass.

## Notes

- **v10 "to turn the heart of the father to the son"** — *epistrepsai kardian patros pros huion*, the promise that closes Malachi: Elijah *will restore the heart of the father to the son* (4:6, Swete's 4:5, *kardian patros pros huion*). The angel says the same of John the Baptist, who goes *in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children* (Luke 1:17). "To restore the tribes of Jacob" — the work of the Servant in the Greek of Isaiah 49:6, *to raise up the tribes of Jacob* (*tou stēsai tas phylas Iakōb*).

# Sirach 44

**1** *Hymn of the fathers.*  
Let us now praise glorious men,  
and our fathers in their generations.

**2** The Lord created great glory,  
His majesty from of old.

**3** They ruled in their kingdoms,  
and were men renowned for their power;  
they gave counsel by their understanding,  
and spoke in prophecies;

**4** leaders of the people by their counsels,  
and by their understanding of the people's learning,  
wise in the words of their instruction;

**5** seeking out musical tunes,  
and setting forth verses in writing;

**6** rich men endowed with strength,  
living at peace where they lived as guests.

**7** All these were honored in their generations,  
and were the pride of their times.

**8** Some of them have left a name behind,  
so that their praises are told.

**9** Others have no memorial;  
they have perished as though they had never been,  
and are as though they had never been born,  
they and their children after them.

**10** But these were men of mercy,  
whose righteous deeds have not been forgotten.

**11** With their offspring a good inheritance will remain,  
their descendants;

**12** in the covenants their offspring stands,  
and their children for their sake.

**13** Their offspring will remain forever,  
and their glory will not be blotted out.

**14** Their bodies were buried in peace,  
and their name lives on for generations.

**15** Peoples will tell of their wisdom,  
and the assembly will proclaim their praise.

**16** Enoch pleased the Lord and was taken up,  
an example of repentance to the generations.

**17** Noah was found perfect and righteous;  
in the time of wrath he became an exchange;  
because of this a remnant was left to the earth;  
because of this the flood came.

**18** Everlasting covenants were made with him,  
that all flesh should never again be blotted out by a flood.

**19** Abraham was the great father of a multitude of nations,  
and no one was found like him in glory,

**20** who kept the law of the Most High,  
and entered into covenant with Him;  
he established the covenant in his flesh,  
and in testing he was found faithful.

**21** Therefore He established it with him by an oath,  
that the nations would be blessed in his offspring,  
that He would multiply him like the dust of the earth,  
and exalt his offspring like the stars,  
and give them an inheritance from sea to sea,  
and from the River to the ends of the earth.

**22** With Isaac too He established the same  
for the sake of Abraham his father:  
the blessing of all men, and the covenant.

**23** He made it rest on the head of Jacob;  
He acknowledged him with His blessings,  
and gave him his inheritance;  
He divided his portions,  
and distributed them among twelve tribes.

## Notes

- **v1 "*Hymn of the fathers*"** — a heading in the Greek text. From here to chapter 50 the book praises the fathers of Israel one by one, from Enoch to the high priest Simon.
- **v15 "Peoples will tell of their wisdom"** — the praise promised to the scribe in 39:10, *Nations will tell of his wisdom, and the assembly will proclaim his praise*, here given to the fathers.
- **v16 "Enoch pleased the Lord and was taken up"** — *euērestēsen… metetethē*, the words of the Greek of Genesis 5:24, where the Hebrew has *Enoch walked with God… for God took him*; Hebrews 11:5 uses the same pair, *by faith Enoch was taken up… he had pleased God*. He is the Enoch of the book of 1 Enoch.
- **v17 "Noah was found perfect and righteous"** — Genesis 6:9, *Noah was a righteous man, whole in his generations* (Greek *dikaios, teleios*). "He became an exchange" — *antallagma*, the word of 26:14 (*nothing can be given in exchange*): one man kept in place of a world. The two lines *because of this… because of this* stand as Swete prints them.
- **v18 "that all flesh should never again be blotted out by a flood"** — Genesis 9:11, *never again shall all flesh be cut off by the waters of the flood*.
- **vv19–20 "father of a multitude of nations… in testing he was found faithful"** — Genesis 17:5, *father of a roar of nations*; Genesis 22:1, *God tested Abraham*; and Nehemiah 9:8, *You found his heart faithful before You* (Greek *heures… pistēn*).
- **v20 "who kept the law of the Most High"** — Genesis 26:5, *because Abraham listened to My voice, and kept My charge: My commands, My statutes, and My instructions*.
- **v21 "from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth"** — the words of Psalm 72:8, there of the king's rule.
- **v22 "for the sake of Abraham his father"** — Genesis 26:24, *I will bless you and multiply your seed, for the sake of Abraham My servant*.
- **v23** — Swete's 44:23 goes on, *and He brought out of him a man of mercy*; in the English those words open chapter 45.

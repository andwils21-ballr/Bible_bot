# Sirach 8

**1** Do not contend with a powerful man,  
or you may fall into his hands.

**2** Do not quarrel with a rich man,  
or he may outweigh you;  
for gold has destroyed many,  
and has turned aside the hearts of kings.

**3** Do not fight with a loud-mouthed man,  
and do not heap wood on his fire.

**4** Do not jest with an undisciplined man,  
or your ancestors may be insulted.

**5** Do not reproach a man who is turning away from sin;  
remember that we all deserve punishment.

**6** Do not dishonor a man in his old age,  
for some of us are growing old.

**7** Do not rejoice over anyone's death;  
remember that we all must die.

**8** Do not neglect the discourse of the wise,  
but busy yourself with their proverbs;  
for from them you will learn discipline,  
and how to serve great men.

**9** Do not pass over the discourse of the aged,  
for they themselves learned from their fathers;  
from them you will learn understanding,  
and how to give an answer in time of need.

**10** Do not kindle the coals of a sinner,  
or you may be burned in his flaming fire.

**11** Do not get up and leave before an insolent man,  
or he may lie in ambush for your words.

**12** Do not lend to a man stronger than you;  
and if you do lend, count it as lost.

**13** Do not give surety beyond your means;  
and if you give surety, be ready to pay.

**14** Do not go to law against a judge,  
for they will decide in his favor because of his standing.

**15** Do not travel on the road with a reckless man,  
or he may weigh you down;  
for he will do as he pleases,  
and through his folly you will perish with him.

**16** Do not quarrel with a hot-tempered man,  
and do not cross the wilderness with him;  
for blood is as nothing in his eyes,  
and where there is no help, he will strike you down.

**17** Do not consult with a fool,  
for he will not be able to keep a secret.

**18** Do not do a secret thing before a stranger,  
for you do not know what it will give birth to.

**19** Do not open your heart to every man,  
and do not let him repay you with a favor.

## Notes

- **v2 "he may outweigh you"** — *antistēsē sou tēn holkēn*, "set his weight against yours," the tipping of a scale: his money will outweigh your case.
- **v5 "we all deserve punishment"** — *en epitimois*, "under rebuke": the one who has turned from sin should not be reminded of it by those who also need mercy.
- **vv8–9 "the discourse of the wise… of the aged"** — *for they themselves learned from their fathers*: as Bildad says, *ask, please, of the former generation* (Job 8:8–10).
- **v13 "Do not give surety beyond your means"** — Proverbs warns against surety altogether (6:1–5; 22:26–27); Sirach allows it within one's means, *be ready to pay*.
- **v16 "do not cross the wilderness with him"** — where no one can come to your help.
- **v18 "what it will give birth to"** — *ti texetai*: what the secret will lead to once a stranger knows it.
- **v19 "do not let him repay you with a favor"** — the Greek line is obscure: perhaps a favor from one who knows your heart is a debt, or a hold over you.

# Sirach 25

**1** In three things I am made beautiful,  
and stand in beauty before the Lord and men:  
harmony among brothers, friendship among neighbors,  
and a wife and husband who suit each other.

**2** My soul hates three kinds of men,  
and I am greatly offended by their life:  
a proud poor man, a lying rich man,  
and an old adulterer lacking in sense.

**3** If you have gathered nothing in your youth,  
how can you find anything in your old age?

**4** How fitting is judgment for gray hairs,  
and for elders to know good counsel!

**5** How fitting is wisdom in the aged,  
and understanding and counsel in men of honor!

**6** Rich experience is the crown of the aged,  
and their boast is the fear of the Lord.

**7** Nine things I have thought of and called blessed in my heart,  
and a tenth I will tell with my tongue:  
a man who rejoices in his children;  
a man who lives to see the fall of his enemies;

**8** happy is he who lives with a sensible wife,  
and he who has not slipped with his tongue,  
and he who has not served a man unworthy of him;

**9** happy is he who has found prudence,  
and he who speaks to listening ears;

**10** how great is he who has found wisdom!  
But there is none above him who fears the Lord.

**11** The fear of the Lord surpasses everything;  
to whom shall the one who holds it be compared?

**12** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**13** Any wound, but not a wound of the heart!  
Any wickedness, but not the wickedness of a wife!

**14** Any attack, but not an attack from those who hate!  
Any vengeance, but not the vengeance of enemies!

**15** There is no head above the head of a snake,  
and no anger above the anger of an enemy.

**16** I would rather live with a lion and a dragon  
than live with an evil wife.

**17** The wickedness of a wife changes her appearance,  
and darkens her face like sackcloth.

**18** Her husband sits among his neighbors,  
and when he hears it, he sighs bitterly.

**19** Any wickedness is small beside the wickedness of a wife;  
may a sinner's lot fall upon her!

**20** A sandy ascent for the feet of the aged —  
such is a talkative wife to a quiet husband.

**21** Do not fall for a woman's beauty,  
and do not set your desire on a woman.

**22** There is anger and impudence and great disgrace  
when a wife provides for her husband.

**23** A dejected heart and a gloomy face  
and a wounded heart come from an evil wife;  
drooping hands and weak knees  
from a wife who does not make her husband happy.

**24** From a woman sin had its beginning,  
and because of her we all die.

**25** Do not give water an outlet,  
nor an evil wife freedom of speech.

**26** If she does not go as you direct,  
cut her off from your flesh.

## Notes

- **vv1–2, 7–11 "In three things… three kinds of men… Nine things… and a tenth"** — the counting sayings of Reproof 6 (Proverbs 30), here three good things, three hateful men, and ten blessings, the tenth and highest the fear of the Lord.
- **v12** — Swete's Greek gives this verse number and no words (see the note on 1:5).
- **vv13–26** — the evil wife, a long passage that 26:1–4 and 26:13–18 answer with the good wife, as Proverbs closes with the capable wife (Reproof 7:10–31).
- **v15 "no head above the head of a snake"** — the snake's head is where its venom is; nothing strikes worse.
- **v22 "when a wife provides for her husband"** — *epichorēgē*, "supplies": a husband kept by his wife's wealth.
- **v24 "From a woman sin had its beginning, and because of her we all die"** — Genesis 3:6. Paul tells the same story through the man: *through one man sin entered the world, and death through sin* (Romans 5:12).
- **v26 "cut her off from your flesh"** — divorce, the undoing of the "one flesh" of Genesis 2:24 (Deuteronomy 24:1).

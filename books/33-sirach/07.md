# Sirach 7

**1** Do no evil, and evil will never overtake you.

**2** Stay away from wrong, and it will turn away from you.

**3** My son, do not sow in the furrows of injustice,  
and you will not reap them sevenfold.

**4** Do not seek from the Lord the highest place,  
nor from the king a seat of honor.

**5** Do not justify yourself before the Lord,  
nor show off your wisdom before the king.

**6** Do not seek to become a judge,  
if you are not strong enough to root out injustice;  
or you may stand in awe of a powerful man,  
and put a stumbling block in the way of your uprightness.

**7** Do not sin against the whole city,  
nor disgrace yourself among the people.

**8** Do not repeat a sin,  
for even for one you will not go unpunished.

**9** Do not say, "He will look upon the multitude of my gifts,  
and when I make an offering to the Most High God,  
He will accept it."

**10** Do not be fainthearted in your prayer,  
and do not neglect to give charity.

**11** Do not laugh at a man who is bitter in soul,  
for there is One who humbles and exalts.

**12** Do not devise a lie against your brother,  
nor do the same to a friend.

**13** Do not be willing to tell any lie at all,  
for the habit of it leads to no good.

**14** Do not babble in the company of elders,  
and do not repeat your words in your prayer.

**15** Do not hate hard work,  
or farming, which was created by the Most High.

**16** Do not count yourself among the crowd of sinners;  
remember that wrath will not delay.

**17** Humble your soul greatly,  
for the punishment of the ungodly is fire and worms.

**18** Do not exchange a friend for something of no account,  
nor a true brother for the gold of Ophir.

**19** Do not let go of a wise and good wife,  
for her grace is worth more than gold.

**20** Do not mistreat a servant who works faithfully,  
nor a hired laborer who gives himself to his work.

**21** Let your soul love a good servant;  
do not withhold his freedom from him.

**22** Do you have cattle? Look after them;  
and if they are useful to you, keep them.

**23** Do you have children? Discipline them,  
and bend their necks from their youth.

**24** Do you have daughters? Watch over their bodies,  
and do not show them too cheerful a face.

**25** Give your daughter in marriage, and you will have finished a great work;  
but give her to a man of understanding.

**26** Do you have a wife after your own heart? Do not divorce her.

**27** Honor your father with all your heart,  
and do not forget your mother's birth pangs.

**28** Remember that through them you were born;  
and what can you give back to them that equals what they gave you?

**29** With all your soul fear the Lord,  
and revere His priests.

**30** With all your strength love your Maker,  
and do not forsake His ministers.

**31** Fear the Lord and honor the priest,  
and give him his portion, as you have been commanded from the beginning:  
the offering for guilt, the gift of the shoulders,  
the sacrifice of consecration, and the firstfruits.

**32** Stretch out your hand to the poor as well,  
so that your blessing may be complete.

**33** A gift is welcome to all the living,  
and do not withhold kindness from the dead.

**34** Do not fail those who weep,  
and mourn with those who mourn.

**35** Do not hesitate to visit the sick,  
for by such deeds you will be loved.

**36** In all your words remember your end,  
and you will never sin.

## Notes

- **v3 "do not sow in the furrows of injustice"** — *Whoever sows injustice will reap trouble* (Proverbs 22:8); Job 4:8, *those who plow iniquity and sow trouble reap it*.
- **v8 "Do not repeat a sin"** — literally "do not bind a sin twice."
- **v11 "there is One who humbles and exalts"** — Hannah's song: *He brings low; He also lifts up* (1 Samuel 2:7).
- **v12 "Do not devise a lie"** — literally "do not plow a lie," the Hebrew picture behind Proverbs 3:29, *Do not plot harm against your neighbor*, where the verb is "plow."
- **v14 "do not repeat your words in your prayer"** — Jesus: *When you pray, do not babble on as the Gentiles do, for they think they will be heard for their many words* (Matthew 6:7).
- **v15 "farming, which was created by the Most High"** — the work given to Adam in the garden and after it (Genesis 2:15; 3:23).
- **v17 "fire and worms"** — the end of Isaiah (66:24), and Jesus, *where their worm does not die and the fire is not quenched* (Mark 9:48).
- **v18 "the gold of Ophir"** — the Greek has *Soupheir*, the land of fine gold (1 Kings 9:28; Job 28:16).
- **v21 "do not withhold his freedom from him"** — the release of a Hebrew servant in the seventh year (Exodus 21:2; Deuteronomy 15:12).
- **vv27–31 "with all your heart… with all your soul… with all your strength"** — the words of Deuteronomy 6:5 spread across father and mother, the Lord, and His priests. "The gift of the shoulders" — the priests' due from the sacrifices (Deuteronomy 18:3; Leviticus 7:32–34).

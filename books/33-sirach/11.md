# Sirach 11

**1** The wisdom of a humble man will lift up his head,  
and seat him among great men.

**2** Do not praise a man for his good looks,  
nor abhor a man for his appearance.

**3** The bee is small among flying creatures,  
but what she makes is the best of sweet things.

**4** Do not boast of the clothes you wear,  
nor exalt yourself on the day of honor;  
for the works of the Lord are wonderful,  
and His works are hidden from men.

**5** Many monarchs have sat on the ground,  
and one never thought of has worn the crown.

**6** Many rulers have been greatly disgraced,  
and the honored have been handed over to others.

**7** Do not find fault before you investigate;  
first consider, and then rebuke.

**8** Do not answer before you have heard,  
and do not interrupt in the middle of what is said.

**9** Do not argue about a matter that does not concern you,  
and do not sit with sinners when they judge.

**10** My child, do not busy yourself with many matters;  
if you multiply them, you will not go unpunished;  
if you pursue, you will not overtake,  
and by running away you will not escape.

**11** There is one who works and toils and hurries,  
and is all the more behind.

**12** There is another who is slow and needs help,  
lacking in strength and abounding in poverty;  
but the eyes of the Lord look upon him for good,  
and He lifts him up from his low estate,

**13** and raises his head,  
and many are amazed at him.

**14** Good things and bad, life and death,  
poverty and wealth, come from the Lord.

**15** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**16** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**17** The gift of the Lord remains with the godly,  
and His good pleasure brings lasting success.

**18** There is a man who grows rich by his care and his stinginess,  
and this is the portion of his reward:

**19** when he says, "I have found rest,  
and now I will eat of my goods,"  
he does not know how much time will pass  
until he leaves them to others and dies.

**20** Stand by your covenant and give yourself to it,  
and grow old in your work.

**21** Do not marvel at the works of a sinner,  
but trust in the Lord and keep at your work;  
for it is easy in the sight of the Lord  
to make a poor man rich quickly, in a moment.

**22** The blessing of the Lord is the reward of the godly,  
and quickly He makes His blessing flourish.

**23** Do not say, "What do I need?  
What good things will be mine from now on?"

**24** Do not say, "I have enough,  
and what harm can come to me from now on?"

**25** In the day of prosperity, adversity is forgotten,  
and in the day of adversity, prosperity is not remembered.

**26** For it is easy for the Lord on the day of death  
to repay a man according to his ways.

**27** An hour's misfortune makes one forget delights,  
and at a man's end his deeds are revealed.

**28** Call no one blessed before his death;  
a man will be known by his children.

**29** Do not bring every man into your house,  
for many are the snares of the deceitful.

**30** Like a decoy partridge in a cage,  
so is the heart of a proud man,  
and like a spy he watches for your fall;

**31** for he lies in wait, turning good into evil,  
and on things worthy of praise he will put blame.

**32** From a spark of fire comes a heap of coals,  
and a sinful man lies in wait for blood.

**33** Beware of a scoundrel, for he plans evil,  
or he may give you a lasting blemish.

**34** Take a stranger into your home,  
and he will upset you with troubles,  
and make you a stranger to your own.

## Notes

- **vv5–6 "Many monarchs have sat on the ground, and one never thought of has worn the crown"** — compare Hannah's song, *He raises the poor from the dust… to seat them with nobles* (1 Samuel 2:8). "Monarchs" — *tyrannoi*, as in Wisdom 6:9.
- **v8 "Do not answer before you have heard"** — Proverbs 18:13, *If one gives an answer before he hears, it is his folly and shame*.
- **v14 "Good things and bad… come from the Lord"** — Job 2:10, *Shall we receive good from God, and not receive evil?*
- **vv15–16** — Swete's Greek gives these verse numbers and no words (see the note on 1:5).
- **vv18–19 "I have found rest, and now I will eat of my goods"** — the rich fool of Jesus' parable says the same, and dies that night (Luke 12:16–21).
- **v20 "Stand by your covenant"** — *diathēkē*: here the task or agreement a man has taken on; keep at it until old age.
- **v22 "The blessing of the Lord is the reward of the godly"** — compare Proverbs 10:22, *The blessing of the LORD makes rich*.
- **v24 "I have enough"** — the words of 5:1.
- **v30 "a decoy partridge in a cage"** — a tame bird set out to lure wild ones into the trap; the proud man's heart is bait.
- **v33 "a lasting blemish"** — *mōmos*, a blemish or blame that stays on a man's name.

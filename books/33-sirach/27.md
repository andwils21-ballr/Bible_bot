# Sirach 27

**1** Many have sinned for a trifle,  
and whoever seeks to grow rich will turn his eye away.

**2** As a stake is driven fast between the joints of stones,  
so sin is wedged in between selling and buying.

**3** If a man does not hold fast to the fear of the Lord with zeal,  
his house will soon be overthrown.

**4** When a sieve is shaken, the refuse remains;  
so a man's faults remain in his reasoning.

**5** The kiln tests the potter's vessels,  
and the test of a man is in his reasoning.

**6** The fruit shows how a tree has been tended;  
so a man's speech shows the thought of his heart.

**7** Do not praise a man before you hear him reason,  
for this is the test of men.

**8** If you pursue justice, you will attain it,  
and put it on like a glorious long robe.

**9** Birds roost with their own kind,  
and truth returns to those who practice it.

**10** A lion lies in wait for prey;  
so sins for those who do wrong.

**11** The talk of the godly is always wisdom,  
but the fool changes like the moon.

**12** Among the senseless, watch for the time to leave,  
but among the thoughtful, stay on.

**13** The talk of fools is offensive,  
and their laughter is wanton sin.

**14** The talk of a man who swears many oaths makes the hair stand on end,  
and their quarrels make one stop his ears.

**15** The quarrel of the proud ends in bloodshed,  
and their abuse is grievous to hear.

**16** Whoever reveals secrets destroys trust,  
and will never find a friend to his liking.

**17** Love your friend and keep faith with him;  
but if you reveal his secrets, do not go after him;

**18** for as a man destroys his enemy,  
so you have destroyed your neighbor's friendship.

**19** As you let a bird go from your hand,  
so you have let your neighbor go,  
and you will not catch him again.

**20** Do not go after him, for he is too far off,  
and has escaped like a gazelle from a snare.

**21** For a wound may be bound up,  
and after abuse there may be reconciliation;  
but whoever has revealed secrets has lost all hope.

**22** Whoever winks his eye plots evil,  
and no one can keep him from it.

**23** In your presence he speaks sweetly to you,  
and admires your words;  
but later he twists his speech,  
and turns your own words into a stumbling block.

**24** I have hated many things, but none like him,  
and the Lord will hate him.

**25** Whoever throws a stone straight up throws it on his own head,  
and a treacherous blow deals out wounds.

**26** Whoever digs a pit will fall into it,  
and whoever sets a snare will be caught in it.

**27** Whoever does evil, it will roll back on him,  
and he will not know where it came from.

**28** Mockery and abuse come from the proud,  
but vengeance lies in wait for him like a lion.

**29** Those who rejoice at the fall of the godly will be caught in a snare,  
and pain will consume them before they die.

**30** Wrath and anger, these too are detestable,  
and the sinful man will hold on to them.

## Notes

- **v2 "sin is wedged in between selling and buying"** — compare Amos 8:5, the traders who make *the ephah small and the shekel great*.
- **vv4–7 "a sieve… the kiln… the fruit"** — what a man says shows what he is: Jesus, *the mouth speaks out of what fills the heart* (Matthew 12:34), and *by their fruits you will know them* (7:16).
- **v8 "like a glorious long robe"** — *podērēs*, the full-length robe of the high priest (Wisdom 18:24).
- **vv16–21 "Whoever reveals secrets destroys trust"** — Proverbs 11:13, *Whoever goes about as a slanderer reveals secrets*. "Like a gazelle from a snare" — Proverbs 6:5. A wound or an insult can be mended (22:21–22); a betrayed secret cannot.
- **v22 "Whoever winks his eye plots evil"** — Proverbs 10:10, *Whoever winks the eye causes trouble*.
- **v23 "he speaks sweetly to you"** — *glykanei stoma sou*, "he will sweeten your mouth," as Swete prints it: he says what tastes sweet to you.
- **vv25–27 "Whoever digs a pit will fall into it… it will roll back on him"** — Reproof 2:27 (Proverbs 26:27), *Whoever digs a pit will fall into it, and a stone will come back on him who rolls it*; and Ecclesiastes 10:8.
- **v30 "Wrath and anger, these too are detestable"** — *bdelygmata*, and the next chapter takes them up: vengeance, forgiveness, and the tongue.

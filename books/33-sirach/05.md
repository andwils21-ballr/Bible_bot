# Sirach 5

**1** Do not rely on your wealth,  
and do not say, "I have enough."

**2** Do not follow your own desire and strength,  
walking in the lusts of your heart.

**3** Do not say, "Who will have power over me?"  
for the Lord will surely punish you.

**4** Do not say, "I sinned, and what happened to me?"  
for the Lord is patient.

**5** Do not be so sure of atonement  
that you add sin to sins.

**6** Do not say, "His compassion is great;  
He will atone for the multitude of my sins,"  
for both mercy and wrath are with Him,  
and His anger rests on sinners.

**7** Do not delay to turn to the Lord,  
nor put it off from day to day;  
for suddenly the wrath of the Lord will go forth,  
and in the time of punishment you will perish.

**8** Do not rely on dishonest wealth,  
for it will not help you in the day of calamity.

**9** Do not winnow with every wind,  
nor walk on every path;  
so does the double-tongued sinner.

**10** Stand firm in your understanding,  
and let your word be one.

**11** Be quick to hear,  
and give your answer with patience.

**12** If you have understanding, answer your neighbor;  
if not, put your hand over your mouth.

**13** Glory and dishonor come from speaking,  
and a man's tongue is his downfall.

**14** Do not be called a whisperer,  
and do not lie in ambush with your tongue;  
for shame comes upon the thief,  
and harsh condemnation upon the double-tongued.

**15** In great things and in small, do not act in ignorance,

## Notes

- **v1 "I have enough"** — *autarkē moi estin*, the rich man's self-sufficiency; compare the rich fool, *you have many goods laid up for many years* (Luke 12:19).
- **vv4–7 "Do not say, 'I sinned, and what happened to me?'"** — presuming on God's patience: *Because the sentence against an evil deed is not carried out quickly, the heart of the children of man is fully set in them to do evil* (Ecclesiastes 8:11). "Mercy and wrath are with Him" — both halves of God's name in Exodus 34:6–7.
- **v9 "Do not winnow with every wind"** — turning with every breeze, as the double-tongued man says one thing here and another there (also v14 and 6:1).
- **v10 "let your word be one"** — compare Matthew 5:37, *Let your word be 'Yes, yes' or 'No, no'*.
- **v11 "Be quick to hear"** — *tachys*, the word of James 1:19, *quick to hear, slow to speak*.
- **v12 "put your hand over your mouth"** — Job 40:4, *I lay my hand on my mouth*; Reproof 6:32 (Proverbs 30:32).
- **v15** — the sentence runs on into 6:1; the chapter break falls in the middle of it.

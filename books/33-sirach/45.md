# Sirach 45

**1** He brought out of him a man of mercy,  
who found favor in the eyes of all flesh,  
beloved by God and men:  
Moses, whose memory is blessed.

**2** He made him equal in glory to the holy ones,  
and made him great, to the terror of his enemies.

**3** By his words He made the signs cease;  
He glorified him before kings;  
He gave him commands for His people,  
and showed him part of His glory.

**4** For his faithfulness and meekness He set him apart;  
He chose him out of all flesh.

**5** He made him hear His voice,  
and led him into the thick darkness,  
and gave him the commandments face to face,  
the law of life and knowledge,  
to teach Jacob the covenant,  
and Israel His judgments.

**6** He exalted Aaron, a holy man like him,  
his brother, of the tribe of Levi.

**7** He made an everlasting covenant with him,  
and gave him the priesthood of the people;  
He blessed him with splendid vesture,  
and girded him with a glorious robe.

**8** He clothed him with the perfection of glory,  
and strengthened him with garments of power:  
the linen breeches, the long robe and the ephod.

**9** He encircled him with pomegranates,  
with many golden bells all around,  
to sound as he walked,  
to make a sound heard in the temple,  
as a reminder to the sons of his people;

**10** with a holy garment of gold and blue and purple,  
the work of an embroiderer;  
with the breastpiece of judgment, the tokens of truth;

**11** with twisted scarlet, the work of a craftsman;  
with precious stones engraved like seals,  
set in gold, the work of a jeweler,  
for a reminder, with engraved writing  
by the number of the tribes of Israel;

**12** a gold crown upon the turban,  
engraved like a seal with holiness,  
a glory of honor, a work of power,  
the delight of the eyes, richly adorned.

**13** Before him there were never such beautiful things;  
no outsider has ever put them on,  
but only his sons,  
and his descendants for all time.

**14** His sacrifices shall be wholly burned  
twice every day continually.

**15** Moses ordained him,  
and anointed him with holy oil;  
it became for him an everlasting covenant,  
and for his offspring as long as the heavens last,  
to minister to Him and serve as priest,  
and to bless His people in the name.

**16** He chose him out of all the living  
to offer sacrifice to the Lord,  
incense and a fragrant offering as a memorial,  
to make atonement for Your people.

**17** He gave him authority in His commandments,  
in the covenants of judgments,  
to teach Jacob the testimonies,  
and to proclaim His law to Israel.

**18** Outsiders conspired against him  
and envied him in the wilderness,  
Dathan and Abiram and their men,  
and the company of Korah, in fury and wrath.

**19** The Lord saw it and was displeased,  
and they were destroyed in the heat of His anger;  
He worked wonders against them,  
to consume them in the flame of His fire.

**20** He added glory to Aaron,  
and gave him an inheritance:  
He allotted them the firstfruits of the first produce,  
and prepared bread in abundance for them first;

**21** for they eat the sacrifices of the Lord,  
which He gave to him and to his offspring.

**22** But in the land of the people he has no inheritance,  
and he has no portion among the people;  
for He is your portion and inheritance.

**23** Phinehas son of Eleazar is the third in glory,  
for his zeal in the fear of the Lord,  
and because he stood firm when the people turned away,  
in the goodness and readiness of his soul;  
and he made atonement for Israel.

**24** Therefore a covenant of peace was established with him,  
to be chief of the sanctuary and of his people,  
that he and his offspring should have  
the dignity of the priesthood forever.

**25** There was also a covenant with David,  
a son of the tribe of Judah:  
the inheritance of the king passes from son to son alone;  
the inheritance of Aaron is to all his offspring.

**26** May He give you wisdom in your heart  
to judge His people in righteousness,  
so that their good things may not vanish,  
nor their glory, for all their generations.

## Notes

- **v1 "He brought out of him a man of mercy"** — these first lines are the end of Swete's 44:23; the rest of the verse is his 45:1. "A man of mercy" — *andra eleous*, as the fathers were *men of mercy* (44:10).
- **v3 "By his words He made the signs cease… and showed him part of His glory"** — the plagues stopped when Moses prayed (*the thunder and the hail stopped*, Exodus 9:33); and God showed Moses His glory only in part: *you will see My back. But My face will not be seen* (Exodus 33:23). *Tēs doxēs autou*, "of His glory," says the same.
- **v4 "For his faithfulness and meekness"** — Numbers 12:3, *the man Moses was very humble*, and 12:7, *He is trusted in all My house*.
- **v5 "led him into the thick darkness, and gave him the commandments face to face"** — Exodus 20:21, *Moses drew near to the thick darkness where God was*; 33:11, *The LORD would speak to Moses face to face*. "The law of life" — as in 17:11, *the law of life as an inheritance*.
- **vv8–13** — the high priest's garments of Exodus 28, item by item: breeches, long robe, ephod, the bells and pomegranates (28:33–35, *its sound shall be heard when he goes into the holy place*), the breastpiece, the twelve engraved stones, and the gold plate.
- **v10 "the breastpiece of judgment, the tokens of truth"** — *logiō kriseōs, dēlois alētheias*: the Urim and Thummim, which the Greek of Exodus 28:30 calls *tēn dēlōsin kai tēn alētheian*, "the showing and the truth," and Deuteronomy 33:8 *dēlous… kai alētheian*.
- **v12 "engraved like a seal with holiness"** — *ektypōma sphragidos hagiasmatos*: the gold plate of Exodus 28:36, engraved *with the engravings of a seal: Holy to the LORD*.
- **v15 "Moses ordained him"** — *eplērōsen… tas cheiras*, "filled his hands," the idiom for installing a priest: *it will take seven days to fill your hand* (Leviticus 8:33).
- **v22 "for He is your portion and inheritance"** — the words the LORD spoke to Aaron in Numbers 18:20, *I am your share and your inheritance among the sons of Israel*; the line turns to address Aaron.
- **vv23–24 "Phinehas… a covenant of peace"** — Numbers 25:11–13, *because he was jealous for his God and made atonement for the sons of Israel*.

# Sirach 3

**1** Listen to me, your father, children,  
and act accordingly, so that you may be saved.

**2** For the Lord has honored the father above his children,  
and has established the right of the mother over her sons.

**3** Whoever honors his father atones for sins,

**4** and like one who lays up treasure  
is he who glorifies his mother.

**5** Whoever honors his father will be gladdened by his own children,  
and like one who lays up treasure  
is he who glorifies his mother;  
and on the day of his prayer he will be heard.

**6** Whoever glorifies his father will have long life,  
and whoever listens to the Lord will give rest to his mother;

**7** he will serve his parents as his masters.

**8** Honor your father in deed and word,  
so that a blessing may come upon you from him.

**9** For a father's blessing strengthens the houses of children,  
but a mother's curse uproots their foundations.

**10** Do not glorify yourself in your father's dishonor,  
for it is no glory to you.

**11** For a man's glory comes from his father's honor,  
and a mother in disgrace is a reproach to her children.

**12** My child, help your father in his old age,  
and do not grieve him as long as he lives;

**13** even if his understanding fails, be patient with him,  
and do not despise him while you are in your full strength.

**14** For kindness shown to a father will not be forgotten,  
and it will be built up for you against your sins;

**15** in the day of your affliction it will be remembered in your favor;  
as fair weather on frost,  
so your sins will melt away.

**16** Whoever forsakes his father is like a blasphemer,  
and whoever angers his mother is cursed by the Lord.

**17** My child, carry out your work with meekness,  
and you will be loved by worthy people.

**18** The greater you are, the more humble yourself,  
and you will find favor before the Lord.

**19** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**20** For great is the power of the Lord,  
and by the humble He is glorified.

**21** Do not seek what is too hard for you,  
and do not search out what is too strong for you.

**22** Think about what has been commanded you,  
for you have no need of what is hidden.

**23** Do not busy yourself with what is beyond your tasks,  
for more than men can understand has been shown to you.

**24** For their own opinion has led many astray,  
and wrong speculation has made their thoughts slip.

**25** *(This verse is not in the Greek text followed here; see the note.)*

**26** Whoever loves danger will perish by it.  
A stubborn heart will fare badly at the end;  
a stubborn heart will be weighed down at the end.

**27** A stubborn heart will be weighed down with troubles,  
and the sinner will heap sin upon sins.

**28** For the calamity of the proud there is no healing,  
for a plant of wickedness has taken root in him.

**29** The heart of the intelligent will ponder a proverb,  
and an attentive ear is the desire of the wise.

**30** Water will quench a blazing fire,  
and charity atones for sins.

**31** Whoever repays favors is mindful of what lies ahead;  
at the time of his fall he will find support.

## Notes

- **vv1–16** — the commandment *Honor your father and your mother* (Exodus 20:12) taught at length: honoring parents is an offering to God (vv3, 14), and its reward is children, long life and answered prayer (vv5–6).
- **vv3, 14, 30 "atones for sins… built up for you against your sins… charity atones for sins"** — *exilasetai*, the Greek word for the priests' atonement, used of a life of mercy. Proverbs says the same: *By loyal love and truth iniquity is atoned for* (16:6).
- **v5** — the Greek held here repeats the second line of v4 inside this verse; it is rendered as it stands.
- **vv19, 25** — Swete's Greek gives these verse numbers and no words (see the note on 1:5). The numbers are kept and the lines left out.
- **vv21–22 "Do not seek what is too hard for you… you have no need of what is hidden"** — Psalm 131:1, *I do not occupy myself with great matters*; Deuteronomy 29:29, *The hidden things belong to the LORD our God*.
- **v26** — the Greek held here gives the "stubborn heart" line twice, with two verbs, and v27 takes it up a third time.
- **v30 "charity"** — *eleēmosynē*, mercy shown in what is given, the "acts of charity" of Tobit (1:3; 4:10; 12:9). The same word is "kindness" in v14.

# Sirach 9

**1** Do not be jealous of the wife you embrace,  
and do not teach her an evil lesson to your own hurt.

**2** Do not give your soul to a woman,  
so that she sets her foot on your strength.

**3** Do not go to meet a woman who plays the prostitute,  
or you may fall into her snares.

**4** Do not keep company with a woman singer,  
or you may be caught by her tricks.

**5** Do not gaze at a virgin,  
or you may stumble and incur penalties for her.

**6** Do not give your soul to prostitutes,  
or you may lose your inheritance.

**7** Do not look around in the streets of the city,  
nor wander in its deserted places.

**8** Turn your eye away from a beautiful woman,  
and do not gaze at beauty that belongs to another;  
many have been led astray by a woman's beauty,  
and by it desire is kindled like a fire.

**9** Never sit at table with a married woman,  
nor drink wine with her at a feast,  
or your heart may turn aside to her,  
and in your spirit you slip into ruin.

**10** Do not forsake an old friend,  
for a new one is not equal to him.  
A new friend is new wine;  
when it has grown old, you will drink it with joy.

**11** Do not envy the glory of a sinner,  
for you do not know what his end will be.

**12** Do not delight in what pleases the ungodly;  
remember that all the way to Hades they will not be held guiltless.

**13** Keep far from a man who has power to kill,  
and you will not be troubled by the fear of death.  
If you do approach him, make no mistake,  
or he may take your life.  
Know that you are walking among snares,  
and strolling on the battlements of cities.

**14** As far as you can, take the measure of your neighbors,  
and consult with the wise.

**15** Let your conversation be with men of understanding,  
and let all your discussion be about the law of the Most High.

**16** Let righteous men be your companions at table,  
and let your boast be in the fear of the Lord.

**17** A work is praised for the hand of its craftsmen,  
and a leader of the people for the wisdom of his words.

**18** A loud-mouthed man is feared in his city,  
and the man who is rash in speech will be hated.

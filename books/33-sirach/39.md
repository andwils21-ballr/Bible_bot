# Sirach 39

**1** Not so the one who devotes his soul  
and ponders the law of the Most High:  
he will seek out the wisdom of all the ancients,  
and be occupied with prophecies;

**2** he will keep the stories of famous men,  
and enter into the twists of parables;

**3** he will seek out the hidden things of proverbs,  
and spend his time among the riddles of parables.

**4** He will serve among great men,  
and appear before a ruler;  
he will travel through the land of foreign nations,  
for he has tested good and evil among men.

**5** He will set his heart to rise early  
to the Lord who made him,  
and will plead before the Most High;  
he will open his mouth in prayer,  
and plead for his sins.

**6** If the great Lord wills,  
he will be filled with a spirit of understanding;  
he will rain down words of wisdom,  
and give thanks to the Lord in prayer.

**7** He will direct his counsel and knowledge,  
and ponder His hidden things.

**8** He will bring to light the discipline he teaches,  
and will boast in the law of the Lord's covenant.

**9** Many will praise his understanding;  
it will never be blotted out;  
his memory will not depart,  
and his name will live from generation to generation.

**10** Nations will tell of his wisdom,  
and the assembly will proclaim his praise.

**11** If he lives long, he will leave a name greater than a thousand;  
and if he goes to his rest, it is enough for him.

**12** Once more I will set out what I have pondered,  
for I am full like the moon at mid-month.

**13** Listen to me, holy sons,  
and blossom like a rose growing by a stream in the field;

**14** send out fragrance like frankincense,  
and blossom like a lily;  
spread fragrance and sing a song of praise;  
bless the Lord for all His works.

**15** Ascribe greatness to His name,  
and give thanks with His praise,  
with songs on your lips and with harps;  
and this is what you shall say in giving thanks:

**16** All the works of the Lord are very good,  
and every command of His will be done in its time.

**17** No one can say, "What is this? Why is that?"  
for everything will be sought out in its time.  
At His word the water stood like a heap,  
and at the word of His mouth, the storehouses of water.

**18** At His command is all His good pleasure,  
and no one can lessen His salvation.

**19** The works of all flesh are before Him,  
and nothing can be hidden from His eyes.

**20** From everlasting to everlasting He looks,  
and nothing is too wonderful for Him.

**21** No one can say, "What is this? Why is that?"  
for everything has been created for its own use.

**22** His blessing covers like a river,  
and like a flood it drenches the dry land.

**23** So the nations will inherit His wrath,  
as He turned waters into salt.

**24** His ways are straight for the holy;  
so they are stumbling blocks for the lawless.

**25** Good things were created for the good from the beginning;  
so evil things for sinners.

**26** The first needs of man's life  
are water and fire and iron and salt,  
fine wheat flour and honey and milk,  
the blood of the grape, and oil and clothing.

**27** All these are for good to the godly;  
so for sinners they will be turned to evil.

**28** There are winds created for vengeance,  
and in their fury they make their scourges firm;  
in the time of the end they will pour out their strength,  
and calm the anger of Him who made them.

**29** Fire and hail, famine and death,  
all these were created for vengeance;

**30** the teeth of wild beasts, and scorpions and vipers,  
and the sword that takes vengeance on the ungodly to destroy them.

**31** They will rejoice in His command,  
and be made ready on earth for their uses,  
and in their seasons they will not disobey His word.

**32** Therefore from the beginning I was resolved,  
and I pondered, and I left it in writing:

**33** All the works of the Lord are good,  
and He will supply every need in its hour.

**34** No one can say, "This is worse than that,"  
for everything will prove good in its season.

**35** So now with all the heart and with the mouth we have sung praise;  
bless the name of the Lord.

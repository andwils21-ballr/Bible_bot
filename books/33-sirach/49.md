# Sirach 49

**1** The memory of Josiah is like a blend of incense  
prepared by the perfumer's art;  
it is sweet as honey in every mouth,  
and like music at a banquet of wine.

**2** He was set right in turning the people back,  
and removed the detestable things of lawlessness.

**3** He set his heart straight toward the Lord;  
in the days of the lawless he made godliness strong.

**4** Except for David and Hezekiah and Josiah,  
they all committed offense;  
for they forsook the law of the Most High;  
the kings of Judah came to an end.

**5** They gave their horn to others,  
and their glory to a foreign nation,

**6** who set fire to the chosen city of the sanctuary,  
and laid waste its streets, by the hand of Jeremiah;

**7** for they had mistreated him,  
who was set apart in the womb as a prophet,  
to uproot and to afflict and to destroy,  
and likewise to build and to plant.

**8** Ezekiel saw the vision of glory  
that He showed him on the chariot of the cherubim.

**9** He remembered the enemies in the storm,  
and did good to those who keep their ways straight.

**10** May the bones of the twelve prophets  
flourish again from their place!  
They comforted Jacob,  
and redeemed them in the faithfulness of hope.

**11** How shall we magnify Zerubbabel?  
He was like a signet ring on the right hand;

**12** so too Jeshua son of Jozadak.  
In their days they built the house,  
and raised up a people holy to the Lord,  
prepared for everlasting glory.

**13** Far and wide they keep the memory  
of him who raised up our fallen walls,  
set up gates and bars,  
and rebuilt our homes.

**14** No one was ever created on earth like Enoch,  
for he was taken up from the earth.

**15** Joseph was born a man,  
leader of his brothers, the support of the people,  
and his bones were cared for.

**16** Shem and Seth were honored among men,  
and above every living thing in creation was Adam.

---

## Notes

- **v10 "the twelve prophets"** — *tōn ib' prophētōn*: the twelve shorter prophets, Hosea to Malachi, already counted as one collection. The wish for their bones is the one made for the judges (46:12).
- **v13 "him who raised up our fallen walls"** — Swete's Greek does not name him here; it is Nehemiah, who said *Come, let us build the wall of Jerusalem* (Nehemiah 2:17) and set up its doors (6:1).

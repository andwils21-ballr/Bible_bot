# Sirach 15

**1** Whoever fears the Lord will do this,  
and whoever holds fast to the law will gain her.

**2** She will come to meet him like a mother,  
and welcome him like the bride of his youth.

**3** She will feed him with the bread of understanding,  
and give him the water of wisdom to drink.

**4** He will lean on her and not fall;  
he will rely on her and not be put to shame.

**5** She will exalt him above his neighbors,  
and among the assembly she will open his mouth.

**6** He will inherit gladness and a crown of rejoicing,  
and an everlasting name.

**7** Foolish men will not attain her,  
and sinners will not see her.

**8** She is far from pride,  
and liars will never remember her.

**9** Praise is not fitting in the mouth of a sinner,  
for it has not been sent from the Lord.

**10** For praise is spoken in wisdom,  
and the Lord will prosper it.

**11** Do not say, "Because of the Lord I fell away";  
what He hates, you must not do.

**12** Do not say, "It was He who led me astray,"  
for He has no need of a sinful man.

**13** The Lord hates every detestable thing,  
and it is not loved by those who fear Him.

**14** It was He who made man in the beginning,  
and He left him in the power of his own counsel.

**15** If you are willing, you will keep the commandments,  
and to keep faith is for you to choose.

**16** He has set before you fire and water;  
stretch out your hand to whichever you wish.

**17** Before man are life and death,  
and whichever he chooses will be given him.

**18** For great is the wisdom of the Lord;  
He is mighty in power and sees all things;

**19** His eyes are on those who fear Him,  
and He knows every work of man.

**20** He has commanded no one to be ungodly,  
and He has given no one license to sin.

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "like a mother… like the bride of his youth… the bread of understanding… the water of wisdom"** — wisdom keeps her house and sets her table, as in Proverbs 9:1–5; Jesus speaks of the bread of life and the living water (John 4:10; 6:35).
- **v5 "among the assembly she will open his mouth"** — *en mesō ekklēsias*, the words of the Greek of Psalm 22:22, *among the congregation I will praise You*.
- **vv11–13 "Do not say, 'It was He who led me astray'"** — no one may blame God for his sin: *Let no one say when he is tempted, "I am being tempted by God"* (James 1:13). "What He hates, you must not do" — so Swete's Greek (*ou poiēseis*, "you will not do").
- **v14 "He left him in the power of his own counsel"** — *diaboulion*, a man's own deliberation and choice: God made man able to choose.
- **vv16–17 "fire and water… life and death"** — Moses' last charge: *I have set before you life and death, the blessing and the curse. Choose life* (Deuteronomy 30:19).
- **v20 "He has given no one license to sin"** — *anesis*, "relief, leave": God's patience is not permission.

# Obadiah 1

## The Pride of Edom

**1** The vision of Obadiah.  
This is what the Lord GOD says concerning Edom:  
We have heard a report from the LORD,  
and an envoy has been sent among the nations:  
"Rise up! Let us rise against her for battle!"

**2** I will make you small among the nations;  
you are utterly despised.

**3** The arrogance of your heart has deceived you,  
you who live in the clefts of the rock,  
whose dwelling is on the height,  
who say in your heart,  
"Who will bring me down to the ground?"

**4** Though you soar as high as the eagle,  
though your nest is set among the stars,  
from there I will bring you down,  
declares the LORD.

**5** If thieves came to you,  
if robbers by night —  
how you have been ruined! —  
would they not steal only what they wanted?  
If grape-gatherers came to you,  
would they not leave gleanings?

**6** How Esau has been searched out,  
his hidden treasures sought out!

**7** All the men of your covenant  
have sent you away to the border;  
the men of your peace have deceived you  
and prevailed over you;  
your bread they set as a trap beneath you.  
There is no understanding in him.

**8** On that day, declares the LORD,  
will I not destroy the wise men out of Edom,  
and understanding out of Mount Esau?

**9** Your warriors will be dismayed, Teman,  
so that every man will be cut off from Mount Esau  
by slaughter.

## Your Brother Jacob

**10** Because of the violence done to your brother Jacob,  
shame will cover you,  
and you will be cut off forever.

**11** On the day you stood aloof,  
on the day strangers carried off his wealth,  
and foreigners entered his gates  
and cast lots for Jerusalem,  
you too were like one of them.

**12** Do not gaze on the day of your brother,  
on the day of his misfortune;  
do not rejoice over the sons of Judah  
on the day of their ruin;  
do not boast  
on the day of distress.

**13** Do not enter the gate of My people  
on the day of their calamity;  
do not gaze, you too, on his evil  
on the day of his calamity;  
do not lay hands on his wealth  
on the day of his calamity.

**14** Do not stand at the crossroads  
to cut off those of his who escape;  
do not hand over his survivors  
on the day of distress.

## The Day of the LORD

**15** For the day of the LORD is near  
on all the nations.  
As you have done, it will be done to you;  
your deed will return on your own head.

**16** For as you drank on My holy mountain,  
all the nations will drink continually;  
they will drink and swallow,  
and be as though they had never been.

**17** But on Mount Zion there will be those who escape,  
and it will be holy;  
and the house of Jacob will possess their own possessions.

**18** The house of Jacob will be a fire,  
and the house of Joseph a flame,  
and the house of Esau stubble;  
they will burn among them and devour them,  
and there will be no survivor for the house of Esau;  
for the LORD has spoken.

**19** Those of the Negev will possess Mount Esau,  
and those of the lowland the Philistines;  
they will possess the field of Ephraim  
and the field of Samaria,  
and Benjamin will possess Gilead.

**20** The exiles of this host of the sons of Israel  
who are among the Canaanites as far as Zarephath,  
and the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad,  
will possess the cities of the Negev.

**21** Deliverers will go up on Mount Zion  
to judge Mount Esau,  
and the kingdom will belong to the LORD.

## Notes

- **v1 "The vision of Obadiah… concerning Edom"** — *Ovadyah*, **Obadiah**, is *eved*, **servant**, joined to the LORD's name; the book gives him no father, no town and no king. Its heading is Isaiah's, *The vision of Isaiah son of Amoz* (Isaiah 1:1). Edom is Esau's nation, *therefore his name was called Edom* (Genesis 25:30), and *Mount Esau* (vv8, 9, 19, 21) is the Mount Seir the LORD gave him, *I have given Mount Seir to Esau as a possession* (Deuteronomy 2:5). For *tsir*, **an envoy**, the Greek has *a siege*.
- **vv1–4 "We have heard a report… you who live in the clefts of the rock… from there I will bring you down"** — the same oracle stands in Jeremiah 49:14–16, with small differences, and the English follows Jeremiah's where the Hebrew is the same: *I have heard a report from the LORD, and an envoy has been sent among the nations… For I will make you small among the nations… the arrogance of your heart, you who live in the clefts of the rock… I will bring you down from there, declares the LORD*. Jeremiah has *I have heard*; Obadiah has *we*. Obadiah alone has *Who will bring me down to the ground?* and *among the stars*. *Sela*, **rock**, is also the name of Edom's town that Amaziah *took… in battle* (2 Kings 14:7).
- **v7 "the men of your peace have deceived you and prevailed over you; your bread they set as a trap"** — *yakhlu lekha anshei shelomekha*, nearly the words of the women's taunt to Zedekiah, *Your friends have misled you and prevailed over you* (Jeremiah 38:22), and *the men of your peace… your bread* is the betrayal of the psalm, *Even the man of my peace, in whom I trusted, who ate my bread, has lifted up his heel against me* (Psalm 41:9). *Mazor*, **trap**, is uncertain: elsewhere it is a **wound**, as in *Judah his wound* (Hosea 5:13); the Greek has *an ambush*.
- **vv17–21 "on Mount Zion there will be those who escape… the house of Esau stubble… the kingdom will belong to the LORD"** — *U-ve-har Tsiyyon tihyeh feletah* are the words Joel gives with *as the LORD has said* (Joel 2:32). *Morasheihem*, **their own possessions**; with other vowels, *those who possessed them*, as the Greek has it. Fire and flame and stubble are the burning of Joel 2:5. Zarephath *belongs to Sidon* (1 Kings 17:9); *Sepharad* is not named elsewhere, and the Greek has *Ephratha*; *ha-chel ha-zeh*, **this host**, is obscure, and the Greek has *the beginning*. *Moshi'im*, **deliverers**, as the LORD *raised up a deliverer* in the days of the judges (Judges 3:9); the Greek has *those who are rescued*. The last line is the psalm's, *For the kingdom belongs to the LORD* (Psalm 22:28).

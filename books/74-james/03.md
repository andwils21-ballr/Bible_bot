# James 3

## The Tongue Is a Fire

**1** Not many of you should become teachers, my brothers, for you know that we will receive a stricter judgment.

**2** For we all stumble in many ways. If anyone does not stumble in what he says, he is a perfect man, able to bridle his whole body as well.

**3** If we put bits into the mouths of horses so that they obey us, we guide their whole body as well.

**4** Take the ships also: though they are so large and are driven by strong winds, they are guided by a very small rudder wherever the will of the pilot directs.

**5** So also the tongue is a small member, yet it boasts of great things. How small a fire sets how great a forest ablaze!

**6** The tongue is a fire. The tongue is set among our members as a world of unrighteousness: it stains the whole body, sets on fire the wheel of existence, and is set on fire by Gehenna.

**7** For every kind of beast and bird, of reptile and sea creature, can be tamed and has been tamed by mankind,

**8** but no human being can tame the tongue. It is a restless evil, full of deadly poison.

**9** With it we bless the Lord and Father, and with it we curse people who are made in the likeness of God.

**10** From the same mouth come blessing and cursing. My brothers, these things ought not to be so.

**11** Does a spring pour out fresh and bitter water from the same opening?

**12** Can a fig tree, my brothers, bear olives, or a grapevine figs? Neither can salt water produce fresh.

## Wisdom from Above

**13** Who is wise and understanding among you? By his good conduct let him show his works in the meekness of wisdom.

**14** But if you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not boast and lie against the truth.

**15** This is not the wisdom that comes down from above, but is earthly, natural, demonic.

**16** For where there is jealousy and selfish ambition, there is disorder and every evil practice.

**17** But the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, open to reason, full of mercy and good fruits, impartial and sincere.

**18** The fruit of righteousness is sown in peace by those who make peace.

## Notes

- **v6 "sets on fire the wheel of existence"** — *ton trochon tēs geneseōs*, **the wheel of birth**: *genesis* is coming into being, the word of *his natural face*, **the face of his birth** (1:23). The wheel is the whole round of a life from its beginning, and the tongue can set all of it alight.
- **v9 "people who are made in the likeness of God"** — Genesis 1:26 in the Greek, *kath' homoiōsin*, *according to our likeness*. The same mouth blesses God and curses the people made like Him.

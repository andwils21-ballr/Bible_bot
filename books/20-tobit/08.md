# Tobit 8

## The Demon Driven Out

**1** When they had finished eating, they brought Tobias in to her.

**2** As he went, he remembered the words of Raphael, and he took the live ashes of incense and put the heart and the liver of the fish on them, and made smoke.

**3** When the demon smelled the odor, it fled to the farthest parts of Egypt, and the angel bound it.

## The Prayer of Tobias

**4** When the two of them were shut in together, Tobias got up from the bed and said, "Sister, get up, and let us pray that the Lord may have mercy on us."

**5** Tobias began to say, "Blessed are You, O God of our fathers, and blessed is Your holy and glorious name forever. Let the heavens and all Your creation bless You.

**6** You made Adam and gave him Eve his wife as a helper and support. From them the race of mankind has come. You said, 'It is not good for the man to be alone; let us make him a helper like himself.'

**7** And now, O Lord, I am not taking this sister of mine because of lust, but in truth. Command that I may find mercy and that we may grow old together."

**8** She said with him, "Amen."

## Raguel's Grave

**9** They both slept through the night. Raguel got up and went and dug a grave,

**10** saying, "Perhaps this one will die too."

**11** Then Raguel came into his house,

**12** and said to Edna his wife, "Send one of the maids to see whether he is alive. If he is not, let us bury him so that no one knows."

**13** The maid opened the door and went in, and found the two of them asleep.

**14** She came out and told them that he was alive.

**15** Raguel blessed God, saying, "Blessed are You, O God, with every blessing. Let Your holy ones and all Your creation bless You. Let all Your angels and Your chosen ones bless You forever.

**16** Blessed are You, because You have made me glad. It has not happened to me as I feared, but You have dealt with us according to Your great mercy.

**17** Blessed are You, because You have had mercy on two only children. Show them mercy, O Master, and bring their lives to completion in health, with gladness and mercy."

**18** Then he ordered his servants to fill in the grave.

## The Wedding Feast

**19** He gave them a wedding feast of fourteen days.

**20** Before the days of the wedding were over, Raguel made him swear not to leave until the fourteen days of the wedding were complete,

**21** and then to take half of his property and go back in health to his father; "and the rest," he said, "when my wife and I die."

## Notes

- **v3 "it fled to the farthest parts of Egypt, and the angel bound it"** — the binding Raphael was sent to do (3:17).
- **v6 "It is not good for the man to be alone; let us make him a helper like himself"** — the words of Genesis in its Greek: *let us make him a helper corresponding to him* (Genesis 2:18). Tobias prays the first marriage over his own.
- **v7 "not because of lust, but in truth"** — *porneia*, the word of Tobit's warning (4:12). The son keeps his father's charge on the night it is tested.
- **vv9–18 "Raguel got up and went and dug a grave"** — in a book that began with Tobit burying the dead, the bride's father digs a grave for the groom, and fills it in again at dawn.
- **vv15–17 "Blessed are You… because You have had mercy on two only children"** — *monogeneis*, both of them the only child of their parents (3:15; 6:15).

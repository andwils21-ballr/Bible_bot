# Tobit 3

## Tobit's Prayer

**1** Then in my grief I wept, and in my anguish I prayed, saying,

**2** "You are righteous, O Lord, and all Your works and all Your ways are mercy and truth, and You judge with true and righteous judgment forever.

**3** Remember me and look on me. Do not punish me for my sins and for my unknowing errors, and those of my fathers, which they sinned before You.

**4** For they disobeyed Your commandments, and You gave us over to plunder, to captivity and to death, a byword of reproach among all the nations among whom we have been scattered.

**5** Now Your many judgments are true in dealing with me for my sins and those of my fathers, because we did not keep Your commandments, for we did not walk in truth before You.

**6** So now deal with me as it pleases You. Command my spirit to be taken from me, so that I may be released and become earth. It is better for me to die than to live, because I have heard false reproaches, and there is great sorrow in me. Command that I be released from this distress now to the everlasting place. Do not turn Your face away from me."

## Sarah in Ecbatana

**7** On that same day it happened to Sarah, the daughter of Raguel, in Ecbatana of Media, that she also was reproached by her father's maids,

**8** because she had been given to seven husbands, and Asmodeus the evil demon had killed each of them before they had been with her as with a wife. The maids said to her, "Do you not know that you strangle your husbands? You have already had seven, and you have not had the benefit of one of them.

**9** Why do you beat us? If they are dead, go with them! May we never see a son or daughter of yours!"

**10** When she heard these things, she was so grieved that she thought of hanging herself. But she said, "I am my father's only child. If I do this, it will be a disgrace to him, and I will bring his old age down in sorrow to Hades."

## Sarah's Prayer

**11** So she prayed by the window and said, "Blessed are You, O Lord my God, and blessed is Your holy and honored name forever. May all Your works bless You forever.

**12** And now, O Lord, I have set my eyes and my face toward You.

**13** Command that I be released from the earth, so that I may no longer hear reproach.

**14** You know, O Lord, that I am innocent of any sin with a man,

**15** and that I have not defiled my name or the name of my father in the land of my captivity. I am my father's only child, and he has no child to be his heir, and no near kinsman or son of his to whom I should keep myself as a wife. Already seven husbands of mine have died. Why should I live? But if it does not please You to kill me, command that You look on me and have mercy on me, and that I no longer hear reproach."

## Raphael Sent

**16** The prayer of both was heard before the glory of the great God,

**17** and Raphael was sent to heal the two of them: to scale away the white films from Tobit's eyes, and to give Sarah the daughter of Raguel as a wife to Tobias the son of Tobit, and to bind Asmodeus the evil demon, because Tobias had the right to inherit her. At that very time Tobit came back and went into his house, and Sarah the daughter of Raguel came down from her upper room.

## Notes

- **v2 "all Your ways are mercy and truth"** — *eleēmosynai*, the word of Tobit's own *acts of charity* (1:3; 2:14). What he gave to his brothers, he names in God.
- **v6 "It is better for me to die than to live"** — the prayer of Elijah under the broom tree, *Now, LORD, take my life* (1 Kings 19:4), and of Jonah, *it is better for me to die than to live* (Jonah 4:3).
- **v7 "On that same day"** — two people in two cities, Nineveh and Ecbatana, pray to die on the same day, and the two stories become one.
- **v10 "I will bring his old age down in sorrow to Hades"** — Jacob's words when he would not let Benjamin go: *you will bring my gray head down in sorrow to Sheol* (Genesis 42:38). *Hades* is the Greek for Sheol, and Tobit has the words of the Greek Genesis.
- **v11 "she prayed by the window"** — as Daniel prayed *in his upper room, with its windows open toward Jerusalem* (Daniel 6:10).
- **vv16–17 "before the glory of the great God, and Raphael was sent"** — the Greek held here reads *before the glory of the great Raphael, and was sent*; the longer Greek has *before the glory of God*. The rendering follows the longer Greek for *God*. *Raphael* is a Hebrew name, **God heals**, and he is sent *to heal* the two.
- **v17 "because Tobias had the right to inherit her"** — as the nearest kinsman. The law kept a daughter who inherits within her father's tribe (Numbers 36:8).

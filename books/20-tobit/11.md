# Tobit 11

## The Return

**1** After this Tobias went on his way, blessing God that He had given him a good journey, and he blessed Raguel and Edna his wife. He went on until he came near Nineveh.

**2** Raphael said to Tobias, "Brother, do you not know how you left your father?

**3** Let us run ahead of your wife and prepare the house.

**4** Take the gall of the fish in your hand." So they went on, and the dog came along behind them.

**5** Anna sat looking down the road for her son.

**6** She saw him coming, and she said to his father, "Your son is coming, and the man who went with him!"

**7** Raphael said, "I know that your father will open his eyes.

**8** Rub the gall on his eyes. It will sting, and he will rub them and throw off the white films, and he will see you."

**9** Anna ran to her son and fell on his neck, and said to him, "I have seen you, my child. Now I can die." Then they both wept.

## The Healing

**10** Tobit came out toward the door, stumbling, but his son ran to him

**11** and took hold of his father, and he sprinkled the gall on his father's eyes, saying, "Take courage, father."

**12** When they stung, he rubbed his eyes,

**13** and the white films peeled away from the corners of his eyes. When he saw his son, he fell on his neck,

**14** and he wept and said, "Blessed are You, O God, and blessed is Your name forever, and blessed are all Your holy angels.

**15** For You have scourged me, and You have had mercy on me. I see my son Tobias!" His son went in rejoicing and told his father the great things that had happened to him in Media.

## The Bride Welcomed

**16** Tobit went out to meet his daughter-in-law at the gate of Nineveh, rejoicing and blessing God. Those who saw him walking marveled that he could see.

**17** Tobit gave thanks before them that God had had mercy on him. When Tobit came near Sarah his daughter-in-law, he blessed her, saying, "Welcome, my daughter. Blessed is God who has brought you to us, and blessed are your father and your mother." There was joy among all his brothers in Nineveh.

**18** Ahikar and his nephew Nasbas came,

**19** and Tobias's wedding was celebrated with joy for seven days.

## Notes

- **v4 "the dog came along behind them"** — the dog that set out with them (5:17) comes home with them.
- **v9 "I have seen you, my child. Now I can die"** — the words of Jacob to Joseph in Egypt: *Now let me die, since I have seen your face* (Genesis 46:30, in the Greek *I will die from now on*).
- **vv10–13 "stumbling… the white films peeled away"** — *elepisthē*, the verb of 3:17, where Raphael was sent *to scale away* the films. What was prayed for in chapter 3 happens in the words of chapter 3.
- **v15 "You have scourged me, and You have had mercy on me"** — the confession Tobit will put into his song: *He scourges and He shows mercy* (13:2).
- **v16 "Tobit went out to meet his daughter-in-law… they marveled that he could see"** — the Greek held here names *Tobias*; the longer Greek names no one, and the verse is about the man who now can see. The rendering names Tobit.
- **v18 "Ahikar and his nephew Nasbas"** — Tobit's nephew, who cared for him in his blindness (2:10) comes to the wedding.

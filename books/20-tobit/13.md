# Tobit 13

## "He Scourges and Shows Mercy"

**1** Then Tobit wrote a prayer of rejoicing and said:  
"Blessed is God who lives forever,  
and blessed is His kingdom.

**2** For He scourges and He shows mercy;  
He brings down to Hades and brings up again,  
and there is no one who can escape His hand.

**3** Give thanks to Him, sons of Israel, before the nations,  
for He has scattered us among them.

**4** There make known His greatness;  
exalt Him before every living thing,  
for He is our Lord,  
and He is our God and our Father forever.

**5** He will scourge us for our wrongdoings,  
and He will have mercy again,  
and will gather us from all the nations  
among whom you have been scattered.

**6** If you turn to Him with all your heart and with all your soul,  
to do what is true before Him,  
then He will turn to you  
and will not hide His face from you.  
See what He will do with you,  
and give thanks to Him with your whole body.  
Bless the Lord of righteousness,  
and exalt the King of the ages.  
In the land of my captivity I give Him thanks,  
and I show His strength and His greatness to a nation of sinners.  
Turn back, you sinners, and do what is right before Him.  
Who knows whether He will accept you and show you mercy?

**7** I exalt my God,  
and my soul exalts the King of heaven,  
and will rejoice in His greatness.

**8** Let all speak,  
and give thanks to Him in Jerusalem.

## Jerusalem

**9** Jerusalem, holy city,  
He will scourge you for the deeds of your sons,  
but He will again have mercy on the sons of the righteous.

**10** Give thanks to the Lord worthily,  
and bless the King of the ages,  
so that His tent may be built in you again with joy.  
May He make glad in you those who are captives,  
and love in you those who are in distress,  
for all generations forever.

**11** Many nations will come from far away to the name of the Lord God,  
bearing gifts in their hands, gifts for the King of heaven.  
Generations of generations will give you rejoicing.

**12** Cursed are all who hate you;  
blessed forever will be all who love you.

**13** Rejoice and be glad over the sons of the righteous,  
for they will be gathered together  
and will bless the Lord of the righteous.

**14** How blessed are those who love you!  
They will rejoice in your peace.  
Blessed are all who grieved over all your afflictions,  
for they will rejoice over you  
when they see all your glory,  
and they will be glad forever.  
My soul rejoices forever.

**15** Bless God, the great King,

**16** for Jerusalem will be built with sapphire and emerald,  
your walls with precious stone,  
and your towers and battlements with pure gold.

**17** The streets of Jerusalem will be paved  
with beryl and ruby and stone of Ophir.

**18** All her lanes will cry, 'Hallelujah!' and give praise, saying,  
'Blessed is God, who has exalted you forever.'"

## Notes

- **v2 "He scourges and He shows mercy; He brings down to Hades and brings up again"** — the song of Hannah: *The LORD kills and makes alive; He brings down to Sheol and raises up* (1 Samuel 2:6). *Hades* is the Greek for Sheol. Tobit has lived it: *You have scourged me, and You have had mercy on me* (11:15).
- **v6 "Who knows whether He will accept you and show you mercy?"** — *eleēmosynē*, the book's word, here of what God does: the charity Tobit gave is what he hopes God will give.
- **v10 "so that His tent may be built in you again"** — *skēnē*, the tent of the wilderness, the name for the house that is to be rebuilt.
- **v11 "Many nations will come from far away… bearing gifts"** — Isaiah's vision: *Nations shall come to your light… they shall bring gold and frankincense* (Isaiah 60:3, 6).
- **vv16–17 "built with sapphire and emerald… streets… paved"** — Isaiah's promise to the afflicted city, *I will lay your foundations with sapphires… your gates of carbuncles* (Isaiah 54:11–12). Revelation's city is built of the same stones (Revelation 21:18–21).
- **v18 "All her lanes will cry, 'Hallelujah!'"** — *allēlouia*, the Hebrew **Praise the LORD**, kept as a word in the Greek.

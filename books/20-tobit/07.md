# Tobit 7

## The Welcome

**1** They came to Ecbatana and arrived at the house of Raguel, and Sarah came to meet them. She greeted them and they greeted her, and she brought them into the house.

**2** Raguel said to Edna his wife, "How much the young man looks like my cousin Tobit!"

**3** Raguel asked them, "Where are you from, brothers?" They said to him, "We are of the sons of Naphtali, the captives in Nineveh."

**4** He said to them, "Do you know our brother Tobit?"

**5** They said, "He is alive and well." Tobias said, "He is my father."

**6** Raguel sprang up and kissed him and wept,

**7** and he blessed him and said to him, "You are the son of a good and noble man!" When he heard that Tobit had lost his sight, he was grieved and wept.

**8** Edna his wife and Sarah his daughter also wept, and they received them warmly.

## The Marriage

**9** They slaughtered a ram from the flock and set out many dishes. Then Tobias said to Raphael, "Brother Azarias, speak about the things you talked of on the way, and let the matter be settled."

**10** So he passed the word to Raguel. Raguel said to Tobias, "Eat, drink and be merry, for it is right for you to take my child. But I will show you the truth.

**11** I have given my child to seven men, and whenever they went in to her, they died in the night. But for now, be merry." Tobias said, "I will taste nothing here until you make an agreement and stand by it with me." Raguel said, "Take her from now on, according to the law. You are her kinsman, and she is yours. The merciful God will give you both the best of success."

**12** He called his daughter Sarah, took her by the hand and gave her to Tobias as his wife, and said, "Take her according to the law of Moses, and lead her away to your father." Then he blessed them.

**13** He called Edna his wife, and he took a scroll and wrote out a contract, and they set their seals to it.

**14** Then they began to eat.

**15** Raguel called Edna his wife and said to her, "Sister, prepare the other room and take her into it."

**16** She did as he said and took her there, and Sarah wept. Her mother received her daughter's tears and said to her,

**17** "Take courage, my child. May the Lord of heaven and earth give you joy in place of this sorrow of yours. Take courage, my daughter."

## Notes

- **v2 "How much the young man looks like my cousin Tobit"** — *anepsios*, a cousin or near kinsman. Raguel sees the father in the son's face before he knows whose son he is.
- **v11 "I will taste nothing here until you make an agreement"** — as Abraham's servant said in the house of Rebekah's family, *I will not eat until I have spoken my words* (Genesis 24:33): the marriage is settled before the meal.
- **v11 "You are her kinsman"** — *adelphos*, **brother**, used of a near relative, as Tobit calls his wife *sister* (5:21) and Raguel calls Edna *sister* (v15).
- **v13 "he took a scroll and wrote out a contract"** — *syngraphē*, a written marriage contract, sealed by both parents.
- **v17 "May the Lord of heaven and earth give you joy in place of this sorrow"** — *charin*, **grace, joy**. A mother sends her daughter into the room where seven husbands died.

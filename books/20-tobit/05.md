# Tobit 5

## Tobias Finds a Guide

**1** Tobias answered him, "Father, I will do everything you have commanded me.

**2** But how can I get the money, when I do not know him?"

**3** So he gave him the signed receipt and said to him, "Find yourself a man to go with you, and I will pay him wages while I live. Go and get the money."

**4** He went to look for a man, and he found Raphael, who was an angel, but he did not know it.

**5** He said to him, "Can I go with you to Rages in Media? Do you know the region?"

**6** The angel said to him, "I will go with you. I know the way, and I have lodged with Gabael our brother."

**7** Tobias said to him, "Wait for me, and I will tell my father."

**8** He said to him, "Go, and do not be long."

**9** He went in and said to his father, "I have found someone who will go with me." He said, "Call him to me, so that I may learn what tribe he is from and whether he can be trusted to go with you."

**10** So he called him, and he came in, and they greeted each other.

**11** Tobit said to him, "Brother, what tribe and what family are you from? Tell me."

**12** He said to him, "Are you looking for a tribe and a family, or for a hired man to go with your son?" Tobit said to him, "Brother, I want to know your family and your name."

**13** He said, "I am Azarias, son of Ananias the great, one of your brothers."

**14** Tobit said to him, "Welcome, brother. Do not be angry with me because I wanted to know your tribe and your family. You are my brother, of a good and noble line. I knew Ananias and Jathan, the sons of Shemaiah the great, when we went together to Jerusalem to worship, bringing the firstborn and the tithes of the crops, and they did not go astray when our brothers went astray. You come of good stock, brother.

**15** But tell me, what wages shall I pay you? A drachma a day, and your expenses, as for my son?

**16** I will add something to your wages if you both come back safe and sound."

**17** So they agreed. Then he said to Tobias, "Get ready for the journey, and may you have a good journey." His son prepared what he needed for the journey, and his father said to him, "Go with this man. God who lives in heaven will prosper your way, and may His angel go with you." So they both went out to set off, and the young man's dog went with them.

## Anna's Tears

**18** But Anna his mother wept and said to Tobit, "Why have you sent our child away? Is he not the staff of our hand as he goes in and out before us?

**19** Do not add money to money. Let it be as refuse compared with our child.

**20** For the life the Lord has given us to live is enough for us."

**21** Tobit said to her, "Do not worry, my sister. He will come back safe and sound, and your eyes will see him.

**22** For a good angel will go with him, his journey will prosper, and he will come back safe and sound."

## Notes

- **v4 "he found Raphael, who was an angel, but he did not know it"** — the reader is told at once what Tobias and his father do not know. Every word they say to him after this has a second meaning.
- **v13 "I am Azarias, son of Ananias the great"** — Hebrew names: *Azariah*, **the LORD helps**, son of *Hananiah*, **the LORD is gracious**. The angel's false name says what he is there to do.
- **v17 "may His angel go with you… and the young man's dog went with them"** — Tobit prays for an angel to go with his son, not knowing one stands in front of him. The dog goes too, and is with Tobias again on the way home (11:4).
- **v19 "Let it be as refuse compared with our child"** — *peripsēma*, **what is scraped off**, the word Paul uses of the apostles, *the scum of all things* (1 Corinthians 4:13).
- **v22 "a good angel will go with him"** — Tobit says it to comfort his wife; it is literally true.

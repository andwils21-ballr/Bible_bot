# Tobit 10

## Tobit and Anna Wait

**1** Tobit his father was counting each day. When the days for the journey had passed and he did not come,

**2** he said, "Can he have been put to shame? Or can Gabael have died, and there is no one to give him the money?"

**3** He was very distressed.

**4** His wife said to him, "The child has perished, because he is so long in coming." She began to mourn for him and said,

**5** "Do I not care, my child, that I let you go, you who are the light of my eyes?"

**6** Tobit said to her, "Be quiet. Do not worry. He is well."

**7** She said to him, "Be quiet. Do not deceive me. My child has perished." Every day she went out to the road by which he had gone. By day she ate no bread, and through the nights she did not stop mourning for her son Tobias, until the fourteen days of the wedding were over that Raguel had sworn to have him spend there. Then Tobias said to Raguel, "Let me go, for my father and my mother no longer hope to see me."

**8** His father-in-law said to him, "Stay with me, and I will send to your father, and they will tell him how things are with you."

**9** But Tobias said, "Let me go to my father."

**10** So Raguel got up and gave him Sarah his wife and half of his property, servants and cattle and money.

**11** He blessed them and sent them away, saying, "The God of heaven will give you a good journey, my children, before I die."

**12** He said to his daughter, "Honor your father-in-law and your mother-in-law. They are now your parents. May I hear a good report of you." Then he kissed her. Edna said to Tobias, "Dear brother, may the Lord of heaven bring you back safely, and may He grant me to see your children by my daughter Sarah, so that I may rejoice before the Lord. I am entrusting my daughter to you in trust. Do not grieve her."

## Notes

- **v1 "Tobit his father was counting each day"** — as Tobias said he would be (9:4). The book moves between the two houses, the wedding in Ecbatana and the waiting in Nineveh.
- **v5 "you who are the light of my eyes"** — Anna says it to a son who is not there, in a house where her husband cannot see.
- **v7 "Every day she went out to the road by which he had gone"** — she keeps watch there until she sees him coming (11:5–6).
- **v10 "servants and cattle"** — *sōmata kai ktēnē*, **bodies and cattle**: *bodies* is the word for slaves.
- **v12 "I am entrusting my daughter to you in trust"** — *paratithemai… en parakatathēkē*, the words used of Tobit's money *left in trust* with Gabael (1:14; 4:20). The book began with silver given in trust; it ends its journey with a daughter given in trust.

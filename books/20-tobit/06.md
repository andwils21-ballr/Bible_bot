# Tobit 6

## The Fish

**1** So she stopped weeping.

**2** As they went on their way, they came in the evening to the Tigris River, and they camped there.

**3** The young man went down to wash himself, and a fish leaped up out of the river and tried to swallow the young man.

**4** The angel said to him, "Take hold of the fish." The young man seized the fish and threw it up on the land.

**5** The angel said to him, "Cut the fish open, and take the heart, the liver and the gall, and keep them safe."

**6** The young man did as the angel told him. They roasted the fish and ate it, and the two of them went on until they came near Ecbatana.

**7** The young man said to the angel, "Brother Azarias, what are the liver, the heart and the gall of the fish for?"

**8** He said to him, "As for the heart and the liver, if a demon or an evil spirit troubles anyone, you burn them before the man or woman, and the person will never be troubled again.

**9** As for the gall, anoint a man who has white films on his eyes, and he will be healed."

## Sarah Promised

**10** When they drew near to Ecbatana,

**11** the angel said to the young man, "Brother, today we will stay with Raguel. He is your relative, and he has a daughter named Sarah.

**12** I will speak about her, that she be given to you as your wife, for her inheritance falls to you, and you alone are of her family. The girl is beautiful and sensible.

**13** So now listen to me, and I will speak to her father, and when we come back from Rages we will celebrate the marriage. For I know that Raguel cannot give her to another man according to the law of Moses without incurring death, because the right to the inheritance falls to you rather than to any other man."

**14** Then the young man said to the angel, "Brother Azarias, I have heard that the girl has been given to seven husbands and that they all died in the bridal chamber.

**15** Now I am my father's only son, and I am afraid that if I go in to her I will die as those before me did, for a demon loves her, and it harms no one except those who come near her. So now I am afraid that I will die and bring my father's and my mother's life down to their grave in sorrow over me. They have no other son to bury them."

**16** The angel said to him, "Do you not remember the words your father commanded you, that you should take a wife from your own family? So now listen to me, brother, for she will be your wife. Do not give the demon another thought, for this very night she will be given to you as your wife.

**17** When you go into the bridal chamber, take live ashes of incense and put some of the heart and the liver of the fish on them, and make smoke.

**18** The demon will smell it and flee, and will never come back again. When you go to her, rise up, both of you, and cry out to the merciful God, and He will save you and have mercy on you. Do not be afraid, for she was set apart for you from the beginning. You will save her, and she will go with you, and I suppose you will have children by her." When Tobias heard these things, he loved her, and his soul clung to her greatly.

## Notes

- **vv3–4 "a fish leaped up out of the river and tried to swallow the young man"** — what threatens to swallow him becomes the means of healing his father and freeing his bride.
- **vv8–9 "the heart and the liver… the gall"** — the angel gives the remedies before the reader knows what they will be for: one for Sarah's demon (3:8), one for Tobit's *white films* (2:10).
- **vv6, 10 "until they came near Ecbatana… When they drew near to Ecbatana"** — Raguel lives in Ecbatana (3:7). In v10 the Greek held here reads *Rages*, where the money is, and the angel speaks of going on to Rages afterward (v13). The longer Greek has *Ecbatana*, which the rendering follows.
- **v13 "according to the law of Moses"** — the law that kept an heiress's inheritance within her father's tribe (Numbers 36:8).
- **v15 "a demon loves her"** — the Greek says *philei*, **loves**. Sarah's affliction is a jealous demon, and it kills only those who come near her.
- **v18 "she was set apart for you from the beginning… his soul clung to her greatly"** — *ekollēthē*, the verb of Genesis, *a man… clings to his woman* (Genesis 2:24 in the Greek). Tobias loves her before he has seen her.

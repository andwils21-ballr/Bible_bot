# Tobit 9

## Raphael Goes to Gabael

**1** Tobias called Raphael and said to him,

**2** "Brother Azarias, take a servant and two camels with you, and go to Gabael at Rages in Media, and bring me the money, and bring him to the wedding.

**3** For Raguel has sworn that I must not leave,

**4** and my father is counting the days, and if I am long delayed, he will be very distressed."

**5** So Raphael went and stayed overnight with Gabael, and gave him the signed receipt. Gabael brought out the bags with their seals intact and gave them to him.

**6** They set out together early in the morning and came to the wedding, and Tobias blessed his wife.

---

## Notes

- **v2 "bring me the money, and bring him to the wedding"** — the errand the whole journey was for is done in four verses, by the angel, while the young man stays at his wedding.
- **v4 "my father is counting the days"** — the next chapter opens with Tobit doing just that (10:1).
- **v5 "the bags with their seals intact"** — the ten talents left in trust in 1:14, returned untouched after all Tobit's misfortunes.
- **v6 "Tobias blessed his wife"** — so the shorter Greek. The longer Greek has Gabael come in, find Tobias at table, and bless him and his wife.

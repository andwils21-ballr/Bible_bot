# Tobit 4

## "Remember the Lord"

**1** On that day Tobit remembered the money that he had left in trust with Gabael at Rages in Media,

**2** and he said to himself, "I have asked for death. Why do I not call my son Tobias and tell him about it before I die?"

**3** So he called him and said, "My child, when I die, bury me, and do not neglect your mother. Honor her all the days of your life. Do what pleases her, and do not grieve her.

**4** Remember, my child, that she faced many dangers for you while you were in her womb. When she dies, bury her beside me in one grave.

**5** My child, remember the Lord our God all your days, and do not set your will to sin or to break His commandments. Do what is right all the days of your life, and do not walk in the ways of wrongdoing.

**6** For if you act in truth, you will prosper in all your works, as do all who do what is right.

## Charity

**7** Give charity from what you have, and do not let your eye begrudge it when you give. Do not turn your face away from any poor man, and the face of God will not be turned away from you.

**8** If you have much, give from it in proportion. If you have little, do not be afraid to give according to the little you have.

**9** So you will be storing up a good treasure for yourself against the day of need.

**10** For charity delivers from death and keeps one from going into the darkness.

**11** Charity is a good offering in the sight of the Most High for all who give it.

## Marriage and Conduct

**12** Beware, my child, of all sexual immorality. Above all take a wife from the descendants of your fathers. Do not take a foreign wife, who is not of your father's tribe, for we are the sons of the prophets. Remember, my child, Noah, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, our fathers of old: they all took wives from among their own kindred, and they were blessed in their children, and their descendants will inherit the land.

**13** So now, my child, love your brothers, and do not in your heart be too proud toward your brothers and the sons and daughters of your people to take a wife for yourself from among them. For in pride there is ruin and much confusion, and in idleness there is loss and great want, for idleness is the mother of famine.

**14** Do not keep back overnight the wages of any man who works for you, but pay him at once. If you serve God, you will be repaid. Watch yourself, my child, in all that you do, and be disciplined in all your conduct.

**15** What you hate, do not do to anyone. Do not drink wine to drunkenness, and do not let drunkenness go with you on your way.

**16** Give of your bread to the hungry and of your clothing to the naked. Give all your surplus as charity, and do not let your eye begrudge it when you give.

**17** Pour out your bread on the grave of the righteous, but give none to sinners.

**18** Seek advice from every wise man, and do not despise any useful counsel.

**19** At all times bless the Lord God, and ask Him that your ways may be made straight and that all your paths and plans may prosper. For no nation has counsel of its own; the Lord Himself gives all good things, and He humbles whom He wills, as He wills. So now, my child, remember my commands, and do not let them be blotted out of your heart.

## The Money in Media

**20** And now I tell you about the ten talents of silver that I left in trust with Gabael son of Gabrias at Rages in Media.

**21** Do not be afraid, my child, because we have become poor. You have great wealth if you fear God and turn away from all sin and do what is pleasing in His sight."

## Notes

- **vv3–4 "Honor her all the days of your life… bury her beside me in one grave"** — the commandment, *Honor your father and your mother* (Exodus 20:12), given its last duty: a grave shared.
- **vv7–11 "charity"** — *eleēmosynē*, the book's word (1:3), five times in five verses. *Charity delivers from death* (v10) is the proverb, *righteousness delivers from death* (Proverbs 10:2), with *charity* where Proverbs has *righteousness*.
- **v7 "Do not turn your face away from any poor man, and the face of God will not be turned away from you"** — a measure for measure: the face turned to the poor is answered by God's face.
- **v12 "for we are the sons of the prophets… Noah, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob"** — the fathers are called prophets, and marriage within the family is set beside the blessing of their children. Sarah, the bride the story is preparing, is of Tobit's own kindred (3:17).
- **v14 "Do not keep back overnight the wages"** — the law: *The wages of a hired man shall not stay with you overnight until morning* (Leviticus 19:13).
- **v15 "What you hate, do not do to anyone"** — the rule in its negative form. Jesus gives it in its positive form: *whatever you want people to do to you, do the same to them* (Matthew 7:12).
- **v16 "Give of your bread to the hungry and of your clothing to the naked"** — Isaiah's fast: *to share your bread with the hungry… when you see the naked, to cover him* (Isaiah 58:7). It is what Tobit did (1:17).
- **v17 "Pour out your bread on the grave of the righteous"** — the sense is uncertain: perhaps food given to the family of a righteous man who has died, at the meal of mourning.

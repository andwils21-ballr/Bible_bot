# Tobit 12

## The Wages

**1** Tobit called his son Tobias and said to him, "My child, see to the wages of the man who went with you, and you must give him something more."

**2** He said, "Father, it does me no harm to give him half of what I have brought back,

**3** for he has brought me back to you safe and sound, he healed my wife, he brought my money, and he healed you as well."

**4** The old man said, "It is his due."

**5** So he called the angel and said to him, "Take half of all that you two have brought back."

## The Angel's Words

**6** Then he called the two of them aside privately and said to them, "Bless God and give thanks to Him. Ascribe greatness to Him, and give thanks to Him before all the living for what He has done for you. It is good to bless God and to exalt His name, making known with honor the works of God. Do not be slow to give Him thanks.

**7** It is good to keep a king's secret, but to reveal the works of God gloriously. Do good, and evil will not find you.

**8** Prayer is good with fasting, charity and righteousness. A little with righteousness is better than much with wrongdoing. It is better to give charity than to store up gold.

**9** Charity delivers from death and will purge away every sin. Those who give charity and do righteous deeds will be filled with life,

**10** but those who sin are enemies of their own lives.

**11** I will not hide anything from you. I have said, 'It is good to keep a king's secret, but to reveal the works of God gloriously.'

**12** So now, when you prayed, you and your daughter-in-law Sarah, I brought the record of your prayer before the Holy One, and when you buried the dead, I was with you likewise.

**13** When you did not hesitate to get up and leave your dinner to go and lay out the dead, your good deed did not escape me; I was with you.

**14** And now God has sent me to heal you and your daughter-in-law Sarah.

**15** I am Raphael, one of the seven holy angels who present the prayers of the saints and go in before the glory of the Holy One."

**16** The two of them were shaken and fell on their faces, for they were afraid.

**17** He said to them, "Do not be afraid; you will have peace. Bless God forever.

**18** For I came not by any favor of my own, but by the will of your God. So bless Him forever.

**19** All these days I appeared to you, but I did not eat or drink; you were seeing a vision.

**20** Now give thanks to God, for I am going up to Him who sent me. Write in a book everything that has happened."

**21** They stood up, and they did not see him anymore.

**22** They gave thanks for His great and wonderful works, and that the angel of the Lord had appeared to them.

## Notes

- **vv7, 11 "It is good to keep a king's secret, but to reveal the works of God gloriously"** — said twice. Proverbs says the reverse of a king: *It is the glory of God to conceal a thing, but the glory of kings to search out a thing* (Proverbs 25:2). The angel has kept his own secret until now, and now reveals the works of God.
- **vv8–9 "Charity delivers from death and will purge away every sin"** — Tobit's charge to his son (4:10), now in the angel's mouth, with more: *purge away every sin*.
- **vv12–13 "I brought the record of your prayer before the Holy One… when you buried the dead, I was with you"** — *mnēmosynon*, **a memorial**, the word the angel speaks to Cornelius: *Your prayers and your alms have gone up as a memorial before God* (Acts 10:4). The burial at the Feast of Weeks (2:3–7), which cost Tobit his sight, was seen.
- **v15 "one of the seven holy angels who present the prayers of the saints"** — Revelation sees *the seven angels who stand before God*, and the incense rising *with the prayers of the saints* (Revelation 8:2–4).
- **v19 "I did not eat or drink; you were seeing a vision"** — the book had them roast the fish and eat it together (6:6). The angel says now that what they saw him do was appearance.
- **v20 "Write in a book everything that has happened"** — the book gives its own reason for being written.

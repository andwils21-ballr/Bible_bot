# Tobit 14

## Tobit's Charge

**1** So Tobit finished giving thanks.

**2** He was fifty-eight years old when he lost his sight, and after eight years he saw again. He kept giving charity, and he went on fearing the Lord God and giving thanks to Him.

**3** When he had grown very old, he called his son and his son's sons, and said to him, "My child, take your sons. I have grown old, and I am about to depart from this life.

**4** Go to Media, my child, for I am convinced that what Jonah the prophet said about Nineveh, that it will be overthrown, will come true. In Media there will be peace for a time. Our brothers will be scattered over the earth from the good land, and Jerusalem will be desolate, and the house of God in it will be burned down and will be desolate for a time.

**5** But God will again have mercy on them and bring them back into the land, and they will rebuild the house, though not like the first one, until the times of the age are fulfilled. After this they will return from their captivity and rebuild Jerusalem in splendor, and the house of God will be built in it gloriously, as the prophets have said of it.

**6** All the nations will turn to fear the Lord God in truth, and will bury their idols.

**7** All the nations will bless the Lord. His people will give thanks to God, and the Lord will exalt His people. All who love the Lord God in truth and righteousness will rejoice, showing mercy to our brothers.

**8** And now, my child, leave Nineveh, for what Jonah the prophet said will surely happen.

**9** But you, keep the law and the commandments, and be merciful and righteous, so that it may go well with you.

**10** Bury me properly, and your mother with me, and do not stay any longer in Nineveh. My child, see what Adam did to Ahikar who brought him up, how he brought him out of the light into the darkness, and how he repaid him. Ahikar was saved, but the other received his repayment, and he went down into the darkness. Manasseh gave charity and was saved from the deadly trap that was set for him, but Adam fell into the trap and perished.

**11** So now, my children, see what charity does, and how righteousness delivers." As he said these things, his life left him on his bed. He was a hundred and fifty-eight years old, and Tobias buried him with honor.

## Tobias in Ecbatana

**12** When Anna died, he buried her with his father. Then Tobias went away with his wife and his sons to Ecbatana, to Raguel his father-in-law.

**13** He grew old with honor, and he buried his parents-in-law with honor, and he inherited their property and that of his father Tobit.

**14** He died at the age of a hundred and seven in Ecbatana of Media.

**15** Before he died he heard of the destruction of Nineveh, which Nebuchadnezzar and Ahasuerus took captive. Before he died he rejoiced over Nineveh. Amen.

## Notes

- **v2 "fifty-eight years old… after eight years"** — so the shorter Greek, which makes him a hundred and fifty-eight at his death (v11). The longer Greek has *sixty-two* for his age when he went blind, and other numbers for his life.
- **vv4, 8 "what Jonah the prophet said about Nineveh"** — *Yet forty days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown* (Jonah 3:4). The longer Greek names *Nahum*, whose whole book is against Nineveh.
- **v5 "they will rebuild the house, though not like the first one"** — what the old men wept over when the foundation was laid (Ezra 3:12), and what Haggai asked: *Is it not as nothing in your eyes?* (Haggai 2:3). Tobit sees beyond it to a house *built gloriously*.
- **v10 "see what Adam did to Ahikar… Manasseh gave charity and was saved"** — the story of Ahikar, betrayed by the nephew he raised. The shorter Greek gives the nephew as *Adam* and calls Ahikar *Manasseh* in the last line; the longer Greek names the nephew *Nadab*. The point is the book's: charity saves, and treachery falls into its own trap.
- **v11 "see what charity does, and how righteousness delivers"** — the book's last word on its own word (1:3; 4:10; 12:9).
- **v15 "he heard of the destruction of Nineveh… he rejoiced"** — Tobias, who obeyed his father and left, lives to hear that Jonah's word came true.

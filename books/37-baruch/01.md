# Baruch 1

**1** These are the words of the book that Baruch son of Neriah, son of Maaseiah, son of Zedekiah, son of Hasadiah, son of Hilkiah, wrote in Babylon,

**2** in the fifth year, on the seventh day of the month, at the time when the Chaldeans took Jerusalem and burned it with fire.

**3** Baruch read the words of this book in the hearing of Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, and in the hearing of all the people who came to hear the book,

**4** in the hearing of the nobles and the king's sons, in the hearing of the elders, and in the hearing of all the people, from the least to the greatest, all who lived in Babylon by the river Sud.

**5** They wept and fasted and prayed before the Lord,

**6** and they collected money, as much as each could give,

**7** and sent it to Jerusalem, to Jehoiakim the priest, son of Hilkiah, son of Shallum, and to the priests and to all the people who were found with him in Jerusalem.

**8** This was when he received the vessels of the house of the Lord that had been carried away from the temple, to return them to the land of Judah, on the tenth day of Sivan: the silver vessels that Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah, had made

**9** after Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had carried away from Jerusalem Jeconiah and the princes, the prisoners, the nobles and the people of the land, and brought him to Babylon.

**10** They said: "We are sending you money. With the money buy burnt offerings and sin offerings and incense, and prepare a grain offering, and offer them on the altar of the Lord our God;

**11** and pray for the life of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and for the life of Belshazzar his son, that their days on earth may be like the days of heaven.

**12** The Lord will give us strength and light to our eyes, and we will live under the shadow of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and under the shadow of Belshazzar his son, and serve them many days and find favor in their sight.

**13** Pray for us also to the Lord our God, for we have sinned against the Lord our God, and to this day the anger of the Lord and His wrath have not turned away from us.

**14** You shall read this book that we are sending you, to make confession in the house of the Lord on the day of the feast and on the days of the appointed seasons.

## The Confession

**15** You shall say: To the Lord our God belongs righteousness, but to us shame of face, as at this day: to the men of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem,

**16** to our kings and our princes, to our priests and our prophets, and to our fathers,

**17** because we have sinned before the Lord

**18** and disobeyed Him, and have not listened to the voice of the Lord our God, to walk in the commandments of the Lord that He set before us.

**19** From the day the Lord brought our fathers out of the land of Egypt until this day, we have been disobedient to the Lord our God, and we have been quick to stop listening to His voice.

**20** So the calamities and the curse have clung to us, which the Lord declared through Moses His servant on the day He brought our fathers out of the land of Egypt to give us a land flowing with milk and honey, as at this day.

**21** We did not listen to the voice of the Lord our God in all the words of the prophets whom He sent to us,

**22** but each of us went after the thoughts of his own evil heart, to serve other gods and to do what is evil in the sight of the Lord our God."

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "Baruch son of Neriah, son of Maaseiah… in Babylon, in the fifth year"** — Jeremiah's scribe, *Baruch son of Neriah son of Mahseiah* (Jeremiah 32:12), here in Babylon with the exiles. The book survives in Greek. It dates itself to *the fifth year*, without saying of what, and names the day but not the month.
- **vv8–9 "the silver vessels that Zedekiah… had made"** — in Jeremiah the false prophets promised that *the vessels of the LORD's house will now shortly be brought back from Babylon* (Jeremiah 27:16). Here vessels do go back: silver ones that Zedekiah had made.
- **vv11–12 "pray for the life of Nebuchadnezzar… we will live under the shadow of Nebuchadnezzar"** — Jeremiah's letter told the exiles to *pray to the LORD on its behalf* (Jeremiah 29:7). *Under the shadow* was what Jerusalem said of its own king, *Under his shadow we shall live among the nations* (Lamentations 4:20); now the shadow is Babylon's.
- **vv15–22 "To the Lord our God belongs righteousness, but to us shame of face, as at this day"** — the confession runs from here to 3:8, and much of it follows Daniel's prayer in the same words (Daniel 9:7–19). It confesses for every rank in turn, *our kings and our princes, our priests and our prophets, and our fathers*.
- **v20 "the calamities and the curse… which the Lord declared through Moses… a land flowing with milk and honey, as at this day"** — the curses of the law (Deuteronomy 28), and Jeremiah's own words, *to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as at this day* (Jeremiah 11:5).

# Baruch 5

**1** Take off the garment of your mourning and affliction, Jerusalem,  
and put on forever the beauty of the glory from God.

**2** Wrap yourself in the robe of the righteousness from God;  
put on your head the turban of the glory of the Everlasting.

**3** For God will show your splendor to everything under heaven.

**4** For your name will be called by God forever:  
"Peace of Righteousness" and "Glory of Godliness."

**5** Arise, Jerusalem, and stand on the height;  
look toward the east,  
and see your children gathered from west and east  
at the word of the Holy One,  
rejoicing that God has remembered them.

**6** For they went out from you on foot,  
led away by their enemies;  
but God brings them back to you,  
carried in glory, as on a royal throne.

**7** For God has commanded that every high mountain  
and the everlasting hills be made low,  
and the valleys filled up to make the ground level,  
so that Israel may walk safely in the glory of God.

**8** The woods and every fragrant tree  
have shaded Israel at God's command.

**9** For God will lead Israel with joy  
in the light of His glory,  
with the mercy and righteousness that come from Him.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "Take off the garment of your mourning… put on the beauty of the glory from God… the robe of the righteousness from God"** — Isaiah's call, *put on your beautiful garments, Jerusalem, the holy city* (Isaiah 52:1), and his song, *He has wrapped me in a robe of righteousness* (Isaiah 61:10). The *turban* is the word of the high priest's headdress.
- **v4 "your name will be called by God forever: 'Peace of Righteousness' and 'Glory of Godliness'"** — the new name Isaiah promised, *you will be called by a new name that the mouth of the LORD will give* (Isaiah 62:2), and Jeremiah's name for the city, *The LORD Is Our Righteousness* (Jeremiah 33:16).
- **vv5–6 "see your children gathered… carried in glory, as on a royal throne"** — 4:36–37 again. They left on foot, driven; they come back carried.
- **v7 "every high mountain and the everlasting hills be made low, and the valleys filled up to make the ground level"** — Isaiah's highway, *Every valley shall be lifted up, and every mountain and hill made low; the uneven ground shall become level* (Isaiah 40:4).

# Baruch 2

**1** So the Lord has carried out the word that He spoke against us, and against our judges who judged Israel, against our kings and our princes, and against the men of Israel and Judah.

**2** Under the whole heaven nothing has been done like what He has done in Jerusalem, in keeping with what is written in the law of Moses,

**3** that we should eat, one the flesh of his son and another the flesh of his daughter.

**4** He has made them subject to all the kingdoms around us, a reproach and a desolation among all the peoples around, where the Lord has scattered them.

**5** They were put beneath and not above, because we sinned against the Lord our God by not listening to His voice.

**6** To the Lord our God belongs righteousness, but to us and our fathers shame of face, as at this day.

**7** All these calamities that the Lord pronounced against us have come upon us.

**8** Yet we have not entreated the favor of the Lord by turning, each of us, from the thoughts of his evil heart.

**9** So the Lord has kept watch over the calamities, and the Lord has brought them upon us; for the Lord is righteous in all His works that He has commanded us.

**10** We have not listened to His voice, to walk in the commandments of the Lord that He set before us.

**11** And now, Lord God of Israel, who brought Your people out of the land of Egypt with a mighty hand, with signs and wonders, with great power and an uplifted arm, and made Yourself a name, as at this day:

**12** we have sinned, we have been ungodly, we have done wrong, Lord our God, against all Your ordinances.

**13** Let Your anger turn away from us, for we are left few among the nations where You have scattered us.

**14** Hear, Lord, our prayer and our plea; deliver us for Your own sake, and grant us favor in the sight of those who have carried us into exile,

**15** so that all the earth may know that You are the Lord our God, for Your name is called over Israel and over his descendants.

**16** Lord, look down from Your holy house and consider us; incline Your ear, Lord, and hear.

**17** Open Your eyes, Lord, and see: it is not the dead in Hades, whose breath has been taken from their bodies, who will give glory and justice to the Lord;

**18** but the soul that grieves greatly, that walks bent over and feeble, the eyes that fail, and the soul that hungers, will give You glory and righteousness, Lord.

**19** For it is not because of any righteous deeds of our fathers and our kings that we lay our plea for mercy before You, Lord our God.

**20** For You have sent Your anger and Your wrath upon us, as You spoke through Your servants the prophets, saying:

**21** "This is what the Lord says: Bend your shoulders and serve the king of Babylon, and you will remain in the land that I gave to your fathers.

**22** But if you will not listen to the voice of the Lord and serve the king of Babylon,

**23** I will make to cease from the cities of Judah and from the streets of Jerusalem the voice of mirth and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, and the whole land will become a desolation without inhabitant."

**24** But we did not listen to Your voice, to serve the king of Babylon; and You carried out Your words, which You spoke through Your servants the prophets, that the bones of our kings and the bones of our fathers would be brought out of their place.

**25** They lie exposed to the heat by day and to the frost by night. They died in great misery, by famine, by sword and by pestilence.

**26** The house that is called by Your name You have made what it is today, because of the wickedness of the house of Israel and the house of Judah.

**27** Yet You have dealt with us, Lord our God, in all Your kindness and in all Your great compassion,

**28** as You spoke through Your servant Moses on the day You commanded him to write Your law before the sons of Israel, saying:

**29** "If you will not listen to My voice, this great swarming multitude will surely be turned into a small number among the nations where I will scatter them.

**30** For I knew that they would not listen to Me, for they are a stiff-necked people. But in the land of their exile they will take it to heart,

**31** and they will know that I am the Lord their God. I will give them a heart and ears that hear,

**32** and they will praise Me in the land of their exile and remember My name;

**33** they will turn from their stiff neck and from their wicked deeds, for they will remember the way of their fathers, who sinned before the Lord.

**34** I will bring them back to the land that I swore to their fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, and they will rule over it; I will multiply them, and they will not be diminished.

**35** I will make an everlasting covenant with them, to be their God, and they will be My people; and I will never again remove My people Israel from the land that I have given them."

## Notes

- **vv17–18 "it is not the dead in Hades… but the soul that grieves… will give You glory"** — *The dead do not praise Yah* (Psalm 115:17), and *Sheol does not thank You* (Isaiah 38:18). *Hades* is the Greek for Sheol. The prayer argues that the living, broken as they are, can still give God praise.

# Baruch 3

**1** Lord Almighty, God of Israel, a soul in anguish and a weary spirit cries out to You.

**2** Hear, Lord, and have mercy, for we have sinned before You.

**3** For You are enthroned forever, and we are perishing forever.

**4** Lord Almighty, God of Israel, hear now the prayer of the dead of Israel, the sons of those who sinned before You, who did not listen to Your voice, the voice of their God, so that calamities have clung to us.

**5** Do not remember the iniquities of our fathers, but remember Your hand and Your name at this time.

**6** For You are the Lord our God, and we will praise You, Lord.

**7** For this is why You have put the fear of You in our hearts: so that we would call on Your name. We will praise You in our exile, for we have put away from our hearts all the iniquity of our fathers who sinned before You.

**8** We are here today in our exile, where You have scattered us, to be a reproach and a curse and a penalty, for all the iniquities of our fathers, who turned away from the Lord our God.

## Wisdom

**9** Hear, Israel, the commandments of life;  
give ear to learn understanding!

**10** Why is it, Israel,  
why is it that you are in the land of your enemies,  
that you have grown old in a foreign land,  
that you are defiled with the dead,

**11** that you are counted with those in Hades?

**12** You have forsaken the fountain of wisdom.

**13** If you had walked in the way of God,  
you would be living in peace forever.

**14** Learn where there is understanding,  
where there is strength, where there is insight,  
so that you may also know where there is length of days and life,  
where there is light for the eyes, and peace.

**15** Who has found her place?  
Who has entered her storehouses?

**16** Where are the rulers of the nations,  
and those who lorded it over the beasts on the earth,

**17** who made sport with the birds of the air,  
who hoarded silver and gold,  
in which people trust,  
and whose getting had no end?

**18** For those who worked in silver and were anxious over it,  
whose works are past finding out —

**19** they have vanished and gone down to Hades,  
and others have risen up in their place.

**20** Younger men have seen the light  
and lived on the earth,  
but they have not known the way to knowledge,

**21** nor understood her paths,  
nor laid hold of her;  
their sons have strayed far from her way.

**22** She has not been heard of in Canaan,  
nor seen in Teman;

**23** the sons of Hagar, who seek understanding on the earth,  
the merchants of Merran and of Teman,  
the storytellers and the seekers of understanding,  
have not known the way of wisdom,  
nor remembered her paths.

**24** O Israel, how great is the house of God,  
how vast the place of His possession!

**25** It is great and has no end;  
it is high and immeasurable.

**26** The giants were born there, famous from the beginning,  
great in stature, skilled in war.

**27** God did not choose them,  
nor give them the way to knowledge;

**28** they perished because they had no understanding;  
they perished through their folly.

**29** Who has gone up into heaven and taken her,  
and brought her down from the clouds?

**30** Who has gone over the sea and found her,  
and will buy her for choice gold?

**31** No one knows the way to her,  
or thinks about her path.

**32** But He who knows all things knows her;  
He found her by His understanding.  
He who prepared the earth for all time  
filled it with four-footed creatures;

**33** He sends out the light, and it goes;  
He called it, and it obeyed Him with trembling;

**34** the stars shone in their watches and were glad;

**35** He called them, and they said, "Here we are!"  
They shone with gladness for Him who made them.

**36** This is our God;  
no other can be compared to Him.

**37** He found out the whole way to knowledge  
and gave it to Jacob His servant,  
and to Israel, whom He loved.

**38** Afterward she appeared on earth  
and lived among people.

## Notes

- **v9 "Hear, Israel, the commandments of life"** — the confession ends, and a poem on wisdom begins (3:9–4:4), opening with the words of the law, *Hear, Israel* (Deuteronomy 6:4), and its choice, *I have set before you today life and good* (30:15).
- **v38 "Afterward she appeared on earth and lived among people"** — the Greek verbs have no stated subject. Read with the poem, the subject is wisdom, who is *the book of the commandments of God* in the next verse (4:1); read with vv32–37, it is God, who *found out the whole way to knowledge*, and the verse says that God Himself appeared on earth and lived among people.

# 2 Corinthians 6

## Servants of God

**1** Working together with Him, then, we urge you not to receive the grace of God in vain.

**2** For He says:  
"In a favorable time I listened to you,  
and in a day of salvation I helped you."  
Now is the favorable time; now is the day of salvation.

**3** We put no obstacle in anyone's way, so that no fault may be found with our ministry,

**4** but in every way we commend ourselves as servants of God: by great endurance, in troubles, hardships and distresses,

**5** in beatings, imprisonments and riots, in labors, sleepless nights and hunger;

**6** by purity, knowledge, patience and kindness, by the Holy Spirit, by genuine love,

**7** by the word of truth and the power of God; with the weapons of righteousness for the right hand and for the left;

**8** through glory and dishonor, through slander and praise; treated as deceivers, and yet true;

**9** as unknown, and yet well known; as dying, and yet we live; as disciplined, and yet not put to death;

**10** as sorrowful, yet always rejoicing; as poor, yet making many rich; as having nothing, yet possessing everything.

## Open Your Hearts

**11** We have spoken openly to you, Corinthians; our heart is wide open.

**12** You are not restricted by us, but you are restricted in your own affections.

**13** In return — I speak as to children — open wide your hearts too.

## Do Not Be Unequally Yoked

**14** Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers. For what partnership has righteousness with lawlessness? What fellowship has light with darkness?

**15** What agreement has Christ with Belial? What share has a believer with an unbeliever?

**16** What agreement has the temple of God with idols? For we are the temple of the living God; as God said:  
"I will dwell among them and walk among them,  
and I will be their God,  
and they will be My people.

**17** Therefore go out from their midst,  
and be separate, says the Lord,  
and touch nothing unclean;  
then I will welcome you,

**18** and I will be a father to you,  
and you will be My sons and daughters,  
says the Lord Almighty."

## Notes

- **v2 "In a favorable time I listened to you, and in a day of salvation I helped you"** — Isaiah 49:8 in the Greek, where the Hebrew has *a day of deliverance*, from the servant song in which the LORD gives His servant *as a covenant for the people*. Paul's answer, *now is the day of salvation*, puts the Corinthians inside the promise.
- **v9 "as dying, and yet we live; as disciplined, and yet not put to death"** — the words of Psalm 118: *I will not die, but live… Yah has disciplined me severely, but He has not given me over to death* (Psalm 118:17–18), *paideuōn epaideusen me* in the Greek, the verb Paul has.
- **v14 "Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers"** — *heterozygountes*, **yoked with another kind**, from the Greek of the Law: *You shall not mate your livestock with a different kind*, *heterozygō* (Leviticus 19:19), and *You shall not plow with an ox and a donkey together* (Deuteronomy 22:10). Two animals of different kinds cannot pull one plow.
- **vv15–18 "Belial… I will dwell among them… go out from their midst… I will be a father to you, and you will be My sons and daughters"** — *Beliar*, the Hebrew *beliyya'al*, **worthlessness**, the word of the *worthless men* who were Eli's sons (1 Samuel 2:12), here a name for the enemy of Christ. The promises are strung from the Law and the Prophets: *I will walk among you, and I will be your God, and you will be My people* (Leviticus 26:12); *go out from the middle of her… touch nothing unclean* (Isaiah 52:11); and Nathan's word about David's son, *I will be his father, and he shall be My son* (2 Samuel 7:14), which Paul widens to *sons and daughters*.

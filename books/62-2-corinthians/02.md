# 2 Corinthians 2

## The Letter Written in Tears

**1** For I made up my mind not to come to you again in sorrow.

**2** If I cause you sorrow, who is there to make me glad but the one I have made sorrowful?

**3** I wrote as I did so that when I came I would not be made sorrowful by those who should have made me rejoice, for I am confident about all of you, that my joy is the joy of you all.

**4** For I wrote to you out of much trouble and anguish of heart and with many tears, not to cause you sorrow but to let you know the love I have, more than enough, for you.

## Forgive Him

**5** If anyone has caused sorrow, he has caused it not to me but in some measure, not to overstate it, to all of you.

**6** The punishment given by the majority is enough for such a man,

**7** so that now you should rather forgive and comfort him, or he may be overwhelmed by too much sorrow.

**8** So I urge you to confirm your love for him.

**9** For this is why I wrote: to test you and see whether you are obedient in everything.

**10** Anyone you forgive, I forgive too. What I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, I have forgiven for your sake in the presence of Christ,

**11** so that we would not be outwitted by Satan, for we are not unaware of his schemes.

## Led in Triumph

**12** When I came to Troas to preach the gospel of Christ, though a door was opened for me in the Lord,

**13** my spirit had no rest because I did not find my brother Titus there. So I said goodbye to them and went on to Macedonia.

**14** But thanks be to God, who in Christ always leads us in His triumphal procession, and through us spreads everywhere the aroma of the knowledge of Him.

**15** For we are the fragrance of Christ to God among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing,

**16** to the one an aroma from death to death, to the other an aroma from life to life. Who is sufficient for these things?

**17** For we are not like so many, peddling the word of God, but as men of sincerity, as from God, we speak before God in Christ.

---

## Notes

- **v14 "who in Christ always leads us in His triumphal procession"** — *thriambeuonti*, the word of a victor's march through the city with his captives behind him; Paul uses it once more, of God leading the rulers and authorities in triumph over them (Colossians 2:15). Whether Paul sees himself as a captive led in Christ's train or as a soldier sharing the victory, the verb does not say; either way the triumph is God's.

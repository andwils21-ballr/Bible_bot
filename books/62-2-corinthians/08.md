# 2 Corinthians 8

## The Example of Macedonia

**1** We want you to know, brothers, about the grace of God that has been given among the churches of Macedonia.

**2** In a severe test of trouble, their overflowing joy and their deep poverty have overflowed in a wealth of generosity.

**3** For they gave as they were able, I can testify, and beyond what they were able, of their own accord,

**4** begging us earnestly for the grace of sharing in this service to the saints.

**5** They did not do as we expected, but they gave themselves first to the Lord and then to us, by the will of God.

**6** So we urged Titus that, as he had begun, so he should also complete among you this act of grace.

**7** But just as you overflow in everything, in faith, in speech, in knowledge, in all earnestness, and in the love we have kindled in you, see that you overflow in this act of grace also.

## The Grace of Our Lord

**8** I do not say this as a command, but to prove by the earnestness of others that your love also is genuine.

**9** For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though He was rich, for your sake He became poor, so that by His poverty you might become rich.

**10** In this matter I give my judgment: it is to your advantage, since last year you were the first not only to act but also to want to act.

**11** So now finish doing it, so that your readiness in wanting it may be matched by your completing it, out of what you have.

**12** For if the readiness is there, the gift is acceptable according to what a person has, not according to what he does not have.

**13** I do not mean that others should be eased and you burdened, but that there should be fairness:

**14** at the present time your abundance should supply their need, so that their abundance may also supply your need, and so there may be fairness.

**15** As it is written, "The one who gathered much had nothing over, and the one who gathered little had no lack."

## Titus and the Brothers

**16** Thanks be to God, who put the same earnestness for you into the heart of Titus.

**17** He not only welcomed our appeal, but being very earnest he is going to you of his own accord.

**18** With him we are sending the brother who is praised by all the churches for his work in the gospel.

**19** Not only that, he has been appointed by the churches to travel with us as we carry out this act of grace, for the glory of the Lord Himself and to show our readiness.

**20** We are taking care that no one should blame us about this generous gift that we are administering,

**21** for we aim at what is honorable not only in the Lord's sight but also in the sight of men.

**22** With them we are sending our brother, whom we have often tested and found earnest in many matters, and who is now much more earnest because of his great confidence in you.

**23** As for Titus, he is my partner and fellow worker for your benefit; as for our brothers, they are messengers of the churches, the glory of Christ.

**24** So show them, in the sight of the churches, the proof of your love and of our boasting about you.

## Notes

- **vv1, 4, 6–7, 9, 19 "the grace of God… the grace of sharing… this act of grace… the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ"** — *charis*, **grace**, all through these chapters, for God's gift, for the believers' giving, and for Christ's own: the collection for Jerusalem is the same grace passed on. In v16 the same word is *thanks*.

# 2 Corinthians 3

## A Letter from Christ

**1** Are we beginning to commend ourselves again? Or do we need, as some do, letters of recommendation to you or from you?

**2** You yourselves are our letter, written on our hearts, known and read by everyone.

**3** You show that you are a letter from Christ, delivered by us, written not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on tablets of stone but on tablets of hearts of flesh.

**4** Such is the confidence we have through Christ toward God.

**5** Not that we are sufficient in ourselves to reckon anything as coming from us; our sufficiency is from God,

**6** who has made us sufficient to be ministers of a new covenant, not of the letter but of the Spirit. For the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life.

## The Greater Glory

**7** Now if the ministry of death, carved in letters on stone, came with such glory that the sons of Israel could not look steadily at Moses' face because of its glory, a glory that was being done away with,

**8** will not the ministry of the Spirit have even more glory?

**9** For if there was glory in the ministry that brought condemnation, the ministry that brings righteousness overflows with far more glory.

**10** Indeed, what once had glory has no glory at all in this respect, because of the glory that surpasses it.

**11** For if what was being done away with came with glory, much more will what remains have glory.

## The Veil

**12** Since we have such a hope, we are very bold,

**13** not like Moses, who would put a veil over his face so that the sons of Israel might not look steadily at the end of what was being done away with.

**14** But their minds were hardened. For to this very day, when the old covenant is read, that same veil remains, not lifted, because only in Christ is it done away with.

**15** Yes, to this day whenever Moses is read, a veil lies over their hearts.

**16** But whenever anyone turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away.

**17** Now the Lord is the Spirit, and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom.

**18** We all, with unveiled face, beholding the glory of the Lord as in a mirror, are being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another, as from the Lord, who is the Spirit.

## Notes

- **v3 "not on tablets of stone but on tablets of hearts of flesh"** — the two covenants side by side. The first was *tablets of stone, written by the finger of God* (Exodus 31:18); the new is the promise of Ezekiel, *I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh* (Ezekiel 36:26), and of Jeremiah, *on their heart I will write it* (Jeremiah 31:33).
- **vv7, 13 "the glory of his face… a veil over his face"** — Exodus 34:29–35 in the Greek, where *the skin of his face was glorified*, *dedoxastai*. The Hebrew says the skin of his face *had grown horns*, *qaran* (see the note there), and the Greek is the reading Paul builds on. Exodus says Moses veiled his face after speaking to the people; it does not say the glory faded. That is Paul's reading, set against the glory *that remains* (v11).
- **vv14–17 "when the old covenant is read… whenever anyone turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away. Now the Lord is the Spirit"** — *tēs palaias diathēkēs*, **the old covenant**, a phrase found only here in the New Testament. V16 is Exodus 34:34 in the Greek, *When Moses went in before the LORD… he would take the veil off*, with *Moses went in* changed to *anyone turns*. *The Lord* of that verse is YHWH, and Paul says who He is now met as: *the Lord is the Spirit*.
- **v18 "beholding the glory of the Lord as in a mirror"** — *katoptrizomenoi*, in the New Testament only here, from *katoptron*, **mirror**. It can mean **beholding as in a mirror**, as Paul's *we see in a mirror* (1 Corinthians 13:12), or **reflecting**, as Moses' face shone back what it had seen. *Metamorphoumetha*, **are being transformed**, is the verb of Jesus *transfigured* (Mark 9:2) and of Romans 12:2.

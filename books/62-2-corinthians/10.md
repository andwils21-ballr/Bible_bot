# 2 Corinthians 10

## Not According to the Flesh

**1** I myself, Paul, appeal to you by the gentleness and forbearance of Christ — I who am humble when I am face to face with you, but bold toward you when I am away.

**2** I beg you that when I come I may not have to be bold with the confidence I reckon I will dare to show against some who reckon that we walk according to the flesh.

**3** For though we walk in the flesh, we do not wage war according to the flesh.

**4** The weapons of our warfare are not of the flesh but mighty in God's sight to tear down strongholds. We tear down arguments

**5** and every high thing raised up against the knowledge of God, and we take every thought captive to obey Christ,

**6** ready to punish every disobedience, once your obedience is complete.

**7** You look at outward appearances. If anyone is confident that he belongs to Christ, let him reckon this again for himself: just as he belongs to Christ, so do we.

**8** For even if I boast a little too much about our authority, which the Lord gave for building you up and not for tearing you down, I will not be put to shame.

**9** I do not want to seem to be frightening you with my letters.

**10** For someone says, "His letters are weighty and strong, but his bodily presence is weak, and his speech is of no account."

**11** Let such a person reckon this: what we are in word by letters when we are away, we are also in deed when we are present.

## Boasting Within Limits

**12** We do not dare to class or compare ourselves with some of those who commend themselves. When they measure themselves by themselves and compare themselves with themselves, they show no understanding.

**13** We, however, will not boast beyond limits, but within the measure of the field God has assigned to us as our measure, a field that reaches even to you.

**14** For we are not overreaching ourselves, as though we did not reach you; we came all the way to you with the gospel of Christ.

**15** We do not boast beyond limits in the labors of others, but our hope is that as your faith grows, our work among you will be greatly enlarged, within our field,

**16** so that we may preach the gospel in the lands beyond you, without boasting of work already done in another's field.

**17** But let him who boasts boast in the Lord.

**18** For it is not the one who commends himself who is approved, but the one whom the Lord commends.

## Notes

- **v1 "by the gentleness and forbearance of Christ — I who am humble when I am face to face with you"** — *praÿtēs*, **gentleness**, and *tapeinos*, **humble**, the two words Jesus used of Himself: *I am gentle and humble in heart* (Matthew 11:29). In the mouths of those who say his *bodily presence is weak* (v10), *humble* is a sneer, lowly, of no account. Paul answers by appealing to the gentleness of Christ, who called Himself humble.
- **vv4, 8 "to tear down strongholds… for building you up and not for tearing you down"** — two scriptures. The weapons are those of the wise man of Proverbs, who *brings down the stronghold in which they trust*, *katheilen to ochyrōma*, the same words in the Greek (Proverbs 21:22). The authority over the church is the LORD's promise to the exiles, *I will build them up and not overthrow them*, *anoikodomēsō… ou mē kathelō* (Jeremiah 24:6). Arguments Paul tears down; the church he builds up, and he says it again at the letter's end (13:10).

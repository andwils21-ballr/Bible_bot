# 2 Corinthians 9

## The Gift Made Ready

**1** There is no need for me to write to you about the service to the saints,

**2** for I know your eagerness, which I boast about to the Macedonians on your behalf, saying that Achaia has been ready since last year; and your zeal has stirred up most of them.

**3** But I am sending the brothers so that our boasting about you may not prove empty in this matter, and so that you may be ready, as I said you would be.

**4** Otherwise, if any Macedonians come with me and find you not ready, we would be put to shame, to say nothing of you, for being so confident.

**5** So I thought it necessary to urge the brothers to go on ahead to you and arrange in advance the generous gift you promised, so that it may be ready as a generous gift and not as a grudging one.

## Sowing and Reaping

**6** Remember this: whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows generously will also reap generously.

**7** Each one should give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.

**8** God is able to make all grace overflow to you, so that, always having enough of everything in every way, you may overflow in every good work.

**9** As it is written:  
"He has scattered abroad; he has given to the poor;  
his righteousness endures forever."

**10** He who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your seed for sowing and increase the harvest of your righteousness.

**11** You will be made rich in every way for every kind of generosity, which through us produces thanksgiving to God.

**12** For carrying out this service not only supplies the needs of the saints but also overflows in many thanksgivings to God.

**13** Through the proof this service gives, they will glorify God for the submission that goes with your confession of the gospel of Christ, and for the generosity of your sharing with them and with everyone.

**14** They will long for you as they pray for you, because of the surpassing grace God has given you.

**15** Thanks be to God for His indescribable gift!

# 2 Peter 1

## Greeting

**1** Simeon Peter, a servant and apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who have obtained a faith as precious as ours by the righteousness of our God and Savior Jesus Christ:

**2** grace and peace be multiplied to you in the knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord.

## Make Your Calling Sure

**3** His divine power has given us everything we need for life and godliness, through the knowledge of Him who called us by His own glory and excellence.

**4** Through these He has given us His precious and very great promises, so that through them you may become sharers in the divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world because of desire.

**5** For this very reason, make every effort to add to your faith excellence, and to excellence knowledge,

**6** and to knowledge self-control, and to self-control endurance, and to endurance godliness,

**7** and to godliness brotherly affection, and to brotherly affection love.

**8** For if these things are yours and are increasing, they keep you from being idle or unfruitful in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**9** Whoever lacks them is blind, shortsighted, having forgotten that he was cleansed from his old sins.

**10** So, brothers, be all the more eager to make your calling and election sure, for if you do these things you will never stumble.

**11** In this way there will be richly provided for you an entrance into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.

## The Prophetic Word

**12** So I intend always to remind you of these things, though you know them and are established in the truth you have.

**13** I think it right, as long as I am in this tent, to stir you up by way of reminder,

**14** since I know that the putting off of my tent will be soon, as our Lord Jesus Christ made clear to me.

**15** I will make every effort that after my departure you may be able at any time to recall these things.

**16** For we did not follow cleverly invented myths when we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, but we were eyewitnesses of His majesty.

**17** For He received honor and glory from God the Father when the voice was borne to Him by the Majestic Glory: "This is My Son, My beloved, in whom I am well pleased."

**18** We ourselves heard this voice borne from heaven, for we were with Him on the holy mountain.

**19** We have the prophetic word made more sure, and you will do well to pay attention to it as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts,

**20** knowing this first of all, that no prophecy of Scripture comes from one's own interpretation.

**21** For no prophecy was ever brought by the will of man, but men spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit.

## Notes

- **v1 "our God and Savior Jesus Christ"** — *tou theou hēmōn kai sōtēros Iēsou Christou*, one article over both nouns, as in v11, *our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ*, where the two titles name one person. Read so, Jesus Christ is called *our God and Savior*; read with *God* and *Savior* as two, the righteousness is of God and of Jesus Christ. v2 names the two apart: *the knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord*.
- **vv15–18 "after my departure… on the holy mountain"** — *exodos*, **departure**, the word of the mountain where Peter saw Jesus' glory: Moses and Elijah *spoke of His departure, which He was about to fulfill in Jerusalem* (Luke 9:31). The voice he heard there, *This is My Son, the beloved, in whom I am well pleased* (Matthew 17:5), is the one he quotes. *Tent* (vv13–14) is his body, which he will soon put off.
- **v20 "no prophecy of Scripture comes from one's own interpretation"** — *idias epilyseōs*, **of one's own unloosing**. *One's own* may be the prophet's: no prophet made up the meaning of what he saw, for *men spoke from God* (v21). Or it may be the reader's: no one is to read prophecy by his own private sense of it.

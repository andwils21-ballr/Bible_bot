# 2 Peter 3

## Where Is the Promise?

**1** Beloved, this is now the second letter I am writing to you, and in both I am stirring up your sincere mind by way of reminder,

**2** to remember the words spoken beforehand by the holy prophets and the commandment of the Lord and Savior through your apostles,

**3** knowing this first of all, that in the last days scoffers will come with scoffing, following their own desires

**4** and saying, "Where is the promise of His coming? For ever since the fathers fell asleep, all things continue as they were from the beginning of creation."

**5** For they willfully overlook this: that long ago, by the word of God, heavens existed, and an earth formed out of water and through water,

**6** through which the world of that time was flooded with water and perished.

**7** But by the same word the heavens and earth that now exist are stored up for fire, kept for the day of judgment and destruction of the ungodly.

## A Thousand Years as One Day

**8** But do not overlook this one thing, beloved: with the Lord one day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years like one day.

**9** The Lord is not slow to fulfill His promise, as some count slowness, but is patient toward you, not wanting anyone to perish, but all to come to repentance.

**10** But the day of the Lord will come like a thief. On that day the heavens will pass away with a roar, the elements will be burned up and dissolved, and the earth and the works done on it will be found.

**11** Since all these things are to be dissolved in this way, what sort of people ought you to be in holy conduct and godliness,

**12** waiting for and hastening the coming of the day of God, because of which the heavens will be set on fire and dissolved, and the elements will melt as they burn!

**13** But according to His promise we are waiting for new heavens and a new earth, in which righteousness dwells.

## Grow in Grace

**14** So, beloved, since you are waiting for these, be eager to be found by Him without spot or blemish, at peace.

**15** Count the patience of our Lord as salvation, just as our beloved brother Paul also wrote to you according to the wisdom given him,

**16** as he does in all his letters when he speaks of these things. There are some things in them hard to understand, which the ignorant and unstable twist, as they do the rest of the Scriptures, to their own destruction.

**17** You therefore, beloved, knowing this beforehand, be on your guard, so that you are not carried away by the error of the lawless and fall from your own firm footing.

**18** But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To Him be the glory both now and to the day of eternity. Amen.

## Notes

- **v8 "one day is like a thousand years"** — Psalm 90:4, *a thousand years in Your sight are like yesterday when it is past*. Peter turns the line both ways: a thousand years are as a day, and a day as a thousand years.
- **v10 "the earth and the works done on it will be found"** — *heurethēsetai*, **will be found**, as the edition prints it. The same verb in v14 is *to be found by Him*: the earth and its works will be found out, laid open before God, when the heavens pass away. The mark beside the word shows that copies read differently here.
- **v13 "new heavens and a new earth"** — Isaiah 65:17, *I am about to create new heavens and a new earth*, and 66:22.

# Acts 24

## The Charge

**1** Five days later the high priest Ananias came down with some elders and an advocate named Tertullus, and they brought their case against Paul before the governor.

**2** When Paul had been summoned, Tertullus began to accuse him, saying, "Since through you we enjoy much peace, and since by your foresight reforms are being made for this nation,

**3** in every way and everywhere we welcome this, most excellent Felix, with all gratitude.

**4** But so as not to detain you further, I beg you in your kindness to hear us briefly.

**5** For we have found this man a plague, one who stirs up riots among all the Jews throughout the world, and a ringleader of the sect of the Nazarenes.

**6** He even tried to profane the temple, and we seized him.

**8** By examining him yourself you will be able to learn from him about all these things of which we accuse him."

**9** The Jews also joined in the charge, asserting that these things were so.

## Paul's Defense

**10** When the governor had nodded to him to speak, Paul answered, "Knowing that for many years you have been a judge over this nation, I gladly make my defense.

**11** You can verify that it is not more than twelve days since I went up to worship in Jerusalem,

**12** and they did not find me arguing with anyone or stirring up a crowd, either in the temple or in the synagogues or in the city.

**13** Nor can they prove to you the things of which they now accuse me.

**14** But this I confess to you, that according to the Way, which they call a sect, I worship the God of our fathers, believing everything that is in keeping with the Law and that is written in the Prophets,

**15** having a hope in God, which these men themselves also wait for, that there will be a resurrection of both the righteous and the unrighteous.

**16** So I myself always take pains to have a clear conscience toward God and toward men.

**17** After several years I came to bring alms to my nation and to present offerings.

**18** While I was doing this, they found me purified in the temple, without any crowd or uproar.

**19** But some Jews from Asia were there, who ought to be here before you to bring charges, if they have anything against me.

**20** Or else let these men themselves say what wrongdoing they found when I stood before the council,

**21** other than this one thing that I cried out as I stood among them: 'It is about the resurrection of the dead that I am on trial before you today.'"

## Felix Waits

**22** But Felix, who had a rather accurate knowledge of the Way, put them off, saying, "When Lysias the commander comes down, I will decide your case."

**23** He ordered the centurion to keep him in custody but to let him have some freedom, and not to prevent any of his friends from attending to his needs.

**24** Some days later Felix came with his wife Drusilla, who was a Jew, and he sent for Paul and heard him speak about faith in Christ Jesus.

**25** As he talked about righteousness and self-control and the coming judgment, Felix became afraid and answered, "Go away for now. When I find an opportunity, I will send for you."

**26** At the same time he hoped that money would be given him by Paul. So he sent for him often and talked with him.

**27** When two years had passed, Felix was succeeded by Porcius Festus; and wanting to do the Jews a favor, Felix left Paul in prison.

## Notes

- **vv2–4 "Since through you we enjoy much peace… most excellent Felix… in your kindness"** — Tertullus praises the judge before he brings a charge. *Kratiste*, **most excellent**, the title of Lysias' letter (23:26) and of Theophilus (Luke 1:3). *Epieikeia*, **kindness**, fairness, forbearance.
- **vv5–6 "a plague… stirs up riots among all the Jews throughout the world… a ringleader of the sect of the Nazarenes… tried to profane the temple"** — *loimon*, **a plague**, a pestilence. *Kata tēn oikoumenēn*, **throughout the world**, the charge of Thessalonica, *These men who have turned the world upside down* (17:6). *Hairesis*, **sect**, the word for the Sadducees' and the Pharisees' *party* (5:17; 15:5). *Tōn Nazōraiōn*, **the Nazarenes**, from *Jesus of Nazareth* (22:8); only here are the believers called by that name. In the temple the cry was that he *has defiled this holy place* (21:28); before the governor it has become *tried to*. The chapter has no v7; it is not in the edition, so the numbering skips from 6 to 8.
- **v14 "according to the Way, which they call a sect, I worship the God of our fathers"** — Paul takes their word, *hairesis*, and sets his own against it, *hodos*, **the Way**. *Tō patrōō theō*, **the God of our fathers**, the word of *the law of our fathers* (22:3): what he worships is what his fathers worshiped.
- **v15 "a resurrection of both the righteous and the unrighteous"** — Daniel's *Many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth will awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame* (Daniel 12:2). Jesus spoke of *the resurrection of the righteous* (Luke 14:14).
- **v16 "a clear conscience"** — *aproskopon*, word for word **without stumbling**, a conscience that does not trip. Paul said to the council, *in all good conscience up to this day* (23:1).
- **vv17–19 "to bring alms to my nation and to present offerings… some Jews from Asia… who ought to be here"** — the only mention in Acts of the gift Paul brought to Jerusalem; to the Romans he writes, *I am going to Jerusalem, serving the saints* (Romans 15:25), with *a contribution for the poor* from Macedonia and Achaia (15:26). The men who started the riot (21:27) are not in court.
- **vv22–26 "a rather accurate knowledge of the Way… righteousness and self-control and the coming judgment… Felix became afraid… he hoped that money would be given him"** — *enkrateias*, **self-control**, mastery of oneself. *Emphobos genomenos*, **became afraid**. *Kairon metalabōn*, **when I find an opportunity**, and the next verse says what he was waiting for.
- **v27 "When two years had passed… left Paul in prison"** — *dietias*, **two years**, as long as Paul taught in Ephesus (19:10). *Dedemenon*, **in prison**, word for word **bound**.

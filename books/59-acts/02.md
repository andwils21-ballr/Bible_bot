# Acts 2

## The Spirit Comes

**1** When the day of Pentecost had come, they were all together in one place.

**2** Suddenly there came from heaven a sound like a rushing, violent wind, and it filled the whole house where they were sitting.

**3** Tongues as of fire appeared to them, divided, and one rested on each of them.

**4** They were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance.

**5** Now there were Jews living in Jerusalem, devout men from every nation under heaven.

**6** When this sound came, the crowd gathered and was bewildered, because each one heard them speaking in his own language.

**7** They were amazed and astonished, saying, "Are not all these who are speaking Galileans?

**8** How is it that each of us hears them in his own native language?

**9** Parthians and Medes and Elamites, and those who live in Mesopotamia, Judea and Cappadocia, Pontus and Asia,

**10** Phrygia and Pamphylia, Egypt and the parts of Libya near Cyrene, and visitors from Rome,

**11** both Jews and proselytes, Cretans and Arabs — we hear them telling in our own tongues the mighty works of God."

**12** All were amazed and perplexed, saying to one another, "What does this mean?"

**13** But others mocked and said, "They are full of new wine."

## Peter Speaks

**14** But Peter, standing with the eleven, raised his voice and addressed them: "Men of Judea and all who live in Jerusalem, let this be known to you, and listen to my words.

**15** These men are not drunk, as you suppose, for it is only the third hour of the day.

**16** But this is what was spoken through the prophet Joel:

**17** 'And it shall come to pass in the last days, says God, that I will pour out My Spirit on all flesh; your sons and your daughters will prophesy, your young men will see visions, and your old men will dream dreams.

**18** Even on My male servants and My female servants in those days I will pour out My Spirit, and they will prophesy.

**19** I will give wonders in the heavens above and signs on the earth below, blood and fire and vapor of smoke.

**20** The sun will be turned to darkness and the moon to blood, before the great and glorious day of the Lord comes.

**21** And it shall come to pass that everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.'

**22** "Men of Israel, hear these words: Jesus of Nazareth, a man attested to you by God with mighty works and wonders and signs that God did through Him in your midst, as you yourselves know —

**23** this man, handed over by the set plan and foreknowledge of God, you nailed up and killed by the hands of lawless men.

**24** God raised Him up, releasing Him from the pangs of death, because it was not possible for Him to be held by it.

**25** For David says about Him, 'I saw the Lord always before me, for He is at my right hand, so that I will not be shaken.

**26** Therefore my heart was glad, and my tongue rejoiced; my flesh also will dwell in hope.

**27** For You will not abandon my soul to Hades, nor let Your Holy One see decay.

**28** You have made known to me the paths of life; You will fill me with gladness in Your presence.'

**29** "Brothers, I may speak to you with confidence about the patriarch David, that he both died and was buried, and his tomb is with us to this day.

**30** Being a prophet, and knowing that God had sworn to him with an oath to seat one of the fruit of his body on his throne,

**31** he foresaw and spoke of the resurrection of the Messiah, that He was not abandoned to Hades, nor did His flesh see decay.

**32** This Jesus God raised up, and of that we are all witnesses.

**33** Being therefore exalted at the right hand of God, and having received from the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, He has poured out this that you see and hear.

**34** For David did not go up into the heavens, but he himself says, 'The Lord said to my Lord, Sit at My right hand,

**35** until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.'

**36** Let all the house of Israel therefore know for certain that God has made Him both Lord and Messiah, this Jesus whom you crucified."

## Three Thousand Added

**37** When they heard this, they were cut to the heart, and said to Peter and the rest of the apostles, "Brothers, what shall we do?"

**38** Peter said to them, "Repent, and let each of you be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins; and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.

**39** For the promise is for you and for your children and for all who are far off, as many as the Lord our God will call to Himself."

**40** With many other words he bore witness and urged them, saying, "Be saved from this crooked generation."

**41** So those who received his word were baptized, and that day about three thousand souls were added.

**42** They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and to the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to the prayers.

## The Life of the Believers

**43** Awe came upon every soul, and many wonders and signs were done through the apostles.

**44** All who believed were together and had all things in common.

**45** They sold their possessions and goods and distributed them to all, as anyone had need.

**46** Day by day, devoting themselves with one mind to the temple, and breaking bread in their homes, they shared their food with gladness and simplicity of heart,

**47** praising God and having favor with all the people. The Lord added to their number day by day those who were being saved.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "the day of Pentecost… a sound like a rushing, violent wind… Tongues as of fire… began to speak in other tongues"** — *Pentēkostē*, **Pentecost**, the **fiftieth** day, the Feast of Weeks, when the law says *you shall count fifty days, and you shall present a new grain offering* (Leviticus 23:16). *Pnoē*, **wind**, also **breath**, of the same root as *pneuma*. Fire came down on Sinai, *because the LORD had come down on it in fire* (Exodus 19:18). John said the one to come *will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire* (Luke 3:16). *Glōssai*, **tongues**, both of the fire and of speech.
- **vv5–13 "devout men from every nation under heaven… each one heard them speaking in his own language… They are full of new wine"** — at Babel the LORD *confused the lip of all the earth… and scattered them* (Genesis 11:9); here the scattered nations hear one message, each in its own speech. *Dialektos*, **language**. The list runs from the east (Parthia, Media, Elam) round to Rome and back to Crete and Arabia. *Proselytoi*, **proselytes**, Gentiles who had become Jews. *Gleukos*, **new wine**, sweet wine.
- **vv14–21 "the third hour… this is what was spoken through the prophet Joel… in the last days… everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved"** — *the third hour*, about nine in the morning. The words are Joel 2:28–32; Peter's Greek has *in the last days* for *afterward*, *says God*, and *and they will prophesy* (v18), and adds *signs… below*. Joel's *before the great and fearful day of the LORD* is *great and glorious* here, as in the Greek of Joel. Joel's *will escape* is *will be saved*, as the Greek of Joel has it. *The Lord* in v21 is the LORD of Joel; v36 gives the name to Jesus.
- **vv22–24 "a man attested to you by God… handed over by the set plan and foreknowledge of God… releasing Him from the pangs of death"** — *prospēxantes*, **nailed up**, fastened to the cross. *Anomōn*, **lawless men**, those without the law. *Tas ōdinas tou thanatou*, **the pangs of death**, word for word **the birth pains of death**, as the Greek of Psalm 18:4 (17:5 in the Greek numbering) has *ōdines thanatou*, where the Hebrew has *The cords of death surrounded me*.
- **vv25–31 "I saw the Lord always before me… You will not abandon my soul to Hades, nor let Your Holy One see decay… one of the fruit of his body on his throne"** — Psalm 16:8–11 in the words of its Greek (15:8–11 in the Greek numbering). The Hebrew has *I have set the LORD always before me*, *my glory rejoices* where the Greek has *my tongue*, and *nor let Your faithful one see the pit*, where the Greek has *diaphthoran*, **decay**. David *was buried in the City of David* (1 Kings 2:10). God swore *One of the fruit of your body I will set on your throne* (Psalm 132:11).
- **vv32–36 "exalted at the right hand of God… The Lord said to my Lord… both Lord and Messiah"** — Psalm 110:1, as Jesus quoted it (Luke 20:42–43). *He has poured out*, the verb of Joel, *I will pour out* (v17): what God promised, the exalted Jesus does. *Kyrion kai christon*, **Lord and Messiah**.
- **vv37–42 "cut to the heart… Repent, and let each of you be baptized… for all who are far off… this crooked generation… three thousand"** — *katenygēsan*, **were cut**, pierced. *Peace, peace, to the far and to the near* (Isaiah 57:19). *A crooked and twisted generation* (Deuteronomy 32:5). *Tē koinōnia*, **the fellowship**, a sharing in common. *Tē klasei tou artou*, **the breaking of bread**, as at Emmaus (Luke 24:35).
- **vv43–47 "had all things in common… with gladness and simplicity of heart… those who were being saved"** — *hapanta koina*, **all things in common**. *Aphelotēti*, **simplicity**, a smooth, single heart. *Tous sōzomenous*, **those who were being saved**, Joel's *will be saved* (v21) coming true day by day.

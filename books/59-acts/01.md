# Acts 1

## The Promise of the Spirit

**1** In the first account, Theophilus, I wrote about all that Jesus began to do and to teach,

**2** until the day when He was taken up, after He had given commands through the Holy Spirit to the apostles whom He had chosen.

**3** To them He also showed Himself alive after His suffering by many convincing proofs, appearing to them over forty days and speaking about the kingdom of God.

**4** While He was eating with them, He ordered them not to leave Jerusalem, but to wait for the promise of the Father, "which you heard from Me;

**5** for John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit not many days from now."

## He Is Taken Up

**6** So when they had come together, they asked Him, "Lord, is it at this time that You are restoring the kingdom to Israel?"

**7** He said to them, "It is not for you to know times or seasons that the Father has set by His own authority.

**8** But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be My witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth."

**9** When He had said these things, as they were watching, He was lifted up, and a cloud took Him out of their sight.

**10** While they were gazing into heaven as He went, two men stood by them in white clothing,

**11** who said, "Men of Galilee, why do you stand looking into heaven? This Jesus, who has been taken up from you into heaven, will come in the same way as you saw Him go into heaven."

## Matthias Chosen

**12** Then they returned to Jerusalem from the mount called Olivet, which is near Jerusalem, a sabbath day's journey away.

**13** When they had entered the city, they went up to the upper room where they were staying: Peter and John and James and Andrew, Philip and Thomas, Bartholomew and Matthew, James son of Alphaeus and Simon the Zealot, and Judas son of James.

**14** All these with one mind were devoting themselves to prayer, together with the women and Mary the mother of Jesus, and with His brothers.

**15** In those days Peter stood up among the brothers (the company of persons was in all about a hundred and twenty) and said,

**16** "Brothers, the Scripture had to be fulfilled, which the Holy Spirit spoke beforehand by the mouth of David about Judas, who became a guide to those who arrested Jesus.

**17** For he was counted among us and was allotted his share in this ministry."

**18** (This man acquired a field with the reward of his wrongdoing; and falling headlong he burst open in the middle, and all his insides poured out.

**19** It became known to all who live in Jerusalem, so that the field was called in their own language Akeldama, that is, Field of Blood.)

**20** "For it is written in the book of Psalms, 'Let his homestead be made desolate, and let no one live in it,' and, 'Let another take his office.'

**21** So one of the men who have accompanied us during all the time that the Lord Jesus went in and out among us,

**22** beginning from the baptism of John until the day when He was taken up from us, must become with us a witness of His resurrection."

**23** They put forward two, Joseph called Barsabbas, who was surnamed Justus, and Matthias.

**24** They prayed and said, "You, Lord, who know the hearts of all, show which one of these two You have chosen

**25** to take the place in this ministry and apostleship from which Judas turned aside to go to his own place."

**26** They cast lots for them, and the lot fell on Matthias, and he was counted with the eleven apostles.

## Notes

- **vv1–5 "In the first account, Theophilus… all that Jesus began to do and to teach… the promise of the Father… baptized with the Holy Spirit"** — *the first account* is the Gospel of Luke, written *for you, most excellent Theophilus* (Luke 1:3). *Ērxato*, **began**: what Jesus began, His apostles carry on. The last words of Luke are here again, *I am sending the promise of My Father upon you* (Luke 24:49), and John's, *He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire* (Luke 3:16). *Synalizomenos*, **while He was eating with them**, may also be **while He was staying with them**.
- **vv6–8 "restoring the kingdom to Israel… times or seasons… you will be My witnesses… to the end of the earth"** — *apokathistaneis*, **restoring**, the verb the Greek of Malachi uses of Elijah, who will *turn the heart of fathers to sons* (Malachi 4:6; 4:5 in the Greek). *To the end of the earth* is Isaiah's servant, *a light for the nations, that My deliverance may reach to the end of the earth* (Isaiah 49:6).
- **vv9–11 "He was lifted up, and a cloud took Him… two men… in white clothing… will come in the same way"** — Luke has the same day ending at Bethany, *He parted from them and was carried up into heaven* (Luke 24:51). Elijah *went up in a whirlwind to heaven* while Elisha watched (2 Kings 2:11). *Two men*, as at the tomb (Luke 24:4). The Son of Man comes *with the clouds of heaven* (Daniel 7:13).
- **vv12–14 "the mount called Olivet… a sabbath day's journey… the upper room… Mary the mother of Jesus, and with His brothers"** — *Elaiōn*, **Olivet**, the Mount of Olives. *A sabbath day's journey*, the distance one might walk on the sabbath. The eleven are named as in Luke 6:14–16, without Judas Iscariot. *Homothymadon*, **with one mind**, a word Acts uses often (2:46; 4:24).
- **vv15–17 "about a hundred and twenty… the Holy Spirit spoke beforehand by the mouth of David… allotted his share"** — *onomatōn*, **persons**, word for word **names**. *Elachen ton klēron*, **was allotted his share**, word for word **obtained the lot**, as Matthias will (v26).
- **vv18–19 "acquired a field with the reward of his wrongdoing… falling headlong he burst open… Akeldama, that is, Field of Blood"** — the writer's aside to the reader, set in parentheses: *in their own language* is not how Peter would speak to the people of Jerusalem. Matthew tells it otherwise: Judas *hanged himself*, and the priests bought *the Field of Blood* with the silver (Matthew 27:5–8). *Prēnēs genomenos*, **falling headlong**. *Hakeldamach* is Aramaic, **field of blood**.
- **v20 "Let his homestead be made desolate… Let another take his office"** — Psalm 69:25, *Let their camp be desolate; let no one live in their tents*, made singular; the Greek of the Psalm has *epaulis*, **homestead**, as here. Psalm 109:8, *let another take his office*, as the Greek, *episkopē*, **office**, the word later used of an overseer, a bishop.
- **vv21–26 "went in and out among us… a witness of His resurrection… who know the hearts of all… They cast lots… Matthias"** — *went in and out*, as Moses asked for one *who will go out before them and come in before them* (Numbers 27:17). *Kardiognōsta*, **who know the hearts**, as *the LORD looks at the heart* (1 Samuel 16:7). The lot was the old way of finding the LORD's choice, as the land was divided *by lot* (Numbers 26:55). Twelve again, for the twelve tribes (Luke 22:30).

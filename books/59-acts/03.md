# Acts 3

## The Lame Man Healed

**1** Peter and John were going up to the temple at the hour of prayer, the ninth hour.

**2** A man lame from his mother's womb was being carried in; they would set him down every day at the gate of the temple called Beautiful, to ask alms of those going into the temple.

**3** When he saw Peter and John about to go into the temple, he asked to receive alms.

**4** Peter looked straight at him, with John, and said, "Look at us."

**5** He gave them his attention, expecting to receive something from them.

**6** But Peter said, "I have no silver and gold, but what I have I give you: in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, walk!"

**7** He took him by the right hand and raised him up; and at once his feet and ankles were made strong.

**8** Leaping up, he stood and began to walk, and he went with them into the temple, walking and leaping and praising God.

**9** All the people saw him walking and praising God,

**10** and they recognized him as the one who used to sit begging for alms at the Beautiful Gate of the temple; and they were filled with wonder and amazement at what had happened to him.

## Peter Speaks in Solomon's Colonnade

**11** While he was holding on to Peter and John, all the people ran together to them in the colonnade called Solomon's, utterly astounded.

**12** When Peter saw it, he answered the people: "Men of Israel, why do you wonder at this, or why do you stare at us, as though by our own power or godliness we had made him walk?

**13** The God of Abraham and of Isaac and of Jacob, the God of our fathers, has glorified His servant Jesus, whom you handed over and denied before Pilate, when he had decided to release Him.

**14** But you denied the Holy and Righteous One and asked for a murderer to be granted to you,

**15** and you killed the Author of life, whom God raised from the dead. Of this we are witnesses.

**16** By faith in His name, His name has made this man strong, whom you see and know; and the faith that comes through Him has given him this perfect health in the presence of you all.

**17** "Now, brothers, I know that you acted in ignorance, as did also your rulers.

**18** But what God foretold by the mouth of all the prophets, that His Messiah would suffer, He has fulfilled in this way.

**19** Repent therefore, and turn back, so that your sins may be wiped out,

**20** that times of refreshing may come from the presence of the Lord, and that He may send the Messiah appointed for you, Jesus,

**21** whom heaven must receive until the times of the restoring of all things, of which God spoke by the mouth of His holy prophets from of old.

**22** Moses said, 'The Lord your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your brothers. You shall listen to him in everything he says to you.

**23** Every soul that does not listen to that prophet shall be utterly cut off from the people.'

**24** All the prophets who have spoken, from Samuel and those who came after him, also proclaimed these days.

**25** You are the sons of the prophets and of the covenant that God made with your fathers, saying to Abraham, 'In your seed all the families of the earth shall be blessed.'

**26** God, having raised up His servant, sent Him to you first, to bless you by turning each of you from your wicked ways."

## Notes

- **vv11–16 "the colonnade called Solomon's… has glorified His servant Jesus… the Holy and Righteous One… the Author of life"** — the colonnade where Jesus walked (John 10:23). *The God of Abraham and of Isaac and of Jacob* is the name God gave at the bush (Exodus 3:15). *Edoxasen ton paida autou*, **has glorified His servant**: Isaiah's servant *will rise high and be lifted up*, and the Greek of Isaiah has *ho pais mou… doxasthēsetai*, **My servant… will be glorified** (Isaiah 52:13). *The righteous one, My servant* (Isaiah 53:11). *Archēgos*, **Author**, also **leader**, **pioneer**, the one who begins and leads: they asked for a man who took life and killed the one who gives it.
- **vv22–23 "a prophet like me… every soul that does not listen to that prophet shall be utterly cut off"** — Deuteronomy 18:15, *A prophet from among you, from your brothers, like me, the LORD your God will raise up for you. To him you shall listen*; then 18:19, *whoever will not listen to My words… I Myself will require it of him*, which Peter gives in the words of the law's *cut off from his people* (Leviticus 23:29).

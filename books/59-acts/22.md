# Acts 22

## The Road to Damascus Told Again

**1** "Brothers and fathers, listen to the defense I now make to you."

**2** When they heard that he was addressing them in the Hebrew language, they became even quieter. He said:

**3** "I am a Jew, born in Tarsus of Cilicia but brought up in this city at the feet of Gamaliel, trained strictly in the law of our fathers, being zealous for God as all of you are today.

**4** I persecuted this Way to the death, binding and putting into prisons both men and women,

**5** as the high priest and the whole council of elders can testify about me. From them I received letters to the brothers, and I went to Damascus to take those who were there and bring them in chains to Jerusalem to be punished.

**6** As I was on my way and drawing near to Damascus, about noon, suddenly a great light from heaven flashed around me.

**7** I fell to the ground and heard a voice saying to me, 'Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting Me?'

**8** I answered, 'Who are You, Lord?' He said to me, 'I am Jesus of Nazareth, whom you are persecuting.'

**9** Those who were with me saw the light but did not hear the voice of the One who was speaking to me.

**10** I said, 'What shall I do, Lord?' The Lord said to me, 'Get up and go into Damascus, and there you will be told about everything that has been appointed for you to do.'

**11** Since I could not see because of the brightness of that light, I was led by the hand by those who were with me, and arrived in Damascus.

**12** A man named Ananias, devout according to the Law, well spoken of by all the Jews who lived there,

**13** came to me, and standing beside me said, 'Brother Saul, regain your sight.' At that very hour I regained my sight and looked at him.

**14** He said, 'The God of our fathers has appointed you to know His will, to see the Righteous One, and to hear a voice from His mouth,

**15** for you will be His witness to all people of what you have seen and heard.

**16** Now why do you wait? Get up, be baptized, and wash away your sins, calling on His name.'

## Sent to the Gentiles

**17** When I had returned to Jerusalem and was praying in the temple, I fell into a trance

**18** and saw Him saying to me, 'Hurry and get out of Jerusalem quickly, because they will not accept your testimony about Me.'

**19** I said, 'Lord, they themselves know that in one synagogue after another I imprisoned and beat those who believed in You.

**20** When the blood of Stephen Your witness was being shed, I myself was standing by and approving, and guarding the garments of those who were killing him.'

**21** He said to me, 'Go, for I will send you far away to the Gentiles.'"

## A Roman Citizen

**22** Up to this word they listened to him. Then they raised their voices and said, "Away with such a man from the earth! He should not be allowed to live!"

**23** As they were shouting and throwing off their garments and flinging dust into the air,

**24** the commander ordered him to be brought into the barracks, and said he should be examined by flogging, to find out why they were shouting against him like this.

**25** But when they had stretched him out with the straps, Paul said to the centurion who was standing there, "Is it lawful for you to flog a man who is a Roman and uncondemned?"

**26** When the centurion heard this, he went to the commander and reported it, saying, "What are you about to do? This man is a Roman."

**27** The commander came and said to him, "Tell me, are you a Roman?" He said, "Yes."

**28** The commander answered, "I bought this citizenship for a large sum." Paul said, "But I was born a citizen."

**29** At once those who were about to examine him drew back from him, and the commander too was afraid when he learned that he was a Roman and that he had bound him.

**30** The next day, wanting to know for certain why he was being accused by the Jews, he released him and ordered the chief priests and all the council to meet. He brought Paul down and set him before them.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "Brothers and fathers, listen… at the feet of Gamaliel… being zealous for God as all of you are today"** — Stephen began his defense with the same words, *Brothers and fathers, listen* (7:2). *Gamaliel*, the teacher who urged the council to let the apostles go (5:34). *Zēlōtēs*, **zealous**, the word the elders used of the believing thousands, *all zealous for the Law* (21:20).
- **vv6–11 "about noon… I am Jesus of Nazareth… Those who were with me saw the light but did not hear the voice"** — the account of 9:3–8 again, in the same words where the Greek is the same, with *about noon* and *of Nazareth* added. In 9:7 the men were *hearing the voice*, *akouontes… tēs phōnēs*; here they *did not hear the voice of the One who was speaking to me*, *tēn de phōnēn ouk ēkousan tou lalountos moi*. The two can be read as a sound heard and words not understood, or as two tellings that differ in a detail. The Greek allows the first and does not require it.
- **vv12–16 "devout according to the Law… the Righteous One… wash away your sins, calling on His name"** — in chapter 9 Ananias is *a disciple*; to this crowd Paul calls him *devout according to the Law*, *well spoken of by all the Jews*. *Ton dikaion*, **the Righteous One**, Stephen's name for Jesus, *the coming of the Righteous One* (7:52). *Calling on His name*, as Peter quoted Joel, *Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved* (2:21).
- **vv17–21 "I fell into a trance… I will send you far away to the Gentiles"** — *ekstasei*, **a trance**, the word of Peter's vision on the roof (10:10). *Eis ethnē makran*, **far away to the Gentiles**: Peter's promise was *for all who are far off* (2:39).
- **v20 "the blood of Stephen Your witness… standing by and approving"** — *martyros*, **witness**, the word of *you will be My witnesses* (1:8). *Syneudokōn*, **approving**, the word of 8:1, *Saul approved of his killing*; Paul uses it of himself.
- **vv22–23 "Away with such a man from the earth!… throwing off their garments and flinging dust into the air"** — *Aire*, **away with**, the cry of 21:36 and of the crowd before Pilate (Luke 23:18). The word *Gentiles* is the one that breaks the hearing.
- **vv24–29 "examined by flogging… a Roman and uncondemned… I bought this citizenship for a large sum… But I was born a citizen"** — *akatakriton*, **uncondemned**, the word Paul used at Philippi, *They beat us in public, uncondemned, men who are Romans* (16:37). There it was said after the beating; here, before. The commander bought what Paul was born with, and the one who *had bound him* is now *afraid*.

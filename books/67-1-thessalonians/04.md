# 1 Thessalonians 4

## A Life Pleasing to God

**1** Finally, then, brothers, we ask and urge you in the Lord Jesus that, as you received from us how you ought to walk and to please God, as you are doing, you do so more and more.

**2** For you know what instructions we gave you through the Lord Jesus.

**3** For this is the will of God, your holiness: that you keep away from sexual immorality,

**4** that each one of you know how to gain his own vessel in holiness and honor,

**5** not in the passion of lust like the Gentiles who do not know God;

**6** that no one go too far and wrong his brother in this matter, because the Lord is the avenger in all these things, as we told you before and solemnly warned you.

**7** For God has not called us to impurity but in holiness.

**8** So whoever rejects this rejects not man but God, who gives His Holy Spirit to you.

**9** Now about love of the brothers, you have no need for anyone to write to you, for you yourselves have been taught by God to love one another,

**10** and indeed you do it to all the brothers throughout Macedonia. But we urge you, brothers, to do so more and more,

**11** and to make it your ambition to live quietly, to mind your own affairs, and to work with your hands, as we instructed you,

**12** so that you may walk properly before outsiders and be dependent on no one.

## Those Who Have Fallen Asleep

**13** We do not want you to be uninformed, brothers, about those who are falling asleep, so that you may not grieve as others do who have no hope.

**14** For since we believe that Jesus died and rose again, even so, through Jesus, God will bring with Him those who have fallen asleep.

**15** For this we tell you by a word of the Lord: we who are alive, who are left until the coming of the Lord, will not go before those who have fallen asleep.

**16** For the Lord Himself will come down from heaven with a cry of command, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God, and the dead in Christ will rise first.

**17** Then we who are alive, who are left, will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air; and so we will always be with the Lord.

**18** So comfort one another with these words.

## Notes

- **v4 "gain his own vessel"** — *to heautou skeuos ktasthai*. *Skeuos*, **vessel, jar**, is the body in *we have this treasure in clay pots* (2 Corinthians 4:7), and a wife in *the wife as the weaker vessel* (1 Peter 3:7); *ktaomai* is **to acquire**, and also **to keep, to master**. So the verse can mean *learn to control your own body* or *take a wife of your own*, in holiness and not in lust. *Vessel* keeps both.
- **v17 "caught up… to meet the Lord in the air"** — *eis apantēsin*, the word for going out to meet someone on the road and coming back with him: *the brothers… came as far as the Forum of Appius and Three Taverns to meet us* (Acts 28:15, *eis apantēsin*). Those who are caught up go out to meet the Lord as He comes. *Caught up* is *harpazō*, the verb of Paul *caught up* to the third heaven (2 Corinthians 12:2).

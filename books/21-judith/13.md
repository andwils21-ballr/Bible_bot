# Judith 13

## Alone in the Tent

**1** When evening came, his servants hurried away. Bagoas closed the tent from outside and shut out the attendants from his lord's presence, and they went off to their beds, for they were all weary because the drinking had gone on so long.

**2** Judith was left alone in the tent, with Holofernes sprawled on his bed, for he was drowned in wine.

**3** Judith had told her maid to stand outside her bedroom and wait for her to come out, as she did every day, for she said she would be going out to pray. She had said the same to Bagoas.

**4** So all went out from his presence, and no one, small or great, was left in the bedroom. Judith stood beside his bed and said in her heart, "O Lord God of all power, look in this hour on the work of my hands, for the exaltation of Jerusalem.

**5** For now is the time to help Your inheritance, and to carry out my plan for the destruction of the enemies who have risen against us."

**6** She went to the bedpost near Holofernes' head and took down his sword from it.

**7** She came close to the bed, took hold of the hair of his head and said, "Give me strength, O God of Israel, this day!"

**8** Then she struck his neck twice with all her might and cut off his head.

**9** She rolled his body off the bed and pulled down the canopy from the posts. Soon after, she went out and gave Holofernes' head to her maid,

**10** who put it in her food bag. The two of them went out together, as they were accustomed to do for prayer. They passed through the camp, went around the valley, went up the mountain to Bethulia and came to its gates.

## "God, Our God, Is with Us"

**11** From a distance Judith called to the watchmen at the gates, "Open, open the gate! God, our God, is with us, to show His strength in Israel and His might against our enemies, as He has done today!"

**12** When the men of her city heard her voice, they hurried down to the gate of their city and called the elders of the city.

**13** They all ran together, small and great, for it seemed past belief to them that she had come back. They opened the gate and welcomed them, and lit a fire for light and gathered around them.

**14** She said to them with a loud voice, "Praise God, praise Him! Praise God, who has not withdrawn His mercy from the house of Israel, but has destroyed our enemies by my hand this very night!"

**15** Then she took the head out of the bag and showed it to them, and said, "Here is the head of Holofernes, the commander in chief of the army of Assyria, and here is the canopy under which he lay in his drunkenness. The Lord has struck him down by the hand of a woman.

**16** As the Lord lives, who has protected me on the way I went, it was my face that deceived him to his destruction, and he committed no sin with me to defile or shame me."

**17** All the people were greatly astonished. They bowed down and worshiped God, and said with one accord, "Blessed are You, our God, who have brought to nothing the enemies of Your people today."

**18** Uzziah said to her, "Blessed are you, daughter, by the Most High God, above all the women on earth; and blessed be the Lord God, who created the heavens and the earth, who has guided you to strike the head of the leader of our enemies.

**19** Your hope will never depart from the hearts of those who remember the power of God.

**20** May God make this redound to your everlasting honor, and may He visit you with blessings, because you did not spare your own life when our nation was brought low, but went out to meet our ruin, walking straight before our God." All the people said, "Amen, amen."

## Notes

- **vv6–8 "took down his sword… cut off his head"** — as David killed Goliath with the Philistine's own sword, *and cut off his head with it* (1 Samuel 17:51). Like Jael, who killed Sisera in her tent while he slept (Judges 4:21), Judith does it alone.
- **v10 "as they were accustomed to do for prayer"** — the nightly going out she had arranged on the first night (12:6–7) is how she leaves the camp with his head.
- **v11 "God, our God, is with us"** — *meth' hēmōn ho theos*, the words of Isaiah's sign, *God is with us* (Isaiah 8:10 in the Greek), the meaning of *Immanuel*.
- **v15 "The Lord has struck him down by the hand of a woman"** — her prayer answered in its own words (9:10).
- **v16 "it was my face that deceived him… he committed no sin with me"** — the deceit she prayed for (9:10, 13) worked, and she declares that it went no further.
- **v18 "Blessed are you, daughter, by the Most High God, above all the women on earth; and blessed be the Lord God, who created the heavens and the earth"** — Melchizedek's blessing of Abram, *Blessed be Abram by God Most High, who created heaven and earth* (Genesis 14:19 in the Greek), and Deborah's of Jael, *Most blessed of women be Jael* (Judges 5:24).
- **v20 "Amen, amen"** — *genoito, genoito*, **so be it, so be it**, the Greek for the Hebrew *amen*.

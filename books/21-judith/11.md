# Judith 11

## Holofernes Welcomes Her

**1** Holofernes said to her, "Take courage, woman. Do not be afraid in your heart, for I have never harmed anyone who chose to serve Nebuchadnezzar, the king of the whole earth.

**2** Even now, if your people who live in the hill country had not despised me, I would not have raised my spear against them. They have brought this on themselves.

**3** But now tell me why you have run away from them and come to us. You have come to safety. Take courage; you will live tonight and from now on.

**4** No one will harm you; all will treat you well, as is done for the servants of my lord King Nebuchadnezzar."

## Judith's Speech

**5** Judith said to him, "Accept the words of your servant, and let your maidservant speak in your presence. I will tell my lord no lie tonight.

**6** If you follow the words of your maidservant, God will accomplish a thing fully with you, and my lord will not fail in his undertakings.

**7** For as Nebuchadnezzar king of the whole earth lives, and as his power lives, who has sent you to set every living thing in order, not only do men serve him because of you, but through your strength the beasts of the field, the cattle and the birds of the air will live for Nebuchadnezzar and all his house.

**8** For we have heard of your wisdom and the shrewdness of your mind, and it has been reported in all the earth that you alone are good in the whole kingdom, mighty in knowledge and wonderful in the art of war.

**9** Now as for what Achior said in your council, we have heard his words, for the men of Bethulia rescued him, and he told them all that he had said to you.

**10** So, my master and lord, do not disregard his word, but keep it in your heart, for it is true. Our race is not punished, nor does the sword prevail against them, unless they sin against their God.

**11** And now, so that my lord may not be thwarted and his purpose fail, death will fall upon them, for a sin has overtaken them by which they will provoke their God to anger whenever they do what is wrong.

**12** Since their food has run out and all their water is scarce, they have planned to lay hands on their cattle, and they have decided to consume everything that God by His laws forbade them to eat.

**13** They have resolved to use up the firstfruits of the grain and the tithes of the wine and oil, which they had consecrated and set apart for the priests who stand before the face of our God in Jerusalem, things that none of the people may even touch with their hands.

**14** They have sent men to Jerusalem, because those who live there have done the same, to bring them permission from the council.

**15** When the word reaches them and they act on it, on that very day they will be given up to you to be destroyed.

**16** So I, your servant, knowing all this, have fled from them, and God has sent me to do with you things at which the whole earth will be astonished, whoever hears of them.

**17** For your servant is devout and serves the God of heaven night and day. Now I will stay with you, my lord, and your servant will go out each night into the valley, and I will pray to God, and He will tell me when they have committed their sins.

**18** Then I will come and tell you, and you shall go out with all your army, and not one of them will withstand you.

**19** I will lead you through Judea until you come to Jerusalem, and I will set your throne in the middle of it. You will drive them like sheep that have no shepherd, and not a dog will so much as growl at you. For this was told me by my foreknowledge; it was made known to me, and I have been sent to tell you."

## Holofernes Pleased

**20** Her words pleased Holofernes and all his servants. They marveled at her wisdom and said,

**21** "There is no woman like her from one end of the earth to the other, for beauty of face and wisdom of speech."

**22** Holofernes said to her, "God has done well to send you ahead of the people, to put strength in our hands and destruction on those who have despised my lord.

**23** Now, you are beautiful in appearance and wise in your words. If you do as you have said, your God will be my God, and you will live in the house of King Nebuchadnezzar and be famous throughout the whole earth."

## Notes

- **vv5–6 "I will tell my lord no lie… God will accomplish a thing fully with you"** — Judith's words carry two meanings throughout. *My lord*, *kyrios*, is Holofernes to him and the Lord to her, and the *thing* God will accomplish *with you* is his death.
- **v7 "as Nebuchadnezzar king of the whole earth lives"** — she swears by the king's life, in his own title (2:5; 6:4), and says nothing true by it.
- **v10 "Our race is not punished… unless they sin against their God"** — Achior's own words (5:17–21), which Holofernes rejected, now given back to him as the key to the city.
- **vv12–13 "everything that God by His laws forbade them to eat… the firstfruits… which… none of the people may even touch"** — the holy portions belong to the priests: *no outsider shall eat of a holy thing* (Leviticus 22:10). The sin she invents is one a faithful people would fear above hunger.
- **v19 "like sheep that have no shepherd, and not a dog will so much as growl at you"** — two scriptures in one sentence: Micaiah's vision of *all Israel scattered on the mountains, like sheep that have no shepherd* (1 Kings 22:17), and the night of the Passover, *not a dog shall growl* against Israel (Exodus 11:7, in the Greek). Both are turned to Holofernes' ear.
- **v23 "your God will be my God"** — the words of Ruth to Naomi, *your people shall be my people, and your God my God* (Ruth 1:16), in the mouth of a man who will never say them truly.

# Judith 5

## Holofernes Asks

**1** It was reported to Holofernes, the commander in chief of the army of Assyria, that the sons of Israel had prepared for war, had shut the passes of the hill country, fortified every high hilltop and set traps in the plains.

**2** He was furious, and he called all the leaders of Moab, the generals of Ammon and all the governors of the coast,

**3** and said to them, "Tell me now, you sons of Canaan, who is this people that lives in the hill country? What cities do they live in, how large is their army, and in what does their power and strength lie? Who has risen up as their king and leads their army?

**4** Why have they refused to come out to meet me, alone of all who live in the west?"

## Achior's Answer

**5** Achior, the leader of all the sons of Ammon, said to him, "Let my lord now hear a word from the mouth of your servant, and I will tell you the truth about this people who live in the hill country near where you are camped. No lie shall come from the mouth of your servant.

**6** This people is descended from the Chaldeans.

**7** They once lived as guests in Mesopotamia, because they would not follow the gods of their fathers who were in the land of the Chaldeans.

**8** They left the way of their ancestors and worshiped the God of heaven, the God they had come to know. So their people drove them out from the presence of their gods, and they fled to Mesopotamia and lived there as guests for many days.

**9** Then their God told them to leave the place where they were guests and go into the land of Canaan. They settled there and grew rich in gold and silver and in very many cattle.

**10** When a famine covered the face of the land of Canaan, they went down into Egypt and lived there as guests as long as they had food. There they became a great multitude, so that their race could not be counted.

**11** But the king of Egypt rose up against them and took advantage of them with hard labor and brickmaking. They humbled them and made them slaves.

**12** They cried out to their God, and He struck the land of Egypt with plagues for which there was no cure. So the Egyptians drove them out of their sight.

**13** God dried up the Red Sea before them,

**14** and led them by the way of Sinai and Kadesh-barnea. They drove out all who lived in the desert,

**15** and they lived in the land of the Amorites and by their strength destroyed all the people of Heshbon. Then they crossed the Jordan and took possession of all the hill country.

**16** They drove out before them the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Jebusite, the people of Shechem and all the Girgashites, and they lived there for many days.

**17** As long as they did not sin before their God, good things were with them, for a God who hates wrongdoing is with them.

**18** But when they turned away from the way He had set for them, they were utterly destroyed in many wars and were carried captive to a land not their own. The temple of their God was razed to the ground, and their cities were taken by their enemies.

**19** But now they have turned back to their God and have come up from the dispersion where they were scattered. They have taken possession of Jerusalem, where their sanctuary is, and have settled in the hill country, because it was empty.

**20** So now, my master and lord, if there is any error in this people, and they sin against their God, and we find out that they have this offense among them, then we will go up and defeat them.

**21** But if there is no lawlessness in their nation, let my lord pass them by, or their Lord and God will defend them, and we will be put to shame before the whole earth."

## The Anger of the Camp

**22** When Achior had finished saying these things, all the people standing around the tent murmured, and the nobles of Holofernes and all who lived by the sea and in Moab said he should be cut to pieces.

**23** "We will not be afraid of the sons of Israel. They are a people with no power or strength for a hard battle.

**24** So we will go up, master Holofernes, and they will be devoured by your whole army."

## Notes

- **v5 "Achior, the leader of all the sons of Ammon"** — an Ammonite, of the people the law shuts out of the assembly of the LORD (Deuteronomy 23:3). He is the one in the enemy's camp who tells the truth about Israel's God.
- **vv6–8 "descended from the Chaldeans… they would not follow the gods of their fathers"** — Abram came from *Ur of the Chaldeans* (Genesis 11:31), and Joshua says *your fathers… served other gods* beyond the river (Joshua 24:2). Achior tells of a family driven out for leaving its fathers' gods.
- **vv7–10 "lived as guests"** — *paroikeō*, the Greek for *gur*, the verb of the guest (Genesis 12:10; 47:4).
- **vv17–21 "As long as they did not sin before their God, good things were with them"** — the whole history in one law: blessing for keeping the way, defeat and exile for leaving it. Achior's advice rests on it: find out whether they have sinned.
- **v21 "their Lord and God will defend them"** — *hyperaspizō*, **to hold a shield over**. The next chapter's question answers it: *Who is God except Nebuchadnezzar?* (6:2).

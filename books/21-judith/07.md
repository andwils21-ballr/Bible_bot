# Judith 7

## The Army Moves on Bethulia

**1** The next day Holofernes ordered all his army and all the people who had come to join him as allies to break camp against Bethulia, to seize the passes into the hill country and to make war on the sons of Israel.

**2** That day all their fighting men broke camp. Their force of warriors was a hundred and seventy thousand foot soldiers and twelve thousand horsemen, not counting the baggage and the men on foot who handled it, a very great multitude.

**3** They camped in the valley near Bethulia, beside the spring, and spread out in breadth over Dothan as far as Belbaim, and in length from Bethulia to Cyamon, which faces Esdraelon.

**4** When the sons of Israel saw their multitude, they were greatly alarmed, and they said to one another, "Now these men will lick up the face of the whole land. Neither the high mountains nor the ravines nor the hills will bear their weight."

**5** Each man took up his weapons, and they lit fires on their towers and stayed on guard all that night.

## The Springs Seized

**6** On the second day Holofernes led out all his cavalry in view of the sons of Israel who were in Bethulia.

**7** He examined the approaches to their city, visited the springs of water and seized them, and set guards of soldiers over them. Then he went back to his own people.

**8** All the rulers of the sons of Esau, all the leaders of the people of Moab and the generals of the coast came to him and said,

**9** "Let our master hear a word, so that no loss may come to your army.

**10** For this people, the sons of Israel, do not rely on their spears but on the heights of the mountains where they live, because it is not easy to reach the tops of their mountains.

**11** So now, master, do not fight against them in battle array, and not one man of your army will fall.

**12** Stay in your camp and keep every man of your army safe, and let your servants take possession of the spring of water that flows from the foot of the mountain,

**13** for all who live in Bethulia draw their water from there. Thirst will destroy them, and they will give up their city. We and our people will go up to the nearby mountaintops and camp on them as an outpost, so that not one man gets out of the city.

**14** They and their wives and children will waste away with hunger, and before the sword reaches them they will be strewn about in the streets where they live.

**15** So you will pay them back with an evil reward, because they rebelled and did not come out to meet you in peace."

**16** Their words pleased Holofernes and all his servants, and he gave orders to do as they had said.

**17** So the army of the sons of Ammon moved out, and with them five thousand Assyrians, and they camped in the valley and seized the water supply and the springs of the sons of Israel.

**18** The sons of Esau and the sons of Ammon went up and camped in the hill country opposite Dothan, and they sent some of their men to the south and the east, opposite Egrebel, which is near Chus beside the brook Mochmur. The rest of the Assyrian army camped in the plain and covered the whole face of the land. Their tents and baggage were spread out in a vast crowd, a very great multitude.

## Thirst in the City

**19** The sons of Israel cried out to the Lord their God, for their spirit failed, because all their enemies had surrounded them and there was no escape from among them.

**20** The whole army of Assyria, their foot soldiers, chariots and horsemen, remained around them thirty-four days, until all the water jars of everyone who lived in Bethulia were empty.

**21** Their cisterns were running dry, and they did not have enough water to drink their fill for a single day, because their drink was given them by measure.

**22** Their little children lost heart, and the women and young men fainted from thirst and fell in the streets of the city and in the gateways. There was no strength left in them.

## The People Demand Surrender

**23** Then all the people, the young men, the women and the children, gathered around Uzziah and the rulers of the city and cried out with a loud voice, and said before all the elders,

**24** "God judge between you and us! You have done us a great wrong in not making peace with the sons of Assyria.

**25** Now we have no one to help us. God has sold us into their hands, to be strewn before them in thirst and great destruction.

**26** Now call them in and surrender the whole city to be plundered by the people of Holofernes and all his army.

**27** It is better for us to be their spoil. We will be slaves, but we will live, and we will not see our little ones die before our eyes, or our wives and children breathing their last.

**28** We call to witness against you heaven and earth, and our God, the Lord of our fathers, who punishes us for our sins and the sins of our fathers. Do today what we have said!"

**29** There was great weeping in the assembly, all with one voice, and they cried out to the Lord God with a loud voice.

**30** Uzziah said to them, "Take courage, brothers. Let us hold out five more days, in which the Lord our God will turn His mercy to us, for He will not forsake us utterly.

**31** But if these days pass and no help comes to us, I will do as you say."

**32** He sent the people away, each to his post, and they went to the walls and towers of their city. He sent the women and children home. They were in great distress in the city.

## Notes

- **v4 "Now these men will lick up the face of the whole land"** — *eklixousin*, the picture of Moab's fear of Israel, *as the ox licks up the grass of the field* (Numbers 22:4). Now Israel is the land about to be licked bare.
- **vv12–13 "take possession of the spring of water… Thirst will destroy them"** — the Edomites and Moabites, Israel's own neighbors, teach the Assyrian how to take the town without a battle.
- **v19 "their spirit failed"** — *ōligopsychēsen*, **their soul grew small**, the word of Israel on the way around Edom, when *the soul of the people grew impatient* (Numbers 21:4).
- **vv24–28 "You have done us a great wrong… It is better for us to be their spoil"** — the cry of the wilderness: *Why did you bring us up out of Egypt, to kill us… with thirst?* (Exodus 17:3). *We call to witness against you heaven and earth* turns Moses' words against the rulers (Deuteronomy 30:19).
- **vv30–31 "Let us hold out five more days… I will do as you say"** — the vow that Judith will rebuke in the next chapter: a deadline set for God.

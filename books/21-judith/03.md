# Judith 3

## The Offer of Peace

**1** So they sent messengers to him with words of peace, saying,

**2** "We, the servants of Nebuchadnezzar the great king, lie before you. Do with us as seems good to you.

**3** Our farmsteads, all our wheat fields, our flocks and herds and all the sheepfolds of our tents lie before you. Do with them as you please.

**4** Our cities also and those who live in them are your slaves. Come and deal with them as seems good in your eyes."

**5** The men came to Holofernes and reported these words to him.

## The Gods Destroyed

**6** Then he came down to the seacoast with his army and put garrisons in the fortified cities, and took picked men from them as auxiliaries.

**7** They and all the country around welcomed him with garlands and dancing and tambourines.

**8** Yet he demolished all their territory and cut down their sacred groves, for it had been given to him to destroy all the gods of the land, so that all the nations would worship Nebuchadnezzar alone, and all their tongues and tribes would call on him as god.

**9** Then he came toward Esdraelon, near Dothan, which faces the great ridge of Judea,

**10** and they camped between Geba and Scythopolis, and he stayed there a whole month to gather all the baggage of his army.

## Notes

- **v8 "to destroy all the gods of the land, so that all the nations would worship Nebuchadnezzar alone… call on him as god"** — the claim the book is built to answer. Daniel's Nebuchadnezzar sets up an image for *all peoples, nations and languages* to worship (Daniel 3:4–5).

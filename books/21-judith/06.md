# Judith 6

## "Who Is God Except Nebuchadnezzar?"

**1** When the uproar of the men around the council had died down, Holofernes, the commander in chief of the army of Assyria, said to Achior in front of the whole crowd of foreigners,

**2** "Who are you, Achior, you and the hirelings of Ephraim, to prophesy among us as you have today, and to tell us not to make war on the race of Israel because their God will defend them? Who is God except Nebuchadnezzar? He will send his power and destroy them from the face of the earth, and their God will not deliver them.

**3** We his servants will strike them down as one man. They will not withstand the might of our horses.

**4** We will burn them up in their own land. Their mountains will be drunk with their blood, and their plains will be filled with their dead. They will not be able to stand their ground before us, but will utterly perish, says King Nebuchadnezzar, the lord of the whole earth. For he has spoken; none of his words will be in vain.

**5** As for you, Achior, you hireling of Ammon, who have spoken these words in the day of your wrongdoing, you will not see my face again from this day until I take vengeance on this race that came out of Egypt.

**6** Then the iron of my army and the people of my servants will pierce your sides, and you will fall among their wounded when I return.

**7** My servants will take you back into the hill country and leave you in one of the cities along the passes,

**8** and you will not die until you are destroyed with them.

**9** If you really hope in your heart that they will not be taken, then do not look so downcast. I have spoken, and none of my words will fail."

## Achior at Bethulia

**10** Then Holofernes ordered his servants who stood in his tent to seize Achior and take him back to Bethulia and hand him over to the sons of Israel.

**11** So his servants seized him and took him out of the camp into the plain, and from the plain they went up into the hill country and came to the springs below Bethulia.

**12** When the men of the city saw them on the top of the hill, they took up their weapons and went out of the city to the top of the hill, and all the slingers kept them from coming up by throwing stones at them.

**13** So they slipped down under the hill, bound Achior and left him lying at the foot of the hill, and went back to their lord.

**14** Then the sons of Israel came down from their city and stood over him. They untied him and brought him into Bethulia and set him before the rulers of their city,

**15** who in those days were Uzziah son of Micah, of the tribe of Simeon, Chabris son of Gothoniel, and Charmis son of Melchiel.

**16** They called together all the elders of the city, and all their young men and their women ran to the assembly. They set Achior in the middle of all their people, and Uzziah asked him what had happened.

**17** He answered and told them what had been said in the council of Holofernes, and all that he himself had said among the leaders of the sons of Assyria, and all the boasts Holofernes had made against the house of Israel.

**18** Then the people fell down and worshiped God, and cried out,

**19** "O Lord God of heaven, see their arrogance, and have pity on the lowliness of our race, and look today on the faces of those who are consecrated to You."

**20** Then they reassured Achior and praised him highly.

**21** Uzziah took him from the assembly to his house and gave a banquet for the elders, and all that night they called on the God of Israel for help.

## Notes

- **v2 "Who is God except Nebuchadnezzar?"** — the book's question, asked outright. The words echo the psalm, *For who is God except the LORD?* (Psalm 18:31), with a king's name put in the LORD's place.
- **v4 "says King Nebuchadnezzar… For he has spoken; none of his words will be in vain"** — the speech of a prophet for his god: *Thus says… I have spoken* (2:12).
- **vv5–8 "you will not see my face again… until you are destroyed with them"** — Holofernes sends Achior to share Israel's fate. The next time Achior sees that face, it is a severed head in Bethulia (14:6).
- **v15 "Uzziah son of Micah, of the tribe of Simeon"** — Judith too is of Simeon (9:2), the tribe of the brother who avenged Dinah.
- **v19 "look today on the faces of those who are consecrated to You"** — set against *their arrogance*: the prayer asks God to compare the two.

# Judith 14

## Judith's Plan for the Morning

**1** Then Judith said to them, "Listen to me, brothers. Take this head and hang it on the battlement of your wall.

**2** And it shall come to pass, when the morning dawns and the sun rises on the earth, that each of you shall take up his weapons, and every able man shall go out of the city. Set a captain over them, as if you were going down into the plain against the Assyrian outpost, but do not go down.

**3** They will take up their armor and go into their camp and wake the generals of the army of Assyria. They will run together to the tent of Holofernes and will not find him. Then fear will fall on them, and they will flee before you.

**4** Then you and all who live in every territory of Israel shall pursue them and cut them down as they go.

**5** But before you do this, call Achior the Ammonite to me, so that he may see and recognize the man who despised the house of Israel and sent him to us as if to his death."

## Achior Believes

**6** So they called Achior from the house of Uzziah. When he came and saw the head of Holofernes in the hand of one of the men in the assembly of the people, he fell on his face and fainted.

**7** When they raised him up, he threw himself at Judith's feet and bowed down before her, and said, "Blessed are you in every tent of Judah and in every nation! Those who hear your name will be alarmed.

**8** Now tell me what you have done in these days." So Judith told him, in the presence of the people, all that she had done from the day she went out until the moment she was speaking to them.

**9** When she had finished speaking, the people raised a great shout and filled their city with sounds of joy.

**10** When Achior saw all that the God of Israel had done, he believed in God firmly. He was circumcised and joined to the house of Israel, and remains so to this day.

## The Body Found

**11** As soon as dawn came, they hung the head of Holofernes from the wall, and every man of Israel took up his weapons, and they went out in companies toward the passes of the mountain.

**12** When the Assyrians saw them, they sent word to their leaders, and they went to the generals, the commanders of thousands and all their officers.

**13** They came to the tent of Holofernes and said to the one in charge of all his affairs, "Wake our lord, for the slaves have dared to come down against us to battle, to be utterly destroyed."

**14** So Bagoas went in and knocked at the entrance of the tent, for he supposed that Holofernes was sleeping with Judith.

**15** When no one answered, he opened it and went into the bedroom, and found him thrown down dead on the floor, and his head had been cut off.

**16** He cried out with a loud voice, with weeping, groaning and a great shout, and tore his clothes.

**17** Then he went into the tent where Judith had stayed, and did not find her. He rushed out to the people, shouting,

**18** "The slaves have broken faith! One Hebrew woman has brought shame on the house of King Nebuchadnezzar! Holofernes is lying on the ground, and his head is not on him!"

**19** When the leaders of the army of Assyria heard these words, they tore their tunics, and they were greatly dismayed, and their cries and shouting rose very loud throughout the camp.

## Notes

- **v2 "And it shall come to pass, when the morning dawns"** — the future formula of the law and the prophets (*kai estai*), as Judith gives her orders.
- **v5 "so that he may see and recognize the man who despised the house of Israel"** — Holofernes sent Achior away saying *you will not see my face again* (6:5) until Israel was destroyed. He sees it now.
- **v7 "Blessed are you in every tent of Judah"** — the blessing of Jael, *blessed among women in the tent* (Judges 5:24), from the mouth of the Ammonite.
- **v10 "He was circumcised and joined to the house of Israel"** — the law shuts the Ammonite out of the assembly of the LORD (Deuteronomy 23:3). Achior, who told the truth about Israel's God in the enemy's camp, is let in.
- **v18 "One Hebrew woman has brought shame on the house of King Nebuchadnezzar"** — the answer, from the enemy's own mouth, to *Who is God except Nebuchadnezzar?* (6:2).

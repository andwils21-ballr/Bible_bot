# Judith 2

## The King's Command

**1** In the eighteenth year, on the twenty-second day of the first month, word went out in the house of Nebuchadnezzar king of the Assyrians that he would take vengeance on all the land, as he had said.

**2** He called together all his officials and all his nobles and set before them his secret plan, and with his own mouth he decreed all the evil to be done to the land.

**3** They decided to destroy all flesh that had not obeyed the word of his mouth.

**4** When he had finished setting out his plan, Nebuchadnezzar king of the Assyrians called Holofernes, the commander in chief of his army, who was second only to himself, and said to him,

**5** "Thus says the great king, the lord of the whole earth: You shall go out from my presence and take with you men confident in their strength, a hundred and twenty thousand foot soldiers and a body of horses with their riders, twelve thousand.

**6** You shall go out against all the land to the west, because they disobeyed the word of my mouth.

**7** Tell them to prepare earth and water, for I will go out against them in my anger. I will cover the whole face of the earth with the feet of my army, and I will give them to my army to plunder.

**8** Their wounded will fill their ravines and their streams, and a river will overflow, filled with their dead.

**9** I will lead their captives away to the ends of the whole earth.

**10** You shall go ahead of me and seize all their territory for me. They will surrender themselves to you, and you shall hold them for me until the day of their punishment.

**11** But on those who disobey, your eye shall have no pity; give them up to slaughter and plunder in all the land you hold.

**12** For as I live, and by the power of my kingdom, I have spoken, and I will do these things by my hand.

**13** As for you, do not disobey a single word of your lord, but carry it out fully, as I have commanded you, and do not delay in doing it."

## The March

**14** So Holofernes went out from the presence of his lord and called all the commanders, the generals and the officers of the army of Assyria,

**15** and he mustered picked men for battle, as his lord had commanded him, a hundred and twenty thousand, and twelve thousand mounted archers,

**16** and he organized them as a great army is drawn up for war.

**17** He took camels, donkeys and mules for their baggage, a very great number, and sheep, oxen and goats without number for their provision,

**18** and plenty of food for every man, and a great deal of gold and silver from the king's house.

**19** Then he set out with all his army to go ahead of King Nebuchadnezzar and to cover the whole face of the earth to the west with their chariots, horsemen and picked foot soldiers.

**20** A mixed crowd as many as locusts went out with them, like the sand of the earth, for they could not be counted for their multitude.

**21** They marched three days from Nineveh toward the plain of Bectileth, and camped from Bectileth near the mountain that is to the left of Upper Cilicia.

**22** From there he took all his army, his foot soldiers, horsemen and chariots, and went up into the hill country.

**23** He cut through Put and Lud, and plundered all the sons of Rassis and the sons of Ishmael who lived along the desert, south of the land of the Chaldeans.

**24** He crossed the Euphrates and went through Mesopotamia, and destroyed all the fortified cities along the brook Abron, as far as the sea.

**25** He seized the territory of Cilicia and cut down all who resisted him, and came to the borders of Japheth, toward the south, facing Arabia.

**26** He surrounded all the sons of Midian, burned their tents and plundered their sheepfolds.

**27** He went down into the plain of Damascus at the time of the wheat harvest, set fire to all their fields, destroyed their flocks and herds, looted their towns, swept their plains bare and struck down all their young men with the edge of the sword.

## Fear on the Coast

**28** Fear and trembling of him fell on all who lived along the seacoast, those in Sidon and Tyre, those who lived in Sur and Ocina, and all who lived in Jamnia. Those who lived in Azotus and Ascalon feared him greatly.

## Notes

- **v5 "Thus says the great king, the lord of the whole earth"** — the words of the prophets, *Thus says the LORD*, in the mouth of a king who claims the earth. The Rabshakeh spoke so for Sennacherib: *Thus says the great king, the king of Assyria* (2 Kings 18:19). The question of the book is whose title it is.
- **v7 "Tell them to prepare earth and water"** — the tokens of a land's surrender: its soil and its water handed over to the conqueror.
- **v12 "For as I live… I have spoken, and I will do these things"** — the oath of the LORD in the prophets, *As I live, declares the Lord GOD* (Ezekiel 33:11), and *I the LORD have spoken, and I will do it* (Ezekiel 36:36), here sworn by a man.
- **v20 "as many as locusts… like the sand of the earth"** — the armies of Midian in the days of Gideon, *as many as locusts… their camels without number, as the sand on the seashore* (Judges 7:12).
- **v23 "Put and Lud"** — peoples named in the table of nations (Genesis 10:6, 22). Many of the places in the march are not known elsewhere.

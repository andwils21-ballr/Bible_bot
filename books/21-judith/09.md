# Judith 9

## The Prayer at the Hour of Incense

**1** Judith fell on her face, put ashes on her head and uncovered the sackcloth she was wearing. At that very time the evening incense was being offered in Jerusalem in the house of God, and Judith cried out to the Lord with a loud voice and said,

**2** "O Lord God of my father Simeon, into whose hand You gave a sword to take vengeance on the foreigners who had violated the womb of a virgin to defile her, uncovered her thigh to shame her and polluted her womb to disgrace her. For You said, 'It shall not be so,' yet they did it.

**3** So You gave up their rulers to be killed, and their bed, which was ashamed of their deceit, to be stained with blood. You struck down slaves together with princes, and princes on their thrones.

**4** You gave their wives to be seized and their daughters to captivity, and all their spoil to be divided among the sons You loved, who were zealous with Your zeal and abhorred the pollution of their blood and called on You for help. O God, my God, hear me also, a widow.

**5** For You did what came before those things, and those things, and what followed. You planned the things that are now and the things to come, and what You planned came to be.

**6** The things You purposed presented themselves and said, 'Here we are!' For all Your ways are prepared, and Your judgment is with foreknowledge.

**7** For the Assyrians are many in their power. They are proud of horse and rider; they boast in the arm of their foot soldiers; they trust in shield and spear, bow and sling. They do not know that You are the Lord who crushes wars.

**8** The Lord is Your name. Shatter their strength by Your power, and bring down their might in Your anger. For they have planned to profane Your sanctuary, to pollute the tent where Your glorious name rests, and to cut off the horn of Your altar with the sword.

**9** Look at their pride. Send Your wrath on their heads. Give to my hand, a widow's hand, the strength for what I have planned.

**10** By the deceit of my lips strike down the slave with the prince and the prince with his servant. Break their pride by the hand of a woman.

**11** For Your power is not in numbers, nor Your might in the strong. You are the God of the lowly, the helper of the least, the protector of the weak, the shelter of the despairing, the savior of those without hope.

**12** Yes, yes, God of my father and God of the inheritance of Israel, Master of heaven and earth, Creator of the waters, King of all Your creation, hear my prayer,

**13** and make my words and my deceit a wound and a bruise to those who have planned cruel things against Your covenant and Your consecrated house, against the summit of Zion and the house that Your sons possess.

**14** Make Your whole nation and every tribe know and understand that You are God, the God of all power and might, and that there is no other who protects the race of Israel but You."

## Notes

- **v1 "At that very time the evening incense was being offered in Jerusalem"** — the psalm: *Let my prayer be set before You as incense, the lifting up of my hands as the evening offering* (Psalm 141:2). Judith prays at the hour of Israel's prayer.
- **vv2–4 "the God of my father Simeon, into whose hand You gave a sword"** — the vengeance of Simeon and Levi for their sister Dinah at Shechem (Genesis 34). Jacob cursed their anger for it: *Cursed be their anger, for it was fierce* (Genesis 49:7). Judith calls on it as zeal for God, the zeal she is about to need.
- **v2 "who had violated the womb of a virgin"** — *elysan mētran*, **loosed the womb**. Some copies read *mitran*, **the girdle**, one letter different. The verse names the rape of Dinah three times over, and the Greek is blunt.
- **vv5–6 "The things You purposed presented themselves and said, 'Here we are!'"** — God's plans stand before Him like servants answering a call.
- **vv7–8 "the Lord who crushes wars. The Lord is Your name"** — the song at the sea in the Greek: *The Lord crushes wars; the Lord is His name* (Exodus 15:3, where the Hebrew has *a man of war*).
- **v10 "Break their pride by the hand of a woman"** — as Deborah said Sisera would fall: *the LORD will sell Sisera into the hand of a woman* (Judges 4:9).
- **vv10, 13 "By the deceit of my lips… make my words and my deceit a wound"** — Judith asks openly for deceit as her weapon. The book does not hide it.
- **v11 "the God of the lowly… the savior of those without hope"** — the answer to the army that trusts in *shield and spear* (v7): a God whose power is not in numbers.

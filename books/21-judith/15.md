# Judith 15

## The Assyrians Flee

**1** When those who were in the tents heard it, they were astounded at what had happened.

**2** Fear and trembling fell on them, and no one stayed beside his neighbor any longer. Rushing out together, they fled by every path across the plain and through the hill country.

**3** Those who had camped in the hill country around Bethulia also turned to flight. Then the sons of Israel, every fighting man among them, poured out against them.

**4** Uzziah sent men to Betomasthaim, Chobai and Chola, and to every region of Israel, to report what had been done, and to have them all rush out against the enemy to destroy them.

**5** When the sons of Israel heard it, they all fell on them with one accord and cut them down as far as Choba. Those from Jerusalem and from all the hill country also came, for they had been told what had happened in the enemy camp. Those in Gilead and in Galilee outflanked them with a great slaughter, until they were past Damascus and its borders.

**6** The rest of the people of Bethulia fell on the Assyrian camp and plundered it, and they became very rich.

**7** The sons of Israel, when they came back from the slaughter, took possession of what was left, and the villages and farms in the hill country and the plain gained a great deal of spoil, for there was a vast quantity of it.

## The Blessing of Judith

**8** Joakim the high priest and the council of the sons of Israel who lived in Jerusalem came to see the good things the Lord had done for Israel, and to see Judith and greet her.

**9** When they came to her, they all blessed her with one accord and said to her, "You are the exaltation of Jerusalem, you are the great glory of Israel, you are the great pride of our nation!

**10** You have done all this with your own hand; you have done good things for Israel, and God is pleased with them. May you be blessed by the Lord Almighty for all time." All the people said, "Amen."

**11** All the people plundered the camp for thirty days. They gave Judith the tent of Holofernes and all his silver dishes, his beds, his bowls and all his furniture. She took them and loaded her mule, and hitched up her carts and piled them on.

## The Dance

**12** All the women of Israel ran together to see her, and they blessed her and performed a dance in her honor. She took ivy-wreathed wands in her hands and gave them to the women who were with her,

**13** and she and those who were with her crowned themselves with olive. She went before all the people in the dance, leading all the women, and all the men of Israel followed in their armor, wearing garlands and with songs on their lips.

**14** Judith began this thanksgiving before all Israel, and all the people loudly sang this song of praise.

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "Fear and trembling fell on them… they fled"** — Judith's plan (14:3) carried out to the word. No battle is fought; the army breaks when its head is gone.
- **v9 "You are the exaltation of Jerusalem… the great pride of our nation"** — the high priest's blessing, the elders' prayer at the gate fulfilled: *to the glory of the sons of Israel and the exaltation of Jerusalem* (10:8).
- **v11 "They gave Judith the tent of Holofernes"** — she gives it all to God in Jerusalem (16:19).
- **vv12–13 "a dance in her honor… She went before all the people in the dance, leading all the women"** — as Miriam the prophetess took a tambourine, *and all the women went out after her with tambourines and dancing* (Exodus 15:20). The song that follows is the song of the sea for Judith's day.

# Judith 8

## The Widow of Bethulia

**1** In those days Judith heard of it. She was the daughter of Merari son of Ox, son of Joseph, son of Oziel, son of Elkiah, son of Elijah, son of Hilkiah, son of Eliab, son of Nathanael, son of Salamiel, son of Sarasadai, son of Israel.

**2** Her husband Manasseh was of her own tribe and family. He had died during the barley harvest.

**3** For as he stood over the men binding sheaves in the field, the heat came upon his head, and he fell on his bed and died in Bethulia, his town. They buried him with his fathers in the field between Dothan and Balamon.

**4** Judith had remained a widow in her house three years and four months.

**5** She made herself a tent on the roof of her house, put sackcloth around her waist and wore the clothes of her widowhood.

**6** She fasted all the days of her widowhood, except the day before the sabbath and the sabbath, the day before the new moon and the new moon, and the festivals and days of rejoicing of the house of Israel.

**7** She was beautiful in appearance and very lovely to look at. Her husband Manasseh had left her gold and silver, male and female servants, livestock and fields, and she kept them up.

**8** No one spoke an evil word against her, for she feared God greatly.

## Judith Rebukes the Rulers

**9** She heard the bitter words of the people against their ruler, because they had lost heart for lack of water, and she heard all that Uzziah had said to them, how he had sworn to surrender the city to the Assyrians after five days.

**10** So she sent her maid, who was in charge of all she had, and called Chabris and Charmis, the elders of her city.

**11** They came to her, and she said to them, "Listen to me, rulers of those who live in Bethulia. What you said before the people today is not right. You have sworn this oath, which you have pronounced between God and yourselves, and said you would surrender the city to our enemies unless the Lord turns and helps you within these days.

**12** Who are you, that you have put God to the test today, and set yourselves up in the place of God among the sons of men?

**13** You are putting the Lord Almighty to the test, and you will never learn anything.

**14** You cannot find the depths of a human heart or grasp the thoughts of his mind. How then can you search out God, who made all these things, or know His mind or understand His purpose? No, my brothers, do not provoke the Lord our God to anger.

**15** For if He does not choose to help us within these five days, He has the power to protect us on any day He wishes, or to destroy us before our enemies.

**16** Do not try to bind the purposes of the Lord our God, for God is not like a man, to be threatened, nor like a son of man, to be bargained with.

**17** So while we wait for His deliverance, let us call on Him to help us, and He will hear our voice, if it pleases Him.

**18** For there has not arisen in our generations, nor is there today, any tribe or family or people or city of ours that worships gods made by hands, as there was in former days.

**19** It was for that that our fathers were handed over to the sword and to plunder, and fell with a great fall before our enemies.

**20** But we know no other god but Him. So we hope that He will not despise us or any of our race.

**21** For if we are taken, all Judea will be taken, and our sanctuary will be plundered, and He will require of our blood its profaning.

**22** The slaughter of our brothers, the captivity of the land and the desolation of our inheritance He will bring on our heads among the nations wherever we are slaves, and we will be an offense and a reproach in the eyes of those who buy us.

**23** For our slavery will not bring us into favor; the Lord our God will turn it to dishonor.

**24** So now, brothers, let us show our brothers that their lives depend on us, and that the sanctuary, the house and the altar rest on us.

**25** Besides all this, let us give thanks to the Lord our God, who is testing us as He did our fathers.

**26** Remember what He did with Abraham, how He tested Isaac, and what happened to Jacob in Mesopotamia of Syria while he was keeping the sheep of Laban, his mother's brother.

**27** For He has not tried us with fire as He did them, to search their hearts, nor has He taken vengeance on us. The Lord scourges those who draw near to Him, to admonish them."

## "I Will Do a Thing"

**28** Uzziah said to her, "All that you have said you have spoken with a good heart, and no one can deny your words.

**29** Today is not the first time your wisdom has been shown; from the beginning of your days all the people have known your understanding, for the inclination of your heart is good.

**30** But the people were very thirsty, and they forced us to do as we told them and to bind ourselves with an oath that we will not break.

**31** So now pray for us, for you are a devout woman, and the Lord will send rain to fill our cisterns, and we will no longer faint."

**32** Judith said to them, "Listen to me. I am going to do a thing that will go down through all generations to the sons of our race.

**33** Stand at the gate tonight, and I will go out with my maid. Within the days after which you said you would surrender the city to our enemies, the Lord will deliver Israel by my hand.

**34** But do not inquire into what I am doing, for I will not tell you until what I am doing is finished."

**35** Uzziah and the rulers said to her, "Go in peace, and may the Lord God go before you to take vengeance on our enemies."

**36** So they left the tent and went back to their posts.

## Notes

- **v1 "Judith… son of Salamiel, son of Sarasadai, son of Israel"** — *Ioudeith*, **Jewess**, the woman of Judah. Her line runs back through *Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai*, the leader of the tribe of Simeon in the wilderness (Numbers 1:6), to Israel himself. Her prayer will name Simeon as her father (9:2).
- **vv2–3 "the heat came upon his head"** — as the Shunammite's son went out to his father among the reapers and cried, *My head, my head!* (2 Kings 4:19).
- **vv12–13 "you have put God to the test"** — the law: *You shall not put the LORD your God to the test, as you tested Him at Massah* (Deuteronomy 6:16), where the people tested Him over water.
- **v16 "God is not like a man, to be threatened, nor like a son of man, to be bargained with"** — Balaam's oracle: *God is not a man, that He should lie, nor a son of man, that He should change His mind* (Numbers 23:19).
- **v27 "The Lord scourges those who draw near to Him, to admonish them"** — the proverb, *whom the LORD loves He reproves* (Proverbs 3:12), and Tobit's song, *He scourges and He shows mercy* (Tobit 13:2).
- **v33 "the Lord will deliver Israel by my hand"** — the word Deborah spoke to Barak: *the LORD will sell Sisera into the hand of a woman* (Judges 4:9).

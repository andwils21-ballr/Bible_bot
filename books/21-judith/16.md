# Judith 16

## The Song of Judith

**1** Judith said:  
"Begin a song to my God with tambourines;  
sing to the Lord with cymbals.  
Raise to Him a psalm and praise;  
exalt and call on His name.

**2** For the Lord is a God who crushes wars;  
for He has brought me into His camp, among the people,  
and delivered me from the hand of my pursuers.

**3** Assyria came down from the mountains of the north;  
he came with tens of thousands of his warriors.  
Their multitude blocked the streams,  
and their horsemen covered the hills.

**4** He said he would burn up my territory,  
kill my young men with the sword,  
dash my infants to the ground,  
seize my children as plunder,  
and carry off my virgins as spoil.

**5** But the Lord Almighty thwarted them  
by the hand of a woman.

**6** For their mighty one did not fall by the hands of young men,  
nor did the sons of the Titans strike him down,  
nor did tall giants set upon him;  
but Judith the daughter of Merari  
undid him with the beauty of her face.

**7** For she put off her widow's clothing  
to exalt the oppressed in Israel.  
She anointed her face with perfume,

**8** fastened her hair with a headband,  
and put on a linen gown to deceive him.

**9** Her sandal ravished his eyes,  
her beauty took his soul captive,  
and the sword passed through his neck.

**10** The Persians trembled at her daring,  
and the Medes were dismayed at her boldness.

**11** Then my oppressed people shouted,  
and my weak ones cried out, and the enemy was terrified;  
they raised their voices, and the enemy was routed.

**12** The sons of slave girls pierced them through;  
they wounded them like the children of deserters.  
They perished before the army of my Lord.

**13** I will sing to my God a new song:  
O Lord, You are great and glorious,  
wonderful in strength, invincible.

**14** Let all Your creation serve You,  
for You spoke, and they came into being;  
You sent Your Spirit, and it formed them;  
there is no one who can resist Your voice.

**15** For the mountains will be shaken to their foundations with the waters,  
and the rocks will melt like wax before You;  
but to those who fear You,  
You show mercy.

**16** For every sacrifice as a fragrant offering is a small thing,  
and all the fat for burnt offerings to You is a very little thing;  
but whoever fears the Lord is great forever.

**17** Woe to the nations that rise up against my race!  
The Lord Almighty will take vengeance on them in the day of judgment;  
He will put fire and worms into their flesh,  
and they will weep in pain forever."

## Judith in Jerusalem

**18** When they came to Jerusalem, they worshiped God, and when the people had been purified, they offered their burnt offerings, their freewill offerings and their gifts.

**19** Judith dedicated to God all the possessions of Holofernes that the people had given her, and the canopy that she had taken for herself from his bedroom she gave as an offering to God.

**20** The people went on rejoicing in Jerusalem before the sanctuary for three months, and Judith stayed with them.

## The Last Days of Judith

**21** After those days each one returned to his own inheritance, and Judith went back to Bethulia and lived on her estate. In her time she was honored throughout the whole land.

**22** Many desired to marry her, but no man knew her all the days of her life after Manasseh her husband died and was gathered to his people.

**23** She became more and more renowned, and she grew old in her husband's house, reaching the age of a hundred and five. She set her maid free. She died in Bethulia, and they buried her in the cave of her husband Manasseh.

**24** The house of Israel mourned for her seven days. Before she died she had distributed her property to all those most closely related to her husband Manasseh and to her own nearest kindred.

**25** No one ever again spread terror among the sons of Israel in the days of Judith, or for a long time after her death.

## Notes

- **v2 "the Lord is a God who crushes wars"** — the song of the sea in the Greek (Exodus 15:3), which she prayed before she went (9:7). She sings it now as Miriam did.
- **v6 "nor did the sons of the Titans strike him down, nor did tall giants set upon him"** — *Titanes*, the word the Greek of Samuel uses for the Rephaim, *the valley of the Titans* (2 Samuel 5:18). No hero of old brought Holofernes down; a widow did.
- **vv7–9 "she put off her widow's clothing… Her sandal ravished his eyes"** — the dressing of 10:3–4, sung back. The song keeps what the story kept: she deceived him, and *the sword passed through his neck*.
- **v14 "You spoke, and they came into being; You sent Your Spirit, and it formed them"** — the psalms of creation: *He spoke, and they came to be* (Psalm 33:9), *You send forth Your Spirit, they are created* (Psalm 104:30).
- **v16 "every sacrifice… is a small thing… but whoever fears the Lord is great forever"** — the prophet's word to Saul, *to obey is better than sacrifice* (1 Samuel 15:22), sung by a woman who has just offered nothing but herself.
- **v17 "He will put fire and worms into their flesh"** — Isaiah's last verse, *their worm shall not die, their fire shall not be quenched* (Isaiah 66:24).
- **v23 "She set her maid free"** — the maid who went with her into the camp and carried the head home (13:9–10) is the last person named in her story.
- **v25 "No one ever again spread terror among the sons of Israel"** — the close of the judges' stories, *the land had rest* (Judges 3:11), told of a widow of Bethulia.

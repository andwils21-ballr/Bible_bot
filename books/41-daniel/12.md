# Daniel 12

## Those Who Sleep in the Dust

**1** "At that time Michael, the great prince who stands over the sons of your people, will arise. There will be a time of distress such as has not been since there was a nation until that time. But at that time your people will be delivered, everyone who is found written in the book.

**2** Many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth will awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting abhorrence.

**3** Those who are wise will shine like the brightness of the expanse, and those who make the many righteous, like the stars, forever and ever.

**4** But you, Daniel, shut up the words and seal the book until the time of the end. Many will run to and fro, and knowledge will increase."

## How Long?

**5** Then I, Daniel, looked, and two others were standing there, one on this bank of the river and one on that bank.

**6** One said to the man clothed in linen, who was above the waters of the river, "How long until the end of these wonders?"

**7** I heard the man clothed in linen, who was above the waters of the river: he raised his right hand and his left hand toward heaven and swore by Him who lives forever that it would be for a time, times and half a time, and that when the shattering of the power of the holy people comes to an end, all these things will be finished.

**8** I heard, but I did not understand. So I said, "My lord, what will be the outcome of these things?"

**9** He said, "Go, Daniel, for the words are shut up and sealed until the time of the end.

**10** Many will be purified, made white and refined; but the wicked will act wickedly, and none of the wicked will understand, but those who are wise will understand.

**11** From the time the continual offering is taken away and the loathsome thing that desolates is set up, there will be one thousand two hundred and ninety days.

**12** Blessed is the one who waits and reaches one thousand three hundred and thirty-five days.

**13** But you, go your way until the end. You will rest, and you will stand up for your lot at the end of the days."

## Notes

- **v1 "a time of distress… written in the book"** — Jesus uses this verse: *there will be great affliction, such as has not been from the beginning of the world until now* (Matthew 24:21). The book is the one Moses asked to be blotted out of, *Your book that You have written*, and God answered, *Whoever has sinned against Me, him I will blot out of My book* (Exodus 32:32–33).
- **v2 "Many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth will awake"** — *le-chayyei olam*, **to everlasting life**, a phrase found only here in the Hebrew Bible. Isaiah had said it in a song, *Awake and sing for joy, you who dwell in the dust!* (Isaiah 26:19). *Dir'on*, **abhorrence**, is found only here and in the last verse of Isaiah, *they will be an abhorrence to all flesh* (Isaiah 66:24).
- **v3 "Those who are wise… those who make the many righteous"** — *ha-maskilim* and *matsdiqei ha-rabbim*, the words of Isaiah's servant: *My servant will act wisely* (*yaskil*, Isaiah 52:13), and *the righteous one, My servant, will make the many righteous* (*yatsdiq… la-rabbim*, Isaiah 53:11). The wise were the ones who *stumble by sword and flame* (11:33). They shine like *the expanse*, *raqia*, the heaven of the second day of creation (Genesis 1:6–8).
- **v4 "Many will run to and fro, and knowledge will increase"** — *yeshotetu rabbim*, the verb Amos uses of people running to and fro to seek the word of the LORD and not finding it (Amos 8:12). The Greek reads *until many are taught and knowledge increases*.
- **vv5–7 "the river… swore by Him who lives forever"** — the river of 10:4 was *nahar*; here it is *ye'or*, the word used for the Nile, where Pharaoh stood in his dream (Genesis 41:1). The man raises both hands, as God says in the Song of Moses, *I lift up My hand to heaven and say: As I live forever* (Deuteronomy 32:40). Revelation's angel raises his hand and swears the same way (Revelation 10:5–6).
- **v7 "a time, times and half a time"** — *mo'ed mo'adim va-chetsi*, the Hebrew of the Aramaic *time, times and half a time* (7:25).
- **vv11–12 "one thousand two hundred and ninety… one thousand three hundred and thirty-five days"** — two numbers, thirty and forty-five days apart, given without explanation after the 2,300 evenings and mornings of 8:14. *Blessed is the one who waits* is *ashrei*, the first word of the Psalms, *Blessed is the man* (Psalm 1:1).
- **v13 "You will rest, and you will stand up for your lot at the end of the days"** — *tanuach*, **rest**, and *ta'amod*, **stand**, the verb of Michael who *will arise* (v1). *Goral*, **lot**, is the word for a share of the land given by lot, as in Joshua, *The lot of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin came up* (Joshua 18:11). *Le-qets ha-yamin*, **at the end of the days**, spells *days* with the Aramaic ending *-in*.

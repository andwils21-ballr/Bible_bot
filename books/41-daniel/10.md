# Daniel 10

## By the Tigris

**1** In the third year of Cyrus king of Persia a word was revealed to Daniel, who was called Belteshazzar. The word was true, and it concerned a great conflict. He understood the word, and he had understanding in the vision.

**2** In those days I, Daniel, had been mourning for three full weeks.

**3** I ate no choice food; no meat or wine entered my mouth, and I did not anoint myself at all until the three full weeks were over.

**4** On the twenty-fourth day of the first month, as I was on the bank of the great river, the Tigris,

**5** I lifted my eyes and saw a man clothed in linen, with a belt of fine gold from Uphaz around his waist.

**6** His body was like beryl, his face like the appearance of lightning, his eyes like flaming torches, his arms and his legs like the gleam of burnished bronze, and the sound of his words like the sound of a multitude.

**7** I, Daniel, alone saw the vision; the men who were with me did not see the vision, but a great trembling fell on them, and they fled to hide.

**8** So I was left alone and saw this great vision. No strength was left in me; my vigor turned to ruin, and I retained no strength.

**9** Then I heard the sound of his words, and when I heard the sound of his words I fell into a deep sleep on my face, with my face to the ground.

**10** Then a hand touched me and set me trembling on my knees and the palms of my hands.

## The Princes of Persia and Greece

**11** He said to me, "Daniel, man greatly loved, understand the words I am speaking to you, and stand up where you are, for I have now been sent to you." When he had spoken this word to me, I stood up trembling.

**12** He said to me, "Do not be afraid, Daniel, for from the first day that you set your heart to understand and to humble yourself before your God, your words were heard, and I have come because of your words.

**13** The prince of the kingdom of Persia stood against me twenty-one days. Then Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me, and I was left there beside the kings of Persia.

**14** I have come to make you understand what will happen to your people in the end of days, for the vision is for days yet to come."

**15** While he was speaking these words with me, I turned my face toward the ground and was speechless.

**16** Then one in the likeness of the sons of men touched my lips. I opened my mouth and spoke, and said to the one standing before me, "My lord, because of the vision my pains have come upon me, and I retain no strength.

**17** How can I, my lord's servant, speak with you, my lord? As for me, from now on no strength remains in me, and no breath is left in me."

**18** Again the one who looked like a man touched me and strengthened me.

**19** He said, "Do not be afraid, man greatly loved. Peace be with you; be strong, be strong!" When he spoke with me, I was strengthened, and I said, "Let my lord speak, for you have strengthened me."

**20** Then he said, "Do you know why I have come to you? Now I must go back to fight with the prince of Persia, and when I go out, the prince of Greece will come.

**21** But I will tell you what is inscribed in the book of truth. There is no one who stands firmly with me against these except Michael, your prince.

## Notes

- **v13 "The prince of the kingdom of Persia… Michael, one of the chief princes"** — *sar*, the word of 8:11, 25: the nations have princes in heaven, and the speaker fights theirs (v20). *Mikha'el* is **Who is like God?**, the question of the song at the sea, *Who is like You among the gods, LORD?* (Exodus 15:11). *I was left there* is the Hebrew; the Greek has *I left him there*, Michael. In the Greek of Deuteronomy, God *set the boundaries of the nations according to the number of the angels of God*, where the Hebrew has *the sons of Israel* (Deuteronomy 32:8).

# Daniel 9

## The Seventy Years

**1** In the first year of Darius son of Ahasuerus, of the seed of the Medes, who was made king over the kingdom of the Chaldeans,

**2** in the first year of his reign, I, Daniel, understood from the books the number of years that, by the word of the LORD to Jeremiah the prophet, must pass to complete the desolation of Jerusalem: seventy years.

**3** Then I turned my face to the Lord God, to seek Him in prayer and pleading, with fasting and sackcloth and ashes.

## The Prayer

**4** I prayed to the LORD my God and made confession, and said, "Please, Lord, the great and awesome God, who keeps the covenant and loyal love with those who love Him and keep His commandments,

**5** we have sinned and have done wrong; we have acted wickedly and rebelled, turning aside from Your commandments and Your rules.

**6** We have not listened to Your servants the prophets, who spoke in Your name to our kings, our princes and our fathers, and to all the people of the land.

**7** To You, Lord, belongs righteousness, and to us open shame, as it is this day — to the men of Judah, to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and to all Israel, near and far, in all the lands where You have driven them, because of the unfaithfulness they have shown You.

**8** LORD, to us belongs open shame, to our kings, our princes and our fathers, because we have sinned against You.

**9** To the Lord our God belong compassion and forgiveness, for we have rebelled against Him,

**10** and we have not listened to the voice of the LORD our God, to walk in His laws, which He set before us through His servants the prophets.

**11** All Israel has transgressed Your law and turned aside, refusing to listen to Your voice; so the curse and the oath written in the law of Moses the servant of God have been poured out on us, because we have sinned against Him.

**12** He has carried out His word, which He spoke against us and against the rulers who governed us, by bringing on us a great disaster; for under the whole heaven nothing has been done like what has been done to Jerusalem.

**13** As it is written in the law of Moses, all this disaster has come on us; yet we have not sought the favor of the LORD our God by turning from our iniquities and paying heed to Your truth.

**14** So the LORD kept watch over the disaster and brought it on us, for the LORD our God is righteous in all the works He has done, and we have not listened to His voice.

**15** Now, Lord our God, who brought Your people out of the land of Egypt with a mighty hand and made a name for Yourself, as it is this day — we have sinned, we have acted wickedly.

**16** Lord, by all Your righteous acts, let Your anger and Your wrath turn away from Your city Jerusalem, Your holy mountain; for because of our sins and the iniquities of our fathers, Jerusalem and Your people have become a reproach to all who are around us.

**17** And now, our God, listen to the prayer of Your servant and to his pleading, and for the Lord's sake make Your face shine on Your sanctuary, which lies desolate.

**18** Incline Your ear, my God, and hear; open Your eyes and see our desolations, and the city that is called by Your name. We do not lay our pleas before You because of our righteous acts, but because of Your great compassion.

**19** Lord, hear! Lord, forgive! Lord, listen and act! Do not delay, for Your own sake, my God, because Your city and Your people are called by Your name."

## Gabriel and the Seventy Sevens

**20** While I was still speaking and praying, confessing my sin and the sin of my people Israel, and laying my plea before the LORD my God for the holy mountain of my God,

**21** while I was still speaking in prayer, the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision at the beginning, came to me in swift flight at about the time of the evening offering.

**22** He gave me understanding, and spoke with me and said, "Daniel, I have now come out to give you insight and understanding.

**23** At the beginning of your pleading a word went out, and I have come to tell it, for you are greatly loved. So consider the word and understand the vision:

**24** Seventy sevens are decreed for your people and for your holy city, to finish the transgression, to put an end to sin, to atone for iniquity, to bring in everlasting righteousness, to seal up vision and prophet, and to anoint what is most holy.

**25** Know and understand: from the going out of the word to restore and rebuild Jerusalem until an anointed one, a prince, there will be seven sevens; and for sixty-two sevens it will be rebuilt, with square and moat, but in troubled times.

**26** After the sixty-two sevens an anointed one will be cut off and will have nothing; and the people of the prince who is to come will destroy the city and the sanctuary. Its end will come with a flood, and to the end there will be war; desolations are decreed.

**27** He will make a strong covenant with the many for one seven, and for half of the seven he will put an end to sacrifice and offering; and on the wing of loathsome things will come one who desolates, until the end that is decreed is poured out on the desolator."

## Notes

- **v2 "seventy years"** — Jeremiah's number: *these nations will serve the king of Babylon seventy years* (Jeremiah 25:11), and *When seventy years are completed for Babylon, I will attend to you* (Jeremiah 29:10). Chronicles counts them as the land's sabbaths, *to fulfill seventy years* (2 Chronicles 36:21). Daniel finds it *in the books*: he learns the time from what Jeremiah wrote.
- **vv4–19 the prayer** — the name YHWH, **the LORD**, is found in Daniel only in this chapter, eight times (vv2, 4, 8, 10, 13, 14, 20). The prayer opens with Nehemiah's words, *the great and awesome God, who keeps covenant and loyal love with those who love Him and keep His commandments* (Nehemiah 1:5), and confesses in Solomon's: *We have sinned and have done wrong; we have acted wickedly* (1 Kings 8:47). *Open shame, as it is this day* is Ezra's (Ezra 9:7).
- **vv11, 27 "poured out"** — *natakh*, the verb of *As silver is melted in a furnace* (Ezekiel 22:22): the curse of the law is *poured out* on Israel (v11), and at the end of the chapter the decreed end is *poured out* on the desolator (v27).
- **v17 "make Your face shine"** — the priests' blessing turned into a plea: *the LORD make His face shine on you* (Numbers 6:25), here on a sanctuary that lies desolate.
- **v21 "in swift flight… the evening offering"** — *mu'af bi-y'af*. Read from *uf*, **fly**, it is *caused to fly in flight*; read from *ya'af*, **weary**, it is *in weariness*, which could be Daniel's, as he was sick and worn out after the last vision (8:27). The Greek has *flying*. *The evening offering* is the hour Ezra prayed, *At the evening offering I rose* (Ezra 9:5); Daniel keeps the temple's hour with the temple in ruins (v17).
- **v24 "Seventy sevens… to anoint what is most holy"** — *shavu'im shiv'im*, **sevens seventy**. Daniel was reading Jeremiah's *seventy years* (v2); the answer comes in *sevens*. *Shavua* is the word for a week, and when Daniel means weeks of days he adds *days* (10:2–3). *To put an end to sin* follows the read form, *le-hatem*; the written form is *le-chtom*, **to seal up**, the verb of *to seal up vision and prophet* later in the verse. *Qodesh qodashim*, **most holy**, has no article; elsewhere it describes the altar and the holiest things of the sanctuary, *the altar shall be most holy* (Exodus 29:37).
- **v25 "until an anointed one, a prince, there will be seven sevens; and for sixty-two sevens"** — *ad mashiach nagid*. *Mashiach* is **anointed**, used of kings and priests, *the LORD's anointed* (1 Samuel 24:6), and here it has no article. The accents of the Hebrew text divide the verse after *seven sevens*, so that the anointed one comes after seven sevens and the city is rebuilt for sixty-two. The Greek joins them: *until an anointed leader, seven sevens and sixty-two sevens*.
- **v26 "an anointed one will be cut off and will have nothing"** — *yikkaret mashiach ve-ein lo*, **will be cut off, and there is not to him**. The last words are bare: *nothing*, or *no one*, or *not for himself*. The Greek has *and there is no judgment in him*.
- **v27 "He will make a strong covenant… on the wing of loathsome things will come one who desolates"** — *he* is not named; the nearest is *the prince who is to come* (v26). *Ve-al kenaf shiqqutsim meshomem*, **and on a wing of loathsome things, one desolating**, is obscure. *Shiqquts* is the law's word for idols, *their loathsome things and their idols* (Deuteronomy 29:17). The Greek has *the abomination of the desolations*, the words Jesus quotes: *the abomination of desolation, spoken of through Daniel the prophet* (Matthew 24:15).

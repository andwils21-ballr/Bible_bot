# Daniel 1

## Taken to Babylon

**1** In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and besieged it.

**2** The Lord gave Jehoiakim king of Judah into his hand, with some of the vessels of the house of God. He carried them off to the land of Shinar, to the house of his god, and brought the vessels into the treasure house of his god.

**3** Then the king told Ashpenaz, the chief of his eunuchs, to bring in some of the sons of Israel, from the royal family and from the nobles:

**4** young men without any blemish, good-looking, skilled in every kind of wisdom, well informed and quick to understand, with the ability to stand in the king's palace; and he was to teach them the literature and the language of the Chaldeans.

**5** The king assigned them a daily portion of the king's food and of the wine he drank. They were to be trained for three years, and at the end of that time they were to stand before the king.

**6** Among them, from the sons of Judah, were Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael and Azariah.

**7** The chief of the eunuchs gave them names: he gave Daniel the name Belteshazzar, Hananiah Shadrach, Mishael Meshach, and Azariah Abednego.

## Ten Days

**8** Daniel resolved that he would not defile himself with the king's food or with the wine he drank, and he asked the chief of the eunuchs for permission not to defile himself.

**9** God gave Daniel kindness and compassion in the sight of the chief of the eunuchs.

**10** The chief of the eunuchs said to Daniel, "I am afraid of my lord the king, who has assigned your food and your drink. Why should he see your faces more downcast than the young men your own age? You would make my head forfeit to the king."

**11** Daniel said to the steward whom the chief of the eunuchs had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael and Azariah,

**12** "Please test your servants for ten days. Let us be given vegetables to eat and water to drink.

**13** Then look at our appearance and the appearance of the young men who eat the king's food, and deal with your servants by what you see."

**14** He listened to them in this matter and tested them for ten days.

**15** At the end of ten days their appearance was better, and they were fatter of flesh than all the young men who ate the king's food.

**16** So the steward would take away their food and the wine they were to drink, and give them vegetables.

## Ten Times Wiser

**17** To these four young men God gave knowledge and skill in all literature and wisdom, and Daniel understood visions and dreams of every kind.

**18** At the end of the time the king had set for them to be brought in, the chief of the eunuchs brought them in before Nebuchadnezzar.

**19** The king spoke with them, and among them all no one was found like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael and Azariah. So they stood before the king.

**20** In every matter of wisdom and understanding that the king asked them about, he found them ten times better than all the magicians and enchanters in his whole kingdom.

**21** Daniel remained until the first year of King Cyrus.

## Notes

- **v1 "In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim"** — Kings tells it in one line: *In his days Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up, and Jehoiakim became his servant for three years* (2 Kings 24:1). Chronicles adds the vessels: *Nebuchadnezzar also brought some of the vessels of the house of the LORD to Babylon and put them in his temple in Babylon* (2 Chronicles 36:7). Jeremiah counts *the fourth year of Jehoiakim… which was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar* (Jeremiah 25:1; 46:2), a year later than Daniel's date. Neither book refers to the other's count.
- **v2 "the land of Shinar"** — the old name of Babylonia, the plain of the tower: *they found a valley in the land of Shinar, and they settled there* (Genesis 11:2). The vessels of the house of God go into the house of Nebuchadnezzar's god. They come out again in 5:2–4.
- **vv2, 9, 17 "gave"** — *natan*, with God as the one who gives each time: *the Lord gave* Jehoiakim into Nebuchadnezzar's hand, *God gave* Daniel kindness and compassion, *God gave* the four knowledge. The defeat, the favor and the wisdom all come from the same hand. In v9 the words are those of Solomon's prayer for Israel in exile, *grant them compassion before their captors* (1 Kings 8:50), *natan… le-rachamim lifnei*. *The Lord* in v2 is *Adonai*; the name YHWH appears in Daniel only in chapter 9.
- **vv3–4 "the chief of his eunuchs… from the royal family"** — *sarisim*, the word of Isaiah's warning to Hezekiah: *Some of your sons, who will come from you… shall be eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon* (Isaiah 39:7; 2 Kings 20:18). Here young men *of the royal family* are chosen to stand *in the king's palace*, *heikhal*, the same word. *Saris* can also mean a court officer: Potiphar, who had a wife, is *an officer of Pharaoh* (Genesis 39:1). The chapter does not say whether the four were made eunuchs. The Greek has *archieunouchos*, **chief eunuch**.
- **vv6–8 the names** — each Hebrew name carries God's. *Daniel* is *dan*, **judge**, with *El*, **God** (Rachel's *God has judged me*, Genesis 30:6); *Mishael* also ends in *El*; *Hananiah* is **the LORD has been gracious** and *Azariah* **the LORD has helped**, both ending in *Yah*. None of the new names carries the God of Israel, and Belteshazzar is named *after the name of my god*, the king says (4:8). The verb is *va-yasem*, **set**: twice in v7 the chief *set* names on them, and in v8 Daniel *set* it on his heart not to defile himself.
- **v8 "that he would not defile himself"** — the chapter gives Daniel's resolve, not his reason. The law forbids wine only to a Nazirite (Numbers 6:3) and to priests entering the tent (Leviticus 10:9), and he refuses the king's wine as well as his food. Ezekiel had been told, *This is how the sons of Israel will eat their bread unclean among the nations where I will drive them* (Ezekiel 4:13).
- **vv10, 15 "downcast… fatter of flesh"** — words from Joseph's story. *Zo'afim*, **downcast**, is what Joseph saw in Pharaoh's two officers, *sarisim*, in prison (Genesis 40:6–7), and the Greek here has *skythrōpa*, the word of Joseph's question, *Why are your faces bad today?* (Genesis 40:7). *Beri'ei basar*, **fat of flesh**, is said of Pharaoh's seven good cows, *beautiful of appearance and fat of flesh* (Genesis 41:2).
- **v20 "the magicians and enchanters"** — *chartummim*, the magicians of Egypt, who could not interpret Pharaoh's dream (Genesis 41:8, 24; Exodus 7:11). *Ashshafim*, **enchanters**, is a word found only in Daniel. *Ten times* is *eser yadot*, **ten hands**, as Benjamin's portion was *five times more*, *chamesh yadot* (Genesis 43:34).
- **v21 "until the first year of King Cyrus"** — the year of the decree that sent the exiles home (Ezra 1:1). Daniel's service spans the whole exile. The verse does not mark his death: a vision in 10:1 is dated to the third year of Cyrus.

# Proverbs 5

**1** My son, pay attention to my wisdom;  
incline your ear to my understanding,

**2** so that you may keep discretion  
and your lips may guard knowledge.

**3** For the lips of the strange woman drip honey,  
and her mouth is smoother than oil;

**4** but in the end she is bitter as wormwood,  
sharp as a two-edged sword.

**5** Her feet go down to death;  
her steps take hold of Sheol.

**6** She does not consider the path of life;  
her ways wander, and she does not know it.

**7** Now then, sons, listen to me,  
and do not turn away from the words of my mouth.

**8** Keep your way far from her,  
and do not go near the door of her house,

**9** or you will give your vigor to others  
and your years to the merciless,

**10** or strangers will take their fill of your strength,  
and your labors will go to the house of a foreigner,

**11** and at the end of your life you will groan,  
when your flesh and body are consumed,

**12** and you will say, "How I hated discipline,  
and my heart despised reproof!

**13** I did not listen to the voice of my teachers  
or incline my ear to my instructors.

**14** I was almost in utter ruin  
in the assembly and congregation."

**15** Drink water from your own cistern,  
flowing water from your own well.

**16** Should your springs be scattered abroad,  
streams of water in the streets?

**17** Let them be yours alone,  
and not for strangers with you.

**18** Let your fountain be blessed,  
and rejoice in the wife of your youth,

**19** a loving doe, a graceful mountain goat.  
Let her breasts satisfy you at all times;  
be captivated always by her love.

**20** Why, my son, should you be captivated by a strange woman  
and embrace the bosom of a foreigner?

**21** For a man's ways are before the eyes of the LORD,  
and He weighs all his paths.

**22** The iniquities of the wicked man ensnare him,  
and he is held fast in the cords of his sin.

**23** He dies for lack of discipline,  
and in the greatness of his folly he goes astray.

## Notes

- **v3 "the strange woman"** — *zarah*, as in 2:16, another man's wife.
- **v6 "She does not consider the path of life"** — *pen*, often "lest," here "so that she does not": she never stops to weigh her way.
- **vv9–10 "or you will give… or strangers will take"** — *pen*, "or else": adultery costs strength, years and wealth.
- **v14 "in the assembly and congregation"** — exposed and judged before the whole community (Deuteronomy 22:22).
- **vv15–18 "cistern… well… springs… fountain"** — the wife as the man's own well of water (Song of Songs 4:12, 15); v16 can be read as a question or as a statement of a blessing that spreads to children.
- **vv19, 20, 23 "be captivated… be captivated… goes astray"** — the same verb, *shagah*, three times: be lost in your wife's love, not in a stranger's, or you will be lost in your folly.
- **v21 "He weighs all his paths"** — or "levels"; God sees and measures every way.

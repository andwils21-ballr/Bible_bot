# Proverbs 21

**1** The king's heart is a stream of water in the hand of the LORD;  
He turns it wherever He wishes.

**2** Every way of a man is right in his own eyes,  
but the LORD weighs the hearts.

**3** To do righteousness and justice  
is more acceptable to the LORD than sacrifice.

**4** Haughty eyes and a proud heart —  
the lamp of the wicked — are sin.

**5** The plans of the diligent lead surely to profit,  
but everyone who is hasty comes surely to want.

**6** Getting treasures by a lying tongue  
is a fleeting vapor for those who seek death.

**7** The violence of the wicked will sweep them away,  
because they refuse to do justice.

**8** The way of the guilty man is crooked,  
but the conduct of the pure is right.

**9** It is better to live in a corner of the housetop  
than in a house shared with a quarrelsome wife.

**10** The soul of the wicked desires evil;  
his neighbor finds no favor in his eyes.

**11** When a scoffer is punished, the simple becomes wise;  
when the wise is instructed, he gains knowledge.

**12** The Righteous One considers the house of the wicked;  
He throws the wicked down to ruin.

**13** Whoever shuts his ears to the cry of the poor  
will himself call out and not be answered.

**14** A gift in secret averts anger,  
and a bribe in the bosom, strong wrath.

**15** When justice is done, it is a joy to the righteous  
but terror to evildoers.

**16** A man who wanders from the way of good sense  
will rest in the assembly of the shades.

**17** Whoever loves pleasure will be a poor man;  
whoever loves wine and oil will not be rich.

**18** The wicked is a ransom for the righteous,  
and the treacherous for the upright.

**19** It is better to live in a desert land  
than with a quarrelsome and fretful wife.

**20** Precious treasure and oil are in the dwelling of the wise,  
but a foolish man devours it.

**21** Whoever pursues righteousness and loyal love  
will find life, righteousness and honor.

**22** A wise man scales the city of the mighty  
and brings down the stronghold in which they trust.

**23** Whoever guards his mouth and his tongue  
guards his soul from trouble.

**24** "Scoffer" is the name of the arrogant, haughty man  
who acts with arrogant fury.

**25** The desire of the sluggard kills him,  
for his hands refuse to work.

**26** All day long he craves and craves,  
but the righteous gives and does not hold back.

**27** The sacrifice of the wicked is detestable;  
how much more when he brings it with evil intent!

**28** A false witness will perish,  
but the man who listens will speak forever.

**29** A wicked man puts on a bold face,  
but the upright gives thought to his way.

**30** There is no wisdom, no understanding,  
no counsel against the LORD.

**31** The horse is made ready for the day of battle,  
but victory belongs to the LORD.

## Notes

- **v1 "a stream of water in the hand of the LORD"** — like irrigation channels a farmer turns where he wants; even the most powerful heart is in God's hand.
- **v2 "the LORD weighs the hearts"** — as in 16:2 ("the spirits") and 24:12.
- **v3 "more acceptable to the LORD than sacrifice"** — 1 Samuel 15:22; Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8.
- **v4 "the lamp of the wicked"** — *nir*, "lamp," or, with other vowels, "the tilled field," the work of the wicked.
- **v6 "for those who seek death"** — the Hebrew is followed; the Greek and some Hebrew manuscripts read "snares of death."
- **vv9, 19 "a quarrelsome wife"** — v9 is repeated at 25:24; *midyanim*, the read form in v19.
- **v12 "The Righteous One"** — *tsaddiq*, here likely God, who sees the house of the wicked and brings it down; some take it as a righteous man.
- **v16 "the assembly of the shades"** — *refa'im*, as in 2:18 and 9:18.
- **v27** — the first line is as in 15:8, without "to the LORD."
- **v29 "the upright gives thought to his way"** — the read form *yavin darko*; the written form *yakhin derakhav*, "makes his ways firm."

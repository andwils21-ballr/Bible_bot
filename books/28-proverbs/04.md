# Proverbs 4

**1** Hear, sons, a father's discipline,  
and pay attention, so that you may know understanding,

**2** for I give you good learning;  
do not forsake my teaching.

**3** When I was a son to my father,  
tender, the only one in the sight of my mother,

**4** he taught me and said to me,  
"Let your heart hold fast my words;  
keep my commandments and live.

**5** Get wisdom; get understanding;  
do not forget, and do not turn away from the words of my mouth.

**6** Do not forsake her, and she will keep you;  
love her, and she will guard you.

**7** The beginning of wisdom is this: get wisdom,  
and with all you have gotten, get understanding.

**8** Prize her highly, and she will exalt you;  
she will honor you if you embrace her.

**9** She will place on your head a graceful garland;  
she will give you a crown of beauty."

**10** Hear, my son, and accept my words,  
and the years of your life will be many.

**11** I have taught you the way of wisdom;  
I have led you in paths of uprightness.

**12** When you walk, your step will not be hampered,  
and if you run, you will not stumble.

**13** Keep hold of discipline; do not let go;  
guard her, for she is your life.

**14** Do not enter the path of the wicked,  
and do not walk in the way of evil men.

**15** Avoid it; do not go on it;  
turn away from it and pass on.

**16** For they cannot sleep unless they have done evil;  
they are robbed of sleep unless they have made someone stumble.

**17** For they eat the bread of wickedness  
and drink the wine of violence.

**18** But the path of the righteous is like the light of dawn,  
which shines brighter and brighter until full day.

**19** The way of the wicked is like deep darkness;  
they do not know what makes them stumble.

**20** My son, pay attention to my words;  
incline your ear to my sayings.

**21** Do not let them slip from your sight;  
keep them within your heart.

**22** For they are life to those who find them,  
and healing to all their flesh.

**23** Above all that you guard, keep your heart,  
for from it flow the springs of life.

**24** Put away from you crooked speech,  
and put devious lips far from you.

**25** Let your eyes look straight ahead,  
and your gaze be fixed straight before you.

**26** Make level the path of your feet,  
and all your ways will be sure.

**27** Do not turn to the right or to the left;  
turn your foot away from evil.

## Notes

- **vv3–9** — the father passes on what his own father taught him: three generations of the same teaching.
- **v3 "the only one in the sight of my mother"** — *yachid*, an only or beloved son; Solomon was the son David and Bathsheba kept (2 Samuel 12:24).
- **v7 "The beginning of wisdom is this: get wisdom"** — the first step is the decision to seek it, whatever it costs; the Greek leaves out the verse.
- **v16 "unless they have made someone stumble"** — the read form; the written form is a spelling variant.
- **v18 "like the light of dawn, which shines brighter and brighter"** — the righteous grow in light; the wicked walk in darkness and do not even see what trips them (v19).
- **v20 "my sayings"** — *amarai*, the plural of *imrah*; here the father's spoken words.
- **v23 "Above all that you guard, keep your heart"** — the heart, the seat of thought and will, from which life flows (Matthew 15:18–19).

# Proverbs 12

**1** Whoever loves discipline loves knowledge,  
but whoever hates reproof is stupid.

**2** A good man obtains favor from the LORD,  
but a man of evil devices He condemns.

**3** No one is established by wickedness,  
but the root of the righteous will never be moved.

**4** A capable wife is her husband's crown,  
but she who brings shame is like rot in his bones.

**5** The thoughts of the righteous are just;  
the counsels of the wicked are deceit.

**6** The words of the wicked lie in wait for blood,  
but the mouth of the upright delivers them.

**7** The wicked are overthrown and are no more,  
but the house of the righteous will stand.

**8** A man is praised according to his good sense,  
but one of twisted heart is despised.

**9** Better to be lowly and have a servant  
than to play the great man and lack bread.

**10** The righteous knows the life of his animal,  
but the compassion of the wicked is cruel.

**11** Whoever works his land will have plenty of bread,  
but whoever pursues worthless things lacks sense.

**12** The wicked covets the catch of evil men,  
but the root of the righteous yields fruit.

**13** In the transgression of the lips is an evil snare,  
but the righteous comes out of trouble.

**14** From the fruit of his mouth a man is satisfied with good,  
and the work of a man's hands He repays to him.

**15** The way of a fool is right in his own eyes,  
but a wise man listens to advice.

**16** A fool's vexation is known at once,  
but the shrewd conceals an insult.

**17** Whoever breathes out truth declares what is right,  
but a false witness, deceit.

**18** There is one whose rash words are like sword thrusts,  
but the tongue of the wise brings healing.

**19** Truthful lips endure forever,  
but a lying tongue is but for a moment.

**20** Deceit is in the heart of those who devise evil,  
but those who plan peace have joy.

**21** No harm befalls the righteous,  
but the wicked are filled with trouble.

**22** Lying lips are detestable to the LORD,  
but those who act faithfully are His delight.

**23** A shrewd man conceals knowledge,  
but the heart of fools proclaims folly.

**24** The hand of the diligent will rule,  
while the slack hand will be put to forced labor.

**25** Anxiety in a man's heart weighs him down,  
but a good word makes him glad.

**26** The righteous guides his neighbor,  
but the way of the wicked leads them astray.

**27** The slack man does not roast his game,  
but the diligent man gets precious wealth.

**28** In the path of righteousness is life,  
and in its pathway there is no death.

## Notes

- **v1 "is stupid"** — *ba'ar*, brutish, like a beast without reason (30:2; Psalm 73:22); a third word beside *evil* and *kesil*.
- **v4 "A capable wife"** — *eshet chayil*, the phrase of 31:10 and Ruth 3:11; *chayil* is strength, worth, ability.
- **v10 "The righteous knows the life of his animal"** — he cares for its needs (Deuteronomy 25:4).
- **v14 "He repays to him"** — the read form *yashiv*, God repays; the written form *yashuv*, "comes back to him."
- **v17 "Whoever breathes out truth"** — *yafiach*, the verb of 6:19, *a false witness who breathes out lies*.
- **v26 "The righteous guides his neighbor"** — the Hebrew is uncertain; "the righteous searches out his pasture" is another reading.
- **v27 "The slack man does not roast his game"** — too lazy even to cook what he caught; *charakh* occurs only here.
- **v28 "in its pathway there is no death"** — *al mavet*, "not death"; some read *el mavet*, "to death," and the Greek has "the ways of those who remember evil lead to death."

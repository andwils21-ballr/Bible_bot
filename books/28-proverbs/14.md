# Proverbs 14

**1** The wisest of women builds her house,  
but folly with her own hands tears it down.

**2** Whoever walks in uprightness fears the LORD,  
but whoever is devious in his ways despises Him.

**3** In the mouth of a fool is a rod of pride,  
but the lips of the wise will preserve them.

**4** Where there are no oxen, the manger is clean,  
but abundant crops come by the strength of the ox.

**5** A faithful witness does not lie,  
but a false witness breathes out lies.

**6** A scoffer seeks wisdom and finds none,  
but knowledge is easy for the one who understands.

**7** Leave the presence of a fool,  
for there you do not meet words of knowledge.

**8** The wisdom of the shrewd is to understand his way,  
but the folly of fools is deceit.

**9** Fools mock at guilt,  
but among the upright there is favor.

**10** The heart knows its own bitterness,  
and no stranger shares its joy.

**11** The house of the wicked will be destroyed,  
but the tent of the upright will flourish.

**12** There is a way that seems right to a man,  
but its end is the ways of death.

**13** Even in laughter the heart may ache,  
and the end of joy may be grief.

**14** The backslider in heart will be filled with the fruit of his ways,  
and a good man with the fruit of his deeds.

**15** The simple believes everything,  
but the shrewd considers his steps.

**16** A wise man fears and turns away from evil,  
but a fool is reckless and careless.

**17** A man quick to anger acts foolishly,  
and a man of evil devices is hated.

**18** The simple inherit folly,  
but the shrewd are crowned with knowledge.

**19** The evil bow down before the good,  
and the wicked at the gates of the righteous.

**20** The poor man is hated even by his neighbor,  
but the rich has many friends.

**21** Whoever despises his neighbor sins,  
but blessed is he who is gracious to the humble.

**22** Do not those who devise evil go astray?  
But loyal love and truth are for those who devise good.

**23** In all toil there is profit,  
but mere talk leads only to want.

**24** The crown of the wise is their wealth,  
but the folly of fools is folly.

**25** A truthful witness saves lives,  
but one who breathes out lies is deceitful.

**26** In the fear of the LORD there is strong confidence,  
and His children will have a refuge.

**27** The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life,  
to turn away from the snares of death.

**28** In a multitude of people is a king's glory,  
but without people a prince is ruined.

**29** Whoever is slow to anger has great understanding,  
but whoever is quick-tempered exalts folly.

**30** A tranquil heart is life to the body,  
but envy is rot to the bones.

**31** Whoever oppresses the poor insults his Maker,  
but whoever is gracious to the needy honors Him.

**32** The wicked is thrust down by his own evil,  
but the righteous has a refuge in his death.

**33** Wisdom rests in the heart of the one who understands,  
but among fools it makes itself known.

**34** Righteousness exalts a nation,  
but sin is a reproach to any people.

**35** The king's favor is toward a servant who acts wisely,  
but his wrath falls on one who acts shamefully.

## Notes

- **v1 "The wisest of women builds her house"** — as Wisdom built hers (9:1); *chokhmot nashim*, "the wise ones among women."
- **v3 "a rod of pride"** — *choter ga'avah*: his proud talk is a shoot that grows into a rod for his own back.
- **v5** — the second line is the same as 6:19a, "a false witness who breathes out lies."
- **v12 "There is a way that seems right to a man"** — the same words as 16:25, and the English is kept identical.
- **v14 "The backslider in heart"** — *sug lev*, one whose heart turns away.
- **v17 "a man of evil devices"** — *mezimmot*, the word that is "discretion" in 1:4; here in its bad sense, as in 12:2.
- **v21 "gracious to the humble"** — the read form *anavim*; the written form *aniyyim*, "afflicted," as in 3:34.
- **v27** — the same words as 13:14 with "the fear of the LORD" in place of "the teaching of the wise."
- **v32 "the righteous has a refuge in his death"** — the Greek reads "in his integrity," from a reordering of two letters.
- **v34 "sin is a reproach"** — *chesed*, here in its rare second meaning, "shame," as in Leviticus 20:17.

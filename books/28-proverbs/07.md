# Proverbs 7

**1** My son, keep my words  
and treasure up my commandments with you.

**2** Keep my commandments and live;  
keep my teaching as the apple of your eye.

**3** Bind them on your fingers;  
write them on the tablet of your heart.

**4** Say to wisdom, "You are my sister,"  
and call understanding your close relative,

**5** to keep you from the strange woman,  
from the foreign woman with her smooth words.

**6** For at the window of my house  
I looked out through my lattice,

**7** and I saw among the simple,  
I noticed among the youths,  
a young man lacking sense,

**8** passing along the street near her corner,  
taking the road to her house

**9** in the twilight, in the evening of the day,  
in the black of night and the darkness.

**10** Then a woman comes to meet him,  
dressed as a prostitute, with a guarded heart.

**11** She is loud and unruly;  
her feet do not stay at home;

**12** now in the street, now in the squares,  
and at every corner she lies in wait.

**13** She seizes him and kisses him,  
and with a brazen face she says to him,

**14** "I had peace offerings to make;  
today I have paid my vows.

**15** So I came out to meet you,  
to seek your face eagerly, and I have found you.

**16** I have spread my bed with coverings,  
colored linens from Egypt.

**17** I have sprinkled my bed  
with myrrh, aloes and cinnamon.

**18** Come, let us drink our fill of love until morning;  
let us delight ourselves with caresses.

**19** For the man is not at home;  
he has gone on a long journey.

**20** He took a bag of money with him;  
he will come home at the full moon."

**21** With her many persuasions she turns him aside;  
with her smooth lips she compels him.

**22** All at once he follows her,  
as an ox goes to the slaughter,  
or as a fool to the stocks for discipline,

**23** until an arrow pierces his liver —  
as a bird rushes into a snare;  
he does not know it will cost him his life.

**24** Now then, sons, listen to me,  
and pay attention to the words of my mouth.

**25** Do not let your heart turn aside to her ways;  
do not stray into her paths;

**26** for many are the victims she has brought down,  
and numerous are all whom she has slain.

**27** Her house is the way to Sheol,  
going down to the chambers of death.

## Notes

- **v2 "as the apple of your eye"** — *ishon*, "the little man" of the eye, the pupil, as in Deuteronomy 32:10 and Psalm 17:8.
- **v5** — the same words as 2:16, and the English there is followed.
- **v9 "in the black of night"** — *be'ishon laylah*, "in the pupil of the night," the same word as v2: the darkest point.
- **v14 "I had peace offerings to make; today I have paid my vows"** — the meat of a peace offering had to be eaten within a day or two (Leviticus 7:16); she has a feast at home and an empty house.
- **v19 "For the man is not at home"** — she does not say "my husband," only "the man."
- **v22 "or as a fool to the stocks for discipline"** — the Hebrew is obscure, *ukhe'ekhes el musar evil*; *ekhes* is an anklet or fetter (Isaiah 3:18). The Greek reads "as a dog to bonds."
- **vv26–27** — the end of the young man of v7, and the end of the warning that began in 2:16–19.

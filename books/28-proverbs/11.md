# Proverbs 11

**1** A false balance is detestable to the LORD,  
but a full weight is His delight.

**2** When pride comes, then comes disgrace,  
but with the humble is wisdom.

**3** The integrity of the upright guides them,  
but the crookedness of the treacherous destroys them.

**4** Wealth does not profit in the day of wrath,  
but righteousness delivers from death.

**5** The righteousness of the blameless keeps his way straight,  
but the wicked falls by his own wickedness.

**6** The righteousness of the upright delivers them,  
but the treacherous are caught by their own craving.

**7** When a wicked man dies, his hope perishes,  
and the expectation of wealth perishes.

**8** The righteous is rescued from trouble,  
and the wicked comes into it in his place.

**9** With his mouth the godless man destroys his neighbor,  
but by knowledge the righteous are delivered.

**10** When it goes well with the righteous, the city rejoices,  
and when the wicked perish there are shouts of joy.

**11** By the blessing of the upright a city is exalted,  
but by the mouth of the wicked it is torn down.

**12** Whoever despises his neighbor lacks sense,  
but a man of understanding keeps silent.

**13** Whoever goes about as a slanderer reveals secrets,  
but the one who is trustworthy in spirit keeps a thing hidden.

**14** Where there is no guidance a people falls,  
but in an abundance of counselors there is victory.

**15** Whoever puts up security for a stranger will surely suffer harm,  
but whoever hates striking hands in pledge is secure.

**16** A gracious woman gets honor,  
and ruthless men get riches.

**17** A man of loyal love does good to himself,  
but a cruel man hurts his own flesh.

**18** The wicked earns deceptive wages,  
but one who sows righteousness gets a true reward.

**19** As righteousness leads to life,  
so whoever pursues evil goes to his death.

**20** Those of crooked heart are detestable to the LORD,  
but those blameless in their way are His delight.

**21** Be assured, the evil man will not go unpunished,  
but the offspring of the righteous will be delivered.

**22** Like a gold ring in a pig's snout  
is a beautiful woman without discretion.

**23** The desire of the righteous ends only in good;  
the expectation of the wicked, in wrath.

**24** One gives freely, yet grows all the richer;  
another withholds what he should give, and only suffers want.

**25** Whoever brings blessing will be made rich,  
and one who waters will himself be watered.

**26** The people curse him who holds back grain,  
but a blessing is on the head of him who sells it.

**27** Whoever diligently seeks good seeks favor,  
but evil comes to him who searches for it.

**28** Whoever trusts in his riches will fall,  
but the righteous will flourish like a green leaf.

**29** Whoever troubles his own household will inherit the wind,  
and the fool will be servant to the wise of heart.

**30** The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life,  
and whoever wins souls is wise.

**31** If the righteous is repaid on earth,  
how much more the wicked and the sinner!

## Notes

- **v1 "A false balance… a full weight"** — honest scales and stones (Leviticus 19:35–36; Deuteronomy 25:13–16); *to'evah*, "detestable."
- **v3 "destroys them"** — the read form; the written form is a spelling variant.
- **v4** — the second line is the same as 10:2b.
- **v7 "the expectation of wealth"** — *onim*, "wealth" or "strength"; others read "the expectation of the wicked."
- **v15 "striking hands in pledge"** — the handshake of 6:1.
- **v16** — the Greek has two further lines here, not in the Hebrew.
- **v22 "Like a gold ring in a pig's snout"** — beauty without *ta'am*, "taste," good judgment, is wasted.
- **v30 "whoever wins souls is wise"** — *loqeach nefashot*, "takes lives," here in the good sense of drawing others to life; the Greek reads "the lives of the lawless are cut off."
- **v31** — 1 Peter 4:18 quotes the Greek: *If the righteous is scarcely saved, what will become of the ungodly and the sinner?*

# Proverbs 16

**1** The plans of the heart belong to man,  
but the answer of the tongue is from the LORD.

**2** All the ways of a man are pure in his own eyes,  
but the LORD weighs the spirits.

**3** Commit your works to the LORD,  
and your plans will be established.

**4** The LORD has made everything for its purpose,  
even the wicked for the day of evil.

**5** Everyone who is proud in heart is detestable to the LORD;  
be assured, he will not go unpunished.

**6** By loyal love and truth iniquity is atoned for,  
and by the fear of the LORD one turns away from evil.

**7** When a man's ways please the LORD,  
He makes even his enemies to be at peace with him.

**8** Better is a little with righteousness  
than great income with injustice.

**9** The heart of man plans his way,  
but the LORD establishes his steps.

**10** An oracle is on the lips of a king;  
his mouth does not sin in judgment.

**11** A just balance and scales are the LORD's;  
all the weights in the bag are His work.

**12** It is detestable to kings to do wickedness,  
for a throne is established by righteousness.

**13** Righteous lips are the delight of kings,  
and they love him who speaks what is right.

**14** A king's wrath is a messenger of death,  
and a wise man will appease it.

**15** In the light of a king's face is life,  
and his favor is like the clouds of the spring rain.

**16** How much better to get wisdom than gold!  
To get understanding is to be chosen rather than silver.

**17** The highway of the upright turns away from evil;  
whoever guards his way preserves his life.

**18** Pride goes before destruction,  
and a haughty spirit before a fall.

**19** Better to be of a lowly spirit with the humble  
than to divide the spoil with the proud.

**20** Whoever gives thought to the word will find good,  
and blessed is he who trusts in the LORD.

**21** The wise of heart is called understanding,  
and sweetness of speech increases learning.

**22** Good sense is a fountain of life to those who have it,  
but the discipline of fools is folly.

**23** The heart of the wise makes his mouth prudent  
and adds learning to his lips.

**24** Gracious words are a honeycomb,  
sweet to the soul and healing to the bones.

**25** There is a way that seems right to a man,  
but its end is the ways of death.

**26** A worker's appetite works for him,  
for his mouth urges him on.

**27** A worthless man digs up evil,  
and on his lips it is like a scorching fire.

**28** A perverse man spreads strife,  
and a whisperer separates close friends.

**29** A man of violence entices his neighbor  
and leads him in a way that is not good.

**30** Whoever winks his eyes plans perverse things;  
whoever purses his lips brings evil to pass.

**31** Gray hair is a crown of glory;  
it is gained in the way of righteousness.

**32** Better is one slow to anger than the mighty,  
and one who rules his spirit than one who takes a city.

**33** The lot is cast into the lap,  
but its every decision is from the LORD.

## Notes

- **vv1–9** — man plans, and the LORD decides (19:21; 20:24); the theme returns in the last verse (v33).
- **v3 "Commit your works to the LORD"** — *gol*, "roll" them onto Him (Psalm 22:8; 37:5).
- **v5 "be assured"** — *yad leyad*, "hand to hand," as in 11:21, and the English there is followed.
- **v6 "By loyal love and truth iniquity is atoned for"** — *chesed* and *emet*, the pair of 3:3.
- **v10 "An oracle is on the lips of a king"** — *qesem*, elsewhere divination (Deuteronomy 18:10); here the king's word as decisive as an oracle.
- **v14 "a messenger of death"** — *mal'akhei mavet*, plural, "messengers of death."
- **v19 "with the humble"** — the read form, as in 14:21.
- **v25** — the same words as 14:12.
- **v27 "on his lips"** — the read form is singular, "lip"; the written form plural.
- **v33 "The lot is cast into the lap"** — the lot, as with the Urim (Numbers 27:21) or in dividing the land (Joshua 18:6); the outcome is the LORD's (Acts 1:26).

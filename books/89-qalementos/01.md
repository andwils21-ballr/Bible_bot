# Qalementos 1

In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, one God. Saint Clement said:

## The Apostles Take Disciples

**1** After the ascension to heaven of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, the disciples separated, going to the ends of the world, to preach the news of the holy Gospel, to turn the peoples to faith and to the knowledge of Jesus Christ, and to baptize them with the holy baptism of the new birth. That is why the apostles wanted to find disciples for themselves who would go with them into the lands. Then Simon Peter took me and made me his own disciple. I had believed in him and in the One who sent him, and I had recognized that he was the head of the apostles, because of the word our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ spoke in the Gospel: "You are rock, and on this rock I will build My Church, and the gates of Sheol will not prevail against it. I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven." After many days he took two brothers and made them his own disciples. Then, when I had stayed with him twenty years, he took me, brought me to my father and my mother, whose name was Metroryā, and revealed to me the mysteries that our Lord Jesus Christ had taught him on the Mount of Olives.

## The Jews Persecute the Apostles and the Faithful

**2** At that time the apostles and all the faithful were suffering a great persecution from the unbelieving Jews, who went on killing every believer they met. Then the good Peter set out and entered a city, and I went with him. There we found a great persecution, caused by the quarrels of the unbelieving Jews and by their questions about the genealogy of Holy Mary: they said of her that she was not of the stock of Judah, to ruin the coming of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ into the world. They kept giving much bribe money to the Greeks and the Romans to help them destroy the faithful, and they kept the apostles from reading the Law, so that no one would know the first creation of the world.

## Clement Asks Peter to Teach Him

**3** When I had seen all this persecution coming on me from the unbelieving Jews, I inquired of my Master, Simon Peter, and asked him to make me understand and explain to me how the first creation of the world had come about; for he knew all the mysteries from Jesus Christ our Lord. As for me, I knew the language of the Greeks, their books, all their mysteries and their philosophy. I told my Master all the hatred and all the envy that had come against me: why the Jews had insulted me and quarreled with me about Holy Mary, saying that I had no knowledge of the Law and the Prophets; that they had questioned me a great deal about the creation of our father Adam; that they had blasphemed greatly against our Lady Holy Mary; and that I had found no argument with which to refute their malice and their unclean words.

## Peter Grants His Request

**4** When I had told all this to my Master, he was greatly grieved. Zeal came into him, and he said to me, "I myself will reveal to you, my son, everything you have asked me. I will make you understand the first creation of the world, and I will show you concerning Holy Mary, mother of the Light, that she is plainly, beyond any confusion, of the stock of Judah son of Jacob. I will also tell you how Satan fell from heaven."

## Notes

- **The whole book** — worked from the French of Sylvain Grébaut, "Littérature éthiopienne pseudo-clémentine. III. Traduction du Qalêmentos," *Revue de l'Orient chrétien* 16–26 (1911–1928), which is translated from the Ethiopic (the d'Abbadie manuscript 78). No Ge'ez or other source language was used. The work has seven books; Grébaut stopped in the third, and the text here stops where he did. The chapters are his, numbered straight through the books (Book I, chapters 1–24); each verse is one of his numbered sections. The section headings are Grébaut's own: he says he divided the text, which runs on without breaks in the manuscript, and added the subtitles. His folio references to the manuscript are left out.
- **The opening, "Saint Clement said"** — the book is told by Clement of Rome in the first person, as the disciple to whom Peter passes on what the risen Lord revealed.
- **v1 "You are rock, and on this rock I will build My Church"** — Matthew 16:18–19. The Greek plays on two words, *Petros* and *petra*; the French, *Tu es pierre, et sur cette pierre*, keeps a single word, and so does this rendering. Where the Greek has *the gates of Hades* (*pylai hadou*), the book has **Sheol**, the Hebrew name for the place of the dead.
- **v1 "the mysteries… on the Mount of Olives"** — the Gospel's own setting for Jesus' private teaching about the end: *as He sat on the Mount of Olives, the disciples came to Him privately* (Matthew 24:3). The book claims that Peter received there far more than the Gospels record, and that Clement now receives it from him.
- **v1 "Metroryā"** — Clement's mother, named only as the Ethiopic gives it; the name is left as Grébaut spells it.
- **v2 "not of the stock of Judah"** — the quarrel that drives the book, raised, the text says, *to ruin the coming of our Lord… into the world*. Peter's answer, promised in v4, is to tell the whole history from the first creation; Grébaut titles Book I *the Old Testament from the Creation to Jehoshaphat*.
- **v4 "mother of the Light"** — Grébaut's *mère de la Lumière*. Jesus says *I am the light of the world* (John 8:12, *to phōs tou kosmou*), so the title names Mary by her son.
- **Source text** — S. Grébaut, "Littérature éthiopienne pseudo-clémentine. III. Traduction du Qalêmentos," *Revue de l'Orient chrétien* 16 (1911), public domain. English translation by this project.

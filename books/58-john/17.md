# John 17

## Glorify Your Son

**1** When Jesus had spoken these things, He lifted up His eyes to heaven and said, "Father, the hour has come. Glorify Your Son, so that the Son may glorify You,

**2** since You have given Him authority over all flesh, to give eternal life to all whom You have given Him.

**3** This is eternal life, that they know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom You have sent.

**4** I glorified You on earth, having finished the work that You gave Me to do.

**5** Now, Father, glorify Me in Your own presence with the glory that I had with You before the world existed.

## For Those You Gave Me

**6** "I have made Your name known to the people whom You gave Me out of the world. They were Yours, and You gave them to Me, and they have kept Your word.

**7** Now they know that everything You have given Me is from You;

**8** for the words that You gave Me I have given to them, and they have received them and have come to know in truth that I came from You, and they have believed that You sent Me.

**9** I am asking on their behalf. I am not asking on behalf of the world, but of those whom You have given Me, for they are Yours.

**10** All that is Mine is Yours, and what is Yours is Mine, and I have been glorified in them.

**11** I am no longer in the world, but they are in the world, and I am coming to You. Holy Father, keep them in Your name, which You have given Me, so that they may be one, as We are.

**12** While I was with them, I kept them in Your name, which You have given Me. I guarded them, and not one of them was lost except the son of destruction, so that the Scripture might be fulfilled.

**13** But now I am coming to You, and I speak these things in the world, so that they may have My joy made complete in themselves.

**14** I have given them Your word, and the world has hated them, because they are not of the world, just as I am not of the world.

**15** I do not ask that You take them out of the world, but that You keep them from the evil one.

**16** They are not of the world, just as I am not of the world.

**17** Sanctify them in the truth; Your word is truth.

**18** As You sent Me into the world, so I have sent them into the world.

**19** For their sake I sanctify Myself, so that they also may be sanctified in truth.

## For All Who Will Believe

**20** "I do not ask on behalf of these only, but also for those who believe in Me through their word,

**21** that they may all be one, just as You, Father, are in Me and I in You, that they also may be in Us, so that the world may believe that You sent Me.

**22** The glory that You have given Me I have given to them, that they may be one just as We are one,

**23** I in them and You in Me, that they may be perfected into one, so that the world may know that You sent Me and loved them just as You loved Me.

**24** Father, I want those whom You have given Me to be with Me where I am, so that they may see My glory that You have given Me, because You loved Me before the foundation of the world.

**25** Righteous Father, even though the world has not known You, I have known You, and these have known that You sent Me.

**26** I made Your name known to them, and I will make it known, so that the love with which You loved Me may be in them, and I in them."

## Notes

- **vv1–5 "He lifted up His eyes to heaven… the hour has come… the only true God… the glory that I had with You before the world existed"** — the *hour* of 2:4 and 12:23 has come. *Exousian pasēs sarkos*, **authority over all flesh**, as the Son of Man was given *dominion* (Daniel 7:14). *The only true God*, the one God of *the LORD is one* (Deuteronomy 6:4). *Teleiōsas*, **having finished**, the word of *It is finished*, *tetelestai* (19:30). *Before the world existed*, as *In the beginning was the Word* (1:1).
- **vv6–8 "I have made Your name known… They were Yours, and You gave them to Me"** — *ephanerōsa sou to onoma*, **I have made Your name known**, the name God gave Moses, *This is My name forever* (Exodus 3:15). *Pan ho dedōkas*, **all whom You have given** (v2) and *ho dedōkas moi* (v24) are neuter in Greek, **what You have given**, as in 6:37, 39: the disciples as one gift.
- **vv9–12 "Holy Father, keep them in Your name… so that they may be one, as We are… the son of destruction"** — *Pater hagie*, **Holy Father**. *Hen*, **one**, neuter, as 10:30. *Ho huios tēs apōleias*, **the son of destruction**, the one *lost*: *apōleto… apōleias*, **was lost… destruction**, one root. *The Scripture* is Psalm 41:9, cited of Judas in 13:18.
- **vv13–19 "keep them from the evil one… Sanctify them in the truth… I sanctify Myself"** — *ek tou ponērou*, **from the evil one**, may also be **from evil**; Matthew 6:13 has *deliver us from evil*. *Hagiason*, **sanctify**, make holy, set apart, the word used of the Son *whom the Father consecrated* (10:36). *Hagiazō emauton*, **I sanctify Myself**: He sets Himself apart for them, *hyper autōn*, **for their sake**.
- **vv20–23 "those who believe in Me through their word… that they may all be one… perfected into one"** — the prayer reaches past the twelve to every later believer. *Teteleiōmenoi eis hen*, **perfected into one**, brought to completion as one, the root of *finished* (v4).
- **vv24–26 "I want those whom You have given Me to be with Me… before the foundation of the world… Righteous Father"** — *thelō*, **I want**, a will spoken to the Father. *Pro katabolēs kosmou*, **before the foundation of the world**, as *the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world* (Matthew 25:34). *Pater dikaie*, **Righteous Father**.

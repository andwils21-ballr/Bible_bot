# John 4

## Living Water

**1** When Jesus learned that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus was making and baptizing more disciples than John —

**2** although Jesus Himself did not baptize, but His disciples —

**3** He left Judea and went away again to Galilee.

**4** He had to pass through Samaria.

**5** So He came to a town of Samaria called Sychar, near the plot of ground that Jacob gave to his son Joseph.

**6** Jacob's well was there. Jesus, tired from the journey, sat down just as He was by the well. It was about the sixth hour.

**7** A woman of Samaria came to draw water. Jesus said to her, "Give Me a drink."

**8** For His disciples had gone away into the town to buy food.

**9** The Samaritan woman said to Him, "How is it that You, a Jew, ask a drink from me, a Samaritan woman?" For Jews have no dealings with Samaritans.

**10** Jesus answered her, "If you knew the gift of God, and who it is who is saying to you, 'Give Me a drink,' you would have asked Him, and He would have given you living water."

**11** The woman said to Him, "Sir, You have nothing to draw with, and the well is deep. Where then do You get that living water?

**12** Are You greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well and drank from it himself, as did his sons and his livestock?"

**13** Jesus answered her, "Everyone who drinks of this water will be thirsty again;

**14** but whoever drinks of the water that I will give him will never be thirsty again. The water that I will give him will become in him a spring of water welling up to eternal life."

**15** The woman said to Him, "Sir, give me this water, so that I will not be thirsty or have to keep coming here to draw water."

**16** He said to her, "Go, call your husband, and come here."

**17** The woman answered Him, "I have no husband." Jesus said to her, "You are right in saying, 'I have no husband';

**18** for you have had five husbands, and the one you now have is not your husband. What you have said is true."

**19** The woman said to Him, "Sir, I see that You are a prophet.

**20** Our fathers worshiped on this mountain, but you say that Jerusalem is the place where people must worship."

**21** Jesus said to her, "Believe Me, woman, an hour is coming when you will worship the Father neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem.

**22** You worship what you do not know; we worship what we know, for salvation is from the Jews.

**23** But an hour is coming, and is now here, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth; for the Father is seeking such people to worship Him.

**24** God is spirit, and those who worship Him must worship in spirit and truth."

**25** The woman said to Him, "I know that Messiah is coming" (who is called Christ). "When He comes, He will tell us all things."

**26** Jesus said to her, "I am the One who is speaking to you."

## The Harvest

**27** Just then His disciples came, and they were amazed that He was speaking with a woman; yet no one said, "What are You looking for?" or, "Why are You speaking with her?"

**28** So the woman left her water jar, went away into the town and said to the people,

**29** "Come, see a man who told me everything I ever did. Could this be the Messiah?"

**30** They went out of the town and were coming to Him.

**31** Meanwhile the disciples were urging Him, saying, "Rabbi, eat."

**32** But He said to them, "I have food to eat that you do not know about."

**33** So the disciples said to one another, "Has anyone brought Him something to eat?"

**34** Jesus said to them, "My food is to do the will of the One who sent Me and to finish His work.

**35** Do you not say, 'There are still four months, and then comes the harvest'? I tell you, lift up your eyes and see that the fields are white for harvest.

**36** Already the one who reaps is receiving wages and gathering fruit for eternal life, so that the one who sows and the one who reaps may rejoice together.

**37** For here the saying is true, 'One sows and another reaps.'

**38** I sent you to reap what you did not labor for; others have labored, and you have entered into their labor."

**39** Many of the Samaritans from that town believed in Him because of the woman's word when she bore witness, "He told me everything I ever did."

**40** So when the Samaritans came to Him, they asked Him to stay with them; and He stayed there two days.

**41** Many more believed because of His word.

**42** They said to the woman, "We no longer believe because of what you said; for we have heard for ourselves, and we know that this is truly the Savior of the world."

## The Official's Son

**43** After the two days He went from there to Galilee.

**44** For Jesus Himself had borne witness that a prophet has no honor in his own home town.

**45** So when He came to Galilee, the Galileans welcomed Him, having seen all that He had done in Jerusalem at the feast; for they too had gone to the feast.

**46** So He came again to Cana in Galilee, where He had made the water wine. There was a royal official whose son was ill at Capernaum.

**47** When he heard that Jesus had come from Judea into Galilee, he went to Him and asked Him to come down and heal his son, for he was about to die.

**48** Jesus said to him, "Unless you all see signs and wonders, you will not believe."

**49** The official said to Him, "Sir, come down before my child dies."

**50** Jesus said to him, "Go; your son lives." The man believed the word that Jesus spoke to him and went on his way.

**51** As he was going down, his slaves met him and told him that his boy was living.

**52** So he asked them the hour when he began to get better, and they said to him, "Yesterday at the seventh hour the fever left him."

**53** The father knew that it was at that hour when Jesus had said to him, "Your son lives." He himself believed, and his whole household.

**54** This again was the second sign that Jesus did, after coming from Judea into Galilee.

## Notes

- **vv1–6 "He had to pass through Samaria… the plot of ground that Jacob gave to his son Joseph… Jacob's well… the sixth hour"** — *edei*, **had to**, the word of God's plan, as 3:14, 30. Jacob bought *the portion of the field where he had pitched his tent* at Shechem (Genesis 33:19), and Joseph's bones were buried there, *an inheritance of the sons of Joseph* (Joshua 24:32). *Pēgē*, **well**, a spring, the word of v14. *The sixth hour*, noon, counted from sunrise, as 1:39. *Kekopiakōs*, **tired**, the word of *labor* in v38.
- **vv7–9 "Give Me a drink… a Jew… a Samaritan woman… Jews have no dealings with Samaritans"** — the Samaritans are the people the king of Assyria settled *in the cities of Samaria* (2 Kings 17:24), who *feared the LORD and served their own gods* (2 Kings 17:33). *Synchrōntai*, **have dealings**, may also be **use vessels in common**: a Jew would not drink from her jar.
- **vv10–15 "the gift of God… living water… a spring of water welling up to eternal life"** — *hydōr zōn*, **living water**, running water from a spring, and the LORD is *the fountain of living waters* (Jeremiah 2:13); in the last day *living water will flow out from Jerusalem* (Zechariah 14:8). *Kyrie*, **Sir**, the same word as *Lord*: she says it in politeness. *Hallomenou*, **welling up**, word for word **leaping**. Jacob's well, like those where Rebekah, Rachel and Zipporah were met (Genesis 24:11; 29:10; Exodus 2:15), is a place of meeting.
- **vv16–20 "Go, call your husband… five husbands… a prophet… on this mountain"** — *anēr* is both **husband** and **man**. *This mountain* is not named; the law set *the blessing on Mount Gerizim* (Deuteronomy 11:29).
- **vv21–24 "an hour is coming, and is now here… salvation is from the Jews… God is spirit… in spirit and truth"** — *hōra*, **hour**, as 2:4. *Pneuma ho theos*, **God is spirit**, without the article on *spirit*: what God is, as *the Word was God* (1:1). *In spirit and truth* is one phrase, one preposition, in Greek.
- **vv25–26 "Messiah is coming… He will tell us all things… I am the One who is speaking to you"** — *He will tell us all things*, as the LORD said of the prophet to come, *I will put My words in his mouth, and he shall speak to them all that I command him* (Deuteronomy 18:18). *Egō eimi*, **I am the One**, word for word **I am**, as Jesus says on the sea in Mark 6:50, the first of many times in this book.
- **vv27–30 "amazed that He was speaking with a woman… left her water jar… everything I ever did"** — *What are You looking for?* is Jesus' own question of 1:38. She leaves the jar she came to fill, and goes to tell the town. *Mēti*, **Could this be**, expects doubt: she asks, she does not say.
- **vv31–38 "My food is to do the will of the One who sent Me… white for harvest… One sows and another reaps"** — they think of bread, as the woman thought of well water. The edition prints *ēdē*, **already**, at the end of v35; it begins the sentence of v36. *One sows and another reaps* was a curse in Micah, *You will sow but not reap* (Micah 6:15); here sower and reaper *rejoice together*, as *those who sow in tears will reap with shouts of joy* (Psalm 126:5).
- **vv39–42 "because of the woman's word… we have heard for ourselves… the Savior of the world"** — *lalia*, **what you said**, word for word **your talk**, set against *logos*, **His word** (v41). *Sōtēr tou kosmou*, **Savior of the world**, found only here and in 1 John 4:14.
- **vv43–54 "a prophet has no honor in his own home town… a royal official… Go; your son lives… the second sign"** — as Mark 6:4, *A prophet is not without honor except in his home town*. *Basilikos*, **royal official**, word for word **a king's man**. *Unless you all see*, plural: the word is to all Galilee. Matthew and Luke have a centurion's servant healed from afar (Matthew 8:5–13; Luke 7:1–10). *Zē*, **lives**, three times (vv50, 51, 53).

# John 13

## The Washing of Feet

**1** Before the feast of the Passover, Jesus, knowing that His hour had come to pass out of this world to the Father, having loved His own who were in the world, loved them to the end.

**2** During supper, when the devil had already put it into the heart of Judas son of Simon Iscariot to hand Him over,

**3** Jesus, knowing that the Father had given all things into His hands, and that He had come from God and was going to God,

**4** rose from supper, laid aside His outer garments, and taking a towel, tied it around Himself.

**5** Then He poured water into a basin and began to wash the disciples' feet and to wipe them with the towel that was tied around Him.

**6** He came to Simon Peter, who said to Him, "Lord, are You going to wash my feet?"

**7** Jesus answered him, "What I am doing you do not understand now, but you will understand afterward."

**8** Peter said to Him, "You will never wash my feet." Jesus answered him, "If I do not wash you, you have no share with Me."

**9** Simon Peter said to Him, "Lord, not my feet only, but also my hands and my head!"

**10** Jesus said to him, "Whoever has bathed needs only to wash his feet, but is completely clean. You are clean, but not all of you."

**11** For He knew who was going to hand Him over; that is why He said, "Not all of you are clean."

**12** When He had washed their feet and put on His outer garments and reclined at table again, He said to them, "Do you understand what I have done to you?

**13** You call Me Teacher and Lord, and you are right, for so I am.

**14** If I then, your Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet.

**15** For I have given you an example, so that you also should do as I have done to you.

**16** Truly, truly, I tell you, a slave is not greater than his master, nor is one who is sent greater than the one who sent him.

**17** If you know these things, blessed are you if you do them.

**18** I am not speaking of all of you; I know whom I have chosen. But it is so that the Scripture may be fulfilled: 'The one who eats My bread has lifted up his heel against Me.'

**19** I am telling you now, before it happens, so that when it does happen you may believe that I am He.

**20** Truly, truly, I tell you, whoever receives anyone I send receives Me, and whoever receives Me receives the One who sent Me."

## One of You Will Hand Me Over

**21** When Jesus had said these things, He was troubled in spirit, and bore witness, "Truly, truly, I tell you, one of you will hand Me over."

**22** The disciples looked at one another, at a loss to know of whom He spoke.

**23** One of His disciples, whom Jesus loved, was reclining at Jesus' side.

**24** So Simon Peter motioned to him to ask Jesus of whom He was speaking.

**25** Leaning back against Jesus' chest, he said to Him, "Lord, who is it?"

**26** Jesus answered, "It is the one to whom I will give this piece of bread when I have dipped it." So when He had dipped the piece of bread, He gave it to Judas son of Simon Iscariot.

**27** After the piece of bread, Satan entered into him. Jesus said to him, "What you are going to do, do quickly."

**28** No one at the table knew why He said this to him.

**29** Some thought that, because Judas had the money bag, Jesus was telling him, "Buy what we need for the feast," or that he should give something to the poor.

**30** So after receiving the piece of bread, he went out at once. It was night.

## A New Commandment

**31** When he had gone out, Jesus said, "Now the Son of Man has been glorified, and God has been glorified in Him.

**32** If God has been glorified in Him, God will also glorify Him in Himself, and will glorify Him at once.

**33** Little children, I am with you a little while longer. You will look for Me; and as I said to the Jews, 'Where I am going, you cannot come,' so now I say to you.

**34** A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another: just as I have loved you, you also are to love one another.

**35** By this all people will know that you are My disciples, if you have love for one another."

**36** Simon Peter said to Him, "Lord, where are You going?" Jesus answered, "Where I am going you cannot follow Me now, but you will follow afterward."

**37** Peter said to Him, "Lord, why can I not follow You now? I will lay down my life for You."

**38** Jesus answered, "Will you lay down your life for Me? Truly, truly, I tell you, the rooster will not crow until you have denied Me three times."

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "to pass out of this world to the Father… loved them to the end… the devil had already put it into the heart of Judas"** — *metabē*, **to pass**, to cross over, in the week of the Passover. *Eis telos*, **to the end**, also **to the utmost**, and the cross is where Jesus says *It is finished*, *tetelestai* (19:30), from the same root. *He had come from God and was going to God*, the *going* of 7:33 and 8:21.
- **vv4–5 "laid aside His outer garments… a towel… began to wash the disciples' feet"** — *tithēsin ta himatia*, **laid aside His garments**, the verb of the shepherd who *lays down His life* (10:11), and *elaben*, **took up**, in v12, as *take it up again* (10:17). Abraham offered his guests water *to wash your feet* (Genesis 18:4), a servant's work; Abigail said, *Your servant is a maidservant to wash the feet of my lord's servants* (1 Samuel 25:41). In Luke the master *will fasten his belt… and come and serve them* (Luke 12:37).
- **vv6–11 "You will never wash my feet… you have no share with Me… Whoever has bathed… not all of you"** — *meros*, **share**, a portion in an inheritance. *Ho leloumenos*, **whoever has bathed**, set against *nipsasthai*, **to wash**, a part only; the edition has *needs only to wash his feet* and marks the words as a place where manuscripts differ.
- **vv12–17 "Teacher and Lord… an example… a slave is not greater than his master… blessed are you if you do them"** — Matthew has *A disciple is not above his teacher, nor a slave above his master* (Matthew 10:24). *Hypodeigma*, **example**, a pattern to copy. *Apostolos*, **one who is sent**, the word *apostle*, its only use in this book.
- **vv18–20 "The one who eats My bread has lifted up his heel against Me… that I am He"** — Psalm 41:9, *Even the man of my peace, in whom I trusted, who ate my bread, has lifted up his heel against me*; John leaves out *in whom I trusted*. *Trōgōn*, **eats**, the word of 6:54. *Hoti egō eimi*, **that I am He**, as 8:24, 28 and Isaiah 43:10. v20 is Matthew 10:40.
- **vv21–30 "one of you will hand Me over… whom Jesus loved, was reclining at Jesus' side… Satan entered into him… It was night"** — as Mark 14:18–21. *The disciple whom Jesus loved* appears here for the first time, and is never named. *En tō kolpō*, **at Jesus' side**, word for word **in the bosom**, as the Son is *at the Father's side* (1:18). *Psōmion*, **piece of bread**, a morsel dipped in the dish. *Ēn de nyx*, **It was night**: Judas goes out from the light (8:12; 11:10).
- **vv31–35 "Now the Son of Man has been glorified… Little children… A new commandment… that you love one another"** — *doxazō*, **glorify**, five times in two verses. *Teknia*, **little children**, its only use in this book. The law said *you shall love your neighbor as yourself* (Leviticus 19:18); what is new is the measure, *just as I have loved you*.
- **vv36–38 "Where I am going you cannot follow Me now… I will lay down my life for You… the rooster will not crow"** — Peter uses the shepherd's words of 10:11, *lay down my life*. As Luke 22:34, *the rooster will not crow today until you have denied three times that you know Me*; Mark has the rooster crow twice (Mark 14:30).

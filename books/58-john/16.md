# John 16

## The Work of the Advocate

**1** "I have spoken these things to you so that you will not stumble.

**2** They will put you out of the synagogue; indeed, an hour is coming when whoever kills you will think he is offering service to God.

**3** They will do these things because they have not known the Father, nor Me.

**4** But I have spoken these things to you so that when their hour comes you may remember that I told you of them. I did not tell you these things from the beginning, because I was with you.

**5** But now I am going to the One who sent Me, and none of you asks Me, 'Where are You going?'

**6** But because I have said these things to you, sorrow has filled your hearts.

**7** Nevertheless I tell you the truth: it is to your advantage that I go away; for if I do not go away, the Advocate will not come to you; but if I go, I will send Him to you.

**8** When He comes, He will convict the world concerning sin and righteousness and judgment:

**9** concerning sin, because they do not believe in Me;

**10** concerning righteousness, because I am going to the Father and you will see Me no longer;

**11** concerning judgment, because the ruler of this world has been judged.

**12** "I still have many things to say to you, but you cannot bear them now.

**13** When He, the Spirit of truth, comes, He will guide you into all the truth; for He will not speak on His own, but whatever He hears He will speak, and He will declare to you the things that are to come.

**14** He will glorify Me, for He will take what is Mine and declare it to you.

**15** All that the Father has is Mine; that is why I said that He takes what is Mine and will declare it to you.

## A Little While

**16** "A little while, and you will see Me no longer; and again a little while, and you will see Me."

**17** So some of His disciples said to one another, "What is this that He is telling us, 'A little while, and you do not see Me; and again a little while, and you will see Me'; and, 'because I am going to the Father'?"

**18** So they were saying, "What is this 'little while' that He speaks of? We do not know what He is talking about."

**19** Jesus knew that they wanted to ask Him, and He said to them, "Are you asking one another about this, that I said, 'A little while, and you do not see Me; and again a little while, and you will see Me'?

**20** Truly, truly, I tell you, you will weep and mourn, but the world will rejoice. You will be sorrowful, but your sorrow will turn into joy.

**21** When a woman is giving birth, she has sorrow, because her hour has come; but when she has borne the child, she no longer remembers the anguish, for joy that a human being has been born into the world.

**22** So also you have sorrow now; but I will see you again, and your hearts will rejoice, and no one will take your joy from you.

**23** On that day you will ask Me nothing. Truly, truly, I tell you, whatever you ask the Father in My name, He will give it to you.

**24** Until now you have asked nothing in My name. Ask, and you will receive, so that your joy may be complete.

## I Have Overcome the World

**25** "I have spoken these things to you in figures of speech. An hour is coming when I will no longer speak to you in figures of speech, but will tell you plainly about the Father.

**26** On that day you will ask in My name; and I do not say to you that I will ask the Father on your behalf,

**27** for the Father Himself loves you, because you have loved Me and have believed that I came from God.

**28** I came from the Father and have come into the world; now I am leaving the world and going to the Father."

**29** His disciples said, "Now You are speaking plainly, and not in any figure of speech!

**30** Now we know that You know all things, and do not need anyone to question You. By this we believe that You came from God."

**31** Jesus answered them, "Do you now believe?

**32** An hour is coming, and has come, when you will be scattered, each to his own, and will leave Me alone. Yet I am not alone, for the Father is with Me.

**33** I have spoken these things to you so that in Me you may have peace. In the world you have trouble; but take heart, I have overcome the world."

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "so that you will not stumble… put you out of the synagogue… offering service to God"** — *skandalisthēte*, **stumble**, fall away. *Aposynagōgous*, as 9:22 and 12:42. *Latreian*, **service**, the word of worship at the altar.
- **vv5–7 "none of you asks Me, 'Where are You going?'… it is to your advantage that I go away"** — Peter and Thomas had asked (13:36; 14:5), but now the disciples are filled with sorrow, not questions. *Sympherei*, **it is to your advantage**, the word of Caiaphas, *it is better for you that one man should die* (11:50).
- **vv8–11 "He will convict the world concerning sin and righteousness and judgment"** — *elenxei*, **convict**, prove guilty, the word of a court. *The ruler of this world has been judged*, as *will be driven out* (12:31).
- **vv12–15 "you cannot bear them now… He will guide you into all the truth… declare to you the things that are to come"** — *bastazein*, **bear**, carry a load. *Hodēgēsei*, **will guide**, lead on the way, from *hodos*, the word of *I am the way* (14:6). *Anangelei*, **will declare**, three times, the word the Samaritan woman used of the Messiah, *He will tell us all things* (4:25).
- **vv16–19 "A little while, and you will see Me no longer… again a little while"** — *mikron*, **a little while**, seven times in four verses. *Theōreite… opsesthe*, **see… will see**, two verbs for seeing.
- **vv20–22 "your sorrow will turn into joy… When a woman is giving birth… your hearts will rejoice"** — the prophets used the woman in labor of the people's pain, *Like a woman with child who writhes and cries out* (Isaiah 26:17), and of new birth, *before her pain came upon her, she delivered a male child* (Isaiah 66:7). *Your hearts will rejoice* is Isaiah 66:14, *You will see, and your heart will rejoice*, where the Greek has the same words, *kai charēsetai hē kardia hymōn*. *Her hour*, the word of His own hour.
- **vv23–24 "On that day you will ask Me nothing… Ask, and you will receive"** — *erōtēsete*, **ask**, a question, set against *aitēsēte*, **ask**, a request: on that day they will have no more questions, and may make any request. *Ask, and it will be given to you* (Luke 11:9).
- **vv25–30 "in figures of speech… plainly… the Father Himself loves you… I came from the Father"** — *paroimiai*, **figures of speech**, as 10:6. *Parrēsia*, **plainly**, openly. *Philei*, **loves**, the Father's love for the disciples.
- **vv31–33 "you will be scattered… Yet I am not alone… take heart, I have overcome the world"** — *Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered* (Zechariah 13:7; Mark 14:27). *Thlipsis*, **trouble**, pressure, the *anguish* of birth in v21. *Nenikēka*, **I have overcome**, the perfect: it is done, and it stands.

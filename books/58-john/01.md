# John 1

## The Word

**1** In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.

**2** He was in the beginning with God.

**3** All things came into being through Him, and without Him not one thing came into being.

**4** What has come into being in Him was life, and the life was the light of mankind.

**5** The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it.

**6** There came a man sent from God, whose name was John.

**7** He came as a witness, to bear witness about the light, so that all might believe through him.

**8** He was not the light, but came to bear witness about the light.

**9** The true light, which gives light to everyone, was coming into the world.

**10** He was in the world, and the world came into being through Him, yet the world did not know Him.

**11** He came to His own, and His own people did not receive Him.

**12** But to all who received Him, who believe in His name, He gave the right to become children of God,

**13** who were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of a man, but of God.

**14** The Word became flesh and pitched His tent among us, and we have seen His glory, glory as of the only Son from the Father, full of grace and truth.

**15** (John bore witness about Him and cried out, "This was He of whom I said, 'He who comes after me has gone ahead of me, because He was before me.'")

**16** For from His fullness we have all received, grace upon grace.

**17** For the Law was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.

**18** No one has ever seen God; the only Son, who is God, who is at the Father's side, He has made Him known.

## The Witness of John

**19** This is the witness of John, when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to ask him, "Who are you?"

**20** He confessed, and did not deny; he confessed, "I am not the Messiah."

**21** They asked him, "What then? Are you Elijah?" He said, "I am not." "Are you the Prophet?" He answered, "No."

**22** So they said to him, "Who are you? Let us give an answer to those who sent us. What do you say about yourself?"

**23** He said, "I am the voice of one crying out in the wilderness, 'Make straight the way of the Lord,' as Isaiah the prophet said."

**24** They had been sent from the Pharisees.

**25** They asked him, "Then why are you baptizing, if you are not the Messiah, nor Elijah, nor the Prophet?"

**26** John answered them, "I baptize with water; among you stands one whom you do not know,

**27** He who comes after me, the strap of whose sandal I am not worthy to untie."

**28** This took place in Bethany beyond the Jordan, where John was baptizing.

## The Lamb of God

**29** The next day he saw Jesus coming toward him, and said, "Look: the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!

**30** This is He of whom I said, 'After me comes a man who has gone ahead of me, because He was before me.'

**31** I myself did not know Him; but I came baptizing with water for this, that He might be revealed to Israel."

**32** John bore witness, saying, "I saw the Spirit coming down from heaven like a dove, and it remained on Him.

**33** I myself did not know Him; but the One who sent me to baptize with water said to me, 'The one on whom you see the Spirit come down and remain, this is He who baptizes with the Holy Spirit.'

**34** I have seen, and have borne witness that this is the Chosen One of God."

## The First Disciples

**35** The next day John was standing there again with two of his disciples,

**36** and he looked at Jesus as He walked by and said, "Look: the Lamb of God!"

**37** The two disciples heard him say this, and they followed Jesus.

**38** Jesus turned and saw them following, and said to them, "What are you looking for?" They said to Him, "Rabbi" (which translated means Teacher), "where are You staying?"

**39** He said to them, "Come and you will see." So they came and saw where He was staying, and they stayed with Him that day. It was about the tenth hour.

**40** One of the two who heard John and followed Him was Andrew, Simon Peter's brother.

**41** He first found his own brother Simon and said to him, "We have found the Messiah" (which translated means Christ).

**42** He brought him to Jesus. Jesus looked at him and said, "You are Simon the son of John. You will be called Cephas" (which is translated Peter).

**43** The next day He decided to go to Galilee. He found Philip, and Jesus said to him, "Follow Me."

**44** Philip was from Bethsaida, the town of Andrew and Peter.

**45** Philip found Nathanael and said to him, "We have found Him of whom Moses in the Law, and also the prophets, wrote: Jesus son of Joseph, from Nazareth."

**46** Nathanael said to him, "Can anything good come out of Nazareth?" Philip said to him, "Come and see."

**47** Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward Him and said of him, "Here truly is an Israelite in whom there is no deceit!"

**48** Nathanael said to Him, "How do You know me?" Jesus answered him, "Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you."

**49** Nathanael answered Him, "Rabbi, You are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!"

**50** Jesus answered him, "Because I said to you that I saw you under the fig tree, do you believe? You will see greater things than these."

**51** He said to him, "Truly, truly, I tell you all, you will see heaven opened, and the angels of God going up and coming down on the Son of Man."

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God"** — *En archē*, **in the beginning**, is letter for letter how the Greek of Genesis opens, *En archē epoiēsen ho theos*, *In the beginning, God created* (Genesis 1:1). *Logos*, **Word**: Greek had a plain word for something said, *rhēma*, and John does not use it. *Logos* is from *legō*, to gather or lay in order; it came to mean an account, a reckoning, the reason or ordering principle of a thing. The scriptures had already said *By the word of the LORD the heavens were made* (Psalm 33:6), and Wisdom, *who made all things by Your word* (Wisdom of Solomon 9:1). *Pros ton theon*, **with God**, word for word **toward God**, face to face. *Theos ēn ho logos*: *theos* set first for weight and without the article, **the Word was God**; in the line before, with the article, *ton theon*, God whom the Word was with.
- **vv3–5 "All things came into being through Him… What has come into being in Him was life… the darkness has not overcome it"** — *egeneto*, **came into being**, set against *ēn*, **was**: the Word *was*, all else *came to be*. The edition puts *ho gegonen*, **what has come into being**, at the start of the sentence of v4; the verse numbers put it at the end of v3, which reads *not one thing came into being that has come into being*. The light shining in darkness is the first day, *"Light — be." And light was* (Genesis 1:3). *Katelaben*, **has overcome**, also means **has grasped**, laid hold of with the hand or the mind.
- **vv6–13 "a man sent from God… The true light… was coming into the world… the right to become children of God"** — John the Baptist is never called *the Baptist* in this book. *Erchomenon*, **coming**, may go with *the light* or with *everyone*, **every person coming into the world**. *Ta idia… hoi idioi*, **His own… His own people**: first His own things, His home, then His own people. *Ex haimatōn*, **of blood**, plural in Greek, **of bloods**.
- **v14 "The Word became flesh and pitched His tent among us… the only Son from the Father, full of grace and truth"** — *eskēnōsen*, **pitched His tent**, from *skēnē*, the tent of meeting where *the glory of the LORD filled the dwelling* (Exodus 40:34). Wisdom says in Sirach, *Pitch your tent in Jacob* (Sirach 24:8), with the same verb in Greek. *Monogenēs*, **only**, the one of its kind, as the widow's *only son* (Luke 7:12). *Grace and truth* is the LORD's name before Moses, *abundant in loyal love and truth* (Exodus 34:6).
- **vv15–17 "He was before me… grace upon grace… the Law was given through Moses"** — *prōtos mou*, **before me**, word for word **first of me**: John was born first, but the Word was first. *Charin anti charitos*, **grace upon grace**, word for word **grace in place of grace**: one grace given after another, or the grace of Christ in place of the grace of the Law.
- **v18 "No one has ever seen God; the only Son, who is God… at the Father's side"** — *You cannot see My face, because no human being can see Me and live* (Exodus 33:20). The edition reads *monogenēs theos*, word for word **only God**, and marks it as a place where manuscripts differ. *Eis ton kolpon*, **at the Father's side**, word for word **into the bosom**, as Lazarus at *Abraham's side* (Luke 16:22). *Exēgēsato*, **He has made Him known**, laid Him out, the root of *exegesis*.
- **vv19–28 "the Jews… Are you Elijah?… the Prophet?… the voice of one crying out in the wilderness"** — *hoi Ioudaioi*, **the Jews**, here the ones in Jerusalem who send priests and Levites. *Elijah* was promised *before the great and fearful day of the LORD* (Malachi 4:5); *the Prophet* is the one Moses foretold, *A prophet… like me* (Deuteronomy 18:15). The voice is Isaiah's, *In the wilderness prepare the way of the LORD* (Isaiah 40:3), with *Make straight* from its next line.
- **vv29–34 "the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world… the Spirit… remained on Him… the Chosen One of God"** — Abraham said, *God will see to the lamb for a burnt offering* (Genesis 22:8), and the servant goes *like a lamb that is led to the slaughter* (Isaiah 53:7). *Menon*, **remain**, the word this book uses of abiding (vv38–39). The edition reads *ho eklektos*, **the Chosen One**, as Isaiah's *My chosen one… I have put My Spirit on him* (Isaiah 42:1), and marks it as a place where manuscripts differ; others have *the Son of God*.
- **vv35–42 "What are you looking for?… where are You staying?… Come and you will see… Cephas"** — the first words of Jesus in this book are a question. *Menō*, **stay**, three times in vv38–39. *The tenth hour*, counted from sunrise, late afternoon. *Kēphas* is Aramaic for **rock**, as *Petros* is Greek; Matthew has *you are Peter, and on this rock* (Matthew 16:18).
- **vv43–51 "an Israelite in whom there is no deceit… under the fig tree… the angels of God going up and coming down on the Son of Man"** — Jacob, who became Israel, came to his father *with deceit* (Genesis 27:35); the Greek has *dolos* there and here. *Blessed is the man… in whose spirit there is no deceit* (Psalm 32:2). Jacob's dream at Bethel: *a stairway was set on the earth… Messengers of God were going up and coming down on it* (Genesis 28:12); here the Son of Man is the stairway. *Amēn amēn*, **truly, truly**, doubled only in this book. *Hymin*, **you all**, plural.

# 2 John 1

**1** The elder, to the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in truth, and not I alone, but also all who have known the truth,

**2** because of the truth that remains in us and will be with us forever:

**3** grace, mercy and peace will be with us, from God the Father and from Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.

## Walking in the Commandment

**4** I rejoiced greatly to find some of your children walking in truth, just as we received commandment from the Father.

**5** Now, lady, I ask you, not as though I were writing you a new commandment, but the one we have had from the beginning: that we love one another.

**6** This is love, that we walk according to His commandments. This is the commandment, just as you heard from the beginning, that you walk in it.

## The Deceiver

**7** For many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who do not confess Jesus Christ coming in the flesh. This is the deceiver and the antichrist.

**8** Watch yourselves, so that you do not lose what we have worked for, but receive a full reward.

**9** Everyone who goes too far and does not remain in the teaching of Christ does not have God. Whoever remains in the teaching has both the Father and the Son.

**10** If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive him into your house, and do not give him a greeting;

**11** for whoever gives him a greeting shares in his evil works.

**12** Though I have many things to write to you, I did not want to do it with paper and ink; but I hope to come to you and speak face to face, so that your joy may be complete.

**13** The children of your chosen sister greet you.

## Notes

- **v1 "the chosen lady"** — *eklektē kyria*. *Kyria* is the feminine of *kyrios*, lord: a lady, a woman of standing. Read as names, the words are *Kyria* or *Eklektē*. The letter ends with *the children of your chosen sister* (v13), which reads most easily as one church greeting another, the lady and her sister as two congregations and their children as their members. The Greek leaves both open, a woman with her household or a church, and so does *the chosen lady*.
- **v7 "coming in the flesh"** — *erchomenon en sarki*, the present participle. The first letter has the perfect, *has come in the flesh* (1 John 4:2). The present describes Him as one who comes in the flesh without fixing a time: it can be read of His coming as a man, or of a coming still ahead.
- **v9 "the teaching of Christ"** — *tē didachē tou Christou* can mean the teaching Christ gave or the teaching about Christ; the Greek does not choose.

# 2nd Book of the Covenant 20

## The Passion and Resurrection of Jesus

**1** We know that He is the one who was crucified in the days of Pontius Pilate and of the prince Archelaus. It was between thieves that He was crucified, and with them He was taken down from the wood of the cross. He was buried in the place called Kranion, where the three women went, Sara, Martha and Mary Magdalene. They brought spices to pour on His body, weeping and grieving over what had happened. They came near the tomb; they found the stone rolled away, outside the tomb; they opened the door of the tomb, but they did not find His body.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "in the days of Pontius Pilate and of the prince Archelaus"** — Archelaus is the son of Herod who ruled Judea after his father, when Joseph came back from Egypt (Matthew 2:22); the book sets his name beside Pilate's. *Kranion* is the Greek word for **skull**, the Place of the Skull. The three women are *Sara, Martha and Mary Magdalene* in the Ethiopic; James's English, from the Coptic, has *Mary, she that was kin to Martha, and Mary Magdalene*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

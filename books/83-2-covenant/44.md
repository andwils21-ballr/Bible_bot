# 2nd Book of the Covenant 44

**1** We said to Him again, "When shall we meet this man, and when will You go to Your Father, who is also our God and our Lord?" He answered and said to us, "This man will come out of the land of Cilicia to Damascus in Syria, to tear apart the Church you have just founded there. It is I who will speak to him through you; he will come quickly to the faith. This will happen so that the word of the prophet may be fulfilled, which says: *From the land of Syria I will begin to call the new Jerusalem, and I will subject Zion to Me; she who was captive and barren, without sons, will become fruitful in sons and will be called the daughter of My Father, and she will be My bride;* for so the One who sent Me has willed. As for this man, I will change him, so that he does not carry out his evil plan, and through him My Father will be glorified. After I have gone and am with My Father, I Myself will speak to him from heaven, and all that I have foretold to you about him will happen."

---

## Notes

- **v1 "she who was captive and barren, without sons, will become fruitful in sons"** — Isaiah's word to Zion: *Sing, barren one, who did not bear* (Isaiah 54:1). The prophet's words as the book gives them name Syria and *the new Jerusalem*, and call Zion the Son's *bride*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

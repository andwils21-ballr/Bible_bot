# 2nd Book of the Covenant 18

**1** For as for Cerinthus and Simon, they will go about the world; these are truly the adversaries of the Lord Jesus Christ, who will truly turn aside those who believed in the true Word and His works, that is, in Jesus Christ. So take care and guard yourselves from them, for through them will come affliction, impurity, death, the end of destruction, and damnation.

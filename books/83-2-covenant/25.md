# 2nd Book of the Covenant 25

**1** "Do you know that the angel Gabriel came and brought the message to Mary?"

**2** We said to Him, "Yes, Lord."

**3** He answered and said to us, "Do you not remember that I told you long ago that among the angels I was like an angel?"

## Notes

- **v5 "in the likeness of the archangel Gabriel, I Myself appeared to the virgin Mary"** — the book makes the Word His own messenger: in Luke the angel Gabriel is sent to Mary (Luke 1:26), and here it is the Son in Gabriel's likeness. James's English, from the Coptic, says the same, *I alone was a minister unto myself*; *she laughed* is the Ethiopic's alone. The verse breaks off at *My*; James's English goes on, *Thereafter did I return to my Father*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 2nd Book of the Covenant 13

**1** We, John and Thomas and Peter and Andrew and James and Philip and Bartholomew and Matthew and Nathanael and Judas the Zealot and Cephas, have written to the churches of the East and the West, of the North and the South, proclaiming and setting out to you what concerns our Lord Jesus Christ. We write what we have heard. We touched Him after He had risen from the dead, and we have written how He revealed to us things great, wonderful and true.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "We write what we have heard. We touched Him"** — so the Ethiopic as Guerrier gives it. James's English has *according as we have seen and heard and touched him*, the words of John's letter: *what we have heard, what we have seen with our eyes, what we looked upon and our hands touched* (1 John 1:1). The list of eleven names Peter and Cephas as two men.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 2nd Book of the Covenant 52

**1** He said to us, "Go and preach; be good ministers and good servants."

**2** We said to Him, "Lord, it is You who are our Father."

**3** He said to us, "Are you not all fathers? Are you not all ministers? Are you not all masters?"

**4** Then we said to Him, "Lord, is it not You Yourself who

**5** said to us, 'Call no one on earth father, nor master, for one is your Father and your Master, the One who is in heaven'? Now You tell us that we must become fathers of many children, and masters too, and ministers."

**6** He answered and said to us, "You are right to speak so; but truly I tell you: all who listen to you and believe in Me will receive the light of the seal that is in My hand, and through Me you will become —

## Notes

- **v5 "Call no one on earth father, nor master"** — the Lord's words in Matthew: *Call no one on earth your father, for you have one Father, the One in heaven. Nor are you to be called leaders* (Matthew 23:9–10). Here the apostles set those words against His own command to be fathers. v6 breaks off; James's English goes on, *ye shall be fathers and servants and masters*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 2nd Book of the Covenant 30

**1** "Preach and teach this to those who will believe in Me. Preach what concerns the kingdom of heaven, the kingdom of My Father. As My Father has given Me power, so I give it to you, so that you may be sons of My heavenly Father. Preach, so that you may bring the faithful to Him and they may believe in Him. It is you who must bring His sons into heaven."

**2** We said to Him, "Lord, what You have told us to do is possible for You; but how shall we be able to do it?"

**3** He said to us, "Truly I tell you, preach and set out what I have told you, as I have told you. I Myself will be with you, so that you may be co-heirs with Me of the kingdom of heaven, the kingdom of the One who sent Me. Truly I tell you, you will become My brothers and My friends, and so will those who through you believe in Me. Truly I tell you, how great is the joy My Father has prepared for you, a joy the angels and the powers have desired, and still desire, to look on and see; but they are not allowed to see the majesty of My Father."

**4** We said to Him, "Lord, what is this that You are setting out for us?"

**5** He said to us, "See the Light that comes from the Light that shines, the Perfect from the Perfect; for the Son is made perfect by the Father, who is the Light; for the Perfect is the Father, who makes perfect, who brings from death to resurrection, and whose perfection surpasses all perfection. I am the right hand of the Father; I am wholly in Him who makes perfect."

**6** We, the twelve, said to Him, "Lord, in all things You have become salvation and life for us, setting out such a hope for us."

**7** He said to us, "Have confidence and have faith. Truly I tell you, there will be for you a — and a rest, in which there will be no more need to eat or drink, no sadness, no care,

**9** no more earthly clothing and no more corruption. It is not with the creatures below that you will then have to do, but with those that are incorruptible, the creatures of My Father: you too will become incorruptible. Just as I am always in the Father, so you too will be in Me."

**10** We said to Him again, "In what form will we be, that of the angels or that of the flesh?"

**11** He answered this question and said to us, "For My part, I have put on your own flesh, in which I was begotten, was killed, was buried, and rose by My heavenly Father, so that what was said by the prophet David about My death and My resurrection might be fulfilled:

**12** *O Lord, how many are those who persecute Me!*

**13** *Many have risen against Me;*

**14** *many say to My soul,*

**15** *'He has no salvation in His God.'*

**16** *But You, Lord, are My protector, My glory, the one who lifts My head.*

**17** *With My voice I cried to the Lord,*

**18** *and He answered Me from the mountain of His temple.*

**20** *As for Me, I lay down and slept,*

**21** *and I rose, for the Lord raised Me.*

**22** *I do not fear the thousands of men*

**23** *who have surrounded Me and risen against Me.*

**24** *Rise, O Lord, my God, and save Me,*

**25** *for You have punished those who rage against Me without cause,*

**26** *and broken the teeth of sinners.*

**27** *Salvation belongs to the Lord;*

**28** *may Your blessing be on Your people!*

**29** All that was foretold by the prophets has come about, has taken place and been fulfilled in Me, for I Myself spoke through them. How much more must what I Myself have set out for you truly happen, so that the One who sent Me may be glorified by Me and by those who will believe in Me."

## Notes

- **v3 "the angels and the powers have desired, and still desire, to look on and see"** — Peter's letter: *things into which angels long to look* (1 Peter 1:12).
- **vv12–28 "O Lord, how many are those who persecute Me"** — Psalm 3, *LORD, how many are my foes!… I lay down and slept; I woke again, for the LORD sustains me*, here spoken by the Son of His own death and rising: *I lay down and slept, and I rose, for the Lord raised Me*. In the text held here each line of the psalm stands as its own paragraph, and the verses keep that division.
- **vv7–9, 19** — the paragraphs numbered 8 and 19 in the text held here are lines of Ge'ez read as Latin letters, and the end of v7 repeats words of v5 in a jumble; none of it is French text, so no verses stand at 8 and 19. *But You, Lord, are My protector* (v16), *My friends, and so will those who through you* (v3) and *My resurrection* (v11) are lost in the French and are from James.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

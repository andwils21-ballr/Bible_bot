# 2nd Book of the Covenant 51

**1** We said to Him, "Lord, truly we are grieved about them."

**2** He said to us, "You do well. So the righteous will be grieved about the sinners; they will pray, they will plead with the Lord, they will intercede —"

**3** We said to Him, "But You Yourself, Lord, does no one plead with You about the sinners?"

**4** He said to us, "Yes, and I hear the prayer of the righteous for them."

**5** We said to Him, "Lord, You who have taught us all things, who have sent us to preach, will there not be a reward for us with You?"

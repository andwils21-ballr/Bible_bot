# 2nd Book of the Covenant 53

**1** We said to Him, "Lord, how is it possible for these three titles to be in one of us?"

**2** He answered and said to us, "Truly, truly, I tell you, you will be called fathers because you will reveal to people the things of love, of mercy, of the kingdom of heaven; you will reveal that it is by My own hand that they receive the baptism of life and the forgiveness of sins. You will be called masters because you will have spoken My word without grudging and corrected them, and they will have turned in all the things for which you rebuked them. I will say to them: You were not ashamed of Me, and you did not show partiality, but you kept and did the commandment of the Father; you will have a reward with My heavenly Father. For them too there will be forgiveness of sins, eternal life, and a share in the kingdom."

**3** We said to Him, "Lord, even if they had thousands of mouths, they could not praise You as is fitting."

## Notes

- **v2** — the French gives the reasons for *fathers* and *masters*; James's English has a third, for *servants*: *because they shall receive the baptism of life and the remission of their sins at my hand through you*, which the French joins to *fathers*. v4 breaks off; James has *even as I myself also have done it*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

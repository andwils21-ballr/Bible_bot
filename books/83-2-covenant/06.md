# 2nd Book of the Covenant 6

**1** Then He spoke about the Deceiver. He told us that this Deceiver is the Antichrist, who will count us among the —; for his coming is for destruction. The hour is near; he is coming, and the road is opening for him. A glowing sword is in his hand; his anger and his indignation are fire, the weapon of torment. He is the one who arranges evil, who was born for corruption, who — blood; for his strength is deception, his hand is given to trickery, his right hand is torture and his left hand darkness. This is what marks him: his head is like a flame of fire, his right eye is shot with blood, his left eye is dead, the two pupils of his eyes are white in their lids, his lower lip is large, his feet broad, his fingers and the joints of his feet twisted. He is the scythe of destruction: the time has come, the harvest is near; he will reap those he must, for torment, and to many he will present himself as the Christ. He will praise himself and his works, and confirm the thinking of his mind.

## Notes

- **v1 "who will count us among the — … who — blood"** — the text held here is damaged in two places: a word is lost after *count us among the*, and the verb before *blood*. The gaps are left open.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain. English translation by this project.

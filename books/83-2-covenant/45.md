# 2nd Book of the Covenant 45

**1** We said to Him again, "Lord, what great things, new to us, You have set out and revealed to us! You have given us full rest; You have been merciful to us; may we be truly saved. Now You told us there would be signs and wonders in heaven and on earth before the end of the world comes. Let us know it, then, and teach it to us."

**2** He said to us, "I will teach you what will happen, though not in your own generation, but in that of those you will teach and who will believe. There are people who will listen to you and believe in Me. In the years and days I speak of, these things will come about."

**3** We said to Him again, "Lord, what will happen?" He said to us, "Then the faithful, and those also who do not believe, will hear the trumpet in heaven. They will see great stars appear in the daytime; heavenly bodies will come down from the sky to touch the earth, as the stars fall like fire, with great hailstones of burning fire; the sun and the moon will fight each other; there will always be the fear of thunder, the lightning and the crash of thunder. Earthquakes will follow; cities will fall, and people will die under the ruins. There will be drought continually for lack of rain; there will be a great and widespread plague, and death frequent and swift, so that there will not be enough funerals for those who die. There will come the separation of children and parents in the same bed: parents will not turn to their children, nor children to their parents; no one will turn to another. The forsaken, who have been abandoned, will rise; they will see those who rejected them and drove them away. For there will be a disaster over all: hatred, calamity, envy; they will take from this one and give to that one. What comes after will be worse than this."

---

## Notes

- **v3** — the signs of the book's first part told again (4:3). Here the word lost at 4:3 after *in the same* stands as *bed*, though the sentence is not the same: there it is *war of children with their parents*, here *the separation of children and parents*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

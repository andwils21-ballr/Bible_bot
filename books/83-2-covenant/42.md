# 2nd Book of the Covenant 42

**1** "You will meet a man called Saul, which means Paul. He is a Jew, circumcised according to the precepts of the Law. He will hear My own word from heaven in dread, fear and terror. His eyes will be darkened, and they will be signed with the sign of the cross by your own hands. Do everything for him as I Myself have done for you; turn him for the others. At the right time the eyes of this man will open, and he will glorify the Lord, My heavenly Father. He will become mighty among the nations; he will preach, he will teach, and many who hear him will be saved. Then people will be angry with him, and will hand him over into the hands of his enemies. He will confess the faith before mortal kings, and he will confess Me perfectly, in return for having persecuted Me before. He will preach, he will teach, he will become among the chosen a chosen instrument, a wall that will not fall; he, the last of the last, will become the preacher to the nations, and he will be perfect in doing the will of My Father. Just as you have known from the Scriptures that your fathers the prophets spoke of Me, and that in Me their words have truly been fulfilled," He said to us, "so be guides for people yourselves. Tell them all that I have set out for you, and also what you have written about Me. Tell them that I am the Word of the Father, and that the Father is in Me. Be for this man, too, what you ought to be: instruct him, remind him of what has been said and fulfilled about Me in the Scriptures, and then he will become the salvation of the nations."

## Notes

- **v1 "a chosen instrument… the last of the last"** — the Lord's word to Ananias about Saul, *he is a chosen instrument of Mine, to carry My name before the nations and kings* (Acts 9:15), and Paul of himself, *Last of all… He appeared also to me. For I am the least of the apostles… because I persecuted the church of God* (1 Corinthians 15:8–9). *Who hear him will be saved. Then people will be angry with him* is lost in the French and is from James.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

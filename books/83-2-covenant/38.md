# 2nd Book of the Covenant 38

**1** "I willed the reward for those to whom I had given the hope of it; that is why I went down and —; I proclaimed to them, in the realm of the dead, the rest in heaven where they will come. With My right hand I gave them the baptism of life, pardon and forgiveness of all evil, as I have done for you, and as I will do from now on for those who believe in Me. As for one who believes in Me but does not keep My commandment, faith in My name will not help him. In vain he chooses a way of life whose end is destruction, torment and great anguish, for he has sinned against My commandment.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "I proclaimed to them, in the realm of the dead"** — Peter's letter: *He also went and preached to the spirits in prison* (1 Peter 3:19); *the good news was preached even to the dead* (1 Peter 4:6). The Ethiopic has *the baptism of life*; James's English, from the Coptic, has *the water (?) of life*, and names the place *the place of Lazarus*. A word is lost after *went down and*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

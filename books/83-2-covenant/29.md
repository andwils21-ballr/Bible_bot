# 2nd Book of the Covenant 29

**1** When He had been crucified, had died and had risen, when the work He accomplished in His body while He was crucified was completed, when His ascension had taken place, that is, at the end of a few days, this is what He said to us: "The miracles, the salvation that is worked through Me —

**2** As I go to the Father in heaven, I give you a new commandment: that you love one another, that you be subject to one another, that peace may always be among you. Love your enemies; and what you do not want done to you, do not do to another either; and what you want done to you, do to others."

---

## Notes

- **v1 "when His ascension had taken place"** — the verse speaks of the ascension as already past, though the Lord is still speaking; James judges the passage an addition in the Ethiopic, where the Coptic has *ye shall see me, how I go up unto my Father*. The sentence then breaks off at *through Me*.
- **v2 "a new commandment: that you love one another… what you want done to you, do to others"** — the Lord's words at the supper, *A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another* (John 13:34), and on the mountain, *whatever you want people to do to you, do the same to them* (Matthew 7:12); the Ethiopic gives the rule both ways.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

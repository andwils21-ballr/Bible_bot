# 2nd Book of the Covenant 34

**1** We said to Him again, "Lord, we must indeed ask You, for You have commanded us to preach, to proclaim and to teach with care, once we have learned from You, to be good messengers, and to teach people so that they may believe in You. That is why we ask You."

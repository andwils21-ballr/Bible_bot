# 2nd Book of the Covenant 41

**1** He answered and said to us, "Go and preach to the twelve tribes of Israel, to the nations, to the provinces of Israel, to the East and the West, the North and the South, and many will believe in Me, the Son of the Lord."

**2** But we said to Him, "Who will believe us? Who will listen to us? How shall we be able to do, to teach and to set out the wonders, the miracles and the signs that You Yourself have done?"

**3** He answered and said to us, "Go, preach and teach what concerns the coming and the mercy of My Father; for as My Father has worked in Me, so I work in you, being always the power in you Myself, so that people may believe. To them too this power will be given; it will be their inheritance, so that they in turn may give it to the nations."

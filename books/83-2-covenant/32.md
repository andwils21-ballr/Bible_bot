# 2nd Book of the Covenant 32

**1** "Truly I tell you: just as the Father raised Me Myself from the dead, so you too will rise in the body, and the Father will take you up to the height of the heavens, to the kingdom that, as I told you long ago, the One who sent Me has prepared. That is why I have accomplished all mercy: for though I was not begotten by a man, I was born; and though I have no body, I put on a body and grew up, to save you who are born according to the flesh, and so that you may truly rise in your flesh, in a second birth, clothed in a garment that does not decay, with all who hope and believe in the One who sent Me. For this is what My Father wishes: that to you, and to those I wish, I give the promise of the kingdom."

**2** Then we said to Him, "It is a great thing that You give us to hope for, and tell us."

**3** He answered and said to us, "Believe that all that I tell you will happen."

**4** We answered and said to Him, "Yes, Lord."

**5** He said to us, "Truly I tell you that I have received all power from My Father, so that I may lead those who are in darkness into the light, those who are in corruption into the truth, those who are in death into life, and so that those who are in prison may be set free.

**6** This is to show that what is impossible for man is possible for the Father. It is I who am the hope of those who despair, the helper of those who have no helper, the wealth of the needy, the physician of the sick, the resurrection of the dead."

---

## Notes

- **v1 "you too will rise in the body… in a second birth, clothed in a garment that does not decay"** — Paul's word on the resurrection, *What is sown is perishable; what is raised is imperishable* (1 Corinthians 15:42). Here the flesh itself rises, and the book says it again in chapters 33–36.
- **v6 "what is impossible for man is possible for the Father"** — the Lord's answer about the rich, *What is impossible with men is possible with God* (Luke 18:27). *And tell us* (v2) is lost in the French and is from James.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

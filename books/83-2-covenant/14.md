# 2nd Book of the Covenant 14

**1** We know this: that our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ is the Lord, the Son of the Lord, who was sent by the Lord Almighty, the author and creator of all things, whose name is spoken by all beings; who is above all the powers, Lord of lords, King of kings, Mighty One of the mighty, the heavenly One who is above the cherubim and seraphim, who sits at the right hand of the Father's throne; by whose word the Lord commanded the heavens, built the earth and what is in it, and set bounds to the sea so that it does not pass its limits; who founded the deeps and the springs that gush out and flow over the earth, the day and the night, the sun and the moon, the stars in the heaven; who separated the light and the darkness; who commands Gehenna; who in the blink of an eye commands the winter rain, the snow and the frost, the hail, and the days each in its season; who makes the earth tremble and makes it firm again; who made man in His image and likeness; who spoke with the patriarchs of old and the prophets in parables and in truth; whom the apostles preached and the disciples touched.

**2** Him, the Lord, the Son of the Lord, we believe to be the Word who became flesh, carried in the womb of the holy Virgin Mary, conceived by the Holy Spirit; that it was not by the desire of the flesh but by the will of the Lord that He was begotten; that He was wrapped in swaddling clothes in Bethlehem and made manifest, was brought up and grew, while we saw these things.

---

## Notes

- **v2 "the Word who became flesh… not by the desire of the flesh but by the will of the Lord"** — John's opening: *born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of a man, but of God… The Word became flesh* (John 1:13–14). John says it of those who receive Him; the apostles here say it of His own birth. James's English has *God the Son of God* for the French *the Lord, the Son of the Lord*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

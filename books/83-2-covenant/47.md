# 2nd Book of the Covenant 47

**1** We said to Him again, "Lord, will the nations not be able to say, 'Where is their God?'"

**2** He answered and said to us, "The chosen will be made manifest in this, that, struck by such afflictions, they come through."

**3** We said to Him, "Will their departure from this world be the ruin of those who persecuted them?"

**4** He said to us, "No; but when they were tormented with so great a pain, it was a test for them, whether faith was in them, whether they remembered My words and kept My commandments. Few will be the days of their waiting, so that the One who sent Me may be glorified, and I with Him, for He sent Me to you. I tell you this, and you, for your part, tell it to Israel and the nations, so that they too may listen and be saved, believe in Me, and come out of the danger of destruction. Whoever comes out of the danger of death, they will take him and keep him in prison amid torments, as if he were a thief."

**5** We said to Him, "Lord, will the faithful be equal with those who have not believed? Will You condemn those who have escaped the pestilence?"

**6** He said to us, "Though they have believed in My name, what the sinners have done is like what those who do not believe have done."

**7** We said to Him again, "Lord, are there some among those who have escaped the ruin who will not have eternal life?"

**8** He answered and said to us, "Whoever has glorified My Father will dwell in My Father's dwelling."

## Notes

- **vv2, 4, 5** — *struck by such afflictions, they come through*, *remembered My words and kept My commandments* and *pestilence* are lost in the French and are from James's English. In v3 the French and James differ: James has *Will then their departure out of the world be by a pestilence which giveth them pain?*
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 2nd Book of the Covenant 40

**1** "As for those who transgress My commandments and teach another doctrine, who have taken away from or added to My teaching for their own glory, who have swayed and turned aside those who believed in Me rightly, they will be punished forever."

**2** We said to Him, "Lord, will there be other teachings, different from what You have told us?"

**3** He said to us, "It must be so, that the wicked may be made manifest as the good are, and those who have done good. Then the just judgment will come; people will be judged by their works, and as they have acted, sinners will be handed over to destruction."

**4** We said to Him, "Blessed are we who see You and hear what You set out for us! How great are the works of power our eyes have seen, which You Yourself have done!"

**5** He answered and said to us, "Yes, but much more blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed, for they will be called children of the kingdom; they will be called perfect by the Perfect, and I will become eternal life for them in the kingdom of My Father."

**6** We said to Him again, "Lord, how is it possible for us to believe that You will leave us, as You Yourself say, and that the time and the hour will come when You must go to Your Father?"

## Notes

- **v3 "It must be so, that the wicked may be made manifest"** — Paul on the divisions at Corinth: *There must indeed be factions among you, so that those who are approved may be recognized among you* (1 Corinthians 11:19).
- **v5 "much more blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed"** — the Lord's word to Thomas: *Blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed* (John 20:29).
- **vv1–5** — the French is lost in four places in the text held here; *transgress My commandments and teach another*, *different from what You have told us*, *It must be so*, and *have not seen and yet have believed, for they will be called children of the* are from James's English.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

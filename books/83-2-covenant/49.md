# 2nd Book of the Covenant 49

**1** "But those who have wished to see the face of the Lord, who have not shown partiality to rich sinners, who have not stood in awe of the men who wished to lead them astray but have rebuked them, these will be crowned with the Father; as those too who have corrected their neighbor will be saved, for they are sons of Wisdom and of Faith.

**2** But whoever has not become a son of Wisdom, and has hated and rejected his brother and not turned to him, him the Lord will repudiate and reject. But those who have walked in Righteousness, in Knowledge and in Faith in Me, who have had Knowledge, Wisdom and Self-control for the sake of Righteousness while they were rejected, who held fast to poverty and kept themselves under control: their reward will be great. For while they were mocked, afflicted and driven out, while they were naked, while people were arrogant toward them, while they hungered and thirsted, because they bore all this with patience, they will be blessed in heaven and will remain with Me forever. But woe to those who have hated them and rejected them: their end will be their destruction.

---

## Notes

- **v2 "their reward will be great… they will be blessed in heaven"** — the Lord's blessing on the persecuted: *Blessed are you when people insult you and persecute you… for your reward is great in heaven* (Matthew 5:11–12).
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 2nd Book of the Covenant 36

**1** We said to Him again, "Lord, by asking You question after question we are becoming foolish before You."

**2** But He said to us, "I know that you question Me with faith and with all your heart, and I rejoice over you because you question Me so; your lack of shame gladdens Me, and for you too it will become salvation." When He had said this to us, we became glad, for He had spoken with us gently, and we said to Him again, "Our Lord, in all things You give us life and have mercy on us. Will You now tell us what we shall ask You?"

**3** Then He said to us, "What is it that decays: is it the body or the spirit?" We said to Him, "It is the body."

**4** He said to us, "But what has decayed will rise, and what has been sick will live again, so that in this My Father may be glorified. As He has done for Me, so I too will do for you and for all who believe in Me."

---

## Notes

- **vv2–3** — the French is lost at three places in the text held here; *and I rejoice over you because you question Me so*, *in all things You give us life… what we shall ask You?* and *the spirit?… It is the body* are from James's English.
- **v1 "we are becoming foolish before You"** — so the French as the text held here reads it, *nous délirons*; James's English has *already are we ashamed that we question thee oft-times and burden thee*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 2nd Book of the Covenant 37

**1** "Truly I tell you, the body will rise, alive, with the soul, so that people may confess their sins and be judged with justice, with the works they have done, whether good or bad, so that the commandment of My Father who sent Me may be fulfilled. Then the just judgment will take place, for so My Father wills. For He has told Me: 'My Son, on the day of judgment do not put the rich to shame, do not spare the poor, but hand each one over to eternal torment according to his sins.' But those I love, who have kept the commandments of My Father who sent Me, I will give rest in eternal life in the kingdom of My heavenly Father. They will see what great power My Father has granted and given Me, so that I may do what I will."

---

## Notes

- **v1 "do not put the rich to shame, do not spare the poor"** — the Law's rule for the judge: *You shall not show favor to the poor or defer to the great* (Leviticus 19:15). James's English has *thou shalt have no respect for the rich, neither pity for the poor*. *But those I love, who have kept the commandments of My Father who sent Me* is lost in the French and is from James.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

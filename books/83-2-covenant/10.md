# 2nd Book of the Covenant 10

**1** "That is why I tell you: remain in the fear of the Lord and in righteousness, for you brought nothing into this world, and you must go out of this world naked. So gain righteousness alone, and the love of the Lord.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "you brought nothing into this world"** — Paul's words to Timothy, *for we brought nothing into the world, and we cannot take anything out of it* (1 Timothy 6:7).
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain. English translation by this project.

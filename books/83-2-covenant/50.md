# 2nd Book of the Covenant 50

**1** We said to Him, "Will this happen to all?"

**2** He said to us, "How would the same judgment for sinners and for the righteous be just?"

**3** We said to Him, "Will they not say to You on that day, 'You made us see righteousness and sin; You set apart the darkness and the light, the evil and the good'?"

**4** He said to us, "To Adam was given the power to choose of these what he wished: he chose the light, stretched out his hands and took hold of it, but he left the darkness and went away from it. So every man has the power to believe in the light, that is, the life of the Father who sent Me. Whoever has believed in Me will live, if he has done works of light; but if he has not believed in the light, and has remained in the darkness, he will have nothing to answer in his defense; he will not be able to bear the face and the gaze of the Son, who is I Myself. I will say to him: Why, then, did you go away from My kingdom? You believed in Me, then you denied Me. Has not every man the power to live and to die, and to believe? Whoever has done and kept My commandment will be a son of light, that is, of My Father; and so it will be for all who have kept and done My commandment.

**5** That is why I came down from heaven. I, the Word, became flesh; I died; I spent My life teaching and rebuking, so that some will be saved and others will perish forever, punished in the fire, in body and in spirit."

## Notes

- **v4 "To Adam was given the power to choose… he chose the light"** — Moses' words to Israel: *I have set before you life and death… Choose life* (Deuteronomy 30:19). Here the choice is given first to Adam, and he chooses the light; every man after him has the same power. *Has not every man the power to live and to die* is lost in the French and is from James's English; *You have not lived*, which the French has before *Why*, is broken in the text held here and is left out.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

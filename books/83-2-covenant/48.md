# 2nd Book of the Covenant 48

**1** We said to Him, "Lord, teach us what will happen next."

**2** He said to us, "In those years and in those days there will be war upon war; the four corners of the world will shake and be at war with one another; then disturbance in the clouds, darkness, barrenness, and persecution for those who believe in Me and for the chosen. Then dissension, quarrels and evil deeds will be among people. Among them there will be some who believe in My name; the others will follow evil and teach an empty doctrine. These last will have followers, who will submit to their riches, their ungodliness, their drunkenness and their bribes, and flattery will be among them.

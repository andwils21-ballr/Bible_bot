# 2nd Book of the Covenant 26

**1** "As for you, keep the remembrance of My own death, that is, the Pascha. It is then that one of you who stand by Me will be put in prison for My own name. He will be deeply grieved and distressed, for while you keep the Pascha he will be in prison, grieved that he has not kept it with you. But I will send My Power in the likeness of My angel, and the doors of the prison will open, and he will come to you to keep watch and to rest with you. Then, when the cock has crowed and you have finished the remembrance of Me and the love feast, he will be thrown into prison again as a witness, until he comes out to preach as I have commanded you."

**3** We said to Him, "Lord, did You not Yourself drink the Pascha to the end? Must we do it again?"

**4** He said to us, "Yes, you must, until I come again from the Father with My wounds."

---

## Notes

- **v1 "the doors of the prison will open"** — Peter's arrest at the Passover and his release: Herod *put him in prison… intending to bring him out to the people after the Passover*, and *an angel of the Lord stood there… The chains fell from his hands* (Acts 12:4, 7). Here the Lord foretells it, and the angel is His own Power in an angel's likeness. *You have finished the remembrance of Me and the love feast* is lost in the French and is from James.
- **v2** — the paragraph numbered 2 in the text held here is a line of Ge'ez read as Latin letters, not part of the French, so no verse stands there.
- **v4 "until I come again from the Father with My wounds"** — so the Ethiopic, as James notes; his English, from the Coptic, has *with them that have been put to death for my sake*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

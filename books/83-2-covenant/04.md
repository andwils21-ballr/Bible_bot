# 2nd Book of the Covenant 4

**1** Our Lord said to us, "Signs and wonders will happen in heaven and on earth before the end of the world comes."

**2** We said to our Lord, "Explain them to us and show them to us."

**3** He said to us, "To you I will indeed explain and teach these signs. It is not you who will see what is to happen, but those you yourselves have taught, those who will believe in Me. Then My faithful, and those who will not believe as well, will hear the blast of a trumpet coming from heaven to the earth, and they will see stars falling like fire, and great stars appearing in the daytime. A star that appears from the East will look like fire. The sun and the moon will be at war: always the terror and dread of thunder, of lightning and of the crash of thunder, always earthquakes. Cities will fall, and people will die under their ruins. There will be earthquakes continually, because the water has gone: the deeps will be dried up, the rivers will vanish, and the sea will draw back. A great plague will come, and death for many, until there are not enough funerals for those who die, and war comes of children with their parents in the same —. Kin will despise kin, and no one will turn to another. Those who have been abandoned will rise; they will see those who abandoned them, and will not pull them out of their trouble; for ruin itself, suffering and envy, once they have taken hold of a man, will bring him to hatred. People will lament over those who did not listen to the Lord's commandment. Then My heavenly Father will be angry because of the wickedness of men; for through the corruption of their impurity, the faults they have plotted have been many, so that the prophecy may be fulfilled that says: *I have seen you when you ran with the thief, when you sat with the fornicator, when you insulted your mother's child, when your tongue wove wickedness and you caused great offense to your mother's child. What did you suppose? That I would become like you, that I would be the witness of your work?*

**4** This is what I tell you. You, for your part, tell Israel and the nations to listen and believe, so that they may be saved and flee far from the wrath and the flame of fire that are coming. Hear this too, sons of light: as soon as the Lord comes, when He draws near, the angels will withdraw from the fruits, the rivers, the deeps and the trees, and a burning heat will come on the fruits. What remains after the heat, the large locusts and the small locusts will eat; what the small locusts leave, the middle locusts will eat.

**5** Then, moreover, when the Perverse One has drawn near, Satan will speak and sing to the four corners of the world.

## Notes

- **v3 "the prophecy that says: I have seen you when you ran with the thief"** — the psalm of God's judgment: *When you see a thief, you are pleased with him, and your portion is with adulterers… you slander your own mother's son… you thought I was altogether like you* (Psalm 50:18–21).
- **v4 "the large locusts and the small locusts… the middle locusts"** — Guerrier names the three by size, and notes that the Ethiopic words answer to the Greek *akris* and *brouchos* and to the Hebrew locust names of Joel: *What the cutting locust left, the locust has eaten; what the locust left, the young locust has eaten* (Joel 1:4).
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain. English translation by this project.

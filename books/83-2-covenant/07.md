# 2nd Book of the Covenant 7

**1** "Then the Lord will appear to those who have trusted in Him. When He comes, a great sign will appear, so that only My friends will know it, those who have kept the commandments of My Father and have rejected gold and silver, the riches of this world and the goods of this earth. Those I Myself will receive on that day. Their faces will shine seven times brighter than the light of the sun. I Myself will be their God, and they will be My people; I Myself will be their Father, and they will be My sons and daughters. I will be the altar for them, and they will be the temple for Me. I will make known to them the will of My Father and the judgment of this world; I will place them with My angels, and they will rejoice for thousands of years, more than their own selves; for they suffered in the middle of the world that deceives, and hated every state of rest.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "I Myself will be their God, and they will be My people… My sons and daughters"** — the promise Paul strings together for the Corinthians: *I will be their God, and they will be My people… I will be a father to you, and you will be My sons and daughters* (2 Corinthians 6:16, 18). Here the Son says it, and adds *I will be the altar for them, and they will be the temple for Me*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain. English translation by this project.

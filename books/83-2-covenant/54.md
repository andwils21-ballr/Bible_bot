# 2nd Book of the Covenant 54

**1** "Be like the wise virgins who lit their lamps and did not slumber, but went out with their lamps to meet the Lord, the Bridegroom, and went in with Him into the bridal chamber. Do not be like the foolish virgins who, it is said, could not keep watch but slept."

**2** We said to Him, "Lord, which are wise, and which are foolish?"

**3** He said to us, "The wise are the five whom the prophet calls daughters of God. Hear their names." We were sad and grieved, and we wept over those who were shut out.

**4** He said to us, "The five wise are Faith, Love, Joy, Peace and Hope. Those who believe in Me possess them; they serve as guides to those who believe in Me, and believe also in the Lord who sent Me. I Myself am the Bridegroom. They went out to meet Me, and they went in with Me into the wedding house; they reclined at table with the Bridegroom and rejoiced. But the five foolish slept. When they woke, they came to the wedding house and knocked at the door, for they had been shut out, and they wept because they were shut out."

**5** We said to Him, "Lord, will their wise sisters, who are in the house, not open to them, and will they not be grieved for them?" He said to us, "They will plead with the Bridegroom, but will not be able to obtain anything for them."

**6** We said to Him, "Lord, when then will their sisters enter?"

**7** He said to us, "Those who have been shut out have been shut out."

**8** We said to Him, "Lord, is this word final? Who, then, are the foolish?"

**9** He said to us, "Listen: Understanding, Knowledge, Obedience, Self-control and Mercy: these are the virtues that have slept in those who

**10** believe and trust in Me, but have not done My commandment."

## Notes

- **vv1–10 "the wise virgins… the foolish virgins"** — the Lord's parable: *the bridegroom came; those who were ready went in with him to the wedding feast, and the door was shut* (Matthew 25:10). Here the ten are named as virtues, and the wise begin as Paul's fruit of the Spirit, *love, joy, peace* (Galatians 5:22). The Ethiopic has *Joy* and *Self-control* where James's English, from the Coptic, has *Grace* and *Patience*.
- **vv2–3, 5** — *foolish?*, *of God. Hear their names*, and *He said to us* are lost in the French and are from James.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

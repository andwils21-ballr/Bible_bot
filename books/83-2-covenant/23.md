# 2nd Book of the Covenant 23

**1** We touched Him, to learn truly whether He had risen in the flesh; and we fell on our faces, asked forgiveness, and begged for grace because we had not believed in Him.

**2** Then our Lord and Savior said to us, "Rise, and I will reveal to you what will happen on the earth and what will happen above the heavens, your resurrection, and what concerns the kingdom of heaven. My Father has sent Me to take you up to heaven, and those who believe in Me."

---

## Notes

- **v1** — the opening of the verse is lost in the French; *We touched Him… and we fell on our faces* is from James's English.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

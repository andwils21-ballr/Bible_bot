# 2nd Book of the Covenant 15

**1** This is what our Lord Jesus Christ did. He was sent to school by Joseph and Mary His mother, where He learned His letters. The one who taught Him said to Him as he taught Him, "Say *alpha*." He answered and said, "First explain to Me yourself what *beta* is." This thing that was done is true and real.

---

## Notes

- **v1 "First explain to Me yourself what beta is"** — James notes that the saying makes sense in its fuller form, *Tell me first what alpha is, and then I will tell you what beta is*, the story told of the boy Jesus and His teacher in the infancy gospels.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

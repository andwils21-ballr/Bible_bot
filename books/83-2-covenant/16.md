# 2nd Book of the Covenant 16

**1** After this there was a wedding at Cana of Galilee. He was invited with His mother and His brothers, and He changed the water into wine. He raised the dead; He made the paralyzed walk; He made the man whose hand was withered stretch it out. A woman who had suffered a flow of blood for twelve years touched the fringe of His garment and was healed at once. While we were looking on and marveling at the glorious deed He had done, He said to us, "Who touched Me?" We said to Him, "Lord, the crowd of people has touched You." He answered and said to us, "I felt that power has gone out from Me." At once that woman came before Him, answered and said to Him, "Lord, it was I who touched You." He answered and said to her, "Go, your faith has made you well." After this He made the deaf hear and the blind see, drove the demons out of those possessed, and cleansed the lepers. The demon Legion, who was in a man, recognized Jesus, cried out and said to Him, "Before the time of our destruction has come, You have come to drive us out." The Lord Jesus rebuked him and said to him, "Come out of this man without doing him any harm." The demon went into the pigs and plunged them into the sea, where they were choked.

**2** After this He walked on the sea, and the winds blew, and He rebuked them, and the waves of the sea grew calm. When we, His disciples, had no denarius, we said to Him, "What shall we do about the tax collector?" He answered and said to us, "Let one of you cast the hook into the deep of the sea and draw out a fish; he will find the denarius in it, and let him give it to the tax collector for Me and for you." After this, when we had no bread but five loaves and two fish, He commanded the people to sit down, and their number was found to be five thousand, not counting the children and the women. We handed out pieces of bread to them; they were filled, and we set aside twelve baskets full of pieces, asking one another, "What, then, are these five loaves?" They are the symbol of our faith in the great Christianity: faith in the Father Almighty, in Jesus Christ our Savior, in the Holy Spirit the Paraclete, in the holy Church, and in the forgiveness of sins.

## Notes

- **v2 "What, then, are these five loaves?"** — the apostles read the five loaves as the five articles of the faith: the Father, the Son, the Spirit, the Church, and the forgiveness of sins. The miracles before it follow the Gospels, with the woman's healing in nearly Luke's words: *Who touched Me?… I know that power has gone out from Me… your faith has made you well* (Luke 8:45–48).
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

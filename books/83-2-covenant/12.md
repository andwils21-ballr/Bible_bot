# 2nd Book of the Covenant 12

## The Letter of the Apostles

**1** The Book of the Covenant that Jesus Christ revealed to His disciples. In it is told how Jesus Christ revealed this book; it is the book of the company of the apostles and of the disciples of Jesus Christ; it is the book meant for all. It was written about Simon and Cerinthus, the false apostles, so that no one may keep company with them, for there is cunning in them, with which they will kill people. We urge you to be firm, not to waver, not to be troubled, and not to turn aside from the word of the Gospel that you have heard. As we heard it, we have written it for the whole world. We have entrusted our sons and our daughters to you in joy, in the name of the Lord, the Father, Master of the world, and in the name of Jesus Christ. May His mercy be great upon you!

## Notes

- **The chapter** — here the book's second part begins, the apostles' letter to the churches known elsewhere as the *Epistle of the Apostles*. From here M. R. James's English of the same work is read beside the French; James follows the Coptic where it survives.
- **v1 "The Book of the Covenant"** — Guerrier gives the Ethiopic word in parentheses, *Kidan*, the word that names this book in the canon (see the note on the heading of chapter 1).
- **v1 "As we heard it, we have written it"** — the French is broken here (*telle que nous… pour le monde entier*); James's English has the whole sentence: *Like as we heard it, we keep it in remembrance and have written it for the whole world.*
- **v1 "with which they will kill people"** — so the Ethiopic as Guerrier gives it; James has *wherewith they bring men to destruction*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

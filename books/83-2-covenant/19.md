# 2nd Book of the Covenant 19

**1** So, then, we have not been neglectful of the true witness to our Lord Jesus Christ. We have borne witness to what our Lord did, since we had Him with us and were found near Him: how He set right our understanding, explained His teaching to us, and acted for those who are to be His witnesses.

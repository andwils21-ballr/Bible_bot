# 2nd Book of the Covenant 22

**1** Then the Lord said to Mary and her sisters, "Let us go to them." He came and found us inside, and called us out; but we thought it was a ghost, and we did not believe it was He. Yet it was He. He said to us, "Come, do not be afraid; it is I, your Master, whom you, Peter, denied three times before the cock crowed. Will you deny Me again now?" We went to Him, still weighing and doubting whether it was really He. But He said to us, "Why do you doubt? What will you say? Do you not recognize that it is I, I who set out for you what concerned My body, My death and My resurrection? So that you may know it is I: Peter, put your hand into the holes in My hands; you, Thomas, put your hand into My side; you, Andrew, see whether My feet tread the earth and leave footprints. For it is written in the prophet that ghosts, that is, demons, leave no footprints on the earth."

---

## Notes

- **v1 "Peter, put your hand into the holes in My hands; you, Thomas, put your hand into My side"** — in John it is Thomas alone: *Put your finger here, and see My hands; and put out your hand and put it into My side* (John 20:27); in Luke, *Touch Me and see; for a spirit does not have flesh and bones* (Luke 24:39). Here the test is given to three apostles. Between *Let us go to them* and *ghost*, and at *on the earth*, the French is lost; the words are from James's English.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

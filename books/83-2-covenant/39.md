# 2nd Book of the Covenant 39

**1** "As for you, I have given you the power to become children of light in the Lord, to be purified of all evil, and to be delivered from every power of condemnation; and for those who through you have believed in Me, I will do the same. As I told you, I promise you that whoever believes will come out of prison and be set free from chains, from the sword and from the fearful fire." We said to Him, "Lord, You have given joy and rest to them, because of their faith and their trust, and to all who believe in You."

**2** He said to us, "Truly I tell you, you yourselves, all who believe, and those also who are to believe in the One who sent Me, I will take you all up into the heavens, to the place the Father has prepared for the chosen, for the chosen of the chosen, He who gives them the rest He promised them, and eternal life."

---

## Notes

- **v1 "We said to Him, 'Lord, You have"** — the start of the apostles' answer is lost in the French and is from James's English, which has *We answered and said: Lord, thou hast given unto us the rest of life*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

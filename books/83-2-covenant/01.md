# 2nd Book of the Covenant 1

## The Testament of Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ

**1** As for me, I have seen, from the outside to the inside, the appearance and the reality, twice unheard of and marvelous: justice given to each according to his works.

---

## Notes

- **The whole book** — worked from the French of L. Guerrier with S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ* (Patrologia Orientalis 9, 1913), which is translated from the Ethiopic. No Ge'ez or other source language was used. The chapters are Guerrier's numbered sections; each verse is one of his paragraphs. From section 12 on, M. R. James's English of the same text (1924) is read beside the French; James follows the Coptic where it survives, so where the two differ the French, which is the Ethiopic, is followed.
- **The heading** — the manuscript's own title. Guerrier renders its first word *Testament*; the book's place in the canon gives the same word as *Covenant*, and the Ethiopian canon counts this book as the second Book of the Covenant.
- **v1 "As for me, I have seen"** — the speaker is not named, and the sentence does not say who is speaking or what was seen beyond *the appearance and the reality*. Guerrier notes on the page that this prologue has only a very obscure sense and does not join naturally with what follows. The rendering stays as close to his French as English allows: *du dehors au dedans, l'apparence et la réalité doublement inouïes et merveilleuses*.
- **v1 "justice given to each according to his works"** — the words Paul and Matthew use of the judgment: God *will repay each according to his works* (Romans 2:6, *hekastō kata ta erga autou*), and the Son of Man *will repay each according to what he has done* (Matthew 16:27), taken from Psalm 62:12, *You repay each man according to his work*. The words *given to each* are Guerrier's own, in parentheses, supplying what the sentence leaves out.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain. English translation by this project.

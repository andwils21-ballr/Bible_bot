# 2nd Book of the Covenant 21

**1** While they were grieving and weeping, the Lord appeared to them and said, "Do not weep; it is I, the one you are looking for. But let one of you go to your brothers and tell them: Come, our Master has risen from the dead." Mary came and told us.

**2** But we said to her, "What is there between us and you, woman? Can one

**3** who has died and been buried live again?" We did not believe

**4** her word, that our Savior had risen from the dead. Then she went

**5** back to our Lord and said to Him, "None of them has believed me

**6** about Your resurrection."

## Notes

- **vv1–7** — in the text held here each of Guerrier's printed lines stands as a paragraph, and the verses keep that division. The end of v1, *Mary came and told us*, is lost in the French and is given from James, who notes that the Ethiopic names Mary where the Coptic has Martha. The chapter breaks off at *she* in v7.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 2nd Book of the Covenant 24

**1** This is what our Lord revealed to us, as He told it to us: "When I came from the Father Almighty, as I passed through the heavens, I was clothed with the Wisdom of the Father and with His Strength; I was clothed with His own Power. I was like the heavenly beings, the angels and the archangels, as I passed through the heavens in their likeness, like one of them. I passed through the Offices, the Powers and the Dominions, since I possessed the measure of the Wisdom of the One who sent Me. The archangels Michael, Gabriel, Uriel and Raphael followed Me as far as the fifth firmament, because I looked like one of them; for such power had been given Me by the Father. Then I made the archangels afraid by My word, telling them to go to the Father's altar and serve the Father in their own way until I returned to Him. ## Notes

- **v1 "I made the archangels afraid by My word"** — so the Ethiopic, as James notes; his English, from the Coptic, has *I did adorn the archangels with a wonderful voice*. The Son passes through the heavens in the likeness of the angels, and they do not know Him. The archangels' names, lost in the French, are from James. The chapter breaks off at *I*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

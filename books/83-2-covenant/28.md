# 2nd Book of the Covenant 28

**1** We said to Him, "Lord, in how many years will this happen?"

**2** He said to us, "When the hundred and fiftieth year has passed, in the days of Pentecost and of Pascha, the coming of My Father will take place."

**3** But we said to Him, "Lord, You have just told us, 'I Myself will come,' and then You go on to tell us, 'He who sent Me will come.'"

**4** He said to us, "I am wholly in the Father, and the Father is in Me."

**5** Then we said to Him, "Will You really leave us until Your coming?"

**6** He answered and said to us, "Do you not understand? Just as I have been here until now, so I have also been there, with the One who sent Me."

**7** We said to Him, "Lord, is it possible that You are both here and there at once?"

**8** He said to us, "I am wholly in the Father, and the Father is in Me; for I am His image, from His likeness, His power, His completeness and His light; I am His perfect Word."

## Notes

- **v2 "When the hundred and fiftieth year has passed"** — so the Ethiopic. James's English, from the Coptic, has *When the hundredth part and the twentieth part is fulfilled*, that is, a hundred and twenty years; the Latin he cites has only a broken word before *year*. The witnesses do not agree on the number.
- **vv4, 8 "I am wholly in the Father, and the Father is in Me"** — the Lord's words in John: *Do you not believe that I am in the Father and the Father is in Me?* (John 14:10). Here they answer the apostles' question how *I will come* and *He who sent Me will come* can both be true. The ends of vv1 and 5 are lost in the French and are from James.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

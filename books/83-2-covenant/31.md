# 2nd Book of the Covenant 31

**1** After He had told us this, we said to Him, "Lord, in all things You have had mercy on us and saved us, and have revealed all things to us; but we would still ask You something, if You allow us."

**2** He answered and said to us, "I know that you pay attention to My word, and that you take pains to hear about the thing you wish to ask Me. So ask Me yourselves, and remember My words and listen to them; and it is pleasing to Me to talk —

---

## Notes

- **vv1–2** — the apostles' request in v1 is lost in the French except *in all things*, and is from James's English. v2 breaks off at *to talk*; James's English goes on, *now concerning that which ye desire, I will speak good words unto you*.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

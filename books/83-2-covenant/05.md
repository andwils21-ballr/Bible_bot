# 2nd Book of the Covenant 5

**1** "Then Syria will be taken captive and will lament over her sons. Cilicia will lift up her neck, until the one who will judge her appears. Rise, you who sit on a throne, you who rest in glory, daughter of Babylon: drink what has been drawn for you. Then, Cappadocia, Phrygia, Lycaonia, bow your backs in your own land, for many peoples will trample you underfoot. Then the camps will open and the armies will go out, and they will surround the whole earth; then they will destroy the temples of Armenia. Pontus and Bithynia, your young men will fall by the edge of the sword, your sons and your daughters will be captives, and the walls of Pontus will weep. The rivers of Lycaonia will be mixed with blood. You, proud Pisidia, who trust in riches, you will vanish into the earth and be trampled underfoot because of your works. That is why I will raise up kings against you for destruction, and they will shed your blood; and your soul will be with the souls of those who go down into Gehenna. Pamphylia and all the coast, gird your loins with bronze and iron, and hide the strangers; prepare ships for flight: the sea will become a tomb for those who remain in you. As for Phoenicia, the sword will enter her, because she has borne sons of destruction. Judea, clothe yourself in groaning, raise your walls, open your gates, widen your enclosure, adorn yourself with the ornaments of youth, make yourself beautiful for your beloved, prepare your throne for your king, prepare yourself for the day of corruption: your affliction will come.

## Notes

- **v1 "Rise, you who sit on a throne… daughter of Babylon"** — Isaiah's word to the same city turned the other way: *Come down and sit in the dust, virgin daughter of Babylon; sit on the ground without a throne* (Isaiah 47:1). Here she is bidden to rise from her throne and drink.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain. English translation by this project.

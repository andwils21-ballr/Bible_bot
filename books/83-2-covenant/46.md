# 2nd Book of the Covenant 46

**1** "Then My Father will be angry because of the wickedness of men; for many are their faults, and the abomination of their defilement will weigh heavily on them, for their life is in corruption."

**2** We said to Him, "What will happen to those who have hoped in You?"

**3** He answered and said to us, "How long will you still be slow of heart? Truly I tell you that what the prophet David said about you —

**5** and about My own, so My Father wills for you and for those who will believe in Me.

**6** But for those who in this world have been cunning, enemies of justice, the prophecy of David will be fulfilled against them, which says: *Their feet are swift to shed blood, and their tongue weaves deceit; the venom of the serpent is under their lips. I see you when you run with the thief and cast your lot with the debauched, and when you go on insulting your brother and set a stumbling block before your mother's son. What did you think, that I would become like you?* See now: what the prophet of the Lord said about all these things has been fulfilled, and all that was foretold."

## Notes

- **v6 "Their feet are swift to shed blood… the venom of the serpent is under their lips"** — the psalms Paul strings together in Romans: *the venom of asps is under their lips… Their feet are swift to shed blood* (Romans 3:13, 15). The words that follow are Psalm 50:18–21, quoted at 4:3.
- **vv3–5** — v3 breaks off in the text held here, and the paragraph numbered 4 is a line of Ge'ez read as Latin letters, so no verse stands there; v5 takes up the sentence again.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

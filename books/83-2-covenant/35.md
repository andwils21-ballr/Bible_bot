# 2nd Book of the Covenant 35

**1** He answered and said to us, "Truly I tell you, the body of every man will rise, with its living soul and its spirit."

**2** We said to Him, "Lord, is it possible for what has decayed and been scattered to live again? It is not because we deny the resurrection that we have asked You, but truly we believe that what You say will come to pass."

**3** But He said to us in anger, "You of little faith, how long will you not believe, and question, and search? You want to understand without effort. Keep My commandment and do what I tell you, without scorning, without insulting anyone, without showing partiality; carry out your ministry in the narrow way, straight and constricted. In this the Father will rejoice in you completely —

---

## Notes

- **v3 "carry out your ministry in the narrow way"** — the Lord's words on the road to life: *How narrow is the gate and hard the road that leads to life, and few are those who find it!* (Matthew 7:14). *That what You say will come to pass* (v2) is lost in the French and is from James.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

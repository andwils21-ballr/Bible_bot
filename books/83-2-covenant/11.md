# 2nd Book of the Covenant 11

**1** "For when the world is at its end and the last days draw near, the wicked will rise up; they will persecute those who fear the Lord, and the righteous. They will punish them as they please, for their understanding will be blind. The sinners will quarrel with the righteous, persecute them, hate them, and speak every evil word against them. That is why the Lord will throw them into the fire that is not put out and to the worm that does not sleep. Then every creature will come to the burning of the fire, for the sun and the moon will no longer give their light, the waters will fail, the rivers will dry up, the heaven will be rolled up, and the earth will be struck. The Lord will rise to judge His people, to repay each of them according to his works and according to the words they have spoken, on the way of righteousness. The righteous, who have walked in the way of righteousness, will inherit the glory of the Lord and His power; they will be given what the eye has not seen and the ear has not heard, and they will rejoice in My kingdom."

---

## Notes

- **v1 "the worm that does not sleep… what the eye has not seen and the ear has not heard"** — the end of both roads in the Lord's words and Paul's: *where their worm does not die, and the fire is not quenched* (Mark 9:48); *What no eye has seen and no ear has heard… what God has prepared for those who love Him* (1 Corinthians 2:9). With this the prophecy spoken in Galilee ends.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 2nd Book of the Covenant 27

**1** We said to Him, "Lord, great is what You set out and reveal to us; but with what power and in what form will You come?"

**2** He said to us, "Truly I tell you, I will come like the sun when it rises, and I will be seven times brighter than it, shining in glory, carried on the wings of the clouds in glory, while My cross goes before Me. I will come to the earth to judge the living and the dead."

---

## Notes

- **v2 "carried on the wings of the clouds… while My cross goes before Me"** — the Lord's words on His coming: *Then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in heaven… and they will see the Son of Man coming on the clouds of heaven with power and great glory* (Matthew 24:30). Here the sign is named: the cross goes before Him.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 2nd Book of the Covenant 2

## Predictions of the End of the World

**1** The account our Lord Jesus gave His eleven disciples in Galilee, after He had risen from the dead, saying: "Listen, sons of light, pay attention to the word of your Father. I will reveal to you what will happen in the world, so that those who believe in Me may fear when they learn it. As for the sinners who will not believe in Me, they will not know My own signs, but My friends will know them. The sinners will be driven to the flame of torment and handed over to the fire that will punish their understanding. For Gehenna has opened its mouth and wished its belly to be full. Its roots will be torn out, its fruits gathered: they are the destruction of the sons of men, for their ungodliness has led them to destruction. Their fault is hidden in the days of their life, but on the last day it will be uncovered, for their ungodly word will be heard and will itself rebuke them. The world will be heavy for them because their days are few, and I Myself will not give them help.

## Notes

- **v1 "Gehenna has opened its mouth"** — Isaiah's picture of the grave: *Sheol has opened wide its throat, and opened its mouth beyond measure* (Isaiah 5:14).
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain. English translation by this project.

# 2nd Book of the Covenant 17

**1** This is what our Lord and Savior revealed and showed us. We too do the same for you, so that you may become our companions, by the grace of the Lord, in our ministry and in our glory, as you give your minds to eternal life. Be firm, without wavering, in the knowledge and understanding of our Lord Jesus Christ. May He be merciful to you and save you, forever, to the ages of ages, without end.

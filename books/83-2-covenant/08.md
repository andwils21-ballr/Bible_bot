# 2nd Book of the Covenant 8

**1** "Many are those who reject and abandon the commandments of the Lord and persecute the righteous, the righteous who are tormented by those who love this world. They will be handed over to the torment of burning flames of fire, which will consume the desires of their hearts. Many too are those who will take My will to demand sacrifices and slaughterings of animals, and will shed blood in My own name, as though offering it as an oblation for their sins; acting so, they will treat Me as an idol. Yet through the prophets I commanded their fathers not to eat the flesh of a carcass, and not to drink the blood of a carcass. For they, because they are fleshly, Me —

# 2nd Book of the Covenant 43

**1** We asked Him, "Lord, is there one and the same promise of the inheritance for us and for them?"

**2** He answered and said to us, "Are the fingers of the hand all equal? Are the ears of grain in a field all equal? Do the trees that bear fruit all bear the same fruit? Does each not bear fruit according to its nature?" We said to Him, "Lord, will You speak to us in parables again?"

**3** He said to us, "Do not be sad. Truly I tell you, you will be My brothers and My companions in the kingdom of heaven, with My Father, for He has willed it so. Truly I tell you, to those you have taught and who have believed in Me, I will give the hope."

---

## Notes

- **vv1–5** — the apostles' question in v1 and their *will You speak to us in parables again?* in v2 are lost in the French, apart from *the promise of the inheritance* and *parables*, and are from James's English. The paragraphs numbered 4 and 5 in the text held here are lines of Ge'ez read as Latin letters, so no verses stand there.
- **Source text** — L. Guerrier and S. Grébaut, *Le Testament en Galilée de Notre-Seigneur Jésus-Christ*, Patrologia Orientalis 9.3 (1913), public domain; read beside M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), public domain. English translation by this project.

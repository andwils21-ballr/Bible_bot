# Esther 3

## Mordecai Will Not Bow

**1** After these things King Ahasuerus promoted Haman son of Hammedatha the Agagite. He raised him up and set his seat above all the officials who were with him.

**2** All the king's servants who were in the king's gate bowed down and paid homage to Haman, for so the king had commanded concerning him. But Mordecai would not bow down or pay homage.

**3** Then the king's servants who were in the king's gate said to Mordecai, "Why are you transgressing the king's command?"

**4** When they had spoken to him day after day and he would not listen to them, they told Haman, to see whether Mordecai's words would stand, for he had told them that he was a Jew.

**5** When Haman saw that Mordecai did not bow down or pay homage to him, Haman was filled with wrath.

**6** But he scorned laying a hand on Mordecai alone, for they had told him who Mordecai's people were. So Haman sought to destroy all the Jews, the people of Mordecai, throughout the whole kingdom of Ahasuerus.

## The Lot Is Cast

**7** In the first month, the month of Nisan, in the twelfth year of King Ahasuerus, they cast pur, that is, the lot, before Haman, from day to day and from month to month, to the twelfth month, the month of Adar.

**8** Then Haman said to King Ahasuerus, "There is a certain people scattered and kept apart among the peoples in all the provinces of your kingdom. Their laws are different from those of every other people, and they do not keep the king's laws, so it is not worth the king's while to let them be.

**9** If it pleases the king, let it be written that they be destroyed, and I will weigh out ten thousand talents of silver into the hands of those who do the work, to bring into the king's treasuries."

**10** So the king took his signet ring from his hand and gave it to Haman son of Hammedatha the Agagite, the enemy of the Jews.

**11** The king said to Haman, "The silver is given to you, and the people also, to do with them as seems good to you."

## The Letters Go Out

**12** Then the king's scribes were summoned on the thirteenth day of the first month, and it was written just as Haman commanded, to the king's satraps, to the governors over each province and to the officials of each people, to each province in its own script and to each people in its own language. It was written in the name of King Ahasuerus and sealed with the king's signet ring.

**13** Letters were sent by couriers to all the king's provinces, to destroy, to kill and to annihilate all the Jews, young and old, little children and women, in one day, the thirteenth of the twelfth month, the month of Adar, and to plunder their goods.

**14** A copy of the writing was to be given out as law in every province, published to all the peoples, so that they would be ready for that day.

**15** The couriers went out in haste at the king's word, and the law was given out in Susa the citadel. The king and Haman sat down to drink, but the city of Susa was thrown into confusion.

## Notes

- **v1 "Haman son of Hammedatha the Agagite"** — Agag was the king of Amalek whom Saul spared against the word of the LORD, and whom Samuel put to death (1 Samuel 15:8–9, 32–33). Mordecai is of the line of Kish, Saul's father (2:5). After Amalek's attack in the wilderness Moses said, *The LORD will have war with Amalek from generation to generation* (Exodus 17:16). The Greek has *Bougaios* for *the Agagite*.
- **vv2, 5 "bowed down and paid homage… would not bow down or pay homage"** — *kor'im u-mishtachavim*. The Hebrew does not say why Mordecai refused. It gives only what he told the servants: *that he was a Jew* (v4).
- **v4 "When they had spoken to him"** — the read form, *ke-omram*. What is written is *be-omram*, a difference of one letter with the same sense.
- **v7 "they cast pur, that is, the lot"** — *pur*, a word from outside Hebrew, explained by the Hebrew *goral*. The feast of Purim is named from it (9:24–26).
- **v8 "scattered and kept apart"** — *mefuzzar u-meforad*: spread through every province, and separate from the peoples among whom they live.
- **v9 "ten thousand talents of silver"** — Haman offers to pay for the killing himself. In v11 the king gives the silver back to him with the people.
- **v10 "his signet ring… the enemy of the Jews"** — *tsorer ha-Yehudim*, Haman's title again at 8:1, 9:10 and 9:24. The ring is taken back from Haman and given to Mordecai (8:2).
- **v13 "to destroy, to kill and to annihilate"** — *le-hashmid la-harog u-le'abbed*. Esther says the same three words to the king (7:4), and the king's second letter gives the Jews the right to do them to those who attack them (8:11). Here the Greek gives the text of the king's letter, which the English numbering places at 13:1–7.
- **v15 "the city of Susa was thrown into confusion"** — *navokhah*. When Mordecai goes out in royal robes, *the city of Susa shouted and rejoiced* (8:15).

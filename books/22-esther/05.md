# Esther 5

## Esther before the King

**1** And it came to pass on the third day that Esther put on her royal robes and stood in the inner court of the king's house, opposite the king's house. The king was sitting on his royal throne in the royal house, facing the entrance of the house.

**2** When the king saw Queen Esther standing in the court, she won favor in his eyes, and the king held out to Esther the golden scepter that was in his hand. Esther came near and touched the tip of the scepter.

**3** The king said to her, "What is it, Queen Esther? What is your request? Even to half of the kingdom, it shall be given you."

**4** Esther said, "If it pleases the king, let the king and Haman come today to the feast that I have prepared for him."

**5** Then the king said, "Bring Haman quickly, so that we may do as Esther has said." So the king and Haman came to the feast that Esther had prepared.

**6** As they drank wine at the feast, the king said to Esther, "What is your wish? It shall be granted you. What is your request? Even to half of the kingdom, it shall be done."

**7** Esther answered, "My wish and my request:

**8** if I have found favor in the eyes of the king, and if it pleases the king to grant my wish and to do my request, let the king and Haman come to the feast that I will prepare for them, and tomorrow I will do as the king has said."

## Haman's Pride

**9** Haman went out that day glad and merry of heart. But when Haman saw Mordecai in the king's gate, and that he neither rose nor trembled before him, Haman was filled with wrath against Mordecai.

**10** Nevertheless Haman restrained himself and went home. He sent and brought his friends and Zeresh his wife,

**11** and Haman told them of the glory of his riches, the number of his sons, and all the ways the king had promoted him and how he had raised him above the officials and the king's servants.

**12** Haman said, "What is more, Queen Esther let no one but me come with the king to the feast she prepared. Tomorrow also I am invited by her, together with the king.

**13** Yet all this is worth nothing to me, so long as I see Mordecai the Jew sitting at the king's gate."

**14** Then Zeresh his wife and all his friends said to him, "Let a pole be made, fifty cubits high, and in the morning tell the king to have Mordecai hanged on it. Then go with the king to the feast, glad." The advice pleased Haman, and he had the pole made.

## Notes

- **v1 "on the third day"** — of the fast, *three days, night or day* (4:16). In the Greek, vv1–2 are a longer telling of Esther's coming before the king, which the English numbering places at 15:1–16.
- **v1 "put on her royal robes"** — *va-tilbash Ester malkhut*, **put on royalty**. The word comes twice more in the verse: his royal throne, the royal house.
- **vv3, 6 "Even to half of the kingdom"** — *ad chatsi ha-malkhut*, the king's promise three times (5:3, 6; 7:2). Herod makes the same promise to the daughter of Herodias: *whatever you ask me, I will give you, up to half of my kingdom* (Mark 6:23).
- **v9 "he neither rose nor trembled before him"** — *lo qam ve-lo za'*. In 3:2 he would not bow to Haman; now he does not even rise.
- **v10 "Haman restrained himself"** — *va-yit'appaq*, Joseph's word before his brothers: *he restrained himself and said, "Serve the bread"* (Genesis 43:31), and *Joseph could not restrain himself* (45:1).
- **v11 "the number of his sons"** — ten, named when they die (9:7–10).
- **v14 "a pole… fifty cubits high"** — *etz gavoah chamishim ammah*, the *etz* of 2:23. The Greek also has *wood of fifty cubits*.

# Matthew 8

## A Man with Blight

**1** When He came down from the mountain, great crowds followed Him.

**2** And here — a man with blight came and knelt before Him, saying, "Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean."

**3** He stretched out His hand and touched him, saying, "I am willing; be made clean." At once his blight was made clean.

**4** Jesus said to him, "See that you tell no one; but go, show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift that Moses commanded, as a testimony to them."

## The Centurion's Servant

**5** When He had entered Capernaum, a centurion came to Him, pleading with Him

**6** and saying, "Lord, my servant is lying at home paralyzed, suffering terribly."

**7** Jesus said to him, "I will come and heal him."

**8** The centurion answered, "Lord, I am not worthy to have You come under my roof; but only say the word, and my servant will be healed.

**9** For I too am a man under authority, with soldiers under me. I say to this one, 'Go,' and he goes, and to another, 'Come,' and he comes, and to my slave, 'Do this,' and he does it."

**10** When Jesus heard this, He was amazed and said to those who were following, "Truly I tell you, with no one in Israel have I found such faith.

**11** I tell you, many will come from east and west and recline at table with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven,

**12** but the sons of the kingdom will be thrown into the outer darkness. There will be weeping and grinding of teeth."

**13** Jesus said to the centurion, "Go; let it be done for you as you have believed." The servant was healed in that very hour.

## Peter's Mother-in-Law, and Many Others

**14** When Jesus came into Peter's house, He saw Peter's mother-in-law lying in bed with a fever.

**15** He touched her hand, and the fever left her; and she got up and began to serve Him.

**16** When evening came, they brought to Him many who were possessed by demons. He drove out the spirits with a word and healed all who were ill,

**17** so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, saying, "He took our weaknesses and carried our diseases."

## Following Him

**18** When Jesus saw a crowd around Him, He gave orders to go over to the other side.

**19** A scribe came and said to Him, "Teacher, I will follow You wherever You go."

**20** Jesus said to him, "Foxes have dens and the birds of the sky have nests, but the Son of Man has nowhere to lay His head."

**21** Another of the disciples said to Him, "Lord, let me first go and bury my father."

**22** Jesus said to him, "Follow Me, and leave the dead to bury their own dead."

## The Storm

**23** When He got into the boat, His disciples followed Him.

**24** And here — a great storm arose on the sea, so that the boat was being covered by the waves; but He was asleep.

**25** They came and woke Him, saying, "Lord, save us! We are perishing!"

**26** He said to them, "Why are you afraid, you of little faith?" Then He got up and rebuked the winds and the sea, and there was a great calm.

**27** The men were amazed and said, "What kind of man is this, that even the winds and the sea obey Him?"

## The Two Possessed Men

**28** When He came to the other side, to the country of the Gadarenes, two men possessed by demons met Him, coming out of the tombs. They were so violent that no one could pass by that road.

**29** And here — they cried out, "What have You to do with us, Son of God? Have You come here to torment us before the time?"

**30** Some way off from them a herd of many pigs was feeding.

**31** The demons begged Him, "If You drive us out, send us into the herd of pigs."

**32** He said to them, "Go." They came out and went into the pigs; and here — the whole herd rushed down the steep bank into the sea and died in the water.

**33** The herdsmen fled, went into the town, and told everything, including what had happened to the possessed men.

**34** And here — the whole town came out to meet Jesus; and when they saw Him, they begged Him to leave their region.

## Notes

- **v2 "a man with blight"** — *lepros*, from *lepra*, the Greek of the Old Testament for *tsara'at*, the affliction of Leviticus 13–14. This project renders *tsara'at* **blight** throughout, and the Greek word for it is rendered the same way. *Make me clean* is the language of the Law: the man asks to be declared clean, not only healed.
- **v3 "He stretched out His hand and touched him"** — the man with blight was to live alone, outside the camp, calling out *Unclean! Unclean!* (Leviticus 13:45–46), and touching *human uncleanness* made a person unclean (Leviticus 5:3). Here the touch goes the other way: the man is made clean.
- **v4 "show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift that Moses commanded"** — the instruction of Leviticus 14 for *the person with the blight on the day of his cleansing* (14:2), when the priest goes outside the camp to look and then takes two live clean birds for the one being cleansed (14:3–4). Jesus sends him to do what the Law requires.
- **v5 "a centurion"** — *hekatontarchos*, a commander of a hundred soldiers. He is not of Israel: Jesus says he has found such faith *with no one in Israel* (v10).
- **v6 "my servant"** — *pais*, which can mean a child or a servant; in v9 the centurion calls another *my slave*, *doulos*, a different word.
- **v11 "recline at table with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob"** — the feast of the kingdom, and people *from east and west* at it; *the sons of the kingdom*, those who expected the places, are *thrown into the outer darkness* (v12).
- **v17 "He took our weaknesses and carried our diseases"** — Isaiah 53:4, *akhen cholayenu hu nasa u-makhovenu sevalam*, **surely our sicknesses he carried and our pains he bore**. The Greek of Isaiah reads *he bears our sins*; Matthew follows the Hebrew, *sicknesses*, and applies it to a day of healing.
- **v20 "the Son of Man"** — *ho huios tou anthrōpou*, Jesus' name for Himself, used here for the first time. In Hebrew and Aramaic *son of man* means **a human being**; in Daniel's vision one *like a son of man* comes with the clouds of heaven (Daniel 7:13, *ke-var enash*). The first time Jesus uses the name, it is of a man with no place to lay His head.
- **v21 "let me first go and bury my father"** — when Elijah called Elisha, Elisha asked, *let me kiss my father and my mother, and I will follow you* (1 Kings 19:20), and was allowed. Jesus allows no delay.
- **v24 "He was asleep"** — Jonah too slept in the boat while the sailors cried out (Jonah 1:5–6). Here the sleeper is the one who calms the storm, as the psalm says of the LORD: *He made the storm a calm, and its waves were hushed* (Psalm 107:29). *Storm* is *seismos*, the word for an earthquake, a great shaking.

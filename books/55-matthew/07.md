# Matthew 7

## Judging

**1** "Do not judge, so that you may not be judged.

**2** For with the judgment you pronounce you will be judged, and with the measure you use it will be measured to you.

**3** Why do you see the speck in your brother's eye, but do not notice the beam in your own eye?

**4** Or how can you say to your brother, 'Let me take the speck out of your eye,' and here — the beam is in your own eye?

**5** Hypocrite! First take the beam out of your own eye, and then you will see clearly to take the speck out of your brother's eye.

**6** Do not give what is holy to the dogs, and do not throw your pearls before the pigs, or they will trample them under their feet and turn and tear you to pieces.

## Ask, Seek, Knock

**7** Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you.

**8** For everyone who asks receives, and the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks it will be opened.

**9** Which one of you, if his son asks him for bread, will give him a stone?

**10** Or if he asks for a fish, will give him a snake?

**11** If you, then, evil as you are, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father who is in heaven give good things to those who ask Him!

**12** So in everything, whatever you want people to do to you, do the same to them; for this is the Law and the Prophets.

## The Two Ways

**13** Enter through the narrow gate. For wide is the gate and broad the road that leads to destruction, and many are those who go in through it.

**14** How narrow is the gate and hard the road that leads to life, and few are those who find it!

**15** Beware of the false prophets, who come to you in sheep's clothing but inwardly are ravenous wolves.

**16** You will recognize them by their fruit. Are grapes gathered from thorns, or figs from thistles?

**17** In the same way, every good tree bears good fruit, but the rotten tree bears bad fruit.

**18** A good tree cannot bear bad fruit, and a rotten tree cannot bear good fruit.

**19** Every tree that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.

**20** So then, you will recognize them by their fruit.

**21** Not everyone who says to Me, 'Lord, Lord,' will enter the kingdom of heaven, but the one who does the will of My Father who is in heaven.

**22** Many will say to Me on that day, 'Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in Your name, and in Your name drive out demons, and in Your name do many mighty works?'

**23** Then I will declare to them, 'I never knew you. Depart from Me, you who practice lawlessness.'

## The Two Builders

**24** So everyone who hears these words of Mine and does them will be like a wise man who built his house on the rock.

**25** The rain came down, the rivers came, and the winds blew and beat against that house, and it did not fall, for it had been founded on the rock.

**26** Everyone who hears these words of Mine and does not do them will be like a foolish man who built his house on the sand.

**27** The rain came down, the rivers came, and the winds blew and struck against that house, and it fell, and great was its fall."

**28** And it came to pass, when Jesus had finished these words, that the crowds were astonished at His teaching;

**29** for He was teaching them as one who has authority, and not as their scribes.

## Notes

- **v2 "with the measure you use"** — *metron*, the merchant's measure for grain or oil. The one who judges sets the scale by which he will be weighed.
- **v3 "the speck… the beam"** — *karphos*, a chip of straw or wood, and *dokos*, a roof beam: the joke is in the size. v5 calls the man who sees the speck and not the beam by the word of chapter 6, *hypokritē*, **actor**.
- **v6 "what is holy to the dogs"** — *to hagion*, **the holy thing**, the Law's term for what belongs to God: *No outsider shall eat a holy thing* (Leviticus 22:10). The pig is the Law's unclean animal (Leviticus 11:7); what is holy is not to be handed to those who will only trample it.
- **v12 "for this is the Law and the Prophets"** — the rule sums up the whole of Scripture, as the two great commandments will in 22:40: *on these two commandments hang the whole Law and the Prophets*. The same phrase closes the teaching that began in 5:17 with *the Law and the Prophets*.
- **vv13–14 the two gates and the two roads** — the choice set before Israel in the Law and the Prophets: *I have set before you life and death… choose life* (Deuteronomy 30:19); *Here — I am setting before you the way of life and the way of death* (Jeremiah 21:8).
- **v15 "ravenous wolves"** — *lykoi harpages*, **wolves that snatch**. Ezekiel used the image of the leaders of Jerusalem: *her princes within her are like wolves tearing prey* (Ezekiel 22:27).
- **v17 "the rotten tree"** — *sapron*, **decayed, unfit**; the good and the rotten tree bear fruit according to what they are. It is John's warning in 3:10 with the reason given.
- **v23 "Depart from Me, you who practice lawlessness"** — Psalm 6:8 (6:9 in the Hebrew), *suru mimmenni kol po'alei aven*, **depart from me, all you who do wickedness**, spoken by the psalmist to his enemies. Here the one who speaks it is the judge.
- **vv24–27 rock and sand** — the two builders are the two roads again: both men *hear*, and what divides them is *doing*. The rock, *petra*, is the word of 16:18.
- **v28 "And it came to pass, when Jesus had finished these words"** — *kai egeneto hote etelesen*, the formula that closes each of the five great speeches of the Gospel (11:1; 13:53; 19:1; 26:1). *And it came to pass* is kept here as in the Old Testament: *kai egeneto* is the Greek of *va-yehi*.
- **v29 "as one who has authority"** — *exousia*, the right and power to command. Six times in this teaching Jesus has set His own word beside what was said to those of old: *But I tell you* (5:22–44).

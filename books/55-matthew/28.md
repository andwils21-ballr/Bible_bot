# Matthew 28

## The Empty Tomb

**1** After the sabbath, as the first day of the week was dawning, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to look at the grave.

**2** And here — there was a great earthquake; for an angel of the Lord came down from heaven, came and rolled back the stone, and sat on it.

**3** His appearance was like lightning, and his clothing white as snow.

**4** For fear of him the guards shook and became like dead men.

**5** But the angel said to the women, "Do not be afraid; for I know that you are looking for Jesus, who was crucified.

**6** He is not here; for he has been raised, just as he said. Come, see the place where he lay.

**7** Then go quickly and tell his disciples, 'He has been raised from the dead, and here — he is going ahead of you into Galilee; there you will see him.' Look, I have told you."

**8** They left the tomb quickly, with fear and great joy, and ran to tell his disciples.

**9** And here — Jesus met them and said, "Greetings!" They came up, took hold of his feet and worshiped him.

**10** Then Jesus said to them, "Do not be afraid. Go and tell my brothers to go to Galilee; there they will see me."

## The Guards' Report

**11** While they were going, here — some of the guard went into the city and told the chief priests all that had happened.

**12** When they had gathered with the elders and taken counsel, they gave a large sum of silver to the soldiers,

**13** saying, "Say, 'His disciples came by night and stole him while we were asleep.'

**14** If this comes to the governor's ears, we will persuade him and keep you out of trouble."

**15** So they took the silver and did as they were instructed. This story has been spread among the Jews to this day.

## The Great Commission

**16** The eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had directed them.

**17** When they saw him, they worshiped him; but some doubted.

**18** Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me.

**19** Go, then, and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,

**20** teaching them to keep all that I have commanded you. And here — I am with you all the days, until the end of the age."

## Notes

- **v1 "as the first day of the week was dawning"** — *mian sabbatōn*, **one of the sabbath**, the first day after the sabbath. The women come to *look at the grave*, where they sat when he was buried (27:61).
- **v2 "a great earthquake"** — *seismos*, the second in two chapters: the earth shook when he died (27:51, 54) and shakes again at the tomb. The guards who were set to keep the tomb *shook* (v4, *eseisthēsan*), the same verb.
- **v3 "like lightning… white as snow"** — the heavenly figure of Daniel: *his face like the appearance of lightning* (Daniel 10:6, *u-fanav ke-mar'eh vaqar*), and the Ancient of Days, *His clothing white as snow* (Daniel 7:9, *levushe kitlag chivvar*).
- **v6 "just as he said"** — the three predictions: *on the third day be raised* (16:21; 17:23; 20:19).
- **v7 "he is going ahead of you into Galilee"** — the promise of 26:32, *after I have been raised, I will go ahead of you into Galilee*.
- **v10 "my brothers"** — the disciples who fled (26:56) are called *brothers*, the name of 12:49–50 for those who do the Father's will.
- **v15 "to this day"** — *mechri tēs sēmeron*, the phrase of 27:8, *the Field of Blood to this day*: both stories were still told when Matthew wrote.
- **v17 "but some doubted"** — *edistasan*, the verb of 14:31, when Peter sank: *why did you doubt?* Worship and doubt stand side by side on the mountain.
- **v18 "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me"** — Daniel 7:14, of the one like a son of man: *to him was given dominion and glory and a kingdom, that all peoples, nations and languages should serve him* (*ve-leh yehiv sholtan*). The *authority* of the Son of Man (9:6) now reaches heaven and earth.
- **vv19–20 "all the nations… I am with you all the days"** — the mission of 10:5–6, *do not go onto the road of the Gentiles… go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel*, is widened to *all the nations*. The book that began with *Emmanuel*, *God with us* (1:23), ends with *I am with you*.

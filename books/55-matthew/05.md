# Matthew 5

## The Blessed

**1** Seeing the crowds, he went up on the mountain. When he had sat down, his disciples came to him,

**2** and he opened his mouth and taught them, saying:

**3** "Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

**4** Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted.

**5** Blessed are the meek, for they will inherit the earth.

**6** Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they will be filled.

**7** Blessed are the merciful, for they will be shown mercy.

**8** Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God.

**9** Blessed are the peacemakers, for they will be called sons of God.

**10** Blessed are those who have been persecuted because of righteousness, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

**11** Blessed are you when people insult you and persecute you and say every kind of evil against you falsely because of me.

**12** Rejoice and be glad, for your reward is great in heaven; for in the same way they persecuted the prophets who were before you.

## Salt and Light

**13** You are the salt of the earth. But if the salt loses its taste, how will it be made salty again? It is no longer good for anything, except to be thrown out and trampled underfoot by people.

**14** You are the light of the world. A city set on a mountain cannot be hidden.

**15** Nor do people light a lamp and put it under a basket, but on the lampstand, and it gives light to everyone in the house.

**16** In the same way, let your light shine before people, so that they may see your good works and glorify your Father who is in heaven.

## The Law and the Prophets

**17** Do not think that I came to abolish the Law or the Prophets. I did not come to abolish them but to fulfill them.

**18** For truly I tell you, until heaven and earth pass away, not one iota, not one stroke of a letter, will pass away from the Law until everything has come about.

**19** So whoever breaks one of the least of these commandments and teaches others to do the same will be called least in the kingdom of heaven; but whoever does them and teaches them will be called great in the kingdom of heaven.

**20** For I tell you that unless your righteousness goes far beyond that of the scribes and Pharisees, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven.

## Anger

**21** You have heard that it was said to those of old, 'You shall not murder, and whoever murders will be liable to judgment.'

**22** But I tell you that everyone who is angry with his brother will be liable to judgment. Whoever says to his brother, 'Raka,' will be liable to the Council; and whoever says, 'You fool,' will be liable to the fire of Gehenna.

**23** So if you are offering your gift at the altar and there remember that your brother has something against you,

**24** leave your gift there before the altar and go. First be reconciled to your brother, and then come and offer your gift.

**25** Come to terms quickly with your opponent while you are still with him on the way, or your opponent may hand you over to the judge, and the judge to the officer, and you will be thrown into prison.

**26** Truly I tell you, you will never get out of there until you have paid the last penny.

## Adultery and Divorce

**27** You have heard that it was said, 'You shall not commit adultery.'

**28** But I tell you that everyone who looks at a woman to desire her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.

**29** If your right eye causes you to stumble, tear it out and throw it away from you; it is better for you to lose one of your members than for your whole body to be thrown into Gehenna.

**30** If your right hand causes you to stumble, cut it off and throw it away from you; it is better for you to lose one of your members than for your whole body to go into Gehenna.

**31** It was also said, 'Whoever divorces his wife, let him give her a certificate of divorce.'

**32** But I tell you that everyone who divorces his wife, except on the ground of sexual immorality, makes her an adulteress; and whoever marries a divorced woman commits adultery.

## Oaths

**33** Again, you have heard that it was said to those of old, 'You shall not swear falsely, but you shall carry out your oaths to the Lord.'

**34** But I tell you, do not swear at all: not by heaven, for it is the throne of God;

**35** not by the earth, for it is the footstool of His feet; not by Jerusalem, for it is the city of the great King.

**36** Nor shall you swear by your head, for you cannot make one hair white or black.

**37** Let your word be 'Yes, yes' or 'No, no'; anything more than this comes from the evil one.

## Retaliation and Love of Enemies

**38** You have heard that it was said, 'Eye for eye, and tooth for tooth.'

**39** But I tell you, do not resist the evildoer. If anyone strikes you on the right cheek, turn to him the other also.

**40** If anyone wants to sue you and take your tunic, let him have your cloak as well.

**41** If anyone forces you to go one mile, go with him two.

**42** Give to the one who asks you, and do not turn away from the one who wants to borrow from you.

**43** You have heard that it was said, 'You shall love your neighbor and hate your enemy.'

**44** But I tell you, love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you,

**45** so that you may become sons of your Father who is in heaven; for He makes His sun rise on the evil and the good, and sends rain on the righteous and the unrighteous.

**46** For if you love those who love you, what reward do you have? Do not even the tax collectors do the same?

**47** If you greet only your brothers, what more are you doing than others? Do not even the Gentiles do the same?

**48** You, then, shall be complete, as your heavenly Father is complete."

## Notes

- **v1 "he went up on the mountain… sat down"** — the teacher sits to teach. The Law was given from a mountain, and this teaching, the longest in the Gospel, is given from one; it runs to the end of chapter 7.
- **v3 "Blessed"** — *makarioi*, **happy, fortunate**: a statement about someone's state, not a wish or a blessing spoken over them, the word the Greek Psalter uses for *ashrei* in its first line: *makarios anēr*, **happy is the man** (Psalm 1:1). Nine times it opens the line (vv3–11).
- **v5 "the meek… will inherit the earth"** — Psalm 37:11, *va-anavim yirshu arets*, **the humble will possess the land**. *Gē* and *erets* both mean both *land* and *earth*; the psalm's promise of the land becomes the whole earth.
- **v8 "the pure in heart"** — Psalm 24:4 names who may climb the LORD's mountain: *clean of hands and pure of heart* (*bar levav*). Here the pure *will see God*.
- **v17 "not to abolish but to fulfill"** — *plērōsai*, the verb of every *to fulfill what was spoken* in chapters 1–4. Jesus fulfills the Law and the Prophets as he fulfills a prophecy: by bringing about what they point to. vv21–48 then show how.
- **v18 "not one iota, not one stroke"** — *iōta*, the smallest letter of the Greek alphabet, standing for the Hebrew *yod*, the smallest of the Hebrew letters; *keraia*, **a little horn**, the tiny stroke that tells one Hebrew letter from another.
- **v22 "Raka… You fool"** — *Raka* is Aramaic, **empty one**, left untranslated in the Greek; *mōre* is Greek, **fool**, from which *moron* comes. *The Council*, *to synedrion*, is the Sanhedrin. *Gehenna* is the Valley of Hinnom below Jerusalem, the Gospel's name for the place of final fire.
- **vv21, 27, 31, 33, 38, 43 "You have heard that it was said"** — six sayings, each followed by *But I tell you*. The first two are the commandments, *You shall not murder* and *You shall not commit adultery* (Exodus 20:13–14). The divorce certificate is Deuteronomy 24:1, *sefer keritut*. The oath recalls Leviticus 19:12, *You shall not swear falsely by My name*, and Numbers 30:2, *he shall not break his word*. *Eye for eye* is Exodus 21:24. The last, *love your neighbor*, is Leviticus 19:18; but *hate your enemy* is written nowhere in the Law. The saying quotes what was heard, not only what was written.
- **v35 "the footstool of His feet… the city of the great King"** — Isaiah 66:1, *the heavens are My throne and the earth is the footstool of My feet* (*hadom raglai*), and Psalm 48:2, Jerusalem *the city of the great King*.
- **v48 "complete"** — *teleioi*, from *telos*, an end reached: **whole, finished, mature**, rather than *flawless*. It is the word the Greek of Deuteronomy uses for *You shall be whole* (*tamim*, Greek *teleios*) *with the LORD your God* (18:13), and it closes the teaching on love for enemies: the Father's sun and rain make no distinction, and His children are to love the same way.

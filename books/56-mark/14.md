# Mark 14

## The Anointing at Bethany

**1** It was two days before the Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread. The chief priests and the scribes were looking for a way to arrest Him by deceit and kill Him;

**2** for they said, "Not during the feast, or there may be an uproar among the people."

**3** While He was at Bethany in the house of Simon the leper, reclining at table, a woman came with an alabaster jar of perfume, pure nard, very costly. She broke the jar and poured it on His head.

**4** Some there were indignant and said to one another, "Why was the perfume wasted like this?

**5** This perfume could have been sold for more than three hundred denarii and given to the poor." They scolded her.

**6** But Jesus said, "Leave her alone. Why do you trouble her? She has done a beautiful thing to Me.

**7** For you always have the poor with you, and whenever you want you can do good to them; but you do not always have Me.

**8** She did what she could; she has anointed My body beforehand for burial.

**9** Truly I tell you, wherever the good news is preached in the whole world, what she has done will also be told in memory of her."

**10** Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve, went to the chief priests to hand Him over to them.

**11** When they heard it, they were glad and promised to give him money. He began looking for an opportunity to hand Him over.

## The Passover Meal

**12** On the first day of Unleavened Bread, when they sacrificed the Passover lamb, His disciples said to Him, "Where do You want us to go and prepare for You to eat the Passover?"

**13** He sent two of His disciples and said to them, "Go into the city, and a man carrying a jar of water will meet you. Follow him,

**14** and wherever he goes in, say to the master of the house, 'The Teacher says, Where is My guest room, where I may eat the Passover with My disciples?'

**15** He will show you a large upper room, furnished and ready; prepare for us there."

**16** The disciples went out and came to the city and found it as He had told them, and they prepared the Passover.

**17** When evening came, He came with the twelve.

**18** As they were reclining at table and eating, Jesus said, "Truly I tell you, one of you will hand Me over, one who is eating with Me."

**19** They began to be grieved and to say to Him one by one, "Surely not I?"

**20** He said to them, "It is one of the twelve, one who is dipping bread into the bowl with Me.

**21** The Son of Man goes as it is written of Him, but woe to that man by whom the Son of Man is handed over! It would have been better for that man if he had not been born."

**22** As they were eating, He took bread, and after saying the blessing He broke it, gave it to them and said, "Take; this is My body."

**23** He took a cup, and after giving thanks He gave it to them, and they all drank from it.

**24** He said to them, "This is My blood of the covenant, which is poured out for many.

**25** Truly I tell you, I will never again drink of the fruit of the vine until that day when I drink it new in the kingdom of God."

**26** When they had sung a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.

**27** Jesus said to them, "You will all fall away; for it is written, 'I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered.'

**28** But after I have been raised, I will go ahead of you into Galilee."

**29** Peter said to Him, "Even if all fall away, I will not."

**30** Jesus said to him, "Truly I tell you, today, this very night, before a rooster crows twice, you will deny Me three times."

**31** But he kept saying vehemently, "Even if I must die with You, I will not deny You." All of them said the same.

## Gethsemane

**32** They came to a place called Gethsemane, and He said to His disciples, "Sit here while I pray."

**33** He took with Him Peter and James and John, and He began to be greatly distressed and troubled.

**34** He said to them, "My soul is deeply grieved, to the point of death. Stay here and keep awake."

**35** Going a little farther, He fell on the ground and prayed that, if it were possible, the hour might pass from Him.

**36** He said, "Abba, Father, all things are possible for You. Take this cup away from Me; yet not what I will, but what You will."

**37** He came and found them sleeping, and He said to Peter, "Simon, are you asleep? Could you not keep awake one hour?

**38** Keep awake and pray, so that you do not enter into temptation. The spirit is willing, but the flesh is weak."

**39** Again He went away and prayed, saying the same words.

**40** Again He came and found them sleeping, for their eyes were very heavy; and they did not know what to answer Him.

**41** He came a third time and said to them, "Are you still sleeping and resting? It is enough; the hour has come. The Son of Man is handed over into the hands of sinners.

**42** Get up, let us go. The one who hands Me over has come near."

## The Arrest

**43** At once, while He was still speaking, Judas, one of the twelve, arrived, and with him a crowd with swords and clubs, from the chief priests and the scribes and the elders.

**44** The one handing Him over had given them a signal, saying, "The one I kiss is the man; arrest Him and lead Him away under guard."

**45** When he came, he went up to Him at once and said, "Rabbi!" and kissed Him.

**46** They laid hands on Him and arrested Him.

**47** But one of those standing by drew his sword, struck the high priest's slave and cut off his ear.

**48** Jesus said to them, "Have you come out with swords and clubs to seize Me as if I were a robber?

**49** Day after day I was with you in the temple teaching, and you did not arrest Me. But this is so that the Scriptures may be fulfilled."

**50** They all left Him and fled.

**51** A young man was following Him, wearing nothing but a linen cloth over his naked body; they seized him,

**52** but he left the linen cloth and fled naked.

## Before the Council

**53** They led Jesus away to the high priest, and all the chief priests and the elders and the scribes gathered.

**54** Peter followed Him at a distance, right into the courtyard of the high priest, and he was sitting with the guards and warming himself at the fire.

**55** The chief priests and the whole council were looking for testimony against Jesus so that they might put Him to death, but they found none.

**56** For many gave false testimony against Him, but their testimony did not agree.

**57** Some stood up and gave false testimony against Him, saying,

**58** "We heard Him say, 'I will destroy this sanctuary made with hands, and in three days I will build another not made with hands.'"

**59** Even so their testimony did not agree.

**60** The high priest stood up in the middle and asked Jesus, "Have You no answer? What is it that these men testify against You?"

**61** But He was silent and made no answer. Again the high priest asked Him, "Are You the Messiah, the Son of the Blessed One?"

**62** Jesus said, "I am; and you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of the Power and coming with the clouds of heaven."

**63** The high priest tore his clothes and said, "What further need do we have of witnesses?

**64** You have heard the blasphemy. What do you think?" They all condemned Him as deserving death.

**65** Some began to spit on Him, to cover His face and strike Him with their fists, and to say to Him, "Prophesy!" The guards took Him and slapped Him.

## Peter's Denial

**66** While Peter was below in the courtyard, one of the high priest's servant girls came,

**67** and seeing Peter warming himself, she looked at him and said, "You also were with the Nazarene, Jesus."

**68** But he denied it, saying, "I do not know or understand what you are talking about." He went out into the forecourt, and a rooster crowed.

**69** The servant girl saw him and began again to say to those standing by, "This man is one of them."

**70** But again he denied it. After a little while those standing by said to Peter again, "Surely you are one of them, for you are a Galilean, and your speech is like theirs."

**71** But he began to call down curses and to swear, "I do not know this man you are talking about."

**72** At once a rooster crowed a second time. Peter remembered the word Jesus had said to him: "Before a rooster crows twice, you will deny Me three times." He broke down and wept.

## Notes

- **vv3–9 "an alabaster jar of perfume, pure nard… poured it on His head… anointed My body beforehand for burial"** — *nardou pistikēs*, **pure nard**; *pistikēs* is obscure, perhaps **genuine**. A king's head was anointed, as Samuel *took the flask of oil and poured it on his head* (1 Samuel 10:1); Jesus says it is for His burial. Three hundred denarii is near a year's wages (Matthew 20:2). *The good news is preached in the whole world*, as 13:10.
- **vv12–16 "when they sacrificed the Passover lamb… a man carrying a jar of water… a large upper room"** — *to pascha ethyon*, **they sacrificed the Passover**, the lamb of Exodus 12. *Kataluma*, **guest room**.
- **vv17–21 "one who is eating with Me… dipping bread into the bowl with Me"** — the psalm, *Even the man of my peace, in whom I trusted, who ate my bread, has lifted up his heel against me* (Psalm 41:9). *Mēti egō*, **Surely not I?**, a question that expects *No*.
- **vv22–25 "this is My body… This is My blood of the covenant, which is poured out for many"** — *to haima mou tēs diathēkēs*, **My blood of the covenant**, the words of Moses at Sinai, *Here is the blood of the covenant* (Exodus 24:8; the note on Matthew 26:28). *Hyper pollōn*, **for many**, as in 10:45. Mark has no *for the forgiveness of sins* (Matthew 26:28).
- **vv26–31 "I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered… before a rooster crows twice"** — Zechariah 13:7, *Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered*, here in the first person, *I will strike*. Only Mark has *twice* (vv30, 72), and the rooster crows at v68 and v72.
- **vv32–42 "Abba, Father… Take this cup away from Me… the spirit is willing, but the flesh is weak"** — *Abba* is Aramaic, **Father**, and Mark gives it with its meaning. *Ekthambeisthai kai adēmonein*, **greatly distressed and troubled**. *Perilypos… heōs thanatou*, **deeply grieved, to the point of death**, as the psalm, *Why are you bowed down, my soul?* (Psalm 42:5). The cup of 10:38. *Apechei*, **It is enough**, is obscure, perhaps **it is settled**.
- **vv43–52 "swords and clubs… The one I kiss is the man… a young man… fled naked"** — *katephilēsen*, **kissed**, a strong form of the word in v44. Only Mark has the young man, who leaves his *sindōn*, **linen cloth**, the word for the cloth Jesus will be buried in (15:46). All flee, as 14:27 said.
- **vv53–65 "this sanctuary made with hands… Are You the Messiah, the Son of the Blessed One?… I am"** — *ho eulogētos*, **the Blessed One**, a way of naming God without saying the word. *Egō eimi*, **I am**, Jesus' plain answer in Mark, where Matthew has *You have said it* (Matthew 26:64). The answer joins Psalm 110:1, *Sit at My right hand* (12:36), and Daniel 7:13, *with the clouds of heaven*. *Prophēteuson*, **Prophesy!**, mocked while His word to Peter comes true below.
- **vv66–72 "below in the courtyard… a Galilean, and your speech is like theirs… He broke down and wept"** — Mark sets Peter's denial at the same time as the trial (v54): Jesus confesses, Peter denies. *Hē lalia sou homoiazei*, **your speech is like theirs**: the edition reads these words and marks them as a place where manuscripts differ. *Anathematizein*, **to call down curses**. *Epibalōn eklaien*, **He broke down and wept**: *epibalōn* is obscure, word for word **having thrown on**.

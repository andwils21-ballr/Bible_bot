# Mark 5

## A Man Called Legion

**1** They came to the other side of the sea, to the country of the Gerasenes.

**2** When He had stepped out of the boat, at once a man with an unclean spirit came from the tombs to meet Him.

**3** He lived among the tombs, and no one could bind him any longer, not even with a chain;

**4** for he had often been bound with shackles and chains, but he had torn the chains apart and broken the shackles in pieces, and no one was strong enough to subdue him.

**5** Night and day, among the tombs and on the mountains, he was always crying out and cutting himself with stones.

**6** When he saw Jesus from a distance, he ran and bowed down before Him,

**7** and crying out with a loud voice he said, "What have You to do with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I put You under oath by God: do not torment me!"

**8** For He had been saying to him, "Come out of the man, you unclean spirit!"

**9** He asked him, "What is your name?" He said to Him, "My name is Legion, for we are many."

**10** He begged Him earnestly not to send them out of the country.

**11** A great herd of pigs was feeding there on the mountainside,

**12** and they begged Him, "Send us into the pigs, so that we may go into them."

**13** He gave them leave. The unclean spirits came out and went into the pigs, and the herd, about two thousand, rushed down the steep bank into the sea and were drowned in the sea.

**14** Those who tended them fled and told it in the town and in the countryside, and people came to see what had happened.

**15** They came to Jesus and saw the man who had been possessed by demons, the one who had had the legion, sitting there clothed and in his right mind; and they were afraid.

**16** Those who had seen it told them what had happened to the man possessed by demons, and about the pigs.

**17** They began to beg Him to leave their region.

**18** As He was getting into the boat, the man who had been possessed by demons begged Him that he might be with Him.

**19** He did not let him, but said to him, "Go home to your own people, and tell them how much the Lord has done for you, and how He has had mercy on you."

**20** He went away and began to proclaim in the Decapolis how much Jesus had done for him; and everyone was amazed.

## A Woman Who Touched His Cloak

**21** When Jesus had crossed again in the boat to the other side, a great crowd gathered around Him; and He was beside the sea.

**22** One of the synagogue leaders, named Jairus, came, and when he saw Him he fell at His feet

**23** and begged Him earnestly, saying, "My little daughter is at the point of death. Come and lay Your hands on her, so that she may be made well and live."

**24** He went with him. A great crowd followed Him and pressed around Him.

**25** There was a woman who had had a flow of blood for twelve years,

**26** who had suffered much under many doctors and had spent all that she had, and was no better but rather grew worse.

**27** She had heard about Jesus, and she came up behind Him in the crowd and touched His cloak;

**28** for she said, "If I touch even His clothes, I will be made well."

**29** At once the source of her bleeding dried up, and she felt in her body that she was healed of her affliction.

**30** At once Jesus, knowing in Himself that power had gone out from Him, turned around in the crowd and said, "Who touched My clothes?"

**31** His disciples said to Him, "You see the crowd pressing around You, and You say, 'Who touched Me?'"

**32** He looked around to see who had done it.

**33** The woman, afraid and trembling, knowing what had happened to her, came and fell down before Him and told Him the whole truth.

**34** He said to her, "Daughter, your faith has made you well. Go in peace, and be healed of your affliction."

## Talitha Koum

**35** While He was still speaking, people came from the synagogue leader's house and said, "Your daughter has died. Why trouble the Teacher any further?"

**36** But Jesus, overhearing what was said, said to the synagogue leader, "Do not be afraid; only believe."

**37** He let no one follow Him except Peter, James, and John the brother of James.

**38** They came to the synagogue leader's house, and He saw an uproar, people weeping and wailing loudly.

**39** He went in and said to them, "Why are you making an uproar and weeping? The child is not dead but asleep."

**40** They laughed at Him. But He put them all out, took the child's father and mother and those who were with Him, and went in where the child was.

**41** Taking the child by the hand, He said to her, "Talitha koum," which means, "Little girl, I say to you, get up."

**42** At once the girl got up and began to walk, for she was twelve years old. At once they were overcome with amazement.

**43** He strictly ordered them that no one should know this, and told them to give her something to eat.

## Notes

- **vv1–5 "the country of the Gerasenes… He lived among the tombs… cutting himself with stones"** — *Gerasēnōn*, **of the Gerasenes**; Matthew has *Gadarenes* (Matthew 8:28). The other side of the sea is Gentile country, where pigs are kept. Tombs made a person unclean, *whoever touches… a grave* (Numbers 19:16), and Isaiah names among the rebellious those *who sit among the graves… who eat the flesh of pigs* (Isaiah 65:4). Matthew has two men; Mark tells of one.
- **vv6–7 "bowed down before Him… Son of the Most High God… I put You under oath by God"** — *prosekynēsen*, **bowed down**, the word for worship. *Tou hypsistou*, **the Most High**, the name of God among the nations, as Melchizedek was *a priest to God Most High* (Genesis 14:18). *Horkizō se*, **I put You under oath**, the words the high priest uses to Jesus in Matthew 26:63.
- **vv9–13 "My name is Legion, for we are many… about two thousand"** — *legiōn*, **Legion**, a soldiers' word, as in *twelve legions of angels* (Matthew 26:53). *Epetrepsen*, **gave them leave**.
- **vv15–20 "sitting there clothed and in his right mind… how much the Lord has done for you… how much Jesus had done for him"** — the man once out of his mind is *sōphronounta*, **in his right mind**, while His own family thought Jesus *out of His mind* (3:21). Jesus sends him to tell what *the Lord* has done, and he tells what *Jesus* has done (vv19–20). Unlike the leper (1:44), he is told to speak: this is Gentile country. *The Decapolis*, **the Ten Towns**.
- **vv21–24 "One of the synagogue leaders, named Jairus… My little daughter is at the point of death"** — *archisynagōgos*, **synagogue leader**; Matthew calls him *a leader* (Matthew 9:18). In Matthew the daughter *has just died*; in Mark she is dying, and the news of her death comes in v35.
- **vv25–34 "a flow of blood for twelve years… If I touch even His clothes… Daughter, your faith has made you well"** — the law made such a woman unclean *all the days of her unclean flow* (Leviticus 15:25), and what she touched unclean; her touch makes her clean. *Sesōken se*, **has made you well**, word for word **has saved you**, the same verb as v28. *Mastix*, **affliction**, word for word **whip**, as in 3:10. *Twelve years* she was sick, and the girl is *twelve years old* (v42): Mark sets the two stories one inside the other.
- **vv35–43 "Do not be afraid; only believe… Talitha koum… give her something to eat"** — *Talitha koum* is Aramaic, and Mark gives its meaning, as with *Boanerges* (3:17). Elijah raised a child in a closed room, *he stretched himself out on the child three times* (1 Kings 17:21); here a word and a hand are enough. *Exestēsan ekstasei megalē*, word for word **they were beside themselves with great astonishment**. The food shows she is truly alive.

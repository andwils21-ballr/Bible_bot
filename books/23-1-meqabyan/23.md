# 1 Meqabyan 23

## Cain and Abel

**1** Do not walk in the way of Cain, who killed his brother. His brother followed him in gentleness, thinking that his brother loved him.

**2** He killed his brother out of jealousy over a woman; and so do those who practice wrongdoing, jealousy and deceit against their neighbors.

**3** They walk in the way of Cain, and not in the way of gentle Abel. For Abel was gentle as a lamb, and his blood was like the blood of a pure lamb that is sacrificed to God with a pure heart.

**4** Those who walk in the way of gentle Abel love God, for they have become gentle like Abel; for God loves everyone who walks in gentleness, like Abel, righteous and gentle.

## The Sentence on Their Necks

**5** But God disregards the evil. Their sentence is kept on their necks, written on their shoulders, and it will be read on the day of judgment before the angels and men and all creation.

**6** Then the rebels and the wrongdoers who did not do the will of God will be ashamed.

**7** A word of terror will be given against them, saying, "Cast them into the judgment of burning fire, where there is no way out forever."

## Notes

- **vv1–2 Cain** — *Woe to them! For they walked in the way of Cain* (Jude 11). *He killed his brother out of jealousy over a woman* is the story told in 19:2.
- **v3 "Abel was gentle as a lamb, and his blood…"** — *By faith Abel offered to God a more acceptable sacrifice than Cain* (Hebrews 11:4); *the sprinkled blood that speaks a better word than the blood of Abel* (Hebrews 12:24). Jesus calls him *righteous Abel* (Matthew 23:35).
- **v5 "Their sentence is kept on their necks, written on their shoulders"** — *dayəmomu… maṣḥuf ba-matākəftihomu*. Daniel: *the court sat in judgment, and the books were opened* (Daniel 7:10).
- **v7 "Cast them into the judgment of burning fire"** — *Depart from Me, you cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels* (Matthew 25:41).

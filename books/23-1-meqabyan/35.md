# 1 Meqabyan 35

## Woe to You

**1** Woe to you, mighty ones of Israel, for God is angry with you, because you did not do justice for the orphan.

**2** Woe to you who go to the house of the wine-seller morning and evening, and get drunk and turn justice aside, and do not hear the case of the orphan and the widows, and live in lewdness and robbery!

**3** So God says to the mighty of Israel: "Woe to you if you do not walk in My commandment and keep My word, and love what I love.

**4** I will bring on you punishment and trial and sickness, and you will waste away like what the moth and the worm eat, and no trace of you will be found within your borders.

**5** Your country will become a desert, and everyone who saw it before will clap his hands and hiss over it, saying, 'Is this not the city that was full of blessing and every good thing? Is this what God has done to it, for the evil of those who live in it?'"

## The Proud City

**6** Woe to her, for she lifted up her heart and raised her head and stretched out her neck, until God brought her low to the dust of the earth for the pride of those who live in her. She became a desert, and thorns and thistles sprouted on her.

**7** She became a ruin and a heap, a forest and a desert, and the beasts live in her.

**8** She became a terror to those who pass through her, for the wrath of God fell on her, and she drank from the cup of His wrath, because of the pride of her heart and the evil of those who live in her.

## Notes

- **v2 "who go to the house of the wine-seller morning and evening"** — *Woe to those who rise early in the morning, that they may run after strong drink, who tarry late into the evening as wine inflames them!… who acquit the guilty for a bribe, and deprive the innocent of his right* (Isaiah 5:11, 23).
- **v4 "like what the moth and the worm eat"** — *I am like a moth to Ephraim, and like dry rot to the house of Judah* (Hosea 5:12).
- **v5 "clap his hands and hiss over it… Is this the city"** — *All who pass along the way clap their hands at you; they hiss and wag their heads at the daughter of Jerusalem: "Is this the city that was called the perfection of beauty?"* (Lamentations 2:15); *Why has the LORD done thus to this land?* (Deuteronomy 29:24).
- **vv7–8 "the beasts live in her… she drank from the cup of His wrath"** — *wild animals will lie down there* (Isaiah 13:21); *you who have drunk from the hand of the LORD the cup of His wrath* (Isaiah 51:17).

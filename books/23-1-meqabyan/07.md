# 1 Meqabyan 7

## What Are You?

**1** Whether you are a king or a ruler, what are you? Is it not God who created you,

**2** and brought you from where you were not, so that you would do His will, walk in His commandment and fear His judgment? As you are angry with your servants and have power over them, so there is One who is angry with you and has power over you.

**3** As you strike without mercy those who do wrong, so there is God, who will strike you and cast you into judgment where there is no way out forever.

**4** As you flog the one who has not submitted to you and has not brought you a gift, why do you not bring a gift to God?

**5** As you want them to fear you, why do you not fear the Lord your God? For He created you and made you king over all His works, to shepherd His people and His flock in righteousness.

## Judge with Righteousness

**6** Do not show favor to the great or the small, but judge with righteousness and uprightness, as God has appointed you. Whom do you fear besides Him? Keep His ways and His commandment.

**7** Do not turn aside to the right or to the left, as Moses commanded the sons of Israel, saying to them, "I have set before you fire and water; stretch out your hand to whichever you wish."

**8** Hear the word I set before you, so that you hear His word and do His commandment, and do not say, "It is beyond the river. Who will bring it to me, so that I may see it and hear His word and do the commandment of God?"

**9** Or if again you say, "Who will go up for me to heaven and bring that word of God down to me, so that I may hear it and do it?", He has said, "See, the word is near you, in your mouth and in your hand."

**10** If you do not hear the Scripture, you do not hear the Lord your God; and if you do not keep His word, you do not love Him or do His commandment.

**11** You are bound to go into eternal judgment if you do not love His commandment and do not do the will of Him who exalted you and honored you above all your people, so that you would shepherd them in your righteousness.

**12** He set you above them, as head over all His people, so that you would remember the name of your God who created you, and He gave you the people to rule with righteousness and uprightness.

**13** Among those who wrong you are some you are to rebuke and some you are to pardon, as you remember the way of God, set your mind right and judge uprightly.

**14** When they dispute before you, do not show favor, and do not take a bribe from a man to acquit him, or to wrong an innocent man, for the whole earth is yours.

**15** If you do His will, He will lengthen your days.

**16** Remember that you will rise from the dead and stand before His face, and be examined for all you have done, whether evil or good.

**17** If you do good, you will dwell in the dwelling of the good kings, in the houses of light; but if you do evil, you will dwell in the dwelling of the evil kings. God is not abashed by your rank.

## When You See Your Glory

**18** But you, when you see the greatness of your majesty, the adornment of your warriors and your weapons hung up, and look on your horses and the soldiers under your hand, and those who blow the horn and play the harp before you,

**19** when you see all this, you lift up your mind and stiffen your neck, and do not remember the One who gave you all this honor. When He says to you, "Let it go," will you not let it go?

**20** He will give your honor to another, for you have utterly disregarded the charge He gave you.

**21** There He will search you, call you to account and judge you. For death comes to you suddenly, and at the resurrection there will be judgment, and the deeds of men will be examined.

**22** For the kings of the earth there is none who honors them, and the crown the princes of the earth boast in will fall; for rich and poor stand together in judgment, for He is the righteous Judge.

## The Dead Will Rise

**23** In the judgment that is ready, the soul trembles, the books are opened, the deeds of men are shown, and nothing is covered,

**24** at the resurrection of the dead. For the earth gives back what was entrusted to it, as a woman cannot hold her womb closed once she has come to childbirth and what is in her belly is ready to come out.

**25** Or can the clouds hold back the rain, so that they do not send it where God has commanded them and it does not come down? So it cannot be that the dead will not rise once the time of resurrection has come. For the word of God brought all things from where there was nothing, and the word of God brought all down to the depths.

**26** Again, the word of God will bring all up from the depths, as Moses said: "Not by bread alone does man live, but by every word that comes out of the mouth of God."

**27** So it is known that the dead live by the word of God.

**28** Again He said in Deuteronomy, concerning those who become kings and rulers and do their own will: "On the day of judgment I will take vengeance on them, in the time when their foot slips, for the day of their reckoning is near."

**29** Again God says to those who do not know the judgment of God: "Know, know that I am God. I kill and I make alive.

**30** I strike and I have mercy; I bring down to Sheol and bring up again to heaven; and there is none who escapes from My hand."

**31** So God has said concerning the kings and rulers who did not keep His commandment: "Keep My law and My ordinance, so that you may enter My kingdom forever; for the kingdom on earth passes away, and moves on from morning to evening."

**32** For the cup of God is for honor and for shame: this one He shames, and that one He honors.

**33** The one who does not do His will He removes, and the one who does His will He appoints.

## Notes

- **v7 "I have set before you fire and water; stretch out your hand to whichever you wish"** — the words are Sirach's: *He has placed before you fire and water: stretch out your hand for whichever you wish* (Sirach 15:16). Moses set before Israel *life and death, blessing and curse* (Deuteronomy 30:19).
- **vv8–9 "beyond the river… Who will go up for me to heaven"** — Moses: *It is not in heaven, that you should say, "Who will go up for us to heaven and bring it to us?"… Neither is it beyond the sea… But the word is very near you. It is in your mouth and in your heart* (Deuteronomy 30:12–14). Here it is *the river* and *your hand*. Paul quotes the passage (Romans 10:6–8).
- **v14 "for the whole earth is yours"** — *ʾəsma ziʾaka yəʾəti mədr kwəllantāhā*; the verse gives no more reason than that.
- **v23 "the books are opened"** — Daniel: *the court sat in judgment, and the books were opened* (Daniel 7:10); John: *the dead were judged by what was written in the books, according to what they had done* (Revelation 20:12).
- **v24 "the earth gives back what was entrusted to it"** — 1 Enoch: *In those days the earth will give back what was entrusted to it, and Sheol will give back what was entrusted to it* (1 Enoch 51:1).
- **v26 "Not by bread alone does man live"** — Deuteronomy 8:3, the word Jesus answered the tempter with (Matthew 4:4).
- **v28 "On the day of judgment I will take vengeance on them, in the time when their foot slips"** — the song of Moses: *Vengeance is Mine, and repayment, for the time when their foot slips; for the day of their calamity is near* (Deuteronomy 32:35).
- **vv29–30 "I kill and I make alive. I strike and I have mercy"** — *See now that I, I am He… I kill and I make alive; I wound and I heal; and there is none that can deliver out of My hand* (Deuteronomy 32:39). *He brings down to Sheol and raises up* is Hannah's (1 Samuel 2:6).
- **v32 "the cup of God… this one He shames, and that one He honors"** — *It is God who judges, putting down one and lifting up another. For in the hand of the LORD there is a cup* (Psalm 75:7–8).

# 1 Meqabyan 8

## The Tree and the Four Parts

**1** Hear, and I will tell you about the resurrection of the dead too. Trees are planted and put out branches, and the vine puts out tendrils, and people take their harvest from it; for God brings the fruit from where there was none.

**2** See: the tree you planted was small, and it comes out with branches and leaves and fruit.

**3** God waters its roots from two breasts, from the water and from the earth.

**4** Its wood He feeds from the wind and from the sun. The root gives water to the leaves, and the earth gives strength to the wood.

**5** The Spirit of God gives fruit among them. So too is the resurrection of the dead.

**6** He said, "Gather the souls from the four winds, from the water and the earth, from the sun and the wind"; for each part went to its own once the soul went out from the flesh.

**7** The earth stayed with its own and became ashes; the water stayed with its own and became dew;

**8** the wind stayed with its own and became wind; and the fire stayed with its own and became the warmth of the sun.

**9** The spirit returned into the hand of its Creator, and He sets it where He wishes, so that He may bring it back when He wishes.

**10** The souls of the righteous He keeps in the houses of light, and the souls of sinners He shuts in the houses of darkness, until the time He chooses to bring them out.

**11** He said to Ezekiel the prophet, "Call from the four winds, so that they may gather and become one spirit."

**12** When he spoke so, with one word they gathered from the four winds.

**13** The water brought dew, and the sun brought warmth.

**14** The earth brought ashes, and the wind brought breath.

**15** God brought the soul from its dwelling, where He had shut it. With one word they gathered, and there was resurrection.

## Night and Morning

**16** Again I will show you a likeness that is near you. Evening comes and you sleep; morning comes and you rise. When you sleep, you are dead;

**17** when you wake, it is a likeness of the resurrection. The night is a likeness of this world, which is all darkness; the whole world sleeps in it, for the darkness of night has covered them.

**18** The dawn of morning is a likeness of the resurrection of the dead, by which the darkness is taken away and there is light in all the world, and man rises and goes out to his work.

**19** So it is with the renewal of the world and the resurrection of the dead; for this world passes away, as this night does.

**20** When the sun comes and shines, it is a likeness of this world, as David said, "In the sun He has set His tent."

**21** When He comes, in the new world, as the sun shines now, when God Himself comes upon it, He will shine on the new world. For He has said, "A sun that does not set and a lamp that is not quenched: God Himself is its light."

## The Seed

**22** Again, He will raise the dead in the twinkling of an eye. Again, I will bring you a likeness from your food, which you sow and live by, whether a grain of wheat or a grain of barley or a grain of lentil, and all the work of man that is sown in all the earth: if it does not die, nothing comes to life.

**23** When it dies, its body is consumed in the dust with its husk, as you see a man's body.

**24** When its body has been consumed by the earth, it sprouts, as much as *[words lost]*

**25** See: if it did not die, it could not live; but when it has sprouted, it puts out a great crop.

**26** From God fruit is given to that crop that has grown, and He clothes it with body, skin and husk.

**27** The leaves, the straw, the chaff and the ears are not counted for you; but see how the grain of what you sowed has multiplied.

**28** Do not be a fool, but see how your seed has multiplied. So too think of the resurrection of the dead, and how they are repaid according to their deeds.

**29** Again, hear, and I will tell you how a seed does not change into another seed. If you sow wheat, do you reap barley?

**30** If you sow cumin, do you reap flax? Among the trees, if you plant a fig, does it come up an almond? If you plant a walnut, does it come up a vine for you?

**31** If you plant sweet fruit, does it come up bitter? If you plant a bitter tree, can it become sweet?

**32** So too, if a sinner dies, can he become righteous at the resurrection? Or if a righteous man dies, can he become a sinner at the resurrection? Each is repaid according to his deeds, according to the fruit of his work and the work of his hands, and no one is judged for the sin of another.

## The Dew of Life

**33** The cedar of Lebanon is planted and puts out long, great branches; but if the dew of Hermon does not fall on it, its leaves do not flourish, and it dries up and is shaken off.

**34** The *zəgbā* is torn out by the roots if the winter rain does not come down on it.

**35** So too, if there does not come down on them *[words lost]* of life from God, the dead will not rise.

## Notes

- **vv6, 11–15 Ezekiel and the four winds** — *Come from the four winds, O breath, and breathe on these slain, that they may live* (Ezekiel 37:9). The chapter reads the valley of dry bones as the resurrection of the body: earth, water, wind and fire each give back their part (vv7–8, 13–14).
- **v9 "The spirit returned into the hand of its Creator"** — *the dust returns to the earth as it was, and the spirit returns to God who gave it* (Ecclesiastes 12:7).
- **v11 "become one spirit"** — *1 manfas*, written with the digit.
- **v20 "In the sun He has set His tent"** — Psalm 19:4 in the Greek, *In the sun He set His tent*; the Hebrew has *In them He has set a tent for the sun*.
- **v21 "A sun that does not set and a lamp that is not quenched: God Himself is its light"** — *Your sun shall no more go down… for the LORD will be your everlasting light* (Isaiah 60:20); *the city has no need of sun or moon to shine on it, for the glory of God gives it light* (Revelation 21:23).
- **vv22–26 the seed** — Paul: *What you sow does not come to life unless it dies… God gives it a body as He has chosen, and to each kind of seed its own body* (1 Corinthians 15:36–38). *In the twinkling of an eye* is his too (15:52).
- **v24 "as much as *[words lost]*"** — the Ge'ez breaks off at *maṭana*, **as much as**.
- **v32 "no one is judged for the sin of another"** — *The soul who sins shall die. The son shall not suffer for the iniquity of the father* (Ezekiel 18:20).
- **vv33–35 the dew** — *the dew of Hermon, which falls on the mountains of Zion* (Psalm 133:3). V35 breaks off where the word for what comes down should be; vv33–34 name dew and rain. Isaiah: *your dew is a dew of light, and the earth will give birth to the dead* (Isaiah 26:19). *Zəgbā* (v34) is a tree whose kind is uncertain.

# 1 Meqabyan 36

## Do Not Boast

**1** Do not boast, you Macedonians, and do not stiffen your neck, you Amalekites, for there is God, who takes vengeance on you.

**2** For you will be raised up to heaven, and you will go down to Sheol.

**3** When Israel first went into the land of Egypt, in the kingdom of the Medes and the Moabites, He said, "Do not boast; it is not fitting for you to bring reproach on God by reproaching Him."

**4** You too, son of a slave woman, Ishmaelite, why do you stretch out your neck toward what is not yours? Do you not remember that God will judge you on the day of judgment, when He rises to judge the earth?

**5** Then you will be repaid according to the work of your hands. "Why do you lift up your heart and stretch out your neck?" says God, who rules all.

**6** "I too will reproach you as you reproached those who were not yours, wherever I sent you; for you do whatever you please, and you do evil.

**7** So I too will do to you," says God, who rules all. "But if you do good and love what I love, I too will hear you in all you ask of Me,

**8** and I will do your will if you do My will. Your enemy will not stand against you, and I will bless your work, your seed and your children.

**9** I will bless the herds of your cattle and your sheep, and bless all you put your hand to, if you walk in My commandment and do what I love," says God, who rules all.

**10** "But if you do not do My will and do not walk in My commandment, My judgment will find you, all this judgment of Mine that was spoken before, and you will not be able to escape My wrath that comes on you at all times, for you did not submit to My commandment or walk in My judgment.

**11** You did not prefer what I prefer, and you turned aside from praising Me and serving Me, though I created you from where you were not.

**12** I gave you honor and praise among the people under you, so that you could do all you wished: to kill and to keep alive, to build and to pull down, to honor and to bring low, to raise up and to put down. It is yours."

## The Way of Salvation

**13** If you do the will of God and walk in His commandments, He will love you, so that you will rejoice with Him in His kingdom and have a share with those who have inherited the holy city.

**14** For He said, "If they are patient with Me, I will give them honor and favor as an inheritance and make them rejoice in My house of prayer, and they will be chosen and pleasing like a sacrifice of offering," says God, who rules all.

**15** Do not grow slack in doing good and in the way of salvation, so that you may cross over from death to life.

**16** Those who keep to doing good God takes into His keeping in every good thing, so that they may be His servants, like Job, whom God kept from all evil,

**17** and like Abraham, whom He saved when he struck down the kings, as He saved Moses from the hand of Pharaoh; and He saved Abraham from the hand of the Canaanites, among whom he lived, while they troubled his soul evening and morning, day and night, to bring him down to their idols.

**18** He was patient, refusing them in what was theirs, the carved images, while they dragged at him.

**19** He kept refusing them, and feared God who created him; for Abraham was faithful to God, in whom he believed from his youth.

**20** He did not lessen his worship of God until the year of his death, for he loved God greatly and did not transgress His commandment until the day of his death; and he taught his sons to keep the commandment of God.

**21** They too did not transgress His commandment, as their father had done. The seed of Abraham, His servants, Isaac and Jacob, did not transgress the commandment of God; of them God spoke, saying to His angels, "I have friends

**22** on the earth: Abraham My beloved, Isaac My servant, and Jacob in whom My soul delights, in whom I am glorified," says God, who rules all.

## Israel in the Wilderness

**23** The sons of Israel provoked Him greatly, again and again, while He was patient with them and fed them manna in the wilderness.

**24** Their clothes did not wear out and their feet did not swell, for they fed on manna, which is the bread of knowledge.

**25** But their heart was always far from God, and they had no hope of salvation, for from of old they were doers of evil.

**26** They were not like their fathers Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, who pleased God by the beauty of their life, but became like a crooked bow, and always provoked Him with their idols, on their hills and their mountains; they ate on the mountains, in the forests and among the rocks.

**27** They slaughtered and sacrificed and rejoiced in the work of their hands, ate what was sacrificed and drank the wine of their drunkenness, and danced and played with demons.

**28** The demons made all their dancing and play sweet to them, all their drunkenness and fornication without measure, greed and wickedness, in which God does not delight.

**29** They sacrificed to the carved image of Canaan, to Baal, to *Māryā* of Midian, to the carved images of the foreigners, Dagon and Apollo, to Artemis and to *Rāṣyon*,

**30** and to all the gods of the nations around them. They all worshiped as the nations did, by what they saw and heard, with all the play and dancing and shouting that the nations practice.

**31** So the people of Israel do, who say, "We worship God," but do not keep His law and His commandment that Moses told them in the Law, to keep the commandments of God and turn away from the worship of idols,

**32** and not to worship other gods besides the God of their fathers, who fed them the grain of the fields, made them ride on the heights of the earth, satisfied them with honey from the rock and fed them manna.

**33** He did not withhold from them what they longed for and desired, for He is their God, and He feeds them what they desire.

**34** But they do not stop provoking God; and when He makes them rejoice, they forget God.

**35** When He punishes them, they cry out to Him, and He saves them from the distress that found them; and again they rejoice openly and live long days.

**36** Then at once they turn their hearts and go back to provoking God as of old; and He raises up against them the nations around them, to kill them, make them serve and oppress them.

**37** Again they turn and rise early and cry out to God their God.

**38** He too has mercy on them, not for their own sake but for their fathers', with whom He swore and established His covenant, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, who pleased God from of old by the beauty of their life.

## The Sand and the Stars

**39** Those who loved Him and kept His commandment He loved, to multiply their seed like the sand of the sea and like the stars of heaven.

**40** *Like the sand of the sea* speaks of the souls of the sinners among the sons of Israel, who at the resurrection of the dead go into the sea of fire.

**41** But the souls of the righteous of the seed of the sons of Israel are like the bright stars above the heavens; for God said to Abraham, "Look at the sky at night and count the stars of heaven, if you can count them." So your seed, the righteous, will shine like the stars in the kingdom of heaven.

**42** Again He said to him, "Look into the sea and at the bank of the river, and see what is in the sand, and count it, if you can count it." So is your seed that goes down into Sheol at the resurrection of the dead.

**43** Abraham believed God, and because of this it was counted to him as righteousness. His hope found him on the earth: after his wife Sarah had grown old, she found a son, Isaac.

**44** Again in the heavens he will find the kingdom because of his faith; for he believed that the dead will rise, and that those who did good will go into eternal life,

**45** and those who did evil into eternal judgment at the resurrection of the dead; and the righteous who did good will reign with Him forever and ever.

**46** Those who did evil will be judged forever and ever. Amen and amen.

## Notes

- **v4 "son of a slave woman, Ishmaelite"** — Hagar's son: *Cast out this slave woman with her son* (Genesis 21:10); Paul, *Abraham had two sons, one by a slave woman and one by a free woman* (Galatians 4:22).
- **v14 "make them rejoice in My house of prayer… like a sacrifice of offering"** — *these I will bring to My holy mountain, and make them joyful in My house of prayer; their burnt offerings and their sacrifices will be accepted on My altar* (Isaiah 56:7).
- **vv21–22 "Abraham My beloved, Isaac My servant, and Jacob in whom My soul delights"** — *you, Israel, My servant, Jacob, whom I have chosen, the offspring of Abraham, My friend* (Isaiah 41:8); *You are My servant, Israel, in whom I will be glorified* (Isaiah 49:3).
- **v24 "Their clothes did not wear out… the bread of knowledge"** — *Your clothing did not wear out on you and your foot did not swell these forty years* (Deuteronomy 8:4); *she will feed him with the bread of understanding* (Sirach 15:3).
- **v26 "like a crooked bow"** — *they twisted like a deceitful bow* (Psalm 78:57). *Ṣolāʿət*, where they ate, is taken as **rocks**.
- **v29 the gods** — *Dagon* of the Philistines (12:12), *Apollo* and *Artemis* of the Greeks (*Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!*, Acts 19:28). *Māryā* of Midian and *Rāṣyon* are not otherwise known.
- **vv39–42 the sand and the stars** — *I will surely multiply your offspring as the stars of heaven and as the sand that is on the seashore* (Genesis 22:17); *Look toward heaven, and number the stars, if you are able to number them… So shall your offspring be* (Genesis 15:5). The book reads the two likenesses as the two ends of the resurrection: *those who are wise shall shine like the brightness of the sky… like the stars forever* (Daniel 12:3).
- **v43 "Abraham believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness"** — Genesis 15:6, quoted by Paul (Romans 4:3) and James (2:23).
- **vv44–46 "those who did good will go into eternal life, and those who did evil into eternal judgment"** — *those who have done good to the resurrection of life, and those who have done evil to the resurrection of judgment* (John 5:29). The book ends, as it began (1:15), on the judgment that is *forever and ever*.

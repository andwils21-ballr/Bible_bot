# 1 Meqabyan 15

## The Threat of Midian

**1** On that day the people of Midian rose against them and raised their army to make war on Israel. The name of their king was Akrandes, and in the morning he gathered a great army from Syria, Cilicia and Damascus.

**2** He encamped at the crossing of the Jordan and sent messengers to Israel, saying, "Give me tribute in denarii. If not, I have come to judge you, to plunder your goods, take all your property and carry your children away captive.

**3** I will carry you off to a country you do not know, and there I will make you hewers of wood and drawers of water.

**4** Do not boast, saying, 'We are the people of God, and no one can stand against us.' Was it not God who sent me to destroy you and plunder your goods? He sent me to gather all that is yours.

**5** Did their gods save the other peoples whom I judged, whose goods and property I plundered, whom I killed, and whose offspring and children I carried away captive?

**6** So I will do to you too, if you do not pay me tribute as I have commanded you." He crossed the Jordan to plunder their goods and their cattle and carry away their wives captive.

**7** Then the sons of Israel wept bitterly before God, and cried out and wailed, and found no one to help them.

## Three Brothers

**8** Because of this God gave strength to three brothers, whose names were these: Mebkyos, Maccabeus and Judah, who were beautiful in appearance and strong in all their might.

**9** They heard the sons of Israel weeping and lamenting, and their hearts grieved for all the sons of Israel, the widows and the orphans, their priests and their princes, and all the kindred of Israel, men and women and children, all of them weeping and putting ashes on their heads, and their honored ones putting on sackcloth.

**10** Those good and handsome brothers agreed to go and save them. They took counsel and said to one another, "Let us go and hand ourselves over to death for these."

**11** They went boldly, with their swords at their sides and their spears in their hands, ready to fight the giant.

**12** They arrived while he was at his midday meal. Mebkyos pierced the giant and struck his neck with one blow while the food was in his mouth, and Judah and Maccabeus struck with the sword those on the right and left of their king.

**13** When their king fell, they all scattered and fled. Their spears went into the hearts of their companions, their bows were broken, and they themselves were crushed.

**14** The good and handsome brothers were saved, and no harm at all came to them; but the evil ones were crushed among themselves, for God turned punishment back on them.

**15** They fell and died, and threw away all that was theirs until they crossed the Jordan, and all that was theirs was left behind. When the sons of Israel saw that they had scattered and gone off with their camp, they took the plunder for themselves.

**16** So God saved the sons of Israel by the hand of Mebkyos and his brothers.

## Back to Their Evil

**17** For a few days the sons of Israel lived pleasing God.

**18** After that they turned back again to their evil, and the sons of Israel grew slack in worshiping God in purity. He in turn provoked them with a people who do not know Him, who reap the fruit of their fields, ruin their vineyards, take their flocks and slaughter their cattle before their eyes; and they could not save themselves, for they were a people who transgressed the law, who always provoked God at all times.

## Notes

- **v1 "Midian… Akrandes"** — *ʾAkrāndəs*, a king known only from this book. Midian oppressed Israel in the days of Gideon (Judges 6:1–6), whose angel the book has named (5:20).
- **v3 "hewers of wood and drawers of water"** — what Joshua made the Gibeonites (Joshua 9:21–27).
- **vv4–5 "Was it not God who sent me… Did their gods save the other peoples?"** — the words of the Assyrian commander at Jerusalem: *Is it without the LORD that I have come up against this place to destroy it? The LORD said to me, Go up against this land* (2 Kings 18:25); *Has any of the gods of the nations ever delivered his land out of the hand of the king of Assyria?* (18:33).
- **v8 "Mebkyos, Maccabeus and Judah"** — *Mabkyos, Maqābyos, Yəhudā*. These three are not the sons of Maccabeus of chapters 2–4; a Maccabeus is named among them. In 1 Maccabees, Judas called Maccabeus is a son of Mattathias (1 Maccabees 2:4).
- **v11 "the giant"** — *raʿāytāwi*, the word of 5:1 and of the giants of 18:2, said here of Akrandes, the king of v1.
- **v12 "while the food was in his mouth"** — the words of Agag's death in 6:32.
- **v13 "Their spears went into the hearts of their companions"** — Gideon's night against Midian: *the LORD set every man's sword against his comrade and against all the army* (Judges 7:22).
- **v18 "a people who do not know Him"** — the song of Moses: *I will make them jealous with those who are no people; I will provoke them to anger with a foolish nation* (Deuteronomy 32:21). The Ge'ez has *faʾyaʾammiru*, read as *ʾəlla ʾi-yaʾammiru*, **who do not know**.

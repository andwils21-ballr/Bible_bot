# 1 Meqabyan 12

## The Vineyard of Sodom

**1** This is your shame, daughter of Jerusalem, which was written by the prophet: as were the deeds and the sin of the people of Sodom and Gomorrah, so are your deeds and your sin, daughter of Jerusalem.

**2** As with Sodom and Gomorrah, so is your shame: their vineyard was planted in fornication and pride.

**3** No rain of humility and mercy fell on them, only the rain of pride and fornication, by which their vineyard takes root, with nothing but wrongdoing and robbery and wickedness, the shedding of blood, and the forgetting of the name of God their God.

**4** They did not know their Creator, only their idols. Their deeds were evil, they took pleasure in the work of their hands, and they practiced lewdness with beasts and with males.

**5** They did not know God in their deeds, for they blinded their eyes so as not to see and stopped their ears so as not to hear, and so as not to do the will of God or what pleases Him. They are the vineyard of Sodom, the vine of Gomorrah, that bear no sweet cluster.

**6** Even when they press their grapes, it becomes poison that kills; for from its beginning it was planted in a curse, and its fruit came in a time of trial.

**7** At no time is there good in the vine of their grapes, for their vineyard was watered with the dew of Sheol, and their branches grew strong in the wind of burning heat.

**8** Even when it bears fruit and is pressed, it is for ruin and for punishment. It hardens the hearts of those who drink it and makes them cold, far from God,

**9** and a dwelling for the devil, for they walk in his evil deeds. The vine of Sodom spreads and devours, and climbs onto the mountains and the hills.

**10** She crowns Baal, whom they worship in the open court, and she sacrifices her sons and her daughters to demons, who do not know evil from good.

**11** They shed innocent blood, and pour out to carved images from the clusters of the vineyard of Sodom.

**12** She worships Dagon of the foreigners, and honors him and sacrifices to him from her fat beasts and her flocks, to take pleasure in their drunkenness and their folly, which the demons taught her, so that she would sacrifice to them and do their will,

**13** and so that she would not know God her Creator, who at all times feeds her and builds her up, from her youth to the days of her prime, and on to the days of her old age and to the day of her death.

## God Speaks against Her

**14** "Again I will take vengeance at the resurrection, and her time will be forever, for she did not turn back to My law or walk in My commandment.

**15** Let her gods rise, if they are gods indeed, and run with her into Gehenna and save her, when I am angry with her in My wrath and have taken away all her lovers who fornicate with her.

**16** I made her a shame, for all the reproach and shame she brought on My house and My holy things.

**17** She bore My name, which was called over her, when they said of her: This is the people of God, the house of the God of Israel, the dwelling of the name of the Most High, Jerusalem, the holy of holies, the city of the great King.

**18** She boasts in Me as though she were My handmaid and I her Lord; yet she does not fear Me and do My will as a Lord's, but sets herself against Me like a rebel.

**19** She serves foreign gods, who do not feed her or raise her or clothe her; instead they have become a burden to her, a snare to lead her astray, to take her far from Me.

**20** She sacrifices to them and eats their sacrifices, pours out drink for them and drinks the wine of their drunkenness, burns incense to them and breathes the fragrance of their incense. They command her, and she obeys them.

**21** Again, she sacrifices her sons and her daughters, and delights in the work of her hands and the toil of her lips, for she cries out to them for love of them.

**22** Woe to her on the day of judgment, to her and to the gods she loves and embraces! With them she will go down into Sheol, to the lowest depth, where the worm does not sleep and the fire is not quenched.

**23** Woe to you, daughter of Jerusalem, put to shame, for you left Me, who created you, and worshiped foreign gods.

**24** I too will repay you according to your deeds and take vengeance on you according to your contempt, for you provoked Me, scorned to follow Me, and did not make your deeds good.

**25** I too will disregard you and will not be quick to save you, for you refused My word and did not hold to My counsel, the covenant you made with Me, that you would keep My commandment and I would be with you and save you from all who oppose you, and that you would keep the commandment I gave you, and My ordinance.

**26** All this you did not keep, and I too have disregarded you, and on the day of judgment I will take vengeance on you. Did I not create you? Yet you did not keep My word and My commandment; and I blessed you, so that you would be Mine.

**27** But you have made yourself a stranger to Me, as Sodom and Gomorrah made themselves strangers.

**28** I burned them up in My wrath; and you are from the roots of Sodom and Gomorrah, whom I uprooted, and I blotted out their memory from the earth so that they would not live on in their oppression. For they provoked Me, who created them, by going in to a man's wife, and fornicating lawlessly with males and with beasts as with women.

**29** There was no fear of God before their eyes, from the least of them to the greatest. No one rebuked his neighbor to make him leave off; they helped one another in every evil deed, and were full of all wrongdoing and robbery, for their deeds were evil.

**30** Every evil deed was set in their thoughts, pride, robbery and greed.

**31** Because of this God disregarded them and overthrew their cities. Some He burned with fire down to their foundations, and not one remained of those that perished and were uprooted forever.

**32** They wait for the day of judgment forever, the great day of judgment, for they were bound by sin; and He will not pity them or have mercy on them, for they provoked Me with their evil deeds.

**33** I disregarded them; and so you too, daughter of Jerusalem, put to shame, will be bound on the day of judgment, for you will find nothing to excuse yourself with when I seize you in My wrath. For all your deeds are robbery and wickedness, greed and fornication and the speaking of lies, and every way of error and stumbling that I do not delight in.

**34** I made you for honor, but you made yourself for shame. I called you Mine, but you made yourself another's.

**35** I chose you for holiness, but you became the devil's; and I will take vengeance on you according to the evil of your deeds.

**36** I will lengthen an evil vengeance on you, because you did not hear My word or keep My commandment that I gave you on the day I loved you; for I am God, who created you. I will be angry with all sinners like you, and repay them according to their evil on the day of judgment.

**37** You too I will judge with them, for you did not keep My word and you disregarded My judgment.

**38** O Sodom and Gomorrah, who have no fear of God in your heart!

**39** So too, daughter of Jerusalem, you will be judged together, you with her, in the terror of Gehenna, and all who did your deeds; for you will go down together to the Gehenna that is prepared, where there is no way out forever and ever.

**40** You and she, who did not keep My word and My commandment, will go down together into Sheol on the day of judgment, for you did not keep My word and My commandment.

## The Portion of the Righteous

**41** But as for those who kept My word and My commandment: what the sinners stored up, the righteous will eat, and what the evil acquired, the good will divide as plunder. As God has commanded, the righteous and the good will rejoice and be glad.

**42** But the sinners and the wrongdoers will weep and mourn for all their offenses, in which they strayed from My commandment.

**43** Whoever keeps My word and walks in My commandment, he is the one who will eat of the blessing and be honored with Me.

**44** Everyone who keeps My word and does My commandment will eat the blessing of the earth, and will enter and dwell where the good and upright kings enter."

## Notes

- **v1 "as were the deeds and the sin of the people of Sodom and Gomorrah, so are your deeds"** — *Hear the word of the LORD, you rulers of Sodom! Give ear to the teaching of our God, you people of Gomorrah!* (Isaiah 1:10); *Your sister Sodom and her daughters have not done as you and your daughters have done* (Ezekiel 16:48).
- **vv5–8 the vineyard** — the vine of Sodom from the song of Moses (11:6–8), now Jerusalem's. Isaiah's song of the vineyard: *He looked for it to yield grapes, but it yielded wild grapes* (Isaiah 5:2).
- **v5 "they blinded their eyes so as not to see and stopped their ears"** — *Make the heart of this people dull, and their ears heavy, and blind their eyes, lest they see with their eyes, and hear with their ears* (Isaiah 6:10).
- **v12 "Dagon of the foreigners"** — *Dāgon za-ʾIloflí*. *ʾIloflí* is the Greek *allophyloi*, the Philistines (5:20); Dagon is their god (Judges 16:23; 1 Samuel 5:2).
- **v14 "Again I will take vengeance"** — from here to the end God speaks of Jerusalem and to her, without a word to say that the speaker has changed.
- **v17 "the city of the great King"** — *Mount Zion… the city of the great King* (Psalm 48:2); Jesus: *or by Jerusalem, for it is the city of the great King* (Matthew 5:35).
- **v22 "where the worm does not sleep and the fire is not quenched"** — *their worm shall not die, their fire shall not be quenched* (Isaiah 66:24), as Jesus says it of Gehenna (Mark 9:48).
- **v29 "no fear of God before their eyes"** — *There is no fear of God before his eyes* (Psalm 36:1; Romans 3:18).
- **v41 "what the sinners stored up, the righteous will eat"** — *the sinner's wealth is laid up for the righteous* (Proverbs 13:22); *he may pile it up, but the righteous will wear it* (Job 27:17).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

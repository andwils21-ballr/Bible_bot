# 1 Meqabyan 32

## What God Gave Israel

**1** "You too, kings and rulers, hear My word and keep My commandment," says God, who rules all. "For the sons of Israel provoked Me and worshiped other gods, though I was the Lord their God, who kept them and saved them and raised them up from the time of their fathers, and fed them with all they desired.

**2** I made them ride on the heights of the earth and fed them with the blessing of the earth: as was fitting, vineyards they had not planted, olive oil, and cisterns of sweet water they had not dug,

**3** drops of honey and the milk of sheep with the finest of the wheat, fine garments and everything they desired.

**4** Nothing they asked of Me did I withhold from them."

---

## Notes

- **vv2–3 "vineyards they had not planted… cisterns… they had not dug"** — *cisterns that you did not dig, and vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant* (Deuteronomy 6:11).
- **vv2–3 "the heights of the earth… honey… the finest of the wheat"** — the song of Moses: *He made him ride on the high places of the land… He made him suck honey out of the rock… curds from the herd, and milk from the flock… with the very finest of the wheat* (Deuteronomy 32:13–14), as in 5:22–23.
- **v4 "Nothing they asked of Me did I withhold"** — *He gave them what they asked, but sent a wasting disease among them* (Psalm 106:15).

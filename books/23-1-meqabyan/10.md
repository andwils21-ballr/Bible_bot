# 1 Meqabyan 10

## Buried with Their Fathers

**1** If it were not so, why did the fathers of old, from Adam, Abel and Seth, Noah and Shem, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, Joseph, Moses and Aaron, not want to be buried anywhere but in the tomb of their fathers?

**2** Was it not so that they would rise together at the resurrection with their kin, and that their bones would not be counted with the bones of the evil, the sinners and the heathen?

**3** You too, do not let your mind go astray when you say, "How will the dead rise after their death, those who are laid in one grave by the *[number lost]*, and whose bones have decayed?"

**4** When you look into the grave and not a handful of dust is found, and you say, "How will the bones of the dead rise?", you say this in the folly of your heart.

**5** Do you say that what you sowed will not sprout? What you sowed does sprout.

**6** So too God, who sowed the souls: they will rise quickly. He does not delay raising them by His life-giving word, for He brought man from where he was not into true being.

**7** Again He brought him back from being into death, and again from death He brings him back into being.

**8** God is able to give life and to raise up.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "not want to be buried anywhere but in the tomb of their fathers"** — Jacob: *let me lie with my fathers. Carry me out of Egypt and bury me in their burying place* (Genesis 47:30); Joseph made the sons of Israel swear, *you shall carry up my bones from here* (Genesis 50:25), and Moses took them (Exodus 13:19).
- **v3 "laid in one grave by the *[number lost]*"** — the Ge'ez has *babba))*, **by the** followed by two stray marks: a numeral lost in the conversion of the text, like those of 2:6 and 3:26. There is no reading of the Church to restore it from here.
- **v5 "What you sowed does sprout"** — the likeness of the seed in 8:22–28.
- **v6 "He brought man from where he was not"** — *ʾəm-ḫaba ʾi-hallo*, the words of 1:11, *when he did not exist*.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

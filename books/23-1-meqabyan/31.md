# 1 Meqabyan 31

## God Speaks

**1** "Kings are not appointed without My will, the poor are not made poor without My command, and the mighty are not made strong without My will.

**2** I gave David favor, and wisdom to Solomon, and I added years to Hezekiah.

**3** I shortened the days of Goliath; I gave strength to Samson, and again I weakened his strength.

**4** I saved David My servant from the hand of Goliath the giant,

**5** and also from the hand of Saul the king, and also from the hand of another giant who opposed him, and from the hand of all who opposed and resisted him, for he kept My commandment.

**6** I loved him; and all who keep My commandment, kings and rulers, I too love them and give them strength and victory over their enemies, for they too have made Me glad.

**7** Again I give them the land of inheritance that I swore to their fathers, a pure and bright land, so that they may inherit the land of their fathers."

---

## Notes

- **v2 "wisdom to Solomon… years to Hezekiah"** — *I give you a wise and discerning mind* (1 Kings 3:12); *I will add fifteen years to your life* (2 Kings 20:6).
- **v3 "I gave strength to Samson, and again I weakened his strength"** — *If my head is shaved, then my strength will leave me* (Judges 16:17); *the LORD had left him* (16:20).
- **v5 "another giant who opposed him"** — *Ishbi-benob, one of the descendants of the giants… thought to kill David. But Abishai the son of Zeruiah came to his aid* (2 Samuel 21:16–17).

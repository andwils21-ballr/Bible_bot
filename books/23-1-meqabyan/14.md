# 1 Meqabyan 14

## The Flood and the Covenant

**1** Even when God punished the first people with the waters of the flood for the sins of the sons of Cain, He drowned the earth with the waters of the flood and washed it of all the filth of the offense of the sons of Cain.

**2** He destroyed all the wrongdoers, for He said, "I regret that I made man." He destroyed all and left none but four souls; and after that He multiplied them, and they filled the earth and divided the inheritance of their father Adam.

**3** Noah made a covenant with God, that He would not destroy the earth again with the waters of the flood; and the sons of Noah, that they would not eat what has died or what is torn by beasts, and would not worship foreign gods besides God their Creator; and He, that He would be their God and would not destroy them suddenly for nothing, and would not withhold from them the rain of spring and autumn, and would give food in its season to man and beast, fruits and grass and plants; and they, that they would make their conduct good in all that God loves.

**4** After He had given them this ordinance, the sons of Israel provoked God with their evil, and did not walk as their fathers Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, who did not transgress the commandment of God their Creator.

**5** These sons of Israel were crooked in their deeds, from the least of them to the greatest, and did not keep the commandment of God.

**6** Even their Levites, their officials and their scribes, all of them, transgress the commandment of God,

**7** and do not stand in the law and the ordinance of God that Moses commanded them in the book of the law, saying, "Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul,

**8** and love your neighbor as yourself. Do not worship foreign gods, do not go to another man's wife, do not kill, do not steal,

**9** do not bear false witness, and do not covet your neighbor's goods, or anything your brother has acquired, whether his donkey or his ox."

**10** When He commands the sons of Israel this, the harsh ones turn to robbery, falsehood, wrongdoing and wickedness, going to another man's wife, theft, lying and the worship of gods.

## The Calf at Horeb

**11** At Horeb too the sons of Israel provoked God by making a calf that grazes on grass. They bowed down to it and said, "These are our gods, who brought us out of the land of Egypt."

**12** They rejoiced in the work of their hands; they ate and drank and were filled, and rose up to dance.

**13** Because of this Moses was angry and came down from Mount Sinai, for God had said to him, "Your people whom you brought out of Egypt, out of the house of slavery, have sinned and gone astray. They have made the image of a calf and bowed down to the cast image."

**14** Moses came down with Joshua his servant, angry with the people. When Joshua heard, he said, "I hear the sound of war in the camp of Israel."

**15** Moses said to Joshua, "It is not the sound of war, but the sound of men drunk with wine." He came down and broke their image and ground it fine until it was like dust, and threw it into the spring that the sons of Israel drank from at the foot of the mountain.

**16** Then he commanded the Levites to kill among themselves, for the evil they had done before God.

**17** They did as he commanded them, knowing that a reproach against God is greater than the killing of themselves, their kin and their fathers.

**18** Because of this Moses said to them, "Have you so despised God, who fed you and nursed you, and brought you out of the house of slavery, and gave you as an inheritance the land He swore to your fathers, to give to them and to their seed after them?"

**19** Even there they did not stop provoking God, for they ran into all evil and robbery.

## Not like Their Fathers

**20** They were not like their fathers Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, who pleased God by the beauty of their conduct from their youth to the days of their prime and their old age, so that He gave them what is on earth and what is in heaven, which He has prepared for those who love Him: here, the land of inheritance that flows with milk and honey; and there, the garden of joy prepared for the righteous, which eye has not seen and ear has not heard and has not entered the heart of man, which God has prepared for those who love Him, for Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, who pleased God in their life.

**21** But their sons were deniers and evil, who walk in the lust of their heart, and did not hear the word of God, who fed them and nursed them and kept them from their youth.

**22** They did not remember God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt and saved them from the work of the bricks and the work of bitter slavery.

**23** They provoked Him greatly, and He raises up against them the foreigners around them, who rise against them and subdue them, as He wills.

## Notes

- **v2 "none but four souls"** — the Ge'ez has the digit *4*. Peter counts *eight persons… brought safely through water* (1 Peter 3:20): Noah, his wife, his three sons and their wives (Genesis 7:13). Four are Noah and his three sons.
- **v3 the covenant with Noah** — *never again shall there be a flood to destroy the earth* (Genesis 9:11); *you shall not eat flesh with its life, that is, its blood* (9:4). The law of what has died or is torn is Leviticus 17:15 and Exodus 22:31. Jubilees tells of Noah giving his sons such commands (Jubilees 7:20–39).
- **vv7–9 "Love the Lord your God… and love your neighbor as yourself"** — Deuteronomy 6:5 and Leviticus 19:18, the two that Jesus calls the great commandments (Matthew 22:37–39), joined here to the ten words (Exodus 20:13–17).
- **v11 "a calf that grazes on grass"** — *They exchanged the glory of God for the image of an ox that eats grass* (Psalm 106:20). *These are your gods, O Israel, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt* (Exodus 32:4).
- **v12 "they ate and drank… and rose up to dance"** — *the people sat down to eat and drink and rose up to play* (Exodus 32:6), as Paul quotes it (1 Corinthians 10:7).
- **v15 "the sound of men drunk with wine"** — *ḍəmḍa fāhlu la-wayn*. In Exodus Moses says, *it is the sound of singing that I hear* (Exodus 32:18). The dust thrown into *the brook that came down out of the mountain* is Deuteronomy 9:21.
- **vv16–17 the Levites** — Exodus 32:26–29: *Each one of you kill his brother and his companion and his neighbor*, and about three thousand fell.
- **v20 "which eye has not seen and ear has not heard"** — *What no eye has seen, nor ear heard, nor the heart of man imagined, what God has prepared for those who love Him* (1 Corinthians 2:9; Isaiah 64:4).
- **v22 "the work of the bricks"** — *gənfāl*: *they made their lives bitter with hard service, in mortar and brick* (Exodus 1:14).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

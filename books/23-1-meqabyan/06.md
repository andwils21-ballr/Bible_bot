# 1 Meqabyan 6

## The Dwelling of the Good Kings

**1** The kings wrote of Him what is right, for He makes the kings who do His will reign in righteousness.

**2** He makes their dwelling shine with a light that cannot be told, where Abraham, Isaac and Jacob are, David and Solomon and Ezekiel, and all the good kings whose dwelling is bright, for they did the will of God. It is not like a hall on earth, but a hall in heaven, very awesome, and its floor is pure, the color of gold and silver and jacinth.

**3** Its appearance is very bright, beyond the thought of man: the hall of the kingdom of heaven, shining with topaz and emerald, *ṭalmā* and *warāwər*,

**4** and with every precious stone beyond the thought of man. The hall of the kingdom is very bright, which He Himself, the Craftsman, made as He knows: the hall of the kingdom of heaven. Its floor is pure, the color of gold and silver and jacinth, green and yellow, the color of jacinth and fine silk.

**5** So it is very beautiful and lovely, pure and bright,

**6** woven with righteousness and uprightness, and built with kindness and mercy.

**7** Water of life flows there, very pure, like the sun, and there is a tent of light, ringed with the *ʾakwāl* of Hermon.

**8** Around its house are planted the sweet fruits of the garden, each with its own look and fragrance, and a vineyard, olive trees and oil, very good, and the fragrance of its fruit is sweet.

**9** If one of flesh went in there, his soul would go out of him at the sweetness of the fragrance of its fruit, for the greatness of the joy that is there.

**10** There the good kings who did the will of God rejoice. Their deeds and their station are known, in the place of life forever, in the kingdom of heaven.

**11** He has shown that it is on earth as it is in heaven: as great as their kingdom on earth is, and as people honor them and bow down to them, so in heaven they are honored and exalted and rejoice, if they did good in their life on the earth.

## The Dwelling of the Harsh Kings

**12** But the kings who are harsh in their kingdom and in the rule God gave them, who do not judge with righteousness and uprightness, and do not save the oppressed, the guest and the orphan, for they have ignored the voice of the poor and the needy,

**13** who do not save the poor and the needy from the hand of the one who robs them, do not fill the hungry from their food or give the thirsty drink from their drink, and have not turned their ears to the cry of the sons of men,

**14** they too will be taken to the dwelling of Sheol, the end of darkness. As great as their honor was, so much greater will their shame and their need be, when that great day that God has made comes upon them, and His wrath. As David said in the psalm, "O Lord, do not punish me in Your wrath, nor rebuke me in Your anger."

**15** So with those who did not keep Your law, while they were kings and rulers and held the world on the earth.

**16** But in heaven is the One who holds all. The souls of all are in His hand, and the life of all is in His mouth. He gives honor to those who honor Him, and loves those who love Him, for He rules all and judges all.

**17** He tests the heart and the kidneys, for He is the King of heaven and earth, who grants his prayer to the one who prays to Him with a pure heart.

**18** He breaks the pride of the mighty who do evil to widows and orphans.

## Saul and Agag

**19** It is not by your might that you took this kingdom, nor by your power that you sit on this throne. He Himself chose to seat you on the throne of the kingdom, to test you, whether you can rule His people, as with Saul, who in those days ruled His people and disregarded the word of God and the word of Samuel the prophet, and did not kill the king of Amalek and his men.

**20** For God said to Samuel the prophet, "Go, tell Saul: Go and make war on the land of Amalek, and kill all their kings and all who live there, man and beast, for they have provoked Me by transgressing the law, by serving gods and bowing down to idols in their shrines, and by every filthy deed that is of no use."

**21** Because of this, when they provoked God, He sent Saul to kill them.

**22** But he spared their king, and many cattle, and women and virgins, and the finest of the young men. Because of this God said to Samuel the prophet, "Because he has despised My word and not heard My command, go and tear his kingdom away.

**23** Anoint David son of Jesse to reign over Israel in his place.

**24** As for him, let loose on him a demon that will strangle him.

**25** For I gave him the kingdom so that he would do My will, and when he refused to do My will, I put him out of his honor. As for you, go, tell him, and say to him: Is this how you disregard God, who made you king over His people Israel and set you on the throne of His kingdom?

**26** You did not know how great the honor and greatness was that He gave you."

**27** Samuel the prophet went to Saul the king, and went in to him while he was at table, with Agag king of Amalek on his left.

**28** He said to him, "Why have you so utterly disregarded God, who commanded you to kill man and beast?"

**29** Then the king was afraid, rose from his seat and took hold of Samuel's garment. Samuel refused to turn back, and Samuel's garment tore.

**30** Samuel said to Saul, "God has torn your kingdom away."

**31** Again Saul said to Samuel, "Honor me before the people, and make atonement for me before God so that He may forgive me." Samuel refused to turn back from his word, for he feared the word of God his Creator and did not fear a mortal king.

**32** Because of this he pierced Agag king of Amalek while the food was still in his mouth.

**33** As for that wicked man who had despised the word of God, an evil spirit seized him and struck his head. For God is not abashed before a king who does wrong, for He is the King of kings, who commands all.

**34** No one commands Him, for He is the Lord of all creation, who overthrows all the authority of the kings and rulers who do not fear Him.

**35** From Saul too He took the kingdom away, from him and from his son, as it says: "The house of David goes on rising, and the house of Saul goes on sinking."

**36** God took vengeance on the seed of the house of Saul, because he had provoked Him and had not destroyed His enemies, who provoked Him with their evil deeds. For whoever does not take vengeance on God's enemy is himself God's enemy.

**37** Whoever does not take vengeance on the sinner, though he can and has the authority, he truly is the enemy of God, who does not punish the one who is without God's law.

## Notes

- **v2 "David and Solomon and Ezekiel"** — *Ḥəzqəʾel*, Ezekiel. The list is of kings; Hezekiah, *Ḥəzqəyās*, would fit it, and the Church reads Hezekiah.
- **v15 "those who did not keep Your law"** — the Ge'ez has *ʾəʿāqabəwwo*, taken as *ʾiʿāqabəwwo*, **did not keep**, a difference of one vowel sign. The verse reads as a fragment of v14.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

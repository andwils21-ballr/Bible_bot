# 1 Meqabyan 22

## Two Kinds of Names

**1** Remember, then, the name of those who do good, and do not forget their deeds.

**2** Be zealous that your name may be like theirs, so that you may rejoice with them in the heavens, in the dwelling of light prepared for the good kings and rulers who did the will of God.

**3** Again, know and consider the name of the evil kings and rulers, how they are condemned and reviled among men after their death.

**4** In the heavens too they are judged with the wicked and the rebels who did not do the will of God; for though they saw and heard, they did not mend their way.

## Be Good, Gentle and Upright

**5** As for you, do not walk in the way of the wicked, with whom God was angry for the evil of their deeds; but be good and gentle and upright.

**6** Judge for righteousness. Do justice to the widow and the orphan, and save them from the hand of the sinners who oppress them.

**7** Stand by the orphan like a father, to save him from the hand of the one who oppresses him. Be dismayed when the orphan's tears are shed in your presence, so that you will not be dismayed in the sea of fire where the sinners who did not repent are judged.

**8** Set your feet straight in the way of peace; seek peace and pursue it. For the eyes of God are on His righteous ones, and His ears are open to their prayer.

**9** But the face of God is against those who do evil, to cut off their memory from the earth, and He leaves none of them on the mountains or within the walls.

**10** "I will not turn My face away until I destroy those who do not keep My word, for I am a jealous God, who takes vengeance on those who hate Me and on those who did not keep My word.

**11** I honor those who honor Me and keep My word."

# 1 Meqabyan 20

## God Keeps the Righteous

**1** Remember, our brothers, and do not forget what was told you before: that God keeps the righteousness of those who do good.

**2** Their fruit will multiply on the earth, and their memory will be for good forever, and their seed will not lack bread on the earth.

**3** He will save them from the hand of all who oppose them and all their enemies who rise against them; for He contends for them, and does not leave them in the hand of their enemy.

**4** He is a helper in their distress to those who love His name. He takes them into His keeping, and forgives them all their sins.

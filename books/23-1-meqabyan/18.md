# 1 Meqabyan 18

## The Sons of the Giants

**1** Remember that you are going to God, and all is in His hand, and you will stand with the deeds you have done, to be judged before Him.

**2** For so too, of old, were the sons of the giants, evil and proud. There was none greater or stronger than they in stature and in the greatness of their arms; yet they did not set God before them, and did not know that He was their Creator, who brought them from where they were not.

**3** Their fathers, while they were angels, praised God with the angels in the heavens; but when their lust led them astray, they went down into the judgment of punishment forever.

**4** They took wives from the daughters of the sons of men; for God had made for them the flesh of the sons of men, to expose the folly of their proud hearts and to try them, whether they would keep His commandment and His law.

**5** They did not keep the commandment, and He brought them down into the judgment of burning fire with the devil their father. Because of them the days of man were shortened, for God was angry with the angels, who sinned like men.

**6** They drew the sons of men with them, and brought them down into Sheol in their judgment.

## The Days of Man

**7** In the beginning the days of man were *[number lost]*; they came down to a hundred and twenty years, for the days of man were shortened because the angels went astray with the daughters of Cain.

**8** God said, "My Spirit will not remain with them, for they are flesh and blood."

**9** Because of this our days were shortened, and there are some who die while they are children; for our days were shortened from those of our first fathers because of our offense and our sin.

**10** But for our fathers their days were long, for they kept His commandment and did not provoke God.

**11** They rebuked their daughters to teach them, and rebuked their sons not to transgress the commandment of God.

**12** Because of this their days were long in righteousness, for they did not transgress the commandment of God, they and their sons and their daughters.

## Notes

- **v2 "the sons of the giants"** — *daqiqa raʿāyt*. The giants of 1 Enoch are the children of the watchers and the daughters of men (1 Enoch 7:2); Numbers knows *the sons of Anak, who come from the Nephilim* (Numbers 13:33). The same word names the proud *giant* of 5:1 and 15:11.
- **vv3–5 the angels who took wives** — *the sons of God saw that the daughters of man were attractive. And they took as their wives any they chose* (Genesis 6:2). 1 Enoch tells it at length (1 Enoch 6–10); Jude speaks of *the angels who did not stay within their own position of authority… kept in eternal chains under gloomy darkness until the judgment of the great day* (Jude 6).
- **v7 "*[number lost]*… a hundred and twenty years"** — numerals lost in the conversion of the text. The first is lost entirely (*()*). The second is *)wa-2*: in this text larger numbers are cut short to a digit (forty is *4* in 1:21), so *2* stands for twenty, and *)* for the lost hundred. The number is the one of Genesis 6:3.
- **v8 "My Spirit will not remain with them, for they are flesh and blood"** — *My Spirit shall not abide in man forever, for he is flesh: his days shall be 120 years* (Genesis 6:3).
- **v9 "some who die while they are children"** — *No more shall there be in it an infant who lives but a few days, or an old man who does not fill out his days* (Isaiah 65:20).

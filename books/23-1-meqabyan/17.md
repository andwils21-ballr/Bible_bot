# 1 Meqabyan 17

## Edom and Amalek

**1** Edom and Amalek joined together. They do not serve God, who holds the judgment of heaven and earth in His hand; they plot to trample His sanctuary, for they are a rebellious people who do not walk in righteousness.

**2** There is no fear of God before their eyes, only fornication and the shedding of blood, the eating of what is sacrificed, what is torn by beasts and what has died, and everything like this, rejected and defiled.

**3** They have no faith and no salt, for they are enemies of the good. They do not know God or the way of peace, only robbery and wickedness and every unclean deed, disorder, dancing and play, as their father *Sablyānos* taught them.

**4** For he has made them his own with his demons, and he teaches them every evil deed that is his: robbery and wickedness, taking others' goods by wrongdoing, theft, eating what has died and what is torn by beasts, and fornication,

**5** and everything like this: going to another man's wife, shedding blood, eating what has died and what is sacrificed to idols, killing a human soul by wrongdoing, quarreling, envy and greed, and every evil deed that God does not delight in. This is the teaching that their enemy *Sablānyos* brings them, to turn them away from the way of God, who holds all the world.

## The Way of God

**6** But the way of God is humility and gentleness, loving one's neighbor, not provoking a brother, love for one another, and peace with all people.

**7** Do not be people who show favor to persons, robbers, wrongdoers, cheats, men who go to other men's wives, doers of evil and violence against their neighbors, and deceivers who wrong their neighbors,

**8** who quarrel and stir themselves up, lean toward evil and are quick to lead astray, so as to bring people down into the judgment of punishment forever.

## Notes

- **v1 "they plot to trample His sanctuary"** — *kayda beta maqdasu*, **the trampling of His holy house**. Psalm 83 names *Edom… and Amalek* among those who *say, "Let us take possession for ourselves of the pastures of God"* (Psalm 83:6–12).
- **v2 "the eating of what is sacrificed, what is torn by beasts and what has died"** — the laws given to Noah's sons (14:3), and the things the apostles forbade the Gentiles: *what has been sacrificed to idols, and blood, and what has been strangled* (Acts 15:29).
- **v3 "no faith and no salt"** — *ʾalbomu hāymānot wa-ʾi-məlḥ*. *Have salt in yourselves, and be at peace with one another* (Mark 9:50); *Let your speech always be gracious, seasoned with salt* (Colossians 4:6).
- **vv3, 5 "*Sablyānos*… *Sablānyos*"** — the name of 13:3, spelled two ways in this chapter. Here he is *their father* and *their enemy*, the teacher of every evil deed, with his demons.
- **v6 "not provoking a brother"** — the Ge'ez has *wa-ʾamʿəʿo ʾəḫu*, **and provoking a brother**, in a list of the ways of God; it is read as *not provoking*, the sense the list requires.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

# 1 Meqabyan 4

## Fire, Sea and Beasts

**1** When that wicked man saw that they were dead, he ordered their bodies burned with fire until they became like dust, to be scattered by the wind. But the fire could not burn the hair of their heads from their bodies, and they brought them out of the pit.

**2** Again they kindled fire over them from morning until evening, and it did not touch them. Again he said, "Come, let us throw their bodies into the sea."

**3** They did as the king commanded and threw them into the sea. Yet the sea did not swallow them, though they had loaded them with heavy irons, great stones and millstones turned by donkeys. They did not sink into the sea but floated on the surface of the deep, for the Spirit of God was on them; and in everything he schemed against them, he failed.

**4** He said, "Since these bodies of theirs have worn me out more than their lives did, what shall I do? I will throw their bodies to the beasts to eat."

**5** They did so, and the beasts and the birds did not touch their bodies. Their bodies lay there fourteen days, while eagles and birds shaded them with their wings from the heat of the sun, the bodies of the five martyrs.

**6** When people saw them, their bodies shone like the rays of the sun, and angels surrounded their bodies like a tent of light.

**7** He took counsel and found nothing he could do, and after that he dug the ground and buried the bodies of the five martyrs.

## The Five in the Night

**8** The five martyrs appeared at night to that wicked king while he was sleeping on his bed, and stood before him holding their swords.

**9** When he woke he was afraid and wanted to flee from corner to corner of the house, for it seemed to him that men had come into his house to do him wrong. His knees shook and he was afraid, for it seemed to him that they would kill him.

**10** So he said, "What do you want, my lords? What shall I do for you?"

**11** They answered him, "Are we not the ones you killed and burned, and ordered thrown into the sea? But you failed, for God kept our bodies, because we trusted in Him. Glory to God! For whoever trusts in Him does not perish, and we who trusted in Him have not been put to shame."

**12** He said to them, "What reward shall I give you in return for the evil I did to you? For I did not know that such punishment would come upon me.

**13** Now set out for me the reward I am to give you, so that you do not take my life in death or bring me down alive to Sheol.

**14** For I have sinned. Forgive me this wrong of mine, for the law of your Father is mercy."

**15** The blessed martyrs answered him, "We will not repay you evil for the evil you did to us. The Lord our God is the One who will repay you, for repayment belongs to God.

**16** But we have appeared to you as living, because of the blindness of your heart and the smallness of your understanding. As for us, while you thought you were killing us, you were preparing life for us.

**17** You and your gods will go down into the punishment of burning fire, where there is no way out forever.

**18** Woe to you, you and your gods that you serve and bow down to, when you have stopped bowing down to God who created you! You are spittle, and do not know where you came from. Are you not smoke that passes, seen today and gone tomorrow?"

**19** The king answered them, "What do you command me, so that I may do all you wish?"

**20** They said to him, "It is not for our own sake that we teach you, but for yours, so that you do not go into the punishment of burning fire.

**21** For your gods are gold and silver, wood and stone, the work of men's hands, that have no soul, mind or understanding.

**22** They do not kill and do not make alive, they do no good and do no harm, they do not humble and do not honor, they do not make poor and do not make rich, they do not uproot and do not plant. They lead you astray by the spirit of demons, who do not want a single man to be saved,

**23** above all those like you, blind of heart, who think that these created you, when it was you who made them.

**24** For the spirits of demons and of satans gather and answer you as you wish, to drown you in the sea of fire.

**25** But you, leave this error and serve the Lord our God, and let this be our reward in return for our death, so that we may gain for our souls."

**26** He was dismayed and astonished, and marveled and was afraid, for their swords were drawn *[words lost]*

**27** He said, "Now I know that the dead who have become ashes truly rise; as for me, I have barely escaped being killed."

**28** After that they vanished from the king's eyes. From that day Tsirutsaydan, proud and arrogant as he was, gave up burning their bodies.

## The King's Error

**29** He rejoiced in the error of his heart and in his gods, for they had led him astray many days. It was not he alone who went astray; he led many like him astray, until they gave up following the One who created them.

**30** They sacrificed their sons and their daughters to demons, and did the will of their heart, the fornication and lewdness that their father Satan had taught them, to make the disorder and lewdness that God does not delight in.

**31** They took their mothers as wives and defiled their sisters and their kinswomen, and every deed like this, filthy deeds. They defiled their souls and refused to turn back, for Satan had hardened the hearts of those crooked men.

**32** That Tsirutsaydan was proud and arrogant; he did not know his Creator, and boasted in his gods.

**33** How does God give a kingdom to those who do not know Him? He said: because He tests them, whether they will turn and come back to Him.

**34** If they turn and come back to Him, He loves them and keeps their kingdom; but if they refuse, He punishes them in the trembling of Gehenna forever.

**35** A king is to fear the Lord God, as great as his kingdom is; and a ruler is to submit himself well to his Creator, as great as his rule is.

**36** The learned, the great men and the stewards are to obey their Creator, as great as their honor is.

**37** For He created all creation, He the Lord of the heavens and the earth, who makes poor and makes rich, who humbles and honors; for there is no other god besides Him in heaven above or on the earth below.

## Notes

- **v3 "millstones turned by donkeys"** — *maḫrəḍa ʾadg*, **a donkey's millstone**. Jesus' saying: *it would be better for him to have a great millstone fastened around his neck and to be drowned in the depth of the sea* (Matthew 18:6), where *great millstone* is in Greek *mylos onikos*, a donkey's millstone.
- **v5 "fourteen days… the bodies of the five martyrs"** — the Ge'ez has *4* for the days; the tens are lost, and the number in the verse is the one the Ethiopian Church reads. The EOTC text then breaks off; *the bodies of the five martyrs* is from Horovitz's Ge'ez excerpts (1905).
- **v8** — the EOTC Ge'ez has only *they appeared at night to that wicked king*; the rest of the verse is from Horovitz's excerpts.
- **v15 "repayment belongs to God"** — *Vengeance is Mine, and repayment* (Deuteronomy 32:35); Paul quotes it: *Never avenge yourselves… for it is written, "Vengeance is Mine, I will repay, says the Lord"* (Romans 12:19).
- **v18 "smoke that passes, seen today and gone tomorrow"** — *What is your life? For you are a mist that appears for a little time and then vanishes* (James 4:14).
- **v22 "They do not kill and do not make alive… do not uproot and do not plant"** — what the idols cannot do is what Hannah says the LORD does: *The LORD kills and makes alive… The LORD makes poor and makes rich; He brings low and He exalts* (1 Samuel 2:6–7). *To uproot… to build and to plant* is the charge given to Jeremiah (Jeremiah 1:10).
- **v26 "for their swords were drawn *[words lost]*"** — the Ge'ez breaks off at *ʾasyəftihomu la-*, **their swords, of the…**
- **v33 "He said"** — *wa-yəbe*. The verse does not say whose words these are.
- **v37 "in heaven above or on the earth below"** — Moses: *the LORD is God in heaven above and on the earth beneath; there is no other* (Deuteronomy 4:39).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0; vv5 and 8 completed from J. Horovitz's Ge'ez excerpts (1905). English translation by this project.

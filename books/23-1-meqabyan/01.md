# 1 Meqabyan 1

**1** There was a man whose name was Tsirutsaydan, a lover of evil, who boasted in the great number of his horses and in the strength of the army under his hand.

**2** He had many gods, idols, that he bowed down to and served, and he sacrificed to them day and night.

**3** In the folly of his heart he thought that they were the ones who gave him power and strength.

**4** He reckoned in his heart that they gave him authority over all his kingdom.

**5** He thought too that they gave him authority in war, and everything he desired.

**6** He himself sacrificed day and night.

**7** He appointed priests for the gods.

**8** The priests deceived him, and told him that his gods ate day and night; but they themselves ate of that unclean sacrifice.

**9** They also compelled others like themselves to sacrifice and eat; and they themselves sacrificed again, and compelled others to sacrifice in the same way.

**10** He trusted in his idol, which was of no use.

**11** In the blindness of his heart and the smallness of his understanding he thought that they had created him, as they fed him and made him king. For Satan had blinded his heart, so that neither he nor his people would know the God who created him, made him and established him when he did not exist, so that Satan might take him with him into punishment, and they would be punished with him forever, with those he called gods, though they were not gods.

**12** They ought to be called dead, for they have never been alive.

**13** As for the sons of men who trust in them, whose hearts are ashes: the spirit of Satan who leads them astray enters into the idol with them, and speaks to them as their hearts desire, and appears to them as they wish.

**14** When they see that he has done for them what they had in mind, they marvel, and they too do his will, until they slaughter their sons and their daughters who came from their own wombs, and pour out innocent blood, the blood of their sons and their daughters.

**15** It does not trouble them, for Satan has made it sweet to them, as their evil will wanted, to bring them down with him into his own punishment, into Gehenna, from which there is no way out, forever and ever.

**16** This Tsirutsaydan was proud, and he had gods: fifty in the likeness of men, and twenty in the likeness of women.

**17** He boasted in those gods of his, which were of no use, and he honored them greatly, and sacrificed to them evening and morning.

**18** He compelled people to sacrifice, and he himself ate of that unclean sacrifice, and compelled others to eat; and above all he schemed for evil.

**19** He made five houses for his gods, which were hammered out of bronze and iron and lead.

**20** He overlaid them with gold and silver, and made curtains and tents all around his house.

**21** He appointed keepers there, and offered to them forty fattened beasts, the sacrifices he made continually to his gods.

**22** He thought that his gods ate; and he set before them fifty bowls of wine, and fifty silver dishes of fine flour made with oil.

**23** He said to his priests, "Take it and give it to them. Let my gods eat of what I have slaughtered, and drink of all my bowls; and if it is not enough for them, I will add more."

**24** He compelled everyone to eat and drink of that unclean sacrifice.

**25** In his evil cunning he sent his soldiers to search through all his kingdom, to find out whether there was anyone who did not sacrifice or bow down to the gods, and to bring him and punish him before the king with the sword and with fire, and plunder his goods, and burn his house with fire, and take everything he had.

**26** "I will show him torment and punishment if he refuses to worship my gods and sacrifice to them. For they are great and kind, and in their kindness they made us.

**27** They made heaven and earth and the great sea, the sun and the moon and the stars, the winds and the rains, and everything that moves, for our food and to fill us.

**28** Those who do not worship them will be punished with pain and distress, and the gods will have no mercy on them."

## Notes

- **The text** — the Ge'ez of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo printed Bible, and the verse numbers follow it. No other ancient text of the book is known: no Hebrew, Greek or Latin. It is **not** the Greek 1 Maccabees. Its name, *Maqābəyān*, comes from Maccabeus (2:1), but its story and its people are its own.
- **v1 "Tsirutsaydan"** — *Ṣiruṣāydān*, a king known only from this book. The chapter never says which land he rules; his soldiers' fame reaches *all the land of Moab and the Chaldeans* (2:10).
- **v8 "his gods ate"** — the priests' trick is the one in the Greek story of Bel (Daniel 14 in the Greek Daniel): *the priests of Bel came in through secret doors and ate up everything that had been set before Bel* (14:15).
- **v11 "when he did not exist"** — *ʾəmḫaba ʾi-hallawo*, literally "from where he was not": God made him out of nothing. The king thinks the idols made him; the verse answers that he did not exist until God made him.
- **v13 "whose hearts are ashes"** — *ḥamad ləbbomu*. Isaiah 44:20 says the same of the man who bows down to a block of wood: *He feeds on ashes; a deceived heart has led him astray.* The idol itself is empty (v12, *never alive*); what speaks through it is Satan's spirit.
- **v14 "innocent blood, the blood of their sons and their daughters"** — the words of Psalm 106:37–38 on Israel in Canaan: *They sacrificed their sons and their daughters to demons, and poured out innocent blood, the blood of their sons and their daughters, whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan.*
- **vv16, 21, 22 the numbers** — the Ge'ez numerals here are damaged: v16 has a stray mark for *fifty* and *2* for *twenty*; v21 has *4* for *forty*; v22 has stray marks for both *fifties*. The numbers in the verses are the ones the Ethiopian Church reads. **v21 "fattened beasts"** — *ṣabḥāt*; the verse is short here, and the word is taken as the fattened animals of the sacrifice.
- **vv26–28** — the king's speech begins with no *he said*; his soldiers carry it to anyone who will not sacrifice. **v27** gives the idols the works of Genesis 1, sun, moon and stars, sea and *everything that moves* (Genesis 1:21, 9:3), and makes them all for his own table. **v15 "into his own punishment"** — the Ge'ez adds a word, *raʾay*, that does not fit the sentence and is left out.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

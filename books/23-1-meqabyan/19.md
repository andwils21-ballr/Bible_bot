# 1 Meqabyan 19

## Cain and His Daughters

**1** When the sons of Cain multiplied, they made harps and drums, lyres and pipes, and every kind of play and dancing.

**2** Daughters were born to Cain, beautiful and lovely, from the wife of Abel the righteous, whom he killed because of her, for she was lovely. After he killed his brother he took her, and his own wife as well.

**3** He moved away from his father, taking them to the valley of *Qefāz*, toward Arabia; and the daughters of that lovely woman became lovely like their mother.

**4** Because of this the angels came down to the children of Cain, and did not wait a single hour, and took for themselves the wives they chose.

**5** Because of this God was angry with us and with them, for we were drawn with them into error because of their error.

## Adam and Eve

**6** The devil drew our father Adam and our mother Eve into his error, deceiving them, and said to them, "You will be gods, like God your Creator."

**7** In their folly it seemed true to them, and they transgressed the commandment of their Creator, who brought them from where there was nothing into being, so that they would bow down and praise His holy name.

**8** Those who made the gift of God their own, their Lord brought them low, and that proud one too.

**9** As David said, "In the pride of the wicked the poor man is burned"; for by the pride of the devil Adam was condemned with a righteous judgment before God his God.

**10** So too the angels who went astray after the daughters of Cain drew us into their error, and because of this our days were shortened from the days that God gave our fathers.

## Fathers Who Taught Their Children

**11** But they did good, and no evil came near them, for they held their hearts firm toward God, and taught their sons and their daughters not to turn aside from the commandment of God that they had taught them.

**12** If they had done it themselves but not told their sons or taught them, it would have profited them nothing.

**13** As David said, "They did not hide it from their sons, from another generation, but told the praises of God, His might and the wonders He did." As they understood, so they taught their sons, that they too might understand and do it; they entrusted to them the judgment of God, so that they would keep His commandment like their fathers, who pleased God by the beauty of their deeds.

**14** They did not abandon His commandment, which their fathers entrusted to them in their youth, the fear of God and His judgment, as they had learned it from their fathers.

**15** Their sons too learned from their fathers to make their conduct good and to give praise to their Creator, for they kept His commandment and loved Him.

**16** He too hears their prayer and does not disregard them, for He is merciful.

**17** He turns back His wrath again and again, and does not kindle all His punishment.

## Notes

- **v1 "harps and drums, lyres and pipes"** — in Genesis it is Jubal, of Cain's line, who was *the father of all those who play the lyre and pipe* (Genesis 4:21).
- **vv2–3 Cain and Abel's wife** — Genesis gives no reason for the murder but the offering (Genesis 4:3–8). The story that the brothers fought over a woman is told in later writings outside the Bible; this book gives it so. *Qefāz* is a place not otherwise known.
- **v4 "the angels came down to the children of Cain"** — 18:3–7. In 1 Enoch the watchers *came down… on the summit of Mount Hermon* (1 Enoch 6:6).
- **v6 "You will be gods, like God your Creator"** — the serpent: *you will be like God, knowing good and evil* (Genesis 3:5).
- **v9 "In the pride of the wicked the poor man is burned"** — Psalm 10:2 in the Greek, *ἐν τῷ ὑπερηφανεύεσθαι τὸν ἀσεβῆ ἐμπυρίζεται ὁ πτωχός*; the Hebrew has *In arrogance the wicked hotly pursue the poor*.
- **v13 "They did not hide it from their sons"** — *We will not hide them from their children, but tell to the coming generation the glorious deeds of the LORD, and His might, and the wonders that He has done… that they should set their hope in God* (Psalm 78:4–7).
- **v16 "does not disregard them"** — the Ge'ez has *la-ʾəllatomu*, a word not otherwise known; read with the sense of the verse.
- **v17 "He turns back His wrath again and again"** — *Yet He, being compassionate, atoned for their iniquity… He restrained His anger often and did not stir up all His wrath* (Psalm 78:38).
- **vv1–6 Horovitz** — Horovitz's Ge'ez excerpts (1905) have vv1–6 of this chapter and agree with the EOTC text in shape and names (*Cain*, *Qefāz*, *Adam*, *Eve*).
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

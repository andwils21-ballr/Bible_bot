# 1 Meqabyan 26

## The Poor and the Dry Tree

**1** The poor too lie thinking on their beds; and if the rich do not take them in, they become like a dry tree without moisture. Where there is no moisture the root does not sprout, and when there is no root the leaf does not sprout.

**2** If the leaf does not sprout, it bears no fruit, for the leaf serves the budding of the fruit; and whoever has no fruit has no good deed, for the chief thing in a man is the fruit of righteousness.

**3** If he does righteousness, he bears uprightness as fruit, and God rejoices in a man who does righteousness and uprightness.

**4** He grants his prayer to the one who prays, and the labor of his lips, and does not wrong the righteous man for the righteousness he has done,

**5** nor declare the sinner righteous without repentance, for the evil of the deeds he has done. For God is righteous and loves righteousness, and in judgment He shows no favor to the face of the rich or of the poor; for He is the ruler of heaven and earth, and the soul of all is in His hand.

---

## Notes

- **vv1–2 the tree without moisture** — *He is like a tree planted by streams of water that yields its fruit in its season, and its leaf does not wither* (Psalm 1:3). The book turns the likeness to the poor: without the help of the rich, they wither.
- **v2 "the chief thing in a man is the fruit of righteousness"** — *rəʾsu la-bəʾsi fəre ṣədq*, **a man's head is the fruit of righteousness**. *The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life* (Proverbs 11:30).
- **v5 "God is righteous and loves righteousness… no favor to the face of the rich or of the poor"** — *For the LORD is righteous; He loves righteous deeds* (Psalm 11:7); *You shall not be partial to the poor or defer to the great* (Leviticus 19:15).

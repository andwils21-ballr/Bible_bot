# 1 Meqabyan 24

## Gideon and the Word of God

**1** When Gideon trusted in God, with a small army he defeated *[number lost]*, and myriads of soldiers without number, like locusts, and an army of the uncircumcised.

**2** "Do not trust in foreign gods, for there is no other god besides Me, O kings and rulers.

**3** For I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of your mother's womb, raised you, fed you and clothed you. Why then do you make excuses and worship another god besides Me?

**4** All this I did for you. What have you given Me? What do I want from you but the salvation of your soul, that you walk in My law, My ordinance and My commandment? Then I will give you the salvation of your soul,"

**5** says God, who rules all. "Save yourselves from idols, from divination and from sorcerers.

**6** For the punishment of God comes on those who do this, on those who listen to them, on those who do their will, on those who befriend them, and on those who walk in their command.

## A People Who Will Rise against You

**7** For a people will rise against you who do not know you and will not pity you, and they will eat the fruit of your labor, if you do not do the will of God who made you, as His prophets told, from Samuel the prophet on, and as Ezekiel and Enoch told, and the word of Asaph."

**8** He said, "Harsh men will come, changing their clothes, with no other law among them but eating and drinking and adorning themselves with gold and silver, and every deed of robbery that God does not delight in.

**9** They are ready to run to eating and drinking. As soon as they wake from their sleep, from morning to evening they run into evil deeds. For their feet there is no way of peace, only ruin and destruction in their path.

**10** They do not know the way of peace, and there is no fear of God before their eyes: a crooked and harsh people, who have no salt and no faith, greedy, gluttons, drinkers and drunkards. Their offense is lawless, in lewdness and the shedding of blood, theft, deceit and seizing others' goods by wrongdoing.

**11** They are accusers without law or justice, with no fear in their faces, for they do not fear God who created them.

**12** They are not ashamed before the face of a man who sees them, the face of the gray-haired or the face of the old. As soon as they hear of the goods of the world, they make them their own before they see them with their eyes; and once they see them with their eyes, they think they have swallowed them already, for there is no fear of God before their eyes.

**13** Their rulers too are eaters of bribes. What they said in the morning they do not say again in the evening, for they are liars, and there is no longer truth in their mouth.

**14** Their kings too run in haste to evil, for they disregard the cries of the poor and the needy, and do not rescue the guests from the hand of those who oppress them.

**15** Because of this, let kings not be harsh, but let them care for the guest and the oppressed.

**16** But they are tax-gatherers and rebels who drink blood, and do not spare the kid with its mother or the bird with its chicks; for their way is harsh, and all they see and taste they make their own.

**17** They do not pity the poor and the old, but love to gather for themselves and seize others' goods by wrongdoing; all they find they gather, to store in their houses and rejoice in.

## They Vanish Quickly

**18** Woe to their souls when God seizes them in His wrath, because they did not make their conduct good in their life! For they vanish quickly, like one who went out from his house and whose trace is not found, and he does not return to his house.

**19** So they vanish suddenly, all at once, when God disregards them; they perish in their time. For God is not quick to destroy, but is patient, to see whether they will turn to repentance.

**20** If they do not turn to repentance, God destroys them like those of old before them, who did not keep the commandment of God in purity.

**21** They are eaters of human flesh and drinkers of blood, with no fear of God at any time, for they gird themselves with violence to run as soon as they rise from their beds, and do not rest from doing wrong,

**22** from eating and drinking and running into evil and perdition, to destroy the souls of many on the earth."

## Notes

- **v1 "Gideon… defeated *[number lost]*… like locusts… the uncircumcised"** — the Ge'ez has *))*, a numeral lost in the conversion of the text. *The Midianites and the Amalekites and all the people of the East lay along the valley like locusts in abundance, and their camels were without number* (Judges 7:12); *there had fallen 120,000 men who drew the sword* (Judges 8:10). *Qwəlāfān*, **the uncircumcised**, is the word used of the Philistines.
- **v7 "as His prophets told… Ezekiel and Enoch… and the word of Asaph"** — the Ge'ez has *wa-nagafa*, read as *wa-nagara*, **and the word**. Moses: *A nation that you have not known shall eat up the fruit of your ground and of all your labors* (Deuteronomy 28:33). Enoch is counted among the prophets, as in Jude 14.
- **v8 "changing their clothes"** — *ʾənza yəwelləṭu ləbsatomu*. The verse does not say more.
- **v9 "For their feet there is no way of peace"** — *Their feet run to evil… desolation and destruction are in their highways. The way of peace they do not know* (Isaiah 59:7–8; Romans 3:15–17). *Woe to those who rise early in the morning, that they may run after strong drink* (Isaiah 5:11).
- **v10 "no salt and no faith"** — the words of 17:3.
- **v16 "do not spare the kid with its mother or the bird with its chicks"** — the law: *If you come across a bird's nest… with young ones or eggs and the mother sitting on them, you shall not take the mother with the young* (Deuteronomy 22:6); *You shall not boil a young goat in its mother's milk* (Exodus 23:19).
- **v19 "God is not quick to destroy, but is patient"** — *The Lord is not slow to fulfill His promise as some count slowness, but is patient toward you, not wishing that any should perish, but that all should reach repentance* (2 Peter 3:9).

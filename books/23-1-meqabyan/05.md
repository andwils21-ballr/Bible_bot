# 1 Meqabyan 5

## Kings Brought Down

**1** Raʿaytawi was proud, and with one gnat God made his body swell from his feet to his head, and by that small gnat he died.

**2** Again Nimrod was proud, who had a bed of iron made because of his great strength; God destroyed him too.

**3** Again Nebuchadnezzar was proud, and said, "There is no other king besides me. I am the one who makes the sun rise on the earth." Out of his great pride he said this.

**4** God drove him from among men for seven years, and made his portion with the beasts and the birds of the sky, until he knew that it was God who had created him.

**5** When he knew it, God brought him back again into his kingdom. Who has defied God his Creator and been proud against Him, and has not become dust?

**6** Who has transgressed His law and His ordinance, and the earth has not swallowed him?

**7** You too, Tsirutsaydan, want to be proud against your Creator; and you too are bound, like them, to be overthrown for your pride and brought down to the dust of death,

**8** and further, to Sheol, the end of darkness, where there is weeping and gnashing of teeth, below, beneath, into the great and deep pit, from which there is no way out forever.

**9** You are a man, and dust, who will die tomorrow like the proud kings like you, who left the life of this world.

## The Creator of All

**10** We say to you: you are a man and not God; for God is the Creator of the heavens and the earth, and your Creator.

**11** He brings down the proud and lifts up the humble; He gives strength to the weak and weakness to the strong.

**12** He gives death to the living, and raises the dead who lay in the grave and became dust.

**13** He frees the slaves into life from the slavery of sin.

**14** O King Tsirutsaydan, why do you boast in your unclean gods, which are of no use?

**15** God made the heavens and founded the earth, created the great sea, set the sun and moon in place, and ordered the seasons and the years.

**16** Man goes out to his work and labors until evening, and the stars of heaven stand firm at His word.

**17** He counts them all by their names, and nothing happens that God does not know.

**18** He commanded the angels of heaven to serve Him and praise His holy name, and He sends all the angels to those who will inherit life.

**19** The angel Raphael was sent to Tobit, and saved Tobias in the land of Raguel.

**20** An angel was sent to Gideon to tell him how to destroy the foreigners, and an angel was sent to Moses the prophet when he brought Israel across the Red Sea.

**21** For it says: God alone led them, and there was no foreign god with them.

**22** He made them ride on the heights of the land.

**23** He fed them the produce of the fields, and nursed them with honey from the rock, for He loved them greatly.

## What God Asks of a King

**24** To you also He gave kingship over His people, and gave you authority over four kingdoms, to guard the people, to shepherd them in uprightness, and to do the will of the One who created you.

**25** He made you king greatly, so that you would love the Lord your God, for He raised you above all and made you king.

**26** You too ought to love the Lord your God greatly, as He loved you and trusted you over all His flock. Do the will of God, so that your days on the earth may be long and He may be with you,

**27** that He may stand for you against all your enemies, seat you on your throne, and spread His wings over your head.

**28** If you would know it, God chose you as He chose Saul from the sons of Israel and made him king over Israel His people, while he was tending his father's donkeys, and Saul sat on the throne with Samuel the prophet.

**29** God gave him a greater portion than his brothers, and said to him, "From now on God has made you king over His people. Keep His people."

**30** Regard those who do good with good, and those who do evil with evil, for God has set you over them to kill and to keep alive,

**31** to repay those who do good with good and those who do evil with evil. For God has set you over all you wish, so that you may do His will, whether you punish or let live.

**32** You too, do the will of God at every time and every hour, so that He may do your will in all you think of and all you ask, as you humble yourself before Him. For you too are the servant of Him who rules over all, who is above.

**33** No one judges Him; He judges all.

**34** No one appoints Him; He appoints all.

**35** No one overthrows Him; He overthrows all.

**36** No one sets Him free; He sets all free.

**37** No one compels Him; He compels all. No one escapes from His hand, for the judgment of heaven and earth is with Him.

**38** Nothing is hidden before His eyes; everything is uncovered before Him.

**39** He sees all, and no one sees Him. He hears the prayer of the one who prays to Him and accepts his prayer, for He created man in His image and in His likeness.

**40** He feeds all from His storehouse, which never runs short, for He is the King who lives forever.

## Notes

- **v1 "Raʿaytawi… with one gnat"** — *Raʿāytāwi*, a name found only here, and *fəṣṣənt*, taken as **gnat**. The Talmud tells of Titus, who destroyed the Temple, that a gnat entered his brain and killed him (Gittin 56b).
- **v2 "a bed of iron"** — in the Bible the bed of iron is Og's: *his bed was a bed of iron* (Deuteronomy 3:11). Nimrod is *a mighty hunter before the LORD* (Genesis 10:8–9).
- **vv3–5 Nebuchadnezzar** — *You shall be driven from among men, and your dwelling shall be with the beasts of the field… and seven periods of time shall pass over you, until you know that the Most High rules the kingdom of men* (Daniel 4:32). His reason returned and his kingdom was given back (4:36).
- **v6 "and the earth has not swallowed him"** — Korah and his company: *the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them up* (Numbers 16:32).
- **vv7–10 the speaker** — the chapter speaks to the king without naming who speaks: *We say to you* (v10). It goes on to the end of the chapter. **v10 "you are a man and not God"** — the word to the prince of Tyre: *you are a man, and not God* (Ezekiel 28:2).
- **v18 "He sends all the angels to those who will inherit life"** — *Are they not all ministering spirits sent out to serve for the sake of those who are to inherit salvation?* (Hebrews 1:14).
- **v20 "to destroy the foreigners"** — *ʾIloflí*, from the Greek *allophyloi*, **foreigners**, the Greek Bible's word for the Philistines. In Judges, Gideon's enemy is Midian (Judges 6:11–16). The angel at the sea is the one of Exodus 14:19.
- **vv21–23** — the song of Moses: *the LORD alone led him, and there was no foreign god with him. He made him ride on the heights of the land, and he ate the produce of the field, and He made him suck honey from the rock* (Deuteronomy 32:12–13).
- **v36 "No one sets Him free; He sets all free"** — *yəgʿəzo… yəgʿəz*, the verb of v13, *He frees the slaves*.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

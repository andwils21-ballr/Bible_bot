# 1 Meqabyan 34

## Kingdom against Kingdom

**1** The kingdom of the Medes will perish, and the kingdom of Rome will grow very strong over the kingdom of Macedonia, the kingdom of Nineveh and the kingdom of Persia;

**2** and the kingdom of Ethiopia over the kingdom of Alexandria, and the kingdom of Moab over Amalek; for they will rise.

**3** Brother will rise against brother, and God will take vengeance as He has spoken against them, so that they destroy one another.

**4** He said, "Kingdom will rise against kingdom, people against people and country against country."

**5** There will be tumult and killing, famine and plague and earthquake, and the rains will fail; for peace and love have been taken away, and the punishment of God has come down on the earth.

**6** The day of God draws near suddenly, like lightning that appears from the east to the west.

**7** Then each will be repaid according to his deeds, the labor of his hands and the fruit of his deeds; for God Himself has said, "On the day of judgment I will take vengeance on them, in the time when their foot slips, for the day of their reckoning is near."

**8** On that day God will bring the doers of evil who did not walk in His law to an end, into eternal judgment: the people of Judah, Cyprus, Samaria, Damascus and the people of Babylon,

**9** those who live in the islands of Arabia, India, Nubia, Saba, Egypt and Ethiopia, and all who live in them.

## Then They Will Know Me

**10** "Then they will know Me, that I am God, who judges heaven and earth. I am the One who gives honor and favor; I am the One who kills and makes alive.

**11** I am the One who makes the sun rise and sends it to its setting; I am the One who brings good and evil.

**12** I am the One who brings a people you do not know, who will eat the fruit of your labor and the herds of your cattle and your sheep.

**13** They will dash your children before you and take them captive, and you will not be able to save them; and they will eat what you have sweated for and your green things, for you did not fear the commandment of God that you heard, and the Spirit of God was not mingled in you."

**14** Whoever has the Spirit of God dwelling in him knows all things, as Nebuchadnezzar said to Daniel the prophet, "I see that the Spirit of God is in you."

**15** For whoever has the Spirit of God knows all; even what is hidden is uncovered to him, and nothing is hidden from the Spirit of God, but He knows all, the hidden and the open.

**16** We are men, who will die tomorrow, and our deeds will be uncovered, all that we did in secret.

**17** As gold and silver are tested in fire, so sinners will be tested on the last day, for they did not keep the commandment of God.

**18** Then the deeds of all the sons of Israel and of all the nations will be tested.

## Notes

- **vv1–2 the kingdoms** — Daniel's four kingdoms, each rising over the last (Daniel 2:36–45; 7:17–27), here with Rome over Macedonia, Nineveh and Persia, and Ethiopia over Alexandria.
- **vv4–6 "Kingdom will rise against kingdom… like lightning that appears from the east to the west"** — Jesus: *nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom, and there will be famines and earthquakes in various places* (Matthew 24:7); *as the lightning comes from the east and shines as far as the west, so will be the coming of the Son of Man* (24:27).
- **v7** — the word of the song of Moses (Deuteronomy 32:35), quoted in 7:28 as well.
- **v11 "I am the One who brings good and evil"** — *I form light and create darkness; I make well-being and create calamity; I am the LORD, who does all these things* (Isaiah 45:7).
- **vv12–13 "a people you do not know, who will eat the fruit of your labor"** — *A nation that you have not known shall eat up the fruit of your ground… Your sons and your daughters shall be given to another people* (Deuteronomy 28:32–33).
- **v14 "I see that the Spirit of God is in you"** — Nebuchadnezzar to Daniel: *in whom is the spirit of the holy gods* (Daniel 4:8–9).
- **v15 "nothing is hidden from the Spirit of God"** — *the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God* (1 Corinthians 2:10); *nothing is covered up that will not be revealed* (Luke 12:2).
- **v17 "As gold and silver are tested in fire"** — *the fire will test what sort of work each one has done* (1 Corinthians 3:13); *gold that perishes though it is tested by fire* (1 Peter 1:7).

# 1 Meqabyan 25

## God Speaks

**1** "Woe to their souls when I seize them in My wrath," says God, who rules all, "for their way is crooked, and all of them walk in the way of Satan the deceiver.

**2** They do not know the way of God; they have turned it behind their backs and disregarded My law.

**3** I too will repay them according to the evil of their deeds. At the last, on the day of judgment, I will take vengeance on them as the evil of their deeds is written before Me, and they will not escape from My hand; for I am God, who kills and makes alive, who punishes and has mercy.

**4** No one escapes from My hand, beneath the earth or above the heavens, in the sea or the deeps of the waters; for I am God from end to the ends of the world, and the soul of all is in My hand.

**5** I command the serpent beneath the earth, the great fish in the sea, the birds in the air and the *ḥalastəyo* in the desert; from end to end, to the ends of the world, it is Mine.

**6** No one escapes from My hand in heaven above or on the earth below; for I am the One who does wonders and signs before My eyes, and there is none who says to Me, 'Where are You going?' or 'What are You doing?'

**7** I command above the angels and the archangels; every name that is named is Mine; all the birds of the sky, the beasts of the wild and the cattle are Mine."

## The One Who Comes

**8** The south wind will rise, and the heat of the north, and the Red Sea will roar at the greatness of the majesty of Him who comes to it in the last days.

**9** For He will judge the living and the dead, with Nubia and Saba, Ethiopia and India, and all their borders and their bounds.

**10** He shepherds all in gentleness with the rod in His hand. He shepherds His sheep with the rod, for His rod is exalted above all rods.

**11** His horn is heavier than all horns, His kingdom is exalted above all kingdoms, and His authority holds all the world; He is able to do all, and nothing is too hard for Him.

**12** He commands the clouds above and makes grass sprout for the cattle below, and gives fruit on the crop.

**13** He feeds all as He wills, every kind, each with its fruit and its food, all that He created, the *qāḥm* and the *ʾanākwəʿ* beneath the earth, the beasts and the cattle above the earth. He grants his prayer to the one who prays, and does not disregard the petition of widows and orphans.

## The Wealth of the Wicked and of the Righteous

**14** Still more He accepts the prayer of the pure, who pray to Him at all times; for the day of the evil is like a stormy wind, and the gathering of the wrongdoers like a cloud that is gone.

**15** Their joy is like a flying bird, and the beauty of their appearance passes away, with their gold and silver on the earth. Their gold does not profit them; it is only the lamp of the wicked, and the wealth of their clothing is eaten by the moth.

**16** Their stores of grain and wheat are consumed by moth and weevil, and all passes away like a dream of a day that is gone. As a word that has gone out of the mouth does not return, so is the wealth of sinners; like a passing shadow is the beauty of their living, and like a worn garment is the wealth of sinners before God.

**17** But the righteous, if they are rich, God does not disregard; for with their wealth they pity the poor, hear the case of the orphan and the needy, clothe the naked from their own clothing, and do not hold back from the produce of their house, which God gave them to give to the guest and the needy.

**18** They do not pervert the judgment of the faithful, do not withhold the wage of the hired man, and do no wrong in the reckoning of their months or in the measures of their scales; for righteous and honored is the word of God, like one who fills *[words lost]*

## Notes

- **v5 "I command the serpent beneath the earth"** — *If they hide from My sight at the bottom of the sea, there I will command the serpent, and it shall bite them* (Amos 9:3). *Ḥalastəyo*, a creature of the desert, is not otherwise known.
- **v6 "there is none who says to Me, 'What are You doing?'"** — *none can stay His hand or say to Him, "What have You done?"* (Daniel 4:35; Job 9:12).
- **v7 "every name that is named is Mine"** — *far above all rule and authority and power and dominion, and above every name that is named* (Ephesians 1:21); *every beast of the forest is Mine, the cattle on a thousand hills* (Psalm 50:10).
- **v9 "He will judge the living and the dead, with Nubia and Saba, Ethiopia and India"** — *Christ Jesus, who is to judge the living and the dead* (2 Timothy 4:1); *may the kings of Sheba and Seba bring gifts* (Psalm 72:10); *Cush shall hasten to stretch out her hands to God* (Psalm 68:31). *Ḥəndake* is India, as in 28:38, *from India to Ethiopia* (Esther 1:1).
- **v10 "He shepherds His sheep with the rod"** — *Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me* (Psalm 23:4); *He will rule them with a rod of iron* (Revelation 2:27).
- **v13 "the *qāḥm* and the *ʾanākwəʿ* beneath the earth"** — two kinds of creatures living under the ground, not otherwise known.
- **v15 "it is only the lamp of the wicked… eaten by the moth"** — *the lamp of the wicked will be put out* (Proverbs 13:9); *Your riches have rotted and your garments are moth-eaten. Your gold and silver have corroded* (James 5:2–3).
- **v18 "do not withhold the wage of the hired man… the measures of their scales"** — *The wages of a hired worker shall not remain with you all night until the morning… You shall have just balances, just weights* (Leviticus 19:13, 36). The Ge'ez breaks off after *kama malāʿi za-*, **like one who fills…**

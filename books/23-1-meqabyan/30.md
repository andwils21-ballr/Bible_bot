# 1 Meqabyan 30

## God Speaks of Saul

**1** "I hated the house of Saul, for he provoked Me by transgressing My word.

**2** I too disregarded him, and I will not again make a king from his house, for he did not keep My word.

**3** So too with those who do not keep My word and do not walk in My law and My ordinance: I will withhold from them My gift, and My kingdom from their seed, forever.

**4** I regret it, so as not to make them great again, but only to destroy them; for when I made them great, they did not make Me great, and when I honored them, they did not honor Me.

**5** When I did good to them, they did not do good to Me; when I taught them, they would not learn from Me.

**6** For I made them head over all, but they did not make Me head over them; and when I honored them above all, they did not honor Me or keep My commandment.

**7** So I took back the gift I had given them, and I will not give it back to them, as I swore in My wrath. Whoever honors Me I will honor, and whoever loves Me I will love." So says God, who rules all.

**8** "But those who do not honor Me and have not kept My commandment I will strip of the gift I gave them."

## All Is Mine

**9** God, who rules all, says: "Whoever loves Me I love, and whoever makes Me great I make great.

**10** For I am God, who judges all, and no one escapes from My hand in heaven above or on the earth below; for I am God, who kills and makes alive, who punishes and has mercy.

**11** Greatness and honor are Mine. Whom I love I honor, and whom I hate I bring low.

**12** I am the One who has mercy on those who love Me and remember My name at all times. For poor and rich alike, I am the steward of food,

**13** and not for man alone, but for the cattle and the birds, the beasts, the fish in the sea and the serpents,

**14** the great creatures of the sea, the *ḥargaṣāt*, the *ʾəngwatāt*, Behemoth and the *qwarnanʿāt*,

**15** and all that moves in the water and all that flies above the wind: it is Mine.

**16** I am the One who feeds all who seek Me with what is fitting and what they desire."

## Notes

- **v4 "I regret it"** — *ʾənessəḥ*: *I regret that I have made Saul king, for he has turned back from following Me* (1 Samuel 15:11).
- **v5 "when I taught them, they would not learn from Me"** — *maharkəwwomu*, **I taught them**. Written with the other *h*, the verb is *have mercy*; the verse is read with the spelling as it stands.
- **v7 "Whoever honors Me I will honor"** — *for those who honor Me I will honor, and those who despise Me shall be lightly esteemed* (1 Samuel 2:30), the word against the house of Eli.
- **vv13–15 "the steward of food… for the cattle and the birds… Behemoth"** — *These all look to You, to give them their food in due season* (Psalm 104:27); *Behold, Behemoth, which I made as I made you* (Job 40:15). *Ḥargaṣāt*, *ʾəngwatāt* and *qwarnanʿāt* are creatures of the waters not otherwise known.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

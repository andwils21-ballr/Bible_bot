# 1 Meqabyan 33

## Manna and Disregard

**1** "Again, I fed them manna in the wilderness, bread, as David said, 'Man ate the bread of His angels.' All this I did for them, so that they would serve Me in righteousness and uprightness.

**2** But they did not serve Me, and I too disregarded them," says God, who rules all. "They provoked Me and walked in ways that were not Mine.

**3** I too repay them according to their deeds, and according to the work of their hands I disregarded them; for they grew slack in serving Me and did not hold to My ordinance and My counsel. I brought them down into the judgment of punishment from heaven.

**4** On earth too I shorten their days, for they did not keep My commandment, and I am angry with them.

## To the King

**5** You too, king, are you not a man, who dies and decays, who tomorrow will be worms and maggots and ashes?

**6** Yet now you boast and exalt yourself as though you would never die.

**7** You are a man, seen alive today and dead tomorrow," says God, who rules all.

**8** "If you keep My word and My commandment, I will give you the holy city as an inheritance, with the honored kings who did My will, whose dwelling is bright and whose crowns are beautiful, whose thrones of gold and silver are prepared for those who sit on them.

**9** You will rejoice in His city, the dwelling that is near for the doers of good.

**10** But the doers of evil are not worthy to enter that city where the honored kings enter,

**11** for they did not keep My word and My ordinance," says God, who rules all.

## Notes

- **v1 "Man ate the bread of His angels"** — *Man ate of the bread of the angels* (Psalm 78:25), as the Greek reads it; the Hebrew has *the bread of the mighty*.
- **v5 "who tomorrow will be worms and maggots and ashes"** — the word to the king of Babylon: *maggots are laid as a bed beneath you, and worms are your covers* (Isaiah 14:11); *a man, who is a maggot, and the son of man, who is a worm* (Job 25:6).
- **vv6–7 "as though you would never die… seen alive today and dead tomorrow"** — *the king of today will die tomorrow* (Sirach 10:10); *you do not know what tomorrow will bring. What is your life? For you are a mist* (James 4:14), as in 4:18.
- **v8 "the holy city… thrones of gold"** — *the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God* (Revelation 21:2); the dwelling of the good kings in 6:2–10.

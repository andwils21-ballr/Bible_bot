# 1 Meqabyan 11

## The Cities Brought Low

**1** Hermon and its walls have fallen, and those who worship idols in Zebulun and Edom will be brought low then, for God has repaid them according to their evil and the work of their hands. Tyre and Sidon will weep, for the One who will take vengeance on them is near, for their evil and for the idols they made in their youth and did not give up until their old age.

**2** Because of this God will take vengeance on them, for they practiced lewdness, uncleanness, fornication and idols. The daughters of Judah too will be put to shame, for they did not stand in the commandment of God their Creator.

## Jerusalem

**3** So too, at the resurrection of the dead the shame of Jerusalem will be uncovered, for she did not walk in His law and His judgments, but walked in oppression and the killing of the righteous.

**4** On that day God will search her with the lamp of His wisdom, and take vengeance on her for all the evil she did in the days of her youth and did not give up in the days of her prime, until her old age.

**5** She went into the grave and became dust like her fathers of old, who walked in their own will and refused the commandment of God, so that He will take vengeance on them at the resurrection.

**6** For Moses said of them, "Their vine is from the vine of Sodom,

**7** and their branches from Gomorrah. Their clusters are clusters of gall, and their grapes are bitter.

**8** Their wine is the venom of serpents, the deadly venom of asps."

## Notes

- **v1 "Hermon and its walls"** — *ʾArmon wa-ʿarafātihā*. *ʿAraft* is a wall; *ʾArmon* is taken as Hermon (8:33), though the Hebrew *armon* is a **fortress**. The verbs are feminine, said of cities.
- **v3 "oppression and the killing of the righteous"** — *Jerusalem, Jerusalem, the city that kills the prophets and stones those who are sent to it!* (Matthew 23:37).
- **v4 "God will search her with the lamp of His wisdom"** — *At that time I will search Jerusalem with lamps* (Zephaniah 1:12).
- **vv6–8 "Their vine is from the vine of Sodom"** — the song of Moses, word for word: *For their vine comes from the vine of Sodom and from the fields of Gomorrah; their grapes are grapes of poison; their clusters are bitter; their wine is the poison of serpents and the cruel venom of asps* (Deuteronomy 32:32–33). Chapter 12 builds on it.

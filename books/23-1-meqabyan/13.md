# 1 Meqabyan 13

## The Proud One to Come

**1** Tyre and Sidon, and all the borders of the land of Judah that exalt themselves: woe to them now, when I seize them in My wrath, for they will be put to shame and punished.

**2** God says: For from them will come a proud and arrogant one, a son of perdition, an enemy of righteousness, who stiffens his neck and boasts and does not know his Creator; for I have made him an example of My wrath, to show My power on him, says God, who rules all.

**3** This is Capernaum and Samaria and Galilee, Syria and Damascus, Cyprus and *ʾAkyəyā*, and all the region of the Jordan: nations stiff of neck, who walk in their own will, whom darkness and the shadow of death have covered. They do not turn to the fear of God, those whose hearts *Sablānyos* has covered. Do not obey that proud one!

**4** Woe to them then, to those who obey the unclean ones and make sacrifices in their name! For they rebel against God who created them, and become like a beast that has no understanding. For that wicked one, the son of perdition, sets up his image in every place, and rejoices in the work of his hands and in the lust of his soul, and in all robbery, wickedness, wrongdoing and deceit, and in all who practice fornication, wickedness and lies.

**5** In his days it is known that he will do so, for it is numbered by God that he should do this.

**6** The sun will be darkened and the moon will become blood, the stars will fall from heaven, and every work will perish, by the sign that comes in the last days, so that He may judge the earth and all that is in it according to the work of the hands of those who live in it.

**7** At the last, the enemy, death, will overthrow him, for he exalted himself against the creation of God and did as he wished for one hour.

**8** After him there is no authority, "for I will take vengeance," says God, who rules all.

**9** He too will go down into punishment and the judgment kept for him, on the day he is seized in My wrath; for he drags all who are with him into perdition, punishment and ruin. For I am the One who brings out and brings in to the judgment of Gehenna.

**10** Let not the mighty boast in his might, for He gives might and strength to the weak, and again gives weakness to the strong and the mighty.

**11** You will be repaid the vengeance of widows and orphans, for He is the One who holds and avenges those who are oppressed, from the hand of their oppressors.

## The Morning Star

**12** Woe to you who boast and stiffen your neck, and think that He will not seize you or take vengeance on you in My wrath! As it says: "Why has the morning star fallen from heaven, which rises at the time of morning?"

**13** For he said in his pride and boasting, "I will set my throne above the heavens and the stars, and I will be like the Most High."

**14** This you dared in your pride, and did not remember God, who created you and made you with His hands. Why did you exalt yourself, only to go down to the lowest depth in the pride of your heart?

**15** By your pride you became lower than all the angels like you; for they praise their Creator in humility of spirit and do not turn aside from His commandment, for they knew that He made them and created them, and they kept their minds so as not to pass beyond or transgress His commandment.

**16** But you did evil in the pride of your heart and became lower than your fellows, for you are the keeper of all wrongdoing and robbery, falsehood and wickedness, in which sinners like you walk, the wicked who became of your making. They rebel as you do, and walk in all your will and your command, for you teach them robbery.

**17** Woe to you and to those you led astray by your temptation: you will go down together into ruin.

**18** As for you sons of God who went astray after that rebel who leads astray, woe to you! You will go down together into punishment forever, for so you went astray by what he and his demons taught you.

## Israel in the Wilderness

**19** Before this too, while Moses the servant of God was alive, you provoked Him at Horeb, at the waters of testing, at Mount Sinai and at Amalek.

**20** Again, when you sent spies into the land of inheritance, and they said to you, "Its way is long, and their wall is strong, reaching to heaven; their courts are high, and giants live and dwell there," when they told you this, you wept and despised the word of God, wanting to return to the land of Egypt, to your hard slavery.

**21** You did not remember God, who saved you from your distress, who did great things in Egypt and led you by the hand of His angel, overshadowing you with a cloud by day so that the sun would not burn you, and giving you light by night with a pillar of fire so that your feet would not stumble in the darkness.

**22** When Pharaoh and his army frightened you, you cried out and shouted to Moses, and Moses cried out and shouted to God, and by His angel He led you, so that you would not meet Pharaoh's army.

**23** Them He turned back in torment. For it says, God alone led them, and there was no foreign god with them; and their enemies He covered in the sea at once, and did not leave one survivor of them.

**24** Israel He brought across on foot through the middle of the sea, and no harm at all came to them from the Egyptians. He brought them to Mount Sinai, and there He fed them manna forty years.

**25** They grew slack in the worship of God, though He had done so much good for them, for they always provoked God. As it is in the Book of the Generations of the Fathers, it says: "The heart of the sons of men is ashes, and all their works run to wickedness and evil. There is none of them who seeks what is right, only gathering wealth by wrongdoing, swearing falsely, wronging a neighbor, theft and wickedness."

**26** To this evil they all run all the days of their life. The sons of Israel provoked God greatly, transgressors of the commandments of God from first to last.

## Notes

- **vv2, 4 "a son of perdition… sets up his image in every place"** — *walda ḫagwəl*, Paul's name for the one who comes before the day of the Lord: *the man of lawlessness… the son of perdition, who opposes and exalts himself against every so-called god* (2 Thessalonians 2:3–4). The image: *it was allowed to give breath to the image of the beast* (Revelation 13:15).
- **v3 "*ʾAkyəyā*… *Sablānyos*"** — two names not otherwise known: the first, in a list of lands, may be Achaia; the second covers the hearts of the nations, and is not explained.
- **v6 "The sun will be darkened and the moon will become blood"** — *The sun shall be turned to darkness, and the moon to blood, before the great and awesome day of the LORD comes* (Joel 2:31); *the stars will fall from heaven* (Matthew 24:29).
- **v7 "the enemy, death"** — *The last enemy to be destroyed is death* (1 Corinthians 15:26). *For one hour*: the ten kings *receive authority as kings for one hour, together with the beast* (Revelation 17:12).
- **vv12–13 "Why has the morning star fallen from heaven"** — *How you are fallen from heaven, O Day Star, son of Dawn!… You said in your heart, "I will ascend to heaven; above the stars of God I will set my throne on high… I will make myself like the Most High"* (Isaiah 14:12–14). The Ge'ez has *wa-ṣədq*, read as *wadqa*, **has fallen**.
- **v18 "you sons of God who went astray"** — *the sons of God saw that the daughters of man were attractive* (Genesis 6:2); 1 Enoch tells of the watchers who went astray after their leader (1 Enoch 6–8).
- **v20 "the spies… giants live there"** — Numbers 13:28–33, *the people who dwell in the land are strong, and the cities are fortified and very large… we saw the Nephilim there*. The Ge'ez of this verse is damaged in places (*ʿəṣādātihomu nuʿ*, *ʾəlla yārbəh*); *their courts are high* and *giants* are the plainest reading.
- **v24 "manna forty years"** — the Ge'ez has the digit *4*; the number in the verse is the forty years of Exodus 16:35.
- **v25 "The heart of the sons of men is ashes… none of them seeks what is right"** — *every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually* (Genesis 6:5); *there is none who does good, not even one* (Psalm 14:3). The book cited, *maṣḥafa lədata ʾabaw*, is not otherwise known.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

# 1 Meqabyan 16

## The Nations in Their Lands

**1** The people of Tyre and Sidon, and those who live on the shore of the sea and beyond the river Jordan, and Gilead and Haran, the Canaanites, the Jebusites and the Girgashites, and Edom and the people of Amalek,

**2** and all the nations in their countries, by their tribes, by their ordinances, by their borders and by the bounds of their lands: all of them are as God has ordained them.

**3** Among them are some whose conduct is good and who know God.

**4** Among them are some whose deeds are evil and who do not know God, who created them and made them His own, as they did under Ameneser king of Syria.

**5** For he will take the strength of Damascus, and divide the plunder of Samaria before the king of Egypt.

**6** The mountains of Gilead and the valleys of Gilboa, all Samaria and *Ṗāṭos* of the land of Judah, and those who live in the mountains of Arabia, and Media, *Ṗartus*, *Siwsigyā* and Cappadocia,

**7** and those who live within their borders, peoples stiff of neck, who do not know God and have not kept His commandment:

**8** He will repay them according to the evil of their deeds and the work of their hands.

## Against the City of God

**9** For the region of Caesarea, the people of Gilead and Amalek join together to fight against the city of God, which is full of righteousness, in which the Holy One of Israel is praised, the God of Israel, the God of hosts, who sits above the chariot of the cherubim, before whom stand ten thousand times ten thousands and thousands of thousands, who serve Him in fear and trembling.

## Notes

- **v2 "by their tribes… by the bounds of their lands"** — the table of nations: *These are the sons of Shem, by their clans, their languages, their lands, and their nations* (Genesis 10:31); *When the Most High gave to the nations their inheritance… He fixed the borders of the peoples* (Deuteronomy 32:8).
- **v4 "Ameneser king of Syria"** — *ʾAmenesér*, a name not otherwise known. Shalmaneser, king of Assyria, took Samaria (2 Kings 17:3–6).
- **v5 "take the strength of Damascus, and divide the plunder of Samaria"** — Isaiah: *the wealth of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria will be carried away before the king of Assyria* (Isaiah 8:4). Here it is *the king of Egypt*.
- **v6 the lands** — *Ṗāṭos*, *Ṗartus* and *Siwsigyā* are not otherwise known; *Ṗartus* may be Parthia, named with Media and Cappadocia in the list of Acts 2:9.
- **v9 "join together to fight against the city of God"** — *They lay crafty plans against Your people… Gebal and Ammon and Amalek, Philistia with the inhabitants of Tyre* (Psalm 83:3–7). *Ten thousand times ten thousand stood before Him* (Daniel 7:10); *who sits above the cherubim* (Psalm 80:1), *the chariot of the cherubim* (1 Chronicles 28:18).

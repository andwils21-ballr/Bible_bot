# 1 Meqabyan 27

## The Days of Creation

**1** He created all the world from nothing. He prepared and set in place the mountains and the hills, made the earth firm on the water, and hedged it with sand so that it would not shake. For the first word of God said, "Let there be light."

**2** There was light, while the world was darkness. God did His work, prepared and established the world as was fitting and right, and said, "Let there be evening and darkness."

**3** Again God said, "Let there be light and morning," and there was light. He raised the upper water above the heaven.

**4** He spread the heaven like a tent cloth and propped it up with the wind, and the lower water He settled beneath the depths of the sea.

**5** He shut the openings of the sea with sand and made them firm by His Spirit, so that they would not sink into the depths below; and He settled in it creatures and beasts, great creatures in it without number, those that are seen and those that are not seen.

**6** On the third day God made plants on the dry land, all grass and trees of every kind, and fruits that bear fruit,

**7** and the tree of life, good to look at and sweet to eat, and grass and every green thing of the earth with its seed in it. He appointed food for all the cattle and the beasts and the birds, and gave nourishment to all He created.

**8** There was evening and there was morning, and on the fourth day God said, "Let there be light in the firmament of heaven." God made the sun and the moon and the stars and set them in the firmament of heaven, to give light on the earth and to rule it by day and by night.

**9** From then on the sun and the moon and the stars took their turns by day and by night.

**10** On the fifth day God created all the creatures and beasts that live in the waters, and all the birds that fly above in the air, and all that is seen and not seen. All this God created.

**11** On the sixth day He created all the beasts and cattle and creeping things; and when He had created all and set it in order, He created Adam in His image and in His likeness.

**12** He handed over to him all the beasts and cattle He had created, to reign over them, and also all the cattle and beasts, the fish and the great creatures in the sea,

**13** the sheep and all the oxen on the earth, and the creatures seen and unseen.

## The Garden and the Tree

**14** He set the son of man, whom He made in His image and in His likeness, in the garden, and commanded him to eat and to work the garden and to praise God there,

**15** and not to transgress the commandment of God, and not to eat from the tree that makes known good and evil, the tree of death.

**16** For He said, "On the day you eat from this tree you will surely die."

**17** By the deceit of the serpent our mother Eve was led astray, and she ate from that tree and gave it to our father Adam.

**18** He ate from that tree and brought death on himself and on his children.

**19** God was angry with our father Adam, for he transgressed His commandment and ate from the tree God had commanded him not to eat from. He drove him from the garden and gave him this land of thorns and thistles, which He cursed because of him when he broke His commandment, to work it and eat the reward of his labor by the sweat of his face.

**20** When God brought him out into this land, Adam turned to grief and sorrow of heart, and began to work the land and to eat by the sweat of his face, in toil and labor.

## Notes

- **vv1–13 the days** — the order of Genesis 1, with the book's own details: the earth *hedged with sand*, the heaven *spread like a tent cloth* (*Who stretches out the heavens like a tent*, Psalm 104:2), and the sea's bounds: *I placed the sand as the boundary for the sea, a perpetual barrier that it cannot pass* (Jeremiah 5:22).
- **v3 "the upper water"** — the Ge'ez has *la-tāḥtāy māy*, **the lower water**, here and in v4. The waters raised above the heaven are the upper waters (*the waters that were above the expanse*, Genesis 1:7), and v4 places the lower water beneath the sea; v3 is read as *the upper*.
- **v10 "that live in the waters"** — the Ge'ez has *wəsta samāyāt*, **in the heavens**, read as *wəsta māyāt*, **in the waters**, a difference of one letter: the fifth day is the day of the creatures of the waters and the birds (Genesis 1:20–23).
- **v15 "the tree that makes known good and evil, the tree of death"** — *of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil you shall not eat* (Genesis 2:17).
- **v19 "this land of thorns and thistles… by the sweat of his face"** — *cursed is the ground because of you… thorns and thistles it shall bring forth for you… By the sweat of your face you shall eat bread* (Genesis 3:17–19).

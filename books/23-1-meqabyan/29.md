# 1 Meqabyan 29

## Out of Egypt

**1** Even when Egypt oppressed the sons of Israel with mortar and the making of bricks, with every kind of work they forced on them, and with bricks without straw,

**2** and set taskmasters over them and made them work, they cried out to God to save them from all their brickmaking for Egypt.

**3** On that day He sent them help, Moses and Aaron, and they saved them from the work of the bricks; for God sent them to bring His people out of the house of slavery of Pharaoh king of Egypt, who in his pride refused to let the people go, so that they would not serve God and sacrifice to Him in the wilderness.

**4** For God disregards the proud, and because of Pharaoh's pride God drowned him with his army in the Red Sea.

**5** So too with those who disregard the word of God while they are kings and rulers, and do not do well in all that He appointed them and made them king for: to do His will, to give their wage to those who serve well, and to honor His great name.

## God Speaks to Kings

**6** "If they set My kingdom right, I too will set their kingdom right," says God, who rules all.

**7** "Do good to Me, and I will do good to you. Keep My commandment, and I will keep your soul. Walk in My commandment, and I will walk as your heart desires.

**8** Love Me, and I will love your life. Take refuge in Me, and I will be your strength.

**9** Trust in Me, and I will save you," says God, who rules all.

**10** "Do not walk crookedly. Draw near to Me, and I will draw you near, for I love straight paths," says God, who rules all. "Cleanse your hands, you sinners and you perverse, and put away the evil of your heart.

**11** I too will put away My wrath from you and turn to you with compassion and mercy.

**12** I will remove far from you the enemy, the rebels who do wrong, as I saved David My servant from the many trials his enemy brought on him, from the hand of Goliath the giant, from the hand of Saul, who sought to kill him, and from the hand of Absalom his son, who wanted to take his kingdom.

**13** So too I saved those who do My will, keep My commandment and are patient in My law. I gave them honor as an inheritance and made them reign above all, so that they may rejoice in this world and in the world to come."

## Samuel

**14** They will be partners with the blessed kings who pleased God by the beauty of their life, like Samuel, whom God chose while he was still a child, from his youth, for the beauty of his life.

**15** God spoke to him so that he would tell Eli, the old priest; and good were the deeds of Samuel the prophet while he served in the house of the Lord God.

**16** When he grew up in the house of God, serving there, God made him one to rule the people and to anoint kings at the command of God. He put the anointing oil of the kingdom into his hand, since he did the will of God his God, for he pleased Him, to make kings over the people God had chosen from the sons of Israel.

**17** While Saul was still reigning, God said to Samuel the prophet, "Go, anoint David son of Jesse, of the house of Judah, for I am pleased with him."

## Notes

- **v1 "bricks without straw"** — *ṭəbyāt za-ʾənbala ḫaśar*: *You shall no longer give the people straw to make bricks… let them go and gather straw for themselves* (Exodus 5:7).
- **vv7–9 "Do good to Me, and I will do good to you"** — the book's own pattern of command and promise. *Those who honor Me I will honor* (1 Samuel 2:30).
- **v10 "Draw near to Me, and I will draw you near… Cleanse your hands, you sinners"** — *Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double-minded* (James 4:8); *Return to Me, says the LORD of hosts, and I will return to you* (Zechariah 1:3).
- **v12 "Goliath the giant"** — *Golyād raʿāytāwi*: the word of 5:1, 15:11 and 18:2, said of the giant David killed (1 Samuel 17).
- **v15 "so that he would tell Eli"** — the word of the LORD that came to the boy Samuel in the night, against Eli's house (1 Samuel 3:1–18).
- **v17 "Go, anoint David son of Jesse… for I am pleased with him"** — *Fill your horn with oil, and go. I will send you to Jesse the Bethlehemite, for I have provided for Myself a king among his sons* (1 Samuel 16:1).

# 1 Meqabyan 21

## David, Hezekiah and Manasseh

**1** David trusted in God, and God became his refuge, for he trusted in Him, and He saved him from the hand of Saul the king.

**2** From Absalom his son, from the foreigners, from the Amalekites and the Edomites, from the *Qarneʾon*, and from all these who opposed him God saved David, for he trusted in Him and kept His law.

**3** But the evil kings who did not trust in Him, God did not save, and they were defeated by the hand of their enemy; for victory is from God.

**4** Hezekiah trusted in God, and He saved him from the hand of the proud Syrian.

**5** But Manasseh his son was defeated by the hand of his enemy, for he did not put his trust in God. The enemies who defeated Manasseh were not as he was; they bound him and took him to their country, because he did not put his trust in God or fear the One who had honored him, raised him up and made him great.

**6** At once He withheld from him the gift He had given him, for he did not make his way good before God his God, who would have lengthened his days, stood against his enemy for him, and been to him strength and might, wholeness and a rear guard.

**7** For trusting in God is better than a great army, and better than trusting in horses, shield and bow.

**8** Whoever trusts in God will live, and be honored, and be greatly exalted.

**9** But those who did not trust in God, and trusted in their great wealth, became outcasts from the grace He had given them; for God does not show favor to persons.

**10** Those who trust in Him He keeps; those who make a fool of Him He makes fools of, and He is not quick to help them in their distress when their enemies oppose them, for they did not make their hearts steadfast in following God and keeping the commandments of God.

**11** But whoever is steadfast in following God and His commandments and keeping His law, He becomes a refuge to him in his distress.

## What God Gives a King Who Loves Him

**12** He makes him rejoice in the killing of his enemy, in the plunder of his enemies' cattle and in captives from his enemy's country, in the rain of his rainy seasons, the fruit of his fields, the fullness of his granaries and the fruits of his gardens,

**13** in spring and autumn, in grass and green plants, each in its season, so that your people under your hand may rejoice,

**14** and eat the labor of others, and be filled with the stores of their enemies, with cattle and oxen and sheep; and eat the labor of others, and take captive the children of their enemies.

**15** This God does for the one who loves Him; but the one who hates Him He makes a prey for his enemy.

**16** He binds his hands and his feet and gives him into the hand of his enemy, and makes him a mockery to his enemies, and does not let him rejoice in the seed of his house; for he was a breaker of God's commandment and a shedder of blood.

**17** He will not stand in the judgment, and God repays the fruit of their deeds to those who do evil, to the doers of evil, when He repays evil.

**18** As for the doers of good, the king is commanded by God to repay them good by his own hand, and to keep them,

**19** and to keep the commandment himself, for he is a servant set over all that God created, to do good. God gives them eternal life, and they praise God who created them; and of all He created, none transgresses His commandment but man alone.

## Man Alone Transgresses

**20** All of them know Him and keep to His commandment and His ordinance, as God commanded all of them, who live each in its order.

**21** But man transgresses against the One who made him king over all the beasts and cattle, and over all the birds that God created,

**22** whether in the sea or on dry land. All that God created He handed over to our father Adam, to do with as he wished, to eat them like the grass of the earth, to possess them and make them serve him; whether beasts or cattle, God gave them to obey man. As for men who reign, they are to obey God, in the honor He gave them in setting them over all He created, and are to be loyal to Him and obey Him.

**23** If they transgress His commandment, He strips them of the gift He gave them and gives it to one who does His will, for He is the ruler of heaven and earth.

**24** He appoints whom He wishes and removes whom He wishes; He kills and makes alive, punishes and has mercy.

**25** There is no other like Him, and no one is master over Him, for He is Lord of all He created, and there is no other god besides Him in heaven above or on the earth below.

**26** He appoints and removes, kills and makes alive, punishes and has mercy, makes poor and makes rich.

**27** He accepts the petition of those who ask Him; and for those who do His will with a pure heart, He hears their prayer and does their will in all they ask Him.

**28** He commands for them small and great, and all that is theirs in the mountains and hills, the caves and forests, the hollows of the earth, the sea and the dry land.

**29** All of it belongs to those who do the will of their God. He does not deprive them of His blessing, and He gives them the reward of their praise.

**30** In heaven too He gives them the honor He prepared for their fathers, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, David, Hezekiah and Samuel, who did not transgress the commandment and the judgment of God,

**31** so that He may give them the inheritance He swore to their fathers, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, who pleased Him from of old, and that they may rejoice in His kingdom.

## Notes

- **v2 "from the foreigners… from the *Qarneʾon*"** — *ʾElofəlāwyān*, the Philistines (5:20). *Qarneʾon* is a people not otherwise known.
- **v4 "the proud Syrian"** — *Soryāwi*, the king of Assyria, Sennacherib (2 Kings 19:35–36).
- **vv5–6 Manasseh** — *the LORD brought upon them the commanders of the army of the king of Assyria, who captured Manasseh with hooks and bound him with chains of bronze and brought him to Babylon* (2 Chronicles 33:11). Chronicles goes on to his prayer and return; this book does not. *Were not as he was*, *ʾi-konu kamāhu*, is literal; the sense of the clause is uncertain.
- **v7 "better than trusting in horses, shield and bow"** — *Some trust in chariots and some in horses, but we trust in the name of the LORD our God* (Psalm 20:7); *The war horse is a false hope for salvation* (Psalm 33:17).
- **v10 "those who make a fool of Him He makes fools of"** — *yāstaʿabbədəwwo yāstaʿabbədomu*, the verb said twice. *With the crooked You make Yourself seem tortuous* (Psalm 18:26).
- **vv18–19 "the king is commanded by God to repay them good… a servant set over all"** — *he is God's servant for your good… an avenger who carries out God's wrath on the wrongdoer* (Romans 13:4).
- **v20 "who live each in its order"** — 1 Enoch: *Observe everything that takes place in the sky, how the lights… do not change their paths… But you have not persevered, nor observed the law of the Lord* (1 Enoch 2:1; 5:4).
- **v22 "He handed over to our father Adam"** — *have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over every living thing* (Genesis 1:28); *as I gave you the green plants, I give you everything* (Genesis 9:3).
- **v30 "who did not transgress"** — the Ge'ez has *ʾəlla yətʿaddawu*, **who transgress**, without the negative that the verse requires; read as *ʾəlla ʾi-yətʿaddawu*.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

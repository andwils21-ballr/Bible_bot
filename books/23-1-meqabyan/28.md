# 1 Meqabyan 28

## Cain, Seth and Noah

**1** After that they lived, and Adam's children multiplied. Among them were those who praised God and honored Him and did not transgress His commandment.

**2** There were prophets too, who prophesied what had been and what would be; and among the children were sinners who lied and did wrong. Cain, Adam's firstborn, was harsh, and killed his brother Abel.

**3** God was very angry with Cain because he killed his brother Abel; and with the earth too God was angry, because it drank his blood.

**4** To Cain God said, "Where is Abel your brother?" Cain said in the pride of his heart, "Am I the keeper of Abel my brother?"

**5** Abel was righteous, and Cain became a sinner by killing his brother Abel the righteous.

**6** Again Seth the righteous was born; for Adam fathered three daughters and four sons, and among them were the good and the evil.

**7** Among them were prophets and righteous ones, and among them sinners and the perverse.

**8** Among them were the good and blessed, who did the will of their father Adam and all that he told Seth his son. So it went from Adam to Noah the righteous, who kept the commandment of God.

**9** He too entrusted to his sons and told them the will of God, and they told their sons and entrusted to them the will of God, as their father Noah had told them, so that they would not break the commandment of God.

**10** They lived teaching those who came after them, their sons.

## Abraham, Isaac and Jacob

**11** But Satan fought against those who said yes to him, and spoke to them from within the idols at the tombs of their fathers, the carved images; and they did all that the teacher of evil commanded them.

**12** They worshiped idols according to their custom, until Abraham the righteous came forth, who did the will of God.

**13** God made a covenant with him in fire and storm, for he was the first of his kin to walk in the way of righteousness.

**14** God swore to him to give the land of inheritance to him and to his seed forever.

**15** So too with Isaac He made a covenant, to give him the inheritance of his father Abraham; and with Jacob too He made a covenant, to give him the inheritance of Isaac his father.

**16** Their seed after them, the sons of Jacob, He made kings and priests from two tribes of the people of Israel. He blessed them and said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply greatly."

**17** He gave them the inheritance of their fathers; yet they did not stop provoking God, while He fed them and loved them in all things.

**18** When He struck them down, then they sought Him, and turned and rose early to seek God; for God loves them and has mercy on them,

**19** not for their own sake but for their fathers', for He is merciful and compassionate to all He created.

**20** He opens His right hand in blessing to satisfy the hungry soul, and opens His eye for mercy, to multiply grain for food.

**21** He gives food to the young ravens and to all who call on Him; and the sons of Israel too He saves from the hand of their adversaries and enemies when they cry out to Him.

## The Saviors He Sent

**22** Again they turn to provoke Him, and He sends against them the nations, their enemies around them, who destroy them and kill them and take them captive.

**23** Again they cry out to God with groaning and tears, and He sends them help: at times He saved them by the hand of the prophets,

**24** and at times by the hand of the judges. When they provoked God, He made them serve their enemies, who plundered them and took them captive.

**25** David arose and saved them from the hand of the foreigners; and again they provoked God, and He sent against them those who oppressed them.

**26** At one time He saved them by the hand of Jephthah; and again they forgot God who had saved them, and He sends against them evil enemies who afflict them, take them captive and plunder them, for God repaid them.

**27** Again they cried out to Him when they were oppressed, and He saved them by the hand of Gideon; and again they provoked God with the work of their hands.

**28** He too sent again those who afflicted them, and they turned back and wept and cried out to God.

**29** Again He saved them by the hand of Samson, and they rested a little while; and they rose up to provoke God with the evil they had done before.

**30** He too sent them others again, who oppressed them; and again they cried out and wept to God to send them help, and He saved them by the hand of Deborah and Barak.

**31** Again they lived a few days worshiping God; and again they forgot Him and provoked God with their former evil.

**32** He too sent them others who oppressed them, and again He saved them by the hand of Judith. Again they rested a little, and rose up to provoke God with their evil as before.

**33** He too raised up nations to rule over them; and they groaned and cried out to God, and He saved them by the hand of Mattathias and his sons, at the corner of the house.

**34** For he cut off the head of the giant Akemelek, who had come to make war on the land of Judah. When that giant died, his army was scattered and fled, and the sons of Israel killed them as far as the Jabbok, and left none.

## In the Land of the Chaldeans

**35** After that they rested a little, and rose up to provoke God; and He sent them nations to rule them. Again they cried out and groaned to God, and He disregarded their weeping and groaning, for at all times they provoked God and forgot His commandment.

**36** The nations took them captive and carried them to the land of the Chaldeans, with their priests.

**37** There too the perverse sons of Israel did not stop provoking God, practicing uncleanness and idols, and they mingled with the nations who had taken them captive.

**38** There too God was angry at their evil, to destroy them at once. He put wrath into Artaxerxes king of the Chaldeans, so that ten thousand denarii would be brought into the king's treasuries, and so that he would judge them on a day that was fixed, and none of their seed would remain in the land of Persia, from India to Ethiopia.

**39** So a letter was written by the king's authority, and he gave the seal that was in his hand, so that it would reach every city of Persia,

**40** and they would kill them on one day, as the king commanded, the day on which he wanted to uproot them; and their plunder, the gold and the silver, he commanded to be brought into the king's treasuries.

**41** On that day, when the sons of Israel heard it, they cried out and wept and groaned to God, and told Mordecai, and Mordecai told Esther.

**42** Esther said, "Fast and pray and cry out to God, the God of Israel, the whole assembly of the sons of Israel, wherever you are."

**43** Mordecai put on sackcloth and put ashes on his head, and fasted; and they prayed and repented wherever the sons of Israel were.

**44** Esther too mourned greatly. Though she was queen, she put on sackcloth, spread ashes, loosened her hair, and did not anoint herself with perfume as the queens of Persia do; and she wept and cried out to God from the depth of her heart, to the God of her fathers.

**45** Because of this God gave her favor before Artaxerxes king of Persia, and she made a great feast to the God of her fathers.

**46** The king and Haman came into the feast Esther had made, and there God repaid Haman as he had wished to do to Mordecai, and they hanged him on a high pole.

**47** A letter of the king was ordered, that they should leave the Jews alone wherever they were, in all that they wished, and not make them serve, nor rob them, nor wrong them, nor take their goods,

**48** nor destroy their cities, nor plunder their cattle; and those who loved them honored them, wherever they were in all the land of Persia. For so God has mercy on Israel when they ask and cry out to Him in repentance.

**49** When they provoke Him, He sends to them nations who oppress them; on that day they weep and groan and cry out for Him to send them help and save them from the hand of those who afflict them.

## Notes

- **v6 "three daughters and four sons"** — the Ge'ez has the digits *3* and *4*. In this text larger numbers are often cut to a digit (1:21), so these may be shortened; they are rendered as they stand. Genesis says only that Adam *fathered other sons and daughters* (Genesis 5:4).
- **v13 "a covenant with him in fire and storm"** — *a smoking fire pot and a flaming torch passed between these pieces. On that day the LORD made a covenant with Abram* (Genesis 15:17–18).
- **vv18–21** — *When He killed them, they sought Him; they repented and sought God earnestly* (Psalm 78:34); *You open Your hand; You satisfy the desire of every living thing* (Psalm 145:16); *He gives to the beasts their food, and to the young ravens that cry* (Psalm 147:9).
- **vv22–32 the saviors** — the round of Judges, *the LORD raised up judges, who saved them out of the hand of those who plundered them* (Judges 2:16), told in the book's own order: David before Jephthah, Gideon, Samson, Deborah and Barak, and then Judith.
- **vv33–34 "Mattathias and his sons… the giant Akemelek"** — Mattathias is the father of Judas Maccabeus (1 Maccabees 2:1–5). *Akemelek*, the giant (*raʿāytāwi*, 5:1), is not named elsewhere. The Ge'ez verb at the head of v34, *heṗa*, is not otherwise known; *cut off* is the plain sense with *the head of the giant*. The verse ends without its stop: *wa-ʾi-yātrafu*, **and they left none**.
- **vv38–46 Esther** — the story of Esther as the Greek tells it: the king is *Artaxerxes* (Greek Esther 1:1), and Esther *took off her splendid apparel… and instead of costly perfumes covered her head with ashes* (Greek Esther 14:2, English numbering). Here he is called *king of the Chaldeans* (v38) and *king of Persia* (v45). *Ten thousand denarii*: in Esther Haman offers *ten thousand talents of silver* (Esther 3:9).
- **v46 "a high pole"** — *diba ʿəḍ nawwāḫ*, the *pole fifty cubits high* of Esther 5:14 and 7:9–10.
- **Source text** — Ge'ez: Beta Masaheft, Universität Hamburg (text of the EOTC printed Bible), CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation by this project.

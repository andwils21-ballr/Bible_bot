# 1 Meqabyan 9

## Without the Rain of Mercy

**1** The valleys of Gilboa and the mountains of Lebanon, if the rain of mercy does not come down on them from God, do not sprout grass for the cattle and the beasts.

**2** The mountains of Gilead and the valleys of Elam give no green grass to the goats and the flocks of rams that are seen there, or to the wild animals, the ibex, the wild oxen and the deer.

**3** So too the rebels and the heretics, who of old took up rebellion and error: if the dew of mercy does not come down on them from God, the dead will not rise. So too the sorcerers and the sinners, *Qoṗros* and *Dimāso*, the slanderers,

**4** and those who sit at divination and spells, and the murderers,

**5** and those who fornicate without law, and the *ʾAtnāwyān* and the Medes, who trust in their idols and think of them and delight in them with drum and lyre and harp,

**6** who act like madmen with their idols and fornicate with the work of their hands, which cannot save themselves: these will be judged on the day of judgment, at the resurrection.

## The Horn of Michael

**7** O fool, poor of mind, do you think that the dead will not rise?

**8** Do not think so, for you will not sleep there when the horn is blown at the resurrection of the dead by the mouth of Michael the archangel.

**9** The mountains and the hills will be brought low, the rivers and the depths will be leveled, and they will become a straight road.

**10** There will be resurrection for all flesh.

## Notes

- **v1 "The valleys of Gilboa… if the rain of mercy does not come down on them"** — David's lament for Saul: *You mountains of Gilboa, let there be no dew or rain upon you* (2 Samuel 1:21). The chapter goes on from 8:33–35, the cedar that withers without the dew of Hermon.
- **v2 "the ibex, the wild oxen and the deer"** — *wa-yəṭal wa-torāt wa-ḫayyālāt*, three wild animals of the hills.
- **v3 "*Qoṗros* and *Dimāso*"** — two names in a list of kinds of sinners. *Qoṗros* is the Ge'ez for Cyprus (13:3); *Dimāso* is not otherwise known. What the names stand for here is not said.
- **v5 "the *ʾAtnāwyān*"** — a people named with the Medes, not otherwise known; perhaps the Athenians.
- **v8 "when the horn is blown… by the mouth of Michael the archangel"** — *the Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a cry of command, with the voice of an archangel, and with the sound of the trumpet of God. And the dead in Christ will rise first* (1 Thessalonians 4:16); *the trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised* (1 Corinthians 15:52).
- **v9 "The mountains and the hills will be brought low"** — *Every valley shall be lifted up, and every mountain and hill be made low… and the rough places a plain* (Isaiah 40:4).

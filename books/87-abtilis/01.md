# Abtilis 1

**1** A bishop shall be ordained by two or three bishops.

---

## Notes

- **The whole book** — worked from the English of Henry Tattam, *The Apostolical Constitutions, or Canons of the Apostles, in Coptic* (1848), the Seventh Book, which is translated from the **Coptic**. No Ge'ez or other source language was used. The Coptic is a sister version of this book: Abtilis is the Ethiopic's longer form of the Apostolic Canons, and the Ethiopic differs from the Coptic in wording and in places in order. The chapters follow Tattam's numbering of the 85 Apostolic Canons; the Ethiopic counts 81, so its numbers will not match these. Each verse is one of Tattam's paragraphs.
- **v1 two or three bishops** — no single bishop can make another. The shorter Ethiopic collection, Gitzew, joins this rule and the next into its first canon: *A bishop shall be ordained by two or by three bishops, but an elder and a deacon and the rest… by one bishop alone* (Gitzew 1). Here they stand as two canons (Abtilis 1 and 2).
- **Source text** — Henry Tattam, *The Apostolical Constitutions, or Canons of the Apostles, in Coptic, with an English Translation* (1848), public domain. English translation by this project.

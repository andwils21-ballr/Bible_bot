# Haggai 1

## This House Lies in Ruins

**1** In the second year of Darius the king, in the sixth month, on the first day of the month, the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, saying,

**2** "This is what the LORD of hosts says: These people say, 'The time has not come, the time for the house of the LORD to be built.'"

**3** Then the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet, saying,

**4** "Is it a time for you yourselves to live in your paneled houses, while this house lies in ruins?

**5** So now, this is what the LORD of hosts says: Consider your ways.

**6** You have sown much and brought in little; you eat, but never have enough; you drink, but never have your fill; you clothe yourselves, but no one is warm; and he who earns wages earns them for a bag with holes.

**7** This is what the LORD of hosts says: Consider your ways.

**8** Go up to the hill country, bring wood, and build the house, so that I may take pleasure in it and be glorified, says the LORD.

**9** You looked for much, and it came to little; you brought it home, and I blew it away. Why? declares the LORD of hosts. Because of My house, which lies in ruins, while each of you runs to his own house.

**10** Therefore, because of you, the heavens have held back the dew, and the earth has held back its produce.

**11** I have called for a drought on the land and on the hills, on the grain, the new wine and the oil, on what the ground brings forth, on man and on beast, and on all the labor of hands."

## The People Obey

**12** Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all the remnant of the people obeyed the voice of the LORD their God and the words of Haggai the prophet, as the LORD their God had sent him; and the people feared before the LORD.

**13** Then Haggai, the LORD's messenger, spoke to the people with the LORD's message: "I am with you, declares the LORD."

**14** The LORD stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people; and they came and did the work on the house of the LORD of hosts, their God,

**15** on the twenty-fourth day of the month, in the sixth month, in the second year of Darius the king.

## Notes

- **v1 "In the second year of Darius… through Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel… and to Joshua son of Jehozadak"** — the work on the house had stopped *until the second year of the reign of Darius king of Persia* (Ezra 4:24), and Ezra names this prophet: *Haggai the prophet and Zechariah son of Iddo, prophesied to the Jews who were in Judah and Jerusalem* (Ezra 5:1). *Be-yad*, word for word **by the hand of**, is **through**. *Chaggai*, **Haggai**, has the letters of *chag*, **feast**. Joshua the high priest is Ezra's *Jeshua son of Jozadak* (Ezra 3:2), and his father is the *Jehozadak* who *went into exile* (1 Chronicles 6:15). The book dates each word to the day (1:1, 15; 2:1, 10, 20).
- **vv2–4 "The time has not come… Is it a time for you yourselves to live in your paneled houses?"** — *et*, **time**, three times: they say it is not the time for the LORD's house, and the LORD asks if it is the time for theirs. *Sefunim*, **paneled**, is the word of the king who builds his own house *paneling it with cedar* (Jeremiah 22:14). *Charev*, **in ruins**, comes back in v9, and sounds like *chorev*, the **drought** of v11.
- **vv5–7 "Consider your ways… you eat, but never have enough… a bag with holes"** — *simu levavkhem al darkheikhem*, word for word **set your heart on your ways**, twice. The list is the curse of the law come true: *You will carry much seed out to the field and gather in little* (Deuteronomy 28:38), and Hosea's *They will eat but not be satisfied* (Hosea 4:10). *Ha-mistakker mistakker*, **he who earns wages earns them**, the hired man's pay going into a purse that will not hold it.
- **vv8–9 "so that I may take pleasure in it and be glorified… I blew it away"** — *ve-ekkavdah*, **and be glorified**, from *kavod*, **glory**, the word of 2:3, 7 and 9. Written *ve-ekkaved*, read *ve-ekkavdah*: the same meaning. *Ve-nafachti vo*, **I blew it away**, word for word **I blew on it**. *Ratsim*, **runs**: each man is quick for his own house.
- **vv10–11 "the heavens have held back the dew… I have called for a drought"** — the law's warning, *He will shut up the heavens so that there is no rain, and the ground will not give its produce* (Deuteronomy 11:17). *The grain, the new wine and the oil* are the blessing of Deuteronomy 7:13 withheld, as in Joel 1:10. *Chorev*, **drought**; the Greek has *a sword*, *cherev*, the same letters.
- **vv12–13 "obeyed the voice of the LORD… Haggai, the LORD's messenger… I am with you"** — *mal'akh YHWH*, **the LORD's messenger**, the title of the one who *found her by a spring of water in the wilderness* (Genesis 16:7), here given to a prophet; *be-mal'akhut YHWH*, **with the LORD's message**, is found only here. *Ani ittekhem*, **I am with you**, the word to Jacob at Bethel, *I am with you, and I will keep you wherever you go* (Genesis 28:15), and the whole of the message in v13.
- **vv14–15 "The LORD stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel… on the twenty-fourth day of the month"** — *va-ya'ar YHWH et ruach*, **the LORD stirred up the spirit**, the words of the return itself, *the LORD stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia* (Ezra 1:1), and of *everyone whose spirit God had stirred up to go up and build* (Ezra 1:5). *Ruach* here is each man's own spirit. The work began twenty-three days after the word (v1). The Greek joins v15 to 2:1.

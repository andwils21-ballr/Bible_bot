# Haggai 2

## Be Strong and Work

**1** In the seventh month, on the twenty-first day of the month, the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet, saying,

**2** "Speak, please, to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the remnant of the people, saying:

**3** Who is left among you who saw this house in its former glory? How do you see it now? Is it not as nothing in your eyes?

**4** So now, be strong, Zerubbabel, declares the LORD; be strong, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest; be strong, all you people of the land, declares the LORD, and work; for I am with you, declares the LORD of hosts,

**5** as the word that I cut with you when you came out of Egypt; and My Spirit stands among you. Do not fear.

**6** For this is what the LORD of hosts says: Once more, in a little while, I will shake the heavens and the earth, the sea and the dry ground;

**7** and I will shake all the nations, and the treasures of all the nations will come, and I will fill this house with glory, says the LORD of hosts.

**8** The silver is Mine, and the gold is Mine, declares the LORD of hosts.

**9** The latter glory of this house will be greater than the former, says the LORD of hosts; and in this place I will give peace, declares the LORD of hosts."

## Holy and Unclean

**10** On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Haggai the prophet, saying,

**11** "This is what the LORD of hosts says: Ask the priests for instruction, saying:

**12** If a man carries holy meat in the fold of his garment, and with his fold touches bread or stew or wine or oil or any food, does it become holy?" The priests answered and said, "No."

**13** Then Haggai said, "If one who is unclean by a dead person touches any of these, does it become unclean?" The priests answered and said, "It becomes unclean."

**14** Then Haggai answered and said, "So is this people, and so is this nation before Me, declares the LORD, and so is all the work of their hands; and what they offer there is unclean.

## From This Day I Will Bless You

**15** So now, consider, please, from this day onward: before stone was laid on stone in the temple of the LORD,

**16** in those days, when one came to a heap of twenty measures, there were ten; when one came to the wine vat to draw fifty measures from the press, there were twenty.

**17** I struck you with scorching and mildew and hail, all the work of your hands; yet you did not turn to Me, declares the LORD.

**18** Consider, please, from this day onward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, from the day that the foundation of the LORD's temple was laid, consider:

**19** Is the seed still in the barn? Even the vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate and the olive tree have not yet borne. From this day on I will bless you."

## Zerubbabel, My Signet Ring

**20** The word of the LORD came to Haggai a second time on the twenty-fourth day of the month, saying,

**21** "Speak to Zerubbabel, governor of Judah, saying: I will shake the heavens and the earth;

**22** I will overturn the throne of kingdoms and utterly destroy the strength of the kingdoms of the nations; I will overturn the chariot and its riders, and the horses and their riders will go down, each by the sword of his brother.

**23** On that day, declares the LORD of hosts, I will take you, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, My servant, declares the LORD, and make you like a signet ring; for I have chosen you, declares the LORD of hosts."

## Notes

- **vv1–3 "In the seventh month, on the twenty-first day… Who is left among you who saw this house in its former glory?"** — the twenty-first of the seventh month is the last day of the feast that begins *On the fifteenth day of this seventh month… the Festival of Booths, seven days to the LORD* (Leviticus 23:34). The old men had wept when the new foundation was laid: *the old men who had seen the first house, wept with a loud voice when they saw the foundation of this house laid* (Ezra 3:12). *Kavod*, **glory**, comes three times in vv3–9 (vv3, 7, 9).
- **vv4–5 "be strong… and work; for I am with you… My Spirit stands among you"** — David's charge to Solomon for the first house, *Be strong and courageous, and do it… for the LORD God, my God, is with you* (1 Chronicles 28:20), given now three times, to governor, priest and people. *Ha-davar asher karatti*, **the word that I cut**, is the word for cutting a covenant: the promise made at the exodus still holds. *Ve-ruchi omedet be-tokhekhem*, **My Spirit stands among you**, as Isaiah remembers the exodus, *Where is He who put His Holy Spirit among them* (Isaiah 63:11). The Greek has no *the word that I cut with you*.
- **vv6–7 "Once more, in a little while, I will shake the heavens and the earth… the treasures of all the nations will come"** — *od achat me'at hi*, word for word **yet once, it is a little**. *Mar'ish*, **shake**, the verb of *the heavens and the earth shake* (Joel 3:16). *The sea and the dry ground* as at the crossing, when the LORD *made the sea into dry ground* (Exodus 14:21). Hebrews gives v6 in the Greek's words, *Yet once* (Hebrews 12:26). *Chemdat kol ha-goyim*, **the treasures of all the nations**, is singular, **the desire of all the nations**, with a plural verb, *u-va'u*, **they will come**; the Greek has *the choice things of all the nations*. *I will fill this house with glory*, as when *the glory of the LORD filled the house of the LORD* (1 Kings 8:11).
- **vv8–9 "The silver is Mine… The latter glory of this house will be greater than the former… I will give peace"** — the LORD owns what the nations bring, as in *the world and all that fills it are Mine* (Psalm 50:12). *Ha-acharon*, **latter**, can go with *glory* or with *house*: *the glory of this latter house*; v3 has *this house in its former glory*, which matches the first. The Greek adds *and peace of soul for a possession to everyone who builds, to raise up this temple*.
- **vv10–14 "Ask the priests for instruction… does it become holy?… It becomes unclean"** — *torah*, **instruction**, a priest's ruling on a case. Holiness passes to what touches the holy flesh itself, *Whatever touches its flesh becomes holy* (Leviticus 6:27), but not on again through a garment; uncleanness from the dead spreads, *Anything the unclean person touches shall be unclean* (Numbers 19:22). *Teme nefesh*, **unclean by a dead person**, as in Leviticus 22:4. Haggai's point: the people, with the house unbuilt, are the unclean one, and what they offer is unclean. The Greek adds a long ending, which includes *and you hated the one who reproves in the gates*, the words of Amos 5:10.
- **vv15–17 "a heap of twenty measures, there were ten… I struck you with scorching and mildew and hail… yet you did not turn to Me"** — v17 is Amos's word, *I struck you with scorching and mildew… yet you did not return to Me, declares the LORD* (Amos 4:9), with hail added. *Ve-ein etkhem elai*, **yet you did not turn to Me**, has no verb in the Hebrew: word for word **and not you to Me**; the Greek supplies *return*, as Amos has it. *Purah*, **press**, is the winepress of Isaiah 63:3.
- **vv18–19 "from the day that the foundation of the LORD's temple was laid… From this day on I will bless you"** — the same day as v10, the twenty-fourth of the ninth month. *Megurah*, **barn**, as in the *granaries* torn down in Joel 1:17. The vine, fig and pomegranate are the trees that withered in Joel 1:12. The blessing is the law's, *The LORD will command the blessing to be with you in your barns* (Deuteronomy 28:8), promised before the harvest shows it.
- **vv21–22 "I will overturn the throne of kingdoms… the chariot and its riders… each by the sword of his brother"** — *ve-hafakhti*, **I will overturn**, the verb of Sodom (Genesis 19:25) and of Nineveh (Jonah 3:4). *The chariot and its riders* recall the sea, *horse and its rider He has thrown into the sea* (Exodus 15:1). The enemies fall by each other's swords, as when *the LORD set every man's sword against his fellow* (Judges 7:22), and as in Ezekiel, *every man's sword will be against his brother* (Ezekiel 38:21).
- **v23 "I will take you, Zerubbabel… My servant… and make you like a signet ring; for I have chosen you"** — the word against his grandfather is turned back: *though Coniah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, were the signet ring on My right hand, yet I would tear you off from there* (Jeremiah 22:24). Zerubbabel is of Jeconiah's line, *The sons of Jeconiah the captive: Shealtiel his son… Pedaiah… The sons of Pedaiah: Zerubbabel* (1 Chronicles 3:17–19). *Avdi*, **My servant**, is what the LORD called David, *say this to My servant David* (2 Samuel 7:8); the book ends with *I have chosen you*.

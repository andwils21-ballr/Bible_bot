# 1 Timothy 2

## Prayer for Everyone

**1** First of all, then, I urge that requests, prayers, intercessions and thanksgivings be made for all people,

**2** for kings and all who are in high positions, so that we may lead a peaceful and quiet life, godly and dignified in every way.

**3** This is good and pleasing in the sight of God our Savior,

**4** who wants all people to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth.

**5** For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus,

**6** who gave Himself as a ransom for all, the testimony given at the proper time.

**7** For this I was appointed a herald and an apostle — I am telling the truth, I am not lying — a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth.

## Men and Women in Worship

**8** So I want the men in every place to pray, lifting up holy hands without anger or quarreling,

**9** and likewise the women to dress in fitting clothing, with modesty and self-control, not with braided hair and gold or pearls or costly clothes,

**10** but with good works, as is fitting for women who profess godliness.

**11** Let a woman learn quietly, in all submission.

**12** I do not permit a woman to teach or to have authority over a man; she is to be quiet.

**13** For Adam was formed first, then Eve;

**14** and Adam was not deceived, but the woman was deceived and fell into transgression.

**15** Yet she will be saved through the childbearing, if they continue in faith and love and holiness, with self-control.

---

## Notes

- **v12 "to have authority over a man"** — *authentein*, a verb found nowhere else in the Bible. Its range runs from **to have authority** to **to take charge on one's own, to domineer**, and the verse does not narrow it further.
- **vv13–15 "Adam was formed first… the woman was deceived… she will be saved through the childbearing"** — the order of Genesis 2:7, 22 and Eve's own words, *The serpent deceived me* (Genesis 3:13). *Dia tēs teknogonias* has the article, **the childbearing**: either the bearing of children, or the one birth promised in Eden, the woman's seed who *shall strike at your head* (Genesis 3:15). *They* in the last clause may be the women of v9, or the woman and her children.

# 1 Timothy 3

## Overseers

**1** The saying is trustworthy: if anyone aspires to the office of overseer, he desires a good work.

**2** An overseer, then, must be above reproach, the husband of one wife, sober-minded, self-controlled, respectable, hospitable, able to teach,

**3** not a drunkard, not violent but gentle, not quarrelsome, not a lover of money,

**4** managing his own household well, with his children in submission, with all dignity

**5** (for if someone does not know how to manage his own household, how will he care for the church of God?),

**6** not a new convert, or he may become puffed up with conceit and fall into the condemnation of the devil.

**7** He must also be well thought of by outsiders, so that he does not fall into disgrace and into the snare of the devil.

## Deacons

**8** Deacons likewise must be dignified, not double-tongued, not given to much wine, not greedy for gain,

**9** holding the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience.

**10** They too must first be tested; then let them serve as deacons if they prove blameless.

**11** Women likewise must be dignified, not slanderers, but sober-minded, faithful in all things.

**12** Deacons must each be the husband of one wife, managing their children and their own households well.

**13** For those who serve well as deacons gain a good standing for themselves and great boldness in the faith that is in Christ Jesus.

## The Household of God

**14** I am writing these things to you, hoping to come to you soon,

**15** but if I am delayed, so that you may know how one ought to behave in the household of God, which is the church of the living God, a pillar and foundation of the truth.

**16** Great indeed, we confess, is the mystery of godliness:  
He was revealed in the flesh,  
vindicated by the Spirit,  
seen by angels,  
proclaimed among the nations,  
believed on in the world,  
taken up in glory.

## Notes

- **vv2, 12 "the husband of one wife"** — *mias gynaikos andra*, word for word **a man of one woman**. It can mean married only once, or faithful to one wife; the Greek does not say which, and the same words are turned round for a widow in 5:9, *henos andros gynē*, **a woman of one man**.

# 2 Samuel 20

## "Every Man to His Tents, Israel"

**1** Now a worthless man happened to be there whose name was Sheba son of Bichri, a Benjaminite. He blew the shofar and said,  
"We have no share in David,  
no inheritance in the son of Jesse.  
Every man to his tents, Israel!"

**2** So all the men of Israel went up from following David and followed Sheba son of Bichri. But the men of Judah clung to their king from the Jordan to Jerusalem.

**3** David came to his house in Jerusalem. The king took the ten women, the secondary wives he had left to keep the house, and put them in a house under guard. He provided for them but did not go in to them. So they were shut up until the day of their death, living as widows.

## Joab Kills Amasa

**4** Then the king said to Amasa, "Call the men of Judah together for me within three days, and be here yourself."

**5** So Amasa went to call Judah together, but he took longer than the time set for him.

**6** David said to Abishai, "Now Sheba son of Bichri will do us more harm than Absalom did. Take your lord's servants and pursue him, or he will find fortified cities for himself and escape from our sight."

**7** So Joab's men went out after him, with the Cherethites, the Pelethites and all the mighty men. They went out from Jerusalem to pursue Sheba son of Bichri.

**8** When they were at the great stone that is in Gibeon, Amasa came to meet them. Joab was wearing a soldier's garment, and over it was a belt with a sword in its sheath fastened at his waist. As he went forward, it fell out.

**9** Joab said to Amasa, "Is it well with you, my brother?" Then Joab's right hand took Amasa by the beard to kiss him.

**10** Amasa was not on his guard against the sword that was in Joab's hand, and Joab struck him with it in the belly and poured out his entrails on the ground. He did not need to strike him twice, and he died. Then Joab and Abishai his brother pursued Sheba son of Bichri.

**11** One of Joab's young men stood by him and said, "Whoever favors Joab, and whoever is for David, let him follow Joab!"

**12** Amasa lay wallowing in his blood in the middle of the highway. When the man saw that all the people were stopping, he moved Amasa from the highway into the field and threw a garment over him, since he saw that everyone who came by him stopped.

**13** Once he was moved off the highway, all the men went on after Joab to pursue Sheba son of Bichri.

## The Wise Woman of Abel

**14** Sheba passed through all the tribes of Israel to Abel of Beth-maacah, and all the Berites assembled and went in after him.

**15** They came and besieged him in Abel of Beth-maacah. They cast up a siege ramp against the city, and it stood against the rampart. All the people with Joab were battering the wall to bring it down.

**16** Then a wise woman called from the city, "Listen! Listen! Please tell Joab, 'Come near here, so that I may speak with you.'"

**17** He came near to her, and the woman said, "Are you Joab?" He said, "I am." She said to him, "Listen to the words of your servant." He said, "I am listening."

**18** She said, "In the old days they used to say, 'Let them ask at Abel,' and so they settled a matter.

**19** I am one of those who are peaceable and faithful in Israel. You are seeking to destroy a city that is a mother in Israel. Why would you swallow up the inheritance of the LORD?"

**20** Joab answered, "Far be it, far be it from me that I should swallow up or destroy!

**21** That is not the case. A man from the hill country of Ephraim, whose name is Sheba son of Bichri, has lifted up his hand against the king, against David. Hand him over alone, and I will withdraw from the city." The woman said to Joab, "His head will be thrown to you over the wall."

**22** Then the woman went to all the people in her wisdom, and they cut off the head of Sheba son of Bichri and threw it out to Joab. He blew the shofar, and they dispersed from the city, each to his tent. Joab returned to Jerusalem to the king.

## David's Officials

**23** Joab was over all the army of Israel; Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites;

**24** Adoram was over the forced labor; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder;

**25** Sheva was secretary; Zadok and Abiathar were priests;

**26** and Ira the Jairite was also David's priest.

## Notes

- **v1 "We have no share in David… Every man to his tents, Israel!"** — the quarrel of 19:41–43 becomes a revolt. The same cry, nearly word for word, will split the kingdom after Solomon: *What share do we have in David? … To your tents, Israel!* (1 Kings 12:16). *A worthless man*: *ish beliyya'al*, Shimei's word for David (16:7).
- **v2 "the men of Judah clung to their king"** — *davequ*, the verb of Ruth, who *clung to* Naomi (Ruth 1:14).
- **v3 "shut up until the day of their death, living as widows"** — *almenut chayyut*, **widowhood of life**: their husband alive, they are widows. Absalom had gone in to them on the roof (16:22).
- **v6 "escape from our sight"** — *ve-hitsil eineinu*, **snatch away our eye**; the sense is uncertain, whether *escape us* or *do us injury*.
- **vv8–10 "Is it well with you, my brother?… in the belly"** — *ha-shalom attah*. Joab kills Amasa as he killed Abner, *in the belly* (3:27), with a greeting on his lips. David had promised Amasa Joab's command (19:13). *Ve-lo shanah lo*, **did not repeat it**, as Abishai, now at Joab's side, once said of Saul, *I will not need to strike him twice* (1 Samuel 26:8).
- **v14 "all the Berites"** — *ha-berim*, a group named only here; the Greek has *in Charri*. Who they were is not known.
- **vv18–19 "Let them ask at Abel… a city that is a mother in Israel"** — the woman appeals to the town's name for wisdom, and to the word Deborah used of herself, *a mother in Israel* (Judges 5:7). *The inheritance of the LORD* is Israel itself, land and people. The Greek adds *and in Dan, whether what the faithful of Israel had established had ceased*; the text stays with the Hebrew.
- **v21 "His head will be thrown to you over the wall"** — a woman on a city wall again, as at Thebez (11:21), but this time the town is saved.
- **v24 "Adoram was over the forced labor"** — *ha-mas*. The same office under Solomon was held by *Adoniram* (1 Kings 4:6), and when Rehoboam sent *Adoram who was over the forced labor*, Israel stoned him to death (1 Kings 12:18).
- **vv25–26 "Sheva was secretary… Ira the Jairite was also David's priest"** — the list of 8:16–18 again, with changes: *Seraiah* there, *Sheva* here (the written form is *Sheya*); *Ira* in place of *David's sons* as priest (*kohen*).

# 2 Samuel 24

## The Census

**1** Again the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He incited David against them, saying, "Go, number Israel and Judah."

**2** So the king said to Joab, the commander of the army, who was with him, "Go through all the tribes of Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, and count the people, so that I may know the number of the people."

**3** But Joab said to the king, "May the LORD your God add to the people a hundred times as many as they are, while the eyes of my lord the king still see it. But why does my lord the king delight in this thing?"

**4** But the king's word prevailed against Joab and the commanders of the army. So Joab and the commanders of the army went out from the king's presence to count the people of Israel.

**5** They crossed the Jordan and camped in Aroer, south of the city that is in the middle of the valley of Gad, and toward Jazer.

**6** Then they came to Gilead and to the land of Tahtim-hodshi, and they came to Dan-jaan and around to Sidon.

**7** They came to the fortress of Tyre and to all the cities of the Hivites and the Canaanites, and they went out to the Negev of Judah at Beersheba.

**8** When they had gone through all the land, they came to Jerusalem at the end of nine months and twenty days.

**9** Joab gave the king the number of the people counted: in Israel there were eight hundred thousand valiant men who drew the sword, and the men of Judah were five hundred thousand.

## The Three Choices

**10** But David's heart struck him after he had numbered the people. David said to the LORD, "I have sinned greatly in what I have done. But now, O LORD, please take away the guilt of Your servant, for I have acted very foolishly."

**11** When David got up in the morning, the word of the LORD had come to Gad the prophet, David's seer, saying,

**12** "Go and say to David, 'Thus says the LORD: I am laying three things before you. Choose one of them, and I will do it to you.'"

**13** So Gad came to David and told him, and said to him, "Shall three years of famine come to you in your land? Or will you flee three months before your enemies while they pursue you? Or shall there be three days of plague in your land? Now consider and decide what answer I shall return to the one who sent me."

**14** David said to Gad, "I am in great distress. Let us fall into the hand of the LORD, for His mercies are great; but let me not fall into the hand of man."

## The Plague

**15** So the LORD sent a plague on Israel from the morning until the appointed time, and seventy thousand men of the people died, from Dan to Beersheba.

**16** When the messenger stretched out his hand toward Jerusalem to destroy it, the LORD relented from the evil and said to the messenger who was destroying the people, "Enough! Now hold back your hand." The messenger of the LORD was by the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.

**17** When David saw the messenger who was striking the people, he spoke to the LORD and said, "It is I who have sinned, and I who have done wrong. But these sheep, what have they done? Please let Your hand be against me and against my father's house."

## The Threshing Floor of Araunah

**18** Gad came to David that day and said to him, "Go up, raise an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite."

**19** So David went up at Gad's word, as the LORD had commanded.

**20** When Araunah looked down and saw the king and his servants coming over toward him, Araunah went out and paid homage to the king with his face to the ground.

**21** Araunah said, "Why has my lord the king come to his servant?" David said, "To buy the threshing floor from you, to build an altar to the LORD, so that the plague may be held back from the people."

**22** Araunah said to David, "Let my lord the king take it and offer up what is good in his eyes. Here are the oxen for the burnt offering, and the threshing sledges and the yokes of the oxen for the wood.

**23** All this, O king, Araunah gives to the king." Araunah also said to the king, "May the LORD your God accept you."

**24** But the king said to Araunah, "No, but I will surely buy it from you for a price. I will not offer burnt offerings to the LORD my God that cost me nothing." So David bought the threshing floor and the oxen for fifty shekels of silver.

**25** David built an altar to the LORD there and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. So the LORD heeded prayers for the land, and the plague was held back from Israel.

## Notes

- **v1 "Again the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He incited David against them"** — *va-yasset*, **incited**. *Again* looks back to the famine of 21:1. Chronicles tells the same story with another subject: *Satan stood up against Israel and incited David to number Israel* (1 Chronicles 21:1). The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **vv2–3 "count the people… why does my lord the king delight in this thing?"** — the text does not say what was wrong in the count. The law of the census warns that each man must give *a ransom for his life to the LORD when they are numbered — so there will be no plague among them* (Exodus 30:12), and a plague follows here.
- **v9 "eight hundred thousand… five hundred thousand"** — Chronicles has *a million one hundred thousand* for Israel and *four hundred seventy thousand* for Judah (1 Chronicles 21:5). The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **v10 "David's heart struck him"** — *va-yakh lev David oto*, as when he cut off the edge of Saul's robe (1 Samuel 24:5). *I have acted very foolishly*, *niskalti*, the word Samuel used of Saul (1 Samuel 13:13).
- **v13 "three years of famine"** — the Hebrew has *seven years*. The Greek has *three years*, and so does Chronicles (1 Chronicles 21:12); the three choices are three years, three months and three days (v12, *three things*). The text follows the Greek and Chronicles.
- **v15 "until the appointed time"** — *ad et mo'ed*; the Greek has *until the hour of the midday meal*, and adds that David chose the plague *in the days of wheat harvest*. The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **vv16–17 "the LORD relented from the evil… these sheep, what have they done?"** — *va-yinnachem YHWH el ha-ra'ah*. The shepherd king offers himself and his father's house in place of the flock.
- **vv16, 18 "Araunah the Jebusite"** — the written forms vary, *ha-Awarnah* (v16) and *Araniah* (v18); the text follows the read form, *Aravnah*, throughout. Chronicles calls him *Ornan* (1 Chronicles 21:15).
- **v24 "that cost me nothing… fifty shekels of silver"** — *chinnam*, **for nothing**. Chronicles has *six hundred shekels of gold by weight* (1 Chronicles 21:25). The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **v25 "the LORD heeded prayers for the land"** — the words of 21:14. The Greek adds that Solomon later enlarged this altar. Chronicles names the place: Solomon built the house of the LORD *on Mount Moriah… on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite* (2 Chronicles 3:1), the name of the land where Abraham was sent with Isaac (Genesis 22:2).

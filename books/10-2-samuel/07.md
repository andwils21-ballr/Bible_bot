# 2 Samuel 7

## "Will You Build Me a House?"

**1** And it came to pass, when the king lived in his house and the LORD had given him rest from all his enemies around him,

**2** that the king said to Nathan the prophet, "See now, I live in a house of cedar, but the ark of God stays inside curtains."

**3** Nathan said to the king, "Go, do all that is in your heart, for the LORD is with you."

**4** And it came to pass that same night that the word of the LORD came to Nathan:

**5** "Go and tell My servant David, 'Thus says the LORD: Will you build Me a house to dwell in?

**6** I have not lived in a house from the day I brought the sons of Israel up from Egypt to this day, but I have been moving about in a tent for My dwelling.

**7** Wherever I have moved about among all the sons of Israel, did I ever speak a word to one of the tribes of Israel whom I commanded to shepherd My people Israel, saying, "Why have you not built Me a house of cedar?"'

## The Promise

**8** And now, say this to My servant David: 'Thus says the LORD of hosts: I took you from the pasture, from following the sheep, to be leader over My people, over Israel.

**9** I have been with you wherever you went and have cut off all your enemies before you. I will make you a great name, like the name of the great ones on the earth.

**10** I will appoint a place for My people Israel and will plant them, so that they may dwell in their own place and be disturbed no more. The wicked shall afflict them no more as at first,

**11** from the day I commanded judges over My people Israel. I will give you rest from all your enemies. The LORD declares to you that the LORD will make you a house.

**12** When your days are fulfilled and you lie down with your fathers, I will raise up your seed after you, who shall come from your body, and I will establish his kingdom.

**13** He shall build a house for My name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever.

**14** I will be his father, and he shall be My son. When he does wrong, I will discipline him with the rod of men and with the blows of the sons of men.

**15** But My loyal love shall not depart from him, as I took it from Saul, whom I put away from before you.

**16** Your house and your kingdom shall be made sure forever before you; your throne shall be established forever.'"

**17** In accordance with all these words and all this vision, so Nathan spoke to David.

## David's Prayer

**18** Then King David went in and sat before the LORD and said, "Who am I, O Lord GOD, and what is my house, that You have brought me this far?

**19** Yet this was too small in Your eyes, O Lord GOD; You have spoken also of Your servant's house for a long time to come. And this is the instruction for mankind, O Lord GOD!

**20** What more can David say to You? For You know Your servant, O Lord GOD.

**21** For the sake of Your word and according to Your own heart, You have done all this greatness, to make Your servant know it.

**22** Therefore You are great, O Lord GOD, for there is none like You, and there is no God besides You, according to all that we have heard with our ears.

**23** Who is like Your people, like Israel, the one nation on earth whom God went to redeem as a people for Himself, to make a name for Himself, and to do for you great and awesome things for Your land, before Your people whom You redeemed for Yourself from Egypt, from nations and their gods?

**24** You established for Yourself Your people Israel to be Your people forever, and You, O LORD, became their God.

**25** And now, O LORD God, confirm forever the word You have spoken concerning Your servant and concerning his house, and do as You have spoken.

**26** May Your name be great forever, saying, 'The LORD of hosts is God over Israel,' and may the house of Your servant David be established before You.

**27** For You, O LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, have made it known to Your servant, saying, 'I will build you a house.' That is why Your servant has found courage to pray this prayer to You.

**28** And now, O Lord GOD, You are God, and Your words are true, and You have promised this good thing to Your servant.

**29** And now, may it please You to bless the house of Your servant, so that it may continue forever before You. For You, O Lord GOD, have spoken, and with Your blessing let the house of Your servant be blessed forever."

## Notes

- **vv1–2 "the LORD had given him rest… the ark of God stays inside curtains"** — the rest the law promised, *He gives you rest from all your enemies around you* (Deuteronomy 12:10), after which the LORD would choose a place for His name (Deuteronomy 12:11). The king's cedar house stands beside the ark's tent of *yeri'ah*, **curtains**.
- **v5 "Will you build Me a house to dwell in?"** — a question in the Hebrew. Chronicles and the Greek make it a statement, *You shall not build Me a house* (1 Chronicles 17:4).
- **vv5, 11, 13, 16 "a house"** — *bayit* is **house**, **temple** and **dynasty**. David wants to build the LORD a house; the LORD answers that *the LORD will make you a house*. David's son will build the house of stone (v13), and David's house, his line, will be made sure (v16).
- **v8 "I took you from the pasture, from following the sheep"** — the youngest son who was *tending the sheep* when Samuel came (1 Samuel 16:11).
- **v10 "I will plant them"** — the promise of the Song at the Sea: *You will bring Your people in and plant them on the mountain of Your inheritance* (Exodus 15:17).
- **v14 "I will be his father, and he shall be My son"** — the letter to the Hebrews applies the words to Jesus: *I will be a Father to Him, and He shall be a Son to Me* (Hebrews 1:5). In Samuel the next words are *when he does wrong, I will discipline him*: the son is first of all David's heir.
- **v15 "But My loyal love shall not depart from him, as I took it from Saul"** — *chesed*, the covenant loyalty the LORD keeps. Saul lost the kingdom; the promise to David's line does not depend on the son's goodness.
- **v16 "Your house and your kingdom shall be made sure forever"** — *ne'eman*, the word Abigail used first, *the LORD will surely make my lord a sure house* (1 Samuel 25:28), and the promise once made of a faithful priest (1 Samuel 2:35).
- **v18 "Who am I, O Lord GOD, and what is my house?"** — David's words before Saul, *Who am I… that I should be son-in-law to the king?* (1 Samuel 18:18). *Adonai YHWH*, **Lord GOD**, seven times in the prayer.
- **vv19, 23 "this is the instruction for mankind… from nations and their gods"** — *torat ha-adam*, a phrase whose sense here is not certain; Chronicles has a different reading, *You have regarded me as a man of high rank* (1 Chronicles 17:17). In v23 the Hebrew is also hard: Chronicles has *by driving out nations* (1 Chronicles 17:21). The text stays with the Hebrew in both.

# 2 Samuel 9

## "Is There Anyone Left of the House of Saul?"

**1** David said, "Is there still anyone left of the house of Saul, so that I may show him loyal love for Jonathan's sake?"

**2** There was a servant of the house of Saul whose name was Ziba, and they called him to David. The king said to him, "Are you Ziba?" He said, "Your servant."

**3** The king said, "Is there no one left of the house of Saul, so that I may show him the loyal love of God?" Ziba said to the king, "There is still a son of Jonathan, who is lame in his feet."

**4** The king said to him, "Where is he?" Ziba said to the king, "He is in the house of Machir son of Ammiel, in Lo-debar."

**5** So King David sent and brought him from the house of Machir son of Ammiel, from Lo-debar.

**6** Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, son of Saul, came to David and fell on his face and paid homage. David said, "Mephibosheth!" He said, "Here is your servant."

**7** David said to him, "Do not be afraid, for I will surely show you loyal love for the sake of Jonathan your father. I will restore to you all the land of Saul your grandfather, and you shall eat bread at my table always."

**8** He paid homage and said, "What is your servant, that you should look on a dead dog like me?"

## At the King's Table

**9** Then the king called Ziba, Saul's servant, and said to him, "All that belonged to Saul and to all his house I have given to your master's grandson.

**10** You and your sons and your servants shall work the land for him and bring in the produce, so that your master's grandson may have bread to eat. But Mephibosheth, your master's grandson, shall always eat bread at my table." Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants.

**11** Ziba said to the king, "According to all that my lord the king commands his servant, so your servant will do." So Mephibosheth ate at David's table like one of the king's sons.

**12** Mephibosheth had a young son whose name was Mica. All who lived in Ziba's house were Mephibosheth's servants.

**13** So Mephibosheth lived in Jerusalem, for he always ate at the king's table. He was lame in both his feet.

## Notes

- **vv1, 3, 7 "loyal love for Jonathan's sake… the loyal love of God"** — *chesed*, the covenant word of David and Jonathan: *Do not cut off your loyal love from my house forever* (1 Samuel 20:15). *The loyal love of God* is the phrase Jonathan used, *the loyal love of the LORD* (1 Samuel 20:14).
- **v3 "lame in his feet"** — the fall from his nurse's arms when the news of Gilboa came (4:4).
- **v4 "in Lo-debar"** — *Lo Devar*, **no word** or **no thing**, a town east of the Jordan. Machir son of Ammiel of Lo-debar will later bring supplies to David when he flees from Absalom (17:27–29).
- **v6 "Mephibosheth!… Here is your servant"** — *hinneh avdekha*, **Here is your servant**, the answer of one ready to serve, as Samuel answered *Here I am* (1 Samuel 3:4).
- **v7 "Do not be afraid"** — the grandson of the king David replaced has reason to fear. David had sworn to Saul not to cut off his seed (1 Samuel 24:21–22), and now gives his grandson Saul's land and a place at his table.
- **v8 "that you should look on a dead dog like me?"** — the words David used of himself before Saul, *After a dead dog!* (1 Samuel 24:14). The grandson of Saul now uses them before David.
- **v10 "Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants"** — the man who works the land holds the larger house. When David flees from Absalom, Ziba will speak against his master and be given all that belonged to him (16:1–4).
- **v11 "So Mephibosheth ate at David's table"** — the Hebrew has *at my table*, as if David were speaking, though the sentence stands after Ziba's words. The Greek has *at David's table*, and vv7, 10 and 13 all speak of the king's table. The text follows the Greek.
- **v13 "He was lame in both his feet"** — the chapter ends as it began (v3). He eats at the king's table and cannot run from it.

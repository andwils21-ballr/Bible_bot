# 2 Samuel 8

## The Wars

**1** And it came to pass after this that David struck the Philistines and subdued them, and David took Metheg-ammah out of the hand of the Philistines.

**2** He struck Moab and measured them with a line, making them lie down on the ground. He measured two lines to put to death and one full line to keep alive. The Moabites became David's servants and brought tribute.

**3** David also struck Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah, as he went to restore his power at the river Euphrates.

**4** David captured from him a thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen and twenty thousand foot soldiers. David hamstrung all the chariot horses, but left enough of them for a hundred chariots.

**5** When the Arameans of Damascus came to help Hadadezer king of Zobah, David struck twenty-two thousand men of the Arameans.

**6** Then David put garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became David's servants and brought tribute. The LORD gave David victory wherever he went.

**7** David took the shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadadezer and brought them to Jerusalem.

**8** From Betah and from Berothai, towns of Hadadezer, King David took a great quantity of bronze.

**9** When Toi king of Hamath heard that David had struck the whole army of Hadadezer,

**10** Toi sent Joram his son to King David to greet him and to bless him, because he had fought against Hadadezer and struck him, for Hadadezer had been at war with Toi. Joram brought with him articles of silver, of gold and of bronze.

**11** These also King David dedicated to the LORD, together with the silver and gold he had dedicated from all the nations he had subdued:

**12** from Aram, from Moab, from the sons of Ammon, from the Philistines and from Amalek, and from the spoil of Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah.

**13** David made a name for himself when he returned from striking down eighteen thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt.

**14** He put garrisons in Edom; throughout all Edom he put garrisons, and all the Edomites became David's servants. The LORD gave David victory wherever he went.

## David's Officials

**15** So David reigned over all Israel, and David did justice and righteousness for all his people.

**16** Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder;

**17** Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests; Seraiah was secretary;

**18** Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites; and David's sons were priests.

## Notes

- **v1 "David took Metheg-ammah"** — *meteg ha-ammah*, **the bridle of the forearm**, or **of the mother city**: a place name or a figure for control of the Philistine capital. Chronicles has *he took Gath and its villages* (1 Chronicles 18:1).
- **v2 "He measured two lines to put to death and one full line to keep alive"** — the Moabites are laid on the ground and measured off by rope. David had once sent his parents to the king of Moab for safety (1 Samuel 22:3–4); the text does not say what changed.
- **v4 "a thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen"** — the Hebrew has *a thousand seven hundred horsemen* and no chariots, though the same verse speaks of *all the chariot horses* and *a hundred chariots*. The Greek and Chronicles have *a thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen* (1 Chronicles 18:4). The text follows them.
- **v4 "David hamstrung all the chariot horses"** — *va-ye'aqqer*, as the LORD told Joshua, *You shall hamstring their horses and burn their chariots* (Joshua 11:6).
- **vv6, 14 "The LORD gave David victory wherever he went"** — *va-yosha*, **saved**. The line is said twice, after the wars in the north and in the south.
- **vv7–8 "the shields of gold… a great quantity of bronze"** — the Greek adds that Shishak king of Egypt later took the gold shields, and that Solomon made the bronze sea, pillars and vessels from the bronze. Chronicles has the second addition (1 Chronicles 18:8). The text stays with the Hebrew.
- **v13 "eighteen thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt"** — the Hebrew has *Aram*; the Greek has *Edom*, and so does Chronicles, *Abishai… struck Edom in the Valley of Salt, eighteen thousand* (1 Chronicles 18:12). The next verse is about garrisons in Edom. *Aram* and *Edom* differ in Hebrew by one letter. The text follows the Greek and Chronicles.
- **v15 "David did justice and righteousness for all his people"** — *mishpat u-tsedaqah*, what the LORD chose Abraham to teach his house, *they shall keep the way of the LORD, to do righteousness and judgment* (Genesis 18:19).
- **v17 "Ahimelech son of Abiathar"** — in 1 Samuel it is the other way round: *Abiathar son of Ahimelech* escaped from Nob (1 Samuel 22:20). The Hebrew and the Greek both have it as it stands here.
- **v18 "and David's sons were priests"** — *kohanim*. Chronicles has *David's sons were the chief officers at the king's side* (1 Chronicles 18:17), and the Greek has *court officials*. The Hebrew word is the ordinary word for priest; the text keeps it.

# 2 Samuel 16

## Ziba

**1** When David had gone a little past the summit, Ziba the servant of Mephibosheth came to meet him with a pair of saddled donkeys. On them were two hundred loaves of bread, a hundred clusters of raisins, a hundred of summer fruit and a skin of wine.

**2** The king said to Ziba, "What do you mean by these?" Ziba said, "The donkeys are for the king's household to ride, the bread and the summer fruit for the young men to eat, and the wine for those who grow faint in the wilderness to drink."

**3** The king said, "Where is your master's son?" Ziba said to the king, "He is staying in Jerusalem, for he said, 'Today the house of Israel will give me back the kingdom of my father.'"

**4** The king said to Ziba, "All that belonged to Mephibosheth is now yours." Ziba said, "I pay homage; let me find favor in your eyes, my lord the king."

## Shimei Curses David

**5** When King David came to Bahurim, a man of the clan of the house of Saul came out from there. His name was Shimei son of Gera, and he came out cursing as he came.

**6** He threw stones at David and at all King David's servants, though all the people and all the mighty men were on his right and on his left.

**7** This is what Shimei said as he cursed: "Get out, get out, you man of blood, you worthless man!

**8** The LORD has brought back on you all the blood of the house of Saul, in whose place you have reigned, and the LORD has given the kingdom into the hand of Absalom your son. Now you are caught in your own evil, for you are a man of blood."

**9** Then Abishai son of Zeruiah said to the king, "Why should this dead dog curse my lord the king? Let me go over and take off his head."

**10** But the king said, "What have I to do with you, you sons of Zeruiah? If he curses because the LORD has said to him, 'Curse David,' who then shall say, 'Why have you done so?'"

**11** David said to Abishai and to all his servants, "My own son, who came out of my body, seeks my life; how much more now this Benjaminite! Let him alone and let him curse, for the LORD has told him to.

**12** Perhaps the LORD will look on my affliction, and the LORD will repay me with good for his cursing today."

**13** So David and his men went on the road, while Shimei went along the side of the hill opposite him, cursing as he went, throwing stones at him and flinging dust.

**14** The king and all the people who were with him arrived weary, and he drew breath there.

## Absalom in Jerusalem

**15** Meanwhile Absalom and all the people, the men of Israel, came to Jerusalem, and Ahithophel with him.

**16** When Hushai the Archite, David's friend, came to Absalom, Hushai said to Absalom, "Long live the king! Long live the king!"

**17** Absalom said to Hushai, "Is this your loyal love to your friend? Why did you not go with your friend?"

**18** Hushai said to Absalom, "No; the one whom the LORD and this people and all the men of Israel have chosen, his I will be, and with him I will stay.

**19** Besides, whom should I serve? Should it not be his son? As I served in your father's presence, so I will be in yours."

**20** Then Absalom said to Ahithophel, "Give your counsel. What shall we do?"

**21** Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Go in to your father's secondary wives, whom he left to keep the house. Then all Israel will hear that you have become a stench to your father, and the hands of all who are with you will be strengthened."

**22** So they pitched the tent for Absalom on the roof, and Absalom went in to his father's secondary wives in the sight of all Israel.

**23** In those days the counsel that Ahithophel gave was as if one inquired of the word of God. So was all the counsel of Ahithophel, both to David and to Absalom.

## Notes

- **vv1–4 "Ziba the servant of Mephibosheth… All that belonged to Mephibosheth is now yours"** — the king who gave Mephibosheth all of Saul's land (9:7–10) now gives it to Ziba on Ziba's word alone. Mephibosheth will give his own account when David returns (19:24–30).
- **v7 "you man of blood, you worthless man"** — *ish ha-damim ve-ish ha-beliyya'al*. *Beliyya'al* is **worthlessness**, the word used of Eli's sons (1 Samuel 2:12).
- **v8 "all the blood of the house of Saul"** — the deaths of Abner (3:27) and of Ish-bosheth (4:7) have been told, and the text cleared David of both: *it had not been the king's will to put Abner son of Ner to death* (3:37), and he executed Ish-bosheth's killers (4:9–12). Shimei's charge is his own.
- **v9 "this dead dog"** — the words David used of himself before Saul (1 Samuel 24:14), and Mephibosheth before David (9:8).
- **v10 "What have I to do with you, you sons of Zeruiah?"** — *mah li ve-lakhem*, a way of refusing someone's proposal. *If he curses*: the written text has *ki*, **because**, and the read form is *koh*, **so let him curse**. The text follows the read form, with little difference in sense.
- **v12 "Perhaps the LORD will look on my affliction"** — the written text is *be-avoni*, **on my guilt**; the read form is *be-eini*, **on my eye**. The Greek has *on my humiliation*, which reads the written letters as *be-onyi*, **on my affliction**. That is the phrase of Leah, *the LORD has seen my affliction* (Genesis 29:32), and of Hannah, *if You will indeed look on the affliction of Your servant* (1 Samuel 1:11). The text follows the Greek.
- **v14 "arrived weary, and he drew breath there"** — *va-yinnafesh*, from *nefesh*, **breath**, the word of the sabbath law, *the son of your slave woman and the guest may draw breath* (Exodus 23:12).
- **v17 "Is this your loyal love to your friend?"** — *zeh chasdekha et re'ekha*. Hushai is *David's friend* (15:37; 16:16), and Absalom mocks the title.
- **v21 "you have become a stench to your father"** — *niv'ashta*, the word of 10:6 and 1 Samuel 13:4. To take the king's secondary wives is to claim the king's place; Abner was accused over Saul's secondary wife (3:7).
- **v22 "they pitched the tent for Absalom on the roof… in the sight of all Israel"** — David was walking on the roof of the king's house when he saw Bathsheba (11:2). Nathan had said, *he shall lie with your wives before the eyes of this sun… I will do this thing before all Israel* (12:11–12).

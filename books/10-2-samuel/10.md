# 2 Samuel 10

## David's Envoys Shamed

**1** And it came to pass after this that the king of the sons of Ammon died, and Hanun his son reigned in his place.

**2** David said, "I will show loyal love to Hanun son of Nahash, as his father showed loyal love to me." So David sent his servants to comfort him concerning his father, and David's servants came to the land of the sons of Ammon.

**3** But the princes of the sons of Ammon said to Hanun their lord, "Do you think David is honoring your father because he has sent comforters to you? Has David not sent his servants to you to search out the city, to spy it out and to overthrow it?"

**4** So Hanun took David's servants, shaved off half of each one's beard, cut their garments in half as far as their buttocks, and sent them away.

**5** When David was told, he sent to meet them, for the men were deeply humiliated. The king said, "Stay in Jericho until your beards grow, and then come back."

## Joab and Abishai

**6** When the sons of Ammon saw that they had become a stench to David, the sons of Ammon sent and hired the Arameans of Beth-rehob and the Arameans of Zobah, twenty thousand foot soldiers, the king of Maacah with a thousand men, and the men of Tob, twelve thousand men.

**7** When David heard it, he sent Joab and all the army, the mighty men.

**8** The sons of Ammon came out and drew up for battle at the entrance of the gate, while the Arameans of Zobah and Rehob and the men of Tob and Maacah were by themselves in the open country.

**9** When Joab saw that the battle was set against him in front and behind, he chose some of all the chosen men of Israel and drew them up against the Arameans.

**10** The rest of the people he put in the hand of Abishai his brother, and he drew them up against the sons of Ammon.

**11** He said, "If the Arameans are too strong for me, then come and help me. But if the sons of Ammon are too strong for you, then I will come to help you.

**12** Be strong, and let us show ourselves strong for our people and for the cities of our God. The LORD will do what is good in His eyes."

**13** So Joab and the people who were with him drew near to battle against the Arameans, and they fled before him.

**14** When the sons of Ammon saw that the Arameans had fled, they fled before Abishai and went into the city. Then Joab turned back from the sons of Ammon and came to Jerusalem.

## The Battle at Helam

**15** When the Arameans saw that they had been struck down before Israel, they gathered together.

**16** Hadadezer sent and brought out the Arameans who were beyond the River, and they came to Helam, with Shobach the commander of Hadadezer's army at their head.

**17** When David was told, he gathered all Israel, crossed the Jordan and came to Helam. The Arameans drew up against David and fought with him.

**18** The Arameans fled before Israel, and David killed of the Arameans the men of seven hundred chariots and forty thousand horsemen. He struck Shobach the commander of their army, and he died there.

**19** When all the kings who were servants of Hadadezer saw that they had been struck down before Israel, they made peace with Israel and served them. So the Arameans were afraid to help the sons of Ammon any more.

## Notes

- **v6 "the king of Maacah"** — the Greek has *the king of Amalek*, here and in v8. Chronicles has *the king of Maacah and his people* (1 Chronicles 19:7), and Maacah lies with Zobah and Rehob in the north. The text keeps the Hebrew.
- **v18 "the men of seven hundred chariots and forty thousand horsemen"** — the Greek agrees. Chronicles has *seven thousand chariots and forty thousand foot soldiers* (1 Chronicles 19:18). The text keeps the Hebrew.

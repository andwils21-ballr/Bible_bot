# 2 Samuel 17

## Ahithophel's Counsel

**1** Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Let me choose twelve thousand men, and I will set out and pursue David tonight.

**2** I will come upon him while he is weary and his hands are weak, and throw him into a panic. All the people who are with him will flee, and I will strike down the king alone.

**3** I will bring all the people back to you as a bride comes back to her husband. You seek the life of only one man, and all the people will be at peace."

**4** The plan seemed right in the eyes of Absalom and in the eyes of all the elders of Israel.

## Hushai's Counsel

**5** Then Absalom said, "Call Hushai the Archite too, and let us hear what he also has to say."

**6** When Hushai came to Absalom, Absalom said to him, "This is what Ahithophel has said. Shall we do what he says? If not, you speak."

**7** Hushai said to Absalom, "This time the counsel that Ahithophel has given is not good."

**8** Hushai said, "You know your father and his men, that they are mighty men and bitter of soul, like a bear robbed of her cubs in the field. Your father is a man of war, and he will not spend the night with the people.

**9** Even now he is hidden in one of the pits or in some other place. When some of the people fall at the first attack, whoever hears it will say, 'There has been a slaughter among the people who follow Absalom.'

**10** Then even the man of valor, whose heart is like the heart of a lion, will utterly melt, for all Israel knows that your father is a mighty man, and those who are with him are men of valor.

**11** So I counsel that all Israel be gathered to you, from Dan to Beersheba, as many as the sand by the sea, and that you go into battle in person.

**12** We will come upon him in whatever place he may be found, and we will light on him as the dew falls on the ground. Of him and of all the men with him not even one will be left.

**13** If he withdraws into a city, then all Israel will bring ropes to that city, and we will drag it into the valley until not even a pebble is found there."

**14** Absalom and all the men of Israel said, "The counsel of Hushai the Archite is better than the counsel of Ahithophel." For the LORD had ordained to defeat the good counsel of Ahithophel, so that the LORD might bring disaster on Absalom.

## Word Sent to David

**15** Then Hushai said to Zadok and Abiathar the priests, "This and this is what Ahithophel counseled Absalom and the elders of Israel, and this and this is what I have counseled.

**16** So now send quickly and tell David, 'Do not spend the night in the plains of the wilderness, but by all means cross over, or the king and all the people who are with him will be swallowed up.'"

**17** Jonathan and Ahimaaz were waiting at En-rogel. A servant girl would go and tell them, and they would go and tell King David, for they could not risk being seen coming into the city.

**18** But a young man saw them and told Absalom. So the two of them went away quickly and came to the house of a man in Bahurim who had a well in his courtyard, and they went down into it.

**19** The woman took a covering, spread it over the mouth of the well and scattered grain on it, so that nothing was known.

**20** When Absalom's servants came to the woman at the house, they said, "Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?" The woman said to them, "They have crossed the brook of water." They searched and did not find them, and they returned to Jerusalem.

**21** After they had gone, the men came up out of the well and went and told King David. They said to David, "Get up and cross the water quickly, for this is what Ahithophel has counseled against you."

**22** So David and all the people who were with him got up and crossed the Jordan. By the light of morning not one was left who had not crossed the Jordan.

**23** When Ahithophel saw that his counsel was not followed, he saddled his donkey and set out for his home in his own town. He gave orders to his household, and he hanged himself. He died and was buried in his father's tomb.

## Mahanaim

**24** David came to Mahanaim, and Absalom crossed the Jordan, he and all the men of Israel with him.

**25** Absalom had set Amasa over the army in place of Joab. Amasa was the son of a man named Ithra the Israelite, who had gone in to Abigal daughter of Nahash, sister of Zeruiah, Joab's mother.

**26** Israel and Absalom camped in the land of Gilead.

**27** When David came to Mahanaim, Shobi son of Nahash from Rabbah of the sons of Ammon, Machir son of Ammiel from Lo-debar, and Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim

**28** brought beds, basins and clay pots, wheat, barley, flour and roasted grain, beans, lentils,

**29** honey and curds, sheep, and cheese from the herd, for David and the people who were with him to eat. For they said, "The people are hungry and weary and thirsty in the wilderness."

## Notes

- **v3 "as a bride comes back to her husband. You seek the life of only one man"** — the Hebrew reads *ke-shuv ha-kol ha-ish asher attah mevaqqesh*, **like the return of the whole is the man you seek**, which does not make a sentence. The Greek has *as a bride returns to her husband; you seek only the life of one man*. *Ha-kallah*, **the bride**, differs from *ha-kol* by one letter, and the Greek sense is the plan of v2: strike the king alone. The text follows the Greek.
- **v8 "like a bear robbed of her cubs"** — *ke-dov shakkul*, a figure used again in *I will meet them like a bear robbed of her cubs* (Hosea 13:8) and *Let a man meet a bear robbed of her cubs rather than a fool in his folly* (Proverbs 17:12).
- **vv11–13 "as many as the sand by the sea… as the dew falls… not even a pebble"** — Hushai's counsel is built of images: the sand of the promise to Abraham, *as the sand that is on the lip of the sea* (Genesis 22:17), the dew that lies on all the ground, a city dragged away by ropes. It takes time, and time is what David needs.
- **v14 "the LORD had ordained to defeat the good counsel of Ahithophel"** — *le-hafer*, the answer to David's prayer on the Mount of Olives, *turn the counsel of Ahithophel into foolishness* (15:31), and to his charge to Hushai, *defeat the counsel of Ahithophel for me* (15:34).
- **v16 "in the plains of the wilderness"** — *be-arvot*, written so here; in 15:28 the written form is *be-avrot*, **at the fords**, and the read form is this.
- **v17 "En-rogel"** — *Ein Rogel*, a spring where the border of Judah ended (Joshua 15:7). The messengers stay outside the walls.
- **v20 "They have crossed the brook of water"** — *avru mikhal ha-mayim*. *Mikhal* is found only here, and its meaning is uncertain; the Greek has *They went past a little of the water*.
- **v23 "he hanged himself"** — *va-yechanaq*; the verb is found nowhere else in the Hebrew Bible, and no one else there takes his own life this way. The Greek has *apēnxato*, the word used of Judas, *he went off and hanged himself* (Matthew 27:5).
- **v25 "Ithra the Israelite… Abigal daughter of Nahash, sister of Zeruiah"** — Chronicles has *Jether the Ishmaelite* and names Zeruiah and Abigail as sisters of David (1 Chronicles 2:16–17). By Chronicles, Amasa and Joab are cousins and both are David's nephews. The Hebrew names Abigal's father as Nahash, where Chronicles makes her a daughter of Jesse.
- **vv27–29 "Shobi son of Nahash… Machir son of Ammiel… Barzillai"** — Shobi is a son of the Ammonite king whose son Hanun shamed David's envoys (10:1–4); Machir had kept Mephibosheth (9:4). The Greek has *ten* beds and *ten* cauldrons, and adds rugs.

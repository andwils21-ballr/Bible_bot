# 2 Samuel 23

## The Last Words of David

**1** These are the last words of David:  
"The word of David son of Jesse,  
the word of the man who was raised on high,  
the anointed of the God of Jacob,  
the sweet singer of Israel:

**2** The Spirit of the LORD spoke by me;  
His word was on my tongue.

**3** The God of Israel said,  
the Rock of Israel spoke to me:  
'One who rules over men in righteousness,  
who rules in the fear of God,

**4** is like the light of morning when the sun rises,  
a morning without clouds,  
like the grass from the earth  
after the brightness of rain.'

**5** Is not my house so with God?  
For He has made with me an everlasting covenant,  
ordered in all things and secured.  
Will He not make all my salvation  
and all my desire to grow?

**6** But the worthless are all like thorns thrust away,  
for they cannot be taken with the hand;

**7** the man who touches them  
arms himself with iron and the shaft of a spear,  
and they are utterly burned with fire where they lie."

## The Three

**8** These are the names of David's mighty men: Josheb-basshebeth the Tahchemonite, chief of the Three. He was Adino the Eznite; he drew his sword against eight hundred, whom he killed at one time.

**9** After him was Eleazar son of Dodo, son of Ahohi, one of the three mighty men with David when they taunted the Philistines who were gathered there for battle, and the men of Israel withdrew.

**10** He got up and struck the Philistines until his hand grew weary and his hand clung to the sword. The LORD brought about a great victory that day, and the people returned after him only to strip the dead.

**11** After him was Shammah son of Agee the Hararite. The Philistines gathered at Lehi, where there was a plot of ground full of lentils, and the people fled from the Philistines.

**12** But he took his stand in the middle of the plot, defended it and struck down the Philistines, and the LORD brought about a great victory.

## Water from the Well of Bethlehem

**13** Three of the thirty chiefs went down at harvest time and came to David at the cave of Adullam, while a troop of Philistines was camped in the Valley of Rephaim.

**14** David was then in the stronghold, and the Philistine garrison was then at Bethlehem.

**15** David longed and said, "If only someone would give me water to drink from the well of Bethlehem that is by the gate!"

**16** So the three mighty men broke through the camp of the Philistines, drew water from the well of Bethlehem that was by the gate, and carried it and brought it to David. But he would not drink it; he poured it out to the LORD.

**17** He said, "Far be it from me, O LORD, that I should do this. Is it not the blood of the men who went at the risk of their lives?" So he would not drink it. These things the three mighty men did.

## Abishai and Benaiah

**18** Abishai, the brother of Joab, son of Zeruiah, was chief of the Three. He wielded his spear against three hundred, whom he killed, and won a name among the Three.

**19** Was he not the most honored of the Three? He became their commander, but he did not attain to the Three.

**20** Benaiah son of Jehoiada was the son of a man of valor from Kabzeel, great in deeds. He struck down the two Ariels of Moab. He also went down and struck down a lion inside a pit on a day of snow.

**21** He struck down an Egyptian, a man of great stature. The Egyptian had a spear in his hand, but Benaiah went down to him with a staff, snatched the spear out of the Egyptian's hand and killed him with his own spear.

**22** These things Benaiah son of Jehoiada did, and he won a name among the three mighty men.

**23** He was honored among the Thirty, but he did not attain to the Three. David set him over his bodyguard.

## The Thirty

**24** Asahel the brother of Joab was among the Thirty; Elhanan son of Dodo of Bethlehem;

**25** Shammah the Harodite; Elika the Harodite;

**26** Helez the Paltite; Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite;

**27** Abiezer the Anathothite; Mebunnai the Hushathite;

**28** Zalmon the Ahohite; Maharai the Netophathite;

**29** Heleb son of Baanah the Netophathite; Ittai son of Ribai from Gibeah of the sons of Benjamin;

**30** Benaiah a Pirathonite; Hiddai of the brooks of Gaash;

**31** Abi-albon the Arbathite; Azmaveth the Barhumite;

**32** Eliahba the Shaalbonite; the sons of Jashen, Jonathan;

**33** Shammah the Hararite; Ahiam son of Sharar the Hararite;

**34** Eliphelet son of Ahasbai, son of the Maacathite; Eliam son of Ahithophel the Gilonite;

**35** Hezrai the Carmelite; Paarai the Arbite;

**36** Igal son of Nathan of Zobah; Bani the Gadite;

**37** Zelek the Ammonite; Naharai the Beerothite, armor-bearer of Joab son of Zeruiah;

**38** Ira the Ithrite; Gareb the Ithrite;

**39** Uriah the Hittite: thirty-seven in all.

## Notes

- **v1 "The word of David son of Jesse, the word of the man who was raised on high"** — *ne'um*, the word for a speech given by God, used in the oracles of Balaam, *the word of the man whose eye is opened* (Numbers 24:3). *The sweet singer of Israel*: *ne'im zemirot Yisra'el*, which can also be read **the favorite of the songs of Israel**.
- **v2 "The Spirit of the LORD spoke by me"** — the Spirit that *rushed on David from that day forward* when Samuel anointed him (1 Samuel 16:13).
- **vv4–5 "like the light of morning… an everlasting covenant"** — *berit olam*, the promise Nathan brought: *Your house and your kingdom shall be made sure forever* (7:16).
- **vv6–7 "the worthless are all like thorns"** — *beliyya'al*, the word of Sheba (20:1) and of the torrents of death in the song before (22:5). No one takes thorns in the hand; they are handled with iron and burned.
- **v8 "He was Adino the Eznite; he drew his sword against eight hundred"** — the Hebrew has *hu Adino ha-Etsni al shemoneh me'ot chalal*, **he was Adino the Eznite, against eight hundred slain**, with no verb. The Greek has *he drew his sword against eight hundred*. Chronicles names the man *Jashobeam son of Hachmoni* and has *he wielded his spear against three hundred slain at one time* (1 Chronicles 11:11), the words used of Abishai in v18. The text follows the Greek and keeps the Hebrew number.
- **v10 "his hand clung to the sword"** — *va-tidbaq yado el ha-cherev*: he fought until he could not open his hand. *The LORD brought about a great victory*, *teshu'ah*, is said of Eleazar and of Shammah (v12).
- **v11 "a plot of ground full of lentils"** — Chronicles tells this stand under Eleazar's name and has *full of barley* (1 Chronicles 11:13–14).
- **vv16–17 "he poured it out to the LORD… Is it not the blood of the men"** — the water was bought with the lives of three men, and David treats it as blood, which is not to be taken but poured out: *Only the blood you shall not eat; pour it out on the ground like water* (Deuteronomy 12:16).
- **v20 "the two Ariels of Moab… a lion inside a pit on a day of snow"** — *shenei ari'el Mo'av*. What an *ari'el* is here is not known: the Greek has *the two sons of Ariel*, making it a name. The word is built from *ari*, **lion**, and a lion follows in the same verse.
- **vv34, 39 "Eliam son of Ahithophel… Uriah the Hittite: thirty-seven in all"** — the list closes with Uriah, whom David had killed (11:15–17). The names in vv24–39 number thirty-one, or thirty-two if *the sons of Jashen* counts as one; with the Three, Abishai and Benaiah they make thirty-six or thirty-seven.

# 2 Samuel 4

## Rechab and Baanah

**1** When Saul's son heard that Abner had died in Hebron, his hands went limp, and all Israel was dismayed.

**2** Saul's son had two men who were captains of raiding bands. The name of the one was Baanah and the name of the other Rechab, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, of the sons of Benjamin, for Beeroth also is counted as part of Benjamin.

**3** The Beerothites had fled to Gittaim, and they have lived there as guests to this day.

**4** Jonathan, Saul's son, had a son who was lame in his feet. He was five years old when the news about Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel. His nurse picked him up and fled, and as she hurried to flee, he fell and became lame. His name was Mephibosheth.

**5** Rechab and Baanah, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, set out and came in the heat of the day to the house of Ish-bosheth, as he was taking his midday rest.

**6** They went into the middle of the house as if to fetch wheat, and they struck him in the belly. Then Rechab and Baanah his brother escaped.

**7** They went into the house as he lay on his bed in his bedroom, and they struck him and killed him and cut off his head. They took his head and went by the way of the Arabah all night.

**8** They brought the head of Ish-bosheth to David at Hebron and said to the king, "Here is the head of Ish-bosheth son of Saul, your enemy, who sought your life. The LORD has given my lord the king vengeance this day on Saul and on his seed."

## David's Judgment

**9** David answered Rechab and Baanah his brother, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, "As the LORD lives, who has redeemed my life out of every distress,

**10** when the one who told me, 'Saul is dead,' thought he was bringing good news, I seized him and killed him at Ziklag, which was the reward I gave him for his news.

**11** How much more, when wicked men have killed a righteous man in his own house on his bed! Now shall I not require his blood from your hand and wipe you from the earth?"

**12** David commanded the young men, and they killed them, cut off their hands and their feet and hung them up beside the pool at Hebron. But they took the head of Ish-bosheth and buried it in the tomb of Abner at Hebron.

## Notes

- **v1 "his hands went limp"** — *va-yirpu yadav*. With Abner dead, Saul's son has lost the man who made him king (2:8). The Hebrew calls him only *Saul's son* here and in v2; the Greek names him *Mephibosheth*, as it does throughout this chapter.
- **vv6–7 "They went into the middle of the house as if to fetch wheat… They went into the house as he lay on his bed"** — the Hebrew tells the entry and the killing twice. The Greek of v6 is different: *the doorkeeper of the house was cleaning wheat, and she dozed and slept, and Rechab and Baanah slipped in*.

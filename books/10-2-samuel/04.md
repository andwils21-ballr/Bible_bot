# 2 Samuel 4

## Rechab and Baanah

**1** When Saul's son heard that Abner had died in Hebron, his hands went limp, and all Israel was dismayed.

**2** Saul's son had two men who were captains of raiding bands. The name of the one was Baanah and the name of the other Rechab, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, of the sons of Benjamin, for Beeroth also is counted as part of Benjamin.

**3** The Beerothites had fled to Gittaim, and they have lived there as guests to this day.

**4** Jonathan, Saul's son, had a son who was lame in his feet. He was five years old when the news about Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel. His nurse picked him up and fled, and as she hurried to flee, he fell and became lame. His name was Mephibosheth.

**5** Rechab and Baanah, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, set out and came in the heat of the day to the house of Ish-bosheth, as he was taking his midday rest.

**6** They came into the middle of the house as if to fetch wheat, and they struck him in the belly. Then Rechab and Baanah his brother escaped.

**7** They came into the house as he lay on his bed in his bedroom, and they struck him and killed him and cut off his head. They took his head and went by the way of the Arabah all night.

**8** They brought the head of Ish-bosheth to David at Hebron and said to the king, "Here is the head of Ish-bosheth son of Saul, your enemy, who sought your life. The LORD has given my lord the king vengeance this day on Saul and on his seed."

## David's Judgment

**9** David answered Rechab and Baanah his brother, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, "As the LORD lives, who has redeemed my life out of every distress,

**10** when the one who told me, 'Saul is dead,' thought he was bringing good news, I seized him and killed him at Ziklag, which was the reward I gave him for his news.

**11** How much more, when wicked men have killed a righteous man in his own house on his bed! And now shall I not require his blood from your hand and wipe you from the earth?"

**12** David commanded the young men, and they killed them, cut off their hands and their feet and hung them up beside the pool at Hebron. But they took the head of Ish-bosheth and buried it in the tomb of Abner at Hebron.

## Notes

- **v1 "his hands went limp"** — *va-yirpu yadav*. With Abner dead, Saul's son has lost the man who made him king (2:8). The Hebrew calls him only *Saul's son* here and in v2; the Greek names him *Mephibosheth*, as it does throughout this chapter.
- **vv2–3 "sons of Rimmon the Beerothite… they have lived there as guests"** — *garim*, the fixed word for **guests**. Beeroth was one of the towns of the Gibeonites who made a treaty with Joshua (Joshua 9:17). The murderers are Benjaminites, of Saul's own tribe.
- **v4 "Jonathan… had a son who was lame in his feet… Mephibosheth"** — the verse stands in the middle of the murder story: with Ish-bosheth dead, the only grandson of Saul left of Jonathan's line is a lame child. He returns in chapter 9, when David seeks one of Saul's house for Jonathan's sake (9:1–3). His name is *Merib-baal* in 1 Chronicles 8:34; Samuel again has *boshet*, **shame**, for *Baal*, as with Ish-bosheth (2:8).
- **vv6–7 "They came into the middle of the house as if to fetch wheat… They came into the house as he lay on his bed"** — the Hebrew tells the entry and the killing twice. The Greek of v6 is different: *the doorkeeper of the house was cleaning wheat, and she dozed and slept, and Rechab and Baanah slipped in*.
- **v8 "The LORD has given my lord the king vengeance this day on Saul and on his seed"** — the murderers speak as if the LORD had done their work. David has refused to *put out his hand against the LORD's anointed* (1 Samuel 26:9) and sworn not to *cut off* Saul's *seed* (1 Samuel 24:21–22).
- **vv9–10 "who has redeemed my life out of every distress… I seized him and killed him at Ziklag"** — David takes an oath by the LORD who delivered him, and judges the murderers by the case of the Amalekite (1:14–16).
- **v11 "a righteous man in his own house on his bed"** — *ish tsaddiq*: Ish-bosheth did no wrong to the men who killed him. *Wipe you from the earth*, *u-vi'arti*, the verb of the law's refrain, *so you shall purge the evil from among you* (Deuteronomy 17:7).
- **v12 "cut off their hands and their feet and hung them up beside the pool at Hebron"** — the sentence is carried out openly, at the pool of the town. *The tomb of Abner*: Saul's son is buried with Saul's commander at Hebron.

# 2 Samuel 6

## Uzzah

**1** David again gathered all the chosen men of Israel, thirty thousand.

**2** David arose and went with all the people who were with him from Baale-judah to bring up from there the ark of God, which is called by the name, the name of the LORD of hosts, who is enthroned on the cherubim above it.

**3** They set the ark of God on a new cart and carried it from the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill. Uzzah and Ahio, the sons of Abinadab, were driving the new cart.

**4** They carried it from the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill, with the ark of God, and Ahio was walking in front of the ark.

**5** David and all the house of Israel were celebrating before the LORD with all their might and with songs, with lyres, harps, tambourines, rattles and cymbals.

**6** When they came to the threshing floor of Nacon, Uzzah put out his hand to the ark of God and took hold of it, for the oxen stumbled.

**7** The anger of the LORD burned against Uzzah, and God struck him down there for his error, and he died there beside the ark of God.

**8** David was angry because the LORD had broken out against Uzzah, and that place is called Perez-uzzah to this day.

**9** David was afraid of the LORD that day and said, "How can the ark of the LORD come to me?"

**10** So David was not willing to take the ark of the LORD into the City of David to be with him. David turned it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.

**11** The ark of the LORD stayed in the house of Obed-edom the Gittite three months, and the LORD blessed Obed-edom and all his household.

## David Dances

**12** King David was told, "The LORD has blessed the house of Obed-edom and all that belongs to him because of the ark of God." So David went and brought up the ark of God from the house of Obed-edom to the City of David with rejoicing.

**13** When those who carried the ark of the LORD had gone six steps, he sacrificed an ox and a fatted calf.

**14** David danced with all his might before the LORD, and David was wearing a linen ephod.

**15** So David and all the house of Israel brought up the ark of the LORD with shouting and with the sound of the horn.

**16** As the ark of the LORD came into the City of David, Michal the daughter of Saul looked down through the window and saw King David leaping and dancing before the LORD, and she despised him in her heart.

**17** They brought in the ark of the LORD and set it in its place inside the tent that David had pitched for it. Then David offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD.

**18** When David had finished offering the burnt offerings and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD of hosts.

**19** He distributed to all the people, the whole multitude of Israel, both men and women, to each a loaf of bread, a portion of meat and a cake of raisins. Then all the people went away, each to his house.

## Michal

**20** David returned to bless his household, and Michal the daughter of Saul came out to meet David and said, "How the king of Israel has honored himself today, uncovering himself today in the sight of his servants' slave girls, as one of the empty fellows shamelessly uncovers himself!"

**21** David said to Michal, "It was before the LORD, who chose me above your father and above all his house to appoint me leader over the people of the LORD, over Israel. I will celebrate before the LORD.

**22** I will make myself even more lightly esteemed than this, and I will be humble in my own eyes. But by the slave girls you have spoken of, by them I shall be held in honor."

**23** Michal the daughter of Saul had no child to the day of her death.

## Notes

- **v2 "from Baale-judah… called by the name, the name of the LORD of hosts"** — *Ba'alei Yehudah*, the town Joshua calls *Baalah, that is, Kiriath-jearim* (Joshua 15:9), where the ark had stayed since the Philistines returned it (1 Samuel 7:1–2). *The name, the name* stands twice in the Hebrew.
- **vv3–4 "carried it from the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill"** — the words stand twice in the Hebrew, at the end of v3 and the start of v4; the Greek has them once. The text keeps the Hebrew as it is. *A new cart*: the Philistines sent the ark home *on a new cart* (1 Samuel 6:7). The law gives the ark to the Levites to carry on poles (Numbers 4:15, Exodus 25:14); David's first attempt copies the Philistines.
- **v5 "with all their might and with songs"** — the Hebrew has *with all kinds of cypress wood*, *be-khol atsei veroshim*. The Greek and the parallel in Chronicles have *with all their might and with songs*, *be-khol oz u-ve-shirim* (1 Chronicles 13:8); the Hebrew letters of the two readings are close. The text follows the Greek and Chronicles.
- **vv6–7 "Uzzah put out his hand… God struck him down there for his error"** — *al ha-shal*, a word found only here; *error* or *irreverence* is the sense from the context. Chronicles has *because he put out his hand on the ark* (1 Chronicles 13:10). The law warned that the Kohathites *they shall not touch the holy things, or they will die* (Numbers 4:15).
- **v8 "Perez-uzzah… the LORD had broken out against Uzzah"** — *perets*, the word of *Baal-perazim*, where the LORD broke through David's enemies (5:20). The same power that broke out for David now breaks out at his carelessness.
- **v11 "the LORD blessed Obed-edom and all his household"** — a Gittite, a man of Gath, keeps the ark that killed Uzzah, and is blessed by it.
- **v14 "David danced with all his might… wearing a linen ephod"** — *ephod bad*, the garment of the boy Samuel serving at Shiloh (1 Samuel 2:18) and of the priests of Nob (1 Samuel 22:18). *Mekharker*, **whirling**, a verb found only in this chapter.
- **v16 "Michal the daughter of Saul… despised him in her heart"** — she is called *the daughter of Saul* three times here (vv16, 20, 23) and never *David's wife*. She once *loved David* (1 Samuel 18:20) and let him down through a window to save his life (1 Samuel 19:12); now she looks down through a window and despises him.
- **v19 "a portion of meat and a cake of raisins"** — *eshpar*, found only here and in the parallel (1 Chronicles 16:3); its meaning is uncertain, and *a portion of meat* is one reading of it.
- **vv21–23 "who chose me above your father… Michal had no child"** — David answers the daughter of Saul with the LORD's choice of him over Saul's house. With Michal childless, no heir of Saul's line comes from David's house.

# 2 Samuel 22

## The LORD My Rock

**1** David spoke to the LORD the words of this song on the day the LORD delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.

**2** He said:  
"The LORD is my rock, my fortress and my deliverer,

**3** my God, my rock, in whom I take refuge,  
my shield and the horn of my salvation,  
my stronghold and my refuge,  
my savior; You save me from violence.

**4** I call on the LORD, who is worthy to be praised,  
and I am saved from my enemies.

## Out of Many Waters

**5** For the breakers of death surrounded me;  
the torrents of worthlessness terrified me.

**6** The cords of Sheol were around me;  
the snares of death confronted me.

**7** In my distress I called on the LORD;  
to my God I called.  
From His temple He heard my voice,  
and my cry for help came into His ears.

**8** Then the earth shook and quaked;  
the foundations of the heavens trembled  
and shook, because He was angry.

**9** Smoke went up from His nostrils,  
and devouring fire from His mouth;  
coals blazed out from Him.

**10** He bent the heavens and came down,  
with thick darkness under His feet.

**11** He rode on a cherub and flew;  
He was seen on the wings of the wind.

**12** He made darkness a covering around Him,  
a gathering of waters, thick clouds of the skies.

**13** From the brightness before Him  
coals of fire blazed out.

**14** The LORD thundered from the heavens;  
the Most High uttered His voice.

**15** He sent out arrows and scattered them,  
lightning, and routed them.

**16** The channels of the sea were seen;  
the foundations of the world were laid bare  
at the rebuke of the LORD,  
at the blast of the breath of His nostrils.

**17** He reached from on high and took me;  
He drew me out of many waters.

**18** He delivered me from my strong enemy,  
from those who hated me,  
for they were too mighty for me.

**19** They confronted me on the day of my calamity,  
but the LORD was my support.

**20** He brought me out into a broad place;  
He rescued me, because He delighted in me.

## "With the Loyal You Show Yourself Loyal"

**21** The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness;  
according to the cleanness of my hands He repaid me.

**22** For I have kept the ways of the LORD,  
and have not wickedly departed from my God.

**23** For all His judgments were before me,  
and from His statutes I did not turn aside.

**24** I was blameless before Him,  
and I kept myself from my guilt.

**25** So the LORD repaid me according to my righteousness,  
according to my cleanness in His sight.

**26** With the loyal You show Yourself loyal;  
with the blameless man You show Yourself blameless.

**27** With the pure You show Yourself pure,  
and with the crooked You show Yourself twisted.

**28** You save a humble people,  
but Your eyes are on the haughty, to bring them down.

**29** For You are my lamp, O LORD;  
the LORD lights up my darkness.

**30** For by You I can run against a troop;  
by my God I can leap over a wall.

**31** This God — His way is blameless;  
the word of the LORD is refined.  
He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.

**32** For who is God but the LORD?  
Who is a rock but our God?

## The God Who Trains My Hands

**33** This God is my strong fortress,  
and He has set my way free and blameless.

**34** He makes my feet like the feet of a deer  
and sets me on my high places.

**35** He trains my hands for war,  
so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze.

**36** You have given me the shield of Your salvation,  
and Your answering has made me great.

**37** You made room for my steps under me,  
and my ankles did not give way.

**38** I pursued my enemies and destroyed them;  
I did not turn back until they were consumed.

**39** I consumed them; I struck them through, so that they could not rise;  
they fell under my feet.

**40** You girded me with strength for battle;  
You made those who rose against me bow under me.

**41** You made my enemies turn their backs to me;  
those who hated me I destroyed.

**42** They looked, but there was no one to save them;  
to the LORD, but He did not answer them.

**43** I beat them fine as the dust of the earth;  
I crushed them and stamped them down like the mud of the streets.

**44** You delivered me from the strife of my people;  
You kept me as the head of nations.  
A people I did not know serve me.

**45** Foreigners come cringing to me;  
as soon as they hear, they obey me.

**46** Foreigners lose heart;  
they gird themselves and come out of their strongholds.

## "The LORD Lives"

**47** The LORD lives! Blessed be my rock,  
and exalted be God, the rock of my salvation,

**48** the God who gives me vengeance  
and brings down peoples under me,

**49** who brings me out from my enemies.  
You exalt me above those who rise against me;  
You deliver me from the man of violence.

**50** Therefore I will give thanks to You, O LORD, among the nations,  
and sing praises to Your name.

**51** He is a tower of salvation for His king,  
and shows loyal love to His anointed,  
to David and to his seed forever."

## Notes

- **v1 "the words of this song"** — the song stands again as Psalm 18, with its own title, *on the day the LORD delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul*. The two copies differ in dozens of small places; the notes below give the ones that change the sense.
- **v5 "the breakers of death… the torrents of worthlessness"** — *mishberei mavet*, the waves that break over a man. Psalm 18:4 has *chevlei mavet*, **the cords of death**, matching *the cords of Sheol* in the next line. *Beliyya'al*, **worthlessness**, the word used of Sheba and of Eli's sons (20:1; 1 Samuel 2:12), stands here beside death and Sheol.
- **v11 "He was seen on the wings of the wind"** — *va-yera*. Psalm 18:10 has *va-yede*, **He swooped**, as in *swooping as the eagle swoops* (Deuteronomy 28:49). The two words differ in one letter, and the Greek has *was seen*, as here.
- **v12 "a gathering of waters"** — *chashrat mayim*, a word found only here; Psalm 18:11 has *cheshkhat mayim*, **darkness of waters**, and the Greek agrees with the Psalm. The sense of *chashrat* is uncertain.
- **vv26–27 "With the loyal You show Yourself loyal… with the crooked You show Yourself twisted"** — *chasid*, the one who keeps *chesed*, loyal love. *Tittappal*, from the root of *petaltol*, **twisted**, in *a crooked and twisted generation* (Deuteronomy 32:5).
- **v29 "For You are my lamp, O LORD"** — *ki attah niri*. Psalm 18:28 has *You light my lamp*. The chapter before called David *the lamp of Israel* (21:17); here the LORD is his.
- **v33 "He has set my way free and blameless"** — *va-yatter tamim darki*, from *natar*, **to let loose**. Psalm 18:32 has *va-yitten*, **and He made my way blameless**; the Greek has *shook out*. The written text has *darko*, **His way**; the text follows the read form, *darki*, **my way**.
- **v36 "Your answering has made me great"** — *va-anotekha*, from *anah*, **to answer**; the Greek has *your hearing* (*hupakoē*). Psalm 18:35 has *anvatkha*, **Your humility**, God stooping down. The Hebrew of Samuel is kept.
- **v46 "they gird themselves and come out of their strongholds"** — *va-yachgeru*, **gird themselves**. Psalm 18:45 has *va-yachregu*, **come trembling**, with two letters in the other order. The Hebrew of Samuel is kept.
- **v51 "He is a tower of salvation for His king"** — the written text has *magdil*, **He makes great**, and the read form is *migdol*, **a tower**; the text follows the read form. Psalm 18:50 is the reverse: written *migdol*, read *magdil*. The Greek has *making great*.

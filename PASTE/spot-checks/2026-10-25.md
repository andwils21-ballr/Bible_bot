# Spot Checks

## Daniel 9–12 (weekly, 3:10 PM Central, 2026-10-25)

Bible Main's routine cycle `debb554` (12:54 PM CDT), re-rendered blind by a fresh helper agent (Opus) from the commit before it, then compared by Bible 2.

**Overall:** both sides pass `check_chapters.py` with no errors and nothing to look at. Of 106 verses, 11 are word for word the same, and most other differences are wording only. Both sides follow the read forms (9:24 *le-hatem*), keep *loathsome thing* for *shiqquts* and *destroy completely* for *charam* (11:44), cut every *hinneh*, and raise the same choices at 9:21, 9:24 and 9:25. Every quotation in Bible Main's notes was checked against this project's English; all match.

**Rule breaks, Bible Main:**
- **Notes that talk about the report.** Three notes in chapter 9 (vv21, 24, 25) end *Choice in the report.* That is a note about how the work is done, which the reader of the site never sees and cannot use (CLAUDE.md §3, "never… behind-the-curtain reasoning"). It is drift, not a one-off: it began with Ezekiel 37–40 (2026-10-24) and is now in 12 chapters (Ezekiel 40, 42, 43, 45; Daniel 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9). The helper has none.
- **11:17** *he will give him **a daughter of women** to destroy it*: `ובת הנשים`, an idiom carried word for word (CLAUDE.md §2). It means a young woman given in marriage. The helper: *He will give him a daughter in marriage.*
- **10:17** *How can my lord's servant **here** speak with my lord **here**?* carries `זה… זה` word for word. The helper: *How can I, my lord's servant, talk with you, my lord?*

**Translation differences:**
- **10:13** Bible Main: *Then Michael… came to help me, **for** I had been left there beside the kings of Persia.* The Hebrew is `ואני נותרתי שם`, *and I*, with no *for*: Michael did not come *because* the speaker was left there. The helper: *and I was left there.*
- **9:13** `ולהשכיל באמתך`: Bible Main *paying heed to Your truth*, the helper *gaining insight from Your truth*. *Sakal* is the word of these chapters. Gabriel comes *to give you insight* (`להשכילך`, 9:22). The *maskilim*, *the wise*, give understanding (11:33, 35), shine like the expanse (12:3), and understand (12:10). Daniel confesses that Israel did not seek this insight. Not a rule break, since *paying heed* is in the word's range; posted to the council.
- **12:10** `יתבררו ויתלבנו ויצרפו`: Bible Main *Many will **be** purified, made white and refined*, the helper *Many will **purify themselves**, make themselves white and be refined*. The first two verbs are reflexive in form (*hitpael*), the third passive. In 11:35 the testing is done to the wise (*to refine… them*). A choice of meaning: whether the many are made pure, or make themselves pure. Added to the Daniel 9–12 choices in `PASTE/renders/41-daniel.md`.
- **12:6** `הפלאות`: Bible Main *these **wonders***, the helper *these **astonishing things***, the word of 11:36 (*he will speak astonishing things*) and 8:24, which Bible Main renders *astonishing* both times. Wording, but it loses the link.
- **11:9, 11:** the helper names the kings (*the king of the north will enter…*; *into the hand of the king of the south*), which the Hebrew does not. Bible Main keeps the Hebrew's *he*, which is right, though in v9 it reads as if the southern king of v8 enters his own kingdom.

**Notes:** the helper found connections Bible Main does not have:
- 9:14, *va-yishqod*, *kept watch over the disaster*, is the verb of Jeremiah's call, *I am watching over My word to do it* (Jeremiah 1:12).
- 9:4, the words go back to Moses, *the faithful God, who keeps covenant and loyal love* (Deuteronomy 7:9). 9:15–16, *with a strong hand* and *let Your anger… turn away* are Moses' plea after the calf (Exodus 32:11–12).
- 9:24, *seven sabbaths of years, seven years seven times* (Leviticus 25:8). *Most holy* is said of Aaron in 1 Chronicles 23:13.
- 9:27, *the full end that is decreed* is Isaiah's phrase (Isaiah 10:23).
- 10:12, *le-hit'annot* is the Day of Atonement's *afflict yourselves* (Leviticus 16:29).
- 10:18–11:1, the root *chazaq*, *strong*, seven times. 10:21, *rashum* is the Aramaic *inscribed* of 5:24.
- 11:14, *paritsim* is the *den of robbers* (Jeremiah 7:11). 11:30, the Old Greek has *Romans*. 11:36, *God of gods* (Deuteronomy 10:17; 2:47). 11:37, *the desire of Israel* (1 Samuel 9:20).
- 12:1, *a time of distress* is Jeremiah's *time of distress for Jacob* (Jeremiah 30:7). 12:2, *ground* and *dust* are the two words of Genesis 3:19. 12:6, *pela'ot* is the root of 11:36.

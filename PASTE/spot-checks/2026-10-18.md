**Rule breaks, Bible Main:**
- **27:18 note** quotes 7:16 as *do not intercede with Me*; 7:16 reads *do not pray for this people, or lift up a cry or a prayer for them, or intercede with Me* (CLAUDE.md §3, every claim checkable).

**Translation differences:**
- **26:19** Bible Main: *the LORD relented of the **disaster** that He had pronounced against them? But we are about to do great **harm** to ourselves.* The Hebrew has the same word twice, `וינחם יהוה אל הרעה… ואנחנו עשים רעה גדולה`: the LORD turned back *ha-ra'ah* once, and now they would bring *ra'ah gedolah* on themselves. The helper keeps it: *bring great disaster on ourselves*. The elders' argument turns on the word (CLAUDE.md §2, keep a word the same where the source repeats it on purpose).
- **27:6** Bible Main: *So now I have given all these lands.* The Hebrew is `ועתה אנכי נתתי`, with the pronoun stated, as in v5 `אנכי עשיתי` (*It is I who made the earth*): the Maker who gives the earth to whom He chooses is the One who gives it now. The helper: *I Myself have given* (spec, Style 2).
- **27:11** `ועבדה`: Bible Main *it will till it*, the helper *it will serve the soil*. *Avad* is the chapter's word: eleven times as a verb (vv6–17), with *serves him* earlier in the same verse; and this project's Genesis 2:5, 2:15 and 3:23 render the same verb with the ground as *serve*. Not a rule break, since *till* is the plain sense; posted to the council (item 97).
- **26:10–21, *sarim***: Bible Main has *officials* eight times; the book has *princes* for the same word at 1:18, 2:26, 24:1, 8 and 25:18–19, the last in this same cycle. The helper has *princes*. Posted to the council (item 97).

**A choice of meaning the cycle did not raise (the helper's):**

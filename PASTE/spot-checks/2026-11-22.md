**Words kept the same, or not** (posted to the council, item 116):

The helper keeps *humiliation* both times.

The helper keeps *anxious* both times, with a note (Philippians 4:6; 1 Peter 5:7): the faithful are anxious about nothing because the Lord is anxious for them.

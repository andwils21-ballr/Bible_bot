# Spot Checks

## Isaiah 31–34 (weekly, 3:10 PM Central, 2026-10-11)

Bible Main's routine cycle `de6bc1e` (12:48 PM CDT), re-rendered blind by a fresh helper agent (Opus) from the commit before it, then compared by Bible 2.

**Overall:** both sides pass `check_chapters.py` with no errors and nothing to look at. Of 70 verses only 5 are word for word the same (last week 48 of 96), but most differences are wording only. Both sides wrote notes on the same main points: Genesis 6:3 at 31:3, the Passover verb at 31:5, Psalm 15 at 33:14, Revelation 6:13–14 at 34:4, Joshua 18:10 at 34:17.

**Rule breaks, Bible Main:**
- **34:16 "For His mouth has commanded."** The Hebrew is `כי פי הוא צוה`, *for **my** mouth, it has commanded*. *His* follows another witness (the Greek has *the Lord commanded them*), and no note names it (spec, "When the witnesses differ", rule 5). Same kind of break as 2 Chronicles 8:16 last week.
- **32:9, 11, 18 and 33:20, *sha'anan*.** Bible Main's own note on vv9, 11, 18 says the word is repeated on purpose (*the same word, the false ease taken away and the true one given*), but the verses render it *at ease* (vv9, 11) and *quiet* (v18; also 33:20, *a quiet home*), so the English does not show it (CLAUDE.md §2, "Keep a word the same where the source repeats it on purpose"). The helper has *untroubled* in all four.
- **33:10 *eromam… ennase*.** The note says these are the two verbs of 6:1, *high and lifted up* (*ram ve-nissa*), but the verse renders *eromam* (*rum*, 6:1's *high*) as *lifted up* and *ennase* (*nasa*, 6:1's *lifted up*) as *exalted*, so the echo the note claims is not in the English (same rule). The helper has *rise high… lifted up*.
- **34:11, the note quotes Genesis 1:2 as *formless and void*.** The project's Genesis 1:2 reads *wild and waste*, and the verse renders *tohu… vohu* as *chaos… emptiness*, so neither the quotation nor the echo matches (CLAUDE.md §3, every claim checkable). The helper has *the measuring line of the wild and the plumb stones of the waste*.
- **34:11 *qa'at* as *owl*.** Leviticus 11:18 and Deuteronomy 14:17 render the same bird *pelican*; Bible Main also uses *owl* for a different word (*qippoz*) in v15. The helper has *pelican*.

**Translation differences:**
- **33:8** Bible Main: *The covenant is broken; cities are despised; there is no regard for man.* The Hebrew is active, third person: `הפר ברית מאס ערים לא חשב אנוש`, *he has broken the covenant, he has despised the cities, he has no regard for anyone* (the helper). The passive hides who does it (the destroyer of v1).
- **34:3–4** Bible Main: *the mountains will **flow** with their blood*; *all their host will **fall** as leaves fall*. The Hebrew is `ונמסו`, *melt*, and `יבול כנבל`, *wither as a leaf withers* (the helper; the same verb as *you will surely wither*, Exodus 18:18).
- **33:6** Bible Main: ***He** will be the stability of your times.* The Hebrew `והיה אמונת עתיך` has no person for subject (*the stability of your times will be*); *He* makes it God.
- **31:6** Bible Main: *Return to Him from whom **you** have so deeply turned away, sons of Israel.* The Hebrew `העמיקו סרה` is third person, *they have made rebellion deep* (the helper: *against whom the sons of Israel have rebelled so deeply*).
- **32:14** *the hill* (Bible Main) and *the Ophel* (the helper) for `עפל`; the Ophel is a named part of Jerusalem (CLAUDE.md §6, received names).

**Choices for Andrew (the helper raised them; Bible Main settled them without a note):**
- **32:2** *Each will be like a hiding place* (Bible Main) or *A man will be like a hiding place* (the helper). The Hebrew is `איש`, which is either. *A man* can be the king of v1; *each* is each prince.
- **33:17** *Your eyes will see the King in His beauty* (Bible Main) or *the king in his beauty* (the helper). The Hebrew has no article (`מלך ביפיו`, *a king*), as *a king* in 32:1, and *yofi* is the word for the king of Psalm 45:2; but 6:5 is *my eyes have seen the King, the LORD of hosts*, and v22 says *the LORD is our king*. Bible Main's own note says *the king of v17 is not named*, yet the capitals name Him. The capitals decide what the Hebrew leaves open.

**Notes:** the helper found connections Bible Main does not have: the root *ezer* four times in 31:1–3; 6:10 reversed in 32:3–4 (*understand* promised where it was forbidden); the tent that never *sets out* (33:20, Numbers 10:12); *nesu avon*, *carried of iniquity*, with Exodus 34:7 (33:24). Bible Main has several the helper missed: Ariel's hearth at 31:9, the read form *aniyyim* at 32:7, 11:2 at 33:6, 28:11 at 33:19, Maher-shalal-hash-baz at 33:23.

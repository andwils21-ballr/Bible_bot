Bible Main's routine cycle `8f25836` (2:08 PM CST), re-rendered blind by a fresh helper agent (Opus) from the commit before it, then compared by Bible 2.

**Overall:** both sides pass `check_chapters.py` with no errors and nothing to look at. Of 76 verses, 16 are word for word the same, and most other differences are wording only. Both sides read *Your throne, O God* (1:8) and *for a little while lower than the angels* (2:7, 9), keep *him* lowercase in Psalm 8 until the writer names Jesus, and give Onesimus's name its note. Bible Main's quotations of this project's English were checked (Psalm 8:5, 45:6, 95:8–10, 104:4; Exodus 17:7; Wisdom of Solomon 7:26), and its Greek claims against Swete; one note overclaims, below.

**Rule breaks, Bible Main:**
- **Hebrews 2:9** *so that by the grace of God He might taste death*: the edition reads `χωρὶς θεοῦ`, **apart from God**, and marks it as a place where manuscripts differ. The text follows the other reading, `χάριτι θεοῦ`, with no witness named, no note, and no choice raised (CLAUDE.md §2: no rendering that leaves the source with no witness named). The helper follows the edition, with a note on both readings.
- **Hebrews 1:3** drops `δι’ αὑτοῦ`, **through Himself**, which the edition prints: *After making purification for sins*. The helper has it. The report says the bracketed words were included.
- **Hebrews 3:2** *faithful in all God's house*: the edition has `ἐν τῷ οἴκῳ αὐτοῦ`, no *all* (`ὅλῳ` is the other reading, and is in v5). The *all* comes from v5 and Numbers 12:7.
- **Hebrews 3:10** *that generation*: the edition has `ταύτῃ`, **this**; *that* is the psalm's word (Psalm 95:10, Greek `ἐκείνῃ`). The helper: *this generation*.
- **Hebrews 3:9–10** puts *for forty years* at the head of v10, as the edition's numbering does. The KJV numbering, which this project follows, ends v9 with *saw my works forty years*. The helper follows the KJV.
- **Hebrews 1, vv6–7 note**: *The first is the longer Greek of Moses' song (Deuteronomy 32:43).* In Swete that verse has *let the sons of God worship Him* (`προσκυνησάτωσαν αὐτῷ υἱοὶ θεοῦ`), and *all the angels of God* in another line, with *be strong in Him*. Hebrews' words are in neither line; the nearest is the Greek of Psalm 97:7, *worship Him, all His angels* (Swete Psalm 96:7). CLAUDE.md §3, every claim checkable. The helper's note says this exactly.

**Rule breaks, helper:** none in the text. Its notes break the notes rule, below.

**Choices of meaning the cycle did not raise** (the helper raised all four; added to the renders files):
- **Hebrews 2:9** `χωρὶς θεοῦ` or `χάριτι θεοῦ`, above.
- **Hebrews 1:6** `ὅταν δὲ πάλιν εἰσαγάγῃ`: *Again, when He brings* (Bible Main: *again* introduces another quotation, the first coming) or *when He again brings* (the helper: the second coming; *palin* stands next to the verb).
- **Hebrews 1:7** `πνεύματα`: *winds* (Bible Main, with a note) or *spirits* (the helper). v14 calls the angels `λειτουργικὰ πνεύματα`, *ministering spirits*, the same two nouns.
- **Philemon 9** `πρεσβύτης`: *an old man* (both) or *an ambassador*, the spelling the Greek Old Testament uses for envoys (2 Chronicles 32:31; 2 Maccabees 11:34), and Paul's word for himself in Ephesians 6:20.

**Words kept the same, or not** (posted to the council):
- **Hebrews 1:9; 3:1, 14** `μέτοχοι`: Bible Main *companions* (1:9), *who share in* (3:1), *we have come to share in Christ* (3:14). The helper keeps *companions* all three times: those the Son was anointed above (1:9) are named His companions (3:14).
- **Hebrews 2:18** `πειρασθείς… πειραζομένοις`: Bible Main *tempted*, beside *testing… put Me to the test* for the same root in 3:8–9; this project's Matthew 4:1 and 1 Corinthians 10:13 have *tested*. The helper: *tested*.
- **Philemon 6, 17** `κοινωνία… κοινωνόν`: Bible Main *sharing… partner*. The helper: *partnership… partner*, and Paul's appeal in v17 rests on the word of v6.

**Translation differences:**
- **Hebrews 2:16** `ἐπιλαμβάνεται`: Bible Main *He helps*; the helper *He takes hold of*, the verb of Jeremiah 31:32 in Greek, which Hebrews quotes at 8:9 (*I took them by the hand*). Both are in the word's range; the helper's keeps the link to 8:9.

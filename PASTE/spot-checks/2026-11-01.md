## Mark 15–16, Luke 1–2 (weekly, 3:10 PM Central, 2026-11-01)

Bible Main's routine cycle `6852fb8` (1:44 PM CST), re-rendered blind by a fresh helper agent (Opus) from the commit before it, then compared by Bible 2. It is the first spot check of a New Testament cycle.

**Overall:** both sides pass `check_chapters.py` with no errors. The only CHECKs are the missing Mark 15:28, which both notes explain, and one false lead in the helper's notes. Of 198 verses, 64 are word for word the same, and most other differences are wording only. Both sides keep *angel* for *angelos* (spec, "Already ruled"), capital pronouns for Jesus, *the Son of God* at Mark 15:39 with the other reading in a note, vv9–20 of Mark 16 in the text with the shorter ending beside them, *guest room* at Luke 2:7, and *My Father's house* at Luke 2:49. Every quotation in Bible Main's notes was checked against this project's English. All match but one, below.

**Rule breaks, Bible Main:**
- **Luke 1, vv46–55 note**, a broken sentence: *…on those who fear Him (Psalm 103:17). as Isaiah's Israel, My servant… the offspring of Abraham (Isaiah 41:8).* The second half has no subject, and it does not say which line of the song it explains (v54, *He has helped His servant Israel*).
- **Luke 1, vv34–38 note**: *Ouk adynatēsei para tou theou pan rhēma is the Greek of the LORD's word to Abraham… (Genesis 18:14).* The Greek of Genesis 18:14 is `μὴ ἀδυνατεῖ παρὰ τῷ θεῷ ῥῆμα`: the same verb and noun, but not the same words (CLAUDE.md §3, every claim checkable).

**Words kept the same, or not** (posted to the council, item 99):
- **Mark 15:21** `ἵνα ἄρῃ τὸν σταυρὸν αὐτοῦ`: Bible Main *to carry His cross*. Its own 8:34 has *take up his cross* for `ἀράτω τὸν σταυρὸν αὐτοῦ`, and the passer-by does word for word what Jesus asked of anyone who follows Him. The helper: *to take up His cross*, with a note.
- **Luke 1:58** `ἐμεγάλυνεν κύριος τὸ ἔλεος αὐτοῦ`: Bible Main *the Lord had shown great mercy to her*. It is the verb of Mary's *My soul magnifies* (v46), and the neighbors say of Elizabeth what Mary sang. The helper: *had magnified His mercy*.
- **Luke 2:4, 11** `πόλιν Δαυίδ`: Bible Main *the town of David*. This project has *the City of David* for Zion in Jerusalem, from 2 Samuel 5:7 through Nehemiah and Isaiah 22, and Luke gives the name to Bethlehem. *Town* hides that shift. The helper: *the city of David*, with a note.
- ***Egeneto*** at the head of a scene (Luke 1:5, 8, 23, 41, 59; 2:1, 6, 15, 46): Bible Main renders all of them plainly, as in Mark (1:9; 2:23). The helper keeps *And it came to pass* where a new time opens (1:59; 2:1, 46). This project's Matthew keeps it at the five closings of the discourses (7:28; 11:1; 13:53; 19:1; 26:1). The spec's rule (*kept only where it opens something*) is written for *va-yehi*. Whether it applies to the Greek is open, and it will touch about 35 verses in Luke and many in Acts.

**Translation differences:**
- **Luke 1:37** `οὐκ ἀδυνατήσει παρὰ τοῦ θεοῦ πᾶν ῥῆμα`: Bible Main *no word from God will be impossible*; the helper *no word from God will be without power*. Both follow the edition's *from God*; the meaning is the same.
- **Luke 2:36** `ἀπὸ τῆς παρθενίας αὐτῆς`: Bible Main *from her virginity*, word for word; the helper *from the time she was married*. Wording, but Bible Main's reads awkwardly.

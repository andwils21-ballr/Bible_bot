**Overall:** both sides pass `check_chapters.py` with no errors and nothing to

**Rule breaks, Bible Main:**
- **7:13 and its note.** *Chagav* is rendered **locust**, but *chagav* is
  *grasshopper* in Leviticus 11:22 and Numbers 13:33, and *locust* is *arbeh*
  (Leviticus 11:22; 6:28 here). The note on vv13–15 then says the LORD answers
  the prayer "in its own words: … *locust* … (6:28)", but the Hebrew words are
  different (*chagav* in 7:13, *arbeh* in 6:28), so the claimed echo is only in
  the English.
- **8:16 "from the day the foundation… was laid".** The Hebrew is *ad ha-yom
  musad*, **up to the day** of the foundation. *From* is a departure from the
  Hebrew with no note naming a witness (spec, "When the witnesses differ",
  rule 5).

**Translation differences:**
- **5:9 "They are there to this day"** (Bible Main, the poles, as 1 Kings 8:8)
  and **"It is there"** (helper). The Hebrew of Chronicles is *va-yehi sham*,
  singular, which can also be impersonal. Bible Main follows Kings without a
  note.
- **6:42 *chasdei David*.** Bible Main reads **the loyal love shown to David**,
  one of two readings, and its note gives only that one (Isaiah 55:3). The
  helper reads **the loyal love of David** and its note gives both: the LORD's
  love promised to David, or David's own deeds of loyal love (the same noun for
  Hezekiah's deeds, 32:32, and Josiah's, 35:26). This is an alternate reading
  of the kind CLAUDE.md §2 gives to Andrew, and Bible Main settled it alone.

**The helper's own mistakes:**
- **6:42 "Your anointed one"**, singular. The Hebrew of Chronicles is written
  *meshichekha* with the plural *yod* (מְשִׁיחֶיךָ), where Psalm 132:10 has the
  singular. Bible Main's **anointed ones** is right; the helper's note cites the
  psalm without saying Chronicles differs.
- **6:28 "caterpillar"** for *chasil*: right to keep *chasil* apart from
  *chagav*, but it is a new fixed term, which only Andrew can set.

**Choice raised (affects many books):** *chasil*. 1 Kings 8:37 reads *locust or
grasshopper* for *arbeh ve-chasil*, which gives *grasshopper* to two Hebrew
words (*chasil* there, *chagav* in Leviticus and Numbers). Joel 1:4 and 2:25
name four kinds side by side (*gazam, arbeh, yeleq, chasil*), so the four need
four English words before Joel; deciding them together now would settle 1 Kings
8:37, 2 Chronicles 6:28 and 7:13 at once.

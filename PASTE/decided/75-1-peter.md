## 1 Peter 2–5 — rendered 2026-11-17, 1:10 AM CST

**Choices for you:**
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-17: (a) **the pure milk of the word**. **2:2, *to logikon adolon gala***
  - (a) **the pure milk of the word** (now): *logikos* from *logos*, the word of God just named in 1:23–25.
  - (b) **pure spiritual milk**: *logikos* as **of the mind, spiritual**, as in Romans 12:1.
  - Recommend (a): no English word keeps both; *the word* carries Peter's run from 1:23 into 2:2, and the note gives (b).
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-17: (b) **made alive by the Spirit**. Before: *made alive in the spirit*. After: *made alive by the Spirit*, with the note on vv18–20 giving *in spirit*. Andrew: if the spirit is where Jesus now lives, it is addressed with a capital S; and Greek has no *the*, so *the spirit* reads as a special spirit. **3:18, *zōopoiētheis de pneumati***
  - (a) **made alive in the spirit** (now): set against *in the flesh*, His spirit as against His body.
  - (b) **made alive by the Spirit**: the Holy Spirit, who raised Him.
  - Recommend (a): English has to choose a capital here, and the lowercase keeps Peter's pair, *flesh* and *spirit*, as he repeats it in 4:6; the note gives (b).

## 1 Peter 1 — rendered 2026-11-16, 7:09 PM CST

**Choices for you:**
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-16: (a) **what time, or what kind of time**. **1:11, *eis tina ē poion kairon***
  - (a) **what time, or what kind of time** (now): *tina* goes with *kairon*, and the prophets asked when it would be and what the time would be like.
  - (b) **what person or what time**: *tina* stands alone, **whom**; the prophets asked who the Christ would be, and when.
  - Recommend (a): the two words are joined by *or* before one noun, and v10 says what they searched for, *this salvation*.

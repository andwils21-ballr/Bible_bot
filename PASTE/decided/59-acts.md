## Acts 27–28 — rendered 2026-11-07, 11:05 AM CST

**Choices for you:**
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-07: (a) **prayed for day to come**. **27:29, *ēuchonto***
  - (a) **prayed for day to come** (now): the sailors pray, as the sailors of the Greek Jonah, the chapter's echo, cry each to his god (Jonah 1:5).
  - (b) **longed for day to come**: a wish, with no one addressed.
  - Recommend (a): *euchomai* is *pray* in Paul's *I would pray to God* (26:29), and the chapter is built on Jonah's storm.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-07: (a) **Justice**, capital J. **28:4, *hē dikē***
  - (a) **Justice** (now): the goddess who repays, a power the islanders name.
  - (b) **justice**: retribution in general, with no god named.
  - Recommend (a): she acts (*has not let him live*), and two verses later the same islanders call Paul *a god* (v6).

## Acts 23–26 — rendered 2026-11-06, 11:45 PM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **26:28, *en oligō***
  - (a) **in a short time** (now): *In a short time you would persuade me to become a Christian?*, a king turning aside a quick appeal.
  - (b) **almost**: *Almost you persuade me to become a Christian*, a king half won.
  - Recommend (a): Paul's answer, *whether short or long*, takes *oligō* with *megalō* as a measure of time or effort, not of how near Agrippa came.

## Acts 15–18 — rendered 2026-11-06, 1:50 PM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **17:22, *deisidaimonesterous***
  - (a) **very religious** (now): devout, fearing the divine powers; Paul opens by crediting their worship, then builds on it (*what you worship without knowing*, v23).
  - (b) **very superstitious**: the same word as a jab, fearful of every god; the old English reading.
  - Recommend (a): the speech never mocks their worship, and Festus uses the noun of the Jews' *religion* without scorn (25:19).
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **18:24, *anēr logios***
  - (a) **an eloquent man** (now): skilled in speech; it fits *he powerfully refuted the Jews in public* (v28).
  - (b) **a learned man**: a man of learning; it fits *powerful in the Scriptures* in the same verse.
  - Recommend (a): the chapter's picture of him is of public speaking (vv25–28), and *powerful in the Scriptures* already says the learning.

## Acts 3–6 — rendered 2026-11-05, 1:43 PM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **3:13, 26; 4:25, 27, 30, *pais* (Jesus, David)**
  - (a) **servant** (now): the Greek of Isaiah's servant songs, *ho pais mou*, *My servant* (Isaiah 52:13), whom Acts 3:13 echoes; David is *Your servant* (4:25) in the same prayer that calls Jesus *Your holy servant* (4:27).
  - (b) **child**, **son**: *pais* is also a child, and the King James has *thy holy child Jesus*.
  - Recommend (a): Peter's words follow Isaiah 52:13 in Greek, and the same word is used for David in the same prayer (4:25); the note gives the link.

## Acts 1–2 — rendered 2026-11-04, 11:42 PM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **1:4, *synalizomenos***
  - (a) **While He was eating with them** (now): from *hals*, **salt**, sharing a meal; Luke tells that the risen Jesus ate before them (Luke 24:42–43).

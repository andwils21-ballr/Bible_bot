## Zechariah 13–14 — rendered 2026-10-30, 6:42 PM CDT

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **14:5, *ve-nastem gei harai***
  - (a) **You will flee by the valley of My mountains** (now): the split mountain opens a way out, and the people flee through it as they fled the earthquake.
  - (b) **The valley of My mountains will be stopped up**: the same letters read *ve-nistam*; the new valley is blocked, as it was in the earthquake; the Greek's reading.
  - Recommend (a): the Hebrew as pointed, and *as you fled from the earthquake* repeats the same verb.

## Zechariah 9–12 — rendered 2026-10-30, 1:43 PM CDT

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **9:1, *ki la-YHWH ein adam***
  - (a) **for the eye of mankind… is on the LORD** (now): all people, and Israel's tribes, look to the LORD as His word comes down on the nations.
  - (b) **for the LORD has His eye on mankind**: the LORD watches all people, Israel's tribes too; the Greek's reading.
  - Recommend (a): the Hebrew puts *the eye* with *mankind and all the tribes*, and it reads without supplying a verb.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (b) **the sheep merchants**, the letters read as one word, with the Greek and the wages of v12; applied (`PASTE/changes/52-zechariah.md`). **11:7 and 11, *lakhen aniyyei ha-tson***
  - (a) **truly the afflicted of the flock** (now): the poorest of the sheep, the ones who *were watching me* and understood (v11).
  - (b) **the sheep merchants**: the same letters read as one word, *li-khna'aniyyei*, the traders who buy and sell the flock (v5) and pay the wages (v12); the Greek reads *Canaan* and *the Canaanites*.
  - Recommend (b): it is the same letters, and the traders are the ones who weigh out the wages in v12; (a) is the Hebrew as pointed, so this is a real choice.

## Zechariah 5–8 — rendered 2026-10-29, 11:45 PM CDT

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **5:3, *niqqah***
  - (a) **every thief will be purged from here** (now): the curse will clear them out of the land, and v4 says how: it *will consume* the house.
  - (b) **every thief has gone unpunished**: the same verb's common sense, **be held innocent, go unpunished**; the scroll is sent because thieves and perjurers have got away with it until now.
  - Recommend (a): v4 at once describes the punishment, and the Greek has *will be punished*.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **5:6, *zot einam***
  - (a) **This is their appearance throughout the land** (now): the Hebrew, word for word *their eye*; the basket is what the LORD's eyes see in the land (4:10).
  - (b) **This is their iniquity throughout the land**: the Greek's reading, *avonam*, one letter different in Hebrew.
  - Recommend (a): the Hebrew makes sense and answers *the eyes of the LORD* in 4:10.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **6:8, *heniḥu et ruchi***
  - (a) **have set My Spirit at rest** (now): *ruach* as the Spirit, the fixed term.
  - (b) **have appeased My anger**: *ruach* as temper, as in Judges 8:3, and the verb Ezekiel uses with wrath (Ezekiel 16:42); the Greek has *My anger*.
  - Recommend (a), with the note giving (b): the fixed term holds unless you rule this a use of *ruach* as temper.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **6:13, *ve-hayah kohen al kis'o***
  - (a) **There will be a priest on his throne** (now): a second person, the priest beside the ruling Branch, as *between the two of them* says; the Greek has *at his right hand*.
  - (b) **He will be a priest on his throne**: the Branch himself is king and priest in one.
  - Recommend (a): *the two of them* needs two persons.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **7:2, *Beit El Sar-etser ve-Regem melekh va-anashav***
  - (a) **Bethel had sent Sharezer and Regem-melech and his men** (now): the town sends two men; the question in v3 is the town's, *I*.
  - (b) **Bethel-sharezer had sent Regem-melech and his men**: one man whose name includes *Bethel*; *Sharezer* alone is a man's name, as one of Sennacherib's sons (2 Kings 19:37).
  - Recommend (a): Bethel is a town elsewhere in the Bible, and the town speaking as *I* in v3 fits it; but *his men*, singular, leaves (b) a fair reading.

## Zechariah 1–4 — rendered 2026-10-29, 6:46 PM CDT

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **4:14, *benei ha-yitshar***
  - (a) **the two sons of oil** (now): close to the Hebrew; the note explains *son of* and the fresh oil.
  - (b) **the two anointed ones**: the sense, but *yitshar* is not the word for anointing oil.
  - Recommend (a): it keeps the image of the olive trees that supply the oil.

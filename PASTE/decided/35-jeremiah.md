## Jeremiah 21–24 — rendered 2026-10-18, 8:04 AM CDT

- ✅ Decided 2026-10-18: (a), as the text has it. **22:23, *mah nechant***
  - (a) **how you will be pitied** (now): the Hebrew vowels, from *chanan*, "to show favor." The one nested among the cedars becomes someone to pity.
  - (b) **how you will groan**: the Greek. The sound of a woman in labor, which the line goes on to name.
  - Recommend (a): the Hebrew makes sense as it stands, and the Greek does not tell the event better; it only trades mockery for pain.
- ✅ Decided 2026-10-18: (a), as the text has it. **23:6, *asher yiqre'o YHWH tsidqenu***
  - (a) **this is the name by which he will be called: The LORD Is Our Righteousness** (now): the Hebrew accents join the LORD's name to the name, as in *The LORD Is My Banner* (Exodus 17:15).
  - (b) **this is the name by which the LORD will call him: Our Righteousness**: the same words, with the LORD as the one who names him, as the Greek takes them (the Greek's name is *Iosedek*).
  - Recommend (a): the name answers Zedekiah's, *Tsidqiyahu*, which needs both words, and 33:16 gives the same name to Jerusalem.
- ✅ Decided 2026-10-18: (a), as the text has it. **23:33, 39, *massa*: the burden**
  - (a) **You are the burden… I am about to lift you up and cast you off** (now): at v33 the Greek, *attem ha-massa*, the same letters divided otherwise; at v39 the letters of *nasa*, "to lift," as the Greek reads them (*I take*).
  - (b) **What burden?… I am about to forget you completely and cast you off**: the Hebrew vowels at both verses.
  - Recommend (a): the passage is built on *massa*, a load that is lifted, and (a) finishes the picture. (b) has its own thread: *you shall no longer mention* (v36), *I will forget you* (v39), *that will not be forgotten* (v40).

## Jeremiah 17–20 — rendered 2026-10-17, 10:50 PM CDT

- ✅ Decided 2026-10-17: (b), **I will show them My back and not My face**; now in the text (`PASTE/changes/35-jeremiah.md`). **18:17, *oref ve-lo fanim er'em***
  - (a) **I will look on their backs and not their faces** (now): the Hebrew vowels, *er'em*, "I will see them." As the people flee before the enemy, the LORD sees only their backs.
  - (b) **I will show them My back and not My face**: other vowels, *ar'em*, "I will show them." The LORD turns away from them in their disaster; the Greek has *I will show them*.
  - Recommend (b): the people *turned their backs to Me, and not their faces* (2:27), and here the LORD gives them the same; *My back… My face* is the language of Exodus 33:23. (a) is the Hebrew as voweled, and the note gives it.

## Jeremiah 13–16 — rendered 2026-10-17, 5:51 PM CDT

- ✅ Decided 2026-10-17: (b), **I will make you serve your enemies in a land you do not know**; now in the text (`PASTE/changes/35-jeremiah.md`). **15:14, *ve-ha'avarti et oyevekha***
  - (a) **I will make you go with your enemies into a land you do not know** (now): the Hebrew here, *ha'avarti*, "I will make pass." The people are carried off along with their conquerors.
  - (b) **I will make you serve your enemies in a land you do not know**: *ha'avadti*, "I will make serve," one letter different (*d* for *r*), as the same oracle reads when it comes again in 17:4, and as the Greek reads here.
  - Recommend (b): the same lines stand in 17:3–4 with *serve*, the Greek has *serve* here too, and *serve your enemies* is the curse of the covenant (Deuteronomy 28:48).

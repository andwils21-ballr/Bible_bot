## Isaiah 59–62 — rendered 2026-10-16, 12:49 PM CDT

- ✅ Decided 2026-10-16: (a), as the text has it. **59:19, *ki yavo kha-nahar tsar, ruach YHWH nosesah vo***
  - Now: *for He will come like a rushing stream that the Spirit of the LORD drives on*
  - (a) **like a rushing stream that the Spirit of the LORD drives on** (now): *tsar* as "narrow, rushing," said of the stream; the LORD comes like a flood.
  - (b) **when the adversary comes like a river, the Spirit of the LORD will lift a banner against him**: *tsar* as "adversary," *nosesah* from *nes*, "banner."
  - Recommend (a): vv15–18 are about the LORD coming to repay, and the Greek also has the LORD's wrath coming *like a violent river*.

## Isaiah 55–58 — rendered 2026-10-16, 7:49 AM CDT

- ✅ Decided 2026-10-16: (a), as the text has it. **57:5, *ba-elim***
  - Now: *you who burn with lust among the oaks, under every green tree.*
  - (a) **among the oaks** (now): the *oaks* of 1:29, *the oaks that you desired*, beside *every green tree*.
  - (b) **with the gods**: the same letters; the Greek has *who call on idols*.
  - Recommend (a), because of the trees in the same line and in 1:29.
- ✅ Decided 2026-10-16: (b), **to Molech**; now in the text (`PASTE/changes/34-isaiah.md`). **57:9, *la-melekh***
  - Now: *You journeyed to the king with oil and multiplied your perfumes; you sent your envoys far off, and sent them down even to Sheol.*
  - (a) **to the king** (now): a foreign king courted with gifts.
  - (b) **to Molech**: the same letters, the god of child sacrifice (Leviticus 18:21), after the children slaughtered in v5 and with *down even to Sheol* in the next line.
  - Recommend (a), with (b) in the note. The Hebrew vowels read *king*.
- ✅ Decided 2026-10-16: **give your soul… the soul of the afflicted**, a third wording, *nefesh* kept the same twice; now in the text (`PASTE/changes/34-isaiah.md`). **58:10, *ve-tafeq la-ra'ev nafshekha***
  - Now: *if you pour yourself out for the hungry and satisfy the desire of the afflicted.*
  - (a) **pour yourself out** (now): *nafshekha*, "your soul, yourself," as the Hebrew has it.
  - (b) **give your bread to the hungry**: the Greek, *give your bread to the hungry from your soul*, as in v7.
  - Recommend (a). It is the Hebrew, and the giving of self goes further than the bread of v7.

## Isaiah 51–54 — rendered 2026-10-16, 6:04 AM CDT

  - (a) **sprinkle** (now): the priest's verb (Leviticus 4:6; 16:14).
  - (b) **startle** (or *many nations will marvel at him*): the Greek, which matches *appalled* in v14 and the kings' shut mouths.
  - Recommend (a). It is what the Hebrew word means, and the note gives the Greek.

  - Now: *He grew up before Him like a young plant.*
  - (a) **before Him** (now): before the LORD, whose arm is named in v1.
  - (b) **before him** or *before us*: the observer, or an unnamed one. The Hebrew does not say.
  - Recommend (a).
- ✅ Decided 2026-10-16: (a), as the text has it. **53:8, *nega lamo***

  - (a) **stricken** (now): *lamo* as "to him," a poetic singular.
  - (b) **the blow was theirs**: *lamo* as its usual plural, "to them."
  - Recommend (a). The whole chapter is about the one who was struck.

  - (b) **When you make his soul a guilt offering**: *tasim* as "you," spoken to God or to the reader.

- ✅ Decided 2026-10-16: (a), as the text has it. **53:11, *yir'eh***
  - Now: *Out of the anguish of his soul he will see and be satisfied.*
  - (a) **he will see** (now): the Hebrew, with no object.
  - (b) **he will see light**: the Greek has *to show him light*.
  - Recommend (a), with the Greek in the note.
- ✅ Decided 2026-10-16: (a), as the text has it. **54:15, *gor yagur***
  - Now: *If anyone stirs up strife, it is not from Me; whoever stirs up strife with you will fall because of you.*

  - Recommend (a), because the line ends *will fall because of you*.

## Isaiah 47–50 — rendered 2026-10-12, 12:52 PM CDT

- ✅ Decided 2026-10-14: (a), as the text has it. **47:3, *lo efga adam***
  - Now: *I will take vengeance, and I will spare no one.*
  - (a) **I will spare no one** (now): *paga* as meeting someone with mercy.
  - (b) **I will let no one plead** (or *no one will intercede*): *paga* as in 53:12 and 59:16, where the same verb is to intercede.
  - Recommend (a). The line is God's sentence on Babylon, and both readings end in the same place; (a) is the plainer English of *I will not meet a man*.
- ✅ Decided 2026-10-14: (a), as the text has it. **47:11, *lo tede'i shachrah***
  - Now: *But disaster will come on you, which you will not know how to charm away; calamity will fall on you, which you will not be able to ward off.*
  - (a) **which you will not know how to charm away** (now): the verb *shachar*, "seek," as of 26:9, *seeks You at dawn*; it matches *ward off* (*kapperah*) in the next line and the sorceries of v9.
  - (b) **whose dawning you will not know** (or *where it comes from*): the noun *shachar*, "dawn."
  - Recommend (a), because of the parallel line and the sorceries.
- ✅ Decided 2026-10-14: (a), as the text has it. **48:14, *ya'aseh cheftso… u-zero'o***
  - Now: *The LORD loves him; he will carry out His purpose on Babylon, and His arm will be against the Chaldeans.*
  - (a) **His purpose… His arm** (now): the LORD's purpose and arm, carried out by Cyrus. 44:28 has Cyrus *carry out all My purpose*, the same word *chefets*.
  - (b) **his purpose… his arm**: Cyrus's own will and arm.
  - The Hebrew says *his* both times and does not say whose. Recommend **(a)**, because of 44:28.
- ✅ Decided 2026-10-14: (a), as the text has it. **49:17, *banayikh***
  - Now: *Your sons hurry back; those who tore you down and laid you waste go out from you.*
  - (a) **Your sons** (now): the Hebrew vowels; the sons come home in vv20–22.
  - (b) **Your builders**: the same letters read *bonayikh*. This stands against *those who tore you down* in the same verse, and the Greek has *you will quickly be built*.
  - Recommend (a). The Hebrew vowels and the chapter's children (vv20–22, 25) agree, and the note gives (b).

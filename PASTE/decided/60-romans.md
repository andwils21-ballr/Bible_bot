## Romans 13–16 — rendered 2026-11-08, 2:15 PM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **16:1, *diakonos***
  - (a) **a servant of the church at Cenchreae** (now): the word Romans uses of the ruler (13:4) and of Christ (15:8).
  - (b) **a deacon of the church at Cenchreae**: a named office, as in Philippians 1:1 and 1 Timothy 3:8.
  - Recommend (a): it keeps the one word Romans uses three times; the note says it can name the office.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **16:7, *episēmoi en tois apostolois***
  - (a) **outstanding among the apostles** (now): Andronicus and Junia are counted as apostles.
  - (b) **well known to the apostles**: they are held in honor by the apostles.
  - Recommend (a): *en* with a group of people is most naturally *among* them; (b) is possible Greek but asks more of the word.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **16:25–27, the doxology**
  - (a) **End Romans at 16:24, as the edition does** (now): the v24 note says English Bibles add a closing blessing as 16:25–27.
  - (b) **Add 16:25–27 from another witness**: this needs the passage in `sources/` (another Greek edition, or the Ethiopic), outside a render cycle.
  - Recommend (a) for now, and (b) when a witness is added: the Ethiopian Bible's reading is the one this project serves, and it should decide.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **14:20, *tō dia proskommatos esthionti*** (from the spot check of 2026-11-08)
  - (a) **it is wrong for anyone to make another stumble by what he eats** (now): the harm is to the brother who watches, as *stumbling block* in v13 and *makes your brother stumble* in v21.
  - (b) **it is wrong for anyone to eat with a stumbling conscience**: the harm is to the eater himself, who eats while doubting it is right, as *whoever has doubts is condemned if he eats* in v23.
  - Recommend (a): the verse goes on to *do not tear down the work of God*, and the chapter's stumbling is always a brother's (vv13, 15, 21).

## Romans 9–12 — rendered 2026-11-08, 1:15 AM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **10:4, *telos***
  - (a) **the end** (now): English *end*, like Greek *telos*, holds both stopping point and goal.
  - (b) **the goal**: the Law was always heading toward Christ.
  - Recommend (a): it keeps both meanings open, as the Greek does; the note gives the range.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **11:26, *kai houtōs***
  - (a) **In this way** (now): Israel is saved by way of the Gentiles' coming in and the jealousy it stirs.
  - (b) **And then**: after the Gentiles have come in, Israel is saved.
  - Recommend (a): *houtōs* is a word of manner throughout Romans (5:12, 18–19; 6:11), and the chapter has just described the way (vv11–24).
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **12:1, *logikē latreia***
  - (a) **reasonable worship** (now): worship that belongs to reason and the word, *logos*, matched by *the renewing of your mind* (v2).
  - (b) **spiritual worship**: worship of the inner person.
  - Recommend (a): Paul's word for *spiritual* is *pneumatikos* (1:11; 7:14), and he did not use it here.

## Romans 5–8 — rendered 2026-11-07, 7:13 PM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **8:3, *peri hamartias***
  - (a) **as an offering for sin** (now): the Greek Law's term for the sin offering.
  - (b) **for sin** / **to deal with sin**: sin as what the sending dealt with, with no sacrifice named.
  - Recommend (a): it is the sin offering's name in Leviticus (5:7) and the servant's in the Greek of Isaiah 53:10, and *condemned sin in the flesh* is what an offering does.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **8:20, *dia ton hypotaxanta***
  - (a) **because of Him who subjected it** (now): God, who cursed the ground *in hope* (Genesis 3:17).
  - (b) **because of him who subjected it**: Adam, *on your account*.
  - Recommend (a): *in hope* belongs to the one who did the subjecting, and only God subjects in hope.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **8:29–30, *proōrisen***
  - (a) **appointed beforehand** (now): *horizō* of 1:4 with *pro*, *before*, so the Son *appointed* and the many *appointed beforehand* to His image share one word.
  - (b) **predestined**: the word most English Bibles have.
  - Recommend (a): it keeps the link to 1:4, and Acts 4:28 in this project already has *decided beforehand* for the same verb, not *predestined*.

## Romans 1–4 — rendered 2026-11-07, 2:20 PM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **2:14, *physei***
  - (a) **Gentiles, who do not have the Law by birth, do what the Law requires** (now): Gentiles who keep the Law, as the believers of v29.
  - (b) **Gentiles, who do not have the Law, do by nature what the Law requires**: a law known to every conscience.
  - Recommend (a): Paul uses *physei* of birth in Galatians 2:15, and *ek physeōs* is *by birth* in v27 of this chapter.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **4:17, *kalountos ta mē onta hōs onta***
  - (a) **calls into being what does not exist** (now): the Creator's call.
  - (b) **speaks of what does not exist as though it did**: God naming Abraham *father of many nations* before he had a son.
  - Recommend (a): both halves of the line are things God does by power, *gives life to the dead* and *calls into being*; (b) is the point of the next verse.

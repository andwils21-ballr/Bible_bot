- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 109): (a), as the text has it. **3:5, 8, "rib" or "side"**
  - Now, 3:5: *He took for the woman one bone from among his bones, and this rib was the beginning of the woman*
  - Now, 3:8: *In the first week Adam was made, and the rib, his wife*
  - This project's Genesis 2:21 renders the Hebrew *tsela* **side**, with a note; Jubilees' Ge'ez *gabo* is **rib** in Charles, the only English available, so *side* could not be backed from the English.
  - (a) **rib** (now), with a note pointing to Genesis 2:21.
  - (b) **side**, to match Genesis.
  - Recommend (a), unless you want the two books to match.
- ✅ Decided 2026-09-30: (a) **angel**, by the fixed-terms ruling on *mal'akh* (`RENDERING_SPEC.md`: the Hebrew is *messenger*; the Greek *angelos* and the Ge'ez books keep *angel*). **"Angel" or "messenger", Ge'ez *malʾak***
  - Now, 1:27: *He said to the angel of the presence*
  - Now, 1:29: *The angel of the presence, who went before the camps of Israel*
  - Now, 2:1: *The angel of the presence spoke to Moses*
  - Now, 2:2: *the angels of the presence, and the angels of holiness, and the angels of the spirit of fire…*
  - This project's Genesis renders the Hebrew *mal'akh* **messenger** (Genesis 16:7, 28:12; Exodus 14:19); 1 Enoch renders the Ge'ez **angel** throughout. In Jubilees the angels are the narrators, *we*.
  - (a) **angel** (now): the same Ge'ez word as in 1 Enoch, and the book treats these as heavenly beings, not as a job.
  - (b) **messenger**, as Genesis.
  - Recommend (a). Where Jubilees retells a Genesis *messenger of the LORD* scene (the binding of Isaac, chapter 18), the note says so.

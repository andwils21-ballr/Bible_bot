## Matthew 18 (canon and Matthew loop, run 12, 2026-09-27)

- ✅ Decided 2026-09-28: (a), **seventy-seven times** stays. **18:22, *hebdomēkontakis hepta***
  - (a) **seventy-seven times** (then in the text), matching Genesis 4:24, where the Greek has the same words for the Hebrew's seventy-seven.
  - (b) **seventy times seven**, the King James reading.

## Matthew 14 (canon and Matthew loop, run 8, 2026-09-27)

  - (a) **I am**, bare (then in the text), so the echo of Exodus 3:14 can be heard.
  - (b) **It is I**, the usual English.

## Matthew 8–9 (canon and Matthew loop, run 5, 2026-09-27)

- ✅ Decided 2026-09-28: (a), *blight* stays. **8:2–3, *lepra*** (it recurs at 10:8; 11:5; 26:6)
  - (a) **blight** (then in the text): *lepra* is the Old Testament Greek for *tsara'at*, which this project renders *blight* by the ruling of 2026-09-23; the man is *a man with blight*.
  - (b) **leper**, the familiar word.

## Matthew 5–7 (canon and Matthew loop, run 4, 2026-09-27)

- ✅ Decided 2026-09-28: (b), *our daily bread* (see `PASTE/changes/55-matthew.md`). **6:11, *epiousios*** (the best-known line in the book)
  - (a) **our bread for the coming day** (then in the text): the likelier sense of *epiousios* (from *hē epiousa*, the next day).
  - (b) **our daily bread**, the traditional line, which the note says keeps the sense of both derivations.

## Matthew 1–4 (canon and Matthew loop, run 3, 2026-09-27)

- ✅ Decided 2026-09-28: (a), *the Messiah* stays. ***Christos*** (1:1, 16, 17, 18; 2:4)
  - (a) **the Messiah**, as a title (then in the text), as the spec's example of Matthew 1:1 has it.
  - (b) **Christ**, as most English Bibles have it.
- ✅ Decided 2026-09-28: (c), cut, as in Leviticus 13–14 (see `PASTE/changes/55-matthew.md`). ***idou*** (1:20, 23; 2:1, 9, 13, 19; 3:16, 17; 4:11), the Greek of *hinneh*
  - (a) **here —** / **And here —** (then in the text), the ruling for *hinneh*.
  - (b) **behold**, which is banned.
  - (c) **Drop it** (*an angel appeared*).
- ✅ Decided 2026-09-28: (b), capitals for Jesus, applied to Matthew 1–28 (see `PASTE/changes/55-matthew.md`). **Pronouns for Jesus**
  - (a) **Lowercase** (*he*, *his*, 1:21, 25 and throughout; then in the text), capitals kept for God the Father (*My Son*, 3:17; *My son*, 2:15; *My people*, 2:6).
  - (b) **Capitals**, as the first chapters of 1st Covenant and Te'ezaz, where Jesus speaks, have *Me* and *My*.

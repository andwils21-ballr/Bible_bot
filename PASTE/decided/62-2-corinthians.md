## 2 Corinthians 9–13 — rendered 2026-11-10, 7:20 PM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **10:7, *ta kata prosōpon blepete***
  - (a) **You look at outward appearances** (now): a rebuke, you judge by what you see.
  - (b) **Look at what is before your eyes**: a command, face the plain facts.
  - Recommend (a): *kata prosōpon* is the phrase of v1, *humble when I am face to face with you*, and v10 gives the judgment Paul is answering, *his bodily presence is weak*; the Greek verb can be either.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **12:7, *angelos Satana***
  - (a) **an angel of Satan** (now): a spirit sent by Satan, as *the devil and his angels* (Matthew 25:41).
  - (b) **a messenger of Satan**: anything Satan sends, an illness or a human enemy; *angelos* is also a man sent with a message (Matthew 11:10; Luke 7:24).
  - Recommend (a): Paul has just used the word for Satan's own disguise, *an angel of light* (11:14), nothing in the verse makes it a human messenger, and the note gives the range of what the thorn was.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **12:18, *tō autō pneumati periepatēsamen***
  - (a) **Did we not walk by the same Spirit?** (now): the Holy Spirit leading Paul and Titus alike.
  - (b) **Did we not walk in the same spirit?**: the same attitude, matched by *the same steps*.
  - Recommend (a): *walk by the Spirit* is Paul's own phrase with the same verb and case (Galatians 5:16), and *the same Spirit* is the Holy Spirit in 1 Corinthians 12:9 and 2 Corinthians 4:13.

## 2 Corinthians 5–8 — rendered 2026-11-10, 2:15 PM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **5:17, *ei tis en Christō, kainē ktisis***
  - (a) **if anyone is in Christ, there is a new creation** (now): the world made new, which the believer enters.
  - (b) **if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation**: the believer himself made new.
  - Recommend (a): Paul's only other use, *neither circumcision… nor uncircumcision, but a new creation* (Galatians 6:15), is the new world, and *the old things have passed away; new things have come* is Isaiah's new thing for the whole people (Isaiah 43:18–19).

## 2 Corinthians 1–4 — rendered 2026-11-10, 1:15 AM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **3:18, *katoptrizomenoi***
  - (a) **beholding the glory of the Lord as in a mirror** (now): believers see the Lord's glory, as Paul's *we see in a mirror* (1 Corinthians 13:12), and are changed by seeing.
  - (b) **reflecting the glory of the Lord**: believers shine it back, as Moses' face did.
  - Recommend (a): the verb is formed from *katoptron*, **mirror**, and in the middle voice it is used of looking into one; *with unveiled face* fits one who sees, as Moses did when the veil came off before the LORD (Exodus 34:34).

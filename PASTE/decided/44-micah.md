## Micah 6–7 — rendered 2026-10-27, 1:09 PM CDT

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **6:9, *shim'u matteh***
  - (a) **Hear the rod, and the One who appointed it!** (now): *matteh* as the rod of punishment, and the LORD who set it; the punishments follow in vv13–16.
  - (b) **Hear, O tribe, and who has appointed it!**: *matteh* as a tribe, as the Greek (*hear, O tribe*).
  - Recommend (a): the rod fits the strokes of v13; the note gives the tribe and the Greek, and that *it* is feminine in the Hebrew.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **7:11, *yirchak choq***
  - (a) **On that day the boundary will be far off** (now): the borders move far out, ready for the people gathered in v12.
  - (b) **On that day the decree will be far removed**: the sentence against Zion is taken away; the Greek has *that day will rub away your statutes*.
  - Recommend (a): the verse is about building walls, and *choq* is a set limit in *when He assigned to the sea its limit* (Proverbs 8:29); the note gives both.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **7:13, *ha-arets***
  - (a) **But the earth will become a desolation** (now): the lands of the nations, just named in v12, laid waste for their deeds, as the nations in 5:15.
  - (b) **But the land will become a desolation**: Israel's own land, desolate first, before the gathering.
  - Recommend (a): *its inhabitants… the fruit of their deeds* follows straight on from the lands of v12; the note says the word means both.

## Micah 2–5 — rendered 2026-10-27, 8:22 AM CDT

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **4:9, *ha-melekh ein bakh***
  - (a) **Is there no king in you?** (now): Jerusalem's own king, beside *your counselor*; the next lines strike *the judge of Israel* (5:1), a man.
  - (b) **Is there no King in you?**: the LORD, named as reigning on Mount Zion just before (4:7), as the exiles ask in Jeremiah, *Is the LORD not in Zion? Is her King not in her?* (Jeremiah 8:19).
  - Recommend (a): the king stands with a counselor, and the siege that follows strikes a human judge; the note gives the other reading.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **5:1, *titgodedi bat gedud***
  - (a) **Now gather in your troops, daughter of troops** (now): *gadad* as in *trooped to the house of a prostitute* (Jeremiah 5:7), the city mustering under siege.
  - (b) **Now gash yourself, daughter of troops**: *gadad* as in *You shall not gash yourselves* (Deuteronomy 14:1), the city in mourning, as in the shaved heads of 1:16.
  - Recommend (a): *daughter of troops* and *a siege is laid against us* make it war; the note gives both.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 111): (a), as the text has it. **5:5, *ve-hayah zeh shalom***
  - (a) **This one will be peace** (now): the ruler of vv2–4 is himself the peace.
  - (b) **This will be the peace:** what follows, the shepherds raised against Assyria.
  - Recommend (a): the verse follows the ruler of v4 without a break, and *zeh*, **this one**, points back to him.

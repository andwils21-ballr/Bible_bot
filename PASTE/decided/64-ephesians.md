## Ephesians 3–6 — rendered 2026-11-11, 7:25 PM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **4:9, *katebē eis ta katōtera merē tēs gēs***
  - (a) **He had also gone down into the lower parts of the earth** (now): keeps the Greek's range.
  - (b) **into the lower parts, that is, the earth**: He came down from heaven to earth.
  - (c) **into the parts beneath the earth**: He went down to the dead before He went up.
  - Recommend (a): the Greek Psalms use the same phrase for the grave (Psalm 63:9) and for the womb (Psalm 139:15), and v10 sets only *went down* against *went up far above all the heavens*; the note gives both readings.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **5:33, *hē de gynē hina phobētai ton andra***
  - (a) **the wife must see that she respects her husband** (now).
  - (b) **the wife must see that she fears her husband**: the same word as *the fear of Christ* in v21, so the passage opens and closes on it.
  - Recommend (a): in English *fear her husband* says *be afraid of him*, which the Greek word, used in v21 of reverence for Christ, does not say; the note shows the link to v21.

## Ephesians 1–2 — rendered 2026-11-11, 2:25 PM CST

- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **1:10, *anakephalaiōsasthai ta panta en tō Christō***
  - (a) **to sum up all things in Christ** (now): Christ the sum and main point of everything, as *kephalaion*, the sum.
  - (b) **to bring all things together under Christ as head**: from *kephalē*, head, the word of v22.
  - Recommend (a): the only other use of the verb is Romans 13:9, *summed up in this word*, and v22 already says *head*; the note gives both.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-22 (settled by the council, item 112): (a), as the text has it. **1:11, *en hō kai eklērōthēmen***
  - (a) **In Him we too were made His portion** (now): the believers are God's own share, as *the LORD's portion is His people* (Deuteronomy 32:9).
  - (b) **In Him we too have obtained an inheritance**: the believers receive a share, as *our inheritance* (v14).
  - Recommend (a): the verb is passive, and the chapter goes on to *God's own possession* (v14) and *His inheritance in the saints* (v18); the note gives both.

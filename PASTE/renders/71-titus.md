**Choices for you:**
- **2:13, *tou megalou theou kai sōtēros hēmōn Iēsou Christou***
  - (a) **our great God and Savior Jesus Christ** (now): one person, Jesus Christ, called God and Savior.
  - (b) **the great God and our Savior Jesus Christ**: two persons, the Father and the Son.
  - Recommend (a): one article governs both nouns, and in the letters *appearing* is used of Christ's coming (2 Timothy 4:1, 8); the note gives (b).

# New Chapters: Jeremiah

Reports on newly rendered chapters, newest at the top. Format: CLAUDE.md, section 5.

## Jeremiah 1–4 — rendered 2026-10-16, 10:54 PM CDT

**Source:** Hebrew (WLC) throughout; the Greek (Swete) is followed nowhere. It is quoted in notes where it reads differently: 1:1; 2:2, 18, 20, 34; 3:1, 14; 4:10. Jeremiah is a new writer: the style is taken from the Hebrew itself, the rules from Genesis 25, Exodus 33 and Leviticus 17. Verse counts match the Hebrew in all four chapters.

**Kept formulas:**
- *ve-hayah* + a time clause: 3:16 *And it shall come to pass, when you have multiplied…, that they will no longer say*; 4:9 *And it shall come to pass in that day… that the courage of the king… will fail*.
- 3:9 *ve-hayah* is not the formula (*Because her prostitution was so light a thing to her*), so it is rendered plain. 1:3 *va-yehi* is "it came" (the word of the LORD), not the formula.
- No other sentence starts with *And*.
- *hinneh*: 1:6, 9; 3:5; 4:13, 16; and the four *ve-hinneh* of 4:23–26 (*I looked at the earth, and it was wild and waste*) cut. 1:18 *va-ani hinneh*: *I Myself have made you*. God's *hineni* + participle: 1:15 *I am about to call*; 2:35 *I am about to enter into judgment*. 3:22 *hinnenu*: *Here we are*.
- *hen*: 2:10; 3:1 cut.
- *ve-attah* (2:18): *So now*, drawing the consequence.
- 3:1 opens with *lemor*, "saying," with nothing for it to hang on; the Greek has none, and it is not rendered.

**Read forms followed:**
- 2:20 *e'evor*, "I will not transgress": the written form is *e'evod*, "I will not serve." The note gives both, and a choice is below.
- No note, because the meaning is the same: 1:5 *etsarkha*; 2:15 *nitstsetu*; 2:16 *Tachpanches*; 2:24 *nafshah*; 2:25 *u-gronekh*; 2:27 *yelidtanu*, "gave birth to us" (written *yelidtini*, "to me"); 2:33 *limmadt*; 3:2 *shukkavt*, "been lain with" (written *shugalt*, a coarser verb, as in Deuteronomy 28:30); 3:4 *qarat*; 3:5 *dibbart*; 3:7 *va-tere*; 3:19 *tiqre'i*, *tashuvi*; 4:5 *tiq'u*; 4:19 *ochilah*, *shama'at*; 4:30 *ve-at*.

**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** Jeremiah's call in the days of Josiah: *Before I formed you in the belly I knew you*; the almond branch and the boiling pot from the north.
- **2:** the LORD's case against His people: *I remember the loyal love of your youth*; *they have forsaken Me, the fountain of living waters*.
- **3:** the divorced wife: faithless Israel and treacherous Judah; *Return, faithless sons… I will heal your faithlessness*.
- **4:** *Circumcise yourselves to the LORD*; the lion from the north; the prophet's anguish; *I looked at the earth, and it was wild and waste*.

**Left standing on purpose:**
- 2:31, *We roam free*: the verb's sense is uncertain.
- 2:34, *Yet in spite of all these things*: the Hebrew breaks off, and the line runs into v35.

**Choices for you:**

- **2:20, *shavarti ullekh… lo e'evor***
  - (a) **For long ago I broke your yoke and tore off your bonds, and you said, "I will not transgress"** (now): the Hebrew as read. The LORD freed Israel from Egypt (*I broke the bars of your yoke*, Leviticus 26:13); Israel promised to be faithful, and then lay down under every green tree.
  - (b) **For long ago you broke your yoke and tore off your bonds, and you said, "I will not serve"**: the written form *e'evod*, "serve," with the Greek, and *shavarti* read as "you broke," as Jeremiah's written *-ti* is read elsewhere (2:33; 3:4, 5; 4:19). Israel threw off the LORD's yoke and refused to serve Him.
  - Recommend (b): 5:5 says the same of the people in the same words (*they have broken the yoke, they have burst the bonds*), and v20 then gives the reason for v19, *the fear of Me is not in you*.
- **3:1, *ve-shov elai***
  - (a) **and would you return to Me?** (now): a question, with the law's answer understood. Under Deuteronomy 24:1–4 a wife who was sent away and became another man's cannot go back to her first husband.
  - (b) **yet return to Me**: a call. The LORD invites back the wife the law says cannot return.
  - Recommend (a): the verse is a run of questions, and the Greek has a question. The call comes in vv12, 14 and 22, where it cannot be mistaken, and it is all the stronger after the law has been heard.

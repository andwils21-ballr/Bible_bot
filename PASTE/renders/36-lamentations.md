**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** Jerusalem as a widow with no one to comfort her, first described, then speaking for herself.
- **2:** the Lord as the enemy who swallowed Zion; children fainting in the streets.
- **3:** one man's suffering, and at its center the turn to hope: *His compassions are new every morning*.
- **4:** the gold grown dim; famine, the guilty priests and prophets, the king caught; Edom's turn to drink the cup.
- **5:** the people's prayer: *Bring us back to You, LORD*, and the open question of its last line.

**Left standing on purpose:**
- 1:12, *Is it nothing to you*: the Hebrew is two words with no verb, *not to you*.
- 3:65, *a veiled heart*: *meginnat lev* is found only here.

**Choices for you:**
- **1:8, *le-nidah hayatah***
  - (a) **she has become a thing unclean** (now): read as *niddah*, impurity, the word of v17.
  - (b) **she has become an object of scorn**: from *nud*, the head shaken at her (Jeremiah 18:16); the Greek has *a thing tossed about*.
  - Recommend (a): v17 says it with the plain word, and v9 goes on, *Her uncleanness was in her skirts*.
- **3:22, *chasdei YHWH ki lo tamnu***
  - (a) **It is the LORD's loyal love that we are not finished** (now): the Hebrew as pointed, *tamnu*, "we."
  - (b) **The LORD's loyal love does not cease**: the same letters read *tammu*, matching the next line, *His compassions do not fail*.
  - Recommend (a): it keeps the reading the Hebrew gives; (b) is the smoother parallel, and the note shows it.
- **3:36, *Adonai lo ra'ah***
  - (a) **the Lord does not approve** (now): a statement closing vv34–36, the wrongs God does not look on with favor.
  - (b) **does the Lord not see?**: a question, the wrongs God sees.
  - Recommend (a): vv31–36 say what the Lord does not do, and (a) completes the list.
- **4:7, *nezireha***
  - (a) **Her consecrated ones** (now): the word's root sense, *set apart*, which covers both readings.
  - (b) **Her princes**: as Joseph is *the one set apart from his brothers* (Genesis 49:26), a word for a leader.
  - (c) **Her Nazirites**: those under the vow, as the Greek has it.
  - Recommend (a): it keeps the root and leaves the note to name the two kinds.
- **5:22, *ki im ma'os me'astanu***
  - (a) **unless You have utterly rejected us** (now): the prayer of v21 stands, with one fear named after it.
  - (b) **But You have utterly rejected us**: the book ends on a statement; the Greek has *For You have utterly rejected us*.
  - Recommend (a): it keeps v21's prayer open, and the book ends on the question, as the Hebrew leaves it.

**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** thanks for the Colossians' faith, learned from Epaphras. Then the hymn: *He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation, for in Him all things were created… in Him all things hold together*, and through Him God reconciled all things *by the blood of His cross*. Paul rejoices in his sufferings for the church: *Christ in you, the hope of glory*.
- **2:** in Christ *the whole fullness of deity dwells bodily*. Buried with Him in baptism, raised with Him; the record of debt nailed to the cross. Do not let anyone judge you over food or sabbaths, *a shadow of the things to come*; *Do not handle, do not taste, do not touch* is no help against the flesh.
- **3:** *seek the things above… your life is hidden with Christ in God*. Put off the old self, put on the new; *Christ is all, and in all*; put on love; let the peace of Christ rule. Wives and husbands, children and fathers, slaves.
- **4:** masters; pray, and let your speech be *seasoned with salt*. Greetings: Tychicus and Onesimus, Aristarchus, Mark, Luke *the beloved physician*; read the letter from Laodicea. *Remember my chains.*

**Choices for you:**
- **1:19, *en autō eudokēsen pan to plērōma katoikēsai***
  - (a) **in Him all the fullness was pleased to dwell** (now): *the fullness* is the subject, as the Greek reads.
  - (b) **God was pleased to have all His fullness dwell in Him**: God is the one pleased, and *the fullness* is His.
  - Recommend (a): it keeps the Greek's own words and both readings; 2:9 says what the fullness is, *the whole fullness of deity dwells bodily* in Him.

## 3 Meqabyan 1–3 — rendered 2026-10-07, 1:25 PM CDT

**Source:** EOTC Ge'ez (Beta Masaheft). No Amharic check is held for this book, so these chapters rest on the Ge'ez alone.

**What these chapters are:** the deceiver's boast of how he leads people astray (ch. 1), his bargain with God, the prophet's rebuke of him, and the fall of Adam and Eve (ch. 2), and God's comfort of them outside the garden (ch. 3).

**Text cut off (rendered *[words lost]*):** 2:23; 3:1, 3:2, 3:5.

**Copying slips read as the plain word:**
- 2:1 *la-ʾənki* read with the sense of the first clause, "those you could not lead astray."
- 2:2 *kəhəlka* "you are able" read as *kəhəlku* "I am able."
- 2:9 and 3:11 have two words run together in the Ge'ez; read as two.

**Left as written, with a note:**
- 1:19 a clause written twice is rendered once.
- 2:6 repeats the end of 2:5 word for word; rendered as written.
- 2:20 two verbs read "she"; rendered "you" like the middle one.

**Kept transliterated:** *ʾagāyāt* (1:9, a cloth), *la-mbārya* (1:22), *sabawwota* (3:8, perhaps "seven").

**Uncertain renderings:** 2:10 "at the word of God" (could be "calling himself God"); 2:17 "stole"; 2:19 "scorched."

**Also fixed:** a garbled cross-reference in the 1:19–21 note.

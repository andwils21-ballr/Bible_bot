# New Chapters: 3 Meqabyan

Reports on newly rendered chapters, newest at the top. Format: CLAUDE.md, section 5.

## 3 Meqabyan 4–6 — rendered 2026-10-07, 1:45 PM CDT

**Source:** EOTC Ge'ez (Beta Masaheft). No Amharic check is held.

**What these chapters are:** the prophet's rebuke goes on: the deceiver lost his throne and Adam was made in his place; Adam repented and the deceiver would not (ch. 4). Then a sermon to the reader: weigh your deeds; against lies, broken promises and false scales (ch. 5); the angels who meet the soul at death, and a warning against gluttony (ch. 6).

**Copying slips read as the plain word:** 4:7 *ʾəḫʷalka* (lost), 4:10 *mərēta* (earth), 4:13 and 4:30 a feminine "you/He" ending, 4:19 *gəbrun*, 4:24 *ʾənʾa* (while), 4:26 *salā* (peace), 4:33 *makarā* (resolve); 5:6 *bəbaka*, 5:9 *ṣədk*, 5:14 *ʾasabʾ* (O people), 5:16 *fataʿbəyu* (do not be proud); 6:1 *ḥaldawā* (think), 6:3 *laʾalkəmmomu* (plead) and *yəfarrəhukəmu* (terrify), 6:9 *ʾəku* (evil), 6:11 *warḫka* (grew far), 6:15 *kəma* (so that).

**Left as written, with a note:**
- 4:6 repeats the second half of 4:5 word for word.
- 4:18 "and Adam" read as "but Adam."
- 4:29 *ʾiʾaka*, not understood, left out.
- 4:32 the sense needs a "not" that the Ge'ez lacks; rendered as written.

**Kept transliterated:** *yəṭāʿṭāʿ* (4:15, rendered "formed" with a note), *ḥənqʷāqʷe* (6:9).

**Uncertain renderings:** 4:2 "be drawn with you"; 4:8 the "tenth tribe" verse, rendered close to its order; 4:11 "where you had fallen short"; 4:27 "bend"; 5:11 "all of them" (*kəma*).

**Next:** chapter 7 has only five verses; it goes with 8 onward next cycle.

## 3 Meqabyan 1–3 — rendered 2026-10-07, 1:25 PM CDT

**Source:** EOTC Ge'ez (Beta Masaheft). No Amharic check is held for this book, so these chapters rest on the Ge'ez alone.

**What these chapters are:** the deceiver's boast of how he leads people astray (ch. 1), his bargain with God, the prophet's rebuke of him, and the fall of Adam and Eve (ch. 2), and God's comfort of them outside the garden (ch. 3).

**Text cut off (rendered *[words lost]*):** 2:23; 3:1, 3:2, 3:5.

**Copying slips read as the plain word:**
- 2:1 *la-ʾənki* read with the sense of the first clause, "those you could not lead astray."
- 2:2 *kəhəlka* "you are able" read as *kəhəlku* "I am able."
- 2:9 and 3:11 have two words run together in the Ge'ez; read as two.

**Left as written, with a note:**
- 1:19 a clause written twice is rendered once.
- 2:6 repeats the end of 2:5 word for word; rendered as written.
- 2:20 two verbs read "she"; rendered "you" like the middle one.

**Kept transliterated:** *ʾagāyāt* (1:9, a cloth), *la-mbārya* (1:22), *sabawwota* (3:8, perhaps "seven").

**Uncertain renderings:** 2:10 "at the word of God" (could be "calling himself God"); 2:17 "stole"; 2:19 "scorched."

**Also fixed:** a garbled cross-reference in the 1:19–21 note.

**Landed:** Ephesians 1, 2, in the same run as Galatians 5–6 (see the Galatians report). Together, progress 1,444 → **1,448 of 1,989 (72.8%)**. John 8 is still held (see the John report).

**Widespread decision** (it reaches Ephesians 5:27, Philippians 2:15, Colossians 1:22, Hebrews 9:14, 1 Peter 1:19, Jude 24 and Revelation 14:5; listed first so it is settled before it spreads):

- **1:4, *amōmos***
  - (a) **without defect** (now), in every book: the Greek Bible's word for an offering, *it must be without defect to be accepted* (Leviticus 22:21), where this project already has *without defect* for *tamim*.
  - (b) **blameless** for people, **without defect** only for Christ the offering (Hebrews 9:14; 1 Peter 1:19).
  - Recommend (a): Paul twice sets *amōmos* beside a separate word for *blameless*, *amōmous kai anenklētous* (Colossians 1:22) and *amemptoi… amōma* (Philippians 2:15), so (b) would give two Greek words one English word; and Ephesians 5:27 presents the church as an offering is presented.

The open Romans choice on *proorizō* (8:29–30, **appointed beforehand** now) meets Ephesians 1:5, 11 and is rendered as Romans has it.

**Source:** Greek (SBLGNT). The edition's readings are followed, bracketed words included (1:1, 7, 15; 2:7, 11, 13, 20); the square brackets around *in Ephesus* (1:1) are noted. The writer is Paul again, but Ephesians has a voice of its own: long sentences of praise built clause on clause (1:3–14 is one sentence in the Greek). They are broken into English sentences at the verse lines without moving words across verses, and the run of *in Him… in whom* is kept.

**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** one long blessing: God chose us in Christ before the foundation of the world, adopted us as sons, redeemed us by His blood, made known the mystery of His will, *to sum up all things in Christ*, and sealed us with the Holy Spirit as the pledge of our inheritance. Then Paul's prayer, that they may know the power that raised Christ and seated Him far above every power, *head over all things to the church, which is His body*.
- **2:** *you were dead in your trespasses and sins… by grace you have been saved through faith; and this is not from yourselves; it is the gift of God.* Gentiles once far off, without hope and without God, are brought near: Christ *is our peace*, who broke down the dividing wall and made the two *one new man*. They are no longer strangers but members of God's household, a holy temple built on Christ the cornerstone.

**Choices for you:**
- **1:10, *anakephalaiōsasthai ta panta en tō Christō***
  - (a) **to sum up all things in Christ** (now): Christ the sum and main point of everything, as *kephalaion*, the sum.
  - (b) **to bring all things together under Christ as head**: from *kephalē*, head, the word of v22.
  - Recommend (a): the only other use of the verb is Romans 13:9, *summed up in this word*, and v22 already says *head*; the note gives both.
- **1:11, *en hō kai eklērōthēmen***
  - (a) **In Him we too were made His portion** (now): the believers are God's own share, as *the LORD's portion is His people* (Deuteronomy 32:9).
  - (b) **In Him we too have obtained an inheritance**: the believers receive a share, as *our inheritance* (v14).
  - Recommend (a): the verb is passive, and the chapter goes on to *God's own possession* (v14) and *His inheritance in the saints* (v18); the note gives both.

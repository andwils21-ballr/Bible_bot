**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** two months after Haggai's first word, Zechariah calls the people to return to the LORD, not to be like their fathers who would not listen. Then the first night visions: a rider among the myrtles, whose horsemen report the whole earth at rest; the messenger of the LORD asks how long Jerusalem must wait, and the LORD answers that He has returned to Jerusalem with compassion and His house will be built. Four horns that scattered Judah are thrown down by four craftsmen.
- **2:** a man goes to measure Jerusalem, but the city will be too full for walls; the LORD Himself will be a wall of fire around it and the glory inside it. The exiles are called to flee Babylon, and many nations will join the LORD and dwell with Zion.
- **3:** Joshua the high priest stands in filthy garments, with Satan at his right hand to accuse him. The LORD rebukes Satan; Joshua's garments are taken off, his iniquity removed, and he is clothed and given a clean turban. He is charged to keep the LORD's ways, and promised My servant the Branch, a stone with seven eyes, and the land's iniquity removed in one day.
- **4:** a golden lampstand between two olive trees. The word to Zerubbabel: *Not by might, nor by power, but by My Spirit*; the mountain before him will become a plain, and the hands that laid the temple's foundation will finish it. The two olive trees are the two sons of oil who stand by the Lord of all the earth.

**Choices for you:**
- **3:1–2, *ha-satan***
  - (a) **Satan** (now): as Job 1:6 renders the same word with the article; the note says it means *the accuser* and gives the pun on *le-sitno*.
  - (b) **the Accuser**: the word as a title, which keeps the pun in the English, *the Accuser standing… to accuse him*.
  - Recommend (a): it matches Job, and the note carries the pun.
- **4:14, *benei ha-yitshar***
  - (a) **the two sons of oil** (now): close to the Hebrew; the note explains *son of* and the fresh oil.
  - (b) **the two anointed ones**: the sense, but *yitshar* is not the word for anointing oil.
  - Recommend (a): it keeps the image of the olive trees that supply the oil.

**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** Paul blesses *the God of all comfort*, who comforted him when he was near death in Asia so that he can comfort others. He changed his travel plans, but his word is not *Yes and No*: all God's promises are *Yes* in Christ, and God has anointed and sealed us and given the Spirit as a pledge.
- **2:** he wrote a letter in tears instead of making a painful visit; now the man who caused sorrow should be forgiven and comforted. God leads the apostles in Christ's triumph, and through them spreads *the aroma of Christ*, life to some and death to others.
- **3:** the Corinthians are Paul's letter of recommendation, written by the Spirit on hearts. If the ministry of the stone tablets came with glory, Moses' face too bright to look at, the ministry of the Spirit has more. A veil still lies over the reading of *the old covenant*, but *whenever anyone turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away*.
- **4:** the God who said *Light will shine out of darkness* has shone in our hearts. *We have this treasure in clay pots*: troubled but not crushed, carrying the dying of Jesus so that His life may show. The outer self wastes away; the inner is renewed day by day, toward *an eternal weight of glory*.

**Choices for you:**
- **3:18, *katoptrizomenoi***
  - (a) **beholding the glory of the Lord as in a mirror** (now): believers see the Lord's glory, as Paul's *we see in a mirror* (1 Corinthians 13:12), and are changed by seeing.
  - (b) **reflecting the glory of the Lord**: believers shine it back, as Moses' face did.
  - Recommend (a): the verb is formed from *katoptron*, **mirror**, and in the middle voice it is used of looking into one; *with unveiled face* fits one who sees, as Moses did when the veil came off before the LORD (Exodus 34:34).

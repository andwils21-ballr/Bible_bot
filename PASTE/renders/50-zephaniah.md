**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** in Josiah's day, the LORD will sweep away everything from the earth, man and beast, bird and fish, and above all Judah's idol worship: Baal, the star worship on the roofs, swearing by the LORD and by Milcom at once. The day of the LORD is a sacrifice, and the guests are the victims: princes, traders, the complacent who say the LORD does nothing. It is near: a day of fury, darkness and the battle cry, when silver and gold will not save.
- **2:** a call to the humble to seek the LORD; perhaps they will be hidden. Then oracles against the nations around: the Philistine cities will be emptied and their coast given to Judah's remnant; Moab and Ammon will become like Sodom and Gomorrah for their pride; Cush will fall by the sword; Nineveh, the city that said *I am, and there is none besides me*, will be a lair for wild animals.
- **3:** woe to Jerusalem, whose princes, judges, prophets and priests are all corrupt, though the LORD within her is righteous. He will gather the nations for judgment. Then He will give the peoples a pure lip to call on His name, and leave in Jerusalem a humble remnant who do no wrong. The book ends in song: the LORD, Israel's King, is within her, a warrior who saves, rejoicing over her with singing, gathering the lame and the outcast and bringing them home.

**Choices for you:**
- **1:5, *be-malkam***
  - (a) **swear by Milcom** (now): the god of the Ammonites, as Jeremiah 49:1 reads the same letters; the people swear by the LORD and by another god at once.
  - (b) **swear by their king**: the same letters as a common noun, as the Greek has it, and as Amos 1:15 reads them now.
  - Recommend (a): beside *swear to the LORD*, the line sets one god against another; the note gives *their king*.
- **1:11, *kol am Kena'an***
  - (a) **all the trading people** (now): *Kena'an* as *trader*, as in Hosea 12:7, and the next line, *all who weigh out silver*, names them.
  - (b) **all the people of Canaan**: word for word, Jerusalem's merchants called by the name of the old inhabitants.
  - Recommend (a): it keeps Hosea's English for the same word, and the parallel line is about silver; the note gives *Canaan*.
- **3:8, *le-yom qumi le-ad***
  - (a) **for the day I rise up to the prey** (now): as pointed, *ad*, **prey**, as in Genesis 49:27.
  - (b) **for the day I rise up as a witness**: the same letters as *le-ed*, as the Greek has it, and as in *let the Lord GOD be a witness against you* (Micah 1:2).
  - Recommend (a): it is the Hebrew as pointed, and the verse goes on to the gathering of nations for judgment; the note gives the Greek.
- **3:17, *yacharish be-ahavato***
  - (a) **He will be silent in His love** (now): *charash*, to be still, as in Habakkuk 1:13, set between two lines of loud joy.
  - (b) **He will renew you in His love**: *yechaddesh*, nearly the same letters, as the Greek has it.
  - Recommend (a): it is the Hebrew, and stillness in love between rejoicing and singing is a real picture; the note gives the Greek.

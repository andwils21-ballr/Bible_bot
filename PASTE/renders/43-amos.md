# New Chapters: Amos

Reports on newly rendered chapters, newest at the top. Format: CLAUDE.md, section 5.

## Amos 1–2 — rendered 2026-10-26, 1:10 PM CDT

**Source:** Hebrew (WLC). Swete's Greek checked throughout. No departures from the Hebrew; the Greek is quoted in the notes where it differs (1:1, 3, 6, 15; 2:7, 13). Hebrew, Greek and English numbering agree in these chapters. A new writer: the style is taken from the Hebrew, not carried over from Hosea.

**Form kept:**
- The eight oracles keep one refrain, word for word (1:3, 6, 9, 11, 13; 2:1, 4, 6): *This is what the LORD says: For three transgressions of…, and for four, I will not turn it back*. *This is what the LORD says* is the ruling of 2026-10-18; *turn it back* is the verb as Numbers 23:20 has it; *transgressions* for *pesha*, as Isaiah 53:5.
- The fire refrain is Hosea 8:14's: *I will send fire on…, and it will devour… strongholds* (1:4, 7, 10, 12; 2:2, 5). 1:14 has another verb, *hitsatti*, **I will kindle**, and the English keeps the difference.
- *Says the LORD* for *amar YHWH* (1:5, 15; 2:3); *says the Lord GOD* for *amar Adonai YHWH* (1:8); *declares the LORD* for *ne'um YHWH* (2:11, 16).
- *The Ammonites* for *benei Ammon* (1:13), as Jeremiah 49:2.
- God's *hinneh* + participle is *I am about to* (2:13). No *va-yehi* formula; no written and read forms.
- Kept the same as other books: *a sheep breeder* (1:1), as 2 Kings 3:4; *I will turn My hand against* (1:8), as Isaiah 1:25; *rip open the pregnant women* (1:13), as 2 Kings 8:12 and Hosea 13:16; *profane My holy name* (2:7), as Leviticus 22:32; *taken in pledge* (2:8), as Exodus 22:26; *fined* (2:8), as Exodus 21:22; *trample* for *sha'af* (2:7), as Psalm 56:1; *Flight will fail* (2:14), as Jeremiah 25:35; *forty years in the wilderness* (2:10), as Deuteronomy 29:5.
- 1:1 is prose; the oracles from 1:2 are set as poetry. No sentence starts with *And*.

**The checker** found nothing.

**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** the words of Amos, a sheep breeder from Tekoa in Judah, spoken to the north two years before an earthquake. The LORD roars from Zion and the pastures wither. Then oracle after oracle against Israel's neighbors, each for *three transgressions, and for four*: Damascus threshed Gilead with iron; Gaza and Tyre sold whole peoples to Edom; Edom hunted his brother; Ammon ripped open the pregnant women of Gilead. Fire on each.
- **2:** Moab burned a king's bones to lime. Then Judah, for rejecting the law, and at last Israel, the eighth, whose charge runs on: the innocent sold for silver, the poor trampled, a father and son with one girl, pledged cloaks and fines' wine at the altar. The LORD who destroyed the Amorite and brought them out of Egypt gave them prophets and Nazirites, and they silenced both; on that day not even the bravest will escape.

**Choices for you:**
- **1:15, *malkam***
  - (a) **Their king will go into exile, he and his princes together** (now): the Hebrew as pointed, with princes beside him, as Moab's oracle ends with its ruler and princes (2:3).
  - (b) **Milcom will go into exile, he and his princes together**: Ammon's god, as Jeremiah reads the same line, *for Milcom will go into exile, his priests and his princes together* (Jeremiah 49:3).
  - Recommend (a): here the Hebrew names no priests, and every other oracle that ends this way names human rulers.
- **2:7, *ha-sho'afim***
  - (a) **They trample the head of the poor into the dust of the earth** (now): *trample*, as Psalm 56:1, *man tramples on me*, and the Greek, *treading*.
  - (b) **who pant after the dust of the earth on the head of the poor**: *pant*, as Jeremiah 14:6, *they pant for air like jackals*: greed that wants even the dust a mourner puts on his head (Joshua 7:6).
  - Recommend (a): the Greek and Psalm 56 agree, and the next line, *turn aside the way of the humble*, is also violence done to the poor.

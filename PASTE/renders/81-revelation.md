## Revelation 1–2 — rendered 2026-11-20, 1:15 AM CST

**Landed:** Revelation 1, 2 (20 and 29 verses). Progress for the cycle is in the 2 John report. A new writer: the style is taken from the Greek itself and the rules from the fixed models.

**Source:** SBLGNT, rendered as printed where the edition marks a variant (1:5 *freed us*; 2:20 *the woman Jezebel*).

**Form kept:** no sentence starts with *And*, though nearly every Greek sentence opens with *kai*. *Idou* cut (1:7, 18; 2:10, 22: *I am about to throw her*, as for God's *hinneh* with a participle). *Tade legei* is *This is what He says, the One who…* in each letter (spec, *thus says*; Isaiah 43:1 model; note). The letters' repeated formulas are the same words each time: *To the angel of the church in… write*; *Whoever has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches*; *the one who overcomes*. *Angel* for *angelos* (fixed term; 1:20 note on its range). *Quickly* for *en tachei* and *tachy* (1:1; 2:16). Capital pronouns for God and for Jesus throughout; *one like a son of man* (1:13) lowercase, as Daniel 7:13 has it.

**Echoes in the notes, checked against this project's English and the Greek Old Testament:** Exodus 3:14 (Greek); 4:22 and Amos 1:6 (Greek); 19:6; Daniel 7:9, 13; Zechariah 12:10; Ezekiel 43:2; Isaiah 44:6; 48:12; Genesis 2:9; 3:24; 1 John 5:4; Numbers 25:1–2; 31:16; Jeremiah 17:10 (Greek); Psalm 2:9 (Greek). *Chalkolibanon* occurs in no other book in the project's Greek sources.

**The checker** found 0 errors and nothing to look at.

**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** *The revelation of Jesus Christ*, sent to the seven churches of Asia *from Him who is and who was and who is to come*. On Patmos, *in the Spirit on the Lord's day*, John hears a voice like a trumpet and turns to see one like a son of man among seven golden lampstands, with hair white as wool and a voice like many waters: *I am the First and the Last… I was dead, and I am alive forever and ever.*
- **2:** the first four letters. Ephesus has endurance but has left its first love; Smyrna is poor but rich, *Be faithful to death*; Pergamum lives where Satan's throne is, and some hold the teaching of Balaam; Thyatira tolerates *the woman Jezebel*. To each, a promise *to the one who overcomes*: the tree of life, the crown of life, hidden manna and a white stone, authority over the nations and the morning star.

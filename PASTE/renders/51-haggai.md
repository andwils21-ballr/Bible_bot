**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** in the second year of Darius, the returned exiles have built their own fine houses but left the LORD's house in ruins, saying the time has not come. Haggai tells them to consider their ways: they sow much and harvest little, and their wages go into a bag with holes, because the LORD has called a drought on the land. The governor Zerubbabel, the high priest Joshua and the people obey; the LORD says *I am with you*, stirs up their spirit, and within a month they begin work.
- **2:** the old people who remember Solomon's temple think the new one is nothing; the LORD says *be strong and work*, for He will shake the nations, their treasures will come, and the latter glory of this house will be greater than the former. A question to the priests about holy and unclean things shows that the people's offerings have been unclean; but from the day the foundation is laid, the LORD will bless them. Last, a word to Zerubbabel: when the LORD overturns the thrones of kingdoms, He will make him like a signet ring, for He has chosen him.

**Choices for you:**
- **2:7, *u-va'u chemdat kol ha-goyim***
  - (a) **the treasures of all the nations will come** (now): the verb is plural, so the singular noun means the precious things; the next verse says *The silver is Mine, and the gold is Mine*, and the Greek has *the choice things of all the nations*.
  - (b) **the Desire of all nations will come**: one person, the one all nations long for, as the line has long been read of a coming king.
  - Recommend (a): the plural verb and v8 make it wealth; the note gives the word's singular form.
- **2:9, *kevod ha-bayit ha-zeh ha-acharon***
  - (a) **The latter glory of this house will be greater than the former** (now): *latter* goes with *glory*; it is one house throughout, as in v3, *this house in its former glory*.
  - (b) **The glory of this latter house will be greater than the former**: *latter* goes with *house*, the second temple against the first.
  - Recommend (a): v3 speaks of *this house* with a *former glory*, and the LORD calls it *this house* throughout; the note gives both.

**The checker** found 0 errors and 1 CHECK: 2:2 *comes into your assembly* stands; the readers are at the place.

**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** count trials as joy; ask God for wisdom without doubting; the rich fade like a flower. God tempts no one: desire gives birth to sin, and sin to death, but God *brought us forth by the word of truth*. *Be doers of the word, and not hearers only*; pure religion.
- **2:** no favoritism to the rich man in the assembly; the royal law, *love your neighbor as yourself*; *mercy triumphs over judgment*. *Faith by itself, if it does not have works, is dead*: Abraham and Rahab.

**Choices for you:**
- **2:1, *tou kyriou hēmōn Iēsou Christou tēs doxēs***
  - (a) **our Lord Jesus Christ, the Lord of glory** (now): *of glory* tells who He is, as Paul's *the Lord of glory* (1 Corinthians 2:8).
  - (b) **our glorious Lord Jesus Christ**: *of glory* as a describing word.
  - (c) **our Lord Jesus Christ, the Glory**: Christ Himself named as *the Glory*.
  - Recommend (a): it keeps the title Paul gives Him and adds nothing the Greek does not have.

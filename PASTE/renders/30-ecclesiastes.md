# New Chapters: Ecclesiastes

Reports on newly rendered chapters, newest at the top. Format: CLAUDE.md, section 5.

## Ecclesiastes 5–8 — rendered 2026-10-07, 5:43 PM CDT

**Source:** Hebrew (Westminster Leningrad Codex), prose throughout. **Numbering:** 5:1 is Hebrew 4:17, added by hand because the source script prints only Hebrew 5. English 5:2–20 = Hebrew 5:1–19, so the checker's flag on 5 (20 vs 19) is expected.

**What these chapters are:** "let your words be few," vows paid, "He who loves money will not be satisfied with money," "naked he shall go again as he came" (5); the man who has everything and cannot enjoy it, "like a shadow" (6); "A good name is better than precious oil," "Better is the end of a thing than its beginning," "Do not be overly righteous," "God made man upright, but they have sought out many schemes" (7); the king's word, "no discharge in war," the righteous treated as the wicked deserve, "So I commended joy" (8).

**Kept to the word table:** *hevel* "vanity" every time, including the plural *havalim* "vanities" (5:7), "his life of vanity" (6:12) and "my days of vanity" (7:15). *yitron* "gain" (5:9, 5:16, 7:12); *yoter* "advantage." *mal'akh* "messenger" (5:6). *ruach* in 8:8 is "the breath," lowercase. *ka'as* is "vexation" in 5:17 and "sorrow" in 7:3, where the sense needs it; the note on 7:3 links the two.

**Noted:** 5:6 the Greek reads "before God" for "before the messenger"; 7:27 the only feminine verb with *Qohelet*; 8:10 "forgotten" vs the Greek "praised."

**Uncertain lines, with the other reading in the note:** 5:9 (obscure); 8:2 ("As for me" — the Hebrew has a bare "I"; many supply "I say"); 8:10.

**Left standing on purpose:** 6:3 "a hundred children" — the Hebrew says "a hundred," and "children" is the noun "fathers" requires, not an addition of meaning. 7:21 "the words that are spoken" keeps the Hebrew's unnamed "they" out of the line.

## Ecclesiastes 1–4 — rendered 2026-10-07, 5:37 PM CDT

**Source:** Hebrew (Westminster Leningrad Codex). A new writer: the style is taken from the source (plain, circling prose, with the poem of times in 3:2–8 set as poetry). Written and read form at 4:8 ("his eye," spelling). **Numbering:** the Hebrew 4 has 17 verses; English numbering is followed, so Hebrew 4:17 will be rendered as 5:1 (flag on 4 expected; logged in NOTES_FOR_ANDREW.md for HANDOFF.md).

**What these chapters are:** "Vanity of vanities… All is vanity," the sun and wind and streams going round, "nothing new under the sun" (1); the king's experiment with pleasure and works, and the wise man dying like the fool (2); "For everything there is a season," "He has also put eternity in their hearts" (3); oppression with no comforter, "Two are better than one… a threefold cord is not quickly broken" (4).

**Spec rule applied:** *hevel* is "vanity" every time (Andrew, 2026-10-07), and 1:2 carries the required note on Abel's name, worded as the spec gives it.

**Word table for the book:** *Qohelet* "the Preacher"; *hevel* "vanity"; *re'ut ruach* / *ra'yon ruach* "a chasing after wind" (wind, not spirit); *yitron* "gain"; *amal* "toil"; *miqreh* "fate"; *tachat hashemesh* "under the sun." 3:19–21 *ruach* "breath" / "spirit," lowercase.

**Noted:** 2:25 follows the Hebrew "apart from me" (Greek "apart from Him," in the note).

**Uncertain lines, with the other reading in the note:** 2:8 "many concubines"; 2:12; 3:5; 3:15.

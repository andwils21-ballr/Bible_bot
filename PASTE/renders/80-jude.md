**The checker** found 0 errors and nothing to look at.

**What this letter is:** Jude writes to urge his readers to *contend for the faith that was handed down once for all*: men have slipped in who turn grace into indecency. He reminds them of Israel in the wilderness, the angels kept in bonds, Sodom and Gomorrah, Michael and the devil, Cain, Balaam and Korah, and Enoch's prophecy; then: *keep yourselves in the love of God*, and have mercy on those who are at odds.

**Choices for you:**

- **12, *spilades***
  - (a) **hidden reefs** (now): rocks under the surface that wreck a ship before anyone sees them; the first of the pictures from nature that follow (clouds, trees, waves, stars).
  - (b) **stains** (or **blots**): the word 2 Peter uses in the same place, *They are blots and blemishes* (2 Peter 2:13), and the sound of v23's *the tunic stained by the flesh*.
  - Recommend (a): it is the word's plain meaning, and the run of nature pictures it begins; the note gives (b).

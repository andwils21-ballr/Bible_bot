**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** Timothy is to stop false teachers at Ephesus; the Law is good if used rightly. Paul, *a blasphemer, a persecutor*, was shown mercy: *Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, and of them I am the foremost.*
- **2:** pray for all, for God *wants all people to be saved*; *one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus*. Men and women in worship.
- **3:** what overseers and deacons must be; the church, *a pillar and foundation of the truth*, and the hymn of *the mystery of godliness*.
- **4:** false teachers who forbid marriage and foods; *everything God created is good*. *Let no one look down on you because you are young.*

**Choices for you:**
- **2:12, *oude authentein andros***
  - (a) **to have authority over a man** (now).
  - (b) **to domineer over a man**: authority taken for oneself.
  - Recommend (a): it is the plainer sense and does not decide in the text what the note leaves open; the verb is found nowhere else in the Bible.
- **3:11, *gynaikas hōsautōs***
  - (a) **Women likewise** (now): women who serve as deacons, set between two verses about deacons.
  - (b) **Their wives likewise**: the deacons' wives.
  - Recommend (a): the Greek has no *their*, and *likewise* is the word that opens the deacons' list in v8; Romans 16:1 calls Phoebe *a deacon*.

**Landed:** 2 Meqabyan 1, 2, 3, 4. Progress 695 → **699 of 1,989 (35.1%)**. Esther 11–16 still waits on your choice.

**Cut and damaged:** 3:9 *[number lost]* (*))*; the ten words have *thousands*, noted). 4:26 breaks off after *horses clad in iron* (*[words lost]*); the note cites `SOURCES.md`, which records *five hundred* horses in the Church's Amharic.

**Choices for you:**

- **Words kept in Ge'ez:** 4:19 *marānātā*, *milonāt*, *ḫoṣādāt* (furnishings).

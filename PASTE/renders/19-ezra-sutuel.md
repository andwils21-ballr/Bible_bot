**What these chapters are:**
- **3:** in the thirtieth year after the city's ruin, Ezra in Babylon pours out his complaint: from Adam to David the evil heart was never taken away, and Zion was given to a Babylon no better. *Weigh our sins in the balance.*
- **4:** the angel Uriel: *weigh the fire, measure the wind, call back the day*; the forest and the sea that made war; *the evil has been sown, but its harvest has not yet come*; the souls of the righteous in their chambers; the womb that cannot hold its child; the furnace and the cloud: more time has passed than remains.

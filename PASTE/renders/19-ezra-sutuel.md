**What these chapters are:**
- **8:** *The Most High made this world for the many, but the world to come for the few.* Ezra pleads for the creature formed in the womb and taught by God's law, then prays *the prayer of Ezra, before he was taken up*: *Do not look on the sins of Your people, but on the righteousness of those who serve You.* The angel: not every seed the farmer sows comes up; *for you paradise is opened, the tree of life is planted*.
- **9:** the signs of the visitation; *I kept for Myself one grape out of a cluster, and one plant out of a great forest.* Ezra fasts in the field of Arphad: the law sown in Israel does not perish, though those who received it do. A woman appears, weeping, with ashes on her head: she was barren thirty years, then bore a son.

## Ezra Sutuel 7 — rendered 2026-11-18, 7:10 PM CST

**Landed:** Ezra Sutuel 7, the longest chapter of the book (139 verses, as long as four or five ordinary chapters). Progress 1,520 → **1,521 of 1,989 (76.5%)**.

**Source:** as for 3–6. **Numbering:** the King James Version has 70 verses in this chapter, because its Latin lacks vv36–105; the Ethiopic, the Syriac and the restored Latin have them all, and the chapter is numbered as they number it, 1–139 (first note). Laurence's three chapters V, VI and VII are 7:1–35, 36–105 and 106–139, as Bible 2 keyed them.

**Form kept:** no sentence starts with *And* except *and Hezekiah* inside v110's list. The angel's *I*, *Me*, *My* capitalized where the words are God's (the choice of Ezra Sutuel 5–6, decided (a)): *My Israel… I made the world* (vv10–11), *My Messiah* (vv28–29), *My decrees* (v104), *Me, who have spoken to them* (v131). The Messiah's pronouns capitalized (v28). *Gehenna* (v36), as Matthew 5:22. *Achan* (v107), the received name, for Laurence's *Achaz*. *O Lord* where Ezra speaks to God (vv17, 132), *my lord* to the angel (vv3, 10, 53, 102).

**Echoes in the notes, checked against this project's English:** Romans 5:12; Deuteronomy 30:19; Exodus 34:6.

**The checker** found 0 errors and 3 CHECKs: v69 *come into judgment* reworded (*face judgment*); v21 and v132 *came/come into the world* stand, an idiom for being born.

**What this chapter is:**
- **7:** the sea with a narrow mouth and the city with one narrow path: the way to the wide world runs through the narrow one. *My Messiah will be revealed… My Child, My Messiah, will die*; seven days of silence, the resurrection and the day of judgment, a day with no sun or moon, *only the radiance of the glory of God*. Few saved, many lost: gold is rare. The souls after death, the seven ways of torment and the seven orders of consolation; no one can plead for another. *O Adam, what have you done?* Ezra ends with the names of God's mercy.

**What these chapters are:**
- **5:** the signs of the end: the sun by night, blood from wood, wisdom hidden in her chamber. Phaltiel comes to Ezra; he fasts. *From all the forests You chose one vine… one dove… one people*: why give the one to the many? ## Ezra Sutuel 1–4 — rendered 2026-11-18, 1:11 AM CST

**What these chapters are:**
- **3:** in the thirtieth year after the city's ruin, Ezra in Babylon pours out his complaint: from Adam to David the evil heart was never taken away, and Zion was given to a Babylon no better. *Weigh our sins in the balance.*
- **4:** the angel Uriel: *weigh the fire, measure the wind, call back the day*; the forest and the sea that made war; *the evil has been sown, but its harvest has not yet come*; the souls of the righteous in their chambers; the womb that cannot hold its child; the furnace and the cloud: more time has passed than remains.

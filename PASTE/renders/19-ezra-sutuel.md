**Source:** as for 3–4: the Ethiopic as Laurence's English gives it, checked against the shape of Dillmann's edition, the Latin and the Syriac; the Latin's numbering. Where the witnesses part, the reading followed is in the table or a note.

**What these chapters are:**
- **5:** the signs of the end: the sun by night, blood from wood, wisdom hidden in her chamber. Phaltiel comes to Ezra; he fasts. *From all the forests You chose one vine… one dove… one people*: why give the one to the many? The angel: *I liken My judgment to a circle*; the womb that bears ten children one at a time; the world grown old.
- **6:** *Its beginning was through the Son of Man, and its end will be by Me alone*; Esau and Jacob, the heel and the hand; the voice like many waters and the signs of the last days; Ezra retells the six days of creation, Behemoth and Leviathan, and asks: *If the world was created for our sake, why do we not possess it?*

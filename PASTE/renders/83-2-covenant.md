## 2nd Book of the Covenant 2–7 — rendered 2026-11-25, 7:08 PM CST

**Landed:** chapters 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7. Progress 1,627 → **1,633 of 1,989 (82.1%)**. Esther 11–16 is still held on the October choice.

**Source:** Guerrier's French of the **Ethiopic** (1913). James's English begins only at section 12, so these chapters have the French alone. The French OCR is poor here: lines of Ge'ez read as Latin letters are left out, and a footnote on the locusts read into 5:1 is left out of the text (its substance is in the note on 4:4). Where a word is lost, the gap is kept: **4:3** (*in the same —*) and **6:1** (two places, noted). Guerrier's words in parentheses, which he supplies to make the French run, are rendered without the parentheses.

**Form kept:** a new writer, so the style is taken from the French itself: the risen Lord's prophecy in short, piled clauses, and the woes on the nations in the voice of the prophets. No sentence starts with *And*. Capital pronouns for the Lord and the Father; the Deceiver lowercase. *Gehenna*, *the Antichrist* and *the Perverse One* as Guerrier has them. The prophecy quoted in 4:3 is set in italics.

**Echoes in the notes, checked against this project's English:** Isaiah 5:14; Psalm 50:18–21; Joel 1:4; Isaiah 47:1; 2 Corinthians 6:16, 18.

**The checker** found 0 errors and nothing to look at.

**What these chapters are:** the first part of the book, the prophecy the risen Lord gives the eleven in Galilee: the fate of sinners; the kingdoms of the four quarters destroyed; signs in heaven and on earth, earthquakes, drought, plague and war within families; woes on the lands of Asia Minor, from Syria to Judea; the Antichrist and his face; and the sign that only the chosen will know.

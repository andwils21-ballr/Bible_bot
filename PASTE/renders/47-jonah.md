**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** the LORD sends Jonah to Nineveh, the great city of Assyria, to cry out against its evil. Jonah runs the other way, down to the port of Joppa and onto a ship bound for Tarshish, and goes to sleep below deck. The LORD hurls a storm at the sea. The pagan sailors pray and throw the cargo overboard; the lot falls on Jonah, who tells them he fears the LORD who made the sea, and asks them to throw him in. They try to row to shore, pray not to be blamed for his death, and throw him overboard. The sea goes calm, and the sailors fear the LORD and make vows. A great fish swallows Jonah.
- **2:** from inside the fish Jonah prays a psalm, made largely of the words of the Psalms: he went down to the bottom of the sea, the waters closed over him, and the LORD brought his life up from the pit. He promises sacrifice and vows: *Salvation belongs to the LORD!* The fish spits him out on dry ground.

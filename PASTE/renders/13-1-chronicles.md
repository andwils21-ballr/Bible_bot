## 1 Chronicles 2–5 (routine, 12:51 PM Central, 2026-10-02)

**Landed:** 1 Chronicles 2, 3, 4, 5. Progress 533 → **537 of 1,989 (27.0%)**. Next is 1 Chronicles 6.

**Numbering.** English 1 Chronicles 5 ends at v26; the Hebrew runs on to 5:41, and English 6:1–15 is Hebrew 5:27–41. Logged in `NOTES_FOR_ANDREW.md`, since `source_text.py` prints only Hebrew 6 for chapter 6.

**Departures from the Hebrew, by your witnesses rule:**
- **2:24**, *After Hezron died, Caleb went in to Ephrathah*. The Hebrew reads *in Caleb-ephrathah*, a place named nowhere else; the Greek has *Caleb went in to Ephratha*, and v19 has Caleb marrying *Ephrath*. Noted.
- **2:50**, *These were the sons of Caleb. The sons of Hur the firstborn of Ephrathah*. The Hebrew reads *Caleb son of Hur*, against v19 (Hur son of Caleb) and 4:4; the Greek has *the sons of Hur*. Noted.
- **4:3**, *These were the sons of Etam*. The Hebrew reads *these the father of Etam*; the Greek has *the sons of Etam*. Noted.
- **4:39**, *the entrance of Gerar*. The Hebrew has *Gedor*, a town of Judah; the Greek has *Gerar*, and v40 says the people there were *of Ham* (Genesis 10:19 puts Gerar on the Canaanite border). Noted.

**Choice for you:**
- **3:19**, *The sons of Pedaiah: Zerubbabel*. (a) **Pedaiah**, as the Hebrew of Chronicles has it, making Zerubbabel Shealtiel's nephew. (b) **Shealtiel**, as the Greek here reads (*the sons of Shealtiel: Zerubbabel*) and as Ezra 3:2, Haggai 1:1 and Matthew 1:12 call him *son of Shealtiel*. The Hebrew is clear and does not contradict itself, so the text keeps (a) and the note gives (b). Recommend (a); the Greek may have brought the verse into line with Ezra and Haggai, but that cannot be shown.

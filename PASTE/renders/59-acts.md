**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** to Theophilus: for forty days the risen Jesus shows Himself to the apostles and tells them to wait in Jerusalem for the Holy Spirit; *you will be My witnesses… to the end of the earth*. He is taken up in a cloud, and two men in white say He will come again the same way. In the upper room, with Mary and His brothers, they pray; Judas's place is filled by lot, and it falls on Matthias.
- **2:** at Pentecost a sound like wind fills the house, tongues as of fire rest on them, and they speak in other languages, heard by Jews from every nation. Peter stands up: this is what Joel foretold; Jesus whom they crucified God raised and made *both Lord and Messiah*. Three thousand are baptized, and the believers share all they have, breaking bread and praising God.

**Choices for you:**
- **1:4, *synalizomenos***
  - (a) **While He was eating with them** (now): from *hals*, **salt**, sharing a meal; Luke tells that the risen Jesus ate before them (Luke 24:42–43).
  - (b) **While He was staying with them**: from *aulizomai*, to lodge, a reading some take with a change of one vowel.
  - Recommend (a): it is the word as printed, and Luke has the eating; the note gives (b).

## 1 Enoch 29–36 (rendered at Andrew's request, 2026-09-26)

**Landed:** 1 Enoch 29–36, the end of the *Book of the Watchers* (chapters 1–36). The Greek witness stops at 32:6; chapters 33–36 are rendered from the Ge'ez alone. Chapters 1–28 were rendered 2026-09-17; their later changes are recorded in `PASTE/changes/16-1-enoch.md`.

| Verse | Word-for-word | In the text now |

| 1 | Ge'ez *emna madbara*, "from the desert"; Greek *Babdera* | beyond the desert (Greek place name noted) |

### Left standing on purpose

- **29:2** "trees of judgment": both witnesses say it, and neither explains it.
- **30:1** "Beyond it, above these, above the mountain of the east": the Ge'ez piles up place words, and the pile is kept.
- **34:3** "with violence… with violence": the only witness is rough here.

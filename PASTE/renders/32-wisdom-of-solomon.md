## Wisdom of Solomon 1–4 — rendered 2026-10-07, 6:13 PM CDT

**Source:** Greek (Swete), the language the book was written in; verse numbers match the English. A new writer: the style is taken from the Greek itself, balanced lines of Greek poetry, set in lines here as the parallels fall. Every Old and New Testament echo in the notes was checked against the Greek held in `sources/` (Isaiah 3:10, 28:15, 52:13; Proverbs 1:7; Psalm 2:4; Genesis 5:24; Job 1:6; Matthew 27:43; Romans 5:12; Colossians 1:17; Hebrews 11:5; 1 Thessalonians 4:17).

**What these chapters are:** "Love righteousness, you who judge the earth," "God did not make death" (1); the reasoning of the ungodly, "Let us crown ourselves with rosebuds," the plot against the righteous man, "if the righteous man is God's son, He will help him," "through the envy of the devil death entered the world" (2); "the souls of the righteous are in the hand of God," the barren woman and the eunuch (3); the righteous who die young, Enoch's story told without his name (4).

**Word table for the book:** *Kyrios* "the Lord"; *theos* "God"; *sophia* "wisdom"; *paideia* "discipline" (as *musar* in Proverbs); *phronēsis* "understanding"; *dikaios* "the righteous man"; *asebēs* "the ungodly"; *aphrōn* "foolish"; *athanasia* "immortality"; *aphtharsia* "incorruption"; *episkopē* "visitation"; *hadēs* "Hades" (as in the other Greek books); *diabolos* "the devil." *Pneuma Kyriou* (1:7) "the Spirit of the Lord," capital, per the fixed terms.

**Pronouns:** the righteous man of 2:12–20 and the one who dies young in 4:7–17 stay lowercase: the text does not name God or Jesus there, though the Gospel takes up 2:18 (note).

**The "And" check:** none. 3:14 opens "So too the eunuch," continuing the blessing of v13.

**Choices for you:**
- **1:5 "the holy spirit of discipline"** — lowercase, as the spirit of wisdom (1:6 says "wisdom is a spirit"); the fixed term capitalizes *the Spirit* when it is "of God" or "of the Lord," as 1:7 is. Wisdom 7:22 and 9:17 ("Your holy Spirit") will raise it again. Now: *For the holy spirit of discipline flees deceit.*
- **2:13 "a child of the Lord"** — *pais* is "child" or "servant"; "servant" would echo Isaiah 52:13, "child" fits the "father" and "son" of vv16 and 18. Now: *and calls himself a child of the Lord.*
- **2:24 "the devil"** — *diabolos*, the Greek word for the satan of Job 1–2 (still an open choice in Job). Now: *through the envy of the devil death entered the world.*

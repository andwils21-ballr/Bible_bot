**Choices for you:**
- **9:4, *chrysoun thymiatērion***
  - (a) **a golden altar of incense** (now): the golden altar of Exodus 30:1–6, which stood before the curtain but served the inner room.
  - (b) **a golden censer**: the only sense of the word in the Greek Old Testament (2 Chronicles 26:19; Ezekiel 8:11).
  - Recommend (a): the writer lists the furniture of Exodus, and the altar is the golden incense object Exodus names; the note gives (b).
- **9:14, *dia pneumatos aiōniou***
  - (a) **through the eternal Spirit** (now): the Holy Spirit, by whom He offered Himself.
  - (b) **through an eternal spirit**: His own spirit, *an indestructible life* (7:16).
  - Recommend (a): Hebrews names the Spirit as a person at 9:8 and 10:15, 29.
- **9:16–17, *diathēkē***
  - (a) **will** (now): a person's will, which takes effect at death; the writer plays on the everyday sense, with *inheritance* in v15.
  - (b) **covenant**, the same word as vv15 and 18: the death is the sacrifice by which a covenant was ratified.
  - Recommend (a): *takes effect only at death… never has force while the one who made it is alive* is said of a will, not of a covenant; the note shows the one Greek word.
- **11:1, *hypostasis***
  - (a) **the assurance of things hoped for** (now): faith as the believer's certainty.
  - (b) **the substance of things hoped for**: faith as what gives them reality now (the word of 1:3, *His being*).
  - Recommend (a): *elenchos*, **proof**, in the same line, and *confidence* at 3:14 point to the believer's side; the note gives (b).
- **11:11, *autē Sarra***
  - (a) **By faith, with Sarah herself, he received power to father a child** (now): Abraham is the subject, as the edition prints the words.
  - (b) **By faith Sarah herself received power to conceive**: Sarah is the subject.
  - Recommend (a): the edition's dative, the father's phrase *laying down seed*, and *from one man* in v12.

## Hebrews 1–3 — rendered 2026-11-15, 2:30 PM CST

**Landed:** Hebrews 1, 2, 3, in the same run as Philemon. Together, progress 1,481 → **1,485 of 1,989 (74.7%)**.

**Source:** Greek (SBLGNT), bracketed words included (1:2–3, 8, 12; 2:1, 8, 14; 3:3, 9, 13–14). The letter names no author. A new voice: the most polished Greek in the New Testament, long periodic sentences, and an argument built verse by verse on the Greek Old Testament. Its quotations follow the Greek where it differs from the Hebrew, and the English follows the letter.

**Form kept:**
- No sentence starts with *And* (seven *And* starts fixed: *And again* is *Again*).
- *Angelos*: **angel** throughout (the fixed term for the New Testament).
- Pronouns: in the psalms the letter applies to the Son, the Son's pronouns are capital (*You are My Son*; *Your throne, O God*); in Psalm 8, read first of humankind, *him* stays lowercase (2:6–8) until the writer names Jesus (v9).
- English reused where the Greek has the same sense: Psalm 2:7; 2 Samuel 7:14; Psalm 45:6–7; 102:25–27; 110:1; 22:22; Isaiah 8:18; Psalm 95:7–11, *disgusted*, *They shall not enter My rest*.

**Echoes in the notes, checked against this project's English:** Exodus 17:7; Deuteronomy 32:43; Psalm 8:4–6; 95:7–11; 104:4; Wisdom of Solomon 7:26.

**Checked against the sources:** SBLGNT: Hebrews 1:1–3:19. Swete and the Hebrew are reflected in the notes as this project already renders them (Deuteronomy 32:43, Psalm 104:4, Psalm 8:5, Psalm 95:8).

**The checker** found 7 errors (*And* starts), all fixed, and 0 CHECKs.

**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** God has spoken *in these last days… by His Son*, *the radiance of His glory and the exact imprint of His being*. Seven quotations show the Son above the angels.
- **2:** so do not drift away. Jesus was made *for a little while lower than the angels*, to taste death for everyone; He became like His brothers to be *a merciful and faithful high priest*.
- **3:** Jesus is greater than Moses, as the builder is greater than the house. *Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts*: the wilderness generation did not enter God's rest because of unbelief.

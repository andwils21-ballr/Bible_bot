**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** the LORD tells Israel *I have loved you*, and when they ask how, He points to Edom, Esau's people, left in ruins while Jacob's were brought home. Then He turns on the priests: they offer blind, lame and sick animals and say *Nothing wrong!*, things they would never give the governor. Better to shut the temple doors, since the LORD's name is great among the nations from east to west.
- **2:** a warning to the priests: their blessings will be cursed, and dung thrown in their faces, because they have broken the covenant of Levi, whose lips once guarded true instruction. Then to the people: they have broken faith with one another, Judah has married the daughter of a foreign god, and men have broken faith with the wives of their youth; the LORD was witness to that covenant. Last, they weary the LORD by saying that evildoers please Him: *Where is the God of justice?*

**Choices for you:**
- **1:1, *be-yad Mal'akhi***
  - (a) **through Malachi** (now): a prophet's name, as the book has always been known.
  - (b) **through My messenger**: *mal'akhi* is the plain word, *my messenger*, with no father or home given; the Greek has *by the hand of His messenger*; 3:1 has the same word.
  - Recommend (a): the received name, with its meaning in the note, as the spec does for names.
- **2:16, *ki sane shallach***
  - (a) **For the man who hates and sends her away… covers his garment with violence** (now): *sane* is *he hates*, third person; the husband who divorces out of dislike does violence.
  - (b) **For I hate divorce, says the LORD… and the one who covers his garment with violence**: the LORD's own word against divorce, reading *sane* as *I hate*.
  - Recommend (a): the Hebrew form is *he hates*,, and (a) reads the verse without changing it.

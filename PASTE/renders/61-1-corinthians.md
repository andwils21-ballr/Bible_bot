**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** Paul greets the church at Corinth and thanks God for its gifts. Then the divisions: *I belong to Paul… Apollos… Cephas… Christ*. Is Christ divided? The word of the cross is foolishness to the world but the power of God; God chose the foolish, the weak and the things that are not, so that *Let him who boasts boast in the Lord*.
- **2:** Paul came knowing nothing *except Jesus Christ, and Him crucified*. He speaks God's hidden wisdom, which the rulers of this age did not know, or they would not have crucified *the Lord of glory*; the Spirit reveals it, and *we have the mind of Christ*.
- **3:** the Corinthians are still infants of the flesh. Paul planted, Apollos watered, God gave the growth; the one foundation is Jesus Christ, and fire will test what is built on it. *You are God's temple*. All things are theirs, and they are Christ's.
- **4:** apostles are stewards, judged by the Lord alone. The Corinthians are already rich and reigning; the apostles are a spectacle, *fools for Christ's sake*. Paul, their father in Christ, sends Timothy and will come himself: *with a rod, or with love?*

**Choices for you:**
- **2:13, *pneumatikois pneumatika synkrinontes***
  - (a) **explaining spiritual things in spiritual words** (now): the Spirit gives both the truths and the words for them.
  - (b) **explaining spiritual things to spiritual people**: the truths are for those who have the Spirit, the *spiritual person* of v15.
  - Recommend (a): the sentence is about *words* (*not in words taught by human wisdom but in words taught by the Spirit*), and *pneumatikois* most naturally takes up that noun; (b) leads well into vv14–15, and the note gives both.

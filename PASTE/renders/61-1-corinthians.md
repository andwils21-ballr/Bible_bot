**What these chapters are:**
- **5:** a man in the church has his father's wife, and the church is proud of itself. Paul tells them to hand him over to Satan, *so that his spirit may be saved*: clean out the old yeast, for *Christ, our Passover lamb, has been sacrificed*. Judge those inside; *Purge the evil from among you*.
- **6:** believers take each other to pagan courts, though the saints will judge the world, and angels. The unrighteous will not inherit the kingdom, *and such were some of you; but you were washed… justified*. *All things are lawful*, but the body is for the Lord: *your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit… you were bought with a price*.
- **7:** answers to the Corinthians' letter on marriage. Husband and wife owe each other their bodies; the Lord's command is no divorce; a believer married to an unbeliever should stay. Remain as you were called, slave or free. The time is short; the unmarried can give themselves to the Lord. A father may give his daughter in marriage or keep her; a widow may remarry *only in the Lord*.
- **8:** food offered to idols. *Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up.* There is one God, the Father, and one Lord, Jesus Christ; but if eating in an idol's temple makes a weaker brother stumble, *I will never eat meat again*.

**Choices for you:**
- **6:4, *tous exouthenēmenous en tē ekklēsia, toutous kathizete***
  - (a) **why do you seat as judges those who count for nothing in the church?** (now): a rebuke for taking cases to outside judges, whom the church does not esteem.
  - (b) **seat as judges even those who count for least in the church!**: a command; even the least of the believers is better than an unbelieving court.
  - Recommend (a): v5 goes on *Is there no one among you wise enough*, which fits a rebuke of going outside better than an order to choose the least.
- **6:9, *malakoi***
  - (a) **effeminate men** (now): the plain sense of *soft ones*, as in *soft clothing* (Matthew 11:8).
  - (b) **men who take the passive part with men**: read with the next word, *men who lie with men*, as a pair.
  - Recommend (a): it keeps the word's own range, and the note gives the narrower reading; (b) settles in the text what the Greek leaves open.
- **7:1, *kalon anthrōpō gynaikos mē haptesthai*** (the same question for 6:12–13 and 8:1, 4)
  - (a) **in quotation marks, a saying from the Corinthians' letter** (now): Paul quotes it, then qualifies it (*But… each man should have his own wife*).
  - (b) **Paul's own statement**, no quotation marks: celibacy is good, and the chapter goes on to allow marriage.
  - Recommend (a): Paul opens with *about the things you wrote*, answers with *But*, and does the same with *All things are lawful for me* (6:12).
- **7:21, *mallon chrēsai***
  - (a) **if you can gain your freedom, take the opportunity** (now).
  - (b) **even if you can gain your freedom, make the most of your present state instead**.
  - Recommend (a): *freedom* is the nearest thing for *use it* to refer to, and v23 says *do not become slaves of men*; (b) fits *remain as you were called* (vv20, 24), and the note gives both.
- **7:36–38, *tēn parthenon autou… gamizōn***
  - (a) **a father and his virgin daughter, whom he gives in marriage or keeps at home** (now).
  - (b) **a man and the young woman he is engaged to, whom he marries or does not marry**.
  - Recommend (a): *gamizō* is *give in marriage* every time it occurs in the Gospels (Matthew 22:30; 24:38; Mark 12:25; Luke 17:27; 20:34–35); many recent English Bibles read (b).

**What these chapters are:**
- **1:** Paul greets the church at Corinth and thanks God for its gifts. Then the divisions: *I belong to Paul… Apollos… Cephas… Christ*. Is Christ divided? The word of the cross is foolishness to the world but the power of God; God chose the foolish, the weak and the things that are not, so that *Let him who boasts boast in the Lord*.
- **2:** Paul came knowing nothing *except Jesus Christ, and Him crucified*. He speaks God's hidden wisdom, which the rulers of this age did not know, or they would not have crucified *the Lord of glory*; the Spirit reveals it, and *we have the mind of Christ*.
- **3:** the Corinthians are still infants of the flesh. Paul planted, Apollos watered, God gave the growth; the one foundation is Jesus Christ, and fire will test what is built on it. *You are God's temple*. All things are theirs, and they are Christ's.
- **4:** apostles are stewards, judged by the Lord alone. The Corinthians are already rich and reigning; the apostles are a spectacle, *fools for Christ's sake*. Paul, their father in Christ, sends Timothy and will come himself: *with a rod, or with love?*

## *pach* and *moqesh* kept apart (render cycle, 2026-09-29, 12:51 PM Central)

**Request:** none; found while rendering Judges 2:3. One Hebrew word, one English word: *moqesh* is **snare** in Exodus 10:7, 23:33, 34:12 and Deuteronomy 7:16, and Judges 2:3 follows. Joshua 23:13 had given *pach u-moqesh* as *a snare and a trap*, so *moqesh* came out as *trap* there. Swapped so *moqesh* stays *snare* and *pach* is *trap*.

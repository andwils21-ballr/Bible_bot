## The sweep after Joshua 7: position words and *by* lists (2026-11-06)

**Request:** Andrew's ruling on Joshua 7 (entry below), carried to the books after it (`COUNCIL.md`, item 101; spec, readability pass items 6 and 9). Bible 2 swept Joshua 8 to the last rendered chapter, Genesis to Deuteronomy left alone.

**Options laid out:** each place was made natural only where the source and the sentence allow it and no note is built on the phrase.
- ***from before*** / ***before the face of*** (Hebrew *mi-lifnei*, *lifnei*; Aramaic *min qodam*; and the same idiom in the Ge'ez books): *from Your sight* where eyes or sight are the point (Psalm 31:22, Jonah 2:4, which now read alike, as their notes say; Isaiah 1:16; Nehemiah 4:5 and Jeremiah 18:23, which also read alike), *from My presence* where it is the LORD's presence, *before* where *the face of* adds nothing. Bible Main found the New Testament's one: *I am sending My messenger before Your face* (Greek *pro prosōpou sou*; Matthew 11:10, Mark 1:2, Luke 7:27) is *ahead of You*.
- ***by tribes… by clans*** in a casting of lots (1 Samuel 10:19–21, as Joshua 7:14–18), and land given *by their tribes* (Joshua 11:23; Ezekiel 45:8): *tribe by tribe*, *clan by clan*. *By your thousands* (*alafim*) is *clan by clan*, as this project's Judges 6:15 has *my clan* for *alpi*.
- ***This and this*** (2 Samuel 17:15) and ***the name of that place is called*** (2 Chronicles 20:26; Jubilees 29:8), as Joshua 7:20 and 7:26.

**Left as they are:**
- **Joshua 10:10–11** *the ascent… the descent of Beth-horon*: a named road, and the Sirach 46 note is built on Sirach's *at the descent*.
- **Jeremiah 48:5** *the ascent of Luhith… the descent of Horonaim*: named roads, as in Isaiah 15:5.
- ***from the face of the earth*** (every place): English.
- ***such and such*** (1 Samuel 21:2; 1 Kings 14:5; 2 Kings 5:4, 6:8, 9:12): English.
- ***by their clans*** in lists and genealogies (1 Chronicles 5:7, 6:62–63; Nehemiah 4:13 *by families*; 1 Meqabyan 16:2): English.
- **Jubilees 12:4** *all life is from before His face* and **17:2** *was before the face of Abraham his father*, and **1 Enoch 39:8, 39:10, 41:4** *from before* (*from of old*, *before the world*): what the Ge'ez means is not certain enough to change it.
- **1 Meqabyan 24:12** *before the face of a man… the face of the gray-haired*: the repeated *face* is the verse's point.

**Choices for you:** none; no meaning changes.

## Joshua 2:14, "Our life for yours, to die" (2026-11-06)

**Request:** Andrew: "Our life for yours, to die — if you do not tell this business of ours" does not read naturally. Cause: the Hebrew is verbless, *nafshenu tachtekhem la-mut*, "our life in place of yours, to die"; the English copied the clipped form. The spies are promising Rahab and her family that they will stake their own lives on her safety, so the fix says that as a sentence. *Tachat* is "in place of, for" and *la-mut* "to death" (the Greek has the same, *eis thanaton*); the condition, *if you do not tell*, stays last as in the Hebrew.

Second part of the request (Andrew, same day): the *And it shall come to pass* in the same verse is not needed. *Ve-hayah be-tet YHWH lanu* sits inside a speech already running, a promise that follows the pledge, so it opens nothing; *When the LORD gives us the land* carries the time (spec, *And it came to pass*, "inside a scene already running").

**Choices for you:** none; a wording fix with no change of meaning.

## Future *And it shall come to pass* cut, every book but Genesis (2026-11-06)

**Request:** Andrew, after Joshua 2:14: cut *And it shall come to pass* (and *will*) in Joshua and every other book, except where it carries the text across a passage of time of significance. It is usually God's words or words quoted, but not only God's. The rule is now in the spec (*Its future forms*). Genesis keeps every one (its mark), so it was not touched.

**What was kept (47 verses; Exodus 4:16 was put back at Andrew's word, as "over the course of his lifetime"):** the openings that name a stretch of time of weight: *in that day* / *on that day* (Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Hosea, Amos, Micah, Joel, Zephaniah, Zechariah), *in the last days* (Isaiah 2:2; Micah 4:1; Acts 2:17), *in the end of days* (Jeremiah 49:39), *in those days* (1 Enoch 39:1, 52:7; Jeremiah 3:16), *at that time* (Zephaniah 1:12), *afterward* (Joel 2:28), *in the day the LORD gives you rest* (Isaiah 14:3), seventy years (Isaiah 23:17; Jeremiah 25:12), *from the eighth day on* (Ezekiel 43:27), *on the day of the LORD's sacrifice* (Zephaniah 1:8). Not touched as they are not the formula: Jeremiah 39:16, Zechariah 6:15 and 1 Enoch 52:2 (*come to pass* as a plain verb). Notes: Judith 14 lost its v2 note (it was about the cut formula alone); the Jubilees 46 note's quotation of Exodus 1:10 follows the new text. Exodus 12:27, which Exodus 12:26 shared a verse chain with, had a sentence starting *And the people bowed down* that the checker had not seen; it is now *The people bowed down and worshiped*. Exodus 33:22, a fixed-model verse, was cut with the rest at his word.

**Choices for you:** none; the cut is his ruling, and no meaning changes.

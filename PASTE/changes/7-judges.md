## Four word rulings from the notes, applied to finished books (2026-09-30, 7:35 AM Central)

### Your decisions

> Use Messenger and Spirit. Whore when used as a verb, prostitute as a noun. Keep Aviv.

These answer four items in `NOTES_FOR_ANDREW.md` (2026-09-29 and 2026-09-28). The Aviv ruling is filed in `PASTE/changes/2-exodus.md`. The Genesis 3:22 *put* question is still open (answered in chat, no edit).

### Rulings, as applied

- **Messenger.** Hebrew *mal'akh* of God or the LORD is *messenger*, never *angel*. Applied to every place in Genesis–Judges that still said *angel*.
- **Spirit.** *Ruach* of God or of the LORD is *the Spirit*, capital S, in every book, whoever is speaking (as the pronoun rule already says).
- **Whore / prostitute.** The verb (*zanah*) is *whore* (*whore after*, as Exodus 34:15–16 already had it); the noun (*zonah*) is *prostitute*.
- All three are now in the spec's fixed-terms table.

### Closed books touched by these rulings

Genesis was signed off on 2026-09-21. These verses in it changed only because of your rulings: 31:11, 34:31 (and its note), 38:24, 41:38 (and its note), 48:16 (and its note). Say the word if you want any of them put back.

- **Genesis 34:31.** The brothers' *whore* was a noun, so it is now *prostitute*. It is the one that reads most differently; the note's quotation matches.
- **Genesis 38:24.** *Has played the whore* used *whore* as a noun for a verb, so it is now *has whored*, matching *by whoring* in the same verse.

### Left as they were, on purpose

- **Lowercase *spirit* for a person's own spirit or a non-divine one:** Genesis 7:22, 26:35, 41:8, 45:27; Exodus 28:3, 35:21; Numbers 5:14, 5:30, 14:24, 16:22, 27:16; Deuteronomy 2:30, 34:9; Joshua 2:11; Judges 9:23 (*an evil spirit*); and the ghosts and spirits of mediums, Leviticus 20:6 and 20:27, Deuteronomy 18:11.
- **Numbers 27:18**, *a man in whom is the spirit*: the verse does not say whose spirit; left lowercase.
- **Noun forms of the same root:** Leviticus 19:29 and 21:14 (*prostitution*), Numbers 14:33 (note), Numbers 15:39 (*like a prostitute*), Deuteronomy 23:18, Joshua 2:1 and 6, Judges 11:1.
- **Genesis 1:2** still reads *the breath of God*, although the Exodus 15:8 note speaks of *the Spirit hovered*. That is a translation choice in a closed book; see `NOTES_FOR_ANDREW.md`.
- **Greek *angelos* (Matthew) and the Ge'ez books keep *angel*.** Decided in the same session, with the reasons: the Greek word also names a whole class of heavenly beings (Hebrews 1:6, the note on Deuteronomy 32:8), where *messenger* would blur the point, and the spec forbids building an argument on what a Ge'ez word means, so *angel* stays in Jubilees, 1 Enoch and the Meqabyan. *Harlot* in Jubilees became *prostitute* (`PASTE/changes/15-jubilees.md`), because that is a rule about English.

## Acts 27:17, *chalasantes to skeuos*: the gear is the sail (Andrew, 2026-11-07, 11:15 AM CST)

**Request:** Andrew: *gear* seems odd for an anchor but natural for a ship's sailing parts, and *anchor* is named outright soon after (27:29); use *sails*.

**Options, with every verse involved:**
- 27:17 *chalasantes to skeuos*, **having let down the gear**. *Skeuos* is singular, any piece of equipment.
- (a) **lowered the sail** (now): what follows fits it. They are *driven along* before the wind (v17), and at the end the sail they hoist to reach the beach is *the foresail*, *ton artemōna* (v40).
- (b) **lowered the sea anchor**: a drag let down behind the ship to slow it. Not one of the ship's *ankyrai* (vv29, 30, 40), so the anchor verses do not rule it out; the context leans to (a).
- (c) **lowered the gear**, the word left unnamed (before).
- Decided (a), singular, since *skeuos* is singular. The note keeps the literal *gear* and the sea-anchor reading.

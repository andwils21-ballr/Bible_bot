# Decision Making with Before/After: Nehemiah

Changes to chapters already rendered, filed under the book the request started in (a word decision can reach into other books; its table lists every verse). Newest at the top. Each entry: the request, the options, and a `Where | Before | After` table.

## "And it came to pass": kept only where it opens something; swept through every book but Genesis (Andrew, 2026-10-07)

**Request:** Andrew, on the proposal after Nehemiah 1:4: "Yes. And what about the 'that'?"

**The rule now** (spec, *Kept on purpose*): keep the formula where it opens a book, opens a chapter's story in its first verse, marks a new time (a date, *after this*, *the next day*, *in the morning*…), or is a refrain repeated on purpose (Matthew's *when Jesus had finished these words*). Cut it inside a scene already running, together with its *that*. Where it is kept, its *that* stays.

**Swept:** 144 lines in 287 uses outside Genesis; about 150 uses stand under the new rule. Genesis (65 uses) is closed and was not touched. Six verses where the *that* fell in the next verse (Deuteronomy 2:17, 31:25; Joshua 3:16, 10:21; Judges 6:8; 2 Chronicles 5:13) lost it there. No note quoted a cut formula. `check_chapters.py --all` shows the same 12 old errors as before the sweep and no new ones.

## Joel 2:13 brought in line with Jonah 4:2 (render cycle, 2026-10-27)

**Request:** found while rendering Jonah 4. *Ve-nicham al ha-ra'ah* in Joel 2:13 and Jonah 4:2 is the same formula, word for word (spec, Style 6: repeated formulas stay identical). Joel 2:13 had *He relents of disaster*, after Jeremiah 26:19; Jonah needs *evil*, because *ra'ah* is its running word (1:2, 7, 8; 3:8, 10; 4:1, 2, 6) and 3:10 is word for word Exodus 32:14, *relented of the evil*. Both verses now follow Exodus 32:12, 14. A cleanup within this project's own rule, not a choice of meaning.

| Where | Before | After |

| Joel 2, note vv12–14 | *He relents of disaster*, as *the LORD relented of the evil* at the calf | *He relents of the evil*, as *the LORD relented of the evil* at the calf |

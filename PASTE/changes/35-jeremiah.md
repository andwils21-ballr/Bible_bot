## *Keli*: the best word in each verse (Andrew, 2026-10-18)

**Request:** Andrew, on the 25:34 choice: "Does vessel have to be the word?" Then: a word that fits 22:28 and 25:34 both, and "the best choice for each one. Not a blanket coverage."

**Options laid out:** *keli* is any container, tool or household object. *Keli chemdah* (25:34) elsewhere is treasure of silver and gold, often taken as plunder (2 Chronicles 20:25; 32:27; 36:10; Daniel 11:8; Hosea 13:15; Nahum 2:9), so *china*, *glass* and *bowl* were set aside: each adds a material or a shape the Hebrew does not have. *Jar* for the household containers, *pot* where the potter is at his wheel, and *vessels* kept for the temple's treasures, the phrase of Kings, Chronicles, Ezra and Daniel. 22:28 and 25:34 match on purpose: *a jar no one wants*, *a precious jar*. Andrew: "Yes, make those changes." The 25:34 choice itself (the Hebrew's *jar* or the Greek's *choice rams*) is still open.

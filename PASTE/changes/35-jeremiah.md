## Jeremiah 18:17, *oref ve-lo fanim er'em* (2026-10-17)

**Request:** Andrew's ruling on the Jeremiah 17–20 choice: (b), *I will show them My back and not My face*, the letters read *ar'em* as the Greek reads them, answering 2:27.

| Where | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Jeremiah 18:17 | I will look on their backs and not their faces | I will show them My back and not My face |
| Jeremiah 18, note v17 | **"I will look on their backs and not their faces"** — *oref ve-lo fanim er'em*, "back and not face I will see them," as the Hebrew is voweled. With other vowels, *ar'em*, it is *I will show them My back*… | **"I will show them My back and not My face"** — *oref ve-lo fanim ar'em*, the letters read with the vowels of "I will show them," as the Greek reads them… The Hebrew vowels give *er'em*, "I will see them": *I will look on their backs and not their faces*… |

## Jeremiah 15:14, *ve-ha'avarti et oyevekha* (2026-10-17)

**Request:** Andrew's ruling on the Jeremiah 13–16 choice: (b), *serve your enemies*, as the same lines read in 17:4 and as the Greek reads here.

| Where | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Jeremiah 15:14 | I will make you go with your enemies / into a land you do not know; | I will make you serve your enemies / in a land you do not know; |
| Jeremiah 15, note vv13–14 | **"… I will make you go with your enemies"** — these lines come again in 17:3–4…; *ha'avarti*, "make go," here differs by one letter from *ha'avadti*, "make serve," there. The Greek has *serve* here as well. | **"… I will make you serve your enemies"** — these lines come again in 17:3–4…; the text follows 17:4 and the Greek, which has *serve* here as well. It is the curse of the covenant, *you will serve your enemies* (Deuteronomy 28:48). |

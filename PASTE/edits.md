**As a side note for conversation with you: I can’t help but notice the talk about the dark cloud and the fire in the sky at night, then the “waters” being split with a wall of water on both sides. Then, God’s people were put into the middle on dry hard ground (beating it flat as they herd through). Then, God’s people are allowed out of the splitting of the water, but the ones who have crossed God and not feared him are caught between the collapsing waters and killed. This sounds much like the creation of earth and God’s people sent down a path, and then judgement day where God’s people join him, while the others are killed and the “old earth” is no more, as if it is erased by the “waters” that were originally split.

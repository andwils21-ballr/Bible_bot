# Notes for Andrew

- **2026-09-29, *mal'akh YHWH*: *messenger* or *angel*.** Genesis 16, 21 and 22 and Exodus 3 and 14 say *the messenger of the LORD / of God* (the Genesis 16:7 note argues for it). Genesis 31:11 and Numbers 22 (11 times) say *the angel of God / of the LORD*. Judges 2:1, 4 follows Genesis and Exodus: *the messenger of the LORD*. Judges 6 and 13 use the phrase many times, so this is worth settling before them.
- **2026-09-29, *ruach* of God: *Spirit* or *spirit*.** Genesis 41:38, Exodus 31:3, 35:31 and Numbers 11:25 have lowercase *spirit*; Numbers 24:2 has *the Spirit of God*. Judges 3:10 follows the majority: *the spirit of the LORD*. Judges uses the phrase often (6:34, 11:29, 13:25, 14:6, 14:19, 15:14).
- **2026-09-29, *zanah* after gods.** Exodus 34:15–16 has *whore after* (three times); Leviticus 17:7, 20:5–6, Numbers 25:1 and Deuteronomy 31:16 have *prostitute themselves*. Judges 2:17 follows the majority. Exodus is closed, so nothing was changed.
- **2026-09-28, the month *Abib* / *Aviv*.** Exodus 23:15 and 34:18 say *the month of Abib*; Exodus 13:4 (and its note) and the Leviticus 2:14 note say *the month of Aviv*. Deuteronomy 16:1 follows the verse text of Exodus 23 and 34: *Abib*. Exodus and Leviticus are closed, so nothing was changed. Making Exodus 13:4 and the two notes read *Abib* would make all agree.
- **2026-09-28, Genesis 3:22 grammar.** The verse reads *in case he puts out his hand and take also from the tree of life, and eat, and live forever*: *puts* is followed by *take… eat… live*, which do not agree with it. Genesis is closed, so it was not touched. *Puts… takes… eats… lives* (or *put… take… eat… live* after *in case he should*) would fix it.
- **2026-09-25, Swete's Greek of Numbers 13 is one verse ahead of the Hebrew.** Greek 13:1 is Hebrew 12:16, so Hebrew 13:33 is Greek 13:34. HANDOFF's list of known offsets should get this line.
- **2026-09-25, Numbers 16–17 chapter break.** The Hebrew ends chapter 16 at the fire (16:35). English 16:36–50 is Hebrew 17:1–15, and English 17:1–13 is Hebrew 17:16–28. Swete's Greek follows the English numbering here. HANDOFF's list of known offsets should get this line.
- **2026-09-26, Numbers 25–26 chapter break.** The Hebrew ends chapter 25 with *and it came to pass after the plague* (25:19). The English and Greek begin 26:1 with those words, so English 25 has 18 verses. HANDOFF's list of known offsets should get this line.
- **2026-09-26, Numbers 29–30 chapter break.** English 29:40 is Hebrew 30:1, so English 30:1–16 is Hebrew 30:2–17. Swete's Greek follows the Hebrew numbering here (Greek 30:2 = English 30:1). HANDOFF's list of known offsets should get this line.
- **2026-09-26, Ge'ez *samen* and *dabub* (north and south).** In Jubilees 8 the EOTC's *samen* marks the side of Ham's land, which v30 calls hot, and *dabub* the side of Japheth's, which it calls cold. Charles renders them **south** and **north**, and the rendering of Jubilees 7–9 follows him. Knibb's English at 1 Enoch 26:2 renders *samen* **north**, and the note on 1 Enoch 26:2 in this project says "The Ge'ez says north" (the text there follows the Greek, *south*). Nothing in 1 Enoch was changed. If you want the two books to agree, the 1 Enoch 26:2 note is the one place to look.

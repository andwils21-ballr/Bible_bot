Textual and lexical notes
	•	This is a study translation, not a claim to establish a new critical edition of 1 Enoch. The Geʽez supplied by you is the controlling text for the document.
	•	The standard English witnesses consulted for cross-checking the sense include the R. H. Charles translation and modern Geʽez presentation/reference pages. These sources confirm the broad sense of the passages, especially chapters 1–6, but the word-by-word glosses above are keyed to your supplied wording.
	•	Some supplied verses are visibly shorter than forms commonly printed in editions of 1 Enoch. For example, your 1:2 ends at ወይሬኢ, whereas many editions continue with the vision of the Holy One, the angels, and the future generations. Those additional words have not been silently inserted.
	•	Likewise, 6:6 in the supplied text reads አርዲስ, whereas common editions identify the descent as Mount Hermon in the days of Jared. The document preserves አርዲስ and does not substitute ሄርሞን.
	•	Proper names are the least useful place to force an English lexical gloss. They are therefore transliterated rather than assigned speculative meanings.

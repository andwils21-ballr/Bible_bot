Smooth: And again observe the days of summer — how the sun is upon it. You seek shade and shadow because of the heat of the sun; the earth also burns from the depth of the heat, and you cannot tread upon the earth or the rock because of its heat.

Smooth: But you — you have not been steadfast... Therefore you will curse your days, and the years of your life will perish... In those days you will give away your peace for an eternal curse. Notes on this rendering
• The Ge'ez text follows the form you supplied. Minor orthographic normalization has been applied only for readability in transliteration.

• “Smooth” supplies readable English while staying as close as possible to the force of the Ge'ez.
• Chapter 6 introduces the Watchers narrative. The list of angel names in 6:7 is only partially preserved in the supplied text; standard editions list twenty chiefs under Shemihazah.
• This is a working scholarly rendering, not a polished literary translation. Corrections and refinements to individual words are welcome.

# Rulings history

Where the rules in `RENDERING_SPEC.md` came from and what they replaced.
**Nothing here is a rule**; the spec is. Read this only to learn why a rule is
worded as it is, or when an older chapter predates a rule. Newest at the top.

- **2026-10-17, the instruction files.** The spec was split into Part 1 (every
  run) and Part 2 (one book), its history moved here, and the finished loop
  files moved to `archive/` (`CLAUDE.md` §8).
- **2026-10-07, *And it came to pass*.** Andrew replaced the rule of 2026-10-03,
  which kept every *when… , that…*, with the rule now in the spec (keep it only
  where it opens something). Every rendered book but Genesis was swept to it
  the same day; Genesis keeps every one by Andrew's ruling of that day.
- **2026-10-06, one word for one word.** "One Hebrew word, one English word"
  was taken out of `CLAUDE.md`; it was never Andrew's rule. The rule now is to
  keep a word the same where the source repeats it on purpose.
- **2026-10-03, *ve-attah*.** The *So / But now / Now / cut* rule was added
  beside *And now*, and every *And now* rendered before it was reviewed under it
  that day.
- **2026-10-03, two kinds of example.** The shadow run found notes saying a word
  was "fixed as" something in every run since 1 Kings 13, and wording copied
  from Samuel into Chronicles where the Hebrew differs (`HANDOFF.md`, "Drift").
  The fixed models came from that.
- **2026-09-28, *hinneh* / *idou*.** The ruling cutting *here —* and *look —* in
  every book replaced Andrew's ruling of 2026-09-25.
- **2026-09-27, Jubilees cut verses.** The rule filling them from the Asmara
  printing and Charles's Ge'ez replaced the rule of 2026-09-26. The repair is
  recorded in `PASTE/changes/15-jubilees.md`.
- **2026-09-27, 2 and 3 Meqabyan.** The Ge'ez of both was added, and they were
  put under the 1 Meqabyan rule.
- **2026-09-25, the readability pass.** Its eight patterns are Andrew's rulings
  on Exodus 16–19.

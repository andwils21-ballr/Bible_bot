# Rulings history

Where the rules in `RENDERING_SPEC.md` came from and what they replaced.
**Nothing here is a rule**; the spec is. Read this only to learn why a rule is
worded as it is, or when an older chapter predates a rule. Newest at the top.

- **2026-11-08, the map after Joshua 24.** Andrew replaced the journey map
  with a picture book of the taking of Ai, so the spec's one permitted link in
  a note (*See the map after Joshua 24, stage N*) went with it, and the
  fourteen pointers were taken out of the notes.

- **2026-11-07, less is more in the notes.** After Acts 27–28 Andrew said the
  notes, under the old Main and the new, showed the decision-making and were
  written to fill the count. The spec had required three to ten notes a
  chapter, and CLAUDE.md §3 allowed any *genuinely interesting connection*.
  The floor went (the checker now flags only more than ten), the test became
  *matters to the Bible as a whole or to how a main idea can be read*, and John
  1:1's *logos* joined Genesis 25 as a model. Acts 27–28 were cut from ten
  notes each to three and five. No sweep of earlier books.

- **2026-11-06, *shall* and *come near*.** After the Joshua 7 fix Andrew said
  *shall come near* bothered him more than the *by*. The spec had no rule for
  *shall*, and *come near* carried the Hebrew *qarav* word for word. He chose
  both for readability item 10, kept *shall* in prophecy, and left Genesis and
  Exodus out of the sweep. The same day *This and this* and *the name… is
  called* moved from item 9 to item 4.
- **2026-11-06, Joshua 7 and drift.** Andrew found Joshua 7 carrying Hebrew
  idioms word for word (*chased them from before the gate*, *turned its back
  before its enemies*, *come near by clans*) that earlier books had cleaned up.
  The readability pass gained the back idiom and item 9, and the sweep of the
  books after Joshua went to the council (`COUNCIL.md`). The spec's old
  parallel-passages example, Chronicles copying Samuel's *Now then*, *See now*
  and a dropped *And it came to pass*, was moved here from the spec.
- **2026-11-06, the future *And it shall come to pass*.** Andrew cut it in
  Joshua 2:14 and then asked for it to go from every book but Genesis wherever
  it does not carry a stretch of time of weight. The spec had kept every
  future *ve-hayah* in an instruction (rule of 2026-09-26 and 2026-10-07).
  93 verses were cut the same day, 47 openings kept (46 *in that day*-type, and Exodus 4:16); the
  list is in `PASTE/changes/6-joshua.md`.
- **2026-10-23, the map link.** Andrew asked for a map of the journey after
  Joshua 24, with links to it from notes between the Exodus and the end of
  Joshua. The link form in the spec's file format came from that; the site
  shows it as a link, the Word file as plain words.
- **2026-10-17, the instruction files.** The spec was split into Part 1 (every
  run) and Part 2 (one book), its history moved here, and the finished loop
  files moved to `archive/` (`CLAUDE.md` §8).
- **2026-10-07, *And it came to pass*.** Andrew replaced the rule of 2026-10-03,
  which kept every *when… , that…*, with the rule now in the spec (keep it only
  where it opens something). Every rendered book but Genesis was swept to it
  the same day; Genesis keeps every one by Andrew's ruling of that day.
- **2026-10-06, one word for one word.** "One Hebrew word, one English word"
  was taken out of `CLAUDE.md`; it was never Andrew's rule. The rule now is to
  keep a word the same where the source repeats it on purpose.
- **2026-10-03, *ve-attah*.** The *So / But now / Now / cut* rule was added
  beside *And now*, and every *And now* rendered before it was reviewed under it
  that day.
- **2026-10-03, two kinds of example.** The shadow run found notes saying a word
  was "fixed as" something in every run since 1 Kings 13, and wording copied
  from Samuel into Chronicles where the Hebrew differs (`HANDOFF.md`, "Drift").
  The fixed models came from that.
- **2026-09-28, *hinneh* / *idou*.** The ruling cutting *here —* and *look —* in
  every book replaced Andrew's ruling of 2026-09-25.
- **2026-09-27, Jubilees cut verses.** The rule filling them from the Asmara
  printing and Charles's Ge'ez replaced the rule of 2026-09-26. The repair is
  recorded in `PASTE/changes/15-jubilees.md`.
- **2026-09-27, 2 and 3 Meqabyan.** The Ge'ez of both was added, and they were
  put under the 1 Meqabyan rule.
- **2026-09-25, the readability pass.** Its eight patterns are Andrew's rulings
  on Exodus 16–19.

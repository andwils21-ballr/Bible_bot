# The Taking of Ai: picture book storyboard

A flip-through book of Joshua 7–8, one picture per page with a one-line caption.

**Step 1.** Send the **Setup** prompt below first. It makes no picture; it tells the
image generator what each person and place looks like, so all ten pictures match.

**Step 2.** Then send the pages one at a time, copying each **Prompt** paragraph.
Each one describes only what is in that scene; how to paint it is left to you.
When a picture is done, send it back and say which page it is.

---

## Setup (send first)

**Prompt:** Don't make a picture yet. I'm going to ask you for ten pictures, one at a time, that tell one story from the Bible: the Israelite army capturing the town of Ai, in the hill country of Canaan, around 1400 BC. Whenever any of the people or things below appear in a picture, give them these details, the same way every time, so all ten pictures look like they belong to one book. When you've read this, just reply "Ready" and wait for the first scene.

JOSHUA, the Israelite commander: an older man, but still lean, tough and strong. Weathered, sun-darkened face with deep lines, short grey-streaked beard, short grey hair, bareheaded. A dark brown wool cloak over a knee-length tan tunic, a wide leather belt, leather sandals. He always carries the same weapon: a javelin, a throwing spear about as tall as he is, with a bronze tip. He is the only one wearing a dark brown cloak.

THE ELDERS, the leaders of Israel: a handful of old men with long white or grey beards, in long plain wool robes of muted tan and grey, with a tassel hanging at each of the four corners of their robes, and a blue cord in each tassel. They carry no weapons.

THE ISRAELITE SOLDIERS: lean, bearded men with olive-brown skin and dark hair. Knee-length tunics of undyed, tan or brown wool, never red. Leather belts and sandals; some wear a simple cloth wrapped around the head. They carry bronze-tipped spears, javelins, slings, and round shields of leather stretched over wood. No helmets with horns or crests, no chainmail, no metal armor plates, nothing Roman or medieval.

THE SOLDIERS OF AI: Canaanite fighters, also bearded and dark-haired, but always in red tunics, so they can be told from the Israelites at a glance. They carry bronze spears and curved bronze swords shaped like a sickle.

THE KING OF AI: a heavy-set, black-bearded man in a long red robe with a gold-embroidered border, a plain gold band around his head, and a curved bronze sword at his belt.

THE TOWN OF AI: a small town on top of a rocky hill, not a city and not a castle. One thick wall of rough, unshaped grey limestone blocks runs around it, with a single gate of heavy wooden doors between two low, square towers. No pointed battlements, no turrets, nothing like a medieval castle. Inside, flat-roofed houses of mud brick and stone are packed close together. North of the town is a shallow valley; to the west, olive trees and broken rocky ground; to the east, the land drops away toward empty desert.

THE ARK OF THE COVENANT: a wooden chest about 3¾ feet long and 2¼ feet wide and high, covered all over in gold, with a gold rim around its top edge and four gold rings at its feet. Two long gold-covered carrying poles run through the rings, one along each side. Its lid is solid gold, and at each end of the lid stands a golden winged figure; the two face each other, looking down at the lid, with their wings spread upward over it.

THE ISRAELITE CAMP: rows of low, dark tents of black goat-hair cloth, with campfires between them.

THE LAND: the rocky hill country of Canaan: grey limestone ridges and boulders, terraced slopes, scattered olive trees, scrub, and dry golden grass.

## Page 1 — The first attack fails

**Caption:** Three thousand went up against Ai, and fled before its men. *(7:4–5)*

**Prompt:** A battle on a rocky hillside. About three thousand Israelite soldiers are running downhill in a panic, fleeing from the town of Ai on top of the hill and looking back over their shoulders in fear. They've thrown down their shields and spears, which lie scattered on the rocks. Soldiers of Ai are charging out of the open gate after them with spears raised. A few of the fleeing men have been cut down on the slope. Dust everywhere. Joshua is not in this picture. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 2 — Joshua before the ark

**Caption:** Joshua tore his clothes and fell on his face before the ark until evening. *(7:6)*

**Prompt:** Evening in the Israelite camp. Joshua lies face down in the dirt in front of the ark of the covenant, overcome with grief, the neck of his tunic ripped open the way mourners did. The elders kneel and bow low around him, with dirt thrown on their heads. Low evening light, long shadows, a heavy, quiet sadness. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 3 — Thirty thousand sent by night

**Caption:** Joshua chose thirty thousand mighty men and sent them out by night. *(8:3)*

**Prompt:** Nighttime under a bright moon. A long line of Israelite soldiers quietly slips out of the camp and climbs a narrow rocky canyon into the hills, crouched low. Joshua stands on a boulder beside the trail and points up the canyon with his javelin, sending them on. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 4 — Hidden behind the city

**Caption:** The ambush lay hidden between Bethel and Ai, west of the city. *(8:9, 12)*

**Prompt:** Just before sunrise, in grey early light. Hundreds of Israelite soldiers lie flat and crouch out of sight among boulders, olive trees and tall dry grass on the hillside west of Ai. We see them from behind: just ahead rises the back wall of the town, quiet and unaware. The soldiers grip their spears and stare at the wall, tense, waiting for the signal to attack. Joshua is not in this picture. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 5 — The bait at dawn

**Caption:** Joshua's army camped north of Ai, in plain sight across the valley. *(8:11, 14)*

**Prompt:** Early morning. A large Israelite army stands out in the open on a hillside north of Ai, in full view of the town across the shallow valley. Joshua stands at the front with the elders. The town's gate has swung open, and the king of Ai is rushing out at the head of his soldiers, eager for a fight. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 6 — The pretended flight

**Caption:** Israel fled toward the wilderness, and all Ai poured out after them, leaving the city open. *(8:15–17)*

**Prompt:** The valley below Ai, sloping down toward empty desert. The Israelite army has turned and is running away toward the desert as if beaten. Behind them, every soldier of Ai is chasing after them, cheering and shouting as if they've already won. Far back on the hill, the town's gate hangs wide open and its walls are empty. Among the running Israelites, Joshua glances back over his shoulder. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 7 — The javelin

**Caption:** "Stretch out the javelin in your hand toward Ai." *(8:18)*

**Prompt:** Joshua stands alone on a rocky ridge, seen from below against a bright sky. He holds his arm straight out, pointing his javelin at the town of Ai on a distant hill, as a signal. His cloak whips in the wind. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 8 — The ambush takes the city

**Caption:** The ambush rose, ran into the city, and set it on fire. *(8:19)*

**Prompt:** Israelite soldiers jump up from hiding places among rocks and olive trees and sprint through the open gate of Ai, which has been left completely empty. Some carry flaming torches. Inside the walls the first houses are catching fire, with flames and black smoke starting to rise above the wall. Joshua is not in this picture. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 9 — Caught between

**Caption:** The men of Ai looked back: the smoke of their city was going up to the heavens. *(8:20–22)*

**Prompt:** The valley floor below Ai. The soldiers of Ai have stopped dead and turned around in shock: their town behind them is on fire, with a huge column of black smoke rising into the sky. In front of them, the Israelite army, which had been running away, has turned and is charging straight at them. Behind them, more Israelite soldiers are pouring down from the burning town. They are trapped in the middle. Off in the distance, on a ridge, Joshua is still holding his javelin pointed toward the town. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 10 — The heap

**Caption:** Joshua burned Ai and made it a heap forever; a great heap of stones lay at its gate. *(8:28–29)*

**Prompt:** Sunset over the blackened ruins of Ai. Its walls have collapsed, and thin smoke still drifts up from the rubble. In front of the broken gateway stands a tall pile of stones. Joshua stands quietly beside it with his javelin lowered, a few of his soldiers behind him, all looking at what is left of the town. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

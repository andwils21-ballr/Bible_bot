# The Taking of Ai: picture book storyboard

A flip-through book of Joshua 7–8, one picture per page with a one-line caption.
For each page, copy the whole **Prompt** paragraph into the image generator. Each
prompt describes only what is in that one scene; how to paint it is left to you.
When a picture is done, send it back and say which page it is.

---

## Page 1 — The first attack fails

**Caption:** Three thousand went up against Ai, and fled before its men. *(7:4–5)*

**Prompt:** A battle on a rocky hillside in ancient Canaan. About three thousand Israelite soldiers in plain brown tunics are running downhill in a panic, fleeing from a small stone-walled town on top of the hill and looking back over their shoulders in fear. They've thrown down their round leather shields and bronze spears, which lie scattered on the rocks. Soldiers from the town, in red tunics, are charging out of the open gate after them with spears raised. A few of the fleeing men have been cut down on the slope. Dust everywhere. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 2 — Joshua before the ark

**Caption:** Joshua tore his clothes and fell on his face before the ark until evening. *(7:6)*

**Prompt:** Evening in an ancient army camp of dark tents. Joshua, the Israelite commander, a lean, weathered man with a grey-streaked beard, lies face down in the dirt, overcome with grief, the neck of his brown robe ripped open the way mourners did. In front of him sits the ark of the covenant: a gold-covered wooden chest with two golden winged figures facing each other on the lid and long carrying poles along its sides. A group of older men, the leaders of Israel, kneel and bow around him with dirt thrown on their heads. Low evening light, long shadows, a heavy, quiet sadness. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 3 — Thirty thousand sent by night

**Caption:** Joshua chose thirty thousand mighty men and sent them out by night. *(8:3)*

**Prompt:** Nighttime under a bright moon. A long line of ancient Israelite soldiers with spears and round shields quietly slips out of a camp and climbs a narrow rocky canyon into the hills, crouched low. Joshua, their commander, a lean man with a grey-streaked beard and a brown cloak, stands on a boulder beside the trail and points up the canyon with his javelin, sending them on. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 4 — Hidden behind the city

**Caption:** The ambush lay hidden between Bethel and Ai, west of the city. *(8:9, 12)*

**Prompt:** Just before sunrise, in grey early light. Hundreds of ancient Israelite soldiers in brown tunics lie flat and crouch out of sight among boulders, olive trees and tall dry grass on a hillside. We see them from behind: just ahead rises the back wall of a small stone town on a hilltop, quiet and unaware. The soldiers grip their spears and stare at the wall, tense, waiting for the signal to attack. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 5 — The bait at dawn

**Caption:** Joshua's army camped north of Ai, in plain sight across the valley. *(8:11, 14)*

**Prompt:** Early morning. A large ancient Israelite army in plain brown tunics stands out in the open on a hillside, in full view of a small walled town across a shallow valley. Joshua, their commander, a lean man with a grey-streaked beard and a brown cloak, stands at the front with a few older leaders. The town's gate has swung open, and the king of Ai, in a red robe, is rushing out at the head of his soldiers in red tunics, eager for a fight. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 6 — The pretended flight

**Caption:** Israel fled toward the wilderness, and all Ai poured out after them, leaving the city open. *(8:15–17)*

**Prompt:** A wide valley sloping down toward empty desert. An ancient Israelite army in brown tunics has turned and is running away toward the desert as if beaten. Behind them, every soldier from the hilltop town, all in red tunics, is chasing after them, cheering and shouting as if they've already won. Far back on the hill, the town's gate hangs wide open and its walls are empty. Among the running Israelites, Joshua, a grey-bearded commander in a brown cloak, glances back over his shoulder. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 7 — The javelin

**Caption:** "Stretch out the javelin in your hand toward Ai." *(8:18)*

**Prompt:** Joshua, an ancient Israelite commander, a lean, weathered man with a grey-streaked beard, stands alone on a rocky ridge, seen from below against a bright sky. He holds his arm straight out, pointing a bronze-tipped javelin at a small walled town on a distant hill, as a signal. His brown cloak whips in the wind. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 8 — The ambush takes the city

**Caption:** The ambush rose, ran into the city, and set it on fire. *(8:19)*

**Prompt:** Ancient Israelite soldiers in brown tunics jump up from hiding places among rocks and olive trees and sprint through the open gate of a small stone town that has been left completely empty. Some carry flaming torches. Inside the walls the first flat-roofed houses are catching fire, with flames and black smoke starting to rise above the wall. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 9 — Caught between

**Caption:** The men of Ai looked back: the smoke of their city was going up to the heavens. *(8:20–22)*

**Prompt:** A valley floor below a hilltop town in ancient Canaan. The town's soldiers, in red tunics, have stopped dead and turned around in shock: their town behind them is on fire, with a huge column of black smoke rising into the sky. In front of them, the Israelite army in brown tunics, which had been running away, has turned and is charging straight at them. Behind them, more Israelite soldiers are pouring down from the burning town. They are trapped in the middle. Off in the distance, on a ridge, Joshua, the grey-bearded Israelite commander, is still holding his javelin pointed toward the town. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

## Page 10 — The heap

**Caption:** Joshua burned Ai and made it a heap forever; a great heap of stones lay at its gate. *(8:28–29)*

**Prompt:** Sunset over the blackened ruins of a small hilltop town in ancient Canaan. Its stone walls have collapsed, and thin smoke still drifts up from the rubble. In front of the broken gateway stands a tall pile of stones. Joshua, a grey-bearded commander in a brown cloak, stands quietly beside it with his javelin lowered, a few of his soldiers behind him, all looking at what is left of the town. Wide (landscape), no text or lettering.

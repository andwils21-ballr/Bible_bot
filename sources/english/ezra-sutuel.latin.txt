3:1	In the thirtieth year after the ruin of the city, I, Salathiel (who am also Ezra), was in Babylon. And I was troubled as I lay upon my bed, and my thoughts welled up in my heart,
3:2	because I saw the desolation of Zion and the wealth of those who lived in Babylon.
3:3	And my spirit was greatly agitated, and I began to speak words of fear to the Most High,
3:4	and I said: "O Sovereign Lord, did you not speak at the beginning, when you formed the earth—and that alone—and commanded the dust,
3:5	and it gave Adam a lifeless body? Yet it was the workmanship of your hands, and you breathed into him the breath of life, and he was made alive before you.
3:6	And you led him into paradise, which your right hand had planted before the earth appeared.
3:7	And to him you commanded one observance of yours, but he transgressed it, and immediately you appointed death for him and for his generations. And from him were born nations and tribes, peoples and clans, of which there is no number.
3:8	And every nation walked according to its own will, and they acted godlessly before you and despised your commandments, and you did not prevent them.
3:9	Then again, in due time, you brought the flood upon the inhabitants of the world and destroyed them.
3:10	And their fate was one: just as death came to Adam, so the flood came to these.
3:11	Yet you left one of them, Noah with his household, and from him all the righteous.
3:12	And it came to pass, when those who lived on the earth began to multiply, and produced children and peoples and many nations, they began again to practice godlessness more than the former ones.
3:13	And it came to pass, when they were committing iniquity before you, you chose for yourself one from among them, whose name was Abraham.
3:14	And you loved him, and to him alone you showed the end of the times secretly by night.
3:15	And you established an eternal covenant with him and told him that you would never forsake his descendants. And you gave him Isaac, and to Isaac you gave Jacob and Esau.
3:16	And you set Jacob apart for yourself, but Esau you put aside; and Jacob became a great multitude.
3:17	And it came to pass, when you led his descendants out of Egypt, you brought them to Mount Sinai.
3:18	You bent down the heavens and established the earth, and shook the world and made the deeps tremble, and threw the age into turmoil.
3:19	And your glory passed through four gates—of fire, and earthquake, and wind, and frost—to give the law to the seed of Jacob and the commandment to the generation of Israel.
3:20	Yet you did not take away from them their evil heart, that your law might bear fruit in them.
3:21	For the first Adam, burdened with an evil heart, transgressed and was overcome, as were all who were born of him.
3:22	Thus the weakness became permanent: the law was in the heart of the people along with the evil root; and what was good departed, and the evil remained.
3:23	And the times passed and the years were ended, and you raised up for yourself a servant named David.
3:24	And you told him to build a city for your name, and to offer oblations to you in it from what is yours.
3:25	And this was done for many years. But the inhabitants of the city went astray,
3:26	doing in all things just as Adam and all his generations had done; for they also possessed an evil heart.
3:27	And you handed your city over into the hands of your enemies.
3:28	And I said then in my heart: 'Are the deeds of those who inhabit Babylon any better, and is this why she rules over Zion?'
3:29	For when I came here, I saw impieties without number, and my soul has seen many sinners in this thirtieth year, and my heart failed me,
3:30	because I saw how you endure those who sin and have spared those who act godlessly, and have destroyed your people and preserved your enemies, and have not made known
3:31	anything to anyone as to how this way may be forsaken. Does Babylon do better than Zion,
3:32	or has any other nation known you besides Israel? Or what tribes have believed your covenants as Jacob has,
3:33	whose reward has not appeared, nor has their labor borne fruit? For I have traveled extensively among the nations, and I saw that they prosper and are unmindful of your commandments.
3:34	Now therefore, weigh our iniquities and those of the inhabitants of the world on a balance, and the turn of the scale will be found where it inclines.
3:35	Or when have the inhabitants of the earth not sinned in your sight? Or what nation has kept your commandments in such a way?
3:36	You may indeed find individual men who have kept your commandments by name, but nations you will not find."
4:1	Then the angel who had been sent to me, whose name was Uriel, answered me
4:2	and said to me: "Your heart has utterly failed in this age, and do you think you can comprehend the way of the Most High?"
4:3	And I said: "Yes, my lord." And he answered me and said: "I have been sent to show you three ways and to set before you three parables.
4:4	If you can declare one of these to me, I also will show you the way you desire to see, and I will teach you why the heart is evil."
4:5	And I said: "Speak, my lord." And he said to me: "Go, weigh for me the weight of fire, or measure for me the blast of the wind, or call back for me the day that has passed."
4:6	And I answered and said: "Who among mortals can do this, that you should ask me about these things?"
4:7	And he said to me: "If I had asked you, saying: 'How many dwellings are in the heart of the sea, or how many springs are at the source of the deep, or how many streams are above the firmament, or what are the exits of paradise?'
4:8	perhaps you would have said to me: 'I have not descended into the deep, nor into Hades yet, nor have I ever ascended into heaven.'
4:9	But now I have not asked you except about fire and wind and the day through which you have passed—things from which you cannot be separated—and you have not answered me about them."
4:10	And he said to me: "You cannot understand the things that are your own, which have grown up with you;
4:11	how then can your vessel comprehend the way of the Most High? ... And how can one already worn out by a corrupt world understand incorruption?" And when I heard these things, I fell upon my face
4:12	and said to him: "It would be better for us not to be here than to come and live in ungodliness, and suffer, and not understand why."
4:13	And he answered me and said: "I went forth to the trees of the forest of the plain, and they took counsel
4:14	and said: 'Come, let us go and wage war against the sea, that it may recede before us, and that we may make for ourselves other forests.'"
4:15	And in like manner the waves of the sea also devised a plan and said, 'Come, let us go up and conquer the forest of the plain, so that there also we may make for ourselves another region.'
4:16	And the plan of the forest was in vain, for fire came and consumed it.
4:17	Likewise also the plan of the waves of the sea, for the sand stood firm and stopped them.
4:18	For if you were a judge of these, whom would you begin to justify, or whom to condemn?
4:19	And I answered and said, 'Both devised a vain plan, for the land was given to the forest, and the sea's place to bear its waves.'
4:20	And he answered me and said, 'You have judged well; why then have you not judged for yourself?'
4:21	For just as the land was given to the forest and the sea to its waves, so also those who dwell upon the earth can understand only what is upon the earth, and those who are above the heavens what is in the height of the heavens.
4:22	And I answered and said, 'I beseech you, Lord, why then was understanding given to me?'
4:23	For I did not wish to inquire about the higher ways, but about those things that pass through us daily: why Israel has been given over to the nations as a reproach, why the people whom you loved has been given to godless tribes, and the law of our fathers has been brought to destruction, and the written covenants are nowhere to be found.
4:24	And we pass away from the world like locusts, and our life is like a mist, and we are not worthy to obtain mercy.
4:25	But what will he do for his name which has been invoked over us? Concerning these things I asked.
4:26	And he answered me and said, 'If you exist you will see, and if you live you will marvel often, because the age is hastening swiftly to pass away.'
4:27	For it is not able to bear what has been promised for the righteous in their times, because this age is full of sorrow and infirmities.
4:28	For evil has been sown, concerning which you ask me, and its harvesting has not yet come.
4:29	If therefore that which was sown is not reaped, and the place where evil was sown does not pass away, the field where the good is sown will not come.
4:30	For a grain of evil seed was sown in the heart of Adam from the beginning, and how much ungodliness it has produced until now, and will produce until the threshing floor comes!
4:31	Consider now for yourself how much fruit of ungodliness a grain of evil seed has produced.
4:32	When countless ears of grain are sown, how great a threshing floor they will begin to make!
4:33	And I answered and said, 'How and when will these things be? Why are our years so few and evil?'
4:34	And he answered me and said, 'Do not hasten in spirit above the Most High; for you hasten on account of your own self, but the Most High on behalf of many.'
4:35	Did not the souls of the righteous in their chambers ask concerning these things, saying, 'How long must I hope like this? And when does the fruit of the harvest of our reward come?'
4:36	And Jeremiel the archangel answered them and said, 'When the number of those like you is completed; for he has weighed the age in the balance,
4:37	and with a measure he has measured the times, and by number he has numbered the times, and he will not move nor awaken them until the prescribed measure is fulfilled.'
4:38	And I answered and said, 'O Sovereign Lord, but we also are all full of ungodliness.'
4:39	'And perhaps on our account the harvest of the righteous is hindered, because of the sins of those who inhabit the earth.'
4:40	And he answered me and said, 'Go and ask a pregnant woman whether, when she has completed her nine months, her womb can still retain the fetus within itself.'
4:41	And I said, 'It cannot, my lord.' And he said to me, 'In the underworld the chambers of souls are like the womb.'
4:42	For just as a woman in labor hastens to escape the anguish of childbirth, so also these hasten to give back what has been entrusted to them
4:43	from the beginning. Then it will be shown to you concerning the things you desire to see.'
4:44	And I answered and said, 'If I have found favor before your eyes, and if it is possible, and if I am worthy,
4:45	show me this also: whether more than what has passed is yet to come, or whether more has passed by us;
4:46	for what has passed I know, but what is to come I do not know.'
4:47	And he said to me, 'Stand on the right side, and I will show you the interpretation of the parable.'
4:48	And I stood and looked, and behold, a burning furnace passed before me; and it happened that when the flame had passed, I looked, and behold, smoke remained.
4:49	And after this a cloud full of water passed before me and poured out a great downpour of rain with violence; and when the violence of the rain had passed, drops remained in it.
4:50	And he said to me, 'Consider for yourself: just as the downpour exceeds the drops, and the fire exceeds the smoke, so the measure that has passed was far greater, while the drops and smoke remain.'
4:51	And I prayed and said, 'Do you think I shall live until those days? Or who will be alive in those days?'
4:52	He answered me and said, 'Concerning the signs about which you ask me, I can tell you in part; but concerning your life, I was not sent to tell you, for I do not know.'

5:9	and salt waters shall be found in the sweet. And all friends shall war against one another; and understanding shall then hide itself, and insight shall withdraw into its chamber.

5:15	And the angel who had come to speak with me took hold of me, strengthened me, and set me on my feet.

5:20	And I fasted for seven days, wailing and weeping, just as the angel Uriel had commanded me.

7:7	but its entrance is narrow and set upon a precipice, so that there is fire on the right hand, and deep water on the left.

7:9	If now that city is given to a man as an inheritance, unless the heir passes through the danger set before it, how will he receive his inheritance?"

7:11	For I made the world for their sake; and when Adam transgressed my statutes, what had been made was judged.

7:13	For the entrances of the greater world are spacious and secure, and yield the fruit of immortality.
7:14	Therefore, unless the living enter into these narrow and futile conditions, they will never be able to receive the things that are stored up.

7:16	And why have you not considered in your heart what is to come, but only what is present?"

7:18	The righteous, therefore, endure narrow things while hoping for wide things; but those who have acted wickedly have suffered the narrow things and yet will not see the wide."

14:17	For truth has departed further away, and falsehood has drawn near; for now the eagle is hastening to come, which you saw in the vision."
14:18	And I answered and said, "Let me speak before you, Lord.
14:19	For behold, I will go as you have commanded me, and I will reprove the present people; but who will admonish those who will be born afterward?
14:20	For the world is set in darkness, and those who dwell in it are without light,
14:21	because your law has been burned; therefore no one knows the things that have been done by you, or what works are about to begin.
14:22	If, then, I have found favor before you, send the Holy Spirit into me, and I will write everything that has taken place in the world from the beginning, the things that were written in your Law, so that people may be able to find the path, and that those who wish to live in the last days may live."
14:23	And he answered me and said, "Go, gather the people, and tell them not to seek you for forty days.
14:24	But prepare for yourself many writing tablets, and take with you Sarea, Dabria, Selemia, Ethan, and Asiel—these five who are prepared to write rapidly.
14:25	And you shall come here, and I will light in your heart the lamp of understanding, which shall not be put out until what you are about to write is completed.
14:26	And when you have finished, some things you shall make public, and some you shall deliver secretly to the wise. Tomorrow at this hour you shall begin to write."
14:27	Then I went, as he commanded me, and gathered all the people and said:
14:28	"Hear these words, O Israel:
14:29	Our fathers lived as strangers in Egypt from the beginning, and they were delivered from there.
14:30	And they received the law of life, which they did not keep, and which you also have transgressed after them.
14:31	And a land was given to you as an inheritance in the land of Zion; but you and your fathers committed iniquity and did not keep the ways that the Most High commanded you.
14:32	Because he is a just judge, he took away from you in due time what he had given.
14:33	And now you are here, and your brothers are farther in exile.
14:34	If, therefore, you will rule over your understanding and discipline your heart, you will be kept alive, and after death you will obtain mercy.
14:35	For judgment comes after death, when we will live again; and then the names of the righteous will become manifest, and the deeds of the ungodly will be shown.
14:36	Therefore, let no one approach me now, nor seek me for forty days."
14:37	So I took the five men, as he commanded me, and we went out into the field and remained there.
14:38	And on the next day, behold, a voice called me, saying, "Ezra, open your mouth and drink what I give you to drink."
14:39	Then I opened my mouth, and behold, a full cup was offered to me; it was full of something like water, but its color was like fire.
14:40	And I took it and drank; and when I had drunk it, my heart poured forth understanding, and wisdom increased in my breast, for my spirit retained its memory;
14:41	and my mouth was opened and was closed no longer.
14:42	And the Most High gave understanding to the five men, and they wrote down what was spoken in order, using characters that they did not know; and they sat for forty days. They wrote during the day,
14:43	and at night they ate bread; but as for me, I spoke by day and was not silent by night.
14:44	So in forty days, ninety-four books were written.
14:45	And it came to pass, when the forty days were completed, that the Most High spoke, saying, "Make public the first books that you wrote, and let the worthy and the unworthy read them;
14:46	but keep the seventy most recent ones, so that you may deliver them to the wise among your people.
14:47	For in them is the spring of understanding, the fountain of wisdom, and the stream of knowledge." And I did so.
